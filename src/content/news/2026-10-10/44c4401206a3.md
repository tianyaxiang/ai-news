# Google teases Fitbit Edge launch next week

Google 预告 Fitbit Edge 将于下周发布。

The Fitbit Edge will launch on October 12th.

Fitbit Edge 将于 10 月 12 日发布。

It looks like Google is getting ready to take the wraps off its rumored Fitbit Edge next Monday. In a post on X, Google posted a picture of what appears to be the side of the fitness tracker, with the caption: “Get an Edge on your health. 10.12.26.”

看起来 Google 正准备在下周一揭开传闻已久的 Fitbit Edge 的面纱。Google 在 X 上发布了一张照片，展示了这款健身追踪器的侧面，并配文称：“Get an Edge on your health. 10.12.26。”

A trove of Fitbit Edge leaks has surfaced over the past couple of weeks. So far, details shared by Dealabs and Android Headlines suggest that the upcoming device could feature a 1.34-inch always-on OLED touchscreen, built-in GPS, and several other sensors, including an accelerometer, barometer, altimeter, and gyroscope. It may also come with optical sensors for measuring heart rate and blood oxygen saturation (SpO2) and electrical sensors for capturing ECGs.

过去几周里，关于 Fitbit Edge 的泄露消息层出不穷。目前，Dealabs 和 Android Headlines 分享的细节显示，这款即将推出的设备可能配备 1.34 英寸常亮 OLED 触摸屏、内置 GPS 以及其他多种传感器，包括加速度计、气压计、高度计和陀螺仪。它还可能配备用于测量心率和血氧饱和度 (SpO2) 的光学传感器，以及用于捕捉心电图 (ECG) 的电传感器。

As reported by Dealabs, the tracker may also be able to notify wearers about potential atrial fibrillation and irregular heart rhythms. The Fitbit Edge is rumored to come with a replaceable lithium-ion battery with up to seven days of battery life. Rumors suggest that it could cost around $180 in the US when it launches next week.

据 Dealabs 报道，该追踪器或许还能向佩戴者发出关于潜在心房颤动和心律不齐的提醒。有传言称，Fitbit Edge 将配备可更换的锂离子电池，续航时间长达七天。传闻还显示，该产品下周在美国发布时的售价可能在 180 美元左右。
