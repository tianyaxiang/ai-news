# Best Battery-Powered Leaf Blowers (2026): Tested for Power, Battery Life, and Noise

Leaves are a problem of addition by subtraction: Just a couple of mature trees can drop enough debris that you need to remove, and in the process, consume hours of your weekends. Mowing leaves into mulch is a compelling option, but you’ll still need to wrangle them off driveways, walkways, flower beds, and patios. Gas blowers have long been the standard, but rising noise bans, finicky engines that are hard to start, and battery advances are cutting into their appeal.

落叶问题就像是“减法中的加法”：仅仅几棵成熟的树木掉落的残枝败叶就足以让你不得不去清理，而这一过程会占用你周末数小时的时间。将落叶粉碎成覆盖物是一个不错的选择，但你仍然需要把它们从车道、人行道、花坛和露台上清理干净。燃油吹叶机长期以来一直是行业标准，但日益增多的噪音禁令、难以启动的挑剔引擎以及电池技术的进步，正在削弱它们的吸引力。

If you’re on the fence about a leaf blower, know that owning one of any kind will reduce the amount of raking you do, but rarely eliminate it. For small yards, or if you (or your neighbor) have just one medium-sized deciduous tree, a rake and a tarp may be all you need. On the other hand, if you live in an arboretum, the long run time of a gas backpack blower may be worth the maintenance headaches—unless you’re willing to invest heavily in multiple batteries.

如果你还在犹豫是否要购买吹叶机，请记住，拥有任何一种吹叶机都能减少你的耙地工作量，但几乎无法完全免除这项工作。对于小院子，或者如果你（或你的邻居）只有一棵中等大小的落叶树，一把耙子和一块防水布可能就足够了。另一方面，如果你住在一个植物园里，燃油背负式吹叶机的超长续航时间可能值得你忍受维护上的麻烦——除非你愿意投入大量资金购买多块电池。

I’ve tested the newest crop of battery-powered blowers on everything from heavy fabric leaves to gravel, mulch, and even knocking over a concrete paver. Today’s models deliver serious clearing power without the gas-can hassle, though run time is always the primary trade-off. Here’s everything you need to know about battery-powered blowers.

我已经测试了最新一批电池驱动的吹叶机，测试对象涵盖了从厚重的织物叶片到砾石、覆盖物，甚至是推倒混凝土铺路砖。如今的机型在无需处理燃油罐麻烦的情况下，提供了强大的清理能力，尽管续航时间始终是主要的权衡因素。以下是你需要了解的关于电池驱动吹叶机的一切。

### Best Overall: Ego Power+ 880 CFM Blower

When factoring in overall performance along with the quality and diversity of Ego’s outdoor tool ecosystem, this blower is the best option for most users. In testing, it delivered gaslike power, easily knocking over a 5-pound brick paver on high speed where lesser machines required turbo mode, and it cleared the test patch of leaves without issue, without relying on turbo. Even with all that power, it was the second-quietest machine on turbo, registering about 89.7 decibels at an arm’s-length distance of 30 inches—though Ego still suggests hearing protection.

### 最佳综合表现：Ego Power+ 880 CFM 吹叶机

综合考虑整体性能以及 Ego 户外工具生态系统的质量和多样性，这款吹叶机是大多数用户的最佳选择。在测试中，它展现出了媲美燃油机的动力，在高速档下就能轻松推倒一块 5 磅重的砖块铺路石，而其他较弱的机器则需要开启涡轮模式才能做到；它在不依赖涡轮模式的情况下，也毫无问题地清理了测试区域的落叶。尽管动力强劲，但它在涡轮模式下是测试中噪音第二小的机器，在 30 英寸（约 76 厘米）的手臂距离处测得约 89.7 分贝——尽管 Ego 仍然建议佩戴听力保护装置。

### Best Budget: Skil Pwr Core 40 Brushless 40V 650 CFM Leaf Blower Kit

The Skil checks two important boxes for a battery-powered leaf blower: It was the least expensive model we tested and tied for the longest run time, at just over 33 minutes on high. Skil, a sister company to Ego, couldn’t match our top pick’s power—it would only knock over the brick on turbo and took a bit longer to push a brick-topped tarp—but it’s capable for most common leaf-blowing jobs. It was also the quietest blower in our test, registering 78.6 decibels on high, though Skil still recommends hearing protection.

### 最佳性价比：Skil Pwr Core 40 无刷 40V 650 CFM 吹叶机套装

Skil 满足了电池驱动吹叶机的两个重要指标：它是我们测试中最便宜的机型，并且续航时间并列第一，在高档位下可运行超过 33 分钟。作为 Ego 的姊妹公司，Skil 的动力无法与我们的首选产品相提并论——它只能在涡轮模式下推倒砖块，且推动覆盖砖块的防水布所需时间稍长——但它足以胜任大多数常见的吹叶工作。它也是我们测试中最安静的吹叶机，在高档位下测得 78.6 分贝，不过 Skil 仍然建议佩戴听力保护装置。
