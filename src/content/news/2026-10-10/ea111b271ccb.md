# Your artwork could be featured in the next Debian release

Debian Linux 项目已针对即将发布的 Forky (Debian 14) 发出社区艺术作品征集令。如果你认为自己具备艺术天赋，你的作品就有可能出现在数百万用户的启动界面、登录提示和桌面壁纸上。提交截止日期为 11 月 26 日。

Continuing Debian’s community-driven ethos, the artwork submission process mirrors that of previous releases. The artwork must feel distinctively “Debian,” integrate cleanly without the need for core software patching, and remain visually appealing for the long term.

秉承 Debian 社区驱动的理念，此次艺术作品提交流程与往届发布保持一致。作品必须具有鲜明的“Debian”特色，能够完美集成而无需修补核心软件，并能长期保持视觉吸引力。

Elise Couper’s “Ceratopsian” theme was chosen as the winning default for Debian 13 Trixie. For anyone who might not be up on the latest from the Toy Story franchise, the release names echo character names from the animated films. Trixie was a triceratops introduced in Toy Story 3, hence the “Ceratopsian” wink in the winning submission.

Elise Couper 的“Ceratopsian”主题被选为 Debian 13 Trixie 的默认获胜作品。对于那些不太了解《玩具总动员》系列最新动态的人来说，Debian 的发布代号均取自该动画电影中的角色名。Trixie 是《玩具总动员 3》中引入的一只三角龙，因此获胜作品中包含了对“角龙类”的致敬。

It makes sense that the next version of Debian continues this trend with Forky, a character introduced in the franchise’s fourth entry. The Debian team cautions against overly busy designs that may cause user fatigue, but if anyone can successfully incorporate Forky’s googly eyes into the design somehow, know that you’d at least win the contest for my Debian desktop wallpaper.

Debian 的下一个版本延续这一趋势，采用在系列第四部中登场的角色 Forky 作为代号，这在情理之中。Debian 团队提醒创作者避免使用过于繁杂的设计以免引起视觉疲劳，但如果有人能设法将 Forky 的大眼睛巧妙地融入设计中，请相信，你至少会赢得我个人 Debian 桌面壁纸的评选。

Debian celebrated its 33rd anniversary in August 2026 and continues to position itself as the “universal operating system,” with support and compatibility stretching across different hardware architectures. Debian’s latest point release, Trixie 13.7, arrived on September 12, 2026.

Debian 于 2026 年 8 月庆祝了其成立 33 周年，并继续将自身定位为“通用操作系统”，其支持和兼容性涵盖了各种不同的硬件架构。Debian 的最新点版本 Trixie 13.7 已于 2026 年 9 月 12 日发布。

Forky (Debian 14) is now in the later stages of its development cycle, with the artwork selection process marking one of the final boxes to be checked before the release is made available. Interested artists can review the submission requirements on the Debian Wiki.

Forky (Debian 14) 目前已进入开发周期的后期阶段，艺术作品的选择过程是该版本发布前需要完成的最后几项工作之一。感兴趣的艺术家可以在 Debian Wiki 上查看提交要求。
