Losing a good backlink is silent. Nobody emails you when a partner redesigns their site and drops your link, or when a competitor gets a mention in a big publication. The large SEO suites will alert you, but they're priced as monthly subscriptions, and often you only need the alert for a handful of domains. This post covers: where backlink data comes from (and why scraping an SEO tool's pages is a bad idea), how to turn "all backlinks" into "what changed since last time", what it costs, with numbers from a real run.

失去一个优质的反向链接往往是悄无声息的。当合作伙伴改版网站删除了你的链接，或者竞争对手在大型出版物中获得提及，没人会发邮件通知你。大型 SEO 套件虽然能提供提醒，但它们通常按月订阅收费，而你往往只需要监控少数几个域名。本文将介绍：反向链接数据的来源（以及为什么抓取 SEO 工具页面不是个好主意）、如何将“所有反向链接”转化为“自上次检查以来的变化”，以及实际运行的成本数据。

Where backlink data comes from: You can't check backlinks by visiting your own site. A backlink lives on someone else's page, so you need a crawler that has seen a large part of the web and kept the link graph. Building that yourself isn't realistic, and scraping a vendor's web UI breaks often and goes against their terms. What's left is to license an index through an API. We use DataForSEO's Backlinks API. For each link it returns the linking page, anchor text, dofollow/nofollow, first and last seen dates, and 0–100 ranks for the linking domain and page.

反向链接数据的来源：你无法通过访问自己的网站来检查反向链接。反向链接存在于他人的页面上，因此你需要一个已经抓取过互联网大部分内容并保留了链接图谱的爬虫。自己构建这样的系统并不现实，而抓取供应商的网页界面不仅容易失效，还违反了它们的服务条款。剩下的选择是通过 API 获取索引授权。我们使用的是 DataForSEO 的 Backlinks API。对于每个链接，它会返回来源页面、锚文本、dofollow/nofollow 状态、首次和最后发现日期，以及来源域名和页面的 0-100 排名。

The diff is the product: A full list of a big site's backlinks is interesting once. What you want every week is: new: links whose first_seen is after your last check, lost: links the crawler found missing since your last check. The core of a DIY version is small: keep a hash of every change you've already reported, and only report what's new.

差异即产品：大型网站的反向链接完整列表看一次就够了。你每周真正需要的是：新增链接（首次发现日期在你上次检查之后）和丢失链接（爬虫在上次检查后发现已消失的链接）。DIY 版本的核心很简单：保存你已经报告过的每个变化的哈希值，只报告新的变化。

Three gotchas we hit while building this: Index lag. A link first seen yesterday may only show up in the index today. If you query strictly "since my last run", you miss it for good. Query with an overlap window instead (we use 48 hours) and drop changes you already reported, using the hashes above. That way each change shows up exactly once. Big domains are noisy. Large sites gain and lose links all the time, many of them sitewide or scraper links. For big sites, monitor referring domains instead of individual links, and sort the changes by the linking domain's rank. Filters don't behave like SQL. On the referring-domains endpoint, an OR filter that includes lost_date silently drops every row where lost_date is null, which means all new domains. We ended up running "new" and "lost" as two separate queries.

我们在构建过程中遇到的三个陷阱：索引延迟。昨天首次发现的链接可能今天才出现在索引中。如果你严格查询“自上次运行以来”的数据，就会永久错过它。建议使用重叠窗口进行查询（我们使用 48 小时），并利用上述哈希值剔除已报告过的变化。这样，每个变化都只会显示一次。大型域名噪音很大。大型网站时刻都在增加和丢失链接，其中许多是全站链接或抓取链接。对于大型网站，建议监控引用域名而非单个链接，并按来源域名的排名对变化进行排序。过滤器不像 SQL 那样工作。在引用域名接口上，包含 lost_date 的 OR 过滤器会静默丢弃所有 lost_date 为空的行，这意味着所有新域名都会被过滤掉。我们最终将“新增”和“丢失”作为两个独立的查询来运行。
