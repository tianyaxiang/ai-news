### AI Agent Implementation: Where to Start

As obvious as it may sound, start implementing AI agents with the simplest tasks. That's where to begin. If you've only used ChatGPT and haven't worked with complex automations, any agent changes the way you work. Change is often difficult. Reshaping a business takes time, both for your AI system and for you personally.

尽管听起来显而易见，但请从最简单的任务开始实施 AI 智能体。这就是起步之处。如果你只使用过 ChatGPT 而没有处理过复杂的自动化流程，那么任何智能体都会改变你的工作方式。改变往往是困难的。重塑业务需要时间，无论是对你的 AI 系统还是对你个人而言。

A common mistake business owners make is jumping into complex development and trying to automate everything at once. Don't do that. Neither you nor your system is ready for it. People don't want to change, and familiar paths are easier to follow. Write down where your day goes. Try a simple exercise: for one day, starting when you wake up, write down everything you do. Note the decisions you make, the actions you take, what follows what and, most importantly, where your time goes.

企业主常犯的一个错误是直接投入复杂的开发，试图一次性自动化所有事情。千万别这样做。你和你的系统都还没准备好。人们不愿改变，遵循熟悉的路径更容易。记录下你一天的时间去向。尝试一个简单的练习：从醒来开始，记录下一天中你所做的每一件事。记下你做出的决定、采取的行动、事情的先后顺序，以及最重要的一点——你的时间花在了哪里。

You don't need to turn the day into a minute-by-minute report. Write each action down so you can see later where it begins and what you get at the end. For example: open an incoming email, identify the client's request and draft a response. Then give the list to an AI tool or the specialist who will automate your processes. It gives you something to examine for recurring work and decide where to start.

你不需要把这一天变成一份逐分钟的报告。写下每一个动作，以便日后查看它的起点和最终结果。例如：打开一封收到的邮件，识别客户的需求并起草回复。然后将这份清单交给 AI 工具或负责自动化流程的专家。这能让你审视重复性工作，并决定从哪里开始。

Choose one simple task. For a first step, look for actions with clear input and a clear result. For example, the input is an email and the output is a short summary of the client's request. You can compare that with the original email much more easily than you can evaluate a task like "deal with all my email." Decide where the result appears and who checks it. With an email summary, a person can open the original alongside the agent's text and see whether an important request was missed. If information is missing, the agent should show that rather than make it up.

选择一个简单的任务。作为第一步，寻找那些输入明确、结果清晰的动作。例如，输入是一封邮件，输出是客户需求的简短摘要。比起评估“处理我所有的邮件”这类任务，你更容易将摘要与原始邮件进行对比。确定结果显示的位置以及由谁来检查。对于邮件摘要，人工可以将原文与智能体的文本并排打开，查看是否遗漏了重要请求。如果信息缺失，智能体应该显示出来，而不是编造内容。

Give yourself and the system time. The first automation changes a familiar routine: you need to hand over a task, receive the result and check it. Your team also needs to know who handles that step now and where to look when something goes wrong. Test the agent on several real examples, including an email that doesn't contain enough information. Keep a person reviewing the output while it still raises questions. Sending a message to a client or taking another important action is a separate step, and the agent doesn't need to do it independently from day one.

给自己和系统一些时间。第一次自动化会改变熟悉的流程：你需要移交任务、接收结果并进行检查。你的团队也需要知道现在由谁负责该步骤，以及在出错时去哪里查找原因。用几个真实的例子测试智能体，包括一封信息不足的邮件。在输出结果仍存疑时，保持人工审核。向客户发送消息或采取其他重要行动是独立的步骤，智能体不需要从第一天起就独立完成这些工作。

Once the first task works in a way you understand and you're used to working with it, you can take on the next. That's how a system grows around your business, one step at a time.
