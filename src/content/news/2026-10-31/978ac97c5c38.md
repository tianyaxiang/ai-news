# Google Pixel Watch 5 Sale: Its Lowest Price Yet (2026)

Save this storySave this storyIf you’ve been waiting for a good deal on Google’s latest smartwatch before you commit, you're in luck. The Google Pixel Watch 5 (the 41-mm model with Wi-Fi) is currently $300, down from $400, marking its lowest price yet. (The larger 45-mm model is also discounted, from $430 to $330.)

如果您一直在等待 Google 最新款智能手表的优惠活动再入手，那么现在机会来了。Google Pixel Watch 5（41 毫米 Wi-Fi 版）目前的售价为 300 美元，低于原价 400 美元，创下了历史最低价。（更大的 45 毫米型号也有折扣，从 430 美元降至 330 美元。）

This early Black Friday sale means you don’t have to wait until the late-November holiday to score the worthwhile discount—and we recommend you don’t. Amazon, Best Buy, and the Google Store are all offering the 25 percent off deal, but I don’t know for how much longer. In other words, now is a great time to score this smartwatch for less.

这次提前开启的“黑色星期五”促销意味着您不必等到 11 月下旬的假期就能享受到超值折扣——我们建议您现在就行动。亚马逊、百思买和 Google 商店目前均提供 25% 的折扣，但我不知道优惠会持续多久。换句话说，现在正是以更低价格入手这款智能手表的绝佳时机。

The Pixel Watch 5 is one of the best smartwatches available for Android users. It’s a refinement of an already brilliant wearable, with double the RAM and storage, plus an upgraded Qualcomm Snapdragon W5 Gen 2 processor that makes this edition 20 percent faster than its predecessor. The domed display is gorgeous, comfortable, and easy to read during workouts, and it provides meaningful upgrades for health and fitness tracking.

Pixel Watch 5 是目前 Android 用户能买到的最好的智能手表之一。它是对这款本已出色的可穿戴设备的进一步完善，内存和存储空间翻倍，并升级了高通骁龙 W5 Gen 2 处理器，使这一代产品的运行速度比上一代提升了 20%。圆顶显示屏外观精美、佩戴舒适，在锻炼时易于读取信息，并为健康和健身追踪提供了实质性的升级。

Beyond being able to track weights and reps, the new Strength Training experience includes a workout builder, audio cues, and rest times. You can build a custom routine in the Google Health app and have the watch guide you through each exercise, and incorporate Gemini AI by asking it to generate a workout based on a photo of your gym equipment. Speaking of Gemini, the Pixel Watch 5 supports certain offline tasks, such as starting a workout, which is nice if you like to leave your watch at home while you exercise.

除了能够追踪重量和重复次数外，全新的力量训练体验还包括锻炼计划生成器、语音提示和休息时间设置。您可以在 Google Health 应用中制定自定义例程，让手表引导您完成每项练习，还可以通过 Gemini AI，根据您健身器材的照片让它为您生成锻炼计划。说到 Gemini，Pixel Watch 5 支持某些离线任务，例如开始锻炼，如果您喜欢在锻炼时把手表留在家里，这非常方便。

Google’s new Health Guardian tools include features for monitoring insulin resistance trends, blood pressure trends, breathing emergencies (available only in select countries), and sleep breathing quality. The watch also retains useful features like ECG, skin temperature tracking, and loss-of-pulse detection. It comes with upgraded sleep tracking, too, with Google claiming 15 percent greater accuracy than the Pixel Watch 4.

Google 新推出的“健康守护者”（Health Guardian）工具包括监测胰岛素抵抗趋势、血压趋势、呼吸紧急情况（仅在部分国家/地区可用）以及睡眠呼吸质量的功能。该手表还保留了心电图（ECG）、皮肤温度追踪和脉搏丢失检测等实用功能。它还配备了升级版的睡眠追踪功能，Google 声称其准确度比 Pixel Watch 4 提高了 15%。

Google estimates up to 40 hours of battery life, but mine only got to around 36 hours in testing before conking out. Fast charging is the real standout, though; just 15 minutes on the charger delivers up to 15 hours of use. If you’re like me and only remember to charge your watch 20 minutes before leaving the house, this is a lifesaver.

Google 预计电池续航时间最长可达 40 小时，但在我的测试中，它在大约 36 小时后就没电了。不过，快速充电确实非常出色；充电仅 15 分钟即可提供长达 15 小时的使用时间。如果您像我一样，总是只在出门前 20 分钟才想起给手表充电，那么这简直是救命稻草。

At its original $400 price, the Pixel Watch 5 is a pricey recommendation, especially since many of its software upgrades will also make their way to the previous generation. But at $300, it’s cheaper than the latest Apple Watch Series 12 and other high-end smartwatches. This is the lowest price I’ve seen for the Pixel Watch 5, so if you’ve been eyeing it, there’s little reason to wait for Black Friday.

以 400 美元的原价来看，Pixel Watch 5 的推荐价值不高，尤其是因为它的许多软件升级也会下放到上一代产品中。但以 300 美元的价格来看，它比最新的 Apple Watch Series 12 和其他高端智能手表更便宜。这是我见过的 Pixel Watch 5 的最低价格，所以如果您一直关注它，实在没有理由等到“黑色星期五”。
