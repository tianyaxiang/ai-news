Along with the rest of the world, we at Ars are mourning Sir Patrick Stewart—the beloved award-winning actor best known as Captain Jean-Luc Picard in Star Trek: The Next Generation and Professor Charles Xavier in the X-Men franchise—who died yesterday at the age of 86.

与世界各地的人们一样，我们 Ars 的全体成员也在哀悼帕特里克·斯图尔特爵士（Sir Patrick Stewart）。这位备受爱戴的获奖演员因在《星际迷航：下一代》中饰演让-卢克·皮卡德船长，以及在《X战警》系列中饰演查尔斯·泽维尔教授而广为人知，他于昨天去世，享年 86 岁。

A statement released by Stewart’s representatives said: We announce with great sorrow the passing of Sir Patrick Stewart yesterday at his home in Los Angeles, in the loving care of his beloved wife Sunny, following a brief and poignant journey with Alzheimer’s Disease and Lewy Body Dementia.

斯图尔特代表发表的一份声明称：我们怀着无比悲痛的心情宣布，帕特里克·斯图尔特爵士于昨天在洛杉矶家中去世。在经历了与阿尔茨海默病和路易体痴呆症短暂而令人心碎的抗争后，他在挚爱妻子桑尼（Sunny）的悉心照料下离世。

He has been held in the warm embrace of family and friends, surrounded by live music, prayer, tears, laughter, and the kind of profound emotion that only he could inspire after a lifetime of devotion to us all. We will forever cherish the peaceful ease and reflection on his face, an enduring reminder of his invincible grace.

他一直被家人和朋友温暖地拥抱着，周围环绕着现场音乐、祈祷、泪水、欢笑，以及那种只有他在奉献了一生后才能激发的深厚情感。我们将永远铭记他脸上那份宁静与沉思，那是他不可战胜的优雅的永恒印记。

Through the actor’s X account, the family has requested that, in lieu of flowers, memorial contributions be made to the Johns Hopkins Alzheimer’s Disease Research Center Fund and the Alzheimer’s Foundation of America Caregiver Resources.

通过这位演员的 X 账号，其家人请求以捐款代替鲜花，向约翰·霍普金斯大学阿尔茨海默病研究中心基金和美国阿尔茨海默病基金会护理人员资源部进行纪念捐赠。

Born in Yorkshire, England, in 1940, the acting bug bit Stewart at the age of 12, when his English teacher handed out scripts for Shakespeare’s The Merchant of Venice and asked young Patrick to read the part of Shylock—a role the adult Stewart would later play for the Royal Shakespeare Theater (RSC), among many other parts.

斯图尔特 1940 年出生于英国约克郡。12 岁时，他的英语老师分发了莎士比亚《威尼斯商人》的剧本，并让年轻的帕特里克朗读夏洛克的台词，从此他便迷上了表演。成年后的斯图尔特后来在皇家莎士比亚剧团（RSC）饰演了这一角色，此外他还出演过许多其他角色。

His signature bald head emerged early, around 19, thanks to alopecia. He briefly worked as a cub reporter and a furniture salesman before winning a scholarship to the Bristol Old Vic Theater School, and he landed his first professional role in 1959, playing a marooned pirate in a production of Treasure Island for the Lincoln Repertory Company.

由于脱发，他标志性的光头在 19 岁左右就出现了。在获得布里斯托尔老维克戏剧学校的奖学金之前，他曾短暂担任过实习记者和家具推销员。1959 年，他获得了第一个职业角色，在林肯剧团制作的《金银岛》中饰演一名被困的海盗。

Stewart worked regularly in theater and on British television, and his big break came in his late 40s. The actor was conducting master classes on Shakespeare at the University of California, Los Angeles, when a TV producer developing TNG heard him lecture and thought he would make a perfect captain for the Enterprise-D.

斯图尔特经常在剧院和英国电视节目中工作，直到 40 多岁时才迎来事业的重大突破。当时，这位演员正在加州大学洛杉矶分校举办莎士比亚大师班，一位正在开发《星际迷航：下一代》（TNG）的电视制片人听了他的讲座，认为他是“进取号-D”船长的完美人选。

Creator Gene Roddenberry wasn’t initially keen on the choice, but Stewart’s audition changed his mind. The rest is television history.

创作者吉恩·罗登贝瑞最初并不看好这个选择，但斯图尔特的试镜改变了他的想法。接下来的故事便成为了电视史的一部分。

On Instagram, fellow thespian Sir Ian McKellen—who first met Stewart when both were young actors with the RSC and co-starred as Magneto in the X-Men films—recalled the day Stewart told him about the Star Trek offer.

在 Instagram 上，同为演员的伊恩·麦克莱恩爵士（Sir Ian McKellen）回忆起斯图尔特告诉他《星际迷航》邀约的那一天。他们两人在 RSC 担任年轻演员时首次相识，后来又在《X战警》电影中分别饰演万磁王和X教授。

“‘But Patrick, think of your stage career in the UK,'” McKellen recalled saying. “‘Do you really want to risk—getting lost in Hollywood for years and years?’ Well, he took the risk and thank goodness for us all that he did.”

“‘但是帕特里克，想想你在英国的舞台事业，’”麦克莱恩回忆道，“‘你真的想冒这个险，在好莱坞迷失多年吗？’好吧，他冒了这个险，谢天谢地，他这么做了。”

Stewart himself never expressed anything but pride in the role. “For huge numbers of people, I will always be Jean-Luc Picard, and that’s fine,” he told The Independent in 2003. “I really believe we created an admirable individual.”

斯图尔特本人对这个角色始终引以为傲。2003 年，他告诉《独立报》：“对于无数人来说，我永远是让-卢克·皮卡德，这很好。我真心认为我们塑造了一个令人钦佩的人物。”

TNG ended in 1994, but it wasn’t long before Stewart created another iconic role, starring as Professor Charles Xavier in 2000’s X-Men, alongside McKellen as Magneto. He reprised the role in six more films and voiced the character in multiple video games.

《星际迷航：下一代》于 1994 年完结，但不久之后，斯图尔特就塑造了另一个标志性角色——在 2000 年的《X战警》中饰演查尔斯·泽维尔教授，与饰演万磁王的麦克莱恩演对手戏。他在随后的六部电影中继续饰演该角色，并为多款电子游戏中的该角色配音。

He was a serious actor who never took himself too seriously, relishing Shakespeare, Ibsen, and Beckett but also throwing himself into comedic appearances on Saturday Night Live, The Daily Show (he was a “special correspondent” for several segments in 2012), and Sesame Street.

他是一位严肃的演员，但从不把自己看得太重。他热爱莎士比亚、易卜生和贝克特的作品，同时也全身心地投入到《周六夜现场》、《每日秀》（他曾在 2012 年担任多个片段的“特约记者”）和《芝麻街》等喜剧节目中。

Who could forget his timeless “Soliloquy on B,” channeling Hamlet? Or his daily readings of Shakespearean sonnets that helped us all get through the COVID-19 pandemic?

谁能忘记他那段模仿哈姆雷特的经典“B之独白”？或者他每天朗读莎士比亚十四行诗，帮助我们所有人度过了新冠疫情？

He voiced Napoleon in 1999’s Animal Farm, CIA Deputy Director Avery Bullock on American Dad and, earlier this year, Sir Nitchfield in the delightful Sheep Detectives.

他曾为 1999 年《动物庄园》中的拿破仑配音，为《美国老爸》中的中情局副局长艾弗里·布洛克配音，并在今年早些时候为令人愉快的《绵羊侦探》中的尼奇菲尔德爵士配音。

In between acting gigs, Stewart found time for charity work, advocating for such causes as domestic violence prevention and PTSD awareness among members of the armed forces, drawing on his own childhood being raised by an abusive father.

在表演工作之余，斯图尔特还抽出时间从事慈善工作，倡导预防家庭暴力和提高武装部队成员对创伤后应激障碍（PTSD）的认识，这源于他童年时期在虐待成性的父亲抚养下长大的经历。

All of this made Stewart one of the most beloved actors of our time. Tributes to Stewart are all over social media today, as celebrities and regular folk alike reflect on everything the actor’s work has meant to them over the years, including these heartfelt words from his lifelong friend McKellen:

所有这些使斯图尔特成为我们这个时代最受爱戴的演员之一。今天，社交媒体上到处都是对斯图尔特的悼念，名人和普通民众都在反思这位演员的作品多年来对他们的意义，其中包括他终身好友麦克莱恩的肺腑之言：

Before everything else, Patrick was defined by being born and bred in Yorkshire, the proud county in the north of England which he loved to the end. You could hear it in his accented voice and in the stern honesty with which he told you what he thought and felt.

首先，帕特里克的特质源于他在约克郡的出生和成长，这是英格兰北部一个他至死都深爱的骄傲郡县。你可以从他带有口音的嗓音中，以及他表达思想和感受时那种严谨的诚实中感受到这一点。

Yorkshiremen have no truck with half-truths or avoidance of big issues. He wore his heart on his sleeve, which made him a man to rely on, to admire and in my case, love.

约克郡人从不接受半真半假的话，也不会回避重大问题。他总是直率地表达自己的情感，这使他成为一个值得信赖、令人钦佩，而在我看来，值得爱戴的人。

Ars staffers share their own reminiscences about Stewart and what he meant to them below. Ken Fisher, founder and editor-in-chief: I had the good fortune to literally bump into Sir Patrick Stewart outside Bond 45 in Midtown Manhattan (when it was on 45th Street).

以下是 Ars 员工分享的关于斯图尔特的回忆以及他对他们的意义。创始人兼主编肯·费舍尔（Ken Fisher）：我有幸在曼哈顿中城的 Bond 45 餐厅外（当时它还在 45 街）真的撞到了帕特里克·斯图尔特爵士。

I was coming out of the restaurant with a couple of colleagues one evening when I turned right into him. When my eyes met his, I entered a state of total confusion. I knew he loved and even played Broadway, but it didn’t register why he was here, right next to me.

一天晚上，我和几位同事从餐厅出来，转身时正好撞上了他。当我的目光与他相遇时，我陷入了彻底的混乱。我知道他热爱甚至出演过百老汇剧目，但我不明白他为什么会出现在这里，就在我身边。

Fortunately, I recovered—but then, for some unknown reason, I said in a pirate voice, “Scuse muh, Capt’n!” But he didn’t miss a beat, smiled, and took a posture of “after you good sir!” I immediately chuckled like a fool and then added that I loved his work both on and off the stage.

幸运的是，我恢复了过来——但随后，出于某种不知名的原因，我用海盗的声音说：“抱歉，船长！”但他没有丝毫迟疑，微笑着摆出一个“请，先生！”的姿势。我立刻像个傻瓜一样笑了起来，然后补充说我非常喜欢他在舞台内外的作品。
