# The Pentagon now has its own version of ChatGPT and Grok
# 五角大楼现已拥有其专属版本的 ChatGPT 和 Grok

The Pentagon has launched versions of OpenAI’s ChatGPT and xAI’s Grok, giving 3 million civilian and military personnel access to generative AI tools that have been tailored to “warfighter needs.”
五角大楼已推出 OpenAI 的 ChatGPT 和 xAI 的 Grok 的专属版本，使 300 万文职和军事人员能够使用专为“作战人员需求”量身定制的生成式 AI 工具。

ChatGPT Mil and Grok for Government — custom versions of the popular consumer generative AI tools — are now part of GenAI.mil, a centralized, secure portal launched last year. GenAI.mil, which offered Google Gemini when it first launched, is designed to give Department of Defense employees access to commercial frontier AI models without sending sensitive government data through ordinary consumer channels.
ChatGPT Mil 和 Grok for Government（这些热门消费级生成式 AI 工具的定制版本）现已成为去年推出的集中式安全门户网站 GenAI.mil 的一部分。GenAI.mil 在最初推出时提供了 Google Gemini，其设计初衷是让国防部员工能够使用商业前沿 AI 模型，而无需通过普通的消费者渠道传输敏感的政府数据。

In particular, the official military version is exempt from the data collection that is difficult to avoid in consumer tech products. And according to the Department of Defense, the generative AI portal has proven popular. GenAI.mil has already onboarded more than 1.7 million unique users out of the department’s 3 million personnel, according to the department.
特别值得一提的是，官方军事版本免除了消费级科技产品中难以避免的数据收集。据国防部称，该生成式 AI 门户网站已广受欢迎。据该部门统计，在 300 万名员工中，GenAI.mil 已经拥有超过 170 万名独立用户。

These latest additions are part of the Pentagon’s broader effort to use AI tools to accelerate work and give the Department of Defense an edge without sacrificing security. They also highlight the absence of Anthropic’s Claude model and the Pentagon’s effort to work with other companies following its dispute with the frontier lab. Anthropic was labeled a supply-chain risk by the Trump administration — a designation that it is currently fighting in court — after it refused to give the Pentagon unrestricted use of its AI tools and instead insisted on certain safety guardrails.
这些最新举措是五角大楼更广泛努力的一部分，旨在利用 AI 工具加速工作流程，并在不牺牲安全性的前提下为国防部提供优势。这也凸显了 Anthropic 的 Claude 模型缺席的事实，以及五角大楼在与该前沿实验室发生纠纷后，转而与其他公司合作的努力。Anthropic 此前曾拒绝给予五角大楼对其 AI 工具的无限制使用权，并坚持设置特定的安全护栏，因此被特朗普政府列为供应链风险——目前该公司正在法庭上对这一认定提出抗辩。

ChatGPT Mil, which came out of the company’s OpenAI for Government program, will offer an experience familiar to commercial ChatGPT users. The tool will focus on chat, files, projects, and custom GPTs, according to the Defense Department. The tool will also support document-heavy, routine unclassified work such as administrative tasks, logistics, planning, and policy. Other features will be added over time.
ChatGPT Mil 源自该公司的“OpenAI for Government”项目，将为商业 ChatGPT 用户提供熟悉的体验。据国防部称，该工具将专注于聊天、文件、项目和自定义 GPT。该工具还将支持文档密集型的日常非机密工作，例如行政任务、物流、规划和政策制定。未来还将添加更多功能。

The Pentagon positioned Grok’s capabilities and potential uses in much broader and more militaristic terms. “Starshield AI’s Grok for Government will provide the warfighter with immediate productivity gains, stronger knowledge continuity and more secure and efficient collaboration,” according to the Defense Department’s press release. (SpaceX’s Starshield AI is a secure satellite network that uses the company’s existing Starlink tech.)
五角大楼以更广泛且更具军事化的术语来定位 Grok 的功能和潜在用途。根据国防部的新闻稿，“Starshield AI 的 Grok for Government 将为作战人员提供即时的生产力提升、更强的知识连续性以及更安全、高效的协作。”（SpaceX 的 Starshield AI 是一个利用该公司现有 Starlink 技术的安全卫星网络。）

The department added that Grok would allow its military “to execute missions faster and with greater precision across numerous operational contexts, ranging from market research analysis for acquisition professionals to supply-chain management for logisticians.” The Pentagon has struck deals with a number of other tech companies in a bid to boost its AI capabilities, including Amazon Web Services, Microsoft, Nvidia, and Reflection AI.
国防部补充说，Grok 将使军队“能够在众多的作战环境中更快、更精确地执行任务，范围涵盖从采购专业人员的市场研究分析到物流人员的供应链管理。”为了提升 AI 能力，五角大楼已与多家其他科技公司达成协议，包括亚马逊云科技 (AWS)、微软、英伟达和 Reflection AI。
