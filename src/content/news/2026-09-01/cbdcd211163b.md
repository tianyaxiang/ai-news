### What Is MagSafe?
### 什么是 MagSafe？

MagSafe is Apple's magnetic technology built into the iPhone 12, iPhone 13, iPhone 14, iPhone 15, iPhone 16, and iPhone 17 lineups, as well as the iPhone Air (the iPhone SE and iPhone 16e are excluded). It consists of a ring of magnets on the back of the phone designed for a faster, more precise wireless charging system. But these magnets also opened up the ability to attach different kinds of accessories, like wallets and power banks. It even makes docking an iPhone to a car mount easier.

MagSafe 是苹果内置于 iPhone 12、13、14、15、16 和 17 系列以及 iPhone Air 中的磁吸技术（iPhone SE 和 iPhone 16e 除外）。它由手机背部的一圈磁铁组成，旨在实现更快、更精准的无线充电。但这些磁铁也开启了连接各种配件的可能性，例如钱包和充电宝。它甚至让 iPhone 连接车载支架变得更加容易。

If you use a case on your iPhone, make sure it comes with MagSafe support (you'll see a ring of magnets on the inside). That way, the MagSafe accessories will have a proper connection. Otherwise, if you use a non-MagSafe case, the accessories will likely slide off due to a weak connection.

如果你给 iPhone 套了手机壳，请确保它支持 MagSafe（你会看到内侧有一圈磁铁）。这样，MagSafe 配件才能实现良好的连接。否则，如果你使用不支持 MagSafe 的手机壳，配件很可能会因为吸力不足而滑落。

### Can Android Phones Use MagSafe Grips?
### 安卓手机可以使用 MagSafe 支架吗？

Most Android phones are incompatible with MagSafe grips. But there are magnetic cases available for top Android phone models enabling MagSafe-like functionality. You can find WIRED-tested magnetic cases for select Android phones here.

大多数安卓手机与 MagSafe 支架不兼容。但目前市面上已有针对主流安卓机型的磁吸手机壳，可以实现类似 MagSafe 的功能。你可以在此处找到经 WIRED 测试过的部分安卓手机磁吸壳。

Over the coming years, thanks to the new Qi2 wireless charging standard that incorporates the Magnetic Power Profile, you'll start seeing more high-end Android phones embed magnets inside the device. Google already has Qi2 devices with the Pixel 10 range. That will allow greater compatibility with MagSafe accessories with or without a magnetic case. The road is bumpy, though. Samsung's latest Galaxy S26 range, for example, are “Qi2 Ready,” meaning you can only access Qi2 charging speeds and features with a magnet case. Hopefully, manufacturers will add native magnet support soon.

未来几年，得益于包含磁力电源配置（Magnetic Power Profile）的新型 Qi2 无线充电标准，你将看到更多高端安卓手机在设备内部嵌入磁铁。谷歌的 Pixel 10 系列已经配备了 Qi2 设备。这将使安卓手机无论是否佩戴磁吸壳，都能与 MagSafe 配件实现更好的兼容。不过，这条路并不平坦。例如，三星最新的 Galaxy S26 系列虽然“支持 Qi2”，但意味着你必须配合磁吸壳才能使用 Qi2 的充电速度和功能。希望制造商们能尽快加入原生的磁吸支持。

### The Different Types of Phone Grips
### 手机支架的类型

MagSafe grips are hardly the only type of phone grips available. There are four main other types of grips you can buy.

MagSafe 支架绝非市面上唯一的手机支架类型。你还可以购买其他四种主要类型的支架：

*   **Cases with attached grips:** These are cases with a grip attached to the back. You get a strong and durable grip that will never fall off, but the trade-off is a thicker phone, and these cases typically are incompatible with wireless charging and may not work with other MagSafe accessories.
    **自带支架的手机壳：** 这类手机壳背面直接连接了支架。你将获得一个坚固耐用、绝不会掉落的支架，但代价是手机会变厚，且通常不支持无线充电，也可能无法与其他 MagSafe 配件配合使用。
*   **Clamp-style grips:** Also called universal phone grips, these clamp to a phone so you can easily take them on and off, and they're not limited to a particular kind of phone. The downside is they're not the fastest to take on and off, and they can sometimes slide up and down the phone.
    **夹式支架：** 也称为通用手机支架，它们通过夹子固定在手机上，方便拆卸，且不局限于特定机型。缺点是拆装速度不够快，有时会在手机上滑动。
*   **Adhesive grips:** You can stick a phone grip to the back of your phone or case. While this will typically give you a well-attached grip that likely won't fall off, the downsides are similar to cases with attached grips. It may block features like wireless charging and restrict you from using other accessories.
    **粘贴式支架：** 你可以将支架粘贴在手机或手机壳背面。虽然这通常能提供稳固的抓握感，不容易掉落，但缺点与自带支架的手机壳类似。它可能会阻碍无线充电等功能，并限制你使用其他配件。
*   **MagSafe grips:** These grips are all about convenience. You can easily take them on and off, but they only work with devices or cases with built-in magnets. While you can dangle a phone from a MagSafe grip without issue, additional force could cause the magnets to separate and fling your phone to the ground.
    **MagSafe 支架：** 这类支架主打便捷。你可以轻松拆卸，但它们仅适用于内置磁铁的设备或手机壳。虽然你可以毫无问题地用 MagSafe 支架悬挂手机，但如果施加额外的外力，磁铁可能会分离，导致手机掉落。
