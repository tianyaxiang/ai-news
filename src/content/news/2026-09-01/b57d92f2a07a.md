# When Muon Meets Task Interference: A Spectral Perspective on Continual Learning and Model Merging

**当 Muon 遇上任务干扰：持续学习与模型合并的谱视角**

---

**Abstract:** Continual learning (CL) and model merging (MM) both aim to obtain a single model that performs well across multiple tasks, challenged respectively by catastrophic forgetting and weight-disentanglement error. In the literature, these difficulties are merely treated separately and mitigated through a variety of solutions, while the geometry induced by the base optimizer is treated as an implementation detail.

**摘要：** 持续学习 (CL) 和模型合并 (MM) 的目标都是获得一个在多个任务上表现良好的单一模型，但它们分别面临着灾难性遗忘和权重解耦误差的挑战。在现有文献中，这些困难通常被分开处理，并通过各种解决方案进行缓解，而基础优化器所诱导的几何结构往往被视为实现细节。

In this work, we show that the two difficulties are in fact two instances of the same phenomenon: a parameter update useful for one task shifts the model's outputs on another. We formalize this shared phenomenon as *task interference* and reduce it to a common layer-wise Frobenius inner product $\langle \Delta W_\ell, J_\ell(x)\rangle_F$. This quantity, in turn, is utilized to expose the role of the optimizer.

在这项工作中，我们证明了这两个困难实际上是同一现象的两种表现：对一个任务有用的参数更新会改变模型在另一个任务上的输出。我们将这种共同现象形式化为“任务干扰”（task interference），并将其简化为通用的层级 Frobenius 内积 $\langle \Delta W_\ell, J_\ell(x)\rangle_F$。进而，利用该量揭示了优化器的作用。

We theoretically derive an upper bound that isolates the spectral norm $\|\Delta W_\ell\|_2$ as an optimizer-controllable factor of task interference, and a per-mode analysis shows that this bound tracks the dominant part of the empirical interference. Specifically, we then identify the recent Muon optimizer as a mechanism that regulates this factor by construction. Our work reveals that its elegant control on spectral norm tightens the interference bound for both CL and MM, positioning Muon as a principled optimizer-centric approach complementary to existing solutions.

我们从理论上推导出了一个上界，将谱范数 $\|\Delta W_\ell\|_2$ 分离为任务干扰中可由优化器控制的因素，且逐模式分析表明，该界限追踪了经验干扰的主要部分。具体而言，我们发现近期提出的 Muon 优化器通过其构造机制调节了这一因素。我们的研究表明，它对谱范数的优雅控制收紧了 CL 和 MM 的干扰界限，使 Muon 成为一种与现有解决方案互补的、基于优化器的原则性方法。

Our theoretical analysis is well validated by experimental results. Replacing the AdamW optimizer with Muon improves accuracy by up to +5.02 points on the eight-task model-merging benchmark across three CLIP backbones. For continual learning, Muon also delivers uniformly positive gains across ten class-incremental protocols, three task-incremental protocols, and the 11-task MTIL benchmark.

我们的理论分析得到了实验结果的充分验证。在三个 CLIP 主干网络上的八任务模型合并基准测试中，将 AdamW 优化器替换为 Muon 后，准确率最高提升了 5.02 个百分点。在持续学习方面，Muon 在十个类增量协议、三个任务增量协议以及 11 任务 MTIL 基准测试中均带来了持续的正向收益。
