# Depth-Aware Pothole Detection Using YOLO and RT-DETR at the Edge
# 基于 YOLO 和 RT-DETR 的边缘端深度感知坑洼检测

**Abstract:** Pothole detection and its severity measurement is still an important challenge in urban infrastructure management, where late maintenance directly contributes to vehicle damage, road accidents, and escalating repair costs. Existing automated approaches depend on 2D RGB images and cannot measure physical depth of potholes.

**摘要：** 坑洼检测及其严重程度评估仍然是城市基础设施管理中的一项重要挑战，维护滞后直接导致车辆损坏、道路交通事故以及维修成本的增加。现有的自动化方法依赖于二维 RGB 图像，无法测量坑洼的物理深度。

In this paper, we present a depth-aware pothole detection framework and then compare five architectures: YOLOv8n, YOLOv8nSeg, YOLOv9t, RTDETRL, and RTDETRX for RGB-D sensor fusion-based detection and automated depth measurement. A custom offline augmentation pipeline is used here to simulate adverse road monitoring conditions.

在本文中，我们提出了一种深度感知坑洼检测框架，并比较了五种架构：YOLOv8n、YOLOv8nSeg、YOLOv9t、RTDETRL 和 RTDETRX，用于基于 RGB-D 传感器融合的检测和自动深度测量。文中采用了自定义的离线增强流水线来模拟恶劣的道路监测环境。

All models are trained on the PothRGBD dataset with an 80% training and 20% validation split and evaluated using Precision, Recall, mAP@50, and mAP@50_95. Before measuring the depth data, all depth maps are corrected for camera tilt using RANSAC ground-plane orthorectification and all zero-valued sensor pixels are cast to NaN before any statistic is computed.

所有模型均在 PothRGBD 数据集上进行训练（80% 训练集，20% 验证集），并使用精确率（Precision）、召回率（Recall）、mAP@50 和 mAP@50_95 进行评估。在测量深度数据之前，所有深度图均使用 RANSAC 地面正射校正进行相机倾斜校正，并在计算任何统计数据之前，将所有零值传感器像素转换为 NaN。

YOLOv8nSeg achieves the highest mAP@50 of 0.9556 and mAP@50_95 of 0.6758 with the most accurate depth estimate of 2.96 cm with the pixel-precise Dseg algorithm. YOLOv8n achieves the fastest inference at 3.6ms. RTDETRX achieves the highest detection confidence at 92.70%.

YOLOv8nSeg 取得了最高的 mAP@50（0.9556）和 mAP@50_95（0.6758），并利用像素级精确的 Dseg 算法实现了 2.96 厘米的最准确深度估计。YOLOv8n 的推理速度最快，仅为 3.6 毫秒。RTDETRX 的检测置信度最高，达到 92.70%。

An important finding is that even after full RANSAC orthorectification, bounding box models overestimate pothole depth by 0.16 to 0.21 cm compared to pixel precise segmentation masks. This confirms that the pavement inclusion bias is structural rather than a calibration artifact.

一个重要的发现是，即使在进行完全的 RANSAC 正射校正后，与像素级精确的分割掩码相比，边界框模型仍会将坑洼深度高估 0.16 到 0.21 厘米。这证实了路面包含偏差是结构性的，而非校准伪影。
