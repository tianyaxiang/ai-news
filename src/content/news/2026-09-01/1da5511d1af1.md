# Dandelion: A Spherical Flower for Neural Simulation of Planetary Dynamics
# Dandelion：用于行星动力学神经模拟的球形 Flower 模型

**Abstract:** Many dynamical processes unfold on the sphere but the default scientific machine learning architectures are Euclidean. Applying these architectures on a regular lat-lon grid causes problems: Cartesian convolutions become distorted at high latitude; 2D FFTs in Fourier neural operators incorrectly assume double periodicity; Cartesian positional encodings in ViTs distort spherical geodesic distances.

**摘要：** 许多动力学过程发生在球面上，但默认的科学机器学习架构通常是欧几里得空间的。将这些架构应用于常规的经纬度网格会引发问题：笛卡尔卷积在高纬度地区会发生畸变；傅里叶神经算子中的二维快速傅里叶变换（FFT）错误地假设了双周期性；视觉 Transformer（ViT）中的笛卡尔位置编码则会扭曲球面的测地线距离。

Recent work moves towards natively spherical primitives, including spherical convolutions (e.g., DeepSphere or DISCO), Spherical Fourier Neural Operators (SFNOs), and geodesic attention. Here we propose Dandelion, a spherical version of Flower, a warp-based neural PDE solver. Layers of Dandelion predict a tangent-plane displacement and transport features along great circles. We obtain a U-Net-like structure by implementing hierarchical pooling entirely in the spherical-harmonic domain. There are thus no convolutions: spatial mixing is achieved only through spherical coordinate changes, or warps.

近期的研究正转向原生球形基元，包括球形卷积（如 DeepSphere 或 DISCO）、球形傅里叶神经算子（SFNOs）以及测地线注意力机制。在此，我们提出了 Dandelion，这是 Flower（一种基于变形的神经偏微分方程求解器）的球形版本。Dandelion 的层级结构通过预测切平面位移并沿大圆传输特征来工作。我们通过完全在球谐函数域内实现分层池化，从而获得了一种类似 U-Net 的结构。因此，该模型中不存在卷积：空间混合仅通过球坐标变换（即变形）来实现。

To compare Dandelion with existing spherical architectures, we release an evolving benchmark suite of challenging, natively-spherical PDE datasets including a modified Galewsky jet, anomalous chained turbulence, Cahn-Hilliard decomposition, spherical Riemann shocks, Held-Suarez dry atmospheric transport and global ocean dynamics. This new benchmark fills the gap in existing spherical datasets which are either too small and stylized, or much too large (ERA5) for model iteration.

为了将 Dandelion 与现有的球形架构进行比较，我们发布了一套不断演进的基准测试套件，其中包含具有挑战性的原生球形偏微分方程数据集，包括改进的 Galewsky 急流、异常链式湍流、Cahn-Hilliard 分解、球形黎曼激波、Held-Suarez 干大气传输以及全球海洋动力学。这一新的基准测试填补了现有球形数据集的空白，因为现有的数据集要么规模太小且过于理想化，要么规模过大（如 ERA5），不便于模型迭代。

Dandelion is best or second-best on every dataset, and the gap to non-warp baselines widens with resolution: at $256\times 512$, Dandelion and Flower2D occupy the top two slots in both single-step prediction and rollout.

Dandelion 在每个数据集上的表现均位列第一或第二，且与非变形基准模型之间的差距随着分辨率的提高而扩大：在 $256\times 512$ 的分辨率下，Dandelion 和 Flower2D 在单步预测和滚动预测中均占据了前两名的位置。
