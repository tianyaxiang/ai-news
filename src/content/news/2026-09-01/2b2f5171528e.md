# The Google TV Streamer now costs $50 more
# Google TV Streamer 价格上涨 50 美元

Google’s streaming device is the latest to get a price hike, after similar increases from Apple and Amazon.
继苹果和亚马逊相继涨价后，谷歌的流媒体设备也加入了涨价行列。

Google raised the price of its 4K streaming box to $149, up $50 from its original $99 price. The new price is currently live at the Google Store and Best Buy, but Amazon appears to still be offering the original price.
谷歌将其 4K 流媒体机顶盒的价格上调至 149 美元，较最初的 99 美元上涨了 50 美元。目前 Google Store 和百思买（Best Buy）已执行新价格，但亚马逊似乎仍维持原价。

The price hike comes just a couple of weeks after Google launched its new lineup of Pixel 11 phones with a $100 price hike over their Pixel 10 counterparts.
此次涨价距离谷歌发布新款 Pixel 11 系列手机仅过去几周，而该系列手机的价格也比 Pixel 10 系列高出 100 美元。

The Google TV Streamer originally launched in 2024 and doubles as a smart home hub. It isn’t the only streaming device getting more expensive — component shortages have been driving up prices on everything from laptops to game consoles.
Google TV Streamer 于 2024 年首次推出，兼具智能家居中枢功能。它并非唯一涨价的流媒体设备——零部件短缺正推高从笔记本电脑到游戏机等各类产品的价格。

Earlier this month, Amazon increased the price of its Fire TV Stick 4K Max by over 40 percent to $84.99. The Apple TV 4K also similarly costs more after Apple’s sweeping price hikes in June, bringing it up to $199 for the 64GB 版本 and $249 for the 128GB version.
本月早些时候，亚马逊将其 Fire TV Stick 4K Max 的价格提高了 40% 以上，达到 84.99 美元。苹果在 6 月份全面涨价后，Apple TV 4K 的价格也随之上涨，64GB 版本售价升至 199 美元，128GB 版本则为 249 美元。
