# Apple caught off guard by AI demand for Mac Mini and Mac Studio
# 苹果因 Mac Mini 和 Mac Studio 的 AI 需求激增而措手不及

Apple's unusually timed announcement of new Mac mini and Mac Studio models this week was driven by unexpectedly strong enterprise appetite for AI hardware, according to The Information.
据《The Information》报道，苹果本周不同寻常地提前发布新款 Mac mini 和 Mac Studio，主要是由于企业对 AI 硬件的需求异常强劲。

Apple normally releases new Mac models in the autumn, closer to October or November, making this week's announcement unusually early, falling just before the anticipated arrival of new iPhone models. The Information says that the AI-driven boom in ‌Mac Studio‌ and ‌Mac mini‌ sales is behind the early launch.
苹果通常在秋季（接近 10 月或 11 月）发布新款 Mac，因此本周的发布显得异常提前，恰好在新一代 iPhone 发布之前。据《The Information》称，此次提前发布背后的推手是 AI 驱动的 Mac Studio 和 Mac mini 销量激增。

Apple noticeably promoted the ability to link multiple Mac Studios together into a single, more capable system for running large frontier AI models, a feature aimed at business and developer customers rather than everyday consumers.
苹果特别强调了将多台 Mac Studio 互联，组成一个更强大的系统以运行大型前沿 AI 模型的能力；这一功能主要面向企业和开发者客户，而非普通消费者。

Apple highlighted the ‌Mac mini‌ and ‌Mac Studio‌'s shift toward business buyers in June, with a "Business at the Park" event involving executives from major companies Ford, Disney, and Anthropic. The ‌Mac mini‌ was said to be the "darling" of the event.
今年 6 月，苹果通过一场名为“Business at the Park”的活动，重点展示了 Mac mini 和 Mac Studio 向企业买家的转型，福特、迪士尼和 Anthropic 等大公司的高管均出席了该活动。据悉，Mac mini 成为了该活动的“宠儿”。

Even so, enterprise's rush toward powerful desktop Macs more broadly took Apple by surprise. The company reportedly did not possess an engineering team dedicated to business customers or staff focused on developer relations, and lacked an enterprise AI strategy.
即便如此，企业对高性能桌面 Mac 的抢购潮还是让苹果感到意外。据报道，苹果此前并未配备专门服务企业客户的工程团队或专注于开发者关系的员工，也缺乏明确的企业级 AI 战略。

Businesses that approached Apple asking to buy access to the company's Private Cloud Compute infrastructure were reportedly turned down. Apple is instead leaning on partners such as WebAI and Mount Thor, which provide AI tools and execution environments built on Apple hardware.
据报道，一些试图向苹果购买其“私有云计算”（Private Cloud Compute）基础设施访问权限的企业遭到了拒绝。苹果转而依赖 WebAI 和 Mount Thor 等合作伙伴，由这些公司提供基于苹果硬件构建的 AI 工具和执行环境。

A surge in demand for Mac hardware to run AI models has coincided with the global memory shortage, leaving many ‌Mac mini‌ and ‌Mac Studio‌ configurations out of stock for months.
运行 AI 模型对 Mac 硬件的需求激增，恰逢全球内存短缺，导致许多 Mac mini 和 Mac Studio 的配置版本出现了长达数月的缺货。

Some enterprise customers are reportedly turning to other hardware such as Nvidia's DGX Spark, a compact AI desktop launched late last year in a form factor similar to the ‌Mac mini‌'s, as Apple's own high end configurations remain difficult to get hold of.
由于苹果的高端配置版本依然难以买到，一些企业客户据称已转向其他硬件，例如英伟达去年底推出的 DGX Spark——这是一款外形与 Mac mini 相似的紧凑型 AI 台式机。
