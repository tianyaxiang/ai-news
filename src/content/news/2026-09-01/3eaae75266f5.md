# JMGO’s very bright all-in-one projector looks ideal for gamers and sports
# 坚果（JMGO）这款超高亮度一体化投影仪，堪称游戏玩家与体育迷的理想之选

JMGO’s 4K 120Hz Iris Ultra Max all-in-one Google TV projector is now available outside China. It gets its name from a new dual-iris system that lets it produce an impressive 10,000:1 native contrast, according to JMGO. It boosts the contrast — always an issue with projectors — by placing two separate physical apertures (irises) along different points of the internal optical path to control light scatter and brightness far more aggressively than a single iris can. It also promises to produce a very bright 6,500 ISO lumen image from its triple-laser light engine.

坚果（JMGO）的 4K 120Hz Iris Ultra Max 一体化 Google TV 投影仪现已在中国以外的市场推出。据坚果介绍，该产品得名于其全新的双光圈系统，能够实现令人印象深刻的 10,000:1 原生对比度。通过在内部光路的不同位置放置两个独立的物理光圈，该系统能比单光圈更有效地控制光线散射和亮度，从而大幅提升投影仪常见的对比度不足问题。此外，它还搭载了三色激光引擎，承诺提供高达 6,500 ISO 流明的超高亮度画面。

Last year at IFA, 6,000 lumens from an all-in-one projector was breaking new ground. At IFA 2026, it’s shaping up to be table stakes, alongside 4k imagery purring along at a smooth 120Hz for fast-moving sports and gaming. Just note that I found JMGO’s N3 Ultimate to be unwatchable at peak brightness due to color distortion — so best wait for the reviews on this one.

去年在 IFA 展会上，一体化投影仪达到 6,000 流明还属于开创性的突破。而在 2026 年的 IFA 上，这似乎已成为行业标配，同时 4K 画面配合流畅的 120Hz 高刷，也为观看高速运动的体育赛事和游戏体验提供了保障。不过需要提醒的是，我曾发现坚果 N3 Ultimate 在峰值亮度下因色彩失真而导致画面难以观看，因此建议大家最好先观望一下这款产品的评测。

Like that previous flagship, the new Iris Ultra Max is mounted on a motorized pan-and-tilt gimbal for useful Wiimote-like control. That, combined with an 0.88–1.7:1 optical zoom and V±130% / H±53% lens shift, gives you unparalleled image placement options that also preserve optical image quality.

与上一代旗舰产品一样，新款 Iris Ultra Max 配备了电动云台，支持类似“体感遥控”的便捷调节。结合 0.88–1.7:1 的光学变焦以及垂直 ±130% / 水平 ±53% 的镜头位移功能，它为你提供了无与伦比的画面摆放灵活性，同时还能确保光学画质不受损。

JGMO’s also tossing in VRR and ALLM to keep latency low and reduce tearing when gaming on the IRIS Ultra Max, with support for Dolby Vision HDR at 4K 120Hz. It will eventually list for $3,499, but that drops to $2,999 for preorders that begin today, ahead of an October 19th ship date.

坚果还为 Iris Ultra Max 加入了 VRR（可变刷新率）和 ALLM（自动低延迟模式），以降低游戏延迟并减少画面撕裂，同时支持 4K 120Hz 下的杜比视界（Dolby Vision）HDR。该产品最终定价为 3,499 美元，但从今天开始的预购价格降至 2,999 美元，产品将于 10 月 19 日正式发货。
