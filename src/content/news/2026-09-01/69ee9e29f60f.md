# Report Supervision

**Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2608.27668 (cs) [Submitted on 27 Aug 2026]**
**计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2608.27668 (cs) [2026年8月27日提交]**

**Title:** Report Supervision
**标题：** 报告监督 (Report Supervision)

**Authors:** Pedro R. A. S. Bassia, Wenxuan Li, Jakob Wasserthal, Jieneng Chen, Xinze Zhou, Zheren Zhu, Chuntung Zhuanga, Sergio Decherchi, Andrea Cavalli, Kang Wang, Yang Yang, Alan Yuille, Zongwei Zhou
**作者：** Pedro R. A. S. Bassia, Wenxuan Li, Jakob Wasserthal, Jieneng Chen, Xinze Zhou, Zheren Zhu, Chuntung Zhuanga, Sergio Decherchi, Andrea Cavalli, Kang Wang, Yang Yang, Alan Yuille, Zongwei Zhou

**Abstract:** Segmentation models can surpass radiologists, classification models, and vision-language models in tumor detection. Importantly, segmentation models outline tumors, allowing radiologists to better verify and trust the AI output. Their main limitation is the scarcity of tumor masks: creating one 3D tumor mask takes up to 30 minutes, so most public CT datasets contain only a few hundred masks, and even the largest private datasets contain only a couple of thousand.
**摘要：** 在肿瘤检测方面，分割模型可以超越放射科医生、分类模型以及视觉-语言模型。重要的是，分割模型能够勾勒出肿瘤轮廓，使放射科医生能够更好地验证并信任人工智能的输出结果。它们的主要局限在于肿瘤掩膜（mask）的稀缺：创建一个3D肿瘤掩膜最多需要30分钟，因此大多数公开的CT数据集仅包含几百个掩膜，即使是最大的私有数据集也仅包含几千个。

**Tumor masks are not produced in clinical routine, but radiology reports are. Public datasets contain tens of thousands of CT-Report pairs, and hospitals contain hundreds of thousands. These reports describe tumors in detail, providing large-scale, informative training data.**
**临床常规中并不产生肿瘤掩膜，但放射科报告却会产生。公开数据集包含数以万计的CT-报告对，而医院则拥有数十万对。这些报告详细描述了肿瘤，提供了大规模且信息丰富的训练数据。**

**Here, we introduce Report Supervision (R-Super), a training framework that uses reports to directly supervise and improve tumor segmentation. R-Super introduces new loss functions that teach segmentation models to segment tumors that match report descriptions of tumor count, sizes, and locations. Reports are only used for training.**
**在此，我们引入了“报告监督”（Report Supervision, R-Super），这是一个利用报告直接监督并改进肿瘤分割的训练框架。R-Super引入了新的损失函数，教导分割模型去分割出与报告中关于肿瘤数量、大小和位置描述相匹配的肿瘤。报告仅用于训练阶段。**

**We evaluated R-Super on kidney and pancreatic tumor segmentation, exploring diverse training data sizes, up to 41,418 CT-Report plus 3,488 pancreatic tumor CT-Mask pairs. On external validation, R-Super increased tumor detection F1-Score and segmentation DSC by up to +15% with respect to mask-only training. It also surpassed alternative methods such as CLIP and multi-task learning.**
**我们在肾脏和胰腺肿瘤分割任务上评估了R-Super，探索了多种训练数据规模，最高达到41,418对CT-报告数据以及3,488对胰腺肿瘤CT-掩膜数据。在外部验证中，与仅使用掩膜的训练相比，R-Super将肿瘤检测的F1分数和分割DSC指标提升了高达15%。它还超越了CLIP和多任务学习等替代方法。**

**Leveraging numerous readily available reports to supplement scarce masks, R-Super strongly improves AI performance when very few training masks are available (e.g., 50), and when many masks are available (e.g., 3,488), unlocking scale in tumor segmentation.**
**通过利用大量现成的报告来补充稀缺的掩膜，R-Super在训练掩膜极少（例如50个）以及掩膜充足（例如3,488个）的情况下，都能显著提升人工智能的性能，从而释放了肿瘤分割任务的规模化潜力。**
