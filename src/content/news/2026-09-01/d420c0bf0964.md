# Alienware’s budget-friendly QD-OLED is discounted for the first time
# Alienware 经济型 QD-OLED 显示器首次迎来折扣

It isn’t the biggest discount, but this QD-OLED panel is already well-priced.
虽然折扣力度不算最大，但这款 QD-OLED 面板本身定价就已经非常实惠。

While memory and storage prices are still high, there are still good discounts on other gaming-related hardware. Take Alienware’s AW2726DM, for example. Dell is selling it for $319.99, a $30 break from the usual price of $349.99. 
尽管内存和存储设备的价格依然居高不下，但其他游戏相关硬件仍有不少好价。以 Alienware AW2726DM 为例，戴尔目前将其售价定为 319.99 美元，较 349.99 美元的原价优惠了 30 美元。

The 27-inch gaming monitor features an QD-OLED panel for deep, immersive black levels, and quantum dots for full and vivid colors. With a 240Hz refresh rate for super smooth gaming, and AMD FreeSync Premium Plus to keep your frames per second and refresh rate locked together, this monitor is a great, budget-friendly choice for PC gamers who don’t want to compromise on contrast and fast response times.
这款 27 英寸游戏显示器采用了 QD-OLED 面板，能够呈现深邃且极具沉浸感的黑色表现，量子点技术则带来了饱满生动的色彩。它拥有 240Hz 的刷新率，可提供极其流畅的游戏体验，并支持 AMD FreeSync Premium Plus 技术，确保帧率与刷新率同步。对于那些既不想在对比度上妥协，又追求快速响应时间的 PC 玩家来说，这是一款极具性价比的选择。

Connectivity is a bit limited, with a DisplayPort 1.4 port that supports up to 240Hz at 2560 x 1440, and two HDMI ports that support up to 120Hz at its peak resolution. While some other screens have USB hubs or audio connections, they aren’t necessary for PC gaming, and sometimes add to the cost. 
接口方面略显局限，配备了一个支持 2560 x 1440 分辨率下 240Hz 刷新率的 DisplayPort 1.4 接口，以及两个在最高分辨率下支持 120Hz 的 HDMI 接口。虽然其他一些显示器配有 USB 集线器或音频接口，但这些对于 PC 游戏来说并非必需，且有时还会增加成本。

The included stand has adjustable swivel, height, pivot, and tilt, with a 100 x 100 VESA mount in case you want to mount it to a monitor arm or use a different stand. It also sports a lengthy three year warranty with coverage for burn-in.
随附的支架支持旋转、高度调节、横竖屏切换和倾斜调节。此外，它还配有 100 x 100 的 VESA 安装孔，方便你将其安装在显示器支臂上或使用其他支架。该产品还提供长达三年的保修服务，其中包括针对烧屏问题的质保。
