# A Deeper Analysis of Block-Sparse Featurizers
# 块稀疏特征提取器（Block-Sparse Featurizers）的深度分析

**Abstract:** The recently introduced block-sparse featurizer (BSF; Fel et al., 2026) is similar to a sparse autoencoder (SAE), but its atomic unit is a small subspace (a block of directions) rather than a single direction. It is designed for features that live on low-dimensional manifolds, which are especially frequent in vision.

**摘要：** 最近推出的块稀疏特征提取器（BSF; Fel et al., 2026）与稀疏自编码器（SAE）类似，但其基本单元是一个小的子空间（即一组方向块），而非单一方向。它专为存在于低维流形上的特征而设计，这类特征在视觉领域尤为常见。

This work studies the BSF's strengths and weaknesses, finding how it still somewhat suffers from classic SAE failure modes, like feature splitting and composition. We propose several architectural changes to the BSF, including a Tournament Top-K selection rule that significantly reduces feature splitting, and we also extend the block paradigm to the crosscoder.

本研究探讨了 BSF 的优势与不足，发现它在一定程度上仍会受到经典 SAE 失效模式的影响，例如特征分裂（feature splitting）和特征组合（composition）问题。我们针对 BSF 提出了一些架构上的改进，包括一种能显著减少特征分裂的“锦标赛 Top-K”（Tournament Top-K）选择规则，并将这种块范式扩展到了交叉编码器（crosscoder）中。
