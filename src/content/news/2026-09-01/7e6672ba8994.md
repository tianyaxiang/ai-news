# Curvature-Aware Radius Shrinkage for Adaptive Nearest Neighbor Classification
# 基于曲率感知半径收缩的自适应最近邻分类

**Abstract:** Nearest neighbor classification relies fundamentally on how locality is defined, yet conventional $k$-NN imposes the same neighborhood cardinality throughout the feature space. This assumption can be inadequate for data whose local geometry varies substantially across the underlying manifold.

**摘要：** 最近邻分类从根本上依赖于局部性的定义方式，然而传统的 $k$-NN 在整个特征空间中强制使用相同的邻域基数。对于在底层流形上局部几何结构差异巨大的数据，这种假设可能并不适用。

We introduce Curvature-Aware Radius Shrinkage for Adaptive Nearest Neighbor Classification (CARSANN), a geometry-driven framework that adapts the spatial support of each neighborhood according to local geometric complexity. CARSANN first estimates intrinsic dimensionality using TwoNN and constructs an intrinsic representation through principal component analysis.

我们引入了“基于曲率感知半径收缩的自适应最近邻分类”（CARSANN），这是一个由几何驱动的框架，它根据局部几何复杂度来调整每个邻域的空间支持范围。CARSANN 首先使用 TwoNN 估计内在维度，并通过主成分分析构建内在表示。

Local mean curvature is then estimated using a shape-operator-based formulation and controls neighborhood scale: highly curved regions receive stronger radius shrinkage, whereas approximately flat regions retain broader spatial support. Unlike methods that modify only the number of neighbors or the local metric, CARSANN explicitly adapts the spatial extent of local evidence.

随后，利用基于形状算子的公式估计局部平均曲率，并以此控制邻域尺度：高曲率区域会经历更强的半径收缩，而近似平坦的区域则保留更广的空间支持。与仅修改邻居数量或局部度量的方法不同，CARSANN 明确地调整了局部证据的空间范围。

Experiments on more than 70 real-world OpenML datasets show that CARSANN consistently improves upon standard $k$-NN and is competitive with adaptive nearest-neighbor methods. In a controlled comparison using the same base neighborhood size, CARSANN achieves higher balanced accuracy on 40 of 45 datasets, increasing mean balanced accuracy from 0.6506 to 0.7528.

在超过 70 个真实世界 OpenML 数据集上的实验表明，CARSANN 在性能上始终优于标准 $k$-NN，并与现有的自适应最近邻方法具有竞争力。在使用相同基础邻域大小的对照实验中，CARSANN 在 45 个数据集中的 40 个上实现了更高的平衡准确率，将平均平衡准确率从 0.6506 提升至 0.7528。

The advantage also persists against $k$-NN with fixed $k=5$. Friedman and Nemenyi tests confirm that the improvements are statistically significant. These results indicate that local manifold curvature can serve as an effective geometric control variable for adapting neighborhood support, providing a complementary paradigm to cardinality-based nearest-neighbor adaptation.

这种优势在与固定 $k=5$ 的 $k$-NN 对比时依然存在。Friedman 和 Nemenyi 检验确认这些改进在统计学上是显著的。这些结果表明，局部流形曲率可以作为一种有效的几何控制变量来调整邻域支持，为基于基数的最近邻自适应方法提供了一种互补的范式。
