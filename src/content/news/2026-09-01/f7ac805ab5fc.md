# Markiplier is now GoPro’s biggest shareholder
# Markiplier 现已成为 GoPro 最大股东

YouTuber Mark “Markiplier” Fischbach has invested enough in GoPro to become its single largest shareholder, with an 8.5 percent stake in the company, Bloomberg reports. Speaking to Bloomberg, Fischbach said he thought GoPro “seems undervalued” and sees his investment as “part of a larger mission to make filmmaking more accessible,” following the release of his first feature film, *Iron Lung*, earlier this year.

据彭博社报道，YouTuber Mark “Markiplier” Fischbach 已向 GoPro 投入了足够的资金，使其成为该公司最大的单一股东，持股比例达到 8.5%。在接受彭博社采访时，Fischbach 表示他认为 GoPro “似乎被低估了”，并将此次投资视为“让电影制作更普及这一宏大使命的一部分”，此前他于今年早些时候发布了自己的首部剧情长片《铁肺》（Iron Lung）。

Fischbach posted a video last week praising GoPro’s new Mission 1 ILS camera and comparing it to the pricier Red Komodo-X, saying he plans to use the Mission 1 ILS in future movies. It includes an affiliate code for the new camera, but we didn’t spot any mention of his involvement in the company in the caption or the video itself.

Fischbach 上周发布了一段视频，盛赞 GoPro 新款 Mission 1 ILS 摄像机，并将其与价格更高的 Red Komodo-X 进行了对比，称他计划在未来的电影中使用 Mission 1 ILS。视频中包含该新款摄像机的推广链接（affiliate code），但我们在视频说明或视频本身中并未发现任何关于他已入股该公司的提及。

Fellow YouTuber Marques Brownlee commented on the news of Fischbach’s investment in a post on Threads with a still from the video, saying, “This is not a line I see crossed very often, but to each their own.”

同为 YouTuber 的 Marques Brownlee 在 Threads 上发布了一张视频截图，评论了 Fischbach 的投资新闻，他写道：“这并不是我经常看到的越界行为，但每个人都有自己的选择。”

GoPro did not immediately respond to a request for comment.

GoPro 未能立即回应置评请求。
