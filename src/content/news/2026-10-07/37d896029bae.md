# HBO Max, Paramount+ 和 Discovery+ “将合并为一个单一服务”

After months of industry and customer speculation, Paramount Skydance has shed some light on the future of the HBO Max and Paramount+ streaming services. Paramount Skydance completed its acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery (WBD) today, ending a monthslong saga that began when Netflix last year announced its own plans to buy WBD. Netflix scrapped those plans in February. Skydance is the name of the company created from the $111 billion merger.

经过数月业内人士和消费者的猜测，Paramount Skydance 终于揭开了 HBO Max 和 Paramount+ 流媒体服务未来的面纱。Paramount Skydance 于今日完成了对华纳兄弟探索公司（WBD）的收购，结束了自去年 Netflix 宣布收购 WBD 计划以来长达数月的风波。Netflix 已于今年 2 月放弃了该计划。Skydance 是这家通过 1110 亿美元合并案所成立的新公司的名称。

In an announcement today, Skydance said that all of the direct-to-consumer streaming services it owns, including the Discovery+ service that Discovery Inc. (which merged with WarnerMedia in 2022 to create WBD) launched in 2021, “will unify into a single service over time.”

在今日的一份公告中，Skydance 表示，其拥有的所有直接面向消费者的流媒体服务，包括 Discovery Inc.（该公司于 2022 年与华纳媒体合并成立了 WBD）在 2021 年推出的 Discovery+ 服务，“将随着时间的推移统一为一个单一服务”。

The brief but informative statement brings some clarity to questions about whether Skydance would get rid of any of the streaming services or continue to offer all of the services individually or as a discounted bundle. The latter theory gained particular credence on October 1, when The Hollywood Reporter published statements that Casey Bloys, HBO Max’s content chief who is expected to lead Skydance’s combined streaming efforts, made at Bloomberg’s 2026 Screentime event: “I would say that I don’t want to comment on what is going to happen or anything like that, but I would point to the HBO Max-Disney bundle, which has been very successful… So could you see something like that happening? That would make a lot of sense.”

这份简短但信息量巨大的声明，澄清了关于 Skydance 是否会砍掉其中某些流媒体服务，还是会继续单独提供或以折扣捆绑包形式提供所有服务的疑问。后一种猜测在 10 月 1 日得到了进一步证实，当时《好莱坞报道》刊登了 HBO Max 内容主管 Casey Bloys 在彭博社 2026 年 Screentime 活动上的发言，他预计将领导 Skydance 合并后的流媒体业务：“我想说，我不想评论未来会发生什么，但我可以指出 HBO Max 与迪士尼的捆绑服务非常成功……所以你认为类似的事情会发生吗？这非常有道理。”

But with today’s announcement, it seems that one day, HBO’s high-budget dramas like *House of the Dragon* and *The Wire* will live under the same streaming service as Paramount+ shows like *Tulsa King* and *Star Trek: Strange New Worlds*, and Discovery+ content like the various *90 Day Fiancé* spinoffs and *Fixer Upper: Welcome Home*.

但随着今天的公告发布，未来某一天，HBO 的高预算剧集（如《龙之家族》和《火线》）将与 Paramount+ 的节目（如《塔尔萨之王》和《星际迷航：奇异新世界》）以及 Discovery+ 的内容（如各种《90天未婚夫》衍生剧和《房屋改造：欢迎回家》）共同存在于同一个流媒体服务之下。

A service combining Paramount+, HBO Max, and Discovery+ should alleviate the content fragmentation that forces streaming customers to subscribe to multiple services and help enhance Skydance’s ability to recommend relevant content to customers. A unified platform among the three services would simplify Skydance’s technological needs compared to operating three services independently, too. That may not necessarily result in lower subscription prices but could potentially lead to more advanced features and a better user experience, especially if Skydance can leverage the best features from each service.

将 Paramount+、HBO Max 和 Discovery+ 合并的服务，有望缓解内容碎片化问题，避免流媒体用户被迫订阅多个服务，并有助于增强 Skydance 向客户推荐相关内容的能力。与独立运营三个服务相比，三者统一的平台也将简化 Skydance 的技术需求。这未必会导致订阅价格下降，但可能会带来更先进的功能和更好的用户体验，特别是如果 Skydance 能够整合各项服务中的最佳功能。

However, uniting the three services could also result in less programming diversity and, as Skydance’s licensing costs grow, higher prices that force customers to pay for unwanted content. People hooked on mature, plot-driven series like *The Last of Us*, for example, may lack interest in unscripted or children’s programming. We’ll learn more about the future of Skydance’s properties in the coming months.

然而，合并这三项服务也可能导致节目多样性减少；随着 Skydance 授权成本的增加，价格上涨可能会迫使用户为他们不感兴趣的内容买单。例如，沉迷于《最后生还者》这类成熟、剧情驱动剧集的观众，可能对无剧本节目或儿童节目缺乏兴趣。我们将在未来几个月内进一步了解 Skydance 旗下资产的未来动向。

In addition to owning multiple streaming services, Skydance now owns CBS and, through its WBD purchase, CNN. The news platforms will operate independently, and Skydance will set up an “Editorial Independence Board” for the two. Skydance also now owns two rival kids’ channels, Cartoon Network and Nickelodeon, but hasn’t said whether it will combine them into a single network.

除了拥有多个流媒体服务外，Skydance 现在还拥有 CBS，并通过收购 WBD 获得了 CNN。这两个新闻平台将独立运营，Skydance 将为两者设立一个“编辑独立委员会”。Skydance 现在还拥有两个竞争对手儿童频道——卡通频道（Cartoon Network）和尼克儿童频道（Nickelodeon），但尚未说明是否会将它们合并为一个网络。
