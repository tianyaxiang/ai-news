### 10 climate tech companies to watch
### 值得关注的 10 家气候科技公司

Each year, MIT Technology Review puts together a list of the most promising climate tech companies in the world. This year, the stakes feel higher than ever. The world is on track for one of its hottest years on record, climate-fueled disasters are taking lives and costing billions, while political shifts and international conflict have stalled progress.
每年，《麻省理工科技评论》都会整理一份全球最具潜力的气候科技公司名单。今年，形势显得比以往任何时候都更加严峻。全球正处于有记录以来最热年份之一的轨道上，气候引发的灾难正在夺走生命并造成数十亿美元的损失，而政治变动和国际冲突也阻碍了进展。

We urgently need to cut emissions and avoid climate change’s most harmful effects, and that’s where our 10 companies come in. They’re working on everything from mobile flood barriers and semi-solid-state batteries to compressed-CO₂ energy storage and next-generation nuclear reactors. They also reflect the biggest shifts in climate tech—including the surging energy demands of AI data centers.
我们迫切需要减少排放并避免气候变化带来的最严重影响，而这正是我们选出的这 10 家公司的用武之地。它们的研究领域涵盖了从移动防洪屏障、半固态电池，到压缩二氧化碳储能和下一代核反应堆等方方面面。它们也反映了气候科技领域最大的转变——包括人工智能数据中心激增的能源需求。

Meet the 10 companies working on some of the biggest challenges in climate tech. Over the coming days, we’ll take a closer look at each of the 10 companies, along with how we chose this year’s list, right here in The Download. So stay tuned!
来认识一下这 10 家致力于解决气候科技领域重大挑战的公司吧。在接下来的几天里，我们将通过《下载》深入探讨这 10 家公司中的每一家，并介绍我们选择今年名单的标准。敬请关注！
