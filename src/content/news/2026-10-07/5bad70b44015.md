# 2026 Climate Tech Companies to Watch
# 2026 年值得关注的 10 家气候科技公司

10 Climate Tech Companies to Watch. Each year, the MIT Technology Review team puts together a list of some of the most promising climate tech companies in the world. Whether early-stage startup or multinational corporation, the businesses we’ve chosen are working on technologies to help us address climate change or adapt to our warming world.

值得关注的 10 家气候科技公司。每年，《麻省理工科技评论》团队都会整理出一份全球最具潜力的气候科技公司名单。无论处于初创阶段还是跨国企业，我们所选出的这些公司都在致力于开发相关技术，以帮助我们应对气候变化或适应日益变暖的世界。

There’s an urgent need for these innovators: We must begin to drastically reduce emissions to avoid the deadliest impacts of climate change, while also contending with its harmful effects. We hope that this list highlights the progress the world is making to tackle the climate crisis, as well as the breadth of solutions required.

这些创新者有着迫切的需求：我们必须开始大幅减少排放，以避免气候变化带来的最致命影响，同时还要应对其已经造成的危害。我们希望这份名单能够凸显全球在应对气候危机方面所取得的进展，以及所需解决方案的广度。

From energy storage powered by carbon dioxide to cleaner ways to make cement and refine critical minerals, these companies are building technologies to address the acute challenges we face. This is the fourth annual edition of this list. Learn more about how we chose the 2026 slate.

从利用二氧化碳驱动的储能技术，到更清洁的水泥制造和关键矿物提炼方式，这些公司正在构建各种技术，以解决我们面临的严峻挑战。这是该榜单发布的第四个年头。了解更多关于我们如何评选 2026 年入选名单的信息。
