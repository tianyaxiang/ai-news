# Tesla’s Model 3 and Model Y can be a backup battery for your house
# 特斯拉 Model 3 和 Model Y 现在可以作为家庭备用电池使用

Some Tesla Model 3 and Model Y owners can use their EV battery to keep the lights on longer during a power outage, now that Tesla’s expanding the Powershare Home Backup feature. It was previously only available on the Cybertruck, and also requires owning Tesla’s Powerwall 3. However, support for the Powerwall 2 and Powerwall Plus is “coming soon.”
随着特斯拉扩展其“Powershare 家庭备用”（Powershare Home Backup）功能，部分 Tesla Model 3 和 Model Y 车主现在可以利用电动汽车电池在停电期间维持电力供应。该功能此前仅适用于 Cybertruck，且同样需要用户拥有 Tesla Powerwall 3。不过，对 Powerwall 2 和 Powerwall Plus 的支持也“即将推出”。

Tesla’s EVs aren’t the first or only ones with bidirectional charging; companies like Kia and GM also offer vehicle-to-home power on their vehicles, while Nissan’s Leaf offered power-to-grid charging years ago.
特斯拉的电动汽车并非首款或唯一具备双向充电功能的车型；起亚（Kia）和通用汽车（GM）等公司也为其车辆提供了“车对家”（vehicle-to-home）供电功能，而日产（Nissan）的 Leaf 车型早在多年前就已提供过“车对电网”（power-to-grid）充电技术。

Tesla says the Model 3 and Model Y can extend home backup power to over two days for Premium and Performance models, which is shorter than the “over three days” Tesla claims you can get from the Cybertruck’s battery.
特斯拉表示，对于高级版（Premium）和高性能版（Performance）车型，Model 3 和 Model Y 可以将家庭备用电力延长至两天以上，这比特斯拉声称 Cybertruck 电池可提供的“三天以上”续航时间要短。

As Electrek points out, Tesla is only guaranteeing Home Backup will work on Model 3 and Model Y vehicles ordered from October 1st onward because not all older models include the PCS2 Lite inverter required to make it all work. So far, the feature is also only mentioned as available in the US and Puerto Rico, but not Canada, and not for the larger Model S or Model X. Tesla’s Powershare Grid Support feature is also still exclusive to the Cybertruck.
正如 Electrek 所指出的，特斯拉目前仅保证 10 月 1 日及之后订购的 Model 3 和 Model Y 车型支持“家庭备用”功能，因为并非所有旧款车型都配备了实现该功能所需的 PCS2 Lite 逆变器。到目前为止，该功能仅在美属地区（美国和波多黎各）可用，尚不支持加拿大，也不适用于更大尺寸的 Model S 或 Model X。此外，特斯拉的“Powershare 电网支持”（Powershare Grid Support）功能目前仍为 Cybertruck 独享。
