# Make a 20-second animatic from inside your AI chat with one MCP connector
# 只需一个 MCP 连接器，即可在 AI 对话中制作 20 秒的动画分镜

I'm the maker of Kleo, so take this with that in mind. Kleo is an MCP server that lets an AI assistant plan and render short videos. Here is the shortest path to try it, with no card.
我是 Kleo 的开发者，请在阅读时知悉这一点。Kleo 是一个 MCP 服务器，允许 AI 助手规划并渲染短视频。以下是无需绑定银行卡即可尝试的最快路径。

Add the connector. In Claude: Settings > Connectors > Add custom connector, name it Kleo, paste https://mcp.kleooai.com/mcp, sign in to Kleo. Claude Code: claude mcp add --transport http kleo https://mcp.kleooai.com/mcp, then /mcp and authenticate. Setup for ChatGPT, ai, Cursor, Codex and OpenCode is at kleooai.com.
添加连接器。在 Claude 中：进入 Settings > Connectors > Add custom connector，将其命名为 Kleo，粘贴 https://mcp.kleooai.com/mcp，并登录 Kleo。在 Claude Code 中：运行 `claude mcp add --transport http kleo https://mcp.kleooai.com/mcp`，然后输入 `/mcp` 并进行身份验证。ChatGPT、ai、Cursor、Codex 和 OpenCode 的设置方法请访问 kleooai.com。

Paste this brief into your assistant: "Use Kleo to plan a 20-second animatic about a paper boat returning to its maker. Animation look, English narration, 9:16. Show me the treatment, confirm that the product is an animatic, and check the exact credit cost against my available balance. Wait for my approval before rendering. Do not start a paid film or buy credits."
将以下提示词粘贴给你的 AI 助手：“使用 Kleo 规划一个 20 秒的动画分镜，主题是一只纸船回到它的制造者身边。动画风格，英文旁白，比例 9:16。向我展示策划方案，确认产出物为动画分镜，并核对所需积分与我的可用余额。在渲染前等待我的批准。不要开始付费影片制作或购买积分。”

Read the treatment, change anything you want, then approve. The starter balance (5 credits) covers one animatic. What is rough: sign-in is required, rendering takes a while, and full films are paid. I'd love feedback on where the first run confused you.
阅读策划方案，根据需要进行修改，然后批准。初始余额（5 个积分）足以制作一个动画分镜。目前尚不完善之处：需要登录、渲染耗时较长，且完整影片需要付费。我很期待收到反馈，了解你在首次使用时哪些地方感到困惑。
