# Ocarina of Time is even better if you use a smartphone app

《时之笛》如果配合智能手机应用使用，体验会更好。

Finding Ocarina of Time’s hidden collectibles is a lot easier with the help of Zelda Notes.

在 Zelda Notes 的帮助下，寻找《时之笛》中隐藏的收集品变得容易多了。

I never liked hunting for Golden Skulltulas in The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. The game has 100 of them tucked all over Hyrule, and even over my many hours with the N64 classic growing up, I can’t remember ever actually finding all of them, or even wanting to.

我从来都不喜欢在《塞尔达传说：时之笛》里搜寻黄金骷髅蜘蛛。游戏中共有 100 只蜘蛛散落在海拉鲁大陆各处，尽管我小时候在 N64 原版上花了很多时间，但我记不清自己是否真的找齐过它们，甚至从未有过这种念头。

Jumping into the Ocarina of Time remake, I expected to ignore the Golden Skulltulas once again. But while poking around Nintendo’s Zelda Notes companion app, I discovered that its maps are a huge help to tracking down all of the elusive golden spiders — and pretty much every other collectible in the game — and now I find myself constantly opening up the app to make sure I haven’t missed a secret.

在开始游玩《时之笛》重制版时，我本打算再次无视这些黄金骷髅蜘蛛。但在研究任天堂的 Zelda Notes 配套应用时，我发现它的地图对于追踪那些难以捉摸的黄金蜘蛛——以及游戏中几乎所有其他收集品——有着巨大的帮助。现在，我发现自己会不断打开这个应用，以确保没有错过任何秘密。

Inside Zelda Notes, which is available in the free Nintendo Switch app (you don’t need a Switch Online subscription to use it), you can peruse a big map of Hyrule and tap on individual areas for a closer look. The app offers just enough direction to nudge me toward an elusive pickup; when zoomed in, the app drops icons on the map showing you the locations of different collectibles, and if you tap on an icon, the app will show a popup noting any items or special requirements you need to fulfill to be able to obtain it.

在 Zelda Notes（可在免费的 Nintendo Switch 应用中获取，无需 Switch Online 会员订阅）中，你可以浏览海拉鲁的大地图，并点击特定区域进行查看。该应用提供的指引恰到好处，能引导我找到那些难以获取的物品；放大地图后，应用会显示不同收集品的位置图标，点击图标则会弹出提示，说明获取该物品所需的道具或特殊条件。

For Skulltula hunting, the map has already saved my sanity many times. While exploring, I’ll often come across the telltale Golden Skulltula sound but have no idea how to get to the tricky spider, even with the help of additional chimes from the new Echo Gem Skulltula tracking item. But with a peek at the Zelda Notes app, I can pinpoint the exact location of where the Skulltula is supposed to be and if I need to use a certain item or change the time of day to be able to get to it.

在搜寻骷髅蜘蛛的过程中，这张地图已经多次拯救了我的理智。探索时，我经常能听到黄金骷髅蜘蛛特有的声音，但即便有新的“回声宝石”追踪道具辅助，我还是不知道该如何接近那些棘手的蜘蛛。但只要看一眼 Zelda Notes 应用，我就能精确定位蜘蛛所在的位置，并确认是否需要使用特定道具或改变游戏内时间才能获取它。

Yes, it can be annoying to have to open my phone and dig through an app to track down all of the Skulltulas and other objects of curiosity scattered around Hyrule. I play video games to try and get away from my phone, not look at it more. And sure, I could just look up an online guide, but that often means wading through walkthroughs of varying quality, trusting an AI summary, or scrubbing through a YouTube video.

诚然，为了追踪散落在海拉鲁各处的骷髅蜘蛛和其他好奇物品，不得不打开手机并翻找应用确实有些烦人。我玩游戏是为了远离手机，而不是盯着它看。当然，我也可以直接查阅在线攻略，但这通常意味着要在质量参差不齐的指南中筛选，或者去信任 AI 的总结，又或是反复拖动 YouTube 视频进度条。

But with Zelda Notes, I’m usually in and out relatively quickly, putting down my phone after seeing a clue or an icon that pushes me in the direction of the next golden spider. I’ve already found more than 70 Golden Skulltulas, and to my surprise, I now want to hunt for the full 100.

但有了 Zelda Notes，我通常能很快完成查询，在看到指引我找到下一只黄金蜘蛛的线索或图标后，就能放下手机。我已经找到了 70 多只黄金骷髅蜘蛛，令我惊讶的是，我现在竟然想把 100 只全部找齐。
