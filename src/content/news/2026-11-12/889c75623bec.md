Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.12974 (cs) [Submitted on 8 Nov 2026] Title: StatPrompt-LLM: A Lightweight Statistical Refinement for Foundation-Style Time-Series Forecasting Authors: Aniket Sinha, Veronica W. Tsou.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.12974 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 8 日] 标题：StatPrompt-LLM：一种用于基础风格时间序列预测的轻量级统计优化方法 作者：Aniket Sinha, Veronica W. Tsou。

Abstract: Time-series forecasting has recently seen a paradigm shift with the introduction of foundation-style models that adapt large pretrained language architectures to sequential data. However, effectively exposing fundamental temporal structures, such as trend, scale, and temporal dependence, to these models remains an open challenge, particularly when the available observations are limited.

摘要：随着将大型预训练语言架构适配到序列数据的基础风格模型的引入，时间序列预测领域近期经历了范式转移。然而，如何有效地向这些模型展示诸如趋势、尺度和时间依赖性等基本时间结构，仍然是一个悬而未决的挑战，特别是在可用观测数据有限的情况下。

In this paper, we conduct an exploratory empirical study across different generations of forecasting architectures and propose StatPrompt-LLM, a lightweight statistical refinement of Time-LLM. StatPrompt-LLM augments the existing Time-LLM prompting mechanism with dataset-level statistical information and introduces a zero-initialised autoregressive bypass as a complementary linear forecasting pathway.

在本文中，我们对不同代际的预测架构进行了探索性实证研究，并提出了 StatPrompt-LLM，这是对 Time-LLM 的一种轻量级统计优化。StatPrompt-LLM 通过数据集层面的统计信息增强了现有的 Time-LLM 提示机制，并引入了一个零初始化的自回归旁路作为补充性的线性预测路径。

We evaluate our framework on electricity and meteorological time-series benchmarks, including ETTh1, ETTh2, and Weather, under full-prefix and reduced-prefix configurations. Under the 10% reduced-prefix configuration, StatPrompt-LLM achieved lower in-sample prediction errors than Time-LLM, with unweighted average relative reductions across datasets of 40.2% in MSE and 21.4% in MAE.

我们在电力和气象时间序列基准（包括 ETTh1、ETTh2 和 Weather）上，在全前缀和缩减前缀配置下评估了我们的框架。在 10% 缩减前缀配置下，StatPrompt-LLM 实现了比 Time-LLM 更低的样本内预测误差，各数据集的 MSE 和 MAE 未加权平均相对减少量分别为 40.2% 和 21.4%。

Full-prefix results were more dataset-dependent. Ablation analyses revealed relatively modest and dataset-dependent changes when selected additional statistical descriptor groups were masked within the combined architecture. Computational profiling indicated additional model parameters and runtime overhead relative to Time-LLM.

全前缀的结果则更依赖于数据集。消融分析显示，当在组合架构中屏蔽选定的额外统计描述符组时，变化相对温和且取决于数据集。计算分析表明，相对于 Time-LLM，该模型增加了额外的模型参数和运行时间开销。
