Title: Rethinking in Spikes: Mitigating Hallucinations in MDLMs with Step-Aware Decoding

标题：在尖峰中反思：通过步长感知解码缓解多模态扩散语言模型（MDLM）中的幻觉

Recent advancements in multimodal diffusion language models (MDLMs) have exhibited strong global modeling capabilities through iterative refinement. We observe that mask uncertainty follows an overall downward trend during iterative denoising, while a few denoising steps exhibit localized entropy spikes closely associated with hallucinations.

多模态扩散语言模型（MDLM）的最新进展通过迭代细化展现出了强大的全局建模能力。我们观察到，在迭代去噪过程中，掩码不确定性总体呈下降趋势，但少数去噪步骤会出现与幻觉密切相关的局部熵尖峰。

We argue that anomalous steps can be identified by measuring the entropy deviation of each denoising step from its neighboring temporal window. We hypothesize that discrete token commitment discards the distributional information at masked positions, limiting the continued interaction between candidate semantics and the context during entropy spikes.

我们认为，可以通过测量每个去噪步骤与其相邻时间窗口之间的熵偏差来识别异常步骤。我们假设，离散标记的确定会丢弃掩码位置的分布信息，从而限制了熵尖峰期间候选语义与上下文之间的持续交互。

With this goal, we present StepRefine, a plug-and-play decoding strategy that leverages semantic context to achieve reliable denoising. The core of our method lies in a step-aware switch between discrete denoising and latent refinement.

基于此目标，我们提出了 StepRefine，这是一种利用语义上下文实现可靠去噪的即插即用解码策略。我们方法的核心在于离散去噪与潜在细化之间的步长感知切换。

The model employs probability-weighted embeddings at entropy spike steps to preserve diverse reasoning hypotheses and performs multiple rounds of continuous refinement. Meanwhile, a refinement controller monitors distributional stability to reduce unnecessary loops and switch the model back to standard discrete denoising.

该模型在熵尖峰步骤采用概率加权嵌入以保留多样的推理假设，并执行多轮连续细化。同时，细化控制器会监控分布稳定性，以减少不必要的循环并将模型切换回标准的离散去噪。

Through extensive experiments, StepRefine demonstrates significant hallucination mitigation across different MDLMs on multimodal benchmarks.

通过大量实验，StepRefine 在多模态基准测试中证明了其在不同 MDLM 上显著缓解幻觉的能力。
