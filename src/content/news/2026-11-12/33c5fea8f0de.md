Try Tabletop: Customizable, collaborative spaces for groups to plan, play and do more together.

尝试 Tabletop：为团队提供可定制的协作空间，以便共同规划、娱乐及完成更多任务。

Roman Nurik Design & Engineering Lead, Google Labs Nov 11, 2026 Whether you’re planning a family trip, running a rec league or hosting a potluck, coordinating as a group is easier when everyone can pitch in, build and decide together in one place.

Roman Nurik，Google Labs 设计与工程主管，2026 年 11 月 11 日。无论您是在规划家庭旅行、组织业余联赛还是举办聚餐，当每个人都能在一个地方共同参与、构建和决策时，团队协作会变得更加轻松。

Today, we’re introducing Tabletop, a new Google Labs experiment built for real-time collaboration. Just add in your links, files, and ideas, then prompt @tabletop — or pick a starter template — to generate custom interactive trip itineraries, dinner polls, expense splitters, and tournament brackets.

今天，我们推出了 Tabletop，这是一项专为实时协作而构建的全新 Google Labs 实验。只需添加您的链接、文件和想法，然后通过 @tabletop 发出指令，或者选择一个入门模板，即可生成自定义的交互式旅行行程、晚餐投票、费用分摊工具和锦标赛对阵表。

Once your space is ready, drop the invite link in your group chat to let everyone jump in and use it at the same time, like in a Google Doc. Tabletop is rolling out in the U.S. (18+) via waitlist. Pull up a chair and sign up at labs.google/tabletop.

一旦您的空间准备就绪，只需将邀请链接发送到群聊中，即可让所有人像使用 Google 文档一样同时加入并使用它。Tabletop 目前正通过候补名单在美国（18 岁以上）推出。欢迎加入我们，并在 labs.google/tabletop 注册。
