Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.12911 (cs) [Submitted on 22 Oct 2026] Title: Uncovering the Blind Spots of OKS-mAP: A Unified Protocol for Occlusion, Joint-Angle, and Temporal Evaluation of 2D Pose Estimators Authors: Yifan Fan.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.12911 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 10 月 22 日] 标题：揭示 OKS-mAP 的盲点：一种用于 2D 姿态估计器的遮挡、关节角度和时间评估的统一协议 作者：Yifan Fan。

Abstract: The dominant metric for 2D human pose estimation, OKS-mAP, has three blind spots: it does not distinguish visible from occluded keypoints, does not measure joint-angle error, and is silent on the temporal stability of tracking-free per-frame inference. We propose a supplementary protocol with three modules: (M1) OKS-mAP stratified by ground-truth visibility, with Delta_occ = mAP(v=1) - mAP(full), quantifying how much the aggregate metric masks occlusion-specific differences; (M2) joint-angle error (RMSE/MAE/P95) plus knee-valgus detection consistency; (M3) frame-to-frame displacement, median-filter residual jitter, and jump rate, before and after OneEuro smoothing.

摘要：2D 人体姿态估计的主流指标 OKS-mAP 存在三个盲点：它无法区分可见关键点与被遮挡关键点，无法测量关节角度误差，且对无跟踪逐帧推理的时间稳定性保持沉默。我们提出了一个包含三个模块的补充协议：(M1) 基于地面真值可见性分层的 OKS-mAP，通过 Delta_occ = mAP(v=1) - mAP(full) 量化聚合指标掩盖了多少遮挡特有的差异；(M2) 关节角度误差（RMSE/MAE/P95）及膝外翻检测一致性；(M3) OneEuro 平滑处理前后的帧间位移、中值滤波残差抖动和跳变率。

We ran it on four datasets (COCO val2017, CrowdPose, OCHuman, PoseTrack18) and four models from two architecture families (YOLO11s-pose, an occlusion-domain fine-tuned variant, ViTPose-B/L), under a common detector-free oracle protocol and an open-source harness. Delta_occ is negative in every combination (-0.19 to -0.47): aggregate mAP overestimates occluded-scene performance in every tested combination.

我们在四个数据集（COCO val2017、CrowdPose、OCHuman、PoseTrack18）和来自两个架构系列（YOLO11s-pose、一个遮挡领域微调变体、ViTPose-B/L）的四个模型上运行了该协议，并采用了通用的无检测器预言机协议和开源工具。Delta_occ 在所有组合中均为负值（-0.19 到 -0.47）：在每个测试组合中，聚合 mAP 都高估了遮挡场景的性能。

Joint-angle error is amplified in occluded layers; angle metrics reveal differences mAP cannot localize, e.g., the smaller occlusion-trained model significantly outperforming a larger transformer in occluded elbows. On PoseTrack18 val, raw jitter matched between the two YOLO models; pixel-domain OneEuro smoothing cut jump rates 9-10% at no matched-scope AP cost; residual jitter persists after detector removal; the transformer pair's jump rate is significantly lower; smoothing benefits replicate across architectures.

关节角度误差在遮挡层中被放大；角度指标揭示了 mAP 无法定位的差异，例如，较小的遮挡训练模型在遮挡肘部的表现显著优于较大的 Transformer 模型。在 PoseTrack18 val 上，两个 YOLO 模型之间的原始抖动相匹配；像素域的 OneEuro 平滑处理在不影响匹配范围 AP 的情况下将跳变率降低了 9-10%；移除检测器后残差抖动依然存在；Transformer 对的跳变率显著更低；平滑处理的益处在不同架构中均可复现。

Stratified mAP tracks full mAP closely (sign-consistency 1.0, 12/12), serving magnitude diagnosis rather than re-ranking; angle-metric difference vectors (especially knee) partially diverge, suggesting independent information. The protocol and harness are open-sourced, complementing rather than replacing OKS-mAP.

分层 mAP 与完整 mAP 紧密相关（符号一致性 1.0, 12/12），主要用于量级诊断而非重新排序；角度指标差异向量（尤其是膝盖）部分发散，表明存在独立信息。该协议和工具已开源，旨在补充而非取代 OKS-mAP。
