# NASA's secret weapon in the lunar space race may be ... procurement reform?

Senior NASA officials held a lengthy news conference last weekend, laying out a series of lunar contract awards, new contract opportunities, and mission decisions. They followed one after the other: landers, nuclear-powered rovers, scouting drones, power generation, storage, and communication satellites. It never seemed to end. “Hopefully, we’re keeping industry on their toes trying to catch up to all the solicitations we’re doing,” said Carlos García-Galán, manager of the Moon Base Program, more than 90 minutes into the news conference. “This is what it takes to do the Moon Base in the timeline we have set forth.”

上周末，美国国家航空航天局（NASA）的高级官员举行了一场冗长的新闻发布会，公布了一系列月球合同授予、新的合同机会以及任务决策。这些项目接踵而至：着陆器、核动力漫游车、侦察无人机、发电、存储以及通信卫星，似乎永无止境。月球基地项目经理卡洛斯·加西亚-加兰（Carlos García-Galán）在发布会进行到90多分钟时表示：“希望我们能让业界保持警惕，努力跟上我们发布的所有招标需求。这就是在既定时间表内完成月球基地建设所必须付出的努力。”

The rapid-fire announcement of awards tells us a few things about NASA’s efforts to return to the Moon and establish a durable settlement there during the next decade. First, the agency is serious about this effort—the Artemis Moon Base program represents the most credible effort to establish a permanent outpost on another world in NASA’s history. Second, NASA is reaching out to all corners of the US commercial space industry, casting an extraordinarily wide net to reach companies that have yet to fly any hardware into orbit. Third, NASA is getting creative about how it’s buying these services, and this may matter most of all.

这些密集发布的合同公告向我们传达了关于NASA在未来十年重返月球并建立持久定居点努力的几点信息。首先，该机构对这项工作是认真的——“阿尔忒弥斯”月球基地计划代表了NASA历史上在另一个星球上建立永久前哨站最可信的努力。其次，NASA正在接触美国商业航天工业的各个角落，撒下了一张极其宽广的网，以覆盖那些尚未有任何硬件进入轨道的公司。第三，NASA在购买这些服务的方式上正变得富有创意，而这可能最为重要。

Changing way NASA buys stuff. There was a lot packed into the announcements this weekend, so to make sense of it all, I called Mike Gold, a lawyer and former NASA associate administrator who set up the Artemis Accords, who is now the president of Redwire Space. He was at NASA at the beginning of Artemis and understands what it takes to get the government moving. He said he was blown away. During a period of seven days, Gold said, NASA put out five proposals for lunar contracts. “The pace that’s occurring, and the nature of the procurements is unprecedented,” Gold said. “You are seeing an all-hands-on-deck approach. Big companies, small companies, new companies, old companies.”

改变NASA的采购方式。这个周末的公告中包含了很多内容，为了理清这一切，我致电了迈克·戈尔德（Mike Gold）。他是一位律师，曾任NASA副局长，参与制定了《阿尔忒弥斯协定》，目前担任Redwire Space公司的总裁。他在“阿尔忒弥斯”计划初期就在NASA工作，深知如何推动政府部门运作。他说自己感到非常震撼。戈尔德表示，在七天的时间里，NASA发布了五项月球合同提案。“这种发生的速度以及采购的性质是前所未有的，”戈尔德说，“你看到的是一种全员参与的方法。大公司、小公司、新公司、老公司都在其中。”

What has impressed Gold most about NASA’s strategy since announcing the Moon Base in March, at the Ignition event, has been the agency’s evolving procurement strategy. The space agency has adopted a flexible procurement strategy similar to one used by the US Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency. There are two components. NASA has largely used a mechanism called Broad Agency Announcements, which ask for a capability such as providing power on the surface of the Moon. This allows NASA to ask industry for ideas without being too prescriptive and levying a bunch of requirements. It’s rather like instead of telling industry precisely what it wants, NASA asks industry what it can do.

自三月份在Ignition活动上宣布月球基地计划以来，NASA的战略中最让戈尔德印象深刻的，是该机构不断演进的采购策略。NASA采用了一种类似于美国国防高级研究计划局（DARPA）所使用的灵活采购策略。该策略包含两个部分。NASA主要使用了一种名为“广泛机构公告”（Broad Agency Announcements）的机制，旨在寻求某种能力，例如在月球表面提供电力。这使得NASA能够在不进行过多规定或强加一堆要求的情况下向业界征求意见。这更像是NASA不再告诉业界它确切想要什么，而是询问业界能做什么。

Critically, Gold said, the space agency has also used a DARPA-style preliminary proposal process. In response to a traditional procurement, interested companies would prepare a detailed proposal, often running dozens, if not hundreds, of pages. This required an extraordinary amount of time. With the Moon Base, NASA is employing a two-step process. After NASA announces the need for a service, interested companies submit a short white paper describing their solution. After a short review, NASA identifies the concepts it wants to pursue further, and only then do companies invest the time and resources to prepare a detailed proposal.

戈尔德指出，至关重要的是，NASA还采用了DARPA式的初步提案流程。在传统的采购模式下，感兴趣的公司需要准备一份详细的提案，篇幅往往长达数十甚至数百页，这需要耗费大量时间。而在月球基地项目中，NASA采用了两步走流程。在NASA宣布服务需求后，感兴趣的公司只需提交一份简短的白皮书来描述其解决方案。经过简短的审查，NASA确定其希望进一步推进的概念，此后公司才会投入时间和资源来准备详细的提案。

Gold said several lunar opportunities interested Redwire, and the company submitted initial proposals in response. But it only had to prepare a full proposal for opportunities that NASA wanted to pursue, including using a variant of its roll-out solar arrays on the Moon to generate power. “We were able to focus the lion’s share of our resources in what the agency really wanted and what we were best at,” he said.

戈尔德表示，Redwire对几个月球项目机会很感兴趣，并提交了初步提案。但该公司只需为NASA希望推进的项目准备完整提案，其中包括在月球上使用其卷轴式太阳能电池阵列的变体来发电。“我们能够将大部分资源集中在机构真正想要的东西以及我们最擅长的领域上，”他说。

An all-hands-on-deck approach. From this shift, it seems clear that NASA is trying to move away from micromanaging every individual lunar-base subsystem and instead seeks to coordinate independently developed commercial capabilities. The space agency is sourcing them from everywhere, from major contractors like Lockheed Martin, SpaceX, Blue Origin, and Northrop Grumman to relatively new startups such as Impulse Space, ispace-US, Voyager Technologies, Quantum Space, and more. With questions emerging about how committed SpaceX is to NASA work in the future, the space agency appears to be seeking to diversify the way it gets cargo to the Moon.

全员参与的方法。从这一转变来看，很明显NASA正试图摆脱对每一个月球基地子系统的微观管理，转而寻求协调独立开发的商业能力。该机构正从各处采购这些能力，从洛克希德·马丁、SpaceX、蓝色起源和诺斯罗普·格鲁曼等主要承包商，到Impulse Space、ispace-US、Voyager Technologies、Quantum Space等相对较新的初创公司。随着外界对SpaceX未来对NASA工作的投入程度产生疑问，NASA似乎正在寻求使其向月球运送货物的方式多样化。

For example, it is leaning into Relativity Space and its large (but unproven) Terran R rocket, as well as orbital transfer vehicles built by Impulse Space. NASA is prioritizing the creation of a whole ecosystem of potential contractors rather than relying on one or two prime承包商. In doing so, NASA is taking a big risk. It is attempting to accelerate its lunar program by buying services from companies whose hardware is not yet operational and, in some cases, exists only as advanced engineering designs. For NASA, ultimately, it is a high-risk, high-reward approach. But in a demanding contest with China to reach and establish settlements on the Moon, NASA’s post-Apollo timidity is no longer tenable.

例如，它正倾向于Relativity Space及其大型（但尚未经过验证的）Terran R火箭，以及由Impulse Space制造的轨道转移飞行器。NASA正在优先考虑创建一个完整的潜在承包商生态系统，而不是依赖一两家主要承包商。这样做，NASA承担了巨大的风险。它试图通过从硬件尚未投入使用、甚至在某些情况下仅存在于先进工程设计阶段的公司购买服务，来加速其月球计划。对于NASA来说，这最终是一种高风险、高回报的方法。但在与中国争夺月球登陆和建立定居点的激烈竞争中，NASA在“阿波罗”计划后的那种畏缩已不再可行。

So how will NASA pay for all of the new stuff? Some of the funding is being repurposed from the previously shuttered Lunar Gateway program, NASA Administrator Jared Isaacman said. For example, as part of the “One Big Beautiful Bill” act passed in 2025, NASA received $2.6 billion for the Gateway. Some of the funding was already appropriated through the agency’s Commercial Lunar Payload Services Program.
