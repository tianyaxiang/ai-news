# Tracking Anthropic-discovered vulnerabilities

We're seven months into Anthropic's flagship Project Glasswing, and it's time for another update on the results. I've been tracking the project to see whether its claims are overhyped or grounded in reality.

Anthropic 的旗舰项目 Project Glasswing 已经进行了七个月，现在是时候对结果进行另一次更新了。我一直在追踪该项目，以观察其声明是被过度炒作还是基于现实。

As we all recall, Anthropic launched Project Glasswing on April 7th, claiming, "We have identified thousands of additional high- and critical-severity vulnerabilities that we are working on responsibly disclosing to open source maintainers." What surprised me was how little evidence the project offered, which made me wonder whether this was more of an overhyped marketing campaign.

正如我们所记得的那样，Anthropic 于 4 月 7 日启动了 Project Glasswing，声称：“我们已经确定了数千个额外的高危和严重漏洞，我们正致力于负责任地向开源维护者披露这些漏洞。”令我惊讶的是，该项目提供的证据少之又少，这让我怀疑这是否更多是一场过度炒作的营销活动。

That said, we shouldn't overlook that AI is an effective tool for finding vulnerabilities. But that story goes well beyond any single frontier model's capabilities.

话虽如此，我们不应忽视人工智能是发现漏洞的有效工具。但这个故事远超任何单一前沿模型的能力范畴。

I started tracking Anthropic's discovered vulnerabilities on April 17th, shortly after launch, in an open source repo called Anthropic Credited CVEs. The goal was to see whether Anthropic's claims lived up to the hype. This was before Anthropic launched its own disclosure ledger, which is only updated in infrequent bulk batches.

我在项目启动后不久的 4 月 17 日开始在一个名为“Anthropic Credited CVEs”的开源仓库中追踪 Anthropic 发现的漏洞。其目的是观察 Anthropic 的声明是否名副其实。这发生在 Anthropic 推出其自己的披露分类账之前，该分类账仅在不频繁的批量更新中进行更新。

My repo tracks every vulnerability attributable to Anthropic using CVE records, GHSAs, Linux kernel commits, and Anthropic's own ledger. I also manually audit advisories to catch CVEs that might otherwise get overlooked.

我的仓库使用 CVE 记录、GHSA、Linux 内核提交和 Anthropic 自己的分类账，追踪每一个可归因于 Anthropic 的漏洞。我还手动审计公告，以捕捉那些可能被忽略的 CVE。

After seven months, I've tracked 442 CVEs attributable to Anthropic. That's an average of just 63 per month. Out of the 93,450 CVEs published this year, that's only about 0.5% of all CVEs.

七个月后，我追踪到了 442 个可归因于 Anthropic 的 CVE。平均每月仅 63 个。在今年发布的 93,450 个 CVE 中，这仅占所有 CVE 的约 0.5%。

For comparison, Anthropic's own public ledger lists just 217 fixed vulnerabilities, so I've tracked more than twice as many as Anthropic has disclosed itself.

作为对比，Anthropic 自己的公共分类账仅列出了 217 个已修复的漏洞，因此我追踪到的数量是 Anthropic 自行披露数量的两倍多。

You may recall that Glasswing launched with the message that we'd entered the Mythos era of vulnerability discovery. But are these vulnerabilities actually useful to attackers? To find out, I've been tracking Anthropic-discovered CVEs against VulnCheck KEV. So far, only 2 of the 442 have been exploited in the wild, just 0.45%. (CVE-2026-61500 and CVE-2026-26980)

你可能还记得 Glasswing 推出时宣称我们已经进入了漏洞发现的“神话（Mythos）”时代。但这些漏洞对攻击者真的有用吗？为了找出答案，我一直在对照 VulnCheck KEV 追踪 Anthropic 发现的 CVE。到目前为止，442 个漏洞中只有 2 个在野外被利用，仅占 0.45%。（CVE-2026-61500 和 CVE-2026-26980）

When Anthropic first launched its ledger in May, it reported 23,019 findings and a 90.8% true positive rate. As of its most recent update on November 2nd, there are 37,966 findings and just 217 fixes. That looks like a staggering failure to me, but let's take a closer look.

当 Anthropic 在 5 月首次推出其分类账时，它报告了 23,019 个发现结果，真实阳性率为 90.8%。截至 11 月 2 日的最新更新，共有 37,966 个发现结果，但仅有 217 个修复。在我看来，这看起来是一个惊人的失败，但让我们仔细看看。

Of the 37,966 findings Anthropic reports, only 7,349 (19.4%) have been made public. Just 217 of those have been marked fixed. The more glaring number is that 4.4% (1,654) of the project's findings have already been marked as duplicates, 7.5x more duplicates than fixes. On top of that, 419 findings have been withdrawn. It seems odd that the ledger now claims a 94.2% true positive rate, up from 90.8% at launch, when more findings have been withdrawn or marked as duplicates than fixed, likely a feedback loop missing to the triage rates listed as “True Positives”.

在 Anthropic 报告的 37,966 个发现中，只有 7,349 个（19.4%）已公开。其中只有 217 个被标记为已修复。更显眼的数字是，该项目 4.4%（1,654 个）的发现已被标记为重复，重复数量是修复数量的 7.5 倍。此外，还有 419 个发现被撤回。分类账现在声称真实阳性率为 94.2%（高于发布时的 90.8%），这似乎很奇怪，因为被撤回或标记为重复的发现比已修复的还要多，这很可能是因为列为“真实阳性”的分类率中缺少了反馈循环。

The trend since my September update is even more telling:

自 9 月份更新以来的趋势更说明问题：

In two months, Anthropic added nearly 12,000 findings and fixed just 15 more. Duplicates went from two to thousands, which is exactly what I warned about in September: the longer findings sit, the more likely someone else finds and fixes them first. I don't know how anyone can read these numbers as anything but damning.

在两个月内，Anthropic 增加了近 12,000 个发现，却仅多修复了 15 个。重复项从两个增加到数千个，这正是我在 9 月份警告过的情况：发现结果搁置的时间越长，其他人先发现并修复它们的可能性就越大。我不明白怎么会有人不认为这些数字是毁灭性的。

You may also notice a correction in my visual. There appears to be a bug on Anthropic's ledger that inflates the number of findings marked as disclosed. Check for yourself using the "disclosed" filter.

你可能还会注意到我的图表中有一个修正。Anthropic 的分类账似乎存在一个错误，夸大了标记为“已披露”的发现数量。你可以使用“已披露”过滤器自行检查。

Having humans triage every finding clearly didn't scale, and Anthropic has said as much. In its OSS Scanner launch post on October 8th, Anthropic said it had found more than 29,000 candidate vulnerabilities but had manually reviewed only about 6,000, and that it remains "bottlenecked on our human capacity to validate these findings." It had also already sent nearly 5,000 unverified reports to maintainers who asked for everything in bulk.

让人工对每一个发现进行分类显然无法扩展，Anthropic 自己也承认了这一点。在 10 月 8 日的 OSS Scanner 发布文章中，Anthropic 表示它已经发现了超过 29,000 个候选漏洞，但仅手动审查了约 6,000 个，并且仍然“受限于我们验证这些发现的人力”。它还已经向要求批量获取所有报告的维护者发送了近 5,000 份未经核实的报告。

So with the Glasswing open source initiative hitting a wall, launching a self-service OSS Scanner that projects can opt into makes sense. Maintainers can now request a scan and triage the findings themselves. Anthropic is upfront that these reports are "fully model-generated, without human review or triage" and that "it is possible reports will be incorrect or invalid."

因此，随着 Glasswing 开源计划陷入僵局，推出一个项目可以选择加入的自助式 OSS Scanner 是有意义的。维护者现在可以请求扫描并自行对发现结果进行分类。Anthropic 直言不讳地表示，这些报告是“完全由模型生成的，没有经过人工审查或分类”，并且“报告可能是不正确或无效的”。

In other words, the triage bottleneck didn't go away. It moved to the maintainers. If the true positive rate is really as high as Anthropic claims, that shouldn't be a problem.

换句话说，分类瓶颈并没有消失。它转移到了维护者身上。如果真实阳性率真的像 Anthropic 声称的那样高，那应该不成问题。

Three days ago, Daniel Stenberg, who maintains curl, announced that the project had received a batch of 45 vulnerability reports from Anthropic. Anthropic rated only one as medium severity and the rest as low. Daniel is moving them over to GitHub at a deliberate pace "to soften the impact for the team," with plans to have them all published by the end of the week. This gives us a rare look at what Anthropic's findings look like once a maintainer actually validates them.

三天前，curl 的维护者 Daniel Stenberg 宣布该项目收到了 Anthropic 发来的一批 45 份漏洞报告。Anthropic 仅将其中一份评为中等严重程度，其余均为低危。Daniel 正以审慎的速度将它们转移到 GitHub 上，以“减轻对团队的影响”，并计划在本周末前全部发布。这让我们难得地看到了当维护者真正验证 Anthropic 的发现时，它们是什么样子。

Of the 34 reports published so far, 19 have been marked as not a vulnerability, 6 have been confirmed as low severity, and 9 are still awaiting assessment. That puts the false positive rate at 55.9% so far, and only 17.6% of findings have been confirmed, all of them low severity. That's a lot of work dumped on open source maintainers for a small handful of low-impact bugs, and when a maintainer has to pace reports just to publish them, that says plenty about the burden.

在迄今为止发布的 34 份报告中，19 份被标记为非漏洞，6 份被确认为低危，9 份仍在等待评估。这使得目前的误报率达到 55.9%，且只有 17.6% 的发现得到了确认，且全部为低危。对于少数几个低影响的漏洞，这给开源维护者带来了大量工作，当维护者不得不通过控制发布节奏来处理报告时，这充分说明了负担之重。

This also isn't new for curl. Back in May, Daniel wrote that five Mythos findings boiled down to a single confirmed vulnerability.

这对 curl 来说也不是什么新鲜事。早在 5 月份，Daniel 就写道，五个 Mythos 的发现最终只归结为一个已确认的漏洞。

It's a far cry from the 94.2% true positive rate Anthropic currently claims on its ledger. It also undercuts the "high- and critical-severity" framing Glasswing launched with. 这与 Anthropic 目前在其分类账上声称的 94.2% 的真实阳性率相去甚远。这也削弱了 Glasswing 推出时所宣称的“高危和严重”的框架。9 月份，我发现 Claude 将 91.5% 的分类账发现评为严重或高危，而维护者仅将 51.3% 的发现评为该级别。我很好奇其他开源项目是否也看到了这种情况。
