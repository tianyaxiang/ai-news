# The unfolding Jensen Huang scandal: a timeline

Nvidia co-founder CEO Jensen Huang has never been as flashy or controversy-prone as AI leaders like OpenAI’s Sam Altman or SpaceXAI’s Elon Musk. Now, the 63-year-old industry kingpin has admitted that he cheated on his wife of 40 years in an affair that could have ramifications beyond Huang’s personal life and potentially impact national security.

英伟达联合创始人兼首席执行官黄仁勋从未像 OpenAI 的萨姆·奥特曼或 SpaceXAI 的埃隆·马斯克那样张扬或充满争议。如今，这位 63 岁的行业巨头承认，他背叛了结婚 40 年的妻子，这段婚外情的影响可能不仅限于黄仁勋的个人生活，还可能危及国家安全。

United States officials believe that Huang’s likely romantic interest, Megaspeed executive Alice Huang, is a Chinese spy who has been feeding information back to Beijing. (Alice Huang, a Chinese citizen, is of no relation to Jensen Huang.) Megaspeed, a Singapore-based AI infrastructure company, had reportedly purchased $4.8 billion in Nvidia chips over the last two years.

美国官员认为，黄仁勋可能的恋人、Megaspeed 高管爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang）是一名中国间谍，一直在向北京输送情报。（爱丽丝·黄是中国公民，与黄仁勋并无亲属关系。）据报道，总部位于新加坡的人工智能基础设施公司 Megaspeed 在过去两年中购买了价值 48 亿美元的英伟达芯片。

The U.S. and Singapore have both previously investigated Megaspeed over suspicions that the firm was illegally sharing chips with China. The Justice Department has since taken over the federal investigation into the company, the Times has reported. The story took a sudden new turn on Wednesday.

美国和新加坡此前都曾因怀疑该公司非法向中国提供芯片而对 Megaspeed 展开调查。据《泰晤士报》报道，司法部此后已接手对该公司的联邦调查。周三，此事出现了突发转折。

Citing five people who worked at Nvidia, the Times reported that Huang also had an extramarital relationship with Kimberly Powell, the company’s vice president of health care, who reports directly to him. Nvidia, Huang and Powell have not confirmed the report. Reached by a Times reporter, Huang reportedly said, “I don’t think you should be talking to me directly,” before hanging up.

《泰晤士报》援引五名英伟达员工的话报道称，黄仁勋还与直接向他汇报工作的公司医疗保健副总裁金伯利·鲍威尔（Kimberly Powell）存在婚外情。英伟达、黄仁勋和鲍威尔均未证实该报道。据报道，当《泰晤士报》记者联系到黄仁勋时，他说：“我认为你不应该直接跟我谈，”随后挂断了电话。

Separately, the Wall Street Journal reported that Nvidia’s board is preparing to oust Huang, and Nvidia’s shares fell in after-hours trading. The U.S. and China are embroiled in a fierce technological arms race to build the most advanced AI infrastructure, which could shape each country’s economic future, military prowess, and capacity for innovation.

另外，《华尔街日报》报道称，英伟达董事会正准备罢免黄仁勋，英伟达股价在盘后交易中下跌。美中两国正陷入一场激烈的技术军备竞赛，旨在构建最先进的人工智能基础设施，这将影响两国的经济未来、军事实力和创新能力。

Nvidia is valuable in this competition as one of the world’s largest makers of AI chips, which are essential for accelerating AI development. But as an American company, Nvidia faces government restrictions on the sale of its most powerful chips to Chinese entities.
