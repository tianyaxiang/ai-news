Anduril Industries has announced the successful demonstration of a new satellite-based sensor designed to track other spacecraft that may be hiding their maneuvers in Earth’s shadow. Funded with Anduril’s internal capital, the company’s oven-sized satellite launched on a SpaceX rideshare mission in July with a package of tech demo payloads: an edge-compute processor, a power-management system adaptable to different mission types, and a long-wave infrared space sensor capable of detecting other objects in orbit.

Anduril Industries 宣布成功演示了一种新型卫星传感器，旨在追踪那些可能隐藏在地球阴影中进行机动的航天器。该卫星由 Anduril 自筹资金开发，大小如烤箱，于 7 月搭乘 SpaceX 的拼车任务发射升空，携带了一系列技术演示载荷：一个边缘计算处理器、一个适用于不同任务类型的电源管理系统，以及一个能够探测轨道上其他物体的长波红外空间传感器。

Anduril reported on the success of the tech demo mission Tuesday. The company’s satellite, named Anduril-216, flies about 590 km (366 mi) above Earth, spending about one-third of each orbit in darkness. Anduril-216’s night passes allow its infrared imager to shine. “This is a unique sensor that allows us to take imagery in long-wave [infrared],” said Gokul Subramanian, Anduril’s senior vice president of engineering. “What’s unique about that is it’s got a huge dynamic range. It has very low solar exclusion, so we’re blocked by the Sun very rarely, and we can see things in full dark.”

Anduril 周二报告了此次技术演示任务的成功。该公司名为 Anduril-216 的卫星在距离地球约 590 公里（366 英里）的高度飞行，每轨道约有三分之一的时间处于黑暗中。Anduril-216 在夜间的运行使其红外成像仪能够发挥作用。Anduril 工程高级副总裁 Gokul Subramanian 表示：“这是一种独特的传感器，使我们能够拍摄长波红外图像。它的独特之处在于拥有巨大的动态范围。它的太阳遮挡率极低，因此很少受到太阳干扰，我们可以在完全黑暗的环境中观察物体。”

Filling a gap: The US military and a growing number of commercial data providers use a network of radars and optical sensors on the ground and in space to monitor satellite traffic. Anduril acquired one of the commercial satellite-tracking companies, ExoAnalytic Solutions, earlier this year. But there’s a gap in optical tracking as satellites fly in Earth’s shadow, so space traffic monitors could temporarily lose track of a spacecraft if it performs a maneuver in orbital night.

填补空白：美国军方和越来越多的商业数据提供商利用地面和太空中的雷达及光学传感器网络来监控卫星交通。Anduril 今年早些时候收购了商业卫星追踪公司 ExoAnalytic Solutions 之一。但在卫星飞入地球阴影时，光学追踪存在盲区，因此如果航天器在轨道夜间进行机动，空间交通监控系统可能会暂时失去对它的追踪。

The Space Force is charged with tracking satellites for two primary reasons. First, military commanders want to know if an adversary moves its satellite in a threatening manner. Second, the Space Force issues traffic alerts to global satellite operators if there’s a risk of a collision. “Right now, we don’t have ground sensors in our adversaries’ sovereign lands, and then in the eclipse period where we are relying heavily on visible sensors, the satellites that are not illuminated by the Sun, we can’t see those,” said Adam Thurn, vice president and general manager of orbital systems at Anduril. “With the long-wave infrared space sensor, we’re looking to close that gap.”

太空军负责追踪卫星主要有两个原因。首先，军事指挥官需要了解对手是否以威胁方式移动其卫星。其次，如果存在碰撞风险，太空军会向全球卫星运营商发布交通警报。Anduril 轨道系统副总裁兼总经理 Adam Thurn 表示：“目前，我们在对手的主权领土内没有地面传感器，而在严重依赖可见光传感器的日食期间，我们无法看到未被太阳照亮的卫星。通过长波红外空间传感器，我们正寻求填补这一空白。”

Anduril’s long-wave infrared sensor works by detecting heat signatures from spacecraft, even in periods of eclipse. It provides a “unique capability for us to witness when an adversary or other resident space objects are maneuvering during those eclipse periods, where in the past we’ve been fairly blind,” Thurn said. In creating the infrared space sensor, engineers applied imaging technology Anduril uses in its autonomous weapons for use in space.

Anduril 的长波红外传感器通过探测航天器的热特征来工作，即使在日食期间也能实现。Thurn 说，这为我们提供了一种“独特的能力，让我们能够观察到对手或其他在轨空间物体在日食期间的机动情况，而过去我们对此几乎是盲目的”。在制造该红外空间传感器时，工程师们将 Anduril 用于自主武器的成像技术应用到了太空中。

“The long-wave infrared capability we have on orbit can do both unresolved and resolved imagery,” Thurn said, suggesting Anduril’s sensor could be used to inspect other satellites in orbit if it flies close enough. For now, Thurn said Anduril is focusing on optimizing its tracking sensor for low-Earth orbit, but it could be used in other orbits, and eventually in cislunar space near the Moon.

“我们在轨道上拥有的长波红外能力既可以进行未解析成像，也可以进行解析成像，”Thurn 说，这表明如果 Anduril 的传感器飞行距离足够近，还可以用于检查轨道上的其他卫星。目前，Thurn 表示 Anduril 正专注于优化其用于近地轨道的追踪传感器，但它也可以用于其他轨道，并最终用于月球附近的顺月空间。

Subramanian said Anduril has no plans to start its own satellite production line. Instead, Anduril foresees buying third-party satellite platforms and integrating them with sensor and compute modules to perform a range of different missions in space. “You’re seeing that today with Anduril-216, where we started with a simple mission, which was how do we track satellites, especially in these eclipse periods where the US currently does not have the ability to maintain custody,” Subramanian said. “And we designed a mission around that.”

Subramanian 表示，Anduril 没有计划建立自己的卫星生产线。相反，Anduril 计划购买第三方卫星平台，并将它们与传感器和计算模块集成，以在太空中执行一系列不同的任务。Subramanian 说：“你在今天的 Anduril-216 上就能看到这一点，我们从一个简单的任务开始，即我们如何追踪卫星，特别是在美国目前无法保持监视的日食期间。我们围绕这一点设计了任务。”

The compute module on Anduril-216 is designed to fuse data from different kinds of sensors, such as infrared or visible imagers, and generate tracking solutions on its own, without intervention or analysis from the ground. Subramanian said Anduril’s current focus is on acquiring space domain awareness data for sale to the US military, not selling entire satellites or sensors to the government. “At present, Anduril is not offering this system as a merchant supplier,” he said. “That could change in the future.”

Anduril-216 上的计算模块旨在融合来自不同类型传感器（如红外或可见光成像仪）的数据，并自主生成追踪方案，无需地面干预或分析。Subramanian 表示，Anduril 目前的重点是获取空间态势感知数据并出售给美国军方，而不是向政府出售整颗卫星或传感器。他说：“目前，Anduril 并没有以商业供应商的身份提供该系统。未来这种情况可能会改变。”
