计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.12932 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 6 日] 标题：URL-Sim：用于动态驾驶场景高保真重建的统一且受控的激光雷达仿真。作者：Kefei Qian, Xipeng Feng, Jie Li, Long Cao, Yuhan Chen, Xiangfei Huang, Guofa Li。

Abstract: Reconstructing high-fidelity LiDAR simulation scenes from real-world driving logs is essential for cost-effective safety validation of autonomous driving systems. While simulators based on Neural Radiance Fields (NeRF) achieve realistic scene reconstruction, their large-scale adoption is limited by slow rendering speeds. Recent 3D Gaussian splatting (3DGS) approaches have improved rendering efficiency through explicit primitive representation and differentiable rasterization.

摘要：从真实驾驶日志中重建高保真激光雷达仿真场景，对于自动驾驶系统的低成本安全验证至关重要。虽然基于神经辐射场（NeRF）的仿真器实现了逼真的场景重建，但其大规模应用受到渲染速度缓慢的限制。最近的 3D 高斯溅射（3DGS）方法通过显式基元表示和可微光栅化提高了渲染效率。

However, existing 3DGS-based methods remain largely restricted to rigid vehicles and produce redundant primitives through unconstrained densification. To address these limitations, we propose URL-Sim, a unified and regulated dynamic LiDAR simulation framework. URL-Sim constructs a dynamic Gaussian scene graph that decouples the modeling of traffic participants, including vehicles, pedestrians, and cyclists, enabling accurate reconstruction of heterogeneous dynamic objects within a single framework.

然而，现有的基于 3DGS 的方法大多局限于刚性车辆，并通过无约束的致密化产生冗余基元。为了解决这些局限性，我们提出了 URL-Sim，这是一个统一且受控的动态激光雷达仿真框架。URL-Sim 构建了一个动态高斯场景图，将交通参与者（包括车辆、行人和骑行者）的建模解耦，从而能够在单一框架内实现异构动态物体的精确重建。

It further introduces a regulated Gaussian densification strategy that ranks primitives with multi-dimensional importance scores and allocates Gaussians to the most critical regions while keeping the total primitive count bounded. Experiments on the Waymo and KITTI-360 datasets demonstrate that URL-Sim achieves higher quantitative accuracy and visual quality than existing baselines under comparable primitive budgets, and accurately recovers the geometry and poses of diverse dynamic traffic participants.

它进一步引入了一种受控的高斯致密化策略，通过多维重要性评分对基元进行排序，并将高斯分布分配给最关键的区域，同时保持总基元数量在限制范围内。在 Waymo 和 KITTI-360 数据集上的实验表明，在相当的基元预算下，URL-Sim 比现有的基准方法实现了更高的定量精度和视觉质量，并能准确恢复各种动态交通参与者的几何形状和姿态。
