Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.12927 (cs) [Submitted on 3 Nov 2026] Title: An ensemble of graph generative models for assisted history matching of the Brugge field case Authors: Gleb Shishaev, Anton Shirokov, Shadfar Davoodi, Mohammed Al-Shargabi, Vasily Demyanov, Daniel Arnold.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.12927 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 3 日] 标题：用于 Brugge 油田案例辅助历史拟合的图生成模型集成。作者：Gleb Shishaev, Anton Shirokov, Shadfar Davoodi, Mohammed Al-Shargabi, Vasily Demyanov, Daniel Arnold。

Abstract: Assisted history matching remains non-unique because many geological realisations can reproduce equivalent production responses with substantial differences between individual well matches. This ambiguity increases when a field comprises several depositional settings and modelling strategies.

摘要：辅助历史拟合仍然具有非唯一性，因为许多地质实现可以重现等效的生产响应，但在单个油井拟合之间存在显著差异。当油田包含多种沉积环境和建模策略时，这种模糊性会增加。

The paper applied a graph autoencoder approach to history matching of a benchmark Brugge field that comprises different depositional zones. Each stratigraphic zone is modelled with its dedicated generative model that comprises a joint latent space for optimisation history matching loops.

本文将图自编码器方法应用于包含不同沉积带的基准 Brugge 油田的历史拟合。每个地层带都由其专用的生成模型进行建模，该模型包含用于优化历史拟合循环的联合潜在空间。

Graph-based generators operate directly on the active cells of the corner-point grid, so its irregular geometry is handled without conversion to a regular lattice. The optimisation search in the 160-dimensional joint latent space is done with the Covariance Matrix Adaptation Evolution Strategy.

基于图的生成器直接在角点网格的活动单元上运行，因此无需转换为规则晶格即可处理其不规则几何形状。在 160 维联合潜在空间中的优化搜索是通过协方差矩阵自适应进化策略完成的。

The multi-component objective function includes static well data mismatch, dynamic production mismatch, and a geological realism penalty based on the Decoder induced latent metric. Latent space diagnostics with topological data analysis are applied prior to history matching to test whether one generator is sufficient or whether a multiple generator formulation is required.

多组件目标函数包括静态井数据失配、动态生产失配以及基于解码器诱导潜在度量的地质真实性惩罚。在历史拟合之前，应用基于拓扑数据分析的潜在空间诊断，以测试单个生成器是否足够，或者是否需要多生成器公式。

The results show that the zone-based formulation gives coherent latent spaces, preserves the main porosity and permeability relations that vary from one zone to another, and supports history matching while keeping the solutions within the learned geological manifold.

结果表明，基于区域的公式提供了连贯的潜在空间，保留了随区域变化的主要孔隙度和渗透率关系，并在将解决方案保持在学习到的地质流形内的同时支持历史拟合。

The matched models reproduce field oil and water production within the prior uncertainty range and provide a comparable well-level match to previously published Brugge studies based on Ensemble Kalman Filter, Particle Swarm Optimisation with principal component analysis of parameterisation, and Multiple Kernel Learning with Particle Swarm Optimisation.

拟合模型在先验不确定性范围内重现了油田的油水产量，并提供了与之前发表的 Brugge 研究相当的井级拟合，这些研究基于集合卡尔曼滤波、参数化主成分分析的粒子群优化以及粒子群优化的多核学习。
