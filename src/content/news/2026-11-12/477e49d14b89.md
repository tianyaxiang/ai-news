Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.12918 (cs) [Submitted on 27 Oct 2026] Title: IntentTwin: Deconfounding Candidate Support and the Oracle-to-Deployment Gap in Offline Web Behavior Prediction Authors: Miaobo Hu, Shuhao Hu, Xiaobo Guo, Xin Wang, Bokun Wang, Daren Zha.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.12918 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 10 月 27 日] 标题：IntentTwin：离线网页行为预测中候选支持的去混淆与预言机到部署的差距 作者：Miaobo Hu, Shuhao Hu, Xiaobo Guo, Xin Wang, Bokun Wang, Daren Zha。

Abstract: Offline web-behavior benchmarks frequently insert the demonstrated action into a finite candidate set and then measure reranking. We show that this protocol can create a candidate-support confound: when the target is outside training support while all distractors are sampled from that support, support membership identifies the gold action without modeling behavior.

摘要：离线网页行为基准测试经常将演示的操作插入到一个有限的候选集中，然后测量重排序效果。我们证明这种协议会产生候选支持混淆：当目标处于训练支持范围之外，而所有干扰项都从该支持范围内采样时，支持成员身份无需建模行为即可识别出黄金操作。

We formalize this effect with support-identifiability rate (SIR), construct support-balanced candidate sets, and evaluate a target-free dual-path proposer that combines support retrieval with open-vocabulary DOM/LM proposals. On filtered OPeRA (4,282/469/992 train/dev/test steps), the original protocol has SIR 1.0000 on target-OOV rows and a support-only rule reaches 1.0000 OOV Exact@1.

我们通过支持可识别率 (SIR) 将这种效应形式化，构建了支持平衡的候选集，并评估了一种结合了支持检索与开放词汇 DOM/LM 提议的无目标双路径提议器。在过滤后的 OPeRA（4,282/469/992 训练/开发/测试步骤）上，原始协议在目标 OOV 行上的 SIR 为 1.0000，且仅基于支持的规则在 OOV Exact@1 上达到了 1.0000。

Support balancing reduces SIR to 0.0000; the strongest conditional reranker obtains 0.7214 overall Exact@1, 0.7988 on IV rows, and 0.5668 on OOV rows. The target-free dual-path system reaches Proposal Recall@16 0.6915 and end-to-end Exact@1 0.5212, compared with oracle conditional Exact@1 0.7450.

支持平衡将 SIR 降低至 0.0000；最强的条件重排序器在整体 Exact@1 上获得 0.7214，在 IV 行上获得 0.7988，在 OOV 行上获得 0.5668。无目标双路径系统达到了 0.6915 的 Proposal Recall@16 和 0.5212 的端到端 Exact@1，相比之下，预言机条件 Exact@1 为 0.7450。

On Mind2Web, the same audit yields SIR 0.2380 and an oracle-to-proposal gap of 0.2125. A 200-row semantic audit labels 0.7150 of exact-key OOV targets as semantically novel, 0.2450 as locator/string variation, and 0.0400 as ambiguous (Cohen's $\kappa=0.8173$). These measurements show why support composition must be controlled alongside candidate accuracy when offline results are used to reason about deployment.

在 Mind2Web 上，同样的审计得出的 SIR 为 0.2380，预言机到提议的差距为 0.2125。一项 200 行的语义审计将 0.7150 的精确键 OOV 目标标记为语义新颖，0.2450 标记为定位器/字符串变体，0.0400 标记为歧义（Cohen's $\kappa=0.8173$）。这些测量结果表明，当使用离线结果来推断部署情况时，为什么必须在控制候选准确性的同时控制支持构成。
