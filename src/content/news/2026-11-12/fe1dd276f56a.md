Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.12988 (cs) [Submitted on 8 Nov 2026] Title: Third Eye: Beyond Immediate Gains in Recursive Self-Improvement Authors: Sujato Dutta, Padala Indira Sneha, Kypu Mahitha, Shashank Vanga, Neeraj Choudhary.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.12988 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 8 日] 标题：《第三只眼：递归自我改进中超越即时收益的探索》 作者：Sujato Dutta, Padala Indira Sneha, Kypu Mahitha, Shashank Vanga, Neeraj Choudhary。

Abstract: Does a useful self-update remain useful after the model updates again? Third Eye separates immediate performance from the outcome of a further native update. We evaluate five 3-8B backbones on math and code: 64 fresh runs, three candidates per run, and three continuations per candidate. Selection and final scoring use disjoint items.

摘要：一次有用的自我更新在模型再次更新后是否依然有用？“第三只眼”（Third Eye）将即时性能与后续原生更新的结果分离开来。我们在数学和代码任务上评估了五个 3-8B 参数的主干模型：进行了 64 次全新运行，每次运行产生三个候选模型，每个候选模型进行三次延续更新。选择过程和最终评分使用不相交的数据项。

In the balanced Qwen3-4B/Llama-3.2-3B comparison, immediate-selected utility is near zero, but continuation reduces it by 1.78 percentage points (simultaneous 95% interval [0.68, 2.88]). Candidate-averaged immediate utility is already negative: selection partly accounts for the apparently neutral first step.

在平衡的 Qwen3-4B/Llama-3.2-3B 对比中，即时选择的效用接近于零，但延续更新使其降低了 1.78 个百分点（同时 95% 置信区间为 [0.68, 2.88]）。候选模型平均后的即时效用已经是负值：选择过程在一定程度上解释了第一步看似中性的结果。

Continuation also lowers utility for SmolLM3-3B and Llama-3.1-8B; Qwen3-8B remains inconclusive. Target and out-of-distribution losses dominate retention changes. Matched recipe and recipient-donor controls identify neither a reliable mitigation nor a single mechanism.

延续更新同样降低了 SmolLM3-3B 和 Llama-3.1-8B 的效用；Qwen3-8B 的结果尚无定论。目标损失和分布外损失主导了保留率的变化。匹配的配方以及接收者-捐赠者对照实验既没有确定可靠的缓解措施，也没有发现单一的机制。

The results support evaluating persistent self-updates as adaptation transitions, with independent scoring and a no-update baseline, rather than interpreting immediate performance as their full value.

研究结果支持将持续的自我更新评估为适应性转换，并应采用独立评分和无更新基准，而不是将即时性能解读为其全部价值。
