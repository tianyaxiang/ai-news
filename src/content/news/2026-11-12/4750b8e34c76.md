# Meta is losing its longtime head of infrastructure

In Brief Posted: 12:12 PM PST · November 11, 2026 Image Credits:Marcin Golba/NurPhoto / Getty Images Lucas Ropek Meta is losing its longtime head of infrastructure A key figure in Meta’s growth into a social media and tech industry powerhouse is leaving the company.

简讯 发布于：太平洋标准时间 2026 年 11 月 11 日中午 12:12 图片来源：Marcin Golba/NurPhoto / Getty Images Lucas Ropek Meta 正在失去其长期担任基础设施负责人的高管。作为 Meta 成长为社交媒体和科技行业巨头的关键人物，他即将离开公司。

Santosh Janardhan, the company’s longtime head of infrastructure and co-head of engineering, will be stepping down, Meta told TechCrunch. He will be replaced by Dave Brown, who joined the company as vice president of infrastructure earlier this year, the company added.

Meta 向 TechCrunch 表示，该公司长期担任基础设施负责人兼工程联席主管的 Santosh Janardhan 即将卸任。公司补充称，他将由 Dave Brown 接替，后者于今年早些时候加入公司担任基础设施副总裁。

Previous to coming aboard at Meta, Brown spent 19 years at Amazon, where he focused on compute, cloud, and AI, his LinkedIn shows. It sounds like Janardhan will remain at Meta for a little bit, although he is expected to leave next year.

据其领英资料显示，在加入 Meta 之前，Brown 在亚马逊工作了 19 年，专注于计算、云和人工智能领域。听起来 Janardhan 还会在 Meta 短暂留任，尽管预计他将于明年离职。

“Santosh will stay on for transition and he is moving into an advisory role on Meta Compute,” said a Meta representative. Janardhan, who originally joined the company in 2009, helped the firm grow into the massive internet presence that it is today.

一位 Meta 发言人表示：“Santosh 将留任以进行过渡，并转任 Meta Compute 的顾问角色。”Janardhan 于 2009 年加入该公司，曾助力公司成长为如今庞大的互联网巨头。

Bloomberg originally reported Janardhan’s departure, citing an internal memo that had recently been circulated. It’s unclear why Janardhan is stepping down from the role. The memo did not specify the reasons for his departure and Meta didn’t immediately respond to a request for clarity on the issue.

彭博社最初报道了 Janardhan 的离职消息，并援引了一份近期流传的内部备忘录。目前尚不清楚 Janardhan 卸任的原因。该备忘录未说明他离职的具体理由，Meta 也未立即回应关于此事的澄清请求。

However, Janardhan’s memo apparently cited the need to “pass the baton.” In recent years, the company’s investment in infrastructure has exploded, as it seeks to keep up with its competitors in the AI race.

然而，Janardhan 的备忘录显然提到了需要“交接棒”。近年来，随着公司寻求在人工智能竞赛中跟上竞争对手的步伐，其在基础设施方面的投资呈爆炸式增长。

Earlier this year, Janardhan was tasked with leading Meta Compute, the company initiative to scale its data center investments and strategy.

今年早些时候，Janardhan 被委以领导 Meta Compute 的重任，这是一项旨在扩大公司数据中心投资和战略的计划。
