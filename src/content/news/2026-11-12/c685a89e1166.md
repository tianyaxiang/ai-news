IBM’s Quantum Developer’s Conference is being held this week, and the company is using the gathering to provide an update to its roadmap: It is aiming to make error-corrected quantum computers before the decade is out. The changes greatly expand the scope of its next-generation Nighthawk general-use processors by adding a version with over 500 programmable qubits. At the same time, it’s stepping back a bit on its plan to link multiple Nighthawk processors together, dropping the intended number of linked systems from nine to two.

IBM 量子开发者大会于本周举行，该公司正借此机会更新其发展路线图：目标是在本十年结束前制造出具备纠错功能的量子计算机。此次调整大幅扩展了其下一代 Nighthawk 通用处理器的范围，增加了一个拥有超过 500 个可编程量子比特的版本。与此同时，该公司在连接多个 Nighthawk 处理器的计划上有所退缩，将原计划连接的系统数量从九个减少到了两个。

The total number of qubits planned for next year will remain the same; how the company is getting there, however, will be different. Meanwhile, the company is touting the performance boost achieved via a major change that was made in the generation of Nighthawk processors released in September: hardware-based qubit resets.

明年计划的量子比特总数保持不变，但公司实现这一目标的方式将会发生变化。与此同时，该公司正在宣传九月份发布的 Nighthawk 处理器版本中实现的一项重大改进所带来的性能提升：基于硬件的量子比特重置。

IBM’s most recent roadmap to error-corrected quantum computing has two parallel tracks. One is focused on technical development, with the company testing technologies it will need to build error-corrected hardware, but that may not be useful outside of that context. The other is public-facing, focused on delivering high-performance processors for people interested in testing algorithms on quantum systems today.

IBM 最新的纠错量子计算路线图包含两条并行轨道。一条专注于技术开发，公司正在测试构建纠错硬件所需的技术，但这些技术在相关背景之外可能并不适用。另一条则面向公众，专注于为那些有兴趣在当前量子系统上测试算法的用户提供高性能处理器。

The plan for Nighthawk r3 in 2027 is apparently to retain that performance-enhancing technology and combine it with other hardware improvements to further boost the number of operations that can be performed without errors becoming too likely. It will also significantly increase the qubit count, with a target of 520 programmable qubits.

2027 年 Nighthawk r3 的计划显然是保留这种性能增强技术，并将其与其他硬件改进相结合，以进一步提高在错误发生概率过高之前所能执行的操作数量。它还将显著增加量子比特数量，目标是达到 520 个可编程量子比特。

The larger size will keep IBM on its roadmap—sort of. Its existing roadmap promised users access to a single system with 1080 qubits. But it was supposed to get there by linking nine 120-qubit Nighthawk chips. Now, the company plans to offer a 1040-qubit machine (which is close to 1080) but will do so by linking just two chips.

更大的规模将使 IBM 勉强维持在其路线图上。其现有的路线图承诺用户可以使用一个拥有 1080 个量子比特的系统，但原本计划通过连接九个 120 量子比特的 Nighthawk 芯片来实现。现在，该公司计划提供一台 1040 量子比特的机器（接近 1080），但将通过仅连接两个芯片来实现。
