计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.12936 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 6 日] 标题：LandslideFM：迈向统一滑坡感知与理解的基础模型 作者：Shanwen Wang, Sirui Wang, Xin Sun, Jungong Han。

Abstract: Intelligent landslide detection is essential for disaster prevention. However, existing models primarily focus on isolated visual tasks, require extensive labeled data, and lack deep analytical and scene-understanding capabilities.

摘要：智能滑坡检测对于防灾至关重要。然而，现有模型主要集中于孤立的视觉任务，需要大量的标注数据，且缺乏深度的分析和场景理解能力。

To address these limitations, we propose LandslideFM, the first unified foundation model that connects visual perception tasks with contextual interpretation of landslides. Specifically, LandslideFM adapts to multiple visual tasks via a Source-Agreement Graduated Trust semi-supervised learning framework that leverages limited labeled data alongside abundant unlabeled data.

为了解决这些局限性，我们提出了 LandslideFM，这是第一个将视觉感知任务与滑坡背景解释相结合的统一基础模型。具体而言，LandslideFM 通过一种“源一致性渐进信任”（Source-Agreement Graduated Trust）半监督学习框架适应多种视觉任务，该框架利用有限的标注数据和大量的未标注数据。

Furthermore, predicted landslide regions provide explicit spatial guidance to a Vision-Language Model adapted through parameter-efficient fine-tuning, enabling comprehensive landslide reasoning. To support training and evaluation in this domain, we also present LandslideVL, the first benchmark for intelligent landslide analysis, combining visual imagery and newly introduced textual prompts.

此外，预测的滑坡区域为通过参数高效微调适配的视觉-语言模型提供了明确的空间引导，从而实现了全面的滑坡推理。为了支持该领域的训练和评估，我们还提出了 LandslideVL，这是第一个结合了视觉图像和新引入文本提示的智能滑坡分析基准。

Comprehensive experiments across public datasets and LandslideVL demonstrate that LandslideFM achieves superior quantitative and qualitative performance. Most importantly, LandslideFM surpasses general-purpose VLMs in specialized landslide reasoning and scene analysis. Our code is released at this https URL.

在公共数据集和 LandslideVL 上进行的全面实验表明，LandslideFM 在定量和定性方面均取得了优异的性能。最重要的是，LandslideFM 在专业的滑坡推理和场景分析方面超越了通用视觉-语言模型（VLM）。我们的代码已在以下网址发布。
