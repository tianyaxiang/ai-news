Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.12941 (cs) [Submitted on 6 Nov 2026] Title: AI Governance Should Prioritize Control and Knowledge Boundaries Over Limiting Intelligence Authors: Hendrik Luuk.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.12941 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 6 日] 标题：人工智能治理应优先考虑控制和知识边界，而非限制智能 作者：Hendrik Luuk。

Abstract: The rapid proliferation of autonomously acting AI agents makes the governance of agentic systems urgent. We propose the competence circuit, summarized as IKC + KC + C, that decomposes an agent's real-world impact into three pathways: intelligence-mediated discovery, procedural know-how, and direct control.

摘要：自主行动人工智能代理的迅速激增使得代理系统的治理变得刻不容缓。我们提出了“能力回路”（competence circuit），概括为 IKC + KC + C，它将代理对现实世界的影响分解为三个路径：智能中介发现、程序性技术诀窍以及直接控制。

Using the canonical fast-takeoff debate as a stress test, we formalize the conditions for intelligence-mediated fast takeoff as a conjunction jointly sufficient for fast catastrophic supremacy, showing that most rely on rapid knowledge acquisition or control attainment rather than intelligence alone.

我们以经典的“快速起飞”（fast-takeoff）辩论作为压力测试，将智能中介的快速起飞条件形式化为导致快速灾难性霸权的联合充分条件，并表明大多数情况依赖于快速的知识获取或控制获取，而非仅仅依赖于智能本身。

We operationalize epistemic intelligence as hypothesis-selection efficiency and validate this framework via stochastic simulation on NK fitness landscapes across six scenarios spanning the conjunctive chain. An analytic bound supported by the simulations shows that intelligence-driven speedup is capped by the ratio of competing intelligence levels regardless of task difficulty.

我们将认知智能操作化为假设选择效率，并通过在跨越联合链的六种场景下的 NK 适应度景观进行随机模拟来验证该框架。模拟支持的分析边界表明，无论任务难度如何，智能驱动的加速都受到竞争智能水平比率的限制。

The simulations also expose a fast-takeoff dilemma: a large intelligence-driven speedup multiplier materializes only when the hypothesis space is noisy, but the absolute timescale remains constrained by the time required for capability-critical tests, so a large multiplier need not imply rapid progress.

模拟还揭示了一个“快速起飞困境”：只有在假设空间存在噪声时，才会出现巨大的智能驱动加速乘数，但绝对时间尺度仍受限于能力关键测试所需的时间，因此巨大的乘数并不一定意味着快速进展。

Simulations also indicate that institutional chokepoints disproportionately slow down superintelligent agents when mandatory authorization delays are large relative to the negligible time these agents spend on unproductive search. We argue that the dominant safety levers are restricting control and enforcing knowledge boundaries rather than limiting intelligence alone, and this applies to agentic systems at any capability level.

模拟还表明，当强制授权延迟相对于这些代理在非生产性搜索上花费的微不足道的时间而言较大时，制度瓶颈会不成比例地减慢超智能代理的速度。我们认为，主要的安全性杠杆是限制控制和强制执行知识边界，而不是仅仅限制智能，这适用于任何能力水平的代理系统。

Purely digital domains weaken these bottlenecks, strengthening the case for architectures that intentionally "put physics in the loop."

纯数字领域削弱了这些瓶颈，从而加强了支持那些有意“将物理学纳入回路”的架构的理由。
