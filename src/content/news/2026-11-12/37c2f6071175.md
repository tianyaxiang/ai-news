It’s been one week since Election Day, and Pete Hegseth is facing a different kind of war. Trump administration officials tell Inner Loop they knew the defense secretary would face a slew of investigations in the next Congress. Now, they think he is on track to be Democrats’ number one target—and Democrats are already planning to make their case as airtight as possible.

距离选举日已经过去一周，皮特·赫格塞斯（Pete Hegseth）正面临一场不同寻常的战争。特朗普政府官员告诉《Inner Loop》，他们早就知道这位国防部长将在下一届国会中面临一系列调查。现在，他们认为他正成为民主党人的头号目标，而民主党人也正在筹划如何让他们的指控尽可能无懈可击。

“Hegseth is now a passenger in whatever comes next,” says one source close to President Donald Trump. “And all the outcomes are potentially damaging.” House Democratic aides tell Inner Loop that Democrats are likely to pursue an impeachment inquiry against Hegseth, and also work to narrow the charges against him to issues that Senate Republicans have also complained about, in an effort to attract their support.

“赫格塞斯现在只能听天由命了，”一位接近唐纳德·特朗普总统的消息人士表示，“而且所有可能的结果都具有破坏性。”众议院民主党助手告诉《Inner Loop》，民主党人很可能会对赫格塞斯展开弹劾调查，并努力将针对他的指控缩小到参议院共和党人也曾抱怨过的问题上，以此争取他们的支持。

Hegseth, who has championed the deeply unpopular and costly Iran war, has spent much of his controversial tenure upending various internal processes at the Defense Department, including firing generals and blocking promotions using criteria that disproportionately affected women and people of color. He has also become known for his notorious role in Signalgate and for military strikes against purported drug traffickers in the Caribbean and Pacific Ocean.

赫格塞斯曾力推那场极不受欢迎且代价高昂的伊朗战争。在他充满争议的任期内，他花费了大量时间颠覆国防部的各项内部流程，包括解雇将军，以及利用对女性和有色人种影响不成比例的标准来阻挠晋升。他还因在“信号门”（Signalgate）中的恶名以及在加勒比海和太平洋地区针对所谓毒贩的军事打击而广为人知。

Some of those topics have also previously been the subject of complaints from Republicans. The Senate Armed Services Committee chair Roger Wicker criticized Hegseth for the flag officer cuts and once reportedly threatened to block a Trump nominee unless the Pentagon handed over documents.

其中一些议题此前也曾遭到共和党人的投诉。参议院军事委员会主席罗杰·威克（Roger Wicker）曾批评赫格塞斯削减将官职位的做法，据报道还曾威胁称，除非五角大楼交出相关文件，否则将阻挠特朗普的一项提名。

Last night, House Republicans managed to table an impeachment effort targeting Hegseth that was brought about by Representative Thomas Massie, a Republican from Kentucky. Massie, among other issues, accused Hegseth of waging the Iran war without approval from Congress and directing “extrajudicial killings.” But even though the effort failed, officials privately believe it is practically inevitable that Hegseth will be impeached again and face a Senate trial next year.

昨晚，众议院共和党人成功搁置了一项针对赫格塞斯的弹劾动议，该动议由肯塔基州共和党众议员托马斯·马西（Thomas Massie）提出。马西指控赫格塞斯在未经国会批准的情况下发动伊朗战争，并指挥了“法外处决”等问题。尽管此次尝试失败了，但官员们私下认为，赫格塞斯明年再次被弹劾并面临参议院审判几乎是不可避免的。

House Democratic leader Hakeem Jeffries suggested as much after the impeachment failed to progress, telling reporters he would consult with his top members on next steps. “We’ll have that conversation at the appropriate time, and that’ll be led by Adam Smith, soon to be chair of the Armed Services Committee, and Jamie Raskin, next chair of the judiciary,” Jeffries said.

在弹劾未能推进后，众议院民主党领袖哈基姆·杰弗里斯（Hakeem Jeffries）暗示了这一点，他告诉记者，他将与核心成员商讨后续步骤。杰弗里斯说：“我们会在适当的时候进行讨论，并将由即将担任军事委员会主席的亚当·史密斯（Adam Smith）和下一任司法委员会主席杰米·拉斯金（Jamie Raskin）牵头。”

Trump officials tell Inner Loop they still don’t expect Democrats to get enough GOP support to remove Hegseth—but they can’t shield him from wall-to-wall coverage of a trial. The impeachment articles brought by Massie were seen by some Republicans as overly broad, which made officials think they could spare Hegseth an impeachment trial by having Senate majority leader John Thune dismiss all the charges in a straight-line vote.

特朗普政府官员告诉《Inner Loop》，他们仍不认为民主党能获得足够的共和党支持来罢免赫格塞斯，但他们无法让他免受审判期间铺天盖地的媒体报道的影响。马西提出的弹劾条款被一些共和党人认为过于宽泛，这让官员们认为，他们可以通过让参议院多数党领袖约翰·图恩（John Thune）通过直接投票驳回所有指控，从而使赫格塞斯免于弹劾审判。

But with Democrats taking over the Senate next year, that option is off the table. It’s not going to be entirely simple, however. Hegseth’s biggest Republican detractors are all leaving Congress at the end of the year. Maine senator Susan Collins, North Carolina senator Thom Tillis, and Louisiana senator Bill Cassidy all lost reelection this midterm cycle, while Iowa senator Joni Ernst and Kentucky senator Mitch McConnell are stepping down after finishing their terms.

但随着民主党明年接管参议院，这一选项已不复存在。然而，事情并不会那么简单。赫格塞斯党内最大的批评者都将在年底离开国会。缅因州参议员苏珊·柯林斯（Susan Collins）、北卡罗来纳州参议员汤姆·提利斯（Thom Tillis）和路易斯安那州参议员比尔·卡西迪（Bill Cassidy）都在本届中期选举中连任失败，而爱荷华州参议员乔尼·恩斯特（Joni Ernst）和肯塔基州参议员米奇·麦康奈尔（Mitch McConnell）在任期结束后也将卸任。

But even with his biggest detractors set to retire, at least five other Republican senators have complained about Hegseth’s performance—getting Democrats closer to the 67 votes needed to convict—like Wicker, South Dakota senator Mike Rounds, Kentucky senator Rand Paul, Alaska senator Lisa Murkowski, and Indiana senator Todd Young. This is why the focus of the Democratic impeachment inquiry is going to have to be specific, and geared to Republican interests, so as to be unassailable.

即便他最大的批评者即将退休，至少还有五位共和党参议员曾对赫格塞斯的表现表示不满，这让民主党人离定罪所需的67票更近了一步。这些人包括威克、南达科他州参议员迈克·朗兹（Mike Rounds）、肯塔基州参议员兰德·保罗（Rand Paul）、阿拉斯加州参议员丽莎·穆尔科斯基（Lisa Murkowski）和印第安纳州参议员托德·扬（Todd Young）。这就是为什么民主党的弹劾调查重点必须具体，并针对共和党的利益，以确保其无懈可击。

Hegseth, for what it’s worth, doesn’t seem to want to go anywhere without a fight. In a televised appearance at the Pentagon earlier this week, Hegseth responded to a question about Vice President JD Vance blaming the Republican midterm losses on Iran, and said Vance was a “critical member of the team” and the war effort. Hegseth then added, “I agree with what the president said, when he said ‘Hey, if I had to do this all over again, would we do it again? Absolutely.’”

无论如何，赫格塞斯似乎并不打算不战而退。本周早些时候在五角大楼的一次电视露面中，赫格塞斯回应了关于副总统JD·万斯（JD Vance）将共和党中期选举失利归咎于伊朗的问题，称万斯是“团队和战争努力的关键成员”。赫格塞斯随后补充道：“我同意总统所说的，当他说‘嘿，如果我必须重来一次，我们还会这样做吗？绝对会。’”

Tech (Heart) Troubles: House Republicans are poised to open an investigation into Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang's interactions with alleged Chinese spy and one-time tech executive Alice Huang, who is not related to the CEO. The growing push for Congress to stand up its own investigation is set to put Republicans on a collision course with Trump, who has continued to publicly defend the CEO of the world's most valuable chip company.

科技（核心）麻烦：众议院共和党人准备对英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）与涉嫌为中国间谍的前科技高管爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang，与黄仁勋无亲属关系）之间的互动展开调查。国会内部要求启动独立调查的呼声日益高涨，这使共和党人与特朗普产生了冲突，后者一直公开为这家全球市值最高芯片公司的首席执行官辩护。

During a congressional “Gang of Eight” briefing on Monday, officials including director of national intelligence Jay Clayton and CIA director John Ratcliffe gave only the broad brushstrokes of the classified intelligence surrounding Huang’s interactions, which left lawmakers deeply unsatisfied, sources say. Meeting attendees did not get clear answers as to why there was no defensive briefing provided to Jensen Huang and whether he was initially left off Trump's trip to China this year as a result of the possible national security risk.

据消息人士称，在周一的国会“八人帮”简报会上，包括国家情报总监杰伊·克莱顿（Jay Clayton）和中央情报局局长约翰·拉特克利夫（John Ratcliffe）在内的官员仅对围绕黄仁勋互动情况的机密情报进行了粗略概述，这让议员们深感不满。与会者没有得到明确的答案，即为什么没有向黄仁勋提供防御性简报，以及他今年是否因为潜在的国家安全风险而被排除在特朗普的中国之行名单之外。

On Tuesday, the chairman of the House Foreign Affairs Committee, Brian Mast, told reporters he would be open to issuing a subpoena to compel Jensen Huang to provide a briefing behind closed doors if he ignored a request from last week to appear on Capitol Hill. Mast, together with Rick Crawford, the chair of the House Intelligence Committee, and John Moolenaar, the chair of the China select subcommittee, had asked Jensen Huang to appear before the three panels later this month.

周二，众议院外交事务委员会主席布莱恩·马斯特（Brian Mast）告诉记者，如果黄仁勋无视上周提出的前往国会山作证的要求，他愿意发出传票，强制黄仁勋进行闭门简报。马斯特与众议院情报委员会主席里克·克劳福德（Rick Crawford）以及中国问题特别小组委员会主席约翰·穆勒纳尔（John Moolenaar）此前已要求黄仁勋在本月晚些时候出席这三个委员会的听证会。

The congressional inquiry follows revelations from The New York Times that secretary of state Marco Rubio drafted a memo for Trump warning that classified intelligence suggested Alice Huang, an executive at Megaspeed, had been recruited to spy by China’s top intelligence agency, the Minis.

此次国会调查源于《纽约时报》披露的消息，称国务卿马可·鲁比奥（Marco Rubio）曾为特朗普起草了一份备忘录，警告称机密情报显示，Megaspeed公司高管爱丽丝·黄已被中国最高情报机构招募为间谍。
