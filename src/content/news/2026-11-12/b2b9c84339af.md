Toyota is reviving the Scion badge as an experimental mobility incubator—starting with an Indiana-built, 25-mph kei truck engineered for American fleets.

丰田正在复兴 Scion 品牌，将其定位为实验性移动出行孵化器，首款产品是一款在印第安纳州制造、时速限制为 25 英里的轻型货车（kei truck），专为美国车队设计。

Toyota is dusting off the Scion badge for a radically different mission. Rather than targeting youth-market hatchbacks, “Scion by Toyota” is being positioned as an experimental mobility incubator — and its first vehicle is an authentic Japanese-style kei truck built in Indiana.

丰田正在为 Scion 品牌赋予一项截然不同的使命。与以往针对年轻市场的掀背车不同，“Scion by Toyota”现在被定位为实验性移动出行孵化器，其首款车型是一款在印第安纳州制造的纯正日式轻型货车。

Kei vehicles, which are built to satisfy Japan’s light vehicle regulations, are enjoying surging popularity around the world, including here in the US. More than 30,000 used kei trucks have been imported into the US since 2020, with roughly 7,500 units entering the country each year.

轻型车（Kei vehicles）是为满足日本轻型汽车法规而制造的，目前在全球范围内人气飙升，在美国也不例外。自 2020 年以来，已有超过 3 万辆二手轻型货车被进口到美国，每年约有 7,500 辆进入该国。

Toyota looks to be the first to test the American market’s appetite for the tiny trucks. The choice to use the Scion brand, which was discontinued in 2016, would seem to indicate that the automaker is hoping to appeal to younger car buyers.

丰田似乎是第一家测试美国市场对这种小型货车需求的车企。选择使用 2016 年停产的 Scion 品牌，似乎表明该汽车制造商希望吸引更年轻的购车群体。

When it lands in the second half of 2027, Scion’s KEI-01 sports a 660cc three-cylinder gas engine paired with a continuously variable transmission. It won’t win any drag races, but it should deliver enough power for stop-and-go driving while using a minimum amount of fuel.

Scion KEI-01 将于 2027 年下半年上市，配备 660cc 三缸汽油发动机和无级变速器。它虽然无法在直线加速赛中获胜，但足以满足走走停停的驾驶需求，同时能将油耗降至最低。

At roughly 11 feet long and under 5 feet wide, the KEI-01 is about half the footprint of a full-size pickup. It can easily thread through cramped alleys, narrow barn aisles, garden gates, or busy campus pathways where standard trucks can’t fit.

KEI-01 车长约 11 英尺，宽不足 5 英尺，占地面积仅为全尺寸皮卡的一半左右。它能轻松穿梭于狭窄的小巷、谷仓通道、花园大门或繁忙的校园道路，而这些地方是标准卡车无法进入的。

Because it is certified under federal Low-Speed Vehicle (LSV) rules — also used for golf carts and golf cart-adjacent vehicles — its top speed is strictly capped at 25 mph.

由于该车根据联邦低速车辆（LSV）规则进行认证（该规则也适用于高尔夫球车及类似车辆），其最高时速被严格限制在 25 英里/小时。

To make the KEI-01 conform to the rules and regs of auto manufacturing in the US, Toyota is investing $34 million into its Princeton, Indiana manufacturing plant to stand up local assembly and add roughly 50 jobs.

为了使 KEI-01 符合美国的汽车制造法规，丰田正投资 3,400 万美元对其位于印第安纳州普林斯顿的工厂进行改造，以建立本地组装线并增加约 50 个工作岗位。

Scion by Toyota is launching the mini-truck strictly as a fleet-focused pilot program targeting corporate campuses, farms, municipalities, and local delivery operators, distributed through a specialized group of existing Toyota dealerships.

Scion by Toyota 将这款微型卡车作为仅针对车队的试点项目推出，目标客户包括企业园区、农场、市政部门和本地配送运营商，并通过特定的丰田经销商网络进行分销。
