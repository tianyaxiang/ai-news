# MADSNet: A Multi-Scale Axial-Attentive Dense Spiking Network for Spike Camera Image Reconstruction

Spike cameras encode dynamic scenes into high-temporal-resolution binary spike streams for efficient high-speed vision. Although spiking neural networks (SNNs) can process such signals with sparse and accumulate-only computation, existing SNN-based reconstruction methods are typically shallow and still lag behind ANN-based models in reconstruction fidelity.

脉冲相机将动态场景编码为高时间分辨率的二进制脉冲流，以实现高效的高速视觉。尽管脉冲神经网络（SNN）可以通过稀疏和仅累加的计算来处理此类信号，但现有的基于SNN的重建方法通常较为浅层，在重建保真度上仍落后于基于ANN的模型。

This paper proposes MADSNet, a Multi-scale Axial-attentive Dense Spiking Network for spike camera image reconstruction. MADSNet adopts a hierarchical spiking encoder--decoder, where a multi-scale spike stem extracts early features, axial spike attention captures long-range dependencies, a dense spiking bottleneck enhances feature reuse, and a spike-to-continuous decoder reconstructs continuous intensity images from spike-domain representations.

本文提出了MADSNet，一种用于脉冲相机图像重建的多尺度轴向注意力密集脉冲网络。MADSNet采用分层脉冲编码器-解码器架构，其中多尺度脉冲主干提取早期特征，轴向脉冲注意力捕获长距离依赖关系，密集脉冲瓶颈增强特征重用，而脉冲转连续解码器则从脉冲域表示中重建连续强度图像。

Experiments on gray-scale and color spike reconstruction show that MADSNet outperforms SSIR by 4.3 dB PSNR and Spk2ImgNet by 1.94 dB on SREDS. Compared with Spk2ImgNet, it achieves about 15$\times$ lower latency and uses only about 1/40 of the estimated energy, demonstrating that carefully designed SNN architectures can improve reconstruction quality while preserving computational efficiency.

在灰度和彩色脉冲重建上的实验表明，MADSNet在SREDS数据集上的表现优于SSIR（PSNR高出4.3 dB）和Spk2ImgNet（高出1.94 dB）。与Spk2ImgNet相比，其延迟降低了约15倍，且估计能耗仅为后者的1/40，这证明了精心设计的SNN架构可以在保持计算效率的同时提高重建质量。
