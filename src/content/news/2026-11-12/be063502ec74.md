Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.12906 (cs) [Submitted on 15 Oct 2026] Title: Brasilidade: Measuring Brazilian Identity for Large Language Models Authors: José Roberto Homeli da Silva, Felipe Jorge do Nascimento, Lucas Coutinho Freitas, William Alberto Cruz Castañeda, Marcellus Amadeus.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.12906 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 10 月 15 日] 标题：Brasilidade：衡量大型语言模型的巴西身份 作者：José Roberto Homeli da Silva, Felipe Jorge do Nascimento, Lucas Coutinho Freitas, William Alberto Cruz Castañeda, Marcellus Amadeus。

Abstract: This work presents a new benchmark that aims to address the limitations of current methods for evaluating LLMs in Brazilian Portuguese. We introduced Brasilidade, an assessment methodology comprised of standardized tasks distributed across four different domains, and we are making available the tasks from its first domain, which address nuances of the language, grammar, and variations of Brazilian Portuguese.

摘要：本研究提出了一个新的基准，旨在解决当前评估巴西葡萄牙语大语言模型（LLM）方法中的局限性。我们引入了 Brasilidade，这是一种包含分布在四个不同领域中的标准化任务的评估方法，并提供了其第一个领域的任务，这些任务涉及巴西葡萄牙语的语言细微差别、语法和变体。

The results show that model size, by itself, does not determine performance; several medium-sized models outperformed larger models in the protocols evaluated. Specific models for Portuguese, in general, did not perform above average, despite their targeted training.

结果表明，模型规模本身并不能决定性能；在所评估的协议中，几个中等规模的模型表现优于大型模型。尽管经过了针对性训练，但专门针对葡萄牙语的模型总体上并未表现出高于平均水平的性能。

Different task levels revealed significant nuances: while some tasks proved easy for most models, others posed extreme challenges, and some yielded mixed results, with wide variations in performance. This clearly illustrates the need for benchmarks that address the limitations of the models in Brazilian Portuguese, allowing future training cycles to seek to overcome them.

不同的任务级别揭示了显著的细微差别：虽然一些任务对大多数模型来说很容易，但另一些任务则构成了极大的挑战，还有一些任务产生了混合的结果，性能差异巨大。这清楚地说明了需要针对巴西葡萄牙语模型局限性的基准测试，以便未来的训练周期能够寻求克服这些问题。
