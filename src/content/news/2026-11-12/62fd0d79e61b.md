Multimodal attributed graphs (MAGs), in which nodes carry text, images, and other modalities while edges encode relations among entities, underlie e-commerce, social media, content platforms, and creative applications.

多模态属性图（MAGs）是电子商务、社交媒体、内容平台和创意应用的基础，其中节点承载文本、图像和其他模态，而边则编码实体之间的关系。

Learning on MAGs has developed along two complementary lines: graph-centric methods redesign how modalities are fused, propagated, and connected within message passing, while foundation-model-centric methods pretrain transferable multimodal graph encoders or bring graph context into multimodal large language models for prediction and generation.

在 MAG 上的学习沿着两条互补的路线发展：以图为中心的方法重新设计了模态在消息传递中如何融合、传播和连接；而以基础模型为中心的方法则预训练可迁移的多模态图编码器，或将图上下文引入多模态大语言模型以进行预测和生成。

In parallel, several benchmarks have appeared with different datasets and evaluation protocols. This survey takes an algorithm-centric and evaluation-grounded view of the field and makes three contributions.

与此同时，一些具有不同数据集和评估协议的基准测试也相继出现。本综述从以算法为中心和以评估为基础的视角审视该领域，并做出了三项贡献。

Modeling: we introduce a unified message-passing formulation and organize methods into five integration levels (feature, propagation, topology, learning, and foundation model), covering discriminative and graph-conditioned generative models; within this formulation we derive representative methods as special cases, compare their assumptions, expressiveness, and cost, and identify cross-modal sensitivity as a property that separates otherwise similar architectures.

建模：我们引入了一种统一的消息传递公式，并将方法组织为五个集成级别（特征、传播、拓扑、学习和基础模型），涵盖了判别式模型和图条件生成模型；在此公式内，我们将代表性方法推导为特例，比较了它们的假设、表达能力和成本，并确定了跨模态敏感性这一属性，该属性区分了原本相似的架构。

Datasets and evaluation: we review recent benchmarks, summarize their datasets and metrics, define model-independent descriptors (label homophily, average degree, clustering, and edge provenance), and map each evaluation protocol to the baselines it uses.

数据集与评估：我们回顾了近期的基准测试，总结了它们的数据集和指标，定义了与模型无关的描述符（标签同质性、平均度、聚类和边来源），并将每个评估协议映射到其使用的基线。

Frontiers: we connect multimodal graph learning to AI agents, covering the multimodal graphs that agents perceive and remember and graph engineering, the practice of organizing agent workflows as explicit graphs; we then discuss open problems, concrete efforts for the community, and research directions, including when graph structure helps in the presence of strong multimodal foundation models.

前沿：我们将多模态图学习与人工智能体联系起来，涵盖了智能体感知和记忆的多模态图，以及图工程（即将智能体工作流组织为显式图的实践）；随后，我们讨论了开放性问题、社区的具体努力以及研究方向，包括在存在强大的多模态基础模型时，图结构在何种情况下会有所帮助。
