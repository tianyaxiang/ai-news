Today we’re sharing the first online retrospective of artist Dr. Esther Mahlangu on Google Arts & Culture, honoring her pioneering spirit on her 91st birthday. More than 45 stories and a rich archive covering her art, life, and legacy are now accessible to a global audience.

今天，我们在谷歌艺术与文化平台上分享艺术家埃丝特·马兰古博士的首个在线回顾展，以纪念她91岁生日并致敬她的开拓精神。超过45个故事以及涵盖其艺术、生活和遗产的丰富档案现已向全球观众开放。

For many years, I have worked closely with Esther and her family. It’s a relationship built on years of research and a deep admiration for her as both an artist and a person. It was out of this long-standing connection that the vision for “‘Then I Knew I Was Good at Painting’: Esther Mahlangu, a Retrospective,” was born.

多年来，我一直与埃丝特及其家人密切合作。这种关系建立在多年的研究以及对她作为艺术家和个人深切敬佩的基础上。正是基于这种长期的联系，我们萌生了举办“‘那时我知道我擅长绘画’：埃丝特·马兰古回顾展”的构想。

My goal has always been to present a comprehensive exploration of her life and work, contributing to my long-standing research on South African women artists who have made an impact on the arts since the early 20th century.

我的目标始终是全面探索她的生活与作品，这也为我长期以来关于20世纪初以来对艺术界产生影响的南非女性艺术家的研究做出了贡献。

Through my research, I gathered a rich collection of her work, including large-scale murals, intimate pieces, and rare archival materials. But the archives didn’t include anything from her life before 1980, so crucial pieces of her history were missing. This void challenged us to find new ways to tell her full story.

通过研究，我收集了她丰富的作品集，包括大型壁画、私密作品和珍贵的档案资料。但这些档案中没有她1980年之前生活的任何记录，因此她历史中至关重要的部分缺失了。这一空白促使我们寻找新的方式来讲述她完整的故事。

In collaboration with Google Arts & Culture, we used AI to visually recreate Esther’s undocumented memories. The process brought us closer to her work and prompted further recollections from Esther herself.

在与谷歌艺术与文化的合作中，我们利用人工智能直观地重现了埃丝特未被记录的记忆。这一过程使我们更深入地了解了她的作品，并激发了埃丝特本人更多的回忆。

This project brought our curatorial and archival work together with the technical expertise of Google DeepMind and Google Arts & Culture Lab, alongside research and design studio Domestic Data Streamers’ synthetic memories initiative.

该项目将我们的策展和档案工作与谷歌DeepMind及谷歌艺术与文化实验室的技术专长相结合，并得到了研究与设计工作室Domestic Data Streamers的“合成记忆”计划的支持。

Through a series of workshops and interviews, we were able to document a missing period of Mam' Esther's history, from when she started painting at age 10 until her first documented works in 1980. These stories — such as the moment she discovered her talent for painting as a 10-year-old using feathers instead of a brush — became the source material for our work.

通过一系列研讨会和访谈，我们得以记录下马兰古女士历史上缺失的一段时期，即从她10岁开始绘画到1980年她首批有记录的作品问世之间的时间。这些故事——例如她10岁时发现自己用羽毛代替画笔绘画的天赋——成为了我们工作的素材。

Following the methodology from the synthetic memories initiative and using archival material, the team generated still images based on Esther’s memories. They then used Veo to turn the images into videos that brought Esther's early days to life.

团队遵循“合成记忆”计划的方法论，并利用档案资料，根据埃丝特的记忆生成了静态图像。随后，他们使用Veo将这些图像转化为视频，使埃丝特的早年时光重现生机。

It is my hope that this retrospective cements Esther’s place as a pivotal voice in global contemporary art, invites audiences to reimagine the possibilities of her archive, and ensures her story and her artistic vision can inspire generations.

我希望这次回顾展能巩固埃丝特作为全球当代艺术关键人物的地位，邀请观众重新构想她档案的可能性，并确保她的故事和艺术愿景能够激励后代。
