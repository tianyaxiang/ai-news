Fractile, a British startup developing AI chips, has raised a $720 million Series C backed by Britain’s Sovereign AI Fund with hopes of “ensuring the U.K. has a greater stake in the hardware that will underpin its future economy,” its government stated.

英国人工智能芯片初创公司 Fractile 完成了 7.2 亿美元的 C 轮融资，该轮融资由英国主权人工智能基金（Sovereign AI Fund）支持。英国政府表示，此举旨在“确保英国在支撑其未来经济的硬件领域拥有更大的话语权”。

Alongside the likes of Axelera, Etched, and OLIX, this makes Fractile one of the top-funded startup players in the race for purpose-built AI chips. Such chips are one of several avenues being explored to reduce the cost of inference — aka using increasingly demanding AI models in production.

与 Axelera、Etched 和 OLIX 等公司一道，Fractile 已成为人工智能专用芯片竞赛中融资额最高的初创企业之一。此类芯片是降低推理成本（即在生产环境中使用要求日益严苛的人工智能模型）的多种探索途径之一。

However, Fractile seeks to go beyond hardware with a full-stack approach that also includes a machine learning compiler to maximize the speed at which LLMs will get executed on its chips. As a result, the company frames itself as a frontier AI company, and its latest round led by Thrive Capital, Founders Fund, Lightspeed Venture Partners, and Redpoint was priced accordingly.

然而，Fractile 试图通过全栈式方法超越单纯的硬件开发，其中包括一个机器学习编译器，旨在最大限度地提高大语言模型（LLM）在其芯片上的运行速度。因此，该公司将自己定位为一家前沿人工智能公司，其由 Thrive Capital、Founders Fund、Lightspeed Venture Partners 和 Redpoint 领投的最新一轮融资也据此进行了定价。

According to the British government, the valuation was “over £5 billion” — quite possibly $7 billion, a figure reported both by the Financial Times and a Reuters source. Still, Fractile’s main differentiator will arguably be its hardware design.

据英国政府称，该公司的估值“超过 50 亿英镑”，很可能达到 70 亿美元，这一数字得到了《金融时报》和路透社消息人士的报道。尽管如此，Fractile 的主要差异化优势可能仍在于其硬件设计。

After a pivot, its approach now revolves on DRAM, the most common type of RAM, but stacked on top of each chip in such a way that it is hoping will bypass the usual trade-off between memory and capacity. This trade-off has become more problematic with the rise of agentic AI, as companies seek to perform longer chains of tasks with large models without compromising on speed.

在经历转型后，其方案现在围绕 DRAM（最常见的内存类型）展开，但通过将 DRAM 堆叠在每个芯片之上，公司希望以此绕过内存与容量之间通常存在的权衡问题。随着代理人工智能（agentic AI）的兴起，这一权衡问题变得更加棘手，因为企业需要在不牺牲速度的前提下，利用大型模型执行更长的任务链。

As compute struggles to keep up, hopes are high for AI chips, despite their limited availability to date. In a LinkedIn post, Fractile CEO Walter Goodwin wrote that “there is still a lot of work ahead” for the company, and that the new funding will enable the startup to “ship [its] first chips and systems next year.”

由于计算能力难以跟上需求，人们对人工智能芯片寄予厚望，尽管目前这些芯片的供应仍然有限。Fractile 首席执行官 Walter Goodwin 在 LinkedIn 上发文称，公司“前方仍有大量工作要做”，而这笔新资金将使这家初创公司能够“在明年交付其首批芯片和系统”。
