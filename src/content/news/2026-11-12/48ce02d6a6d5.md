Summary
I’ve discovered a voltage fault injection vulnerability on the GA100 die, allowing an operator to blow arbitrary fuses. Utilizing this vulnerability, I’ve converted my CMP 170HX 8GB card into what is effectively an A100-80G! Further, my unlocked cards actually perform ~1.2x faster than a retail A100 by having all functional SMs enabled (~120-126, die-lottery dependent), rather than the yield-safe 108 SMs NVIDIA chose for all production A100 cards. Finally, CMP-disabled features like NVLink, PCIe Gen4, NVJPG, NVDEC, MIG are all unlockable with this approach, though I haven’t specifically tested NVJPG/NVDEC/MIG. Here’s how my “A100-80G” appears and functions.

NVIDIA uses fuses for product segmentation, security features, and other fun access control features - and crucially, there are several functions where blowing a fuse on an NVIDIA die can grant you more access, not less. In a properly secured design, a blown fuse should always induce a more restrictive state, because a blown fuse cannot be unblown in an economically feasible way. NVIDIA’s design allows one to relax restrictions by blowing more fuses! In this post, I will go over the GA100 die, its fuse controller, the use of VFI, and future research directions. I’ve decided to publish some of the work that enabled my research here.

NVIDIA 使用熔丝来进行产品细分、安全功能以及其他有趣的访问控制功能——关键在于，在某些功能中，熔断 NVIDIA 芯片上的熔丝反而能让你获得更多权限，而非更少。在设计良好的安全系统中，熔断熔丝应该总是导致更受限的状态，因为熔断的熔丝在经济上是无法恢复的。而 NVIDIA 的设计却允许通过熔断更多熔丝来放宽限制！在这篇文章中，我将介绍 GA100 核心、其熔丝控制器、VFI（电压故障注入）的使用以及未来的研究方向。我决定在此发布一些促成我这项研究的工作成果。

GA100 versions
The GA100 is an NVIDIA die that has been used to power almost 20 different SKUs - the A100 being the most well-known variant. I won’t delve into every SKU, but I will present the GA100 manifestations that matter to my work, covering the different versions of the CMP 170HX and the A100. As fabbed, the GA100 die has 128 SMs, 12 FBPs, 5 NVDEC units, a PCIe Gen4 x16 controller, and depending on the framebuffer size, comes with 4-6 stacks of HBM co-packaged with the die. NVIDIA uses eFuses for yield-control and product segmentation–undifferentiated GA100s turn into more restrictive variants based on defect testing and manufacturer demand.

GA100 版本
GA100 是一款 NVIDIA 芯片，已被用于驱动近 20 种不同的 SKU，其中 A100 是最广为人知的变体。我不会深入探讨每一个 SKU，但我会介绍与我的工作相关的 GA100 表现形式，涵盖 CMP 170HX 和 A100 的不同版本。在制造时，GA100 核心拥有 128 个 SM、12 个 FBP、5 个 NVDEC 单元、一个 PCIe Gen4 x16 控制器，并根据帧缓冲区大小，配备 4-6 堆栈与核心封装在一起的 HBM。NVIDIA 使用电子熔丝（eFuses）进行良率控制和产品细分——未经区分的 GA100 会根据缺陷测试和制造商需求转化为更受限的变体。
