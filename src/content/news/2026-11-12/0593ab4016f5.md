# Why the data center you dread might never even be built

The data center boom is running into serious real-world obstacles, from widespread local opposition and permitting delays to a scramble for electricity. Before those challenges even come into play, though, there’s a more basic problem: A lot of the projects being announced may never get built in the first place.

数据中心的热潮正面临严峻的现实障碍，从广泛的当地反对和审批延误，到对电力的争夺。然而，在这些挑战出现之前，还有一个更基本的问题：许多宣布的项目可能根本就不会动工。

With billions of dollars in AI infrastructure investment at stake, separating realistic projects from speculative ones is becoming increasingly important. On this episode of TechCrunch’s Equity podcast, hosts Rebecca Bellan and Tim De Chant are joined by Brent Nelson, senior managing director of markets and strategy at Ascend Analytics, to separate the data center projects that are likely to get built from the ones that may never get off the ground.

随着数十亿美元的人工智能基础设施投资处于风险之中，将现实项目与投机项目区分开来变得越来越重要。在本期 TechCrunch 的 Equity 播客中，主持人 Rebecca Bellan 和 Tim De Chant 邀请了 Ascend Analytics 市场与战略高级董事总经理 Brent Nelson，共同探讨如何区分那些可能建成的数据中心项目与那些可能永远无法启动的项目。
