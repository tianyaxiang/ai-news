T-Mobile violated a New York state law by imposing a 50GB-per-month data cap and reducing speeds to less than a megabit per second on home Internet plans it is required to offer people with low incomes, New York government officials alleged. T-Mobile agreed to a settlement requiring it to scrap the data and speed limits, to offer the required low-income plans to more parts of the state, and to make it easier for customers to find information about the plans.

纽约州政府官员指控，T-Mobile 违反了纽约州法律，对其必须向低收入人群提供的家庭互联网套餐实施了每月 50GB 的数据上限，并将网速降至每秒不到 1 兆比特。T-Mobile 已达成和解协议，同意取消数据和速度限制，在州内更多地区提供所需的低收入套餐，并让客户更容易找到有关这些套餐的信息。

The settlement requires T-Mobile to eliminate the speed limits and expand availability of the plans within 120 days. The alleged legal violation and the settlement are related to T-Mobile’s wireless home Internet service, not the company’s mobile plans. Governor Kathy Hochul and New York Attorney General Letitia James announced the settlement on Monday, saying that a state investigation found T-Mobile violated the New York Affordable Broadband Act (ABA).

该和解协议要求 T-Mobile 在 120 天内取消速度限制并扩大套餐的覆盖范围。此次涉嫌违规及和解协议仅涉及 T-Mobile 的无线家庭互联网服务，而非其移动通信套餐。州长凯西·霍楚尔（Kathy Hochul）和纽约州总检察长莱蒂西亚·詹姆斯（Letitia James）于周一宣布了该和解协议，称州政府的调查发现 T-Mobile 违反了《纽约可负担宽带法案》（ABA）。

The state law doesn’t specifically mention data caps, but it requires home Internet providers with at least 20,000 customers to offer plans at specific prices and minimum speeds to people who meet income-eligibility requirements. T-Mobile’s policy of reducing speeds after customers exceed a monthly data cap violates the law, New York officials said.

虽然该州法律并未明确提及数据上限，但它要求拥有至少 2 万名客户的家庭互联网服务提供商，必须向符合收入资格要求的人群提供特定价格和最低网速的套餐。纽约州官员表示，T-Mobile 在客户超出每月数据上限后降低网速的政策违反了该法律。

“An investigation by the Office of the Attorney General (OAG) found T-Mobile’s offerings did not meet the ABA’s requirements, which mandate that home Internet providers offer high-speed broadband to income-eligible households for $15 per month at speeds of at least 25 Mbps, or $20 per month for speeds of at least 200 Mbps,” the press release said.

新闻稿称：“总检察长办公室（OAG）的一项调查发现，T-Mobile 提供的服务不符合 ABA 的要求。该法案规定，互联网服务提供商必须向符合收入条件的家庭提供每月 15 美元、网速至少 25 Mbps 的高速宽带，或每月 20 美元、网速至少 200 Mbps 的宽带服务。”

New York says in the settlement with T-Mobile that “after the data cap is reached, the customer’s maximum download speed is reduced from 25 Mbps to 600 Kbps.” New York’s press release said “a typical customer could hit the data cap after about four days of usage.”

纽约州在与 T-Mobile 的和解协议中指出，“在达到数据上限后，客户的最大下载速度会从 25 Mbps 降至 600 Kbps。”纽约州的新闻稿称，“普通客户在使用约四天后就可能达到数据上限。”

T-Mobile also “only offers ABA Internet plans to customers in homes that are eligible for 5G home Internet,” preventing residents in 4G-only areas from accessing the plans, the settlement says. The company also prevented search engines from indexing the webpage that provides detailed information about eligibility for and enrollment in ABA plans, it says.

和解协议还提到，T-Mobile “仅向符合 5G 家庭互联网条件的住户提供 ABA 互联网套餐”，这使得仅有 4G 网络覆盖地区的居民无法获得这些套餐。此外，该公司还阻止搜索引擎索引提供有关 ABA 套餐资格和注册详细信息的网页。

Although the settlement includes a list of the New York AG’s findings, T-Mobile did not admit to any violation. “Respondent neither admits nor denies the OAG’s Finding in paragraph 1-14 above,” the document says.

尽管和解协议中列出了纽约州总检察长的调查结果，但 T-Mobile 并未承认任何违规行为。文件中写道：“被申请人既不承认也不否认上述第 1-14 段中总检察长办公室的调查结果。”

New York started enforcing the Affordable Broadband Act in January 2025 after winning a nearly four-year court battle against broadband industry lobby groups that tried to kill the law. The groups that sued New York in a failed effort to block the law included CTIA, the wireless industry association that represents T-Mobile and other carriers.

在赢得了一场针对试图废除该法律的宽带行业游说团体长达近四年的法律诉讼后，纽约州于 2025 年 1 月开始执行《可负担宽带法案》。曾起诉纽约州并试图阻止该法律实施（但最终失败）的团体包括代表 T-Mobile 及其他运营商的无线行业协会 CTIA。

When contacted by Ars, T-Mobile did not explain why it imposed the restrictions on low-income plans. “We have a long history of helping customers in New York stay connected through low-cost broadband, and we’re updating our plan to meet the state’s requirements while maintaining the same low price,” T-Mobile said in a statement.

当 Ars 联系 T-Mobile 时，该公司并未解释为何对低收入套餐施加这些限制。T-Mobile 在一份声明中表示：“我们长期以来一直致力于通过低成本宽带帮助纽约客户保持连接，我们正在更新我们的套餐以满足州政府的要求，同时保持相同的低价。”

The settlement with New York applies to T-Mobile’s fixed wireless Internet plans, which can be used for home Internet service as an alternative to wired broadband. The fixed wireless Internet relies on T-Mobile’s cellular network but provides access at a single location, delivering wireless signals to receivers installed at customer homes.

与纽约州达成的和解协议适用于 T-Mobile 的固定无线互联网套餐，该套餐可作为有线宽带的替代方案用于家庭互联网服务。固定无线互联网依赖于 T-Mobile 的蜂窝网络，但在单一地点提供接入，通过安装在客户家中的接收器传输无线信号。

It took almost 18 months for New York to investigate T-Mobile’s compliance and reach a settlement with the company. “In June 2025, the New York Department of Public Service contacted the OAG and relayed concerns about certain features of the plan that T-Mobile was offering to customers eligible for reduced-price Internet service under the ABA,” the settlement said.

纽约州花费了近 18 个月的时间调查 T-Mobile 的合规情况并与该公司达成和解。和解协议称：“2025 年 6 月，纽约州公共服务部联系了总检察长办公室，转达了对 T-Mobile 向符合 ABA 减价互联网服务条件的客户所提供的套餐中某些功能的担忧。”

New York officials said the AG’s office “found that T-Mobile was only offering ABA plans in areas where it had deployed 5G wireless service, even though it offered full-price Internet plans that could deliver 25 Mbps download speeds in areas where it had 4G service. In addition, T-Mobile specifically coded its website to hide its ABA plan information from search engines, making it difficult for New Yorkers searching for low-priced Internet to find their plans.”

纽约州官员表示，总检察长办公室“发现 T-Mobile 仅在其部署了 5G 无线服务的地区提供 ABA 套餐，尽管它在拥有 4G 服务的地区也提供能够达到 25 Mbps 下载速度的全价互联网套餐。此外，T-Mobile 还专门编写了网站代码，以向搜索引擎隐藏其 ABA 套餐信息，这使得寻找低价互联网服务的纽约人难以找到这些套餐。”

The settlement requires T-Mobile to stop intentionally limiting download speeds, and says the “temporary reduction of download speeds due solely to network congestion shall not constitute a violation of this Assurance.” There’s also an exception in the state law, which says ISPs shall provide 25Mbps “subject to exceptions adopted by the Public Service Commission.”

和解协议要求 T-Mobile 停止故意限制下载速度，并指出“仅因网络拥塞导致的临时下载速度降低不构成对本保证的违反”。州法律中也有一项例外条款，规定互联网服务提供商应提供 25 Mbps 的网速，“但公共服务委员会采纳的例外情况除外”。
