The last few years have seen significant debate over the widespread use of AI-powered tools in game development and the responsible disclosure of that usage to players. That debate has exploded into a full-blown controversy in the indie game development space this week, as Big Walk developer House House has publicly and belatedly disclosed minimal AI use in its popular co-operative puzzle exploration game.

过去几年里，关于在游戏开发中广泛使用人工智能工具以及向玩家负责任地披露此类使用情况的争论一直非常激烈。本周，随着《Big Walk》的开发商 House House 公开且迟迟地披露了其热门合作解谜探索游戏中对人工智能的少量使用，这场争论在独立游戏开发领域演变成了一场全面的争议。

This particular controversy has been brewing behind the scenes for months, as judges and jury members for the prestigious Independent Games Festival Awards discussed the issue in private discussion groups. But the debate started to break into public view on Monday when 19 members of the 50-plus-member jury released a signed statement outlining a stance “declining to vote for any game that uses generative AI in any capacity.”

这场特殊的争议已经在幕后酝酿了数月，享有盛誉的独立游戏节（IGF）奖项的评委和评审团成员一直在私下讨论组中探讨这一问题。但直到周一，当 50 多名评审团成员中的 19 人发布了一份联合声明，明确表示“拒绝为任何以任何形式使用生成式人工智能的游戏投票”时，这场辩论才开始进入公众视野。

Noting “a significant increase in the number of submitted games developer with generative AI tools,” the group of jurors called out AI’s ecological, economic, psychological, and labor harms in rejecting tools whose use, they said, is still “not a foregone conclusion in our industry.” The jurors also urged the IGF as a whole to “adopt a strong anti-AI policy for future years that disallows games containing any AI-generated work.”

该评审团指出“使用生成式人工智能工具开发的游戏数量显著增加”，他们在抵制这些工具时强调了人工智能在生态、经济、心理和劳动力方面造成的危害，并表示这些工具的使用在“我们行业中并非不可避免”。评审团还敦促 IGF 整体“在未来几年采取强有力的反人工智能政策，禁止包含任何人工智能生成内容的游戏参赛”。

Although the jurors’ statement didn’t name any specific games, by Tuesday morning, one critical darling in particular was increasingly being noted as a central part of the debate. In a statement provided to Aftermath (and added to the game’s FAQ page shortly thereafter), developer House House admitted publicly that “LLMs were used sparingly” near the end of Big Walk‘s development “to help work through some difficult technical challenges and bugs.”

尽管评审团的声明没有点名任何具体游戏，但到了周二上午，一款备受好评的作品越来越被视为这场辩论的核心。开发商 House House 在提供给 Aftermath 的一份声明中（随后不久被添加到游戏的常见问题页面）公开承认，在《Big Walk》开发末期，“为了帮助解决一些困难的技术挑战和漏洞”，他们“少量使用了大语言模型（LLM）”。

That limited AI use—which House House says represents “well under 0.1% of Big Walk‘s code—stemmed in part from a thorny Unity engine bug that was slowing the game down with “infinite length” shadows during some game situations. After consulting with a Unity engineer who determined a fix “wasn’t possible,” House House said it consulted an LLM chatbot, which suggested a possible fix that the developers “had to modify… extensively” into the 10 lines of code that ended up in the final game (At least one social media user says the bug was indeed fixable with significant human effort, though).

这种有限的人工智能使用——House House 称其占《Big Walk》代码的“远不到 0.1%”——部分源于一个棘手的 Unity 引擎漏洞，该漏洞在某些游戏场景中会导致“无限长度”的阴影，从而拖慢游戏速度。在咨询了一位认为“无法修复”该问题的 Unity 工程师后，House House 表示他们咨询了一个人工智能聊天机器人，机器人提出了一个可能的修复方案，开发人员“不得不进行大量修改”，最终将其转化为游戏中的 10 行代码（不过，至少有一位社交媒体用户表示，通过大量的人力投入，该漏洞确实是可以修复的）。

House House said it didn’t disclose this LLM usage for Big Walk‘s Steam release earlier this year because Valve “does not request disclosure of code or chatbot usage.” Indeed, Valve’s AI disclosure form for developers specifically notes that it is concerned with AI-generated content, not “efficiency gains” from “modern game development environments [that] have AI powered tools built into them.”

House House 表示，他们没有在今年早些时候《Big Walk》的 Steam 发布时披露这种大语言模型的使用情况，因为 Valve “并不要求披露代码或聊天机器人的使用情况”。事实上，Valve 面向开发者的 AI 披露表格明确指出，他们关注的是人工智能生成的内容，而不是“现代游戏开发环境（内置了人工智能工具）”所带来的“效率提升”。

The IGF Awards make no such distinction for AI usage, however, which led House House to mention its AI-assisted bug fix in submitting the game for awards consideration. A version of that disclosure is being made public now because “it has become a matter of public interest,” House House said. “This is not an endorsement of, or argument for, the use of AI tools, however minimal,” House House wrote. “We have some concern that voluntary disclosures like this might contribute to the normalization of this technology, or its treatment as ‘inevitable.’ We support the work of those who are trying to limit the harm generative AI is doing to videogames and our world, and apologize that Big Walk’s LLM usage may not meet their standards.”

然而，IGF 奖项对人工智能的使用并没有做出这种区分，这促使 House House 在提交游戏参评时提到了其人工智能辅助修复漏洞的情况。House House 表示，该披露信息的一个版本现在被公开，是因为“这已成为公众关注的问题”。House House 写道：“这并非是对使用人工智能工具的认可或辩护，无论其程度多么微小。我们担心，像这样的自愿披露可能会助长这种技术的正常化，或将其视为‘不可避免’。我们支持那些试图限制生成式人工智能对电子游戏和我们的世界造成伤害的人们的工作，并为《Big Walk》对大语言模型的使用可能不符合他们的标准而道歉。”

Learning of House House’s AI disclosure was particularly painful for some IGF judges who had previously felt a special connection to Big Walk. In a blog post discussing his feelings, IGF jurist Austin Walker called the game “an achievement” that was “remarkable” and “beautiful,” featuring puzzles that “capture a range of play that is largely abandoned by the digital form.” Fellow jurist Damon Reece shared in a blog post that the game is “seriously special to me” and that it gave him “a profound and powerful emotional reaction to being in a space that felt like home.”

得知 House House 的人工智能披露信息，对于一些此前对《Big Walk》有着特殊情感的 IGF 评委来说尤其痛苦。IGF 评委 Austin Walker 在一篇讨论自己感受的博文中称这款游戏是“一项成就”，是“非凡”且“美丽”的，其谜题“捕捉到了数字形式中很大程度上被遗弃的游戏范围”。另一位评委 Damon Reece 在博文中分享说，这款游戏对他来说“非常特别”，让他“在身处一个感觉像家一样的地方时，产生了深刻而强烈的情感反应”。

But when it comes to IGF awards consideration, Walker and Reece were among those who said that even minimal use of AI tools overrode that deep and abiding respect for the work itself. “We have our hands on the lever here,” Reece wrote. “This is pain that has to happen [emphasis in original] to try and steer our culture away from this. We need to choose to speak loudly and clearly on this, to prioritize human creativity and labour, to tell the culture and the world that this is unacceptable to us. To do otherwise is rank cowardice.”

但当谈到 IGF 奖项评选时，Walker 和 Reece 等人表示，即使是极少量地使用人工智能工具，也抵消了他们对作品本身那种深厚而持久的尊重。Reece 写道：“我们掌握着控制权。这种痛苦是必须发生的（原文强调），以试图引导我们的文化远离这一点。我们需要选择对此大声且清晰地表达立场，优先考虑人类的创造力和劳动，告诉文化界和全世界，这对我们来说是不可接受的。否则就是彻头彻尾的懦弱。”

This isn’t the first time AI usage has noticeably affected game award judging. Last year, the Indie Game Awards (which are completely separate from the IGF Awards) rescinded awards for Clair Obscur: Expedition 33 after the developers admitted that some in-game textures were made using AI tools (these were later patched out). “The Indie Game Awards have a hard stance on the use of gen AI throughout the nomination process and during the ceremony itself,” they said at the time.

这并不是人工智能的使用第一次明显影响游戏奖项的评选。去年，独立游戏奖（Indie Game Awards，与 IGF 奖完全独立）在开发商承认游戏内的一些纹理是使用人工智能工具制作后（这些纹理后来被补丁移除），撤销了《Clair Obscur: Expedition 33》的奖项。他们当时表示：“独立游戏奖在整个提名过程和颁奖典礼期间对使用生成式人工智能持强硬立场。”
