Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.12976 (cs) [Submitted on 8 Nov 2026] Title: Partition, Refine, and Detect: Preserving Novel Category Capacity in Open-Vocabulary Object Detection Authors: Wei Zhang, Zhu Teng, Zhixiang Chi, Junnan Zou, Yi Liu, Jianping Fan.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.12976 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 8 日] 标题：分割、精炼与检测：在开放词汇目标检测中保留新类别容量 作者：Wei Zhang, Zhu Teng, Zhixiang Chi, Junnan Zou, Yi Liu, Jianping Fan。

Abstract: Open-vocabulary object detection (OVD) expects the model trained on base classes to recognize both base and novel classes at inference. Typical methods rely on CLIP-style vision-language embeddings, yet the learned space is often biased toward base categories, with limited effective capacity preserved for novel concepts. Such a base-dominated representation can compress novel semantics, leading to semantic collapse and mis-calibrated confidence at inference.

摘要：开放词汇目标检测 (OVD) 要求在基类上训练的模型在推理时能够识别基类和新类别。典型方法依赖于 CLIP 风格的视觉-语言嵌入，然而所学习的空间往往偏向于基类，为新概念保留的有效容量有限。这种以基类为主导的表示可能会压缩新类别的语义，导致推理时的语义崩溃和置信度校准偏差。

To address this issue, we present PR-OVD, a partition-and-refine framework that explicitly reserves capacity for novel categories and strengthens category separation, while being seamlessly integrated into different detector architectures. Specifically, PR-OVD first performs coarse partitioning to build global divisions for base, novel, and an explicit background partition in both visual and textual modalities, preventing object-irrelevant embeddings from leaking into category-specific representations.

为了解决这个问题，我们提出了 PR-OVD，这是一个分割与精炼框架，它明确为新类别保留了容量并加强了类别分离，同时可以无缝集成到不同的检测器架构中。具体而言，PR-OVD 首先执行粗略分割，在视觉和文本模态中为基类、新类别以及明确的背景分区建立全局划分，防止与对象无关的嵌入泄漏到特定类别的表示中。

It then applies category-level refinement within each partition to sharpen decision boundaries and alleviate the lack of fine-grained region-level pretraining in CLIP. To further provide reliable visual anchors for novel categories, we design a cognitively inspired Structure Prompt (SP) that encodes both egocentric attributes and allocentric context, facilitating stable partition-and-refine learning.

随后，它在每个分区内应用类别级精炼，以锐化决策边界并缓解 CLIP 中缺乏细粒度区域级预训练的问题。为了进一步为新类别提供可靠的视觉锚点，我们设计了一种受认知启发的结构提示 (SP)，它同时编码了自我中心属性和异中心上下文，从而促进了稳定的分割与精炼学习。

Extensive experiments on two OVD benchmarks show that PR-OVD delivers an average 6.5% proportional improvement on novel categories over the state-of-the-art methods. Notably, PR-OVD also benefits other open-vocabulary tasks, including segmentation and classification, further confirming its effectiveness and generality.

在两个 OVD 基准测试上的大量实验表明，PR-OVD 在新类别上的表现平均比现有最先进方法提升了 6.5%。值得注意的是，PR-OVD 也使其他开放词汇任务受益，包括分割和分类，进一步证实了其有效性和通用性。
