Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.12937 (cs) [Submitted on 6 Nov 2026] Title: Tracing Psychometric Inference in Large Language Models Authors: Chih-Hao Hsu, Feng-Chun B. Chou, Pin-Hao A. Chen.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.12937 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 6 日] 标题：追踪大型语言模型中的心理测量推理 作者：Chih-Hao Hsu, Feng-Chun B. Chou, Pin-Hao A. Chen。

Abstract: Do large language models represent psychological constructs internally, or do they only generate outputs that resemble psychometric structure? We address this question using a cross-persona paradigm across 14 model variants from two families (Llama 3 and Qwen 2.5, 0.5B-14B, base and instruct).

摘要：大型语言模型是在内部表征心理结构，还是仅仅生成了类似于心理测量结构的输出？我们通过跨角色范式，针对来自两个系列（Llama 3 和 Qwen 2.5，0.5B-14B，基础版和指令版）的 14 个模型变体探讨了这一问题。

Given responses on one psychological scale from real individuals (N = 272 Taiwanese university students), models predict responses on six other scales. At the behavioral level, larger variants reproduce and amplify human cross-scale correlations; amplification persists after correcting the seven-scale human correlations for measurement attenuation.

基于真实个体（N = 272 名台湾大学生）在一种心理量表上的反应，模型预测了其他六种量表上的反应。在行为层面，较大的变体能够重现并放大人类的跨量表相关性；在对七种量表的人类相关性进行测量衰减校正后，这种放大效应依然存在。

We then examine whether this structure is reflected internally. Contrastive direction analysis finds geometry aligned with the human matrix in larger instruct variants, but little alignment in base variants under this assay. Across models, geometry strength is associated with behavioral amplification (r = 0.68, p = 0.008; partial r = 0.69 controlling for log size).

随后，我们检查了这种结构是否在内部得到反映。对比方向分析发现，在较大的指令版变体中，几何结构与人类矩阵相一致，但在基础版变体中，通过此项分析发现的一致性很小。在各模型中，几何强度与行为放大效应相关（r = 0.68, p = 0.008；控制对数规模后的偏相关 r = 0.69）。

To relate internal structure to output behavior, a matched activation-probing paradigm shows that probe slopes vary less than behavioral slopes (1.38-1.77 vs 0.52-1.53). A synthetic control shows that ridge probing can inflate these slopes; after subtracting a baseline based on a reliability proxy from another analysis, adjusted slopes remain associated with behavior (r = 0.88).

为了将内部结构与输出行为联系起来，匹配的激活探测范式显示，探测斜率的变化小于行为斜率（1.38-1.77 对比 0.52-1.53）。一项合成对照实验表明，岭探测可能会夸大这些斜率；在减去基于另一项分析中可靠性代理的基线后，调整后的斜率仍与行为相关（r = 0.88）。

We term the measured probe-behavior gap readout attenuation. These results establish decodability and geometric alignment under the tested assays, without showing that the measured directions mediate the cross-persona answers.

我们将测得的探测-行为差距称为读出衰减。这些结果确立了在所测试分析下的可解码性和几何一致性，但并未表明所测得的方向介导了跨角色的回答。
