When Toyota first introduced the Scion marque in 2003, it had one primary goal – sell cars to the younger generation. Its cars were cheaper and available in fun colors with fun names like “High Voltage Yellow” and “xPRESSO.” When I was a kid in the early 2000s, I often dreamed of one day owning a tC in Black Cherry Pearl and enjoyed lovingly calling xBs “toaster cars.” Over half of new Scions sold were to people under 35, and the tC boasted the youngest average buyer age in the entire industry at a sprightly 29. Their sales numbers peaked in 2006, but dropped nearly 75% by 2010. After sustained financial struggle, Toyota retired the marque in 2016. But now, a decade later, everyone’s favorite toaster brand is seemingly back.

当丰田在2003年首次推出Scion品牌时，其主要目标只有一个——向年轻一代销售汽车。这些车价格更便宜，并提供诸如“高压黄”和“xPRESSO”等有趣名称的鲜艳颜色。当我在21世纪初还是个孩子时，我经常梦想着有一天能拥有一辆黑樱桃珍珠色的tC，并喜欢亲切地称呼xB为“烤面包机车”。超过一半的新Scion车主年龄在35岁以下，而tC以29岁的平均购车年龄创下了全行业最年轻的纪录。该品牌的销量在2006年达到顶峰，但到2010年下降了近75%。在持续的财务困境后，丰田于2016年停用了该品牌。但现在，十年过去了，大家最喜欢的“烤面包机”品牌似乎又回来了。

Today, Toyota announced the official revival of Scion, but instead of being a quirky and budget-conscious line, they are becoming Toyota’s cornerstone of curiosity. Now called “Scion by Toyota,” it is being launched as a “freedom lab” where drivers can, and are encouraged to challenge conventional ways of thinking about mobility through design, vehicle concepts, and features.

今天，丰田宣布正式重启Scion品牌。但它不再是一个古怪且注重预算的产品线，而是成为了丰田好奇心的基石。现在更名为“Scion by Toyota”，它被定位为一个“自由实验室”，驾驶者可以在这里通过设计、车辆概念和功能，挑战关于出行的传统思维方式，并受到鼓励去这样做。

Their first production vehicle is the KEI-01, an ultra-compact utility truck, otherwise known as a kei truck. Toyota will be using its existing manufacturing infrastructure in Indiana to “produce” them for sale in the American market. “Produce” is in quotation marks because the KEI-01 is quite literally a rebadged Toyota Pixis, which is a rebadged Daihatsu Hijet Jumbo Cab. Based on its initial imagery it is seemingly also still right-hand drive, and there’s no word on if a LHD variant will be available in the future.

他们的第一款量产车是KEI-01，这是一款超紧凑型多功能卡车，也就是所谓的“K-car”卡车（轻型卡车）。丰田将利用其在印第安纳州现有的制造基础设施来“生产”这些车辆，以供美国市场销售。“生产”一词加了引号，因为KEI-01实际上就是贴牌的丰田Pixis，而Pixis本身又是贴牌的大发Hijet Jumbo Cab。根据初步图像显示，它似乎仍然是右舵驾驶，目前尚无消息表明未来是否会提供左舵版本。

With its friendly little 660cc engine and CVT transmission, the KEI-01 was certified to meet American low-speed vehicle safety standards, meaning it has a limited top speed of less than 25mph and is only legal on roads with a posted speed limit of less than 35mph. This makes it not really usable as a daily driver, but keeps it in this beautiful sweet spot where it has a shocking number of commercial and agricultural uses.

凭借其友好的660cc小排量发动机和CVT变速箱，KEI-01已获得认证，符合美国低速车辆安全标准，这意味着它的最高时速被限制在25英里/小时以下，且仅能在限速低于35英里/小时的道路上合法行驶。这使得它无法作为日常代步车使用，但也让它处于一个绝佳的平衡点，在商业和农业领域拥有惊人的用途。

The KEI-01 has a 600-pound payload (which means cargo and passengers) and a three-way fold-down cargo bed, making it a well-equipped workhorse for use on farms and in warehouses. It’s only about 5 feet wide and 11 feet long, which makes it great for use as a delivery or work vehicle in dense, urban areas like New York City. Believe it or not, it also has 4WD, which means it can traverse uneven terrain in more rural areas with ease. And don’t worry if it’s hot outside, because heat and air conditioning are standard.

KEI-01拥有600磅的载荷能力（包括货物和乘客）以及一个三面可折叠的货斗，使其成为农场和仓库中装备精良的“工作马”。它宽约5英尺，长约11英尺，非常适合在纽约市等人口稠密的城市地区用作配送或工作车辆。信不信由你，它还配备了四驱系统，这意味着它可以轻松穿越农村地区崎岖的地形。如果外面天气炎热也不必担心，因为暖气和空调是标准配置。

It’s worth noting that, while Kei trucks are currently sold new in the U.S. as “mini trucks” through independent distributors, this one is sold directly by Toyota. Before you rush to your local Toyota dealer to place a deposit, I’m going to have to burst your bubble a bit. Initially, the KEI 01 is only going to be available in a low-volume, niche pilot program entirely focused on fleet usage beginning in late 2027. Depending on performance and owner feedback, availability of the KEI-01 may eventually be expanded, but no further information is available from Toyota at the time.

值得注意的是，虽然目前美国市场上已有独立经销商以“迷你卡车”的名义销售全新的K-car卡车，但这款车是由丰田直接销售的。在你冲向当地丰田经销商支付定金之前，我得给你泼点冷水。最初，KEI-01仅会通过一个专注于车队使用的小规模利基试点项目提供，该项目将于2027年底开始。根据表现和车主反馈，KEI-01的供应范围最终可能会扩大，但丰田目前尚未提供更多信息。

Two other mobility concepts accompany the KEI-01 in the Scion by Toyota lineup, the SxS Concept and the BXE Concept.

除了KEI-01之外，Scion by Toyota系列还伴随有另外两款出行概念车：SxS Concept和BXE Concept。

The SxS Concept was unveiled at the 2025 SEMA Show as a purpose-built side-by-side all-terrain vehicle. Developed by Toyota North America, the off-road concept is outfitted with a turbocharged four-cylinder from the Tacoma making in the realm of 300hp. You won’t have to worry about your turbo spool scaring away local fauna, as it is equipped with a “Silent Mode,” which allows the driver to switch from the boisterous internal combustion system to a much more civilised fully electric driving mode. Unlike the KEI-01, the SxS Concept is still just that – a concept, with no current plans to have a version available to the public.

SxS Concept在2025年SEMA展会上亮相，是一款专门制造的全地形并排越野车。这款越野概念车由丰田北美公司开发，配备了来自Tacoma的涡轮增压四缸发动机，功率约为300马力。你不必担心涡轮增压器的声音会吓跑当地的动物，因为它配备了“静音模式”，允许驾驶员从喧闹的内燃机系统切换到更文明的纯电动驾驶模式。与KEI-01不同，SxS Concept目前仅仅是一个概念，尚无向公众发售的计划。

Last, but not least, is the BKE Concept. The BKE concept is entirely focused on personal mobility in the outdoors, filling the gap between the utility of the KEI-01 and the pure performance of the SxS Concept. Its small form factor and rugged tires make it an ideal off-roader for traversing narrow paths while being portable enough to stow away when not in use or easily tote around in the back of a car.

最后但同样重要的是BKE Concept。BKE概念车完全专注于户外个人出行，填补了KEI-01的实用性与SxS Concept纯粹性能之间的空白。其小巧的外形和坚固的轮胎使其成为穿越狭窄路径的理想越野工具，同时足够便携，不使用时可以收纳，或者轻松地放在车后备箱中携带。
