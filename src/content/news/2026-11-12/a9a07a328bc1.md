# The best taxi app in China is not the one you think

中国最好的打车软件并不是你以为的那一个。

Every guide to getting around China tells you to install DiDi. That advice is not wrong, and it is also not the thing that decides whether your trip goes smoothly. The app is the easy part. The hard part is the thirty seconds when the car arrives and neither of you can confirm you have found each other.

每一份中国出行指南都会告诉你安装滴滴。这个建议没错，但它并不是决定你行程是否顺利的关键。软件本身很简单，难点在于车辆到达后的那三十秒，双方都无法确认是否找到了彼此。

What the three real options are: DiDi has an English interface and is what most visitors end up using. It links to an international card, though whether your particular card works depends on the region your account is registered in and on the card issuer, so try it before you are standing on a kerb at midnight. The in-app chat translates, which matters more than the map. Amap (高德) is the map app Chinese drivers themselves use. It has an English mode now, and ride-hailing lives inside it. The advantage: the driver is using the same map as the app that dispatched them, so the pickup point is not a translation of a pickup point — it is the same one. The Alipay mini-program: If you already have Alipay set up with a foreign card, ride-hailing is one of its mini-programs, and it avoids installing another app. Convenient; slightly more clicks; the same underlying dispatch networks. Any of the three will call a car. Which one you pick matters far less than the next part.

真正的三个选择是：滴滴，它有英文界面，也是大多数游客最终使用的软件。它可以绑定国际信用卡，但你的卡能否使用取决于账户注册地区和发卡行，所以请在深夜站在路边之前先试好。应用内的聊天翻译功能比地图更重要。高德地图，这是中国司机自己使用的地图软件。它现在有英文模式，且内置了打车功能。其优势在于：司机使用的地图与派单软件是同一个，因此上车点不是翻译后的位置，而是完全一致的。支付宝小程序：如果你已经设置好了绑定外卡的支付宝，打车功能就在其中，无需安装额外软件。方便，点击次数稍多，但底层派单网络是一样的。这三个软件都能叫到车，你选哪一个并不重要，重要的是接下来的部分。

The part nobody warns you about: All three apps describe your destination with a name. "Grand Millennium Hotel." "West Lake scenic area." "Tianfu Square." A driver does not navigate to a name. He navigates to an address, in characters, and often to a specific entrance of a building that has four of them. When the app pins a name and you cannot read the characters under it, you are asking him to solve a puzzle at 60 km/h.

没人提醒你的部分是：这三个软件都用名称来描述目的地，比如“千禧大酒店”、“西湖景区”或“天府广场”。司机导航的不是名称，而是汉字地址，通常还是拥有四个入口的建筑中的某一个特定入口。当软件定位了一个名称，而你又看不懂下面的汉字时，你实际上是在让司机以时速60公里去解一道谜题。

Three failure modes follow, in order of how often they happen: The pin is right and the entrance is wrong. Large hotels and stations have multiple vehicle entrances. The app drops you at one; your ticket is at another. The name is local, not official. Small guesthouses and restaurants are known by a name that appears nowhere on the building. The driver calls. He has one question — where exactly are you — and you cannot answer it in his language.

接下来会出现三种失败情况，按发生频率排序：定位正确但入口错误。大型酒店和车站有多个车辆入口，软件把你放在一个入口，而你的票在另一个。名称是本地叫法而非官方名称。小型旅馆和餐馆的名称在建筑上根本找不到。司机打来电话，他只有一个问题——你到底在哪里——而你无法用他的语言回答。

What actually fixes it: A card. Paper, or a screenshot, that shows the destination in large Chinese characters, with the pinyin underneath in case he reads it aloud to a colleague, and a QR code that opens the exact spot in Amap. I built one because I got tired of passing my phone through a car window on a motorway: sinostay.com/tools/taxi-card. Type a destination, print or save the card, hand it over. No account, nothing to install, works for a hotel you have not booked yet.

真正能解决问题的是：一张卡片。纸质的或截图都可以，上面用大号汉字写明目的地，下方标注拼音（以防他读给同事听），并附上一个能在高德地图中打开确切位置的二维码。我制作了一个这样的工具，因为我厌倦了在高速公路上把手机伸出车窗：sinostay.com/tools/taxi-card。输入目的地，打印或保存卡片，递给司机即可。无需账户，无需安装，即使是还没预订的酒店也能用。

Two more habits worth having: Screenshot the Chinese name of your hotel from the booking confirmation before you leave the room. Not the English name. The characters. For stations, add the exit or the square. "Hangzhou East Railway Station" is a building the size of an airport; the difference between the north and south drop-off is twenty minutes on foot. All 1,269 stations in the dataset carry their Chinese name for exactly this reason.

还有两个值得养成的习惯：离开房间前，从预订确认信息中截取酒店的中文名称，不要截英文名，要截汉字。对于车站，要加上出口或广场信息。“杭州东站”大得像个机场，南北落客点之间步行需要二十分钟。数据集中的所有1,269个车站都标注了中文名，正是出于这个原因。

The short version: Install DiDi or Amap, link a card, and test it once in a city you know before you rely on it. Then solve the real problem: make sure that when the car pulls up, the driver can read where you are going without asking you a question. Everything else — which app, which button, which promo code — is noise. All of this sits on sinostay, alongside the hotel, payment and rail details that took me longer to work out than they should have.

简而言之：安装滴滴或高德，绑定一张卡，并在你熟悉的城市先测试一次，然后再依赖它。接着解决真正的问题：确保车辆到达时，司机能看懂你要去哪里，而无需问你任何问题。其他的一切——用哪个软件、点哪个按钮、用什么优惠码——都是干扰项。所有这些信息都可以在 sinostay 上找到，还有我花了不少时间才弄明白的酒店、支付和铁路详情。
