Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.12977 (cs) [Submitted on 8 Nov 2026] Title: Selective Surgical Action-Center Localization: Retaining Localization Gains through Supervised Scoring Authors: Yanchong Xue, Wanting Zhang, Lingtao Yu, Guochao Zhang.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.12977 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 8 日] 标题：选择性手术动作中心定位：通过监督评分保留定位增益 作者：Yanchong Xue, Wanting Zhang, Lingtao Yu, Guochao Zhang。

Abstract: Surgical action-center localization requires both an accurate reference point and a decision about whether to report it. When frames with and without a reportable center are mixed, the benefit of improved localization depends on which correct candidates survive selection. We present a selective localization pipeline combining heatmap and coordinate supervision with a logistic-regression scorer.

摘要：手术动作中心定位既需要准确的参考点，也需要决定是否对其进行报告。当包含可报告中心和不包含可报告中心的帧混合在一起时，改进定位的收益取决于哪些正确的候选结果能在筛选中保留下来。我们提出了一种选择性定位流程，结合了热图和坐标监督以及逻辑回归评分器。

The scorer learns to identify predictions with a reportable center and a localization error within a specified tolerance. The internal evaluation uses 5,000 frames from 50 surgical sources, five source-grouped folds and two random seeds. Matched locator and scoring comparisons track correct candidates and their rejection.

该评分器学习识别具有可报告中心且定位误差在指定容差范围内的预测。内部评估使用了来自 50 个手术源的 5,000 帧图像、五个源分组折叠和两个随机种子。匹配的定位器和评分比较跟踪了正确的候选结果及其被拒绝的情况。

At an 80% acceptance quota per source and a tolerance of 0.10 times the image diagonal, our method retains 46.08 correct outputs per 100 inputs. The baseline locator with the same scoring approach retains 44.20. The gain is 1.88 (descriptive 95% source-paired bootstrap interval, 0.94 to 2.79).

在每个源 80% 的接受配额和 0.10 倍图像对角线的容差下，我们的方法每 100 个输入保留 46.08 个正确输出。使用相同评分方法的基准定位器保留 44.20 个。增益为 1.88（描述性 95% 源配对自助法区间，0.94 至 2.79）。

Under entropy-based selection, increased rejection of correct predictions offsets the higher supply of correct predictions on average. For the coordinate-supervised locator, supervised scoring reduces these rejections from 6.15 to 2.99 per 100 inputs. Evaluating retained correct outputs reveals whether a selection rule preserves or erodes an improvement in localization.

在基于熵的选择下，对正确预测的拒绝增加平均抵消了正确预测的更高供应量。对于坐标监督定位器，监督评分将这些拒绝率从每 100 个输入 6.15 次降低到 2.99 次。评估保留的正确输出揭示了选择规则是保留还是削弱了定位的改进。
