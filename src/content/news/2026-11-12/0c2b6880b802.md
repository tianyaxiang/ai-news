Top chip manufacturer TSMC is ready to invest $1 billion in Anthropic and OpenAI. That would give the Taiwanese chipmaker direct stakes in leading AI developers, even as its own contract chipmaking business has profited enormously from the AI data center buildout.

顶级芯片制造商台积电（TSMC）准备向 Anthropic 和 OpenAI 投资 10 亿美元。此举将使这家台湾芯片制造商直接持有领先人工智能开发商的股份，尽管其自身的代工业务已从人工智能数据中心的建设中获利颇丰。

The chipmaker’s board approved plans to invest up to $500 million in OpenAI preferred stock reserved for large investors and another $500 million to purchase Anthropic common shares at the latter’s expected IPO price, according to Reuters. Such an investment may give markets “a shot of confidence as the artificial-intelligence trade shows signs of strain,” The Wall Street Journal reported.

据路透社报道，该芯片制造商董事会已批准计划，向 OpenAI 预留给大型投资者的优先股投资至多 5 亿美元，并以 Anthropic 预期的首次公开募股（IPO）价格购买其 5 亿美元的普通股。据《华尔街日报》报道，此类投资可能会在人工智能交易显示出压力迹象时，为市场注入“一剂强心针”。

But analysts also warned that such circular financing among investors, suppliers, and customers has made it difficult to evaluate real market demand for AI-related hardware and services, according to the Journal. TSMC’s chip fabrication plants physically produce the vast majority of Nvidia’s chips designed for AI data center workloads and make most of Apple’s custom chips used in iPhones, Mac laptops and desktops, plus Apple’s planned AI computing server.

但据《华尔街日报》报道，分析师也警告称，投资者、供应商和客户之间这种循环融资的做法，使得评估人工智能相关硬件和服务的真实市场需求变得困难。台积电的晶圆厂负责生产英伟达（Nvidia）为人工智能数据中心工作负载设计的大部分芯片，并制造苹果公司用于 iPhone、Mac 笔记本电脑和台式机的大部分定制芯片，以及苹果计划中的人工智能计算服务器。

Most of these fabs are located in Taiwan, but TSMC also operates fabs in Japan and China, and it has an expanding US manufacturing cluster in Arizona. It is also building a new fab in Germany. Meanwhile, Anthropic and OpenAI are both purchasing huge quantities of AI hardware manufactured by TSMC for their data center projects.

这些晶圆厂大多位于台湾，但台积电在日本和中国也设有工厂，并在美国亚利桑那州拥有不断扩大的制造集群。此外，它还在德国建设一座新工厂。与此同时，Anthropic 和 OpenAI 都在为其数据中心项目购买大量由台积电制造的人工智能硬件。

So TSMC’s announced investment certainly seems to fit into the broader trend of circular financing that has become common during the AI boom, even as tech stocks have gyrated recently as investors have grown increasingly jittery. Still, TSMC has plenty of money to spare on such a bet.

因此，台积电宣布的这项投资似乎确实符合人工智能热潮中常见的循环融资大趋势，尽管近期科技股因投资者日益焦虑而出现波动。不过，台积电仍有充足的资金进行此类押注。

Ongoing demand for AI-related chips has enabled the Taiwanese chipmaker to report six consecutive quarters of record profit, with the latest example being the company earning more than $23 billion in profit during the July through September 2026 quarter, according to Reuters. TSMC’s planned investment in Anthropic and OpenAI also pales in comparison with circular financing deals from major US tech companies.

据路透社报道，对人工智能相关芯片的持续需求使这家台湾芯片制造商连续六个季度创下利润纪录，最新例子是该公司在 2026 年 7 月至 9 月的季度中获得了超过 230 亿美元的利润。台积电对 Anthropic 和 OpenAI 的计划投资，与美国大型科技公司的循环融资交易相比也显得相形见绌。

The most notable example is Nvidia, which has invested $30 billion in OpenAI and $10 billion in Anthropic so far, in addition to putting tens of billions of dollars into many other AI and robotics companies.

最显著的例子是英伟达，该公司迄今已向 OpenAI 投资 300 亿美元，向 Anthropic 投资 100 亿美元，此外还向许多其他人工智能和机器人公司投入了数百亿美元。
