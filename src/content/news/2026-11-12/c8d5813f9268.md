The idea that Americans should be offered a greater choice of smaller cars has support from strange bedfellows indeed. After all, who could forget President Trump—usually one to take a maximalist approach to any problem—announcing last year that he wanted the Department of Transportation to make Japan’s diminutive “Kei” cars legal here in the US. Ars’ attempts to get DoT to tell us more about the potential influx of little vehicles have mostly fallen on deaf ears. But this week, there has been movement on the topic, with Toyota announcing that it will bring back the Scion brand with the KEI-01.

关于美国应该提供更多小型汽车选择的观点，确实得到了意想不到的支持。毕竟，谁能忘记特朗普总统——他通常对任何问题都采取最大化处理方式——去年宣布他希望交通部让日本小巧的“K-Car”（轻自动车）在美国合法化。Ars 试图从交通部了解更多关于这些小型车辆潜在涌入的信息，但大多石沉大海。然而本周，该话题有了进展，丰田宣布将通过 KEI-01 复兴 Scion 品牌。

What’s a Kei car? In the US, the preponderance of humongous SUVs is a consequence largely of government policies, specifically corporate average fuel economy standards that made it less profitable to sell smaller, efficient cars. Japan’s Kei cars are what happens if the government instead restricts the size, engine capacity, and power output. A Kei car must fit within the following dimensions: 11.2 feet (3.4 m) long, 4.9 feet (1.5 m) wide, and 6.6 feet (2 m) tall, with an engine displacement of no more than 0.66 L and an output of 63 hp (47 kW) or less.

什么是 K-Car？在美国，巨型 SUV 的泛滥很大程度上是政府政策的结果，特别是企业平均燃油经济性标准，这使得销售小型、高效的汽车利润较低。而日本的 K-Car 则是政府限制尺寸、发动机排量和功率输出后的产物。K-Car 必须符合以下尺寸：长 11.2 英尺（3.4 米）、宽 4.9 英尺（1.5 米）、高 6.6 英尺（2 米），发动机排量不超过 0.66 升，输出功率不超过 63 马力（47 千瓦）。

Scion is back? Toyota killed off its Scion sub-brand more than a decade ago, ending the haggle-free approach and cars aimed at younger buyers in favor of selling more Toyotas. But now it’s back as “Scion by Toyota,” which the automaker will apparently use to experiment with less traditional ideas. But forget about a cool-but-boxy EV in the mold of the Honda N-One or Nissan Sakura. “The future of mobility will not be defined by one vehicle, one technology, or one way of thinking,” said Simon Humphries, Toyota’s chief branding officer and head of design.

Scion 回归了？丰田在十多年前砍掉了 Scion 子品牌，结束了其无议价销售模式和针对年轻买家的车型，转而专注于销售更多的丰田汽车。但现在它以“Scion by Toyota”的名义回归，这家汽车制造商显然将利用它来试验一些非传统的想法。但别指望会有像本田 N-One 或日产 Sakura 那样酷炫且方正的电动汽车。“未来的出行方式不会由单一的车辆、单一的技术或单一的思维方式来定义，”丰田首席品牌官兼设计主管 Simon Humphries 表示。

Before anyone gets too excited, the KEI-01 will be classified as a “low-speed vehicle,” which means it can’t go any faster than 25 mph (40 km/h). So if you were hoping to use one of these but need to drive on anything but surface streets, be prepared to get honked at a lot, and not in a good way. In fact, unless you’re buying a fleet, Toyota won’t sell you a KEI-01 at all, at least not to begin with.

在大家过于兴奋之前，需要说明的是，KEI-01 将被归类为“低速车辆”，这意味着它的时速不能超过 25 英里（40 公里/小时）。因此，如果你希望使用它，但需要在非地面道路上行驶，那么请做好被频繁鸣笛的准备，而且这可不是什么好事。事实上，除非你是购买车队，否则丰田根本不会向你出售 KEI-01，至少在初期是这样。

Oh, and it’s only available as right-hand drive, since that’s the side of the road Japan drives on. But it does feature all-wheel drive, so if you own a ranch—or perhaps a whole load of golf courses—the utility becomes a little more obvious. Toyota says the KEI-01 is suited to small business owners in urban environments, farmers and ranchers, organizations that have campuses that need fleets of facility vehicles, delivery drivers, and outdoor enthusiasts. The trucks will be shipped to Toyota’s assembly plant in Indiana as knock-down kits, adding 50 jobs to the plant in mid-2027.

哦，它只提供右舵版本，因为日本是靠左行驶的。但它确实配备了全轮驱动，所以如果你拥有一个牧场——或者可能是一大片高尔夫球场——它的实用性就变得更加明显了。丰田表示，KEI-01 适合城市环境中的小企业主、农民和牧场主、拥有需要设施车辆车队的园区组织、送货司机以及户外运动爱好者。这些卡车将以散件形式运往丰田位于印第安纳州的组装厂，并在 2027 年年中为该工厂增加 50 个工作岗位。
