Title: From Relevant Posts to Attributable Spans: Explicit Symptom Evidence for Depression Questionnaire Inference

标题：从相关帖子到可归因片段：用于抑郁症问卷推断的显式症状证据

Longitudinal social-media histories offer an opportunity to assess depression, potentially supporting the inference of standardized depression questionnaire responses. However, this task requires evidence that is semantically relevant to a symptom and attributable to the user, while being sufficiently specific to support an individual questionnaire item.

纵向社交媒体历史记录为评估抑郁症提供了机会，并可能支持对标准化抑郁症问卷回答的推断。然而，这项任务需要与症状在语义上相关且可归因于用户的证据，同时这些证据必须足够具体，以支持单个问卷项目。

Existing approaches rely on semantic similarity to retrieve posts, which can introduce misleading or non-attributable evidence, particularly in multi-symptom posts and discussions of others' experiences. We propose SREE (Symptom Retrieval and Evidence-Grounded InfErence), an evidence-grounded pipeline that extracts first-person and symptom-specific text spans, selects a representative subset using a Facility Location-inspired objective, and performs Evidence-Gated Inference over the selected evidence.

现有的方法依赖语义相似度来检索帖子，这可能会引入误导性或不可归因的证据，特别是在涉及多种症状的帖子和讨论他人经历的内容中。我们提出了 SREE（症状检索与证据基础推断），这是一个基于证据的流水线，它提取第一人称和特定于症状的文本片段，使用受设施选址问题启发的目标函数选择一个具有代表性的子集，并对所选证据执行证据门控推断。

We evaluate evidence retrieval on BDI-Sen and evidence-conditioned questionnaire inference on eRisk 2019-2021. Compared with state-of-the-art retrieval approaches, SREE achieves relative improvements of 70% in post-level precision and 59% in F1, while retaining at least 30% fewer posts. On multi-symptom sentences, it also outperforms dense retrieval in precision and F1 even with twice the retrieval budget.

我们在 BDI-Sen 上评估了证据检索，并在 eRisk 2019-2021 上评估了基于证据的问卷推断。与最先进的检索方法相比，SREE 在帖子级精度上实现了 70% 的相对提升，在 F1 分数上实现了 59% 的提升，同时保留的帖子数量减少了至少 30%。在多症状句子上，即使在检索预算增加一倍的情况下，其精度和 F1 分数也优于密集检索。

Across all three eRisk collections, SREE achieves the highest exact item-level agreement while remaining competitive on ordinal and aggregate metrics. These results highlight the importance of localizing attributable evidence and controlling its composition before inferring depression questionnaire responses.

在所有三个 eRisk 数据集中，SREE 都实现了最高的项目级精确一致性，同时在序数和汇总指标上保持了竞争力。这些结果强调了在推断抑郁症问卷回答之前，定位可归因证据并控制其构成的重要性。
