Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.12978 (cs) [Submitted on 8 Nov 2026] Title: Trajectory-Regularized Distribution Distillation for One-Step Generation Authors: An Zhao, Yixiang Zhou, Zejian Li, Yi Guo, Pei Chen, Lingyun Sun.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.12978 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 8 日] 标题：用于一步生成的轨迹正则化分布蒸馏 作者：An Zhao, Yixiang Zhou, Zejian Li, Yi Guo, Pei Chen, Lingyun Sun。

Abstract: Distilling flow matching models for one-step generation generally follows two paradigms. Trajectory alignment supervises the student with the teacher's probability flow ODE, but its performance degrades under very small sampling budgets. Distribution matching directly optimizes the generated distribution, but estimating its reverse Kullback-Leibler gradient typically requires a separate network to track the evolving student score.

摘要：用于一步生成的流匹配模型蒸馏通常遵循两种范式。轨迹对齐利用教师的概率流常微分方程（ODE）来监督学生模型，但在极小的采样预算下其性能会下降。分布匹配直接优化生成的分布，但估计其反向 Kullback-Leibler 梯度通常需要一个独立的网络来跟踪不断演化的学生分数。

We propose trajectory-regularized distribution distillation (TRD), which combines distribution-level and trajectory-level supervision through a momentum-mixture policy predicted in a single student forward pass. This policy serves simultaneously as a one-step generator, an estimator of the student's marginal velocity that removes the need for a second trainable network in distribution matching, and a closed-form time-dependent velocity field supervised along the teacher ODE trajectory.

我们提出了轨迹正则化分布蒸馏（TRD），它通过在单次学生前向传递中预测的动量混合策略，结合了分布级和轨迹级的监督。该策略同时充当一步生成器、学生边际速度的估计器（消除了分布匹配中对第二个可训练网络的需求），以及沿教师 ODE 轨迹进行监督的闭式时间相关速度场。

Experiments on SANA-1.6B and Stable Diffusion 3.5 show that TRD outperforms representative consistency and distribution matching baselines in generation quality and training efficiency for both one-step and few-step generation, with a trade-off in sample diversity.

在 SANA-1.6B 和 Stable Diffusion 3.5 上的实验表明，TRD 在一步和少步生成任务中，其生成质量和训练效率均优于代表性的一致性模型和分布匹配基线，但在样本多样性方面存在权衡。
