计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.12975 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 8 日] 标题：用于广义类别发现的属性证据学习与多模态提示。作者：Wei Zhang, Zhu Teng, Zhixiang Chi, Junnan Zou, Wenxin Luo, Linfeng Ye, Jianping Fan, Konstantions N. Plataniotis。

Abstract: Generalized category discovery (GCD) aims to discover novel categories from unlabeled data by leveraging limited labeled samples from old classes. Existing methods that rely solely on visual cues often neglect the multimodal nature of human perception, prompting recent approaches to incorporate textual information such as category tags and attributes. However, these approaches typically entangle global tags and local attributes within a shared global image representation, rendering attributes unreliable and potentially providing misleading cues that can drive the model toward incorrect predictions.

摘要：广义类别发现 (GCD) 旨在通过利用旧类别的有限标记样本，从未标记数据中发现新类别。仅依赖视觉线索的现有方法往往忽略了人类感知的多模态特性，促使近期研究开始结合类别标签和属性等文本信息。然而，这些方法通常将全局标签和局部属性纠缠在共享的全局图像表示中，导致属性不可靠，并可能提供误导性线索，从而驱动模型做出错误的预测。

To this end, we propose AptM-GCD, a novel multimodal framework that exploits apt textual guidance through attribute evidence learning and multimodal prompt reasoning. Specifically, we design an Attribute-Evidence Learning (AEL) mechanism that treats attributes as localized visual evidence rather than global category descriptors. By interacting attribute embeddings with patch-level visual tokens, AEL identifies reliable attribute-related regions and extracts discriminative local evidence, thereby reducing the impact of noisy or irrelevant attribute cues.

为此，我们提出了 AptM-GCD，这是一个新颖的多模态框架，通过属性证据学习和多模态提示推理来利用恰当的文本指导。具体而言，我们设计了一种属性证据学习 (AEL) 机制，将属性视为局部视觉证据，而非全局类别描述符。通过将属性嵌入与补丁级视觉标记进行交互，AEL 能够识别可靠的属性相关区域并提取具有判别力的局部证据，从而减少噪声或无关属性线索的影响。

Building upon the extracted evidence, we further develop a Multimodal Prompt Learning (MPL) mechanism to bridge local attribute semantics and global visual understanding. MPL converts fine-grained textual evidence into visual prompts and injects them into a multimodal prompt transformer, enabling attribute information to enhance category discrimination through contextual reasoning without enforcing direct global alignment between modalities.

基于提取的证据，我们进一步开发了多模态提示学习 (MPL) 机制，以连接局部属性语义与全局视觉理解。MPL 将细粒度的文本证据转换为视觉提示，并将其注入多模态提示转换器中，使属性信息能够通过上下文推理增强类别判别能力，而无需在模态之间强制执行直接的全局对齐。

Extensive experiments on six datasets demonstrate that AptM-GCD consistently achieves SoTA performance across both fine-grained and generic benchmarks. Moreover, the proposed framework exhibits strong generalization ability, delivering consistent gains under unimodal and class-imbalanced GCD settings.

在六个数据集上的大量实验表明，AptM-GCD 在细粒度和通用基准测试中均持续达到最先进 (SoTA) 的性能。此外，所提出的框架表现出强大的泛化能力，在单模态和类别不平衡的 GCD 设置下均能实现持续的性能提升。
