# Apple is shuttering its 28-year-old online community forum

Apple’s online community forum will close on November 20. On Tuesday night, Apple posted a message to the Apple Support Community announcing the forum’s closure: The Apple Support Community will permanently close on November 20, 2026. Starting today, the site will no longer accept new posts and is available in a read-only state. You can continue to find trusted guidance and support on the official Apple Support site. We want to thank the community members who have contributed their time and expertise to help others with their Apple products.

苹果公司的在线社区论坛将于 11 月 20 日关闭。周二晚上，苹果在 Apple Support Community 发布了一则公告，宣布了论坛关闭的消息：Apple Support Community 将于 2026 年 11 月 20 日永久关闭。从今天开始，该网站将不再接受新帖子，并进入只读状态。用户仍可在苹果官方支持网站上获取可信的指导和支持。我们感谢那些贡献了时间和专业知识来帮助他人解决苹果产品问题的社区成员。

For 28 years, the forum has provided Apple users with a way to get quick technical advice about their products. That advice usually came from fellow users, though Apple employees occasionally chimed in. As with any community forum, the helpfulness and accuracy of the advice varied. Apple reportedly told MacRumors that it is shutting down the forum after evaluating its support strategy and customer needs.

28 年来，该论坛为苹果用户提供了一种获取产品快速技术建议的途径。这些建议通常来自其他用户，尽管苹果员工偶尔也会参与其中。正如任何社区论坛一样，建议的实用性和准确性各不相同。据报道，苹果告诉 MacRumors，在评估了其支持策略和客户需求后，决定关闭该论坛。

The Apple Support Community dates to 1998, when the online forum was known as Apple Tech Exchange. Two years later, Apple renamed the community forum to Apple Discussions. In 2011, Apple revamped the forum, adding dedicated communities for specific devices and the ability to track responses to posted questions, while renaming it Apple Support Communities. By 2022, the forum was called Apple Support Community, as MacRumors noted this week.

Apple Support Community 的历史可以追溯到 1998 年，当时该在线论坛被称为 Apple Tech Exchange。两年后，苹果将该社区论坛更名为 Apple Discussions。2011 年，苹果对论坛进行了改版，增加了针对特定设备的专属社区，并增加了跟踪已发布问题回复的功能，同时将其更名为 Apple Support Communities。正如 MacRumors 本周指出的那样，到 2022 年，该论坛被称为 Apple Support Community。

Based on yesterday’s announcement, it seems Apple now prefers that its customers rely on official Apple employees for technical support. The forum may also have become less popular as rival forums grow, including Reddit and the technical support communities on the MacRumors and AppleInsider forums.

根据昨天的公告，苹果似乎现在更希望其客户依赖苹果官方员工来获取技术支持。随着 Reddit 以及 MacRumors 和 AppleInsider 论坛上的技术支持社区等竞争对手论坛的发展，该论坛的受欢迎程度可能也有所下降。
