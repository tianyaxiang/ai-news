# Duolingo, Strava, and Ticketmaster Are All Caught Up in EU’s New Digital Fairness Rules

The European Commission announced a sweeping set of updates to consumer law on Wednesday to protect European Union citizens from “harmful digital practices”—whether while online shopping, browsing social media, or doing their Duolingo lesson.

欧盟委员会周三宣布了一系列全面的消费者法更新，旨在保护欧盟公民免受“有害数字行为”的影响——无论是在网购、浏览社交媒体，还是在完成多邻国（Duolingo）课程时。

Within the package, the Commission unveiled its Digital Fairness Act to address harmful practices online. Proposed rules include banning dark patterns—practices that manipulate consumers into making decisions they would not otherwise make, such as falsely claiming a product is running low. They will also curb dynamic pricing by ticket retailers, like Ticketmaster; force influencers to disclose when they are being paid to promote a product; outrule personalized advertising and pricing targeting minors or taking advantage of sensitive data; make digital subscriptions easier to cancel; and give consumers a right to interact with human customer service.

作为该方案的一部分，欧盟委员会公布了《数字公平法案》，以解决在线有害行为。拟议规则包括禁止“暗黑模式”——即操纵消费者做出非自愿决策的行为，例如虚假声称产品库存不足。这些规则还将限制 Ticketmaster 等票务零售商的动态定价；强制网红在付费推广产品时进行披露；禁止针对未成年人或利用敏感数据的个性化广告和定价；简化数字订阅的取消流程；并赋予消费者与人工客服互动的权利。

The package “represents the most significant reform of EU consumer protection rules in more than 20 years” according to Michael McGrath, the EC Consumer Protection Commissioner, noting that existing consumer rules were developed before ecommerce, the attention economy, and use of personal data for marketing took off. “The digital fairness act will make sure the digital economy works for consumers and not against them,” he said at a press conference.

欧盟消费者保护专员迈克尔·麦格拉思（Michael McGrath）表示，该方案“代表了欧盟消费者保护规则 20 多年来最重大的改革”。他指出，现有的消费者规则是在电子商务、注意力经济和个人数据营销应用普及之前制定的。他在新闻发布会上表示：“《数字公平法案》将确保数字经济为消费者服务，而不是与他们作对。”

The bill will also enable all consumers to turn off addictive design features on digital services, such as autoplay of videos and streaks—including on apps like Strava and Duolingo.

该法案还将使所有消费者能够关闭数字服务中令人上瘾的设计功能，例如视频自动播放和连胜记录——这包括 Strava 和多邻国等应用程序。

Some of these patterns the EU has already restricted for children through its Kids Act, which in addition to banning under-13s from accessing social media and chatbots, targets addictive designs in those services. Some European rights groups have called for those protections to be extended to all consumers and, teasing the Digital Fairness Act in September’s State of the Union address, President Ursula von der Leyen said: “But it is not only minors who are at risk. Addictive design, for example, is harming everyone. That is why we need a wider framework.”

欧盟此前已通过《儿童法案》限制了其中一些模式，该法案除了禁止 13 岁以下儿童使用社交媒体和聊天机器人外，还针对这些服务中的成瘾性设计。一些欧洲权利组织呼吁将这些保护措施扩大到所有消费者。欧盟委员会主席乌尔苏拉·冯德莱恩（Ursula von der Leyen）在 9 月的“盟情咨文”中预告了《数字公平法案》，她表示：“但面临风险的不仅仅是未成年人。例如，成瘾性设计正在伤害每一个人。这就是为什么我们需要一个更广泛的框架。”

Some of the risks are also already regulated by the EU Digital Services Act. On Friday, the European Commission said ByteDance’s TikTok had breached those rules with the “rabbit hole effect” of its “For You” feed algorithm which personalizes content recommended to users. The tech regulator had previously found TikTok’s “addictive design” broke the rules by including features such as infinite scroll, autoplay, and push notifications, in addition to the algorithm. In July, it found Meta to be in breach of the act due to similar features on Instagram and Facebook. If confirmed, both companies may now face fines of up to 6 percent of worldwide annual turnover.

其中一些风险已受到欧盟《数字服务法案》的监管。周五，欧盟委员会表示，字节跳动的 TikTok 因其“为你推荐”（For You）信息流算法产生的“兔子洞效应”（即向用户个性化推荐内容）违反了相关规则。科技监管机构此前曾认定，TikTok 的“成瘾性设计”违反了规定，除了算法外，还包括无限滚动、自动播放和推送通知等功能。7 月，该机构发现 Meta 因 Instagram 和 Facebook 上存在类似功能而违反了该法案。如果最终确认，两家公司可能面临最高相当于其全球年度营业额 6% 的罚款。

The new regulation is designed to fill the gaps between this, the Digital Markets Act, and August’s AI Act, says executive vice president Henna Virkkunen, “not duplicate this.” The package should simplify consumer protection rules across the bloc’s 27 members and be a “strong safety net in the digital world,” she adds.

执行副主席亨娜·维尔库宁（Henna Virkkunen）表示，新法规旨在填补该法案与《数字市场法案》及 8 月份《人工智能法案》之间的空白，而不是“重复这些法规”。她补充说，该方案应能简化欧盟 27 个成员国的消费者保护规则，并成为“数字世界中强大的安全网”。

The Commission aims to accelerate cross-border cooperation through strengthened enforcement rules. These would also give the regulator direct powers to investigate large-scale impact and fine firms up to 6 percent of their worldwide annual turnover. Non-EU companies would be required to appoint a local legal representative to cooperate with authorities.

欧盟委员会旨在通过加强执法规则来加速跨境合作。这些规则还将赋予监管机构直接调查大规模影响的权力，并对企业处以最高相当于其全球年度营业额 6% 的罚款。非欧盟公司将被要求指定一名当地法律代表，以配合当局的工作。
