The AI industry is still reeling from Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s honeypot scandal, but some in the industry see it as an issue that’s much larger than one man. The Chinese government has been active in economic espionage for decades, and with semiconductors at the center of the AI boom, there’s intense pressure to infiltrate any company involved.

人工智能行业仍未从英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋的“蜜罐”丑闻中平复，但业内一些人认为，这个问题远非一人所能概括。几十年来，中国政府一直活跃于经济间谍活动，而随着半导体成为人工智能热潮的核心，渗透相关企业的压力也随之剧增。

At the same time, many of the executives getting rich off that boom have made themselves easy targets. As CEO of investment intel firm Heron Intelligence, Nate Singer has a unique view into the problem. His firm works on corporate due diligence: Essentially, making sure companies know the relevant dirt before making a major acquisition or executive hire.

与此同时，许多因这波热潮而致富的高管们也让自己成为了容易被盯上的目标。作为投资情报公司 Heron Intelligence 的首席执行官，内特·辛格（Nate Singer）对这一问题有着独特的见解。他的公司致力于企业尽职调查：本质上，就是确保公司在进行重大收购或聘用高管之前，了解相关的负面信息。

And while the Nvidia case is particularly severe, Singer says there are lots of similar cases in the semiconductor industry that have stayed under the radar. “We’ve got former employees talking about behavior at senior executive levels,” Singer told TechCrunch, saying executives are “really crossing lines that should not be crossed.”

尽管英伟达的案例尤为严重，但辛格表示，半导体行业中还有许多类似的案例未被察觉。“我们有前员工谈论高管层面的行为，”辛格告诉 TechCrunch，并称这些高管“确实越过了不该越过的界限”。

In a widely circulated LinkedIn post, Singer detailed one case in which a CEO’s wife made an unexpected board room appearance, “screaming about her husband and another company exec flying around in the corporate jet with a couple young ladies (who also turned out to be state-sponsored actors).” It’s an alarming story, but an unfortunately common one.

在一篇广为流传的领英（LinkedIn）帖子中，辛格详细描述了一个案例：一位首席执行官的妻子意外出现在董事会会议室，“尖叫着控诉她的丈夫和另一位公司高管带着几位年轻女士（后来发现她们是受国家指派的特工）乘坐公司喷气式飞机到处飞行。”这是一个令人震惊的故事，但不幸的是，这种情况很常见。

Singer says he’s seen at least three reports of similar cases, and suspects many more have gone unreported. “There’s a lot more than this one woman,” Singer said. “They are embedded in these companies.”

辛格表示，他至少见过三份类似的报告，并怀疑还有更多案例未被上报。“不仅仅是这一个女人，”辛格说，“她们已经渗透到了这些公司内部。”

Semiconductor companies are already big targets for international trade secret theft. There have been dozens of prosecutions for similar cases in recent years, including a case involving the fabrication powerhouse TSMC in 2007. In 2017, four executives at an unnamed “Bay Area semiconductor equipment manufacturer” were charged with stealing trade secrets for use at a competing company in China.

半导体公司本身就是国际商业机密窃取的主要目标。近年来，已有数十起类似案件被起诉，其中包括 2007 年涉及晶圆代工巨头台积电（TSMC）的案件。2017 年，一家未具名的“湾区半导体设备制造商”的四名高管被指控窃取商业机密，用于中国的一家竞争对手公司。

According to the indictment, the technology in question “related to the high-volume manufacturing of semiconductor wafers to be used in lighting and electronic devices, such as flat screen televisions and smart phones.“ Any sexual elements in such cases rarely become public, in part because of the severe consequences for executives and their companies.

根据起诉书，涉案技术“与用于照明和电子设备（如平板电视和智能手机）的半导体晶圆的大规模制造有关”。此类案件中的任何性相关因素很少公开，部分原因是这对高管及其公司会造成严重后果。

But according to Singer, the ongoing threat has become a kind of open secret in the industry. “There are a lot of senior people in the industry that know this is happening, and are effectively condoning it,” he told TechCrunch.

但据辛格称，这种持续存在的威胁已成为业内的一种公开秘密。“业内有很多高层人士知道这种情况正在发生，并且实际上是在纵容它，”他告诉 TechCrunch。
