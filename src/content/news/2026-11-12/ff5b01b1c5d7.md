One of the most essential pieces of technology that makes your phone’s camera work was created in 1974, and it hasn’t changed much since. When you snap a photo with your smartphone, the camera captures light and converts it into electrical signals to build a digital image. But the Bayer filter—a red, green, and blue layer that sits above the sensor and adds color to your images—absorbs and wastes around 60 percent of that light before it can reach the sensor. To make a red pixel, the filter blocks and throws away blue and green light.

使手机摄像头能够工作的最核心技术之一诞生于 1974 年，此后几乎没有发生过太大变化。当你用智能手机拍照时，摄像头会捕捉光线并将其转换为电信号以构建数字图像。然而，拜耳滤镜（Bayer filter）——即位于传感器上方并为图像添加色彩的红、绿、蓝层——在光线到达传感器之前，会吸收并浪费掉约 60% 的光线。为了生成一个红色像素，滤镜会阻挡并丢弃蓝色和绿色光。

Now, the Boston company Metalenz has acquired an Israeli startup called PxE Holographic Imaging, which has developed a new optical layer called a HoloCoder. This microscopic structure sits directly on top of the image sensor and diffracts light, bending the light waves rather than absorbing them.

现在，波士顿公司 Metalenz 收购了一家名为 PxE Holographic Imaging 的以色列初创公司，该公司开发了一种名为 HoloCoder 的新型光学层。这种微观结构直接位于图像传感器之上，通过衍射光线来弯曲光波，而不是吸收它们。

With the HoloCoder redirecting the light waves toward the sensor’s pixels instead of blocking them, the camera sensor can collect up to a claimed 2.5 times more light. Such a leap in light-gathering ability could dramatically improve a smartphone's low-light photography and dynamic range.

由于 HoloCoder 将光波重定向至传感器的像素而非阻挡它们，据称摄像头传感器可以收集多达 2.5 倍的光线。这种光线采集能力的飞跃可以显著改善智能手机的弱光摄影效果和动态范围。

There's a surprising secondary benefit too: HoloCoder technology can capture 3D depth information at the same time. Most smartphones use secondary cameras, dedicated depth sensors, or AI guesswork to figure out the distance between the camera and the subject in order to judge how much depth should be shown in features like portrait mode. By merging light sensing and depth sensing into one layer, the HoloCoder promises to simplify the electronics on the back of your smartphone, saving space and processing power.

此外还有一个令人惊喜的次要优势：HoloCoder 技术可以同时捕捉 3D 深度信息。大多数智能手机使用辅助摄像头、专用深度传感器或人工智能推测来计算相机与拍摄对象之间的距离，从而判断人像模式等功能中应呈现多少深度。通过将光感应和深度感应合并为一层，HoloCoder 有望简化智能手机背部的电子元件，从而节省空间和处理能力。

The acquisition of PxE—details of which were not disclosed—makes sense for Metalenz. The company launched publicly in 2021 and pioneered the commercialization of metasurfaces, a flat-lens system with nanostructures that bend light, reducing the need for multiple lens elements over the camera sensor while also capturing more light. The company has shipped more than 300 million of these metasurface optics to consumer devices today.

对于 Metalenz 而言，收购 PxE（具体细节未披露）是合理的。该公司于 2021 年公开亮相，并率先实现了超构表面（metasurfaces）的商业化。这是一种带有纳米结构的平面透镜系统，能够弯曲光线，减少了摄像头传感器上方对多个透镜元件的需求，同时还能捕捉更多光线。目前，该公司已向消费设备出货超过 3 亿个此类超构表面光学元件。

The technology can also capture polarization data, and Metalenz is working with Qualcomm to create a more effective, secure facial biometric authentication system for Android called Polar ID to rival Apple's Face ID, expected to arrive on consumer devices at the end of 2027.

该技术还可以捕捉偏振数据。Metalenz 正在与高通合作，为安卓系统开发一种更有效、更安全的名为 Polar ID 的面部生物识别认证系统，以抗衡苹果的 Face ID，预计将于 2027 年底在消费设备上推出。

Rob Devlin, Metalenz CEO and cofounder, tells WIRED that PxE's approach mirrors his own company's by patterning microscopic structures directly onto an image sensor to unlock light data that conventional camera systems throw away.

Metalenz 首席执行官兼联合创始人 Rob Devlin 向《连线》（WIRED）杂志表示，PxE 的方法与他自己的公司如出一辙，即通过在图像传感器上直接刻印微观结构，来解锁传统摄像头系统所丢弃的光学数据。

“Apple, Samsung, Google—everyone is fighting to improve the visible camera every year, right?" Devlin says. "A lot of what they're doing is investing hundreds of millions of dollars into software to try to somehow improve it, but the downside of that is that once information is lost when you're absorbing all of that light, you can't really buy it back, even if you're throwing all of the software into the world at it.”

“苹果、三星、谷歌——大家每年都在努力改进可见光摄像头，对吧？”Devlin 说道，“他们所做的大量工作是投入数亿美元开发软件，试图以某种方式进行改进。但其弊端在于，一旦你在吸收所有光线时丢失了信息，即使你投入全世界所有的软件资源，也无法真正将其找回。”

Low-light photography and night-time video capture could see dramatic improvements with the HoloCoder system. In high-contrast scenes—like taking a selfie in front of a bright window—the sensor can capture more light and better re-create the scene without blowing out highlights or drowning shadows in noise.

借助 HoloCoder 系统，弱光摄影和夜间视频拍摄可能会有显著改善。在高对比度场景中——例如在明亮的窗前自拍——传感器可以捕捉更多光线，并更好地还原场景，而不会导致高光过曝或阴影淹没在噪点中。

PxE's technology isn't on the market yet, but as Metalenz has already developed a supply chain, processed an image pipeline, and set up machine learning algorithms on how to take advantage of these new data streams, Devlin thinks PxE's HoloCoder will be able to fit neatly into its platform and “completely disrupt" the visible sensing market. While the HoloCoder functions as a diffractive optic rather than a true metasurface, Devlin says Metalenz has already replicated the technology in the lab using its own metasurface optics.

PxE 的技术尚未上市，但由于 Metalenz 已经建立了供应链、处理了图像流水线，并针对如何利用这些新数据流设置了机器学习算法，Devlin 认为 PxE 的 HoloCoder 将能够完美融入其平台，并“彻底颠覆”可见光传感市场。虽然 HoloCoder 的功能是衍射光学元件而非真正的超构表面，但 Devlin 表示，Metalenz 已经在实验室中使用其自有的超构表面光学元件复制了该技术。

“It's really then bringing all of that information together through a single approach," Devlin says. "We would do a metasurface design where now from a single image sensor, you're getting all of the information that is in light: You're getting polarization, you're getting color without filtering, and you're getting this depth channel. It's like the one sensor to rule them all.”

“这实际上是通过单一方法将所有信息整合在一起，”Devlin 说，“我们将进行一种超构表面设计，现在通过单个图像传感器，你就能获得光线中包含的所有信息：你获得了偏振信息、无需滤镜的色彩信息，以及这个深度通道。这就像是统治一切的单一传感器。”

For now, Devlin says the two technologies will be separate products for different markets, but in the long term they could fuse into one Metalenz system. He expects we could see PxE's HoloCoder used in consumer electronics as early as late 2027 or early 2028.

目前，Devlin 表示这两项技术将作为针对不同市场的独立产品，但从长远来看，它们可能会融合为一个 Metalenz 系统。他预计我们最早可能在 2027 年底或 2028 年初看到 PxE 的 HoloCoder 应用于消费电子产品中。

Simplifying and shrinking the hardware with metasurface optics was just the first phase for the company—the real goal has always been to unlock that new information channel, which Devlin thinks can be transformational for other categories, like autonomous robots and autonomous vehicles. Instead of relying on large modules with multiple sensors and computationally expensive chips to align all that spatial data in real time, a single-sensor system could do the job in a smaller, cheaper design while using less computing power.

利用超构表面光学元件简化和缩小硬件只是该公司的第一阶段——真正的目标始终是解锁那个新的信息通道，Devlin 认为这对自动机器人和自动驾驶汽车等其他领域具有变革意义。与其依赖带有多个传感器和计算昂贵的芯片的大型模块来实时对齐所有空间数据，单传感器系统可以在更小、更便宜的设计中完成工作，同时消耗更少的计算能力。

“It is a real problem in the sense that, in the color filter, you lose a lot of light,” says Arka Majumdar, who researches low-energy opto-electronics at the University of Washington. He points out that other industry heavyweights, including Samsung and a startup called Eyeo, have been actively researching their own alternatives to Bayer filters, called “color routers.”

“从某种意义上说，这是一个真正的问题，因为在滤色片中，你会损失大量光线，”华盛顿大学研究低能耗光电子学的 Arka Majumdar 说道。他指出，包括三星和一家名为 Eyeo 的初创公司在内的其他行业巨头，一直在积极研究拜耳滤镜的替代方案，即所谓的“色彩路由器”（color routers）。
