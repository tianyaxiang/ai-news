Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.12934 (cs) [Submitted on 6 Nov 2026] Title:EarthRecord: From Pixels to Land Change with Multimodal Agents over Four Decades of Satellite Imagery Authors:Haoran Zhang, Huizhen Sun, Hanbin Wen, Jie Leng, Jiaxin Zhu, Zhaohua Sun.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.12934 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 6 日] 标题：EarthRecord：利用多模态智能体处理四十年的卫星影像以实现从像素到土地变化的分析。作者：Haoran Zhang, Huizhen Sun, Hanbin Wen, Jie Leng, Jiaxin Zhu, Zhaohua Sun。

Abstract: While multimodal agents now carry out complex data analysis with tools, their ability to turn decades of satellite imagery into reliable land-change analysis, crucial for Earth observation science, remains unmeasured. Existing benchmarks fall short: they pose questions on curated images or give agents preprocessed inputs, leaving out the raw multi-sensor archives where such analysis begins.

摘要：尽管多模态智能体目前能够利用工具进行复杂的数据分析，但它们将数十年卫星影像转化为可靠的土地变化分析（这对地球观测科学至关重要）的能力尚未得到评估。现有的基准测试存在不足：它们通常针对精选图像提出问题，或向智能体提供预处理后的输入，从而忽略了此类分析所依赖的原始多传感器档案。

We introduce EarthRecord, a benchmark built on the raw record of 56 sites: four decades of Landsat, MODIS, VIIRS, and Sentinel-2 data, with cloud masking and cross-sensor harmonization left to the agent. It poses 238 land-change questions across ten analysis tasks, scored against withheld references from global products, agreement among methods, and visual interpretation.

我们引入了 EarthRecord，这是一个基于 56 个站点原始记录构建的基准测试：包含四十年的 Landsat、MODIS、VIIRS 和 Sentinel-2 数据，并将云掩膜和跨传感器协调任务留给智能体处理。该基准测试涵盖了十项分析任务中的 238 个土地变化问题，通过与全球产品的预留参考数据、方法间的一致性以及视觉解译进行评分。

Two controls test what answers rest on: the closed book removes the record, and instrument pairs alter one satellite's data while nothing changes on the ground. Evaluations of twelve multimodal agents reveal that leading agents complete most analyses end to end but falter when an answer requires mapping from imagery.

两项对照实验用于测试答案的依据：闭卷测试移除了记录，而仪器对测试则在地面情况不变的情况下改变一颗卫星的数据。对十二个多模态智能体的评估显示，领先的智能体能够端到端地完成大部分分析，但在需要从影像进行映射才能得出答案时表现不佳。

Further analysis shows that the record moves answers from prior knowledge to the pixels but not yet to the real change on the ground: when one satellite's data are altered, half of the right answers follow the alteration. EarthRecord thus provides a principled platform for measuring and advancing agents from pixels to land change.

进一步分析表明，记录将答案的来源从先验知识转移到了像素上，但尚未完全落实到真实的地面变化：当一颗卫星的数据被篡改时，一半的正确答案会随之改变。因此，EarthRecord 为衡量和推动智能体从像素到土地变化的分析能力提供了一个原则性的平台。
