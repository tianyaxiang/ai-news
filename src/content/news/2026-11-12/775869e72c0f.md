计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.12933 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 6 日] 标题：用于自主 X 射线实验的可审计人机代理团队 作者：Gunik Luthra, Ronald J. Pandolfi, Benjamin T. Diroll, Ricky Ronquillo, Ruipeng Li, Christopher B. Murray, Marcus M. Noack, Kevin G. Yager, Esther H. R. Tsai。

Abstract: Closed-loop autonomous experimentation can accelerate scientific discovery, but implementation effort limits accessibility. Agentic systems may lower this barrier, although sustained operation on physical instruments remains largely unexplored. Here, using coding-agent infrastructure grounded in validated scientific methods, we demonstrate autonomous synchrotron X-ray experiments on nanoparticle superlattices under evolving, human-defined objectives.

摘要：闭环自主实验可以加速科学发现，但实施难度限制了其可及性。代理系统或许能降低这一门槛，尽管在物理仪器上的持续运行仍未得到充分探索。在此，我们利用基于已验证科学方法的编码代理基础设施，演示了在不断演变的人类定义目标下，针对纳米粒子超晶格进行的自主同步加速器 X 射线实验。

Complete human-agent and agent-agent transcripts enable an audit that reconstructs decision provenance and characterizes the division of cognitive labor. Across three experiments, 61-67% of messages involved interpretation or judgment, while the share of methodological and scientific contributions increased from 46% in the setup-focused first experiment to 56-61% in later experiments.

完整的人机和机机交互记录使得审计成为可能，从而能够重构决策来源并刻画认知劳动的分工。在三次实验中，61-67% 的信息涉及解释或判断，而方法论和科学贡献的比例从以设置为主的第一次实验中的 46% 增加到后续实验中的 56-61%。

Provenance showed that 59-68% of error corrections involved multiple participants and that about half of all decisions incorporated the input of another participant. These results may guide design principles for multi-agent scientific systems and inform future agent harnesses, model training, and accessible autonomous experimentation.

来源分析显示，59-68% 的错误修正涉及多名参与者，且约一半的决策采纳了其他参与者的意见。这些结果可能为多代理科学系统的设计原则提供指导，并为未来的代理框架、模型训练及可访问的自主实验提供参考。
