# Congress to call Jensen Huang in to answer for alleged Chinese spy affair

据彭博社援引两位众议院共和党人的消息报道，美国国会正准备传唤英伟达（Nvidia）首席执行官黄仁勋，要求他就其与一名涉嫌中国间谍的绯闻进行说明。

The report comes a week after House Republicans Brian Mast, John Moolenaar, and Rick Crawford sent a letter to Huang asking him to appear before their committees for a joint, closed-door briefing in regards to his relations with Alice Huang, the Megaspeed executive and suspected Chinese spy.

在此报道发布的一周前，众议院共和党人布莱恩·马斯特（Brian Mast）、约翰·穆勒纳尔（John Moolenaar）和里克·克劳福德（Rick Crawford）曾致信黄仁勋，要求他出席委员会的联合闭门简报会，就其与Megaspeed高管、涉嫌中国间谍的爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang）之间的关系做出解释。

House China committee Chairman Moolenaar said Wednesday on Fox Business that Huang has until the end of the day to respond to the request. If he doesn’t agree to the briefing, he’ll likely be subpoenaed, said Moolenaar.

众议院中国问题特别委员会主席穆勒纳尔周三在福克斯商业频道（Fox Business）上表示，黄仁勋必须在当天结束前对该请求做出回应。穆勒纳尔称，如果他不接受简报会邀请，很可能会收到传票。

Mast, who chairs the House Foreign Affairs Committee, on Wednesday said his committee is preparing a subpoena for Huang to send if he doesn’t respond. Nvidia did not immediately respond to TechCrunch’s request for comment.

众议院外交事务委员会主席马斯特周三表示，如果黄仁勋不做出回应，他的委员会已准备好向其发出传票。英伟达未立即回应TechCrunch的置评请求。
