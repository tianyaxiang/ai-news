Reinforcement Learning has achieved tremendous success in recent years. Extending its scope from the traditional complex games such as Atari, Go, Pokeman, and Chess, reinforcement learning has caught researchers' attention on modelling real-life scenarios.

强化学习近年来取得了巨大的成功。其应用范围已从雅达利（Atari）、围棋（Go）、宝可梦（Pokeman）和国际象棋等传统的复杂游戏，扩展到对现实生活场景的建模，并引起了研究人员的关注。

Personalized medicine and the dynamic treatment regimen are two such preliminary research fields; researchers are focusing their mindsets currently. Estimating the optimal dynamic treatment regimens from the observational real clinical data is being popularized among the researchers, widening its scope towards more advanced versions of reinforcement learning algorithms.

个性化医疗和动态治疗方案是两个初步的研究领域，目前研究人员正专注于此。从观察到的真实临床数据中估算最佳动态治疗方案在研究人员中日益普及，这将其应用范围扩大到了更高级的强化学习算法版本。

This research is about a research study that focused on reinforcement learning techniques that have been approached in the context of cancer chemotherapy treatments. Primarily this thesis addresses the result of the study by means of the state-of-the-art on reinforcement learning techniques used in generating the optimal policy for sequential treatments, including the existing online reinforcement learning approaches.

本研究探讨了在癌症化疗背景下所采用的强化学习技术。该论文主要通过最先进的强化学习技术来阐述研究结果，这些技术用于生成序贯治疗的最佳策略，包括现有的在线强化学习方法。

Thereby a simulation for cancer chemotherapy via traditional Q-learning would be presented. Secondly, the study will extend towards using offline reinforcement learning in the optimal policy generation. Thus, the proposed solution follows a data-driven approach, which works on the real human colon cancer clinical data.

因此，文中展示了通过传统 Q-learning 进行癌症化疗的模拟。其次，该研究进一步扩展到使用离线强化学习来生成最佳策略。因此，所提出的解决方案遵循数据驱动的方法，并基于真实的人类结肠癌临床数据进行工作。

The novelty of the proposed solution resides in the implementation of an offline reinforcement learning approach by means of a model-based architecture using the conservative Q-learning and the model-based offline policy optimization algorithm. As per the experimental results and the evaluations, it was revealed that the proposed Chemo RL model caters well the problem of cancer chemotherapy treatment scheduling and serves as a supportive model for the junior oncologists in deciding their treatment decisions.

该方案的创新之处在于实现了一种离线强化学习方法，即通过使用保守 Q-learning 和基于模型的离线策略优化算法的架构。根据实验结果和评估显示，所提出的 Chemo RL 模型能够很好地解决癌症化疗治疗调度问题，并可作为初级肿瘤科医生制定治疗决策时的辅助模型。
