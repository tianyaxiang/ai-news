The Trump administration’s fight to stop suspected leaks of top-secret military information on LinkedIn can’t sweep in “a vast number of confidential sources” of random journalists “who have no conceivable relationship” to the Department of Justice’s investigation, an appeals court ruled Tuesday.

上诉法院周二裁定，特朗普政府为阻止LinkedIn上涉嫌泄露绝密军事信息的行为，不能将与司法部调查“毫无关联”的众多随机记者的“大量机密来源”一并纳入调查范围。

The order came after the DOJ asked LinkedIn to turn over 18 months of communications from targeted journalists who collectively published more than 1,000 articles on many different topics over that time period. The request was not limited to journalists involved in a controversial report from a news outlet that Trump said exposed confidential military information in a foreign country.

该裁决是在司法部要求LinkedIn提交目标记者18个月的通讯记录后作出的，这些记者在此期间共同发表了1000多篇涉及不同主题的文章。该请求并未局限于涉及某新闻机构争议报道的记者，特朗普曾称该报道泄露了某外国的机密军事信息。

Instead, the DOJ cast a wide net, requesting extensive LinkedIn data on journalists who had any possible link to the journalists around the time the report was drafted. The majority of journalists have LinkedIn accounts and publish their contact information to encourage sources to connect on the platform, LinkedIn argued.

相反，司法部撒下了大网，要求获取在报道起草期间与目标记者有任何可能联系的记者的广泛LinkedIn数据。LinkedIn辩称，大多数记者都有LinkedIn账户，并公开联系方式以鼓励消息来源在平台上进行联系。

So allowing that government overreach went beyond harming targeted journalists and threatened to undermine a critical platform where journalists frequently connect with confidential sources, judges ruled. Even more concerning, the Trump administration secured a secrecy order barring LinkedIn from telling anyone about the sweeping request for a year.

法官裁定，允许这种政府越权行为不仅伤害了目标记者，还威胁到了一个记者经常与机密来源联系的关键平台。更令人担忧的是，特朗普政府还获得了一项保密令，禁止LinkedIn在一年内向任何人透露这一广泛的请求。

Judge: Security and free speech can coexist. Penning the unanimous opinion from a three-judge panel, Ronald Reagan-nominated judge J. Harvie Wilkinson III rejected LinkedIn’s request to quash the order entirely and ordered the US district court to substantially narrow the order to avoid posing a grave threat to press freedoms.

法官：安全与言论自由可以共存。由罗纳德·里根提名的法官J·哈维·威尔金森三世（J. Harvie Wilkinson III）撰写了三名法官小组的一致意见，他驳回了LinkedIn完全撤销该命令的请求，并责令美国地方法院大幅缩小该命令的范围，以避免对新闻自由构成严重威胁。

Without such an intervention, the US risked silencing dissidents who raise alarms about “events of large importance and governmental behavior of dubious legality and integrity,” Wilkinson wrote. “A request by the government to scoop up reporters’ records wholesale and ascertain the identity of their sources would tip the balance against informed discourse in a manner inimical to democratic health.”

威尔金森写道，如果没有这种干预，美国就有可能让那些对“重大事件和合法性及诚信存疑的政府行为”发出警报的异见人士噤声。“政府要求大规模搜集记者记录并确定其消息来源身份的请求，将以损害民主健康的方式破坏知情讨论的平衡。”

“Many of the users identified have no conceivable relationship to the leak investigation,” Wilkinson wrote. “Instead, they are affiliated with media outlets, universities, and law firms which may have been relevant to the host of other stories the targeted journalists were investigating during the same considerable time period.”

“许多被识别出的用户与泄密调查毫无关联，”威尔金森写道。“相反，他们隶属于媒体机构、大学和律师事务所，这些机构可能与目标记者在同一时期调查的其他大量报道有关。”

As Wilkinson explained, the district court failed to consider LinkedIn’s claims that the request imposed undue burdens and threatened the First Amendment rights of users who would have no way of knowing that their communications were being reviewed by the government.

正如威尔金森所解释的那样，地方法院未能考虑LinkedIn的主张，即该请求施加了不当负担，并威胁到了用户的宪法第一修正案权利，而这些用户根本无法知道他们的通讯正在受到政府的审查。

The lower court reasoned that the request imposed no burden on LinkedIn because the company could feasibly respond to it, then ruled that, regardless of how these matters were weighed, the balance would tip toward the government’s interest in obtaining the LinkedIn data. This was an error that must be corrected, the appeals court ruled.

下级法院认为，该请求对LinkedIn没有造成负担，因为该公司在可行范围内可以做出回应，并裁定无论这些事项如何权衡，天平都应倾向于政府获取LinkedIn数据的利益。上诉法院裁定，这是一个必须纠正的错误。

“It is not appropriate for district courts to approve requests purely because the records requested are logistically feasible to compile,” Wilkinson wrote. He further emphasized that “serious security and speech claims are in tension in this case. There is space under law for both.”

“地方法院仅仅因为所请求的记录在后勤上可行就批准请求是不合适的，”威尔金森写道。他进一步强调，“本案中，严肃的安全主张与言论主张存在冲突。法律为两者都留有空间。”

Press advocates celebrate win. The appeals court has ordered the district court to go back and assess how the disclosure order can be narrowed. That includes entertaining a very limited alternative to the government’s secrecy order that LinkedIn had proposed—which would have notified a single attorney of the news outlet under investigation about the government’s request.

新闻倡导者庆祝胜利。上诉法院已责令地方法院重新评估如何缩小披露令的范围。这包括考虑LinkedIn提出的对政府保密令的一种非常有限的替代方案——即通知被调查新闻机构的一名律师有关政府的请求。

The Trump administration fought that limited request, arguing that the news outlet’s attorney may not be trustworthy or could possibly tip others off to the LinkedIn probe, but Wilkinson rejected those arguments. Noting that the justice system often deals with “imperfectly informed litigants,” Wilkinson said that “the government should be given an opportunity to explain why LinkedIn’s narrow disclosure would somehow compromise its investigation.”

特朗普政府反对这一有限的请求，辩称该新闻机构的律师可能不可信，或者可能会向他人泄露LinkedIn调查的消息，但威尔金森驳回了这些论点。威尔金森指出，司法系统经常处理“信息不完全的诉讼当事人”，并表示“政府应该有机会解释为什么LinkedIn的有限披露会以某种方式损害其调查。”

“Unless the government’s response to LinkedIn’s proposal is more persuasive than anything the government has presented thus far, LinkedIn’s narrow exception to the breadth of the Secrecy Order must be adopted,” Wilkinson wrote.

“除非政府对LinkedIn提议的回应比政府迄今为止提出的任何理由都更具说服力，否则必须采纳LinkedIn对保密令范围的有限例外，”威尔金森写道。

LinkedIn, which is owned by Microsoft, does not seem to expect the government to be able to defend the sweeping request on remand. While a narrowed order may not protect all irrelevant users from being caught in the government’s hunt for the military leaker, it would stop Trump from accessing a broad range of journalists’ interactions with confidential sources.

由微软拥有的LinkedIn似乎并不认为政府能够在发回重审时为这一广泛的请求辩护。虽然缩小的命令可能无法保护所有无关用户免受政府搜捕军事泄密者的波及，但它将阻止特朗普获取记者与机密来源之间广泛的互动信息。

In a statement, a Microsoft spokesperson told Ars that “this is an important ruling for the right to privacy and press freedom.” “Microsoft has consistently defended journalists’ ability to protect confidential sources and the principle that government demands for information should face meaningful judicial scrutiny and be balanced against First Amendment protections,” Microsoft’s spokesperson said.

在一份声明中，微软发言人告诉Ars：“这对隐私权和新闻自由来说是一项重要的裁决。”微软发言人表示：“微软一贯捍卫记者保护机密来源的能力，并坚持政府的信息需求应接受有意义的司法审查，并与第一修正案的保护相平衡的原则。”

Several civil rights groups backed LinkedIn in the fight, including the Reporters Committee for Freedom of the Press (RCFP). In a statement to Ars, RCFP President Bruce D. Brown celebrated the ruling as a win for all journalists, not just those who rely on LinkedIn to connect with confidential sources.

包括新闻自由记者委员会（RCFP）在内的几个民权组织在这次斗争中支持LinkedIn。RCFP主席布鲁斯·D·布朗在给Ars的一份声明中称赞该裁决是所有记者的胜利，而不仅仅是那些依赖LinkedIn与机密来源联系的记者。

“This ruling is a big deal and will quickly become one of the leading opinions in the area—one we will be quoting from it for a long time,” Brown said. “The court recognized that confidential sources are the ‘lifeblood’ of investigative.”

“这项裁决意义重大，很快将成为该领域的主要意见之一——我们将长期引用它，”布朗说。“法院认识到，机密来源是调查工作的‘命脉’。”
