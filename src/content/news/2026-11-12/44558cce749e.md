CoreWeave is rewriting how it pays for the AI boom, according to the company’s third-quarter earnings, reported on Tuesday. CoreWeave, a neocloud that rents out the computing power behind AI, said it’s increasingly asking customers to pay up front, borrowing against specific contracts, and even pursuing short-term deals that could lead to higher prices.

根据周二发布的第三季度财报，CoreWeave 正在重写其为人工智能热潮买单的方式。作为一家出租人工智能背后计算能力的新型云服务商，CoreWeave 表示，公司正越来越多地要求客户预付款项，以特定合同作为抵押进行借贷，甚至寻求可能带来更高价格的短期交易。

The new strategies could help CoreWeave finance its growth more efficiently while squeezing more cash from existing infrastructure. But these bets only hold if today’s demand and pricing power continue. CoreWeave reported $3.66 billion in revenue, up 168% from the year prior, but it also posted a $709 million net loss and $846 million in quarterly interest expense.

这些新策略有助于 CoreWeave 更高效地为其增长提供资金，同时从现有基础设施中榨取更多现金。但这些赌注的前提是当前的需求和定价能力能够持续。CoreWeave 报告营收为 36.6 亿美元，同比增长 168%，但同时也出现了 7.09 亿美元的净亏损，季度利息支出达 8.46 亿美元。

“We will not use the unsecured debt market to fund our business at current yields, nor do we need to,” CoreWeave CEO Michael Intrator said during Tuesday’s earnings call. Instead, CoreWeave’s plan is to rely on loans backed by specific customer contracts, drawing on funds as needed to finance most of the infrastructure it still has to build to deliver on its significant backlog of $132.5 billion.

CoreWeave 首席执行官迈克尔·因特拉托（Michael Intrator）在周二的财报电话会议上表示：“我们不会以当前的收益率使用无担保债务市场来为我们的业务提供资金，我们也不需要这样做。”相反，CoreWeave 的计划是依赖以特定客户合同为担保的贷款，根据需要提取资金，以资助其为交付 1325 亿美元的巨额积压订单而必须建设的大部分基础设施。

CoreWeave also said it expects about $9 billion in customer prepayments and deposits over the next three months, including more than $3 billion tied to signing new customer contracts for capacity it has already built, which requires no additional spending on hardware. Rivals Nebius and IREN have also relied on hefty Microsoft prepayments to pay for computing capacity, signaling that the industry is moving toward asking Big Tech to pay up for their buildouts.

CoreWeave 还表示，预计未来三个月将获得约 90 亿美元的客户预付款和定金，其中包括超过 30 亿美元与签署新客户合同有关，这些合同针对的是公司已经建成的产能，无需额外的硬件支出。竞争对手 Nebius 和 IREN 也依赖微软的大额预付款来支付计算能力，这表明该行业正趋向于要求大型科技公司为其基础设施建设买单。

Upfront payments could alleviate CoreWeave’s immediate borrowing needs, but they also commit the company to providing services at a price that might be less attractive down the road if compute demand continues to surge. CoreWeave is also looking to capitalize on that growing demand by signing more shorter-term contracts that can command much higher prices.

预付款可以缓解 CoreWeave 的燃眉之急，但也使公司必须以某种价格提供服务，如果未来计算需求持续激增，这种价格可能显得不再那么具有吸引力。CoreWeave 也正寻求通过签署更多能够获得更高价格的短期合同，来利用这种不断增长的需求。

The company raised prices across its offerings by about 25% in July and by another 10% later in the quarter. Prices are still climbing this quarter, in a way that “more than offset[s] cost increases,” said Intrator. “We are also allocating more capacity to short-term opportunities, where the pricing justifies it, selling capacity at more than $40 million per megawatt annually, while pricing for new infrastructure on four- and five-year contracts is moving to $20 million to $25 million per megawatt,” the chief executive said.

该公司在 7 月份将其所有产品的价格提高了约 25%，并在该季度晚些时候又提高了 10%。因特拉托表示，本季度价格仍在攀升，其涨幅“足以抵消成本增长”。这位首席执行官说：“我们还将更多产能分配给定价合理的短期机会，以每年每兆瓦超过 4000 万美元的价格出售产能，而四年期和五年期合同的新基础设施定价则在每兆瓦 2000 万至 2500 万美元之间。”

CoreWeave’s financing strategy now revolves around those opportunities. In August, the company closed a $2.6 billion loan, its first designed specifically for shorter-term customer contracts. Known as DDTL 5.5, it lets CoreWeave borrow in installments as it needs the money. While short-term deals let CoreWeave charge higher prices and change them more frequently, it’s a trade-off. Lenders give more weight to longer-term contracts, even if they’re priced lower, because they value predictable cash flow and consistency to repay the loan above all.

CoreWeave 的融资策略现在围绕这些机会展开。8 月份，该公司敲定了一笔 26 亿美元的贷款，这是其首笔专门针对短期客户合同设计的贷款。该贷款被称为 DDTL 5.5，允许 CoreWeave 在需要资金时分期借款。虽然短期交易让 CoreWeave 可以收取更高的价格并更频繁地调整价格，但这是一种权衡。贷款方更看重长期合同，即使其定价较低，因为他们最看重的是可预测的现金流和偿还贷款的一致性。

Even though Wall Street reacted positively to CoreWeave’s earnings, bond investors are much less sanguine. Last month, yields on some of the company’s bonds hit above 15%, highlighting that investors are concerned about the debt needed to fund data center buildouts before those investments see a return.

尽管华尔街对 CoreWeave 的财报反应积极，但债券投资者却远没有那么乐观。上个月，该公司部分债券的收益率飙升至 15% 以上，凸显出投资者对在投资获得回报之前为数据中心建设提供资金所需的债务感到担忧。

In short, CoreWeave is finding new ways to expand without relying on unsecured debt. The strategy it has chosen could boost profits, but it also carries greater risk around contract renewals and pricing — and it just goes to show how dependent the AI infrastructure boom is on sustained, and as yet unproven, demand.

简而言之，CoreWeave 正在寻找无需依赖无担保债务的扩张新途径。它所选择的策略可能会提高利润，但也带来了合同续签和定价方面的更大风险——这也恰恰表明，人工智能基础设施的繁荣是多么依赖于持续且尚未得到验证的需求。
