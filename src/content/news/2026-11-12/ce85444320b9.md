Title: I Saw the Future of Robotics in a Virtual Duck That Chases My Cat

标题：我在一只追逐我猫的虚拟鸭子身上看到了机器人技术的未来

Save this storySave this storyAllow me to introduce you to the robot cat butler I’m developing with help from AI. Using OpenAI’s GPT-6 Sol, I taught a virtual version of Microduck—a cute robot from Pollen Robotics and Hugging Face—to walk around a room and identify any bengal cats in its vicinity. (The clip above shows the robot waddling toward a target feline; its eye view is shown top right.)

保存这篇文章。请允许我向大家介绍我正在人工智能的帮助下开发的机器人猫管家。利用 OpenAI 的 GPT-6 Sol，我教会了一个虚拟版的 Microduck（来自 Pollen Robotics 和 Hugging Face 的可爱机器人）在房间里走动，并识别附近所有的孟加拉猫。（上面的视频片段显示了机器人摇摇晃晃地走向目标猫咪；右上角显示了它的视角。）

I’m still waiting patiently for a physical Microduck—please hurry up and ship it, guys—but simulated behavior is relatively simple, so it should transfer over to the real thing. I plan to have the robot automatically dispense a treat for my pet bengal, Leona, perhaps by bowing its head to release one from a 3D-printed, head-mounted storage chamber.

我仍在耐心地等待实体的 Microduck——伙计们，请快点发货——但模拟行为相对简单，因此应该可以迁移到实物上。我计划让机器人自动为我的孟加拉猫 Leona 分发零食，也许是通过低头从一个 3D 打印的头部存储仓中释放出一块零食。

I was able to code and train the virtual robot in just a few hours, which is, quite frankly, incredible. Previously, this kind of thing might have taken me weeks to figure out. It was possible because OpenAI’s latest models are remarkably good at understanding and manipulating 3D representations of the physical world and then translating that into useful code, agentic actions, and robot training.

我只用了几个小时就完成了虚拟机器人的编码和训练，坦率地说，这令人难以置信。以前，这类事情可能需要我花几周时间才能弄明白。之所以能做到这一点，是因为 OpenAI 最新的模型非常擅长理解和操纵物理世界的 3D 表示，并将其转化为有用的代码、代理行动和机器人训练。

Real roboticists are using the latest models to do some amazing stuff. When OpenAI released Astra this past September, University of California, Berkeley professor Ken Goldberg and his students saw demos that showcased its ability to render 3D images. They wondered if it could help with the simulations required to train different kinds of robots. These simulations need to be incredibly detailed and extremely accurate to ensure that sophisticated behavior—a humanoid that can dance or a robot hand that can grasp—will transfer faithfully to the real world.

真正的机器人专家正在利用这些最新模型做一些了不起的事情。当 OpenAI 在去年 9 月发布 Astra 时，加州大学伯克利分校的教授 Ken Goldberg 和他的学生们看到了展示其 3D 图像渲染能力的演示。他们想知道它是否能帮助完成训练不同类型机器人所需的模拟。这些模拟需要极其详细且高度精确，以确保复杂的行为（如会跳舞的人形机器人或能抓取物体的机械手）能够忠实地迁移到现实世界中。

One member of Goldberg’s lab gave Astra access to an Nvidia tool called Newton, showed it a video clip of a real robot in action, and asked if it could create a simulation of the scene. “What blew us all away was that within an hour it had produced a full 3D simulation with all the parameters set—the position of the camera, the lighting, the background, all the materials,” Goldberg tells me.

Goldberg 实验室的一名成员让 Astra 使用了 Nvidia 名为 Newton 的工具，向它展示了一段真实机器人操作的视频片段，并询问它是否能创建该场景的模拟。“让我们所有人感到震惊的是，它在一小时内就生成了一个完整的 3D 模拟，并设置了所有参数——摄像机位置、灯光、背景和所有材质，”Goldberg 告诉我。

Astra is also able to automate much of the drudgery involved with training a robot in simulation, Goldberg says, by running experiments and examining the results. This is precisely what I found while training the virtual Microduck. Along with GPT-6 Sol, I used an agentic harness from Nous Research called Hermes and a Nvidia DGX Spark, a personal computer that contains a powerful AI chip.

Goldberg 说，Astra 还能够通过运行实验和检查结果，自动化在模拟中训练机器人时涉及的大部分繁琐工作。这正是我在训练虚拟 Microduck 时所发现的。除了 GPT-6 Sol，我还使用了 Nous Research 开发的名为 Hermes 的代理框架，以及一台包含强大 AI 芯片的个人电脑 Nvidia DGX Spark。

I’ve previously written about using agents and coding models to configure, program, and debug physical robots. Normally, this kind of work requires significant expertise; modern coding models make it a cinch. But OpenAI’s newest model went far beyond just writing code. It used a piece of Nvidia software called Isaac Sim to feed instructions to a model that controlled the virtual robot, watch videos of it in action to assess how well things were working, and suggest tweaks to its training regime.

我之前写过关于使用代理和编码模型来配置、编程和调试物理机器人的文章。通常，这类工作需要丰富的专业知识；而现代编码模型让这一切变得轻而易举。但 OpenAI 的最新模型远不止于编写代码。它使用了一款名为 Isaac Sim 的 Nvidia 软件，向控制虚拟机器人的模型提供指令，观看其操作视频以评估运行效果，并建议对其训练方案进行调整。

This process allowed me to train the Microduck to walk around without toppling over. I initially tried getting GPT-6 Sol to teach the robot to moonwalk—I fed it a GIF of Michael Jackson sliding across the stage, which it tried to copy—but after hours of effort, it only resulted in a very jaunty backward jog. (The robots don’t have us totally beat quite yet.)

这个过程让我能够训练 Microduck 在走动时不会摔倒。我最初尝试让 GPT-6 Sol 教机器人跳太空步——我给它喂了一张迈克尔·杰克逊在舞台上滑行的 GIF 动图，它试图模仿——但经过几个小时的努力，结果只是变成了一种非常轻快的倒退慢跑。（机器人还没有完全打败我们。）

Jim Fan, a researcher at Nvidia who has collaborated with Goldberg, says the way AI can now control robots mirrors how software agents function. LLMS can use models trained to control physical actions—visual language action (VLA) models—just as they access different software on a computer.

与 Goldberg 合作过的 Nvidia 研究员 Jim Fan 表示，人工智能现在控制机器人的方式反映了软件代理的功能。大语言模型（LLM）可以使用经过训练来控制物理动作的模型（即视觉语言动作模型，VLA），就像它们在计算机上访问不同的软件一样。

This week, Fan, Goldberg, and others open sourced an agentic framework for robots called Aspire, which allows a robot to discover and use new skills. One example: GPT-6 Astra figured out how to use a pair of robot arms to solve a Rubik’s cube. “It picks it up, tries to twist it, but has the wrong angle,” Fan says. “Then it reasons for a very, very long time, but it eventually is able to solve it because it learns the skills over time.”

本周，Fan、Goldberg 和其他人开源了一个名为 Aspire 的机器人代理框架，它允许机器人发现并使用新技能。一个例子是：GPT-6 Astra 弄清楚了如何使用一对机械臂来解魔方。“它拿起魔方，试图扭动它，但角度不对，”Fan 说。“然后它进行了很长很长时间的推理，但最终还是能够解开它，因为它随着时间的推移学会了这些技能。”

At the Conference on Robot Learning (CoRL), held in Austin, Texas, this week, Fan gave a keynote speech in which he talked about the incredible realization that AI models can automate a lot of the work needed to have a robot do sophisticated things. Fan showed off a demo in which Astra was put in control of a robot arm and told to paint a picture of the Golden Gate Bridge. Using some paint and brushes nearby, it duly completed the task.

在本周于德克萨斯州奥斯汀举行的机器人学习会议（CoRL）上，Fan 发表了主题演讲，谈到了一个令人难以置信的发现：AI 模型可以自动化完成让机器人执行复杂任务所需的大量工作。Fan 展示了一个演示，其中 Astra 被置于机械臂的控制之下，并被要求画一幅金门大桥的画。它利用附近的颜料和画笔，顺利完成了任务。

Of course, getting robots to work reliably and efficiently is still extremely difficult, requiring significant engineering expertise. The vision system on my robot duck does not work well enough to be deployed in a real product. Still, the ease and speed of training robots with AI could soon have an enormous impact on manufacturing and e-commerce, and Goldberg says a number of industrial robotics and automation firms have shown an interest in the approach.

当然，让机器人可靠且高效地工作仍然极其困难，需要大量的工程专业知识。我的机器人鸭子上的视觉系统还不足以部署到实际产品中。尽管如此，利用人工智能训练机器人的简便性和速度很快就会对制造业和电子商务产生巨大影响，Goldberg 表示，许多工业机器人和自动化公司已经对这种方法表现出了兴趣。

Much like vibe coding a few years ago, we may well see breakthroughs in “vibe robotics” as hobbyists and engineers realize how easy it is to program and train robots.
