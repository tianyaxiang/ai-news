Entering into the world of venture capital has never been easy, especially as a first-time fund manager. But it’s especially daunting right now. According to PitchBook data, firms raising their fourth fund “or later” captured a whopping 88.2% of U.S. VC fundraising dollars through the first three quarters of this year, while first-time funds captured a measly 4.5%. Just 81 first-time funds closed in the first nine months of 2026. That puts the year on track to be the worst for first-time managers in at least a decade, according to PitchBook’s estimate.

进入风险投资领域从来都不是一件容易的事，尤其是对于首次担任基金管理人的人来说。但目前的情况尤其令人望而生畏。根据 PitchBook 的数据，在今年前三个季度，正在募集第四期或更后期基金的公司占据了美国风险投资筹款总额的 88.2%，而首次募集的基金仅占可怜的 4.5%。2026 年前九个月仅有 81 只首次基金完成募集。据 PitchBook 估计，这使得今年有望成为至少十年来首次基金管理人表现最差的一年。

Olaf Sakkers has spent the past five years proving that difficult doesn’t mean impossible. He founded his firm RedBlue Capital in 2021 and closed its first fund with roughly $5 million in capital commitments in 2023. He has now held a first close on a second fund that he says will total at least $50 million.

Olaf Sakkers 在过去五年里一直在证明“困难”并不意味着“不可能”。他于 2021 年创立了 RedBlue Capital 公司，并于 2023 年完成了首期基金的募集，资本承诺额约为 500 万美元。目前，他已完成第二期基金的首次交割，他表示该基金总额将至少达到 5000 万美元。

Raised in South Africa, he studied anthropology at Princeton, focusing on global health and health policy, and wrote his thesis on HIV in his home country. He moved to Israel after graduating and was drafted into the Israel Defense Forces, where he says he had a desk job liaising with the U.S. military, and where he became interested in autonomous vehicles.

他在南非长大，在普林斯顿大学学习人类学，专注于全球健康和卫生政策，并以家乡的艾滋病毒（HIV）问题为题撰写了毕业论文。毕业后，他移居以色列并应征入伍，他称自己在以色列国防军中从事文职工作，负责与美军联络，正是在那里，他开始对自动驾驶汽车产生兴趣。

An introduction led to Michael Granoff, founder of the venture firm Maniv Mobility and an early backer of electric-vehicle startup Better Place. Granoff, who had money to invest, hired him on the spot, Sakkers says. Neither had been a venture investor at that point, so they built a fund “almost by accident,” learning as they went.

通过一次介绍，他结识了风险投资公司 Maniv Mobility 的创始人、电动汽车初创公司 Better Place 的早期支持者 Michael Granoff。Sakkers 说，当时手头有资金可供投资的 Granoff 当场就聘用了他。那时他们两人都没有做过风险投资，所以他们“几乎是偶然地”建立了一只基金，并在实践中不断学习。

Sakkers says he ran the investment process day to day, backing Israeli founders who were bringing cybersecurity skills into the automotive world, and later expanding to the U.S. and other markets. By 2021, Sakkers says, he was an equal partner in Maniv’s economics, but he wanted something different — more formal and scalable — so struck out on his own.

Sakkers 表示，他负责日常的投资流程，支持那些将网络安全技能引入汽车领域的以色列创始人，随后将业务扩展到美国和其他市场。Sakkers 说，到 2021 年，他已成为 Maniv 的平等合伙人，但他想要一些不同的东西——更正式、更具可扩展性——于是他选择自立门户。

Today, RedBlue backs what he calls the “real economy,” including robotics, logistics, manufacturing and energy. Getting to this point has been a test of endurance as much as salesmanship, seemingly. Sakkers says that institutional limited partners, outfits like endowments and pension funds, have been the toughest to win over because his checks are small (and theirs are comparatively huge) but also because their risk model is built to “avoid getting a zero,” which is why they tend to gravitate toward established brands.

如今，RedBlue 支持他所称的“实体经济”，包括机器人、物流、制造和能源领域。走到今天这一步，似乎既是对耐力的考验，也是对推销能力的考验。Sakkers 表示，机构有限合伙人（如捐赠基金和养老基金）是最难争取的，因为他的投资额很小（而他们的资金规模相对巨大），而且他们的风险模型旨在“避免归零”，这就是他们倾向于选择知名品牌的原因。

Says Sakkers, it’s akin to founders who come to be seen as “inevitable,” after they’ve previously founded another company. Meanwhile first-time founders often battle against the “gravity of perception,” meaning a more dominant brand can bend how people perceive a newer one. His answer has been to let the results speak, but it takes time. “It’s a long-term game of building trust, demonstrating performance over time,” Sakkers says.

Sakkers 说，这类似于那些在创办过公司后被视为“必然成功”的创始人。与此同时，首次创业者往往要与“认知的重力”作斗争，这意味着更强势的品牌会扭曲人们对新品牌的看法。他的应对之道是让结果说话，但这需要时间。Sakkers 说：“这是一场建立信任、随时间推移展示业绩的长期博弈。”

The first fund was a proof of concept, backed by LPs he had known for a while, including family offices. The second draws on family offices and institutions. RedBlue writes small checks, $50,000 to $100,000, mostly joining deals led by other investors rather than leading them. That lets it do more deals and be “the last investor that they take” on by promising practical help, including introductions to lead investors like Andreessen Horowitz and Thrive Capital, and to customers.

第一期基金是一个概念验证，由他认识已久的有限合伙人（包括家族办公室）支持。第二期基金则吸引了家族办公室和机构投资者。RedBlue 的投资额较小，在 5 万至 10 万美元之间，主要是参与其他投资者领投的交易，而不是自己领投。这使它能够参与更多交易，并通过承诺提供实际帮助（包括向 Andreessen Horowitz 和 Thrive Capital 等领投方以及客户进行引荐），成为创业者“最后接受的投资者”。

Founders expect little from VCs, in Sakker’s view, so RedBlue tries to overdeliver on the support part. By his account, the results are strong. Of 28 investments, he says that 15 have been marked up, and he cites a net internal rate of return of “significantly above 50%.” There has been just one exit — an acqui-hire — so most gains from the tiny fund remain on paper and unrealized.

在 Sakkers 看来，创始人对风险投资人的期望值很低，因此 RedBlue 试图在支持方面提供超额服务。据他所述，结果非常理想。在 28 项投资中，他表示有 15 项实现了估值提升，并称净内部收益率“远高于 50%”。目前仅有一项退出案例——即人才收购——因此这只小型基金的大部分收益仍停留在账面上，尚未实现。

Public records support part of Sakker’s story. HappyRobot, which RedBlue backed early, raised a $150 million Series C at a $1.2 billion valuation in August. Westmag, which makes motors for drones and robots and which Sakkers backed at pre-seed alongside Nat Friedman, emerged in June with an $11 million seed led by Andreessen Horowitz. Flai, an AI startup for car dealerships, followed its 2025 seed with a $27 million Series A. And Humble Robotics, a driverless-truck startup in whose $24 million seed round RedBlue participated, announced a $51 million Series A led by Obvious Ventures earlier this month.

公开记录支持了 Sakkers 的部分说法。RedBlue 早期支持的 HappyRobot 在 8 月以 12 亿美元的估值完成了 1.5 亿美元的 C 轮融资。生产无人机和机器人电机的 Westmag 公司（Sakkers 与 Nat Friedman 在种子轮前共同投资）在 6 月完成了由 Andreessen Horowitz 领投的 1100 万美元种子轮融资。汽车经销商 AI 初创公司 Flai 在 2025 年种子轮之后，又完成了 2700 万美元的 A 轮融资。而 RedBlue 参与了 2400 万美元种子轮融资的无人驾驶卡车初创公司 Humble Robotics，本月初宣布完成了由 Obvious Ventures 领投的 5100 万美元 A 轮融资。

Sakkers argues that discipline is his edge. RedBlue publishes thesis essays, such as one on robotics, and runs on a custom database that he built with Claude Code to log relationships, meeting notes and introductions. That lets him and his lone full-time principal, based in New York, work asynchronously. AI agents, he says, are “useless if they just have to guess,” so everything gets written down. He contrasts that with Benchmark’s more artisanal model, which he says suits a firm with a record he lacks.

Sakkers 认为纪律性是他的优势。RedBlue 会发布投资论文（例如关于机器人技术的论文），并运行着一个他用 Claude Code 构建的自定义数据库，用于记录关系、会议纪要和引荐信息。这使他和他在纽约的唯一一名全职负责人能够异步工作。他说，人工智能代理“如果只能靠猜，那就毫无用处”，所以一切都要记录在案。他将此与 Benchmark 更具工匠精神的模式进行了对比，称那种模式适合拥有他所缺乏的业绩记录的公司。

Long exit timelines for physical-world companies don’t worry him, either: even a $50 million to $100 million fund, he says, can sell stakes on the secondary market. The data isn’t uniformly grim. Smaller venture capital funds between $1 million and $10 million consistently outperform larger funds of $100 million or more at the top end of net TVPI (total value to paid-in capital, which compares a fund’s total value to the money invested in it) across recent vintages tracked by the software outfit Carta.

实体世界公司较长的退出周期并没有让他感到担忧：他说，即使是 5000 万至 1 亿美元的基金，也可以在二级市场上出售股份。数据也并非一律惨淡。根据软件公司 Carta 追踪的近期年份数据，规模在 100 万至 1000 万美元之间的小型风险投资基金，在净 TVPI（总价值与实缴资本之比，用于比较基金总价值与投入资金）的高端表现上，始终优于 1 亿美元或以上的大型基金。

In the meantime, Sakkers is out there knocking on doors, making introductions between disparate parties, and calling up reporters like this one to tell his story. It’s not easy to make a mark in a world where the spoils accrue to the few, but he says he never seriously considered a safer job. “Life is short, and regrets last forever,” he says.

与此同时，Sakkers 奔波于各处，在不同方之间牵线搭桥，并致电像我这样的记者来讲述他的故事。在一个利益向少数人集中的世界里，想要留下印记并不容易，但他表示自己从未认真考虑过一份更安稳的工作。“生命短暂，遗憾永存，”他说。
