Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.12995 (cs) [Submitted on 8 Nov 2026] Title: Capacity Allocation at the Source: Sparse Target Optimization for LLM Knowledge Editing Authors: Dongyao Chen, Jiayun Lei, Zhiyuan Ji, Zhiying Deng, Wei Liu, Xiaobo Sun.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.12995 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 8 日] 标题：源头容量分配：大语言模型知识编辑的稀疏目标优化 作者：Dongyao Chen, Jiayun Lei, Zhiyuan Ji, Zhiying Deng, Wei Liu, Xiaobo Sun。

Abstract: Knowledge editing has emerged as a promising approach for efficiently updating embedded knowledge in large language models (LLMs). It first computes an ideal target hidden state that steers the LLM toward the new output, and then edits the model parameters so that the original hidden state is mapped to this target.

摘要：知识编辑已成为一种高效更新大语言模型（LLM）中嵌入知识的有前景的方法。它首先计算出一个理想的目标隐藏状态，引导 LLM 输出新的结果，然后编辑模型参数，使原始隐藏状态映射到该目标。

While most existing methods focus on the second stage of parameter editing in preserving other knowledge, we find that the upstream construction of the target hidden state also plays an important role in allocating the model's limited capacity space. We first establish a theoretical connection between interference in the parameter space and interference between target hidden states, showing that the two are positively correlated.

虽然大多数现有方法侧重于参数编辑的第二阶段以保留其他知识，但我们发现目标隐藏状态的上游构建在分配模型有限的容量空间方面也起着重要作用。我们首先建立了参数空间中的干扰与目标隐藏状态之间的干扰之间的理论联系，表明两者呈正相关。

Building on this insight, we propose a simple yet effective $\ell_1$ regularization method that prunes away unnecessary ``tentacles'' of each knowledge vector, retaining only the essential components. With fewer extraneous tentacles, each edit affects less other knowledge, enabling better coexistence. Extensive experiments across multiple LLMs and benchmark datasets verify the effectiveness of our method.

基于这一见解，我们提出了一种简单而有效的 $\ell_1$ 正则化方法，该方法剪除了每个知识向量中不必要的“触角”，仅保留核心组件。由于多余的触角减少，每次编辑对其他知识的影响也随之减小，从而实现了更好的共存。在多个 LLM 和基准数据集上的广泛实验验证了我们方法的有效性。
