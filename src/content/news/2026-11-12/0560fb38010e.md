Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.12903 (cs) [Submitted on 14 Oct 2026] Title: SELD: Detecting Silent Evidence Loss in Context Compression beyond the Answer Authors: Wentao Lei, Li Liu.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.12903 (cs) [2026年10月14日提交] 标题：SELD：检测上下文压缩中超越答案的静默证据丢失 作者：Wentao Lei, Li Liu。

Abstract: Context compression is now routine for language models with long inputs, but a compressed context is useful only if it keeps the evidence for the answer. Existing checks compare the reader model's answer or its most likely words, and thus miss a frequent failure, silent evidence loss: key evidence is removed while the answer stays the same, leaving the answer unsupported and risky for other models that reuse the context.

摘要：对于具有长输入的语言模型而言，上下文压缩现已成为常规操作，但压缩后的上下文只有在保留了答案证据的情况下才有用。现有的检查方法通过比较阅读模型的答案或其最可能的词汇来进行，因此会漏掉一种常见的故障，即“静默证据丢失”：关键证据被删除，而答案保持不变，这使得答案失去了支撑，并对其他重用该上下文的模型造成风险。

To address this challenge, we propose SELD (Silent Evidence Loss Detector), which needs no training and no labels. Its principle is that when evidence is lost, a reader model's preferences among the answers it leaves unsaid shift even if its answer does not, so comparing these preferences before and after compression reveals losses that answer checks cannot see.

为了应对这一挑战，我们提出了 SELD（静默证据丢失检测器），它无需训练，也无需标签。其原理是：当证据丢失时，即使阅读模型的最终答案没有改变，它在未选答案之间的偏好也会发生偏移，因此通过比较压缩前后的这些偏好，可以发现答案检查无法察觉的丢失情况。

We also build SEL-Pairs, a benchmark of length-matched evidence and control deletions from three question-answering datasets. Quantitative and qualitative experiments with five reader models on it show that SELD consistently outperforms strong and recent baselines across compressors, compression rates and models, identifying the evidence-removing edit in 83% of matched pairs with unchanged answers, against 70% for the strongest baseline.

我们还构建了 SEL-Pairs，这是一个包含来自三个问答数据集的长度匹配证据和对照删除的基准测试。在五个阅读模型上进行的定量和定性实验表明，SELD 在各种压缩器、压缩率和模型中始终优于强大且最新的基线，在答案未改变的匹配对中，SELD 识别出证据删除编辑的比例为 83%，而最强基线的比例为 70%。

These results show that evidence loss in compressed contexts can be detected even when the answer is unchanged.

这些结果表明，即使答案未改变，压缩上下文中的证据丢失也是可以被检测到的。
