### Jensen Huang accused of having another affair — with an Nvidia exec

英伟达（Nvidia）首席执行官黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）此前已因承认与一名被指控为中国间谍的女性有染而受到调查。如今，《纽约时报》报道称，黄仁勋还被怀疑与公司的一名直属下属——英伟达医疗保健副总裁金伯利·鲍威尔（Kimberly Powell）——存在另一段婚外情。

Powell, like Huang, is married, the outlet says. The Times says it was told of the alleged affair by five employees at the company. The sources said that Huang and Powell were captured on video “being affectionate at a San Jose, Calif., hotel” during a conference earlier this year.

据该媒体称，鲍威尔和黄仁勋一样，均已婚。报道称，《纽约时报》从公司五名员工处获悉了这段所谓的婚外情。消息人士表示，今年早些时候在加利福尼亚州圣何塞举行的一次会议期间，黄仁勋和鲍威尔被视频拍到在一家酒店内“举止亲昵”。

Like many companies, Nvidia has rules discouraging superiors from having relationships with employees in subordinate roles, the Times notes. TechCrunch reached out to Nvidia for comment. The company’s board has initiated an investigation into Huang’s publicly admitted affair.

《纽约时报》指出，和许多公司一样，英伟达有相关规定，不鼓励上级与下属员工建立恋爱关系。TechCrunch已联系英伟达寻求置评。公司董事会已针对黄仁勋公开承认的婚外情展开调查。

Additionally, two House Republicans said Wednesday that Congress is gearing up to subpoena Huang over the spy allegations if he doesn’t voluntarily respond to their summons to a closed-door briefing. Late Wednesday, after the reports of the second affair were published, the Wall Street Journal reported that the Nvidia board was preparing to oust Huang, who co-founded the company in 1993.

此外，两名众议院共和党人周三表示，如果黄仁勋不主动回应他们关于闭门简报会的传唤，国会准备就间谍指控向他发出传票。周三晚间，在第二段婚外情的报道发布后，《华尔街日报》报道称，英伟达董事会正准备罢免于1993年共同创立该公司的黄仁勋。
