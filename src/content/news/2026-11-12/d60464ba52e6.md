Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.12904 (cs) [Submitted on 14 Oct 2026] Title: Actionable Feedback: Improving Model Capabilities from User Edit Data on Clinical Documentation Authors: Daniel P. Jeong, Yewon Byun, John Giorgi, Peng Xu, Zachary C. Lipton, Michael Oberst.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.12904 (cs) [2026年10月14日提交] 标题：可操作的反馈：利用临床文档的用户编辑数据改进模型能力 作者：Daniel P. Jeong, Yewon Byun, John Giorgi, Peng Xu, Zachary C. Lipton, Michael Oberst。

Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly being deployed in clinical documentation workflows, where clinicians review and edit their outputs before use. In principle, these clinician edits represent a scalable source of supervision for model training. In practice, not all edits represent actionable feedback: for instance, on data drawn from real-world deployment of an AI clinical documentation product, our review suggests that over 45% of edited outputs contain user-added information that is not directly supported by the inputs to the model. Naively fine-tuning on such edits risks teaching the model to hallucinate.

摘要：大型语言模型（LLMs）正越来越多地被部署在临床文档工作流程中，临床医生会在使用前审查并编辑其输出内容。原则上，这些临床医生的编辑为模型训练提供了一种可扩展的监督来源。但在实践中，并非所有编辑都代表可操作的反馈：例如，根据从某人工智能临床文档产品的实际部署中提取的数据，我们的审查表明，超过45%的编辑后输出包含用户添加的信息，而这些信息并未得到模型输入内容的直接支持。盲目地对这些编辑进行微调，可能会导致模型产生幻觉。

In this paper, we develop a method to address this challenge. Given that real clinician edits on deployed documentation tools are not publicly available, we first develop a taxonomy of clinician-editing behaviors, informed by data from a deployed AI clinical documentation product. Then, we develop a method for generating semi-synthetic clinician-edit data, informed by our taxonomy, on a discharge-summary generation task based on MIMIC-III, a public dataset. Finally, we introduce simple filtering methods for identifying actionable edits, with accuracy evaluated by expert review.

在本文中，我们开发了一种方法来应对这一挑战。鉴于已部署文档工具上的真实临床医生编辑数据并未公开，我们首先根据已部署的人工智能临床文档产品的数据，建立了一套临床医生编辑行为分类法。随后，我们基于该分类法，在基于公共数据集MIMIC-III的出院小结生成任务上，开发了一种生成半合成临床医生编辑数据的方法。最后，我们引入了简单的过滤方法来识别可操作的编辑，并由专家评审评估其准确性。

We show empirically, on our semi-synthetic dataset, that naive fine-tuning on raw edited output increases ungrounded content, while our approach of progressively filtering toward grounded edits enables improvement on clinician-alignment metrics while retaining faithfulness to the input context.

我们在半合成数据集上的实证结果表明，对原始编辑输出进行盲目微调会增加无根据的内容，而我们采用的逐步过滤至有根据编辑的方法，能够在保持对输入上下文忠实度的同时，提升临床医生对齐指标。
