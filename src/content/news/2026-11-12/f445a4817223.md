Title: China’s Nvidia Honeypot Isn’t Surprising. Jensen Huang Fell for It Anyway

There’s a specific mantra drilled into government workers, military officers, and intelligence personnel during counterintelligence briefings: “If she’s a 10 and you’re a 5, she’s a spy!” Or, as the US Army’s Counterintelligence Command posted on Valentine’s Day: “It doesn’t take a mathematician to figure out ‘10 + 5 = honeytrap.’” The very beautiful woman who is suddenly interested in your very ordinary existence probably has ulterior motives, is the message.

在反间谍简报中，政府工作人员、军官和情报人员被灌输着一条特定的准则：“如果她是10分，而你是5分，那她就是间谍！”或者正如美国陆军反间谍司令部在情人节发布的那样：“不需要数学家也能算出‘10 + 5 = 蜜罐陷阱’。”其传达的信息是：那个突然对你平凡生活产生兴趣的美丽女性，很可能别有用心。

Jensen Huang surely doesn’t live a typical life—he’s a centibillionaire, the swashbuckling CEO of Nvidia, one of the hottest and most geopolitically consequential companies of the 21st century. Surely he’s aware of the possibility that he’d cross paths with intelligence assets. In fact, it’s hard to think of many companies in the world that would be a higher priority target for foreign intelligence agencies.

黄仁勋显然过着非同寻常的生活——他是亿万富翁，是英伟达（Nvidia）这位叱咤风云的首席执行官，而英伟达是21世纪最热门、地缘政治影响最深远的公司之一。他肯定意识到自己可能会与情报人员产生交集。事实上，世界上很难找到比英伟达更受外国情报机构青睐的目标公司了。

All of that makes the stock-shaking news this week all the more surprising. How did Huang get embroiled in a spy scandal that mixes infidelity, international espionage, the Trump administration, and the economic behemoth of the multitrillion-dollar AI industry?

这一切使得本周震动股市的消息显得格外令人惊讶。黄仁勋是如何卷入一场涉及不忠、国际间谍活动、特朗普政府以及数万亿美元人工智能行业这一经济巨头的间谍丑闻的呢？

The story kicked off just before Halloween, with a six-byline story in The New York Times reporting that Huang was the target of a Chinese spy plot. Starting somewhere between 2024 and 2025, Huang allegedly had an affair with Alice Huang (no relation), a one-time executive at a Singapore-based cloud provider known as Megaspeed. US intelligence agencies warned top US officials that they believed she was a Chinese intelligence asset. So far, Nvidia’s board, which has launched its own investigation, says it has “no reason to believe that either national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised,” but that’s far from the only concern.

故事始于万圣节前夕，《纽约时报》刊登了一篇由六名记者署名的报道，称黄仁勋成为了中国间谍阴谋的目标。据称，在2024年至2025年间的某个时候，黄仁勋与新加坡云服务提供商Megaspeed的前高管爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang，两人无亲属关系）发生了婚外情。美国情报机构曾警告美国高层官员，认为她是中国的间谍资产。目前，英伟达董事会已启动内部调查，并表示“没有理由相信国家安全或英伟达的机密受到损害”，但这远非唯一的担忧。

As one would expect, China has denied any nefarious plot centered on Jensen Huang. A Chinese foreign ministry spokesperson on Monday said, “The narrative about so-called Chinese spies is entirely based on groundless speculation—it’s nothing more than slander and smearing.”

不出所料，中国否认了任何针对黄仁勋的邪恶阴谋。中国外交部发言人周一表示：“所谓中国间谍的叙事完全是基于毫无根据的猜测，这不过是诽谤和抹黑。”

But according to reporting by The New York Times and the Financial Times, US intelligence officials also claim that Alice Huang, also known as Huang Le, was reporting to China’s Ministry of State Security intelligence agency. On Monday, Megaspeed said on LinkedIn that US authorities found “no evidence of any violations concerning the illicit transfer of chips or any illegal activities." (There’s no indication so far that Alice Huang, a one-time Chinese state journalist, is an employee or “officer” of Chinese intelligence; she’s believed to be an “asset,” an outsider who aids an intelligence agency, sometimes for pay, sometimes not.)

但据《纽约时报》和《金融时报》报道，美国情报官员还声称，爱丽丝·黄（又名黄乐）曾向中国国家安全部情报机构汇报工作。周一，Megaspeed在领英（LinkedIn）上表示，美国当局“没有发现任何关于非法转移芯片或任何非法活动的违规证据”。（目前没有迹象表明曾任中国国家媒体记者的爱丽丝·黄是中国情报机构的雇员或“官员”；她被认为是一名“资产”，即协助情报机构的外部人员，有时有偿，有时无偿。）

China, though, has elevated economic espionage to a core pillar of its economic and military development, stealing the designs for everything from US fighter jets to solar panels and commercial paint recipes. “Honeypots” or “honey traps”—positioning an alluring operative to seduce or develop a relationship with a key target—are one of intelligence’s most infamous and well-established strategies. French officials have warned specifically about Chinese honeypots since at least 2011, when intelligence reports cited a pharmaceutical researcher who was seduced and then blackmailed by a Chinese spy operation.

然而，中国已将经济间谍活动提升为经济和军事发展的核心支柱，窃取了从美国战斗机到太阳能电池板以及商业油漆配方等各种设计。“蜜罐”或“蜜罐陷阱”——即安排一名极具吸引力的特工去诱惑或与关键目标建立关系——是情报界最臭名昭著且行之有效的策略之一。法国官员至少从2011年起就专门针对中国的“蜜罐”发出过警告，当时的情报报告提到了一名被中国间谍行动诱惑并随后遭到勒索的制药研究员。

Over the past quarter of a century, as Silicon Valley emerged as a center of political and economic power, it has become a key focus of Chinese intelligence. In one plot, a Chinese national named Fang Fang spent years trying to cozy up to Bay Area politicians, including then US Representative Eric Swalwell. (While intelligence officials at the time reportedly believed Fang to be an operative, law enforcement officials never brought any charges against her). When traveling to China, US business executives have become accustomed to leaving their regular phones and computers at home and using burner devices to prevent the installation of spyware or malware (as I did, when I traveled to China for WIRED in 2019 to report on Huawei). In 2012, when Leon Panetta was making his first trip to China as defense secretary, he and his team were advised to bathe and shower in the dark in the event his hotel room was bugged. Last year, the threat of honeypots prompted the US government to explicitly ban personnel working in China and Hong Kong from engaging in romantic or sexual relationships with Chinese citizens. More recently, US officials joined with allies to warn about China’s increasingly sophisticated “LinkedIn espionage”: using the business-focused social networking site to recruit and build relationships with unwitting executives.

在过去的四分之一个世纪里，随着硅谷成为政治和经济权力的中心，它已成为中国情报机构的重点关注对象。在一次阴谋中，一名叫方芳的中国公民花了数年时间试图讨好湾区的政治人物，包括时任美国众议员埃里克·斯沃韦尔。（尽管当时情报官员据称认为方芳是一名特工，但执法部门从未对她提出任何指控）。在前往中国旅行时，美国企业高管已习惯将常用的手机和电脑留在家里，改用一次性设备，以防止被安装间谍软件或恶意软件（正如我2019年为《连线》杂志报道华为前往中国时所做的那样）。2012年，当莱昂·帕内塔以国防部长身份首次访华时，他和他的团队被建议在黑暗中洗澡，以防酒店房间被安装窃听器。去年，蜜罐的威胁促使美国政府明确禁止在中国和香港工作的人员与中国公民建立恋爱或性关系。最近，美国官员与盟友一道，对中国日益复杂的“领英间谍活动”发出警告：利用这个以商业为导向的社交网站来招募不知情的企业高管并与他们建立关系。

Alice Huang’s Megaspeed, for its part, is a major Pacific region player in cloud services focused on the advanced computer chips that are the heart of Nvidia’s business. The Biden administration had sharply limited the export of cutting-edge chips globally to slow China’s development of AI, but Jensen Huang has successfully lobbied the Trump administration to loosen those restrictions.

爱丽丝·黄所在的Megaspeed是太平洋地区云服务的主要参与者，专注于英伟达业务核心的先进计算机芯片。拜登政府曾大幅限制尖端芯片的全球出口以减缓中国人工智能的发展，但黄仁勋已成功游说特朗普政府放宽了这些限制。

Speaking at a press conference on Monday, Donald Trump denied having prior knowledge of Alice Huang’s potential espionage. “I didn’t hear about it then, but a lot of people have heard about it, and Jensen totally denies that,” Trump said. “I asked him that question: ‘Did you have any idea that she was a spy?’ He said, ‘I had no idea that she was a spy.’” On Tuesday, the Times reported that Marco Rubio had briefed Trump in May, right before Jensen Huang joined the president on Air Force One for a trip to China.

唐纳德·特朗普在周一的新闻发布会上否认事先知晓爱丽丝·黄可能存在的间谍活动。“我当时没听说过，但很多人都听说过，而黄仁勋完全否认了这一点，”特朗普说。“我问过他那个问题：‘你知不知道她是间谍？’他说，‘我根本不知道她是间谍。’”周二，《纽约时报》报道称，马可·卢比奥曾在5月份向特朗普作过简报，就在黄仁勋随总统乘坐“空军一号”前往中国之前。

The veracity of Trump's claims will surely be investigated, especially in the context of the famous Watergate maxim: “What did the president know and when did he know it?” Nvidia’s CEO has been publicly linked to the Megaspeed executive since at least 2024 and Alice’s husband, Kang Weigh, has reportedly known Jensen since the 2010s.
