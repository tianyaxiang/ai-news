# SEO Priorities for 2027: Why Earned Authority Matters More Than Link Volume
# 2027年SEO优先事项：为何“赢得的权威性”比外链数量更重要

SEO success in 2027 may depend less on accumulating backlinks and more on building earned authority that search and AI systems can recognize across the web. Search Engine Land's SEO priorities for 2027 guide argues that being discoverable is no longer enough. Brands increasingly need to be understood, trusted, and cited across conventional search results, AI answer engines, and agentic AI experiences.
2027年的SEO成功可能不再仅仅取决于积累反向链接，而更多地取决于建立起能够被网络搜索和人工智能系统识别的“赢得的权威性”（Earned Authority）。Search Engine Land的《2027年SEO优先事项指南》指出，仅仅被发现已不再足够。品牌越来越需要被传统搜索结果、AI问答引擎以及代理式AI体验所理解、信任并引用。

That changes the practical goal of off-site SEO. A link from a credible publication can still be valuable, but the guidance places greater weight on the broader evidence behind it: original stories, expert commentary, consistent brand information, topical depth, and unlinked mentions in authoritative places. For businesses, this is a shift from treating link volume as a headline metric to treating visibility as the outcome of a credible, coordinated presence.
这改变了站外SEO的实际目标。来自权威出版物的链接固然仍有价值，但该指南更看重其背后的广泛证据：原创报道、专家评论、一致的品牌信息、主题深度以及在权威平台上的无链接提及。对于企业而言，这意味着从将“外链数量”视为核心指标，转向将“可见度”视为可信且协调的品牌形象所带来的结果。

### From backlink acquisition to authority signals
### 从反向链接获取到权威性信号

The central distinction is not that backlinks have become irrelevant. Rather, Search Engine Land's guidance suggests that link count alone is an incomplete measure of authority in an environment where AI systems synthesize information from multiple sources. A reputable mention without a hyperlink may still help establish that a brand is a recognized source or participant in its category.
核心区别并非反向链接变得无关紧要。相反，Search Engine Land的指南认为，在AI系统从多个来源综合信息的环境下，仅凭链接数量已不足以衡量权威性。一次没有超链接的权威提及，依然有助于确立品牌在其领域内作为公认来源或参与者的地位。

The publication also cites industry analysis indicating that branded web mentions correlate more strongly with AI Overview visibility than traditional backlinks. Correlation does not prove that a mention directly causes visibility. Still, the finding supports a practical conclusion: companies should pay attention to the places where their name, people, products, and expertise are discussed, not only to the links pointing to their sites.
该刊物还引用行业分析指出，品牌在网络上的提及与“AI概览”（AI Overview）可见度之间的相关性，比传统反向链接更强。相关性并不证明提及直接导致了可见度，但这一发现支持了一个实际结论：企业不仅应关注指向其网站的链接，还应关注其名称、人员、产品和专业知识被讨论的地方。

### SEO focus
### SEO 重点

| Approach | Link-volume approach | Earned-authority approach |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Primary objective** | Acquire more backlinks | Earn trusted citations and recognition across relevant surfaces |
| **Off-site signals** | Hyperlinked referring pages | Links, authoritative brand mentions, expert commentary, and original stories |
| **Content role** | Create pages that can attract links | Build comprehensive topic and entity-based content clusters |
| **Measurement** | Backlink volume and rankings | Brand mentions, AI referrals, visibility, and authority signals alongside rankings |

| 方法 | 外链数量导向 | 赢得的权威性导向 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **主要目标** | 获取更多反向链接 | 在相关平台获得可信引用与认可 |
| **站外信号** | 带超链接的引用页面 | 链接、权威品牌提及、专家评论及原创报道 |
| **内容角色** | 创建能吸引链接的页面 | 构建基于主题和实体的综合内容集群 |
| **衡量指标** | 反向链接数量与排名 | 品牌提及、AI引流、可见度及权威性信号（结合排名） |

This distinction should also discourage low-quality link schemes. They may add to a backlink total without creating the trust, relevance, or real-world recognition that earned authority requires. Digital PR, useful proprietary information, and credible external contributions are harder to produce, but they create assets that can support search visibility, reputation, and audience trust at the same time.
这种区别也应抑制低质量的链接方案。它们或许能增加外链总数，却无法建立“赢得的权威性”所必需的信任、相关性或现实世界的认可。数字公关、有用的专有信息和可信的外部贡献虽然制作难度更高，但它们能同时支持搜索可见度、声誉和受众信任。

### What businesses should change in content, PR, and reporting
### 企业在内容、公关和报告方面应做出的改变

A more authority-led strategy starts with work that is genuinely worth citing. Original research is one route, but it is not the only one. A company can also contribute informed commentary based on direct experience, publish useful analysis from its own operations, or explain a complex topic with enough depth to become a reliable reference. The priority is to create evidence of expertise rather than to publish content solely because it targets a keyword.
以权威性为导向的策略始于真正值得引用的内容。原创研究是一条途径，但并非唯一途径。企业还可以基于直接经验贡献有见地的评论，发布来自自身运营的有用分析，或以足够的深度解释复杂主题，从而成为可靠的参考资料。优先事项是创造专业知识的证据，而不是仅仅为了针对关键词而发布内容。

Search Engine Land recommends building topical authority through comprehensive clusters organized around topics and entities. In practice, that means a business should make its core subject areas clear, connect related pages meaningfully, and ensure that its expertise, authors, products, and brand are represented consistently.
Search Engine Land建议通过围绕主题和实体组织的综合集群来建立主题权威性。在实践中，这意味着企业应明确其核心主题领域，有意义地连接相关页面，并确保其专业知识、作者、产品和品牌得到一致的呈现。

The publication also highlights E-E-A-T signals: experience, expertise, authoritativeness, and trust. These are not a shortcut or a single ranking factor to optimize in isolation. They are a useful framework for assessing whether a site makes clear who is responsible for its claims, why that source has relevant experience, and whether readers can trust the information.
该刊物还强调了E-E-A-T信号：经验（Experience）、专业性（Expertise）、权威性（Authoritativeness）和信任度（Trust）。这些不是捷径，也不是可以孤立优化的单一排名因素。它们是一个有用的框架，用于评估网站是否明确了谁对其声明负责、该来源为何具备相关经验，以及读者是否可以信任这些信息。

For a lean marketing team, the most practical changes are often organizational rather than technical:
对于精简的营销团队来说，最实际的改变往往是组织层面的，而非技术层面的：

*   **Coordinate SEO and PR** so original content, expert insights, and media outreach reinforce the same priority topics.
    **协调SEO与公关**，使原创内容、专家见解和媒体外联能够强化相同的优先主题。
*   **Turn internal expertise into citable material**, such as research findings, customer-pattern analysis, or informed commentary with clear limits and evidence.
    **将内部专业知识转化为可引用的材料**，例如研究发现、客户模式分析或带有明确界限和证据的专业评论。
*   **Maintain brand consistency** across owned channels and external profiles, since the guidance treats consistency as an important authority signal.
    **保持品牌一致性**，覆盖自有渠道和外部资料，因为该指南将一致性视为重要的权威性信号。
*   **Audit AI mentions of the brand** to understand how AI systems describe the company, its products, and its area of expertise.
    **审计AI对品牌的提及**，以了解AI系统如何描述公司、产品及其专业领域。
*   **Track AI-referred sessions** in analytics alongside organic search traffic, referral traffic, rankings, and qualified outcomes.
    **在分析工具中追踪AI引流会话**，并将其与自然搜索流量、引荐流量、排名和转化成果一并考量。

Measurement needs to follow the same broader view. Ranking position and link growth remain useful diagnostics, but neither tells the full story if discovery takes place through AI-generated answers or through unlinked brand citations. Teams should connect off-site visibility to outcomes that matter, including relevant referral traffic, branded demand, leads, or sales where their analytics setup supports that attribution.
衡量指标也需要遵循同样的广阔视角。排名位置和链接增长仍然是有用的诊断指标，但如果发现过程是通过AI生成的答案或无链接的品牌引用发生的，那么这两者都无法说明全部情况。团队应将站外可见度与重要的业务成果联系起来，包括相关的引荐流量、品牌需求、潜在客户或销售额（前提是分析设置支持此类归因）。

There is also an important restraint here. Businesses should not pursue mentions in every possible publication or use AI visibility as a promise of immediate commercial results. The more defensible approach is to focus on sources, topics, and commentary that are genuinely relevant to the audience a business wants to reach. Authority is earned through repeated evidence and credible recognition, not through a one-off campaign.
这里还有一个重要的限制。企业不应追求在所有可能的出版物中被提及，也不应将AI可见度视为即时商业成果的保证。更稳妥的方法是专注于与目标受众真正相关的来源、主题和评论。权威性是通过反复的证据和可信的认可赢得的，而不是通过一次性的活动。
