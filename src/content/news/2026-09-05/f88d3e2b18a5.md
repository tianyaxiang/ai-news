# Tail-Likelihood Reinforcement Learning

**Abstract:** Reinforcement learning typically optimizes average reward. For generative policies, the average can hide an important distinction: two policies can achieve the same mean reward while having very different chances of producing a rare but high-reward rollout. This matters as sampling increases during training and inference, since its benefit depends on retaining probability mass on high-reward outcomes.

**摘要：** 强化学习通常优化的是平均奖励。对于生成式策略而言，平均值往往会掩盖一个重要的区别：两个策略可能达到相同的平均奖励，但在产生罕见但高奖励的执行序列（rollout）方面却有着截然不同的概率。这一点至关重要，因为随着训练和推理过程中采样量的增加，其收益取决于能否在高奖励结果上保留概率质量。

We propose to optimize this coverage directly. Rather than considering only expected reward, we consider all of its upper tails: for each reward threshold, how likely is the policy to exceed it? This turns a continuous reward into a family of binary success events.

我们提出直接优化这种覆盖范围。与其仅考虑期望奖励，我们考虑了其所有的上尾（upper tails）：对于每一个奖励阈值，策略超过该阈值的可能性有多大？这实际上将连续的奖励转化为了一系列二元成功事件。

We introduce Tail-Likelihood Reinforcement Learning (TailRL), which maximizes the log-probability of exceeding a randomly chosen reward threshold. Its gradient gives more weight to rare, high-reward rollouts and can be interpreted as a mixture of Best-of-(k) gradients. TailRL requires only a simple modification to the advantage function, making it compatible with existing reinforcement learning pipelines.

我们引入了尾部似然强化学习（Tail-Likelihood Reinforcement Learning, TailRL），它通过最大化超过随机选择的奖励阈值的对数概率来工作。其梯度为罕见的高奖励执行序列赋予了更高的权重，并可被解释为“Best-of-(k)”梯度的一种混合。TailRL 仅需对优势函数（advantage function）进行简单的修改，使其能够兼容现有的强化学习流程。

Across object localization, maze navigation, GUI grounding, and code optimization, TailRL leverages rare high-reward training samples to avoid suboptimal solutions and yields models that benefit more from additional samples at inference time.

在物体定位、迷宫导航、GUI 基础任务和代码优化等任务中，TailRL 利用罕见的高奖励训练样本来避免次优解，并产出在推理阶段能从额外采样中获得更多收益的模型。
