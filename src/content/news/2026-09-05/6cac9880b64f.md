# WireSeg-32K: A Physics-Grounded Synthetic Dataset for Wire Instance Segmentation
# WireSeg-32K：用于线缆实例分割的物理驱动合成数据集

Deformable linear objects such as wires and cables are difficult to segment because they are thin, highly deformable, and frequently self-occluded, while large-scale instance-level annotations are expensive to obtain in real scenes. Existing resources either focus on cable tracing or semantic segmentation under constrained settings, or generate visually plausible images without physically grounded wire deformation.
线缆等可变形线性物体由于其细长、高度可变形且频繁发生自遮挡的特性，难以进行分割，而在真实场景中获取大规模的实例级标注成本高昂。现有的资源要么侧重于受限环境下的线缆追踪或语义分割，要么生成的图像虽然视觉上合理，却缺乏基于物理规律的线缆形变。

We present WireSeg-32k, a synthetic dataset for wire instance segmentation with 32,000 RGB images, instance masks, depth maps, and a complementary real-world test set with annotations. To generate this dataset, we develop DeformX, a co-simulation pipeline that couples Cosserat-rod dynamics with photorealistic Isaac Sim rendering, enabling physically plausible, contact-consistent wire shapes, CAD-based wire assets, and diverse visually grounded scenes.
我们提出了 WireSeg-32k，这是一个用于线缆实例分割的合成数据集，包含 32,000 张 RGB 图像、实例掩码、深度图，以及一个带有标注的补充性真实世界测试集。为了生成该数据集，我们开发了 DeformX，这是一个将 Cosserat 杆动力学与逼真的 Isaac Sim 渲染相结合的联合仿真流水线，能够实现物理上合理且接触一致的线缆形状、基于 CAD 的线缆资产以及多样化的视觉场景。

As a simple baseline, LoRA fine-tuning SAM3 on WireSeg-32k alone improves real-world mAP@75 by 10.2% over the off-the-shelf model, showing that physically grounded synthetic data can transfer to real wire perception.
作为一个简单的基准测试，仅在 WireSeg-32k 上对 SAM3 进行 LoRA 微调，其真实场景下的 mAP@75 指标较现成模型提升了 10.2%，这表明物理驱动的合成数据可以迁移到真实的线缆感知任务中。
