# The Geometry of Ignorance: LLMs Know When to Temper Bayesian Priors
# 无知的几何学：大语言模型懂得何时调节贝叶斯先验

**Abstract:** What does a language model predict when it has few clues? The answer lurks in its unembedding geometry: a single direction of the unembedding matrix encodes the unigram distribution of the training corpus, which serves as the Bayesian prior the model falls back on when uncertain. 

**摘要：** 当语言模型线索不足时，它会预测什么？答案隐藏在其反嵌入（unembedding）的几何结构中：反嵌入矩阵中的一个特定方向编码了训练语料库的一元模型（unigram）分布，这正是模型在不确定时所依赖的贝叶斯先验。

This structure --- which we term the *direction of ignorance* --- appears in all four model families examined (\texttt{Llama}, \texttt{Qwen}, \texttt{Gemma}, and \texttt{Pythia}), ranging from 0.4B to 405B parameters. Projecting the final prediction state onto this direction yields a per-token *prior loading factor* $\lambda$, which, empirically, declines steadily as the context becomes more informative. 

这种结构——我们将其称为“无知方向”（direction of ignorance）——存在于所有四个被研究的模型家族（Llama、Qwen、Gemma 和 Pythia）中，参数规模从 4 亿到 4050 亿不等。将最终预测状态投影到该方向上，可以得到每个 token 的“先验加载因子” $\lambda$。经验表明，随着上下文信息量的增加，该因子会稳步下降。

Formally, the same projection decomposes the prediction state into two orthogonal vectors that correspond exactly to the two factors of a tempered Bayesian update: a unigram prior raised to the exponent $\lambda$ and a context-driven likelihood. This geometric-probabilistic interpretation calibrates $\lambda$, making it meaningfully comparable across model sizes and families, with larger models generally exhibiting lower prior reliance in the high-context limit. 

从形式上讲，这一投影将预测状态分解为两个正交向量，它们恰好对应于调节后贝叶斯更新的两个因子：以 $\lambda$ 为指数的一元先验，以及由上下文驱动的似然函数。这种几何-概率解释校准了 $\lambda$，使其在不同模型规模和家族之间具有可比性；在上下文信息充足的情况下，更大的模型通常表现出对先验的更低依赖。

Finally, we show that the direction of ignorance is causally active: raising or lowering $\lambda$ at the final prediction state steers the prediction toward or away from the unigram prior in KL 散度 (KL divergence).

最后，我们证明了“无知方向”具有因果活性：在最终预测状态下提高或降低 $\lambda$，可以在 KL 散度上引导预测结果向一元先验靠拢或远离。
