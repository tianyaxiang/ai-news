# Nintendo’s next two Direct events are both happening next week
# 任天堂下周将连续举办两场直面会

Nintendo just announced two Direct events for next week, scheduled back to back. First up is the Legend of Zelda 40th Anniversary Direct on September 8th at 10AM ET / 7AM PT. It’s set to last around 30 minutes, streaming live on Nintendo’s YouTube channel or the Nintendo Today app.
任天堂刚刚宣布将于下周连续举办两场直面会。首场是定于美东时间 9 月 8 日上午 10 点（太平洋时间上午 7 点）举行的《塞尔达传说》40 周年纪念直面会。该活动预计持续约 30 分钟，届时将在任天堂 YouTube 频道或“Nintendo Today”应用程序上进行直播。

Images recently leaked showing a special-edition 40th anniversary Zelda Switch 2 console and controller, plus there’s the Ocarina of Time remake that’s on the way, as well as the live-action Zelda movie expected to hit theaters sometime next year.
近期泄露的图片展示了一款 40 周年纪念版塞尔达主题 Switch 2 主机及手柄。此外，备受期待的《时之笛》重制版也在开发中，而真人版《塞尔达传说》电影预计将于明年某个时间上映。

The next day, Nintendo is also running a regular Direct showcase at 10AM ET / 7AM PT that will run for around 45 minutes, focusing on Switch 2 games that will launch this winter, followed by a Nintendo Treehouse livestream with gameplay. These events usually include a preview of upcoming Nintendo Classics library additions too, but there’s no word on which games will be featured. 次日，任天堂还将举办一场常规直面会，时间同样为美东时间上午 10 点（太平洋时间上午 7 点），时长约 45 分钟。本次活动将聚焦于今年冬季发售的 Switch 2 游戏，随后还将进行“任天堂树屋”（Nintendo Treehouse）实机演示直播。此类活动通常还会预告任天堂经典游戏库的新增内容，但目前尚不清楚具体会包含哪些游戏。鉴于 Switch 2 的价格上涨了 50 美元，你认为哪些游戏能够支撑起这个更高的定价呢？
