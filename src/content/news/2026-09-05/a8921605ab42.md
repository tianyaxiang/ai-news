# Equation Recast for Canonical Operator Learning Across Parametric PDEs
# 方程重构：跨参数偏微分方程的规范算子学习

**Abstract:** Learning solution operators across broad parameter ranges can require substantial coverage of both input functions and physical parameters, particularly for purely data-driven parametric models. In addition, the resulting models may fail silently outside the training distribution.

**摘要：** 在广泛的参数范围内学习解算子，通常需要对输入函数和物理参数进行大量的覆盖，对于纯数据驱动的参数化模型尤其如此。此外，所得模型在训练分布之外可能会出现“静默失效”（即在没有预警的情况下产生错误结果）。

We introduce equation recast, which reformulates parametric operator learning as the learning of a single canonical operator. Parameter-induced operator variations are derived analytically from the governing equation and absorbed into effective sources, enabling zero-shot prediction across new parameter regimes.

我们引入了“方程重构”（equation recast）方法，将参数化算子学习重新表述为单一规范算子的学习。由参数引起的算子变化通过控制方程进行解析推导，并被吸收到有效源项中，从而实现了跨新参数范围的零样本预测。

Across multi-parameter, nonlinear, and singular PDE settings, equation recast supports extrapolation, integrates sparse heterogeneous datasets in a shared canonical representation, and uses loss of convergence as an internal warning signal for failure of the recast iteration.

在多参数、非线性及奇异偏微分方程（PDE）场景中，方程重构支持外推，能够在共享的规范表示中整合稀疏的异构数据集，并利用收敛损失作为重构迭代失败的内部预警信号。

In high-fidelity tokamak simulations for nuclear fusion, the framework unifies electron-temperature data across four device geometries through canonical-domain mapping within one jointly trained operator. Equation recast provides a route toward reusable neural PDE solvers combining equation-guided transfer, data efficiency, and monitorable inference.

在核聚变的托卡马克高保真模拟中，该框架通过单一联合训练算子内的规范域映射，统一了四种不同设备几何结构下的电子温度数据。方程重构为构建可复用的神经偏微分方程求解器提供了一条新途径，结合了方程引导的迁移学习、数据高效性以及可监控的推理过程。
