# Mesh-Native Physics-Informed Graph Surrogates for TCAD-in-the-Loop Design Space Exploration
## 用于 TCAD 闭环设计空间探索的网格原生物理信息图代理模型

**Abstract:** High-fidelity TCAD simulation of drift-diffusion transport remains the workhorse of emerging FinFET device design, but it is computationally expensive, especially for 3D structures where runtime escalates steeply with mesh complexity. This sharply limits multi-objective design space exploration.

**摘要：** 高保真漂移-扩散输运 TCAD 仿真仍然是新兴 FinFET 器件设计的核心工具，但其计算成本高昂，尤其是在 3D 结构中，运行时间会随着网格复杂度的增加而急剧上升。这严重限制了多目标设计空间的探索。

Existing machine-learning surrogates map a fixed set of design parameters to a few scalar device metrics, discarding the underlying physics and losing transferability across device geometries and families. A physics-informed graph attention network (GAT) surrogate is proposed. It operates directly on the tetrahedral TCAD mesh and predicts, at every mesh node, the electrostatic potential together with the electron and hole quasi-Fermi levels, the fundamental unknowns of the drift-diffusion system.

现有的机器学习代理模型通常将一组固定的设计参数映射为少数几个标量器件指标，这不仅丢弃了底层物理特性，还导致了在不同器件几何结构和系列之间的可迁移性丧失。本文提出了一种物理信息图注意力网络（GAT）代理模型。它直接在四面体 TCAD 网格上运行，并在每个网格节点上预测静电势以及电子和空穴准费米能级，这些正是漂移-扩散系统的基本未知量。

Training combines a data loss with finite-volume current-continuity residuals, embedding carrier-transport physics into the objective. Operating on the mesh as a graph, the surrogate inherits size generalization: a model trained on few-fin meshes applies unchanged to substantially larger arrays, bounded at inference only by GPU memory.

训练过程结合了数据损失和有限体积电流连续性残差，将载流子输运物理特性嵌入到目标函数中。由于将网格视为图进行操作，该代理模型具备了尺寸泛化能力：在少量鳍片网格上训练的模型可以直接应用于规模大得多的阵列，推理时的限制仅取决于 GPU 内存。

Per-node uncertainty from a deep ensemble drives an active-learning loop that screens large candidate pools in seconds and forwards only the most informative designs for full simulation. Benchmarked against Sentaurus Device on multi-fin tri-gate FinFETs, the surrogate reproduces the three drift-diffusion fields with sub-volt per-field RMSE and reaches a per-design throughput orders of magnitude higher than the full simulator.

通过深度集成（Deep Ensemble）获得的节点级不确定性驱动了一个主动学习循环，该循环能在几秒钟内筛选出庞大的候选池，并仅将最具信息量的设计提交进行全量仿真。在多鳍片三栅极 FinFET 上与 Sentaurus Device 进行基准测试对比显示，该代理模型能够以亚伏特级的场 RMSE 重现三个漂移-扩散场，且单次设计的吞吐量比全量仿真器高出几个数量级。

The advantage grows with device size: on large multi-fin arrays that are prohibitively slow to simulate directly, inference still completes in under a second per device, enabling Pareto-front exploration across device scales infeasible for direct TCAD sweeps.

这种优势随着器件尺寸的增加而愈发显著：在直接仿真速度极慢的大型多鳍片阵列上，该模型仍能在每个器件不到一秒的时间内完成推理，从而实现了跨器件尺度的帕累托前沿（Pareto-front）探索，这是直接进行 TCAD 扫描所无法实现的。
