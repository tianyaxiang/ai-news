### What to Look For in a Mac Monitor
### 选购 Mac 显示器时应注意什么

Just about any monitor can work with a Mac. It doesn’t need to be made by Apple or have any official certification. There’s a case to be made for using a cheap 1080p monitor, but most Mac users will want something a bit more premium. As you can see from options like the Dell 27 Plus 4K, that doesn’t have to mean overly expensive. Either way, here are the four elements to consider when shopping for a good monitor to go with your Mac.

几乎任何显示器都能与 Mac 配合使用。它不需要由苹果制造，也不需要任何官方认证。虽然使用廉价的 1080p 显示器也是一种选择，但大多数 Mac 用户会想要更高品质的产品。正如 Dell 27 Plus 4K 等选项所示，高品质并不意味着价格昂贵。无论如何，在为 Mac 选购显示器时，有四个要素需要考虑。

**Size and resolution:** 27- and 32-inch monitors are the most common sizes these days, but there are larger options. I would also consider a 34-inch ultrawide monitor if you like the wider 21:9 aspect ratio with the curved shape. With Apple, resolution is king. There's a reason it invests so much in high pixel density for every screen it sells, even in midrange options like the MacBook Air. Pixel density is what gives a screen its sharpness, and you need a lot more pixels when they're stretched across a large external monitor. If you want to keep fidelity up, I wouldn’t buy anything less than 4K, and bumping up to 5K or 6K on a 32-inch monitor can be helpful. You should also consider the refresh rate: a 120-Hz refresh rate is what the MacBook Pro supports, offering smoother animation, especially in games.

**尺寸与分辨率：** 27 英寸和 32 英寸是目前最常见的显示器尺寸，当然也有更大的选择。如果你喜欢 21:9 的宽屏比例和曲面设计，我也会推荐 34 英寸的超宽屏显示器。对于苹果设备而言，分辨率至关重要。苹果之所以在其销售的每一块屏幕（即使是 MacBook Air 这样的中端产品）上投入大量资金以实现高像素密度，是有原因的。像素密度决定了屏幕的清晰度，当像素被拉伸到大型外接显示器上时，你需要更多的像素。如果你想保持画质，建议至少选择 4K 分辨率；对于 32 英寸显示器，提升至 5K 或 6K 会更有帮助。此外，你还应考虑刷新率：MacBook Pro 支持 120Hz 刷新率，这能提供更流畅的动画效果，尤其是在游戏中。

**Adjustability:** Apple monitors and iMacs aren’t exactly known for adjustability. They often have none at all, and cost more when they do. That isn’t the best for your posture and ergonomics. Famously, the discontinued Pro Display XDR charged an extra $1,000 for a Pro Stand with proper adjustability (now a $400 add-on for the newer Studio Display XDR). For ergonomics, the top of the screen you're working on should be as close to eye level as possible, and that ranges depending on someone’s height. If a monitor doesn’t have height adjustability, you’ll need a separate monitor stand or arm. Beyond height adjustment, many monitors also have stands that can swivel, tilt, and rotate, all of which matter when using multiple monitors. This is also needed if you want to use a second monitor vertically, which has become increasingly popular.

**可调节性：** 苹果的显示器和 iMac 一向以“不可调节”著称。它们通常完全无法调节，即便可以，价格也会更高。这对你的坐姿和人体工学并不友好。众所周知，已停产的 Pro Display XDR 若要配备具备适当调节功能的 Pro Stand，需额外支付 1,000 美元（现在新款 Studio Display XDR 的支架选配价格为 400 美元）。从人体工学角度来看，屏幕顶部应尽可能与视线齐平，具体高度因人而异。如果显示器本身不支持高度调节，你需要额外购买显示器支架或悬臂。除了高度调节外，许多显示器还支持旋转、倾斜和横竖屏切换，这些功能在使用多显示器时非常重要。如果你想将第二台显示器竖屏使用（这已变得越来越流行），这些功能更是必不可少。

**Ports:** Even the cheapest monitors will always have HDMI, which is all you need to connect directly to a Mac Mini, Mac Studio, Mac Pro, or MacBook Pro. Some monitors have USB-C that supports video, which will let you connect to one of a Mac’s Thunderbolt ports. The Mac Mini comes with three Thunderbolt 4 ports, HDMI, and an Ethernet jack. The Mac Mini Pro model has the same ports, except three of them support faster Thunderbolt 5 instead of 4. You’ll need to use at least one of these Thunderbolt ports to connect more than one external monitor. These monitors also tend to have other ports, such as USB-A. These can be useful, as the Mac Mini doesn't have any on its own.

**接口：** 即使是最便宜的显示器通常也配备 HDMI 接口，这足以直接连接 Mac Mini、Mac Studio、Mac Pro 或 MacBook Pro。一些显示器配备支持视频传输的 USB-C 接口，这让你能够连接到 Mac 的雷雳（Thunderbolt）接口。Mac Mini 配备了三个雷雳 4 接口、一个 HDMI 接口和一个以太网口。Mac Mini Pro 机型接口相同，但其中三个接口支持更快的雷雳 5 标准。如果你需要连接多台外接显示器，至少需要使用其中一个雷雳接口。这些显示器通常还带有其他接口，如 USB-A，这非常有用，因为 Mac Mini 本身并不提供此类接口。

**Image quality:** Apple prides itself on the image quality of its Macs, so in the case of the Mac Mini, you’ll likely want to get something worthy of your Mac. This is especially important for content creators, photographers, and designers. So, consider a monitor’s brightness, color accuracy, color coverage, and contrast. While some IPS displays offer decent color and contrast, mini-LED or OLED displays will guarantee better image quality. These also have significantly higher peak brightness in HDR content, which really brings games and movies to life.

**图像质量：** 苹果以其 Mac 设备的图像质量为傲，因此对于 Mac Mini，你可能希望选择一台与之匹配的显示器。这对内容创作者、摄影师和设计师尤为重要。因此，请考虑显示器的亮度、色彩准确度、色域覆盖和对比度。虽然一些 IPS 显示器能提供不错的色彩和对比度，但 Mini-LED 或 OLED 显示器能保证更好的画质。它们在 HDR 内容中也具有显著更高的峰值亮度，能让游戏和电影画面更加生动。

### How Many Monitors Can a Mac Mini Support?
### Mac Mini 最多支持几台显示器？

The latest Mac Mini with the M6 chip can support up to three external displays, using the three Thunderbolt ports or one HDMI port on the back. Regardless of the resolution (up to 8K) or refresh rate (up to 240 Hz), if you're just plugging in a single monitor, any one of these ports will do. Use whichever your monitor has or whichever is the most convenient. But when you need multiple displays, that's where things get complicated.

搭载 M6 芯片的最新款 Mac Mini 最多可支持三台外接显示器，通过背部的三个雷雳接口或一个 HDMI 接口连接。无论分辨率（最高 8K）或刷新率（最高 240Hz）如何，如果你只连接一台显示器，使用上述任何一个接口均可。根据你显示器支持的接口或最方便的方式选择即可。但当你需要连接多台显示器时，情况就会变得复杂。
