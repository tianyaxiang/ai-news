# Gmail to end support for "Send as" for third-party addresses, such as @yahoo.com
# Gmail 将停止支持使用第三方地址（如 @yahoo.com）进行“代发邮件”

**Send emails from a different address or alias**
**使用其他地址或别名发送邮件**

Important: Starting January 2027, Gmail will no longer support the "Send as" feature for third-party email addresses, such as @yahoo.com or @outlook.com. This change does not affect Google Workspace aliases or other Gmail addresses you own. Learn about changes to third-party email account support in Gmail.
重要提示：自 2027 年 1 月起，Gmail 将不再支持使用第三方电子邮件地址（如 @yahoo.com 或 @outlook.com）的“代发邮件”(Send as) 功能。此更改不会影响 Google Workspace 别名或您拥有的其他 Gmail 地址。了解有关 Gmail 中第三方电子邮件账户支持变更的更多信息。

If you own another email address, you can send mail as that address. For example: Yahoo, Outlook, or other non-Gmail address; Your work, school, or business domain or alias, like @yourschool.edu or youralias@gmail.com; Another Gmail address.
如果您拥有其他电子邮件地址，则可以以该地址的名义发送邮件。例如：Yahoo、Outlook 或其他非 Gmail 地址；您的工作、学校或企业域名或别名（如 @yourschool.edu 或 youralias@gmail.com）；其他 Gmail 地址。

Tip: You can send emails from up to 99 different email addresses.
提示：您最多可以使用 99 个不同的电子邮件地址发送邮件。

**Step 1: Add an address you own**
**第 1 步：添加您拥有的地址**

On your computer, open Gmail. In the top right, click Settings > See all settings. Click the Accounts and import or Accounts tab. In the "Send mail as" section, click Add another email address. Enter your name and the address you want to send from. Click Next Step > Send verification. For school or work accounts, enter the SMTP server (for example, smtp.gmail.com or smtp.yourschool.edu) and the username and password on that account. Click Add Account.
在电脑上打开 Gmail。点击右上角的“设置” > “查看所有设置”。点击“账号和导入”或“账号”标签页。在“代发邮件”部分，点击“添加其他电子邮件地址”。输入您的姓名以及您想要用于发送邮件的地址。点击“下一步” > “发送验证”。对于学校或工作账号，请输入 SMTP 服务器（例如 smtp.gmail.com 或 smtp.yourschool.edu）以及该账号的用户名和密码。点击“添加账号”。

**Step 2: Confirm the address**
**第 2 步：确认地址**

Sign in to the account you added. Open the confirmation message you got from Gmail. Click the link.
登录您添加的账号。打开您从 Gmail 收到的确认邮件。点击其中的链接。

**Step 3: Change the "From" address**
**第 3 步：更改“发件人”地址**

In the message, click the "From" line. (If you don't see this, click the space next to the recipient's email.) Select the address to send from.
在邮件撰写界面，点击“发件人”行。（如果您没看到此选项，请点击收件人邮箱旁边的空白处。）选择要用于发送邮件的地址。

**I can't find my confirmation email**
**我找不到确认邮件**

Check your Spam or Bulk Mail folders for a message from send-as-noreply@google.com. If you're trying to add your work or school account, ask your administrator to configure your domain alias and email alias.
请检查您的“垃圾邮件”或“批量邮件”文件夹，查看是否有来自 send-as-noreply@google.com 的邮件。如果您尝试添加工作或学校账号，请联系您的管理员配置域名别名和电子邮件别名。

**My recipients see my Gmail address**
**收件人看到了我的 Gmail 地址**

If your recipient uses Outlook or another mail service, they might see something like, "From yourname@gmail.com on behalf of othername@otherdomain.com." Your recipients might also see your original @gmail.com address if you: Set up an out of office reply; Create a filter with automated response; Have a full mailbox, and your recipient gets notified.
如果您的收件人使用 Outlook 或其他邮件服务，他们可能会看到类似“发件人：yourname@gmail.com，代发人：othername@otherdomain.com”的信息。如果出现以下情况，收件人也可能会看到您原始的 @gmail.com 地址：设置了外出回复；创建了带有自动回复的过滤器；邮箱已满且收件人收到了通知。

**Remove an email address or alias**
**移除电子邮件地址或别名**

If you don’t want to send emails from an alias or if the alias is invalid, you can remove the email or alias. If you send an email from an invalid alias, you get a bounce email. On your computer, open Gmail. At the top right, click Settings > See all settings. Click the Accounts and import tab or Accounts tab. In the "Send mail as" section, next to the email address you want to remove, click Delete.
如果您不想再使用某个别名发送邮件，或者该别名无效，您可以将其移除。如果您使用无效的别名发送邮件，将会收到退信。在电脑上打开 Gmail。点击右上角的“设置” > “查看所有设置”。点击“账号和导入”或“账号”标签页。在“代发邮件”部分，点击您要移除的电子邮件地址旁边的“删除”。

Tip: If you get a bounce email from a valid email address and need to reverify it, you must delete the "Send mail as" email address then add it again.
提示：如果您从一个有效的电子邮件地址收到退信并需要重新验证，您必须先删除该“代发邮件”地址，然后再重新添加。

**Always send from a different address**
**始终使用其他地址发送邮件**

If you want to always send from your other address, you'll need to change both your default "From" and "reply-to" address. If you only change the "From" address, replies will go to your original Gmail address by default.
如果您希望始终使用其他地址发送邮件，则需要同时更改默认的“发件人”和“回复至”地址。如果您只更改了“发件人”地址，回复邮件默认仍会发送到您原始的 Gmail 地址。

**Change default "From" address**
**更改默认“发件人”地址**

To always send email from a different address or alias: On your computer, open Gmail. In the top right, click Settings > See all settings. Click the Accounts and import or Accounts tab. In the "Send mail as" section to the right of the address you want to use, click Make default.
要始终使用其他地址或别名发送邮件：在电脑上打开 Gmail。点击右上角的“设置” > “查看所有设置”。点击“账号和导入”或“账号”标签页。在“代发邮件”部分，点击您想要使用的地址右侧的“设为默认值”。

**Change default "reply-to" address**
**更改默认“回复至”地址**

When you send a message, replies will go to your original Gmail address by default. To choose a different address, follow these steps: On your computer, open Gmail. In the top right, click Settings > See all settings. Click the Accounts and Import or Accounts tab. In the "Send mail as" section, click Edit info next to your email address. Click Specify a different "reply to" address. Add a reply-to address. If necessary, click Next Step. Click Save Changes.
发送邮件时，回复默认会发送到您原始的 Gmail 地址。要选择其他地址，请按照以下步骤操作：在电脑上打开 Gmail。点击右上角的“设置” > “查看所有设置”。点击“账号和导入”或“账号”标签页。在“代发邮件”部分，点击您的电子邮件地址旁边的“编辑信息”。点击“指定不同的‘回复至’地址”。添加一个“回复至”地址。如有必要，点击“下一步”。点击“保存更改”。

**"Couldn't reach server," "TLS Negotiation failed" error**
**“无法连接到服务器”、“TLS 协商失败”错误**

If you see one of these error messages, you might need to select a different port number and authentication type. Make sure you have used the correct outgoing mail server for your provider. You might need to contact your third party email provider for the correct settings.
如果您看到这些错误消息之一，您可能需要选择不同的端口号和身份验证类型。请确保您使用了正确的服务提供商发件服务器。您可能需要联系您的第三方电子邮件服务提供商以获取正确的设置。

**Select a secured connection**
**选择安全连接**

Check with your other mail service for their recommended port number, authentication type, or outgoing mail server. Make sure your third party provider supports SSL or TLS with a valid certificate. Here are some common combinations: SSL with port 465; TLS with port 25 or 587.
请向您的其他邮件服务商咨询其推荐的端口号、身份验证类型或发件服务器。确保您的第三方服务提供商支持带有有效证书的 SSL 或 TLS。以下是一些常见的组合：SSL 使用端口 465；TLS 使用端口 25 或 587。

**My other mail service doesn't support SSL or TLS**
**我的其他邮件服务不支持 SSL 或 TLS**

We recommend you send your email over a secure (encrypted) connection. However, if your other mail service doesn't support these secured connections or doesn't use a valid certificate, you can choose port 25, then select the Unsecured connection option that appears. If you do this, your information won't be encrypted for your protection.
我们建议您通过安全（加密）连接发送电子邮件。但是，如果您的其他邮件服务不支持这些安全连接或不使用有效证书，您可以选择端口 25，然后选择出现的“不安全连接”选项。如果这样做，您的信息将不会被加密保护。

**Use Gmail aliases**
**使用 Gmail 别名**

Filter using your Gmail alias: An easy way to sort your email is to add categories after your username. For example, messages sent to the following aliases will all go to janedoe@gmail.com: janedoe+school@gmail.com, janedoe+notes@gmail.com, janedoe+important.emails@gmail.com.
使用 Gmail 别名进行过滤：整理电子邮件的一个简单方法是在用户名后添加类别。例如，发送到以下别名的邮件都会进入 janedoe@gmail.com：janedoe+school@gmail.com、janedoe+notes@gmail.com、janedoe+important.emails@gmail.com。

**Step 1: Choose aliases**
**第 1 步：选择别名**

Think of how you want to sort your email, then choose an alias for each category. For example: Use yourname+work@gmail.com for work emails; Use yourname+news@gmail.com to sign up for newsletters; Use yourname+shopping@gmail.com to create an account with an online retailer.
考虑您希望如何整理邮件，然后为每个类别选择一个别名。例如：使用 yourname+work@gmail.com 处理工作邮件；使用 yourname+news@gmail.com 订阅新闻通讯；使用 yourname+shopping@gmail.com 在在线零售商处创建账户。

**Step 2: Filter your messages**
**第 2 步：过滤您的邮件**

Create filters to take automatic actions, like: Adding a label or a star; Forwarding to another account; Archiving or deleting.
创建过滤器以执行自动操作，例如：添加标签或星标；转发到其他账号；归档或删除。

**Send from a work or school group alias**
**使用工作或学校群组别名发送邮件**

If you use Gmail with your work or school account, you can send from a group alias. To receive the verification email to send from a group alias, you need to give delegates access to the group.
如果您使用工作或学校账号使用 Gmail，则可以使用群组别名发送邮件。要接收用于群组别名发送邮件的验证邮件，您需要授予代理人访问该群组的权限。

Important: Aliases aren’t private and sometimes they’re visible to others. For example, if you search Gmail for messages from bill@school.edu, you might find messages from alias@school.edu. You'll need access to the group's permissions to change access for other members.
重要提示：别名并非私密，有时会被他人看到。例如，如果您在 Gmail 中搜索来自 bill@school.edu 的邮件，可能会找到来自 alias@school.edu 的邮件。您需要拥有群组权限才能更改其他成员的访问权限。
