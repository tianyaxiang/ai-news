# Solving the Needle-in-a-Haystack Problem in Mammography Vision-Language Model with Differentiable Subset Sampling
# 利用可微子集采样解决乳腺钼靶视觉语言模型中的“大海捞针”问题

**Abstract:** There is growing interest in adopting CLIP-style vision-language model (VLM) pretraining for mammography. However, models that directly employ the standard CLIP architecture and training objective exhibit limited zero-shot performance in clinically important tasks such as cancer, finding-type, and BI-RADS predictions. We argue that this underwhelming performance is due to neglecting two characteristics of mammography data: (1) its high-res nature, and (2) homogeneity of radiology reports, largely driven by a predominance of negative/benign findings on examinations.

**摘要：** 目前，采用 CLIP 风格的视觉语言模型（VLM）预训练技术应用于乳腺钼靶影像的兴趣日益浓厚。然而，直接使用标准 CLIP 架构和训练目标函数的模型，在癌症检测、病灶类型识别以及 BI-RADS 分类等临床重要任务中，零样本（zero-shot）表现有限。我们认为，这种不尽如人意的表现源于忽视了乳腺钼靶数据的两个特征：（1）高分辨率特性；（2）放射科报告的同质性，这主要是由于检查中绝大多数结果为阴性或良性所致。

We propose TopKSigLIP, a VLM designed to address these two limitations through a novel architecture and learning objectives. Instead of downscaling high-res mammography images to satisfy GPU memory constraints, TopKSigLIP introduces TopK-Patch module that learns to sample a sparse set of high-res patches likely to contain lesions, sidestepping the resolution-batch size tradeoff of VLM training. The sampled patch locations additionally serve as a built-in localization tool.

我们提出了 TopKSigLIP，这是一种旨在通过新颖架构和学习目标来解决上述两个局限性的 VLM。TopKSigLIP 没有为了满足 GPU 内存限制而对高分辨率乳腺钼靶图像进行降采样，而是引入了 TopK-Patch 模块，该模块能够学习采样出一组可能包含病灶的稀疏高分辨率切片，从而规避了 VLM 训练中分辨率与批次大小之间的权衡问题。采样出的切片位置还可以作为内置的定位工具。

To address report homogeneity, we replace the contrastive loss, which falsely repels semantically similar pairs, with a Sup-sigmoid loss. Sup-sigmoid loss extends the sigmoid loss from SigLIP with soft labels derived from structured data. TopKSigLIP outperforms existing open-source mammography and general medical VLMs on both internal and external benchmarks on density assessment, BI-RADS classification, finding subtyping, and cancer prediction under zero-shot evaluation.

为了解决报告同质性问题，我们用 Sup-sigmoid 损失函数取代了对比损失函数（后者会错误地排斥语义相似的配对）。Sup-sigmoid 损失函数扩展了 SigLIP 中的 sigmoid 损失，并结合了从结构化数据中导出的软标签。在零样本评估中，TopKSigLIP 在密度评估、BI-RADS 分类、病灶亚型分析和癌症预测等内部及外部基准测试中，均优于现有的开源乳腺钼靶及通用医学 VLM。

TopKSigLIP remains competitive under linear probing despite using a significantly smaller vision encoder and smaller training batches than baselines. The TopK-Patch module additionally achieves superior lesion localization over post-hoc Grad-CAM. Code and weights are made public.

尽管 TopKSigLIP 使用了比基准模型显著更小的视觉编码器和更小的训练批次，但在线性探测（linear probing）下依然保持了极强的竞争力。此外，TopK-Patch 模块在病灶定位方面也实现了优于事后 Grad-CAM 的表现。代码和权重已公开。
