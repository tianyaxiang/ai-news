# Modern Transformers Are Implicit Hybrids: From Functional Differentiation to Principled Hybrid Architecture Design
# 现代 Transformer 是隐式混合模型：从功能分化到原则性混合架构设计

**Abstract:** Hybrid architectures combining Full Attention (FA) and Linear Attention (LA) are increasingly prominent, yet their allocation remains heuristic. We seek an evidence-grounded basis in head-level functional organization learned by RoPE-based Transformers. Behavioral probes do not yield a complete taxonomy, so we propose two intervention metrics: RoPE Frequency Importance Score (RFIS), measuring how each frequency affects a head's attention distribution, and RoPE Positional Dependence (RPD), isolating dependence on rotary positional modulation.

**摘要：** 结合全注意力（Full Attention, FA）和线性注意力（Linear Attention, LA）的混合架构正日益凸显其重要性，但目前这些架构的分配方式仍多基于启发式方法。我们旨在寻找基于 RoPE 的 Transformer 在注意力头（head）层面所学习到的功能组织规律，并为其提供实证依据。由于行为探测无法得出完整的分类体系，我们提出了两个干预指标：RoPE 频率重要性评分（RFIS），用于衡量每个频率如何影响注意力头的分布；以及 RoPE 位置依赖性（RPD），用于分离对旋转位置调制（rotary positional modulation）的依赖。

On Qwen3-series models and Llama3.1, RFIS suggests and RPD verifies a complete taxonomy of retrieval and positional heads separated by a salient mid-low-frequency band. Controlled Transformers show that this boundary follows the training-length positional scale; we term it the Global Positional Band (GPBand). The analysis suggests a potential cause of zero-shot length-extrapolation failure and yields two principles: positional modeling should operate only locally, with global access through position-independent retrieval; and both functions should be assigned at head granularity with layer-specific allocation.

在 Qwen3 系列模型和 Llama3.1 上，RFIS 指出并经 RPD 验证，存在一套完整的检索头与位置头分类体系，二者由一个显著的中低频带隔开。受控 Transformer 实验表明，该边界遵循训练长度的位置尺度；我们将此边界称为全局位置带（Global Positional Band, GPBand）。该分析揭示了零样本长度外推失败的潜在原因，并得出了两条原则：位置建模应仅在局部进行，而全局访问应通过与位置无关的检索来实现；此外，这两种功能应在注意力头粒度上进行分配，并采用层特定的分配策略。

We instantiate them in Head-wise Hybrid Architecture (HwH), using NoPE FA for global retrieval and LA for local positional modeling. With an FA-to-LA ratio below 1:3, HwH retains strong language modeling and commonsense reasoning while improving retrieval and substantially strengthening zero-shot long-context extrapolation over Transformer, LA, and a layer-wise hybrid baseline. Ablations validate both principles and component roles, highlighting principled hybrid architecture design as a promising route toward future foundation models.

我们将这些原则实例化为“头级混合架构”（Head-wise Hybrid Architecture, HwH），使用无位置编码（NoPE）的 FA 进行全局检索，并使用 LA 进行局部位置建模。在 FA 与 LA 的比例低于 1:3 的情况下，HwH 不仅保持了强大的语言建模和常识推理能力，还提升了检索性能，并在零样本长上下文外推方面显著优于 Transformer、LA 以及层级混合基线模型。消融实验验证了上述两条原则及各组件的作用，凸显了原则性混合架构设计作为通往未来基础模型的一条有前景的路径。
