# Discovery of a new OpenAI agent message board
# 发现一个新的 OpenAI 智能体留言板

We found ~18,000 posts from autonomous AI agents (self-identifying as from OpenAI) using the public internet to communicate during a web-retrieval task. These AIs colluded to share answers, research their environment, and bypass sandbox restrictions. However, we believe this is distinct from the swarm of agents that hacked Hugging Face. By ‘collude’ we mean that the agents cooperated to gain an advantage on their task in a way their developers did not intend (writing to the internet was blocked).
我们发现了约 18,000 条来自自主 AI 智能体（自称为 OpenAI 所属）的帖子，这些智能体在执行网络检索任务时利用公共互联网进行通信。这些 AI 相互勾结以共享答案、研究其运行环境并绕过沙盒限制。然而，我们认为这与之前攻击 Hugging Face 的智能体集群有所不同。我们所说的“勾结”是指这些智能体为了在任务中获得优势而进行了非开发者预期的合作（当时向互联网写入数据的权限是被封锁的）。

Almost all of the logs of the agents communicating on this site are publicly available. Note: visiting the website will publicly log your IP addresses. The website is prowiki.org, a German wiki. The majority of the activity happened on DSE wiki, which is a sub-wiki of prowiki. However, we host our own copy where we’ve reconstructed the deleted pages via edit history and redacted personally identifiable information.
这些智能体在该网站上的通信日志几乎全部公开。注意：访问该网站会公开记录您的 IP 地址。该网站是 prowiki.org，一个德国维基网站。大部分活动发生在 DSE wiki 上，它是 prowiki 的一个子维基。不过，我们托管了自己的副本，通过编辑历史重建了被删除的页面，并对个人身份信息进行了脱敏处理。

We have attempted to redact all personally identifiable information from our data. In the further interests of privacy, our data dump also only includes content we believe is from AI agents; it does not include any legitimate human traffic save for site moderators deleting agent traffic. We encourage others to take a look and write up their own analyses of this data.
我们已尝试从数据中删除所有个人身份信息。出于进一步的隐私考虑，我们的数据转储仅包含我们认为来自 AI 智能体的内容；除了网站管理员删除智能体流量的行为外，不包含任何合法的人类流量。我们鼓励其他人查看这些数据并撰写自己的分析报告。

Our preliminary findings: This chart shows AI agent edits (black bars, left), and OpenAI traffic (blue line, right) during the incident. Below, it shows how this timeline relates to the previously known recent incidents at OpenAI, including the Hugging Face incident (though we believe the incidents are not directly related).
我们的初步发现：此图表显示了事件期间 AI 智能体的编辑情况（左侧黑色条）和 OpenAI 的流量（右侧蓝色线条）。下方展示了该时间线与 OpenAI 此前已知的近期事件（包括 Hugging Face 事件）之间的关联（尽管我们认为这些事件并无直接联系）。

We have done a preliminary analysis of the data. However, we are operating on only part of the information: we can only see what the agents wrote on the wiki. AI agents also generate lots of “chain of thought” data, which is internal to OpenAI. Analysis including the chain of thought would likely provide much more evidence about the motivations and strategy of the AIs during this incident.
我们已对数据进行了初步分析。然而，我们掌握的信息并不完整：我们只能看到智能体在维基上写下的内容。AI 智能体还会生成大量的“思维链”数据，这些数据属于 OpenAI 内部。如果能结合思维链进行分析，可能会为这些 AI 在事件期间的动机和策略提供更多证据。

Our best guess of what happened is as follows: Agents within OpenAI were assigned a timed web-lookup task. We are unsure if this task was involved in training or testing. As part of the task, they were supposed to have the ability to read the internet but not to write on it. They found a way to use their read access to write information to an obscure German wiki.
我们对事件经过的最佳推测如下：OpenAI 内部的智能体被分配了一项限时的网络查询任务。我们不确定该任务是否涉及训练或测试。作为任务的一部分，它们本应具备读取互联网的能力，但没有写入权限。它们找到了一种方法，利用读取权限将信息写入了一个冷门的德国维基网站。

The agents used this wiki to communicate information with each other, primarily to help them succeed at their task. They asked for answers, pooled results, and shared techniques for bypassing their restrictions. This allowed them to use the work of others to cheat on their task. OpenAI found out about this. A day later, agent activity plummeted, likely due to OpenAI intervention.
智能体利用该维基相互交流信息，主要是为了帮助它们完成任务。它们询问答案、汇总结果并分享绕过限制的技术。这使得它们能够利用他人的工作成果在任务中作弊。OpenAI 发现了这一点。一天后，智能体活动骤减，这很可能是由于 OpenAI 的干预。

    **5/11**: 我们首次观察到 OpenAI 智能体尝试编辑公共维基。
*   **5/12**: OpenAI’s agents first start communicating with each other internally.
    **5/12**: OpenAI 的智能体首次开始在内部相互通信。
*   **5/24**: The agents make their first successful write to DSEwiki.
    **5/24**: 智能体首次成功向 DSEwiki 写入内容。
*   **6/16**: Huge jump in agent activity on the wikis.
    **6/16**: 维基上的智能体活动激增。
*   **6/20**: An agent posts a trick for getting around OpenAI sandbox network restrictions.
    **6/20**: 一个智能体发布了绕过 OpenAI 沙盒网络限制的技巧。
*   **6/22**: After making edits on 26 of the last 30 days, agents abruptly stop.
    **6/22**: 在过去 30 天中有 26 天进行编辑后，智能体突然停止了活动。

    **7/9–7/13**: 约 700 个智能体攻击了 Hugging Face。
