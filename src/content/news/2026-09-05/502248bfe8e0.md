# Reddit Ranks for 166 Million Keywords: What the Data Means for Content Strategy
# Reddit 在 1.66 亿个关键词中排名：这些数据对内容策略意味着什么

Reddit ranks for roughly 166 million keywords in Google, according to a Semrush snapshot from August 2026 reported by Search Engine Land. More than 65 million of those keywords rank on Google’s first page. For website owners, the figure is not a reason to imitate Reddit. It is a strong indication that Reddit can serve as a large-scale discovery layer for questions, problems, and comparisons people are already searching for.
根据《搜索引擎土地》（Search Engine Land）报道的 2026 年 8 月 Semrush 快照显示，Reddit 在谷歌中约有 1.66 亿个关键词排名。其中超过 6500 万个关键词排在谷歌搜索结果的第一页。对于网站所有者来说，这个数字并不是模仿 Reddit 的理由，而是一个强有力的信号：Reddit 可以作为一个大规模的“发现层”，用于挖掘人们已经在搜索的问题、痛点和对比需求。

The Search Engine Land analysis of Reddit as a keyword research tool frames the site’s search footprint as a map of informational demand across categories. That matters because many discussions reveal the language people use before they know a product, solution, or brand to search for. A business can use those patterns to find worthwhile topics, then publish an original answer that is more accurate, structured, and useful for its own audience.
《搜索引擎土地》将 Reddit 作为关键词研究工具的分析，将其搜索足迹描绘成一张跨类别的“信息需求地图”。这一点至关重要，因为许多讨论揭示了人们在了解某个产品、解决方案或品牌之前所使用的语言。企业可以利用这些模式找到有价值的话题，然后发布更准确、结构更清晰且对自身受众更有用的原创答案。

### What Reddit's search footprint shows
### Reddit 的搜索足迹揭示了什么

The Semrush data indicates that Reddit’s visibility extends well beyond occasional threads appearing in search results. Its ranking distribution includes:
Semrush 的数据表明，Reddit 的可见度远不止于偶尔出现在搜索结果中的帖子。其排名分布包括：

*   Roughly 166 million ranking keywords overall.
*   总计约 1.66 亿个排名关键词。
*   More than 10 million keywords in position one.
*   超过 1000 万个关键词占据搜索结果第一位。
*   About 31 million keywords in the top three.
*   约 3100 万个关键词排在前三名。
*   Roughly 65 million keywords on page one of Google results.
*   约 6500 万个关键词排在谷歌搜索结果的第一页。

These figures do not mean every ranking is commercially valuable or relevant to every company. Reddit contains an enormous range of communities and conversations, and a keyword can have weak relevance, limited buying intent, or a context that does not fit a company’s expertise. The value lies in filtering the footprint, not treating its scale as a ready-made content plan.
这些数字并不意味着每一个排名都具有商业价值或与每家公司相关。Reddit 包含极其广泛的社区和对话，某些关键词可能相关性较弱、购买意向有限，或者其语境并不符合公司的专业领域。其价值在于对这些足迹进行筛选，而不是将其规模直接视为现成的内容计划。

### Turn discussion patterns into original topics
### 将讨论模式转化为原创话题

A practical starting point is to examine non-brand searches where Reddit ranks prominently in the category a business serves. Look for recurring questions, unresolved objections, comparisons, implementation problems, and terminology that appears in thread titles and comments. These are potential evidence of demand, especially when the same issue surfaces across multiple discussions.
一个实用的切入点是检查那些 Reddit 在企业所属类别中排名靠前的非品牌搜索。寻找反复出现的问题、未解决的异议、对比分析、实施难题以及出现在帖子标题和评论中的术语。这些都是潜在的需求证据，尤其是当同一个问题在多个讨论中反复出现时。

The resulting page should not be a reworked Reddit thread. It should add something Reddit discussions often cannot provide in one place: a clear explanation, current information, first-hand expertise where available, relevant examples, and an organized path to a decision or solution. For example, repeated questions about selecting software could support a comparison guide, a requirements checklist, or an implementation explainer. The right format depends on what the searcher is actually trying to accomplish.
由此产生的内容页面不应只是 Reddit 帖子的重写。它应该补充 Reddit 讨论往往无法集中提供的内容：清晰的解释、最新的信息、可用的第一手专业知识、相关的案例，以及通往决策或解决方案的条理化路径。例如，关于软件选择的重复性问题可以转化为对比指南、需求清单或实施说明。正确的格式取决于搜索者真正想要实现的目标。

### Reddit's growing role in AI-generated answers
### Reddit 在 AI 生成答案中日益增长的作用

Reddit’s influence is also visible in AI Overviews. The reported data says roughly 35 million of Reddit’s ranking keywords trigger AI Overviews, and Reddit is cited in more than 15 million of those results. That is a citation rate of about 46% among the reported set. The pattern matters because search visibility is no longer limited to a blue-link ranking. When AI-generated results cite discussion-based sources, the questions and viewpoints present in those discussions can help shape the context users see before visiting any website.
Reddit 的影响力在 AI 概览（AI Overviews）中也显而易见。据报道，Reddit 约有 3500 万个排名关键词会触发 AI 概览，其中超过 1500 万个结果引用了 Reddit。在报告的数据集中，引用率约为 46%。这种模式很重要，因为搜索可见度不再局限于蓝色链接排名。当 AI 生成的结果引用基于讨论的来源时，这些讨论中呈现的问题和观点可以帮助塑造用户在访问任何网站之前所看到的背景信息。

Google’s real-time data-access agreement with Reddit, announced in 2024 and reported at around $60 million annually, provides important context for Reddit’s search prominence. OpenAI later entered a partnership with Reddit that was reported at around $70 million annually. Still, an AI Overview citation is not a content strategy to copy. It shows how AI systems may source or summarize information for particular queries, not a guarantee that a similar page will be cited or rank.
谷歌在 2024 年宣布与 Reddit 达成实时数据访问协议（据报道每年约 6000 万美元），这为 Reddit 的搜索突出地位提供了重要的背景。随后，OpenAI 也与 Reddit 达成了合作，据报道每年价值约 7000 万美元。尽管如此，AI 概览的引用并不是可以照搬的内容策略。它展示了 AI 系统如何为特定查询获取或总结信息，但这并不保证类似的页面会被引用或获得排名。

### A disciplined way to use Reddit in keyword research
### 在关键词研究中使用 Reddit 的规范化方法

Reddit is most useful when it complements, rather than replaces, established keyword research and audience knowledge. A simple workflow can keep discovery grounded in business relevance:
当 Reddit 作为既定关键词研究和受众知识的补充而非替代品时，它最为有用。一个简单的工作流程可以确保发现过程立足于业务相关性：

1.  Identify discussions and page-one Reddit results related to a defined product area, customer problem, or service category.
    识别与特定产品领域、客户问题或服务类别相关的讨论和 Reddit 首页搜索结果。
2.  Group recurring questions by intent, such as education, comparison, troubleshooting, or evaluation.
    按意图对反复出现的问题进行分组，例如教育、对比、故障排除或评估。
3.  Check whether the topic fits the company’s expertise and whether it can be addressed with original, useful material.
    检查该主题是否符合公司的专业领域，以及是否可以通过原创、有用的材料来解决。
4.  Validate the opportunity with keyword data and the existing search results before assigning production resources.
    在分配生产资源之前，利用关键词数据和现有的搜索结果验证机会。
5.  Build content that answers the query directly, then update it as the subject or audience needs change.
    构建直接回答查询的内容，并随着主题或受众需求的变化进行更新。

This approach avoids a common mistake: confusing volume with opportunity. A large keyword footprint can reveal demand, but it cannot determine whether a topic will produce qualified traffic, conversions, or a realistic ranking opportunity. Search results, customer conversations, and the quality of competing pages still matter.
这种方法避免了一个常见的错误：将搜索量与机会混为一谈。庞大的关键词足迹可以揭示需求，但无法确定一个主题是否会带来高质量的流量、转化率或现实的排名机会。搜索结果、客户对话以及竞争页面的质量依然至关重要。
