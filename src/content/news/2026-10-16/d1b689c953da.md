# Steam can now show game achievements in separate ‘sets’

Steam 现在可以将游戏成就显示为独立的“成就集”。

Developers with games on Steam can split achievements from a base game and its DLC into sets with their own progress bars.

在 Steam 上拥有游戏的开发者可以将基础游戏及其 DLC 的成就拆分为各自拥有进度条的成就集。

Valve is introducing a major new feature for Steam achievements called “achievement sets” that lets developers list different groups of achievements for a base game and DLCs.

Valve 正在为 Steam 成就引入一项名为“成就集”的重大新功能，允许开发者为基础游戏和 DLC 列出不同的成就分组。

“In games that support sets, you still unlock achievements by playing the game as usual,” Valve says. “But when you browse what you have unlocked afterward, Steam will break the long list of game achievements into the developer-created sets, making it easier to see what you have completed and to find other related achievements you can unlock next.”

Valve 表示：“在支持成就集的游戏中，你依然可以像往常一样通过游玩来解锁成就。但当你随后浏览已解锁内容时，Steam 会将长长的成就列表拆分为开发者创建的成就集，让你更容易查看已完成的内容，并找到接下来可以解锁的其他相关成就。”

Achievement sets have their own progress bars, and if you finish a set, you’ll get a “set complete” trophy that’s visible on your profile.

成就集拥有各自的进度条；如果你完成了一个成就集，你将获得一个可在个人资料中显示的“成就集完成”奖杯。

Steam will support seasonal achievement sets that developers can archive, too. “We think there are a lot of fun ways games can take advantage of the temporary nature this can add, such as adding achievements to season-based games or enabling time-limited holiday achievements,” Valve says.

Steam 还将支持开发者可以归档的季节性成就集。Valve 表示：“我们认为游戏可以通过多种有趣的方式利用这种临时性，例如为基于赛季的游戏添加成就，或启用限时的节日成就。”

The company is also refreshing the Steam Achievements page to show “a summary of your overall progress, progress in each achievement set, and details on individual achievements.”

该公司还在更新 Steam 成就页面，以显示“你整体进度的摘要、每个成就集的进度以及单个成就的详细信息”。

The new achievements features are available in the latest Steam client beta, Valve says.

Valve 表示，这些新的成就功能现已在最新的 Steam 客户端测试版中提供。
