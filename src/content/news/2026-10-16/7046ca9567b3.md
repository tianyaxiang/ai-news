Bluesky’s AI tool now lets you make games and apps to share on social

Bluesky 的 AI 工具现在允许你制作游戏和应用程序，以便在社交媒体上分享。

Attie launched earlier this year as a way to build your own feeds with AI, but Bluesky is expanding what it can do.

Attie 于今年早些时候推出，旨在通过 AI 构建你自己的信息流，但 Bluesky 正在扩展其功能。

Bluesky’s Attie AI tool, which initially launched earlier this year as a way to use AI to build your own feeds, now lets you make “small” social apps like games, polls, and dashboards that you can then share with others as their own websites. In an interview at TechCrunch Disrupt, Bluesky CEO Toni Schneider said to think of Attie as “social vibe coding or vibe coding for your community.”

Bluesky 的 Attie AI 工具最初于今年早些时候推出，用于利用 AI 构建个人信息流，现在它允许你制作诸如游戏、投票和仪表板之类的“小型”社交应用，并将其作为独立网站分享给他人。在 TechCrunch Disrupt 的一次采访中，Bluesky 首席执行官 Toni Schneider 表示，可以将 Attie 视为“社交氛围编程”或“为你所在的社区进行氛围编程”。

You can make your own Attie app by typing in a prompt. A simple poll I made about Pokémon Red and Pokémon Blue took a few minutes to generate. If you want to check out something more full-featured, the Attie website currently highlights a few examples like a “Flappy Butterfly” Flappy Bird clone, a drum machine, a quiz, and an idle clicker about a celery man, and each has a custom link on the attie.site domain and attribution for who made the social app at the bottom of the page. The apps I’ve messed around with are pretty lightweight novelties, though I liked the drum machine.

你可以通过输入提示词来制作自己的 Attie 应用。我制作的一个关于《宝可梦 红》和《宝可梦 蓝》的简单投票只用了几分钟就生成了。如果你想查看功能更全的内容，Attie 网站目前重点展示了一些示例，例如“Flappy Butterfly”（Flappy Bird 的克隆版）、鼓机、测验以及一个关于芹菜人的放置类点击游戏。每个应用在 attie.site 域名下都有一个自定义链接，页面底部还标注了社交应用的制作者。我尝试过的这些应用大多是相当轻量的新奇玩意，不过我挺喜欢那个鼓机的。
