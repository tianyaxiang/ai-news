Withings now charges $100 per year to sync your data to other apps.

Withings 现在开始收取每年 100 美元的费用，用于将您的数据同步到其他应用程序。

The feature will continue to be free for users who created a Withings account on or before October 12th, 2026.

对于在 2026 年 10 月 12 日或之前创建 Withings 账户的用户，该功能将继续免费提供。

If you’re a new user of Withings’ fitness-tracking smartwatches or smart scales and want to sync and access your health data through third party apps like Apple Health or Google Health it’s now going to cost you.

如果您是 Withings 健身追踪智能手表或智能秤的新用户，并希望通过 Apple Health 或 Google Health 等第三方应用程序同步和访问您的健康数据，现在需要付费。

According to a discussion on Withings’ support forums spotted by MacRumors, new Withings accounts created from October 13th, 2026, and onwards will require a Withings Plus subscription to use the “automatic sync to third-party apps” feature.

据 MacRumors 在 Withings 支持论坛上发现的讨论显示，2026 年 10 月 13 日及之后创建的新 Withings 账户，需要订阅 Withings Plus 才能使用“自动同步到第三方应用程序”功能。

The subscription costs $9.95 per month or $99.50 per year but Withings’ devices come with a free one month trial that includes the option to sync their data to third-party apps. When the trial is over the syncing is paused but the data in those apps isn’t deleted.

该订阅费用为每月 9.95 美元或每年 99.50 美元，但 Withings 设备附带一个月的免费试用期，其中包括将数据同步到第三方应用程序的选项。试用结束后，同步功能会暂停，但这些应用程序中的数据不会被删除。

Existing users who already have a Withings account created on or before October 12th, 2026, will continue to have access to automatic third-party app syncing for free, according to official comments shared by a member of Withings’ support team.

根据 Withings 支持团队成员分享的官方评论，在 2026 年 10 月 12 日或之前已创建 Withings 账户的现有用户，将继续免费享有自动同步第三方应用程序的功能。

And because the feature is tied to an existing users’ account and not a specific device, the third-party syncing will continue to be available without a subscription if they buy and register new watches, scales, or other Withings hardware.

由于该功能与现有用户的账户绑定，而非特定设备，因此如果他们购买并注册新的手表、秤或其他 Withings 硬件，无需订阅即可继续使用第三方同步功能。
