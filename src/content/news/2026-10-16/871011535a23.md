Google Antigravity provides an agentic science workbench that empowers biologists to discover, structurally evaluate, and prioritize promising targets quickly, compressing what used to be a fragmented search over multiple databases into a single session. It also helps researchers scan through millions of previously uncharacterized proteins in the UniProt database using ProtNLM, a model that predicts biological function from amino acid sequences.

Google Antigravity 提供了一个智能科学工作台，使生物学家能够快速发现、进行结构评估并确定有前景的靶点优先级，将以往分散在多个数据库中的搜索过程压缩到单一会话中。它还通过 ProtNLM（一种根据氨基酸序列预测生物功能的模型）帮助研究人员扫描 UniProt 数据库中数百万个此前未被表征的蛋白质。

Antimicrobial peptides (AMPs), particularly bacteriocins, offer a promising alternative to traditional antibiotics because they have naturally evolved to target fundamental vulnerabilities in competing bacteria, from cell envelopes to essential intracellular machinery, making it difficult for pathogens to develop resistance. The exponential growth of genomic and metagenomic sequencing has populated global databases with billions of natural amino acid sequences. This pool of natural data has already produced globally used bacteriocins like Nisin, alongside recent genome-mined breakthroughs such as Darobactin, Dynobactin, and Lariocidin.

抗菌肽（AMPs），特别是细菌素，为传统抗生素提供了一种有前景的替代方案，因为它们在自然进化中能够靶向竞争细菌的基本弱点，从细胞包膜到重要的细胞内机制，这使得病原体难以产生耐药性。基因组和宏基因组测序的指数级增长已使全球数据库中充斥着数十亿条天然氨基酸序列。这一天然数据池已经产生了像乳酸链球菌素（Nisin）这样全球通用的细菌素，以及近期通过基因组挖掘取得的突破性成果，如 Darobactin、Dynobactin 和 Lariocidin。

Many, however, remain locked within the unannotated "dark matter" of protein databases, with their function uncharacterized because they aren’t similar to known proteins. For example, the world's leading public resource of protein sequence and functional information, UniProt, holds millions of sequences with unknown functionality, transferred directly from genome sequencing projects. To bridge this gap, Google developed ProtNLM (Protein Natural Language Model), a deep learning model trained to directly read the underlying evolutionary “grammar” of amino acid sequences.

然而，许多蛋白质仍然被锁定在蛋白质数据库未注释的“暗物质”中，由于它们与已知蛋白质不相似，其功能尚未被表征。例如，全球领先的蛋白质序列和功能信息公共资源 UniProt 拥有数百万条功能未知的序列，这些序列直接来自基因组测序项目。为了弥补这一差距，Google 开发了 ProtNLM（蛋白质自然语言模型），这是一种经过训练的深度学习模型，可以直接读取氨基酸序列潜在的进化“语法”。

This allows the model to predict biological function directly from raw protein sequences, enriching millions of previously unnamed UniProt entries with high-confidence functional labels. Overview of the ProtNLM pipeline translating raw amino acid sequences into natural-language biological annotations for unreviewed UniProtKB/TrEMBL entries. The functional annotation scope of the system covers protein names, function comments, subcellular locations, and GO terms, in addition to keywords.

这使得该模型能够直接从原始蛋白质序列中预测生物功能，为数百万个此前未命名的 UniProt 条目提供了高置信度的功能标签。ProtNLM 流水线概述：将原始氨基酸序列转换为未经审查的 UniProtKB/TrEMBL 条目的自然语言生物学注释。该系统的功能注释范围涵盖蛋白质名称、功能注释、亚细胞位置和 GO 术语，以及关键词。

Collaborative intelligence. Scientific discovery is rarely a single step process; finding viable candidate proteins requires human intuition and iteration to incorporate new empirical evidence. Working in Antigravity, the researcher and AI agent begin by setting a focused biological goal: find and computationally validate novel AMPs in Streptococcus pseudopneumoniae. During an interactive, multi-turn session the researcher steers the scientific strategy, deciding which questions to ask and what evidence to collect, while the agent handles the heavy lifting of cross-database orchestration, structural analysis, and literature synthesis.

协作智能。科学发现很少是一个单一的步骤；寻找可行的候选蛋白质需要人类的直觉和迭代来整合新的经验证据。在 Antigravity 中工作时，研究人员和 AI 智能体首先设定一个明确的生物学目标：在假肺炎链球菌（Streptococcus pseudopneumoniae）中寻找并计算验证新型抗菌肽。在交互式的多轮会话中，研究人员引导科学策略，决定提出哪些问题以及收集哪些证据，而智能体则负责处理跨数据库编排、结构分析和文献综合等繁重工作。
