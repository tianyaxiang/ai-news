# Apps to distract you from the endless cycle of doomscrolling

You open your phone to check the time or a quick message. The next thing you know, an hour has passed and you’ve scrolled through endless celebrity drama, cat videos, awful news stories, influencer rants, and whatever else the algorithm decided to throw at you. Even though you probably don’t want to keep wasting your time and energy on this mind-numbing content, you do it again the next day.

你打开手机只是为了看时间或回一条简短的消息。转眼间，一个小时过去了，你已经在无尽的名人八卦、猫咪视频、糟糕的新闻报道、网红的抱怨以及算法推送给你的其他任何内容中刷了很久。尽管你可能不想再把时间和精力浪费在这些令人麻木的内容上，但第二天你还是会重蹈覆辙。

Doomscrolling, the habit of spending excessive amounts of time consuming content on social media, has become incredibly widespread. A survey from last year found that 64% of Americans say they doomscroll. Researchers have warned that doomscrolling can negatively affect several aspects of your well-being, including your mental health and attention span. Spending long periods scrolling can lead to brain fatigue, difficulty focusing, and disrupted sleep. And if a lot of the content you’re consuming is negative or stressful, it can leave you feeling disheartened, anxious, and emotionally drained.

“末日刷屏”（Doomscrolling）——即在社交媒体上花费大量时间浏览内容的行为——已经变得极其普遍。去年的一项调查发现，64%的美国人表示自己有这种习惯。研究人员警告称，末日刷屏会对你的身心健康产生负面影响，包括心理健康和注意力持续时间。长时间刷屏会导致大脑疲劳、难以集中注意力以及睡眠障碍。如果你浏览的内容大多是负面或令人压抑的，这会让你感到沮丧、焦虑和情绪耗竭。

It’s hard to break the cycle of doomscrolling, but there are plenty of apps that provide content that’s engaging and productive. Of course, you could always read a book or go for a walk (we have a guide on how to stop doomscrolling), but this list is for when you have a few spare minutes and want something to do on your phone that isn’t endless scrolling.

打破末日刷屏的循环很难，但有很多应用程序可以提供既吸引人又有成效的内容。当然，你也可以选择读书或散步（我们有关于如何停止末日刷屏的指南），但这份清单是为你准备的——当你只有几分钟空闲时间，想在手机上做点什么而不是无休止地刷屏时，这些应用会派上用场。

Finch Image Credits:Finch / Finch is a self-care pet app that’s designed to help you build healthy habits. You care for a cute virtual pet by completing small daily goals, such as drinking enough water, going on a walk, and practicing mindfulness. You can write about your day, complete a breathing exercise, or send good vibes to a friend by adding them to the app. The more self-care you do, the more your pet thrives. The app offers a fun way to trade mindless scrolling for small moments of self-care. Finch is available for free on iOS and Android.

Finch（图片来源：Finch）是一款旨在帮助你养成健康习惯的自我护理宠物应用。你通过完成日常小目标来照顾一只可爱的虚拟宠物，例如喝足够的水、散步和练习正念。你可以在应用中记录生活、完成呼吸练习，或者通过添加好友来向朋友传递正能量。你进行的自我护理越多，你的宠物就越茁壮成长。该应用提供了一种有趣的方式，让你用小小的自我护理时刻来替代无意识的刷屏。Finch 在 iOS 和 Android 上均可免费下载。

Libby Image Credits:Libby / Libby is a free reading app that connects you to your local public library’s digital collection of ebooks, audiobooks, and magazines. All you need is a library card to borrow thousands of titles, which makes it easy to turn screen time into something productive. You can explore a new book during a commute or while waiting at a doctor’s office, turning moments when your fingers might otherwise itch to scroll into opportunities to read. Libby is available for free on iOS and Android.

Libby（图片来源：Libby）是一款免费阅读应用，它能让你连接到当地公共图书馆的电子书、有声读物和杂志数字馆藏。你只需要一张借书证就可以借阅数千种书籍，这让你很容易将屏幕时间转化为有成效的活动。你可以在通勤途中或在医生办公室候诊时探索一本新书，将那些原本可能让你忍不住刷屏的时刻转化为阅读的机会。Libby 在 iOS 和 Android 上均可免费下载。

Dudel Draw Image Credits:Dudel Draw If you want to take a break from doomscrolling and try something creative, Dudel Draw might be a good app for you. Each day, the app gives you a random shape that you turn into a drawing. These daily shapes vary from basic geometrical forms to more complex and abstract designs. You can also get your friends in on the creativity with some fun competition by comparing your different creations, which could provide a nice break from sending each other endless TikToks or Instagram Reels. Dudel Draw is available for free on iOS.

Dudel Draw（图片来源：Dudel Draw）如果你想从末日刷屏中休息一下，尝试一些有创意的事情，Dudel Draw 可能是一个不错的选择。每天，该应用都会给你一个随机形状，让你将其变成一幅画。这些日常形状从基本的几何图形到更复杂、抽象的设计不等。你还可以通过比较彼此的作品，与朋友进行有趣的创意竞赛，这能让你从互相发送无尽的 TikTok 或 Instagram Reels 中解脱出来，获得一段美好的休息时光。Dudel Draw 在 iOS 上可免费下载。

Radio Garden Image Credits:Radio Garden If you still want to feel connected to the world without scrolling through social media, you can check out Radio Garden. The app lets you listen to over 25,000 live radio stations from across the globe. Once you open the app, you’ll see green dots marking cities and towns. Tap any dot to listen to radio stations broadcasting from that location. You can add favorites or search for radio stations, countries, and places. Radio Garden is free, but it also offers an ad-free premium plan for $2.99 per month. The app is available on both iOS and Android.

Radio Garden（图片来源：Radio Garden）如果你想在不刷社交媒体的情况下依然感受到与世界的联系，可以看看 Radio Garden。该应用让你能够收听全球超过 25,000 个直播电台。打开应用后，你会看到标记着城市和城镇的绿点。点击任何一个点，即可收听来自该地点的广播电台。你可以添加收藏，或搜索电台、国家和地点。Radio Garden 是免费的，但也提供每月 2.99 美元的无广告高级套餐。该应用在 iOS 和 Android 上均可使用。
