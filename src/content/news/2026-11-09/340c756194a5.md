Saturday Night Live cast member Jeremy Culhane unveiled his impression of Elon Musk last night, depicting the SpaceX and Tesla CEO as an awkward loser who fumbles his attempt to befriend the hosts of Weekend Update.

《周六夜现场》演员杰里米·卡尔汉（Jeremy Culhane）昨晚展示了他对埃隆·马斯克的模仿，将这位 SpaceX 和特斯拉首席执行官描绘成一个笨拙的失败者，在试图与《周末更新》主持人套近乎时显得手忙脚乱。

This isn’t the first time the show has lampooned the world’s richest man (and one-time SNL host), with Mike Myers dropping by occasionally to wield a chainsaw before freezing and muttering, “Glitch!” Since Musk was in the news this week for spending half a billion dollars on candidates who lost in the midterms, then announcing plans to halt Cybertruck production, it’s maybe not surprising that Culhane’s version is a little less obnoxious and a little more desperate to be liked, doing his best to laugh off his setbacks and remind the audience, “My car did a flip!”

这并不是该节目第一次讽刺这位世界首富（也是曾经的《周六夜现场》主持人），迈克·迈尔斯（Mike Myers）偶尔会客串，挥舞着电锯，然后僵住并嘟囔着：“故障！”由于马斯克本周因在中期选举中为落选候选人花费五亿美元，随后又宣布计划停止 Cybertruck 生产而登上新闻，卡尔汉的版本显得不那么令人讨厌，反而更渴望被喜欢，他尽力对自己的挫折一笑置之，并提醒观众“我的车翻了个跟头！”，这或许并不令人意外。

Eventually, Weekend Update co-host Colin Jost asks a pointed question about Musk’s week: “You moved to Texas because California was run by Democrats. You moved all your companies to Texas because you didn’t want to pay taxes. You even built your own town in Texas. And now Texas is run by Democrats. Now you’re going to have to pay taxes. So how does that make you feel?” Culhane nods along with each step of the question, then answers, “Bad?”

最终，《周末更新》联合主持人科林·约斯特（Colin Jost）就马斯克这一周的情况提出了一个尖锐的问题：“你搬到德克萨斯州是因为加州由民主党人管理。你把所有公司都搬到德克萨斯州是因为你不想交税。你甚至在德克萨斯州建立了属于自己的城镇。而现在德克萨斯州也由民主党人管理了。现在你必须交税了。那么你对此有何感想？”卡尔汉随着问题的每一个要点点头，然后回答道：“糟糕？”

To show that he’s a good sport about all this, Culhane also asks his chatbot Grok to do “an epic vulgar roast of me, Elon Musk.” Grok delivers, describing Musk as “the worst thing South Africa has ever done” and suggesting, “Maybe if you have 100 more kids, one of them will talk to you.”

为了表现出他对此事很有风度，卡尔汉还要求他的聊天机器人 Grok 对“我，埃隆·马斯克，进行一场史诗般的粗俗吐槽”。Grok 照做了，将马斯克描述为“南非做过的最糟糕的事情”，并建议道：“也许如果你再生 100 个孩子，其中一个会愿意和你说话。”

This week’s episode also featured a spoof of the Elizabeth Holmes documentary “You Can See Everything,” featuring Jane Wickline’s uncanny take on the Theranos founder (though most of the jokes focus on episode host Druski). SNL seems to have its knives out for tech executives this season, starting with Wickline’s impression of Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei as a stuttering prophet of AI doom.

本周的节目还恶搞了伊丽莎白·霍姆斯（Elizabeth Holmes）的纪录片《你可以看到一切》（You Can See Everything），简·威克莱恩（Jane Wickline）对这位 Theranos 创始人的模仿惟妙惟肖（尽管大部分笑话集中在当期主持人 Druski 身上）。本季《周六夜现场》似乎对科技高管们磨刀霍霍，首先是威克莱恩模仿 Anthropic 首席执行官达里奥·阿莫代（Dario Amodei），将其描绘成一个结结巴巴的 AI 末日预言家。

A couple weeks later, James Austin Johnson caricatured data center backer/”Marty Supreme” co-star Kevin O’Leary. Asked about the widespread public opposition to data centers, Johnson-as-O’Leary smoothly replies, “All I want is to buy up 40,000 acres of God’s untouched splendor and replace it with a very loud computer that steals your job.”

几周后，詹姆斯·奥斯汀·约翰逊（James Austin Johnson）讽刺了数据中心支持者兼《Marty Supreme》联合主演凯文·奥利里（Kevin O’Leary）。当被问及公众对数据中心的广泛反对时，扮演奥利里的约翰逊圆滑地回答道：“我想要的只是买下 4 万英亩上帝未触及的壮丽土地，并用一台非常吵闹、会抢走你工作的电脑取而代之。”
