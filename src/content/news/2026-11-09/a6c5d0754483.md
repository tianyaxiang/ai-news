Here’s the full text of Jensen Huang’s memo to Nvidia. In the memo, Nvidia’s CEO acknowledges having an affair with a suspected Chinese spy.

以下是黄仁勋致英伟达备忘录的全文。在这份备忘录中，英伟达首席执行官承认与一名疑似中国间谍的人员有染。

On Sunday, Nvidia announced it was launching an investigation into CEO Jensen Huang’s relationship with Alice Huang, an AI executive and agent of the Chinese government. In addition to the Board of Directors announcing the investigation, Huang sent a memo to Nvidia employees acknowledging the affair. You can read the full text below.

周日，英伟达宣布对首席执行官黄仁勋与人工智能高管兼中国政府代理人 Alice Huang 之间的关系展开调查。除了董事会宣布调查外，黄仁勋还向英伟达员工发送了一份备忘录，承认了这段婚外情。全文如下。

To my colleagues at NVIDIA, I owe my family and all of you an apology. I was unfaithful to my wife. My actions hurt her and our children. There is no excuse for what I did. I am deeply sorry.

致英伟达的同事们：我欠我的家人和你们所有人一个道歉。我背叛了我的妻子。我的行为伤害了她和我们的孩子。我所做的一切没有任何借口。我深表歉意。

Throughout my life, my family has given me love and support. They deserve my honesty and loyalty. Instead, I let them down and damaged the life we built together. I take full responsibility for what I did and the hurt I caused, and I accept the criticism that comes with it.

在我的一生中，我的家人给予了我爱与支持。他们理应得到我的诚实与忠诚。然而，我辜负了他们，破坏了我们共同建立的生活。我对我所做的一切以及造成的伤害承担全部责任，并接受随之而来的批评。

To my wife and family: I love you. I know an apology cannot undo what I have done. I hope our family will heal and that, over time, I can earn back your trust.

致我的妻子和家人：我爱你们。我知道道歉无法挽回我所做的一切。我希望我们的家庭能够愈合，并希望随着时间的推移，我能重新赢得你们的信任。

To my colleagues at NVIDIA, including those I have worked alongside for decades, and to our board, partners, and shareholders around the world: you deserve better from me. Leading NVIDIA is the privilege of my life. My responsibility is to serve you and be worthy of your trust. I have fallen short of that responsibility. I am fully committed to NVIDIA and will work to earn back your confidence.

致英伟达的同事们，包括那些与我共事数十年的伙伴，以及我们在全球的董事会成员、合作伙伴和股东：你们理应得到更好的对待。领导英伟达是我一生的荣幸。我的责任是服务于你们，并配得上你们的信任。我未能履行这一责任。我将全心全意致力于英伟达，并努力重新赢得你们的信心。

Regarding the national security concerns raised in news reports, NVIDIA’s Board of Directors has initiated an independent investigation. I support the investigation and will cooperate fully. I love our country and our company. I am committed to protecting NVIDIA and supporting America’s national security. I am proud and grateful to be an American.

关于新闻报道中提出的国家安全担忧，英伟达董事会已启动独立调查。我支持这项调查并将全力配合。我热爱我们的国家和我们的公司。我致力于保护英伟达并支持美国的国家安全。我为自己是一名美国人感到自豪和感激。

My family bears no responsibility for my choices. I do. Please give them privacy. — Jensen

我的家人对我的选择不负任何责任。责任在我。请给予他们隐私。—— 黄仁勋
