I’ve made about a dozen different kinds of coffee drinks with the Ninja AutoBarista, and I have absolutely no idea what sort of grind size, tamping pressure, water temperature, or extraction timing is necessary to pull espresso shots for any of those drinks. The AutoBarista takes care of these considerations for you, making it possible to brew 13 different drinks with just the press of a button. The AutoBarista seeks to create the experience of having a coffee shop inside of your home, but without having to master the complicated, time-consuming nuances of making perfect espresso. It makes drip coffee and espresso-and-milk drinks, like cappuccinos or flat white. The fully automatic espresso machine can even make a pretty fast cold brew or cold-pressed espresso (just add ice, or else it’s more like lukewarm brew).

我用 Ninja AutoBarista 制作了大约十二种不同的咖啡饮品，但我完全不知道制作这些饮品的浓缩咖啡需要什么样的研磨度、压粉压力、水温或萃取时间。AutoBarista 会为你处理这些考量，只需按一下按钮即可冲泡 13 种不同的饮品。AutoBarista 旨在让你在家中就能体验到咖啡店的感觉，而无需掌握制作完美浓缩咖啡那些复杂且耗时的细微差别。它既能制作滴滤咖啡，也能制作卡布奇诺或平白咖啡等浓缩咖啡加奶饮品。这台全自动浓缩咖啡机甚至可以快速制作冷萃咖啡或冷压浓缩咖啡（只需加冰，否则它更像是温咖啡）。

The AutoBarista’s versatility comes from its “Grind iQ” feature. This technology allows the machine to pick up on your coffee bean size, roast type, and freshness, auto-calibrating the grind size, weight, and dose of each brew to make your drink of choice. As you customize the number of shots in your drink, the intensity of the shot, and the temperature, Grind iQ adjusts as well. If using something like Grind iQ sounds like bowling with the bumpers up, or playing Mario Kart with steer assist, then this is not a machine for you — stick with your pour overs and enjoy your gooseneck electric kettle. This is not a machine for coffee snobs who want to control every last detail of the brewing process. Rather, it’s for people who love coffee, but have never analyzed their espresso pucks.

AutoBarista 的多功能性源于其“Grind iQ”功能。这项技术使机器能够识别咖啡豆的大小、烘焙类型和新鲜度，并自动校准每次冲泡的研磨度、重量和剂量，以制作你选择的饮品。当你自定义饮品中的浓缩咖啡份数、浓度和温度时，Grind iQ 也会随之调整。如果使用像 Grind iQ 这样的功能听起来就像打保龄球时升起护栏，或者玩《马力欧卡丁车》时开启转向辅助，那么这台机器就不适合你——还是坚持你的手冲咖啡，享受你的鹅颈电水壶吧。这不是为那些想要控制冲泡过程每一个细节的咖啡鉴赏家准备的机器。相反，它是为那些热爱咖啡，但从未分析过咖啡粉饼的人准备的。

With the caveat that I am not invested in the coffee brewing process, I will say that I think the AutoBarista makes good drinks. On one side of the machine, it can pull shots of espresso or brew drip coffee, while on the other, it has an automatic milk frother and stainless steel pitcher, which allows you to customize settings based on your desired temperature and heaviness of the foam. It also includes a setting for frothing plant-based milk. To keep the coffee tasting good, the AutoBarista always rinses itself before and after making a drink. This is a useful feature — I often find myself manually running water through my Nespresso machine, since I don’t use it every day. But the water catches in a drip tray, which fills up after making just a few drinks, so if you live with another person, then emptying the tray will be a daily nuisance.

虽然我并不热衷于咖啡冲泡过程，但我不得不说，我认为 AutoBarista 制作的饮品味道不错。机器的一侧可以萃取浓缩咖啡或冲泡滴滤咖啡，而另一侧则配有自动奶泡机和不锈钢奶缸，让你能够根据所需的温度和奶泡厚度自定义设置。它还包含一个用于打发植物奶的设置。为了保持咖啡口感，AutoBarista 在制作饮品前后都会自动冲洗。这是一个有用的功能——我经常发现自己需要手动给 Nespresso 机器过水，因为我不是每天都用它。但这些水会积聚在接水盘中，制作几杯饮品后就会装满，所以如果你和别人住在一起，那么每天清理接水盘将是一件麻烦事。

While the design of the machine makes it easy to pull out the tray, I’ve accidentally overfilled it and spilled coffee-tinted water on my kitchen floor more than once. That’s the dealbreaker. When you pull out the drip tray, you’ll also see the knock box, which collects your spent espresso pucks. Be warned: if you forget to empty the knock box for even just a few days, you may get jump-scared by some blue mold next time you go to dump the pucks. Maybe I should’ve seen that coming, but scrubbing the moldy knock box made me feel ready to go back to my beloved OXO Cold Brew Coffee Maker, which costs just six percent of the price of the AutoBarista.

虽然机器的设计使得拉出接水盘很容易，但我已经不止一次不小心让它溢出，把带有咖啡色的水洒在厨房地板上了。这就是让我无法接受的地方。当你拉出接水盘时，你还会看到收集废弃咖啡粉饼的渣盒。请注意：如果你忘记清理渣盒，哪怕只是几天，下次去倒粉饼时，你可能会被上面的蓝色霉菌吓一跳。也许我早该预料到这一点，但刷洗发霉的渣盒让我觉得还是回到我心爱的 OXO 冷萃咖啡壶比较好，它的价格仅为 AutoBarista 的百分之六。

In the AutoBarista’s defense, most people probably wouldn’t find this machine so arduous to care for. I just happen to be someone who makes one big batch cold brew every other week and tries to keep my morning rituals minimal. If you have more patience than I do before 8 AM, then you may very well love this machine and find it low maintenance. Ninja excels at making multi-use kitchen products, and the AutoBarista is no exception, combining a drip coffee maker, espresso machine, milk frother, and grinder, and food scale into one device. But if you’re not planning to take advantage of the AutoBarista’s versatility, then it’s not worth paying its $999.99 price.

为 AutoBarista 说句公道话，大多数人可能不会觉得这台机器的维护如此繁琐。我恰好是一个每两周制作一大批冷萃咖啡，并尽量简化早晨仪式的人。如果你在早上 8 点前比我有耐心，那么你很可能会喜欢这台机器，并觉得它维护起来很简单。Ninja 擅长制造多用途厨房产品，AutoBarista 也不例外，它将滴滤咖啡机、浓缩咖啡机、奶泡机、研磨机和食品秤整合到了一台设备中。但如果你不打算利用 AutoBarista 的多功能性，那么支付 999.99 美元的价格就不值得了。
