# The Apple MacBook Pro M5 is on Sale, and a Top MacBook to Buy Right Now

Save this storySave this storyLast month, Apple refreshed the 14-inch MacBook Pro with the latest M6 chip. It's powerful, has great battery life, and the mini-LED screen is bright. There are all kinds of great attributes about this laptop, but you probably shouldn't buy it. At least, not right now.

上个月，苹果公司推出了搭载最新 M6 芯片的 14 英寸 MacBook Pro。它性能强劲，电池续航出色，mini-LED 屏幕亮度也很高。这款笔记本电脑虽然有很多优点，但你现在可能不应该购买它。至少，目前还不是时候。

While the updated M6 is a perfectly capable laptop in its own right, it fits a bit awkwardly into the current lineup of MacBooks. It's identical to the previous-gen M5 model, which came out in late 2025. But here's the problem: Despite having the exact same laptop specs (save for the chip difference), you can currently get the M5 for $200 less on Amazon and Walmart.

虽然升级后的 M6 本身是一款非常出色的笔记本电脑，但在当前的 MacBook 产品线中，它的定位略显尴尬。它与 2025 年底发布的上一代 M5 机型完全相同。但问题在于：尽管两者的笔记本规格完全一致（除了芯片差异），你目前在亚马逊和沃尔玛购买 M5 机型可以节省 200 美元。

If you're thinking that upgrading from the M5 to the M6 must be worth $200, I'm not so sure. The M6 comes with two additional CPU and GPU cores, which is the origin of most of its gains. While that's not nothing, it's one of the smaller gen-over-gen Apple Silicon performance bumps in the past six years. The main difference is in AI performance, thanks to the second 16-core Neural Engine and faster memory bandwidth on the M6. In day-to-day work—or even in content creation workflows—I just don't think the difference will be very noticeable between the two models. That's especially true since the base 14-inch MacBook Pro isn't a true pro laptop in terms of performance. It uses the same M5 chip that's in the MacBook Air and iPad Pro, after all. The only other real difference is in wireless connectivity, where you get the bump to Wi-Fi 7 with the M6 model.

如果你认为从 M5 升级到 M6 值得多花 200 美元，我对此并不确定。M6 多了两个 CPU 和 GPU 核心，这是它大部分性能提升的来源。虽然这并非毫无意义，但这却是过去六年来 Apple Silicon 历代性能提升幅度较小的一次。主要的区别在于人工智能性能，这得益于 M6 上第二个 16 核神经网络引擎和更快的内存带宽。在日常工作甚至内容创作流程中，我认为这两款机型之间的差异不会非常明显。尤其是考虑到入门级 14 英寸 MacBook Pro 在性能上并非真正的专业级笔记本电脑。毕竟，它使用的 M5 芯片与 MacBook Air 和 iPad Pro 中的芯片相同。另一个真正的区别在于无线连接，M6 机型升级到了 Wi-Fi 7。

If you're going to buy the 14-inch MacBook Pro M5, though, you should also be considering the 15-inch MacBook Air. The main difference between these two laptops is the quality of the display. The mini-LED panel on the MacBook Pro is much brighter and more colorful, allowing for some very impressive HDR performance. It also has more ports, such as HDMI and an SD card slot. My favorite small feature is its USB-C ports on both sides of the laptop, allowing for charging from either direction.

不过，如果你打算购买 14 英寸的 MacBook Pro M5，也应该考虑一下 15 英寸的 MacBook Air。这两款笔记本电脑的主要区别在于显示屏质量。MacBook Pro 上的 mini-LED 面板亮度更高、色彩更丰富，能带来非常出色的 HDR 表现。它还拥有更多的接口，例如 HDMI 和 SD 卡槽。我最喜欢的一个小功能是它机身两侧的 USB-C 接口，允许从任一侧进行充电。

15 英寸的 MacBook Air 今天实际上也在打折。配备 16GB 内存和 1TB 存储空间的机型价格达到了自 6 月以来的最低点：1,549 美元。这比苹果的零售价便宜了 150 美元。
