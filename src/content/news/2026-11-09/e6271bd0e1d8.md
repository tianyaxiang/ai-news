Nvidia’s board of directors announced Sunday that it’s launching an independent investigation following reports that CEO Jensen Huang was targeted by a Chinese spy operation. Nvidia also published a message that Huang sent to the company admitting that he had an affair with Alice Huang, a former executive at Singapore-based chip company Megaspeed (though he didn’t mention her by name).

英伟达董事会周日宣布，在有报道称首席执行官黄仁勋成为中国间谍行动的目标后，公司将启动一项独立调查。英伟达还发布了黄仁勋发给公司的一条信息，承认他与总部位于新加坡的芯片公司 Megaspeed 的前高管 Alice Huang 发生了婚外情（尽管他未提及对方姓名）。

A memo circulating among U.S. policymakers earlier this year claimed that Alice Huang had been recruited by China’s Ministry of State Security to spy on Nvidia’s CEO. “I owe my family and all of you an apology,” Jensen Huang wrote. “I was unfaithful to my wife. My actions hurt her and our children. There is no excuse for what I did. I am deeply sorry.”

今年早些时候在美方决策者中流传的一份备忘录声称，Alice Huang 已被中国国家安全部招募，以监视英伟达的首席执行官。“我欠我的家人和你们所有人一个道歉，”黄仁勋写道。“我对我的妻子不忠。我的行为伤害了她和我们的孩子。我所做的一切没有任何借口。我深感抱歉。”

Huang added that he’d “fallen short” of his responsibility to the company. “To my colleagues at NVIDIA, including those I have worked alongside for decades, and to our board, partners, and shareholders around the world: you deserve better from me,” he said. As the AI race between the United States and China heats up, chips, especially Nvidia’s chips, have been a major focus.

黄仁勋补充说，他“辜负了”对公司的责任。“对于我在英伟达的同事，包括那些与我共事数十年的同事，以及我们的董事会、合作伙伴和全球股东：你们理应得到更好的我，”他说。随着美中人工智能竞赛的升温，芯片，尤其是英伟达的芯片，已成为关注的焦点。

Huang also seems to have a close relationship with President Donald Trump, who’s been a cheerleader of the AI (or “superintelligence”) industry. In its announcement, Nvidia’s board said that it has “no reason to believe” that either national security or company secrets were compromised. It also said that Huang “fully supports the review and has committed to cooperating with it,” and that the board has “confidence in Jensen and his leadership team.”

黄仁勋似乎与唐纳德·特朗普总统关系密切，后者一直是人工智能（或称“超级智能”）行业的支持者。英伟达董事会在公告中表示，他们“没有理由相信”国家安全或公司机密受到损害。公告还称，黄仁勋“完全支持此次审查并承诺配合”，董事会“对黄仁勋及其领导团队充满信心”。

The investigation will be conducted by independent directors working with outside legal counsel. In response to earlier reports about the CEO’s relationship with Alice Huang, Nvidia had said that the pair’s interactions were “not unusual,” and that the company does not share classified information with customers. Other companies involved in the AI boom have warned employees to be wary of possible spies, including those offering sex or romance.

此次调查将由独立董事与外部法律顾问合作进行。针对此前有关首席执行官与 Alice Huang 关系的报道，英伟达曾表示两人的互动“并不罕见”，且公司不会与客户分享机密信息。其他参与人工智能热潮的公司也已警告员工要警惕可能的间谍，包括那些提供性或浪漫诱惑的人。
