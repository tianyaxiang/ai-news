Almost seven years ago, while clutching a laptop, my phone, and a notebook in a sea of Tesla fans, I watched head designer Franz von Holzhausen throw a metal, baseball-sized ball at the driver’s-side window of the Cybertruck. The result was cracked glass — actually, two by the end of the presentation — and some awkward jokes and chuckling by CEO Elon Musk.

大约七年前，我怀揣着笔记本电脑、手机和笔记本，置身于特斯拉粉丝的海洋中，亲眼目睹了首席设计师 Franz von Holzhausen 将一个棒球大小的金属球扔向 Cybertruck 的驾驶侧车窗。结果玻璃碎了——实际上在演示结束时碎了两块——首席执行官埃隆·马斯克也因此讲了一些尴尬的笑话并发出阵阵轻笑。

I have witnessed a number of stunning, odd, and fantastical moments at Tesla events. But the Cybertruck reveal was the most interesting — and not because of the postapocalyptic design. It was the crowd. When the truck rolled out onto the stage, I watched attendees gasp, some in revulsion. There was mumbling (“This certainly can’t be the Cybertruck?” I heard Tesla superfans whisper). And yes, there were cheers.

我在特斯拉的活动中见证过许多令人震惊、古怪且充满幻想的时刻。但 Cybertruck 的发布会是最有趣的——这并非因为其后启示录风格的设计，而是因为现场的观众。当卡车驶上舞台时，我看到观众们倒吸一口凉气，有些人甚至表现出厌恶。现场议论纷纷（我听到特斯拉的超级粉丝低语：“这肯定不是 Cybertruck 吧？”）。当然，也有欢呼声。

The momentary shock of Musk’s biggest fans was quickly replaced by support, acceptance, and finally, endorsement. It moved like a wave over the crowd. By the time attendees were waiting in line for Cybertruck rides, the mood had shifted. Everyone was a fan. That fandom carried over to Cybertruck reservations, which skyrocketed to 1 million, Musk said during a third-quarter earnings call in 2023.

马斯克最忠实粉丝们短暂的震惊很快被支持、接受，最终转变为认可所取代。这种情绪像波浪一样在人群中蔓延。当与会者排队等待试乘 Cybertruck 时，气氛已经发生了转变。每个人都成了粉丝。马斯克在 2023 年第三季度财报电话会议上表示，这种狂热延续到了 Cybertruck 的预订量上，预订量飙升至 100 万辆。

But it never made the leap to actual sales. Industry experts estimate about 71,000 Cybertrucks have been sold since the first deliveries began in November 2023. And now the Cybertruck is headed to the scrap pile. Tesla will stop making the Cybertruck next year, Musk said Friday. Was the vehicle too edgy? Maybe, but this was always going to be a niche market. I believe more practical reasons dampened sales. Musk promised the Cybertruck would have a 500-mile range and a price tag under $40,000. It achieved neither, senior reporter Sean O’Kane noted in his report.

但它从未实现向实际销量的跨越。行业专家估计，自 2023 年 11 月首次交付以来，Cybertruck 的销量约为 71,000 辆。现在，Cybertruck 即将走向报废。马斯克周五表示，特斯拉明年将停止生产 Cybertruck。这款车是否太前卫了？也许吧，但这注定是一个小众市场。我认为更实际的原因抑制了销量。马斯克曾承诺 Cybertruck 的续航里程将达到 500 英里，价格低于 40,000 美元。资深记者 Sean O’Kane 在其报道中指出，这两点均未实现。

Deals! Image Credits: Bryce Durbin. Zipline has had quite the week. Strike that — quite the year. The autonomous drone delivery startup, which was founded in 2014, has steadily scaled over the past few years. But 2026 has taken the company to new heights. In early 2026, Zipline raised $600 million and quickly added another $200 million to that Series H round. The company planned to use these funds to accelerate its expansion to at least four U.S. cities in 2026.

交易！图片来源：Bryce Durbin。Zipline 这一周过得相当充实。不，应该说这一年过得相当充实。这家成立于 2014 年的自动无人机配送初创公司在过去几年里稳步扩张。但 2026 年将公司带到了新的高度。2026 年初，Zipline 筹集了 6 亿美元，并迅速为该 H 轮融资追加了 2 亿美元。该公司计划利用这些资金加速其在 2026 年向至少四个美国城市的扩张。

Now it has even more money to grow even faster. Zipline announced this week it raised $1.5 billion and ramped up its partnership with Walmart to add 300 drone delivery sites across the United States. Zipline is now valued at $21.5 billion.

现在，它拥有了更多的资金来更快地增长。Zipline 本周宣布筹集了 15 亿美元，并加强了与沃尔玛的合作，将在美国各地增加 300 个无人机配送点。Zipline 目前的估值为 215 亿美元。

Other deals that got my attention … Electric truck startup Harbinger raised a $240 million Series D funding round as it expands its portfolio of products, including battery storage and defense applications. No word on the company’s valuation. Humble Robotics raised $51 million in a Series A round, bringing total funding to $75 million less than a year after founding. The round was led by Obvious Ventures with participation from insiders Eclipse, Energy Impact Partners, and others. Static Media acquired automotive news site CarScoops, adding to its existing portfolio that includes 34 brands such as Engadget, Jalopnik, and SlashGear.

其他引起我注意的交易……电动卡车初创公司 Harbinger 筹集了 2.4 亿美元的 D 轮融资，以扩大其产品组合，包括电池存储和国防应用。目前没有关于该公司估值的消息。Humble Robotics 在 A 轮融资中筹集了 5100 万美元，使其在成立不到一年后的总融资额达到 7500 万美元。本轮融资由 Obvious Ventures 领投，内部人士 Eclipse、Energy Impact Partners 等参与。Static Media 收购了汽车新闻网站 CarScoops，将其加入到现有的投资组合中，该组合已包含 Engadget、Jalopnik 和 SlashGear 等 34 个品牌。

Also, the micromobility startup spun out of Rivian in 2025, quietly started and then walked away from an electric two-wheeler pilot project in Kenya earlier this year, as it sought to focus its resources on its consumer e-bike and autonomous vehicle ambitions.

值得阅读的内容及其他花絮。图片来源：Bryce Durbin。此外，这家 2025 年从 Rivian 分拆出来的微型交通初创公司，今年早些时候在肯尼亚悄悄启动并随后退出了一个电动两轮车试点项目，因为它试图将资源集中在消费级电动自行车和自动驾驶汽车的雄心上。

Amazon has made Uber Eats its official restaurant delivery partner for Prime members in the United States, effectively ending the e-commerce giant’s existing deal with Grubhub. The Luce, Ferrari’s first electric car and one of its most polarizing models, is “fully allocated” for 2027.

亚马逊已将 Uber Eats 指定为其美国 Prime 会员的官方餐厅配送合作伙伴，实际上终止了这家电子商务巨头与 Grubhub 现有的协议。法拉利的首款电动汽车 Luce 是其最具争议的车型之一，目前 2027 年的配额已“全部分配完毕”。

Ford unveiled what it’s calling a “privacy promise” — an “explicit commitment” to keep customers’ vehicle data safe and give them control over what they share. Ford also launched a website designed to educate customers about how, where, and why the automaker uses their data, and to show them how to change those settings.

福特公布了所谓的“隐私承诺”——这是一项“明确的承诺”，旨在确保客户的车辆数据安全，并让他们能够控制自己分享的内容。福特还推出了一个网站，旨在向客户普及汽车制造商如何、在何处以及为何使用他们的数据，并向他们展示如何更改这些设置。

May Mobility and Forterra advanced to phase three of the Department of Defense’s Robotic Operations for Autonomous Delivery and Sustainment (ROADS) prize challenge, an initiative to test and adopt commercial self-driving vehicles in the military. Serve Robotics announced plans to launch a new business line called Physical AI Services. The company will still operate and expand its autonomous delivery operations and become a technology supplier to other companies building robots.

May Mobility 和 Forterra 进入了美国国防部“自动配送与保障机器人操作”（ROADS）奖项挑战赛的第三阶段，该计划旨在测试和采用商业自动驾驶车辆用于军事用途。Serve Robotics 宣布计划推出名为“物理人工智能服务”（Physical AI Services）的新业务线。该公司将继续运营和扩展其自动配送业务，并成为其他机器人制造公司的技术供应商。

The predictions of AI agents booking restaurants, trips, and even rides for the masses hasn’t quite materialized yet. Uber reported during its third-quarter earnings call that only four rides were ordered through an AI agent during its busiest week in October: the week of Halloween. Waymo is shaking up its Uber relationship, confirming months of speculation. The company will end its exclusivity with Uber in the Austin and Atlanta markets starting in January 2028.

关于人工智能代理为大众预订餐厅、旅行甚至乘车的预测尚未完全实现。Uber 在第三季度财报电话会议上报告称，在 10 月份最繁忙的一周（万圣节周）里，通过人工智能代理预订的行程仅有四次。Waymo 正在调整其与 Uber 的关系，证实了数月以来的猜测。该公司将于 2028 年 1 月起终止在奥斯汀和亚特兰大市场与 Uber 的排他性合作。
