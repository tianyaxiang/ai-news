# Chatbots might be coming to Apple’s HomeView

Code in the iOS 27.3 beta suggests that Apple could add support for chatbots to its smart home hub. There’s a flag listed for a “Conversational Apps” category. While there are no details about what apps would fall under that heading, it seems reasonable that AI chatbots like ChatGPT and Claude could be added to HomeView.

iOS 27.3 测试版中的代码表明，苹果公司可能会为其智能家居中枢增加对聊天机器人的支持。代码中列出了一个名为“对话式应用”（Conversational Apps）类别的标志。虽然目前尚无关于哪些应用会被归入该类别的详细信息，但将 ChatGPT 和 Claude 等人工智能聊天机器人加入 HomeView 似乎是合理的。

According to pdfu on X, that new beta also adds support for bidirectional audio routing and session management, but says it’s “still not fully wired up.”

据 X 平台上的 pdfu 称，该新测试版还增加了对双向音频路由和会话管理的支持，但表示这些功能“尚未完全连接就绪”。

Apple has also added anti-spoofing protection that will require a user to authenticate themselves with an iPhone after three failed attempts. It will also have a lockout system with increasingly longer wait times, starting at one hour for the second lockout, and climbing to eight for the fourth.

苹果还增加了防欺骗保护功能，要求用户在三次尝试失败后使用 iPhone 进行身份验证。该系统还将包含一个锁定机制，等待时间会逐渐延长，第二次锁定时为一小时，第四次则增加到八小时。

Neither feature has been officially announced by Apple, and there’s no word on when they might become available.

苹果尚未正式宣布这两项功能，也没有关于它们何时可能推出的消息。
