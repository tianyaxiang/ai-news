Apple is racing to make Siri more agentic. Future versions of Siri will be able to control third-party apps and perform more complex tasks.

苹果正在竞相让 Siri 具备更强的代理能力。未来版本的 Siri 将能够控制第三方应用程序并执行更复杂的任务。

Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman says CEO John Ternus is pressuring AI czar Craig Federighi to accelerate the Siri roadmap and deliver a powerful AI agent that can compete with OpenAI’s Dots or Meta’s Muse. While Federighi has delivered a functional version of Siri AI on an incredibly short timeline, the company has no illusions that it’s keeping pace with its rivals.

彭博社的马克·古尔曼（Mark Gurman）表示，首席执行官约翰·特努斯（John Ternus）正在向人工智能主管克雷格·费德里吉（Craig Federighi）施压，要求其加快 Siri 的路线图，并交付一款能够与 OpenAI 的 Dots 或 Meta 的 Muse 相抗衡的强大人工智能代理。尽管费德里吉在极短的时间内交付了一个功能性的 Siri AI 版本，但该公司清楚地意识到，其并未跟上竞争对手的步伐。

Two significant improvements are expected to be made to Siri in the coming months. One will give the assistant fine-grained control over Apple’s first-party apps, allowing it to do things like rotate and crop images in Photos and organize Safari tabs. Those capabilities are expected to land with iOS 27.3. The bigger change, integration with third-party apps, is likely to land later, in the Spring, according to Gurman. That would let Siri place an order through Grubhub or book a dinner reservation through OpenTable, for example.

预计在未来几个月内，Siri 将迎来两项重大改进。其中一项将使该助手能够对苹果的自有应用程序进行精细化控制，例如在“照片”中旋转和裁剪图像，以及整理 Safari 标签页。这些功能预计将随 iOS 27.3 一同推出。据古尔曼称，更大的变革——即与第三方应用程序的集成——可能会在春季晚些时候实现。例如，这将允许 Siri 通过 Grubhub 下订单或通过 OpenTable 预订晚餐。

But that’s just the start. Apple is expected to push Siri to become more agentic and enable features that will allow it to perform ongoing tasks, not just one-offs. Gurman says: Dots, Muse and Grok can keep track of goals, determine what needs to happen next and continue pursuing objectives without the user initiating every individual step. Apple ultimately wants to go in this direction, I’m told, and I believe the company could make Siri more like an agent as early as next year — not that it has much of a choice, given current consumer and market demands.

但这仅仅是个开始。苹果预计将推动 Siri 变得更具代理属性，并启用相关功能，使其能够执行持续性任务，而不仅仅是一次性操作。古尔曼表示：Dots、Muse 和 Grok 能够跟踪目标、确定下一步需要做什么，并在无需用户发起每一个单独步骤的情况下持续追求目标。据我所知，苹果最终希望朝着这个方向发展，我相信该公司最早可能在明年就让 Siri 更像一个代理——考虑到当前的消费者和市场需求，它其实别无选择。
