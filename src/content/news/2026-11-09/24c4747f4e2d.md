# Nvidia is investigating Jensen Huang’s relationship with a Chinese spy

英伟达（Nvidia）宣布，针对首席执行官黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）与 Megaspeed 高管爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang）之间的关系展开调查。据报道，爱丽丝·黄曾被中国国家安全部招募，负责对该公司进行间谍活动。董事会发布的一份声明写道：

The NVIDIA Board is initiating an independent investigation in light of recent reporting that founder and CEO Jensen Huang has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort. A committee of independent directors, assisted by outside independent counsel, will oversee the investigation.

英伟达董事会表示，鉴于近期有报道称创始人兼首席执行官黄仁勋成为外国情报工作的目标，董事会正在启动一项独立调查。一个由独立董事组成的委员会将在外部独立法律顾问的协助下监督此次调查。

The Board prioritizes U.S. national and economic security, which is paramount to the interests of NVIDIA and its shareholders. While the Board has no reason to believe that either national security or NVIDIA secrets were compromised, the Board’s inquiry will examine all available information. Jensen fully supports the review and has committed to cooperating with it.

董事会将美国国家和经济安全放在首位，这对于英伟达及其股东的利益至关重要。虽然董事会没有理由认为国家安全或英伟达的机密受到了损害，但董事会的调查将审查所有可用信息。黄仁勋完全支持此次审查，并承诺予以配合。

The Board has confidence in Jensen and his leadership team and will provide further updates as appropriate. The New York Times reported last month that Huang had developed a “close relationship” with the Megaspeed executive, which he later admitted was romantic in nature in a memo to the company. US officials believe she was feeding information directly back to Beijing.

董事会对黄仁勋及其领导团队充满信心，并将适时提供进一步的更新。《纽约时报》上个月报道称，黄仁勋与这位 Megaspeed 高管建立了“亲密关系”，他后来在给公司的一份备忘录中承认这种关系具有浪漫性质。美国官员认为，她一直在直接向北京输送信息。

Megaspeed is a customer of Nvidia and is the subject of a Commerce Department investigation into whether it was smuggling AI chips to China. The company’s AI chips are viewed as crucial to the US maintaining technological supremacy. Both the Biden and Trump administrations have restricted their sale to China.

Megaspeed 是英伟达的客户，目前正受到美国商务部的调查，以确定其是否在向中国走私人工智能芯片。该公司的 AI 芯片被视为美国保持技术领先地位的关键。拜登政府和特朗普政府都限制了这些芯片对中国的销售。

President Trump has loosened those restrictions but still bars sales of Nvidia’s most powerful chips to Chinese firms, and the Commerce Department must approve individual firms to handle the chips. Just as importantly, Jensen Huang has gotten quite cozy with Donald Trump, and there is concern about what insights into the administration’s deliberations around AI he may have been privy to. And whether any of that information may have been shared with Alice Huang, and eventually found its way to Beijing.

特朗普总统放宽了这些限制，但仍禁止向中国公司出售英伟达最强大的芯片，且商务部必须批准个别公司才能处理这些芯片。同样重要的是，黄仁勋与唐纳德·特朗普关系密切，人们担心他可能获悉了政府关于人工智能审议的内幕信息，以及这些信息是否可能被分享给爱丽丝·黄，并最终传到了北京。
