Answer: Seedance 2.5 renders clips from 1 to 30 seconds at 480P, 720P and 1080P, billed per output second from 5 credits ($0.05/s) at 480P to 21 credits ($0.21/s) at 1080P on the standard route. It accepts prompts up to 30,000 characters, up to nine reference images plus video and audio references, and exposes audio, web search, output format and last-frame controls. Most Seedance write-ups stop at "it makes video". The controls are where the pipeline lives: a thirty-second ceiling changes how you cut, a 30,000-character prompt changes how you write, and the last-frame return changes how shots chain.

Seedance 2.5 可渲染 1 到 30 秒的视频片段，分辨率支持 480P、720P 和 1080P。在标准路径下，按输出秒数计费，480P 为每秒 5 积分（0.05 美元/秒），1080P 为每秒 21 积分（0.21 美元/秒）。它支持最高 30,000 字符的提示词，最多可添加 9 张参考图以及视频和音频参考，并提供音频、网页搜索、输出格式和末帧控制选项。大多数关于 Seedance 的文章只停留在“它能制作视频”这一层面。实际上，控制选项才是其核心流程所在：30 秒的上限改变了剪辑方式，30,000 字符的提示词改变了写作方式，而末帧返回功能则改变了镜头衔接方式。

The controls, one by one Control Options What it changes Duration 1–30 s billed per output second Resolution 480P · 720P · 1080P 5 / 12 / 21 credits per second Generate audio with / without native audio in the output Content pre-check on / off whether the optional check is sent; platform rules still apply Web search on / off allows the model to consult search for the prompt Output format mp4 or mov container, not quality Return last frame on / off hands back the final frame as an asset

控制选项逐一解析：时长（1-30 秒，按输出秒数计费）；分辨率（480P/720P/1080P，每秒 5/12/21 积分）；生成音频（输出中是否包含原生音频）；内容预检（开启/关闭，决定是否发送可选检查，平台规则依然适用）；网页搜索（开启/关闭，允许模型为提示词进行搜索）；输出格式（mp4 或 mov 容器，不代表质量）；返回末帧（开启/关闭，将最终帧作为资产返回）。

Two of these are underrated. Return last frame gives you the first frame of the next shot for free, which is how a multi-shot sequence stays continuous. Output format matters when the next stage is an NLE that prefers mov. Writing a 30,000-character prompt that earns the length The limit is not an invitation to write prose. A long prompt works when each block owns one decision: SHOT — 30s one-take, 720P, 16:9. SUBJECT — the mechanic from @Image1, oil-stained overalls, short beard. ACTION — from t=0 he rolls out from under the car; at ~t=12 he stands, wipes his hands, and looks toward the bay door. CAMERA — slow push-in to a medium shot, then a gentle arc right. LIGHT — one sodium worklight overhead, hard falloff, deep shadow behind. AUDIO — workshop ambience, intermittent impact wrench, no music. That is ~90 words. The remaining 29,910 characters are for what actually needs them: continuity across thirty seconds, wardrobe changes at named times, second-subject blocking, and the exact text that has to appear on a prop.

其中有两个功能被低估了。“返回末帧”可以免费提供下一个镜头的首帧，这是保持多镜头序列连续性的关键。当后续处理阶段是偏好 mov 格式的非线性编辑软件（NLE）时，输出格式的选择就显得尤为重要。编写 30,000 字符的提示词以充分利用长度限制：这个限制并非让你写散文。长提示词的有效方式是每个区块负责一个决策：镜头（30 秒长镜头，720P，16:9）；主体（来自 @Image1 的机械师，油渍工装，短胡须）；动作（从 0 秒开始他从车底滚出；约 12 秒时站起，擦手，看向车间门）；摄像机（缓慢推近至中景，随后向右平滑弧形移动）；光线（头顶一盏钠灯，硬光衰减，身后有深影）；音频（车间环境音，间歇性的冲击扳手声，无音乐）。这大约 90 个词。剩下的 29,910 个字符应留给真正需要的地方：30 秒内的连续性、特定时间的服装更换、第二主体的调度，以及道具上必须出现的精确文字。

References: the part that decides the shot Up to nine images. Spend them by role: identity, wardrobe, prop, environment, light. Two references with the same job cancel each other. Video references are accepted, and they add an input-duration charge on top of output seconds — a ten-second reference feeding a ten-second render bills twenty seconds. Audio references condition pacing, not lips. If exact dialogue sync is the requirement, place the line in post.

参考资料：决定镜头的关键。最多可使用 9 张图片。按角色分配：身份、服装、道具、环境、光线。两个功能相同的参考会相互抵消。支持视频参考，但会额外收取输入时长费用——10 秒的参考视频用于 10 秒的渲染，总计收费 20 秒。音频参考用于调节节奏，而非口型。如果需要精确的对白同步，请在后期制作中添加。

Where the controls mislead "Without audio" is not silence. It asks the model not to generate a track; it does not promise a clean plate. The pre-check toggle is not a safety switch. Turning off the optional check does not exempt the job from the platform's own review. mp4 versus mov is a container choice. It says nothing about bitrate or codec profile; check the file if the downstream stage is picky. 30 seconds is not 30 seconds of story. A single unbroken take needs a reason to stay unbroken; otherwise six shots read better.

控制选项的误导之处：“无音频”并不等于静音。它只是要求模型不生成音轨，并不保证提供纯净的画面。预检开关也不是安全开关。关闭可选检查并不能使任务免于平台的自身审核。mp4 与 mov 只是容器选择，与比特率或编解码器配置无关；如果下游阶段要求严格，请检查文件。30 秒并不等于 30 秒的叙事。一个完整的不间断镜头需要保持不间断的理由；否则，六个镜头组合起来的效果可能更好。

A pipeline that uses the ceiling properly Block the shot at 480P in ten-second chunks — 50 credits a chunk. When the blocking holds, render the real thing at 720P at full duration (360 credits for thirty seconds). Chain sequences via return last frame, so shot two starts on the frame shot one ended with. Keep audio off while exploring; turn it on for the take you keep, and place dialogue in post.

正确利用时长上限的流程：先以 480P 分段（每段 10 秒，50 积分）进行分镜预演。当预演效果满意后，再以 720P 全时长进行正式渲染（30 秒需 360 积分）。通过“返回末帧”衔接序列，使第二个镜头从第一个镜头的最后一帧开始。在探索阶段保持音频关闭；在确定最终版本时开启音频，并将对白留到后期制作。

FAQ Is 4K available on 2.5? No. 2.5 runs 480P to 1080P; Seedance 2.0 carries the 4K tier. Do both routes offer the 30-second ceiling? The ceiling is a 2.5 capability; the route decides moderation and price, not duration. Does a long prompt cost more? No — credits are per output second. Length costs you latency, not credits. Can I get the final frame as an image? Yes, with the last-frame control on; it lands in your library like any other asset.
