When I tried a prototype of Snap’s Specs at CES in Las Vegas earlier this year, the experience left something to be desired. The company’s AR glasses — which the company calls a “new kind of computer” — were heavy (they weighed around 8 ounces) and fit rather uncomfortably on my face. The device was also hot, and made my face feel tingly — a common side-effect of smart glasses where massive computing is happening under the hood.

今年早些时候，当我在拉斯维加斯的国际消费类电子产品展览会（CES）上试用 Snap 的 Specs 原型机时，体验并不尽如人意。这款被公司称为“新型计算机”的增强现实（AR）眼镜非常沉重（重约 8 盎司），戴在脸上很不舒服。设备发热严重，让我的脸部感到刺痛——这是智能眼镜在进行大规模计算时常见的副作用。

Fast forward to late October, when I met back up with the Snap team at a Four Seasons in downtown San Francisco. By this time, the company had debuted Specs publicly during an event with CEO Evan Spiegel, and pre-orders of the company’s long-gestating headset were already underway. What I can say is this: Specs has improved the form factor significantly since my first brush with the device. The glasses now weigh nearly half of what the previous version did, slimming down to 132 grams, or approximately 4.6 ounces. They were quite comfortable and, during the hour-ish that I demo-ed the device, it did not heat up like the previous version had.

时间快进到 10 月下旬，我在旧金山市中心的一家四季酒店再次见到了 Snap 团队。此时，该公司已在首席执行官埃文·斯皮格尔（Evan Spiegel）出席的活动中公开展示了 Specs，并且这款酝酿已久的头显设备已经开启了预售。我可以肯定地说：与我第一次接触该设备时相比，Specs 的外形设计有了显著改进。现在的眼镜重量几乎只有之前版本的一半，缩减到了 132 克（约 4.6 盎司）。它们戴起来非常舒适，在我试用设备的一个多小时里，它并没有像之前版本那样发热。

Specs now comes in two sizes — 47mm and 52mm. The purpose here is purely to accommodate different head sizes, ensuring the glasses fit snugly on whoever’s face happens to be wearing them. They also now come equipped with spring hinges, which make the device more flexible and comfortable. However, if the body of the device has improved greatly, its soul is still a work-in-progress. The glasses can be fun — but they also still seem to lack a dedicated purpose, and one struggles to imagine how, specifically, this expensive head gear ($2,200 a pop) would prove useful in a daily capacity.

Specs 现在有两种尺寸可选——47 毫米和 52 毫米。其目的纯粹是为了适应不同的头型，确保眼镜能贴合佩戴者的脸部。它们现在还配备了弹簧铰链，使设备更加灵活舒适。然而，尽管设备的机身有了很大改进，但其核心功能仍处于完善阶段。这款眼镜可能很有趣，但它似乎仍然缺乏明确的用途，人们很难想象这种昂贵的头戴设备（每台 2200 美元）究竟如何在日常生活中发挥作用。

When I asked Snap staff what the device was really for — fun and games or productivity — the answer was vague. The device attempts to be a bunch of things at once and, in the process, ends up feeling a little rudderless. I have racked my brain to come up with some daily use-cases for Specs. Some of the things I came up with were: Watching TV or movies while you’re on a train or some other form of public transportation. There are places where you just have to hang out for awhile and, not infrequently, one way to pass the time is streaming. Specs provides a much more immersive form of mobile entertainment than simply watching a video on your cell phone. The device’s integrations with HBO Max and YouTube make it an appealing entertainment option for users who are in-transit.

当我问及 Snap 的工作人员该设备真正的用途是娱乐游戏还是生产力工具时，得到的回答很模糊。该设备试图同时兼顾多种功能，结果却显得有些漫无目的。我绞尽脑汁想出了一些 Specs 的日常使用场景。其中包括：在火车或其他公共交通工具上观看电视或电影。在某些地方你不得不消磨时间，而流媒体播放往往是打发时间的一种方式。与单纯在手机上看视频相比，Specs 提供了一种更具沉浸感的移动娱乐形式。该设备与 HBO Max 和 YouTube 的集成，使其成为通勤用户的一种诱人娱乐选择。

Reading. Because of the immersive nature of Snap’s headset, I feel like they could be a good option for reading while traveling. When you’re reading with Specs, it feels like you’re locked into whatever text you happen to be looking at. It weirdly helped me focus. An integration for newspapers or Kindle could make the glasses a good way to read while you’re mobile (I’m envisioning the ideal setting being on a plane, train, or the back of a car). Taking easily shareable weird videos. Like many of Meta’s smart glasses, Specs allows users to take photographs and videos, which is an odd but fun component of the user experience. The ability to generate GoPro-like videos and easily text them to your friends is something that I could see the casual user doing on a somewhat regular basis.

阅读。由于 Snap 头显的沉浸式特性，我觉得它们可能是旅行时阅读的好选择。当你戴着 Specs 阅读时，感觉就像被锁定在你正在看的文字上一样。这奇怪地帮助我集中了注意力。如果能集成报纸或 Kindle，这款眼镜可能会成为移动阅读的好工具（我设想的理想场景是在飞机、火车或汽车后座上）。拍摄易于分享的奇特视频。像许多 Meta 的智能眼镜一样，Specs 允许用户拍摄照片和视频，这是用户体验中一个古怪但有趣的组成部分。能够生成类似 GoPro 的视频并轻松通过短信发送给朋友，是我认为普通用户会经常做的事情。

By contrast, here are some of the areas I’m still not totally sold on as Specs use-cases: Specs as a daily workspace and productivity tool. Snap is selling Specs as a way to work. The company claims its headset can “transform almost any location into an immersive, flexible workspace.” However, I have a really hard time imagining Specs being something that the average worker is going to want to incorporate into their workflows. It’s less convenient to strap the glasses to your head than it is to simply sit at your computer typing. What can Specs provide that a cell phone and laptop can’t? The social stigma of wearing weird, large glasses and gesticulating to operate them would also seem to be a deterrent.

相比之下，以下是我对 Specs 的使用场景仍持保留意见的领域：将 Specs 作为日常工作空间和生产力工具。Snap 将 Specs 作为一种工作方式进行销售。该公司声称其头显可以“将几乎任何地点转变为沉浸式、灵活的工作空间”。然而，我很难想象普通员工会愿意将 Specs 纳入他们的工作流程。把眼镜戴在头上远不如坐在电脑前打字方便。Specs 能提供手机和笔记本电脑无法提供的功能吗？佩戴奇怪的大眼镜并挥动手臂进行操作所带来的社会偏见，似乎也是一个阻碍因素。

A widely used gaming device. Due to the prohibitive cost of the device (again, nearly $2200), I have a really hard time imagining that there is going to be a huge audience for Specs as a gaming device. Most Xboxes are under half the price of this device. Are the games on Specs fun? Sure, kind of. But are teenagers going to be clamoring for them come Christmas time? That I’m not so sure about.

作为一种广泛使用的游戏设备。由于该设备价格高昂（再次强调，近 2200 美元），我很难想象 Specs 作为游戏设备会有庞大的受众群体。大多数 Xbox 的价格还不到这款设备的一半。Specs 上的游戏好玩吗？当然，还行。但青少年会在圣诞节时抢购它们吗？这一点我不太确定。

Specs is impressive for how the Snap team has managed to integrate a variety of user experiences into one unified device. There are a lot of impressive achievements here — specifically the spatial synching tech that allows two users to see the same thing simultaneously — that, with some further development, could be deployed towards some really interesting use cases. The bottom line is that Snap’s device can continue to grow and transform as the company iterates. It’s an interesting product that — like a lot of its peers — doesn’t necessarily seem to have a clear purpose but, in time, could become many things. What the device currently offers, however, may not be compelling enough to encourage people to shell out the cash that Snap is asking for.

Snap 团队成功地将多种用户体验整合到一个统一的设备中，这一点令人印象深刻。这里有很多令人瞩目的成就——特别是允许两名用户同时看到相同内容的“空间同步”技术——如果经过进一步开发，可以应用于一些非常有趣的场景。归根结底，随着公司的迭代，Snap 的设备可以继续成长和转型。这是一款有趣的产品，像许多同类产品一样，它似乎没有明确的用途，但随着时间的推移，它可能会演变成多种事物。然而，该设备目前提供的功能可能还不足以吸引人们支付 Snap 所要求的价格。
