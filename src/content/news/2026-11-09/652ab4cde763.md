# Person of Interest

A few days ago, I started watching Person of Interest, and I haven’t been able to stop. Aside from the fact that the show feels even more relevant today than it did 15 years ago, when it first aired, it’s brought back so many memories of the early 2010s, when I first started getting into tech and developing an interest in computers.

几天前，我开始看《疑犯追踪》（Person of Interest），就再也停不下来了。除了这部剧在今天比 15 年前首播时显得更加切合实际之外，它还勾起了我对 2010 年代初的许多回忆，那时我刚开始接触科技，并对计算机产生了兴趣。

It’s reminded me of all the forums and online communities I used to be part of as a teenager, the whole SOPA and PIPA controversy, the shutdown of Megaupload, and just what the Internet was like back then. I also find it pretty crazy and, frankly, a little sad to think that something like the Machine, which seemed like pure science fiction at the time, isn't all that far from our reality today.

它让我想起了青少年时期参与过的所有论坛和在线社区，想起了 SOPA 和 PIPA 的争议、Megaupload 的关闭，以及那时互联网的样子。我也觉得很疯狂，坦白说，想到像“机器”（the Machine）这样在当时看起来纯属科幻的东西，如今离我们的现实并不遥远，这让人感到有些悲哀。

But beyond all that, watching it has made me incredibly nostalgic, and considering it’s already 15 years old, I think it’s aged remarkably well.

但除此之外，观看这部剧让我感到无比怀旧，考虑到它已经有 15 年的历史了，我认为它依然非常耐看。
