# Apple’s M5 MacBook Air is down to its lowest price since July

苹果 M5 MacBook Air 价格降至七月以来的最低点

You can get a temporary break from the $200 price hike that hit both Air sizes this summer.

今年夏天，两款 MacBook Air 机型均经历了 200 美元的涨价，而现在你终于可以获得暂时的价格优惠。

The latest 13-inch MacBook Air is currently available for $1,099.99, marking the second time we’ve seen this $200 discount following the company’s hefty price increases over the summer. Although the base model — which features Apple’s M5 processor, 16GB of RAM, and 512GB of storage — often sold for around $950 to $1,000 shortly after it launched in May, savings have been rare since July, making the current deal worth flagging. Amazon, Best Buy, and B&H Photo are all honoring the price across all color options.

最新的 13 英寸 MacBook Air 目前售价为 1,099.99 美元。这是继该公司夏季大幅涨价后，我们第二次看到 200 美元的折扣。尽管这款配备 M5 处理器、16GB 内存和 512GB 存储空间的基础款机型在 5 月发布后不久常以 950 至 1,000 美元左右的价格出售，但自 7 月以来优惠已十分罕见，因此目前的促销活动值得关注。亚马逊、百思买（Best Buy）和 B&H Photo 均在所有颜色选项中提供此价格。

If you’d prefer something with a larger screen, a six-speaker array instead of four, and slightly more power (with two additional GPU cores in the M5), Apple’s 15-inch MacBook Air is also on sale for $200 off. Amazon, Best Buy, and B&H Photo are each selling the 16GB RAM / 512GB SSD configuration in most colors for $1,299.99.

如果你更倾向于拥有更大屏幕、六扬声器阵列（而非四扬声器）以及更强性能（M5 芯片多出两个 GPU 核心）的机型，苹果 15 英寸 MacBook Air 目前也降价 200 美元。亚马逊、百思买和 B&H Photo 正在以 1,299.99 美元的价格销售大多数颜色的 16GB 内存/512GB SSD 配置版本。

Although it’s pricier than the newer MacBook Neo, the Air offers better value in numerous ways. It’s an ideal choice if you want faster performance, as it offers double the RAM and a speedier CPU with more cores. You also get twice as much storage, better speakers, a backlit keyboard, and a larger, higher-resolution screen. Given said differences, it’s easy to see how the Air’s improvements might make it worth the extra cost for some people.

尽管 Air 比新款 MacBook Neo 更贵，但在许多方面它提供了更好的价值。如果你追求更快的性能，它是理想的选择，因为它提供了双倍的内存和核心数更多、速度更快的 CPU。你还能获得双倍的存储空间、更好的扬声器、背光键盘以及更大、分辨率更高的屏幕。考虑到这些差异，不难看出 Air 的改进对于某些人来说是物有所值的。

不过，如果你还在犹豫不决，我建议阅读我的同事 Antonio G. Di Benedetto 的评测，这两篇评测均发表于夏季涨价之前。
