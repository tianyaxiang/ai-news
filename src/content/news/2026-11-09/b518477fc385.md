### Keep the sentence you just read on a web page, without leaving the page (This week in Graphium, Nov 2 - 8)

本周我致力于三个环节：阅读时摘录内容、整理知识体系，以及与他人分享。

1. Keep an important sentence while you read an article Select a sentence in Chrome and press the toolbar icon, and it becomes a memo in Graphium and is added as a quote to a note for that page. I want to catch as much as possible of what I read, so every clip from the same page goes into one note. It works with the desktop app. (v0.112.0)

1. 在阅读文章时保留重要句子：在 Chrome 中选中一个句子并点击工具栏图标，它就会变成 Graphium 中的一条备忘录，并作为引用添加到该页面的笔记中。我希望尽可能多地捕捉阅读内容，因此来自同一页面的所有剪辑都会进入同一篇笔记。此功能适用于桌面应用程序。（v0.112.0）

2. Quote a paper open beside your note, straight into the note Select text in a PDF opened beside your note and choose "Quote in note", and it goes in at the cursor. I wanted to drop the round trip to the material view. The quote remembers the selected sentence on its own, so even after you rewrite it into a comment, "Open source" takes you back to that sentence. (v0.121.0)

2. 直接将笔记旁打开的论文引用到笔记中：选中笔记旁 PDF 中的文本并选择“引用到笔记”，它就会出现在光标处。我希望省去切换回资料视图的往返操作。引用会自动记住选中的句子，因此即使你将其重写为评论，“打开源文件”功能也能带你回到原句。（v0.121.0）

3. Look after your knowledge without deciding each item yourself Press "Tidy up for me" and the AI runs the check and the source check, then merges duplicates and fixes unbacked sentences. Upkeep that asks you to decide each item one by one is hard to keep up. Where sources disagree, it writes both views down with their sources. Everything it does can be undone from the upkeep history. (v0.117.0, v0.118.0)

3. 无需逐项决策即可打理知识：点击“为我整理”，AI 会运行检查和来源核对，然后合并重复项并修复缺乏依据的句子。要求逐项决策的维护工作很难坚持。当来源存在分歧时，它会记录下两种观点及其来源。它所做的一切都可以从维护历史记录中撤销。（v0.117.0, v0.118.0）

4. Share some things with everyone and keep others inside your team You can now register several shared folders that different people can read, and pick where something goes the first time you share it. Templates may go to the whole organization while findings stay inside a department; what you share decides how far it should reach. Who can read what is left to the folder permissions. (v0.122.0)

4. 与所有人分享部分内容，同时将其他内容保留在团队内部：现在你可以注册多个供不同人员阅读的共享文件夹，并在首次分享时选择存放位置。模板可以发送给整个组织，而研究结果则保留在部门内部；你分享的内容决定了其传播范围。谁能阅读什么内容取决于文件夹权限。（v0.122.0）

5. Light work on a local model, only the hard work on a strong one AI models can now be assigned in three tiers: default, standard and precise. When I measured the main AI features with five models, chat was fine even on a model running on my own machine, while extracting procedures and making plans fell apart without a strong one. Choosing by difficulty rather than by feature seemed easier to get right. (v0.125.0)

5. 轻量任务使用本地模型，复杂任务使用强力模型：AI 模型现在可以分为三个等级：默认、标准和精确。当我用五个模型测试主要 AI 功能时，聊天功能即使在本地机器运行的模型上表现也很好，但提取流程和制定计划的任务如果没有强力模型就会出错。按难度而非功能来选择模型似乎更容易获得正确结果。（v0.125.0）
