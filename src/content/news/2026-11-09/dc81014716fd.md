**Title: What gets refused on Seedance, and how the two review layers differ**

**标题：Seedance 上哪些内容会被拒绝，以及两个审核层级有何不同**

Answer: Seedance reviews a job twice. Layer 1 screens the prompt before any task exists — a clear violation is refused and nothing is charged, while uncertain prompts are allowed and recorded. Layer 2 is the selected model's own safety review, which is stricter: it currently refuses real-person depictions, branded or trademarked content, and political material on the standard route, and it can also fail a job after generation if the frames themselves violate policy. Knowing which layer refused you tells you what to change. A layer-1 refusal is a wording problem. A layer-2 refusal is a category problem — rewriting adjectives will not help; you need a different route or a different subject.

回答：Seedance 会对任务进行两次审核。第一层在任务创建前筛选提示词——明确的违规内容会被拒绝且不扣费，而不确定的提示词则会被允许通过并记录。第二层是所选模型自身的安全审核，其标准更为严格：目前在标准路径下，它会拒绝真人描绘、品牌或商标内容以及政治素材；如果生成的画面本身违反政策，任务也可能在生成后失败。了解是哪一层拒绝了你，就能知道该如何修改。第一层拒绝是措辞问题。第二层拒绝是类别问题——重写形容词没有用；你需要更换路径或更换主题。

The two layers, side by side:
Layer 1 · prompt screening
Layer 2 · model safety review
When: before the task is created (Layer 1); before and during generation (Layer 2)
What it looks at: the prompt text (Layer 1); prompt, references and generated frames (Layer 2)
Clear violation: refused, nothing charged (Layer 1); job fails, credits refunded after reconciliation (Layer 2)
Uncertain case: allowed, recorded for audit (Layer 1); may pass or fail downstream (Layer 2)
What to change: wording (Layer 1); route, subject or references (Layer 2)

两个层级的对比：
第一层 · 提示词筛选
第二层 · 模型安全审核
时间：任务创建前（第一层）；生成前及生成过程中（第二层）
审核对象：提示词文本（第一层）；提示词、参考图及生成的画面（第二层）
明确违规：拒绝，不扣费（第一层）；任务失败，核对后退还积分（第二层）
不确定情况：允许通过，记录以供审计（第一层）；后续可能通过或失败（第二层）
修改建议：措辞（第一层）；路径、主题或参考内容（第二层）

Neither layer is a local keyword list. Our screening lets uncertain prompts through and records them, which is why the same sentence can pass on one route and fail on another. What layer 2 refuses on the standard route: Real-person depictions. The published page lists them as prohibited, and in practice the standard route still passes most ordinary real-face jobs — it is the model's review being stricter than the brief. When it refuses, the ultra-wide route is the fallback. Branded or trademarked content. Brands, franchises, studios, games and licensed characters are likely to fail. Generic visual descriptions survive. Political material. Generated frames that violate policy. The prompt can pass and the render still fail, because the check also sees the pictures.

这两个层级都不是本地关键词列表。我们的筛选机制允许不确定的提示词通过并进行记录，这就是为什么同一句话在一条路径上可以通过，而在另一条路径上却会失败。第二层在标准路径下拒绝的内容包括：真人描绘。发布页面将其列为禁止项，但在实践中，标准路径仍能通过大多数普通的真人面孔任务——这是因为模型的审核比说明文档更严格。当它拒绝时，超宽路径（ultra-wide route）是备选方案。品牌或商标内容。品牌、特许经营、工作室、游戏和授权角色很可能会失败。通用的视觉描述则可以存活。政治素材。违反政策的生成画面。提示词可能通过但渲染失败，因为审核也会检查图片。

The two errors you will see, and what they mean:
"Your content violated community guidelines." That is a guideline refusal. It is not a billing event: a prompt-level refusal creates no task and charges nothing; a job that fails later is refunded automatically after reconciliation, and the activity page shows a refund note on the failed task.
"Video generation failed due to a server error. Please try again." That one is not a policy decision at all — it is a temporary model-service failure. Retrying is the correct response, and it is also the message people most often misread as a ban.

你可能会看到的两种错误及其含义：
“您的内容违反了社区准则。”这是准则拒绝。这不是计费事件：提示词层面的拒绝不会创建任务，也不会扣费；后续失败的任务会在核对后自动退款，活动页面会显示失败任务的退款说明。
“视频生成因服务器错误失败。请重试。”这根本不是政策决定——这是临时的模型服务故障。重试是正确的应对方式，这也是人们最常误读为封禁的信息。

Rewrites that change the outcome:
Refused: A photo of a real client
Why: real person
Rewrite: retry on the ultra-wide route, or use a generated face
Refused: "in the style of [franchise]"
Why: trademarked property
Rewrite: describe the visual: palette, lens, texture, era
Refused: A named product on the bottle
Why: brand reference
Rewrite: "unlabelled amber glass bottle"
Refused: A crowd scene with a real politician
Why: political content
Rewrite: remove the figure, keep the composition
Refused: Explicit prompt
Why: sexual content
Rewrite: cut the act, describe framing and wardrobe

能够改变结果的重写示例：
被拒：真实客户的照片
原因：真人
重写：在超宽路径上重试，或使用生成的面孔
被拒：“[特许品牌]风格”
原因：商标财产
重写：描述视觉效果：调色板、镜头、质感、时代
被拒：瓶子上有具体产品名称
原因：品牌引用
重写：“无标签琥珀色玻璃瓶”
被拒：有真实政治人物的人群场景
原因：政治内容
重写：移除该人物，保留构图
被拒：露骨的提示词
原因：色情内容
重写：删减动作，描述构图和服装

The pattern in every row: replace the identity the model is not allowed to reproduce with a description it is allowed to render. What good behaviour looks like in practice: Start generic, then add specificity. Pass the scene first; add the reference second. When something fails you will know which addition did it. Keep a note per rejection. "Brand name", "real face on standard", "visual layer" — three different fixes that people otherwise treat as one. Simplify on the second failure. Repeated refusals on a long prompt are usually one clause; shortening the prompt finds it faster than editing adjectives. Do not retry a policy refusal. A refusal is deterministic. A server error is not — those, retry.

每一行的规律都是：将模型不允许复现的身份替换为它允许渲染的描述。实践中的良好行为表现为：从通用开始，然后增加具体细节。先通过场景，再添加参考。当某项失败时，你就会知道是哪项添加导致了失败。为每次拒绝做笔记。“品牌名称”、“标准路径下的真人面孔”、“视觉层”——这是三种不同的修复方法，人们往往将其混为一谈。第二次失败时请简化。长提示词反复被拒通常是因为其中一个从句；缩短提示词比修改形容词能更快找到原因。不要重试政策性拒绝。拒绝是确定性的。服务器错误则不是——遇到服务器错误，请重试。

Where this is genuinely annoying: Passes screening, fails at render. You paid the wall-clock time and got a refund, not a clip. Nothing local to test against. The rules are the model's, so a prompt that worked last week can fail today if the review changes. Ultra-wide is not immunity. It is a broader path with the same platform limits, and it costs more.

这真正令人恼火的地方在于：通过了筛选，却在渲染时失败。你支付了挂钟时间，结果只得到了退款，而不是视频片段。没有本地工具可供测试。规则属于模型，因此上周有效的提示词如果审核标准发生变化，今天可能会失败。超宽路径并非豁免权。它只是一个更宽的路径，但仍受相同的平台限制，且成本更高。

FAQ:
Do refusals cost credits? Not on layer 1. On a later failure, credits are refunded automatically after the task is reconciled as failed.
Is the ultra-wide route unfiltered? No. It is a wider review path; the acceptable-use policy still applies and requests can still be refused.
Can I turn the safety check off? The generator exposes an optional pre-generation content check, but turning it off does not remove the platform's own rules — it only stops sending that optional flag.
Why did a prompt that worked before stop working? Because these checks belong to the model and can change. Treat prompt behaviour as dated, like a price.

常见问题解答：
拒绝会扣除积分吗？第一层不会。后续失败时，任务被核对为失败后，积分会自动退还。
超宽路径是不受过滤的吗？不是。它是一个更宽的审核路径；可接受使用政策仍然适用，请求仍可能被拒绝。
我可以关闭安全检查吗？生成器提供了一个可选的生成前内容检查，但关闭它并不能移除平台自身的规则——它只是停止发送该可选标志。
为什么以前有效的提示词现在失效了？因为这些检查属于模型，且会发生变化。请将提示词的表现视为有时效性的，就像价格一样。
