**Adjust Your Phone’s Settings**

**调整手机设置**

Before diving in, I strongly recommend increasing your phone’s touchscreen sensitivity. Here’s how: On an Android phone: Go to Settings, Display and touch, Touch sensitivity, and toggle Increased sensitivity on. These settings may vary slightly on different Android phones. On an iPhone: Go to Settings, Accessibility, Touch, tap Haptic Touch or 3D & Haptic Touch, then select Fast. You should also consider tweaking some of the settings in Settings, Accessibility, Touch, and Touch Accommodations.

在开始之前，我强烈建议提高手机的触屏灵敏度。方法如下：安卓手机：进入“设置”，找到“显示与触摸”或“触控灵敏度”，开启“增加灵敏度”选项。不同安卓机型的设置路径可能略有差异。iPhone：进入“设置”，点击“辅助功能”，选择“触控”，点击“触感触控”或“3D触控与触感触控”，然后选择“快”。此外，你也可以考虑在“设置”、“辅助功能”、“触控”以及“触控调节”中进行一些微调。
