# Epic Games Launcher is coming to SteamOS

Epic Games confirmed that its Linux launcher will officially support SteamOS and Steam Decks.

Epic Games 确认其 Linux 启动器将正式支持 SteamOS 和 Steam Deck。

In October, Epic Games said that it was “investigating” making Fortnite compatible with Valve’s Linux-based SteamOS. At the time, Epic Games Launcher was in preview, but SteamOS was not specifically listed as a supported distro. Now, Communications Director Jake Jones has confirmed SteamOS compatibility, along with a handful of other distros, to GamingOnLinux.

今年 10 月，Epic Games 表示正在“研究”如何让《堡垒之夜》兼容 Valve 基于 Linux 的 SteamOS。当时，Epic Games Launcher 尚处于预览阶段，且 SteamOS 并未被明确列为受支持的发行版。现在，传播总监 Jake Jones 向 GamingOnLinux 证实了 SteamOS 以及其他几种发行版的兼容性。

In response to a question about the AppImage being replaced with Debian / Ubuntu .deb downloads, Jones said: “The new Epic Games Launcher is still in Preview and we plan to support as many Linux distributions as possible, including Fedora, SteamOS, Cachy, Omarchy, and more. We’re testing and evaluating packaging formats for distribution, and we’ll restore AppImage this month.”

在回应关于 AppImage 是否会被 Debian / Ubuntu .deb 下载包取代的问题时，Jones 表示：“新的 Epic Games Launcher 仍处于预览阶段，我们计划支持尽可能多的 Linux 发行版，包括 Fedora、SteamOS、Cachy、Omarchy 等。我们正在测试和评估分发用的打包格式，并将在本月恢复 AppImage。”

That doesn’t necessarily mean Fortnite will come to SteamOS. Easy Anti-Cheat (EAC) Linux v2 support still remains an open question. When it announced its work to bring Fortnite to Linux, the company said that the Steam Deck “does not support the required features for our EAC v2 solution, but we are actively talking with Valve and hope to support it in the future.”

这并不一定意味着《堡垒之夜》会登陆 SteamOS。Easy Anti-Cheat (EAC) Linux v2 的支持问题仍然悬而未决。当该公司宣布将《堡垒之夜》引入 Linux 时，曾表示 Steam Deck “不支持我们 EAC v2 解决方案所需的功能，但我们正在与 Valve 积极沟通，并希望在未来提供支持。”
