For years, mysterious electrical anomalies have been flummoxing grid managers around the world. Every 60 seconds, the frequency on the grid would drop a tiny bit. Even weirder: The sags happened at the exact same time on power grids across countries and continents. It was a mystery that Dirk Witthaut, a researcher at Forschungszentrum Jülich in Germany, couldn’t pass up. Germany’s largest transmission system operator, Tennet Germany, brought the puzzle to Witthaut, and it took years of investigative and modeling work to determine the cause. From Europe to Australia, the U.S. to Malaysia, the same pattern shows up in the data. Such frequency drops aren’t unheard of, but they are troubling. And the regularity with which they have been occurring can make the phenomenon feel even more ominous.

多年来，神秘的电力异常现象一直困扰着世界各地的电网管理者。每隔 60 秒，电网频率就会出现微小的下降。更奇怪的是，这种下降在不同国家和大陆的电网中几乎同时发生。这是一个德国于利希研究中心（Forschungszentrum Jülich）的研究员德克·维特豪特（Dirk Witthaut）无法忽视的谜题。德国最大的输电系统运营商 Tennet Germany 将这一难题交给了维特豪特，经过多年的调查和建模工作，他们终于确定了原因。从欧洲到澳大利亚，从美国到马来西亚，数据中都显示出同样的模式。这种频率下降并非闻所未闻，但确实令人担忧。而这种现象发生的规律性，更让其显得有些不祥。

Balancing the grid The grid’s orderly operation relies on the frequency being kept within a certain range. If the oscillations get get too large, or if grid operators don’t know the cause and respond improperly, they can lead to blackouts. Even if the sags don’t cause blackouts, they can damage equipment in power plants if they go unchecked, especially in hydropower turbines. Hydropower plants have historically been a main driver of so-called ultra-low frequency oscillations (UFLOs). Every turbine vibrates, and if those vibrations occur with a certain regularity, known as the resonant frequency, they can magnify, sometimes to dangerous levels. Think of a wine glass breaking before an opera singer’s mighty pipes — that’s resonance.

电网平衡：电网的有序运行依赖于将频率保持在一定范围内。如果振荡幅度过大，或者电网运营商因不明原因而应对不当，就可能导致停电。即使这些下降不会引发停电，如果任其发展，也会损坏发电厂的设备，尤其是水力发电机组。从历史上看，水电站一直是所谓的超低频振荡（UFLO）的主要诱因。每个涡轮机都会振动，如果这些振动以某种规律发生（即共振频率），它们就会被放大，有时甚至达到危险的程度。想象一下歌剧演员的高音震碎酒杯——这就是共振。

So Witthaut and his team started investigating hydropower, the most obvious potential culprit. They used a computer model of a hydropower plant control system on the Scandinavian grid to test the hypothesis. While they managed to find conditions that could generate a resonance that peaked once per minute, they couldn’t explain why it persisted for so long. Typically, resonances in mechanical systems will come and go, and so will the accompanying signal on the electrical grid. But this frequency drop kept happening minute after minute after minute. Every 60 seconds, without fail. It then dawned on the team that anything operating with such clockwork required, well, a clock. How else could they explain the uncanny timing?

因此，维特豪特和他的团队开始调查最明显的潜在罪魁祸首——水力发电。他们利用斯堪的纳维亚电网水力发电厂控制系统的计算机模型来验证这一假设。虽然他们成功找到了能产生每分钟一次共振峰值的条件，但无法解释为什么这种现象会持续这么久。通常情况下，机械系统中的共振会时有时无，电网上的伴随信号也是如此。但这种频率下降却一分钟接一分钟地持续发生，每 60 秒一次，从未间断。团队随后意识到，任何以这种时钟般规律运行的事物都需要一个“时钟”。否则，他们该如何解释这种不可思议的同步性呢？

Searching for clues To that hypothesis, the researchers started close to home, installing more than 80 smart power quality meters around the Jülich campus. The research site is outfitted with medium- and low-voltage distribution lines, transformers, solar panels, and battery energy storage systems that serve as uninterruptible power supplies (UPS). It also has a high-performance computer system as well as the usual office computers powered by much smaller UPS devices. The power quality meters sampled a range of attributes, including frequency, voltage, current, and power, once every three seconds. Before long, the research team had its answer.

寻找线索：为了验证这一假设，研究人员从身边做起，在于利希校园周围安装了 80 多台智能电能质量监测仪。该研究基地配备了中低压配电线路、变压器、太阳能电池板以及作为不间断电源（UPS）使用的电池储能系统。此外，这里还有高性能计算机系统以及由小型 UPS 设备供电的普通办公电脑。电能质量监测仪每三秒采样一次包括频率、电压、电流和功率在内的一系列属性。不久之后，研究团队就找到了答案。

The measurements recorded on the transformer attached to the battery energy storage system peaked every minute, just like on the bigger power grids. It turns out that many stationary batteries, from data center UPSes to large grid-scale batteries, check their state-of-charge every minute. Since these devices are internet-connected, their clocks are in near perfect synchronicity. Every time one checks its charge, the disturbance is imperceptible on the wide grid. But when they all do it together, that disruption becomes apparent. To find evidence that UPSes were the main culprit on the grid, Witthaut and his team turned to data provided by grid operators. They found their answer in Ireland.

连接在电池储能系统上的变压器所记录的测量值每分钟都会出现峰值，就像在大型电网上一样。事实证明，许多固定式电池，从数据中心的 UPS 到大型电网级电池，都会每分钟检查一次充电状态。由于这些设备都连接在互联网上，它们的时钟几乎处于完美的同步状态。每当其中一个检查充电状态时，这种干扰在广阔的电网上是察觉不到的。但当它们同时进行检查时，这种干扰就变得显而易见了。为了寻找 UPS 是电网主要罪魁祸首的证据，维特豪特和他的团队转向了电网运营商提供的数据。他们在爱尔兰找到了答案。

There, data centers have grown to consume a large fraction of the country’s electricity. By 2022, data centers represented 18% of the load on Ireland’s grid, and each facility is served by a fleet of UPSes. Sure enough, between 2016 and 2021, the minute-by-minute frequency drop on the Irish grid quadrupled from 3 milli-Hertz (mHz) in 2016 to 12 mHz in 2021. More recently, some drops have exceeded 20 mHz. Ireland’s 50 Hz grid is built to withstand swings of plus or minus 200 mHz, though the grid operator would rather limit the swings to 100 mHz. In that range, 20 mHz isn’t a lot, but data centers aren’t the only loads on the grid. If other loads also cause the frequency to sag, the extra bump from every UPS checking its state-of-charge could push the grid toward blackout conditions.

在爱尔兰，数据中心消耗了该国很大一部分电力。到 2022 年，数据中心占爱尔兰电网负荷的 18%，且每个设施都配备了一系列 UPS。果不其然，在 2016 年至 2021 年间，爱尔兰电网每分钟的频率下降幅度翻了两番，从 2016 年的 3 毫赫兹（mHz）增加到 2021 年的 12 毫赫兹。最近，一些下降幅度甚至超过了 20 毫赫兹。爱尔兰的 50 赫兹电网设计可承受正负 200 毫赫兹的波动，尽管电网运营商更希望将波动限制在 100 毫赫兹以内。在这个范围内，20 毫赫兹并不算多，但数据中心并不是电网上唯一的负载。如果其他负载也导致频率下降，那么每个 UPS 检查充电状态所带来的额外冲击，可能会将电网推向停电的边缘。

The solution The problem is entirely preventable. Rather than check state-of-charge exactly on the minute, Witthaus and team recommend that UPS and grid-scale battery manufacturers add some randomization to the schedule. While UPSes are driving the problem today, there’s a risk that the phenomenon could persist. The researchers also tested IoT devices. In devices as diverse as a smart oven and Raspberry Pi clusters, they were able to detect periodic power fluctuations. The fix is also a simple software patch, but many IoT devices go unpatched. A recent study found that less than 3% of IoT devices are running the latest firmware. Regulators may soon have to step in to demand changes, but they can only address their corner of the grid. Given the global nature of the issue, it’ll ultimately require global coordination. The mystery may be solved, but the problem could remain for years to come.

解决方案：这个问题完全是可以预防的。维特豪特和他的团队建议，UPS 和电网级电池制造商不要在整点时刻检查充电状态，而应在时间安排中加入一些随机性。虽然 UPS 是目前导致该问题的主要原因，但这种现象仍有持续存在的风险。研究人员还测试了物联网设备。在智能烤箱和树莓派集群等多种设备中，他们都检测到了周期性的功率波动。修复方法同样是一个简单的软件补丁，但许多物联网设备并未进行更新。最近的一项研究发现，只有不到 3% 的物联网设备运行着最新的固件。监管机构可能很快就需要介入并要求做出改变，但他们只能解决电网中各自管辖范围的问题。鉴于该问题的全球性，最终需要全球范围内的协调。谜题或许已经解开，但问题可能会在未来几年内持续存在。
