Sequoia Capital announced some major changes this week, including the closure of its London office, the merger of its early-stage and growth teams, and the end of its Scouts program. Now, the Financial Times has published a report painting all of this as an attempt to undo the legacy of former leader Roelof Botha, who stepped down as the firm’s steward last year.

红杉资本本周宣布了一些重大变革，包括关闭其伦敦办事处、合并早期投资团队与成长型投资团队，以及终止其“星探”（Scouts）项目。目前，《金融时报》发布了一篇报道，将这一切描述为试图抹去前领导人 Roelof Botha 的遗产，后者于去年卸任了该公司的掌舵人职务。

An unnamed source told the FT that Botha “loved process and hierarchy,” while “the rest of the team didn’t.” Apparently, not everyone is on-board with the changes, with another source firing back that this seems like an “erasure of Sequoia’s heritage, not Roelof’s legacy.”

一位匿名消息人士告诉《金融时报》，Botha “热爱流程和等级制度”，而“团队的其他成员则不然”。显然，并非所有人都支持这些变革，另一位消息人士反驳称，这看起来像是“对红杉传统的抹杀，而非对 Roelof 遗产的清除”。

The break between Botha and his old firm reportedly deepened after Botha joined the SpaceX board this summer. (Botha and SpaceX CEO Elon Musk previously worked together at PayPal.) A source told the FT that Sequoia’s partners were blindsided by the announcement, because Botha “didn’t ask compliance, he didn’t get sign off from the partnership” — even though his new role could complicate the firm’s ability to trade its $20 billion stake.

据报道，在 Botha 今年夏天加入 SpaceX 董事会后，他与老东家之间的裂痕加深了。（Botha 和 SpaceX 首席执行官 Elon Musk 此前曾在 PayPal 共事。）一位消息人士告诉《金融时报》，红杉的合伙人对这一公告感到措手不及，因为 Botha “没有咨询合规部门，也没有获得合伙人团队的批准”——尽管他的新角色可能会使该公司交易其 200 亿美元股份的能力变得复杂。

A Sequoia spokesperson said that the firm continues to “collaborate closely” with Botha.

红杉资本的一位发言人表示，该公司继续与 Botha “保持密切合作”。
