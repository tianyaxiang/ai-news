# More Motion Is Not Always Better Motion: Corpus Composition Governs Whether Augmentation Helps SMPL-Based Parkinsonian Gait Severity Estimation

**更多动作并不总是更好的动作：语料库构成决定了数据增强是否有助于基于 SMPL 的帕金森步态严重程度评估**

***

**Abstract:** We grade MDS-UPDRS gait severity from SMPL motion using three frozen MotionAGFormer encoders as featurizers, reaching macro-F1 0.58 on a hidden, multi-site test set. Because the system's members differ only in their lifting corpus, evaluating encoders singly on that test set isolates what that corpus contributes.

**摘要：** 我们使用三个冻结的 MotionAGFormer 编码器作为特征提取器，从 SMPL 动作中评估 MDS-UPDRS 步态严重程度，在一个隐藏的多中心测试集上达到了 0.58 的宏观 F1 分数（macro-F1）。由于系统各成员之间的唯一区别在于其训练语料库，因此在测试集上单独评估编码器，可以分离出该语料库的具体贡献。

***

Six pools drawn from one inertial dataset, varying only in which walking tasks they include, score between 0.32 and 0.53, and just one of them beats the 0.51 of an encoder given no outside motion at all. What separates them is not how much data they hold but whether they carry a contrast in walking speed, the variation this representation appears to depend : a further pool adding a third collection site at fixed task composition does worse still.

从一个惯性数据集中提取的六个数据池（仅在包含的行走任务上有所不同）得分在 0.32 到 0.53 之间，其中只有一个超过了未引入任何外部动作数据的编码器所取得的 0.51 分。区分它们的关键不在于数据量的大小，而在于它们是否包含行走速度的对比——这似乎是该表征所依赖的变异性：另一个在固定任务构成下增加第三个采集点的数据池表现甚至更差。

***

The same rule explains why exact synthetic motion and monocularly reconstructed web video both fail to help. Modifying the learned representation itself, rather than the corpus behind it, cost every variant that attempted it.

同样的规律也解释了为什么精确的合成动作和单目重建的网络视频都无法提供帮助。尝试修改学习到的表征本身（而非修改其背后的语料库）的每一个变体，最终都付出了性能下降的代价。
