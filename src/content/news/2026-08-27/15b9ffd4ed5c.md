# Amazon knocks $150 off Pixel 11 phones, with up to $200 in gift cards
# 亚马逊 Pixel 11 系列手机直降 150 美元，另赠最高 200 美元礼品卡

If you’ve been on the fence about Google’s latest Pixel phones, this might be your best chance to save some cash. Amazon is offering an extra $150 off the Pixel 11, Pixel 11 Pro, and Pixel 11 Pro XL when you use the coupon code PIXEL11 at checkout. While the discounted price appeared at checkout for most phone and watch combinations, it didn’t apply to the Pixel Pro Fold.

如果你还在犹豫是否要购买谷歌最新的 Pixel 手机，这可能是你省钱的最佳时机。亚马逊目前提供额外优惠：在结账时使用优惠码“PIXEL11”，购买 Pixel 11、Pixel 11 Pro 和 Pixel 11 Pro XL 即可立减 150 美元。虽然大多数手机和手表的组合在结账时都能看到折扣价，但该优惠不适用于 Pixel Pro Fold。

That deal stacks with the introductory offer running at several retailers, where you can choose between a gift card that scales with the model of phone you’re buying, or additional credit towards a Pixel Watch 5. For example, the base model Pixel 11 would normally cost $899, but the introductory bundle, which ends on August 27th, includes a $100 gift card, and with the code, you’ll pay just $749. The Pixel 11 Pro and Pro XL bundles include either a $200 gift card, or $250 towards the cost of a Pixel Watch 5.

该优惠可以与多家零售商正在进行的促销活动叠加。你可以选择根据所购机型获取相应金额的礼品卡，或者选择购买 Pixel Watch 5 的额外抵扣额度。例如，基础款 Pixel 11 原价为 899 美元，但 8 月 27 日截止的促销套装包含一张 100 美元的礼品卡，叠加优惠码后，你只需支付 749 美元。Pixel 11 Pro 和 Pro XL 的套装则包含 200 美元礼品卡，或 250 美元的 Pixel Watch 5 抵扣额度。

The Pixel 11 phones added a variety of new features to Google’s first-party Android phones, most notably around the cameras. In addition to improved sensors across the lineup, the new Magic Capture feature automatically pulls photos and clips out of a video recording, letting you focus on what’s in front of you.

Pixel 11 系列为谷歌自家的 Android 手机增加了一系列新功能，其中最引人注目的是相机方面的升级。除了全系传感器性能的提升外，全新的“魔法捕捉”（Magic Capture）功能可以自动从视频录制中提取照片和片段，让你能够专注于眼前发生的事情。
