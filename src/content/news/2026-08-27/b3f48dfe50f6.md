# France reaches 94.9% fiber coverage in 2026
# 法国2026年光纤覆盖率达到94.9%

This map is published by Arcep with the aim of improving information for the general public and professionals regarding the deployment of fiber-to-the-home (FttH) networks. Established at both the municipal level and the technical level (generally sub-municipal), it is part of the fixed broadband and ultra-fast broadband observatory set up by the Authority and is accompanied by the publication of underlying data as open data.

该地图由法国电子通信与邮政监管局（Arcep）发布，旨在为公众和专业人士提供有关光纤到户（FttH）网络部署的更好信息。该地图涵盖了市镇级和技术级（通常为市镇以下级别）的数据，是该机构建立的固定宽带和超高速宽带监测系统的一部分，并同步发布了相关的开放数据。

The advanced mode offers four additional views: a departmental view that allows for visualizing the FttH coverage rate by department; an advanced municipal view that provides additional information on the operators deploying the networks and their deployment intentions; a view by "mutualization point" rear zone (ZAPM) that allows for visualizing the FttH infrastructure at this technical level; and a view of FttH regulatory zones that identifies municipalities in very high-density areas, distinguishing low-density pockets from the rest of the very high-density areas.

高级模式提供了四种额外的视图：部门视图，可查看各省的FttH覆盖率；高级市镇视图，可获取有关网络部署运营商及其部署意图的更多信息；互联点后方区域（ZAPM）视图，可在该技术层面可视化FttH基础设施；以及FttH监管区域视图，用于识别高密度区域的市镇，并区分高密度区域内的低密度地块与其余高密度区域。

Operators transmit detailed data on their fiber optic network deployment to Arcep in their reference files (known as "IPE" files). The Authority then aggregates the data obtained using INSEE geographical codes to obtain an absolute number of premises connectable to the FttH network for each municipality.

运营商将其光纤网络部署的详细数据以参考文件（即“IPE”文件）的形式传输给Arcep。随后，监管机构使用法国国家统计与经济研究所（INSEE）的地理代码对所获数据进行汇总，从而得出每个市镇可接入FttH网络的场所绝对数量。

The FttH coverage rate of a municipality evaluates the proportion of housing or professional premises connectable to one or more FttH networks. It is an estimate that relates the total number of premises connectable to the FttH network declared by operators to an estimate of the total number of premises in the municipality, evaluated from the operators' IPEs or from data published by INSEE in the absence of IPEs.

市镇的FttH覆盖率评估了可接入一个或多个FttH网络的住宅或商业场所的比例。这是一个估算值，将运营商申报的可接入FttH网络的场所总数，与根据运营商IPE或在缺乏IPE时根据INSEE发布的数据所估算的市镇场所总数进行对比得出。
