# The Switch 2’s $50 price increase is happening next week
# Switch 2 将于下周涨价 50 美元

This is a friendly reminder that the Nintendo Switch 2 will jump in price by $50 starting on September 1st. That’s next Tuesday! The company gave plenty of notice, but the time has finally come. The base 256GB model that ships without a game will go from $449.99 to $499.99. Nintendo recently announced two bundles (both well over $500) shipping this fall that include a game with a slight discount.

温馨提示：任天堂 Switch 2 的价格将于 9 月 1 日起上调 50 美元。也就是下周二！任天堂此前已提前发布了充分的预告，但现在涨价时刻终于要到了。不含游戏的 256GB 基础版机型价格将从 449.99 美元涨至 499.99 美元。任天堂近期还宣布了将于今年秋季发售的两款捆绑套装（售价均远超 500 美元），其中包含一款游戏并提供小额折扣。

If you’re on the fence for a Switch 2, now is a pretty good time to buy one — not just because of the upcoming price increase. The game library is stellar and growing quickly, and the console itself is great, too. You’ll need one to play the remake of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time, The Duskbloods, and plenty of other upcoming games.

如果你还在犹豫是否要入手 Switch 2，现在是一个相当不错的购买时机——这不仅仅是因为即将到来的涨价。其游戏库阵容强大且增长迅速，主机本身的表现也非常出色。你需要一台 Switch 2 来游玩《塞尔达传说：时之笛》重制版、《The Duskbloods》以及许多其他即将推出的游戏。

The Nintendo Switch 2 has a 7.9-inch LCD display, magnetically attached Joy-Con 2 controllers, backwards compatibility with most original Switch games, and exclusive titles including Donkey Kong Bananza and Kirby Air Riders.

任天堂 Switch 2 配备了 7.9 英寸 LCD 显示屏、磁吸式 Joy-Con 2 手柄，支持大多数原版 Switch 游戏的向下兼容，并拥有包括《大金刚：香蕉狂热》(Donkey Kong Bananza) 和《卡比空骑》(Kirby Air Riders) 在内的独占游戏。

Not that the Switch 2 was ever cheap, but it was one of a few devices left out there that didn’t quickly get more expensive due to RAMageddon. Alas, no tech product will escape RAMageddon unscathed, especially one like the Switch 2 that has speedy RAM and storage. Almost all competing handhelds cost more than before, too, though Nintendo’s $50 price increase is soft compared to the $200-plus increase for the Steam Deck OLED.

Switch 2 本身并不便宜，但它曾是少数几款没有因为“内存危机”(RAMageddon) 而迅速涨价的设备之一。遗憾的是，没有任何科技产品能在这场内存危机中幸免，尤其是像 Switch 2 这样配备高速内存和存储空间的设备。几乎所有竞品掌机的价格也都比以前更高了，不过与 Steam Deck OLED 超过 200 美元的涨幅相比，任天堂 50 美元的涨价幅度还算温和。
