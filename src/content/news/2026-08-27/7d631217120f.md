# How to Format Your TDS Draft: A New and Improved Guide
# 如何排版您的 TDS 草稿：一份全新升级的指南

Whether you're a first-time author or a TDS veteran, welcome to our formatting guide! As of August 2026, we launched our bespoke author platform to replace the WordPress site we'd previously used. You can expect a much easier, more intuitive, and more streamlined drafting experience here, but in case you'd like some guidance on how to upload your work to TDS, you're in the right place.

无论您是首次投稿的作者还是 TDS 的资深用户，欢迎阅读我们的排版指南！自 2026 年 8 月起，我们推出了定制的作者平台，以取代之前使用的 WordPress 站点。在这里，您可以体验到更简单、更直观、更精简的写作流程。如果您需要关于如何将作品上传至 TDS 的指导，那么您来对地方了。

### Essential Elements
### 核心要素

Whenever you'd like to start a new article draft, look for the + New Article button on the top-right corner of the page. You'll immediately see a pop-up modal inviting you to add key details about your work, like the title, subtitle, main category, and tags. If you're not sure about some of these, that's fine — the only required field at this stage is the article's title. And you can always edit these details later if you'd like.

每当您想开始撰写新文章草稿时，请点击页面右上角的“+ New Article”（新建文章）按钮。您会立即看到一个弹出窗口，提示您添加作品的关键信息，如标题、副标题、主分类和标签。如果您对其中某些内容尚不确定也没关系——现阶段唯一必填的字段是文章标题。而且，您随时可以在之后编辑这些详细信息。

Once you click on the Create Article button you'll reach the editor screen. The other required element to think about now is the featured image. Look to the sidebar to find the options for choosing or creating one.

点击“Create Article”（创建文章）按钮后，您将进入编辑器界面。现在需要考虑的另一个必要元素是特色图片（Featured Image）。请查看侧边栏，找到选择或创建图片的选项。

If you've created a custom image for your article, fantastic — click on Upload image and choose it from your computer. If you've already uploaded your image to your media library, or decided to repurpose an image from an older article, pick Choose from library and select the image you want.

如果您已经为文章制作了自定义图片，那太棒了——点击“Upload image”（上传图片）并从电脑中选择即可。如果您已经将图片上传到媒体库，或者决定重复使用旧文章中的图片，请选择“Choose from library”（从库中选择）并选中您想要的图片。

You also have the option to generate your image using AI directly from your draft using — you guessed it — the Generate with AI button. Click on it and a new modal will appear. Just type in your prompt — feel free to be as descriptive and specific as you want — and click Generate. After a few seconds you'll see several options to choose from. If you ever need some additional guidance on choosing a featured image, or want to refresh your memory around our guidelines, here's a FAQ that covers the topic.

您还可以选择直接在草稿中使用 AI 生成图片，只需点击——没错——“Generate with AI”（使用 AI 生成）按钮。点击后会弹出一个新窗口。只需输入您的提示词（Prompt）——请尽情发挥，描述得越具体越好——然后点击“Generate”（生成）。几秒钟后，您将看到多个选项供选择。如果您在选择特色图片时需要额外指导，或者想回顾我们的相关准则，请查看这份涵盖该主题的常见问题解答（FAQ）。

### Adding the contents of your article
### 添加文章内容

With the essential elements out of the way, it's time to actually upload your article. Our streamlined UI will get your draft ready for review with minimal hassle. To add regular text, simply start typing. To add any other kind of content or media, always start with the / key. A block menu will pop up, showing all the available content types you can add.

核心要素准备就绪后，现在是时候上传您的文章了。我们精简的 UI 界面能让您的草稿以最少的麻烦进入审核状态。要添加普通文本，直接输入即可。要添加任何其他类型的内容或媒体，请始终以“/”键开头。此时会弹出一个区块菜单，显示所有您可以添加的内容类型。

Use the up and down arrows to scroll through the list, choose the block you want at that point in your article, and go from there. Let's take a closer look at your options.

使用上下箭头键浏览列表，选择您在文章当前位置所需的区块，然后继续操作。让我们详细了解一下您的选项。

#### Text
#### 文本

Self-explanatory! Just type in or paste your written content. Whenever you start a new paragraph, it's set to text by default, so you don't actually need to use the / key (unless you really want to). To add special styling to any part of your text, highlight the relevant portion and you'll see a menu pop up: You can choose between bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, and inline code. This is also where you'll find the link button and alignment options. The text box symbol is where you should go if you want to leave a comment for our editors. Click on it, write your comment, tag someone specific from our team if you'd like (using the @ symbol), and click on Comment to submit it. Tip: to find all your comments in one neat space, look to your sidebar, and click on the Threads tab.

不言自明！只需输入或粘贴您的文字内容即可。每当您开始新段落时，系统默认设置为文本，因此您实际上不需要使用“/”键（除非您确实想用）。要为文本的任何部分添加特殊样式，请选中相关部分，您会看到一个弹出菜单：您可以选择加粗、斜体、下划线、删除线和行内代码。这里也是您找到链接按钮和对齐选项的地方。如果您想给我们的编辑留言，请点击文本框图标。点击它，写下您的评论，如果需要，还可以标记我们团队的特定成员（使用“@”符号），然后点击“Comment”（评论）进行提交。提示：要在一个整洁的空间内查看所有评论，请查看侧边栏并点击“Threads”（讨论串）选项卡。

#### Headings
#### 标题

Creating a clear structural hierarchy within your article helps both readers and search engines navigate your content. Add a new heading whenever you start a new section, and choose the right one depending on its position within your article. When you type /heading you'll see six levels available. Always start new sections with H2, and go down the hierarchy from there: for example, Section 1 in H2, Section 1.1 in H3, Section 1.11 in H4, etc. Please don't use H1 headings in the body of the article.

在文章中创建清晰的结构层级有助于读者和搜索引擎导航您的内容。每当开始一个新章节时，请添加一个新标题，并根据其在文章中的位置选择合适的层级。当您输入“/heading”时，您会看到六个可用级别。请始终以 H2 开始新章节，并以此向下延伸：例如，第 1 节用 H2，第 1.1 节用 H3，第 1.11 节用 H4，以此类推。请勿在文章正文中使用 H1 标题。

#### Lists
#### 列表

You have the option to create both bulleted and numbered lists, depending on your needs — just choose the right block from the list and you're good to go. Tip: use the tab button to nest a secondary list within your primary list.

您可以根据需要创建项目符号列表或编号列表——只需从列表中选择正确的区块即可。提示：使用 Tab 键可以在主列表中嵌套二级列表。

#### Blockquote
#### 引用区块

Choose the blockquote block to highlight specific points or ideas, or when you're quoting longer passages from external sources. Using blockquotes sparingly can be very effective, but don’t overdo it — just like bold or italicized text, they can quickly become distracting.

选择引用区块（Blockquote）来突出特定观点或想法，或者在引用外部来源的长段落时使用。适度使用引用区块效果显著，但不要过度使用——就像加粗或斜体文本一样，用多了会分散读者的注意力。

#### Separator
#### 分隔符

Sometimes, a heading might feel insufficient for creating a visual break between or within sections. In those cases, use the Separator block — just make sure you select the Dotted style, which is the one we chose as our default on TDS. (If for some reason you absolutely need to use a different style of separator, that's probably ok. Just leave us a comment explaining your choice.)

有时，仅靠标题不足以在章节之间或内部创建视觉间隔。在这种情况下，请使用分隔符（Separator）区块——只需确保选择“Dotted”（点状）样式，这是我们在 TDS 上选择的默认样式。（如果出于某种原因您必须使用其他样式的分隔符，通常也是可以的。只需给我们留个言解释一下您的选择即可。）

#### Table
#### 表格

Tables are great for comparing benchmark results, product features, and more. The Table block makes creating sleek tables very easy. The + buttons below and to the right of the table will add rows and columns, respectively. The other settings in the pop-up menu allow you to toggle the header row and column, align the table vertically, delete rows and columns, and delete the table entirely.

表格非常适合比较基准测试结果、产品功能等。表格区块让创建精美的表格变得非常简单。表格下方和右侧的“+”按钮分别用于添加行和列。弹出菜单中的其他设置允许您切换标题行和列、垂直对齐表格、删除行和列，以及彻底删除表格。

#### Code and GitHub Gists
#### 代码与 GitHub Gists

Many TDS articles include code — sometimes lots of code. The text block we covered above gives you access to inline code styling, however that's not a practical solution for more than, say, a short snippet. You have two great options to choose from:

许多 TDS 文章都包含代码——有时甚至包含大量代码。我们上面提到的文本区块提供了行内代码样式，但这对于较长的代码片段来说并不实用。您有两个很好的选择：

**Code block (代码区块)**
Choose a code block when you'd like to input code directly into the draft and choose a language-specific syntax highlighting for it. Just paste your code into the block — then, either keep it in Auto mode and our site will choose the most appropriate highlights for it, or scroll down the list of available programming languages and choose the relevant one. If the block only contains outputs, natural-language prompts, etc., rather than working code, just pick Markdown, which will keep it in plaintext.

当您想直接在草稿中输入代码并为其选择特定语言的语法高亮时，请选择代码区块。只需将代码粘贴到区块中——然后，您可以保持“Auto”（自动）模式，我们的网站会自动选择最合适的语法高亮，或者向下滚动可用编程语言列表并选择相应的语言。如果区块中仅包含输出结果、自然语言提示词等，而非可运行的代码，请选择“Markdown”，这会将其保持为纯文本格式。
