# Nvidia is about to be a hundred-billion-dollar-a-quarter company
# 英伟达即将成为单季度营收千亿美元的公司

Nvidia is about to be a hundred-billion-dollar-a-quarter company. The chip company made over $96 billion in the past quarter, most of it from its data center business.
英伟达即将成为单季度营收千亿美元的公司。这家芯片巨头在过去一个季度实现了超过 960 亿美元的营收，其中大部分来自其数据中心业务。

Nvidia’s predicting it will pull in $108 billion in revenue within just a few months. It wouldn’t be the first company to rake in over $100 billion in quarterly revenue — Amazon, Apple, and Alphabet have repeatedly reached the milestone.
英伟达预测，在短短几个月内，其营收将达到 1080 亿美元。这并非首家单季度营收突破 1000 亿美元的公司——亚马逊、苹果和 Alphabet 此前已多次达到这一里程碑。

Nvidia said in its latest earnings report that it brought in a record $96.2 billion in overall revenue in the past quarter, a jump of over $10 billion from the previous quarter. Its data center revenue alone more than doubled year-over-year to a record $89 billion, and the company’s profits more than doubled to $59.7 billion.
英伟达在最新的财报中表示，上季度总营收达到创纪录的 962 亿美元，较上一季度增长超过 100 亿美元。仅其数据中心业务的营收就同比翻了一番多，达到创纪录的 890 亿美元；公司利润也翻了一番多，达到 597 亿美元。

Nvidia’s “edge computing” category, which includes its consumer gaming business, accounted for just $7.2 billion of its revenue last quarter. That’s a 27 percent increase year-over-year, but Nvidia acknowledged that it experienced “slower consumer PC sales that were tempered by elevated memory and systems prices.”
英伟达的“边缘计算”类别（包括其消费级游戏业务）在上季度仅贡献了 72 亿美元的营收。尽管这比去年同期增长了 27%，但英伟达承认，由于内存和系统价格上涨，消费级 PC 的销售速度有所放缓。

Component shortages continue to drive up prices for Nvidia’s consumer GPUs, and it also warned of price hikes for its AI chips ahead of Wednesday’s earnings report.
零部件短缺持续推高了英伟达消费级 GPU 的价格，该公司还在周三财报发布前警告称，其 AI 芯片价格也将上涨。
