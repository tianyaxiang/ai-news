# XCancel and Nitter are receiving C&D letters from XCorp
# XCancel 和 Nitter 收到来自 XCorp 的停止侵权函 (C&D)

"On Monday 24th August at 8PM EST, we received a letter from X Corp. asking to cease and desist the service XCancel. The service XCancel is stopped until further notice. We are seeking legal advice and won't share more details for now. Thank you for the trust you have put in these two years of XCancel."

“美东时间 8 月 24 日周一晚 8 点，我们收到了 X Corp. 发来的信函，要求停止 XCancel 服务。XCancel 服务即刻起暂停，直至另行通知。我们目前正在寻求法律建议，暂时不会分享更多细节。感谢大家在 XCancel 这两年里给予的信任。”

"On 24 August 2026 cease and desist letters have been sent by X Corp. demanding a permanent takedown of Nitter instances and the project's repository. nitter.net is offline and development has stopped for the time being. I'm seeking legal advice and won't be commenting further on the specifics for now. Thank you to everyone who used, hosted, packaged, donated and contributed to Nitter over the past seven years."

“2026 年 8 月 24 日，X Corp. 发出了停止侵权函，要求永久下架所有 Nitter 实例及该项目的代码仓库。nitter.net 目前已离线，开发工作也已暂时停止。我正在寻求法律建议，目前不会对具体细节发表进一步评论。感谢过去七年来所有使用、托管、打包、捐赠以及为 Nitter 做出贡献的人们。”
