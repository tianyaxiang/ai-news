# Is Agentic AI Just Automation?
# 代理式 AI 仅仅是自动化吗？

Why most of the agents are just flowcharts in disguise, and what to build instead. I wrote most of this at an airport several months ago and then left it in drafts. The noise has only gotten louder since, so here it is.
为什么大多数 AI 智能体（Agent）只是伪装成智能体的流程图，以及我们应该构建什么。几个月前，我在机场写下了这篇文章的大部分内容，随后将其搁置在草稿箱中。自那以后，市场上的噪音越来越大，所以现在是时候把它发布出来了。

A few months ago, on our way back from a client workshop, we were discussing the scope of what constitutes Agentic AI and all of us quoted examples from our experience. There was a discussion on why automation can’t solve it and how most of the agentic AI use cases are nothing but glorified automation. The question was, “Give me one real use case for an agent. Not a demo. Not automation. A real one. Because everything I have seen so far, I could have built with a workflow engine and a model call.”
几个月前，在从客户研讨会返回的路上，我们讨论了什么是“代理式 AI”（Agentic AI）的范畴，大家都引用了各自经验中的例子。讨论中提到，为什么自动化无法解决某些问题，以及大多数代理式 AI 的用例不过是“美化版”的自动化。当时有人问：“给我一个真正的智能体用例。不是演示，不是自动化，是一个真正的用例。因为到目前为止我所看到的一切，我都可以用工作流引擎加一个模型调用来实现。”

We all had been in agentic AI conversations for months at that point. We had architected these systems. We had pitched them. And still, sitting there with a tea and twenty minutes at our hand to spend, we had to think long enough to give an example that survived thirty seconds of scrutiny. That bothered me enough to write it down.
那时，我们已经参与代理式 AI 的讨论好几个月了。我们设计过这些系统，也向客户推销过它们。然而，坐在那里喝着茶、有二十分钟空闲时间时，我们却不得不苦思冥想，才能给出一个能经得起三十秒推敲的例子。这让我感到不安，促使我写下了这些思考。

### The test I reached for first
### 我最初尝试的测试方法

My instinct was to define agentic by the shape of the work. Does the task require the system to make decisions mid-execution that could not have been anticipated at the start? If yes, agent. If no, automation.
我的直觉是根据工作的形态来定义“代理式”。任务是否要求系统在执行过程中做出在开始时无法预见的决策？如果是，那就是智能体；如果不是，那就是自动化。

I thought of an example from aviation maintenance, because that is where I had spent the previous six months. An A320 aircraft lands with an ECAM warning, AIR PACK 1 FAULT.
我想到一个航空维修的例子，因为那是过去六个月我所处的领域。一架 A320 飞机降落时出现 ECAM（电子中央飞机监控）警告：AIR PACK 1 FAULT（1号空调组件故障）。

*   **What automation does:** triggers a checklist, notifies the duty engineer, maybe pulls the MEL reference. Done.
*   **What traditional ML does:** predicts probability of component failure from historical fault patterns, produces a confidence score. The engineer still decides.
*   **What I think an agent would do:** it starts with the ECAM message, queries the CFDS (a centralised fault display system) for fault history and finds Pack 1 has thrown the same fault three times in six weeks. That changes the picture, so it pulls the TSM (troubleshooting manual) identifies four candidate root causes. It checks AMOS (a database used in aviation all over the world) for parts on hand and finds that two of those four need a Flow Control Valve, which is not at that station. It then checks which causes are deferrable under MEL (minimum equipment list), finds one deferrable under Category C for ten days, looks at the aircraft’s next ten rotations to understand the operational cost of deferring, and drafts a recommendation: defer under Cat C, order the FCV for arrival before day eight, flag for deep inspection at the next base maintenance slot.
*   **自动化会做什么：** 触发检查清单，通知值班工程师，或许调出 MEL（最低设备清单）参考。结束。
*   **传统机器学习会做什么：** 根据历史故障模式预测组件故障概率，生成置信度分数。最终仍由工程师做决定。
*   **我认为智能体会做什么：** 它从 ECAM 消息开始，查询 CFDS（集中故障显示系统）获取故障历史，发现 1 号组件在六周内出现了三次同样的故障。这改变了情况，于是它调出 TSM（故障排除手册），确定了四个潜在的根本原因。它检查 AMOS（全球航空业通用的数据库）中现有的零件，发现其中两个原因需要“流量控制阀”，而该站点没有库存。接着，它检查 MEL 下哪些原因可以延期，发现其中一个可以在 C 类（Category C）下延期十天，查看飞机接下来的十次航程以了解延期的运营成本，并起草建议：在 C 类下延期，订购流量控制阀并在第八天前送达，标记在下一次基地维护时进行深度检查。

Nobody pre-programmed that decision tree. The plan emerged from the process rather than preceding it.
没有人预先编程过这个决策树。这个计划是在过程中产生的，而不是预先设定的。

I still was not convinced with myself. And on reflection, I believe, I was right.
我当时对自己还没完全信服。但反思后，我相信我是对的。

### Why that example fails
### 为什么那个例子失败了

Because you can draw that flowchart. CFDS, then TSM, then AMOS, then MEL, then rotations. It is a complicated flowchart with branches, but a competent engineer could have specified it in advance. Complexity is not the same as open-endedness. A tree with two hundred branches is still a tree.
因为你可以画出那个流程图。先 CFDS，再 TSM，再 AMOS，再 MEL，最后是航程。这是一个带有分支的复杂流程图，但一位称职的工程师完全可以预先指定它。复杂性不等于开放性。一棵有两百个分支的树，依然是一棵树。

This is the mistake almost every agentic pitch deck makes, mine included. We show something intricate and expect the intricacy to be self-evidently agentic. It is not. Intricacy is just a bigger flowchart, and flowcharts have a technology already, and it is cheaper, faster and far more reliable than any LLM you will put in that slot.
这是几乎所有代理式 AI 演示文稿（Pitch Deck）都会犯的错误，包括我自己的。我们展示了一些复杂的东西，并期望这种复杂性不言自明地代表“代理式”。事实并非如此。复杂性只是一个更大的流程图，而流程图已经有了成熟的技术，它比你放入该位置的任何大语言模型（LLM）都要便宜、快速且可靠得多。

### So where is the actual line?
### 那么真正的界限在哪里？

**Known unknowns versus unknown unknowns**
**已知未知与未知未知**

Here is the distinction I have settled on, and it is narrower than most people in this market are willing to admit.
这是我最终确定的区别，它比市场上大多数人愿意承认的要狭窄得多。

**Automation handles known unknowns.** You do not know whether the part is in stock, but you know that stock is a thing you need to check.
**自动化处理“已知未知”。** 你不知道零件是否有库存，但你知道“库存”是你需要检查的事项。

**Agents handle unknown unknowns.** You do not know what you need to check, and you will only find out by looking.
**智能体处理“未知未知”。** 你根本不知道需要检查什么，只有通过探索才能发现。

Take a use case that genuinely breaks automation: due diligence on an acquisition. The instruction is one line: "Identify material risks in this target."
以一个真正打破自动化的用例为例：收购尽职调查。指令只有一行：“识别该目标公司的重大风险。”

No one can draw that flowchart upfront. The agent reads three thousand pages of contracts and notices an unusual indemnity clause in a supplier agreement. That clause references a regulatory filing, so it goes and fetches the filing. The filing hints at an informal environmental inquiry that appears nowhere in the data room. It then goes looking through news archives and court records to size the exposure and surfaces it as a risk that was never on anyone’s checklist.
没有人能预先画出那个流程图。智能体阅读了三千页合同，注意到供应商协议中有一条不同寻常的赔偿条款。该条款引用了一份监管备案文件，于是它去获取了该文件。文件中暗示了一项在数据室中完全没有提及的非正式环境调查。随后，它去搜索新闻档案和法庭记录来评估风险敞口，并将其作为一个从未出现在任何人检查清单上的风险呈现出来。

Nobody knew to look for that. The agent found a thread and pulled it. You cannot enumerate what you do not know exists.
没人知道要查这个。智能体发现了一条线索并顺藤摸瓜。你无法列举出你根本不知道其存在的事物。

A second one, closer to the consumer world. A brand has a moisturiser generating complaints. Sentiment analysis flags them and a rule routes them to a team. Fine, that is automation and it works.
第二个例子，更贴近消费领域。一个品牌的保湿霜引发了投诉。情感分析标记了这些投诉，规则将其路由给团队。很好，这是自动化，而且它运作良好。

The agentic version notices that the complaints cluster in humid climates, in summer months, and disproportionately from one retail chain. Nobody asked it to look at that intersection. It hypothesises a cold chain or formulation stability issue, pulls batch numbers, cross references manufacturing and logistics logs, and raises a possible recall exposure before a human has connected any of those dots.
代理式版本会注意到，投诉集中在潮湿气候、夏季月份，且不成比例地来自某一家零售连锁店。没有人要求它去查看这些交叉点。它假设是冷链或配方稳定性问题，提取批号，交叉比对制造和物流日志，并在人类将这些点联系起来之前，就提出了潜在的召回风险。

That property, where discovery changes direction in a way you could not have mapped, is the whole thing. Everything else is scaffolding.
这种“发现过程改变了方向，且这种改变是你无法预先规划的”特性，才是核心所在。其他一切都只是脚手架。

### Why real examples are so hard to find
### 为什么真正的例子如此难找

This is the part that took me longest to understand, and I think it is the most useful idea here. There could be gaps but still I want to crystallise the information.
这是我花最长时间才理解的部分，我认为这是这里最有用的观点。虽然可能存在疏漏，但我还是想把这些信息提炼出来。

Enterprises have spent years deliberately engineering open-ended state out of their processes. That was rational. Human judgment is expensive, inconsistent and does not scale. So organisations built SOPs for every scenario, approval hierarchies with fixed triggers, rule based systems in ERP, CRM and WMS, and workflows where every decision point had a...
企业多年来一直致力于刻意将“开放性状态”从其流程中剔除。这是理性的。人类的判断昂贵、不一致且难以规模化。因此，组织为每种场景建立了标准作业程序（SOP）、具有固定触发条件的审批层级、ERP/CRM/WMS 中的基于规则的系统，以及每个决策点都有明确定义的流程……
