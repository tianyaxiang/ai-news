### Introducing: the Kids issue
### 导读：儿童特刊

If the desire to limit kids' use of technology was once a subcurrent, it has become a raging flood. Countries around the world are banning children from social media. Schools across the US are ditching iPads and Chromebooks for actual books. Kids themselves seem to be embracing this tech skepticism too: the hottest gadget for Gen Alpha is a vintage Sony Walkman.
如果说限制儿童使用科技产品曾经只是一种暗流，那么现在它已经演变成了一场汹涌的洪水。世界各国都在禁止儿童使用社交媒体。美国各地的学校正在放弃 iPad 和 Chromebook，转而使用真正的书籍。孩子们自己似乎也接受了这种对科技的怀疑态度：对于“阿尔法世代”（Gen Alpha）来说，最热门的电子产品竟然是复古的索尼随身听。

A surprising—maybe troubling—number of people who work in big tech also keep their kids at arm’s distance from technology. They lock down their phones, if they have phones at all, and keep them off social media. Hell, even Mark Zuckerberg doesn’t publicly post his children’s faces on Facebook or Instagram.
令人惊讶——或许也令人不安的是——许多在大型科技公司工作的人也将自己的孩子与科技保持距离。他们会锁定孩子的手机（如果他们有手机的话），并让他们远离社交媒体。见鬼，就连马克·扎克伯格也不会在 Facebook 或 Instagram 上公开他孩子的面孔。

Yet there is no hiding from technology. It permeates nearly everything, everywhere. We have to prepare our children to live in the actual world we have actually created, not the one we wish we had. How can we help kids survive and thrive in what we have wrought? That's what the new Kids issue of MIT Technology Review is all about.
然而，科技无处不在，我们无处可藏。它渗透到几乎所有地方的每一件事物中。我们必须让孩子们准备好生活在我们所创造的现实世界中，而不是我们希望拥有的那个世界。我们该如何帮助孩子们在我们所造就的环境中生存并茁壮成长？这正是《麻省理工科技评论》新一期“儿童特刊”的主题。

With the help of the editors of *Anyway*, a fantastic magazine for teens and tweens, we explore how young people really feel about AI, the support networks helping kids through the polycrisis, and what happens when a child's robot best friend dies. We also ask why kids outlearn AI, whether monitoring apps are really keeping children safe online, how schools can encourage smarter AI use, and what happens when technology begins to reshape childhood itself, courtesy of exclusive new fiction from author and AI ethicist Jenny Williams.
在面向青少年和青春期前儿童的精彩杂志《Anyway》编辑们的帮助下，我们探讨了年轻人对人工智能的真实感受、帮助孩子们度过“多重危机”的支持网络，以及当一个孩子的机器人挚友“死亡”时会发生什么。我们还探讨了为什么孩子比人工智能学得更快、监控应用程序是否真的能保障儿童在线安全、学校如何鼓励更明智地使用人工智能，以及当科技开始重塑童年本身时会发生什么——这部分内容由作家兼人工智能伦理学家珍妮·威廉姆斯（Jenny Williams）提供的独家新小说呈现。

Together, these stories examine how childhood is changing in an age of AI—and how we can help kids navigate the world we've made. Subscribe now to read the print issue in full.
这些故事共同审视了在人工智能时代童年是如何改变的，以及我们如何帮助孩子们驾驭我们所创造的世界。立即订阅以阅读印刷版的全部内容。

### Bill Gates says we’ve passed AI’s danger thresholds. Now what? —Mat Honan
### 比尔·盖茨称我们已经跨越了人工智能的危险阈值。接下来该怎么办？——Mat Honan

It’s a glorious day in Kirkland, Washington, an affluent Seattle suburb on the eastern shore of Lake Washington. The temperature is in the mid-80s, the sky is incapable of being any more blue, and the view is gorgeous. And vaguely terrifying. Because if the scene is placid, the messenger is not.
在华盛顿州柯克兰（Kirkland），这是一个位于华盛顿湖东岸、西雅图富裕的郊区，天气好极了。气温在华氏 80 多度（约 29 摄氏度），天空蓝得不能再蓝，景色美不胜收。但同时也隐约让人感到恐惧。因为如果说眼前的景象是平静的，那么传达信息的人却并非如此。

Seated across from me at a conference room table, Bill Gates is rocking back and forth in his chair. And the more he has to say—about the threats of terror or economic collapse or just losing control of our AI systems—the more agitated I find myself becoming, too.
坐在会议桌对面，比尔·盖茨在椅子上前后摇晃。他说的越多——关于恐怖主义威胁、经济崩溃，或者仅仅是失去对人工智能系统的控制——我就发现自己也变得越发不安。

The philanthropist and former Microsoft CEO says he has been growing increasingly alarmed by the rate at which AI technology is advancing, especially since guardrails are not keeping pace. “We’ve crossed the threshold in terms of [AI’s] bio-capabilities, cyber-capabilities, psychosocial capabilities, job-market-destruction capabilities, and even the lack of control,” he said. “I’m just stunned at the lack of concern and discussion outside of the industry.”
这位慈善家兼前微软首席执行官表示，他对人工智能技术的发展速度越来越感到担忧，尤其是因为安全护栏的建设并没有跟上步伐。“在人工智能的生物能力、网络能力、社会心理能力、破坏就业市场的能力，甚至失控风险方面，我们都已经跨越了阈值，”他说。“我对行业之外缺乏关注和讨论感到震惊。”

Read the full interview with Bill Gates about the AI risks he says we’ve already crossed—and what we should do now.
阅读对比尔·盖茨的完整采访，了解他所说的我们已经跨越的人工智能风险，以及我们现在应该做什么。
