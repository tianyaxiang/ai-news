### A startup claims it’s found a drug to make your blood young
### 一家初创公司声称发现了一种能让血液“返老还童”的药物

—Antonio Regalado
—安东尼奥·雷加拉多 (Antonio Regalado)

I knew I’d officially become a “longevity influencer” when a company called Generation Lab offered me the chance to write about—and even receive—their new rejuvenation treatment. This wasn’t just any antiaging treatment, either. A company fact sheet says that it “blocks the systemic spread of aging in the bloodstream, reawakens the body’s own repair mechanism and restores health and youth to multiple tissues.”
当一家名为 Generation Lab 的公司邀请我撰写关于他们的新型回春疗法，甚至邀请我亲自体验时，我就知道自己正式成为了一名“长寿网红”。这可不是普通的抗衰老疗法。公司的一份情况说明书称，它能“阻断衰老在血液系统中的传播，重新唤醒人体自身的修复机制，并使多种组织恢复健康与活力。”

The approach is based on research by Generation Lab’s irascible scientific founder, Irina Conboy. She found that joining the circulatory systems of old and young mice improved the old animals’ ability to heal from injury. Conboy now says she has found a combination of two existing drugs that can produce youthful effects without the need for any bodily fluid exchange. But Generation Lab won’t reveal what the drugs are, making the proposition hard to take seriously. Read the full story on the drug combo that claims to “stop the spread of aging.”
该方法基于 Generation Lab 脾气火爆的科学创始人伊琳娜·康博伊 (Irina Conboy) 的研究。她发现，将年老小鼠和年轻小鼠的循环系统连接起来，可以提高年老动物的伤口愈合能力。康博伊现在表示，她已经找到了一种由两种现有药物组成的组合，无需进行任何体液交换就能产生年轻化的效果。但 Generation Lab 拒绝透露这些药物的具体成分，这使得该提议很难被认真对待。阅读关于这种声称能“阻止衰老蔓延”的药物组合的完整报道。

### How to sign up for a virtual power plant—and decide whether you should
### 如何加入虚拟电厂——以及判断你是否应该加入

Your thermostat may not look like a power plant. Neither does your electric vehicle, home battery, or HVAC system. But utility and energy companies increasingly want to treat them like one. That’s the idea behind a virtual power plant, or VPP, a collection of household devices (such as smart thermostats, EV chargers, and solar panels) that a utility can control, usually by commanding them to draw less electricity during peak hours. In exchange, participants receive a discount on their energy bills and, in some cases, a signing bonus. Here’s how to sign up for a VPP—and decide whether it’s worth it.
你的恒温器看起来可能不像发电厂。你的电动汽车、家用电池或暖通空调系统也不像。但公用事业和能源公司越来越希望将它们视为发电厂。这就是虚拟电厂 (VPP) 背后的理念：它是一系列家用设备（如智能恒温器、电动汽车充电器和太阳能电池板）的集合，公用事业公司可以对其进行控制，通常是通过指令让它们在高峰时段减少用电。作为交换，参与者可以获得电费折扣，在某些情况下还能获得签约奖金。以下是如何注册 VPP 以及判断它是否值得的方法。

### One more thing
### 还有一件事

**The problem with thinking you’re part Neanderthal**
**认为自己拥有尼安德特人基因的问题**

There’s a theory that many of us have an “inner Neanderthal.” The idea is that Homo sapiens and a cousin species once bred, leaving some people today with a trace of Neanderthal DNA. This DNA is arguably the 21st century’s most celebrated discovery in human evolution. But in 2024, a pair of French geneticists called into question the theory’s very foundations. They proposed that what scientists interpret as interbreeding could instead be explained by population structure—the way genes concentrate in smaller, isolated groups. Find out what it all means for human evolution.
有一种理论认为，我们许多人内心都有一个“尼安德特人”。该观点认为，智人与一个近亲物种曾经进行过繁衍，导致今天的一些人身上留下了尼安德特人的 DNA 痕迹。这种 DNA 可以说是 21 世纪人类进化史上最著名的发现。但在 2024 年，两位法国遗传学家对该理论的基础提出了质疑。他们提出，科学家所解释的“杂交”可能可以用种群结构来解释——即基因在较小、孤立的群体中集中的方式。了解这对人类进化意味着什么。
