# The Easiest Ways to Share Anything Between Android and iOS
# 在 Android 和 iOS 之间共享内容的简易方法

Whisper it quietly, but certain bricks in Apple’s walled garden might be getting taken away. Thanks to some recent upgrades added to Android by Google, it's now easier than ever to share files between an iPhone and an Android phone, without installing any new apps (though that's still an option too).
小声说一句，苹果“围墙花园”里的某些砖块可能正在被拆除。得益于谷歌近期对 Android 系统进行的一些升级，现在在 iPhone 和 Android 手机之间共享文件变得前所未有的简单，且无需安装任何新应用（当然，安装应用依然是一个可选方案）。

So if you need to send a photo, contact card, web link, or anything else, these are the options you have available. As is often the case with Android, they may vary depending on the make and model of your handset, though you should find at least one of these methods works for you.
因此，如果你需要发送照片、联系人名片、网页链接或其他任何内容，以下是你可用的方案。正如 Android 系统常见的情况一样，具体操作可能会因手机品牌和型号而异，但你应该至少能找到一种适合你的方法。

### Share Files via AirDrop
### 通过 AirDrop 共享文件

This is the big one: Google recently figured out a way to add support for AirDrop—Apple's previously closed-off, device-to-device sharing protocol—to newer Pixel phones. It doesn't appear that Apple was involved in the process, but the company hasn't gotten in Google's way since, and the functionality has been rolled out to other phones (including the recent Pixel and Galaxy handsets).
这是重头戏：谷歌最近找到了一种方法，为较新的 Pixel 手机增加了对 AirDrop 的支持——这是苹果此前封闭的设备间共享协议。苹果似乎并未参与这一过程，但自那以后也没有阻碍谷歌，且该功能已推广到其他手机（包括近期的 Pixel 和 Galaxy 机型）。

Some prep work may be required. On Samsung phones, you need to open Settings, then choose Connected devices > Quick Share and enable Share with Apple devices—though there's no such toggle switch on Pixel phones—it'll just work right away. It needs enabling on iOS too: From Settings, tap General > AirDrop, and Everyone for 10 Minutes.
可能需要进行一些准备工作。在三星手机上，你需要打开“设置”，选择“已连接设备” > “快速共享”（Quick Share），并启用“与 Apple 设备共享”——虽然 Pixel 手机上没有这个开关，但它直接就能用。在 iOS 端也需要开启：进入“设置”，点击“通用” > “隔空投送”（AirDrop），并选择“所有人，10 分钟”。

With that done, whenever you tap the share button anywhere in any Android app, you can choose Quick Share from the resulting pop-up panel to communicate with a nearby iPhone. The iPhone should show up in the available devices, so select it to start the transfer—which will still need to be accepted on the iPhone.
完成这些设置后，无论你在任何 Android 应用中点击分享按钮，都可以从弹出的面板中选择“快速共享”来与附近的 iPhone 通信。iPhone 应该会出现在可用设备列表中，选中它即可开始传输——当然，传输请求仍需在 iPhone 上点击接受。

It will work the other way too, starting from AirDrop on an iPhone, as long as the Android device is set up to receive from any other device. From Settings, tap either Connection preferences (Google Pixel phones) or Connected devices (Samsung Galaxy phones), then select Quick Share > Who can share with you.
反向操作也同样可行，即从 iPhone 的 AirDrop 发起，只要 Android 设备已设置为允许接收来自任何设备的文件即可。在 Android 设置中，点击“连接偏好设置”（Google Pixel 手机）或“已连接设备”（三星 Galaxy 手机），然后选择“快速共享” > “谁可以与您共享”。

### Share Files via QR code
### 通过二维码共享文件

Google hasn't stopped with AirDrop on Android. It has also recently pushed out a QR-code-based option for sharing from Android to iOS. It's a bit more clunky than AirDrop, but it'll work no matter what kind of Android phone you've got.
谷歌在 Android 上的动作并未止步于 AirDrop。最近，它还推出了一种基于二维码的 Android 到 iOS 共享方案。虽然它比 AirDrop 稍微繁琐一点，但无论你使用哪种 Android 手机，它都能正常工作。

Again, you need to go through Quick Share, which pops up as an option whenever you try to share something in Android—no matter what the app or item you want to share. You won't see an iPhone appear if you don't have the AirDrop functionality, but you should see a Use QR code option on the right, under Share with iPhone and other devices, which after a few moments shrinks down to just a QR code symbol.
同样，你需要通过“快速共享”功能，当你尝试在 Android 中分享任何内容时，它都会作为选项弹出——无论你想分享什么应用或项目。如果你没有 AirDrop 功能，你不会看到 iPhone 出现在列表中，但你应该能在“与 iPhone 和其他设备共享”下方看到“使用二维码”选项，几秒钟后它会缩减为一个二维码图标。

Tap this option to get your QR code on screen. On the iPhone side, AirDrop still needs enabling (via General > AirDrop > Everyone for 10 Minutes), and the iPhone camera can then be pointed at the QR code to get to a special Google web address. That will lead to a simple download page for the shared file or content.
点击此选项即可在屏幕上显示二维码。在 iPhone 端，仍需开启 AirDrop（通过“通用” > “隔空投送” > “所有人，10 分钟”），然后用 iPhone 相机扫描该二维码，即可跳转到一个特殊的谷歌网页地址。该地址会导向一个简单的下载页面，用于获取共享的文件或内容。

Google says transferred files remain in the cloud for 24 hours, after which they'll be securely deleted. They don't count toward your Google Drive storage quota, and you can share up to 1,000 files in a single session, to a maximum of 20 iPhones (or iPads or Macs, which also support AirDrop).
谷歌表示，传输的文件会在云端保留 24 小时，之后会被安全删除。这些文件不占用你的 Google Drive 存储配额，单次会话最多可共享 1,000 个文件，最多可发送给 20 台 iPhone（或同样支持 AirDrop 的 iPad 或 Mac）。

### Other Sharing Options
### 其他共享方案

You do have a multitude of sharing options to pick from if you want to get something from Android to iOS. You can simply attach a file to an email, for example, or use a messaging app that supports both Android and iOS, like WhatsApp. Bear in mind that certain messaging apps compress photos and videos before sending, though many apps let you turn compression off in the settings.
如果你想将内容从 Android 传输到 iOS，还有多种共享方案可供选择。例如，你可以简单地将文件作为附件发送到电子邮件，或者使用同时支持 Android 和 iOS 的即时通讯应用，如 WhatsApp。请记住，某些通讯应用在发送前会压缩照片和视频，不过许多应用允许你在设置中关闭压缩功能。

Cloud storage apps are another helpful method you can use, though as with messaging apps you want something that works well on any platform (so not iCloud). Google Drive and Dropbox are two apps that are simple to use, run well on Android and iOS, and have smart sharing features built in, for example.
云存储应用是另一种实用的方法，但和通讯应用一样，你需要选择在任何平台上都能良好运行的服务（因此不建议使用 iCloud）。例如，Google Drive 和 Dropbox 就是两个简单易用、在 Android 和 iOS 上运行良好且内置智能共享功能的应用。

For iPhone-to-iPhone and Android-to-Android connections and file transfers, everything is much more straightforward: You can just use AirDrop or Quick Share through the relevant share panels, and the receiving device should pop up (assuming you've set the right availability options, as described above).
对于 iPhone 到 iPhone 以及 Android 到 Android 的连接和文件传输，一切就简单多了：你只需通过相关的分享面板使用 AirDrop 或“快速共享”，接收设备就会弹出（前提是你已按照上述说明设置了正确的可用性选项）。

One option you can't use is Bluetooth: Apple locks down Bluetooth on the iPhone to some extent, so while you can connect accessories like wireless earbuds, you can't use it for wireless file transfers.
有一种你无法使用的方法是蓝牙：苹果在一定程度上限制了 iPhone 的蓝牙功能，因此虽然你可以连接无线耳机等配件，但无法将其用于无线文件传输。
