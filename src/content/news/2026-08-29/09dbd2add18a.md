# U.S. sanctions against the A/I Collective
# 美国针对 A/I Collective 的制裁

A/I (Autistici/Inventati, pronounced [au’tistiʧi]-[iŋ’vɛntati], or [iŋvɛn’tati]) was born in 2001 from an encounter of individuals and collectives of the autonomous anticapitalist movement interested in technology and active in the digital rights struggle.
A/I (Autistici/Inventati，发音为 [au’tistiʧi]-[iŋ’vɛntati] 或 [iŋvɛn’tati]) 诞生于 2001 年，由一群对技术感兴趣并积极投身于数字权利斗争的自治反资本主义运动的个人和集体共同创立。

We believe that this world is far from being the best world possible. We react by providing a platform and tools for digital self-defense addressing the need of free communication for activists and other individuals.
我们认为，这个世界远非理想世界。我们通过提供数字自卫平台和工具来做出回应，以满足活动人士和其他个人对自由交流的需求。

All of our services are provided for free, without any form of control or commoditization of the personal user data. We don’t receive any form of compensation for our work.
我们所有的服务均免费提供，不对个人用户数据进行任何形式的控制或商品化。我们不会因工作获得任何形式的报酬。

We are volunteers exploiting the experiences we accumulated over years of technological, political and legal research while doing radical activism, and we are motivated and inspired by the principles of solidarity and self organization.
我们是一群志愿者，利用多年来在激进活动中积累的技术、政治和法律研究经验，并受到团结和自组织原则的激励与启发。

Our financial strategy relies exclusively on voluntary donations. We provide our services strictly for non-commercial use, in order to provide support exclusively to individuals or groups we feel we have an affinity with.
我们的财务策略完全依赖于自愿捐款。我们严格将服务用于非商业用途，旨在仅为我们认同的个人或团体提供支持。

In order to verify the existence of these pre-conditions, each and every service request is processed manually by one of our volunteers and is in a dialogical form. All requests are anonymized and will be destroyed.
为了核实这些前提条件，每一项服务请求都由我们的志愿者手动处理，并以对话形式进行。所有请求均已匿名化，并将被销毁。

If you would like to use our services, please check if you agree with our manifesto, pledge respect to our policy and read carefully our privacy policy. Don’t send a request without having read these documents!
如果您想使用我们的服务，请确认您是否同意我们的宣言，承诺遵守我们的政策，并仔细阅读我们的隐私政策。在阅读这些文档之前，请勿发送请求！
