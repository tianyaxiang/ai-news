### About the role
### 关于职位

As Senior Open Source Maintainer you will be the primary technical owner and public face of the project. You’ll drive the roadmap, ship releases, support a global user base, and champion the project across the wider industry. This is a highly visible individual contributor role with enormous scope. No two days are the same, and you’ll have full autonomy to decide how you spend your time.
作为资深开源维护者，你将是该项目的主要技术负责人和公众形象代表。你将推动路线图规划、发布版本、支持全球用户群，并在更广泛的行业内推广该项目。这是一个高曝光度的个人贡献者职位，职责范围极广。每天的工作内容都各不相同，你将拥有完全的自主权来决定如何分配你的时间。
