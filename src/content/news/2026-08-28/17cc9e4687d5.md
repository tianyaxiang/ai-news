# Stop Giving Your AI Agent a Search Box and Start Giving It Typed Tools, Hard Bounds, and a Gate It Cannot Talk Past
# 别再给你的 AI Agent 一个搜索框了，请给它类型化工具、硬性边界以及它无法逾越的“守门人”

In the second article of this series, I retired the query router and made retrieval a fixed, fused pipeline where every question runs hybrid search and typed graph traversal. The union is reranked once with the relationship paths visible, and the grounded bundle carries those paths as evidence. I deployed it on Azure next to the first article’s stack, ran the same synthetic insurance corpus through it, and measured what changed. Entity fragmentation dropped from 149 machine-extracted concepts to 120 on identical documents. The contradiction detector fired during the seed without being asked. The system now walks its own graph.

在本系列文章的第二篇中，我弃用了查询路由器（query router），将检索过程改为一个固定的融合流水线，即每个问题都会同时运行混合搜索和类型化图遍历。合并后的结果会经过一次重排序，关系路径清晰可见，且生成的证据包（grounded bundle）会携带这些路径作为支撑。我将其部署在 Azure 上，与第一篇文章的架构并列，运行了相同的合成保险语料库，并测量了变化。在处理相同文档的情况下，实体碎片化程度从 149 个机器提取的概念下降到了 120 个。矛盾检测器在种子数据导入期间自动触发。现在，系统可以自主遍历其图谱了。

What it still cannot do is decide that one walk was not enough. That sentence is the whole reason this third article exists, so let me be precise about it. The pipeline I built is fixed: one retrieval pass, then one generation. Everything the answer will need has to be fetched before any reasoning begins, by a system that has not yet reasoned. For most questions that is exactly right since it is fast, cheap, auditable and predictable. But there is a class of questions where you only discover what to retrieve after you have partially understood what you already retrieved, and for that class, a fixed pipeline is the wrong shape no matter how good its single pass is.

它仍然无法做到的是：判断一次遍历是否足够。这句话正是撰写第三篇文章的全部原因，所以让我说得精确一点。我构建的流水线是固定的：一次检索，然后一次生成。在任何推理开始之前，所有答案所需的信息都必须由一个尚未进行推理的系统预先获取。对于大多数问题，这确实是正确的，因为它快速、廉价、可审计且可预测。但有一类问题，你只有在部分理解了已检索到的内容后，才能发现还需要检索什么。对于这类问题，无论单次检索做得多好，固定的流水线都是错误的形态。

The fashionable answer to this is an AI agent, and I will be honest about my hesitation in using the word. Most of what gets published under “agentic RAG” right now is a retry loop bolted onto vector search, a framework tutorial, or an opinion piece with no running system underneath it. I did not want to add to that pile. So this article takes the agent question seriously in the only way I know how, to define the design precisely, name the experiments that could prove it wrong, run them on the same corpus and the same Azure stack as the first two parts, and report the numbers including the ones that do not flatter the idea.

目前流行的解决方案是 AI Agent，但我必须坦诚我对使用这个词的顾虑。现在以“Agentic RAG”（代理式检索增强生成）为名发布的内容，大多只是在向量搜索上加了一个重试循环、一个框架教程，或者是一篇没有实际运行系统支撑的观点文章。我不想随波逐流。因此，本文将以我所知的唯一方式认真对待 Agent 这个课题：精确定义设计，列出可能证明其错误的实验，在与前两部分相同的语料库和 Azure 架构上运行这些实验，并报告数据——包括那些对该理念不利的数据。

The claim I am testing is narrow and, I strongly believe, more interesting than the buzz: an agent’s reasoning is bounded by the vocabulary of its tools. Give a model a search box and a retry budget, and iteration buys you rephrased guesses. Give it the knowledge layer’s actual vocabulary, like typed traversal, timelines, diffs, entity resolution, a contradiction register, and iteration becomes something closer to navigation. The first two articles built that vocabulary. This one hands the model the steering wheel, puts hard bounds on the wheel, and measures what happens including the question I care most about: when the model is in control of its own loop, does the contradiction gate survive, or does a helpful agent route around governance the moment it becomes inconvenient?

我正在验证的观点很狭窄，但我坚信它比那些炒作更有趣：Agent 的推理能力受限于其工具的词汇表。给模型一个搜索框和重试预算，迭代只会换来换个说法的猜测。给它知识层的实际词汇，比如类型化遍历、时间线、差异对比、实体解析、矛盾寄存器，迭代就会变得更接近于“导航”。前两篇文章构建了这些词汇。本文将把方向盘交给模型，为方向盘设置硬性边界，并测量会发生什么，包括我最关心的问题：当模型控制自己的循环时，矛盾检测的“守门人”机制还能存续吗？还是说，一个“乐于助人”的 Agent 在治理机制变得不便时，会绕过它？

Everything runs on the infrastructure the series already pays for. No new services are added, and I mean that literally since the agent is code and tokens, not resources. The implementation lands in the same repository as Part 2, as a separate additive commit, so everything the previous article describes stays exactly where its readers found it.

所有内容都在本系列已有的基础设施上运行。没有添加任何新服务，我是字面意思上的“没有添加”，因为 Agent 本质上是代码和 Token，而不是资源。实现代码存放在与第二部分相同的仓库中，作为一个独立的增量提交，因此上一篇文章描述的所有内容都保持原样，读者可以随时查阅。

### Part I - The design revision
### 第一部分 - 设计修订

1. Where Part 2 ended, honestly
1. 坦诚地回顾第二部分的终点

A quick recap for readers joining here, and the usual honest accounting for readers returning. Part 1 built a persistent knowledge layer next to a RAG pipeline where an evidence layer holding chunked, embedded source documents, and a structured knowledge layer holding what the organization understands, like concepts, decisions, relationships, and contradictions as first-class objects with owners and an unresolved status the system refuses to resolve on its own. 

为新读者快速回顾，也为老读者进行一次诚实的总结。第一部分在 RAG 流水线旁构建了一个持久化知识层，其中包含一个存储分块、嵌入式源文档的证据层，以及一个存储组织理解内容的结构化知识层——例如概念、决策、关系和矛盾。这些被视为一等对象，拥有所有者，且系统拒绝在未明确的情况下自行解决其“未决”状态。

Part 2 replaced the router that chose between them with fusion: both engines run on every query, a bounded two-hop typed traversal joins hybrid search, and one external reranker orders the union with the relationship paths presented as evidence. Time moved inside the edges, where every relationship carries a validity window and an ingestion timestamp, and entity resolution became a two-threshold pipeline with embedding blocking and gray-zone adjudication.

第二部分用“融合”取代了两者之间的路由器：每个查询都会同时运行两个引擎，一个有界的两跳类型化遍历与混合搜索相结合，并由一个外部重排序器对合并后的结果进行排序，同时将关系路径作为证据呈现。时间维度被引入到边（edges）中，每个关系都带有有效期和摄入时间戳，实体解析则变成了一个包含嵌入阻塞（embedding blocking）和灰度区域裁决的双阈值流水线。

The live deployment kept the series honest in both directions. The good: 149 fragmented concepts became 120 like-for-like, the ingest-time detector opened a contradiction on its own during the seed, and every grounded bundle carried an average of ten typed, time-valid relationship paths that search alone cannot produce. The humbling: recall on the golden set was 0.75 with the graph and 0.75 without it, because twenty-one documents are too few for entry-point misses to occur, and the honest reading was that fusion currently earns its cost through grounding quality rather than recall.

实际部署让本系列在两个方向上都保持了诚实。好的方面：149 个碎片化概念在同等条件下减少到了 120 个，摄入时检测器在种子数据导入期间自动发现了一个矛盾，且每个证据包平均携带十条类型化、时间有效的关系路径，这是单纯搜索无法产生的。令人谦卑的方面：在黄金数据集上，有图谱和无图谱的召回率均为 0.75，因为 21 份文档太少，不足以出现入口点缺失的情况。诚实地讲，融合架构目前的价值在于提升了证据的质量，而非召回率。

One property of that system matters more than any of its numbers for what follows. It is a fixed pipeline. The same stages run in the same order for every question. Nothing in it can look at an intermediate result and decide to go back for more. Retrieval sufficiency and the judgment of whether we now hold enough to answer is decided implicitly, once, before the model has reasoned about anything.

对于接下来的内容，该系统的一个特性比任何数据都重要：它是一个固定的流水线。每个问题都按相同的顺序运行相同的阶段。系统中没有任何部分可以查看中间结果并决定“回头再取”。检索是否充分，以及我们是否掌握了足够的信息来回答问题，是在模型进行任何推理之前，被隐式地、一次性地决定的。

2. The question one pass cannot answer
2. 单次检索无法回答的问题

Let me make the ceiling concrete with the corpus’s own material, because an architectural limitation you cannot demonstrate is just an opinion. The roof inspection requirement evolved: a general threshold of twenty years from June 2025, then an analysis email explaining why a stricter threshold was coming, then a fifteen-year threshold scoped to H3 new business from 1 March 2026. Part 2 answers “which rule applied on 20 February?” cleanly - that is temporal filtering, and filtering is what fixed pipelines do well. Now ask a question one step more demanding: “Did any of our recorded claims involve properties that would have been flagged under the March roof threshold but passed under the rule that was actually in force when they were filed?” Answering that requires a sequence. First discover that there were two thresholds and when each was in force, then extract the property attributes from each claim file...

让我用语料库本身的材料来具体说明这个“天花板”，因为无法演示的架构限制仅仅是一种观点。屋顶检查要求经历了演变：最初是 2025 年 6 月起 20 年的通用阈值，随后是一封解释为何要实施更严格阈值的分析邮件，最后是 2026 年 3 月 1 日起针对 H3 新业务的 15 年阈值。第二部分可以清晰地回答“2 月 20 日适用哪条规则？”——这是时间过滤，而过滤正是固定流水线擅长的。现在问一个更具挑战性的问题：“我们记录的索赔中，是否有房产在 3 月份的屋顶阈值下会被标记，但在其提交时实际生效的规则下却通过了？”回答这个问题需要一个序列：首先发现存在两个阈值以及各自的生效时间，然后从每个索赔文件中提取房产属性……
