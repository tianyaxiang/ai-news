# Expanding our support for scientists
# 扩大我们对科学家的支持

As Claude becomes increasingly capable at scientific research, we are focusing on building products and programs to support the research community. In June, we launched Claude Science, a product that integrates the tools that researchers most commonly use, produces auditable artifacts, and provides flexible access to computing resources.
随着 Claude 在科学研究方面的能力日益增强，我们正致力于构建产品和项目以支持科研社区。今年 6 月，我们推出了 Claude Science，这是一款集成了研究人员最常用工具的产品，它能够生成可审计的工件（artifacts），并提供灵活的计算资源访问权限。

We have also continued to broaden our AI for Science program, which provides free credits to researchers working on high-impact scientific projects. Starting today, we are announcing a significant expansion of these efforts. We are opening 10,000 seats for scientists around the world to access Claude subscriptions for free and at discounted rates for one year through our new Claude team plan for scientists. Standard seats will be free and premium seats with 5x usage limits will be available for $15 per month. Over the coming months, we intend to extend this program well beyond the initial 10,000 seats.
我们还持续扩大了“AI for Science”项目，为从事高影响力科学项目的研究人员提供免费额度。从今天开始，我们宣布大幅扩展这些工作。我们为全球科学家开放了 10,000 个名额，通过全新的“科学家 Claude 团队计划”，他们可以免费或以折扣价获得为期一年的 Claude 订阅。标准席位将免费提供，而拥有 5 倍使用限额的高级席位每月仅需 15 美元。在接下来的几个月里，我们计划将该项目扩展至远超最初的 10,000 个名额。

Alongside these subscriptions, we are also expanding the scope and scale of our AI for Science program. To date, we’ve largely supported scientists using Claude for biological sciences. We are now looking to offer credits for researchers in other scientific fields as well, including those working on ambitious, compute-heavy research, such as the kind that resulted in progress on the Riemann zeta function and Claude’s work on protein design. By helping scientists access and increase their usage of Claude through subscriptions, credits, and products like Claude Science, we aim to radically accelerate scientific discovery.
除了这些订阅服务外，我们还在扩大“AI for Science”项目的范围和规模。迄今为止，我们主要支持的是将 Claude 用于生物科学的研究人员。现在，我们希望为其他科学领域的研究人员提供额度，包括那些从事雄心勃勃、计算密集型研究的科学家，例如在黎曼ζ函数方面取得进展的研究，以及 Claude 在蛋白质设计方面的工作。通过订阅、额度以及像 Claude Science 这样的产品，帮助科学家访问并增加 Claude 的使用量，我们的目标是彻底加速科学发现。

### Ways to access Claude
### 访问 Claude 的方式

To register for our Claude team plan for scientists, please complete the verification form here. You must be a principal investigator or equivalent at an academic or nonprofit research institution to qualify; once verified, you can add the researchers in your lab to your plan. As your lab makes use of your allotted credits and requires more usage than standard or premium plans provide, you can apply to our AI for Science program for up to $50,000 in credits per project. Any researcher is eligible to apply.
要注册我们的“科学家 Claude 团队计划”，请在此处填写验证表。您必须是学术或非营利研究机构的首席研究员（PI）或同等职位才有资格申请；验证通过后，您可以将实验室的研究人员添加到您的计划中。当您的实验室用完分配的额度，且需要超出标准或高级计划所提供的用量时，您可以向我们的“AI for Science”项目申请每个项目最高 50,000 美元的额度。任何研究人员均可申请。

For now, researchers working in biology and chemistry will still be limited to our Opus-class models. Claude Fable models will continue to block professional biology and drug development queries because of their potential dual-use risks. We’re working in partnership with the US government to establish an access program for life sciences professionals to use Mythos-class models for life sciences research and development. We have now enrolled our first participants, and expect to share more and increase access soon.
目前，从事生物学和化学研究的研究人员仍将被限制使用我们的 Opus 级模型。由于存在潜在的双重用途风险，Claude Fable 模型将继续拦截专业的生物学和药物开发查询。我们正与美国政府合作，建立一个访问计划，供生命科学专业人士使用 Mythos 级模型进行生命科学研发。我们现已招募了首批参与者，并期待在不久后分享更多信息并扩大访问权限。
