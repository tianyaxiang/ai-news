# VIPER: An Expert-Curated Benchmark for Vision-Language Models in Veterinary Pathology
# VIPER：兽医病理学视觉语言模型专家策划基准

**Title:** VIPER: An Expert-Curated Benchmark for Vision-Language Models in Veterinary Pathology
**标题：** VIPER：兽医病理学视觉语言模型专家策划基准

**Abstract:** Pathology vision-language models are advancing rapidly, yet existing benchmarks remain focused on human tissue, particularly oncology, leaving non-human pathology largely unaddressed. This gap is especially important in toxicologic pathology, where microscopic tissue examination of laboratory animals is a core component of preclinical drug safety assessment.
**摘要：** 病理学视觉语言模型正在迅速发展，但现有的基准测试仍集中于人类组织（尤其是肿瘤学领域），导致非人类病理学研究在很大程度上被忽视。这一差距在毒理学病理学中尤为重要，因为实验动物的显微组织检查是临床前药物安全性评估的核心组成部分。

To address it, we introduce VIPER, the first expert-curated benchmark for vision-language model evaluation in toxicologic pathology. VIPER contains 1,251 questions associated with 419 H&E-stained rat histology images across seven organ systems, covering multiple-choice, KPrim, and free-text formats. All questions were curated and validated by board-certified veterinary pathologists.
为了解决这一问题，我们推出了 VIPER，这是首个用于毒理学病理学视觉语言模型评估的专家策划基准。VIPER 包含 1,251 个问题，关联了涵盖七个器官系统的 419 张 H&E 染色大鼠组织学图像，题型涵盖多项选择题、KPrim 题和自由文本问答。所有问题均由获得认证的兽医病理学家策划并验证。

In total, we benchmarked 16 models, including two newly introduced veterinary-pathology models, seven human pathology-specialized models, and seven general-purpose frontier models. The results identify a substantial domain gap between veterinary and human pathology, expose the risk of over-diagnosis of normal tissue in frontier models, and show that domain-specific training remains critical for visually grounded predictions. VIPER data and evaluation code are available at this https URL.
我们总共对 16 个模型进行了基准测试，包括两个新推出的兽医病理学模型、七个专门针对人类病理学的模型以及七个通用前沿模型。结果表明，兽医病理学与人类病理学之间存在巨大的领域差距，揭示了前沿模型在正常组织诊断中存在过度诊断的风险，并证明了领域特定训练对于实现视觉基础预测仍然至关重要。VIPER 的数据和评估代码可在该链接获取。
