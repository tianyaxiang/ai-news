# Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash lets you build with more control
# Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash 让开发者拥有更强的创作控制力

Omni now delivers studio-quality video production, including the ability to extend a scene, first and last frame interpolation, crisp 4K upscaling, faster prototyping, and more.
Omni 现已提供影棚级的视频制作能力，包括场景扩展、首尾帧插值、清晰的 4K 超分辨率放大、更快的原型设计等功能。

We are introducing Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash, a production-ready update for developers that offers improved control over generative video. You can now extend scenes, specify start and end frames for smooth transitions, and generate high-resolution 4K output. Start building today by accessing the model through Google AI Studio or the Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform.
我们正式推出 Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash，这是一次面向开发者的生产就绪型更新，为生成式视频提供了更强的控制力。现在，您可以扩展场景、指定起始帧和结束帧以实现平滑过渡，并生成高分辨率的 4K 输出。立即通过 Google AI Studio 或 Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform 访问该模型，开启您的创作之旅。

"Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash" gives developers better control over generative video projects. Extend scenes up to 40 seconds with improved visual consistency and narrative flow. Set start and end frames to create smooth, professional camera movements and transitions. Use 360p previews to iterate faster and save money during your creative process. Upscale your final projects to 4K resolution for a polished, professional look.
“Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash”为开发者提供了对生成式视频项目更好的控制力。您可以将场景扩展至最长 40 秒，并获得更好的视觉一致性和叙事连贯性。通过设置起始帧和结束帧，您可以创建平滑、专业的运镜和转场效果。利用 360p 预览功能，您可以在创作过程中更快地进行迭代并节省成本。最终项目可放大至 4K 分辨率，呈现出精致、专业的视觉效果。

Today, we’re introducing Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash, a new suite of creative controls and generative video capabilities to support developers. Gemini Omni brought real-world reasoning to generative creation, and today’s updates make Omni 1.1 production-ready for professional use via the Gemini API in Google AI Studio. Whether you’re building generative video workflows, creative tools, or media editing software, these updates make generative video more controllable, faster to iterate on, and polished for real-world deployment.
今天，我们推出了 Gemini Omni 1.1 Flash，这是一套全新的创意控制工具和生成式视频功能，旨在为开发者提供支持。Gemini Omni 将现实世界的推理能力引入了生成式创作，而今天的更新使 Omni 1.1 能够通过 Google AI Studio 中的 Gemini API 投入专业生产环境。无论您是在构建生成式视频工作流、创意工具还是媒体编辑软件，这些更新都让生成式视频更具可控性、迭代速度更快，并能为实际部署提供更精良的成品。

### Extend scenes for longer storytelling
### 扩展场景，实现更长的叙事

Scene extension allows you to take an existing video and continue generating footage seamlessly from where it left off. With Omni 1.1, the model can now analyze up to 10 seconds of prior context — a leap from previous models that only referenced the final second. The result is improved visual consistency and narrative adherence, letting you build longer stories or branch into new creative directions. You can extend videos in 10-second increments up to a total cumulative length of 40 seconds.
场景扩展功能允许您基于现有视频，从中断处无缝继续生成后续画面。借助 Omni 1.1，模型现在可以分析长达 10 秒的前置上下文——这比以往仅参考最后一秒的模型有了质的飞跃。其结果是视觉一致性和叙事连贯性的显著提升，让您可以构建更长的故事或拓展出新的创意方向。您可以以 10 秒为增量扩展视频，总时长最长可达 40 秒。

### Specify first and last frames
### 指定起始帧与结束帧

Achieve smooth transitions and camera movements by specifying the starting and ending frames of a shot. Omni 1.1 generates continuous video between two keyframes, making it ideal for complex camera orbits, zoom transitions, or seamless looping clips.
通过指定镜头的起始帧和结束帧，实现平滑的转场和运镜效果。Omni 1.1 能够在两个关键帧之间生成连续的视频，非常适合复杂的轨道运镜、缩放转场或无缝循环片段。

### Draft videos more efficiently in 360p
### 以 360p 分辨率更高效地进行视频草稿制作

Generate lightweight 360p previews to iterate on your ideas quickly and cost-effectively before committing to high-resolution rendering.
生成轻量级的 360p 预览，以便在进行高分辨率渲染之前，快速且低成本地对您的创意进行迭代。
