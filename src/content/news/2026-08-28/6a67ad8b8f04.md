# 3 new ways to plan and book travel in Search
# 在搜索中规划和预订旅行的 3 种新方式

Our latest upgrades for AI Mode in Search can help you track flight prices, view points or miles rates, and book your dream hotel.
我们在搜索中对 AI 模式进行的最新升级，可以帮助您追踪机票价格、查看积分或里程兑换率，并预订您心仪的酒店。

Planning a trip comes with lots of moving parts and countless options to consider. With AI Mode in Search, you can already get real-time data to compare flights and hotels, find the best recommendations across the web, and craft the perfect itinerary. Now, we’re upgrading that experience with new ways to track flight prices, view points or miles rates, and book your dream hotel, right in AI Mode.
规划一次旅行涉及许多变动因素和无数需要考虑的选择。通过搜索中的 AI 模式，您已经可以获取实时数据来比较航班和酒店、在全网寻找最佳推荐，并制定完美的行程。现在，我们正在升级这一体验，让您能够直接在 AI 模式中追踪机票价格、查看积分或里程兑换率，并预订您心仪的酒店。

**Ask AI Mode to track flight prices for you.**
**让 AI 模式为您追踪机票价格。**

Today, we’re bringing Google Flights’ popular price tracking feature directly into AI Mode, letting you set up price alerts as you chat, without breaking your flow. Describe where and when you want to fly and AI Mode will show you the best options available with the latest prices from more than 300 partner airlines and travel sites. If you’re ready to book, you can build your full itinerary in AI Mode and buy your tickets through the airline’s website or your preferred booking platform.
今天，我们将 Google Flights 广受欢迎的价格追踪功能直接引入 AI 模式，让您在聊天时即可设置价格提醒，无需中断操作流程。只需描述您想去的地方和时间，AI 模式就会为您展示来自 300 多家合作航空公司和旅游网站的最新价格及最佳选择。如果您准备预订，可以在 AI 模式中构建完整的行程，并通过航空公司官网或您首选的预订平台购买机票。

Want to wait and see if prices drop? Just ask something like “track these flight prices for me.” Once you confirm, you’ll get an email if prices change for the destination and dates you have in mind, so you can jump on deals the moment they hit your inbox. Flight price tracking in AI Mode is now available in more than 180 countries and territories around the world.
想等等看价格是否会下降？只需询问类似“帮我追踪这些机票价格”的问题。确认后，如果您心仪的目的地和日期价格发生变动，您将收到电子邮件通知，这样您就能在优惠信息到达收件箱的第一时间抓住机会。目前，AI 模式中的机票价格追踪功能已在全球 180 多个国家和地区推出。

**See the cost in points or miles.**
**查看积分或里程兑换成本。**

Redeeming your hard-earned rewards is another great way to save on travel. Now, AI Mode in Search can show you the cost in points or miles for flights and hotels. Try asking something like “I want to travel from Atlanta to Miami using my AA miles. Help me find some options for nonstop flights departing Oct 9 and returning on Oct 12”.
兑换您辛苦赚取的奖励是节省旅行开支的另一种好方法。现在，搜索中的 AI 模式可以为您显示航班和酒店的积分或里程兑换成本。试着问类似这样的问题：“我想用我的美国航空（AA）里程从亚特兰大飞往迈阿密。帮我找一些 10 月 9 日出发、10 月 12 日返回的直飞航班选项。”

Right away, you’ll see how many miles you need to book matching flights, based on the latest data directly from our partners. It works the same way for hotels: just tell AI Mode where and when you’re traveling and ask to see the cost in points for the rewards program you have in mind. When you’re ready to book, you can follow the link to the partner site and complete your rewards redemption. Viewing points and miles rates in AI Mode is now globally available.
您将立即根据我们合作伙伴提供的最新数据，看到预订相应航班所需的里程数。酒店预订也是如此：只需告诉 AI 模式您的旅行地点和时间，并询问您心仪的奖励计划所需的积分。当您准备预订时，可以点击链接跳转至合作伙伴网站完成奖励兑换。目前，在 AI 模式中查看积分和里程兑换率的功能已在全球范围内可用。

We’re launching this capability with initial support for Alaska Airlines / Hawaiian Airlines, American Airlines, Choice Hotels International, Hilton, and Wyndham Hotels & Resorts. In the coming weeks, we’ll also add support for Accor, Flying Blue, Hyatt, LATAM Airlines, and Lufthansa Group.
我们首批支持的合作伙伴包括阿拉斯加航空/夏威夷航空、美国航空、精选国际酒店集团（Choice Hotels）、希尔顿和温德姆酒店及度假村。在接下来的几周内，我们还将增加对雅高酒店集团、法荷航蓝天飞行（Flying Blue）、凯悦酒店集团、南美航空和汉莎航空集团的支持。

**Book your dream hotel right in AI Mode.**
**直接在 AI 模式中预订您心仪的酒店。**

When you find a hotel you love, making the reservation should feel quick and effortless. Today, we’re introducing hotel booking through AI Mode in Search, so you can discover and book your next hotel in one seamless conversation. Simply tell AI Mode about your upcoming trip and your hotel preferences to get a visual list of options, complete with guest reviews and key factors to compare.
当您找到心仪的酒店时，预订过程应该既快捷又轻松。今天，我们推出了通过搜索中的 AI 模式进行酒店预订的功能，让您可以在一次无缝的对话中发现并预订下一家酒店。只需告诉 AI 模式您即将进行的旅行和酒店偏好，即可获得一份包含客房评论和关键对比因素的直观列表。

Once you’ve found the right hotel, just ask AI Mode to help you complete the booking. Then, select “Continue on Google” in the list of booking options — it’ll appear next to any of our integrated partners, which include hotel chains and online travel sites. From there, it’s just a few taps to pick your room type, review details like the cancellation policy, and complete the booking securely via Google Pay. When you book hotels through AI Mode, the hotel or booking platform will act as the merchant of record and handle any customer service.
一旦找到合适的酒店，只需让 AI 模式帮您完成预订。然后，在预订选项列表中选择“在 Google 上继续”（Continue on Google）——该选项会出现在我们任何已集成的合作伙伴旁边，包括连锁酒店和在线旅游网站。接下来，只需轻点几下即可选择房型、查看取消政策等详情，并通过 Google Pay 安全完成预订。当您通过 AI 模式预订酒店时，酒店或预订平台将作为记录商户并处理任何客户服务事宜。

Hotel booking in AI Mode has started rolling out in the U.S. in English and will be available over the coming weeks with the following partners: Booking.com, Choice Hotels International, Expedia, Hilton, Hotels.com, IHG Hotels & Resorts, Marriott International, Priceline, Trip.com, and Wyndham Hotels & Resorts.
AI 模式中的酒店预订功能已开始在美国以英语推出，并将在未来几周内通过以下合作伙伴提供：Booking.com、精选国际酒店集团、Expedia、希尔顿、Hotels.com、洲际酒店集团（IHG）、万豪国际集团、Priceline、携程（Trip.com）以及温德姆酒店及度假村。

Head over to AI Mode in Search today to start tracking prices, exploring rewards, and booking your next getaway.
立即前往搜索中的 AI 模式，开始追踪价格、探索奖励并预订您的下一次旅行吧。
