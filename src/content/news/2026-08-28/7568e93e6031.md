# Announcing our first Maintainers in Residence
# 宣布首批“驻场维护者”（Maintainers in Residence）

Aug. 26, 2026 · Lori Lorusso, Jakub Beránek on behalf of the Funding team
2026年8月26日 · Lori Lorusso, Jakub Beránek（代表资助团队）

We are very happy to announce the Rust Project's first round of Maintainers in Residence: Gen Li (@rami3l), Chris Denton (@ChrisDenton), Alejandra González (@blyxyas), León Liehr (@fmease), and Maintainer Grant recipients: Jason Newcomb (@Jarcho) and Jonas Böttiger (@joboet). These contributors will be funded for their rust-lang maintenance activities for (at least) the following 12 months!
我们非常高兴地宣布 Rust 项目首批“驻场维护者”（Maintainers in Residence，简称 MiR）：Gen Li (@rami3l)、Chris Denton (@ChrisDenton)、Alejandra González (@blyxyas)、León Liehr (@fmease)，以及获得“维护者资助”（Maintainer Grant）的 Jason Newcomb (@Jarcho) 和 Jonas Böttiger (@joboet)。这些贡献者在未来至少 12 个月内，将获得针对其 Rust 语言维护工作的资助！

The funding of the Maintainer in Residence (MiR) and Maintainer Grantee roles is possible thanks to generous donations to the Rust Foundation Maintainers Fund (RFMF) from Google, AWS, OpenAI, the Rust Project Leadership Council and also individual sponsors. We also want to thank the people who advocated for maintainer funding within their companies; Tyler Mandry from Google, Niko Matsakis and Jess Izen from AWS and Predrag Gruevski from OpenAI, and also the whole Rust Leadership Council and our funding advisors.
“驻场维护者”(MiR) 和“维护者资助”角色的资金支持，得益于 Google、AWS、OpenAI、Rust 项目领导委员会以及个人赞助者对 Rust 基金会维护者基金 (RFMF) 的慷慨捐赠。我们还要感谢在各自公司内积极倡导维护者资助计划的人士：来自 Google 的 Tyler Mandry，来自 AWS 的 Niko Matsakis 和 Jess Izen，来自 OpenAI 的 Predrag Gruevski，以及整个 Rust 领导委员会和我们的资助顾问。

If you would like to help us support even more Rust contributors, consider donating to RFMF. The Rust Foundation has published a press release and a blog post, where you can learn more about the sponsors and the supported contributors. Read more below to learn about the MiR program, how we chose the funded contributors, and of course who they are!
如果您希望帮助我们支持更多的 Rust 贡献者，请考虑向 RFMF 捐款。Rust 基金会已发布新闻稿和博客文章，您可以在其中了解更多关于赞助商和受资助贡献者的信息。请继续阅读下文，了解 MiR 项目、我们如何选择受资助的贡献者，当然还有他们是谁！

### Background
### 背景

The Maintainer in Residence program, established in RFC 3931, is designed to provide stable financial support for Rust contributors, so that they can truly focus on crucial maintenance activities. Currently, there are three categories of support that we offer:
根据 RFC 3931 设立的“驻场维护者”项目，旨在为 Rust 贡献者提供稳定的经济支持，使他们能够真正专注于关键的维护工作。目前，我们提供三种类别的支持：

* Full-time MiR: funded for 5 days/week of Rust Project work
* 全职 MiR：资助每周 5 天的 Rust 项目工作
* Half-time MiR: funded for ~2.5 days/week of Rust Project work
* 半职 MiR：资助每周约 2.5 天的 Rust 项目工作
* Maintainer Grant: funded for ~1 day/week of Rust Project work
* 维护者资助：资助每周约 1 天的 Rust 项目工作

Funding for this program comes from the Rust Foundation Maintainers Fund, which was launched recently, and the whole program is managed by the Rust Funding team. When deciding who to fund, we took a systematic approach. First, we looked at Rust teams to understand their maintenance baseline (the smallest number of maintainers they need to ensure a healthy long-term status of the given project or repository), and how far they currently are from that baseline.
该项目的资金来自近期启动的 Rust 基金会维护者基金，整个项目由 Rust 资助团队管理。在决定资助对象时，我们采取了系统化的方法。首先，我们考察了各个 Rust 团队，以了解他们的维护基准（即确保给定项目或仓库长期健康状态所需的最少维护者人数），以及他们目前距离该基准还有多远。

From there, we identified and prioritized Rust teams who were both critically underfunded, and have a high impact on the language and its users. These teams (in no particular order) were rustdoc, rustup, cargo, compiler, libs, clippy, rustfmt, rust analyzer and mods. The next step was pairing these teams with maintainers looking for funding. And it turns out that finding such maintainers for some teams turned out to be much more difficult than we originally assumed!
在此基础上，我们确定并优先考虑了那些资金严重不足，且对语言及其用户具有重大影响的 Rust 团队。这些团队包括（排名不分先后）：rustdoc、rustup、cargo、compiler、libs、clippy、rustfmt、rust analyzer 和 mods。下一步是将这些团队与寻求资助的维护者进行匹配。结果发现，为某些团队找到这样的维护者比我们最初预想的要困难得多！

For example, some maintainers are already employed, some do not want to be funded, and while we did our best to promote our funding efforts, not everyone looking for funding actually asked us for it. We also realized that some teams on our list have essentially no active members, which makes it tricky to onboard new contributors, even if they would like to help out.
例如，一些维护者已经有工作，一些人不想接受资助，而且尽管我们尽了最大努力宣传我们的资助计划，但并非所有寻求资助的人都向我们提出了申请。我们还意识到，名单上的一些团队基本上没有活跃成员，这使得即使有新人愿意帮忙，也很难引导他们加入。

In the end, we decided to start by supporting six contributors, who will help maintain several critical Rust projects and teams and who could start immediately. However, we are not stopping there. Our funding efforts are ongoing, so stay tuned for more MiR announcements in the near future! If you would like to learn more about our process, check out our recent post. And now, without further ado, let's meet our newly funded maintainers!
最终，我们决定先支持六位贡献者，他们将帮助维护几个关键的 Rust 项目和团队，并且能够立即开始工作。然而，我们不会止步于此。我们的资助工作仍在继续，敬请期待未来更多的 MiR 公告！如果您想了解更多关于我们流程的信息，请查看我们最近的文章。现在，废话不多说，让我们来认识一下新获得资助的维护者们！
