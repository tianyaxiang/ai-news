***

**Walk:** Velocity-tracking gait.
**Sit & stand:** Sits down, holds the pose, stands back up on its own.
**Kick:** A one-shot boot, then straight back to walking.
**Grab:** Dips the beak to the ground, scoops, and pops back upright.
**Roller skating:** Roller skating locomotion when the skates are equipped.
**Get back up:** Flat on its back to standing, all by itself, ready for the next command.
**行走：** 速度追踪步态。
**坐下与站立：** 自行坐下、保持姿势并重新站起。
**踢球：** 一次性踢球动作，随后立即恢复行走。
**抓取：** 将喙部探向地面，铲起物体，然后弹回直立状态。
**轮滑：** 装上滑轮后的轮滑移动方式。
**起身：** 从平躺状态自动站起，随时准备执行下一个指令。
