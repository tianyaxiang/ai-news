# Speedo’s new smart goggles module can track all four swim strokes
# Speedo 新款智能泳镜模块可追踪四种泳姿

Speedo is launching a removable smart module for its Vanquisher goggles that can analyze a swimmer’s performance across all four swim strokes: freestyle, backstroke, breaststroke, and butterfly. The new Speedo iQ system uses swim-specific sensors to analyze head movement in the water, time breaths, and track distance per stroke.

Speedo 正在为其 Vanquisher 系列泳镜推出一款可拆卸的智能模块，该模块能够分析游泳者在四种泳姿（自由泳、仰泳、蛙泳和蝶泳）下的表现。全新的 Speedo iQ 系统利用游泳专用传感器来分析水中的头部运动、呼吸时间以及每划水的距离。

Unlike other smart goggles, such as those from Form, Speedo’s iQ system doesn’t come with a built-in display. Instead, the Speedo iQ module automatically transmits data to its accompanying app, allowing swimmers to see their performance when they get out of the water. Additionally, Speedo notes that you can simply transfer the module to another pair of Vanquisher goggles if they need to be replaced.

与其他智能泳镜（如 Form 品牌）不同，Speedo 的 iQ 系统没有内置显示屏。相反，Speedo iQ 模块会自动将数据传输到配套的应用程序中，让游泳者在出水后查看自己的表现。此外，Speedo 指出，如果泳镜需要更换，用户只需将该模块转移到另一副 Vanquisher 泳镜上即可。

The Speedo iQ system doesn’t require a subscription, and it’s also legal to use during World Aquatics competitions. The Vanquisher goggles with the iQ module included cost $189. They’re available to order now in a variety of colors.

Speedo iQ 系统无需订阅，且在世界泳联（World Aquatics）的比赛中使用也是合规的。包含 iQ 模块的 Vanquisher 泳镜售价为 189 美元，目前已提供多种颜色供订购。
