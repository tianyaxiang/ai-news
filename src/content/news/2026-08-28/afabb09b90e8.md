# Google’s new Fitbit Air brings Pokémon Sleep to your wrist
# 谷歌新款 Fitbit Air 将《Pokémon Sleep》带到你的手腕上

Google is giving Pokémon fans a new reason to wear a Fitbit. The company announced Thursday a special-edition Fitbit Air that works with Pokémon Sleep, combining Fitbit’s health and fitness tracking and the sleep-focused Pokémon game. The announcement comes as Pokémon celebrates its 30th anniversary this year.
谷歌为宝可梦粉丝们提供了一个佩戴 Fitbit 的新理由。该公司周四宣布推出一款特别版 Fitbit Air，它可与《Pokémon Sleep》联动，将 Fitbit 的健康与健身追踪功能与这款以睡眠为主题的宝可梦游戏相结合。此次发布正值宝可梦系列诞生 30 周年之际。

Pokémon Sleep launched in 2023, turning something as ordinary as going to bed into a Pokémon game. Players can earn in-game rewards for getting to sleep before their set bedtime, while the amount and quality of sleep they get helps drive their progress. You start with a big, sleepy Snorlax. The more you sleep, the more “Drowsy Power” your Snorlax builds up, which in turn attracts more Pokémon for you to discover.
《Pokémon Sleep》于 2023 年推出，将睡觉这样一件平凡的事情变成了一款宝可梦游戏。玩家在设定的就寝时间前入睡即可获得游戏内奖励，而睡眠的时长和质量则有助于推动游戏进度。游戏开始时，你会拥有一只又大又困的卡比兽。你睡得越多，卡比兽积累的“睡意之力”就越强，从而吸引更多宝可梦供你发现。

The Fitbit Air adds another layer to that experience. It can sync with both the Google Health app and Pokémon Sleep, allowing users to track metrics including daily movement, heart rate, and cardio load during the day, then use the device to record sleep duration and quality overnight.
Fitbit Air 为这种体验增添了新的维度。它可以同时与 Google Health 应用和《Pokémon Sleep》同步，允许用户追踪包括日常运动、心率和全天心肺负荷在内的各项指标，并在夜间使用该设备记录睡眠时长和质量。

This isn’t the first wearable that can connect with Pokémon Sleep. The app already supports several wearables, including Apple Watch, the Fitbit series, Galaxy Watch, and Google Pixel Watch. Players can also use Pokémon Sleep without a wearable at all. For Pokémon’s 30th anniversary, however, it’s a particularly clever crossover. It turns something people already do every night into another opportunity to engage with the franchise, while giving Google a playful way to make wearable health tracking feel more fun.
这并不是第一款可以连接《Pokémon Sleep》的可穿戴设备。该应用此前已支持多种可穿戴设备，包括 Apple Watch、Fitbit 系列、Galaxy Watch 和 Google Pixel Watch。玩家甚至无需佩戴任何设备也能使用《Pokémon Sleep》。然而，对于宝可梦 30 周年纪念来说，这是一种特别巧妙的跨界合作。它将人们每晚必做的事情变成了与该系列互动的又一契机，同时也为谷歌提供了一种有趣的方式，让可穿戴健康追踪变得更加生动。

Google has also recently teamed up with athlete Stephen Curry for a special edition of the Pixel Watch for $579, featuring an exclusive design built for workouts. The special-edition device is available to preorder for $129 and starts shipping September 15. It will be sold online and in stores through the Google Store and Target. The standard Fitbit Air, meanwhile, costs $99.
谷歌最近还与运动员斯蒂芬·库里（Stephen Curry）合作推出了售价 579 美元的 Pixel Watch 特别版，采用了专为锻炼打造的独家设计。这款特别版设备预售价为 129 美元，将于 9 月 15 日开始发货。它将通过 Google Store 和塔吉特（Target）在线上及线下门店销售。与此同时，标准版 Fitbit Air 的售价为 99 美元。

Google introduced its new Fitbit Air in May, featuring a slimmer design, improved health and fitness tracking, and an AI-powered coach that provides personalized guidance based on users’ activity, sleep, and wellness data.
谷歌于今年 5 月推出了新款 Fitbit Air，其特点是设计更轻薄、健康与健身追踪功能得到改进，并配备了人工智能教练，可根据用户的活动、睡眠和健康数据提供个性化指导。
