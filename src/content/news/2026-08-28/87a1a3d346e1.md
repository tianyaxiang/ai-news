# Agentic AI Is Rewriting The Analytics Stack But There's One Skill It Still Can't Touch
# 代理式 AI 正在重写分析技术栈，但有一项技能它依然无法触及

Over the past few years, I am sure we all have witnessed and experienced a slow but significant change in the way we work. We have upskilled ourselves often and my analytics stack kept rebuilding itself underneath me. I remember when I first read about the prowess of Generative AI, my thought stopped at “I will never have to spend hours on StackOverflow to debug my code again." 

在过去的几年里，我相信我们都见证并经历了一种缓慢但重大的工作方式变革。我们不断提升自己的技能，而我的分析技术栈也在我脚下不断重构。我记得当我第一次读到生成式 AI 的强大能力时，我的想法仅仅停留在：“我再也不用花几个小时在 StackOverflow 上调试代码了。”

At work today, I use AI models to write, design, code, sound board my ideas, complete tasks at breakneck speed, help me draft proposals for executives, create analytical stories, test my hypotheses, prepare me for important meetings and difficult discussions and so much more. However, the more time I spend integrating AI to make my life “easy”, I realize that there is some tough work humans do that AI cannot exactly replicate. Generative AI cannot set aspirations, make decisions when times are tough, build trust among stakeholders, or hold itself accountable for good or bad. That work remains deeply human.

在今天的工作中，我使用 AI 模型来写作、设计、编码、交流想法、以惊人的速度完成任务、协助起草高管提案、创建分析故事、测试假设、为重要会议和艰难讨论做准备等等。然而，随着我花越来越多的时间整合 AI 来让生活变得“轻松”，我意识到人类所做的一些艰苦工作是 AI 无法完全复制的。生成式 AI 无法设定愿景、在困难时期做出决策、在利益相关者之间建立信任，也无法为结果的好坏承担责任。这些工作依然深深地属于人类。

As agentic AI absorbs more of the analytics workflow, the scarcity in your tech stack will not be speed or quality of execution but the ability to generate an original thought, apply business judgment, and decide what deserves attention in the first place.

随着代理式 AI（Agentic AI）吸收了越来越多的分析工作流，你技术栈中的稀缺资源将不再是执行的速度或质量，而是产生原创思想、运用商业判断力，以及首先决定什么值得关注的能力。

### Independent Thought Is Still a Competitive Advantage
### 独立思考依然是竞争优势

I've come to believe that AI won't replace human jobs. But AI can certainly help us execute work faster than ever before. At that point, the real question becomes: what do we do with the time that AI gives back to us? Do we use it to think more deeply, explore new ideas, and solve harder problems? Or do we gradually stop doing those things ourselves?

我逐渐相信 AI 不会取代人类的工作，但它确实能帮助我们比以往任何时候都更快地执行工作。在这一点上，真正的问题变成了：我们如何利用 AI 节省下来的时间？我们是用它来深入思考、探索新想法并解决更难的问题？还是逐渐停止自己做这些事情？

Compared to earlier this year, I am noticing a shift in my own behavior. I increasingly catch myself asking Copilot to interpret patterns in data before looking at it myself. I see myself preferring convenience over challenging myself. Why spend twenty minutes exploring a dataset when an AI can lay the ground for me in seconds?! The convenience is undeniable.

与今年年初相比，我注意到自己的行为发生了转变。我越来越频繁地在自己查看数据之前，就让 Copilot 去解读数据中的模式。我发现自己更倾向于便利，而不是挑战自我。当 AI 可以在几秒钟内为我打好基础时，为什么还要花二十分钟去探索数据集呢？！这种便利性是不可否认的。

However, this dependency on AI is making me nervous. And that has nothing to do with the agent getting it wrong (because often it doesn't). I get nervous thinking how easy it is to skip the most valuable part of analysis: forming my own initial point of view! I would have simply received someone else's version of it, dressed up as mine.

然而，这种对 AI 的依赖让我感到不安。这与代理程序是否出错无关（因为它通常不会出错）。我感到紧张是因为想到跳过分析中最有价值的部分是多么容易：即形成我自己的初步观点！我只是接收了别人的版本，并将其包装成我自己的。

That's my big anxiety around the whole AI-and-jobs conversation: not me becoming obsolete, but unoriginal. Imagine going through an entire career without regularly having a single original thought. I do not want the intellectual friction that produces insights, creativity and conviction to go away because I derive a great sense of accomplishment working through the challenges.

这就是我围绕“AI 与工作”这一话题最大的焦虑：不是我变得过时，而是变得平庸。想象一下，整个职业生涯中都没有产生过一个原创想法。我不希望那种产生洞察力、创造力和信念的思维摩擦消失，因为我在克服挑战的过程中获得了巨大的成就感。

My thought is that in a world where intelligence becomes abundant, independent thought becomes scarce.

我的想法是，在一个智能变得唾手可得的世界里，独立思考将变得稀缺。

### The Business Translation Problem
### 商业翻译问题

Working in analytics & AI for over seven years now, I know that the analysts who struggle are rarely the ones who aren’t the strongest in writing SQL queries or code in Python; it's the analysts who can't translate what the data says and what the business should do next.

在分析和 AI 领域工作了七年多，我知道那些感到吃力的分析师，很少是因为不擅长编写 SQL 查询或 Python 代码；而是因为他们无法将数据所表达的内容转化为业务下一步应该采取的行动。

Today's AI can do far more than generate SQL or summarize dashboards. With access to semantic layers (a business-aware abstraction that sits with your organization’s AI model and helps AI understand metrics, definitions, relationships, and organizational context), modern agents can understand metrics, detect patterns, recommend actions, and in some cases, even execute actions autonomously (Agentic AI) with the knowledge of business rules, historical decisions and enterprise risks.

今天的 AI 能做的远不止生成 SQL 或总结仪表板。通过访问语义层（一种与组织 AI 模型结合的业务感知抽象，帮助 AI 理解指标、定义、关系和组织背景），现代代理可以理解指标、检测模式、推荐行动，在某些情况下，甚至可以利用业务规则、历史决策和企业风险的知识自主执行行动（代理式 AI）。

I am saying that the analytics stack is being rewritten because I am seeing AI become increasingly capable of both analysis and execution. And yet, a critical gap remains.

我说分析技术栈正在被重写，是因为我看到 AI 在分析和执行两方面都变得越来越强大。然而，一个关键的鸿沟依然存在。

The challenge with using AI at work now isn't understanding the business. Increasingly, AI can. The challenge is getting AI to own the consequences of business decisions (maybe we will reach there too tomorrow). Humans routinely make decisions that contradict the data because they understand the strategic priorities, organizational politics, cultural nuances, regulatory risks, or simply a belief about where the business needs to go next. These decisions are not always objectively correct, but someone must ultimately be accountable for making them. But AI, in my opinion, cannot determine when the rules should be broken. That judgement is still entirely human. So, the analytics stack may be changing but accountability isn't and that's the layer Agentic AI still can't touch.

现在在工作中利用 AI 的挑战不在于理解业务。AI 越来越能做到这一点了。真正的挑战在于让 AI 为商业决策的后果负责（也许未来我们能做到）。人类经常做出与数据相悖的决策，因为他们理解战略重点、组织政治、文化细微差别、监管风险，或者仅仅是对业务未来走向的信念。这些决策并不总是客观正确的，但最终必须有人为此负责。但在我看来，AI 无法决定何时应该打破规则。这种判断力依然完全属于人类。因此，分析技术栈可能会改变，但责任归属不会，而这正是代理式 AI 依然无法触及的层面。

### How I Use AI to Work Faster Without Letting It Think for Me
### 我如何利用 AI 提高工作效率，同时不让它代替我思考

As Agentic AI is taking over more of the work that used to consume our days from query writing to documentation, reporting, analysis, research and even framing recommendations into a story, the question is no longer whether AI can do parts of our job. The question is what we do with the time it gives back. Do we reinvest that time into learning something new and deeper, develop a stronger judgment, or work on a more ambitious problem? Or do we gradually outsource those capabilities too?

随着代理式 AI 接管了更多曾经占据我们日常的工作——从查询编写到文档记录、报告、分析、研究，甚至将建议构建成故事——问题不再是 AI 是否能完成我们工作的一部分。问题在于我们如何利用它节省下来的时间。我们是将其重新投入到学习更深入的新知识、培养更强的判断力，还是去解决更具雄心的问题？还是说我们也会逐渐将这些能力外包出去？

For me, the real career question in this AI era is: what should I delegate to AI, and what should I deliberately keep my hands on?

对我来说，在这个 AI 时代，真正的职业问题是：我应该把什么委托给 AI，又应该刻意保留什么？

**This Quarter**
**本季度**

*   Audit where you spend most of your time each week. Separate the work that is mechanical from the work that requires judgment.
    *   审计你每周大部分时间花在哪里。将机械性工作与需要判断力的工作区分开来。
*   Delegate the mechanical work to AI freely - write up long summaries, documentation, data preparation, first drafts, exploratory queries. Save your energy for questions that matter and what the answers to those questions actually mean.
    *   自由地将机械性工作委托给 AI——撰写长篇摘要、文档、数据准备、初稿、探索性查询。把你的精力留给重要的问题，以及这些问题的答案究竟意味着什么。
*   Before asking AI for an interpretation, spend five minutes forming your own hypothesis.
    *   在要求 AI 进行解读之前，花五分钟形成你自己的假设。
*   Use the AI agents to challenge your thinking, not replace it.
    *   利用 AI 代理来挑战你的思维，而不是取代它。
