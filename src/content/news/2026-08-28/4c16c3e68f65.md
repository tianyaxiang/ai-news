# GTA VI looks just as great as we could hope for
# 《GTA VI》的表现正如我们所期待的那样出色

Netflix and Rockstar Games finally debuted their “extended look” at Grand Theft Auto VI. It showed that the new game looks to keep much of the spirit of GTA — exploration, driving, crimes, shooting, and cinematic story scenes. But everything just looks much better than previous entries, with impressive graphics, densely-packed rooms, and detailed environments. The footage was captured on PS5, according to the end of the video.

Netflix 和 Rockstar Games 终于发布了《侠盗猎车手 VI》（GTA VI）的“加长版预览”。视频显示，这款新作保留了 GTA 系列的核心精髓——探索、驾驶、犯罪、射击以及电影般的剧情场景。但一切看起来都比前作有了显著提升，拥有令人印象深刻的图形表现、细节丰富的室内空间以及精致的环境设计。据视频结尾显示，这些画面均在 PS5 上录制。

A key focus of the game seems to be how protagonists Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos work together. In one scene, it appears you play as Duval as he hangs out of the window of a car that Caminos is driving so he can shoot at police chasing after them. Later, though, it looks like the player controls Caminos as the driver while Duval and another character keep shooting the police.

游戏的一个核心重点似乎是两位主角 Jason Duval 和 Lucia Caminos 之间的协作。在一个场景中，玩家似乎扮演 Duval，他从 Caminos 驾驶的车窗探出身子，向追捕他们的警察射击。而在随后的片段中，玩家似乎转而控制驾驶车辆的 Caminos，同时 Duval 和另一名角色继续向警察开火。

Another part of the video shows a mission where Duval and Caminos are split up. As enemies barge into an upper floor of a building, Duval, who is a security detail for the mission, takes them on by hiding behind cover and shooting at them. Caminos, who is on a different floor, sneaks around on foot to try and evade detection. At one point, while she is struggling on the ground against an enemy, you can see button prompts to press to escape their grasp.

视频的另一部分展示了一个 Duval 和 Caminos 分头行动的任务。当敌人闯入大楼高层时，负责安保工作的 Duval 通过掩体进行还击。而身处另一楼层的 Caminos 则徒步潜行，试图避开敌人的搜查。在某个时刻，当她与敌人进行地面缠斗时，屏幕上出现了按键提示，引导玩家摆脱敌人的控制。

Much of the video included tightly-edited scenes and cutscenes from the game, but there were a few other brief glimpses of gameplay, like a weapon wheel menu and activities driving a boat, playing basketball, underwater diving, racing on a track, and parachuting.

视频中包含了大量精心剪辑的游戏场景和过场动画，但也简短地展示了一些其他玩法，例如武器轮盘菜单，以及驾驶快艇、打篮球、潜水、赛道竞速和跳伞等活动。

The game appears to be quite large. As part of a preview by The New York Times, Rockstar North co-studio head Rob Nelson said that a February playthrough of the game took 80 hours, including both the main story and some optional content. The map is three times as big as the playable map of Red Dead Redemption 2, Rockstar tells the NYT.

这款游戏的规模似乎非常庞大。在《纽约时报》的一篇预览报道中，Rockstar North 工作室联席负责人 Rob Nelson 表示，在二月份的一次通关测试中，完成主线剧情及部分支线内容耗时 80 小时。Rockstar 向《纽约时报》透露，游戏地图的面积是《荒野大镖客：救赎 2》可游玩地图的三倍。

You’ll be able to switch between the two protagonists at “any time,” though you can also choose to have one character tag along while you’re running around the world or experience things as just one of them, according to IGN, which also got a preview of the game. Duval and Caminos also don’t necessarily have to be a romantic couple; that’s up to the player. (There’s a specific mechanic for holding hands.)

据同样参与了游戏预览的 IGN 报道，玩家可以“随时”在两位主角之间切换。你也可以选择让其中一名角色跟随你探索世界，或者只扮演其中一人进行体验。Duval 和 Caminos 也不一定非要是情侣关系，这完全取决于玩家的选择。（游戏中甚至有一个专门的牵手机制。）

Snapmatic, GTA’s take on Instagram, returns in GTA VI, though you can’t actually post on it “due to Jason and Lucia being a couple on the run and not wanting to leave a digital footprint,” according to IGN.

Snapmatic（GTA 版的 Instagram）将在《GTA VI》中回归。不过据 IGN 称，你无法在上面发布内容，因为“Jason 和 Lucia 是一对逃亡中的情侣，他们不想留下任何数字足迹”。

The six-star wanted system is also officially back, and police can remember what you look like, meaning you may need to change outfits to lose them, IGN says. If you want to steal a parked car to get away from a sticky situation, you might want to look for an older car: more expensive cars will be harder to steal and you may need certain equipment to break into certain cars.

IGN 还提到，六星通缉系统正式回归，且警察会记住你的外貌，这意味着你可能需要更换服装来摆脱追捕。如果你想偷一辆停在路边的车来逃离困境，最好找一辆旧车：昂贵的车辆将更难窃取，你可能需要特定的工具才能撬开车门。

The deep dive follows several apparent video leaks of the game from a person or group identifying themselves as “CyberLeek,” which Rockstar said were “heartbreaking.”

此次深度预览之前，曾有自称“CyberLeek”的个人或组织泄露了多段游戏视频，Rockstar 对此表示感到“心碎”。

Thursday’s extended look will be available on Rockstar’s YouTube channel and the GTA VI website at 9PM ET. The game is scheduled to release on November 19th at a starting price of $79.99, but you won’t be able to buy it on a physical disc.

周四发布的加长版预览视频将于美东时间晚上 9 点在 Rockstar 的 YouTube 频道和《GTA VI》官网上线。游戏定于 11 月 19 日发售，起售价为 79.99 美元，但该作将不会提供实体光盘版本。
