### How Do We Actually Use Sigmoid?
### 我们实际上是如何使用 Sigmoid 的？

We already know the equation of the sigmoid function:
我们已经知道了 Sigmoid 函数的公式：
$$\sigma(x) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-x}}$$

Before we proceed, let's see how we use it in logistic regression. For example, we want to predict whether a student will pass or fail based on the number of hours they studied. We are using the logistic regression model here. First, it calculates a score:
在继续之前，让我们看看它在逻辑回归中是如何使用的。例如，我们想根据学生的学习时长来预测他们是否会通过考试。这里我们使用逻辑回归模型。首先，它会计算一个分数：
$$z = wx + b$$

Let's say the score for a student is: $z = 2$. This score is not a probability. It is just the linear combination of parameters. Now we pass it through the sigmoid function:
假设某位学生的分数是：$z = 2$。这个分数并不是概率，它只是参数的线性组合。现在我们将其通过 Sigmoid 函数：
$$\sigma(z) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-z}}$$

We get:
我们得到：
$$\sigma(2) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-2}} \approx 0.88$$

The sigmoid function always produces an output between 0 and 1. Here the output is approximately 0.88 or 88%. In logistic regression, this can be interpreted as an 88% probability of the student passing the exam. We can then use a threshold, such as 0.5, to make the final classification. In short, the flow can be like:
Sigmoid 函数的输出始终在 0 到 1 之间。这里的输出约为 0.88 或 88%。在逻辑回归中，这可以解释为学生通过考试的概率为 88%。然后，我们可以使用一个阈值（例如 0.5）来进行最终分类。简而言之，流程如下：
$$\text{Score} \rightarrow \text{Sigmoid} \rightarrow \text{Probability} \rightarrow \text{Class}$$

That's how we commonly use the sigmoid function in logistic regression.
这就是我们通常在逻辑回归中使用 Sigmoid 函数的方式。

### But What Is This “e”?
### 但这个“e”到底是什么？

Now, let's once again look at the sigmoid equation:
现在，让我们再次看看 Sigmoid 公式：
$$\sigma(z) = \frac{1}{1 + e^{-z}}$$

The first thing we notice is the $e$. We know that it is a mathematical constant and its value is $e \approx 2.71828$. But what exactly is 'e'? Why is this number present in the sigmoid equation? Let's take a step back and understand where this number comes from. One thing is that here we are not trying to discover 'e', but the goal is to understand the significance of 'e' and see where it naturally appears. Now let's go to the bank and see what we can observe.
我们注意到的第一件事就是 $e$。我们知道它是一个数学常数，其值约为 $e \approx 2.71828$。但“e”到底是什么？为什么这个数字会出现在 Sigmoid 公式中？让我们退一步，了解这个数字的来源。这里我们并不是要重新发现“e”，而是要理解“e”的意义，并看看它是在哪里自然出现的。现在，让我们去银行看看能观察到什么。

### Let's Start with a Simple Bank Example
### 让我们从一个简单的银行例子开始

Imagine we deposited Rs.100 into a bank account. Let's say the bank is giving us a 100% annual interest rate. If the bank adds the entire year's interest at the end of the year, we earn Rs.100 in interest. So after one year, we have:
想象一下，我们在银行存入了 100 卢比。假设银行给我们 100% 的年利率。如果银行在年底一次性结算全年的利息，我们就能获得 100 卢比的利息。所以一年后，我们有：
$$100 + 100 = 200$$

We can also write it as:
我们也可以写成：
$$100(1 + 1) = 200$$

Rs.100 became Rs.200 after one year. But now let's change one thing. What if the bank doesn't wait until the end of the year to add the interest? What if it adds the interest twice a year? The annual interest rate is still 100%. But now the year is divided into two periods. So for each six-month period we get half of the annual interest rate:
100 卢比在一年后变成了 200 卢比。但现在我们改变一下条件。如果银行不等年底才结算利息呢？如果它一年结算两次利息呢？年利率仍然是 100%，但现在一年被分成了两个周期。因此，在每个六个月的周期里，我们获得年利率的一半：
$$\frac{1}{2} = 0.5 = 50\%$$

During the first six months, we get:
在前六个月里，我们得到：
$$100(1 + \frac{1}{2}) = 150$$

After six months, we have Rs.150. During the next six months, the interest is calculated on this new amount:
六个月后，我们有 150 卢比。在接下来的六个月里，利息是基于这个新金额计算的：
$$150(1 + \frac{1}{2}) = 225$$

Then we have:
所以我们有：
$$100(1 + \frac{1}{2})^2 = 225$$

Why did we get Rs.225 instead of Rs.200? Because the interest earned during the first six months also earned interest during the second six months. In simple terms we can say 'interest earns interest'. This is the basic idea behind compound interest.
为什么我们得到的是 225 卢比而不是 200 卢比？因为前六个月赚取的利息在后六个月也产生了利息。简单来说，就是“利滚利”。这就是复利背后的基本思想。

### What Happens When We Compound More Frequently?
### 当我们更频繁地复利时会发生什么？

Now let's make the compounding more frequent.
现在让我们增加复利的频率。

If we compound four times a year:
如果我们一年复利四次：
$$100(1 + \frac{1}{4})^4 \approx 244.14$$

If we compound 12 times a year:
如果我们一年复利 12 次：
$$100(1 + \frac{1}{12})^{12} \approx 261.30$$

If we compound every day:
如果我们每天复利：
$$100(1 + \frac{1}{365})^{365} \approx 271.46$$

Observe the pattern. As we increase the number of compounding periods, the final amount keeps increasing. The reason is that growth is being applied repeatedly to an amount that has already increased.
观察一下这个规律。随着我们增加复利周期的次数，最终金额不断增加。原因在于，增长是反复应用于已经增加的金额上的。

### Where Does e Come From?
### “e”是从哪里来的？

The Rs.100 is not the important part here. Let's remove it and look at the growth factor:
100 卢比在这里并不重要。让我们去掉它，看看增长因子：
$$(1 + \frac{1}{n})^n$$

Here, 'n' represents the number of times we compound during the year. For example:
这里，“n”代表一年中我们复利的次数。例如：
$$(1 + \frac{1}{1})^1 = 2$$
$$(1 + \frac{1}{2})^2 = 2.25$$
$$(1 + \frac{1}{4})^4 \approx 2.4414$$
$$(1 + \frac{1}{12})^{12} \approx 2.613$$
$$(1 + \frac{1}{365})^{365} \approx 2.7146$$

As we make the compounding more and more frequent, the value gets closer and closer to 2.71828…. This number is called 'e'.
随着我们让复利变得越来越频繁，这个值越来越接近 2.71828…。这个数字被称为“e”。
$$e \approx 2.71828$$

Mathematically, we can express this idea using a limit:
在数学上，我们可以用极限来表达这个思想：
$$e = \lim_{n \rightarrow \infty} (1 + \frac{1}{n})^n$$

The notation may look complex, but the idea is simple. Here, we are asking: "What value does this expression approach as 'n' becomes larger and larger?" As 'n' increases, $(1 + \frac{1}{n})^n$ gets closer and closer to 2.71828…. That limiting value...
这个符号看起来可能很复杂，但思想很简单。在这里，我们问的是：“当 'n' 变得越来越大时，这个表达式趋近于什么值？”随着 'n' 的增加，$(1 + \frac{1}{n})^n$ 越来越接近 2.71828…。那个极限值……
