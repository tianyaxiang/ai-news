# Mapping Woody Vegetation from Multi-Source Imagery and Prediction Fusion for Enhanced Data Efficiency and Accuracy
# 利用多源影像与预测融合技术提升木本植被测绘的数据效率与准确性

**Abstract:** Tree cover maps are a fundamental remote sensing product, used to derive ecological insights about the landscape and are essential to change detection, vegetation mapping and fire monitoring programs. However, comprehensive tree cover mapping requires reliable and high-quality imagery, free of cloud and weather defects to ensure accurate model outputs. Deep learning approaches can generate high quality maps with minimal human intervention but require large amounts of human annotated data to be successful.

**摘要：** 树木覆盖图是一种基础的遥感产品，用于获取有关景观的生态见解，对于变化检测、植被测绘和火灾监测项目至关重要。然而，全面的树木覆盖测绘需要可靠且高质量的影像，且需排除云层和天气缺陷，以确保模型输出的准确性。深度学习方法可以在极少人工干预的情况下生成高质量地图，但需要大量的人工标注数据才能取得成功。

In this work we propose a framework consisting of methods that aim to improve the data efficiency and robustness of deep learning models using data fusion techniques to segment woody vegetation defined as vegetation over the height of 2m across the state of New South Wales, Australia. To improve robustness against varying image quality, we propose an image composition method that normalizes the imagery and removes defects, whilst also minimizing the reliance on individual image quality by proposing a prediction fusion method. The two methods resulted in an error reduction of 38.2% and 53.6% respectively compared to single-source imagery.

在这项工作中，我们提出了一个框架，包含多种旨在提高深度学习模型数据效率和鲁棒性的方法。该框架利用数据融合技术，对澳大利亚新南威尔士州高度超过2米的木本植被进行分割。为了提高模型应对不同影像质量的鲁棒性，我们提出了一种影像合成方法，通过归一化处理和缺陷去除来优化影像；同时，通过提出一种预测融合方法，最大限度地降低了对单一影像质量的依赖。与单一来源影像相比，这两种方法分别使误差降低了38.2%和53.6%。

To address deep learning approaches' limitation of requiring large amounts of data, we apply label transfer to multiple sources of imagery as a form of data augmentation to improve data efficiency. Learning from multiple image sources was shown to be the biggest improvement in performance, resulting in an error reduction between 28.1% to 76.2% across the different validation experiments, whilst reducing the standard deviation of performance across image dates by a factor of 13.

为了解决深度学习方法需要大量数据的局限性，我们将标签迁移（label transfer）应用于多种影像来源，作为一种数据增强形式来提高数据效率。研究表明，从多源影像中学习是性能提升最显著的因素，在各项验证实验中，误差降低了28.1%至76.2%，同时将不同影像日期下的性能标准差降低了13倍。
