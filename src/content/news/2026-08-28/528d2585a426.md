# The GTA VI ‘extended look’ is now streaming on YouTube
# 《GTA VI》“加长版预览”现已在 YouTube 上线

After initially debuting on Netflix, the official version of Rockstar’s big GTA VI preview is more widely available.
在最初于 Netflix 首播后，Rockstar 备受瞩目的《GTA VI》官方预览视频现已在更广泛的平台上发布。

Rockstar has officially published its “extended look” at Grand Theft Auto VI on YouTube and on its website, as promised. The in-depth preview, which “entirely” features footage captured from the PS5 version of the game, initially premiered on Netflix at 3PM ET. But because Rockstar allowed creators to post reaction videos, you’ve technically been able to watch the video on YouTube before the studio’s official upload.
正如承诺的那样，Rockstar 已正式在 YouTube 及其官网上发布了《侠盗猎车手 VI》（Grand Theft Auto VI）的“加长版预览”。这段深度预览视频“完全”采用了 PS5 版本的游戏实机画面，最初于美东时间下午 3 点在 Netflix 首播。但由于 Rockstar 允许创作者发布反应视频，实际上你在官方上传之前，就已经能在 YouTube 上看到相关内容了。

The nearly 27-minute preview largely focuses more on atmosphere, protagonists Jason Duval and Lucia Caminos, and highlighting locations in the game instead of diving deep on specific game mechanics. It feels more like slices of a movie than a video game trailer. But there are a few sections showing off playable shootouts and police car chases as well as activities like shooting a basketball and scuba diving. Other outlets that got previews from Rockstar have more details on gameplay that weren’t included in the video.
这段近 27 分钟的预览视频主要侧重于氛围营造、主角 Jason Duval 和 Lucia Caminos 的刻画，以及对游戏内场景的展示，而非深入探讨具体的游戏机制。它给人的感觉更像是电影片段，而非传统的游戏预告片。不过，视频中也有部分片段展示了可操作的枪战、警车追逐，以及投篮和潜水等活动。其他从 Rockstar 获得预览机会的媒体则提供了更多视频中未包含的游戏玩法细节。

GTA VI is set to release on November 19th at a starting price of $79.99.
《GTA VI》定于 11 月 19 日发售，起售价为 79.99 美元。
