Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.07111 (cs) [Submitted on 4 Nov 2026] Title: Consistent yet Contextual: Learning Author-Level Mixtures of Style for Authorship Attribution Authors: Omkar Yadav, Michael Jiang, Kenan Alkiek, Jian Zhu, David Jurgens.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.07111 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 4 日] 标题：一致且语境化：学习用于作者归属的作者级风格混合模型 作者：Omkar Yadav, Michael Jiang, Kenan Alkiek, Jian Zhu, David Jurgens。

Abstract: Approaches to authorship verification or attribution typically model the style of a document, asking whether two documents were likely written by the same author. While recent approaches have been performant, such approaches largely ignore modeling the authors themselves and how varied their style is.

摘要：作者身份验证或归属的方法通常对文档的风格进行建模，以判断两篇文档是否可能由同一作者撰写。尽管近期的方法表现良好，但这些方法在很大程度上忽略了对作者本身及其风格多样性的建模。

Here, we introduce multiple alternatives for modeling the author and demonstrate that allowing an author's representation to capture multiple consistent but distinct writing styles yields significant performance improvements.

在此，我们引入了多种作者建模的替代方案，并证明允许作者的表示捕捉多种一致但独特的写作风格，可以显著提升性能。

Using our new authorship model, we then examine when an author writes in a way that is most idiomatic of their style across four scenarios: (i) variation in the size of their writing audiences, (ii) temporal distance from when the model was trained, (iii) the effect of preceding context, and (iv) the effect of genre norms on style.

利用我们新的作者模型，我们随后在四种场景下考察了作者何时以最符合其风格的方式进行写作：(i) 写作受众规模的变化，(ii) 距离模型训练时间的跨度，(iii) 前置语境的影响，以及 (iv) 体裁规范对风格的影响。

Across all four experiments, we demonstrate that authors vary substantially in how idiolectic their writing is. Our results have clear implications for both the modeling and data used for authorship verification and attribution, showing that authors need to be represented with their multiple styles and that some settings produce text that is much more informative about who wrote that text.

在所有四项实验中，我们证明了作者在写作的个人特质程度上存在显著差异。我们的研究结果对作者身份验证和归属所使用的建模及数据具有明确的启示，表明需要用多种风格来表示作者，并且某些环境产生的文本在揭示作者身份方面具有更高的信息量。
