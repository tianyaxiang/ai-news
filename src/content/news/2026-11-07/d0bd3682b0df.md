# Just about anyone can rent access to White House IP addresses

据 Krebs on Security 和 The Register 报道，白宫似乎有人正在运行某种软件，可能允许外部人员仅需“几美元”即可租用总统网络的访问权限。这一发现来自安全公司 Layer3 Intel 和 Synthient，他们在白宫网络中发现了住宅代理活动。

A residential proxy opens up a way for people to rent access to a home IP address and route their internet traffic through it, allowing them to conceal where their activity is actually coming from. Some apps and browser extensions include third-party software development kits (SDKs) that put proxy nodes on a user’s device — whether users know it or not. Third-party services can then sell access to the residential proxies on users’ devices.

住宅代理为人们提供了一种租用家庭 IP 地址访问权限并路由其互联网流量的方式，从而使他们能够隐藏其活动的真实来源。一些应用程序和浏览器扩展程序包含第三方软件开发工具包 (SDK)，这些工具包会在用户设备上放置代理节点——无论用户是否知情。随后，第三方服务便可以出售对用户设备上这些住宅代理的访问权限。

As noted by the cybersecurity firm Spur, people can legitimately use residential proxies for purposes like ad verification and quality assurance testing, but bad actors can also use them to bypass geolocation controls and defensive filters. Hackers have used residential proxies for all kinds of malicious activity, including to create botnets and carry out account takeovers.

正如网络安全公司 Spur 所指出的，人们可以合法地将住宅代理用于广告验证和质量保证测试等目的，但恶意行为者也可以利用它们绕过地理位置控制和防御过滤器。黑客曾利用住宅代理进行各种恶意活动，包括创建僵尸网络和实施账户接管。

At the White House, Layer3 Intel found that 28 residential proxy services routed traffic out of the network from October 5th to November 3rd. It notes that this activity typically happens between 5PM ET and midnight. “That pattern suggests a person rather than a public guest Wi-Fi network: someone regularly on the White House network late into the evening, whose device is infected with a proxy SDK,” Layer3 says. The SDK in question might be linked to the watch party app Rave, according to Synthient, which says the app “bundles 12 proxy SDKs into the platform.”

在白宫，Layer3 Intel 发现从 10 月 5 日到 11 月 3 日，有 28 个住宅代理服务将流量从该网络中路由出去。该公司指出，这种活动通常发生在美东时间下午 5 点到午夜之间。Layer3 表示：“这种模式表明是某个人而非公共访客 Wi-Fi 网络所为：有人在深夜经常使用白宫网络，其设备感染了代理 SDK。”据 Synthient 称，相关的 SDK 可能与观看派对应用 Rave 有关，该应用“在平台中捆绑了 12 个代理 SDK”。

Synthient found “a number of Windows 10 and Windows 11 devices on the White House’s network beaconing to known proxy providers like Geonode, Infatica and Mellowtel.” As noted by Krebs on Security, Infatica is a proxy service founded by Vladimir Fomenko, the owner of King Servers, which was linked to 2016 cyberattacks on election systems in Illinois and Arizona.

Synthient 发现“白宫网络上有许多 Windows 10 和 Windows 11 设备正在向 Geonode、Infatica 和 Mellowtel 等已知代理提供商发送信号。”正如 Krebs on Security 所指出的，Infatica 是一家由 Vladimir Fomenko 创立的代理服务公司，他是 King Servers 的所有者，该公司曾与 2016 年针对伊利诺伊州和亚利桑那州选举系统的网络攻击有关。

Layer3 warns that the residential proxy is “public access,” adding that “anybody can obtain it by signing up to a public residential proxy service, paying a few dollars and sending requests through it.”

Layer3 警告称，该住宅代理属于“公共访问”权限，并补充说“任何人都可以通过注册公共住宅代理服务、支付几美元并通过其发送请求来获取该权限。”
