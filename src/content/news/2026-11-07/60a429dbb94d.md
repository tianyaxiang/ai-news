Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.07065 (cs) [Submitted on 4 Nov 2026] Title: Stochastic Dynamics of Generative AI: From Autoregressive Models to Interacting LLM Networks and Neural SDEs Authors: Vikram Krishnamurthy, Yiming Zhang, Luke Snow.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.07065 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 4 日] 标题：生成式人工智能的随机动力学：从自回归模型到交互式大语言模型网络及神经随机微分方程。作者：Vikram Krishnamurthy, Yiming Zhang, Luke Snow。

Abstract: This paper develops a stochastic dynamical-systems perspective on three classes of generative AI models. First, we formulate large language models (LLMs) as high-dimensional nonlinear autoregressive models with attention-based dependencies. This formulation connects pretraining by conditional maximum likelihood to sequential token generation and provides a probabilistic interpretation of LLM operation.

摘要：本文从随机动力系统的视角对三类生成式人工智能模型进行了研究。首先，我们将大语言模型（LLMs）表述为具有基于注意力依赖关系的高维非线性自回归模型。这一表述将通过条件最大似然进行的预训练与序列化标记生成联系起来，并为大语言模型的运行提供了概率解释。

Second, we consider networks of interacting LLMs and characterize their collective behavior through mean-field ordinary differential equations. These dynamics provide a framework for understanding emergent preferential attachment and persistent structural biases, including type-dependent glass-ceiling effects.

其次，我们考虑了交互式大语言模型网络，并通过平均场常微分方程来刻画它们的集体行为。这些动力学为理解涌现的优先连接和持续的结构性偏见（包括与类型相关的“玻璃天花板”效应）提供了一个框架。

Third, we consider neural stochastic differential equations (SDEs) as continuous-time generative models for stochastic trajectories, including those observed at irregular time intervals. We discuss their training with particular emphasis on Wiener-space cubature methods based on rough-path analysis as an efficient alternative to Monte Carlo estimation.

第三，我们将神经随机微分方程（SDEs）视为随机轨迹的连续时间生成模型，包括在不规则时间间隔内观测到的轨迹。我们讨论了它们的训练过程，特别强调了基于粗糙路径分析的维纳空间求积法，将其作为蒙特卡洛估计的一种高效替代方案。

Together, the three settings address probabilistic prediction, information exchange and emergent behavior in interacting networks, and path-space generative modeling.

总之，这三种设置涵盖了概率预测、交互网络中的信息交换与涌现行为，以及路径空间生成建模。
