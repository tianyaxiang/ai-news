Some Roku OS users have been sad to learn that their favorite screensaver will no longer be available as of December 1. This week, affected users reported receiving an alert on their device that they will not be able to use the “Aquatic Life” screensaver after this month, as first reported by Cord Cutters News.

一些 Roku OS 的用户感到难过，因为他们得知自己最喜欢的屏幕保护程序将于 12 月 1 日起下架。据 Cord Cutters News 最先报道，本周受影响的用户收到设备提醒，称本月之后将无法再使用“水生生物”（Aquatic Life）屏幕保护程序。

The screensaver has been an option for Roku OS users for 10 years. Roku has been licensing the animation, which is called the Marine Aquarium, from artist Jim Sachs through Prolific Publishing. Sachs is the creator and copyright holder of the Marine Aquarium. He developed the screensaver through his company SereneScreen.

该屏幕保护程序已为 Roku OS 用户提供了 10 年。Roku 一直通过 Prolific Publishing 公司向艺术家 Jim Sachs 授权这款名为“海洋水族馆”（Marine Aquarium）的动画。Sachs 是“海洋水族馆”的创作者和版权所有者，他通过自己的公司 SereneScreen 开发了这款屏幕保护程序。

He tells Ars Technica that the licensing agreement with Roku is up for renewal every October and can be canceled by either party for any reason. A day after learning that Fox was planning to buy Roku, a deal that closed today, Sachs told Prolific that he wouldn’t be renewing Roku’s license. “I can’t be part of the Fox world,” he tells Ars, pointing to Fox News’ controversial style of news and entertainment.

他告诉 Ars Technica，与 Roku 的授权协议每年 10 月到期续签，任何一方均可因任何理由取消。在得知福克斯（Fox）计划收购 Roku（该交易已于今日完成）的第二天，Sachs 就告知 Prolific 他不会续签 Roku 的授权。“我无法成为福克斯世界的一部分，”他向 Ars 表示，并指出了福克斯新闻（Fox News）备受争议的新闻和娱乐风格。

Roku will automatically switch the Aquatic Life screensaver on affected devices to the Roku City screensaver, another popular animation that includes subtle advertisements, according to the user notice. The Aquatic Life screensaver provided an ad-free, relaxing display for years for some Roku users.

根据用户通知，Roku 将自动把受影响设备上的“水生生物”屏幕保护程序切换为“Roku 城市”（Roku City）屏幕保护程序，这是另一款包含隐性广告的流行动画。“水生生物”屏幕保护程序多年来一直为部分 Roku 用户提供无广告、令人放松的显示效果。

Numerous people online have lamented the impending loss of the screensaver for Roku OS. “When my head is in overdrive (very often with my ADHD), this screensaver grounds me and lets me exhale and become focused with myself again. It may sound over the top, but it’s true. It takes a lot for me to chill and relax, but this Aquatic Life [screensaver] is my go to. Totally gutted 😢,” a Reddit user wrote this week.

许多网友对 Roku OS 即将失去这款屏幕保护程序表示惋惜。一位 Reddit 用户本周写道：“当我的大脑超负荷运转时（患有 ADHD 的我经常这样），这个屏幕保护程序能让我平静下来，让我呼出一口气，重新专注于自己。这听起来可能有点夸张，但确实如此。我很难让自己冷静放松，但这款‘水生生物’[屏幕保护程序]是我的首选。真的太难过了😢。”

Sachs told Ars he’s interested in placating fans of the Marine Aquarium and said Roku has asked him to restore the license. When asked what his biggest concerns about Fox buying Roku are, Sachs said: It seems that Fox simply wants another venue for spreading their philosophy, which I consider repugnant. I can’t stand the idea of my artwork being used to lure even one person down that rabbit hole.

Sachs 告诉 Ars，他有兴趣安抚“海洋水族馆”的粉丝，并表示 Roku 已请求他恢复授权。当被问及对福克斯收购 Roku 的最大担忧时，Sachs 说：“福克斯似乎只是想寻找另一个传播其理念的渠道，而我认为这种理念令人反感。我无法忍受我的艺术作品被用来诱导哪怕一个人陷入那个深渊。”

The decision to not renew the licensing contract has “major financial consequences,” Sachs said. “Our needs are very small, with a high property tax bill being the only significant expense,” he said. “We’ll be okay but can’t start any of the projects I had planned.”

Sachs 表示，不续签授权合同的决定带来了“重大的财务后果”。他说：“我们的需求很小，高额的房产税账单是唯一的重大开支。我们会没事的，但无法启动我计划中的任何项目。”

According to Sachs, the licensing “agreement was $80,000 per year for exclusive rights to all set-top devices, or $60,000 per year for non-exclusive. They have always opted for exclusive.” Ars asked Roku to confirm the details of the licensing agreement but didn’t hear back ahead of publication.

据 Sachs 称，该授权“协议规定，所有机顶盒设备的独家版权费用为每年 8 万美元，非独家版权费用为每年 6 万美元。他们一直选择独家版权。”Ars 曾要求 Roku 确认授权协议的细节，但在发布前未收到回复。

The licensing deal with Roku was Sachs’ primary form of income, he told Ars, noting low sales of the version of the screensaver for computers. Marine Aquarium fans can still buy the screensaver for Windows, macOS, iOS, and Android, but it seems unlikely that it will return to the Roku platform.

Sachs 告诉 Ars，与 Roku 的授权交易是他主要的收入来源，并指出该屏幕保护程序的电脑版本销量很低。“海洋水族馆”的粉丝仍然可以购买适用于 Windows、macOS、iOS 和 Android 的版本，但它似乎不太可能回到 Roku 平台。

“One of the best reasons to buy Roku was the themes and less ads than competitors. But they haven’t added any new themes in years aside from sponsored ones, and they increased the amount of ads,” a Reddit user posted this week in response to Roku’s loss of the Aquatic Life screensaver.

“购买 Roku 的最好理由之一就是它的主题和比竞争对手更少的广告。但除了赞助主题外，他们多年来没有增加任何新主题，而且还增加了广告数量，”一位 Reddit 用户本周在回应 Roku 失去“水生生物”屏幕保护程序时写道。

Sachs, meanwhile, says he has “no delusions” that pulling the license will have any meaningful impact. However, he’s hopeful that another streaming company will look to offer the screensaver. “I’m assuming that Fox will not be reversing their whole rage-based business model and take responsibility for all the harm it has caused. I’d be happy to restore the license under those circumstances. They could even have it for free,” he said.

与此同时，Sachs 表示他“没有幻想”撤回授权会产生任何重大影响。不过，他希望另一家流媒体公司能考虑提供这款屏幕保护程序。他说：“我假设福克斯不会改变其基于愤怒的商业模式，也不会为其造成的伤害承担责任。在这种情况下，我很乐意恢复授权。他们甚至可以免费使用。”

Fox closed its acquisition of Roku today for $22 billion.

福克斯今日以 220 亿美元完成了对 Roku 的收购。
