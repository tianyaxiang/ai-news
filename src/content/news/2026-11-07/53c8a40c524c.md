# OpenAI valued at $1.25 trillion, as rival Anthropic nears its IPO

OpenAI 估值达到 1.25 万亿美元，竞争对手 Anthropic 即将进行首次公开募股 (IPO)。

CEO Sam Altman said recently that OpenAI is prioritizing safety instead of going ‘guns blazing’ toward an IPO.

首席执行官山姆·奥特曼 (Sam Altman) 最近表示，OpenAI 正在优先考虑安全性，而不是“火力全开”地推进 IPO。

OpenAI is now valued at $1.25 trillion in a new round of funding that has raised another $11 billion, according to Bloomberg. That would make it one of the world’s most valuable companies, not long after raising $122 billion from investors in March at a valuation of $852 billion.

据彭博社报道，OpenAI 在新一轮融资中筹集了 110 亿美元，目前估值已达 1.25 万亿美元。这使其成为全球最有价值的公司之一，而就在不久前的 3 月，该公司刚以 8520 亿美元的估值从投资者那里筹集了 1220 亿美元。

Echoing FT and Wall Street Journal reports from last week, Bloomberg says OpenAI is still pursuing additional investments that could increase the amount to $20 billion, and that it had initially hoped to raise as much as $30 billion at a higher valuation of $1.4 trillion. That was before tech stocks dropped, and the results of the midterms showed how much public opinion has turned against AI companies and data center projects.

彭博社援引《金融时报》和《华尔街日报》上周的报道称，OpenAI 仍在寻求额外投资，这可能会将融资总额提高到 200 亿美元。该公司最初曾希望以 1.4 万亿美元的更高估值筹集多达 300 亿美元。那是在科技股下跌以及中期选举结果显示公众舆论对人工智能公司和数据中心项目产生强烈抵触情绪之前的情况。

OpenAI’s rival Anthropic filed its S-1 to go public last week. Earlier this year, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman said it’s holding off on its own IPO until it can make more confident claims about its models’ safety. He didn’t give a specific timeline for the IPO, but said at the time, “I think it’s also kind of bad for the world if OpenAI waits too long to go public.”

OpenAI 的竞争对手 Anthropic 上周提交了 IPO 申请文件 (S-1)。今年早些时候，OpenAI 首席执行官山姆·奥特曼表示，在能够对其模型的安全性做出更自信的声明之前，公司将推迟 IPO。他没有给出具体的 IPO 时间表，但当时表示：“我认为如果 OpenAI 等待太久才上市，对世界来说也是一种损失。”
