Security researchers have discovered hundreds of copies of a Chinese-made AI hacking tool publicly exposed to the internet for anyone to access, potentially allowing outsiders to launch cyberattacks against unsuspecting targets. The hacking software, called Artex, is an open source autonomous penetration testing tool, meaning software that automatically probes computer systems for weaknesses, and it’s been linked to several cyberattacks since its public release in July.

安全研究人员发现，数百份中国制造的 AI 黑客工具副本被公开暴露在互联网上，任何人都可以访问，这可能使外部人员能够对毫无戒心的目标发动网络攻击。这款名为 Artex 的黑客软件是一款开源的自动化渗透测试工具，即能够自动探测计算机系统漏洞的软件，自 7 月公开发布以来，它已与多起网络攻击事件相关联。

As open source software, it’s free for anyone to download and run on their own servers, making it a popular tool for those who cannot access Anthropic or OpenAI’s most advanced cybersecurity AI models for discovering security vulnerabilities. Microsoft has previously described Artex as a system of AI agents that can conduct cyberattacks “from reconnaissance through data exfiltration with little needed human interaction.”

作为开源软件，任何人都可以免费下载并在自己的服务器上运行，这使其成为那些无法使用 Anthropic 或 OpenAI 最先进的网络安全 AI 模型来发现安全漏洞的人群中的热门工具。微软此前曾将 Artex 描述为一个 AI 代理系统，能够在几乎无需人工干预的情况下，执行从侦察到数据窃取的全套网络攻击。

In a blog post, cybersecurity firm Censys, which scans the internet for exposed or vulnerable systems, said that it identified more than 800 distinct deployments of Artex that were accessible from the web without needing a password to log in. Silas Cutler, principal security researcher at Censys who wrote the blog post, said that not every instance of Artex is operated maliciously and many are used for authorized testers.

网络安全公司 Censys 在一篇博文中表示，该公司通过扫描互联网上的暴露或易受攻击系统，识别出 800 多个无需密码即可从网络访问的 Artex 独立部署实例。撰写该博文的 Censys 首席安全研究员 Silas Cutler 表示，并非所有 Artex 实例都是恶意操作的，许多实例被用于授权测试。

But it’s unclear why hundreds of these Artex systems are exposed to the web, as the software does not require them to be accessible from the internet. The findings underscore how even powerful hacking tools can be misconfigured to be publicly accessible by anyone, increasing the chance that someone will find and misuse them.

但目前尚不清楚为何数百个 Artex 系统会暴露在网络上，因为该软件并不要求必须从互联网访问。这些发现强调了即使是强大的黑客工具也可能因配置错误而变得对公众开放，从而增加了被他人发现并滥用的风险。

The publicly accessible systems also expose attack tools, such as implants (hidden malicious software) used to compromise and maintain persistent access to a victim’s network. Security companies have linked the use of Artex to several broad-scale cyberattacks, including against the Korean finance industry in September.

这些可公开访问的系统还暴露了攻击工具，例如用于入侵并维持对受害者网络持久访问权限的植入程序（隐藏的恶意软件）。安全公司已将 Artex 的使用与多起大规模网络攻击联系起来，包括 9 月份针对韩国金融业的攻击。

Cybersecurity giant CrowdStrike attributed the attacks to a Chinese-speaking hacker who used an Artex instance in the attacks, in part because the hacker had left an “open directory” hosting the system. Censys said that its data showed the Artex instance in question had been publicly exposed for months, and appeared to contain more attack data that extended “well beyond South Korean banks,” including payment and gaming companies.

网络安全巨头 CrowdStrike 将这些攻击归因于一名使用 Artex 实例的讲中文的黑客，部分原因是该黑客留下了一个托管该系统的“开放目录”。Censys 表示，其数据显示，相关的 Artex 实例已公开暴露数月，且似乎包含更多攻击数据，其范围“远超韩国银行”，还涉及支付和游戏公司。

Artex was also used in an attack on IT support giant ConnectWise earlier in October, which allowed Chinese-linked hackers to spy on thousands of organizations whose data and files are hosted in Microsoft’s cloud. Microsoft, which has visibility into the attack, said in a blog post that the hackers used Artex to break into customers’ cloud environments and steal their data at such speed that defenders had “little time to detect and respond.”

Artex 还被用于 10 月初针对 IT 支持巨头 ConnectWise 的攻击，该攻击使与中国有关联的黑客能够监视数千个在微软云端托管数据和文件的组织。对此次攻击具有可见性的微软在博文中表示，黑客使用 Artex 入侵客户的云环境并窃取数据的速度极快，以至于防御者“几乎没有时间进行检测和响应”。

Many of the attacks linked to Artex involved exploiting simple vulnerabilities, such as guessing weak or known passwords, and a lack of multi-factor authentication. The findings show that simple vulnerabilities, including misconfigurations, can expose both the attacker and their targets. Artex has since announced it would go closed-source, effectively limiting further releases for paid customers. There are still dozens of copies of Artex’s code available on code-sharing sites that can still be used in active attacks.

许多与 Artex 相关的攻击都涉及利用简单的漏洞，例如猜测弱密码或已知密码，以及缺乏多因素身份验证。这些发现表明，包括配置错误在内的简单漏洞可能会同时暴露攻击者及其目标。Artex 此后宣布将转为闭源，从而有效地限制了付费客户的后续发布。目前在代码共享网站上仍有数十份 Artex 代码副本，这些代码仍可用于主动攻击。
