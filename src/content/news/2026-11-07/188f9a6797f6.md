The early iPhone Duo reviews were a bit hard on Apple’s new foldable for the lack of Duo-optimized apps available at the time of launch. But Apple says things are changing quickly on the software front. The company on Friday announced there are now over 100,000 apps built for the Duo live on the App Store, and it’s seeing thousands more being added to the store every day.

早期的 iPhone Duo 评测对苹果这款新折叠屏手机颇有微词，主要是因为发布时缺乏针对 Duo 优化的应用程序。但苹果表示，软件方面的情况正在迅速改善。该公司周五宣布，App Store 上现在已有超过 10 万款专为 Duo 构建的应用程序，并且每天还有数千款新应用上架。

The news was timed alongside the release of over a dozen more notable Duo-ready apps, including Microsoft Word and Outlook, Zoom, sports apps from the NBA and NHL, shopping apps like Etsy and LTK, productivity apps like Goodnotes and Extra, the Halide Mark III camera app, and others. These join the iPhone Duo-optimized apps that were already available, like Netflix, YouTube, Instagram, and WhatsApp.

此消息发布的同时，还有十多款值得关注的 Duo 适配应用上线，包括 Microsoft Word 和 Outlook、Zoom、来自 NBA 和 NHL 的体育应用、Etsy 和 LTK 等购物应用、Goodnotes 和 Extra 等生产力应用、Halide Mark III 相机应用等。这些应用加入了此前已有的 Netflix、YouTube、Instagram 和 WhatsApp 等 iPhone Duo 优化应用行列。

Non-optimized apps are definitely less than ideal, as they come with a black bar on the side of the screen, which takes up valuable space. Sometimes, they hide important functions under an overflow menu. Apple’s data seems to suggest that at least some iOS developers may have needed extra time to get their apps ready for its latest form factor.

未优化的应用程序显然不够理想，因为屏幕侧面会出现黑条，占据了宝贵的空间。有时，它们还会将重要功能隐藏在溢出菜单中。苹果的数据似乎表明，至少部分 iOS 开发者需要额外的时间来为这种最新的外形规格准备其应用程序。

The company said that more than 2,000 app developers attended its over 50 in-person workshops that were hosted across 19 cities worldwide. Here, the developers could use the Duo and work on their apps with Apple’s support. The Duo has also proven to be more popular than perhaps Apple had planned for, given its high price point. (It starts at $1,999 for the 256GB model.)

该公司表示，超过 2,000 名应用开发者参加了其在全球 19 个城市举办的 50 多场线下研讨会。在这些研讨会上，开发者可以使用 Duo 并在苹果的支持下开发他们的应用。鉴于其高昂的价格（256GB 型号起售价为 1,999 美元），Duo 的受欢迎程度似乎也超出了苹果的预期。

The device quickly sold out within hours of orders opening up, and now, Duo ship times have shifted into the next year. On its earnings call this week, Apple said it’s “hard to predict” when the Duo constraints leading to those delays will improve.

该设备在开放订购后的几小时内便迅速售罄，目前 Duo 的发货时间已排到了明年。在本周的财报电话会议上，苹果表示“很难预测”导致这些延迟的 Duo 供应限制何时能得到改善。

“It’s been wonderful to see how quickly developers have embraced iPhone Duo, and we’re grateful for their creativity and innovation,” said Susan Prescott, Apple’s vice president of Worldwide Developer Relations, in a statement referring to the 100,000-app milestone. “The enthusiasm has been incredible, and we’re excited to see the pace of updates continue to accelerate as more and more apps optimize for iPhone Duo,” she added.

苹果全球开发者关系副总裁苏珊·普雷斯科特（Susan Prescott）在谈到 10 万款应用的里程碑时表示：“看到开发者如此迅速地拥抱 iPhone Duo，真是太棒了，我们感谢他们的创造力和创新精神。”她补充道：“这种热情令人难以置信，随着越来越多的应用针对 iPhone Duo 进行优化，我们很高兴看到更新速度持续加快。”

Other notable apps that have been updated for iOS 27 and optimized for the iPhone Duo include the following: Spotify, CBS, Tubi, BBC iPlayer (U.K.), Yahoo Fantasy Sports, Sportsnet (Canada), Facebook Messenger, Google Chat, ChatGPT, Grok Bot, Microsoft Copilot, Excel, PowerPoint, SAP Concur, monday.com, PDF Expert, Documents, iA Writer, Substack, The Washington Post, Yahoo News, FOX News, Kalshi, Yahoo Finance, Canva, Suno, Tinder, Google Translate, Strava, Alma, Withings, MyFitnessPal, Bevel, Transit, Citi Bike, ParkMobile, Viator, Tesla Robotaxi, Priceline, Kroger, Domino’s, Taco Bell, Untappd, The Weather Channel, FOX Weather, and RainViewer.

其他已更新至 iOS 27 并针对 iPhone Duo 进行优化的知名应用包括：Spotify、CBS、Tubi、BBC iPlayer（英国）、Yahoo Fantasy Sports、Sportsnet（加拿大）、Facebook Messenger、Google Chat、ChatGPT、Grok Bot、Microsoft Copilot、Excel、PowerPoint、SAP Concur、monday.com、PDF Expert、Documents、iA Writer、Substack、华盛顿邮报、雅虎新闻、福克斯新闻、Kalshi、雅虎财经、Canva、Suno、Tinder、谷歌翻译、Strava、Alma、Withings、MyFitnessPal、Bevel、Transit、Citi Bike、ParkMobile、Viator、特斯拉 Robotaxi、Priceline、Kroger、达美乐披萨、塔可钟、Untappd、The Weather Channel、FOX Weather 和 RainViewer。

Not all these updates have yet to fully roll out, we should note. Spotify, for instance, is still showing the black bar on the side, and the App Store doesn’t yet say an update is available on our review unit of the Duo. However, given that this list comes directly from Apple, we expect to see these updates soon.

我们需要指出的是，并非所有这些更新都已完全推出。例如，Spotify 的侧面仍然显示黑条，而且在我们评测用的 Duo 设备上，App Store 尚未显示有更新可用。不过，鉴于这份名单直接来自苹果，我们预计很快就能看到这些更新。
