Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.07113 (cs) [Submitted on 4 Nov 2026] Title: What Patients Say They Can't Access: Mining Cancer Screening Barriers from Online Communities Authors: Ruimin Sun, Aokun Chen, Arjun Arunasalam.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.07113 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 4 日] 标题：患者称无法获得什么：从在线社区挖掘癌症筛查障碍 作者：Ruimin Sun, Aokun Chen, Arjun Arunasalam。

Abstract: Cancer screening disparities persist across the U.S., yet the patient-reported barriers driving low uptake remain poorly characterized at scale. While clinical surveys capture structured responses, they miss the organic, unsolicited discourse where patients candidly describe real-world obstacles.

摘要：美国的癌症筛查差异依然存在，但导致筛查率低下的患者报告障碍在大规模研究中仍缺乏充分的描述。虽然临床调查可以获取结构化的反馈，但它们错过了患者坦诚描述现实障碍的自发性、非主动的讨论。

In this paper, we conduct a large-scale analysis of over 56,000 posts across six cancer-specific Reddit communities to surface barriers to cancer screening among U.S. patients. We apply a pipeline combining keyword filtering, barrier triage, open coding, and codebook synthesis to systematically extract and categorize patient-reported barriers from 576 U.S.-located posts.

在本文中，我们对六个癌症相关的 Reddit 社区中的 56,000 多条帖子进行了大规模分析，以揭示美国患者在癌症筛查方面遇到的障碍。我们应用了一个结合关键词过滤、障碍分类、开放编码和代码本合成的流程，从 576 条位于美国的帖子中系统地提取并分类了患者报告的障碍。

Our qualitative analysis reveals recurring structural, informational, and psychosocial barriers that vary meaningfully by cancer type and community context. These findings lay the groundwork for future work aimed at proactively identifying and addressing barriers at scale - spanning a broader range of cancer types and populations - and informing the design of targeted interventions, navigator programs, and health system policies that translate patient-reported obstacles into actionable solutions.

我们的定性分析揭示了反复出现的结构性、信息性和社会心理障碍，这些障碍因癌症类型和社区背景的不同而有显著差异。这些发现为未来的工作奠定了基础，旨在主动识别并大规模解决这些障碍——涵盖更广泛的癌症类型和人群——并为设计有针对性的干预措施、导航项目和卫生系统政策提供参考，从而将患者报告的障碍转化为可行的解决方案。
