Hybrid Mesh-Gaussian rendering combines compact textured surfaces with flexible 3D Gaussian primitives, offering an efficient alternative to purely Gaussian scene representations for structured environments.

混合网格-高斯渲染将紧凑的纹理表面与灵活的 3D 高斯基元相结合，为结构化场景提供了一种比纯高斯场景表示更高效的替代方案。

Existing scene-reconstruction hybrids commonly follow an additive formulation, where Gaussian residuals compensate for errors left by the mesh. However, not every Mesh-Gaussian conflict is caused by missing content: residual geometry errors, occlusion mismatch, texture-projection artifacts, and view-dependent appearance can make the same mesh content consistent in one view but inconsistent in another.

现有的场景重建混合方法通常遵循加法公式，即利用高斯残差来补偿网格留下的误差。然而，并非所有的网格-高斯冲突都是由内容缺失引起的：残余几何误差、遮挡不匹配、纹理投影伪影以及视点相关的外观，都可能导致同一网格内容在某个视角下表现一致，而在另一个视角下却表现不一致。

We therefore formulate hybrid rendering as a multi-view inconsistency problem and present Gaussolve, a subtract-and-refill framework that selectively dissolves inconsistent mesh content before residual Gaussians refill the revealed regions.

因此，我们将混合渲染表述为一个多视角不一致性问题，并提出了 Gaussolve，这是一个“减法与填充”框架，它能够在残差高斯填充被揭示区域之前，有选择地溶解不一致的网格内容。

We introduce a lightweight Neural Dissolve Field, parameterized by scalar spherical-harmonic Gaussians, to learn where and from which viewing directions mesh content should be dissolved. A continuous transmittance fusion rule progressively exposes residual Gaussians behind the dissolved mesh, preserving their reconstruction gradients and avoiding hard visibility discontinuities.

我们引入了一种轻量级的神经溶解场（Neural Dissolve Field），通过标量球谐高斯函数进行参数化，以学习在何处以及从哪些视角方向溶解网格内容。一种连续的透射率融合规则能够逐步显露溶解网格后的残差高斯，从而保留其重建梯度并避免出现硬性的可见性不连续。

Experiments on structured and semi-structured scenes demonstrate higher visual fidelity with fewer Gaussian primitives and faster rendering.

在结构化和半结构化场景上的实验表明，该方法在减少高斯基元数量的同时，实现了更高的视觉保真度和更快的渲染速度。
