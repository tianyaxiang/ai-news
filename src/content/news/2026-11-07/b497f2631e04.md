计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.07081 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 4 日] 标题：自动驾驶中重叠透视相机的广角视图合成 作者：Kaixuan Zhang, Zhipeng Xiong, Minxian Li, Mingwu Ren, Xiatian Zhu。

Abstract: Multi-perspective-camera systems are widely adopted since they provide broad visual coverage for autonomous driving, motivating the synthesis of a unified wide view from spatially separated observations. In this work, we formulate wide view synthesis (WVS), which aims to synthesize a geometrically consistent wide view at a specified virtual viewpoint from multiple perspective cameras.

摘要：多视角相机系统因能为自动驾驶提供广泛的视觉覆盖而被广泛采用，这促使人们从空间上分离的观测结果中合成统一的广角视图。在这项工作中，我们提出了广角视图合成（WVS），旨在从多个透视相机中合成指定虚拟视点下几何一致的广角视图。

Unlike conventional image stitching, WVS requires reasoning about 3D geometry and visibility to handle depth-dependent parallax and occlusions arising from spatially separated camera centers. While vanilla 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) enables high-fidelity view synthesis, its reliance on per-scene optimization limits its applicability to driving scenes.

与传统的图像拼接不同，WVS 需要对 3D 几何和可见性进行推理，以处理因相机中心空间分离而产生的深度相关视差和遮挡。虽然原始的 3D 高斯泼溅（3DGS）能够实现高保真的视图合成，但其对场景优化（per-scene optimization）的依赖限制了其在驾驶场景中的应用。

Furthermore, WVS introduces additional challenges, e.g., peripheral stretching under perspective projection and photometric inconsistencies across cameras. To address these challenges, we propose Wide-View Gaussian Splatting (WV-GS), a feed-forward Gaussian-splatting framework that synthesizes wide views without per-scene optimization.

此外，WVS 还带来了额外的挑战，例如透视投影下的边缘拉伸以及跨相机的光度不一致性。为了解决这些挑战，我们提出了广角高斯泼溅（WV-GS），这是一个无需场景优化的前馈高斯泼溅框架，用于合成广角视图。

Specifically, we introduce Piecewise Perspective Rendering (PPR) to mitigate peripheral stretching through local perspective projections sharing a common camera center, and Spatial Affine Photometric Modeling (SAPM) to account for camera-dependent photometric variations during training. We further introduce WideDrive, a synthetic benchmark providing dense ground-truth wide views and adapt a LiDAR-projected sparse-reference protocol to real-world datasets.

具体而言，我们引入了分段透视渲染（PPR），通过共享公共相机中心的局部透视投影来减轻边缘拉伸；并引入了空间仿射光度建模（SAPM），以在训练过程中处理与相机相关的光度变化。我们还推出了 WideDrive，这是一个提供密集地面实况广角视图的合成基准，并将激光雷达投影的稀疏参考协议应用于真实世界数据集。

Extensive experiments on multiple datasets demonstrate the effectiveness of WV-GS for WVS. Dataset and code will be publicly released.

在多个数据集上的大量实验证明了 WV-GS 在 WVS 任务中的有效性。数据集和代码将公开发布。
