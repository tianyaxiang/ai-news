Many people are not big fans of game publisher EA right now for good reason, but for those who remember a better time in the 1990s, there’s a little good news. Twenty-five classic games from that EA era have been lightly remastered and updated, then placed for sale on Steam and GOG, including several Wing Commander games and nearly the entire Ultima series.

由于种种原因，目前许多人对游戏发行商 EA 并不买账，但对于那些怀念 20 世纪 90 年代美好时光的玩家来说，有一个好消息。来自 EA 那个时代 25 款经典游戏经过轻度重制和更新后，已在 Steam 和 GOG 上架销售，其中包括多款《银河飞将》（Wing Commander）游戏和几乎完整的《创世纪》（Ultima）系列。

In a lengthy blog post published this week, Jim Vessella, a senior producer at EA, described the efforts that went into remastering Wing Commander I-IV and Prophecy, Privateer 2, and Road Rash. It’s a long, detailed story that’s worth reading yourself, but here’s a top-level summary. After working on the mostly widely acclaimed Command & Conquer: Remastered Collection in 2019, Vessella writes that he learned about and visited a room at EA’s Redwood Shores campus that contained a large archive of poorly labeled or sorted physical media from ages past.

在本周发布的一篇长篇博文中，EA 高级制作人 Jim Vessella 描述了重制《银河飞将》1-4 代、《银河飞将：预言》（Prophecy）、《私掠者 2》（Privateer 2）以及《暴力摩托》（Road Rash）所付出的努力。这是一个漫长且详尽的故事，值得亲自阅读，但以下是核心摘要。Vessella 写道，在 2019 年参与制作广受好评的《命令与征服：重制版合集》后，他得知并参观了 EA 红木海岸园区的一个房间，里面存放着大量来自过去、标签混乱或分类不当的物理介质档案。

The story that ensues involves building a small team of contractors, poring through the footage 17 hours a day on swing shifts to label and digitize it, and then coordinating with several key community members—particularly Matt Wells, who has made community enhancement patches for some of the Wing Commander games—to put together remasters of those games. The flagship offering of those remasters is much, much higher-quality full-motion video sequences than have ever been available before, but other enhancements include 60 frames-per-second gameplay, a plethora of newly available .INI tweaks, and full controller support.

随后的故事涉及组建一个小型的承包商团队，他们每天轮班工作 17 小时，仔细查看素材以进行标记和数字化，然后与几位关键的社区成员（特别是曾为部分《银河飞将》游戏制作社区增强补丁的 Matt Wells）协调，共同完成这些游戏的重制。这些重制版的主打卖点是比以往任何时候都更高质量的全动态视频序列，其他改进还包括 60 帧的游戏体验、大量新增的 .INI 调整选项以及完整的控制器支持。

For Privateer 2—the little-remembered sequel to Wing Commander: Privateer—the crew couldn’t find the original, high-quality remasters of the FMV scenes, and ended up getting in touch with the game’s original cinematics director, Steve Hilliker, who had the tapes in his attic. Hilliker had always wanted to take a new color-grading pass, and he did just that, while also remastering and upscaling all of the cutscenes. Similar updates were given to Wing Commander Prophecy.

对于《私掠者 2》——《银河飞将：私掠者》那部鲜为人知的续作——团队无法找到原始的高质量 FMV 场景重制素材，最终联系上了该游戏的原始电影导演 Steve Hilliker，他在阁楼里保存着这些录像带。Hilliker 一直想进行一次新的调色处理，他不仅完成了这项工作，还对所有过场动画进行了重制和分辨率提升。《银河飞将：预言》也获得了类似的更新。

The collection is on sale on GOG and Steam right now; previous owners of the old versions of the games on GOG and the EA storefront get these remasters for free. None of the games cost more than $5 each. One good story doesn’t undo a lot of bad news and questionable decisions from EA, but this shows that a few passionate people can do something remarkable for game preservation, even if some of the work was done on a volunteer basis outside normal working hours.

该合集目前正在 GOG 和 Steam 上发售；此前在 GOG 和 EA 商店购买过旧版游戏的玩家可以免费获得这些重制版。所有游戏的价格均不超过 5 美元。一个好的故事并不能抵消 EA 带来的许多负面新闻和令人质疑的决策，但这表明，即使部分工作是在正常工作时间之外以志愿者身份完成的，少数充满热情的人也能为游戏保存做出非凡的贡献。
