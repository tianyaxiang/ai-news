Title: After blocking Muse, Amazon now lets users text Alexa to help them shop
标题：在封杀 Muse 后，亚马逊现在允许用户通过短信向 Alexa 求助购物

Amazon is jumping on the currently most popular bandwagon for interacting with AI agents: texting. The company on Friday said it is launching a feature that lets customers chat with its Alexa AI agent over text messages to help them shop, much like you can with AI agents like Muse or Instinct. The move comes after Amazon blocked Meta’s Muse from querying its site in September. The company is rolling the features out just before the start of the holiday shopping season.
亚马逊正赶上目前与人工智能代理交互最流行的潮流：短信。该公司周五表示，它正在推出一项功能，允许客户通过短信与 Alexa 人工智能代理聊天以辅助购物，这与使用 Muse 或 Instinct 等人工智能代理的方式非常相似。在此举之前，亚马逊曾在 9 月封杀了 Meta 的 Muse 对其网站的查询权限。该公司正赶在假日购物季开始前推出这些功能。

The e-commerce giant said select U.S. customers will get a text asking them to enable the shopping feature. Those with access can text Alexa to shop on Amazon using natural language queries, spanning everything from searching for items, adding them to cart, and purchasing. Image Credits:Amazon
这家电子商务巨头表示，部分美国客户将收到一条短信，邀请他们启用该购物功能。获得权限的用户可以使用自然语言查询向 Alexa 发送短信，在亚马逊上进行购物，涵盖从搜索商品、加入购物车到下单购买的所有环节。图片来源：亚马逊

The text agent builds on the recent shopping-focused upgrades to Alexa, and so can manage lists, keep tabs on prices, search for items across the web, compare products, and check the status of orders. Users can also share a picture of an item or someone wearing an outfit to search for similar listings on Amazon. The company said over time, Alexa will learn users’ preferences and suggest items accordingly.
该短信代理基于近期 Alexa 针对购物功能的升级，因此可以管理列表、追踪价格、在全网搜索商品、比较产品以及查看订单状态。用户还可以分享一张商品图片或某人穿着的服装照片，以在亚马逊上搜索类似的商品。该公司表示，随着时间的推移，Alexa 将学习用户的偏好并据此推荐商品。

The company said users who don’t get invited to use this feature can sign up to a waitlist. The feature will be rolled out more broadly in the coming weeks. As the market for consumer-focused AI agents balloons, platforms like Amazon are building their own to tap this new avenue of user interaction. Last month, delivery company DoorDash said that third-party agents had so far only placed hundreds of orders in total on its platform, and that it plans to concentrate on developing its own text-based agent, launched in September, further.
该公司表示，未收到邀请的用户可以加入候补名单。该功能将在未来几周内更广泛地推出。随着面向消费者的 AI 代理市场不断膨胀，像亚马逊这样的平台正在构建自己的代理，以开拓这种新的用户交互渠道。上个月，外卖公司 DoorDash 表示，第三方代理在其平台上总共仅下了数百个订单，因此该公司计划专注于进一步开发其于 9 月推出的自有短信代理。
