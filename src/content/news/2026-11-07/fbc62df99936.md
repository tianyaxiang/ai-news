# Edit screen recordings with AI without losing the steps

Published by Valmera. Adapted from our public screen-recording guide. Prepared and independently reviewed with AI agents; the worked example is fictional. A useful software tutorial lets someone repeat the task. Its edit must preserve three things for each step: the starting state, the action and the visible result. Removing a silent pause is helpful when it removes waiting; it is harmful when it removes the only view of a setting the viewer must copy. This guide shows how to brief an AI video editor, plan cuts, review zooms and revise a tutorial without losing that chain. The worked example is fictional teaching material, not a recorded Valmera test or a performance claim.
