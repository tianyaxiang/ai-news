Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.07009 (cs) [Submitted on 4 Nov 2026] Title: TraCE: Trajectory-Aware Concepts for Explainable Video Understanding Authors: Wooil Lee, Junpyo Hong, Jongseo Lee, Tuomas Oikarinen, Tsui-Wei Weng, Jinwoo Choi.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.07009 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 4 日] 标题：TraCE：用于可解释视频理解的轨迹感知概念 作者：Wooil Lee, Junpyo Hong, Jongseo Lee, Tuomas Oikarinen, Tsui-Wei Weng, Jinwoo Choi。

Abstract: Video understanding models often rely on motion, yet their explanations can obscure which motion sources support a prediction. We identify motion-source under-specification as a limitation of existing video XAI: region-centric explanations can be source-ambiguous, while motion vocabularies restricted to single-person pose sequences are source-incomplete.

摘要：视频理解模型通常依赖于运动，但其解释往往掩盖了哪些运动源支持了预测。我们将运动源定义不足视为现有视频可解释人工智能（XAI）的一个局限性：以区域为中心的解释可能存在源歧义，而仅限于单人姿态序列的运动词汇表则存在源不完整的问题。

We propose TraCE (Trajectory-Aware Concepts for Explainable Video Understanding), a concept-based framework that discovers source-flexible motion concepts by clustering optical-flow trajectories from training videos. These concepts represent dynamic evidence from people, objects, and interactions, while object and scene concepts account for static context.

我们提出了 TraCE（用于可解释视频理解的轨迹感知概念），这是一个基于概念的框架，通过对训练视频中的光流轨迹进行聚类来发现源灵活的运动概念。这些概念代表了来自人、物体和交互的动态证据，而物体和场景概念则解释了静态背景。

The resulting typed trajectory/object/scene interface supports class prediction for action recognition and question-conditioned concept matching for video question answering (VQA). We evaluate TraCE across five random seeds on six benchmarks: NTU RGB+D Mutual, Chiral SSv2, UCF-101, KTH, TOMATO, and STAR.

由此产生的类型化轨迹/物体/场景接口支持动作识别的类别预测，以及视频问答（VQA）中基于问题的概念匹配。我们在六个基准测试上通过五个随机种子对 TraCE 进行了评估：NTU RGB+D Mutual、Chiral SSv2、UCF-101、KTH、TOMATO 和 STAR。

Relative to non-interpretable baselines, TraCE maintains competitive task performance, with an average accuracy drop of 1.0 points for action recognition and a nominal gain of 0.4 points for VQA. Motion perturbation and concept-removal tests provide complementary evidence of directional semantic alignment and decision faithfulness, while sample-level interventions illustrate targeted model debugging.

相对于不可解释的基准模型，TraCE 保持了具有竞争力的任务性能，动作识别的平均准确率下降了 1.0 个百分点，而 VQA 则有 0.4 个百分点的名义增长。运动扰动和概念移除测试为方向性语义对齐和决策忠实度提供了补充证据，而样本级干预则展示了针对性的模型调试。
