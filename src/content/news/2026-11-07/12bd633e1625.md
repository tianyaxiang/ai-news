Mark Cuban keeps getting emails about a $294 diabetes cure he has never sold.

马克·库班不断收到关于一种他从未销售过的294美元糖尿病疗法的电子邮件。

"Every single week I get emails from little old men and ladies saying, 'Will my cure for diabetes work?' or 'I just ordered your cure for diabetes for $294,'" the 68-year-old billionaire investor said on an August episode of Pivot, the podcast hosted by Kara Swisher and Scott Galloway.

“每周都有老先生和老太太给我发邮件说：‘我的糖尿病疗法有效吗？’或者‘我刚花了294美元买了你的糖尿病疗法，’”这位68岁的亿万富翁投资者在8月份的《Pivot》播客节目中说道，该节目由卡拉·斯威舍（Kara Swisher）和斯科特·加洛韦（Scott Galloway）主持。

Those ads are deepfakes built with an AI copy of his face and voice, Cuban said, and Meta (NASDAQ:META) runs them anyway. "Facebook knows the little old ladies are getting ripped off," he said. He called the platform "the worst of the worst."

库班表示，这些广告是利用他面部和声音的AI复制品制作的深度伪造内容，但Meta（纳斯达克股票代码：META）依然在投放它们。“Facebook知道这些老太太正在被坑，”他说。他称该平台是“最糟糕中的最糟糕”。

Cuban said some of the ads even include a disclaimer admitting the person on screen is AI-generated and the clip is meant as entertainment. By the time anyone gets to it, he argued, the pitch has already worked. "And to Facebook, that's good enough," he said.

库班指出，一些广告甚至包含免责声明，承认屏幕上的人是AI生成的，且视频仅供娱乐。他认为，当人们看到这些声明时，推销已经生效了。“而对Facebook来说，这就足够了，”他说。

"The point is, that defines the culture of the organization," Cuban said. A company that lets scammers go after people's grandparents, he argued, has shown what it will tolerate at every level.

“重点是，这定义了该组织的文化，”库班说。他认为，一家允许诈骗者针对人们祖父母的公司，已经表明了它在各个层面上所能容忍的底线。

Cuban does run a drug company — he co-founded Cost Plus Drugs, an online pharmacy that sells low-cost generic medications.

库班确实经营着一家制药公司——他是Cost Plus Drugs的联合创始人，这是一家销售低成本仿制药的在线药店。

On Nov. 6, 2025, Reuters reported on internal Meta documents showing the company expected roughly $16 billion, or about 10% of its 2024 revenue, to flow in from ads pushing scams and prohibited products. By Meta's own internal count, users across its apps were seeing some 15 billion "higher risk" ads a day — the ones with obvious markers of fraud. One late 2024 document put that category of value at about $7 billion a year.

2025年11月6日，路透社报道了Meta的内部文件，显示该公司预计约有160亿美元（约占其2024年收入的10%）来自推广诈骗和违禁产品的广告。根据Meta内部统计，其应用的用户每天会看到约150亿条“高风险”广告——即带有明显欺诈特征的广告。一份2024年末的文件显示，该类别的年价值约为70亿美元。

In a May 2025 presentation, Meta's own safety staff estimated the company's platforms play a part in a third of the scams that succeed in the U.S. Before Meta bans an advertiser, its automated systems must peg the account as at least 95% likely to be running a scam, the documents say. Below that threshold, the suspected scammer simply pays more per ad and stays live.

在2025年5月的一份演示文稿中，Meta的安全人员估计，该公司平台参与了美国三分之一的成功诈骗案件。文件称，在Meta封禁广告商之前，其自动化系统必须认定该账户至少有95%的可能性在进行诈骗。低于该阈值时，涉嫌诈骗者只需支付更高的单条广告费用即可继续投放。

Meta spokesman Andy Stone told Reuters the documents "present a selective view that distorts Meta's approach to fraud and scams," and called the company's 10% estimate "rough and overly-inclusive." Complaints from users about scam ads had fallen 58% over the prior 18 months, he said at the time, and the company pulled down more than 134 million scam ads in 2025. The same documents note the Securities and Exchange Commission is looking into Meta over advertising tied to financial fraud.

Meta发言人安迪·斯通（Andy Stone）告诉路透社，这些文件“呈现了一种扭曲Meta处理欺诈和诈骗方式的选择性观点”，并称公司10%的估算“粗略且过于宽泛”。他当时表示，用户对诈骗广告的投诉在过去18个月里下降了58%，公司在2025年下架了超过1.34亿条诈骗广告。同一文件指出，美国证券交易委员会（SEC）正在就与金融欺诈相关的广告对Meta进行调查。

Meanwhile, Meta's newest AI product has been causing headaches of its own. A Toronto man let the company's AI agent run his Facebook Marketplace listings on Sept. 26, and it gave a buyer his street address and said yes to a lowball offer. He later traced part of the problem to a permission setting he'd picked.

与此同时，Meta最新的AI产品也引发了自身的问题。9月26日，一名多伦多男子让该公司的AI代理管理他的Facebook Marketplace列表，结果AI向买家泄露了他的街道地址，并接受了一个极低的报价。他后来发现部分问题源于他所选择的一个权限设置。

Older Americans lose more to this kind of crime than any other age group. People 60 and older told the FBI's Internet Crime Complaint Center they lost $7.7 billion in 2025, across 201,266 complaints — 59% more money than the year before, according to the bureau's 2025 Internet Crime Report. Investment schemes did the worst damage at $3.5 billion, with tech and customer support scams taking another $1.04 billion.

美国老年人在此类犯罪中遭受的损失超过了其他任何年龄段。根据联邦调查局（FBI）的《2025年互联网犯罪报告》，60岁及以上的人群向FBI互联网犯罪投诉中心报告称，他们在2025年损失了77亿美元，涉及201,266起投诉，损失金额比前一年增加了59%。投资计划造成的损失最为严重，达35亿美元，技术和客户支持诈骗则造成了另外10.4亿美元的损失。

AI showed up in more than 3,100 of those complaints, and together those victims lost over $352 million. That's only counting the people who spoke up — plenty never file at all.

AI出现在超过3,100起投诉中，受害者总共损失了超过3.52亿美元。这仅仅是统计了那些发声的人——还有许多人从未进行过投诉。

Anytime you see a famous face advertising something pop up in your feed, assume it's fake — Cuban himself included in that. Run the product's name through a search with the word "scam" next to it. Then think about who in your family is on Facebook the most – and send them a message warning them about it.

每当你在信息流中看到名人代言广告时，请假设它是假的——包括库班本人在内。在搜索产品名称时，加上“诈骗”（scam）一词进行搜索。然后想一想你家里谁最常使用Facebook，并给他们发条消息提醒他们。

And if you suspect anything fishy, file a report the same day to the Internet Crime Complaint Center at ic3.gov. The FBI froze $32.9 million worth of funds tied to older victims' cases in 2025, the agency says, but it can only chase money it knows about.

如果你发现任何可疑之处，请当天向互联网犯罪投诉中心（ic3.gov）提交报告。FBI表示，他们在2025年冻结了价值3290万美元与老年受害者案件相关的资金，但他们只能追回他们已知晓的资金。
