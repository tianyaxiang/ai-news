Those of us missing the John Wick universe can take heart, because Chad Stahelski—who created that franchise—is coming back early next year with a new dystopian action film. It’s called Karōshi (a Japanese term that means “death overwork”), and Lionsgate released a killer trailer that puts this film at the top of my list of “must see” movies of 2027.

对于那些怀念《疾速追杀》（John Wick）宇宙的人来说，可以感到欣慰了，因为该系列的创作者查德·斯塔赫斯基（Chad Stahelski）将于明年初携一部全新的反乌托邦动作片回归。这部电影名为《过劳死》（Karōshi，日语意为“因过度工作而死亡”），狮门影业发布了一支精彩的预告片，使该片位列我2027年“必看”电影清单的首位。

Dying from working too hard is a very real phenomenon, not just in Japan and much of Asia, but also in Sweden. The physical cause is usually stress-related heart attacks or strokes, as well as suicide (karōjisatsu in Japan). Writer/director Takashi Doscher has transported that concept to a grim, futuristic corporate Manhattan, specifically a company called Ikigai Technologies.

因过度工作而死亡是一个非常现实的现象，不仅在日本和亚洲大部分地区存在，在瑞典也是如此。其生理原因通常是与压力相关的心脏病发作或中风，以及自杀（日本称为“过劳自杀”）。编剧兼导演塔卡西·多舍（Takashi Doscher）将这一概念移植到了一个阴郁、充满未来感的企业化曼哈顿，具体设定在一家名为“Ikigai Technologies”的公司。

Per the official premise: Karōshi is an action-thriller set in a near-future Manhattan, where corporate loyalty and cultural tradition have reshaped everyday life. When a mysterious outsider infiltrates a powerful company after a worker’s death, he exposes the violent system hidden beneath its polished surface.

根据官方剧情简介：《过劳死》是一部动作惊悚片，背景设定在近未来的曼哈顿，那里的企业忠诚度和文化传统重塑了日常生活。当一名神秘的局外人在一名员工死亡后潜入一家强大的公司时，他揭露了隐藏在其光鲜表面下的暴力体系。

Teo Yoo (Past Lives) stars as the aptly named Hiro, who is clearly out for vengeance against the offending corporation. The cast also includes Takehiro Hira, so memorable in the 2024 miniseries Shōgun, as well as Giancarlo Esposito, Cynthia Erivo, Bill Camp, Richard Harmon, Isabel May, and Show Kasamatsu.

刘台午（Teo Yoo，《过往人生》主演）饰演主角“Hiro”（名字寓意贴切），他显然是为了向这家违规公司复仇而来。演员阵容还包括在2024年迷你剧《幕府将军》（Shōgun）中令人印象深刻的平岳大（Takehiro Hira），以及吉安卡罗·埃斯波西托（Giancarlo Esposito）、辛西娅·艾莉佛（Cynthia Erivo）、比尔·坎普（Bill Camp）、理查德·哈蒙（Richard Harmon）、伊莎贝尔·梅（Isabel May）和笠松将（Show Kasamatsu）。

The trailer opens with Hira’s character lauding the contribution of Ikigai Technologies in helping shape “modern life for millions,” adding praise for employees’ “daily commitment and sacrifice.” Then we see one distraught worker deliberately falling backward to his death from the top of the company’s skyscraper.

预告片以平岳大饰演的角色开场，他赞扬了Ikigai Technologies在帮助塑造“数百万人的现代生活”方面的贡献，并称赞了员工们的“日常奉献与牺牲”。随后，我们看到一名心烦意乱的员工从公司摩天大楼的顶端向后坠落身亡。

Enter our protagonist, Hiro, who suspects something more sinister is going on behind the company’s embrace of the samurai path, marked by “integrity” and “honor”—and also violence. It’s not long before Hiro is battling all kinds of opponents in fine Wickian fashion, including using a paper umbrella to shield himself from the blood spurting from the neck of an opponent he’s just killed—a clever flourish. John Wick would be proud.

主角Hiro登场，他怀疑该公司所推崇的武士道——以“正直”和“荣誉”为标志，同时也伴随着暴力——背后隐藏着更险恶的事情。没过多久，Hiro便以典型的“疾速追杀”风格与各种对手展开激战，包括用一把纸伞挡住刚被他击杀的对手颈部喷出的鲜血——这是一个巧妙的动作设计。约翰·威克一定会为此感到自豪。

Karōshi hits theaters on February 5, 2027.

《过劳死》将于2027年2月5日上映。
