Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.07054 (cs) [Submitted on 4 Nov 2026] Title: Reclassification Is All You Missed: Surfacing Variant Reinterpretation Need in Online Health Communities at Population Scale Authors: Kamalakkannan Ravi, Mengshu Nie, Annapurna Poduri, Cong Liu.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.07054 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 4 日] 标题：重分类是你错过的一切：在人口规模的在线健康社区中挖掘变异重新解读的需求 作者：Kamalakkannan Ravi, Mengshu Nie, Annapurna Poduri, Cong Liu。

Abstract: Genetic variants are routinely reclassified in ClinVar after a patient is tested, sometimes from uncertain to pathogenic, yet patient recontact is inconsistent, with no clear duty and responsibility diffused among labs, providers, and patients. Many affected people discuss their results in public online health communities that clinical recontact often does not reach.

摘要：在患者接受检测后，ClinVar 会定期对遗传变异进行重新分类，有时会从“意义未明”变为“致病性”，但患者的后续联系工作并不一致，实验室、医疗提供者和患者之间缺乏明确的职责划分。许多受影响的人在公共在线健康社区讨论他们的结果，而临床后续联系往往无法触及这些渠道。

We built PRISM, an agentic large-language-model pipeline that screens these posts, checks whether the variant was later reclassified, and drafts a disclaimer-bearing outreach message, in a no-contact dry run. Across 3.9 million posts from 32 communities, agent-predicted reinterpretation-relevant discussion is rare (0.288%) but concentrated in specialist genetics communities (6.30% versus 0.10% in general medical-advice communities) and growing fivefold since 2014.

我们构建了 PRISM，这是一个代理型大语言模型流水线，用于筛选这些帖子，检查变异是否在后期被重新分类，并起草带有免责声明的联系信息，进行了一次非接触式的预演。在来自 32 个社区的 390 万条帖子中，代理预测的与重新解读相关的讨论很少（0.288%），但集中在专业遗传学社区（6.30%，而一般医疗建议社区仅为 0.10%），且自 2014 年以来增长了五倍。

PRISM surfaces 123 candidate classification differences, 5 of them from an uncertain submission to pathogenic or likely pathogenic. On a held-out set of 30 real-post drafts, a clinician judged 27 factually grounded and 15 fit to send: factual accuracy is necessary but not sufficient for clinically acceptable outreach. PRISM contributes a population-scale reinterpretation-need map with code and data to be released upon publication.

PRISM 挖掘出了 123 个候选分类差异，其中 5 个是从“意义未明”提交变为“致病性”或“可能致病性”。在一组预留的 30 份真实帖子草稿中，临床医生判定 27 份事实依据充分，15 份适合发送：事实准确性是临床可接受的联系所必需的，但仅有准确性是不够的。PRISM 提供了一份人口规模的重新解读需求图谱，相关代码和数据将在发表后发布。
