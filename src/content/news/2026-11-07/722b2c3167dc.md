While you might be pondering whether to replace a smartphone with one that has a slightly better camera or a second screen, the Federal Aviation Administration is planning a much bigger leap in technology for the country’s air traffic control system.

当您还在考虑是否要更换一部摄像头稍好或带有副屏的智能手机时，美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）正计划为其国家空中交通管制系统进行一次技术上的重大飞跃。

The FAA said yesterday that it will stop using floppy disks as part of a long-awaited upgrade that will give air traffic controllers faster access to data needed for crucial safety alerts. The new Enterprise Information Display System (E-IDS) will be installed in 435 facilities, replacing several information display systems dating to the 1980s.

FAA昨日表示，作为一项期待已久的升级计划的一部分，该机构将停止使用软盘。此次升级将使空中交通管制员能够更快地获取关键安全警报所需的数据。全新的企业信息显示系统（E-IDS）将安装在435个设施中，以取代多个可追溯至20世纪80年代的信息显示系统。

The FAA “is continuing its historic modernization of America’s air traffic control system by replacing outdated floppy disks that contain vital weather and other critical information with a streamlined, 21st-century system,” the agency said. E-IDS will be “deployed to operational control positions at more than 400 facilities across all air traffic control domains,” and “provide operationally essential information for controlling aircraft,” the FAA said.

FAA表示，该机构“正在通过用精简的21世纪系统取代包含重要天气及其他关键信息的过时软盘，继续对美国空中交通管制系统进行历史性的现代化改造。”FAA称，E-IDS将“部署到所有空中交通管制领域中400多个设施的运行控制岗位上”，并“为管制飞机提供运行所需的基本信息”。

The change has been in the works for over six years. “No more floppy disks or paper strips,” then-Acting FAA Administrator Chris Rocheleau told the House Appropriations Committee last year. The FAA said E-IDS will replace older tools that have “technical end-of-life issues” and address two of the aviation agency’s top safety concerns: distribution of weather alerts to pilots (known as PIREP for Pilot Report); and timely distribution of Notices to Airmen (NOTAM), which include information on abnormal statuses and hazards.

这项变革已经筹备了六年多。时任FAA代理局长的克里斯·罗什洛（Chris Rocheleau）去年对众议院拨款委员会表示：“不再有软盘或纸条了。”FAA表示，E-IDS将取代那些存在“技术寿命终止问题”的旧工具，并解决该航空机构最关注的两大安全问题：向飞行员分发天气警报（即飞行员报告，简称PIREP）；以及及时分发包含异常状态和危险信息的飞行员通告（NOTAM）。

“E-IDS gives air traffic controllers the information they need to make timely decisions and keep the flying public safe,” FAA Administrator Bryan Bedford said. “We’re moving away from outdated, manual processes and replacing technology that once relied on floppy disks with a modern system that allows controllers to instantly share critical flight alerts, restricted airspace schedules and pilot reports across the National Airspace System.”

FAA局长布莱恩·贝德福德（Bryan Bedford）表示：“E-IDS为空中交通管制员提供了及时决策并保障飞行公众安全所需的信息。我们正在摒弃过时的手动流程，用现代系统取代曾经依赖软盘的技术，使管制员能够在国家空域系统中即时共享关键飞行警报、限制空域时刻表和飞行员报告。”

Upgrades will take a year. It’ll take a year or so to make the upgrades at every facility. The first sites to get E-IDS will be Atlantic City International Airport, Nashville International Airport, Greater Rochester International Airport, and data processing hubs in Atlanta and Salt Lake City. The FAA said it plans to complete the upgrade at 435 sites by the end of 2028.

升级将耗时一年。在每个设施完成升级大约需要一年左右的时间。首批安装E-IDS的地点将是大西洋城国际机场、纳什维尔国际机场、大罗切斯特国际机场，以及位于亚特兰大和盐湖城的数据处理中心。FAA表示，计划在2028年底前完成全部435个地点的升级。

“Good news America: floppy disks are out, 21st century tech is in,” US Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy said. Duffy posted a video yesterday about the upgrade in which a newscaster describing the FAA’s outdated technology says, “the FAA systems are essentially running on Windows 95.”

美国交通部长肖恩·达菲（Sean Duffy）表示：“美国的好消息：软盘被淘汰了，21世纪的技术进场了。”达菲昨日发布了一段关于此次升级的视频，视频中一位新闻主播在描述FAA过时的技术时说：“FAA的系统本质上是在Windows 95上运行的。”

Like other Trump appointees, Duffy was quick to give credit to the president, although the plan was shepherded through by officials in both the Biden and Trump administrations. “The greatest nation in the world deserves a state-of-the-art air traffic control system, and under President Trump, we’re delivering just that,” Duffy said.

与其他特朗普任命的官员一样，达菲很快将功劳归于总统，尽管该计划是由拜登和特朗普两届政府的官员共同推动完成的。达菲说：“世界上最伟大的国家理应拥有最先进的空中交通管制系统，而在特朗普总统的领导下，我们正在实现这一目标。”

In August 2020, the FAA awarded a contract to Leidos to design and develop E-IDS. Leidos said in a press release at the time that E-IDS is part of the FAA’s Next Generation Air Transportation System (NextGen) modernization project, which encompasses numerous upgrades going back to 2008.

2020年8月，FAA授予Leidos公司一份合同，用于设计和开发E-IDS。Leidos当时在新闻稿中表示，E-IDS是FAA“下一代航空运输系统”（NextGen）现代化项目的一部分，该项目涵盖了自2008年以来的众多升级工程。

The FAA said E-IDS will replace the Information Display System 4 (IDS-4), Controller Equipment Information Display System (ACE-IDS), NAS Information Display System (NIDS), and En Route Information Display System (ERIDS). IDS-4, one of the systems being replaced, runs on MS-DOS and “has been in constant use since the early 1990s as a vital informational resource/tool for air traffic controllers,” according to a 2018 press release issued by Systems Atlanta, a vendor that had an FAA contract to support the system.

FAA表示，E-IDS将取代信息显示系统4（IDS-4）、管制员设备信息显示系统（ACE-IDS）、NAS信息显示系统（NIDS）以及航路信息显示系统（ERIDS）。据曾拥有FAA系统支持合同的供应商Systems Atlanta在2018年发布的新闻稿称，被取代的系统之一IDS-4运行在MS-DOS上，“自20世纪90年代初以来一直作为空中交通管制员的重要信息资源/工具被持续使用”。

As PCMag notes, IDS-4 appears to run on desktop PCs with 3.5-inch floppy disk drives.

正如《PCMag》所指出的，IDS-4似乎是在配备3.5英寸软盘驱动器的台式机上运行的。

Obsolete tech “cumbersome for controllers.” An April 2026 report by the Department of Transportation said, “Controllers have used a system that is driven by floppy disks and an antiquated operation system for critical information such as weather, NOTAMs and airport status.” With E-IDS, those controllers will have “a single platform that pulls that data into one view. Instead of searching for info, they [will] have immediate access to everything they need to make split-second decisions,” the report said.

过时的技术对管制员来说“很麻烦”。美国交通部2026年4月的一份报告称：“管制员一直使用一个由软盘和过时操作系统驱动的系统来获取天气、飞行员通告和机场状态等关键信息。”报告称，有了E-IDS，这些管制员将拥有“一个将数据整合到单一视图中的平台。他们无需再搜索信息，而是可以立即获取做出瞬间决策所需的一切。”

The out-of-date systems were criticized in a September 2026 report by the US Government Accountability Office (GAO). “Legacy display systems rely on obsolete technology (e.g., floppy disks) and interfaces from the 1980s, inefficient data organization, and manual update methods,” the report said. “The systems are cumbersome for controllers to search, retrieve, and display information. Multiple diverse display systems create inefficient activities needed to sustain these systems.”

这些过时的系统在2026年9月美国政府问责局（GAO）的一份报告中受到了批评。报告称：“遗留显示系统依赖于过时的技术（如软盘）和20世纪80年代的界面、低效的数据组织以及手动更新方法。这些系统对于管制员搜索、检索和显示信息来说非常繁琐。多种多样的显示系统导致了维持这些系统所需的低效活动。”

The GAO report said these information display systems are used in Air Route Traffic Control Centers, Terminal Radar Approach Control facilities, and control towers. The systems display textual and graphical information, “such as flight approach charts, combined with Notice to Airmen and weather information. This information is needed to support controller-to-pilot flight operations,” the GAO said.

GAO报告称，这些信息显示系统被用于航路交通管制中心、终端雷达进近管制设施和控制塔。GAO表示，这些系统显示文本和图形信息，“例如飞行进近图，并结合了飞行员通告和天气信息。这些信息对于支持管制员与飞行员之间的飞行操作是必不可少的。”
