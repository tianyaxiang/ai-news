Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.06976 (cs) [Submitted on 4 Nov 2026] Title: On the Opportunities and Challenges of Prefill-to-Autoregressive Cross-Model Transfer Authors: Zhenyi Shen, Junru Lu, Wei Liu, Jiangnan Ye, Jiazheng Li, Di Yin, Xing Sun, Tameem Adel, Yali Du, Lin Gui, Yulan He.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.06976 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 4 日] 标题：关于预填充到自回归跨模型迁移的机遇与挑战 作者：Zhenyi Shen, Junru Lu, Wei Liu, Jiangnan Ye, Jiazheng Li, Di Yin, Xing Sun, Tameem Adel, Yali Du, Lin Gui, Yulan He。

Abstract: Prefill-to-Autoregressive Cross-model Transfer (PACT), a form of latent communication between language models, combines a larger model's prompt representations with a smaller model's efficient generation, offering a promising way to balance capability and inference cost.

摘要：预填充到自回归跨模型迁移（PACT）是一种语言模型间的潜在通信形式，它将较大模型的提示表示与较小模型的高效生成相结合，为平衡能力与推理成本提供了一种有前景的方法。

However, existing systems provide limited support for independently evaluating large-small model collaboration, and improved task performance alone does not establish whether the receiver benefits from the sender's information.

然而，现有系统对独立评估大模型与小模型协作的支持有限，且仅凭任务性能的提升并不能确定接收方是否真正受益于发送方的信息。

We introduce PLatour, an open-source PACT framework that connects frozen models through lightweight, zero-initialized linear bridges trained directly with task rewards and supports hybrid-attention architectures.

我们引入了 PLatour，这是一个开源的 PACT 框架，它通过轻量级、零初始化的线性桥接连接冻结模型，这些桥接直接通过任务奖励进行训练，并支持混合注意力架构。

PLatour significantly outperforms open-source baselines on generation tasks and surpasses Mostik's reported results on shared mathematics and coding benchmarks using a substantially smaller sender.

PLatour 在生成任务上显著优于开源基准，并在共享的数学和编码基准测试中，通过使用规模小得多的发送方，超越了 Mostik 报告的结果。

However, controlled experiments show that the gains on some tasks can be reproduced by uninformative sender signals, self-communication, or receiver-only training.

然而，对照实验表明，某些任务上的增益可以通过无信息的发送方信号、自通信或仅训练接收方来复现。

We find that trainable bridges can act as parameter-efficient adaptation mechanisms without exploiting useful sender information.

我们发现，可训练的桥接可以在不利用有用发送方信息的情况下，充当参数高效的适配机制。

We therefore propose PACT-Check to quantify how much of a trained PACT system's gain genuinely comes from communication. Finally, we discuss the opportunities and challenges of PACT systems. Code is available at this https URL.

因此，我们提出了 PACT-Check，用于量化已训练的 PACT 系统中究竟有多少增益真正来自通信。最后，我们讨论了 PACT 系统的机遇与挑战。代码可在该 https 链接获取。
