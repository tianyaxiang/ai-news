Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.07069 (cs) [Submitted on 4 Nov 2026] Title: Finite-Time Convergence and Regret Guarantees for Neural Reinforcement Learning in POMDPs Authors: Jingjuan Huang, Zhiyao Zhang, Alvaro Velasquez, Nathaniel D. Bastian, Jia Liu.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.07069 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 4 日] 标题：POMDP 中神经强化学习的有限时间收敛与遗憾保证 作者：Jingjuan Huang, Zhiyao Zhang, Alvaro Velasquez, Nathaniel D. Bastian, Jia Liu。

Abstract: Reinforcement learning (RL) in Partially Observable Markov Decision Processes (POMDPs) is computationally challenging due to the curse of long-range dependence on history. Fortunately, recent studies have shown that a range of POMDPs that satisfy the filter stability assumption can be well approximated by a superstate Markov Decision Process (MDP) based on a truncated history in a finite-memory window, with a value gap that decays with the window length.

摘要：由于对历史的长期依赖性，部分可观测马尔可夫决策过程 (POMDP) 中的强化学习在计算上具有挑战性。幸运的是，最近的研究表明，一系列满足滤波器稳定性假设的 POMDP 可以通过基于有限记忆窗口内截断历史的超状态马尔可夫决策过程 (MDP) 进行良好近似，且其价值差距随窗口长度的增加而衰减。

However, existing finite-time convergence analyses on such superstate MDP surrogates are either limited to over-simplified linear function approximation or rely on an i.i.d. sampling oracle that is violated by POMDP trajectories, both of which severely limit the applicability of these theoretical results in practice. This motivates us to bridge the gap by investigating RL in POMDPs with nonlinear neural value function approximation.

然而，现有的针对此类超状态 MDP 替代方案的有限时间收敛分析，要么局限于过度简化的线性函数近似，要么依赖于被 POMDP 轨迹所违背的独立同分布 (i.i.d.) 采样预言机，这两者都严重限制了这些理论结果在实践中的适用性。这促使我们通过研究具有非线性神经价值函数近似的 POMDP 强化学习来弥补这一差距。

Our main contributions are threefold: i) We first establish a new finite-state MDP point-wise approximation error bound, which lays the foundation for our RL algorithm design and analysis in POMDPs; ii) By applying Temporal-Difference (TD) learning to POMDP-generated superstate transitions and targeting the Bellman fixed point of the superstate MDP, we prove a finite-time critic error bound with nonlinear neural value function approximation; and iii) By combining the neural critic function approximation error bound with the policy mirror descent framework, we obtain a finite-time sublinear regret against the optimal POMDP value.

我们的主要贡献有三点：i) 我们首先建立了一个新的有限状态 MDP 点对点近似误差界，这为我们在 POMDP 中的强化学习算法设计和分析奠定了基础；ii) 通过将时序差分 (TD) 学习应用于 POMDP 生成的超状态转换，并针对超状态 MDP 的贝尔曼不动点，我们证明了具有非线性神经价值函数近似的有限时间评论家误差界；iii) 通过将神经评论家函数近似误差界与策略镜像下降框架相结合，我们获得了相对于最优 POMDP 值的有限时间次线性遗憾。

To the best of our knowledge, such a finite-time sublinear regret based on neural function approximation is the first in the literature for finite-memory POMDPs. Our numerical experiments also verify our theoretical results.

据我们所知，这种基于神经函数近似的有限时间次线性遗憾在有限记忆 POMDP 的文献中尚属首次。我们的数值实验也验证了我们的理论结果。
