Tesla will end production of the Cybertruck at its Texas Gigafactory next year. It’s a uniquely toxic electric vehicle, between its divisive design and Elon Musk’s embrace of you name it. That toxicity certainly contributed to subpar sales, along with a number of recalls.

特斯拉将于明年停止在其德克萨斯州超级工厂生产 Cybertruck。无论是其充满争议的设计，还是埃隆·马斯克（Elon Musk）对各种事物的拥护，都使其成为一款极其“有毒”的电动汽车。这种负面影响，加上多次召回事件，无疑导致了其销售表现不佳。

But the end of the Cybertruck is also another sign that the era of big EVs—particularly big trucks—is waning. That’s great news for pedestrian and biker safety, but also for the battery supply chain at a time when more people need to get behind the electric wheel.

但 Cybertruck 的终结也标志着大型电动汽车——尤其是大型卡车——的时代正在衰落。这对行人和骑行者的安全来说是个好消息，同时在更多人需要转向电动驾驶的当下，这也利好电池供应链。

The Cybertruck uses a massive 123 kilowatt-hour battery that clocks in at around 1,600 pounds, or around a quarter of its weight. That’s on par with the battery that powered Ford’s F-150 Lightning, another electric truck that was canceled late last year. (The company also killed a three-row EV in 2024.) Ford’s behemoth batteries ranged from 98 kilowatt-hours up to 131 kilowatt-hours.

Cybertruck 使用了一块重达 1600 磅（约占其总重量的四分之一）的 123 千瓦时巨型电池。这与福特 F-150 Lightning 所使用的电池相当，后者是另一款在去年年底被取消的电动卡车。（该公司还在 2024 年砍掉了一款三排座电动汽车。）福特这些庞大电池的容量从 98 千瓦时到 131 千瓦时不等。

Seeing both trucks canceled within a year of each other, while automakers have announced a bevy of smaller EVs and gas-powered autos with electric motors that can extend range, indicates there may be a shift afoot. Ford is planning to launch the Fathom next year. The sub-$30,000 electric pickup is expected to be about the size of the automaker’s compact gas-powered Maverick pickup.

这两款卡车在一年内相继被取消，而与此同时，汽车制造商们却宣布了一系列小型电动汽车以及带有增程电机的燃油车，这表明市场可能正在发生转变。福特计划明年推出 Fathom。这款售价低于 3 万美元的电动皮卡预计尺寸将与福特紧凑型燃油皮卡 Maverick 相当。

Though Ford hasn’t announced the battery sizes available in various trims, it’s a safe bet they won’t be anywhere near the size of the F-150 Lightning or Cybertruck. The company is also planning to relaunch the Lightning as an extended-range model.

虽然福特尚未公布各配置车型的电池容量，但可以肯定的是，它们远不会达到 F-150 Lightning 或 Cybertruck 的水平。该公司还计划将 Lightning 作为增程车型重新推出。

Startup Slate is also launching a tiny electric pickup that can be converted to an SUV, with a bare-bones base model starting below $25,000. It also features a svelte battery and has seen a massive surge in preorders—though, to be fair, so did the Cybertruck, and we saw that turned out.

初创公司 Slate 也正在推出一款可以改装成 SUV 的小型电动皮卡，其基础款起售价低于 2.5 万美元。它配备了轻量化电池，预订量激增——不过平心而论，Cybertruck 当初也是如此，而我们都看到了结果。

Then there’s the Rivian R2, which launched this year. While the SUV isn’t as tiny or cheap as Ford and Slate’s vehicles—its battery has 88 kilowatt-hours of capacity and weighs in at around 1,200 pounds—it’s still smaller than the company’s flagship R1S. Rivian has also teased the R3, an even more-slimmed down SUV, in the coming years.

此外还有今年推出的 Rivian R2。虽然这款 SUV 不像福特和 Slate 的车型那样小巧或便宜——其电池容量为 88 千瓦时，重约 1200 磅——但它仍然比该公司的旗舰车型 R1S 更小。Rivian 还预告了未来几年将推出更精简的 SUV 车型 R3。

That’s not to say big EVs are dead. The Kia EV9, for example, is among the top 10 EVs by sales in the US, as is the R1S. There’s also a torrid resale market for the F-150 Lightning. But the rest of the top 10 new EV sales are all crossovers, sedans, or mid-sized SUVs with moderately sized batteries, and a number of newly announced EVs are trending on the smaller side or function as extended range vehicles.

这并不是说大型电动汽车已经消亡。例如，起亚 EV9 和 R1S 均位列美国电动汽车销量前十。F-150 Lightning 的二手市场也依然火爆。但其余销量前十的电动汽车均为跨界车、轿车或中型 SUV，且电池容量适中；许多新发布的电动汽车也趋向小型化，或作为增程车型存在。

Building smaller vehicles is, of course, one way to make EVs cheaper. With less weight to propel, you can rely on a smaller battery. And given that the battery pack is the most costly component of an EV, that can in turn help automakers offer more affordable vehicles at a time when the price of so many other consumer goods is skyrocketing. That includes the current crop of EVs: The average transaction price for a new model is now $55,800, according to Cox Automotive.

制造小型车辆当然是降低电动汽车成本的一种方式。由于需要驱动的重量更轻，可以使用更小的电池。鉴于电池组是电动汽车中最昂贵的部件，这反过来又能帮助汽车制造商在其他消费品价格飙升的时期提供更实惠的车型。这也包括当前的电动汽车：根据考克斯汽车公司（Cox Automotive）的数据，新车型的平均交易价格目前为 55,800 美元。

Going small—or smaller, anyway—also means making the most of a limited battery supply chain. The vast majority of Cybertruck drivers are doing the same thing with their vehicles as Hyundai Ioniq 5 drivers: getting groceries, commuting, and driving kids to soccer games. But a standard range Ioniq 5 driver is doing it with a battery about half the size.

转向小型化——或者至少是更小一点——也意味着充分利用有限的电池供应链。绝大多数 Cybertruck 车主的使用场景与现代 Ioniq 5 车主并无二致：买菜、通勤、接送孩子参加足球比赛。但标准续航版 Ioniq 5 的电池容量仅为前者的一半左右。

After President Donald Trump’s administration killed EV incentives last year, sales plummeted. The Iran War and rising gas prices have led to an uptick in interest, though sales still lag behind the global average. (Used hybrid sales have also picked up, reflecting the pain people have been feeling at the pump.) Even with a Democratic-controlled Congress, EV incentives coming back under Trump are about as likely as defense secretary Pete Hegseth putting pronouns in his email signature. But should Democrats take the White House in 2028, marking the culmination of Woke 2.0, support for would-be EV buyers becomes much more likely.

在唐纳德·特朗普政府去年取消电动汽车激励措施后，销量大幅下滑。伊朗战争和油价上涨带动了人们的兴趣回升，尽管销量仍落后于全球平均水平。（二手混合动力汽车的销量也有所增长，反映出人们在加油站感受到的压力。）即使在民主党控制国会的情况下，特朗普执政期间恢复电动汽车激励措施的可能性，就像国防部长皮特·海格塞斯（Pete Hegseth）在电子邮件签名中加入代词一样渺茫。但如果民主党在 2028 年入主白宫，标志着“觉醒 2.0”（Woke 2.0）达到顶峰，那么对潜在电动汽车买家的支持将更有可能实现。

Automakers pivoting to smaller cars could be much better-positioned to meet pent-up demand if that happens, while also lowering the risks of delivery delays.

如果这种情况发生，转向小型汽车的汽车制造商将更有能力满足积压的需求，同时降低交付延迟的风险。

Even Elon’s announcement killing the Cybertruck points to where the market is heading: He claims Tesla is ending production to make room for cranking out more Cybercabs. The battery size in those vehicles? Just 48 kilowatt-hours.

就连埃隆宣布停产 Cybertruck 也指明了市场的发展方向：他声称特斯拉停产是为了腾出空间，以生产更多的 Cybercab。这些车辆的电池容量是多少？仅为 48 千瓦时。
