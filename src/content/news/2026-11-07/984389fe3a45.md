计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.06993 (cs) [2026年11月4日提交] 标题：用于视觉-语言模型鲁棒转导式适应的批次条件语义锚点 作者：Mohammed Rahman Sherif Khan Mohammad, Ardhendu Behera, Sandip Pradhan, Swagat Kumar, Amr Ahmed。

Abstract: Transductive adaptation improves vision-language models at test time by refining predictions on an unlabeled batch, but current methods face a key trade-off. Aggressive approaches exploit batch structure but can drift when batches are sparse, have prior shift, or cover little of the label space. Conservative methods better preserve CLIP text semantics, but fixed semantic references can under-adapt even when the batch provides strong corrective evidence.

摘要：转导式适应通过在未标记的批次上优化预测来改善测试时的视觉-语言模型，但当前的方法面临一个关键的权衡。激进的方法利用批次结构，但在批次稀疏、存在先验偏移或覆盖的标签空间较小时可能会发生漂移。保守的方法能更好地保留 CLIP 文本语义，但即使在批次提供强有力的纠正证据时，固定的语义参考也可能导致适应不足。

We propose JPTA (Joint Prior and Transport Adaptation), a prior-aware framework that casts transductive VLM adaptation as batch-conditioned semantic reference estimation. Instead of treating CLIP text prototypes as fixed priors or freely replaceable, JPTA views them as semantic references that shift toward image-side batch structure only under calibrated evidence. It estimates batch priors and soft image-side prototypes to form transported semantic references whose impact is scaled by batch reliability.

我们提出了 JPTA（联合先验与传输适应），这是一个具有先验感知能力的框架，它将转导式 VLM 适应视为批次条件下的语义参考估计。JPTA 不会将 CLIP 文本原型视为固定的先验或可随意替换的对象，而是将其视为仅在经过校准的证据下才向图像侧批次结构偏移的语义参考。它通过估计批次先验和软图像侧原型来形成传输后的语义参考，其影响程度由批次可靠性进行缩放。

Across standard benchmarks, low effective-class regimes, all-class evaluation, and online streams, JPTA consistently outperforms TransCLIP and StatA, with the largest gains on sparse and prior-shifted batches. Anchor-drift, active-support, and absent-class-mass diagnostics show that JPTA fixes effective-class mismatch without unconstrained transductive drift, indicating that robust transductive VLM adaptation should recalibrate semantic references using reliable batch evidence rather than choose between unrestricted transduction and fixed prototypes.

在标准基准测试、低有效类别方案、全类别评估和在线流中，JPTA 的表现始终优于 TransCLIP 和 StatA，在稀疏和存在先验偏移的批次上增益最为显著。锚点漂移、主动支持和缺失类别质量诊断表明，JPTA 在不产生无约束转导漂移的情况下修复了有效类别不匹配问题，这表明鲁棒的转导式 VLM 适应应使用可靠的批次证据重新校准语义参考，而不是在无限制转导和固定原型之间进行选择。
