计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.07063 (cs) [2026年11月4日提交] 标题：SD-LoRA：用于少样本视觉-语言适应的训练时结构蒸馏至LoRA方法 作者：Mohammed Rahman Sherif Khan Mohammad, Ardhendu Behera, Sandip Pradhan, Swagat Kumar, Yonghuai Liu。

Abstract: Few-shot adaptation of vision-language models must balance two competing goals: learning fine-grained visual distinctions from very limited supervision and preserving the efficiency that makes frozen CLIP-like models attractive for deployment. Existing lightweight adaptation methods, including prompt tuning, LoRA, and cache-based adapters, typically supervise global image embeddings and therefore underuse token-level structure.

摘要：视觉-语言模型的少样本适应必须平衡两个相互竞争的目标：从极其有限的监督中学习细粒度的视觉区分，以及保持使冻结的类CLIP模型在部署时具有吸引力的效率。现有的轻量级适应方法，包括提示调优（prompt tuning）、LoRA和基于缓存的适配器，通常监督全局图像嵌入，因此未能充分利用标记级（token-level）结构。

Conversely, structure-aware methods exploit local evidence but often introduce additional inference-time computation. We propose SD-LoRA, a structural distillation framework that uses token-level graph reasoning only during training and transfers its effects into a lightweight LoRA-adapted CLIP model.

相反，结构感知方法利用局部证据，但往往会引入额外的推理时计算。我们提出了SD-LoRA，这是一个结构蒸馏框架，仅在训练期间使用标记级图推理，并将其效果转移到轻量级的LoRA适配CLIP模型中。

During training, a heterogeneous graph teacher models patch-patch, global-local, and visual-text relations over high-resolution ViT tokens. Rather than retaining this graph at test time, SD-LoRA transfers its structural bias into the shared CLIP-LoRA backbone through implicit distillation and Prototype Predictive Alignment, which aligns graph-conditioned representations with support-derived class prototypes.

在训练过程中，一个异构图教师模型对高分辨率ViT标记上的补丁间（patch-patch）、全局-局部以及视觉-文本关系进行建模。SD-LoRA并没有在测试时保留该图，而是通过隐式蒸馏和原型预测对齐（Prototype Predictive Alignment）将其结构偏差转移到共享的CLIP-LoRA主干中，该对齐过程将图条件表示与从支持集导出的类原型进行对齐。

At inference, the graph teacher is discarded; prediction requires only a single CLIP-LoRA forward pass and a cache-based classifier. Across 11 few-shot benchmarks, SD-LoRA improves over a strong CLIP-LoRA baseline by 2.4/1.8/3.0/2.4/2.0 percentage points in the 1/2/4/8/16-shot settings, respectively, without additional graph computation at test time.

在推理时，图教师模型被丢弃；预测仅需要单次CLIP-LoRA前向传播和基于缓存的分类器。在11个少样本基准测试中，SD-LoRA在1/2/4/8/16-shot设置下，相较于强大的CLIP-LoRA基线分别提升了2.4/1.8/3.0/2.4/2.0个百分点，且在测试时无需额外的图计算。
