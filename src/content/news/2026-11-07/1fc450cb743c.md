Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.06984 (cs) [Submitted on 4 Nov 2026] Title: Resource-Efficient Reinforcement Learning Post-Training through Elastic Expert Parallelism Authors: Jia Wei, Ziyi Qiu, Yi Xu, Xuehai Qian.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.06984 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 4 日] 标题：通过弹性专家并行实现资源高效的强化学习后训练 作者：Jia Wei, Ziyi Qiu, Yi Xu, Xuehai Qian。

Abstract: Reinforcement learning post-training improves the capabilities of large language models, but generating training responses is a major computational bottleneck. In mixture-of-experts models, workers that finish their assigned responses may remain occupied because their experts are still needed by workers processing longer responses. Consequently, approximately one-third of occupied worker time in the baseline occurs after local response generation finishes.

摘要：强化学习后训练提升了大语言模型的能力，但生成训练响应是一个主要的计算瓶颈。在混合专家模型中，完成分配响应的工作节点可能会保持占用状态，因为处理较长响应的其他节点仍需要这些节点上的专家。因此，在基准测试中，约三分之一的工作节点占用时间发生在本地响应生成完成之后。

Here we present FlexFloor, a system that enables earlier resource release during synchronous response generation while preserving the expert computation required by ongoing responses. FlexFloor jointly adapts response scheduling and the active group of workers. It uses historical response lengths and memory constraints to guide batching and prompt placement, then progressively releases workers while maintaining expert availability and correct communication.

本文介绍了 FlexFloor，这是一个在同步响应生成过程中实现更早资源释放，同时保留正在进行的响应所需专家计算的系统。FlexFloor 联合调整了响应调度和活跃工作节点组。它利用历史响应长度和内存约束来指导批处理和提示词放置，随后在保持专家可用性和正确通信的同时，逐步释放工作节点。

Compared with the verl baseline, FlexFloor generates approximately 65.40% more response tokens per unit of occupied computing time and increases response generation throughput by 52.74%, with little observed variation in rollout reward. By making reinforcement learning post-training more efficient, FlexFloor supports faster development and iterative improvement of large language models and AI agents.

与 verl 基准相比，FlexFloor 在单位占用计算时间内生成的响应 token 数量增加了约 65.40%，并将响应生成吞吐量提高了 52.74%，且观察到的 rollout 奖励变化极小。通过提高强化学习后训练的效率，FlexFloor 支持了大语言模型和 AI 智能体更快的开发与迭代改进。
