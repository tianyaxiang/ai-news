Title: After the midterms, is the AI industry in for a rough two years?

标题：中期选举后，人工智能行业是否将迎来艰难的两年？

Democrats’ historic victories in this week’s U.S. midterm elections could also transform the tech industry. The public voted on 41 data center-related ballot measures on Tuesday — in all but one, the majority voted to impose more restrictions on data center development. And even when data centers weren’t on the ballot directly, they were a major talking point, with newly-elected Democratic governors and congressmembers likely to support additional regulation.

民主党在本周美国中期选举中的历史性胜利也可能改变科技行业。周二，公众就 41 项与数据中心相关的投票提案进行了表决——除一项外，其余提案的多数票均支持对数据中心的发展施加更多限制。即使在数据中心未直接列入投票议程的情况下，它们也是一个主要的讨论话题，新当选的民主党州长和国会议员很可能会支持额外的监管。

On the latest episode of TechCrunch’s Equity podcast, Kirsten Korosec, Sean O’Kane, and Anthony Ha discuss what the election will mean for the tech industry moving forward — not just when it comes to data centers (which OpenAI is already trying to rebrand), but also increased government scrutiny of Big Tech.

在 TechCrunch 的《Equity》播客最新一期节目中，Kirsten Korosec、Sean O’Kane 和 Anthony Ha 讨论了此次选举对科技行业未来的意义——这不仅涉及数据中心（OpenAI 已经在尝试为其重塑品牌），还包括政府对大型科技公司日益加强的审查。
