A shipment of Nvidia hardware valued at $11.4 million vanished in transport between Oakland and Texas over the summer, and more than two months later, police have announced no arrests or recovery of the cargo.

今年夏天，一批价值 1140 万美元的英伟达（Nvidia）硬件在从奥克兰运往德克萨斯州的途中失踪。两个多月过去了，警方尚未宣布任何逮捕行动，也未找回这批货物。

As reported by The Mercury News, six pallets of unknown Nvidia equipment left Oakland on August 29 on a truck bound for Fort Worth, Texas. The hardware was reportedly picked up as scheduled by a truck bearing the logo of a food transportation company before vanishing off the grid.

据《水星报》报道，8 月 29 日，六托盘不明英伟达设备从奥克兰装车，原定运往德克萨斯州沃斯堡。据称，一辆印有某食品运输公司标志的卡车按计划提走了这批硬件，随后便从监控中消失。

The theft wasn’t noticed until early September, when the buyers realized that the shipment had never been received. Police attempting to trace the truck’s route found it had made several multi-hour stops after picking up the hardware before losing the truck somewhere in San Bernardino County, California.

直到 9 月初，买家发现一直未收到货物，这起盗窃案才被察觉。警方在追踪卡车路线时发现，该车在提货后曾多次停留数小时，随后在加利福尼亚州圣贝纳迪诺县附近失去了踪迹。

Oakland police confirmed that there was a grand theft report filed on September 13 and that the investigation remains ongoing. Authorities have not said what Nvidia hardware was stolen or exactly how the theft was carried out.

奥克兰警方证实，已于 9 月 13 日收到一份重大盗窃案报告，目前调查仍在进行中。当局尚未透露被盗的具体是何种英伟达硬件，也未说明盗窃案的具体实施过程。

The theft in Oakland is the latest in a wave of increasingly bold hardware heists. As Ars’ Ashley Belanger reported recently, thieves have made off with more than $100 million in similar shipments since June.

奥克兰发生的这起盗窃案是近期一系列日益猖獗的硬件抢劫案中的最新一起。正如 Ars 的 Ashley Belanger 最近报道的那样，自 6 月以来，窃贼已窃取了价值超过 1 亿美元的类似货物。

According to investigators cited by NBC News, the criminals have shown planning and sophistication by employing techniques like GPS location spoofing and even arranging collisions with police escort vehicles. Stolen components can be stripped down and sold in gray-market transactions or shipped overseas where access to US tech hardware may be restricted.

据 NBC 新闻援引的调查人员称，犯罪分子表现出了极高的策划能力和手段，他们使用了 GPS 位置欺骗等技术，甚至安排车辆与警方护送车辆相撞。被盗的组件可以被拆解并在灰色市场出售，或者运往那些对美国科技硬件获取有限制的海外地区。

Nvidia’s hardware has been a specific target. In 2025, police investigated burglaries of Nvidia hardware, valued at more than $7.5 million, around Fremont and Union City, California. As reported by The Mercury News, police believe the thefts were carried out by a group that obtained vehicles for the heist in Southern California before traveling north to the Bay Area.

英伟达的硬件一直是重点目标。2025 年，警方调查了加利福尼亚州弗里蒙特和联合市周边价值超过 750 万美元的英伟达硬件盗窃案。据《水星报》报道，警方认为这些盗窃案是由一个团伙所为，该团伙在南加州获取了作案车辆，随后北上前往湾区。

Professor Olaf Groth of UC Berkeley’s Haas School of Business told ABC News that “if a chip costs about $550,000, then they go for about $1 million or more abroad.” Those margins, he said, may be incentivizing criminals: “Because cargoes are getting bigger. The heists are getting more complex and that will point toward a professionalization of the teams that are hijacking these.”

加州大学伯克利分校哈斯商学院的 Olaf Groth 教授告诉 ABC 新闻：“如果一颗芯片的成本约为 55 万美元，那么它们在海外的售价约为 100 万美元或更高。”他说，这些利润空间可能会刺激犯罪分子：“因为货物规模越来越大。抢劫手段越来越复杂，这表明劫持这些货物的团伙正在走向专业化。”

Not every would-be heist crew gets it right, though. A theft in September, reported by Wired, saw two trailers marked with Nvidia and PlusAI logos stolen from outside of a warehouse facility in California. The trailers were recovered by police nearby, presumably after the criminals cut the locks to reveal they were each loaded with 20,000 pounds of sand. PlusAI is a company working on autonomous trucking solutions with Nvidia, and the sand was in the trailers to simulate freight loads.

不过，并非每个抢劫团伙都能得手。据《连线》杂志报道，9 月发生的一起盗窃案中，两辆印有英伟达和 PlusAI 标志的拖车在加州一家仓库外被盗。警方在附近找回了这些拖车，推测窃贼在剪断锁具后发现每辆车里装的都是 2 万磅沙子。PlusAI 是一家与英伟达合作开发自动驾驶卡车解决方案的公司，这些沙子是放在拖车里用于模拟货运负载的。
