Meta recently launched its Muse agents — a new generation of cuddly agentic helpers. Now, the company has launched Spaces, a new way to communicate and collaborate with them. As far as I can tell, Spaces are basically breakout conversations that revolve around a particular topic — although Meta refers to them as “custom experiences.” They live in the sidebar of the interface where you interact with your Muse agent.

Meta 最近推出了 Muse 智能体——新一代可爱的代理助手。现在，该公司又推出了 Spaces，这是一种与它们沟通和协作的新方式。据我所知，Spaces 本质上是围绕特定主题展开的独立对话，尽管 Meta 将其称为“自定义体验”。它们位于你与 Muse 智能体交互界面的侧边栏中。

Clicking on the Space brings up the conversation, where Muse works toward a particular goal on your behalf. For instance, if you wanted to plan a vacation, you could open a new Space focused on that particular project, and whenever you want to work on it with Muse, you would click to open that particular tab. Or, if you’re focused on collecting recipes for an upcoming family event, you’d open a new Space for that particular project. Other examples Meta gives are a holiday gift tracker, a wedding planner, or a space for DIY projects. “Describe an idea in chat, and Muse builds a space for you, whether that’s a tracker, a planner or a project board,” Meta says.

点击某个 Space 即可调出对话，Muse 会在其中代表你朝着特定目标工作。例如，如果你想计划一次度假，可以开启一个专注于该项目的 Space，每当你想要与 Muse 一起处理该项目时，只需点击打开该标签页即可。或者，如果你正忙于为即将到来的家庭活动收集食谱，也可以为该项目开启一个新的 Space。Meta 给出的其他例子还包括节日礼物追踪器、婚礼策划工具或 DIY 项目空间。Meta 表示：“在聊天中描述一个想法，Muse 就会为你构建一个空间，无论是追踪器、策划工具还是项目看板。”

Spaces can also be easily integrated with third-party apps and information, Meta says. “Connect the apps you use, and your spaces keep themselves up to date in the background, so new concert dates, price changes, or savings progress show up without you having to ask,” the company says. On Friday, Mark Zuckerberg, Meta’s founder and CEO, posted about the new feature on X: “Playing around with spaces in Muse lately — tell Muse what you need and it builds it. I’ve made a BBQ game, an event tracker, and a home control dashboard. Excited to see what you build.”

Meta 表示，Spaces 还可以轻松与第三方应用程序和信息集成。“连接你使用的应用程序，你的空间会在后台自动保持更新，因此新的演唱会日期、价格变动或储蓄进度无需你询问就会自动显示，”该公司称。周五，Meta 创始人兼首席执行官马克·扎克伯格在 X 上发布了关于这一新功能的消息：“最近一直在玩 Muse 中的 Spaces——告诉 Muse 你需要什么，它就会为你构建出来。我已经制作了一个烧烤游戏、一个活动追踪器和一个家庭控制仪表板。很期待看到你们的作品。”

Muse agents — and the cuddly avatars that enclose them — have been greeted with a relatively warm reception over the past few months. During its earnings call in October, Zuckerberg said that its agents had 10 million users in the U.S. and Canada — less than two months after the agents debuted. The company is obviously busy building out the user experience around the agents. Spaces is actually the second new Muse feature that Meta has debuted over the last week. On Thursday, the company also announced Muse Voice, which allows users to video chat with their agent and give it commands verbally.

在过去几个月里，Muse 智能体及其可爱的化身受到了相对热烈的欢迎。在 10 月份的财报电话会议上，扎克伯格表示，其智能体在美国和加拿大拥有 1000 万用户，而这距离智能体发布还不到两个月。该公司显然正忙于围绕这些智能体构建用户体验。Spaces 实际上是 Meta 在过去一周内推出的第二个 Muse 新功能。周四，该公司还宣布了 Muse Voice，允许用户与他们的智能体进行视频聊天并口头下达指令。

The company is in competition with other cute and cuddly AI agents — including OpenAI’s dots and SpaceXAI’s Grokbot, which were debuted with similarly cartoonish personas.

该公司正与其他可爱且讨喜的 AI 智能体展开竞争，包括 OpenAI 的 dots 和 SpaceXAI 的 Grokbot，它们在发布时也采用了类似的卡通化形象。
