# Deus Ex

A slight shift of focus, and the future could still turn out like Deus Ex after all…

— Deus Ex lead writer Sheldon Pacotti, speaking in 2014

只要稍微转换一下焦点，未来终究还是有可能变成《杀出重围》（Deus Ex）那样的世界……

——《杀出重围》首席编剧谢尔顿·帕科蒂（Sheldon Pacotti），2014年发言

The reviews that greeted Ion Storm’s Deus Ex upon its release in June of 2000 ranged along a continuum from cautiously to wildly positive. Computer Gaming World, the Gray Lady of the hobby, came with one of the most nuanced takes. Reviewer Thierry Nguyen spoke of “loving” the game in the abstract, but lamented “deep flaws that sometimes frustrated me, as they detracted from its ambitious design”; these included performance issues, poor artificial intelligence on the part of the enemies, and a sense of contrivance that clung to some of the environments and plot points. The excitable lads at Australia’s PC Powerplay, on the other hand, placed no such qualifiers on their declaration of love for Deus Ex. In a special feature published when the game was not yet three months old, they declared it to be quite simply The Best Game Ever: “Every action within Deus Ex’s expansive environments has far-reaching consequences, making it seem like the player is ensconced within a living, breathing world.”

2000年6月，Ion Storm工作室的《杀出重围》发布时，所获评价从谨慎赞赏到极度狂热，跨度极大。作为游戏界“老牌权威”的《电脑游戏世界》（Computer Gaming World）给出了最细致入微的评价之一。评论家蒂埃里·阮（Thierry Nguyen）表示他在抽象层面上“热爱”这款游戏，但也感叹其存在“深层的缺陷，有时让我感到沮丧，因为它们削弱了其雄心勃勃的设计”；这些缺陷包括性能问题、敌人拙劣的人工智能，以及部分环境和情节中挥之不去的刻意感。另一方面，澳大利亚《PC Powerplay》杂志那群兴奋的年轻人则毫无保留地表达了对《杀出重围》的爱。在该游戏发布不到三个月时发表的一篇专题文章中，他们直截了当地将其称为“史上最佳游戏”：“《杀出重围》广阔环境中的每一个动作都会产生深远的影响，让玩家仿佛置身于一个鲜活、真实的世界之中。”

For better or for worse, the critical consensus over the quarter-century since Deus Ex was a brand-new game has hewed more to PC Powerplay than Computer Gaming World. As the years have passed, it has been praised more and more fulsomely, not only for its gameplay but for being a full-blown work of interactive speculative fiction, full of uncanny insights into the ways the world has changed since it was released just as the Dot-com Bubble was bursting. YouTube in this Year of Our Lord 2026 is overflowing with videos that declare it to be “The Most Important Videogame Ever Made,” the game that “predicted the future,” the proud home of “some of the most insightful philosophy that you will ever witness in the gaming medium.” It’s become fodder for a certain species of painfully earnest commentator who desperately wants games to be culturally relevant true-blue art, whilst also continuing to allow him to run around and blow shit up in the manner to which he’s become accustomed. All too often, this causes such folks to make claims for Deus Ex that have only a tangential relationship with its reality, lacking the necessary grounding in the cultural circumstances that produced it. Which is no surprise really; some of these folks weren’t even born yet when Deus Ex was new on store shelves.

无论好坏，在《杀出重围》问世后的四分之一个世纪里，评论界的共识更多地倾向于《PC Powerplay》而非《电脑游戏世界》。随着岁月流逝，人们对它的赞誉愈发溢美，不仅是因为其游戏性，更因为它是一部成熟的互动式推想小说，对互联网泡沫破裂时期发布至今的世界变化有着惊人的洞察。在2026年的今天，YouTube上充斥着各种视频，宣称它是“史上最重要的电子游戏”、“预言了未来的游戏”，以及“你在游戏媒介中能见证的最深刻哲学思想”的骄傲载体。它已成为某些极其严肃的评论员的素材，这些人迫切希望游戏能成为具有文化意义的纯粹艺术，同时又想继续以他们习惯的方式四处奔跑、狂轰滥炸。这种情况往往导致这些人对《杀出重围》的评价与其现实情况仅有微弱的联系，缺乏对其诞生时文化背景的必要了解。这其实并不奇怪；其中一些人在《杀出重围》刚上架时甚至还没出生。

My favorite anecdote in this vein involves the name of the protagonist: J.C. Denton. It was theorized for years that the “J.C.” stood for “Jesus Christ,” pointing to the literal deus ex machina (“God in the machine”) of one of the game’s three possible endings, in which Denton merges with an artificial intelligence — in the Singularity sense, not that of the algorithms controlling the enemies who run through the game’s corridors shooting at you — to take over the world. At a Game Developers Conference postmortem in 2017, Deus Ex’s mastermind Warren Spector, who had always stayed mum on the subject prior to this event, brought forth a revelation that caused jaws to drop almost audibly throughout the room: the initials really did stand for “Jesus Christ.” But then came the deflating coda. Rather than having any deeper thematic or philosophical implications, the name of the protagonist came from a friend of Spector’s with the first name of Denton, who was so nice and helpful that it could become downright “irritating,” as Spector put it. “Jesus Christ, Denton!” he would say at these junctures. When production on Deus Ex began, Spector thought that the phrase had a nice ring to it. So much for philosophy.

我最喜欢的一则相关轶事是关于主角的名字：J.C. Denton。多年来，人们一直推测“J.C.”代表“耶稣基督”（Jesus Christ），指向游戏三个结局之一中字面意义上的“机械降神”（deus ex machina），即Denton与人工智能融合——是奇点意义上的融合，而非那些在走廊里向你射击的敌人所使用的算法——从而统治世界。在2017年的游戏开发者大会（GDC）回顾会上，此前对此事一直保持沉默的《杀出重围》策划人沃伦·斯佩克特（Warren Spector）揭露了一个让全场观众几乎惊掉下巴的事实：这两个首字母确实代表“耶稣基督”。但随后是一个令人泄气的结尾。这个名字并没有什么更深层的内涵或哲学意义，而是来自斯佩克特的一位名叫Denton的朋友，他为人太好、太乐于助人，以至于用斯佩克特的话说，有时会变得“令人恼火”。每当这时，他就会说：“天哪，Denton！”（Jesus Christ, Denton!）。当《杀出重围》开始制作时，斯佩克特觉得这句话听起来不错。哲学意义什么的，到此为止吧。

Like Planescape: Torment and to some extent even Alpha Centauri, Deus Ex has become as much an aspirational ideal as a concrete artifact. And that’s fine; we all need things to which to aspire, after all. Still, in this article I’m going to do my best to drill down on the less hyperbolic reality of Deus Ex. If this meaning pushing back at times against its popular reputation as a sui generis masterpiece, know that that doesn’t mean it isn’t a fine and groundbreaking game, a game which dares to attempt to do a lot of difficult things and manages to succeed at a remarkable number of them.

就像《异域镇魂曲》（Planescape: Torment）甚至在某种程度上的《半人马座阿尔法星》（Alpha Centauri）一样，《杀出重围》已经不仅仅是一个具体的产品，更成为了一种令人向往的理想。这很好；毕竟我们都需要有追求的目标。不过，在本文中，我将尽力深入挖掘《杀出重围》不那么夸张的现实。如果这意味着有时要反驳它作为“独一无二的杰作”的盛名，请知晓，这并不代表它不是一款优秀且具有开创性的游戏——它敢于尝试许多困难的事情，并成功完成了其中相当多的一部分。

The secret organization that J.C. Denton works for is the Federal Emergency Management Agency. How’s that for a deep state?

J.C. Denton效力的秘密组织是联邦紧急事务管理局（FEMA）。这算不算深层政府？

As I’ve already implied, discussions of Deus Ex tend to break down along two axes: the innovations of its gameplay and the statements it makes — or seems to make — about the very future in which we are now privileged or condemned to be living. The gameplay is both the easier subject to get a handle on and the one where the more grandiose claims require less qualification, so let’s start there.

正如我所暗示的，关于《杀出重围》的讨论往往沿着两个轴线展开：其游戏性的创新，以及它对我们现在有幸或不幸身处其中的未来所做出的（或看似做出的）陈述。游戏性是一个更容易把握的主题，也是那些宏大主张不需要太多限定条件的地方，所以我们就从这里开始吧。

Whatever else it happens to be, Deus Ex is the greatest game that Looking Glass Studios never made, steeped in that creative collective’s ethic of the “immersive sim.” As longtime readers of this site know, Looking Glass’s very first game, Ultima Underworld, appeared in 1992, right about the same time that id Software released Wolfenstein 3D, their dry run for DOOM. For the balance of the decade, Looking Glass was the yin to id’s yang. Both studios worked with first-person 3D, but, while id emphasized straightforward shooter gameplay, Looking Glass stubbornly stuck to a much more complex, multi-variant gameplay model that strove to drop the player right into a believable 3D world. It was a far more difficult path to walk, with far fewer commercial rewards to be found along the way, yet it resulted in a catalog of games that are still well remembered and widely recognized as formative to what gaming has become in the decades since. It might not be going too far astray to call Looking Glass the Velvet Underground of gaming.

无论它究竟是什么，《杀出重围》都是Looking Glass工作室从未制作过的最伟大的游戏，它沉浸在该创意团队“沉浸式模拟”（immersive sim）的理念之中。正如本站的老读者所知，Looking Glass的第一款游戏《创世纪：地下世界》（Ultima Underworld）于1992年问世，大约与id Software发布《德军总部3D》（Wolfenstein 3D）——他们为《毁灭战士》（DOOM）所做的预演——处于同一时期。在接下来的十年里，Looking Glass与id Software形成了阴阳互补的关系。两家工作室都致力于第一人称3D游戏，但id强调直接的射击游戏体验，而Looking Glass则固执地坚持一种更复杂、多变的游戏模式，力求将玩家直接置入一个可信的3D世界中。这是一条艰难得多的道路，沿途获得的商业回报也少得多，但它造就了一系列至今仍被铭记的游戏，并被广泛认为是塑造了此后几十年游戏形态的关键。将Looking Glass称为游戏界的“地下丝绒乐队”（Velvet Underground）或许并不为过。
