You have entered the pwn.college DOJO, an education platform for learners to develop and practice core cybersecurity skills in a hands-on fashion.
Entering, you receive your "white belt" , signifying the beginning of your hacker life.
From here, you will learn cybersecurity by diving deep into the core of computing, using that journey to absorb cybersecurity concepts.
This will involve melding your mind to your terminal, whispering instructions to the CPU, and strumming bits directly onto networks.
That terminal cursor blinking above?
It will be your stalwart companion through this adventure as you practice, earn your belts , and, eventually, make perfect.

你已经进入了 pwn.college 道场（DOJO），这是一个供学习者以实践方式培养和练习核心网络安全技能的教育平台。
进入后，你将获得你的“白带”，象征着你黑客生涯的开始。
从这里开始，你将通过深入计算核心来学习网络安全，并利用这段旅程吸收网络安全概念。
这需要将你的思维与终端融合，向 CPU 低语指令，并将比特直接拨动到网络上。
上方闪烁的终端光标看到了吗？
在你练习、赢得腰带并最终达到完美的过程中，它将成为你在这场冒险中忠实的伙伴。

Because of this, we would appreciate that you do not post writeups, walkthrough videos, and livestreams of challenge solutions to the internet.
Obviously, we can't enforce this, but we worked hard to make all of this public, and we would appreciate your help in keeping pwn.college a viable educational platform.

Eventually, hackers continue their journey beyond pwn.college, becoming certain in their skills, achieving brown belt status (and able to, for example, usefully contribute to the cybersecurity industry and academia), before finally graduating to hacking masters: black belts.
But this, unfortunately, must happen outside of the dojo (for now!).
Some suggestions:

最终，黑客们会继续他们在 pwn.college 之外的旅程，对自己的技能充满信心，达到棕带水平（例如，能够为网络安全行业和学术界做出有益贡献），最后毕业成为黑客大师：黑带。
但遗憾的是，这（目前）必须在道场之外进行。
一些建议：

Capture The Flags (CTFs) are a great way to practice your hacking skills in a fun and ethical way. The most popular way to find upcoming events is at https://ctftime.org. There is also a list of introductory CTFs here. If you are at ASU, feel free to check out and join ASU’s enigmatic hacking club.

夺旗赛（CTFs）是以有趣且合乎道德的方式练习黑客技能的好方法。查找即将举行的活动最流行的方式是访问 https://ctftime.org。这里还有一个入门级 CTF 列表。如果你在 ASU，欢迎查看并加入 ASU 神秘的黑客俱乐部。

Wargames are another great way to practice your hacking skills. Whereas CTFs are short (normally 48 hour) events, wargames are not time-based. You can find a list of wargames at https://github.com/zardus/wargame-nexus.

战争游戏（Wargames）是练习黑客技能的另一种好方法。CTF 通常是短期的（通常为 48 小时）活动，而战争游戏则不受时间限制。你可以在 https://github.com/zardus/wargame-nexus 找到战争游戏列表。

There are many other awesome learning platforms, such as CryptoHack and OST2 that will help teach you explore new topics!

还有许多其他很棒的学习平台，例如 CryptoHack 和 OST2，它们将帮助你探索新的主题！
