# Underdog shows off a 32MB speech model in new demo

AI companies across the world are racing to make bigger and better models, but the problem is all of them need massive amounts of processing power. On the other end, some researchers and startups are creating smaller models that can run on edge devices, like your phone or computer.

全球各地的 AI 公司都在竞相开发更大、更强的模型，但问题在于它们都需要巨大的算力。另一方面，一些研究人员和初创公司正在开发可以在手机或电脑等边缘设备上运行的小型模型。

One such startup, Underdog, has released a game built with its new voice model called Woof that’s only 32MB large. The game features a room with a retro tech theme, a typewriter, and a CRT monitor. There is a desk lamp, which makes a sound when you click its switch. The intro warns users about “intrusive” cloud providers in the town — ostensibly a jab at cloud-based models. You can interact with the typewriter and music tuner.

其中一家名为 Underdog 的初创公司发布了一款游戏，该游戏基于其名为 Woof 的新语音模型构建，大小仅为 32MB。游戏场景是一个复古科技主题的房间，配有打字机和 CRT 显示器。房间里有一盏台灯，点击开关时会发出声音。游戏开场警告用户提防镇上“侵入性”的云服务提供商——这显然是在讽刺基于云的模型。你可以与打字机和音乐调谐器进行交互。

You can type anything on the typewriter using your keyboard, and the game will speak it back. You can also rotate the tuner to generate different kinds of music. All of this works within the browser through Underdog’s speech generation model, which, the company says, can run on most devices without any internet connection. The model has 31 million parameters and is open for everyone to download.

你可以使用键盘在打字机上输入任何内容，游戏会将其朗读出来。你还可以旋转调谐器来生成不同类型的音乐。所有这些功能都在浏览器中通过 Underdog 的语音生成模型实现，该公司表示，该模型可以在大多数设备上运行，且无需任何互联网连接。该模型拥有 3100 万个参数，并已开放供所有人下载。

While such a small model likely won’t replace bigger models, it may still be enough to drive local assistant experiences or create personalized podcasts. This demo comes weeks after Underdog launched Bark, a 167MB speech-to-text model also designed to run on edge devices for transcription and dictation.

虽然这种小型模型不太可能取代大型模型，但它可能足以驱动本地助手体验或创建个性化播客。在此次演示几周前，Underdog 还推出了 Bark，这是一个 167MB 的语音转文本模型，同样旨在在边缘设备上运行，用于转录和听写。

Sigil Wen, the founder of Underdog, told TechCrunch that he started building the app because, instead of using notetakers like Granola, he wanted to build his own system with AI models that can run on users’ devices and let them own their data.

Underdog 的创始人 Sigil Wen 告诉 TechCrunch，他开始构建这款应用是因为他不想使用 Granola 之类的笔记工具，而是想构建自己的系统，利用可以在用户设备上运行的 AI 模型，让用户能够掌控自己的数据。

“I think we will be able to run most capable models of today on the devices you already own within months. We just want to train the best open models that are American-made, that are open, that are capable and allow people to have private AI,” he said, referring to his manifesto.

“我认为在几个月内，我们将能够在你现有的设备上运行当今大多数强大的模型。我们只是想训练出最好的美国制造的开源模型，它们功能强大，并能让人们拥有私有的 AI，”他在谈到自己的宣言时说道。

In addition to the two voice models mentioned above, Underdog has also released a 27-billion-parameter model that can run on laptops, along with a waitlisted app that the company is positioning as a locally hosted alternative to Muse and Instinct.

除了上述两个语音模型外，Underdog 还发布了一个可以在笔记本电脑上运行的 270 亿参数模型，以及一款处于候补名单中的应用程序，该公司将其定位为 Muse 和 Instinct 的本地托管替代方案。
