# Rocco’s Black Friday Sale: Smart Fridges Hit Their Lowest Prices of 2026

Save this story. If you've been coveting one of Rocco’s smart designer minifridges, you might be interested to know the brand's biggest sale of 2026 starts today, featuring the brand-new Rocco Essential for under $1,000 and the OG Classic in this year’s new Beige colorway, as well as white and two rare blue colors, for $1,495—as low as the Classic ever gets. Today also marks the rerelease of the Classic's Icy Blue and Royal Blue, two rarely seen colors that Rocco says are not coming back after this year.

保存此文章。如果您一直垂涎 Rocco 的智能设计师迷你冰箱，您可能会感兴趣：该品牌 2026 年最大的促销活动于今日开启。全新的 Rocco Essential 售价不到 1,000 美元，而经典的 OG Classic 冰箱（包括今年新款米色、白色以及两种罕见的蓝色）售价为 1,495 美元，这是 Classic 系列有史以来的最低价。今天也标志着 Classic 系列“冰川蓝”和“皇家蓝”的重新发布，Rocco 表示这两种罕见的颜色在今年之后将不再生产。

The Essential was just released in August and just began shipping in September; I'm currently testing it now. It's only been about a week and a half, but early impressions are positive. It has the same smart sight system as the Classic, but the biggest difference is obviously the look. Vintage-inspired fluted glass and rounded edges are out; sleek minimalism is in. The tinted dark glass door takes up the entire front, with a color-matched metal handle you can swap out for midcentury-inspired oak for an extra $95.

Essential 系列于 8 月刚刚发布，并于 9 月开始发货；我目前正在对其进行测试。虽然只用了大约一周半的时间，但初步印象是积极的。它拥有与 Classic 相同的智能视觉系统，但最大的区别显然在于外观。复古风格的长笛纹玻璃和圆角设计已成过去，取而代之的是时尚的极简主义。深色调的玻璃门占据了整个正面，配有颜色匹配的金属把手，您还可以额外支付 95 美元将其更换为中世纪风格的橡木把手。

It's only got one temperature zone as opposed to the Classic's two, but my favorite part so far has been the interior—instead of the Classic's old-fashioned wire racks, the Essential sports smooth-glide drawers lined with a rubbery material and grip strips. This makes it much more accommodating to oddly shaped drinks and prevents cans (it can hold up to 77) from rolling around every time you open your Rocco. It also gets colder than the Classic model—down to 34 degrees Fahrenheit instead of 37. My main frustration so far is that the sight system has been a little spotty and slow to recognize when drinks have been removed, but I'm having that problem with the Classic as well, so it's not specific to this model. All colors (Cream, Sage, Graphite) of the Essential are currently discounted.

与 Classic 的两个温区相比，它只有一个温区，但我目前最喜欢的部分是内部结构——Essential 没有采用 Classic 那种老式的金属丝架，而是采用了带有橡胶衬里和防滑条的平滑滑动抽屉。这使得它能更好地容纳形状各异的饮料，并防止罐装饮料（最多可容纳 77 罐）在您每次打开 Rocco 时滚动。它的制冷温度也比 Classic 型号更低，可降至华氏 34 度（Classic 为 37 度）。我目前的主要困扰是视觉系统在识别饮料被取出时偶尔会不稳定且反应缓慢，但我在 Classic 上也遇到了同样的问题，所以这并非该型号特有的问题。Essential 的所有颜色（奶油色、鼠尾草绿、石墨灰）目前均有折扣。

The handsome, vintage-inspired model is the one that started it all. Its thick steel body comes in a range of fun colors, the fluted glass refracts potentially drink-damaging light, and it comes with a color-matched metal tray so it can double as a bar cart. It's got two isolated temperature zones for red and white wine (or extra-crispy Cokes), plus five full-sized racks and one half-rack—enough for 88 cans or 27 bottles of wine. The wire racks don't pull out as smoothly as the Essential's rubber-lined drawers, but this is otherwise a top-of-the-line beverage cooler with an impressive 10-year warranty.

这款美观且具有复古灵感的型号是该品牌的开山之作。其厚实的钢制机身有多种有趣的颜色可供选择，长笛纹玻璃可以折射可能损坏饮料的光线，并且它还配有一个颜色匹配的金属托盘，因此可以兼作吧台推车。它拥有两个独立的温区，分别用于存放红葡萄酒和白葡萄酒（或超冰爽的可乐），外加五个全尺寸架子和一个半尺寸架子，足以容纳 88 罐饮料或 27 瓶葡萄酒。虽然金属丝架的拉出顺滑度不如 Essential 的橡胶衬里抽屉，但除此之外，这是一款顶级的饮料冷却器，并提供令人印象深刻的 10 年保修。

You can also control the interior lights from your phone. The sight system, however, is buggy—my Classic is currently displaying a rack of Dr. Pepper and Stella Artois bottles I had left over from a party in July (they were consumed not long after that), so I would not recommend buying one of these for the sight system alone. (Note this sale is on the Beige, White, Royal Blue, and Icy Blue Classic only.) Back in Blue: I’m partial to the Classic’s forest green, but even I've got to admit the blue hues look pretty good. They sold out almost immediately around this time last year, so if you've been wanting one and you see this, I wouldn't wait to add it to your cart.

您还可以通过手机控制内部灯光。然而，视觉系统存在故障——我的 Classic 目前显示着我 7 月份聚会后剩下的一架 Dr. Pepper 和 Stella Artois 瓶装饮料（它们在那之后不久就被喝光了），所以我建议不要仅仅为了视觉系统而购买它。（请注意，此次促销仅适用于米色、白色、皇家蓝和冰川蓝色的 Classic 型号。）回归蓝色：我个人偏爱 Classic 的森林绿，但即使是我也不得不承认，蓝色调看起来确实不错。去年这个时候它们几乎一上架就售罄了，所以如果您一直想要一台并且看到了这个促销，我建议您尽快将其加入购物车。
