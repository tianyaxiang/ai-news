title: "Uncertainty calibration in Bayesian M/EEG source imaging"

---

M/EEG source imaging requires accurate localization and reliable uncertainty estimates, yet posterior credible regions are rarely assessed for empirical calibration. We present a simulation-based framework for uncertainty quantification and calibration of empirical Bayesian inverse methods with closed-form conditional Gaussian posterior summaries.

M/EEG 源成像需要精确的定位和可靠的不确定性估计，但后验可信区域很少进行经验校准评估。我们提出了一个基于模拟的框架，用于对具有闭式条件高斯后验摘要的经验贝叶斯反演方法进行不确定性量化和校准。

We compare Bayesian minimum norm (BMN) with sLORETA depth weighting and sparse Bayesian learning (SBL) with sparse basis field expansions (S-FLEX). S-FLEX allows retained coefficients to contribute nonzero marginal posterior variance at locations where voxel-wise SBL yields degenerate variances. For BMN, we derive joint source- and noise-variance updates through evidence maximization.

我们将贝叶斯最小范数 (BMN) 与 sLORETA 深度加权进行了比较，并将稀疏贝叶斯学习 (SBL) 与稀疏基场展开 (S-FLEX) 进行了比较。S-FLEX 允许保留的系数在体素级 SBL 产生退化方差的位置贡献非零的边际后验方差。对于 BMN，我们通过证据最大化推导了联合源方差和噪声方差的更新。

We test whether adaptive noise learning yields near-calibrated uncertainties. Both methods retain residual miscalibration, motivating post-hoc isotonic recalibration of credible-region levels using empirical coverage. Simulations assess fixed- and free-orientation models across signal-to-noise ratios (SNR), source sparsity levels, and subject-specific head models, using oracle, baseline-estimated, and adaptively learned noise.

我们测试了自适应噪声学习是否能产生接近校准的不确定性。这两种方法都保留了残余的校准偏差，因此促使我们使用经验覆盖率对可信区域水平进行事后保序重校准。模拟评估了在不同信噪比 (SNR)、源稀疏度水平和特定受试者头部模型下，使用预言机、基线估计和自适应学习噪声的固定和自由方向模型。

Before recalibration, SBL+S-FLEX yields lower Wasserstein localization error, smaller posterior uncertainty, and coverage closer to nominal credibility than BMN+sLORETA. Isotonic recalibration improves both methods, with larger gains for conservative BMN+sLORETA posteriors; transfer across mismatched SNR, sparsity, and head models remains condition dependent.

在重校准之前，与 BMN+sLORETA 相比，SBL+S-FLEX 产生了更低的 Wasserstein 定位误差、更小的后验不确定性以及更接近标称可信度的覆盖率。保序重校准改善了这两种方法，对于保守的 BMN+sLORETA 后验分布，其增益更大；在不匹配的信噪比、稀疏度和头部模型之间的迁移仍然取决于具体条件。

Noise specifications yield broadly consistent reconstruction and uncertainty trends within each estimator. In real auditory MEG data, SBL+S-FLEX provides more focal localization and tighter uncertainties than BMN+sLORETA, suggesting greater statistical power for identifying bilateral auditory sources.

噪声规范在每个估计器内产生了大致一致的重建和不确定性趋势。在真实的听觉 MEG 数据中，SBL+S-FLEX 提供了比 BMN+sLORETA 更聚焦的定位和更紧凑的不确定性，这表明在识别双侧听觉源方面具有更大的统计功效。
