Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.07126 (cs) [Submitted on 4 Nov 2026] Title: Adapting LLM Judges to Observed Human Annotation Policy through Soft Prompt Tuning Authors: Tamás Janusko, Sven Kleinkop, Ricardo Bochnia, Gunnar Hempel, Steffen Tomschke, Jürgen Anke, Maik Thiele.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.07126 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 4 日] 标题：通过软提示微调使大语言模型评判者适应观察到的人类标注策略 作者：Tamás Janusko, Sven Kleinkop, Ricardo Bochnia, Gunnar Hempel, Steffen Tomschke, Jürgen Anke, Maik Thiele。

Abstract: Large Language Models (LLMs) are increasingly employed as automated judges for evaluating chatbot responses. While proprietary models can achieve high agreement with human annotators, they remain costly and difficult to deploy in practical settings. Lightweight open-source LLM judges provide an attractive alternative but typically exhibit lower agreement with human ratings.

摘要：大语言模型（LLMs）正越来越多地被用作评估聊天机器人回复的自动化评判者。虽然专有模型可以与人类标注者达成高度一致，但它们在实际应用中仍然成本高昂且难以部署。轻量级的开源大语言模型评判者提供了一种有吸引力的替代方案，但通常与人类评分的一致性较低。

In this paper, we investigate whether soft prompt tuning can align open-source LLM judges more closely with human annotation policy. We train soft prompts on the FairBotBench benchmark and evaluate the adapted judges across the dimensions of bias, toxicity, and personalization.

在本文中，我们研究了软提示微调是否能使开源大语言模型评判者更紧密地与人类标注策略保持一致。我们在 FairBotBench 基准测试上训练软提示，并从偏见、毒性和个性化维度评估了经过调整的评判者。

Our findings demonstrate that hybrid soft-prompt tuning, which combines textual prompts with learned continuous prompt embeddings, consistently improves agreement with human annotations over the corresponding untuned baselines. For Qwen 3.5 9B, the adapted judges surpass both hosted reference judges across all three evaluation dimensions.

我们的研究结果表明，结合了文本提示和学习到的连续提示嵌入的混合软提示微调，在与人类标注的一致性方面，始终优于相应的未微调基准。对于 Qwen 3.5 9B 模型，经过调整的评判者在所有三个评估维度上均超过了托管的参考评判者。
