Companies are using more AI than ever, but they’re not spending more on it. A new report from Ramp shows AI spending flatlined over late summer even as token volumes rose, a sign that price competition and savvier customers may be capping revenue for OpenAI and Anthropic.

企业对人工智能的使用比以往任何时候都多，但支出却并未增加。Ramp 的一份新报告显示，尽管代币使用量有所上升，但人工智能支出在夏末趋于平稳，这表明价格竞争和更精明的客户可能正在限制 OpenAI 和 Anthropic 的收入。

According to Ramp’s data, token spend only grew 1.5% across August and September. To compare, in July, it grew 42% from the previous month. As Anthropic gears up for its IPO, the data suggests investors might be wrong to assume AI companies will keep growing at breakneck speed.

根据 Ramp 的数据，8 月和 9 月的代币支出仅增长了 1.5%。相比之下，7 月份的支出较前一个月增长了 42%。随着 Anthropic 准备首次公开募股（IPO），这些数据表明，投资者认为人工智能公司将保持高速增长的假设可能是错误的。

“Almost the entire spend on AI is from venture-backed, high-growth tech companies,” Ramp’s lead economist Ara Kharazian told TechCrunch. “That’s fine as long as AI spend continues to rise, but if it starts to slow down because of something in the market or some other factor, they will all start to slow down simultaneously, and that could lead to other cascading effects.”

“几乎所有的人工智能支出都来自风险投资支持的高增长科技公司，”Ramp 的首席经济学家 Ara Kharazian 告诉 TechCrunch。“只要人工智能支出持续增长，这没问题；但如果由于市场或其他因素导致支出开始放缓，它们将同时放缓，这可能会引发连锁反应。”

Kharazian said he’s already seeing evidence that the top 1% of firms — the most “AI-pilled” — have stuttered slightly in their spend in August and September, coming up just below the $10,000 per-employee they were hitting at their summer peaks. A slowdown among that cohort is “sufficient to slow down revenue entirely, despite the fact that we’re seeing rising volumes,” he said.

Kharazian 表示，他已经看到证据表明，排名前 1% 的公司（即最“沉迷于人工智能”的公司）在 8 月和 9 月的支出略有停滞，略低于夏季高峰期每名员工 10,000 美元的水平。他说，尽管使用量在上升，但这一群体的放缓“足以导致整体收入增长放缓”。

Ramp data shows growth in spending among median and top 10%, but it’s not enough to make up the difference. Median firms spend $12-$15 per employee per month, and the top 10% spend $600-$1,000. “If you look at the use cases today, it’s not necessarily clear they will get up to that $10,000 per employee per month mark that the 1% is at,” Kharazian said.

Ramp 的数据显示，中位数公司和前 10% 公司的支出有所增长，但不足以弥补差距。中位数公司每月每名员工支出 12 至 15 美元，而前 10% 的公司支出为 600 至 1,000 美元。Kharazian 说：“如果你看看今天的用例，并不确定它们会达到那 1% 公司所处的每月每名员工 10,000 美元的水平。”

While there’s plenty of demand for AI coding agents in industries like software and finance, “it’s not necessarily true that a doctor’s office or a car dealership will need to spend that much on AI.” “The era of hypergrowth may be over, and now that we know there may not be as much juice to squeeze out of the top 1%,” he continued.

虽然软件和金融等行业对人工智能编码代理的需求很大，但“医生办公室或汽车经销商不一定需要在那方面投入那么多资金。”他继续说道：“超高速增长的时代可能已经结束，现在我们知道，从那 1% 的公司身上可能榨不出那么多油水了。”

October data did see a re-acceleration, with token spend up 8.9% from September 23 to October 23, but Kharazian said the headwinds on spend growth are strong. Kharazian (who has published a series of blogs called “Cracks in the AI Thesis”) said the slowdown isn’t due to a demand problem. Again, AI volumes are up. Instead he points to the competitive nature of the marketplace.

10 月份的数据确实出现了重新加速，9 月 23 日至 10 月 23 日期间代币支出增长了 8.9%，但 Kharazian 表示，支出增长面临的阻力依然很大。Kharazian（他曾发表过一系列名为《人工智能论点的裂痕》的博客）表示，放缓并非源于需求问题。再次强调，人工智能的使用量在上升。相反，他指出了市场的竞争性质。

“OpenAI and Anthropic price cuts have…just made it cheaper to use AI,” he said. In September, OpenAI cut API prices in half for GPT-6 Sol and GPT-6 Luna. Answering the price war, Anthropic has also made significant reductions to pricing for Fable 5.1 and Opus 5.5 — a decision that the company said in a recent filing slashed revenue growth by about $600 million.

“OpenAI 和 Anthropic 的降价……只是让使用人工智能变得更便宜了，”他说。9 月份，OpenAI 将 GPT-6 Sol 和 GPT-6 Luna 的 API 价格减半。为了应对价格战，Anthropic 也大幅降低了 Fable 5.1 和 Opus 5.5 的定价——该公司在最近的一份文件中表示，这一决定使其收入增长减少了约 6 亿美元。

Both companies have also been offering enterprise discounts that are intended to lock-in spend, but in reality are hitting their revenue targets. Kharazian said this dynamic means it has very much become a buyer’s market. Subsidizing use of expensive technology in order to lock in customers has always been a part of the Big Tech playbook.

两家公司也都提供了旨在锁定支出的企业折扣，但实际上却影响了它们的收入目标。Kharazian 表示，这种动态意味着市场已在很大程度上变成了买方市场。为了锁定客户而补贴昂贵技术的使用，一直是大型科技公司的策略之一。

Just recall how cheap it used to be to take an Uber or Lyft ride, and now that there are few other ride-hail players, the cost of going from A to B has risen significantly. Kharazian says those dynamics don’t work in the AI world because there’s just too much competition.

回想一下过去乘坐 Uber 或 Lyft 有多便宜，而现在由于几乎没有其他网约车竞争对手，从 A 点到 B 点的成本已经大幅上升。Kharazian 表示，这些动态在人工智能领域行不通，因为竞争实在太激烈了。

“Meta and Nvidia are also introducing enterprise offerings with pretty cheap models or open source models,” Kharazian said. He also pointed to the range of other open model companies from China and the United States, as well as a new company called Jev — a lightweight tool for choosing between a few predetermined outputs — that is increasingly among the top performers on Ramp.

“Meta 和 Nvidia 也在推出带有相当便宜的模型或开源模型的企业产品，”Kharazian 说。他还指出了来自中国和美国的其他一系列开放模型公司，以及一家名为 Jev 的新公司——这是一种用于在几个预定输出之间进行选择的轻量级工具——它在 Ramp 上的表现越来越突出。

“For a lot of AI use cases, you just need a simple Yes/No,” Kharazian said. “So you take your already cheap models, you shift them over to Jev, and now your already cheap stuff gets even cheaper.” The ability to complete workloads at a much cheaper cost is another factor driving the spending slowdown.

“对于许多人工智能用例，你只需要一个简单的‘是/否’，”Kharazian 说。“所以你使用已经很便宜的模型，将它们转移到 Jev，现在你原本便宜的东西变得更便宜了。”能够以更低的成本完成工作负载是导致支出放缓的另一个因素。

That’s partly due to the discounts from AI companies, but also because companies are just getting better at being thrifty. “As you get really advanced in using AI, you get better at figuring out how to control it,” Kharazian said. “You’re more likely to use defaults that prevent usage of very expensive models for long-running tasks. You’re more likely to impose some budgets on employees. You’re more likely to offload some of the spend from standard and light models to even cheaper light models via routers.”

这部分归功于人工智能公司的折扣，但也因为公司变得越来越节俭。“当你在使用人工智能方面变得非常先进时，你就会更擅长控制它，”Kharazian 说。“你更有可能使用默认设置，防止在长时间运行的任务中使用非常昂贵的模型。你更有可能对员工设定一些预算。你更有可能通过路由器将部分支出从标准和轻量级模型转移到更便宜的轻量级模型上。”

Notion co-founder Akshay Kothari recently posted internal data that matched Ramp’s insights. As he succinctly put it: “tokens go brrr. costs go ahhh.”

Notion 联合创始人 Akshay Kothari 最近发布了与 Ramp 的见解相符的内部数据。正如他简洁地总结的那样：“代币使用量激增，成本却让人惊呼。”

“A lot of the assumptions that we had about the trajectory of AI spend are starting to break down,” Kharazian said. “You can use more AI, but use it for less. That’s great for the customers…but it’s not necessarily great for the model companies. And it does introduce new questions around their revenue trajectories.”

“我们对人工智能支出轨迹的许多假设开始破灭，”Kharazian 说。“你可以使用更多的人工智能，但花费更少。这对客户来说很好……但对模型公司来说不一定。这确实引发了关于它们收入轨迹的新问题。”

Bloomberg reported Friday that Anthropic’s potential valuation sank from the $2 trillion estimated in early October to just $1.2 to $1.3 trillion. The company is also reportedly expected to receive a high “junk” rating from credit agencies. OpenAI also lowered its proposed valuation to $1.25 trillion from $1.4 trillion and slashed its latest fundraising goals by about $10 billion.

彭博社周五报道称，Anthropic 的潜在估值从 10 月初估计的 2 万亿美元跌至仅 1.2 万亿至 1.3 万亿美元。据报道，该公司预计还将从信贷机构获得较高的“垃圾级”评级。OpenAI 也将其拟议估值从 1.4 万亿美元下调至 1.25 万亿美元，并将其最新的融资目标削减了约 100 亿美元。

Much of the thesis for hypergrowth spending is that AI will undoubtedly introduce new use cases that will accelerate things.
