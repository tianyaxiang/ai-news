Several tanker ships linked to Iran have been loitering over subsea Internet cables in the Gulf of Oman near the Strait of Hormuz—a possible indication that the Iranian government is preparing to attack such undersea infrastructure in response to any renewed US and Israeli strikes.

几艘与伊朗有关的油轮一直在霍尔木兹海峡附近的阿曼湾海底互联网电缆上方徘徊，这可能表明伊朗政府正准备攻击此类海底基础设施，以回应美国和以色列可能发动的任何新一轮打击。

The movements of three sanctioned tankers and an Iranian fishing boat have been tracked near major undersea cables by the maritime intelligence firm Windward in October, according to the Financial Times. Six active cables currently cross through the contested Strait of Hormuz, linking the Middle East to Europe, Africa and Asia, with two more cables having segments under construction in the region.

据《金融时报》报道，海事情报公司 Windward 在 10 月份追踪到三艘受制裁的油轮和一艘伊朗渔船在主要海底电缆附近活动。目前有六条活跃电缆穿过争议不断的霍尔木兹海峡，将中东与欧洲、非洲和亚洲连接起来，另有两条电缆的部分路段正在该地区建设中。

Four countries, including Iraq, Kuwait, Bahrain and Qatar, depend entirely upon such subsea telecommunications connections running through the Strait of Hormuz chokepoint to link with the outside world. But other countries also depend heavily on the region’s undersea cable network—one third of India’s westbound Internet traffic goes through the Persian Gulf and Strait of Hormuz.

包括伊拉克、科威特、巴林和卡塔尔在内的四个国家完全依赖穿过霍尔木兹海峡这一咽喉要道的海底电信连接与外界联系。但其他国家也严重依赖该地区的海底电缆网络——印度三分之一的西向互联网流量都要经过波斯湾和霍尔木兹海峡。

One cable under construction, the 2Africa cable, is owned by a tech consortium that includes the US company Meta. That was one of the major cable corridors visited by the drifting Iran-linked ships. Windward used both ship-tracking data and satellite images to track the vessels as they slowed down and looped around near multiple cables, with some ships then “going dark” by switching off their automatic identification system (AIS) transponders that normally broadcast their location and other information.

正在建设中的 2Africa 电缆由包括美国 Meta 公司在内的科技财团拥有。这也是那些漂流的伊朗相关船只所经过的主要电缆走廊之一。Windward 利用船舶跟踪数据和卫星图像追踪到这些船只在多条电缆附近减速并绕行，其中一些船只随后通过关闭通常用于广播位置和其他信息的自动识别系统 (AIS) 应答器而“隐身”。

Such movement and behavioral patterns suggest the Iran-linked ships may be “mapping the cable routes, or placing charges or mines on them while dark,” Windward told the Financial Times. Windward highlighted the examples of two sanctioned tankers linked to Iran that spent days drifting “directly over regional cable corridors… both with no commercial reason to stop” in a blog post on October 26. Both ships carried no commercial cargo.

Windward 对《金融时报》表示，这种移动和行为模式表明，这些与伊朗有关的船只可能正在“测绘电缆路线，或者在隐身状态下在电缆上放置炸药或水雷”。Windward 在 10 月 26 日的一篇博客文章中重点提到了两艘与伊朗有关的受制裁油轮，它们在“区域电缆走廊上方直接漂流了数日……且均无任何商业理由停靠”。这两艘船都没有运载商业货物。

Since the US and Israel first launched a major war with Iran in February 2026, Iran’s response beyond targeting US military bases, ships and aircraft has included attacking commercial shipping in the Strait of Hormuz and hitting oil refineries and other energy infrastructure along with airports in various Gulf countries. Iran has also made a point of threatening or attacking US Big Tech infrastructure in the Middle East, including launching multiple drone strikes on Amazon data centers.

自 2026 年 2 月美国和以色列首次对伊朗发动大规模战争以来，伊朗的反应除了针对美国军事基地、舰船和飞机外，还包括攻击霍尔木兹海峡的商业航运，以及打击海湾各国的炼油厂、其他能源基础设施和机场。伊朗还特别威胁或攻击了美国在中东的大型科技基础设施，包括对亚马逊数据中心发动多次无人机袭击。

In May 2026, a spokesperson for Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps claimed Iran would “impose fees on Internet cables” belonging to US tech companies and other owners. But more recently, multiple Iranian officials and commanders of Iran’s Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps (IRGC) have warned that they could directly attack the undersea cables running through the Strait of Hormuz. This came after President Donald Trump repeatedly threatened to renew major US military strikes on Iran and warned he could “annihilate” the country during a speech at the United Nations in New York on September 22.

2026 年 5 月，伊朗伊斯兰革命卫队的一位发言人声称，伊朗将对属于美国科技公司和其他所有者的“互联网电缆征收费用”。但最近，多位伊朗官员和伊朗伊斯兰革命卫队 (IRGC) 指挥官警告称，他们可能会直接攻击穿过霍尔木兹海峡的海底电缆。此前，唐纳德·特朗普总统在 9 月 22 日于纽约联合国发表讲话时，多次威胁要恢复对伊朗的大规模军事打击，并警告称他可以“消灭”该国。

“Seven major cables run through the Strait of Hormuz, supporting global Internet operations and commercial data transmission,” one IRGC spokesperson said on October 20. “We remind the enemy of this.” The latest phase of the war has involved more limited US military strikes against Iranian targets, even as Trump has alternated between threats and touting the possibility of a diplomatic breakthrough.

10 月 20 日，一位革命卫队发言人表示：“有七条主要电缆穿过霍尔木兹海峡，支持着全球互联网运营和商业数据传输。我们提醒敌人注意这一点。”战争的最新阶段涉及美国对伊朗目标进行更有限的军事打击，尽管特朗普在威胁和宣扬外交突破的可能性之间反复摇摆。

Meanwhile, Iran has increased drone strikes and other attacks on commercial ships to exert its own pressure on the United States, with October marking the highest number of ship attacks since the war began. Much damage has already been done. The limited fleet of cable-laying ships used for installation and repair has not been able to venture safely into the region since the war began, which forced the Meta-backed 2Africa cable to pause its planned expansion.

与此同时，伊朗增加了对商船的无人机袭击和其他攻击，以向美国施加压力，10 月份的船舶遇袭数量创下了战争开始以来的最高纪录。许多损害已经造成。自战争开始以来，用于安装和维修的有限的电缆铺设船队一直无法安全进入该地区，这迫使 Meta 支持的 2Africa 电缆暂停了其计划的扩张。

The war has also compelled US tech companies and Gulf countries to accelerate efforts to develop alternative cable routes bypassing the Strait of Hormuz. The Middle East is not the only part of the world where threats to the Internet’s undersea backbone have intensified. Subsea infrastructure is increasingly at risk from sabotage and gray-zone warfare from the Baltic Sea to the Pacific Ocean.

战争还迫使美国科技公司和海湾国家加快努力，开发绕过霍尔木兹海峡的替代电缆路线。中东并不是世界上唯一一个互联网海底骨干网威胁加剧的地区。从波罗的海到太平洋，海底基础设施正日益面临破坏和灰色地带战争的风险。
