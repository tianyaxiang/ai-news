简讯发布于：太平洋标准时间2026年11月6日上午8:50。图片来源：Tomasz Śmigla / Getty Images。作者：Tim De Chant。五角大楼向电池初创公司6K提供2.5亿美元贷款以建设美国工厂。

Batteries are everywhere, even on the battlefield, which helps explain the Pentagon’s recent interest in building more domestic manufacturing capacity. The 12-year-old, North Andover, Massachusetts-based startup 6K announced Thursday that it won conditional approval for a $250 million loan from the Department of Defense to build a factory in Tennessee.

电池无处不在，甚至在战场上也是如此，这有助于解释五角大楼近期对建立更多国内制造能力的兴趣。这家总部位于马萨诸塞州北安多弗、成立12年的初创公司6K周四宣布，已获得国防部2.5亿美元贷款的附带条件批准，用于在田纳西州建设一家工厂。

The plant will use a unique microwave-powered process to produce battery materials that don’t rely on Chinese suppliers. The company previously received a $50 million grant from the Department of Energy under the Bipartisan Infrastructure Law, though that 2023 grant was canceled by the Trump administration in a chaotic sweep of Biden-era awards, forcing 6K to reapply.

该工厂将采用独特的微波驱动工艺来生产不依赖中国供应商的电池材料。该公司此前曾根据《两党基础设施法》获得能源部5000万美元的拨款，但该笔2023年的拨款在特朗普政府对拜登时期奖项的混乱清理中被取消，迫使6K重新申请。

The factory was originally slated to open in 2024, but the grant cancelation and uncertainties in the EV market forced the company to rejigger its plans. 6K’s manufacturing process uses microwaves to heat metals into a plasma and then deposit them onto a surface, reducing the number of steps required to make cathode material, a key battery component.

该工厂原定于2024年开业，但拨款的取消和电动汽车市场的不确定性迫使该公司调整了计划。6K的制造工艺利用微波将金属加热成等离子体，然后将其沉积在表面上，从而减少了制造关键电池组件——正极材料所需的步骤。

The company says the process can work on a number of different battery types, including lithium iron phosphate (LFP).

该公司表示，该工艺适用于多种不同类型的电池，包括磷酸铁锂（LFP）电池。
