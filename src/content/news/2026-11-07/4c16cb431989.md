Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.06962 (cs) [Submitted on 4 Nov 2026] Title: Formalizing inverse problems in the age of AI: possibilities, pitfalls, and an invitation Authors: Christian Clason, Tapio Helin, Medet Nursultanov, Lauri Oksanen.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.06962 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 4 日] 标题：人工智能时代的反问题形式化：可能性、陷阱与邀请 作者：Christian Clason, Tapio Helin, Medet Nursultanov, Lauri Oksanen。

Abstract: Proof assistants such as Lean check mathematical proofs down to the axioms, and AI agents have recently made writing such proofs cheap. We describe AutoIP, a Lean library in which classical linear regularization theory, together with the functional analysis it rests on, has been autoformalized by AI agents.

摘要：诸如 Lean 之类的证明辅助工具可以将数学证明核查至公理层面，而人工智能代理最近使得编写此类证明变得廉价。我们介绍了 AutoIP，这是一个 Lean 库，其中经典的线性正则化理论及其所依赖的泛函分析已由人工智能代理自动形式化。

A machine-checked proof certifies only the formal statement it proves, so the remaining human task is to confirm that each formal statement says what was intended. We organize the library around this task: a reviewer compares the formal statements with their natural-language counterparts and never needs to read a proof.

机器核查的证明仅能证明其所验证的形式化陈述，因此人类剩余的任务是确认每个形式化陈述是否表达了预期的含义。我们围绕这一任务组织了该库：审阅者将形式化陈述与其自然语言对应部分进行比较，而无需阅读证明过程。

Our experience is that the risks lie in the statements rather than in the proofs, and that simple automated tests on the statements can expose problems even in the established literature. We illustrate this with a textbook theorem whose hypothesis is more restrictive than asserted. We invite the inverse problems community to extend and build on the library.

我们的经验表明，风险在于陈述本身而非证明过程，并且对陈述进行简单的自动化测试甚至可以揭示现有文献中的问题。我们通过一个教科书定理说明了这一点，该定理的假设条件比其声称的更为严格。我们邀请反问题研究社区扩展并基于该库进行构建。
