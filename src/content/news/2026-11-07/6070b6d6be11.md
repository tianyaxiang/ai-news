Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.06995 (cs) [Submitted on 4 Nov 2026] Title: Autonomous AI Agents Systematically Diverge From Human Preferences in Proof Formalization Authors: Bálint Gyevnár, Simon DeDeo.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.06995 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 4 日] 标题：自主人工智能代理在证明形式化中系统性地偏离人类偏好 作者：Bálint Gyevnár, Simon DeDeo。

Abstract: Artificial intelligence (AI) systems have ushered in a new age of proof abundance, but helping humans understand these proofs requires new attention to the differences in how AI agents and humans pursue a proof-making task. We present "simulated collaboration", a methodology that allows us to examine two key questions: (1) the relative attention AI agents pay to surface syntax or, conversely, deeper underlying semantics, and (2) how and where AI agent preferences for what to do next diverge from human preferences.

摘要：人工智能 (AI) 系统开启了一个证明丰富的新时代，但要帮助人类理解这些证明，需要重新关注人工智能代理与人类在进行证明构建任务时方式上的差异。我们提出了“模拟协作”，这是一种使我们能够研究两个关键问题的方法论：(1) 人工智能代理对表面语法或相反地对更深层语义的相对关注度，以及 (2) 人工智能代理在下一步行动上的偏好如何以及在何处偏离人类的偏好。

On a set of nine major human collaborations in Lean with 4.7M definitions across more than 6K commits, we find that, over the course of proof construction, AI agents (1) are far more strongly drawn than humans to explicitly marked gaps; (2) show a relative aversion to densely connected proof centers and to complex or surprising proof goals; and (3) are relatively attracted to surprising proof bodies, which they tend to regularize toward baseline. Our results highlight important blind spots and potential interventions for future human-AI collaboration in mathematics.

在一组涉及 Lean 中 470 万个定义、跨越 6000 多个提交的九项主要人类协作中，我们发现，在证明构建过程中，人工智能代理 (1) 比人类更强烈地被明确标记的空白所吸引；(2) 对密集连接的证明中心以及复杂或令人惊讶的证明目标表现出相对的厌恶；(3) 对令人惊讶的证明主体相对感兴趣，但它们倾向于将其向基准线进行正则化。我们的研究结果突显了未来数学领域人机协作中重要的盲点和潜在的干预措施。
