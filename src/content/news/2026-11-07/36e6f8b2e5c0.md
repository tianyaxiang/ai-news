计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.07048 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 4 日] 标题：调整适应过程：人工智能中的达尔文进化、拉马克进化和智能体导向进化 作者：Andrew W. Lo, Chaoyi Zhao。

Abstract: Evolution has shaped intelligence for billions of years. Artificial intelligence (AI) now raises a striking possibility: intelligence may begin to direct its own evolution. As AI agents become capable of modifying and evaluating their own behavior, how quickly could they improve, and how can that improvement remain safe?

摘要：进化塑造了数十亿年的智能。人工智能（AI）现在提出了一个引人注目的可能性：智能可能开始引导自身的进化。随着人工智能体能够修改和评估自身的行为，它们能以多快的速度改进，以及这种改进如何保持安全？

We develop a theoretical framework comparing Darwinian, Lamarckian, and agent-directed evolution following environmental change. Darwinian evolution relies on natural selection, Lamarckian evolution adds inherited learning, and agent-directed evolution allows agents to generate and evaluate behavioral variants and choose how extensively to search.

我们开发了一个理论框架，比较了环境变化后的达尔文进化、拉马克进化和智能体导向进化。达尔文进化依赖于自然选择，拉马克进化增加了遗传学习，而智能体导向进化则允许智能体生成并评估行为变体，并选择搜索的广度。

When learning is sufficiently effective, these mechanisms exhibit progressively faster adaptation. Generating more variants accelerates adaptation, while optimal search effort depends on learning ability, problem difficulty, and experimentation costs.

当学习足够有效时，这些机制表现出越来越快的适应速度。生成更多的变体可以加速适应，而最优的搜索努力程度则取决于学习能力、问题难度和实验成本。

However, an agent's ability to direct its improvement does not ensure that improvements are evaluated against the intended objective. When the evaluation target is misspecified, additional search accelerates convergence toward the wrong behavior, even as measured performance improves.

然而，智能体引导自身改进的能力并不能确保改进是根据预期目标进行评估的。当评估目标设定错误时，额外的搜索会加速向错误行为的收敛，即使测得的性能有所提高。

In recursive self-improvement, such errors may persist across generations, posing challenges for AI safety. Independent evaluation and oversight must keep pace with autonomous AI development.

在递归自我改进中，此类错误可能会代代相传，从而对人工智能安全构成挑战。独立的评估和监督必须与自主人工智能的发展保持同步。

The central challenge is not simply to accelerate the evolution of intelligence, but to preserve humanity's ability to guide its direction, limit its speed, and intervene when necessary.

核心挑战不仅仅是加速智能的进化，而是要保持人类引导其方向、限制其速度并在必要时进行干预的能力。
