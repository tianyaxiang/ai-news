Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.06989 (cs) [Submitted on 4 Nov 2026] Title: The Two-Ledger Model: Transaction-Grain Cost Accounting for Multi-Agent AI Systems Across Clouds, Models, and Systems of Record Authors: Vivek Acharya.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.06989 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 4 日] 标题：双账本模型：跨云、模型和记录系统的多智能体 AI 系统交易级成本核算 作者：Vivek Acharya。

Abstract: Enterprises run business transactions through multi-agent AI systems spanning clouds, large language models (LLMs), orchestration harnesses, and systems of record. Finance receives AI cost only at settlement grain, aggregated by SKU and charge period. The business question (what did this order, claim, or case cost in AI?) cannot be answered from billing data, because providers never see enterprise transaction boundaries.

摘要：企业通过跨云、大语言模型 (LLM)、编排工具和记录系统的多智能体 AI 系统运行业务交易。财务部门仅在结算层面接收 AI 成本，并按 SKU 和计费周期进行汇总。由于服务提供商无法获知企业的交易边界，因此无法从账单数据中回答业务问题（即该订单、索赔或案例的 AI 成本是多少？）。

The FinOps Open Cost and Usage Specification (FOCUS) gives billing data one schema across providers but remains settlement-grain even as it adds AI token and requester attribution. We formalize this grain mismatch, derive seven requirements, and propose the Two-Ledger Model: a settlement ledger of FOCUS-conformant billing data joined to an attribution ledger of invocation-grain usage records built on OpenTelemetry GenAI telemetry.

FinOps 开放成本与使用规范 (FOCUS) 为不同提供商的账单数据提供了统一的模式，但即使增加了 AI Token 和请求者归因，它仍然停留在结算层面。我们对这种粒度不匹配进行了形式化定义，推导出了七项要求，并提出了“双账本模型”：一个由符合 FOCUS 标准的账单数据组成的结算账本，与一个基于 OpenTelemetry GenAI 遥测技术构建的调用级使用记录归因账本相结合。

A layered correlation spine (trace context, A2A/MCP metadata, business-key correlation, probabilistic matching) assigns each record to its business transaction with recorded method and confidence; a versioned rate card prices usage in near real time; period reconciliation ties both ledgers to the invoice, and the unexplained residual identifies shadow AI.

分层关联主干（追踪上下文、A2A/MCP 元数据、业务键关联、概率匹配）将每条记录分配给其对应的业务交易，并记录了匹配方法和置信度；版本化费率表可近乎实时地对使用量进行定价；周期对账将两个账本与发票关联起来，而无法解释的残差则用于识别影子 AI。

Costs are reported per order, claim, or outcome with agentic cost metrics. A simulation with known ground truth shows the spine sustaining cost coverage above 99% as trace propagation falls to zero, the reconciliation residual recovering planted unmetered spend within 0.24 percentage points while flagging provider billing errors, and mid-flight gating cutting p99 transaction cost by up to 61% at a 2-4% false-pause rate. We propose the attribution ledger as an invocation-grain FOCUS profile for standardization.

成本按订单、索赔或结果进行报告，并附带智能体成本指标。一项基于已知事实的模拟显示，即使在追踪传播降至零的情况下，该主干仍能保持 99% 以上的成本覆盖率；对账残差在 0.24 个百分点内恢复了预设的未计量支出，同时标记出了提供商的计费错误；飞行中门控（mid-flight gating）在 2-4% 的错误暂停率下，将 p99 交易成本降低了高达 61%。我们建议将归因账本作为一种调用级的 FOCUS 配置文件进行标准化。
