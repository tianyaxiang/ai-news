Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.06953 (cs) [Submitted on 3 Nov 2026] Title: SteelAgent: LLM-Orchestrated, Physics-Informed Steel Property Prediction with Audited Generalization and Calibrated Uncertainty Authors: Aleksandr Volkov, Roman Sultimov, Mikhail Kuzin, Yury Maximov.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.06953 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 3 日] 标题：SteelAgent：大语言模型编排、物理信息驱动的钢材性能预测，具备审计泛化能力与校准不确定性。作者：Aleksandr Volkov, Roman Sultimov, Mikhail Kuzin, Yury Maximov。

Abstract: Steel property models can perform well on random test splits even when they extrapolate poorly to unfamiliar alloys. SteelAgent couples metallurgical descriptors and machine-learning models with a language-model interface for property prediction, uncertainty reporting and inverse design.

摘要：钢材性能模型在随机测试集划分上表现良好，但往往难以泛化至未知的合金材料。SteelAgent 将冶金描述符和机器学习模型与大语言模型接口相结合，用于性能预测、不确定性报告及逆向设计。

We examine how the choice of held-out unit changes the conclusions drawn from SteelBench tensile-strength evaluations. For gradient boosting, pooled out-of-fold $R^2$ fell from 0.859 on a random split to 0.113 when steel families were held out.

我们研究了留出单元的选择如何改变从 SteelBench 抗拉强度评估中得出的结论。对于梯度提升模型，当留出钢材系列时，汇总的折外 $R^2$ 从随机划分下的 0.859 下降至 0.113。

Empirical coverage of nominal 90% prediction intervals ranged from 91.0% under random splitting to 37.6% on held-out sources. The effect of physics-derived features depended on the evaluation. They increased gradient-boosting $R^2$ by 0.2534 in a 17-family holdout analysis and decreased it by 0.358 in a separate four-grade laboratory analysis.

标称 90% 预测区间的经验覆盖率在随机划分下为 91.0%，而在留出源上则降至 37.6%。物理衍生特征的效果取决于评估方式。在 17 个系列的留出分析中，它们使梯度提升模型的 $R^2$ 提高了 0.2534，而在另一项四等级实验室分析中，该值则下降了 0.358。

We report group-level errors and a synthetic regression experiment illustrating how calibration can deteriorate on held-out groups. These results motivate reporting the evaluation population alongside a property estimate or compliance score.

我们报告了组级误差，并通过一项合成回归实验说明了校准在留出组上可能如何恶化。这些结果表明，在提供性能估计或合规性评分时，有必要同时报告评估群体。
