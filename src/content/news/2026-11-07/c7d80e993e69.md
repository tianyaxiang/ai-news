There are optimistic plans for spaceflight, and then there are optimistic plans. AST SpaceMobile is a Texas-based, publicly traded company building very large satellites to provide direct-to-cell communications on Earth. But it has been having trouble finding rides to space. So on Friday, AST announced that it is developing a medium-lift rocket, named Blacklift, which will have the capacity to lift 20 metric tons to low-Earth orbit. Oh, and the company said it would start launching commercially in late 2028.

航天飞行计划有乐观的，也有“极其”乐观的。AST SpaceMobile 是一家总部位于德克萨斯州的上市公司，正在制造超大型卫星，旨在地球上提供直接连接手机的通信服务。但该公司一直难以找到进入太空的“顺风车”。因此，AST 在周五宣布，他们正在开发一种名为“Blacklift”的中型运载火箭，其近地轨道运载能力将达到 20 公吨。哦，顺便提一下，该公司称其将于 2028 年底开始商业发射。

“The Blacklift rocket is the springboard to significantly expand our total addressable market, providing AST SpaceMobile with regular unconstrained access to orbit and providing for most favorable owner’s economics in the industry,” said Abel Avellan, founder, chairman, and CEO of AST SpaceMobile, in a news release. To anyone with even a basic understanding of rockets, that timeline is wildly, fantastically, and stupidly optimistic. Still, the announcement is worth examining because it underscores two important trends in today’s launch industry.

AST SpaceMobile 创始人、董事长兼首席执行官 Abel Avellan 在新闻稿中表示：“Blacklift 火箭是我们大幅扩大潜在市场总额的跳板，它将为 AST SpaceMobile 提供定期且不受限制的轨道进入能力，并带来行业内最有利的业主经济效益。”对于任何对火箭有基本了解的人来说，这个时间表简直是疯狂、离奇且愚蠢的乐观。尽管如此，这一声明仍值得审视，因为它凸显了当今发射行业的两个重要趋势。

First, it’s becoming increasingly apparent that satellite operators planning to deploy large constellations need to secure launch capacity years in advance, as Amazon did nearly half a decade ago. And if they can’t secure that capacity on favorable terms, they are now considering taking the extraordinary step of developing their own rockets. And secondly, since investors are now recognizing the worsening launch crisis that Ars has been writing about for months, there is apparently capital to be raised for new entrants into the medium-lift launch market.

首先，越来越明显的是，计划部署大型卫星星座的运营商需要提前数年锁定发射能力，正如亚马逊在近五年前所做的那样。如果他们无法以优惠条件获得这种能力，他们现在正考虑采取开发自有火箭这一非同寻常的举措。其次，由于投资者现在已经意识到《Ars》几个月来一直在报道的日益严重的发射危机，显然有资本愿意投向中型运载火箭市场的新进入者。

AST is not the only company making fantastical claims of late. A firm called Cowboy Space said in October it was building a large rocket named Big Iron (full credit—that’s an excellent name) to launch orbital data centers. It also claimed its large rocket would make its first flight in 2028. Sure thing, y’all.

最近做出离奇声明的不仅仅是 AST 一家公司。一家名为 Cowboy Space 的公司在 10 月份表示，他们正在制造一枚名为“Big Iron”（不得不说，这名字起得真棒）的大型火箭，用于发射轨道数据中心。他们还声称这枚大型火箭将在 2028 年进行首次飞行。当然，你们说了算。

Let’s start with a little history. SpaceX became the first purely commercial launch startup to announce plans to build a new medium-lift rocket about two decades ago, in 2005. The company holds the record for the fastest time to orbit with the Falcon 9 vehicle, with a development time of 4.7 years. But SpaceX had some significant advantages. By then, the company had been operating for more than three years, had extensive experience with the Merlin engine, and was less than a year away from its first launch of the smaller Falcon 1 rocket.

让我们先回顾一下历史。大约二十年前的 2005 年，SpaceX 成为第一家宣布计划建造新型中型运载火箭的纯商业发射初创公司。该公司凭借“猎鹰 9 号”火箭保持着最快进入轨道的纪录，研发周期为 4.7 年。但 SpaceX 当时拥有一些显著优势。那时，该公司已经运营了三年多，在“梅林”发动机方面拥有丰富的经验，并且距离其小型“猎鹰 1 号”火箭的首次发射已不足一年。

In retrospect, it’s also clear that the company had an insanely talented team of engineers. Finally, the Falcon 9 was quite a bit smaller than the recently announced vehicles, Blacklift and Big Iron. In its initial form, Falcon 9 had a lift capacity of about 10 tons. What about other companies? Since 2015, a number of other US companies—United Launch Alliance (Vulcan), Rocket Lab (Neutron), Relativity Space (Terran R), Firefly/Northrop Grumman (Eclipse), and Stoke Space (Nova) have announced medium to heavy lift vehicles, ranging in payload capacity from about 7 to 27 metric tons (Blue Origin’s New Glenn is much larger than any of these).

回过头来看，很明显该公司当时拥有一支才华横溢的工程师团队。最后，“猎鹰 9 号”比最近宣布的 Blacklift 和 Big Iron 火箭要小得多。在最初的版本中，“猎鹰 9 号”的运载能力约为 10 吨。其他公司的情况如何？自 2015 年以来，其他多家美国公司——联合发射联盟（Vulcan）、火箭实验室（Neutron）、相对论空间（Terran R）、萤火虫/诺斯罗普·格鲁曼（Eclipse）和 Stoke Space（Nova）——都宣布了中重型运载火箭，有效载荷能力从约 7 吨到 27 吨不等（蓝色起源的“新格伦”火箭比这些都要大得多）。

Of these, only Vulcan has reached orbit. It took 8.7 years, with an incredibly experienced launch team behind the vehicle and about $2 billion in aggregate federal support across its suppliers. Broadly speaking, the track records of these existing launch companies suggest that developing a new medium- or heavy-lift rocket takes five to nine years from public announcement to first orbital launch. Moreover, many of these companies either started with smaller rockets or brought other valuable experience to bear.

在这些公司中，只有 Vulcan 成功进入了轨道。它耗时 8.7 年，背后拥有经验极其丰富的发射团队，并获得了供应商总计约 20 亿美元的联邦支持。从广义上讲，这些现有发射公司的记录表明，开发一种新的中型或重型运载火箭，从公开宣布到首次轨道发射需要五到九年的时间。此外，这些公司中的许多要么是从小型火箭起步，要么带来了其他宝贵的经验。

AST SpaceMobile’s counterargument is that it won’t be starting from scratch. The company said Friday that it has signed an agreement with Firefly to deliver engines for the first three Blacklift rockets, provide engineering services over a three-year period, and acquire the perpetual license to manufacture the Miranda and vacuum-variant Vira engines from Firefly. Nine Miranda engines will power Blacklift’s first stage.

AST SpaceMobile 的反驳理由是他们不会从零开始。该公司周五表示，已与萤火虫公司签署协议，由后者为前三枚 Blacklift 火箭提供发动机，提供为期三年的工程服务，并获得萤火虫公司 Miranda 发动机及真空版 Vira 发动机的永久制造许可。九台 Miranda 发动机将为 Blacklift 的第一级提供动力。

Yes, engines are typically the hardest part of a rocket’s development. And procuring Miranda engines will shave some time off Blacklift’s development. But it will probably be a year or two, not five. Procuring the Miranda engine—which likely remains at least two years from being tested in flight on the Eclipse rocket—does not eliminate the enormous engineering, manufacturing, testing, and regulatory effort required to develop a medium-lift rocket.

是的，发动机通常是火箭研发中最困难的部分。采购 Miranda 发动机确实会缩短 Blacklift 的研发时间，但可能只会缩短一两年，而不是五年。采购 Miranda 发动机——该发动机在 Eclipse 火箭上进行飞行测试可能还需要至少两年时间——并不能消除开发中型运载火箭所需的巨大工程、制造、测试和监管工作。

AST SpaceMobile is also paying a pretty penny for this privilege. It has committed $1.3 billion to Firefly for the engine license ($800 million), 27 Miranda engines and nine Vira engines ($250 million), and engineering support ($250 million). If we look just at the engine costs for a single Blacklift rocket, that’s $83 million per vehicle for the first three.

AST SpaceMobile 为此也付出了高昂的代价。他们已向萤火虫公司承诺投入 13 亿美元，用于发动机许可（8 亿美元）、27 台 Miranda 发动机和 9 台 Vira 发动机（2.5 亿美元）以及工程支持（2.5 亿美元）。如果仅看单枚 Blacklift 火箭的发动机成本，前三枚火箭每枚的成本就高达 8300 万美元。

The bottom line is that AST SpaceMobile has made a launch timeline claim that is profoundly disconnected from the historical record of every comparable rocket development program this century. Maybe they’re a bunch of geniuses who will vibe-code the vehicle with Claude or Grok and shock us all. More likely, though, is that this is a company that desperately needs to launch about 90 more of its massive BlueBird direct-to-cell satellites.

归根结底，AST SpaceMobile 所提出的发射时间表与本世纪所有同类火箭研发项目的历史记录严重脱节。也许他们是一群天才，能用 Claude 或 Grok “灵感编程”出这枚火箭并震惊我们所有人。但更有可能的是，这是一家迫切需要再发射约 90 颗其大型 BlueBird 直接连接手机卫星的公司。
