# China reportedly recruited AI exec to spy on Jensen Huang

据《纽约时报》报道，中国情报机构招募了 Megaspeed 高管 Alice Huang，旨在监视英伟达（Nvidia）首席执行官黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）。

The Ministry of State Security (MSS) enlisted her to glean information about American AI chips and “potentially influence the company to push for its most advanced chips to be made available to China.” Megaspeed is a customer of Nvidia’s, and Alice Huang also has “relationships with executives at the highest levels of the A.I. chip industry,” including leaders at Gigabyte, the NYT says.

中国国家安全部（MSS）招募她是为了搜集有关美国人工智能芯片的情报，并“可能影响该公司，推动其向中国提供最先进的芯片”。据《纽约时报》称，Megaspeed 是英伟达的客户，Alice Huang 还与“人工智能芯片行业最高层的高管”保持着关系，其中包括技嘉（Gigabyte）的领导层。

Last week, the NYT reported that Jensen Huang was seen as the target of a “Chinese spy plot” and shared details about Alice Huang and Jensen Huang’s relationship, but the information that China’s MSS had recruited her is new.

上周，《纽约时报》报道称黄仁勋被视为“中国间谍阴谋”的目标，并分享了有关 Alice Huang 与黄仁勋之间关系的细节，但中国国家安全部招募她的消息是新披露的。

The NYT describes the relationship between Alice Huang and Jensen Huang, who are not related, as “close.” President Trump, who is friendly with Jensen Huang, was briefed last year about the relationship, but didn’t take any action. Yesterday, three House committees asked Jensen Huang to appear for a joint, closed-door briefing on his “interactions” with Alice Huang.

《纽约时报》将 Alice Huang 与黄仁勋（两人并无亲属关系）之间的关系描述为“亲密”。与黄仁勋交好的特朗普总统去年曾获悉这一关系，但并未采取任何行动。昨天，众议院三个委员会要求黄仁勋出席一场联合闭门简报会，就他与 Alice Huang 的“互动”进行说明。
