# OpenAI reportedly raises an additional $11 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation

In Brief Posted: 2:53 PM PST · November 6, 2026 Image Credits:Samuel Boivin/NurPhoto / Getty Images Lucas Ropek OpenAI reportedly raises an additional $11 billion at a $1.25 trillion valuation OpenAI has reportedly raised $11 billion so far in a new funding round that values the AI lab at $1.25 trillion.

简讯 发布时间：2026年11月6日下午2:53（太平洋标准时间） 图片来源：Samuel Boivin/NurPhoto / Getty Images Lucas Ropek 据报道，OpenAI以1.25万亿美元的估值额外筹集了110亿美元。据报道，OpenAI在一轮新的融资中已筹集了110亿美元，使该人工智能实验室的估值达到1.25万亿美元。

Bloomberg reported on the new funding on Friday, citing sources familiar with the matter. The $11 billion is part of a larger funding round that hasn’t closed yet, according to the outlet. The round is expected to close in the coming weeks and could total about $20 billion, a source told Bloomberg.

彭博社周五援引知情人士的话报道了这笔新融资。据该媒体称，这110亿美元是一轮尚未结束的更大规模融资的一部分。一位消息人士告诉彭博社，该轮融资预计将在未来几周内完成，总额可能达到约200亿美元。

In late September, Bloomberg reported that OpenAI was looking to raise $30 billion in the upcoming round. The current figures fall well short of that target. TechCrunch has reached out to OpenAI for comment and will update this story if it responds.

9月下旬，彭博社曾报道称OpenAI计划在即将到来的融资轮次中筹集300亿美元。目前的数字远未达到该目标。TechCrunch已联系OpenAI寻求置评，若有回复将更新此报道。

The news comes only hours after Bloomberg also reported that potential buyers of shares in the IPO of OpenAI’s chief competitor, Anthropic, are eyeing a substantially lower valuation than Anthropic had targeted. Bloomberg writes that some potential buyers believe Anthropic’s valuation could be as low as $1.1 trillion, compared with the $2 trillion that investors were said to be expecting roughly a month ago.

此消息传出仅数小时前，彭博社还报道称，OpenAI主要竞争对手Anthropic在IPO中的潜在股票买家所预期的估值远低于Anthropic的目标。彭博社写道，一些潜在买家认为Anthropic的估值可能低至1.1万亿美元，而大约一个月前投资者预期的估值据称高达2万亿美元。

OpenAI previously intimated that it would go public this year but subsequently delayed its plans. There are expectations that the company will IPO next year at some point. The market for AI hangs in a particularly precarious place. The Democratic landslide in the U.S. midterms — and overwhelmingly negative voter sentiment around data centers — has caused jitters throughout the market, with many anticipating potential regulatory challenges in the future.

OpenAI此前曾暗示今年将上市，但随后推迟了计划。外界普遍预期该公司明年某个时候会进行IPO。人工智能市场目前处于一个特别不稳定的境地。美国中期选举中民主党的大获全胜，以及选民对数据中心压倒性的负面情绪，已导致整个市场感到不安，许多人预计未来将面临潜在的监管挑战。
