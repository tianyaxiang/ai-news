Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.07042 (cs) [Submitted on 4 Nov 2026] Title: Follow the Policy, Fail the Benchmark: Auditing and Revising Policy-Based Decision Benchmarks Authors: Yanru Xiao, Yifei Cai.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.07042 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 4 日] 标题：遵循政策，却未通过基准测试：审计与修订基于政策的决策基准 作者：肖彦如（音译）、蔡一飞（音译）。

Abstract: Policy-based decision benchmarks evaluate whether systems apply written rules to financial and operational cases. Yet a rule-following response can be marked wrong when the benchmark misstates the required decision. We audit nine public policy-based decision benchmarks through source tracing, program execution, and controlled checks.

摘要：基于政策的决策基准旨在评估系统是否将书面规则应用于财务和运营案例。然而，当基准测试错误地陈述了所需决策时，符合规则的回答可能会被标记为错误。我们通过来源追踪、程序执行和受控检查，对九个公开的基于政策的决策基准进行了审计。

We find unsupported targets, reference errors that survive replay or propagate as solved steps, and evaluators that reject acceptable answers or credit incomplete decisions. To measure the scoring impact, we rescore unchanged model responses.

我们发现了缺乏支持的目标、在重放中存续或作为已解决步骤传播的参考错误，以及拒绝可接受答案或对不完整决策给予肯定的评估器。为了衡量评分影响，我们对未更改的模型响应进行了重新评分。

On the 96 LegalBench SARA numeric cases, correcting the scorer raises acceptance by more than 80 percentage points for each of three models, without changing references or responses. In RuleArena Tax, restoring a required zero floor changes 27 of 300 references and narrows an observed model gap.

在 96 个 LegalBench SARA 数值案例中，在不更改参考资料或响应的情况下，修正评分器使三个模型中每一个的接受率提高了 80 多个百分点。在 RuleArena Tax 中，恢复所需的零下限改变了 300 个参考资料中的 27 个，并缩小了观察到的模型差距。

A controlled federal-exemption task with explicit ownership and eligibility assumptions also shows that errors in supplied intermediate answers can affect final decisions. We present BAR (Benchmark Audit and Revision), a versioned collection of audit evidence and revisions for reevaluation.

一项具有明确所有权和资格假设的受控联邦豁免任务也表明，所提供的中间答案中的错误可能会影响最终决策。我们提出了 BAR（基准审计与修订），这是一个用于重新评估的审计证据和修订的版本化集合。
