Title: The Hackintosh Reborn
标题：黑苹果重生

A "Hackintosh" is the feat of running macOS on non-Apple hardware, and has a history dating back to the late 1980s. Almost a decade ago, I released a hackintosh guide on running macOS 10.14 through a VM with an x86 host. At the time KVM and PCI passthrough were relatively new technologies, and this guide allowed near-native performance with GPU acceleration.

“黑苹果”（Hackintosh）是指在非苹果硬件上运行 macOS 的壮举，其历史可以追溯到 20 世纪 80 年代末。大约十年前，我发布了一份关于通过 x86 主机上的虚拟机运行 macOS 10.14 的黑苹果指南。当时，KVM 和 PCI 直通技术还相对较新，而这份指南实现了接近原生的 GPU 加速性能。

A few years passed by, and we saw Apple release the first ARM-based Mac in 2021, the M1 MacBook Air. The timing was impeccable, Intel was unable to create competitive processors (due to being stuck on the 14nm node), and Apple had powerful and efficient ARM chips already being shipped in their iPhones. The long battery life, the fanless design, the instant wake, what's not to like? The benefits of switching to ARM were clear, however to most, this was thought to be the final chapter in the hackintosh community. x86 versions of macOS would soon be discontinued, and the hackintosh community would slowly fade away. The latest version of macOS 27 (Golden Gate) was released this year, and is no longer compatible with x86...

几年过去了，苹果在 2021 年发布了首款基于 ARM 的 Mac——M1 MacBook Air。时机恰到好处，英特尔因受困于 14nm 工艺而无法制造出具有竞争力的处理器，而苹果的 iPhone 已经在使用强大且高效的 ARM 芯片。超长的续航、无风扇设计、即时唤醒，谁能不爱呢？转向 ARM 的好处显而易见，但对大多数人来说，这被认为是黑苹果社区的终章。x86 版本的 macOS 即将停产，黑苹果社区也将逐渐消亡。今年发布的最新版 macOS 27 (Golden Gate) 已不再兼容 x86……

Just a few days ago, I decided to create an iOS app for the first time. It required XCode 26 for development, so I used my spare ThinkPad T490 as my development machine and installed macOS 26 using a posted guide. The installation was smooth and painless. XCode 26 has integrations with Claude Code and OpenAI Codex, and this was my first foray into AI-assisted coding. After some initial tinkering with x86 workarounds, I was pleased that I had a functional iOS app within a day, without touching a single line of code!

就在几天前，我决定第一次尝试开发 iOS 应用。它需要 XCode 26 进行开发，所以我用闲置的 ThinkPad T490 作为开发机，并按照网上发布的指南安装了 macOS 26。安装过程非常顺利且轻松。XCode 26 集成了 Claude Code 和 OpenAI Codex，这是我首次尝试 AI 辅助编程。在对 x86 变通方案进行了一些初步摸索后，我很高兴能在一天之内就拥有了一个功能完备的 iOS 应用，而且全程没写一行代码！

So how does this all tie in? Well, this is exactly what the Hackintosh community could hardly envision five years ago: the rise of AI. Waiting patiently and gently until now, the hackintosh and the hackios community has seen an absolute explosion in development, activity, and success in the past few weeks! 我所提到的仅仅是冰山一角，AirDrop、视频解码加速，甚至在 x86 上运行转译后的 macOS 27 等方面都有进一步的发展。尽管我们感叹 AI 的成本，但仍有一线希望：你或许真的能够拥有并掌控属于自己的硬件。
