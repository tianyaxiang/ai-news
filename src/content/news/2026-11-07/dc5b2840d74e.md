Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.06969 (cs) [Submitted on 4 Nov 2026] Title: Interactive Backdoor Attacks in LLM-Based Proactive Dialogue Systems Authors: Zhiyang Xia, Yuming Shang, Xi Zhang.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.06969 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 4 日] 标题：基于大语言模型的主动对话系统中的交互式后门攻击 作者：Zhiyang Xia, Yuming Shang, Xi Zhang。

Abstract: LLM-based proactive dialogue systems provide goal-directed support through sustained interaction. Fine-tuning their underlying models on externally supplied dialogue data introduces backdoor risks. Existing LLM backdoor studies primarily focus on eliciting an attacker-specified response upon trigger activation.

摘要：基于大语言模型（LLM）的主动对话系统通过持续的交互提供目标导向的支持。在外部提供的对话数据上对其底层模型进行微调会引入后门风险。现有的 LLM 后门研究主要集中在触发激活后诱导攻击者指定的响应。

We present an interactive backdoor attack framework that uses training-data poisoning to steer post-trigger, multi-turn interactions toward an attacker-specified objective. Poisoned fine-tuning dialogues implant a trigger-conditioned guidance-policy bias that ordinary user utterances can activate, without deployment-time prompt modification or further attacker intervention.

我们提出了一种交互式后门攻击框架，该框架利用训练数据投毒，将触发后的多轮交互引导至攻击者指定的目标。被投毒的微调对话植入了一种触发条件下的引导策略偏差，普通用户的话语即可激活该偏差，且无需在部署时修改提示词或进行进一步的攻击者干预。

Specifically, we instantiate three complementary attacks under progressively expanded post-trigger interaction settings: (1) Direct Attack, which targets an immediate response deviation upon trigger activation, retaining the conventional trigger--response setting; (2) Constrained Progressive Attack, which progressively advances the attack objective along a predefined multi-turn trajectory; and (3) Unconstrained Progressive Attack, which extends target-directed steering to open-ended interactions with varied user continuations.

具体而言，我们在逐步扩展的触发后交互设置下实例化了三种互补的攻击方式：（1）直接攻击，旨在触发激活后立即产生响应偏差，保留了传统的触发-响应设置；（2）受限渐进攻击，沿着预定义的多轮轨迹逐步推进攻击目标；以及（3）非受限渐进攻击，将目标导向的引导扩展到具有不同用户后续话语的开放式交互中。

Using psychological counseling as a representative setting, experiments on two LLM backbones and two dialogue datasets show that all three attacks effectively achieve their objectives under their respective interaction settings while largely preserving normal utility. Separate defense evaluations show that the tested training-data filters retain nearly all poisoned dialogues, while three post-admission baselines leave substantial residual attack-specific success.

以心理咨询作为代表性场景，在两个 LLM 主干模型和两个对话数据集上的实验表明，所有三种攻击在各自的交互设置下均能有效实现其目标，同时在很大程度上保持了正常效用。独立的防御评估显示，所测试的训练数据过滤器保留了几乎所有被投毒的对话，而三种准入后的基线方法仍留有大量的残留攻击成功率。
