Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.07072 (cs) [Submitted on 4 Nov 2026] Title: Automated Feedback to Peer Tutors Increases Their Talk Moves and Learner Reasoning but Not the Balance of Talk Authors: Dorottya Demszky, Yann Hicke, Joy Yun, Mariah Olson, Max Karpawich, Anees Iqbal, Matt Wu, Jimmy Huang, Akshay Ravikumar, Drew Bent.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.07072 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 4 日] 标题：针对同伴导师的自动化反馈增加了他们的谈话技巧和学习者的推理能力，但并未改变谈话平衡。作者：Dorottya Demszky, Yann Hicke, Joy Yun, Mariah Olson, Max Karpawich, Anees Iqbal, Matt Wu, Jimmy Huang, Akshay Ravikumar, Drew Bent。

Abstract: Automated feedback on classroom discourse improves instruction in professional and online teaching contexts, and recent work suggests that delivering similar feedback to learners may strengthen these effects by sharing responsibility for participation. Whether this holds in small-group volunteer tutoring has not been tested. We present results from a randomized controlled trial across two four-week SAT mathematics bootcamps on the this http URL platform, involving 1,030 volunteer tutors, 7,443 learners and 6,508 recorded sessions.

摘要：针对课堂话语的自动化反馈改善了专业和在线教学环境中的教学效果，近期研究表明，向学习者提供类似的反馈可能通过分担参与责任来增强这些效果。这种方法在小组志愿辅导中是否适用尚未经过测试。我们展示了一项随机对照试验的结果，该试验在 this http URL 平台的两个为期四周的 SAT 数学训练营中进行，涉及 1,030 名志愿导师、7,443 名学习者和 6,508 场录制课程。

Tutors were randomly assigned to receive no feedback, automated feedback on their own discourse, or that feedback together with participation feedback delivered to their learners under one of two motivational framings. Feedback to tutors increased eliciting of learner ideas, uptake of learner contributions, and learner reasoning by 8-15%, and the pattern replicated across both bootcamps.

导师被随机分配到以下组别：不接收反馈、接收关于自身话语的自动化反馈，或在两种激励框架之一下接收上述反馈以及提供给学习者的参与反馈。对导师的反馈使引导学习者想法、采纳学习者贡献以及学习者推理的能力提高了 8-15%，且这一模式在两个训练营中均得到复现。

However, tutor talk time, which averaged 91.5% in the control group, did not change in any condition, and learner-facing feedback did not further change discourse. Interviews with 17 tutors indicate that they adopted the moves the feedback recommended but were limited by learners who did not respond.

然而，在对照组中平均占比为 91.5% 的导师谈话时间在任何条件下均未发生改变，且面向学习者的反馈也未能进一步改变话语模式。对 17 名导师的访谈表明，他们采纳了反馈建议的技巧，但受到了不予回应的学习者的限制。

Interviews with 13 learners suggest why the learner-facing feedback had little effect: it reported a group-level rather than a learner-level statistic, some learners preferred to listen, and learner-to-learner interaction was rare in these sessions. Automated feedback can change how tutors invite learner thinking, but shifting how much learners talk likely requires changes beyond feedback.

对 13 名学习者的访谈揭示了为何面向学习者的反馈效果甚微：它报告的是群体层面的统计数据而非个人层面的统计数据，部分学习者更倾向于倾听，且这些课程中学习者之间的互动很少。自动化反馈可以改变导师引导学习者思考的方式，但要改变学习者的谈话量，可能需要反馈之外的变革。
