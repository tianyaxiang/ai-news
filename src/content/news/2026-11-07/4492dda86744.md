Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.06996 (cs) [Submitted on 4 Nov 2026] Title: Trust Responsive Runtime Authorization for Agentic AI with Empirical Characterization of Policy Behavior and Parameter Sensitivity Authors: Ahsan Habib Shihab, Imre Felde, Ramis Akhmedov, Stefan Gubo, Berik Sabdenaliyev, Madina Mansurova, Amir Mosavi.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.06996 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 4 日] 标题：面向智能体 AI 的信任响应式运行时授权：策略行为与参数敏感性的实证表征。作者：Ahsan Habib Shihab, Imre Felde, Ramis Akhmedov, Stefan Gubo, Berik Sabdenaliyev, Madina Mansurova, Amir Mosavi。

Abstract: The policy that converts runtime estimates of an autonomous agent's condition into operational authority is rarely specified or measured. We empirically characterize the Trust Responsive Authorization Policy (TRAP), which maps an Authorization Readiness Score (ARS), a weighted combination of runtime trust, operational stability, and contextual risk, onto discrete authorization levels through an asymmetric rule: escalation requires consecutive observations above a band edge plus a margin, while a sharp drop in readiness restricts authority at once.

摘要：将自主智能体状态的运行时估计转化为操作权限的策略很少被明确规定或测量。我们对“信任响应式授权策略”（TRAP）进行了实证表征，该策略将“授权就绪分数”（ARS）映射到离散的授权级别。ARS 是运行时信任、操作稳定性和上下文风险的加权组合，其映射遵循一种非对称规则：权限升级需要连续观测值高于频带边缘加上一个余量，而就绪状态的急剧下降则会立即限制权限。

On one matched set of 100 Monte Carlo traces, we compare TRAP with static, trust-only, risk-only, and symmetric policies. Under abrupt degradation, TRAP restricts at onset and re-escalates sooner after recovery than the symmetric policy, with the highest and, descriptively, least variable stability among the adaptive policies, although more of its post-recovery escalations occur below the selected trust threshold. Paired bootstrap intervals exclude zero for the remaining differences.

在一组匹配的 100 条蒙特卡洛轨迹上，我们将 TRAP 与静态策略、仅信任策略、仅风险策略以及对称策略进行了比较。在突发性退化情况下，与对称策略相比，TRAP 在退化开始时即进行限制，并在恢复后更快地重新升级权限。在所有自适应策略中，TRAP 表现出最高且描述性上变异性最小的稳定性，尽管其恢复后的升级更多发生在选定的信任阈值之下。配对自助法区间排除了剩余差异为零的可能性。

In one-factor sweeps, recovery time rises with the escalation margin and run length, shifts with the score weights, and is unchanged by the restriction threshold. In an exploratory post-freeze experiment, when the same degradation is spread over 5-20 intervals, TRAP restricts only on the 4 traces that retain a single-interval drop above that threshold; averaged over all 100 traces, it holds authority above what readiness supports for 15.9-18.9 of the 20 degradation intervals, against 0.8 under abrupt degradation and about 10 for the symmetric policy. Its restriction advantage therefore depends on degradation being abrupt.

在单因素扫描中，恢复时间随升级余量和运行长度的增加而增加，随分数权重的变化而偏移，且不受限制阈值的影响。在一项探索性的冻结后实验中，当同样的退化分散在 5-20 个区间时，TRAP 仅在 4 条保持单区间下降超过该阈值的轨迹上进行限制；在所有 100 条轨迹的平均值中，它在 20 个退化区间中有 15.9-18.9 个区间保持了高于就绪状态所支持的权限，而突发性退化下该值为 0.8，对称策略下约为 10。因此，其限制优势取决于退化是否为突发性的。
