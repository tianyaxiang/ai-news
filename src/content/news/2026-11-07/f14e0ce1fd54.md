Blockchain analysis firm Chainalysis said that hackers have obtained sensitive personal information—including names, usernames, password hashes, and live tokens—for all of its users following a breach of its network security. The accomplishment could be a major coup for the attackers. Both Chainalysis and public sources have revealed a long list of high-profile customers, including government agencies such as the FBI, US Immigration and Customs Enforcement agency, and the US Air Force, as well as private companies such as Microsoft, Coinbase, and Barclays. It’s unclear if the data theft exposed customers to additional threats.

区块链分析公司 Chainalysis 表示，在一次网络安全漏洞事件后，黑客获取了其所有用户的敏感个人信息，包括姓名、用户名、密码哈希值和实时令牌。这一成果对攻击者而言可能是一次重大胜利。Chainalysis 和公开来源均显示，该公司拥有众多知名客户，包括联邦调查局（FBI）、美国移民及海关执法局（ICE）和美国空军等政府机构，以及微软、Coinbase 和巴克莱银行等私营企业。目前尚不清楚此次数据窃取是否使客户面临额外威胁。

Last Friday, Chainalysis said it had been the target of a cybersecurity incident in October that was similar to the hacks of other platforms during the same month. The external actor used AI agent-assisted offensive techniques to gain unauthorized access to Chainalysis systems and internal tools. An email the company sent to affected customers said some “non-sensitive customer data, such as user IDs, full names, email addresses, and organization role labels for users provisioned on our platform were accessed.” It’s not clear why the company considered such information “non-sensitive.”

上周五，Chainalysis 表示其在 10 月份遭遇了一起网络安全事件，与当月其他平台的黑客攻击类似。外部攻击者利用人工智能代理辅助的攻击技术，未经授权访问了 Chainalysis 的系统和内部工具。该公司发送给受影响客户的电子邮件称，部分“非敏感客户数据，如用户 ID、全名、电子邮件地址以及平台上配置用户的组织角色标签”已被访问。目前尚不清楚该公司为何认为此类信息属于“非敏感”信息。

In a Wednesday update, the company said that part of the “ongoing multifront cyberattack” involved an “on-path attack” that exposed a subset of customer passwords, and that the attack vector had been identified and fixed. Also known as an adversary-in-the-middle attack, on-path attacks happen when a hacker gains access to a piece of infrastructure sending traffic back and forth between two parties. Chainalysis has yet to say where the infrastructure compromise occurred and if the data obtained from it was TLS encrypted.

在周三的更新中，该公司表示，“持续的多方面网络攻击”的一部分涉及“路径攻击”（on-path attack），导致部分客户密码泄露，目前攻击向量已被识别并修复。路径攻击也被称为中间人攻击，发生在黑客获取了在双方之间传输流量的基础设施访问权限时。Chainalysis 尚未说明基础设施受损的具体位置，也未说明从中获取的数据是否经过了 TLS 加密。

The company said later that day that an investigation two days earlier (on Monday) had revealed that “certain customer information including names, usernames, password hashes and live tokens for all Chainalysis users were exposed to a malicious actor.” It also said it had detected “abnormal activity” and, in response, took down its login page and know-your-transaction service, which monitors threats to cryptocurrency holdings in real time. The company has also removed access to its Reactor and Data Solutions services.

该公司当天晚些时候表示，两天前（周一）的调查显示，“包括所有 Chainalysis 用户的姓名、用户名、密码哈希值和实时令牌在内的特定客户信息已暴露给恶意行为者”。该公司还表示已检测到“异常活动”，并因此关闭了登录页面和“了解你的交易”（KYT）服务，该服务用于实时监控加密货币资产的威胁。此外，该公司还移除了对其 Reactor 和数据解决方案服务的访问权限。

In an update on Friday, Chainalysis said it had begun phasing in customer access to KYT and Address Screening and expected more customers to follow. It asked customers to submit their trusted IP addresses so they can be added to an allow list and to prepare to reset credentials once they regain access. The credential rotation is mandatory. The company says its investigation has yet to reveal any evidence that any investigation data has been accessed or Chainalysis attribution data has been manipulated.

在周五的更新中，Chainalysis 表示已开始逐步恢复客户对 KYT 和地址筛选服务的访问，并预计会有更多客户跟进。它要求客户提交受信任的 IP 地址以便加入白名单，并准备在重新获得访问权限后重置凭据。凭据轮换是强制性的。该公司称，目前的调查尚未发现任何证据表明调查数据已被访问，或 Chainalysis 的归因数据已被篡改。

Several important questions remain unanswered. As noted, the source that made the on-path attack possible is still unknown. It’s not clear how useful the information obtained in such an attack would be unless the attackers also had the ability to decrypt the traffic they were monitoring. If that were the case, the attackers might have gained broad control of the company’s infrastructure. Chainalysis also didn’t explain why it waited until Wednesday to disclose the data theft of all customers’ personal information when its investigation discovered the exposure two days earlier. Further, the company has provided no details about the AI agents used in the attack and how they worked.

仍有几个重要问题悬而未决。如前所述，导致路径攻击发生的源头尚不清楚。除非攻击者同时具备解密其监控流量的能力，否则此类攻击中获取的信息有多大用处尚不明确。如果情况确实如此，攻击者可能已经获得了对该公司基础设施的广泛控制权。Chainalysis 也没有解释为什么在两天前调查发现数据泄露后，直到周三才披露所有客户个人信息被盗的情况。此外，该公司没有提供关于攻击中所用 AI 代理及其工作方式的任何细节。
