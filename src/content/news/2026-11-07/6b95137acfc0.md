计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.07055 (cs) [2026年11月4日提交] 标题：通过竞争性流匹配扩展质量-覆盖范围边界 作者：Wenxuan Peng, Richard Zhang, Haitian Zheng, Jaskirat Singh, Tsai-Shien Chen, Eli Shechtman, Bharath Hariharan, Hadar Averbuch-Elor, Zongze Wu。

Abstract: Flow matching (FM) generators often face a quality-coverage tradeoff. Inference-time techniques such as classifier-free guidance raise quality, but at the cost of coverage. We hypothesize this limitation stems from a basic property of the objective: an intermediate noisy image could correspond to many clean images, yet the model can only predict a single velocity, at best averaging over the multiple possibilities.

摘要：流匹配（FM）生成器通常面临质量与覆盖范围之间的权衡。诸如无分类器引导（classifier-free guidance）之类的推理时技术提高了质量，但却以牺牲覆盖范围为代价。我们推测这一局限性源于目标函数的一个基本属性：中间的噪声图像可能对应于许多清晰图像，然而模型最多只能预测单一速度，即对多种可能性进行平均。

To mitigate this, we introduce competition among predictions during training. We achieve this by augmenting the predictor with a randomly drawn low-dimensional conditioning code $z$. For each training example, we sample $K$ values of $z$ to produce $K$ candidate velocities that compete to explain the target, and apply the loss only to the best-matching candidate.

为了缓解这一问题，我们在训练过程中引入了预测之间的竞争。我们通过为预测器增加一个随机抽取的低维条件编码 $z$ 来实现这一点。对于每个训练样本，我们采样 $K$ 个 $z$ 值以产生 $K$ 个候选速度，这些速度竞争以解释目标，并且仅对最佳匹配的候选者应用损失函数。

This winner-take-all objective allows different modes to shape different predictions, rather than being averaged into a single compromise. Furthermore, this term naturally enables a new form of guidance, auxiliary conditioning scaling, which complements traditional guidance methods. Together, these two guidance mechanisms show that our method substantially improves the quality-coverage frontier.

这种胜者全得（winner-take-all）的目标函数允许不同的模式塑造不同的预测，而不是被平均成单一的折中方案。此外，该项自然地实现了一种新的引导形式——辅助条件缩放（auxiliary conditioning scaling），它补充了传统的引导方法。这两种引导机制共同表明，我们的方法显著改善了质量-覆盖范围的边界。

We validate on ImageNet-256, using two different testbeds, SiT and RAEv2. Given less than 3% of the original SiT pretraining compute for post-training, at the 67% Recall point, our method reduces $\mathrm{FD}_{r,6}$ from 19.22 to 5.91. On RAEv2, at the 70% Recall point, it reduces $\mathrm{FD}_{r,6}$ from 16.83 to 3.18.

我们在 ImageNet-256 上使用两个不同的测试平台 SiT 和 RAEv2 进行了验证。在后训练阶段仅使用不到原始 SiT 预训练计算量 3% 的情况下，在 67% 的召回率点，我们的方法将 $\mathrm{FD}_{r,6}$ 从 19.22 降低至 5.91。在 RAEv2 上，在 70% 的召回率点，它将 $\mathrm{FD}_{r,6}$ 从 16.83 降低至 3.18。
