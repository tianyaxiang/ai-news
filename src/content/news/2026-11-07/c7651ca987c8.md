# Amazon is now making a Melania Trump TV series

亚马逊目前正在制作一部关于梅拉尼娅·特朗普的电视剧。

Melania Trump is the focus of a new two-part Prime Video show debuting later this month.

梅拉尼娅·特朗普是本月晚些时候即将首播的一部两集 Prime Video 节目的焦点。

Following the Melania documentary movie that premiered earlier this year, Amazon is now planning to release a two-part Prime Video series focusing on the first lady. The show, Melania: The Series, is set to debut on November 20th.

继今年早些时候首映的梅拉尼娅纪录片之后，亚马逊现在计划发布一部聚焦于这位第一夫人的两集 Prime Video 系列剧。该剧名为《梅拉尼娅：系列剧》（Melania: The Series），定于 11 月 20 日首播。

“MELANIA, The Series reveals never-before-told stories, offering unprecedented insight into Melania Trump’s life, including the extraordinary days immediately following the 47th Presidential Inauguration,” according to a description in a press release.

根据一份新闻稿中的描述，“《梅拉尼娅：系列剧》揭示了从未公开过的故事，提供了对梅拉尼娅·特朗普生活的空前洞察，包括第 47 届总统就职典礼后紧接着的非凡日子。”

Here’s what Amazon says the two episodes will be about: I. Family & Trust: Melania Trump sets priorities as she prepares to become America’s First Lady. But inside the White House, trust is tested, and the incoming First Lady must protect those closest to her while navigating the demands of power.

以下是亚马逊对这两集内容的介绍：第一集：家庭与信任：梅拉尼娅·特朗普在准备成为美国第一夫人时设定了优先事项。但在白宫内部，信任受到了考验，这位即将上任的第一夫人必须在应对权力需求的同时保护她最亲近的人。

II. Pride & Honor: The honor of serving as First Lady comes with an immediate call to action. Moments after the inauguration, First Lady Melania Trump sets her mission in motion for the American people.

第二集：骄傲与荣誉：担任第一夫人的荣誉伴随着立即采取行动的号召。在就职典礼后的片刻，第一夫人梅拉尼娅·特朗普便为美国人民启动了她的使命。

Brett Ratner, who directed the Melania film, directed both episodes of the series.

曾执导过梅拉尼娅纪录片的布莱特·拉特纳（Brett Ratner）执导了该系列剧的两集。
