To study a complex system, scientists often begin with a small, controlled environment, such as a petri dish or terrarium, where they can vary conditions and observe the effects. Robotics needs environments that make this kind of investigation possible—researchers should be able to change the training data or model design, repeat a test, and trace the cause of a failure.

为了研究复杂的系统，科学家们通常从一个小型、受控的环境开始，例如培养皿或玻璃容器，在这些环境中他们可以改变条件并观察效果。机器人技术需要能够实现这种研究的环境——研究人员应该能够更改训练数据或模型设计、重复测试并追踪失败的原因。

Today we're introducing a framework for creating these environments; we call them FoundationWorlds. Each pairs a controlled, simulated world targeted at studying robotics in a specific environment with tools for automatically generating tasks and training demonstrations, as well as metrics for measuring a robot's behavior over time.

今天，我们引入了一个用于创建这些环境的框架；我们称之为“基础世界”（FoundationWorlds）。每一个基础世界都将一个针对特定环境研究机器人技术的受控模拟世界，与自动生成任务和训练演示的工具，以及衡量机器人随时间推移表现的指标相结合。

Together, they provide a setting for studying questions such as how models improve as more training data becomes available, and how to best combine high-level reasoning with precise physical control. Our first foundation world is ToyBlocks: an open simulation framework built around a robot arm and blocks on a tabletop.

它们共同提供了一个研究问题的环境，例如随着更多训练数据的可用，模型如何改进，以及如何最好地将高级推理与精确的物理控制相结合。我们的第一个基础世界是 ToyBlocks：一个围绕桌面上的机械臂和积木构建的开放式模拟框架。

The setting is deliberately simple, but the robot must still reason about how the blocks interact and how to arrange them while pushing, flipping, and stacking. ToyBlocks automatically generates demonstrations, which are recordings of a robot completing tasks in simulation, without requiring time-consuming teleoperation, in which a person remotely controls the robot.

该设置被刻意简化，但机器人仍然必须推理积木如何相互作用，以及如何在推动、翻转和堆叠时排列它们。ToyBlocks 会自动生成演示（即机器人在模拟中完成任务的记录），而无需耗时的远程操作（即由人远程控制机器人）。

Researchers can easily vary these training experiences. For example, a team could generate more pushing or flipping demonstrations, then evaluate performance on tasks kept out of training. Starting with ToyBlocks as our first FoundationWorld, we aim to extend the approach to increasingly complex environments.

研究人员可以轻松改变这些训练体验。例如，团队可以生成更多的推动或翻转演示，然后评估在训练中未涉及的任务上的表现。以 ToyBlocks 作为我们的第一个“基础世界”，我们的目标是将这种方法扩展到日益复杂的环境中。

How ToyBlocks works Building on our earlier work in open robot learning, including MolmoAct 2, MolmoBot, and MolmoSpaces, ToyBlocks gives researchers a way to study robotics questions in a deliberately constrained domain. As a FoundationWorld, ToyBlocks is designed around four requirements: (1) realistic physics and appearance, (2) automatic generation of tasks and demonstrations at scale, (3) challenges that combine reasoning with dexterity, and (4) broad coverage of the tasks possible within a domain.

ToyBlocks 的工作原理：基于我们在开放机器人学习方面的早期工作（包括 MolmoAct 2、MolmoBot 和 MolmoSpaces），ToyBlocks 为研究人员提供了一种在刻意受限的领域中研究机器人问题的方法。作为“基础世界”，ToyBlocks 的设计围绕四个要求：(1) 逼真的物理效果和外观，(2) 大规模自动生成任务和演示，(3) 结合推理与灵巧性的挑战，以及 (4) 对领域内可能任务的广泛覆盖。

Open-ended domains require modeling a wide range of objects and interactions, making it infeasible to fulfill all these requirements. ToyBlocks’ narrow scope, on the other hand, makes it possible to fulfill them all. Within ToyBlocks' smaller setting, researchers can work toward investigating how a robot can learn to perform any reasonable task while being confident that the simulated arm and blocks behave very similarly to their physical counterparts.

开放式领域需要对广泛的物体和交互进行建模，这使得满足所有这些要求变得不可行。另一方面，ToyBlocks 的狭窄范围使其能够满足所有这些要求。在 ToyBlocks 较小的设置中，研究人员可以致力于研究机器人如何学习执行任何合理的任务，同时确信模拟的机械臂和积木的行为与其物理对应物非常相似。

A physical robot gripper above wooden blocks in the ToyBlocks setup. ToyBlocks is not just about picking and stacking blocks: pictures and letters on the blocks' faces allow for searching, sorting, and arranging tasks. A robot may need to turn a block to see a hidden face before deciding where it belongs.

ToyBlocks 设置中木块上方的物理机器人夹爪。ToyBlocks 不仅仅是关于拾取和堆叠积木：积木面上的图片和字母允许进行搜索、分类和排列任务。机器人可能需要转动积木以查看隐藏的面，然后再决定它属于哪里。

Domain randomization – varying block size, table height, and other physical properties from scene to scene – tests how well models generalize as the geometry changes. ToyBlocks automatically generates skills (behaviors for short tasks) and task demonstrations that can be used to train models or adapt previously trained models through additional training (fine-tuning).

领域随机化——在不同场景间改变积木大小、桌子高度和其他物理属性——测试了模型在几何形状改变时的泛化能力。ToyBlocks 自动生成技能（短任务的行为）和任务演示，这些可用于训练模型或通过额外训练（微调）来适配先前训练的模型。

High-level reasoning models decide what to do; low-level action models control the robot’s movements. The framework can also be used to easily design experiments and validate hypotheses. ToyBlocks uses reinforcement learning (RL) and motion planning, which computes how the robot should move, to generate skills for short-horizon tasks, which involve brief sequences of actions.

高级推理模型决定做什么；低级动作模型控制机器人的运动。该框架还可用于轻松设计实验和验证假设。ToyBlocks 使用强化学习 (RL) 和运动规划（计算机器人应如何移动）来为短视界任务生成技能，这些任务涉及简短的动作序列。

Skill training takes place in Isaac Lab, Nvidia's open-source framework for robot learning in simulation. Through trial and error, these policies – models that choose the robot’s actions – learn non-prehensile skills such as pushing a block across the table or flipping it onto another face without picking it up.

技能训练在 Nvidia 的开源机器人模拟学习框架 Isaac Lab 中进行。通过反复试验，这些策略（选择机器人动作的模型）学习非抓取技能，例如将积木推过桌面或在不拾起它的情况下将其翻转到另一面。

Skill generation can draw on privileged information – values the simulator can supply directly but a real robot cannot, such as a block's exact position and orientation – instead of estimating it from camera images. This enables the automatic generation of skills that would require teleoperation in the real world.

技能生成可以利用特权信息（模拟器可以直接提供但真实机器人无法获取的值，例如积木的确切位置和方向），而不是从摄像机图像中进行估计。这使得自动生成在现实世界中需要远程操作的技能成为可能。

To generate demonstration data, a task-and-motion planner (TAMP) automatically selects and sequences the skills needed to solve a task and plans the robot’s movements between them. Running each plan in simulation produces a dataset of recorded demonstrations along with labels describing which task or skill the robot performs.

为了生成演示数据，任务与运动规划器 (TAMP) 会自动选择并排序解决任务所需的技能，并规划机器人在此期间的运动。在模拟中运行每个计划会产生一个记录的演示数据集，以及描述机器人执行了哪个任务或技能的标签。

ToyBlocks scales data generation through generating and executing hundreds of such plans in parallel on a single graphics processing unit (GPU), reaching up to 24 times real-time performance. Acting from sensor observations adds a perception problem: a model has to estimate a block’s pose – its position and orientation – which the simulator could supply directly.

ToyBlocks 通过在单个图形处理器 (GPU) 上并行生成和执行数百个此类计划来扩展数据生成，性能最高可达实时速度的 24 倍。根据传感器观测采取行动增加了一个感知问题：模型必须估计积木的姿态（其位置和方向），而模拟器可以直接提供这些信息。

To reflect real-world conditions, ToyBlocks trains models that map raw sensor observations to actions. Execution of Molmo 2 (a high-level model) and ManiFlow (a low-level model) trained in ToyBlocks. Molmo 2 selects skills such as rotation and flipping, indicating the relevant block via a pointing interface, while ManiFlow controls the simulated arm to execute the commanded skills.

为了反映现实世界的条件，ToyBlocks 训练将原始传感器观测映射到动作的模型。在 ToyBlocks 中训练的 Molmo 2（高级模型）和 ManiFlow（低级模型）的执行情况。Molmo 2 选择旋转和翻转等技能，通过指向界面指示相关积木，而 ManiFlow 则控制模拟机械臂执行所命令的技能。
