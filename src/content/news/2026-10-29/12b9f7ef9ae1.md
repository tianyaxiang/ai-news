# New 14‑inch MacBook Pro with M6

Apple introduces the new 14‑inch MacBook Pro with M6.

Apple 今日发布了搭载 M6 芯片的全新 14 英寸 MacBook Pro，为大学生、商务用户、创作者等群体带来了更强的性能、强大的端侧 AI 能力以及更快的连接速度。

Supercharged by M6, the 14-inch MacBook Pro features incredible performance and next-level AI capabilities, enhanced connectivity, and up to 24 hours of battery life, delivering even more value.

得益于 M6 芯片的强劲动力，14 英寸 MacBook Pro 具备卓越的性能、进阶的 AI 能力、增强的连接性以及长达 24 小时的电池续航，提供了更高的价值。

CUPERTINO, CALIFORNIA Apple today announced the new 14-inch MacBook Pro with M6, delivering even more performance, powerful on-device AI capabilities, and faster connectivity for college students, business users, creators, and more.

加利福尼亚州库比蒂诺 —— Apple 今日宣布推出搭载 M6 芯片的全新 14 英寸 MacBook Pro，为大学生、商务人士、创作者等用户提供更出色的性能、强大的端侧 AI 功能和更快的连接速度。

M6 features an advanced 12-core CPU with the world’s fastest CPU core, so everything from productivity tasks to more demanding pro workflows feels incredibly fast and responsive.

M6 芯片配备先进的 12 核 CPU，拥有全球最快的 CPU 核心，无论是处理日常办公任务还是要求严苛的专业工作流，都能感受到极速与灵敏的响应。

The 12-core GPU delivers up to 30 percent faster graphics and up to 25 percent faster AI workloads when compared to M5, making the 14-inch MacBook Pro with M6 a compelling value for users who need serious power.

与 M5 相比，12 核 GPU 的图形处理速度提升最高可达 30%，AI 工作负载处理速度提升最高可达 25%，这使得搭载 M6 的 14 英寸 MacBook Pro 对于需要强劲性能的用户而言极具价值。

For those upgrading from M1, the performance gains are even more significant, with up to 11x faster graphics and up to 13x faster AI performance.

对于从 M1 芯片升级的用户来说，性能提升更为显著，图形处理速度最高提升至 11 倍，AI 性能最高提升至 13 倍。

With additional Neural Accelerators in the GPU and a Dual 16-core Neural Engine, M6 turns MacBook Pro into an even more powerful platform for AI — from taking advantage of Siri AI and Apple Intelligence features across apps and macOS to running LLMs locally, all while preserving user privacy.

通过 GPU 中新增的神经加速器和双 16 核神经网络引擎，M6 将 MacBook Pro 打造成了一个更强大的 AI 平台——从在各类应用和 macOS 中利用 Siri AI 和 Apple Intelligence 功能，到在本地运行大语言模型（LLM），同时还能保护用户隐私。

In addition, the 14-inch MacBook Pro features Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6, while superfast storage makes working with large files, exporting projects, and running demanding AI workflows fly.

此外，14 英寸 MacBook Pro 支持 Wi-Fi 7 和蓝牙 6，超高速存储让处理大型文件、导出项目以及运行高负载 AI 工作流变得轻而易举。

And with phenomenal battery life of up to 24 hours, it delivers the same remarkable performance whether plugged in or on battery, so users can work, create, and pursue their passions from anywhere.

凭借长达 24 小时的惊人电池续航，无论是在插电还是使用电池供电时，它都能提供同样出色的性能，让用户随时随地工作、创作并追求热爱。

It also offers the advanced features users love, including the stunning Liquid Retina XDR display with a nano-texture option, a 12MP Center Stage camera, an immersive six-speaker sound system, and a wide array of ports.

它还具备用户喜爱的先进功能，包括令人惊叹的 Liquid Retina XDR 显示屏（可选配纳米纹理玻璃）、1200 万像素居中人像摄像头、沉浸式六扬声器音响系统以及丰富的接口。

Together with macOS 27, the new 14-inch MacBook Pro with M6 is a major upgrade for existing Mac users and those coming to Mac for the first time.

配合 macOS 27，搭载 M6 的全新 14 英寸 MacBook Pro 对于现有 Mac 用户以及首次使用 Mac 的用户来说，都是一次重大的升级。

Available in space black and silver, it can be pre-ordered beginning tomorrow, October 28, with availability starting Friday, November 6.

该产品提供深空黑色和银色可选，将于 10 月 28 日（明日）开启预购，并于 11 月 6 日（周五）正式发售。
