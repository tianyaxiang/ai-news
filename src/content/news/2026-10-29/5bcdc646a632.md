# AI for Science expands to foundations and research institutions

### Chapter 01: Science funders and societies
We’re highlighting 20 scientific foundations, institutions, and research groups from around the world that we’ve partnered with in an expansion of our AI for Science program, which gives free Claude credits to researchers working on high-impact scientific projects.

### 第一章：科学资助机构与协会
我们重点介绍了全球 20 家科学基金会、研究机构和研究小组，我们已与它们建立合作伙伴关系，以扩展我们的“AI for Science”计划。该计划旨在为从事高影响力科学项目的研究人员提供免费的 Claude 额度。

We started the AI for Science program in May 2025 with the belief that AI could significantly speed up scientific progress. Our goal is to increase access to AI tools and resources to enable researchers to deepen and expand their own scientific work. (Over the last few months, we’ve also launched our discounted Team plan for scientists and have supported hundreds of projects across biology, medicine, physics, chemistry, climate science, and mathematics.)

我们于 2025 年 5 月启动了“AI for Science”计划，坚信人工智能可以显著加快科学进步。我们的目标是增加对人工智能工具和资源的获取，使研究人员能够深化和扩展其科学工作。（在过去几个月中，我们还为科学家推出了折扣团队计划，并支持了生物学、医学、物理学、化学、气候科学和数学领域的数百个项目。）

Scientists have told us that they’re working with Claude to generate hypotheses, design experiments, swiftly analyze complex data, and communicate findings. With scientists at the center of this transformation, the field is setting the questions and developing the research projects that will help us tackle some of humanity’s toughest problems.

科学家们告诉我们，他们正在利用 Claude 来生成假设、设计实验、快速分析复杂数据并交流研究结果。随着科学家成为这一变革的核心，该领域正在设定研究课题并开发研究项目，这将有助于我们解决人类面临的一些最棘手的问题。

Over the past year, we’ve started to expand this work and partner with scientific organizations to build collaborative research projects. For instance, we partnered with HHMI Janelia Research Campus to develop the Model Hardware Standard (MHS), a shared specification that lets AI agents safely operate lab instruments. MHS is currently in research preview.

在过去的一年中，我们开始扩展这项工作，并与科学组织合作建立协作研究项目。例如，我们与霍华德·休斯医学研究所（HHMI）Janelia 研究园区合作开发了模型硬件标准（MHS），这是一种允许人工智能代理安全操作实验室仪器的共享规范。MHS 目前处于研究预览阶段。

We also support the Allen Institute, a leader in creating large open datasets and tools to advance research on the brain, cells, and the immune system. The institute is building teams of AI agents to analyze that data, turning months of manual analysis into hours.

我们还支持艾伦研究所（Allen Institute），该机构在创建大型开放数据集和工具以推进大脑、细胞和免疫系统研究方面处于领先地位。该研究所正在组建人工智能代理团队来分析这些数据，将数月的体力分析工作缩短至数小时。

AI for Science began by supporting individual scientists. By partnering with institutions and foundations, we’re now able to reach more researchers across many fields. Many of these projects will also produce public goods that benefit the wider scientific community: open datasets, open-source software, open standards, and shared libraries. We look forward to seeing how AI resources can expand what scientists can attempt and lead to new discoveries.

“AI for Science”最初始于对个人科学家的支持。通过与机构和基金会合作，我们现在能够触及更多领域的研究人员。其中许多项目还将产生造福更广泛科学界的公共产品：开放数据集、开源软件、开放标准和共享库。我们期待看到人工智能资源如何扩展科学家们能够尝试的领域，并带来新的发现。

### Science funders and societies
Science funders and societies play an enormous role in finding and funding original, high-impact projects. We’re sharing Claude credits with the following organizations, which will distribute these resources to ambitious researchers aiming to use AI for scientific research:

### 科学资助机构与协会
科学资助机构和协会在发现和资助原创性、高影响力项目方面发挥着巨大作用。我们正在与以下组织共享 Claude 额度，它们将把这些资源分配给旨在利用人工智能进行科学研究的雄心勃勃的研究人员：

The Alfred P. Sloan Foundation. Founded in 1934, it supports original research and education in science, technology, engineering, mathematics, and economics.

阿尔弗雷德·P·斯隆基金会（The Alfred P. Sloan Foundation）。该基金会成立于 1934 年，支持科学、技术、工程、数学和经济学领域的原创研究与教育。

The Simons Foundation. This foundation advances the frontiers of research in mathematics and basic sciences through grants and its Flatiron Institute.

西蒙斯基金会（The Simons Foundation）。该基金会通过拨款及其下属的弗拉特隆研究所（Flatiron Institute），推动数学和基础科学的研究前沿。

Schmidt Sciences. Founded in 2024 by Eric and Wendy Schmidt, it works to accelerate scientific knowledge and breakthroughs with the most promising, advanced tools to support a thriving planet. The organization prioritizes research in areas poised for impact, including AI and advanced computing, astrophysics, biosciences, climate, and space—and supports researchers in a variety of disciplines through its science systems program.

施密特科学（Schmidt Sciences）。该组织由埃里克和温迪·施密特于 2024 年创立，致力于利用最有前景、最先进的工具加速科学知识和突破，以支持地球的繁荣发展。该组织优先考虑具有影响力的研究领域，包括人工智能与先进计算、天体物理学、生物科学、气候和空间，并通过其科学系统计划支持各学科的研究人员。

Open Source for Science Fund, a program of Renaissance Philanthropy. This is a time-bound philanthropic fund at Renaissance Philanthropy sustaining and evolving the open-source software stack that underpins science.

科学开源基金（Open Source for Science Fund），这是 Renaissance Philanthropy 的一个项目。这是一个在 Renaissance Philanthropy 设立的有时限的慈善基金，旨在维持和发展支撑科学的开源软件栈。

The US National Science Foundation. This $9 billion federal agency established by Congress in 1950 promotes the progress of science, advances national health and defense, and funds competitive research and education, accounting for about 25% of federal support for basic research at US colleges and universities.

美国国家科学基金会（The US National Science Foundation）。该机构由美国国会于 1950 年成立，拥有 90 亿美元预算，旨在促进科学进步、增进国家健康与国防，并资助竞争性研究和教育，约占美国高校基础研究联邦支持总额的 25%。

CAS, a division of the American Chemical Society. For more than a century, CAS has indexed the world’s published chemistry, and it maintains the CAS Registry®, the standard system for identifying chemical substances.

美国化学学会下属的 CAS。一个多世纪以来，CAS 一直在为全球已发表的化学研究建立索引，并维护着用于识别化学物质的标准系统——CAS Registry®。

### Institutions
Leading research institutions produce some of the world’s most important scientific advances, data, instruments, and tools. These institutional partners will use Claude credits inside their organizations to enable scientific discovery:

### 研究机构
领先的研究机构产出了世界上一些最重要的科学进展、数据、仪器和工具。这些机构合作伙伴将在其组织内部使用 Claude 额度，以促进科学发现：

Space Telescope Science Institute. STScI runs science operations for NASA’s Hubble, James Webb, and Roman space telescopes. Managed by the Association of Universities for Research in Astronomy (AURA), STScI also holds one of the largest astronomy data archives used by scientists worldwide.

太空望远镜科学研究所（Space Telescope Science Institute）。STScI 负责 NASA 哈勃、詹姆斯·韦伯和罗曼太空望远镜的科学运营。在大学天文研究协会（AURA）的管理下，STScI 还拥有全球科学家使用的最大的天文数据档案库之一。

Wellcome Sanger Institute. One of the world’s leading genomics centers, it generates genomic and functional data at scale, with work that spans human genetics, biodiversity, and drug development.

惠康桑格研究所（Wellcome Sanger Institute）。作为全球领先的基因组学中心之一，它大规模生成基因组和功能数据，研究工作涵盖人类遗传学、生物多样性和药物开发。

The Francis Crick Institute. Based in London, it’s one of Europe’s largest biomedical research institutes, with more than 100 research groups studying the biology behind health and disease.

弗朗西斯·克里克研究所（The Francis Crick Institute）。该研究所位于伦敦，是欧洲最大的生物医学研究机构之一，拥有 100 多个研究小组，致力于研究健康与疾病背后的生物学机制。

Imperial College London, Department of Life Sciences. One of Europe’s largest life science departments, it conducts research ranging from the structure of single molecules to whole ecosystems, with a focus on health, disease, and climate resilience.

伦敦帝国理工学院生命科学系（Imperial College London, Department of Life Sciences）。作为欧洲最大的生命科学系之一，它开展的研究范围从单分子结构到整个生态系统，重点关注健康、疾病和气候适应力。

EMBL. The European Molecular Biology Laboratory is Europe’s intergovernmental life science laboratory, with six sites across the continent delivering both research and services across experimental and computational approaches in biology. EMBL’s European Bioinformatics Institute (EMBL-EBI) hosts a number of key open data resources that biologists use every day, such as Ensembl, Protein Data Bank in Europe, and Europe PMC.

欧洲分子生物学实验室（EMBL）。这是欧洲的政府间生命科学实验室，在欧洲大陆设有六个站点，提供生物学实验和计算方法方面的研究与服务。EMBL 的欧洲生物信息学研究所（EMBL-EBI）托管着许多生物学家每天使用的关键开放数据资源，例如 Ensembl、欧洲蛋白质数据库（Protein Data Bank in Europe）和 Europe PMC。

Gladstone Institutes. An independent biomedical research institute in San Francisco, it integrates AI and data science across its work on cardiology, neurology, infectious diseases, cancer, and autoimmune diseases.

格拉德斯通研究所（Gladstone Institutes）。这是一家位于旧金山的独立生物医学研究机构，在其心脏病学、神经学、传染病、癌症和自身免疫性疾病的研究工作中整合了人工智能和数据科学。
