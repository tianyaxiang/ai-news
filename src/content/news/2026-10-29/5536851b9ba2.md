Adam Mosseri says negativity around AI is ‘going to get worse’

亚当·莫塞里（Adam Mosseri）表示，围绕人工智能的负面情绪“将会变得更糟”。

The Instagram boss said that worries about AI come down to ‘a fear of being left behind in some way.’

这位 Instagram 的负责人表示，对人工智能的担忧归根结底是“一种以某种方式被时代抛弃的恐惧”。

Asked about AI and the trend of college students booing tech CEOs at graduation ceremonies, Instagram head Adam Mosseri acknowledged in an interview that a lot of people really don’t like AI, and it’s not just young people and creatives.

在被问及人工智能以及大学生在毕业典礼上对科技公司首席执行官发出嘘声的趋势时，Instagram 负责人亚当·莫塞里在采访中承认，很多人确实不喜欢人工智能，而且这不仅仅局限于年轻人和创意工作者。

Appearing on the Colin and Samir podcast to talk about the launch of widescreen and long-form video for Instagram, Mosseri said he thinks there’s a trend of “more anxiety and more frustration and more anger about AI,” particularly in creative industries: “It’s not only getting worse, it’s going to continue to get worse. I think we’re going to see more frustration and anger, some of it founded, some of it unfounded, over the next couple of years, and that’s going to make it hard for a lot of people, not just in the industry.”

莫塞里在参加 Colin and Samir 播客节目讨论 Instagram 推出宽屏和长视频功能时表示，他认为目前存在一种“对人工智能感到更加焦虑、沮丧和愤怒”的趋势，尤其是在创意产业中：“这不仅在恶化，而且还会继续恶化。我认为在未来几年里，我们将看到更多的沮丧和愤怒，其中一些是有根据的，另一些则是毫无根据的，这将使很多人感到困难，不仅仅是在这个行业内。”

Mosseri claimed Instagram doesn’t rank AI creators differently, but raised the question of ranking quality, saying, “There’s a lot of great AI content out there, there’s also a lot of AI slop. Do we rank AI content as well as we rank non-AI content? Probably not.” Talking about AI slop again after previously bringing it up in a New Year’s slideshow dump, he said he thinks the term AI “slop” is used “too liberally.”

莫塞里声称 Instagram 并未对人工智能创作者进行区别排名，但他提出了质量排名的问题，并表示：“市面上有很多很棒的人工智能内容，但也有很多垃圾内容。我们对人工智能内容的排名是否和非人工智能内容一样好？可能不是。”在继此前的新年幻灯片总结中提到“AI 垃圾内容”（AI slop）后，他再次谈及此话题，并表示他认为“AI 垃圾内容”这个词被“滥用得太厉害了”。

Instagram began cracking down on AI-generated profiles in August, after launching a label for AI accounts earlier this year, but misleading AI content like celebrity deepfakes remains an issue. Earlier this month, Mosseri said Instagram is “actively working out what to do” about AI fakes, but he noted, “I don’t think it’s practical for us to try and ban AI content from the platform.”

继今年早些时候为人工智能账户推出标签后，Instagram 于 8 月开始打击人工智能生成的个人资料，但诸如名人深度伪造（deepfakes）等误导性人工智能内容仍然是一个问题。本月早些时候，莫塞里表示 Instagram 正在“积极研究如何处理”人工智能造假问题，但他指出：“我认为试图禁止平台上的所有人工智能内容是不切实际的。”

Mosseri said Instagram is “now starting to require AI accounts to label themselves, because we think it’s important that people know if what they’re looking at is real or not real.” He also acknowledged there are fears around AI, including from creators: “I think people are concerned about a lot of things. I think they’re concerned about owning their content and models getting trained on their content. I think they’re concerned— there’s people who are concerned about safety. They’re concerned about job displacement, having AI displace whatever it is they do, if you’re a content creator or an engineer or a mathematician. I think there’s concerns about— I mean, fundamentally what I think it comes down to when you really have, like, a heart-to-heart with someone is a fear of being left behind in some way.”

莫塞里表示，Instagram “现在开始要求人工智能账户进行自我标注，因为我们认为让人们知道他们看到的内容是真是假非常重要。”他还承认人们对人工智能存在恐惧，包括创作者：“我认为人们担心很多事情。我认为他们担心自己的内容所有权，以及模型在他们的内容上进行训练。我认为他们担心——有些人担心安全问题。他们担心工作被取代，担心人工智能取代他们所做的任何事情，无论你是内容创作者、工程师还是数学家。我认为存在一些担忧——我的意思是，从根本上讲，当你真正与某人进行深入交谈时，我认为归根结底就是一种以某种方式被时代抛弃的恐惧。”
