AI labs are buying data from dead startups to train their models When Keelan Miskell began winding down his crypto startup, he didn’t think its data was worth much. But he’s since turned the company’s digital archives into tens of thousands of dollars, selling its codebase and workspace data. He’s part of a growing market in which companies that help shut down startups sell their data to AI labs. Demand has grown as training labs hit the limits of public and synthetic datasets, with buyers seeking real-world records to help train AI agents. But the sales raise legal and ethical questions. Read the full story on the AI labs buying up the remains of failed startups. —Matthew King

人工智能实验室正在购买倒闭初创公司的数据来训练模型。当基兰·米斯凯尔（Keelan Miskell）开始关闭他的加密货币初创公司时，他并不认为其数据有多大价值。但此后，他通过出售公司的代码库和工作空间数据，将这些数字档案变成了数万美元。他所处的市场正在增长，专门协助关闭初创公司的企业会将数据出售给人工智能实验室。随着训练实验室达到公共和合成数据集的极限，买家寻求真实世界的记录来帮助训练人工智能代理，需求随之增长。但这些销售引发了法律和伦理问题。阅读关于人工智能实验室收购失败初创公司遗留资产的完整报道。——马修·金（Matthew King）

Are we there yet? Interspecies communication Humans are far from the only species with a gift for gab, and our furry, feathered, and finned friends could have a lot to tell us. Decoding how animals communicate with one another could reveal the inner lives of other species—and give humans a window onto how they see the world. A growing arsenal of devices and advances in AI have made it easier to study their howls, squeaks, and clicks, but making sense of it all remains complex. Find out how close we are to interspecies communication. —Cassandra Willyard

我们到了吗？跨物种交流。人类远非唯一拥有交流天赋的物种，我们毛茸茸、长羽毛和长鳍的朋友们可能有很多话要告诉我们。解码动物之间如何交流可以揭示其他物种的内心世界，并为人类提供一个观察它们如何看待世界的窗口。越来越多的设备和人工智能的进步使得研究它们的嚎叫、吱吱声和咔哒声变得更加容易，但要理解这一切仍然很复杂。了解我们距离跨物种交流还有多远。——卡桑德拉·威利亚德（Cassandra Willyard）

Narrated: what it would take to build a city on the moon The US and China are racing to build bases on the moon, with NASA hoping to establish a small lunar city by the end of the century. But the moon is a tremendously hostile place to set up camp. A sprawling lunar base must be safe for its inhabitants, protected from falling asteroids, endless cosmic radiation, razor-sharp lunar dust, and torturous temperature swings. It also must be somewhat self-sustaining. That will require autonomous robots, nuclear power, and a way to use the moon’s resources to keep the base operating. But the chief obstacle may be one of political will.
