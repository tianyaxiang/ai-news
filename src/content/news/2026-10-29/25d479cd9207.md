In Brief Posted: 3:47 PM PDT · October 28, 2026 Image Credits:Apple Lauren Forristal Apple’s touchscreen MacBook Pro pushes laptop pricing to a new extreme Apple has officially entered the touchscreen MacBook Pro era, and apparently, it wants to drain your wallet while it’s at it.

简讯发布于：太平洋夏令时间 2026 年 10 月 28 日下午 3:47 图片来源：Apple Lauren Forristal 苹果的触摸屏 MacBook Pro 将笔记本电脑价格推向了新的极端。苹果已正式进入触摸屏 MacBook Pro 时代，显然，它在进入的同时也想掏空你的钱包。

The new touchscreen MacBook Pro starts at $2,899, which is already a hefty price tag in this economy. But if you want the absolute top-of-the-line model with every possible upgrade, prepare to cough up around $14,549.

新款触摸屏 MacBook Pro 起售价为 2,899 美元，在当前的经济环境下，这已经是一个相当昂贵的价格。但如果你想要配备所有可能升级的顶级型号，准备好支付约 14,549 美元。

As MacRumors first reported, Apple’s configurator prices the fully loaded 14-inch model at $14,249 and the 16-inch version at $14,549. Both come equipped with Apple’s top-tier M5 Max chip, featuring an 18-core CPU and 40-core GPU.

正如 MacRumors 最先报道的那样，苹果的配置器显示，顶配版 14 英寸机型售价为 14,249 美元，16 英寸版本售价为 14,549 美元。两者均配备了苹果顶级的 M5 Max 芯片，拥有 18 核 CPU 和 40 核 GPU。

You can blame the ongoing memory shortage and AI’s seemingly endless appetite for hardware for some of the pricing pressure. Moving to a 4TB SSD costs an additional $1,000, while upgrading to 8TB adds $3,000. Want the new 16TB option? That’ll set you back another $7,000.

你可以将部分价格压力归咎于持续的内存短缺以及人工智能对硬件看似无止境的需求。升级到 4TB 固态硬盘需要额外支付 1,000 美元，而升级到 8TB 则需增加 3,000 美元。想要新的 16TB 选项吗？那将再花费你 7,000 美元。

Another $2,000 gets you 128GB of memory, while nano-texture glass adds $150. For some perspective, the previous 16-inch MacBook Pro topped out at $10,149. The new model raises that ceiling by a whopping $4,400.

再花 2,000 美元可以获得 128GB 内存，而纳米纹理玻璃则需增加 150 美元。作为参考，上一代 16 英寸 MacBook Pro 的最高售价为 10,149 美元。新款机型将这一上限提高了惊人的 4,400 美元。

Absurd prices aside, the launch marks a significant shift for Apple, nearly a decade after it first experimented with the Touch Bar. The company eventually abandoned that feature, but now it’s ready to embrace a touchscreen MacBook Pro. Preorders open October 30, with availability beginning November 6.

抛开荒谬的价格不谈，这次发布标志着苹果的一次重大转变，距离它首次尝试 Touch Bar 已经过去了近十年。该公司最终放弃了该功能，但现在它已准备好拥抱触摸屏 MacBook Pro。预购于 10 月 30 日开启，11 月 6 日正式发售。
