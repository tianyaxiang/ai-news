Laptops with OLED screens aren’t particularly special. They’ve been around for a decade. You can currently buy a touchscreen OLED machine for as little as $700, and even the iPad Pro has had an OLED screen since 2024.

配备 OLED 屏幕的笔记本电脑并不稀奇，它们已经存在十年了。目前，你花 700 美元就能买到一台触控 OLED 笔记本电脑，甚至 iPad Pro 自 2024 年起也采用了 OLED 屏幕。

So, why did Apple wait over a decade to release its own touchscreen OLED MacBook Pro? The simple answer for the long wait is that the company wanted to do it “the Apple way.” But in this case, “the Apple way” involves somehow cranking up the display to 2,000 nits, solving both the brightness and glare problems that OLED laptops have always suffered from.

那么，为什么苹果花了十多年时间才发布自己的触控 OLED MacBook Pro？对于漫长的等待，简单的回答是苹果公司想以“苹果的方式”来做。但在这种情况下，“苹果的方式”意味着要设法将显示屏亮度提升至 2000 尼特，从而解决 OLED 笔记本电脑一直以来存在的亮度和眩光问题。

Having seen the thing with my own eyes at a media event today in Los Angeles, I can tell you that this screen is the real deal.

今天在洛杉矶的媒体活动上，我亲眼见证了这款产品，我可以告诉你，这块屏幕确实名不虚传。

Apple is using a tandem OLED panel, the same layer-stacking technology used in the OLED iPad Pro. The real innovation here, however, is that the panel has been optimized to hit a new peak brightness of 2,000 nits, and that’s a result that matters.

苹果使用的是串联式 OLED 面板，这与 OLED iPad Pro 中使用的层叠技术相同。然而，真正的创新在于该面板经过优化，达到了 2000 尼特的新峰值亮度，这是一个至关重要的成果。

That big number is meaningless on its own, but here’s the context. Nits are the standard measure of screen brightness used across the consumer tech industry. A cheap OLED laptop maxes out around 300 nits. A higher-end OLED laptop might get you up to 1,000 nits of brightness—but only in brighter and more colorful High Dynamic Range content like specific streaming apps, YouTube videos, and games.

单看这个数字本身毫无意义，但结合背景来看就不同了。尼特是消费电子行业衡量屏幕亮度的标准单位。廉价 OLED 笔记本电脑的最高亮度约为 300 尼特。高端 OLED 笔记本电脑可能达到 1000 尼特，但这仅限于在特定的流媒体应用、YouTube 视频和游戏等更明亮、色彩更丰富的高动态范围（HDR）内容中。

In other words, the vast majority of the time you’re using an OLED, it’ll feel like a dimmer screen than normal. The iPad Pro can get up to 1,600 nits for HDR content, and 1,000 nits for the less bright, less colorful Standard Dynamic Range content. That’s closer.

换句话说，在绝大多数使用 OLED 的时间里，你会觉得屏幕比平时更暗。iPad Pro 在显示 HDR 内容时最高可达 1600 尼特，而在亮度较低、色彩较少的标准动态范围（SDR）内容中则为 1000 尼特。这已经接近了。

But this MacBook Pro’s screen is on an entirely different level. It’s a ground-up redesign of the tandem OLED layer arrangement in the iPad Pro, even down to the manufacturing process. And the result is that it can blast your eyes with 2,000 nits of brightness both in SDR and HDR.

但这款 MacBook Pro 的屏幕处于完全不同的水平。它是对 iPad Pro 串联式 OLED 层排列的彻底重新设计，甚至深入到了制造工艺层面。其结果是，它在 SDR 和 HDR 模式下都能以 2000 尼特的亮度冲击你的视觉。

That means whether you’re looking at some generic wallpaper, a website, or a HDR-compatible movie, you’ll feel that extra brightness. That’s why, I assume, Apple gave it the name of PROLED XDR. In a world where the words “OLED” and “HDR” don’t necessarily indicate how good the experience is, more weighty jargon is needed.

这意味着无论你是看普通的壁纸、网页还是兼容 HDR 的电影，都能感受到那种额外的亮度。我想这就是苹果将其命名为 PROLED XDR 的原因。在这个“OLED”和“HDR”字眼并不一定代表体验优劣的世界里，需要更具分量的术语。

As with all tech, bigger numbers don’t always add up to a noticeable difference for the user. But when it comes to HDR performance, we’ve been waiting a long time for OLED to catch up.

和所有技术一样，更大的数字并不总是意味着用户能感受到明显的差异。但在 HDR 性能方面，我们已经等待 OLED 追赶上来很久了。

HDR, or High Dynamic Range, serves as a measure of color depth, brightness, and contrast—and in the past, this has always involved a trade-off. An OLED screen can turn each individual pixel any color, and it can turn off individual pixels in the display. This allows OLED to have great color control and unbeatable contrast, since when you turn a pixel off, it renders absolutely black.

HDR（高动态范围）是衡量色彩深度、亮度和对比度的标准，过去这总是伴随着权衡。OLED 屏幕可以将每个像素点显示为任何颜色，也可以关闭显示屏中的单个像素。这使得 OLED 具有出色的色彩控制和无与伦比的对比度，因为当像素关闭时，它呈现的是纯粹的黑色。

But OLEDs couldn’t get super bright, and other screen tech like Mini-LED could get much brighter, which is why Apple stuck with Mini-LED for years, using it on the MacBook Pro and Studio Display XDR. This technology allowed Apple to have the laptops with the best HDR performance out there.

但 OLED 无法达到极高的亮度，而 Mini-LED 等其他屏幕技术可以更亮，这就是为什么苹果多年来一直坚持在 MacBook Pro 和 Studio Display XDR 上使用 Mini-LED 的原因。这项技术使苹果能够拥有市场上 HDR 性能最好的笔记本电脑。

With the 2026 MacBook Pro, that compromise no longer exists. This OLED screen has both the brightness and contrast in its pocket, and you can see it.

随着 2026 款 MacBook Pro 的推出，这种妥协已不复存在。这款 OLED 屏幕同时兼顾了亮度和对比度，而且效果显而易见。

An OLED panel has lots of more benefits over conventional backlighting beyond just contrast. Mini-LED struggles with an effect called blooming, where bright parts of images bleed light to the surrounding area, which is where lots of detail is lost in the image. OLED completely fixes that, giving each pixel a much wider range of control (up to completely turning off).

除了对比度之外，OLED 面板相比传统背光技术还有许多其他优势。Mini-LED 存在一种称为“光晕”的效应，即图像的明亮部分会将光线溢出到周围区域，导致图像丢失大量细节。OLED 完全解决了这个问题，为每个像素提供了更广泛的控制范围（甚至可以完全关闭）。

As part of the Apple event I attended, we were shown a selection of HDR images with lots of contrast, a number of which had fields of darkness at the edges that made it look like the image simply disappeared into the bezel. Very pretty. We also watched a darker scene in the show Widow’s Bay in HDR, which was an excellent showcase of the range of detail this screen can show.

在我参加的苹果活动中，我们观看了几张对比度极高的 HDR 图像，其中一些图像边缘有大片黑暗区域，看起来就像图像直接融入了边框一样，非常漂亮。我们还观看了《Widow’s Bay》中一个较暗的 HDR 场景，这极好地展示了该屏幕所能呈现的细节范围。

The new screen helps this MacBook stay exceedingly light, as well. Much of the savings in weight comes from the OLED design, since the screen uses thinner and lighter materials than screens past. Despite the computer using the same aluminum chassis as the previous MacBook Pro, the new 14-inch MacBook Pro weighs just three pounds, which is 12 percent lighter than before.

新屏幕也让这款 MacBook 保持了极轻的重量。大部分重量的减轻归功于 OLED 设计，因为该屏幕使用了比以往屏幕更薄、更轻的材料。尽管电脑采用了与上一代 MacBook Pro 相同的铝制机身，但新款 14 英寸 MacBook Pro 的重量仅为 3 磅，比之前轻了 12%。

To put the new screen into context, we were also shown a comparison between Apple’s OLED and the OLED of a high-end competitive Windows laptop. The difference was striking, especially in off-axis viewing. At its worst, there were visible rainbow effects on the Windows machine’s screen.

为了更好地理解这块新屏幕，我们还观看了苹果 OLED 与一款高端 Windows 竞品笔记本电脑 OLED 的对比。差异非常显著，尤其是在侧视角度下。最糟糕的是，Windows 机器的屏幕上出现了明显的彩虹纹效应。

There’s a catch, though. While Apple’s screen can get up to 2,000 nits, it’s normally capped at 1,000 nits. When the laptop’s built-in adaptive brightness sensor thinks you need a boost, the screen’s brightens increases up to 2,000 nits. So the brightness only hits the high end when you’re outside or working under really harsh lighting conditions. The previous-gen MacBook Pros also did this, but to a lesser degree. With this machine, you’re getting 400 more nits of SDR brightness right out of the box, with the ability to crank it up to twice as much.

不过，这里有个前提。虽然苹果的屏幕最高可达 2000 尼特，但通常限制在 1000 尼特。当笔记本电脑内置的自适应亮度传感器认为你需要增强亮度时，屏幕亮度才会提升至 2000 尼特。因此，只有在户外或在光线非常强烈的环境下工作时，亮度才会达到最高值。上一代 MacBook Pro 也有类似功能，但程度较轻。而在这款机器上，你开箱即可获得比以往多 400 尼特的 SDR 亮度，并有能力将其提升至两倍。

Glare is another issue with conventional, low-brightness OLED laptops that the MacBook Pro improves. When deeper blacks are rendered on the screen, it often results in harsher reflections. Sometimes it can get so bad, it feels like you’re looking into a mirror. The brightness alone on the MacBook Pro goes a long way toward overpowering glare and reflections, but Apple is also using what it claims is a “state-of-the-art” anti-reflective coating. Almost every laptop screen uses some kind antireflective coating, so one can hope that Apple’s “state-of-the-art” coating is at least on par with those, but I can attest that this is the very best I’ve seen.

眩光是传统低亮度 OLED 笔记本电脑的另一个问题，而 MacBook Pro 对此进行了改进。当屏幕呈现更深邃的黑色时，往往会导致更强烈的反射。有时情况会变得很糟糕，感觉就像在照镜子。MacBook Pro 的亮度本身在克服眩光和反射方面发挥了很大作用，但苹果还使用了其声称“最先进”的防反射涂层。几乎每台笔记本电脑屏幕都使用某种防反射涂层，所以人们可以期待苹果的“最先进”涂层至少能与它们持平，但我可以证明这是我见过最好的。
