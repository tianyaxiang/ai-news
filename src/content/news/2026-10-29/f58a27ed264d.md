# Apple charges $14,549 for a fully specced touchscreen MacBook Pro

苹果公司为顶配版触控屏 MacBook Pro 定价 14,549 美元。

The maximum configuration is $4,400 more expensive than a fully specced version of the last MacBook Pro.

该最高配置版本比上一代 MacBook Pro 的顶配版贵了 4,400 美元。

With an OLED display and touchscreen capabilities, it’s no surprise that the new MacBook Pro Apple announced today is more expensive than the MBP announced earlier this year. But upgrading the base models that start at $2,899 for the 14-inch, and $3,399 for the 16-inch, with every premium hardware option that Apple offers pushes those prices to an eye-watering $14,249 and $14,549, respectively.

得益于 OLED 显示屏和触控功能，苹果今天发布的新款 MacBook Pro 比今年早些时候发布的机型更贵也就不足为奇了。然而，如果将起售价分别为 2,899 美元（14 英寸）和 3,399 美元（16 英寸）的基础款升级到苹果提供的所有高端硬件选项，其价格将分别飙升至令人咋舌的 14,249 美元和 14,549 美元。

Both of those premium configurations include the M5 Max chip with an 18-core CPU and 40-core GPU, $150 worth of nano-texture glass, 128GB of memory that adds an extra $2,000, and 16TB of storage which is the real budget breaker here thanks to the ongoing memory shortage.

这两款高端配置均包含配备 18 核 CPU 和 40 核 GPU 的 M5 Max 芯片、价值 150 美元的纳米纹理玻璃、增加 2,000 美元的 128GB 内存，以及由于持续的存储芯片短缺而成为真正“预算杀手”的 16TB 存储空间。

Maximizing the configuration of the previous 16-inch version of the MacBook Pro topped out at $10,149, according to MacRumors, but today’s $14,549 price is a result of the 16TB storage option that Apple now offers which adds an extra $7,000 to the new MacBook Pro’s price. If you only upgrade the new 16-inch model to 8TB of storage, a $3,000 option, the fully specced price is $10,549, or $400 more expensive than its predecessor.

据 MacRumors 报道，上一代 16 英寸 MacBook Pro 的顶配价格最高为 10,149 美元，而今天 14,549 美元的价格主要是因为苹果现在提供了 16TB 存储选项，这为新款 MacBook Pro 增加了 7,000 美元的成本。如果仅将新款 16 英寸机型升级到 8TB 存储（该选项需 3,000 美元），其顶配价格则为 10,549 美元，仅比前代产品贵 400 美元。
