AI breakthroughs in robotics won’t change your life any time soon. The hype around humanoid robots is reaching fever pitch. Much of it comes from the idea that the same AI advances behind ChatGPT and Claude could enable robots to imitate human movement the way chatbots imitate human language. But many researchers are skeptical. They argue that such assumptions minimize the challenges of using an intelligence built on language and images to master the infinite variability of the physical world. They also point out that conflating humanoids with so-called generalist machines is misleading. Across robotics labs, tensions have emerged over whether current forms of AI are all that’s needed to perfect all-purpose humanoids or whether an entirely new path is required. Read the full story on what it will take to build truly general-purpose robots. —Jamie Condliffe

人工智能在机器人领域的突破短期内不会改变你的生活。围绕人形机器人的炒作正达到狂热的程度。很大程度上源于这样一种观点：ChatGPT 和 Claude 背后的 AI 进步，能够让机器人像聊天机器人模仿人类语言那样模仿人类动作。但许多研究人员对此持怀疑态度。他们认为，这种假设低估了利用建立在语言和图像基础上的智能来掌握物理世界无限变异性的挑战。他们还指出，将人形机器人与所谓的通用机器混为一谈具有误导性。在各大机器人实验室中，关于当前的 AI 形式是否足以完善通用人形机器人，还是需要一条全新的路径，各方已出现分歧。阅读关于构建真正通用机器人所需条件的完整报道。——Jamie Condliffe

10 Climate Tech Companies to Watch: WaveSave and its portable rubber dam. WaveSave is one of MIT Technology Review’s 10 Climate Tech Companies to Watch 2026, available exclusively to subscribers. As sea levels rise and flood risks worsen, WaveSave has emerged with an ingeniously simple tool: a portable rubber dam that’s quick to install when flooding starts. Anyone can unfurl the SlamDam on the ground along a riverbank or shoreline and pump it full of water until it swells into place. And there it stands, up to 1.3 meters high, shielding nearby property or structures from damage as waters rise. WaveSave now plans to take its unusual approach to flood protection much further. —Amy Nordrum
