# US plans livestream of execution by firing squad

美国计划直播行刑队处决

The planned execution of Nidal Hasan will be ‘public,’ Pete Hegseth said in an interview. The United States’ execution of the Fort Hood shooter will be livestreamed, anonymous officials from the Defense Department told the BBC and Associated Press.

皮特·赫格塞斯（Pete Hegseth）在接受采访时表示，尼达尔·哈桑（Nidal Hasan）的计划处决将是“公开的”。据英国广播公司（BBC）和美联社报道，国防部匿名官员称，美国将对这名胡德堡枪击案凶手进行直播处决。

The planned execution of Nidal Hasan, the former US Army major convicted of killing 13 people at the Texas military base, was announced this week. He is scheduled to be executed by firing squad on December 3rd at 2PM ET.

本周宣布了对尼达尔·哈桑的处决计划，他曾是美国陆军少校，因在德克萨斯州军事基地杀害13人而被定罪。他定于美国东部时间12月3日下午2点被行刑队处决。

The Verge contacted YouTube, Twitch, TikTok, Meta, Rumble, and Kick to ask if they plan to allow the livestream to be aired on their platforms. None responded before publication.

《The Verge》联系了YouTube、Twitch、TikTok、Meta、Rumble和Kick，询问他们是否计划允许在其平台上播放此次直播。截至发稿时，没有任何平台做出回应。

“We’ll make sure that people are able to watch it, that it’s public, because people need to understand that there’s serious consequences for these types of things,” US Defense Secretary Pete Hegseth said in an interview with Real America’s Voice.

美国国防部长皮特·赫格塞斯在接受“Real America’s Voice”采访时表示：“我们将确保人们能够观看，确保它是公开的，因为人们需要明白，这类事情会带来严重的后果。”
