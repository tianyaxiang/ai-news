# Watch the trailer for ‘The Altruists,’ Netflix’s show about the FTX scandal

It’s a great time to be alive if you follow tech news and love messy drama. We’re not just getting a Nathan Fielder documentary about Elizabeth Holmes this fall. Netflix’s “The Altruists,” which follows Sam Bankman-Fried and Caroline Ellison through the scandal that crushed FTX and landed them in prison, is coming out on November 19. It’s just in time for Thanksgiving, assuming that another infamous Sam doesn’t ruin our holiday plans again. Here’s the trailer, starring Anthony Boyle as Sam Bankman-Fried and Julia Garner as Caroline Ellison:

如果你关注科技新闻又喜欢混乱的戏剧性事件，现在正是最好的时代。今年秋天，我们不仅能看到内森·菲尔德（Nathan Fielder）关于伊丽莎白·霍姆斯（Elizabeth Holmes）的纪录片，Netflix 的《利他主义者》也将于 11 月 19 日上线。该剧讲述了萨姆·班克曼-弗里德（Sam Bankman-Fried）和卡罗琳·埃里森（Caroline Ellison）经历导致 FTX 崩溃并使他们入狱的丑闻。这正好赶上感恩节，前提是别再有另一个臭名昭著的“萨姆”来破坏我们的假期计划。以下是预告片，由安东尼·鲍伊（Anthony Boyle）饰演萨姆·班克曼-弗里德，朱莉娅·加纳（Julia Garner）饰演卡罗琳·埃里森：

We can’t say we ever expected that the ethical disconnects behind effective altruism were going to pique the interest of Hollywood, but hopefully, Netflix will do the strangeness of this story justice. “From the very beginning, I was so fascinated by both Sam’s and Caroline’s real stories,” co-showrunner Graham Moore said in an interview with Netflix this summer. “Those are both characters I could not have possibly made up had I tried — I’m not nearly a good enough writer to create characters as fascinating as either Sam Bankman-Fried or Caroline Ellison.”

我们从未预料到“有效利他主义”背后的道德脱节会引起好莱坞的兴趣，但希望 Netflix 能公正地呈现这个故事的离奇之处。联合剧集主管格雷厄姆·摩尔（Graham Moore）在今年夏天接受 Netflix 采访时表示：“从一开始，我就对萨姆和卡罗琳的真实故事着迷。这两个角色即使我想编也编不出来——我的写作水平远不足以创造出像萨姆·班克曼-弗里德或卡罗琳·埃里森那样迷人的角色。”
