On Thursday, at the Golden Age of American Innovation Summit, President Trump awarded Elon Musk, Jensen Huang, Sergey Brin, and AMD’s Lisa Su the National Medal of Science, the nation’s top science prize. Dell Technologies’ Michael Dell and Microsoft’s Satya Nadella also received the National Medal of Technology and Innovation.

周四，在“美国创新黄金时代峰会”上，特朗普总统向埃隆·马斯克（Elon Musk）、黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）、谢尔盖·布林（Sergey Brin）以及 AMD 公司的苏姿丰（Lisa Su）颁发了国家科学奖章，这是该国最高科学奖项。戴尔科技公司的迈克尔·戴尔（Michael Dell）和微软公司的萨提亚·纳德拉（Satya Nadella）也获得了国家技术与创新奖章。

Together, the awardees have donated nearly $6 billion to efforts tied to Trump and his administration, according to The New Republic. Alphabet and Microsoft, for example, donated to the president’s inauguration fund and White House ballroom. AMD gave to a pro-Trump Super PAC, and Michael and Susan Dell pledged $6.25 billion to help fund the Trump Accounts, the administration’s savings program for children.

据《新共和》杂志报道，这些获奖者总共向与特朗普及其政府相关的项目捐赠了近 60 亿美元。例如，Alphabet 和微软曾向总统就职基金和白宫宴会厅捐款。AMD 向一个支持特朗普的超级政治行动委员会（Super PAC）捐款，迈克尔·戴尔和苏珊·戴尔夫妇则承诺捐赠 62.5 亿美元，用于资助特朗普账户（Trump Accounts），这是该政府为儿童设立的储蓄计划。

Trump said the honorees had helped “pioneer an incredible future for America,” and that because of them, “we will always be on top. No one else can compete with us.” Nadella received his medal right after the administration indefinitely suspended Microsoft from the H-1B program, which lets U.S. companies hire skilled foreign workers.

特朗普表示，这些获奖者帮助“为美国开创了一个不可思议的未来”，并称因为他们，“我们将永远处于领先地位。没有其他人能与我们竞争。”就在纳德拉获得奖章后不久，政府无限期暂停了微软参与 H-1B 签证计划的资格，该计划允许美国公司聘用外籍技术工人。
