Apple will reportedly debut its first touchscreen MacBook in three weeks.

据报道，苹果公司将在三周内推出其首款触屏 MacBook。

The rumored late-October event will also include a new iPad Mini, Bloomberg reports.

据彭博社报道，传闻中将于 10 月下旬举行的发布会还将包括新款 iPad Mini。

Apple is set to introduce a new MacBook Pro with a touchscreen and an updated iPad Mini “on or around” October 27th, Bloomberg reports. If true, that would put the event just two weeks after the October 13th event Apple announced today, which is rumored to focus on smart home products.

据彭博社报道，苹果公司计划在 10 月 27 日“前后”推出配备触控屏的新款 MacBook Pro 和更新版 iPad Mini。如果属实，此次发布会距离苹果今天宣布的 10 月 13 日发布会仅两周之隔，而 10 月 13 日的活动据传将聚焦于智能家居产品。

Bloomberg says that the late October event will include an “online video presentation” and an “in-person component for the press,” which it reports is similar to what Apple is planning for the October 13th event.

彭博社称，10 月下旬的发布会将包括“在线视频演示”和“媒体线下参与环节”，据报道，这与苹果为 10 月 13 日活动所做的计划类似。

At this Mac and iPad event, Bloomberg reports that Apple will announce new MacBook Pros that are lighter than what’s available now, have touchscreen OLED displays, and iPhone-like Dynamic Island interfaces. Apple is also apparently planning to announce MacBook Pros that retain the current design but have the M6 chip introduced with the new Mac Mini. And the iPad Mini, which was last refreshed in 2024, will be updated to shift the front-facing camera onto the landscape side of the tablet and to add a “revamped speaker system,” Bloomberg says.

据彭博社报道，在这次 Mac 和 iPad 发布会上，苹果将宣布推出比现有产品更轻、配备触控 OLED 显示屏以及类似 iPhone“灵动岛”界面的新款 MacBook Pro。苹果显然还计划发布保留当前设计但搭载新款 Mac Mini 所用 M6 芯片的 MacBook Pro。彭博社表示，自 2024 年最后一次更新以来，iPad Mini 也将进行升级，将前置摄像头移至平板电脑的横向侧边，并增加“改进后的扬声器系统”。
