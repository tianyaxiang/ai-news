# Paramount is making a Cyberpunk 2077 film

派拉蒙影业正在制作一部《赛博朋克 2077》电影。

CD Projekt Red’s hit sci-fi franchise is being adapted into a movie, but there’s no word yet on when it might be released.

CD Projekt Red 旗下的热门科幻系列作品正在被改编成电影，但目前尚无关于上映日期的消息。

Cyberpunk 2077 is heading to the big screen. Deadline reports that CD Projekt Red’s popular sci-fi video game franchise is being adapted into a live-action film by Paramount Pictures. There’s no details just yet on when the film might premiere. But Deadline says that Lorenzo di Bonaventura, who is known for producing the Transformers films, will be a producer on the movie.

《赛博朋克 2077》即将登上大银幕。据 Deadline 报道，CD Projekt Red 旗下广受欢迎的科幻游戏系列正由派拉蒙影业改编为真人电影。目前尚无关于电影何时首映的细节，但 Deadline 称，曾制作《变形金刚》系列电影的洛伦佐·迪·博纳文图拉将担任该片的制片人。

“We’re excited about Paramount as a future partner who shares our creative ambitions for the Cyberpunk franchise,” says CD Projekt Red joint-CEO Michał Nowakowski. “We look forward to sharing more details at the appropriate time.”

CD Projekt Red 联席首席执行官米哈乌·诺瓦科夫斯基表示：“我们很高兴能与派拉蒙成为未来的合作伙伴，他们与我们对《赛博朋克》系列有着共同的创作抱负。我们期待在适当的时候分享更多细节。”

Despite its rocky 2020 launch, Cyberpunk 2077 has gone on to become a huge success for CD Projekt Red, with the company recently announcing that the game has surpassed more than 40 million copies sold. The franchise has also been adapted into a hit Netflix anime, and a second season of that show is debuting later this month. A sequel to Cyberpunk 2077, which CD Projekt Red is calling “Cyberpunk 2,” is in development as well, with the studio reporting recently that the game has 184 people working on it.

尽管 2020 年发布时历经波折，《赛博朋克 2077》最终还是为 CD Projekt Red 取得了巨大成功，该公司近期宣布游戏销量已突破 4000 万份。该系列还被改编成了热门的 Netflix 动画剧集，其第二季将于本月晚些时候首播。《赛博朋克 2077》的续作（CD Projekt Red 暂称其为“赛博朋克 2”）也正在开发中，工作室近期报告称该项目目前有 184 人参与开发。

The news of the Cyberpunk 2077 movie is arriving shortly after the completion of Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery’s megamerger under the Skydance name. Paramount is also working on a Call of Duty film for 2028 and the Street Fighter movie debuting on October 16th.

关于《赛博朋克 2077》电影的消息是在派拉蒙与华纳兄弟探索公司以 Skydance 名义完成巨额合并后不久传出的。派拉蒙目前还在筹备一部定于 2028 年上映的《使命召唤》电影，以及将于 10 月 16 日首映的《街头霸王》电影。
