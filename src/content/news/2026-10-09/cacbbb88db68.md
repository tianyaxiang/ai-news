### Roundtables: A Conversation With the Creator of AI-Designed Viruses
### 圆桌会议：与人工智能病毒设计者的对话

Available only for MIT Technology Review subscribers. Friday, October 16, 2026 Can AI design new life forms? In 2025, Stanford University PhD student Samuel King came up with a preliminary answer when he used a generative AI model to propose genetic blueprints for microscopic viruses. It isn’t yet an example of AI-generated life, but that could be next.
仅限《麻省理工科技评论》订阅者阅读。2026年10月16日，星期五。人工智能可以设计新的生命形式吗？2025年，斯坦福大学博士生塞缪尔·金（Samuel King）给出了一个初步答案，他利用生成式人工智能模型提出了微观病毒的基因蓝图。这还不是人工智能生成生命的例子，但下一步可能就是了。

Join senior AI reporter James O'Donnell as he interviews King about his work, being named one of MIT Technology Review's Innovators Under 35 and new ways of seeing biology. Going live on October 16th at 18:30 BST / 1:30pm EDT / 10:30am PDT Register now Speakers: James O'Donnell, AI Reporter, and Samuel King, Bioengineering PhD Candidate, Stanford University/Arc Institute
