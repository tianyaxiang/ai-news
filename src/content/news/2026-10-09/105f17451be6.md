### What a Solo Texas Realtor's Website Actually Needs — and What It Costs in 2026

Originally published at jy-labs.com. A working scope from a current build for a solo agent covering Austin and Houston: the four jobs the site has to do, the Texas compliance items templates get wrong, why IDX is not a day-one decision, and honest 2026 cost ranges.

最初发布于 jy-labs.com。这是一份针对奥斯汀和休斯顿地区独立经纪人的当前网站构建工作范围：网站必须完成的四项任务、模板中常见的德克萨斯州合规性错误、为什么 IDX 不应作为首日决策，以及 2026 年真实的成本范围。

The problem: A solo Texas agent comparing a $59/month template with a $12,000 custom quote cannot tell what the money buys. This post is the scope from a current Austin-and-Houston build, with the client unnamed and no traffic numbers because the site has not launched.

问题所在：一位德克萨斯州的独立经纪人在比较每月 59 美元的模板和 12,000 美元的定制报价时，无法分辨这些钱到底买了什么。本文是当前奥斯汀和休斯顿地区一个构建项目的工作范围，由于网站尚未上线，客户名称保密且没有流量数据。

The approach: The four jobs the site has to do. Most agent websites fail because they were built to look like a brochure. A solo agent's site has four jobs, in this order: Capture a lead from a specific traffic source. Usually a yard sign, an open house flyer, or a business card. Satisfy Texas disclosure requirements on every page. Not optional, not a footer afterthought. Let the agent update listings and open houses without calling the developer. Weekly, from a phone, without breaking the layout. Load fast on a phone on cell data, because that is where the yard-sign scan happens. Everything else — the bio page, the testimonials, the neighborhood guides — is worth doing, but it is worth doing after those four.

方法：网站必须完成的四项任务。大多数经纪人网站失败的原因是它们被设计得像一本宣传册。独立经纪人的网站按优先级顺序有四项任务：从特定流量来源获取潜在客户。通常是庭院招牌、开放参观传单或名片。在每个页面上满足德克萨斯州的披露要求。这不是可选项，也不是页脚的附带考虑。让经纪人无需致电开发人员即可更新房源和开放参观信息。每周通过手机操作，且不破坏布局。在手机使用蜂窝数据时快速加载，因为庭院招牌的扫描就是在这里发生的。其他所有内容——个人简介页面、客户感言、社区指南——都值得做，但应在完成这四项任务之后再做。

The Texas compliance layer is the part templates get wrong. This is the piece that turns a generic website project into a real estate website project, and it is where off-the-shelf themes consistently fall short. TREC requires that every broker and sales agent provide a link on the homepage to the Information About Brokerage Services form, labeled "Texas Real Estate Commission Information About Brokerage Services," in at least a 10 point font, in a readily noticeable place. That is Rule 531.20(b), and the label wording and font size are both specified — a link that says "Disclosures" does not satisfy it.

德克萨斯州的合规层是模板容易出错的地方。这部分内容将一个通用的网站项目转变为房地产网站项目，而现成的模板往往无法达到要求。德克萨斯州房地产委员会（TREC）要求每位经纪人和销售代理必须在主页上提供指向“经纪服务信息”（Information About Brokerage Services）表格的链接，标签必须为“Texas Real Estate Commission Information About Brokerage Services”，字体至少为 10 号，且位于显眼位置。这是第 531.20(b) 条规则，标签措辞和字体大小都有明确规定——仅标注为“披露”（Disclosures）的链接是不符合要求的。

TREC publishes its own guidance on posting the IABS and the Consumer Protection Notice, and it is worth reading directly rather than trusting a theme vendor's checklist. On the build, that meant a compliance block rendered by the theme itself rather than dropped into each page by hand, so it cannot be forgotten on a new page or lost when someone edits a layout. It carries the broker attribution, the license lines, the TREC links using the prescribed labels, Equal Housing Opportunity, and the REALTOR and MLS marks. Nine pages, one source of truth, verified by a script that fails the build if any page is missing it.

TREC 发布了关于发布 IABS 和消费者保护通知的指南，直接阅读这些指南比信任模板供应商的检查清单更有价值。在本次构建中，这意味着合规模块由主题本身渲染，而不是手动放入每个页面，因此在创建新页面或编辑布局时不会被遗忘或丢失。它包含经纪人归属信息、执照行、使用规定标签的 TREC 链接、公平住房机会声明以及 REALTOR 和 MLS 标志。九个页面，一个真实来源，并通过脚本进行验证，如果任何页面缺失这些内容，构建将失败。

Two related items that surprise agents: Your own completed IABS is the document that should be posted, not a re-typeset copy someone made for you. It is your disclosure to make. If you refer clients to an affiliated mortgage business, that is a RESPA matter. A per-transaction Affiliated Business Arrangement Disclosure delivered at referral and a standing notice on the website are two different things, and one does not substitute for the other. On this build, the disclosure wording is the client's to approve with her own counsel, and the page ships with a placeholder until she does. I write the plumbing; I do not write someone's legal disclosures. Nothing in this section is legal advice. It is a list of the things that made it into the scope, and the reason each one is there.

两件让经纪人感到惊讶的相关事项：应该发布的是你自己填写完成的 IABS 文档，而不是别人为你重新排版的副本。这是你必须做出的披露。如果你将客户推荐给关联的抵押贷款业务，这属于《房地产结算程序法》（RESPA）范畴。在推荐时提供的单笔交易关联业务安排披露与网站上的常设通知是两码事，前者不能替代后者。在此次构建中，披露措辞由客户与其法律顾问确认，在确认之前，页面将使用占位符。我负责编写底层架构，不负责编写他人的法律披露内容。本节内容不构成法律建议。这只是进入工作范围的项目列表及其原因。

QR codes need their own landing pages, not your homepage. The single highest-leverage decision on this build cost nothing: the QR codes do not point at the homepage. A scan from an Austin yard sign lands on an Austin page. A Houston flyer lands on a Houston page. Same structure, same compliance footer, market-specific headline and market-specific contact path. The template is built to spin up five of these without new development, so a specific neighborhood, a single open house, or a print campaign can each have its own destination.

二维码需要专属落地页，而不是你的主页。本次构建中杠杆率最高且零成本的决定是：二维码不指向主页。扫描奥斯汀庭院招牌的二维码会跳转到奥斯汀页面，扫描休斯顿传单的二维码则跳转到休斯顿页面。结构相同，合规页脚相同，但标题和联系路径针对特定市场。该模板无需额外开发即可生成五个此类页面，因此特定社区、单次开放参观或印刷广告活动都可以拥有自己的目标页面。

Two reasons this matters more than it sounds like it should. First, relevance: someone standing in front of a house in Cedar Park does not want a statewide value proposition. Second, attribution: separate landing pages tell you which signs and which markets actually produce scans, which is information a single homepage cannot give you.

这比听起来更重要的原因有二。首先是相关性：站在锡达帕克（Cedar Park）某栋房子前的人，并不想看全州范围的价值主张。其次是归因：独立的落地页能告诉你哪些招牌和哪些市场真正产生了扫描，这是单一主页无法提供的信息。

IDX is not a day-one decision, and treating it like one is what blows the budget. Every agent asks about MLS search on the first call. It is the most expensive feature on the list and usually the least urgent, because a solo agent's early leads come from signs, open houses, and referrals — not from a stranger running a property search on a new domain with no traffic.

IDX 不应作为首日决策，将其视为首日决策只会导致预算超支。每位经纪人在第一次通话时都会询问 MLS 搜索功能。这是列表中最昂贵的功能，通常也是最不紧急的，因为独立经纪人的早期潜在客户来自招牌、开放参观和推荐，而不是来自在没有流量的新域名上进行房产搜索的陌生人。

Current monthly costs, if you want a sense of what the feature actually carries: Showcase IDX (WordPress plugin): $94.95–$124.95/mo. No setup fee; MLS pass-through fees run roughly $0–$33/mo depending on your board. Placester: $59–$129/mo. IDX adds about $25/mo per MLS on top. Sierra Interactive: $299.95–$599.95/mo. Monthly billing triggers a $500 setup fee that annual billing avoids. Those are list prices as of this year and they move, so check before you budget.

如果你想了解该功能的实际成本，目前的月费如下：Showcase IDX（WordPress 插件）：每月 94.95–124.95 美元。无设置费；MLS 转嫁费用约为每月 0–33 美元，具体取决于你所在的委员会。Placester：每月 59–129 美元。每个 MLS 额外增加约 25 美元/月。Sierra Interactive：每月 299.95–599.95 美元。按月计费会产生 500 美元的设置费，按年计费可免除。这些是今年的标价且会变动，因此在制定预算前请务必核实。

The structural point holds either way: IDX is a recurring cost with a board-approval process attached, and it does not get cheaper if you add it later. So this build phases it. Phase one is lead capture, compliance, and a small set of hand-curated featured properties that link out to the existing tour microsites. Phase two is the MLS feed, once there is traffic for it to serve. The landing pages ship with a marked slot where the market's search widget will go, so adding it later is configuration rather than a rebuild.

无论如何，结构性要点在于：IDX 是一项带有委员会审批流程的经常性成本，以后再添加并不会更便宜。因此，本次构建采用了分阶段策略。第一阶段是潜在客户获取、合规性以及一小部分人工精选的特色房源，这些房源链接到现有的参观微型网站。第二阶段是 MLS 数据源，待有流量后再接入。落地页预留了放置市场搜索小部件的位置，因此后续添加只是配置问题，而非重建。

What it actually costs. Three honest tiers, with what drives the number in each: DIY builder — roughly $20–$60/month. Squarespace, Wix, or similar, plus a domain. You get a presentable site and you do all the work, including the compliance items. Viable if your lead flow is entirely referral and the site is a business card. Agent platform — roughly $60–$600/month. Placester at the low end, Sierra Interactive at the high end, IDX bundled or bolted on. You get MLS search and a CRM without.

实际成本。三个真实的层级，以及决定每个层级价格的因素：DIY 构建者——每月约 20–60 美元。使用 Squarespace、Wix 或类似平台，加上域名。你得到一个像样的网站，但所有工作（包括合规事项）都由你自己完成。如果你的潜在客户完全来自推荐，且网站仅作为名片使用，此方案可行。经纪人平台——每月约 60–600 美元。低端如 Placester，高端如 Sierra Interactive，包含或附加 IDX。你获得 MLS 搜索功能和 CRM 系统。
