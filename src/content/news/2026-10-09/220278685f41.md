# Building on our commitment to American scientific discovery

Anthropic is deepening its support for American scientific research by committing $150 million over three years to the Genesis Mission, a federal initiative to accelerate scientific and technological discovery through AI. The funding will go toward making Claude available to more than 15 agencies that are part of the mission, including NASA, the National Institutes of Health, and the National Science Foundation.

Anthropic 正在深化对美国科学研究的支持，承诺在三年内向“创世纪任务”（Genesis Mission）投入 1.5 亿美元。这是一项旨在通过人工智能加速科学和技术发现的联邦计划。这笔资金将用于向该任务所属的 15 个以上的机构提供 Claude 使用权限，其中包括美国国家航空航天局（NASA）、美国国立卫生研究院（NIH）和美国国家科学基金会（NSF）。

We first announced our partnership with the US Department of Energy (DOE) and the Genesis Mission last December. Since then, we have worked with DOE to bring Claude to scientists across the national laboratories. Today, we announced this new commitment as part of the Science: A New Golden Age Summit hosted by the White House Office of Science and Technology Policy in Washington, DC.

我们于去年 12 月首次宣布了与美国能源部（DOE）及“创世纪任务”的合作伙伴关系。自那时起，我们一直与能源部合作，将 Claude 带给各国家实验室的科学家们。今天，作为在华盛顿特区由白宫科技政策办公室主办的“科学：新的黄金时代”峰会的一部分，我们宣布了这一新的承诺。

Supporting American scientists: We share the administration’s view, laid out in “Science: A New Golden Age,” that AI can have a profound and positive impact on scientific discovery, and we are glad to back its efforts to support American scientists.

支持美国科学家：我们认同政府在《科学：新的黄金时代》中提出的观点，即人工智能可以对科学发现产生深远而积极的影响，我们很高兴能支持其为美国科学家提供的各项举措。

Researchers at federal agencies and national laboratories work on some of the world’s most difficult scientific problems. Through our commitment, we aim to help them use Claude to meaningfully advance their work. Over the next three years, we will: Provide Claude, Claude Code, and API credits to several hundred Genesis Mission research projects; Partner closely with agencies and national laboratories on the administration’s top science priorities, including fusion energy and quantum computing; and Provide training, onboarding, and technical support to scientists on Genesis Mission projects, and help agencies new to the Genesis Mission get their first projects running.

联邦机构和国家实验室的研究人员致力于解决世界上一些最艰巨的科学难题。通过我们的承诺，我们旨在帮助他们利用 Claude 有效地推进工作。在接下来的三年里，我们将：为数百个“创世纪任务”研究项目提供 Claude、Claude Code 和 API 额度；就政府的重点科学优先事项（包括聚变能源和量子计算）与各机构及国家实验室密切合作；并为“创世纪任务”项目的科学家提供培训、入职指导和技术支持，同时帮助新加入该任务的机构启动其首批项目。

Accelerating scientific research: This commitment is part of our broader effort to support scientific research. Earlier this year we launched Claude Science, an AI workbench that integrates the tools and packages researchers most commonly use, and opened 10,000 free and discounted Claude seats for academic scientists. We also expanded our AI for Science program, which gives credits to researchers on high-impact projects, and kicked off a research preview of the Model Hardware Standard, a shared specification for AI agents to safely operate lab instruments. Our commitment to the Genesis Mission is our next step toward helping researchers everywhere.

加速科学研究：这一承诺是我们支持科学研究的更广泛努力的一部分。今年早些时候，我们推出了 Claude Science，这是一个集成了研究人员最常用工具和软件包的人工智能工作台，并为学术界科学家开放了 10,000 个免费和折扣 Claude 席位。我们还扩展了“人工智能科学”（AI for Science）计划，为从事高影响力项目的研究人员提供额度，并启动了“模型硬件标准”（Model Hardware Standard）的研究预览，这是一项供人工智能代理安全操作实验室仪器的共享规范。我们对“创世纪任务”的承诺是我们帮助全球研究人员迈出的下一步。
