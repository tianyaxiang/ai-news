# Small Phones Have Vanished. Enough Phone Wants to Bring Them Back

Do you hate the size of modern-day smartphones? Wonder why Apple stopped manufacturing iPhone Minis? Turned off by the unwieldy size of an unfolded iPhone Duo? Well, then you might be interested in the Enough Phone.

你讨厌现代智能手机的尺寸吗？想知道为什么苹果停止生产 iPhone Mini 了吗？对展开后笨重的 iPhone Duo 感到反感吗？那么，你可能会对 Enough Phone 感兴趣。

It's a brand-new Android handset whose biggest draw is its small size. With a 5.3-inch screen, it's tiny in an era when most smartphone displays sit at around 6.3 inches, like the iPhone 17.

这是一款全新的安卓手机，其最大的卖点就是小巧的尺寸。在大多数智能手机屏幕都在 6.3 英寸左右（如 iPhone 17）的时代，它 5.3 英寸的屏幕显得非常迷你。

Most smartphone manufacturers have abandoned the small phone. Apple stopped selling the iPhone Mini in 2023 because of poor sales; on the Android side, Asus was one of the few phone makers churning out compact phones under its Zenfone line, but it paused its mobile division early this year to focus on AI, robotics, and smart glasses. Sony also discontinued its compact Xperia 5 series in 2023 (and has stopped selling its phones in the US).

大多数智能手机制造商已经放弃了小屏手机。苹果因销量不佳在 2023 年停止销售 iPhone Mini；在安卓阵营，华硕曾是少数通过 Zenfone 系列生产紧凑型手机的厂商之一，但今年年初其移动部门暂停了业务，转而专注于人工智能、机器人和智能眼镜。索尼也在 2023 年停产了其紧凑型 Xperia 5 系列（并已停止在美国销售手机）。

Smaller players have jumped in to fill the void, like Unihertz and its keyboard-equipped tiny phones. Clicks—a relatively new company that makes keyboard accessories for smartphones—is launching the Communicator in December, a 4-inch Android phone with a keyboard. Other devices focus more on digital well-being and don't offer the traditional mobile experience, resembling “dumb phones,” including the Light Phone III, Minimal Phone, or Mudita Kompakt.

一些小型厂商已介入填补这一空白，例如 Unihertz 及其配备键盘的微型手机。相对较新的智能手机键盘配件公司 Clicks 将于 12 月推出 Communicator，这是一款带有键盘的 4 英寸安卓手机。其他设备则更侧重于数字健康，不提供传统的移动体验，类似于“功能机”，包括 Light Phone III、Minimal Phone 或 Mudita Kompakt。

But the Enough Phone, launching today on Kickstarter for $499 (with a final price estimated around $599), takes a slightly more unique approach. When you buy it, you can choose Android 17 as the operating system and access all your favorite apps on the compact screen. Or you can choose OdysseyOS from Sleke—an Android fork with a dumb-phone-like software experience.

但今天在 Kickstarter 上以 499 美元（最终售价预计约为 599 美元）推出的 Enough Phone 采取了一种更为独特的方法。购买时，你可以选择安卓 17 作为操作系统，在紧凑的屏幕上使用所有你喜欢的应用程序。或者，你也可以选择 Sleke 开发的 OdysseyOS——这是一个提供类似功能机软件体验的安卓分支系统。

The handsets are expected to ship in May of 2027.

这些手机预计将于 2027 年 5 月发货。

Austin Boer, Brennan Jordan, and William Stults are the trio behind the Enough Phone. Boer and Jordan manage Sleke—a company founded in 2024 that sells refurbished Google Pixel smartphones running its restricted OdysseyOS, which lets users access utility apps like Uber, Google Authenticator, and Spotify, but strips away social media, games, browsers, and anything else designed to keep you glued to your screen.

Austin Boer、Brennan Jordan 和 William Stults 是 Enough Phone 背后的三人组。Boer 和 Jordan 管理着 Sleke，这是一家成立于 2024 年的公司，销售运行其受限版 OdysseyOS 的翻新谷歌 Pixel 手机。该系统允许用户使用 Uber、Google Authenticator 和 Spotify 等实用工具，但剔除了社交媒体、游戏、浏览器以及任何旨在让你沉迷于屏幕的内容。

Stults, on the other hand, helps run an ecommerce platform called Dumbwireless, founded with his girlfriend. The duo vet dumb phones on the market and sell them on the platform alongside a wireless plan powered by Gigs to make switching to a dumb phone as frictionless as possible.

另一方面，Stults 协助运营着一个名为 Dumbwireless 的电子商务平台，这是他和女友共同创立的。两人会对市场上的功能机进行评估，并在平台上销售，同时提供由 Gigs 支持的无线套餐，旨在让用户切换到功能机的过程尽可能顺畅。

Stults met Boer and Jordan years ago through Dumbwireless, which carries the Sleke Phone. Sleke's approach was entirely software-based, as it upcycles Google Pixel phones, but a smartphone with a dumbed-down operating system doesn't need much screen space. Stults also liked that, out of all the dumb phones he's tried, Sleke's OdysseyOS has the least friction—you can still access many of the apps necessary to get through the day, like Uber, Spotify, and WhatsApp. You don't have to go cold turkey.

Stults 多年前通过销售 Sleke 手机的 Dumbwireless 平台结识了 Boer 和 Jordan。Sleke 的方法完全基于软件，通过升级改造谷歌 Pixel 手机实现，但搭载简化版操作系统的智能手机并不需要太大的屏幕空间。Stults 还认为，在他尝试过的所有功能机中，Sleke 的 OdysseyOS 带来的阻力最小——你仍然可以使用许多日常必需的应用程序，如 Uber、Spotify 和 WhatsApp。你无需彻底戒断。

Once the major mobile players made it clear they no longer wanted to bother with small phones, the idea for the Enough Phone was born. Boer, Jordan, and Stults have no direct experience in the smartphone or feature phone industry (though a few of Sleke's engineers do). When I asked what each of their roles is, Jordan said they haven't given each other titles yet.

当主流手机厂商明确表示不再愿意费心生产小屏手机时，Enough Phone 的构想便诞生了。Boer、Jordan 和 Stults 在智能手机或功能机行业并无直接经验（尽管 Sleke 的几位工程师有）。当我询问他们各自的角色时，Jordan 表示他们还没有给彼此设定头衔。

The Enough Phone is a 5.3-inch Android handset with a polycarbonate build. It looks simple—it only comes in black—but you'll notice the four Torx screws on the back. Like the Fairphone, you can remove those screws and replace the phone’s battery, in compliance with upcoming European Union regulations. The phone is 12 millimeters thick to accommodate a chunky battery—around 3,500 to 4,000 mAh, as the company is still finalizing some specs—for “all-day” battery life. That’s pretty thick for a phone; the iPhone 13 Mini is a little more than half that thickness, and the iPhone 17 measures 7.8 mm.

Enough Phone 是一款采用聚碳酸酯机身的 5.3 英寸安卓手机。它看起来很简单——只有黑色可选——但你会注意到背面的四颗梅花螺丝。像 Fairphone 一样，你可以拆下这些螺丝并更换电池，以符合欧盟即将出台的法规。该手机厚度为 12 毫米，以容纳一块大容量电池（约 3,500 至 4,000 mAh，公司仍在确定具体规格），从而实现“全天候”续航。对于手机来说，这相当厚；iPhone 13 Mini 的厚度仅为其一半多一点，而 iPhone 17 的厚度为 7.8 毫米。

It's powered by a midrange MediaTek Dimensity 7500 processor, with 8 GB of RAM and 128 GB of internal storage. It has a hybrid SIM slot that lets you expand storage via microSD, with eSIM support and 5G connectivity. No, there's no headphone jack—Boer says internal space was limited, and the team wanted to keep the price under $600. There's a fingerprint sensor integrated into the side-mounted power button, NFC for contactless payments, a 32-megapixel selfie camera, and a single 50-megapixel Sony camera on the back. It will work on T-Mobile, and the company plans to get it certified on AT&T. (You also can get a mobile plan directly from Enough Phone.)

它搭载中端联发科天玑 7500 处理器，配备 8GB 内存和 128GB 内部存储。它拥有一个混合 SIM 卡槽，允许通过 microSD 卡扩展存储，并支持 eSIM 和 5G 连接。不，它没有耳机插孔——Boer 表示内部空间有限，且团队希望将价格控制在 600 美元以下。侧边电源键集成了指纹传感器，支持 NFC 非接触式支付，前置 3200 万像素自拍镜头，背面配有一颗 5000 万像素的索尼摄像头。它可以在 T-Mobile 网络上使用，公司计划获得 AT&T 的认证。（你也可以直接从 Enough Phone 获取移动套餐。）

If the crowdfunding campaign hits its goal, you’ll be able to get a midrange Android phone in a size that's hard to find on the market. However, things like camera quality and battery run time are still big unknowns. If you go with Sleke's OdysseyOS, you're effectively buying a “smart” dumb phone, since you’re software-limiting your hardware so it can’t run any of the infinitely scrolling apps that demand your attention. Stults says you might be able to switch between Sleke's OS and Android in the future, but that's not confirmed.

如果众筹活动达到目标，你将能够以市场上难得一见的尺寸获得一款中端安卓手机。然而，摄像头质量和电池续航时间等问题仍是巨大的未知数。如果你选择 Sleke 的 OdysseyOS，你实际上是在购买一款“智能”功能机，因为你通过软件限制了硬件，使其无法运行任何需要你持续关注的无限滚动应用程序。Stults 表示，未来或许可以在 Sleke 的系统和安卓系统之间切换，但这尚未得到证实。

OdysseyOS has an app store that Sleke curates. Users can manually request that an Android app be made available, but Sleke will make the final call. News apps, browsers, multimedia apps, social media, and games aren't allowed, but utility-focused apps are, like banking, smart home, two-factor authentication, and so on. For messaging, you can use Beeper, which brings all of your chat apps together in one place—so you can still respond to Instagram DMs even without the Instagram app. If you want to look something up, Sleke offers a Perplexity-powered search bar to give you text-based answers. You can scan QR codes too. Jordan thinks most customers will opt for Sleke's OdysseyOS.

OdysseyOS 拥有一个由 Sleke 策划的应用商店。用户可以手动请求上架某款安卓应用，但最终决定权在 Sleke 手中。新闻应用、浏览器、多媒体应用、社交媒体和游戏是不允许的，但以实用为主的应用是被允许的，例如银行、智能家居、双重身份验证等。在消息传递方面，你可以使用 Beeper，它将你所有的聊天应用整合在一起——这样即使没有安装 Instagram 应用，你仍然可以回复 Instagram 私信。如果你想查询信息，Sleke 提供了一个由 Perplexity 支持的搜索栏，为你提供基于文本的答案。你也可以扫描二维码。Jordan 认为大多数客户会选择 Sleke 的 OdysseyOS。
