# Gemini for Home now plugs into AT&T’s smart security system

AT&T 的 Connected Life 系统现已支持由 Gemini 驱动的功能，可以汇总家庭事件等信息。

AT&T’s smart home security system now integrates with Gemini for Home. The company says its Connected Life system is the “first” home security product to integrate with Google Home Premium, enabling a bunch of Gemini-powered features that can summarize home events, search your video history, and more.

AT&T 的智能家居安防系统现已与 Gemini for Home 集成。该公司表示，其 Connected Life 系统是“首个”集成 Google Home Premium 的家庭安防产品，支持一系列由 Gemini 驱动的功能，可以汇总家庭事件、搜索视频历史记录等。

With the update, Gemini for Home can provide detailed descriptions about events captured by your security cameras, such as who delivered a package and how many items they dropped off. You can also search recorded video footage using natural language questions, like “What dug up the yard last night?” or “Who let the dogs out?”, as well as review daily summaries of activity in and outside your home.

通过此次更新，Gemini for Home 可以提供安防摄像头所捕捉事件的详细描述，例如谁投递了包裹以及投递了多少件物品。你还可以使用自然语言问题搜索录制的视频片段，例如“昨晚是什么东西挖了院子？”或“谁把狗放出来的？”，并查看家中内外的每日活动摘要。

These features are only available with AT&T’s professional $29.99 / month Connected Life monitoring plan. AT&T is also adding the ability to automatically arm or disarm your home based on your proximity to your home.

这些功能仅适用于 AT&T 每月 29.99 美元的专业 Connected Life 监控套餐。AT&T 还增加了根据你与家的距离自动布防或撤防的功能。

AT&T launched its Connected Life system last December in partnership with Google and Abode. It’s now taking advantage of Google’s Gemini-powered Home APIs, which allow smart home brands and developers to build AI-powered features into their devices. ADT has also hinted at bringing Gemini for Home features into its security systems.

AT&T 于去年 12 月与 Google 和 Abode 合作推出了 Connected Life 系统。目前，该系统正在利用 Google 基于 Gemini 的 Home API，这使得智能家居品牌和开发人员能够将人工智能功能构建到其设备中。ADT 也暗示将把 Gemini for Home 功能引入其安防系统。
