# Roundtables: Inside the Quest for Cognitive Immortality

Available only for MIT Technology Review subscribers. Tuesday, November 17, 2026 In an effort to reach an elusive continuity of the self, some far-thinking technologists are working on ways to keep your brain going—even beyond death.

仅限《麻省理工科技评论》订阅者访问。2026年11月17日，星期二。为了实现难以捉摸的自我延续，一些具有前瞻性的技术专家正在研究让大脑持续运作的方法——即使在死亡之后。

Join science editor Mary Beth Griggs and senior editor for biomedicine Antonio Regalado for a conversation exploring possible paths to keep your mind going, including gradual brain replacement and uploading our memories to the cloud. Going live at 17:00 GMT / 12pm EST / 9am PST Register Now

加入科学编辑 Mary Beth Griggs 和生物医学高级编辑 Antonio Regalado 的对话，共同探讨保持思维延续的可能途径，包括逐步替换大脑以及将我们的记忆上传到云端。直播时间：格林威治标准时间 17:00 / 美国东部时间中午 12 点 / 美国太平洋时间上午 9 点。立即注册。

Speakers: Antonio Regalado Senior Editor, Biomedicine Mary Beth Griggs Science Editor Deep Dive Biotechnology and health

演讲者：Antonio Regalado，生物医学高级编辑；Mary Beth Griggs，科学编辑。深度报道：生物技术与健康。

An AI “mind-reading” tool can reconstruct what you’re looking at from a brain scan Scientists hope it could be used to reconstruct a person’s inner thoughts, mental images, or even dreams. By Jessica Hamzelou archive page

一种人工智能“读心”工具可以通过脑部扫描重建你所看到的内容。科学家们希望它能被用于重建一个人的内心想法、心理图像，甚至是梦境。作者：Jessica Hamzelou，存档页面。

Young organs may not be a fountain of youth for recipients New studies pour cold water on a prominent rejuvenation idea. By Jessica Hamzelou archive page

年轻器官可能并非接受者的青春之泉。新的研究给一个著名的复兴理念泼了冷水。作者：Jessica Hamzelou，存档页面。

We’re still figuring out the side effects of GLP-1 weight-loss drugs Could the drugs affect hair, nails, and vision? By Jessica Hamzelou archive page

我们仍在研究 GLP-1 减肥药物的副作用。这些药物会影响头发、指甲和视力吗？作者：Jessica Hamzelou，存档页面。

Weight-loss drugs show signs of slowing biological aging, say drugmakers Novo Nordisk and Eli Lilly found the popular medicines reduce molecular markers of age. By Antonio Regalado archive page

制药商称，减肥药物显示出减缓生物衰老的迹象。诺和诺德和礼来公司发现，这些热门药物可以减少衰老的分子标志物。作者：Antonio Regalado，存档页面。
