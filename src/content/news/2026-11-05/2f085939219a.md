You can save a heck of lot of money on Google’s Pixel 11 phones through November 8th, whether you want the best-for-the-least Pixel 11, or the slightly pricier Pixel 11 Pro that takes better photos. Even the big Pixel 11 Pro XL with a longer-lasting battery is hundreds off its original price.

The Pixel 11 Pro deal is the most attractive of the bunch, as the $1,099.99 phone with 256GB storage and 12GB of RAM is down to just $799.99 at Amazon, Best Buy, and at the Google Store, beating its October Prime Day price by $50. It’s $100 less than the base Pixel 11 model sold for at launch, and it’s the phone to get if you want great camera performance, plus extra features like HiLight that, while cool, weren’t really worth paying a premium to access. I’d be very surprised if the price dropped more for Black Friday.

Pixel 11 Pro 的优惠最具吸引力，这款配备 256GB 存储空间和 12GB 内存的手机在亚马逊、百思买和谷歌商店的售价已降至 799.99 美元，比 10 月份的 Prime Day 价格还低 50 美元。这比基础款 Pixel 11 发布时的售价还要低 100 美元。如果您追求出色的拍照性能，以及像 HiLight 这样虽然很酷但此前不值得溢价购买的额外功能，那么这款手机非常值得入手。如果黑色星期五的价格还能进一步下调，我会感到非常惊讶。

There’s absolutely nothing wrong with opting for the Pixel 11 instead, especially at its attractive $699.99 ($200 off) price that now matches the cost of the iPhone 17E, following Apple’s September price bump. The Pixel 11 is the most affordable phone that supports Google’s software features that are exclusive to this year’s phones, including Magic Capture, Camera Looks, and Proactive Assistance. It has fast 25W wireless charging (up from 15W with the Pixel 10), and has 12GB of RAM, like the Pixel 11 Pro.

选择 Pixel 11 也完全没有问题，尤其是其 699.99 美元（优惠 200 美元）的诱人价格，在苹果 9 月份涨价后，现在已经与 iPhone 17E 的价格持平。Pixel 11 是支持谷歌今年独家软件功能（包括 Magic Capture、Camera Looks 和 Proactive Assistance）的最实惠机型。它拥有 25W 的快速无线充电（高于 Pixel 10 的 15W），并与 Pixel 11 Pro 一样配备了 12GB 内存。

I’m still happily rocking my Pixel 9 Pro, but if I were going to upgrade right now, I’d get the Pixel 11 Pro. Yes, I want the better cameras and HiLight. But really, the main reason I’m lured to the Pro is because the trade-in offers are really generous right now. It’s just $10 more to opt for the Pro over the base Pixel 11. My base Pixel 9 Pro with 128GB of storage can fetch $530 in trade-in credit at the Google Store and $525 at Best Buy, which seems great.

我目前还在愉快地使用我的 Pixel 9 Pro，但如果我现在要升级，我会选择 Pixel 11 Pro。没错，我想要更好的摄像头和 HiLight 功能。但实际上，我被 Pro 版本吸引的主要原因是目前的以旧换新优惠非常慷慨。选择 Pro 版本仅比基础款 Pixel 11 多花 10 美元。我那台 128GB 存储空间的基础款 Pixel 9 Pro 在谷歌商店可以获得 530 美元的以旧换新抵扣额，在百思买则为 525 美元，这看起来非常划算。
