In Brief Posted: 2:13 PM PST · November 4, 2026 Image Credits:Nothing Lucas Ropek Nothing CEO says company’s mysterious AI-first device is dropping next month Nothing, the smartphone startup behind the distinctive Nothing Phone, is working on an AI product, but what it is remains unclear.

简讯 发布于：太平洋标准时间 2026 年 11 月 4 日下午 2:13 图片来源：Nothing 作者：Lucas Ropek Nothing 首席执行官称该公司神秘的“AI 优先”设备将于下月发布。Nothing 是一家以其独特的 Nothing Phone 而闻名的智能手机初创公司，目前正在研发一款人工智能产品，但具体是什么尚不清楚。

On Wednesday, CEO Carl Pei posted on X that the company’s first “AI-native product” ships in December. “It’s a tool, but one built with the personality and care that have come to define a Nothing product. It has no new screen, and it couldn’t have been an app,” Pei wrote.

周三，首席执行官裴宇（Carl Pei）在 X 上发文称，该公司的首款“AI 原生产品”将于 12 月出货。裴宇写道：“它是一个工具，但它的打造融入了 Nothing 产品所特有的个性和关怀。它没有新屏幕，也不可能是一个应用程序。”

Pei has teased an AI-first device for some time and the company has also said that it plans to release a pair of AI-linked smart glasses, as well as earbuds with AI integrations. Pei’s description adds up to a screenless non-app that uses AI? He also calls it a “productivity tool.”

裴宇此前已多次预告过一款“AI 优先”设备，该公司也曾表示计划发布一款与 AI 关联的智能眼镜，以及集成 AI 功能的耳塞。裴宇的描述是否指向了一款使用 AI 的无屏幕非应用程序设备？他还将其称为“生产力工具”。

Adding further mystique to the announcement, Pei seems to downplay any link to AI wearables. “The whole industry is hunting for its iPhone moment: the pendant, the pin, the glasses, the companion, some magical consumer device that makes AI part of everyone’s life,” Pei wrote. “I admire the boldness, but I read this moment differently.”

为了给这一公告增添更多神秘感，裴宇似乎淡化了其与 AI 可穿戴设备之间的任何联系。裴宇写道：“整个行业都在寻找属于自己的‘iPhone 时刻’：吊坠、胸针、眼镜、伴侣设备，或是某种能让 AI 融入每个人生活的神奇消费电子产品。我很钦佩这种大胆的尝试，但我对当下的解读有所不同。”

No firm release date has been announced, but Nothing fans should know soon enough what the company has in store for them.

目前尚未公布确切的发布日期，但 Nothing 的粉丝们很快就会知道该公司为他们准备了什么。
