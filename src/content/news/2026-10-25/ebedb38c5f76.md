# Turning one car photo into a short cinematic stunt clip

A creative demo does not always need a shoot day. If you already have one clear photo of a car, you can mock a short stunt clip and drop it into a pitch, a portfolio, or a social concept board. I start with a daylight side or three-quarter photo. The whole vehicle stays in frame, the background is simple, and I do not crop the wheels. A cluttered driveway or a motion-blurred plate makes the body look warped later, so I reshoot rather than over-edit.

创意演示并不总是需要专门的拍摄日。如果你已经有一张清晰的汽车照片，就可以制作一段简短的特技剪辑，并将其放入提案、作品集或社交概念板中。我通常从一张日光下的侧面或四分之三角度照片开始。整辆车保持在画面内，背景简洁，且我不裁剪车轮。杂乱的车道或带有运动模糊的车牌会让车身在后期看起来变形，所以我宁愿重拍也不愿过度编辑。

Then I pick a short template instead of a long prompt. A ramp-flight style shot runs about fifteen seconds and reads like a wide establishing move. A side kickflip is closer to six seconds. A head-on kickflip is about eight seconds and works when you want the grille in the hero frame. Short clips are easier to review than a one-minute sequence. I generate those clips with Flying Car AI, an independent creative tool that turns one photo of a car or bike into a short cinematic stunt video. It is not an official Tesla product. The output is a mock commercial, not a claim that the car can fly.

然后，我选择一个简短的模板，而不是冗长的提示词。坡道飞行风格的镜头大约持续十五秒，看起来像是一个广角定场镜头。侧面踢翻动作大约六秒。正面踢翻动作大约八秒，当你想要将进气格栅作为主角画面时非常适用。短视频比一分钟的序列更容易审核。我使用 Flying Car AI 生成这些剪辑，这是一款独立的创意工具，可以将一张汽车或摩托车的照片转化为简短的电影级特技视频。它不是特斯拉的官方产品。输出结果是模拟广告，而非声称汽车可以飞行。

Before I keep a render I check that the silhouette still matches the source photo, the wheels stay attached, and the camera does not jump. If the background smears, I try one cleaner photo rather than stacking filters. For a demo, one honest still beats three edited angles.

在保留渲染结果之前，我会检查轮廓是否仍与原始照片匹配，车轮是否保持连接，以及镜头是否出现跳动。如果背景模糊，我会尝试使用一张更干净的照片，而不是堆叠滤镜。对于演示而言，一张真实的静态图胜过三个经过编辑的角度。
