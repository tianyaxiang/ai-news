English · 简体中文 🌐 cindy.app · ⬇️ 下载 Cindy 是一款开箱即用的开源 AI 智能体。它将多种工具框架（harnesses）、模型和工具整合到一个智能体中，能够在你的项目和应用程序中完成实际工作。它从第一天起即可使用，并可随时间推移由你塑造。Cindy 在你自己的机器上本地运行，使用你的真实文件和已登录的应用程序。

The first supported harnesses are Claude Code and Codex — more are being added, and a native harness is in the works. Models and harnesses mix freely and can switch mid-task while your workspace, memory, skills and tools stay continuous; one task can even be planned, executed in parallel, and reviewed by agents on different harness × model combos. She can drive your browser, computer and phone, and take work from IM and schedules.

首批支持的工具框架是 Claude Code 和 Codex——更多框架正在添加中，原生框架也正在开发中。模型和框架可以自由组合，并可在任务执行过程中切换，同时保持工作区、记忆、技能和工具的连续性；甚至可以由使用不同“框架 × 模型”组合的智能体来规划、并行执行并审查同一个任务。它可以操控你的浏览器、电脑和手机，并处理来自即时通讯软件和日程安排的工作。

This repository is the open-source client for Cindy — the desktop and mobile apps plus their shared packages, organized as a pnpm monorepo. The client is free to use, and its source code is open under Apache-2.0. Bring models your way: sign in to the official Cindy service (usage deducted transparently), authorize the Claude Code / Codex Coding Plan you already pay for and keep using it inside Cindy — no duplicate bill — connect your own API keys, or use local models. See cindy.app for service details, pricing, and downloads.
