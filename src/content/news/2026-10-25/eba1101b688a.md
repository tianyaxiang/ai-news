Microduck A tiny biped robot that moves using reinforcement learning policies. Get yours here · Cheat sheet · Training the policies · How it works · Contributing This repo is the duck's brain. About 25 cm and 800 g of robot, run by a handful of daemons on a Rockchip RK3566: a 50 Hz control loop driving fifteen servos from neural policies, the radios and the camera, and the update machinery that gets new software onto a robot without bricking it. Everything you need to run a Microduck is here. If you want one, get yours here.

CONTRIBUTING.md Building, testing, layout, conventions, releasing. Docs index Everything, including the design pages and the open problems. Under the hood Rust, no framework, one workspace. robotd owns the control loop and the motor bus; updaterd installs signed releases and rolls them back when a robot comes up unhealthy; configd owns wifi and identity; btd is the Bluetooth path a phone uses; padd reads the gamepad; mediad streams the camera over WebRTC; tofd serves the depth sensor. They talk over one JSON-RPC contract on Unix sockets, and every client — the app, the console, the gamepad, your script — sends exactly the same calls.

CONTRIBUTING.md：构建、测试、布局、规范、发布。文档索引：所有内容，包括设计页面和待解决的问题。底层技术：Rust，无框架，单一工作区。robotd 负责控制循环和电机总线；updaterd 安装签名版本，并在机器人运行不健康时进行回滚；configd 负责 WiFi 和身份识别；btd 是手机使用的蓝牙路径；padd 读取游戏手柄；mediad 通过 WebRTC 流式传输摄像头画面；tofd 提供深度传感器服务。它们通过 Unix 套接字上的单一 JSON-RPC 协议进行通信，每个客户端——应用程序、控制台、游戏手柄、你的脚本——发送的调用完全相同。
