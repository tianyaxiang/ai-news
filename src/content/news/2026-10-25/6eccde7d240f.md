I am very lost in Los Angeles. I’m also sweating because I'm running as fast as I can, slapping my hands against the wet glasses frame on my face to boost the volume of the pulsing techno beats blasting into my ears—and anyone else’s if they happen to be nearby. It’s only the second time I’ve gone on a run in the city, and I decide to let myself get completely off course.

我在洛杉矶迷路了。我满头大汗，因为我正以最快的速度奔跑，拍打着脸上湿漉漉的镜框，试图调大正在我耳边轰鸣的脉冲电子乐音量——如果旁边有人，他们也能听到。这是我第二次在这座城市跑步，我决定让自己彻底偏离路线。

“Hey Meta,” I pant. “I need to get back; tell me where to go.” The music dims, and the AI agent barks turn-by-turn directions in my ears. After getting turned around a couple of times, I eventually make it back. The navigation wasn’t perfect, but it got me where I needed to be.

“嘿 Meta，”我喘着气说，“我需要回去，告诉我怎么走。”音乐声减弱，AI 助手在我的耳边发出逐向导航指令。在绕了几次弯路后，我最终回到了目的地。导航并不完美，但它确实把我带到了该去的地方。

This feature has long been available in Meta’s smart glasses. But now I’m wearing the new Ray-Ban Meta Audio, camera-free glasses the company announced in September. The only big change between these and Meta’s other glasses is the lack of a camera, but losing that one feature makes a world of difference.

这项功能在 Meta 的智能眼镜中早已存在。但我现在戴的是新款 Ray-Ban Meta Audio，这是该公司在九月份宣布的无摄像头眼镜。这些眼镜与 Meta 其他眼镜之间唯一的重大区别就是没有摄像头，但失去这一项功能却带来了天壤之别。

It makes sense that Meta started with cameras on its glasses. After all, its primary breadwinner is Facebook, a visual platform driven by all the photographs and memes your uncle can use AI to generate. The platform is designed to capture content and beam it to the world—sometimes whether people like it or not.

Meta 最初在眼镜上配备摄像头是有道理的。毕竟，其主要的收入来源是 Facebook，这是一个由你叔叔可以用 AI 生成的各种照片和表情包所驱动的视觉平台。该平台旨在捕捉内容并将其传播到世界各地——有时无论人们是否喜欢。

It took waves of privacy controversies for Meta to finally ditch the cameras. People have found the camera-enabled glasses creepy, calling Meta’s products “pervert glasses” because they have been used by weirdo pickup artists to record people without consent. Some users also shared techniques for removing the indicator light on the glasses designed to let people around the wearer know they’re recording. (Meta released a software fix for this that disables the cameras entirely if the indicator light has been tampered with.)

Meta 经历了多轮隐私争议才最终放弃了摄像头。人们觉得带有摄像头的眼镜令人毛骨悚然，称 Meta 的产品为“变态眼镜”，因为它们曾被一些怪异的搭讪艺术家用来在未经同意的情况下录制他人。一些用户还分享了移除眼镜上指示灯的技术，该指示灯本意是让佩戴者周围的人知道他们正在录像。（Meta 发布了一个软件修复程序，如果指示灯被篡改，该程序会完全禁用摄像头。）

It didn’t help that the company tried to put facial recognition technology on the glasses, which it denied at first, then later admitted to, and removed. In September, the company was sued for its facial recognition systems. (Meta insists that all the very public pushback did nothing to encourage it to release audio-only glasses.)

该公司试图在眼镜上加入人脸识别技术，起初予以否认，后来又承认并将其移除，这并没有起到什么帮助。九月份，该公司因其人脸识别系统被起诉。（Meta 坚称，所有这些公开的抵制并没有促使其发布纯音频眼镜。）

All that goes away when cameras are removed. The Meta Audio glasses don’t record audio either, except for when you’re using the AI chatbot. Those requests are then saved in the Meta AI app. There is the ability to do live language translation, but it requires going into the Meta AI app first. It’s a shame it took Meta this long to remove the cameras—even with what feels like such a key feature missing, it makes the glasses all the better.

当摄像头被移除后，所有这些问题都消失了。Meta Audio 眼镜也不会录制音频，除非你正在使用 AI 聊天机器人。这些请求随后会保存在 Meta AI 应用程序中。它具备实时语言翻译功能，但需要先进入 Meta AI 应用程序。遗憾的是 Meta 花了这么长时间才移除摄像头——即使缺失了这样一个看似关键的功能，它反而让眼镜变得更好了。

Courtesy of MetaI’ve been wearing the Burbank style. I personally prefer the rounder Clubmaster, and when a friend saw me wearing them for the first time, he laughed and said, “Those are a little thick.”

图片由 Meta 提供。我一直戴着 Burbank 款式。我个人更喜欢圆形的 Clubmaster，当一位朋友第一次看到我戴着它们时，他笑着说：“这些有点厚。”

The frames may be thick, but the Ray-Ban Meta Audio might just be the perfect running glasses. They’ve got transition lenses that adjust quickly to sunlight. The speakers deliver solid sound quality, letting you blast music while also piping in crucial ambient sounds, like that car barreling toward you. Call quality is great, too, and people I’ve spoken with over the phone haven’t noticed I’m speaking through my glasses. That said, the sound does leak out and doesn’t have the best bass response.

镜框可能很厚，但 Ray-Ban Meta Audio 可能是完美的跑步眼镜。它们配备了能快速适应阳光的变色镜片。扬声器提供了扎实的音质，让你在播放音乐的同时，也能听到关键的环境声音，比如那辆向你疾驰而来的汽车。通话质量也很棒，我通过电话交谈过的人都没有注意到我是通过眼镜在说话。话虽如此，声音确实会外泄，而且低音表现也不是最好的。

They’re lightweight—43 grams for the Burbank model and 47 grams for the Clubmaster, noticeably lighter than the 51.5-gram camera-equipped Gen 3 Wayfarer. A 12-hour battery life means all-day wear is truly viable. It’s nice not to worry about battery life or deal with earbuds or headphones.

它们很轻——Burbank 型号为 43 克，Clubmaster 为 47 克，明显比 51.5 克的带摄像头第三代 Wayfarer 轻。12 小时的电池续航意味着全天佩戴确实可行。不用担心电池续航或处理耳塞或耳机，这感觉真好。

I also like that I can walk around peacefully without people thinking I’m recording them. There are legitimate reasons to have cameras on smart glasses. (Anything with augmented reality features will need them.) But removing cameras entirely is the right choice for people who want to use smart glasses for anything that isn’t capturing content.

我也喜欢我可以平静地四处走动，而不必担心人们认为我在录制他们。智能眼镜配备摄像头有其合理的理由。（任何具有增强现实功能的产品都需要它们。）但对于那些想将智能眼镜用于非内容捕捉用途的人来说，完全移除摄像头是正确的选择。

Frankly, these are probably the only smart glasses I’d willingly spend my own money on. I’ve tried a lot of smart glasses, especially this year. Just about all of them end up back in their case, tossed into a drawer at my desk. Maybe somebody (cough Google cough) will beat Meta eventually. For now, the Ray-Ban Meta Audio are the only smart glasses I actually want to keep on my face.

坦率地说，这可能是我唯一愿意自掏腰包购买的智能眼镜。我尝试过很多智能眼镜，尤其是在今年。几乎所有这些眼镜最终都回到了盒子里，被扔进我桌子的抽屉里。也许最终会有人（咳咳，谷歌）击败 Meta。但就目前而言，Ray-Ban Meta Audio 是我唯一真正想一直戴在脸上的智能眼镜。
