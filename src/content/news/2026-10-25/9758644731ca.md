In Brief Posted: 9:30 AM PDT · October 24, 2026 Image Credits: SOPA Images / Getty Images Anthony Ha Instacart reportedly discussed selling itself with DoorDash and Uber

简讯 发布时间：太平洋夏令时间 2026 年 10 月 24 日上午 9:30 图片来源：SOPA Images / Getty Images 作者：Anthony Ha 据报道，Instacart 曾与 DoorDash 和 Uber 商讨出售事宜。

Instacart might sell itself to a larger competitor, according to multiple news reports The Information reported Friday that the grocery delivery company has hired an investment bank to help it evaluate its options, and that it’s held acquisition talks with DoorDash.

据多家新闻报道，Instacart 可能会将自己出售给规模更大的竞争对手。《The Information》周五报道称，这家杂货配送公司已聘请一家投资银行来帮助其评估各种选择，并已与 DoorDash 进行了收购谈判。

And Bloomberg reported that the company has also talked to Uber — although Uber reportedly walked away from those discussions. Instacart currently has a market cap of more than $13 billion, and its stock price is up 25% for the year to date.

彭博社报道称，该公司也与 Uber 进行了接触，尽管据报道 Uber 已经退出了这些讨论。Instacart 目前的市值超过 130 亿美元，今年迄今为止其股价已上涨 25%。

The company could see this as a good time to sell at a desirable price, before the grocery delivery market gets more competitive — but Bloomberg noted that these are early discussions that might not result in a sale. An Instacart spokesperson declined to comment.

该公司可能认为，在杂货配送市场竞争变得更加激烈之前，现在是以理想价格出售的好时机，但彭博社指出，这些讨论尚处于早期阶段，可能不会达成最终交易。Instacart 发言人拒绝置评。

The reports of a potential sale caused the company’s share price to spike more than 9.2% on Friday, while Uber and DoorDash stock declined.

有关潜在出售的报道导致该公司股价在周五飙升超过 9.2%，而 Uber 和 DoorDash 的股价则出现下跌。
