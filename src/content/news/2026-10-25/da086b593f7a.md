Robert Seacord 7h Report this post Balloting has completed for C++26. Unfortunately it was Disapproved P-Members voting: 12 in favor out of 20 = 60 % (requirement >= 66.66%) (P-Members having abstained are not counted in this vote.) Member bodies voting: 9 negative votes out of 22 = 41 % (requirement <= 25%) Sort of a sad day for the C++ community. Hopefully we can heal and move on from this in a productive manner.

C++26 的投票已经结束。遗憾的是，该草案未获通过。参与投票的 P 成员中，12 票赞成，共 20 票（赞成率 60%，要求 >= 66.66%）（弃权的 P 成员不计入此次投票）。在成员机构投票中，22 票中有 9 票反对（反对率 41%，要求 <= 25%）。这对 C++ 社区来说是一个令人难过的日子。希望我们能够治愈伤痛，并以富有成效的方式继续前进。
