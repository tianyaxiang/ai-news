# A Leakage-Aware Multimodal Evaluation Framework for Early Intraoperative Acute Kidney Injury Prediction
# 一种用于术中早期急性肾损伤预测的防泄露多模态评估框架

**Abstract:** Postoperative acute kidney injury (AKI) after major non-cardiac surgery carries substantial morbidity, yet early intraoperative risk stratification remains difficult. In this retrospective cohort study, we propose SynerT, a waveform-only hybrid temporal backbone that combines a causal dilated TCN with a hierarchy of dilated recurrent layers to encode early intraoperative physiologic trajectories for AKI risk prediction.

**摘要：** 大型非心脏手术后的术后急性肾损伤（AKI）会导致严重的并发症，但术中早期的风险分层仍然十分困难。在这项回顾性队列研究中，我们提出了 SynerT，这是一种仅基于波形的混合时间主干网络，它结合了因果扩张时间卷积网络（TCN）和分层扩张循环层，用于编码术中早期生理轨迹以进行 AKI 风险预测。

Building on SynerT, we further design two model variants that extend the backbone with structured clinical context: SynerT-MM, a late-fusion multimodal extension that integrates hemodynamic burden summaries and preoperative covariates, and SynerTStack, a leakage-safe stacked ensemble that combines cross-validated predictions from SynerT-MM with strong tabular baselines at the meta-learning stage.

在 SynerT 的基础上，我们进一步设计了两种通过结构化临床背景扩展主干网络的模型变体：SynerT-MM，一种集成了血流动力学负荷摘要和术前协变量的后期融合多模态扩展模型；以及 SynerT-Stack，一种防泄露的堆叠集成模型，它在元学习阶段将 SynerT-MM 的交叉验证预测结果与强大的表格基准模型相结合。

All models are evaluated under a strict leakage-aware framework on VitalDB, a high-fidelity perioperative database, with prediction restricted to information available within the first 60 intraoperative minutes. Among 2,413 waveform-usable cases (180 AKI-positive; 7.46% prevalence), SynerT fell well below strong structured-data baselines, demonstrating that waveform-only temporal modeling is insufficient under strict early constraints.

所有模型均在 VitalDB（一个高保真围手术期数据库）上，在严格的防泄露框架下进行评估，预测仅限于术后前 60 分钟内可获取的信息。在 2,413 个波形可用的病例中（180 例 AKI 阳性；患病率为 7.46%），SynerT 的表现远低于强大的结构化数据基准模型，这表明在严格的早期约束条件下，仅依靠波形的时间建模是不够的。

SynerT-MM recovered discrimination by incorporating hemodynamic burden summaries and preoperative covariates, and SynerT-Stack achieved the best overall performance across AUROC, AUPRC, and F1-max. Cross-fitted Platt recalibration substantially corrected calibration defects in both multimodal variants, and decision-curve analysis confirmed the recalibrated stacked model delivered the strongest net clinical benefit across low-to-intermediate thresholds.

SynerT-MM 通过整合血流动力学负荷摘要和术前协变量恢复了判别能力，而 SynerT-Stack 在 AUROC、AUPRC 和 F1-max 指标上均取得了最佳的整体性能。交叉拟合的 Platt 校准显著修正了两种多模态变体的校准缺陷，决策曲线分析证实，经过校准的堆叠模型在低至中等阈值范围内提供了最强的临床净获益。
