Who is TechCrunch Founder Summit for? All founders end up wrestling with the extensive, complicated mechanics of building a company from the ground up. There’s fundraising, hiring, legal, marketing, PR, and leadership, as well as the investors needing the means to vet, invest in, and develop founders as they build out their skills. Founder Summit is the best way to cover all of the challenging questions with community members who have been there, and lived to tell the tale, through constructive failure and massive success.

TechCrunch 创始人峰会面向谁？所有创始人在从零开始创建公司的过程中，最终都会面临繁杂且复杂的机制问题。这包括融资、招聘、法律、市场营销、公关和领导力等，同时投资者也需要途径来考察、投资并培养创始人，帮助他们提升技能。创始人峰会是解决所有这些挑战性问题的最佳方式，你将与那些“过来人”交流，他们通过建设性的失败和巨大的成功，积累了宝贵的经验。
