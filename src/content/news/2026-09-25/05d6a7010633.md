# AgroBench: A Reproducible Multimodal Benchmark for Weakly Supervised Crop Yield Learning from County Statistics and Pixel Observations

**AgroBench：一个基于县级统计数据和像素观测进行弱监督作物产量学习的可复现多模态基准**

***

Reliable agricultural yield statistics are typically reported at coarse administrative scales, whereas modern geospatial machine learning methods require spatially explicit, pixel level supervision. This mismatch has limited the development of large-scale benchmarks for crop yield learning using multimodal Earth observation data.

可靠的农业产量统计数据通常以粗略的行政尺度进行报告，而现代地理空间机器学习方法则需要空间明确的像素级监督。这种不匹配限制了利用多模态地球观测数据进行作物产量学习的大规模基准测试的发展。

A reproducible benchmark, AgroBench, is presented for transforming publicly available U.S. county level crop yield statistics into weakly supervised pixel-level crop time series. Each crop pixel time series is paired with a county-level yield value as a weak supervisory signal rather than a directly measured pixel-level yield label.

本文提出了一个名为 AgroBench 的可复现基准，旨在将公开的美国县级作物产量统计数据转换为弱监督的像素级作物时间序列。每个作物像素时间序列都与一个县级产量值配对，作为弱监督信号，而非直接测量的像素级产量标签。

Our geospatial data generation pipeline integrates USDA crop yield statistics with crop-specific land cover masks, Sentinel 2 multispectral imagery, Sentinel-1 synthetic aperture radar observations, climatic variables, and terrain information to produce temporally aligned multimodal sequences describing individual crop pixels throughout the growing season.

我们的地理空间数据生成流程整合了美国农业部（USDA）的作物产量统计数据、特定作物的土地覆盖掩膜、Sentinel-2 多光谱影像、Sentinel-1 合成孔径雷达观测数据、气候变量以及地形信息，从而生成在时间上对齐的多模态序列，用以描述整个生长季节中单个作物像素的情况。

The resulting benchmark contains over 13 million observations from 788,654 unique crop pixels spanning 5,107 county year combinations across eight growing seasons (2017 to 2024) for five major U.S. crops. To facilitate standardized evaluation, we establish a crop yield prediction benchmark using a Leave-One-Year-Out evaluation protocol and provide baseline results using representative machine learning models.

该基准包含来自 788,654 个独立作物像素的超过 1300 万条观测数据，涵盖了八个生长季节（2017 年至 2024 年）中 5,107 个县-年份组合，涉及美国五种主要作物。为了促进标准化评估，我们建立了一个作物产量预测基准，采用了“留一年法”（Leave-One-Year-Out）评估协议，并使用具有代表性的机器学习模型提供了基准结果。

By releasing the complete data generation pipeline, benchmark dataset, and evaluation protocol, AgroBench provides a reproducible foundation for future research in weakly supervised learning, multimodal remote sensing, spatiotemporal modeling, and geospatial foundation models for agriculture.

通过发布完整的数据生成流程、基准数据集和评估协议，AgroBench 为弱监督学习、多模态遥感、时空建模以及农业地理空间基础模型领域的未来研究提供了一个可复现的基础。
