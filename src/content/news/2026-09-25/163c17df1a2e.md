# Sadly, this $1,549 RTX 5070-equipped gaming PC is a very good deal
# 令人遗憾的是，这台配备 RTX 5070 的 1549 美元游戏电脑确实非常划算

While PC component prices remain high, you can save a good bit of money on a system by purchasing a prebuilt desktop. Walmart has a well-equipped CyberPowerPC gaming PC on sale for $1,549, almost $600 off its original price of $2,139. It’s powered by an Intel Core i7-14700KF processor, and paired with 32GB of 6000MHz DDR5 memory and an Nvidia RTX 5070 Founders Edition with 12GB of VRAM. The case is a CyberPowerPC MA-O1, an in-house design with extensive cooling options and three dedicated knobs for adjusting internal RGB lighting colors.

尽管目前电脑硬件价格依然居高不下，但通过购买品牌整机，你可以在系统配置上省下一大笔钱。沃尔玛（Walmart）目前正在促销一台配置出色的 CyberPowerPC 游戏电脑，售价为 1549 美元，较 2139 美元的原价优惠了近 600 美元。该机搭载英特尔酷睿 i7-14700KF 处理器，配备 32GB 6000MHz DDR5 内存以及拥有 12GB 显存的 Nvidia RTX 5070 公版显卡。机箱采用 CyberPowerPC 自家的 MA-O1 设计，具备出色的散热方案，并配有三个专用旋钮，可用于调节内部 RGB 灯光颜色。

While it might not feel like it at first blush, this is a solid value for a gaming PC that’s ready to rock out of the box. The RTX 5070 in this machine is likely to run you upwards of $800 by itself at the moment (it launched in 2025 for $549), and 32GB of fast memory can be $400 or more. For just a small premium, you get those components packed into a nice case with a panoramic glass panel and a 1TB PCIe 4.0 SSD inside. The whole kit is tested, with a one year warranty for parts and labor. It also includes a keyboard and mouse, so you can start gaming right away once it arrives.

虽然乍看之下可能感觉不到，但对于一台开箱即用的游戏电脑来说，这确实物超所值。目前这台机器中的 RTX 5070 显卡单买价格可能高达 800 美元以上（该显卡于 2025 年发布时售价为 549 美元），而 32GB 的高速内存售价也可能在 400 美元以上。只需支付少许溢价，你就能获得这些组件，并装配在一个带有全景玻璃侧板的精美机箱内，同时还内置了 1TB PCIe 4.0 固态硬盘。整机经过测试，并提供一年零件和人工保修。此外，它还附赠键盘和鼠标，到货后即可立即开始游戏。
