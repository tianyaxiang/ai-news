# A 3D Pose-Based Ensemble Framework for Cricket Shot Classification and Automated Biomechanical Analysis
# 基于3D姿态的板球击球分类与自动生物力学分析集成框架

**Abstract:** Cricket is one of the most celebrated sports world-wide, and technological advancement has become deeply embedded in how the modern game is analyzed and coached. Cricket shot classification and automated performance analysis add a further dimension to this trend.
**摘要：** 板球是全球最受欢迎的运动之一，技术进步已深度融入现代板球比赛的分析与教练指导中。板球击球分类和自动表现分析为这一趋势增添了新的维度。

Traditional approaches rely on RGB video features or static images, which are sensitive to environmental variations such as camera angle, lighting, and background clutter, and often fail to capture the underlying biomechanics of batting actions.
传统方法依赖于RGB视频特征或静态图像，这些方法对摄像机角度、光照和背景杂乱等环境变化非常敏感，且往往无法捕捉击球动作背后的生物力学特征。

In this paper, we propose a system to improve cricket coaching that takes raw video data, extracts batsmen from video frames using YOLO, and extracts 3D pose data from video frames using MeTRAbs. The system produces sequential skeletal pose data of 30 body points and captures the biomechanical features of a batsman.
在本文中，我们提出了一种旨在改进板球教练指导的系统。该系统接收原始视频数据，利用YOLO从视频帧中提取击球手，并使用MeTRAbs提取3D姿态数据。该系统能够生成包含30个身体关节点的序列骨架姿态数据，并捕捉击球手的生物力学特征。

As part of the system, we also propose a deep learning ensemble for shot classification of four shots: flick, pull, defense, and drive. The ensemble performed well, compared to existing classification works, achieving 97.68% accuracy.
作为该系统的一部分，我们还提出了一种深度学习集成模型，用于对四种击球动作进行分类：弹击（flick）、拉击（pull）、防守（defense）和驱动击球（drive）。与现有的分类研究相比，该集成模型表现优异，准确率达到了97.68%。

In addition, we analyzed the misclassification rates to identify cases where shots were incorrectly classified and examined their possible causes. Our proposed system allows novice players to obtain useful feedback, such as important joint angles relative to expert batsmen, which can also be useful for injury prevention.
此外，我们分析了误分类率，以识别击球动作被错误分类的情况，并探讨了其潜在原因。我们提出的系统使新手球员能够获得有用的反馈，例如与专业击球手相比的关键关节角度，这对于预防运动损伤也具有参考价值。

The shot classifier also helps track class-wise shots over time for further analysis. In addition to novice players, coaches can use the system for player evaluation.
该击球分类器还有助于长期跟踪各类击球动作，以便进行进一步分析。除了新手球员外，教练也可以利用该系统进行球员评估。
