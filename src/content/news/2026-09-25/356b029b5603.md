# nnFoundation: 3D Foundation Models for Radiology
# nnFoundation：放射学 3D 基础模型

**Abstract:** Radiological artificial intelligence has advanced rapidly, yet most systems remain narrowly task-specific, data-intensive, and fragile under domain shift. Foundation models promise more transferable and data-efficient solutions, but existing approaches are limited in scale, evaluated narrowly, and often assume that a single pretrained model can support diverse downstream tasks.

**摘要：** 放射学人工智能发展迅速，但大多数系统仍局限于特定任务，且高度依赖数据，在面对领域偏移（domain shift）时表现脆弱。基础模型有望提供更具迁移性和数据效率的解决方案，但现有方法在规模上受到限制，评估范围狭窄，且往往假设单一的预训练模型即可支持多种下游任务。

Here we present nnFoundation, complementary convolutional and transformer-based 3D radiological foundation models. Developed within the Human Radiome Project (THRP), nnFoundation is trained on 2.1 million CT, MRI, and PET image volumes from 125 institutional and public datasets.

在此，我们提出了 nnFoundation，这是一套互补的卷积和基于 Transformer 的 3D 放射学基础模型。nnFoundation 在“人类放射组计划”（Human Radiome Project, THRP）框架下开发，利用来自 125 个机构和公共数据集的 210 万个 CT、MRI 和 PET 图像卷进行训练。

We evaluate them across 108 tasks spanning segmentation, detection, classification, report generation, and image retrieval, including evaluations under domain shift, by external partners and in low-data and low-compute regimes. Across all task types, our convolution- and transformer-based nnFoundation models consistently outperform both prior 3D foundation models and training from scratch, establishing state-of-the-art performance for radiological imaging.

我们对这些模型进行了 108 项任务的评估，涵盖分割、检测、分类、报告生成和图像检索，包括在领域偏移下的评估、由外部合作伙伴进行的评估，以及在低数据和低算力环境下的评估。在所有任务类型中，我们的卷积和 Transformer 架构 nnFoundation 模型始终优于先前的 3D 基础模型以及从零开始训练的模型，确立了放射影像领域的最新技术水平（SOTA）。

However, performance follows a consistent task-dependent structure: the convolutional nnFoundation model dominates spatially localized tasks, whereas the transformer-based nnFoundation model excels in tasks requiring global semantic reasoning and in frozen-feature settings. Dynamically aligning the foundation model topology with the dataset characteristics post-hoc further improves transfer across heterogeneous 3D settings.

然而，性能表现遵循一致的“任务依赖”结构：卷积型 nnFoundation 模型在空间局部化任务中表现占优，而基于 Transformer 的 nnFoundation 模型在需要全局语义推理的任务以及冻结特征（frozen-feature）设置中表现更佳。在事后（post-hoc）动态调整基础模型拓扑结构以匹配数据集特征，进一步提升了模型在异构 3D 环境下的迁移能力。

These results show that transferable 3D radiological performance is governed not by a single universal model, but by the interplay of scalable pretraining, complementary architectures, and dataset-aware adaptation. We release nnFoundation models integrated into nnU-Net and nnDetection, enabling immediate application across established radiology workflows.

这些结果表明，可迁移的 3D 放射学性能并非由单一的通用模型决定，而是由可扩展的预训练、互补的架构以及数据集感知适应性共同作用的结果。我们发布了集成于 nnU-Net 和 nnDetection 中的 nnFoundation 模型，支持在现有的放射学工作流程中直接应用。
