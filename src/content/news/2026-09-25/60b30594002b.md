# Anonymous Men Have Turned Cyberharassment Into a Group Sport—Here’s One Woman’s Side of the Story
# 匿名男性将网络骚扰变成了一项集体运动——听听一位受害女性的讲述

Last year, Maddie Kowalski was a regular sophomore attending the University of Florida. Then a series of burner accounts on X started proliferating explicit, nonconsensual videos of her. This week, Zoë Schiffer sits down with WIRED senior writer EJ Dickson to discuss the burnerverse, a sprawling, loosely organized network of anonymous accounts that have turned the sexual humiliation of young women into an online participatory sport.

去年，Maddie Kowalski 还是佛罗里达大学的一名普通大二学生。随后，X 平台上一系列匿名小号开始大量传播未经她本人同意的私密视频。本周，Zoë Schiffer 与《连线》（WIRED）资深撰稿人 EJ Dickson 坐下来探讨了“小号宇宙”（burnerverse）——这是一个庞大且组织松散的匿名账号网络，他们将对年轻女性的性羞辱变成了一种在线参与式运动。

***

Zoë Schiffer: A quick content warning before we dive in. This conversation includes descriptions of nonconsensual sexual content and references to self-harm. Please take care while listening.

Zoë Schiffer：在深入探讨之前，先做一个简短的内容预警。本次对话包含对非自愿性内容的描述以及关于自残的提及。请在收听时注意心理调节。

***

Maddie Kowalski: When I heard that someone had sent a nude photograph of me to his friends at FSU, and in this picture I was heavily intoxicated.

Maddie Kowalski：当我听说有人把我的一张裸照发给了他在佛罗里达州立大学（FSU）的朋友时，照片里的我处于严重醉酒状态。

Zoë Schiffer：Yik Yak 是一个在大学生中很受欢迎的平台，只要你在特定的地理范围内，就可以匿名发帖。

Maddie Kowalski：一切发生得太快了，我根本无法理解这一切。

Zoë Schiffer：显然，她从未同意过这一切——无论是被拍摄，还是这些视频被发布到网上。但现在，这些内容已经传遍了 X 平台。

Maddie Kowalski：那一刻，我感觉整个世界都崩塌了。所有东西被疯狂分享和转发，我却无力阻止。

Zoë Schiffer: The videos had officially gone viral. #MaddieKowalski was trending on X nationwide, and it was viewed more than 8.5 million times. People on X were placing bets on whether by the end of the week she would either start an OnlyFans or kill herself.

Zoë Schiffer: Can you talk us through what exactly is the burnerverse? How is it organized? Who exactly is part of it?

***

EJ Dickson: So, the burnerverse is a very loosely organized network. It's unfair to even say network because it's just kind of an amorphous community of college sports fan burner accounts. So, they're all anonymous or mostly anonymous accounts. And I think it's important to note that a lot of them are pretty innocuous, but there is a segment of this community that has emerged that is pretty violently racist and misogynistic and antisemitic. And this is the corner of the subculture that has sort of made a sport out of systemically doxing women.

EJ Dickson：“小号宇宙”是一个组织非常松散的网络。说它是“网络”甚至都不准确，它更像是一个由大学体育迷小号组成的无定形社区。它们都是匿名或半匿名的账号。我认为需要指出的是，其中许多账号其实并无恶意，但该社区中出现了一部分群体，他们表现出极其暴力的种族主义、厌女症和反犹太主义倾向。正是这部分亚文化群体，将系统性地人肉搜索（doxing）女性变成了一种“运动”。
