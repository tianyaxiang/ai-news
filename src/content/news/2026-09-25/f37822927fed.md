# Now Google Chrome shares tabs to new devices that save where you were
# 现在 Google Chrome 可以在不同设备间同步标签页，并保存你的浏览进度

Google’s latest browser updates aim to help you research complex topics.
Google 最新的浏览器更新旨在帮助你研究复杂的课题。

Google is rolling out some new Chrome browser features that are designed to make it easier to research and study complicated topics. The cross-device tab switching feature is getting a memory upgrade, alongside new capabilities for Gemini in Chrome that can analyze more types of media and generate interactive study quizzes.
Google 正在推出一些新的 Chrome 浏览器功能，旨在让研究和学习复杂课题变得更加轻松。跨设备标签页切换功能迎来了一次“记忆升级”，同时 Chrome 中的 Gemini 也获得了新能力，可以分析更多类型的媒体内容并生成互动学习测验。

Chrome has an existing feature that allows you to send a tab from your mobile to a computer (or vice versa) so you can quickly access the same webpage when switching devices. Now, this feature will also remember exactly where you had scrolled to on that tab when you switch, sparing you from having to manually find where you left off — a useful timesaver if you’re reading lengthy research documents. The update also preserves any half-filled forms, letting you complete them on desktop if doing so on mobile is tedious, or switch to mobile to finish them on the go.
Chrome 现有的功能允许你将标签页从手机发送到电脑（反之亦然），以便在切换设备时快速访问同一个网页。现在，该功能在切换时还会准确记住你滚动到的位置，省去了手动寻找上次阅读进度的麻烦——如果你正在阅读冗长的研究文档，这将非常节省时间。此次更新还会保留未填完的表单，如果你觉得在手机上填写繁琐，可以在电脑上完成，或者切换到手机以便随时随地继续填写。

Now you don’t have to scroll or search for keywords to pick up where you left off.
现在，你无需再滚动页面或搜索关键词来寻找上次中断的地方。

Gemini in Chrome is also getting some new studying-focused perks. Capabilities that allow the AI bot to analyze, visualize, and summarize documents, webpages, and YouTube videos are being expanded to more types of media “to help you get more out of all of the educational content the web has to offer,” according to Google’s blog announcement. This includes podcasts and videos hosted outside of YouTube.
Chrome 中的 Gemini 也获得了一些专注于学习的新功能。根据 Google 的博客公告，AI 机器人分析、可视化和总结文档、网页及 YouTube 视频的能力正在扩展到更多类型的媒体，旨在“帮助你从网络提供的所有教育内容中获得更多收获”。这包括托管在 YouTube 之外的播客和视频。

“Listening to a podcast on ancient Rome, or reviewing a recording of your last philosophy lecture?,” Google said. “Gemini in Chrome can identify key takeaways, find specific information, or clarify confusing moments for almost any audio or video file once you’ve played it through.”
“正在听关于古罗马的播客，或者复习上一节哲学课的录音？”Google 表示，“一旦你播放了音频或视频文件，Chrome 中的 Gemini 就能识别关键要点、查找特定信息，或为你澄清其中令人困惑的片段。”

Chrome’s Gemini integration can also now generate interactive quizzes based on the content that’s currently displayed on your open tab, providing another way to test your knowledge on subjects outside of just swotting up on notes and study materials. This feature is currently rolling out to the US and India in English on desktop, and will expand to other regions and mobile devices “in the coming months.”
Chrome 的 Gemini 集成现在还可以根据当前打开的标签页内容生成互动测验，除了死记硬背笔记和学习资料外，这为你提供了一种测试知识掌握程度的新方法。该功能目前正在美国和印度以英语形式在桌面端推出，并将在“未来几个月内”扩展到其他地区和移动设备。
