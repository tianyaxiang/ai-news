### A congressional representative just proposed killing America’s border tower program
### 一位国会议员提议终止美国的边境塔项目

Delia Ramirez, a Democratic US representative from Illinois, has announced plans to introduce legislation to terminate the surveillance tower program along the country’s southern border. The announcement comes just days after publication of an MIT Technology Review investigation, “Dying on Camera,” which looked at deaths near these towers. Nearly one in four deaths we analyzed between 2015 and early 2026 occurred within their advertised range. Ramirez, who sits on the Homeland Security Committee, cited our reporting that nearly “1,100 people have died within range of a billion-dollar surveillance tower system between 2015 and 2026,” adding, “The towers that we have paid a billion dollars to just don’t work.” Here’s what she’s proposing and what it could mean for the border surveillance program. —Eileen Guo
来自伊利诺伊州的美国民主党众议员 Delia Ramirez 宣布计划提出立法，以终止美国南部边境的监控塔项目。此前几天，《麻省理工科技评论》发表了题为《镜头下的死亡》（Dying on Camera）的调查报道，关注了这些监控塔附近的死亡事件。在我们分析的 2015 年至 2026 年初期间，近四分之一的死亡事件发生在这些监控塔的覆盖范围内。身为国土安全委员会成员的 Ramirez 引用了我们的报道，指出“2015 年至 2026 年间，有近 1100 人死于价值十亿美元的监控塔系统范围内”，并补充道：“我们花费十亿美元建造的这些塔根本不起作用。”以下是她的提议内容及其对边境监控项目可能产生的影响。——Eileen Guo

### Roundtables: the deadly failures of the virtual border wall
### 圆桌会议：虚拟边境墙的致命失败

The US spent billions building the “virtual wall” of surveillance towers along its southern border, promising they will help detect and apprehend border crossers and save lives. But MIT Technology Review has documented more than a thousand people who moved through areas watched by these towers without being caught—and ultimately died there.
美国耗资数十亿美元在南部边境建造了由监控塔组成的“虚拟墙”，承诺这些设施将有助于发现和抓捕越境者并挽救生命。但《麻省理工科技评论》记录了超过一千人在这些监控塔监视的区域内穿行而未被发现，并最终在那里丧生。

### AI is dominating the conversation at Climate Week —Casey Crownhart
### AI 成为气候周讨论的焦点 ——Casey Crownhart

AI is the unavoidable topic at this year’s New York Climate Week, with tension growing over its costs and benefits. The technology is attracting more attention and money to energy technologies, some of which are low- or zero-emission. But the data center buildout has come with a hefty environmental toll, and a whole lot of natural gas is coming online to meet the demand.
