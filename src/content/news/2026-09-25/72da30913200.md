# HYDRO: Towards Non-Reversible Face De-Identification Using a High-Fidelity Hybrid Diffusion and Target-Oriented Approach
# HYDRO：一种基于高保真混合扩散与目标导向的不可逆人脸去标识化方法

**Abstract:** Target-oriented face de-identification models aim to anonymize the identity of a target individual across different images or video frames, such that the target can no longer be reliably recognized, while maintaining key characteristics of the visual data.
**摘要：** 目标导向的人脸去标识化模型旨在跨不同图像或视频帧对目标个体的身份进行匿名化处理，使得目标无法再被可靠识别，同时保持视觉数据的关键特征。

Such models commonly leverage generative encoder-decoder architectures to manipulate facial appearances, enabling them to produce realistic high-fidelity de-identification results, while ensuring considerable attribute-retention capabilities.
此类模型通常利用生成式编码器-解码器架构来操纵面部外观，从而能够产生逼真的高保真去标识化结果，同时确保相当程度的属性保留能力。

However, target-oriented models also carry the risk of inadvertently preserving subtle identity cues, making them (potentially) reversible and susceptible to reconstruction attacks.
然而，目标导向模型也存在无意中保留细微身份线索的风险，使其（潜在地）具有可逆性，并容易受到重构攻击。

To address this problem, we introduce in this paper a novel (robust) face de-identification approach, called HYDRO, that combines target-oriented models with a dedicated diffusion process specifically designed to destroy any imperceptible information that may allow learning to reverse the de-identification procedure.
为了解决这一问题，我们在本文中提出了一种名为 HYDRO 的新型（鲁棒）人脸去标识化方法。该方法将目标导向模型与专门设计的扩散过程相结合，旨在消除任何可能允许学习反向推导去标识化过程的不可察觉信息。

HYDRO first de-identifies the given face image, injects noise into the de-identification result to impede reconstruction, and then applies a diffusion-based recovery step to improve fidelity and minimize the impact of the noising process on the data characteristics.
HYDRO 首先对给定的人脸图像进行去标识化处理，向去标识化结果中注入噪声以阻碍重构，随后应用基于扩散的恢复步骤，以提高保真度并最大限度地减少噪声过程对数据特征的影响。

To further improve image fidelity and better retain gaze directions, a novel Eye Similarity Discriminator (ESD) is also introduced and incorporated it into the training of HYDRO.
为了进一步提高图像保真度并更好地保留注视方向，我们还引入了一种新型眼部相似度判别器（Eye Similarity Discriminator, ESD）并将其整合到 HYDRO 的训练中。

Extensive quantitative and qualitative experiments on three diverse datasets demonstrate that HYDRO exhibits state-of-the-art (SOTA) fidelity and attribute-retention capabilities, while being the only target-oriented method resilient against reconstruction attacks.
在三个不同数据集上进行的广泛定量和定性实验表明，HYDRO 展现出了最先进（SOTA）的保真度和属性保留能力，同时也是唯一能够抵御重构攻击的目标导向方法。

In comparison to multiple SOTA competitors, HYDRO reduces the success of reconstruction attacks by 85.7% on average.
与多个 SOTA 竞争对手相比，HYDRO 将重构攻击的成功率平均降低了 85.7%。
