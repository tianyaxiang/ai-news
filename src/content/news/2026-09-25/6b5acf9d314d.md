# COPE: Continual Personalization of LLMs under Sparse User Feedback via User Embeddings and Self-Evaluation
# COPE：通过用户嵌入和自我评估在稀疏用户反馈下实现大语言模型的持续个性化

While Large Language Models (LLMs) have achieved remarkable results across various benchmarks, their alignment with normative values often results in homogenized responses that fail to address diverse user preferences.
尽管大语言模型（LLMs）在各种基准测试中取得了显著成果，但它们与规范性价值的对齐往往导致回答趋于同质化，无法满足多样化的用户偏好。

Existing training-free methods often occupy valuable context windows through prompt engineering, while training-based methods typically remain static post-training, failing to support the continual optimization required in real-world settings.
现有的免训练方法通常会通过提示工程（Prompt Engineering）占用宝贵的上下文窗口，而基于训练的方法在训练完成后通常保持静态，无法支持现实场景中所需的持续优化。

To address these challenges, we propose COPE (Continual Optimization with Personalized embedding and self-Evaluation), a novel optimization framework tailored for real-world-motivated interaction settings with sparse user feedback.
为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了 COPE（基于个性化嵌入和自我评估的持续优化），这是一种专为具有稀疏用户反馈的现实交互场景量身定制的新型优化框架。

Our framework assigns learnable personalized embeddings to each user and synergistically integrates preference capture, self-evaluation calibration, and personalized response optimization within a single update step.
我们的框架为每个用户分配了可学习的个性化嵌入，并在单个更新步骤中协同集成了偏好捕捉、自我评估校准和个性化响应优化。

A key innovation of our method is the use of self-evaluation to generate proxy rewards, enabling continuous model updates even when explicit user feedback is unavailable.
我们方法的一个关键创新在于利用自我评估来生成代理奖励（proxy rewards），即使在缺乏明确用户反馈的情况下，也能实现模型的持续更新。

Experiments show that COPE consistently outperforms strong training-free and training-based baselines under sparse feedback, and remains complementary to Retrieval-Augmented Prompting (RAP).
实验表明，在稀疏反馈条件下，COPE 的表现始终优于强大的免训练和基于训练的基准模型，并且与检索增强提示（RAP）具有互补性。

Further analyses confirm COPE's reliable self-evaluation, meaningful preference patterns, stable general capabilities, and robustness under shifting preferences and alternative evaluators.
进一步的分析证实了 COPE 具有可靠的自我评估能力、有意义的偏好模式、稳定的通用能力，以及在偏好发生变化和使用替代评估器时的鲁棒性。
