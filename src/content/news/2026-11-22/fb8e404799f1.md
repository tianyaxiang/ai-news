# The FBI and USDA were reportedly hacked

据报道，美国联邦调查局（FBI）和美国农业部（USDA）遭到黑客攻击。

The list of hacks hitting the US government over the last few months keeps growing.

过去几个月里，针对美国政府的黑客攻击名单不断增加。

Friday, it was reported that both the US Department of Agriculture (USDA) and the FBI were hacked. In reporting on the FBI hack, CNN noted that this is the second such attack on the agency in the last few months, this time targeting the National Crime Information Center (NCIC), which federal, state, and local law enforcement use to share information, including warrants and missing persons. President Donald Trump later confirmed the hack in an interview with Fox News. NBC first reported the USDA hack on Friday.

周五有报道称，美国农业部和联邦调查局均遭到黑客攻击。在报道联邦调查局遭袭事件时，美国有线电视新闻网（CNN）指出，这是该机构在过去几个月内第二次遭遇此类攻击。此次攻击的目标是国家犯罪信息中心（NCIC），该中心供联邦、州和地方执法部门共享包括逮捕令和失踪人员信息在内的数据。唐纳德·特朗普总统随后在接受福克斯新闻采访时证实了此次黑客攻击。美国全国广播公司（NBC）于周五率先报道了美国农业部遭袭的消息。

According to Reuters: While the payments database is the known extent of the intrusion so far, the former officials warned that the compromised system could provide a backdoor to more sensitive information, including food safety data and research on animal-borne illnesses, according to the report.

据路透社报道：虽然目前已知的入侵范围仅限于支付数据库，但前官员们警告称，受损系统可能会为获取更敏感的信息提供后门，其中包括食品安全数据和动物源性疾病研究。

The breaches come following a week of major outages across several sectors, including at the FAA and NSA, which the government has sought to reassure the public were not the result of a cyberattack.

在此次泄露事件发生前的一周，包括美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）和国家安全局（NSA）在内的多个部门发生了重大故障，政府曾试图向公众保证这些故障并非网络攻击所致。

President Trump suggested that the breach of the FBI might not be an entirely bad thing, telling Fox News that, “Maybe we’ll learn about the voter fraud, all the voter fraud that took place,” and “wouldn’t it be great to learn about Russia, Russia, Russia?” referring to investigations into his ties to Russia.

特朗普总统暗示，联邦调查局遭到的入侵可能并非完全是坏事。他在接受福克斯新闻采访时表示：“也许我们能了解到选民欺诈，所有发生的选民欺诈事件，”并称“如果能了解到关于俄罗斯、俄罗斯、俄罗斯的事情，那不是很好吗？”他指的是针对其与俄罗斯关系的调查。

The FBI suffered a breach, reported in late September, that allegedly compromised personal records of “all FBI employees and applicants.” This was followed by a hack of DHS, reported in October, comprising 40,000 employee records. In the wake of these major outages, the White House national cyber director, Sean Cairncross, stepped down this week.

联邦调查局在9月下旬曾遭遇一次泄露事件，据称泄露了“所有联邦调查局员工和申请人”的个人记录。随后，10月份又报道了国土安全部（DHS）遭黑客攻击，涉及4万名员工的记录。在这些重大故障发生后，白宫国家网络总监肖恩·凯恩克罗斯（Sean Cairncross）于本周辞职。
