John Roberts, the voice of Linda Belcher, is just happy to finish a thing. The comedian and musician tries not to overthink things and get it done.

琳达·贝尔彻（Linda Belcher）的配音演员约翰·罗伯茨（John Roberts）只要能完成一件事就很开心。这位喜剧演员兼音乐家尽量不去过度思考，而是直接把事情做完。

You probably know John Roberts best as the voice of Linda Belcher on *Bob’s Burgers*. The show just wrapped up its 16th season and shows no signs of slowing down anytime soon. The show remains a reliable source of laughs, top-notch puns, and frankly wholesome fun.

你可能最熟悉约翰·罗伯茨作为《鲍勃汉堡店》（Bob’s Burgers）中琳达·贝尔彻的配音演员的身份。该剧刚刚结束了第16季，目前没有任何放缓的迹象。这部剧依然是笑声、顶级双关语和坦率说来健康有趣的可靠来源。

Comedy is just part of Roberts’ life, however. In addition to his appearances on *Gravity Falls* and *Comedy Bang! Bang!*, he’s also a musician with deep roots in New York City’s queer nightlife scene. He just released his latest record, *I Am*, on Friday, and it dabbles in ’80s pop, ’90s house, and even dashes of ’00s rave.

然而，喜剧只是罗伯茨生活的一部分。除了在《怪诞小镇》（Gravity Falls）和《喜剧大爆炸》（Comedy Bang! Bang!）中的客串外，他也是一位在纽约市酷儿夜生活圈中有着深厚根基的音乐家。他刚于周五发行了最新唱片《I Am》，其中融合了80年代流行乐、90年代浩室音乐，甚至还带有一丝00年代锐舞音乐的色彩。

But while the record sounds made for the dance floor, it actually tackles some pretty heavy subject matter, like mortality, aging, and grief following the loss of his mother. Despite that, Roberts is still having fun, as you can see in the video for “Complicated,” which revels in the queer cult camp of *Scorpio Rising*, William Friedkin’s *Cruising*, and *The Wolf Man*.

虽然这张唱片听起来像是为舞池而作，但它实际上探讨了一些相当沉重的主题，比如死亡、衰老以及失去母亲后的悲伤。尽管如此，罗伯茨依然乐在其中，正如你在《Complicated》的视频中所看到的那样，该视频沉浸在《天蝎座升起》（Scorpio Rising）、威廉·弗莱德金的《虎口巡航》（Cruising）以及《狼人》（The Wolf Man）等酷儿邪典电影的夸张风格中。
