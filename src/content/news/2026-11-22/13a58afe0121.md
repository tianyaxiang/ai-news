The Xiaomi 18 Pro proves two screens aren’t better than one. The rear display isn’t good for much, but these are great phones in almost every other respect.

小米 18 Pro 证明了双屏幕并不比单屏幕更好。虽然背部显示屏的用途有限，但这些手机在其他几乎所有方面都表现出色。

Xiaomi introduced a second screen to its flagship phones with last year’s 17 Pros, but didn’t release them outside China. That means the new 18 Pro and Pro Max are the first chance I’ve had to find out if a screen on the back of a phone is any more useful now than it was when Xiaomi last tried it on 2021’s Mi 11 Ultra.

小米在去年的 17 Pro 系列旗舰手机中引入了副屏，但并未在中国以外的市场发布。这意味着全新的 18 Pro 和 Pro Max 是我第一次有机会验证，手机背部的屏幕在今天是否比小米 2021 年在小米 11 Ultra 上尝试时更有用。

After a week or two with both of Xiaomi’s new Pro phones, I’m not entirely sold. The rear display has a single, obvious use case — it’s helped me take some of the best selfies I’ve ever managed — but it isn’t very useful otherwise.

在体验了这两款小米新 Pro 手机一两周后，我并没有完全被说服。背部显示屏有一个明显的用途——它确实帮我拍出了一些我拍过的最好的自拍——但在其他方面并没有太大用处。

The rest of the phones are great, with good cameras, big batteries, and a more flexible take on the privacy display seen in the Samsung Galaxy S26 Ultra. There’s a lot to like. But with prices higher than ever thanks to the RAM crisis, it’s hard not to wonder how much buyers are paying for a second screen they could probably do without.

手机的其他部分表现优异，拥有出色的摄像头、大容量电池，以及比三星 Galaxy S26 Ultra 更灵活的隐私显示功能。这些手机有很多值得称赞的地方。但由于内存危机导致价格居高不下，人们不禁会想，消费者为这块可能并不需要的副屏多付了多少钱。

There are two Xiaomi 18 Pro phones: the £1,199 Pro (around $1,570) and the £1,399 Pro Max (around $1,830). In the UK and Europe, they’re priced to compete directly with the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max.

小米 18 Pro 系列有两款机型：售价 1199 英镑（约合 1570 美元）的 Pro 和售价 1399 英镑（约合 1830 美元）的 Pro Max。在英国和欧洲，它们的定价旨在与 iPhone 18 Pro 和 Pro Max 直接竞争。

The rear screens also vary slightly in size between the phones, though at either 2.7 or 2.9 inches, there’s not much in it. Wrapping around the two main rear camera lenses, they look a lot like the displays you’ll find on the front of foldables like the Galaxy Z Flip 8, though smaller than most of those, and much more limited in functionality.

两款手机的背部屏幕尺寸略有不同，但无论是 2.7 英寸还是 2.9 英寸，差别都不大。它们环绕在两个主摄像头镜头周围，看起来很像 Galaxy Z Flip 8 等折叠屏手机外侧的显示屏，尽管尺寸更小，功能也更加受限。

Unlike flip phones, you can’t use the small screen to access the phone’s settings, and you certainly can’t run full-blown apps. Instead, there’s a natural-language AI tool to create your own apps, powered by Google’s Gemini 4 Argon. It’s good for simple tools or games, but your creations can’t connect to the internet or access other apps, so anything you make will be quite purposefully limited.

与翻盖手机不同，你无法通过这块小屏幕访问手机设置，更无法运行完整的应用程序。取而代之的是一个由谷歌 Gemini 4 Argon 驱动的自然语言 AI 工具，用于创建你自己的应用。它适合制作简单的工具或游戏，但你创作的内容无法连接互联网或访问其他应用，因此你制作的任何东西都会受到刻意的功能限制。

The only thing that’s convinced me the rear screen is at all worthwhile is its camera viewfinder. The UI’s a little fiddly at this scale, but you can take photos, record videos, or create a photo booth-inspired picture set, all using the rear main camera. I’ve taken the best selfies of my life with this phone, which may be reason enough for some people to buy it.

唯一让我觉得这块副屏有价值的功能是它的相机取景器。虽然在这么小的尺寸下操作界面有些繁琐，但你可以使用后置主摄像头拍照、录像或制作类似照相亭风格的照片集。我用这款手机拍出了我人生中最好的自拍，对于某些人来说，这或许已经足够成为购买它的理由了。
