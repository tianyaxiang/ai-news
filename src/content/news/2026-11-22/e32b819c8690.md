# Amazon is complaining about NYC Council on shipping labels, too

亚马逊也在就运输标签问题向纽约市议会提出投诉。

Amazon is taking its accusations that the Council is driving up prices offline and onto your packages.

亚马逊正在将其关于市议会推高价格的指控从线上转移到你的包裹上。

Amazon is leaving no venue unexplored in its fight against the New York City Council and the Delivery Protection Act. The company is now putting labels on shipments claiming that the Council could add up to $5 to any Amazon order.

亚马逊在与纽约市议会及《快递保护法案》（Delivery Protection Act）的博弈中，不放过任何一个阵地。该公司目前正在运输包裹上贴上标签，声称市议会的举措可能会使每笔亚马逊订单增加高达 5 美元的成本。

The Delivery Protection Act would effectively ban the sort of third-party arrangements Amazon currently uses to deliver many packages, including small companies and independent contractors. Last-mile delivery firms operating warehouses would need to be licensed by the city and directly employ their workers.

《快递保护法案》实际上将禁止亚马逊目前用于配送许多包裹的第三方合作模式，包括小型公司和独立承包商。运营仓库的“最后一公里”配送公司将需要获得城市许可，并直接雇佣其员工。

Earlier this week, Amazon started showing notifications in its mobile app and on its website reading “Attention NYC Council could add up to $5 per order.” Now the company is including an almost identical message on shipping labels.

本周早些时候，亚马逊开始在其移动应用程序和网站上显示通知，内容为“注意：纽约市议会可能会使每笔订单增加高达 5 美元的费用”。现在，该公司正在运输标签上加入几乎相同的信息。

Amazon claims that the bill, if passed, “would likely mean slower and more expensive delivery for millions of customers in New York City,” and has threatened to move its warehouses outside of the city.

亚马逊声称，如果该法案获得通过，“对于纽约市数百万客户而言，这很可能意味着配送速度变慢且成本更高”，并威胁要将其仓库搬出该市。

However, labor unions, Council Speaker Julie Menin, and Mayor Zohran Mamdani have countered that the company is attempting to “threaten New Yorkers and intimidate this City Council” and is acting out of “greed,” pointing to Amazon’s market cap of nearly $3 trillion.

然而，工会、市议会议长朱莉·梅宁（Julie Menin）和市长佐兰·马姆达尼（Zohran Mamdani）反驳称，该公司正试图“威胁纽约市民并恐吓市议会”，其行为出于“贪婪”，并指出亚马逊的市值已接近 3 万亿美元。
