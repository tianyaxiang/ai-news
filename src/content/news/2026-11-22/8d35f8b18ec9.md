The DropGrand Theft Auto VI. I mean, this literally could be the whole newsletter. But it appears this game is everything we’ve been waiting for, and I anticipate spending too much of the rest of my life playing it. No regrets.

精选：《侠盗猎车手 VI》。我的意思是，这简直可以作为整篇通讯的内容。但看来这款游戏确实是我们一直期待的一切，我预计余生的大部分时间都会花在玩它上面。绝不后悔。

The GTA VI soundtrack. I can’t think of many games whose soundtracks have this kind of power: It’s a 34-song compilation, including a bunch of new music from huge artists. I’m going to listen to this while I work, which I assume will make all my writing cooler and more crime-filled.

《GTA VI》原声带。我想不出有多少游戏的配乐能有这种力量：这是一张包含 34 首歌曲的合辑，其中包括许多大牌艺人的新歌。我打算在工作时听这个，我想这会让我的写作变得更酷、更有犯罪感。

The Teenage Engineering EP/BC-1. I’ve been thinking about rebooting my physical media collection, and I can’t think of a better way to start than with this beautiful CD player. Like all Teenage Engineering gear, it is somehow retro and futuristic at the same time, and it’ll slide neatly into a synth setup or just look extremely rad in your living room.

Teenage Engineering EP/BC-1。我一直在考虑重启我的实体媒体收藏，我想不出比这款精美的 CD 播放器更好的开始了。像所有 Teenage Engineering 的设备一样，它既复古又充满未来感，可以完美融入合成器设置，或者仅仅是放在客厅里看起来就非常酷。

Alfred 6. A long-awaited update to the launcher and workflow tool so many Mac users love. I see some nice interface changes here but otherwise not a massively different product… but that’s actually probably the right call. Alfred is very good at being Alfred.

Alfred 6。这是许多 Mac 用户喜爱的启动器和工作流工具期待已久的更新。我看到了一些不错的界面变化，但除此之外并没有太大的不同……但这可能正是正确的做法。Alfred 做好它自己就足够了。

The TRMNL Frame. A 31.5-inch, programmable color E Ink screen that you can hang on your wall. This either immediately excites you, and $1,299 is a small price to pay, or does nothing for you and is way too expensive. I accept both reactions equally. I also want one so badly.

TRMNL Frame。这是一个可以挂在墙上的 31.5 英寸可编程彩色电子墨水屏。这要么会让你立刻兴奋起来，觉得 1299 美元物有所值，要么让你毫无感觉，觉得它太贵了。我同样接受这两种反应。我也非常想要一个。

Tasks in Gemini. A stupid, stupid, stupid name for an otherwise useful product! Gemini now has a lot of the same “do stuff for you” tools that Muse and Dots offer — this time with much better access to your Google data. I’m slowly working through my inbox with this one.

Gemini 中的 Tasks。对于一个本来很有用的产品来说，这名字真是蠢、蠢、蠢透了！Gemini 现在拥有许多与 Muse 和 Dots 提供的“代办”工具相同的功能——而且这次对您的 Google 数据有了更好的访问权限。我正在用它慢慢处理我的收件箱。

Who Wants This? Fun new podcast in which two writers and comedians attempt to make sense of current trends, and whether anyone actually… wants this. The first episode is about protein and proteinmaxxing, and I both enjoyed the hang and learned a lot. It also made me feel like everyone has just completely lost their minds.

《Who Wants This?》这是一个有趣的新播客，两位作家兼喜剧演员试图解读当前的趋势，以及是否真的有人……想要这些东西。第一集是关于蛋白质和“蛋白质最大化”的，我既享受了收听过程，也学到了很多。它也让我觉得每个人都彻底疯了。

The Altruists. We are due for many, many fictionalized versions of the FTX story. I’ve heard and read some mixed things about this new Netflix series, but I dig the idea of trying to play it as both a love story and a scam story. It really is both those things!

《The Altruists》。我们注定会看到许多关于 FTX 故事的虚构版本。关于这部新的 Netflix 剧集，我听到和读到了一些褒贬不一的评价，但我很喜欢它试图将其既作为爱情故事又作为诈骗故事来呈现的构思。它确实兼具这两者！

ClockwiseTV. Respect to Hank Green for being one of the few people bold enough to try and compete with YouTube. The pitch here is smart and simple: curated content for kids, on a timer, no ads, no autoplay. This is immediately going on all my iPads.

ClockwiseTV。向 Hank Green 致敬，他是少数几个有胆量尝试与 YouTube 竞争的人之一。这里的卖点既聪明又简单：为孩子们精心策划的内容，有定时器，没有广告，没有自动播放。我所有的 iPad 上都要立刻装上它。

“CIA Meeting Cold Open.” Last week’s SNL was really good overall (though maybe I just love John Mulaney?) but the cold open, with the tech billionaires learning how not to fall for a honeypot, absolutely slayed me. The spray bottle on Elon Musk makes me laugh every single time.

“CIA Meeting Cold Open”。上周的《周六夜现场》（SNL）整体非常棒（虽然可能只是因为我喜欢 John Mulaney？），但那个关于科技亿万富翁学习如何不掉入美人计陷阱的开场小品，简直让我笑翻了。对 Elon Musk 使用喷雾瓶的桥段每次都让我大笑。

Screen shareYou may not know Tom Hovey, but I can practically guarantee you’ve seen his work. That’s because, in addition to many other cool projects, Tom’s also the artist behind those illustrated graphics of everyone’s bakes on The Great British Baking Show. His unique style has been all over that show, and my TV screen, for many years.

屏幕共享：您可能不认识 Tom Hovey，但我几乎可以保证您见过他的作品。这是因为，除了许多其他很酷的项目外，Tom 还是《英国烘焙大赛》中那些展示每个人烘焙作品的插图背后的艺术家。多年来，他独特的风格一直充斥着那个节目，也充斥着我的电视屏幕。

I found Tom’s work recently on Threads, when he went massively viral after debunking the idea that all those food drawings are AI with a video to prove how he does it. He’s been working with digital tools for a decade, he said, “but never ever AI.”

我最近在 Threads 上发现了 Tom 的作品，当时他因为发布了一段视频，证明了那些食物绘画并非 AI 生成，而是他亲手绘制的，从而在网上疯传。他说，他使用数字工具工作已经十年了，“但从未使用过 AI。”
