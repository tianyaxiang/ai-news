# Best Laptops for Travel (2026): Which to Take on the Road

As a laptop reviewer, I have plenty of devices to choose from when I travel for work. In some cases, my favorite overall laptop doesn’t translate into the one I want to travel with. When I’m squeezed into a plane, I’ll choose maximum battery life and portability over features like a bigger screen or more ports.

作为一名笔记本电脑评测员，我在因公出差时有大量的设备可供选择。在某些情况下，我最喜欢的笔记本电脑并不一定是我旅行时想带的那一台。当我在飞机上被挤在狭小的空间里时，我会选择超长的续航和便携性，而不是更大的屏幕或更多的接口等功能。

Fortunately, there are many great travel laptops, and you’ll still appreciate them when you aren’t on the road. Of the hundreds of laptops I’ve tested in my life, here are the ones I always choose to travel with.

幸运的是，市面上有许多出色的旅行笔记本电脑，即使在不旅行时，你也会欣赏它们的表现。在我一生测试过的数百台笔记本电脑中，以下是我出差时总是会选择的几款。

### The Smallest Laptop to Travel With: Dell XPS 13 (2026)

### 最适合旅行的小型笔记本电脑：Dell XPS 13 (2026)

If there’s an obvious pick for the best travel laptop, it’s the Dell XPS 13. It’s been the best compact laptop you can buy for years, and that’s even more true in its latest incarnation. The key dimension to pay attention to on the XPS 13 is the depth, which is crucial for air travel. At 7.9 inches, it’s among the smallest laptops you can buy.

如果说最佳旅行笔记本电脑有一个显而易见的选择，那就是 Dell XPS 13。多年来，它一直是你所能买到的最好的紧凑型笔记本电脑，而在其最新版本中，这一点表现得尤为突出。XPS 13 需要关注的关键尺寸是深度，这对航空旅行至关重要。其 7.9 英寸的深度使其成为你能买到的最小巧的笔记本电脑之一。

Those economy-class seat-back tray tables on airplanes are only around 10 inches deep, and while that’s technically enough to accommodate even a 16-inch MacBook Pro, whether you can actually open the lid and use the laptop is another question. The XPS 13 is even smaller than the MacBook Neo, despite having a larger screen.

飞机经济舱座椅背后的托盘桌深度仅约 10 英寸，虽然从技术上讲，这足以容纳一台 16 英寸的 MacBook Pro，但你是否真的能打开盖子并使用它则是另一回事。尽管屏幕更大，但 XPS 13 的体积甚至比 MacBook Neo 还要小。

### The Lightest Travel Laptop: Asus Zenbook A14

### 最轻便的旅行笔记本电脑：Asus Zenbook A14

If weight is your primary concern, the Asus Zenbook A14 is another contender. Despite being larger overall than the XPS 13 (with a bigger screen), the Zenbook A14 weighs 2.16 pounds—a hair lighter than the Dell. That’s seriously impressive, and I still remember how astounded I was when I first picked it up.

如果你最关心的是重量，那么 Asus Zenbook A14 是另一个强有力的竞争者。尽管整体尺寸比 XPS 13 更大（屏幕也更大），但 Zenbook A14 的重量仅为 2.16 磅，比戴尔那款还要轻一点点。这确实令人印象深刻，我至今仍记得第一次拿起它时有多么惊讶。

Asus debuts its “ceraluminum” material in the chassis, which gives you the sturdiness of aluminum with the lightness of plastic. It’s not quite as rigid as metal, but it’s not as flimsy as ultra-light laptops in the past.

华硕在其机身中首次采用了“陶瓷铝”（ceraluminum）材料，它既有铝材的坚固，又有塑料的轻盈。它虽然不像纯金属那样刚硬，但也不像过去那些超轻笔记本电脑那样脆弱。

### Well-Balanced Alternatives: Apple MacBook Air (M5, 2026)

### 均衡的选择：Apple MacBook Air (M5, 2026)

The 13-inch MacBook Air also makes a great travel laptop. It’s relatively small, thanks to its 13.6-inch screen. In terms of portability, its main advantage over the XPS 13 and Zenbook A14 is its thinness. At 0.42 inches, it’s the thinnest laptop out there—and it doesn’t stick huge rubber feet on the bottom to compensate like other laptops do, such as the Razer Blade 14.

13 英寸的 MacBook Air 也是一款出色的旅行笔记本电脑。得益于 13.6 英寸的屏幕，它的体积相对较小。在便携性方面，它相比 XPS 13 和 Zenbook A14 的主要优势在于其纤薄程度。0.42 英寸的厚度使其成为目前市面上最薄的笔记本电脑，而且它不像 Razer Blade 14 等其他笔记本电脑那样，为了弥补厚度而在底部安装巨大的橡胶垫。
