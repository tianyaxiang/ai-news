我不了解楼主的具体情况。我接下来要说的话是给其他人的建议，并非对楼主的批评。

If you suspect that you may be the target of a layoff, DO NOT SIMPLY QUIT. In this scenario you are a potential liability to your employer. They will almost certainly pay you to leave if they decide to part ways.

如果你怀疑自己可能是裁员的目标，千万不要直接辞职。在这种情况下，你是雇主的潜在负债。如果他们决定让你走，几乎肯定会支付补偿金。

It is always easier to find a job when you have a job. Maybe you think if there are layoffs and you are retained that it will be a miserable working environment. Fine. DO NOT QUIT. After a round of layoffs you are relatively safe and you can use that time to find another job.

在职时找工作总是更容易。也许你认为如果发生裁员而你被留下了，工作环境会很糟糕。没关系，千万别辞职。在一轮裁员之后，你相对安全，可以利用这段时间去寻找下一份工作。
