### What You Will Build
A Make scenario that watches your Google Sheets expense log and syncs every new or updated row into a Notion budget tracker, with monthly totals and budget remaining. ### The Workflow
Make watches the expense log for a new or updated row, and the scenario starts the moment one changes. Make maps the row's fields into the Notion budget tracker, and Notion rolls each category up into a November month total of $541.00 and subtracts it from the $800.00 monthly budget — leaving $259.00. Both figures update with every sync.

### 工作流程
Make 会监控支出日志中是否有新增或更新的行，一旦发生变化，场景即刻启动。Make 将行字段映射到 Notion 预算跟踪器中，Notion 将每个类别汇总为 11 月总计 541.00 美元，并从 800.00 美元的月度预算中扣除，剩余 259.00 美元。这两个数字会在每次同步时更新。

### Step 1: Build the Google Sheets Expense Log
Each row holds a date, a merchant, a category, and an amount. Keep the category names identical to the ones in your Notion tracker, because Make matches on those names. November's fictional demo rows total $541.00: Food & Drink $312.40, Transport $96.10, Software $58.00, and Office $74.50.

### 第一步：构建 Google Sheets 支出日志
每一行包含日期、商家、类别和金额。请确保类别名称与 Notion 跟踪器中的名称完全一致，因为 Make 是根据这些名称进行匹配的。11 月的虚构演示行总计 541.00 美元：餐饮 312.40 美元、交通 96.10 美元、软件 58.00 美元、办公 74.50 美元。

### Step 2: Trigger on a New or Updated Row
In Make, set the scenario to watch the expense log for a new or updated row. A new expense or a corrected amount starts it — nothing syncs twice.

### 第二步：针对新增或更新行触发
在 Make 中，设置场景以监控支出日志中的新增或更新行。一笔新的支出或修正后的金额都会启动该流程——没有任何内容会被重复同步。

### Step 3: Map the Fields into Notion
Map the fields into the Notion budget tracker (copy as-is):
{
"Date": "expense date",
"Merchant": "expense merchant",
"Category": "expense category — must match the tracker category names exactly",
"Amount": "expense amount",
"Month total (Notion formula)": "sum of Amount for the month, per category — November demo total $541.00",
"Budget remaining (Notion formula)": "Monthly budget − Month total — November demo: $800.00 − $541.00 = $259.00"
}
Date goes to the date property, merchant to the name, category to the category select, and amount to the amount field. One row in Sheets becomes one synced entry in Notion.

### 第三步：将字段映射到 Notion
将字段映射到 Notion 预算跟踪器（按原样复制）：
{
"日期": "支出日期",
"商家": "支出商家",
"类别": "支出类别——必须与跟踪器中的类别名称完全匹配",
"金额": "支出金额",
"月度总计（Notion 公式）": "该月每个类别的金额总和——11 月演示总计 541.00 美元",
"剩余预算（Notion 公式）": "月度预算 - 月度总计——11 月演示：800.00 美元 - 541.00 美元 = 259.00 美元"
}
日期对应日期属性，商家对应名称，类别对应类别选择，金额对应金额字段。Sheets 中的一行对应 Notion 中同步的一条条目。

### Step 4: Monthly Totals and Budget Remaining in Notion
Let Notion do the maths. The tracker rolls each category up into a November month total of $541.00, and subtracts it from your $800.00 monthly budget — leaving $259.00. Both figures update with every sync.

### 第四步：Notion 中的月度总计和剩余预算
让 Notion 进行计算。跟踪器将每个类别汇总为 11 月总计 541.00 美元，并从 800.00 美元的月度预算中扣除，剩余 259.00 美元。这两个数字会在每次同步时更新。

### Step 5: Test with the Dummy Data
Drop in the fictional November rows, then check the month total reads $541.00 and the remaining figure reads $259.00.

### 第五步：使用虚拟数据进行测试
输入虚构的 11 月数据行，然后检查月度总计是否显示为 541.00 美元，剩余金额是否显示为 259.00 美元。

### The Finished Result
Every expense synced, a November total of $541.00, and $259.00 left of an $800.00 budget — visible in Notion the moment the sheet changes.

### One Honest Limit
The sync is only as good as the sheet — if a category name is misspelled in Sheets, it will not match the Notion tracker, and the row lands uncategorised or fails to sync. Keep your category names identical in both places. This tracks a budget, it does not replace an accountant.

### 一个诚实的局限性
同步的效果取决于表格的质量——如果 Sheets 中的类别名称拼写错误，它将无法与 Notion 跟踪器匹配，导致该行无法分类或同步失败。请确保两处的类别名称完全一致。这只是预算跟踪，不能替代会计。
