# The Manosphere Isn’t Ready for Brothercore

On a sweltering July morning, in the middle of an empty playground in suburban Denver, two extremely fit, sweaty, shirtless male influencers are tussling in the dirt.

在一个闷热的七月早晨，丹佛郊区一个空荡荡的操场中央，两名身材极好、汗流浃背且赤裸上身的男性网红正在泥地上扭打。

“The guy on top who’s in control, we call that position riding,” one of the grapplers, Carlos Monteon Jr. pauses to explain to a group of eight similarly fit, sweaty, nearly all shirtless men who are watching the action in a circle. Monteon is a wrestler and influencer from Arizona, with pomelo-sized biceps, glossy shoulder-length hair, and more than 675,000 followers across TikTok and Instagram. He turns to his demo partner, Johnny Cole, a rangy, then 28-year-old content creator (59,000 TikTok followers) with a penchant for slutty tank tops. “And this one is called the Saturday night ride.”

“上面那个占据控制权的人，我们称这个姿势为骑乘，”其中一名摔跤手卡洛斯·蒙特翁（Carlos Monteon Jr.）停下来向围成一圈观看的八名同样身材健硕、汗流浃背且几乎都赤裸上身的男子解释道。蒙特翁是一位来自亚利桑那州的摔跤手兼网红，拥有柚子般大小的二头肌、光泽的及肩长发，在 TikTok 和 Instagram 上拥有超过 67.5 万名粉丝。他转向他的演示搭档约翰尼·科尔（Johnny Cole），一位当时 28 岁、身材修长且偏爱性感背心的内容创作者（拥有 5.9 万名 TikTok 粉丝）。“而这一招叫做‘周六夜骑乘’。”

Monteon and Cole continue scuffling. The match ends with Monteon straddling Cole, their faces inches apart, their torsos glistening. The scene looks like a JV wrestling match.

蒙特翁和科尔继续扭打。比赛以蒙特翁跨坐在科尔身上结束，两人脸贴着脸，躯干闪烁着汗水。这一幕看起来就像一场初级校队的摔跤比赛。

The other men pair up to take turns grappling. Two cameramen circle them as a drone hums overhead.

其他人两两配对，轮流进行格斗。两名摄影师围着他们拍摄，头顶上方还有一架无人机在嗡嗡作响。

“Yes, testosterone!” one influencer yells. “And estrogen!” Cole adds. “Both of them together, as one!”

“没错，睾酮！”一名网红大喊。“还有雌激素！”科尔补充道。“两者合二为一！”

I’m at Hey Brother House, a content incubator that hosts weeklong retreats for male social media influencers. The organization teaches men and boys “to embody healthy masculinity, fully process emotions, embrace authenticity, and show up as compassionate, empathetic community leaders,” according to its website.

我目前身处“嘿兄弟之家”（Hey Brother House），这是一个为男性社交媒体网红举办为期一周静修活动的内容孵化器。根据其网站介绍，该组织旨在教导男性和男孩“体现健康的男子气概，充分处理情绪，拥抱真实自我，并以富有同情心和共情能力的社区领袖身份出现。”

Cole, the founder of Hey Brother, helms the project with two other house leaders. Jonathan Jacobs, an affable 37-year-old who owns a digital marketing agency in Los Angeles, is wearing a brown cap that says “Male Friendship.” Matthew Echelman, a 35-year-old Brooklyn-based filmmaker, has hired two crew members to film the whole thing for a possible future documentary. Funded by a grant of more than $180,000 from an initiative called the Young Men and Media Collective, Hey Brother House’s goal is to flood 18-to-25-year-old men’s social media feeds with content that will (hopefully) make them less terrible.

“嘿兄弟”的创始人科尔与另外两名负责人共同领导该项目。乔纳森·雅各布斯（Jonathan Jacobs）是一位 37 岁的和蔼可亲的洛杉矶数字营销公司老板，他戴着一顶写有“男性友谊”字样的棕色帽子。马修·埃切尔曼（Matthew Echelman）是一位 35 岁的布鲁克林电影制作人，他雇佣了两名工作人员全程拍摄，以备未来制作纪录片。在名为“青年与媒体集体”（Young Men and Media Collective）倡议提供的超过 18 万美元资助下，“嘿兄弟之家”的目标是用内容充斥 18 至 25 岁男性的社交媒体信息流，希望以此让他们变得不那么糟糕。

The house itself is a rented stucco McMansion next to the Green Valley Ranch Golf Club, a residential community just outside Denver. In some respects, it looks like your average fratty domicile: There’s a Nintendo Switch console in the living room, where the boys play Mario Kart; a basement decked out with fitness equipment, a sauna, air hockey, and Foosball; and a makeshift indoor putting green. But there’s also a strict house alcohol ban, remote-controlled bidets, a box of wigs, swag boxes filled with fuchsia “Redefining Masculinity” T-shirts, and a wall covered in Post-Its of “gifts” the men can give each other, including “laying down on couch or blanket in park with my head on your lap.” There’s even a room—the “Man Box”—for testimonials, where the “cast members” can speak directly to the camera about their day. It’s reminiscent of reality TV, except most everyone is aware of bell hooks’ All About Love: New Visions, and they are explicitly here to make friends.

这栋房子本身是位于丹佛郊外绿谷牧场高尔夫俱乐部旁的一栋租来的灰泥豪宅。在某些方面，它看起来就像普通的兄弟会宿舍：客厅里有任天堂 Switch 游戏机，男孩们在那里玩《马里奥赛车》；地下室配备了健身器材、桑拿房、空气曲棍球和桌上足球；还有一个临时的室内推杆果岭。但这里也有严格的禁酒令、遥控坐浴盆、一箱假发、装满紫红色“重新定义男子气概”T 恤的礼品盒，以及一面贴满“礼物”便签的墙，这些礼物是男人们可以互相给予的，包括“躺在公园的沙发或毯子上，把头枕在你的膝盖上”。甚至还有一个名为“男人盒子”（Man Box）的房间用于发表感言，在那里，“演员们”可以直接对着镜头讲述他们的一天。这让人想起真人秀，只是每个人都知道贝尔·胡克斯（bell hooks）的《关于爱：新愿景》（All About Love: New Visions），而且他们来这里的明确目的就是交朋友。

These guys are all in Denver because, as Cole puts it while he smokes Marlboro Lights on the porch, “men and boys are not doing well. And they have not been doing well for a long time.” The evidence supporting this is manifold: Young men are less likely than their female counterparts to finish high school and then enroll in college. They’re dropping out of the job market, and they’re more likely to report loneliness and isolation, as well as more likely to die by suicide.

这些人都在丹佛，正如科尔在门廊抽着万宝路香烟时所说：“男人和男孩们现在的处境并不好。而且他们已经很久没有好转了。”支持这一点的证据有很多：年轻男性完成高中学业并进入大学的可能性低于女性。他们正在退出就业市场，更有可能报告感到孤独和与世隔绝，也更有可能自杀。

Part of the problem is the manosphere, the social media ecosystem populated by looksmaxxers, incels, AI-touting hustle bros, and peptide-injecting fitness influencers. Initially a relatively fringe coalition, the “manosphere” has evolved into an online force to be reckoned with, thanks to the massive success of influencers like Jordan Peterson, a former academic who has advocated for all-meat diets and climate-change denialism; and, later, Andrew Tate, a failed kickboxer turned minor webcam impresario, who is currently facing more than 40 criminal charges in the UK and Romania, including trafficking and sex crimes. By preaching traditional masculinity as a path toward self-empowerment, so-called manosphere influencers have appealed to an audience of young men disillusioned by the rise of identity politics. Social media algorithms have happily amplified that message: By 2023, clips featuring Tate had more than 13 billion views on social media platforms.

问题的一部分在于“男人圈”（manosphere），这是一个充斥着外貌至上主义者、非自愿独身者（incels）、鼓吹人工智能的奋斗兄弟以及注射肽类药物的健身网红的社交媒体生态系统。最初，“男人圈”只是一个相对边缘的联盟，但由于乔丹·彼得森（Jordan Peterson）等网红的巨大成功，它已演变成一股不可忽视的网络力量。彼得森曾是一名学者，主张全肉饮食和气候变化否定论；后来，失败的自由搏击选手转行成为网络摄像头企业家的安德鲁·泰特（Andrew Tate）也加入了这一行列，他目前在英国和罗马尼亚面临 40 多项刑事指控，包括人口贩运和性犯罪。通过宣扬传统男子气概作为自我赋权的途径，所谓的“男人圈”网红吸引了对身份政治兴起感到幻灭的年轻男性受众。社交媒体算法乐于放大这一信息：到 2023 年，泰特的视频片段在社交媒体平台上的浏览量已超过 130 亿次。

During the 2024 election, an estimated 67 percent of white male zoomers voted for Trump, resulting in a flurry of discourse about the manosphere’s power in channeling the frustrations of young men. “[They’ve] never had brotherhood or friendship,” Cole tells me. “They’ve never had another man look them in the eyes and say they’re worthy.” Men like Tate are successful, he says, because they “weaponize this insecurity and this fragility and give them a scapegoat and say, ‘If you buy my course and you learn how to drop-ship, or come to my alphas-only elite men's circle, then you’ll become a high-value man.’”

在 2024 年大选期间，估计有 67% 的白人男性 Z 世代投票给了特朗普，这引发了关于“男人圈”在引导年轻男性挫败感方面所发挥作用的大量讨论。“他们从未拥有过兄弟情谊或友谊，”科尔告诉我。“他们从未有过另一个男人直视他们的眼睛并告诉他们，他们是有价值的。”他说，像泰特这样的男人之所以成功，是因为他们“将这种不安全感和脆弱性武器化，给他们一个替罪羊，并说：‘如果你购买我的课程并学习如何代发货，或者加入我仅限阿尔法男性的精英圈子，那么你就会成为一个高价值的男人。’”
