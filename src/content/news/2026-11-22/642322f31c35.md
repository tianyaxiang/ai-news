# The Outages Are Just Getting Started

Save this storySave this storyWelcome to Kernel Panic, a weekly newsletter by Lily Hay Newman and Matt Burgess from inside the new world of privacy and digital security. To receive this newsletter in your inbox each week, sign up here.Over the last week, you have surely noticed the disruptive streak of technical outages that hit everything from Outlook to Apple, a half dozen US banks, United Airlines, and even the NSA’s classified network. While there’s no evidence that these were cyberattacks, speculation has run wild about the cause—including a pervasive feeling that AI is to blame.

欢迎阅读《Kernel Panic》，这是一份由 Lily Hay Newman 和 Matt Burgess 撰写的每周通讯，带您深入了解隐私与数字安全的新世界。如需每周在收件箱中接收此通讯，请在此注册。在过去的一周里，您肯定注意到了那一连串破坏性的技术故障，它们影响了从 Outlook 到苹果、六家美国银行、联合航空，甚至是美国国家安全局（NSA）的机密网络等方方面面。虽然没有证据表明这些是网络攻击，但关于其原因的猜测甚嚣尘上，其中就包括一种普遍的担忧，即人工智能（AI）难辞其咎。

So let’s just be clear: The incidents were definitely not caused by some sort of coordinated AI hacking spree and, taken individually, they all seem to have routine explanations. But as AI impacts the dynamics of digital defense more and more, security researchers and analysts have increasingly concluded that AI pressures are starting to play a role in real-world tech failures.

因此，我们需要明确一点：这些事件绝对不是由某种协同的 AI 黑客攻击造成的，而且单独来看，它们似乎都有常规的解释。但随着 AI 对数字防御动态的影响日益加深，安全研究人员和分析师越来越得出结论：AI 带来的压力正开始在现实世界的技术故障中扮演角色。

AI-assisted vulnerability discovery has unleashed a torrent of software bugs. Attackers are then using AI tools to exploit those vulnerabilities faster, which puts more pressure than ever on organizations to quickly patch their systems. This leaves often under-resourced IT teams scrambling to evaluate a massive number of patches and push the updates to systems on which countless people and organizations rely. Researchers and practitioners have told us again and again that AI’s biggest impact is that it speeds everything up.

AI 辅助的漏洞发现引发了大量的软件错误。攻击者随后利用 AI 工具更快地利用这些漏洞，这给组织带来了前所未有的压力，迫使它们必须迅速修补系统。这使得资源往往不足的 IT 团队疲于奔命，忙于评估海量的补丁，并将更新推送到无数个人和组织所依赖的系统中。研究人员和从业者反复告诉我们，AI 最大的影响在于它加快了一切进程。

Microsoft’s massive 365 outage last week was caused by a flawed update in its infrastructure exacerbated by other network failures. Officials said this week that the NSA outage to its internal communication system was due to botched patching following October’s critical PHP updates. Apple also said its snafus this week related to an internal update. AI vulnerability discovery and the resulting patching are “putting tremendous strain on systems that weren’t built to handle that pace of change,” US senator Mark Warner said in a statement on Tuesday. And more “instability” could be coming, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella said this week, because a “patch needs to go instantly from being available to being patched in production because it’s being exploited in the wild.”

微软上周大规模的 365 服务中断是由其基础设施中一个有缺陷的更新引起的，并因其他网络故障而加剧。官员们本周表示，NSA 内部通信系统的中断是由于 10 月份关键的 PHP 更新后修补不当造成的。苹果公司也表示，本周的故障与内部更新有关。美国参议员马克·华纳（Mark Warner）在周二的一份声明中表示，AI 漏洞发现及其随后的修补工作“给那些并非为应对这种变革速度而构建的系统带来了巨大压力”。微软首席执行官萨提亚·纳德拉（Satya Nadella）本周表示，未来可能会出现更多“不稳定”情况，因为“补丁一旦可用，就需要立即应用到生产环境中，因为它正在被野外利用”。

External pressures and bugs in patches are not new, and they’ve always caused tech failures. Researchers also point out that many organizations around the world have a significant amount of what’s often called “tech debt,” because they have cut corners and failed to invest in upgrading and maintaining their digital systems for years. This practice was always risky, but organizations that didn’t have the resources or didn’t want to devote them to digital security could gamble with out-of-date systems and missing safeguards and typically avoid the worst consequences.

外部压力和补丁中的错误并非新鲜事，它们一直都会导致技术故障。研究人员还指出，全球许多组织都存在大量所谓的“技术债务”，因为它们多年来一直在走捷径，未能投资于升级和维护其数字系统。这种做法一直存在风险，但那些没有资源或不愿将资源投入到数字安全中的组织，过去往往可以通过使用过时的系统和缺失的安全防护来赌一把，并通常能避免最严重的后果。

“Historically, even a couple of years ago, a vendor releases a patch for a nasty bug. You might have some time to do some validation and apply it,” says Joe Slowik, the director of threat research and cyber engineering at Dataminr. “Now, that may not even be an option, because we’re talking about turnaround times that completely defeat the ability to do patch verification.”

Dataminr 威胁研究与网络工程总监 Joe Slowik 表示：“从历史上看，即使是在几年前，当供应商发布针对严重漏洞的补丁时，你可能还有一些时间来进行验证并应用它。现在，这甚至可能不再是一个选项，因为我们所谈论的周转时间完全剥夺了进行补丁验证的能力。”

IT administrators are leaning on AI more as well to accelerate their evaluation and prepare their systems for updates, but current AI tools still can’t replace human analysis and planning. “AI can help, but people still need to understand what went wrong, check that the patch addresses the cause, and look for similar issues elsewhere,” says Lea Schönherr, a tenure-track faculty member at CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security, a German national research institution. “Rolling out patches across widely used systems also takes time.”

IT 管理员也越来越依赖 AI 来加速评估并为系统更新做准备，但目前的 AI 工具仍然无法取代人类的分析和规划。德国国家研究机构 CISPA 亥姆霍兹信息安全中心（CISPA Helmholtz Center for Information Security）的终身教职人员 Lea Schönherr 表示：“AI 可以提供帮助，但人们仍然需要了解哪里出了问题，检查补丁是否解决了根本原因，并寻找其他地方是否存在类似问题。在广泛使用的系统中部署补丁也需要时间。”

Matthew Prince, CEO and cofounder of the web infrastructure giant Cloudflare, predicts that “it’s going to feel like the sky is falling for two years.” He suspects, though, that eventually AI acceleration will help defenders get on top of common vulnerabilities and better secure software in general. “These new threats and new security vulnerabilities won’t happen as often, and the background noise will actually tick down quite a bit,” he predicts.

网络基础设施巨头 Cloudflare 的首席执行官兼联合创始人 Matthew Prince 预测，“未来两年感觉就像天要塌下来一样”。不过他推测，最终 AI 的加速将帮助防御者掌握常见漏洞，并总体上更好地保护软件。他预测：“这些新的威胁和新的安全漏洞不会那么频繁地发生，背景噪音实际上会减少很多。”

Still, the evidence that AI has fundamentally changed cybersecurity is mounting. This week the total number of vulnerabilities found in 2026 surpassed 100,000. To put that into perspective, in 2022, the final year before LLMs went mainstream, there were around 25,000 recorded vulnerabilities. And this year’s figure, with more than a month still to go, is double the combined totals of 2024 and 2025.

尽管如此，AI 已经从根本上改变了网络安全的证据正在不断增加。本周，2026 年发现的漏洞总数超过了 10 万个。为了对比说明，在 LLM 成为主流之前的最后一年（2022 年），记录在案的漏洞约为 2.5 万个。而今年的数字，在距离年底还有一个多月的情况下，已经是 2024 年和 2025 年总和的两倍。

In the last few days, researchers disclosed that they used AI to find critical vulnerabilities in PHP (for the second time), open source web server software NGINX, and programming language Java. Forbes also reported on 35 vulnerabilities across iPhones, Macs, iPads, Apple Watches, and other products from the company that had been discovered by researchers using AI.

在过去几天里，研究人员披露他们利用 AI 发现了 PHP（第二次）、开源 Web 服务器软件 NGINX 以及编程语言 Java 中的关键漏洞。《福布斯》还报道称，研究人员利用 AI 在 iPhone、Mac、iPad、Apple Watch 以及苹果公司的其他产品中发现了 35 个漏洞。

There are important nuances to consider, though. Not all AI-discovered vulnerabilities are ultimately that impactful. “The number of vulnerabilities has certainly exploded, but the number of vulnerabilities that you need to care about has remained somewhat constant,” Slowik says. “The caveat is that the pace at which they’re being used or weaponized has increased dramatically.”

不过，有一些重要的细微差别需要考虑。并非所有 AI 发现的漏洞最终都具有那么大的影响力。Slowik 说：“漏洞的数量确实呈爆炸式增长，但你需要关心的漏洞数量却保持相对恒定。需要注意的是，它们被利用或武器化的速度已经大幅增加。”

Only 300 vulnerabilities have been added to the US Cybersecurity and Infrastructure Security Agency’s catalog of exploited vulnerabilities this year—meaning the flaws that hackers successfully used to their advantage is far lower than the total number of discovered bugs. VulnCheck, which runs its own database, puts the number of known exploited vulnerabilities higher, at 1,500 so far in 2026, which is still just 1.5 percent of the total number of bugs discovered this year.

今年只有 300 个漏洞被添加到美国网络安全与基础设施安全局（CISA）的已利用漏洞目录中，这意味着黑客成功利用的缺陷远低于发现的漏洞总数。运营自己数据库的 VulnCheck 将已知被利用的漏洞数量定得更高，2026 年迄今为 1500 个，这仍然仅占今年发现的漏洞总数的 1.5%。
