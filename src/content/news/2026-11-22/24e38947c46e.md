I’m in California to visit Oratomic and then Inkhaven. I woke up this morning to the news that Levent Alpöge says that he’s gotten Anthropic models to prove the Generalized Riemann Hypothesis, producing 17.4 million (!!) lines of Lean code.

我正在加利福尼亚州拜访Oratomic，随后要去Inkhaven。今天早上醒来时，我看到新闻说Levent Alpöge声称他已经让Anthropic的模型证明了广义黎曼猜想，并生成了1740万（！！）行Lean代码。

At a dinner last night, someone asked me “so, how much longer until an AI proves the Riemann Hypothesis?  Still several more years?”  I replied, don’t be sure of such things anymore.  After all, AI has already proved that the nontrivial zeros of the Riemann zeta function are at most 3/8 away from the critical line (Quanta just published its article on that breakthrough yesterday!), and that already carries most of the implications for number theory that a full proof of RH would have.  So who the hell knows what else is possible, and how quickly?

昨晚在晚餐时，有人问我：“那么，人工智能还需要多久才能证明黎曼猜想？还需要几年吗？”我回答说，不要再对这类事情如此笃定了。毕竟，人工智能已经证明了黎曼ζ函数的非平凡零点距离临界线最多只有3/8（《量子杂志》昨天刚刚发表了关于这一突破的文章！），这已经涵盖了黎曼猜想的完整证明对数论的大部分意义。所以，谁他妈知道还有什么是不可能的，以及它们会以多快的速度实现呢？

For those tuning in from home, the Riemann Hypothesis says (in its easiest-to-understand version) that the number of prime numbers from 1 to n is very close to what it would be if each integer x>1 had an independent 1/ln(x) probability of being prime, up to a standard statistical error term that never exceeds Õ(√n).  While we’ve known since 1896 that the number of primes from 1 to n is asymptotic to n/ln(n) (that’s the famous Prime Number Theorem), you could’ve imagined the deviations being much larger.

对于在家收看的朋友，黎曼猜想（以最易于理解的版本）指出，从1到n的素数数量非常接近于如果每个整数x>1都有独立的1/ln(x)概率成为素数时的预期值，其误差项不超过标准的统计误差项Õ(√n)。虽然我们自1896年以来就知道从1到n的素数数量渐近于n/ln(n)（这就是著名的素数定理），但你本可以想象偏差会大得多。

David Hilbert famously said, “If I were to awaken after having slept for a thousand years, my first question would be: Has the Riemann Hypothesis been proven?”  Meanwhile G. H. Hardy, on a ship that he feared might sink, sent his colleagues a telegram claiming to have proven RH, so that if the worst came to pass, at least the world might believe that a proof sank with him.

大卫·希尔伯特曾有名言：“如果我沉睡千年后醒来，我的第一个问题将是：黎曼猜想被证明了吗？”与此同时，G. H. 哈代在一艘他担心会沉没的船上，给同事发了一封电报，声称自己已经证明了黎曼猜想，这样即使最坏的情况发生，至少世人可能会相信一个证明随他一同沉没了。

Many will of course question whether the proof announced today will stand.  They’ll point out that, despite how heavily the world is now “lean-ing” on it, the Lean formal verification system still has issues, from kernel bugs (which have, in the past, allowed proofs of 0=1) to uncertain soundness of the underlying type theory.  In my view, these issues can and should urgently be fixed!  Until they are, a sufficiently motivated AI could almost certainly exploit them to prove a false statement!

当然，许多人会质疑今天宣布的证明是否站得住脚。他们会指出，尽管世界现在如此严重地“依赖”（lean-ing）它，但Lean形式化验证系统仍然存在问题，从内核漏洞（过去曾允许证明0=1）到底层类型理论的不确定可靠性。在我看来，这些问题可以也应该被紧急修复！在修复之前，一个动机充足的人工智能几乎肯定可以利用它们来证明一个错误的陈述！

All the same, I see zero indications that that’s happening right now.  I note that, so far, not one of the AI results with Lean certificates has needed to be retracted (while several without Lean certificates have needed to be). So I’m not 100% sure, but my bet is that the proof will stand.

尽管如此，我目前没有看到任何迹象表明这种情况正在发生。我注意到，到目前为止，还没有一个带有Lean证书的人工智能结果需要被撤回（而几个没有Lean证书的结果则需要被撤回）。所以我不敢百分之百确定，但我打赌这个证明是成立的。

Mathematicians of course won’t be satisfied with 17 million lines of Lean.  They’ll be satisfied when, and only when, they’re able to explain and distill and reconstruct the ideas for themselves.  And that will probably take years, since human mathematicians move only at biological speeds.  And while they’re working on it, how many more such wonders will there be, in this age of wonders and terrors?

数学家们当然不会满足于1700万行的Lean代码。只有当他们能够亲自解释、提炼并重构这些思想时，他们才会感到满足。这可能需要数年时间，因为人类数学家的工作速度仅限于生物学范畴。而在他们研究的过程中，在这个充满奇迹与恐怖的时代，还会有多少这样的奇迹出现呢？

My 9-year-old son, informed that RH was proved, replied that he’s not even surprised at this point.  Maybe you need to have come of age in the human math era to be surprised by such things.

我9岁的儿子在得知黎曼猜想被证明后，回答说他现在甚至都不感到惊讶了。也许你需要是在人类数学时代成长起来的，才会对这类事情感到惊讶。

The P=?NP problem now moves firmly into place as the Final Boss of the Math We’ve Cared About, the position I always thought it should occupy.  But for how much longer?  For how much longer will anything last that we currently take for granted?

P=?NP问题现在稳固地成为了我们所关心的数学问题的“最终BOSS”，这正是我一直认为它应该占据的位置。但还能持续多久呢？我们目前认为理所当然的一切，还能持续多久呢？

It was only a couple days ago that my wife and I had the following conversation:

就在几天前，我和妻子进行了如下对话：

Dana: I turned off the irrigation, yet somehow water in our house is still leaking. We need to…

Dana：我关掉了灌溉系统，但不知何故，我们房子里的水还在漏。我们需要……

Me: Look, a lot of the smartest people I know now think that recursive self-improvement and the end of the entire known world are coming in 12-18 months. This leak…

我：听着，我认识的许多最聪明的人现在都认为，递归自我改进和整个已知世界的终结将在12到18个月内到来。这个漏水……

Dana: We should still fix the leak.

Dana：我们还是应该修好漏水。
