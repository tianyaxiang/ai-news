At this year’s World Economic Forum meeting in Davos, US President Donald Trump stated that people from Somalia have low IQs. “I always say these are low-IQ people,” he said in his speech. His son, Donald Trump Jr, made a similar statement about Haitian people in 2024 (Trump’s government has since ended the protected status of both Somali and Haitian migrants in the US).

在今年达沃斯世界经济论坛会议上，美国总统唐纳德·特朗普声称索马里人智商低下。他在演讲中说：“我总是说这些人是低智商人群。”他的儿子小唐纳德·特朗普在2024年也对海地人发表了类似的言论（特朗普政府此后已终止了索马里和海地移民在美国的受保护身份）。

These statements and their consequences are symptoms of a growing trend in Western politics: the re-emergence of racial views of human nature that have been largely in hiding for the past 70 years. Narratives about the innate intellectual inferiority of black or brown people compared with white people—which can still be found in academic journals today—are fuelling far-right propaganda in some of the world’s largest democracies.

这些言论及其后果是西方政治中一种日益增长的趋势的症状：过去70年里基本处于隐蔽状态的关于人性的人种观点正在重新抬头。关于黑人或棕色人种在智力上天生劣于白人的叙事——如今仍能在学术期刊中找到——正在助长一些世界大国中的极右翼宣传。

Such claims underpin misinformation strategies on social media and bolster political movements. These narratives are sometimes euphemistically referred to as “race science” or more recently “race realism.” But, as we argue, they are not actually scientific.

此类主张支撑着社交媒体上的虚假信息策略，并为政治运动推波助澜。这些叙事有时被委婉地称为“种族科学”，最近则被称为“种族现实主义”。但正如我们所论证的那样，它们实际上并非科学。

One member of this community, Nathan Cofnas, recently attracted public attention by accusing the late Jason Arday of plagiarism. What got less attention was that Cofnas has links to a network of people who make supposedly scientific claims about the innate intelligence, criminality, or sexuality of different racial groups.

该群体的一名成员内森·科夫纳斯（Nathan Cofnas）最近因指控已故的杰森·阿迪（Jason Arday）剽窃而引起公众关注。较少受到关注的是，科夫纳斯与一个网络有关联，该网络中的人对不同种族群体的天生智力、犯罪倾向或性取向提出所谓的科学主张。

Some within this network are affiliated with universities, giving their work the mask of legitimacy. This group produces work that has the appearance of science, but which is methodologically deeply flawed and fails to engage with rigorous scientific research. Yet they regularly succeed in publishing this work in (a handful of) mainstream academic journals. How do they manage to do this, and what are the consequences?

该网络中的一些人隶属于大学，这为他们的工作披上了合法性的外衣。该群体所产出的作品具有科学的外表，但在方法论上存在严重缺陷，且未能参与严谨的科学研究。然而，他们却经常成功地将这些作品发表在（少数几家）主流学术期刊上。他们是如何做到这一点的，其后果又是什么？

Scientific shortcomings: biology and genetics. Contemporary race scientists position themselves as persecuted but legitimate scientists who are prevented from pursuing their work for ideological reasons. They sometimes refer to what they defend as hereditarianism. This is the idea that genetic differences play a key causal role in determining differences in human behavior and intelligence between human groups—such as races—over purely environmental differences.

科学缺陷：生物学与遗传学。当代种族科学家将自己定位为受到迫害但合法的科学家，声称他们因意识形态原因而无法开展工作。他们有时将自己捍卫的观点称为“遗传决定论”。这一观点认为，在决定人类群体（如种族）之间的人类行为和智力差异时，遗传差异起着关键的因果作用，而非纯粹的环境差异。

Yet scientific evidence has shown repeatedly and from many points of view that hereditarian thinking is both flawed and wrong. Race scientists make extremely simplistic claims such as: “Black people have innately lower intelligence than white people, because selection pressures resulted in the evolution of higher intelligence in white people.” No part of this claim can be scientifically supported; not from the point of view of genetics, evolutionary biology, or psychology.

然而，科学证据已从多个角度反复证明，遗传决定论的思维既有缺陷又是错误的。种族科学家提出极其简单化的主张，例如：“黑人天生智力低于白人，因为选择压力导致白人进化出了更高的智力。”这一主张的任何部分都无法得到科学支持；无论是从遗传学、进化生物学还是心理学的角度来看皆是如此。

In the 1970s, the influential evolutionary biologist Richard Lewontin famously showed that there is more genetic variation within human groups than between them. Socially defined racial groups just aren’t that genetically different from one another. There is genetic variation—though we’re actually a relatively genetically similar species compared to closely related primates.

20世纪70年代，具有影响力的进化生物学家理查德·列万廷（Richard Lewontin）曾著名地指出，人类群体内部的遗传变异远大于群体之间的变异。社会定义的种族群体在遗传上并没有那么大的差异。虽然存在遗传变异，但与近亲灵长类动物相比，我们实际上是一个遗传上相对相似的物种。

But this variation doesn’t fall neatly into clusters that correlate with how we typically categorize races. Skin tone and color do vary between geographical locations. So do some other physiological features like hair texture, or the ability to process oxygen or milk products. But these differences are associated with characteristics of the environment rather than with racial groups.

但这种变异并不会整齐地划分为与我们通常分类种族相对应的集群。肤色确实因地理位置而异。其他一些生理特征，如头发质地，或处理氧气或乳制品的能力也是如此。但这些差异与环境特征有关，而非与种族群体有关。

Some genetic variants associated with white skin color, for example, may have originated in Africa. Similarly, the ability to digest milk is seen in some populations in Europe, Africa, and Asia, rather than being an exclusively “Aryan” characteristic, as far right groups sometimes claim. We now know considerably more about the human genome than Lewontin did in the 1970s, but his conclusions are supported today. Knowing someone’s race just doesn’t tell us a great deal about their genetics.

例如，一些与白皮肤相关的遗传变异可能起源于非洲。同样，消化乳制品的能力在欧洲、非洲和亚洲的一些人群中都能见到，而不是像极右翼团体有时声称的那样，是“雅利安人”独有的特征。我们现在对人类基因组的了解比20世纪70年代的列万廷要多得多，但他的结论在今天依然成立。了解一个人的种族并不能告诉我们太多关于其遗传学的信息。

Recent scientific advances allowing us to examine ancient DNA from archaeological sites help explain why genetic differences do not fall neatly along racial lines. This research paints a picture of constant mixing between human groups, as waves of migration washed to and fro across the globe. It ultimately makes any belief in the genetic “purity” of European or African populations meaningless.

最近的科学进展使我们能够检查考古遗址中的古DNA，这有助于解释为什么遗传差异不会整齐地沿着种族界限划分。这项研究描绘了一幅人类群体不断融合的图景，随着一波又一波的迁徙在全球范围内往复。这最终使得任何关于欧洲或非洲人口遗传“纯洁性”的信仰都变得毫无意义。

While human genetics is fascinatingly complex, race scientists ignore this complexity in favor of a far-too-simplistic view of humanity. And this view is not supported by the work of scientists who have used rigorous scientific methods to further our understanding of our species.

虽然人类遗传学极其复杂，但种族科学家却忽视了这种复杂性，转而支持一种过于简单化的人性观。而这种观点并没有得到那些使用严谨科学方法来加深我们对自身物种理解的科学家的支持。

Challenges from social science. When race scientists say that black people have lower intelligence than white people, they typically base that on claims of differences in IQ test results. But the data underlying these claims is often shaky, to say the least. Hereditarians have relied heavily on a database that claims to provide average IQs for countries worldwide: the “national IQ” database.

来自社会科学的挑战。当种族科学家声称黑人的智力低于白人时，他们通常基于智商测试结果差异的说法。但支撑这些说法的证据充其量往往是站不住脚的。遗传决定论者严重依赖一个声称提供全球各国平均智商的数据库：“国家智商”数据库。

Researchers have repeatedly demonstrated that this database is deeply methodologically flawed. It isn’t possible to measure intelligence in the same way in very different cultures, with different exposure to formal education. That’s because we know that education is one of several other factors linked to differences in IQ.

研究人员已反复证明，该数据库在方法论上存在严重缺陷。在受正规教育程度不同的截然不同的文化中，不可能以相同的方式衡量智力。这是因为我们知道，教育是与智商差异相关的若干因素之一。

The methods used to put the database together did not come even close to meeting typical standards of scientific rigor. There’s even analysis suggesting that low IQ samples were deliberately chosen to represent sub-Saharan Africa, to make the IQ for black populations artificially low. Meanwhile, the Flynn effect, showing that IQ has risen considerably during the 20th and 21st centuries, clearly demonstrates that IQ scores are heavily determined.

用于构建该数据库的方法甚至远未达到科学严谨性的典型标准。甚至有分析表明，为了使黑人群体的智商被人为地压低，研究者特意选择了低智商样本来代表撒哈拉以南非洲。与此同时，“弗林效应”表明智商在20世纪和21世纪有了显著提高，这清楚地证明了智商分数在很大程度上是受决定的。
