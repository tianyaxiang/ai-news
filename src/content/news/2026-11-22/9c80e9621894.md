Thank you for the ordering by how digested the results are. That ordering is the whole story, and it is worth being explicit about why.

For the first two results the digestion path is already visible: human-readable proof, strategy broadly clear, Lean formalisation on top. A reader can form a judgement in an afternoon. The third has no such path yet. A Lean-only artifact of that size is a different kind of object, and the question “is it right” splits into questions that resolve on very different timescales: does the headline statement in Lean say what we mean by GRH; does the archive compile against a known-good Lean with only standard axioms; is the strategy something a number theorist recognises as a proof. The Zulip thread can answer the second relatively quickly. The third is the one that has always taken years.

对于前两个结果，消化路径已经清晰可见：人类可读的证明、大致明确的策略，以及之上的 Lean 形式化。读者可以在一个下午内形成判断。第三个结果目前还没有这样的路径。如此规模的纯 Lean 产物是另一种对象，“它是否正确”的问题被拆解为在不同时间尺度上解决的问题：Lean 中的标题陈述是否表达了我们所指的 GRH；归档文件是否能通过仅使用标准公理的已知良好 Lean 版本编译；该策略是否为数论学家所认可的证明。Zulip 线程可以相对快速地回答第二个问题。而第三个问题历来都需要数年时间。

The two precedents are both uncomfortable. Wiles announced in 1993, the gap surfaced within months, and the repair took until 1995. Mochizuki published in a refereed journal in 2012 and the people who work on the problem still do not accept it. In neither case could the answer be read off the artifact.

这两个先例都令人不安。怀尔斯在1993年宣布证明，几个月内就出现了漏洞，修复工作一直持续到1995年。望月新一在2012年于同行评审期刊上发表了论文，但研究该问题的人至今仍不接受它。在这两种情况下，答案都无法直接从产物中解读出来。

Which makes the interesting question not “is it true” but “what observable event would settle it, and who is entitled to say so”. For anyone trying to price this rather than just follow it, writing that down before the claim exists is the only version that is worth anything. Written afterwards it collapses into an argument about credibility, which is precisely the failure mode this whole episode is about.

这使得有趣的问题不再是“它是否为真”，而是“什么可观察的事件能解决它，以及谁有权做出定论”。对于任何试图为此定价而非仅仅关注它的人来说，在主张存在之前写下这些才是唯一有价值的版本。事后撰写则会沦为关于可信度的争论，而这恰恰是整个事件所体现的失败模式。

Disclosure: I work on https://science.markets, which runs a market on this question. The resolution rule is fixed before the market opens and cannot be edited: https://science.markets/markets/riemann-zeta-critical-line/ — it requires a complete unconditional proof in ZFC, independently checked with no unresolved gap, and it says in advance that finite numerical verification does not count and that independence from ZFC leaves it unresolved. Being forced to write down what does not count was the useful part.

披露：我在 https://science.markets 工作，该平台针对此问题开设了一个市场。解决规则在市场开放前已固定且不可编辑：https://science.markets/markets/riemann-zeta-critical-line/ ——它要求在 ZFC 中提供完整的无条件证明，并经过独立检查且无未解决的漏洞；它预先声明有限的数值验证不计入，且独立于 ZFC 的证明被视为未解决。被迫写下哪些内容不算数，这部分工作很有用。
