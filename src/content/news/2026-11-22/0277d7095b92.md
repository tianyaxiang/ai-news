Two AI music vendors charge the same two prices. Suno and Udio both ask $10 a month for the first paid tier and $30 for the second, and both drop to $8 and $24 if you pay for a year. That symmetry is why the comparison keeps being answered with a coin flip, and it is the least useful fact on either page.

两家人工智能音乐供应商收取相同的两种价格。Suno 和 Udio 的首个付费层级每月收费 10 美元，第二个层级收费 30 美元；如果按年支付，价格则分别降至 8 美元和 24 美元。这种对称性导致人们在比较时往往只能靠掷硬币来决定，而这恰恰是页面上最无用的信息。

The two products meter completely different things. One sells you credits and then limits how many files you may keep. The other sells credits with no printed download limit and a credit rate low enough that the number of songs stops being the constraint. I read both pricing pages with the yearly toggle on, and both vendors' credit rules, on 22 November 2026. Here is what $10 actually buys at each.

这两款产品计量的对象完全不同。一家向你出售积分，然后限制你可以保留的文件数量。另一家出售积分时没有标注下载限制，且积分消耗率足够低，使得歌曲数量不再成为制约因素。我在 2026 年 11 月 22 日查看了这两家供应商开启年度计费后的定价页面及积分规则。以下是 10 美元在各自平台实际能买到的内容。

The ladders line up; the allowances do not. Suno bills $96, $288 and $1,200 a year. Udio bills $96 and $288. Both exclude tax, and Udio's page notes that rates vary by location.

层级设置虽然对齐，但配额却不尽相同。Suno 的年度账单分别为 96 美元、288 美元和 1,200 美元。Udio 的年度账单则为 96 美元和 288 美元。两者均不含税，且 Udio 的页面注明费率因地区而异。

What a credit buys, from each vendor's own rules: Suno's v6 FAQ is explicit: "Each time you generate, you make two songs with a total cost of 10 credits." That is 5 credits a song, and Max Mode costs more when you switch it on for longer or more faithful results. At Pro's 2,500 credits that is 250 generations, or 500 songs a month.

根据各供应商的规则，积分的购买力如下：Suno 的 v6 常见问题解答明确指出：“每次生成都会制作两首歌曲，总成本为 10 积分。”即每首歌 5 积分，若开启“最大模式”以获得更长或更精准的结果，成本会更高。在 Pro 计划的 2,500 积分下，这意味着每月可生成 250 次，即 500 首歌曲。

Udio's help centre is equally explicit, in different units: a 32-second song costs 1 credit, a 130-second song costs 2 credits, and every Create, Extend, Remix, Inpaint or Edit produces two songs. At Standard's 2,400 credits that is 600 generations of two-minute songs, or 1,200 songs a month.

Udio 的帮助中心同样明确，但单位不同：一首 32 秒的歌曲消耗 1 积分，130 秒的歌曲消耗 2 积分，且每次创建、扩展、混音、重绘或编辑都会产生两首歌曲。在标准版 2,400 积分下，这意味着每月可生成 600 次两分钟的歌曲，即 1,200 首歌曲。

Both are derived figures that assume you keep everything and never retry. Nobody works that way — a realistic session throws away more than half — but the ratio between them survives the correction, and the ratio is 2.4 to 1 in Udio's favour on the same $10.

以上均为推算数值，假设你保留所有内容且从不重试。实际上没人会这样做——现实中的创作过程会丢弃超过一半的作品——但两者之间的比例在修正后依然成立，即在同样的 10 美元投入下，Udio 的产出比是 Suno 的 2.4 倍。

The number that decides a real job: downloads. This is the sentence that should decide most purchases. Suno's paid cards print a download allowance — 20 a month on Pro, 60 on Premier, 120 on Platinum — and its free card prints none at all. Commercial rights, stem separation and downloads arrive together on the first paid tier.

决定实际工作的关键数字：下载量。这应该是决定大多数购买行为的关键。Suno 的付费卡片明确标注了下载配额——Pro 每月 20 次，Premier 60 次，Platinum 120 次——而免费卡片则完全没有。商业权利、分轨分离和下载功能均在首个付费层级中同时提供。

Udio's plan cards and comparison table list credits, concurrency, voices, editing and cover art. They do not print a download quota, and I am not going to invent one. What can be said from the page is that Udio's metering is built around credits and generation limits, and that a buyer whose deliverable is a folder of cleared tracks should get the download and licensing terms confirmed in writing before paying.

Udio 的计划卡片和对比表列出了积分、并发数、音色、编辑和封面艺术。它们没有标注下载配额，我也不会凭空捏造。从页面可以确定的是，Udio 的计量方式围绕积分和生成限制构建，如果买家的交付物是一文件夹已获授权的曲目，在付款前应以书面形式确认下载和许可条款。

The top-up price is where the two diverge sharply. Both vendors sell extra credits. Only Udio prints the price on the pricing page: 100 credits for $3.00, or 1,000 credits for $25.00, and Udio's help centre notes that purchased credits never expire.

充值价格是两者差异巨大的地方。两家供应商都出售额外积分。只有 Udio 在定价页面上公布了价格：100 积分 3 美元，或 1,000 积分 25 美元，且 Udio 的帮助中心注明购买的积分永不过期。

What the free tiers reveal about the product: Suno's free plan gives 50 credits a day, which is five generations and ten songs daily, but no downloads and no commercial rights. You can create a great deal and keep none of it. Udio's free plan gives 10 credits a day and 100 a month, capped at three full-length songs a day.

免费层级揭示的产品特性：Suno 的免费计划每天提供 50 积分，即每天可生成 5 次、制作 10 首歌曲，但没有下载权限和商业权利。你可以创作大量作品，却无法保留任何一个。Udio 的免费计划每天提供 10 积分，每月额外赠送 100 积分，限制每天最多制作 3 首完整歌曲。
