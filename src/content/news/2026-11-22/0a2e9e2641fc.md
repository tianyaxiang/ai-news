# The UN fails to agree on rules regulating ‘killer robots’

联合国未能就监管“杀人机器人”的规则达成一致。

One of the items on the agenda for UN diplomats meeting in Geneva this week was working towards rules regarding the use of autonomous weapons, or so-called “killer robots.” Eventually, representatives left the meetings agreeing to “start discussing options” after a largely fruitless week of negotiations.

本周在日内瓦召开会议的联合国各国外交官，其议程之一是致力于制定有关使用自主武器（即所谓的“杀人机器人”）的规则。最终，在经历了基本毫无成果的一周谈判后，与会代表们仅同意“开始讨论相关方案”。

According to NPR: Before the negotiations got underway, the International Committee of the Red Cross, the guardian of the Geneva Conventions, said it was urgent for legally binding rules to be drafted within a year. That call was backed by the U.N. secretary-general and by Pope Leo.

据美国国家公共电台（NPR）报道：在谈判开始前，《日内瓦公约》的守护者红十字国际委员会曾表示，迫切需要在一年内起草具有法律约束力的规则。这一呼吁得到了联合国秘书长和教皇利奥的支持。

The compromise reached was that countries would instead have two years to propose regulations. Negotiations between countries on which regulations to adopt and how to enforce them will likely drag on well beyond that. Additionally, room was left for some of those yet-to-be-adopted regulations to remain “optional.”

最终达成的妥协是，各国将有两年的时间来提出监管建议。各国之间关于应采取哪些法规以及如何执行这些法规的谈判，很可能会持续更长时间。此外，会议还留有余地，允许其中一些尚未通过的法规保持“可选”状态。

Humanitarian organizations and some countries see a need to urgently adopt legally binding rules as semi-autonomous weapons and drones have been responsible for a significant number of civilian deaths in conflicts around the globe, including in Ukraine, Gaza, and Sudan.

人道主义组织和一些国家认为，迫切需要通过具有法律约束力的规则，因为半自主武器和无人机已在全球各地的冲突中造成了大量平民死亡，包括在乌克兰、加沙和苏丹的冲突中。

According to NPR, Russia, Israel, and the US proved to be the primary obstructions: On day one, Russia delayed proceedings by objecting to the meeting’s agenda. Then the United States proposed a five-year window to discuss nonbinding, optional guidelines for autonomous weapons.

据NPR报道，俄罗斯、以色列和美国被证明是主要的阻碍者：第一天，俄罗斯通过反对会议议程推迟了进程。随后，美国提议设立一个五年的窗口期，用于讨论针对自主武器的非约束性、可选准则。
