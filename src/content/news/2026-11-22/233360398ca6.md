Chess.com’s next move is a new hub for classic games, from mahjong to sudoku.

Chess.com 的下一步棋是打造一个涵盖麻将到数独等经典游戏的新中心。

AI changed the game at Chess.com, helping it expand to 11 other classics this year. Chess.com is ready to go a lot bigger than chess. After launching Gambit, a gambling-free poker site, earlier this year, the company is now adding 10 more games: backgammon, checkers, go, hearts, mahjong, shogi, sudoku, solitaire, xiangqi, and a Scrabble alternative called word. They’re all part of Chess’s new Classic Games platform, which is launching its public beta on Saturday on the web and through a new app for iOS and Android.

人工智能改变了 Chess.com 的游戏规则，帮助其在今年扩展到其他 11 款经典游戏。Chess.com 准备在国际象棋之外大展拳脚。继今年早些时候推出无赌博性质的扑克网站 Gambit 后，该公司现在又增加了 10 款游戏：双陆棋、跳棋、围棋、红心大战、麻将、将棋、数独、纸牌、象棋，以及一款名为“word”的拼字游戏替代品。它们都属于 Chess 新推出的“经典游戏”（Classic Games）平台，该平台将于周六在网页端以及 iOS 和 Android 的新应用上开启公测。

“Our cofounders have had many people come up to them and say, ‘Hey, I run a mahjong night on Wednesdays’ or ‘I go to this local bar in Chicago with like five people, and we love playing go, but we lack a Chess.com,’” chief growth officer Albert Cheng says in an interview with The Verge. “The way that Chess.com has grown this community of almost 300 million members has not really happened for a lot of these other types of classic games.”

“我们的联合创始人经常遇到有人对他们说：‘嘿，我每周三都会组织麻将之夜’，或者‘我和五个人去芝加哥的一家当地酒吧，我们很喜欢下围棋，但我们缺少一个像 Chess.com 这样的平台，’”首席增长官 Albert Cheng 在接受 The Verge 采访时表示。“Chess.com 发展出近 3 亿会员社区的方式，在许多其他类型的经典游戏中并未真正实现过。”

Classic Games is aimed at filling that gap. Some of the new games, like shoji or xiangqi, are strategy games similar to chess while others are further afield, like sudoku, solitaire, and word. The Classic team chose the new games through “a mix of community feedback, market research, and then just our own confidence in our ability to serve that game well,” Cheng says. It’s focused on classics that “have some sort of lifelong journey of learning and a community.”

“经典游戏”平台旨在填补这一空白。一些新游戏，如将棋或象棋，是类似于国际象棋的策略游戏，而另一些则大不相同，如数独、纸牌和“word”。Cheng 表示，经典游戏团队选择这些新游戏是基于“社区反馈、市场调研，以及我们对自己能够很好地服务这些游戏的信心”。该平台专注于那些“具有某种终身学习过程和社区属性”的经典游戏。

Classic Games will feel familiar if you’ve ever played a match on Chess.com. Each game has lessons, leaderboards, and a rating system. Multiplayer games like checkers or mahjong have options for matches against other players or bots, and they use the same bot characters that Chess.com has. (Martin remains the lowest-rated bot across the board, whether it’s on Chess, Gambit, or Classic.) Not all of the games have the same play modes — word and mahjong have puzzles like chess, while sudoku has a set of daily grids and solitaire has daily deals.

如果你曾在 Chess.com 上玩过比赛，那么“经典游戏”会让你感到熟悉。每款游戏都有课程、排行榜和评分系统。像跳棋或麻将这样的多人游戏提供了与其他玩家或机器人对战的选项，并且它们使用了与 Chess.com 相同的机器人角色。（无论是在国际象棋、Gambit 还是经典游戏平台，Martin 依然是评分最低的机器人。）并非所有游戏都有相同的游戏模式——“word”和麻将像国际象棋一样有谜题，而数独有每日网格，纸牌则有每日挑战。

The expansion hasn’t put Chess.com itself on the back burner, according to Cheng. If anything, it’s never been better. “Chess is more and more so in the public zeitgeist than I’ve ever seen it, frankly, during my time here,” Cheng says. “Our daily active users are higher than they’ve ever been during the company’s history.” He declined to share exactly how many daily users Chess.com currently has but said the site is “doing amazingly well.”

据 Cheng 称，这次扩张并没有让 Chess.com 本身受到冷落。事实上，它的表现从未如此出色。“坦率地说，在我任职期间，国际象棋在公众舆论中的地位比以往任何时候都要高，”Cheng 说。“我们的日活跃用户数达到了公司历史上的最高水平。”他拒绝透露 Chess.com 目前的具体日活跃用户数，但表示该网站“表现非常出色”。

AI played a key role in helping Chess.com launch 11 new games in a matter of months with a team of only a few people. Earlier this year, Cheng told The Verge his team built Gambit with the help of AI, mainly for coding tasks. He says they used “almost entirely the same approach” for Classic, with AI helping to speed up the coding process again.

人工智能在帮助 Chess.com 仅用几个人组成的团队在几个月内推出 11 款新游戏方面发挥了关键作用。今年早些时候，Cheng 告诉 The Verge，他的团队在 AI 的帮助下构建了 Gambit，主要用于编码任务。他说，他们在“经典游戏”项目中使用了“几乎完全相同的方法”，AI 再次帮助加快了编码过程。

“Because these games are built on top of the Chess.com code base and design system and bot characters and rating systems, it’s a huge advantage and allows us to move very quickly alongside AI to bring these types of games to market with a very small team,” Cheng says. “As we create more and more games, the adjacent games that can be created to it just happen much faster because the infrastructure is there, as opposed to standing everything up from scratch.”

“因为这些游戏是建立在 Chess.com 的代码库、设计系统、机器人角色和评分系统之上的，这是一个巨大的优势，使我们能够与 AI 并肩快速行动，用极小的团队将这些类型的游戏推向市场，”Cheng 说。“随着我们创造的游戏越来越多，可以创建的相邻游戏会发生得更快，因为基础设施已经存在，而不是一切从零开始。”

AI wasn’t used for Classic Games’ art and design, Cheng says. “The look and feel of it is very carefully crafted by humans, and we’re proud of that,” he says. “The truth is, it really is a blend. It’s a small team of really talented folks that have good product and design judgment, and they’re leveraging AI.”

Cheng 表示，AI 并未用于“经典游戏”的艺术和设计。“它的外观和感觉是由人类精心打造的，我们对此感到自豪，”他说。“事实是，这确实是一种融合。这是一个由非常有才华的人组成的小团队，他们拥有良好的产品和设计判断力，并且正在利用 AI。”

“We are fortunate in some ways that AI and chess have been intermixed for 30-ish years.” Chess.com’s comfort with AI may come from the fact that human chess players have now spent decades playing and learning alongside machines. “We are fortunate in some ways that AI and chess have been intermixed for 30-ish years,” Cheng says, calling back to Garry Kasparov’s 1996 chess match against IBM’s Deep Blue supercomputer. “Even before the company was founded 20 years ago, the idea that AI was super powerful at the game of chess was embedded in how the cofounders thought about the company.”

“在某些方面，我们很幸运，人工智能和国际象棋已经交织了大约 30 年。”Chess.com 对 AI 的适应可能源于人类棋手已经与机器共同下棋和学习了几十年。“在某些方面，我们很幸运，人工智能和国际象棋已经交织了大约 30 年，”Cheng 说道，并回顾了加里·卡斯帕罗夫 1996 年与 IBM 深蓝超级计算机的国际象棋比赛。“甚至在公司 20 年前成立之前，AI 在国际象棋游戏中极其强大这一理念就已经植根于联合创始人对公司的构想之中了。”

Elsewhere in the games industry, there’s been increasing tension over the use of AI. Cheng says that while Chess.com may be using AI to “accelerate” coding tasks, the creative side of things has become more important. “On the art and the design side, the ratios are shifting a decent amount, where we’re hiring more of those folks,” he says, adding that the company has “shipped a lot more major features in Chess.com than in previous years” using what they’ve learned from Classic Games’ faster development process.

在游戏行业的其他地方，关于使用 AI 的紧张情绪日益加剧。Cheng 表示，虽然 Chess.com 可能正在使用 AI 来“加速”编码任务，但创意方面变得更加重要。“在艺术和设计方面，比例正在发生相当大的变化，我们正在招聘更多这方面的人才，”他说，并补充道，利用从“经典游戏”更快的开发过程中学到的经验，公司“在 Chess.com 上发布的主要功能比往年多得多”。

Chess.com might not be done growing its library of games, either. Cheng said it will likely expand to “a lot more” games in the future.

Chess.com 可能还没有停止扩充其游戏库。Cheng 表示，未来它很可能会扩展到“更多”游戏。
