# Our go-to earbuds for taking calls have fallen to their lowest price to date

我们首选的通话耳机已降至历史最低价。

The Liberty 5 Pro have the best voice call clarity of any earbuds we’ve tested, and they’re marked down for Black Friday.

Liberty 5 Pro 是我们测试过的所有耳机中语音通话清晰度最好的，目前正值“黑色星期五”促销。

If you’re looking for a new pair of earbuds, the Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro are on sale for $129.99 at Amazon, Best Buy, and Walmart right now. That’s the lowest price we’ve seen for Anker’s excellent wireless earbuds, which normally retail for $169.99 and come in four colors (black, white, blue, and pink) all of which are discounted through November 30th as part of Anker’s ongoing Black Friday promo.

如果您正在寻找一副新耳机，Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro 目前在亚马逊、百思买和沃尔玛的售价为 129.99 美元。这是我们见过的安克（Anker）这款出色无线耳机的最低价格，其通常零售价为 169.99 美元，有四种颜色（黑色、白色、蓝色和粉色）可选，作为安克“黑色星期五”促销活动的一部分，所有颜色均在 11 月 30 日前享受折扣。

While the Liberty 5 Pro have a number of great features — including support LDAC for high-res audio and an IP55 dust and water resistance rating — the call quality is what stands out. The microphone does an impressive job cutting out background noise, and my colleague John Higgins praised the Liberty 5 Pro for filtering out emergency vehicles, arborists cutting tree branches, and even loud children.

虽然 Liberty 5 Pro 拥有许多出色的功能，包括支持 LDAC 高解析度音频以及 IP55 级防尘防水，但其通话质量最为突出。麦克风在消除背景噪音方面表现出色，我的同事约翰·希金斯（John Higgins）称赞 Liberty 5 Pro 能够过滤掉救护车、修剪树枝的园艺工，甚至是吵闹的孩子发出的声音。

The active noise cancellation is excellent, which helps when you’re trying to hear someone on the other end of the phone whose earbuds aren’t as clear as yours. The charging case also features a handy 0.96-inch screen, so you can adjust the ANC and sound profile on the fly.

其主动降噪功能非常出色，当您试图听清电话另一端的人说话，而对方的耳机清晰度不如您时，这会非常有帮助。充电盒还配备了一个实用的 0.96 英寸屏幕，方便您随时调整主动降噪和声音配置。
