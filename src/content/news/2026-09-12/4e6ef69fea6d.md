# The wireless earbuds with unbeatable call quality got their first discount
# 拥有无敌通话质量的无线耳机迎来了首次降价

Anker’s Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro came out swinging in May with incredible call quality, something that competing ear buds have struggled to get right. They’ve become part of our reviewer John Higgins’ rotation of earbuds for more than just taking calls; The Liberty 5 Pro have fantastic audio once you make some tweaks in their app, and they stand out thanks to an IP55 rating and LDAC high-res audio codec support on Android.

Anker 的 Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro 在五月发布时便以惊人的通话质量一鸣惊人，这是竞争对手的耳机一直难以做好的地方。它们已经成为我们评测员 John Higgins 日常使用的耳机之一，不仅仅是为了通话；Liberty 5 Pro 在通过配套 App 进行一些调整后，音质表现非常出色，并且凭借 IP55 防护等级和对 Android 系统 LDAC 高解析度音频编码的支持脱颖而出。

What’s more, they’re typically cheaper than the competition at $169.99, but you can snag them for $149.99 at Amazon, Best Buy, and at Anker. They come in four colors (black, blue, white, pink), and include a screen-equipped charging case that lets you make adjustments to active noise cancellation, sound profiles, speak-to-chat and enabling Dolby Atmos head tracking. Your mileage may vary with the usefulness of the case, but it’s nice not to have to use the app for quick tweaks.

更重要的是，它们通常比竞争对手便宜，定价为 169.99 美元，但现在你可以在亚马逊、百思买（Best Buy）和 Anker 官网以 149.99 美元的价格入手。它们有四种颜色可选（黑、蓝、白、粉），并配备了一个带屏幕的充电盒，让你能够直接调整主动降噪、声音配置文件、通话检测（speak-to-chat）以及开启杜比全景声头部追踪功能。虽然这个充电盒的实用性因人而异，但无需打开 App 就能进行快速设置确实很方便。

I’ve been eagerly awaiting any sort of deal on the Liberty 5 Pro since John’s review dropped. Clearly, Anker caught wind of its success and kept them locked at $170 for months (not even a small price drop during June’s Prime Day sale). What could have lead to the price drop? Likely the debut of the AirPods 5 that start at $129 but have a more feature-rich version that’s $20 more — the same current price as Anker’s Liberty 5 Pro.

自从 John 的评测发布以来，我就一直热切期待 Liberty 5 Pro 能有任何折扣。显然，Anker 意识到了这款产品的成功，几个月来一直将其价格锁定在 170 美元（甚至在六月的 Prime Day 大促期间都没有降价）。是什么导致了这次降价？很可能是 AirPods 5 的发布，其起售价为 129 美元，但功能更丰富的版本贵 20 美元——这与 Anker Liberty 5 Pro 目前的价格相同。
