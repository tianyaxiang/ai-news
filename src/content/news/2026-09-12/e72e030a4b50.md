# Two-Parameter Flow Map Learning for Continuous-Time Diffeomorphic Image Registration
# 用于连续时间微分同胚图像配准的双参数流映射学习

**Abstract:** Diffeomorphic image registration is central to medical image analysis, enabling anatomically consistent alignment across subjects. Most learning-based diffeomorphic methods model autonomous ODEs (ordinary differential equations) by parameterizing a stationary velocity field and recovering deformations via scaling-and-squaring. While non-autonomous ODEs with time-dependent velocities increase expressiveness, existing approaches rely on numerical integration to implicitly enforce flow structure that entangles model expressiveness with discretization accuracy.

**摘要：** 微分同胚图像配准是医学图像分析的核心，能够实现跨受试者的解剖结构一致性对齐。大多数基于学习的微分同胚方法通过参数化平稳速度场并利用“缩放与平方”（scaling-and-squaring）方法恢复形变，从而对自治常微分方程（ODE）进行建模。虽然具有时间依赖速度的非自治 ODE 增强了模型的表达能力，但现有方法依赖数值积分来隐式强制执行流结构，这使得模型的表达能力与离散化精度相互纠缠。

We propose a framework to directly learn the continuous-time solution of a non-autonomous ODE formulated as a two-parameter flow map. By enforcing cocycle consistency, a fundamental structural property of time-varying flows, we learn the flow maps without time discretization and velocity integration during training. The framework recovers diffeomorphic mappings at inference using a small number of compositions.

我们提出了一种框架，旨在直接学习表述为双参数流映射的非自治 ODE 的连续时间解。通过强制执行余循环一致性（cocycle consistency，时变流的一种基本结构属性），我们在训练过程中无需进行时间离散化和速度积分即可学习流映射。该框架在推理阶段仅需少量组合操作即可恢复微分同胚映射。

Our proposed framework seamlessly incorporates standard registration backbones and improves alignment accuracy consistently across nine datasets while preserving diffeomorphic structure. Notably, the proposed method achieves an average Dice improvement of 2.1% on brain MRI benchmarks, a 12% TRE reduction on lung CT, and a 2.6% Dice gain on cardiac MRI and ultrasound datasets.

我们提出的框架能够无缝集成标准的配准骨干网络，在保持微分同胚结构的同时，在九个数据集上持续提升了配准精度。值得注意的是，该方法在脑部 MRI 基准测试中实现了平均 2.1% 的 Dice 系数提升，在肺部 CT 上实现了 12% 的目标配准误差（TRE）降低，并在心脏 MRI 和超声数据集上获得了 2.6% 的 Dice 系数增益。
