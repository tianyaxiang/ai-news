# Where to preorder the Apple AirPods 5
# 如何预购 Apple AirPods 5

The Apple AirPods 5 will hit shelves on September 18th, but you can line up your purchase ahead of time.
Apple AirPods 5 将于 9 月 18 日上架，但你可以提前预订。

The Apple AirPods 5 are bringing active noise cancellation to the masses, and they’re now available for preorder at a variety of retailers. They’ll officially launch on September 18th, the same day as the Apple Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4, as well as the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max.
Apple AirPods 5 将主动降噪功能带给了大众，目前已在多家零售商处开启预购。它们将于 9 月 18 日正式发售，与 Apple Watch Series 12 和 Ultra 4，以及 iPhone 18 Pro 和 Pro Max 同步上市。

There are two versions this time around, just like with the last-gen model. The standard $129 model adds active noise cancelation, which was previously only found on the more expensive $179 AirPods 4 with ANC models. They’re available from Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart, and Apple.
此次共有两个版本，与上一代机型相同。标准版售价 129 美元，增加了主动降噪功能，该功能此前仅见于售价 179 美元、更昂贵的 AirPods 4（带主动降噪版）上。该版本可在亚马逊、百思买、沃尔玛和苹果官网购买。

The upgraded $149 model features a wireless charging case, longer battery life, and touch controls on the earbud stem that lets you adjust the volume without pulling out your phone. You can preorder this model at Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart, and Apple.
升级版售价 149 美元，配备无线充电盒、更长的电池续航，以及耳机柄上的触控功能，让你无需掏出手机即可调节音量。你可以在亚马逊、百思买、沃尔玛和苹果官网预购此版本。

我们正在撰写新款 AirPods 5 的评测。如果你在我们的评测发布前就准备购买，可以在下方找到更多预购链接。
