### Meta-Learning for Data-Efficient Plant Growth Estimation via Vision Transformers and Fuzzy Clustering
### 基于视觉 Transformer 和模糊聚类的植物生长数据高效评估元学习研究

**Abstract:** Accurate plant growth estimation is essential for greenhouse monitoring, yet obtaining labeled data remains costly and time-consuming. To address this, we propose a few-shot regression framework that combines Vision Transformer (ViT) feature embeddings, clustering-based task construction, and gradient-based meta-learning, and show that task construction in embedding space is a primary driver of performance.

**摘要：** 精确的植物生长评估对于温室监测至关重要，但获取标注数据既昂贵又耗时。为了解决这一问题，我们提出了一种少样本回归框架，该框架结合了视觉 Transformer (ViT) 特征嵌入、基于聚类的任务构建以及基于梯度的元学习，并证明了在嵌入空间中进行任务构建是提升性能的主要驱动力。

The approach leverages an unlabeled image pool to organize data into structured tasks using fuzzy c-means clustering, enabling efficient learning from a small number of labeled samples. We systematically evaluate meta-learning methods and show that second-order methods (e.g., Model-Agnostic Meta-Learning variants such as MAML++) outperform classical baselines in the few-shot regime.

该方法利用未标注的图像池，通过模糊 C 均值聚类将数据组织成结构化任务，从而能够从少量标注样本中进行高效学习。我们系统地评估了各种元学习方法，结果表明，在少样本场景下，二阶方法（例如 MAML++ 等模型无关元学习变体）的表现优于传统的基准模型。

Furthermore, intra-cluster support selection has a limited and dataset-dependent impact. Experiments on two plant datasets show that structured task design combined with meta-learning enables reliable plant growth estimation under severe label scarcity.

此外，簇内支持集选择的影响有限，且取决于具体数据集。在两个植物数据集上的实验表明，结构化任务设计与元学习相结合，能够在极度缺乏标注数据的情况下实现可靠的植物生长评估。
