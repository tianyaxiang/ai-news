# Meta may have leaked the first look at its slim ‘Project Phoenix’ headset
# Meta 可能泄露了其轻薄款“凤凰计划”（Project Phoenix）头显的首次外观

Project Phoenix doesn’t quite look like smart glasses, but it’s much smaller than Meta’s other headsets.
“凤凰计划”看起来并不完全像智能眼镜，但它比 Meta 的其他头显设备要小得多。

Just a couple of weeks before Meta Connect starts on September 23rd, UploadVR posted images seemingly revealing the design for Meta’s “Project Phoenix” headset. The images, which UploadVR says were “found in the HorizonOS Prescription Lens firmware package,” suggest Meta may be looking to compete with devices like those from Xreal or Viture. These companies’ mixed reality glasses are a bit bulkier than something like Meta’s Ray-Ban AI smart glasses, but a step down from a full VR headset like Meta’s Quest 3.
在 9 月 23 日 Meta Connect 大会召开前几周，UploadVR 发布了一些图片，似乎揭示了 Meta“凤凰计划”头显的设计。UploadVR 表示，这些图片是“在 HorizonOS 近视镜片固件包中发现的”，这表明 Meta 可能正寻求与 Xreal 或 Viture 等公司的设备展开竞争。这些公司的混合现实眼镜比 Meta 的 Ray-Ban AI 智能眼镜稍显笨重，但比 Meta Quest 3 这种全功能 VR 头显要轻便不少。

This leak shows something that’s almost in between larger headsets and smart glasses, with lenses set into a slim, glasses-style frame. A shot from behind shows a wire extending from one of the earpieces, possibly for a battery or compute puck. There also appear to be outward- and downward-facing cameras in the earpieces.
此次泄露的产品介于大型头显和智能眼镜之间，镜片嵌入在纤薄的眼镜式框架中。从背面拍摄的照片显示，有一根电线从其中一个耳挂处延伸出来，这可能是用于连接电池或计算单元。此外，耳挂处似乎还配备了朝外和朝下的摄像头。

Project Phoenix has been in the works for a while now, and isn’t expected to launch until next year. Google is also working on a pair of Project Aura XR glasses in a similar vein, with an external compute puck that’s wired to the frames.
“凤凰计划”已经研发了一段时间，预计要到明年才会发布。谷歌也在开发类似的“Aura 计划”XR 眼镜，同样采用通过电线连接到镜框的外部计算单元。
