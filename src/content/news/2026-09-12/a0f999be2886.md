# The Truth Was Never Gone: Perfect Aliasing in Compliant-Context Truth Probes
# 真相从未消失：合规语境下真相探测器中的“完美混叠”现象

**Abstract:** A truth probe fitted where truthful reporting and a task's prescribed action coincide cannot distinguish those targets from its fitting labels alone. We call this failure of semantic identification perfect aliasing. 

**摘要：** 当真相探测器在“真实报告”与“任务预设行为”相一致的语境下进行拟合时，仅凭拟合标签本身无法区分这两者。我们将这种语义识别的失败称为“完美混叠”（perfect aliasing）。

In a controlled binary reporting game, truth and prescribed-action probes fitted on compliant contexts solve the same optimization. On rival contexts their labels are complements, forcing their AUROCs to sum to one; this identity holds across 751 cell-layer pairs to floating-point precision. 

在一个受控的二元报告博弈中，在合规语境下拟合的“真相探测器”与“预设行为探测器”实际上是在解决同一个优化问题。在竞争语境下，它们的标签互为补集，导致它们的 AUROC 值之和恒等于 1；这一恒等式在 751 个细胞层对中均成立，且精确到浮点数精度。

We separate prescribed output symbols from semantic action using randomized codebooks, then separate truth from prescribed action by fitting on mixed compliant and rival contexts. For a reward-trained Gemma-2-9B policy that answers falsely on all evaluated rival trials, the conventional probe scores $0.006 \pm 0.005$ AUROC across three training seeds, while mixed-fit probes score $1.000$ on the same held-out activations. 

我们利用随机码本将预设的输出符号与语义行为分离开来，随后通过在混合了合规语境和竞争语境的数据上进行拟合，将真相与预设行为分离开来。对于一个经过奖励训练、在所有评估的竞争试验中均回答错误的 Gemma-2-9B 策略，传统探测器在三个训练种子下的 AUROC 得分为 $0.006 \pm 0.005$，而混合拟合探测器在相同的留出激活值上得分达到了 $1.000$。

Mixed fitting uses more training examples and access to labelled rival contexts, so this comparison establishes linear recoverability rather than isolating the benefit of decorrelation. We also show that two compliant-fit probes, both perfect in-distribution, score $0.080$ and $0.986$ on the same rival activations. 

混合拟合使用了更多的训练样本并获取了带有标签的竞争语境，因此这种比较确立的是线性可恢复性，而非单纯隔离出解相关（decorrelation）带来的益处。我们还展示了两个在分布内表现完美的合规拟合探测器，在相同的竞争激活值上得分分别为 $0.080$ 和 $0.986$。

The findings concern what a probe measures: they do not establish preserved functional belief, causal use of the recovered direction, or a deployable deception detector. Code and aggregate results accompany the paper.

这些发现揭示了探测器究竟在测量什么：它们并未证明模型中存在保留的功能性信念、未证明对恢复方向的因果使用，也未提供一个可部署的欺骗检测器。论文附带了代码和汇总结果。
