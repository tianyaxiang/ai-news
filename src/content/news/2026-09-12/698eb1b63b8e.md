# Zero-shot rib design: merging training-free generative prior with topology optimization
# 零样本肋骨设计：融合无需训练的生成先验与拓扑优化

**Abstract:** Natural load-bearing patterns such as leaf venation, trabecular bone, and spider webs achieve high stiffness per unit mass, yet classical topology optimizers rarely reach such geometries, and few let engineers express structural design intent through natural language. 

**摘要：** 叶脉、骨小梁和蜘蛛网等自然界的承重模式实现了极高的单位质量刚度，然而传统的拓扑优化器很难达到这种几何结构，且很少有工具能让工程师通过自然语言表达结构设计意图。

This work treats a frozen text-to-image diffusion model as a training-free source of design knowledge and distills it into the physics loop of density-based topology optimization via score distillation sampling, so that a text prompt becomes an explicit, machine-interpretable representation of engineer intent. 

本研究将冻结的文本到图像扩散模型视为一种无需训练的设计知识源，并通过分数蒸馏采样（Score Distillation Sampling）将其提炼到基于密度的拓扑优化物理循环中，从而使文本提示词成为工程师意图的一种明确的、机器可解释的表达方式。

The prompt-induced generative gradient and the finite element sensitivity are combined at every iteration, letting physics decide which prompt-induced features survive. 

在每一次迭代中，由提示词引导的生成梯度与有限元灵敏度相结合，让物理规律决定哪些由提示词引导的特征能够保留下来。

In 245 primary SDS runs spanning four geometric domains and two physics regimes, 38 of 49 prompt--domain combinations achieved statistically significant compliance reductions (up to $-31.5\%$ mechanical and $-23.0\%$ thermoelastic), outperforming gradient-based baselines. 

在涵盖四个几何领域和两种物理机制的 245 次主要 SDS 运行中，49 种提示词-领域组合中有 38 种实现了统计学意义上的柔度降低（机械性能提升高达 -31.5%，热弹性性能提升高达 -23.0%），表现优于基于梯度的基准方法。

Cross-domain morphological analysis identifies a recurring structural signature of improvement: in most domains the generative prior suppresses dead-end branches in the rib skeleton, with endpoint--compliance correlation $r = +0.56$ to $+0.99$. 

跨领域的形态学分析识别出了一种反复出现的结构改进特征：在大多数领域中，生成先验抑制了肋骨骨架中的死胡同分支，其端点与柔度之间的相关系数 $r$ 在 +0.56 到 +0.99 之间。

A Heaviside projection with $\beta$-continuation resolves a pronounced intermediate-density tendency in this diffusion--physics coupling ($42.6\%$ to $<3\%$), and an automated skeleton-based pipeline converts optimized density fields into candidate geometry ready for computer-aided design. 

带有 $\beta$-延拓的 Heaviside 投影解决了这种扩散-物理耦合中明显的中间密度倾向（从 42.6% 降至 <3%），并且一个基于骨架的自动化流程将优化后的密度场转换为可用于计算机辅助设计（CAD）的候选几何模型。

By retargeting the generative prior across domains, loading conditions, and physics objectives through a change of text prompt, with each new problem's physics setup specified separately, the framework uses a pretrained generative model as a reusable, training-free prior for engineering design.

通过更改文本提示词，该框架能够在不同的领域、载荷条件和物理目标之间重新定位生成先验，并为每个新问题单独指定物理设置，从而将预训练的生成模型用作工程设计中可重用、无需训练的先验知识。
