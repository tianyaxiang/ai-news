# GEOSTEER: Geodesic Optimization for Activation Steering in Large Language Models
# GEOSTEER：大语言模型中激活引导的测地线优化

**Abstract:** Activation steering provides a lightweight way to control large language models (LLMs) by modifying their hidden activations at inference time. Among these approaches, norm-preserving steering aims to change model behavior without altering the activation norm, reducing the risk of representation collapse and degradation. However, existing norm-preserving methods are limited by predefined steering trajectories and by their reliance on one-step updates, which may fail to capture the complex structure of activation distributions.

**摘要：** 激活引导（Activation steering）提供了一种轻量级的方法，通过在推理时修改大语言模型（LLM）的隐藏激活值来控制模型。在这些方法中，保持范数（norm-preserving）的引导旨在改变模型行为而不改变激活值的范数，从而降低表征崩溃和退化的风险。然而，现有的保持范数的方法受到预定义引导轨迹和对单步更新依赖的限制，这可能无法捕捉激活分布的复杂结构。

We propose GeoSteer, an optimization-based method for norm-preserving activation steering. GeoSteer formulates steering as a Riemannian optimization problem and updates activations through a sequence of small geodesic steps on the representation manifold. To avoid fixed steering directions, GeoSteer learns a nonlinear activation-space objective that distinguishes desired from undesired activations, and uses this function to adaptively guide each steering step.

我们提出了 GeoSteer，这是一种基于优化的保持范数激活引导方法。GeoSteer 将引导过程表述为一个黎曼优化问题，并通过在表征流形上进行一系列微小的测地线步长更新来调整激活值。为了避免固定的引导方向，GeoSteer 学习了一个非线性的激活空间目标函数，用以区分期望与非期望的激活，并利用该函数自适应地引导每一步的更新。

This multistep formulation yields smoother, more stable, and more consistent steering behavior while preserving the activation norm. Across TruthfulQA, RealToxicityPrompts, and UltraFeedback benchmarks, GeoSteer consistently improves over state-of-the-art activation steering baselines. These results suggest that norm-preserving steering can be made more effective by replacing predefined one-step edits with adaptive, geometry-aware optimization.

这种多步公式化方法在保持激活范数的同时，产生了更平滑、更稳定且更一致的引导行为。在 TruthfulQA、RealToxicityPrompts 和 UltraFeedback 基准测试中，GeoSteer 的表现始终优于最先进的激活引导基准方法。这些结果表明，通过用自适应的、具有几何感知能力的优化方法取代预定义的单步编辑，可以使保持范数的引导更加有效。
