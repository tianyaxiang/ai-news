# Artificial Intelligence Algorithms for the Detection of Pathologies Related to Lung Cancer through Image Analysis using Convolutional Neural Networks and Data Augmentation: a systematic mapping of the literature

**利用卷积神经网络和数据增强进行图像分析以检测肺癌相关病理的人工智能算法：文献系统映射研究**

***

**Abstract:** Lung cancer is one of the leading causes of death worldwide, and its early diagnosis is crucial to improving patients' prognosis and quality of life. However, the process of interpreting medical images for the detection of lung cancer is complex and requires trained experts. In this context, artificial intelligence (AI) and deep learning (DL) emerge as potential tools to automate and optimize image analysis.

**摘要：** 肺癌是全球主要的死亡原因之一，早期诊断对于改善患者的预后和生活质量至关重要。然而，解读用于检测肺癌的医学影像过程复杂，且需要训练有素的专家。在此背景下，人工智能 (AI) 和深度学习 (DL) 作为自动化和优化图像分析的潜在工具应运而生。

***

The objective of this work is to review the most recent and relevant applications of AI and DL in the field of radiology for the detection of lung cancer. To this end, an exhaustive search was carried out in scientific databases such as PubMed, IEEEXPLORE, Scopus and Web of Science, and 96 articles published from 2015 to the present addressing the use of AI and DL in biomedical engineering were selected.

本研究旨在回顾人工智能和深度学习在放射学领域用于肺癌检测的最新且相关的应用。为此，研究人员对 PubMed、IEEEXPLORE、Scopus 和 Web of Science 等科学数据库进行了详尽的检索，筛选出了 2015 年至今发表的 96 篇涉及人工智能和深度学习在生物医学工程中应用的论文。

***

Emphasis is placed on the use of convolutional neural networks (CNN) with transfer learning and Data Augmentation as promising techniques to improve the accuracy and efficiency of the image interpretation process. The results show that the use of AI and DL can offer an effective alternative for the early diagnosis of lung cancer, with high sensitivity and specificity.

研究重点关注了卷积神经网络 (CNN) 结合迁移学习和数据增强技术的应用，这些技术被认为是提高图像解读过程准确性和效率的有前景的方法。结果表明，利用人工智能和深度学习可以为肺癌的早期诊断提供一种有效的替代方案，并具有高灵敏度和高特异性。

***

However, current limitations and challenges that must be addressed to guarantee its responsible and safe application in clinical practice are also identified, such as the lack of standardized data, the explainability of the models, patient privacy, and the ethical and social implications. It is concluded that the use of AI and DL can have a positive impact on the care of patients with lung cancer, but further research and regulation are required to ensure its quality and reliability.

然而，研究也指出了为确保其在临床实践中负责任且安全地应用所必须解决的当前局限性和挑战，例如缺乏标准化数据、模型的可解释性、患者隐私以及伦理和社会影响。结论认为，人工智能和深度学习的使用可以对肺癌患者的护理产生积极影响，但仍需进一步的研究和监管以确保其质量和可靠性。
