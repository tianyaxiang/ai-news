# Final, final, final call for TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 Side Events
# TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 官方周边活动申请：最后、最后、最后通牒

The absolute last chance to apply to host an official Side Event during TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 is tonight, September 11, at 11:59 p.m. PT. No more extensions. No more chances.
申请举办 TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 官方周边活动的最后机会就在今晚（太平洋时间 9 月 11 日晚上 11:59）。不会再有延期，也不会再有其他机会。

Don’t just bring people together. Create value. If you’re bringing your community to Disrupt, don’t just give them another place to gather. Give them a reason to show up — and give your organization a reason to host.
不要只是把人聚在一起，要创造价值。如果你打算带领你的社区参加 Disrupt，不要只给他们提供一个聚会场所，要给他们一个出席的理由，并给你的组织一个举办活动的理由。

A Side Event can help you:
周边活动可以帮助你：

Turn connections into opportunities: Put your team directly in front of founders, investors, customers, partners, and other decision-makers who can unlock your next deal, partnership, investment, hire, or collaboration.
将人脉转化为机遇：让你的团队直接接触到创始人、投资者、客户、合作伙伴以及其他决策者，他们能为你开启下一笔交易、合作、投资、招聘或协作的大门。

Build relationships that go deeper: Create the kind of conversations that are harder to have on a crowded conference floor — and deepen relationships with the people most relevant to your business.
建立更深层的关系：创造在拥挤的会议现场难以进行的深度对话，并与对你业务最重要的人建立更紧密的联系。

Put your brand at the center: Own the conversation around a topic your audience cares about and give your brand a meaningful role in the Disrupt ecosystem.
将你的品牌置于核心地位：主导受众关心的议题，让你的品牌在 Disrupt 生态系统中发挥重要作用。

Activate your community: Bring your existing network together while creating opportunities for members to meet new people, exchange ideas, and make valuable connections of their own.
激活你的社区：在汇聚现有网络的同时，为成员创造结识新朋友、交流想法并建立属于他们自己的宝贵人脉的机会。

Extend your reach beyond your guest list: Approved Side Events may be promoted through TechCrunch’s Side Events page, Disrupt agenda, mobile app, newsletters, articles, and social channels.
扩大你的影响力：获批的周边活动可以通过 TechCrunch 的周边活动页面、Disrupt 日程表、移动应用、时事通讯、文章和社交渠道进行推广。

Make it easier for your network to attend Disrupt: Give your community 25% off Disrupt tickets, making your Side Event part of a larger experience — not a stand-alone gathering.
让你的网络更容易参加 Disrupt：为你的社区提供 25% 的 Disrupt 门票折扣，使你的周边活动成为更大体验的一部分，而不仅仅是一个孤立的聚会。

This is your last shot: The opportunity is bigger than hosting a party or meetup. Use Disrupt week to create relationships, generate opportunities, strengthen your brand, and put your organization at the center of the conversations shaping what’s next.
这是你最后的机会：这个机会不仅仅是举办一场派对或聚会。利用 Disrupt 周来建立关系、创造机会、加强品牌建设，并将你的组织置于塑造未来的对话中心。

Applications close tonight at 11:59 p.m. PT. This is the final deadline. There will be no more extensions. To be considered for the official Side Events lineup, submit your application before midnight. Don’t just attend Disrupt. Make something happen around it.
申请将于今晚太平洋时间 11:59 截止。这是最终期限，不会再有延期。若想进入官方周边活动名单，请在午夜前提交申请。不要只是参加 Disrupt，要围绕它创造些什么。

Not hosting? You can still be a part of Disrupt: You don’t need to host a Side Event to make Disrupt work for your business. Get a ticket and use the week to source new customers and partners, meet investors, find talent, see what’s next in tech, and have the conversations that can move your business forward.
不举办活动？你依然可以参与 Disrupt：你不需要举办周边活动也能让 Disrupt 为你的业务服务。购买门票，利用这一周的时间寻找新客户和合作伙伴、会见投资者、发掘人才、洞察科技前沿，并进行能够推动你业务发展的对话。

Regular pricing ends September 25. Get your ticket before prices rise — and make sure you’re in the room for the people, ideas, and opportunities shaping what’s next. Get your ticket to TechCrunch Disrupt 2026.
常规票价将于 9 月 25 日截止。在价格上涨前购票，确保你身处现场，与那些塑造未来的人、思想和机遇同在。立即购买 TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 门票。
