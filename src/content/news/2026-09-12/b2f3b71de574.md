# Mexican student creates an acoustic fire extinguisher to put out fire in seconds
# 墨西哥学生发明声学灭火器，数秒内即可扑灭火焰

16-year-old Mexican student creates an acoustic fire extinguisher that uses sound waves to put out fires in seconds. Por Carlos Valencia, 7 September, 2026.
16 岁的墨西哥学生发明了一种利用声波在数秒内扑灭火焰的声学灭火器。作者：Carlos Valencia，2026 年 9 月 7 日。

Ángela Karime Venegas Hernández is 16 years old and has an idea no one else had. She studies at CETIS 78 in Altamira, Tamaulipas, and one day decided to put out a candle without blowing, without water, without anything but sound.
Ángela Karime Venegas Hernández 今年 16 岁，她有一个前所未有的想法。她在塔毛利帕斯州阿尔塔米拉市的 CETIS 78 学校就读，有一天她决定尝试在不吹气、不用水、仅靠声音的情况下熄灭一支蜡烛。

She assembled a device with a 12-volt battery, a frequency generator, and a speaker that emits 30 pulses per second. That vibration pushes oxygen away from the flames, and the fire simply goes out in five to eight seconds.
她组装了一台设备，其中包括一个 12 伏电池、一个频率发生器和一个每秒发射 30 次脉冲的扬声器。这种振动将氧气从火焰周围推开，火势在五到八秒内就会熄灭。

She tested it over and over, more than 100 times, until confirming that it also worked on wood, flammable liquids, cooking grease, and electronic equipment. It does not pollute, leave residue, or hurt the person using it.
她反复进行了 100 多次测试，最终确认该装置对木材、易燃液体、烹饪油脂和电子设备同样有效。它不会造成污染、留下残留物，也不会伤害使用者。

What began as a school experiment now has a name of its own, “Vortex Tech”, and is taking Ángela to represent Mexico at an international fair, where she will present her way of combating one of humanity’s oldest dangers to the world.
这项最初的学校实验现在有了自己的名字——“Vortex Tech”。它将带领 Ángela 代表墨西哥参加国际博览会，届时她将向世界展示她对抗人类最古老威胁之一的新方法。
