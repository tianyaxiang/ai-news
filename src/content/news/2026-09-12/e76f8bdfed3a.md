# The 9 Best TV Shows to Stream This Month (September 2026)
# 本月最值得观看的 9 部流媒体剧集（2026 年 9 月）

September is typically the time when television gets serious again. The summer’s biggest TV shows have aired their finales, the fall schedule is kicking into high gear, and streamers are rolling out everything from high-concept sci-fi to prestige dramas. There’s plenty of familiar IP in the mix, but also some genuinely original shows worth putting on your to-watch list.
九月通常是电视节目重回正轨的时期。夏季最热门的剧集已经播出了大结局，秋季档节目正全面铺开，流媒体平台也纷纷推出从高概念科幻到精品剧集等各类内容。其中既有许多熟悉的 IP，也有一些值得加入观看列表的原创佳作。

While plenty of outlets will tell you what everyone is watching (see: Reacher), that’s not our goal. As always, the TV shows we’re watching this month reflect the kinds of stories WIRED covers every day—shows that explore technology, science, politics, culture, and the sometimes unsettling ways they intersect.
虽然许多媒体会告诉你大众都在看什么（比如《侠探杰克》），但这并非我们的目标。一如既往，我们本月关注的剧集反映了《连线》（WIRED）每天报道的故事类型——即那些探索科技、科学、政治、文化及其有时令人不安的交集点的剧集。

From returning favorites to ambitious new series—and at least one show that might rekindle your love of traditional anime—these are the TV shows we think you should make time to stream this month.
从回归的热门剧集到雄心勃勃的新作，以及至少一部可能重燃你对传统动画热情的作品，以下是我们认为你本月应该抽出时间观看的剧集。

### The Paper
### 《报社》（The Paper）

What happens when a newspaper attempts not just to survive, but to thrive, in a world where digital media has become the norm? The Paper, a spinoff of The Office from Greg Daniels and Michael Koman, follows the staff of a struggling Midwestern paper as they attempt to revive it.
当数字媒体成为常态，一家报社不仅要生存，还要寻求发展，会发生什么？《报社》是格雷格·丹尼尔斯（Greg Daniels）和迈克尔·科曼（Michael Koman）继《办公室》（The Office）之后推出的衍生剧，讲述了一家中西部陷入困境的报社员工试图重振旗鼓的故事。

Ultimately, its idealistic new editor-in-chief Ned Sampson (Star Wars and Harry Potter alum Domhnall Gleeson) and his hungry young reporter Mare Pritti (Chelsea Frei) end up grappling with what journalism looks like in the digital age. Season 2, which arrived on September 9, finds their Toledo Truth Teller newly revitalized and searching for its next big scoop, making the show a surprisingly sharp comedy about the future of journalism—and who gets to shape it.
最终，理想主义的新任主编内德·桑普森（由《星球大战》和《哈利·波特》演员多姆纳尔·格里森饰演）和他渴望成功的年轻记者梅尔·普里蒂（切尔西·弗雷饰演）开始探索数字时代的新闻业究竟是什么样。9 月 9 日上线的第二季中，他们的《托莱多真相报》焕然一新，正在寻找下一个大新闻。这使得该剧成为一部关于新闻业未来——以及谁有权塑造它——的犀利喜剧。

### Lego Star Wars: The Mandalorian
### 《乐高星球大战：曼达洛人》（Lego Star Wars: The Mandalorian）

Though this summer’s The Mandalorian and Grogu (which is streaming on Disney+ now) seriously underperformed at the box office, Lucasfilm is partnering with Lego to take another stab at the fan-favorite Star Wars series—which might be exactly what the franchise needs right now. Lucasfilm and Lego are going back to the beginning, reimagining all three seasons of the Pedro Pascal–starring live-action series as a 49-minute animated comedy.
尽管今年夏天上映的《曼达洛人与格罗古》（目前正在 Disney+ 上线）在票房上表现惨淡，但卢卡斯影业正与乐高合作，再次尝试这部粉丝喜爱的《星球大战》系列——这或许正是该系列目前所需要的。卢卡斯影业和乐高回到了起点，将佩德罗·帕斯卡主演的三季真人剧集重新构思为一部 49 分钟的动画喜剧。

For anyone who somehow managed to miss Mando and Grogu until now, the story follows Din Djarin (Pascal in the live-action series, Gavin Hammon in this iteration), a bounty hunter known as the Mandalorian, who takes a job transporting a mysterious Force-sensitive child. That child, of course, is Grogu, aka Baby Yoda. What starts as a simple assignment turns into a galaxy-spanning adventure as the pair become unlikely partners. Consider this version an entertaining way to play catch-up.
对于那些至今还没看过“曼达洛人与格罗古”故事的人来说，剧情讲述了丁·贾林（真人版由帕斯卡饰演，本版由加文·哈蒙配音）的故事。他是一名被称为“曼达洛人”的赏金猎人，接下了一项运送一名神秘原力敏感儿童的任务。那个孩子当然就是格罗古，也就是“尤达宝宝”。原本简单的任务演变成了一场跨越银河系的冒险，两人成为了意想不到的搭档。将此版本视为补课的有趣方式吧。

### South Park
### 《南方公园》（South Park）

It’s hard to remember a time when the world didn’t have South Park. For nearly 30 years, audiences have watched as four potty-mouthed fourth-graders—Stan, Kyle, Cartman, and Kenny—navigate life in the bizarre mountain town of South Park, Colorado, in this very much adults-only animated comedy. Along the way, they’ve encountered alien life forms, celebrities, and Elon Musk (yes, the real one). They’ve also watched Kenny die on at least 100 occasions.
很难想象没有《南方公园》的世界是什么样。近 30 年来，观众们看着四个满口脏话的四年级学生——斯坦、凯尔、卡特曼和肯尼——在科罗拉多州南方公园这个古怪的山城里生活，这部动画喜剧绝对是成人专属。一路走来，他们遇到过外星生命、名人，甚至埃隆·马斯克（没错，就是那个真人）。他们还目睹了肯尼至少 100 次死亡。

Last year, the seemingly never-ending comedy reinvented itself as a particularly pointed political satire by taking aim at figures including JD Vance, Pete Hegseth, Kristi Noem, Donald Trump, and Donald Trump’s manhood. The White House, unsurprisingly, had thoughts. And so did the Emmys; the show just took home the 2026 award for ​​Outstanding Animated Program. Clearly, creators Trey Parker and Matt Stone have no plans to take their foot off the gas; they just announced they’re taking a page out of Trump’s renaming book and rechristening the show South America for its 29th season, which premieres on September 16.
去年，这部看似永无止境的喜剧通过抨击 JD·万斯、皮特·海格塞斯、克里斯蒂·诺姆、唐纳德·特朗普以及特朗普的男性气概等人物，将自己重塑为一部极其尖锐的政治讽刺剧。不出所料，白宫对此有话要说。艾美奖也是如此；该剧刚刚斩获了 2026 年“最佳动画节目奖”。显然，主创特雷·帕克和马特·斯通并不打算放慢脚步；他们刚刚宣布要效仿特朗普的改名策略，将第 29 季的剧名改为《南美洲》（South America），该季将于 9 月 16 日首播。

### Slow Horses
### 《流人》（Slow Horses）

What if television’s best spy show was also one of its funniest? If John le Carré and (the original) The Office had a baby, it would look and sound a lot like Slow Horses, which returns for a sixth season on September 16.
如果电视上最好的间谍剧同时也是最有趣的剧集会怎样？如果约翰·勒卡雷的作品和（原版）《办公室》结合，那它看起来和听起来会非常像《流人》，该剧将于 9 月 16 日回归第六季。

Based on Mick Herron’s Slough House book series, the series follows a dysfunctional team of MI5 misfits who are banished to the infamous Slough House unit, where they work under the notorious Jackson Lamb (Gary Oldman), a foul-mouthed, flatulent, chain-smoking wreck of a man who also happens to be the most brilliant operative in the room. It’s a prestige spy thriller that doesn’t take itself too seriously, bouncing effortlessly between shoot-outs and the kind of droll office banter you’d expect from the best workplace comedies. In a career full of memorable performances, and an Oscar to show for it, Slow Horses just might be the role Oldman is best remembered for. It’s also the project that has made Jack Lowden oddsmakers’ top pick for the next James Bond.
该剧改编自米克·赫伦的《泥沼屋》系列小说，讲述了一群被流放到臭名昭著的“泥沼屋”部门的军情五处失意特工。他们在恶名昭著的杰克逊·兰姆（加里·奥德曼饰）手下工作，兰姆是一个满口脏话、爱放屁、烟不离手的邋遢男人，但他恰好也是屋里最聪明的特工。这是一部不那么严肃的精品间谍惊悚剧，在枪战和职场喜剧中那种冷幽默的办公室闲聊之间切换自如。在奥德曼充满难忘表演的职业生涯中（并以此获得奥斯卡奖），《流人》可能正是他最令人铭记的角色。这也是让杰克·劳登成为博彩公司眼中下一任詹姆斯·邦德热门人选的项目。
Watch on Apple TV | 在 Apple TV 上观看

### City of Blood
### 《血之城》（City of Blood）

In City of Blood, an eight-episode miniseries from Germany, climate change has turned Berlin into a sweltering city teetering on the edge of collapse—and vampires are ready to take advantage of the chaos. While the privileged few live beneath a climate-controlled dome, the rest of the city’s denizens are left to battle the heat, crime, and corruption that have become part of everyday life. But when estranged sisters Bea (Lea Drinda) and Phoebe (Soma Pysall) reunite, they uncover a nefarious political plot and learn that, thanks to that aforementioned undead population, blood has become the city’s most coveted commodity. It’s a perfectly strange mix of climate dystopia, family drama, and vampire thriller. And it arrives on September 16.
在德国八集迷你剧《血之城》中，气候变化将柏林变成了一座摇摇欲坠的酷热城市——而吸血鬼正准备利用这场混乱。当少数特权阶层生活在气候可控的穹顶之下时，城市里的其他人只能在成为日常生活一部分的高温、犯罪和腐败中挣扎。但当疏远的姐妹比阿（莉亚·德林达饰）和菲比（索玛·皮萨尔饰）重逢时，她们揭露了一个邪恶的政治阴谋，并得知由于上述不死族群的存在，血液已成为这座城市最抢手的商品。这是一部完美融合了气候反乌托邦、家庭伦理和吸血鬼惊悚元素的奇特剧集。该剧将于 9 月 16 日上线。

### MobLand
### 《黑帮之地》（MobLand）

Season 2 of MobLand is almost here—whether Tom Hardy likes it or not. (Despite all the recent headlines surrounding the Oscar winner’s behind-the-scenes drama, he has already signed on for season 3.) The highly underrated, or perhaps just underseen, crime drama returns with the Harrigan family’s criminal empire under threat from rival forces, forcing their street-smart fixer Harry Da Souza (yep, Hardy) to navigate increasingly bloody internal tensions.
《黑帮之地》第二季即将到来——不管汤姆·哈迪喜不喜欢。（尽管最近围绕这位奥斯卡得主幕后风波的头条新闻不断，但他已经签约了第三季。）这部被严重低估，或者说只是观看人数不足的犯罪剧回归了，哈里根家族的犯罪帝国正受到敌对势力的威胁，迫使他们精明强干的“清理人”哈里·达·索萨（没错，就是哈迪）去应对日益血腥的内部矛盾。
