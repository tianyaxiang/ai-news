# We unfolded the iPhone Duo
# 我们折叠了 iPhone Duo

You only get one chance at a first impression, and getting to introduce Apple’s first folding phone is certainly a strong way to start things off as Apple’s CEO. On The Vergecast today, we’re talking all about Apple’s launch event: how John Ternus did in his first appearance as CEO, what we make of the new iPhones, and whether Apple is stepping into dangerous territory with its new AI features on the Apple Watch.

第一印象只有一次机会，而作为苹果 CEO，能够亲自发布苹果首款折叠屏手机，无疑是一个强有力的开场。在今天的《The Vergecast》节目中，我们将全面探讨苹果的发布会：John Ternus 作为 CEO 的首次亮相表现如何，我们对新款 iPhone 有何看法，以及苹果在 Apple Watch 上引入的新 AI 功能是否正在踏入危险的领域。

First up, Allison Johnson joins me to discuss the new iPhones, which she had a chance to test in Cupertino. Both of us have some questions about the new variable aperture camera on the iPhone 18 Pro — and whether the real upgrade simply lies in the wider aperture, not the ability to stop it down. Then we dig into the iPhone Duo and how Apple decided to pick and choose the best parts of Android foldables.

首先，Allison Johnson 将加入我的节目，讨论她在库比蒂诺亲手测试过的新款 iPhone。我们两人都对 iPhone 18 Pro 上新的可变光圈摄像头存有一些疑问——真正的升级是否仅仅在于更大的光圈，而不是缩小光圈的能力。随后，我们将深入探讨 iPhone Duo，以及苹果是如何决定博采众长，汲取安卓折叠屏手机的精华的。

After that, Victoria Song joins me to discuss the new Apple Watches, which she also had a chance to test out. We dive into the new sensor array and whether it allows for legitimately new metrics or just more vibesy health data. And then we both break down a little while figuring out what it means that Apple added the ability for both new Apple Watches to have an always-on microphone that summarizes all of your conversations.

之后，Victoria Song 将加入讨论新款 Apple Watch，她也有机会进行了实测。我们深入研究了新的传感器阵列，探讨它究竟是带来了真正有意义的新指标，还是仅仅提供了更多“感觉良好”的健康数据。随后，我们还深入分析了苹果为两款新 Apple Watch 加入的“全天候麦克风”功能——它可以总结你所有的对话，这究竟意味着什么。

This is an Apple-packed episode, so from there we jump to a special segment with Nilay Patel and David Imel, who speak with AI researcher CJ Trowbridge about the state of running local LLMs on the Mac. Finally, it’s the long-awaited return of the Thunder Round, as we race through stories so that Allison can make it to the airport. We’re chatting OpenAI math drama, digital ocarinas, and more.

这是一期内容满满的苹果专题节目，接下来我们将进入一个特别环节，由 Nilay Patel 和 David Imel 与 AI 研究员 CJ Trowbridge 对话，探讨在 Mac 上运行本地大语言模型（LLM）的现状。最后，期待已久的“雷霆轮”（Thunder Round）环节回归，我们将快速浏览各类新闻，以便 Allison 能赶上飞机。我们将聊聊 OpenAI 的数学争议、电子陶笛等等。
