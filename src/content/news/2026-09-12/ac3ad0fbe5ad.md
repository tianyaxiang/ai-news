### This founder is making cheaper, cleaner steel
### 这位创始人正在制造更廉价、更清洁的钢铁

The steel industry isn’t exactly known for innovation. Very little has changed about purifying iron ore since the process was invented and commercialized in the 1850s. But Laureen Meroueh, founder of Hertha Metals, has an idea that could change that. Meroueh may have found a way to clean up steelmaking without driving up the price. Her new furnace turns iron ore into refined liquid steel in a single step and swaps coal for natural gas. Together, those changes slash emissions by at least half, she says, and cut costs by 25% compared with steelmaking as usual. Here’s how she plans to make steel cleaner without making it more expensive. —Bridget Reed Morawski
钢铁行业向来不以创新著称。自 19 世纪 50 年代铁矿石提炼工艺发明并商业化以来，该过程几乎没有什么变化。但 Hertha Metals 的创始人 Laureen Meroueh 有一个想法，可能会改变这一现状。Meroueh 可能找到了一种在不推高价格的情况下实现钢铁生产清洁化的方法。她的新型熔炉只需一步即可将铁矿石转化为精炼液态钢，并用天然气取代了煤炭。她表示，这些改变加在一起，可以将排放量至少减少一半，并比传统炼钢成本降低 25%。以下是她计划如何在不增加成本的前提下实现钢铁清洁生产的方法。——Bridget Reed Morawski
