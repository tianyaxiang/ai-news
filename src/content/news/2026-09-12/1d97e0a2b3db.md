# Ask HN: Can we please limit the AI news flood?
# Ask HN：我们能限制一下 AI 新闻的泛滥吗？

Over past couple months I noticed that HN feed is almost exclusively AI or AI-adjacent news. Meanwhile the legitimately, broadly-hacker stuff gets left out for the most part.
在过去的几个月里，我注意到 Hacker News (HN) 的信息流几乎被 AI 或与 AI 相关的新闻所垄断。与此同时，那些真正属于广泛意义上的“黑客”内容却在很大程度上被冷落了。

I noticed that because the things I find genuinely interesting that I post here now get zero traction, which is the stuff that I believe would previously be met with some discussion.
我之所以注意到这一点，是因为我现在发布的一些我个人觉得非常有趣的内容，却完全无人问津；而这些内容在过去，我相信是能够引发一些讨论的。

Note that I don't care that my submissions were ignored, I don't need that validation. Problem is that if my stuff gains no traction, I am myself not seeing similar stuff posted by others, stuff I am genuinely also interested in and would want to hear about from this community.
请注意，我并不在意我的投稿被忽视，我不需要那种认可。问题在于，如果我的内容没有热度，我自己也看不到其他人发布的类似内容——那些我真心感兴趣、并希望从这个社区了解到的内容。

Here are two most recent examples:
以下是两个最近的例子：

- Lenovo showed a model that incorporates solid-state air cooling. That technology has been around for a while but it looks like it matured and is production-ready. This allows for super-light and slim designs, which, in turn, should allow for bigger batteries installed and I think it's some sort of breakthrough: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49580229
- 联想展示了一款采用固态空气冷却技术的机型。这项技术已经存在一段时间了，但看起来它已经成熟并具备了量产条件。这使得超轻薄设计成为可能，进而可以容纳更大的电池，我认为这算是一种突破：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49580229

- Brax Industries devices, genuinely interesting, open-source and innovative, especially the wall display, with its modularity and on-device processing compatible with Home Assistant. I also appreciate the very clean design: https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49643215
- Brax Industries 的设备，非常有趣、开源且具有创新性，尤其是那款壁挂式显示器，它具有模块化特性，且其设备端处理功能兼容 Home Assistant。我也很欣赏它简洁的设计：https://news.ycombinator.com/item?id=49643215

As said, I honestly believe this would gain some traction here on HN a year or two ago. I no longer find HN a good source of "what's new" in the industry, it's almost exclusively AI talk now.
如前所述，我真心认为一两年前这些内容在 HN 上是会获得一定关注的。我不再觉得 HN 是了解行业“新鲜事”的好渠道了，因为它现在几乎全是关于 AI 的讨论。

So I think the point I am trying to make is that maybe YC could start curating the content somehow to give those easily-drowned-out articles a visibility bump?
所以我想要表达的观点是，或许 YC（Y Combinator）可以开始以某种方式对内容进行策展，给那些容易被淹没的文章增加一些曝光度？

Or, at the very least, introduce simple tagging, so we can start filtering stuff out.
或者，至少引入简单的标签功能，这样我们就可以开始过滤掉一些内容了。
