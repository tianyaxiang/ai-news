# Shedding Light: A Benchmark for Evaluating Lighting Understanding in Generative Image Models
# 照亮真相：评估生成式图像模型光照理解能力的基准测试

**Abstract:** Accurate modelling of illumination is central to realistic image synthesis and scene understanding. Yet, there is little exploration into whether image generative models are good at this task or whether physical plausibility remains a key challenge for them. Clearly, significant progress has been made in realistic image synthesis, but do models truly understand lighting in a physically accurate manner?

**摘要：** 精确的光照建模对于逼真的图像合成和场景理解至关重要。然而，目前关于图像生成模型是否擅长此项任务，或者物理合理性是否仍是其面临的关键挑战，相关研究还很少。显然，逼真图像合成领域已经取得了重大进展，但模型是否真的以物理精确的方式理解了光照？

To answer this question, this work proposes a benchmark to assess the lighting understanding and harmonisation capabilities of generative models. Our key insight is that evaluating lighting understanding for such models only requires testing how well they insert novel objects into real photographs whilst maintaining consistent illumination.

为了回答这个问题，本研究提出了一个基准测试，旨在评估生成模型的光照理解和融合能力。我们的核心见解是：评估此类模型的光照理解能力，只需测试它们在将新物体插入真实照片时，保持光照一致性的表现如何。

To do so, we use a multi-illumination dataset with images containing simple objects serving as ``light probes'', and prompt models to inpaint the same object onto the original image, then compare the generated results against the ground-truth light probes. We then estimate the lighting direction, colour and radiance distribution from the inpainted probes, providing a quantitative measure of illumination accuracy and photometric realism.

为此，我们使用了一个多光照数据集，其中包含作为“光照探针”的简单物体图像。我们提示模型将相同的物体修复（inpaint）到原始图像中，然后将生成的结果与作为基准（ground-truth）的光照探针进行比较。随后，我们从修复后的探针中估算光照方向、颜色和辐射分布，从而提供光照准确性和光度真实性的定量衡量指标。

Our work establishes a scalable evaluation protocol to systematically assess how well generative models capture and reproduce real-world lighting, offering a foundation for benchmarking the photometric accuracy of any future models. All code and data are available at this https URL.

我们的工作建立了一套可扩展的评估协议，用于系统地评估生成模型捕捉和再现现实世界光照的能力，为未来所有模型的光度准确性基准测试奠定了基础。所有代码和数据均可在该链接获取。
