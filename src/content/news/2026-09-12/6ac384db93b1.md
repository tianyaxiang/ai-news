# How Much Velocity Does Off-Ball Space Value Need? A Broadcast-Viewport Benchmark
# 无球空间价值需要多少速度信息？基于广播视角的基准测试

**Abstract:** Velocity-aware pitch control is standard, but under a broadcast viewport half the players are off screen and on-screen velocities come from a drifting calibration. We ask at which layer of broadcast off-ball analysis velocity changes the answer. Inheriting our off-screen imputation protocol (three Metrica matches, 44 m viewport, block-bootstrap CIs), we score four velocity regimes -- none, viewport-legal observed, true-for-visible, true-for-all -- against a velocity-aware ground truth at three layers: imputation, the control surface, and team verdicts.

**摘要：** 速度感知型球场控制（Pitch Control）已是行业标准，但在广播视角下，半数球员处于画面之外，且画面内的速度数据源于漂移的校准。我们探讨了在广播无球分析的哪个层级上，速度信息会改变分析结果。基于我们既有的离屏补全协议（使用三场 Metrica 比赛数据、44 米视角、分块自助法置信区间），我们在补全、控制面（Control Surface）和团队结论三个层级上，对比了四种速度机制（无速度、视角内观测速度、可见球员真实速度、全员真实速度）与速度感知型基准真值之间的差异。

Velocity is nearly useless for imputation (-0.2 pp against a 12--14 pp velocity-free surface MAE), first-order for the surface (-1.5 to -1.8 pp, 11--15% of that MAE), and ten times smaller for verdicts (-0.12 to -0.19 pp). The velocity that matters is the visible channel: perfect occluded-player velocity adds 2--6% of the visible gain, and no last-seen decay policy we tested exceeds that. Omitting velocity blurs the surface (per-frame |e| 2.2--2.6 pp) with small time-averaged bias (per cell <=0.4 pp), whereas imputation error is a structured bias against the defending team's deep zone (5--9 pp).

速度信息对于补全几乎没有用处（相对于 12-14 个百分点的无速度表面平均绝对误差 MAE，仅改善了 -0.2 个百分点），对于控制面则是首要因素（改善 -1.5 至 -1.8 个百分点，占该 MAE 的 11-15%），而对于团队结论的影响则小十倍（-0.12 至 -0.19 个百分点）。真正起作用的是可见通道的速度：即使拥有完美的被遮挡球员速度数据，也仅能增加 2-6% 的可见增益，且我们测试的所有“最后可见位置衰减”策略均未超过这一水平。忽略速度会使控制面变得模糊（单帧误差 |e| 为 2.2-2.6 个百分点），但时间平均偏差较小（每单元 <=0.4 个百分点）；相比之下，补全误差则表现为针对防守方深区的结构性偏差（5-9 个百分点）。

At a fixed velocity window, a noise ladder of eleven jitter settings, including sigma_v-matched pairs, is ordered to first order by one velocity-noise axis sigma_v with break-even ~1 m/s; eleven SoccerNet-GSR clips from one match through our pipeline measure sigma_v=1.65 m/s yet recover 24--36% of the benefit: 43% of the variance is frame-common, which the surface tolerates, and the residual is heavy-tailed and clustered, which Gaussian controls matched on component RMS do not reproduce (+0.03 vs. +0.36). The share of velocity-free error that velocity removes grows with viewport width (7% at 36 m, 21% at 60 m): fix imputation on tight shots, velocity on wide ones. Code and logs are released.

在固定的速度窗口下，包含 sigma_v 匹配对在内的 11 种抖动设置噪声阶梯，按单一速度噪声轴 sigma_v 进行一阶排序，盈亏平衡点约为 1 米/秒；通过我们的流水线处理来自一场比赛的 11 个 SoccerNet-GSR 剪辑，测得 sigma_v=1.65 米/秒，但仍恢复了 24-36% 的收益：其中 43% 的方差是帧间共有的（控制面可容忍），而残差呈现重尾和聚类分布，这是基于分量均方根（RMS）匹配的高斯控制无法复现的（+0.03 对比 +0.36）。速度信息所能消除的无速度误差份额随视角宽度增加而增长（36 米视角下为 7%，60 米视角下为 21%）：在特写镜头中应优先修复补全问题，而在广角镜头中则应优先处理速度数据。代码和日志已发布。
