# MHE-Former: Multi-Hypothesis Transformers via Entropy Maximization for 3D Mesh Recovery
# MHE-Former：基于熵最大化多假设 Transformer 的 3D 网格恢复

**Abstract:** Monocular 3D hand and body mesh recovery often suffers from severe occlusion and ambiguity. Traditional deterministic methods typically regress a single optimal solution, leading to overconfident predictions.
**摘要：** 单目 3D 手部和身体网格恢复任务经常受到严重遮挡和歧义性的困扰。传统的确定性方法通常回归单一的最优解，这往往会导致过度自信的预测。

In this paper, we introduce an exploration--exploitation paradigm for ambiguous mesh recovery with multi-hypothesis learning and selection. Specifically, during exploration, based on our probabilistic formulation and entropy maximization, we propose a novel multi-hypothesis method referred to as MHE-Former. It is a Transformer-based multi-hypothesis framework, ensuring high training efficiency and label friendliness while generating plausible and diverse hypotheses.
在本文中，我们引入了一种用于歧义网格恢复的“探索-利用”（exploration-exploitation）范式，通过多假设学习与选择来解决该问题。具体而言，在探索阶段，基于我们的概率公式和熵最大化原则，我们提出了一种名为 MHE-Former 的新型多假设方法。这是一个基于 Transformer 的多假设框架，在确保高训练效率和标签友好性的同时，能够生成合理且多样的假设。

During exploitation, we propose Hypothesis Selection, a context-aware process for multiple predictions. Especially leveraging VLM's powerful visual understanding and reasoning capabilities, it allows users to choose the most plausible and desired estimate with additional evidence and natural language intent.
在利用阶段，我们提出了“假设选择”（Hypothesis Selection），这是一个针对多重预测的上下文感知过程。特别是通过利用视觉语言模型（VLM）强大的视觉理解和推理能力，该过程允许用户结合额外的证据和自然语言意图，选择最合理且符合预期的估计结果。

Extensive experiments demonstrate that our framework achieves state-of-the-art performance in accuracy and diversity across multiple datasets. The user preference study further shows the practicality of our hypothesis selection process.
大量实验表明，我们的框架在多个数据集上的准确性和多样性方面均达到了最先进（SOTA）的性能。用户偏好研究进一步证明了我们假设选择过程的实用性。
