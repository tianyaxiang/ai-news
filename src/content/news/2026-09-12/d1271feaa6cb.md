# The Trump Alien ‘Disclosure Speech’ Rumors Are Reaching a Fever Pitch
# 关于特朗普“外星人披露演讲”的传言正愈演愈烈

In 2006, Donald Trump was firing people on *The Apprentice* and Dr. Phil was daytime television's reigning tough-love therapist. Twenty years later, the former is president of the United States, the latter, his informal UFO ambassador—recently trusted with unreleased government files on unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAP) to gauge how the public might handle what's inside.
2006年，唐纳德·特朗普还在《飞黄腾达》节目里解雇选手，而菲尔博士（Dr. Phil）则是日间电视节目中当之无愧的“严厉爱心”治疗师。二十年后，前者已是美国总统，后者则成了他非正式的“UFO大使”——最近，菲尔博士被委托处理有关不明异常现象（UAP）的未公开政府文件，以评估公众对其中内容的接受程度。

"Here's what comes in stages. First they acknowledge it, then they confirm it's real, then they tell you where it's from," Dr. Phil said in an August 7 Instagram video discussing UAP disclosure. "And somewhere down that road, maybe, is the big one—the government of the United States standing at a podium and saying out loud, we are not alone."
“事情会分阶段进行。首先他们会承认它的存在，然后确认它是真实的，接着告诉你它来自哪里，”菲尔博士在8月7日讨论UAP披露的Instagram视频中说道。“而在那条路上的某个节点，或许就是那个重磅时刻——美国政府站在讲台上大声宣布：我们并不孤单。”

For most of 2026, a rumor has consumed the world of UFOlogy: President Trump is sitting on a finished speech confirming humanity isn't alone. Whether the “disclosure speech” actually exists is a topic of rabid discussion on X, the r/UFO subreddit, and dozens of extraterrestrial-curious podcasts, but the reasons so many people believe in it are very real. Humans are arguably experiencing the most consequential era for the UFO scene since the ’40s and ’50s, when a mysterious crash outside Roswell, New Mexico, and unexplained radar blips over Washington, DC, birthed decades of legends of little green men and flying-saucers.
2026年的大部分时间里，一个传言席卷了UFO研究界：特朗普总统手中握有一份已经准备好的演讲稿，旨在确认人类并不孤单。这份“披露演讲”是否真实存在，已成为X平台、r/UFO子版块以及数十个对外星文明感兴趣的播客中激烈讨论的话题，但人们之所以深信不疑，原因非常现实。可以说，人类正经历着自40年代和50年代以来UFO领域最重要的时期——当时，新墨西哥州罗斯威尔郊外的神秘坠毁事件和华盛顿特区上空无法解释的雷达信号，催生了数十年来关于“小绿人”和飞碟的传说。

There's no doubt that the government has been engaging with the topic. Secretary of State Marco Rubio recently confirmed that Stephen Miller, one of the most powerful figures in the White House, has been handed the UAP portfolio.
毫无疑问，政府一直在介入这一议题。国务卿马尔科·鲁比奥最近证实，白宫最有权势的人物之一斯蒂芬·米勒已被委以处理UAP事务的重任。

Tim Gallaudet, a retired Navy rear admiral who helped run the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration during Trump's first term, testified before Congress about UAP in 2024, and now sits on a UAP Science Advisory Council reporting to the Office of the Director of National Intelligence. He called the increased government interest “a total change in executive-branch transparency from the last 80 years."
蒂姆·加洛德（Tim Gallaudet）是一位退役海军少将，曾在特朗普第一任期内协助管理国家海洋和大气管理局。他在2024年就UAP问题在国会作证，目前是向国家情报总监办公室汇报的UAP科学咨询委员会成员。他称政府日益增长的关注是“行政部门在透明度方面与过去80年相比的彻底转变”。

But he’s unsure if that would ever culminate in a speech from a sitting president, because they would “have to acknowledge that we're not in control, that we're not the highest level of intelligence on the planet, and that's going to be a very hard thing for any government to admit."
但他不确定这最终是否会演变成现任总统的演讲，因为他们“必须承认我们并不处于掌控之中，我们不是地球上最高等级的智慧生命，这对任何政府来说都是一件极难承认的事情。”

A White House spokesperson tells WIRED via email that “No such speech is planned—rather, the administration continues to release files as they become available, and the public to make up their own minds about the information uncovered.”
白宫发言人通过电子邮件告诉《连线》（WIRED）杂志：“目前没有此类演讲计划——相反，政府将继续在文件可用时进行发布，并由公众自行对所披露的信息做出判断。”

For believers, the modern UAP disclosure began in 2017, when a New York Times article revealed a secret Pentagon program quietly investigating UAPs. The Pentagon went on to confirm in 2020 that leaked videos of Navy pilots tracking seemingly impossible objects were real. A 2021 intelligence report conceded the phenomena existed and remained unexplained, and in 2022 the government established an official investigation office. Then, from 2022 to 2025, Congress held yearly public hearings where pilots, military officers, and intelligence veterans (including Gallaudet) testified under oath.
对于信徒们来说，现代UAP披露始于2017年，当时《纽约时报》的一篇文章揭露了五角大楼一个秘密调查UAP的项目。五角大楼随后在2020年证实，海军飞行员追踪看似不可能物体的泄露视频是真实的。2021年的一份情报报告承认这些现象确实存在且无法解释，2022年政府成立了官方调查办公室。随后，从2022年到2025年，国会每年举行公开听证会，飞行员、军官和情报老兵（包括加洛德）在会上宣誓作证。

What’s also novel is that UAP disclosure has bipartisan support. Both the House task force on declassifying federal secrets and the House UAP Caucus are made up of legislators from across the aisle.
同样新颖的是，UAP披露得到了两党的支持。众议院联邦机密解密特别工作组和众议院UAP核心小组均由来自两党的立法者组成。

This year, the drumbeat of government interest turned into a drumroll about a potential Trump speech. In January, filmmaker Mark Christopher Lee claimed a disclosure speech was imminent, pegged to the 79th anniversary of the alleged Roswell crash on July 8. Few took him seriously.
今年，政府关注的节奏演变成了关于特朗普可能发表演讲的舆论造势。1月，电影制作人马克·克里斯托弗·李声称披露演讲即将到来，并将其与7月8日所谓的罗斯威尔坠毁事件79周年纪念日挂钩。但很少有人当真。

But in February, the president's daughter-in-law, Lara Trump, gave the idea new legs, saying on the Pod Force One podcast she'd heard Trump had a speech ready touching on "some sort of extraterrestrial life." Around the same time, Barack Obama mused in a podcast interview with Brian Tyler Cohen that aliens are “real” before conceding “I haven’t seen them.” Trump then accused his predecessor of disclosing classified information, and announced that he was directing agencies to release their files on "alien and extraterrestrial life."
但在2月，总统儿媳劳拉·特朗普（Lara Trump）为这一想法注入了新动力，她在《Pod Force One》播客中表示，她听说特朗普准备了一份涉及“某种外星生命”的演讲稿。大约在同一时间，巴拉克·奥巴马在接受布莱恩·泰勒·科恩的播客采访时沉思道，外星人是“真实存在的”，随后又承认“我没见过他们”。特朗普随即指责其前任泄露机密信息，并宣布他已指示各机构发布有关“外星生命”的文件。

What followed was bizarre even by this administration's standards. In May, Trump posted an AI-generated image of himself escorting a handcuffed gray alien, and a mysterious loading page appeared on the domain aliens.gov, leading to rampant speculation within the UFO community that the big reveal was coming. But when the White House finally launched aliens.gov late in the month, it ended up being a Star Wars–style website that functioned as an ICE tip line—with aliens being a reference to undocumented people.
随后的事情即使以本届政府的标准来看也十分离奇。5月，特朗普发布了一张由人工智能生成的图片，图中他正押送着一个戴手铐的“小灰人”。同时，aliens.gov域名出现了一个神秘的加载页面，导致UFO社区内疯狂猜测重大揭秘即将到来。但当白宫在月底最终上线aliens.gov时，它竟是一个《星球大战》风格的网站，功能是作为美国移民及海关执法局（ICE）的举报热线——这里的“aliens”指的是无证移民。

The machinery of disclosure kept grinding forward. Since May, the government has released five batches of declassified videos and documents onto the Department of War's website. Detractors say the files lack context and the videos prove little.
披露的机器仍在继续运转。自5月以来，政府已在战争部（Department of War）网站上发布了五批解密视频和文件。批评者认为这些文件缺乏背景信息，视频也证明不了什么。

Even after the Roswell date passed, rumors about a disclosure speech intensified. In late July, Liberation Times—an independent news website with a record of breaking UAP stories—reported that a speech confirming some UFOs are of non-human origin had been prepared for Trump.
即使在罗斯威尔纪念日过去之后，关于披露演讲的传言反而愈演愈烈。7月下旬，《解放时报》（Liberation Times）——一家以报道UAP独家新闻而闻名的独立新闻网站——报道称，一份确认部分UFO属于非人类起源的演讲稿已为特朗普准备就绪。

The site’s founder, Christopher Sharp, tells WIRED the story began with a call from a source he deeply trusts. "They basically said, ‘Look, I'm quite shaken, because it looks like Trump may make some kind of announcement on UAP disclosure’." Another source described the speech itself, he says, alleging it basically reads, “we're not alone, we have captured some of these or retrieved some of these things, they're of non-human origin, and that there is a reverse engineering effort."
该网站创始人克里斯托弗·夏普（Christopher Sharp）告诉《连线》杂志，这个故事始于他深信不疑的消息源打来的一通电话。“他们基本上说，‘听着，我很震惊，因为看起来特朗普可能会就UAP披露发表某种声明’。”他说，另一位消息人士描述了演讲内容，声称其大意是：“我们并不孤单，我们已经捕获或回收了其中一些东西，它们属于非人类起源，并且目前正在进行逆向工程研究。”

Sharp was also told Trump wouldn't deliver it unless unspecified conditions were met. "If you're the president, you have to be sure that there's firm evidence," he says. "The worst thing you can do is come out with this speech and then whatever you put out could be debunked straight away ... You'll go down in history as a laughingstock."
夏普还被告知，除非满足某些未指明的条件，否则特朗普不会发表这篇演讲。“如果你是总统，你必须确保有确凿的证据，”他说。“最糟糕的事情莫过于发表了演讲，结果你所展示的一切立刻被驳倒……那样你将作为笑柄载入史册。”
