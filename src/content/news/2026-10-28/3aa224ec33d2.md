Insurance tech company Guidewire announced Tuesday the acquisition of the agentic underwriting insurance company Hyperexponential for $1.7 billion in cash. The deal, expected to close sometime next year, is to help bring AI agents to work alongside humans to underwrite, analyze, and price decisions.

保险科技公司 Guidewire 周二宣布以 17 亿美元现金收购代理承保保险公司 Hyperexponential。该交易预计将于明年某个时候完成，旨在帮助引入 AI 代理与人类协同工作，以进行承保、分析和定价决策。

The deal is worth around $2 billion in total, with $400 million in additional capital reserved if certain revenue targets are hit. Backed by Andreessen Horowitz and Battery Ventures, Hyperexponential was founded in 2017 and has raised nearly $90 million to date, according to PitchBook. So this marks a significant return for investors.
