# Expanding our partnership with GSA to bring Claude to all levels of government

我们正在通过一项新协议扩大与总务管理局（GSA）的合作伙伴关系，为政府合作伙伴提供 Claude Enterprise、Claude for Government 和 Claude API 40% 的折扣。这是该折扣首次适用于所有产品界面，包括目前直接从我们这里购买，以及未来几个月通过云服务提供商购买的情况。该折扣还扩展到了州、地方、部落和领地政府。

We are also launching a dedicated program to help agencies implement and make the most of their Claude use. These programs are commonly leveraged by our private sector customers and we’re looking forward to bringing those best practices to our public sector partners in the coming weeks.

我们还启动了一项专门计划，旨在帮助各机构实施并充分利用 Claude。这些计划通常被我们的私营部门客户所采用，我们期待在未来几周内将这些最佳实践带给我们的公共部门合作伙伴。

Approximately 500 US federal, state, and local government organizations now use Claude. Weekly Claude for Government active users are up about eightfold since February, and usage across federal civilian agencies has grown about 20-fold in a year. Those organizations run some of the most important systems in the country, usually on tight budgets and with long procurement cycles. This agreement is built to support that work, so they can apply Claude to hard problems without a large upfront commitment.

目前约有 500 个美国联邦、州和地方政府组织正在使用 Claude。自 2 月以来，Claude for Government 的每周活跃用户增长了约 8 倍，联邦民事机构的使用量在一年内增长了约 20 倍。这些组织运行着国内一些最重要的系统，通常预算紧张且采购周期较长。这项协议旨在支持这些工作，使他们能够在无需大量前期投入的情况下将 Claude 应用于棘手的问题。

The discounts offered through the agreement are estimated to save the government $1 billion. Agencies are not bound by a minimum spend commitment and will be able to pay for the usage they consume.

据估计，通过该协议提供的折扣将为政府节省 10 亿美元。各机构不受最低消费承诺的约束，只需为其实际使用的量付费。

Meeting government security requirements
Agencies can use Claude for their most sensitive unclassified work. Claude for Government, which delivers Claude's coding and agentic work capabilities and provides near-feature parity with commercial offerings at a FedRAMP High authorization level, was made generally available on September 30.

满足政府安全要求
各机构可以将 Claude 用于其最敏感的非机密工作。Claude for Government 于 9 月 30 日全面发布，它提供了 Claude 的编码和代理工作能力，并在 FedRAMP High 授权级别上提供了与商业产品近乎一致的功能。

Agencies building their own tools can use Claude through Amazon Bedrock or Google Cloud’s Vertex AI. On Bedrock, Claude is authorized for FedRAMP High, with select models authorized for DoD IL4 and IL5 workloads in AWS GovCloud. On Vertex AI, Claude is authorized for FedRAMP High.

自行构建工具的机构可以通过 Amazon Bedrock 或 Google Cloud 的 Vertex AI 使用 Claude。在 Bedrock 上，Claude 已获得 FedRAMP High 授权，部分模型已获得 AWS GovCloud 中 DoD IL4 和 IL5 工作负载的授权。在 Vertex AI 上，Claude 已获得 FedRAMP High 授权。

Claude Enterprise is in process for FedRAMP Moderate authorization through the FedRAMP 20x program, marking the next evolution of how we are bringing the same innovative technology available commercially to the government.

Claude Enterprise 正通过 FedRAMP 20x 计划申请 FedRAMP Moderate 授权，这标志着我们将商业领域现有的创新技术引入政府的下一个演进阶段。
