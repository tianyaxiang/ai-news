The end of the year is fast approaching and with it, the pre-Christmas release of Dune: Part Three, the final installment in Denis Villeneuve’s acclaimed adaptation of sci-fi author Frank Herbert’s novel(s). Warner Bros. gave us a broody and haunting extended teaser in March and a July trailer jam-packed with tantalizing hints of what to expect, along with plenty of Easter eggs to delight avid book fans. Now we’ve got one last action-packed trailer setting up the final act for iconic anti-hero Paul Atreides (Timothée Chalamet). (Spoilers for first two films in the franchise below.)

年底将至，丹尼斯·维伦纽瓦（Denis Villeneuve）改编自科幻作家弗兰克·赫伯特（Frank Herbert）小说的《沙丘：第三部》（Dune: Part Three）也即将在圣诞节前上映，这是该系列的最终章。华纳兄弟曾在三月发布了一段忧郁而令人难忘的加长预告片，七月又发布了一支充满诱人线索和大量彩蛋的预告片，让原著书迷们大饱眼福。现在，我们终于迎来了最后一部动作场面密集的预告片，为标志性反英雄保罗·厄崔迪（提莫西·查拉梅饰）的终章拉开序幕。（以下内容包含前两部电影的剧透。）

Previously, 2021’s Dune culminated in the brutal defeat of House Atreides by rival House Harkonnen, with Paul and his mother, Lady Jessica (Rebecca Ferguson), fleeing to the desert and taking refuge among the Fremen. Among them is Chani (Zendaya), whom Paul has been seeing in visions all along. In Dune: Part Two (2024), Paul ingratiated himself with the Fremen, learned to ride a sandworm, and won Chani’s love. A pregnant Lady Jessica had her own plans for Paul as a messiah figure to fulfill a Bene Gesserit prophecy. Ultimately, Paul challenges the emperor, defeats his champion, and demands the Princess Irulan (Florence Pugh) as his wife. The other Great Houses reject Paul as ruler, so he launches a holy war against them. And Chani rides off on a sandworm after refusing to bow down to Paul.

回顾2021年的《沙丘》，故事以厄崔迪家族被宿敌哈克南家族残酷击败告终，保罗和他的母亲杰西卡夫人（丽贝卡·弗格森饰）逃往沙漠，在弗雷曼人中寻求庇护。其中就包括保罗在幻象中一直看到的契妮（赞达亚饰）。在《沙丘2》（2024）中，保罗赢得了弗雷曼人的信任，学会了骑沙虫，并赢得了契妮的芳心。怀孕的杰西卡夫人则有自己的计划，她希望将保罗塑造成救世主，以实现贝尼·杰瑟里特姐妹会的预言。最终，保罗挑战皇帝，击败了其冠军斗士，并要求迎娶伊勒琅公主（弗洛伦斯·皮尤饰）。由于其他大家族拒绝承认保罗的统治地位，他便对他们发动了圣战。而契妮在拒绝向保罗屈服后，骑着沙虫离去。

Dune: Part Three draws on Herbert’s novel Dune: Messiah and will take place roughly 17 years after the end of Part Two, according to Villeneuve, as Paul faces the consequences of his earlier actions. Specifically, an assassination plot is brewing, and Chani is “involved.” Per the official synopsis: Dune: Part Three is set nearly two decades after Paul Atreides seized control of the Imperium. Now a ruthless Emperor, Paul must face the consequences of his reign as old allies return, terrifying new threats emerge, and betrayal lurks in every shadow. Haunted by visions of Imperial collapse and the reappearance of his long-lost love, Paul is drawn into a sweeping conspiracy, with Chani at the heart of its unfolding mystery. As enemies close in, Paul must confront the true cost of power and the fate of those he loves the most.

《沙丘：第三部》取材于赫伯特的小说《沙丘救世主》。据维伦纽瓦透露，故事背景设定在《沙丘2》结束约17年后，保罗将面临他此前行为所带来的后果。具体而言，一场暗杀阴谋正在酝酿，而契妮也“卷入其中”。根据官方剧情简介：《沙丘：第三部》的故事发生在保罗·厄崔迪夺取帝国控制权近二十年后。如今已成为冷酷皇帝的保罗，必须面对他统治带来的后果：旧盟友回归，可怕的新威胁浮现，背叛潜伏在每一个阴影中。被帝国崩溃的幻象和失散已久的爱人重现所困扰，保罗被卷入了一场宏大的阴谋，而契妮正是这一谜团的核心。随着敌人步步紧逼，保罗必须直面权力的真正代价以及他最爱之人的命运。

Chalamet is back as Paul Atreides, along with Zendaya, Pugh, and Ferguson. Josh Brolin reprises his role as weapons master Gurney Halleck; Javier Bardem is back as Stilgar, leader of the Fremen tribe at Sietch Tabr; and Jason Momoa returns as Duncan Idaho/Hayt. Joining the cast are Anya Taylor-Joy as Paul’s younger sister, Alia; Robert Pattinson as the villainous Scytale, who plots to dethrone Paul; Isaach de Bankolé as Paul’s former Fedaykin, Farok; Nakoa-Wolf Momoa (son of Jason) as Paul and Chani’s son, Leto II Atreides; and Ida Brooke as Paul and Chani’s daughter, Ghanima Atreides. Dune: Part Three opens in theaters on December 18, 2026, going head to head with Avengers: Doomsday.

查拉梅回归饰演保罗·厄崔迪，赞达亚、皮尤和弗格森也将回归。乔什·布洛林继续饰演武器大师哥尼·哈莱克；哈维尔·巴登回归饰演塔布尔穴地弗雷曼部落首领斯第尔格；杰森·莫玛回归饰演邓肯·艾达荷/海特。新加入的演员包括：安雅·泰勒-乔伊饰演保罗的妹妹阿丽亚；罗伯特·帕丁森饰演阴险的斯凯特尔，他密谋推翻保罗；伊萨赫·德·班克尔饰演保罗的前费代金战士法洛克；纳科亚-沃尔夫·莫玛（杰森之子）饰演保罗与契妮的儿子雷托二世；艾达·布鲁克饰演保罗与契妮的女儿加尼玛。《沙丘：第三部》将于2026年12月18日在影院上映，并将与《复仇者联盟：末日》正面交锋。
