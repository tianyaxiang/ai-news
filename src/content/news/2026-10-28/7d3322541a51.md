# Quince’s Merino Wool Tee Doesn’t Cost a Fortune

I've been singing the praises of merino wool in these pages for over five years. Most people have come around to the fact that merino wool is a super fiber; it's both sustainable and quite remarkable in its capabilities. Merino wool somehow manages to be comfortable in 95-degree heat, and it'll insulate you against below-freezing temperatures. Unlike synthetic fibers derived from petroleum, merino wool resists odors and is naturally breathable. What's not to love? The price.

在过去的五年里，我一直在这类文章中对美利奴羊毛赞不绝口。大多数人都已经意识到美利奴羊毛是一种超级纤维；它既可持续，又具备相当卓越的性能。美利奴羊毛在华氏95度（约35摄氏度）的高温下依然能让人感到舒适，同时也能在冰点以下的温度中提供保暖。与源自石油的合成纤维不同，美利奴羊毛具有抗异味和天然透气的特性。有什么理由不喜欢它呢？唯一的缺点就是价格。

Merino wool isn't cheap. That's why I love these merino T-shirts from Quince: They're just $42.

美利奴羊毛并不便宜。这就是为什么我喜欢Quince的这些美利奴羊毛T恤：它们仅售42美元。

The Quince All-Season T-shirt is 100 percent extra-fine merino wool with a 165-gsm weave, which is perfect even on the warmest summer days where I live. (Admittedly, I think the hottest it got this summer was 92 Fahrenheit). I like the relaxed fit here as well. It's neither skintight technical nor overly loose and baggy. It fits like a T-shirt is supposed to. That's what Quince does well in a number of categories: offer a solid, no-nonsense, competitively priced merino wool T-shirt that fits well and feels great.

Quince全季T恤采用100%超细美利奴羊毛，织物密度为165克/平方米，即使在我居住地最炎热的夏日里穿着也非常完美。（诚然，我认为今年夏天最高气温达到了华氏92度，约33摄氏度）。我也喜欢它宽松的剪裁。它既不像专业运动装那样紧身，也不会过于松垮。它穿起来就像一件T恤该有的样子。这正是Quince在多个品类中做得出色的地方：提供一件扎实、实用、价格具有竞争力，且合身舒适的美利奴羊毛T恤。

As an added bonus, Quince offers free shipping and free returns for 365 days. I haven't actually tested either—I haven't needed to, my Quince T-shirt is still going strong after two years of use. But there are plenty of customer reviews on Reddit suggesting the company does honor that offer.

作为额外福利，Quince提供免费送货和365天免费退货服务。我实际上并没有测试过这两项服务——因为我不需要，我的Quince T恤在穿了两年后依然完好如初。但在Reddit上有许多客户评论表明，该公司确实履行了这一承诺。

Not sold on the idea of a wool T-shirt? I understand. When I started testing merino, I too had visions of lumberjacks in plaid and itchy sweaters someone's grandma had knitted. None of that is merino wool. Merino sheep have thinner, softer wool, which has evolved to keep them comfortable across a wide range of temperatures and is comfortable to wear next to your skin.

对羊毛T恤的概念还不买账？我理解。当我开始测试美利奴羊毛时，我也曾联想到身穿格子衬衫的伐木工，或是祖母织的扎人的毛衣。但那些都不是美利奴羊毛。美利奴羊的羊毛更细、更软，这种进化使它们能在各种温度下保持舒适，并且贴身穿着也非常舒服。

One wool myth that's not just a myth is care. Merino isn't cotton. Quince's care instructions for the All Season T-shirt say to wash it in cold water and lay flat to dry. The latter is important, as hanging merino wool to dry will stretch it out (because of the water weight). Can you throw it in the dryer? Yes. I have, for testing purposes only. It will shrink, and it will wear it out much, much faster, but you can do it.

关于羊毛的一个并非传言的“传言”是护理问题。美利奴羊毛不是棉。Quince对这款全季T恤的护理说明建议用冷水洗涤并平铺晾干。后者非常重要，因为悬挂晾干会导致美利奴羊毛拉伸变形（由于水分的重量）。可以把它扔进烘干机吗？可以。我为了测试目的试过。它会缩水，而且会磨损得非常非常快，但你确实可以这样做。
