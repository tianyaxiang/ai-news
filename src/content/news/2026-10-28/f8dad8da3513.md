Title: From Writing Advice to Manuscript Governance: A Constrained-Transformation Framework for Academic Revision

标题：从写作建议到手稿治理：学术修订的约束转换框架

Abstract: Academic revision presents a governance problem when legitimate writing objectives conflict. Writing-process and social-science research show that revision is recursive and that wording carries epistemic and disciplinary commitments. Those literatures identify important constraints but do not settle which objective should prevail when concision, outlet form or stylistic variation conflicts with evidence and claim boundaries.

摘要：当合理的写作目标发生冲突时，学术修订便构成了一个治理问题。写作过程研究和社会科学研究表明，修订是一个递归过程，且措辞承载着认识论和学科承诺。这些文献确定了重要的约束条件，但并未解决当简洁性、发表形式或文体变化与证据及主张边界发生冲突时，应优先考虑哪一个目标的问题。

This paper models revision as an admissibility problem under ordered constraints. Task-relative invariants, contextual constraints and mutable presentation choices define the admissible space; a claim-preservation contract links evidence and judgement to an authorised claim boundary. Four governance propositions specify preservation, precedence, context and sufficiency.

本文将修订建模为有序约束下的可采纳性问题。任务相关不变量、上下文约束和可变呈现选择定义了可采纳空间；主张保留契约将证据和判断与授权的主张边界联系起来。四项治理主张明确了保留、优先、上下文和充分性。

The Academic Writing Filter implements the model by screening candidate revisions; it is not a universal definition or classifier of good academic writing. The contribution is a technology-independent mechanism for changing presentation without silently changing the research argument.

“学术写作过滤器”通过筛选候选修订版本来实现该模型；它并非对优秀学术写作的通用定义或分类器。其贡献在于提供了一种与技术无关的机制，能够在不暗中改变研究论点的前提下更改呈现方式。
