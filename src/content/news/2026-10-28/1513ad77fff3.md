### How to Plan an HRMS and Payroll Rollout for a 50-Person Company in India

将人力资源和薪酬管理从电子表格中迁移出来，是成长型公司可以进行的高回报软件项目之一。这也是最容易出错的项目之一，因为薪酬错误会直接影响员工的银行账户。以下是一个适用于约 30 到 150 名员工团队的实用推广计划。

1. Fix your master data first Before choosing any tool, clean up the basics: employee codes, joining dates, departments, reporting managers, bank details, PAN, UAN and ESIC numbers. Most payroll errors after go-live come from bad master data, not bad software.

1. 首先整理主数据。在选择任何工具之前，请先清理基础信息：员工编号、入职日期、部门、汇报经理、银行详细信息、PAN（永久账号）、UAN（通用账号）和 ESIC（员工国家保险公司）号码。上线后的大多数薪酬错误都源于主数据质量差，而非软件本身的问题。

2. Write down your salary structure List every component you pay: basic, HRA, special allowance, conveyance, bonus, overtime, incentives, and every deduction (PF, ESI, professional tax, TDS, loans, advances). Note which components are fixed and which change monthly. This document becomes the spec for configuration.

2. 写下你的薪酬结构。列出你支付的每一个组成部分：基本工资、房屋租金补贴 (HRA)、特殊津贴、交通费、奖金、加班费、激励奖金，以及每一项扣除额（公积金 PF、员工国家保险 ESI、专业税、源头扣缴税 TDS、贷款、预付款）。注明哪些部分是固定的，哪些是每月变动的。这份文档将成为配置的规范。

3. Decide what attendance feeds payroll Will attendance come from biometric devices, a mobile app with geo-tagging, or manager approval? Decide leave types, carry-forward rules and late-mark policies now. Payroll is only as accurate as the attendance data behind it.

3. 确定哪些考勤数据会影响薪酬。考勤是来自生物识别设备、带有地理标记的移动应用程序，还是经理审批？现在就决定请假类型、结转规则和迟到扣分政策。薪酬的准确性完全取决于其背后的考勤数据。

4. Run parallel payroll for two months Never switch over in one go. Run the new system alongside your old sheet for at least two cycles and compare every payslip. Investigate every difference, even small ones. When two months match, you can retire the spreadsheet with confidence.

4. 进行两个月的并行薪酬核算。切勿一次性切换。将新系统与旧的电子表格并行运行至少两个周期，并对比每一份工资单。调查每一个差异，即使是很小的差异。当两个月的数据完全吻合时，你就可以放心地弃用电子表格了。

5. Give employees self-service early Payslips, leave balances, tax declarations and reimbursement claims in a self-service portal remove a huge share of HR's daily questions. Launch it in the first month so people get used to it.

5. 尽早为员工提供自助服务。在自助服务门户中提供工资单、假期余额、税务申报和报销申请，可以减少人力资源部门日常工作中很大一部分的咨询量。在第一个月就推出该功能，以便员工能够适应。

6. Plan statutory reports Make sure the system produces PF ECR files, ESI contribution data, professional tax summaries and Form 16 without manual edits. Test them before the first filing date, not on it.

6. 规划法定报告。确保系统能够生成公积金 ECR 文件、ESI 缴费数据、专业税汇总和 16 号表格，且无需手动编辑。在首次申报日期之前进行测试，而不是在当天才测试。

Off-the-shelf or custom? Ready-made HRMS products work well when your policies are standard. If you have multiple locations, shift-based pay, piece-rate workers or unusual incentive rules, a custom or customised HRMS usually pays for itself quickly. Teams like DipanshuTech build HRMS and payroll systems around a company's actual policies rather than forcing the policies to fit the software. Whatever you choose, the order matters: clean data, documented rules, parallel runs, then go-live.
