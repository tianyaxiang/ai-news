The Dallas-based investment firm Disruptive, whose past bets include Palantir, Airbnb, and Spotify, announced a $10 billion fund to back at least 10 late-stage companies, with checks of up to $1 billion, it said Tuesday. It’s one of the biggest fundraises of the year so far, aiming to back (what else?!) the capital-intensive fields of AI and defense.

总部位于达拉斯的投资公司 Disruptive 周二宣布，已筹集 100 亿美元基金，用于支持至少 10 家后期阶段的公司，单笔投资额最高可达 10 亿美元。该公司此前的投资项目包括 Palantir、Airbnb 和 Spotify。这是今年迄今为止规模最大的融资之一，旨在支持（还能是什么呢？！）资本密集型的人工智能和国防领域。

The Wall Street Journal first reported earlier this month that the firm was seeking to raise the fund and by then had already secured at least $7.5 billion in capital commitments. The firm was founded in 2012 by Alex Davis, grandson of billionaire oil magnate Marvin Davis.

《华尔街日报》本月初率先报道称，该公司正在寻求筹集该基金，当时已获得至少 75 亿美元的资本承诺。该公司由亿万富翁石油大亨马文·戴维斯（Marvin Davis）的孙子亚历克斯·戴维斯（Alex Davis）于 2012 年创立。

This is technically the firm’s first blind-pool fund, meaning investors commit money without knowing which companies it will back. Until now, the firm raised money through deal-by-deal special purpose vehicles. Disruptive has nonetheless made headlines in recent years for pouring money into companies like chip startup Groq and the AI voice-generation startup ElevenLabs.

从技术上讲，这是该公司首个盲池基金，意味着投资者在投入资金时并不知道基金将支持哪些公司。在此之前，该公司通过逐笔交易的特殊目的载体（SPV）筹集资金。尽管如此，Disruptive 近年来因向芯片初创公司 Groq 和人工智能语音生成初创公司 ElevenLabs 等企业注资而频频登上头条。

Bloomberg reported that the firm told LPs it has returned more than $6 billion on the roughly $1.9 billion it has put into startups. According to the Dallas Business Journal, the new vehicle is the largest venture fund raised in Texas history.

据彭博社报道，该公司向有限合伙人（LP）表示，其在初创企业上投入的约 19 亿美元资金已获得超过 60 亿美元的回报。据《达拉斯商业周刊》报道，该新基金是德克萨斯州历史上筹集规模最大的风险投资基金。
