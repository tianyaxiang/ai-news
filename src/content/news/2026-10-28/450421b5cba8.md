# Meta Employees Denounce Interference in Brazilian Elections

Employees at Meta, the parent company of Facebook, Instagram, and WhatsApp, have expressed concern over what they describe as “obvious” and “biased” favoritism toward members of the Bolsonaro family during Brazilian elections.

Meta（Facebook、Instagram 和 WhatsApp 的母公司）的员工对他们所描述的在巴西选举期间对博索纳罗家族成员“明显”且“有偏见”的优待表示担忧。

In a statement, the company’s workers held Meta’s leadership responsible for decisions that, they argue, could undermine the credibility of the democratic process.

在一份声明中，该公司员工认为 Meta 的领导层应对相关决策负责，他们指出这些决策可能会破坏民主进程的公信力。

The statement was signed by members of the tech sector branch of the United Kingdom’s Communication Workers Union, CWU. In the text, the employees voice concern regarding recent revelations about the company’s management and point to the risk of interference in the elections of other nations.

该声明由英国通信工人工会（CWU）技术部门的分支成员签署。在文中，员工们表达了对近期有关公司管理层披露信息的担忧，并指出了干预其他国家选举的风险。

“Since 2016, the political leanings of Meta executives and company leaders has been no secret,” the document states.

文件中写道：“自 2016 年以来，Meta 高管和公司领导层的政治倾向已不是什么秘密。”

The employees emphasize, however, that they consider this systematic and user-perceptible application of political favoritism to be unprecedented.

然而，员工们强调，他们认为这种系统性且用户可察觉的政治偏袒行为是前所未有的。

“But this is the first time to our knowledge that favours made to politicians such as the Bolsonaros have been programmatically made user-facing to such an obvious and biased extent,” the statement reads.

声明写道：“但据我们所知，这是第一次通过程序化手段，将给予博索纳罗等政客的优待以如此明显和偏颇的方式呈现给用户。”

The text also expresses concern that the company’s actions could damage public trust in democratic institutions and in digital platforms themselves.

文中还表达了担忧，认为公司的行为可能会损害公众对民主机构以及数字平台本身的信任。

“The last thing we want is to sit back and watch our employer interfering in the democratic processes of countries like Brazil,” the employees say.

员工们表示：“我们最不希望看到的就是袖手旁观，看着我们的雇主干预像巴西这样的国家的民主进程。”

According to the group, a loss of credibility in elections and politicians could also erode trust in the technologies developed by the company. The employees warn that this scenario sets a dangerous precedent for the future.

该团体认为，选举和政客公信力的丧失也可能削弱人们对该公司所开发技术的信任。员工们警告称，这种情况为未来开创了一个危险的先例。

“We Meta employees want to build technologies and platforms that millions of people use and enjoy,” the text states. “In undermining the credibility of the elections and politicians, it also unfairly undermines the credibility of what we build, and sets an incredibly dangerous precedent for the future.”

文中写道：“我们 Meta 员工希望构建数以百万计的人们所使用和喜爱的技术与平台。在破坏选举和政客公信力的同时，这也变相地破坏了我们所构建事物的公信力，并为未来开创了一个极其危险的先例。”

At the end of the statement, the employees call on the company to respect democratic principles.

在声明的最后，员工们呼吁公司尊重民主原则。

“We call for fairness and democracy to be respected,” the document concludes.

文件总结道：“我们呼吁尊重公平与民主。”

On Saturday, October 24, a protest against Meta’s interventions in the Brazilian elections took place in front of the company’s headquarters in King’s Cross, central London. The demonstration was organized by the “Alliance for Democracy and Brazilian Sovereignty,” a collective of movements bringing together Brazilians in England.

10 月 24 日星期六，一场抗议 Meta 干预巴西选举的示威活动在位于伦敦市中心国王十字区的公司总部前举行。此次示威由“民主与巴西主权联盟”组织，这是一个汇集了在英巴西人的运动集合体。

As employees of Meta, we are disturbed by the revelations of the past few days and weeks regarding our management.

作为 Meta 的员工，我们对过去几天和几周内有关我们管理层的披露信息感到不安。

Since 2016, the political leanings of Meta executives and company leaders has been no secret.

自 2016 年以来，Meta 高管和公司领导层的政治倾向已不是什么秘密。

But this is the first time to our knowledge that favours made to politicians such as the Bolsonaros have been programmatically made user-facing to such an obvious and biased extent.

但据我们所知，这是第一次通过程序化手段，将给予博索纳罗等政客的优待以如此明显和偏颇的方式呈现给用户。

We Meta employees want to build technologies and platforms that millions of people use and enjoy.

我们 Meta 员工希望构建数以百万计的人们所使用和喜爱的技术与平台。

The last thing we want is to sit back and watch our employer interfering in the democratic processes of countries like Brazil.

我们最不希望看到的就是袖手旁观，看着我们的雇主干预像巴西这样的国家的民主进程。

In undermining the credibility of elections and politicians, it also unfairly undermines the credibility of what we build, and sets an incredibly dangerous precedent for the future.

在破坏选举和政客公信力的同时，这也变相地破坏了我们所构建事物的公信力，并为未来开创了一个极其危险的先例。

We call for fairness and democracy to be respected.

我们呼吁尊重公平与民主。

Will they do the same in America though? Will Twitter employees rise against their feudal lord and speak on his internal action?

不过，他们会在美国也这样做吗？Twitter 的员工会反抗他们的封建领主，并就其内部行动发声吗？

Yes, I do believe the employees who have for years worked to push leftist propaganda and filter out conservative content to tell me now they have supposedly seen manipulation in favor of a non-left-wing politician.

是的，我确实相信那些多年来致力于推动左翼宣传并过滤保守派内容的员工，现在却告诉我他们声称看到了有利于非左翼政客的操纵行为。
