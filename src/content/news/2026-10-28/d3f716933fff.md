FAQ What is WHIP? WHIP is an open standard for sending live video to a service using WebRTC. Your provider gives you a server URL and a stream key, called a bearer token. Before to join a conference call each client had to run custom code. That means FFmpeg/OBS would never be able to join.

常见问题解答：什么是 WHIP？WHIP 是一种使用 WebRTC 向服务发送实时视频的开放标准。你的提供商会给你一个服务器 URL 和一个流密钥，称为承载令牌（bearer token）。在加入电话会议之前，每个客户端都必须运行自定义代码。这意味着 FFmpeg/OBS 永远无法加入。

Why is my screenshare blurry? A lack of control. Your browser is picking a bitrate, codec or resolution that doesn't match what you need. How are OBS and FFmpeg different from browser screensharing? OBS and FFmpeg let you choose your resolution, frame rate, bitrate, and codec. You can tune those settings for the content you're sharing and the hardware and connection you have.

为什么我的屏幕共享很模糊？因为缺乏控制。你的浏览器选择的比特率、编解码器或分辨率与你的需求不符。OBS 和 FFmpeg 与浏览器屏幕共享有何不同？OBS 和 FFmpeg 让你能够选择分辨率、帧率、比特率和编解码器。你可以根据所共享的内容以及你拥有的硬件和连接来调整这些设置。
