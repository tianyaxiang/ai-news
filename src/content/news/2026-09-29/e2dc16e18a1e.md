AI is making answers cheaper than ever before. In a few seconds, we can obtain an explanation, a snippet of code, a schedule, or even seemingly definitive life advice. However, what is truly scarce has not disappeared: knowing which questions are worth asking, discerning which evidence is credible, turning advice into real work, and taking responsibility for the final judgment.
AI 正在让答案变得前所未有地廉价。几秒钟里，我们可以得到一份解释、一段代码、一张计划表，甚至一个看似笃定的人生建议。可真正稀缺的东西并没有因此消失：知道什么问题值得追问，辨别什么证据可以相信，把建议变成真实作品，并为最后的判断承担责任。

This is a lifelong learning guide for ordinary people. It does not require you to be a genius, an expert, or a person of iron will, nor does it promise that any single tool will change your destiny. Its goal is to accompany you in an era of accelerating change, learning again and again: facing unfamiliar problems, leveraging AI while retaining your own judgment, completing a real task, and carrying that experience to the next stage of your journey.
这是一份面向普通人的终身学习指南。它不要求你先成为天才、专家或意志坚定的人，也不许诺某个工具会替你改变命运。它想做的，是陪你在变化加速的时代里，一次次重新学习：面对陌生问题，借助 AI，也保留自己的判断；完成一件真实的事，再把经验带到下一段路。

The project began in 2017 with the "Lipu English Learning Guide." English was once the entire map; today, it is a foundational path on that map. We continue to move outward, entering AI learning, project development, resource-layer entrepreneurship, and life review and recovery. This is not because the past is no longer important, but because all training ultimately points to the same question: As the world keeps changing, can I still continue to learn, continue to create, and continue to be a participant in my own life?
项目在 2017 年从《离谱的英语学习指南》开始。英语曾是全部地图，如今它成为地图上的一条基础路径。我们继续向外走，进入 AI 学习、项目开发、资源层创业、人生复盘与恢复。不是因为过去不再重要，而是因为所有训练最终都指向同一个问题：当世界不断改变，我还能不能继续学习、继续创造、继续成为生活的参与者？

I am Han Xiankai, also known as Lipu, currently the Chairman of China Token Cloud Computing Co., Ltd. I put the methods here to the test in learning, development, enterprise services, and real life. Identities and business relationships are stated clearly; capabilities, products, and income results only accept evidence left by work, users, costs, and time.
我是韩先凯，也叫离谱，现任中国词元云计算有限公司董事长。我把这里的方法放进学习、开发、企业服务和真实生活中检验。身份与商业关系会被写在明处；能力、产品与收入结果，只接受作品、用户、成本和时间留下的证据。

### The Guide's Core Cycle:
### 这份指南反复练习一个循环：

**Discover Problems → Active Learning → Collaborate with AI → Complete Real Tasks → Save Evidence → Review and Transfer**
**发现问题 → 主动学习 → 与 AI 协作 → 完成真实任务 → 保存证据 → 复盘迁移**

It also consistently distinguishes between three types of information:
它也始终区分三种信息：

1. **Research Conclusions:** Provide sources and explain the scope of the evidence.
   **研究结论：** 提供来源，并说明证据能覆盖到哪里；
2. **Personal Experience:** Retain the warmth of the story; do not pass off a single life path as a universal law.
   **个人经验：** 保留故事的温度，不把一条人生路径冒充成普遍规律；
3. **Hypotheses to be Verified:** Allow imagination into the discussion, but it must be tested by the next round of action.
   **待验证假设：** 允许想象进入讨论，但必须交给下一轮行动检验。

### 01 Building Foundations
### 01 建立基础

Put problems, language, and daily actions on the same map. The lifelong learning system starts from real problems, current baselines, and minimal tasks, allowing every round of learning to be continued, retested, and transferred.
先把问题、语言和每日动作放到同一张地图上。终身学习系统从真实问题、当前基线和最小任务出发，让每轮学习都能被继续、复测与迁移。

*   **Foundational Capability (English):** Use English to connect with global knowledge, technical documentation, and international AI tools. Approach cross-cultural collaboration through intelligibility rather than accent similarity.
    **基础能力：英语：** 用英语连接全球知识、技术文档与国际 AI 工具，以可理解度而不是口音相似度进入跨文化协作。
*   **Grammar & Expression:** Do not start by memorizing complete rules. Start by ensuring time, responsibility, conditions, and certainty are clearly understood. Use a high-impact structure for a 14-day retest.
    **语法基础与真实表达：** 不从背完整规则开始，而从时间、责任、条件与确定性是否被听清开始，用一项高影响结构完成十四天复测。
*   **Writing & Asynchronous Delivery:** Keep unassisted drafts, fact sources, and reasons for revisions so that emails, reports, decisions, and handovers remain usable even after the author is offline.
    **写作与异步交付：** 保留无辅助初稿、事实来源和修改理由，让邮件、报告、决定与交接在作者离线后仍然可用。

---

### 02 Amplifying Capabilities with Tools
### 02 借工具放大能力

Let AI accelerate questioning and verification, but keep judgment, testing, and responsibility in human hands.
让 AI 加速提问和验证，但把判断、测试与责任留在人手中。

*   **Using AI to Learn Everything:** Let AI help with questioning, research, and feedback, while keeping fact-checking and final judgment in human hands.
    **使用 AI 学习一切：** 让 AI 帮助提问、研究和反馈，同时把事实核验与最终判断留在人手中。
*   **AI Projects & Resource-Layer Entrepreneurship:** Move from requirements, prototypes, code, and testing to model integration, governance, enterprise delivery, and commercial validation.
    **AI 项目与资源层创业：** 从需求、原型、代码和测试走向模型接入、治理、企业交付与商业验证。

---

### 03 Entering Real Life
### 03 进入真实生活

Bring learning into work, family, relationships, and the reality the author is currently undertaking.
把学习带进工作、家庭、关系和作者正在承担的现实。

*   **Overseas Job Hunting & Remote Collaboration:** Break down job descriptions into recruitment communication, project specifications, follow-up questions, and asynchronous writing. Use real samples to judge what is missing in an interview.
    **海外求职与远程协作：** 把岗位描述拆成招聘沟通、项目说明、陌生追问和异步写作，用真实样本判断这一场面试还缺什么。
*   **Family & Student Learning:** Let children participate in defining goals; let parents protect the environment, privacy, and safety. Use 14-day evidence to replace monitoring, doing work for them, and added anxiety.
    **家庭与中学生学习：** 让孩子参与定义目标，让家长保护环境、隐私与安全，用十四天证据替代监控、代做和焦虑加码。
*   **Life Review & Recovery:** Acknowledge failure and costs; rebuild judgment, order, and action after disorder.
    **人生复盘与恢复：** 承认失败和代价，在失序之后重新建立判断、秩序与行动。

### If you only do one thing today:
### 如果今天只做一件事：

Create a **Learning Status** document. Write down the real problem you are facing, the current evidence, and the next minimal task, then complete it. Do not wait for the road to become wide before setting off; many paths only reveal themselves after your feet have landed.
创建一份 **学习状态**，写下正在面对的真实问题、当前证据和下一项最小任务，然后完成它。不要等道路变得宽阔才出发；很多路，正是在脚落下之后才显现。
