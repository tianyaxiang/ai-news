Earlier this year, we partnered with XPRIZE and Range Media Partners to launch the Future Vision XPRIZE, a global competition calling for films that envision a hopeful, technology-enabled future.
今年早些时候，我们与 XPRIZE 和 Range Media Partners 合作发起了“Future Vision XPRIZE”，这是一项全球性竞赛，旨在征集能够展望充满希望、由技术赋能的未来的电影作品。

Last week in Los Angeles, independent filmmaker Jeff Synthesized was named the grand prize winner for The Gifted.
上周在洛杉矶，独立电影制作人 Jeff Synthesized 凭借《The Gifted》荣获大奖。

Inspired by a deeply personal story, the film centers on an 11-year-old boy who recreates his late mother’s voice and essence out of love and code.
这部电影的灵感源于一个非常私人的故事，讲述了一名 11 岁的男孩出于对母亲的爱，利用代码重现了已故母亲的声音和神韵。

What emerges across the town’s networks quietly cares for his entire community — reflecting the filmmaker’s belief that technology’s greatest promise lies in empathy and human connection.
通过城镇网络呈现出的这一切，默默地关怀着整个社区——这反映了电影制作人的信念：技术最大的承诺在于共情与人际连接。

Chosen from more than 2,500 entries worldwide, the solo-developed project receives $100,000 alongside $2.5 million in feature production funding.
该项目从全球 2500 多部参赛作品中脱颖而出，作为独立开发的作品，它获得了 10 万美元奖金以及 250 万美元的剧情片制作资金。

Through Google’s 100 ZEROS initiative, we are partnering with Range Media Partners to help bring this story to the big screen.
通过 Google 的“100 ZEROS”计划，我们正与 Range Media Partners 合作，助力将这个故事搬上大银幕。
