# Bose starts adding Auracast to its headphones
# Bose 开始为其耳机添加 Auracast 支持

The QuietComfort Ultra Headphones Gen 2 are the first Bose headphones to support this underrated Bluetooth tech.
QuietComfort Ultra Headphones Gen 2 是首款支持这一被低估的蓝牙技术的 Bose 耳机。

A new firmware update for the $449 Bose QuietComfort Ultra Headphones Gen 2 adds support for Bluetooth LE Audio and Auracast as beta features. The flagship Ultra headphones are the first from Bose to support the audio sharing technology, which the company plans to bring to more devices soon.
针对售价 449 美元的 Bose QuietComfort Ultra Headphones Gen 2 的一项新固件更新，为其添加了作为测试功能的蓝牙 LE Audio 和 Auracast 支持。这款旗舰级 Ultra 耳机是 Bose 首款支持该音频共享技术的产品，公司计划在不久后将其推广至更多设备。

Only a few major manufacturers include Auracast, and only on select headphones or earbuds — including Sony, JBL headphones, Samsung Galaxy Buds, and Sennheiser, which also released an Auracast transmitter this year. Some smaller companies, like EarFun, have added support. But Bose is one of the most recognizable names in headphones, so the fact that it’s finally jumping in is big news for Auracast.
目前仅有少数几家主要制造商在部分耳机或耳塞产品中加入了 Auracast 功能，包括索尼、JBL、三星 Galaxy Buds 以及今年还发布了 Auracast 发射器的森海塞尔。一些小型公司（如 EarFun）也已添加了支持。但 Bose 是耳机领域最具知名度的品牌之一，因此它终于加入这一阵营对 Auracast 来说是一个重大的利好消息。

Auracast was officially announced back in 2022, but the adoption process has been slow. The technology allows a device to broadcast Bluetooth audio to any number of supported headphones or speakers at the same time. This could be for gate announcements while waiting at the airport, a performance at a local venue, or a college lecture. You can even use Auracast at home to watch TV while kids are asleep or for extra hearing assistance — some Samsung and LG TVs have the functionality buried in their menus.
Auracast 于 2022 年正式发布，但普及过程一直较为缓慢。该技术允许设备同时向任意数量的支持该功能的耳机或扬声器广播蓝牙音频。这可以应用于机场候机时的登机口广播、当地场馆的演出或大学讲座。你甚至可以在家中使用 Auracast 在孩子睡觉时观看电视，或将其作为额外的听力辅助——一些三星和 LG 电视的菜单中已经内置了此功能。

The firmware update, labeled 10.12.12, has been quietly rolling out the past few weeks. Alongside Bluetooth LE and Auracast, it adds improved audio over wired USB connections, including balance for gaming audio and chat, and better compatibility for web conferencing apps.
版本号为 10.12.12 的固件更新在过去几周内已悄然推送。除了蓝牙 LE 和 Auracast 外，它还改进了通过有线 USB 连接的音频表现，包括游戏音频与语音聊天的平衡调节，以及对网络会议应用更好的兼容性。

Bose spokesperson Tim Williams told The Verge via email that the QC Headphones (Gen 2), which I recently reviewed, should get the beta firmware next.
Bose 发言人 Tim Williams 通过电子邮件告诉 The Verge，我最近评测过的 QC Headphones (Gen 2) 预计将紧随其后获得该测试版固件。
