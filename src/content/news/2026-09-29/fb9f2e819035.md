### One more thing: The gig workers who are training humanoid robots at home
### 还有一件事：在家训练人形机器人的零工劳动者

When Zeus, a medical student in Nigeria, returns to his apartment from a long day at the hospital, he straps his iPhone to his forehead and records himself doing chores. Zeus is a data recorder for Micro1, which sells the data he collects to robotics firms. As these companies race to build humanoids, videos from workers like Zeus have become the hottest new way to train them. Micro1 has hired thousands of them in more than 50 countries, including India, Nigeria, and Argentina. The jobs pay well locally, but raise thorny questions around privacy and informed consent. The work can be challenging—and weird. Read the full story. ——Michelle Kim

当尼日利亚的医学生 Zeus 在医院忙碌一天回到公寓后，他会将 iPhone 绑在额头上，录制自己做家务的过程。Zeus 是 Micro1 的数据记录员，该公司将他收集的数据出售给机器人公司。随着这些公司竞相开发人形机器人，像 Zeus 这样的劳动者拍摄的视频已成为训练机器人的最新热门方式。Micro1 已在包括印度、尼日利亚和阿根廷在内的 50 多个国家雇佣了数千名此类员工。这些工作在当地薪酬不错，但引发了关于隐私和知情同意的棘手问题。这项工作既充满挑战，又显得有些怪异。阅读完整报道。——Michelle Kim
