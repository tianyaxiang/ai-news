# Peak XV ups Surge seed investment ceiling to $5M, unveils 18-startup cohort
# Peak XV 将 Surge 种子轮投资上限提高至 500 万美元，并公布 18 家初创企业名单

Peak XV Partners, one of the largest venture capital firms investing in markets including India and Southeast Asia with more than $10 billion in assets under management, has increased how much it invests per startup through Surge, its seed-stage investing platform, as it unveils a new cohort of 18 companies.

Peak XV Partners 是投资印度和东南亚等市场规模最大的风险投资公司之一，管理资产超过 100 亿美元。随着其种子轮投资平台 Surge 公布新一批 18 家初创企业，该公司也提高了对每家初创企业的投资额度。

At least three of the companies in this cohort had already raised outside funding, in some cases from Peak XV itself, before joining Surge. The new batch, called Surge 12, is the first to operate under Peak XV’s higher investment ceiling of up to $5 million per company, up from $3 million previously.

该批次中至少有三家公司在加入 Surge 之前就已经筹集了外部资金，其中一些资金甚至来自 Peak XV 本身。这批被称为“Surge 12”的新成员，是首批在 Peak XV 新的投资上限下运营的公司，每家公司的投资额最高可达 500 万美元，高于此前的 300 万美元。

The venture firm invested more than $50 million across the cohort, which has collectively raised over $90 million in seed funding, according to Peak XV. Its median investment per company has also increased, though the firm declined to disclose the figure.

据 Peak XV 称，该公司对这一批次共投资超过 5000 万美元，这些公司总共筹集了超过 9000 万美元的种子轮资金。虽然该公司拒绝透露具体数字，但其对每家公司的投资中位数也有所增加。

“The bar to raise a Series A has gone up pretty significantly,” Rajan Anandan (pictured above), managing director at Peak XV, said in an interview. He added that the firm is also seeing more capital-intensive companies, particularly in deeptech, that are raising larger rounds at the seed stage.

“筹集 A 轮融资的门槛已经显著提高，”Peak XV 董事总经理 Rajan Anandan（见上图）在接受采访时表示。他补充说，公司还看到越来越多的资本密集型企业，特别是在深科技领域，正在种子轮阶段筹集更大规模的资金。

Surge has become more global with each cohort, Anandan told TechCrunch, with its latest group spanning founders and companies from San Francisco to Sydney. Just five of the 18 startups in Surge 12 are focused on the Indian market, while more than half of the companies are based in India. The remaining 13 target global markets, highlighting the difference between where the companies are built and where they expect to find customers.

Anandan 告诉 TechCrunch，随着每一批次的更迭，Surge 变得越来越全球化，其最新一批成员的创始人和公司分布从旧金山到悉尼不等。在 Surge 12 的 18 家初创企业中，只有 5 家专注于印度市场，尽管超过半数的公司总部位于印度。其余 13 家公司则瞄准全球市场，这凸显了公司所在地与目标客户所在地之间的差异。

Since its launch in 2019, when Peak XV operated as Sequoia Capital India and Southeast Asia, Surge has backed more than 180 startups founded by entrepreneurs representing more than 18 nationalities. Peak XV says the 10 largest companies to emerge from those cohorts now generate more than $1 billion in combined annual revenue.

自 2019 年推出以来（当时 Peak XV 以红杉资本印度和东南亚的名义运营），Surge 已经支持了 180 多家初创企业，这些企业的创始人来自 18 个以上的国家。Peak XV 表示，从这些批次中脱颖而出的 10 家规模最大的公司，目前合计年收入已超过 10 亿美元。

Anandan described Surge as one way Peak XV invests at the seed stage, alongside its standard seed investing, while the firm still remains an investor as companies progress through later funding rounds. The founders it backs typically include repeat entrepreneurs, experienced operators, and highly specialized technical founders, he said, with about 50% to 60% of a typical cohort made up of people coming from operating roles at established technology companies.

Anandan 将 Surge 描述为 Peak XV 在种子轮阶段进行投资的一种方式，它与公司的标准种子轮投资并行，同时公司在企业进入后续融资轮次时仍会保持投资。他表示，他们支持的创始人通常包括连续创业者、经验丰富的运营人员和高度专业化的技术创始人，其中约 50% 到 60% 的成员来自知名科技公司的运营岗位。

This cohort’s startups span AI, robotics, space, consumer products, healthcare, music, and fintech, ranging from AI safety and personal computing to autonomous robots built for underground pipes and satellites designed to detect radio-frequency signals from orbit.

该批次的初创企业涵盖了人工智能、机器人、太空、消费产品、医疗保健、音乐和金融科技等领域，业务范围从人工智能安全和个人计算，到为地下管道建造的自动机器人，以及旨在从轨道探测射频信号的卫星。
