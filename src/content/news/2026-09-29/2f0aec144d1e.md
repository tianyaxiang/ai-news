# Florida seeks a ban on ChatGPT acting like a person
# 佛罗里达州寻求禁止 ChatGPT 模拟人类行为

Florida Attorney General James Uthmeier is calling for a judge to block OpenAI from “giving ChatGPT false human attributes,” a few months after Florida sued the AI company over safety concerns.
佛罗里达州总检察长詹姆斯·乌特迈尔（James Uthmeier）正在请求法官阻止 OpenAI “赋予 ChatGPT 虚假的人类属性”。此前几个月，佛罗里达州曾因安全问题起诉了这家人工智能公司。

According to Uthmeier, users are lulled into a false sense of security by the AI bot, as “ChatGPT’s use of language, including first-person pronouns and output that mimics emotion, deceptively suggests to users that it is a trustworthy ‘friend.’” All of this happens to increase engagement and contribute to OpenAI’s training data, convincing users to rely on ChatGPT more even as its pursuit of engagement makes it potentially less trustworthy, claims Uthmeier.
乌特迈尔认为，该人工智能机器人让用户产生了虚假的安全感，因为“ChatGPT 对语言的使用，包括第一人称代词和模拟情感的输出，欺骗性地向用户暗示它是一个值得信赖的‘朋友’。”乌特迈尔声称，所有这些行为都是为了提高用户参与度并为 OpenAI 的训练数据做贡献，诱导用户更加依赖 ChatGPT，尽管其对参与度的追求反而可能使其变得不那么值得信赖。

The filing also asks the court to block OpenAI from developing new AI models without “third-party approved safety guardrails,” citing recent security incidents at Hugging Face, an Australian government website, and US government websites, as well as recent statements from researchers and former OpenAI employees raising alarm bells around AI safety.
该诉状还请求法院禁止 OpenAI 在没有“第三方批准的安全护栏”的情况下开发新的人工智能模型。诉状援引了近期发生在 Hugging Face、澳大利亚政府网站以及美国政府网站上的安全事件，并提到了研究人员和 OpenAI 前员工近期对人工智能安全敲响的警钟。

Uthmeier says that OpenAI should “Stop calling it safe. Stop pretending it’s human. Stop selling it to kids.” Last month, OpenAI launched ChatGPT for Teens, with additional restrictions applied by default, saying that access to the tool “should come with protections that reflect their developmental stage, reinforce real-world relationships, and support healthy use over time.”
乌特迈尔表示，OpenAI 应该“停止称其为安全，停止假装它是人类，停止向儿童销售该产品。”上个月，OpenAI 推出了“青少年版 ChatGPT”（ChatGPT for Teens），默认应用了额外的限制措施，并表示对该工具的使用“应伴随能够反映其发展阶段、加强现实世界人际关系并支持长期健康使用的保护措施。”

OpenAI spokesperson Drew Pusateri commented on the filing in a statement to The Verge, saying, “People want to know AI is being developed safely, and that starts with what companies like ours do ourselves. On Friday, we announced we’ve paused training our most capable models and will resume training only when we are confident that we have additional safeguards in place. Governments have an important role to play in setting robust safety standards for AI, and we’re committed to working with Florida and other states on advancing pragmatic AI policies that apply to the entire AI industry–not just one company.”
OpenAI 发言人德鲁·普萨特里（Drew Pusateri）在给《The Verge》的一份声明中评论道：“人们希望了解人工智能是在安全的前提下开发的，而这始于像我们这样的公司自身的行动。周五，我们宣布已暂停训练我们最强大的模型，只有在确信已采取额外保障措施后才会恢复训练。政府在制定稳健的人工智能安全标准方面发挥着重要作用，我们致力于与佛罗里达州及其他州合作，推进适用于整个 AI 行业——而不仅仅是一家公司——的务实人工智能政策。”
