# Block Parallelism For Efficient Distributed Long-Context Diffusion Language Model Training
# 用于高效分布式长上下文扩散语言模型训练的块并行（Block Parallelism）技术

Block diffusion language models (BDLMs) combine autoregressive dependencies across blocks with parallel denoising within blocks, but long-context training is constrained by distributed attention communication and activation memory.
块扩散语言模型（BDLMs）结合了跨块的自回归依赖和块内的并行去噪，但长上下文训练受到分布式注意力通信和激活内存的限制。

Conventional context parallelism (CP) shards the combined clean-plus-corrupted sequence by position, communicating shared clean K/V together with block-specific corrupted K/V and their gradients.
传统的上下文并行（CP）按位置对“干净+损坏”的组合序列进行分片，并同时通信共享的干净 K/V 以及特定于块的损坏 K/V 及其梯度。

We observe that the BDLM objective separates over target blocks. We introduce block parallelism (BP), a new distributed parallelism dimension that assigns each corrupted-block computation to one rank.
我们观察到 BDLM 的目标函数在目标块上是可分离的。因此，我们引入了块并行（BP），这是一种新的分布式并行维度，将每个损坏块的计算分配给一个计算节点（rank）。

To scale BP to long contexts, we introduce context-sharded block parallelism (CSBP), which also shards the shared clean sequence across those ranks. CSBP keeps corrupted K/V and gradients local, avoids replicated clean prefixes, and preserves BDLM training semantics.
为了将 BP 扩展到长上下文，我们引入了上下文分片块并行（CSBP），它同时在这些节点间对共享的干净序列进行分片。CSBP 将损坏的 K/V 和梯度保留在本地，避免了干净前缀的重复，并保持了 BDLM 的训练语义。

On 16 H200 GPUs at 256K context, CSBP improves throughput over the best baseline by 1.18-1.45x for supervised fine-tuning and 1.27-1.33x for conversion of autoregressive models to BDLMs, while matching or reducing peak HBM. Full-model speedup reaches 1.61x at 512K.
在 16 张 H200 GPU、256K 上下文长度的配置下，CSBP 在监督微调（SFT）任务中比最佳基准提升了 1.18-1.45 倍的吞吐量，在将自回归模型转换为 BDLM 的任务中提升了 1.27-1.33 倍，同时匹配或降低了峰值 HBM 使用量。在 512K 上下文下，全模型加速比达到了 1.61 倍。

On eight H100 GPUs, CSBP accelerates DFlash2 speculative-decoder training by 2.48x at 512K and 7.59x at 1M. In matched 12-hour DiffusionGemma 26B-A4B SFT runs, CSBP achieves higher pass rates at every trained checkpoint on SWE-bench Verified and Terminal-Bench Lite.
在 8 张 H100 GPU 上，CSBP 将 DFlash2 推测解码器训练速度在 512K 上下文下提升了 2.48 倍，在 1M 上下文下提升了 7.59 倍。在 12 小时的 DiffusionGemma 26B-A4B SFT 对比实验中，CSBP 在 SWE-bench Verified 和 Terminal-Bench Lite 的每个训练检查点上都实现了更高的通过率。
