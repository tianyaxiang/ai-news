# WZPlanner: Safe End-to-End Path Planning for Autonomous Driving in Work Zones
# WZPlanner：面向自动驾驶施工区域的安全端到端路径规划

**Abstract:** Work zones alter lane geometry through temporary traffic controls and closures that may be absent from on-board maps, challenging autonomous vehicle (AV) perception and planning. Generalization is also limited by scarce public datasets with structured geometric supervision. We present WorkZonePlan, a dataset comprising 149K+ synthetic and 5K+ real-world multimodal samples with 3D annotations for lane boundaries, work zone boundaries, and driving trajectory options. It also provides 76 closed-loop CARLA scenarios replayed under three weather conditions, yielding 228 Bench2Drive-format evaluation routes.

**摘要：** 施工区域通过临时交通管制和封闭改变了车道几何结构，而这些信息可能在车载地图中缺失，从而对自动驾驶汽车（AV）的感知和规划构成了挑战。此外，由于缺乏具有结构化几何监督的公开数据集，模型的泛化能力也受到限制。我们提出了 WorkZonePlan 数据集，包含超过 14.9 万个合成样本和 5000 个真实世界多模态样本，并提供了车道边界、施工区域边界和驾驶轨迹选项的 3D 标注。该数据集还提供了 76 个在三种天气条件下重放的闭环 CARLA 场景，生成了 228 条 Bench2Drive 格式的评估路线。

We introduce WAVE (Work-zone-focused AV data generation in Virtual and rEal Environments), a semi-automated pipeline for creating the dataset, and BoundaryFormer (BF), a transformer-based model that jointly predicts lane and work zone boundary polynomials and driving trajectories. BF uses slot attention for boundary prediction. Ablations show that a separate trajectory decoder using boundary slot features substantially improves trajectory prediction over a slot-attention-only approach.

我们引入了 WAVE（虚拟与现实环境中的施工区域自动驾驶数据生成），这是一个用于创建该数据集的半自动化流水线；同时提出了 BoundaryFormer (BF)，这是一种基于 Transformer 的模型，可联合预测车道和施工区域边界多项式以及驾驶轨迹。BF 使用槽注意力机制（slot attention）进行边界预测。消融实验表明，使用边界槽特征的独立轨迹解码器，在轨迹预测性能上显著优于仅使用槽注意力机制的方法。

Building on this finding, BF++ offers Camera and Camera+LiDAR variants with metric ground-plane encoding, typed boundary/trajectory queries, long-range point anchors, image-space curve refinement, and conservative gated LiDAR fusion. On the 211 routes common to all four models at the evaluation freeze, BF++-Camera and BF++-Camera+LiDAR achieve Driving Scores of 63.0 and 64.4, respectively, compared with 59.3 for SimLingo and 26.1 for TransFuser++ (TF++). BF++ is 40 times smaller than SimLingo and more than 10 times smaller than TF++, while achieving higher Driving Scores. These results support jointly predicting lane boundaries, work zone boundaries, and driving trajectories as a promising direction toward safer AV operation in work zones.

基于这一发现，BF++ 提供了摄像头版和摄像头+激光雷达版，具备度量地平面编码、类型化边界/轨迹查询、长距离点锚点、图像空间曲线细化以及保守门控激光雷达融合等特性。在评估冻结时所有四个模型共有的 211 条路线上，BF++-Camera 和 BF++-Camera+LiDAR 的驾驶得分分别为 63.0 和 64.4，而 SimLingo 为 59.3，TransFuser++ (TF++) 为 26.1。BF++ 的体积比 SimLingo 小 40 倍，比 TF++ 小 10 倍以上，同时实现了更高的驾驶得分。这些结果证明，联合预测车道边界、施工区域边界和驾驶轨迹是实现更安全的自动驾驶施工区域作业的一个有前景的方向。
