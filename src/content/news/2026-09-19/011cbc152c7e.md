# LinePilot Digitizer: Line-Plot Recovery with Manual and Automatic Calibration
# LinePilot Digitizer：支持手动与自动校准的折线图数据恢复工具

**Abstract:** Recovering numerical series from line plots requires accurate axis calibration and reliable curve extraction. We present LinePilot Digitizer (LinePilot), which combines continuous color-based curve recovery with three calibration modes: LinePilot (standard), LinePilot (enhanced), and LinePilot (OCR).

**摘要：** 从折线图中恢复数值序列需要精确的坐标轴校准和可靠的曲线提取。我们提出了 LinePilot Digitizer (LinePilot)，它将基于连续色彩的曲线恢复技术与三种校准模式相结合：LinePilot（标准版）、LinePilot（增强版）和 LinePilot（OCR 版）。

We also introduce DigitizerBench, the first dedicated benchmark for systematically evaluating digitizer performance, using an orthogonal design spanning signal, rendering, and plot-structure factors with complementary automatic and human-guided evaluations.

我们还推出了 DigitizerBench，这是首个专门用于系统评估数字化工具性能的基准测试集。它采用了正交设计，涵盖了信号、渲染和图表结构等因素，并结合了自动评估与人工引导评估。

We evaluate performance using failure-penalized capped normalized root-mean-square error (FPC-NRMSE), which assigns unit loss to missing, unusable, or catastrophically inaccurate outputs.

我们使用“故障惩罚截断归一化均方根误差”（FPC-NRMSE）来评估性能，该指标会对缺失、不可用或出现严重偏差的输出结果赋予单位损失值。

On DigitizerBench-Full, LinePilot (OCR) achieves the lowest mean FPC-NRMSE (0.672) and highest trusted usability (38.2%) among the tested automatic pipelines. On DigitizerBench-Lite, LinePilot (enhanced) achieves the lowest mean FPC-NRMSE (0.081), 100% output success, and highest trusted usability (93.3%).

在 DigitizerBench-Full 测试集上，LinePilot (OCR) 在所有参与测试的自动化流程中实现了最低的平均 FPC-NRMSE (0.672) 和最高的信任可用性 (38.2%)。在 DigitizerBench-Lite 测试集上，LinePilot (增强版) 实现了最低的平均 FPC-NRMSE (0.081)、100% 的输出成功率以及最高的信任可用性 (93.3%)。

The orthogonal benchmark design further enables factor analysis to identify the factors that most significantly affect digitizer performance. Together, the three calibration modes provide a practical trade-off between automation, user control, and accuracy within a shared curve-recovery workflow.

这种正交基准设计进一步支持了因子分析，从而识别出对数字化工具性能影响最显著的因素。这三种校准模式共同提供了一种实用的平衡方案，在统一的曲线恢复工作流中兼顾了自动化程度、用户控制权和准确性。
