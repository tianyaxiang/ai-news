# How to Measure Whether AI Search Engines Cite Your Website
# 如何衡量 AI 搜索引擎是否引用了您的网站

If your organic clicks are falling, the missing layer may be the answer page that now appears before your link. GEO and AEO measure whether ChatGPT, Perplexity, Gemini, Claude, and AI Overviews cite your site, and whether their answers support or weaken your position.
如果您的自然搜索点击量正在下降，缺失的一环可能是现在出现在您链接之前的“答案页面”。GEO（生成式引擎优化）和 AEO（答案引擎优化）旨在衡量 ChatGPT、Perplexity、Gemini、Claude 和 AI Overviews 是否引用了您的网站，以及它们的回答是支持还是削弱了您的立场。

Search visibility now has another layer. Traditional SEO asks whether a page can be found, ranked, and clicked. Generative search adds a different question: when a user asks for an answer, does the system select your page as a source? A citation is not the same as a ranking. An AI answer can mention your brand without linking to your site, link to a competitor, summarize your offer inaccurately, or omit your page entirely.
搜索可见性现在有了新的维度。传统的 SEO 关注页面是否能被找到、排名和点击。而生成式搜索提出了一个不同的问题：当用户寻求答案时，系统是否会将您的页面选为来源？“引用”与“排名”并不等同。AI 的回答可能会提及您的品牌但不链接到您的网站，可能会链接到竞争对手，可能会不准确地总结您的产品，或者完全忽略您的页面。

For a founder or marketing leader, the practical issue is not whether the technology is interesting. It is whether potential customers are forming their opinion from an answer that includes your company. GEO, or generative engine optimization, focuses on being selected and represented by AI systems. AEO, or answer engine optimization, focuses on making the answer useful, clear, and easy to reuse. Both depend partly on familiar SEO signals such as content quality, authority, structure, and technical accessibility. They also depend on how well your claims match the wording and intent of natural-language questions.
对于创始人或营销负责人来说，实际问题不在于这项技术是否有趣，而在于潜在客户是否正通过包含您公司的回答来形成他们的观点。GEO（生成式引擎优化）专注于被 AI 系统选中并呈现；AEO（答案引擎优化）则专注于使回答变得有用、清晰且易于复用。两者在一定程度上都依赖于熟悉的 SEO 信号，如内容质量、权威性、结构和技术可访问性。它们还取决于您的主张与自然语言问题的措辞和意图匹配程度。

### What a useful GEO check measures
### 有效的 GEO 检测衡量什么

A meaningful check should use the same query across several AI answer surfaces. In the ZenRadar survey, the five surfaces were ChatGPT, Perplexity, Claude, Gemini, and AI Overviews. They do not behave identically, so one result cannot stand in for all of them. For each run, record the query, timestamp, locale, market, full answer, cited URLs, competitor mentions, and important omissions.
一次有意义的检测应该在多个 AI 答案界面上使用相同的查询。在 ZenRadar 的调查中，这五个界面分别是 ChatGPT、Perplexity、Claude、Gemini 和 AI Overviews。它们的表现并不完全相同，因此一个结果不能代表全部。对于每次运行，请记录查询内容、时间戳、地区、市场、完整回答、引用的 URL、竞争对手提及情况以及重要的遗漏信息。

A citation may be positive, neutral, or based on a claim that your own page does not support. A page can also be cited for a feature that is no longer available. This is why a screenshot is not enough. Two answers can look similar while sending users to different pages. A repeatable log makes it possible to compare changes over time and to connect a change in citations with a content update, a product change, or a competitor campaign.
引用可能是正面的、中立的，也可能基于您的页面本身并不支持的说法。一个页面也可能因为某个不再可用的功能而被引用，这就是为什么仅靠截图是不够的。两个看起来相似的回答可能会将用户引导至不同的页面。一份可重复的日志能够对比随时间发生的变化，并将引用的变化与内容更新、产品变更或竞争对手的活动联系起来。

### Why pricing changes so much
### 为什么定价差异如此之大

The public entry prices in the ZenRadar survey illustrate the difference between a dedicated GEO product and a module added to an existing SEO suite. Otterly.ai was listed from $29 per month, ZenRadar from 49 to 199 euros, and Meteoria from 75 euros. SE Ranking AI Monitoring was listed at 79 euros, Ahrefs Brand Radar at 119 euros, and Semrush AI Toolkit at $139.95. The module prices are additional to the main SEO subscription. Those numbers are a snapshot, not a permanent quote.
ZenRadar 调查中公开的入门价格展示了专用 GEO 产品与现有 SEO 套件中附加模块之间的区别。Otterly.ai 起价为每月 29 美元，ZenRadar 为 49 至 199 欧元，Meteoria 为 75 欧元。SE Ranking AI Monitoring 标价 79 欧元，Ahrefs Brand Radar 为 119 欧元，Semrush AI Toolkit 为 139.95 美元。这些模块价格是在主要 SEO 订阅费用之外额外收取的。这些数字只是一个快照，而非永久报价。

The important question is what the price buys. Four factors usually matter most:
重要的问题是这些价格能买到什么。通常有四个因素最为关键：

1. **How many surfaces are queried live.** Each request to an AI system has a cost for the provider. Covering five surfaces is materially different from covering two.
1. **实时查询的界面数量。** 对 AI 系统的每次请求都会产生服务成本。覆盖五个界面与覆盖两个界面有着本质区别。
2. **How often the queries run.** A daily check across five surfaces creates more requests than a monthly check across the same surfaces.
2. **查询运行的频率。** 每天在五个界面上进行检查所产生的请求量，远高于每月检查一次。
3. **How many queries and formulations are tracked.** Ten formulations monitored every day are not equivalent to 100 formulations monitored once a month.
3. **跟踪的查询和措辞数量。** 每天监控 10 种措辞与每月监控 100 种措辞是完全不同的。
4. **How many markets and languages are included.** A French query and its English equivalent may produce different answers, sources, and competitors.
4. **包含的市场和语言数量。** 法语查询及其英语对应版本可能会产生不同的回答、来源和竞争对手。

Currency also matters. A dollar price and an euro price should be compared after conversion, and a module should be evaluated together with the subscription it requires.
货币也同样重要。美元价格和欧元价格应在换算后进行比较，且模块应与其所需的订阅服务一并评估。

### A free scan is not continuous monitoring
### 免费扫描不等同于持续监控

A free scan can answer a narrow question at one moment. It may tell you what one engine said about one query at the time you clicked the button. It cannot, by itself, show whether the answer changes tomorrow, after a content update, or when a competitor publishes a new comparison page.
免费扫描只能在某一时刻回答一个狭窄的问题。它可能告诉您在您点击按钮时，某个引擎对某个查询的回答。但它本身无法显示该回答在明天、内容更新后，或竞争对手发布新的对比页面时是否会发生变化。

Continuous monitoring finances repetition. A provider that checks several surfaces daily must pay for repeated live requests and store the resulting evidence. If a free offer claims to monitor many queries, languages, and engines without charge, ask which limits are hidden: number of surfaces, number of queries, frequency, retention, or historical reporting.
持续监控需要重复投入资金。每天检查多个界面的服务商必须为重复的实时请求付费并存储结果证据。如果某个免费服务声称可以免费监控大量查询、语言和引擎，请询问其隐藏的限制条件：界面数量、查询数量、频率、数据保留期限或历史报告功能。

### A concrete measurement workflow
### 具体的衡量工作流程

Suppose your company sells project-management software to small agencies. Test the query "best project management tool for a 20-person services company" in all five surfaces, using the same English locale and the same day. Run it once a week for four weeks, then repeat it after publishing an updated comparison page.
假设您的公司向小型代理机构销售项目管理软件。在所有五个界面上测试查询“best project management tool for a 20-person services company”（适合 20 人服务公司的最佳项目管理工具），使用相同的英语地区设置和同一天进行测试。每周运行一次，持续四周，然后在发布更新后的对比页面后再次运行。

A useful result might look like this:
一个有用的结果可能如下所示：

| Surface | Citation | What the answer emphasizes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ChatGPT | Your page cited | Ease of setup and agency pricing |
| Perplexity | Competitor cited | Integrations and reporting |
| Claude | No citation | General selection criteria |
| Gemini | Your page cited | Security and team permissions |
| AI Overview | Partial citation | Shortlist with mixed claims |

| 界面 | 引用情况 | 回答强调的内容 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| ChatGPT | 引用了您的页面 | 设置简易度和代理机构定价 |
| Perplexity | 引用了竞争对手 | 集成功能和报告 |
| Claude | 未引用 | 通用选择标准 |
| Gemini | 引用了您的页面 | 安全性和团队权限 |
| AI Overview | 部分引用 | 包含混合主张的候选名单 |

This pattern does not prove that one surface is more important than another. It shows where your message is present, where it is absent, and where the answer may send a buyer elsewhere. The next step is to improve the underlying page, clarify the claim, or address a missing comparison rather than changing headings in isolation.
这种模式并不能证明某个界面比另一个更重要。它展示了您的信息出现在哪里、缺失在哪里，以及回答可能将买家引导至何处。下一步是改进底层页面、澄清主张或补充缺失的对比内容，而不是孤立地修改标题。

If you already pay for a broad SEO suite, an added GEO module may be economical because it uses an existing subscription. If your main need is AI visibility, a dedicated tool can avoid paying for features you do not use. Compare the number of surfaces, monitoring frequency, tracked queries, markets, history, and exportable evidence before choosing.
如果您已经购买了综合 SEO 套件，添加一个 GEO 模块可能更经济，因为它利用了现有的订阅。如果您的主要需求是 AI 可见性，那么专用工具可以避免为您不使用的功能付费。在选择之前，请比较界面数量、监控频率、跟踪查询、市场、历史记录和可导出证据等因素。
