# Can Vision-Language Models Judge Olympic Diving? From Reasoning to Scores in Zero-Shot Action Quality Assessment

**视觉-语言模型能担任奥运跳水裁判吗？从零样本动作质量评估的推理到评分**

---

Automated action quality assessment (AQA) in Olympic sports remains a challenging task due to the complexity of human motion and the subjectivity inherent in expert judging. This work evaluates the capability of open-source Vision-Language Models (VLMs) to perform zero-shot action quality assessment on Olympic diving videos using the AQA-7 benchmark dataset.

由于人体运动的复杂性以及专家评审固有的主观性，奥运体育项目中的自动化动作质量评估（AQA）仍然是一项艰巨的任务。本研究利用 AQA-7 基准数据集，评估了开源视觉-语言模型（VLM）在奥运跳水视频中执行零样本动作质量评估的能力。

In this regard, a regression-based framework is proposed to leverage both the semantic reasoning and phase-level sub-scores generated by the VLMs, combining TF-IDF vectorization, dimensionality reduction, and ensemble learning to predict final competition scores.

对此，研究提出了一种基于回归的框架，旨在利用 VLM 生成的语义推理和阶段性子分数，通过结合 TF-IDF 向量化、降维和集成学习来预测最终的比赛得分。

Experimental results show that standalone VLMs achieve moderate Spearman correlations below 0.32, while the proposed ensemble regression framework substantially improves performance in the reported evaluation, reaching a Spearman correlation of 0.67 with a four-model configuration.

实验结果表明，独立 VLM 的 Spearman 相关系数仅在 0.32 以下，表现平平；而所提出的集成回归框架在评估中显著提升了性能，在四模型配置下达到了 0.67 的 Spearman 相关系数。

Textual reasoning features consistently outperformed raw numerical sub-scores, highlighting the richness of VLM-generated explanations for action quality analysis. These findings suggest that VLMs hold strong potential as assistive tools for explainable and semi-automated sports performance evaluation.

文本推理特征的表现始终优于原始数值子分数，这凸显了 VLM 生成的解释在动作质量分析中的丰富价值。这些发现表明，VLM 作为可解释且半自动化的运动表现评估辅助工具，具有巨大的潜力。
