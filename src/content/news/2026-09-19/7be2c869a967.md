# RGS: Reflection-aware Gaussian Splatting via Learning Geometry Continuity for Reflective Objects
# RGS：通过学习反射物体的几何连续性实现反射感知高斯溅射

**Abstract:** Gaussian Splatting has significantly improved the quality of novel view synthesis with explicit Gaussian representation. However, we observed that existing 3D Gaussian Splatting methods (3DGS) often suffer from surface collapse issues on reflective regions, and thus produce inferior geometry and low-quality specular. 

**摘要：** 高斯溅射（Gaussian Splatting）通过显式的高斯表示显著提升了新视角合成的质量。然而，我们观察到现有的 3D 高斯溅射方法（3DGS）在反射区域往往会出现表面塌陷问题，从而导致几何结构较差且镜面反射质量低下。

In this work, we propose a physically-based deferred rendering framework, named Reflection-aware Gaussian Splatting (RGS), that can accurately model specular regions and improve novel view synthesis performance. Specifically, we found that a powerful 3D foundation model can provide a strong 3D geometric prior to foster correct geometric modeling. 

在这项工作中，我们提出了一种基于物理的延迟渲染框架，命名为反射感知高斯溅射（RGS），该框架能够精确建模镜面反射区域并提升新视角合成的性能。具体而言，我们发现强大的 3D 基础模型可以提供强有力的 3D 几何先验，从而促进正确的几何建模。

Based on this, we propose a cross-view shape consistency regularization to regularize the geometry surface with the large model prior and cross-view constraints. In this manner, our RGS can produce smoother geometric surfaces on reflective regions while reducing geometric hollows. 

基于此，我们提出了一种跨视角形状一致性正则化方法，利用大模型先验和跨视角约束来规范几何表面。通过这种方式，我们的 RGS 能够在反射区域生成更平滑的几何表面，同时减少几何空洞。

To further improve rendering results on reflective regions, we present a reflection-aware densification strategy that is designed to capture specular variations across various views. With this strategy, our RGS is able to render novel views of objects in higher quality. Extensive experiments demonstrate our method consistently renders high-quality reflective objects, achieving state-of-the-art performance.

为了进一步改善反射区域的渲染效果，我们提出了一种反射感知致密化策略，旨在捕捉不同视角下的镜面反射变化。借助该策略，我们的 RGS 能够以更高的质量渲染物体的新视角。大量实验证明，我们的方法能够持续渲染出高质量的反射物体，达到了业内领先水平。
