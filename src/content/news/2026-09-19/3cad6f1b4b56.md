# Riemannian--Lorentz Fusion of Vision Transformers and State-Space Models
# 视觉 Transformer 与状态空间模型的黎曼-洛伦兹融合

Scaling deep learning faces critical bottlenecks: data exhaustion, exponential training costs, and resource concentration. Model merging combines pre-trained checkpoints without gradient descent, offering orders-of-magnitude savings versus retraining.
深度学习的扩展正面临严峻瓶颈：数据枯竭、指数级增长的训练成本以及资源集中化。模型合并（Model merging）无需梯度下降即可结合预训练检查点，与重新训练相比，可节省几个数量级的成本。

Combining independently trained vision models is difficult when their architectures and parameter shapes differ. Existing weight-space merging methods generally assume aligned, shape-compatible checkpoints, whereas a Vision Transformer (ViT) and a state-space model (SSM) implement token mixing with different operators.
当视觉模型的架构和参数形状不同时，合并独立训练的模型变得十分困难。现有的权重空间合并方法通常假设检查点是对齐且形状兼容的，然而视觉 Transformer (ViT) 和状态空间模型 (SSM) 使用不同的算子来实现 Token 混合。

We study a hybrid Heterogeneous merging setting that retains both architectures while aligning parameter groups by semantic role. Our proposed Riemannian--Lorentz Parameter Fusion (RLPF) method projects aligned groups to common coordinates, lifts selected coordinates to the Lorentz hyperboloid model of hyperbolic space, computes a regularized geodesic barycenter, and decodes the result into the two branches. A learned gate then combines branch logits for each input. Component groups use fixed curvature values, with normalization parameters treated as Euclidean.
我们研究了一种混合异构合并设置，在保留两种架构的同时，按语义角色对齐参数组。我们提出的黎曼-洛伦兹参数融合 (RLPF) 方法将对齐的组投影到公共坐标系，将选定的坐标提升到双曲空间的洛伦兹双曲面模型，计算正则化的测地线重心，并将结果解码到两个分支中。随后，一个学习到的门控机制会为每个输入合并分支的 Logits。组件组使用固定的曲率值，而归一化参数则被视为欧几里得空间处理。

In the results available in this manuscript, the fine-tuned system obtains 82.37\% on CIFAR-10, 75.04\% on Oxford-IIIT Pet, and 78.58\% top-1 accuracy on ImageNet-1K; the corresponding best-parent accuracies are 76.54\%, 71.42\%, and 76.42\%. On ImageNet-1K, the reported pre-fine-tuning initialization reaches 77.80\%. These results support further study of geometry-aware heterogeneous fusion, but not a training-free single-checkpoint merge: RLPF is a two-branch hybrid whose gate and reported final models are trained.
根据本文提供的结果，微调后的系统在 CIFAR-10 上达到了 82.37%，在 Oxford-IIIT Pet 上达到了 75.04%，在 ImageNet-1K 上达到了 78.58% 的 Top-1 准确率；相应的最佳父模型准确率分别为 76.54%、71.42% 和 76.42%。在 ImageNet-1K 上，报告的微调前初始化准确率达到了 77.80%。这些结果支持对几何感知异构融合的进一步研究，但并不支持无需训练的单检查点合并：RLPF 是一种双分支混合模型，其门控机制和报告的最终模型均经过了训练。
