# Disney’s first CTO is Character.AI’s former CEO
# 迪士尼任命首位首席技术官，由 Character.AI 前 CEO 担任

You would think that a multimedia conglomerate as large as Disney would have a few chief technology officers by now, but the company has just appointed someone to the position for the very first time.
你可能认为像迪士尼这样庞大的多媒体企业集团早就应该有几位首席技术官了，但该公司直到现在才首次任命了这一职位。

Today, Disney announced that Karandeep Anand is joining the company as its new CTO. Anand will report directly to recently appointed CEO Josh D’Amaro, and he will head up the company’s infrastructure, product, engineering, and data / AI platforms teams.
今日，迪士尼宣布 Karandeep Anand 将加入公司担任新任首席技术官（CTO）。Anand 将直接向近期上任的首席执行官 Josh D’Amaro 汇报，并负责领导公司的基础设施、产品、工程以及数据/人工智能平台团队。

Anand comes to Disney from chatbot startup Character.AI, where he served as CEO for just over a year and oversaw the company’s push into microdramas.
Anand 在加入迪士尼之前任职于聊天机器人初创公司 Character.AI，他在那里担任了刚满一年的首席执行官，并负责监督该公司在微短剧领域的业务拓展。

Anand jumping ship to Disney comes as a bit of a surprise considering how this time last year, the company was sending Character.AI cease and desist letters due to the startup’s tools infringing on copyrighted IP. But Anand’s hiring could be a sign of how Disney wants to move forward with AI now that its partnership with OpenAI has fallen apart.
考虑到去年此时，迪士尼还曾因 Character.AI 的工具侵犯其版权知识产权而向该公司发送过停止侵权函（cease and desist letters），Anand 跳槽至迪士尼着实令人有些意外。但此次聘用可能预示着，在与 OpenAI 的合作破裂后，迪士尼正寻求在人工智能领域寻求新的发展方向。
