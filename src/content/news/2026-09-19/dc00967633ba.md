# Lenovo’s Yoga Slim 7X is the most laptop that $1,000 can currently buy
# 联想 Yoga Slim 7X 是目前 1,000 美元预算下性价比最高的笔记本电脑

It features an OLED screen and a Snapdragon X2 Elite CPU.
它配备了 OLED 屏幕和骁龙 X2 Elite 处理器。

Anyone shopping for a Windows laptop with a $1K budget should head to Best Buy, where for the rest of the day you can get a great deal on a capable Lenovo laptop. The Yoga Slim 7X is a slim 14-inch machine with a 2K 16:10 OLED touchscreen, Snapdragon’s high-end X2 Elite processor with 12 cores, plus 16GB of DDR5 RAM and a 1TB SSD. The specs are about the best you can get these days for $1,000, as worthwhile laptop deals are in short supply during RAMageddon.
任何预算在 1,000 美元左右寻找 Windows 笔记本电脑的用户都应该去百思买（Best Buy）看看，在今天结束之前，你可以以极具性价比的价格买到这款性能出色的联想笔记本。Yoga Slim 7X 是一款轻薄的 14 英寸设备，配备 2K 16:10 OLED 触摸屏、12 核骁龙 X2 Elite 高端处理器、16GB DDR5 内存以及 1TB SSD。在当前内存价格波动（RAMageddon）导致高性价比笔记本电脑稀缺的情况下，这套配置几乎是 1,000 美元预算下你能买到的最佳选择。

The Yoga Slim 7X seems like a great alternative to spending more (and getting less) with Microsoft’s latest Surface Laptop. In fact, you’d spend $1,549 on a similar model and still get half the storage that’s in this particular Yoga Slim 7X configuration. Its Arm-based processor can run most Windows apps very well, and yields superior battery life compared to laptops with x86 processors.
对于那些不想花更多钱却买到更低配置的微软最新款 Surface Laptop 的用户来说，Yoga Slim 7X 是一个绝佳的替代方案。事实上，如果你购买配置相似的 Surface Laptop，需要花费 1,549 美元，而且存储空间仅为这款 Yoga Slim 7X 的一半。其基于 Arm 架构的处理器可以非常流畅地运行大多数 Windows 应用程序，并且与 x86 架构处理器的笔记本电脑相比，续航表现更为出色。

The laptop includes three USB-C ports, and a hardware switch for disabling the webcam. It supports Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 5.3. It seems like a great machine for work, watching movies, or playing some games that aren’t too graphically intensive.
这款笔记本电脑包含三个 USB-C 接口，并配有一个用于禁用摄像头的物理开关。它支持 Wi-Fi 7 和蓝牙 5.3。对于办公、观看电影或运行一些对图形性能要求不高的游戏来说，它是一台非常棒的设备。
