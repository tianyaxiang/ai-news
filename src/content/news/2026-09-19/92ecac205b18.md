# Open ultrasound foundation model for robust segmentation and clinical measurement across heterogeneous settings
# 用于异构环境下稳健分割与临床测量的开放式超声基础模型

**Abstract:** Ultrasound is the most widely deployed imaging modality worldwide, yet clinical AI remains fragmented into narrow single-task models that fail when device, operator, or anatomy changes. Here we present SonoCorpus, an open resource unifying 456,963 images and 1,626,085 expert masks from 53 public datasets spanning 24 clinical applications and 17 countries, and SonoBase, an interactive segmentation foundation model pretrained on it.

**摘要：** 超声是全球应用最广泛的成像方式，但临床人工智能领域目前仍处于碎片化状态，局限于单一任务模型，一旦设备、操作人员或解剖结构发生变化，这些模型往往会失效。本文介绍了 SonoCorpus，这是一个开放资源库，整合了来自 17 个国家、涵盖 24 种临床应用的 53 个公共数据集，包含 456,963 张图像和 1,626,085 个专家标注掩码；同时还推出了 SonoBase，这是一个基于该资源预训练的交互式分割基础模型。

Across fifteen evaluation datasets introducing new organs, devices, operators, and geographies, SonoBase outperforms SAM2, MedSAM2, and the concept-promptable MedSAM3 on every dataset and matches per-dataset specialist models trained on the same data; on fully external data it exceeds the accuracy these baselines achieve on their own in-distribution benchmarks.

在引入了新器官、新设备、新操作人员和新地理区域的 15 个评估数据集上，SonoBase 在所有数据集上的表现均优于 SAM2、MedSAM2 和基于概念提示的 MedSAM3，并与在相同数据上训练的特定数据集专家模型表现相当；在完全外部的数据上，其准确性甚至超过了这些基准模型在其自身分布内基准测试中所达到的水平。

Ejection fraction derived from its segmentations falls within inter-observer variability (6.63% error), with fewer misclassifications at the defibrillator-candidacy threshold than either promptable baseline (13% versus 18--42%); fetal head-circumference (1.81 mm) and gestational-age (1.2 days) errors fall below inter-observer variability.

由其分割结果推导出的射血分数处于观察者间变异范围内（误差为 6.63%），在除颤器适用阈值处的误分类率低于两个可提示基准模型（13% 对比 18%–42%）；胎儿头围（1.81 毫米）和孕龄（1.2 天）的误差均低于观察者间的变异水平。

Where a baseline fails outright, one in four test cases, SonoBase recovers a usable segmentation in 81% of them, including on handheld probes operated by minimally trained users in two low- and middle-income countries (Sierra Leone and Tanzania). Five labeled examples can help the model adapt to a new setting, and the identical training protocol transfers well to newer models such as SAM3, locating the advantage in ultrasound-specific pretraining rather than any single architecture.

在基准模型完全失效的四分之一测试案例中，SonoBase 有 81% 的情况能恢复出可用的分割结果，其中包括在两个中低收入国家（塞拉利昂和坦桑尼亚）由受过极少培训的用户操作手持式探头所采集的图像。仅需五个标注示例即可帮助模型适应新环境，且相同的训练协议可以很好地迁移到 SAM3 等较新的模型上，这表明其优势在于超声领域的特定预训练，而非依赖于某种单一架构。

To ensure reproducibility and enable the community to build on SonoBase as a platform, we release all checkpoints, optimizer states, data-split indices, deduplication hashes, and starter code.

为了确保可重复性并使社区能够以 SonoBase 为平台进行开发，我们发布了所有的检查点（checkpoints）、优化器状态、数据拆分索引、去重哈希值以及入门代码。
