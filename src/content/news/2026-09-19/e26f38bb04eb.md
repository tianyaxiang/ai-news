### Next-Level Skin (and Scalp) Care
### 进阶版皮肤（及头皮）护理

The must-have gadget for women of almost any age is an LED mask. These masks promise to use red light to erase wrinkles, brighten your skin, and keep acne at bay. I haven't seen much change in my wrinkles in my year-plus of regularly using a red light mask, but I can confirm it makes my skin brighter and calms down acne and scars much faster when I'm regularly treating my skin. HigherDose is a favorite mask of mine because the three-strap design lets it hang on my head rather than squeeze it, making it one of the most comfortable masks I've worn.
几乎任何年龄段的女性都必备的一款小工具就是 LED 面罩。这些面罩承诺利用红光来消除皱纹、提亮肤色并抑制痤疮。虽然我定期使用红光面罩一年多以来，皱纹并没有太大变化，但我可以确认，在定期护理皮肤时，它确实让我的皮肤更亮，并能更快地平复痘痘和疤痕。HigherDose 是我最喜欢的面罩，因为它的三带设计让它能“挂”在头上而不是紧紧挤压，这使它成为我戴过最舒适的面罩之一。

My other favorite LED tool actually sits on my head and also comes from HigherDose. The brand's Red Light Hat treats your head with red light to rejuvenate your scalp and hair follicles for better hair growth, and as someone with a sensitive scalp and a hair-pulling problem, using this cap regularly genuinely improved my scalp and hair. It's the healthiest it's ever been, and my scalp is the least itchy, too. I love that this hat also has a battery pack, so I can comfortably wear it anywhere while I treat my head.
我另一个最喜欢的 LED 工具是戴在头上的，同样来自 HigherDose。该品牌的红光帽通过红光照射头部，以焕活头皮和毛囊，促进头发生长。作为一个头皮敏感且有拔发习惯的人，定期使用这顶帽子确实改善了我的头皮和发质。这是我头发最健康的状态，头皮也不怎么痒了。我喜欢这顶帽子自带电池组的设计，这样我在护理头部时可以舒适地戴着它去任何地方。

### A Cool Tote
### 一款时髦的托特包

Look, nothing says mom life like looking for the perfect bag. I'm in a massive tote phase of my life; I somehow need to either lug an armload of items for myself and my son (or just myself!) or I only need a tiny belt bag. There's no in between.
听着，没有什么比寻找完美的包包更能代表“妈妈生活”了。我正处于人生中极度依赖托特包的阶段；我要么需要为自己和儿子（或者仅仅为了我自己！）扛着一大堆东西，要么就只需要一个小腰包。没有中间地带。

The Yardsale Day Bag has been my go-to for a variety of needs, from a stylish personal item for a flight to a gym bag to the aerial studio. It's got a waterproof bottom that makes me happy to bring it to the zoo's splash pad, and a magnetic middle separator you can flex to separate the middle of the bag into two medium sections to better divide all your items.
Yardsale Day Bag 是我应对各种需求的首选，无论是作为飞行时的时尚随身包，还是去空中瑜伽馆的健身包。它有一个防水底座，让我可以放心地带它去动物园的戏水区。它还有一个磁性中间隔板，你可以灵活地将包内空间分成两个中等区域，以便更好地分类存放物品。

### Her Google Calendar IRL
### 将她的谷歌日历实体化

My latest favorite family planning gadget is a digital wall calendar. These handy devices take the Google Calendar I live by (and just about any other digital calendar, if your mom isn't a Google girl) and put it on a physical device I can quickly tap into without needing to grab my phone or bark at Alexa. The Skylight Calendar 2 is my favorite, because it not only includes to-do lists, meal planning, and a recipe book but also doubles as a digital photo frame.
我最近最喜欢的家庭规划小工具是数字挂历。这些便捷的设备将我赖以生存的谷歌日历（如果你的妈妈不用谷歌，它也支持几乎任何其他数字日历）呈现在一个实体设备上，我无需拿起手机或对着 Alexa 大喊大叫，就能快速查看。Skylight Calendar 2 是我的最爱，因为它不仅包含待办事项列表、膳食规划和食谱，还可以兼作数字相框。

### For the Expecting Mom
### 给准妈妈的礼物

I'm expecting again, and this time I'm on a mission to find a good pregnancy pillow. I tried a large body pillow on the advice of another site for my first pregnancy, and it was honestly an awful experience—the pillow was too large and firm for me to comfortably use, let alone have room for both it and my growing midsection in my bed.
我又怀孕了，这次我的任务是找到一个好用的孕妇枕。第一次怀孕时，我听从了另一个网站的建议尝试了一个大型全身枕，老实说那是一次糟糕的体验——枕头太大太硬，我无法舒适地使用，更不用说在床上同时容纳它和我日益隆起的腹部了。
