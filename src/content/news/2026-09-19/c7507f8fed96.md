# Randomized SVD Approximations for Spectral Co-Clustering of Word-Document Matrices
# 用于词-文档矩阵谱共聚类的随机 SVD 近似方法

**Abstract:** Spectral co-clustering is a useful tool for discovering latent structure in word-document matrices, but its reliance on singular value decomposition (SVD) can make standard formulations expensive on high-dimensional data. 

**摘要：** 谱共聚类（Spectral co-clustering）是发现词-文档矩阵中潜在结构的有效工具，但由于其依赖于奇异值分解（SVD），使得标准算法在处理高维数据时计算成本高昂。

This paper presents two randomized approximations for normalized spectral co-clustering of bipartite text data when the numbers of document and word clusters may differ. 

本文针对文档簇和词簇数量可能不同的二分文本数据，提出了两种用于归一化谱共聚类的随机近似方法。

The first method uses randomized SVD through random projection, while the second combines partial SVD with element-wise random sampling. 

第一种方法通过随机投影实现随机 SVD，而第二种方法则将部分 SVD 与元素级随机采样相结合。

Across real-world and synthetic datasets, both methods reduce runtime relative to the full-SVD baseline, but their behavior depends on matrix sparsity. 

在真实数据集和合成数据集上的实验表明，这两种方法相对于全 SVD 基准均缩短了运行时间，但其表现取决于矩阵的稀疏性。

The random projection method is the more reliable approximation across the tested settings, whereas the sampling-based method is most useful on denser matrices and provides limited benefit on already sparse text data. 

随机投影方法在所测试的各种设置下表现出更可靠的近似效果，而基于采样的方法在处理较稠密的矩阵时最为有效，但在处理本身已非常稀疏的文本数据时，其优势有限。

These results show that randomized approximations for spectral co-clustering should be selected according to the underlying structure of the data.

这些结果表明，在进行谱共聚类时，应根据数据的底层结构来选择合适的随机近似方法。
