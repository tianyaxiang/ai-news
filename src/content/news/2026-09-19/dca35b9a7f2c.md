# Y Combinator insurance tech alum Angle Health hits $2.7B valuation
# Y Combinator 保险科技校友企业 Angle Health 估值达到 27 亿美元

These days, it’s rare to hear of a startup founded in 2019, or one not focused on AI agents, raising a hefty Series C. But health insurance startup Angle Health announced Friday that it raised a $200 million Series C, alongside a $400 million tender offer, at a $2.7 billion valuation.
如今，一家 2019 年成立且非专注于 AI 智能体的初创公司获得巨额 C 轮融资已属罕见。但健康保险初创公司 Angle Health 周五宣布，公司已完成 2 亿美元的 C 轮融资，并伴随 4 亿美元的要约收购，估值达到 27 亿美元。

The round was led by Vitruvian Partners with participation from Town Hall Ventures, Blumberg Capital, Portage Ventures, PruVen Capital, and Y Combinator. The tender offer allows employees to cash out some of their shares, the company told TechCrunch. It expects the round to close later this month.
本轮融资由 Vitruvian Partners 领投，Town Hall Ventures、Blumberg Capital、Portage Ventures、PruVen Capital 和 Y Combinator 跟投。该公司向 TechCrunch 表示，此次要约收购允许员工套现部分股份。预计该轮融资将于本月晚些时候完成。

Angle Health, a winter 2020 YC alum, helps small businesses obtain and manage what’s known as “level-funded health plans.” These are plans that land between fully insured and self-funded plans. In fully insured plans, the carrier takes all the risks; such plans are more expensive but costs are predictable. With self-funded plans, the company covers the expenses and costs can be unpredictable.
Angle Health 是 YC 2020 年冬季班的校友企业，致力于帮助小型企业获取并管理所谓的“水平融资健康计划”（level-funded health plans）。这类计划介于全额保险和自筹资金计划之间。在全额保险计划中，保险公司承担所有风险；此类计划费用较高，但成本可预测。而在自筹资金计划中，公司需自行承担费用，成本可能难以预测。

With level-funded plans, businesses make predictable payments to carriers, are insured against higher-than-expected costs, and can potentially save money by getting a share of the surplus back if expenses stay low. Angle says these plans can make health insurance more affordable for small businesses. It’s an AI-powered platform that helps small businesses choose and manage plans, integrating with payroll and HR systems. The startup says it serves over 5,000 businesses and is profitable.
通过水平融资计划，企业向保险公司支付可预测的费用，并针对超出预期的成本进行投保；如果医疗支出较低，企业还有望通过获得部分盈余返还来节省开支。Angle 表示，这些计划能让小型企业的健康保险负担得起。该平台由 AI 驱动，可帮助小型企业选择和管理保险计划，并与薪资和人力资源系统集成。这家初创公司表示，目前已服务超过 5,000 家企业，且已实现盈利。
