# Learning-Induced Dynamical Transition in Recurrent Neural Networks
# 循环神经网络中学习诱导的动力学转变

**Abstract:** Learning in recurrent neural networks can fundamentally reshape their underlying dynamics, transforming initially chaotic activity into stable task-dependent behavior. 

**摘要：** 循环神经网络中的学习过程可以从根本上重塑其底层动力学，将最初的混沌活动转化为稳定的、依赖于任务的行为。

We develop a non-equilibrium dynamical mean-field theory (DMFT) to describe this transition during learning. We show that a slow feedback-driven learning process generates an evolving effective feedback strength that drives the network through a transition from chaotic to stable dynamics defined by a bifurcation of the DMFT solution. 

我们开发了一种非平衡动力学平均场理论（DMFT）来描述学习过程中的这一转变。研究表明，缓慢的反馈驱动学习过程会产生一种不断演化的有效反馈强度，该强度驱动网络经历从混沌动力学到稳定动力学的转变，这一转变由 DMFT 解的分岔所定义。

By deriving the two-time correlation function throughout learning, we identify a critical feedback strength and a corresponding learning rate dependent critical time separating these regimes. The transition arises from the progressive deformation of an effective dynamical landscape by the growing learned feedback structure. 

通过推导整个学习过程中的双时相关函数，我们确定了一个临界反馈强度以及相应的、依赖于学习率的临界时间，用以区分这些状态。这种转变源于不断增长的学习反馈结构对有效动力学景观的渐进式变形。

Starting from the untrained state, the theory predicts the time evolution of the network output during training and shows quantitative agreement with numerical simulations.

从未经训练的状态开始，该理论预测了训练期间网络输出的时间演化，并与数值模拟结果表现出定量上的一致性。
