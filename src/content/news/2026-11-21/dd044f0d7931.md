# Webhook Delivery, Retries & Queue Mechanics: Tenant-Aware Scheduling, Poison-Pill DLQs, and Safe Replay at Scale

在规模化场景下实现可靠的 Webhook 投递，本质上是对有限故障和受控恢复的实践。向客户终端发送 HTTP POST 请求看似简单，但当你在数千个多租户终端上运行数百万个事件时，情况就变得复杂了。每个终端都有其独特的延迟特征、速率限制、中断时长和消费者端 Bug。有三种故障模式会反复导致事件驱动平台瘫痪：租户在速率限制期间的“饥饿”：单个嘈杂或受限的租户会迫使简单的 Worker 或共享的退避循环陷入停滞，从而降低无关租户的投递服务等级协议（SLA）。“毒丸”Worker 崩溃：格式错误、模式不匹配或过大的负载如果未在执行前被隔离，会导致 Worker 崩溃或污染重试批次。自引发的重试 DDoS：在没有速率控制的情况下重放大量积压任务或死信队列（DLQ），会淹没正在恢复的消费者，引发另一次中断或 429/5xx 错误的级联反应。本指南涵盖了在生产级 Webhook 流水线中解决这三个问题的架构模式。

Reliable webhook delivery at scale is an exercise in bounded failure and controlled recovery. Dispatching an HTTP POST to a customer endpoint looks trivial until you run millions of events across thousands of multi-tenant endpoints. Each endpoint has its own latency profile, rate limits, outage durations, and consumer bugs. Three failure modes repeatedly take down event-driven platforms: Tenant starvation during rate limiting: A single noisy or rate-limited tenant forces naive workers or shared backoff loops to stall, degrading delivery SLAs for unrelated tenants. Poison-pill worker crashes: Malformed, schema-mismatched, or oversized payloads crash workers or poison replay batches if they aren’t isolated before execution. Self-inflicted replay DDoS: Replaying a large backlog or DLQ without rate controls floods recovering consumers, triggering another outage or a cascade of 429 / 5xx errors. This guide covers the architectural patterns for solving all three in a production webhook pipeline.

行业实践是什么样的？Webhook 提供商之间的重试策略差异巨大，这就是为什么你自己的流水线需要明确、可调的语义，而不是直接复制默认设置。Stripe 对实时模式的投递采用指数退避策略，最长重试三天。沙盒环境的投递仅在几小时内重试三次。Stripe 未在其文档中公布确切的时间间隔，尽管第三方文章描述为：立即重试，随后延迟约 5 分钟、30 分钟、2 小时、5 小时和 10 小时，之后每 12 小时重试一次。请将此时间表视为社区报告，而非官方信息。Stripe 将任何非 2xx 的响应（包括 4xx）视为失败，并在连续失败约三天后禁用该终端。为许多 SaaS 产品提供 Webhook 支持的 Svix，其记录的重试时间表为：立即重试，随后是 5 秒、5 分钟、30 分钟、2 小时、5 小时、10 小时，最后再等待 10 小时。如果终端连续 5 天失败，它将被禁用，操作员可以手动重试或从特定日期“恢复失败”的消息。GitHub 不会自动重新投递失败的 Webhook。如果你的服务器宕机或超过响应超时时间，它会将投递记录为失败，并期望你手动或通过 REST API 进行重新投递。GitHub.com 记录的超时时间为 10 秒，而某些 GitHub Enterprise Server 版本记录为 30 秒。提供商之间的重试预算差异巨大，因此不要假设发送方一定会进行重试。开放规范 Standard Webhooks 定义了 `webhook-id`、`webhook-timestamp` 和 `webhook-signature` 头部。该规范指出，唯一标识符在失败 Webhook 的重试过程中保持不变，通常用作幂等键。时间戳反映了尝试时间，有助于防止重放攻击。

What Industry Practice Looks Like Retry policies vary widely across webhook providers, which is one reason your own pipeline needs explicit, tunable semantics rather than copied defaults. Stripe retries live-mode deliveries with exponential backoff for up to three days. Sandbox deliveries are retried only three times over a few hours. Stripe does not publish the exact intervals in its docs, though third-party write-ups describe an immediate retry followed by delays of roughly 5 minutes, 30 minutes, 2 hours, 5 hours, and 10 hours, then every 12 hours. Treat that schedule as community-reported, not official. Stripe treats any non-2xx response as a failure, including 4xx, and disables an endpoint after about three days of continuous failures. Svix, which powers webhooks for many SaaS products, documents a schedule of immediately, then 5 seconds, 5 minutes, 30 minutes, 2 hours, 5 hours, 10 hours, and a final 10 hours. An endpoint that fails continuously for 5 days is disabled, and operators can manually retry or “recover failed” messages from a given date. GitHub does not automatically redeliver failed webhook deliveries. It records a delivery as failed if your server is down or exceeds its response timeout, and expects you to redeliver manually or through the REST API. The documented timeout is 10 seconds on GitHub.com, while some GitHub Enterprise Server versions document 30 seconds. Retry budgets differ enormously between providers, so don’t assume a sender will retry at all. Standard Webhooks, an open specification, defines webhook-id, webhook-timestamp, and webhook-signature headers. The spec says the unique identifier stays the same across retries of a failed webhook and is often used as an idempotency key. The timestamp reflects the attempt, so it helps prevent replay attacks.

1. 如何在不拖累其他租户的情况下处理 Retry-After 头部。为什么盲目的指数退避会忽略提供商的速率窗口？带有抖动（Jitter）的指数退避可以在不造成“惊群效应”的情况下平滑处理瞬时网络波动、DNS 故障和短暂的 5xx 中断。典型的延迟计算公式为 `min(cap, base_delay * 2^attempt)` 并应用随机化（例如，完全抖动会在 0 到该上限之间选择一个随机值）。然而，盲目的指数退避将 429（请求过多）或 503（带有 Retry-After）仅仅视为普通的失败。这会产生两个问题：在明确的冷却期间过早重试：如果接收方响应 429 并带有 `Retry-After: 120`，在 2 或 4 秒后重试会浪费 Worker 周期并污染日志。一些 API 会持续计算你在受限期间发出的请求，因此过早重试可能会延长等待时间。当指定了短窗口时延迟过长：如果接收方在第 5 次尝试时要求 `Retry-After: 5`，而盲目的调度程序跳到了 30 分钟，这会造成不必要的长时间等待。Worker 如何处理等待同样重要：阻塞共享 Worker：如果 Worker 在持有连接或全局池槽位时休眠 `Retry-After` 秒，少数受限的终端可能会占用整个平台的并发资源。FIFO 队列中的队头阻塞：如果许多租户的投递共享一个 FIFO 队列，而一条失败的消息阻塞了队头，或者重试被简单地重新入队，那么瓶颈之后的健康租户就会经历不必要的延迟。核心规则：将 `Retry-After` 视为该租户/终端的调度元数据，而不是阻塞全局 Worker 或延迟无关租户的理由。

1. Handling Retry-After Headers Without Starving Other Tenants Why Blind Exponential Backoff Ignores Provider Rate Windows Exponential backoff with jitter smooths over transient network blips, DNS hiccups, and brief 5xx outages without creating a thundering herd. A typical delay is min(cap, base_delay * 2^attempt) with randomization applied (for example, full jitter picks a random value between 0 and that bound). However, blind exponential backoff treats a 429 Too Many Requests (or a 503 with Retry-After) as just another generic failure. That creates two problems: Premature retries during explicit cooldowns: If a receiver responds with 429 and Retry-After: 120, retrying after 2 or 4 seconds wastes worker cycles and pollutes logs. Some APIs keep counting requests made while you are throttled, so retrying too early can lengthen the wait. Overly long delays when a short window is specified: If a receiver says Retry-After: 5 on attempt 5, a blind schedule that jumps to 30 minutes waits far longer than necessary. How a worker handles the wait matters just as much: Blocking a shared worker: If a worker sleeps for Retry-After seconds while holding a connection or slot from a global pool, a handful of throttled endpoints can tie up concurrency for the entire platform. Head-of-line blocking in a FIFO queue: If deliveries for many tenants share one FIFO queue and a failing message stalls the head, or retries are requeued naively, healthy tenants behind the bottleneck see unnecessary latency. Core rule: Treat Retry-After as scheduling metadata for that tenant/endpoint, not a reason to stall global workers or delay unrelated tenants.

安全地解析和验证 `Retry-After`。`Retry-After` 定义在 RFC 9110（第 10.2.3 节）中。它通常随 429 (RFC 6585) 和 503 (RFC 9110) 一起发送，RFC 也允许服务器将其包含在其他响应中，例如 413 和 3xx 重定向。对于 429 和 503，服务器可能会包含它，因此缺失也是正常的。在调度下一次尝试前进行防御性解析：支持两种格式。值要么是非负整数秒数（`Retry-After: 30`），要么是 GMT 格式的 HTTP 日期（`Retry-After: Sun, 06 Dec 2026 07:28:00 GMT`）。对于日期，减去当前时间。如果结果为零或负数，你可以立即重试，但需遵守你自己的下限。设置安全边界。永远不要盲目信任终端。强制执行最小下限（例如 1 秒）和最大上限（例如 15 分钟到 1 小时）。如果头部缺失、无法解析或数值大得离谱，请回退到你设定的上限指数退避时间表。添加抖动。类似于 `min(max_delay, retry_after_delay) + random(0, jitter_ms)` 的操作可以防止同一终端的多个事件在速率窗口结束时在同一毫秒内被唤醒。在优先级队列中调度租户感知的重试延迟。为了防止受限租户拖累其他租户，将资格时间（`next_attempt_at`）与 Worker 执行解耦，并隔离每个租户和每个终端的节奏：使用时间排序的延迟队列。不要让受限消息阻塞消费者，而是计算 `next_attempt_at = now + effective_delay`，并将作业存储在以该时间为键的结构中。

Parsing and Validating Retry-After Safely Retry-After is defined in RFC 9110 (section 10.2.3). It is commonly sent with 429 (RFC 6585) and 503 (RFC 9110), and the RFCs let servers include it on other responses too, such as 413 and 3xx redirects. For 429 and 503 the server may include it, so its absence is normal. Parse it defensively before scheduling the next attempt: Support both formats. The value is either a non-negative integer number of seconds (Retry-After: 30) or an HTTP-date in GMT (Retry-After: Sun, 06 Dec 2026 07:28:00 GMT). For a date, subtract the current time. If the result is zero or negative, you may retry immediately, subject to your own floor. Clamp to safe bounds. Never trust an endpoint blindly. Enforce a minimum floor (for example, 1 second) and a maximum cap (for example, 15 minutes to 1 hour). If the header is missing, unparseable, or absurdly large, fall back to your capped exponential backoff schedule. Add jitter. Something like min(max_delay, retry_after_delay) + random(0, jitter_ms) prevents many events for the same endpoint from waking on the same millisecond when a rate window ends. Scheduling Tenant-Aware Retry Delays in Priority Queues To keep a throttled tenant from starving others, decouple eligibility time (next_attempt_at) from worker execution and isolate per-tenant and per-endpoint pacing: Use a time-ordered delayed queue. Instead of letting a throttled message block a consumer, compute next_attempt_at = now + effective_delay and store the job in a structure keyed by that.
