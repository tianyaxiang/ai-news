Deep vision models operating in resource-constrained environments depend heavily on low-bit quantization, yet compressing weights below 4 bits frequently induces catastrophic accuracy collapse. While mixed-precision recovery strategies aim to rescue accuracy by selectively retaining high-precision parameters, the fundamental mechanics of where and why these models collapse remain poorly understood.

在资源受限环境下运行的深度视觉模型严重依赖低位量化，然而将权重压缩至 4 位以下经常会导致灾难性的精度崩溃。尽管混合精度恢复策略旨在通过选择性地保留高精度参数来挽救精度，但这些模型在何处以及为何崩溃的基本机制仍未得到充分理解。

In this study, we systematically analyze the anatomy of quantization collapse across standard Vision CNNs (including ResNet and EfficientNet backbones) evaluated on the full 50,000-image ImageNet-1k validation set. First, we map the exact progression of degradation, demonstrating that collapse is highly non-linear and concentrated in late-stage pointwise projections and depthwise layers.

在本研究中，我们系统地分析了在完整的 50,000 张图像 ImageNet-1k 验证集上评估的标准视觉 CNN（包括 ResNet 和 EfficientNet 主干网络）中量化崩溃的剖析。首先，我们绘制了退化的精确演变过程，证明了崩溃具有高度非线性，且集中在后期的逐点投影层和深度卷积层中。

Second, we introduce a structured, multi-step recovery waterfall that evaluates selective integer precision (INT4/INT8) and selective floating-point scales. We show that keeping less than 5% of critical layer activations in 8-bit precision recovers up to 92% of the accuracy lost during aggressive sub-4-bit compression.

其次，我们引入了一种结构化的多步恢复瀑布模型，用于评估选择性整数精度（INT4/INT8）和选择性浮点尺度。我们证明，将不到 5% 的关键层激活值保持在 8 位精度，可以恢复在激进的 4 位以下压缩过程中损失的高达 92% 的精度。

Finally, we bridge the hardware reality gap by evaluating deployment performance on standard ARM and x86 CPU architectures. We show that while low-bit quantization reduces memory storage requirements, standard runtime software bit-unpacking introduces a 1.48x to 21.5x execution slowdown depending on batch size, highlighting a critical trade-off between model footprint and actual edge throughput.

最后，我们通过评估在标准 ARM 和 x86 CPU 架构上的部署性能，弥合了硬件现实差距。我们表明，虽然低位量化减少了内存存储需求，但标准的运行时软件位解包会根据批处理大小导致 1.48 倍到 21.5 倍的执行减速，这突显了模型占用空间与实际边缘吞吐量之间的关键权衡。
