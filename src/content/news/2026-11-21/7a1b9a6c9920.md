Bayesian Optimization (BO) can be substantially more sample-efficient when informed by prior knowledge about the problem. In practice, however, such knowledge is often available only in natural language, e.g., expert intuitions or documentation, rather than as the explicit probability distribution landscape that prior-guided BO methods may require.

当贝叶斯优化（BO）能够利用关于问题的先验知识时，其样本效率可以显著提高。然而在实践中，此类知识通常仅以自然语言形式存在（例如专家的直觉或文档），而非先验引导的 BO 方法所要求的显式概率分布图景。

Leveraging it effectively demands both an expressive mapping from language to priors and robustness to information that may be misleading. We propose HapBO (Harnessed $\alpha$-$\pi$ Bayesian Optimization), which elicits beliefs once from a human expert, a large language model (LLM), or both, and deterministically compiles a structured specification into a reusable belief function ($\pi: \mathcal{X} \to[0,1]$), with a rich representation not restricted to a Gaussian family.

有效地利用这些知识既需要从语言到先验的表达性映射，也需要对可能具有误导性的信息保持稳健性。我们提出了 HapBO（Harnessed $\alpha$-$\pi$ Bayesian Optimization），它从人类专家、大型语言模型（LLM）或两者处一次性获取信念，并将结构化规范确定性地编译为可重用的信念函数（$\pi: \mathcal{X} \to[0,1]$），其丰富的表示形式并不局限于高斯族。

Building on $\alpha$-$\pi$BO, HapBO applies this belief function through a bounded positive multiplier on any nonnegative acquisition, preserving positive acquisition support and requiring no LLM calls during optimization. We prove that, for the Expected Improvement acquisition function (EI), HapBO preserves the standard simple-regret rate up to a bounded factor, even under adversarial beliefs.

基于 $\alpha$-$\pi$BO，HapBO 通过对任何非负采集函数施加有界正乘数来应用此信念函数，从而保留了正采集支持，且在优化过程中无需调用 LLM。我们证明，对于期望改进（EI）采集函数，即使在对抗性信念下，HapBO 也能在有界因子内保持标准的简单遗憾率。

We evaluate HapBO on 40 HPOBench tasks, a synthetic suite in 6-15 dimensions and two real-world applications: cortical neurostimulation and constrained solar design. HapBO achieves the lowest aggregate HPOBench regret and strong real-world task performance, with zero in-loop token cost.

我们在 40 个 HPOBench 任务、一个 6-15 维的合成套件以及两个现实世界应用（皮层神经刺激和约束太阳能设计）上评估了 HapBO。HapBO 实现了最低的 HPOBench 汇总遗憾值和强大的现实任务性能，且循环内 Token 成本为零。

In real-world applications it improves the optimization outcome even at budgets of 12-20 queries, and at a small fraction of the wall-clock and token costs of other LLM-based methods.

在现实应用中，即使在 12-20 次查询的预算下，它也能改善优化结果，且其挂钟时间和 Token 成本仅为其他基于 LLM 方法的一小部分。
