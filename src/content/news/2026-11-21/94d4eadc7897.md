When the Steam Machine first started shipping in June, Valve said it was producing fewer units at a higher price than initially planned because it couldn’t secure supplies of scarce RAM and storage components at a reasonable cost.

当 Steam Machine 于六月首次开始发货时，Valve 表示其产量低于预期且价格高于原定计划，原因是无法以合理的成本获得稀缺的内存和存储组件。

Now, as the last of the initial batch of Steam Machine reservations are finally being fulfilled, Valve says those supply and cost issues will continue into the foreseeable future and may even require a price change. “We are continuing to build more Steam Machine units, but memory and storage costs continue to increase at unprecedented levels,” Valve wrote in a blog post on Thursday. “As a result we don’t yet know the number we are able to make, and at what price we’ll be able to offer them. The 2TB model in particular will be especially constrained.”

目前，随着首批 Steam Machine 预订订单终于完成交付，Valve 表示这些供应和成本问题在可预见的未来仍将持续，甚至可能导致价格变动。Valve 在周四的一篇博客文章中写道：“我们正在继续生产更多的 Steam Machine，但内存和存储成本仍在以前所未有的水平上涨。因此，我们尚不清楚能够生产的数量，以及能以何种价格提供这些产品。特别是 2TB 型号，供应将尤为紧张。”

If Valve does have to raise the price of the Steam Machine, it will be in good company. All three major console manufacturers instituted historically atypical price increases in 2025, with additional price hikes happening this year. For the Steam Machine, though, Valve may be more susceptible to supply-related price shocks than its larger competitors in the gaming hardware space.

如果 Valve 不得不提高 Steam Machine 的价格，它并不孤单。三大主机制造商在 2025 年都实施了历史上罕见的涨价，今年也出现了进一步的涨价。然而，对于 Steam Machine 而言，Valve 在游戏硬件领域可能比其规模更大的竞争对手更容易受到供应相关价格波动的影响。

Valve “likely [doesn’t] have the same kind of priority as a Nintendo, Sony, or Microsoft when it comes to suppliers,” F-Squared analyst Michael Futter told us earlier this year. And while the bigger players can still negotiate somewhat with chip suppliers, Valve’s Steam Machine is “a niche product that cannot benefit in the same way when it comes to procurement,” Niko Partners analyst Daniel Ahmad said.

F-Squared 分析师 Michael Futter 今年早些时候告诉我们，在供应商眼中，Valve “可能不像任天堂、索尼或微软那样拥有同等的优先权”。Niko Partners 分析师 Daniel Ahmad 表示，虽然大型厂商仍能与芯片供应商进行一定程度的谈判，但 Valve 的 Steam Machine 是“一款小众产品，在采购方面无法享受同样的优势”。

While Valve has not publicly discussed total Steam Machine sales, estimates based on public Steam Top Sellers charts suggest the company was selling 12,000 to 15,000 a week back in July. By contrast, the PS5 has averaged over 300,000 weekly unit sales worldwide in its roughly six years on the market.

虽然 Valve 尚未公开讨论 Steam Machine 的总销量，但根据公开的 Steam 畅销榜估算，该公司在七月份的周销量约为 1.2 万至 1.5 万台。相比之下，PS5 在上市约六年的时间里，全球平均每周销量超过 30 万台。

For now, at least, Valve says it has fulfilled an expectation it set in July that “everyone in the reservation queue will have the option to purchase by the end of this year.” The company now says there is “no waitlist remaining” for the 512GB Steam Machine model and that “purchase invites” have been sent to the remaining randomized 2TB reservation holders (who placed orders back in April). The 512GB model was even briefly back in stock on the Steam Store yesterday, even as Valve continues to pare down the waitlist for 2TB stock. Valve says it plans to release additional updates on Steam Machine availability in January.

至少目前，Valve 表示已经实现了七月份设定的预期，即“预订队列中的每个人都将在今年年底前获得购买机会”。该公司现表示，512GB 型号的 Steam Machine 已“没有剩余候补名单”，并且已向剩余的 2TB 随机预订持有者（四月份下单的用户）发送了“购买邀请”。尽管 Valve 仍在继续缩减 2TB 型号的候补名单，但 512GB 型号昨天甚至在 Steam 商店短暂恢复了库存。Valve 表示计划在一月份发布关于 Steam Machine 供应情况的进一步更新。
