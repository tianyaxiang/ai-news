Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.24999 (cs) [Submitted on 18 Nov 2026] Title: PsyWorld: Profile-Conditioned World Modeling for Human Behavior Prediction Authors: Ziyuan Qin, Yang Qiao, Summer B. Frandsen, Michael T. Treadway, Liang Zhao.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.24999 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 18 日] 标题：PsyWorld：用于人类行为预测的特征条件化世界模型 作者：Ziyuan Qin, Yang Qiao, Summer B. Frandsen, Michael T. Treadway, Liang Zhao。

Abstract: Understanding how stable psychometric traits and time-varying states jointly inform behavior is an important problem in computational behavioral science. Existing personalized agents commonly condition actions directly on a static profile, leaving realized state transitions unused as an explicit source of supervision.

摘要：理解稳定的心理测量特征与随时间变化的状态如何共同影响行为，是计算行为科学中的一个重要问题。现有的个性化智能体通常直接基于静态特征来设定行动条件，而未将已实现的状态转换作为明确的监督来源加以利用。

We introduce a profile-conditioned state world model that uses observed environment--behavior state transitions to learn transition-informed representations for behavioral prediction. During training, a posterior encoder quantizes realized transitions, while a prior predicts the resulting codes from the psychometric profile and pre-transition context.

我们引入了一种特征条件化状态世界模型，该模型利用观察到的环境-行为状态转换来学习用于行为预测的转换感知表征。在训练过程中，后验编码器对已实现的转换进行量化，而先验模型则根据心理测量特征和转换前背景预测生成的代码。

The prior code and predicted next state then condition macro-level behavior and simulator-grounded execution. Thus, the world model provides structured transition supervision instead of treating personality as a direct action descriptor.

先验代码和预测的下一状态随后会设定宏观行为和基于模拟器的执行条件。因此，该世界模型提供了结构化的转换监督，而不是将人格视为直接的行动描述符。

We evaluate the framework in VEET, a controlled behavioral simulator with human trajectories, and in AgentSociety as a structurally different synthetic environment. A participant-disjoint split of 240 training, 25 validation, and 48 test participants evaluates generalization to unseen people.

我们在 VEET（一个包含人类轨迹的受控行为模拟器）以及结构截然不同的合成环境 AgentSociety 中评估了该框架。通过将 240 名训练参与者、25 名验证参与者和 48 名测试参与者进行不重叠划分，评估了模型对未见人群的泛化能力。

On VEET, the model reaches 0.904 Top-1 accuracy (95% CI: [0.895, 0.913]) and 0.0033 Macro KL. Ablations associate continuous next-state modeling with most of the Top-1 gain, while discrete codes and prior alignment mainly improve Top-3 and distributional alignment.

在 VEET 上，该模型达到了 0.904 的 Top-1 准确率（95% 置信区间：[0.895, 0.913]）和 0.0033 的 Macro KL。消融实验表明，连续的下一状态建模贡献了大部分 Top-1 增益，而离散代码和先验对齐则主要改善了 Top-3 和分布对齐效果。

These results establish transition-informed world-model supervision as useful for subject-generalized behavior prediction, without implying causal psychological mechanisms or calibrated long-horizon state dynamics.

这些结果确立了基于转换感知的世界模型监督在主体泛化行为预测中的有效性，且无需暗示因果心理机制或校准的长程状态动力学。
