Title: What a Calibrated Probability Hides: Belief-Level Post-Training for Decision Models

标题：校准概率掩盖了什么：决策模型的信念级后训练

Abstract: A calibrated decision model can predict how often each answer is chosen. The same prediction can reflect disagreement among people who are confident in their choices or uncertainty among people who share a belief. Disagreement calls for identifying whose perspective applies, while uncertainty calls for gathering more evidence. Standard post-training scores a single prediction against one observed choice at a time, learning the pooled distribution without distinguishing these two sources of uncertainty.

摘要：一个经过校准的决策模型可以预测每个答案被选择的频率。同样的预测既可能反映出对自身选择充满信心的人们之间的分歧，也可能反映出持有相同信念的人们之间的不确定性。分歧需要识别出适用的是哪种观点，而不确定性则需要收集更多的证据。标准的后训练方法一次针对一个观察到的选择对单个预测进行评分，在学习汇总分布时无法区分这两种不确定性来源。

We introduce Belief-Grouped Policy Optimization (BGPO). The policy proposes a list of beliefs, estimates their prevalence, and predicts a choice distribution for each. During training, BGPO uses Bayes' rule to estimate how likely each belief was to generate an observed choice. These estimates weight the choice's contribution to each belief's log-score reward. To discover rare beliefs, the policy also proposes lists after seeing a few observed choices and retains them only if they predict held-out choices well.

我们引入了信念分组策略优化（BGPO）。该策略提出一系列信念，估计它们的流行程度，并为每个信念预测一个选择分布。在训练过程中，BGPO 使用贝叶斯规则来估计每个信念产生观察到的选择的可能性。这些估计值会对该选择对每个信念的对数分数奖励的贡献进行加权。为了发现罕见的信念，该策略还在观察到少量选择后提出列表，并且仅在它们能很好地预测留存选择时才保留这些列表。

At inference, the policy uses only the question and combines the belief-specific distributions by prevalence to produce a calibrated aggregate prediction. On benchmarks covering opinions, conversation safety, emotions elicited by artwork, and photo aesthetics, BGPO achieves the lowest conditional, worst-group, and overall Jensen-Shannon divergence across model initializations. It improves conditional divergence on both text and image tasks. On a synthetic image task with known beliefs, BGPO recovers the underlying groups more accurately than baselines trained with belief labels.

在推理时，该策略仅使用问题，并根据流行程度组合特定于信念的分布，以产生校准后的汇总预测。在涵盖观点、对话安全、艺术品引发的情绪以及照片美学等基准测试中，BGPO 在各种模型初始化中实现了最低的条件、最差组和整体 Jensen-Shannon 散度。它改善了文本和图像任务上的条件散度。在具有已知信念的合成图像任务中，BGPO 比使用信念标签训练的基线更准确地恢复了潜在的群体。
