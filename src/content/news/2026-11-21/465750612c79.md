I've been testing smart bird feeders for more than two years in my own backyard, and Birdfy has been—and still is—the brand I trust and recommend most. These feeders are well-made, reliable, and reasonably priced. The brand's app is also excellent; it's easy to navigate and use without shelling out for an additional subscription. Better still, Birdfy just rolled out free lifetime AI ID (which is quite decent) with any bird feeder, including the lowest-priced models.

我在自家后院测试智能喂鸟器已经超过两年了，Birdfy 一直是我最信任和推荐的品牌。这些喂鸟器做工精良、性能可靠且价格合理。该品牌的应用程序也非常出色；它易于导航和使用，无需额外支付订阅费用。更棒的是，Birdfy 刚刚为所有喂鸟器（包括价格最低的型号）推出了免费的终身 AI 识别功能（效果相当不错）。

If you don't want to gift a traditional feeder, you might consider Birdfy's smart birdbath and nesting box, each of which comes equipped with the same high-quality camera(s). Below are the models I've tested—in some cases for years—and recommend, including a couple I personally plan to buy as gifts this year.

如果你不想赠送传统的喂鸟器，可以考虑 Birdfy 的智能鸟浴盆和筑巢箱，它们都配备了同样高质量的摄像头。以下是我测试过（有些已经测试了多年）并推荐的型号，其中包括几款我今年打算亲自购买作为礼物的型号。

Birdfy Feeder 1 (No Solar): WIRED first reviewed this feeder in 2022. Back then we loved the concept, but felt its AI ID didn't quite cut it. Birdfy has come a long way since then on the AI front. The feeder body is largely the same, but the app and AI ID (now free) are both much improved, offering a seamless and user-friendly experience that makes it easy to sort through which birds visited that day.

Birdfy Feeder 1（无太阳能版）：《连线》杂志在 2022 年首次评测了这款喂鸟器。当时我们很喜欢这个概念，但觉得它的 AI 识别功能还不够好。从那时起，Birdfy 在 AI 方面取得了长足进步。喂鸟器主体基本没变，但应用程序和 AI 识别（现在免费）都有了很大改进，提供了无缝且用户友好的体验，可以轻松筛选出当天造访的鸟类。

Birdfy Rookie Smart Bird Feeder: Birdfy's “beginner” model, this unit is basically a gravity pet feeder with a camera on the side. All you need to do is snap three pieces together, charge the camera, and pair it to the Birdfy app. If you know anyone who loves birds but is intimidated by the thought of having to set up and calibrate a smart bird feeder, this could be the perfect gift for them.

Birdfy Rookie 智能喂鸟器：这是 Birdfy 的“入门级”型号，本质上是一个侧面带有摄像头的重力式宠物喂食器。你只需要将三个部件拼在一起，给摄像头充电，然后将其与 Birdfy 应用程序配对即可。如果你认识喜欢鸟类但又对设置和校准智能喂鸟器感到畏惧的人，这可能是送给他们的完美礼物。

Birdfy Metal 2 4K Smart Bird Feeder: Birdfy's Metal 2 4K is the brand's flagship release for this year, with Ultra 4K resolution and dual-band (2.4 GHz and 5.0 GHz) Wi-Fi connection. The metal body claims to resist squirrels, and the muted green color is a welcome change from Birdfy's usual bright plastics.

Birdfy Metal 2 4K 智能喂鸟器：Birdfy Metal 2 4K 是该品牌今年的旗舰产品，具有超高清 4K 分辨率和双频（2.4 GHz 和 5.0 GHz）Wi-Fi 连接功能。金属机身号称可以防松鼠，柔和的绿色外观也改变了 Birdfy 以往明亮的塑料质感，令人耳目一新。

Birdfy Bamboo Feeder With Camera: Smart bird feeders aren't always the most aesthetically pleasing thing you could put in your yard, but Birdfy's 2K bamboo model was the first smart feeder I've ever had that actually garnered compliments. The natural wood and waterfall-style black roof complement any landscaping or exterior decor, and the camera and overall setup ran flawlessly for me during my months of testing.

Birdfy 带摄像头竹制喂鸟器：智能喂鸟器并不总是院子里最美观的装饰，但 Birdfy 的 2K 竹制型号是我用过的第一款真正获得赞美的智能喂鸟器。天然木材和瀑布式黑色屋顶与任何景观或户外装饰相得益彰，在我的几个月测试中，摄像头和整体设置运行得非常完美。

Birdfy Bath Pro: This interesting product pairs a plastic birdbath (on an optional metal stand) with a solar-powered camera, so you can keep an eye on visiting birds without having to deal with seed mess. When I first tested it, I was worried birds would splash water on the camera lens, but the most popular activity at the bath is by far drinking the water.

Birdfy Bath Pro：这款有趣的产品将塑料鸟浴盆（配有可选的金属支架）与太阳能摄像头结合在一起，让你无需处理种子残渣就能观察造访的鸟类。我最初测试时，担心鸟儿会把水溅到镜头上，但事实证明，鸟浴盆最受欢迎的功能其实是喝水。

Birdfy Nest Duo: Though I do love a smart bird feeder, Birdfy's Nest Duo is the absolute zenith of bird-watching for me. A black-capped chickadee moved in, laid eggs, and raised chicks, and the cameras and app on the Nest stayed working, giving me 24-hour updates with interior night vision and views of the entry hole's exterior to see who was coming and going.

Birdfy Nest Duo：虽然我很喜欢智能喂鸟器，但 Birdfy 的 Nest Duo 对我来说才是观鸟的巅峰之作。一只黑顶山雀搬了进来，产卵并抚育了幼鸟，Nest 上的摄像头和应用程序一直正常工作，通过内部夜视功能和入口外部视图，为我提供 24 小时的实时更新，让我能看到谁进进出出。
