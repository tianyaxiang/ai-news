Google’s flagship search experience is evolving into an AI experience with stunning speed. Even time-tested elements of search are getting AI makeovers, and the latest to get this treatment is the search homepage on Google.com.

谷歌的旗舰搜索体验正以惊人的速度向人工智能体验演变。即便是久经考验的搜索元素也在进行人工智能改造，而最新接受这一改造的便是 Google.com 的搜索主页。

A redesign that’s been in testing for the last few weeks is now official, bringing easier access to AI chats and dropping the dedicated search button entirely. Google search VP Robby Stein confirmed on X that Google is deploying the new desktop search page after a successful test.

一项已测试数周的重新设计现已正式推出，它不仅让用户能更轻松地访问人工智能聊天功能，还完全取消了专用的搜索按钮。谷歌搜索副总裁罗比·斯坦（Robby Stein）在 X 上证实，在测试成功后，谷歌正在部署全新的桌面搜索页面。

The page will now have a few more buttons under the search bar, all connected to AI features. There’s an image generation button, one for analyzing files, and one for brainstorming with Gemini. The “I’m Feeling Lucky” button remains, but the Google Search button that used to sit next to it is gone.

该页面现在在搜索栏下方增加了几个按钮，全部与人工智能功能相关。其中包括一个图像生成按钮、一个用于分析文件的按钮，以及一个用于与 Gemini 进行头脑风暴的按钮。“手气不错”（I’m Feeling Lucky）按钮得以保留，但过去位于其旁边的“Google 搜索”按钮已被移除。

The search button has obviously been a part of Google’s search page forever. However, it wasn’t in a very convenient location in the modern interface. When you begin typing a query, the autocomplete suggestions cover that button. You have to click out of the text field to access the button, which is an extra step compared to simply mashing the Enter key to run your search.

搜索按钮显然一直是谷歌搜索页面的一部分。然而，在现代界面中，它的位置并不十分方便。当你开始输入查询内容时，自动补全建议会遮挡该按钮。你必须点击文本框外才能使用该按钮，与直接敲击回车键进行搜索相比，这多了一步操作。

The other big change brings your recent AI conversations to the forefront. If you’re logged into Google, the search homepage will have an expandable sidebar on the left with your recent AI Mode search threads. This sidebar will also be accessible on the results page.

另一个重大变化是将你最近的人工智能对话置于显眼位置。如果你已登录谷歌账号，搜索主页左侧将出现一个可展开的侧边栏，显示你最近的人工智能模式搜索记录。该侧边栏在搜索结果页面中同样适用。

According to Stein, the sidebar made a “big difference” in how people made progress on their web searches, calling it a “simpler way to pick up where you left off.” It’s easy to feel frustrated by changes like this if you don’t like Google’s new AI approach to search.

据斯坦称，该侧边栏在人们进行网络搜索的方式上产生了“巨大影响”，他称这是一种“从中断处继续搜索的更简单方式”。如果你不喜欢谷歌这种新的人工智能搜索方式，很容易对这类变化感到沮丧。

But at the same time, the loss of functionality is minimal. Google probably has usage data that shows that particular search button was rarely used. Most people probably just hit Enter or click an autocomplete suggestion to go to the results.

但与此同时，功能的损失微乎其微。谷歌可能掌握的使用数据表明，那个特定的搜索按钮很少被使用。大多数人可能只是按下回车键或点击自动补全建议来获取结果。

This change says more about Google’s priorities and the future of search. AI features get prominent buttons, and your AI conversations now have a whole sidebar UI. Meanwhile, traditional search is increasingly de-emphasized.

这一变化更多地反映了谷歌的优先事项和搜索的未来。人工智能功能获得了显眼的按钮，而你的人工智能对话现在拥有了完整的侧边栏界面。与此同时，传统搜索正日益被淡化。

There may come a day when AI Mode takes over completely, forcing you to manually select the old-fashioned version of search. It does feel like Google is inching in that direction.

也许有一天，人工智能模式会完全取代传统搜索，迫使你手动选择旧版的搜索方式。谷歌确实看起来正在向那个方向缓慢迈进。
