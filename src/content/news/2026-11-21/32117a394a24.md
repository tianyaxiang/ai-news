计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.25061 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 18 日] 标题：Infer3D：逆向生成建模实现稳健的 3D 重建 作者：Amani Kiruga, Mark Hamilton, Joshua B. Tenenbaum, William T. Freeman, Vincent Sitzmann, Yilun Du, Ayush Tewari。

Abstract: Humans can understand the 3D structure of scenes even from highly unfamiliar or extreme viewpoints. Cognitive science suggests this generalization stems from a top-down generative inference strategy. In contrast, state-of-the-art 3D reconstruction neural networks typically rely on amortized, feedforward inference. While fast, this pattern-matching approach degrades when faced with out-of-distribution (OOD) scene conditions unseen during training.

摘要：人类即使在极其陌生或极端的视角下也能理解场景的 3D 结构。认知科学表明，这种泛化能力源于一种自上而下的生成式推理策略。相比之下，最先进的 3D 重建神经网络通常依赖于摊销式的前馈推理。虽然这种模式匹配方法速度很快，但在面对训练中未见的分布外 (OOD) 场景条件时，其性能会下降。

Inspired by the generative nature of human vision, we propose Infer3D, an inverse generative modeling framework that recovers the latent variables of a generative model to best match the monocular input. To prevent degenerate flat outputs, we couple the generative image prior with a frozen 3D lifting network that constrains the optimization space to a valid, multi-view-consistent 3D manifold.

受人类视觉生成特性的启发，我们提出了 Infer3D，这是一个逆向生成建模框架，通过恢复生成模型的潜在变量来最佳匹配单目输入。为了防止出现退化的平面输出，我们将生成式图像先验与一个冻结的 3D 提升网络相结合，将优化空间限制在一个有效的、多视图一致的 3D 流形上。

Across a variety of controlled synthetic and real-world tasks, Infer3D demonstrates superior OOD reconstruction accuracy compared to feedforward networks trained on equal or larger datasets. Furthermore, mirroring how human perception requires more time to process confusing stimuli, we introduce an adaptive inference strategy that expends more test-time compute on ambiguous OOD examples while efficiently routing in-distribution inputs.

在各种受控的合成任务和现实世界任务中，与在相同或更大规模数据集上训练的前馈网络相比，Infer3D 展示了更优越的 OOD 重建精度。此外，模仿人类感知需要更多时间来处理令人困惑的刺激，我们引入了一种自适应推理策略，在处理模糊的 OOD 样本时消耗更多的测试时间计算资源，同时高效地处理分布内输入。
