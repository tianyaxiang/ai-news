Multiplying your Switch 2’s storage costs less than usual right now. SSD costs are still sky-high, but at least there are decent deals on microSD Express cards.

目前增加 Switch 2 存储空间的成本比平时更低。固态硬盘（SSD）的价格依然居高不下，但好在 microSD Express 存储卡有一些不错的优惠。

Expanding your Nintendo Switch 2’s 256GB of built-in storage with a microSD card is pricier than it was with Nintendo’s original console. That’s because microSD Express cards, while physically identical to ones you might have lying around, have faster read and write speeds.

使用 microSD 卡扩展任天堂 Switch 2 的 256GB 内置存储空间，比在任天堂原版主机上要昂贵。这是因为 microSD Express 卡虽然在外观上与你手头可能有的卡片完全相同，但其读写速度更快。

Thankfully, Samsung’s 256GB model has dropped to $44.99 at Amazon ahead of Black Friday, which is a few bucks off its usual price but still well below its $79.99 MSRP. Sandisk’s 256GB card, meanwhile, is available for $49.99 ($10 off Amazon’s typical list price) from Amazon and Sandisk, if you prefer one brand over the other for some reason.

值得庆幸的是，在黑色星期五到来之前，三星的 256GB 型号在亚马逊上的价格已降至 44.99 美元，这比其通常价格便宜了几美元，但仍远低于 79.99 美元的建议零售价。与此同时，如果你出于某种原因更青睐某个品牌，闪迪（Sandisk）的 256GB 卡在亚马逊和闪迪官网的售价为 49.99 美元（比亚马逊的典型标价优惠 10 美元）。

For those who don’t want to think about upgrading their Switch 2’s storage again, both brands’ 512GB microSD Express cards are selling for a fair $99.99 on Amazon. That gives you double the storage for roughly twice the price of a 256GB card, as it should be.

对于那些不想再考虑升级 Switch 2 存储空间的用户来说，这两个品牌的 512GB microSD Express 卡在亚马逊上的售价均为 99.99 美元，价格合理。这让你以大约两倍于 256GB 卡的价格获得了双倍的存储空间，这很符合逻辑。

I suspect the 256GB option will be enough for most Switch 2 players, unless you buy a lot of games. The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake is 24.9 gigabytes, for example. Other Nintendo-developed games tend to be similar in install size, though it’s all over the place for third-party game makers; Resident Evil Requiem (which also recently dropped to $39.99 on Amazon) is relatively small at 28.7 gigs, while The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Remastered is about 50. Some games take up even more storage.

我猜对于大多数 Switch 2 玩家来说，256GB 的选项已经足够了，除非你购买了大量的游戏。例如，《塞尔达传说：时之笛》重制版的大小为 24.9GB。其他由任天堂开发的游戏安装大小也大致相当，不过第三方游戏开发商的作品则参差不齐；《生化危机：安魂曲》（最近在亚马逊上也降至 39.99 美元）相对较小，为 28.7GB，而《巫师 3：狂猎——重制版》则约为 50GB。有些游戏占用的存储空间甚至更多。
