Before a banker can recommend an acquisition to their client, they need to make sense of financial statements, customer contracts and management presentations. Hebbia’s Matrix helps bring that research together, linking answers to the documents behind them. Hebbia tested OpenAI’s GPT‑6 Astra to improve two parts of that work: finding the sources a question requires and building a presentation that follows the banker’s brief.

在银行家向客户推荐收购之前，他们需要理清财务报表、客户合同和管理层演示文稿。Hebbia 的 Matrix 有助于整合这些研究，将答案与背后的文档联系起来。Hebbia 测试了 OpenAI 的 GPT-6 Astra，以改进这项工作的两个部分：寻找问题所需的来源，以及制作符合银行家简报的演示文稿。

“We’ve been strong partners with OpenAI over the course of Hebbia’s lifetime, and the leaps GPT-6 Astra has made in terms of quality outputs and accuracy have truly been surprising. GPT-6 Astra shines compared to any other model we’ve tested before on these two fronts.”—Kavita Anand, Member of Technical Staff, Product, Hebbia

“在 Hebbia 发展的整个过程中，我们一直是 OpenAI 的坚定合作伙伴，GPT-6 Astra 在输出质量和准确性方面取得的飞跃确实令人惊讶。在这两个方面，与我们之前测试过的任何其他模型相比，GPT-6 Astra 都表现出色。”——Kavita Anand，Hebbia 产品技术人员

From source documents to presentations. It could work like this: a banker is helping their client assess a strategic acquisition of an industrial-software company, with roughly 2,000 files to review. What makes its revenue durable? How does it compare with peers? In Hebbia’s hypothetical example, the banker could load the data room into Matrix and examine customer concentration, contract terms and churn in cited cells. Matrix could bring in peer filings and industry news to help develop the deal thesis, then turn that research into a diligence summary and valuation deck in the firm’s template.

从源文档到演示文稿。工作流程可能是这样的：一位银行家正在帮助其客户评估一家工业软件公司的战略收购，需要审查大约 2,000 份文件。是什么使其收入具有持久性？它与同行相比如何？在 Hebbia 的假设案例中，银行家可以将数据室加载到 Matrix 中，并检查引用单元格中的客户集中度、合同条款和流失率。Matrix 可以引入同行备案文件和行业新闻来帮助制定交易论点，然后将这些研究转化为公司模板中的尽职调查摘要和估值演示文稿。

In that workflow, GPT‑6 Astra selects documents or Matrix cells to support an answer, then turns the analysis into native PowerPoint slides that follow the user’s structure and guidelines. “Traceability is really important to our users because they’re basing millions of dollars of decisions on what the AI actually comes back with,” Kavita says. “Citations help with traceability as well as giving users confidence that the AI found the right answer.”

在该工作流程中，GPT-6 Astra 选择文档或 Matrix 单元格来支持答案，然后将分析转化为遵循用户结构和指南的原生 PowerPoint 幻灯片。“可追溯性对我们的用户来说非常重要，因为他们是基于人工智能实际返回的内容来做出数百万美元的决策的，”Kavita 说。“引用不仅有助于可追溯性，还能让用户确信人工智能找到了正确的答案。”

In Hebbia’s tests, GPT‑6 Astra combined broader source coverage with highly relevant citations. It scored 13.6 percentage points above the best alternative tested on citing the required types of sources, while 99.5% of the excerpts it cited were relevant to the question. Hebbia tested a prerelease build in August-September 2026, using the same financial research cases and grading criteria for each model. Domain experts wrote or audited the criteria against source documents.

在 Hebbia 的测试中，GPT-6 Astra 将更广泛的来源覆盖范围与高度相关的引用结合在一起。在引用所需类型的来源方面，它的得分比测试的最佳替代方案高出 13.6 个百分点，而其引用的摘录中有 99.5% 与问题相关。Hebbia 在 2026 年 8 月至 9 月期间测试了一个预发布版本，对每个模型使用了相同的金融研究案例和评分标准。领域专家根据源文档编写或审核了这些标准。

“When you’re looking at a data room with thousands of documents, that’s extremely important. Even a single percentage-point gain can yield a lot of marginal benefit,” Kavita says. A presentation also has to look like work the banker would send to a client. “I don’t want to create a deck and send that to my partner that looks like AI made it,” Kavita says. “I want it to be made in my firm’s branding, and I want it to look like a human-made slide.”

“当你查看一个包含数千份文档的数据室时，这一点极其重要。即使是一个百分点的提升也能产生很大的边际效益，”Kavita 说。演示文稿看起来也必须像是银行家会发送给客户的作品。“我不想创建一个看起来像是人工智能制作的演示文稿并发送给我的合伙人，”Kavita 说。“我希望它是用我公司的品牌制作的，并且看起来像是人工制作的幻灯片。”

GPT‑6 Astra led on both following the brief and presentation quality. In Hebbia’s tests, it met 97.9% of the applicable presentation requirements, putting it 14.4 percentage points ahead of the best alternative tested on that measure. Other models traded off polish and adherence to the brief; GPT‑6 Astra ranked first on both. “In the past, we’ve seen models that really shine on one or the other of these dimensions where they’re really good at craft, but the instruction following and adhering to guidelines is not strong, or vice versa. GPT‑6 Astra is the first model we’ve seen where it’s number one on both of those fronts,” Kavita says.

GPT-6 Astra 在遵循简报和演示质量方面均处于领先地位。在 Hebbia 的测试中，它满足了 97.9% 的适用演示要求，在该指标上比测试的最佳替代方案领先 14.4 个百分点。其他模型在润色和遵循简报之间进行了权衡；GPT-6 Astra 在这两方面均排名第一。“过去，我们见过在这些维度中的某一个方面表现出色的模型，它们非常擅长制作，但在遵循指令和遵守准则方面不强，反之亦然。GPT-6 Astra 是我们见过的第一个在这两个方面都排名第一的模型，”Kavita 说。

In Hebbia, users can open a citation to see the underlying document and highlighted passage; slide footnotes lead back to the source material, too. A review could be as specific as checking a customer-concentration figure against the source table and correcting the reporting period before the deck goes to the investment committee. Hebbia wants bankers to spend less time reformatting decks and checking sources, and more time assessing a deal.

在 Hebbia 中，用户可以打开引用以查看基础文档和突出显示的段落；幻灯片脚注也会引导回源材料。审查可以具体到根据源表检查客户集中度数字，并在演示文稿提交给投资委员会之前更正报告期。Hebbia 希望银行家减少在重新格式化演示文稿和检查来源上花费的时间，而将更多时间用于评估交易。

Kavita describes what a better first draft could make possible: “What’s amazing about GPT-6 Astra is its ability to create really precise outputs and artifacts that adhere to user guidelines. You can imagine the hours this will save professionals when you get a deck that is closer to your final output. The overnighters that you have to pull get reduced to a few minutes of reviewing GPT-6 Astra’s output.”—Kavita Anand, Member of Technical Staff, Product, Hebbia

Kavita 描述了更好的初稿可能带来的可能性：“GPT-6 Astra 的神奇之处在于它能够创建非常精确的输出和符合用户指南的工件。你可以想象，当你得到一份更接近最终输出的演示文稿时，这将为专业人士节省多少时间。你必须熬夜完成的工作减少到只需几分钟审查 GPT-6 Astra 的输出。”——Kavita Anand，Hebbia 产品技术人员

A future role for agents. Kavita sees a future role for agents that monitor information in the background and surface signals for finance professionals to assess. “Imagine getting an important signal a few hours before your competitors,” she says. “You can get started thinking about it and see and process more opportunities that a human is not manually hunting. It can unlock a lot of value for our users.” Hebbia wants to build on these results to help finance professionals assess deals with research they can trace back to its sources.

代理的未来角色。Kavita 看到了代理在后台监控信息并为金融专业人士评估提供信号的未来角色。“想象一下，在竞争对手之前几个小时获得重要信号，”她说。“你可以开始思考它，并看到和处理更多人类没有手动寻找的机会。它可以为我们的用户释放巨大的价值。”Hebbia 希望在这些结果的基础上，帮助金融专业人士通过可以追溯到来源的研究来评估交易。
