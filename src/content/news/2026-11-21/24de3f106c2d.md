Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.25001 (cs) [Submitted on 18 Nov 2026] Title: Density Feedback Control for Transferability of Generative Sparse Adversarial Attacks Authors: Dongsu Song, Jay Hoon Jung.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.25001 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 18 日] 标题：用于生成式稀疏对抗攻击可迁移性的密度反馈控制 作者：Dongsu Song, Jay Hoon Jung。

Abstract: Generator-based sparse adversarial attacks train encoder--decoder networks to produce transferable sparse perturbations for black-box robustness evaluation. Their perturbation density, however, is set only indirectly by a fixed sparsity coefficient whose mapping to density is unknown and shifts during training, so reaching a target density requires re-tuning; this fixed pressure also yields little transferability gain once density reduction begins.

摘要：基于生成器的稀疏对抗攻击通过训练编码器-解码器网络，为黑盒鲁棒性评估生成可迁移的稀疏扰动。然而，它们的扰动密度仅通过一个固定的稀疏系数间接设定，该系数与密度的映射关系未知且会在训练过程中发生偏移，因此达到目标密度需要重新调整；一旦开始降低密度，这种固定的压力也几乎无法带来可迁移性的提升。

We propose Density Feedback Control (DFC), which tightens or relaxes this coefficient at the end of each epoch according to the gap between the observed hard-mask density and the target, without changing the generator architecture or loss. At a 10% target density, DFC brings the mean density of three base attacks within one percentage point of the target in a single run and improves the transfer attack success rate (ASR) of two of them on CNN and Transformer targets, including under per-image $\ell_0$ budgets, while perturbing fewer pixels.

我们提出了密度反馈控制（DFC），它在每个 epoch 结束时根据观测到的硬掩码密度与目标之间的差距来收紧或放松该系数，而无需更改生成器架构或损失函数。在 10% 的目标密度下，DFC 在单次运行中将三种基础攻击的平均密度控制在目标值的一个百分点以内，并提高了其中两种攻击在 CNN 和 Transformer 目标上的迁移攻击成功率（ASR），包括在每张图像 $\ell_0$ 预算下的情况，同时扰动的像素更少。

Tracing GPG's training, we observe that as DFC relaxes the coefficient, the transfer ASR recovers with the density and overtakes the fixed-coefficient baseline with fewer pixels. For EGS-TSSA, which restricts perturbations to source-model feature-importance regions, DFC yields no consistent gain; we analyze how this restriction interacts with density feedback.

追踪 GPG 的训练过程，我们观察到随着 DFC 放松系数，迁移 ASR 随密度恢复，并以更少的像素超过了固定系数的基准。对于将扰动限制在源模型特征重要性区域的 EGS-TSSA，DFC 没有产生一致的增益；我们分析了这种限制如何与密度反馈相互作用。
