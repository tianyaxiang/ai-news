Teenage Engineering’s first CD player puts the spinning disc on display.

Teenage Engineering 推出的首款 CD 播放器将旋转的光盘直接展示出来。

The EP/BC–1 can wirelessly stream audio to Bluetooth headphones or multiple devices using Auracast.

EP/BC–1 可以通过 Auracast 将音频无线传输至蓝牙耳机或多个设备。

Teenage Engineering announced what it describes as “a modern take on a classic CD player” with its new EP/BC–1 featuring a slab-like design that leaves the spinning disc exposed instead of hiding it under a lid or inside a slide-out tray. The company says the EP/BC–1 is the first entry in its new broadcast series of products and was designed to bring “physical audio formats into a modern context, evolved from a shared obligation within the company to preserve the formats they grew up with.”

Teenage Engineering 发布了其新款 EP/BC–1，并将其描述为“对经典 CD 播放器的现代诠释”。该设备采用平板式设计，将旋转的光盘直接暴露在外，而不是将其隐藏在盖子下或滑出式托盘中。该公司表示，EP/BC–1 是其全新广播系列产品的首款产品，旨在将“实体音频格式带入现代语境，这是源于公司内部保护他们成长过程中所接触格式的共同使命。”

The EP/BC-1 is available now through Teenage Engineering’s online store for $299. The CD player joins the company’s existing EP–series of audio devices and has the same dimensions as its KO II synthesizer that launched in 2023 so it can be paired with, and connected to, other Teenage Engineering gear including the recently-announced EP-35 battery-powered amp.

EP/BC-1 现已在 Teenage Engineering 在线商店发售，售价为 299 美元。这款 CD 播放器加入了该公司现有的 EP 系列音频设备行列，其尺寸与 2023 年推出的 KO II 合成器相同，因此可以与包括近期发布的 EP-35 电池供电放大器在内的其他 Teenage Engineering 设备进行配对和连接。

Although it’s designed to play a nearly 45 year old medium, the EP/BC-1 features modern conveniences like variable speed playback, cue markers, a jog wheel for forward and reverse search, and built-in beat detection. There’s a very retro-looking series of digital displays showing track, index, time information, and audio levels, and around the edges you’ll find both digital and analog audio connections including a headphone jack and a USB 2.0 port.

尽管它是为播放一种已有近 45 年历史的媒介而设计的，但 EP/BC-1 具备变速播放、提示标记、用于快进和快退的旋钮以及内置节拍检测等现代便利功能。设备上有一系列外观非常复古的数字显示屏，用于显示曲目、索引、时间信息和音频电平；在边缘处，你可以找到包括耳机插孔和 USB 2.0 端口在内的数字和模拟音频接口。

If you want to go completely wireless the CD player has a rechargeable battery and supports both Bluetooth connections to headphones and Auracast streaming to multiple devices that support it. The EP/BC-1 is compatible with standard CDs as well as discs full of MP3 files, and when using the former it can rip tracks to 44.1 Hz/16-bit WAV files. If you want MP3s or other formats, you’ll need to convert them yourself.

如果你想实现完全无线化，这款 CD 播放器内置了可充电电池，并支持连接蓝牙耳机以及通过 Auracast 向多个支持该技术的设备进行流媒体传输。EP/BC-1 兼容标准 CD 以及存有 MP3 文件的光盘；在使用标准 CD 时，它可以将曲目翻录为 44.1 Hz/16-bit 的 WAV 文件。如果你需要 MP3 或其他格式，则需要自行转换。
