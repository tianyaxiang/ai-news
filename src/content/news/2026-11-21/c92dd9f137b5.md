The Federal Communications Commission ordered California to stop enforcing its Carrier of Last Resort (COLR) rules against AT&T, setting up another battle between the Trump administration and California over the state’s right to enforce its own telecom laws and regulations. AT&T’s status as a Carrier of Last Resort in California requires it to offer telephone service to any potential customer in its territory. AT&T wants to stop offering basic phone service to new customers and discontinue service for 184,000 residential customers and 15,000 business customers on June 1, 2027.

美国联邦通信委员会（FCC）下令加利福尼亚州停止对美国电话电报公司（AT&T）执行“最后服务提供商”（COLR）规则，这引发了特朗普政府与加州之间关于该州是否有权执行自身电信法律法规的又一场博弈。AT&T 作为加州“最后服务提供商”的身份，要求其必须向其服务区域内的任何潜在客户提供电话服务。AT&T 希望停止向新客户提供基础电话服务，并计划于 2027 年 6 月 1 日起停止为 18.4 万名住宅客户和 1.5 万名商业客户提供服务。

The order released today granted AT&T’s request to preempt state rules, following up on the FCC’s previous approval of AT&T’s petition to discontinue service. AT&T had tried to achieve the same result in court, but a federal judge rejected its request to override California’s rules in July. The FCC order probably isn’t the last word, as California could sue the commission. California has stood alone against AT&T, which has received relief from COLR obligations in 20 of the 21 states in its wireline service territory.

今天发布的命令批准了 AT&T 提出的优先于州规则的请求，这是继 FCC 此前批准 AT&T 停止服务申请后的后续行动。AT&T 此前曾试图在法庭上实现同样的结果，但一名联邦法官在 7 月份驳回了其推翻加州规则的请求。FCC 的这项命令可能并非最终定论，因为加州可能会起诉该委员会。在 AT&T 有线服务覆盖的 21 个州中，有 20 个州已免除了其 COLR 义务，而加州是唯一坚持对抗 AT&T 的州。

Carr: California rules “designed for a bygone era” “California’s Carrier of Last Resort framework may have made sense when it was established 30 years ago,” FCC Chairman Brendan Carr said in a statement released with today’s order. “But those rules were designed for a bygone era and no longer make sense in today’s competitive, advanced marketplace.” If AT&T ends service on its old copper lines, many homes and businesses would not be offered modern fiber networks. AT&T claims a wireless home phone service that connects to the cellular network will be good enough, and the FCC agreed.

卡尔：加州规则“专为过去时代设计”。FCC 主席布伦丹·卡尔（Brendan Carr）在随今天命令发布的一份声明中表示：“加州的‘最后服务提供商’框架在 30 年前建立时或许有其合理性。但这些规则是为过去的时代设计的，在当今竞争激烈、技术先进的市场中已不再适用。”如果 AT&T 终止其旧铜线服务，许多家庭和企业将无法获得现代光纤网络。AT&T 声称连接到蜂窝网络的无线家庭电话服务已足够使用，FCC 对此表示赞同。

“The FCC has granted AT&T’s applications to replace legacy voice service with a modern, IP-based alternative in certain areas of the state. California’s state requirements are blocking AT&T from proceeding with a federally authorized transition,” Carr said. California says its rules allow wireless replacements if they meet the state’s standards for basic phone service, but that AT&T must demonstrate that its wireless phone service would meet the COLR obligations. California’s requirements include flat-rate options for unlimited voice calls, discounted Lifeline rates for eligible low-income customers, and telephone relay service for people who are deaf and hard of hearing.

卡尔说：“FCC 已批准 AT&T 在加州特定地区用现代化的 IP 替代方案取代传统语音服务的申请。加州的州级要求正在阻碍 AT&T 进行联邦授权的转型。”加州方面表示，如果无线替代方案符合该州的基础电话服务标准，其规则是允许此类替代的，但 AT&T 必须证明其无线电话服务能够满足 COLR 义务。加州的要求包括无限语音通话的统一费率选项、为符合条件的低收入客户提供的折扣“生命线”（Lifeline）费率，以及为聋哑和听障人士提供的电话中继服务。

Gomez: State officials know best. FCC Democrat Anna Gomez voted against the order, saying that state telecom commissions “are often the last line of defense for consumers. They know their communities, they take their complaints, and they answer to the people who rely on these services in a way that a federal agency in Washington cannot always match.” Gomez said the California Public Utilities Commission (CPUC) has an open proceeding on how to modernize its COLR rules, and that the FCC should let that process play out.

戈麦斯：州政府官员最了解情况。FCC 民主党委员安娜·戈麦斯（Anna Gomez）投票反对该命令，称州电信委员会“往往是消费者的最后一道防线。他们了解自己的社区，处理他们的投诉，并以华盛顿的联邦机构无法比拟的方式对依赖这些服务的人民负责。”戈麦斯表示，加州公用事业委员会（CPUC）目前正在就如何实现 COLR 规则现代化进行公开程序，FCC 应让该程序顺利完成。

Large technology transitions work best “when the state regulator is a partner with a real role,” but the FCC is treating the CPUC “as an obstacle to be cleared,” she said. The CPUC “proceeding is the right place for California to weigh what a modern basic service requirement should look like, with input from the people who will actually be making that switch,” Gomez said. “AT&T has said it does not plan to discontinue service before June 1, 2027, which leaves time for the CPUC to finish its work. Rather than wait, this item tells the CPUC to write its new consumer protections around a federal ruling it opposed.”

她说，大型技术转型只有在“州监管机构作为拥有实际角色的合作伙伴”时效果最好，但 FCC 却将 CPUC 视为“需要清除的障碍”。戈麦斯表示，CPUC 的“程序是加州权衡现代基础服务要求应具备何种形态的正确场所，并应听取那些真正将进行转换的人们的意见。AT&T 已表示不打算在 2027 年 6 月 1 日之前停止服务，这为 CPUC 完成其工作留出了时间。该项目非但不等待，反而要求 CPUC 围绕其所反对的联邦裁决来制定新的消费者保护措施。”

In March, the FCC issued an order that made it easier for carriers to discontinue copper networks. The order asserted that state rules are subject to preemption if they conflict with the FCC’s discontinuance authorizations and authority. AT&T cited the order in its lawsuit against California. Trump officials and telecom companies have been trying to block California’s rules for years. The first Trump administration lost an attempt to preempt state net neutrality laws throughout the country in 2019, and California later defeated ISPs in a lawsuit that challenged its net neutrality law in court.

今年 3 月，FCC 发布了一项命令，使运营商更容易停止铜线网络服务。该命令断言，如果州规则与 FCC 的停止服务授权和权力相冲突，则该州规则应被优先取代。AT&T 在其针对加州的诉讼中引用了该命令。特朗普政府官员和电信公司多年来一直试图阻止加州的规则。特朗普第一届政府在 2019 年试图在全国范围内优先于州网络中立性法律的尝试以失败告终，加州随后在法庭上击败了挑战其网络中立性法律的互联网服务提供商（ISP）。

California’s net neutrality law and other telecom regulations are again being threatened by rules the Trump administration applied to federal grants, but California rejected US demands to stop enforcing its laws this month. In that case, California may have to choose between enforcing its telecom laws and receiving $1.42 billion to deploy broadband to 270,571 locations.

加州的网络中立性法律和其他电信法规再次受到特朗普政府应用于联邦拨款的规则的威胁，但加州本月拒绝了美国政府停止执行其法律的要求。在这种情况下，加州可能不得不在执行其电信法律与获得 14.2 亿美元用于在 270,571 个地点部署宽带之间做出选择。
