Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.24960 (cs) [Submitted on 11 Nov 2026] Title: TETHER: Training-Free, Elastic, ToC-Anchored Hierarchy Extraction and Recovery in Design-Driven Long Documents Authors: Yu Zou, Yan Chen, Jialei Zeng, Xiaorui Zhou, Aixi Zhong, Qi Zhou, Haoran You, Ke Jiang, Changyu Hu.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.24960 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 11 日] 标题：TETHER：设计驱动型长文档中无需训练、弹性、基于目录锚定的层级提取与恢复。作者：Yu Zou, Yan Chen, Jialei Zeng, Xiaorui Zhou, Aixi Zhong, Qi Zhou, Haoran You, Ke Jiang, Changyu Hu。

Abstract: Converting complex documents into structured knowledge constitutes the critical infrastructure urgently required for deploying Large Language Models (LLMs) in downstream research. However, blind to the Table of Contents (ToC) as a macro-structure, existing bottom-up paradigms rely on localized visual cues, inducing heuristic fragility in high-entropy layouts where visual prominence decouples from logical depth; meanwhile, direct LLM extraction lacks spatial grounding, triggering hallucinations in long contexts.

摘要：将复杂文档转换为结构化知识，是部署大语言模型（LLM）进行下游研究时迫切需要的关键基础设施。然而，现有的自下而上范式由于忽略了作为宏观结构的目录（ToC），往往依赖于局部视觉线索，这在视觉显著性与逻辑深度脱节的高熵布局中会导致启发式脆弱性；同时，直接的 LLM 提取缺乏空间基础，容易在长上下文中引发幻觉。

To address this, we introduce TETHER, a training-free framework that elevates the ToC from discarded noise to a vital structural prior, enabling robust adaptation across design-driven long documents. TETHER operates via a two-stage neuro-symbolic mechanism: first, it constructs a document skeleton from the raw ToC using a Large Multimodal Model (LMM) empowered by spatio-semantic constraints; second, it employs an anchor-based alignment strategy to tether content blocks to this skeleton, effectively resolving boundary ambiguities.

为了解决这一问题，我们引入了 TETHER，这是一个无需训练的框架，它将目录从被丢弃的噪声提升为至关重要的结构先验，从而实现了对设计驱动型长文档的稳健适应。TETHER 通过一个两阶段的神经符号机制运行：首先，利用受时空语义约束增强的大型多模态模型（LMM），从原始目录构建文档骨架；其次，采用基于锚点的对齐策略将内容块锚定到该骨架上，从而有效解决边界模糊问题。

Evaluated across a multilingual benchmark of ESG reports, product manuals, and annual reports, TETHER delivers highly precise, state-of-the-art (SOTA) zero-shot hierarchy recovery, outperforming deep hierarchical recovery models, dedicated parsers, and general-purpose LMMs. Moreover, stress tests highlight TETHER's resilience to sparse omissions and textual noise, demonstrating that its precise semantic alignment is fundamentally anchored in topological and depth fidelity.

在涵盖 ESG 报告、产品手册和年度报告的多语言基准测试中，TETHER 实现了高精度、最先进（SOTA）的零样本层级恢复，性能优于深度层级恢复模型、专用解析器和通用 LMM。此外，压力测试突显了 TETHER 对稀疏遗漏和文本噪声的鲁棒性，证明了其精确的语义对齐从根本上锚定在拓扑和深度保真度上。

Crucially, ablations confirm the framework's architectural irreducibility, while achieving 90% token reduction and drastic speedups over end-to-end baselines, establishing a compelling equilibrium between structural precision and deployment scalability.

至关重要的是，消融实验证实了该框架架构的不可约性，同时实现了 90% 的 Token 缩减，并比端到端基线模型大幅提升了速度，在结构精度与部署可扩展性之间建立了令人信服的平衡。
