In a swirl of questions, fears, and outrage, the US Army announced Thursday that it would delay a plan to blow up 68 deteriorating barrels of lead azide, which would launch toxic gas and lead into the air. A federal appeals court further halted plans later on Thursday with an emergency order.

在一片质疑、恐惧和愤怒声中，美国陆军周四宣布，将推迟引爆 68 桶正在变质的叠氮化铅的计划，该计划会导致有毒气体和铅释放到空气中。周四晚些时候，一家联邦上诉法院通过紧急命令进一步叫停了该计划。

The plume, containing neurotoxic lead, could potentially drift 100 miles north of the Army installation housing it in Middletown, Iowa, according to modeling released by the Army earlier this week. The Army had planned to carry out two controlled detonations, set off simultaneously around noon Friday, to destroy the barrels where they lie, which is in earth-covered concrete containment structures (called earth-covered magazines or ECMs).

根据陆军本周早些时候发布的模型，含有神经毒性铅的烟羽可能会飘散到位于爱荷华州米德尔敦的陆军设施以北 100 英里处。陆军原计划在周五中午左右同时进行两次受控引爆，以销毁存放在土覆盖混凝土掩体（称为土覆盖弹药库或 ECM）中的这些桶。

Prior to the court stay, the Army rescheduled the blast until November 23 to provide more time to communicate about the situation with Iowans, who have reported being unaware of the detonations until earlier this week. “Public communication is essential to public trust,” Acting Secretary of the Army Adam Telle said in a post on social media at 7:31 pm ET Thursday.

在法院下达暂缓令之前，陆军将爆炸时间重新安排到 11 月 23 日，以便有更多时间与爱荷华州民众沟通，这些民众此前表示直到本周初才得知引爆计划。陆军代理部长亚当·泰勒（Adam Telle）在周四东部时间晚上 7:31 的社交媒体帖子中表示：“公众沟通对于公众信任至关重要。”

“Therefore, the Army will continue to actively engage with state and local officials throughout the region to distribute accurate, up-to-date information.” Telle claimed that the Army could not delay the detonation any further than Monday due to the state of deterioration of the compound, which is extremely explosive and sensitive.

“因此，陆军将继续与该地区的州和地方官员积极接触，以发布准确、最新的信息。”泰勒声称，由于该化合物处于变质状态，极易爆炸且敏感，陆军无法将引爆时间推迟到周一之后。

“This will delay the detonation until the latest possible moment prior to a forecasted temperature drop, which would risk further destabilization of the material.” Lead azide is a compound used in detonators for munitions and is stockpiled by the US military.

“这将把引爆时间推迟到预报气温下降前的最后一刻，因为气温下降可能会导致材料进一步不稳定。”叠氮化铅是一种用于弹药雷管的化合物，由美军储备。

In March, the US Army identified that the stockpile in the Iowa facility—the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant (IAAAP)—had deteriorated. The lead azide in the 68 barrels, reported to total around 9,700 pounds (4.4 tons), is suspended in a mixture of water and ethanol within the ECMs, but has degraded and crystallized over time.

今年 3 月，美国陆军发现爱荷华州设施——爱荷华陆军弹药厂（IAAAP）——的库存已经变质。据报道，这 68 桶叠氮化铅总重约 9,700 磅（4.4 吨），悬浮在 ECM 内的水和乙醇混合物中，但随着时间的推移已经降解并结晶。

Temperature fluctuations can cause lead azide to dissolve into the water/alcohol solution and recrystallize into needles or platelets, the Army said. In crystallized forms, lead azide becomes extremely sensitive to any physical shock, friction, or heat. The Army determined the current state is a “catastrophically hazardous condition.”

陆军表示，温度波动会导致叠氮化铅溶解在水/酒精溶液中，并重新结晶成针状或片状。在结晶状态下，叠氮化铅对任何物理冲击、摩擦或热量都极其敏感。陆军认定目前的状态是“灾难性的危险状况”。

In an environmental assessment released Wednesday, the Army said that trying to move the lead azide from its current position for any reason was ruled out. Any sloshing or physical shock from loading, transporting, or burying the barrels for safer detonation would create “an unacceptable safety risk and a high probability of accidental, uncontrolled detonation.”

在周三发布的一份环境评估中，陆军表示，出于任何原因试图将叠氮化铅从当前位置移走都是不可行的。在装载、运输或掩埋这些桶以进行更安全引爆的过程中，任何晃动或物理冲击都会造成“不可接受的安全风险，并极有可能导致意外的、失控的爆炸”。

The military considered neutralizing the lead azide using a chemical reagent, but again ruled it out because it said the process would require handling the lead azide over an extended period, with barrels neutralized at a rate of 3 to 10 per day.

军方曾考虑使用化学试剂中和叠氮化铅，但再次排除了这一方案，因为该过程需要长时间处理叠氮化铅，每天只能中和 3 到 10 桶。

“The chemical neutralization process requires significantly more ‘hands-on’ time and physical manipulation of the drums by personnel over a prolonged 7- to 22-day period, drastically increasing the probability of accidental, fatal detonation during handling,” the Army wrote in its assessment.

陆军在评估中写道：“化学中和过程需要人员在长达 7 到 22 天的时间内进行更多的‘手工’操作和对桶的物理处理，这大大增加了处理过程中发生意外致命爆炸的可能性。”

In all, the Army considered a controlled, mitigated “Blow-in-Place” plan the best option, and it specifically planned for a fall detonation. The Army said the fall weather would “take advantage of periods of reduced thermal cycling and to ensure the lowest impact to facility operations and local agriculture (harvesting).”

总而言之，陆军认为受控、缓解性的“原地引爆”计划是最佳选择，并特别计划在秋季进行引爆。陆军表示，秋季天气将“利用热循环减少的时期，并确保对设施运营和当地农业（收获）的影响降至最低”。

According to an emergency permit for the detonation from the Environmental Protection Agency, the plan is to place explosive charges and an accelerant on or near each of the ECMs. The Army intends to try to mitigate the detonation’s impact with a few simple methods.

根据环境保护局颁发的紧急引爆许可证，计划是在每个 ECM 上或附近放置爆炸装药和助燃剂。陆军打算尝试通过几种简单的方法来减轻引爆的影响。

Those include lime-fill and sand-fill barriers around the ECMs, water cannons and misters to control lead dust in the wake of the explosion, and timing the detonation with ideal weather conditions—avoiding high winds that could increase the area exposed to the plume or, conversely, a thermal inversion that could trap the plume in one place.

这些方法包括在 ECM 周围设置石灰填充和沙子填充屏障，使用水炮和喷雾器在爆炸后控制铅尘，并选择理想的天气条件进行引爆——避免可能扩大烟羽暴露区域的大风，或者相反，避免可能将烟羽困在某一地点的逆温现象。

Modeling released Wednesday—based on the conditions for the scheduled detonation on Friday—suggested the plume could deposit lead dust at an amount between 5 and 100 micrograms per square foot in a stretch up to 25 miles north, nearly to Wapello, Iowa. Those levels are above those considered hazardous for floors and windows, respectively.

周三发布的模型——基于周五预定引爆的条件——显示，烟羽可能会在向北延伸 25 英里（几乎到达爱荷华州瓦佩洛）的范围内，以每平方英尺 5 到 100 微克的量沉积铅尘。这些水平分别高于被认为对地板和窗户有害的水平。

Meanwhile, airborne lead, at a level of 0.18 micrograms per cubic meter, would reach nearly 100 miles north, toward Cedar Rapids, Iowa. The EPA standard for lead levels in the air is 0.15 micrograms per cubic meter.

与此同时，空气中的铅含量为每立方米 0.18 微克，将向北扩散近 100 英里，到达爱荷华州锡达拉皮兹。美国环保署空气中铅含量的标准为每立方米 0.15 微克。

During the detonation, the Army advised residents in the immediate area to consider leaving or at least staying indoors with windows closed. It also recommended people wash their cars if they park outside. Similarly, local fire departments reportedly planned to hose down children’s playgrounds to remove any coating of lead dust in the aftermath, and schools planned closures.

在引爆期间，陆军建议附近地区的居民考虑离开，或者至少留在室内并关好窗户。它还建议人们如果将车停在室外，请清洗车辆。同样，据报道，当地消防部门计划在事后用水冲洗儿童游乐场以清除任何铅尘覆盖物，学校也计划停课。

The Army’s plan was to communicate clearly with the public about the plans, including holding an open-house event on October 15. But while Middletown residents may have been aware of the detonations, Iowans in the projected path of the plume were surprised by the news this week.

陆军的计划是就这些计划与公众进行清晰的沟通，包括在 10 月 15 日举行开放日活动。但是，虽然米德尔敦的居民可能已经知道引爆计划，但处于烟羽预计路径上的爱荷华州民众对本周的消息感到惊讶。

In its environmental risk assessment, the Army said it planned to mitigate the lead fallout with “rapid excavation” of soil and dust and treat it with a “stabilization agent.” This was to prevent it from remaining in the environment of the military facility, from which it could drift or make its way into groundwater. But the efforts would not mitigate the airborne plume, and lead does not break down in the environment.

在其环境风险评估中，陆军表示计划通过对土壤和灰尘进行“快速挖掘”并使用“稳定剂”处理来减轻铅沉降的影响。这是为了防止其残留在军事设施的环境中，从而飘散或进入地下水。但这些努力无法减轻空气中的烟羽，而且铅在环境中不会分解。
