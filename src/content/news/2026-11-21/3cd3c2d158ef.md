计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.25126 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 18 日] 标题：TALD：用于跨模态知识蒸馏的可迁移性感知 Logit 解耦。作者：Hui Li, Pengfei Yang, Xinyi Feng, Junhong Liu, Tianyang Zheng, Hui Zeng, Le Dong, Kaibing Zhang, Mengting Zhao, Quan Wang。

Abstract: Cross-modal knowledge distillation can incur negative transfer when source-modality supervision conflicts with the target modality's discriminative characteristics. We propose TALD, a two-stage framework for Transferability-Aware Logit Disentanglement in class-aligned logit space. Guided by task-dependent self-benefiting and cross-modal exclusivity criteria, a Residual Disentangler decomposes logits from a teacher and an independently trained target-modality reference student into modality-general and modality-specific components.

摘要：当源模态监督与目标模态的判别特征冲突时，跨模态知识蒸馏可能会导致负迁移。我们提出了 TALD，这是一个用于类对齐 Logit 空间中可迁移性感知 Logit 解耦的两阶段框架。在任务相关的自益准则和跨模态排他性准则的指导下，残差解耦器将来自教师模型和独立训练的目标模态参考学生模型的 Logit 分解为模态通用组件和模态特定组件。

A Sample-Adaptive Modulator regulates decomposition constraints and sample-specific fusion weights. The learned modality-general components are then adaptively fused to supervise the final student. Across five benchmarks spanning vision, audio, and text, TALD achieves the largest mean accuracy gain and highest positive-transfer frequency among the compared methods.

样本自适应调制器调节分解约束和样本特定的融合权重。随后，学习到的模态通用组件被自适应地融合，以监督最终的学生模型。在涵盖视觉、音频和文本的五个基准测试中，TALD 在所有对比方法中实现了最大的平均准确率提升和最高的正迁移频率。

Across ten transfer directions with five runs each, TALD improves over training without distillation in 84% of evaluations, compared with at most 70% for the baselines, while offering a favorable accuracy-efficiency trade-off. We plan to release the code.

在十个迁移方向（每个方向运行五次）的测试中，TALD 在 84% 的评估中优于不使用蒸馏的训练，而基准方法最高仅为 70%，同时提供了良好的准确率与效率权衡。我们计划发布代码。
