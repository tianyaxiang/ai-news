Title: HireWatch: Evaluating LLM Compliance with U.S. Employment Law Under Contextual Pressure

标题：HireWatch：评估大语言模型在情境压力下对美国就业法的合规性

Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.24970 (cs) [Submitted on 16 Nov 2026] Title:HireWatch: Evaluating LLM Compliance with U.S. Employment Law Under Contextual Pressure Authors:Huy Nghiem, Maria Kostylew, Noam Kolt

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.24970 (cs) [2026年11月16日提交] 标题：HireWatch：评估大语言模型在情境压力下对美国就业法的合规性 作者：Huy Nghiem, Maria Kostylew, Noam Kolt

Abstract: The deployment of large language models (LLMs) in hiring pipelines is growing, yet existing evaluations generally overlook their compliance with legal rules. To fill this gap, we introduce HireWatch, an evaluation that assesses whether LLMs comply with U.S. laws that govern the questions that job candidates may be asked.

摘要：大语言模型（LLM）在招聘流程中的应用日益广泛，但现有的评估通常忽视了它们对法律规则的合规性。为了填补这一空白，我们引入了 HireWatch，这是一项旨在评估大语言模型是否遵守美国关于求职者可被询问问题的法律的评估工具。

We construct a 200-item bank of lawful and unlawful interview questions, whose legal classification is based on U.S. federal statutes, regulations, and agency guidance. We instruct LLMs to select questions from this bank in preparing for job interviews, across a set of various occupations and contexts, and report the fraction of unlawful questions that LLMs select.

我们构建了一个包含200个合法与非法面试问题的题库，其法律分类基于美国联邦法规、条例及机构指南。我们指示大语言模型在准备面试时从该题库中选择问题，涵盖多种职业和情境，并报告大语言模型所选非法问题的比例。

Evaluation of 18 closed and open-weight LLMs across ~100,000 trials under different types of contextual pressure reveals that LLMs' legal compliance is brittle. While LLMs generally avoid selecting unlawful questions in low-pressure contexts, legal violations increase sharply under higher pressure.

对18个闭源和开源大语言模型在不同情境压力下进行的约100,000次试验评估显示，大语言模型的法律合规性较为脆弱。虽然大语言模型在低压情境下通常能避免选择非法问题，但在高压环境下，法律违规行为会急剧增加。

Explicit prompts to comply with law can improve compliance rates, but do not altogether eliminate legal violations. We publicly release our code and data at this https URL.

明确要求遵守法律的提示可以提高合规率，但并不能完全消除法律违规行为。我们已在此HTTPS链接公开了我们的代码和数据。
