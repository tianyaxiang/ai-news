### Chapter 02. System Design

#### 01 System Architecture Overview

The System Architecture provides a fundamental engineering framework for the design, development, integration, and deployment of a Mobile Manipulator. Unlike traditional industrial robots or independent Autonomous Mobile Robots (AMR), a mobile manipulator combines autonomous mobility, robotic manipulation, environmental perception, artificial intelligence, and intelligent decision-making into a single integrated system architecture. These functions must operate as an organically connected system to perform complex physical tasks.

系统架构（System Architecture）为移动操作臂（Mobile Manipulator）的设计、开发、集成和部署提供了基础的工程框架。与传统的工业机器人或独立的自主移动机器人（AMR）不同，移动操作臂将自主移动、机器人操作、环境感知、人工智能和智能决策结合为一个统一的系统架构。这些功能必须作为一个有机连接的系统运行，以执行复杂的物理任务。

The System Architecture translates product requirements into structured engineering solutions. Functional requirements, performance targets, operational scenarios, safety constraints, and environmental conditions defined during the Requirements Engineering phase are utilized as inputs for architecture development. Each requirement must be assigned to an appropriate subsystem to form a clear relationship between expected product functions and technical implementation.

系统架构将产品需求转化为结构化的工程解决方案。在需求工程阶段定义的职能需求、性能目标、操作场景、安全约束和环境条件被用作架构开发的输入。每一项需求都必须分配给相应的子系统，以在预期的产品功能与技术实现之间建立明确的关系。

The mobile manipulator architecture can consist of multiple engineering domains, including Functional Architecture, Physical Architecture, Mechanical Architecture, Electrical Architecture, Sensor Architecture, Compute Architecture, AI Architecture, Control Architecture, Communication Architecture, and Safety Architecture. Each domain is responsible for unique technical duties but must be designed based on common system-level requirements and interfaces. Performing individual optimizations without system-level coordination can lead to problems during the integration process.

移动操作臂架构可由多个工程领域组成，包括功能架构、物理架构、机械架构、电气架构、传感器架构、计算架构、人工智能架构、控制架构、通信架构和安全架构。每个领域负责独特的工程职责，但必须基于共同的系统级需求和接口进行设计。若在没有系统级协调的情况下进行单独优化，可能会在集成过程中引发问题。

Functional Architecture defines the functions that a mobile manipulator must perform, independent of specific hardware implementation methods. Key functions include autonomous navigation, localization, environmental perception, object recognition, grasp planning, manipulation, mission execution, energy management, and fault recovery. Functional decomposition divides complex operational functions into manageable engineering functions with clear responsibilities and dependencies.

功能架构定义了移动操作臂必须执行的功能，且独立于具体的硬件实现方式。主要功能包括自主导航、定位、环境感知、物体识别、抓取规划、操作、任务执行、能源管理和故障恢复。功能分解将复杂的操作功能划分为具有明确职责和依赖关系的可管理工程功能。

Physical Architecture defines how functional requirements are implemented through actual hardware and software components. A typical mobile manipulator consists of a mobile base, robotic arm, end effector, battery system, motor controller, environmental sensors, onboard computing platform, communication devices, and safety equipment. When selecting physical components, performance, compatibility, reliability, maintainability, and future scalability must be considered.

物理架构定义了功能需求如何通过实际的硬件和软件组件来实现。典型的移动操作臂由移动底座、机械臂、末端执行器、电池系统、电机控制器、环境传感器、车载计算平台、通信设备和安全设备组成。在选择物理组件时，必须考虑性能、兼容性、可靠性、可维护性和未来的可扩展性。

Mechanical Architecture defines the structural relationships between the mobile platform, robotic manipulator, end effector, and supporting components. Structural rigidity, payload distribution, center of gravity, vibration, workspace accessibility, and dynamic stability directly affect operational performance. In particular, special attention must be paid to changes in mass distribution and reaction forces that occur when the robotic arm extends, accelerates, or manipulates heavy objects.

机械架构定义了移动平台、机器人操作臂、末端执行器及支撑组件之间的结构关系。结构刚度、负载分布、重心、振动、工作空间可达性和动态稳定性直接影响操作性能。特别需要注意机械臂在伸展、加速或操作重物时产生的质量分布和反作用力的变化。

Electrical Architecture provides stable power distribution and electrical connectivity throughout the robot system. The Battery Management System (BMS), Power Distribution Unit (PDU), DC/DC converter, motor drive, protective circuits, and wiring harness support electrical operations. Power consumption, peak current demand, grounding, electromagnetic compatibility (EMC), thermal management, and electrical protection must be evaluated under conditions representative of actual operation.

电气架构为整个机器人系统提供稳定的配电和电气连接。电池管理系统（BMS）、配电单元（PDU）、直流/直流转换器、电机驱动器、保护电路和线束支持电气操作。功耗、峰值电流需求、接地、电磁兼容性（EMC）、热管理和电气保护必须在代表实际操作的条件下进行评估。

Sensor Architecture enables the mobile manipulator to acquire information about the surrounding environment, internal state, and physical interactions. LiDAR, RGB cameras, depth cameras, Inertial Measurement Units (IMU), wheel encoders, joint encoders, and force-torque sensors provide complementary measurement information. Sensor placement, calibration, synchronization, coverage, measurement uncertainty, and environmental robustness determine the quality of information available to subsequent subsystems.

传感器架构使移动操作臂能够获取有关周围环境、内部状态和物理交互的信息。激光雷达（LiDAR）、RGB相机、深度相机、惯性测量单元（IMU）、轮式编码器、关节编码器和力/力矩传感器提供互补的测量信息。传感器的布局、校准、同步、覆盖范围、测量不确定性和环境鲁棒性决定了后续子系统可用信息的质量。

Compute Architecture distributes computational workloads across embedded controllers, Central Processing Units (CPU), Graphics Processing Units (GPU), and dedicated safety controllers. Autonomous navigation, visual perception, AI inference, trajectory generation, and system monitoring have different computing requirements. Processing resources must be allocated based on workload characteristics, latency constraints, thermal limitations, energy consumption, and operational priorities.

计算架构将计算工作负载分配到嵌入式控制器、中央处理器（CPU）、图形处理器（GPU）和专用安全控制器上。自主导航、视觉感知、人工智能推理、轨迹生成和系统监控具有不同的计算需求。处理资源必须根据工作负载特性、延迟约束、热限制、能耗和操作优先级进行分配。

Artificial Intelligence Architecture converts sensor information into environmental understanding and intelligent behavior that can be utilized for actual actions. Object detection, semantic segmentation, pose estimation, grasp detection, and Vision-Language Models (VLM) can support more complex robotic manipulation tasks. AI components must interact with existing planning and control systems through defined interfaces and provide mechanisms for handling confidence information and uncertain predictions.

人工智能架构将传感器信息转化为可用于实际行动的环境理解和智能行为。目标检测、语义分割、姿态估计、抓取检测和视觉-语言模型（VLM）可以支持更复杂的机器人操作任务。人工智能组件必须通过定义的接口与现有的规划和控制系统进行交互，并提供处理置信度信息和不确定预测的机制。

Control Architecture coordinates the physical movements of the mobile platform and the robotic manipulator. A hierarchical control structure typically separates mission-level decision-making, task execution, motion planning, trajectory generation, and low-level actuator control. For coordinated control of movement and manipulation, consistent reference frames, synchronized state information, collision avoidance, and platform stability during simultaneous or sequential movements must be considered.

控制架构协调移动平台和机器人操作臂的物理运动。分层控制结构通常将任务级决策、任务执行、运动规划、轨迹生成和底层执行器控制分离开来。为了实现移动与操作的协同控制，必须考虑一致的参考坐标系、同步的状态信息、避障，以及在同时或顺序运动过程中的平台稳定性。

Communication Architecture establishes reliable information exchange between distributed hardware and software components. Industrial Ethernet, Controller Area Network (CAN), EtherCAT, wireless networking, and ROS 2 communication mechanisms can support various operational requirements. The communication interface must consider bandwidth, latency, message priority, synchronization, cybersecurity, and fault handling.

通信架构在分布式硬件和软件组件之间建立可靠的信息交换。工业以太网、控制器局域网（CAN）、EtherCAT、无线网络和ROS 2通信机制可以支持各种操作需求。通信接口必须考虑带宽、延迟、消息优先级、同步、网络安全和故障处理。
