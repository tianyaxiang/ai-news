Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.23456 (cs) [Submitted on 17 Nov 2026] Title: VeriMath: Human-in-the-Loop Generation and Verification of Curriculum-Aligned Mathematics Assessment Items.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.23456 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 17 日] 标题：VeriMath：课程对齐数学评估项目的“人在回路”生成与验证。

Abstract: Automatic generation of mathematics assessment items is a growing area in educational artificial intelligence, yet classroom deployment remains challenging. Generated questions must satisfy multiple constraints: mathematical correctness, curriculum alignment, appropriate cognitive difficulty, and educator reviewability.

摘要：数学评估项目的自动生成是教育人工智能领域一个不断发展的方向，但其在课堂中的部署仍然充满挑战。生成的题目必须满足多重约束：数学正确性、课程对齐、适当的认知难度以及教育工作者的可审查性。

While prior work has advanced text generation, reliable assessment authoring requires granular control over localized examination formats and expert educational judgment. To address this challenge, we present VeriMath, a generalized, human-in-the-loop framework for generating rigorous, curriculum-constrained mathematics assessment drafts.

尽管先前的研究在文本生成方面取得了进展，但可靠的评估命题需要对本地化考试格式进行精细控制，并结合专业的教育判断。为了应对这一挑战，我们提出了 VeriMath，这是一个通用的“人在回路”框架，用于生成严谨且符合课程约束的数学评估草稿。

Using annotated reference items, the framework anchors generation to the requested examination section and cognitive reasoning level, then applies sequential automated verification and guided repair before submitting drafts for expert review.

该框架利用标注过的参考题目，将生成过程锚定在要求的考试部分和认知推理水平上，随后在提交给专家评审前，应用顺序自动化验证和引导式修复。

We evaluate VeriMath through a national curriculum case study using 4,555 mathematics items from the Vietnamese Grade 12 curriculum and 50 distinct generation requirements covering multiple-choice, true/false, and short-answer formats.

我们通过一项国家课程案例研究对 VeriMath 进行了评估，该研究使用了越南 12 年级课程中的 4,555 道数学题，以及涵盖选择题、判断题和简答题格式的 50 项不同生成需求。

Across two model backbones, the framework produces verifier-accepted drafts for 94% (47/50) of requirements per run. Blind expert evaluations confirm high mathematical accuracy and curriculum alignment, with strict teacher-readiness assessments accepting up to 56.8% of generated items.

在两个模型主干上，该框架在每次运行中能为 94% (47/50) 的需求生成经验证器接受的草稿。盲测专家评估证实了其高数学准确性和课程对齐度，严格的教师就绪评估接受了高达 56.8% 的生成题目。

Analysis of rejected drafts reveals persistent domain-agnostic challenges involving formatting, statement validity, and solution-step completeness. These findings demonstrate that VeriMath can bridge generative AI and dependable educational applications by producing highly reviewable assessment drafts within tightly constrained national curriculum frameworks.

对被拒绝草稿的分析揭示了在格式、陈述有效性和解题步骤完整性方面存在的持续性领域无关挑战。这些发现表明，VeriMath 通过在严格的国家课程框架内生成高度可审查的评估草稿，能够架起生成式人工智能与可靠教育应用之间的桥梁。
