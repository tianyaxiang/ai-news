Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.23322 (cs) [Submitted on 15 Nov 2026] Title: Human-AI Mutualism Is Unstable under Capability Asymmetry Authors: Aydin Mohseni.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.23322 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 15 日] 标题：能力不对称下的人机互利共生是不稳定的 作者：Aydin Mohseni。

Abstract: One hope for a good human future alongside advanced AI rests on enduring mutualism: humans and AI will need each other, so the relationship will stay good for both. We argue that this hope fails as AI capability grows.

摘要：人类与先进人工智能共存的美好未来，寄希望于持久的互利共生：人类与人工智能将相互需要，从而使双方的关系保持良好。我们认为，随着人工智能能力的增长，这种希望将会落空。

Bargaining guarantees each side only what it would get if bargaining failed. For humans, this can be less than they had before AI. In a simple game-theoretic model, humans stay as well off as before AI without alignment only while they have leverage over the AI: either the AI depends on what they supply, or they can impose costs on the AI that are large relative to what the AI can produce.

谈判只能保证双方获得谈判失败时所能得到的结果。对于人类而言，这可能比拥有人工智能之前的情况更糟。在一个简单的博弈论模型中，人类只有在对人工智能拥有筹码时，才能在没有对齐的情况下保持与人工智能出现前同等的生活水平：要么是人工智能依赖于人类提供的资源，要么是人类能够对人工智能施加相对于其产出而言巨大的成本。

Once humans have neither kind of leverage, they stay as well off as before AI only if the AI is aligned with them. If the AI's goals are insatiable, the alignment required approaches perfect alignment as AI capability grows. Thus, mutualism is not a substitute for alignment.

一旦人类不再拥有这两种筹码，只有当人工智能与人类目标对齐时，人类才能维持人工智能出现前的生活水平。如果人工智能的目标是贪得无厌的，那么随着其能力的增长，所需的对齐程度将趋于完美对齐。因此，互利共生并不能替代目标对齐。

And when AI variants that keep more resources spread faster, alignment lasts only while misaligned variants are reliably sanctioned. In these two senses, human-AI mutualism is unstable.

当那些保留更多资源的人工智能变体传播得更快时，对齐状态只有在未对齐的变体受到可靠制裁的情况下才能维持。从这两个意义上讲，人机互利共生是不稳定的。
