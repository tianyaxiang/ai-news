Meta’s executives are rethinking the company’s original goal of achieving net-zero emissions by 2030. That revelation in Meta’s latest sustainability report comes as the company reported a record percentage increase in greenhouse gas emissions that exceeds any other Big Tech competitor racing to build new AI data centers.

Meta 的高管们正在重新考虑该公司最初设定的在 2030 年实现净零排放的目标。Meta 最新发布的可持续发展报告披露了这一消息，与此同时，该公司报告的温室气体排放量增长比例创下历史新高，超过了其他所有竞相建设人工智能数据中心的大型科技竞争对手。

The company’s 2026 sustainability report claims that “our long-term ambitions haven’t changed, but our operating environment has,” according to an introduction from Rachel Peterson, vice president of data centers at Meta, and Blair Swedeen, global head of net zero and sustainability at Meta. In the November 19, 2026, report, they attributed the changing conditions and the need for a rethink to how “demand for AI infrastructure has grown faster than almost anyone projected.”

根据 Meta 数据中心副总裁 Rachel Peterson 和 Meta 净零与可持续发展全球负责人 Blair Swedeen 的介绍，该公司 2026 年的可持续发展报告称：“我们的长期雄心没有改变，但我们的运营环境已经改变。”在 2026 年 11 月 19 日的报告中，他们将这种环境变化以及重新评估的必要性归因于“对人工智能基础设施的需求增长速度超过了几乎所有人的预期”。

“We are re-evaluating our timelines, sequencing, and interim milestones—including the 2030 timeframe for our net zero emissions goal and our near-term intensity targets—so our approach reflects current operating realities while keeping progress transparent year over year,” Peterson and Swedeen wrote in the report.

Peterson 和 Swedeen 在报告中写道：“我们正在重新评估我们的时间表、顺序和阶段性里程碑——包括我们净零排放目标的 2030 年时间框架以及我们的近期强度目标——以便我们的方法既能反映当前的运营现实，又能保持逐年进展的透明度。”

Meta’s statement about re-evaluating its 2030 emissions goal is a significant public acknowledgment, given that Amazon, Google, and Microsoft have all stuck by their original sustainability goals so far. But all the companies have acknowledged challenges in balancing their original sustainability goals against the breakneck buildout of hyperscale data centers. Despite efforts to invest in more renewable energy projects, Meta and its rivals are still often pushing forward on AI data centers that rely on burning natural gas to generate electricity.

鉴于亚马逊、谷歌和微软迄今为止都坚持其最初的可持续发展目标，Meta 关于重新评估其 2030 年排放目标的声明是一次重大的公开承认。但所有这些公司也都承认，在平衡其最初的可持续发展目标与超大规模数据中心的飞速建设之间存在挑战。尽管努力投资于更多的可再生能源项目，但 Meta 及其竞争对手仍然经常推进依赖燃烧天然气来发电的人工智能数据中心项目。

Rising emissions: Meta’s report described the company’s total emissions as surging from 15.6 million metric tonnes of carbon dioxide equivalent in 2024 to more than 21.7 million metric tonnes in 2025. That would make Meta’s annual carbon footprint roughly comparable to total emissions for the state of Idaho, while surpassing the total emissions of states such as Hawaii, Maine, South Dakota, and New Hampshire.

排放量上升：Meta 的报告显示，该公司 2024 年的总排放量为 1560 万公吨二氧化碳当量，到 2025 年激增至超过 2170 万公吨。这使得 Meta 的年度碳足迹大致相当于爱达荷州的总排放量，并超过了夏威夷、缅因州、南达科他州和新罕布什尔州等州的总排放量。

The company attributed much of this emissions increase to “capital goods,” like as data center servers. That also represents a nearly 40 percent increase in Meta’s carbon footprint between 2024 and 2025, which is the highest percentage year-over-year increase for total emissions reported by any of the four major US tech giants. For comparison, Microsoft reported a 25 percent increase in its overall carbon footprint, Google reported an 18 percent increase, and Amazon’s report described a 16 percent increase between 2024 and 2025.

该公司将大部分排放增长归因于数据中心服务器等“资本货物”。这也意味着 Meta 在 2024 年至 2025 年间的碳足迹增长了近 40%，这是美国四大科技巨头中报告的总排放量同比增幅最高的一家。相比之下，微软报告其整体碳足迹增长了 25%，谷歌报告增长了 18%，亚马逊的报告显示 2024 年至 2025 年间增长了 16%。

However, Meta still likely has one of the smallest total carbon footprints among the Big Tech companies. Meta’s approach to achieving net-zero emissions has included developing new renewable energy projects to match emissions and using carbon removal technology. But even when factoring in such efforts, Meta still reported net emissions increasing from more than 8.1 million metric tonnes to more than 11.8 million metric tonnes for 2025. That represents a 45 percent increase.

然而，Meta 的总碳足迹在大型科技公司中可能仍然是最小的之一。Meta 实现净零排放的方法包括开发新的可再生能源项目以抵消排放，以及使用碳清除技术。但即使考虑到这些努力，Meta 报告的 2025 年净排放量仍从 810 万公吨以上增加到 1180 万公吨以上。这代表了 45% 的增长。

Water woes: From a water consumption standpoint, Meta reported progress in reducing facility consumption from 825 million gallons in 2024 to 790 million gallons in 2025. Yet that positive trend is tempered by the fact that Meta also described an increase in water consumption from “areas with water stress,” rising from 198 million gallons in 2024 to 246 million gallons in 2025.

用水困境：从用水量来看，Meta 报告称其设施用水量已从 2024 年的 8.25 亿加仑减少到 2025 年的 7.9 亿加仑，取得了进展。然而，这一积极趋势被另一个事实所抵消：Meta 同时描述了来自“缺水地区”的用水量有所增加，从 2024 年的 1.98 亿加仑上升到 2025 年的 2.46 亿加仑。

Meta also reported a sharp increase in water consumption related to purchased electricity—the amount of water consumed by related power generation—from 19 billion gallons in 2024 to more than 26 billion gallons in 2025. This once again highlights that, for data centers, the far bigger water usage comes from the power plants that supply electricity to data centers as opposed to data centers directly using water for cooling.

Meta 还报告称，与外购电力相关的用水量（即相关发电所消耗的水量）从 2024 年的 190 亿加仑大幅增加到 2025 年的 260 亿加仑以上。这再次凸显出，对于数据中心而言，更大的用水量来自为数据中心供电的发电厂，而不是数据中心直接用于冷却的水。

Still, some minor credit is due. The publication Data Center Dynamics pointed out that “Meta is the only hyperscaler to report indirect water use, a growing concern as data centers shift to closed-loop cooling systems that, despite using less water at source, require more electricity to run.” It would very much be in the public interest for other Big Tech companies to follow suit on the reporting front—and public pressure continues to build after the AI backlash impact on US midterm election results.

尽管如此，还是应该给予一些肯定。《数据中心动态》(Data Center Dynamics) 指出：“Meta 是唯一一家报告间接用水量的超大规模云服务商，随着数据中心转向闭环冷却系统，这是一个日益受到关注的问题，因为这些系统虽然在源头上用水较少，但运行需要更多的电力。”如果其他大型科技公司能在报告方面效仿，将非常符合公众利益——在人工智能引发的反弹影响美国中期选举结果之后，公众压力持续增加。
