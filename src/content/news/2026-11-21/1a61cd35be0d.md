Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.23365 (cs) [Submitted on 17 Nov 2026] Title: Training Language Models for Introspective Oversight Authors: Alex Lin Wang, Linus Yifeng Tang, Dami Choi, Neil Chowdhury.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.23365 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 17 日] 标题：训练用于内省式监督的语言模型 作者：Alex Lin Wang, Linus Yifeng Tang, Dami Choi, Neil Chowdhury。

Abstract: Language models increasingly exhibit behaviors that are difficult to detect in transcripts, such as reward hacking, deception, and sycophancy. Asking the assistant follow-up questions about its actions is unreliable, since models are generally not trained to report their own misbehavior. As a result, approaches to oversight typically involve querying external models. These face limitations: external transcript monitoring operates only on visible model outputs, and activation decoders lack full context and are prone to confabulation.

摘要：语言模型日益表现出在文本记录中难以察觉的行为，例如奖励作弊、欺骗和谄媚。询问助手关于其行为的后续问题是不可靠的，因为模型通常并未经过报告自身不当行为的训练。因此，监督方法通常涉及查询外部模型。这些方法面临局限性：外部文本监控仅针对可见的模型输出进行操作，而激活解码器缺乏完整上下文且容易产生幻觉。

We introduce introspective oversight, which trains a new role in the model to answer arbitrary questions about the assistant, rewarding answers that are verified against the assistant's actual behavior. We implement this by adding a separate, third-person observer role that answers these queries, which we can train solely for accuracy and separately from the assistant.

我们引入了内省式监督，通过训练模型中的一个新角色来回答关于助手的任意问题，并对根据助手实际行为验证后的答案给予奖励。我们通过增加一个独立的第三方观察者角色来实现这一点，该角色负责回答这些查询，我们可以将其与助手分开，仅针对准确性进行训练。

On five held-out oversight evaluations, an observer trained on Qwen3.6-27B obtains a mean AUROC of 0.907, outperforming honesty training (0.864), transcript monitoring (0.828), and activation oracles (0.796). We further find that training an observer makes the assistant itself easier to monitor, with every baseline method improving on average when applied to the fine-tuned assistant. These results suggest that training models to answer verifiable questions about themselves is a tractable approach to overseeing unintended behavior.

在五个留出的监督评估中，基于 Qwen3.6-27B 训练的观察者获得了 0.907 的平均 AUROC，优于诚实训练（0.864）、文本监控（0.828）和激活预言机（0.796）。我们进一步发现，训练观察者使助手本身更容易被监控，当应用于经过微调的助手时，所有基准方法平均都有所改进。这些结果表明，训练模型回答关于其自身的可验证问题是监督非预期行为的一种可行方法。
