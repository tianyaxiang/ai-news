Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.25011 (cs) [Submitted on 18 Nov 2026] Title: Near-Quadratic Sample Compression for All VC Classes Authors: Yang Cai, Vineet Gupta, Yanchen Jiang, Christopher Liaw, Aranyak Mehta, Shay Moran, Grigoris Velegkas, Di Wang, Manfred K. Warmuth.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.25011 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 18 日] 标题：所有 VC 类的近二次样本压缩 作者：Yang Cai, Vineet Gupta, Yanchen Jiang, Christopher Liaw, Aranyak Mehta, Shay Moran, Grigoris Velegkas, Di Wang, Manfred K. Warmuth。

Abstract: The sample compression conjecture is a longstanding open problem in learning theory, originating in the work of Littlestone and Warmuth in the 1980s. At its heart is a connection between simplification and generalization: if a predictor consistent with a training sample is defined using only a small part of that sample, then it is likely to generalize.

摘要：样本压缩猜想是学习理论中一个长期存在的未决问题，起源于 20 世纪 80 年代 Littlestone 和 Warmuth 的工作。其核心在于简化与泛化之间的联系：如果一个与训练样本一致的预测器仅使用该样本的一小部分来定义，那么它很可能具有泛化能力。

Sample compression schemes formalize this idea, and many natural learning algorithms can be analyzed this way. The sample compression conjecture of Floyd and Warmuth (1995) asserts that every binary concept class of VC dimension $d$ admits a sample compression scheme of size $O(d)$; a weaker conjecture asks only for size polynomial in $d$.

样本压缩方案将这一思想形式化，许多自然学习算法都可以通过这种方式进行分析。Floyd 和 Warmuth (1995) 的样本压缩猜想断言，每个 VC 维数为 $d$ 的二元概念类都承认一个大小为 $O(d)$ 的样本压缩方案；一个较弱的猜想仅要求大小为 $d$ 的多项式。

Both have remained open: the first bound depending only on $d$, due to Moran and Yehudayoff (2016), is exponential in $d$. We prove the weaker conjecture by constructing a sample compression scheme of size $O(d^2 \log^2 d)$.

两者都尚未解决：Moran 和 Yehudayoff (2016) 给出的第一个仅依赖于 $d$ 的界限是关于 $d$ 的指数级。我们通过构建一个大小为 $O(d^2 \log^2 d)$ 的样本压缩方案证明了较弱的猜想。
