Today Anthropic, PBC announced the closing of its initial public offering of an aggregate of 1,127,000,000 shares of its Class A common stock at a public offering price of $83.00 per share, including the full exercise by the underwriters of their option to purchase an additional 147,000,000 shares of Class A common stock from Anthropic at the initial public offering price. The offering consisted of 1,103,351,354 shares sold by Anthropic and 23,648,646 shares sold by certain selling stockholders. The aggregate gross proceeds from the offering, before deducting underwriting discounts and commissions and other offering expenses, were approximately $93.5 billion, of which approximately $91.6 billion was received by Anthropic. Anthropic did not receive any proceeds from the sale of shares by the selling stockholders.

Anthropic, PBC 今日宣布完成其首次公开募股，共计发行 11.27 亿股 A 类普通股，发行价为每股 83.00 美元。其中包括承销商全额行使了以首次公开募股价格从 Anthropic 额外购买 1.47 亿股 A 类普通股的选择权。此次发行包括 Anthropic 出售的 1,103,351,354 股股票以及部分售股股东出售的 23,648,646 股股票。在扣除承销折扣、佣金及其他发行费用前，此次发行的总收益约为 935 亿美元，其中约 916 亿美元由 Anthropic 获得。Anthropic 未从售股股东的股票出售中获得任何收益。

Anthropic’s Class A common stock began trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on November 19, 2026 under the symbol “ANT.” In addition to the shares of Class A common stock sold in the initial public offering, Anthropic closed the concurrent sale of an aggregate of 313,253,007 shares of Class N common stock to Advanced Micro Devices, Inc., Broadcom Inc., Google LLC, Micron Technology, Inc., Microsoft Corporation, NVIDIA Corporation, and TSMC Partners, Ltd. at the public offering price of $83.00 per share, in a private placement for an aggregate purchase price of approximately $26.0 billion. The sale of these shares of Class N common stock was not registered under the Securities Act of 1933, as amended (the “Securities Act”). Aggregate gross proceeds from the initial public offering, including the shares sold by the selling stockholders, and the concurrent private placement were approximately $119.5 billion, before deducting underwriting discounts and commissions and other offering expenses.

Anthropic 的 A 类普通股于 2026 年 11 月 19 日开始在纳斯达克全球精选市场交易，股票代码为“ANT”。除首次公开募股中出售的 A 类普通股外，Anthropic 还完成了向 Advanced Micro Devices, Inc.、Broadcom Inc.、Google LLC、Micron Technology, Inc.、Microsoft Corporation、NVIDIA Corporation 和 TSMC Partners, Ltd. 同步出售总计 313,253,007 股 N 类普通股的交易，发行价为每股 83.00 美元，私募总购买价格约为 260 亿美元。这些 N 类普通股的出售未根据修订后的《1933 年证券法》（简称“证券法”）进行注册。包括售股股东出售的股票在内，首次公开募股及同步私募的总收益在扣除承销折扣、佣金及其他发行费用前约为 1,195 亿美元。
