The Federal Communications Commission yesterday voted to let state and local prisons use jamming technology to block contraband cellphones, despite warnings that jammers will also block lawful phone calls outside the prisons.

美国联邦通信委员会（FCC）昨日投票决定，允许州和地方监狱使用干扰技术来屏蔽违禁手机，尽管有警告称，这些干扰器也会屏蔽监狱外的合法通话。

Wireless industry lobby group CTIA said a recent test it conducted with the Virginia Tech Applied Research Corporation and South Carolina Department of Corrections confirmed concerns that the jammers would block legitimate communications, including 911 calls.

无线行业游说团体美国无线通信和互联网协会（CTIA）表示，其近期与弗吉尼亚理工大学应用研究公司及南卡罗来纳州惩教署共同进行的一项测试证实了人们的担忧，即干扰器会阻断包括911报警电话在内的合法通信。

CTIA told the FCC in an October 1 filing: The pilot test was limited in the number of micro-jammers deployed, the spectrum bands they were authorized to jam, and the power levels at which they were permitted to operate. Even with these constraints, the micro-jamming system blocked 911 calls, interfered with public safety and other communications, and caused interference that impacted wireless network infrastructure up to two and a half miles away.

CTIA在10月1日提交给FCC的文件中称：试点测试在部署的微型干扰器数量、获准干扰的频谱频段以及允许运行的功率水平方面都受到了限制。即便在这些约束条件下，微型干扰系统仍阻断了911通话，干扰了公共安全及其他通信，并造成了影响范围远达2.5英里的无线网络基础设施干扰。

At the same time, the pilot results show substantial gaps in jamming the targeted “contraband” devices, with testing scenarios in outdoor inmate recreation areas showing failure rates—meaning “contraband” device call completion—in one-half to two-thirds of call attempts.

与此同时，试点结果显示，在干扰目标“违禁”设备方面存在巨大漏洞，在户外囚犯娱乐区的测试场景中，失败率（即“违禁”设备通话成功率）占通话尝试次数的二分之一到三分之二。

CTIA said the pilot test shows “that current jamming technology seems to be both over and underinclusive at once—it risks blocking lawful communications even as it fails to block a significant portion of the targeted contraband device communications.”

CTIA表示，试点测试表明“当前的干扰技术似乎同时存在过度和不足的问题——它在未能阻断大量目标违禁设备通信的同时，却冒着阻断合法通信的风险。”

The FCC approved the jamming order at its meeting yesterday. “Today, we are finally authorizing state and local prisons and jails to use targeted jamming against contraband cell phones for the first time,” FCC Chairman Brendan Carr said. “The technology has advanced to the point where it can be focused with geographic precision. No facility will be required to do it.”

FCC在昨日的会议上批准了这项干扰令。FCC主席布伦丹·卡尔（Brendan Carr）表示：“今天，我们终于首次授权州和地方监狱对违禁手机使用定向干扰技术。该技术已进步到可以实现地理精度聚焦的程度。任何设施都不会被强制要求使用。”

The Communications Act prohibits willful or malicious interference with licensed or authorized radio communications, and the FCC has previously interpreted this to prohibit non-federal entities from using jamming technology on contraband devices at prisons. Yesterday’s vote provides an exception for state and local correctional facilities.

《通信法》禁止蓄意或恶意干扰已许可或授权的无线电通信，FCC此前曾据此解释，禁止非联邦实体在监狱内对违禁设备使用干扰技术。昨日的投票为州和地方惩教设施提供了一个例外。

The FCC says its jammer prohibitions do not apply to federal agencies because the federal government’s use of spectrum is not regulated by the agency. Carr said that “contraband cell phones continue to pour into prisons by the thousands each year,” with “estimates indicat[ing] that as many as 500,000 inmates nationwide have regular access to these devices.”

FCC表示，其干扰禁令不适用于联邦机构，因为联邦政府对频谱的使用不受该机构监管。卡尔称，“违禁手机每年仍以数千部的数量涌入监狱”，据估计“全国有多达50万名囚犯经常使用这些设备”。

He said the phones have been used to plan scams, drug trafficking, and violent attacks. “In Georgia, an incarcerated gang leader directed a drive-by shooting that killed two 13-year-old boys at a birthday party,” Carr said, referring to a 2024 shooting.

他表示，这些手机已被用于策划诈骗、毒品走私和暴力袭击。卡尔在提到2024年的一起枪击案时说：“在佐治亚州，一名被监禁的帮派头目指挥了一场驾车枪击事件，导致两名13岁男孩在生日派对上丧生。”

Carr defended the plan against criticism that it will block legitimate communications outside the prisons, saying it includes safeguards to prevent that. “Those that want to use the technology will apply to the FCC, explain how they plan to deploy it, and show through testing that it stays inside the designated area and doesn’t interfere with lawful communications outside the prison walls,” Carr said.

卡尔为该计划进行了辩护，反驳了关于其会阻断监狱外合法通信的批评，称该计划包含防止此类情况的保障措施。卡尔说：“那些想要使用该技术的机构将向FCC提出申请，解释其部署计划，并通过测试证明干扰范围保持在指定区域内，且不会干扰监狱墙外的合法通信。”

“Communities nearby will receive notice before operations start. We’re also seeking comment on next steps, including ways to close remaining loopholes on other frequencies and the possibility of quiet zones around correctional facilities.”

“附近的社区将在行动开始前收到通知。我们也在就后续步骤征求意见，包括如何填补其他频率上的剩余漏洞，以及在惩教设施周围设立静默区的可能性。”

Freedom of the Press Foundation Chief of Advocacy Seth Stern said the FCC should have considered the benefits of contraband phones being used to document abuses inside prisons.

新闻自由基金会倡导主管塞斯·斯特恩（Seth Stern）表示，FCC本应考虑到违禁手机在记录监狱内部虐待行为方面的益处。

“Prisons are the most secretive government institutions in the country and people wishing to expose abuses and inhumane conditions from the inside often do not have many options,” Stern wrote in a letter to FCC Commissioner Anna Gomez’s office, which was included on the FCC docket.

斯特恩在致FCC委员安娜·戈麦斯（Anna Gomez）办公室的一封信中写道：“监狱是国内最隐秘的政府机构，希望从内部揭露虐待和非人道状况的人往往没有太多选择。”该信件已被收录在FCC的案卷中。

“Last year’s Oscar-nominated film, The Alabama Solution, used footage incarcerated people captured with contraband cell phones to show Americans some of the awful things that go on in state prisons that they’d have no other way of seeing. The same footage bolstered an ongoing DOJ investigation into Alabama prisons and was crucial evidence in a civil lawsuit by Steven Davis, who was brutally killed by prison guards, that eventually settled.”

“去年获得奥斯卡提名的电影《阿拉巴马解决方案》（The Alabama Solution）使用了被监禁者用违禁手机拍摄的镜头，向美国人展示了州立监狱中发生的一些可怕事情，而这些事情他们本无从知晓。同样的镜头支持了司法部对阿拉巴马州监狱正在进行的调查，并成为史蒂文·戴维斯（Steven Davis）民事诉讼中的关键证据，戴维斯被监狱看守残忍杀害，该案最终达成了和解。”

Jamming contraband cellphones was supported by prison phone companies ViaPath (aka Global Tel*Link) and NCIC Correctional Services, the National District Attorneys Association, the Correctional Leaders Association, and National Association of Police Organizations.

干扰违禁手机的行为得到了监狱电话公司ViaPath（又名Global Tel*Link）和NCIC惩教服务公司、全国地方检察官协会、惩教领导人协会以及全国警察组织协会的支持。

“Inmates have used these phones to coordinate attacks against correctional officers and their families, as well as inmate violence, strikes, disturbances, and full-scale riots,” the National Association of Police Organizations said. “They also use contraband phones to organize deliveries of illegal drugs and weapons into correctional facilities. These dangers underscore the need to authorize indoor jamming in state and local correctional facilities.”

全国警察组织协会表示：“囚犯利用这些手机协调针对惩教人员及其家属的袭击，以及囚犯间的暴力、罢工、骚乱和全面暴动。他们还利用违禁手机组织非法毒品和武器进入惩教设施。这些危险凸显了在州和地方惩教设施中授权室内干扰的必要性。”

Gomez, the only Democrat on the three-member commission, said she’s concerned about potential unintended consequences, but voted with Republicans to allow jamming after safeguards were added to the order.

作为三人委员会中唯一的民主党人，戈麦斯表示她对潜在的意外后果感到担忧，但在命令中增加了保障措施后，她与共和党人投票支持了干扰授权。

“For the last 90 years, the Federal Communications Commission has prohibited jamming by non-federal actors and even prohibited jamming equipment to be introduced into the American market,” Gomez said. “That is because jamming devices are specifically designed to interfere with communications devices in order to render them un-usable.”

戈麦斯说：“在过去的90年里，联邦通信委员会一直禁止非联邦主体进行干扰，甚至禁止干扰设备进入美国市场。这是因为干扰设备是专门为干扰通信设备而设计的，目的是使其无法使用。”
