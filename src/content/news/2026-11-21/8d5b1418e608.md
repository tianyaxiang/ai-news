# Here are the Apple deals actually worth picking up ahead of Black Friday

以下是黑色星期五前真正值得入手的苹果优惠活动

From AirPods to Apple Watches, many Apple devices are already seeing notable discounts ahead of November 27th.

从 AirPods 到 Apple Watch，许多苹果设备在 11 月 27 日之前就已经出现了显著的折扣。

A new pair of AirPods or the latest Apple Watch can make a great holiday gift, but they can also take a pretty big dent out of your budget. That’s especially true this year, as rising hardware prices have made MacBooks and other Apple devices even more expensive.

一副新的 AirPods 或最新的 Apple Watch 是很棒的节日礼物，但它们也可能让你的预算大打折扣。今年尤其如此，因为硬件价格的上涨使得 MacBook 和其他苹果设备变得更加昂贵。

Fortunately, Black Friday offers a chance to save, especially since great deals are already available on a wide range of Apple devices. Some discounts even extend to recent releases like the Apple Watch Series 12 and touchscreen MacBook Pro, which have dropped to new lows.

幸运的是，黑色星期五提供了一个省钱的机会，尤其是因为各种苹果设备已经有了不错的优惠。一些折扣甚至延伸到了 Apple Watch Series 12 和触屏版 MacBook Pro 等近期发布的产品上，它们的价格已降至新低。

Most of the discounts below are promoted as Black Friday deals, but we’ve also included a few that aren’t technically part of the shopping event but have “coincidentally” dropped in price in the last week or so. Black Friday isn’t until November 27th, however, so some prices could fall further in the coming days.

以下大多数折扣都是作为黑色星期五优惠进行推广的，但我们也收录了一些严格来说不属于此次购物活动，但在过去一周左右“巧合地”降价的产品。不过，黑色星期五要到 11 月 27 日才开始，因此未来几天某些价格可能会进一步下跌。

Apple is also kicking off its own Black Friday shopping event that day, offering up to $300 in gift cards with select purchases. That said, if you don’t want to wait, we think the deals below are already worth taking advantage of.

苹果当天也将开启自己的黑色星期五购物活动，购买指定产品最高可获赠 300 美元的礼品卡。话虽如此，如果你不想等待，我们认为以下优惠已经值得入手了。
