In the Andromeda Galaxy, a giant star about seven times as massive as the Sun spent nearly a decade slowly swallowing its much smaller companion. The process, known as common envelope evolution, was proposed in the 1970s to explain how some of the tightest pairs of stars in the Universe form, but no one had ever caught it in the act.

在仙女座星系中，一颗质量约为太阳七倍的巨星花了近十年时间缓慢吞噬其较小的伴星。这一过程被称为“共同包层演化”（common envelope evolution），早在20世纪70年代就被提出，用以解释宇宙中一些最紧密的恒星对是如何形成的，但此前从未有人亲眼目睹过这一过程。

In a new Nature study, astronomers led by Viraj Karambelkar, an astrophysicist at Columbia University, finally witnessed the entire spectacle from the decades before it began to the eruption that ended it in late 2025. “It lasts for a few years, maybe a few decades,” Karambelkar said. “Stars evolve on time scales of millions of years. So, it’s really like a blip on the entire cosmic time scale.”

在《自然》杂志的一项新研究中，由哥伦比亚大学天体物理学家维拉吉·卡兰贝尔卡（Viraj Karambelkar）领导的天文学家团队终于目睹了整个奇观，从它开始前的几十年一直到2025年底结束时的爆发。“它持续几年，也许几十年，”卡兰贝尔卡说，“恒星的演化是以数百万年为时间尺度的。所以，在整个宇宙的时间尺度上，这真的就像是一个瞬间。”

Some pairs of stars orbit so close to each other that they could not have formed at that distance, so their orbits must have shrunk. Common envelope evolution is the leading explanation for that shrinkage. When one star in a binary grows old, it expands into a giant, and its outer layers can engulf its companion. The two then share a single, loosely bound envelope of gas.

有些恒星对的轨道距离非常近，以至于它们不可能在那种距离下形成，因此它们的轨道必然发生了收缩。共同包层演化是解释这种收缩的主要理论。当双星系统中的一颗恒星变老并膨胀成巨星时，其外层可能会吞没伴星。随后，两者共享一个松散的共同气体包层。

As the engulfed star drags through it, it loses orbital energy to friction and spirals inward. Depending on how much of the envelope gets lost, the pair either ends up in a much tighter orbit or merges. Karambelkar’s team spotted a candidate system where this might be happening while searching Andromeda for stars changing in brightness, using the Wide-field Infrared Transient Explorer (WINTER) telescope at Palomar Observatory and data from NASA’s Near-Earth Object Wide-field Infrared Survey Explorer (NEOWISE) mission.

随着被吞没的恒星在包层中穿行，它会因摩擦而损失轨道能量并向内盘旋。根据包层损失程度的不同，这对恒星最终要么进入更紧密的轨道，要么合并。卡兰贝尔卡的团队在利用帕洛玛天文台的广角红外瞬变探测器（WINTER）望远镜和NASA近地天体广角红外巡天探测器（NEOWISE）任务的数据搜索仙女座星系中亮度变化的恒星时，发现了可能正在发生这种情况的候选系统。

One giant had been brightening for seven years. “When a star first swallows its companion, it has a very subtle effect on its brightness,” Karambelkar said. “It slowly brightens, and a lot of light comes out in the infrared.” In April 2025, Karambelkar’s team published a paper suggesting that this system, named AT 2025abao, was undergoing an engulfment in progress.

其中一颗巨星已经变亮了七年。“当一颗恒星首次吞噬其伴星时，对亮度的影响非常微妙，”卡兰贝尔卡说，“它会缓慢变亮，并释放出大量的红外光。”2025年4月，卡兰贝尔卡的团队发表了一篇论文，指出这个名为AT 2025abao的系统正在经历吞噬过程。

The researchers reconstructed the history of the system using observations from about a dozen telescopes and surveys, going back to photographic sky plates from 1989. Until 2018, the star barely changed. Modeling those quiet years revealed a giant late in its life, about 350 times wider than the Sun. Its companion was impossible to see, however.

研究人员利用大约十几台望远镜和巡天项目的观测数据重建了该系统的历史，追溯到了1989年的照相天图。直到2018年，这颗恒星几乎没有变化。对这些平静年份的建模显示，这是一颗处于生命末期的巨星，宽度约为太阳的350倍。然而，它的伴星却无法被观测到。

“The giant star outshines the smaller star by a factor of about 18,000,” Karambelkar said. To get around it, the team estimated the mass of the smaller companion based on the amount of dust the system had shed before the engulfment. The estimates suggested a star of roughly 0.7 solar masses.

“这颗巨星的亮度比那颗较小的恒星高出约18,000倍，”卡兰贝尔卡说。为了解决这个问题，团队根据该系统在吞噬前抛出的尘埃量估算了较小伴星的质量。估算结果显示，那是一颗质量约为0.7个太阳质量的恒星。

In October 2025, the star brightened roughly 100-fold in just 25 days, shining like 1.5 million Suns at its peak. “At one point, the rate at which the orbit was decaying became very, very fast,” Karambelkar said, comparing the release of the gravitational energy to an explosion. “The companion just fell very quickly, very deep inside the envelope of the star, and it crashed into the core. That violent crash set off a bomb inside the star, and that explosion caused it to brighten very quickly and eject some of the material.”

2025年10月，这颗恒星在短短25天内亮度增加了约100倍，峰值时亮度相当于150万个太阳。“在某一时刻，轨道衰减的速度变得非常非常快，”卡兰贝尔卡说，他将引力能的释放比作一场爆炸，“伴星迅速坠入恒星包层的深处，并撞向了核心。那次剧烈的撞击在恒星内部引爆了一颗‘炸弹’，爆炸导致它迅速变亮并喷射出部分物质。”

The eruption had all the hallmarks of a luminous red nova, a type of outburst known to come from merging stars—but an event the researchers weren’t expecting. “Honestly, our minds were blown,” Karambelkar said. He said that this was evidence that this explosion was a consequence of the inspiraling of a binary companion.

这次爆发具备了亮红新星的所有特征，这是一种已知源于恒星合并的爆发类型，但研究人员此前并未预料到会发生这种情况。“老实说，我们感到非常震惊，”卡兰贝尔卡说。他表示，这证明了此次爆炸是双星伴星向内盘旋的结果。
