Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.25000 (cs) [Submitted on 18 Nov 2026] Title: Watch Where You Linearize: Predicting and Correcting Model-Merging Interference Authors: Ido Nitzan Hidekel, Dan Raviv.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.25000 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 18 日] 标题：注意你的线性化位置：预测并纠正模型合并干扰 作者：Ido Nitzan Hidekel, Dan Raviv。

Abstract: Model merging combines separately fine-tuned models by adding their task vectors to a shared backbone. Interference is typically assessed only after constructing and evaluating a candidate merge, making coefficient search expensive. We show that a first-order directional derivative of task A's outputs along task B's vector can predict interference, but only when evaluated at the appropriate model.

摘要：模型合并通过将任务向量添加到共享主干中来结合单独微调的模型。干扰通常仅在构建并评估候选合并后才进行评估，这使得系数搜索成本高昂。我们证明，任务 A 的输出沿任务 B 向量的一阶方向导数可以预测干扰，但前提是必须在合适的模型处进行评估。

At the receiver model, this derivative forecasts nearby merges per task and per example, halves the accuracy error of a no-change baseline, and identifies most examples whose predictions flip. For full multi-task merges, a single evaluation is insufficient; two evaluations along the merge path are needed.

在接收模型处，该导数可以预测每个任务和每个样本的附近合并情况，将无变化基线的准确率误差减半，并识别出大多数预测结果发生翻转的样本。对于完整的多任务合并，单次评估是不够的；需要沿合并路径进行两次评估。

At the shared pretrained backbone, however, the derivative is nearly orthogonal to realized changes in our vision experiments and only partly predictive in LoRA language-model pilots. Controls link this failure to displacement along the receiver's own task vector, not simply parameter distance; the mismatch appears within the first few dozen fine-tuning steps.

然而，在共享的预训练主干上，该导数在我们的视觉实验中与实际变化几乎正交，并且在 LoRA 语言模型试点中仅具有部分预测能力。对照实验将这种失败归因于沿接收者自身任务向量的位移，而不仅仅是参数距离；这种不匹配出现在微调的前几十步内。

Freezing receiver-local responses produces a surrogate affine in the merge coefficients. We use it to construct LR-Merge, a label-free correction that optimizes coefficients through convex teacher matching on unlabeled probes, without backpropagation through merged networks.

冻结接收者局部响应会产生一个关于合并系数的仿射代理。我们利用它构建了 LR-Merge，这是一种无标签校正方法，通过在未标记探针上进行凸教师匹配来优化系数，而无需通过合并后的网络进行反向传播。

LR-Merge improves all tested closed-form, isotropic, and sparsified base mergers, with gains generally increasing as more tasks are merged. Using pretrained rather than receiver-local responses instead degrades the base merge. LR-Merge matches exact teacher matching through a merged network, which takes roughly three times longer to converge and performs similarly at an equal compute budget.

LR-Merge 改进了所有经过测试的闭式、各向同性和稀疏化基础合并器，且随着合并任务数量的增加，收益通常会增加。使用预训练响应而非接收者局部响应反而会降低基础合并的效果。LR-Merge 的效果与通过合并网络进行的精确教师匹配相当，后者收敛速度慢约三倍，但在相同的计算预算下表现相似。

Once built, the surrogate answers new coefficient and task-subset queries in seconds.

一旦构建完成，该代理可以在几秒钟内回答新的系数和任务子集查询。
