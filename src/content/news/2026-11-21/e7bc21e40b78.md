Anthropic mathematician claims to have solved prestigious Millennium Prize problem with Claude. An Anthropic mathematician’s latest breakthrough is another sign of how quickly AI is transforming mathematics.

Anthropic 数学家声称已利用 Claude 解决了著名的千禧年大奖难题。一位 Anthropic 数学家的最新突破，再次表明了人工智能正在以多快的速度改变数学领域。

Anthropic mathematician Levent Alpöge claims he has proved the Riemann hypothesis, one of mathematics’s most notoriously difficult problems, with the help of Claude, cracking a puzzle that has eluded some of the world’s best mathematicians for decades. If verified, it would mark yet another seismic achievement for AI in mathematics and add to the upheaval already unleashed by OpenAI’s previous, often inelegant forays into the field.

Anthropic 数学家 Levent Alpöge 声称，在 Claude 的帮助下，他已经证明了黎曼猜想——这是数学界最臭名昭著的难题之一，破解了这个几十年来一直困扰着全球顶尖数学家的谜题。如果得到证实，这将标志着人工智能在数学领域取得的又一项震撼性成就，并加剧 OpenAI 此前在这一领域往往显得笨拙的尝试所引发的动荡。

Alpöge’s solution has yet to be verified independently. Anthropic has not commented or involved itself in Alpöge’s announcement. In a document on his website, Alpöge says he texted Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei about the solution “late Sunday night” and suggests he held off publication until after the company’s IPO on Thursday.

Alpöge 的解决方案尚未经过独立验证。Anthropic 公司尚未对此发表评论，也未参与 Alpöge 的公告。Alpöge 在其网站上的一份文件中表示，他曾在“周日深夜”就该解决方案给 Anthropic 首席执行官 Dario Amodei 发过短信，并暗示他推迟了发布时间，直到该公司周四首次公开募股（IPO）之后。

The Riemann hypothesis is one of seven Millennium Prize problems, which are widely considered some of the most formidable open challenges in mathematics. First proposed in 1859, the Riemann hypothesis concerns the distribution of prime numbers and how zeroes of a function are involved. It’s regarded as one of the most important unsolved problems in mathematics.

黎曼猜想是七大千禧年大奖难题之一，这些难题被广泛认为是数学界最艰巨的公开挑战。黎曼猜想于 1859 年首次提出，涉及素数的分布以及函数零点如何参与其中。它被认为是数学中最重要的未解难题之一。

A statement on Alpöge’s website says Claude was integral to developing the proof: “I believe I have, working with Claude, discovered a proof of the Riemann Hypothesis. In fact, the method easily extends much further, resolving the so-called Generalized Riemann Hypothesis, and I expect, with more time, the Extended Riemann Hypothesis too. Claude and I have also formalized the arguments without assumptions. It is natural to feel that this is an end, in my case a loss of a principal guiding light, but what I saw during the course of progress so amazed me that I believe it is one dawn across science of something far greater.”

Alpöge 网站上的一份声明称，Claude 在开发该证明过程中不可或缺：“我相信，在与 Claude 的合作下，我已经发现了黎曼猜想的证明。事实上，该方法很容易进一步扩展，解决了所谓的广义黎曼猜想，而且我预计，随着时间的推移，扩展黎曼猜想也能得到解决。我和 Claude 还对这些论点进行了无假设的形式化。感到这是一个终点是很自然的，对我个人而言，这是一种主要指路明灯的丧失，但我在进展过程中所见到的景象令我如此惊叹，以至于我相信这是科学界一个更伟大事物的黎明。”
