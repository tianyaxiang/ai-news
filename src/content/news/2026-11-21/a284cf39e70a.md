The representative in the US House expected to chair the House Intelligence Committee in January, Jim Himes (D-Conn.), has threatened to withhold support for funding government-owned systems from SpaceX amid the ongoing inquiry into the use of Starshield in Ukraine.

预计将于一月份担任众议院情报委员会主席的美国众议员吉姆·海姆斯（民主党，康涅狄格州）威胁称，在针对乌克兰使用“星盾”（Starshield）系统的调查进行期间，他将不再支持为SpaceX的政府所有系统提供资金。

“SpaceX’s potentially unilateral modification of a U.S. Government contract, combined with its lack of responsiveness to congressional oversight, calls into question every contract the U.S. Government has with SpaceX,” Himes wrote in a letter to SpaceX founder Elon Musk on Friday. CNN first reported the letter’s existence.

“SpaceX可能单方面修改美国政府合同的行为，加上其对国会监督缺乏回应，使美国政府与SpaceX签署的每一份合同都受到了质疑，”海姆斯在周五致SpaceX创始人埃隆·马斯克的信中写道。美国有线电视新闻网（CNN）率先报道了这封信的存在。

The network also reported that Himes sent a classified communication to Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton with a similar message that it would be difficult for Congress to authorize funding for systems with SpaceX as the primary vendor.

该电视网还报道称，海姆斯向国家情报总监杰伊·克莱顿发送了一份机密通讯，传达了类似的信息，即国会很难批准为以SpaceX为主要供应商的系统提供资金。

SpaceX has developed the consumer Starlink service over the last decade, which provides high-speed Internet from low-Earth orbit to user terminals on Earth. For government customers, SpaceX has also developed Starshield, a suite of satellite services that includes a militarized version of Starlink communications.

在过去十年中，SpaceX开发了面向消费者的“星链”（Starlink）服务，通过低地球轨道为地球上的用户终端提供高速互联网。针对政府客户，SpaceX还开发了“星盾”系统，这是一套卫星服务，其中包括军事化的“星链”通信版本。

The US government has purchased Starshield services and extended access to Ukraine, although the terms governing that access are largely classified. During the last several weeks, a controversy has erupted over interruptions to Starshield service in parts of Ukraine, cutting off connectivity to drones used to defend against Russian attacks.

美国政府已购买了“星盾”服务并将其接入权限扩展至乌克兰，尽管管理该权限的条款大多属于机密。在过去几周里，关于乌克兰部分地区“星盾”服务中断的争议爆发，导致用于防御俄罗斯袭击的无人机连接中断。

On October 19, for example, Ukraine President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said of Starshield on social media, “Unfortunately, we have established that the equipment stops functioning at speeds of 130–161 kilometers per hour in various sectors of the front. This is slowing down our frontline logistics and limiting the use of various types of drones, particularly mid-strike drones and interceptors of jet-powered ‘shaheds.’” Additional disruptions were reported a week ago.

例如，10月19日，乌克兰总统弗拉基米尔·泽连斯基在社交媒体上谈到“星盾”时表示：“不幸的是，我们已经确定，该设备在前方不同区域以每小时130至161公里的速度移动时会停止工作。这减缓了我们的前线后勤速度，并限制了各种类型无人机的使用，特别是中程打击无人机和喷气式‘见证者’拦截机。”一周前又有关于中断的报道。

The central question is who decided to curtail the performance of Starshield assets in Ukraine. Accounts have variously implicated Musk, SpaceX, the Pentagon, and the White House. Musk has said that US officials control Starshield access, while defending SpaceX’s right to restrict the use of commercial Starlink service in war.

核心问题是谁决定削减乌克兰境内“星盾”资产的性能。各种说法分别指向了马斯克、SpaceX、五角大楼和白宫。马斯克表示，美国官员控制着“星盾”的访问权限，同时也为SpaceX在战争中限制商业“星链”服务使用的权利进行了辩护。

“My ask to you is straightforward,” Himes wrote to Musk. “Explain the changes that were made to the Starshield service in October 2026, why those changes were made, and whether those changes were made unilaterally by SpaceX or were directed by a U.S. Government official.”

“我对你的要求很简单，”海姆斯在写给马斯克的信中说。“解释2026年10月对‘星盾’服务所做的更改，为什么要进行这些更改，以及这些更改是SpaceX单方面做出的，还是由美国政府官员指示的。”

The communications from Himes suggest that Congressional Democrats, who will control the House and Senate in January following decisive victories in the Midterm elections, are prepared to not just investigate the disruption of these SpaceX services in Ukraine, but to take action that could threaten SpaceX’s broader national security business.

海姆斯的通讯表明，在期中选举取得决定性胜利后，将于一月份控制参众两院的国会民主党人准备采取行动，不仅要调查SpaceX在乌克兰的服务中断问题，还要采取可能威胁到SpaceX更广泛国家安全业务的行动。

“It is difficult for me to continue supporting the authorization of funding for these SpaceX-developed, government-owned systems, until I receive satisfactory answers to my questions about the Starshield service in Ukraine,” Himes wrote in his letter to Musk.

“在我收到关于乌克兰‘星盾’服务问题的满意答复之前，我很难继续支持为这些由SpaceX开发、政府所有的系统授权提供资金，”海姆斯在给马斯克的信中写道。

Various options are open to Congressional Democrats, as well as some Republicans who have raised similar concerns, including demanding classified briefings and documents and restricting funding through appropriations to the US Department of Defense and other federal departments.

国会民主党人以及一些提出类似担忧的共和党人有多种选择，包括要求进行机密简报和提供文件，以及通过对美国国防部和其他联邦部门的拨款来限制资金。

Notably, the US Space Force awarded SpaceX a $2.29 billion contract earlier this year to develop the Space Data Network Backbone intended to support the Golden Dome missile defense initiative. That program, along with SpaceX’s work building intelligence satellites for the National Reconnaissance Office, could become a focus of congressional scrutiny.

值得注意的是，美国太空军今年早些时候授予SpaceX一份价值22.9亿美元的合同，用于开发旨在支持“金穹”（Golden Dome）导弹防御计划的太空数据网络骨干网。该项目以及SpaceX为国家侦察局建造情报卫星的工作，可能会成为国会审查的焦点。

It seems unlikely that the US government would sever ties with SpaceX for space-based data services or launches, as the company is currently providing capabilities that no other US company can match. But Democrats are clearly serious about putting restrictions on Musk’s ability to influence geopolitical conflicts that have traditionally been the purview of governments rather than corporations.

美国政府似乎不太可能切断与SpaceX在天基数据服务或发射方面的联系，因为该公司目前提供的能力是其他任何美国公司都无法比拟的。但民主党人显然是认真的，他们试图限制马斯克影响地缘政治冲突的能力，而这些冲突传统上属于政府而非企业的管辖范围。
