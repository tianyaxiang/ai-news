YouTube is reportedly preparing to settle social media addiction claims for $3 billion.

据报道，YouTube 正准备以 30 亿美元达成和解，以应对有关社交媒体成瘾的指控。

The potential settlement would also require YouTube to introduce new safeguards for teens, Bloomberg reports.

据彭博社报道，这项潜在的和解协议还将要求 YouTube 为青少年引入新的保护措施。

YouTube is preparing to pay $3 billion to thousands of school districts to settle claims that its “addictive design” harmed kids, according to Bloomberg. Sources tell the publication that YouTube reached the agreement “in principle,” and that the deal would also include new safeguards for teens.

据彭博社报道，YouTube 正准备向数千个学区支付 30 亿美元，以解决有关其“成瘾性设计”伤害儿童的指控。消息人士告诉该媒体，YouTube 已“原则上”达成协议，且该协议还将包括针对青少年的新保护措施。

The settlement would allow Google to avoid a wave of lawsuits filed by US school districts that accuse YouTube, Meta, Snap, and TikTok of negatively impacting the mental health of teen users.

这项和解将使谷歌能够避免美国各学区发起的一系列诉讼，这些诉讼指控 YouTube、Meta、Snap 和 TikTok 对青少年用户的心理健康产生了负面影响。

In August, Meta agreed to pay $17.1 billion over 10 years to settle lawsuits filed by a group of 47 states, along with several districts and territories. It also agreed to implement stricter safeguards for teens, but is pushing TikTok and YouTube to do the same.

今年 8 月，Meta 同意在 10 年内支付 171 亿美元，以解决由 47 个州以及多个地区和领地提起的诉讼。该公司还同意为青少年实施更严格的保护措施，但正在推动 TikTok 和 YouTube 也采取同样的行动。
