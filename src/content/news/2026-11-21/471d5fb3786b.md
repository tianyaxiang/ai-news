# Internet Archive Staffers Are Unionizing

Staffers at the Internet Archive, the nonprofit “digital library” that runs the Wayback Machine, are voting to join the Communications Workers of America (CWA). More than 80 of the 124 eligible US staffers voted in favor of unionizing, and they are currently awaiting voluntary recognition of the union from Internet Archive founder Brewster Kahle. Kahle did not immediately respond to WIRED’s request for comment. If Kahle does not grant voluntary recognition, employees will vote whether to certify the union in a process run by the National Labor Relations Board.

运营“互联网档案馆”（Wayback Machine）的非营利性“数字图书馆”——互联网档案馆（Internet Archive）的员工们正在投票加入美国通信工人工会（CWA）。在124名符合条件的美国员工中，有超过80人投票支持成立工会，目前他们正在等待互联网档案馆创始人布鲁斯特·卡勒（Brewster Kahle）对工会的自愿承认。卡勒没有立即回应《连线》（WIRED）杂志的置评请求。如果卡勒不给予自愿承认，员工们将通过美国国家劳资关系委员会（NLRB）主持的程序投票决定是否认证该工会。

The Internet Archive, which also offers free catalogs preserving tens of millions of digital and physical media items, was founded by Kahle in 1996. Kahle’s mission was to provide “Universal Access to All Knowledge.” Will Howes, an Internet Archive engineer, says that the organizing process has demonstrated how “aligned” employees are in their passion for Kahle’s goals. “Frankly, we really see the formation of this union as accepting [Kahle’s] challenge to make this workplace the best place it can be in pursuit of a mission, universal access to all knowledge,” Howes tells WIRED.

互联网档案馆由卡勒于1996年创立，该机构还提供免费目录，保存了数以千万计的数字和实体媒体项目。卡勒的使命是提供“通向所有知识的普遍访问权”。互联网档案馆的工程师威尔·豪斯（Will Howes）表示，组织工会的过程证明了员工们在追求卡勒的目标方面是多么“一致”。豪斯告诉《连线》：“坦率地说，我们确实将工会的成立视为接受（卡勒的）挑战，即为了实现‘通向所有知识的普遍访问权’这一使命，将这个工作场所建设得尽可能好。”

Microfiche digitization technician Danielle Leard tells WIRED that the union is “very optimistic” that Kahle will voluntarily recognize it and come to the bargaining table. They add that among the union’s core goals are securing wage increases and additional benefits, and ensuring that these benefits are consistently available to employees across the company. “We believe the first and most primary goal of our union is to make sure that the Internet Archive reflects the wants and values of the people that work there, and the people that make the archive,” Leard says.

缩微胶片数字化技术员丹妮尔·里尔德（Danielle Leard）告诉《连线》，工会“非常乐观”地认为卡勒会自愿承认工会并坐到谈判桌前。他们补充说，工会的核心目标包括确保加薪和增加福利，并确保这些福利在整个公司范围内持续提供给员工。里尔德说：“我们认为工会的首要目标是确保互联网档案馆能够反映在那里工作的人以及构建该档案的人的愿望和价值观。”

Howes and Leard tell WIRED that the Internet Archive—which claims to have archived more than 1 trillion web pages—employs a highly diverse set of workers, including librarians, engineers, physical media specialists, and facility coordinators who maintain the archive’s building, a former Christian Scientist church in San Francisco. Leard says that the staff’s diversity has made the organizing process, which began in February 2025, “a unique challenge,” but one that’s “really rewarding.”

豪斯和里尔德告诉《连线》，互联网档案馆声称已经存档了超过1万亿个网页，其雇佣的员工群体非常多元化，包括图书管理员、工程师、实体媒体专家以及负责维护档案大楼（位于旧金山的一座前基督科学教会教堂）的设施协调员。里尔德表示，员工的多样性使得始于2025年2月的组织过程成为“一项独特的挑战”，但也是“非常有意义的”。

The vote comes slightly more than two months after staffers at the Wikimedia Foundation, the nonprofit behind Wikipedia, voted to join CWA. Howes says the simultaneous efforts are “very powerful” given that both organizations represent “core infrastructure” of the internet. Howes says that the union wants to ensure that the 30-year-old project continues to thrive in the long term—and that its workers do too. Leard says this means providing paths for developing skills and getting promoted within the nonprofit.

此次投票距离维基百科背后的非营利组织——维基媒体基金会（Wikimedia Foundation）的员工投票加入CWA仅过去两个多月。豪斯表示，考虑到这两个组织都代表了互联网的“核心基础设施”，这种同步的努力“非常有力量”。豪斯说，工会希望确保这个拥有30年历史的项目能够长期繁荣发展，同时也确保其员工能够如此。里尔德表示，这意味着要为员工提供在非营利组织内部发展技能和获得晋升的途径。

Leard says that the Internet Archive’s unionization drive is especially important in the present moment, amid waves of tech layoffs and widespread anxiety about “emerging technologies” like AI. The Internet Archive hasn’t been immune from the consequences of AI. A Nieman Lab report from January found that some news websites, struggling to prevent companies like OpenAI or Anthropic from harvesting their articles, have started blocking web crawlers from the Internet Archive, arguing it gives AI companies a back door to their content. (The archive relies on web crawlers to preserve news articles in near-real time.)

里尔德表示，在科技行业裁员潮和对人工智能等“新兴技术”普遍感到焦虑的当下，互联网档案馆的工会化运动显得尤为重要。互联网档案馆也未能幸免于人工智能带来的后果。尼曼实验室（Nieman Lab）1月份的一份报告发现，一些新闻网站为了防止OpenAI或Anthropic等公司抓取其文章，已经开始屏蔽互联网档案馆的网络爬虫，理由是这为人工智能公司获取其内容提供了后门。（该档案库依赖网络爬虫来近乎实时地保存新闻文章。）
