SpaceX’s successful recovery of its Starship rocket last weekend cleared a major hurdle on NASA’s path to the Moon, opening up possibilities for higher launch rates and an all-important, long-delayed orbital refueling demo next year.

SpaceX 上周末成功回收其星舰（Starship）火箭，扫清了 NASA 登月计划中的一大障碍，为提高发射频率以及明年进行至关重要且久拖未决的轨道燃料补给演示创造了可能。

For SpaceX’s purposes, Saturday’s mid-air rocket catch will help engineers learn how to rapidly reuse Starships. That should expedite SpaceX’s deployment of its newest generation of Starlink Internet satellites. NASA, meanwhile, believes a higher launch cadence will lead to faster learning as SpaceX develops a deep space version of Starship to ferry astronaut crews to the surface of the Moon.

对于 SpaceX 而言，周六的空中火箭回收将帮助工程师掌握如何快速重复使用星舰。这有望加快 SpaceX 部署其最新一代星链（Starlink）互联网卫星的速度。与此同时，NASA 认为，随着 SpaceX 开发用于运送宇航员往返月球表面的深空版星舰，更高的发射频率将带来更快的技术迭代。

Early next year, SpaceX plans to bring online a second Starship launch site at Kennedy Space Center, Florida. A second Starship factory is also under construction at Kennedy, joining SpaceX’s existing SpaceX production line at Starbase, Texas. With two launch sites, SpaceX will have the pieces in place to take the next leap with Starship.

明年年初，SpaceX 计划在佛罗里达州肯尼迪航天中心启用第二个星舰发射场。第二座星舰工厂也正在肯尼迪航天中心建设中，它将与 SpaceX 位于德克萨斯州星际基地的现有生产线相辅相成。拥有两个发射场后，SpaceX 将具备实现星舰下一次飞跃的必要条件。

The Starship refueling demonstration has slipped two years since late 2024, effectively a day-for-day delay as SpaceX ironed out technical problems with the vehicle. SpaceX may have turned a corner with Saturday’s first-ever catch of Starship at the launch tower. Other Starship lander tests are coming next year. The first will be NASA’s Artemis III mission, followed by an uncrewed Starship landing demonstration on the Moon. The former requires no orbital refueling, and the latter will need heaps of it.

自 2024 年底以来，星舰的燃料补给演示已推迟了两年，随着 SpaceX 不断解决该飞行器的技术问题，这实际上是逐日的延误。周六在发射塔首次成功回收星舰，可能标志着 SpaceX 迎来了一个转折点。明年还将进行其他星舰着陆器测试。首先是 NASA 的阿尔忒弥斯 3 号（Artemis III）任务，随后是星舰的无人月球着陆演示。前者不需要轨道燃料补给，而后者则需要大量补给。

Ars recently spoke with Steve Creech, the program manager for NASA’s Human Landing System. In this role, Creech oversees NASA’s contracts with SpaceX and Blue Origin to develop crew-rated lunar landers for Artemis.

Ars 最近采访了 NASA 人类着陆系统项目经理史蒂夫·克里奇（Steve Creech）。在该职位上，克里奇负责监督 NASA 与 SpaceX 和蓝色起源（Blue Origin）签订的合同，旨在为阿尔忒弥斯计划开发载人月球着陆器。

Different landers, different priorities. For Artemis III, NASA will launch a four-person crew to low-Earth orbit on a Space Launch System rocket and an Orion spacecraft. Orion will first dock with Blue Origin’s Blue Moon lander for two days of tests, following its launch on a New Glenn rocket. The astronauts will open hatches and enter Blue Moon’s pressurized crew cabin to test the vehicle’s environmental control and life support systems.

不同的着陆器，不同的优先级。对于阿尔忒弥斯 3 号任务，NASA 将使用太空发射系统（SLS）火箭和猎户座（Orion）飞船将四名宇航员送入近地轨道。猎户座飞船在通过新格伦（New Glenn）火箭发射后，将首先与蓝色起源的“蓝月”（Blue Moon）着陆器对接，进行为期两天的测试。宇航员将打开舱门进入“蓝月”的加压乘员舱，以测试该飞行器的环境控制和生命保障系统。

Then, the astronauts will return to Orion and undock from Blue Moon to set up for a rendezvous and docking with SpaceX’s Starship in a slightly lower orbit, keeping it below most of SpaceX’s Starlink constellation. Orion will stay attached to Starship for just two hours, and the astronauts won’t enter the SpaceX vehicle.

随后，宇航员将返回猎户座飞船并与“蓝月”分离，准备在稍低的轨道上与 SpaceX 的星舰进行交会对接，该轨道将保持在 SpaceX 大部分星链卫星星座的下方。猎户座飞船与星舰的对接时间仅为两小时，宇航员不会进入 SpaceX 的飞行器。

NASA changed the plan for the Artemis III mission earlier this year, rerouting the flight plan from a lunar mission to a low-Earth orbit mission. This means the Artemis program’s first crewed lunar landing will be on Artemis IV, targeted to launch before the end of 2028.

NASA 在今年早些时候更改了阿尔忒弥斯 3 号任务的计划，将飞行计划从登月任务改为近地轨道任务。这意味着阿尔忒弥斯计划的首次载人登月任务将推迟至阿尔忒弥斯 4 号，目标是在 2028 年底前发射。

So, why is Artemis III seemingly testing so much more with Blue Origin than with Starship? Creech told Ars that a big reason is to maintain SpaceX’s focus on orbital refueling. Blue Origin no longer requires in-space refueling for Artemis landing missions. Also, SpaceX has extensive experience with life support systems with its Dragon crewed spacecraft. Blue Origin has comparatively little.

那么，为什么阿尔忒弥斯 3 号似乎在蓝色起源身上进行的测试比在星舰上多得多？克里奇告诉 Ars，一个主要原因是让 SpaceX 专注于轨道燃料补给。蓝色起源在阿尔忒弥斯着陆任务中不再需要太空燃料补给。此外，SpaceX 通过其载人龙飞船在生命保障系统方面拥有丰富的经验，而蓝色起源则相对较少。

“The bigger challenges for [SpaceX] were the prop transfer and getting to the uncrewed landing demo,” Creech said. “The thing that we’re going to learn more with Starship is, when we dock with Orion, they will have control of the attitude, control of the stack. That integrated stack control will be on the Starship side. So, with that huge of a vehicle, I think we’re going to learn more on that.

“对于 [SpaceX] 来说，更大的挑战是推进剂传输以及实现无人着陆演示，”克里奇说。“我们通过星舰将学到更多的是，当我们与猎户座对接时，他们将控制姿态和组合体。这种集成的组合体控制将由星舰方面负责。因此，对于如此巨大的飞行器，我认为我们将从中获得更多经验。”

“With Blue, we’re going to have Orion control the stack for this mission,” Creech said. “When we get to a later mission, the Blue lander with fully loaded prop will have to control the stack. But with Blue, trying to mitigate risk, we’re going to have Orion do that.”

“对于蓝色起源，我们将在本次任务中让猎户座控制组合体，”克里奇说。“当我们进入后续任务时，装满推进剂的‘蓝月’着陆器将不得不控制组合体。但为了降低风险，在蓝色起源的任务中，我们还是会由猎户座来执行此操作。”

Artemis III will be Blue Origin’s first time flying a crew cabin for orbital spaceflight. Blue Origin has experience with suborbital space tourism, but those flights were brief and didn’t require the level of advanced life support necessary for longer durations. Blue Origin and NASA will emphasize those test objectives on Artemis III.

阿尔忒弥斯 3 号将是蓝色起源首次进行载人轨道飞行。蓝色起源虽然有亚轨道太空旅游的经验，但那些飞行时间很短，不需要长时间飞行所需的先进生命保障水平。蓝色起源和 NASA 将在阿尔忒弥斯 3 号任务中重点测试这些目标。

“I think SpaceX’s part was really, we need to focus on the docking,” Creech said. “If we added a crew cabin, that would slow us down on the other things and not add to what we’re already doing.”

“我认为 SpaceX 的部分确实是我们需要专注于对接，”克里奇说。“如果我们增加一个乘员舱，那会拖慢我们在其他方面的工作，而且对我们已经在做的事情没有增益。”

The Starship on Artemis III will have a full complement of Raptor engines and a docking port on its nose. Blue Moon will not have any main engines when it links up with Orion on Artemis III, only a set of smaller thrusters for pointing control and minor maneuvers.

阿尔忒弥斯 3 号上的星舰将配备全套猛禽（Raptor）发动机，并在头部设有对接端口。而“蓝月”在阿尔忒弥斯 3 号与猎户座对接时，不会配备任何主发动机，仅有一组用于姿态控制和微小机动的较小推进器。

“With Blue, you don’t have a BE-7 engine, you don’t have the cryo tanks,” Creech said. “They’re representing the mass of the lander when it comes back from the surface because we think that’s the most critical case. But it does have the crew cabin.”

“对于蓝色起源，你没有 BE-7 发动机，也没有低温储罐，”克里奇说。“它们代表了着陆器从月面返回时的质量，因为我们认为这是最关键的情况。但它确实拥有乘员舱。”

While there’s no Starship life support demonstration on Artemis III, NASA added a new milestone to SpaceX’s Moon lander contract to test parts of its crew cabin on the ground. The additional testing involves construction of a prototype crew compartment, called Cabin One, at SpaceX’s test site at Starbase, Texas.

虽然阿尔忒弥斯 3 号没有星舰的生命保障演示，但 NASA 在 SpaceX 的月球着陆器合同中增加了一个新的里程碑，即在地面测试其乘员舱的部分组件。这项额外的测试包括在 SpaceX 位于德克萨斯州星际基地的测试场建造一个名为“一号舱”（Cabin One）的乘员舱原型。

NASA levied new test requirements on Blue Origin, too. The company plans to launch its first Blue Moon test mission early next year after a monthslong delay caused by a rocket explosion on Blue Origin’s launch pad in May. This version of Blue Moon, known as Mark 1, has a single hydrogen-fueled BE-7 main engine. Future human-rated landers from Blue Origin will have three.

NASA 也对蓝色起源提出了新的测试要求。在 5 月份因火箭在发射台上爆炸导致数月延误后，该公司计划于明年年初发射其首次“蓝月”测试任务。这个版本的“蓝月”被称为 Mark 1，配备一台氢燃料 BE-7 主发动机。蓝色起源未来的载人着陆器将配备三台。

“One of the things we added into the program in the last year is, we really felt like we needed a test of the three-engine configuration,” Creech said.

“我们在过去一年中加入项目的一项内容是，我们确实认为需要对三发动机配置进行测试，”克里奇说。
