Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.23454 (cs) [Submitted on 17 Nov 2026] Title: LusoClin: A Synthetic Clinical Note Dataset for Medical Information Retrieval in European Portuguese Authors: Carla Teixeira Lopes, Daniel Félix.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.23454 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 17 日] 标题：LusoClin：用于欧洲葡萄牙语医学信息检索的合成临床记录数据集 作者：Carla Teixeira Lopes, Daniel Félix。

Abstract: Access to large-scale clinical text collections is crucial for advancing medical information retrieval research, including clinical note search, patient cohort identification, and domain-adapted retrieval models. However, real clinical notes are rarely available due to privacy regulations, which particularly limits progress for low-resource languages such as European Portuguese.

摘要：获取大规模临床文本集对于推进医学信息检索研究至关重要，包括临床记录搜索、患者队列识别和领域自适应检索模型。然而，由于隐私法规的限制，真实的临床记录很难获得，这尤其限制了欧洲葡萄牙语等低资源语言的研究进展。

We present LusoClin, a publicly released dataset of synthetic clinical notes in European Portuguese, created to support privacy-preserving research on clinical text retrieval. LusoClin was generated by pre-processing real clinical data, fine-tuning the open-source Llama 3.2 (3B) instruction-tuned model using Quantized Low-Rank Adaptation, and applying prompt-based clinical note generation.

我们推出了 LusoClin，这是一个公开的欧洲葡萄牙语合成临床记录数据集，旨在支持临床文本检索的隐私保护研究。LusoClin 是通过预处理真实临床数据、使用量化低秩自适应（QLoRA）微调开源 Llama 3.2 (3B) 指令微调模型，并应用基于提示的临床记录生成技术而创建的。

To facilitate semantic indexing and clinical search, the notes are automatically annotated with clinical entities using a BERT-based model. We evaluate LusoClin in terms of utility, fidelity, diversity, and privacy using automatic metrics and human evaluation.

为了促进语义索引和临床搜索，这些记录使用基于 BERT 的模型自动标注了临床实体。我们通过自动指标和人工评估，从实用性、保真度、多样性和隐私性等方面对 LusoClin 进行了评估。

Results indicate that the dataset captures clinically relevant language patterns and can support downstream IR applications such as clinical document retrieval and domain-specific model adaptation, although annotators can often distinguish synthetic from real notes and some medical inconsistencies persist.

结果表明，该数据集捕捉到了临床相关的语言模式，并能支持临床文档检索和特定领域模型适配等下游信息检索（IR）应用，尽管评估人员通常能区分合成记录与真实记录，且仍存在一些医学上的不一致之处。
