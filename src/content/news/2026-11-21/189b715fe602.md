Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.23410 (cs) [Submitted on 17 Nov 2026] Title: RFBench: A Simulator-Grounded Multimodal Benchmark for Passive RF Layout Design and Understanding Authors: Jiarui Cui, Boxun Yan.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.23410 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 17 日] 标题：RFBench：一个用于无源射频布局设计与理解的模拟器基础多模态基准测试。作者：Jiarui Cui, Boxun Yan。

Abstract: While multimodal language models can interpret diagrams and generate structured outputs, their capacity to connect passive radio-frequency (RF) design intent, layout geometry, and electromagnetic behavior remains largely untested. To address this gap, we introduce RFBench, a simulator-grounded dataset and benchmark for passive RF layout design and understanding.

摘要：尽管多模态语言模型能够解读图表并生成结构化输出，但它们在连接无源射频 (RF) 设计意图、布局几何形状和电磁行为方面的能力在很大程度上仍未得到验证。为了填补这一空白，我们引入了 RFBench，这是一个用于无源射频布局设计与理解的模拟器基础数据集和基准测试。

The dataset contains 10,000 two-port planar layouts simulated using ADS Momentum, with each layout represented by editable geometric primitives, a raster image, a 16x16 occupancy grid, complex S-parameters at 201 frequency points, a structural category, and an RF response label.

该数据集包含 10,000 个使用 ADS Momentum 模拟的双端口平面布局，每个布局由可编辑的几何图元、栅格图像、16x16 占用网格、201 个频率点的复数 S 参数、结构类别以及射频响应标签表示。

Building on these aligned representations, we define seven tasks spanning specification-conditioned layout generation, representation translation, response prediction, simulator-based validation, design diagnosis and improvement, structural understanding, and response classification.

基于这些对齐的表示，我们定义了七项任务，涵盖了基于规范的布局生成、表示转换、响应预测、基于模拟器的验证、设计诊断与改进、结构理解以及响应分类。

Extensive baselines across seven multimodal language models reveal systematic weaknesses in generating structurally valid layouts and predicting electromagnetically faithful responses. We show that RF-specific fine-tuning increases T1a structural validity from 0.0% to 83.2% and the T3 primitive-input within-2-dB rate from 17.7% to 62.8%, demonstrating the value of domain adaptation while leaving considerable room for further progress.

对七种多模态语言模型进行的广泛基准测试揭示了它们在生成结构有效的布局和预测电磁准确的响应方面存在系统性弱点。我们展示了针对射频的微调将 T1a 结构有效性从 0.0% 提高到了 83.2%，并将 T3 图元输入 2dB 以内的准确率从 17.7% 提高到了 62.8%，这证明了领域适应的价值，同时也留下了相当大的进一步改进空间。

RFBench establishes a common testbed for evaluating multimodal RF capabilities and guiding the development of models that connect design intent and layout representations with physical behavior.

RFBench 建立了一个通用的测试平台，用于评估多模态射频能力，并指导能够将设计意图和布局表示与物理行为相连接的模型开发。
