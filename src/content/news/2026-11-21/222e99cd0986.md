Abstract: The rapid scale-up of AI infrastructure has made datacenter reliability a cornerstone of the cloud business economy. Since hardware failures in hyper-scale AI clusters can stall multi-million dollar training workloads, the downtime of individual machines can cause outsized economic losses. Minimizing machine downtime, therefore, is more important than ever.

摘要：人工智能基础设施的快速扩展已使数据中心可靠性成为云商业经济的基石。由于超大规模人工智能集群中的硬件故障可能会导致价值数百万美元的训练工作负载停滞，单台机器的停机时间可能会造成巨大的经济损失。因此，最大限度地减少机器停机时间比以往任何时候都更加重要。

To address this, we present Hardware Diagnoser (HardwareDx), an AI-driven hardware diagnosis system that applies a graph foundation model approach to automated datacenter maintenance. It allows a single model to diagnose many platforms and accelerate onboarding of new hardware products.

为了解决这个问题，我们提出了硬件诊断器（HardwareDx），这是一个人工智能驱动的硬件诊断系统，它将图基础模型方法应用于自动化数据中心维护。它允许单个模型诊断多个平台，并加速新硬件产品的上线。

Deployed at production scale at Google, HardwareDx yields substantial efficiency gains across diverse compute architectures. Real-world evaluations demonstrate that it reduces production downtime by 18.3% on GPU platforms and 16.1% on TPUs. Furthermore, it significantly accelerates hardware deployment, cutting quality assurance (QA) time by 28.3% for GPUs and 25.1% for TPUs.

HardwareDx 在 Google 的生产规模上进行了部署，在各种计算架构中均产生了显著的效率提升。现实世界的评估表明，它将 GPU 平台上的生产停机时间减少了 18.3%，将 TPU 平台上的停机时间减少了 16.1%。此外，它显著加快了硬件部署速度，将 GPU 的质量保证（QA）时间缩短了 28.3%，将 TPU 的质量保证时间缩短了 25.1%。

These results establish HardwareDx as a field-tested solution for maximizing the reliability and cost efficiency of next generation AI fleets. Comments: Subjects: Artificial Intelligence (cs.AI); Hardware Architecture (cs.AR).
