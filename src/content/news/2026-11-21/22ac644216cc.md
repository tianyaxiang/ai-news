Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.25108 (cs) [Submitted on 18 Nov 2026] Title: Long-Range Video Presentation Attack Detection: A Three-Class Benchmark Across 1-100 m and Two Sensor Types Authors: Lambert Igene, Mohammad Zahir Uddin Chowdhury, Afzal Hossain, Sandip Purnapatra, Peter Johnson, Stephanie Schuckers.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.25108 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 18 日] 标题：远程视频呈现攻击检测：跨越 1-100 米距离和两种传感器的三分类基准测试。作者：Lambert Igene, Mohammad Zahir Uddin Chowdhury, Afzal Hossain, Sandip Purnapatra, Peter Johnson, Stephanie Schuckers。

Abstract: Face presentation attack detection (PAD) systems deployed in border control and airport surveillance are constrained by a critical assumption: subjects are captured at close range, typically under one meter. No existing benchmark systematically addresses multi-distance capture, diverse environmental conditions, and concurrent spoofing and occlusion-based attacks, leaving deployed models underprepared for real-world scenarios.

摘要：部署在边境管制和机场监控中的人脸呈现攻击检测（PAD）系统受到一个关键假设的限制：拍摄对象是在近距离（通常在一米以内）被捕获的。目前尚无基准测试能够系统地解决多距离捕获、多样化环境条件以及欺骗和基于遮挡的并发攻击问题，这使得已部署的模型在应对现实场景时准备不足。

We introduce a video-based PAD dataset of 374,000+ frames from 261 unique identities collected across five standoff distances (1 m-100 m) under indoor and outdoor conditions using a DSLR camera (C) and a mobile sensor (M), with three-class labels: live, spoof, and props (occlusions); models are trained and evaluated on video clips rather than single frames.

我们引入了一个基于视频的 PAD 数据集，包含来自 261 个独特身份的 374,000 多帧图像，这些图像是在室内和室外条件下，使用单反相机 (C) 和移动传感器 (M) 在五个防区距离（1 米至 100 米）内采集的，并带有三个类别标签：真实、欺骗和道具（遮挡）；模型是在视频片段而非单帧图像上进行训练和评估的。

We fine-tune and benchmark four CNN architectures: MobileNetV3, MobileNet, EfficientNet, and ResNet-50. The higher-resolution DSLR maintains near-perfect spoof classification rate across all distances, while the lower-resolution mobile sensor degrades sharply beyond 50 m, with props classification rate dropping to 55% at 75 m for MobileNetV3.

我们对四种 CNN 架构进行了微调和基准测试：MobileNetV3、MobileNet、EfficientNet 和 ResNet-50。高分辨率的单反相机在所有距离上都保持了近乎完美的欺骗分类率，而低分辨率的移动传感器在超过 50 米后性能急剧下降，其中 MobileNetV3 在 75 米处的道具分类率降至 55%。

EfficientNet achieves the best overall performance (98.48% accuracy, ACER 2.01%). Generalization tests on unseen attack datasets yield 89-94% accuracy, validating cross-domain robustness. These results establish capture distance and sensor resolution as critical but underexplored axes of PAD difficulty.

EfficientNet 实现了最佳的整体性能（准确率 98.48%，ACER 2.01%）。在未见过的攻击数据集上进行的泛化测试得出了 89-94% 的准确率，验证了跨域鲁棒性。这些结果确立了捕获距离和传感器分辨率作为 PAD 难度中关键但尚未被充分探索的维度。
