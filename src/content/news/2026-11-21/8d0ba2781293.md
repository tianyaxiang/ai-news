Save this storySave this storyListening to the in-game radio stations while you cruise around in Grand Theft Auto VI, you might hear a recruitment ad for the Vice City Police Department, which makes no secret of the kind of maladjusted psychos it's looking for.“Have you always dreamed of abusing your power without consequence?” one spot begins. “Well, now you can, when you join the Vice City Police Department, VCPD. You’ll have fun learning the ins and outs of applying excessive force.” The commercial goes on to suggest that cadets will be trained in racial profiling and planting evidence.

当你一边在《侠盗猎车手6》（GTA VI）中驾驶，一边收听游戏内的广播电台时，可能会听到一则针对罪恶都市警察局（VCPD）的招聘广告，它毫不掩饰自己正在寻找那种心理扭曲的疯子。“你是否一直梦想着能毫无后果地滥用职权？”广告开头这样说道，“好吧，现在加入罪恶都市警察局（VCPD），你就能实现了。你会乐于学习如何运用过度武力。”广告接着暗示，学员们将接受种族定性（racial profiling）和栽赃陷害方面的培训。

Given that blistering analysis of American law enforcement, you might imagine that actual cops aren’t big fans of the latest GTA title, which was released after a 13-year wait on Thursday. Yet as millions of gamers have started to explore every stash house and back alley in the sun-drenched wilderness of a fictionalized South Florida, even police departments have attempted to exploit this monocultural mania to elevate their public messaging or recruit new officers.

鉴于对美国执法部门如此尖锐的剖析，你可能会认为真正的警察并不喜欢这款等待了13年才于周四发布的游戏。然而，随着数百万玩家开始探索这个虚构的南佛罗里达阳光地带的每一个藏身处和后巷，甚至连警察局也试图利用这种单一文化的狂热来提升其公共宣传效果或招募新警员。

It’s a curious strategy given that the series rewards theft and drug dealing while enticing you to run over pedestrians, but evidently the mere presence of law enforcement in the games (as the primary antagonists, mind you) was enough to inspire waves of AI slop celebrating the thin blue line.

考虑到该系列游戏奖励盗窃和毒品交易，并诱导玩家碾压行人，这是一种奇怪的策略。但显然，执法部门在游戏中（请注意，是作为主要反派）的存在，足以激发出一波又一波赞美“细蓝线”（thin blue line）的AI垃圾内容。

An Instagram channel of the San Diego Police Department, for example, on Thursday posted an AI-generated image of an SDPD cruiser set against a sunset over the San Diego Bay. Its neon pinks and purples match the saturated aesthetic of GTA, and in case there was any doubt what the department was going for, the nonsensical phrase “Join San Diego PD VI” appears at the top. “Want to drive a police car, we can make that happen!” reads the caption. “Apply Now!”

例如，圣地亚哥警察局的一个Instagram账号周四发布了一张由AI生成的图片，背景是圣地亚哥湾的日落，画面中有一辆圣地亚哥警局的巡逻车。其霓虹粉和紫色的色调与GTA的高饱和度美学相吻合，为了防止人们对该部门的意图产生怀疑，图片顶部还出现了“加入圣地亚哥警局VI”这种毫无意义的短语。配文写道：“想开警车吗？我们可以帮你实现！立即申请！”

Another police department in Washington state shared GTA-like imagery on its Instagram page, encouraging viewers to try “the real-life version” of GTA VI by pursuing a career in law enforcement. “No cheat codes,” the caption advertised. “No respawns. Just real people, real challenges, and a career that makes a difference.” A wave of self-serious GTA slop also flooded the feeds of police departments in Canada, from Edmonton to Toronto—though the Calgary Police Service tried something more lighthearted by having one of their traffic officers react to clips of dangerous and illegal driving maneuvers in the game.

华盛顿州的另一个警察局也在其Instagram页面上分享了类似GTA的图像，鼓励观众通过从事执法工作来尝试“现实版”的GTA VI。“没有作弊码，”配文宣传道，“没有重生。只有真实的人、真实的挑战，以及一份能带来改变的职业。”一波自以为是的GTA垃圾内容也充斥了加拿大从埃德蒙顿到多伦多等地的警察局社交媒体，尽管卡尔加里警察局尝试了一种更轻松的方式，让其一名交通警察对游戏中危险和违法的驾驶动作片段做出反应。

Among police departments that were not seeking to add to their ranks through GTA VI fever, many posted content aimed at reminding people that laws exist in real life. “Keep It In The Game VI,” advised a typical post on the Instagram account of the Glendale Police Department in California, complete with a GTA-fied police cruiser and view of the city. “Follow posted speed limits, respect the law, and keep your wanted level at ZERO!” the caption warned. The Denver Police Department, in similarly scolding fashion, informed followers: “Grand theft auto is still illegal.”

在那些没有试图通过GTA VI热潮来扩充队伍的警察局中，许多部门发布的内容旨在提醒人们现实生活中法律依然存在。“留在游戏里吧VI，”加利福尼亚州格伦代尔警察局Instagram账号上的一篇典型帖子建议道，配图是一辆“GTA化”的警车和城市景观。“遵守限速，尊重法律，并将你的通缉等级保持在零！”配文警告说。丹佛警察局也以类似的训诫口吻告知粉丝：“侠盗猎车（Grand theft auto）在现实中依然是违法的。”

The communications teams for these agencies should have perhaps reconsidered trying to farm engagement and clout from the massive success of GTA VI. As many commenters are noting, the game portrays cops as corrupt and quick to violence. Players have discovered that the Vice City squad will brutalize you if you start filming them during a questionable arrest. One clip showed a cop pulling his weapon on a character who had done nothing worse than stand in the street to take a selfie.

这些机构的公关团队或许应该重新考虑是否要从GTA VI的巨大成功中获取关注度和影响力。正如许多评论者所指出的，游戏将警察描绘成腐败且容易诉诸暴力的人。玩家们发现，如果你在罪恶都市警察进行可疑逮捕时拍摄他们，他们会残暴地对待你。一段视频显示，一名警察对一个仅仅站在街上自拍的角色拔出了武器。

A certain dose of this authoritarian cruelty ought to appeal to the right-wing cranks upset that GTA VI is too “woke” when it comes to gender, race, and politics overall. It’s a little different when the most visible villains of the game are choosing to overlook its vicious take on their profession in order to spam epic social media posts.

这种程度的威权式残暴本应吸引那些对GTA VI在性别、种族和政治方面过于“觉醒”（woke）而感到不满的右翼怪人。但当游戏中最为显眼的反派们为了在社交媒体上发布“史诗级”帖子而选择无视游戏对其职业的恶毒刻画时，情况就变得有些不同了。

Fox News, just for comparison, dutifully took the tired law-and-order line on GTA VI, with talking heads aghast at kids’ exposure to virtual gun violence (never mind actual gun deaths in the US) and an atmosphere of moral depravity. Elon Musk famously declared that he couldn’t play GTA games because they compelled you to shoot cops.

相比之下，福克斯新闻尽职尽责地对GTA VI采取了陈旧的“法律与秩序”立场，评论员们对孩子们接触虚拟枪支暴力感到震惊（完全不顾美国现实中的枪支死亡事件），并指责游戏营造了一种道德沦丧的氛围。埃隆·马斯克曾宣称他无法玩GTA游戏，因为游戏强迫玩家射杀警察。

Both stances, however insincere, suggest a modicum of regard for law enforcement that the police departments themselves immediately jettisoned in favor of GTA cringe. They of course won’t win any cool points for it, but at least this way they can pretend to connect with the general population.

这两种立场，无论多么虚伪，都表现出对执法部门的一丝尊重，而警察局自己却为了蹭GTA的热度而立即抛弃了这种尊重。他们当然不会因此赢得任何好感，但至少通过这种方式，他们可以假装与大众建立了联系。

More baffling still is that the Russian army has generated its own GTA VI recruitment ads to blast out on its Telegram channel, and NATO has trumpeted its defensive security alliance with military-industrial GTA sludge, boasting that “even the best gamers would have a challenge” eluding fighter jets and submarines. Trying to parse Vice President JD Vance’s depiction of a Trumpian “golden age” with GTA graphics is a fool’s errand. In the most dystopian development of all, a US Army battalion in Georgia granted 43 eligible soldiers four days off to play the new GTA in return for reenlisting for another two years of service.

更令人费解的是，俄罗斯军队在Telegram频道上发布了自己制作的GTA VI招聘广告，北约也大肆宣传其与军工复合体GTA垃圾内容的防御安全联盟，吹嘘说“即使是最顶尖的玩家在躲避战斗机和潜艇时也会面临挑战”。试图用GTA的画面来解读副总统JD·万斯对特朗普式“黄金时代”的描述简直是徒劳。最反乌托邦的发展是，佐治亚州的一个美国陆军营批准了43名符合条件的士兵四天假期去玩新GTA，作为他们续签两年服役合同的交换条件。

What do any of these institutions or their missions have to do with a video game where you rob convenience stores in flip-flops? Doesn’t matter. When the zeitgeist is this all-consuming, you fire up ChatGPT and find an angle, no matter how confusing. Surely someone, somewhere, will be tricked into thinking you have anything to do with it.

这些机构或其使命与一款让你穿着人字拖抢劫便利店的电子游戏有什么关系？没关系。当时代精神如此具有吞噬性时，你只需打开ChatGPT找个切入点，无论它多么令人困惑。总会有人在某个地方被误导，认为你与这款游戏有什么关联。
