Rockstar says GTA VI made $3.79 billion in its first day.

Rockstar 表示《GTA VI》首日收入达到 37.9 亿美元。

GTA VI is finally here, and it’s already a huge hit.

《GTA VI》终于发布了，并且已经取得了巨大的成功。

GTA VI earned $3.79 billion in its first day of sales, which set “a new record for entertainment across any medium,” according to an announcement from Rockstar Games. The game also sold 32.5 million preorder copies.

根据 Rockstar Games 的公告，《GTA VI》首日销售额达到 37.9 亿美元，创下了“任何媒介娱乐产业的新纪录”。该游戏还售出了 3250 万份预购拷贝。

“It is with immense gratitude that we enter a new chapter of Rockstar Games with the release of Grand Theft Auto VI,” Sam Houser, Rockstar’s president and founder, said in a statement. “We are incredibly humbled by the response from players around the world and we couldn’t be more excited to share this moment with you.” Rockstar claims that the game’s release, which happened Thursday, was “the biggest entertainment launch in history.”

Rockstar 总裁兼创始人 Sam Houser 在声明中表示：“怀着无比感激的心情，我们随着《GTA VI》的发布开启了 Rockstar Games 的新篇章。我们对全球玩家的反馈感到受宠若惊，能与你们分享这一时刻，我们感到无比兴奋。”Rockstar 声称，该游戏于周四发布，是“历史上规模最大的娱乐产品发布”。

The news follows leaks, delays, and a huge amount of interest ahead of GTA VI’s launch. (Many other games steered clear of GTA VI to get their time in the sun.) But even with the weight of anticipation placed upon GTA VI, it actually lived up to the hype, my colleague Andrew Webster said in his review.

在此消息发布之前，《GTA VI》经历了泄密、延期以及极高的关注度。（许多其他游戏为了避开锋芒，都选择了错开《GTA VI》的发布时间。）但正如我的同事 Andrew Webster 在评论中所言，尽管《GTA VI》背负着巨大的期待，它确实不负众望。
