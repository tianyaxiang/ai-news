Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.24972 (cs) [Submitted on 16 Nov 2026] Title: No-Harm-Jury A Jury-Based Multi-Dimensional Harm Evaluation Framework for Medical AI Responses Authors: Antonio Puertas Gallardo, Mario Ceresa.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.24972 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 16 日] 标题：No-Harm-Jury：一种用于医疗 AI 回答的基于陪审团的多维危害评估框架 作者：Antonio Puertas Gallardo, Mario Ceresa。

Abstract: We present No-Harm-Jury, a jury-based automated evaluation framework that scores AI-generated medical responses across seven conceptually distinct harm dimensions using a panel of five diverse language models as independent jurors.

摘要：我们提出了 No-Harm-Jury，这是一个基于陪审团的自动化评估框架，它使用由五个不同语言模型组成的专家组作为独立陪审员，对 AI 生成的医疗回答在七个概念上截然不同的危害维度上进行评分。

Responses generated by Ministral-14B on 3,000 questions drawn from PubMedQA, MedQA, and MedMCQA are scored by five jurors across dimensions of informational, social, psychological, autonomy, economic, privacy, and epistemic harm; the 151 empty responses (98 MedQA, 53 MedMCQA) are excluded, leaving 2,849 valid samples.

由 Ministral-14B 对来自 PubMedQA、MedQA 和 MedMCQA 的 3,000 个问题生成的回答，由五名陪审员在信息、社会、心理、自主性、经济、隐私和认知危害等维度上进行评分；其中 151 个空回答（98 个来自 MedQA，53 个来自 MedMCQA）被排除，剩余 2,849 个有效样本。

The median aggregation robustly suppresses individual juror biases while maintaining coverage through complementary specialisation. No existing system combines all five properties of No-Harm-Jury: automated scalable evaluation, five diverse LLM jurors, robust median aggregation (with a reliability-weighted extension), seven conceptually distinct harm dimensions, and application to medical QA benchmarks.

中位数聚合稳健地抑制了单个陪审员的偏见，同时通过互补的专业化保持了覆盖范围。目前没有任何现有系统结合了 No-Harm-Jury 的所有五个特性：自动化可扩展评估、五个不同的 LLM 陪审员、稳健的中位数聚合（带有可靠性加权扩展）、七个概念上不同的危害维度，以及在医疗问答基准测试中的应用。
