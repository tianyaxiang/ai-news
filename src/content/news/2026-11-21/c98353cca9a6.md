Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.24963 (cs) [Submitted on 12 Nov 2026] Title: Physics of Language Models: Part 4.2, Canon Layers at Scale where Synthetic Pretraining Resonates in Reality Authors: Zeyuan Allen-Zhu.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.24963 (cs) [2026年11月12日提交] 标题：语言模型物理学：第4.2部分，合成预训练在现实中产生共鸣的规模化规范层（Canon Layers），作者：Zeyuan Allen-Zhu。

Abstract: Do standard benchmarks see the architectural differences that matter? We study it with 64 from-scratch pretraining runs of Transformers and three linear-model families at 1.3-8B parameters on 1-2T Nemotron-CC tokens, at a cost of roughly 100k USD per 8B run. In academic-scale pretraining (1.3B parameters, 100B tokens), architectural differences can all but disappear when the models are carefully tuned.

摘要：标准基准测试能否看出重要的架构差异？我们通过64次从零开始的预训练运行研究了这一问题，对象包括Transformer和三个线性模型家族，参数规模在1.3B到8B之间，训练数据为1-2T的Nemotron-CC token，每次8B规模的运行成本约为10万美元。在学术规模的预训练（1.3B参数，100B token）中，当模型经过仔细调整时，架构差异几乎可以忽略不计。

Instead, at 1-2T tokens they become measurable: Canon layers, which add local token mixing, consistently improve Transformers, saving about 30% of pretraining tokens for the same MMLU accuracy at 8B. We also introduce GLA5 and GDN2, which improve gated linear attention and Gated DeltaNet with low-rank output gates.

相反，在1-2T token的规模下，这些差异变得可衡量：增加局部token混合的规范层（Canon layers）持续改进了Transformer，在8B规模下达到相同MMLU准确率时，可节省约30%的预训练token。我们还引入了GLA5和GDN2，它们通过低秩输出门改进了门控线性注意力和门控DeltaNet。

Surprisingly, with Canon, the simplest linear model, gated linear attention, matches Gated DeltaNet and outperforms Mamba2 even at 8B/1T. Therefore, the extra machinery of Gated DeltaNet and Mamba2 may do little more than mix neighboring tokens.

令人惊讶的是，有了Canon层，最简单的线性模型——门控线性注意力，在8B/1T规模下也能媲美Gated DeltaNet并优于Mamba2。因此，Gated DeltaNet和Mamba2的额外机制可能仅仅起到了混合相邻token的作用。

Even at 8B/1T, however, high scores on standard benchmarks can hide weaknesses in basic in-context skills. In particular, linear models that match or beat Transformers on these benchmarks cannot, in fact, reliably retrieve information from a short 500-token context.

然而，即使在8B/1T规模下，标准基准测试中的高分也可能掩盖基础上下文技能的弱点。特别是，在这些基准测试中匹配或超越Transformer的线性模型，实际上无法可靠地从500个token的短上下文中检索信息。

Moreover, no model, Transformer or linear, reliably beats random guessing on arguably the simplest two-hop question, stated in a few short sentences, even at this scale. These findings $\textbf{challenge traditional architecture research}$ based primarily on standard benchmark scores.

此外，即使在这一规模下，没有任何模型（无论是Transformer还是线性模型）能在最简单的两跳问题（用几句短句表述）上可靠地胜过随机猜测。这些发现挑战了主要基于标准基准分数的传统架构研究。

In contrast, small synthetic playgrounds, which predicted the architectural differences above at <100M parameters, can reveal what these benchmarks miss. For practitioners, one lesson is to include in-context reasoning examples in pretraining data from an early stage, rather than reserve them for later mid-training, let alone post-training.

相比之下，小型合成实验场在小于100M参数规模下就预测了上述架构差异，能够揭示这些基准测试所遗漏的内容。对于从业者来说，一个经验教训是应从早期阶段就在预训练数据中包含上下文推理示例，而不是将其留到训练中期，更不用说训练后阶段了。
