EXECUTIVE SUMMARY A new report from the AI evaluation nonprofit METR finds that some AI capabilities are continuing to improve along an exponential trajectory—but it also indicates that models aren’t quite as good at tasks that require judgment as they are at coding. The report is an update to the organization’s time horizon plot, which was widely shared—and widely misinterpreted, owing to the complexity of the measures it used.

执行摘要：人工智能评估非营利组织 METR 的一份新报告发现，一些人工智能能力正沿着指数轨迹持续提升，但也表明模型在需要判断力的任务上并不像在编程任务上那样出色。该报告是对该组织“时间跨度图”（time horizon plot）的更新，该图表此前被广泛传播，但由于其所用衡量标准的复杂性，也遭到了广泛误读。

METR evaluated models by having them attempt a large suite of tasks, each of which had also been completed by humans. Each task’s difficulty was scored by how long it took the human testers to finish it—and then models were rated according to the length of the tasks that they could complete. That plot showed that AI systems were rapidly improving—every four months, the length of the tasks that frontier models could successfully complete doubled.

METR 通过让模型尝试大量人类也曾完成的任务来评估它们。每项任务的难度根据人类测试者完成所需的时间来评分，随后根据模型能够完成的任务时长对其进行评级。该图表显示，人工智能系统正在迅速进步——前沿模型能够成功完成的任务时长每四个月就会翻一番。

The updated plot adds more tasks—some that are estimated to take humans far longer than those in the original suite, and others that are more open-ended. One of the biggest questions in AI right now is the extent to which progress on “verifiable” tasks—where it’s possible to check automatically whether an AI system has succeeded or failed—carry over into tasks on which success or failure is harder to measure.

更新后的图表增加了更多任务，其中一些预计耗时远超原始任务集中的任务，另一些则更具开放性。目前人工智能领域最大的问题之一是，“可验证”任务（即可以自动检查人工智能系统是否成功或失败的任务）的进展，在多大程度上可以转化为难以衡量成功或失败的任务。

We know how to teach neural networks how to do verifiable tasks: Researchers have the systems attempt one many times, strengthen the neural pathways that contribute to success, and weaken the pathways that contribute to failures. That’s why AI systems have become so good at coding and math, two domains where it’s possible to determine automatically whether a solution does or doesn’t work.

我们知道如何教神经网络执行可验证的任务：研究人员让系统多次尝试，加强有助于成功的神经通路，并削弱导致失败的通路。这就是为什么人工智能系统在编程和数学方面变得如此出色，因为在这两个领域，可以自动确定解决方案是否有效。

It’s much harder to apply this technique, known as reinforcement learning, to tasks where success is less well defined or takes longer to ascertain—such as choosing a new research direction. For the same reason, it’s tough to evaluate AI performance on these tasks, so METR’s original time horizon plot didn’t include any of them. But it’s precisely this type of task that will prove essential if AI systems are to automate research and development.

将这种被称为强化学习的技术应用于成功定义不明确或需要较长时间才能确定的任务（例如选择新的研究方向）要困难得多。出于同样的原因，评估人工智能在这些任务上的表现非常困难，因此 METR 最初的时间跨度图没有包含任何此类任务。但如果人工智能系统要实现研发自动化，恰恰是这类任务至关重要。

And AI research and development is an especially hot topic right now. Parts of the AI community are still reeling from a different, heavily redacted METR report from earlier this month, which claimed that AI might be speeding up the pace of AI progress at Anthropic by a factor of two. That report sparked fears that the company (which just had a blockbuster IPO) could soon achieve full recursive self-improvement, a regime in which AI systems autonomously design their own successors with no human involvement.

人工智能研发目前是一个特别热门的话题。人工智能社区的部分成员仍对本月初另一份经过大量删减的 METR 报告感到震惊，该报告称人工智能可能会使 Anthropic 的人工智能进步速度提高一倍。这份报告引发了人们的担忧，即该公司（刚刚进行了大规模 IPO）可能很快实现完全的递归自我改进，即人工智能系统在没有人类参与的情况下自主设计其继任者。

METR researchers have worked to make sure their new suite of tasks includes some of the fuzzier, judgment-laden ones that could be most relevant to AI research and development. In one task, they ask models to design new AI evaluations that METR could use in its research and then have human METR employees score the results. Even though the most advanced models are now better than almost every human at software engineering, here they fall short: Not one of the AI-designed evaluations was good enough for METR employees to consider it useful for their research.

METR 的研究人员努力确保他们的新任务集包含一些更模糊、更依赖判断力的任务，这些任务可能与人工智能研发最相关。在一项任务中，他们要求模型设计 METR 可用于研究的新型人工智能评估方法，然后由 METR 的人类员工对结果进行评分。尽管最先进的模型在软件工程方面现在已经超过了几乎所有人，但在这一点上它们却表现不佳：没有一个人工智能设计的评估方案好到足以让 METR 员工认为其对研究有用。

In another task, models have to score philosophical arguments. This task is intended to capture the extent to which models can exercise good judgment—though METR writes in the report that employees disagree about how relevant the task is for automation of AI R&D. On this task, too, models fell short of human performance.

在另一项任务中，模型必须对哲学论点进行评分。这项任务旨在捕捉模型在多大程度上能够运用良好的判断力——尽管 METR 在报告中写道，员工们对于该任务与人工智能研发自动化之间的相关性存在分歧。在这项任务上，模型的表现也未达到人类水平。

Though it’s difficult to score these new tasks—and even harder to determine what the scores mean—experts I spoke with appreciate that METR is incorporating new types of evaluations into its work. “We’re at the point where AI evaluations need to get more creative,” says Sayash Kapoor, a computer scientist and incoming professor at UC Berkeley. “Trying to push the envelope of the space of evaluations is a welcome change.”

虽然这些新任务很难评分，而且更难确定分数意味着什么，但我交谈过的专家们都很赞赏 METR 将新型评估纳入其工作之中。“我们正处于人工智能评估需要更具创造力的阶段，”加州大学伯克利分校的计算机科学家、候任教授 Sayash Kapoor 表示，“试图拓展评估领域的边界是一个受欢迎的改变。”

Kapoor says he wasn’t surprised to see that models fall short of performance on more open-ended tasks, such as designing an AI evaluation—in a preprint from August, he and his collaborators reported that AI models are not yet able to reproduce human computer science research on their own. And in a Wednesday blog post, OpenAI reported that its models don’t do as well on the real-world tasks its employees assign them as they do on the easier, more verifiable tasks in METR’s original suite.

Kapoor 表示，看到模型在更开放的任务（如设计人工智能评估）上表现不佳，他并不感到惊讶——在 8 月份的一份预印本中，他和合作者报告称，人工智能模型尚无法独立复现人类的计算机科学研究。而在周三的一篇博客文章中，OpenAI 报告称，其模型在员工分配的现实世界任务中的表现，不如在 METR 原始任务集中那些更简单、更易验证的任务中表现得好。

Still, people disagree about how to interpret current performance differences on easily verifiable and hard-to-verify tasks, Kapoor says. Though he thinks that research taste—the ability to choose promising questions, hypotheses, and experiments—will prove a major bottleneck in automating AI R&D, others contend that models trained on easy-to-verify tasks will eventually be able to successfully complete open-ended tasks as well.

不过，Kapoor 指出，人们对于如何解读当前在易验证和难验证任务上的表现差异仍存在分歧。虽然他认为研究品味（即选择有前景的问题、假设和实验的能力）将成为人工智能研发自动化的主要瓶颈，但其他人则认为，在易验证任务上训练的模型最终也将能够成功完成开放式任务。

And progress on verifiable tasks doesn’t seem to be slowing down. Even if the models do lag behind human performance on open-ended tasks right now, the new report suggests that they might be getting better. Some of the newest models, such as Opus 5.5, tend to achieve what METR calls “borderline above the bar” performance on the evaluation design tasks—not yet usable for METR's research, but promising. Just a year ago, every model was scoring in the “clearly below the bar” category.

可验证任务的进展似乎并没有放缓。即使模型目前在开放式任务上落后于人类表现，新报告也表明它们可能正在进步。一些最新的模型（如 Opus 5.5）在评估设计任务上往往能达到 METR 所称的“勉强及格”水平——虽然还不能用于 METR 的研究，但前景可期。就在一年前，所有模型的得分都处于“明显不及格”类别。
