Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.25113 (cs) [Submitted on 18 Nov 2026] Title: Do Deep Stereo Models Learn Shape Priors? Authors: Linbo Tang, Aneel Damaraju, Todd Zickler.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.25113 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 18 日] 标题：深度立体模型是否学习了形状先验？作者：Linbo Tang, Aneel Damaraju, Todd Zickler。

Abstract: Localizing object boundaries is important for embodied visual systems, since small boundary errors can change the inferred geometry of physical surfaces. We study whether deep stereo models produce boundaries consistent with simple shape priors when local image and disparity cues are weak.

摘要：定位物体边界对于具身视觉系统非常重要，因为微小的边界误差可能会改变对物理表面几何形状的推断。我们研究了当局部图像和视差线索较弱时，深度立体模型生成的边界是否与简单的形状先验一致。

We create a synthetic stereo dataset consisting of objects drawn from a highly constrained family of shapes, with randomized depth, slant, color and texture. Despite the simplicity of this shape space, we find that several state-of-the-art stereo models retain systematic boundary errors, even when finetuned or trained from scratch on the dataset.

我们创建了一个合成立体数据集，其中包含从高度受限的形状族中提取的物体，并具有随机的深度、倾斜度、颜色和纹理。尽管这个形状空间很简单，但我们发现即使在数据集上进行微调或从头开始训练，几种最先进的立体模型仍然保留了系统性的边界误差。

We then introduce a simple generative stereo model that jointly predicts disparity and a distance field representing object boundaries. The model improves boundary localization and produces contours that adhere more closely to the underlying shape space.

随后，我们引入了一种简单的生成式立体模型，该模型联合预测视差和表示物体边界的距离场。该模型改善了边界定位，并生成了更贴合底层形状空间的轮廓。

These results expose limitations in the boundary predictions of current stereo architectures when local evidence is weak, and motivate further study of whether generative models can better combine regional grouping evidence with global geometric structure.

这些结果揭示了当前立体架构在局部证据较弱时边界预测的局限性，并促使人们进一步研究生成模型是否能更好地将区域分组证据与全局几何结构相结合。
