Google is making some big changes to its smart home offerings, including killing the Nest app.

谷歌正在对其智能家居产品进行重大调整，其中包括关闭 Nest 应用程序。

Google is announcing some significant changes to its smart home software. Starting next year, the company will shut down the Nest app in favor of the Google Home app and retire Google Assistant on its smart home devices.

谷歌宣布对其智能家居软件进行重大调整。从明年开始，该公司将关闭 Nest 应用程序，转而支持 Google Home 应用程序，并停用其智能家居设备上的 Google Assistant。

The Nest app shutdown takes place on January 25th, 2027, meaning you’ll have to manage your Nest products, including Nest thermostats, cameras, and Nest Protect alarms, from the Google Home app instead. If you get the Google Home app set up, Google will move over your Nest devices, including cameras and video history, to the Home app on that January 25th date. The company will also automatically transfer Nest Aware (1st Gen) subscriptions to Google Home Premium. Google says it will begin deleting Nest account data on March 25th, 2027.

Nest 应用程序将于 2027 年 1 月 25 日关闭，这意味着您必须改用 Google Home 应用程序来管理您的 Nest 产品，包括 Nest 恒温器、摄像头和 Nest Protect 报警器。如果您设置了 Google Home 应用程序，谷歌将在 1 月 25 日当天将您的 Nest 设备（包括摄像头和视频历史记录）迁移到 Home 应用程序中。该公司还将自动把 Nest Aware（第一代）订阅转移到 Google Home Premium。谷歌表示，将于 2027 年 3 月 25 日开始删除 Nest 账户数据。

The changes for Google Assistant will take place later: On August 2nd, 2027, the Gemini for Home voice assistant will replace Google Assistant on smart speakers and smart displays, the company says. Google argues that Gemini for Home allows users to ask more complex questions and speak naturally instead of needing to use dedicated commands or specific language.

Google Assistant 的相关调整将在稍后进行：该公司表示，2027 年 8 月 2 日，Gemini for Home 语音助手将取代智能音箱和智能显示屏上的 Google Assistant。谷歌认为，Gemini for Home 允许用户提出更复杂的问题并进行自然对话，而无需使用专用指令或特定语言。

Gemini for Home was announced last year, so it’s not exactly a shock that Google is going to move on from Google Assistant in the smart home (especially when the company also killed Google Assistant on phones earlier this year). The company says it plans to share more updates about the changes ahead of the August 2027 transition.

Gemini for Home 于去年发布，因此谷歌将在智能家居领域放弃 Google Assistant 并不令人意外（尤其是该公司今年早些时候已经在手机上停用了 Google Assistant）。该公司表示，计划在 2027 年 8 月的过渡期之前分享有关这些调整的更多更新。
