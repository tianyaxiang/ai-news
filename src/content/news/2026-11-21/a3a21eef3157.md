Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.25166 (cs) [Submitted on 18 Nov 2026] Title: CoRelate: Coordinated Human-Object Motion Synthesis through Interaction Relations Authors: Zhiwen Qiu, Daisy Lin, Diyu Zhou, Jialin Wu, Rosita Ji, Saleh Kalantari.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.25166 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 18 日] 标题：CoRelate：通过交互关系实现协调的人机运动合成 作者：Zhiwen Qiu, Daisy Lin, Diyu Zhou, Jialin Wu, Rosita Ji, Saleh Kalantari。

Abstract: Human-object motion synthesis requires coordinated human participation as objects move toward specified goals. However, reaching object targets does not ensure that contact is established and maintained, while adjustments that improve contact may interfere with task completion.

摘要：人机运动合成要求在物体向指定目标移动时，人类参与者能够进行协调。然而，到达物体目标点并不能确保接触的建立与维持，而旨在改善接触的调整可能会干扰任务的完成。

We introduce CoRelate, a diffusion framework that coordinates full-body human-object motion through explicit interaction relations and task feedback for rigid and articulated objects. We represent intended participation through hand-part relations that specify the participating hand, its part-relative reference position, and participation interval.

我们引入了 CoRelate，这是一个扩散框架，通过显式的交互关系和针对刚体及关节物体的任务反馈，来协调全身人机运动。我们通过手部部件关系来表示预期的参与，这些关系指定了参与的手部、其相对于部件的参考位置以及参与的时间间隔。

These relations serve both as generation conditions and as references for measuring interaction realization. At each denoising step, a temporal refiner trained jointly with the generator combines relation deviations, task residuals, and motion features from the current prediction to jointly update human and object motion across the sequence.

这些关系既作为生成条件，也作为衡量交互实现情况的参考。在每个去噪步骤中，与生成器联合训练的时间细化器会结合关系偏差、任务残差以及来自当前预测的运动特征，以在整个序列中联合更新人类和物体的运动。

After generation, temporal motion correction addresses remaining contact failures by optimizing local motion segments while preserving object trajectories and protected human motion. Experiments demonstrate improved object-goal satisfaction, contact F1, and complete interaction success over the evaluated baselines.

生成后，时间运动校正通过优化局部运动片段来解决剩余的接触失败问题，同时保留物体轨迹和受保护的人体运动。实验表明，与评估的基准相比，该方法在物体目标满足度、接触 F1 分数以及完整交互成功率方面均有所提升。

Ablation studies support the benefits of relation conditioning and the complementary roles of task and relation feedback.

消融研究支持了关系条件化的益处，以及任务反馈与关系反馈之间的互补作用。
