Title: ICARE: cross-domain text classification with incremental class-aware representation and distillation learning

标题：ICARE：具有增量类感知表示和蒸馏学习的跨域文本分类

The rise of powerful, fine-tuned large language models (LLMs) necessitates robust natural language inference (NLI) systems. These NLI systems ideally train across various tasks, encompassing diverse concepts like sentiment analysis, news classification, and coreference resolution. However, this continual, multi-tasking approach can lead to catastrophic forgetting, where previously learned tasks are lost, especially in domains lacking specific labels.

功能强大的微调大语言模型（LLM）的兴起，使得构建稳健的自然语言推理（NLI）系统成为必要。这些 NLI 系统理想情况下应在各种任务中进行训练，涵盖情感分析、新闻分类和共指消解等多种概念。然而，这种持续的多任务处理方法可能导致灾难性遗忘，即先前学习的任务会丢失，尤其是在缺乏特定标签的领域中。

Rehearsing past examples with labels can further exacerbate this issue by causing overfitting, hindering the system's ability to classify new categories. To tackle these challenges, this research proposes ICARE, a novel framework with two key components: an instance selection module and a knowledge distillation method. The former is based on representation learning, which meticulously selects training examples to present to the NLI system.

使用带标签的过去示例进行复习可能会加剧这一问题，导致过拟合，从而阻碍系统对新类别进行分类的能力。为了应对这些挑战，本研究提出了 ICARE，这是一个包含两个关键组件的新型框架：实例选择模块和知识蒸馏方法。前者基于表示学习，能够精心挑选训练示例以呈现给 NLI 系统。

The latter, knowledge distillation with feature and logit-level transfer, facilitates knowledge transfer between tasks. This leverages pruning techniques and optimized objective functions to transfer both essential feature-level and decision-making (logit-level) knowledge, mitigating catastrophic forgetting while adapting to new patterns. Extensive evaluations on benchmark text classification datasets have demonstrated ICARE's effectiveness.

后者通过特征和逻辑层面的迁移进行知识蒸馏，促进了任务间的知识传递。该方法利用剪枝技术和优化的目标函数来迁移必要的特征层面和决策层面（逻辑层面）知识，在适应新模式的同时减轻了灾难性遗忘。在基准文本分类数据集上的广泛评估证明了 ICARE 的有效性。

The framework achieves a significant improvement in target accuracy and reduction in the ``forgetting metric''. These results solidify ICARE's ability to preserve and leverage learned knowledge across diverse tasks, ultimately enhancing the adaptability and performance of NLI systems.

该框架在目标准确率上取得了显著提升，并降低了“遗忘指标”。这些结果巩固了 ICARE 在不同任务中保存和利用已学知识的能力，最终增强了 NLI 系统的适应性和性能。
