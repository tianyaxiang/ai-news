In fact, no brand even comes close to Pura's sheer breadth of available fragrances—with more than 150 options from big names like Anthropologie, Nest, LAFCO, Brooklyn Candle Company, Studio McGee, and more. These diffusers are also highly convenient to use in that you can control them from your phone via an app (the Home, Plus, and Car also have buttons on the actual devices).

事实上，没有哪个品牌在香氛种类上能与 Pura 相提并论——它提供了超过 150 种选择，涵盖了 Anthropologie、Nest、LAFCO、Brooklyn Candle Company、Studio McGee 等知名品牌。Pura 甚至还有特别的季节性联名款，比如本月推出的以纽约市芭蕾舞团《胡桃夹子》为灵感的香氛。这些扩香机使用起来也非常方便，你可以通过手机应用程序进行控制（Home、Plus 和 Car 型号在设备本身上也设有按钮）。

If you're in the market for a Pura diffuser, our top pick for most people is the new Pura Home. The first Pura I ever owned was the 3 model, and I've also had the 4, but the 5 (rebranded “Home”) improves on just about everything, with dual-band (2.4 GHz and 5.0 GHz) Wi-Fi compatibility; faster diffusion; control buttons on the device itself; and even a new way to load cartridges, where you can slide out the bottom portion (which is flat to sit on a surface), seat the scents, and click it into place. QR codes on the cartridges inform your app which scents they are and when they were loaded, so it can, in turn, remind you when it's time to replace them. I also like that this model is totally silent.

如果你打算购买 Pura 扩香机，我们为大多数人首选推荐的是新款 Pura Home。我拥有的第一台 Pura 是 3 型号，我也用过 4 型号，但 5 型号（更名为“Home”）在几乎所有方面都有所改进，包括双频（2.4 GHz 和 5.0 GHz）Wi-Fi 兼容性、更快的扩香速度、设备上的控制按钮，以及一种新的装载补充装的方式：你可以滑出底部（底部是平的，可以放置在平面上），放入香氛，然后卡入到位。补充装上的二维码会告知应用程序它们是什么香型以及何时装入的，从而可以在需要更换时提醒你。我还喜欢这个型号完全静音的特点。
