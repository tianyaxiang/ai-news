# Can AI help scientists identify promising drug candidates?

人工智能能否帮助科学家识别有前景的候选药物？

November 20, 2026. Research Release. Science. Can AI help scientists identify promising drug candidates? Introducing MedChemBench, a benchmark that tests how well AI models read molecular structures, interpret experimental results, and choose which compounds to test next.

2026年11月20日。研究发布。科学。人工智能能否帮助科学家识别有前景的候选药物？我们推出了 MedChemBench，这是一个旨在测试人工智能模型在读取分子结构、解读实验结果以及选择后续测试化合物方面表现的基准测试。

We’re introducing MedChemBench, a benchmark that evaluates AI models on reasoning tasks drawn from medicinal chemistry research. It tests whether models can connect a molecule’s structure with experimental evidence to explain results, compare compounds, and predict their properties. Building AI that can reliably support this work is one step towards helping scientists develop better medicines.

我们推出了 MedChemBench，这是一个根据药物化学研究中的推理任务来评估人工智能模型的基准。它测试模型是否能够将分子结构与实验证据联系起来，以解释结果、比较化合物并预测其属性。构建能够可靠支持这项工作的人工智能，是帮助科学家开发更好药物的一步。

One of the hardest parts of this work is that improving one property of a molecule can make another worse. A compound might act strongly against a disease-related protein but be poorly absorbed or break down too quickly in the body. Medicinal chemists work through these tradeoffs by designing, making, and testing related compounds, then deciding what to try next. For AI to help, it needs to connect changes in molecular structure with experimental evidence and reason about what compounds are worth pursuing.

这项工作最困难的部分之一是，改善分子的一个属性可能会导致另一个属性变差。某种化合物可能对疾病相关蛋白有很强的抑制作用，但吸收性较差或在体内分解过快。药物化学家通过设计、制造和测试相关化合物来权衡这些利弊，然后决定下一步尝试什么。为了提供帮助，人工智能需要将分子结构的变化与实验证据联系起来，并推断哪些化合物值得进一步研究。

MedChemBench measures these abilities through 99 problems drawn from published research. At the xhigh reasoning setting, GPT‑6.2 Astra earned 84.5% of the available credit, compared with 83.3% for GPT‑6 Astra, 82.0% for GPT‑6.1 Sol, and 58.2% for GPT‑5.6 Sol. GPT‑6 Astra was strongest at reconstructing molecular structures and explaining experimental evidence. Selecting compounds and predicting their numerical properties proved harder for that model.

MedChemBench 通过从已发表研究中提取的 99 个问题来衡量这些能力。在“xhigh”推理设置下，GPT-6.2 Astra 获得了 84.5% 的可用分数，相比之下，GPT-6 Astra 为 83.3%，GPT-6.1 Sol 为 82.0%，GPT-5.6 Sol 为 58.2%。GPT-6 Astra 在重建分子结构和解释实验证据方面表现最强。对于该模型而言，选择化合物和预测其数值属性则更具挑战性。

MedChemBench draws on 117 papers and patents and includes 761 distinct molecular structures. For most problems, models receive the context available at a chosen point in a research program: the biological target, the researchers’ goals, the compounds studied so far, and selected experimental findings.

MedChemBench 参考了 117 篇论文和专利，包含 761 种不同的分子结构。对于大多数问题，模型会接收到研究项目中特定阶段的可用背景信息：生物靶点、研究人员的目标、迄今为止研究的化合物以及选定的实验结果。

The benchmark evaluates four components: Molecular structures (correct atom connections and specified stereochemistry), Scientific reasoning (explanations supported by evidence), Ranking and selection (choices that meet requirements), and Property predictions (agreement with numerical values and categories).

该基准评估四个组成部分：分子结构（正确的原子连接和指定的立体化学）、科学推理（有证据支持的解释）、排名与选择（符合要求的选择）以及属性预测（与数值和类别的一致性）。

One MedChemBench problem draws on research into inhibitors of PRMT5, a protein studied in cancer drug discovery. The model receives molecular structures and results from earlier experiments. It must predict how three related compounds will behave: how strongly they block PRMT5 in cells, how readily they cross a cell barrier, and how quickly liver enzymes break them down in a laboratory assay.

MedChemBench 的一个问题借鉴了对 PRMT5 抑制剂的研究，这是一种在癌症药物发现中研究的蛋白质。模型接收分子结构和早期实验的结果，必须预测三种相关化合物的表现：它们在细胞中阻断 PRMT5 的强度、它们穿过细胞屏障的难易程度，以及它们在实验室分析中被肝酶分解的速度。

Later experiments, withheld from the model, showed a tradeoff. Compound 23 blocked PRMT5 in cells at a lower concentration, while compound 24 crossed the cell barrier more readily and broke down more slowly in the liver-enzyme assay. Predicting these properties together tests whether a model can connect molecular structures with several aspects of compound behavior.

未提供给模型的后续实验显示了一种权衡。化合物 23 在较低浓度下即可阻断细胞中的 PRMT5，而化合物 24 更容易穿过细胞屏障，且在肝酶分析中分解得更慢。同时预测这些属性测试了模型是否能将分子结构与化合物行为的多个方面联系起来。

Before a model can reason about a compound’s properties, it needs to identify the molecule correctly. Chemical drawings encode atom connections, ring structures, and stereochemistry. MedChemBench tests whether models preserve this information when reconstructing molecules from the drawings and chemical groups provided.

在模型能够推断化合物的属性之前，它需要正确识别分子。化学图示编码了原子连接、环结构和立体化学。MedChemBench 测试模型在根据提供的图示和化学基团重建分子时，是否保留了这些信息。

One problem asks models to assemble a molecule from a central structure and two attached groups while preserving an 18-atom ring. At the highest reasoning setting (xhigh), GPT‑6 Astra reconstructed this compound correctly in all ten responses. GPT‑5.6 Sol and GPT‑5.6 Luna failed across all ten responses. In the illustrated answers, GPT‑5.6 Sol produced a 15-atom ring, while GPT‑5.6 Luna split the molecule into two disconnected fragments.

一个问题要求模型从中心结构和两个连接基团组装一个分子，同时保留一个 18 原子环。在最高推理设置（xhigh）下，GPT-6 Astra 在所有十次回答中都正确重建了该化合物。GPT-5.6 Sol 和 GPT-5.6 Luna 在所有十次回答中均失败。在图示答案中，GPT-5.6 Sol 生成了一个 15 原子环，而 GPT-5.6 Luna 将分子拆分为两个不相连的片段。

We evaluated eleven GPT models at different reasoning-effort settings. Across the full benchmark, Astra earned 83.3% of the available credit at the shared xhigh setting, compared with 82.0% for GPT‑6.1 Sol, 75.1% for GPT‑6.1 Terra, and 58.2% for GPT‑5.6 Sol. At max effort, GPT‑6 Astra reached 83.8%. Higher reasoning settings improved scores, while large differences between models remained.

我们在不同的推理努力设置下评估了 11 个 GPT 模型。在整个基准测试中，Astra 在共享的 xhigh 设置下获得了 83.3% 的可用分数，相比之下，GPT-6.1 Sol 为 82.0%，GPT-6.1 Terra 为 75.1%，GPT-5.6 Sol 为 58.2%。在最大努力设置下，GPT-6 Astra 达到了 83.8%。更高的推理设置提高了分数，但模型之间仍然存在巨大差异。
