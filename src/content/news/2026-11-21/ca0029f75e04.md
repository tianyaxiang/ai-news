Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.24995 (cs) [Submitted on 18 Nov 2026] Title: EgoPass-Bench: Identifying Objects That May Affect the Wearer's Passage in Egocentric Video Authors: Hongyu Wang, Chuhan Li, Congjin Xu, Xuandong Zhao, Hua Shen.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.24995 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 18 日] 标题：EgoPass-Bench：识别第一人称视频中可能影响佩戴者通行的物体 作者：Hongyu Wang, Chuhan Li, Congjin Xu, Xuandong Zhao, Hua Shen。

Abstract: Understanding an egocentric environment requires reasoning about how surrounding entities affect the observer's actions. Existing evaluations of multimodal foundation models (MFMs) examine observer localization, motion trajectories, and action feasibility, but leave the discovery and interpretation of evolving observer-entity interactions insufficiently assessed.

摘要：理解第一人称视角环境需要推理周围实体如何影响观察者的行为。现有的多模态基础模型（MFM）评估考察了观察者定位、运动轨迹和行为可行性，但对不断演变的观察者与实体间交互的发现和解释评估不足。

We introduce EgoPass-Bench, a benchmark for observer-relative interaction understanding in real-world egocentric video. EgoPass-Bench comprises 284 manually reviewed evaluation clips recorded using Ray-Ban Meta (Gen 2) smart glasses and 2,201 distinct questions across five tasks assessing motion perception, interaction judgments about specified entities, and relevant-entity discovery without target cues.

我们引入了 EgoPass-Bench，这是一个用于理解现实世界第一人称视频中观察者相关交互的基准。EgoPass-Bench 包含 284 个使用 Ray-Ban Meta（第二代）智能眼镜录制并经人工审核的评估片段，以及涵盖五个任务的 2,201 个不同问题，旨在评估运动感知、针对特定实体的交互判断以及在无目标提示下的相关实体发现。

Our evaluation of eight frame-based and six native-video configurations reveals a pronounced discrepancy between motion perception and interaction understanding. The strongest evaluated model, GPT-6 Astra, correctly recognizes object motion in 91.37% of 394 comparisons involving the same targets, but correctly identifies changes in passage constraints in only 66.50%.

我们对八种基于帧的配置和六种原生视频配置的评估揭示了运动感知与交互理解之间存在显著差异。评估中最强的模型 GPT-6 Astra 在涉及相同目标的 394 次比较中，有 91.37% 能正确识别物体运动，但在识别通行约束变化时，正确率仅为 66.50%。

Across all 574 entity-discovery questions, it trails the two-person human baseline by $23.65 \pm 7.45$ percentage points (paired 95% CI), while exceeding the always-empty baseline (36.76%) by at least 18.9 percentage points. Further analysis reveals frequent inclusion of entities that neither pose a plausible physical conflict nor constrain passage, showing that accurate motion recognition does not ensure reliable relevant-entity discovery.

在所有 574 个实体发现问题中，它比双人人类基准落后 $23.65 \pm 7.45$ 个百分点（配对 95% 置信区间），同时比“始终为空”的基准（36.76%）至少高出 18.9 个百分点。进一步分析显示，模型经常包含既不构成合理物理冲突也不限制通行的实体，这表明准确的运动识别并不能确保可靠的相关实体发现。
