# OpenAI engineer arrested in bugging of executive conference room

联邦检察官表示，一名来自沙特阿拉伯的员工在公司高管会议室安装了录音设备，并在包中留下了另一台无人看管的设备。

An OpenAI engineer concealed a recording device in a conference room used by executives at the company, the Department of Justice claims.

据旧金山联邦检察官办公室称，一名 OpenAI 工程师因涉嫌在公司旧金山总部的高管会议室隐藏录音设备而被捕。

An OpenAI engineer has been arrested on suspicion of hiding an audio recording device in an executive conference room at the company’s San Francisco headquarters, federal prosecutors said Tuesday.

35 岁的沙特公民阿卜杜勒拉赫曼·阿尔福赞（Abdulrahman Alfozan）于周五被捕，并被控使用或试图使用设备拦截口头通讯。他于周二在联邦法院首次出庭，目前仍被拘留。

Abdulrahman Alfozan, 35, a Saudi national, was arrested Friday and charged with using or attempting to use a device to intercept oral communications. He made his initial appearance in federal court Tuesday and remains in custody.

检察官称，阿尔福赞自 2025 年 7 月起在 OpenAI 担任工程师。OpenAI 人员于 9 月 30 日发现了该设备。起诉书称，它被插在高管会议室电视机背面的 USB 端口上，并用卫生纸遮盖。

Alfozan has worked as an engineer at OpenAI since July 2025, prosecutors said. OpenAI personnel found the device Sept. 30. It was plugged into a USB port on the back of a television in an executive conference room and had been concealed in toilet paper, the complaint says.

监控录像显示，在设备被发现之前，阿尔福赞曾多次出现在该房间附近，尽管他并未获得进入该房间或参加会议的授权。

Surveillance footage shows Alfozan near the room on multiple dates before the discovery, though he was not authorized to enter the room or attend meetings there, prosecutors said.

起诉书称，在安装设备之前，阿尔福赞曾使用生产力工具询问有关 OpenAI 的办公地址、会议室、房间容量和日程安排，以及领导层和外部合作伙伴会议地点的信息。

Before the device was installed, Alfozan used a productivity tool to ask about OpenAI’s building addresses, meeting rooms, room capacities, and calendars, as well as the locations of leadership and outside partner meetings, the complaint alleges.

检察官表示，与阿尔福赞配偶相关联的一个聊天机器人账户在 9 月 24 日至 9 月 30 日期间提交了关于构建、安装、隐藏和远程访问录音设备的提示。

A chatbot account tied to Alfozan’s spouse, which shared identifiers with his own account, submitted prompts between Sept. 24 and Sept. 30 about building, installing, obscuring, and remotely accessing a recording device, prosecutors said.

美国联邦检察官克雷格·米萨基安（Craig Missakian）在一份声明中表示：“对我们来说，这是一个国家安全问题。”

“For us it is a matter of national security,” U.S. Attorney Craig Missakian said in a statement.

如果罪名成立，阿尔福赞将面临最高五年的监禁和 25 万美元的罚款，随后是三年的监督释放。

If convicted, Alfozan faces up to five years in prison and a $250,000 fine, followed by three years of supervised release.

OpenAI 在 X 上表示，在发现该设备后，员工利用公司的 AI 模型确定了其性质和来源，并将此事移交给了联邦调查局。

OpenAI said on X that after discovering the device, employees used the company’s AI models to determine what it was and where it came from, and referred the matter to the FBI.
