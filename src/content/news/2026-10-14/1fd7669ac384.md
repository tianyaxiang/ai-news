# Gemini 4 Argon for Robotics

Gemini 4 Argon marks a step change in physical understanding, code generation, and visual-semantic reasoning—unlocking new workflows across the robotics stack and accelerating progress toward physical AGI.

Gemini 4 Argon 在物理理解、代码生成和视觉语义推理方面实现了跨越式发展，为机器人技术栈解锁了新的工作流程，并加速了通向物理通用人工智能（AGI）的进程。

You can see this leap in capability on the Embodied Reasoning (ERQA) benchmark, which tests a model's ability to understand and solve physical, spatial problems in 3D environments. Using pure reasoning, Argon scores 87%, outperforming every other frontier model.

你可以在具身推理（ERQA）基准测试中看到这种能力的飞跃，该测试旨在评估模型在 3D 环境中理解和解决物理及空间问题的能力。凭借纯粹的推理能力，Argon 获得了 87% 的分数，超越了所有其他前沿模型。

As a general-purpose physical reasoning model, Argon can be applied across the entire robotics lifecycle. In this post, we share representative prompts for Argon across four key areas: Robot control, Real2Sim, Physical understanding, and Research and self-improvement.

作为一种通用物理推理模型，Argon 可以应用于整个机器人生命周期。在本文中，我们分享了 Argon 在四个关键领域的代表性提示词：机器人控制、实物到仿真（Real2Sim）、物理理解以及研究与自我提升。

### Unlocking robot control with Argon

### 利用 Argon 解锁机器人控制

**1. Spot mobile manipulation: Putting in the trash bin with MuJoCo practice**
Argon controls a Boston Dynamics Spot® quadruped to learn a new skill: stepping on a foot-pedal trash bin to throw away soda cans. Argon creates an articulated MuJoCo digital twin of a foot-pedal trash bin to practice its moves before stepping on the physical pedal and dropping soda cans into the open bin.

**1. Spot 移动操作：通过 MuJoCo 练习进行垃圾投放**
Argon 控制波士顿动力公司的 Spot® 四足机器人学习一项新技能：踩踏脚踏式垃圾桶以丢弃汽水罐。Argon 创建了一个脚踏式垃圾桶的 MuJoCo 铰接式数字孪生模型，在踩下物理踏板并将汽水罐投入打开的垃圾桶之前，先进行动作练习。

**2. Zero-shot control**
Argon controls a simple robotic arm to place a fallen mug upright and put the crumpled napkin inside. Argon reasons with a visual coordinate harness and action primitives to perform this task over several turns.

**2. 零样本控制**
Argon 控制一个简单的机械臂将倒下的马克杯扶正，并将揉皱的餐巾纸放入其中。Argon 通过视觉坐标系和动作原语进行推理，在几个回合内完成了这项任务。

**3. Omni control**
Argon steers robots in the Gemini Omni World Model to rearrange five scattered Scrabble tiles (G, R, A, N, O) into a clean row spelling A - R - G - O - N. Instead of specifying one static prompt, Argon steers in a closed visual loop. It writes timestamped action prompts, inspecting intermediate rollout keyframes, and refining its prompts.

**3. 全能控制**
Argon 在 Gemini Omni 世界模型中引导机器人，将五个散乱的拼字游戏字母块（G, R, A, N, O）重新排列成整齐的一行，拼出 A - R - G - O - N。Argon 并非使用单一的静态提示词，而是在闭环视觉中进行引导。它编写带有时间戳的动作提示词，检查中间的推演关键帧，并不断优化其提示词。
