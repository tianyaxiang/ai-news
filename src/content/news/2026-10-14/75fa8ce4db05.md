# Trump commercial railing against his enemies kept airing after court blocked it

Trump commercials paid for by the US government continued airing on TV the day after a judge blocked the ads because they likely violate a US law against government-funded publicity or propaganda. In the most widely aired commercial, Trump railed against his perceived enemies and said he would “liberate America from these villains once and for all.”

由美国政府出资的特朗普竞选广告在法官下令禁播后的第二天仍继续在电视上播出，原因在于这些广告可能违反了美国关于禁止政府资助宣传或政治鼓吹的法律。在播放最广泛的一则广告中，特朗普抨击了他眼中的敌人，并声称他将“彻底将美国从这些恶棍手中解放出来”。

The campaign also included a commercial praising Trump’s record as president, another in which Trump calls the US the “most exceptional nation ever to exist in human history,” and commercials related to the Iran war and the capture of Venezuelan President Nicolás Maduro. US District Judge Jennifer Rochon issued a temporary restraining order (TRO) blocking the ads on Sunday, October 11, which took effect at 6:45 pm that night.

该竞选活动还包括一则赞扬特朗普总统任期内政绩的广告，另一则广告中特朗普称美国是“人类历史上最卓越的国家”，此外还有涉及伊朗战争和逮捕委内瑞拉总统尼古拉斯·马杜罗的广告。美国地方法院法官詹妮弗·罗雄（Jennifer Rochon）于10月11日星期日发布了临时限制令（TRO）以阻止这些广告，该命令于当晚6点45分生效。

The order said the government must cancel the ads, “including those placements for the evening of October 11, 2026.” The Trump administration had told the court on October 10 that the Department of Homeland Security (DHS) advertising purchases were set to expire on October 11 and would not be renewed. Despite that, the ads continued airing the night of October 11 and on part of October 12.

该命令要求政府必须取消这些广告，“包括2026年10月11日晚间的投放”。特朗普政府曾在10月10日告知法院，国土安全部（DHS）的广告购买合同定于10月11日到期，且不会续签。尽管如此，这些广告在10月11日晚间以及10月12日的部分时间里仍在继续播出。

This was also a week after Trump’s October 5 statement that he would start paying for the ads “myself, and with money I raised for MAGA, Inc.” A US attorney yesterday filed a status report saying that the government is now complying with the order and issued an apology. “As Your Honor is aware, the Court emailed the TRO to the parties yesterday at 6:46 p.m.,” US Attorney Jamie McDonald wrote in a filing.

这距离特朗普10月5日发表声明称他将“亲自并利用为MAGA公司筹集的资金”来支付广告费用已过去了一周。一名美国检察官昨日提交了一份状态报告，称政府目前正在遵守该命令并表示道歉。美国检察官杰米·麦克唐纳（Jamie McDonald）在一份文件中写道：“正如法官阁下所知，法院于昨日下午6点46分通过电子邮件将临时限制令发送给了各方。”

“Counsel for the Government emailed the TRO to agency counsel at 7:58 p.m. Agency counsel did not become aware of Government counsel’s email until this morning. The undersigned apologizes to the Court and the plaintiffs.” The ads aired as part of a $20 million purchase order that US Customs and Border Protection (CBP) made on September 20 with LMD Agency, an ad firm. The DHS told the court that the ad placements through October 11 cost $19 million.

“政府法律顾问于晚上7点58分将临时限制令通过电子邮件发送给了机构法律顾问。机构法律顾问直到今天早上才注意到政府法律顾问的邮件。本人谨向法院及原告致歉。”这些广告是美国海关及边境保卫局（CBP）于9月20日与广告公司LMD Agency签订的2000万美元采购订单的一部分。国土安全部告知法院，截至10月11日的广告投放费用为1900万美元。
