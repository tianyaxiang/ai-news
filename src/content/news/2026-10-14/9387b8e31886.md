Datacenter blocklists are still the default proxy defence in a lot of fraud stacks. The addresses showing up in proxy supply this week mostly were not in datacenters. Between 6–12 October 2026 (UTC), the IP99 risk feed captured 11,860,625 proxy exit addresses it had not seen in the previous 7 days (+27% vs the prior week).

数据中心黑名单仍然是许多欺诈防御体系中的默认代理防御手段。本周出现在代理供应中的地址大多不在数据中心内。在 2026 年 10 月 6 日至 12 日（UTC）期间，IP99 风险信息源捕获了 11,860,625 个在过去 7 天内未曾见过的代理出口地址（较前一周增长 27%）。

IP99 counts an address as "captured" when it enters the feed with a proxy-class signal (proxy, VPN, dial-up pool, cloud phone, or hijacked shared proxy) and was not in the live 7-day risk mirror. Addresses that go quiet and return can be counted again. The page describes what 53 upstream collection sources delivered - not the total number of proxies in the world.

当一个地址带有代理类信号（代理、VPN、拨号池、云手机或被劫持的共享代理）进入信息源，且不在 7 天实时风险镜像中时，IP99 会将其计为“已捕获”。静默后重新出现的地址可能会被再次计算。该页面描述的是 53 个上游收集源提供的数据，而非全球代理的总数。

Why the 2% matters If your defence is mainly an ASN or hosting blocklist, you are mostly looking at that 2.2% datacenter slice. Residential and mobile space is where home-broadband proxies and dial-up pools live. An address that keeps turning up in proxy supply is a proxy exit whatever its ISP label says. Top ASNs in this week's capture window (by address count) include large access networks such as AS4134 and AS4837. That is a clue about where supply is being sold from, not a claim that every customer on those networks is a proxy.

为什么 2% 很重要：如果你的防御主要依赖 ASN 或托管黑名单，你关注的主要是那 2.2% 的数据中心部分。住宅和移动网络空间才是家庭宽带代理和拨号池的所在地。无论 ISP 标签如何，一个不断出现在代理供应中的地址就是一个代理出口。本周捕获窗口中的顶级 ASN（按地址数量计）包括 AS4134 和 AS4837 等大型接入网络。这只是关于供应来源的线索，并不代表这些网络上的每个客户都是代理。
