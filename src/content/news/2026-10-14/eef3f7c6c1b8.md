# Firefox Enterprise

Firefox Enterprise puts customer privacy, independence and control first.

Firefox Enterprise 将客户隐私、独立性和控制权放在首位。

The browser is where work, data, identity, and AI converge. That makes it a critical layer for governance and compliance — and one many enterprise technology stacks still don’t fully cover.

浏览器是工作、数据、身份和人工智能汇聚的地方。这使其成为治理和合规的关键层，也是许多企业技术栈尚未完全覆盖的领域。

Deploy Firefox Enterprise on-premises, within your own infrastructure. Define and audit where your data, policies and management live.

在您自己的基础设施内本地部署 Firefox Enterprise。定义并审计您的数据、策略和管理所在的位置。

Prevent sensitive data from leaving the browser and give employees, contractors and BYOD users secure, identity-based access to internal applications — without the infrastructure of traditional remote access.

防止敏感数据离开浏览器，并为员工、承包商和自带设备（BYOD）用户提供对内部应用程序的安全、基于身份的访问，而无需传统的远程访问基础设施。

Built on Mozilla’s independent engine, Firefox Enterprise adds browser diversity that reduces shared failure risk and vendor lock-in – while integrating seamlessly with your current security stack.

Firefox Enterprise 基于 Mozilla 的独立引擎构建，增加了浏览器多样性，从而降低了共享故障风险和供应商锁定，同时能与您现有的安全栈无缝集成。

On-prem browser governance for regulated and security-sensitive organizations

面向受监管和安全敏感型组织的本地浏览器治理。

The browser everyone loves can be used at your organization, too.

每个人都喜爱的浏览器也可以在您的组织中使用。

Centralized management of Thunderbird email, calendar and contacts, right from the Firefox Enterprise console.

直接从 Firefox Enterprise 控制台集中管理 Thunderbird 电子邮件、日历和联系人。

Give your IT staff a direct path to the people behind the product, helping them resolve issues quicker and with less guesswork.

为您的 IT 人员提供与产品背后团队直接沟通的渠道，帮助他们更快地解决问题，减少猜测。

Standard support is included with Firefox Enterprise, and is available as a paid upgrade if you’re using Firefox as your company's browser.

Firefox Enterprise 包含标准支持；如果您将 Firefox 用作公司浏览器，也可通过付费升级获得支持。
