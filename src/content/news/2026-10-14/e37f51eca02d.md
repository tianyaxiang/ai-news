# It looks like Apple Music is getting a new icon

Apple Music 似乎要换新图标了。

Apple Music might be going monochrome. Apple Music might be going monochrome.

Apple Music 可能要走单色调路线了。

Apple Music’s iconic red app icon has reportedly been replaced with a black-and-white version in the latest iOS 27.2 beta, 9to5Mac reports. The eighth note on the icon also appears to have a slightly sharper angle than the original. A new Apple Music logo was also spotted last month on the Apple Music Hall website, so the company could be refreshing its app icon to match.

据 9to5Mac 报道，在最新的 iOS 27.2 测试版中，Apple Music 标志性的红色应用图标已被替换为黑白版本。图标上的八分音符看起来也比原来的角度更锐利一些。上个月，Apple Music Hall 网站上也出现了新的 Apple Music 标志，因此该公司可能正在更新其应用图标以保持一致。

According to 9to5Mac, the new icon is a black music note on a white background in light mode, or the opposite in dark mode. The icon’s not the only thing that’s been drained of color, either — 9to5Mac also says Apple Music widgets now have black backgrounds and the app now uses a splash of blue in the tab bar to show the active tab.

据 9to5Mac 称，新图标在浅色模式下为白底黑音符，深色模式下则相反。不仅图标失去了色彩，9to5Mac 还表示，Apple Music 小组件现在也采用了黑色背景，并且该应用现在使用一抹蓝色来标识标签栏中的当前活动标签。

A user on X shared images of the new icon in the different color modes, including the tinted version. So if the new monochrome version of the app makes it to a public iOS release, you might be able to use the tinted homescreen option to make it look a bit like the previous red version again.

一位 X 用户分享了新图标在不同色彩模式下的图片，包括着色版本。因此，如果新的单色版应用最终在 iOS 正式版中发布，你或许可以使用主屏幕着色选项，让它看起来有点像以前的红色版本。
