The feds are looking into reports of Tesla’s robotaxis speeding through school zones.

联邦政府正在调查有关特斯拉自动驾驶出租车在学校区域超速行驶的报告。

NHTSA has received a petition requesting an investigation into Model Y and Cybercab vehicles exceeding active school zone speed limits.

美国国家公路交通安全管理局（NHTSA）收到了一份请愿书，要求对Model Y和Cybercab车辆在活跃学校区域超过限速的行为展开调查。

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration says it’s evaluating allegations that Tesla’s 1,000 or so robotaxis using its automated driving system are exceeding “posted speed limits in active school zones.” Waymo’s robotaxis have also faced similar inquiries, including for illegally passing school buses and hitting a child near a California elementary school.

美国国家公路交通安全管理局表示，正在评估有关特斯拉约1000辆使用其自动驾驶系统的出租车在“活跃学校区域超过规定限速”的指控。Waymo的自动驾驶出租车也曾面临类似的质询，包括非法超车校车以及在加州一所小学附近撞伤儿童的事件。

NHTSA: The Office of Defects Investigation (ODI) received a DP on September 23, 2026, requesting an investigation into model year (MY) 2026 Tesla Robotaxis (Model Y and Cybercab) with the Automated Driving System (ADS) engaged. The Petitioner alleges that the ADS exceeds posted speed limits in active school zones.

NHTSA：缺陷调查办公室（ODI）于2026年9月23日收到一份缺陷请愿书（DP），要求对处于自动驾驶系统（ADS）运行状态下的2026款特斯拉自动驾驶出租车（Model Y和Cybercab）进行调查。请愿人声称，该自动驾驶系统在活跃学校区域超过了规定限速。

The Cybercab launched earlier this year, and is already under investigation for its lack of side mirrors, pedals, and a steering wheel. NHTSA has also opened an investigation into Tesla’s Full Self-Driving system for traffic violations, like blowing past red lights and driving on the wrong side of the street.

Cybercab于今年早些时候发布，目前已因缺乏侧视镜、踏板和方向盘而受到调查。NHTSA还针对特斯拉的“全自动驾驶”（Full Self-Driving）系统发起了调查，原因涉及闯红灯和逆行等交通违规行为。
