# The Apple HomeView Is Like a Giant Apple Watch for the Home

Save this story. It’s a big year for Apple firsts. The company launched the first MacBook Neo, unveiled its first folding iPhone in September, and installed a new CEO for the first time in 15 years. Now comes the Apple HomeView, Apple’s first smart display.

这是苹果公司充满“第一次”的一年。该公司推出了首款 MacBook Neo，九月份发布了首款折叠屏 iPhone，并十五年来首次更换了首席执行官。现在，苹果的首款智能显示屏 Apple HomeView 问世了。

Rivals like Google and Amazon have made countless screen-enabled smart speakers since voice assistants invaded the home a decade ago. But Apple stuck with a smaller smart home ecosystem centered on its HomePod speakers, even removing the iPad as a hub device a few years ago, which forced users to buy an Apple TV or HomePod to get started. Maybe that’s why it hasn’t found much success in the home.

自十年前语音助手进入家庭以来，谷歌和亚马逊等竞争对手已经制造了无数带屏幕的智能音箱。但苹果一直坚持以 HomePod 音箱为核心的小型智能家居生态系统，甚至在几年前取消了 iPad 作为中枢设备的功能，这迫使用户必须购买 Apple TV 或 HomePod 才能使用。也许这就是它在家庭市场一直未取得太大成功的原因。

Announced Tuesday at an event in New York City, the 7-inch smart display arrives alongside several other new smart home devices as part of Apple’s bid to revitalize its lagging ecosystem. The HomeView costs $299 and comes in two versions—a speaker base and a wall-mounted display—both priced the same. Preorders are live, with sales kicking off on November 13.

这款 7 英寸智能显示屏于周二在纽约市的发布会上亮相，随同其他几款新的智能家居设备一同推出，这是苹果重振其滞后生态系统计划的一部分。HomeView 售价 299 美元，提供两种版本——音箱底座版和壁挂式显示屏版——价格相同。预购现已开启，将于 11 月 13 日正式发售。

A Big Apple Watch. The first thing I thought of after seeing the Apple HomeView in action is that it looks like a blown-up Apple Watch. The 7-inch square screen has multiple “faces” you can swipe through, exactly like the smartwatch. You can even customize the face’s colors and add different complications for more glanceable information.

“大号 Apple Watch”。在看到 Apple HomeView 的实际演示后，我首先想到的是它看起来就像一个放大版的 Apple Watch。这款 7 英寸的方形屏幕拥有多个可以滑动的“表盘”，与智能手表完全一样。你甚至可以自定义表盘颜色，并添加不同的复杂功能，以便更直观地查看信息。

The faces can range from a clock, your favorite photos of friends, family, or pets (you can even use shared albums) to weather updates and, yes, Snoopy animations. Swipe up for the home screen, which is more like an iPhone or iPad, with apps neatly arranged alongside widgets. Except the HomeView doesn’t run iOS or iPadOS—it runs the generically named HomeView software.

表盘内容多种多样，包括时钟、你最喜欢的亲友或宠物照片（甚至可以使用共享相册）、天气更新，当然还有史努比动画。向上滑动即可进入主屏幕，它更像 iPhone 或 iPad，应用程序与小组件整齐地排列在一起。不同之处在于，HomeView 运行的不是 iOS 或 iPadOS，而是名为 HomeView 的通用软件。

It doesn’t have an App Store. Instead, Apple has a curated selection of apps, including FaceTime, Messages, and Safari. A handful of third-party media apps like Spotify are available, and developers can bring over existing CarPlay apps. If your iPhone already has one of these CarPlay apps installed, it can automatically appear on the HomeView after setup.

它没有应用商店。相反，苹果提供了一系列精选应用，包括 FaceTime、信息和 Safari。目前提供少数第三方媒体应用（如 Spotify），开发者也可以移植现有的 CarPlay 应用。如果你的 iPhone 上已经安装了这些 CarPlay 应用，它们在设置完成后会自动出现在 HomeView 上。

(There are no video streaming apps like YouTube, Netflix, or Disney+, but if those companies choose to make their apps available on the CarPlay framework, they will show up on the HomeView; for now, you'll have to consume videos by using the Safari browser.)

（目前没有 YouTube、Netflix 或 Disney+ 等视频流媒体应用，但如果这些公司选择在 CarPlay 框架上提供其应用，它们就会出现在 HomeView 上；目前，你只能通过 Safari 浏览器观看视频。）

To get the most out of the HomeView, you need an iPhone with Apple Intelligence support (an iPhone 15 Pro or newer)—you can’t set it up with an iPad. Apple says you can still set it up with an older iPhone and access some of Siri AI's smarts (like broad world knowledge), but smarter app actions and personal context queries require an iPhone with Apple Intelligence.

要充分利用 HomeView，你需要一部支持 Apple Intelligence 的 iPhone（iPhone 15 Pro 或更新机型）——你无法通过 iPad 进行设置。苹果表示，你仍然可以使用旧款 iPhone 进行设置并访问 Siri AI 的部分智能功能（如广泛的常识），但更智能的应用操作和个人语境查询需要支持 Apple Intelligence 的 iPhone。

The aluminum hardware, powered by Apple’s A18 chip, comes in four colors: silver, starlight, rose pink, and space gray (Apple color-matches the woven cable, too). The standard HomeView has a speaker base with a screen that tilts manually up or down for angled viewing, but you can also buy a wall-mountable version.

这款铝制硬件由苹果 A18 芯片驱动，有四种颜色可选：银色、星光色、玫瑰粉色和深空灰色（苹果还为编织线缆进行了颜色匹配）。标准版 HomeView 配有一个音箱底座，屏幕可手动上下倾斜以调整视角，你也可以购买壁挂式版本。

The latter offers three mounts to choose from, one of which is DIY-friendly and has a visible power cord that needs to run to an outlet. The other two are designed for professional installers, either hardwired or connected via Power over Ethernet (PoE).

后者提供三种支架供选择，其中一种适合 DIY，有一根可见的电源线需要连接到插座。另外两种专为专业安装人员设计，可通过硬接线或以太网供电 (PoE) 连接。

Technically, the primary speakers—two full-range drivers—are built into the screen. But the standard HomeView has a base that houses a down-firing woofer and two force-canceling passive radiators for stronger bass. It sounds pretty good based on a very limited listening experience, and can fill a large room quite well. The wall-mounted variant won’t have that extra oomph but Apple claims it tunes the audio for wall placement.

从技术上讲，主扬声器（两个全频驱动单元）内置在屏幕中。但标准版 HomeView 的底座内含一个向下发声的低音单元和两个力抵消被动辐射器，以提供更强劲的低音。根据非常有限的试听体验，它的音质相当不错，足以很好地覆盖大房间。壁挂式版本虽然没有这种额外的低音效果，但苹果声称已针对墙面安装对音频进行了调校。

The 7-inch Liquid Retina display has familiar elements, like True Tone, which adjusts the screen’s color temperature to match the room’s lighting. The ambient light sensor can also reduce display brightness from 500 nits to 0.3 nits, which is the lowest brightness Apple has ever engineered on a screen. That’ll be helpful if you place the HomeView on the nightstand.

这款 7 英寸 Liquid 视网膜显示屏具备熟悉的元素，例如原彩显示技术，可根据室内光线调节屏幕色温。环境光传感器还能将显示亮度从 500 尼特降低至 0.3 尼特，这是苹果在屏幕上实现过的最低亮度。如果你将 HomeView 放在床头柜上，这将非常有用。

That is, if you're comfortable placing a camera-equipped display in the bedroom. A 12-megapixel Center Stage camera and four-microphone array sit on the front, but they aren’t just for FaceTime calls. The camera handles facial recognition, allowing the software to adapt to whoever is looking at the screen. Apple says all of this data stays on device.

前提是你愿意在卧室里放置一台配备摄像头的显示屏。正面配有一个 1200 万像素的“人物居中”摄像头和四麦克风阵列，但它们不仅仅用于 FaceTime 通话。摄像头负责面部识别，使软件能够适应正在注视屏幕的人。苹果表示，所有这些数据都保留在设备本地。

“Trust is becoming increasingly important with rising consumer expectations around privacy and security,” writes Anshika Jain, principal analyst at Counterpoint, in an email to WIRED. “In fact, Apple could actually benefit from its privacy-first approach.”

Counterpoint 首席分析师 Anshika Jain 在给《连线》杂志的电子邮件中写道：“随着消费者对隐私和安全期望的提高，信任变得越来越重要。事实上，苹果实际上可以从其‘隐私至上’的方法中受益。”

You can add up to six iCloud accounts, and the user interface will adapt to each person, like Google’s Pixel Tablet. That means your wife can tint every app icon green and have no widgets on her home screen, but if you stand in front of the HomeView, it’ll switch to your setup. You won’t be able to view other household members’ sensitive messages and personal information.

你可以添加最多六个 iCloud 账户，用户界面会像谷歌的 Pixel Tablet 一样适应每个人。这意味着你的妻子可以将所有应用图标设为绿色，并且主屏幕上不放任何小组件，但如果你站在 HomeView 前，它会切换到你的设置。你将无法查看其他家庭成员的敏感信息和个人资料。

For this personal content to show up, you don’t just have to stand in front of the HomeView—your iPhone needs to be on the same Wi-Fi network and must have been recently unlocked.

要显示这些个人内容，不仅需要你站在 HomeView 前，你的 iPhone 还必须连接在同一个 Wi-Fi 网络上，并且必须在近期解锁过。

The HomeView can also recognize voices, which helps if Dad asks Siri AI to “Play music.” It will play from Dad’s music library and his preferred music app, rather than another iCloud user’s music. If you have multiple HomePods or HomeViews, you can manage your speaker groups in the HomeView’s interface.

HomeView 还能识别语音，如果爸爸要求 Siri AI“播放音乐”，它会很有帮助。它会从爸爸的音乐库和他偏好的音乐应用中播放，而不是播放其他 iCloud 用户的音乐。如果你有多个 HomePod 或 HomeView，你可以在 HomeView 的界面中管理你的音箱组。

The HomeView also supports Apple’s Handoff tech, so you can bring your iPhone close to send a Safari recipe to the smart display, or have it continue the podcast you were listening to on your commute. In the middle of a FaceTime or phone call? Put your iPhone near the HomeView, and it’ll transfer it over.

HomeView 还支持苹果的接力 (Handoff) 技术，因此你可以将 iPhone 靠近它，将 Safari 中的食谱发送到智能显示屏上，或者让它继续播放你在通勤路上听的播客。正在进行 FaceTime 或电话通话？将 iPhone 靠近 HomeView，它就会自动转移通话。

押注 Siri AI。像任何智能显示屏或智能音箱一样，Siri AI 是 HomeView 的核心动力。毕竟，这是与之交互的主要方式。
