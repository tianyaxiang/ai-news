11月13日正式发售。

Apple HomeView 让整个家庭保持互联、娱乐和信息同步。一切尽在掌握，且全程保护隐私。

为你的音乐、视频、FaceTime 通话和照片提供震撼的音效与精美的视觉效果。

回复短信、拨打电话、挥手问候。多种连接方式，甚至支持免提操作。

Design. The domed aluminum base is fitted with a stainless steel hinge that lets you adjust the display.2 Available in four colors. Shown in Silver.

设计。圆顶铝金属底座配有不锈钢铰链，可调节显示屏角度。2 提供四种颜色可选。图中所示为银色。

Display. The 7-inch Liquid Retina display includes True Tone for comfortable viewing in any light.3 Brightness ranges from 500 nits for sunny rooms to our lowest-ever 0.3 nits for the bedside. Select features respond to your proximity to show more information the closer you get.

显示屏。7 英寸 Liquid Retina 显示屏支持原彩显示技术，在任何光线下都能提供舒适的观看体验。3 亮度范围从适合明亮房间的 500 尼特，到适合床头的最低 0.3 尼特。部分功能可感应你的距离，越靠近显示的信息越多。

Camera. The 12MP Center Stage camera is perfect for video calls, keeping everyone centered in the frame even as they move around or when more people join in.

摄像头。1200 万像素人物居中摄像头非常适合视频通话，即使人们走动或有更多人加入，也能确保每个人都处于画面中心。

Mics and Speakers. Four beamforming microphones pick up voices near and far, perfect for Siri requests from across the room and crystal-clear FaceTime calls with reduced background noise. Powerful speakers in the base and display deliver Spatial Audio for rich, room-filling sound.4

麦克风与扬声器。四个波束成形麦克风可拾取远近声音，非常适合在房间另一端呼叫 Siri，并能通过降低背景噪音实现清晰的 FaceTime 通话。底座和显示屏中的强劲扬声器可提供空间音频，带来丰富且充满整个房间的音效。4

Apple HomeView (Wall Mounted). Available in Space Gray and Silver, the wall-mounted option brings the HomeView experience into places like the entrance or the hallway.5 Choose from wired and plug-in wall mount options.6 Shown in Silver.

Apple HomeView（壁挂式）。提供深空灰色和银色版本，壁挂式选项可将 HomeView 体验带入入口或走廊等空间。5 可选择有线或插头式壁挂安装方式。6 图中所示为银色。

Apple HomeView is designed to bring more joy and convenience to everyone in your household. Siri AI comes built in for personalized AI assistance that’s truly useful in your everyday life. And with a vibrant display and powerful acoustics, your music, videos, and calls look and sound amazing. Meet the new heart of your home.

Apple HomeView 旨在为家中的每个人带来更多乐趣与便利。内置 Siri AI 提供个性化的智能助理服务，在日常生活中真正实用。凭借生动的显示屏和强劲的音响效果，你的音乐、视频和通话将呈现出色的视听体验。迎接你家中的新核心。

With a collection of customizable faces, Apple HomeView brings beauty to any room. Relive your favorite memories with the stunning Photos face. Choose playful faces like Snoopy or informative options like Calendar, Weather, and more. Even enjoy interactive artwork with the Flow face, which reacts to your movements with mesmerizing colors and light.

通过一系列可自定义的表盘，Apple HomeView 为任何房间增添美感。使用精美的“照片”表盘重温美好回忆。选择史努比等趣味表盘，或日历、天气等实用选项。甚至可以通过“流光”表盘欣赏互动艺术，它会随着你的动作呈现迷人的色彩与光影。

Whether a single image or a rotating gallery, photos from your collections or shared albums are beautifully framed.

无论是单张图片还是轮播画廊，来自你收藏或共享相册的照片都能以精美的方式呈现。

Select faces change throughout the day. They wake with you in the morning, wind down with you at night, and more.

部分表盘会随时间变化。它们会在清晨唤醒你，在夜晚陪伴你休息，等等。

Siri AI, powered by Apple Intelligence, comes built into Apple HomeView to help you find what you need and get more done while protecting your privacy.1 Siri AI adapts to each person and surfaces what matters most to them — all they have to do is ask.

由 Apple Intelligence 驱动的 Siri AI 内置于 Apple HomeView 中，旨在帮助你找到所需内容并高效完成任务，同时保护你的隐私。1 Siri AI 会适应每个人，并呈现对他们最重要的信息——只需开口询问即可。

Pull up a party invitation from your calendar, find RSVPs buried in your messages, or play the perfect soundtrack to match the vibe. Siri AI works with your paired iPhone or iPad to find relevant content just for you.1

从日历中调出派对邀请，查找隐藏在信息中的回复，或播放与氛围相符的完美配乐。Siri AI 可与你配对的 iPhone 或 iPad 协作，为你找到相关内容。1

Ask about virtually any topic, from quick dinner recipes to DIY home repairs to game-night trivia. Siri AI taps into broad world knowledge to give you highly specific, up-to-date answers.

询问几乎任何主题，从快速晚餐食谱到家居 DIY 维修，再到游戏之夜的冷知识。Siri AI 利用广泛的世界知识，为你提供高度具体且最新的答案。

Siri AI lets you ask open-ended questions and go back and forth with natural, rich conversations. And your conversations with Siri on HomeView sync to the Siri app on your other Apple devices so you can pick up where you left off.

Siri AI 允许你提出开放式问题，并进行自然、丰富的双向对话。你在 HomeView 上与 Siri 的对话会同步到其他 Apple 设备上的 Siri 应用中，以便你随时继续之前的操作。

Apple Intelligence is designed to protect your privacy at every step. It draws on your personal information without collecting it, and Private Cloud Compute — running on Apple silicon — handles requests without ever storing your data or making it accessible to anyone, including Apple. And because Apple HomeView is made to be shared, everyone’s personal information stays separate and secure.

Apple Intelligence 在每一步都旨在保护你的隐私。它在不收集个人信息的情况下调用这些信息，而运行在 Apple 芯片上的“私有云计算”在处理请求时，绝不会存储你的数据，也不会让任何人（包括 Apple）访问这些数据。由于 Apple HomeView 专为共享而设计，每个人的个人信息都保持独立且安全。

The mighty yet compact speakers pack a serious punch. Two custom full-range drivers in the display keep everything sounding crisp and clean while delivering room-filling Spatial Audio.4 Computational audio adjusts consistency and balance in real time to deliver incredible sound every time, from anywhere. And a down-firing woofer and two force-cancelling passive radiators are built into the base — for deep, rich bass.

强大而紧凑的扬声器表现惊人。显示屏中的两个定制全频驱动单元确保声音清晰纯净，同时提供充满房间的空间音频。4 计算音频实时调整一致性和平衡，确保在任何位置都能获得出色的音效。底座内置一个向下发声的低音单元和两个抵消力的被动辐射器，带来深沉、丰富的低音。

Fills the room with rich, textured sound for a high-fidelity listening experience.4

以丰富、有质感的声音填满房间，带来高保真的聆听体验。4
