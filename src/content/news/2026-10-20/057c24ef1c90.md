# Elon Musk’s proposed SpaceX stock giveaway would go into ‘Trump Accounts’

埃隆·马斯克提议的 SpaceX 股票赠送计划将进入“特朗普账户”。

The White House said 60 million of the tax-advantaged accounts for kids were created automatically in October.

白宫表示，10月份自动为儿童创建了6000万个税收优惠账户。

President Donald Trump announced this afternoon that Elon Musk’s SpaceX shares for children will be given through Trump Accounts. Musk said earlier today that he would give one of his SpaceX shares to “every kid in America” sometime next year. Musk had thanked Trump Account booster Brad Gerstner for “helping enable this,” and Trump appeared to confirm the plan in a post on Truth Social.

唐纳德·特朗普总统今天下午宣布，埃隆·马斯克的 SpaceX 儿童股票将通过“特朗普账户”发放。马斯克今天早些时候表示，他将在明年某个时候向“美国每个孩子”赠送一股他的 SpaceX 股票。马斯克曾感谢“特朗普账户”的推动者布拉德·格斯特纳“促成了此事”，而特朗普似乎也在“真实社交”（Truth Social）的一篇帖子中证实了该计划。

Given the value of SpaceX’s stock on Friday, 72 million shares would be worth approximately $10.5 billion, according to CNBC. “This will be the first of many,” Musk said.

据 CNBC 报道，按照周五 SpaceX 的股价计算，7200万股股票价值约105亿美元。马斯克表示：“这将是多次赠送中的第一次。”

Michael and Susan Dell, Ray and Barbara Dalio, Gerstner, and SpaceX president Gwynne Shotwell have all also promised contributions to Trump Accounts. In October, the White House said that after automatically enrolling some 60 million children, over 70 million accounts had been created.

迈克尔和苏珊·戴尔、雷和芭芭拉·达利奥、格斯特纳以及 SpaceX 总裁格温·肖特韦尔也都承诺向“特朗普账户”捐款。白宫在10月份表示，在自动为约6000万名儿童注册后，已创建了超过7000万个账户。
