Why the median is zero
The median input price spread across comparable models is exactly 0.0%, which is not an artifact of rounding—it is the structural norm. Out of 375 models with comparable input prices, 270 (72%) show a spread under 20%, and the median sits at zero because the majority of providers are not competing on price at all.

为什么中位数是零
可比模型之间的输入价格中位数价差恰好为0.0%，这并非四舍五入的结果，而是结构性的常态。在375个具有可比输入价格的模型中，270个（72%）的价差低于20%，中位数之所以为零，是因为大多数供应商根本不在价格上进行竞争。

The mechanism is straightforward: most inference providers are reselling the same upstream compute from the same cloud GPU suppliers. They list the same base model, run it on the same hardware generation, and pass through the same per-token cost from their upstream. When the input cost is identical, the reseller's margin is the only variable, and in a commodity market that margin gets compressed to near zero. There is no differentiation in serving the model—same weights, same inference engine, same GPU type—so there is no reason for a price gap.

其机制很简单：大多数推理供应商都在转售来自同一云GPU供应商的上游算力。他们列出相同的基准模型，在相同的硬件代际上运行，并转嫁来自上游的相同每token成本。当输入成本相同时，转售商的利润率是唯一的变量，而在大宗商品市场中，这一利润率会被压缩至接近零。在模型服务方面没有差异化——相同的权重、相同的推理引擎、相同的GPU类型——因此没有理由出现价格差距。

The second mechanism is list-price resale. Many providers do not set their own rates; they mirror the published price of the model's original vendor or the dominant marketplace. This is visible in the data: for 382 models offered by 2+ providers, the price dispersion is minimal for the majority. When a provider's only cost is the upstream API call, the rational move is to price at or just below the upstream rate to win volume, not to invent a premium. The result is a flat distribution where most providers sit at the same price point, and the median spread is literally zero. This is not a sign of a healthy competitive market; it is a sign of a pass-through market where no one owns the compute.

第二种机制是标价转售。许多供应商并不设定自己的费率；他们直接镜像模型原始供应商或主导市场发布的定价。这一点在数据中显而易见：对于由两家以上供应商提供的382个模型，大多数模型的价格离散度极小。当供应商唯一的成本是上游API调用时，理性的做法是以等于或略低于上游费率的价格来赢得交易量，而不是凭空增加溢价。结果就是一种平坦的分布，大多数供应商处于相同的价格点，中位数价差确实为零。这并非健康竞争市场的标志，而是无人拥有算力的转手市场的标志。

Where the spreads actually come from
The large spreads do not come from the commodity resellers—they come from two distinct business models that sit at opposite ends of the pricing spectrum. The first cluster is GPU-hour hosts. These providers (e.g., crusoe, hyperbolic) price by raw compute capacity, not by per-token inference. They rent you a GPU for a fixed hourly rate and let you run any model on it. Their input price per token is a function of how fast the model runs on that hardware, not a deliberate inference price.

价差的真正来源
巨大的价差并非来自大宗商品转售商，而是来自处于定价谱系两端的两种截然不同的商业模式。第一类是GPU小时租赁商。这些供应商（如crusoe、hyperbolic）按原始算力容量定价，而非按每token推理定价。他们以固定的每小时费率向你出租GPU，并允许你在上面运行任何模型。他们每token的输入价格是模型在该硬件上运行速度的函数，而非刻意设定的推理价格。

When a model like openai/gpt-oss-120b runs on a rented GPU, the per-token cost can be wildly different depending on the host's utilization, power cost, and hardware generation. That is why the worst case in the dataset is openai/gpt-oss-120b at $0.03/M on wandb versus $0.8/M on crusoe—a +2566.7% spread. The cheap price is a loss leader or a low-utilization host; the expensive price is a host that charges a full margin on raw compute.

当像openai/gpt-oss-120b这样的模型在租赁的GPU上运行时，每token的成本会因主机的利用率、电力成本和硬件代际而产生巨大差异。这就是为什么数据集中最糟糕的情况是openai/gpt-oss-120b在wandb上为0.03美元/百万token，而在crusoe上为0.8美元/百万token——价差高达+2566.7%。低价是亏本引流或低利用率主机所致；高价则是对原始算力收取全额利润的主机。

The second cluster is specialised inference providers. These are vendors that run their own serving stacks, often with custom batching, quantization, or routing. They price to cover their engineering cost, not just the GPU. For example, mistral-large-latest is $0.5/M on mistral's own platform but $8/M on azure—a +1500.0% spread. Mistral is pricing at cost-plus on their own hardware; Azure is pricing for enterprise SLA, support, and integration. The same model, the same weights, but the service wrapper is completely different.

第二类是专业推理供应商。这些供应商运行自己的服务堆栈，通常带有自定义批处理、量化或路由功能。他们的定价旨在覆盖工程成本，而不仅仅是GPU成本。例如，mistral-large-latest在Mistral自己的平台上为0.5美元/百万token，但在Azure上为8美元/百万token——价差为+1500.0%。Mistral是在自己的硬件上按成本加成定价；而Azure的定价则包含了企业级SLA、支持和集成服务。同一个模型，同样的权重，但服务包装完全不同。

The 42 models with spread >200% (11% of n) are almost entirely in this bucket. These are not price errors; they are two different products sold under the same model name.

What the spread table hides

Long-context requests—where the model must read a large prompt—are priced differently by many providers. The $0.03/M rate on wandb for openai/gpt-oss-120b likely assumes a standard context length; a long-context request can push that price up by an order of magnitude.
