简讯 发布时间：2026年10月19日太平洋夏令时间下午4:39 图片来源：Kyle Grillot/Bloomberg / Getty Images Lucas Ropek 报道称，Nscale与Anthropic正在商谈另一笔价值450亿美元的计算交易。

A little over a month ago, Anthropic and AI infrastructure provider Nscale announced a $45 billion compute deal. Now, the duo may be about to do it again. Bloomberg reports that the two companies are said to be on the verge of announcing yet another $45 billion compute deal, citing people familiar with the matter. Those sources cautioned that talks are still under way and that the details could change.

一个多月前，Anthropic与人工智能基础设施提供商Nscale宣布了一项450亿美元的计算交易。现在，这两家公司可能又要故技重施了。据彭博社援引知情人士的消息报道，这两家公司即将宣布另一笔价值450亿美元的计算交易。这些消息来源提醒称，谈判仍在进行中，细节仍有可能发生变化。

If the deal goes through, it would be the latest in a long string of giant compute deals Anthropic has struck over the past year. TechCrunch reached out to Nscale and Anthropic for more information and will update this story if we hear back.

如果该交易达成，这将是Anthropic在过去一年中签署的一系列巨额计算交易中的最新一笔。TechCrunch已联系Nscale和Anthropic寻求更多信息，如有回复，我们将更新此报道。

The massive amount of money being sunk into AI compute deals has spooked a certain segment of investors, who worry that companies are spending too much money for too little profit. Still, Anthropic has pressed on with such partnerships, driven by the belief that it will all pay off in the end.

投入到人工智能计算交易中的巨额资金已经吓坏了部分投资者，他们担心这些公司投入了过多的资金，却只获得了微薄的利润。尽管如此，Anthropic仍坚持推进此类合作，坚信最终会获得回报。

Both Anthropic and Nscale are also currently pursuing IPOs. Anthropic is still expected to go public this year. Nscale is also seeking to go public, although Bloomberg reported today that the company is experiencing investor pushback over its proposed valuation of $30 billion.

Anthropic和Nscale目前都在寻求首次公开募股（IPO）。Anthropic仍有望在今年上市。Nscale也在寻求上市，尽管彭博社今天报道称，该公司因其300亿美元的拟议估值而面临投资者的抵制。
