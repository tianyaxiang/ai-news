Title: Apple teases MacBook Pro event next week

苹果预告下周举办 MacBook Pro 发布会

Following Apple’s smart home-focused launch earlier in the month, Apple is hosting another product event soon.

继本月初以智能家居为重点的发布会之后，苹果即将举办另一场产品发布会。

Apple has sent invites to a second product launch this month, and it’s scheduled for October 28th at 1PM ET in Los Angeles.

苹果已发出本月第二场产品发布会的邀请函，活动定于美国东部时间 10 月 28 日下午 1 点在洛杉矶举行。

At the event, Apple is set to announce new Macs and a refreshed iPad Mini, according to Bloomberg. The big announcement is expected to be a new MacBook Pro with an OLED touchscreen that’s also lighter and has an iPhone-like Dynamic Island. But Apple is also planning on revealing a 14-inch MacBook Pro and a new iMac with the M6 chip, which was recently introduced with the Mac Mini. Apple’s updated iPad Mini will apparently finally get an OLED screen as well and put the selfie cam on the landscape edge.

据彭博社报道，苹果将在活动中发布新款 Mac 和更新版的 iPad Mini。备受瞩目的重头戏预计是一款全新的 MacBook Pro，它将配备 OLED 触摸屏，机身更轻，并拥有类似 iPhone 的“灵动岛”设计。此外，苹果还计划推出 14 英寸 MacBook Pro 以及搭载 M6 芯片的新款 iMac（该芯片近期已随 Mac Mini 一同发布）。据悉，升级后的 iPad Mini 也将终于用上 OLED 屏幕，并将自拍摄像头移至横向边缘。

“Can’t wait to pro and tell next week,” Apple’s SVP Marketing Greg Joswiak says in a teaser on X.

“迫不及待想在下周展示专业新品了，”苹果营销高级副总裁格雷格·乔斯维亚克（Greg Joswiak）在 X 平台上预告道。

This second October launch follows Apple’s smart home-focused “Welcome home” event on October 13th, which included the new HomeView smart home hub, an updated Apple TV 4K and Siri Remote, and a second-generation HomePod Mini.

这是苹果继 10 月 13 日以智能家居为主题的“欢迎回家”（Welcome home）发布会之后的第二场活动。此前的发布会内容包括全新的 HomeView 智能家居中枢、更新版的 Apple TV 4K 和 Siri Remote，以及第二代 HomePod Mini。
