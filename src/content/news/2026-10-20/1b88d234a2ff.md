By Carl Hu, Justin Johnson, Parish Aggarwal, Kaushik Rangadurai, Senthil Manickavelu, Tianwei Zhang. Generative Recommenders (GenRecs) represent a paradigm shift from scoring every ad to reasoning about ad content and user preferences together in order to predict the best outcome for advertisers. Leveraging the world knowledge built into LLMs makes ad matching more intelligent and precise, compounding gains for advertisers. GenRecs Tokenization is the foundational data representation that makes this possible. Rather than maintaining a dedicated ID for every item, GenRecs Tokenization represents each item with a set of tokens from a stable semantic vocabulary. Paired with Meta’s Generative Ads Model (GEM) and Meta’s Adaptive Ranking Model, GenRecs Tokenization has delivered a +0.35% lift in conversions across surfaces for advertisers.

GenRecs improves the way Meta’s ads recommendation systems make predictions today. Short for Generative Recommenders, GenRecs shifts our approach from scoring every individual ad candidate to leveraging LLM architecture to deeply reason about ad content and user preferences simultaneously. By pairing pre-trained world knowledge with a semantic understanding of what an ad actually represents, the system moves beyond click-pattern matching to understand underlying user preferences. This allows the model to predict the most relevant ad for each person, even when surfacing brand-new products with no past product, ad or company engagement history.

GenRecs 改进了 Meta 广告推荐系统当前的预测方式。GenRecs 是生成式推荐系统的缩写，它将我们的方法从对每个候选广告进行评分，转变为利用 LLM 架构同时深入推理广告内容和用户偏好。通过将预训练的通用知识与对广告实际含义的语义理解相结合，该系统超越了点击模式匹配，转而理解潜在的用户偏好。这使得模型能够为每个人预测最相关的广告，即使是在展示没有任何过往产品、广告或公司互动历史的全新产品时也是如此。

GenRecs Tokenization is the foundational data representation that makes this possible. Instead of maintaining a dedicated ID for every concept in the world, language models compose a small vocabulary of semantic tokens to represent any concept. Because tokens describe what an item (e.g., ads, products, services, etc.) is, the model can relate an item it has never seen engagement for, to items it knows well. A newly launched “trail shoe” inherits what the model has already learned about that category, rather than starting a new representation with no signal.

The tokenizer combines that content signal with representations learned from how people engage with the items, then quantizes the result into a short sequence of discrete tokens. Similar items share tokens, much as related words share roots. For example, there’s overlap between two running shoe ads from the previous season to the current season, so the new campaign immediately benefits from everything the model has already learned about the previous season’s products. At the heart of GenRecs Tokenization is a three-stage pipeline that: Encodes each item into a semantic embedding space. Decomposes that embedding into independent subspaces. Quantizes by mapping each subspace to an entry in a codebook.

分词器将该内容信号与从人们与项目互动方式中学习到的表示相结合，然后将结果量化为一小段离散的标记序列。相似的项目共享标记，就像相关的词共享词根一样。例如，上一季和本季的两则跑鞋广告之间存在重叠，因此新的广告系列可以立即受益于模型此前对上一季产品所学到的一切。GenRecs 分词的核心是一个三阶段流水线：将每个项目编码到语义嵌入空间；将该嵌入分解为独立的子空间；通过将每个子空间映射到码本中的条目来进行量化。

Maximizing ads recommendation quality requires diverse combinations of these stages depending on entity and feature semantics. For example, for many ad representations, GenRecs Tokenization uses prefix n-grams that combine a learned encoder, residual decomposition, learned vector codebooks, and token parametrization. Independently of downstream ranking gain, tokenizer model quality can be evaluated against intrinsic measures like: Cluster purity: The items that share a code tend to share a category, giving the model a cleaner signal per token; Distribution balance: Items spread evenly across semantic IDs instead of crowding into a few.

最大化广告推荐质量需要根据实体和特征语义，对这些阶段进行多种组合。例如，对于许多广告表示，GenRecs 分词使用前缀 n-gram，结合了学习编码器、残差分解、学习向量码本和标记参数化。标记质量预测排序准确性。除了下游的排序增益外，分词器模型的质量还可以通过以下内在指标进行评估：聚类纯度：共享代码的项目往往属于同一类别，从而为模型提供更清晰的单标记信号；分布平衡：项目均匀分布在各个语义 ID 中，而不是集中在少数几个 ID 上。

Codebook utilization: A larger share of codes holds at least one item, so the vocabulary expresses more distinctions.
