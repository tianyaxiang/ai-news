Tolan, the AI companion designed to help people manage their daily lives, navigate relationships and emotions, and accomplish their goals, has just expanded its runway with another $30 million in capital. The startup’s investors in the latest round include Union Square Ventures, Khosla Ventures, and Homebrew, doubling the total amount it has raised to date and pushing its valuation beyond the previous $120 million, according to a new report by Upstarts Media. (The startup didn’t disclose its new valuation, but PitchBook estimates it at more than $268 million, with “medium confidence.”)

Tolan 是一款旨在帮助人们管理日常生活、处理人际关系与情绪并实现目标的 AI 伴侣，该公司刚刚获得了 3000 万美元的新一轮融资。据 Upstarts Media 的最新报道，该初创公司本轮融资的投资者包括 Union Square Ventures、Khosla Ventures 和 Homebrew，这使其融资总额翻了一番，估值也超过了此前的 1.2 亿美元。（该公司未披露其最新估值，但 PitchBook 估计其估值超过 2.68 亿美元，置信度为“中等”。）

Users engage with Tolan’s app through a cartoonish character that’s meant to offer support and help them get through their to-dos. Image Credits: Tolan on App Store. Tolan generates revenue from its tiered memberships, which are required for full access to all features. The company told Upstarts Media its annual recurring revenue is now in the “double-digit millions.”

用户通过一个卡通形象与 Tolan 的应用程序进行互动，该形象旨在提供支持并帮助用户完成待办事项。图片来源：App Store 上的 Tolan。Tolan 通过分级会员制度产生收入，用户需要付费才能使用所有功能。该公司告诉 Upstarts Media，其年度经常性收入目前已达到“千万美元级别”。

The startup is one of the few AI companions that have found some traction, as it claims to have more than 250,000 active users who have had more than 70 million conversations over the past year. Around 70% of its users are women, and 40% are parents, Tolan’s team told the news outlet. (Anecdotally, the app is said to appeal in particular to neurodivergent people, who may benefit from additional support, whether that’s emotional support or help with organizing tasks.)

该初创公司是为数不多获得一定市场吸引力的 AI 伴侣之一，据称其拥有超过 25 万名活跃用户，在过去一年中进行了超过 7000 万次对话。Tolan 团队告诉该媒体，其用户中约 70% 为女性，40% 为父母。（据传，该应用对神经多样性人群特别有吸引力，他们可能从额外的支持中受益，无论是情感支持还是任务整理方面的帮助。）

Compared with other companions, Tolan seems to have found a balance between being helpful without being perceived as too weird. Compare that with the public shaming that Friend’s pendant-based AI companion faced; users dubbed Friend “very cringe,” The New York Times called it “hated,” and Wired said it “has no soul.” Meanwhile, another former top contender in the space, Character.AI, recently filed for bankruptcy after its founder’s return to Google. (Its remaining staff is transitioning to Disney, which bought some of its assets.)

与其他伴侣相比，Tolan 似乎在提供帮助与避免显得过于怪异之间找到了平衡。相比之下，Friend 的吊坠式 AI 伴侣曾遭遇公众羞辱；用户称 Friend “非常尴尬”，《纽约时报》称其“令人讨厌”，《连线》杂志则称其“没有灵魂”。与此同时，该领域的另一位前顶级竞争者 Character.AI 在其创始人回归谷歌后，最近申请了破产。（其剩余员工正在过渡到迪士尼，迪士尼收购了其部分资产。）

However, Tolan could end up facing increased competition from the more recent crop of AI agents, which help users plan, organize, and accomplish their tasks and goals, not just talk their way through them. For instance, Meta gave its AI agent Muse a cute avatar for its users to talk to, which it also plans to release as a hardware product in the future. The startup also has to navigate the risks of offering AI-powered emotional support, given that multiple lawsuits have been filed against general-purpose AI app makers alleging that chatbot interactions contributed to users’ suicides.

然而，Tolan 最终可能会面临来自近期涌现的 AI 智能体的激烈竞争，这些智能体不仅能与用户对话，还能帮助用户规划、组织并完成任务和目标。例如，Meta 为其 AI 智能体 Muse 设计了一个可爱的头像供用户交流，并计划在未来将其作为硬件产品发布。此外，鉴于已有多起针对通用 AI 应用开发商的诉讼，指控聊天机器人互动导致用户自杀，该初创公司还必须应对提供 AI 驱动的情感支持所带来的风险。
