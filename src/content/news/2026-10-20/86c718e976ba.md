SideQuest has made sideloading Quest titles a breeze—one‑click, no terminal sets. From a dev perspective, that’s a huge lift in adoption.

SideQuest 让侧载 Quest 游戏变得轻而易举——只需一键，无需终端设置。从开发者的角度来看，这对提升采用率有巨大的帮助。

AmblyoPunch is a VR vision‑training app built with Unity and Oculus SDK for Meta Quest. It uses Gabor‑pattern coins and red spikes to execute a four‑stage dichoptic training pipeline.

AmblyoPunch 是一款使用 Unity 和 Oculus SDK 为 Meta Quest 构建的 VR 视力训练应用程序。它利用加博尔（Gabor）图案硬币和红色尖刺来执行四阶段双眼视觉训练流程。

The app auto‑detects the weaker eye and applies suppression/differential brightness to target the rebalancing phase. In custom mode, developers can tweak binocular disparities and vergence angles to model esotropia or exotropia.

该应用程序会自动检测较弱的眼睛，并应用抑制或差异亮度来针对重新平衡阶段进行训练。在自定义模式下，开发者可以调整双眼视差和辐辏角，以模拟内斜视或外斜视。

With SideQuest’s streamlined installation, we can ship focused eye‑care experiences to enthusiasts and clinicians alike. Check the build: https://www.meta.com/en-gb/experiences/amblyopunch/1239507485902689/

借助 SideQuest 的简化安装流程，我们可以向爱好者和临床医生提供专注的眼部护理体验。查看构建版本：https://www.meta.com/en-gb/experiences/amblyopunch/1239507485902689/
