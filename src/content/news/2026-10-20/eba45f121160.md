Then the Guidelines: Topicality: Lobsters is focused pretty narrowly on computing; tags like art don't imply every piece of art is on-topic. Some rules of thumb for great stories to submit: Will this improve the reader's next program? Will it deepen their understanding of their last program? Will it be more interesting in five or ten years? Is this compelling material that will seed a discussion, or is it just an artifact? Pending an upcoming deploy, we no longer allow submissions which are code repositories. While, at first blush, this may seem odd for a community which is primarily centered around programming but it does eliminate the bulk of the "low effort, self promotional" submissions we've been seeing on the site.

接着是指南：主题相关性：Lobsters 的关注点相当狭窄，仅限于计算；像“艺术”这样的标签并不意味着每一件艺术品都是相关主题。提交优秀文章的一些经验法则：这能改善读者下一个程序吗？这能加深他们对上一个程序的理解吗？五年或十年后它会更有趣吗？这是能引发讨论的引人入胜的材料，还是仅仅是一个产物？在即将到来的部署中，我们将不再允许提交代码仓库。虽然乍一看，对于一个主要以编程为中心的社区来说这似乎很奇怪，但这确实消除了我们在网站上看到的绝大多数“低质量、自我推销”的提交。

We've also found that being dropped into a new codebase doesn't start discussions because the reader has to have enough subject matter experience to immediately recognize what's novel or otherwise worth talking about. Instead, we are more interested in content around code bases which cover why it was created, the purpose it serves, the challenges overcome, and the reasons why it's a useful artifact in the ecosystem. Some things that are off-topic here but popular on larger, similar sites: entrepreneurship; management; careers; news about companies that employ a lot of programmers; investing; world events; anthropology; self-help; personal productivity systems; last-resort customer service requests via public shaming; "I wanted to see what this site's amazing users think about this off-topic thing"; and defining the single morally correct economic and political system for the entire world when we can't even settle tabs vs. spaces.

我们还发现，直接丢出一个新的代码库并不能引发讨论，因为读者必须具备足够的专业经验，才能立即识别出其中有什么新颖之处或值得讨论的地方。相反，我们对围绕代码库的内容更感兴趣，这些内容应涵盖其创建原因、服务目的、克服的挑战，以及它为何是生态系统中一个有用的产物。以下是一些在这里属于离题但在大型类似网站上很受欢迎的内容：创业、管理、职业生涯、关于雇佣大量程序员的公司的消息、投资、世界大事、人类学、自助、个人生产力系统、通过公开羞辱进行的最后手段式客户服务请求；“我想看看这个网站出色的用户对这个离题的事情有什么看法”；以及在我们连制表符与空格之争都无法解决时，去定义全世界唯一道德正确的经济和政治制度。

We're listing "careers" as off-topic to try to head off posts about interviewing, promotions, burnout, and farming. We have the job tag because it's nice to help people connect to jobs, but the topic of getting a good job, weaving jobs into a career over your lifetime, and work is a different thing than the actual programming itself. Self-promotion: It's great to have authors participate in the community, but we do not want the community to be exploited as a write-only tool for product announcements or driving traffic to their work. As a rule of thumb, self-promotional activity should be less than a quarter of one's stories and comments in the last year. New users (first 70 days) are not allowed to submit self-promo.

我们将“职业生涯”列为离题，以试图阻止关于面试、晋升、职业倦怠和刷简历的帖子。我们有“工作”标签，因为帮助人们找到工作是件好事，但关于如何找到好工作、如何将工作融入终身职业生涯以及工作本身的话题，与实际的编程本身是两码事。自我推销：作者参与社区是件好事，但我们不希望社区被当作发布产品公告或为自己的作品引流的“只写”工具。作为经验法则，自我推销活动应少于过去一年中个人发布文章和评论总数的四分之一。新用户（前 70 天）不允许提交自我推销内容。

This is how we've been enforcing the rule for a while, better to be explicit. It's unfortunately common that authors see their work posted, join the site, but then only use us for traffic. Hopefully this time helps them understand the difference between a symbiotic and parasitic relationship. The second adjustment is that the vibecoding tag is now marked as inactive. It still appears on old stories but can't be applied to new ones. From my perspective, the tag was overloaded with six differing responsibilities: First, it was the tag which was trying to capture the discussions related to the use and implementation of AI/LLM tools. Second, it was a marker for content which is partially related to AI/LLM tools, tooling or companies.

这是我们一段时间以来执行该规则的方式，明确出来会更好。不幸的是，常见的情况是作者看到自己的作品被发布，加入网站，然后却只把我们当作流量来源。希望这次调整能帮助他们理解共生关系与寄生关系之间的区别。第二个调整是“vibecoding”标签现在被标记为无效。它仍然会出现在旧文章上，但不能应用于新文章。从我的角度来看，这个标签被赋予了六种不同的职责：第一，它是试图捕捉与 AI/LLM 工具的使用和实现相关的讨论的标签。第二，它是部分与 AI/LLM 工具、工具链或公司相关的内容的标记。

Third, it was a marker for content which includes a discussion on or output from AI/LLM tools in a part of a larger work. Fourth, it was a marker for content which was generated using AI/LLM tools. Fifth, it was being used in competition to the spam flag, for really low-quality content which was generated via AI/LLM tools but without apparent significant human authorship. Sixth, it was being used as a tag of shame by people who really dislike AI/LLM tools and used it to insult people at every passing mention. The last couple years have been the biggest upheaval in the field for decades. While it's acceptable, within limits, to express frustration towards circumstance or technology, it's not acceptable to direct that behavior towards individuals whether they be members of the community or not.

第三，它是标记在更大作品中包含对 AI/LLM 工具的讨论或其输出的内容的标签。第四，它是标记使用 AI/LLM 工具生成的内容的标签。第五，它被用作垃圾信息标记的竞争对手，用于标记那些通过 AI/LLM 工具生成但没有明显人类创作痕迹的极低质量内容。第六，它被那些非常讨厌 AI/LLM 工具的人用作“羞辱标签”，在每次提到时都用来侮辱他人。过去几年是该领域几十年来最大的动荡期。虽然在一定限度内表达对环境或技术的挫败感是可以接受的，但将这种行为指向个人（无论他们是否是社区成员）是不可接受的。

Other people can't be your punching bags and Lobsters can't be your boxing ring. That's not acceptable. While `vibecoding` is no longer an active tag, we still feel that a subset of the tooling related discussions are relevant to the site.
