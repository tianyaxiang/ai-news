Marble Skill Taxonomy
An open, structured taxonomy of what children learn across the primary/elementary years — decomposed into fine-grained "micro-topics", wired into a prerequisite graph, and aligned to national curriculum standards. Produced by Marble. Version: v1 · Topics: 1,591 · Prerequisite edges: 3,225 · Subjects: 8

大理石技能分类法（Marble Skill Taxonomy）
这是一套开放且结构化的分类法，涵盖了儿童在小学阶段的学习内容——将其分解为细颗粒度的“微主题”，通过先修关系图进行连接，并与国家课程标准对齐。由 Marble 制作。版本：v1 · 主题：1,591 个 · 先修关系边：3,225 条 · 学科：8 个

See it
Every dot is a micro-topic, colored by subject; height is age; each thread is a prerequisite (full-quality video). Explore it interactively at withmarble.com/curriculum — tap any concept to trace everything a learner must master before it.

查看方式
每一个点都是一个微主题，按学科着色；高度代表年龄；每一条线都是一个先修关系（全高清视频）。请访问 withmarble.com/curriculum 进行交互式探索——点击任何概念，即可追踪学习者在掌握该概念前必须完成的所有内容。

What this is
Most curriculum data is either a flat list of standards or locked inside a product. This dataset is a connected graph of learning: 1,591 micro-topics — a single, teachable idea (e.g. "Building sentences", "Apparent brightness of stars"), each with a plain-language description, mastery evidence criteria, a type (conceptual / procedural / representational / language / meta), a subject + domain, and an approximate age range.

这是什么
大多数课程数据要么是标准的平面列表，要么被锁定在产品内部。该数据集是一个互联的学习图谱：包含 1,591 个微主题——即单一的可教概念（例如“造句”、“恒星的视亮度”），每个主题都配有通俗易懂的描述、掌握程度评估标准、类型（概念型/程序型/表征型/语言型/元认知型）、学科+领域以及大致的年龄范围。

3,225 prerequisite dependencies — a directed acyclic graph: "topic X depends on prerequisite Y", each edge tagged hard/soft and carrying a one-line reason.
Curriculum alignment — each micro-topic links to the standards it was distilled from (NGSS, Common Core, the UK National Curriculum, and more).

3,225 个先修依赖关系——一个有向无环图：“主题 X 依赖于先修主题 Y”，每条边都标记为“硬”或“软”依赖，并附有一行理由。
课程对齐——每个微主题都链接到其提取来源的课程标准（如 NGSS、Common Core、英国国家课程等）。

Teaching research — 1,051 records covering the current Science and Mathematics topics: teaching approaches, concrete learning activities, feedback, assessment guidance and linked sources. Coverage is partial; the other 540 topics have no research in this release.
Domain clusters — 176 parent-friendly one-paragraph summaries per (subject, domain, age band).

教学研究——1,051 条记录，涵盖当前的科学和数学主题：教学方法、具体的学习活动、反馈、评估指导及相关来源。覆盖范围不完整；其余 540 个主题在此版本中暂无研究内容。
领域集群——针对每个（学科、领域、年龄段）提供 176 条家长友好的单段式总结。

Teaching research
data/teaching-research.json adds how a topic might be taught to the graph of what children learn. It covers 547 Science and 504 Mathematics topics. coverage.missingTopicIds and per-subject counts make the 540 topics without research explicit; absence is not a judgement about whether a topic can be taught. The file has schemaVersion: 1 and taxonomy version: "v1".

教学研究
data/teaching-research.json 将“如何教授主题”添加到了“儿童学习内容”的图谱中。它涵盖了 547 个科学主题和 504 个数学主题。coverage.missingTopicIds 和各学科统计数据明确指出了 540 个尚无研究的主题；缺失并不代表该主题无法教授。该文件具有 schemaVersion: 1 和分类法版本 "v1"。

Each records[] entry joins topics.json through topicId and contains:
Field Meaning
pedagogy Original teaching summary, practices, representations and misconceptions.
learn Portable learning guidance (schemaVersion: 2): a summary, activities, educational constraints and review notes.
learn.activities[] Learner actions, intended insight, feedback, rationale and supporting source claims. No product artifact types or fit rankings.
assess Original diagnostic/task, response levels, error patterns and next teaching moves; nullable in the public interface.
sources Claim, named source, optional URL and the original author's verification flag.
confidence Original authoring confidence; not an independent quality score.
provenance Original authoring model and source record timestamps. The update timestamp also reflects the learning-guidance rewrite.
publicationNotes Paths and curriculum codes for text omitted under the codes-only publication policy.

每个 records[] 条目通过 topicId 与 topics.json 关联，并包含：
字段 含义
pedagogy 原始教学总结、实践、表征和误区。
learn 可移植的学习指导（schemaVersion: 2）：总结、活动、教育约束和审阅笔记。
learn.activities[] 学习者行动、预期见解、反馈、基本原理和支持性来源声明。不含产品工件类型或适用性排名。
assess 原始诊断/任务、反应水平、错误模式和后续教学步骤；在公共接口中可为空。
sources 声明、命名来源、可选 URL 和原始作者的验证标记。
confidence 原始编写信心；非独立质量评分。

publicationNotes 在“仅代码”发布政策下省略文本的路径和课程代码。
