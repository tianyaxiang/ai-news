# From Visual Cues to Spoken Narration: Rethinking Audio Description
# 从视觉线索到口述旁白：重新思考音频描述

**Abstract:** Audio Description (AD) provides spoken narration of visual events during dialogue gaps, making movies accessible to visually impaired audiences. The problem requires determining both what (which visual event) and when (position for inserting the AD) to narrate, to achieve the best user experience.
**摘要：** 音频描述（Audio Description, AD）通过在对话间隙对视觉事件进行口述旁白，使视障观众能够欣赏电影。为了实现最佳的用户体验，该问题需要同时确定“叙述什么”（即哪个视觉事件）以及“何时叙述”（即插入音频描述的位置）。

Prior work has largely reduced the problem to video captioning of pre-segmented video clips, i.e., what is largely predefined and when is ignored entirely. We propose Cue2Narrate, a two-stage pipeline that jointly predicts what and when to narrate in longer untrimmed movie clips.
既往研究大多将该问题简化为对预先分割的视频片段进行视频字幕生成，即“叙述什么”在很大程度上是预定义的，而“何时叙述”则被完全忽略。我们提出了 Cue2Narrate，这是一个两阶段流水线，能够联合预测在较长的未剪辑电影片段中“叙述什么”以及“何时叙述”。

A dual-head audio-visual localizer predicts two temporally distinct windows per AD utterance: a visual cue window and a spoken narration window. A LoRA-adapted VLM then generates concise ADs from the predicted visual evidence, trained with a Description Ranking Loss that ranks captions (negative samples) of the same frames lower than the GT AD.
该模型采用双头视听定位器，为每段音频描述预测两个时间上不同的窗口：视觉线索窗口和口述旁白窗口。随后，一个经过 LoRA 适配的视觉语言模型（VLM）根据预测的视觉证据生成简洁的音频描述。模型训练采用了描述排序损失（Description Ranking Loss），确保同一帧的负样本字幕排名低于真实标注（GT）的音频描述。

To benchmark this new problem statement, we introduce the LongLSMDC benchmark with up to 8-min movie clips (~6.5min on average). On LongLSMDC, Cue2Narrate outperforms video-only and audio-only localization baselines by 5--12 points in avg. mAP. Under both predicted- and GT-window evaluation, Cue2Narrate improves AD generation over the corresponding fine-tuned base VLM. These results establish the first benchmark for multi-segment AD generation on long-form clips.
为了对这一新问题进行基准测试，我们引入了 LongLSMDC 基准数据集，其中包含长达 8 分钟的电影片段（平均时长约 6.5 分钟）。在 LongLSMDC 上，Cue2Narrate 在平均 mAP 指标上比仅视频和仅音频的定位基线高出 5 到 12 个百分点。无论是在预测窗口还是真实窗口的评估下，Cue2Narrate 在音频描述生成方面的表现均优于相应的微调基础 VLM。这些结果确立了长视频片段多段音频描述生成的首个基准。
