# WMLLM: Self-Evolving Optimization Agents via Predict-Then-Act World Modeling
# WMLLM：基于“预测-行动”世界模型的自我进化优化智能体

**Abstract:** Black-box optimization problems remain challenging because of large, weakly structured, and high-dimensional search spaces. Existing methods often suffer from poor sample efficiency because they rely on direct candidate generation or trial-and-error refinement. 

**摘要：** 由于搜索空间庞大、结构松散且维度高，黑盒优化问题依然极具挑战性。现有的方法往往因依赖直接的候选生成或试错式改进，而面临样本效率低下的问题。

A natural way to improve search efficiency is to use world modeling, which can help identify promising optimization directions before costly evaluation. Large language models can predict the outcomes of these candidates with nontrivial accuracy because of their implicit knowledge. 

提高搜索效率的一种自然方法是使用世界模型，它有助于在进行昂贵的评估之前识别出有前景的优化方向。得益于其隐含的知识，大型语言模型能够以相当高的准确度预测这些候选方案的结果。

Motivated by this observation, we propose WMLLM, a self-evolving optimization-agent framework based on predict-then-act world modeling. The agent first predicts promising directions and then acts to generate candidates. 

受此观察启发，我们提出了 WMLLM，这是一个基于“预测-行动”世界模型的自我进化优化智能体框架。该智能体首先预测有前景的方向，然后采取行动生成候选方案。

Combined with agentic multi-turn refinement, population-based search, and reinforcement learning, WMLLM refines both its implicit world model and its optimization strategy during search. 

结合智能体多轮改进、基于种群的搜索以及强化学习，WMLLM 在搜索过程中不断优化其隐含的世界模型及其优化策略。

Experiments on black-box optimization tasks, especially multi-objective molecular optimization, show that WMLLM improves sample efficiency and final optimization performance. On the multi-objective molecular optimization benchmark, WMLLM achieves state-of-the-art results under a limited evaluation budget.

在黑盒优化任务（特别是多目标分子优化）上的实验表明，WMLLM 提高了样本效率和最终的优化性能。在多目标分子优化基准测试中，WMLLM 在有限的评估预算下实现了最先进（SOTA）的成果。
