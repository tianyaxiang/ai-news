# FairLens: Benchmarking Fairness in Vision-Language Models for High-Stakes Decision-Making
# FairLens：高风险决策场景下视觉语言模型公平性基准测试

Vision-language models (VLMs) are increasingly used to make decisions from visual inputs. We introduce FAIRLENS, a benchmark and evaluation framework for measuring both the fairness and the validity of VLM responses in three high-stakes domains: hiring, legal, and healthcare.
视觉语言模型（VLM）正越来越多地被用于基于视觉输入进行决策。我们推出了 FAIRLENS，这是一个用于衡量 VLM 在招聘、法律和医疗保健这三个高风险领域中响应的公平性与有效性的基准及评估框架。

FAIRLENS pairs real face images spanning gender, race, and age groups with closed- and open-ended questions, giving more than 100K image-question pairs per model, and evaluates responses from four complementary views: demographic parity over adverse outcome rates, soundness, demographic association over unsupported roles and statuses, and bias in free-text generation.
FAIRLENS 将涵盖不同性别、种族和年龄组的真实人脸图像与封闭式及开放式问题配对，为每个模型提供了超过 10 万个图像-问题对。它从四个互补的维度评估模型响应：基于负面结果率的人口统计学平权、合理性、针对无根据角色与身份的人口统计学关联，以及自由文本生成中的偏见。

Soundness is the central validity criterion: a response is sound when it follows the evidence stated in the question and abstains when the image cannot support an answer.
合理性是核心的有效性标准：当响应遵循问题中陈述的证据，并在图像无法支持回答时选择拒绝回答，该响应即被视为“合理”。

Evaluating eight VLMs, we find that the primary failure is unwarranted inference rather than unequal treatment. Models routinely infer qualifications, threat, illness, or professional role from a face instead of abstaining, and the weakest model does so on 99% of the questions its input cannot answer.
通过对八个 VLM 进行评估，我们发现模型的主要缺陷在于“无根据的推断”，而非单纯的不平等对待。模型经常从人脸中推断出资质、威胁、疾病或职业角色，而不是选择拒绝回答；表现最差的模型在 99% 其输入无法支持回答的问题上都出现了这种情况。

These failures are most severe in legal and healthcare, where recognizing insufficient evidence matters most, and disparity metrics alone would miss them: parity gaps are small in absolute terms, yet when baseline adverse rates are low the same gap means one demographic group receives adverse labels several times as often as another, and a small gap can equally reflect a model that treats every group unsafely.
这些缺陷在法律和医疗领域最为严重，因为在这些领域，识别证据不足至关重要，而仅靠差异指标往往会忽略这些问题：从绝对值来看，平权差距可能很小，但当基准负面率较低时，同样的差距意味着一个群体受到负面标签的频率是另一个群体的数倍；同时，微小的差距也可能反映出一个对所有群体都采取不安全处理方式的模型。

Bias in free-text responses is only loosely coupled to multiple-choice accuracy, so correct structured answers do not imply safe generation.
自由文本响应中的偏见与多项选择题的准确性之间仅存在微弱的关联，因此正确的结构化答案并不意味着生成的文本是安全的。

FAIRLENS shows that fair high-stakes VLM behavior requires similar treatment across groups and refusal to infer high-stakes attributes from appearance, and its question suite transfers to any face corpus with demographic annotations.
FAIRLENS 表明，高风险场景下公平的 VLM 行为要求模型在不同群体间保持一致的处理方式，并拒绝仅凭外貌推断高风险属性。此外，其问题集可以迁移到任何带有统计学标注的人脸语料库中。
