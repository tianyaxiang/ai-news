# CliffRank: A Dual-Branch Framework for Activity-Cliff Ranking Prediction
# CliffRank：用于活性悬崖排序预测的双分支框架

**Abstract:** Activity-cliff ranking remains difficult because local structural changes can cause large activity differences, while high-quality data that resolve the underlying mechanisms remain limited.
**摘要：** 活性悬崖（Activity-cliff）排序仍然是一个难题，因为局部的结构变化可能导致巨大的活性差异，而能够解析其潜在机制的高质量数据依然有限。

To use available activity labels more effectively, we combine absolute-activity regression with ranking-consistency learning. CliffRank trains two parallel predictors with mean squared error, a thresholded listwise loss, and Pairwise Preference Consistency (PPC), which aligns relative ordering in the preference-probability space.
为了更有效地利用现有的活性标签，我们将绝对活性回归与排序一致性学习相结合。CliffRank 训练了两个并行预测器，采用了均方误差、阈值列表损失以及成对偏好一致性（PPC），后者在偏好概率空间中对齐了相对排序。

On three antimicrobial peptide datasets, CliffRank with ESM2-t12 achieved the highest mean Spearman correlation of 0.5393 and mean Recall@50 of 21.4, although the leading method varied across individual datasets.
在三个抗菌肽数据集上，结合 ESM2-t12 的 CliffRank 实现了 0.5393 的最高平均 Spearman 相关系数和 21.4 的平均 Recall@50，尽管领先的方法在不同数据集之间有所差异。

On three small-molecule datasets, CliffRank with PNA, where PPC was activated after 120 epochs, achieved the highest mean Spearman correlation of 0.6890, while its mean Recall@50 of 30.4 matched that of ACANet-PNA.
在三个小分子数据集上，结合 PNA（并在 120 个 epoch 后激活 PPC）的 CliffRank 实现了 0.6890 的最高平均 Spearman 相关系数，其 30.4 的平均 Recall@50 与 ACANet-PNA 持平。

The PPC results also define its practical limits. Asymmetric initialization improved the MolCLR-GIN averages but did not improve every target. For PNA without pretrained weights, delayed PPC improved selected metrics, but no schedule was best for both mean Spearman correlation and mean Recall@50.
PPC 的结果也界定了其应用极限。非对称初始化改善了 MolCLR-GIN 的平均表现，但并未改善所有目标。对于没有预训练权重的 PNA，延迟 PPC 改善了部分指标，但没有一种调度方案能同时在平均 Spearman 相关系数和平均 Recall@50 上达到最优。

Future work should evaluate more targets and antimicrobial peptide systems, develop adaptive PPC schedules, and incorporate protein or membrane context when available.
未来的工作应评估更多的目标和抗菌肽系统，开发自适应的 PPC 调度方案，并在条件允许时纳入蛋白质或膜环境信息。
