### Child-monitoring apps might need a reboot
### 儿童监控应用或许需要“重启”

Digital harms have become the defining fear of American parents. In response, they’re increasingly turning to content-monitoring apps that scan their children’s texts, photos, emails, and chats, issuing alerts whenever an algorithm flags something it deems dangerous. These tools have had genuine successes, preventing suicide attempts, intercepting predators, and averting school shootings. But they can also cause harm themselves, from false alarms and unnecessary interventions to damaged trust and anxiety. Child-safety researchers say better approaches exist. Here’s what they think those could look like. —Kelly Clancy
数字伤害已成为美国家长最核心的恐惧。作为回应，他们越来越多地转向内容监控应用，这些应用会扫描孩子的短信、照片、电子邮件和聊天记录，并在算法标记出其认为危险的内容时发出警报。这些工具确实取得了一些成功，例如预防自杀企图、拦截不法分子以及避免校园枪击事件。但它们本身也可能造成伤害，从误报和不必要的干预，到破坏信任和引发焦虑。儿童安全研究人员表示，存在更好的方法。以下是他们认为这些方法可能的样子。——Kelly Clancy

### Agriculture relies on fossil fuels. It’s costing us.
### 农业依赖化石燃料，我们正为此付出代价

Fertilizer prices have been on a roller coaster this year, driven in part by trade disruptions and high natural-gas prices. That’s because natural gas is both an energy source and a chemical input in the production of ammonia, a key fertilizer ingredient. The war in Iran has pushed natural-gas prices higher and disrupted fertilizer trade through the Strait of Hormuz—and its closure could make access harder still for some of the world’s poorest countries. Let’s take a closer look at the surging fertilizer prices, and how a few climate-friendly alternatives could bring farmers some relief. —Casey Crownhart
今年化肥价格像坐过山车一样波动，部分原因是贸易中断和天然气价格高企。这是因为天然气既是能源，也是生产氨（化肥的关键成分）的化学原料。伊朗战争推高了天然气价格，并扰乱了通过霍尔木兹海峡的化肥贸易——如果海峡关闭，世界上一些最贫穷的国家获取化肥的难度可能会进一步加大。让我们深入了解一下飙升的化肥价格，以及一些气候友好型替代方案如何能为农民带来缓解。——Casey Crownhart

**Colossal Biosciences said it cloned red wolves. Is it for real?**
**Colossal Biosciences 声称克隆了红狼，这是真的吗？**

Last year, Colossal Biosciences announced that it had cloned four red wolves, surprising scientists working to save the species and even people whose canids supplied the DNA. They also disagreed about the company’s big idea: de-extinction. Colossal kept the location of the mystery clones secret, and even their purpose was murky to some scientists. Just how they might restore red wolf populations was unclear. Perhaps the most curious question, though, was whether the company had cloned red wolves at all. Can cloning really help save the red wolf? And what should we really be trying to preserve? Read the full story to find out. —Boyce Upholt
去年，Colossal Biosciences 宣布已克隆出四只红狼，这让致力于拯救该物种的科学家，甚至提供 DNA 的犬科动物主人都感到惊讶。他们对该公司宏大的“复活灭绝物种”理念也存在分歧。Colossal 对这些神秘克隆体的所在地保密，甚至在一些科学家看来，它们的目的也很模糊。它们究竟如何能恢复红狼种群尚不清楚。不过，最令人好奇的问题或许是：该公司是否真的克隆了红狼？克隆真的能帮助拯救红狼吗？我们真正应该努力保护的是什么？阅读全文以了解详情。——Boyce Upholt
