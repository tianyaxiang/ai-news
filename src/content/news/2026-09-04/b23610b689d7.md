# Sim2Signal: Sim-to-Real Benchmarks for Traffic Signal Control
# Sim2Signal：交通信号控制的仿真到现实基准测试

Reinforcement learning achieves strong traffic signal control performance in simulation, yet policies trained in simulators often fail once deployed in the real world, a failure known as the Sim-to-Real gap. When RL is applied to traffic signal control, this gap arises from several sources: sensing, action execution, traffic dynamics, and the control objective. Their relative impact and the reliability of existing Sim-to-Real mitigation methods remain insufficiently understood, and the field lacks a standard benchmark for systematically measuring the gap and evaluating mitigation methods.

强化学习在仿真环境中实现了强大的交通信号控制性能，然而在模拟器中训练的策略一旦部署到现实世界中往往会失效，这种失败被称为“仿真到现实”（Sim-to-Real）差距。当强化学习应用于交通信号控制时，这种差距源于多个方面：感知、动作执行、交通动态以及控制目标。目前，这些因素的相对影响以及现有“仿真到现实”缓解方法的可靠性尚不明确，且该领域缺乏一个用于系统性衡量差距并评估缓解方法的标准基准。

We present Sim2Signal, a benchmark that decomposes the Sim-to-Real gap into observation, action, transition, and reward gaps, corresponding to mismatches in the four components of the underlying MDP, and induces each gap in isolation under a shared protocol. We evaluate 18 mitigation methods on 2 base controllers, across 33 gap settings and 10 calibrated networks built from 5 real-world locations.

我们提出了 Sim2Signal，这是一个将“仿真到现实”差距分解为观测、动作、转移和奖励差距的基准测试。这些差距对应于底层马尔可夫决策过程（MDP）中四个组件的不匹配，并在统一协议下独立诱导每种差距。我们针对 2 个基础控制器，在 33 种差距设置和基于 5 个真实地点构建的 10 个校准网络上评估了 18 种缓解方法。

We find that direct transfer consistently degrades performance across all four gap sources, but the severity of the degradation does not predict the effectiveness of mitigation. Instead, mitigation effectiveness depends strongly on the network and gap setting: outside the action gap, a method that helps in one case may fail in another. The most effective methods generally estimate what the gap changes, rather than make the policy insensitive through domain randomization or invariant representations.

研究发现，直接迁移在所有四个差距来源中都会持续导致性能下降，但下降的严重程度并不能预测缓解措施的有效性。相反，缓解措施的有效性在很大程度上取决于网络和差距设置：除了动作差距外，在一种情况下有效的方法在另一种情况下可能会失效。最有效的方法通常是估计差距的变化情况，而不是通过域随机化或不变表示来使策略对差距不敏感。
