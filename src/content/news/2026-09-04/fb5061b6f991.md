# You can watch the coolant flow inside Ugreen’s liquid-cooled power bank
# 你可以亲眼看到绿联液冷充电宝内部的冷却液流动

The $149.99 MagFlow Pro is considerably more expensive than other 10,000mAh power banks.
售价 149.99 美元的 MagFlow Pro 比其他 10,000mAh 充电宝要昂贵得多。

Does your power bank have “CryoPulse technology” or a “Cyber Window”? If you disappointingly answered “no” to both, you might want to consider upgrading to Ugreen’s new MagFlow Pro Magnetic Power Bank. It’s available starting today for $149.99, which puts it on the really expensive end of power banks with only a 10,000mAh battery, but those fancy-sounding features are part of what Ugreen is claiming to be the world’s first liquid-cooling system on a power bank with wireless charging.
你的充电宝拥有“CryoPulse 技术”或“赛博视窗（Cyber Window）”吗？如果你遗憾地回答“没有”，那么你或许该考虑升级到绿联新款 MagFlow Pro 磁吸充电宝了。该产品即日起发售，售价 149.99 美元。对于一款仅有 10,000mAh 容量的充电宝来说，这个价格相当昂贵，但这些听起来很高级的功能，正是绿联所宣称的全球首款搭载无线充电液冷系统的充电宝的核心卖点。

Inside the MagFlow Pro is a micro-pump that’s used to circulate coolant and draw heat away from components including the power bank’s Qi2.2-compatible wireless charging coil. You can see the coolant flowing through a (cyber) window on the front of the power bank. That system works alongside copper foil and a design that keeps charging components separated to help reduce heat buildup to prolong the life of the power bank’s battery, the life of your phone, and to help maintain up to 25W of power delivery. It should result in leaving the MagFlow Pro cooler to the touch after a long charging session.
MagFlow Pro 内部配备了一个微型泵，用于循环冷却液，并将热量从包括 Qi2.2 兼容无线充电线圈在内的组件中带走。你可以通过充电宝正面的“赛博”视窗看到冷却液的流动。该系统配合铜箔以及将充电组件分离的设计，有助于减少热量积聚，从而延长充电宝电池和手机的使用寿命，并帮助维持最高 25W 的无线充电功率。这意味着在长时间充电后，MagFlow Pro 的机身触感会更加凉爽。

In addition to charging devices wirelessly, the power bank has a built-in USB-C cable lanyard and a USB-C port that can both charge devices at up to 45W, but that’s also the maximum output of the MagFlow Pro, so connecting multiple devices will reduce charging rates.
除了无线充电功能外，这款充电宝还自带一根 USB-C 线缆挂绳，并配有一个 USB-C 接口，两者均支持最高 45W 的充电功率。不过，这也是 MagFlow Pro 的总输出上限，因此同时连接多个设备时，充电速度会相应降低。
