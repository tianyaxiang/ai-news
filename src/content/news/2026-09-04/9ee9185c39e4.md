# A Survey on Self-Improving Test-Time Intelligence: Feedback-Driven Adapting, Learning, and Scaling at Inference

**《关于自我提升测试时智能的综述：推理阶段的反馈驱动适应、学习与扩展》**

***

The ability of AI systems to improve their behavior during deployment is becoming increasingly important. As inference moves beyond the static execution of a fixed trained model, a growing body of work studies how models can refine their behavior on the fly by exploiting test-time information and additional computation.

人工智能系统在部署过程中提升自身行为的能力正变得日益重要。随着推理过程超越了对固定训练模型的静态执行，越来越多的研究开始探讨模型如何通过利用测试时信息和额外计算资源，在运行过程中实时优化其行为。

These developments have largely evolved along two directions: methods that modify the model's state using test-time signals, and methods that improve predictions through extra inference-time resources such as more sampling and tool use. However, these directions are often studied in separate communities with different terminology, making their connections harder to see.

这些进展主要沿着两个方向演进：一是利用测试时信号修改模型状态的方法，二是利用推理阶段的额外资源（如增加采样和工具使用）来改进预测的方法。然而，这些方向通常在不同的研究社区中被独立探讨，且术语各异，导致它们之间的联系难以被察觉。

In this survey, we present feedback-driven Test-Time Intelligence (TTI) as a unified perspective for understanding such deployment-time improvement. We use this view to relate test-time adaptation, test-time learning, and test-time scaling, highlighting both their distinctions and their growing overlap in hybrid systems.

在本综述中，我们提出了“反馈驱动的测试时智能”（Test-Time Intelligence, TTI），将其作为理解此类部署阶段改进的统一视角。我们利用这一视角将测试时适应（Test-Time Adaptation）、测试时学习（Test-Time Learning）和测试时扩展（Test-Time Scaling）联系起来，并强调了它们之间的区别以及在混合系统中日益增长的重叠性。

This unified framework helps connect previously fragmented ideas and provides a clearer conceptual foundation for studying inference-time self-improvement. We review major methodological paradigms, representative applications, and open challenges across vision, language, multimodal learning, generative models, robotics, and healthcare.

这一统一框架有助于连接此前碎片化的观点，并为研究推理阶段的自我提升提供了更清晰的概念基础。我们回顾了视觉、语言、多模态学习、生成模型、机器人技术和医疗保健等领域的主要方法论范式、代表性应用以及尚未解决的挑战。

Our goal is to provide a coherent foundation and research roadmap for the study of self-improving AI systems at test time.

我们的目标是为测试时自我提升 AI 系统的研究提供一个连贯的基础和研究路线图。
