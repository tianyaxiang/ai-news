# Beyond Textual Chain-of-Thought: A Survey on Action-Grounded Reasoning in Autonomous Driving
# 超越文本思维链：自动驾驶中基于动作的推理综述

**Abstract:** Chain-of-thought (CoT) reasoning powers generative models by eliciting intermediate steps before producing an answer. In autonomous driving, the answer is a continuous action. Thus its reasoning must share the same spatiotemporal structure as the physical world. This survey studies the resulting shift from textual CoT to action-grounded reasoning.

**摘要：** 思维链（CoT）推理通过在生成答案前引出中间步骤，为生成式模型提供了动力。在自动驾驶中，答案是连续的动作。因此，其推理过程必须与物理世界共享相同的时空结构。本综述研究了从文本思维链向基于动作的推理转变的过程。

Surveying 171 papers, including 130 method papers and 41 benchmarks, datasets, surveys, and analysis papers, we propose a representation-centered taxonomy that treats the form of the intermediate state as the organizing axis. We systematize the 130 methods into four categories: language-based, visual-spatial, latent-dynamic, and externalized reasoning, further divided into 13 subtypes tied to distinct regions of interests.

我们调研了 171 篇论文，包括 130 篇方法类论文以及 41 篇基准测试、数据集、综述和分析类论文，并提出了一种以表征为中心的分类法，将中间状态的形式作为组织轴。我们将这 130 种方法系统化为四大类：基于语言的推理、视觉空间推理、潜在动态推理和外化推理，并进一步细分为 13 个与不同关注区域相关的子类型。

Our synthesis shows that the open frontier of reasoning in driving agents lies in intermediate representations that can be grounded in the real world, coupled to real-time action, and verified under safety-critical systems.

我们的综合分析表明，驾驶智能体推理的未来前沿在于：开发能够扎根于现实世界、与实时动作耦合，并在安全关键系统中得到验证的中间表征。
