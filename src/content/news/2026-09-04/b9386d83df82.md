# Evidential Deep Learning for Multi-Modal Anti-UAV Detection
# 用于多模态反无人机检测的证据深度学习

**Abstract:** Anti-UAV systems increasingly fuse multiple sensors, yet their detection heads provide no per-modality reliability signal. This study evaluates whether evidential deep learning (EDL) heads, Dempster-Shafer (DS) evidence fusion, and uncertainty-driven temporal sensor gating improve anti-UAV detection through a controlled ablation on three benchmarks: thermal tracking (AntiUAV600), RGB-audio-RF classification (TRIDENT), and RGB-IR tracking (MM-UAV).

**摘要：** 反无人机系统正越来越多地融合多种传感器，但其检测头无法提供针对各模态的可靠性信号。本研究通过在三个基准测试（热成像跟踪 AntiUAV600、RGB-音频-射频分类 TRIDENT 以及 RGB-红外跟踪 MM-UAV）上进行受控消融实验，评估了证据深度学习（EDL）头、Dempster-Shafer（DS）证据融合以及基于不确定性的时间传感器门控机制是否能提升反无人机检测性能。

The EDL training objective improves accuracy over retrained sigmoid baselines (+5.9 percentage points in accuracy and a tripled tracker-on-absent rate in E1; +4.8 percentage points in classification accuracy in E2, surviving a clip-clustered bootstrap, p = 0.011) and ranks classification errors substantially better (entropy UAUC approximately 0.94 vs. 0.51).

EDL 训练目标在准确率上优于重训练的 Sigmoid 基准模型（在 E1 中准确率提升 5.9 个百分点，目标缺失时的跟踪率提升至三倍；在 E2 中分类准确率提升 4.8 个百分点，并通过了剪辑聚类引导检验，p = 0.011），并且在分类错误的排序能力上表现显著更好（熵 UAUC 约为 0.94 对比 0.51）。

The remaining components do not support their respective hypotheses. DS fusion does not outperform simple probability averaging. Dirichlet vacuity adds no ranking power beyond predictive entropy and inverts at the detection level, where extreme background imbalance causes it to encode class membership rather than error likelihood, a failure also observed for entropy and sigmoid confidence.

其余组件未能支持其各自的假设。DS 融合的表现并未优于简单的概率平均法。狄利克雷空虚度（Dirichlet vacuity）在预测熵之外并未增加任何排序能力，且在检测层面出现反转——由于极端的背景不平衡，它编码的是类别归属而非错误可能性；这种失效现象在熵和 Sigmoid 置信度中同样存在。

Temporal gating preserves accuracy only when nearly inactive and yields no realised latency saving on shared-backbone hardware. The benefit of evidential learning therefore arises primarily from its training objective rather than its uncertainty estimate; a crop-level control further localises the detection-level breakdown to anchor-level evaluation rather than the learned representation.

时间门控机制仅在几乎不工作时才能保持准确率，且在共享骨干网硬件上并未实现实际的延迟节省。因此，证据学习的优势主要源于其训练目标，而非其不确定性估计；裁剪层面的控制进一步将检测层面的失效定位到了锚点评估层面，而非学习到的特征表示层面。
