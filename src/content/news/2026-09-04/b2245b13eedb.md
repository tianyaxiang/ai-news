# Google Keeps AdX as Court Favors Behavioral Remedies Over an Ad Tech Breakup
# 谷歌保留 AdX：法院倾向于行为补救措施而非拆分广告技术业务

Google will not be forced to sell its AdX advertising exchange under the current remedies path in its U.S. ad tech antitrust litigation. U.S. District Judge Leonie M. Brinkema rejected the Department of Justice's proposed AdX divestiture while leaving the court's underlying monopoly findings intact.
根据美国广告技术反垄断诉讼目前的补救措施路径，谷歌将不会被迫出售其 AdX 广告交易平台。美国地方法院法官莱昂妮·M·布林克马（Leonie M. Brinkema）驳回了司法部提出的剥离 AdX 的要求，同时维持了法院此前关于谷歌垄断的认定。

The result is a significant shift in emphasis: rather than dismantling Google's ad tech stack, the court is pursuing behavioral remedies intended to make parts of the ecosystem more open and contestable. The decision matters because Google AdX, DFP and Ad Manager sit at important points in the process that connects publishers' advertising inventory with buyers. A forced sale could have reshaped ownership of that infrastructure.
这一结果标志着重点的重大转变：法院并未拆解谷歌的广告技术堆栈，而是寻求通过行为补救措施，旨在使生态系统的部分环节更加开放且具有竞争性。这一裁决意义重大，因为谷歌的 AdX、DFP 和广告管理器（Ad Manager）处于连接发布商广告库存与买家的关键环节。强制出售本可能重塑该基础设施的所有权结构。

The court instead appears set to focus on interoperability, data-sharing concepts for qualified competitors and technical oversight. The detailed remedy terms remained sealed for a short period, so the final practical obligations still need to be disclosed. The Justice Department's August 25, 2026 order regarding issues raised at the July status conference is the primary public court document in this remedies phase. It provides context for a process that follows the court's 2025 monopoly findings and now centers on how competition can be improved without a structural breakup.
法院目前似乎倾向于关注互操作性、针对合格竞争对手的数据共享概念以及技术监管。详细的补救条款在短期内仍处于保密状态，因此最终的实际义务仍有待披露。司法部于 2026 年 8 月 25 日发布的关于 7 月份状态会议所提问题的命令，是此补救阶段主要的公开法庭文件。它为后续流程提供了背景，该流程紧随法院 2025 年的垄断认定，目前的核心在于如何在不进行结构性拆分的情况下改善竞争。

### What the court's remedies direction changes
### 法院的补救方向有何改变

The plaintiffs had sought a structural remedy that would require Google to divest AdX. That approach would have separated a central exchange from Google's wider publisher ad-serving and ad-management operations. Judge Brinkema rejected that proposal, according to the court's preview filing and subsequent reporting, and did not order a breakup of the AdX, DFP and Ad Manager bundle.
原告曾寻求一种结构性补救措施，要求谷歌剥离 AdX。该方案本会将这一核心交易平台从谷歌更广泛的发布商广告投放和广告管理业务中分离出来。根据法院的预告文件及后续报道，布林克马法官驳回了该提议，并未下令拆分 AdX、DFP 和广告管理器这一组合。

Instead, the remedies direction accepts substantial behavioral measures proposed in the litigation. These measures are designed to increase competitive pressure while Google continues operating its ad tech products. The final scope matters: a broad interoperability obligation can alter how rival exchanges, publisher tools and bidders interact, while a narrow obligation may produce more limited change.
相反，补救方向采纳了诉讼中提出的实质性行为措施。这些措施旨在谷歌继续运营其广告技术产品的同时，增加竞争压力。最终的范围至关重要：广泛的互操作性义务可以改变竞争对手的交易平台、发布商工具和竞价方之间的互动方式，而狭窄的义务可能只会产生有限的改变。

### Remedies approach
### 补救措施对比

| Feature | AdX divestiture sought by plaintiffs | Court's behavioral remedies direction |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **功能** | **原告寻求的 AdX 剥离** | **法院的行为补救方向** |
| Google's ownership of AdX | AdX would have been sold or separated | Google retains AdX |
| **谷歌对 AdX 的所有权** | AdX 将被出售或分离 | 谷歌保留 AdX |
| Ad tech stack | Structural breakup of AdX, DFP and Ad Manager was sought | No breakup of the bundle |
| **广告技术堆栈** | 寻求对 AdX、DFP 和广告管理器进行结构性拆分 | 不拆分该组合 |
| Competitive mechanism | Changed ownership and control | Openness, data-sharing concepts and interoperability measures |
| **竞争机制** | 改变所有权和控制权 | 开放性、数据共享概念及互操作性措施 |
| Oversight | Not the central remedy described | A Remedies Technical Committee is part of the remedies framework |
| **监管** | 非核心补救措施 | 补救技术委员会是补救框架的一部分 |

### What behavioral remedies could involve
### 行为补救措施可能涉及的内容

Based on the materials currently described, the framework can include several connected elements:
根据目前描述的材料，该框架可能包含几个相互关联的要素：

*   **More open ad tech interfaces**, which could make it easier for eligible services to interact with parts of Google's ad technology.
    **更开放的广告技术接口**，这可能使符合条件的第三方服务更容易与谷歌广告技术的部分环节进行交互。
*   **Data-sharing concepts for qualified competitors**, intended to reduce informational barriers that can limit effective competition.
    **针对合格竞争对手的数据共享概念**，旨在减少可能限制有效竞争的信息壁垒。
*   **Interoperability measures**, which could help rival bidders, exchanges and publisher technology participate more readily.
    **互操作性措施**，这有助于竞争对手的竞价方、交易平台和发布商技术更顺畅地参与其中。
*   **A Remedies Technical Committee**, providing a technical governance mechanism around the court-approved framework.
    **补救技术委员会**，围绕法院批准的框架提供技术治理机制。

These are not equivalent to a sale. Google remains the owner and operator of AdX, and the consequences will depend on the final terms, qualification requirements, implementation details and enforcement. It would be premature to assume that any particular interface, dataset or commercial term will become available until the sealed materials are released and the court's requirements are clear.
这些措施不等同于出售。谷歌仍是 AdX 的所有者和运营商，其后果将取决于最终条款、资格要求、实施细节和执行力度。在保密材料发布且法院要求明确之前，断定任何特定的接口、数据集或商业条款将会开放还为时过早。

### Why the outcome matters for advertisers and publishers
### 这一结果对广告主和发布商为何重要

The ruling preserves continuity for organizations that already rely on Google's ad tech infrastructure. There is no court-ordered requirement, based on the currently reported remedies direction, for publishers or advertisers to move away from Google's tools because AdX has changed hands. At the same time, the goal of behavioral remedies is to improve the ability of rivals to compete with Google's integrated stack.
该裁决为已经依赖谷歌广告技术基础设施的机构保持了连续性。根据目前报道的补救方向，法院并未要求发布商或广告主因 AdX 易手而放弃使用谷歌的工具。与此同时，行为补救措施的目标是提高竞争对手与谷歌集成堆栈竞争的能力。

If the resulting obligations are implemented effectively, publishers may have more practical options for working with competing exchanges and ad-serving services. Advertisers and agencies could also see a market in which alternative routes to inventory and bidding have a better chance to compete. That is a potential market effect, not a guaranteed outcome. Advertising prices are influenced by many factors, including campaign demand, available inventory, targeting choices, auction design and the services a buyer or publisher uses. The ruling does not set ad prices or promise lower costs. Its significance lies in whether the remedies reduce barriers that have made competition harder in the ad tech markets at issue.
如果由此产生的义务得到有效执行，发布商在与竞争对手的交易平台和广告投放服务合作时，可能会有更多实际选择。广告主和代理商也可能看到一个市场，其中通往库存和竞价的替代路径有更好的竞争机会。这是一种潜在的市场效应，而非必然结果。广告价格受多种因素影响，包括广告活动需求、可用库存、定向选择、拍卖设计以及买方或发布商使用的服务。该裁决并未设定广告价格，也未承诺降低成本。其意义在于这些补救措施是否能降低在相关广告技术市场中阻碍竞争的壁垒。

### Practical steps for businesses using digital advertising
### 使用数字广告的企业应采取的实际步骤

For smaller advertisers, publishers and ad tech providers, the immediate task is observation rather than a rushed platform change. Google retains its stack, and the detailed obligations have yet to be publicly disclosed. Still, the remedies process is a reason to understand current dependencies and integration options.
对于较小的广告主、发布商和广告技术提供商而言，当务之急是观察，而不是匆忙更换平台。谷歌保留了其堆栈，且详细义务尚未公开披露。尽管如此，补救流程是一个了解当前依赖关系和集成选项的契机。

Businesses can prepare by documenting which platforms handle their ad serving, inventory management, bidding and reporting. Publishers can also assess whether they use alternative demand sources or exchanges, while advertisers can review how much reporting and buying access depends on a single provider. Those baseline answers will make it easier to judge new opportunities if court-ordered interfaces or data access change the available choices.
企业可以通过记录哪些平台处理其广告投放、库存管理、竞价和报告来做好准备。发布商还可以评估是否使用了替代的需求来源或交易平台，而广告主则可以审查其报告和购买权限在多大程度上依赖于单一提供商。如果法院强制要求的接口或数据访问改变了现有选择，这些基础答案将使企业更容易判断新的机会。

For technology providers, the key question is whether the final framework creates usable paths to integrate with publishers, advertisers or Google's systems. An obligation is commercially meaningful only if it is technically accessible, clearly governed and capable of supporting reliable day-to-day workflows.
对于技术提供商而言，关键问题在于最终框架是否创造了与发布商、广告主或谷歌系统集成的可行路径。只有在技术上可访问、治理清晰且能够支持可靠的日常工作流程时，一项义务才具有商业意义。
