# HybridInfer: Thermal-Aware Reinforcement-Learning Tier Routing for On-Device, Edge, and Cloud LLM Inference
# HybridInfer：面向端侧、边缘侧与云端大模型推理的感知热量强化学习分层路由

**Abstract:** On-device inference with small language models keeps user data local, works offline, and incurs no per-query cost, so the on-device tier is preferred when it is adequate. It is thermally constrained, however, and I find the constraint is sharper than a slowdown: on a flagship Snapdragon device, sustained on-device generation destabilizes the GPU inference runtime, which crashes or silently wedges after a few consecutive queries.

**摘要：** 使用小型语言模型进行端侧推理能够确保用户数据本地化、支持离线运行且无需单次查询成本，因此在满足需求的情况下，端侧推理是首选方案。然而，端侧推理受到热量限制，且我发现这种限制不仅仅表现为运行速度变慢：在旗舰级骁龙设备上，持续的端侧生成任务会使 GPU 推理运行时变得不稳定，导致其在连续几次查询后崩溃或静默卡死。

The failure lies in the current toolchain (OpenCL kernel compilation and long-prompt prefill on the mobile GPU), recurs even when the device is cool, and is worst for long generations. Multi-tier routers across on-device, edge, and cloud models can relieve this pressure, but existing routers are thermal-blind and typically evaluated in simulation or on non-mobile hardware.

这种故障源于当前的工具链（移动 GPU 上的 OpenCL 内核编译和长提示词预填充），即使在设备处于低温状态下也会反复出现，且在长文本生成任务中表现最为严重。跨端侧、边缘侧和云端模型的多层路由器可以缓解这一压力，但现有的路由器缺乏热量感知能力，且通常仅在模拟环境或非移动硬件上进行评估。

I present HybridInfer, a thermal-aware reinforcement-learning router for a three-tier hierarchy (on-device Llama 3.2 3B, edge Llama 3.1 8B with retrieval, cloud GPT-4o) that uses the phone's thermal headroom and a query-complexity estimate as state and selects a tier by an offline-trained Q-learning policy. Its reward trades quality against latency, cost, and a thermal penalty, plus a locality bonus crediting on-device execution.

我提出了 HybridInfer，这是一种针对三层架构（端侧 Llama 3.2 3B、带检索功能的边缘侧 Llama 3.1 8B、云端 GPT-4o）的感知热量强化学习路由器。它利用手机的热余量和查询复杂度估计作为状态，并通过离线训练的 Q-learning 策略来选择推理层级。其奖励机制在质量、延迟、成本和热量惩罚之间进行权衡，并增加了对端侧执行的本地化奖励。

I show this bonus is a precondition for thermal-aware routing: without it the optimal policy offloads every query. On a real Android benchmark of 210 prompts, the learned router attains significantly higher quality than two hand-tuned heuristics (paired Wilcoxon, p < 0.02) at the lowest cost of any adaptive condition. Always-on-device conditions match per-query quality on servable queries but are three to six times slower and fail on long queries, so routing wins on latency, reliability, and coverage rather than quality.

我证明了这种本地化奖励是实现感知热量路由的前提：如果没有它，最优策略会将所有查询卸载到云端。在包含 210 个提示词的真实 Android 基准测试中，该学习型路由器在所有自适应方案中以最低成本实现了显著高于两种人工调优启发式算法的质量（配对 Wilcoxon 检验，p < 0.02）。虽然“始终端侧运行”方案在可处理的查询上能达到同等的单次查询质量，但其速度慢了三到六倍，且在长查询任务中会失败。因此，路由方案在延迟、可靠性和覆盖范围方面更具优势，而非单纯追求质量。

To my knowledge this is the first use of on-device thermal headroom to select among LLM inference tiers of differing capability on real hardware.

据我所知，这是首次在真实硬件上利用端侧热余量来选择不同能力的大模型推理层级。
