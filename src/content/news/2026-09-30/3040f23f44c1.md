# Why Clipping Matters in AdaGrad? Toward a High-Probability Theory under Generalized Smoothness
# 为什么梯度裁剪对 AdaGrad 至关重要？迈向广义平滑性下的高概率理论

**Abstract:** We analyze the original same-step coordinate-wise AdaGrad under generalized smoothness and heavy-tailed noise with bounded variance. In this setting, local curvature may grow sub-quadratically with the gradient norm, and stochastic gradients are assumed to have only bounded conditional second moments.

**摘要：** 我们在广义平滑性和具有有界方差的重尾噪声条件下，分析了原始的同步坐标级 AdaGrad 算法。在此设定下，局部曲率可能随梯度范数呈次二次增长，且假设随机梯度仅具有有界的条件二阶矩。

We show that unclipped AdaGrad can become \emph{anisotropically miscalibrated}: under heavy-tailed noise, the adaptive denominator can learn the geometry of rare noise shocks rather than the local curvature of the objective, leading to a persistent directional distortion that blocks finite-horizon Euclidean progress.

我们证明了未经过裁剪的 AdaGrad 可能会出现“各向异性校准失调”（anisotropically miscalibrated）：在重尾噪声下，自适应分母可能会学习到罕见噪声冲击的几何结构，而非目标函数的局部曲率，从而导致持续的方向性畸变，阻碍了有限时间内的欧几里得空间收敛。

We then prove that clipping repairs this failure mode. Our main result is a finite-horizon high-probability guarantee for the original non-lagged AdaGrad update, yielding $\frac1T\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\|\nabla f(x_t)\|^2=\mathcal{O}\left(\frac{d\big(\sqrt{\log T} + \log \frac{1}{\delta}\big)}{\sqrt{T}}\right),$ and hence $\widetilde{\mathcal O}(\varepsilon^{-2})$ complexity.

随后，我们证明了梯度裁剪能够修复这一失效模式。我们的主要结果是针对原始非滞后 AdaGrad 更新的有限时间高概率保证，其收敛速度为 $\frac1T\sum_{t=0}^{T-1}\|\nabla f(x_t)\|^2=\mathcal{O}\left(\frac{d\big(\sqrt{\log T} + \log \frac{1}{\delta}\big)}{\sqrt{T}}\right)$，从而实现了 $\widetilde{\mathcal O}(\varepsilon^{-2})$ 的复杂度。

This shows that, for AdaGrad under heavy-tailed noise, clipping is a structural stabilizer of the adaptive geometry rather than merely a robustness heuristic.

这表明，对于重尾噪声下的 AdaGrad 而言，梯度裁剪不仅是一种鲁棒性启发式方法，更是自适应几何结构的结构性稳定器。
