# More Ways to Disrupt: New 2026 Side Events from KOTRA, WayFounder, Enterprise Ireland, SafetyWing + Descope
# 更多颠覆方式：KOTRA、WayFounder、Enterprise Ireland、SafetyWing 和 Descope 带来的 2026 年全新周边活动

TechCrunch Disrupt is where the tech community comes together to build, debate, and connect. And during Disrupt Week, that conversation doesn’t stop when the doors to Moscone West close. Our Side Events program brings founders, investors, operators, and tech leaders together across San Francisco for everything from intimate roundtables and founder gatherings to happy hours, dinners, workshops, and community meetups. Whether you’re looking to make a few new connections, find your next collaborator, go deep on a specific industry, or just get out of conference mode for a minute, there’s a lot happening beyond the show floor. Explore the 2026 Side Events, and find your people.

TechCrunch Disrupt 是科技界汇聚一堂、共同建设、辩论和交流的平台。在 Disrupt 周期间，当 Moscone West 会展中心的大门关闭时，对话并未停止。我们的周边活动项目将旧金山各地的创始人、投资者、运营者和科技领袖汇聚在一起，活动形式多样，包括私密圆桌会议、创始人聚会、欢乐时光、晚宴、研讨会和社区见面会等。无论你是想建立新的人脉、寻找下一个合作伙伴、深入了解某个特定行业，还是仅仅想暂时从会议模式中抽离出来，展厅之外都有丰富多彩的活动。快来探索 2026 年的周边活动，找到志同道合的伙伴吧。
