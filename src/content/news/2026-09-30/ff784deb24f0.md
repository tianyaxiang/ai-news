### Best Smartwatch for iPhone Users: Apple Watch Series 12
### iPhone用户的最佳智能手表：Apple Watch Series 12

While not a major upgrade from its predecessor, the Apple Watch Series 12 is the best everyday smartwatch for most iPhone users.
虽然相比前代产品没有重大升级，但 Apple Watch Series 12 依然是大多数 iPhone 用户日常佩戴的最佳智能手表。

**Why WIRED recommends:** The Series 12’s health capabilities are more comprehensive than the Series 11, thanks to a new Health Sensing System and faster S11 chip that increases the breadth, accuracy, and frequency of several health metrics. Most notably, the Series 12 can now track heart rate as often as every five seconds and heart rate variability (HRV) every five minutes or so. With its new Readiness score, the Series 12 also makes a stronger case as a fitness tracker. The streamlined watchOS 27 makes everyday navigation easier. Fast charging gets a boost, too, with 15 minutes offering up to 12 hours of battery life.
**WIRED 推荐理由：** 得益于全新的健康传感系统和更快的 S11 芯片，Series 12 的健康功能比 Series 11 更全面，提升了多项健康指标的广度、准确性和频率。最值得注意的是，Series 12 现在可以每五秒追踪一次心率，每五分钟左右追踪一次心率变异性 (HRV)。凭借全新的“准备度评分”(Readiness score)，Series 12 作为健身追踪器的表现也更具说服力。精简的 watchOS 27 让日常操作更加便捷。快速充电功能也得到了增强，充电 15 分钟即可提供长达 12 小时的续航。

**The trade-offs:** Battery life is still rated at just 24 hours, so you’ll need to charge it daily. Many of watchOS 27’s features are also coming to older Apple Watches. The new Audio Intelligence features raise some privacy questions around the watch’s use of its microphone.
**不足之处：** 电池续航时间仍仅为 24 小时，因此你需要每天充电。watchOS 27 的许多功能也会下放到旧款 Apple Watch 上。全新的音频智能功能引发了一些关于手表麦克风使用方面的隐私担忧。

### Best Smartwatch for Android Users: Google Pixel Watch 5
### Android用户的最佳智能手表：Google Pixel Watch 5

The Google Pixel Watch 5 is Google’s best smartwatch, but many of the software upgrades have rolled out to the Pixel Watch 4. So, if you catch last year’s model significantly discounted, it’s worth considering.
Google Pixel Watch 5 是 Google 目前最好的智能手表，但许多软件升级也已推送至 Pixel Watch 4。因此，如果你能以大幅折扣买到去年的型号，它也值得考虑。

**Why WIRED recommends:** It’s the most polished Wear OS smartwatch, with a sleek domed design, slick performance, and repairable build. The screen gets bright, the health and fitness features are impeccably accurate, and there’s even support for satellite messaging during emergencies. The dual-band GPS is more accurate than ever and now accounts for atmospheric interference and multipath errors. Fast charging delivers 15 hours in just 15 minutes.
**WIRED 推荐理由：** 这是目前最精致的 Wear OS 智能手表，拥有时尚的圆顶设计、流畅的性能和可维修的结构。屏幕亮度很高，健康和健身功能极其精准，甚至支持紧急情况下的卫星通讯。双频 GPS 比以往任何时候都更准确，现在还能考虑到大气干扰和多径误差。快速充电 15 分钟即可使用 15 小时。

**The trade-offs:** While you can now use generative AI to create a custom watch face, the feature is somewhat limited. There are also a few features exclusive to Pixel phones, such as controlling the phone’s camera, AI-powered smart replies, and call screening. Finally, Google still hasn’t introduced additional charging options or 3-in-1 or 2-in-1 docks for its Pixel portfolio.
**不足之处：** 虽然你现在可以使用生成式 AI 创建自定义表盘，但该功能仍有一定局限性。此外，还有一些功能是 Pixel 手机独有的，例如控制手机摄像头、AI 智能回复和通话筛选。最后，Google 尚未为其 Pixel 产品组合推出额外的充电选项或三合一/二合一充电底座。
