# Cosine Similarity Is Not Evidence: Measuring the Noise Floor of Interpretability Transfer Under Quantization
# 余弦相似度并非证据：量化下可解释性迁移的噪声基准测量

**Abstract:** A statistic reported without the quantity needed to interpret it is not evidence. We develop that thesis for a concrete practice in AI safety. Interpretability artifacts are calibrated on full-precision weights, deployed on quantized ones, and certified as surviving the change by scale-invariant statistics (cosine similarity, correlation, AUROC) that are reported without their noise floor.

**摘要：** 如果一个统计数据在报告时没有提供解释它所需的必要量值，那么它就不能作为证据。我们针对人工智能安全领域的一项具体实践展开了这一论点。目前，可解释性工件（Interpretability artifacts）通常在全精度权重上进行校准，在量化权重上进行部署，并通过尺度不变统计量（如余弦相似度、相关性、AUROC）来证明其在变化后依然有效，但这些统计量在报告时往往忽略了其噪声基准（noise floor）。

For the difference-in-means direction estimator, the split-half floor is governed by one dimensionless number, $\kappa = n\rho^2/d$. The closed form $\mathbb{E}[\cos] \approx (1+4/\kappa)^{-1}$ is classical; the missing input is the class separation $\rho$, which we measure on real activations; no compression-transfer study we know of reports it.

对于均值差方向估计器，其分半（split-half）噪声基准由一个无量纲数 $\kappa = n\rho^2/d$ 决定。其闭式解 $\mathbb{E}[\cos] \approx (1+4/\kappa)^{-1}$ 是经典结论；其中缺失的输入是类间分离度 $\rho$，我们通过真实激活值对其进行了测量；据我们所知，目前没有任何压缩迁移研究报告过这一指标。

On Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct, $\rho = 33$--$61$ across depth, so two independent runs of the estimator agree to $0.978$--$0.994$ by sampling alone. A published cosine of $0.996$ between full-precision and quantized refusal directions therefore cannot be read as preservation without the $n$ it was computed at, which is not reported.

在 Qwen2.5-1.5B-Instruct 模型上，不同深度的 $\rho$ 值在 $33$ 到 $61$ 之间，这意味着仅通过采样，两次独立的估计器运行结果就能达到 $0.978$ 到 $0.994$ 的一致性。因此，如果报告中未说明计算时所用的样本量 $n$，那么全精度与量化拒绝方向之间 $0.996$ 的余弦相似度就不能被解读为“保持不变”。

Where $n$ is known, we judge each low-bit cosine against the split-half null measured within that quantized model, because a full-precision null assumes the low-bit estimator has the same variance. That assumption is exactly what a null exists to test. The result is plain: at INT4 the direction rotated, and the deficit exceeds the estimator's own noise. At INT8 we detect no movement, which is not an equivalence claim.

在已知 $n$ 的情况下，我们将每个低比特余弦值与在该量化模型内部测得的分半零假设（null）进行对比，因为全精度零假设预设了低比特估计器具有相同的方差，而这一假设正是零假设所要检验的对象。结果显而易见：在 INT4 量化下，方向发生了旋转，且偏差超过了估计器自身的噪声。在 INT8 下我们未检测到偏移，但这并不等同于两者完全等价。

We also show that a scale-invariant statistic cannot distinguish translation from attenuation of a transferred decision variable, although the two call for opposite remedies. We close with reporting recommendations that cost one forward pass. Code, data, and a one-cell reproduction are released at this https URL.

我们还证明了尺度不变统计量无法区分迁移决策变量的平移（translation）与衰减（attenuation），尽管这两种情况需要截然不同的补救措施。最后，我们提出了仅需一次前向传播成本的报告建议。代码、数据及单单元复现脚本已发布于此链接。
