# Razer’s low-latency wireless gaming keyboard is almost half off
# 雷蛇（Razer）低延迟无线游戏键盘近乎半价

Woot has the Razer DeathStalker V2 Pro TKL on sale for $130, a significant discount from its usual $219.99 price point. This keyboard is built for competitive gaming, with a low latency 2.4GHz wireless connection, and low-profile linear optical switches for faster activations that require a lighter touch than regular mechanical switches. The TKL layout cuts the numpad, but affords you more desk space for your mouse.

Woot 目前正在促销 Razer DeathStalker V2 Pro TKL 键盘，售价为 130 美元，较其 219.99 美元的常规价格有大幅优惠。这款键盘专为竞技游戏打造，配备低延迟的 2.4GHz 无线连接，并采用短键程线性光学轴体，触发速度更快，且比普通机械轴体更轻盈。TKL（无数字小键盘）布局省去了数字键区，为鼠标留出了更多的桌面空间。

Razer claims the battery will last up to 200 hours with the RGB lighting off, or up to 50 hours with the lights at half brightness. If you’re interested in staying wired, or using the keyboard wirelessly with other devices, it has both USB-C and Bluetooth connectivity. You can also enable Snap Tap (Razer’s term for Simultaneous Opposing Cardinal Directions, or SOCD), which lets you quickly swap between two keys for strafing in first person shooters, for example, although it’s worth noting that Valve has banned the tech in Counter-Strike 2.

雷蛇官方称，在关闭 RGB 灯效的情况下，电池续航可达 200 小时；若将灯光亮度调至一半，续航则为 50 小时。如果你倾向于有线连接，或者需要将键盘无线连接到其他设备，它同时支持 USB-C 和蓝牙连接。你还可以开启 Snap Tap 功能（雷蛇对“同时反向指令”或 SOCD 的称呼），该功能允许你在第一人称射击游戏中快速切换两个按键以实现侧移，不过值得注意的是，Valve 已在《反恐精英 2》（Counter-Strike 2）中禁用了该技术。
