# Fixed Points Without Fixed Diffusion: Implicit Neural Sheaves for Convergent Test-Time Computation
# 无需固定扩散的定点：用于收敛测试时计算的隐式神经层（Implicit Neural Sheaves）

**Abstract:** Implicit Graph Neural Networks (IGNNs) define node representations as fixed points of message-passing operators, enabling effectively infinite-depth propagation, iteration-independent parameterization, and flexible test-time computation. Yet these benefits depend on the equilibrium being unique and attainable by fixed-point iteration. Existing constructions often impose constraints on recurrent updates to obtain these guarantees, limiting the transformations available at equilibrium. This raises a central question: can IGNNs gain expressiveness through richer, edge-dependent transformations while retaining the inherent strengths of their equilibrium formulation?

**摘要：** 隐式图神经网络（IGNNs）将节点表示定义为消息传递算子的定点，从而实现了有效的无限深度传播、与迭代次数无关的参数化以及灵活的测试时计算。然而，这些优势依赖于平衡点的唯一性以及通过定点迭代的可达性。现有的构建方法通常会对循环更新施加约束以获得这些保证，从而限制了平衡状态下可用的变换。这提出了一个核心问题：IGNNs 能否在保留其平衡公式固有优势的同时，通过更丰富、依赖于边的变换来获得更强的表达能力？

We introduce SheafDEQ, a subhomogeneous deep-equilibrium architecture with adaptive neural-sheaf propagation. Its learned, matrix-valued sheaf restriction maps can align, mix, or reverse neighbouring representations. Under mild regularity conditions, we prove that SheafDEQ admits a unique equilibrium reached globally by fixed-point iteration from any positive initialization. Contractivity further guarantees convergence under bounded communication staleness.

我们引入了 SheafDEQ，这是一种具有自适应神经层（neural-sheaf）传播的次齐次深度平衡架构。其学习到的矩阵值层限制映射（sheaf restriction maps）可以对相邻表示进行对齐、混合或反转。在温和的正则性条件下，我们证明了 SheafDEQ 存在一个唯一平衡点，且从任何正初始化出发，通过定点迭代均可全局达到该平衡点。收缩性进一步保证了在有限通信延迟下的收敛性。

We evaluate SheafDEQ on distributed-inference tasks requiring repeated nonlocal aggregation and on community detection whose rewiring increasingly favours cross-community interactions. SheafDEQ improves over fixed-propagation implicit baselines on Sums, MNIST Terrain, and Coordinates, and on community detection as connectivity becomes increasingly heterophilic. Continued-iteration diagnostics show decreasing residuals and low prediction sensitivity after 100 iterations for initialization scales from 0.001 to 10, while delayed-update experiments show low sensitivity to bounded communication staleness.

我们在需要重复非局部聚合的分布式推理任务以及重连线日益偏向跨社区交互的社区检测任务上评估了 SheafDEQ。在 Sums、MNIST Terrain 和 Coordinates 数据集上，以及随着连接性变得越来越异质（heterophilic）的社区检测任务中，SheafDEQ 的表现均优于固定传播的隐式基准模型。持续迭代诊断显示，在 0.001 到 10 的初始化尺度下，经过 100 次迭代后残差减小且预测敏感度较低；同时，延迟更新实验表明该模型对有限的通信延迟具有较低的敏感度。
