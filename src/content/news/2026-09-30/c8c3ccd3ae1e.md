# I Found National Coffee Day Deals Worth Making Coffee at Home For (2026)
# 我找到了值得让你在家自制咖啡的“全国咖啡日”优惠（2026年）

For some reason, September 29 is a popular date for holidays. Happy Goose Day, Poisoned Blackberries Day, and Attend Your Grandchild’s Birthday Day to those who celebrate. But I personally will be celebrating National Coffee Day. (Not to be confused with International Coffee Day, which is the day after tomorrow.)
不知为何，9月29日是一个热门的节日日期。祝庆祝“鹅节”（Goose Day）、“毒黑莓日”（Poisoned Blackberries Day）和“参加孙辈生日日”（Attend Your Grandchild’s Birthday Day）的朋友们节日快乐。但我个人将庆祝“全国咖啡日”。（别把它和后天的“国际咖啡日”搞混了。）

Created by the National Coffee Association around 2009, National Coffee Day used to be known for free and discounted coffee from places like Dunkin’ and Panera, but most big chains this year seem to be going with new releases rather than discounts. (See: Krispy Kreme’s coffee-flavored donuts and Starbucks’ new aerocano.) If you’re looking for deals, you might have to make your own coffee at home this year. Luckily, we’ve found some legitimately good sales—as good or even better than those seen during recent shopping holidays—to make this an occasion worth raising a mug to.
“全国咖啡日”由美国全国咖啡协会（National Coffee Association）于2009年左右设立，过去以Dunkin’和Panera等连锁店提供的免费或折扣咖啡而闻名。但今年，大多数大型连锁店似乎更倾向于推出新品而非折扣。（例如：Krispy Kreme的咖啡味甜甜圈和星巴克的新品“气泡美式”）。如果你在寻找优惠，今年可能得选择在家自制咖啡了。幸运的是，我们发现了一些真正给力的促销活动——其力度甚至比近期的购物节还要好——足以让你举杯庆祝。
