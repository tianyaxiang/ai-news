# Xbox’s Mythic Achievements are here and they’re just like PlayStation Platinum trophies
# Xbox 的“神话成就”（Mythic Achievements）现已推出，功能类似于 PlayStation 的白金奖杯

Microsoft is officially announcing its new Xbox Mythic Achievements today, and they’re already available for Xbox Insiders to test. Mythic Achievements work a lot like Sony’s PlayStation Platinum trophies and are unlocked once you’ve earned every original achievement for a game.
微软今日正式宣布推出全新的 Xbox“神话成就”，目前已面向 Xbox Insider 测试人员开放。神话成就的运作方式与索尼 PlayStation 的白金奖杯非常相似，只要你解锁了一款游戏的所有原始成就，即可获得该神话成就。

“Mythic Achievements have already been added to eligible games you completed in the past, including Xbox 360 titles,” says the Xbox team. “The next time you visit your profile, you may discover that some of your favorite games have already reached Mythic status.”
Xbox 团队表示：“神话成就已经添加到了你过去完成的符合条件的游戏中，包括 Xbox 360 游戏。当你下次访问个人资料时，可能会发现你最喜欢的一些游戏已经达到了‘神话’状态。”

Xbox profiles will soon include a new gaming tab that lets you track Gamerscore milestones and Mythic Achievements. The main Achievements list is also being updated to make it easier to track Mythic status. “A new progress bar at the top of each game’s achievement list shows how close you are to Mythic status, with an option to focus on the original achievements you still need,” says team Xbox.
Xbox 个人资料页面即将新增一个游戏标签页，让你能够追踪玩家分数（Gamerscore）里程碑和神话成就。主要的成就列表也正在更新，以便更轻松地追踪神话状态。Xbox 团队表示：“每个游戏成就列表顶部新增的进度条会显示你距离神话状态还有多远，并提供了一个选项，让你专注于尚未完成的原始成就。”

Mythic Achievements leaked as part of an Xbox PC app last week, complete with a unique unlock sound. Mythic Achievements are now in testing just weeks after Xbox CEO Asha Sharma revealed that Xbox would be getting a platinum trophy equivalent later this year.
上周，神话成就作为 Xbox PC 应用程序的一部分被泄露，并配有独特的解锁音效。在 Xbox 首席执行官 Asha Sharma 透露 Xbox 将在今年晚些时候推出相当于白金奖杯的功能仅几周后，神话成就现已进入测试阶段。
