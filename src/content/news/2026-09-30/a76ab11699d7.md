# Elon Musk’s AI-powered Grokipedia is updating again
# 埃隆·马斯克的 AI 驱动版“Grokipedia”再次更新

After a months-long pause, Grokipedia is showing some activity once more.
在经历了数月的沉寂后，Grokipedia 再次展现出活跃迹象。

Grokipedia, the AI-powered online encyclopedia from SpaceXAI, appears to be updating articles once again after a months-long pause. In August, Lawfare reported that articles on Grokipedia hadn’t reviewed edits since April, but the platform’s live updates site is now showing various recent changes to pages — though as I write this, many are just a note that says “recheck all references and sources.”
由 SpaceXAI 开发的 AI 在线百科全书 Grokipedia 在沉寂数月后，似乎又开始更新文章了。今年 8 月，《Lawfare》曾报道称 Grokipedia 自 4 月以来就未再审核过任何编辑内容，但该平台的实时更新页面目前显示，许多页面近期已发生变动——尽管在我撰写本文时，其中许多变动仅是一条“重新检查所有参考资料和来源”的备注。

The site seems to have become active again relatively recently. The page for President Barack Obama has a note that says it was “Fact-checked by Grok” two days ago, and the page for Elon Musk was fact-checked while I was writing this, within the last hour. However, the page for Honolulu, which I clicked on from Obama’s page, was last fact-checked 7 months ago, so it appears that not every page has a recent scan.
该网站似乎是在近期才恢复活跃的。巴拉克·奥巴马（Barack Obama）总统的页面显示其在两天前经过了“Grok 事实核查”，而埃隆·马斯克（Elon Musk）的页面则是在我撰写本文时（即过去一小时内）完成核查的。不过，我从奥巴马页面点击进入的檀香山（Honolulu）页面，其最后一次事实核查还是在 7 个月前，因此看来并非所有页面都进行了近期扫描。

While writing this article, I suggested an update to the Gears of War: E-Day page to note the game’s October 6th release date, and Grokipedia accepted the suggestion and now lists the date. I also requested an article about Meta’s Muse chatbot, and that request is “In Review.”
在撰写本文时，我建议更新《战争机器：E-Day》（Gears of War: E-Day）页面，以标注该游戏 10 月 6 日的发布日期，Grokipedia 采纳了这一建议，目前已列出了该日期。我还提交了一篇关于 Meta 公司 Muse 聊天机器人的文章请求，该请求目前处于“审核中”状态。

SpaceXAI didn’t immediately reply to a request for comment, and Elon Musk still hasn’t posted on X about Grokipedia since February. (Musk claimed about a year ago that Grokipedia would be “a massive improvement over Wikipedia.”) However, Benji Taylor, the head of design at X and SpaceXAI, posted last week about Grokipedia, saying that “We haven’t forgotten about Grokipedia” and that “v0.2 will be better than ever” (even though v0.2 technically launched in November).
SpaceXAI 未立即回复置评请求，而埃隆·马斯克自 2 月以来也未在 X 上发布过关于 Grokipedia 的内容。（马斯克大约一年前曾声称，Grokipedia 将会是“对维基百科的一次巨大改进”。）不过，X 和 SpaceXAI 的设计主管本吉·泰勒（Benji Taylor）上周发布了关于 Grokipedia 的消息，称“我们没有忘记 Grokipedia”，并表示“v0.2 版本将会比以往任何时候都更好”（尽管 v0.2 版本在技术上已于去年 11 月发布）。
