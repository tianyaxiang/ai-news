# ENAS: An Efficient Hardware-Aware Neural Architecture Search Framework for TinyML on Resource-Constrained Microcontrollers
# ENAS：一种面向资源受限微控制器 TinyML 的高效硬件感知神经架构搜索框架

We present **ENAS**, a hardware-aware Neural Architecture Search (NAS) framework that combines a static feasibility check, a cell-based search space supporting standard, depthwise-separable, and bottleneck blocks with optional skip connections, and a three-stage hybrid search strategy (random $\rightarrow$ top-$K$ $\rightarrow$ mutation) with persistent cross-run caching.

我们提出了 **ENAS**，这是一个硬件感知的神经架构搜索（NAS）框架。它结合了静态可行性检查、支持标准卷积、深度可分离卷积及带有可选跳跃连接的瓶颈模块的单元搜索空间，以及一种包含跨运行持久化缓存的三阶段混合搜索策略（随机 $\rightarrow$ Top-$K$ $\rightarrow$ 变异）。

Unlike many existing NAS frameworks that rely on GPU acceleration, ENAS is designed to operate efficiently without requiring GPUs, making it suitable for resource-constrained development environments.

与许多依赖 GPU 加速的现有 NAS 框架不同，ENAS 的设计目标是在无需 GPU 的情况下高效运行，使其非常适合资源受限的开发环境。

We evaluate ENAS on two TinyML benchmarks, Visual Wake Words and Melanoma Cancer, across eight microcontrollers with memory footprints ranging from 20\,KB to 1\,MB SRAM and nine input image resolutions. Our experimental results show that ENAS achieves mean search-time speedups of $2.41{\times}$ and $1.70{\times}$ on the Visual Wake Words and Melanoma Cancer datasets, respectively, while maintaining competitive test accuracy compared with the recent NanoNAS framework.

我们在两个 TinyML 基准测试（Visual Wake Words 和 Melanoma Cancer）上评估了 ENAS，测试涵盖了内存占用从 20 KB 到 1 MB SRAM 不等的八种微控制器，以及九种不同的输入图像分辨率。实验结果表明，在 Visual Wake Words 和 Melanoma Cancer 数据集上，ENAS 的平均搜索时间分别提升了 $2.41$ 倍和 $1.70$ 倍，同时保持了与近期 NanoNAS 框架相当的测试准确率。

A measured resource analysis further shows that ENAS-selected models use substantially lower peak activation RAM, the binding constraint for microcontroller deployment at matched accuracy. Additionally, ENAS achieves $79.4\%$ test accuracy on an STM32H743-based microcontroller, outperforming the greedy CPU-only baseline by $2.6$ percentage points.

资源分析进一步表明，ENAS 选择的模型使用了显著更低的峰值激活 RAM，这是在相同准确率下微控制器部署的关键约束条件。此外，ENAS 在基于 STM32H743 的微控制器上达到了 $79.4\%$ 的测试准确率，比仅使用 CPU 的贪婪基准模型高出 $2.6$ 个百分点。
