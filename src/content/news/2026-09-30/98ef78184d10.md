# OpenAI’s Dots Are Always-On AI Agents—and Its Answer to Meta’s Muse
# OpenAI 的 Dots：全天候 AI 智能体，旨在对标 Meta 的 Muse

Dots are OpenAI’s new always-on AI agents, announced at the company’s DevDay 2026 event on Tuesday in San Francisco. These agents complete tasks on a user’s behalf and are depicted as cute blobs you can personalize and assign multistep projects to complete.

Dots 是 OpenAI 在周二于旧金山举行的 DevDay 2026 大会上发布的新型全天候 AI 智能体。这些智能体可以代表用户完成任务，形象上被设计为可个性化的可爱“小圆点”（blobs），用户可以为其分配多步骤的项目任务。

OpenAI’s Dots are powered by the startup’s GPT-6 Astra model. A core selling point is their “always-on” design—unlike a single-turn chatbot experience, these agents constantly crawl the web and attempt to complete whatever task you assign to them. Dots also pull context from connected apps and learn more about your preferences over time.

OpenAI 的 Dots 由该公司最新的 GPT-6 Astra 模型驱动。其核心卖点在于“全天候”设计——与单轮对话的聊天机器人不同，这些智能体会持续抓取网络信息，并尝试完成你分配给它们的任何任务。Dots 还能从关联的应用程序中提取上下文，并随着时间的推移不断学习你的偏好。

In one demo example shared by OpenAI, a user’s Dot helps plan dinner. The Dot proactively saw that the user’s calendar showed they were working through dinner and messaged with two GrubHub food delivery options with pricing for each. The user followed up with their food pick and when they wanted it ordered. In another example, a user collaborated with the agent to launch a new website.

在 OpenAI 分享的一个演示案例中，用户的 Dot 协助规划晚餐。Dot 主动发现用户的日历显示其晚餐时间仍在工作，于是发送了两条 GrubHub 外卖选项，并附带了各自的价格。用户随后回复了选择的食物及预定时间。在另一个例子中，用户与该智能体协作上线了一个新网站。

Users can message Dots through ChatGPT, Slack, and Microsoft Teams, with the user’s context shared with Dots across modes. In addition, Pro users can join a waitlist to text with their Dots in iMessage or RCS messaging on Android.

用户可以通过 ChatGPT、Slack 和 Microsoft Teams 与 Dots 进行消息交互，用户的上下文信息会在不同模式间共享。此外，Pro 用户可以加入候补名单，以便在 iMessage 或 Android 的 RCS 消息中与 Dots 进行文字交流。

The Dots rollout starts today for subscribers to ChatGPT’s Pro tier that costs $100 a month. OpenAI often launches new features first for this higher subscription tier and then rolls them out to a larger subset of users over time. Users can start by controlling one Dot, but the company is expected to eventually roll out the ability to control multiple agents at the same time.

Dots 于今日起向每月 100 美元的 ChatGPT Pro 订阅用户开放。OpenAI 通常会先为这一高阶订阅层级推出新功能，随后再逐步向更广泛的用户群体推广。用户目前可以控制一个 Dot，但预计该公司最终将推出同时控制多个智能体的功能。

The Dots launch is also part of OpenAI doubling down on its efforts to attract more enterprise clients. On stage at DevDay, CEO Sam Altman announced “specialist Dots” for enterprise customers tuned for specific tasks, like accounting, email marketing, and legal analysis. It adds to the trend of AI agent coworkers being integrated into more professional workflows.

Dots 的发布也是 OpenAI 加大力度吸引更多企业客户的举措之一。在 DevDay 的舞台上，CEO Sam Altman 宣布为企业客户推出“专家型 Dots”，这些智能体针对会计、电子邮件营销和法律分析等特定任务进行了优化。这进一步推动了 AI 智能体同事融入更多专业工作流程的趋势。

Dots are designed to get explicit approval before taking more sensitive actions on a user's behalf, like installing software or changing a password. Users can enable a Custom Rules tool to set explicit boundaries that they don't want agents to cross, as well as tasks that need more direct permission to complete.

Dots 被设计为在代表用户执行敏感操作（如安装软件或更改密码）之前，必须获得明确的批准。用户可以启用“自定义规则”（Custom Rules）工具，设定智能体不得逾越的明确界限，以及需要更直接授权才能完成的任务。

As users connect their data and use agents, it's worth keeping potential security and privacy risks top of mind. OpenAI built safety guardrails into these Dots, but even so, asking agents to automate aspects of your life involves trusting the tools won’t accidentally share information you didn’t intend or get tricked by an online adversary into taking actions you didn’t want. Exercise caution when interacting with this nascent software and connecting data from your pre-existing apps. Also, if your OpenAI account is set to allow model training on your data, this setting carries over to your interactions with the agents.

当用户连接数据并使用智能体时，务必时刻关注潜在的安全和隐私风险。尽管 OpenAI 在这些 Dots 中内置了安全护栏，但让智能体自动化处理生活中的某些方面，意味着你需要信任这些工具不会意外泄露你不希望分享的信息，也不会被网络攻击者诱导执行你不希望的操作。在与这种新兴软件交互并连接现有应用程序的数据时，请务必谨慎。此外，如果你的 OpenAI 账户设置为允许使用你的数据进行模型训练，该设置同样适用于你与智能体的交互。

Personal agents are currently having a breakthrough moment. While this year started with early adopters in Silicon Valley latching onto tools like OpenClaw, the recent release of Meta’s Muse agent reached a larger user base that is now experimenting with personalized agents. Muse rocketed to the top of smartphone app download charts, but Dots has come for Meta's lunch.

个人智能体目前正处于突破性时刻。虽然今年年初硅谷的早期采用者开始使用 OpenClaw 等工具，但 Meta 最近发布的 Muse 智能体触达了更广泛的用户群，他们现在正尝试使用个性化智能体。Muse 迅速冲上了智能手机应用下载榜榜首，但 Dots 的出现显然是冲着 Meta 的市场份额来的。
