# Offline Policy Evaluation as a decision support tool for designing Adaptive Experiments
# 离线策略评估：作为设计自适应实验的决策支持工具

We investigate how historical data from fixed randomized experiments (A/B tests) can be used to inform the deployment of adaptive experiments based on contextual bandits.
我们研究了如何利用来自固定随机实验（A/B 测试）的历史数据，为基于上下文多臂老虎机（contextual bandits）的自适应实验部署提供参考。

Given data collected under a static allocation, our goal is to assess which adaptive policies, if any, would have outperformed the original design and under what conditions.
在给定静态分配下收集的数据时，我们的目标是评估哪些自适应策略（如果有的话）会优于原始设计，以及在何种条件下表现更优。

To this end, we combine off-policy evaluation (OPE) with a controlled warm-start simulation. From logged A/B test data exhibiting heterogeneous treatment effects, we estimate nuisance components and use doubly robust estimators to rank a portfolio of pre-specified adaptive and non-adaptive policies.
为此，我们将离线策略评估（OPE）与受控的“热启动”（warm-start）模拟相结合。通过展示异质性处理效应的记录 A/B 测试数据，我们估计了干扰成分，并使用双重稳健估计器（doubly robust estimators）对一系列预先指定的自适应和非自适应策略进行了排序。

When ground truth is available, we then deploy the same offline-trained policies in a simulator that reuses the exact data-generating reward probabilities, providing a safe, ground-truth-anchored environment to study the offline-to-online transition under warm starting.
当存在基准真值（ground truth）时，我们将这些离线训练的策略部署到一个模拟器中，该模拟器复用了精确的数据生成奖励概率，从而提供了一个安全且以真值为锚点的环境，用于研究热启动下的离线到在线（offline-to-online）转换。

Using synthetic randomized controlled trials with known heterogeneity structures and an oracle policy, our results indicate that adaptive, context-aware policies improve upon fixed allocations when meaningful heterogeneity is present, while providing little benefit in its absence.
通过使用具有已知异质性结构和预言机策略（oracle policy）的合成随机对照试验，我们的结果表明：当存在显著的异质性时，自适应且具备上下文感知能力的策略优于固定分配；而在缺乏异质性时，这些策略几乎没有优势。

We reinforce our findings on standard open benchmarks (Hillstrom, Criteo Uplift, and LaLonde), reinterpreted through a policy-value and regret perspective.
我们通过策略价值和遗憾（regret）的视角，在标准的开放基准测试（Hillstrom、Criteo Uplift 和 LaLonde）上进一步验证了我们的发现。

Overall, our results provide a practical methodology for deciding when adaptive experimentation is worth deploying and how to select among competing adaptive policies using existing A/B test data.
总的来说，我们的研究结果提供了一种实用的方法论，用于决定何时值得部署自适应实验，以及如何利用现有的 A/B 测试数据在多种竞争性自适应策略中进行选择。
