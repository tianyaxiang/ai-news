The Fairphone 6 Plus is modular, repairable, and $100 off right now. You can replace everything from the cameras to the battery, potentially saving future you hundreds in repair costs.

Fairphone 6 Plus 采用模块化设计，可维修，目前优惠 100 美元。从摄像头到电池，所有部件均可更换，这可能会为您未来节省数百美元的维修费用。

Unlike most smartphones, the Fairphone 6 Plus is designed for home repair, with easily replaceable parts that could help extend its lifespan well into the future. That makes its midrange price point easier to justify, especially now that it’s dropped to a new low of $552.49 (about $98 off) at Amazon as part of the retailer’s ongoing Black Friday sale.

与大多数智能手机不同，Fairphone 6 Plus 专为居家维修而设计，其易于更换的部件有助于延长手机的使用寿命。这使其定价更具合理性，尤其是现在作为亚马逊“黑色星期五”促销活动的一部分，其价格已降至 552.49 美元（约优惠 98 美元）的新低。

The 6.3-inch Fairphone 6 Plus features 12 user-replaceable parts, including the battery, screen, USB-C port, and cameras, all of which you can swap out with the included screwdriver. Replacement parts are relatively affordable, too; a new battery costs $39.99, which helps offset its middling battery life and lack of wireless charging. It also comes with a five-year warranty and up to six Android OS upgrades, making it a good option if you want a phone you can keep using for years.

这款 6.3 英寸的 Fairphone 6 Plus 拥有 12 个用户可更换部件，包括电池、屏幕、USB-C 接口和摄像头，所有这些都可以使用随附的螺丝刀进行更换。替换零件也相对实惠；一块新电池售价 39.99 美元，这在一定程度上弥补了其平庸的续航能力和缺乏无线充电功能的不足。此外，它还提供五年保修和最多六次安卓系统升级，如果您想要一部可以长期使用的手机，它是一个不错的选择。

Although repairability is its marquee feature, the 6 Plus is also a solid midrange Android device. Fairphone prioritizes ethically sourced materials, making it a strong choice for environmentally conscious buyers who don’t want to sacrifice performance. Its Snapdragon 7s Gen 4 processor handles everyday tasks well, and its cameras take decent photos, though they’re not quite on par with those of pricier phones.

尽管可维修性是其主打功能，但 6 Plus 也是一款稳健的中端安卓设备。Fairphone 优先考虑符合道德标准的原材料，对于那些既想保护环境又不愿牺牲性能的买家来说，这是一个强有力的选择。其骁龙 7s Gen 4 处理器可以很好地处理日常任务，摄像头也能拍出不错的照片，尽管与更昂贵的手机相比仍有差距。

What’s more, it offers a clean, bloatware-free Android experience and modular back panels, which you can swap for optional accessories like a card holder or crossbody strap. Our own Allison Johnson particularly liked the phone’s dedicated switch for turning on customizable focus modes called Moments, which can simplify your homescreen and limit access to distracting apps — something we could all use a little less of.

此外，它还提供了纯净、无臃肿软件的安卓体验，以及可更换的模块化后盖，您可以将其更换为卡夹或斜挎带等可选配件。我们的同事 Allison Johnson 特别喜欢该手机用于开启名为“Moments”的可定制专注模式的专用开关，它可以简化您的主屏幕并限制对干扰性应用程序的访问——这正是我们大家都需要减少的东西。
