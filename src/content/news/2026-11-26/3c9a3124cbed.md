In a powerful explosion that launched gray-white clouds over southeastern Iowa, the Army detonated 68 barrels containing 9,700 pounds (4.4 tons) of degraded lead azide on Wednesday at about 9 am CT, preventing a spontaneous unmitigated explosion of the crystallized, highly unstable compound.

周三上午9点左右，在一场威力巨大的爆炸中，美国陆军在爱荷华州东南部引爆了68桶总重9700磅（约4.4吨）的降解叠氮化铅，以此防止这种高度不稳定的结晶化合物发生自发性失控爆炸，爆炸产生的灰白色云团笼罩了当地上空。

The detonation occurred at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant (IAAAP) in Middletown, Iowa, a town of fewer than 400 people. Residents within a 5-mile area of the facility were advised to evacuate, while people farther out were told to shelter in place, close windows and doors tightly, turn off fans, and set indoor air systems to recirculate.

此次引爆发生在位于爱荷华州米德尔敦的爱荷华陆军弹药厂（IAAAP），该镇人口不足400人。工厂方圆5英里内的居民被建议撤离，而更远处的居民则被告知就地避难，紧闭门窗，关闭风扇，并将室内空气系统设置为循环模式。

Local Iowa outlet KWQC caught the plume from the two earth-covered containers, which were detonated simultaneously. A KWQZ journalist, at a reportedly safe distance from the blast, said on the live broadcast that he could see and then feel the shockwave roll through the crop field in which he was standing. He put a mask on for the rest of the broadcast.

当地媒体KWQC拍摄到了两个土覆盖容器同时引爆后产生的烟云。一名据称处于安全距离的KWQC记者在直播中表示，他先是看到了冲击波，随后感受到了冲击波穿过他所站的农田。在随后的直播中，他戴上了口罩。

Lead is a neurotoxin that does not break down in the environment and poses significant health risks, particularly to young children and pregnant people. In the hours leading to the detonation, the Army released maps showing modeled drift and distribution of the plume. They indicated that airborne lead would travel around 100 miles to the southwest of the facility, while dust deposition would cover around 200 square miles in that direction.

铅是一种在环境中无法降解的神经毒素，对健康构成重大风险，尤其是对幼儿和孕妇。在引爆前的几个小时里，陆军发布了显示烟云漂移和分布模型的地图。地图显示，空气中的铅将向工厂西南方向传播约100英里，而粉尘沉降将覆盖该方向约200平方英里的区域。

Researchers and government workers have already collected air and soil samples from the area, which will act as baseline readings for lead levels in the aftermath and fallout of the detonation. In preparation for the blast, the Army surrounded the earth-covered containers with sand-and lime fill. They set up water cannons and misters intended to keep dust down. They also covered the top of the container with fire-suppressant foam.

研究人员和政府工作人员已经从该地区采集了空气和土壤样本，这些样本将作为爆炸后铅含量水平的基准读数。为准备此次爆炸，陆军用沙子和石灰填充物包围了土覆盖容器。他们设置了水炮和喷雾器以抑制粉尘，并用灭火泡沫覆盖了容器顶部。

The explosion follows days of confusion, outrage, questions, and delays. Although the Army said that it has known about the hazardous state of the lead azide since March, Iowans were largely unaware of the situation and the planned detonation until last week. At that time, the Army had planned to detonate the barrels on Friday, November 20. But public alarm and an emergency court filing from environmental groups led to a delay, which was initially scheduled for Monday, then Tuesday, and finally to today.

此次爆炸发生前，经历了数日的混乱、愤怒、质疑和延期。尽管陆军声称自3月起就已知晓叠氮化铅的危险状态，但爱荷华州民众直到上周才大体知晓这一情况及计划中的引爆行动。当时，陆军原计划于11月20日周五引爆这些桶，但公众的恐慌以及环保组织提交的紧急法院文件导致了延期，引爆时间最初定于周一，随后推迟至周二，最终定在今天。

A federal appeals court initially issued a stay on the Army’s permit to conduct the detonation, and, on Monday, Trump said he ordered the military to halt the operation until the court lifted the stay and Governor-elect Rob Sand, a Democrat, signed off. Ultimately, the court ruled this week that the Army could move ahead with plans, given no better option. But questions continue to swirl about how the lead azide got to such a “catastrophically hazardous condition.” On Tuesday, The Washington Post reported that it had viewed emails that suggest the Army knew since at least September 2025, not this past March.

联邦上诉法院最初发布了针对陆军引爆许可的暂缓令。周一，特朗普表示他已下令军方停止行动，直到法院解除暂缓令且当选州长、民主党人罗布·桑德（Rob Sand）签字批准。最终，法院本周裁定，鉴于没有更好的选择，陆军可以继续执行计划。然而，关于叠氮化铅为何会达到如此“灾难性的危险状态”，质疑声依然不断。周二，《华盛顿邮报》报道称，其查阅的电子邮件显示，陆军至少从2025年9月起就已知情，而非今年3月。
