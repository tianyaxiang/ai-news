# Guilty: Huawei faces steep penalties for stealing T-Mobile, Cisco trade secrets

On Tuesday, a US jury convicted one of China’s key telecommunications and AI giants, Huawei Technologies, of stealing trade secrets from US tech firms and evading sanctions against Iran. Although the longrunning charges stem back to 2018, the verdict coming now threatens to inflame US-China tensions at a sensitive time, just as Donald Trump prepares to increase tariffs on China.

周二，美国一个陪审团裁定中国主要的电信和人工智能巨头之一华为技术有限公司窃取美国科技公司的商业机密并逃避对伊朗的制裁。尽管这些长期存在的指控可追溯至2018年，但此时作出的裁决可能会在敏感时期加剧美中紧张关系，正值唐纳德·特朗普准备提高对华关税之际。

In a press release, however, the US Department of Justice celebrated the outcome as critical to protecting the US financial system, the global telecommunications industry, and US national security. The jury convicted Huawei of charges including bank fraud, conspiracy to defraud the US, money laundering conspiracy, and “substantive” violations of the International Emergency Economic Powers Act.

然而，美国司法部在一份新闻稿中称赞这一结果对于保护美国金融体系、全球电信行业以及美国国家安全至关重要。陪审团裁定华为犯有多项罪名，包括银行欺诈、共谋欺诈美国、共谋洗钱以及“实质性”违反《国际紧急经济权力法》。

Among crimes, Huawei was found guilty of “repeatedly” lying to banks about its business in Iran. Huawei also frequently lied to the US government that “it did not assist Iran’s government, military, or intelligence community,” the DOJ said. In reality, the DOJ proved at trial that Huawei was conducting business in Iran under the name “Skycom” to evade sanctions, while providing Iran with “turnkey services with respect to an Internet backbone and broader accessibility for mobile networks.”

在各项罪行中，华为被认定“反复”向银行谎报其在伊朗的业务。司法部表示，华为还经常向美国政府撒谎，称其“没有协助伊朗政府、军队或情报机构”。事实上，司法部在庭审中证明，华为以“Skycom”的名义在伊朗开展业务以逃避制裁，同时为伊朗提供“关于互联网骨干网和更广泛移动网络接入的交钥匙服务”。

Further, Huawei helped the Iranian regime “monitor and censor its users’ access to the Internet” and provided surveillance cameras to monitor protests, the DOJ said. In a statement, the acting principal deputy assistant attorney general for national security, M. Scott Proctor, said Huawei “empowered our enemies” and “harmed our national security.”

此外，司法部称，华为帮助伊朗政权“监控和审查其用户的互联网访问”，并提供监控摄像头来监视抗议活动。负责国家安全的代理首席副助理司法部长M. Scott Proctor在一份声明中表示，华为“增强了我们敌人的力量”并“损害了我们的国家安全”。

As the DOJ successfully argued, Huawei lied about its Iranian business specifically so that it could continue obtaining billions in US dollars by participating in a global marketplace that gave it necessary access to steal US tech. Among companies targeted, US Attorney for the Eastern District of New York Joseph Nocella Jr. said that Huawei blatantly stole from T-Mobile and Cisco, as well as a semiconductor storage company called CNEX and an antenna manufacturer called Quintel.

正如司法部成功论证的那样，华为就其伊朗业务撒谎，专门是为了能够通过参与全球市场继续获取数十亿美元的美元，而该市场为其提供了窃取美国技术的必要途径。在被针对的公司中，纽约东区联邦检察官Joseph Nocella Jr.表示，华为公然从T-Mobile和思科，以及一家名为CNEX的半导体存储公司和一家名为Quintel的天线制造商那里窃取了技术。

“The misappropriated intellectual property included trade secret information and copyrighted works, such as source code and user manuals for Internet routers, antenna technology, and robot testing technology,” the DOJ said.

司法部表示：“被挪用的知识产权包括商业机密信息和受版权保护的作品，例如互联网路由器的源代码和用户手册、天线技术以及机器人测试技术。”

One of the stolen ideas was the design of a T-Mobile robot called “Tappy,” which helped the US firm “assess the responsiveness of touch screens by mimicking a human touch,” the DOJ said. To copy the design, Huawei signed a non-disclosure agreement, then sent photos and technical details back to China. Even bolder, when that intel was deemed insufficient, “a Huawei employee stole a part of the robot in his backpack and carried it out of T-Mobile’s controlled laboratory,” the DOJ said.

被窃取的创意之一是T-Mobile一款名为“Tappy”的机器人设计，司法部称该机器人帮助这家美国公司“通过模拟人类触摸来评估触摸屏的响应能力”。为了复制该设计，华为签署了一份保密协议，然后将照片和技术细节发回中国。更胆大妄为的是，当这些情报被认为不足时，“一名华为员工将机器人的一部分偷走并装进背包，带出了T-Mobile的受控实验室，”司法部表示。

Huawei’s tactics weren’t always sophisticated, but they were effective, the DOJ said, building into “a long-running conspiracy to steal intellectual property of US companies.” At trade shows and tours of rivals’ facilities, Huawei employees would pretend to “get lost,” the DOJ said, so they could wander into restricted areas and take photos of hardware, The New York Times reported.

司法部表示，华为的策略并不总是很复杂，但却很有效，演变成“一场长期窃取美国公司知识产权的阴谋”。据《纽约时报》报道，司法部称，在贸易展览和参观竞争对手设施时，华为员工会假装“迷路”，以便溜进限制区域并拍摄硬件照片。

Huawei engineers would also sometimes hide their activity by spelling words backwards. For example, Huawei was able to copy the source code used on Cisco routers—which the DOJ said was “identified as the ‘crown jewel’ of Cisco technology”—by taking notes in which they spelled Cisco as “ocsic.” Another time, the DOJ noted that a Huawei employee was caught “photographing proprietary Fujitsu equipment” while wearing a badge that falsely identified him as an employee of “Weihua.”

华为工程师有时还会通过倒拼单词来隐藏他们的活动。例如，华为通过做笔记将思科拼写为“ocsic”，从而复制了思科路由器上使用的源代码——司法部称其为“思科技术的‘皇冠上的明珠’”。另一次，司法部指出，一名华为员工在佩戴伪造其为“Weihua”员工的胸牌时，被抓到“拍摄富士通的专有设备”。

During the trial, Meredith Arfa, a federal prosecutor, noted that Huawei employees were sometimes paid bonuses for their crimes, since the theft was so integral to Huawei’s business model, NYT reported. Roman Rozhavsky, the assistant director of the FBI’s Counterintelligence and Espionage Division, said that Huawei spent years expanding its international enterprise “through theft of trade secrets, fraud, and other criminal conduct at the expense of US companies and the rule of law.”

据《纽约时报》报道，在庭审期间，联邦检察官Meredith Arfa指出，华为员工有时会因其犯罪行为获得奖金，因为这种窃取行为是华为商业模式不可或缺的一部分。联邦调查局反间谍部门助理局长Roman Rozhavsky表示，华为多年来一直“通过窃取商业机密、欺诈和其他犯罪行为，以牺牲美国公司和法治为代价”来扩张其国际企业。

Huawei has been banned from selling its tech in the US since 2019, so NYT noted that the guilty verdict is unlikely to rattle the company’s finances much. However, the financial penalties stemming from the verdict could be steep, as the DOJ explained that they include “criminal fines, forfeiture, and restitution.” Sentencing is scheduled for September 14.

自2019年以来，华为一直被禁止在美国销售其技术，因此《纽约时报》指出，这一有罪判决不太可能对该公司的财务状况造成太大冲击。然而，判决带来的经济处罚可能会很重，正如司法部所解释的那样，其中包括“刑事罚款、没收和赔偿”。判决日期定于9月14日。

Huawei continues to deny any wrongdoing and rejects US claims that the Chinese Communist Party directed the thefts. NYT noted that Huawei’s investors have argued that the US fight is politically motivated and seeks to hobble “a Chinese initiative to build 5G networks around the world.”

华为继续否认任何不当行为，并拒绝接受美国关于中国共产党指使这些窃取行为的说法。《纽约时报》指出，华为的投资者认为，美国的打压是出于政治动机，旨在阻碍“中国在全球建设5G网络的倡议”。

Outside the courthouse following the verdict, Brian Heberlig, a lawyer for Huawei, told reporters that it’s significant that the jury did not convict Huawei of racketeering charges or conspiracy to obstruct justice. According to Heberlig, the examples of US technology theft merely represented “isolated misconduct by company employees,” which inevitably happens in a company so large, with “hundreds of thousands of people.”

判决后在法院外，华为的律师Brian Heberlig告诉记者，陪审团没有判定华为犯有敲诈勒索罪或妨碍司法公正共谋罪，这一点意义重大。据Heberlig称，美国技术被窃取的例子仅仅代表了“公司员工的个别不当行为”，这在一家拥有“数十万员工”的大公司中是不可避免会发生的。

Heberlig confirmed that Huawei planned to appeal and considered the jury’s opposition to racketeering charges to be a “profound rejection of the United States’ narratives about what Huawei is.”

Heberlig证实华为计划上诉，并认为陪审团对敲诈勒索指控的反对是对“美国关于华为是什么的叙述的深刻否定”。
