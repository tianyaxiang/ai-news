# Now Google’s Familiar Faces AI feature works on Walmart’s $35 smart home camera too

现在，谷歌的“熟悉面孔”（Familiar Faces）人工智能功能也适用于沃尔玛售价 35 美元的智能家居摄像头。

A couple of Walmart’s budget-friendly smart home cameras will be able to recognize people around your house now using Google’s familiar face detection feature. A new Google Home update brings Familiar Faces support to the $35 Onn Outdoor Camera Plug-In and $77 Onn Floodlight Camera Hardwired. However, a Google Home Premium subscription is still required to access the feature.

沃尔玛几款经济实惠的智能家居摄像头现在能够利用谷歌的“熟悉面孔”检测功能来识别你家周围的人。谷歌 Home 的一项新更新为售价 35 美元的 Onn 室外插电式摄像头和 77 美元的 Onn 硬接线泛光灯摄像头带来了“熟悉面孔”支持。不过，要使用该功能，仍需订阅 Google Home Premium。

Both cameras and Google’s Nest Cam Outdoor (wired, 2nd gen) also have a new remote control in the Google Home app you can use to start or stop a 30-second security siren while watching the camera’s live video feed.

这两款摄像头以及谷歌的 Nest Cam 室外摄像头（有线，第二代）在 Google Home 应用中还新增了一个遥控功能，你可以在观看摄像头实时视频流时，使用它来启动或停止 30 秒的安全警报。

Earlier this month, Google announced that Gemini Live would get an upgrade “to our latest speech-to-speech architecture for significantly faster response times and a smoother experience.” Now it says those improvements are rolling out over the coming weeks, along with new smart home control, as “Gemini Live now supports all your daily actions, from setting timers to turning off the lights.”

本月早些时候，谷歌宣布 Gemini Live 将升级至“我们最新的语音到语音架构，以实现更快的响应速度和更流畅的体验”。现在谷歌表示，这些改进将在未来几周内推出，同时还将带来新的智能家居控制功能，因为“Gemini Live 现在支持你所有的日常操作，从设置定时器到关灯”。
