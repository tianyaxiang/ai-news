**Title: The loneliest peptide party in New York**

**标题：纽约最冷清的肽类派对**

The peptide curious are looking for answers, and finding dubious products instead. by Victoria Song. Photography by Jack Califano / The Verge. Nov 25, 2026, 6:00 PM UTC.

对肽类产品感到好奇的人们正在寻求答案，却只找到了可疑的产品。作者：Victoria Song。摄影：Jack Califano / The Verge。2026年11月25日，下午6:00（协调世界时）。

On a rainy fall evening, an experimental art venue in Chelsea Market had a small line out the door. There are always lines in New York City. Viral food trends, gallery exhibitions, pop-ups, and parties. The folks in line looked like your average joe, ready for a Friday night on the town. You wouldn’t look twice — except, perhaps, for a man with a wizard beard and a laser sword strapped to his back.

在一个秋雨绵绵的傍晚，切尔西市场的一家实验艺术场馆门外排起了小队。在纽约市，排队总是常态，无论是为了网红美食、画廊展览、快闪店还是派对。排队的人看起来就像普通市民，准备享受周五的夜生活。你可能不会多看他们一眼——除非，那个人留着巫师般的胡须，背上还绑着一把激光剑。

Inside, the man with a laser sword stood in the middle of the cavernous bottom floor of a two-story warehouse strewn with large bean bags. On the wall were psychedelic projections of jellyfish, shrimp, and other sea creatures. A bizarre choice, considering peptides won’t induce any trippy vision quests, and not a single person was getting groovy.

在室内，那个带着激光剑的男人站在两层仓库宽敞的底层中央，地上散落着巨大的懒人沙发。墙上投射着水母、虾和其他海洋生物的迷幻影像。这是一个奇怪的选择，毕竟肽类物质不会引发任何迷幻的视觉探索，而且现场没有一个人在随着音乐起舞。

The industrial beams had posters with QR codes that you could scan to get samples of Afterglow, a new type of peptide product. According to seemingly discarded pamphlets around the venue, Afterglow isn’t a standard injectable or oral peptide. The company makes strips that dissolve under the tongue. The “Afterglow Peptide Field Guide” also described what each sample does, dosing recommendations, and potential side effects.

工业风的横梁上贴着带有二维码的海报，扫描即可获得一种名为 Afterglow 的新型肽类产品样品。根据场馆内随处可见的宣传册，Afterglow 并非标准的注射或口服肽，该公司生产的是舌下含服的薄片。这份《Afterglow 肽类实地指南》还描述了每种样品的作用、剂量建议以及潜在的副作用。

At the upstairs bar, bartenders mixed custom cocktails called Afterburn — complete with bioluminescent veggie nectar — and Margatrutide. (For the record, a bioluminescent veggie nectar cocktail tastes as awful as you think.) Welcome to a peptide launch party.

在楼上的酒吧，调酒师调制了名为 Afterburn（配有生物发光蔬菜花蜜）和 Margatrutide 的特调鸡尾酒。（郑重声明，生物发光蔬菜花蜜鸡尾酒的味道和你想象的一样糟糕。）欢迎来到肽类产品发布派对。

Bioluminescent veggie nectar cocktails for the peptide curious. Peptide parties are old news in San Francisco, but they’re somewhat more novel in New York City. Milling around the edges of the venue were various reporters and photographers. We were all trying to get a sense of why anyone had bothered to come out. Not that many did. Despite the venue’s size, the main floor had plenty of empty space.

为肽类好奇者准备的生物发光蔬菜花蜜鸡尾酒。肽类派对在旧金山早已不是新鲜事，但在纽约市还算比较新奇。场馆边缘徘徊着各路记者和摄影师，我们都在试图弄清楚为什么会有人特意赶来。其实来的人并不多，尽管场馆很大，但主楼层仍有大片空地。

One person I spoke to was a fencer with sore joints. They’d heard that one of the peptides might help with recovery. Another had a father in the wellness industry and had heard vague things about how effective peptides could be. One woman, staring at a projected jellyfish, merely shrugged. Her friend was more into “wellness stuff” and she had no plans, so, why not check out a party?

我交谈过的一位击剑运动员关节酸痛，听说某种肽可能有助于恢复。另一位受访者的父亲从事健康产业，他听说过肽类产品效果显著，但具体细节模糊。一位盯着投影水母的女士只是耸了耸肩，她的朋友更热衷于“健康产品”，而她正好没安排，所以就来看看派对。

A man, staring into his bag of Afterglow samples, was mostly interested in weight loss but scared about the safety of gray market products. A few feet away, another man was furiously reading the Afterglow pamphlet. “I like to know what I put in my body,” he said. The only problem was he didn’t remember which sample he had tried.

一名盯着自己 Afterglow 样品袋的男子主要对减肥感兴趣，但担心灰色市场产品的安全性。几步之外，另一名男子正在仔细阅读 Afterglow 的宣传册，他说：“我喜欢了解自己摄入了什么。”唯一的问题是，他不记得自己试用的是哪种样品了。

A common refrain: Well, this beats doing research online. This, at least, was a social event where you could theoretically connect with other people. And there were clusters of strangers speaking to each other. Mostly, near the bar. Everywhere else, people stared awkwardly on their phones, their eyes occasionally roaming the room without meeting anyone else’s gaze for more than a second or two.

一个常见的说法是：这总比在网上查资料强。至少这是一个社交活动，理论上你可以与他人建立联系。确实有一些陌生人在交谈，主要是在酒吧附近。而在其他地方，人们尴尬地盯着手机，目光偶尔扫视房间，却不会与他人的视线交汇超过一两秒。

After a while, they’d disappear into the back room and reappear with a goody bag full of peptide samples. Scanning a QR code got you one of two goodie bags at the Afterglow launch peptide party.

过了一会儿，他们会消失在后室，然后带着装满肽类样品的礼品袋重新出现。在 Afterglow 肽类发布派对上，扫描二维码即可获得两种礼品袋中的一种。

After 90 minutes, I decided to leave. For the umpteenth time that evening, I’d walked up to someone, asked about peptides, and got a noncommittal “I really don’t know anything — I just came to learn more.” (When asked if they had, they invariably shrugged and pointed to the goody bag.) It was clear that the only peptide evangelists here were likely associated with the launch.

90分钟后，我决定离开。那天晚上，我无数次走到别人面前询问关于肽的问题，得到的都是模棱两可的回答：“我真的什么都不知道，我只是来了解一下。”（当问及他们是否了解时，他们总是耸耸肩，指着礼品袋。）很明显，这里唯一的肽类推崇者可能都与发布会有关。

The whole thing, to me, seemed as if someone had tried to draw a cool party from memory. The last thing I saw was a person draped in a ghost sheet sitting alone in the corner. As it turned out, the man with the laser sword was a performance artist who’d been invited to attend. Out of journalistic integrity, I asked if he had ever tried peptides. He told me he was more interested in the unifying energy of the universe. When I got up to leave, he waved and said, “Giggles.”

对我来说，整件事就像是有人试图凭记忆画出一场酷炫的派对。我看到的最后一个人是披着鬼魂床单独自坐在角落里的家伙。事实证明，那个带着激光剑的男人是一位受邀参加的表演艺术家。出于新闻操守，我问他是否尝试过肽类产品，他告诉我他更关心宇宙的统一能量。当我起身离开时，他挥了挥手说：“咯咯笑。”

If I were peptide curious, the Afterglow launch party was a pretty awful way to learn anything about these substances. To be fair, Afterglow’s Partiful invitation mostly described this event as “an environment great for aura farming” and the “hard launch of [its] first of many experiments in culture.”

如果我对肽类产品感到好奇，Afterglow 发布派对绝对不是了解这些物质的好地方。平心而论，Afterglow 在 Partiful 上的邀请函主要将此次活动描述为“一个适合‘气场养成’的环境”以及“其众多文化实验中的首次正式发布”。

Nevertheless, as part of the press, I did walk away with a smattering of Afterglow’s products. In total, I got packets of what Afterglow calls Charge (NAD+), Rejuvenate (glutathione), Wolverine Blend (BPC-157 / TB-500), Strength Blend (CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin), Glow Blend (GHK-Cu / BPC-157 / TB-500), Connect (PT-141), GLP-2 (tirzepatide), and GLP-3 (retatrutide). Or at least that’s what the packaging says. There’s no way to truly verify gray market peptides for safety or efficacy because they’re unregulated products.

尽管如此，作为媒体的一员，我确实带走了一些 Afterglow 的产品。总共包括 Afterglow 所称的 Charge (NAD+)、Rejuvenate (谷胱甘肽)、Wolverine Blend (BPC-157 / TB-500)、Strength Blend (CJC-1295 / Ipamorelin)、Glow Blend (GHK-Cu / BPC-157 / TB-500)、Connect (PT-141)、GLP-2 (替尔泊肽) 和 GLP-3 (瑞他鲁肽)。至少包装上是这么写的。由于这些是未经监管的产品，无法真正验证灰色市场肽类的安全性和有效性。
