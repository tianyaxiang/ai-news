# The 36 Best Gifts for Dads (2026)

Save this story. It occurs to me that, as a new(ish) father, and someone who reviews products for a living, I am a nightmare to get gifts for. I’m not alone. Most of my friends have no idea what to get their dad, and end up brainstorming in group chats with anyone they can get their hands on.

保存这篇文章。作为一个新手（或准新手）父亲，同时也是一名以评测产品为生的人，我意识到要给我送礼物简直是一场噩梦。我并不孤单。我的大多数朋友都不知道该给他们的父亲送什么，最终只能在群聊中与任何能联系到的人一起头脑风暴。

Fear not, I’m here to make gifting a little easier this year (or at the very least, contribute to the brainstorm). Below are some of the best WIRED-tested gifts for the dad or father figure in your life, including travel gadgets, dad-approved apparel, and best-in-class earbuds. I’ve personally gifted a few of these to my dad, with great results. Updated November 2026: I swapped and added a few new picks, including watches and apparel.

别担心，我来这里是为了让今年的送礼变得更容易一些（或者至少能为你的头脑风暴提供一些思路）。以下是一些经过《连线》（WIRED）测试的、最适合你生命中父亲或长辈的礼物，包括旅行小工具、父亲认可的服装以及同类产品中最好的耳机。我个人曾给父亲送过其中几样，效果非常好。2026年11月更新：我更换并增加了一些新的选择，包括手表和服装。

### For the Fitness Dad

### 健身老爸专区

Theragun Relief: Another classic gift I’ve seen win over not just Dad, but everyone else in the room, too (everyone wanted a turn). This massage gun has the signature Theragun triangle grip, which makes it far easier to target hard-to-reach places compared to other brands that have a straight grip. It comes with three ball head attachments (standard, dampener, and thumb), and lasts up to 120 minutes on a single charge.

Theragun Relief：这是另一款经典的礼物，我发现它不仅赢得了父亲的青睐，还征服了房间里的每一个人（每个人都想试用一下）。这款按摩枪拥有 Theragun 标志性的三角形握把，与其他采用直柄设计的品牌相比，它能更容易地触及难以够到的部位。它配有三个球形按摩头（标准型、减震型和拇指型），单次充电可使用长达 120 分钟。

Shokz OpenRun Pro 2: These are my favorite open ear headphones for anyone, not just Dad. Because they leave your ears open, you can easily hear the world around you, along with your music. That’s why I love them for outdoor runs and bike rides. This premium model uses a mix of bone conduction (think vibrations) and air conduction (think tiny speakers pointed toward your ears) to deliver sound that’s clearer than most smaller open earbuds.

Shokz OpenRun Pro 2：这是我最喜欢的开放式耳机，不仅适合父亲，也适合任何人。因为它们让你的耳朵保持开放，你可以在听音乐的同时轻松听到周围的声音。这就是我喜欢在户外跑步和骑行时使用它们的原因。这款高端型号结合了骨传导（想象一下振动）和气传导（想象一下指向耳朵的小型扬声器）技术，提供的声音比大多数小型开放式耳机更清晰。

Garmin Forerunner 165: This Garmin watch is just right for the dad who is getting really into running and wants to dig into more advanced metrics and detailed training plans than what’s offered on most smartwatches. Like any good fitness tracker, the Forerunner 165 has superb battery life (up to 11 days), a colorful touchscreen, and plenty of buttons that make it easy to control with gloved or sweaty hands.

Garmin Forerunner 165：这款 Garmin 手表非常适合那些开始热衷于跑步，并希望深入研究比大多数智能手表所提供的更高级指标和详细训练计划的父亲。像任何优秀的健身追踪器一样，Forerunner 165 拥有出色的电池续航能力（长达 11 天）、彩色触摸屏，以及许多按钮，即使戴着手套或手上有汗水也能轻松操控。

Lectric XP4 750 Electric Bike: I can’t think of many dads who don’t love an electric bike. The Lectric XP4 750 isn’t just one of WIRED’s top electric bike picks, it’s one of the best-selling electric vehicles in America, ranking third behind the Tesla Model Y and Model 3 in 2024. WIRED reviewer Michael Venutolo-Mantovani says it only took 20 minutes to assemble and lasted well over 50 miles of riding on a single charge.

Lectric XP4 750 电动自行车：我想不出有几个父亲会不喜欢电动自行车。Lectric XP4 750 不仅是《连线》评选出的顶级电动自行车之一，还是美国最畅销的电动交通工具之一，2024 年的销量仅次于特斯拉 Model Y 和 Model 3，排名第三。《连线》评测员 Michael Venutolo-Mantovani 表示，组装它仅需 20 分钟，且单次充电后的续航里程超过 50 英里。

### For the Travel Dad

### 旅行老爸专区

Anker Nano Power Bank With Instacord: This is my favorite power bank, full stop. If I am traveling, going abroad, camping, or just headed to work, I take this power bank. It’s the built-in, retractable USB-C cord that does it for me—it’s there when you need it, and out of the way when you don’t. It’s small enough to pack in any bag and light enough that I barely notice it.

Anker Nano 带线充电宝：这是我最喜欢的充电宝，没有之一。无论是旅行、出国、露营还是去上班，我都会带上它。内置的可伸缩 USB-C 线是它的杀手锏——需要时它就在那里，不需要时也不会碍事。它小到可以装进任何包里，轻到我几乎感觉不到它的存在。

Apple AirTag (2nd Generation): I’ve lost count of how many times my Apple AirTag Bluetooth tracker has saved me from the hassle of looking for something. Keys: located. Luggage: tracked. Backpack: safe and sound. Sure, it’s not the most exciting gift in the world, but it’s the epitome of useful and practical. As long as they use an iPhone, that is.

Apple AirTag（第二代）：我已经数不清我的 Apple AirTag 蓝牙追踪器帮我省去了多少寻找东西的麻烦。钥匙：找到了。行李：追踪到了。背包：安然无恙。当然，它不是世界上最令人兴奋的礼物，但它是实用主义的典范。前提是他们必须使用 iPhone。

GoRuck Huckberry X GoRuck GR1 Slick Backpack (26L): “If you told me I could only have one bag in my life, the 26L version of the GoRuck GR1 would be my pick,” says WIRED operations manager and outdoor expert Scott Gilbertson. It’s absolutely in buy-it-for-life territory, built with ultra-durable, water-resistant 1000D Cordura and tested by US Army Special Forces.

GoRuck Huckberry X GoRuck GR1 Slick 背包（26L）：“如果你告诉我这辈子只能拥有一个包，我会选择 26L 版的 GoRuck GR1，”《连线》运营经理兼户外专家 Scott Gilbertson 说道。它绝对属于“终身使用”级别的产品，采用超耐用、防水的 1000D Cordura 面料制成，并经过美国陆军特种部队的测试。

Rimowa Original Cabin: The best aluminum carry-on suitcase. Aluminum luggage is sleek and durable, with a shell that just looks better with little dents and dings (unlike polycarbonate luggage). Aluminum itself is recyclable and repairable, and the clamps that hold the Rimowa together won’t fail or break as easily as your typical zipper.

Rimowa Original Cabin：最好的铝制登机箱。铝制行李箱既时尚又耐用，外壳上出现的小凹痕和划痕反而让它看起来更有质感（不像聚碳酸酯行李箱）。铝材本身可回收且易于维修，固定 Rimowa 箱体的锁扣也不像普通拉链那样容易损坏或断裂。

Owala FreeSip Water Bottle: A reusable water bottle is something I thought I only needed one of. Turns out, the more the merrier. I now have four and have realized there’s a good place for each one (in my case, one for the house, one for my work bag, and one for the nightstand). The Owala FreeSip has been my favorite.

Owala FreeSip 水杯：我曾以为我只需要一个可重复使用的水杯。事实证明，越多越好。我现在有四个，并意识到每一个都有其合适的用途（对我来说，一个放在家里，一个放在工作包里，一个放在床头柜上）。Owala FreeSip 一直是我的最爱。
