Last night, we had friends and colleagues over for dinner. There are two separate shocks here. The first shock is that we now, finally, know the right answer to this central mystery of algorithms going back to the late 1960s, when Volker Strassen first showed that ω<3 (he got ω≤log27) … and that the true answer is actually a rational number (!). But the even bigger shock is that the AI was able to prove any lower bound of the form ω>2. I never expected this challenge to be all that much easier than P ≠ NP.

Over the decades, I often heard colleagues express extreme confidence that ω=2, and I expressed skepticism whenever this came up—not because I had any strong argument for ω>2, but just because I saw that there was no strong argument for ω=2 either. So, I’m glad to have been vindicated about that! But wait, there was more. 几十年来，我经常听到同事们极其自信地认为 ω=2，每当提到这一点时，我都会表示怀疑——不是因为我有支持 ω>2 的有力论据，仅仅是因为我看到也没有支持 ω=2 的有力论据。所以，我很高兴自己被证明是正确的！但等等，还有更多。在吃甜点时，我们看到了 OpenAI 的另一项发布，证明了计算傅里叶变换所需的线性门真实数量是 N (log N)1/2+o(1)。

The upper bound here improves, not only over the O(N log N) that stood since the 1960s (i.e., the Fast Fourier Transform or FFT), but also over the O(N log0.9999999999999 N) (give or take some 9’s) that OpenAI published back in the October 6 Mathocalypse. Again, though, the real shock here is that there’s also a matching lower bound (!!). 这里的上界不仅改进了自 20 世纪 60 年代以来一直存在的 O(N log N)（即快速傅里叶变换或 FFT），还改进了 OpenAI 在 10 月 6 日“数学启示录”中发布的 O(N log0.9999999999999 N)（忽略一些 9）。然而，真正的冲击在于，这里还有一个相匹配的下界（！！）。所有这些来自远古时代的问题现在都被……直接回答了，而不是通过一点一滴的线索缓慢地揭示。

To give an idea of how much things have changed, the other day Yupan Liu put a preprint on the arXiv proving that QIP(2) = PSPACE—that is, quantum interactive protocols with only two messages have the full power of those with three, or any polynomial number. As recently as 2025, this would’ve considered been one of the top complexity results of the year, and the fact that Yupan had used AI to help achieve it would’ve been strange and wonderful.

为了说明情况发生了多大变化，前几天 Yupan Liu 在 arXiv 上发布了一篇预印本，证明了 QIP(2) = PSPACE——也就是说，只有两条消息的量子交互协议拥有与三条或任意多项式数量消息协议相同的全部能力。就在 2025 年，这还被认为是年度顶级复杂性成果之一，而 Yupan 使用人工智能来辅助实现这一事实本应是既奇怪又美妙的。
