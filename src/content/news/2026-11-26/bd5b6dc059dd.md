In an extraordinary decision, the Federal Communications Commission on Wednesday granted SpaceX conditional authorization to deploy a constellation of up to 1 million Starmind satellites designed to serve as orbital data centers. If fully deployed, this constellation would increase the number of satellites in orbit by roughly 50-fold.

在一项非同寻常的决定中，美国联邦通信委员会（FCC）于周三批准了 SpaceX 的有条件授权，允许其部署多达 100 万颗旨在作为轨道数据中心运行的“星智”（Starmind）卫星星座。如果完全部署，该星座将使轨道上的卫星数量增加约 50 倍。

“The United States is moving decisively to ensure that this breakthrough technology creates opportunity, drives investment, and delivers lasting benefits for the American people,” FCC Chairman Brendan Carr said in a statement on Wednesday. “This action opens a bold new frontier for the computing infrastructure needed to power America’s leadership and growth.”

FCC 主席布伦丹·卡尔（Brendan Carr）在周三的一份声明中表示：“美国正在果断采取行动，确保这项突破性技术能够创造机遇、推动投资，并为美国人民带来持久的利益。这一举措为支撑美国领导地位和增长所需的计算基础设施开辟了一个大胆的新前沿。”

The decision, announced a day before the Thanksgiving holiday in the United States, represents a major regulatory victory for SpaceX and its founder, Elon Musk, who has allied himself with the Trump administration.

该决定在美国感恩节假期前一天宣布，对于 SpaceX 及其创始人埃隆·马斯克（Elon Musk）而言，这是一次重大的监管胜利，马斯克目前已与特朗普政府结盟。

This announcement clears a major hurdle to deploying data centers in space, although it does not establish that orbital data centers are technically or economically viable. Nevertheless, the FCC authorization appears designed to give the company wide latitude as it works through these remaining issues.

这一公告扫清了在太空部署数据中心的主要障碍，尽管它并未证实轨道数据中心在技术或经济上是可行的。不过，FCC 的授权似乎旨在为该公司在解决这些遗留问题时提供广泛的自由度。

For example, SpaceX has seven years to deploy a single operational Starmind satellite under the order. SpaceX has until November 2035 to deploy 100,000 operational satellites, and November 2040 for the full constellation. SpaceX, therefore, has a 14-year regulatory runway to deploy the most ambitious industrial project ever approved for spaceflight.

例如，根据该命令，SpaceX 有七年的时间来部署第一颗可运行的“星智”卫星。SpaceX 必须在 2035 年 11 月前部署 10 万颗可运行卫星，并在 2040 年 11 月前完成整个星座的部署。因此，SpaceX 拥有 14 年的监管缓冲期，用于部署有史以来获批的最雄心勃勃的航天工业项目。

The authorization includes some conditional aspects. SpaceX has proposed operating in six orbits, ranging from 550 km to about 1,000 km. The company sought a waiver from FCC rules to allow satellites above 600 km to be disposed of into “Earth disposal orbits or heliocentric orbits.” That is, instead of burning them up in Earth’s atmosphere, SpaceX wants the flexibility to move retired satellites into other orbits around Earth, or potentially into orbit around the Sun.

该授权包含一些附带条件。SpaceX 提议在 550 公里至约 1000 公里的六个轨道上运行。该公司寻求豁免 FCC 的规定，以允许 600 公里以上的卫星被处置到“地球处置轨道或日心轨道”中。也就是说，SpaceX 希望拥有灵活性，将退役卫星转移到地球周围的其他轨道，或可能转移到太阳轨道，而不是让它们在地球大气层中烧毁。

The FCC did not grant this waiver request “because SpaceX has not provided sufficient information for the Commission to determine whether its alternative disposal methods are compliant with the Commission’s requirements.” The company will need to obtain a waiver before deploying any Starmind satellites that are not intended to be disposed of via atmospheric reentry.

FCC 没有批准这一豁免请求，理由是“SpaceX 未提供足够的信息供委员会确定其替代处置方法是否符合委员会的要求”。在部署任何不打算通过大气层重返方式处置的“星智”卫星之前，该公司必须获得豁免。

However, in other ways the FCC appears to have been incredibly lenient with SpaceX. This is particularly the case with environmental concerns raised by numerous parties that commented on SpaceX’s application to the FCC.

然而，在其他方面，FCC 对 SpaceX 似乎表现得极为宽容。在众多对 SpaceX 的 FCC 申请发表评论的各方所提出的环境担忧方面，这一点尤为明显。

Half a dozen organizations, such as the Secure World Foundation and American Astronomical Society, raised concerns about deploying so many satellites into orbit, citing collision risks, orbital debris, atmospheric pollution from so many satellite reentries, impacts on astronomical observations, and more. The FCC also received approximately 1,500 public comments concerning SpaceX’s application.

包括“安全世界基金会”（Secure World Foundation）和美国天文学会（American Astronomical Society）在内的六个组织对在轨道上部署如此多的卫星表示担忧，理由包括碰撞风险、轨道碎片、大量卫星重返大气层造成的污染、对天文观测的影响等。FCC 还收到了约 1500 条关于 SpaceX 申请的公众评论。

Some of these environmental issues, the FCC said, lay beyond the scope of its review. For example, on concerns about the environmental implications of thousands or potentially tens of thousands of rocket launches, the FCC said, “Potential concerns related to the emissions of rocket launches and the subsequent impacts on the human environment as an indirect result of the Starmind system are outside of the Commission’s purview.”

FCC 表示，其中一些环境问题超出了其审查范围。例如，针对有关数千甚至数万次火箭发射对环境影响的担忧，FCC 表示：“与火箭发射排放以及作为‘星智’系统间接结果而对人类环境产生的后续影响相关的潜在担忧，不在委员会的管辖范围内。”

The FCC also rejected calls for further environmental scrutiny of pollution from satellite reentries, including concerns about aluminum oxides and their potential effects on the ozone layer. “Other concerns, including impacts on the atmosphere caused by satellites burning up on re-entry, remain speculative and not supported in the record,” the FCC authorization states.

FCC 还拒绝了对卫星重返大气层造成的污染进行进一步环境审查的要求，包括对氧化铝及其对臭氧层潜在影响的担忧。FCC 的授权书指出：“包括卫星重返大气层烧毁对大气造成的影响在内的其他担忧，目前仍处于推测阶段，且缺乏记录支持。”

As part of its decision, the FCC cited a significant policy change adopted in October, when the agency concluded that space-based activities generally fall outside its obligations under the National Environmental Policy Act. The FCC reasoned that as these activities occur beyond US territory, they don’t constitute federal actions requiring environmental review. As a result, the agency declined to conduct a comprehensive environmental assessment of SpaceX’s Starmind constellation.

作为其决定的一部分，FCC 引用了 10 月份通过的一项重大政策变更，当时该机构得出结论，认为基于空间的活动通常不在其《国家环境政策法》规定的义务范围内。FCC 的理由是，由于这些活动发生在美属领土之外，它们不构成需要环境审查的联邦行动。因此，该机构拒绝就 SpaceX 的“星智”星座进行全面的环境评估。
