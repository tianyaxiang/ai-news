# Google’s patent for custom AI video feeds paints a bleak picture of entertainment’s future

Google 关于定制 AI 视频流的专利为娱乐业的未来描绘了一幅黯淡的图景。

Imagine an endless feed of AI slop in which you are the main character of every story.

想象一下，在一个无尽的 AI 垃圾内容流中，你是每一个故事的主角。

Last year, Google filed a patent for a system called My Channel that’s designed to create feeds filled with personalized, AI-generated videos and advertisements based on information the company knows about its users. The patent has been rejected, but unfortunately, Google might appeal or resubmit an amended version, and we could see this monstrosity come to market.

去年，Google 申请了一项名为“My Channel”的系统专利，旨在根据公司掌握的用户信息，创建充满个性化 AI 生成视频和广告的推送流。该专利已被驳回，但不幸的是，Google 可能会提出上诉或重新提交修改版本，我们或许会看到这个怪物进入市场。

First spotted by Patentlyze, the patent — which was filed last May and published last week — describes how My Channel would use a person’s demographic data and viewing history to create its own prompts that would then be fed into an LLM to help generate AI videos tailored to that person’s specific interests. In addition to factoring in what kinds of videos a user tends to watch, the system would capture contextual data from their device like IP addresses, GPS locations, and specific details about the user’s age.

该专利最初由 Patentlyze 发现，于去年 5 月提交并于上周公布。它描述了 My Channel 如何利用个人的统计数据和观看历史来创建提示词，随后输入大语言模型（LLM），以生成针对该用户特定兴趣的 AI 视频。除了考虑用户倾向于观看的视频类型外，该系统还会捕获设备中的上下文数据，如 IP 地址、GPS 位置以及用户年龄的具体细节。

My Channel could also base its prompts on current news like sports scores and things happening on social media, and all of the video generation would take place on Google’s servers.

My Channel 还可以根据体育比分和社交媒体上的热点新闻来生成提示词，所有的视频生成过程都将在 Google 的服务器上完成。

According to the patent, My Channel would be able to churn out entire movies of various genres. If a user watches a lot of videos about Christopher Nolan’s The Odyssey, the system might serve up something like Ash Koosha’s Odysseus: The Fall. The patent details how My Channel might be able to connect to other streaming services — which would give it even more information about users and, presumably, access to useful training data for video generation.

根据专利，My Channel 将能够批量制作各种类型的完整电影。如果用户观看了大量关于克里斯托弗·诺兰《奥德赛》的视频，系统可能会推送类似 Ash Koosha 的《奥德赛：陨落》之类的作品。专利详细说明了 My Channel 如何连接到其他流媒体服务，这将使其获得更多关于用户的信息，并据推测，还能获取用于视频生成的有用训练数据。

It all seems like a convoluted way to whip up AI knockoffs of professionally produced art, but what’s most unsettling about the patent are its mentions of My Channel creating digital avatars of users and turning them into “the protagonist or lead character” in the content it pumps out.

这一切看起来像是炮制专业艺术作品的 AI 仿制品的复杂手段，但该专利最令人不安的地方在于，它提到 My Channel 可以创建用户的数字头像，并将他们变成其输出内容中的“主角或领衔角色”。

Setting aside the fact that My Channel would use photos, videos, and audio clips of people to create these avatars, the system sounds like it is designed to generate a form of “entertainment” that is deeply at odds with what makes engaging with art a worthwhile, enjoyable 
experience.

撇开 My Channel 会使用人们的照片、视频和音频片段来创建这些头像的事实不谈，该系统听起来像是为了生成一种“娱乐”形式，而这种形式与让艺术参与变得有价值且令人愉悦的体验背道而驰。

It can be fun to metaphorically project one’s self into fictional narratives to understand how characters feel, but what Google is proposing is giving people access to an endless supply of bespoke content that doesn’t at all encourage them to use their imaginations to empathize with or relate to the characters.

将自己隐喻性地投射到虚构叙事中以理解角色的感受可能很有趣，但 Google 提议的是让人们获得源源不断的定制内容，这根本不会鼓励他们发挥想象力去同情或共鸣角色。

The patent makes it seem like Google thinks that viewers would be entertained enough just by seeing themselves onscreen. That’s bleak in and of itself because My Channel sounds very much like an unholy cross between JibJab and the panoptic elements of Google.

该专利让人觉得 Google 认为观众只要在屏幕上看到自己就足够感到娱乐了。这本身就很凄凉，因为 My Channel 听起来非常像是 JibJab 与 Google 全景监控元素的邪恶结合体。

My Channel feels like an even more depressing idea when you consider how the service would make it that much easier for its users to avoid broadening their horizons by being exposed to new things that exist outside their comfort zones.

当你考虑到这项服务会让用户更容易通过避免接触舒适区之外的新事物来拒绝拓宽视野时，My Channel 听起来就更令人沮丧了。

If Google were able to strike the right licensing deals with other streaming services, My Channel users could presumably gorge themselves on artless AI regurgitations of preexisting content. That might keep some people glued to the service — a goal that every streaming platform strives for — but it would also discourage them from engaging with entertainment that isn’t expressly crafted to appeal to their assumed tastes.

如果 Google 能够与其他流媒体服务达成合适的许可协议，My Channel 的用户大概可以沉溺于对现有内容进行毫无艺术感的 AI 翻版中。这可能会让一些人粘在服务上——这是每个流媒体平台都在追求的目标——但这也会阻碍他们接触那些并非专门为迎合其既定品味而制作的娱乐内容。

When people refer to AI-generated videos as “slop,” they’re talking about the fact that generative models are incapable of generating art with the same kind of creative intention that makes human-made stories compelling.

当人们将 AI 生成的视频称为“垃圾”时，他们指的是生成式模型无法像人类创作的故事那样，以同样的创作意图生成艺术作品。

Video generation models can mimic visual elements of the data (read: movies and series) they’re trained on, but they cannot truly think or feel in the way that directors, writers, and actors can. This reality is why it has been so baffling to see AI-boosting entertainment companies, streamers, and studios insisting that the technology will fundamentally revolutionize the industry for the better. It feels much more likely that gen AI is going to keep flooding the internet with garbage no one wants to watch.

视频生成模型可以模仿它们所训练数据（即电影和剧集）的视觉元素，但它们无法像导演、编剧和演员那样真正地思考或感受。这一现实解释了为什么看到那些推崇 AI 的娱乐公司、流媒体平台和制片厂坚持认为该技术将从根本上改善行业时，会让人感到如此困惑。更有可能的情况是，生成式 AI 将继续用没人想看的垃圾内容充斥互联网。
