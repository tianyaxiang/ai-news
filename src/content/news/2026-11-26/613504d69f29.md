# How data centers are driving a US natural-gas boom

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY In May 2025, my colleague David Rotman wrote a feature story describing how AI could keep us dependent on natural gas. Just 18 months later, I’ve lost count of how many times he’s already been proved right. Sure, it’s technically possible to meet the world’s rising electricity demand with other power sources, including renewables like wind and solar supported by batteries. But the reality in the US so far is that the data center boom is helping to fuel a massive natural-gas resurgence.

执行摘要：2025年5月，我的同事大卫·罗特曼（David Rotman）撰写了一篇专题报道，描述了人工智能如何使我们持续依赖天然气。仅仅18个月后，我已经数不清他被证明是正确的次数了。当然，从技术上讲，通过包括风能和太阳能等由电池支持的可再生能源在内的其他电源来满足全球日益增长的电力需求是可能的。但到目前为止，美国的现实情况是，数据中心的繁荣正在助推天然气的巨大复苏。

Just one example: Plans for the Meta data center in Louisiana that David focused on in his story have expanded, and its power needs have too. The local utility went from three planned natural-gas plants to 10. And as I wrote in a new story this week, data center developers are now looking beyond the grid and planning to build their own off-grid gas power plants. How did we get here? If you want to understand the future of the grid, one way to do it is by looking at interconnection queues—lists of power plants that have requested a connection to the system.

仅举一例：大卫在报道中重点关注的路易斯安那州Meta数据中心的计划已经扩大，其电力需求也随之增加。当地公用事业公司将计划建设的天然气发电厂从3座增加到了10座。正如我本周在一篇新报道中所写的那样，数据中心开发商现在正着眼于电网之外，计划建设自己的离网天然气发电厂。我们是如何走到这一步的？如果你想了解电网的未来，观察互联队列（即申请接入系统的发电厂名单）是一种方法。

In recent years these queues have been dominated by renewables like wind and solar power, which are generally the cheapest way to build new energy capacity today. But there’s been a shift in some places, particularly for PJM, the country’s largest power grid operator, which covers parts of 13 states in an area stretching从 Illinois to Washington, DC. PJM has seen a major uptick in natural-gas proposals. In August, the operator announced that it had accepted proposals for 715 total projects with a capacity of over 200 gigawatts to join its queue. Nearly half that total capacity represented natural-gas plants.

近年来，这些队列一直以风能和太阳能等可再生能源为主，它们通常是当今建设新能源产能最便宜的方式。但在某些地方，情况发生了转变，特别是对于美国最大的电网运营商PJM而言，该运营商覆盖了从伊利诺伊州到华盛顿特区一带的13个州的部分地区。PJM的天然气项目提案大幅增加。8月，该运营商宣布已接受总计715个项目、容量超过200吉瓦的提案加入其队列。其中近一半的总容量来自天然气发电厂。

If all the projects in the queue were built, it would increase the fossil-fuel share on PJM’s grid from 61% to 67%, according to an analysis by Kevin Kircher, an energy modeler and assistant professor of mechanical engineering at Purdue. PJM’s queue is reflective of a bigger trend across the US. Natural gas is already the single biggest source of electricity in the country, and it has basically become the default to meet exploding power demand.

根据普渡大学能源建模师兼机械工程助理教授凯文·基尔彻（Kevin Kircher）的分析，如果队列中的所有项目都建成，PJM电网中化石燃料的份额将从61%增加到67%。PJM的队列反映了美国的一个更大趋势。天然气已经是美国最大的单一电力来源，并且基本上已成为满足爆炸式电力需求的默认选择。

This AI-driven rush is already affecting greenhouse-gas emissions for tech companies. Meta’s emissions are up 45% over last year’s, according to the company’s recently released 2026 Sustainability Report. That’s even higher than the jumps from fellow hyperscalers Google (18%), Amazon (16%), and Microsoft (25%). The company is also “reevaluating” its 2030 goal to reach net-zero emissions, according to the report, though there aren’t details about how or when it might reach a decision there.

这种由人工智能驱动的浪潮已经影响了科技公司的温室气体排放。根据Meta公司最近发布的《2026年可持续发展报告》，其排放量比去年增加了45%。这甚至高于其他超大规模云服务商谷歌（18%）、亚马逊（16%）和微软（25%）的增幅。报告称，该公司还在“重新评估”其2030年实现净零排放的目标，尽管目前尚无关于其如何或何时做出决定的细节。

And there’s another tidbit in that Meta report worth noting: The company said that when the grid can’t deliver power “on the necessary timeline,” it will look to other methods, including building its own on-site power generation. This fits into a related and growing trend that I’ve been tracking recently: Data center developers are looking to bypass long wait times to hook up to the grid by building their own power plants instead.

Meta的报告中还有一点值得注意：该公司表示，当电网无法“在必要的时间表内”供电时，它将寻求其他方法，包括建设自己的现场发电设施。这符合我最近一直在追踪的一个相关且日益增长的趋势：数据中心开发商正寻求通过建设自己的发电厂来绕过接入电网的漫长等待时间。

It’s not new for large industrial facilities to supply their own power. Chemical plants often have natural-gas infrastructure on-site to generate electricity and heat, for example (though they’re generally connected to the grid as well). Mining operations are often remote, requiring an off-grid power source. But the desire to come online quickly means there could be a massive boom in off-grid power plants for data centers. Over 100 such facilities have been proposed in the US, with a total capacity of 115 gigawatts (that’s more than double the entire peak power demand of California). The vast majority of these sites have been proposed since the beginning of 2025.

大型工业设施自行供电并不新鲜。例如，化工厂通常在现场拥有天然气基础设施来产生电力和热能（尽管它们通常也连接到电网）。采矿作业通常位于偏远地区，需要离网电源。但快速上线的需求意味着数据中心的离网发电厂可能会出现大规模繁荣。美国已提议建设超过100个此类设施，总容量为115吉瓦（这超过了加利福尼亚州整个峰值电力需求的两倍）。绝大多数此类站点都是自2025年初以来提出的。

There are alternative ways to help meet growing electricity demand: Wind and solar are inexpensive options, and pairing them with batteries could improve their reliability, for example. I’ve also written before about demand flexibility—efforts to limit power drawn from the grid when demand is high. Right now, though, the evidence continues to stack up that the AI boom in the US is basically synonymous with a natural-gas boom.

还有其他方法可以帮助满足日益增长的电力需求：例如，风能和太阳能是廉价的选择，将它们与电池配对可以提高其可靠性。我之前也写过关于需求灵活性——即在需求高时限制从电网获取电力的努力。然而，目前的证据不断堆积，表明美国的人工智能繁荣基本上等同于天然气繁荣。
