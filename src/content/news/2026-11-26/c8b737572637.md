Some customers have raised concerns about Backblaze’s privacy policy taking effect next month, which says the backup and cloud storage company may sell certain personal information to advertisers.

一些客户对 Backblaze 即将于下个月生效的隐私政策表示担忧，该政策称这家备份和云存储公司可能会向广告商出售某些个人信息。

Backblaze’s current privacy policy says that in addition to the personal information that users may provide, such as their email address when creating an account, it may also “automatically” collect information, such as “your software, the operating system you use when accessing our service, your Internet Protocol address, and the date and time of each request you make to Backblaze.”

Backblaze 目前的隐私政策称，除了用户在创建账户时可能提供的个人信息（如电子邮件地址）外，它还可能“自动”收集信息，例如“您的软件、您访问我们服务时使用的操作系统、您的互联网协议地址，以及您向 Backblaze 发出的每次请求的日期和时间”。

The current policy adds: “Under no circumstances do we rent, trade, or share your address or e-mail address with any other company for their marketing purposes without your consent.” It also makes no mention of advertisers.

目前的政策补充道：“在未经您同意的情况下，我们绝不会为了任何其他公司的营销目的而租用、交易或分享您的地址或电子邮件地址。”该政策也没有提及广告商。

On December 1, a new privacy policy takes effect. It includes a table listing the types of “general personal information” that Backblaze says it has been collecting and sharing with third parties, including “advertising and marketing partners” since December 1, 2025.

12 月 1 日，一项新的隐私政策将生效。其中包含一个表格，列出了 Backblaze 自 2025 年 12 月 1 日起一直在收集并与包括“广告和营销合作伙伴”在内的第三方共享的“一般个人信息”类型。

This information includes identifiers (such as “name, address, Internet Protocol address, [and] email address”); commercial information (such as “records of personal property, products or services purchased, obtained, or considered, or other purchasing or consuming histories or tendencies”; geolocation data that “can be used to determine a device’s physical location”; and “sensory or surveillance data” (which includes “call recordings or other audio, electronic, or visual monitoring or surveillance”).

这些信息包括标识符（如“姓名、地址、互联网协议地址 [和] 电子邮件地址”）；商业信息（如“个人财产记录、购买、获得或考虑的产品或服务，或其他购买或消费历史或倾向”）；可用于“确定设备物理位置”的地理位置数据；以及“感官或监控数据”（包括“通话录音或其他音频、电子或视觉监控”）。

This has concerned some users, but a Backblaze spokesperson told Ars Technica that “Backblaze does not sell or share sensory or surveillance data, such as call recordings, for targeted advertising, and we do not disclose it to advertising or marketing partners.”

这引起了一些用户的担忧，但 Backblaze 的一位发言人告诉 Ars Technica：“Backblaze 不会为了定向广告而出售或分享感官或监控数据（如通话录音），我们也不会将其披露给广告或营销合作伙伴。”

They added: The Privacy Policy table lists one set of recipients across all data categories, which is why it can read differently. Our legal team will review the table for clarity as part of our next review cycle.

他们补充道：隐私政策表格在所有数据类别中列出了一组接收者，这就是为什么它读起来可能会有不同理解的原因。我们的法律团队将在下一个审查周期中对该表格进行审查，以确保其清晰度。

Notably, no “sensitive personal information,” such as social security numbers, credit card numbers, or “precise geolocation,” is shared with advertisers, the policy states. However, identifiers, commercial information, “Internet or Other Similar Network Activity,” and “geolocation data” may be “sold or shared for targeted Advertising.”

该政策指出，值得注意的是，没有任何“敏感个人信息”（如社会安全号码、信用卡号码或“精确地理位置”）会与广告商共享。然而，标识符、商业信息、“互联网或其他类似网络活动”以及“地理位置数据”可能会被“出售或共享用于定向广告”。

Critically, the upcoming policy “only applies to the information collected in respect of your account and not to the content that you store on our system.” Backblaze’s broader terms of service also state that: “Files stored using Backblaze Computer Backup are encrypted prior to being sent to us.”

关键在于，即将生效的政策“仅适用于与您的账户相关的信息，而不适用于您存储在我们系统上的内容。”Backblaze 更广泛的服务条款也声明：“使用 Backblaze 计算机备份存储的文件在发送给我们之前已进行加密。”

However, some customers remain concerned that the privacy policy doesn’t more clearly and distinctly explain that Backblaze won’t share or sell content from customers’ backup files. In response, Backblaze’s representative explained: This is because the Privacy Notice’s sale and sharing disclosures relate to website and marketing data, such as information collected through cookies and similar technologies on our site. They do not apply to files customers store with us.

然而，一些客户仍然担心隐私政策没有更清晰、明确地解释 Backblaze 不会分享或出售客户备份文件中的内容。对此，Backblaze 的代表解释说：这是因为隐私声明中的出售和共享披露涉及网站和营销数据，例如通过我们网站上的 Cookie 和类似技术收集的信息。它们不适用于客户存储在我们这里的文件。

Renatta Siewert, Backblaze’s director of communications and brand programs, reiterated to Ars that Backblaze doesn’t share the contents of backup or storage files with advertisers: Backblaze takes the privacy and security of our customers’ data seriously. We updated our Privacy Notice to keep pace with the changing legal landscape, which increasingly requires companies to spell out how they collect and use marketing-related information.

Backblaze 通信与品牌项目总监 Renatta Siewert 向 Ars 重申，Backblaze 不会与广告商共享备份或存储文件的内容：Backblaze 非常重视客户数据的隐私和安全。我们更新了隐私声明以跟上不断变化的法律环境，该环境日益要求公司明确说明其如何收集和使用与营销相关的信息。

This update gives customers a clearer, more specific view of what we collect and why. These disclosures are separate from the files customers entrust to Backblaze for backup and storage, which we do not sell or share with advertisers, data brokers, or analytics partners. Customers can use this link to opt out of the selling or sharing of their personal information for cross-context behavioral advertising.

此次更新让客户更清晰、更具体地了解我们收集的内容及其原因。这些披露与客户委托给 Backblaze 进行备份和存储的文件是分开的，我们不会将这些文件出售或分享给广告商、数据经纪人或分析合作伙伴。客户可以使用此链接选择退出为跨上下文行为广告而进行的个人信息出售或共享。
