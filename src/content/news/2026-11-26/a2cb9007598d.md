# Shop Small: Our Favorite Gifts From Independent Businesses (2026)

Save this story. We get it—Ordering from Amazon is easy. But aside from being community anchors in their respective towns and neighborhoods, small, independent businesses offer some critical things the big guys don’t: curation and specialization. If you don't know anything about stationery, it helps to buy it from someone who lives and breathes fountain pens and Japanese notebooks. You may not want to sort through the millions of toys on Walmart's website, but a mom who really knows toys can help you find just the right gift for just the right age.

保存这篇文章。我们明白，在亚马逊上下单确实很方便。但除了作为各自城镇和社区的支柱外，小型独立企业还提供了一些大型平台所不具备的关键优势：精选与专业化。如果你对文具一窍不通，那么从那些沉浸于钢笔和日本笔记本世界的人那里购买会大有裨益。你可能不想在沃尔玛网站上数以百万计的玩具中进行筛选，但一位真正了解玩具的妈妈可以帮你为合适的年龄找到最合适的礼物。

For Small Business Saturday, November 28, and throughout the holiday shopping season, consider shopping at an independent business, whether in your own city or via one of the links below. Each store is one that someone on our Reviews team has visited in person or bought something from and loved. Some are in our hometowns, some we happened upon on vacation, and others are places we regularly shop. Here’s what we’d buy.

在11月28日的“小企业星期六”以及整个假日购物季期间，请考虑在独立企业购物，无论是在你所在的城市，还是通过下方的链接。我们测评团队的成员都曾亲自到访过这些商店，或者购买过他们的产品并非常喜爱。其中一些位于我们的家乡，一些是我们度假时偶然发现的，还有一些是我们经常光顾的地方。以下是我们推荐购买的商品。

### Our Favorite Planner for Pen People
### 我们为笔友挑选的最爱手账

Yoseka Stationery; Brooklyn, New York. This locally owned Greenpoint shop is recommended by Gear editor Julian Chokkattu as being a mecca for writers and sketchers. Its brick-lined walls hold shelves’ worth of every type of pen, paper, and notebook imaginable, with an emphasis on Asian stationery like Hobonichi's popular Techo paper planners (which we highly recommend). Yoseka’s Shop store allows customers to buy pens individually, so you can put together a sampler or select only the colors you know your recipient will want.

Yoseka Stationery（纽约布鲁克林）。这家位于绿点区（Greenpoint）的本地商店被装备编辑Julian Chokkattu推荐为作家和素描爱好者的圣地。其砖墙内陈列着各种你能想象到的笔、纸和笔记本，重点推崇如Hobonichi流行的Techo纸质手账（我们强烈推荐）等亚洲文具。Yoseka的网店允许顾客单独购买钢笔，因此你可以组合成一个样品套装，或者只挑选你确定收礼人会喜欢的颜色。

### A Vase for Your Best Bud
### 送给挚友的花瓶

Heath Ceramics; Sausalito, California. Founded in 1948 by Edith and Brian Heath, Heath Ceramics still makes the same ceramic dinnerware, vases, and tiles the founders made back then, which are fully back in style now due to their midcentury modern flair. As a fifth-generation San Francisco Bay Area native, I still have a set of green Heath cereal bowls my grandmother bought when the Sausalito showroom—which is still in operation and is still attached to the factory—was relatively new.

Heath Ceramics（加州索萨利托）。Heath Ceramics由Edith和Brian Heath于1948年创立，至今仍在生产创始人当年制作的同款陶瓷餐具、花瓶和瓷砖。由于其现代主义中世纪风格，这些产品如今又重新流行起来。作为旧金山湾区的第五代原住民，我至今仍保留着一套绿色的Heath麦片碗，那是我祖母在索萨利托展厅（至今仍在运营，且仍与工厂相连）刚开业不久时买的。

### Some Assembly Encouraged
### 鼓励动手组装

Treehouse Kid and Craft; Athens, Georgia. WIRED operations manager Scott Gilbertson loves this locally owned toy store and community space known for its quirky and original toys, from vintage-inspired matchbox stuffies to keychain-sized digital cameras and retro candy. (There’s also a sister location, Clubhouse Kid and Craft, just outside of Atlanta, though that one has different owners.) As a side note, I knew few kids—including my own—who haven't loved Magna-Tiles.

Treehouse Kid and Craft（佐治亚州雅典）。WIRED运营经理Scott Gilbertson非常喜欢这家本地玩具店和社区空间，它以古怪且原创的玩具而闻名，从复古风格的火柴盒毛绒玩具到钥匙扣大小的数码相机和怀旧糖果，应有尽有。（在亚特兰大郊外还有一家姊妹店Clubhouse Kid and Craft，不过那家店的所有者不同。）顺便提一下，我认识的孩子——包括我自己的孩子——几乎没有不喜欢Magna-Tiles磁力片的。

### Old-School Stitch for Modern Gear
### 现代装备的复古缝制

Kai D Utility; Brooklyn, New York. Part of the growing “slow fashion” movement, this vintage-inspired boutique sells shirts, pants, jackets, and even underwear made painstakingly by hand right in Brooklyn and meant to last a lifetime. It's mostly men's garments online and accessories like the waxed-cotton pouch above, though there are some women's cashmere coats in the physical store.

Kai D Utility（纽约布鲁克林）。作为日益增长的“慢时尚”运动的一部分，这家复古风格的精品店销售在布鲁克林手工精心制作的衬衫、裤子、夹克甚至内衣，旨在陪伴你一生。其网店主要销售男装和如上文提到的蜡棉小袋等配饰，不过实体店里也有一些女士羊绒大衣。

### The Original Carry-All
### 原创万能包

Fair and Square Imports; McKinney, Texas. Though I live in San Diego, one of my best friends lives in the Dallas area near McKinney. Walking around the adorable historic downtown is one of our favorite pastimes when I visit, and a shop we always find ourselves back at is Fair and Square Imports. The shop is focused on fair trade goods, working with artisan cooperatives and Fair Trade partners from around the world. As you peruse the shop, you’ll find all kinds of home goods, ranging from ornaments for the holidays, beautiful home pieces, bath goods, kids’ books, and so much more.

Fair and Square Imports（得克萨斯州麦金尼）。虽然我住在圣地亚哥，但我最好的朋友之一住在达拉斯附近的麦金尼。每当我去看望她时，在迷人的历史街区漫步是我们最喜欢的消遣方式，而我们总是会回到Fair and Square Imports这家店。该店专注于公平贸易商品，与世界各地的工匠合作社和公平贸易伙伴合作。当你浏览这家店时，你会发现各种各样的家居用品，从节日装饰品、精美的家居摆件、沐浴用品到儿童书籍等等，应有尽有。

### A Full House in Full Color
### 满屋缤纷色彩

Woonwinkel; Portland, Oregon. Owned by a local color designer, this downtown Portland gift shop is hard to miss with its cheery window displays and decidedly mod vibe. It features all manner of colorful art and gifts by independent artists; Woonwinkel's website lets you sort selections by Black-owned, POC-owned, USA-made, and eco-friendly, though the Shop site does not. In any case, if you or a giftee likes bright, stylish things with lots of color, this is the place.

Woonwinkel（俄勒冈州波特兰）。这家位于波特兰市中心的礼品店由一位当地色彩设计师拥有，其欢快的橱窗展示和鲜明的现代风格让人过目难忘。店里陈列着各种由独立艺术家创作的色彩斑斓的艺术品和礼品；Woonwinkel的网站允许你按“黑人拥有”、“有色人种拥有”、“美国制造”和“环保”等类别筛选商品，尽管其购物页面本身并不具备此功能。总之，如果你或收礼人喜欢明亮、时尚且色彩丰富的物品，这里就是你的不二之选。
