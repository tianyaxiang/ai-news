# Tidal bans AI-generated music

The music streaming service Tidal said Tuesday that it would ban tracks it believes are completely AI-generated starting next month, a move its leader believes “will resonate with both artists and listeners alike.”

音乐流媒体服务 Tidal 周二表示，从下个月开始，将禁止其认为完全由人工智能生成的曲目。其负责人认为，此举“将引起艺术家和听众的共鸣”。

The service will remove the AI tracks starting Dec. 9, making Tidal the first music streaming platform to ban AI music entirely. It comes nearly five months after Tidal said it would demonetize any AI-powered song, force those artists to label their tracks as either AI-generated or AI-assisted and ban AI songs that were associated with “fraudulent activity,” a category that includes songs impersonating established artists and efforts to “deceive listeners.”

该服务将于 12 月 9 日起移除这些人工智能曲目，使 Tidal 成为首个全面禁止人工智能音乐的流媒体平台。此前近五个月，Tidal 曾表示将取消任何人工智能驱动歌曲的获利资格，强制艺术家将其曲目标记为“人工智能生成”或“人工智能辅助”，并禁止与“欺诈活动”相关的人工智能歌曲——此类活动包括冒充知名艺术家的歌曲以及旨在“欺骗听众”的行为。

“But that’s enough, as 43% of tracks distributed to us in the last 90 days have been detected as AI-generated. Something more drastic needs to be done,” wrote Robert Andersen, the Tidal Lead at parent company Block, in an X thread on Tuesday. “We’ll begin removing all wholly AI-generated tracks from our catalog, and you’ll see them completely gone by the beginning of the year.”

“但这已经足够了，因为在过去 90 天里，分发给我们的曲目中有 43% 被检测为人工智能生成。必须采取更严厉的措施，”母公司 Block 的 Tidal 负责人罗伯特·安德森（Robert Andersen）周二在 X 平台上写道。“我们将开始从目录中删除所有完全由人工智能生成的曲目，到年初时，你们将看到它们被彻底清除。”

Andersen said that 96% of all AI-generated songs receive zero plays. The move will cleave the platform of 29 million tracks, he added, and have it reject between 80,000 to 85,000 songs a day.

安德森表示，所有人工智能生成的歌曲中，有 96% 的播放量为零。他补充说，此举将使该平台减少 2900 万首曲目，并使其每天拒绝 8 万至 8.5 万首歌曲。

“We encourage our peers to follow suit,” he wrote.

“我们鼓励同行效仿，”他写道。

The move comes as competitors such as Spotify and Apple Music continue to test approaches to combat the rise of AI-generated tracks proliferating on their platforms, many of which the services have labeled some version of “slop” or “spam.” Apple Music said it would also begin labeling AI-generated or AI-assisted tracks on its platform, while Spotify said it would label artists it deemed AI creations as an “AI Persona.”

此举正值 Spotify 和 Apple Music 等竞争对手继续测试应对其平台上激增的人工智能生成曲目的方法之际，这些曲目中有许多被服务商标记为某种形式的“垃圾内容”或“垃圾信息”。Apple Music 表示也将开始在其平台上标记人工智能生成或辅助的曲目，而 Spotify 则表示会将它认为属于人工智能创作的艺术家标记为“人工智能角色”（AI Persona）。

Spotify said in a blog post last month it also removed 50 million slop or spam tracks from users’ recommendations this year, which came after it removed 75 million of those songs from its catalog entirely. The revelation came among a number of updates regarding Spotify’s efforts to combat AI spam.

Spotify 上个月在一篇博客文章中表示，今年还从用户的推荐中删除了 5000 万首垃圾内容或垃圾信息曲目，此前它已从其目录中彻底删除了 7500 万首此类歌曲。这一披露是 Spotify 为打击人工智能垃圾信息所做努力的一系列更新的一部分。

“This work isn’t finished,” Spotify added. “AI moves fast, and bad actors adapt just as fast. But as AI makes music more abundant, we believe listeners will value human artistry even more. Our job is to make Spotify the place they find it.”

“这项工作还没有结束，”Spotify 补充道。“人工智能发展迅速，不良行为者的适应速度也同样快。但随着人工智能使音乐变得更加丰富，我们相信听众会更加珍视人类的艺术创作。我们的工作就是让 Spotify 成为他们寻找这些艺术创作的地方。”
