# Etsy clarifies AI terms after seller backlash

After backlash from sellers frustrated by Etsy’s new AI policies, which appeared to allow the company to train AI on user artwork, Etsy has now clarified its stance. In a note published on Monday, CEO Kruti Patel Goyal discussed recent tweaks to the company’s terms of service and admitted that her team “should’ve given you more context on what the updates meant for your creative content.”

在 Etsy 的新人工智能政策引发卖家不满（这些政策似乎允许公司利用用户艺术作品训练人工智能）后，Etsy 现已澄清其立场。在周一发布的一份说明中，首席执行官 Kruti Patel Goyal 讨论了公司服务条款的近期调整，并承认她的团队“本应就这些更新对你们的创意内容意味着什么提供更多背景信息”。

“Our recent terms update was meant to reflect the evolving technologies we’re using to build better tools and experiences for sellers and the Etsy community,” Goyal wrote this week. “It was not meant to enable us to train AI models for the purpose of creating copycats of your items. We do not and will not do that.”

“我们最近的条款更新旨在反映我们正在使用的不断发展的技术，以构建更好的工具和体验，服务于卖家和 Etsy 社区，”Goyal 本周写道。“这并非为了让我们能够训练人工智能模型来制造你们商品的仿制品。我们没有，也不会那样做。”

Aaron Meshon, a longtime professional illustrator and Etsy seller, told Ars that even though the company had dialed back its AI language somewhat, the changes were mere “sugar coating.” He said he still plans on shutting down his 15-year-old Etsy store on December 1. “I would say I’m losing 20 percent of my yearly income,” he said. “It’s a few mortgage payments.”

长期担任专业插画师和 Etsy 卖家的 Aaron Meshon 告诉 Ars，尽管该公司在一定程度上缩减了其人工智能相关的措辞，但这些改变仅仅是“粉饰太平”。他说，他仍计划在 12 月 1 日关闭他经营了 15 年的 Etsy 店铺。“我想我会损失 20% 的年收入，”他说。“那可是好几笔抵押贷款的钱。”

He noted that Etsy’s announcement also seems to be rather tone-deaf, given that many retailers make a substantial portion of their annual revenue in the run-up to the end-of-year holiday season. “This is the worst two-to-three weeks out of the year to do this, bar none,” he said.

他指出，考虑到许多零售商在年底假期前夕会获得大部分年度收入，Etsy 的公告似乎相当不合时宜。“这是全年最糟糕的两到三周，毫无疑问，”他说。

### AI training

### 人工智能训练

The fracas began earlier this month, when the company first proposed new contractual language. The updated terms read: This also includes the right for us and our third party partners and vendors to use Your Content to develop, train, test and improve artificial intelligence and machine learning technologies, including to improve the Services and/or help keep them safe, subject to any applicable opt-out rights described in our Privacy Policy.

这场风波始于本月初，当时该公司首次提出了新的合同条款。更新后的条款写道：这也包括我们及我们的第三方合作伙伴和供应商有权使用您的内容来开发、训练、测试和改进人工智能及机器学习技术，包括改进服务和/或帮助保障其安全，但须遵守我们隐私政策中描述的任何适用退出权利。

As a result, a slew of vendors pulled up stakes, with some saying they had “burned their Etsy shop to the ground” or that they “just killed our Etsy store after 13 years.” Etsy’s change is reminiscent of a recent dust-up involving Meta, where the tech giant had wanted to “require Meta Business account users to allow extensive AI training on external websites linked on Facebook.” Meta then indefinitely paused that move.

结果，大批卖家撤离，一些人表示他们已经“彻底烧毁了他们的 Etsy 店铺”，或者“在经营 13 年后刚刚关闭了我们的 Etsy 店铺”。Etsy 的这一变动让人想起最近涉及 Meta 的一场争端，当时这家科技巨头曾希望“要求 Meta 商业账户用户允许在 Facebook 链接的外部网站上进行广泛的人工智能训练”。Meta 随后无限期暂停了该举措。

Last month, Etsy also told sellers that it would “set clearer restrictions on AI use,” despite the fact that AI-generated items remain for sale all over the site. “Today, many active buyers say they can’t tell which items involve AI-generated content,” James Ossman, the company’s vice president of customer operations, wrote on October 22. “It’s become a top-three barrier to shopping more often on Etsy.”

上个月，Etsy 还告诉卖家，它将“对人工智能的使用设定更明确的限制”，尽管事实上人工智能生成的商品在网站上随处可见。公司客户运营副总裁 James Ossman 在 10 月 22 日写道：“今天，许多活跃买家表示，他们无法分辨哪些商品涉及人工智能生成的内容。这已成为阻碍他们在 Etsy 上更频繁购物的三大障碍之一。”
