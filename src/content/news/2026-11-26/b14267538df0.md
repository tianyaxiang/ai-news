Kagi, a small, Palo Alto, California-based software company that provides a subscription-based search engine as an alternative to Google, has launched a Gmail-like e-mail service. Dubbed Kagi Mail, it makes many of the same claims as the search engine product: privacy, personal data sovereignty, customization, and control. Additionally, Kagi is rolling out a calendar as part of the mail service.

Kagi 是一家位于加利福尼亚州帕洛阿尔托的小型软件公司，提供基于订阅的搜索引擎作为 Google 的替代品，该公司现已推出类似 Gmail 的电子邮件服务。该服务被称为 Kagi Mail，其主打卖点与搜索引擎产品基本一致：隐私保护、个人数据主权、定制化和自主控制。此外，Kagi 还随邮件服务一同推出了日历功能。

Right now, both the email and calendar interfaces are web-only, but native Android and iOS apps are planned for the fourth quarter of next year. The email interface, currently in public beta, is minimalist. It emphasizes keyboard commands, as well as a private and ad-free experience. (You can see more details in Kagi’s blog post about what they mean by private.)

目前，电子邮件和日历界面仅支持网页版，但原生 Android 和 iOS 应用程序计划于明年第四季度推出。目前处于公测阶段的电子邮件界面设计极简，强调键盘快捷键操作，并提供私密且无广告的体验。（关于 Kagi 对“私密”的定义，可在其博客文章中查看更多详情。）

Other feature offerings include unlimited aliases and domains, an anti-lock-in policy for user data, 50GB of storage, rule-based filtering with Sieve, an MCP server, and several optional, off-by-default AI features, like chatting with an agent to search email history, do large-scale re-labeling, or define additional filtering rules. Some major features are still missing, though. As noted, there are no native apps yet. Critically, there is no contacts address book, but that is also coming in 2027 (Q1 in this case) according to the roadmap.

其他功能包括无限别名和域名、防止用户数据锁定的政策、50GB 存储空间、基于 Sieve 的规则过滤、MCP 服务器，以及几项默认关闭的可选 AI 功能，例如与智能助手对话以搜索邮件历史、进行大规模重新标记或定义额外的过滤规则。不过，一些主要功能目前仍然缺失。如前所述，尚无原生应用程序。关键的是，目前还没有联系人地址簿，但根据路线图，该功能将于 2027 年第一季度推出。

Like the search engine, Kagi Mail is a paid service. It’s not included in the $5 per month basic Kagi plan, nor is it in the $10 per month unlimited search plan. It is, however, included in the $25 per month Kagi Ultimate subscription, which also offers frontier AI models. If you want to get Kagi Mail without the $25 per month subscription, you can subscribe for $9 per month or $99 per year, and there’s a 60-day trial right now.

与搜索引擎一样，Kagi Mail 是一项付费服务。它不包含在每月 5 美元的基础 Kagi 套餐或每月 10 美元的无限搜索套餐中。不过，它包含在每月 25 美元的 Kagi Ultimate 订阅中，该订阅还提供前沿 AI 模型。如果您不想订阅每月 25 美元的套餐，也可以选择每月 9 美元或每年 99 美元的单独订阅，目前提供 60 天试用期。

Opinion: An alternative more of us should maybe consider. That’s a lot to pay for email when Gmail has historically been such a strong product. To most people, it seems ridiculous to imagine paying for a subscription, and that’s understandable. According to the company, Kagi Search has just over 75,000 paying subscribers. Compared to the number of people using Google Search or even Bing or DuckDuckGo, that’s not much.

观点：一个我们或许应该考虑的替代方案。考虑到 Gmail 历来是一款非常强大的产品，为电子邮件支付如此高的费用确实不少。对大多数人来说，为电子邮件付费似乎很荒谬，这也可以理解。据该公司称，Kagi Search 目前拥有超过 75,000 名付费订阅用户。与使用 Google 搜索，甚至 Bing 或 DuckDuckGo 的用户数量相比，这并不算多。

But the pitch is that with Google, the user is the product, not the customer, and that paying anywhere from $5 to $25 per month (there are multiple plans; unlimited search queries is in the $10 per month plan) helps users escape from that devil’s bargain. Kagi also argues that Google’s search results—and increasingly its email as well—are polluted or corrupted by ad sales incentives.

但其核心主张是：在使用 Google 时，用户是产品而非客户；而每月支付 5 到 25 美元（有多种套餐可选；每月 10 美元的套餐包含无限搜索查询）可以帮助用户摆脱这种“魔鬼交易”。Kagi 还认为，Google 的搜索结果——以及日益严重的电子邮件服务——正受到广告销售激励的污染或破坏。

On that, several Ars staffers agree—my colleague Lee Hutchinson wrote at length about why he ditched Google Search for Kagi a year ago. A couple other Ars staffers have since made the same move, myself included—but to be fair, search is a very big part of our day-to-day work, and that’s not true to the same degree for everybody. The pitch for email is a little harder, because most people probably don’t perceive as much impact of Google’s business incentives on Gmail.

对此，几位 Ars 的员工表示赞同——我的同事 Lee Hutchinson 曾详细撰文讲述了他一年前为何放弃 Google 搜索转而使用 Kagi。此后，包括我在内的其他几位 Ars 员工也做出了同样的举动。但平心而论，搜索是我们日常工作中非常重要的一部分，而这对每个人来说并非同等重要。推广电子邮件服务的难度稍大，因为大多数人可能并没有感受到 Google 的商业激励对 Gmail 产生了多大影响。

That has maybe changed recently, as Google has begun inserting Gemini into Gmail aggressively, but Kagi has AI features, too—the difference is that they’re generally more conservative and sensible. (You can disable either Gemini in Gmail or Kagi’s AI features in Kagi Mail, too.) Email is also different because there are already more direct competitors to Kagi Mail. The most popular in this category is Proton Mail, which goes even further on privacy and security with end-to-end encryption, but that company is embroiled in controversies right now. There’s also Tuta, which Kagi specifically flags and praises in its blog post as a good existing alternative. Based on a few hours of use, Kagi Mail seems like it’s going in the right direction, but it will need refinement to permanently unseat Gmail.

这种情况最近可能有所改变，因为 Google 已开始积极地将 Gemini 植入 Gmail，但 Kagi 也有 AI 功能——不同之处在于，Kagi 的功能通常更保守、更合理。（您也可以选择禁用 Gmail 中的 Gemini 或 Kagi Mail 中的 AI 功能。）电子邮件领域的情况也不同，因为 Kagi Mail 已经面临更多直接竞争对手。该类别中最受欢迎的是 Proton Mail，它通过端到端加密在隐私和安全方面走得更远，但该公司目前正陷入争议之中。此外还有 Tuta，Kagi 在其博客文章中特别指出并称赞其为一个不错的现有替代方案。经过几个小时的使用，Kagi Mail 似乎正朝着正确的方向发展，但它仍需进一步完善才能彻底取代 Gmail。
