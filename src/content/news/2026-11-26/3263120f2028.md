### Best iPhone Duo Cases
Creating cases for foldables is challenging for case makers, and they inevitably change the iPhone Duo’s feel significantly. These are the best iPhone Duo cases I’ve tested so far. Whether you prefer a thin case or demand chunky hinge protection, there’s an option here for you, but I will likely test and add more cases over the next few months.

### 最佳 iPhone Duo 保护壳
为折叠屏手机制造保护壳对厂商来说充满挑战，它们不可避免地会显著改变 iPhone Duo 的手感。这些是我目前测试过的最好的 iPhone Duo 保护壳。无论你喜欢轻薄款还是需要厚实的铰链保护，这里都有适合你的选择，但我可能会在未来几个月内测试并增加更多的保护壳。

### Best Case: dbrand Grip Case
If your main worry is dropping your phone, this Dbrand case is the grippiest I’ve tried. It’s also thick enough to provide decent drop protection without being so thick that it’s hard to use Touch ID on the power button or access the USB-C port. Protection includes a raised lip around the cover screen, a molded cover for the camera module that doesn’t interfere with image capture, and a proper hinge cover. It also works just fine with Qi2 and MagSafe chargers, and other magnetic accessories.

### 最佳保护壳：dbrand Grip Case
如果你最担心的是手机掉落，这款 Dbrand 保护壳是我试过防滑性最好的。它的厚度足以提供不错的防摔保护，同时又不会厚到让你难以使用电源键上的触控 ID 或访问 USB-C 接口。保护功能包括覆盖屏幕周围的凸起边缘、不影响拍摄的模压摄像头模块盖，以及一个合适的铰链盖。它与 Qi2 和 MagSafe 充电器以及其他磁吸配件也能完美兼容。

### Best Stand Case: Moft Dynamic Folio
Famous for its origami-style folding folios, cases, and wallets, Moft is a brand I like. The Dynamic Folio works great because it’s a simple cover that attaches magnetically. It extends beyond the Duo’s dimensions, with a solid camera surround to provide a little protection. The cover opens and folds to act as a stand or serve as a comfortable grip. The stand works with the iPhone Duo open or closed in landscape or portrait, and is surprisingly stable.

### 最佳支架保护壳：Moft Dynamic Folio
Moft 以其折纸风格的折叠保护套、手机壳和钱包而闻名，是我喜欢的品牌。Dynamic Folio 的表现非常出色，因为它是一个通过磁吸连接的简单保护盖。它的尺寸超出了 Duo 本身，并配有坚固的摄像头边框以提供一定的保护。保护盖可以打开并折叠，既能作为支架，也能作为舒适的握把。无论 iPhone Duo 是处于打开还是闭合状态，横屏还是竖屏，支架都能使用，而且出奇地稳定。

### Best Slim Case: Mous Limitless Case
Slim and very good-looking, this Mous case has a back panel made with Aramid synthetic fiber, with a matte black surround that provides a little grip. It’s a one-piece design with its own flexible hinge (no adhesive required), and it doesn’t add too much bulk to the iPhone Duo. The buttons and port are easily accessible, and there’s a raised lip around the camera module and cover screen. The minimal hinge cover doesn’t interfere with opening and closing the phone.

### 最佳轻薄保护壳：Mous Limitless Case
这款 Mous 保护壳轻薄且外观精美，背板采用芳纶合成纤维制成，哑光黑色边框提供了一定的抓握力。它采用一体式设计，自带柔性铰链（无需粘合剂），不会给 iPhone Duo 增加太多负担。按键和接口易于使用，摄像头模块和外屏周围有凸起边缘。极简的铰链盖不会影响手机的开合。

### Best Thick Case: Smartish Gripzilla Case
Maybe the simplicity of a big, chunky, one-piece case appeals. Smartish has you covered with the Gripzilla. This iPhone Duo case protects the phone from every angle, with heavily reinforced corners providing serious drop protection and an indented hinge cover that makes it very comfortable and secure to hold. The finish is pretty damn grippy, though it doesn’t quite match Dbrand’s case above. It is thicker, though, with lips around the cover screen and camera.

### 最佳厚实保护壳：Smartish Gripzilla Case
也许你更喜欢那种大而厚实的一体式保护壳的简洁感。Smartish 的 Gripzilla 就能满足你。这款 iPhone Duo 保护壳从各个角度保护手机，加固的边角提供了强大的防摔保护，凹陷的铰链盖让握持非常舒适且稳固。其表面防滑性极佳，虽然略逊于上面提到的 Dbrand 保护壳，但它更厚，且在外屏和摄像头周围有凸起边缘。
