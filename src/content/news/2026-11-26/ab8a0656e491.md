WhatsApp’s new scheduled messages let you choose the right time to send

WhatsApp 新推出的定时发送消息功能让您可以选择合适的发送时间。

You can schedule WhatsApp texts you might forget to send up to two weeks ahead of time.

您可以提前两周设置 WhatsApp 消息的定时发送，以免遗忘。

WhatsApp’s 3 billion users are getting an option that Meta says is one of the most requested features, with the ability to schedule messages in advance for both private conversations and group chats. Most messaging platforms have had this for a very long time, but now it’s on WhatsApp too.

WhatsApp 的 30 亿用户即将获得一项 Meta 称之为“呼声最高”的功能，即能够为私人对话和群聊提前安排消息发送。大多数消息平台早已具备此功能，现在 WhatsApp 也终于跟进了。

If you think of a text at an inconvenient time or don’t want to forget to fire off a birthday message at 4AM, you can draft it and set it to send anywhere from 10 minutes to two weeks later. The feature works with photos and videos, too.

如果您在不方便的时候想到了要发的消息，或者不想忘记在凌晨 4 点发送生日祝福，您可以先起草好内容，并设置在 10 分钟到两周后的任意时间发送。该功能同样适用于照片和视频。

To schedule a message, press and hold the send button and pick the date and time you want it to arrive. It will look like any other message to the person receiving it. You can see all future messages you have lined up, and delete them beforehand if you change your mind.

要定时发送消息，请长按发送按钮，然后选择您希望消息送达的日期和时间。对于接收者来说，它看起来与普通消息无异。您可以查看所有已排程的未来消息，如果改变主意，也可以提前将其删除。

Even if your phone is off or out of battery, your scheduled message will still show up at the time you chose, and it remains end-to-end encrypted like all your personal messages, so no one else can read it, not even us.

即使您的手机关机或没电，定时消息仍会在您选择的时间发送。它与您所有的个人消息一样，保持端到端加密，因此除了您之外，没有任何人（包括我们）可以读取这些内容。

Scheduled messages work with text, photos and videos, and are rolling out globally over the coming weeks. It’s one more way to show up for the people who matter to you, now at exactly the right time.

定时消息功能支持文本、照片和视频，并将在未来几周内向全球推出。这是在恰当的时间向您在意的人表达关心的又一种方式。
