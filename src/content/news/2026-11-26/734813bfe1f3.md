# Amazon loses its latest attempt to get out of handling more recall notices

亚马逊在试图摆脱处理更多召回通知的最新尝试中败诉。

A court ruled in favor of the US CPSC’s argument that Amazon is responsible for recalled products that other companies sell on its platforms.

法院裁定支持美国消费品安全委员会（CPSC）的观点，即亚马逊应对其平台上其他公司销售的召回产品负责。

Amazon is on the hook to recall more than 400,000 hazardous products after losing its legal battle against the US Consumer Product Safety Commission (CPSC). The US District Court for the District of Maryland ruled in favor of the CPSC, which argued that Amazon is a distributor of goods, not a logistics provider, making it responsible for carrying out recalls of third-party products.

在输掉与美国消费品安全委员会（CPSC）的法律诉讼后，亚马逊必须召回超过 40 万件危险产品。美国马里兰州联邦地区法院裁定支持 CPSC，该委员会认为亚马逊是商品分销商而非物流提供商，因此有责任对第三方产品执行召回。

Amazon sued the CPSC in March 2025 after the agency ordered Amazon to recall over 400,000 “Fulfilled by Amazon” products that pose a “substantial product hazard,” including faulty carbon monoxide detectors, hair dryers without electrocution protection, and children’s sleepwear that doesn’t meet federal flammability standards.

2025 年 3 月，在 CPSC 命令亚马逊召回超过 40 万件存在“重大产品隐患”的“亚马逊物流”（Fulfilled by Amazon）产品后，亚马逊起诉了该机构。这些产品包括有缺陷的一氧化碳探测器、缺乏触电保护的吹风机，以及不符合联邦易燃性标准的儿童睡衣。

As a result, Amazon will be required to implement the following measures, initially outlined in the CPSC’s January 2025 order:

因此，亚马逊将被要求执行 CPSC 在 2025 年 1 月的命令中初步概述的以下措施：

Notification: On the day CPSC publishes the recall releases, Amazon must notify the public by posting the recall releases to Amazon.com; notify original purchasers directly via email and by providing information regarding the recalls on each purchaser’s “Your Orders” page. Amazon must provide one round of notice of the recalls to Fulfilled by Amazon participants that sold the subject products.

通知：在 CPSC 发布召回公告当天，亚马逊必须通过在 Amazon.com 上发布召回公告来通知公众；通过电子邮件直接通知原始购买者，并在每位购买者的“您的订单”页面上提供有关召回的信息。亚马逊必须向销售相关产品的“亚马逊物流”参与者提供一轮召回通知。

Incentive to Remove Products from Commerce: Amazon must issue a full refund in the amount of the purchase price to any consumer who submits proof of destruction or disposal of a subject product.

从商业流通中移除产品的激励措施：对于任何提交了相关产品销毁或处置证明的消费者，亚马逊必须按购买价格全额退款。

Maintaining Records of Recalls: Amazon must maintain the recall releases on the “Recalls and Product Safety Alerts” webpage on amazon.com for a minimum of five years. Amazon must submit monthly progress reports for five years and maintain records of its actions to comply with the Order for the same period.

保存召回记录：亚马逊必须在 amazon.com 的“召回和产品安全警报”网页上保留召回公告至少五年。亚马逊必须在五年内提交月度进度报告，并在此期间保留其遵守该命令的行动记录。

“We have a longstanding commitment to removing unsafe and recalled products from our store and notifying customers, backed by an industry-leading recalls process,” Amazon spokesperson Sammy Brill says in an emailed statement to The Verge. “We’re reviewing our options following the ruling and look forward to continuing to work with the CPSC to put customer safety first.”

“我们长期致力于从我们的商店中移除不安全和被召回的产品并通知客户，这有行业领先的召回流程作为支持，”亚马逊发言人萨米·布里尔（Sammy Brill）在给《The Verge》的一份电子邮件声明中表示。“我们正在审查裁决后的选择，并期待继续与 CPSC 合作，将客户安全放在首位。”
