One night in October, hackers staged one of the most unusual cyber attacks in history: cranking up the temperature in refrigeration systems at hundreds of supermarkets across France and destroying millions of euros of food. Hackers have targeted commercial fridges and freezers before, but never at this scale and never in the heart of Europe.

十月的一个夜晚，黑客们发动了历史上最不同寻常的网络攻击之一：他们调高了法国各地数百家超市制冷系统的温度，导致价值数百万欧元的食品损毁。此前虽有黑客针对商用冰箱和冷柜进行过攻击，但从未达到如此规模，也从未发生在欧洲腹地。

The brazen attack left cyber professionals unsure as to who might have executed such a madcap operation. But French intelligence agencies found traces of Farsi and other clues in the digital trail pointing to an elite hacking unit of the Islamic Revolutionary Guard Corps. The unit allegedly behind the hack, Shahid Kaveh, was already well known to Western intelligence agencies.

这场肆无忌惮的攻击让网络安全专家们无法确定是谁实施了如此疯狂的行动。但法国情报机构在数字踪迹中发现了波斯语痕迹及其他线索，指向了伊斯兰革命卫队的一支精英黑客部队。据称是此次黑客攻击幕后黑手的“沙希德·卡维”（Shahid Kaveh）部队，早已为西方情报机构所熟知。

Named after Mahmoud Kaveh, an Iranian commander who died during the 1980s Iran-Iraq war, it is one of several hacking units overseen by the Revolutionary Guards’ Cyber Electronic Command. The group, which often goes by the online persona CyberAv3ngers, has a history of targeting critical infrastructure.

该部队以在20世纪80年代两伊战争中阵亡的伊朗指挥官马哈茂德·卡维（Mahmoud Kaveh）的名字命名，是革命卫队网络电子司令部监管下的多个黑客部队之一。该组织常以“CyberAv3ngers”的网名活动，有针对关键基础设施进行攻击的记录。

In 2023 and 2024, it hacked Israeli-made industrial control devices inside water, manufacturing, and transportation systems across several US states. Operational disruption was minimal, but the attackers left a message each time: “Every equipment ‘made in Israel’ is CyberAv3ngers legal target.”

2023年和2024年，该组织入侵了美国多个州的水利、制造和交通系统内以色列制造的工业控制设备。虽然运营中断程度很小，但攻击者每次都会留下信息：“每一件‘以色列制造’的设备都是CyberAv3ngers的合法目标。”

State-backed Iranian hackers have rarely targeted Europe in this way, however. October’s hacks, experts say, mark an escalation in the Islamic republic’s cyber warfare strategy and a new willingness to hit European infrastructure. “Iran has very clearly messaged that the global economy will be disrupted as a consequence of it being shut out from the world,” said Ellie Geranmayeh of the European Council on Foreign Relations.

然而，伊朗国家支持的黑客此前很少以这种方式针对欧洲。专家表示，十月份的黑客攻击标志着伊朗伊斯兰共和国网络战战略的升级，以及其打击欧洲基础设施的新意愿。欧洲外交关系委员会的埃莉·杰兰马耶（Ellie Geranmayeh）表示：“伊朗非常明确地传达了一个信息，即由于它被世界孤立，全球经济将因此受到干扰。”

While France and other European countries have largely stayed out of the US and Israel’s campaign against Iran, which started in February, the Islamic republic increasingly views all Nato countries as legitimate targets, she continued. “This is about sowing panic in Europe and increasing that global pain threshold.”

她继续说道，尽管法国和其他欧洲国家在很大程度上没有参与美国和以色列自二月份以来针对伊朗的行动，但伊朗伊斯兰共和国正日益将所有北约国家视为合法目标。“这是为了在欧洲播下恐慌，并提高全球的痛苦承受门槛。”

Similar attacks were staged this month against supermarkets in 10 other countries across Europe, though no group has yet been blamed. Iran has denied any role in the freezer hacks, but France is seeking international arrest warrants for three Iranians allegedly involved: Hamid Reza Lashgarian, believed by US intelligence to be head of the Guards’ Cyber Electronic Command, and two Shahid Kaveh leaders, 37-year-old Mahdi Lashgarian and 35-year-old Reza Salarvand. Both Lashgarians were placed under US sanctions in 2024.

本月，欧洲其他10个国家的超市也发生了类似的攻击事件，尽管目前还没有组织被指控。伊朗否认与这些冷柜黑客攻击有任何关联，但法国正在寻求对三名涉案伊朗人发出国际逮捕令：被美国情报部门认为是卫队网络电子司令部负责人的哈米德·礼萨·拉什加里安（Hamid Reza Lashgarian），以及两名“沙希德·卡维”部队的领导人——37岁的马赫迪·拉什加里安（Mahdi Lashgarian）和35岁的礼萨·萨拉万德（Reza Salarvand）。这两名拉什加里安均于2024年受到美国制裁。

Often staffed with young engineers and software developers, the Revolutionary Guards’ cyber units have developed formidable skills in espionage and cyber warfare. “Iran is a country of engineers, and people continue to underestimate its capabilities,” said Sanam Vakil, Middle East director at Chatham House think-tank. “It has a long history of successful cyber operations,” she added, noting that Iran had learnt through its own experience of cyber attacks, most notably the Stuxnet assault on its nuclear program by the US and Israel in 2009.

革命卫队的网络部队通常由年轻的工程师和软件开发人员组成，在间谍活动和网络战方面已发展出强大的技能。“伊朗是一个工程师之国，人们依然低估了它的能力，”智库查塔姆研究所（Chatham House）中东部主任萨南·瓦基尔（Sanam Vakil）说。“它有着悠久且成功的网络行动历史，”她补充道，并指出伊朗通过自身遭受网络攻击的经历学到了很多，其中最著名的是2009年美国和以色列对其核计划发动的“震网”（Stuxnet）攻击。

French intelligence agencies have said the group used a Chinese-made scanning tool to scour the public Internet for connected refrigeration units. Infrastructure linked to the group also conducted reconnaissance using Modbus, a digital language widely used to control industrial machinery—a technique Shahid Kaveh has employed since at least April, according to the authorities.

法国情报机构称，该组织使用了一种中国制造的扫描工具，在公共互联网上搜寻联网的制冷设备。据当局称，与该组织有关联的基础设施还使用Modbus（一种广泛用于控制工业机械的数字语言）进行了侦察——这是“沙希德·卡维”部队至少自四月份以来一直在使用的技术。

The modus operandi described by the French authorities was consistent with the Iranian attacks in the US in 2023 and 2024, said Cynthia Kaiser, former deputy cyber director at the FBI. Near-identical hacks to those have taken place this year, though CyberAv3ngers did not claim responsibility, and US authorities stopped short of explicitly blaming Iran.

前联邦调查局网络副局长辛西娅·凯撒（Cynthia Kaiser）表示，法国当局描述的作案手法与2023年和2024年伊朗在美国发动的攻击一致。今年也发生了与此几乎相同的黑客攻击，尽管CyberAv3ngers并未认领，美国当局也未明确指责伊朗。

Kaiser said it was also possible that a third state could have contracted Shahid Kaveh to conduct the European attacks. “Even if Iranians were behind the keyboard, these attacks really appear more consistent with Russian-type targeting,” she said. “It wouldn’t surprise me if we eventually uncovered collaboration behind the scenes.”

凯撒表示，也有可能是第三方国家雇佣了“沙希德·卡维”部队来实施针对欧洲的攻击。“即使是伊朗人在键盘后面操作，这些攻击看起来确实更符合俄罗斯式的目标选择，”她说。“如果我们最终发现幕后存在合作，我也不会感到惊讶。”

Russia, like Iran, has sophisticated cyber capabilities and has shown increasing willingness to use them against Europe. Vakil at Chatham House acknowledged Russian involvement remained a possibility, noting that the strength of the relationship between the two countries was often under-appreciated by Western officials. “Their ties have grown through the context of these wars,” she said, referring to Russia’s full-scale invasion of Ukraine and Iran’s conflict with the US and Israel.

与伊朗一样，俄罗斯拥有先进的网络能力，并表现出越来越强烈的意愿将其用于针对欧洲。查塔姆研究所的瓦基尔承认俄罗斯的参与仍是一种可能，并指出两国关系的紧密程度往往被西方官员低估。“他们的联系是在这些战争的背景下加强的，”她指的是俄罗斯对乌克兰的全面入侵以及伊朗与美国和以色列的冲突。

In March, Russian state-sponsored hacking group NoName claimed responsibility for a series of attacks on Israeli targets. Whether or not Shahid Kaveh was working on behalf of Moscow or another power, the operation would have to be sanctioned by the senior leadership of the IRGC, according to Gil Messing, chief of staff at Israeli cyber security group Check Point Software.

三月份，俄罗斯国家支持的黑客组织NoName声称对一系列针对以色列目标的攻击负责。以色列网络安全集团Check Point Software的幕僚长吉尔·梅辛（Gil Messing）表示，无论“沙希德·卡维”部队是否代表莫斯科或其他势力行事，该行动都必须得到伊斯兰革命卫队高层的批准。

Cyber weapons have a limited shelf life, as once a specific software or hardware vulnerability has been exploited, it tends to be almost immediately patched by other potential targets. “The decision to make such a thing and burn out this capability is something that would have to be authorized by senior levels,” Messing said. “This is not a very sophisticated attack, but it sends a very strong message.”

网络武器的保质期有限，因为一旦特定的软件或硬件漏洞被利用，其他潜在目标往往会立即修补该漏洞。“决定制造这样的事件并消耗这种能力，是必须由高层授权的，”梅辛说。“这不是一次非常复杂的攻击，但它传递了一个非常强烈的信号。”
