# EPA reports ‘zero’ airborne lead following controlled Army detonation in Iowa

美国环保署报告称，在爱荷华州进行受控陆军爆炸后，空气中铅含量为“零”。

Hours after detonating 4.4 tons of lead azide in Iowa, officials reported early test results from the surrounding areas.

在爱荷华州引爆 4.4 吨叠氮化铅数小时后，官员们报告了周边地区的初步测试结果。

Saying it was necessary to prevent the possible spontaneous explosion of a stockpile of material used in igniters and blasting caps, the US Army conducted a controlled detonation of over 9,700 pounds of lead azide today at the Iowa Army Ammunition Plant (IAAAP) in Middletown, Iowa. Videos of the detonation showed plumes of smoke several hundred feet high drifting south before quickly dissipating.

美国陆军称，为了防止用于引信和雷管的库存材料发生可能的自发爆炸，今天在爱荷华州米德尔敦的爱荷华陆军弹药厂（IAAAP）对超过 9,700 磅的叠氮化铅进行了受控引爆。爆炸视频显示，数百英尺高的烟柱向南飘散，随后迅速消散。

Following the explosion, the Army confirmed that all of the barrels at the plant had been destroyed without any secondary explosions or infrastructure damage. “I believe, absolutely, that we reduced, as close to zero as it’s absolutely humanly possible, the risk to the health and safety of the people of Middletown and the extended communities throughout Iowa,” said Maj. Gen. Eric Shirley during a press conference held two hours after the blast, according to the Associated Press.

爆炸发生后，陆军证实工厂内的所有桶均已被销毁，没有发生二次爆炸或基础设施损坏。据美联社报道，埃里克·雪莉少将在爆炸发生两小时后举行的新闻发布会上表示：“我绝对相信，我们已将对米德尔敦人民及爱荷华州广大社区居民的健康和安全风险降至人类所能达到的近乎零的水平。”

The US Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) has been monitoring the area around the ammunition plant leading up to today’s event. According to a statement from the EPA following the detonation, “real-time air monitors are reporting zero detection of airborne lead throughout the region” an hour after the detonation.

在美国环保署（EPA）在今天事件发生前一直对弹药厂周边地区进行监测。根据环保署在爆炸后发表的一份声明，爆炸发生一小时后，“实时空气监测仪报告称，整个地区未检测到空气中的铅”。

The Army has used lead azide in munitions for nearly a century but began transitioning away from the material around 15 years ago because it can be difficult to transport and store. Over time, the material can degrade and crystallize and eventually become unstable enough to detonate at ambient temperatures.

陆军在弹药中使用叠氮化铅已有近一个世纪的历史，但大约 15 年前开始弃用这种材料，因为它难以运输和储存。随着时间的推移，该材料会降解并结晶，最终变得不稳定，以至于在环境温度下即可发生爆炸。

Ahead of today’s detonation, the Federal Emergency Management Agency (FEMA) supported a recommended, but voluntary, temporary relocation of all the residents of Middletown and immediate surrounding areas that began yesterday. A cone-shaped evacuation zone extends about five miles southwest of the ammunition plant to account for the post-explosion dispersion and deposition path of dust.

在今天引爆之前，联邦紧急事务管理局（FEMA）支持并建议米德尔敦及周边地区的居民从昨天开始自愿临时搬迁。一个圆锥形的疏散区从弹药厂向西南延伸约五英里，以应对爆炸后粉尘的扩散和沉降路径。

To reduce the amount of lead dust released into surrounding areas and the environment, the Army said it took several additional precautions as part of today’s detonation. Walls made of four 16,000-gallon water tanks were installed to capture lead particulate, while the aging earth-covered magazines holding the barrels were covered with tarps and lead containment screens and flooded with foam.

为了减少释放到周边地区和环境中的铅尘量，陆军表示在今天的引爆过程中采取了多项额外预防措施。他们安装了由四个 16,000 加仑水箱组成的墙体来捕获铅颗粒，同时用防水布和铅防护网覆盖了存放这些桶的老旧土掩弹药库，并注入了泡沫。
