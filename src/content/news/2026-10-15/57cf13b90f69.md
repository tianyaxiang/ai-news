A protein is a chain of amino acids held together by what are called peptide bonds. On their own, those bonds are extremely stable; they’re estimated to have a half-life of several hundred years. But in Earth’s environment, most proteins don’t last nearly that long. That’s because organisms have evolved a handful of ways to catalyze the break-up of peptide bonds, which have diversified into countless specialized protein-digesting enzymes.

蛋白质是由被称为肽键的化学键连接在一起的氨基酸链。这些化学键本身极其稳定，估计其半衰期长达数百年。但在地球环境中，大多数蛋白质的寿命远没有那么长。这是因为生物体已经进化出多种催化肽键断裂的方法，并分化出无数种专门的蛋白质消化酶。

Proteases are everywhere and generally make short work of any proteins that find their way into the environment. Now, researchers have basically short-circuited the diversification process, using AI-based protein design tools to take the catalytic core of two types of protease and replace the entire rest of the protein. The resulting enzymes could be tuned to cut specific proteins in half, and the researchers demonstrated their effectiveness by using them to cut proteins associated with neurological diseases.

蛋白酶无处不在，通常能迅速分解进入环境中的任何蛋白质。现在，研究人员利用基于人工智能的蛋白质设计工具，提取了两种蛋白酶的催化核心，并替换了蛋白质的其余部分，从而基本上绕过了自然界的进化分化过程。由此产生的酶可以被调节以将特定蛋白质切成两半，研究人员通过利用这些酶切割与神经系统疾病相关的蛋白质，证明了它们的有效性。

The basic peptide bond isn’t especially interesting-looking—it’s what you get when you link an amino group to an acid, releasing a water molecule in the process. As their name implies, amino acids have both an amino and an acid group, allowing them to form peptide bonds on two sides of the amino acid. This is critical for building up the long chains found in proteins.

基本的肽键看起来并不特别引人注目——它是氨基与酸结合并在此过程中释放出一个水分子后形成的。顾名思义，氨基酸同时具有氨基和酸基，这使得它们能够在氨基酸的两侧形成肽键。这对构建蛋白质中的长链至关重要。

Breaking these bonds apart simply reverses the process by breaking apart the peptide bond and a water molecule, and linking their parts. Because both components are chemically stable, the reaction requires an enzyme to occur with any frequency. Without an enzyme, a typical peptide bond would be stable for hundreds of years in water.

断裂这些化学键的过程只是上述过程的逆转，即通过分解肽键和水分子，并将它们的组成部分重新连接起来。由于这两个组分在化学上都很稳定，该反应需要酶的参与才能频繁发生。如果没有酶，典型的肽键在水中可以保持数百年的稳定。

Life has evolved a handful of enzyme mechanisms that manage this catalysis. The research at issue here relied on two different ones. One is a zinc protease, where the positive charge of the zinc atom coordinates the peptide bond that’s being broken, key parts of the protease, and a water molecule. The protease partially pulls a proton off the water molecule, activating it to attack and break up the peptide bond.

生命已经进化出几种能够实现这种催化作用的酶机制。此处涉及的研究依赖于其中两种不同的机制。一种是锌蛋白酶，其中锌原子的正电荷协调着正在断裂的肽键、蛋白酶的关键部分以及一个水分子。蛋白酶从水分子中部分夺取一个质子，激活它去攻击并断裂肽键。

The second protease the researchers use is called a cysteine protease. In this case, a cysteine (one of the amino acids) in the enzyme is activated to attack the peptide bond, creating a temporary intermediate where part of the protein being cut is chemically linked to the enzyme. A water molecule eventually breaks this intermediate apart, releasing the second part of the cut protein.

研究人员使用的第二种蛋白酶被称为半胱氨酸蛋白酶。在这种情况下，酶中的一种氨基酸——半胱氨酸被激活以攻击肽键，从而产生一个临时的中间体，使被切割蛋白质的一部分与酶发生化学连接。水分子最终会分解这个中间体，释放出被切割蛋白质的第二部分。

Both of these mechanisms require a handful of specific amino acids from the protease to be located in close proximity within the catalytic center of the protease. Other amino acids in the protease help ensure these key catalytic amino acids are brought together in the right place with the right orientation. The rest of the protease may also enforce a degree of specificity on what proteins get cut.

这两种机制都要求蛋白酶中的少数特定氨基酸位于蛋白酶催化中心内非常靠近的位置。蛋白酶中的其他氨基酸有助于确保这些关键的催化氨基酸以正确的方向聚集在正确的位置。蛋白酶的其余部分也可能对切割哪些蛋白质施加一定程度的选择性。

The people behind the new work, primarily from Nobel-winner David Baker’s lab at the University of Washington, viewed designing a protease as a challenge. For one, the stability of the peptide bonds means that reactions wouldn’t take place without an effective catalyst. And to get that catalyst requires an extremely precise arrangement of specific amino acids. So it wasn’t obvious that the AI protein design tools the lab developed were up to the task.

这项新工作的研究人员主要来自诺贝尔奖得主大卫·贝克（David Baker）在华盛顿大学的实验室，他们将设计蛋白酶视为一项挑战。首先，肽键的稳定性意味着如果没有有效的催化剂，反应就不会发生。而要获得这种催化剂，需要对特定氨基酸进行极其精确的排列。因此，该实验室开发的人工智能蛋白质设计工具是否能胜任这项任务，当时并不明确。

Both projects took a similar approach. Just like evolution, they started with an existing protease. One was a zinc protease used in a crayfish’s digestive system. The other was a cysteine protease from yeast. In both cases, the protein structure was known, as were the key amino acids that catalyze cleaving other proteins. The design process started with software called RFdiffusion2, which creates a backbone for the enzyme.

这两个项目采取了类似的方法。就像进化一样，它们从现有的蛋白酶开始。一种是用于小龙虾消化系统的锌蛋白酶，另一种是来自酵母的半胱氨酸蛋白酶。在这两种情况下，蛋白质的结构以及催化切割其他蛋白质的关键氨基酸都是已知的。设计过程始于名为 RFdiffusion2 的软件，该软件用于创建酶的骨架。

With a backbone laid out, the process next moved to ProteinMPNN, a tool that works along the backbone, populating it with specific amino acids. ProteinMPNN selects each amino acid based on a series of constraints. For starters, the amino acid must be compatible with the backbone folding up into its planned structure. Next, in critical areas, each amino acid chosen needs to be compatible with the formation of the enzyme’s catalytic site. And finally, in nearby areas, it has to be compatible with latching on to the intended target.

在确定骨架后，该过程进入 ProteinMPNN 阶段，这是一种沿着骨架工作并填充特定氨基酸的工具。ProteinMPNN 基于一系列约束条件选择每个氨基酸。首先，氨基酸必须与骨架折叠成预定结构相兼容。其次，在关键区域，所选的每个氨基酸都需要与酶催化位点的形成相兼容。最后，在邻近区域，它必须与锁定目标蛋白质相兼容。
