# Introducing Firefox Enterprise

Firefox Mozilla introduces Firefox Enterprise October 12, 2026 Mozilla Institutions are recalculating who controls the technology they rely on. Mozilla has an independent option to offer. For years, organizations consolidated technology around a small number of providers to make things simpler. Fewer systems. Tighter integrations. More economies of scale. But that consolidation went well beyond technology. It consolidated dependency, too.

Mozilla 宣布推出 Firefox Enterprise，日期为 2026 年 10 月 12 日。各机构正在重新评估究竟是谁在掌控他们所依赖的技术，而 Mozilla 提供了一个独立的选择。多年来，各组织为了简化流程，将技术整合到少数几家供应商手中。系统更少，集成更紧密，规模经济效应更显著。但这种整合远不止于技术层面，它同时也整合了依赖性。

The resulting risks have been debated, studied, and challenged along the way, but concentration continued. More work, data and critical infrastructure kept moving to the same few providers, while credible alternatives narrowed. The deeper that dependency runs, the harder and more costly it becomes to change course. When something fails, changes or falls outside an institution’s control, that dependency becomes a risk for CIOs and CISOs to manage.

由此产生的风险在这一过程中不断被讨论、研究和质疑，但集中化趋势仍在继续。更多的工作、数据和关键基础设施不断向少数几家供应商转移，而可靠的替代方案却在减少。这种依赖性越深，改变方向的难度和成本就越高。当出现故障、变更或超出机构控制范围的情况时，这种依赖性就成了首席信息官（CIO）和首席信息安全官（CISO）必须应对的风险。

Nowhere are these stakes more visible than in Europe. Now institutions are re-assessing. Governments are pushing for greater digital autonomy, while regulated industries face growing pressure to find and manage their dependencies. There’s a name for much of this debate about technological control: “sovereignty.” What digital sovereignty means in practice varies. Across Europe, governments are investing in the security and resilience of critical IT infrastructure, reducing strategic dependencies and putting technological autonomy higher on national agendas.

这种利害关系在欧洲表现得最为明显。现在，各机构正在进行重新评估。各国政府正在推动更大的数字自主权，而受监管的行业则面临着寻找和管理其依赖关系的巨大压力。关于技术控制的这场辩论有一个名称：“主权”。数字主权在实践中的含义各不相同。在整个欧洲，各国政府都在投资关键 IT 基础设施的安全性和韧性，减少战略依赖，并将技术自主权提升到国家议程的更高位置。

Different approaches, same underlying concern. Strip away the regional policy definitions and industry distinctions and a pretty practical, universal question emerges: how much technological dependency should we risk? And a very specific, very important layer has been largely missing from that conversation: the browser. The overlooked layer of modern business The browser. Applications run there. Employees authenticate in it. Sensitive data transits through it. Files are uploaded and downloaded and AI tools are increasingly used through it. That raises the same questions of dependency and control: whose browser technology are you relying on, and who controls the infrastructure used to manage it?

方法各异，但核心担忧相同。剥离掉区域政策定义和行业区别，一个非常实际且普遍的问题浮出水面：我们应该承担多少技术依赖风险？而在这个讨论中，一个非常具体且重要的层面一直被忽视：浏览器。现代商业中被忽视的层面——浏览器。应用程序在其中运行，员工在其中进行身份验证，敏感数据通过它传输。文件在其中上传和下载，人工智能工具也越来越多地通过它使用。这引发了关于依赖和控制的同样问题：你依赖的是谁的浏览器技术，又是谁在控制用于管理它的基础设施？

Introducing Firefox Enterprise Firefox Enterprise 是为有严格安全、治理和合规要求的组织而构建的。它将基于 Mozilla 独立 Gecko 引擎的 Firefox 浏览器与组织可以在其自身基础设施上运行的管理控制台相结合。通过该控制台，IT 团队可以集中管理浏览器策略和访问权限，管控数据在浏览器中的流动方式，并设置围绕 AI 使用的控制措施。IT 和安全团队可以更好地控制其数据、IT 基础设施和浏览器环境，从而有助于加强技术独立性和运营韧性。

Introducing Firefox Enterprise Firefox Enterprise 是为有严格安全、治理和合规要求的组织而构建的。它将基于 Mozilla 独立 Gecko 引擎的 Firefox 浏览器与组织可以在其自身基础设施上运行的管理控制台相结合。通过该控制台，IT 团队可以集中管理浏览器策略和访问权限，管控数据在浏览器中的流动方式，并设置围绕 AI 使用的控制措施。IT 和安全团队可以更好地控制其数据、IT 基础设施和浏览器环境，从而有助于加强技术独立性和运营韧性。

“If all your options are built with the same technology underneath, ‘choice’ is a lie. So is control,” said Anthony Enzor-DeMeo, CEO of Mozilla. “We built Firefox differently. And we’re bringing its independent architecture to institutions that are ready to rethink their dependencies and risk.”

“如果你的所有选择在底层都是由相同的技术构建的，那么‘选择’就是一个谎言。控制权也是如此，”Mozilla 首席执行官 Anthony Enzor-DeMeo 表示。“我们以不同的方式构建了 Firefox。我们正在将其独立的架构带给那些准备好重新思考其依赖关系和风险的机构。”

Bringing the browser under control We’re starting with the browser because we see it as a key, but under-governed, layer. Firefox Enterprise gives institutions more control over the browser and the infrastructure managing it. And because Firefox runs on Gecko, the only major browser engine developed outside Google and Apple, institutions have another path towards reducing dependence on a single architecture.

将浏览器纳入控制范围 我们从浏览器入手，是因为我们认为它是关键但管理不足的层面。Firefox Enterprise 让机构能够更好地控制浏览器及其管理基础设施。由于 Firefox 运行在 Gecko 上（这是除谷歌和苹果之外开发的唯一主要浏览器引擎），机构有了另一条减少对单一架构依赖的途径。

Finally, Firefox Enterprise is built to live alongside the systems that IT operations already use. Keep your identity provider. Keep your SIEM. Keep your MDM. Just add a browser whose management infrastructure you operate, so you decide where data lives and have greater visibility into how it moves. For us, supporting the goal of technological sovereignty comes down to four things: More control. More transparency. More choice. More resilience. Learn more about Firefox Enterprise.

最后，Firefox Enterprise 的构建旨在与 IT 运营部门已经使用的系统并存。保留您的身份提供商，保留您的 SIEM，保留您的 MDM。只需添加一个由您运营管理基础设施的浏览器，这样您就可以决定数据存储的位置，并对数据的流动方式拥有更高的可见性。对我们而言，支持技术主权的目标归结为四点：更多的控制、更多的透明度、更多的选择、更多的韧性。了解更多关于 Firefox Enterprise 的信息。

A big bet on familiar ground We know this territory. Independence isn’t a new direction for Mozilla. We’ve spent more than 25 years building and securing browser technology that intentionally works outside of the dominant architecture of the day. Firefox is used by roughly 200 million people every month and is already deployed across thousands of organizations. That independence has always mattered to the web. Now it has practical value for institutions looking to reduce technological concentration — and dependency — in their own environments.

在熟悉的领域下大赌注 我们了解这个领域。独立性对 Mozilla 来说并不是一个新的方向。我们花了 25 年多的时间构建和保护浏览器技术，这些技术特意在当今主流架构之外运行。Firefox 每月约有 2 亿用户，并已在数千个组织中部署。这种独立性对网络一直很重要。现在，对于那些希望减少自身环境中技术集中化和依赖性的机构来说，它具有实际价值。

This is the beginning Our ambition is big but simple: make technology independence practical. That means putting institutions in real control of the infrastructure and data they depend on, without asking them to rebuild or start over. Closing blind spots. Defining where policies, access and data live. Keeping core management within the institution’s environment. And bringing an overlooked layer — the browser — under institutional control.

这是开始 我们的雄心很大但很简单：让技术独立变得切实可行。这意味着让机构真正掌控他们所依赖的基础设施和数据，而无需他们重建或从头开始。消除盲点。定义策略、访问权限和数据的存储位置。将核心管理保留在机构的环境内。并将一个被忽视的层面——浏览器——纳入机构的控制之下。

Firefox Enterprise is unveiling across Europe with an initial sales focus on France, Germany, Austria and Switzerland, with more markets to follow. Thunderbird Enterprise is also in development, extending this customer-controlled approach to email and communications. We’ll have more to share as that work progresses.
