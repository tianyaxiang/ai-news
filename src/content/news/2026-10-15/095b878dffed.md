https://github.com/theonedev/wrapper MIT licensed, written with Rust, and tested intensively on Windows amd64/arm64, Linux amd64/arm64, Mac Silicon. It is used by OneDev to run as service on these platforms. Wrapper configuration is a subset of JSW, focusing on service wrapping only, without JVM monitoring.
