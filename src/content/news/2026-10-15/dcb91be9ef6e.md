Director Greta Gerwig could have made pretty much any movie she wanted after 2023’s Barbie racked up nearly $1.5 billion at the box office. She chose to make Narnia: The Magician’s Nephew for Netflix, an adaptation of C.S. Lewis’ sixth novel in The Chronicles of Narnia series.

在 2023 年的《芭比》斩获近 15 亿美元票房后，导演格蕾塔·葛韦格本可以拍摄任何她想拍的电影。但她选择了为 Netflix 制作《纳尼亚传奇：魔法师的外甥》，这是 C.S. 刘易斯《纳尼亚传奇》系列中第六部小说的改编作品。

Gerwig has said little publicly about the film, other than that she was inspired by 1960s-era David Bowie, although she said it would not be a musical. Netflix just released the trailer, set to “Stairway to Heaven,” and it’s quite the psychedelic ride.

除了提到自己受到 20 世纪 60 年代大卫·鲍伊的启发外，葛韦格对这部电影公开谈论的内容很少，并表示它不会是一部音乐剧。Netflix 刚刚发布了预告片，配乐为《天堂阶梯》，呈现出一种迷幻的视觉体验。

The first book in the series, The Lion, the Witch, and the Wardrobe, was published in 1950, and Lewis did not initially intend to write any more Narnia adventures. But then an acquaintance asked how the London lamppost ended up in the middle of Narnia’s woodland, and Lewis was intrigued enough by the question to start a prequel, The Magician’s Nephew, in order to find an answer.

该系列的第一本书《狮子、女巫和魔衣橱》于 1950 年出版，刘易斯最初并不打算再写任何纳尼亚冒险故事。但后来一位熟人问起伦敦的街灯是如何出现在纳尼亚森林中央的，刘易斯对这个问题产生了浓厚兴趣，于是开始创作前传《魔法师的外甥》以寻找答案。

Lewis found it difficult to finish the book, however, writing four other Narnia books first before completing The Magician’s Nephew in 1955. There have been several adaptations, in various media, of The Chronicles of Narnia, most recently the early 2000s film franchise adapting the first three novels in the series.

然而，刘易斯发现完成这本书很困难，他在 1955 年完成《魔法师的外甥》之前，先写了其他四本纳尼亚书籍。《纳尼亚传奇》在各种媒体上都有过多次改编，最近一次是 21 世纪初改编该系列前三部小说的电影系列。

Those films showed diminishing returns, however, and by 2011 the film rights had expired. There were initial plans to adapt the fourth novel, The Silver Chair. The original book was set in 1900, but Gerwig shifts the setting to the 1950s, postwar London.

但这些电影的收益逐渐递减，到 2011 年电影版权已经到期。最初曾有计划改编第四部小说《银椅》。原著背景设定在 1900 年，但葛韦格将背景转移到了 20 世纪 50 年代的战后伦敦。

Digory Kirke (David McKenna) lives next door to Polly Plummer (Beatrice Campbell), and the two embark on a magical journey to other worlds after Digory’s eccentric uncle, Andrew Ketterley (Daniel Craig), tricks them into trying on magic rings. Then Digory accidentally unleashes the evil empress Jadis (Emma Mackey, who played Physicist Barbie in Barbie) from a sleeping spell, and chaos breaks out.

迪戈里·柯克（大卫·麦肯纳饰）住在波莉·普拉默（碧翠丝·坎贝尔饰）隔壁。在迪戈里古怪的叔叔安德鲁·凯特利（丹尼尔·克雷格饰）诱骗他们试戴魔法戒指后，两人踏上了前往其他世界的奇幻旅程。随后，迪戈里意外解除了邪恶女皇贾迪斯（曾在《芭比》中饰演物理学家芭比的艾玛·麦基饰）的沉睡咒语，引发了一场混乱。

The cast also includes Kobna Holdbrook-Smith as a London cabby named Frank; Ava Jager as Polly’s sister Violet; Carey Mulligan as Digory’s mother, Flora; Tom Bonington as Mr. Potts; Ciaran Hinds as the adult Digory; and Meryl Streep voicing Aslan. Denise Gough, Susan Wokoma, Gerard Monaco, James Murray, and Samantha Spiro also appear in as-yet-undisclosed roles.

演员阵容还包括饰演伦敦出租车司机弗兰克的科布纳·霍尔布鲁克-史密斯；饰演波莉姐姐维奥莱特的艾娃·贾格；饰演迪戈里母亲弗洛拉的凯瑞·穆里根；饰演波茨先生的汤姆·博宁顿；饰演成年迪戈里的塞伦·希德；以及为阿斯兰配音的梅丽尔·斯特里普。丹妮丝·高夫、苏珊·沃科马、杰拉德·莫纳科、詹姆斯·默里和萨曼莎·斯皮罗也出演了尚未公开的角色。

Narnia: The Magician’s Nephew will open in theaters on February 12, 2027. It will start streaming on Netflix on April 2, 2027. It’s the first Netflix film to have a wide theatrical release. Gerwig has a two-picture deal for the Netflix Narnia series, so we might get her take on The Lion, the Witch, and the Wardrobe next (assuming she’s following the books’ chronology).

《纳尼亚传奇：魔法师的外甥》将于 2027 年 2 月 12 日在影院上映，并于 2027 年 4 月 2 日在 Netflix 上线。这是第一部进行大规模院线发行的 Netflix 电影。葛韦格与 Netflix 签署了纳尼亚系列两部电影的协议，因此我们接下来可能会看到她对《狮子、女巫和魔衣橱》的诠释（假设她遵循书籍的时间顺序）。
