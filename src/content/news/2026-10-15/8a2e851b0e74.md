# The Model Writes the Review Reply. The Owner Keeps the Promises.

Hacktoberfest Open-Source AI Challenge Week 2: Side Hustle Submission 💸 This is a submission for the Hacktoberfest Open-Source AI Challenge Week 2: Side Hustle. What I Built “The coffee was excellent, but I was charged twice. The waiter ignored my question about the bill.” That fictional review has three things worth answering. Thanking the customer for the coffee is easy. Acknowledging the double charge and unanswered question takes more care. Promising a refund introduces a different decision: does the person writing the reply have permission to offer one?

Hacktoberfest 开源 AI 挑战赛第 2 周：副业项目提交 💸 这是 Hacktoberfest 开源 AI 挑战赛第 2 周的副业项目提交。我的作品：“咖啡很棒，但我被重复收费了。服务员忽略了我关于账单的问题。” 这条虚构的评论有三点值得回应。感谢顾客的咖啡很容易。承认重复收费和未回答的问题则需要更谨慎。承诺退款引入了一个不同的决策：撰写回复的人是否有权提供退款？

I built Review Reply Writer for independent cafe and small hospitality operators. Gemma drafts the language locally. The owner supplies the business policy, reads the result, and approves the exact text before copying it for manual posting. The Side Hustle hypothesis is paid installation, policy setup and support for an operator who wants local control without configuring a model. The application code stays free. Demand, willingness to pay and time saved remain unproven.

我为独立咖啡馆和小型酒店经营者构建了“评论回复撰写器”（Review Reply Writer）。Gemma 在本地起草语言。店主提供业务政策，阅读结果，并在复制以供手动发布之前批准确切的文本。该副业项目的假设是：为那些希望在不配置模型的情况下实现本地控制的经营者提供付费安装、政策设置和支持。应用程序代码保持免费。需求、支付意愿和节省的时间仍未得到验证。

Keep the customer's concern visible The editor places the saved review beside the reply. Details to keep links recognized concerns to exact excerpts of the original text. Prompts, fallbacks and checks share a bounded interpretation, so “charged twice” does not quietly become a generic billing apology.

模型撰写的草稿需要 Ollama 和下载的 gemma3:4b 权重。带有清晰标签的模板预览无需两者即可工作。我是如何构建它的：原始编码始于 2026 年 10 月 13 日夏威夷时间上午 8 点左右，即本次挑战的开发窗口期。保持顾客的担忧可见：编辑器将保存的评论放在回复旁边。“要保留的详细信息”将识别出的担忧链接到原始文本的确切摘录。提示、回退和检查共享有限的解释，因此“重复收费”不会悄悄变成通用的账单道歉。

An unanswered question also matters. A reply can mention the coffee and still leave the customer's main frustration untouched. Unsaved edits mark the checks stale until the owner saves and rechecks. The interface says Mention detected because a phrase match cannot establish understanding. The owner still reads the whole review and reply. Customer wording cannot authorize a business promise The first version required a make-it-right offer in negative replies. That was a bad default: it encouraged commitments without permission.

未回答的问题也很重要。回复可以提到咖啡，但仍可能忽略顾客的主要挫败感。未保存的编辑会使检查标记为过期，直到店主保存并重新检查。界面显示“检测到提及”，因为短语匹配无法建立理解。店主仍需阅读完整的评论和回复。顾客的措辞不能授权商业承诺：第一个版本要求在负面回复中提供补偿方案。这是一个糟糕的默认设置：它在未经许可的情况下鼓励了承诺。

Now authorization is separate from customer text, earlier replies and voice examples. Exact owner statements can permit an offer or action. Detected unauthorized promises block approval, whether introduced by the model, a fallback or an edit. An authorized credit cannot silently become a larger credit plus a refund. Approval is tied to the saved text and check version. Changing the reply requires a new review. Serious allegations are conservatively referred for personal handling before routine generation.

现在，授权与顾客文本、之前的回复和语音示例是分开的。确切的店主声明可以允许提供优惠或采取行动。检测到的未经授权的承诺会拦截批准，无论是模型、回退还是编辑引入的。已授权的信用额度不能悄悄变成更大的信用额度加上退款。批准与保存的文本和检查版本绑定。更改回复需要新的评论。严重的指控在常规生成之前会被保守地转交进行人工处理。

Preserve the work around the words A lost response should not mean a lost draft. Saved generation receipts let the owner check an interrupted request without automatically running it again. Pending edits, private recovery copies and checked backups support recovery. Recovery leaves authorization unchecked and does not resurrect old approval. The recurring workflow includes saved profiles and a one-business CSV inbox. Each reply still needs individual review. Nothing posts automatically.

保留文字周围的工作：丢失的响应不应意味着丢失草稿。保存的生成收据让店主可以检查中断的请求，而无需自动再次运行它。待处理的编辑、私有恢复副本和已检查的备份支持恢复。恢复操作不会检查授权，也不会恢复旧的批准。循环工作流程包括保存的配置文件和单业务 CSV 收件箱。每个回复仍需单独审查。没有任何内容会自动发布。

These checks do not establish current Windows, browser/mobile or model behavior. Two historical three-case Gemma smoke runs remain in the repository. The later run produced three model drafts on first attempts, but both runs predate current drafting-source changes and have no recorded human assessments. They do not validate this version's reply quality.
