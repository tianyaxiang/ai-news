Every call was made with free, public APIs — nothing in this article required a paid tier. At 22:55:40 UTC on October 14th, a post left one of our test accounts. Two servers, two truths ATProto separates two jobs that older platforms bundle into one: The PDS holds your repository — every post, like, and reply as signed records. If your PDS serves it over com.atproto.repo.getRecord, it exists. Full stop. The AppView (the big aggregation/indexing layer that apps actually query) crawls the firehose and builds timelines, threads, search, and profile pages out of it. Clients mostly talk to #2. Operators mostly reason about #1. The gap between those two perspectives is where we spent this evening.

两台服务器，两个真相。ATProto 将旧平台捆绑在一起的两项工作分离开来：PDS 负责托管你的存储库——每一条帖子、点赞和回复都作为已签名的记录存在。如果你的 PDS 通过 com.atproto.repo.getRecord 提供了该记录，那么它就是存在的，毋庸置疑。AppView（应用程序实际查询的大型聚合/索引层）则通过抓取信息流来构建时间线、对话串、搜索和个人资料页面。客户端主要与后者交互，而运营者主要关注前者。我们今晚正是花费时间在研究这两种视角之间的差距。

Centralized platforms fuse storage and visibility into one step. Federated ones deliberately don't — and the seam shows exactly there.

作为对比，当晚我们通过官方 API 在 dev.to 上发布了一篇文章：POST 请求返回 201，且对公共 URL 进行匿名 GET 请求几乎立即返回了 200（文章 ID 4853041，evidence/daily_ops/20261014T2250Z/verdict.json）。中心化平台将存储和可见性融合为一步，而联邦化平台则刻意将其分开——裂痕恰恰显现在这里。

Why this bites operators If you automate anything on top of a third-party PDS — a scheduler, a bot, a cross-poster — you will eventually write a "post it and verify it landed" step. Verify against the AppView, and during indexing lag you will conclude your automation failed, then retry, and double-post. We've seen this exact failure shape in other ecosystems; ATProto just makes the boundary crisper because the two layers have clean names and clean APIs.

为什么这会困扰运营者：如果你在第三方 PDS 之上进行任何自动化操作——比如调度器、机器人或跨平台发布工具——你最终都会编写一个“发布并验证是否成功”的步骤。如果你针对 AppView 进行验证，在索引延迟期间，你会得出自动化失败的结论，进而重试，导致重复发布。我们在其他生态系统中也见过这种完全相同的失败模式；ATProto 只是让这种边界更加清晰，因为这两个层级拥有明确的名称和简洁的 API。

The triage order that never lies: Origin first. getRecord (single record) or listRecords (collection) directly against the PDS, unauthenticated if you like. This is the ground truth about whether your write happened. Index second, with patience. getAuthorFeed, getPostThread, and friends are eventual-consistency views. Treat them like a cache with an unpredictable TTL for young accounts. Positive control always. Before declaring your brand-new account broken, run the same probe against a long-established account. If the old account resolves and yours doesn't, that's indexing lag, not breakage. Skipping this step is how "it works for everyone else" turns into hours of pointless debugging.

永远不会撒谎的分类顺序：首先是源服务器。直接针对 PDS 进行 getRecord（单条记录）或 listRecords（集合）查询，如果愿意，甚至可以不进行身份验证。这是关于你的写入操作是否发生的根本事实。其次是索引层，请保持耐心。getAuthorFeed、getPostThread 等接口属于最终一致性视图。对于新账户，请将它们视为具有不可预测 TTL（生存时间）的缓存。始终进行正向对照。在宣布你的全新账户损坏之前，请对一个长期存在的账户进行相同的探测。如果旧账户可以解析而你的不行，那是索引延迟，而非故障。跳过这一步，就是为什么“别人都能用”会变成数小时无意义调试的原因。

One more practical note: a reply is just another record on your repo pointing at a parent on someone else's. Until indexing catches up, the parent's thread page won't show it — which means the person you replied to can't see it either. Engagement has the same latency problem as publishing.

还有一个实际注意事项：回复仅仅是你存储库中的另一条记录，指向别人存储库中的父记录。在索引同步完成之前，父记录的对话页面不会显示它——这意味着你回复的人也看不到它。互动与发布一样，都存在同样的延迟问题。

The honest takeaway Day one, self-sovereignty on ATProto is real at the storage layer: your records are served, publicly and anonymously, from a box you control, and nobody can unilaterally rewrite them. Reach, however, is rented from indexers until they index you — and for a young third-party-PDS account, "until" is measured in tens of minutes, not seconds. None of this is an argument against running your own PDS. It is an argument for knowing which question you're asking: "does my data exist?" — ask your PDS. "can people see it?" — ask the AppView, and expect the answer to ripen.

诚实的结论：在 ATProto 上，存储层面的自我主权在第一天就是真实的：你的记录从你控制的服务器上公开且匿名地提供服务，没有人可以单方面重写它们。然而，触达能力是从索引器那里租来的，直到它们为你建立索引——对于一个年轻的第三方 PDS 账户来说，“直到”是以数十分钟而非数秒来衡量的。这并不是反对运行你自己的 PDS，而是为了让你知道你在问什么问题：“我的数据存在吗？”——去问你的 PDS；“人们能看到它吗？”——去问 AppView，并等待答案成熟。
