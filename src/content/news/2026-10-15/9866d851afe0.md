# Save up to 20 percent on gift cards for REI, Barnes & Noble, and more

# 在 REI、Barnes & Noble 等商店购买礼品卡最高可享 8 折优惠

You can save up to 20 percent on gift cards from 30 retailers, ranging from books and tech to food and travel.

While we cover a lot of deals on specific products, it’s rare that we find worthwhile savings on gift cards that let you save on essentially anything the store sells. However, until October 19th, you can save up to twenty percent on gift cards when you buy them through the Google Play store. There are a variety of discounts and retailers available, including 20 percent off $50 at Barnes and Noble, 20 percent off $100 at REI, and 10 percent off $100 at GameStop.

虽然我们报道过许多特定产品的优惠活动，但很少能发现礼品卡方面的超值折扣，因为礼品卡能让你在购买商店出售的任何商品时省钱。不过，在 10 月 19 日之前，通过 Google Play 商店购买礼品卡最高可享 8 折优惠。目前有多种折扣和零售商可供选择，包括 Barnes and Noble 50 美元礼品卡 8 折、REI 100 美元礼品卡 8 折以及 GameStop 100 美元礼品卡 9 折。

You don’t need an Android phone or tablet to save, but you’ll need to sign up for a Google Play account if you don’t have one already. In addition to the gift cards, Google is offering discounts on subscriptions like Calm and DuoLingo, as well as bonuses on in-app purchases in games like Pokémon Go and PUBG Mobile.

你不需要拥有安卓手机或平板电脑也能享受优惠，但如果你还没有 Google Play 账户，则需要注册一个。除了礼品卡，谷歌还为 Calm 和 DuoLingo 等订阅服务提供折扣，并为《Pokémon Go》和《PUBG Mobile》等游戏内的应用内购买提供奖励。
