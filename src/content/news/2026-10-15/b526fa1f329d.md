# Iosevka is an open-source, sans-serif and slab-serif monospace font

The Iosevka’s monospace family is provided in a slender outfit by default: glyphs are exactly 1/2em wide. Compared to the competitors, you could fit more columns within the same screen width.

Iosevka 等宽字体系列默认采用纤细的造型：字形宽度恰好为 1/2em。与同类竞品相比，您可以在相同的屏幕宽度内显示更多的列数。

Iosevka provides two widths, Normal and Extended. If you prefer more breeze between the character, choose Extended and enjoy.

Iosevka 提供“标准”（Normal）和“扩展”（Extended）两种宽度。如果您偏好字符之间有更宽的间距，可以选择“扩展”版本来使用。

Terminal emulators have a stricter compatibility requirements for fonts. Therefore, Iosevka and Iosevka Slab all contain two specialized families, Term and Fixed, targeting terminal users.

终端模拟器对字体有更严格的兼容性要求。因此，Iosevka 和 Iosevka Slab 都包含了专为终端用户设计的两个特殊系列：Term 和 Fixed。

In these families, the symbols will be narrower to follow terminals’ ideology of column count. In the Fixed families, the ligation will be disabled to ensure better compatibility in certain environments.

在这些系列中，符号会变得更窄，以符合终端对列数的定义。在 Fixed 系列中，连字功能会被禁用，以确保在特定环境下具有更好的兼容性。

Iosevka provides 146 configurable characters with 19 stylistic sets, which gives you incredible ability to customize one variant that fills exactly what you need. You can either select one pre-defined stylistic set, or cherry-pick your flavor.

Iosevka 提供了 146 个可配置字符和 19 种样式集，让您拥有极大的自由度来定制完全符合您需求的变体。您可以选择预定义的样式集，也可以根据个人喜好进行挑选。

Iosevka’s monospace families supports not only one ligation set, but also language-specific ligations. Enable the corresponded OpenType feature in your editor, and the correct ligation will appear.

Iosevka 的等宽字体系列不仅支持一套连字，还支持特定语言的连字。在编辑器中启用相应的 OpenType 功能，即可显示正确的连字。

Leveraging advanced OpenType techniques, Iosevka also supports long ligatures like long arrows or horizontal bars built up with equal signs.

利用先进的 OpenType 技术，Iosevka 还支持长连字，例如长箭头或由等号组成的水平横线。

The quasi-proportional families: Iosevka Aile, and Iosevka Etoile, are made for documents and writing. They provide more variety on character widths, including a slightly wider letter w and m, and narrower i and l.

准比例字体系列 Iosevka Aile 和 Iosevka Etoile 专为文档和写作而设计。它们提供了更多样的字符宽度，包括稍宽的 w 和 m，以及更窄的 i 和 l。
