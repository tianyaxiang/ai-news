# Evercade Nexus 评测：迄今为止最棒的复古掌机

Specialist hardware manufacturer Blaze Entertainment has spent the 2020s quietly carving out a niche for itself as the go-to option for retro gaming. With a stable of reliable hardware, from the original Evercade handheld to the Evercade Alpha bartop arcade cabinet, it offers numerous ways to play, all built around an ever-expanding library of physical cartridges. Each brings a curated selection of classic titles and lost curiosities to modern players.

专业硬件制造商 Blaze Entertainment 在 2020 年代悄然开辟了一片天地，成为复古游戏的首选品牌。从最初的 Evercade 掌机到 Evercade Alpha 桌面街机，该公司凭借一系列可靠的硬件，围绕不断扩充的实体卡带库，为玩家提供了多种游玩方式。每一款卡带都为现代玩家带来了精心挑选的经典游戏和被遗忘的珍品。

The Evercade Nexus is more than just the latest addition to its console lineup. While it retains compatibility with every previously released Evercade cartridge, it’s also the biggest leap forward the line has taken so far, both in hardware and in what it might offer with games going forward. It’s a real level up—and it’s rather brilliant.

Evercade Nexus 不仅仅是其主机产品线中的最新成员。虽然它保留了对之前所有 Evercade 卡带的兼容性，但它也是该系列迄今为止在硬件和未来游戏潜力方面迈出的最大一步。这确实是一次升级，而且非常出色。

Bigger and Better: The most obvious change is the sheer size. Where Blaze’s previous handhelds were about on par with the original Game Boy Advance, aimed at pocketable play, the Evercade Nexus is closer in scale to a Nintendo Switch Lite. It measures 215 x 111 x 34 millimeters and weighs 410 grams—still easily portable, but more in the “buy a protective hard case and throw it in your bag” kind of way.

更大更好：最明显的变化是尺寸。Blaze 之前的掌机大小与初代 Game Boy Advance 相当，主打便携，而 Evercade Nexus 的尺寸更接近 Nintendo Switch Lite。它的三围是 215 x 111 x 34 毫米，重量为 410 克——虽然依然便携，但更倾向于“买个保护硬壳包扔进包里”的那种便携方式。

The size increase works in its favor, though. The larger design feels more substantial, with more real estate for your hands for better comfort. The control layout remains broadly the same as the Evercade EXP—a D-pad, ABXY face buttons, shoulder buttons, and triggers all return, with a few beneficial tweaks. Notably, the triggers are now chunkier and curved to meet your fingers, while all four shoulder inputs and the rear of the device are now nicely textured for better grip.

不过，尺寸的增加带来了好处。更大的设计让手感更扎实，手部空间更充裕，舒适度也更高。按键布局与 Evercade EXP 基本保持一致——十字键、ABXY 功能键、肩键和扳机键悉数回归，并进行了一些有益的调整。值得注意的是，扳机键现在更厚实且带有弧度，更贴合手指；同时，四个肩键和机身背面都增加了纹理，提供了更好的握持感。

The big change is the addition of twin symmetrical thumbsticks. This has implications for Evercade going forward—more on that later—but it also improves Tate Mode (pronounced tah-tay, meaning vertical in Japanese), a returning feature from the Evercade EXP. As on that model, tapping the dedicated mode-shift button rotates the screen 90 degrees, letting you play vertically scrolling arcade games in the intended orientation, rather than crammed into a tiny pillar-boxed view on the widescreen display.

最大的变化是增加了对称的双摇杆。这对 Evercade 的未来发展有着深远影响（稍后详述），同时也改进了从 Evercade EXP 延续下来的“Tate 模式”（日语意为垂直）。与前代机型一样，点击专用的模式切换按钮可将屏幕旋转 90 度，让你能以预期的方向游玩纵向卷轴街机游戏，而不是在宽屏显示器上挤在一个狭小的黑边窗口里。

On the EXP, you’d hold the console on the left to use the D-pad for movement, which required duplicate A+B buttons on that side and cluttered the inputs. On the Nexus, Tate Mode rotates in the opposite direction, letting you use the right-hand thumbstick and the default ABXY buttons. It feels much better—again aided by the increased overall size—and keeps the device's design looking much tidier.

在 EXP 上，你需要握住主机的左侧使用十字键进行移动，这要求左侧必须有重复的 A+B 键，导致按键布局显得杂乱。而在 Nexus 上，Tate 模式向相反方向旋转，让你能够使用右侧摇杆和默认的 ABXY 键。得益于整体尺寸的增加，这种操作手感好得多，也让设备外观看起来更加整洁。

Whichever orientation you play in, the screen is bigger—a 5.89-inch IPS panel with an 840 × 512-pixel resolution for a 167 pixels-per-inch density, versus the original's 4.3-inch LCD—and brighter, now boasting 500-plus nits. It’s clear and colorful, and games look fantastic on it.

无论以哪种方向游玩，屏幕都变得更大了——这是一块 5.89 英寸的 IPS 面板，分辨率为 840 × 512 像素，像素密度为 167 PPI（相比之下，原版为 4.3 英寸 LCD），亮度也更高，达到了 500 尼特以上。屏幕清晰且色彩丰富，游戏画面看起来非常棒。

The only minor build-quality demerits are the D-pad, which feels a little too raised and prone to rocking—a slightly more flush fit would have been better—and the glossy finish under the face buttons and around the front trim. These accents are aesthetically pleasing, standing out from the matte plastic of the rest of the unit, but they attract micro-scratches far too easily. Those small gripes aside, the Nexus is a huge improvement on its predecessors, feeling less like a quirky gadget and more like a serious, modern gaming handheld.

唯一的做工瑕疵在于十字键，它感觉有点太高且容易晃动——如果能做得更平整一点会更好；此外，功能键下方和前框周围的亮面涂层也存在问题。这些装饰虽然美观，与机身其余部分的磨砂塑料形成对比，但非常容易产生细微划痕。撇开这些小毛病不谈，Nexus 比前代产品有了巨大进步，它不再像是一个古怪的小玩意，而更像是一款严肃的现代游戏掌机。

Up and Downgrades: The Nexus doesn’t actually see any boosts in processor power or memory. It still uses the 1.5-GHz quad-core chip in the EXP with 512 MB of RAM. That may not sound like much in 2026, but it’s significantly more powerful than the original hardware it emulates, and it ensures Evercade’s universal cross-device compatibility for every cartridge.

升级与降级：Nexus 在处理器性能或内存方面实际上并没有提升。它仍然使用 EXP 中那颗 1.5 GHz 四核芯片和 512 MB 内存。在 2026 年，这听起来可能不算什么，但它比其模拟的原始硬件强大得多，并确保了 Evercade 每张卡带在所有设备上的通用兼容性。

The downside is that the Nexus still feels slow at the system level. Booting it up takes about 35 to 40 seconds on average, and it can take about the same to load up a cartridge. Games don't have any performance issues, but getting there is sluggish. Disappointingly, there’s also no quick-suspend feature, forcing you to power off fully and back on between play sessions.

缺点是 Nexus 在系统层面依然感觉迟缓。平均开机时间约为 35 到 40 秒，加载卡带的时间也差不多。游戏本身没有任何性能问题，但进入游戏的过程很慢。令人失望的是，它也没有快速挂起功能，这意味着你在两次游玩之间必须完全关机再重新启动。

There are some upgrades to the underlying tech, though. One material improvement is the battery, which jumped to a 5,000-mAh cell from the EXP's 3,000 mAh. Even with that bigger, brighter screen sucking up juice, the Nexus offers around five hours of play between charges, roughly an hour longer. On the audio front, the integrated front-facing stereo speakers are solid, offering louder, clearer output. Even better, the Nexus now supports Bluetooth, finally allowing you to connect wireless headphones, while still offering a 3.5-mm jack for wired headsets.

不过，底层技术确实有一些升级。一个实质性的改进是电池，从 EXP 的 3,000 mAh 提升到了 5,000 mAh。即使更大的高亮屏幕会消耗更多电量，Nexus 的续航时间仍能达到约 5 小时，比前代多出约一小时。音频方面，内置的前置立体声扬声器表现扎实，输出声音更大、更清晰。更好的是，Nexus 现在支持蓝牙，终于可以连接无线耳机，同时也保留了 3.5 毫米有线耳机接口。

Bluetooth isn’t the only connectivity upgrade. The Nexus now supports the Wi-Fi 6 standard and can connect to 5-GHz networks (previous models could only use slower 2.4-GHz ones). That’s useful for downloading console updates and Evercade’s free Game of the Month. It also enables the new EverSync feature, unique to the Nexus, which allows multiple units to communicate wirelessly. Much like Nintendo’s GameShare, it’s set to allow multiplayer across devices even with only one cartridge, although I haven't been able to test this feature yet.

蓝牙并不是唯一的连接升级。Nexus 现在支持 Wi-Fi 6 标准，可以连接 5 GHz 网络（之前的型号只能使用较慢的 2.4 GHz 网络）。这对于下载主机更新和 Evercade 每月免费游戏非常有用。它还启用了 Nexus 独有的 EverSync 功能，允许设备之间进行无线通信。就像任天堂的 GameShare 一样，它旨在实现仅需一张卡带即可跨设备联机，尽管我目前还无法测试此功能。

The underlying OS also gets a refresh. Hop into the settings menu and you'll find new customization tools. These let you adjust the color palette for the menu background and the glowing LED Nexus logo, select onscreen borders for non-widescreen games, and even tweak aspect ratios and virtual scan lines.

底层操作系统也进行了更新。进入设置菜单，你会发现新的自定义工具。这些工具允许你调整菜单背景和发光 LED Nexus 标志的配色方案，为非宽屏游戏选择屏幕边框，甚至可以调整纵横比和虚拟扫描线。
