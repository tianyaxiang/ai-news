Jensen Huang might be plotting a return and more of the latest Nvidia drama. Megaspeed claims Alice Huang hasn’t worked there since August of 2025.

围绕英伟达联合创始人兼前首席执行官黄仁勋的戏剧性事件正在迅速演变。我们最新一期的《Stepback》通讯应该能让你了解最新情况，但自今天早上以来，已经出现了一些新的进展。其中包括关于黄仁勋可能正在寻求重返首席执行官职位的传言，以及 Megaspeed 发表的一份声明，声称 Alice Huang 自 2025 年 8 月起就不再为该公司工作，这引发了更多的问题而非答案。

Some big names, including Elon Musk and Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney, have publicly thrown their support behind a public letter demanding Nvidia “bring Jensen back.” The public letter calling for Jensen Huang to be reinstated has over 150,000 signatures, but it’s difficult to verify their authenticity. Business Insider reports that signatures include “Tim Cruise, Hollywood actor” and “Xi Jingping,” likely a misspelling of Chinese president Xi Jinping.

包括埃隆·马斯克和 Epic Games 首席执行官蒂姆·斯威尼在内的一些大人物已经公开支持一封要求英伟达“让黄仁勋回来”的公开信。这封呼吁恢复黄仁勋职务的公开信拥有超过 15 万个签名，但很难核实其真实性。《商业内幕》报道称，签名中包括“好莱坞演员蒂姆·克鲁斯”和“Xi Jingping”（这很可能是对中国国家主席习近平的拼写错误）。

The Financial Times reports that Jensen Huang has discussed the possibility of returning as CEO with close allies, as some Silicon Valley insiders and employees attempt to pressure the board. Not all believe Jensen Huang should be allowed to return to his seat. According to the Financial Times, one ally acknowledged that, “Having an affair with a subordinate is a fireable offense.”

据《金融时报》报道，黄仁勋已与亲密盟友讨论了重返首席执行官职位的可能性，与此同时，一些硅谷内部人士和员工正试图向董事会施压。并非所有人都认为应该允许黄仁勋重返其职位。据《金融时报》报道，一位盟友承认：“与下属发生婚外情是可被解雇的行为。”

23andMe founder Anne Wojcicki told Bloomberg that “Power corrupts, and people start to think they are invincible … We are all complicit in making these people think they are gods. And people do stupid things when they think they are untouchable.” Wojcicki endured a messy public divorce from Google co-founder Sergey Brin, after it was revealed he was having an affair with an employee.

23andMe 创始人安妮·沃西基告诉彭博社：“权力会腐蚀人，人们开始认为自己是不可战胜的……我们都在助长这些人认为自己是神。当人们认为自己不可触碰时，就会做出愚蠢的事情。”沃西基曾经历过与谷歌联合创始人谢尔盖·布林混乱的公开离婚，此前有消息称布林与一名员工有染。

According to Bloomberg, many women in Silicon Valley see Jensen Huang as simply another example of a rich man abusing their position and “leveraging power to pursue sexual relationships with their employees.” Megaspeed released a statement saying that Alice Huang left the company “effective Aug. 30, 2025, and currently has no role with the company,” adding that “Megaspeed does not comment on Ms. Huang’s personal and private matters.”

据彭博社报道，硅谷的许多女性将黄仁勋视为富人滥用职权并“利用权力追求与员工性关系”的又一个例子。Megaspeed 发表声明称，Alice Huang 已于“2025 年 8 月 30 日起离职，目前在公司没有任何职务”，并补充说“Megaspeed 不会对黄女士的个人和私事发表评论”。
