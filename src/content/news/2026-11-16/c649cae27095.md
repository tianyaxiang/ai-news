# This AI Tamagotchi Turns Everyday Objects Into Needy Virtual Pets

Save this story. You forgot to water the plant. Instead of seeing yellowing leaves, you see a cute plant-shaped pet on a tiny screen, looking sad and dejected. Maybe that'll give you the push you needed to grab the watering can. As you pour water into the soil, the plant pet on the screen gets excited and happy.

保存这篇报道。你忘了给植物浇水。你看到的不是发黄的叶子，而是一个小屏幕上可爱的植物形状宠物，看起来既悲伤又沮丧。也许这会给你提供拿起喷壶所需的动力。当你往土壤里浇水时，屏幕上的植物宠物会变得兴奋和快乐。

That's the idea behind OATs, or Object Agent Twins—micro-agent AI avatars on tiny E Ink displays designed to pair with everyday household objects. The tech was created by Daehwa Kim, a fifth-year PhD student in Carnegie Mellon's Future Interfaces Group. Kim presented the project at the ACM Symposium on User Interface Software and Technology in Detroit in early November, a forum where academics and industry experts explore human-computer interfaces, and she earned two Honorable Mention awards.

这就是 OAT（物体代理孪生）背后的理念——这是一种安装在微型电子墨水屏上的微型代理 AI 头像，旨在与日常家居用品配对。这项技术由卡内基梅隆大学未来界面小组的五年级博士生 Daehwa Kim 开发。Kim 在 11 月初底特律举行的 ACM 用户界面软件与技术研讨会上展示了该项目，这是一个学术界和行业专家探索人机界面的论坛，她凭借该项目获得了两个荣誉提名奖。

You might remember Tamagotchi, the popular egg-shaped toys from the ’90s that housed digital pets requiring real-time care and attention. Kim’s goal was to bring a similar experience to physical objects in our lives. “We bring this joy of caring from virtual to physical objects,” she tells WIRED. While folks have encouraged Kim to sell OAT kits online, she hasn't decided whether to pursue it as a business opportunity.

你可能还记得 90 年代流行的蛋形玩具“拓麻歌子”（Tamagotchi），里面住着需要实时照料和关注的数字宠物。Kim 的目标是将类似的体验带入我们生活中的实物。她告诉《连线》杂志：“我们将这种照料的乐趣从虚拟物体带到了现实物体上。”虽然人们鼓励 Kim 在网上销售 OAT 套件，但她尚未决定是否将其作为商业机会来追求。

Courtesy of Daehwa Kim. Kim says everyone spends all day staring at mobile devices, and AI agents—even if they're designed to handle tasks on your behalf—still demand attention from your smartphone screen. OATs were a way to bring those interactions out into the real world.

图片由 Daehwa Kim 提供。Kim 说，每个人整天都盯着移动设备看，而 AI 代理——即使它们旨在代表你处理任务——仍然需要你从智能手机屏幕上分出注意力。OAT 是一种将这些互动带入现实世界的方式。

Giving AI a physical body isn't new, as companies rush to create “the next smartphone,” whether that's failed attempts like the Humane Ai Pin, OpenAI's upcoming hardware created in collaboration with famed ex-Apple designer Jony Ive, or Meta's Muse Charm. The pocket-sized gadget houses Meta's popular Muse AI agent and is expected to launch in December. Increasingly, though, companies have leaned on “cuteness” in their strategy for widespread adoption.

赋予 AI 实体并不新鲜，因为各家公司都在竞相创造“下一代智能手机”，无论是像 Humane Ai Pin 这样失败的尝试，还是 OpenAI 与著名苹果前设计师 Jony Ive 合作开发的即将推出的硬件，亦或是 Meta 的 Muse Charm。这款袖珍小工具内置了 Meta 受欢迎的 Muse AI 代理，预计将于 12 月发布。然而，越来越多的公司在推广普及的策略中开始依赖“可爱”这一元素。

OATs have an E Ink display connected to internal and external sensors that can track things like moisture, temperature, humidity, light, occupancy, and motion. (The hardware is relatively cheap, around $20 for the main module.) The sprites are created with Google Gemini's Nano Banana image-generation service, creating a suite of emotions for various states based on the sensor data. You can set all of this up through a web portal, where you can name your OAT and choose which sensors to use for the object you're attaching it to.

OAT 配备了一个电子墨水屏，连接到内部和外部传感器，可以跟踪水分、温度、湿度、光照、占用情况和运动等数据。（硬件相对便宜，主模块大约 20 美元。）这些精灵图像是使用 Google Gemini 的 Nano Banana 图像生成服务创建的，根据传感器数据为各种状态创建了一套表情。你可以通过门户网站设置所有这些内容，在那里你可以为你的 OAT 命名，并选择要为你所附加的物体使用哪些传感器。

The web app will generate “Positive” and “Negative” behaviors based on your object's name and description. So if the OAT is attached to a plant, positive triggers could be “consistent air temperature readings” and “low humidity levels” if the plant of choice prefers dry environments. However, you can customize these behaviors to suit your specific plant type.

该网络应用程序将根据你物体的名称和描述生成“积极”和“消极”的行为。因此，如果 OAT 附着在植物上，积极的触发因素可能是“持续的空气温度读数”，如果所选植物喜欢干燥环境，则可能是“低湿度水平”。不过，你可以自定义这些行为以适应你特定的植物类型。

It can be used for very simple things. Want to use your skateboard more often? Attach the OAT to your board, and if it hasn't detected motion for a few days, the avatar will look sad and lonely. You don't want that, do you? As Kim says, “These kinds of inanimate objects can be more expressive.” When you hop on the board, the OAT avatar will be happy, with fun faces.

它也可以用于非常简单的事情。想更频繁地使用滑板吗？把 OAT 贴在滑板上，如果它几天没检测到运动，头像就会看起来悲伤而孤独。你不想那样，对吧？正如 Kim 所说：“这些无生命的物体可以更具表现力。”当你跳上滑板时，OAT 头像会变得快乐，并做出有趣的表情。

The catch is that the system's battery life is really short, showing why this is more of a proof of concept than anything else. OATs last around three to four days, but paired with the sound-level sensor, they'll only last a day. It has a USB-C port for charging the lithium-ion polymer battery.

问题在于该系统的电池寿命非常短，这表明它更多是一个概念验证而非其他。OAT 大约能持续三到四天，但如果配合声级传感器使用，它们只能持续一天。它有一个用于为锂离子聚合物电池充电的 USB-C 端口。

Kim says she distributed OATs to many people and found that they used them in three different ways. One is, “Am I being used?” Like when you attach an OAT to a coffee machine and have the avatar get excited when the machine starts grinding beans. The second is for warning people near an object. For example, 3D printers release toxic fumes during use, and attaching an OAT to one can warn people that the device is operational. And the third is to track their own behaviors. Someone attached an OAT to a dumbbell to track how often they lifted weights, encouraging themselves to exercise regularly so they wouldn't disappoint the AI pet.

Kim 说她把 OAT 分发给了许多人，发现他们以三种不同的方式使用它。一种是“我正在被使用吗？”比如当你把 OAT 贴在咖啡机上，当机器开始磨豆时，头像会变得兴奋。第二种是警告物体附近的人。例如，3D 打印机在使用过程中会释放有毒烟雾，在上面贴一个 OAT 可以警告人们设备正在运行。第三种是跟踪他们自己的行为。有人把 OAT 贴在哑铃上以跟踪他们举重的频率，鼓励自己定期锻炼，这样就不会让 AI 宠物失望。

The demo at the symposium was popular, and Kim turned it into a competition to see who could come up with the most interesting ideas about what objects to pair with the OATs. Kim's website features a gallery with sprites people made, from an animated conference badge to a panda avatar for an Owala water bottle.

研讨会上的演示很受欢迎，Kim 将其变成了一场竞赛，看看谁能想出关于将什么物体与 OAT 配对的最有趣的点子。Kim 的网站上有一个展示人们制作的精灵的画廊，从动画会议胸牌到 Owala 水瓶的熊猫头像应有尽有。

Courtesy of Daehwa Kim. But the demo also ignited a huge discussion about anthropomorphizing artificial intelligence, and whether it’s proper to give these sprites humanlike traits and emotions to make people feel connected with them.

图片由 Daehwa Kim 提供。但这次演示也引发了关于人工智能拟人化的大规模讨论，以及赋予这些精灵人类特征和情感以使人们感到与它们产生联系是否得当。

It's already a topic in play within the AI industry. Companies often refer to their chatbot behaviors in human terms, like when they “hallucinate.” Anthropic announced a “dreaming” feature this summer where its AI agent cleaned up its local memory as a background process. These anthropomorphized terms can distort moral judgments about AI and cause people to overly trust the tools.

这已经是 AI 行业内的一个热门话题。公司经常用人类术语来描述聊天机器人的行为，比如当它们“产生幻觉”时。Anthropic 今年夏天宣布了一项“做梦”功能，其 AI 代理在后台进程中清理其本地内存。这些拟人化的术语可能会扭曲对 AI 的道德判断，并导致人们过度信任这些工具。

Kim says that's why OATs have very limited interactions, restricted to expressive emotions and the E Ink display. “Anthropomorphism can be very useful in certain ways, like helping people to change their behaviors,” Kim says. “People name their car, and they name their bike. I rarely see people who name their iPhone. I feel like in the future of computing we will have more of a relationship with AI, but we should definitely be careful not to abuse this."

Kim 说，这就是为什么 OAT 的交互非常有限，仅限于表达情感和电子墨水屏显示。“拟人化在某些方面非常有用，比如帮助人们改变行为，”Kim 说。“人们会给他们的车起名字，也会给他们的自行车起名字。我很少看到有人给他们的 iPhone 起名字。我觉得在未来的计算中，我们将与 AI 建立更多的关系，但我们绝对应该小心，不要滥用这一点。”
