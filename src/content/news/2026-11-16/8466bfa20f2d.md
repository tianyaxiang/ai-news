# An overview of Jensen Huang’s messy days

The StepbackColumnAITechAn overview of Jensen Huang’s messy daysThings moved fast for the now-former CEO of Nvidia. by Terrence O'Brien Nov 15, 2026, 1:00 PM UTC.

《The Stepback》专栏：人工智能科技——黄仁勋混乱时期的概述。对于这位英伟达前任首席执行官来说，一切发生得太快了。作者：Terrence O'Brien，2026年11月15日，世界标准时间下午1:00。

Terrence O'Brien is the Verge’s weekend editor. He’s covered the tech industry for over 18 years and knows a thing or two about synths. This is The Stepback, a weekly newsletter breaking down one essential story from the tech world.

Terrence O'Brien 是《The Verge》的周末编辑。他报道科技行业已超过18年，对合成器也颇有了解。这是《The Stepback》，一份每周解析科技界重要新闻的通讯。

How it started: On October 29th, The New York Times reported that Nvidia co-founder and showman CEO Jensen Huang appeared to be the target of a Chinese spy plot. Any major tech CEO potentially being compromised by the Chinese government is concerning — especially Jensen Huang.

事情的起因：10月29日，《纽约时报》报道称，英伟达联合创始人兼明星CEO黄仁勋似乎成为了中国间谍计划的目标。任何大型科技公司的CEO如果可能被中国政府渗透，都是令人担忧的，尤其是黄仁勋。

“If you could draw the network diagram of the entire US AI ecosystem, Nvidia would be at the center of that,” Kyle Chan, a foreign policy fellow at the Brookings Institution’s John L. Thornton China Center, previously told The Verge. “If you could draw the diagram of the entire Nvidia ecosystem, a single man is standing at the heart of all of that.”

布鲁金斯学会约翰·桑顿中国中心的外交政策研究员凯尔·陈（Kyle Chan）此前曾告诉《The Verge》：“如果你能画出整个美国人工智能生态系统的网络图，英伟达将处于中心位置。如果你能画出整个英伟达生态系统的图表，那么一个人正站在这一切的核心。”

The Times reported that Jensen Huang had developed a “close relationship” with Alice Huang (no relation), an executive at Singapore-based data center company Megaspeed. Alice Huang had been on the American government’s radar for some time, perhaps as early as 2024.

《纽约时报》报道称，黄仁勋与总部位于新加坡的数据中心公司 Megaspeed 的高管 Alice Huang（两人无亲属关系）建立了“亲密关系”。Alice Huang 早已被美国政府盯上，时间可能最早可追溯至2024年。

Her company was investigated by the US Commerce Department over suspicions it was illegally smuggling Nvidia’s AI chips into China in violation of US government trade restrictions. Singapore authorities confiscated her passport back in June of 2025, eventually barring her from the country, after she reportedly gave “inaccurate information” to police.

她的公司曾因涉嫌违反美国政府贸易限制、非法将英伟达的人工智能芯片走私到中国而受到美国商务部的调查。据报道，在 Alice Huang 向警方提供“不准确信息”后，新加坡当局于2025年6月没收了她的护照，并最终禁止她入境。

And perhaps more concerningly, some US officials believed she was passing information, possibly about Nvidia’s designs or the White House’s deliberations over trade restrictions, directly back to Beijing.

更令人担忧的是，一些美国官员认为她一直在将信息直接传回北京，这些信息可能涉及英伟达的设计方案或白宫关于贸易限制的审议内容。

This made her relationship with Jensen Huang concerning for multiple reasons. Nvidia’s chips are considered crucial to America’s AI superiority and a matter of national security by the White House. The government has restricted the sale of Nvidia’s most advanced chips to China since the Biden administration.

这使得她与黄仁勋的关系因多种原因而变得令人担忧。英伟达的芯片被白宫视为美国人工智能优势的关键，也是国家安全问题。自拜登政府执政以来，美国政府一直限制向中国出售英伟达最先进的芯片。

But Jensen Huang has routinely pushed to loosen those trade restrictions. Meanwhile, he had gotten quite cozy with President Trump, once even putting him on speakerphone on-stage at the All-In Summit.

但黄仁勋经常推动放宽这些贸易限制。与此同时，他与特朗普总统的关系也相当密切，甚至曾有一次在“All-In”峰会的舞台上通过免提电话与特朗普通话。

Then last week, things escalated quickly. On Friday, November 6th, the Times reported that Alice Huang was specifically recruited by China’s Ministry of State Security to spy on Nvidia’s CEO. Though, there is some disagreement among US intelligence officials over the strength of evidence tying Alice Huang to Beijing.

上周，事态迅速升级。11月6日星期五，《纽约时报》报道称，Alice Huang 是被中国国家安全部专门招募来监视英伟达CEO的。不过，美国情报官员对于将 Alice Huang 与北京联系起来的证据力度存在一些分歧。

While the paper did not confirm it at the time, rumors suggested the “close relationship” between Jensen Huang and Alice Huang was romantic in nature. On Sunday, November 8th, Jensen Huang admitted to having an extramarital affair, but did not name the Megaspeed exec and alleged Chinese spy specifically.

虽然该报当时并未证实，但有传言称黄仁勋与 Alice Huang 之间的“亲密关系”带有浪漫色彩。11月8日星期日，黄仁勋承认自己有婚外情，但并未具体点名那位 Megaspeed 高管及所谓的中国间谍。

Nvidia’s board announced that it was launching an investigation into its CEO shortly after. Many believed that Jensen Huang would survive the scandal. His company’s importance to America’s booming AI industry and his crucial role at the company seemed to make him nearly untouchable.

此后不久，英伟达董事会宣布对首席执行官展开调查。许多人认为黄仁勋能挺过这场丑闻。他的公司对美国蓬勃发展的人工智能产业的重要性，以及他在公司中的关键角色，似乎使他几乎不可撼动。

He was, after all, the only CEO the company had ever known with no clear and obvious succession plan in place, and he sat atop an unusual corporate structure with around 60 direct reports.

毕竟，他是该公司历史上唯一的CEO，且没有明确的继任计划，他所处的公司架构也非常特殊，拥有约60名直接下属。

Then on Wednesday, November 11th, The New York Times reported that Jensen Huang had a second affair — this one with one of his direct reports, Kimberly Powell, Nvidia’s vice president of health care.

11月11日星期三，《纽约时报》报道称黄仁勋还有第二段婚外情——对象是他的一名直接下属，英伟达医疗保健副总裁金伯利·鲍威尔（Kimberly Powell）。

Whether that was the last straw or if they were afraid it would be merely the tip of the iceberg, the board was ready to push Jensen Huang out, according to reports circulating that evening. According to Bloomberg, he had accepted his fate Wednesday evening reportedly looking “broken and shattered” and telling people, “I’m a piece of shit.”

据当晚流传的报道，无论这是压死骆驼的最后一根稻草，还是董事会担心这只是冰山一角，董事会都已准备好将黄仁勋赶下台。据彭博社报道，他在周三晚上接受了自己的命运，据称当时他看起来“心碎且崩溃”，并对人说：“我真是个渣滓。”

On Thursday, November 12th, Nvidia announced that Jensen Huang had resigned as CEO of Nvidia.

11月12日星期四，英伟达宣布黄仁勋已辞去英伟达首席执行官一职。

How it’s going: CFO Colette Kress was immediately named interim CEO, and acknowledged to employees that this has been an “extraordinarily difficult period.” Harvey Jones was also named the first board chair in the company’s history, already signaling there would be some changes to the corporate structure.

现状：首席财务官科莱特·克雷斯（Colette Kress）立即被任命为临时首席执行官，并向员工承认这是一个“极其困难的时期”。哈维·琼斯（Harvey Jones）也被任命为公司历史上首位董事会主席，这预示着公司架构将发生一些变化。

In a memo to employees, Jones said the company would “search for a permanent successor looking at both internal and external candidates.” But the destabilizing impact was immediate. Nvidia’s stock closed at an all-time high of $239.24 on October 6th. It closed at $162.85 on November 13th.

琼斯在给员工的备忘录中表示，公司将“寻找一位永久继任者，同时考虑内部和外部候选人”。但这种破坏性的影响是立竿见影的。英伟达股价在10月6日收于239.24美元的历史高点，而在11月13日收于162.85美元。

It’s hard to overstate the importance of Nvidia to the US’s AI arms race with China, and the industry in general. The company has invested $99 billion in various AI labs, startups, and neo clouds. Plus has announced plans to mobilize over $500 billion in financing for building out AI data centers and other infrastructure.

英伟达对于美国与中国的人工智能军备竞赛以及整个行业的重要性怎么强调都不为过。该公司已在各种人工智能实验室、初创公司和新云服务上投资了990亿美元。此外，该公司还宣布计划筹集超过5000亿美元的资金，用于建设人工智能数据中心和其他基础设施。
