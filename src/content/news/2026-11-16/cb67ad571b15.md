United Launch Alliance’s Vulcan rocket and 40 Amazon Leo broadband satellites fell back to Earth minutes after liftoff Sunday, a crushing setback that will ripple across all sectors of the US space industry.

联合发射联盟（ULA）的“火神”火箭及搭载的40颗亚马逊Leo宽带卫星在周日升空数分钟后坠回地球，这一沉重打击将在美国航天工业的各个领域引发连锁反应。

The Vulcan rocket is on contract to launch roughly half of the US military’s national security space missions in the next few years, and Sunday’s mission was the first of 38 Vulcan launches booked by Amazon to build out its network of broadband Internet satellites aimed at competing with SpaceX’s Starlink.

“火神”火箭已签约承担未来几年美国军方约一半的国家安全航天任务。周日的任务是亚马逊预订的38次“火神”发射中的首次，旨在构建其宽带互联网卫星网络，以与SpaceX的“星链”竞争。

Sunday’s failure is expected to ground the rocket for an indefinite period of time. Vulcan is no stranger to close calls, having overcome failures with its strap-on solid rocket boosters on two of its four previous flights.

预计周日的失败将导致该火箭无限期停飞。“火神”火箭并非第一次经历险情，在其此前四次飞行中，有两次曾克服了助推器故障。

In each case, the rocket corrected for those problems and continued into orbit to deploy its payloads. The incidents trigged months-long reviews and investigations before ULA could resume Vulcan flights.

在这些案例中，火箭都修正了问题并继续进入轨道部署载荷。这些事故在ULA恢复“火神”飞行前，引发了长达数月的审查和调查。

Sunday’s launch succumbed to a different kind of failure. The six solid rocket boosters appeared to perform well in the first two minutes after Vulcan’s liftoff from Cape Canaveral Space Force Station, Florida, at 12:40 pm EDT (17:40 UTC).

周日的发射遭遇了另一种类型的故障。在佛罗里达州卡纳维拉尔角太空军基地于美国东部时间下午12:40（协调世界时17:40）发射后的前两分钟内，六枚固体火箭助推器表现良好。

In a statement, ULA said the rocket “suffered an anomaly 10 minutes into the flight and failed to deliver the Amazon mission to orbit.” The company said it did not immediately have “any specific data to understand what occurred.”

ULA在一份声明中表示，火箭“在飞行10分钟后出现异常，未能将亚马逊任务送入轨道”。该公司称，目前尚无“任何具体数据来了解发生了什么”。

“ULA exists to provide assured access to space for our nation and for the customers who depend on us,” the company said in a statement. “That purpose is why we take on some of the hardest missions in spaceflight, and why we hold ourselves to the standard of mission success. Today, we did not meet that standard.”

“ULA的存在是为了确保我国及依赖我们的客户能够进入太空，”该公司在声明中表示。“这一目标正是我们承担航天领域最艰巨任务的原因，也是我们坚持任务成功标准的原因。今天，我们没有达到这一标准。”

Engineers will investigate the failure to “find the cause, determine the appropriate corrective action, and return to flight as quickly as possible,” ULA said. “ULA is committed to full transparency with our customers, and they will have visibility into the investigation process, which utilizes rigorous methodology and corrective action. We will provide further updates when new information becomes available.”

ULA表示，工程师们将调查此次故障，以“找出原因，确定适当的纠正措施，并尽快恢复飞行”。“ULA致力于对客户保持完全透明，他们将能够了解调查过程，该过程采用了严格的方法和纠正措施。一旦有新信息，我们将提供进一步更新。”

ULA’s Vulcan rocket is powered by two BE-4 main engines made by Blue Origin. Credit: United Launch Alliance

ULA的“火神”火箭由蓝色起源公司制造的两台BE-4主发动机提供动力。图片来源：联合发射联盟。

Something amiss The first indications of trouble Sunday appeared to crop up before the 10-minute mark of the flight, the point in time when ULA initially said the anomaly occurred.

出现异常：周日出现问题的最初迹象似乎在飞行10分钟之前就已显现，而ULA最初称异常发生在10分钟时。

Multiple sources told Ars the anomaly that impacted the launch occurred in the booster stage of the rocket, not the Centaur V upper stage or the solid rocket boosters. It could not immediately be determined whether the booster stage issue was caused by the two methane-burning BE-4 engines that power it, some other part of the booster that feeds propellant into the engines, or another problem.

多位消息人士告诉Ars，影响发射的异常发生在火箭的助推级，而非半人马座V级（Centaur V）上面级或固体火箭助推器。目前尚无法立即确定助推级的问题是由为其提供动力的两台甲烷燃烧BE-4发动机引起的，还是由助推器中向发动机输送推进剂的其他部分或其他问题引起的。

Heading northeast from Cape Canaveral, Vulcan jettisoned its spent solid rocket boosters on time, leaving the launcher flying on the power of its twin BE-4 main engines supplied by Blue Origin, Jeff Bezos’s space company.

“火神”火箭从卡纳维拉尔角向东北方向飞行，按计划抛弃了耗尽的固体火箭助推器，随后依靠杰夫·贝索斯旗下蓝色起源公司提供的两台BE-4主发动机继续飞行。

The BE-4s, each designed to produce more than a half-million pounds of thrust, were supposed to shut down just shy of the flight’s five-minute mark. However, they continued firing for at least 30 additional seconds.

每台BE-4发动机的设计推力超过50万磅，本应在飞行接近5分钟时关闭。然而，它们至少又持续工作了30秒。

Such an overburn could be an indication the booster stage was not performing as expected and operated longer to try to compensate for the underperformance.

这种过度燃烧可能表明助推级未按预期运行，通过延长工作时间来试图弥补性能不足。

A mission animation on ULA’s broadcast, driven by real-time telemetry data from the rocket, showed Vulcan start to spin moments after booster engine shutdown. The spin continued through separation of the rocket’s Centaur V upper stage a few seconds later.

ULA广播中的任务动画由火箭的实时遥测数据驱动，显示“火神”在助推发动机关闭后不久开始旋转。这种旋转在几秒钟后火箭半人马座V级分离时仍在持续。

ULA’s launch commentator confirmed ignition of the Centaur’s two RL10 engines, and the animation showed them vectoring thrust in an apparent attempt to put the rocket back on course. ULA ended its live broadcast a few minutes later without discussion of the anomaly. The company then released a statement on social media declaring the launch a failure.

ULA的发射评论员确认了半人马座两台RL10发动机的点火，动画显示它们正在进行矢量推力调整，显然是试图让火箭回到轨道。几分钟后，ULA结束了直播，未讨论此次异常。随后，该公司在社交媒体上发表声明，宣布发射失败。

This was the first total mission failure for United Launch Alliance since its formation in 2006 with the merger of the rocket divisions of Boeing and Lockheed Martin.

这是联合发射联盟自2006年由波音和洛克希德·马丁公司的火箭部门合并成立以来的首次任务彻底失败。

Wider impacts Sunday’s incident is bad news for ULA’s two primary customers: Amazon and the US Space Force. It’s also a potential setback for NASA’s Artemis program to return astronauts to the Moon.

更广泛的影响：周日的事故对ULA的两个主要客户——亚马逊和美国太空军来说是个坏消息。这也可能对NASA旨在将宇航员送回月球的“阿尔忒弥斯”计划造成挫折。

First, Amazon. The loss of 40 Amazon Leo satellites onboard the Vulcan rocket is, by itself, a secondary concern next to the inevitable grounding of the company’s primary launch provider. Amazon has 37 more Vulcan launches reserved with ULA, each of which is slated to deliver 40 or more Amazon Leo satellites to orbit.

首先是亚马逊。损失“火神”火箭上搭载的40颗亚马逊Leo卫星，与该公司主要发射服务商不可避免的停飞相比，只是次要问题。亚马逊在ULA还预订了37次“火神”发射任务，每次计划将40颗或更多的亚马逊Leo卫星送入轨道。

The Amazon Leo constellation today consists of 396 satellites, a fraction of the network’s planned total of 3,292 first-generation spacecraft. Amazon officials told Ars in October that the company has enough satellites to start initial commercial Internet service by the end of the year, but more launches are required to realize the network’s full capacity.

亚马逊Leo星座目前由396颗卫星组成，仅为其计划总数3,292颗第一代航天器的一小部分。亚马逊官员10月份告诉Ars，公司有足够的卫星在年底前启动初步商业互联网服务，但要实现网络全部容量，还需要更多发射。

Amazon is setting up its service as the first head-to-head competitor in the consumer satellite broadband market with the Starlink network owned by Elon Musk and SpaceX. OneWeb and other companies also offer Internet connectivity from space, but their business models focus on beaming capacity to traditional telco providers, not directly to consumers.

亚马逊正将其服务定位为消费级卫星宽带市场中，与埃隆·马斯克及SpaceX旗下“星链”网络直接竞争的首个对手。OneWeb和其他公司也提供太空互联网连接，但其商业模式侧重于向传统电信运营商提供容量，而非直接面向消费者。
