# M6 Mac Mini Early Black Friday Sale (2026)

All configurations of the Mac Mini are on sale today on Amazon, starting at $779 for the base M6 model. That's $120 off the full retail price that Apple charges.

亚马逊今日对所有配置的 Mac Mini 进行了促销，基础款 M6 机型起售价为 779 美元。这比苹果官方零售价优惠了 120 美元。

For nearly half of this year, the Mac Mini was unavailable to buy; it was either sold out at retailers or subject to wait times that stretched up to three months long. But at the end of August, Apple launched a new model, which not only introduced the M6 (or M5 Pro) chip inside, but also restocked the Mac Mini so that you can actually just go out and buy one.

今年近一半的时间里，Mac Mini 都处于缺货状态；要么是零售商售罄，要么是等待时间长达三个月。但在八月底，苹果发布了新款机型，不仅搭载了 M6（或 M5 Pro）芯片，还补充了库存，让消费者能够直接购买。

The sharp escalation in demand even took Apple by surprise, by its own admission. AI enthusiasts were buying these up for the quick and simple access to an always-on computer with enough memory to run models locally in an affordable package.

苹果承认，需求的急剧增长甚至让公司感到意外。人工智能爱好者们纷纷抢购这些设备，因为它们能以实惠的价格提供快速、简单的访问体验，且具备足够的内存来本地运行模型。

The M6 comes with 16 GB of RAM, which is barely enough to run some smaller models. Amazon also sells a 24-GB model (with 512 GB of storage) for $1,150. If you want to bump up the storage capability to 32 GB, you'll have to buy the machine directly from Apple. Either way, the extra storage will make your Mini quite a bit more expensive than the base model, but it's still $150 off the retail price.

M6 机型配备 16 GB 内存，仅能勉强运行一些较小的模型。亚马逊还以 1,150 美元的价格出售 24 GB 内存（及 512 GB 存储）的版本。如果你想将内存提升至 32 GB，则必须直接从苹果购买。无论哪种方式，额外的内存都会使你的 Mini 比基础款贵出不少，但仍比零售价便宜 150 美元。

Best Buy is also selling the previous-gen M4 model for $649 if you're really trying to save cash—or just need a basic computer for work. The M6 does offer a substantial upgrade to GPU performance over the M4, though, so if you're planning to do any on-device AI work, it's worth the extra $120.

如果你确实想省钱，或者只需要一台基础办公电脑，百思买（Best Buy）目前也在以 649 美元的价格出售上一代 M4 机型。不过，M6 的 GPU 性能相比 M4 有显著提升，因此如果你计划进行任何设备端 AI 工作，多花这 120 美元是值得的。

This isn't the lowest price I've seen the Mac Mini reach, but it's close. For one day in October, it dropped by an extra $10. I can't say for sure whether we'll see an additional price decrease during Black Friday. My gut says no, but if you're worried about it, you might want to put it in your shopping cart and wait before clicking Buy.

这不是我见过的 Mac Mini 最低价格，但已经很接近了。十月份曾有一天价格额外下调了 10 美元。我无法确定黑色星期五期间是否还会进一步降价。我的直觉是不会，但如果你担心这一点，可以先把它加入购物车，等一等再下单。

The best deal in the Mac world heading into Black Friday, however, is the M5 MacBook Air. It's currently $1,099, and that still gets you 16 GB of unified memory.

然而，在迈向黑色星期五的过程中，Mac 产品线中最划算的交易是 M5 MacBook Air。它目前售价 1,099 美元，且依然配备 16 GB 统一内存。
