# AI DJs Completely Kill My Groove

I usually can’t pinpoint the moment I stop using a software feature, with most tools drifting out of fashion without an expiration date. That’s not the case with YouTube Music’s “Beyond the Beat,” an AI-generated DJ that explains songs to you.

我通常无法准确指出自己是在什么时候停止使用某项软件功能的，大多数工具都是在不知不觉中过时，并没有明确的“过期日期”。但 YouTube Music 的“Beyond the Beat”却并非如此，这是一个会为你讲解歌曲的 AI 生成 DJ。

YouTube Music is my music-streaming service of choice. And since I’m a software reporter, I always enable the beta features available through YouTube Labs, which include Beyond the Beat. YouTube’s AI DJ randomly interjects for about 30 seconds between songs, teeing up the vibe shift and providing context about the next track.

YouTube Music 是我首选的音乐流媒体服务。由于我是一名软件记者，我总是会开启 YouTube Labs 提供的测试版功能，其中就包括“Beyond the Beat”。YouTube 的 AI DJ 会在歌曲之间随机插话约 30 秒，预告氛围的转变并提供下一首曲目的背景信息。

More listeners are encountering synthetic commentary built into their music streaming apps. While YouTube launched this experimental AI DJ in 2025, Spotify first introduced a similar tool back in 2023. It doubled down on its AI DJ efforts in November, expanding the different personalities available to users and adding more controls over the commentary.

越来越多的听众开始在他们的音乐流媒体应用中遇到这种合成评论。虽然 YouTube 在 2025 年才推出这款实验性 AI DJ，但 Spotify 早在 2023 年就引入了类似的工具。去年 11 月，Spotify 加大了对 AI DJ 的投入，增加了用户可选的个性化设置，并提供了更多对评论内容的控制选项。

These AI DJs aren’t just annoying. Their attempts to provide helpful context and mimic human candor ultimately fall flat. Lucky for me, both features are optional and avoidable.

这些 AI DJ 不仅仅是令人讨厌。它们试图提供有用的背景信息并模仿人类的坦率，但最终都显得苍白无力。幸运的是，这两个功能都是可选且可以关闭的。

After months of leaving it enabled, a bad experience with Beyond the Beat this week pushed me to swear off AI DJs altogether. I was listening to a pop divas playlist as part of my morning workout and got slapped with 30 seconds of exposition after a Slayyyter track:

在开启该功能数月后，本周一次糟糕的“Beyond the Beat”体验让我发誓彻底弃用 AI DJ。当时我正在听一份流行天后歌单作为晨间锻炼的背景音乐，在播放完一首 Slayyyter 的歌后，我被迫听了 30 秒的解说：

“Hey Boy delivers that high-energy surge perfectly, but we're shifting our focus to a sharper, more visceral reality. In Rock Music, Charli XXC [sic] weaves in a biting, dark sense of humor regarding the physical toll of her relentless 2024 and 2025 touring. She turns the genuine nerve damage she sustained on the road into a strange sort of battle scar, reclaiming the exhaustion of a weary party girl as a badge of honor. It's an unflinching look at the cost behind the spectacle, where the grind of the industry becomes the actual subject matter. Here is Rock Music by Charli XXC [sic].”

“《Hey Boy》完美地传递了那种高能量的冲击，但现在我们将焦点转向一种更尖锐、更直观的现实。在《Rock Music》中，Charli XXC（原文如此）融入了一种尖刻而黑暗的幽默感，讲述了她 2024 年和 2025 年不间断巡演带来的身体损耗。她将自己在路上遭受的真实神经损伤转化为一种奇怪的伤疤，将疲惫派对女孩的精疲力竭重新定义为一种荣誉勋章。这是对光鲜亮丽背后的代价的一次直面审视，工业的磨砺成为了真正的主题。接下来是 Charli XXC（原文如此）的《Rock Music》。”

What a lengthy paragraph of platitudes with little substance. That’s not even mentioning how the AI DJ butchered the pronunciation of a little-known, indie act called Charli XCX. This flub reflected my overall experience with YouTube Music’s AI DJ. It took me out of the groove and didn’t feel additive.

多么冗长的一段空话，几乎没有任何实质内容。更不用说这个 AI DJ 还把一个名为 Charli XCX 的小众独立艺人的名字读得一塌糊涂。这次失误反映了我对 YouTube Music AI DJ 的整体感受：它打断了我的节奏，并没有带来任何增值体验。

In another example, it went in-depth on the meaning of Olivia Rodrigo’s Expectations remix featuring Chappell Roan, calling it a "structural interpretation” that unpacks the “nuances of queer dating.” Then YouTube Music played the original track, sans Roan; so much for the queer yearning.

在另一个例子中，它深入探讨了 Olivia Rodrigo 与 Chappell Roan 合作的《Expectations》混音版的含义，称其为一种“结构性解读”，剖析了“酷儿约会的细微差别”。结果 YouTube Music 播放的却是原版曲目，根本没有 Roan 的参与；所谓的“酷儿渴望”也就无从谈起了。

Curious how Spotify’s AI DJ experience would compare, I upgraded my account to the premium tier for access to that beta software. One of the biggest recent changes to Spotify’s AI DJ was adding new voices to the lineup, so I went into my settings and played the introduction clips for each of the eight English-language voices.

出于好奇，想看看 Spotify 的 AI DJ 体验如何，我将账户升级到了高级会员以使用该测试版软件。Spotify AI DJ 最近最大的变化之一是增加了新的语音选项，所以我进入设置，播放了八种英语语音各自的介绍片段。

Maybe I was already a bit miffed from my YouTube Music experience because these voices immediately started to frustrate me. Multiple voices leaned into filler words such as “you know” and “like,” as the AI described the next songs it would play. Knowing an AI voice is being trained to use filler words to sound more human gave me the uncanny-valley ick.

也许是因为 YouTube Music 的体验让我有些恼火，这些声音立刻让我感到沮丧。当 AI 描述接下来要播放的歌曲时，多个语音选项都倾向于使用“你知道的”、“像”之类的口头禅。想到 AI 语音被训练使用这些口头禅来模仿人类，这让我产生了一种恐怖谷效应带来的不适感。

Spotify’s AI DJ was fairly different from YouTube Music’s version. On Spotify, the AI DJ is also an algorithmically guided experience that picks songs for you based on past listening habits and song requests. Overall, Spotify’s synthetic commentary felt tighter and more tolerable than YouTube’s blabbering DJ. That said, neither software experience was enjoyable enough for me to return.

Spotify 的 AI DJ 与 YouTube Music 的版本有很大不同。在 Spotify 上，AI DJ 同样是一种基于算法的体验，它会根据你过去的收听习惯和点歌记录为你挑选歌曲。总的来说，Spotify 的合成评论比 YouTube 那喋喋不休的 DJ 感觉更紧凑、更能让人接受。话虽如此，这两种软件体验都无法让我产生再次使用的兴趣。

Although Spotify claims it has “80 percent of listeners coming back to DJ week after week,” according to the company’s launch blog, I won’t be one of them. My rejection of AI DJs isn’t based on how they audibly sound. The voices, from both companies, are decent and sound convincingly human. The content of their commentary is what truly turns me off. Whether it’s empty platitudes or mimicking human mistakes, I’ve heard enough.

尽管根据 Spotify 的发布博客，该公司声称“80% 的听众每周都会回来使用 DJ 功能”，但我不会是其中之一。我拒绝 AI DJ 并不是因为它们听起来怎么样。两家公司的语音都很不错，听起来也很有说服力。真正让我反感的是它们评论的内容。无论是空洞的陈词滥调，还是对人类错误的拙劣模仿，我都已经听够了。
