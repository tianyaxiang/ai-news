Due to the Microsoft outage on Friday, Phantasialand did not realize that there was too much chlorine in the pool. 15 guests were injured.

由于周五发生的微软系统故障，Phantasialand乐园未能及时发现泳池内氯含量过高。共有15名游客受伤。

We were all exposed to this problem: On Friday, there was a disruption that paralyzed the US software corporation Microsoft. Among other things, the email program Outlook.

我们都受到了这一问题的影响：周五发生了一起导致美国软件巨头微软瘫痪的故障，其中包括电子邮件程序Outlook。

Phantasialand was also affected. Due to the disruption, no emails were received, as the company informed WDR on Saturday. Thus, no one noticed that the maintenance security system of the sauna area had long since sounded an alarm via email. Because the automatic dosing system had pumped too much chlorine into the pool.

Phantasialand也受到了波及。据该公司周六向WDR透露，由于故障，邮件无法送达。因此，没有人注意到桑拿区的维护安全系统早已通过电子邮件发出了警报，因为自动加药系统向泳池中注入了过量的氯。

As a result, guests at the Hotel Ling Bao of Phantasialand in Brühl were injured. They complained of health problems after visiting the pool. It was temporarily closed, but has since been reopened, as the fire department informed WDR on Saturday.

这导致位于布吕尔（Brühl）的Phantasialand乐园灵宝酒店（Hotel Ling Bao）的客人们受伤。他们在前往泳池后出现了健康问题。据消防部门周六向WDR通报，泳池曾一度关闭，但目前已重新开放。

Because of the complaints from hotel visitors, rescue workers and the fire department were called. 13 guests complained of respiratory irritation; ultimately, the number of affected people rose to a total of 15.

由于酒店游客的投诉，救援人员和消防队被召集到现场。最初有13名客人抱怨呼吸道受到刺激，最终受影响人数上升至15人。

Initially, it was said that five guests were taken to the hospital; later, the number was corrected to four, one of whom remained in the hospital for observation due to a moderate injury.

最初有消息称五名客人被送往医院，随后人数被更正为四人，其中一人因中度受伤留院观察。

In the hotel, tests by emergency forces revealed a defect in the automatic dosing system for the pool. This had led to a significantly excessive chlorine content. The water was therefore replaced.

在酒店内，应急部队的测试显示泳池的自动加药系统存在缺陷，这导致氯含量严重超标。因此，泳池水已被更换。
