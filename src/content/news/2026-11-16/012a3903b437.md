# The Sony Aibo was a robot revolution dressed like a dog

索尼 Aibo：披着狗皮的机器人革命

In the 1990s, Sony was absolutely convinced that we were on the precipice of a robot takeover. Pretty soon, the company decided, we’d all be living among countless humanoid robots. Some would be our coworkers; others would be our friends; and at least one would be our pet. And “pet” was exactly what Sony was going for when it created the Aibo. It was supposed to be part of the family.

在 20 世纪 90 年代，索尼坚信我们正处于机器人时代的边缘。该公司当时认定，很快我们所有人都会生活在无数人形机器人之中。其中一些会成为我们的同事，另一些会成为我们的朋友，而至少会有一个成为我们的宠物。当索尼创造 Aibo 时，它追求的目标正是“宠物”。它本应成为家庭的一员。

On this episode of Version History, we go back in time and tell the story of the Aibo. David Pierce is joined by The Verge’s Victoria Song and Jennifer Pattison Tuohy, and together they discuss the origins of this remarkable robot dog, and its impressive success and longevity. They also try to figure out why Aibo had such an effect on its owners, what Sony got right (and wrong) about robots, and whether the idea of a robo-pet will ever be a global phenomenon.

在本期《版本历史》（Version History）节目中，我们将回到过去，讲述 Aibo 的故事。大卫·皮尔斯（David Pierce）与《The Verge》的维多利亚·宋（Victoria Song）和詹妮弗·帕蒂森·图希（Jennifer Pattison Tuohy）共同探讨了这只非凡机器狗的起源，以及它令人印象深刻的成功与长寿。他们还试图探究为什么 Aibo 对其主人产生了如此大的影响，索尼在机器人领域做对了（以及做错了）什么，以及机器宠物这一概念是否会成为全球性的现象。
