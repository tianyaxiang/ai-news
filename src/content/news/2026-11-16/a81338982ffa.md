Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2: cheaper, faster, and one API change to check Anthropic released Claude Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 on November 9. Price: $8 per million input tokens and $40 per million output, lower than 5.1. An optional fast mode generates up to 2.5× faster for $16/$80. Speed: output is up to 30% faster than 5.1. Availability: Fable 5.2 is on every platform, including AWS, Google Cloud and Azure, as claude-fable-5-2. Mythos 5.2's cyber capabilities go through Anthropic's Cyber Verification Program. Which model to start with: Anthropic still tells most teams to start on Opus 5.5 and move up to Fable only for the hardest work. Its own numbers are the main reason to look anyway: on FrontierCode 2.0, Fable 5.2 at High effort scored about level with Opus 5.5 at Max effort (45.3% vs 45.5%) for roughly half the cost per task. That's Anthropic's benchmark, not ours.
