# How the world’s fires changed while humanity hit pause

Wildfires occur worldwide, and they can sometimes become serious threats to people’s lives. A series of massive bushfires from 2019 to 2020 swept across southeastern Australia and destroyed thousands of homes. Recently, the 2025 Los Angeles wildfires spread widely across the city, resulting in massive damage to local communities. Many things can cause wildfires, but humans are the only factor that can also make preventative efforts.

野火在世界各地发生，有时会对人们的生命构成严重威胁。2019年至2020年间，一系列大规模丛林大火席卷了澳大利亚东南部，摧毁了数千所房屋。最近，2025年洛杉矶野火在城市中大范围蔓延，对当地社区造成了巨大破坏。许多因素都可能引发野火，但人类是唯一能够采取预防措施的因素。

Measuring people’s impact on fire is difficult because of the mix of human and natural influences. A team led by Jianghao Wang, a professor at the Chinese Academy of Sciences, recently published a study in Cell Reports Sustainability comparing the COVID-19 era with other years. Data from the 2020 lockdowns suggest that lower human activity at this time had the net effect of lowering average fire activity.

由于人类和自然影响相互交织，衡量人类对火灾的影响十分困难。由中国科学院教授王江浩领导的一个研究团队最近在《细胞报告：可持续性》（Cell Reports Sustainability）上发表了一项研究，将新冠疫情时期与其他年份进行了比较。2020年封锁期间的数据表明，当时人类活动的减少在净效应上降低了平均火灾活动水平。

Untangling humans from the weather: Fire doesn’t just happen anywhere. Although plenty of flammable materials exist in natural settings, not every place experiences wildfires. Some prairies burn almost every year, while many forests go without big fires for hundreds of years. Researchers explain these differences in terms of fire regimes, which are patterns describing when, how often, and how widely and intensely fires occur.

理清人类与天气的关系：火灾并非随处发生。尽管自然环境中存在大量易燃物质，但并非每个地方都会发生野火。一些草原几乎每年都会燃烧，而许多森林则几百年都没有发生过大火。研究人员用“火灾状况”（fire regimes）来解释这些差异，这是一种描述火灾发生时间、频率、范围和强度的模式。

Weather is a major factor affecting fire regimes. An abundance of rain helps grow grass and trees that can become fuel for fires. Whether they do or not, however, can depend on the moisture inside plants; plants with enough moisture do not burn easily. By contrast, dry conditions make them more likely to catch fire. When a drought lasts, vegetation becomes increasingly flammable. Strong winds also accelerate fire growth and spread enormously.

天气是影响火灾状况的主要因素。充足的降雨有助于草木生长，这些草木可能成为火灾的燃料。然而，它们是否会燃烧取决于植物内部的水分；水分充足的植物不易燃烧。相比之下，干燥的环境使它们更容易着火。当干旱持续时，植被的易燃性会增加。强风也会极大地加速火灾的增长和蔓延。

Human activity often combines with weather conditions to cause fires. Even routine outdoor recreation can accidentally start a fire. More people venture outdoors during sunny, drier times of the year, increasing the risk of accidental fires when dry vegetation is more likely to be present. The double-sided role of humans in fire also adds to the difficulty. Humans do start fires, but they also put them out.

人类活动通常与天气条件结合引发火灾。即使是日常的户外休闲活动也可能意外引发火灾。在一年中阳光充足、气候干燥的时期，更多人会到户外活动，这增加了在干燥植被更容易出现时发生意外火灾的风险。人类在火灾中的双重角色也增加了研究难度。人类确实会引发火灾，但他们也会扑灭火灾。

Firefighters contain fires that could have gone worse if no action had been taken and can keep them from spreading. Humans can also take preventative actions, such as clearing flammable material. Sometimes the line between starting fires and preventing them becomes blurred. One way to clear out flammable material is to intentionally set fires to make future fires more manageable. However, these fires are hard to distinguish from other fires through satellite monitoring.

消防员可以控制那些如果不采取行动可能会恶化的火灾，并防止其蔓延。人类还可以采取预防措施，例如清理易燃物。有时，引发火灾和预防火灾之间的界限变得模糊。清理易燃物的一种方法是人为纵火，以使未来的火灾更易于管理。然而，通过卫星监测很难将这些火灾与其他火灾区分开来。

A natural experiment in staying home: An experiment is one way to find out how humans affect wildfires. If everyone stayed home under the same weather, their impact on fires would be revealed. This is why Wang’s research team focused on the COVID-19 era. It was what’s called a “natural experiment,” in which experimental conditions arose without any action by researchers.

居家隔离的自然实验：实验是找出人类如何影响野火的一种方法。如果每个人都在相同的天气条件下待在家里，他们对火灾的影响就会显现出来。这就是王江浩的研究团队关注新冠疫情时期的原因。这被称为“自然实验”，即实验条件是在没有研究人员干预的情况下产生的。

Wang explained: “Human activity and climate normally vary together, because seasons, farming calendars, and weather all shape both fire and human behavior. The early-2020 lockdowns produced an abrupt, large-scale drop in human mobility, and the timing differed across countries while the weather did not change in the same way. This gave us a natural experiment.”

王江浩解释说：“人类活动和气候通常是共同变化的，因为季节、农业日历和天气都会影响火灾和人类行为。2020年初的封锁导致人类流动性突然大幅下降，各国的时间安排各不相同，而天气并没有以同样的方式变化。这为我们提供了一个自然实验。”

The researchers defined the period of restricted mobility by checking regional lockdown orders and mobile phone location data. Data from NASA and the National Oceanic and Atmospheric Administration’s Suomi NPP satellite helped the team track fires. Its VIIRS sensor detects heat signatures from fires at a spatial resolution of 375 meters. Each hot spot the satellite detects counts as an active-fire detection, meaning one large fire can appear as multiple spots.

研究人员通过查看区域封锁令和手机位置数据来定义流动性受限的时期。来自美国国家航空航天局（NASA）和美国国家海洋和大气管理局（NOAA）的Suomi NPP卫星数据帮助该团队追踪火灾。其VIIRS传感器以375米的空间分辨率探测火灾的热信号。卫星探测到的每个热点都被计为一次火灾探测，这意味着一场大火可能会显示为多个热点。

However, the satellite records both wildfires and prescribed burns without distinguishing between them. As a result, the intent behind each fire remains unknown. In the study, detections during the 2020 restriction periods were compared with those from the same weeks in 2013–2019. Weather conditions weren’t exactly the same as in past years, which was accounted for.

然而，卫星记录了野火和计划烧除，但没有区分两者。因此，每场火灾背后的意图仍然未知。在研究中，2020年限制期间的探测结果与2013年至2019年相同周数的数据进行了比较。天气条件与往年并不完全相同，这一点已被纳入考量。

The results show that areas under lockdown in the first half of 2020 had about 12.3 percent fewer active-fire detections than the recent-past average. The drop varied by vegetation type. In forests, fires decreased by about 27.7 percent, while grasslands decreased by 8.4 percent. Shrublands, savannas, and croplands didn’t show much difference. The researchers interpret this to mean that humans are a major cause of fires in forests and grasslands.

结果显示，2020年上半年处于封锁状态的地区，火灾探测次数比近期平均水平减少了约12.3%。这种下降因植被类型而异。森林火灾减少了约27.7%，草原火灾减少了8.4%。灌木丛、稀树草原和农田没有表现出太大差异。研究人员据此认为，人类是森林和草原火灾的主要诱因。

Controlling for weather factors was important in the study. Without adjustments, the drop was only 5.8 percent, which was not statistically significant. After controlling for weather, however, the estimated decline increased to 12.3 percent. If weather in 2020 were more favorable to fires than in previous years, it might have masked some of the human impact.

在研究中，控制天气因素非常重要。如果不进行调整，下降幅度仅为5.8%，在统计学上并不显著。然而，在控制了天气因素后，估计的下降幅度增加到了12.3%。如果2020年的天气比往年更有利于火灾发生，那么它可能会掩盖部分人类的影响。

The 12.3 percent decrease in fire detections doesn’t mean that humans directly caused this share of fires. Restricting human activity shrank both humans’ impact on starting fires and likely limited their impact on extinguishing them. The 12.3 percent figure is the net effect after these two opposing forces partly cancel out. Wang said, “The 12.3 percent is a net effect, not the share of fires caused by humans; opposing changes in ignition and suppression mean it does not capture the full magnitude of human influence.”

火灾探测次数减少12.3%并不意味着人类直接导致了这一比例的火灾。限制人类活动既减少了人类引发火灾的影响，也可能限制了人类扑灭火灾的影响。12.3%的数字是这两种相反力量部分抵消后的净效应。王江浩说：“12.3%是一个净效应，而不是人类造成的火灾份额；点火和灭火方面的相反变化意味着它无法捕捉到人类影响的全部程度。”
