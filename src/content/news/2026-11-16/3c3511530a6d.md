这款出色的 OLED 电视以极低的价格提供了卓越的对比度和丰富的游戏功能。

LG’s B6E was already one of the most affordable OLED TVs you could buy, but it’s an even better deal now that it’s included as part of Best Buy’s latest doorbuster event. Now through the end of today, November 15th, the 55-inch model is 50 percent off, dropping it to an all-time low of $699.99 ($700 off) at Best Buy. If you want a larger 4K set, the 65- and 77-inch models are also discounted, dropping them to $999.99 ($900 off) and $1,499.99 ($1,200 off), respectively.

LG B6E 本身就是市面上最实惠的 OLED 电视之一，而随着它被纳入百思买（Best Buy）最新的限时抢购活动，性价比进一步提升。即日起至 11 月 15 日今天结束，55 英寸机型可享五折优惠，在百思买的售价降至 699.99 美元的历史最低价（优惠 700 美元）。如果您需要更大的 4K 电视，65 英寸和 77 英寸机型也参与了折扣，价格分别降至 999.99 美元（优惠 900 美元）和 1,499.99 美元（优惠 1,200 美元）。

Although it’s not as bright as LG’s pricier C6, the B6E still delivers the excellent contrast and deep blacks OLEDs are often praised for. It’s nearly identical to the standard B6, except it comes with a simpler remote that lacks the motion controls and voice features found on LG’s Magic Remote. It supports Dolby Vision and HDR10 for more lifelike colors and detail in bright and dark scenes, along with Dolby Atmos for more immersive audio. And with Apple AirPlay and Google Cast built in, you can easily stream content from your phone or tablet and access a variety of streaming apps, including Netflix, HBO Max, and YouTube via LG’s webOS platform.

尽管亮度不及 LG 更昂贵的 C6 机型，但 B6E 依然具备 OLED 电视备受赞誉的卓越对比度和深邃黑色表现。它与标准版 B6 几乎完全相同，区别仅在于配备了一个更简单的遥控器，缺少了 LG 动感应遥控器（Magic Remote）上的体感控制和语音功能。它支持杜比视界（Dolby Vision）和 HDR10，能在明暗场景中呈现更逼真的色彩和细节，并支持杜比全景声（Dolby Atmos）以提供更具沉浸感的音频体验。此外，内置的 Apple AirPlay 和 Google Cast 功能让您可以轻松从手机或平板电脑投屏，并通过 LG 的 webOS 平台访问 Netflix、HBO Max 和 YouTube 等多种流媒体应用。

The B6 also offers a lot of features gamers will appreciate. It has a 120Hz refresh rate for modern gaming consoles, which is still plenty fast even if it doesn’t rival the 165Hz refresh rate on the C6. It also offers variable refresh rate support — including Nvidia G-Sync and AMD FreeSync Premium — to help minimize screen tearing. You also get a zippy Alpha 8 Gen 3 AI processor and four HDMI 2.1 ports, making it easy to connect multiple consoles. It may lack some of the more advanced features found on LG’s more premium OLED TVs, but at 50 percent off, the B6 is a great option if you want the benefits of OLED without spending a fortune.

B6 还提供了许多游戏玩家会喜欢的功能。它拥有 120Hz 的刷新率，足以满足现代游戏主机的需求，虽然不及 C6 的 165Hz 刷新率，但速度依然非常快。它还支持可变刷新率（包括 Nvidia G-Sync 和 AMD FreeSync Premium），有助于减少画面撕裂。此外，它还配备了快速的 Alpha 8 Gen 3 AI 处理器和四个 HDMI 2.1 接口，方便连接多台游戏主机。虽然它可能缺少 LG 更高端 OLED 电视的一些高级功能，但在五折优惠下，如果您想在不花费巨资的情况下体验 OLED 的优势，B6 是一个绝佳的选择。
