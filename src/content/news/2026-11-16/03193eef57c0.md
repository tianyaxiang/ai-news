Rachelle Hampton won’t admit how long she spent charting the use of ‘murmur’ in Twilight. Normal Gossip host Rachelle Hampton is obsessed with I Like to Watch and splurges on good butter.

蕾切尔·汉普顿（Rachelle Hampton）不愿透露她花了多久时间来统计《暮光之城》中“低语”（murmur）一词的使用频率。《Normal Gossip》的主持人蕾切尔·汉普顿沉迷于《I Like to Watch》节目，并且在购买优质黄油上从不吝啬。

Rachelle Hampton is a writer and co-owner at Defector Media, and the host of Normal Gossip, a podcast of anonymous listener-submitted stories about everyday drama. Whether that’s lies about luxury wool in a literal knitting circle, the impeachment of a hypocritical HOA president, or a COVID outbreak at a destination wedding with an absurd 25 bridesmaids, each story is unique in its absurdity, yet weirdly relatable.

蕾切尔·汉普顿是 Defector Media 的作家兼共同所有者，也是播客《Normal Gossip》的主持人。该节目专门讲述听众匿名提交的日常八卦故事。无论是关于编织圈里奢侈羊毛的谎言、虚伪的业主委员会主席被弹劾，还是目的地婚礼上因荒谬的25名伴娘而引发的新冠疫情爆发，每个故事都因其荒诞而独特，却又让人感到莫名地感同身受。

What is your most indispensable tool (analog or digital, app or hardware)? I think it has to be the Notes app. My entire life is in there! My relationship to the Notes app definitely changes depending on what’s going on in my life at the time.

你最不可或缺的工具（模拟或数字、应用程序或硬件）是什么？我想一定是备忘录（Notes）应用。我的整个人生都在里面！我对备忘录应用的使用方式，绝对会随着我生活中发生的事情而改变。

Which is the most underappreciated? I absolutely hate texting. (I write all day, I don’t want to do it in my free time!!) But as someone who also refuses to delete their texts, I have to acknowledge that my most significant personal archive is probably in my Messages inbox.

哪一个最被低估？我非常讨厌发短信。（我整天都在写作，不想在空闲时间还做这件事！！）但作为一个拒绝删除短信的人，我必须承认，我最重要的个人档案可能就在我的短信收件箱里。

What is the first app you install on a new phone or computer? It’s a tie between Spotify and whatever phone game I’m currently addicted to, which for the last two years has been Township. I’ve never played Sims or Animal Crossing, but I’m told that it’s something like that. I just like planting my little crops.

你在新手机或电脑上安装的第一个应用程序是什么？Spotify 和我目前沉迷的任何手机游戏并列第一，过去两年里我沉迷的是《Township》。我从没玩过《模拟人生》或《动物森友会》，但听说它们差不多。我只是喜欢种植我的小作物。

How many tabs do you have open right now? I maintain inbox zero across my entire digital life, so I close all my tabs after I finish a task. Right now I have three tabs that I will close immediately after finishing this questionnaire: the first is the Google Doc I’m typing in, the second is the landing page for this series, and the third is a Google tab where I just searched Animal Crossing to make sure I had the name right.

你现在打开了多少个标签页？我在整个数字生活中都保持“收件箱零邮件”的状态，所以完成任务后我会关闭所有标签页。现在我有三个标签页，填完这份问卷后我会立即关闭它们：第一个是我正在输入的 Google 文档，第二个是本系列的着陆页，第三个是刚才为了确认名字拼写而搜索《动物森友会》的 Google 标签页。

Which social media platform do you use the most? It’s a tie between Reddit and YouTube. Hosting a twice-weekly podcast about the internet for three years either gave me some sort of PTSD or cured me of my social media addiction, depending on how you want to look at it. I can’t spend more than twenty minutes max on IG or X or Bluesky without feeling claustrophobic.

你使用最多的社交媒体平台是什么？Reddit 和 YouTube 并列。主持关于互联网的每周两次播客三年，要么让我患上了某种创伤后应激障碍，要么治好了我的社交媒体成瘾，这取决于你怎么看。我在 IG、X 或 Bluesky 上待的时间最多不能超过二十分钟，否则就会感到幽闭恐惧。

What is your happy place online? I don’t think I’ve ever been as happy online as I was on Tumblr between 2013 and 2016. I’m always subconsciously chasing that high.

你在网上的快乐源泉是什么？我想我再也没有像 2013 年到 2016 年在 Tumblr 上那样快乐过了。我总是在潜意识里追寻那种快感。

What is your favorite gadget you’ve ever owned? My older brother bought me a tabletop Klipsch Bluetooth speaker for Christmas a couple of years ago, and it’s still going strong. The sound quality is incredible, and the design is super sexy, so it doesn’t immediately read as a Bluetooth speaker, which I love. I hope I never have to replace it.

你拥有过的最喜欢的电子产品是什么？几年前圣诞节，我哥哥送了我一个 Klipsch 桌面蓝牙音箱，它至今仍表现出色。音质令人难以置信，设计也非常迷人，看起来不像是一个普通的蓝牙音箱，我非常喜欢。我希望永远不需要更换它。

Which tech trend do you wish would go away? One of the stances I’m proudest of is that I’ve never (intentionally) used ChatGPT or Midjourney or any of the other generative AI tools / large language models. It’s getting harder and harder to avoid now that Gemini is built into Google and its various products. Stop trying to write my emails, Gemini!!

你希望哪种科技趋势消失？我最引以为豪的立场之一就是，我从未（刻意）使用过 ChatGPT、Midjourney 或任何其他生成式 AI 工具/大语言模型。现在 Gemini 已经内置到 Google 及其各种产品中，想要避开它变得越来越难。Gemini，别再试图帮我写邮件了！！

What creation are you most proud of? In 2020, when I was a staff writer at Slate, I spent a [redacted amount of time] methodically charting and ranking all 349 times Stephenie Meyer used the word murmur in her iconic Twilight saga. The idea had been germinating somewhere in the recesses of my brain for over a decade, only to be released by the surprise 2020 release of Midnight Sun.

你最引以为豪的作品是什么？2020 年，当我在《Slate》担任专职作家时，我花了[已删除时间]系统地整理并排名了斯蒂芬妮·梅尔在标志性的《暮光之城》系列中 349 次使用“低语”一词的情况。这个想法在我脑海深处酝酿了十多年，直到 2020 年《午夜阳光》的意外发布才得以实现。

What is your current obsession? I’ve rewatched every episode of Netflix’s I Like to Watch with drag queens Trixie Mattel and Katya an embarrassing amount of times. There are certain episodes, like this one where Trixie and Katya react to Wake Up Dead Man: A Knives Out Mystery, that I can quote front to back.

你目前沉迷于什么？我已经把 Netflix 的《I Like to Watch》节目看了无数遍，尴尬的是我甚至记不清次数了，节目里有变装皇后 Trixie Mattel 和 Katya。有些剧集，比如她们对《利刃出鞘：抽丝剥茧》做出反应的那一集，我甚至能从头到尾背诵下来。
