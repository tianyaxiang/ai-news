In Malaysia’s lush southern peninsula, a data center known as A201 is set to open next year, packed with hundreds of millions of dollars in Nvidia’s most advanced artificial intelligence servers.

在马来西亚郁郁葱葱的南部半岛，一个名为 A201 的数据中心计划于明年启用，其中配备了价值数亿美元的英伟达最先进人工智能服务器。

The data center plans to supply this computing power to a Chinese tech giant. The setup is legal and profitable. It will also sidestep U.S. rules that ban sales of the most advanced A.I. chips directly to Chinese firms.

该数据中心计划向一家中国科技巨头提供这种计算能力。这种安排既合法又有利可图，同时也绕过了美国禁止直接向中国公司出售最先进人工智能芯片的规定。

The company operating the data center is a client of Cantor Fitzgerald, the financial services firm formerly led by Howard Lutnick, the commerce secretary, who handed off leadership of it to his sons. Mr. Lutnick is now writing the rules that would have a substantial effect on the data center’s business.

运营该数据中心的公司是建达公司（Cantor Fitzgerald）的客户。建达是一家金融服务公司，此前由现任商务部长霍华德·卢特尼克（Howard Lutnick）领导，他已将公司领导权移交给了自己的儿子。卢特尼克先生目前正在制定的规则，将对该数据中心的业务产生重大影响。

Mr. Lutnick has been seen as a force slowing rules that would clamp down on the sale of Nvidia chips to data centers like A201, which sit outside China but provide A.I. computing power to Chinese firms.

卢特尼克先生被视为阻碍相关规则出台的一股力量，这些规则本旨在限制向 A201 这类数据中心出售英伟达芯片；这些数据中心位于中国境外，却为中国公司提供人工智能计算能力。

The Trump administration, concerned about ceding the country’s advantage in A.I., bans the sale of cutting-edge chips to China. But it has been embroiled in an internal debate for much of the past two years over how far to go in limiting chip sales to data centers in Southeast Asia.

特朗普政府担心失去美国在人工智能领域的优势，因此禁止向中国出售尖端芯片。但在过去两年里，政府内部一直陷入一场争论，即在限制向东南亚数据中心出售芯片的问题上，应该采取多大程度的限制。

On one side of the debate are China hawks, who see the data centers as a way for Beijing to do an end run around the chip sales ban. On the other are officials, most prominently Mr. Lutnick, who are pushing against going too far in limiting the ability of U.S. companies like Nvidia to sell their products in Asia.

争论的一方是对华鹰派，他们认为这些数据中心是北京绕过芯片销售禁令的一种手段。另一方则是以卢特尼克先生为首的官员，他们反对过度限制英伟达等美国公司在亚洲销售产品的能力。

Recently, Mr. Lutnick has been advocating a less stringent version of those controls under consideration, which could cut back on Nvidia’s business by giving the U.S. government more oversight of chip sales to international data centers. It is the latest example of Mr. Lutnick’s soft touch on chip regulation, which has prompted criticism from lawmakers and others in the administration.

最近，卢特尼克先生一直在倡导采取一种较宽松的管制方案，该方案通过加强美国政府对国际数据中心芯片销售的监管，可能会削减英伟达的业务。这是卢特尼克先生在芯片监管方面采取“软手段”的最新例证，并已招致立法者和政府内部其他人士的批评。

Cantor Fitzgerald has had business dealings with several data center companies in Southeast Asia that cater to Chinese clients, and could be affected by the regulations Mr. Lutnick is drafting, a review by The New York Times has found.

《纽约时报》的一项调查发现，建达公司与东南亚几家服务于中国客户的数据中心公司有业务往来，并可能受到卢特尼克先生正在起草的法规的影响。

Cantor Fitzgerald served as a designated sales agent for an equity offering of up to $1 billion this year by Bitdeer, the Singaporean firm that will operate data center A201, in addition to having other financial ties to various global data center businesses. The relationship between the two companies began as early as 2024, when Cantor Fitzgerald helped Bitdeer arrange a stock offering of up to $250 million.

建达公司今年担任了比特小鹿（Bitdeer）高达 10 亿美元股权发行的指定销售代理，该公司是一家新加坡公司，将负责运营 A201 数据中心；此外，建达还与全球多家数据中心企业存在其他财务联系。两家公司的关系最早始于 2024 年，当时建达帮助比特小鹿安排了一项高达 2.5 亿美元的股票发行。

Mr. Lutnick steered Cantor Fitzgerald for decades, before recusing himself when he joined the Trump administration last year. The Commerce Department says Mr. Lutnick has complied with all ethics agreements, and his former business is now run by his sons.

卢特尼克先生掌舵建达公司数十年，去年加入特朗普政府时进行了回避。商务部表示，卢特尼克先生遵守了所有道德协议，他之前的业务现在由他的儿子们经营。

These dealings are just a tiny portion of Cantor’s business, which stretches from investment banking and trading to real estate. Other investment banks have similar partnerships, and there is no evidence that Cantor’s investments influenced Mr. Lutnick’s deliberations.

这些交易只是建达业务的一小部分，该公司的业务范围涵盖投资银行、交易和房地产。其他投资银行也有类似的合作关系，且没有证据表明建达的投资影响了卢特尼克先生的决策。

But the overlapping relationships are the latest indication of the potential conflicts that exist given Mr. Lutnick’s role in shaping rules around U.S. technology exports. And they demonstrate how the U.S. ban on selling chips directly to China has led to an explosion of data centers in Southeast Asia aimed at Chinese customers. It is now up to Mr. Lutnick to decide whether to tighten the rules in ways that would cut off this avenue.

但这些重叠的关系是卢特尼克先生在制定美国技术出口规则中所扮演的角色可能引发潜在冲突的最新迹象。它们也展示了美国禁止直接向中国出售芯片的禁令，是如何导致东南亚针对中国客户的数据中心激增的。现在，卢特尼克先生需要决定是否要收紧规则以切断这一途径。
