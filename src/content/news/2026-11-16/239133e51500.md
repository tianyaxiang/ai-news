Title: Logitech Yeti 2 Review: An All-In-One Studio Setup

The original Blue Yeti microphone was first released in 2009, and it remains the most ubiquitous microphone for home recording 17 years later. Chances are, if you’ve watched a YouTube video, Twitch stream, or listened to a podcast, you’ve heard (and probably seen) this microphone at least once.

最初的 Blue Yeti 麦克风于 2009 年首次发布，17 年后的今天，它仍然是家庭录音中最普及的麦克风。如果你看过 YouTube 视频、Twitch 直播或听过播客，你很可能至少见过或听过这款麦克风一次。

Long after absorbing Blue and phasing out the brand name, Logitech has finally released its highly anticipated successor: the Yeti 2. The follow-up maintains the same general shape and look but drastically changes the microphone’s functionality. Instead of simply recording audio, the Yeti 2 has a handful of features to monitor and manipulate the audio passing through it, seamlessly recording production-quality audio with zero delay. While some of the design feels a bit underdeveloped, the Yeti 2 is a deserving sequel to the iconic original model and an easy shoe-in as the best USB microphone you can buy.

在收购 Blue 并逐步淘汰该品牌名称许久之后，罗技终于发布了备受期待的继任者：Yeti 2。这款后续产品保持了大致相同的形状和外观，但彻底改变了麦克风的功能。Yeti 2 不仅仅是录制音频，它还具备多种功能来监控和处理通过它的音频，能够零延迟地无缝录制出制作级的音频。虽然部分设计感觉还有些欠缺，但 Yeti 2 无疑是这款标志性原始型号的优秀续作，并轻松成为你可以买到的最佳 USB 麦克风。

The Yeti 2's biggest upgrades stares you directly in the face after taking it out of the box. The built-in screen and dial allow for on-the-fly adjustments right on the microphone. No software required. While I was a skeptic at first, I quickly found this little screen to be significantly more convenient than the alternative. It can be used to adjust gain, enable different post-processing settings, and configure the sound profile of the microphone.

Yeti 2 最大的升级在你开箱后就会直接映入眼帘。内置的屏幕和旋钮允许直接在麦克风上进行即时调整，无需任何软件。虽然我起初持怀疑态度，但我很快发现这个小屏幕比其他替代方案方便得多。它可用于调节增益、启用不同的后期处理设置以及配置麦克风的声音配置文件。

The microphone comes with a basic desk-mounted stand in the box, which uses two rubber grommets and a felt panel underneath to isolate it from the desk. While I was initially worried about this picking up resonance from typing, the isolation mount and noise cancellation settings manage to make it sound normal even when placed directly next to a keyboard.

这款麦克风包装内附带一个基本的桌面支架，它使用两个橡胶垫圈和一个底部的毛毡垫来将其与桌面隔离开来。虽然我最初担心这会拾取打字时的共振，但隔离支架和降噪设置即使在将其直接放在键盘旁边时，也能让声音听起来很正常。

The most impressive part of this microphone was how little I had to do when using it, since the automatic settings are so dialed-in. During my entire time with the microphone, I didn’t need to adjust the gain at all. Outside of specifically testing functionality, I didn’t even have any reason to open the Logitech G Hub app. All of the important settings are able manage themselves entirely from the microphone itself.

这款麦克风最令人印象深刻的部分是使用时几乎无需操作，因为自动设置已经非常精准。在我使用这款麦克风的整个过程中，我根本不需要调整增益。除了专门测试功能外，我甚至没有任何理由打开 Logitech G Hub 应用程序。所有重要的设置都能完全通过麦克风本身进行管理。
