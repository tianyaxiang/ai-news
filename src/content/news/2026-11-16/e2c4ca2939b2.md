娱乐专栏音乐：听听这个：Armand Hammer 的《Snakes & Ladders》。Armand Hammer 的最新专辑是他们最好的作品之一。作者：Terrence O'Brien，2026 年 11 月 15 日，世界标准时间晚上 9:49。

I open with this caveat: I haven’t had a ton of time to digest the surprise Armand Hammer record that dropped this week. Billy Woods and Elucid rarely miss, though, and my initial reaction is that Snakes & Ladders ranks among their best. It doesn’t have the seamless throughline production-wise that their collabs with Alchemist (Haram and Mercy) do, but it’s still clearly the singular vision of the two men at its center.

我先声明一点：我还没有太多时间去消化本周突然发布的这张 Armand Hammer 唱片。不过，Billy Woods 和 Elucid 鲜有失手，我的初步反应是《Snakes & Ladders》跻身于他们最好的作品之列。虽然在制作上，它不像他们与 Alchemist 合作的专辑（如《Haram》和《Mercy》）那样具有无缝衔接的连贯性，但它依然清晰地展现了这两位核心人物独特的愿景。

Ominous noises dance in the background, blasts of noise add dashes of industrial rage, bass lines throb, and saxophone bleats call out to the sky. Woods and Elucid have always had excellent taste in production, but here they seem to have brought out the best in collaborators like clipping, Kenny Segal, and Child Actor. They’ve also successfully rallied them around a specific sound palette that keeps the record from actually sounding like it has beats from 11 different producers.

背景中回荡着不祥的噪音，阵阵轰鸣增添了工业狂怒的色彩，低音线条跳动，萨克斯的鸣叫声向天空呼喊。Woods 和 Elucid 在制作方面一直有着极佳的品味，但在这里，他们似乎激发了 clipping、Kenny Segal 和 Child Actor 等合作者最好的状态。他们还成功地将这些人凝聚在一种特定的音色调色板周围，使这张唱片听起来并不像是出自 11 位不同制作人之手的节拍拼凑。

Of course, what makes Armand Hammer stand out from many of their contemporaries is their unique and verbose flows. Their lyrical mastery even makes guest rappers like $ilkmoney, who you can practically hear stalking around the mic as he seethes on “Risk,” up their game.

当然，Armand Hammer 之所以能从许多同辈艺人中脱颖而出，是因为他们独特且冗长的说唱流派。他们对歌词的掌控力甚至让像 $ilkmoney 这样的客座说唱歌手也提升了表现，在《Risk》中，你几乎能听到他在麦克风前徘徊并愤怒嘶吼的声音。

On songs like “Gravity,” Billy Woods navigates burgeoning romance with shocking directness, while also acknowledging obliquely how hard it can be to say what you mean.

在《Gravity》等歌曲中，Billy Woods 以令人震惊的直接方式处理了萌芽中的恋情，同时也含蓄地承认了表达真实意图有多么困难。

“Vespers” is another highlight where it’s Elucid’s turn to get vulnerable: “Graveside” is another highlight where it’s Elucid’s turn to get vulnerable. In some ways, this record feels like a culmination of what Billy Woods and Elucid have been working towards on their non-Alchemist-produced Armand Hammer records like We Buy Diabetic Test Strips, but also their recent solo records like Golliwog and Revelator.

《Vespers》是另一个亮点，轮到 Elucid 展示脆弱的一面；《Graveside》则是另一个亮点，同样展现了 Elucid 的脆弱。在某种程度上，这张唱片感觉像是 Billy Woods 和 Elucid 在非 Alchemist 制作的 Armand Hammer 专辑（如《We Buy Diabetic Test Strips》）以及他们近期个人专辑（如《Golliwog》和《Revelator》）中所追求目标的结晶。
