# Get ready for the game with new football features in Search
# 准备好迎接比赛：Google 搜索推出全新橄榄球功能

Get ready for the game with new football features in Search. Football season is officially here in the U.S., and with our latest upgrades, Search is ready to be your ultimate game-day guide. Today, we’re introducing new features designed to help you dive deeper into every moment — including a Live Game Feed, more detailed player and league stats, and the ability to link your fantasy football account for tailored recommendations. 🏈

准备好迎接比赛：Google 搜索推出全新橄榄球功能。美国的橄榄球赛季正式拉开帷幕，随着我们最新的升级，搜索功能已准备好成为您的终极比赛日指南。今天，我们推出了一系列旨在帮助您深入了解每一个精彩瞬间的新功能，包括实时比赛动态（Live Game Feed）、更详细的球员和联赛统计数据，以及关联梦幻橄榄球（Fantasy Football）账户以获取个性化建议的功能。🏈

**Never miss a play with the Live Game Feed.** No one wants to miss a game-changing play. Now, with the new Live Game Feed in Search, you can catch up on all the action and follow each game in real-time as it unfolds. To try it out, simply search for an ongoing game and look for the red “Live” icon. You’ll see an overall recap of the game so far, alongside a dynamic timeline featuring play-by-play updates, top social commentary, key video highlights, and AI-powered insights. The Live Game Feed for professional football is now available on mobile in the U.S. in English. Later this month, we’ll also introduce support for collegiate teams and bring the feed to even more people around the world.

**通过“实时比赛动态”不错过任何精彩瞬间。** 没人想错过改变比赛局势的关键一球。现在，借助搜索中全新的“实时比赛动态”，您可以随时掌握赛况，实时追踪每场比赛的进展。想要体验此功能，只需搜索一场正在进行的比赛，并寻找红色的“Live”（直播）图标即可。您将看到比赛目前的整体回顾，以及包含逐球更新、热门社交评论、关键视频集锦和 AI 驱动洞察的动态时间轴。目前，职业橄榄球的实时比赛动态功能已在美国的移动端以英语提供。本月晚些时候，我们将增加对大学球队的支持，并将此功能推广给全球更多用户。

**Keep tabs on matchups, standings, and stats.** Whether you’re glued to your TV screen or checking in on the go, new upgrades in Search make it easier to see how the season is shaping up. Now, when you search for a matchup, you’ll see a new carousel that lets you check the scores for other games happening across the league, without needing to search for them individually. Want to see how your favorite players are stacking up? We’re rolling out new stats this season that will show you the league leaders across categories like passing touchdowns or rushing yards, as well as more detailed player metrics like sacks, fumbles, and yards after catch. And coming soon, Search will also show you who’s favored to win the championship, plus brackets for the playoffs. All of these updates, including the new matchup carousel, will be available globally on mobile.

**随时掌握对阵、排名和统计数据。** 无论您是守在电视机前，还是在移动中查看赛况，搜索中的新升级都能让您更轻松地了解赛季走势。现在，当您搜索某场对阵时，会看到一个新的轮播栏，无需单独搜索即可查看联赛中其他比赛的比分。想了解您最喜爱的球员表现如何？本赛季我们推出了新的统计数据，将展示传球达阵或冲球码数等类别的联赛领先者，以及擒杀、掉球和接球后推进码数等更详细的球员指标。不久之后，搜索还将显示夺冠热门球队以及季后赛对阵图。所有这些更新（包括新的对阵轮播栏）都将在全球移动端上线。

**Manage your fantasy matchups with confidence.** When you’re setting your fantasy lineup before kickoff, getting tailored recommendations can help you make the right call. Now, you can link your Yahoo Fantasy or Sleeper account with Search and ask for customized insights through AI Mode. To get started, simply ask AI Mode for help with your fantasy lineup, and you’ll see an option to securely link your fantasy account. Once connected, Search can bring in the context from your live roster and all of your leagues to help with anything from start/sit recommendations to waiver wire targets — no manual data entry or screenshots needed. You can even ask AI Mode for an assessment of your draft picks or a weekly recap on happenings across the league. We built this feature for fantasy fans of all levels, and we’ll continue improving it throughout the season. Be sure to send us ideas or feedback through AI Mode using the thumbs up or thumbs down button on each response and let us know how we can make it even better. The ability to connect fantasy football apps with Search is now available in the U.S. in English.

**自信管理您的梦幻橄榄球对阵。** 在开球前设置梦幻阵容时，获取个性化建议能帮助您做出正确的决定。现在，您可以将 Yahoo Fantasy 或 Sleeper 账户与搜索关联，并通过 AI 模式获取定制化的洞察。开始时，只需向 AI 模式询问有关梦幻阵容的建议，您就会看到一个安全关联账户的选项。一旦连接，搜索就能结合您当前阵容和所有联赛的背景信息，为您提供从“首发/替补”建议到“自由球员”目标等各种帮助，无需手动输入数据或截图。您甚至可以要求 AI 模式评估您的选秀球员，或获取每周联赛动态回顾。我们为各水平的梦幻橄榄球粉丝打造了此功能，并将在整个赛季中持续改进。请务必通过 AI 模式中每条回复的“点赞”或“点踩”按钮向我们发送想法或反馈，让我们知道如何做得更好。目前，将梦幻橄榄球应用与搜索关联的功能已在美国以英语提供。

Ready for kickoff? Try out the new football features on Search and dive deeper into every game this season.
