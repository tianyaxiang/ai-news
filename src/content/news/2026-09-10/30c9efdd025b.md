# Everything Apple announced at its fall iPhone event, from the foldable iPhone Duo to an always-listening Apple Watch
# 苹果秋季发布会全汇总：从折叠屏 iPhone Duo 到全天候监听的 Apple Watch

Apple on Wednesday unveiled its latest iPhones and Apple Watches at its first fall event under newly appointed CEO John Ternus. The main event was the tech giant’s highly anticipated first foldable phone, the iPhone Duo. Apple also announced its iPhone 18 Pro lineup, along with new AirPods, and more. We’ve compiled all of the announcements from today’s “Surprise and Shine” event.
苹果周三举行了新任首席执行官 John Ternus 上任后的首场秋季发布会，正式发布了最新的 iPhone 和 Apple Watch。本次发布会的重头戏是这家科技巨头备受期待的首款折叠屏手机——iPhone Duo。此外，苹果还发布了 iPhone 18 Pro 系列、新款 AirPods 等产品。我们为您整理了今天这场“Surprise and Shine”发布会的所有公告。

**iPhone Duo**
**iPhone Duo**

Apple’s first foldable, the iPhone Duo, features a 7.6-inch Retina display when unfolded, while its outer display measures 5.4 inches. The company said that the new device was “Inspired by the versatility of iPad.” The inner display features an “under-display” camera that gives users an all-screen feel. Apple says the iPhone Duo delivers up to 31 hours of video playback on its inner display and up to 44 hours on its outer display. The phone features a 48-megapixel fusion main camera and a 48-megapixel ultrawide camera with 2x optical zoom. As with other foldable phones, users can take rear-camera selfies and look at previews on the outer display. The Duo’s hinge is made from Grade 5 aluminum and comprises more than 100 specially engineered components designed to ensure smooth opening and closing. The iPhone Duo starts at $1,999 with 256GB of storage and is available in two colors. Pre-orders for Apple’s new foldable open October 16, with the device hitting stores on October 23.
苹果的首款折叠屏手机 iPhone Duo 展开后拥有一块 7.6 英寸的视网膜显示屏，外屏尺寸为 5.4 英寸。苹果表示，这款新设备的设计灵感“源于 iPad 的多功能性”。内屏采用了“屏下”摄像头，为用户带来全面屏的视觉体验。苹果称，iPhone Duo 在内屏上可提供长达 31 小时的视频播放时间，外屏则可达 44 小时。该手机配备了 4800 万像素的融合主摄和一颗支持 2 倍光学变焦的 4800 万像素超广角镜头。与其他折叠屏手机一样，用户可以使用后置摄像头进行自拍，并通过外屏查看预览。Duo 的铰链由 5 级钛金属制成，包含 100 多个精密工程组件，确保开合顺滑。iPhone Duo 起售价为 1999 美元（256GB 存储空间），提供两种颜色选择。这款新折叠屏手机将于 10 月 16 日开启预订，10 月 23 日正式上市。

**iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max**
**iPhone 18 Pro 与 iPhone 18 Pro Max**

The new iPhone 18 Pro lineup retains the same design as its predecessors, but its biggest upgrade comes in the form of enhanced cameras. The new Pro series camera features a 48-megapixel sensor with a variable aperture controlled by six thin blades. This system is expected to improve low-light photography while also increasing shooting speed. The camera software also introduces new Pro controls, including white balance, shutter speed, aperture, and a histogram. In addition, new “Photographic Styles” offer greater customization with texture and grain controls. The new camera can record 4K video in Dolby HDR Vision and apply cinematic effects after shooting the footage. The iPhone 18 Pro starts at $1,199, while the iPhone 18 Pro Max starts at $1,299, which is $100 more than last year’s models. Pre-orders will open Saturday, with both devices officially available on September 18.
新款 iPhone 18 Pro 系列保留了与前代相同的设计，但最大的升级在于影像系统。全新的 Pro 系列摄像头配备了 4800 万像素传感器，并采用由六片薄叶片控制的可变光圈。该系统预计将改善低光摄影效果，同时提高拍摄速度。相机软件还引入了新的专业控制功能，包括白平衡、快门速度、光圈和直方图。此外，全新的“摄影风格”提供了更丰富的纹理和颗粒度控制，自定义程度更高。新相机支持录制 4K 杜比 HDR 视频，并可在拍摄后应用电影级特效。iPhone 18 Pro 起售价为 1199 美元，iPhone 18 Pro Max 起售价为 1299 美元，比去年机型贵了 100 美元。预订将于周六开启，两款设备均于 9 月 18 日正式发售。

**Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4**
**Apple Watch Series 12 与 Apple Watch Ultra 4**

The Apple Watch Series 12 and Apple Watch Ultra 4 aren’t getting a major hardware makeover, but they are getting some significant software updates. Apple is taking aim at the growing wave of AI-powered wearables with new “Audio Intelligence” features that can rewind moments and help users remember details from their everyday conversations. The smartwatches are introducing a “Live Rewind” feature that shows the previous 15 seconds of a conversation as a text snippet, and a “Siri Recap” feature that’s designed to help you remember key moments from daily conversations, like takeaways from a team meeting or a parent-teacher conference. A new “Sound Recognition” feature can listen for sounds such as sirens, alarms, doorbells, and a baby crying to provide alerts for people who are hard of hearing. The Apple Watch Series 12 starts at $399, while the Apple Watch Ultra 4 costs $499. Pre-orders open today, with availability in stores beginning September 18.
Apple Watch Series 12 和 Apple Watch Ultra 4 在硬件上没有重大改动，但获得了重要的软件更新。苹果正瞄准日益增长的 AI 可穿戴设备浪潮，推出了全新的“音频智能”功能，可以回溯瞬间并帮助用户记忆日常对话的细节。这两款智能手表引入了“实时回溯”（Live Rewind）功能，可将过去 15 秒的对话以文本片段形式显示；此外还有“Siri 总结”（Siri Recap）功能，旨在帮助用户记住日常对话中的关键时刻，例如团队会议或家长会的要点。全新的“声音识别”功能可以监听警报声、门铃声和婴儿啼哭声，为听障人士提供提醒。Apple Watch Series 12 起售价为 399 美元，Apple Watch Ultra 4 售价为 499 美元。今日开启预订，9 月 18 日正式到店发售。

**AirPods 5**
**AirPods 5**

The latest generation of Apple’s wireless earbuds, the AirPods 5, introduces several upgrades, including improved audio quality, stronger active noise cancellation, and expanded Siri and Apple Intelligence features. Apple says AirPods 5 can reduce background noise by 50% more than the previous generation. They also feature volume control on the stem, which was previously only available on Apple’s more expensive AirPods Pro. Transparency mode on AirPods 5 has also been improved to allow users to hear nearby voices and ambient sounds more naturally. The AirPods 5 offer up to five hours of listening with active noise cancellation on a single charge. The charging case brings total listening time to up to 22 hours. AirPods 5 start at $129, while the version with a Wireless Charging Case costs $149. Pre-orders begin today, with the earbuds set to arrive on September 18.
苹果最新一代无线耳机 AirPods 5 带来了多项升级，包括音质提升、更强的主动降噪功能，以及扩展的 Siri 和 Apple Intelligence 功能。苹果表示，AirPods 5 的背景噪音消除能力比上一代提升了 50%。耳机柄上还增加了音量控制功能，此前该功能仅在更昂贵的 AirPods Pro 上提供。AirPods 5 的通透模式也得到了改进，让用户能更自然地听到周围的人声和环境音。AirPods 5 在开启主动降噪的情况下，单次充电可提供长达 5 小时的聆听时间，配合充电盒总续航可达 22 小时。AirPods 5 起售价为 129 美元，配备无线充电盒的版本售价为 149 美元。今日开启预订，将于 9 月 18 日正式到货。

**Revamped Health App**
**焕然一新的健康 App**

Apple announced a redesigned Apple Health app that takes advantage of Apple Intelligence to make better sense of your health data. The updates introduce a new Insights tab with a summary of the most timely health information, along with personalized guidance, assessments, a new “Readiness” score, and a calculation of your “Health Age.” The updates also introduce a new Longevity tab that uses metrics from your Apple Watch and iPhone to assess your overall health across factors such as sleep, movement, heart health, and more. The revamped Health app rolls out later this year, starting in U.S. English.
苹果宣布重新设计了 Apple Health App，利用 Apple Intelligence 更好地解读用户的健康数据。此次更新引入了全新的“洞察”（Insights）标签页，汇总了最及时的健康信息，并提供个性化指导、评估、全新的“准备度”（Readiness）评分以及“健康年龄”计算。更新还引入了“长寿”（Longevity）标签页，利用 Apple Watch 和 iPhone 的数据，从睡眠、运动、心脏健康等多个维度评估用户的整体健康状况。焕然一新的健康 App 将于今年晚些时候推出，首发支持美式英语。
