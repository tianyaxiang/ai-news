# iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max

**PRESS RELEASE September 9, 2026**
**Apple debuts iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max**
**新闻稿 2026年9月9日**
**苹果发布 iPhone 18 Pro 和 iPhone 18 Pro Max**

The new Pro lineup introduces variable aperture with the most advanced camera in an iPhone, incredible battery life, next‑level performance, and Siri AI, all powered by A20 Pro. iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max feature the most advanced camera Apple has ever made and deliver massive leaps in battery life and performance.
全新的 Pro 系列产品引入了可变光圈技术，配备了 iPhone 史上最先进的摄像头、惊人的电池续航、更上一层楼的性能以及 Siri AI，所有这些均由 A20 Pro 芯片驱动。iPhone 18 Pro 和 iPhone 18 Pro Max 搭载了苹果有史以来最先进的摄像头，并在电池续航和性能方面实现了巨大飞跃。

CUPERTINO, CALIFORNIA Apple today announced iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max, delivering the most advanced pro camera system in an iPhone, and huge leaps in battery life and performance. The new 48MP Fusion Main camera, now with variable aperture, brings creative control that wasn’t possible before, and new Pro controls let users customize their experience in the Camera app.
加利福尼亚州库比蒂诺——苹果公司今日发布了 iPhone 18 Pro 和 iPhone 18 Pro Max，带来了 iPhone 史上最先进的专业摄像头系统，并在电池续航和性能上实现了巨大飞跃。全新的 4800 万像素 Fusion 主摄现支持可变光圈，带来了前所未有的创作控制力，全新的“专业控制”（Pro controls）功能让用户可以在相机 App 中自定义拍摄体验。

Both models feature a smaller and even more useful Dynamic Island, as well as A20 Pro and a next-generation vapor chamber that together deliver the highest sustained performance in iPhone history. iPhone 18 Pro Max also delivers the largest increase in battery life ever on iPhone, thanks to advancements in Apple silicon and a larger battery. Featuring iOS 27 with Apple Intelligence and Siri AI, iPhone 18 Pro models bring powerful AI capabilities together with personal context to become an intelligent personal hub with privacy and security at its core.
两款机型均配备了更小巧且更实用的“灵动岛”，以及 A20 Pro 芯片和新一代均热板，共同实现了 iPhone 史上最高的持续性能表现。得益于 Apple 芯片的进步和更大的电池，iPhone 18 Pro Max 还实现了 iPhone 史上最大的电池续航提升。iPhone 18 Pro 系列搭载了集成 Apple Intelligence 和 Siri AI 的 iOS 27 系统，将强大的 AI 能力与个人情境相结合，使其成为以隐私和安全为核心的智能个人中心。

iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max are available in four elegant finishes: black, silver, glacier, and an all-new burgundy. Pre-orders begin Saturday, September 12, with availability beginning Friday, September 18.
iPhone 18 Pro 和 iPhone 18 Pro Max 提供四种优雅配色：黑色、银色、冰川色以及全新的勃艮第红。预购将于 9 月 12 日（周六）开启，并于 9 月 18 日（周五）正式发售。

“With iPhone 18 Pro, we’ve made advancements to the areas our users care about most: camera, performance, battery, and intelligence. And we didn’t just make incremental improvements in these areas — we took huge leaps forward to create the best Pro iPhone we’ve ever made,” said Greg Joswiak, Apple’s senior vice president of Worldwide Marketing.
“在 iPhone 18 Pro 上，我们在用户最关心的领域——摄像头、性能、电池和智能化方面取得了进步。我们不仅仅是在这些领域做了渐进式的改进，而是实现了巨大的飞跃，打造出了我们有史以来最好的 Pro 系列 iPhone，”苹果全球营销高级副总裁 Greg Joswiak 表示。

“iPhone 18 Pro makes it easy for anyone to capture stunning photos and videos using variable aperture, while providing more versatility for creators and more control for pros. Paired with impactful upgrades to Photographic Styles and powerful video capabilities, iPhone 18 Pro unlocks entirely new ways for users to express themselves.”
“iPhone 18 Pro 让任何人都能通过可变光圈轻松捕捉令人惊叹的照片和视频，同时为创作者提供了更多多功能性，为专业人士提供了更多控制权。配合‘摄影风格’的重大升级和强大的视频功能，iPhone 18 Pro 为用户开启了全新的自我表达方式。”

### An Advanced Camera System That Unlocks More Creativity Across Photo and Video
### 开启照片与视频创作更多可能的先进摄像头系统

The new 48MP Fusion Main camera with variable aperture is the most advanced camera Apple has ever made, bringing aperture control to iPhone for the first time along with even more creative control. Six laser-cut blades smoothly transition between different apertures, making it incredibly easy to capture the best shot by automatically adjusting for depth of field and lighting.
全新的 4800 万像素 Fusion 主摄配备可变光圈，是苹果有史以来最先进的摄像头，首次将光圈控制引入 iPhone，带来了更强的创作控制力。六片激光切割叶片可在不同光圈间平滑切换，通过自动调节景深和光线，让捕捉最佳镜头变得异常简单。

Combined with a new sensor that improves image quality, the result is better low-light photography, crisper detail in the back of group photos, and more unique shots. Variable aperture also provides more control for creative pros by giving them the ability to manually adjust any of the four aperture settings in the Camera app, and an API is available to developers for even more control across the aperture range in their apps.
结合提升画质的新型传感器，其成像效果在低光摄影、合影背景细节清晰度以及拍摄独特镜头方面表现更佳。可变光圈还为专业创作者提供了更多控制权，使他们能够在相机 App 中手动调节四种光圈设置中的任意一种；同时，苹果还向开发者开放了 API，以便他们在各自的 App 中实现对光圈范围的更精细控制。

### Pro controls
### 专业控制 (Pro controls)

Pro controls is a new customizable experience in the Camera app for iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max that makes it easier than ever for users to access their most used settings and introduces new options for manual control. Users can now adjust settings such as lens aperture, shutter speed, and white balance, or use a histogram to check exposure levels throughout a scene.
“专业控制”是 iPhone 18 Pro 和 iPhone 18 Pro Max 相机 App 中一种全新的可自定义体验，让用户能比以往更轻松地访问最常用的设置，并引入了手动控制的新选项。用户现在可以调节镜头光圈、快门速度和白平衡等设置，或使用直方图来检查场景中的曝光水平。

### Introducing Apple Reference Image
### 推出 Apple 参考图像 (Apple Reference Image)

Users can now prove the authenticity of a photo taken on iPhone 18 Pro models with Apple Reference Image, powered by the new sensor in the Main camera that can sign every pixel it sees. When a photo is taken in the new Reference mode, the camera captures signed sensor data that Private Cloud Compute develops into an unalterable reference image.
用户现在可以通过“Apple 参考图像”功能证明 iPhone 18 Pro 系列所拍照片的真实性。该功能由主摄中的新型传感器驱动，能够对所见的每一个像素进行签名。当在新的“参考模式”下拍摄照片时，相机会捕获已签名的传感器数据，并通过 Private Cloud Compute 生成一张不可篡改的参考图像。
