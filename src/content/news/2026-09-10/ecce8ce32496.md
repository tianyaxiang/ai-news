# Automattic CEO Matt Mullenweg placed on leave
# Automattic 首席执行官 Matt Mullenweg 被停职

Matt Mullenweg, the CEO of WordPress.com owner Automattic, has been placed on a paid leave of absence, as reported earlier by 404 Media. In an internal message by the outlet, Mullenweg claims Automattic chief financial officer Mark Davies “conspired” with board members to place him on leave.
据 404 Media 此前报道，WordPress.com 母公司 Automattic 的首席执行官 Matt Mullenweg 已被带薪停职。在该媒体获取的一条内部消息中，Mullenweg 声称 Automattic 首席财务官 Mark Davies 与董事会成员“密谋”将其停职。

“They voted to put me on a paid leave of absence. I voted against that,” Mullenweg wrote, referring to Davies, along with Automattic board members Ann Dunwoody, Toni Schneider, and Sue Decker, 404 Media reports.
据 404 Media 报道，Mullenweg 在文中提到 Davies 以及 Automattic 董事会成员 Ann Dunwoody、Toni Schneider 和 Sue Decker 时写道：“他们投票决定让我带薪休假。我投了反对票。”

Mullenweg, who also co-founded WordPress.org, has headed up Automattic since 2014. In 2024, Mullenweg waged a public pressure campaign against a prominent WordPress host, a move that divided the open-source community and ignited a legal battle that’s still going on today.
Mullenweg 同时也是 WordPress.org 的联合创始人，自 2014 年起一直领导 Automattic。2024 年，Mullenweg 对一家知名的 WordPress 主机服务商发起了一场公开施压运动，此举导致开源社区分裂，并引发了一场至今仍在持续的法律纠纷。

Automattic spokesperson Megan Fox confirmed the company’s decision in an emailed statement to The Verge. “Matt Mullenweg is currently on leave from Automattic,” Fox writes. “Mark Davies, Automattic’s CFO, will lead the company as interim CEO. The Board has full confidence in Mark’s leadership and in the team’s ability to execute against the company’s priorities.” Mullenweg will remain on Automattic’s board, 404 Media reports.
Automattic 发言人 Megan Fox 在发给《The Verge》的电子邮件声明中证实了该公司的决定。Fox 写道：“Matt Mullenweg 目前已从 Automattic 离职休假。Automattic 首席财务官 Mark Davies 将担任临时首席执行官领导公司。董事会对 Mark 的领导能力以及团队执行公司优先事项的能力充满信心。”据 404 Media 报道，Mullenweg 将继续留在 Automattic 董事会。

“Automattic’s board has decided to ask Matt to step away from his CEO role and take a leave of absence,” Schneider said on the company’s internal message board, according to 404 Media. “We have asked Mark Davies to assume the interim CEO role while Matt is out. We’ve worked closely with Mark over the years and we’re confident he will steer the ship well while Matt is away.”
据 404 Media 报道，Schneider 在公司内部留言板上表示：“Automattic 董事会已决定要求 Matt 卸任首席执行官职务并休假。我们已要求 Mark Davies 在 Matt 缺席期间担任临时首席执行官。多年来我们与 Mark 密切合作，我们相信在他（Matt）离开期间，Mark 能很好地掌舵公司。”
