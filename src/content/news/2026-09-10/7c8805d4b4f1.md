# AirPods 5

**PRESS RELEASE September 9, 2026**
**新闻稿 2026年9月9日**

Apple introduces AirPods 5, featuring best-in-class open‑ear Active Noise Cancellation. With industry-leading ANC in an open-ear design, improved sound quality, hands-free access to Siri AI, and Live Translation, AirPods 5 deliver the best audio experience Apple has ever offered in this style of AirPods, at an incredible price. AirPods 5 deliver the industry’s best Active Noise Cancellation in an open-ear design, an all-new multiport acoustic architecture, and next-generation Adaptive EQ for even more immersive sound.
Apple 今日发布 AirPods 5，在开放式设计中提供业界领先的主动降噪 (ANC) 功能，并以出色的性价比带来更佳的音质。凭借开放式设计中行业领先的降噪技术、改进的音质、免提 Siri AI 访问以及实时翻译功能，AirPods 5 提供了 Apple 此类耳机中迄今为止最佳的音频体验。AirPods 5 采用全新的多端口声学架构和新一代自适应均衡 (Adaptive EQ)，带来更具沉浸感的音效。

**CUPERTINO, CALIFORNIA**
**加利福尼亚州库比蒂诺**

Apple today announced AirPods 5, delivering the industry’s best Active Noise Cancellation (ANC) in an open-ear design and even better sound quality, at an outstanding value. AirPods 5 feature an all-new multiport acoustic architecture and next-generation Adaptive EQ for even more immersive sound. Their breakthrough open-ear ANC removes up to 50 percent more external noise compared to AirPods 4 with Active Noise Cancellation, alongside a more natural Transparency mode.
Apple 今日宣布推出 AirPods 5，在开放式设计中提供业界最佳的主动降噪 (ANC) 功能，并以卓越的性价比带来更佳的音质。AirPods 5 采用全新的多端口声学架构和新一代自适应均衡，音效更加沉浸。其突破性的开放式主动降噪功能相比配备主动降噪的 AirPods 4，可多消除高达 50% 的外部噪音，同时提供更自然的通透模式。

Combined with Siri AI and iPhone, AirPods enable users to draw on their personal context and get answers with broad world knowledge, entirely hands-free. Users can also respond to Siri using head gestures, or use Live Translation to help connect across languages. AirPods 5 offer the most affordable way to experience some of the most powerful AirPods features — along with greater durability with improved dust, sweat, and water resistance — at $129. For users who want even more, AirPods 5 with Wireless Charging Case add longer battery life and on-stem volume control for $149. Customers can pre-order starting today, with availability in stores beginning Friday, September 18.
结合 Siri AI 和 iPhone，AirPods 让用户能够完全免提地利用个人背景信息获取答案，并获得广泛的世界知识。用户还可以通过头部动作回应 Siri，或使用实时翻译功能跨语言交流。AirPods 5 以 129 美元的价格，提供了体验 AirPods 最强大功能的最实惠方式，同时具备更强的防尘、抗汗和防水性能，耐用性更高。对于追求更多功能的用户，配备无线充电盒的 AirPods 5 售价为 149 美元，增加了更长的电池续航和耳机柄音量控制功能。用户即日起可进行预订，并将于 9 月 18 日（周五）在店内发售。

“We’re thrilled that with AirPods 5, we’re able to bring Active Noise Cancellation — one of our most beloved features — to our most affordable AirPods,” said Dave Pakula, Apple’s vice president of Hardware Engineering. “Both AirPods 5 models deliver a world-class listening experience and enable features like Live Translation for even more people. Paired with Siri AI, AirPods are a game changer for unlocking powerful hands-free capabilities.”
“我们非常高兴能通过 AirPods 5 将我们最受欢迎的功能之一——主动降噪——带到我们最实惠的 AirPods 产品线中，”Apple 硬件工程副总裁 Dave Pakula 表示。“两款 AirPods 5 机型都提供了世界级的聆听体验，并为更多用户带来了实时翻译等功能。配合 Siri AI，AirPods 将彻底改变用户解锁强大免提功能的方式。”

**Best-in-Class Active Noise Cancellation**
**业界领先的主动降噪**

Customers love to use ANC to quiet the world around them so they can rest on a noisy flight, get lost in their favorite music, or stay focused on work. AirPods 5 remove up to 50 percent more noise compared to AirPods 4 with ANC thanks to a new multiport acoustic architecture and improved computational audio algorithms. Along with the benefits of ANC, AirPods 5 feature an improved Transparency mode that more naturally represents voices and nearby sounds, helping users stay connected to their surroundings.
用户喜欢使用主动降噪功能来屏蔽周围环境，以便在嘈杂的飞行中休息、沉浸在喜爱的音乐中或专注于工作。得益于全新的多端口声学架构和改进的计算音频算法，AirPods 5 相比配备主动降噪的 AirPods 4 可多消除高达 50% 的噪音。除了主动降噪的优势外，AirPods 5 还配备了改进的通透模式，能更自然地呈现人声和周围声音，帮助用户与环境保持联系。

**Re-Engineered Sound Quality**
**重新设计的音质**

Inspired by AirPods Pro 3 and re-engineered for AirPods 5, a new multiport acoustic architecture and next-generation Adaptive EQ deliver richer, more detailed sound across an even wider range of ear geometries. This makes Personalized Spatial Audio more immersive and dynamic, so the user feels like they’re surrounded by whatever they are listening to.
受 AirPods Pro 3 启发并为 AirPods 5 重新设计，全新的多端口声学架构和新一代自适应均衡在更广泛的耳形范围内提供更丰富、更细腻的音质。这使得个性化空间音频更加沉浸和动态，让用户感觉仿佛被所听的内容环绕。

**Intelligent Features, Hands-Free**
**智能功能，解放双手**

For users with an Apple Intelligence-supported iPhone, AirPods 5 allow users to tap into helpful intelligent features hands-free and on the go. Siri AI, which is rolling out in beta in iOS 27, is a profoundly more capable and conversational assistant, offering personal context understanding across messages, emails, photos, and more, as well as app actions and broad world knowledge.
对于使用支持 Apple Intelligence 的 iPhone 的用户，AirPods 5 让用户可以在移动中免提使用实用的智能功能。Siri AI（随 iOS 27 测试版推出）是一个能力更强、更具对话感的助手，能够理解跨信息、电子邮件、照片等内容的个人背景信息，并执行应用操作及提供广泛的世界知识。

With Siri Interactions on AirPods, users can discreetly respond to Siri AI by simply nodding their head yes or shaking their head no. Live Translation, powered by Apple Intelligence, breaks down barriers by letting users communicate across languages. After setting up Live Translation in the Translate app and downloading their desired languages, AirPods users can access the feature by simultaneously pressing both stems, by saying “Siri, start Live Translation,” or by using the Action button on iPhone.
通过 AirPods 上的 Siri 交互，用户只需点头表示“是”或摇头表示“否”，即可低调地回应 Siri AI。由 Apple Intelligence 驱动的实时翻译功能通过让用户跨语言交流来打破障碍。在“翻译”App 中设置并下载所需语言后，AirPods 用户可以通过同时按压两个耳机柄、说“Siri，开始实时翻译”或使用 iPhone 上的操作按钮来访问该功能。

**AirPods 5 with Wireless Charging Case**
**配备无线充电盒的 AirPods 5**

For users who want even more, AirPods 5 with Wireless Charging Case deliver great improvements to the AirPods experience. Force sensor with volume swipe arrives on AirPods 5, a first for the open-ear form factor, adding the ability to quickly adjust volume.
对于追求更多功能的用户，配备无线充电盒的 AirPods 5 为 AirPods 体验带来了巨大提升。AirPods 5 首次在开放式耳机形态中引入了支持音量滑动调节的力度感应器，增加了快速调节音量的功能。
