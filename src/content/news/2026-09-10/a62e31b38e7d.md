# iPhone Duo

**PRESS RELEASE September 9, 2026**
**新闻稿 2026年9月9日**

Apple unveils iPhone Duo. iPhone Duo features a breakthrough foldable design that’s beautiful, versatile, and durable, opening up possibilities that feel entirely new yet remarkably familiar. iPhone Duo features the largest display ever on iPhone, alongside a reimagined iOS experience.
苹果公司发布 iPhone Duo。iPhone Duo 采用突破性的折叠设计，兼具美感、多功能性与耐用性，开启了既全新又熟悉的无限可能。iPhone Duo 配备了 iPhone 史上最大的显示屏，并搭载了重构的 iOS 体验。

**CUPERTINO, CALIFORNIA** Apple today introduced iPhone Duo, the first foldable iPhone. Opened, it is the thinnest iPhone ever, with a 7.6-inch inner display that provides a large canvas for viewing content, gaming, and multitasking, and a special nano-texture finish that minimizes glare for an unparalleled viewing experience. Closed, the 5.4-inch outer display delivers 90 percent of the screen area of iPhone 18 Pro in a compact, pocketable design. Both displays share the same aspect ratio, so content scales proportionally, ensuring visual continuity.
**加利福尼亚州库比蒂诺** 苹果公司今日推出首款折叠屏 iPhone——iPhone Duo。展开后，它是史上最薄的 iPhone，配备 7.6 英寸内屏，为观看内容、游戏和多任务处理提供了广阔的画布，其特殊的纳米纹理涂层可最大限度减少眩光，带来无与伦比的视觉体验。闭合时，5.4 英寸外屏以紧凑、便携的设计提供了 iPhone 18 Pro 90% 的屏幕面积。两块显示屏共享相同的纵横比，确保内容按比例缩放，实现视觉上的连贯性。

iPhone Duo is built to last with an innovative hardware design and a precision hinge that offers a smooth, satisfying feel with every use. Powered by the new A20 Pro, iPhone Duo features an advanced thermal management system with a custom-designed vapor chamber and a dual-battery architecture, delivering pro performance and impressive all-day battery life. The advanced camera system takes stunning photos and videos, with the folding design unlocking fun new ways to use the camera that aren’t possible on any other iPhone.
iPhone Duo 采用创新的硬件设计和精密铰链，经久耐用，每次开合都带来顺滑、令人满意的触感。iPhone Duo 搭载全新的 A20 Pro 芯片，配备先进的散热管理系统（含定制均热板）和双电池架构，提供专业级的性能和出色的全天候续航。先进的摄像头系统可拍摄精美的照片和视频，折叠设计更解锁了其他 iPhone 无法实现的全新趣味拍摄方式。

iOS 27 is reimagined, remaining familiar and intuitive while adapting to the versatile ways users can interact with iPhone Duo. And with Apple Intelligence and Siri AI, iPhone Duo brings powerful AI capabilities together with a user’s personal context. iPhone Duo is available in two refined colors — star white and night sky — and crafted from grade 5 titanium that’s been polished to a mirror finish. Pre-orders begin Friday, October 16, with availability beginning Friday, October 23.
iOS 27 经过重构，在保持熟悉与直观的同时，适配了用户与 iPhone Duo 交互的多种方式。借助 Apple Intelligence 和 Siri AI，iPhone Duo 将强大的 AI 能力与用户的个人情境相结合。iPhone Duo 提供两种精致配色——星光白与夜空黑，机身采用 5 级钛金属打造，并经过镜面抛光处理。10 月 16 日（周五）开启预购，10 月 23 日（周五）正式发售。

“iPhone Duo is the most transformational change to iPhone since the original. With an entirely new design and intuitive experiences, it shows what’s possible when hardware and software are engineered together and redefines what it means to use a foldable phone,” said John Ternus, Apple’s CEO. “With the largest display ever on iPhone that still fits easily in your pocket, combined with the power of A20 Pro, iPhone Duo helps you stay more immersed and productive wherever you go, whether you’re enjoying content, multitasking across apps, or gaming.”
“iPhone Duo 是自初代 iPhone 以来最具变革性的升级。凭借全新的设计和直观的体验，它展示了软硬件深度集成所能达到的高度，并重新定义了折叠屏手机的使用体验，”苹果公司首席执行官 John Ternus 表示。“iPhone Duo 拥有 iPhone 史上最大的显示屏，却依然能轻松放入口袋。结合 A20 Pro 的强劲性能，无论你是欣赏内容、多任务处理还是玩游戏，它都能让你在任何地方保持沉浸感与高效。”

**Two Displays, One Breakthrough Design**
**双屏合一，突破性设计**

iPhone Duo delivers Apple’s largest iPhone display in an entirely new design. Closed, iPhone Duo is comfortable to use one-handed and fits easily in a pocket, with a 5.4-inch outer Super Retina XDR display that provides 90 percent of the screen area of iPhone 18 Pro. Opened, the 7.6-inch inner Super Retina XDR display is 50 percent larger than iPhone 18 Pro Max, offering an immersive canvas for everything users do. A premium nano-texture finish reduces glare and reflections, creating a matte surface that minimizes crease visibility and feels great to touch.
iPhone Duo 以全新的设计带来了苹果最大的 iPhone 显示屏。闭合时，iPhone Duo 单手操作舒适，可轻松放入口袋，其 5.4 英寸外置超视网膜 XDR 显示屏提供了 iPhone 18 Pro 90% 的屏幕面积。展开后，7.6 英寸内置超视网膜 XDR 显示屏比 iPhone 18 Pro Max 大 50%，为用户的一切操作提供了沉浸式画布。优质的纳米纹理涂层减少了眩光和反射，营造出哑光表面，最大限度地降低了折痕可见度，触感极佳。

**Designed for Durability**
**为耐用而生**

iPhone Duo is built to last and feels exceptionally solid with every use. It’s crafted from grade 5 titanium for incredible strength, with an enclosure that has an elegant mirror-polished finish and a 3D-printed hinge cover with a contrasting micro-blasted finish. Ceramic Shield protects the back of iPhone Duo, and Ceramic Shield 2 on the front has 3x better scratch resistance than the previous generation. iPhone Duo is also splash-, water-, and dust-resistant with an IP68 rating.
iPhone Duo 经久耐用，每次使用都倍感坚固。它采用 5 级钛金属打造，强度惊人；机身拥有优雅的镜面抛光饰面，3D 打印的铰链盖则采用了对比鲜明的微喷砂工艺。超瓷晶面板保护着 iPhone Duo 的背部，而正面的超瓷晶面板 2 代抗刮擦能力比上一代提升了 3 倍。此外，iPhone Duo 还具备 IP68 级防溅、抗水和防尘性能。
