# Apple starts preorders for Watch Series 12, Ultra 4 with new Health Sensing System
# 苹果开启 Watch Series 12 与 Ultra 4 预购，搭载全新健康传感系统

Apple announced the Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 today. Both include a new “Health Sensing System” and S11 chip that give the devices the “most accurate heart rate sensing in a wearable,” Apple claimed during its fall event.

苹果今日发布了 Watch Series 12 和 Ultra 4。苹果在秋季发布会上宣称，两款设备均搭载了全新的“健康传感系统”（Health Sensing System）和 S11 芯片，使其成为“可穿戴设备中拥有最精准心率监测功能”的产品。

Both watches will use the new S11 chip alongside the new “Health Sensing System,” allowing the watches to deliver higher-frequency heart rate and heart rate variability (HRV) measurements than prior Apple Watches. The Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 will run watchOS 27.

两款手表均采用全新的 S11 芯片及“健康传感系统”，使其能够比以往的 Apple Watch 提供更高频率的心率和心率变异性（HRV）测量。Watch Series 12 和 Ultra 4 将运行 watchOS 27 系统。

The Health Sensing System includes redesigned photodiodes organized in a ring to capture light more efficiently and a larger electrode surface area for better skin contact. Additionally, the green LEDs on each Watch’s optical heart sensor are supposed to be bigger and more power-efficient and measure the user’s heart rate every five seconds. Users will be able to view their heart rate in real time with a new heart rate watch face special feature (also known as a complication).

健康传感系统包含重新设计并呈环状排列的光电二极管，能更高效地捕捉光线，同时增大了电极表面积以实现更好的皮肤接触。此外，每款手表光学心率传感器上的绿色 LED 灯体积更大且能效更高，每五秒钟即可测量一次用户心率。用户可以通过全新的心率表盘特殊功能（即复杂功能）实时查看心率。

The new watches can take HRV measurements up to 24 times more frequently than their predecessors, Apple said. HRV is an indicator of how well the body is able to adapt to changes such as stress and exercise.

苹果表示，新款手表进行 HRV 测量的频率比前代产品高出 24 倍。HRV 是衡量身体适应压力和运动等变化能力的一项指标。

“Overnight vitals now includes recovery HRV measurements analyzed against a user’s personal baseline, providing a more complete view of their health status. A new daytime vitals view lets users toggle between overnight and daytime metrics, and can potentially spot shifts in their metrics before they show up overnight,” Apple said in a press release.

苹果在新闻稿中称：“‘夜间生命体征’现在包含针对用户个人基准进行分析的恢复期 HRV 测量，从而提供更全面的健康状况视图。全新的‘日间生命体征’视图允许用户在夜间和日间指标之间切换，并可能在指标出现夜间异常前就发现其变化。”

Apple claims that the new wearables have the most accurate heart rate sensing in any wearable based on a study “on a diverse population of over 1,000 participants.”

苹果声称，基于一项“针对超过 1,000 名不同背景参与者”的研究，新款可穿戴设备拥有目前所有可穿戴设备中最精准的心率监测功能。

The Watch Series 12 has an optional ceramic finish in midnight blue and pearl white. There are also titanium finishes and aluminium finishes. The aluminum versions are supposed to be 60 percent “tougher” than prior aluminum models, per Apple.

Watch Series 12 提供午夜蓝和珍珠白两种可选的陶瓷材质版本。此外还有钛金属和铝金属版本。据苹果称，铝金属版本的坚固程度比前代铝金属型号提升了 60%。

The new Watches will also introduce “readiness” scores, which Apple says “analyze a user’s recent activity, training load, vitals, and sleep score to offer insight into their capacity for the day ahead” on a scale from 0 to 10. This score updates throughout the day based on the user’s activity.

新款手表还引入了“准备度”（readiness）评分，苹果称该功能会“分析用户近期的活动、训练负荷、生命体征和睡眠评分，以提供对当天身体状态的洞察”，评分范围为 0 到 10 分。该分数会根据用户全天的活动情况实时更新。

Apple will update the iOS Health app later this year, allowing Watch Series 12 and Ultra 4 users to access a new “Health Age” score. “The Health app also includes movement evaluations that measure flexibility, strength, balance, and more using vision-based AI models on iPhone and heart rate data from Apple Watch. Users will be able to schedule and purchase new labs that feature over 50 key biomarkers for $119 directly through the Health app at approximately 2,000 Quest Diagnostics locations in the US,” Apple said.

苹果将于今年晚些时候更新 iOS 健康 App，允许 Watch Series 12 和 Ultra 4 用户获取全新的“健康年龄”（Health Age）评分。苹果表示：“健康 App 还包含运动评估功能，利用 iPhone 上的视觉 AI 模型和 Apple Watch 的心率数据来测量柔韧性、力量、平衡性等。用户还可以直接通过健康 App 预约并购买包含 50 多项关键生物标志物的实验室检测服务，价格为 119 美元，可在全美约 2,000 个 Quest Diagnostics 网点进行。”

Apple Intelligence features: Apple is also incorporating more Apple Intelligence-based features into the new watches. These features include the ability to use the Shazam song detection feature, Live Rewind, which Apple said “shows the previous 15 seconds of a conversation as a text snippet” when users double-press the Digital Crown, and Siri Recap, which, per Apple, “can create high-level summaries of conversations that users can review later to jog their memory.”

Apple Intelligence 功能：苹果还在新款手表中整合了更多基于 Apple Intelligence 的功能。这些功能包括使用 Shazam 歌曲识别功能；“实时回放”（Live Rewind），苹果称当用户双击数码表冠时，该功能会“以文本片段形式显示过去 15 秒的对话内容”；以及 Siri 摘要（Siri Recap），据苹果介绍，该功能“可以创建对话的高级摘要，供用户稍后查看以唤起记忆”。

Both watches are available for preorder today and will ship on September 18. The Watch Series 12 starts at $399, and the Ultra 4 starts at $799.

两款手表即日起开启预购，并将于 9 月 18 日发货。Watch Series 12 起售价为 399 美元，Ultra 4 起售价为 799 美元。
