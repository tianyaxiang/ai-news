### TCL QM7L Mini-LED TV Review (2026)

Are we entering the era of TV “dupes”? Because to the untrained eye, or even a reasonably trained one, it’s hard to distinguish TCL’s mini-LED-powered QM7L from a lower-end OLED. So when your brother-in-law plops down in front of your new TV to watch the Lions and says, “Wow, you splurged, eh?” you can just nod and give a wink. Dupe accomplished.

我们是否正在进入电视“平替”时代？因为对于外行，甚至是受过一定训练的眼睛来说，很难将 TCL 的 Mini-LED QM7L 与低端 OLED 电视区分开来。所以，当你的姐夫一屁股坐在你的新电视前看雄狮队比赛并说：“哇，你花大钱了吧？”时，你只需点点头，眨眨眼。平替成功。

I know this because the QM7L arrived close on the heels of a new budget OLED, the Roku Pro Series, so I was able to compare the two side by side. Results: surprising. Although it has a potentially problematic design element and an AI that’s not exactly Claude-level sophisticated, the QM7L might just be the best midrange TV you can buy right now.

我之所以知道这一点，是因为 QM7L 在一款新的预算型 OLED 电视（Roku Pro 系列）之后不久就送到了，所以我能够将两者进行对比。结果令人惊讶。尽管它有一个潜在的设计缺陷，且其人工智能并不像 Claude 那样先进，但 QM7L 可能就是你目前能买到的最好的中端电视。

Simple Setup, But Where Does the Soundbar Go? Available in sizes ranging from 55 inches (which I tested) to a mammoth 98 inches, the QM7L boasts a virtually borderless screen that stands on a pedestal instead of the usual feet. That’s a win if you own a small piece of furniture that might not accommodate the width of such feet, and I’d argue it’s more aesthetically pleasing too.

安装简单，但回音壁放哪儿？QM7L 提供从 55 英寸（我测试的尺寸）到巨大的 98 英寸等多种规格，拥有几乎无边框的屏幕，并采用底座支架而非传统的双脚支撑。如果你拥有的家具较小，无法容纳双脚支架的宽度，这算是一个优势，而且我认为它在美学上也更令人愉悦。

However, it does pose a problem: Because the pedestal base sticks out in front by a little over 4 inches, it doesn’t allow a soundbar to sit snug up to the screen. You could balance it on top of the base, but that looks awkward. And because it slopes down, the soundbar doesn’t even sit level.

然而，这确实带来了一个问题：由于底座向前突出了 4 英寸多，它不允许回音壁紧贴屏幕放置。你可以将其平衡地放在底座上方，但这看起来很别扭。而且由于底座是向下倾斜的，回音壁甚至无法保持水平。

What’s more, although the base has two height options, the taller one leaves the bottom of the TV just 2.25 inches off your TV stand. That may be enough clearance for ultra-compact soundbars like the Bose TV Speaker, but something like the Sonos Arc, which measures 3 inches high, ends up blocking a sliver of the screen.

此外，虽然底座有两种高度可选，但较高的高度也仅让电视底部距离电视柜 2.25 英寸。这对于像 Bose TV Speaker 这样超紧凑的回音壁来说可能足够了，但像 3 英寸高的 Sonos Arc 这样的设备，最终会遮挡住一小部分屏幕。

The pedestal is also kind of a pain to assemble, with little help from the tiny diagrams in TCL’s otherwise decent setup guide. There are eight small screws used to connect the riser to the base, four more to attach it to the TV, and a bit of confusion over which of 10 mounting holes you’re meant to use.

底座的组装也相当麻烦，TCL 虽然提供了不错的安装指南，但其中微小的图示几乎帮不上忙。连接立柱和底座需要八颗小螺丝，将其固定到电视上还需要四颗，而且对于 10 个安装孔中该使用哪几个，也让人感到困惑。

Setting up the TV itself is largely a breeze, especially if you already have the Google Home app on your phone. Even though I’m an iPhone guy, I was able to scan an onscreen QR code and use the app to move quickly through the setup process. An account sign-in and Wi-Fi network selection later, I just had to wait on a software update before heading to the couch.

电视本身的设置非常轻松，特别是如果你手机上已经安装了 Google Home 应用。尽管我是 iPhone 用户，但我还是能够通过扫描屏幕上的二维码，利用该应用快速完成设置流程。在登录账户并选择 Wi-Fi 网络后，我只需等待软件更新，然后就可以去沙发上享受了。

TCL’s unremarkable remote has sharp edges, the usual mishmash of Google TV buttons, and a pretty disorganized layout overall. On the plus side, it’s backlit and comes with six app-shortcut buttons. One head-scratcher: The penultimate TV-setup screen notes that I can “customize the star button on the remote,” using it as a shortcut for this or that, which sounds great except that there is no star button.

TCL 的遥控器平平无奇，边缘锋利，上面堆砌着常见的 Google TV 按钮，整体布局相当混乱。好的一面是，它带有背光，并配有六个应用程序快捷键。一个令人费解的地方是：倒数第二个电视设置屏幕提示我可以“自定义遥控器上的星号按钮”，将其用作各种快捷方式，这听起来很棒，但问题是遥控器上根本没有星号按钮。

Gemini Comes to Google TV. Speaking of hallucinations (kidding!), Google Gemini comes baked into the Google TV UI, which is reasonably easy to navigate but not without a few annoyances. For example, the top menu includes a Live TV page—home to various FAST channels, simple enough—but also a TCL page that contains … other FAST channels.

Gemini 登陆 Google TV。说到幻觉（开个玩笑！），Google Gemini 被内置在 Google TV 的用户界面中，导航相当容易，但并非没有烦恼。例如，顶部菜单包含一个直播电视页面——汇集了各种免费流媒体频道（FAST），这很简单——但还有一个 TCL 页面，里面包含……其他免费流媒体频道。

As for Gemini, it’s nice to have but hardly essential. Just talk into the remote and you can ask for, say, movies about sharks, or learn who invented the Steadicam. The voice recognition is responsive, and the responses arrive quickly. Too bad it’s not hands-free; you have to press and hold a button on the remote to use it.

至于 Gemini，有它很好，但并非必不可少。只需对着遥控器说话，你就可以询问，比如关于鲨鱼的电影，或者了解是谁发明了斯坦尼康（Steadicam）。语音识别反应灵敏，响应速度也很快。可惜它不是免提的；你必须按住遥控器上的按钮才能使用它。

Gemini also dishes up “watch now” options, and you can use it to create custom images and video clips, if you want. Unfortunately, while Gemini can accommodate basic TV commands (“Start Netflix,” “Mute the sound,” etc.), it can’t do things like open picture settings or toggling off motion-smoothing—a bummer because those commands would actually save you a little time.

Gemini 还提供“立即观看”选项，如果你愿意，还可以用它创建自定义图像和视频剪辑。遗憾的是，虽然 Gemini 可以处理基本的电视指令（“启动 Netflix”、“静音”等），但它无法执行打开图像设置或关闭运动平滑等操作——这很令人沮丧，因为这些指令实际上可以为你节省一些时间。

Instead, such requests turn into basic web searches, returning only information on how to access those settings yourself. I was a bit disappointed with Google TV’s screensaver. It lets you draw from various sources, including an art gallery and your own photo library, but it doesn’t offer any “art mode” options like a mat overlay or automated brightness reduction.

相反，这些请求会变成基本的网页搜索，只返回关于如何自行访问这些设置的信息。我对 Google TV 的屏幕保护程序感到有些失望。它允许你从各种来源提取内容，包括艺术画廊和你自己的照片库，但它不提供任何“艺术模式”选项，例如遮罩叠加或自动亮度调节。

Great Picture, Surprising Sound. Software quibbles notwithstanding, the QM7L is a joy to behold. Whatever you care to make of TCL's marketing lingo (SQD Mini-LED, the “S” stands for “Super”), the results can’t be denied: dazzling color and ocean-floor blacks that really do bring OLED to mind.

画质出色，音效惊人。尽管软件方面存在一些小问题，但 QM7L 的视觉效果令人愉悦。无论你如何看待 TCL 的营销术语（SQD Mini-LED，“S”代表“超级”），其结果是不可否认的：令人眼花缭乱的色彩和深邃的黑色，确实让人联想到 OLED。

In fact, I’d argue that this TV “pops” more than the aforementioned Roku OLED—not because the colors are more vibrant, but because there’s greater brightness behind them. While watching Pixar’s Coco, for example, both TVs produced glorious hues, but my eye was more drawn to the TCL because the reds, greens, and blues just seemed to burst a bit more.

事实上，我认为这台电视比前面提到的 Roku OLED 更具“冲击力”——不是因为色彩更鲜艳，而是因为它们背后有更高的亮度。例如，在观看皮克斯的《寻梦环游记》时，两台电视都呈现了绚丽的色调，但我的目光更多地被 TCL 吸引，因为红色、绿色和蓝色似乎更加突出。

Even watching something like East of Eden on Netflix, with its decidedly muted color palette, it was the brightness that stood out; the QM7L revealed a bit more detail inside the Trask house. Interestingly, I also spotted a difference in the...

即使是观看 Netflix 上的《伊甸园之东》这类色彩明显柔和的影片，其亮度也十分突出；QM7L 在 Trask 家的场景中展现了更多的细节。有趣的是，我还发现了……之间的差异。
