# Microsoft’s massive Outlook outage might be over

在经历了近一天的全球电子邮件中断后，微软表示问题已经得到解决，且与网络攻击或人工智能无关。

Service is recovering for Microsoft Outlook and 365 after an almost two-day outage impacting millions of users around the world. While some apps and features are still running in a degraded state on Friday morning, some people (including Verge staffers) reported that access to their emails had been restored, though the flow of Outlook mail might be delayed.

在影响了全球数百万用户近两天后，微软 Outlook 和 365 的服务正在恢复。尽管周五上午部分应用程序和功能仍处于降级运行状态，但一些用户（包括《The Verge》的工作人员）报告称，他们的电子邮件访问权限已恢复，不过 Outlook 邮件的传输可能会有延迟。

Just before noon on Friday, Microsoft said it’s seeing “broad recovery,” and that the problem “was a technical issue not related to cyber or AI / agents.”

周五中午之前，微软表示已看到“广泛恢复”，并称该问题“是一个与网络或人工智能/代理无关的技术问题”。

Microsoft: We are deeply sorry for the disruption this incident has caused our customers and partners. We know the trust they place in our services and take that responsibility extremely seriously. We are seeing broad recovery and stability across our services. This was a technical issue not related to cyber or AI / agents. The incident was introduced by an issue during an earlier network capacity upgrade that surfaced following a recent network change. We have addressed the underlying issue and continue to restore service to remaining customers and regions. Given the scale of demand, we restored services gradually, validating stability at each stage. While this took longer, it helped ensure a stable restoration. We will provide a full post-incident report once our review is complete.

微软：对于此次事件给我们的客户和合作伙伴造成的干扰，我们深表歉意。我们深知他们对我们服务的信任，并极其认真地对待这一责任。我们看到我们的服务正在全面恢复并趋于稳定。这是一个与网络或人工智能/代理无关的技术问题。该事件是由早先一次网络容量升级期间出现的问题引起的，该问题在最近一次网络变更后显现出来。我们已经解决了根本问题，并继续为剩余的客户和地区恢复服务。鉴于需求规模巨大，我们采取了逐步恢复服务的方式，并在每个阶段验证稳定性。虽然这花费了更长时间，但有助于确保稳定的恢复。我们将在审查完成后提供完整的事故后报告。

Senior Verge correspondent Tom Warren reports that according to his sources, the outage is linked to Azure VNet config changes. Microsoft has said that it hasn’t found any evidence of the outage being connected to “malicious activity,” and that a full report will be shared after the issues are mitigated. In a statement reported by Sky News tech correspondent Rowland Manthorpe, Microsoft described the outage as a “technical issue,” and says it isn’t related to “cyber, AI/agents, DDOS, etc.”

《The Verge》资深记者 Tom Warren 报道称，据其消息来源，此次中断与 Azure VNet 配置更改有关。微软表示，尚未发现任何证据表明此次中断与“恶意活动”有关，并将在问题缓解后分享完整报告。在天空新闻科技记者 Rowland Manthorpe 报道的一份声明中，微软将此次中断描述为“技术问题”，并表示它与“网络、人工智能/代理、DDOS 等”无关。

The scope of the outage also affected features in other Microsoft services, including SharePoint Online, Teams, Copilot AI, and 365 apps.

此次中断的范围还影响了其他微软服务的功能，包括 SharePoint Online、Teams、Copilot AI 和 365 应用程序。
