# Atlas Coffee Club’s Black Friday Deals (2026)

Save this storySave this storyA coffee subscription is one of my favorite gifts for the person who has everything. It's fun; it's educational; and it's consumable, so it won't clutter up their house. We have a whole guide to our favorites, but Atlas has topped the list for years, and just launched its Black Friday deal.

咖啡订阅是我送给那些“什么都不缺”的人最喜欢的礼物之一。它既有趣又具有教育意义，而且是消耗品，不会让对方的家里堆满杂物。我们有一份关于我们最爱产品的完整指南，但 Atlas 多年来一直位居榜首，并且刚刚推出了黑色星期五优惠活动。

Once or twice a month—or quarterly—your recipient will receive specialty roasted beans from a different region of the world, along with a colorful postcard and tasting notes. The location will be a surprise—anywhere from Thailand to Burundi to China.

每月一到两次（或每季度一次），收礼人将收到来自世界不同地区的特色烘焙咖啡豆，以及一张精美的明信片和品鉴笔记。产地将是一个惊喜——可能是从泰国到布隆迪再到中国的任何地方。

For Black Friday, now through December 7, you'll get a free bag with any two-bag subscription purchase. You can choose whole bean or ground (or Nespresso or Keurig pods), roast type, regular or decaf, and—new this year—"Smooth & Balanced" or “Bright & Expressive” profiles. (A subscription to the latter, for more adventurous drinkers, runs an additional $2 per bag.)

在黑色星期五期间，即日起至 12 月 7 日，购买任何两袋装订阅服务即可获赠一袋。您可以选择咖啡豆或咖啡粉（或 Nespresso/Keurig 胶囊）、烘焙类型、常规或低咖啡因，以及今年新增的“顺滑平衡”或“明亮表现”风味。 （对于更喜欢冒险的饮用者，选择后者需每袋额外支付 2 美元。）
