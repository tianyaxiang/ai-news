If you run a hearing aid centre or an eye care clinic in an Indian town, your website has one job: help a worried patient (or their son or daughter) feel safe enough to call or walk in. Here is a practical way to plan it without overbuilding.

如果您在印度城镇经营助听器中心或眼科诊所，您的网站只有一个任务：帮助焦虑的患者（或其子女）感到足够安心，从而拨打电话或亲自到访。以下是一种无需过度建设的实用规划方法。

Know who is actually visiting Many visitors to a hearing aid site are adult children searching for a parent. Eye care visitors are often parents looking for a child's eye check-up, or people searching for cataract and spectacle options near them. Write for that person, in simple language, and offer Hindi or your local language if your patients use it.

了解谁在访问：许多助听器网站的访问者是为父母寻找服务的成年子女。眼科诊所的访问者通常是为孩子寻找视力检查的家长，或是寻找附近白内障和眼镜服务的人。请用简单的语言为这些人撰写内容，如果您的患者使用印地语或当地语言，请提供相应的语言版本。

Pages that matter Home: clinic name, city, one line on what you do, and a big call or WhatsApp button. Services: hearing tests, hearing aid fitting and servicing, eye check-ups, spectacles, contact lenses. One short paragraph each, in plain words. About the doctor or audiologist: name, qualifications and registration details exactly as they appear on your certificates. Add a real photo. Visit us: address, a Google Maps link, opening hours, landmark directions, parking notes, and whether home visits are available. FAQ: how long a test takes, whether to bring old reports, whether appointments are needed.

关键页面：首页：诊所名称、城市、一行简短的服务介绍，以及醒目的电话或 WhatsApp 按钮。服务：听力测试、助听器验配与维修、视力检查、眼镜、隐形眼镜。每项服务用一段简短、通俗的文字说明。关于医生或听力学家：姓名、资质和注册详情，需与证书上的信息完全一致。添加一张真实照片。到访我们：地址、谷歌地图链接、营业时间、地标指引、停车说明以及是否提供上门服务。常见问题：检查需要多长时间、是否需要携带旧报告、是否需要预约。

Write carefully about health Avoid promises like "100% cure" or "guaranteed results". Stick to what you actually offer and let the clinician explain outcomes in person. Only list brands you actually stock and only show patient stories if you have written permission.

谨慎撰写健康相关内容：避免使用“100%治愈”或“保证效果”之类的承诺。坚持陈述您实际提供的服务，并让临床医生亲自解释治疗结果。仅列出您实际库存的品牌，且仅在获得书面许可的情况下展示患者案例。

Make it easy on a phone Most people will open your site on a mid-range Android phone with patchy data. Keep images small, use a readable font size (older patients especially), keep strong contrast, and make buttons large enough to tap. A tap-to-call button and a WhatsApp appointment link should be visible without scrolling.

确保手机端易用：大多数人会在网络不稳定的中端安卓手机上打开您的网站。保持图片精简，使用易读的字体大小（特别是考虑到老年患者），保持高对比度，并确保按钮足够大以便点击。点击拨号按钮和 WhatsApp 预约链接应在无需滚动页面的情况下即可看到。

Booking without complexity A simple enquiry form with name, phone number and preferred time is enough to begin with. Reply quickly, since patients often contact two or three clinics. Make sure your Google Business Profile has the same phone number, hours and address as the website.

简化预约流程：一个包含姓名、电话号码和首选时间的简单咨询表单足以作为起点。请快速回复，因为患者通常会同时联系两到三家诊所。确保您的谷歌商家资料（Google Business Profile）中的电话、营业时间和地址与网站保持一致。

A simple launch checklist Test every phone number and map link yourself. Read each page aloud: would an elderly person understand it? Check how it loads on mobile data. Add a short privacy note on the form explaining how you use patient contact details. Ask a few existing patients to try booking and tell you where they got stuck.

简单的上线检查清单：亲自测试每一个电话号码和地图链接。大声朗读每一页内容：老年人能听懂吗？检查在移动数据下的加载情况。在表单上添加简短的隐私说明，解释您如何使用患者的联系方式。邀请几位现有患者尝试预约，并询问他们在哪里遇到了困难。
