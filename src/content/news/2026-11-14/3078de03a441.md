### Contra “Glue work considered harmful”

Sean Goedecke, Glue work considered harmful (emphasis here, and in the rest of the quotes, in original): So why doesn’t glue work get you promoted, if it’s so crucial to shipping projects? Are companies stupid? Are they deliberately leaving value on the table? No, I don’t think so. Companies don’t reward glue work because they don’t want you prioritizing it. And they don’t want you prioritizing it because they want you shipping features. Glue work is hard. If you’re capable of doing glue work well, they want you to use that ability to deliver projects instead of improving general efficiency.

Sean Goedecke 在《Glue work considered harmful》（此处及下文引用的重点均为原文所有）中写道：那么，如果“粘合工作”（glue work）对于交付项目如此关键，为什么它不能让你获得晋升呢？公司是愚蠢的吗？他们是在故意放弃价值吗？不，我不这么认为。公司不奖励粘合工作，是因为他们不希望你优先考虑它。而他们不希望你优先考虑它，是因为他们希望你专注于交付功能。粘合工作很难。如果你有能力做好粘合工作，他们希望你利用这种能力去交付项目，而不是去提升整体效率。

This is an argument to settle for mediocrity. If you’re like most people who have worked in big tech, it is plainly obvious to you that they are leaving value on the table. (Whether it is deliberate or not is a matter of what’s in people’s hearts.) What big tech employers want is largely incoherent. They want you delivering features. They also know that somebody’s got to do the glue work to keep the company running, and they have trouble describing or capturing its value.

这是一种安于平庸的论调。如果你像大多数在大厂工作过的人一样，你会清楚地看到他们确实在放弃价值。（这是否是故意的，取决于人们内心的想法。）大型科技雇主的需求在很大程度上是不连贯的。他们希望你交付功能。他们同时也知道，必须有人去做粘合工作来维持公司的运转，但他们却难以描述或捕捉其价值。

Here, and throughout his post, Goedecke is quite accurately describing some of the incentives presented to employees at big tech. My problem with the post is that he tells you to internalize them instead of keeping in mind that a better world is possible.

在此处以及整篇文章中，Goedecke 非常准确地描述了大型科技公司提供给员工的一些激励机制。我对这篇文章的问题在于，他建议你内化这些机制，而不是时刻铭记一个更美好的世界是可能的。

The core problem is that you’re deciding for yourself what the company needs instead of doing your job. Isn’t it your job to make your team run smoother? No! Your job is to execute the mission of your company’s leadership.

核心问题在于，你是在自作主张地决定公司需要什么，而不是在做你的本职工作。让团队运行得更顺畅难道不是你的工作吗？不！你的工作是执行公司领导层的使命。

If glue work is critical to the mission of a company’s leadership, how does neglecting it serve the mission?

如果粘合工作对公司领导层的使命至关重要，那么忽视它又如何能服务于该使命呢？

It is better to execute that mission at 60% efficiency than to spend all your time increasing efficiency in general (or even worse, to execute some other mission at 100% efficiency). Why? For two main reasons: first, you’re inevitably going to burn out, which will be bad for everyone; second, it’s better to let your team get used to operating at the base efficiency level of the company instead of artificially removing friction for a brief period.

以 60% 的效率执行该使命，要好过将所有时间都花在提升整体效率上（或者更糟，以 100% 的效率去执行其他使命）。为什么？主要有两个原因：第一，你不可避免地会精疲力竭，这对每个人都不利；第二，让你的团队习惯于在公司的基础效率水平上运作，要好过人为地在短时间内消除摩擦。

There are two very different things being conflated here: saving some gas in the tank for when there are real deadlines, which is great advice, versus not trying to make your coworkers’ lives better, which is a real case of misaligned incentives.

这里混淆了两个截然不同的概念：为应对真正的截止日期而保留精力（这是很好的建议），与不去尝试改善同事的工作生活（这确实是激励机制错位的案例）。

Besides, the premise is fundamentally wrong: there is a lot of glue work that, if you give it the respect it deserves, removes friction permanently. I’m obviously biased here, but I think the cargo-nextest project is a great example of glue work (running tests!) done so well that the problem mostly vanishes. More recently, at Oxide I wrote an agent skill called flake-review, backed by a catalog I wrote over a few hours. I’ve since used the skill to fix almost a dozen test flakes, making the entire team’s lives better.

此外，这个前提从根本上就是错误的：有很多粘合工作，如果你给予它应有的尊重，是可以永久消除摩擦的。我显然有偏见，但我认为 cargo-nextest 项目就是一个很好的例子，它将粘合工作（运行测试！）做得如此出色，以至于问题几乎消失了。最近，在 Oxide，我编写了一个名为 flake-review 的代理技能，并由我花几个小时编写的目录提供支持。此后，我利用该技能修复了近十几个测试不稳定问题，改善了整个团队的工作生活。

Should you never do glue work? No, you should do glue work tactically. That is, you should do this kind of extra work for the projects you lead — the projects whose success you’re accountable for — in order to make sure they succeed. You won’t be rewarded for the glue work specifically, but you will be rewarded for the success of the project. For other projects, you should just do your regular job.

你是否应该永远不做粘合工作？不，你应该有策略地进行粘合工作。也就是说，你应该为你所领导的项目——即你对其成功负有责任的项目——做这类额外工作，以确保它们取得成功。你不会因为粘合工作本身而获得奖励，但你会因为项目的成功而获得奖励。对于其他项目，你只需做好你的本职工作即可。

With the most emphatic of feelings, fuck that. I do glue work whenever I’m the best situated person to do the glue work. The idea that you shouldn’t help your peers, simply because someone in middle management has trouble articulating the value of helping your peers, should be deeply offensive to any serious professional.

我强烈地表示：去他的。只要我是最适合做粘合工作的人，我就会去做。那种认为你不应该帮助同事，仅仅因为某个中层管理人员无法阐明帮助同事的价值的想法，对任何严肃的专业人士来说都应该是极具冒犯性的。

Is this a deeply cynical take about how to succeed in office politics? I don’t actually think so. Large tech companies operate at something like 20-60% efficiency at any given time (as they get larger, they get less efficient).

这是一种关于如何在办公室政治中取得成功的极度愤世嫉俗的观点吗？我其实并不这么认为。大型科技公司在任何时候的运营效率大约只有 20-60%（随着规模扩大，效率会变得更低）。

A large chunk of people’s working lives is wasted on solvable problems whose value organizations don’t recognize. How can one not be cynical about this kind of unnecessary waste and suffering? It baffles me.

人们很大一部分的工作时间都被浪费在了一些组织无法识别其价值的可解决问题上。面对这种不必要的浪费和痛苦，人怎么能不愤世嫉俗呢？这让我感到困惑。

So, what is going on here? Any organization beyond a certain size requires legibility to function. The requirement for legibility pulls in all the things many people recoil at: performance reviews, OKRs, KPIs and whatnot.

那么，这里到底发生了什么？任何超过一定规模的组织都需要“可读性”（legibility）才能运作。对可读性的要求引入了许多人所反感的一切：绩效评估、OKR、KPI 等等。

Some big tech firms have figured out ways of making some of that glue work more legible. For example, Meta has fairly large internal tools and developer infrastructure teams, where goals are set around measurable indicators like performance numbers. But there will always be some glue work that will resist all attempts to measure it, and to the extent that firms don’t care about it, they will suffer.

一些大型科技公司已经找到了让部分粘合工作更具可读性的方法。例如，Meta 拥有相当庞大的内部工具和开发者基础设施团队，其目标是围绕性能数据等可衡量指标设定的。但总会有一些粘合工作会抵制所有衡量尝试，而如果公司不重视这些工作，他们就会遭受损失。

What does one do instead? Well, getting rid of performance reviews certainly helps! For the last few years of my work life at Oxide, the distinction between glue work and feature work has largely vanished; the question I’ve repeatedly asked my coworkers and manager is “how can I be helpful?”, not “what would look good on my performance review?” But I accept that larger organizations will need a greater degree of legibility. I think all the usual answers apply: don’t overhire (and certainly don’t incentivize managers to hire as many people as possible), don’t require all work to be legible, and trust your employees with the rest.

那么，应该怎么做呢？好吧，取消绩效评估肯定有帮助！在 Oxide 工作的最后几年里，粘合工作和功能工作之间的界限在很大程度上消失了；我反复问同事和经理的问题是“我能提供什么帮助？”，而不是“什么在我的绩效评估中看起来更好？”但我承认，大型组织确实需要更高程度的可读性。我认为所有常见的答案都适用：不要过度招聘（当然也不要激励经理尽可能多地招聘人员），不要要求所有工作都必须具备可读性，并信任你的员工去处理其余的工作。
