# My Mac Writing Stack in 2026, From Dictation to Send: 7 Apps, One Job Each

在 Mac 上进行的写作大多并非真正的写作。它往往是开始、停滞、重写第一行、寻找十分钟前复制的链接，以及反复修改一个句子直到它听起来不再那么令人烦躁。因此，我不再寻找一款全能应用，而是构建了一个工具栈，让每个工具各司其职。以下是按照文字实际流转顺序排列的工具清单。

### Stage 1: Getting words out
**1. Wispr Flow**
Hold a key, talk, and the text lands in whatever app you are in. It is the fastest way I know to get a messy first draft out of your head, especially long prompts and long emails. Flow is free on Mac, Windows, iPhone and Android, with generous weekly usage; Pro removes the limits and runs about $12 to $15 a month depending on billing. wisprflow.ai

**2. Superwhisper**
如果你想要对听写结果有更多控制权，这是首选。自定义模式允许你针对不同任务设置语气、结构和格式，你可以选择模型，而且它支持离线工作。免费试用版包含 3,000 字的 Pro 功能，之后会降级为免费版。superwhisper.com
这两款选其一即可，不要同时使用。如果你想要零配置，选 Flow；如果你喜欢微调，选 Superwhisper。

### Stage 2: Somewhere to think
**3. Bear**
Where drafts live before they become messages. Plain Markdown, tags instead of folders, and a minimal editor that stays out of the way. The free version is plenty to start; Bear Pro adds iCloud sync across your devices, OCR search and more themes. bear.app

### Stage 3: Finishing the sentence
**4. DraftKey**
这是其他工具忽略的阶段：你写出了句子的前半部分，但后半部分却卡住了。DraftKey 会在光标后以灰色显示建议的后续词汇，按下 Tab 键即可采纳。继续打字，建议就会自动消失。它还具备重写功能：按下 Command + Option，选择“更简短”、“更友好”、“更专业”或输入你自己的要求，预览结果后再插入。它适用于你启用的应用中的兼容文本框（密码框和某些自定义编辑器除外）。要求 Apple Silicon、macOS 14+、8 GB 内存。每台 Mac 免费 500 字，之后一次性付费 4.99 美元即可在最多三台 Mac 上使用。披露：我参与了 DraftKey 的开发，请酌情参考此推荐。getdraftkey.com

### Stage 4: Not typing the same thing twice
**5. Raycast Snippets**
Every canned reply, address, meeting link and sign-off I type more than twice becomes a snippet with a short keyword. Type the keyword in any app and it expands in place. Dynamic placeholders handle dates and clipboard contents. Snippets are part of Raycast's free core features. raycast.com/core-features/snippets

**6. Maccy**
写作意味着复制：链接、引用、错误信息、来自另一个对话的一行文字。Maccy 让你的剪贴板历史记录触手可及。输入几个字母，按下回车即可。免费、开源 (MIT)、macOS 14+。maccy.app

### Stage 5: Showing instead of explaining
**7. CleanShot X**
Some messages are better as a screenshot with an arrow on it. CleanShot X handles screenshots, screen recordings, annotation and scrolling capture, and its quick overlay lets you drag the result straight into an email or chat. $35 one-time with a year of updates, or via Setapp. cleanshot.com

### The whole pipeline
| Stage | Tool | Shortcut habit |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Get words out | Wispr Flow or Superwhisper | Hold key, talk |
| Think | Bear | Draft in Markdown |
| Finish sentences | DraftKey | Tab to accept, Command + Option to rewrite |
| Reuse text | Raycast Snippets, Maccy | Keyword or clipboard shortcut |
| Show it | CleanShot X | Capture, annotate, drag |
