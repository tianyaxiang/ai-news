The reason was that late last year, I came to the realization that we really need a viable alternative to Android and iOS. Before that, I still believed that using Degoogled ROMs such as LineageOS, GrapheneOS, or others would be enough. You would just install F-Droid or another alternative app store, enable the developer settings, and then you would be good to go.

原因在于去年年底，我意识到我们确实需要 Android 和 iOS 之外的可行替代方案。在此之前，我一直认为使用去谷歌化的 ROM（如 LineageOS、GrapheneOS 等）就足够了。你只需要安装 F-Droid 或其他替代应用商店，开启开发者选项，然后就可以正常使用了。

Recent events and the trends we can observe tell that things are moving in the opposite direction, the direction of people (users and developers) increasingly losing control over their devices and their apps. iOS was always closed, and Apple has more control over your device than you. Android was supposed to be the open alternative. Deep down in theory that is still true, in practice, Google is trying very hard to close that gap gradually.

最近发生的事件和我们观察到的趋势表明，事情正朝着相反的方向发展，即人们（用户和开发者）对自己的设备和应用的控制权正日益丧失。iOS 一直是封闭的，苹果对你设备的控制权比你本人还要大。Android 本应是开放的替代方案。从理论深处来看这或许依然成立，但在实践中，谷歌正极力试图逐步缩小这一差距。

Users have been losing control for a while, and recently we found that to run software on your devices you will need to pay for Google’s permission. It gets worse; even paying you will need to follow a cumbersome process to install software that can take 24 hours. A movement was started to try to stop this, but I think it is a losing battle.

用户失去控制权已经有一段时间了，最近我们发现，要在设备上运行软件，你需要为谷歌的许可付费。更糟糕的是，即使付费，你还需要遵循繁琐的流程来安装软件，这可能需要耗费 24 小时。虽然有人发起运动试图阻止这种情况，但我认为这是一场注定失败的战斗。

The above is completely the oposite of the freedom we still have on our computers, where one can easily change the software their device uses with little control from the supplier. You bought it with Windows, but you can replace it with Linux, FreeBSD, OpenBSD, ReactOS, Haiku, etc. And then run your or another person’s apps as you wish (the device is yours).

上述情况与我们在电脑上依然享有的自由完全相反，在电脑上，人们可以轻松更换设备使用的软件，而几乎不受供应商的控制。你买的时候预装的是 Windows，但你可以将其替换为 Linux、FreeBSD、OpenBSD、ReactOS、Haiku 等。然后随心所欲地运行你或他人开发的应用程序（设备是你自己的）。

On mobile you are locked down, and they are trying to make it increasingly difficult to run anything else. Even app developers and companies are incentivized to lock their apps to only these 2 systems for “security” reasons; alternative ROMs are being blocked. Our lives are now dependent on these mobile devices (almost everything is an app or requires an app); that’s a fact of modern times.

在移动端，你被锁定了，他们正试图让运行其他系统变得越来越困难。甚至连应用开发者和公司也因为“安全”原因被激励将应用锁定在这两个系统上；替代 ROM 正在被封锁。我们的生活现在依赖于这些移动设备（几乎所有事情都是一个应用或需要一个应用）；这是现代社会的事实。

Ahead of the disenfranchisement of people without a smartphone or people with the old phone, we are close to witnessing a person being required to become a customer of two foreign companies to be a citizen in their own country. And I just don’t want to get started on all the digital sovereignty aspects of it. They affect people, organizations, and governments, and it is clear that they nowadays are more important than ever.

面对没有智能手机或使用旧手机的人群被剥夺权利的现状，我们即将目睹一个人为了在自己的国家成为一名公民，而被迫成为两家外国公司的客户。我甚至不想开始讨论其中涉及的所有数字主权问题。这些问题影响着个人、组织和政府，显而易见，它们在当今比以往任何时候都更加重要。

What would an alternative look like? The truth is that I’m not 100% sure, but I do know we need one. I guess it would be nice that it would be open-source (at least the core of it), with multiple stores or ways to provide/get software, and with a clear focus on interoperability. It shouldn’t also be a copy of the existing ecosystems.

替代方案应该是什么样的？事实是我并不百分之百确定，但我确实知道我们需要一个。我想如果它是开源的（至少核心部分是开源的），拥有多个商店或提供/获取软件的途径，并且明确关注互操作性，那会很好。它也不应该只是现有生态系统的复制品。

This means a Windows Phone (belonging to Microsoft) wouldn’t be a real alternative, since it would also be closed and belong to a foreign big tech company. Tiny and completely distinct systems, popping like mushrooms, wouldn’t also be a real alternative, since they would fail to get any sort of traction and attention. They might be interesting for hobbyists to experiment with, but nobody will invest in them if there aren’t sufficient people using and building for them.

这意味着 Windows Phone（属于微软）不会是一个真正的替代方案，因为它同样是封闭的，且属于一家外国大型科技公司。像雨后春笋般涌现的微小且完全不同的系统也不是真正的替代方案，因为它们无法获得任何吸引力和关注。它们对于爱好者来说可能很有趣，但如果没有足够多的人使用并为它们开发，就不会有人投资它们。

Starting from scratch is also very hard; we have a few examples of failed attempts. So, our best bet is to put the weight of all the people who are uncomfortable with this situation behind something that already exists, that already has some community, that can be developed independently. The best candidates I could find are: SailfishOS, Nura (previously called PostmarketOS), Ubuntu Touch.

从零开始也非常困难；我们已经有几个失败的例子。因此，我们最好的选择是让所有对现状感到不安的人支持那些已经存在、拥有一定社区基础且可以独立开发的系统。我能找到的最佳候选者是：SailfishOS、Nura（以前称为 PostmarketOS）和 Ubuntu Touch。

These will be the ones I will be exploring, testing, and perhaps contributing to. Regardless of my preference, I don’t mind which one wins; I just think one of them needs to take off.

这些将是我探索、测试并可能为其做出贡献的对象。无论我的偏好如何，我不介意哪一个胜出；我只是认为其中之一必须成功发展起来。
