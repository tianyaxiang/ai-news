Donald Trump Jr. agreed to end his advisory role with the prediction market Kalshi shortly after Trump Jr.’s venture capital firm made a large investment in its competitor, Polymarket. “We have mutually agreed to end Don’s advisory role after his firm’s significant investment in a competitor this past summer. We are grateful for his contributions in helping us market Kalshi to more customers,” Kalshi said in a statement provided to Ars and other news outlets this week.

在小唐纳德·特朗普（Donald Trump Jr.）的风险投资公司对其竞争对手 Polymarket 进行大额投资后不久，他便同意结束与预测市场平台 Kalshi 的顾问关系。Kalshi 本周在提供给《Ars》及其他新闻媒体的一份声明中表示：“在小唐纳德的公司今年夏天对竞争对手进行重大投资后，我们双方已达成一致，结束他的顾问角色。我们感谢他为帮助我们将 Kalshi 推向更多客户所做的贡献。”

Trump Jr. may still have a financial interest in Kalshi, which has benefited from the Trump administration’s legal and regulatory support for prediction markets. Trump Jr. “was handed about $300,000 worth of equity in Kalshi after joining the prediction market platform as a strategic adviser in early 2025,” the Financial Times reported in June. “Kalshi’s donation of shares to the US president’s eldest son granted him a stake in the private company when it was valued at under $2 billion, less than a tenth of its $22 billion valuation in a funding round last month,” the Financial Times article said. Kalshi is reportedly in talks for a new funding round that would value it at $65 billion.

小唐纳德可能仍持有 Kalshi 的经济利益，该公司一直受益于特朗普政府对预测市场提供的法律和监管支持。《金融时报》6 月报道称，小唐纳德“在 2025 年初加入该预测市场平台担任战略顾问后，获得了价值约 30 万美元的 Kalshi 股权”。该报道指出：“Kalshi 向美国总统长子赠送股份时，这家私营公司的估值不到 20 亿美元，仅为上个月融资轮中 220 亿美元估值的十分之一。”据报道，Kalshi 目前正在商谈新一轮融资，估值有望达到 650 亿美元。

Trump Jr. simultaneously has an interest in Polymarket via his role as a partner at 1789 Capital and as an advisor for Polymarket. The venture firm’s investment of $300 million in Polymarket was reported in August 2026, building on a smaller investment it made in Polymarket in August 2025. “We have been discussing this internally since August when news of 1789’s investment in Polymarket became public,” a Kalshi spokesperson said. “Kalshi reached out to Don Jr. this fall to discuss his firm’s increased investment in our top competitor and his role on its advisory board… both parties understood that advising two competitors in a growing sector was complicating things for both of us.”

与此同时，小唐纳德通过其在 1789 Capital 的合伙人身份以及作为 Polymarket 顾问的身份，在 Polymarket 中也拥有利益。据报道，该风险投资公司在 2026 年 8 月向 Polymarket 投资了 3 亿美元，此前它曾在 2025 年 8 月进行过一笔较小的投资。Kalshi 发言人表示：“自 8 月 1789 Capital 投资 Polymarket 的消息公开以来，我们一直在内部讨论此事。Kalshi 在今年秋天联系了小唐纳德，讨论他所在公司对我们主要竞争对手的追加投资以及他在该竞争对手顾问委员会中的角色……双方都明白，在同一个增长中的行业里同时担任两家竞争对手的顾问，让双方的处境都变得复杂。”

Kalshi CEO admitted “optics” weren’t good. The Trump administration has helped prediction markets grow with a permissive regulatory approach taken by the Commodity Futures Exchange Commission (CFTC). The CFTC has also sued 11 states in attempts to block state regulation of prediction markets, and filed briefs supporting Kalshi in its own cases against individual states. Attorneys general in many states accuse Kalshi of running an illegal gambling operation. Kalshi co-founder and CEO Tarek Mansour told MS Now in an interview last month, “I get asked a lot about Don. I mean, I think that is a fair question… We are facing sort of an optics issue, I think.”

Kalshi 首席执行官承认“观感”不佳。特朗普政府通过商品期货交易委员会（CFTC）采取的宽松监管方式，帮助了预测市场的增长。CFTC 还起诉了 11 个州，试图阻止各州对预测市场进行监管，并提交了支持 Kalshi 对抗各州的法律简报。许多州的司法部长指控 Kalshi 在经营非法赌博业务。Kalshi 联合创始人兼首席执行官塔雷克·曼苏尔（Tarek Mansour）上个月在接受《MS Now》采访时表示：“我经常被问到关于小唐纳德的问题。我的意思是，我认为这是一个合理的问题……我认为我们正面临某种观感上的问题。”

Mansour claimed the relationship was not political, and it began as a way to market Kalshi at a time when it was much smaller and the public didn’t know much about prediction markets. “The key thing is we did it before the election. It is not a political decision, and it still isn’t. That’s the key thing,” Mansour said. In fact, Kalshi named Trump Jr. as a strategic advisor over two months after the November 2024 presidential election. Mansour announced Trump Jr.’s appointment as strategic advisor in a post on January 13, 2025, a week before Donald Trump’s second inauguration.

曼苏尔声称这种关系并非出于政治目的，最初只是为了在 Kalshi 规模较小且公众对预测市场了解不多时进行推广。曼苏尔说：“关键在于我们在大选前就这么做了。这不是一个政治决定，现在也不是。这是关键所在。”事实上，Kalshi 是在 2024 年 11 月总统大选结束两个多月后才任命小唐纳德为战略顾问的。曼苏尔于 2025 年 1 月 13 日（即唐纳德·特朗普第二次就职典礼前一周）发帖宣布了这一任命。

Trump Jr. announced his position with Kalshi in an X post on the same day and indicated that he decided to help Kalshi on Election Day. “On Election night at Mar-a-Lago, while biased outlets called the race a coin toss, my family and close friends used the prediction market Kalshi to know we won hours ahead of the fake news media,” Trump Jr. wrote at the time. “I immediately knew I had to contribute to their mission. Today, I am proud to announce that I am joining Kalshi as a strategic advisor.”

小唐纳德当天在 X 平台上发帖宣布了他在 Kalshi 的职位，并表示他是在选举日当天决定帮助 Kalshi 的。小唐纳德当时写道：“在海湖庄园的选举之夜，当那些有偏见的媒体称选情难分胜负时，我的家人和密友通过预测市场 Kalshi，比假新闻媒体提前几个小时就知道我们赢了。我立刻意识到我必须为他们的使命做出贡献。今天，我很自豪地宣布，我将作为战略顾问加入 Kalshi。”

CEO claims no “regulatory involvement” for Trump Jr. After Mansour’s claim that “we did it before the election,” MS Now Host Stephanie Ruhle said, “But you didn’t say after, you know what, maybe this is a conflict of interest.” Mansour’s response suggested that Trump Jr. did not help Kalshi on regulatory matters. “We never had any policy regulatory involvement,” he said. “It was always about getting the word out about prediction markets, how they work, looking at the forecasts, making sure people understand that it’s not a poll; it’s actually probabilities. That was always our focus… we have advisors from both sides of the aisle. We have Democratic advisors; we have Republican advisors.”

首席执行官声称小唐纳德没有“监管参与”。在曼苏尔声称“我们在大选前就这么做了”之后，《MS Now》主持人斯蒂芬妮·鲁尔（Stephanie Ruhle）说：“但你没说在大选之后，你难道没意识到这可能存在利益冲突吗？”曼苏尔的回应暗示小唐纳德并未在监管事务上帮助过 Kalshi。他说：“我们从未有过任何政策监管方面的参与。我们的重点始终是宣传预测市场、解释其运作方式、查看预测结果，并确保人们明白这不是民意调查，而是概率。这始终是我们的重点……我们有来自两党的顾问。我们既有民主党顾问，也有共和党顾问。”

Mansour said Kalshi sought to work with anyone “that can help us get the message out… anyone who likes prediction markets, and Don does like prediction markets, we wanted to work with, because we wanted to get the word out about what prediction markets are. Don has 10 million followers on Twitter… he has a huge following, and he likes the idea of going direct, tweeting.” The Kalshi spokesperson said this week that “Don Jr. has been a great marketing consultant for us—he helped us ‘go direct’ early on, which was a big part of our growth online.”

曼苏尔表示，Kalshi 寻求与任何“能帮助我们传达信息的人”合作，“任何喜欢预测市场的人，而小唐纳德确实喜欢预测市场，我们都想与他合作，因为我们想让人们了解什么是预测市场。小唐纳德在 Twitter 上有 1000 万粉丝……他拥有庞大的追随者群体，而且他喜欢直接发布推文的想法。”Kalshi 发言人本周表示：“小唐纳德一直是我们出色的营销顾问——他很早就帮助我们实现了‘直接传播’，这是我们在线增长的重要组成部分。”

Kalshi has also paid former US Senator Blanche Lincoln (D-Ark.) for lobbying services since 2024. As a Democratic senator in 2010, Lincoln helped write the law that now regulates prediction markets and said the US should not let event contracts be used for sports gambling. As a Kalshi lobbyist, she supports event contracts based on the results of sporting contests and opposes state regulation of prediction markets. Trump Media and Technology Group last year announced plans for a “Truth Predict” prediction market for Truth Social, but appears to have scaled back the plan.

自 2024 年以来，Kalshi 还向美国前参议员布兰奇·林肯（民主党，阿肯色州）支付了游说服务费。作为 2010 年的民主党参议员，林肯曾参与起草了目前监管预测市场的法律，并表示美国不应允许将事件合约用于体育博彩。作为 Kalshi 的游说者，她现在支持基于体育赛事结果的事件合约，并反对各州对预测市场进行监管。特朗普媒体与技术集团去年宣布了为“真相社交”（Truth Social）平台建立“真相预测”（Truth Predict）市场的计划，但目前看来已缩减了该计划。
