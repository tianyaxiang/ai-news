# FAA ground stop shuts down flights nationwide

FAA 全美停飞令导致航班大面积停航

A communications problem is reportedly affecting Air Route Traffic Control Centers.

据报道，通信故障正在影响各空中航线交通管制中心。

Takeoffs from airports throughout the US largely stopped on Friday after the FAA issued a national ground stop “due to extraordinary circumstances and for reasons of safety,” while planes in the air will be allowed to land. The FAA tweeted just after 5PM ET that, “There has been a temporary telecommunications outage - out of an abundance of caution all flights have been halted. Every flight has been accounted for and international flights will be handled on a case by case basis.” The flight tracking site FlightAware lists 10,494 US flights as delayed and 3,396 flights canceled as of 6:16PM ET.

周五，在 FAA 以“特殊情况和安全原因”为由发布全国性停飞令后，美国各地的机场基本停止了起飞，但空中的飞机仍被允许降落。FAA 在美东时间下午 5 点后发推文称：“发生了暂时的电信中断，出于谨慎考虑，所有航班已暂停。每架航班的情况均已掌握，国际航班将视具体情况处理。”截至美东时间下午 6:16，航班追踪网站 FlightAware 显示美国有 10,494 架次航班延误，3,396 架次航班取消。

According to CNN, the last nationwide ground stop in 2023 lasted for about 90 minutes after the NOTAM (Notice to Air Missions) system for pilots went down, and the last one prior to that was after the attacks on September 11th, 2001.

据 CNN 报道，2023 年上一次全国性停飞令持续了约 90 分钟，原因是飞行员使用的 NOTAM（空中任务通知）系统瘫痪；而在此之前，上一次全国性停飞发生在 2001 年 9 月 11 日恐怖袭击之后。

Speaking to reporters at the White House on Friday afternoon, Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin told reporters that there is currently no reason to suspect a cyberattack or other national security threat, or any connection to the recent Microsoft Outlook outage, but that so far no cause has been ruled out for the “telecommunications outage.”

周五下午，国土安全部长马克韦恩·穆林（Markwayne Mullin）在白宫对记者表示，目前没有理由怀疑这是一次网络攻击或其他国家安全威胁，也与近期微软 Outlook 的故障无关，但目前尚未排除导致此次“电信中断”的任何原因。

When asked about any possible connection to the cloud-based AI Strategic Management of Airspace, Routes and Trajectories (SMART) system that was activated nationwide on November 10th, he said they were looking into that. Air Space Intelligence (ASI), the company that designed the system and was awarded a 12-year $875 million contract in June to deploy it, denied any connection, tweeting “The current ground stop is not based on or a result of the SMART system, any product of ASI or our integration with FAA and ATC.”

当被问及这是否与 11 月 10 日在全国范围内启用的基于云的 AI 空域、航线和轨迹战略管理（SMART）系统有关时，他表示正在调查。设计该系统并于 6 月获得 12 年期 8.75 亿美元部署合同的 Air Space Intelligence (ASI) 公司否认了任何关联，并在推特上表示：“目前的停飞并非基于或源于 SMART 系统、ASI 的任何产品，或我们与 FAA 及 ATC 的集成。”

Mullin also said international flights and departures are being handled on a case-by-case basis, while NBC News and the New York Times report that some flights headed to Europe and Asia have taken off from Boston and New York.

穆林还表示，国际航班和离港航班正在逐案处理，而 NBC 新闻和《纽约时报》报道称，一些飞往欧洲和亚洲的航班已从波士顿和纽约起飞。

Jon Ostrower of The Air Current reports that the outage began with the Chicago air traffic control center, followed by centers in Boston, Washington, New York, and Minneapolis, and as the FAA issued a ground stop for aircraft flying east, more facilities began reporting problems. He says the problems are disrupting both the radar and radio frequencies used by air traffic controllers and pilots, while a CNN update on Friday cited an unnamed source claiming the FAA “doesn’t know yet what is going on,” with no clear answers about the cause.

《The Air Current》的乔恩·奥斯特罗尔（Jon Ostrower）报道称，故障始于芝加哥空中交通管制中心，随后波士顿、华盛顿、纽约和明尼阿波利斯的中心也受到影响。随着 FAA 对向东飞行的飞机发布停飞令，更多设施开始报告问题。他表示，这些问题干扰了空中交通管制员和飞行员使用的雷达和无线电频率。周五 CNN 的更新援引一位匿名消息人士的话称，FAA“尚不清楚发生了什么”，目前对故障原因尚无明确答案。
