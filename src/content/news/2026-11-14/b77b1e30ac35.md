# The new Apple TV has a YouTube problem

新款 Apple TV 遇到了 YouTube 问题。

A software update and a reboot should get YouTube working on your new Apple TV 4K.

软件更新和重启应该能让 YouTube 在你的新款 Apple TV 4K 上正常运行。

People who received the brand new Apple TV 4k today saw some strange error messages as soon as they opened the YouTube app. As the first units arrived, the app was initially only showing a black screen with a QR code and an error message saying “YouTube is not yet available on this device.”

今天收到全新 Apple TV 4K 的用户在打开 YouTube 应用时，立即看到了一些奇怪的错误信息。随着首批设备到货，该应用最初只显示一个带有二维码的黑屏，并附有一条错误信息，称“YouTube 尚未在此设备上提供”。

In some cases the app was functional, but had a transparent red bar stuck at the bottom of the screen saying, “YouTube app is approved for device testing only and not for production use.”

在某些情况下，应用虽然可以运行，但屏幕底部却卡着一条透明的红色横条，上面写着：“YouTube 应用仅供设备测试使用，不得用于生产环境。”

Installing the new tvOS 27.2 update and rebooting wasn’t enough to resolve the issue for The Verge’s Tom Warren, he also had to uninstall and reinstall the app.

对于 The Verge 的 Tom Warren 来说，安装新的 tvOS 27.2 更新并重启并不能解决这个问题，他还必须卸载并重新安装该应用。

The Verge’s John Higgins said in his review of the new Apple TV 4K that it’s “one of the best streaming devices available,” but expensive compared to other streaming boxes.

The Verge 的 John Higgins 在对新款 Apple TV 4K 的评测中表示，它是“目前市面上最好的流媒体设备之一”，但与其他流媒体机顶盒相比价格昂贵。
