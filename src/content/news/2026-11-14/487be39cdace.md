# FAA orders third nationwide ground stop in its history

On Friday afternoon, days after expanding use of an AI tool, the Federal Aviation Administration issued a nationwide ground stop, halting departures from all US airports. Secretary of Transportation Sean Duffy announced the ground stop on X, citing a “communication issue impacting all Air Route Traffic Control Centers.”

周五下午，在扩大使用人工智能工具几天后，美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）发布了全国范围内的停飞令，暂停了所有美国机场的航班起飞。交通部长肖恩·达菲（Sean Duffy）在 X 上宣布了这一停飞令，称原因是“影响所有航路交通管制中心的通信问题”。

Duffy promised updates as more information is known and said FAA staff were troubleshooting the problem. Department of Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin said that the ground stop was not prompted by a security threat. “This is a telecommunications outage out of the FAA. Out of an abundance of caution, we have grounded all of the flights,” Mullin said. “There’s no indication as of yet that this is a nefarious attack against us.”

达菲承诺在获得更多信息后会发布最新消息，并表示 FAA 工作人员正在排查故障。国土安全部长马克韦恩·马林（Markwayne Mullin）表示，此次停飞并非由安全威胁引起。“这是 FAA 的电信中断。出于谨慎考虑，我们停飞了所有航班，”马林说，“目前没有任何迹象表明这是一次针对我们的恶意攻击。”

Mullin said the FAA would handle incoming international flights on a case-by-case basis. This is only the third nationwide ground stop issued by the FAA. The first, and most memorable, came during the attacks of September 11, 2001, when the FAA stopped all civilian departures nationwide and subsequently ordered all airborne aircraft to land. Commercial flights did not take off again until September 13.

马林表示，FAA 将根据具体情况处理入境的国际航班。这是 FAA 历史上第三次发布全国性停飞令。第一次也是最令人难忘的一次发生在 2001 年 9 月 11 日的袭击事件期间，当时 FAA 停止了全国所有民用航班的起飞，并随后下令所有在空中的飞机降落。商业航班直到 9 月 13 日才恢复起飞。

Flights were also grounded for less than two hours the morning of January 11, 2023, after its Notice to Air Missions system suffered an outage. Unfortunately for travelers, based on what is known so far, this is likely to be a lengthy ground stop. Airlines have already canceled flights scheduled to leave later Friday, and American Airlines warned that disruptions are expected for the rest of the day.

2023 年 1 月 11 日上午，在“空中任务通知”（Notice to Air Missions）系统发生故障后，航班也曾停飞不到两个小时。对于旅客来说不幸的是，根据目前已知的情况，这很可能是一次长时间的停飞。航空公司已经取消了原定于周五晚些时候起飞的航班，美国航空（American Airlines）警告称，预计当天余下时间航班都会受到干扰。

“This is going to take some time,” Mullin said. “Flights are accounted for, and there should be no fear to the public at all.”

“这需要一些时间，”马林说，“所有航班都在掌控之中，公众完全不必恐慌。”
