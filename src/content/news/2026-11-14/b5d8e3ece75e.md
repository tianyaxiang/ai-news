# ChatGPT Wallet lets OpenAI’s Dots agents make purchases for you

OpenAI 表示，ChatGPT Wallet 不会与 AI 代理共享您的真实信用卡信息。

OpenAI says ChatGPT Wallet won’t share your actual credit card info with its AI agents.

在今年早些时候推出 Dots AI 代理平台后，OpenAI 正在为这些代理推出一款钱包。ChatGPT Wallet 从今天开始向美国 18 岁及以上、使用美国账单地址以及“符合条件的美国发行的美国运通、大通、万事达和 Visa 卡”的 ChatGPT Pro 订阅用户推出。

After launching its Dots AI agents platform earlier this year, OpenAI is launching a wallet for the agents. ChatGPT Wallet is rolling out starting today for ChatGPT Pro subscribers in the US aged 18 and older using a US billing address and “eligible US-issued American Express, Chase, Mastercard and Visa cards.”

将卡片添加到钱包后，您的 Dots 代理可以在响应您的提示时使用它进行购买，但它们会在每次结账前与您确认。OpenAI 的支持页面称，使用 ChatGPT Wallet 的每笔购买都需要通过 Face ID 或指纹扫描等生物识别验证进行安全检查。

After adding a card to the wallet, your Dots agents can use it to buy things in response to your prompts, but they’ll check in with you each time before checking out. OpenAI’s support page says every purchase with ChatGPT Wallet requires a security check with biometric verification like Face ID or a fingerprint scan.

用户必须批准 ChatGPT Wallet 的每笔购买。在您审查并确认 AI 代理的购买行为后，OpenAI 表示，类似于使用“点击支付”、Apple Pay 或 Google Wallet，它会用支付令牌替换您的信用卡详细信息，因此您的真实卡片信息不会与您的 Dots 代理或您购买的商家共享。

Users have to approve every purchase from ChatGPT Wallet. After you review the AI agent’s purchase and sign off on it, OpenAI says that, similar to using tap to pay, Apple Pay, or Google Wallet, it substitutes your credit card details with a payment token, so your actual card info isn’t shared with your Dots agent or the business you’re buying from.

OpenAI 表示，最终预计“用户能够通过设置明确的参数（例如消费限额、购买类别或时间段）并预先批准这些购买，从而将购买行为完全委托给他们的代理”。

Eventually, OpenAI says it expects “users to be able to delegate purchases entirely to their agent by setting clear parameters—such as spending limits, purchase categories, or time periods—and pre-approve those purchases.”

ChatGPT Wallet 可能无法在所有地方使用。一些零售商对 AI 代理有限制，或者完全屏蔽了它们。今年早些时候，亚马逊屏蔽了 Meta 的 Muse 代理。OpenAI 表示，为了让代理能够使用 ChatGPT Wallet，“网站需要确保代理不会被无意中屏蔽，并且支付和风险系统需要识别代理进行的合法购买”。

ChatGPT Wallet might not work everywhere. Some retailers have restrictions on AI agents or block them altogether. Earlier this year, Amazon blocked Meta’s Muse agents. OpenAI says that in order for agents to use ChatGPT Wallet, “websites need to ensure agents are not inadvertently blocked, and payment and risk systems need to recognize legitimate purchases made by agents.”
