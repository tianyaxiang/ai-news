# Jensen Huang was reckless with the environment, too

Nvidia’s environmental footprint extends way beyond its own offices.

英伟达的环境足迹远不止于其自身的办公场所。

Under cofounder Jensen Huang, Nvidia has become the engine for the AI boom — and one of the tech industry’s biggest environmental liabilities. Its sought-after chips have triggered a new wave of water- and chemical-intensive factories, drained power grids from inside massive data centers, and set the stage for a looming e-waste problem.

在联合创始人黄仁勋的领导下，英伟达已成为人工智能繁荣的引擎，同时也成为了科技行业最大的环境负债之一。其备受追捧的芯片引发了新一轮高耗水、高耗化学品的工厂建设浪潮，耗尽了大型数据中心的电力供应，并为即将到来的电子垃圾问题埋下了隐患。

Under Huang’s leadership, Nvidia clashed with environmental groups seeking to hold the company accountable for its so-called “hidden” emissions. Huang has talked up the idea of using AI to fight climate change while his company’s supply chain emissions ballooned. Nvidia’s investments, meanwhile, have supported the buildout of data centers that have sparked intense backlash for both their local and global impact. Now, after the CEO’s shocking resignation amid a sprawling sex-and-espionage scandal, advocates are urging Nvidia to turn the page.

在黄仁勋的领导下，英伟达与那些试图让公司为其所谓“隐性”排放负责的环保组织发生了冲突。黄仁勋在大谈利用人工智能应对气候变化的同时，其公司的供应链排放量却在激增。与此同时，英伟达的投资支持了数据中心的建设，这些数据中心因其对当地和全球的影响而引发了强烈抵制。如今，随着这位首席执行官在一场涉及性与间谍活动的丑闻中突然辞职，倡导者们正敦促英伟达翻开新的一页。

“Jensen Huang has failed to put climate responsibility at the heart of the company’s growth strategy, to slow down the unchecked expansion of data centres or to prevent its chips from being deployed in unethical applications like automated warfare,” Avex Li, Greenpeace East Asia’s project lead for AI supply chain campaign, said in a press release Thursday. The organization is now pushing interim CEO Colette Kress to commit Nvidia to decarbonizing by investing in renewable energy, as well as ensuring its technology is used in a way that “respects human rights.” Nvidia didn’t immediately respond to requests for comment from The Verge.

绿色和平东亚分部人工智能供应链项目负责人李艾维克斯（Avex Li）在周四的新闻稿中表示：“黄仁勋未能将气候责任置于公司增长战略的核心，未能减缓数据中心的无序扩张，也未能阻止其芯片被部署在自动化战争等不道德的应用中。”该组织目前正敦促临时首席执行官科莱特·克雷斯（Colette Kress）承诺英伟达通过投资可再生能源实现脱碳，并确保其技术的使用方式“尊重人权”。英伟达未立即回应《The Verge》的置评请求。

Huang has projected optimism about how AI can boost the adoption of renewable energy, even as the planet-heating emissions of tech companies — including his own — have grown in the generative AI era. “If you want to turn a corner on climate change, if you want a future that’s sustainable, lean into A.I. It is the best opportunity we have to get there,” Huang said in a September episode of The Ezra Klein Show, arguing that electricity demand for AI is leading companies to buy more sustainable energy.

尽管在生成式人工智能时代，包括英伟达在内的科技公司所产生的全球变暖排放量不断增加，但黄仁勋对人工智能如何促进可再生能源的采用仍表现出乐观态度。黄仁勋在九月份的一期《埃兹拉·克莱恩秀》（The Ezra Klein Show）节目中表示：“如果你想在气候变化问题上实现转折，如果你想要一个可持续的未来，那就拥抱人工智能。这是我们实现这一目标的最佳机会。”他认为，人工智能的电力需求正在促使企业购买更多可持续能源。

In the same interview, however, he admitted data centers will rely on more fossil fuels in the near term, and that building up the infrastructure for AI will likely result in “an enormous amount of pain and suffering.” He compared AI to a surgeon who has “to cut you open to save you.”

然而，在同一次采访中，他也承认数据中心在短期内将依赖更多的化石燃料，并且建设人工智能基础设施可能会导致“巨大的痛苦和折磨”。他将人工智能比作一位“必须切开你的身体才能救你”的外科医生。

Nvidia’s supply-chain emissions have grown 725 percent since 2020, according to an analysis of the company’s public disclosures by research analyst Ketan Joshi and several environmental organizations, including Greenpeace and Climate Action Against Disinformation, published in September. It’s an increase of about 9.4 million metric tons of planet-heating pollution, roughly equivalent to the emissions from nearly 25 gas plants over a year. Supply chain emissions the company disclosed for fiscal year 2026 reached around 10.7 million metric tons of CO2, according to Nvidia’s latest sustainability report.

根据研究分析师克坦·乔希（Ketan Joshi）及包括绿色和平组织和“反虚假信息气候行动”（Climate Action Against Disinformation）在内的多个环保组织于九月份发布的一份对英伟达公开披露信息的分析报告，自2020年以来，英伟达的供应链排放量增长了725%。这相当于增加了约940万公吨的全球变暖污染物，大致相当于近25座天然气发电厂一年的排放量。根据英伟达最新的可持续发展报告，该公司披露的2026财年供应链排放量达到了约1070万公吨二氧化碳。

Joshi and his co-authors approximated how much greenhouse gas pollution comes from the use of chips the company sells, a category Nvidia doesn’t include in its sustainability reports. Those emissions could have reached 21 million metric tons in 2025, according to the report, based on estimated chip power consumption. That level of pollution puts Nvidia on par with Russia’s state-owned coal company, according to Joshi.

乔希及其合著者估算了英伟达所售芯片在使用过程中产生的温室气体污染量，这一类别并未包含在英伟达的可持续发展报告中。报告显示，基于芯片功耗的估算，这些排放量在2025年可能已达到2100万公吨。乔希认为，这一污染水平使英伟达与俄罗斯国有煤炭公司的排放量相当。

Those “indirect” emissions — from its supply chain and the use of its products — make up the bulk of Nvidia’s climate impact. The carbon footprint of facilities Nvidia actually owns and operates looks much smaller in comparison because Nvidia designs chips that other companies like TSMC then manufacture. Greenhouse gas emissions coming directly from Nvidia-operated facilities and vehicles only reached 9,822 metric tons in fiscal year 2026, the company’s sustainability report shows. And in 2025, the company reached a goal of matching 100 percent of its facilities’ electricity use with clean energy purchases.

这些来自供应链和产品使用的“间接”排放构成了英伟达气候影响的主要部分。相比之下，英伟达实际拥有和运营的设施的碳足迹看起来要小得多，因为英伟达负责设计芯片，而制造则由台积电（TSMC）等其他公司完成。英伟达的可持续发展报告显示，2026财年，英伟达运营的设施和车辆直接产生的温室气体排放量仅为9822公吨。此外，该公司在2025年实现了通过购买清洁能源来抵消其设施100%电力消耗的目标。

Greenpeace wants Nvidia to extend that goal to its entire manufacturing supply chain. When Greenpeace activists disrupted Huang’s speech at Nvidia GTC in Berlin last month, he responded onstage by saying, “I’ll talk to you guys later … All important issues, but let me get through my keynote.” In the Thursday press release, Greenpeace says Nvidia has yet to meet with Greenpeace despite the organization’s attempts to “seek dialogue” with the company over the past two years.

绿色和平组织希望英伟达将这一目标扩展到其整个制造供应链。上个月，当绿色和平组织的活动人士在柏林英伟达GTC大会上打断黄仁勋的演讲时，他在台上回应道：“我会稍后再和你们谈……这些都是重要议题，但请让我先讲完我的主题演讲。”在周四的新闻稿中，绿色和平组织表示，尽管该组织在过去两年中多次尝试与英伟达“寻求对话”，但英伟达至今仍未与他们会面。

“One of Huang’s defining legacies is inflating a speculative AI bubble, pouring billions of dollars into infrastructure with little regard for the real-world consequences inflicted on local communities and the climate,” Katrin Wu, Big Tech campaigner at Greenpeace International, said in an email to The Verge. “Huang may be gone, but the ‘tech colonialism’ Nvidia perpetuates continues.”

国际绿色和平组织大型科技行业活动家卡特琳·吴（Katrin Wu）在给《The Verge》的一封电子邮件中表示：“黄仁勋留下的标志性遗产之一就是吹大了一个投机性的人工智能泡沫，投入数十亿美元建设基础设施，却几乎不顾及对当地社区和气候造成的现实后果。黄仁勋可能已经离职，但英伟达所延续的‘科技殖民主义’仍在继续。”

Beyond Nvidia’s day-to-day operations, Huang threw his weight around as the CEO of one of the world’s most valuable companies — chumming it up with President Donald Trump and lobbying for the US to allow sales of its powerful chips to China.

除了英伟达的日常运营外，黄仁勋作为全球最有价值公司之一的首席执行官，在政界也颇具影响力——他与唐纳德·特朗普总统交好，并游说美国政府允许其向中国销售高性能芯片。
