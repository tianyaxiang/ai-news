# This Startup Is Betting on AI Microdramas to Get More Black Stories in Hollywood

A zombie virus has overtaken South Los Angeles. Authorities, hoping to contain the outbreak, have decided to barricade the neighborhood, leaving residents to make do on their own. So begins *They Left Us to Die*, a new microdrama from media company Mansa that runs 30 episodes and is entirely AI-generated.

一种僵尸病毒席卷了南洛杉矶。当局为了控制疫情，决定封锁该社区，任由居民自生自灭。这就是媒体公司 Mansa 推出的全新微短剧《他们留我们等死》（*They Left Us to Die*）的开端，该剧共 30 集，完全由人工智能生成。

“The plague was made here, made on us, on purpose,” says Imani Cross, an emergency room nurse desperate for a cure to help her brother DJ, who’s been infected.

“这场瘟疫是人为制造的，是专门针对我们制造的，”急诊室护士伊曼妮·克罗斯（Imani Cross）说道。她正拼命寻找治愈方法，以救助她那已被感染的弟弟 DJ。

On Instagram, the trailer drew mostly favorable reaction when it was released on October 29. A cascade of fire emoji filled the comments section as anticipation mounted. But the following day, as the first episode was set to go live at midnight, new tensions began to stir. Why had Mansa created a show with zero human roles? Weren’t actors David Oyelowo and Nate Parker, the company’s founders, about uplifting artists of color, not erasing them?

10 月 29 日，该剧预告片在 Instagram 上发布后，反响大多正面。随着期待值上升，评论区被一连串的火焰表情包刷屏。然而次日，当第一集定于午夜上线时，新的矛盾开始酝酿。为什么 Mansa 要制作一部完全没有人类演员参与的剧集？该公司的创始人、演员大卫·奥耶罗沃（David Oyelowo）和内特·帕克（Nate Parker）难道不是为了提升有色人种艺术家的地位，而不是抹杀他们吗？

“Mansa was one of the first vertical apps that was supposed to support and cultivate black talent, but here they are … another AI sellout,” said one user on Threads. “Why would any self-respecting casting director, producer, or director ever consider hiring Oleyowo again after this?” posted another. Actress Yvette Nicole Brown responded with a single note of disapproval: “*sigh* Mmkay,” she wrote, adding a gif of Viola Davis expressing irritation.

“Mansa 本应是首批支持和培养黑人人才的垂直应用之一，但看看他们现在……又一个向 AI 妥协的叛徒，”一位 Threads 用户写道。另一位用户发帖称：“在发生这种事之后，任何有自尊的选角导演、制片人或导演怎么还会考虑聘用奥耶罗沃？”女演员伊薇特·尼科尔·布朗（Yvette Nicole Brown）则表达了不满：“*叹气* 行吧，”她写道，并配上了一张维奥拉·戴维斯（Viola Davis）表示恼火的动图。

Parker wasn’t surprised by the backlash. “When we hear the words AI, it immediately drives us to the realities of what we are facing and the real lives it’s impacting,” Parker tells WIRED.

帕克对这种强烈抵制并不感到意外。“当我们听到‘人工智能’这个词时，它立刻让我们联想到我们所面临的现实，以及它对真实生活产生的影响，”帕克告诉《连线》（WIRED）杂志。

The problem is that while Hollywood is desperate for more expansive Black stories, not everyone agrees on the best way to make them happen.

问题在于，尽管好莱坞迫切需要更多元化的黑人故事，但并非所有人都认同实现这一目标的最佳方式。

As part of Mansa’s development strategy, the company says it is using AI as a chess piece rather than a replacement for human-centered content. The power comes in leveraging a show like *They Left Us to Die* as a way to bring more awareness to what Black audiences will support. He describes the approach as “holding the line on our very existence” in an industry that’s consolidating, and being able to “use all the tools that we have at our disposal to make sure we can staff up around shows that get greenlit.”

作为 Mansa 发展战略的一部分，该公司表示，他们将人工智能视为一颗棋子，而非以人为本内容的替代品。其核心在于利用像《他们留我们等死》这样的剧集，来提高人们对黑人观众支持内容的认知。他将这种方法描述为在行业整合的大背景下“坚守我们的生存底线”，并能够“利用我们所掌握的一切工具，确保我们能为获得批准的项目配备人员”。

In the shrinking Hollywood of today, a series about a Black ER nurse fighting off a zombie virus unleashed on her neighborhood by white people would likely struggle to find the backing of a major studio without first having proved its value 10 times over. Mansa’s strategy is to test the appetite of audiences with its AI shows. Parker believes this will demonstrate to studios that there is a demand for those kinds of stories before committing to a live production.

在当今不断萎缩的好莱坞，一部关于黑人急诊室护士对抗白人向其社区释放僵尸病毒的剧集，如果不能先证明其价值十倍以上，恐怕很难获得大型制片厂的支持。Mansa 的策略是通过 AI 剧集来测试观众的胃口。帕克认为，这将向制片厂证明此类故事存在市场需求，从而促使他们投入真人制作。

Parker says studio heads are even more reluctant in this climate. But with microdramas, “you get to test a piece of content, not just test it in a way of proof of concept like a short film, but you get to monetize it,” he says. “You get to track data around engagement and see if this is something the audience wants.”

帕克表示，在当前环境下，制片厂负责人更加谨慎。但他认为，通过微短剧，“你不仅可以像短片那样进行概念验证，还可以将其商业化。你可以追踪参与度数据，看看这是否是观众想要的内容。”

As attention spans shrink, microdramas have sprung up as a preferred portal of entertainment for both studios and young people. They’re vertically shot, minute-long soaps that are produced at a fraction of the cost of a TV show and mirror back the kind of frenzied consumption that has come to define modern entertainment. As streamers like ReelShort and DramaBox dominate the industry, stories anchored around romance, billionaire fantasies, revenge, and supernatural sagas have become the genres driving a boom largely fueled by women viewers.

随着注意力持续时间的缩短，微短剧已成为制片厂和年轻人青睐的娱乐入口。这些竖屏拍摄、每集一分钟的肥皂剧制作成本仅为电视剧的一小部分，反映了现代娱乐那种狂热的消费模式。随着 ReelShort 和 DramaBox 等流媒体平台主导行业，以浪漫、亿万富翁幻想、复仇和超自然传奇为核心的故事，已成为推动这一热潮的主要类型，而其受众很大程度上是女性观众。

An Omdia analysis of mobile usage in the fourth quarter of 2025 found that users spent an average of 10 more minutes per day on ReelShort (36 minutes) compared to Prime Video (26 minutes) and Netflix (20 minutes), with the firm predicting that the microdrama market will reach $14 billion in 2027.

Omdia 对 2025 年第四季度移动使用情况的分析发现，用户每天在 ReelShort 上花费的时间（36 分钟）比 Prime Video（26 分钟）和 Netflix（20 分钟）平均多出 10 分钟。该公司预测，微短剧市场规模将在 2027 年达到 140 亿美元。

Hoorae Media, Issa Rae’s production company, expanded into the sector this year with two microdramas, including *Screen Time*, a couples thriller comedy that, as of October, had over 580 million views across TikTok and its in-house microdrama app, PineDrama. There are also upstarts Juccee Shorts and Urban Operas, which is launching with six series later this month, that are angling to offer more diverse stories for audiences.

伊萨·雷（Issa Rae）旗下的制作公司 Hoorae Media 今年也进军该领域，推出了两部微短剧，其中包括情侣惊悚喜剧《屏幕时间》（*Screen Time*）。截至 10 月，该剧在 TikTok 及其内部微短剧应用 PineDrama 上的播放量已超过 5.8 亿次。此外，新兴平台 Juccee Shorts 和 Urban Operas（本月晚些时候将推出六部剧集）也正致力于为观众提供更多元化的故事。

Among Gen Z, watching short-form video is now more common than watching movies, accounting for 26 percent of weekly media time among young adults aged 18 to 24, according to a recent survey by the entertainment analytics firm Luminate.

根据娱乐分析公司 Luminate 最近的一项调查，在 Z 世代中，观看短视频已比看电影更为普遍，占 18 至 24 岁年轻人每周媒体使用时间的 26%。

Streamers are also investing more into the format and have even started to redesign their own user experience to keep pace with Gen Z’s twitchy consumption habits. Between April and August, Peacock, HBO Max, Prime Video, Paramount+, Netflix, and Disney+ all launched a short-form vertical feed on their respective mobile app.

流媒体平台也在加大对该形式的投入，甚至开始重新设计用户体验，以跟上 Z 世代那种快速切换的消费习惯。今年 4 月至 8 月间，Peacock、HBO Max、Prime Video、Paramount+、Netflix 和 Disney+ 都在各自的移动应用中推出了短视频竖屏信息流。

Maria Rua Aguete, global head of media and entertainment at Omdia, suspects that AI will change the economics of the industry. But more cheaply produced content doesn’t automatically mean more viewers. “Ultimately, audiences will decide what succeeds,” she says, adding that there is a “significant opportunity” to tap into overlooked demos.

Omdia 全球媒体与娱乐主管玛丽亚·鲁阿·阿格特（Maria Rua Aguete）认为，人工智能将改变行业的经济模式。但更低廉的制作成本并不自动意味着更多的观众。“最终，观众将决定什么会成功，”她说道，并补充称，挖掘被忽视的受众群体存在“重大机遇”。

The backlash in response to *They Left Us to Die* is a direct symptom of this rapid evolution. “What Nate is trying to do, no matter how practical and logical it is, he’s not reading the room and understanding that people don’t care about that. They just see technology as stealing their opportunities,” says J. Christopher Hamilton, an associate professor of communications at Syracuse and entertainment lawyer.

对《他们留我们等死》的抵制是这一快速演变的直接症状。“无论内特试图做的事情多么务实和合乎逻辑，他都没有看清形势，不明白人们并不关心这些。他们只把技术视为窃取他们机会的工具，”雪城大学传播学副教授兼娱乐律师 J. 克里斯托弗·汉密尔顿（J. Christopher Hamilton）表示。

The reality of Hollywood is dire. The $110 billion acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery by Paramount Skydance is projected to eliminate up to 4,500 film and television jobs in Los Angeles, according to the county’s Department of Economic Opportunity and Film Office. That reality can feel doubly crushing for Black artists and industry workers because they’re already vastly underrepresented.

好莱坞的现实十分严峻。据洛杉矶县经济机会与电影办公室称，派拉蒙天空之舞（Paramount Skydance）以 1100 亿美元收购华纳兄弟探索公司（Warner Bros. Discovery），预计将导致洛杉矶地区多达 4500 个影视岗位流失。对于黑人艺术家和行业从业者来说，这种现实可能显得加倍沉重，因为他们本已处于严重的代表性不足状态。
