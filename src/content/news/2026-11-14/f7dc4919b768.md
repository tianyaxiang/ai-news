The self-styled visionaries of Silicon Valley have built careers claiming to see the future. Yet five weeks before the midterms, many gathered at the White House to pose with Trump and endorse his “Super Intelligence” agenda, despite growing opposition to AI and data centers. Their decision to lash themselves to President Trump was a catastrophic misreading of the political landscape. Now, not only have they jeopardized their companies’ fates, but they’ve also raised questions about their vision and judgment. Here’s why Silicon Valley’s tech leaders may be less visionary than they think.

硅谷自诩的远见者们靠声称能预见未来建立了职业生涯。然而，在期中选举前五周，尽管公众对人工智能和数据中心的反对声日益高涨，许多人还是聚集在白宫与特朗普合影，并支持他的“超级智能”议程。他们选择与特朗普总统捆绑在一起，是对政治格局灾难性的误读。现在，他们不仅危及了各自公司的命运，还引发了人们对其远见和判断力的质疑。这就是为什么硅谷的科技领袖们可能并不像他们自认为的那样具有远见。

Nvidia privately confirmed it could track chips to fight smuggling, even as industry publicly questioned feasibility. Nvidia representatives told Illinois representative Bill Foster that it was possible to implement the anti-smuggling measures called for in the Chip Security Act, Foster told MIT Technology Review on Thursday, despite an industry group claiming they were “potentially infeasible.” Foster, a Democrat who serves on the Committee on Science, Space, and Technology, is a cosponsor of the act, which would introduce geotracking requirements to verify the location of advanced AI chips and prevent their smuggling to China. The act is drawing renewed attention following Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s resignation and reported affair. Find out what the proposed tracking measures would involve. —James O'Donnell

英伟达私下确认可以追踪芯片以打击走私，尽管行业公开质疑其可行性。伊利诺伊州众议员比尔·福斯特（Bill Foster）周四告诉《麻省理工科技评论》，英伟达代表曾向他表示，实施《芯片安全法案》所要求的反走私措施是可能的，尽管一个行业组织声称这些措施“可能不可行”。福斯特是众议院科学、空间和技术委员会的民主党成员，也是该法案的共同发起人。该法案将引入地理追踪要求，以核实先进人工智能芯片的位置并防止其被走私到中国。随着英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋的辞职及相关绯闻曝光，该法案再次受到关注。了解拟议的追踪措施将涉及哪些内容。——詹姆斯·奥唐奈（James O'Donnell）

More autonomy, fewer NDAs: Volunteers have new requests for brain implant trials. Scientists have been working on ways to decode brain activity for decades. Teams have developed brain implants that can control robotic limbs and computers, and even “speak.” The people who volunteer for trials of these devices play a vital role in that research, and they ought to be recognized for their contributions, says Ian Burkhart, who had one implanted in his own brain back in 2014. He’s been working on a set of guidelines for ethical trials to respect participants’ autonomy—and access their own brain data whenever they want. Read the full story on the push to give volunteers in brain implant trials more rights. —Jessica Hamzelou

更多的自主权，更少的保密协议：志愿者对脑机接口试验提出新要求。几十年来，科学家们一直致力于解码大脑活动的方法。研究团队已经开发出可以控制机械肢体、计算机甚至实现“说话”的脑部植入物。伊恩·伯克哈特（Ian Burkhart）于2014年在大脑中植入了此类设备，他表示，参与这些设备试验的志愿者在研究中发挥了至关重要的作用，他们的贡献应当得到认可。他一直在制定一套伦理试验指南，旨在尊重参与者的自主权，并让他们能够随时访问自己的大脑数据。阅读关于赋予脑机接口试验志愿者更多权利的完整报道。——杰西卡·汉泽洛（Jessica Hamzelou）
