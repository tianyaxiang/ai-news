Matthew Holman, a senior astrophysicist at the Smithsonian Astrophysical Observatory, was sifting through data from the Vera C. Rubin Observatory in Chile when something odd caught his eye. Running a custom-made computer program, he and his colleague Kevin Napier had already spotted more than 2,000 little bright dots in the telescope data, corresponding to newly discovered small objects in the distant solar system. But this one was different: It seemed to be big.

史密森尼天体物理天文台的资深天体物理学家马修·霍尔曼（Matthew Holman）在筛选智利薇拉·鲁宾天文台（Vera C. Rubin Observatory）的数据时，发现了一些异常情况。通过运行一个定制的计算机程序，他和同事凯文·纳皮尔（Kevin Napier）已经在望远镜数据中发现了2000多个小亮点，这些亮点对应着太阳系远端新发现的小型天体。但这个天体与众不同：它看起来很大。

Holman managed his expectations. After all, astronomers in the business of finding distant objects are used to disappointment. “We all have ‘found Mars’ … in terms of a false positive,” Holman tells Smithsonian magazine. They find a bright object that seems really big, then, with a closer look, learn that it’s nothing very remarkable after all. “We’re all really cautious when you see something that looks like it’s large.”

霍尔曼控制了自己的预期。毕竟，从事寻找遥远天体工作的天文学家们早已习惯了失望。“我们都曾有过‘发现火星’的经历……指的是假阳性，”霍尔曼告诉《史密森尼》杂志。他们发现一个看起来很大的明亮天体，但仔细观察后，却发现它并没有什么特别之处。“当我们看到看起来很大的东西时，我们都会非常谨慎。”

Still, this object grabbed Holman’s attention, so he tried to verify it with Napier and Rubin solar system lead scientist Mario Jurić. They found the celestial body in previous photos of the sky throughout the past decade, which helped them zero in on its orbital path—and the more historical data they found, the more confident they became.

尽管如此，这个天体还是引起了霍尔曼的注意，于是他尝试与纳皮尔以及鲁宾天文台太阳系首席科学家马里奥·尤里奇（Mario Jurić）共同核实。他们在过去十年拍摄的天空照片中找到了这个天体，这有助于他们锁定其轨道路径——找到的历史数据越多，他们就越有信心。

“We know that it’s real,” Holman says. “Every time you look at the predicted location, there it is.” They even found it in photographic plates from 1937, stored in the Harvard plate stacks, he says.

“我们确定它是真实存在的，”霍尔曼说。“每次你查看预测的位置，它就在那里。”他说，他们甚至在哈佛大学底片库中保存的1937年的照相底片中也发现了它。

The object, called 2026 ED138, is 46 times farther from the sun than Earth is, and it takes 281.1 years to complete an orbit. Now, the central question is whether it’s big enough to be classified as a new dwarf planet, which would put it among the rare ranks of only five other objects in our solar system.

这个被称为2026 ED138的天体距离太阳的距离是地球的46倍，绕太阳运行一周需要281.1年。现在，核心问题是它是否足够大，可以被归类为一颗新的矮行星，如果被确认，它将跻身于太阳系中仅有的另外五个同类天体的稀有行列。

“This is incredibly exciting,” Paul Byrne, a planetary scientist at the Washington University in St. Louis who was not involved in the discovery, tells National Geographic’s Robin George Andrews. “It heralds a new era of solar system and planetary science.”

“这令人无比兴奋，”未参与此次发现的圣路易斯华盛顿大学行星科学家保罗·伯恩（Paul Byrne）告诉《国家地理》的罗宾·乔治·安德鲁斯（Robin George Andrews）。“这预示着太阳系和行星科学进入了一个新时代。”

Dwarf planets are smaller planets that orbit a star and have a nearly round shape, which happens naturally with objects above a certain size, as their gravitational force pulls evenly on all sides. But dwarf planets are not large enough for their gravity to clear their orbital paths of similar-size debris—the eight planets of our solar system, meanwhile, have achieved that feat. (Pluto’s designation was changed from planet to dwarf planet in 2006 for this reason—its orbit is littered with icy Kuiper Belt objects.)

矮行星是绕恒星运行且形状接近球体的小型行星。当物体达到一定大小时，由于引力向四周均匀拉扯，这种形状会自然形成。但矮行星的引力不足以清除其轨道路径上大小相似的碎片——而我们太阳系的八大行星则做到了这一点。（冥王星在2006年被从行星降级为矮行星正是因为这个原因——它的轨道上布满了冰冷的柯伊伯带天体。）

Quick fact: Dynamic dwarf planet

快速事实：动态矮行星

ED138 is likely big enough to be round and, therefore, a dwarf planet—estimates for its size currently range from 370 miles to 930 miles across. But astronomers will have to confirm that measurement, and from the object’s brightness alone, that’s impossible. A highly reflective, smaller object, perhaps covered in gleaming ice caps, might appear just as bright as a less reflective, duller body that’s much larger.

ED138的大小很可能足以使其呈球形，因此被视为一颗矮行星——目前对其直径的估计范围在370英里到930英里之间。但天文学家必须确认这一测量结果，仅凭天体的亮度是无法做到的。一个反射率高、体积较小且可能覆盖着闪亮冰盖的天体，其亮度可能与一个反射率低、颜色暗淡但体积大得多的天体看起来一样。

Astronomers have two ways to get the required data. They could observe the world in infrared light, which reveals the thermal energy that it emits after being warmed by the sun. “From that you can figure out how big it is, because we know approximately what temperature it should have,” Holman says.

天文学家有两种方法获取所需数据。他们可以通过红外光观测该天体，红外光能揭示它在被太阳加热后发出的热能。“通过这一点，你可以算出它有多大，因为我们大致知道它应该具有的温度，”霍尔曼说。

Alternatively, astronomers could watch the dwarf planet candidate pass in front of a star. If they know how much light comes from that star and how fast ED138 is moving, they can calculate the body’s size based on how long it obscures the light.

或者，天文学家可以观察这颗矮行星候选者从一颗恒星前方经过的过程。如果他们知道该恒星发出的光量以及ED138的移动速度，就可以根据它遮挡光线的时间长短来计算该天体的大小。

Holman says people are working on making those observations with telescopes such as the Atacama Large Millimeter/submillimeter Array (ALMA). The dwarf planet candidate isn’t extremely easy to see at the moment, he adds, but “in the next few months it will be, and people will try.”

霍尔曼表示，人们正致力于利用阿塔卡马大型毫米波/亚毫米波阵列（ALMA）等望远镜进行这些观测。他补充说，这颗矮行星候选者目前并不容易观测到，但“在接下来的几个月里将会变得容易，人们会尝试去观测。”

Right now, astronomers recognize five dwarf planets: Pluto, Eris, Makemake and Haumea in the distant Kuiper Belt, and Ceres in the main asteroid belt between Mars and Jupiter. Experts have long suspected that there could be more of these objects, but many of them could have highly elliptical orbits that keep them too far away for us to see most of the time. What we observe is potentially the “tip of the iceberg of a few that are in the close part of their orbit,” Holman says. “There must be a lot that we just haven’t yet detected.”

目前，天文学家认可的矮行星有五颗：位于遥远柯伊伯带的冥王星、阋神星、鸟神星和妊神星，以及位于火星和木星之间主小行星带的谷神星。专家们长期以来一直怀疑可能还有更多此类天体，但其中许多天体可能拥有高度椭圆的轨道，使它们大部分时间都处于我们无法观测到的遥远距离。霍尔曼说，我们所观察到的可能只是“处于轨道近处的一小部分天体的冰山一角”。“肯定还有很多我们尚未探测到的天体。”

The discovery also hints at a relatively unexplored area of our solar system—the region that’s in front of the dense, starry core of the Milky Way from our perspective. It’s harder to pick out objects against all the background light in that zone, and ED138 has been traveling through it for the past 25 to 30 years, Holman notes. “There’s a swath on the sky where we really don’t have a good idea of what is there,” he adds.

这一发现还暗示了我们太阳系中一个相对未被探索的区域——从我们的视角来看，该区域位于银河系密集星核的前方。霍尔曼指出，在该区域，由于背景光的影响，很难辨认出天体，而ED138在过去25到30年里一直穿行其中。“在天空中有一片区域，我们确实不太清楚那里有什么，”他补充道。

What’s more, ED138 is in the southern hemisphere’s sky, and a lot of astronomical observations have focused on the northern hemisphere. But Rubin is poised to help change that: The new telescope, which has the world’s largest digital camera and captured its first images last year, is one of several observatories operating out of Chile’s Atacama Desert.

此外，ED138位于南半球的天空，而许多天文观测都集中在北半球。但鲁宾天文台有望改变这一现状：这座新望远镜拥有世界上最大的数码相机，并于去年拍摄了首批图像，它是智利阿塔卡马沙漠中运行的几座天文台之一。

“Rubin is giving us an unprecedented capacity to explore the sky through a combination of speed, depth and precision,” Jurić says in a statement.

“鲁宾天文台通过速度、深度和精度的结合，赋予了我们探索天空的前所未有的能力，”尤里奇在一份声明中表示。

“I was surprised that it found such an object so early,” Megan Schwamb, a planetary scientist at Queen’s University Belfast in Northern Ireland and a member of the Rubin solar system operations team, tells CNN’s Jacopo Prisco. “This was not on my bingo card for this year.”

“我很惊讶它这么早就发现了这样一个天体，”北爱尔兰贝尔法斯特女王大学的行星科学家、鲁宾太阳系运营团队成员梅根·施万布（Megan Schwamb）告诉CNN的雅各布·普里斯科（Jacopo Prisco）。“这不在我今年的预料之中。”

Understanding more about how and where ED138 formed could reveal new details about the early days of the solar system. And its existence offers a clear reminder that surprising objects remain to be found on the fringes of our planetary neighborhood.

深入了解ED138的形成方式和地点，可能会揭示关于太阳系早期阶段的新细节。它的存在也清楚地提醒我们，在我们的行星邻域边缘，仍有令人惊讶的天体等待被发现。

“Is there a Mars- or Earth-size or bigger object out there?” Holman says. “That’s not ruled out.”

“那里是否存在火星、地球大小或更大的天体？”霍尔曼说。“这并没有被排除。”
