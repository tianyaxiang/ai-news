A global outage taking down a wide range of Microsoft 365 services remains ongoing Friday morning. The outage started midday Thursday, impacting users of Microsoft email, cloud, and AI services. Microsoft has documented the full list of services impacted by the error, while moving early Friday morning to protect customers around the world from losing emails during the extended disruptions.

周五上午，一场导致大范围 Microsoft 365 服务中断的全球性故障仍在持续。此次故障始于周四中午，影响了微软电子邮件、云服务和人工智能服务的用户。微软已记录了受该错误影响的完整服务列表，并于周五清晨采取行动，以保护全球客户在长时间中断期间不会丢失电子邮件。

“Recovery actions are ongoing,” the Microsoft 365 Status account confirmed on X. “To help preserve delayed mail during this event, we’ve extended email timeout to 48 hours.” Similar to recent world-shaking outages of widely used tech like Amazon Web Services and CrowdStrike, the Microsoft disruption looks like “one of the biggest outages of all time,” Ciaran Martin, the former head of the UK’s National Cyber Security Centre, told NBC News. Possibly even “worse,” he suggested.

Microsoft 365 状态账号在 X 上确认：“恢复行动正在进行中。为了在此次事件期间保留延迟的邮件，我们将电子邮件超时时间延长至 48 小时。”英国国家网络安全中心前负责人西亚兰·马丁（Ciaran Martin）告诉美国全国广播公司（NBC），与亚马逊云科技（AWS）和 CrowdStrike 等近期震惊全球的广泛技术故障类似，此次微软中断看起来是“有史以来最大的故障之一”。他甚至暗示，情况可能“更糟”。

Businesses, hospitals, police departments, and schools were among those scrambling to work around the outage. In Japan, it disrupted government IT infrastructure, according to deputy chief cabinet secretary Masanao Ozaki. NBC’s report documented various harms, noting that “few public bodies were immune from the outage.” In some cases, doctors couldn’t send patients “important” updates on medication changes or follow-up care.

企业、医院、警察局和学校都在忙于应对此次故障带来的影响。据日本内阁官房副长官尾身朝幸（Masanao Ozaki）称，此次故障扰乱了日本的政府 IT 基础设施。NBC 的报道记录了各种损害，并指出“几乎没有公共机构能免受此次故障的影响”。在某些情况下，医生无法向患者发送关于药物变更或后续护理的“重要”更新。

The financial sector suffered, too, with people blocked from making bank transfers since they couldn’t receive verification codes. Justice systems were also hobbled. The Supreme Court in the Philippines saw its legal work derailed, NBC reported. And one US sheriff’s office in Colorado reported that the outage made it difficult for people to post bond for prisoners’ immediate release. It’s unclear how many users were affected, but Microsoft estimated in January that the total number of paid Microsoft 365 commercial subscriptions exceeded 450 million.

金融业也遭受了打击，人们因无法收到验证码而无法进行银行转账。司法系统也陷入瘫痪。据 NBC 报道，菲律宾最高法院的法律工作受到阻碍。美国科罗拉多州的一个治安官办公室报告称，此次故障导致人们难以缴纳保释金以让囚犯立即获释。目前尚不清楚有多少用户受到影响，但微软在 1 月份估计，Microsoft 365 付费商业订阅总数已超过 4.5 亿。

Microsoft blames failed server health checks. On a status page, Microsoft said “we have no evidence of malicious activity associated with this incident” and promised to “share a full Post-Incident Report with root cause and defense-in-depth actions after mitigation is complete.” “We’re aggressively pursuing all pathways to reduce impact,” Microsoft said on X. But Microsoft’s public statements make clear that the tech giant was blindsided by the outage and struggled for hours to find mitigations that could reliably restore user access.

微软将原因归咎于服务器健康检查失败。微软在一个状态页面上表示，“我们没有证据表明此次事件与恶意活动有关”，并承诺“在缓解措施完成后，分享一份包含根本原因和纵深防御措施的完整事后报告”。微软在 X 上表示：“我们正在积极寻求一切途径来减少影响。”但微软的公开声明清楚地表明，这家科技巨头对此次故障感到措手不及，并花费数小时寻找能够可靠恢复用户访问的缓解措施。

Shortly after detecting the outage, Microsoft confirmed that the main issue was “a portion of infrastructure” that “is experiencing elevated load across multiple regions.” This was not caused by increased traffic, Microsoft said, but “a problem that’s causing servers to fail automated health checks and become unavailable.” Once it became clear that the failed health checks were to blame, Microsoft said it took steps to prevent additional server outages while validating alternate health check methods.

在检测到故障后不久，微软确认主要问题是“部分基础设施”在“多个区域经历了负载升高”。微软表示，这并非由流量增加引起，而是“一个导致服务器无法通过自动健康检查并变得不可用的问题”。一旦明确健康检查失败是罪魁祸首，微软表示已采取措施防止更多服务器中断，同时验证替代的健康检查方法。

But four hours later, the company admitted that it was still in the weeds. “We’re opening all available pathways to restore service, as our actions taken thus far have not provided the anticipated relief,” Microsoft said. “We found a core process on affected servers is becoming overwhelmed, causing capacity loss. To validate a potential recovery, we’re isolating a portion of infrastructure, restarting affected components, and reintroducing traffic to confirm the services remain stable before applying the change more broadly.”

但四小时后，该公司承认仍深陷困境。微软表示：“我们正在开辟所有可用途径来恢复服务，因为我们迄今采取的行动尚未提供预期的缓解效果。我们发现受影响服务器上的一个核心进程不堪重负，导致容量损失。为了验证潜在的恢复情况，我们正在隔离部分基础设施，重启受影响的组件，并重新引入流量，以在更广泛地应用更改之前确认服务保持稳定。”

Even small wins seemed to turn into new setbacks. As Microsoft worked to resolve the issue, some of the servers it brought back online would “become unresponsive again once they receive traffic,” giving the company yet another problem to solve. Realizing that no quick solutions were coming, Microsoft moved to extend email timeout to 48 hours. As “encouraging” recovery efforts remained ongoing, Microsoft advised users that “some intermittent issues may continue” when traffic is restored.

即使是小小的胜利似乎也会变成新的挫折。当微软努力解决问题时，一些恢复在线的服务器在“接收流量后再次变得无响应”，这给公司带来了另一个需要解决的问题。意识到没有快速解决方案，微软决定将电子邮件超时时间延长至 48 小时。随着“令人鼓舞的”恢复工作持续进行，微软建议用户在流量恢复时“一些间歇性问题可能会持续存在”。

Microsoft’s most recent updates confirmed that it’s seeing “broader improvements in North America, sustained improvements across much of Europe, and encouraging recovery signals in Asia Pacific.” “Some users may still experience intermittent access issues or delayed email delivery as mitigation continues across remaining affected infrastructure,” Microsoft said. “Our focus remains on stabilizing recovered infrastructure, completing mitigation in remaining impacted areas, and reducing delayed email queues and backlogs while we validate service consistency across connection methods.”

微软最新的更新确认，它看到“北美地区有更广泛的改善，欧洲大部分地区有持续的改善，亚太地区有令人鼓舞的恢复信号”。微软表示：“随着缓解措施在剩余受影响的基础设施中继续进行，一些用户可能仍会遇到间歇性的访问问题或电子邮件延迟发送。我们的重点仍然是稳定已恢复的基础设施，完成剩余受影响区域的缓解工作，并在验证跨连接方式的服务一致性的同时，减少延迟的电子邮件队列和积压。”

DownDetector, which tracks user reports of service issues globally, showed that connectivity losses peaked Thursday afternoon and dropped off substantially by Friday morning. In comments, an Outlook user described the pain of monitoring email recovery, complaining that outgoing messages seem to send fine but incoming messages are received inconsistently. Some Copilot users seemed distressed that access wasn’t restored as quickly as Outlook. One Microsoft 365 user pondered, “How many offices had people with practically nothing to do? How much has this cost?”

追踪全球服务问题用户报告的 DownDetector 显示，连接中断在周四下午达到顶峰，并在周五上午大幅下降。在评论中，一位 Outlook 用户描述了监控电子邮件恢复的痛苦，抱怨发出的邮件似乎发送正常，但收到的邮件却断断续续。一些 Copilot 用户似乎对访问权限没有像 Outlook 那样迅速恢复感到苦恼。一位 Microsoft 365 用户思考道：“有多少办公室的人几乎无事可做？这造成了多大的损失？”
