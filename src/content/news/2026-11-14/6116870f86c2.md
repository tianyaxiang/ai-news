# FAA expands AI advice to manage air traffic congestion across entire US airspace

The US government has expanded the use of an AI tool for advising air traffic controllers to assist in managing air traffic across the entire national airspace system. Airline executives have voiced support for the rollout, but the US labor union representing air traffic controllers has said it was not involved in designing or testing the AI-driven system.

美国政府已扩大使用一种人工智能工具，旨在为航空交通管制员提供建议，以协助管理整个国家空域系统的空中交通。航空公司高管对此次推广表示支持，但代表航空交通管制员的美国工会表示，他们并未参与该人工智能驱动系统的设计或测试。

The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) says the $875 million SMART system has helped reroute 180 flights since being implemented in airspace over Washington, DC, on September 21. The agency claims that this saved an average of 10 minutes per flight and almost 80 gallons of fuel per flight—and it is apparently hoping such results can be replicated nationwide through the expanded deployment.

美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）表示，自 9 月 21 日在华盛顿特区上空实施以来，耗资 8.75 亿美元的 SMART 系统已帮助 180 个航班重新规划了航线。该机构声称，此举平均为每个航班节省了 10 分钟的飞行时间，并节省了近 80 加仑的燃油，显然希望通过此次扩大部署，能在全国范围内复制这些成果。

“After a successful initial rollout to the National Capital Region in September, the FAA will now use SMART in the airspace of all 22 centers across the country to reduce delays, reduce fuel consumption and improve the travel experience for the flying public,” according to the agency statement posted on November 10.

根据该机构 11 月 10 日发布的声明：“继 9 月在国家首都地区成功进行初步推广后，FAA 现在将在全国所有 22 个中心的空域使用 SMART 系统，以减少延误、降低燃油消耗并改善飞行公众的旅行体验。”

The FAA fact sheet describes SMART as a cloud-based system that combines information about “weather patterns, flight paths, traffic flow and controller staffing metrics” to predict air traffic patterns. It is designed to make scheduling recommendations, identify optimal flight routes, and determine the best order of arrivals for aircraft.

FAA 的情况说明书将 SMART 描述为一个基于云的系统，它结合了有关“天气模式、飞行路径、交通流量和管制员人员配置指标”的信息，以预测空中交通模式。该系统旨在提供调度建议、识别最佳飞行路线，并确定飞机的最佳到达顺序。

However, a human team of FAA specialists based in DC is ultimately responsible for validating the SMART system recommendations before passing them along to air traffic control facilities across the country, according to the agency. Furthermore, the AI-powered SMART system “cannot be used to replace the critical role air traffic controllers play or take over control of an aircraft,” the FAA clarified in the September 21 announcement about the initial deployment.

然而，据该机构称，位于华盛顿特区的一个 FAA 人类专家团队最终负责验证 SMART 系统的建议，然后再将其传递给全国各地的空中交通管制设施。此外，FAA 在 9 月 21 日关于初步部署的公告中明确表示，人工智能驱动的 SMART 系统“不能用于取代航空交通管制员的关键作用，也不能接管飞机的控制权”。

Local air traffic controllers can decide whether or not to accept the SMART system recommendations. The previous FAA announcement included supportive statements from Delta Air Lines CEO Ed Bastian and American Airlines CEO Robert Isom. But the National Air Traffic Controllers Association, which represents more than 15,000 members, also told Flying magazine that it was not consulted or involved in the “design, testing, or implementation of SMART and therefore cannot speak to its effect on the air traffic control workforce.”

当地航空交通管制员可以决定是否接受 SMART 系统的建议。FAA 此前的公告中包含了达美航空首席执行官埃德·巴斯蒂安（Ed Bastian）和美国航空首席执行官罗伯特·伊索姆（Robert Isom）的支持性声明。但代表超过 15,000 名成员的全国航空交通管制员协会也告诉《Flying》杂志，他们没有被咨询，也没有参与“SMART 的设计、测试或实施，因此无法评价其对航空交通管制人员的影响”。

Following the initial deployment in airspace above DC, the FAA expanded the SMART system’s coverage to air route traffic control centers in Atlanta along with Jacksonville and Miami in Florida, according to The Dallas Morning News. The FAA also shared an example with The Dallas Morning News about how SMART recommended a shorter and faster route for a cargo flight arriving at Dallas-Fort Worth International Airport, although the newspaper noted that the agency did not identify the flight involved and has not provided information about how SMART may have assisted passenger flight traffic.

据《达拉斯晨报》报道，继在华盛顿特区上空进行初步部署后，FAA 将 SMART 系统的覆盖范围扩大到了亚特兰大以及佛罗里达州杰克逊维尔和迈阿密的航路交通管制中心。FAA 还与《达拉斯晨报》分享了一个案例，说明 SMART 如何为一架抵达达拉斯-沃斯堡国际机场的货运航班推荐了更短、更快的航线，尽管该报指出，该机构并未指明涉及的航班，也没有提供关于 SMART 如何协助客运航班交通的信息。
