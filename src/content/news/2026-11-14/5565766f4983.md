# Microsoft 365 Crawls Back to Life After a 24-Hour Outage

Save this storySave this storySorry, Margaret, your email is working again. After a widespread outage shut down Microsoft 365 services for businesses, governments, and tens of millions of other users around the world, the essential cloud services are now (mostly) back online, according to the company’s status page.

保存这篇报道。抱歉，玛格丽特，你的电子邮件又能用了。据微软状态页面显示，在一次大规模宕机导致全球企业、政府和数千万其他用户的 Microsoft 365 服务中断后，这些核心云服务现已（基本）恢复在线。

The outage debacle began around lunchtime on the East Coast on Thursday, with users reporting Outlook outages to the service-tracking website Downdetector just after 12 pm ET. Within the hour, Microsoft said it was investigating reports of an outage that at the time was limited to North America. It quickly spread to impact more than 70 countries around the world.

此次宕机事故始于周四东海岸午餐时间，用户在美东时间中午 12 点刚过便向服务追踪网站 Downdetector 报告了 Outlook 故障。一小时内，微软表示正在调查当时仅限于北美地区的宕机报告。随后，故障迅速蔓延，影响了全球 70 多个国家。

The full cause of Microsoft’s unprecedented outage remains unknown, but the company repeatedly ruled out a cyberattack as the cause in statements to its status page. It is also ruling out AI or AI agents playing a role in the debacle.

微软此次史无前例的宕机事件的具体原因尚不清楚，但该公司在其状态页面的声明中多次排除了网络攻击的可能性。同时，微软也排除了人工智能或 AI 代理在事故中起作用的可能性。

“We are seeing broad recovery and stability across our services,” a Microsoft spokesperson says in a statement. “This was a technical issue not related to cyber or AI/agents. The incident was introduced by an issue during an earlier network capacity upgrade that surfaced following a recent network change. We have addressed the underlying issue and continue to restore service to remaining customers and regions.”

“我们看到各项服务正在全面恢复并趋于稳定，”微软发言人在一份声明中表示。“这是一个技术问题，与网络攻击或人工智能/代理无关。该事件是由早先一次网络容量升级期间出现的问题所引发，并在最近一次网络变更后显现出来。我们已经解决了根本问题，并继续为剩余的客户和地区恢复服务。”

The company has promised a “full post-incident report” after it has fully reviewed what went wrong.

该公司承诺在全面审查事故原因后，将发布一份“完整的事故后报告”。

As Outlook, Teams, Copilot, and other Microsoft 365 systems come back online, users will likely see their inboxes turn into a mess. “You're probably going to get a lot of notifications when things come back,” says Jonathan Leitschuh, an independent researcher.

随着 Outlook、Teams、Copilot 和其他 Microsoft 365 系统恢复在线，用户的收件箱可能会变得一团糟。“当系统恢复时，你可能会收到大量通知，”独立研究员乔纳森·莱奇舒（Jonathan Leitschuh）表示。

Microsoft said it had increased the queue time for emails to 48 hours in an attempt to ensure that every message that people tried to send while its systems were down gets delivered. Leitschuh points out that, even with the bulk of systems restored, users may receive “a flood of notifications a few hours down the pike” as subsystems come back online.

微软表示，已将电子邮件的排队时间延长至 48 小时，以确保人们在系统宕机期间尝试发送的每条信息都能送达。莱奇舒指出，即使大部分系统已经恢复，随着子系统陆续上线，用户在未来几个小时内仍可能收到“海量的通知”。

The total impact of the Microsoft outage may never be fully known, but it’s safe to say it’s already significant. Governments from Japan to the United States reported disruptions to a bevy of functions. Airlines reported problems with their ability to communicate with customers. Many law enforcement and emergency services ground to a halt. And hospitals reported widespread interruptions to their operations.

微软此次宕机的全部影响可能永远无法完全知晓，但可以肯定的是，其影响已经非常巨大。从日本到美国的各国政府均报告了多项职能的中断。航空公司报告称其与客户沟通的能力受到影响。许多执法和应急服务陷入瘫痪。医院也报告了其运营受到大范围干扰。

Ultimately, the episode serves as a reminder of the risks involved when the whole world relies on a single company for a critical service. “We're very interdependent on these systems in modern life,” says Leitschuh. Next time, delayed emails may be the least of our worries.

归根结底，这一事件提醒人们，当全世界都依赖单一公司提供关键服务时，所涉及的风险不容忽视。“在现代生活中，我们对这些系统有着极高的相互依赖性，”莱奇舒说。下一次，电子邮件延迟可能只是我们最不需要担心的问题了。
