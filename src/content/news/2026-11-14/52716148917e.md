ZigZag Visit zigzag.diy ZigZag is a visual embroidery editor built in Rust for performance, with built-in MCP (Model Context Protocol) support so AI agents can create and edit designs alongside you. Turn SVG artwork into stitches, fine-tune it in the editor, and export PES, DST, EXP, JEF, or VP3 for broad compatibility with embroidery machines—or send designs directly to a Brother Skitch (PP1) over Bluetooth.

Stitch types: Fill (tatami) - Staggered rows, any angle, with underlay (edge walk + rows) and pull compensation. Satin - Zigzag column between two rails, with center/contour/zigzag underlay, pull compensation and short stitches on tight curves; plain, S-stitch or zig-zag top stitching; stitches square across or leaning (lean=8); several threads across the column (multicolor satin). Strokes - (any width); Ink/Stitch-style two-rail paths with rungs; filled shapes: rings use inner/outer outline, bars and crescents split at their tips, letters and other shapes that branch or have a counter are cut into columns where the branches part (a P into its stem and bowl, a T into bar and stem), or border follows the outline. Run - Running stitch, single or triple (bean). Contour - Rows that follow the outline inward, or one spiral out from the center (Ink/Stitch's circular fill). Blanket - E-stitch: a line with prongs into the shape. Appliqué - Placement line → stop → tack-down → stop → satin cover. Manual - Every path point is a needle point. Meander - One wandering line through the shape, quilting style: light and soft. Ripple - Open running-stitch rows following the outline, or one spiral. Cross-stitch - A grid of X's, sewn as rows of half-crosses out and back. Tartan - Woven stripes in several threads (a mirrored sett you edit in the panel), each thread its own color block.

针迹类型：填充（榻榻米）- 交错行，任意角度，带有底线（边缘走线 + 行）和拉伸补偿。缎纹 - 两条轨道之间的之字形列，带有中心/轮廓/之字形底线，拉伸补偿和紧密曲线上的短针迹；平针、S 型针迹或之字形顶针；针迹横向方形或倾斜（lean=8）；列间多根线（多色缎纹）。描边 - （任意宽度）；Ink/Stitch 风格的两轨路径及横档；填充形状：环使用内外轮廓，条形和新月形在尖端处分割，字母和其他有分支或有孔的形状被切割成列（P 分割为茎和碗，T 分割为横杠和茎），或者边界跟随轮廓。运行 - 运行针迹，单线或三线（豆针）。轮廓 - 沿轮廓向内排列的行，或从中心向外螺旋（Ink/Stitch 的圆形填充）。毯边 - E 型针迹：带有刺入形状的叉齿的线。贴布绣 - 放置线 → 停止 → 固定 → 停止 → 缎纹覆盖。手动 - 每个路径点都是一个针点。蜿蜒 - 穿过形状的一条游走线，绗缝风格：轻盈柔软。波纹 - 沿轮廓的开放式运行针迹行，或单螺旋。十字绣 - X 网格，缝制为半十字绣的往返行。格子 - 多种线编织的条纹（您在面板中编辑的镜像设置），每根线都有自己的色块。

Satins wider than 7 mm are auto-split (shorter, staggered stitches) and use auto spacing (narrow columns more open, wide ones denser). Planner features: Remove overlaps: fill hidden under later objects isn't sewn (keeps 0.5 mm of overlap). Cap order: center-out, bottom-up, grouped by color, still respecting layering. Routing: travel stitches stay out of sight: straight across where a straight line stays inside the fill, otherwise round the inside of its outline (Ink/Stitch's underpath), otherwise any way through the fill (round its holes, through its necks), under a satin that's about to be sewn, or under whatever is sewn later on top. An object's last piece is sewn toward the end the next object can be reached from. So satin lettering sewn stroke by stroke needs cuts only between letters when each stroke is followed by one that covers the way to it.

宽度超过 7 毫米的缎纹会自动分割（更短、交错的针迹）并使用自动间距（窄列更稀疏，宽列更密集）。规划器功能：移除重叠：隐藏在后续对象下的填充物不会被缝制（保留 0.5 毫米的重叠）。封盖顺序：从中心向外、从下向上、按颜色分组，同时尊重分层。路由：旅行针迹保持不可见：在直线保持在填充物内部的地方直接穿过，否则绕过其轮廓内部（Ink/Stitch 的底路径），否则通过填充物的任何方式（绕过其孔洞，穿过其颈部），在即将缝制的缎纹下方，或在稍后缝制在顶部的任何东西下方。对象的最后一部分被缝制到可以到达下一个对象的末端。因此，逐笔缝制的缎纹字母仅在字母之间需要剪断，前提是每一笔后面都跟着一个覆盖其路径的笔画。
