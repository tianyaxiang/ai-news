# The smallest version of Camp Snap’s retro digital camcorder fits on your keychain

Camp Snap 推出的复古数码摄像机最小版本现已可以挂在钥匙扣上。

The Camp Snap CS-8 Micro is just three inches long and adds a tiny LCD screen that’s only used as a viewfinder.

Camp Snap CS-8 Micro 的长度仅为三英寸，并增加了一个仅用作取景器的小型 LCD 屏幕。

Last week, Camp Snap launched a miniaturized version of its screen-free CS-8 video camera that was half the size of the original with a smaller grip designed to be easier for kids to hold. But it turns out the company wasn’t done shrinking its hardware. Camp Snap’s new CS-8 Micro is even smaller than the CS-8 Mini. It’s around three inches long and weighs just 1.5 ounces so you can hang it from your keys and, like its larger predecessors, it can capture video using one of five built-in filters.

上周，Camp Snap 推出了其无屏 CS-8 摄像机的微缩版本，其尺寸仅为原版的一半，并配有更小的握把，旨在方便儿童持握。但事实证明，该公司并未停止缩小其硬件的步伐。全新的 Camp Snap CS-8 Micro 比 CS-8 Mini 还要小。它的长度约为三英寸，重量仅为 1.5 盎司，因此你可以将其挂在钥匙上；与之前的较大机型一样，它可以使用五种内置滤镜中的一种来拍摄视频。

The CS-8 Micro will be available starting on November 17th at 11AM ET through Camp Snap’s online store, Target, and Urban Outfitters for $39.99, and similar to the collectible Kodak Charmera, the Micro is being sold in sealed mystery boxes. Colors like hot pink, lilac, lime, and orange will be easier to find, while vintage classic (black) and glow in the dark will be much rarer.

CS-8 Micro 将于美国东部时间 11 月 17 日上午 11 点起通过 Camp Snap 在线商店、Target 和 Urban Outfitters 发售，售价为 39.99 美元。与具有收藏价值的柯达 Charmera 类似，Micro 将以密封的盲盒形式出售。亮粉色、丁香紫、青柠色和橙色等颜色较容易买到，而复古经典黑和夜光版则更为稀有。

Unlike the original CS-8 and CS-8 Mini, the new Micro does include an LCD display, but it’s only used as a viewfinder. You can’t watch, edit, or delete anything you’ve captured on the camera itself. You need to transfer the videos to a phone or computer using a USB-C cable to enjoy them, or move its included 4GB microSD into a card reader.

与最初的 CS-8 和 CS-8 Mini 不同，新款 Micro 确实包含一个 LCD 显示屏，但它仅用作取景器。你无法在摄像机本身上观看、编辑或删除所拍摄的任何内容。你需要使用 USB-C 线将视频传输到手机或电脑上才能观看，或者将随附的 4GB microSD 卡放入读卡器中。

As Camp Snap has been progressively shrinking its retro camcorder, it’s also reduced its resolution. The original captured videos at 4K, while the CS-8 Mini did 2.7K. The CS-8 Micro is limited to 720p, but carries forward features like an 8x digital zoom and a selection of five filters that include vintage effects that add flicker and grain to emulate the look of 8mm film. Camp Snap estimates the Micro’s 4GB microSD card can hold 45 to 60 minutes of video, while its battery life is estimated to be 30 to 45 minutes on a full charge.

随着 Camp Snap 不断缩小其复古摄像机的尺寸，其分辨率也有所降低。原版机型支持 4K 视频拍摄，而 CS-8 Mini 支持 2.7K。CS-8 Micro 则限制在 720p，但保留了 8 倍数码变焦和五种滤镜选择等功能，其中包括能增加闪烁和颗粒感以模拟 8 毫米胶片效果的复古滤镜。Camp Snap 估计 Micro 的 4GB microSD 卡可存储 45 到 60 分钟的视频，而其电池在充满电后的续航时间预计为 30 到 45 分钟。
