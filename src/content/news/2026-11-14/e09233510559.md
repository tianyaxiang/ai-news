GLP-1 medications are everywhere these days. The peptide-based drugs are both highly effective and wildly popular for diabetes, weight loss and a growing range of connected chronic health conditions, including cardiovascular disease and obstructive sleep apnea.

如今，GLP-1 药物随处可见。这些基于肽的药物在治疗糖尿病、减肥以及包括心血管疾病和阻塞性睡眠呼吸暂停在内的一系列相关慢性健康问题方面，既高效又广受欢迎。

But, amid the boom, the latest data suggests millions of Americans have turned to unapproved versions and are using the potent drugs in ways doctors never intended, raising questions about what that real-world use means for safety and efficacy.

然而，在这一热潮中，最新数据表明，数百万美国人已转向使用未经批准的版本，并以医生未曾预料的方式使用这些强效药物，这引发了人们对这种现实使用方式对安全性和有效性意味着什么的质疑。

In a study published this week, researchers at the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention broke down survey data of GLP-1 use collected between July and August of 2025. Based on responses from over 8,000 US adults, the researchers estimate that about one in 10 US adults (9.8 percent) used a glucagon-like peptide-1 receptor agonist, aka GLP-1 medication, in 2025. That’s around 27 million adults. The study was published in the CDC’s Morbidity and Mortality Weekly Report.

在本周发表的一项研究中，美国疾病控制与预防中心（CDC）的研究人员分析了 2025 年 7 月至 8 月期间收集的 GLP-1 使用调查数据。根据 8000 多名美国成年人的反馈，研究人员估计，2025 年约有十分之一（9.8%）的美国成年人使用了胰高血糖素样肽-1 受体激动剂，即 GLP-1 药物。这相当于约 2700 万成年人。该研究发表在 CDC 的《发病率与死亡率周报》上。

Among GLP-1 users, nearly half (44 percent) reported using their GLP-1 in a way that was different from how it was prescribed. Users skipped doses, delayed getting refills, and used smaller doses than prescribed, among other changes. More than half of the people modifying how they used the drug said the changes were the result of cost or availability reasons.

在 GLP-1 使用者中，近一半（44%）的人表示他们使用 GLP-1 的方式与处方规定不同。使用者会跳过剂量、推迟续药，或使用比处方更小的剂量等。超过一半改变用药方式的人表示，这些改变是出于成本或药物可获得性的原因。

Cost and access barriers were most pronounced among younger GLP-1 users. Fifty-eight percent of adults 14–39 years old who modified their use cited cost as a barrier, compared with just 20 percent of adults 65 or older who said the same.

成本和获取障碍在年轻的 GLP-1 使用者中最为明显。在 14 至 39 岁改变用药方式的成年人中，有 58% 的人将成本视为障碍，而 65 岁及以上的成年人中只有 20% 的人持相同看法。

Modified use of GLP-1 medications was particularly common among those using compounded versions of GLP-1 medications. Among all GLP-1 users, at least 23 percent used a compounded version; an additional 9 percent said they didn’t know whether their GLP-1 was compounded or not.

在那些使用复方（compounded）GLP-1 药物的人群中，改变用药方式的情况尤为普遍。在所有 GLP-1 使用者中，至少有 23% 的人使用了复方版本；另有 9% 的人表示不知道自己使用的 GLP-1 是否为复方药物。

Potential risks: These versions of GLP-1 drugs are made by compounding pharmacies and are intended to mimic a medication approved by the Food and Drug Administration, such as Wegovy or Ozempic. But compounded drugs themselves are not approved by the FDA; the regulatory agency does not review their safety, effectiveness, or quality before they’re sold.

潜在风险：这些版本的 GLP-1 药物由配药药房制造，旨在模仿食品药品监督管理局（FDA）批准的药物，如 Wegovy 或 Ozempic。但复方药物本身并未获得 FDA 批准；该监管机构在药物销售前不会审查其安全性、有效性或质量。

While compounded drugs are considered more accessible and affordable, they generally have higher rates of preparation errors, contamination, and other manufacturing problems compared to non-compounded drugs. Among people who used these unapproved, potentially riskier GLP-1s, 62 percent were not taking them as prescribed. That’s compared to just 36 percent of GLP-1 users who took brand-name drugs.

虽然复方药物被认为更易获得且价格更实惠，但与非复方药物相比，它们通常具有更高的制备错误、污染和其他制造问题的发生率。在使用这些未经批准、潜在风险更高的 GLP-1 药物的人群中，62% 的人未按处方服用。相比之下，服用品牌药物的 GLP-1 使用者中这一比例仅为 36%。

The potential risks of taking unapproved GLP-1 drugs at all or taking them in unapproved ways may be missed by users, the CDC researchers note. A 2025 study assessing websites selling compounded GLP-1 medications found that nearly 40 percent of sites stated or implied the drugs were FDA-approved, making it harder for consumers to differentiate the compounded versions from FDA-approved ones.

CDC 研究人员指出，用户可能会忽略服用未经批准的 GLP-1 药物或以未经批准的方式服用这些药物的潜在风险。2025 年一项评估销售复方 GLP-1 药物网站的研究发现，近 40% 的网站声称或暗示这些药物已获得 FDA 批准，这使得消费者更难区分复方版本和 FDA 批准的版本。

Further, nearly 80 percent of sites used brand names in their advertising, which could further mislead consumers. The researchers noted that using brand names violated FDA regulatory standards.

此外，近 80% 的网站在广告中使用了品牌名称，这可能会进一步误导消费者。研究人员指出，使用品牌名称违反了 FDA 的监管标准。

“Given the substantial use of compounded GLP-1 medications, understanding the safety and quality of compounded GLP-1 medications is an important public health consideration,” the authors conclude. They also call for more research on the common modifications people are using, such as dose and frequency adjustments, saying the data on the safety and effectiveness of these real-world practices “could help guide the development and implementation of clinical guidelines and support appropriate medication use.”

作者总结道：“鉴于复方 GLP-1 药物的大量使用，了解其安全性和质量是一个重要的公共卫生考量。”他们还呼吁对人们常用的修改方式（如剂量和频率调整）进行更多研究，并表示关于这些现实实践的安全性和有效性的数据“有助于指导临床指南的制定和实施，并支持适当的药物使用。”
