As of this morning, Microsoft is still scrambling to restore service following yesterday’s global outage, with millions of users unable to access Outlook and other vital tools. It’s not the end of the world, but it does seem fitting that this should happen on the day a physicist predicted would be the end of humanity: November 13, 2026. And it falls on a Friday, too.

截至今天早上，微软仍在努力恢复因昨天全球性中断而瘫痪的服务，数百万用户无法访问 Outlook 及其他重要工具。这虽然不是世界末日，但巧合的是，这恰好发生在一位物理学家预言人类将走向终结的日子：2026 年 11 月 13 日。而且那天正好还是个星期五。

The physicist in question is Heinz von Foerster, who published a 1960 paper in the journal Science predicting that by today, overpopulation would reach the breaking point. It’s not a new fear. English economist Thomas Malthus—one of the early pioneers in modeling population dynamics—made a similar prediction in 1798, claiming that a rapidly growing population would eventually exhaust the available food supply, and humanity would starve to death.

这位物理学家是海因茨·冯·福斯特（Heinz von Foerster），他在 1960 年发表于《科学》杂志的一篇论文中预言，到今天，人口过剩将达到临界点。这种担忧并非新鲜事。英国经济学家托马斯·马尔萨斯（Thomas Malthus）——人口动态建模的早期先驱之一——曾在 1798 年做出过类似的预测，声称人口的快速增长最终会耗尽现有的食物供应，导致人类饿死。

His model rested on the assumption of exponential growth, but the equation is only applicable under specific conditions, such as scientists growing bacteria in a lab in a perfectly controlled environment. Even then, while growth occurs exponentially for a time, it does not continue forever. Population dynamics are notoriously tricky to calculate because there are so many variables to consider and so much unpredictability in the system.

他的模型基于指数增长的假设，但该方程仅适用于特定条件，例如科学家在实验室的完美受控环境中培养细菌。即便如此，虽然增长在一段时间内呈指数级，但它不会永远持续下去。人口动态的计算极其复杂，因为需要考虑的变量太多，且系统存在极大的不可预测性。

In his 1960 analysis, Von Foerster accurately measured past trends in population growth, but like Malthus, he assumed those trends would carry into the future largely unchanged. So he projected that human population growth would march inexorably toward infinity. We wouldn’t starve to death; von Foerster thought we would be ingenious enough to solve the food supply problem. Instead, “they will be squeezed to death,” he wrote.

在 1960 年的分析中，冯·福斯特准确地衡量了过去的人口增长趋势，但像马尔萨斯一样，他假设这些趋势在未来将基本保持不变。因此，他推测人类人口增长将不可阻挡地趋向无穷大。我们不会饿死；冯·福斯特认为我们足够聪明，能够解决食物供应问题。相反，他写道：“他们会被挤死。”

Plenty of scholars objected at the time, dismissing von Foerster’s conclusion as “ridiculous” and “manifestly absurd.” The global population did not keep accelerating at an exponential rate; the baby boom petered out just a few years after von Foerster published his paper. “He got the picture right just as that picture ceased to be right,” mathematical biologist Joel Cohen of Rockefeller University and Columbia University recently told Popular Science. “The all-time peak happened in the early 1960s.”

当时许多学者表示反对，认为冯·福斯特的结论“荒谬”且“明显不合理”。全球人口并没有继续以指数速度增长；在冯·福斯特发表论文几年后，婴儿潮就逐渐平息了。“就在那幅图景不再正确的时候，他却把它描绘对了，”洛克菲勒大学和哥伦比亚大学的数学生物学家乔尔·科恩（Joel Cohen）最近告诉《大众科学》杂志。“历史最高峰出现在 20 世纪 60 年代初。”

Nor did von Foerster foresee the impacts of longer life expectancy and the sharp decline in child mortality rates. We’re always doomed. Nevertheless, the paper was highly cited and received a fair amount of media coverage at the time. As Alan MacEachern, a historian at Western University, pointed out at The Conversation: The doomsday idea persisted in part because its predictions for future populations turned out to be highly accurate—at least for a while.

冯·福斯特也没有预见到预期寿命延长和儿童死亡率急剧下降的影响。我们总是注定要面临末日。尽管如此，这篇论文在当时被广泛引用，并获得了相当多的媒体报道。正如西安大略大学的历史学家艾伦·麦克伊琴（Alan MacEachern）在《对话》杂志上指出的那样：末日论之所以能持续存在，部分原因在于它对未来人口的预测在一段时间内确实非常准确。

Its core equation yielded projections that were better than those of most demographers throughout the 1970s and 1980s. Only around 1993 did that accuracy begin to falter and the global population began falling behind the forecasts—because the world began to do what von Foerster and his colleagues recommended it do. Today, the global birth rate is slightly less than half the 1960 rate.

其核心方程得出的预测在整个 20 世纪 70 年代和 80 年代都优于大多数人口统计学家的预测。直到 1993 年左右，这种准确性才开始动摇，全球人口开始落后于预测值——因为世界开始采取冯·福斯特及其同事所建议的措施。今天，全球出生率略低于 1960 年水平的一半。

Doomsday predictions are nothing new; they date at least back to the first century CE, when an ascetic sect interpreted the Jewish uprising against the Romans in 66–70 as a harbinger of the coming Messiah. The Christian religion has produced a ton of incorrect predictions of the end times; among those making them was Martin Luther, who believed the world would end no later than 1600.

末日预言并不新鲜；它们至少可以追溯到公元一世纪，当时一个苦行教派将公元 66-70 年犹太人反抗罗马人的起义解读为弥赛亚降临的先兆。基督教产生过大量关于世界末日的错误预言；其中就包括马丁·路德（Martin Luther），他认为世界末日不晚于 1600 年到来。

Puritan minister Cotton Mather kept updating his predictions, first calling for the end in 1697, then 1716, then 1736. (He would probably have kept updating them had he not died in 1728.) My personal favorite is Mary Bateman, an Englishwoman in Leeds who claimed in 1806 that her hen was laying eggs inscribed with the words “Christ is coming.” It was a hoax: Bateman had actually used a corrosive ink to etch the eggs before putting them back inside the hens.

清教徒牧师科顿·马瑟（Cotton Mather）不断更新他的预言，先是预言 1697 年是末日，然后是 1716 年，接着是 1736 年。（如果他没有在 1728 年去世，他可能会继续更新这些预言。）我个人最喜欢的是玛丽·贝特曼（Mary Bateman），这位利兹的英国女性在 1806 年声称她的母鸡下的蛋上刻有“基督降临”的字样。这是一个骗局：贝特曼实际上是在将鸡蛋放回母鸡体内之前，用腐蚀性墨水刻上去的。

Astrologers have come up with their fair share of doomsday predictions, usually involving rare planetary alignments and comets, but scientists have not been immune. Jacob Bernoulli thought the Earth would be destroyed by a comet on April 5, 1719, for example. And in 1974, astrophysicist John Gribbin co-authored The Jupiter Effect, which predicted several catastrophes arising from the combined gravitational force of aligned planets. (Gribbin has since distanced himself from that hypothesis.)

占星家也提出了不少末日预言，通常涉及罕见的行星排列和彗星，但科学家也未能幸免。例如，雅各布·伯努利（Jacob Bernoulli）认为地球将在 1719 年 4 月 5 日被一颗彗星摧毁。1974 年，天体物理学家约翰·格里宾（John Gribbin）合著了《木星效应》（The Jupiter Effect），预言了行星排列产生的引力合力将引发多场灾难。（格里宾后来已与该假说保持距离。）

There hasn’t been a successful doomsday prediction yet. So relax, once Microsoft is up and running again, we’ll probably all be fine—at least until 2060, which none other than Isaac Newton believed could herald the end of the world. Or maybe it won’t happen until 2129 or 2239 (based on interpretations of the Talmud) or 2280 (based on interpretations of the Qur’an). Or we can cling to the prophecy of a mystic named Baba Vanga, who thought the world wouldn’t end until 5078.

目前还没有一个成功的末日预言。所以放轻松，一旦微软恢复正常运行，我们大概都会安然无恙——至少能撑到 2060 年，艾萨克·牛顿（Isaac Newton）本人曾认为那一年可能是世界末日的先兆。又或许末日要到 2129 年或 2239 年（基于对《塔木德》的解读）或 2280 年（基于对《古兰经》的解读）才会发生。或者，我们可以寄希望于一位名叫巴巴·万加（Baba Vanga）的神秘主义者的预言，她认为世界直到 5078 年才会终结。
