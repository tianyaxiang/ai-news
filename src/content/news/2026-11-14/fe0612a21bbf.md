# Someone's impersonating this blog

Someone's impersonating this blog Published on November 13, 2026 I was googling the name of my website, as one does occasionally, and was surprised to see a link to one of my blog posts on mataroa.site. At first I figured it was just an alternate domain name for mataroa.blog, but no, when you type mataroa.site into your browser, it shows you my blog. It's the only thing that's hosted there.

有人正在冒充我的博客。发布于 2026 年 11 月 13 日。我像往常一样用谷歌搜索我的网站名称，惊讶地发现 mataroa.site 上有一个指向我博客文章的链接。起初我以为这只是 mataroa.blog 的一个备用域名，但并不是，当你把 mataroa.site 输入浏览器时，它显示的是我的博客。那是该网站上托管的唯一内容。

My next thought was that someone bought me a URL, but that can't be the case either: using a custom domain on mataroa requires a premium account, which I don't have. For anything but a plain redirect, I would have needed to set up the A record my end. I then realized that the version of my blog on that site doesn't have the latest post. The most recent update on the homepage is my Aug 21 entry.

我接下来的想法是有人帮我买了一个网址，但这也不可能：在 mataroa 上使用自定义域名需要高级账户，而我并没有。除了简单的重定向外，我需要在自己这边设置 A 记录。随后我意识到，该网站上的博客版本并没有最新的文章。主页上最近的更新是我 8 月 21 日的条目。

When you scroll down and start clicking some of the older links, you get 404 pages. You also get a 404 if you try to post a comment, load the RSS feed or view standalone pages like the AI statement. Someone scraped my blog and created a website to impersonate me. They're also impersonating mataroa, since using the name implies some sort of official connection.

当你向下滚动并开始点击一些旧链接时，会跳转到 404 页面。如果你尝试发表评论、加载 RSS 订阅或查看像 AI 声明这样的独立页面，也会得到 404 错误。有人抓取了我的博客并创建了一个网站来冒充我。他们也在冒充 mataroa，因为使用这个名字暗示了某种官方联系。

They're re-hosting the stolen images on their own server, and they all have a watermark. Bizarrely, the watermark is mattbee.mataroa.blog, my real URL. I assume this is to somehow make it seem legitimate, like it's an officially-sanctioned mirror, but I don't know who they think is going to care. It's not like they're stealing from a site with name recognition.

他们将窃取的图片重新托管在自己的服务器上，并且所有图片都有水印。奇怪的是，水印是我的真实网址 mattbee.mataroa.blog。我推测这是为了让它看起来合法，就像是一个官方授权的镜像，但我不知道他们认为谁会在意。毕竟他们窃取的又不是什么知名网站。

When you view the source of a blog post, it's identical to the rendered HTML except that there are two Cloudflare scripts at the bottom, one to obfuscate the mailto: link in the footer (which doesn't work1) and one for analytics. When you view the source of the index, there's a hidden link at the bottom to a Chinese github.io page for a website cloning tool.

当你查看博客文章的源代码时，它与渲染后的 HTML 完全相同，只是底部有两个 Cloudflare 脚本，一个用于混淆页脚中的 mailto: 链接（该功能无效），另一个用于分析。当你查看索引页的源代码时，底部有一个指向某个中文 github.io 页面的隐藏链接，该页面是一个网站克隆工具。

I have no idea why I was targeted for impersonation. It should be obvious this isn't a high-value website.2 It's a personal blog where I write what's relevant to me, and that rarely overlaps with anything other people care about. If the subject matter didn't tip them off, it should be clear from the 0 comments on most posts that this is not an enterprise ripe for monetization.

我不知道为什么我会被选为冒充目标。显而易见，这不是一个高价值的网站。这是一个个人博客，我写的是与我相关的内容，这些内容很少与其他人关心的事情重叠。如果主题内容没能提醒他们，那么大多数文章 0 评论的事实应该足以说明，这不是一个适合变现的企业。

If they just wanted a fake blog to scam adsense, surely a large language model would happily spew out more SEO-friendly slop than anything I have to offer. If their goal was to leech off the popularity of mataroa, why not impersonate one of the blogs with multiple front-page appearances on the orange website? Surely they have more SEO juice than me. Nothing about this makes any sense.

如果他们只是想要一个假博客来骗取 Adsense 广告费，大型语言模型肯定能比我提供的内容更轻松地炮制出符合 SEO 的垃圾信息。如果他们的目标是蹭 mataroa 的热度，为什么不冒充那些在橙色网站（Hacker News）首页多次出现的博客呢？它们肯定比我有更多的 SEO 权重。这一切都毫无道理。

Anyway, the URL is registered with Namecheap, so I sent them an abuse report. I have no idea if they'll act on it. They don't seem to have a good track record of giving a shit unless the content is actually hosted with them. It's hosted with Cloudflare, and the abuse reporting form they insist you use straight-up doesn't work. Click "Submit an abuse report" and it'll take you to a completely blank page. Very encouraging.

总之，该网址是在 Namecheap 注册的，所以我向他们发送了滥用报告。我不知道他们是否会采取行动。除非内容确实托管在他们那里，否则他们似乎并不怎么关心。该网站托管在 Cloudflare 上，而他们坚持要求使用的滥用报告表格根本无法使用。点击“提交滥用报告”只会跳转到一个完全空白的页面。真是令人鼓舞。

I'd have to dox myself to do a DMCA takedown, and I don't have the legal resources to do anything when it gets ignored. So if neither evil corporation cares to do anything, I guess I can just get fucked. I figure the best thing I can do to protect myself is document everything here. If you see a link to mataroa.site, it's not affiliated with me or mataroa. It seems like it isn't set up to scam people or distribute malware at the moment, but it probably will in the future. Be careful.

我必须公开个人信息才能进行 DMCA 下架处理，而如果被无视，我也没有法律资源去采取任何行动。所以，如果这两家邪恶公司都不打算做点什么，我想我也只能自认倒霉了。我觉得我能做的最好的自我保护就是在这里记录下一切。如果你看到 mataroa.site 的链接，它与我和 mataroa 没有任何关系。目前它似乎还没有被用来诈骗或传播恶意软件，但未来很可能会这样。请小心。
