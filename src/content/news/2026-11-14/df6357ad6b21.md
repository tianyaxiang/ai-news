# CMF’s milk carton-shaped Bluetooth speaker is down to a new low price

Nothing’s funky, compact Bluetooth speaker launched in October and is already on sale ahead of Black Friday.

Nothing 旗下这款造型独特、小巧的蓝牙音箱于 10 月发布，目前已在“黑色星期五”促销活动前开启降价。

Bluetooth speakers are often boring boxes in plain colors, but not this one! As part of Nothing’s ongoing early Black Friday promo, you can grab its unique, milk carton-shaped CMF Speaker S from Amazon and Nothing for just $39. That’s a solid $20 discount and the lowest price we’ve seen on Nothing’s newfangled wireless speaker, which hit the market about a month ago.

蓝牙音箱通常是外观平庸、颜色单调的方盒子，但这款产品与众不同！作为 Nothing 早期“黑色星期五”促销活动的一部分，你现在可以以 39 美元的价格从亚马逊或 Nothing 官网购入这款独特的牛奶盒造型 CMF Speaker S。这相当于 20 美元的实惠折扣，也是这款于一个月前上市的新型无线音箱迄今为止的最低价格。

The CMF Speaker S is the smaller of the two recently-released models — the larger Series M is also on sale at Amazon for $79 ($30 off) — and sports a 45mm driver with two passive radiators to help with low-end frequencies. It can run for up to 18 hours on a single charge and carries an IP68 rating for water and dust resistance. Despite its carefree aesthetic, it also supports stereo pairing with a second Series S speaker, Bluetooth 6, fast pairing via multiple methods (including NFC and Google Fast Pair), and audio over USB-C.

CMF Speaker S 是近期发布的两款型号中较小的一款（较大的 Series M 目前也在亚马逊以 79 美元的价格促销，优惠 30 美元），它配备了一个 45 毫米驱动单元和两个用于增强低频表现的无源辐射器。单次充电可续航长达 18 小时，并具备 IP68 级防尘防水性能。尽管外观设计随性，它还支持与另一台 Series S 音箱进行立体声配对、蓝牙 6 技术、多种方式快速配对（包括 NFC 和 Google Fast Pair）以及通过 USB-C 传输音频。

In addition to their great sound and compact footprint, both the Speaker S and Speaker M feature removable faceplates and multifunction front knobs. Not only can you swap them out to customize the colors, but CMF plans to share STL files so anyone with a 3D printer can create their own attachments and looks.

除了出色的音质和紧凑的体积外，Speaker S 和 Speaker M 都配备了可拆卸面板和多功能前置旋钮。你不仅可以更换它们来定制颜色，CMF 还计划分享 STL 文件，让任何拥有 3D 打印机的人都能制作属于自己的配件和外观。
