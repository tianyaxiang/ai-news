# Starship flight 15 powers to orbit—setting up a possible catch on Saturday

STARBASE, Texas—Starship lifted off right on schedule Friday from South Texas, making its 15th test flight in more than three years. The largely successful launch took place in the early evening; as the massive rocket rose into the atmosphere, the Sun sank toward the western horizon. Conditions were ideal for the launch, with the atmospheric winds so calm the rocket’s white plume did not dissipate for many minutes. The weather in South Texas is not always comfortable; summers are long and hot. Friday evening was perfect at Starbase.

德克萨斯州星际基地——“星舰”于周五按计划从南德克萨斯州准时升空，完成了三年多来的第15次试飞。这次基本成功的发射发生在傍晚时分；随着巨大的火箭升入大气层，太阳正向西方的地平线落下。发射条件非常理想，大气风力平静，火箭留下的白色尾迹在数分钟内都没有消散。南德克萨斯州的天气并不总是那么舒适，夏季漫长而炎热。但周五晚上的星际基地天气堪称完美。

The first stage ascended toward space flawlessly, releasing the upper stage above the Gulf of Mexico. From there, the Starship upper stage ignited its Raptor engines and powered its way into a nominal orbit. This was the second time SpaceX has flown Starship into low-Earth orbit. When it came time for the first stage to return to the ground, it dropped like a rock. However, due to an unspecified issue, the vehicle was commanded to divert into the Gulf rather than return to the Starbase facility to be caught by the launch tower. It created a fantastically powerful explosion, even for observers more than 10 miles away.

第一级火箭完美地向太空攀升，并在墨西哥湾上空释放了二级火箭。随后，“星舰”二级火箭点燃了“猛禽”发动机，并成功进入预定轨道。这是SpaceX第二次将“星舰”送入近地轨道。当第一级火箭需要返回地面时，它像石头一样坠落。然而，由于一个未说明的问题，该飞行器被指令转向坠入墨西哥湾，而不是返回星际基地由发射塔回收。即便对于10英里外的观察者来说，这也产生了一场极其震撼的爆炸。

Primary mission, a success. The primary aim of Friday’s launch was the deployment of 26 Starlink V3 satellites from a narrow door at the base of the spacecraft. This occurred about 40 minutes into Starship’s flight, and all 26 satellites—which have solar panels that stretch 60 meters end to end when fully deployed—safely made it into orbit, where they will now use onboard propulsion to raise their altitude. This means that Starship is now an operational, orbital rocket. It is the largest and most powerful machine humans have ever launched into space.

主要任务取得成功。周五发射的主要目标是从航天器底部的狭窄舱门部署26颗星链V3卫星。这一过程发生在“星舰”飞行约40分钟后，所有26颗卫星——其太阳能电池板完全展开后全长达60米——均安全进入轨道，并将利用自带的推进系统提升高度。这意味着“星舰”现在已成为一种可投入运营的轨道火箭。它是人类有史以来发射到太空中的最大、最强大的机器。

The challenge for the company now is to make Starship reusable and dramatically increase its flight rate. In that sense, the first part of Friday’s mission was not a success. SpaceX had hoped to return the first-stage booster, refurbish it, and fly it again. The company has previously landed and reflown Starship boosters but has yet to do so with the latest iteration of the vehicle, version 3. However, SpaceX still has a chance to make history on Saturday with the potential return of the Starship upper stage.

该公司目前面临的挑战是实现“星舰”的可重复使用，并大幅提高其飞行频率。从这个意义上说，周五任务的第一部分并不算成功。SpaceX曾希望回收第一级助推器，进行翻新并再次飞行。该公司此前曾着陆并重复使用过“星舰”助推器，但尚未在最新的第三代飞行器上实现这一目标。不过，SpaceX仍有机会在周六通过“星舰”二级火箭的潜在回收创造历史。

SpaceX has never attempted to catch the vehicle’s upper stage. Doing so will require Starship to pass a series of tests in orbit and subsequently during its return to Earth to ensure it is fully healthy. This is critical because Starship will be passing over parts of Mexico and the United States before returning to Starbase, and SpaceX does not want to endanger people on the ground. Additionally, it does not want to risk damage to its launch towers. If Starship passes these health checks, it would land at about 3:15 pm local time in Texas (21:15 UTC). If it does not, the company has earlier options to de-orbit the vehicle into an ocean.

SpaceX此前从未尝试过回收该飞行器的二级火箭。要做到这一点，需要“星舰”在轨道上以及随后的返回地球过程中通过一系列测试，以确保其状态完全健康。这一点至关重要，因为“星舰”在返回星际基地前将飞越墨西哥和美国的部分地区，SpaceX不希望危及地面人员的安全。此外，它也不想冒损坏发射塔的风险。如果“星舰”通过了这些健康检查，它将于德克萨斯当地时间下午3:15（世界标准时间21:15）左右着陆。如果未能通过，该公司还有更早的方案，即让飞行器脱离轨道坠入海洋。

Testing the heat shield is critical. Successfully returning Starship’s upper stage would be historic, as well as significant for SpaceX’s efforts to reuse the massive rocket. It would also give the company valuable information about the vehicle’s performance during reentry. One of the biggest hurdles left in Starship’s development is the vehicle’s heat shield, which must protect the upper stage during its return through Earth’s atmosphere. More than that, SpaceX aims to make Starship rapidly reusable, and to do this, Starship’s tiles must be light and resilient.

测试隔热罩至关重要。成功回收“星舰”二级火箭不仅具有历史意义，对SpaceX实现该巨型火箭的重复使用也意义重大。这也将为公司提供关于飞行器在再入大气层期间性能的宝贵数据。目前“星舰”研发中最大的障碍之一是隔热罩，它必须在二级火箭返回穿越地球大气层时提供保护。不仅如此，SpaceX的目标是实现“星舰”的快速重复使用，为此，隔热瓦必须既轻便又耐用。

SpaceX has been experimenting with tiles since the beginning of the Starship program, trying to find the right combination of low mass, heat resistance, and durability so the tiles require minimal refurbishment between launches. For this flight, SpaceX said it is applying learnings from the previous test flight in September, including additional retention mechanisms on tiles in areas deemed to be at the highest risk of falling off during ascent and addressing areas with a flow path for plasma to get behind the tiles. Another problem on recent flights has been heat incursion into the small gaps between the edges of the tiles. According to SpaceX, this Starship has a large portion of the heat shield equipped with a curved tile design that has shown the ability to reduce heating in the gaps between tiles.

自“星舰”项目启动以来，SpaceX一直在进行隔热瓦实验，试图找到低质量、耐热性和耐用性的最佳组合，以便在发射间隙将隔热瓦的翻新工作量降至最低。对于此次飞行，SpaceX表示应用了9月份上次试飞的经验，包括在上升过程中被认为最容易脱落的区域增加了隔热瓦固定机制，并解决了等离子体进入隔热瓦背后的流动路径问题。近期飞行中另一个问题是热量侵入隔热瓦边缘的小缝隙。据SpaceX称，这艘“星舰”的大部分隔热罩采用了弧形隔热瓦设计，已证明能够减少隔热瓦缝隙间的受热。
