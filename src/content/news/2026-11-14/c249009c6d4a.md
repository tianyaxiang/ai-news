### Railway and Aerospace Protocols — Ch.11: Future Critical Communication

STATERA Library · Series 07 · Volume 08 · Chapter 11 · EN Source: https://github.com/Statera-Library/07_Vol_08_Railway_and_Aerospace_Protocols/blob/main/Chapter_11_Future_Critical_Communication/Chapter_11_Future_Critical_Communication_EN.md

Volume 08. Railway and Aerospace Protocols Chapter 11. Future Critical Communication 11.01. DO-326A Cybersecurity DO-326A defines an aviation security process for addressing intentional unauthorized electronic interaction with aircraft systems throughout their lifecycle.

第 08 卷：铁路与航空协议，第 11 章：未来关键通信，11.01 DO-326A 网络安全。DO-326A 定义了一套航空安全流程，旨在解决在其整个生命周期内针对飞机系统的蓄意未经授权的电子交互行为。

Unlike traditional safety engineering, which primarily considers accidental failures and unintended conditions, aviation cybersecurity must consider deliberate actions performed by capable adversaries. The objective is not simply to protect individual computers or networks, but to ensure that security threats cannot produce unacceptable effects on aircraft operation, safety, or continued airworthiness.

与主要考虑意外故障和非预期状况的传统安全工程不同，航空网络安全必须考虑有能力的对手所采取的蓄意行动。其目标不仅仅是保护单个计算机或网络，而是确保安全威胁不会对飞机的运行、安全或持续适航性产生不可接受的影响。

The standard is closely associated with airworthiness security because modern aircraft increasingly depend on interconnected digital systems. Avionics networks, maintenance interfaces, wireless communication, ground systems, software-loading mechanisms, and external data services create pathways through which information enters or leaves the aircraft.

该标准与适航安全密切相关，因为现代飞机日益依赖互联的数字系统。航空电子网络、维护接口、无线通信、地面系统、软件加载机制以及外部数据服务，共同构成了信息进出飞机的数据通路。

Security engineering must therefore examine the complete system boundary rather than treating cybersecurity as an isolated information-technology function. Connectivity that improves operational capability can simultaneously increase the number of potential attack paths requiring analysis and protection.

因此，安全工程必须审查完整的系统边界，而不是将网络安全视为孤立的信息技术功能。在提高运行能力的同时，连接性也可能增加需要分析和防护的潜在攻击路径数量。

A central concept is the identification of assets that require protection. These assets can include avionics functions, software, configuration data, communication services, operational information, cryptographic material, and interfaces whose compromise could affect aircraft behavior.

一个核心概念是识别需要保护的资产。这些资产可能包括航空电子功能、软件、配置数据、通信服务、运行信息、加密材料以及一旦受损可能影响飞机行为的接口。

Security analysis examines how these assets interact with external and internal systems and determines where unauthorized access, manipulation, corruption, or disruption could occur. Asset identification provides the foundation for relating cybersecurity concerns to actual aircraft functions instead of evaluating threats only as abstract network events.

安全分析旨在审查这些资产如何与外部和内部系统交互，并确定在何处可能发生未经授权的访问、篡改、破坏或中断。资产识别为将网络安全问题与实际飞机功能相关联提供了基础，而非仅仅将威胁评估为抽象的网络事件。

Threat modeling considers potential adversaries, attack paths, interfaces, and possible consequences of unauthorized interaction. An attacker may attempt to modify information, inject false commands, disrupt communication, obtain protected information, or exploit software and configuration weaknesses.

威胁建模考虑了潜在的对手、攻击路径、接口以及未经授权交互可能导致的后果。攻击者可能会尝试修改信息、注入虚假指令、中断通信、获取受保护信息或利用软件及配置漏洞。

The important engineering question is not merely whether an attack is technically possible, but what aircraft-level effect could result if the attack succeeds. Cybersecurity assessment therefore connects threat scenarios with system functions and operational consequences, allowing security requirements to be derived according to the significance of the resulting effects.

重要的工程问题不仅仅是攻击在技术上是否可行，而是如果攻击成功，可能会导致什么样的飞机级影响。因此，网络安全评估将威胁场景与系统功能及运行后果联系起来，从而允许根据所产生影响的严重程度来推导安全需求。

Security risk assessment evaluates identified threats together with their potential impact and the difficulty or feasibility of successful exploitation. This allows engineering effort to focus on scenarios that could create meaningful aircraft consequences rather than applying identical protection to every interface.

安全风险评估会对已识别的威胁及其潜在影响以及成功利用的难度或可行性进行评估。这使得工程工作能够集中于可能产生重大飞机后果的场景，而不是对每个接口都应用相同的保护措施。

The resulting security objectives can then be allocated to systems, networks, software, hardware, operational procedures, and supporting infrastructure. In this sense, DO-326A promotes a risk-based engineering process in which cybersecurity controls are justified by the threats and aircraft effects they are intended to address.

由此产生的安全目标随后可分配给系统、网络、软件、硬件、运行程序和支持基础设施。从这个意义上讲，DO-326A 提倡一种基于风险的工程流程，其中网络安全控制措施的合理性由其旨在解决的威胁和飞机影响来证明。

Security requirements may include authentication, authorization, integrity protection, access control, network segregation, secure configuration, controlled software loading, monitoring, and protection of sensitive communication. No single mechanism is sufficient for every architecture.

安全需求可能包括身份验证、授权、完整性保护、访问控制、网络隔离、安全配置、受控软件加载、监控以及敏感通信的保护。没有任何单一机制适用于所有架构。

Instead, multiple protection layers can be combined so that failure or compromise of one mechanism does not automatically expose critical aircraft functions. This defense-in-depth principle is particularly important where external connectivity must coexist with safety-critical avionics and where complete physical isolation is impractical.

相反，可以将多个保护层结合起来，使得单一机制的失效或受损不会自动导致关键飞机功能暴露。这种纵深防御原则在外部连接必须与安全关键型航空电子设备共存且无法实现完全物理隔离的情况下尤为重要。

Network architecture is therefore a major cybersecurity consideration. Critical avionics domains should be protected from less trusted domains through appropriate partitioning, gateways, filtering, controlled interfaces, and information-flow restrictions.

因此，网络架构是网络安全的一个主要考量因素。关键航空电子域应通过适当的分区、网关、过滤、受控接口和信息流限制，与信任度较低的域隔离开来。

A communication gateway should not be considered merely a protocol converter; it may also represent a security boundary between systems with different levels of trust. The design must determine which information is permitted to cross that boundary, in which direction, under what conditions, and how abnormal communication is detected or contained.

通信网关不应仅仅被视为协议转换器；它还可能代表不同信任级别系统之间的安全边界。设计必须确定允许哪些信息跨越该边界、传输方向、传输条件，以及如何检测或遏制异常通信。

Legacy deterministic networks such as MIL-STD-1553 or ARINC 429 were developed primarily around reliability, predictable communication, and controlled system integration rather than contemporary cybersecurity threats. Their constrained communication behavior can reduce some forms of uncontrolled network interaction, but determinism alone does not provide cybersecurity.

诸如 MIL-STD-1553 或 ARINC 429 等传统的确定性网络，其开发主要围绕可靠性、可预测的通信和受控的系统集成，而非针对当代的网络安全威胁。它们受限的通信行为可以减少某些形式的非受控网络交互，但仅靠确定性并不能提供网络安全保障。

When such networks are connected through gateways to Ethernet, wireless links, maintenance systems, or external services, the resulting attack surface must be evaluated at the complete architecture level. A trusted legacy bus can become exposed indirectly through a poorly protected integration path.

当此类网络通过网关连接到以太网、无线链路、维护系统或外部服务时，必须在完整的架构层面评估由此产生的攻击面。一个受信任的传统总线可能会通过保护不力的集成路径被间接暴露。

Modern Ethernet-based avionics networks such as AFDX, TTE, and emerging TSN-based architectures provide greater bandwidth and connectivity, but increased connectivity also expands cybersecurity considerations. Switches, end systems, configuration data, synchronization mechanisms, gateways, and network-management functions can become security-relevant assets.

现代基于以太网的航空电子网络（如 AFDX、TTE 以及新兴的基于 TSN 的架构）提供了更大的带宽和连接性，但连接性的增加也扩大了网络安全的考量范围。交换机、终端系统、配置数据、同步机制、网关和网络管理功能都可能成为与安全相关的资产。

Deterministic scheduling can constrain expected communication patterns, which may assist monitoring, but it does not replace authentication, integrity protection, secure configuration,

确定性调度可以约束预期的通信模式，这可能有助于监控，但它不能取代身份验证、完整性保护和安全配置。
