# Amazon has a plan to index the world’s online shopping data

亚马逊计划为全球在线购物数据建立索引。

Listing the details of everything for sale everywhere is meant to make Amazon more useful than Reddit for people and AI agents.

将全球各地所有在售商品的详细信息进行编目，旨在使亚马逊对人类和人工智能代理而言比 Reddit 更有用。

Amazon has plans to build a vault of data on every product sold anywhere on the internet, even products Amazon doesn’t sell, and make the information available through its own site, according to Reuters. The project, called the “World Product Graph,” is meant to make it less likely that shoppers leave Amazon to do their product research on Reddit or Google, and make it more likely shoppers end up at Amazon even if they’re searching for a product sold elsewhere.

据路透社报道，亚马逊计划建立一个数据库，涵盖互联网上销售的每一件产品，即使是亚马逊自身未销售的产品，并使其信息能够通过亚马逊网站获取。这个名为“世界产品图谱”（World Product Graph）的项目旨在降低购物者离开亚马逊去 Reddit 或谷歌进行产品研究的可能性，并增加购物者即使在搜索其他地方销售的产品时最终也会来到亚马逊的可能性。

The same goes for AI agents — if Amazon has the largest stockpile of shopping data, AI agents may rely on its index of products over others, so even people shopping with bots will end up on its pages.

人工智能代理的情况也是如此——如果亚马逊拥有最大的购物数据储备，人工智能代理可能会优先依赖其产品索引，因此即使是使用机器人购物的人最终也会进入亚马逊的页面。

Links to outside retailers’ products have already started popping up on Amazon through the “shop direct” feature it launched last year. The company also has its own AI shopping assistant, Alexa for Shopping, previously called Rufus. The World Product Graph project would also be aimed at fixing issues with the AI by providing it more data.

通过去年推出的“直接购买”（shop direct）功能，外部零售商的产品链接已经开始出现在亚马逊上。该公司还拥有自己的人工智能购物助手 Alexa for Shopping（此前称为 Rufus）。“世界产品图谱”项目也旨在通过提供更多数据来解决该人工智能存在的问题。

According to Reuters, an internal Amazon document on the project says “Many Rufus answers are factually wrong or incomplete” and running users’ Amazon searches with the AI is expensive. Adding more data is reportedly supposed to make the system more accurate and able to use cheaper models.

据路透社报道，一份关于该项目的亚马逊内部文件称，“许多 Rufus 的回答在事实上是错误的或不完整的”，并且使用该人工智能运行用户的亚马逊搜索成本高昂。据称，增加更多数据旨在使系统更准确，并能够使用成本更低的模型。

Whether this actually ships is still up in the air. The internal document reportedly says the project “depends on discovering how brands organize their products on their own websites,” which could involve Amazon crawling other retailers’ sites. Reuters says it’s unclear “if or when this plan may be implemented,” but said the document on it included an October 1st, 2027 release date.

该项目是否会真正发布仍未确定。据报道，内部文件称该项目“取决于发现品牌如何在其自身网站上组织产品”，这可能涉及亚马逊抓取其他零售商的网站。路透社表示，目前尚不清楚“该计划是否或何时会实施”，但指出相关文件包含了一个 2027 年 10 月 1 日的发布日期。

Amazon said in a statement to Reuters that “Draft, outdated documents often paint an incomplete picture and the existence of a brainstorming document such as this one doesn’t necessarily reflect our current plans.”

亚马逊在给路透社的一份声明中表示：“草拟的、过时的文件往往描绘出一幅不完整的图景，而像这样一份头脑风暴文件的存在并不一定反映我们当前的计划。”
