# Best Mosquito Repellents for Your Yard: What Works and What Doesn’t (2026)
# 2026年庭院驱蚊指南：哪些有效，哪些无效

Mosquitoes suck. Literally, figuratively, and every other way. These bloodsuckers have been a worldwide menace since the beginning of recorded history. A popularly reposted infographic lists them as—by far—the world’s deadliest animal, thanks to the parade of nasty diseases they can transmit. West Nile, Zika, chikungunya, malaria, dengue—these are no longer illnesses confined to the global south. In recent years, all have been discovered in bugs in the United States.

蚊子真的很讨厌。无论是从字面意义、比喻意义还是其他任何角度来看都是如此。自人类有记录的历史以来，这些吸血鬼一直是全球性的威胁。一张广为流传的信息图表显示，由于它们能传播一系列可怕的疾病，蚊子是迄今为止世界上最致命的动物。西尼罗河病毒、寨卡病毒、基孔肯雅热、疟疾、登革热——这些疾病已不再局限于全球南方国家。近年来，在美国的蚊虫中也发现了所有这些病毒。

Ridding your property of mosquitoes is a decidedly Quixotic effort. You can stem the tide for a while, but you can’t ever win the war. Once whatever abatement method you use has worn off, they swoop back in, thirstier than ever. Nevertheless, you have to do something. Left unattended, mosquitoes can quickly turn from a nuisance to an epidemic that makes your outdoor space completely unusable.

想要彻底清除你领地内的蚊子，这绝对是一项堂吉诃德式的徒劳之举。你可以暂时遏制它们的攻势，但永远无法赢得这场战争。一旦你使用的驱蚊方法失效，它们就会卷土重来，而且比以往更加饥渴。尽管如此，你还是必须采取行动。如果置之不理，蚊子很快就会从一种困扰演变成一场瘟疫，让你无法使用户外空间。

Outside of wide-scale community mosquito spraying programs, most personal and home-based mosquito abatement tools focus on repelling them rather than trying to outright kill them. Insecticides like DEET work by being rubbed onto the skin, while metofluthrin and allethrin work in vapor form, creating a cloud of repellent that works without you having to make physical contact with the chemicals. Trapping mosquitoes is another potential option, luring them into a chamber from which they can’t escape.

除了大规模的社区蚊虫喷洒计划外，大多数个人和家庭驱蚊工具的重点在于驱赶蚊子，而不是试图直接杀死它们。像避蚊胺（DEET）这样的杀虫剂通过涂抹在皮肤上起作用，而甲氟菊酯（metofluthrin）和丙烯菊酯（allethrin）则以蒸汽形式发挥作用，形成一层驱蚊云，让你无需与化学物质直接接触即可生效。诱捕蚊子是另一种选择，即将它们引诱进一个无法逃脱的腔室中。

For years I’ve tried innumerable technologies and tactics to rid my property—and my very susceptible skin—from mosquitoes. Here’s what I’ve uncovered to date that works—and what doesn’t.

多年来，我尝试了无数的技术和策略来清除我领地内的蚊子，并保护我那极易招蚊子的皮肤。以下是我迄今为止发现的有效方法和无效方法。

### Best Personal Repellent: Thermacell E-ZoneGuard E55
### 最佳个人驱蚊器：Thermacell E-ZoneGuard E55

This pint-sized repeller weighs a little more than a pound and recharges via a USB-C cable. Power it up and a heating element inside warms a vial of metofluthrin, releasing mosquito-repulsing vapor in your immediate vicinity, about 10 feet in every direction. It’s simple and extremely portable, making it easy to move from one area to another.

这款小巧的驱蚊器重量刚过一磅，通过USB-C线充电。启动后，内部的加热元件会加热一瓶甲氟菊酯，在你的周围（约10英尺范围内）释放出驱蚊蒸汽。它简单且极其便携，可以轻松地在不同区域间移动。

The system works. It warms up in just a few minutes and effectively keeps bugs at bay, provided you keep the repeller close by. Best of all, I was able to take it with me as I moved around the yard—but be warned, there’s some lag time for the vapor cloud to reform each time you relocate. A small chemical replacement cartridge lasts for about 36 hours; refills are $39 for a 2-pack.

该系统确实有效。它只需几分钟即可预热，只要你把它放在身边，就能有效驱赶蚊虫。最棒的是，我在院子里走动时可以随身携带它——但要注意，每次你移动位置后，蒸汽云重新形成需要一点时间。一个小型的化学替换药剂盒可以使用约36小时；补充装售价为39美元（两盒装）。

### A Battery-Free Option: Thermacell Fuel-Zoneguard Base Mosquito Repeller
### 无需电池的选择：Thermacell Fuel-Zoneguard Base 驱蚊器

This butane-powered device offers a twist on the E-ZoneGuard line, containing no battery at all, which may be preferred in some exceptionally off-the-grid environments. Instead, a butane cartridge twists into the system. When activated (no flame needed), it heats up a replaceable chemical mat that slides into a groove on top of the device, this one using allethrin instead of metofluthrin.

这款丁烷驱动的设备是E-ZoneGuard系列的一个变体，完全不需要电池，这在一些极度偏远的环境中可能更受欢迎。它通过将丁烷罐旋入系统来工作。启动时（无需明火），它会加热一张可更换的化学垫片，该垫片滑入设备顶部的凹槽中。这款产品使用的是丙烯菊酯而非甲氟菊酯。

One downside: The vapor shield promised by the Fuel-Zoneguard stretches only about 7½ feet in each direction, more restrictive than the E-Zoneguard, and a butane canister will only get you about 12 hours of operation. Allethrin may also be less effective than metofluthrin against pests. That said, I experienced great results with this product and found the freedom from cables to be liberating. Refills run $19 for 24 hours of extra fuel and insecticide mats (sold together).

缺点是：Fuel-Zoneguard提供的蒸汽保护范围仅在每个方向约7.5英尺，比E-ZoneGuard更受限，且一个丁烷罐只能维持约12小时的运行。丙烯菊酯在驱虫效果上可能也不如甲氟菊酯。尽管如此，我使用该产品的效果非常好，而且摆脱电缆束缚的感觉很棒。补充装售价19美元，包含24小时的额外燃料和杀虫垫片（成套销售）。

### Best Large-Scale Repellent Gear: Thermacell LIV Smart Mosquito Repellent System 2.0
### 最佳大规模驱蚊装备：Thermacell LIV 智能驱蚊系统 2.0

Take Thermacell’s E-Zoneguard system and make it yard-sized, and you’ve got LIV, a semi-permanent solution that uses the same repellent chemical (metofluthrin) but dramatically expands its coverage potential.

把Thermacell的E-Zoneguard系统放大到庭院规模，你就得到了LIV。这是一种半永久性的解决方案，使用相同的驱蚊化学物质（甲氟菊酯），但极大地扩展了覆盖范围。

The system is built around a hub that plugs into wall power and connects to your home’s Wi-Fi. Up to 10 freestanding repellers connect to this hub via cables. You string them in a chain to surround your deck, your pool, or whatever else you want to protect from mosquitoes. Arranged in a rough circle, the resultant cloud of repellent serves as a large-scale barrier that can protect the whole family from being attacked. It’s all controlled via a mobile app (with Alexa support allegedly in the works), so you can fire the system up before you even head outside.

该系统围绕一个连接墙壁电源并接入家庭Wi-Fi的集线器构建。最多可有10个独立驱蚊器通过电缆连接到此集线器。你可以将它们串联起来，环绕你的露台、游泳池或任何你想保护的区域。将它们大致排列成一个圆圈，形成的驱蚊云就像一道大规模屏障，可以保护全家人免受蚊虫叮咬。一切都通过移动应用程序控制（据称Alexa支持正在开发中），因此你甚至可以在出门前就启动系统。

It’s one of the most effective systems I’ve tested, in large part because it pumps out so much repellent at precise locations. While nothing is perfect, LIV is about as close as it gets, protecting my entire swimming pool and deck at the touch of a button on its mobile app.

这是我测试过的最有效的系统之一，很大程度上是因为它能在精确的位置喷射出大量的驱蚊剂。虽然没有什么是完美的，但LIV已经非常接近完美了，只需在手机App上轻轻一点，就能保护我的整个游泳池和露台。

The only catch is that LIV is far from inexpensive, and Thermacell no longer sells it as a DIY solution; you’ll need a professional installer to complete the job. If saving your skin (and potentially your health) from mosquitoes is important to you, it could be worth it.

唯一的缺点是LIV价格昂贵，而且Thermacell不再将其作为DIY方案销售；你需要专业的安装人员来完成安装。如果你认为保护皮肤（以及潜在的健康）免受蚊虫叮咬很重要，那么这笔投资是值得的。
