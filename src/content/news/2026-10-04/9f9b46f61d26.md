# "very likely" Means "uncertain"? How LLMs Diverge from Humans in Linguistic Uncertainty Quantification
# “很有可能”意味着“不确定”？大语言模型在语言不确定性量化上与人类有何不同

**Abstract:** Humans express uncertainty verbally via markers (e.g., "possible," "likely"), yet most LLM uncertainty quantification (UQ) relies on costing likelihood- or consistency-based signals. From a cognitive perspective, accurate verbal uncertainty reflects metacognitive monitoring, representing knowledge boundaries ("knowing that you don't know") to support regulation and information seeking.

**摘要：** 人类通过语言标记（如“可能”、“很有可能”）来表达不确定性，然而大多数大语言模型（LLM）的不确定性量化（UQ）依赖于基于似然度或一致性的信号。从认知角度来看，准确的语言不确定性反映了元认知监控，代表了知识边界（即“知道自己不知道”），从而支持调节和信息寻求行为。

In this paper, we investigate how LLMs diverge from humans in verbal uncertainty quantification and whether verbal markers can reliably quantify LLM uncertainty. We curate a corpus of human uncertainty markers from psychology and decision-science literature and benchmark LLMs against it. We observe that LLMs encode verbal uncertainty with numerical levels that differ substantially from those of humans.

在本文中，我们研究了大语言模型在语言不确定性量化方面与人类的差异，以及语言标记是否能可靠地量化大语言模型的不确定性。我们从心理学和决策科学文献中整理了一个人类不确定性标记语料库，并以此为基准对大语言模型进行了评估。我们观察到，大语言模型对语言不确定性的数值编码水平与人类存在显著差异。

We then introduce METHODNAME, a novel optimization-based algorithm that learns an optimal uncertainty profile over uncertainty markers directly from LLM outputs. By fitting a marker-uncertainty mapping to best explain empirical correctness, METHODNAME discovers how much probability mass each verbal marker should convey, rather than estimating uncertainty via repeated sampling.

随后，我们引入了 METHODNAME，这是一种基于优化的新型算法，可以直接从大语言模型的输出中学习不确定性标记的最佳不确定性分布。通过拟合标记与不确定性之间的映射关系以最好地解释经验正确性，METHODNAME 能够发现每个语言标记应传达的概率质量，而不是通过重复采样来估计不确定性。

METHODNAME enables a direct, marker-level comparison of confidence semantics between humans and LLMs, disentangling mismatch and revealing systematic confidence disparities in verbal expressions.

METHODNAME 实现了人类与大语言模型之间置信度语义的直接、标记级比较，从而理清了不匹配之处，并揭示了语言表达中系统性的置信度差异。
