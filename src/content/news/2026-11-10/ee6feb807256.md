Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.08704 (cs) [Submitted on 5 Nov 2026] Title: Contextuality and Contradictions: Exploring Real-World Identifications of AI-Generated Writing Authors: Michelle Morgenstern, Julia Witte Zimmerman, Tabia Tanzin Prama, Kristine Harootunian, June Clark, Bryn Loftness, Calla Beauregard, Juniper Lovato.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.08704 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 5 日] 标题：《语境与矛盾：探索人工智能生成写作的现实识别》，作者：Michelle Morgenstern, Julia Witte Zimmerman, Tabia Tanzin Prama, Kristine Harootunian, June Clark, Bryn Loftness, Calla Beauregard, Juniper Lovato。

Abstract: As AI-generated content spreads into nearly every domain of human interaction, practices and discourses of AI detection, both formal and informal, have become an everyday part of how people interact with text and with each other. In this paper, we explore the real-world practices and discourses of AI identification that have emerged in response to a shifting discursive landscape where the human provenance of a text can no longer be taken for granted.

摘要：随着人工智能生成的内容扩散到人类互动的几乎每一个领域，正式和非正式的人工智能检测实践与话语已成为人们处理文本及彼此互动日常的一部分。在本文中，我们探讨了在话语环境不断变化的背景下所出现的现实世界人工智能识别实践与话语，在这种环境下，文本的人类来源已不再被视为理所当然。

We pair qualitative coding with computational analysis of lexical change, grounded in linguistic anthropological perspectives on language and communication, to investigate AI identifications among English Wikipedia editors and students. We find that these identifications draw on a diversity of evidentiary types, from discrete textual features and judgments about content to circular sense-making claims and presumptions about the author's moral character, and that they are contradictory, dynamic, and contextually dependent.

我们将定性编码与词汇变化的计算分析相结合，立足于语言与交流的语言人类学视角，调查了英语维基百科编辑和学生群体中对人工智能的识别情况。我们发现，这些识别依赖于多种证据类型，从离散的文本特征和对内容的判断，到循环的意义构建主张以及对作者道德品质的推测；这些识别结果是矛盾的、动态的，且具有语境依赖性。

In practice, what often motivates an AI identification is not the presence of any specific textual element, but pragmatic infelicity: a perceived mismatch between the elements present and those deemed appropriate within the text's particular context. Consequently, real-world identifications of AI-generated writing do not merely neutrally point out the perceived presence of "AI-like" features, but can be leveraged as a discursive resource to maintain and define sociolinguistic norms: what can be said in a given context, what is appropriate, and, ultimately, what is human.

在实践中，促使人们进行人工智能识别的往往不是任何特定文本元素的存在，而是语用上的不当：即文本中呈现的元素与在该特定语境下被认为合适的元素之间存在感知上的错位。因此，现实中对人工智能生成写作的识别不仅仅是中立地指出“类人工智能”特征的存在，还可以被用作一种话语资源，以维护和定义社会语言规范：即在特定语境下可以说什么、什么是合适的，以及归根结底，什么是人类。

Further, the design and implementation of systems and tools that treat AI-likeness as a context-free property of text may therefore inherit, and enforce at scale, the norms of whichever contexts they were trained on.

此外，那些将“类人工智能”视为文本的无语境属性而进行设计和实现的系统与工具，可能会继承并大规模强制执行其训练所基于的特定语境下的规范。
