# Innovating with AI in the special chemicals industry

SponsoredIn partnership withInfosys As companies look to accelerate innovation with artificial intelligence, cloud computing, and advanced modeling, the primary challenge is connecting new technologies to reliable data, scientific expertise, and the practical realities of bringing products to market.

由 Infosys 赞助。随着各公司寻求利用人工智能、云计算和高级建模来加速创新，其面临的主要挑战是如何将新技术与可靠的数据、科学专业知识以及产品上市的实际情况相结合。

For specialty chemicals manufacturer Lubrizol, digital transformation is all about giving researchers the tools to test ideas faster, make better-informed decisions, and develop more sustainable materials without compromising performance. Girish Rao, vice president of information technology at Lubrizol, says the company is building a digital foundation that connects business processes, modern data platforms, and cloud infrastructure to support its research and development (R&D) teams.

对于特种化学品制造商路博润（Lubrizol）而言，数字化转型的核心在于为研究人员提供工具，以便他们能更快地测试想法、做出更明智的决策，并在不影响性能的前提下开发更可持续的材料。路博润信息技术副总裁 Girish Rao 表示，公司正在构建一个连接业务流程、现代数据平台和云基础设施的数字基础，以支持其研发（R&D）团队。

Their goal is to combine decades of accumulated technical data with the deep expertise of chemists and engineers who understand customer needs as well as the physical constraints of their work. Data scientists can help identify patterns, test new hypotheses, and model possibilities before researchers conduct physical experiments. But, Rao emphasizes, “it's not about replacing scientific judgment; it is about giving our scientists better tools.”

他们的目标是将数十年来积累的技术数据与化学家和工程师的深厚专业知识相结合，这些专家不仅了解客户需求，也深谙其工作中的物理限制。数据科学家可以在研究人员进行物理实验之前，帮助识别模式、测试新假设并对各种可能性进行建模。但 Rao 强调：“这并不是要取代科学判断，而是要为我们的科学家提供更好的工具。”

Scientists, data scientists, and IT teams approach problems from different perspectives, prioritizing experimental validity, model performance, and platform security and scalability, respectively. By bringing these groups together in “fusion teams,” Lubrizol aligns them around shared objectives, starting with small pilots to build trust. "Once the trust is built between the team members and they know what value they're about to create," Rao says, "things go a lot better."

科学家、数据科学家和 IT 团队从不同的角度处理问题，分别优先考虑实验有效性、模型性能以及平台的安全性和可扩展性。通过将这些群体整合为“融合团队”，路博润使他们围绕共同目标保持一致，并从小型试点项目开始建立信任。Rao 说：“一旦团队成员之间建立了信任，并且他们知道自己即将创造什么价值，事情就会进展得顺利得多。”

Cloud infrastructure also gives the company flexibility to access computing resources and modern tools without making the substantial investments required to build and maintain its own data centers. These capabilities could prove especially valuable in advancing sustainability. Digital modeling can help researchers evaluate more formulations, explore materials such as starch and corn-based alternatives, and assess trade-offs across a product's life cycle.

云基础设施还使公司能够灵活地访问计算资源和现代工具，而无需投入大量资金来建设和维护自己的数据中心。这些能力在推动可持续发展方面可能尤为宝贵。数字建模可以帮助研究人员评估更多的配方，探索淀粉和玉米基替代品等材料，并评估产品生命周期中的权衡取舍。

Promising a material in a laboratory is only the first step. Scaling production while meeting performance expectations, regulatory requirements, and financial constraints is still a tightrope walk, but AI's ability to model multiple variables could help teams navigate those competing demands.

在实验室中发现一种有前景的材料仅仅是第一步。在满足性能预期、监管要求和财务限制的同时扩大生产规模仍然如履薄冰，但人工智能模拟多个变量的能力可以帮助团队应对这些相互冲突的需求。

Looking to the future, Rao expects AI, automation, advanced modeling, digital twins, and connected data to converge as laboratories become more intelligent and manufacturing plants more efficient. The opportunity, he suggests, lies in bringing these capabilities together responsibly and at scale. “It's not just a single technology. I think it's the combination of all of these, and it'll be the ability to connect all these processes that are going to generate tremendous amounts of data."

展望未来，Rao 预计随着实验室变得更加智能化、制造工厂变得更加高效，人工智能、自动化、高级建模、数字孪生和互联数据将会融合。他认为，机遇在于以负责任的方式大规模整合这些能力。“这不仅仅是一项单一的技术。我认为这是所有这些技术的结合，而连接所有这些流程的能力将产生海量的数据。”
