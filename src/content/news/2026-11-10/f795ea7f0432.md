In sports, a “dead rubber” is a match that takes place after the outcome of the series or championship has already been decided. The people in charge hate this—if you wrap up a title two-thirds of the way through a season, the viewers evaporate, as there’s nothing left at stake. But anyone who decided to skip this year’s Brazilian Grand Prix this past weekend missed one of the year’s most entertaining races, as a combination of a mixed-up starting order, some very fast drivers at the back, and a wet track that dried and then saw rain again resulted in action aplenty.

在体育运动中，“死局”（dead rubber）是指在系列赛或锦标赛结果已定之后进行的比赛。赛事负责人对此深恶痛绝——如果赛季还没过三分之二冠军就已产生，观众就会流失，因为比赛已毫无悬念。但任何决定错过上周末巴西大奖赛的人，都错过了一场年度最精彩的比赛。起跑顺序的混乱、后方车手的极速追赶，以及赛道由湿转干又再次降雨的复杂情况，共同造就了极其丰富的赛事看点。

A peculiarity of F1 is that even if you manage to win a championship, there’s no guarantee you’ll be in a position to defend it. Take McLaren’s Lando Norris, who took the crown in 2025 after a nail-biting fight with his teammate Oscar Piastri and Red Bull’s Max Verstappen. Sadly for Lando, McLaren did not start this year in a position to win races, and even with several upgrades, the team has produced a car that is still very tricky to drive. But this weekend in Brazil, Norris demonstrated the form that earned him the number 1.

F1的一大特点是，即使你赢得了冠军，也无法保证你有能力卫冕。以迈凯伦车队的兰多·诺里斯为例，他在2025年与队友奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里及红牛车队的马克斯·维斯塔潘展开激战后夺冠。遗憾的是，迈凯伦车队今年开局并不具备夺冠实力，即便经过多次升级，车队打造的赛车依然难以驾驭。但在本周末的巴西站，诺里斯展现出了他作为卫冕冠军的实力。

Rain on Saturday during qualifying helped jumble the usual running order. It was heavy enough that drivers used the wet weather tire with deep treads, which usually generates so much spray that visibility disappears and running has to be suspended. But with fewer cars on track in the later knockout qualifying sessions, this was not a problem for once. More disruption came from grid penalties for Verstappen and Ferrari’s Lewis Hamilton, both of whom needed to use extra power unit components outside of the season’s allowance.

周六排位赛期间的降雨打乱了常规的排位顺序。雨势大到车手们必须使用深纹湿地胎，这通常会产生大量水雾，导致能见度消失并不得不暂停比赛。但由于在后续的淘汰赛阶段赛道上的车辆较少，这次并没有出现问题。维斯塔潘和法拉利车队的刘易斯·汉密尔顿因超出赛季配额使用了额外的动力单元组件而受到发车位处罚，这进一步加剧了排位格局的混乱。

Norris claimed pole, with this year’s new champion, Kimi Antonelli, alongside in his Mercedes. Row two was filled with the unusual sight of Liam Lawson’s Racing Bull and Nico Hulkenberg’s Audi, followed by Red Bull’s Isack Hadjar and Oliver Bearman’s Haas. Next was George Russell in the other Mercedes, then the even more unusual sight of Fernando Alonso’s Aston Martin, normally found much farther back. One could say the same about William’s Alex Albon, who qualified ninth ahead of the second McLaren of Oscar Piastri.

诺里斯夺得杆位，今年的新科冠军基米·安东内利驾驶梅赛德斯赛车位列其旁。第二排出现了罕见的景象：利亚姆·劳森的红牛二队赛车和尼科·霍肯伯格的奥迪赛车，紧随其后的是红牛车队的伊萨克·哈贾尔和哈斯车队的奥利弗·贝尔曼。接下来是另一辆梅赛德斯赛车乔治·拉塞尔，再往后是费尔南多·阿隆索的阿斯顿·马丁，这非常罕见，因为他通常排在更靠后的位置。威廉姆斯车队的亚历克斯·阿尔本排在第九，领先于迈凯伦的另一位车手奥斯卡·皮亚斯特里，情况也大抵如此。

It looked like it was going wrong for Norris right from the start of the race. Antonelli made a better start and passed him into turn 1. Lawson followed through into second place at the next corner. But Norris kept his head down, and by lap six, he was past the Racing Bull, with about five seconds to make up on Antonelli. Well-timed pit stops saw Norris switch to the right tires at the right time, and he made none of his rivals’ mistakes, even when the rain returned in the final few laps—at least until the podium ceremony, when he tried to fizz his champagne bottle but instead shattered it.

比赛一开始，诺里斯似乎就陷入了困境。安东内利起步更好，在1号弯超越了他。劳森紧随其后，在下一个弯道升至第二。但诺里斯稳住阵脚，到第六圈时已经超越了红牛二队，此时他与安东内利之间还有约五秒的差距。凭借精准的进站时机，诺里斯在正确的时间换上了正确的轮胎，没有犯下任何竞争对手那样的错误，即使在最后几圈雨水重返赛道时也是如此——至少在领奖台仪式之前是这样，当时他试图喷洒香槟，结果却把瓶子弄碎了。

Keeping one’s tires in the right operating window was vitally important in Brazil, but more than a few struggled with it. The Mercedes duo appeared to struggle to generate sufficient tire temperatures, and their cars looked difficult to handle, prompting plenty of complaints from both Russell and Antonelli to the garage. Russell had been progressing well and ran in the top three early in the race but spun after leaving the pit lane for his first stop, ending up facing the wrong way.

在巴西，将轮胎保持在正确的工作温度区间至关重要，但不少车手都为此挣扎。梅赛德斯双雄似乎难以让轮胎达到足够的温度，赛车看起来难以操控，这引发了拉塞尔和安东内利对车队的多次抱怨。拉塞尔在比赛初期表现良好，一度处于前三名，但在第一次进站离开维修区后发生打滑，导致赛车方向反转。

A few laps later, Antonelli spun his own car from the lead, this time going into turn 1, letting Norris back into first. Antonelli then did it again four laps later, ending fourth. Eventually, the Mercedes finished with Russell in fourth and Antonelli in fifth. But the team sealed its Constructors’ Championship, so there was still plenty of celebrating despite the relatively mediocre results (by Mercedes’ high standards).

几圈后，安东内利在领先位置发生打滑，这次是在1号弯，让诺里斯重回第一。四圈后，安东内利再次打滑，最终排名第四。最终，梅赛德斯车队以拉塞尔第四、安东内利第五的成绩完赛。但车队已经锁定了车队总冠军，因此尽管成绩相对平庸（以梅赛德斯的高标准来看），车队依然有充分的理由庆祝。

It’s worth watching the in-car footage from the first couple of laps from both Hamilton and Verstappen, who lined up 20th and 21st out of 22 cars. By the end of lap 1, Hamilton was already in ninth and making things look easy—then again, Brazil was his 400th F1 race start, and he has won more than a quarter of them. But within four laps, his charge had been blunted. Getting his rear tires up to temperature was key to making up so much ground, but with the track drying, the intermediate tires everyone started the race on had a finite life.

值得一看的是汉密尔顿和维斯塔潘前几圈的车载画面，他们分别排在22辆赛车中的第20和第21位。第一圈结束时，汉密尔顿已经升至第九，看起来游刃有余——毕竟这是他在F1的第400场比赛，而他赢得了其中超过四分之一的胜利。但四圈之内，他的冲锋势头就减弱了。让后轮达到工作温度是追赶的关键，但随着赛道变干，所有人起步时使用的半雨胎寿命有限。

With his used up, Hamilton started sliding back down the order. On two occasions, he ended up racing his teammate Charles Leclerc, who started mid-pack with his own penalty. Unlike in Mexico, Italy, or Holland, the two red cars were able to play nice with each other. But neither managed their tires well, and the best they could do on the road was seventh (Leclerc) and ninth (Hamilton), which became eighth for Leclerc after he was given a five-second penalty for rejoining the track unsafely after running off track while battling Lawson.

随着轮胎耗尽，汉密尔顿开始掉队。他有两次与队友查尔斯·勒克莱尔展开较量，后者因受罚从中游起步。与在墨西哥、意大利或荷兰不同，这两辆红色赛车这次配合得很好。但两人都没能处理好轮胎，最终在赛道上的最好成绩分别是第七（勒克莱尔）和第九（汉密尔顿）。勒克莱尔在与劳森缠斗时冲出赛道，因不安全重返赛道被罚五秒，最终排名降至第八。

Verstappen’s first lap wasn’t quite as good as Hamilton’s—the Red Bull was also never entirely within its setup window all weekend. But taking longer to turn on the rear tires probably contributed to their longevity. While Hamilton’s charge through the pack was blunted, Verstappen was in.
