The global pledge to use AI for climate good is a cruel joke. A pledge to make AI less of an environmental disaster is floating around United Nations climate negotiations this month, but it’s just lip service.

利用人工智能造福气候的全球承诺是一个残酷的笑话。本月，一项旨在减少人工智能环境灾难的承诺正在联合国气候谈判中流传，但这不过是空谈。

At this point, reading through the latest splashy pledge to use AI for the good of the environment feels like downing a nonalcoholic beer. Admittedly, I’m suspicious from the onset. How can this be good? But I love beer and our planet enough to give it a whirl — and hey, why not try a little harm reduction?

在这一点上，阅读关于利用人工智能造福环境的最新宏大承诺，感觉就像在喝无酒精啤酒。诚然，我从一开始就持怀疑态度。这怎么可能是好事呢？但我热爱啤酒和我们的星球，足以让我尝试一下——嘿，为什么不尝试减少一点危害呢？

Negotiators from more than 190 countries around the world are in Antalya, Turkey, this month for the annual United Nations climate summit, called COP31. Turkey previewed its Antalya Artificial Intelligence Pledge ahead of the convening, inviting delegates from other countries to endorse the pledge before its official launch on November 16th.

来自全球 190 多个国家的谈判代表本月齐聚土耳其安塔利亚，参加名为 COP31 的年度联合国气候峰会。土耳其在会议召开前预告了其《安塔利亚人工智能承诺》，并邀请其他国家的代表在 11 月 16 日正式发布前签署该承诺。

The pledge actually includes some common sense measures that could curb planet-heating pollution from energy-hungry AI data centers. Unfortunately, like that nonalcoholic beer, it’s deeply unsatisfying.

该承诺实际上包含了一些常识性措施，可以遏制能源密集型人工智能数据中心造成的全球变暖污染。不幸的是，就像那杯无酒精啤酒一样，它让人深感不满。

At first glance, many of the ingredients you’d expect from a real commitment on AI’s environmental impact are here. It sets out to ensure that data centers are increasingly powered by clean energy, calling for internationally recognized standards for responsible AI development. Similarly, it pushes for reporting requirements so that there’s greater transparency into the impact data centers — which run primarily on fossil fuels — already have on the environment.

乍一看，你对一项关于人工智能环境影响的真正承诺所期望的许多要素都在这里。它旨在确保数据中心越来越多地由清洁能源供电，并呼吁建立国际公认的负责任人工智能开发标准。同样，它还推动了报告要求，以便对主要依靠化石燃料运行的数据中心已经对环境产生的影响有更高的透明度。

And yet, there is no there there because, as the text states clearly, “these objectives of the pledge are voluntary and non-prescriptive, create no new obligations, and are to be implemented in accordance with national circumstances.” In other words, these are voluntary commitments that countries can choose to follow or not follow based on their whims.

然而，这其中并无实质内容，因为正如文本明确指出的那样，“这些承诺目标是自愿和非强制性的，不产生新的义务，并根据各国国情实施。”换句话说，这些是各国可以根据自己的意愿选择遵守或不遵守的自愿承诺。

There’s also no way this pledge will garner the support of the most influential player in this game. The US has way more data centers than any other country, but Donald Trump has pulled the nation out of the global climate accord and has no official delegates at the summit in Turkey.

此外，这项承诺也不可能获得该领域最具影响力参与者的支持。美国的各种数据中心数量远超其他任何国家，但唐纳德·特朗普已让美国退出了全球气候协定，且在土耳其峰会上没有官方代表。

The last point in the pledge is to ensure that advances in generative AI can be used to create more renewable energy and reduce greenhouse gas emissions, particularly in economically developing countries. To that end, research published in a Nature journal in August found that AI causes more carbon emissions by boosting fossil fuel production than it would help avoid through gains in renewable energy.

承诺的最后一点是确保生成式人工智能的进步可用于创造更多可再生能源并减少温室气体排放，特别是在经济发展中国家。为此，8 月份发表在《自然》杂志上的一项研究发现，人工智能通过促进化石燃料生产所产生的碳排放，远超其通过可再生能源收益所能避免的排放量。

When it comes down to it, this pledge offers a placebo effect at best. In the face of data centers demanding tremendous amounts of electricity and spurring the development of more fossil fuel infrastructure, negotiators can’t be seen as doing nothing about the issue. And with global average temperatures still on the rise, we could all use an emotional support beverage.

归根结底，这项承诺充其量只能提供一种安慰剂效应。面对数据中心对电力的大量需求以及对更多化石燃料基础设施发展的刺激，谈判代表们不能表现得对这一问题无所作为。随着全球平均气温持续上升，我们都需要一种情感慰藉饮料。
