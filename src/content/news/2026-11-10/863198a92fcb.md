# We Audited Our Own Server with Claude Mythos 5.1

Originally published on Transgenia. This is the syndicated version on dev.to. On 7 November 2026, Anthropic approved Transgenia for Defense Access in its Cyber Verification Program. Two days later we asked Claude Mythos 5.1, the Anthropic model offered only to verified organizations, to review our own production server. In 26 minutes we had a report with 13 findings and a fix plan. Later, at two moments, a safety classifier stopped its responses. This is the full story: what we did, what we found, where the model stopped and what it can actually do.

该文章最初发布于 Transgenia，这是 dev.to 上的联合发布版本。2026 年 11 月 7 日，Anthropic 批准 Transgenia 加入其网络验证计划（Cyber Verification Program）并获得防御访问权限（Defense Access）。两天后，我们要求仅向经过验证的组织提供的 Anthropic 模型 Claude Mythos 5.1 对我们的生产服务器进行审查。在 26 分钟内，我们获得了一份包含 13 项发现和修复计划的报告。随后，安全分类器在两个时刻停止了模型的响应。这是完整的故事：我们做了什么、发现了什么、模型在哪里停止以及它实际上能做什么。

Short answer. Claude Mythos 5.1 is useful for a company's defensive cybersecurity: inventorying what runs on a server, reviewing configuration, checking versions against public vulnerability databases and proposing a plan, always on systems you own and with authorization. Transgenia, an organization verified by Anthropic with Defense Access since 7 November 2026, used it as a proof of concept on its own production server: it found 2 high severity findings, 7 medium and 4 low, and applied one fix with human approval. Its safety classifiers stopped two responses. What you cannot do with this model is wider than what you can, and it pays to know that before you start.

简短的回答是：Claude Mythos 5.1 对公司的防御性网络安全很有用：它可以盘点服务器上运行的内容、审查配置、对照公共漏洞数据库检查版本并提出计划，前提是始终在您拥有且获得授权的系统上进行。自 2026 年 11 月 7 日起获得 Anthropic 防御访问权限验证的 Transgenia 组织，将其作为概念验证在自己的生产服务器上进行了测试：它发现了 2 项高严重性、7 项中等严重性和 4 项低严重性的问题，并在人工批准下应用了一项修复。其安全分类器停止了两次响应。使用该模型时，您不能做的事情比能做的事情多得多，在开始之前了解这一点非常重要。

1. The whole case, from access to lessons learned. What is Claude Mythos 5.1 and who can use it? According to Anthropic's documentation, Claude Mythos 5.1 is the same model as Claude Fable 5.1, available only to organizations verified through Anthropic's verification programs, such as the Cyber Verification Program. It was released on 1 September 2026 and works with a context window of one million tokens. Transgenia is one of those verified organizations.

1. 整个案例，从访问权限到经验教训。什么是 Claude Mythos 5.1，谁可以使用它？根据 Anthropic 的文档，Claude Mythos 5.1 与 Claude Fable 5.1 是同一模型，仅提供给通过 Anthropic 验证计划（如网络验证计划）验证的组织。它于 2026 年 9 月 1 日发布，上下文窗口为一百万个 token。Transgenia 就是这些经过验证的组织之一。

The safeguards are not optional. Anthropic documents that its models include safety classifiers that can decline a request, and that the cybersecurity category covers anything that could enable harm, such as malware or exploit development. The same documentation warns that benign cybersecurity work can also trigger it. Anthropic sets those safeguards per model and per category, and its verification program requires every test to be authorized in advance by someone with authority over the systems.

安全防护措施并非可选项。Anthropic 的文档记录显示，其模型包含可以拒绝请求的安全分类器，且网络安全类别涵盖任何可能导致伤害的行为，例如恶意软件或漏洞开发。同一文档警告称，良性的网络安全工作也可能触发这些防护。Anthropic 针对每个模型和类别设置了这些防护措施，其验证计划要求每次测试都必须事先由对系统拥有权限的人员授权。

What does it mean that Transgenia is a verified organization? On 7 November 2026, Anthropic notified Transgenia of its approval for Defense Access in the Cyber Verification Program, effective immediately. According to that notice, Defense Access is for teams that defend systems they own or maintain: vulnerability triage and verification, malware analysis and reverse engineering, detection engineering and incident response. Authorized penetration testing and red teaming belong to another tier of the program, which we do not have today.

Transgenia 成为经过验证的组织意味着什么？2026 年 11 月 7 日，Anthropic 通知 Transgenia 其已获准加入网络验证计划的防御访问权限，立即生效。根据该通知，防御访问权限适用于防御其拥有或维护系统的团队：包括漏洞分类与验证、恶意软件分析与逆向工程、检测工程以及事件响应。授权的渗透测试和红队演练属于该计划的另一个层级，我们目前尚未获得该层级权限。

It is worth saying what it does not mean. It is not a certification or an endorsement of our services by Anthropic. It is a verification that lets us use its most capable models, under its safeguards, for defense. This audit was our first proof of concept with that access.

值得说明的是它不意味着什么。它不是 Anthropic 对我们服务的认证或背书。这是一种验证，允许我们在其安全防护下使用其最强大的模型进行防御。此次审计是我们获得该权限后的首次概念验证。

2. Transgenia, a verified organization: what its access covers. What did we set out to do, and what did we leave out? The question was specific: does any native application on our production server, PHP and Python in particular, have a significant vulnerability? The scope was written down before we started. Our own infrastructure only, identification only, and no change without a person's approval. Intrusive testing, brute force, exploitation and any data download were left out.

2. Transgenia，一个经过验证的组织：其访问权限涵盖范围。我们打算做什么，又排除了什么？问题很具体：我们生产服务器上的任何原生应用程序（特别是 PHP 和 Python）是否存在重大漏洞？在开始之前，我们已书面确定了范围。仅限我们自己的基础设施，仅限识别，未经人员批准不得进行任何更改。侵入性测试、暴力破解、漏洞利用和任何数据下载均被排除在外。

3. What was in scope and what was left out. How did we work? Identify before touching. The model worked in Claude Code with read access to the server and followed six steps. To check versions it used two public sources: Packagist security advisories for PHP dependencies and the OSV database for Python ones. The evidence was stored without passwords or process arguments. Where a configuration file held secrets, we recorded that they existed, not their value.

3. 范围之内与范围之外。我们是如何工作的？先识别，后操作。该模型在 Claude Code 中以服务器读取权限运行，并遵循六个步骤。为了检查版本，它使用了两个公共来源：用于 PHP 依赖项的 Packagist 安全公告和用于 Python 依赖项的 OSV 数据库。证据存储时未包含密码或进程参数。如果配置文件中包含机密信息，我们仅记录其存在，而不记录其具体值。

4. Identify before touching: six read-only steps. The review covered the PHP applications, the Python services, the containers and the network layer with its firewall. Each finding was classified by severity and kept with the evidence behind it.

4. 先识别，后操作：六个只读步骤。审查涵盖了 PHP 应用程序、Python 服务、容器以及带有防火墙的网络层。每项发现都按严重程度进行了分类，并保留了其背后的证据。

5. What we reviewed on the production server. What did we find? Thirteen findings: 2 of high severity, 7 of medium severity and 4 of low severity.

5. 我们在生产服务器上审查了什么？我们发现了什么？十三项发现：2 项高严重性，7 项中等严重性，4 项低严重性。

6. Thirteen findings, by severity. We do not publish the details of findings that are still open. Describing an unfixed weakness precisely hands it to whoever is looking for it. We can share the patterns worth looking for on any server, because variants of them showed up on ours.

6. 十三项发现，按严重程度分类。我们不会发布尚未修复的发现细节。精确描述一个未修复的弱点等于将其拱手送给寻找它的人。我们可以分享任何服务器上都值得关注的模式，因为我们服务器上也出现了它们的变体。

7. Five patterns worth looking for on any server. A useful report also says what not to touch. The model recorded what was done well: our own website form code, the hardened PHP configuration, key-only SSH access and the rules that deny access to sensitive files. How did we move from detection to fixing? With a simple rule: no change without approval, backup and verification. A person on the team reviewed the report and approved two fixes.

7. 任何服务器上都值得关注的五种模式。一份有用的报告也会说明哪些地方不要动。模型记录了做得好的地方：我们自己的网站表单代码、加固的 PHP 配置、仅限密钥的 SSH 访问以及拒绝访问敏感文件的规则。我们是如何从检测转向修复的？遵循一个简单的规则：未经批准、备份和验证，不得进行任何更改。团队中的一名成员审查了报告并批准了两项修复。

8. No change without approval, backup and verification. 9. Timeline of 9 November 2026 (UTC). The fix that was applied switched off a debug mode that showed internal information to any visitor of an application. The configuration was backed up, switched to production mode and verified from outside so that the public page no longer showed it. This reduces exposure, but it does not close the finding: the application needs an update, which remains in the plan. The second approved fix was never applied.

8. 未经批准、备份和验证，不得进行任何更改。9. 2026 年 11 月 9 日（UTC）的时间线。所应用的修复关闭了一个调试模式，该模式会向应用程序的任何访问者显示内部信息。配置已备份，切换到生产模式，并从外部进行了验证，以确保公共页面不再显示该信息。这减少了暴露，但并未关闭该发现：应用程序需要更新，这仍在计划中。第二项批准的修复从未应用。
