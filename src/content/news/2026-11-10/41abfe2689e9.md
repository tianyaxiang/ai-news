Hotel and resort giant Wyndham has acknowledged a cybersecurity incident after a good-faith security researcher discovered a bug that allowed anyone to access customers’ personal information.

酒店和度假村巨头温德姆（Wyndham）承认发生了一起网络安全事件，此前一位善意的安全研究人员发现了一个漏洞，该漏洞允许任何人访问客户的个人信息。

Security researcher Robert Wiggins at What Security exclusively told TechCrunch that he found a password inside the source code of Wyndham’s Android app, which granted access to an internal company database, hosted by tech company Salesforce.

What Security 的安全研究员罗伯特·威金斯（Robert Wiggins）独家告诉 TechCrunch，他在温德姆安卓应用程序的源代码中发现了一个密码，该密码可以访问由科技公司 Salesforce 托管的内部公司数据库。

Wiggins told TechCrunch that the credential exposed personal information including customer names, email addresses, and Wyndham member numbers. The database also stores customer login tokens (called JWTs) that keep customers logged-in to their apps without having to repeatedly enter their own passwords. The database also contained Wyndham files and documents.

威金斯告诉 TechCrunch，该凭据泄露了包括客户姓名、电子邮件地址和温德姆会员号码在内的个人信息。该数据库还存储了客户登录令牌（称为 JWT），使客户无需重复输入密码即可保持登录状态。数据库中还包含温德姆的文件和文档。

According to Wiggins, the database had millions of entries, but he was unable to determine an accurate number of exposed customer records. Wyndham touts some 129 million enrolled rewards members.

据威金斯称，该数据库有数百万条记录，但他无法确定泄露客户记录的确切数量。温德姆声称拥有约 1.29 亿名注册奖励会员。

The researcher said Wyndham has no way to report security flaws, and asked for TechCrunch’s help in alerting the company. Soon after TechCrunch made contact with Wyndham’s cybersecurity staff, the exposed credential was revoked, preventing any further access to the database.

该研究人员表示，温德姆没有报告安全漏洞的渠道，因此请求 TechCrunch 协助通知该公司。在 TechCrunch 与温德姆的网络安全人员取得联系后不久，泄露的凭据即被撤销，从而阻止了对数据库的进一步访问。

“Our team has taken steps to address the issue,” a Wyndham spokesperson told TechCrunch. “Our security teams have no evidence of any unauthorized access,” the statement added, without providing any further details about the claim.

“我们的团队已采取措施解决该问题，”温德姆的一位发言人告诉 TechCrunch。“我们的安全团队没有发现任何未经授权访问的证据，”声明补充道，但未提供有关该说法的任何进一步细节。

Wyndham’s spokesperson would not say, when asked, whether the company had the technical means, such as server logs, to determine if anyone had access to the customer database prior to Wiggins’ discovery.

当被问及公司是否具备技术手段（如服务器日志）来确定在威金斯发现之前是否有人访问过客户数据库时，温德姆的发言人拒绝置评。

TechCrunch also asked Wyndham whether it planned to notify customers whose information was exposed, or disclose the incident to U.S. regulators, such as filing a notice with the U.S. Securities and Exchange Commission, as public companies are required to do for material cybersecurity incidents. Wyndham would not say.

TechCrunch 还询问温德姆是否计划通知信息被泄露的客户，或向美国监管机构披露该事件（例如向美国证券交易委员会提交通知），正如上市公司在发生重大网络安全事件时所要求的那样。温德姆拒绝回应。

Wyndham’s spokesperson also would not say for how long the password was embedded in its customer app, or how Wyndham plans to ship a long-term fix for the app.

温德姆的发言人也没有说明该密码在客户应用程序中嵌入了多久，或者温德姆计划如何为该应用程序提供长期修复方案。

App and software developers generally avoid including passwords or credentials in publicly released software and apps — a process known as “hardcoding” — as anyone with a copy of the software can extract the password and use it to access its associated server or database.

应用程序和软件开发人员通常会避免在公开发布的软件和应用程序中包含密码或凭据（这一过程被称为“硬编码”），因为任何拥有该软件副本的人都可以提取密码并利用它访问相关的服务器或数据库。

Given the simplicity with which credentials can be abused by anyone, many cases of hardcoded credentials are automatically scored with a maximum vulnerability severity rating.

鉴于凭据可以被任何人轻易滥用，许多硬编码凭据的情况会被自动评定为最高漏洞严重等级。

For Wiggins, this is his second public discovery of hardcoded credentials in two weeks, after finding a near-identical vulnerability in Applebee’s app that exposed customer data. Wiggins relied on AI tools for both discoveries, which make it easier to identify simple security weaknesses, such as hardcoded credentials, by scouring thousands of lines of decompiled app code every second.

对于威金斯来说，这是他在两周内第二次公开发现硬编码凭据，此前他在 Applebee 的应用程序中发现了几乎相同的漏洞，导致客户数据泄露。威金斯在两次发现中都依赖了人工智能工具，这些工具通过每秒扫描数千行反编译的应用程序代码，更容易识别出硬编码凭据等简单的安全弱点。

When asked about Wyndham’s response, Wiggins told TechCrunch that he was glad the company fixed the issue, but noted that without visibility into any past activity, someone with malicious intent could have leaked large amounts of customers’ personal information.

当被问及温德姆的反应时，威金斯告诉 TechCrunch，他很高兴该公司解决了这个问题，但指出由于无法查看过去的活动，别有用心的人可能已经泄露了大量客户的个人信息。

The company would not say whether it planned to set up a vulnerability disclosure program to allow outside security researchers and hackers to privately report security vulnerabilities. Michael Francess, Wyndham’s cybersecurity director who responded to the incident, told TechCrunch that researchers can alert [email protected] with details of potential security issues.

该公司没有说明是否计划建立漏洞披露计划，以允许外部安全研究人员和黑客私下报告安全漏洞。负责处理此事件的温德姆网络安全总监迈克尔·弗朗西斯（Michael Francess）告诉 TechCrunch，研究人员可以将潜在安全问题的详细信息发送至 [email protected] 进行提醒。

The company’s spokesperson would not say whether it plans to change its website to allow researchers to alert the company.

该公司发言人没有说明是否计划更改其网站，以便研究人员能够向公司发出警报。

This is the latest known security incident at Wyndham in recent years. In 2024, a security researcher found several Wyndham hotel check-in desks were running remote-access spyware that was leaking screenshots of guest records and booking information to the open web.

这是温德姆近年来已知的最新安全事件。2024 年，一位安全研究人员发现几家温德姆酒店的前台正在运行远程访问间谍软件，该软件将客人记录和预订信息的截图泄露到了公共网络上。

Soon after our reporting, the spyware app found on the hotel computers, called pcTattletale, was hacked and its banks of customer data stolen, forcing the company’s owner, Bryan Fleming, to shut down the company. Fleming, a former resident of Michigan, was later arrested by federal authorities and convicted of making, selling, and advertising unlawful spyware.

在我们的报道发布后不久，在酒店电脑上发现的名为 pcTattletale 的间谍软件遭到黑客攻击，其客户数据库被盗，迫使该公司所有者布莱恩·弗莱明（Bryan Fleming）关闭了公司。弗莱明曾是密歇根州居民，后来被联邦当局逮捕，并因制造、销售和宣传非法间谍软件而被定罪。
