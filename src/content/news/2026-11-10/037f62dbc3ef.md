YouTube is officially returning to the Billboard charts, ending a disagreement over how music streams should count toward chart rankings. Announced Monday, YouTube is set to resume sharing its streaming data with Billboard in 2027.

YouTube 正式回归 Billboard 排行榜，结束了关于音乐流媒体播放量应如何计入排行榜排名的分歧。周一宣布，YouTube 将于 2027 年恢复与 Billboard 分享其流媒体数据。

The decision follows recent changes to Billboard’s chart formula, which now gives more weight to ad-supported streams. The disagreement began in December 2025, when YouTube said it would stop sharing its streaming data with Billboard. The company argued that the chart formula favored paid subscriptions while not giving enough credit to fans who listen for free with ads.

这一决定是在 Billboard 近期调整了排行榜计算公式之后做出的，新公式提高了广告支持流媒体的权重。双方的分歧始于 2025 年 12 月，当时 YouTube 表示将停止与 Billboard 分享流媒体数据。该公司认为，当时的排行榜公式偏袒付费订阅，而没有给予通过广告免费收听的粉丝足够的认可。

At the time, Lyor Cohen, YouTube’s global head of music, criticized the system in a blog post, saying it didn’t reflect how people listen to music today. “Billboard uses an outdated formula that weights subscription-supported streams higher than ad-supported. This doesn’t reflect how fans engage with music today and ignores the massive engagement from fans who don’t have a subscription. Streaming is the primary way people experience music, making up 84% of U.S. recorded music revenue,” Cohen wrote.

当时，YouTube 全球音乐主管 Lyor Cohen 在一篇博文中批评了该系统，称其无法反映当今人们收听音乐的方式。“Billboard 使用了一种过时的公式，将订阅支持的流媒体权重设置得高于广告支持的流媒体。这不能反映当今粉丝与音乐互动的方式，也忽视了没有订阅的粉丝所带来的巨大参与度。流媒体是人们体验音乐的主要方式，占美国录制音乐收入的 84%，”Cohen 写道。

Under Billboard’s previous formula, 1,000 paid streams counted the same as 2,500 ad-supported streams. YouTube felt this gave too much importance to paid listening, especially when millions of fans discover and support artists through free streaming.

在 Billboard 此前的公式下，1,000 次付费流媒体播放量等同于 2,500 次广告支持的流媒体播放量。YouTube 认为这过分强调了付费收听，尤其是考虑到数百万粉丝是通过免费流媒体发现并支持艺人的。

Billboard has now updated its formula, reducing the gap between the two types of streams. Under the new system, 1,000 paid streams will equal 1,500 ad-supported streams when calculating album consumption units, compared with 2,500 under the previous formula. Paid streams will still count for more, but free streams will now have a bigger impact on chart positions.

Billboard 现已更新其公式，缩小了两种流媒体类型之间的差距。根据新系统，在计算专辑消费单位时，1,000 次付费流媒体播放量将等同于 1,500 次广告支持的流媒体播放量，而此前为 2,500 次。付费流媒体的权重依然更高，但免费流媒体现在对排行榜排名的影响将有所增加。

YouTube’s return could make a difference for many artists, especially independent musicians and those with large international fan bases. Music videos, live performances, lyric videos, and other content on the platform help artists reach new audiences. With YouTube’s data back in the mix, more of that activity will now count toward their Billboard performance.

YouTube 的回归可能会对许多艺人产生影响，尤其是独立音乐人和拥有庞大国际粉丝群的艺人。平台上的音乐视频、现场表演、歌词视频和其他内容有助于艺人触达新的受众。随着 YouTube 数据重新纳入统计，这些活动中将有更多部分计入他们的 Billboard 表现。

For Billboard, getting YouTube’s data back also means having a more complete picture of how people listen to and discover music. With so many fans using the platform, leaving its streams out meant missing a major part of music consumption.

对于 Billboard 而言，重新获得 YouTube 的数据也意味着能够更全面地了解人们如何收听和发现音乐。由于有如此多的粉丝使用该平台，将其流媒体数据排除在外意味着错失了音乐消费的重要组成部分。
