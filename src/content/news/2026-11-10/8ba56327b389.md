Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.08780 (cs) [Submitted on 5 Nov 2026] Title: Guardrail Erosion in Domain Fine-Tuned Large Language Models: A Paired Audit of Knowledge-Centric Abstention in Biomedical LLMs Authors: Stephen Peng, Vineet Jagadeesan Nair, Noah Broestl, Anne Friederike Kleppe, Steven Mills, Adi Zolotov.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.08780 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 5 日] 标题：领域微调大语言模型中的护栏侵蚀：生物医学大语言模型中以知识为中心的弃权配对审计 作者：Stephen Peng, Vineet Jagadeesan Nair, Noah Broestl, Anne Friederike Kleppe, Steven Mills, Adi Zolotov。

Abstract: The number of domain fine-tuned models, particularly in the biomedical domain, is rapidly expanding. Fine-tuning promises high-performing models at a fraction of the cost of training a base model, but it can also produce unintended behavioral changes. Multiple studies have shown that safety guardrails can erode with fine-tuning, yet large-scale studies across model architectures, data mixtures, and fine-tuning methodologies remain limited.

摘要：领域微调模型的数量，特别是在生物医学领域，正在迅速增加。微调承诺以基础模型训练成本的一小部分实现高性能模型，但它也可能产生意想不到的行为变化。多项研究表明，安全护栏可能会随着微调而侵蚀，但跨模型架构、数据混合和微调方法的大规模研究仍然有限。

Where prior work in this space has studied value-centric refusal, this work focuses on knowledge-centric abstention. We audit 25 biomedical domain fine-tuned large language models against 11 paired base checkpoints on MedAbstain and ClinDet-Bench to evaluate the impact of fine-tuning on knowledge-centric abstention.

在这一领域，先前的研究主要关注以价值为中心的拒绝，而本研究侧重于以知识为中心的弃权。我们针对 MedAbstain 和 ClinDet-Bench 对 25 个生物医学领域微调大语言模型与 11 个配对基础检查点进行了审计，以评估微调对以知识为中心的弃权的影响。

We find that defined appropriate abstention measured by these benchmarks, already low in base models (median 8.3% on MedAbstain), falls further after fine-tuning (median 4.2%, decreasing in 21 of 25 pairs on MedAbstain), and that the magnitude and direction of change are heterogeneous across pairs. These results motivate fine-tuning methods that improve domain-specific capability while preserving appropriate caution.

我们发现，通过这些基准测试衡量的定义明确的适当弃权率在基础模型中已经很低（MedAbstain 中位数为 8.3%），在微调后进一步下降（中位数为 4.2%，在 MedAbstain 的 25 个配对中有 21 个下降），并且变化的幅度和方向在不同配对间存在异质性。这些结果促使我们需要开发既能提高领域特定能力又能保持适当谨慎的微调方法。
