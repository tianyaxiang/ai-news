This week, as countries meet in Antalya, Turkey for the United Nations climate conference, one topic likely to dominate the conversation is artificial intelligence. The host has already called on countries to commit to ensuring that AI is designed, powered, and deployed in ways that support climate action.

本周，随着各国齐聚土耳其安塔利亚参加联合国气候变化大会，人工智能很可能成为主导讨论的话题之一。东道国已经呼吁各国做出承诺，确保人工智能的设计、供能和部署方式能够支持气候行动。

But so far, industries around the world aren’t doing nearly enough to harness the technology to actually drive down the greenhouse-gas emissions heating up the planet, despite some very optimistic claims from AI companies. In 2024, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman declared that AI would unleash “astounding triumphs,” including “fixing the climate.” The year before, in a 2023 op-ed, the head of sustainability at Google and her coauthor wrote that AI could reduce global emissions 5% to 10% by 2030.

然而到目前为止，尽管人工智能公司做出了一些非常乐观的声明，但全球各行各业在利用该技术真正减少导致地球变暖的温室气体排放方面，做得还远远不够。2024年，OpenAI首席执行官萨姆·奥特曼（Sam Altman）宣称，人工智能将带来“惊人的胜利”，包括“修复气候”。在此前一年的2023年，谷歌可持续发展负责人及其合著者在一篇评论文章中写道，到2030年，人工智能可以将全球排放量减少5%至10%。

Some academic researchers have examined these AI-related climate claims. A peer-reviewed study published last year, led by researchers at the London School of Economics’ Grantham Research Institute and the firm Systemiq, estimated that AI could help cut global emissions by the equivalent of 3.2 to 5.4 billion tons of carbon dioxide annually by 2035—roughly on par with the US’s annual energy-related emissions on the high end, even after subtracting AI’s own energy footprint. Those potential gains are mostly concentrated in three sectors: power, meat and dairy, and vehicles.

一些学术研究人员对这些与人工智能相关的气候主张进行了审查。去年发表的一项同行评审研究由伦敦政治经济学院格兰瑟姆气候变化与环境研究所和Systemiq公司的研究人员领导，该研究估计，到2035年，人工智能每年可帮助减少相当于32亿至54亿吨二氧化碳的全球排放量——即使扣除人工智能自身的能源足迹，这一高位估值也大致相当于美国每年的能源相关排放量。这些潜在收益主要集中在三个领域：电力、肉类和乳制品，以及交通工具。

But projections like these are only as good as our willingness to test them against present-day reality. So is there evidence that the largest companies in these three sectors are beginning to integrate AI in line with the study’s projections, and if so, do the results match expectations? Or is the 2035 number still mostly aspirational?

但这类预测的价值取决于我们是否愿意用当下的现实去检验它们。那么，是否有证据表明这三个领域的大型企业正开始按照研究的预测整合人工智能？如果是这样，结果是否符合预期？还是说2035年的目标数字仍然主要是一种愿景？

Companies across these sectors have announced plenty of initial efforts to incorporate AI into various business practices. But an AI-assisted review of public information from significant market actors in each sector, conducted and reviewed by the second author of this piece, found that few have announced they’re pursuing the specific emissions-reducing routes that informed the projections in the study—and fewer still claim they’ve used them to achieve meaningful climate progress.

这些行业的公司已经宣布了许多将人工智能纳入各种商业实践的初步努力。然而，由本文第二作者进行并审查的一项针对各行业重要市场参与者公开信息的人工智能辅助审查发现，很少有公司宣布他们正在追求研究预测中所述的特定减排路径，更少有公司声称他们已经利用这些路径取得了有意义的气候进展。

The power sector is where the earlier study’s underlying math is most concrete: AI-driven forecasting of supply and demand could increase the amount of renewable electricity that reaches the grid from already-built solar and wind facilities by up to 20%, which could prevent an estimated 1.8 billion tons of emissions a year. Yet of the grid operators reviewed in China, the United States, and Europe, only one deployed a known meaningful, working AI program of this sort, according to the analysis.

电力行业是上述研究基础数据最具体的部分：人工智能驱动的供需预测可以将已建成的太阳能和风能设施输送到电网的可再生电力增加多达20%，这每年可防止约18亿吨的排放。然而，根据分析，在对中国、美国和欧洲的电网运营商进行审查后发现，只有一家部署了此类已知且有效的、具有实际意义的人工智能程序。

A partnership between the UK’s National Grid ESO and Open Climate Fix halved the grid operator’s solar-forecast error (the difference between the amount of incoming sunlight predicted and the amount that actually reaches solar panels), avoiding hundreds of thousands of tons of carbon dioxide a year. Beyond this example in the UK, though, many other grid operators in the EU, the US, and China had little to show in the public record in terms of the AI integration envisioned by the study.

英国国家电网ESO与Open Climate Fix之间的合作将该电网运营商的太阳能预测误差（预测的入射阳光量与实际到达太阳能电池板的阳光量之间的差异）减半，每年避免了数十万吨的二氧化碳排放。然而，除了英国的这个例子之外，欧盟、美国和中国的许多其他电网运营商在公开记录中几乎没有展示出研究中所设想的人工智能整合成果。

The meat and dairy sector offers the starkest contrast between technology readiness and market reality. To achieve the 2035 emission reductions anticipated in the earlier study, AI would need to close the taste and cost gap between traditional proteins and alternative ones, such as lab-grown or plant-based burgers and milk substitutes. In one of the clearest efforts to achieve this, the AI and food tech company NotCo recently formed a joint venture with Kraft Heinz using its platform Giuseppe, which analyzes plant molecular data to pinpoint combinations replicating the taste, texture, and smell of animal products.

肉类和乳制品行业在技术准备程度与市场现实之间形成了最鲜明的对比。为了实现上述研究中预期的2035年减排目标，人工智能需要缩小传统蛋白质与替代蛋白质（如实验室培育或植物基汉堡及牛奶替代品）之间的口味和成本差距。在实现这一目标的最明确努力之一中，人工智能和食品科技公司NotCo最近与卡夫亨氏（Kraft Heinz）成立了一家合资企业，利用其Giuseppe平台分析植物分子数据，以精准找出能够复制动物产品口感、质地和气味的组合。

But few other alternative-protein companies had such a deeply integrated way of using AI in product development, the AI-assisted review suggests. Meanwhile, the sector seems to be facing more fundamental problems, including stubbornly slow mainstream consumer adoption and declining funding levels. Most notably, venture capital flowing into cultivated meat fell from a $989 million peak in 2021 to around $83 million last year.

但人工智能辅助审查显示，很少有其他替代蛋白质公司在产品开发中以如此深度整合的方式使用人工智能。与此同时，该行业似乎正面临更根本的问题，包括主流消费者的接受度增长缓慢，以及资金水平下降。最值得注意的是，流入培育肉领域的风险投资从2021年9.89亿美元的峰值降至去年的约8300万美元。

The transportation sector offers some hopeful examples of the type of AI integration necessary to achieve the study’s projections, particularly among battery manufacturers and ride-sharing platforms. CATL—which controls roughly 40% of the global market for electric-vehicle batteries—launched an AI system trained on more than 100,000 battery design cases and 600 terabytes of test data. It cut the time needed to screen candidate materials by two orders of magnitude, according to the company. Independent researchers also found that the ride-sharing platform Didi Chuxing was able cut per-trip emissions 16% on average by using AI-optimized routing to minimize detours.

交通运输行业提供了一些有望实现研究预测所需的人工智能整合范例，特别是在电池制造商和网约车平台中。控制着全球约40%电动汽车电池市场的宁德时代（CATL）推出了一套人工智能系统，该系统接受了超过10万个电池设计案例和600太字节测试数据的训练。据该公司称，这将其筛选候选材料所需的时间缩短了两个数量级。独立研究人员还发现，网约车平台滴滴出行通过使用人工智能优化路线以减少绕路，平均每单行程排放量减少了16%。

There’s another big problem in the climate case for AI, though: Oil and gas companies are also interested in these tools for many reasons, including the potential to increase production of fossil fuels. Technology firms that committed to sustainability goals in the past should answer questions about how their tools could be used to boost old, dirty energy as well as clean, renewable energy.

然而，人工智能的气候论证还存在另一个大问题：石油和天然气公司也出于多种原因对这些工具感兴趣，包括其增加化石燃料产量的潜力。过去承诺实现可持续发展目标的科技公司，应该回答关于其工具如何被用于促进陈旧、肮脏的能源，以及如何促进清洁、可再生能源的问题。

Finally, it’s also worth noting that integrating AI is just the first step, and it remains unclear to what degree the technology can accelerate the sorts of improvements and innovations envisioned.
