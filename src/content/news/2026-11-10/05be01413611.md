---

Understanding building facades is central to urban digital twins, heritage modelling, and sustainable city analysis, yet existing LiDAR benchmarks stop at geometry and semantic segmentation and offer little support for reasoning about the functional role of each architectural element.

理解建筑立面对于城市数字孪生、遗产建模和可持续城市分析至关重要，然而现有的激光雷达（LiDAR）基准测试仅停留在几何和语义分割层面，对于推理每个建筑元素的功能角色几乎没有提供支持。

We introduce HFX3D, a large-scale urban facade benchmark of approximately 492 million LiDAR points from 26 buildings in Halifax, Canada, captured across four seasons, day and night, and varied weather.

我们引入了 HFX3D，这是一个大规模城市立面基准数据集，包含来自加拿大哈利法克斯 26 栋建筑的约 4.92 亿个激光雷达点，涵盖了四季、昼夜以及多变的天气条件。

Each facade is annotated at three tiers, semantic, instance, and per-instance functional attributes, making it, to our knowledge, the first facade dataset to pair geometry and semantics with functional labels.

每个立面都在语义、实例和实例级功能属性三个层面上进行了标注，据我们所知，这是第一个将几何、语义与功能标签相结合的立面数据集。

Annotations are produced by a semi-automatic pipeline that combines geometry-guided heuristics with expert validation.

标注是通过一种半自动流程生成的，该流程结合了几何引导的启发式方法与专家验证。

We benchmark representative point-based, graph-based, convolutional, and transformer architectures and find that semantic segmentation is already challenging under severe class imbalance, while instance separation of repetitive structures and functional reasoning from geometry are substantially harder.

我们对具有代表性的基于点、基于图、卷积以及 Transformer 的架构进行了基准测试，发现语义分割在严重的类别不平衡下已经极具挑战性，而重复结构的实例分离以及基于几何的功能推理则更为困难。

The benchmark establishes a new foundation for function-aware 3D urban understanding.

该基准测试为具备功能感知能力的 3D 城市理解奠定了新的基础。
