Some galaxies appear to contain all the ingredients needed to make stars, yet have failed to produce almost any. Scientists have puzzled over why for years. Recent findings suggest that these “dark galaxies”—that is, galaxies that contain gas and dark matter but few or no stars—may be lurking throughout the cosmos. Finding them could help astronomers understand why some galaxies form stars while others remain dark, and test the best models of galaxy formation.

有些星系似乎具备了制造恒星所需的所有要素，却几乎没有产生任何恒星。多年来，科学家们一直对其中的原因感到困惑。最近的研究结果表明，这些“暗星系”——即包含气体和暗物质但几乎没有或完全没有恒星的星系——可能潜伏在整个宇宙中。发现它们有助于天文学家理解为什么有些星系会形成恒星而另一些则保持黑暗，并检验现有的星系形成模型。

Researchers’ most successful model of the cosmos, known as Lambda-CDM, assumes the existence of two invisible ingredients: dark energy, the mysterious property thought to be driving the accelerating expansion of the universe, as well as cold dark matter, a hypothetical substance that physicists think makes up about 85 percent of the universe’s matter.

研究人员最成功的宇宙模型，即“Λ-CDM模型”，假设存在两种不可见的成分：暗能量（被认为是驱动宇宙加速膨胀的神秘属性）以及冷暗物质（物理学家认为其构成了宇宙约85%物质的假想物质）。

Clumps of dark matter, called haloes, provide the gravitational scaffolding for galaxies to form and grow, according to the model. Gas collects inside these haloes, eventually cooling and collapsing to form stars. But astronomers are still trying to understand why this process appears to happen in some haloes but not others. One possibility is that some contain vast quantities of hydrogen gas, yet form very few stars. That would make them extremely difficult to spot.

根据该模型，被称为“晕”的暗物质团为星系的形成和生长提供了引力支架。气体聚集在这些晕中，最终冷却并坍缩形成恒星。但天文学家仍在试图理解为什么这一过程似乎发生在某些晕中，却未发生在其他晕中。一种可能性是，有些晕中含有大量的氢气，却几乎不形成恒星。这使得它们极难被发现。

Now, researchers have found a promising candidate for such a dark galaxy and published their findings in the Monthly Notices of the Royal Astronomical Society. The object, known as AGC 322753, contains roughly 2 billion times the sun's mass in hydrogen, yet appears to have no stars, though the researchers note that it is too big to be totally starless. However, if confirmed, it would be an unusually massive example of a starless galaxy.

现在，研究人员发现了一个此类暗星系的潜在候选对象，并将研究结果发表在《皇家天文学会月刊》上。该天体被称为AGC 322753，其氢含量约为太阳质量的20亿倍，但似乎没有恒星，尽管研究人员指出它体积太大，不可能是完全没有恒星的。然而，如果得到证实，它将成为一个异常巨大的无恒星星系样本。

Researchers at Swinburne University of Technology in Australia made the discovery by comparing observations from two existing radio surveys, one conducted by the Arecibo Observatory in Puerto Rico and the other by China's Five-Hundred-Meter Aperture Spherical Radio Telescope. Both telescopes had picked up a massive cloud of hydrogen.

澳大利亚斯威本理工大学的研究人员通过对比两项现有射电巡天观测结果发现了这一天体，其中一项由波多黎各的阿雷西博天文台进行，另一项由中国的“中国天眼”（FAST）进行。两台望远镜都探测到了这一巨大的氢云。

Although other researchers have identified several dark galaxy candidates over the past decade, this one stands out for another reason: It may be rotating. Hydrogen gas emits radio waves at a signature wavelength, which shortens when the gas moves toward Earth and stretches when it moves away. The signal from AGC 322753 shows two distinct peaks, suggesting that one side of the gas cloud is moving towards us while the other is receding.

尽管其他研究人员在过去十年中已经确定了几个暗星系候选对象，但这个星系因另一个原因而引人注目：它可能正在旋转。氢气会以特定的波长发射无线电波，当气体向地球移动时波长会缩短，远离时则会拉长。来自AGC 322753的信号显示出两个明显的峰值，这表明氢云的一侧正向我们移动，而另一侧正在远离。

This could mean that the gas has settled into an organized, rotating structure, much like the discs in ordinary galaxies, despite apparently having formed no stars. "The theoretical models and the simulations show us that dark galaxies shouldn't rotate, so this is extremely interesting," says Marco Monaci, an author of the study at Swinburne University of Technology.

这意味着尽管该天体似乎没有形成恒星，但气体可能已经形成了一种有组织的旋转结构，就像普通星系中的盘状结构一样。斯威本理工大学的研究作者马可·莫纳奇（Marco Monaci）表示：“理论模型和模拟显示暗星系不应该旋转，所以这一点非常有趣。”

If AGC 322753 really is a rotating disc with no stars, astronomers will have to explain how the gas organized into such a structure without producing them. That isn’t the only puzzle. Most models of galaxy formation predict that a cloud holding this much hydrogen should have formed at least a few stars, as its densest patches collapse under their own gravity and ignite.

如果AGC 322753确实是一个没有恒星的旋转盘，天文学家将不得不解释气体是如何在不产生恒星的情况下组织成这种结构的。这并不是唯一的谜题。大多数星系形成模型预测，拥有如此多氢气的云团至少应该形成了一些恒星，因为其最稠密的区域会在自身引力作用下坍缩并点燃。

The paper’s coauthors suggest it may be very unlikely that AGC 322753 contains absolutely no stars. So, instead, they think it may contain stars too faint to see–an “almost dark” galaxy. If so, they must be very faint indeed, because even deep optical images from the DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys, which search for starlight rather than the hydrogen gas detected by radio telescopes, reveal none.

该论文的合著者认为，AGC 322753完全没有恒星的可能性很小。因此，他们认为它可能包含太暗而无法观测到的恒星——即一个“近乎黑暗”的星系。如果是这样，这些恒星一定非常暗淡，因为即使是来自DESI遗产成像巡天（DESI Legacy Imaging Surveys）的深度光学图像——该巡天旨在寻找星光而非射电望远镜探测到的氢气——也未能发现任何恒星。

From this, the team estimates that AGC322753 contains at least 40 times more mass in hydrogen than in stars, raising questions about what exactly suppressed stars from forming. But further observations, such as ones made by the Very Large Array, a network of radio antennas in New Mexico, will be needed to confirm whether AGC 322573 is truly a dark—or almost dark—galaxy.

据此，研究团队估计AGC 322753的氢质量至少是恒星质量的40倍，这引发了关于究竟是什么抑制了恒星形成的疑问。但仍需要进一步的观测，例如利用位于新墨西哥州的射电天线阵列“甚大阵列”（VLA）进行观测，以确认AGC 322753是否真的是一个暗星系或近乎黑暗的星系。

"Finding out more about it really is an essential next step at the moment. It's intriguing, and I want to know more," says Robert Minchin at the National Radio Astronomy Observatory. Minchin knows how misleading such signals can be. He was involved in the discovery of VIRGOHI21, a dark galaxy candidate found in the early 2000s that was later reinterpreted as a long stream of hydrogen gas pulled from a nearby galaxy by gravity, known as a tidal tail.

美国国家射电天文台的罗伯特·明钦（Robert Minchin）表示：“目前，进一步了解它确实是至关重要的一步。它很吸引人，我想知道更多。”明钦深知此类信号可能具有多大的误导性。他曾参与发现VIRGOHI21，这是一个在21世纪初发现的暗星系候选对象，后来被重新解释为从附近星系被引力拉出的一条长长的氢气流，即所谓的“潮汐尾”。

Gas torn away like this can keep some of its original motion and mimic the signature of rotation, so Minchin says more observations are needed to rule out the same explanation for AGC 322753. Yet there is no obvious nearby galaxy it could have come from.

像这样被撕裂的气体可以保持其部分原始运动状态，并模拟出旋转的特征，因此明钦表示需要更多的观测来排除AGC 322753存在同样解释的可能性。然而，附近并没有明显的星系可以作为其来源。

Rhys Taylor, an astronomer at the Astronomical Institute of the Czech Academy of Sciences in Prague, says tidal debris is unlikely to explain the observations. Reproducing the candidate dark galaxy’s distinctive double-peaked profile through such a process “is technically possible, but it’s fantastically unlikely,” Taylor says.

布拉格捷克科学院天文研究所的天文学家里斯·泰勒（Rhys Taylor）表示，潮汐碎片不太可能解释这些观测结果。泰勒说，通过这种过程重现该候选暗星系独特的双峰轮廓“在技术上是可能的，但可能性极低”。

Taylor and Minchin both flag another concern, though. The two radio surveys that the researchers used placed the hydrogen at noticeably different positions in the sky. That suggests AGC 322753 may be an extended cloud of gas rather than a compact galaxy, another possibility that follow-up observations could help untangle.

不过，泰勒和明钦都提出了另一个担忧。研究人员使用的两项射电巡天观测将氢气定位在天空中明显不同的位置。这表明AGC 322753可能是一个延伸的气体云，而不是一个致密的星系，这是后续观测有助于厘清的另一种可能性。

Whether AGC 322753 turns out to be completely dark or merely exceptionally faint, understanding objects like it could still help astronomers work out why some galaxies form stars while others apparently do not, says Elisabeth Adams at the Planetary Science Institute. One possible reason is a galaxy that spins so fast that its gas stays too spread out to collapse. Another is that ultraviolet light from the universe’s earliest stars heated the gas, keeping it too warm to condense. Studying such almost-dark galaxies could help tell these phenomena apart, revealing how the conditions of the early universe.

行星科学研究所的伊丽莎白·亚当斯（Elisabeth Adams）表示，无论AGC 322753最终被证明是完全黑暗还是仅仅极其暗淡，了解此类天体仍能帮助天文学家弄清楚为什么有些星系会形成恒星，而另一些似乎不会。一个可能的原因是星系旋转太快，导致其气体过于分散而无法坍缩。另一个原因是来自宇宙早期恒星的紫外线加热了气体，使其温度过高而无法凝结。研究这些近乎黑暗的星系有助于区分这些现象，揭示宇宙早期的状况。
