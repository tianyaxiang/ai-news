The short version: Anthropic shipped Claude Fable 5.2 and Claude Mythos 5.2 on November 9. On the surface it is a routine upgrade: faster, cheaper, higher benchmark scores. The interesting part is the structure — one underlying model, two different doors into it — and the fact that Anthropic is now pricing by the task, not just by the token.

简而言之：Anthropic 于 11 月 9 日发布了 Claude Fable 5.2 和 Claude Mythos 5.2。从表面上看，这是一次常规升级：速度更快、价格更低、基准测试分数更高。有趣之处在于其架构——同一个底层模型，却有两扇不同的入口——以及 Anthropic 现在开始按任务而非仅仅按 Token 进行定价的事实。

Three numbers to hold on to: Price: $8/$40 per million tokens, down 20% from 5.1's $10/$50. Speed: output up to 30% faster than 5.1, plus a fast mode at up to 2.5x speed for double the price ($16/$80). Benchmarks: 49.1% on FrontierCode 2.0, ahead of Opus 5.5 (45.5%), GPT-6 Astra (42.8%), and Gemini 4 Carbon (41.1%).

有三个数字值得关注：价格：每百万 Token 8 美元/40 美元，较 5.1 版本的 10 美元/50 美元下降了 20%。速度：输出速度比 5.1 快 30%，此外还有最高 2.5 倍速度的快速模式，价格翻倍（16 美元/80 美元）。基准测试：在 FrontierCode 2.0 上得分为 49.1%，领先于 Opus 5.5 (45.5%)、GPT-6 Astra (42.8%) 和 Gemini 4 Carbon (41.1%)。

Here is the counterintuitive part: Anthropic still recommends Opus 5.5 as the default for most coding and knowledge work. Why isn't the strongest model the default? The answer is in the two calculations below.

反直觉的部分在于：Anthropic 仍然建议将 Opus 5.5 作为大多数编程和知识工作的默认模型。为什么最强的模型不是默认选项？答案在于下方的两项计算。

One model, two doors: Fable 5.2 and Mythos 5.2 are the same underlying model. The only difference is the guardrails. Fable 5.2: ships with cybersecurity and biosecurity guardrails, publicly available. Mythos 5.2: loosens some of those guardrails, only for organizations that pass Cyber Verification or the Life Sciences Verification Program.

一个模型，两扇门：Fable 5.2 和 Mythos 5.2 是同一个底层模型。唯一的区别在于护栏（安全限制）。Fable 5.2：配备网络安全和生物安全护栏，面向公众开放。Mythos 5.2：放宽了部分护栏，仅供通过网络验证或生命科学验证计划的组织使用。

Anthropic says Fable 5.2's guardrails falsely block about 55% fewer requests than 5.1 (about 60% fewer in cybersecurity, about 40% in biology), at the same strength. So the same capability comes in two versions: a filtered one for everyone, and a fuller one for verified institutions. Model capability is now explicitly layered by trust.

Anthropic 表示，在保持相同强度的情况下，Fable 5.2 的护栏误拦截请求的数量比 5.1 版本减少了约 55%（网络安全领域减少约 60%，生物学领域减少约 40%）。因此，同样的能力以两个版本呈现：面向大众的过滤版，以及面向已验证机构的完整版。模型能力现在明确地按信任度进行了分层。

For agent developers, that means the same prompts and toolchains may behave differently depending on which door you walk through. If you build agents in security or biology, check which door you are on first.

对于智能体开发者而言，这意味着相同的提示词和工具链可能会根据你选择的入口而表现出不同的行为。如果你正在开发安全或生物学领域的智能体，请先确认你使用的是哪扇门。

Cheaper per token, but the real bill is per task: Fable 5.2's per-token price is twice Opus 5.5's. Looked at that way, it is the expensive one. Anthropic's argument is a different ledger: On FrontierCode High effort, Fable 5.2 roughly matches Opus 5.5's best coding score at about half the cost per task.

按 Token 计算更便宜，但实际账单是按任务计算的：Fable 5.2 的单 Token 价格是 Opus 5.5 的两倍。从这个角度看，它反而更贵。Anthropic 的论点基于不同的账本：在 FrontierCode 高难度任务中，Fable 5.2 以大约一半的任务成本，大致达到了 Opus 5.5 的最佳编程分数。

The logic is simple: fewer steps, a higher chance of getting it right the first time, so total tokens go down. But that conclusion has a precondition: it only holds for long, hard tasks. For short tasks and routine coding, Opus 5.5's per-token advantage is still there — which is exactly why Anthropic recommends it as the default.

逻辑很简单：步骤更少，一次性做对的几率更高，因此总 Token 消耗量下降。但这一结论有一个前提：它仅适用于漫长且困难的任务。对于短任务和常规编程，Opus 5.5 的单 Token 价格优势依然存在——这正是 Anthropic 建议将其作为默认模型的原因。

Before picking a model, look at your own task mix: the higher the share of long tasks, the more Fable 5.2 pays off.

在选择模型之前，请审视你自己的任务组合：长任务的比例越高，Fable 5.2 的性价比就越高。

Four takeaways for agent developers: 1. Route by task length first. Long, hard tasks go to Fable 5.2; routine work stays on Opus 5.5. 2. Run your own task distribution through the math before trusting the per-token price. 3. Fast mode is a rescue, not a default. 2.5x speed at double the price fits deadline-driven long tasks, not everyday use. 4. Security and biology work: check your access first. Fable's guardrails will block some requests; false blocks are down 55%, but if your use case sits on the guardrail boundary, go through verification.

给智能体开发者的四个要点：1. 首先根据任务长度进行路由。漫长、困难的任务交给 Fable 5.2；常规工作保留在 Opus 5.5 上。2. 在相信单 Token 价格之前，先用你自己的任务分布进行计算。3. 快速模式是应急手段，而非默认选项。双倍价格下的 2.5 倍速度适用于有截止期限的长任务，而非日常使用。4. 安全和生物学工作：先检查你的访问权限。Fable 的护栏会拦截部分请求；虽然误拦截减少了 55%，但如果你的用例处于护栏边界，请务必通过验证。

The honest limitations: Every benchmark above is self-reported by Anthropic; the methodology is in the system card, and nobody had independently reproduced it at launch. 1M-token context, 128K max output, knowledge cutoff June 2026. Fable 5.1 is now a legacy model; the Pro tier includes 1 Fable reset in the launch week.

诚实的局限性：上述所有基准测试均由 Anthropic 自行报告；方法论包含在系统卡中，发布时无人能独立复现。100 万 Token 上下文，128K 最大输出，知识截止日期为 2026 年 6 月。Fable 5.1 现在已成为旧版模型；Pro 层级包含发布周内 1 次 Fable 重置机会。
