# Apple’s $50 Intelligence Usage Packs extend access to some AI features

Logic Pro 是苹果首款针对 Apple Intelligence 扩展使用权限收费的应用程序。

Logic Pro is Apple’s first app to charge for extended usage of Apple Intelligence.

在九月初发布了一篇概述部分 Apple Intelligence 功能使用限制的支持文章后，据 9to5Mac 报道，苹果公司已更新 Logic Pro，增加了一个以 49.99 美元一次性购买价格“购买更多使用量”的选项。该软件内置了一个人工智能助手，可以执行诸如“开始创作新歌曲、根据单个提示设置人声轨道或优化现有项目”等任务。

After publishing a support article in early September outlining usage limits for some Apple Intelligence features, the company has updated Logic Pro with an option to “Buy More Usage” for a one-time purchase price of $49.99, as spotted by 9to5Mac. It has a built-in AI assistant to do things like “start a new song, set up a vocal track from a single prompt, or refine an active project.”

这些苹果所谓的“智能使用包”（Intelligence Usage Packs）将允许用户继续访问某些具有使用限制的 Apple Intelligence 功能，因为这些功能依赖于 OpenAI 或 Google Gemini 的云端 AI 模型。使用包仅限购买该服务的 Apple 账户使用（不支持家人共享），正如苹果在其网站上所解释的那样，任何未使用的额外额度将逐月结转，但在购买 12 个月后过期。

These Intelligence Usage Packs, as Apple calls them, will allow users to continue accessing certain Apple Intelligence features that have usage limits because they rely on cloud-based AI models from OpenAI or Google Gemini. The Usage Packs can only be used by the Apple Account that purchased them (Family Sharing isn’t supported) and, as Apple explains on its website, any unused additional usage will roll over from month to month, but will expire 12 months after it was purchased.

对于 Logic Pro，苹果表示使用限制“受与助手对话复杂程度的影响”。发现使用权限耗尽的用户可以在应用程序主菜单的“智能功能 > 显示使用情况”（Intelligence Features > Show Usage）部分找到购买智能使用包的选项。目前，只有 Logic Pro 提供了购买更多使用量的选项，但预计本月将扩展至其他 Apple Creator Studio 应用程序，包括 Pixelmator Pro、Final Cut Pro、Motion，以及即将获得苹果 AI 聊天机器人访问权限的 Pages、Keynote 和 Numbers。

For Logic Pro, Apple says the usage limit is “impacted by the complexity of conversations you have with the assistant.” Users finding themselves running out of access can find the option to buy an Intelligence Usage Pack in the Intelligence Features > Show Usage section of the app’s main menu. Currently, only Logic Pro has the option to buy more usage, but it’s expected to be added to other Apple Creator Studio apps this month, including Pixelmator Pro, Final Cut Pro, Motion, plus Pages, Keynote, and Numbers that are getting access to Apple’s AI chatbot.
