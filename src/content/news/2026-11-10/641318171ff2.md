# Nvidia CEO's affair becomes a national security problem

Nvidia’s chief executive Jensen Huang has admitted having an affair two days after the FT reported his alleged romantic relationship with a woman the US intelligence community believes was working as a spy for China.

英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋在《金融时报》报道其与一名被美国情报界认为是中国间谍的女性存在所谓恋情两天后，承认了这段婚外情。

Huang revealed the affair in an internal message to employees on Sunday that was obtained by the FT. “I owe my family and all of you an apology,” Huang said in the message. “I was unfaithful to my wife. My actions hurt her and our children. There is no excuse for what I did. I am deeply sorry.”

黄仁勋在周日发给员工的一封内部信息中披露了此事，该信息被《金融时报》获取。黄仁勋在信息中表示：“我欠我的家人和你们所有人一个道歉。我背叛了我的妻子。我的行为伤害了她和我们的孩子。我所做的一切没有任何借口。我深表歉意。”

In a separate statement, Nvidia said its board would open an independent investigation “in light of recent reporting that founder and CEO Jensen Huang has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort.” Huang said he would fully cooperate. “My responsibility is to serve you and be worthy of your trust,” he said. “I have fallen short of that responsibility. I am fully committed to Nvidia and will work to earn back your confidence.”

英伟达在另一份声明中表示，鉴于近期有关创始人兼首席执行官黄仁勋成为外国情报工作目标的报道，公司董事会将展开独立调查。黄仁勋表示他将全力配合。他说：“我的责任是服务于你们，并值得你们的信任。我辜负了这一责任。我完全致力于英伟达，并将努力赢回你们的信心。”

The FT reported on Friday that the US believed Huang had a romantic relationship with a Chinese woman who was recruited to spy for China’s Ministry of State Security intelligence agency. American officials this year wrote a memo, based on information from US intelligence, which said the Nvidia boss had been romantically involved with Alice Huang, an executive at Megaspeed, a Singapore-based customer of the chipmaker, according to people familiar with the document.

《金融时报》周五报道称，美国方面认为黄仁勋与一名被中国国家安全部招募为间谍的中国女性存在恋情。据知情人士透露，美国官员今年根据美国情报信息撰写了一份备忘录，称这位英伟达老板与芯片制造商的客户——总部位于新加坡的Megaspeed公司高管Alice Huang存在恋情。

The affair is a damaging blow to the Nvidia chief, who in recent years has become the face of the AI boom, one of the technology’s loudest champions, and transformed the Silicon Valley group into the first $5 trillion company. It also comes at a critical juncture for the wider AI sector, which faces a deepening political backlash in the US and growing investor concern over whether the hundreds of billions of dollars spent on infrastructure will pay off.

这起婚外情对这位英伟达掌门人造成了沉重打击。近年来，他已成为人工智能热潮的代言人，是该技术最坚定的支持者之一，并将这家硅谷公司打造成了首家市值达到5万亿美元的企业。此事发生之际，整个AI行业正处于关键时刻，面临着美国国内日益加剧的政治反弹，以及投资者对于数千亿美元基础设施投入能否获得回报的担忧。

Nvidia’s Frankfurt-listed shares were down more than 6 percent in early trading on Monday. Huang’s admission of an affair comes after Nvidia previously dismissed suggestions of an improper relationship with Alice Huang, saying the interactions were “nothing unusual or unexpected” when dealing with an Nvidia customer.

英伟达在法兰克福上市的股票周一早盘下跌超过6%。此前，英伟达曾否认与Alice Huang存在不正当关系，称这些互动在与英伟达客户打交道时“并无异常或意外”，而黄仁勋此次承认婚外情正是在此背景下发生的。

The American government memo, which said US intelligence believed the Megaspeed executive had been recruited by the MSS, was drafted just before President Donald Trump traveled to Beijing for a summit with Xi Jinping. Jensen Huang has developed a close relationship with Trump, which has frustrated US national security officials who believe he is too willing to sell advanced chips to China that could harm American security.

这份称美国情报部门认为该Megaspeed高管已被中国国安部招募的美国政府备忘录，是在唐纳德·特朗普总统前往北京与习近平主席举行峰会前夕起草的。黄仁勋与特朗普建立了密切关系，这令美国国家安全官员感到沮丧，他们认为黄仁勋过于乐意向中国出售可能损害美国安全的先进芯片。

Asked about Huang on Sunday, Trump told reporters he had done an “amazing job” for the US. Asked if he regretted not acting on the intelligence about the affair, Trump said: “No, I don’t think so. I think he’s an honorable guy. I think he probably has an excuse for it. You have to hear it.” “I have not spoken to him about it. I just know he’s a fine person, but you never know what is going on,” Trump added.

周日被问及黄仁勋时，特朗普告诉记者，他为美国做了“了不起的工作”。当被问及是否后悔没有根据有关婚外情的情报采取行动时，特朗普说：“不，我不这么认为。我认为他是一个正派的人。我想他可能有什么理由。你得听听他的解释。”特朗普补充道：“我还没有和他谈过这件事。我只知道他是个好人，但你永远不知道发生了什么。”

After broadly staying out of politics, Huang started to build relations with Trump after the president called him out of the blue on his birthday. He quickly became one of Trump’s favorite executives, accompanying him on state visits, including to China earlier this year and the UK last year.

在长期远离政治之后，黄仁勋在特朗普总统于他生日当天突然打来电话后，开始与特朗普建立关系。他很快成为特朗普最青睐的高管之一，陪同他进行国事访问，包括今年早些时候访问中国和去年访问英国。

Huang has repeatedly lobbied Trump to adopt a more permissive approach to shipping Nvidia’s AI chips to China, after the company was entirely cut off from the critical market by new export controls in 2025. The resulting deal—with Trump permitting the export of Nvidia’s older-generation H200 chips to China, with the US government taking a cut of sales—had a minimal impact on the Silicon Valley company’s sales, as Beijing pushed back against its technology. But it further angered China hawks in the US administration.

在2025年新出口管制导致英伟达完全被切断与这一关键市场的联系后，黄仁勋多次游说特朗普，希望对向中国出口英伟达AI芯片采取更宽松的态度。随后的协议——即特朗普允许向中国出口英伟达的旧一代H200芯片，并由美国政府从销售额中抽成——对这家硅谷公司的销售影响甚微，因为北京方面对该技术进行了抵制。但这进一步激怒了美国政府中的对华鹰派。

The US government had already been investigating Megaspeed over concerns that it was smuggling Nvidia chips into China. Nvidia on Sunday said a committee of independent directors, helped by external lawyers, would oversee the investigation. “The board prioritizes US national and economic security, which is paramount to the interests of Nvidia and its shareholders,” the company said. “While the board has no reason to believe that either national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised, the board’s inquiry will examine all available information.”

美国政府此前已在调查Megaspeed，原因是有担忧称其在向中国走私英伟达芯片。英伟达周日表示，一个由独立董事组成的委员会将在外部律师的协助下监督此次调查。公司表示：“董事会将美国国家和经济安全放在首位，这对英伟达及其股东的利益至关重要。虽然董事会没有理由相信国家安全或英伟达的机密受到损害，但董事会的调查将审查所有可用信息。”

Huang, who co-founded Nvidia as a video-game graphics card company in 1993, is one of the longest-serving founder-CEOs in Silicon Valley. More than most technology groups, Nvidia’s success is tethered to that of its CEO. Huang leads a close and loyal core team of about half a dozen executives, acting as its chief salesman on the international stage while directly managing dozens of direct reports who keep the company running.

黄仁勋于1993年联合创立了英伟达，最初是一家视频游戏显卡公司，他是硅谷任职时间最长的创始人兼首席执行官之一。与其他大多数科技集团相比，英伟达的成功与这位首席执行官紧密相连。黄仁勋领导着一个由大约六名高管组成的紧密且忠诚的核心团队，他在国际舞台上担任首席推销员，同时直接管理着数十名负责公司运营的下属。

He has credited this “flat” organizational structure for the speed at which the company operates, helping it to stay ahead of rivals in the AI chip market and increasing its market valuation from roughly $1 trillion in 2023 to $4.5 trillion today.

他将这种“扁平化”的组织结构归功于公司的高效运作，这帮助英伟达在AI芯片市场保持领先地位，并将其市值从2023年的约1万亿美元提升至今天的4.5万亿美元。

Elon Musk, a close ally of Huang, was among the first to offer support, agreeing with a social media post that he was “one of the greatest entrepreneurs of this generation” and “irreplaceable for America’s AI leadership.” But John Moolenaar, the Republican head of the House China committee who, with other lawmakers, has requested Huang to testify before Congress, said: “It is vital that Jensen Huang address our national security concerns in Congress related to China’s access to sensitive technology and information.”

黄仁勋的亲密盟友埃隆·马斯克是首批表示支持的人之一，他认同社交媒体上的一篇帖子，称黄仁勋是“这一代最伟大的企业家之一”，并且“对于美国的AI领导地位不可替代”。但众议院中国问题特别委员会共和党主席约翰·穆勒纳尔（与多位议员一起要求黄仁勋到国会作证）表示：“黄仁勋必须在国会解决我们关于中国获取敏感技术和信息方面的国家安全担忧，这一点至关重要。”
