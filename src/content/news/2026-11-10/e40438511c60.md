Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.08639 (cs) [Submitted on 5 Nov 2026] Title: Forcing a Letter Suppresses Refusal: Mechanistic Evidence from WMDP Authors: Stepan Epifantsev, Anna Antipova, Oleg Chernikov, Leon Strelkov, Alexey Dontsov.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.08639 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 5 日] 标题：强制选择字母会抑制拒绝：来自 WMDP 的机制证据 作者：Stepan Epifantsev, Anna Antipova, Oleg Chernikov, Leon Strelkov, Alexey Dontsov。

Abstract: Safety benchmarks for large language models (LLMs) commonly use multiple-choice question (MCQ) format. However, prior studies documented that MCQ format reduces refusal rates, which may significantly affect the safety behavior of the model. We provide a first mechanistic explanation of this effect on the Weapons of Mass Destruction Proxy (WMDP) dataset across two models - Llama-3.1-8B and Qwen3-8B.

摘要：大型语言模型（LLM）的安全基准测试通常使用多项选择题（MCQ）格式。然而，先前的研究记录表明，MCQ 格式会降低拒绝率，这可能会显著影响模型的安全行为。我们针对大规模杀伤性武器代理（WMDP）数据集，在 Llama-3.1-8B 和 Qwen3-8B 两个模型上，首次提供了这种效应的机制解释。

First, we use the tuned lens technique to show that when processing MCQ the model commits to an answer-letter token very quickly, which is in contrast to the gradual refusal build-up in open-ended formats. Additionally, we observe that activations on the MCQ format lie roughly 3x further from the refusal direction, compared with their open-ended counterparts.

首先，我们使用调优透镜（tuned lens）技术表明，当处理 MCQ 时，模型会非常迅速地确定一个答案字母标记，这与开放式格式中拒绝倾向的逐渐积累形成对比。此外，我们观察到，与开放式格式相比，MCQ 格式下的激活状态距离拒绝方向大约远 3 倍。

Moreover, ablating the attention heads that carry the MCQ format answer increases the refusal, which is a direct evidence that the MCQ answer-selection competes with refusal. These findings indicate that MCQ safety scores primarily measure knowledge capabilities and do not assess the safety-mechanisms.

此外，消融携带 MCQ 格式答案的注意力头会增加拒绝率，这直接证明了 MCQ 答案选择与拒绝倾向之间存在竞争。这些发现表明，MCQ 安全评分主要衡量的是知识能力，而非评估安全机制。
