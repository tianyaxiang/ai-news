简讯 发布时间：太平洋标准时间 2026 年 11 月 9 日下午 3:28 图片来源：Jose Sarmento Matos/Bloomberg / Getty Images Marina Temkin 据报道，黄仁勋在一年前聘请了一家曾处理过高调性丑闻的公关公司。

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang hired Heller Inc., a crisis communications firm known for handling sex scandals, at least a year before he publicly acknowledged an extramarital affair with a woman suspected of spying for China, Wired reported.

据《连线》杂志报道，英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋在公开承认与一名涉嫌为中国从事间谍活动的女子发生婚外情至少一年前，就聘请了以处理性丑闻而闻名的危机公关公司 Heller Inc.。

Heller Inc. founder Risa Heller has advised prominent men through personal scandals and sexual misconduct accusations, according to a 2023 New York Magazine profile. Her reported clients include former congressman Anthony Weiner, celebrity chef Mario Batali, and former New Yorker writer Jeffrey Toobin.

根据《纽约杂志》2023 年的一篇人物专访，Heller Inc. 的创始人丽莎·海勒（Risa Heller）曾为多位陷入个人丑闻和性行为不端指控的知名男性提供咨询。据报道，她的客户包括前国会议员安东尼·韦纳（Anthony Weiner）、名厨马里奥·巴塔利（Mario Batali）以及前《纽约客》撰稿人杰弗里·图宾（Jeffrey Toobin）。

Wired also reported that Nvidia’s internal communications team was told to copy Heller Inc. on press inquiries about Huang shortly after an October 2025 New York Times report about whether Nvidia was helping Chinese companies evade U.S. chip export restrictions.

《连线》还报道称，在 2025 年 10 月《纽约时报》发布关于英伟达是否在帮助中国公司规避美国芯片出口限制的报道后不久，英伟达内部公关团队就被告知，在处理有关黄仁勋的媒体咨询时，需抄送 Heller Inc.。

The Times reported that Megaspeed, a then little-known Singapore-based data center company with ties to China, had acquired nearly $2 billion worth of Nvidia chips. It described Megaspeed CEO Alice Huang, the woman later linked to Jensen Huang, standing next to him at a Taipei gathering of Nvidia customers.

《纽约时报》报道称，Megaspeed 是一家当时鲜为人知的总部位于新加坡的数据中心公司，与中国有联系，该公司已购买了价值近 20 亿美元的英伟达芯片。报道描述了后来与黄仁勋产生关联的女子——Megaspeed 首席执行官 Alice Huang——在台北的一次英伟达客户聚会上站在他身旁的情景。

Heller and Nvidia didn’t immediately respond to requests for comment. On Sunday, Nvidia published a statement from Huang acknowledging an affair with a former Megaspeed executive, though he didn’t name Alice Huang.

Heller 和英伟达没有立即回应置评请求。周日，英伟达发布了一份黄仁勋的声明，承认与一名 Megaspeed 前高管有染，但他并未点名 Alice Huang。

Nvidia’s board separately announced an independent investigation, citing reports that Huang “has been the target of a foreign intelligence effort.”

英伟达董事会另行宣布将进行独立调查，理由是有报道称黄仁勋“一直是外国情报工作的目标”。
