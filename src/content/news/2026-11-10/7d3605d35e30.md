### Unlocking any car from X brand in most of the world

解锁全球大部分地区 X 品牌汽车

November 7 2026 I've always had a soft spot for this particular car brand. Our family has owned a few of them over the years, and they've served us incredibly well. In 2023, I bought a new four wheel drive/SUV from them, and it was the first time I had access to their connected car platform.

2026 年 11 月 7 日，我一直对这个特定的汽车品牌情有独钟。多年来，我们家拥有过几辆该品牌的汽车，它们的使用体验非常出色。2023 年，我购买了他们的一款新款四驱/SUV，这是我第一次使用他们的车联网平台。

Having looked at platforms like this before (previous works), I was sceptical about how secure they really were. I suspected there was something there, but life kept getting in the way of actually looking. This July, I collaborated with Sam Curry to take a look at the security of a major automaker's connected car systems. Throughout this post, I'll refer to them simply as "the automaker".

由于之前研究过类似的平台（过往工作），我对它们的安全性持怀疑态度。我怀疑其中存在问题，但生活琐事一直阻碍我进行深入调查。今年 7 月，我与 Sam Curry 合作，对一家大型汽车制造商的车联网系统安全性进行了研究。在本文中，我将统称其为“该汽车制造商”。

When you typically think of car hacking, it's often easy to jump into the hardware side of things, but my background sits pretty squarely in compromising internet-facing web applications and infrastructure. Regardless of all the hardware bits and bobs, it all connects back to some service that, by design, has to be reachable from the internet.

提到汽车黑客攻击，人们通常很容易想到硬件层面，但我的背景主要集中在攻破面向互联网的 Web 应用程序和基础设施上。无论硬件设备如何复杂，它们最终都必须连接到某个根据设计必须能够从互联网访问的服务。

The journey started with a simple question: what infrastructure does the app talk to when I remotely unlock my car? To work this out, I downloaded the APK and decompiled it, eventually reconstructing the API requests that were being made.

The admin toolRunning connected car infrastructure can be quite complex, and managing it requires a mix of admin tools, staging, dev, and QA environments, to make sure that customers get a clean experience. Whether all of that should be sitting on the public internet, available to anyone willing to do some basic reconnaissance, is a different story.

In Australia, a number plate gets you the VIN (vehicle identification number) fairly easily through state-based registration lookup sites. This isn't really a security flaw. There are plenty of legitimate reasons to want a VIN, like checking a car's true repair and insurance history before buying second-hand. If we could go from VIN to a subscriber or email, we'd complete the chain.

在澳大利亚，通过各州的注册查询网站，可以很容易地根据车牌号获取 VIN（车辆识别码）。这本身并不是一个安全漏洞。获取 VIN 有很多正当理由，例如在购买二手车前检查车辆真实的维修和保险记录。如果我们能从 VIN 关联到订阅者或电子邮件，我们就能完成整个链条。

Now, how do we send commands to any car we want? Surely this admin tool would let us do that? Unfortunately, no. It let us provision features to a car, add ourselves to a user's account as a secondary user, and even generate remote access codes. But for all that functionality, none of it actually got us remote control of a vehicle.

现在，我们如何向任何我们想要的汽车发送指令呢？这个管理工具肯定能做到吧？遗憾的是，不行。它允许我们为汽车配置功能、将自己添加为用户账户的次要用户，甚至生成远程访问代码。但尽管有这些功能，它们都无法让我们真正实现对车辆的远程控制。

The identity platformSam and I then spent two weeks digging into how the automaker manages their central identity system: the software they use, the way commands are issued, and how consumers authenticate.

身份平台：随后，Sam 和我花了两个星期深入研究该汽车制造商如何管理其核心身份系统：他们使用的软件、指令的发布方式以及消费者的身份验证方式。

When a user goes through the authentication flow for the automaker's mobile app, requests are made to login.redacted-identity.com.au, and after going through an MFA flow, a consumer auth token is finally issued. This auth token is then used for subsequent requests to api.redacted-telematics.com.au (both REST APIs and GraphQL). Once you have that auth token, it's pretty much game over. You can control a user's car (remote unlock/lock, horns, engine, etc).

当用户通过汽车制造商移动应用程序的身份验证流程时，请求会发送到 login.redacted-identity.com.au，在经过多因素身份验证（MFA）流程后，最终会颁发一个消费者身份验证令牌。该令牌随后用于对 api.redacted-telematics.com.au 的后续请求（包括 REST API 和 GraphQL）。一旦你拥有了这个身份验证令牌，游戏基本就结束了。你可以控制用户的汽车（远程解锁/锁定、鸣笛、启动引擎等）。

So if we could mint a token for any user, we'd finally be able to control anyone's connected car. This is the angle we went for.

因此，如果我们能为任何用户伪造一个令牌，我们最终就能控制任何人的联网汽车。这就是我们采取的切入点。

The automaker is a heavy user of ForgeRock's identity software (OpenAM/OpenIDM). This is the cornerstone of their authentication mechanisms. Pulling the subdomains for *.redacted-identity.com.au, we quickly found their OpenIDM instance at openidm.redacted-identity.com.au. This is the system that manages every identity registered on the automaker's connected car platform.

该汽车制造商大量使用 ForgeRock 的身份软件（OpenAM/OpenIDM）。这是其身份验证机制的基石。通过拉取 *.redacted-identity.com.au 的子域名，我们很快在 openidm.redacted-identity.com.au 找到了他们的 OpenIDM 实例。这是管理该汽车制造商车联网平台上注册的每一个身份的系统。
