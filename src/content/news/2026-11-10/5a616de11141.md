# David Ellison says AI could be the ‘biggest creative unlock in modern history’

Paramount Skydance’s CEO compared AI to CGI, but said it ‘will never be a replacement’ for artists.

派拉蒙天空之舞（Paramount Skydance）的首席执行官将人工智能比作计算机生成图像（CGI），但他表示，人工智能“永远不会取代”艺术家。

During Paramount Skydance’s latest earnings call, CEO David Ellison said “it’s hard to imagine” AI won’t have a bigger impact on the film industry than CGI. In response to a question about the company’s outlook for AI, Ellison said: “I think when you zoom out and look at production, AI really has the potential to be the biggest creative unlock in modern history. The last time we saw anything close to this was really the, you know, advent of CGI and digital composites in the early 90s. That technology gave birth to amazing stories like Jurassic Park and Terminator 2, and it’s hard to imagine that AI is not going to have a more significant impact than that. And I personally believe that’s going to be an incredible unlock on creativity.”

在派拉蒙天空之舞最近的财报电话会议上，首席执行官大卫·埃里森（David Ellison）表示，“很难想象”人工智能对电影行业的影响不会超过 CGI。在回应有关公司人工智能前景的问题时，埃里森说道：“我认为，当你放眼整个制作领域时，人工智能确实有潜力成为现代史上最大的创意解锁。我们上一次看到类似的情况，实际上是 90 年代初 CGI 和数字合成技术的出现。那项技术催生了《侏罗纪公园》和《终结者 2》等精彩故事，很难想象人工智能不会产生比那更重大的影响。我个人认为，这将是对创造力的一次不可思议的解锁。”

Ellison gave Terminator as an example, describing how it cost $4 million to make, but “if you wanted to make that picture today, shot for shot, it’s $60 to 70 million.” He went on to suggest AI might be able to help with that: “We think these tools will help unlock more original storytelling by being able to make the budgets more digestible to be able to take that amount of risk.”

埃里森以《终结者》为例，描述了它当年的制作成本仅为 400 万美元，但“如果你今天想逐镜头重拍这部电影，成本将达到 6000 万到 7000 万美元。”他进而提出人工智能或许能对此有所帮助：“我们认为，这些工具将使预算变得更易于消化，从而能够承担相应的风险，进而帮助解锁更多原创故事。”

Ellison said AI will “significantly” reshape Paramount Skydance’s business, saying the company has been developing its own AI tools. As a result of those tools, Ellison claims, “content will be easier to produce, projects across the organization will speed up, and how we work will transform.” He emphasized that “all of this makes the value of premium IP and live events more valuable, not less.”

埃里森表示，人工智能将“显著”重塑派拉蒙天空之舞的业务，并称公司一直在开发自己的人工智能工具。埃里森声称，得益于这些工具，“内容制作将变得更容易，整个组织的各个项目进度将加快，我们的工作方式也将发生转变。”他强调，“所有这些都使得优质知识产权（IP）和现场活动的价值变得更高，而不是更低。”

Ellison also noted that “in a world of AI slop, we think there will always be a premium for truly hand-crafted storytelling.” He added, “We also continue to believe that artificial intelligence is a tool for artists and will never be a replacement for them.”

埃里森还指出，“在充斥着人工智能垃圾内容的世界里，我们认为真正手工打造的叙事作品将永远具有溢价空间。”他补充道：“我们也始终坚信，人工智能是艺术家的工具，永远不会取代他们。”
