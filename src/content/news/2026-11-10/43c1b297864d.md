Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.08631 (cs) [Submitted on 5 Nov 2026] Title: BEVCast: Path-Adaptive BEV for Efficient End-to-End Autonomous Driving Authors: Qiren Wang, Mingyu Guo, Zixuan Wang, Weisong Shi.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.08631 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 5 日] 标题：BEVCast：用于高效端到端自动驾驶的路径自适应鸟瞰图 (BEV)。作者：Qiren Wang, Mingyu Guo, Zixuan Wang, Weisong Shi。

Abstract: Camera-based driving models build a dense bird's-eye-view (BEV) grid and spend the same computation on every cell of it, although the vehicle crosses only a few of those cells within the planning horizon. We present BEVCast, a two-stage BEV whose resolution adapts to the predicted driving path.

摘要：基于摄像头的驾驶模型会构建密集的鸟瞰图 (BEV) 网格，并对每个单元格投入相同的计算量，尽管车辆在规划范围内仅会经过其中少数几个单元格。我们提出了 BEVCast，这是一种分辨率可根据预测驾驶路径进行调整的两阶段 BEV。

A coarse pass encodes the whole scene on a grid of 4.096 m cells. A heat head trained on the recorded path assigns a heat to every coarse cell and the cells with the highest heat are refined into 1.024 m cells under a fixed budget. The remaining cells keep their coarse resolution, so the trajectory head reads a mixed-resolution BEV that still covers the entire scene and needs neither an HD map nor a navigation command.

粗略处理阶段在 4.096 米的网格上对整个场景进行编码。一个基于记录路径训练的热度头为每个粗略单元格分配热度，在固定预算下，热度最高的单元格被细化为 1.024 米的单元格。其余单元格保持其粗略分辨率，因此轨迹头读取的是一种混合分辨率的 BEV，它既覆盖了整个场景，又不需要高清地图或导航指令。

Against a full grid of 1.024 m cells with the same architecture and training recipe, BEVCast at a 10% refinement budget encodes 28% of the BEV queries and needs 66% of the BEV encoder time at batch size 1. On nuScenes its mean displacement error is 1.078 m against 1.149 m and its drivable area violation rate is 1.46% against 2.29%.

与采用相同架构和训练方案的 1.024 米全网格相比，BEVCast 在 10% 的细化预算下编码了 28% 的 BEV 查询，并在批处理大小为 1 时仅需 66% 的 BEV 编码器时间。在 nuScenes 数据集上，其平均位移误差为 1.078 米（对比 1.149 米），可行驶区域违规率为 1.46%（对比 2.29%）。

On NAVSIM navtest its mean displacement error is 0.577 m against 0.598 m and its extended predictive driver model score is 82.51 against 81.28. Next to published planners BEVCast reaches a comparable error on both benchmarks without a navigation command. The resolution of a driving BEV can therefore follow the task instead of the grid.

在 NAVSIM navtest 上，其平均位移误差为 0.577 米（对比 0.598 米），扩展预测驾驶员模型得分为 82.51（对比 81.28）。与已发布的规划器相比，BEVCast 在无需导航指令的情况下，在两个基准测试中均达到了相当的误差水平。因此，驾驶 BEV 的分辨率可以跟随任务需求，而非固定网格。
