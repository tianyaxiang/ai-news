Six months before the Nvidia spy scandal emerged, the New York-based short seller firm Culper Research had already raised questions about Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s unusually close relationship with Alice Huang, an executive at Megaspeed, a Singapore-based company that buys Nvidia chips. She is now suspected to be a Chinese spy.

在英伟达间谍丑闻曝光六个月前，总部位于纽约的做空机构 Culper Research 就已对英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋与 Megaspeed 高管爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang）之间不同寻常的亲密关系提出了质疑。Megaspeed 是一家总部位于新加坡的英伟达芯片采购商，而爱丽丝·黄目前被怀疑是中国间谍。

The Nvidia CEO admitted to an extramarital affair over the weekend, and the company has now opened a formal investigation into his relationship with Alice Huang (who shares his last name but is not related). U.S. intelligence officials believe she was recruited by the Chinese government to collect intelligence on Jensen Huang and influence his position regarding U.S. export controls on advanced AI chips.

这位英伟达首席执行官在周末承认了婚外情，公司目前已针对他与爱丽丝·黄（两人同姓但并无亲属关系）的关系展开了正式调查。美国情报官员认为，她是被中国政府招募，旨在收集有关黄仁勋的情报，并影响他在美国对先进人工智能芯片出口管制方面的立场。

Culper 在 5 月 13 日发布的报告再次受到关注，因为该机构在调查 Megaspeed 与英伟达的联系时，发现了黄仁勋与爱丽丝·黄之间的关联。最新消息：我们正在做空英伟达（$NVDA）。我们认为英伟达存在严重的中国问题。请查看我们网站上现已发布的完整报告和披露信息，链接见个人简介。pic.twitter.com/oTaJyL7S2R— Culper (@CulperResearch) 2026 年 5 月 13 日

In particular, the report called the relationship between the two executives “uncomfortably close,” noting that the Nvidia CEO was spotted partying and socializing with Alice Huang on multiple occasions. The report even claimed that some Nvidia employees wondered whether they had a “special relationship.”

报告特别指出，这两位高管之间的关系“亲密得令人不安”，并提到英伟达首席执行官曾多次被目击与爱丽丝·黄一同参加派对和社交活动。报告甚至声称，一些英伟达员工曾怀疑他们是否存在“特殊关系”。

Culper Research also theorized the China business that Nvidia lost to export controls had been rerouted through Southeast Asia, and that Megaspeed, a major chip buyer in the region, was secretly financed by Chinese tech giant Alibaba through shell companies. It pointed to financial records that showed Megaspeed’s total liabilities and equity had grown from $33 million at the end of 2023 to around $3 billion by the end of 2024, much of it in the form of refundable deposits from an unidentified source.

Culper Research 还推测，英伟达因出口管制而失去的中国业务已通过东南亚转道，而该地区的主要芯片买家 Megaspeed 则是由中国科技巨头阿里巴巴通过空壳公司秘密资助的。报告指出，财务记录显示 Megaspeed 的总负债和权益已从 2023 年底的 3300 万美元增长到 2024 年底的约 30 亿美元，其中大部分是以来自不明来源的可退还押金形式存在的。

Financial Times reported Monday that documents show Alibaba lent money to Megaspeed through subsidiaries. Culper is a short seller, so it stood to financially benefit from making these allegations, and those about possible illegal chip diversion have not been independently verified. Its report is available on the web and was posted to X back in May.

《金融时报》周一报道称，文件显示阿里巴巴通过子公司向 Megaspeed 提供了贷款。Culper 是一家做空机构，因此它会从提出这些指控中获得经济利益，且有关非法转移芯片的指控尚未得到独立证实。其报告可在网上查阅，并已于 5 月发布在 X 平台上。
