EXECUTIVE SUMMARY As a California state senator, Scott Wiener authored legislation aimed at curbing the risks of advanced AI. Now, after winning a tight congressional race for former Speaker of the House Nancy Pelosi’s open San Francisco seat, he could play a key role in setting federal AI policy. I spoke with Wiener on Friday, only a few hours after he had been declared the victor. But he was already thinking about AI risk.

执行摘要：作为加利福尼亚州参议员，斯科特·维纳（Scott Wiener）曾起草旨在遏制先进人工智能风险的立法。如今，在赢得前众议院议长南希·佩洛西（Nancy Pelosi）留下的旧金山空缺席位的激烈国会竞选后，他可能在制定联邦人工智能政策方面发挥关键作用。我在周五与维纳进行了交谈，当时距离他被宣布获胜仅过去几个小时，但他已经在思考人工智能的风险问题。

The previous day, the AI nonprofit METR had released a report that had been widely interpreted as evidence that Anthropic had achieved recursive self-improvement, a phenomenon in which AI meaningfully contributes to its own advancement and AI progress potentially spins out of control. “It’s very concerning,” Wiener said, citing the report as evidence that we are quickly running out of time to regulate AI. “It was my hope that Congress would take decisive action before [the end of] this year,” he said. “That does not appear to be happening.”

前一天，人工智能非营利组织 METR 发布了一份报告，该报告被广泛解读为 Anthropic 已实现“递归自我改进”的证据。这是一种人工智能能够显著促进自身进步，并可能导致人工智能发展失控的现象。“这非常令人担忧，”维纳说，他引用该报告作为证据，表明我们监管人工智能的时间正迅速耗尽。“我曾希望国会在今年（年底）之前采取果断行动，”他说，“但目前看来这似乎不会发生。”

Wiener has been increasingly focused on AI in recent years, and he’s made many of the AI safety movement’s concerns his own: During our 20-minute conversation, he used the phrase “recursive self-improvement” half a dozen times, for example.“We need to slow this down,” he says. “Because it’s moving very quickly, and we could sleepwalk into a disaster.”

近年来，维纳越来越关注人工智能，并将人工智能安全运动的许多担忧转化为自己的关切：例如，在我们 20 分钟的谈话中，他六次使用了“递归自我改进”这个词。“我们需要放慢速度，”他说，“因为它发展得太快了，我们可能会在不知不觉中陷入灾难。”

Wiener has already had real influence on AI policy. SB 1047, a bill that he authored in 2024, was set to become the first real piece of frontier AI legislation in the country before it was vetoed by California governor Gavin Newsom; he then sponsored SB 53, a lighter-touch bill that Newsom signed. Both bills have inspired proposed federal legislation.

维纳已经在人工智能政策上产生了实际影响。他于 2024 年起草的法案 SB 1047 原本有望成为美国首部真正的前沿人工智能立法，但在被加州州长加文·纽森（Gavin Newsom）否决后，他随后发起了 SB 53 法案，这是一项监管力度较轻的法案，并最终由纽森签署。这两项法案都启发了联邦层面的立法提案。

Wiener’s congressional campaign received over $3 million from Public First Action, a nonprofit in favor of AI regulation that receives substantial funding from Anthropic. That contribution led opponents to criticize him for being too close to the AI industry, but Wiener says that couldn’t be further from the truth. “Public First supported me because I was the first lawmaker in the country to introduce AI regulation and the first to pass it and get it implemented,” he wrote in a message after our interview.

维纳的国会竞选活动从“Public First Action”获得了超过 300 万美元的资助，这是一个支持人工智能监管的非营利组织，其资金主要来自 Anthropic。这笔捐款导致反对者批评他与人工智能行业走得太近，但维纳表示事实远非如此。“Public First 支持我，因为我是全国第一个引入人工智能监管、第一个通过并实施该监管的立法者，”他在采访后的一条信息中写道。

“The attacks on me for [Public First’s] support were shockingly cynical—literally attacking a candidate who actually regulated AI over Big Tech’s objection and falsely claiming the candidate is somehow too close to Big Tech.” Wiener says that if Congress forms a select committee on artificial intelligence—something that Democrats have discussed since September—he’d be eager to serve.

“因为（Public First 的）支持而对我进行的攻击令人震惊且愤世嫉俗——这简直是在攻击一位在大型科技公司反对下实际监管了人工智能的候选人，并虚假地声称该候选人与大型科技公司走得太近。”维纳表示，如果国会成立人工智能特别委员会——这是民主党人自 9 月以来一直在讨论的事情——他将非常渴望加入。

And he’s enthusiastic about many of the policy ideas floating around Congress. “I think it’s really important to have a strong, empowered, nimble federal regulator,” he says, noting that Representative Ro Khanna, a fellow Democrat from California, has proposed legislation to establish such a regulator. He also highlights the importance of whistleblower protections, testing and auditing requirements, and a kill switch requirement, and he wants to see Congress leverage its oversight powers to hold the AI labs to account.

他对国会中流传的许多政策理念充满热情。“我认为拥有一个强大、有权、灵活的联邦监管机构非常重要，”他说，并指出来自加州的民主党同僚罗·康纳（Ro Khanna）众议员已经提出了建立此类监管机构的立法。他还强调了举报人保护、测试和审计要求以及“终止开关”（kill switch）要求的重要性，并希望看到国会利用其监督权力让人工智能实验室承担责任。

“This is about the future of humanity, and we want to make sure that the public has a say and that the public knows what's going on,” he says. And as a longtime state legislator, he is opposed to broad federal preemption of state AI laws, which some of the proposed federal legislation would require. “States are, as Brandeis says, laboratories,” he notes. “You want the states to be able to continue to be nimble in ways that Congress sometimes is not.”

“这关乎人类的未来，我们希望确保公众有发言权，并让公众了解正在发生的事情，”他说。作为一名资深的州立法者，他反对联邦政府对州人工智能法律进行广泛的优先权凌驾（preemption），而这正是某些联邦立法提案所要求的。“正如布兰代斯（Brandeis）所言，各州是实验室，”他指出，“你希望各州能够继续保持国会有时所缺乏的灵活性。”

Above all, Wiener says, he wants Congress to pass policies with real teeth. “It’s critically important that when Congress acts, that we do so in a way that is truly meaningful and that meets the moment,” he says. That means taking seriously the possibility of AI catastrophe, something he discusses in the sort of language typically used by AI safety experts.

最重要的是，维纳表示，他希望国会通过具有实际约束力的政策。“至关重要的是，当国会采取行动时，我们必须以真正有意义且符合时代需求的方式进行，”他说。这意味着要认真对待人工智能灾难的可能性，他用人工智能安全专家通常使用的语言讨论了这一问题。

“We’re in a moment that is very dangerous in terms of the risk of loss of control over AI,” he told me. “We need to deal with recursive self-improvement. Once you’re in that realm, that really is potentially a recipe for total loss of control.” He’ll have some allies in the fight against catastrophic AI risk. Senator Bernie Sanders, for instance, has met with many of the AI safety movement’s most influential figures and has spoken repeatedly about AI and existential risk.

“在人工智能失控风险方面，我们正处于一个非常危险的时刻，”他告诉我，“我们需要处理递归自我改进问题。一旦进入那个领域，那确实可能成为导致彻底失控的导火索。”在对抗人工智能灾难性风险的斗争中，他将会有一些盟友。例如，参议员伯尼·桑德斯（Bernie Sanders）已经会见了许多人工智能安全运动中最具影响力的人物，并多次谈到人工智能和生存风险。

But there’s still a large gap between Wiener’s Bay Area, AGI-pilled perspective and the national mood on AI, which is anchored by opposition to data centers. The fact that voters approved 40 out of 41 anti-data-center ballot measures on Tuesday might seem to be a good sign for Wiener and others who support AI regulation, but Wiener says he sees this movement as far more tied to local concerns than to bigger questions about AI risk.

然而，维纳那种湾区式的、深信通用人工智能（AGI）的观点与全国对人工智能的情绪之间仍存在巨大鸿沟，后者的核心是对数据中心的反对。周二选民投票通过了 41 项反数据中心投票提案中的 40 项，这对于维纳和其他支持人工智能监管的人来说似乎是个好迹象，但维纳表示，他认为这场运动更多是与当地关切相关，而非与人工智能风险的宏大问题相关。

“People who are really opposed to where AI is going—this is an area where they can actually do something about it,” he says. “But the data center revolt—to me, I think it’s more so that people are upset that there could be something like this in their community, with all the impacts near people’s homes, driving up electric bills and using water. I think that’s the bigger issue for most people, from what I can tell.”

“那些真正反对人工智能发展方向的人——这是一个他们可以采取实际行动的领域，”他说，“但数据中心的反抗——对我来说，我认为更多是因为人们对社区里出现这样的设施感到不满，因为它对人们家附近产生影响，推高了电费并消耗了水资源。据我所知，这对大多数人来说是更大的问题。”

Wiener describes himself as only “cautiously optimistic” about Congress’s ability to rein in AI, given its poor track record on regulating the tech industry. He also has concerns about the ability to get legislation past President Donald Trump, who called concerns about catastrophic AI risk a “hoax” in September and has demonstrated a stark unwillingness to collaborate with China, something that Wiener sees as essential to preventing the worst consequences of AI.

维纳形容自己对国会约束人工智能的能力仅持“谨慎乐观”态度，鉴于其在监管科技行业方面表现不佳。他还担心能否让立法通过唐纳德·特朗普（Donald Trump）总统的关卡，特朗普曾在 9 月称对人工智能灾难性风险的担忧是“骗局”，并表现出与中国合作的强烈不情愿，而维纳认为这种合作对于防止人工智能的最坏后果至关重要。

But he says Trump’s unpredictability provides some measure of hope, though it’s a fraught kind of hope. “So far what we’ve seen are some problematic things, but not complete disasters,” Wiener says. “I hope that it doesn’t take a disaster to spur the president into acting.”

但他表示，特朗普的不可预测性提供了一定程度的希望，尽管这是一种充满变数的希望。“到目前为止，我们看到了一些有问题的事情，但还没有发生彻底的灾难，”维纳说，“我希望不需要一场灾难来促使总统采取行动。”
