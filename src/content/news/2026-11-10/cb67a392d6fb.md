# AI hardware startup Nominal triples valuation to $3B in 8 months

Nominal, a four-year-old, Austin-based startup that offers an AI platform purpose-built for engineering and testing complex hardware, like aircraft, nuclear reactors, and smart factories, has raised another big round of financing that triples its valuation.

Nominal 是一家成立四年的初创公司，总部位于奥斯汀。该公司提供一个专为工程设计和测试复杂硬件（如飞机、核反应堆和智能工厂）而构建的 AI 平台，近期完成了一轮大规模融资，使其估值翻了三倍。

On Monday, it announced a $240 million Series C at a $3 billion valuation led by Lightspeed Venture Partners and co-led by Insight Partners. This follows the company’s announcement in March of a $155 million Series B extension at a $1 billion valuation, led by Trae Stephens at Founders Fund.

周一，该公司宣布完成 2.4 亿美元的 C 轮融资，估值达到 30 亿美元，由 Lightspeed Venture Partners 领投，Insight Partners 联合领投。在此之前，该公司曾在 3 月份宣布完成 1.55 亿美元的 B 轮融资扩展，当时估值为 10 亿美元，由 Founders Fund 的 Trae Stephens 领投。

That round was a “preemptive” deal — meaning an offer initiated by the investor, not the startup. Other investors in the company include Sequoia, General Catalyst, and Lux Capital.

那轮融资是一笔“先发制人”的交易，意味着这是由投资者而非初创公司发起的要约。该公司的其他投资者还包括红杉资本 (Sequoia)、General Catalyst 和 Lux Capital。

Nominal says that it has 80 commercial and government organizations as customers. One of its key customers is Anduril, the defense company co-founded by Stephens.

Nominal 表示，其拥有 80 家商业和政府机构客户。其主要客户之一是 Anduril，这是一家由 Stephens 共同创立的国防公司。

Nominal also won a government contract in October when the Air Force selected it for a $71.2 million Strategic Funding Increase (STRATFI) investment, which is a program that requires matching funds from outside the Air Force.

10 月份，Nominal 还赢得了一份政府合同，美国空军选中该公司进行 7120 万美元的战略资金增加 (STRATFI) 投资，该计划要求获得空军以外的配套资金。

Nominal CEO and co-founder Cameron McCord (pictured) is a former U.S. Navy submarine officer and an Anduril alum.

Nominal 首席执行官兼联合创始人 Cameron McCord（如图所示）曾是美国海军潜艇军官，也是 Anduril 的前员工。
