A Message to Our Company NVIDIA founder and CEO Jensen Huang sent the following email to NVIDIA employees earlier today. November 8, 2026

致公司全体员工：NVIDIA 创始人兼首席执行官黄仁勋于今日早些时候向 NVIDIA 员工发送了以下电子邮件。2026 年 11 月 8 日

I owe my family and all of you an apology. I was unfaithful to my wife. My actions hurt her and our children. There is no excuse for what I did. I am deeply sorry.

我欠我的家人和你们所有人一个道歉。我背叛了我的妻子。我的行为伤害了她和我们的孩子。我所做的一切没有任何借口。我深感抱歉。

Throughout my life, my family has given me love and support. They deserve my honesty and loyalty. Instead, I let them down and damaged the life we built together. I take full responsibility for what I did and the hurt I caused, and I accept the criticism that comes with it.

在我的一生中，我的家人给予了我爱与支持。他们理应得到我的诚实与忠诚。然而，我辜负了他们，破坏了我们共同建立的生活。我对我所做的一切以及造成的伤害承担全部责任，并接受随之而来的批评。

To my wife and family: I love you. I know an apology cannot undo what I have done. I hope our family will heal and that, over time, I can earn back your trust.

致我的妻子和家人：我爱你们。我知道道歉无法挽回我所做的一切。我希望我们的家庭能够愈合，并希望随着时间的推移，我能重新赢得你们的信任。

To my colleagues at NVIDIA, including those I have worked alongside for decades, and to our board, partners, and shareholders around the world: you deserve better from me. Leading NVIDIA is the privilege of my life. My responsibility is to serve you and be worthy of your trust. I have fallen short of that responsibility. I am fully committed to NVIDIA and will work to earn back your confidence.

致我在 NVIDIA 的同事们，包括那些与我共事数十载的伙伴，以及我们全球的董事会成员、合作伙伴和股东：你们理应得到更好的对待。领导 NVIDIA 是我一生的荣幸。我的职责是服务于你们，并配得上你们的信任。我未能履行这一职责。我将全心全意致力于 NVIDIA，并努力重新赢得你们的信心。

Regarding the national security concerns raised in news reports, NVIDIA’s Board of Directors has initiated an independent investigation. I support the investigation and will cooperate fully. I love our country and our company. I am committed to protecting NVIDIA and supporting America’s national security. I am proud and grateful to be an American. My family bears no responsibility for my choices. I do. Please give them privacy. Jensen

关于新闻报道中提出的国家安全担忧，NVIDIA 董事会已启动一项独立调查。我支持这项调查并将全力配合。我热爱我们的国家和我们的公司。我致力于保护 NVIDIA 并支持美国的国家安全。我为自己是一名美国人感到自豪和感激。我的家人对我的选择不负任何责任。责任在于我。请给予他们隐私。黄仁勋
