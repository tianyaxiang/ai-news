# Sure mate, buy me a bloody coffee

I don’t usually get involved in the internet reply-fest du jour, but this time I thought I’d give it a go. It involves coffee, just in case you wondered. My sister-in-law gave me this espresso cup. It’s pretty cool. BTW: I’ve used a bit of the antipodean vernacular in this post. You have been warned.

我通常不会参与互联网上每日必有的回复狂欢，但这一次我想尝试一下。如果你好奇的话，这和咖啡有关。我嫂子送了我这个浓缩咖啡杯，它真的很酷。顺便说一句：我在本文中用了一些澳大利亚/新西兰方言，先提醒你一下。

There was a bit of a kerfuffle last week on the IndieWeb about whether or not people should have a “buy me a coffee” (or equivalent) link on their blog. The argument of the article that sparked the debate being that this is rampant capitalism, everything is getting monetised these days, it’s not going to cover your hosting costs, hosting doesn’t cost much anyway, so why bother, and please just stop. Well, that was my impression.

上周在 IndieWeb 上发生了一场小小的骚动，讨论人们是否应该在博客上放置“请我喝杯咖啡”（或类似）的链接。引发这场辩论的文章认为，这是猖獗的资本主义，如今一切都在被货币化，这根本覆盖不了你的托管成本，反正托管费也不贵，所以何必呢，请停止吧。嗯，这就是我的印象。

I can understand the perspective. It seems that everything on the internet is being monetised in some way or form. “Don’t forget to like and subscribe!”, “Join my newsletter!”, “Read my Substack!”, “Buy from my affiliate links!”, “And now a word from this video’s sponsor…”, etc., etc., etc., ad nauseam. And I get that, I really do. There’s just so much advertising everywhere that it’s hard to get away from.

我能理解这种观点。互联网上的一切似乎都在以某种形式被货币化。“别忘了点赞订阅！”、“加入我的通讯！”、“阅读我的 Substack！”、“通过我的联盟链接购买！”、“现在由本视频赞助商说两句……”等等，没完没了。我明白，我真的明白。到处都是广告，让人难以逃避。

I agree: it’d be nice if there were islands of quiet in the online world where no call to monetary action existed. And I get it that personal blogs don’t strictly need a virtual tip jar. My guess is that very few people are making any real money from them. But it’s not about the money. It’s more about recognition for the hard work that writing entails and about being seen for that work and someone going “Hey mate, love yer work, here’s a couple of bucks, good on ya!”.

我同意：如果在线世界里能有一些没有金钱诱导的宁静岛屿，那该多好。我也明白个人博客并不一定需要虚拟小费罐。我猜很少有人能从中赚到真正的钱。但这与钱无关。这更多的是对写作所付出的辛勤工作的认可，是希望自己的工作被看见，并听到有人说：“嘿伙计，喜欢你的作品，给你几块钱，干得好！”

So I’m throwing my hat into the ring on the side of the “Yep, have a ‘buy me a coffee’ button” group. If you look around my blog, you’ll see a buy me a coffee button at the bottom of my articles. I don’t get much money through those links. If anything, I’ve received all of about 10 € over the blog’s entire lifetime. It’s definitely not enough to cover my hosting costs. That doesn’t matter.

所以我决定加入“是的，应该有‘请我喝杯咖啡’按钮”的阵营。如果你浏览我的博客，你会看到文章底部有一个“请我喝杯咖啡”的按钮。我通过这些链接赚不到多少钱。事实上，在博客存在的整个生命周期里，我总共只收到了大约 10 欧元。这绝对不足以支付我的托管费用。但这并不重要。

What does matter is that each time someone drops me a tip via the buy-me-a-coffee button, I am absolutely overjoyed. Someone read an article! It was useful to them! And they liked it! Wicked! It’s brilliant. It’s feedback. Something I’ve written was valuable to someone! Yay! [insert short dance in front of laptop]

真正重要的是，每当有人通过“请我喝杯咖啡”按钮给我小费时，我都会感到无比高兴。有人读了一篇文章！它对他们有用！而且他们喜欢它！太棒了！这很精彩。这是反馈。我写的东西对某人有价值！耶！[此处插入在笔记本电脑前的一段短舞]

I rarely get emails about my blog articles. Most responses have come from someone giving a tip and saying, “Hey, nice article”. That’s so cool! It’s not money I can live off (otherwise I’d be living under a bridge); it’s a signal, it’s feedback, and it’s one of the small pieces of connection I get from the wider world after having put my thoughts and experience online.

我很少收到关于博客文章的电子邮件。大多数回应来自那些给小费并说“嘿，好文章”的人。这太酷了！这不是我能赖以生存的钱（否则我就得住在桥底下了）；这是一个信号，一种反馈，也是我在网上分享了自己的想法和经验后，从更广阔的世界中获得的一点点联系。

Yes, most of the stuff I write is for me (or more realistically, for my future self). Yet, when I write, I often have some imaginary “reader” in mind–someone who is going to benefit from the knowledge or experience I’m documenting. So, if someone out there thinks “That was good, I’ll throw this bloke a couple of Euros his way and say hello”, then that’s bloody awesome.

是的，我写的大部分东西都是为了我自己（或者更现实地说，是为了未来的自己）。然而，当我写作时，我脑海中经常有一个想象中的“读者”——一个将从我记录的知识或经验中受益的人。所以，如果有人觉得“这写得不错，我给这家伙几欧元，打个招呼”，那真是太棒了。

To be honest, if someone just dropped me a line via email and said “Hey, cool post, gudday mate” I’d also be over the moon. For instance, the other night I was at the Hannover TeX user group meeting and someone mentioned that they’d stumbled across an article of mine (one that I’ve referred to myself many times) and found it useful. I was totally stoked!

老实说，如果有人只是通过电子邮件给我发条信息说“嘿，很酷的文章，你好啊伙计”，我也会欣喜若狂。例如，前几天我在汉诺威 TeX 用户组会议上，有人提到他们偶然发现了我的一篇文章（我自己也多次引用过的那篇），并觉得它很有用。我当时简直太兴奋了！

Imagine a busker playing their guitar on a street corner, a hat sitting in front of them to collect what cash bystanders might throw their way. Are they monetising what they’re doing? Strictly speaking, sure. Are they paying rent from it? Most likely not. Is this the evil tendrils of capitalism threading their way into every facet of our lives? Ah, that would be a no.

想象一下，一个街头艺人在街角弹吉他，面前放着一顶帽子，用来收集路人可能扔进去的现金。他们在将自己的行为货币化吗？严格来说，是的。他们靠这个付房租吗？很可能不是。这是资本主义的邪恶触角伸向我们生活的方方面面吗？啊，那倒不是。

Are you obliged to put money into their hat? Not if you don’t want to. If you enjoy their music, you can give them a nod and a smile and a bit of applause. That’s great feedback for the musician. And if you really like what they’re playing, you can throw a few coins into their hat. That’s how busking works.

你有义务往他们的帽子里放钱吗？如果你不想，就不必。如果你喜欢他们的音乐，你可以向他们点头微笑，并鼓掌。这对音乐家来说是很好的反馈。如果你真的很喜欢他们的演奏，你可以往帽子里扔几个硬币。街头表演就是这样运作的。

For small creators on the IndieWeb it’s exactly the same: people putting their stuff out there and maybe–just maybe–someone will connect with them and say “Hey mate, I liked what you wrote. Nice stuff!” And, on the off chance that someone liked something enough, they might buy you a virtual coffee. It’s the same act as someone moving forward from the crowd gathered around the busker, dropping coins into their hat, nodding and giving them a smile.

对于 IndieWeb 上的小型创作者来说，情况完全一样：人们发布自己的内容，也许——仅仅是也许——会有人与他们产生共鸣，说“嘿伙计，我喜欢你写的。写得好！”而且，如果有人非常喜欢，他们可能会请你喝杯虚拟咖啡。这和有人从围观街头艺人的人群中走出来，往帽子里扔硬币、点头微笑的行为是一样的。

I can tell you, getting that recognition and some nice words via my buy-me-a-coffee link not only makes my day, it makes my frikken week. The money doesn’t go far, but shit, it’s a nice feeling to know that something I created was sufficiently worthwhile for someone that they tipped me for it.

我可以告诉你，通过“请我喝杯咖啡”链接获得那种认可和几句好话，不仅让我这一天过得很开心，甚至让我这一周都心情愉悦。钱虽然不多，但该死，知道我创作的东西对某人来说足够有价值，以至于他们愿意为此给我小费，这种感觉真好。

Will the money ever cover my hosting costs? I don’t reckon, eh? Am I over the moon if someone buys me a coffee? You betcha! And I think that’s the case for a lot of creators with such links on their blog: they’d be totally stoked to get a small donation and a “hullo” if someone liked one of their posts.

这些钱能覆盖我的托管成本吗？我不这么认为，嗯？如果有人请我喝咖啡，我会欣喜若狂吗？当然会！我想很多在博客上放置此类链接的创作者也是如此：如果有人喜欢他们的文章，并给他们一点小额捐赠和一句“你好”，他们会感到非常兴奋。

So sure, mate, buy me a bloody coffee! Or buy someone else a coffee. But if you don’t want to, you don’t have to. No one’s forcing you. And if you don’t want to dish out for a coffee, why not just drop me a line and let me know what you thought? That would rock!

所以，当然可以，伙计，请我喝杯咖啡吧！或者请别人喝杯咖啡。但如果你不想，也没关系。没人强迫你。如果你不想花钱买咖啡，为什么不给我发条信息，告诉我你的想法呢？那会很棒的！
