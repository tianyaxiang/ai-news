# RIP, Harvest

I’ve been a self-employed contractor for about a decade now, and I’m bummed about Harvest.

我做自由职业承包商已经大约十年了，Harvest 的现状让我感到很沮丧。

Harvest was a time-tracking and invoicing service. You’d track how many hours or weeks you spent on a project, put together an invoice, email it to the client, and close it out when they paid. There was a little Stripe integration for accepting credit and ACH payments on the invoice web view. A historical chart of total billed/paid amounts over time, and a table of invoices. Harvest was exactly what you want from small-business software: it solved a well-defined problem with a reliable, simple interface, and did so at a reasonable monthly price. They managed to avoid scope creep and never tried to become an accounting platform, or a blockchain for washing machines. I loved Harvest and raved about it to every self-employed person I met.

Harvest 曾是一款时间追踪和发票开具服务。你可以追踪在项目上花费的小时数或周数，整理出一份发票，通过电子邮件发送给客户，并在他们付款后结清。它有一个小型的 Stripe 集成，用于在发票网页视图中接受信用卡和 ACH 付款。它还提供随时间变化的已开票/已付款总额的历史图表，以及发票列表。Harvest 正是小企业软件所应有的样子：它通过可靠、简洁的界面解决了明确的问题，且月费合理。他们成功避免了功能蔓延，从未试图成为会计平台或洗衣机区块链。我非常喜欢 Harvest，并向我遇到的每一位自由职业者大力推荐它。

Back in 2025 Harvest got bought out by Bending Spoons, the company that gutted Vimeo. I was worried they might ruin it, but was busy with other things and didn’t have time to look for new invoicing software.

2025 年，Harvest 被 Bending Spoons 收购，这家公司曾掏空了 Vimeo。我当时很担心他们会毁了它，但由于忙于其他事务，没时间寻找新的发票软件。

Long story short, they raised my price from $13.32 to $133.98 per month by moving everyone to a new “enterprise” plan and “unlimited” usage billing model, which is, as far as I can tell, exactly what I had before. There’s also a “flex” plan which costs $12.65 per month… but that plan now skims an additional 2% on top of the usual ~3% card transaction fees. To be clear, that 3% pays for the whole credit card network, plus all the people working at Stripe and Mastercard, plus the financial risk of chargebacks and fraud, etc. Harvest isn’t doing any of that work. I don’t know about you, but I don’t think I need to spend 2% of my gross revenue to send a couple PDFs a month and wrap a Stripe web form.

长话短说，他们通过将所有人迁移到新的“企业”计划和“无限”使用计费模式，将我的价格从每月 13.32 美元提高到了 133.98 美元，而据我所知，这和我之前使用的服务完全一样。还有一个每月 12.65 美元的“灵活”计划……但该计划现在要在通常约 3% 的信用卡交易费之外，额外抽取 2%。需要明确的是，那 3% 支付的是整个信用卡网络、Stripe 和万事达卡的所有员工，以及拒付和欺诈等财务风险。Harvest 并没有做这些工作。我不知道你怎么想，但我认为我不必为了每月发送几份 PDF 并封装一个 Stripe 网页表单，就花费我总收入的 2%。

I wrote to Harvest about this, and discovered that they don’t read customer emails any more. Instead they have a Large Language Model repeatedly vomit thousands of words at you. I’m no stranger to enshittification, but man, I did not anticipate the speed at which Bending Spoons would drive a perfectly good company into the ground.

我为此写信给 Harvest，结果发现他们不再阅读客户邮件了。取而代之的是，他们让大语言模型反复向你倾倒数千字的内容。我对“平台腐烂”（enshittification）并不陌生，但天哪，我没想到 Bending Spoons 让一家优秀公司倒闭的速度会这么快。

Time to find a new invoicing service, I guess. Maybe bookkeeping too—Intuit’s Quickbooks Online seems to have completely jumped the shark. Any of y’all freelancers have a system you like?

看来是时候寻找新的发票服务了。也许记账软件也要换——Intuit 的 Quickbooks Online 似乎已经彻底走下坡路了。你们这些自由职业者有什么喜欢的系统吗？
