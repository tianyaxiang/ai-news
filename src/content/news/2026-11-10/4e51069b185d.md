Geothermal company Fervo couldn’t have picked a better time to deliver its first power plants. In its third-quarter earnings report, released Monday, the company said it now has $11.9 billion worth of contracted revenue on the books, up from $7.2 billion last quarter. Fervo has been hitting its deadlines recently, which undoubtedly has helped to drive orders.

地热公司 Fervo 交付其首批发电厂的时机再好不过了。在周一发布的第三季度财报中，该公司表示其账面合同收入现已达到 119 亿美元，高于上一季度的 72 亿美元。Fervo 近期一直按时完成目标，这无疑推动了订单的增长。

As hyperscalers scour the earth for electricity to power their AI data centers, Fervo is on track to complete a 396 megawatt power plant for Google by the end of 2028. The geothermal company connected its first 100 megawatt power plant to the grid earlier this year. Both projects are built at Cape Station in Utah, a resource that could generate 8.4 gigawatts of electricity. Previously, experts told Fervo the site could only produce about 4 gigawatts.

随着超大规模数据中心运营商在全球范围内搜寻电力以支持其人工智能数据中心，Fervo 有望在 2028 年底前为谷歌建成一座 396 兆瓦的发电厂。这家地热公司今年早些时候将其首座 100 兆瓦发电厂接入了电网。这两个项目均位于犹他州的 Cape Station，该资源总计可产生 8.4 吉瓦的电力。此前，专家曾告诉 Fervo 该地点仅能生产约 4 吉瓦的电力。

The Google project, now called Cape Google 400, will be grid connected, co-founder and CEO Tim Latimer said in an earnings call Monday. The arrangement means that Google can draw power from the deal and sell it to other customers if it’s not ready to use it. “We built Cape Station on our schedule. Google can develop a potential data center on their schedule, and neither party is waiting on the other,” he said.

联合创始人兼首席执行官 Tim Latimer 在周一的财报电话会议上表示，这个被称为“Cape Google 400”的谷歌项目将实现并网。该安排意味着谷歌可以从协议中获取电力，如果尚未准备好使用，也可以将其出售给其他客户。他说：“我们按计划建设了 Cape Station。谷歌可以按他们的进度开发潜在的数据中心，双方互不耽误。”

Latimer’s statement also underlines an advantage that geothermal has over competing generating sources like large nuclear or natural gas power plants — the phased nature of its construction. Fervo’s newer wells are deeper and hotter, each capable of generating 15 megawatts gross power, and several are linked together into what the company calls a GeoBlock. The first completed GeoBlock generated 33 megawatts, while recent ones hit 50 megawatts. They’re sized to match the demand of a typical data center hall, so hyperscalers can bring them online as they’re completed.

Latimer 的声明还强调了地热能相较于大型核电站或天然气发电厂等竞争能源的优势——即其建设的分阶段特性。Fervo 的新井更深、温度更高，每口井可产生 15 兆瓦的总功率，多口井连接在一起形成了该公司所谓的“GeoBlock”。第一个完成的 GeoBlock 产生了 33 兆瓦的电力，而最近的则达到了 50 兆瓦。它们的规模旨在匹配典型数据中心机房的需求，因此超大规模运营商可以在项目完成后立即将其投入使用。

As a business, Fervo is still in the early stages. It ended the quarter with an operating loss of $43.7 million, though it holds cash and cash equivalents of $1.9 billion and long-term debt of $301.6 million, which should give it plenty of runway. Much will depend on the company bringing down the cost of its power plants. While hyperscalers are hungry for power today, they may not always be as price-indiscriminate.

作为一家企业，Fervo 仍处于早期阶段。本季度结束时，其运营亏损为 4370 万美元，但该公司拥有 19 亿美元的现金及现金等价物，长期债务为 3.016 亿美元，这应能为其提供充足的运营空间。未来的发展很大程度上取决于公司能否降低发电厂的成本。虽然超大规模运营商目前对电力需求迫切，但他们未来未必会一直对价格不敏感。

Yet if Fervo can deliver on its promises, it could end up winning on price. The first phase of its Cape Station project cost $7,000 per kilowatt of capacity, the company said. The cost for its Google project is $5,500 per kilowatt, a significant drop that suggests Fervo is on the right path. With subsidies, that’s about equivalent to a new natural gas power plant running on fuel that costs $6 per million BTUs. Still about twice what natural gas costs today, but trending closer to where it needs to be.

然而，如果 Fervo 能兑现承诺，它最终可能会在价格上胜出。该公司表示，其 Cape Station 项目第一阶段的建设成本为每千瓦容量 7000 美元。其谷歌项目的成本为每千瓦 5500 美元，这一显著下降表明 Fervo 正走在正确的道路上。在补贴的情况下，这大约相当于一座使用燃料成本为每百万英热单位 6 美元的新型天然气发电厂。虽然这仍约为当前天然气成本的两倍，但正趋向于目标水平。

But if Fervo can hit its long-term target of $3,000 per kilowatt, it could significantly undercut natural gas power, even at today’s prices. That’s a pretty ambitious target, but the company has made significant strides in just a few years. No wonder Google is getting in early.

但如果 Fervo 能实现每千瓦 3000 美元的长期目标，即使按今天的价格计算，它也可能大幅低于天然气发电的成本。这是一个相当雄心勃勃的目标，但该公司在短短几年内已经取得了重大进展。难怪谷歌会尽早入局。
