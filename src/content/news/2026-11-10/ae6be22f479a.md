Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.08778 (cs) [Submitted on 5 Nov 2026] Title: Semantic Conditional Similarity (SCS): A Formal Mathematical Framework Authors: Mehmet Murat Albayrakoğlu.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.08778 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 5 日] 标题：语义条件相似度 (SCS)：一种形式化数学框架 作者：Mehmet Murat Albayrakoğlu。

Abstract: Semantic Textual Similarity (STS) operates on document pairs, returning a single scalar per comparison. In a multi-document setting where several sources may have contributed to one or more targets, pairwise STS cannot disentangle each source's marginal semantic contribution from their shared vocabulary.

摘要：语义文本相似度 (STS) 对文档对进行操作，每次比较返回一个标量。在多个来源可能对一个或多个目标做出贡献的多文档设置中，成对的 STS 无法将每个来源的边际语义贡献与其共享词汇区分开来。

In this paper, Semantic Conditional Similarity (SCS) is introduced as a conditional form of STS that computes similarity between two documents after eliminating semantic elements shared with a third via set-theoretic operations.

本文引入了语义条件相似度 (SCS) 作为 STS 的一种条件形式，它通过集合论运算消除与第三个文档共享的语义元素后，计算两个文档之间的相似度。

SCS makes four contributions: 1) it introduces directionality, partitioning pairwise relations into lateral (inter-source) and vertical (source-to-target) classes; 2) conditioning induces role-dependent asymmetry by extending the comparison domain from unordered pairs to ordered, conditioned triples of a target, a reference, and a conditioning document.

SCS 做出了四项贡献：1) 它引入了方向性，将成对关系划分为横向（源间）和纵向（源到目标）类别；2) 条件化通过将比较域从无序对扩展到目标、参考和条件文档的有序条件三元组，诱导了依赖于角色的不对称性。

3) SCS introduces inter-document conditioning by subtracting shared content from a third document, complementing the intra-document conditioning in recent C-STS work; and 4) the set-theoretic decomposition achieves threshold-free partitioning of shared and residual content, requiring no arbitrary similarity cutoff.

3) SCS 通过从第三个文档中减去共享内容引入了文档间条件化，补充了近期 C-STS 工作中的文档内条件化；4) 集合论分解实现了共享内容和剩余内容的无阈值划分，无需任意的相似度截断。

Although developed for the $n \to 1$ configuration, the framework extends to $1 \to n$ via vertical role reversal and to $m \to n$ via independent composition of source- and target-side conditioning. With the established $1 \to 1$ case, these span the full spectrum of document cardinality.

尽管该框架是为 $n \to 1$ 配置开发的，但它可以通过垂直角色反转扩展到 $1 \to n$，并通过源侧和目标侧条件化的独立组合扩展到 $m \to n$。加上已建立的 $1 \to 1$ 情况，这些涵盖了文档基数的全部范围。

SCS scores should be interpreted ordinally, as rankings of marginal contributions rather than absolute measurements. The broader implication is that metric space axioms commonly assumed in similarity formalizations constrain expressiveness in role-differentiated multi-document settings, and that extending the domain to conditioned triples can recover information that pairwise-symmetric comparisons suppress.

SCS 分数应按序数解释，即作为边际贡献的排名，而非绝对测量值。更广泛的含义是，相似度形式化中通常假设的度量空间公理限制了角色区分的多文档设置中的表达能力，而将域扩展到条件三元组可以恢复成对对称比较所抑制的信息。
