Meta Is Quietly Building a Media Theater in VR. That's Exactly the Runtime Amblyopia Training Needed. Meta's Horizon OS 2.10 PTC is rolling out, and one line in the release notes deserves more engineering attention than it's getting: a dedicated Theater environment built specifically for watching media inside the headset. Read that as a platform signal, not a feature. The headset is being repositioned as a screen you inhabit for long stretches — video, work, passive watching. For most users that's convenience. For the roughly 1 in 50 adults with amblyopia, it's something closer to an unexpected runtime.

Meta 正在 VR 中悄悄构建一个媒体影院，这正是弱视训练所需的运行环境。Meta 的 Horizon OS 2.10 PTC 版本正在推出，发布说明中有一行内容值得比目前更多的工程关注：一个专门为在头显内观看媒体而构建的影院环境。请将其视为一种平台信号，而非仅仅是一个功能。头显正在被重新定位为一个你可以长时间沉浸其中的屏幕——用于视频、工作和被动观看。对于大多数用户来说，这是一种便利；但对于大约 50 分之一的弱视成年人来说，这更接近于一种意想不到的运行环境。

Architecturally, a Quest already ships two independent displays, one per eye. Every media app that ignores that leaves a whole channel of the pipeline idle. Amblyotube, by Seven Sports, is recreational and educational software for visual coordination and attention exercises in VR — it treats the two-eye feed as the product surface. Instead of adhesive patches or anaglyph glasses, you watch YouTube-style content while the app renders a deliberately different experience per eye.

从架构上讲，Quest 本身就配备了两个独立的显示屏，每只眼睛各一个。任何忽略这一点的媒体应用程序都让整个管线中的一个通道处于闲置状态。由 Seven Sports 开发的 Amblyotube 是一款用于 VR 视觉协调和注意力训练的娱乐与教育软件，它将双眼输入视为产品界面。你无需使用遮盖贴或红蓝眼镜，只需观看 YouTube 风格的内容，同时应用程序会为每只眼睛渲染出刻意不同的视觉体验。

In pipeline order: • Dominant Eye Shader — adjustable blur/contrast/brightness/gamma/opacity on the stronger eye. At 100% opacity it's full digital patching; the design goal is usually partial transparency, so both eyes keep processing. • Lazy Eye Sharpener (MFBF) — an AI engine detects human figures and selectively sharpens the weaker eye's view, with red silhouette outlines, yellow-green highlights, and pulse controls (adjustable Hz and depth). • Wedge Occlusion — masks parts of both visual fields so each eye sees unique portions, eliminating binocular rivalry by construction. • Magenta Focus Cue — magenta reads high-visibility in the amblyopic eye, grey in the dominant one; the brain is nudged to fuse both into one 3D percept.

管线流程如下：• 主视眼着色器——可调节较强眼睛的模糊度、对比度、亮度、伽马值和不透明度。在 100% 不透明度下，它相当于完全的数字遮盖；设计目标通常是部分透明，以便双眼都能持续处理信息。• 弱视眼锐化器 (MFBF)——AI 引擎检测人体轮廓，并有选择地锐化较弱眼睛的视野，辅以红色轮廓线、黄绿色高光和脉冲控制（可调节频率和深度）。• 楔形遮挡——遮蔽部分视野，使每只眼睛看到独特的部分，从结构上消除双眼竞争。• 品红焦点提示——品红色在弱视眼中具有高可见度，而在主视眼中则显示为灰色；大脑会被引导将两者融合为一个 3D 感知。

Setup matters: you select whether the affected eye is Left or Right — pick wrong and you train the dominant eye instead. Condition presets (Mild / Moderate / Severe) tune defaults, and an integrated LogMAR check using Landolt C optotypes at a virtual 3-meter distance, up to 30 trials per eye, can auto-update the therapy profile. Sessions run 30–40 minutes daily, hard cap 1 hour, ages 13+. Room View streams the same content over AR pass-through.

设置至关重要：你需要选择受影响的眼睛是左眼还是右眼——如果选错，你训练的反而会是主视眼。病情预设（轻度/中度/重度）用于调整默认值，集成的 LogMAR 检查使用虚拟 3 米距离处的 Landolt C 视力表，每只眼睛最多可进行 30 次测试，并能自动更新治疗方案。训练课程每天进行 30–40 分钟，上限 1 小时，适用于 13 岁以上人群。房间视图 (Room View) 则通过 AR 透视功能播放相同内容。

Scope, precisely: supplementary vision training and an assistive tool for binocular coordination and depth perception. Not a diagnosis, treatment, or cure. Amblyopia care belongs with an eye care professional. But the platform shift is real. If the OS is finally optimizing for long passive viewing sessions, the same session budget can carry a training loop — and that combination didn't exist in a consumer headset until recently.

明确其范围：这是一种辅助性的视觉训练和用于双眼协调及深度感知的辅助工具。它并非诊断、治疗或治愈手段。弱视护理应由眼科专业人员进行。但平台层面的转变是真实的。如果操作系统最终开始针对长时间的被动观看进行优化，那么同样的会话时间预算就可以承载一个训练循环——而这种组合直到最近才在消费级头显中出现。
