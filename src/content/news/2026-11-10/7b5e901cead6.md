简讯 发布时间：2026年11月9日太平洋标准时间下午5:27 图片来源：Ruhani Kaur/Bloomberg / Getty Images Marina Temkin 据报道，Anthropic计划推进其首次公开募股（IPO）计划。

Launching a major tech IPO would seem risky right now, shortly after one of Nasdaq’s biggest sell-offs and amid revelations that a CEO central to the AI boom had an affair with a suspected Chinese spy. But that isn’t deterring Anthropic, which is moving ahead with its listing plans, according to The Wall Street Journal.

在纳斯达克经历了一次大规模抛售，且有消息披露一位人工智能热潮中的核心首席执行官与一名疑似中国间谍有染之后，此时进行大型科技公司IPO似乎风险重重。但据《华尔街日报》报道，这并未阻挡Anthropic的脚步，该公司正继续推进其上市计划。

Anthropic plans to set a valuation range this week and begin trading on Thursday or Friday of next week, the Journal reported. Investors will look to Anthropic’s valuation to gauge the strength of the AI boom.

据《华尔街日报》报道，Anthropic计划在本周设定估值区间，并于下周四或周五开始交易。投资者将通过Anthropic的估值来衡量人工智能热潮的强劲程度。

Some institutional investors are now pushing for a valuation below the $1.5 trillion target discussed as recently as last week, a figure that was itself considerably below the $2 trillion floated a few months ago, the report said. OpenAI, Anthropic’s main rival, was reportedly valued at $1.25 trillion last week after the ChatGPT maker raised $11 billion in fresh capital, short of the $20 billion it had initially sought.

报道称，一些机构投资者目前正推动将估值降至低于上周讨论的1.5万亿美元目标，而这一数字本身已远低于几个月前传出的2万亿美元。据报道，Anthropic的主要竞争对手OpenAI在上周筹集了110亿美元的新资本后，估值为1.25万亿美元，低于其最初寻求的200亿美元。
