# MSI’s refurbished QD-OLED gaming monitor is on sale for under $300

微星（MSI）翻新版 QD-OLED 游戏显示器现正促销，售价不到 300 美元。

The 1440p, 240Hz panel offers a potentially sizable upgrade from older IPS panels.

这款 1440p 分辨率、240Hz 刷新率的面板，相比旧款 IPS 面板能带来显著的性能提升。

One of the best upgrades you can make to your PC gaming setup is an OLED monitor. Fortunately, MSI’s factory-reconditioned Mag 272QP X24 is on sale at Woot through November 18th with a 180-day manufacturer warranty for $290.99, which is more than $100 cheaper than the full MSRP of $399.99.

升级 PC 游戏设备最好的方式之一就是更换 OLED 显示器。幸运的是，微星原厂翻新的 Mag 272QP X24 显示器目前正在 Woot 网站促销，活动持续至 11 月 18 日，售价为 290.99 美元，并附带 180 天原厂保修，比 399.99 美元的建议零售价便宜了 100 多美元。

MSI’s 27-inch screen is well-featured for the price, with 1440p resolution, a 240Hz refresh rate, and both G-Sync and FreeSync compatibility, which help reduce screen tearing when your FPS is below the max refresh rate.

微星这款 27 英寸显示器在同价位产品中配置出色，拥有 1440p 分辨率、240Hz 刷新率，并同时兼容 G-Sync 和 FreeSync 技术，有助于在帧率低于最高刷新率时减少画面撕裂。

PC games are particularly well-suited to take advantage of the deep black levels OLED panels produce, too, giving every game you play a deeper, more immersive feel. MSI’s budget OLED also features quantum dots for fuller, more vivid colors.

PC 游戏尤其能发挥 OLED 面板深邃黑色的优势，让玩家体验到更具沉浸感的画面。微星这款入门级 OLED 显示器还采用了量子点技术，使色彩表现更加饱满、生动。

It doesn’t cut corners on ports either, with two HDMI 2.1, one DisplayPort 1.4a, and one USB Type-C DP Alt input.

在接口方面它也没有缩水，配备了两个 HDMI 2.1 接口、一个 DisplayPort 1.4a 接口以及一个支持 DP Alt 模式的 USB Type-C 接口。
