If you scroll X this week, the vibe-coding argument is still going. One camp says it gets you a demo, not a product. Another jokes that you are too scared to touch the output because everything might break. A quieter complaint is more useful if you are trying to ship a marketing site: a lot of current vibe-coding tools have no source of truth. That last gap is what blows up after launch.

如果你这周刷 X，关于“氛围编程”（vibe-coding）的争论仍在继续。一方认为它只能做出演示，而非产品；另一方则调侃说，你根本不敢触碰生成的代码，生怕一切都会崩溃。如果你正试图发布一个营销网站，一个更值得关注的隐忧是：许多现有的氛围编程工具缺乏“事实来源”（source of truth）。正是这一缺失，导致了上线后的崩盘。

You paste a one-line brief into a chat. The model returns a polished homepage, a pricing table, three testimonials, and a line about thousands of happy customers. It looks shippable. Then you notice the monthly plan is a number you never provided. The quoted company does not exist. The feature list includes a dashboard you have not built. The site runs. The facts are fiction. The failure is architectural, not a bad prompt.

你将一段简短的说明粘贴到聊天框中。模型随即返回了一个精美的首页、价格表、三条用户评价，以及一行关于“数千名满意客户”的文案。看起来可以直接发布了。但随后你发现，月度套餐的价格是你从未提供过的；引用的公司并不存在；功能列表中包含了一个你根本没开发过的仪表盘。网站运行起来了，但事实却是虚构的。这种失败源于架构，而非提示词写得不好。

A coding agent is excellent at producing markup and components. It is not automatically excellent at remembering what is true about your business. In a typical vibe-coding loop it goes like this: You describe the company in a prompt. The model fills every gap so the page looks complete. You tidy typography, spacing, and the hero. You start a new chat, or the context window rolls over. The next page — or the next edit — invents a slightly different version of the same company.

编程代理擅长生成标记语言和组件，但它并不擅长自动记住关于你业务的真实情况。在典型的氛围编程循环中，过程通常是这样的：你在提示词中描述公司，模型填补所有空白，使页面看起来完整。你整理了排版、间距和首屏。当你开启新对话或上下文窗口刷新后，生成的下一个页面——或下一次编辑——就会编造出一个略有不同的公司版本。

There is no durable record of the legal name, the offers, the prices, the claims you will and will not make, who can be quoted, and which URLs already exist. The chat is the database. Chats are a terrible database. This is why "it runs" is not "it ships" for a marketing site. Shipping means a visitor, a journalist, or an AI answer engine can quote a sentence and you will still stand behind it next month.

这里没有关于法定名称、产品报价、价格、你承诺或不承诺的声明、谁可以被引用，以及哪些 URL 已经存在的持久记录。聊天记录成了数据库，而聊天记录是糟糕的数据库。这就是为什么对于营销网站来说，“能运行”并不等于“能发布”。发布意味着访客、记者或 AI 问答引擎引用了你的一句话，而你下个月依然能为这句话负责。

Lock the facts before you generate the pages. Before you ask any AI website builder or coding agent to produce a homepage, write down a source of truth a human can review. Keep it boring and specific. If a field is unknown, mark it unknown. A blank section is safer than a fluent lie.

在生成页面前锁定事实。在要求任何 AI 网站构建器或编程代理制作首页之前，先写下一份人类可以审核的事实来源。保持内容枯燥且具体。如果某个字段未知，就标记为未知。留白比流畅的谎言更安全。

The minimum brief I now refuse to skip: Legal and brand (Legal entity, public brand name, domain, locations, and the one sentence you use to describe what you sell). Offers (Each product or service in one paragraph, who it is for, and what it is not). Proof you can defend (Only testimonials, case studies, logos, and metrics you can point to a source for. If you cannot, the section stays empty). Commercial facts (Prices, plan names, what is included, what is not, and the refund or trial policy). Constraints (Claims the model must never invent: customer counts, awards, "trusted by" logos, performance numbers, invented case studies. URLs you already own and want to keep).

我拒绝跳过的最低限度说明包括：法律与品牌（法人实体、公开品牌名称、域名、地点，以及描述你销售内容的一句话）；产品报价（每个产品或服务用一段话描述，说明受众及非受众）；可辩护的证据（仅限有据可查的评价、案例研究、Logo 和指标，否则留空）；商业事实（价格、套餐名称、包含与不包含的内容、退款或试用政策）；约束条件（模型绝不能编造的声明：客户数量、奖项、信任背书 Logo、性能数据、虚构案例，以及你已拥有并希望保留的 URL）。

Fabricated specifics do not just fail to convert. They get quoted, and then corrected in public. Once those facts live outside the chat, page generation becomes a projection of a record, not a fresh improvisation. That is the difference between prompting a homepage and operating a site: Change a price in one place, and every plan table can be updated from the same record.

编造的细节不仅无法转化客户，还会被引用，随后在公开场合被纠正。一旦这些事实脱离了聊天记录，页面生成就变成了对记录的投射，而非即兴创作。这就是“提示生成首页”与“运营网站”的区别：在一个地方修改价格，所有套餐表都能根据同一记录更新。

If you are still deciding whether to launch one page or a small site, that choice is about traffic and offers, not about whether the facts are stable. The source of truth should be the same in either case. Launch on the URL you plan to keep, then add pages as sales and search questions show up.

如果你还在纠结是发布单页还是小型网站，这个选择取决于流量和产品，而不是事实是否稳定。无论哪种情况，事实来源都应保持一致。在计划保留的 URL 上发布，然后随着销售和搜索需求的出现逐步增加页面。

Ask the tool, or your own agent workflow, these questions before you trust it with a live domain: Where are the business facts stored after the first prompt? If I start a new chat, does it still know the approved price and the claims I forbade? What does it do when it does not know a number — leave a gap, ask, or invent one? When I correct a fact, do existing pages update from that correction?

在将实时域名交给工具或代理工作流之前，请问它这些问题：首次提示后，业务事实存储在哪里？如果我开启新对话，它是否还记得已批准的价格和禁止的声明？当它不知道某个数字时会怎么做——留空、询问还是编造？当我修正一个事实时，现有页面是否会随之更新？

If the only memory is the current thread, you do not have a website system. You have a demo generator. Those questions sit next to the ownership and export checks in our guide to evaluating an AI website builder. The demo will always look fine. The source of truth is what you are left holding after the context window is gone.

如果唯一的记忆就是当前对话线程，那你拥有的不是网站系统，而是演示生成器。这些问题与所有权和导出检查一起，构成了我们评估 AI 网站构建器的指南。演示看起来总是很完美，但当上下文窗口消失后，你手中剩下的才是事实来源。

We do not invent customer results or traffic lifts, and a fact marked unknown should stay blank rather than become a statistic. That is the product idea: an AI website builder that researches and builds from a record you can review, not from a disposable chat. Whether you start with one page or a full site, the facts should not have to be re-explained — or re-invented — each time you ask for a change.

我们不会编造客户成果或流量增长，标记为未知的事实应保持空白，而不是变成统计数据。这就是我们的产品理念：一个基于可审核记录而非一次性聊天记录进行研究和构建的 AI 网站生成器。无论你是从单页还是全站开始，每次要求修改时，都不应重新解释或重新编造事实。

If you are vibe-coding the site yourself, steal the same pattern. Keep a SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md (or a small CMS collection) that every prompt is required to read, and treat any fact not in that file as out of bounds. Write the brief above. Have a second person who knows the business strike anything they cannot defend. Decide landing page vs website, and reserve the URLs you want to keep. Generate the first preview only after the brief is approved. Reject any invented metric, logo, or quote on sight. Do not leave it to "fix later." Make one factual change — a price, a service name — and confirm every affected page updates from the same record.

如果你正在自己进行氛围编程，请借鉴这一模式。维护一个 SOURCE_OF_TRUTH.md 文件（或小型 CMS 合集），要求每个提示词都必须读取它，并将文件中不存在的事实视为禁区。写好上述说明，让另一位了解业务的人删掉他们无法辩护的内容。决定是做落地页还是网站，并预留好域名。只有在说明获得批准后才生成第一个预览。当场拒绝任何编造的指标、Logo 或引语。不要留到“以后再修”。尝试修改一个事实（如价格或服务名称），并确认所有受影响的页面都根据同一记录进行了更新。
