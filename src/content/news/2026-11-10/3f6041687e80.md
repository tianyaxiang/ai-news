计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.08561 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 5 日] 标题：通过正未标记学习实现面向建筑物损坏测绘的标签高效地理空间基础模型适配 作者：Hengwei Zhao, Qirui Wang, Zhuo Zheng, Xiaoyan Lu, Rusty Feagin, Wenzhe Jiao。

Abstract: Geospatial foundation models (GFMs) are reshaping remote sensing by shifting the field from task-specific modeling toward general-purpose geospatial intelligence. Mapping the damage to buildings stands to benefit substantially from this shift, as GFMs pretrained on large-scale Earth observation data provide generalizable geospatial representations that can facilitate effective responses to sudden-onset disasters.

摘要：地理空间基础模型（GFMs）正在重塑遥感领域，将该领域从特定任务建模转向通用地理空间智能。建筑物损坏测绘将从这一转变中受益匪浅，因为在大型地球观测数据上预训练的 GFM 提供了可泛化的地理空间表征，能够促进对突发灾害的有效响应。

Although parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) facilitates GFM adaptation, parameter efficiency does not necessarily translate into label efficiency. Building damage mapping remains challenging due to scarce pre-existing labeled data or limited time for annotation. When weak supervision is introduced to alleviate the labeling bottleneck, we identify the difficulty of learning sufficiently discriminative representations as a key challenge for label-efficient GFM adaptation.

尽管参数高效微调（PEFT）促进了 GFM 的适配，但参数效率并不一定转化为标签效率。由于现有的标记数据稀缺或标注时间有限，建筑物损坏测绘仍然具有挑战性。当引入弱监督来缓解标注瓶颈时，我们发现学习足够具有判别力的表征是实现标签高效 GFM 适配的关键挑战。

To address this challenge, we propose a Positive-Unlabeled GFM Adaptation (PUMA) framework that requires annotations for only a small number of damaged buildings. More specifically, PUMA integrates non-contrastive weakly supervised representation refinement to mitigate the impact of noisy supervision on representation learning from a gradient perspective, and latent-state-based label disambiguation to enable reversible supervision refinement without requiring class prior estimation.

为了解决这一挑战，我们提出了一个正未标记 GFM 适配（PUMA）框架，该框架仅需要少量受损建筑物的标注。更具体地说，PUMA 集成了非对比弱监督表征细化，从梯度角度减轻了噪声监督对表征学习的影响，并利用基于潜在状态的标签消歧，在无需类别先验估计的情况下实现可逆的监督细化。

Extensive experiments on disaster benchmark datasets from around the world demonstrated the effectiveness and robustness of PUMA. A real-world application to the 2025 Los Angeles wildfires further highlighted its potential to accurately map damage under highly limited supervision. More broadly, these findings suggest that weakly supervised PEFT offers a label-efficient pathway from GFMs to real-world applications.

在世界各地的灾害基准数据集上进行的广泛实验证明了 PUMA 的有效性和鲁棒性。在 2025 年洛杉矶野火中的实际应用进一步突显了其在极度有限的监督下准确测绘损坏情况的潜力。更广泛地说，这些发现表明，弱监督 PEFT 为从 GFM 到实际应用提供了一条标签高效的途径。
