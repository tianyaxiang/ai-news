Two of the top lawmakers backing the Pentagon’s Golden Dome missile defense shield are among the wave of Republicans leaving Congress in the coming Democratic takeover, raising questions about the future of President Donald Trump’s signature second-term defense initiative. The shakeup on Capitol Hill comes after military officials signaled in recent months that they might backpedal from one element Trump asked for in Golden Dome: the capability to shoot down missiles in their initial boost phase shortly after launch.

支持五角大楼“金穹”（Golden Dome）导弹防御系统的两位顶级立法者，正处于共和党人因民主党即将接管国会而离职的浪潮之中，这引发了人们对特朗普总统第二个任期标志性国防计划未来的质疑。在国会山发生人事变动之际，军方官员近几个月暗示，他们可能会在特朗普对“金穹”提出的一个要求上退缩，即在导弹发射后不久的初始助推阶段将其击落的能力。

The Golden Dome has escaped serious congressional scrutiny since Trump announced the program in January 2025, one week into his second term in the White House. That lack of scrutiny will change as Democrats take responsibility for writing the Pentagon’s budget, introduce limits on funding, and put forth bills to strip out Golden Dome’s most controversial component and give the program a new name. That’s the opinion of a half-dozen current and former officials in industry and government.

自特朗普于2025年1月（其白宫第二任期开始一周后）宣布该计划以来，“金穹”一直未受到国会的严格审查。随着民主党人开始负责起草五角大楼预算、引入资金限制，并提出法案以剔除“金穹”中最具争议的组成部分并为该计划重新命名，这种缺乏审查的局面将会改变。这是六位现任及前任行业和政府官员的观点。

Ars spoke with sources before and after last week’s congressional elections to get a sense of how a new Democratic Congress might make its imprint on missile defense. It’s important to know that much of what the Trump administration calls “Golden Dome” was already in development (or in some cases, operational) when Trump signed the executive order outlining the initiative 22 months ago.

Ars在上次国会选举前后采访了相关消息人士，以了解新一届民主党国会可能如何对导弹防御系统产生影响。需要指出的是，特朗普政府所称的“金穹”中，很大一部分在22个月前特朗普签署概述该倡议的行政命令时，就已经处于开发阶段（在某些情况下甚至是已投入使用）。

Trump’s order created a new program to consolidate the Pentagon’s existing missile defense efforts, accelerate work already underway on new Ground-Based Interceptors (GBIs), and deploy a new layer of orbiting sensors and Space-Based Interceptors (SBIs) to defend the US homeland against both traditional ballistic missiles and new emerging threats like drones and hypersonic weapons.

特朗普的命令创建了一个新项目，旨在整合五角大楼现有的导弹防御工作，加速已在进行中的新型陆基拦截器（GBIs）的研发，并部署一层新的轨道传感器和天基拦截器（SBIs），以保护美国本土免受传统弹道导弹以及无人机和高超音速武器等新兴威胁的侵害。

The first tranche of its funding—approximately $24 billion—was part of Trump’s so-called “One Big Beautiful Bill,” along with some of the White House’s other contentious policy priorities. The bill passed Congress on a party-line vote. This meant the budget for Golden Dome was not part of the normal military budgeting process in Congress, which usually involves both parties. Instead, it passed Congress through a controversial shortcut known as reconciliation.

其第一笔资金（约240亿美元）是特朗普所谓的“一个美好大法案”的一部分，该法案还包含白宫其他一些有争议的政策优先事项。该法案在国会以党派投票方式通过。这意味着“金穹”的预算并非国会正常军事预算程序的一部分，而该程序通常涉及两党。相反，它通过一种被称为“和解程序”（reconciliation）的争议性捷径在国会获得通过。

Seven House members of the caucus either lost their reelection bids or did not run for reelection at all in the 2026 midterms. These include the caucus’s co-chair, Rep. Jeff Crank (R-Colo.), and the chair of the House subcommittee overseeing Golden Dome, Rep. Scott DesJarlais (R-Tenn.). Both are among the Golden Dome’s biggest cheerleaders in Congress.

该核心小组的七名众议院议员在2026年中期选举中要么竞选连任失败，要么根本没有参加连任竞选。其中包括该小组的联合主席杰夫·克兰克（Jeff Crank，共和党，科罗拉多州）和负责监督“金穹”的众议院小组委员会主席斯科特·德贾莱斯（Scott DesJarlais，共和党，田纳西州）。两人都是国会中“金穹”计划最大的支持者。

“When you use the words Golden Dome, now it’s political, and when you politicize national security, you get a political response, so it is damaging,” said John Plumb, who supervised military space policy for the Biden administration, in a panel discussion at the CyberSat conference this week.

“当你使用‘金穹’这个词时，它就变成了政治问题；而当你将国家安全政治化时，你就会得到政治上的回应，所以这是有害的，”拜登政府时期负责军事太空政策的约翰·普拉姆（John Plumb）在本周的CyberSat会议小组讨论中说道。

The Congressional Budget Office concluded that a national missile defense system meeting the requirements set out in Trump’s executive order would cost about $1.2 trillion. The American Enterprise Institute released a report last year with a menu of potential Golden Dome architectures, ranging from a quarter-trillion dollars (without SBIs) to $3.6 trillion at the high end.

国会预算办公室得出的结论是，满足特朗普行政命令中要求的国家导弹防御系统将耗资约1.2万亿美元。美国企业研究所去年发布的一份报告列出了一系列潜在的“金穹”架构，成本从2500亿美元（不含天基拦截器）到最高3.6万亿美元不等。

The military envisions at least hundreds, and perhaps thousands, of Space-Based Interceptors in the uppermost layer of Golden Dome. These interceptors would loiter in orbit to wait for a missile attack. SBIs have never been placed into orbit before, despite decades of research and development. The idea has been around at least since the 1980s, when former President Ronald Reagan pushed the Strategic Defense Initiative, or “Star Wars,” weapons program.

军方设想在“金穹”的最上层部署至少数百甚至数千枚天基拦截器。这些拦截器将在轨道上徘徊，等待导弹袭击。尽管经过了数十年的研究和开发，天基拦截器此前从未被送入轨道。这一构想至少可以追溯到20世纪80年代，当时前总统罗纳德·里根推动了战略防御倡议，即“星球大战”武器计划。
