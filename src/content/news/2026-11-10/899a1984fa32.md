Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.08649 (cs) [Submitted on 5 Nov 2026] Title: VoluCompose: Constructing Compact Volumetric Visual Representations for Medical Image Understanding Authors: Zanting Ye, Yunrui Lin, Shengyuan Liu, Jing Hao, Chen Hu, Yefeng Zheng, Lijun Lu.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.08649 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 5 日] 标题：VoluCompose：构建用于医学图像理解的紧凑体积视觉表示 作者：Zanting Ye, Yunrui Lin, Shengyuan Liu, Jing Hao, Chen Hu, Yefeng Zheng, Lijun Lu。

Abstract: Medical vision-language models can interpret volumetric images, but processing full volumes is costly because hundreds of slices produce long, redundant visual-token sequences. Existing compression methods often couple content selection with budget determination. We observe that neighboring slices share visual features and that attention concentration varies across visual encoders.

摘要：医学视觉-语言模型能够解读体积图像，但处理完整体积的成本很高，因为数百个切片会产生冗长且冗余的视觉标记序列。现有的压缩方法通常将内容选择与预算确定耦合在一起。我们观察到相邻切片共享视觉特征，且注意力集中度在不同的视觉编码器之间存在差异。

We propose VoluCompose, a training-free, model-agnostic framework that separates what visual content to retain from how many tokens to allocate. Its hyperbolic token composition module groups contiguous slices into ordered support units and uses the Lorentz distance to account for feature magnitude and direction during token allocation, anchor selection, and refinement.

我们提出了 VoluCompose，这是一个无需训练、与模型无关的框架，它将保留哪些视觉内容与分配多少标记分离开来。其双曲标记组合模块将连续切片分组为有序的支持单元，并利用洛伦兹距离在标记分配、锚点选择和细化过程中考虑特征的大小和方向。

Its capacity control module uses visual-encoder attention entropy to allocate more tokens when attention is more dispersed. We evaluate VoluCompose on two tasks across three datasets and four backbones. Across nine calibrated settings, VoluCompose attains the highest mean quality-retention hypervolume among the compared methods.

其容量控制模块利用视觉编码器的注意力熵，在注意力更加分散时分配更多的标记。我们在三个数据集和四个骨干网络上的两个任务中评估了 VoluCompose。在九种校准设置中，VoluCompose 在对比方法中获得了最高的平均质量-保留超体积。

It achieves a mean relative quality of 121.68% against the uncompressed references while retaining 22.78% of visual tokens on average. Compression remains robust even at a 10% token budget. Across settings, the lowest ROUGE-L ratio to the uncompressed reference is 96.4% for VoluCompose. The corresponding minima for the compression baselines range from 15.0% to 70.5%.

它实现了相对于未压缩参考基准 121.68% 的平均相对质量，同时平均保留了 22.78% 的视觉标记。即使在 10% 的标记预算下，压缩效果依然稳健。在各项设置中，VoluCompose 相对于未压缩参考基准的最低 ROUGE-L 比率为 96.4%，而压缩基准的相应最小值范围为 15.0% 至 70.5%。
