# Jensen Huang’s Troubles Leave the AI Industry Reeling

No executive has pulled the strings in the artificial intelligence era quite like Nvidia’s CEO Jensen Huang. He parlayed the boom into incredible wealth, became a close confidant of President Donald Trump, and emerged as a prominent voice in US-China relations. That now all stands in the balance.

在人工智能时代，没有哪位高管能像英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）那样左右局势。他利用这一热潮积累了惊人的财富，成为唐纳德·特朗普总统的亲密知己，并成为美中关系中一位举足轻重的人物。而这一切现在都岌岌可危。

Over the last few days, Huang’s status as an AI titan has come under scrutiny amid an admission that he had an affair with Alice Huang, an executive at a Singapore-based company whom US intelligence services concluded was a Chinese intelligence asset.

过去几天里，黄仁勋作为人工智能巨头的地位受到了审视，此前他承认与一家新加坡公司的高管爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang）有染，而美国情报部门认定该女子是中国的情报资产。

Nvidia’s board of directors said that the company would launch an investigation into the incident. Huang, the company said, “fully supports the review and has committed to cooperating with it.” Huang earned $36 million last year, owned about 3.5 percent of Nvidia as of March, according to a corporate filing—which also noted that Huang is an at-will employee who can be let go anytime without severance.

英伟达董事会表示，公司将对该事件展开调查。公司称，黄仁勋“全力支持此次审查，并承诺予以配合”。根据一份公司文件显示，黄仁勋去年收入为3600万美元，截至3月持有英伟达约3.5%的股份；该文件还指出，黄仁勋是一名随意雇佣的员工，公司可以随时将其解雇且无需支付遣散费。

How exactly the investigation shakes out, and what it means for Huang’s future at the company, remains to be seen. But the incident at least puts his position at risk, and raises questions about how the industry will respond. “He’s the person who decides who gets the chips and who doesn't,” says David Yoffie, a professor at Harvard Business School who tracks the chip industry.

调查结果究竟如何，以及这对黄仁勋在公司的未来意味着什么，仍有待观察。但这一事件至少使他的职位面临风险，并引发了关于行业将如何应对的质疑。哈佛商学院研究芯片行业的教授大卫·约菲（David Yoffie）表示：“他就是那个决定谁能得到芯片、谁不能得到芯片的人。”

Nvidia, which Huang cofounded in 1993, controls 90 percent of the market for chips used in training frontier AI models. With those chips in short supply, Huang has the power to determine which areas of AI get a boost next. Nvidia has also bankrolled much of the AI buildout, investing billions in the very companies buying its chips, such as OpenAI and Anthropic. When Elon Musk wants to build a new data center, Yoffie says, he can put in a call to Huang.

黄仁勋于1993年共同创立的英伟达，控制着用于训练前沿人工智能模型的芯片市场90%的份额。由于这些芯片供应短缺，黄仁勋有权决定人工智能的哪些领域将获得下一步的发展动力。英伟达还为人工智能的建设提供了大量资金，向购买其芯片的公司（如OpenAI和Anthropic）投资了数十亿美元。约菲说，当埃隆·马斯克想要建立一个新的数据中心时，他可以直接给黄仁勋打电话。

Internally, Huang closely manages even small details at Nvidia, responds to emails from any one of its roughly 60,000 staffers, and raises his voice to get points across, according to current and former employees. He’s a regular on cable TV and at tech conferences, where he’s been trying to deflate concerns about an AI market bubble and boost the positioning of his company.

据现任和前任员工称，在公司内部，黄仁勋对英伟达的细节管理非常严格，会回复约6万名员工中任何一人的电子邮件，并会提高嗓门来表达观点。他经常出现在有线电视节目和科技会议上，试图消除人们对人工智能市场泡沫的担忧，并提升其公司的地位。

But Nvidia has no other executive anywhere close to Huang’s prominence, and no apparent potential successor. “We have no idea who would replace him,” Yoffie says. “You put someone new into that position, if you're a customer, who knows what that's going to mean.”

但英伟达没有其他高管能达到黄仁勋那样的知名度，也没有明显的潜在继任者。约菲说：“我们不知道谁能取代他。如果你是客户，换了一个新的人坐在那个位置上，谁知道这意味着什么呢。”

Ryan Fedasiuk, a senior fellow at the think tank Foundation for American Innovation, says that Huang has played such an important role—and become so close to the president—that he has become load-bearing for the entire US AI industry. “On the one hand, Huang is irreplaceable. He built Nvidia into the juggernaut it is today, and he was the visionary behind the GPU, which has sustained the intelligence revolution,” Fedasiuk says. But he says that the company, which is indispensable to the AI industry, has to come first. “He has to be replaceable as the CEO of the world's largest company, at $4 trillion in capex.”

智库“美国创新基金会”（Foundation for American Innovation）高级研究员瑞安·费达修克（Ryan Fedasiuk）表示，黄仁勋发挥了如此重要的作用，且与总统关系如此密切，以至于他已成为整个美国人工智能行业的支柱。费达修克说：“一方面，黄仁勋是不可替代的。他将英伟达打造成了今天的巨头，他是GPU背后的远见者，而GPU支撑了这场智能革命。”但他表示，对于人工智能行业不可或缺的英伟达公司必须放在首位。“作为这家拥有4万亿美元资本支出的全球最大公司的首席执行官，他必须是可以被替换的。”

Nvidia shares fell more than 4 percent on Monday, even as broader stock market indices rose. The board’s investigation will most likely focus on whether Huang violated any company policies, Yoffie says. But the FBI could also investigate whether laws were broken, and Congress may decide to look into whether Huang’s affair influenced the US government’s policy on selling chips to China.

周一，尽管大盘股指上涨，但英伟达股价仍下跌了超过4%。约菲表示，董事会的调查很可能集中在黄仁勋是否违反了任何公司政策上。但联邦调查局（FBI）也可能调查是否存在违法行为，国会也可能决定调查黄仁勋的婚外情是否影响了美国政府对华芯片销售的政策。

Nvidia didn’t immediately respond to WIRED’s request for comment. The scandal also raises serious questions about whether China may have gained sensitive information about US AI companies and the government’s strategy on China, Fedasiuk says.

英伟达没有立即回应《连线》（WIRED）杂志的置评请求。费达修克表示，这起丑闻还引发了严重的质疑：中国是否可能获取了有关美国人工智能公司及政府对华战略的敏感信息。

Huang’s affair appears especially damning given how outspoken he has been in recent years against calls for more aggressive technology export controls on China. His credibility on the topic could be permanently in doubt.

考虑到黄仁勋近年来一直直言不讳地反对对中国实施更激进的技术出口管制，他的这段婚外情显得尤为严重。他在这一议题上的信誉可能会永久性地受到质疑。

Both the first Trump administration and the Biden administration imposed export controls designed to limit China’s access to Nvidia’s chips, as well as technology required to mint advanced semiconductors. In his second term, however, Trump loosened these controls, giving China access to older generations of Nvidia chips; it was better to make China reliant on US technology, the argument went.

特朗普第一任政府和拜登政府都实施了出口管制，旨在限制中国获取英伟达的芯片以及制造先进半导体所需的技术。然而，特朗普在第二任期内放宽了这些限制，允许中国获取旧一代的英伟达芯片；其理由是，让中国依赖美国技术会更好。

Sam Bresnick, a research fellow at Georgetown University’s Center for Security and Emerging Technology, says the Huang scandal is likely to accelerate efforts to crack down on chip exports. “There was going to be more of an impetus to control what chips are getting sent to China anyway,” he says. “I expect there to be more scrutiny on what's going on, what's getting sent to China. I think there's also going to be more scrutiny from the Commerce Department.”

乔治城大学安全与新兴技术中心研究员萨姆·布雷斯尼克（Sam Bresnick）表示，黄仁勋的丑闻可能会加速打击芯片出口的力度。他说：“无论如何，控制运往中国的芯片的动力都会增强。我预计未来对相关情况以及运往中国的产品会有更严格的审查。我认为商务部也会加强监管。”
