# Microsoft are rejecting your mail

Skip to main content Microsoft are rejecting your mail November 9th, 2026 by pdw On Friday morning, we became aware that a significant amount of email to Microsoft-run mail systems was being rejected. This included mail to hotmail.com and outlook.com. The messages were being rejected with the following message: 550 5.7.1 Message rejected: Message lacks SPF/DKIM/DMARC Auth Records

跳过主要内容 Microsoft 正在拒收您的邮件 2026 年 11 月 9 日，作者：pdw。周五早上，我们注意到大量发往 Microsoft 运营的邮件系统的电子邮件被拒收。这包括发往 hotmail.com 和 outlook.com 的邮件。这些邮件被拒收时显示以下信息：550 5.7.1 Message rejected: Message lacks SPF/DKIM/DMARC Auth Records（邮件被拒：邮件缺少 SPF/DKIM/DMARC 验证记录）。

We checked our logs, and found that we’d seen this message over 500 times on Thursday, but not once before that. The affected messages all had completely valid SPF and DKIM, but did not have a DMARC record. We added a DMARC record with a “none” policy to some of the affected domains, and sure enough, mail was then accepted again. A quick fedi-survey confirmed that we were not alone. We don’t normally re-use blog photos, but this one seemed too perfect.

我们检查了日志，发现周四出现了超过 500 次此类信息，而在此之前一次都没有。受影响的邮件都拥有完全有效的 SPF 和 DKIM，但没有 DMARC 记录。我们为部分受影响的域名添加了策略为“none”的 DMARC 记录，果然，邮件随后又被接收了。一次快速的联邦宇宙调查证实，我们并非个例。我们通常不会重复使用博客照片，但这张照片似乎太贴切了。

DMARC records are a way of publicly stating what you want recipients to do with email that claims to be from your domain, but which doesn’t pass verification checks (DKIM or SPF) and thus is potentially forged. Your DMARC policy can be one of the following: reject – recipients should reject mail that doesn’t pass DKIM or SPF. This is the real purpose of DMARC: if you’re confident that all legitimate outbound mail is properly authenticated, you can tell recipients to assume that anything else is forged, and bin it. quarantine – recipients should treat such mail as suspicious. none – your domain has no policy on what to do with mail that doesn’t pass DKIM or SPF. Publishing a policy of none is effectively the same as not publishing a policy at all.

DMARC 记录是一种公开声明的方式，用于告知收件人应如何处理那些声称来自您的域名，但未通过验证检查（DKIM 或 SPF）且因此可能被伪造的电子邮件。您的 DMARC 策略可以是以下之一：reject（拒绝）——收件人应拒绝未通过 DKIM 或 SPF 的邮件。这是 DMARC 的真正目的：如果您确信所有合法的出站邮件都已正确验证，您可以告诉收件人将其他任何邮件视为伪造并将其丢弃。quarantine（隔离）——收件人应将此类邮件视为可疑邮件。none（无）——您的域名对于未通过 DKIM 或 SPF 的邮件没有处理策略。发布“none”策略实际上等同于根本不发布策略。

Microsoft are now rejecting mail without a DMARC policy, but accepting mail with a policy of none. This is a massive and pointless change in their policy for incoming mail, made with no notice whatsoever. Last year Microsoft, introduced a policy that required domains sending more than 5,000 messages per day to have a DMARC record. This change was announced in advance. This latest change appears to be completely unannounced, which makes us wonder if it was even intentional. Of course, trying to contact Microsoft in order to resolve mail issues such as this is futile.

Microsoft 现在正在拒收没有 DMARC 策略的邮件，但会接收策略为“none”的邮件。这是他们针对入站邮件策略的一项重大且毫无意义的变更，且完全没有事先通知。去年，Microsoft 引入了一项政策，要求每天发送超过 5,000 封邮件的域名必须拥有 DMARC 记录。该变更曾提前宣布。而最近的这次变更似乎完全没有预告，这让我们怀疑它是否是有意为之。当然，试图联系 Microsoft 以解决此类邮件问题是徒劳的。

Update: apparently it wasn’t intentional, but they haven’t figured out how to roll it back yet. We’ve just applied p=none DMARC records to a large number of our customers’ domains. We generally try to avoid making unsolicited changes to DNS zones, but in this case we feel that it is the best option. If you’re in contact with Microsoft users affected by this, please encourage them to complain to Microsoft; it’s very likely that they are losing other legitimate email from other providers because of this unannounced change. Better still, encourage them to move to an email provider that is pro-active in investigating email deliverability issues. Posted in Email, Services

更新：显然这不是故意的，但他们还没想出如何回滚。我们刚刚为大量客户的域名应用了 p=none 的 DMARC 记录。我们通常尽量避免对 DNS 区域进行未经请求的更改，但在这种情况下，我们认为这是最佳选择。如果您与受此影响的 Microsoft 用户有联系，请鼓励他们向 Microsoft 投诉；很有可能因为这一未预告的变更，他们正在丢失来自其他提供商的合法邮件。更好的是，鼓励他们转向一家在调查邮件送达问题上积极主动的电子邮件提供商。发布于 Email, Services
