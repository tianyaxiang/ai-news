RBA Warns AI Productivity Gains Unproven as Data Centre Capex Pressures Inflation & Financial Stability

澳储行警告：人工智能生产力收益尚未得到证实，数据中心资本支出加剧通胀与金融稳定压力

Speaking at the UBS Australasia Conference in Sydney, Reserve Bank of Australia (RBA) Assistant Governor (Economic) Sarah Hunter cautioned that empirical evidence of artificial intelligence meaningfully lifting macroeconomic productivity or displacing net employment across Australia remains limited.

在悉尼举行的瑞银澳大利亚会议上，澳大利亚储备银行（RBA）助理行长（经济）莎拉·亨特（Sarah Hunter）警告称，目前尚缺乏实证证据表明人工智能能够显著提升澳大利亚的宏观经济生产力或导致净就业流失。

However, the central bank highlighted that the unprecedented domestic surge in AI data centre capital expenditure—surging tenfold over recent years—is exerting intense aggregate demand pressure on Australia's power grid, construction sector, and engineering workforce.

然而，央行强调，澳大利亚国内人工智能数据中心资本支出的空前激增（近年来增长了十倍）正对澳大利亚的电网、建筑行业和工程劳动力施加巨大的总需求压力。

Hunter warned that this infrastructure boom risks stoking persistent inflation and keeping interest rates elevated, while increased corporate interconnectedness through shared cloud and foundation model providers introduces novel systemic risks to domestic financial stability.

亨特警告称，这种基础设施繁荣可能会加剧持续性通胀并使利率保持高位，同时通过共享云和基础模型提供商带来的企业间互联性增强，也为国内金融稳定引入了新的系统性风险。

Bond University Legal Study Demands AI Governance Overhaul and Dedicated Australian Legislation

邦德大学法律研究要求彻底改革人工智能治理并制定专门的澳大利亚法律

A major research study published in the Bond Law Review by Dr. David Millhouse, Honorary Adjunct Senior Research Fellow at Bond University's Faculty of Law, has issued an urgent call for dedicated Australian artificial intelligence legislation.

邦德大学法学院名誉兼职高级研究员大卫·米尔豪斯（David Millhouse）博士在《邦德法律评论》上发表的一项重大研究，紧急呼吁制定专门的澳大利亚人工智能法律。

The paper, titled Artificial Intelligence and the Law, warns that existing Australian common law, corporations law, and privacy statutes fail to keep pace with algorithmic deployment in corporate boardrooms and professional services.

这篇题为《人工智能与法律》的论文警告称，现有的澳大利亚普通法、公司法和隐私法规已无法跟上企业董事会和专业服务领域算法部署的步伐。

Millhouse stressed that "AI governance is not optional and not delegable," warning that company directors, trustees, and legal practitioners face escalating personal legal exposure for negligence, algorithmic bias, and unvetted automated decisions as organisations adopt autonomous agents without statutory guardrails.

米尔豪斯强调，“人工智能治理不是可选项，也不能被转嫁”，并警告称，随着组织在没有法定护栏的情况下采用自主智能体，公司董事、受托人和法律从业者因疏忽、算法偏见和未经审查的自动化决策而面临的个人法律风险正在不断升级。

主权技术资本外流：尽管获得2000万澳元国家重建基金（NRF）资助，澳大利亚量子先驱Diraq仍在美国注册成立

Silicon quantum pioneer Diraq, spun out from the University of New South Wales (UNSW) by Professor Andrew Dzurak, announced its corporate re-domiciliation as a US-incorporated entity.

由安德鲁·祖拉克（Andrew Dzurak）教授从新南威尔士大学（UNSW）分拆出来的硅量子先驱Diraq宣布，其公司总部迁至美国，成为一家美国注册实体。

The move comes only months after the Albanese Government's flagship AU$15 billion National Reconstruction Fund (NRF) made its maiden AU$20 million equity investment into Diraq.

此举距离阿尔巴尼斯政府旗下规模150亿澳元的国家重建基金（NRF）首次向Diraq进行2000万澳元的股权投资仅过去数月。

The development has re-ignited national debate across Canberra and the local DeepTech ecosystem regarding sovereign tech retention, IP security, and whether Australian venture markets can provide the massive scale of late-stage compute and hardware capital required to retain cutting-edge AI and quantum assets domestically.

这一进展重新点燃了堪培拉及当地深科技生态系统关于主权技术保留、知识产权安全，以及澳大利亚风险投资市场是否能够提供保留尖端人工智能和量子资产所需的后期计算和硬件资本的全国性辩论。

ANZ Banking Group officially revealed that it has stood up an “enterprise AI control plane” to provide unified operational oversight across its expanding fleet of foundation models and autonomous AI agents.

澳新银行集团正式宣布，已建立一个“企业人工智能控制平面”，为其不断扩展的基础模型和自主人工智能智能体群提供统一的运营监督。

Announced by Group CEO Nuno Matos, the control plane augments ANZ’s existing AI gateways and governance councils by delivering real-time technical telemetry, cost allocation, prompt and token tracking, and strict traceability of agentic decisions.

该控制平面由集团首席执行官努诺·马托斯（Nuno Matos）宣布，通过提供实时技术遥测、成本分配、提示词与Token追踪以及智能体决策的严格可追溯性，增强了澳新银行现有的AI网关和治理委员会的功能。

The architecture addresses critical compliance requirements under APRA CPS 234 information security standards as financial institutions scale from simple conversational interfaces to multi-step autonomous execution in credit analysis, fraud detection, and developer workflows.

随着金融机构从简单的对话界面扩展到信贷分析、欺诈检测和开发工作流中的多步自主执行，该架构满足了APRA CPS 234信息安全标准下的关键合规要求。

澳大利亚信号局（ASD）和澳大利亚网络安全中心（ACSC）发布了增强版指南，以可证明的基于结果的验证取代了第三方软件供应链的清单式合规。
