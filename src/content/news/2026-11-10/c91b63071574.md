In the financial sector, the utilization of real-world data faces significant constraints due to strict privacy regulations, compliance requirements, and the need for advanced domain expertise. In this context, the development of agentic AI in the financial domain requires synthetic data capable of avoiding the risks of using real data while reflecting demographics, household structures, and asset statuses to reproduce diverse, realistic financial scenarios.

在金融领域，由于严格的隐私法规、合规要求以及对高级领域专业知识的需求，真实世界数据的利用面临巨大限制。在此背景下，金融领域代理式 AI 的开发需要能够规避使用真实数据风险的合成数据，同时这些数据需反映人口统计学、家庭结构和资产状况，以重现多样化且真实的金融场景。

To address these challenges, Elith and NVIDIA have released Nemotron-Personas-Finance-Japan, a financial domain extension of the open-source persona dataset "Nemotron-Personas-Japan." To our knowledge, it is the first open persona dataset at million-record scale that pairs Japan’s official statistics with household finance and financial literacy data. Built upon official government statistics, this dataset was developed using NeMo SDG-PGMs, a probabilistic graphical model framework for synthetic data generation (SDG), and NeMo Data Designer, an enterprise synthetic data generation system.

为了应对这些挑战，Elith 和 NVIDIA 发布了 Nemotron-Personas-Finance-Japan，这是开源角色数据集“Nemotron-Personas-Japan”的金融领域扩展版本。据我们所知，这是首个百万记录规模的开放角色数据集，它将日本官方统计数据与家庭财务及金融素养数据相结合。该数据集基于政府官方统计数据，利用用于合成数据生成（SDG）的概率图形模型框架 NeMo SDG-PGMs 以及企业级合成数据生成系统 NeMo Data Designer 开发而成。

Furthermore, to enhance the practical utility of the dataset in financial use cases, it was designed by incorporating insights and feedback provided by leading domestic financial institutions and industry associations, including Sompo Japan, Tokio Marine Holdings, Bank of Fukuoka, Mizuho Financial Group, Meiji Yasuda Life Insurance, and the Financial Data Utilization Association (FDUA). This dataset expands the environment where Japanese developers and enterprises can develop and evaluate AI tailored to local and industry requirements, without using real customer data. Released under the CC BY 4.0 license, it is available for commercial use. From building conversational agents to customer simulation and model evaluation, it supports practical financial AI development.

此外，为了增强该数据集在金融用例中的实际效用，其设计过程中纳入了多家国内领先金融机构和行业协会提供的见解与反馈，包括日本财产保险（Sompo Japan）、东京海上控股、福冈银行、瑞穗金融集团、明治安田生命保险以及金融数据利用协会（FDUA）。该数据集扩展了日本开发者和企业在不使用真实客户数据的情况下，针对本地和行业需求开发及评估 AI 的环境。该数据集以 CC BY 4.0 许可协议发布，可用于商业用途。从构建对话代理到客户模拟和模型评估，它为实际的金融 AI 开发提供了支持。

The Nemotron-Personas collection, including Nemotron-Personas-Finance-Japan, is designed to seamlessly integrate with the user simulation framework NeMo User Sim, enabling instant execution of simulations drawn from diverse distributions consistent with the real world.

包括 Nemotron-Personas-Finance-Japan 在内的 Nemotron-Personas 系列旨在与用户模拟框架 NeMo User Sim 无缝集成，从而能够即时执行符合现实世界多样化分布的模拟。

What’s in the Dataset? 9M personas total (1M records x 9 personas each), written in natural Japanese 50 fields per record: 9 persona fields, 11 persona attributes, and 30 contextual fields based on official demographic and labor statistics ~1.5B tokens total, including ~750M persona tokens ~950k unique names 1600+ occupation categories reflecting Japan's workforce Comprehensive coverage across demographic, regional, asset distribution, and personality trait axes Diverse persona types: Occupations, sports, arts, travel, cooking, finance, financial behavior, and financial concerns Natural language persona attributes: Cultural background, skills and expertise, career goals and aspirations, hobbies and interests, household/parental/financial background, attitudes toward money, and financial planning Available for both commercial and non-commercial use under the CC BY 4.0 license

数据集包含什么？总计 900 万个角色（100 万条记录 x 每个记录 9 个角色），以自然日语编写。每条记录包含 50 个字段：9 个角色字段、11 个角色属性以及 30 个基于官方人口统计和劳动力统计的上下文字段。总计约 15 亿个 Token，包括约 7.5 亿个角色 Token。约 95 万个唯一姓名。1600 多种反映日本劳动力的职业类别。涵盖人口统计、区域、资产分布和人格特质维度的全面覆盖。多样化的角色类型：职业、体育、艺术、旅行、烹饪、金融、金融行为和金融顾虑。自然语言角色属性：文化背景、技能与专长、职业目标与抱负、爱好与兴趣、家庭/父母/财务背景、金钱态度和财务规划。在 CC BY 4.0 许可下可用于商业和非商业用途。

How We Built It Data Generation Pipeline It was built using NVIDIA’s framework for synthetic data generation, NeMo Data Designer. This compound AI system enables complex Jinja templating, Pydantic validation, structured outputs, automated retries, and support for multiple generation backends. This is the essential toolset required for generating such large-scale synthetic datasets. In addition, the following models were utilized: NeMo SDG-PGMs (Apache-2.0): A probabilistic graphical model framework for statistically grounded data generation gemma-4-31B-it (Apache-2.0): For Japanese text generation

我们是如何构建它的？数据生成流水线。它是使用 NVIDIA 的合成数据生成框架 NeMo Data Designer 构建的。这个复合 AI 系统支持复杂的 Jinja 模板、Pydantic 验证、结构化输出、自动重试以及对多个生成后端的支持。这是生成此类大规模合成数据集所需的基本工具集。此外，还使用了以下模型：NeMo SDG-PGMs (Apache-2.0)：用于统计基础数据生成的概率图形模型框架；gemma-4-31B-it (Apache-2.0)：用于日语文本生成。

Enhanced Cultural Context Nemotron-Personas-Finance-Japan was designed to align with Japan's official demographic and labor statistics while taking into account key considerations essential for AI training: Education: Where national statistics broadly aggregate degree levels, we introduced finer granularity to enable the model to reflect diverse educational pathways. Occupation: We incorporated additional categories (such as business owners and specialized professions) to broaden the spectrum of occupations used for training. Life Stages: We modeled scenarios that rarely surface prominently in statistics, such as students, retirees, and periods of unemployment, to represent more realistic personas. Household Finances: In addition to individual income and spending propensity, we incorporated household-level financial and real assets to replicate diverse economic backgrounds. Digital & Financial Literacy: By including internet and smartphone usage along with financial literacy, we enabled the model to precisely capture consumer profiles in financial use cases.

增强的文化背景。Nemotron-Personas-Finance-Japan 的设计旨在与日本官方人口统计和劳动力统计保持一致，同时考虑了 AI 训练的关键要素：教育：在国家统计数据广泛汇总学位水平的地方，我们引入了更细的粒度，使模型能够反映多样化的教育路径。职业：我们纳入了额外的类别（如企业主和专业职业），以拓宽用于训练的职业范围。人生阶段：我们模拟了统计数据中很少显著出现的场景，如学生、退休人员和失业期，以呈现更真实的角色。家庭财务：除了个人收入和消费倾向外，我们还纳入了家庭层面的金融和实物资产，以复制多样化的经济背景。数字与金融素养：通过将互联网和智能手机使用情况与金融素养相结合，我们使模型能够精确捕捉金融用例中的消费者画像。

Private By Design Every persona is fully synthetic. No real individuals are represented, and the dataset is designed to reduce privacy risk. Demographics and household finances follow official statistics and public survey data, and names follow real Japanese naming patterns. Personas are built from these distributions, not from records about real people.

隐私设计。每个角色都是完全合成的。不代表任何真实个人，且数据集旨在降低隐私风险。人口统计和家庭财务遵循官方统计数据和公共调查数据，姓名遵循真实的日本命名模式。角色是根据这些分布构建的，而非来自关于真实个人的记录。

Who This Data Is For Nemotron-Personas-Finance-Japan is built first for teams creating financial AI in Japan, at banks and insurers and at the startups and integrators that serve them. Each persona carries household finances and financial literacy alongside demographics. Teams can use them to build customer-service agents, evaluate sales conversations/marketing

该数据面向谁？Nemotron-Personas-Finance-Japan 首先是为在日本创建金融 AI 的团队而构建的，包括银行、保险公司以及为它们提供服务的初创公司和集成商。每个角色除了人口统计信息外，还携带家庭财务和金融素养数据。团队可以使用它们来构建客户服务代理、评估销售对话/营销。
