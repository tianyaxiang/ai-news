# SpaceX now fundamentally an "infrastructure company," senior official says

For a long time, SpaceX has been synonymous with the development of newer, larger, and ever-more-reusable rockets. But that began to change over the last decade with the introduction of Starlink, and the change has accelerated during the last 18 months as the company has pivoted to data and artificial intelligence.

长期以来，SpaceX 一直是更新、更大、且更具可重复使用性的火箭开发的代名词。但随着星链（Starlink）的推出，这种情况在过去十年中开始发生变化，并且在过去 18 个月中，随着公司转向数据和人工智能领域，这种变化进一步加速。

At last week’s UP.Summit conference in Florida, the vice president of launch at SpaceX, Kiko Dontchev, offered some of the most revealing comments to date about the company’s changing focus, how it is embracing AI, and why the Falcon 9 rocket—which has dominated global launch for a decade and remains the world’s preeminent launch vehicle—is firmly on the road to retirement.

在佛罗里达州上周举行的 UP.Summit 会议上，SpaceX 发射副总裁基科·东切夫（Kiko Dontchev）发表了迄今为止最坦率的评论，谈到了公司重点的转变、如何拥抱人工智能，以及为什么猎鹰 9 号火箭——这款统治全球发射市场十年并仍是世界首屈一指的运载火箭——正坚定地走在退役的道路上。

“In the last couple years, when I talk about SpaceX, yes, we are a rocket company, but fundamentally we are an infrastructure company at this point,” Dontchev said. “We’re building infrastructure on the ground and low Earth orbit, soon on the Moon, and obviously eventually Mars.”

“在过去几年里，当我谈论 SpaceX 时，是的，我们是一家火箭公司，但从根本上说，我们现在是一家基础设施公司，”东切夫说。“我们正在地面和近地轨道上建设基础设施，很快就会在月球上，显然最终会在火星上建设。”

Using AI at SpaceX Earlier this year, SpaceX acquired one of founder Elon Musk’s other companies, xAI. SpaceX engineers were already using xAI’s tools, Dontchev said, but in recent months, employees have really started to figure out how artificial intelligence can speed up their work.

在 SpaceX 使用人工智能：今年早些时候，SpaceX 收购了创始人埃隆·马斯克旗下的另一家公司 xAI。东切夫表示，SpaceX 的工程师们已经在利用 xAI 的工具，但近几个月来，员工们真正开始摸索人工智能如何加速他们的工作。

“What’s happened at SpaceX is we’ve really gone through a huge acceleration of adoption,” Dontchev said. “Even just a year ago, we weren’t adopting it that quickly, but really starting this year, we started to adopt it extremely rapidly. And the acceleration we have seen in our ability to do engineering is unlike anything I’ve ever seen at the company in my 16 years.”

“在 SpaceX 发生的事情是，我们确实经历了采用率的巨大飞跃，”东切夫说。“就在一年前，我们还没有这么快地采用它，但从今年开始，我们开始极其迅速地采用它。我们在工程能力方面所看到的加速，是我在公司 16 年来从未见过的。”

Tasks that would take SpaceX employees hours to days now take seconds to minutes, he said. And it’s not just the engineers. Dontchev said the work of identifying foreign object debris, or FOD, in aerospace parts has been largely automated using the AI assistant Grok. This debris can cause catastrophic damage to engines and other spacecraft components.

他说，过去需要 SpaceX 员工花费数小时甚至数天才能完成的任务，现在只需几秒钟到几分钟。而且不仅仅是工程师，东切夫表示，识别航空航天部件中外来物碎片（FOD）的工作，已通过人工智能助手 Grok 基本实现了自动化。这些碎片可能会对发动机和其他航天器组件造成灾难性损坏。

“We’ve automated being able to detect FOD,” he said. “We’ll have an inspector go in and borescope a tube, and we find that humans are about 94 percent perfect (at) finding a piece of it. Grok analyzing the same video is 99.9 percent perfect.”

“我们已经实现了 FOD 检测的自动化，”他说。“我们会让检查员进去用内窥镜检查管道，我们发现人类在发现碎片方面的准确率约为 94%。而 Grok 分析同样的视频，准确率则达到了 99.9%。”

SpaceX has also automated visual inspections of other hardware it produces, such as Starlink customer terminals, due to similar gains in accuracy. Dontchev said AI is now being used at every level of the company’s operations.

由于准确性得到了类似的提升，SpaceX 还对其生产的其他硬件（如星链用户终端）的目视检查实现了自动化。东切夫表示，人工智能现在已被用于公司运营的各个层面。

“We’ve truly started to integrate it to where every single person, including the technicians and the technician leaders, are starting to use it in their day-to-day, and it’s completely leveled the playing field and accelerated people’s growth,” he said.

“我们已经真正开始将其整合到每个人——包括技术人员和技术主管——的日常工作中，这完全拉平了竞争环境，并加速了员工的成长，”他说。

Since SpaceX’s founding in 2002, a community of spaceflight enthusiasts gravitated to the company because of Musk’s vision for humanity becoming a multiplanetary species, beginning with the settlement of Mars. But concern has grown in this group that SpaceX is drifting away from that vision.

自 2002 年 SpaceX 成立以来，一群航天爱好者被该公司吸引，是因为马斯克关于人类成为多行星物种的愿景，而这一愿景始于火星殖民。但该群体中越来越担心 SpaceX 正在偏离这一愿景。

Musk has recently made a hard pivot toward the deployment of orbital data centers, envisioning hundreds of thousands of solar-powered satellites providing computing services to users on Earth. Musk has largely stopped talking about sending humans to Mars, except in passing. Dontchev insisted that human settlement and Mars remain part of the core of SpaceX’s aspirations.

马斯克最近将重心转向了轨道数据中心的部署，设想通过数十万颗太阳能卫星为地球用户提供计算服务。除了偶尔提及，马斯克已基本不再谈论将人类送往火星。东切夫坚称，人类定居和火星仍然是 SpaceX 核心愿景的一部分。

“We have come to understand that this tool is critical to us accelerating our pace of innovation,” he said of artificial intelligence. “Without AI, I think we still would have gotten to the Moon. I think we still get to Mars with SI. We’re going to get there so much faster.”

“我们已经认识到，这个工具对于我们加速创新步伐至关重要，”他在谈到人工智能时说。“如果没有人工智能，我认为我们仍然会到达月球。我认为即使没有它，我们最终也能到达火星。但有了它，我们将更快地到达那里。”

Why Falcon 9 is fading Dontchev arrived at SpaceX just before the company flew its first Falcon 9 rocket in mid-2010. But because the company was never able to solve second-stage reuse, SpaceX needs to move on, he said.

为什么猎鹰 9 号正在淡出：东切夫在 2010 年年中 SpaceX 发射第一枚猎鹰 9 号火箭之前加入了公司。但他表示，由于公司始终无法解决二级火箭的重复使用问题，SpaceX 需要继续前进。

“Falcon 9, I love that rocket to heart,” he said. “I’ve launched many of them, all of them, in fact. But it’s still not fully reusable. We still throw that second stage away every single flight. Starship—we’re going to catch both the first stage and the second stage.”

“猎鹰 9 号，我发自内心地热爱那枚火箭，”他说。“我发射过很多次，事实上，所有的发射我都参与过。但它仍然无法完全重复使用。我们每次飞行仍然要丢弃二级火箭。而星舰（Starship）——我们将回收第一级和第二级。”

For 100 launches a year, it is possible to fly a partially reusable rocket, he said. But for a company that aspires to launch thousands of rockets a year, and eventually maybe even 10,000, the cost and time lost in manufacturing a new second stage for every launch is simply not sustainable.

他说，对于每年 100 次的发射任务，使用部分可重复使用的火箭是可行的。但对于一家渴望每年发射数千枚、最终甚至可能达到 10,000 枚火箭的公司来说，每次发射都要制造一个新的二级火箭所带来的成本和时间损失是根本无法持续的。

It is also significant that Dontchev spoke less about Starship’s payload capacity and more about its productivity per launch. The first orbital Starship launch, in late September, carried larger Starlink V3 satellites. And in the aggregate, this single mission carried 10 times the bandwidth of the Starlink payload launched by a Falcon 9.

同样值得注意的是，东切夫谈论星舰的载荷能力较少，而更多地谈论其单次发射的生产力。9 月下旬进行的首次星舰轨道发射任务搭载了更大的星链 V3 卫星。总的来说，这次单次任务所承载的带宽是猎鹰 9 号发射的星链载荷的 10 倍。

Flying Starship from Florida, he said, will further improve capacity compared to Texas launches. Launching from Florida offers more favorable trajectories to certain orbits, potentially allowing Starship to carry more satellites without expending propellant on dogleg maneuvers. Dontchev said a single Florida Starship launch will be able to carry up to 20 times the bandwidth of a single Falcon 9.

他说，与德克萨斯州的发射相比，从佛罗里达州发射星舰将进一步提高运载能力。从佛罗里达发射可以为某些轨道提供更有利的轨迹，使星舰有可能在不消耗推进剂进行变轨机动的情况下携带更多卫星。东切夫表示，佛罗里达州单次星舰发射所能承载的带宽最高可达单次猎鹰 9 号发射的 20 倍。

To that end, the pad infrastructure for Starship’s first launch tower at Kennedy Space Center’s Launch Complex 39A is nearly finished. The company is also developing two additional pads in Florida. “Pad 39A is effectively complete,” he said. “We’re going to transport boosters and ships over barge here to the Cape. We’re hoping to actually do the first transports this year in December, and then maybe even fly by the end of the year.”

为此，肯尼迪航天中心 39A 发射台的星舰首个发射塔的基础设施已接近完工。该公司还在佛罗里达州开发另外两个发射台。“39A 发射台实际上已经完工，”他说。“我们将通过驳船将助推器和飞船运送到卡纳维拉尔角。我们希望在今年 12 月进行首次运输，甚至可能在年底前进行飞行。”
