It’s difficult to overstate Nvidia’s power in today’s AI landscape. The company’s chips are the bedrock upon which advanced AI technology in the US is being built, and demand has consistently outpaced supply for years. Nvidia has also been an influential investor in a wide range of up-and-coming AI companies in recent years, with about $99 billion worth of equity investments in frontier AI labs, neoclouds, infrastructure companies, and more. Its chief executive has even become close with President Donald Trump.

在当今的人工智能领域，英伟达（Nvidia）的影响力难以估量。该公司的芯片是美国构建先进人工智能技术的基石，多年来需求一直持续超过供应。近年来，英伟达还成为了一系列新兴人工智能公司的重要投资者，在尖端人工智能实验室、新一代云服务商、基础设施公司等方面拥有约990亿美元的股权投资。其首席执行官甚至与唐纳德·特朗普总统建立了密切关系。

This is why, as both cofounder and longtime CEO of Nvidia, Jensen Huang would be a valuable target for a foreign adversary with sights on the American AI industry. Huang is in hot water after news broke of his extramarital affair with an alleged Chinese spy, Alice Huang (no relation to Jensen) is an executive at Megaspeed, a Singapore-based cloud provider and a top Nvidia customer.

正因如此，作为英伟达的联合创始人兼长期首席执行官，黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）对于觊觎美国人工智能产业的外国对手来说，是一个极具价值的目标。在有消息称黄仁勋与一名涉嫌为中国间谍的女子爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang，与黄仁勋无亲属关系）发生婚外情后，他陷入了困境。爱丽丝·黄是总部位于新加坡的云服务提供商Megaspeed的高管，而该公司也是英伟达的重要客户。

Questions are swirling around how the affair may have influenced Huang’s reported push to sell advanced chips to China — and potentially even influenced President Trump’s own decisions surrounding AI export controls. US officials believe the Megaspeed executive is a foreign intelligence asset that has funneled information straight to the Chinese government, per The New York Times.

外界纷纷质疑这段婚外情是否影响了黄仁勋推动向中国销售先进芯片的计划，甚至可能影响了特朗普总统在人工智能出口管制方面的决策。据《纽约时报》报道，美国官员认为这名Megaspeed高管是一名外国情报人员，直接向中国政府输送信息。

“It’s not entirely surprising that the CEO of arguably one of the most important, if not the most important, AI chip companies is the target of some sort of intelligence-gathering from countries like China,” Hanna Dohmen, a senior research analyst at CSET, said in an interview with The Verge.

“作为人工智能芯片领域最重要（如果不是唯一最重要的话）的公司之一，其首席执行官成为中国等国家情报搜集的目标，这并不完全令人惊讶，”CSET高级研究分析师汉娜·多门（Hanna Dohmen）在接受《The Verge》采访时表示。

Nvidia spokespeople had said Huang’s relationship with the Megaspeed executive was strictly business. Their interactions were described as “not unusual or unexpected” in statements to the press. Nvidia’s line was, “Meetings with customers are part of a CEO’s job.” But at some point, their relationship became personal.

英伟达发言人曾表示，黄仁勋与这名Megaspeed高管的关系纯属业务往来。在给媒体的声明中，他们的互动被描述为“并不罕见或出人意料”。英伟达的口径是：“与客户会面是首席执行官工作的一部分。”但在某个时间点，他们的关系变得私人化了。

The revelation is particularly concerning because of Huang’s lobbying role. He’d been trying to convince the Trump administration and Congress to loosen export controls for sending advanced semiconductors to China, ostensibly so that Nvidia could sell more chips. We now know this was happening around the same time that Huang was spotted at the Taiwan dinner with Alice Huang in May 2025.

这一披露之所以特别令人担忧，是因为黄仁勋在游说中扮演的角色。他一直试图说服特朗普政府和国会放宽向中国出口先进半导体的管制，表面上是为了让英伟达能销售更多芯片。我们现在知道，这与2025年5月黄仁勋被目击与爱丽丝·黄在台湾共进晚餐的时间大致吻合。
