Abstract:We propose, HaNDF, object-conditional neural distance fields as data-driven priors for modeling the plausible hand-object configurations, in the product manifold of hand articulations. While recent unconditional generative priors showed success in modeling human pose, hands are versatile articulated bodies often found in interaction with other objects. This makes it crucial to develop a generative prior which can model the plausible hand poses conditioned on the object under interaction.

摘要：我们提出了 HaNDF，即物体条件下的神经距离场，作为一种数据驱动的先验，用于在手部关节的乘积流形中建模合理的手部-物体配置。虽然最近的无条件生成先验在人体姿态建模方面取得了成功，但手部是多功能的关节体，经常与其他物体发生交互。因此，开发一种能够根据交互物体建模合理手部姿态的生成先验至关重要。

To this end, our HaNDF proposes a geometry-aware, object-conditioned neural distance field, representing the plausible articulations in the zero-level set of a conditional field. We leverage tools from Riemannian geometry to (i) represent plausible hands in the zero level-set of an object-induced neural field, and (ii) project a given pose onto this field. Using HaNDF, we can also optimize for a plausible condition, determining the object under interaction.

为此，我们的 HaNDF 提出了一种几何感知、物体条件下的神经距离场，在条件场的零水平集中表示合理的手部关节运动。我们利用黎曼几何工具来 (i) 在物体诱导的神经场的零水平集中表示合理的手部姿态，以及 (ii) 将给定的姿态投影到该场上。使用 HaNDF，我们还可以针对合理的条件进行优化，从而确定交互中的物体。

Our extensive evaluations demonstrate that our HaNDF provides a flexible and powerful prior for hand-object interaction, suitable for applications such as pose refinement, reconstruction under occlusion, and physically plausible manipulation synthesis.

我们广泛的评估表明，HaNDF 为手部-物体交互提供了一种灵活且强大的先验，适用于姿态细化、遮挡下的重建以及物理上合理的操纵合成等应用。
