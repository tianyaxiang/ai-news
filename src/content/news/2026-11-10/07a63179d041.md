Nvidia’s stock slid more than 4% on Monday, the first trading day after its board said it would investigate CEO Jensen Huang over his relationship with Alice Huang, an executive U.S. intelligence believes was spying for China. Securities lawyers were already moving. At least four firms, including Schall, Brown & Schwartz and Levi & Korsinsky, announced investigations Monday into whether Nvidia misled investors.

周一，英伟达（Nvidia）股价下跌超过4%，这是该公司董事会宣布将调查首席执行官黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）与其关系人爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang）之间关系后的首个交易日。据美国情报部门认为，爱丽丝·黄是一名涉嫌为中国从事间谍活动的行政人员。证券律师们早已闻风而动。包括Schall, Brown & Schwartz和Levi & Korsinsky在内的至少四家律师事务所周一宣布，将调查英伟达是否存在误导投资者的行为。

Robert Bartlett, a Stanford Law professor and securities law expert, expects them to have plenty of company. “I would definitely wager that there’ll be a filing,” Bartlett said in an interview Monday morning, adding that he wouldn’t be surprised if a case had been filed by the end of the day. Plaintiffs’ lawyers have good reasons to be first in line; under federal securities law, the first complaint triggers a public notice, and investors then have 60 days to ask the court to be named lead plaintiff. A firm that files early gets a head start on signing up investors with large losses, as well as on shaping the story of the case.

斯坦福大学法学教授、证券法专家罗伯特·巴特利特（Robert Bartlett）预计，后续会有更多律所加入。巴特利特在周一上午的采访中表示：“我敢打赌肯定会有诉讼提交。”他补充说，如果当天结束前就有案件被立案，他也不会感到惊讶。原告律师有充分的理由争当第一；根据联邦证券法，首份诉状会触发公开通知，投资者随后有60天的时间向法院申请成为首席原告。率先提交诉讼的律所可以在招募损失惨重的投资者方面抢占先机，并能主导案件的叙事方向。

Bartlett was right, in fact. A case surfaced Monday afternoon in the Northern District of California, where Nvidia is headquartered. Rosen Law Firm, a New York-based plaintiffs’ firm that specializes in securities class actions, filed it on behalf of Mark Kistenmacher, a retail investor who made seven purchases totaling roughly $20,500 this year, according to a schedule attached to the complaint. (Rosen was lead counsel in a $250 million settlement with Alibaba and ranks among the most active filers of such cases.)

事实上，巴特利特是对的。周一下午，英伟达总部所在的加利福尼亚北区联邦地区法院出现了一起诉讼。总部位于纽约、专门从事证券集体诉讼的罗森律师事务所（Rosen Law Firm）代表散户投资者马克·基斯滕马赫（Mark Kistenmacher）提起了诉讼。根据诉状附带的时间表，基斯滕马赫今年进行了七次购买，总额约为20,500美元。（罗森律所曾是阿里巴巴2.5亿美元和解案的首席律师，也是此类案件中最活跃的诉讼方之一。）

The proposed class covers anyone who bought Nvidia securities between Oct. 9, 2025, and Nov. 8, 2026, and the suit names both Nvidia and Huang. The complaint accuses them of misleading investors about Huang’s ties to Megaspeed, the Singapore-based data center outfit where Alice Huang was considered a top exec, and it points to three statements as proof: Nvidia spokesperson John Rizzo’s late-October claim that Huang’s interactions with Alice Huang were “not unusual or unexpected,” the company’s even earlier claim that it found “no evidence of diversion” of its chips, and Jensen Huang’s Oct. 30 comment to Bloomberg that Nvidia’s dealings with Megaspeed were “normal and not unusual.” All three were false or misleading, the suit says, because Huang was having an affair with Alice Huang.

拟议的集体诉讼涵盖了在2025年10月9日至2026年11月8日期间购买英伟达证券的所有人，诉讼对象包括英伟达公司和黄仁勋本人。诉状指控他们就黄仁勋与Megaspeed的关系误导了投资者。Megaspeed是一家总部位于新加坡的数据中心公司，爱丽丝·黄被认为是该公司的核心高管。诉状列举了三项声明作为证据：英伟达发言人约翰·里佐（John Rizzo）10月下旬声称黄仁勋与爱丽丝·黄的互动“并不罕见或出人意料”；公司更早前声称未发现其芯片存在“挪用证据”；以及黄仁勋10月30日对彭博社发表的评论，称英伟达与Megaspeed的交易“正常且并不罕见”。诉讼称，这三项声明均属虚假或误导性陈述，因为黄仁勋当时正与爱丽丝·黄保持婚外情。

Kistenmacher is asking for unspecified damages, interest and attorneys’ fees, and he wants a jury trial. While much remains unknown, the legal theory here doesn’t depend on anything the board’s investigation may turn up about Huang’s private life. When The New York Times first reported on Huang’s ties to Alice Huang last month, Rizzo really did tell the Times that the relationship was “not unusual or unexpected.” Rizzo separately told another outlet, The Hill, that “Megaspeed has been no different than many other regional cloud customers. Meetings with customers are part of a CEO’s job; Jensen talks with thousands of customer executives every year.” Of course, the world now knows that was not true.

基斯滕马赫要求获得未指明的损害赔偿、利息和律师费，并要求进行陪审团审判。尽管目前仍有许多未知数，但此处的法律理论并不依赖于董事会调查可能揭露的关于黄仁勋私生活的任何细节。上个月《纽约时报》首次报道黄仁勋与爱丽丝·黄的关系时，里佐确实告诉该报，这种关系“并不罕见或出人意料”。里佐还分别告诉另一家媒体《国会山报》（The Hill）：“Megaspeed与其他许多区域性云客户没有什么不同。与客户会面是首席执行官工作的一部分；黄仁勋每年都会与数千名客户高管交谈。”当然，全世界现在都知道那不是事实。

Worse, the Times reports that U.S. intelligence had been monitoring Alice Huang for more than a year. From a securities perspective, Bartlett said, the company’s earlier statement was “clearly a misleading statement.” And because Huang is CEO, Bartlett said, “his knowledge gets imputed to the company.” In other words, Nvidia is treated as having known what its chief executive knew. The Kistenmacher complaint makes the same argument. Bartlett predicted a “classic fraud on the market” claim, in which investors argue they paid prices that reflected false or incomplete public information, and lost money when the truth emerged.

更糟糕的是，《纽约时报》报道称，美国情报部门对爱丽丝·黄的监控已超过一年。巴特利特表示，从证券角度来看，该公司早先的声明“显然是误导性陈述”。而且由于黄仁勋是首席执行官，巴特利特说，“他的知情权会被归咎于公司”。换句话说，英伟达被视为已知晓其首席执行官所知的一切。基斯滕马赫的诉状也提出了同样的论点。巴特利特预测这是一起“典型的市场欺诈”索赔案，投资者认为他们支付的价格反映了虚假或不完整的公开信息，并在真相大白时蒙受了损失。

This early lawsuit relies on that theory, and Monday’s drop of $7.97 per share, or 4.27%, gives the attorneys involved a lot to work with. Damages in such cases are tied to the stock’s decline, and Nvidia is one of the most valuable companies in the world, so you can imagine a settlement could prove exceedingly expensive. That means a large payment from the company and its directors-and-officers insurers, but typically no personal consequences for the CEO or the board. It is also a civil matter, so Huang isn’t facing prison over it. The irony, as Bartlett noted, is that the suit is brought in the name of Nvidia’s shareholders, and shareholders ultimately pay for the settlement, directly or through insurance premiums.

这起早期诉讼正是基于这一理论，而周一股价每股下跌7.97美元（即4.27%）为相关律师提供了充足的论据。此类案件的赔偿金额与股价跌幅挂钩，而英伟达是全球市值最高的公司之一，因此可以想象，和解金可能会极其昂贵。这意味着公司及其董事和高管责任险承保人将支付巨额赔偿，但通常不会对首席执行官或董事会成员产生个人后果。这也是一起民事案件，因此黄仁勋不会因此面临牢狱之灾。正如巴特利特所指出的，讽刺的是，诉讼是以英伟达股东的名义提起的，而股东最终将通过直接支付或保险费的形式承担和解费用。

Bartlett sorted Nvidia’s wider exposure into buckets. The first is the securities fraud claim, which is the clearest immediate problem for the company and potentially for Huang. The second is governance: what the board knew and when. It’s possible directors were kept in the dark, but the board also has oversight duties, and once the Times began reporting on the Megaspeed connection, the company should have had reporting lines to bring a critical risk like this to directors. If it didn’t, or if directors didn’t act, board-level liability is possible.

巴特利特将英伟达面临的更广泛风险进行了分类。第一类是证券欺诈索赔，这是公司目前面临的最直接问题，也可能波及黄仁勋。第二类是公司治理：董事会何时知情以及知情程度。董事们可能一直被蒙在鼓里，但董事会也负有监督职责。一旦《纽约时报》开始报道Megaspeed的关联，公司就应该建立报告渠道，将此类重大风险告知董事。如果公司没有这样做，或者董事们没有采取行动，那么董事会层面的责任就可能成立。

A third category, one that would change everything, would arise only if the board’s investigation finds Huang knew about the intelligence connection. That would be “a whole other ball game,” as Bartlett said. (The board said in a statement to the public Sunday that it has no reason to believe national security or Nvidia secrets were compromised.) There’s also the question of government action. The SEC can seek an officer-and-director bar for intentional securities-fraud violations, which Bartlett said is technically on the table. But he doubts the current SEC under Chairman Paul Atkins would have the appetite, given President Trump’s support for Huang.

第三类风险如果发生将改变一切，即董事会的调查发现黄仁勋确实知晓该情报关联。正如巴特利特所言，那将是“完全不同的另一回事”。（董事会在周日向公众发表的声明中表示，没有理由相信国家安全或英伟达的机密受到损害。）此外还有政府行动的问题。美国证券交易委员会（SEC）可以针对蓄意的证券欺诈行为寻求禁止相关人员担任高管或董事，巴特利特认为这在技术上是可能的。但他怀疑在主席保罗·阿特金斯（Paul Atkins）领导下的现任SEC是否有此意愿，考虑到特朗普总统对黄仁勋的支持。

A roughly two-year clock after discovery of the facts would leave room for a future administration to act, but the window would be tight, and Bartlett posited that’s pretty unlikely when asked about it.
