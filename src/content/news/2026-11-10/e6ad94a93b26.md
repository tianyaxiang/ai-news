OTTAWA, ON, November 9, 2026 – Payments Canada today announced the launch of Canada’s Real-Time Rail (RTR). This new national payment system will transform day-to-day payments for Canadian businesses and consumers by enabling instant speed and settlement, real-time cash-flow visibility and data-rich messaging through existing banking and payment channels.

安大略省渥太华，2026年11月9日——加拿大支付协会（Payments Canada）今日宣布推出加拿大的实时支付系统（RTR）。这一全新的国家支付系统将通过现有的银行和支付渠道，实现即时速度与结算、实时现金流可见性以及数据丰富的报文传输，从而改变加拿大企业和消费者的日常支付方式。

The launch of Canada’s RTR marks a major milestone in modernizing the critical infrastructure that underpins Canada’s economy and future growth. The RTR will enable participating members, including financial institutions, credit unions and payment service providers, to exchange, clear and settle payments securely in real time for their customers, 24 hours a day, every day of the year.

加拿大RTR的推出标志着现代化关键基础设施建设的一个重要里程碑，该基础设施支撑着加拿大的经济和未来增长。RTR将使包括金融机构、信用合作社和支付服务提供商在内的参与成员能够全年每天24小时为客户安全地实时交换、清算和结算支付。

“A stronger, more productive economy relies on modern financial infrastructure that can match our ambition,” said the Honourable François-Philippe Champagne, Minister of Finance and National Revenue. “The launch of Canada’s Real-Time Rail lays the foundation for a more dynamic and competitive digital economy, driving innovation, expanding choice and creating new opportunities for Canadians while positioning our country for long-term growth.”

“一个更强大、更具生产力的经济依赖于能够匹配我们雄心的现代金融基础设施，”财政与国家税收部长弗朗索瓦-菲利普·香槟阁下表示。“加拿大实时支付系统的推出为更具活力和竞争力的数字经济奠定了基础，它将推动创新、扩大选择并为加拿大人创造新的机会，同时为我国的长期增长做好定位。”

“The launch of Canada’s Real-Time Rail marks the beginning of a new era for payments in our country,” said Susan E. By collectively leveraging this rail, our ecosystem will unleash its full potential, enabling our members to build capabilities that drive innovation, resilience and growth for businesses and consumers across the nation.”

“加拿大实时支付系统的推出标志着我国支付新时代的开始，”加拿大支付协会总裁兼首席执行官苏珊·E·霍金斯表示。“作为专门为我国设计的国家资产，RTR为支付生态系统提供了现代化的建设基础。通过共同利用这一轨道，我们的生态系统将释放其全部潜力，使我们的成员能够构建推动全国企业和消费者创新、韧性和增长的能力。”

The RTR will drive significant cost savings and economic growth in Canada over the next decade. The C.D. Howe Institute projects that, with its functionalities at launch, the RTR will generate up to $14.5 billion in cost savings from lower processing costs, reduced use of cash and cheques, and reduced float (the period during which funds are temporarily unavailable to the recipient). Extending beyond these cost savings, the RTR is expected to generate $16 billion in economic gains, growing to $27 billion as new functionalities are introduced over time.

RTR将在未来十年内推动加拿大的显著成本节约和经济增长。C.D.豪研究所预计，凭借其发布时的功能，RTR将通过降低处理成本、减少现金和支票的使用以及减少浮存金（资金暂时无法提供给收款人的期间），产生高达145亿加元的成本节约。除了这些成本节约之外，预计RTR还将产生160亿加元的经济收益，并随着时间的推移引入新功能，这一数字将增长至270亿加元。

“The Real-Time Rail is an important step in modernizing Canada's payment infrastructure and ensuring it keeps pace with Canadians' evolving needs,” said Tiff Macklem, Governor of the Bank of Canada. “The Bank will oversee the system so that it operates safely and reliably while ensuring Canadians can benefit from innovations in financial services.”

“实时支付系统是加拿大支付基础设施现代化并确保其跟上加拿大人不断变化的需求的重要一步，”加拿大央行行长蒂夫·麦克勒姆表示。“央行将监督该系统，以确保其安全可靠地运行，同时确保加拿大人能够从金融服务创新中受益。”

Owned and operated by Payments Canada, and built in collaboration with delivery partners CGI, Deloitte Canada, IBM Canada and Interac Corp., the RTR leverages ISO 20022 data-rich messaging. Through this shared technical language, the RTR holds the potential to strengthen Canada’s economic sovereign autonomy and support its global competitiveness.

RTR由加拿大支付协会拥有和运营，并与交付合作伙伴CGI、德勤加拿大、IBM加拿大和Interac公司合作构建，利用了ISO 20022数据丰富报文标准。通过这种共享的技术语言，RTR有潜力加强加拿大的经济主权自主权并支持其全球竞争力。

Canada is the first G7 country to deploy a real-time payment system with mandatory centralized fraud services for use at launch. These services will be used alongside participants’ existing fraud controls to mitigate the risk of fraud for Canadian businesses and consumers at multiple levels. Canada’s deliberately sequenced RTR rollout pairs day-one security with rapid scalability. With additional participants onboarding continually in the coming months, including all major Canadian financial institutions and other members applying for access, the RTR will be available to more than nine in 10 Canadians within the first year after launch.

加拿大精心安排的RTR推广计划将首日安全性与快速可扩展性相结合。随着未来几个月更多参与者持续加入，包括所有主要的加拿大金融机构和其他申请接入的成员，RTR将在发布后的第一年内覆盖超过十分之九的加拿大人。

The launch of the RTR reflects our ‘Team Canada’ approach, bringing together regulators, members, stakeholders and delivery partners around a shared vision. Built in Canada and designed to serve all Canadians, the RTR provides accessible payment infrastructure through existing banking and payment channels. This infrastructure supports a diverse country and a broad range of communities, including small businesses, newcomers and people in rural areas.

RTR的推出反映了我们的“加拿大团队”方针，将监管机构、成员、利益相关者和交付合作伙伴聚集在共同的愿景之下。RTR在加拿大构建并旨在服务所有加拿大人，通过现有的银行和支付渠道提供可访问的支付基础设施。该基础设施支持着一个多元化的国家和广泛的社区，包括小企业、新移民和农村地区的人们。

Payments Canada research found that 60 per cent of Canadian businesses are ready to adopt real-time payments to address everyday challenges such as forecasting cash flow and waiting to send and receive funds. More than half of surveyed consumers (52 per cent) also expressed interest and intent to use real-time payments.

加拿大支付协会的研究发现，60%的加拿大企业已准备好采用实时支付，以解决诸如现金流预测以及等待发送和接收资金等日常挑战。超过半数的受访消费者（52%）也表达了使用实时支付的兴趣和意愿。

To meet this evolving need, the RTR serves as a secure platform for innovation. With it, system participants can build and offer products and services that benefit consumers and businesses across the country, supporting a changing economy and Canada's future prosperity.

为了满足这一不断变化的需求，RTR充当了一个安全的创新平台。有了它，系统参与者可以构建并提供造福全国消费者和企业的各种产品与服务，支持不断变化的经济和加拿大的未来繁荣。

Canada’s Real-Time Rail is an important step toward a more productive, innovative and inclusive economy, and the work will continue to ensure the country’s payment infrastructure meets Canadians’ evolving needs.

加拿大的实时支付系统是迈向更具生产力、创新性和包容性经济的重要一步，相关工作将继续进行，以确保国家的支付基础设施满足加拿大人不断变化的需求。
