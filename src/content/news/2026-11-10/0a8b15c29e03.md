Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.08730 (cs) [Submitted on 5 Nov 2026] Title: Structuring Survey Histories for LLM Digital Twins: A Controlled Study of Background, Decision Procedure, and Evaluation Profiles Authors: Iris Ye, Tianze Deng, Ozan Candogan.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.08730 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 5 日] 标题：为大语言模型数字孪生构建调查历史：关于背景、决策程序和评估配置文件的对照研究 作者：Iris Ye, Tianze Deng, Ozan Candogan。

Abstract: LLM-based digital twins aim to predict how individuals would respond in new settings from representations of their prior responses. Existing evidence that compression of survey histories need not reduce predictive accuracy suggests that representation structure, rather than information volume alone, may be an important design choice.

摘要：基于大语言模型（LLM）的数字孪生旨在通过个体既往反应的表征，预测其在新的环境下的反应。现有证据表明，压缩调查历史并不一定会降低预测准确性，这暗示了表征结构而非单纯的信息量，可能是一个重要的设计选择。

We study BDE, a fixed representation that organizes each respondent's survey history into Background, Decision procedure, and Evaluation. We evaluate it on Twin-2K-500 (Toubia et al., 2025) by predicting each respondent's held-out answers on 17 tasks.

我们研究了 BDE，这是一种将每位受访者的调查历史组织为背景（Background）、决策程序（Decision procedure）和评估（Evaluation）的固定表征。我们在 Twin-2K-500（Toubia 等人，2025）数据集上对其进行了评估，通过预测每位受访者在 17 项任务中的留存答案来进行验证。

In a paired evaluation with 100 respondents, a fully inferred BDE profile improves overall accuracy over the raw survey transcript by 1.27 percentage points (pp) on gpt-5.4-nano (p < 0.01) and 1.19 pp on gpt-5.4-mini (p < 0.001). Three analyses then examine the schema, extraction depth, and component composition.

在针对 100 名受访者的配对评估中，完全推断的 BDE 配置文件在 gpt-5.4-nano 模型上将整体准确率比原始调查记录提高了 1.27 个百分点（p < 0.01），在 gpt-5.4-mini 模型上提高了 1.19 个百分点（p < 0.001）。随后，三项分析分别考察了模式、提取深度和组件构成。

Against a length-matched free-form profile written from the same transcript by the same extractor, BDE improves pricing accuracy on both simulators (2.15 pp on nano, p < 0.01; 1.38 pp on mini, p < 0.05), while its overall advantage is significant only on nano (1.05 pp, p < 0.05). Allowing deeper inference during extraction yields no statistically significant gain on either simulator.

与由同一提取器根据相同记录编写的长度匹配的自由格式配置文件相比，BDE 在两个模拟器上的定价准确性均有所提高（nano 上提高 2.15 个百分点，p < 0.01；mini 上提高 1.38 个百分点，p < 0.05），但其整体优势仅在 nano 上显著（1.05 个百分点，p < 0.05）。在提取过程中允许更深层的推断并未在任何模拟器上产生统计学上的显著增益。

By contrast, adding Evaluation to a Background--Decision procedure profile improves overall accuracy by 1.44 pp on nano (p < 0.001) and 1.22 pp on mini (p < 0.001). These results indicate that the advantage over raw transcripts is not explained by compression or length alone, and attribute it primarily to profile composition rather than deeper extraction.

相比之下，在“背景-决策程序”配置文件中加入“评估”部分，使 nano 上的整体准确率提高了 1.44 个百分点（p < 0.001），mini 上的准确率提高了 1.22 个百分点（p < 0.001）。这些结果表明，相对于原始记录的优势并不能仅用压缩或长度来解释，其主要归因于配置文件的构成，而非更深层的提取。
