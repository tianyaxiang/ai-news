# We Gave Doctor Handwriting a Voice (and 10 Languages)

What I Built Pill-o-Talk is a public web app that helps people understand their prescriptions. I built it with my teammate Annika Bhattacharjee- @bhattacharjeeannika at UB Hacking.

我所构建的 Pill-o-Talk 是一款公共网络应用程序，旨在帮助人们理解他们的处方。这是我在 UB Hacking 活动中与队友 Annika Bhattacharjee (@bhattacharjeeannika) 共同开发的。

It does three things: Reads the prescription: you upload it and the app interprets what it says, including hard-to-read handwriting or print. Translates it: it explains the prescription in 10 languages. Builds a medicine schedule: it turns the instructions into a clear plan for when to take each medicine, working through difficult handwriting and medical jargon.

它具备三项功能：读取处方：你上传处方后，应用程序会解读其内容，包括难以辨认的手写体或印刷体。翻译处方：它能以 10 种语言解释处方内容。制定用药时间表：它将说明转化为清晰的服药计划，能够处理复杂的手写字迹和医学术语。

Never guesses: when part of a prescription is unclear, Pill-o-Talk doesn't fill in the gaps or invent advice. It flags the unclear part and warns you wherever you need to check with your doctor or pharmacist and be cautious.

绝不猜测：当处方的某一部分不清晰时，Pill-o-Talk 不会自行填补空白或编造建议。它会标记出不清晰的部分，并提醒你在需要时咨询医生或药剂师，务必保持谨慎。
