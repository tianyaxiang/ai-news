# Jensen Huang pulls out of ElevenLabs Summit amid Nvidia controversy

In Brief Posted: 12:09 PM PST · November 9, 2026 Image Credits: Jose Sarmento Matos/Bloomberg / Getty Images Ivan Mehta Jensen Huang pulls out of ElevenLabs Summit amid Nvidia controversy.

简讯 发布于：太平洋标准时间 2026 年 11 月 9 日中午 12:09 图片来源：Jose Sarmento Matos/Bloomberg / Getty Images Ivan Mehta 黄仁勋在英伟达争议中退出 ElevenLabs 峰会。

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang has pulled out of the ElevenLabs Summit scheduled for this Wednesday in New York, ElevenLabs, the voice AI company, confirmed Monday. He has also dropped out of other speaking engagements, including the Anthropic CEO Forum. Nvidia is an investor in ElevenLabs.

语音人工智能公司 ElevenLabs 周一证实，英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋已退出定于本周三在纽约举行的 ElevenLabs 峰会。他还退出了包括 Anthropic 首席执行官论坛在内的其他演讲活动。英伟达是 ElevenLabs 的投资者。

The withdrawals come after Huang admitted to an extramarital affair over the weekend, and Nvidia opened an investigation into his relationship with a possible Chinese spy. “I owe my family and all of you an apology. I was unfaithful to my wife. My actions hurt her and our children. There is no excuse for what I did. I am deeply sorry,” Huang said in a memo to the company.

此次退出发生在上周末黄仁勋承认婚外情，以及英伟达对其与一名疑似中国间谍的关系展开调查之后。“我欠我的家人和你们所有人一个道歉。我背叛了我的妻子。我的行为伤害了她和我们的孩子。我的所作所为没有任何借口。我深表歉意，”黄仁勋在给公司的一份备忘录中表示。

The incident has rattled the AI industry, raising questions about Huang’s staying power as CEO and Nvidia’s own China ambitions.

这一事件震动了人工智能行业，引发了人们对黄仁勋作为首席执行官的留任能力以及英伟达自身中国雄心的质疑。
