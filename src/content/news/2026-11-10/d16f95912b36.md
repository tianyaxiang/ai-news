Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.08643 (cs) [Submitted on 5 Nov 2026] Title: FinEvolveKG: Controlled and Attributable Prompt Evolution for Financial Knowledge Graph Construction Authors: Abhinav Arun, Jayita Bhattacharyya, Fabrizio Dimino, Bhaskarjit Sarmah, Stefano Pasquali.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.08643 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 5 日] 标题：FinEvolveKG：用于金融知识图谱构建的可控且可归因的提示演化 作者：Abhinav Arun, Jayita Bhattacharyya, Fabrizio Dimino, Bhaskarjit Sarmah, Stefano Pasquali。

Abstract: Financial institutions construct knowledge graphs (KGs) from shared SEC 10-Ks to serve risk analysis, portfolio management, and investment research, whose analytical needs differ. Extraction must adapt without fragmenting shared grounding, typing, and temporal rules. Hand-authored persona prompts and fine-tuning add cost. Weight-free prompt optimization offers an alternative, but unconstrained evolution of a monolithic prompt can rewrite shared rules alongside persona guidance, obscuring changes and complicating review.

摘要：金融机构利用共享的 SEC 10-K 文件构建知识图谱 (KG)，以服务于风险分析、投资组合管理和投资研究，而这些领域的分析需求各不相同。提取过程必须在不破坏共享基础、类型和时间规则的前提下进行调整。人工编写的角色提示词和微调增加了成本。无权重的提示词优化提供了一种替代方案，但对单一提示词进行无约束的演化可能会在引入角色指导的同时重写共享规则，从而掩盖变更并使审查复杂化。

We introduce FinEvolveKG, a KG-construction harness separating reusable general and persona-policy blocks and mechanically confining GEPA, a reflective prompt optimizer, to the designated block. During persona adaptation, the approved general block remains byte-stable and every optimizer edit is localized to a separately versioned persona policy. A dual objective balances generic temporal-KG quality with persona-relevant facts, motifs, and multi-hop connectivity.

我们引入了 FinEvolveKG，这是一个知识图谱构建框架，它将可重用的通用模块与角色策略模块分离开来，并从机制上将反射式提示词优化器 GEPA 限制在指定的模块内。在角色适配过程中，已批准的通用模块保持字节稳定，且优化器的每一次编辑都局限于单独版本化的角色策略中。双重目标平衡了通用时间知识图谱的质量与角色相关的事实、主题和多跳连接性。

We evaluate five mutation scopes on ticker-disjoint 10-K splits, with three seeds for the persona-conditioned arms (v3-v5) across three personas. On the three-seed mean, the complete two-stage block-constrained workflow (v4) improves over the frozen prompt baseline (v1) by 19.4-30.9% and over the tested unconstrained full-prompt workflow (v5) by 2.2-6.4%, leading v5 on both objective components and winning all nine paired-seed comparisons with the lowest seed variance in every persona.

我们在股票代码不重叠的 10-K 数据集拆分上评估了五种变异范围，并为角色条件分支 (v3-v5) 在三个角色中分别设置了三个随机种子。在三个种子的平均值上，完整的两阶段模块约束工作流 (v4) 比冻结提示词基线 (v1) 提高了 19.4-30.9%，比测试的无约束全提示词工作流 (v5) 提高了 2.2-6.4%，在两个目标组件上均领先于 v5，并在所有九项配对种子比较中获胜，且在每个角色中均表现出最低的种子方差。

Without further optimization, v4 also exceeds v5 across all evaluated out-of-distribution (OOD) shifts. An SME audit supports using the generated triplets as ground truth; a blinded two-SME audit favors v4 over v5 in 70.1% of contrast-enriched judgments. These results show that mechanically attributable evolution can coexist with strong empirical performance, without attributing the gains causally to the boundary.

在无需进一步优化的情况下，v4 在所有评估的分布外 (OOD) 偏移中也优于 v5。中小企业 (SME) 审计支持将生成的元组作为事实依据；一项由两名 SME 进行的盲审显示，在 70.1% 的对比增强判断中，他们更倾向于 v4 而非 v5。这些结果表明，机械上可归因的演化可以与强大的实证性能共存，且无需将收益因果归因于边界。
