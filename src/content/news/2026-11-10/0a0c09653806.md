The US military has published a Request for Information on a federal procurement website signaling interest in deploying a “proliferated cislunar constellation” consisting of an unspecified number of satellites performing an undisclosed mission in the vicinity of the Moon.

美国军方在联邦采购网站上发布了一份信息征询书，表明其有兴趣部署一个由数量不详的卫星组成的“扩散式地月空间星座”，这些卫星将在月球附近执行未公开的任务。

The announcement follows statements in recent months from the Space Force’s acquisitions chief for advanced programs, Maj. Gen. Stephen Purdy, that the military was getting serious about extending its reach to the Moon. The Space Force’s most distant satellites today are in geostationary orbit, some 22,000 miles (36,000 kilometers) over the equator, collecting routine surveillance and missile warning data.

在此之前，太空军负责高级项目采购的主管斯蒂芬·珀迪（Stephen Purdy）少将近几个月曾表示，军方正认真考虑将触角延伸至月球。目前，太空军距离最远的卫星位于赤道上方约 22,000 英里（36,000 公里）的地球静止轨道上，负责收集常规监视和导弹预警数据。

Most recently, Purdy spoke of a shift in Pentagon leadership’s thinking about the Moon following an executive order signed by President Donald Trump last December. The order set out the Trump administration’s space policy. One section of the order deals with the US government’s ability to detect, characterize, and counter threats to US space interests from low-Earth orbit to cislunar space, the term of art for the region of space around the Moon.

最近，珀迪提到，在唐纳德·特朗普总统去年 12 月签署一项行政命令后，五角大楼领导层对月球的看法发生了转变。该命令阐述了特朗普政府的太空政策。其中一部分涉及美国政府探测、识别并反制从近地轨道到地月空间（月球周围空间的专业术语）内威胁美国太空利益的能力。

“I’ve got the Secretary of War and the Chairman of the Joint Chiefs full on stating in public: ‘This is a warfighting domain,’” Purdy said at Space Systems Command’s Industry Days event in October, according to a report in Air & Space Forces Magazine. “And so, I would ask you all, please jump on my cislunar bandwagon because I need some more friends here, along with our deep, passionate crew that have been working on this for years.”

据《空天军杂志》报道，珀迪在 10 月份太空系统司令部的行业日活动上表示：“陆军部长和参谋长联席会议主席都在公开场合明确表示：‘这是一个作战领域。’因此，我请求大家加入我的地月空间计划，因为除了我们多年来一直致力于此的深情团队外，我还需要更多的伙伴。”

Officials have emphasized the military’s role at the Moon is to protect US interests there as the United States and China ramp up their lunar programs, with a series of robotic missions culminating in crewed lunar landings in the next few years. Some US officials foresee a competition for real estate and resources at the Moon, where there are a finite number of safe landing zones that can access the Moon’s water ice at the south pole.

官员们强调，随着美国和中国加强各自的月球计划，并将在未来几年内进行一系列以载人登月为最终目标的机器人任务，军方在月球上的作用是保护美国的利益。一些美国官员预见到月球上的土地和资源竞争，因为能够获取月球南极水冰的安全着陆区数量有限。

“We have a role to play, but we’re not the lead,” said Gen. Chance Saltzman, who was the top general in the Space Force until his retirement in August. “I just don’t want to get caught flat-footed when we start to have to protect US interests out there, so we’re along for the ride.”

“我们有自己的角色，但我们不是主导者，”直到 8 月退休前一直担任太空军最高将领的钱斯·萨尔茨曼（Chance Saltzman）将军说，“我只是不想在开始保护那里的美国利益时措手不及，所以我们参与其中。”

Now, the Pentagon has taken a preliminary step toward a “Cislunar Space System,” a constellation of military satellites the military says it is considering deploying around the Moon. The new RFI was released by the Department of the Air Force and dated November 2. It asks companies to inform the military on their ability to provide “rapid” and “low-risk” solutions to support the government’s “technical and programmatic objectives.”

目前，五角大楼已朝着“地月空间系统”迈出了初步步伐，军方称正考虑在月球周围部署这一军事卫星星座。这份新的信息征询书由空军部发布，日期为 11 月 2 日。它要求各公司告知军方其提供“快速”和“低风险”解决方案的能力，以支持政府的“技术和项目目标”。

The document provided few details about what the Pentagon wants to do around the Moon with the Cislunar Space System, other than it will consist of up to five spacecraft, each with a government-provided payload, to meet military “mission needs” in late 2029. The RFI hints at a vision for a much larger fleet of satellites in cislunar space.

该文件几乎没有提供关于五角大楼打算利用地月空间系统在月球周围做什么的细节，只提到它将由最多五颗航天器组成，每颗航天器都携带政府提供的有效载荷，以满足 2029 年底的军事“任务需求”。信息征询书暗示了在地月空间部署规模更大的卫星群的愿景。

The first five satellites “may serve as seed vehicles that will inform the acquisition strategy for a potential future proliferated cislunar constellation,” the RFI said. “The government intends to award up to five firm fixed price preliminary design study contracts upon government consideration of the market information collected with this RFI and all other sources of information relevant to the effort,” military officials wrote on the government procurement website.

信息征询书称，首批五颗卫星“可能作为种子航天器，为未来潜在的扩散式地月星座的采购策略提供参考”。军方官员在政府采购网站上写道：“政府打算在考虑通过此次信息征询书及所有其他相关信息来源收集到的市场信息后，授予最多五份固定价格的初步设计研究合同。”

Responses are due by November 24. It is unclear when the Pentagon might move forward with firm contracts, and the language in the RFI does not commit the military to doing so. The standard commercial satellite development cycle can run two or three years, so it’s a safe bet that military officials would want deals signed sometime in 2027 to meet their 2029 schedule goal.

回复截止日期为 11 月 24 日。目前尚不清楚五角大楼何时会推进确定合同，且信息征询书中的措辞并未承诺军方一定会这样做。标准的商业卫星开发周期可能需要两到三年，因此可以肯定的是，军方官员希望在 2027 年左右签署协议，以实现其 2029 年的时间表目标。
