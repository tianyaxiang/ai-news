The price of Xbox Game Pass Ultimate has fluctuated between $20 and $30 per month over the last year, but you can get three months of the top plan for around $19 per month. At Newegg, using the coupon code BNWF2656 at checkout knocks $11 off the usual cost of a three-month subscription. That brings the price down to just $57.99 for three months.

过去一年里，Xbox Game Pass Ultimate 的价格在每月 20 美元到 30 美元之间波动，但现在你可以以每月约 19 美元的价格获得三个月的顶级会员套餐。在 Newegg 结账时使用优惠码 BNWF2656，可以在三个月订阅的原价基础上减免 11 美元，使三个月的总价降至 57.99 美元。

In addition to full access to over 500 games on both Xbox consoles and Windows PC machines, the upgraded Ultimate plan includes EA Play and Ubisoft Plus Classics, which have their own libraries and benefits, and Fortnite Crew for full access to the Fortnite battles passes. On Xbox, Ultimate enables online multiplayer, as well.

除了可以畅玩 Xbox 主机和 Windows PC 上的 500 多款游戏外，升级后的 Ultimate 套餐还包含 EA Play 和 Ubisoft Plus Classics（拥有各自的游戏库和权益），以及可获得完整《堡垒之夜》战斗通行证的 Fortnite Crew 会员资格。在 Xbox 上，Ultimate 会员还支持在线多人游戏功能。

Previously a straightforward proposition, the service has undergone a variety of changes over the last year that has made it slightly less appealing. Xbox Game Pass Ultimate was previously $29.99 per month, but earlier this year Microsoft removed the Call of Duty games from the library, and in the process dropped the price to $22.99.

该服务曾经非常简单明了，但过去一年经历的各种变化使其吸引力有所下降。Xbox Game Pass Ultimate 此前每月收费 29.99 美元，但今年早些时候，微软从游戏库中移除了《使命召唤》系列游戏，并将价格下调至 22.99 美元。

More recently, Microsoft added hourly limits to cloud gaming. All Game Pass plan subscribers previously enjoyed an unlimited amount of cloud gaming on their computer or mobile device, but now plans scale the number of included hours, with the Ultimate plan including 15 hours per month. That news came just weeks before announcing pay-as-you-go plans for non-Game Pass subscribers.

最近，微软对云游戏增加了时长限制。此前，所有 Game Pass 订阅用户都可以在电脑或移动设备上无限畅玩云游戏，但现在各套餐对包含的时长进行了分级，其中 Ultimate 套餐每月包含 15 小时。这一消息发布仅几周后，微软就宣布了针对非 Game Pass 订阅用户的按需付费计划。
