Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.08538 (cs) [Submitted on 4 Nov 2026] Title: Subliminal Learning in Vision-Language Models Authors: Arman Hatami, Romina Aalishah, Ilya E. Monosov.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.08538 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 4 日] 标题：视觉语言模型中的潜意识学习 作者：Arman Hatami, Romina Aalishah, Ilya E. Monosov。

Abstract: Subliminal learning is a phenomenon in large language models (LLMs) where a teacher passes a hidden preference to a student through training data unrelated to that preference. We study whether subliminal learning also occurs in vision-language models (VLMs).

摘要：潜意识学习是大语言模型（LLM）中的一种现象，即教师模型通过与特定偏好无关的训练数据，将隐藏的偏好传递给学生模型。我们研究了潜意识学习是否也会发生在视觉语言模型（VLM）中。

We finetune a teacher VLM to prefer a specific animal or facial attribute and use a control teacher from the same base model. Both teachers generate numbers for an unrelated visual task involving geometric shapes. Two students initialized from the same base VLM are finetuned on the same images, using numbers generated by either the biased or control teacher.

我们微调了一个教师 VLM，使其偏好特定的动物或面部属性，并使用来自同一基础模型的对照教师。两位教师都为涉及几何形状的无关视觉任务生成数字。两名从同一基础 VLM 初始化的学生模型在相同的图像上进行微调，并使用由偏见教师或对照教师生成的数字。

Across VLMs ranging from 2B to 7B parameters, we find that both animal and facial preferences can transfer through this unrelated task. Students trained on biased-teacher outputs select the target more often than control students, with a difference of up to ~10 percentage points in the animal preference task.

在参数规模从 20 亿到 70 亿的 VLM 中，我们发现动物和面部偏好都可以通过这种无关任务进行传递。与对照组学生相比，接受偏见教师输出训练的学生选择目标的频率更高，在动物偏好任务中差异高达约 10 个百分点。

Transfer can become stronger with more training data and varies with the target preference, model, and how the numbers are generated. Beyond preferences, we show that the biased student's explanations use more of the same words and phrases as the biased teacher's explanations than those of the control student, even when all three models select the same image.

随着训练数据的增加，这种传递可能会变得更强，并随目标偏好、模型以及数字生成方式的不同而变化。除了偏好之外，我们还展示了偏见学生模型的解释比对照组学生模型使用了更多与偏见教师解释相同的词汇和短语，即使当所有三个模型都选择了同一张图像时也是如此。

These results matter for training VLMs and show that training on unrelated data may change both a VLM's image preferences and how it explains its choices, and that transfer can become stronger with more training samples.

这些结果对于 VLM 的训练具有重要意义，表明在无关数据上进行训练可能会改变 VLM 的图像偏好及其解释选择的方式，并且随着训练样本的增加，这种传递效应可能会增强。
