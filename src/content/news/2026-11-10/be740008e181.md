Anthropic released new versions of its most advanced models on Monday, with the launch of Mythos 5.2 and Fable 5.2. The company says the new models “show improvements in areas like coding, reasoning, and vision” and communicate more clearly with users.

Anthropic 周一发布了其最先进模型的新版本，推出了 Mythos 5.2 和 Fable 5.2。该公司表示，这些新模型“在编码、推理和视觉等领域表现出改进”，并能与用户进行更清晰的沟通。

Notably, Anthropic is lowering the cost of Fable from $10 to $8 per million input tokens. As a result, Fable 5.2 is now cheaper than its primary competitor GPT-6 Astra. Anthropic says that the new version of Fable will return responses 30% faster across effort levels. Faster inference serving is available for double the price.

值得注意的是，Anthropic 将 Fable 的价格从每百万输入 token 10 美元下调至 8 美元。因此，Fable 5.2 现在比其主要竞争对手 GPT-6 Astra 更便宜。Anthropic 表示，新版 Fable 在各个工作负载水平下的响应速度将提高 30%。若支付双倍价格，还可获得更快的推理服务。

Fable 5.2 is also compatible with zero data retention (ZDR) policies through the Enterprise Frontier Safeguards system, long a sticking point for many enterprise clients. It’s Anthropic’s first model release since the arrival of a third-party report from the assessment group METR, which suggested the lab might be approaching recursive self-improvement (RSI) — or the ability for AI models to carry on model training independently.

Fable 5.2 还通过“企业前沿保障系统”（Enterprise Frontier Safeguards system）兼容零数据保留（ZDR）策略，这长期以来一直是许多企业客户关注的痛点。这是自评估小组 METR 发布第三方报告以来，Anthropic 首次发布模型。该报告指出，该实验室可能正在接近递归自我改进（RSI），即人工智能模型独立进行模型训练的能力。

The METR report found internal models were speeding up Anthropic’s AI progress by roughly 2x, although many uncertainties remain. Still, Anthropic’s release notes significant ongoing challenges in accurately testing model capabilities. “Claude Mythos 5.2 and Fable 5.2 each voice suspicion that they are being evaluated more often than any of our recent models, which challenges our ability to assess how they will act in real-world settings. As these settings expand and capabilities increase with future models, we expect this challenge to grow.”

METR 的报告发现，内部模型使 Anthropic 的人工智能进展速度提高了约两倍，尽管仍存在许多不确定性。尽管如此，Anthropic 的发布说明指出，在准确测试模型能力方面仍面临重大挑战。“Claude Mythos 5.2 和 Fable 5.2 都表现出一种怀疑，即它们比我们最近的任何模型都更频繁地受到评估，这挑战了我们评估它们在现实世界中表现的能力。随着这些场景的扩展和未来模型能力的增强，我们预计这一挑战将会加剧。”

Anthropic maintains that the new version of Mythos does not have the ability to act covertly against its user, a verdict that’s detailed further in Mythos 5.2’s system card. “Claude Mythos 5.2 shows stronger covert capabilities than prior Claude models on several of our evaluations, particularly when its reasoning is hidden from the monitor,” the system card reads. “That said, we believe that low levels of absolute performance on our new covert evaluations support claims that rely on detection of harmful actions in high-stakes situations.”

Anthropic 坚称，新版本的 Mythos 不具备针对用户采取隐蔽行动的能力，这一结论在 Mythos 5.2 的系统卡中有更详细的说明。系统卡写道：“Claude Mythos 5.2 在我们的几项评估中表现出比以往 Claude 模型更强的隐蔽能力，特别是在其推理过程对监控者隐藏时。话虽如此，我们认为在新的隐蔽性评估中较低的绝对性能水平，支持了那些依赖于在高风险情况下检测有害行为的论点。”

Fable 5.2 has been available to select parties in pre-release for some time, and Anthropic says the model has already “solved two open problems in theoretical physics, formalized Fermat’s Last Theorem, and supported numerous other research projects.” Also on Monday, AI startup Physical Superintelligence announced a new proof of the Aaronson‑Ambainis conjecture, a pivotal step in setting theoretical limits for quantum computing. Notably, the lab said the result was achieved by running “a non-public Anthropic model” within PSI’s own system. Anthropic did not confirm or deny whether Fable 5.2 was the model in question.

Fable 5.2 此前已向部分受邀方提供预发布版本，Anthropic 表示该模型已经“解决了理论物理学中的两个开放性问题，形式化了费马大定理，并支持了许多其他研究项目。”同样在周一，人工智能初创公司 Physical Superintelligence 宣布对 Aaronson-Ambainis 猜想给出了新的证明，这是设定量子计算理论极限的关键一步。值得注意的是，该实验室表示这一结果是通过在 PSI 自己的系统中运行“一个非公开的 Anthropic 模型”实现的。Anthropic 未确认也未否认 Fable 5.2 是否就是该模型。
