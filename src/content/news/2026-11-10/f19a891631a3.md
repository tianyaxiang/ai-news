### Here’s what Trump said he knew about Jensen Huang

在白宫举行的新闻发布会上，特朗普总统对于他何时得知黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）的婚外情一事含糊其辞。

President Trump held a White House press briefing earlier today and responded to several questions about his knowledge of Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s affair with an alleged Chinese spy. Huang said this weekend that he had been “​​unfaithful” to his wife, and the company has opened an investigation into Huang.

特朗普总统今天早些时候举行了白宫新闻发布会，回应了关于他了解英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋与一名涉嫌中国间谍发生婚外情一事的多个提问。黄仁勋本周末表示，他对自己妻子“不忠”，公司已针对黄仁勋展开调查。

Recent articles from The New York Times indicated that Trump had been aware of a relationship between Huang and Alice Huang, the suspected spy, before information about it became public. In October, The New York Times reported that Trump had been briefed “roughly a year ago” on intelligence that Huang and the alleged spy, Alice Huang, had developed a close relationship, but he didn’t do anything with the information. Yesterday, the NYT reported that Secretary of State Marco Rubio helped draft a memo to Trump officials about the relationship before Trump visited China in May.

《纽约时报》近期的文章指出，在相关信息公开之前，特朗普就已经知晓黄仁勋与涉嫌间谍的爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang）之间的关系。十月份，《纽约时报》报道称，特朗普在“大约一年前”就已收到情报简报，获悉黄仁勋与该名涉嫌间谍爱丽丝·黄关系密切，但他并未就此采取任何行动。昨天，《纽约时报》报道称，在特朗普五月份访问中国之前，国务卿马可·鲁比奥（Marco Rubio）曾协助起草了一份关于此关系的备忘录，并提交给了特朗普的官员们。

In the press conference, Trump wasn’t specific about when he learned of the affair. But Trump said Huang told him today that he had “no idea” the person was a spy.

在新闻发布会上，特朗普没有明确说明他是什么时候得知这段婚外情的。但特朗普表示，黄仁勋今天告诉他，自己“完全不知道”那个人是间谍。

When asked about when he was first informed of the issue, Trump responded: “I would say that when I heard about it, first of all, I was amazed because I view him as a very high-quality person. I’ve known him a long time. It’s the largest, by value, the largest or one of the largest companies in the world. He did that with a chip. When you think about it, you’ve got a chip that’s 10 years advanced, can’t be… the Blackwell can’t even be copied. He’s an amazing guy.”

当被问及何时首次获悉此事时，特朗普回答道：“我想说，当我听说这件事时，首先，我感到很惊讶，因为我认为他是一个非常有素质的人。我认识他很久了。英伟达是世界上市值最大，或者说是最大的公司之一。他靠一块芯片做到了这一点。当你仔细想想，你拥有一块领先十年、无法被……Blackwell甚至无法被复制的芯片。他是一个了不起的人。”

Regarding whether he would discuss the case with President Xi next week, Trump said: “No, I don’t think so. Maybe, I’m sure, President Xi will ask me about it. I just know Jensen very well. I just think he’s a very honorable man. He built a great company. American company. I think he’s a very honorable guy. I feel very confident that he’ll be in good shape.”

关于下周访问时是否会与习近平主席讨论此案，特朗普表示：“不，我不这么认为。也许，我肯定习近平主席会问我这件事。我只是非常了解黄仁勋。我只是认为他是一个非常正直的人。他建立了一家伟大的公司。一家美国公司。我认为他是一个非常正直的人。我非常有信心他会没事的。”
