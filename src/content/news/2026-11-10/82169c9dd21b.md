It’s barely been two months since the controversial Paramount and Warner Bros. merger, and we’ve already got a shockingly unexpected ouster: Co-CEOs of the rebooted DC Universe, James Gunn and Peter Safran, are stepping down from that role despite the Hollywood rumor mill assuming that the duo would be safe.

派拉蒙与华纳兄弟之间充满争议的合并案才过去不到两个月，我们就迎来了一次令人震惊的意外人事变动：尽管好莱坞传闻一直认为这对组合地位稳固，但重启后的 DC 宇宙联席首席执行官詹姆斯·古恩（James Gunn）和彼得·萨夫兰（Peter Safran）还是卸任了该职位。

It’s unclear whether the pair chose to resign or were forced out, or what will happen to their planned slate of projects beyond 2028’s Superman: Man of Tomorrow, which is in post-production. Producer Jeremy Latcham—a protege of Marvel’s Kevin Feige who worked with Gunn on Guardians of the Galaxy among other MCU films—will replace them.

目前尚不清楚两人是主动辞职还是被迫离职，也不清楚在已进入后期制作阶段的 2028 年电影《超人：明日之子》（Superman: Man of Tomorrow）之后，他们原定的项目计划将何去何从。制片人杰里米·拉查姆（Jeremy Latcham）将接替他们的职位，他曾是漫威凯文·费奇（Kevin Feige）的门生，并曾与古恩合作过《银河护卫队》及其他漫威电影宇宙作品。

Per Gunn and Safran’s joint statement: Four years ago, we set out to unite DC under one creative vision. In that time, we helped restore Superman to the heart of DC, while opening new doors and telling new stories in film and television with Peacemaker, Creature Commandos, Lanterns and Clayface.

根据古恩和萨夫兰的联合声明：四年前，我们着手将 DC 统一在一个创意愿景之下。在此期间，我们帮助超人重回 DC 的核心地位，同时通过《和平使者》、《生物突击队》、《绿灯侠》和《泥面人》在电影和电视领域开启了新的大门，讲述了新的故事。

We’re immensely proud of the team that turned that ambition into something real, including the actors, crews and executives we had the opportunity to collaborate with. We’ve shared a creative history with Jeremy going back to Marvel, and we’re eager to see the perspective he brings to DC’s next chapter.

我们为将这一雄心变为现实的团队感到无比自豪，其中包括我们有幸合作过的演员、剧组人员和高管。我们与杰里米在漫威时期就有着共同的创作经历，我们很期待看到他为 DC 的下一个篇章带来怎样的视角。

We’re excited to return to what drew us to this job in the first place, which is telling the stories we love, including the next chapter of Clark and Lex’s journey in Man of Tomorrow.

我们很高兴能回归最初吸引我们从事这份工作的初衷，那就是讲述我们热爱的故事，包括《明日之子》中克拉克和莱克斯旅程的下一个篇章。

DCU fans are mourning the news and what it could mean for the franchise. My feelings are mixed. On the one hand, I’m on record that I’ve found the rebooted DC Universe films released thus far to be enjoyable but rather forgettable, even when the positives outweigh the negatives.

DC 宇宙的粉丝们正在为这一消息及其对该系列可能产生的影响感到哀伤。我的心情很复杂。一方面，我曾公开表示，虽然重启后的 DC 宇宙电影在优点多于缺点的情况下依然令人愉悦，但它们往往让人过目即忘。

But HBO has done right by the DCU: I loved both seasons of Peacemaker; Lanterns is a shining beacon of what is possible when someone is allowed to get really creative within the genre; and I’m very much looking forward to next year’s Jimmy Olsen (Skylar Gisondo) series The People v. Gorilla Grodd—described as a “mockumentary crime comedy superhero series.”

但 HBO 对 DC 宇宙的处理非常到位：我非常喜欢《和平使者》的两季内容；《绿灯侠》展现了当创作者在类型片中获得充分发挥空间时所能达到的高度，是一座闪亮的灯塔；我也非常期待明年由斯凯勒·吉桑多（Skylar Gisondo）饰演吉米·奥尔森的剧集《人民诉大猩猩格鲁德》（The People v. Gorilla Grodd），该剧被描述为一部“伪纪录片风格的犯罪喜剧超级英雄剧集”。

I also love Gunn’s more irreverent sensibility with superhero fare and his rapport with fans. At least now he will get to focus on what he does best—and Gunn seems to feel the same way, per his personal Instagram post.

我也很喜欢古恩在超级英雄题材中那种更具颠覆性的感性，以及他与粉丝之间的融洽关系。至少现在他可以专注于自己最擅长的事情了——根据古恩个人 Instagram 的发文，他似乎也持同样的看法。

Gunn says he had mixed feelings about taking the job at first but “couldn’t pass up the unique opportunity,” adding that he has no regrets and is glad he took it. But Gunn also noted that he sometimes found the job trying, with very little time to recharge between projects; that his ADHD made it challenging to juggle so many projects at once; and that he disliked “the at-times corporate nature of the job,” thanking Safran for “taking the brunt of that.”

古恩表示，他起初对接受这份工作心情复杂，但“无法错过这个独特的机会”，并补充说他不后悔，很高兴自己接受了挑战。但古恩也指出，他有时会觉得这份工作很累人，项目之间几乎没有时间充电；他的多动症（ADHD）使他难以同时兼顾这么多项目；他还表示不喜欢“工作中时而出现的企业化性质”，并感谢萨夫兰“承担了大部分压力”。

He concluded, “I will miss being the co-caretaker of these great characters, but I’m also incredibly relieved and excited to get back to pure filmmaking.”

他总结道：“我会怀念作为这些伟大角色的共同守护者的时光，但我同时也感到无比轻松，并对回归纯粹的电影制作感到兴奋。”

Into the unknown: While Gunn and Safran reported directly to Warner Bros. Discovery CEO David Zaslav—unusual for the industry—Latcham will report to Dana Goldberg and Josh Greenstein, co-chairs of the Skydance Motion Picture Group.

进入未知领域：虽然古恩和萨夫兰直接向华纳兄弟探索公司首席执行官大卫·扎斯拉夫（David Zaslav）汇报（这在业内并不常见），但拉查姆将向天空之舞电影集团（Skydance Motion Picture Group）联席主席达娜·戈德堡（Dana Goldberg）和乔什·格林斯坦（Josh Greenstein）汇报。

“Jeremy brings a deep reverence for DC and its passionate fanbase, recognizing that they are the very foundation of this storied, multigenerational brand,” Goldberg and Greenstein said in a statement. “Under his leadership, every film and television show will be ushered from start to finish through the prism of the legions of fans who love and champion it. Jeremy’s track record exemplifies the excellence he will bring to building out DC and the many beloved characters within it.”

“杰里米对 DC 及其热情的粉丝群体怀有深深的敬意，他认识到他们正是这个拥有悠久历史、跨越几代人的品牌的基础，”戈德堡和格林斯坦在声明中表示。“在他的领导下，每一部电影和电视节目都将通过那些热爱并支持它的广大粉丝的视角，从始至终地进行引导。杰里米的过往业绩证明了他将为构建 DC 及其众多深受喜爱的角色所带来的卓越表现。”

Latcham issued his own statement: DC’s pantheon of characters is among the most iconic in American popular culture. I’ve had the privilege to help bring some of the most celebrated superhero stories to life on the big screen, and I’m excited and honored for the opportunity to now lead DC Studios into its next era.

拉查姆发表了自己的声明：DC 的角色群像在美国流行文化中最具标志性。我有幸帮助将一些最著名的超级英雄故事搬上大银幕，现在有机会带领 DC 工作室进入下一个时代，我感到非常兴奋和荣幸。

The chance to steward the incredible cast of DC characters to the screen in a way that excites lifelong fans while introducing their timeless stories to the next generation is a massive responsibility that I do not take lightly. I am grateful to Dana and Josh for putting their trust in me. I can’t wait to work night and day alongside filmmakers, comic book creators, artists and storytellers of every kind, across film, television and animation, to build something ambitious for longtime DC fans and newcomers alike.

有机会以一种既能让终身粉丝感到兴奋，又能向下一代介绍其永恒故事的方式，将 DC 令人难以置信的角色阵容带上银幕，这是一项巨大的责任，我不会掉以轻心。我感谢达娜和乔什对我的信任。我迫不及待地想与电影制作人、漫画创作者、艺术家和各类讲故事的人并肩作战，在电影、电视和动画领域日夜工作，为 DC 的老粉丝和新观众打造一些雄心勃勃的作品。

Latcham has the superhero bona fides, having been a producer on some of Marvel’s biggest hits: Guardians, Iron Man, Iron Man 2, The Avengers, Age of Ultron, and 2017’s Spider-Man: Homecoming. He left to create his own production company, which spearheaded 2023’s Dungeons and Dragons: Honor Among Thieves and 2018’s Bad Times at the El Royale.

拉查姆拥有制作超级英雄电影的资历，曾担任漫威多部大片的制片人，包括《银河护卫队》、《钢铁侠》、《钢铁侠 2》、《复仇者联盟》、《奥创纪元》以及 2017 年的《蜘蛛侠：英雄归来》。后来他离开并创立了自己的制作公司，该公司主导了 2023 年的《龙与地下城：侠盗荣耀》和 2018 年的《皇家酒店谋杀案》。

The DCU should be in capable hands, even if the overall vision changes. I’d like to see the DCU move away from the connected universe model and back to standalone films where the storytelling and character development aren’t beholden to setting up the next three films. But I’m not the head of a major studio.

即使整体愿景发生变化，DC 宇宙应该也处于能干的人手中。我希望看到 DC 宇宙摆脱互联宇宙模式，回归到独立的电影中，让故事讲述和角色发展不再受制于为后续三部电影做铺垫。但我毕竟不是大型电影公司的主管。
