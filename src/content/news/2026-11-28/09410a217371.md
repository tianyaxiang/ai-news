Title: Context Scaling: A New Frontier of AI Scaling

标题：上下文扩展：人工智能扩展的新前沿

Many failures of deployed AI arise not from weak inference but from a mismatch between the state a task depends on and the context available when the system acts. Relevant user state, repository revisions, tool traces, sensor readings, or environment changes may be unavailable, distorted, or poorly selected. We call the effort to reduce this bottleneck context scaling.

许多已部署人工智能的失败并非源于推理能力薄弱，而是源于任务所依赖的状态与系统行动时可用的上下文之间的不匹配。相关的用户状态、存储库修订、工具跟踪、传感器读数或环境变化可能无法获取、被扭曲或选择不当。我们将减少这一瓶颈的努力称为“上下文扩展”。

We frame a model as $P_\theta(d\mid c)$ within a system governed by three budgets: training $B_\theta$, decision-time $B_d$, and context-infrastructure $B_c$. Pretraining scaling invests in $\theta$; decision-time scaling searches, refines, or verifies decisions under supplied context; context scaling improves the system's capacity to acquire, represent, deliver, evaluate, and persist deployment state for model use.

我们将模型构建为受三个预算约束的系统中的 $P_\theta(d\mid c)$：训练预算 $B_\theta$、决策时间预算 $B_d$ 和上下文基础设施预算 $B_c$。预训练扩展投资于 $\theta$；决策时间扩展在提供的上下文下搜索、细化或验证决策；上下文扩展则提高了系统获取、表示、交付、评估和持久化部署状态以供模型使用的能力。

The three programs interact, but only $B_c$ targets what the system can make available before a decision. This view makes the model-infrastructure pair the unit of analysis. We define effective-context value as a task- and intervention-dependent attribution, separate deployment state from model-visible context, and identify availability, fidelity, selection, and utilization failures.

这三个程序相互作用，但只有 $B_c$ 针对的是系统在决策前可以提供的内容。这种观点使“模型-基础设施”对成为分析的单位。我们将有效上下文价值定义为一种依赖于任务和干预的归因，将部署状态与模型可见的上下文区分开来，并识别出可用性、保真度、选择和利用方面的失败。

We then organize current mechanisms through a five-operation context lifecycle and propose controlled budget interventions and utility-cost curves for evaluation. The resulting agenda centers on grounded reporting, interaction-data and full-cost infrastructure, and safety and governance for persistent, adaptive systems.

随后，我们通过一个五操作的上下文生命周期来组织当前的机制，并提出受控的预算干预和效用成本曲线以进行评估。由此产生的议程集中于扎实的报告、交互数据和全成本基础设施，以及针对持久、自适应系统的安全与治理。
