# Pop!_OS 26.04 LTS Has Landed

Pop!_OS 26.04 LTS is here after nearly a year of thoughtful feature development. With tons of improvements, new features, and the usual bug fixes, Pop!_OS 26.04 LTS feels right at home from the first boot. And when you're ready to make it your own, you can customize it until it looks and feels exactly the way you think a desktop should.

经过近一年的深思熟虑的功能开发，Pop!_OS 26.04 LTS 现已发布。凭借大量的改进、新功能以及常规的错误修复，Pop!_OS 26.04 LTS 在首次启动时就让人感到非常亲切。当你准备好打造属于自己的系统时，你可以对其进行自定义，直到它看起来和用起来完全符合你心目中桌面应有的样子。

It all starts with Constellations. What are Constellations? Constellations are COSMIC’s all-in-one bundled themes. With a single click you can change the wallpaper, accent colors, icons, and fonts. It's literally a one-click switch for a full aesthetic change of your desktop. Instead of having to go to Desktop > Wallpaper, then Desktop > Appearance, then Desktop Panel, then Desktop Dock, then Desktop Window Management, all you do is go to Settings > Desktop > Appearance, select between dark or light mode, enable or disable automatic mode switching, and then select your theme. Selecting a Constellation in Pop!_OS 26.04. You can also apply the Frosted Glass effect to a Constellation, and edit nearly every aspect of a Constellation. Once you have your Constellation exactly as you want, export it so you have it as a backup, or to share with others. With Constellations, the desktop is truly yours to customize and keep.

这一切都始于“星座”（Constellations）。什么是“星座”？“星座”是 COSMIC 的一体化捆绑主题。只需点击一下，你就可以更改壁纸、强调色、图标和字体。这确实是一键切换桌面的整体美学风格。你无需再依次进入“桌面 > 壁纸”、“桌面 > 外观”、“桌面面板”、“桌面停靠栏”以及“桌面窗口管理”，只需前往“设置 > 桌面 > 外观”，在深色或浅色模式之间进行选择，启用或禁用自动模式切换，然后选择你的主题即可。在 Pop!_OS 26.04 中选择“星座”主题。你还可以为“星座”应用毛玻璃效果，并编辑“星座”的几乎每一个方面。一旦你将“星座”调整到满意的状态，就可以将其导出作为备份，或与他人分享。有了“星座”，桌面真正成为了你可以自定义并保留的专属空间。

Also in 26.04: Drawing tablet support. Pop!_OS 26.04 LTS adds drawing tablet support, which brings precise, natural pen input and unlocks features like tilt sensitivity. That opens the door for graphic design, photo editing, animation, note-taking, CAD, and GIS applications. This release also adds an on-screen keyboard with touch and gamepad input.

26.04 版本还包括：绘图板支持。Pop!_OS 26.04 LTS 增加了对绘图板的支持，带来了精确、自然的笔触输入，并解锁了倾斜灵敏度等功能。这为图形设计、照片编辑、动画制作、笔记记录、CAD 和 GIS 应用程序打开了大门。此版本还增加了支持触摸和游戏手柄输入的屏幕键盘。

Automatic updates. You can now enable automatic updates during the initial setup or within Settings > System & accounts > System & application updates. No missing crucial updates to the OS! Automatic updates enabled.

自动更新。你现在可以在初始设置期间或在“设置 > 系统与账户 > 系统与应用程序更新”中启用自动更新。不会再错过操作系统的关键更新！已启用自动更新。

Native applications. Additionally, the growing family of native applications includes COSMIC Viewer, COSMIC System Monitor, and a fully redesigned COSMIC Store. COSMIC Viewer, COSMIC System Monitor and redesigned COSMIC Store.

原生应用程序。此外，不断壮大的原生应用程序家族包括 COSMIC Viewer、COSMIC System Monitor 以及经过全面重新设计的 COSMIC Store。COSMIC Viewer、COSMIC System Monitor 和重新设计的 COSMIC Store。

Additional features to enjoy: remote desktop support, accessibility improvements, shake to find cursor, scalable cursors, improved screen magnification, adjustable pointer size, configurable workspaces wrapping, improved gaming with pointer constraints, fullscreen improvements, automatic GPU selection improvements when working on hybrid systems.

其他值得体验的功能：远程桌面支持、辅助功能改进、摇动鼠标查找光标、可缩放光标、改进的屏幕放大功能、可调节指针大小、可配置的工作区循环切换、改进了带有指针约束的游戏体验、全屏改进、在混合系统上工作时改进的自动 GPU 选择。
