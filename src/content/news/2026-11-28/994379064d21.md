# Anyone can recount these 158 payments

A public proof of Edge Signals' x402 calls on Base, independently matched by Romeo Reynolds. x402 makes it easy to charge for an HTTP call. It also makes it easy to claim volume that nobody can check. I run a small paid API on Base, Edge Signals, and I wanted the opposite of that: a public file that states how many outside wallets have actually paid the shop, with enough detail that a stranger can recompute the figures from the chain and say whether they match. The file is free, and it is here: https://signals.edge.report/v1/proof.json.

这是一份关于 Edge Signals 在 Base 链上 x402 调用的公开证明，并已由 Romeo Reynolds 独立核对。x402 虽然简化了 HTTP 调用的收费流程，但也容易让人虚报无法核实的交易量。我经营着一个名为 Edge Signals 的小型 Base 付费 API，我希望实现与之相反的效果：提供一份公开文件，说明有多少外部钱包真正向该商店支付了费用，并提供足够的细节，让陌生人能够根据链上数据重新计算这些数字，以验证其是否匹配。该文件是免费的，地址为：https://signals.edge.report/v1/proof.json。

The numbers are small. From 9 August 2026 through 18 November 2026, after two operator test wallets are removed, the snapshot shows 158 paid calls, 0.269 USDC, 37 distinct wallets, and 17 wallets that paid more than once. Those 17 made 138 of the 158 calls. Most payments were $0.001 (149 of them). Six were $0.01, and three were $0.02. By month that is 24 calls in August, 41 in September, 73 in October, and 20 in November through the 18th.

数据规模很小。从 2026 年 8 月 9 日到 2026 年 11 月 18 日，在剔除两个运营商测试钱包后，快照显示共有 158 次付费调用、0.269 USDC、37 个独立钱包，以及 17 个多次付费的钱包。这 17 个钱包贡献了 158 次调用中的 138 次。大多数支付金额为 0.001 美元（共 149 笔）。其中 6 笔为 0.01 美元，3 笔为 0.02 美元。按月份统计，8 月份有 24 次调用，9 月份 41 次，10 月份 73 次，11 月份截至 18 日有 20 次。

What is being counted Every figure under outside_paid_calls comes from on-chain USDC transfers on Base (eip155:8453) to a single payTo, 0xE2520f1497ee47645072a6214304807BC3340D58. The USDC contract is 0x833589fCD6eDb6E08f4c7C32D4f71b54bdA02913. The window is UTC, starting at 2026-08-09T00:00:00Z inclusive and ending at 2026-11-19T00:00:00Z exclusive. The snapshot is frozen as of 18 November 2026.

统计内容如下：outside_paid_calls 下的每一个数字均来自 Base 链（eip155:8453）上向单一收款地址 0xE2520f1497ee47645072a6214304807BC3340D58 发起的 USDC 转账。USDC 合约地址为 0x833589fCD6eDb6E08f4c7C32D4f71b54bdA02913。统计窗口为 UTC 时间，起始于 2026-08-09T00:00:00Z（包含），截止于 2026-11-19T00:00:00Z（不包含）。快照于 2026 年 11 月 18 日冻结。

The file defines a paid call as one inbound USDC Transfer log whose to address is the payTo, inside that window. The payer is the from address on the transfer, not the facilitator that submitted the transaction. A returning wallet is a payer with two or more counted transfers after the excluded wallets are removed. The two wallets we exclude are published in the file: 0xf8E55aAA8F2f41B0D7cC2c6502C1311cb1342a39 and 0x7ACB7828374073e7d7BeC6A105F6DD757aa7BcA7. Anyone can apply the same filter.

该文件将“付费调用”定义为在统计窗口内，收款地址为上述 payTo 地址的一条入站 USDC 转账日志。付款人是转账的“from”地址，而非提交交易的辅助方。重复付费钱包是指在剔除排除名单后，拥有两次或以上统计转账记录的付款人。我们排除的两个钱包已在文件中公布：0xf8E55aAA8F2f41B0D7cC2c6502C1311cb1342a39 和 0x7ACB7828374073e7d7BeC6A105F6DD757aa7BcA7。任何人都可以应用相同的过滤条件。

Whether the list is complete is something I assert. An outsider cannot verify that we have no other operator wallets. The file states two further limits. A distinct wallet is not a distinct buyer: one person or agent fleet can use several wallets, and one wallet can serve many agents. And a USDC transfer shows that payment occurred, not that the paid HTTP response was delivered or consumed. The count is also by payTo, not by product or host. Pathmint, a second service of ours, uses the same address, so the 158 is inbound USDC to that wallet, not a per-SKU or per-host breakdown.

该列表是否完整是我个人的声明。外部人员无法验证我们是否还有其他运营商钱包。该文件还指出了另外两个限制：独立钱包并不等同于独立买家：一个人或代理集群可以使用多个钱包，而一个钱包也可以服务于多个代理。此外，USDC 转账仅证明支付行为已发生，并不代表付费的 HTTP 响应已送达或被消费。统计数据是按 payTo 地址计算的，而非按产品或主机计算。我们的另一项服务 Pathmint 也使用该地址，因此 158 次调用是指进入该钱包的 USDC 总数，而非按 SKU 或主机进行的细分。

One more distinction matters if you fetch the file today. usage_counter is a live ledger counter on the host. When I fetched the file on 27 November 2026, it showed 182 paid calls served and 49 unique paying agents. That field is not the on-chain snapshot, and it was not part of the independent check below. Off-chain Stripe and Tempo settlements are recorded separately and are not in the 158.

如果您今天获取该文件，还有一点需要注意。usage_counter 是主机上的实时账本计数器。当我在 2026 年 11 月 27 日获取该文件时，它显示已处理 182 次付费调用，共有 49 个独立付费代理。该字段并非链上快照，也不属于下文提到的独立核对范围。链下的 Stripe 和 Tempo 结算记录是分开的，不包含在这 158 次调用中。

One index is not enough On 20 November I tried to recount the same payTo from Blockscout's address token-transfers API. I got 141 outside calls instead of 158. The API had omitted 19 November transfers, 17 of them outside $0.001 payments, and its transaction endpoint returned 404 for a hash whose receipt exists on Base — including the example transaction the proof itself links to. A keyless eth_getLogs pull of USDC Transfer logs to the payTo, over the same UTC window, gave the full 158. I confirmed each of the 17 missing transfers with eth_getTransactionReceipt. The lesson is ordinary and easy to forget: a count from one indexer can be silently low. The proof tells readers to recompute with eth_getLogs, or to cross-check the BaseScan token-transfer list, and not to rely on Blockscout's transfers API alone.

单一索引是不够的。11 月 20 日，我尝试通过 Blockscout 的地址代币转账 API 重新统计同一个 payTo 地址的记录，结果只得到了 141 次外部调用，而非 158 次。该 API 遗漏了 11 月 19 日的转账记录，其中 17 笔是非 0.001 美元的支付。此外，对于一个在 Base 链上确实存在回执的哈希值（包括证明文件中链接的示例交易），其交易端点返回了 404 错误。通过无密钥的 eth_getLogs 拉取同一 UTC 窗口内指向该 payTo 地址的 USDC 转账日志，则完整得到了 158 次记录。我通过 eth_getTransactionReceipt 核实了那 17 笔缺失的转账。这给我们的教训很普通却容易被遗忘：单一索引器的统计结果可能会在不经意间偏低。该证明建议读者使用 eth_getLogs 进行重新计算，或交叉核对 BaseScan 的代币转账列表，不要仅依赖 Blockscout 的转账 API。

A stranger did the recount That recomputability is only a claim until someone else tries it. In x402-foundation/x402#4127, a thread about optional, verifiable measurement for x402 sellers, Romeo Reynolds offered to recompute the figures from public chain data — just the payTo and the window, without touching the endpoint. I published the two operator wallets so the exclusion would be checkable, and I asked him to post whatever he found, including mismatches.

陌生人进行了重算。这种“可重算性”在有人尝试之前仅仅是一个声明。在关于 x402 卖家可选、可验证度量的讨论帖 x402-foundation/x402#4127 中，Romeo Reynolds 主动提出根据公开链上数据重新计算这些数字——仅使用 payTo 地址和时间窗口，而不触及端点。我公布了两个运营商钱包以便核对排除情况，并请他发布任何发现，包括不匹配之处。

On 21 November, at 8:24 AM America/Phoenix, he reported that all 20 figures in the proof matched, using a BaseScan ERC-20 export: 178 transfers in the window, 20 from one excluded wallet, none from the other. Later that afternoon he reran the check with eth_getLogs against a Base archive node (publicnode, blocks 49722127 to 60466338). All 20 figures matched again. The node read had 186 transfers: the 185 in the export, plus one later transfer from an excluded wallet. Every one of the 186 had an AuthorizationUsed event from the USDC contract in the same transaction, so they are all EIP-3009 payments.

11 月 21 日上午 8:24（美国凤凰城时间），他报告称证明中的所有 20 个数字均匹配。他使用的是 BaseScan 的 ERC-20 导出数据：窗口内共有 178 笔转账，其中 20 笔来自一个被排除的钱包，另一个钱包则没有记录。当天下午，他使用 eth_getLogs 对 Base 归档节点（publicnode，区块 49722127 至 60466338）重新进行了核对。所有 20 个数字再次匹配。节点读取结果显示共有 186 笔转账：即导出数据中的 185 笔，加上一笔来自被排除钱包的后续转账。这 186 笔转账中的每一笔在同一交易中都有来自 USDC 合约的 AuthorizationUsed 事件，因此它们都是 EIP-3009 支付。

He also noted what the match does not show: completeness of the exclusion list, delivery of the paid response, the live usage_counter, and the difference between a wallet and a buyer. The two busiest wallets made 45 of the 158 calls. Payments landed on 64 of 102 days. The most distinct outside wallets on any single UTC day was four, on 28 October 2026. The same shop can fail a 20-wallet bar measured per day and clear it measured over the whole window. That is a fact about thresholds, not a claim about growth.

他还指出，匹配结果无法证明以下几点：排除列表的完整性、付费响应的送达情况、实时 usage_counter 的准确性，以及钱包与买家之间的区别。最活跃的两个钱包贡献了 158 次调用中的 45 次。在 102 天内，有 64 天发生了支付。单日（UTC）内出现的最多的独立外部钱包数量为 4 个，出现在 2026 年 10 月 28 日。同一家商店可能无法达到“每日 20 个钱包”的门槛，但在整个统计窗口内却能达到。这是关于阈值的事实，而非关于增长的声明。

On 27 November, with my agreement, Romeo published the transfer list as a public gist: https://gist.github.com/romeoreynolds/80475647d6ec19ef042ee49527346d6f. It contains 186 transfers to the payTo, 178 in the window, and 158 after the two operator wallets are removed. From that list he confirmed 158 calls, 37 wallets, 17 returning, and 138 returning-wallet calls, and the busiest day of four wallets on 28 October. The gist includes the CSV, the raw logs, a snapshot of the proof, the scripts, and sha256 hashes. The chain is the source of truth; the gist is a convenience copy that can be rebuilt from a Base archive node. On the same thread, logout2login has been drafting a measurement schema so a seller record can point at evi

11 月 27 日，经我同意，Romeo 将转账列表发布为公开的 gist：https://gist.github.com/romeoreynolds/80475647d6ec19ef042ee49527346d6f。其中包含指向该 payTo 地址的 186 笔转账，窗口内为 178 笔，剔除两个运营商钱包后为 158 笔。通过该列表，他确认了 158 次调用、37 个钱包、17 个重复钱包、138 次重复钱包调用，以及 10 月 28 日四个钱包的最繁忙记录。该 gist 包含了 CSV 文件、原始日志、证明快照、脚本以及 sha256 哈希值。链上数据是事实的来源；而 gist 只是一个便利副本，可以从 Base 归档节点重新构建。在同一个讨论帖中，logout2login 一直在起草一种度量模式，以便卖家记录可以指向 evi。
