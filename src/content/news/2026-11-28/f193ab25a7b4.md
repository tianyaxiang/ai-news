Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.33266 (cs) [Submitted on 24 Nov 2026] Title: FrIdéo: French News Outlet Ideology Scoring via Multi-Source Evidence Fusion and Distancing-Aware Lexical Scoring Authors: Amr Sobhy.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.33266 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 24 日] 标题：FrIdéo：通过多源证据融合和距离感知词汇评分对法国新闻媒体进行意识形态评分 作者：Amr Sobhy。

Abstract: Outlet ideology is a standard explanatory variable in media framing, media effects, and political communication research, but continuous, ecosystem-wide coverage lets it enter as graded exposure rather than a coarse bin. For France, existing references are either broad but non-analytic or analytic but sparse and source-specific, and none we are aware of offers ecosystem-wide coverage on a unified continuous scale.

摘要：媒体意识形态是媒体框架、媒体效应和政治传播研究中的一个标准解释变量，但持续的、全生态系统的覆盖使其能够以分级暴露而非粗略分类的形式进入研究。对于法国而言，现有的参考资料要么范围广泛但缺乏分析性，要么具有分析性但零散且针对特定来源，据我们所知，没有任何研究能在统一的连续尺度上提供全生态系统的覆盖。

We contribute FrIdéo: to our knowledge the first ecosystem-wide continuous ideology scores for the French press, a transparent composite score for 30 French news outlets, fusing nine independent families of evidence with empirical-Bayes-style shrinkage toward a structural prior. For the text-based family we introduce symmetric plain/qualified scoring, which separates the plain use of ideologically loaded vocabulary from its qualified (distanced) use on both sides, a distinction bag-of-words methods miss.

我们贡献了 FrIdéo：据我们所知，这是首个针对法国新闻界的全生态系统连续意识形态评分，为 30 家法国新闻媒体提供了透明的综合评分，融合了九个独立的证据族，并采用了向结构化先验收缩的经验贝叶斯风格。对于基于文本的证据族，我们引入了对称的“普通/限定”评分，将意识形态负载词汇的普通使用与其在双方的限定（疏离）使用区分开来，这是词袋模型所忽略的区别。

The resulting scale runs from L'Humanité on the far left to Valeurs actuelles on the far right; the far-right pole is near-indistinguishable and CNews ties Le Figaro. Because the text family reads current articles, it also captures editorial change that static sources miss, such as the Journal du Dimanche's sharp rightward shift after its 2023 ownership change. The ordering is robust to perturbation and to dropping any single family, and agrees with held-out Media Bias Fact Check labels. We release the scores, confidence intervals, and replication code.

由此产生的量表从极左的《人道报》（L'Humanité）延伸至极右的《当代价值》（Valeurs actuelles）；极右翼两极几乎无法区分，CNews 与《费加罗报》（Le Figaro）持平。由于文本证据族读取的是当前文章，它还能捕捉到静态来源所忽略的编辑方针变化，例如《星期日报》（Journal du Dimanche）在 2023 年所有权变更后出现的剧烈右转。该排序对扰动和剔除任何单一证据族具有稳健性，并与留出的“媒体偏见事实核查”（Media Bias Fact Check）标签一致。我们发布了评分、置信区间和复现代码。
