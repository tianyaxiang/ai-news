# Switzerland Confirms AI Agents Breached Critical Infrastructure Operators in Its First Agentic Attack Cases

TL;DR what: Switzerland's National Cyber Security Centre confirmed its first cyberattacks carried out by autonomous AI agents, with four of five reported attacks breaching the victim's systems. impact: Three of the attacks targeted critical infrastructure operators in at least two sectors and two succeeded, though no major disruptions have been reported. fix: Swiss, Italian and UK agencies recommend patching critical flaws on internet-facing systems within 24 hours, isolating systems that cannot be patched, enforcing MFA or passkeys, and correlating logs for bursts of agent traffic. who: Critical infrastructure operators and small and medium-sized organizations with exposed services, leaked credentials, weak API authentication or slow patch cycles.

简要概述：瑞士国家网络安全中心确认了首批由自主人工智能代理实施的网络攻击，在报告的五起攻击中，有四起成功入侵了受害者的系统。影响：其中三起攻击针对至少两个行业的关键基础设施运营商，且有两起成功，目前尚未报告重大中断。修复建议：瑞士、意大利和英国机构建议在24小时内修补面向互联网系统的关键漏洞，隔离无法修补的系统，强制执行多因素身份验证（MFA）或密钥，并关联日志以监测代理流量的突发情况。受影响对象：关键基础设施运营商以及存在暴露服务、凭据泄露、API身份验证薄弱或补丁周期缓慢的中小型组织。

Autonomous AI agents have broken into critical infrastructure operators in Switzerland. Florian Schütz, director of the Swiss National Cyber Security Centre (NCSC), told public broadcaster SRF that attackers using AI agents breached systems in four of five attacks reported in recent days. These are the first confirmed cases of this kind in the country. Three of the five targeted critical infrastructure operators and two of those three succeeded. No major disruptions have been reported. The victims have not been named, and attribution is unknown. Switzerland is not an isolated case. In the same week, Italy's CSIRT Italia and Britain's NCSC both published guidance on AI-driven intrusion campaigns, and the European Commission has confirmed an earlier breach by an AI attack framework. Taken together, the reports show attackers have moved agentic offensive tooling out of research labs and into routine operations against real targets.

自主人工智能代理已入侵瑞士的关键基础设施运营商。瑞士国家网络安全中心（NCSC）主任弗洛里安·舒茨（Florian Schütz）向公共广播公司SRF表示，在近日报告的五起攻击中，攻击者利用人工智能代理入侵了四个系统。这是该国首次确认此类案件。五起攻击中有三起针对关键基础设施运营商，其中两起成功。目前尚未报告重大中断。受害者身份未被披露，攻击来源尚不明确。瑞士并非个例。同一周内，意大利计算机安全事件响应小组（CSIRT Italia）和英国国家网络安全中心（NCSC）均发布了关于人工智能驱动入侵活动的指南，欧盟委员会也确认了此前一起由人工智能攻击框架引发的入侵事件。综合来看，这些报告表明攻击者已将代理式攻击工具从研究实验室转移到针对真实目标的日常行动中。

What the Swiss Agency Disclosed The Swiss NCSC first published a security advisory on Tuesday. At that point it said the agents had compromised the victims' IT systems in every known case. In Wednesday's broadcast interview the figure became four of five. Neither source explains the change. The victims include small and medium-sized companies, and all of them reported voluntarily, so the real number of incidents is likely higher than the reported count. The targeted operators come from at least two critical infrastructure sectors. Switzerland's definition covers suppliers of electricity, drinking water and telecommunications. The advisory called the cases "a shift in the cyber threat landscape." 5 attacks reported in recent days, all by voluntary disclosure 4 of 5 resulted in a breach of IT systems 3 targeted critical infrastructure operators; 2 of those succeeded At least 2 critical infrastructure sectors affected 0 major service disruptions reported so far

瑞士机构披露的内容：瑞士NCSC于周二首次发布安全公告。当时称代理在所有已知案例中均已入侵受害者的IT系统。在周三的广播采访中，这一数字变为五分之四。两个来源均未解释这一变化。受害者包括中小型企业，且均为自愿报告，因此实际事件数量可能高于报告数量。受攻击的运营商来自至少两个关键基础设施行业。瑞士的定义涵盖电力、饮用水和电信供应商。该公告称这些案例是“网络威胁格局的转变”。近日报告了5起攻击，均为自愿披露；5起中有4起导致IT系统被入侵；3起针对关键基础设施运营商，其中2起成功；至少2个关键基础设施行业受影响；目前报告0起重大服务中断。

How Agentic Offensive Frameworks Operate The Swiss agency calls the tooling "agentic offensive frameworks": AI agents that find security weaknesses and exploit them without a human driving each step. Its advisory named ARTEX and CyberStrikeAI as examples of agents reachable over the internet. It did not say these tools were used in the five Swiss cases. According to the agency, the agents mostly scan for known, unpatched weaknesses and can run many attacks in parallel. When defenses respond, they change methods. The result is a much shorter window for detection and response. The agency also says the number of AI attack tools freely reachable online has nearly doubled since October. That figure counts available tools, not attacks or victims. CSIRT Italia's bulletin gives more operational detail. In cases it observed firsthand, AI-directed agents compromised a large number of systems within a few hours. The agents adjusted their actions within seconds, ran in parallel without human input, and chained vulnerabilities, misconfigurations and compromised credentials together. The bulletin names four frameworks: ARTEX, CyberStrikeAI, PentAGI and DeepSeek Harness. DeepSeek Harness is an open-source agent whose Red Team Mode uses a built-in prompt to get around the underlying model's safety limits. ⚠️ Why single-event detection fails — CSIRT Italia reports that each individual agent action looks like normal user or API traffic. Detection depends on context: where requests originate, how often they arrive, how much data moves, and the order in which actions happen. A rule that inspects one request at a time will not catch them.

代理式攻击框架的运作方式：瑞士机构将这些工具称为“代理式攻击框架”：即无需人工干预即可发现并利用安全漏洞的人工智能代理。其公告列举了ARTEX和CyberStrikeAI作为可通过互联网访问的代理示例，但未说明这些工具是否用于瑞士的五起案件。据该机构称，这些代理主要扫描已知的未修补漏洞，并能并行执行多次攻击。当防御系统做出反应时，它们会改变方法，导致检测和响应的时间窗口大幅缩短。该机构还表示，自10月以来，在线可自由访问的人工智能攻击工具数量几乎翻了一番。该数字统计的是可用工具，而非攻击或受害者。意大利CSIRT的公告提供了更多操作细节。在其亲眼观察的案例中，人工智能引导的代理在几小时内入侵了大量系统。代理在几秒钟内调整行动，在无人干预的情况下并行运行，并将漏洞、错误配置和被盗凭据串联起来。公告列举了四个框架：ARTEX、CyberStrikeAI、PentAGI和DeepSeek Harness。DeepSeek Harness是一个开源代理，其红队模式使用内置提示词来绕过底层模型的安全限制。⚠️ 为什么单事件检测会失败——意大利CSIRT报告称，每个单独的代理操作看起来都像正常的流量或API请求。检测取决于上下文：请求的来源、到达频率、数据移动量以及操作发生的顺序。逐个检查请求的规则无法捕捉到它们。

Old Weaknesses, New Tempo Britain's NCSC reports the same pattern. "Since September 2026, the NCSC has seen a marked rise in AI-assisted cyber attacks," the agency said. Some incidents took internet-facing services offline. Others gave attackers a foothold for more agent-driven activity. The UK agency stresses that the entry points are not new: credentials committed to code repositories, misconfigured infrastructure-as-code, default or missing authentication, and weak APIs. What has changed is speed, persistence and cost. An agent can probe thousands of targets for very little money and keep trying after the first attempt fails. The UK NCSC also notes that the frameworks are noisy. Their bursts of requests and high error rates make them detectable for organizations that correlate logs across sources.

旧漏洞，新节奏：英国NCSC报告了同样的模式。该机构表示：“自2026年9月以来，NCSC观察到人工智能辅助的网络攻击显著增加。”一些事件导致面向互联网的服务离线，另一些则为攻击者提供了更多代理驱动活动的立足点。英国机构强调，切入点并不新鲜：提交到代码仓库的凭据、配置错误的基础设施即代码（IaC）、默认或缺失的身份验证以及薄弱的API。改变的是速度、持久性和成本。一个代理可以用极低的成本探测数千个目标，并在首次尝试失败后持续尝试。英国NCSC还指出，这些框架会产生大量噪音。它们的突发请求和高错误率使得能够跨来源关联日志的组织可以检测到它们。

The European Commission Precedent The European Commission has confirmed that attackers using ARTEX broke into its human resources, medical reimbursement and translation systems. The intrusion was first detected on October 22, about five weeks before the Swiss disclosures. It is the most prominent confirmed breach so far tied to a named agentic framework, and it shows that large institutions with mature security programs are affected too. Raphael Reischuk, co-founder of Switzerland's National Test Institute for Cybersecurity, expects a larger wave of attacks and says companies are underestimating the risk. Agency Mitigation Guidance The three agencies agree on one main goal: reduce the time attackers have. Their published recommendations overlap: Switzerland: apply critical security updates to internet-facing systems within 24 hours of release Italy: patch

欧盟委员会的先例：欧盟委员会已确认，使用ARTEX的攻击者入侵了其人力资源、医疗报销和翻译系统。该入侵事件于10月22日首次被发现，比瑞士披露的时间早了约五周。这是迄今为止与命名代理框架相关的最显著的确认入侵事件，表明拥有成熟安全计划的大型机构也受到影响。瑞士国家网络安全测试研究所联合创始人拉斐尔·赖舒克（Raphael Reischuk）预计将出现更大规模的攻击浪潮，并表示企业低估了风险。机构缓解指南：这三个机构在主要目标上达成一致：减少攻击者拥有的时间。它们发布的建议存在重叠：瑞士：在发布后24小时内对面向互联网的系统应用关键安全更新；意大利：修补。
