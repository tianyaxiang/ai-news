Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.33263 (cs) [Submitted on 24 Nov 2026] Title: What, Who, and When: Structured Harm Assessment for Mental Health Safety via Short Reasoning. Authors: Catarina Botelho, António Farinhas, John Mendonça, Areti Vassilopoulos, Inês Gonçalves, Maya D'Eon, Hugo Pitorro, Laura Cabello, Carrie Spadine, Nuno M. Guerreiro, Ricardo Rei.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.33263 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 24 日] 标题：什么、谁和何时：通过简短推理进行心理健康安全的结构化伤害评估。作者：Catarina Botelho, António Farinhas, John Mendonça, Areti Vassilopoulos, Inês Gonçalves, Maya D'Eon, Hugo Pitorro, Laura Cabello, Carrie Spadine, Nuno M. Guerreiro, Ricardo Rei。

Abstract: The growing use of large language models (LLMs) for mental health support raises important safety concerns. Existing guard models primarily identify categories of harmful content, often overlooking the context needed to determine an appropriate response. We introduce a clinically grounded taxonomy that characterizes harm along three dimensions: what the harm is, who is involved, and when it occurs.

摘要：大型语言模型（LLM）在心理健康支持中的日益普及引发了重要的安全担忧。现有的防护模型主要识别有害内容的类别，往往忽略了确定适当响应所需的背景信息。我们引入了一种基于临床的分类法，从三个维度来描述伤害：伤害是什么、涉及谁以及伤害何时发生。

It further separates harm characterization from intervention decisions, thus enabling safety policies to be specified outside the model weights through explicit, clinician-curated instructions that can be updated and audited. We validate the taxonomy with expert clinicians and validate a panel of LLM judges to scale annotation. Relabeling public benchmarks in this taxonomy shows that they rarely contain harm directed at the user, which accounts for nearly half of the harm in real conversations.

它进一步将伤害表征与干预决策分离开来，从而使安全策略能够通过可更新和可审计的、由临床医生策划的明确指令，在模型权重之外进行指定。我们与临床专家一起验证了该分类法，并验证了一组 LLM 评判员以扩展标注规模。使用该分类法对公共基准进行重新标注显示，它们很少包含针对用户的伤害，而这在真实对话中占伤害总数近一半。

We then train MindGuard2, a 35B-A3B reasoning-based guard model using supervised fine-tuning on short reasoning traces followed by reinforcement learning with a reasoning-length penalty, to minimize inference overhead while preserving the benefits of reasoning. On two public benchmarks and real-world conversations, MindGuard2 achieves balanced accuracy comparable to the substantially larger GLM-5.3 at approximately 10x lower median latency, advancing the accuracy-latency Pareto frontier.

随后，我们训练了 MindGuard2，这是一个 35B-A3B 基于推理的防护模型。该模型通过对简短推理轨迹进行监督微调，并辅以带有推理长度惩罚的强化学习，旨在最大限度地减少推理开销，同时保留推理带来的优势。在两个公共基准和真实对话中，MindGuard2 实现了与规模大得多的 GLM-5.3 相当的平衡准确率，且中位延迟降低了约 10 倍，推动了准确率-延迟帕累托前沿的发展。

Furthermore, it achieves low false-positive rates at high recall, helping balance the need to detect unsafe situations against the risk of unnecessary interventions that may disrupt support and undermine user trust. We release a safety guardrail model with SOTA performance trained on synthetic data, together with synthetic conversations and annotations of public benchmarks in our taxonomy.

此外，它在高召回率下实现了低误报率，有助于平衡检测不安全情况的需求与不必要干预的风险，因为后者可能会破坏支持并损害用户信任。我们发布了一个在合成数据上训练的、具有 SOTA（当前最佳）性能的安全防护模型，以及合成对话和我们分类法下的公共基准标注。
