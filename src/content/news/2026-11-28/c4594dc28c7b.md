Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.33304 (cs) [Submitted on 24 Nov 2026] Title: The Platonic Representation Hypothesis in Jacobian-Space: Language and Vision Models Agree Approximately Up to a Rotation Authors: Ross Murphy, Gabriel Brostow.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.33304 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 24 日] 标题：雅可比空间中的柏拉图表征假说：语言模型与视觉模型在旋转意义上近似一致 作者：Ross Murphy, Gabriel Brostow。

Abstract: The Platonic Representation Hypothesis (PRH) posits that AI models converge toward a shared representation of the world, even when trained on different data modalities. Previous work has found evidence for the PRH in the activations of vision and language models. In this work, we ask whether vision and language models also converge in how they act on these representations: precisely, in how these internal representations affect the models' output.

摘要：柏拉图表征假说（PRH）认为，人工智能模型即使在不同的数据模态上进行训练，也会趋向于对世界形成一种共享的表征。先前的研究已在视觉和语言模型的激活中发现了支持 PRH 的证据。在这项工作中，我们探讨了视觉模型和语言模型在如何作用于这些表征方面是否也存在趋同：具体而言，即这些内部表征如何影响模型的输出。

At a chosen layer of a transformer, the Jacobian lens gives every word in the vocabulary (or class in an image classifier) a vector in the hidden state: the vector that most increases the model's tendency to generate that word, at that position or later, or to predict that class. Collectively, these vectors make up what is referred to as the model's J-space.

在 Transformer 的选定层中，雅可比透镜为词汇表中的每个单词（或图像分类器中的每个类别）在隐藏状态中赋予一个向量：该向量最能增加模型在该位置或后续位置生成该单词的倾向，或预测该类别的倾向。这些向量共同构成了所谓的模型“J 空间”（J-space）。

We find evidence of the PRH in J-space across vision and language models, and furthermore, we find that J-spaces mostly converge up to a rotation. That is, a single rotation, fitted on a subset of words, approximately maps one model's J-space onto another's, and so applies to words and classes it was not fitted on.

我们在视觉和语言模型的 J 空间中发现了支持 PRH 的证据，此外，我们还发现 J 空间在旋转意义上基本趋于一致。也就是说，通过在部分单词子集上拟合出的单一旋转矩阵，可以将一个模型的 J 空间近似映射到另一个模型的 J 空间上，因此该方法也适用于未参与拟合的单词和类别。

A word's vector, rotated from a text-only language model's J-space into the visual patch tokens of a vision-language model, changes the object the vision-language model says is in the image (and only in the image region where it is added). Rotated into an image generator's text conditioning, a word's vector changes the object in the generated image. Rotated into an ImageNet classifier (trained without text), it raises that object's class score above almost all others, and vice versa. This suggests that models converge not only in how they represent the world, but also in how they act on it.

将一个单词的向量从纯文本语言模型的 J 空间旋转到视觉-语言模型的视觉补丁标记中，会改变该视觉-语言模型对图像中物体内容的描述（且仅限于添加该向量的图像区域）。将其旋转到图像生成器的文本调节中，该单词的向量会改变生成图像中的物体。将其旋转到 ImageNet 分类器（未经过文本训练）中，它会将该物体的类别得分提升至高于几乎所有其他类别，反之亦然。这表明，模型不仅在如何表征世界方面趋于一致，在如何作用于世界方面也同样如此。
