Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.33165 (cs) [Submitted on 4 Oct 2026] Title:AutoformBench: A Lean Formalization Benchmark for Advanced Mathematics Authors:Charles Arnal, Ahmad Rammal, Niket Patel, Fabian Gloeckle, Bartosz Piotrowski, Amaury Hayat, Julia Kempe, Rémi Munos, Vivien Cabannes.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.33165 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 10 月 4 日] 标题：AutoformBench：用于高等数学的 Lean 形式化基准测试 作者：Charles Arnal, Ahmad Rammal, Niket Patel, Fabian Gloeckle, Bartosz Piotrowski, Amaury Hayat, Julia Kempe, Rémi Munos, Vivien Cabannes。

Abstract: AI systems are increasingly capable of producing machine-checked Lean proofs of advanced mathematical results. Real mathematical formalization, however, is not isolated theorem proving: it requires building the definitions and intermediate lemmas that connect a result to a formal library.

摘要：人工智能系统在生成高等数学成果的机器验证 Lean 证明方面能力日益增强。然而，真正的数学形式化并非孤立的定理证明：它需要构建将结果与形式化库连接起来的定义和中间引理。

Existing evaluations often provide isolated theorem statements in settings where much of this supporting theory is already available in Mathlib. We introduce AutoformBench, a benchmark of 341 Lean 4 formalization tasks drawn from 24 advanced mathematical sources.

现有的评估通常在 Mathlib 中已提供大部分支持理论的环境下，提供孤立的定理陈述。我们引入了 AutoformBench，这是一个包含 341 个 Lean 4 形式化任务的基准测试，这些任务取自 24 个高等数学来源。

Lean-experienced mathematicians formalized and audited each target statement, leaving the corresponding Lean proof to the evaluated agent. Each problem is embedded in a broader mathematical development: agents receive the informal proof but must translate it into Lean while constructing the missing definitions, intermediate lemmas, and supporting infrastructure.

具有 Lean 经验的数学家对每个目标陈述进行了形式化和审核，并将相应的 Lean 证明留给被评估的智能体。每个问题都嵌入在更广泛的数学发展中：智能体接收非正式证明，但必须在构建缺失的定义、中间引理和支持性基础设施的同时，将其翻译成 Lean。

We compare the performance of frontier models on these problems. We find a largely shared difficulty frontier: success depends less on mathematical domain than on models' ability to construct missing formal infrastructure.

我们比较了前沿模型在这些问题上的表现。我们发现了一个大致共享的难度边界：成功与否与其说是取决于数学领域，不如说是取决于模型构建缺失形式化基础设施的能力。
