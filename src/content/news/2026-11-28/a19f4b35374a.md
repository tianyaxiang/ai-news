# Allstate has been suffering an outage since Monday

One of the biggest insurance companies in the country has been dealing with a major outage for five days.

作为全国最大的保险公司之一，该公司已经历了五天的大规模服务中断。

On Monday, November 23rd, Allstate began dealing with an outage that has impacted its website, mobile apps, and roadside assistance service. It’s just the latest company, including Microsoft and JPMorgan Chase, to suffer an outage in the last few weeks. As of Friday afternoon, access to accounts through the app and site appeared to still be down.

11月23日星期一，Allstate 开始遭遇服务中断，影响了其网站、移动应用程序和道路救援服务。继微软和摩根大通之后，该公司成为过去几周内又一家遭遇服务中断的企业。截至周五下午，通过应用程序和网站访问账户的功能似乎仍处于瘫痪状态。

When asked for comment, the company pointed us to a notice on its site reading: Allstate customers can reach us in many ways, including online, by phone and through local offices. Some access has been restricted over the last 48 hours, but we continue to be available for customers and respond to and settle claims. To the extent service interruptions impact a customer’s ability to make a payment, they won’t lose coverage or be charged late fees.

当被要求置评时，该公司向我们指引了其网站上的一则通知，内容如下：Allstate 的客户可以通过多种方式联系我们，包括在线、电话以及通过当地办事处。过去48小时内部分访问受到限制，但我们仍继续为客户提供服务，并处理和结算索赔。如果服务中断影响了客户的付款能力，他们不会失去保险保障，也不会被收取滞纳金。

Opening the app pops up a notification saying the company is working to “restore access as soon as possible,” and encourages customers to contact the company by phone to file and manage claims. Users have flooded X and Reddit with complaints about the outage, some of whom have either been left waiting for roadside assistance or have been unable to insure new vehicles.

打开该应用程序会弹出一条通知，称公司正致力于“尽快恢复访问”，并鼓励客户通过电话联系公司以提交和管理索赔。用户在 X 和 Reddit 上大量投诉此次中断，其中一些人要么在等待道路救援，要么无法为新车投保。

Allstate hasn’t publicly identified a cause for the outage.

Allstate 尚未公开说明此次服务中断的原因。
