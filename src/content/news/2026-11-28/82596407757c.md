# YouTube Ad Punisher – Auto-reloads YouTube pages when an ad plays

当广告播放时自动重新加载 YouTube 页面——保留您的播放进度。惩罚这些广告。

Ad density on YouTube is channel-dependent and changes over time — some channels front-load several ads, others run none. Traditional ad blockers can get detected by YouTube's anti-adblock system ("Ad blockers are not allowed here"), and the skip button takes a few seconds to appear. You sit there, hostage.

YouTube 上的广告密度取决于频道且会随时间变化——有些频道会在开头放置多个广告，而有些则完全没有。传统的广告拦截器可能会被 YouTube 的反拦截系统检测到（提示“此处不允许使用广告拦截器”），而且跳过按钮需要几秒钟才会出现。你只能坐在那里，像个人质一样。

Reload the page. That's it. Two reloads and the ad is gone. Every time.

重新加载页面。就是这样。重新加载两次，广告就会消失。每次都有效。

🎯 YouTube Ad Punisher automates this: the moment an ad appears, it captures your exact playback timestamp, reloads the page, and YouTube auto-seeks back to where you were. No ads. No waiting. No skip button hunting.

🎯 YouTube Ad Punisher 将此过程自动化：当广告出现时，它会捕获您精确的播放时间戳，重新加载页面，然后 YouTube 会自动跳转回您之前的位置。没有广告。无需等待。无需寻找跳过按钮。

Auto escape: after 3 reloads, the extension automatically switches to a helper tab for 4 seconds (George's discovery, now automated), then switches back. No manual tab switching needed.

自动逃逸：重新加载 3 次后，扩展程序会自动切换到辅助标签页 4 秒（这是 George 的发现，现已自动化），然后切回原页面。无需手动切换标签页。

Ads keep coming after reload? Switch to another tab for a few seconds, then switch back. Chrome throttles background tabs, which lets the ad state settle — George discovered this works every time.

重新加载后广告还在？切换到另一个标签页几秒钟，然后再切回来。Chrome 会限制后台标签页，这能让广告状态稳定下来——George 发现这招每次都管用。

无痕模式：请参阅上方的“无痕模式”部分。

Reload (primary) — reloads the page 1-3 times. YouTube re-evaluates ad serving on each reload; after 1-2 cycles the ad slot is empty and the video plays clean. Timestamp is preserved via &t=Xs URL param + JS seek.

重新加载（主要方式）——重新加载页面 1-3 次。YouTube 会在每次重新加载时重新评估广告投放；经过 1-2 个周期后，广告位就会变空，视频即可正常播放。时间戳通过 &t=Xs URL 参数和 JS 跳转来保留。

yout-ube.com redirect (fallback) — if reloads don't clear the ad after 3 attempts, redirects to yout-ube.com (an ad-free YouTube mirror). The URL includes &loop=0 to prevent the infinite replay issue that the mirror has by default.

yout-ube.com 重定向（备用方式）——如果重新加载 3 次后仍无法清除广告，则重定向到 yout-ube.com（一个无广告的 YouTube 镜像站）。URL 中包含 &loop=0，以防止该镜像站默认存在的无限循环播放问题。

Detect — watches for .ad-showing class, skip buttons, and ad overlays (every 500ms + MutationObserver)

检测——监视 .ad-showing 类、跳过按钮和广告覆盖层（每 500 毫秒一次 + MutationObserver）。

Capture — grabs video.currentTime and saves it to sessionStorage

捕获——获取 video.currentTime 并将其保存到 sessionStorage 中。

Reload — location.replace() with &t=Xs in the URL

重新加载——使用 URL 中的 &t=Xs 执行 location.replace()。

Restore — YouTube auto-seeks to the timestamp; playback resumes

恢复——YouTube 自动跳转到该时间戳；恢复播放。

Cooldown — 3-second guard prevents reload loops

冷却——3 秒保护机制，防止重新加载循环。

YouTube's ad system serves ads as part of the page load. When you reload, YouTube re-evaluates whether to serve an ad based on your session state. After 1-2 reloads, the ad slot is typically empty and the video plays directly. This is a client-side, zero-cost workaround — no filter lists, no network blocking, no detection by YouTube's anti-adblock system.

YouTube 的广告系统将广告作为页面加载的一部分进行投放。当您重新加载时，YouTube 会根据您的会话状态重新评估是否投放广告。经过 1-2 次重新加载后，广告位通常会变空，视频直接播放。这是一种客户端、零成本的变通方法——无需过滤列表，无需网络拦截，也不会被 YouTube 的反拦截系统检测到。

If millions of users did this, each reload = a full page load = server request. YouTube would see a spike in page loads but a drop in ad impressions. It's not a DDoS (no amplification, no botnet), but it's a passive protest: every reload is a vote that says "I will not watch your ad." The ad revenue model breaks when reloads are cheaper than ad views.

如果有数百万用户这样做，每次重新加载 = 一次完整的页面加载 = 一次服务器请求。YouTube 会看到页面加载量激增，但广告展示量下降。这不是 DDoS（没有放大攻击，没有僵尸网络），而是一种消极抗议：每一次重新加载都是一张选票，表达着“我不会看你的广告”。当重新加载的成本低于广告观看的价值时，广告收入模式就会崩溃。
