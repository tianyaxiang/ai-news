Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.33176 (cs) [Submitted on 21 Oct 2026] Title: Psychophysics of Duration Perception in Multimodal Large Language Models Authors: Auss Abbood, Zihao Fu, Nigel Collier.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.33176 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 10 月 21 日] 标题：多模态大语言模型中持续时间感知的心理物理学 作者：Auss Abbood, Zihao Fu, Nigel Collier。

Abstract: Studies of time perception in language models focus mainly on text-based settings. Yet multimodal models are increasingly used for tasks that require perceiving the passage of time, making the limits of their temporal perception important to understand. We measure the limits of duration perception from audio against a human baseline to address this gap.

摘要：语言模型中时间感知的研究主要集中在基于文本的环境中。然而，多模态模型越来越多地被用于需要感知时间流逝的任务，因此了解其时间感知的局限性变得至关重要。为了弥补这一差距，我们通过人类基准来测量音频持续时间感知的极限。

Drawing from psychophysics, we operationalise duration perception as a melody recognition task, testing when a melody becomes unrecognisable as it is slowed down. Of 21 tested models, most cannot recognise well-known melodies without labelled in-context examples.

借鉴心理物理学，我们将持续时间感知操作化为旋律识别任务，测试旋律在减速到什么程度时变得无法识别。在 21 个受测模型中，大多数在没有标注上下文示例的情况下无法识别知名旋律。

With in-context examples, seven recognise melodies reliably, most peaking within the median human recognition band. Generalisation is limited: (i) recognition peaks shift with in-context example speed, (ii) melody titles induce prediction bias, and (iii) providing examples with scientific pitch notation does not prevent accuracy declining at long note durations for most models.

在提供上下文示例的情况下，有七个模型能够可靠地识别旋律，且大多数模型的表现峰值处于人类识别的中位数区间内。其泛化能力有限：(i) 识别峰值会随上下文示例的速度而偏移，(ii) 旋律标题会诱导预测偏差，以及 (iii) 对于大多数模型而言，提供带有科学音高记谱法的示例并不能阻止准确率在长音符持续时间下的下降。

In humans, the recognition window is a property of the perceiver; in current models, it is largely a property of the prompt.

在人类中，识别窗口是感知者的一种属性；而在当前模型中，它在很大程度上是提示词的一种属性。
