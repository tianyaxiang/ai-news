Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.33303 (cs) [Submitted on 24 Nov 2026] Title: Certify What You Plant: Shortcut-Free Proof Depth in Logical Reasoning Data Authors: Baptiste Juran, Dimitri Kachler, Damien Sileo.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.33303 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 24 日] 标题：《验证你的植入：逻辑推理数据中无捷径的证明深度》 作者：Baptiste Juran, Dimitri Kachler, Damien Sileo。

Abstract: Procedurally generated logic problems are used to both evaluate and train reasoning in language models. Their difficulty is usually set by planting a proof, that is, by sampling a proof of the desired depth and stating its premises as the problem. However, a planted proof is only one possible proof. A problem can also have shorter proofs, or several unrelated ones, and its stated depth then overstates what solving it requires.

摘要：程序生成的逻辑问题常被用于评估和训练语言模型的推理能力。其难度通常通过“植入证明”来设定，即采样一个所需深度的证明，并将该证明的前提作为问题陈述。然而，植入的证明只是众多可能证明中的一种。一个问题可能存在更短的证明，或多个互不相关的证明，因此其标称深度往往夸大了解决该问题所需的实际难度。

We measure what a problem actually requires, relative to a stated set of inference rules. For each problem, we compute the steps every proof must make, the shortest proof, the number of minimal proofs and the indispensable premises, and export certificates that an independent checker verifies.

我们根据一组既定的推理规则，衡量问题实际所需的要求。对于每个问题，我们计算所有证明必须包含的步骤、最短证明、最小证明的数量以及必要前提，并导出可由独立检查器验证的证书。

Audits of eight datasets find depth that is forced (ProofWriter, Blocksworld), problems labelled depth 5 that require about two specific rule applications (FLDx2), and answers that need no particular step (ZebraLogic). We then construct problems whose every proof has a requested structure, using a catalog of shortcuts proved once per rule set. Every constructed problem is checked in full and comes with its own certificate.

对八个数据集的审计发现，有些深度是强制性的（如 ProofWriter, Blocksworld），有些标记为深度 5 的问题实际上仅需约两次特定的规则应用（如 FLDx2），还有些答案无需任何特定步骤即可得出（如 ZebraLogic）。随后，我们利用每个规则集预先证明的捷径目录，构建了每个证明都具有特定结构的问题。每个构建的问题都经过全面检查，并附带其专属证书。

In training, the best data mixes depths and problem types. At equal size, such mixtures of certified problem types, including model-checking problems that forward generation does not produce, transfer better than FLDx2-style derivation data for Qwen3 models from 0.6B to 8B.

在训练中，最佳数据是深度和问题类型的混合。在规模相等的情况下，这种包含模型检查问题（前向生成无法产生的问题）的认证问题类型混合，对于 0.6B 到 8B 参数规模的 Qwen3 模型而言，其迁移效果优于 FLDx2 风格的推导数据。
