# Books, bikes, and The Burbs: These are Ars staff’s holiday gift picks for 2026

The holiday shopping season is upon us, and as is our custom, we’re here with a list of Ars staffers’ favorite recent purchases that could make good gifts. (Or maybe they might be a gift you get yourself!) Rather than try to write a definitive “best gifts for your sister or brother” type of guide, we simply polled Ars staffers on stuff they’ve actually bought this year that they recommend.

假日购物季已经到来，按照惯例，我们整理了一份 Ars 员工近期最喜爱的购物清单，这些商品或许会成为不错的礼物（或者干脆作为送给自己的礼物！）。我们并没有试图撰写一份权威的“送给兄弟姐妹的最佳礼物”指南，而是简单地询问了 Ars 的员工，让他们分享今年购买并推荐的物品。

The thinking is that if we’re into it, hopefully some of you or your friends or family might be. As such, there’s no rhyme or reason to these selections, really; it’s just a list of stuff we bought that we’re happy about. It’s heavy on interesting books and cycling accessories, but there are some gadgets and the like in here, too.

我们的想法是，如果我们喜欢这些东西，希望你们或你们的朋友家人也会喜欢。因此，这些精选内容并没有什么特定的逻辑或理由；这仅仅是一份我们购买后感到满意的物品清单。其中包含大量有趣的书籍和骑行配件，但也涵盖了一些小工具等产品。

For each gadget (or whatever else it might be), we have a brief description of how or why we’ve been using this particular thing and why we would recommend it. Our picks are ordered by price, from lowest to highest. Note, though, that the prices of these products are listed as they were when the list was written.

对于每一件小工具（或其他任何物品），我们都简要描述了我们使用它的方式或原因，以及推荐它的理由。我们的精选按价格从低到高排序。不过请注意，这些产品的价格是撰写本文时的价格。

Online retailers often adjust prices frequently, especially around Black Friday, so just a heads up: The price might be different by the time you read this. Also, a callout to the Ars commentariat: If you have your own holiday buying recommendations, feel free to share them. We or some fellow readers might take your advice if you suggest something good! Happy holidays!

在线零售商经常频繁调整价格，尤其是在黑色星期五前后，所以提醒一下：当你读到这篇文章时，价格可能已经变了。此外，向 Ars 的评论区读者们发出呼吁：如果你有自己的节日购物推荐，欢迎分享。如果你推荐的东西不错，我们或其他读者可能会采纳你的建议！节日快乐！

### Under $100

### 100 美元以下

**Dungeon Crawler Carl by Matt Dinniman – $13**
I understand the convenience and space-savings that e-readers bring. My 2011 Kindle Touch goes with me everywhere I carry my travel backpack, but I’m still a softy for physical books when I’m sitting at home on a lazy Sunday. What started as something of a fanfic send-up of LitRPGs has quickly blossomed into a massive sensation of a series filled with far more depth, heart, and character than the initial premise might indicate.

**《Dungeon Crawler Carl》，作者：Matt Dinniman – 13 美元**
我理解电子阅读器带来的便利和节省空间的优势。我那台 2011 年的 Kindle Touch 总是放在我的旅行背包里随身携带，但当我在慵懒的周日坐在家里时，我依然对纸质书情有独钟。这部作品最初像是对 LitRPG（文学角色扮演游戏）的一种同人戏仿，但很快就发展成了一部轰动性的系列作品，其深度、情感和角色塑造远超最初的设定。

**Undersea War: The Battle to Control the Cables and Pipelines That Connect Our World by Elisabeth Braw – $30**
**Lightning Beneath the Sea: The Race to Wire the World and the Dawn of the Information Age by James M. Tabor – $32**
Two excellent books that help describe how the Internet’s undersea backbone became a globe-spanning reality were published in 2026. Together, they form a great gift pairing for anyone interested in how humanity first achieved near-instantaneous communication across the world’s oceans—and how the first trans-Atlantic telegraph cable paved the way for a global network of subsea data cables that now faces growing risks from geopolitical conflicts.

**《Undersea War: The Battle to Control the Cables and Pipelines That Connect Our World》，作者：Elisabeth Braw – 30 美元**
**《Lightning Beneath the Sea: The Race to Wire the World and the Dawn of the Information Age》，作者：James M. Tabor – 32 美元**
2026 年出版了两本出色的书籍，有助于描述互联网的海底骨干网是如何成为全球性现实的。对于任何对人类如何首次实现跨洋近乎即时的通信，以及第一条跨大西洋电报电缆如何为如今面临地缘政治冲突风险的全球海底数据电缆网络铺平道路感兴趣的人来说，这两本书是绝佳的礼物组合。

**The Burbs Collector’s Edition 4K Blu-ray – $32**
It’s a little-remembered film for most people, but I absolutely love The Burbs. Sure, it’s more Halloween fare than Christmas, but last year I picked The Thing, so clearly “1980s movies for October” is my December viewing MO. Starring Tom Hanks, Carrie Fisher, and Corey Feldman, this is a dry, hilarious send-up of suburban life with a horror-adjacent mystery that keeps you guessing.

**《The Burbs》收藏版 4K 蓝光碟 – 32 美元**
对大多数人来说，这是一部被遗忘的电影，但我非常喜欢《The Burbs》。当然，它更像是万圣节的题材而非圣诞节，但去年我选了《怪形》（The Thing），所以很明显，“10 月看 80 年代电影”就是我 12 月的观影习惯。这部电影由汤姆·汉克斯、凯丽·费雪和科里·费尔德曼主演，是对郊区生活的一种冷幽默、滑稽的讽刺，其中夹杂着恐怖悬疑元素，让人猜不透结局。

**How Apollo Flew to the Moon by David Woods – $35**
If you’re a technically minded Ars reader looking for a nice book to snuggle down with over the holidays, David Woods’ How Apollo Flew to the Moon could be just the thing. (There’s also an audiobook version.) Woods has an accessible style that breaks down complex engineering challenges and their solutions into easily digestible chunks that clarify without oversimplifying.

**《How Apollo Flew to the Moon》，作者：David Woods – 35 美元**
如果你是一位具有技术头脑的 Ars 读者，正在寻找一本适合在假期里静心阅读的好书，David Woods 的《How Apollo Flew to the Moon》可能正合你意。（也有有声书版本。）Woods 的写作风格通俗易懂，他将复杂的工程挑战及其解决方案拆解成易于消化的片段，在不进行过度简化的情况下阐明了问题。
