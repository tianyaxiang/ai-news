# Hard Drive pricing has gotten bad

For years now, I’ve made it an annual tradition to replace one or two hard drives on Black Friday. I typically run around ten physical disks, and swapping a couple out each year means I almost never have a drive older than five years in my systems. I’ve even previously made videos about shucking disks around this time of year.

多年来，我一直保持着一个年度传统，那就是在“黑色星期五”更换一两块硬盘。我通常运行着大约十块物理磁盘，每年更换几块意味着我的系统中几乎没有超过五年的硬盘。我甚至以前还制作过关于在每年的这个时候拆解硬盘（shucking）的视频。

But current hardware pricing is totally screwed. So what is a lifelong self-hoster to do in times like these? With disk capacities increasing massively over the last 10 years I can now fit 20TB per slot. This means I could realistically go from ten spinners to five, and not really feel it too acutely. This will mean redoing my zpool layouts, which in turn means shuffling some data around as ZFS doesn’t like changes to the layout of existing pools. But, at these prices, I’m starting to think the hassle might be worth it.

但目前的硬件定价简直是一团糟。那么，像我这样终身的自托管用户在这样的时期该怎么办呢？随着过去 10 年磁盘容量的大幅增加，我现在每个插槽可以容纳 20TB 的容量。这意味着我实际上可以将十块机械硬盘减少到五块，而且不会有太明显的感受。这将意味着我需要重做 zpool 布局，进而意味着需要重新整理一些数据，因为 ZFS 不喜欢更改现有存储池的布局。但是，面对这样的价格，我开始觉得这些麻烦或许是值得的。

Let’s take a look at disk prices over the past 8 years in today’s post.

让我们在今天的文章中回顾一下过去 8 年的磁盘价格。

The 20TB WD EasyStore I bought for $249.99 in November 2024 is, today, on sale, $549.99 this Black Friday. $300 more than 2 years ago for the same product, from the same store. It shows just how bad hard drive pricing has gotten (BestBuy 20TB listing).

我在 2024 年 11 月以 249.99 美元购买的 20TB WD EasyStore，在今年的“黑色星期五”促销价为 549.99 美元。同一家商店的同一款产品，比两年前贵了 300 美元。这充分说明了硬盘定价已经糟糕到了什么程度（BestBuy 20TB 商品列表）。

Shucking is homelab speak for taking a perfectly good external drive out of its USB enclosure and sticking the SATA drive in your server. At $12.50/TB it was a fantastically affordable way to get massive amounts of storage. At $27.50/TB, absolutely not.

“拆解”（Shucking）是家庭实验室圈子的术语，指将完好的外置硬盘从 USB 外壳中取出，并将 SATA 硬盘安装到服务器中。在 12.50 美元/TB 的价格下，这是一种获取海量存储空间的极其经济实惠的方式。但在 27.50 美元/TB 的价格下，绝对不行。

In November 2018 I bought five 10TB EasyStores for $179.99 each. At the time I wrote about doing the rounds of my local Best Buys to pickup more drives on Black Friday as quantities were limited at each location (My 2018 server build log).

2018 年 11 月，我以每块 179.99 美元的价格购买了五块 10TB 的 EasyStore。当时我写过关于在“黑色星期五”跑遍当地 Best Buy 门店去抢购更多硬盘的经历，因为每个地点的库存都很有限（我的 2018 年服务器构建日志）。

October 2023 was my best haul on a per-TB basis. Three 18TB drives put 54TB in my hands for $599.97. This year’s “deal” is fifty bucks shy of that entire order. Best Buy was still advertising those 18TB drives for $199.99 in November 2023. November 2023 deal archive

2023 年 10 月是我按每 TB 计算收获最好的一次。三块 18TB 的硬盘以 599.97 美元的价格让我获得了 54TB 的容量。今年的“优惠”比那整个订单只便宜了五十美元。Best Buy 在 2023 年 11 月时还在以 199.99 美元的价格促销那些 18TB 的硬盘。（2023 年 11 月优惠存档）

Buying refurbished or recertified enterprise drives used to be an easy way to bring the cost down. In August 2024 I bought four recertified 14TB Ultrastars from ServerPartDeals for $529.96 before tax. That was 56TB at $9.46/TB, even cheaper than the EasyStores and no shucking to do.

购买翻新或重新认证的企业级硬盘曾经是降低成本的简单方法。2024 年 8 月，我从 ServerPartDeals 购买了四块重新认证的 14TB Ultrastar 硬盘，税前价格为 529.96 美元。那是 56TB 的容量，单价为 9.46 美元/TB，甚至比 EasyStore 更便宜，而且不需要拆解。

The sad truth is that across the board prices are up a minimum of 58%, and sometimes as much as 218% in just a year or two.

令人悲哀的事实是，在短短一两年内，全线产品的价格至少上涨了 58%，有时甚至高达 218%。

When the EasyStore is that bad, my first instinct is to shop around. Maybe I should try another retailer (to be clear I’m not blaming BestBuy for any of this), buy an enterprise drive, see what’s available recertified. But follow those listings back to the companies that actually make the drives and the choices narrow pretty quickly. There are three big HDD makers left. How did we end up with so few?

当 EasyStore 的价格如此糟糕时，我的第一反应是货比三家。也许我应该尝试其他零售商（声明一下，我并没有责怪 BestBuy），购买企业级硬盘，看看有哪些重新认证的产品。但如果追溯这些产品背后的实际制造商，选择范围很快就会缩小。目前只剩下三家大型硬盘制造商了。我们怎么会沦落到只剩下这么几家呢？

Maxtor bought Quantum’s hard drive business in 2001, then Seagate bought Maxtor in 2006. Hitachi combined IBM’s disk business with its own to form HGST in 2003, and Toshiba bought Fujitsu’s HDD business in 2009. Seagate bought Samsung’s HDD business in December 2011, and Western Digital bought HGST in March 2012. The FTC required WD to sell desktop-drive manufacturing assets to Toshiba to keep a third supplier in that market. That left Seagate, Western Digital and Toshiba.

迈拓（Maxtor）在 2001 年收购了昆腾（Quantum）的硬盘业务，随后希捷（Seagate）在 2006 年收购了迈拓。日立（Hitachi）在 2003 年将 IBM 的磁盘业务与自身合并成立了 HGST，东芝（Toshiba）在 2009 年收购了富士通（Fujitsu）的硬盘业务。希捷在 2011 年 12 月收购了三星的硬盘业务，西部数据（Western Digital）在 2012 年 3 月收购了 HGST。联邦贸易委员会（FTC）要求西数将桌面硬盘制造资产出售给东芝，以保持市场上存在第三家供应商。这使得市场上只剩下希捷、西部数据和东芝。

The 2011 Thailand floods hit while those last two deals were underway. WD halted production at all its Thai plants, and its average hard drive selling price went from $45 in fiscal 2011 to $62 in fiscal 2012. There was also a price-fixing case further up the supply chain. NHK Spring pleaded guilty in 2019 to fixing prices for the suspension assemblies that hold the read/write heads, and the court approved a $28.5 million fine. WD’s 2012 annual report · Ninth Circuit opinion

2011 年泰国洪水发生时，最后两笔交易正在进行中。西数停止了其所有泰国工厂的生产，其硬盘平均售价从 2011 财年的 45 美元涨至 2012 财年的 62 美元。供应链上游还存在一起价格操纵案件。NHK Spring 在 2019 年承认操纵了用于固定读写磁头的悬挂组件的价格，法院批准了 2850 万美元的罚款。（西数 2012 年年度报告 · 第九巡回法院意见书）

It’s hard to have anything positive to say in this post. The HDD manufacturing market is so consolidated nowadays - just like the memory market which is equally screwed - that it’s hard to see this as anything but mass market collusion by the HDD manufacturing cartel.

在这篇文章中，很难说出任何积极的话。如今的硬盘制造市场高度集中——就像同样糟糕的内存市场一样——除了将其视为硬盘制造卡特尔的大规模市场共谋之外，很难有其他解释。

Three companies is an awfully short list when you need to buy a hard drive. And with 89% of WD’s revenue coming from its cloud business in fiscal 2026, I don’t reckon those of us buying a couple of drives for a home server have much impact here. Just like with Nvidia and Micron, consumer markets just aren’t valuable enough so we have to make do with the crumbs instead.

当你需要购买硬盘时，三家公司这个名单实在太短了。而且，随着 2026 财年西数 89% 的收入来自其云业务，我认为我们这些为家庭服务器购买几块硬盘的用户在这里并没有什么影响力。就像英伟达（Nvidia）和美光（Micron）的情况一样，消费市场已经不再那么有价值，所以我们只能凑合着吃点残羹冷炙。

As of November 26, 2026, the 20TB is 2.2× what I paid for the exact same SKU in 2024. It’s also $270 more than I paid for it in May 2025. And if you’re thinking you’ll just buy a smaller one, Best Buy’s 8TB Black Friday special is $299.99. That’s $37.50/TB and fifty bucks more than my 20TB cost two years ago. Best Buy’s EasyStore listings

截至 2026 年 11 月 26 日，这款 20TB 硬盘的价格是我 2024 年购买同款产品时价格的 2.2 倍。它也比我 2025 年 5 月购买时贵了 270 美元。如果你想买个小容量的，Best Buy 的 8TB“黑色星期五”特价是 299.99 美元。这相当于 37.50 美元/TB，比我两年前买的 20TB 硬盘还要贵五十美元。（Best Buy 的 EasyStore 商品列表）

The jump gets sillier when you price a whole NAS. Four of those 20TB drives would have been $999.96 at my 2024 price. This week they’d be $2,199.96. That’s another $1,200 before you’ve bought a case, a board or a single stick of RAM. And the price per usable terabyte only gets worse once you set aside a drive for parity.

当你计算整台 NAS 的价格时，这种涨幅就更荒谬了。按照我 2024 年的价格，四块 20TB 硬盘总共只需 999.96 美元。而本周它们的价格是 2,199.96 美元。在还没买机箱、主板或单根内存条之前，就已经多花了 1,200 美元。而且一旦你预留一块硬盘用于奇偶校验，每可用 TB 的价格只会变得更糟。

If I can wish for one thing in 2027, it would be for hardware pricing to return to reasonable levels. I fear it may take a lot longer than that, though. So making do with less is going to be the new normal for a while. And in that creative constraint, maybe we’ll have some fun along the way instead.

如果我能对 2027 年许下一个愿望，那就是希望硬件定价能回到合理的水平。但我担心这可能需要比那更长的时间。因此，在一段时间内，凑合着用更少的资源将成为新常态。而在这种创造性的限制中，也许我们反而能从中找到一些乐趣。

Amp modellers are dead. I don't say that lightly or to be hyperbolic. It's just true. NAM-A2 is here and it is awesome.

吉他音箱模拟器（Amp modellers）已经过时了。我不是随口说说，也不是在夸大其词。这是事实。NAM-A2 已经发布，而且它非常棒。

Plane radar is a neat little project that pulls nearby ADS-B traffic, plots each aircraft by distance and bearing, and shows the details directly on a cute little screen.

“飞机雷达”（Plane radar）是一个很酷的小项目，它能获取附近的 ADS-B 交通信息，按距离和方位绘制每架飞机的位置，并直接在一个可爱的小屏幕上显示详细信息。
