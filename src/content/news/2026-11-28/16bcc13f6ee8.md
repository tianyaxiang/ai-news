Abstract: A language model can name a brand without classifying it correctly. This study measures these outcomes separately for 1,000 B2B SaaS brands across 333 product categories. On Gemma 4 12B, with no external retrieval, category prompts elicit 510 brands (51.0%). A separate five-candidate category probe ranks the reference label first for 787 brands, a distractor first for 194, and yields little separation for 19. Among the 981 separated rankings, the error rate is 19.8%. It falls to 3.5% for the 547 brands with a winner-to-runner-up log-probability margin above 0.6. These are within-cohort measurements, not calibrated confidence estimates.

摘要：语言模型可以在无法正确分类的情况下说出品牌名称。本研究针对 333 个产品类别中的 1,000 个 B2B SaaS 品牌分别衡量了这些结果。在没有外部检索的情况下，Gemma 4 12B 模型通过类别提示引出了 510 个品牌（51.0%）。一项独立的五候选类别探测显示，787 个品牌的参考标签排名第一，194 个品牌的干扰项排名第一，19 个品牌几乎没有区分度。在 981 个有区分的排名中，错误率为 19.8%。对于 547 个胜出者与亚军对数概率差值大于 0.6 的品牌，该错误率降至 3.5%。这些是队列内的测量值，而非校准后的置信度估计。

Exploratory regressions associate technical coverage with recall and an owned-properties composite with category accuracy, but the composites use different adjustment sets and do not establish causal effects. Across four Gemma generations, measured recall is 39.8%, 44.3%, 51.1% and 51.0%. A separate representation-based sentiment measure has little linear association with customer ratings (r=0.03, n=921). The findings support reporting recall and category accuracy separately. Failure to elicit a brand does not establish its absence from model parameters or training data.

探索性回归将技术覆盖率与召回率联系起来，并将自有属性综合指标与类别准确性联系起来，但这些综合指标使用了不同的调整集，并未建立因果关系。在四代 Gemma 模型中，测得的召回率分别为 39.8%、44.3%、51.1% 和 51.0%。一项基于表征的情感测量指标与客户评分之间的线性关联度很低（r=0.03, n=921）。研究结果支持将召回率和类别准确性分开报告。未能引出某个品牌并不代表其不存在于模型参数或训练数据中。
