How cutting-edge prosthetics can help probe the human mind. There are a lot of remarkable things about Avi Davidson. A horrific injury as a teenager left him paralyzed and without a left hand. Today, his experience informs his work as a trauma therapist. He's also taking part in a clinical trial of new tech that allows him to move—and feel with—a prosthetic hand. Before the trial started, researchers scanned his brain to see how his missing left hand was represented in his brain. Will that change? And what might that mean for people who are born without limbs? Here’s what a bionic hand might reveal about the mind. —Jessica Hamzelou

尖端假肢如何帮助探索人类思维。Avi Davidson 有许多非凡之处。青少年时期的一场可怕事故导致他瘫痪并失去了左手。如今，他的这段经历为他作为创伤治疗师的工作提供了参考。他目前正在参与一项新技术临床试验，该技术使他能够移动假肢手并获得触觉。在试验开始前，研究人员扫描了他的大脑，以观察他缺失的左手在大脑中是如何呈现的。这种情况会改变吗？这对天生残疾的人意味着什么？仿生手或许能揭示关于思维的奥秘。——Jessica Hamzelou
