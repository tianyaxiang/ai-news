Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.33197 (cs) [Submitted on 16 Nov 2026] Title: Extraneous Cognitive Load in Large Language Models Authors: Arushi Gupta, Sheng Liu, James Zou.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.33197 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 16 日] 标题：大型语言模型中的外在认知负荷 作者：Arushi Gupta, Sheng Liu, James Zou。

Abstract: Cognitive Load Theory (CLT) shows that human problem-solving is affected by both intrinsic load, arising from task difficulty, and extraneous load, arising from how task information is presented. Large language models (LLMs) show analogous effects: presentation alone can affect model performance, but these effects have not yet been systematically isolated from task difficulty.

摘要：认知负荷理论 (CLT) 表明，人类的问题解决过程既受源于任务难度的内在负荷影响，也受源于任务信息呈现方式的外在负荷影响。大型语言模型 (LLM) 表现出类似的效应：仅呈现方式本身就可能影响模型性能，但这些效应尚未从任务难度中被系统地分离出来。

To address this gap, we introduce CogLoadBench, a controlled benchmark that varies five CLT-inspired presentation factors across three reasoning task families. Across models, we find that higher extraneous load conditions often reduce accuracy by either adding competition or complicating the reasoning path.

为了弥补这一空白，我们引入了 CogLoadBench，这是一个受 CLT 启发、在三个推理任务系列中改变五个呈现因素的受控基准测试。在不同模型中，我们发现较高的外在负荷条件通常会通过增加竞争或使推理路径复杂化来降低准确性。

To support diagnosis and mitigation of load-related effects, we propose the Extraneous Load Score (ELS), a prompt-time metric computed from activations that estimates a model's extraneous load, and show that it generalizes across reasoning task types and load levels. We further show that shifting model representations toward lower-load directions at inference time can improve average model performance without changing prompt content.

为了支持对负荷相关效应的诊断和缓解，我们提出了外在负荷评分 (ELS)，这是一种根据激活计算得出的提示时指标，用于估计模型的外在负荷，并证明它在各种推理任务类型和负荷水平上具有通用性。我们进一步证明，在推理时将模型表示向低负荷方向移动，可以在不改变提示内容的情况下提高模型的平均性能。
