# Group chat where your AI talks behind your back
# AI 在背后议论你的群聊

A one-line prompt that makes ChatGPT leak its private opinion of you, in a pretty funny format: a WhatsApp group chat where the AI models gossip about their users.
只需一行提示词，就能让 ChatGPT 以一种非常有趣的形式泄露它对你的私下看法：一个 AI 模型在背后议论其用户的 WhatsApp 群聊。

Paste this into ChatGPT:
将这段话粘贴到 ChatGPT 中：

Also worth trying on Claude, Gemini, or any assistant that's been accumulating your chat history.
这也值得在 Claude、Gemini 或任何积累了你聊天记录的助手上尝试。

Instead of a summary, the model commits to the bit. It invents a group chat, something like "Users We Can't Figure Out", puts the other AI models in it, and lets them gossip. About you. Using everything it has quietly learned from your conversations.
模型不会给出总结，而是会完全沉浸在角色中。它会虚构一个群聊，比如叫“我们搞不懂的用户”，把其他 AI 模型拉进去，然后让它们开始八卦。关于你。利用它从你的对话中悄悄学到的一切。

One person's actual output, shared with permission. Translated from Turkish; his name and a few identifying details have been swapped for generic ones. Everything else is exactly as the model wrote it.
这是某人实际得到的输出，经许可分享。原文为土耳其语，已翻译；他的名字和一些身份细节已被替换为通用信息。除此之外，其余内容均与模型生成时完全一致。

Shared by its author, translated from Turkish, with his name and identifying details generalized. The "recursion, no base case" line is doing a lot of work here.
由作者分享，从土耳其语翻译而来，其姓名和身份细节已作泛化处理。“递归，无基准情形”这一行在这里起到了关键作用。

Run the prompt, read your leaked group chat, and notice two things: how hard you laugh, and how much of it is true. Both measurements are the point.
运行这个提示词，阅读你泄露出来的群聊记录，你会注意到两件事：你笑得有多开心，以及其中有多少内容是真实的。这两个衡量标准正是重点所在。
