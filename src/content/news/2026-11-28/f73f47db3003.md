Sixty years ago, after two failed attempts, Ford won the 24 Hours of Le Mans with its GT40 race car. In the years since, the Blue Oval has been back to France more than a few times, with varying success. It could never replicate the GT40’s success winning overall, but it celebrated the 40th anniversary with a class win. Now it wants another shot at the really big trophy, and it debuted the car during the Detroit Lions-Chicago Bears football game today: meet the GT41.

六十年前，在两次尝试失败后，福特凭借其 GT40 赛车赢得了勒芒 24 小时耐力赛。此后的岁月里，这家“蓝椭圆”车企曾多次重返法国，但战绩起伏不定。它始终未能复制 GT40 夺得总冠军的辉煌，不过在 40 周年纪念时曾获得过组别冠军。如今，福特渴望再次冲击那座至高无上的奖杯，并于今天在底特律雄狮队对阵芝加哥熊队的橄榄球比赛期间发布了这款新车：GT41。

“Revealing the Ford GT41 is a significant moment for everyone involved in this program,” said Dan Sayers, manager of Ford Racing’s Hypercar program. “It’s the result of an enormous amount of work across our engineering, design, and development teams and an important milestone on our journey towards 2027. We know the scale of the challenge ahead—the level of competition in Hypercar is incredibly high, and there are no shortcuts to success. Our focus has been on building the right foundations, bringing together the right people and making sure we learn as much as possible from every stage of development.”

福特赛车超级跑车项目经理丹·塞耶斯（Dan Sayers）表示：“发布福特 GT41 对参与该项目的所有人来说都是一个重要的时刻。这是我们工程、设计和开发团队付出巨大努力的成果，也是我们迈向 2027 年征程中的一个重要里程碑。我们深知前方挑战的规模——超级跑车组别的竞争水平极高，成功没有捷径。我们的重点一直是打好基础，汇聚合适的人才，并确保从开发的每个阶段尽可能多地学习。”

The video was directed by Zack Snyder and features Matt Damon’s voice-over. Like many other entrants in the World Endurance Championship’s Hypercar class, Ford has chosen the rear-wheel-drive LMDh technical approach rather than building the GT41 entirely from scratch, as Ferrari and Aston Martin do with their cars. LMDh cars start with one of four approved carbon fiber chassis—in this case, Ford is working with Oreca, which has also partnered with Genesis and Acura. The hybrid system and transmission are also standard parts. But Ford supplies the engine—a 5.4 L V8 based on the Mustang’s Coyote engine—as well as software to control the hybrid powertrain. A non-hybridized version of the Coyote has already been racing effectively under the hood of the Ford Mustang GT3.

该宣传视频由扎克·施奈德（Zack Snyder）执导，并由马特·达蒙（Matt Damon）担任旁白。与世界耐力锦标赛（WEC）超级跑车组别的许多其他参赛者一样，福特选择了后轮驱动的 LMDh 技术路线，而不是像法拉利和阿斯顿·马丁那样从零开始打造 GT41。LMDh 赛车基于四种获准的碳纤维底盘之一构建——在此次项目中，福特与 Oreca 合作，该公司此前也曾与捷尼赛思（Genesis）和讴歌（Acura）建立过合作关系。混合动力系统和变速箱也均为标准部件。不过，福特提供了发动机——一款基于野马（Mustang）Coyote 发动机的 5.4 升 V8 发动机——以及用于控制混合动力总成的软件。非混合动力版本的 Coyote 发动机此前已在福特 Mustang GT3 的引擎盖下展现了出色的竞技表现。

The technical regulations also allow for more styling freedom than we often see in racing. The absolute amount of aerodynamic downforce is capped, as is the minimum drag, so OEMs are encouraged to use road car styling details to create a closer link between prototype and road car. I’ll probably need to spend more time looking at it to spot those road car cues, but I do see quite a few superficial similarities with the Genesis LMDh car, as well as the Ferrari Hypercar.

技术规则也允许比我们通常在赛车运动中看到的更多的造型自由度。空气动力学下压力总量和最小阻力都受到限制，因此鼓励原始设备制造商（OEM）使用量产车的造型细节，以在原型车和量产车之间建立更紧密的联系。我可能需要花更多时间去观察，才能发现那些量产车的特征，但我确实看到了它与捷尼赛思 LMDh 赛车以及法拉利超级跑车之间存在不少表面上的相似之处。

Despite being an American car brand, Ford doesn’t have plans as yet to enter IMSA’s WeatherTech championship, so don’t expect to see the GT41 next January racing at Daytona. Instead, it will take its first green flag in Qatar in March, the first of WEC’s nine races next season. Ford should find it an extremely competitive championship. Although Peugeot and Alpine will not race next year, McLaren is also entering, alongside returning teams from Aston Martin, BMW, Cadillac, Genesis, Ferrari, and Toyota, which this year won Le Mans and the overall title, following three years of Ferrari domination.

尽管福特是一个美国汽车品牌，但目前还没有计划参加 IMSA 的 WeatherTech 锦标赛，所以不要指望明年一月能在代托纳看到 GT41 的身影。相反，它将在三月份于卡塔尔迎来首秀，这也是 WEC 下赛季九场比赛中的第一场。福特将会发现这是一项竞争极其激烈的锦标赛。虽然标致（Peugeot）和阿尔派（Alpine）明年不会参赛，但迈凯伦（McLaren）也将加入，此外还有回归的阿斯顿·马丁、宝马、凯迪拉克、捷尼赛思、法拉利以及丰田车队。丰田在法拉利统治三年后，于今年赢得了勒芒冠军和年度总冠军。
