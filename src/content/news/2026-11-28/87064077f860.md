A lot of “free converters” are upload forms with a size cap, a watermark, and a server you have to trust with unreleased icons. When the job is a folder of UI assets, I want the opposite: batch in the tab, files stay on the device, download a ZIP. SVG Buddy is a client-side conversion suite for that workflow.

许多所谓的“免费转换器”其实就是带有大小限制、水印的上传表单，你还得把未发布的图标交给一个必须信任的服务器。当任务是一整个文件夹的 UI 资源时，我想要的是完全相反的体验：在浏览器标签页中批量处理，文件留在本地设备上，最后下载一个 ZIP 压缩包。SVG Buddy 就是为这种工作流程设计的客户端转换套件。

Official FAQ language is direct: conversions run in the browser with Canvas and JavaScript; image bytes stay on your device unless you download the results. The universal converter batch-processes between SVG, PNG, JPG, and WEBP, including raster-to-SVG vectorization and SVG-to-raster export. Dedicated tools cover PNG-to-SVG tracing with palette control, SVG-to-PNG rasterization at 1×–4× with optional transparency, JPG-to-SVG color quantization for flat artwork, WEBP-to-PNG decoding with alpha, and an SVG optimizer that strips metadata and tightens coordinates. You can drop files, pick them, or paste with Ctrl+V. Finished batches download together as one ZIP.

A batch I actually trust Put only one kind of job in the drop zone (all icons, or all blog diagrams — not both). Convert a single sample and open it in a real viewer at 100% and at the target size. If the sample is good, batch the rest and Download All as ZIP. Spot-check two more files from the ZIP, especially anything with thin strokes or pale colors on white. For traces, inspect node count. Limits worth stating Client-side Canvas conversion is not Adobe Illustrator’s Pathfinder, and it is not a print RIP. Complex filters, unusual color spaces, and tiny hairlines may need a professional editor after the batch. The ZIP is a convenience, not a proof of visual quality.

值得说明的局限性：客户端 Canvas 转换不是 Adobe Illustrator 的路径查找器（Pathfinder），也不是打印 RIP。复杂的滤镜、不常见的色彩空间以及极细的线条在批量处理后可能仍需要专业编辑器进行调整。ZIP 压缩包只是一种便利，而非视觉质量的保证。
