COPENHAGEN, Denmark—If we lived in the world that Volvo’s product planners expected back when the current XC90 debuted in 2015, both it and the smaller but related XC60 (which arrived in 2017) might have been the last of their kind as everything went electric. As we know, the future hasn’t turned out quite like that, especially in the critically important US market.

丹麦哥本哈根——如果我们生活在沃尔沃产品规划者在2015年现款XC90首次亮相时所预期的那个世界里，那么它和体型较小但与之相关的XC60（2017年推出）可能就是同类车型中的最后一代了，因为当时一切都在向电动化转型。然而众所周知，未来并没有完全按照那个方向发展，尤其是在至关重要的美国市场。

At the same time, Europe and China are electrifying, which seems like an argument against spending resources on developing all-new replacements for models that might not have an eight-year lifespan in most markets. So instead, Volvo has given the XC60 and XC90 another lifecycle refresh. The exterior styling tweaks are minimal; the bigger three-row XC90 had already been given a new look last year, and the XC60 now gains a similar grille and the same matrix headlights, for instance.

与此同时，欧洲和中国正在推进电动化，这似乎成为了反对投入资源开发全新替代车型的理由，因为这些车型在大多数市场可能无法维持八年的生命周期。因此，沃尔沃转而为XC60和XC90进行了又一次生命周期更新。外观造型的改动很小；更大的三排座XC90去年已经换上了新面貌，而现在的XC60也获得了类似的格栅和相同的矩阵式大灯。

And that’s no bad thing—both were, and remain, handsome shapes that are popular with Volvo’s customer base. Instead, the Swedish automaker’s attention has been on bringing the technology up to date for model year 2028 standards. Which means a much bigger battery for the PHEVs, with sufficient capacity for a couple of days of daily driving for most of us before needing to be recharged. There’s also new, modern infotainment and interiors that are now even nicer to spend time in than before.

这并非坏事——这两款车过去和现在都拥有深受沃尔沃客户群喜爱的俊朗外形。这家瑞典汽车制造商的注意力已转向将技术升级至2028款车型的标准。这意味着插电式混合动力车型（PHEV）配备了更大的电池，其容量足以满足我们大多数人两天的日常驾驶需求，无需频繁充电。此外，新车还配备了现代化的信息娱乐系统，内饰也比以往更加舒适宜人。

We first saw the revamped 2028 XC60 (and learned about its technical details and those of the XC90) in September, but with the cars going into production now and due to reach the first US customers in the first quarter of next year, we got the opportunity to spend some seat time with the updated cars. Charge! Both the midsize XC60 and three-row XC90 use the same PHEV powertrain.

我们于九月份首次见到了改款的2028款XC60（并了解了它和XC90的技术细节），但随着这些车辆现已投入生产，并预计于明年第一季度交付给首批美国客户，我们获得了亲身体验这些升级车型的机会。充电吧！中型XC60和三排座XC90均采用了相同的PHEV动力总成。

Under the hood, there’s a 2.0 L four-cylinder direct-injection gasoline engine that generates 247 hp (184 kW) and 226 lb-ft (306 Nm) together with a 70 hp (50 kW), 122 ft-lb (165 Nm) electric motor, both of which drive the front wheels. At the rear axle there’s a second electric motor, with an output of 174 hp (130 kW) and 228 lb-ft (309 Nm). Combined output for the hybrid system is 455 hp (339 kW) and 560 lb-ft (759 Nm).

引擎盖下是一台2.0升四缸直喷汽油发动机，可产生247马力（184千瓦）和226磅-英尺（306牛·米）的扭矩，并配有一台70马力（50千瓦）、122磅-英尺（165牛·米）的电动机，两者共同驱动前轮。后轴上还有第二台电动机，输出功率为174马力（130千瓦），扭矩为228磅-英尺（309牛·米）。混合动力系统的综合输出功率为455马力（339千瓦），扭矩为560磅-英尺（759牛·米）。

There’s also a much bigger battery, with a net capacity of 37.9 kWh (41.2 kWh gross). If you set the car’s mode to Pure, which relies solely on the rear electric motor for propulsion, that translates to an EPA range estimate of 78 miles (126 km) for the XC60 and 73 miles (116 km) for the larger, draggier XC90. Which is significant—when the average driver drives fewer than 30 miles a day, it means these PHEVs carry enough electrons for a couple of days of tailpipe-emissions-free driving before it needs to be plugged in again or the engine to fire up.

此外，新车还配备了更大的电池，净容量为37.9千瓦时（总容量41.2千瓦时）。如果将车辆模式设置为“纯电”（Pure），仅依靠后置电动机驱动，XC60的EPA预估续航里程为78英里（126公里），而体型更大、风阻更高的XC90则为73英里（116公里）。这意义重大——考虑到普通驾驶员每天的行驶里程不到30英里，这意味着这些PHEV车型携带的电量足以支撑两天的零尾气排放驾驶，之后才需要再次充电或启动发动机。

When it is time to plug it back in, expect it to take about four hours with a level 2 charger of at least 16 A, or 27 hours if you’re limited to 120 V. In Hybrid mode, where you get the benefit of both electric motors and the gas engine, Volvo expects a combined efficiency of 67 mpge in the XC60, and 61 mpge for the XC90.

当需要充电时，使用至少16安培的二级充电器大约需要四个小时，如果仅限于120伏电压，则需要27个小时。在混合动力模式下，您可以同时利用电动机和汽油发动机的优势，沃尔沃预计XC60的综合能效为67 mpge，XC90为61 mpge。

Even if the gas engine is running and you’re in Hybrid or Power mode (which keeps the engine on all the time), you can drive these Volvos as if they were EVs with one-pedal driving: just move the transmission from D to B. The Volvos are always using their electric motors to slow the car before the friction brakes intervene, and in B you get the same amount of lift-off regen regardless of mode. For city traffic, it’s very effective.

即使在汽油发动机运行且处于混合动力或动力模式（发动机始终开启）时，您也可以像驾驶电动汽车一样使用单踏板模式驾驶这些沃尔沃车型：只需将变速箱从D挡切换到B挡即可。沃尔沃车型总是先利用电动机减速，然后再介入摩擦制动，而在B挡下，无论处于何种模式，您都能获得相同的松油门能量回收效果。对于城市交通来说，这非常有效。

In power mode, with all 455 hp unleashed, you can chirp the wheels on a wet day, but these model year 2028 cars are about one large male human heavier than the outgoing model year, both of which were already pretty heavy. So don’t expect performance-car performance. The XC60 is the sweeter to drive on a winding road, but both are entirely at home cruising on a highway or creeping along in a city, which is where cars like these spend the vast majority of their time.

在动力模式下，当455马力全部释放时，在潮湿天气下可能会出现轮胎打滑，但这些2028款车型比上一代车型重了一个成年男性的重量，而上一代车型本身就已经很重了。因此，不要指望它们能达到性能车的表现。XC60在蜿蜒道路上驾驶感更佳，但两款车在高速公路巡航或城市缓慢行驶时都表现得游刃有余，而这正是此类车型绝大部分时间的使用场景。

A first-drive event like this isn’t the best place to get a good read on real-world efficiency. We usually have to share cars with other journalists, and there’s limited time to drive on roads that may not be representative—too sinuous, or not enough traffic, for example. We need to try all the different drive modes and see how cars respond to different inputs. And it’s rare you’ll spend the entire day with one car, finishing at the same point you started.

像这样的首次试驾活动并不是评估真实世界能效的最佳场合。我们通常需要与其他记者共用车辆，且在可能不具代表性的道路上（例如过于蜿蜒或车流量不足）驾驶的时间有限。我们需要尝试所有不同的驾驶模式，观察车辆对不同输入的响应。而且，你很少能整天只开一辆车，并回到出发点结束行程。

That said, I can’t give you a definite read on range and efficiency for these two plug-ins. But my unscientific estimate is that my drive partner and I, in an XC60, drove about 60 km in a mix of city driving and higher speeds on the way to Malmo in Sweden, which is on the other side of a gigantic bridge, and we used about half the battery pack to do so.

话虽如此，我无法为您提供这两款插电式混合动力车在续航和能效方面的确切数据。但根据我的非科学估算，我和我的试驾伙伴驾驶一辆XC60，在前往瑞典马尔默（位于一座巨大桥梁的另一端）的途中，结合了城市驾驶和高速行驶，总共行驶了约60公里，期间消耗了大约一半的电池电量。
