Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.33173 (cs) [Submitted on 21 Oct 2026] Title: The Bundesrat corpus: A dataset of political discourse in the German Federal Council Authors: Adama Njie, Leandro Laperne, Laksh Agarwal, Ali Torkayesh, Sandra Venghaus.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.33173 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 10 月 21 日] 标题：联邦参议院语料库：德国联邦参议院政治话语数据集 作者：Adama Njie, Leandro Laperne, Laksh Agarwal, Ali Torkayesh, Sandra Venghaus。

Abstract: The Bundesrat is Germany's second legislative chamber and a constitutional veto player within its political system. The members of the Bundesrat are delegates of state governments. Tracing the political discourse in the Bundesrat is challenging as it has a limited machine-readable corpus available.

摘要：德国联邦参议院是德国的第二个立法机构，也是其政治体系中具有宪法否决权的参与者。联邦参议院的成员由各州政府代表组成。追踪联邦参议院的政治话语具有挑战性，因为目前可用的机器可读语料库非常有限。

To address this issue, we present a speaker-attributed corpus of 1,067 Bundesrat plenary sessions from the chamber's first session on 7 September 1949 to 10 July 2026. In this period, a total of 53,362 speeches by 2,473 individuals were delivered in the chamber, including written statements.

为了解决这一问题，我们提出了一个包含 1,067 次联邦参议院全体会议的发言人归属语料库，涵盖了从 1949 年 9 月 7 日该机构首次会议到 2026 年 7 月 10 日期间的内容。在此期间，共有 2,473 人在议会发表了 53,362 次演讲，其中包括书面声明。

Each speech includes a speech ID, the full German text, the speaker's name, the date and location of the session, the federal state that the speaker represents, the speaker's role (if applicable), the type of contribution, and the speaker's gender.

每篇演讲都包含演讲 ID、完整的德语文本、发言人姓名、会议日期和地点、发言人所代表的联邦州、发言人的角色（如适用）、贡献类型以及发言人的性别。

The dataset provides interdisciplinary research opportunities across computational political science, legislative studies, and parliamentary discourse analysis, coupled with discourse from the Bundestag.

该数据集为计算政治学、立法研究和议会话语分析等跨学科研究提供了机会，并可与德国联邦议院的话语相结合。
