The new iPad Mini is getting its first major discount for Black Friday. Apple’s most portable tablet offers a larger OLED screen and water resistance.

新款 iPad Mini 迎来了“黑色星期五”的首个重大折扣。苹果这款最便携的平板电脑配备了更大的 OLED 屏幕，并具备防水功能。

We were big fans of Apple’s new iPad Mini when we reviewed it in early November, but its steep starting price of $699 was hard to swallow. Thankfully, the small tablet is already getting a sizable discount for Black Friday. Right now, Amazon and Walmart are both selling the 2026 iPad Mini with Wi-Fi and 128GB of storage starting at $599 ($100 off), its lowest price to date.

我们在 11 月初评测苹果新款 iPad Mini 时对其评价很高，但其 699 美元的起售价确实让人难以接受。好在，这款小尺寸平板电脑在“黑色星期五”期间已经有了相当大的折扣。目前，亚马逊和沃尔玛都在以 599 美元（优惠 100 美元）的价格出售 2026 款 Wi-Fi 版 128GB 存储空间的 iPad Mini，这是该机型迄今为止的最低价。

The new Mini is a notable upgrade over its predecessor, with a larger 8.5-inch OLED display that delivers deeper blacks and better contrast than the previous model’s 8.3-inch LCD. Despite the bigger screen, the tablet itself is smaller and lighter, making it easier to carry. Apple also added IP67 dust and water resistance, so you have less reason to worry about where you use it.

新款 Mini 相比前代产品有显著升级，其 8.5 英寸的 OLED 显示屏比上一代 8.3 英寸的 LCD 屏幕能呈现更深邃的黑色和更好的对比度。尽管屏幕变大了，但平板电脑本身却更小、更轻，携带更加方便。苹果还增加了 IP67 级防尘防水功能，让你不必再担心使用场景。

The upgraded A20 Pro chip also made apps load faster and demanding games run better in Pierce’s testing, while battery life held steady at around nine hours of screentime. At $599, though, the new Mini is still relatively expensive if you mainly want a tablet for browsing the web or streaming shows.

在皮尔斯的测试中，升级后的 A20 Pro 芯片使应用程序加载速度更快，大型游戏运行也更流畅，同时电池续航时间稳定在约 9 小时的屏幕使用时间。不过，如果你购买平板电脑主要是为了浏览网页或观看流媒体节目，599 美元的新款 Mini 仍然相对昂贵。

The last-gen iPad Mini, which runs on Apple’s A17 Pro chip, is available for under $500 if you’d rather save another $100 and can live without the OLED screen and water resistance. But if you’ve been waiting for a more worthwhile upgrade to Apple’s smallest tablet, the current Black Friday discount makes the new model easier to justify.

如果你想再省下 100 美元，并且可以接受没有 OLED 屏幕和防水功能，那么搭载苹果 A17 Pro 芯片的上一代 iPad Mini 目前售价不到 500 美元。但如果你一直在等待苹果这款最小尺寸平板电脑进行一次更值得的升级，那么现在的“黑色星期五”折扣让购买新款机型变得更加合理。
