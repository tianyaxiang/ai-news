Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.33175 (cs) [Submitted on 21 Oct 2026] Title: Feature-Based Explainability of Transformer Scoring Models: A Fidelity-First Framework for Automated Writing Assessment Authors: Michael Hemenway, Edward W. Wolfe.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.33175 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 10 月 21 日] 标题：基于特征的 Transformer 评分模型可解释性：一种用于自动写作评估的“保真度优先”框架 作者：Michael Hemenway, Edward W. Wolfe。

Abstract: Transformer-based automated writing scoring models can achieve strong agreement with human scores while providing limited transparency into the response characteristics associated with their predictions. This study evaluated a feature-based post-hoc explanation approach in which explanatory fidelity was established before feature importance was interpreted.

摘要：基于 Transformer 的自动写作评分模型可以与人工评分达成高度一致，但在其预测所关联的响应特征方面透明度有限。本研究评估了一种基于特征的事后解释方法，该方法在解释特征重要性之前先确立了解释的保真度。

English Language Arts responses from three writing prompts in Grades 6 and 8 were analyzed, with about 12,700 responses per prompt and separate DeBERTa-v3-base research models for Expression and Conventions. Each response was represented by 61 interpretable linguistic and writing-quality features.

研究分析了来自 6 年级和 8 年级三个写作提示的英语语言艺术答卷，每个提示约有 12,700 份答卷，并分别为“表达”和“规范”构建了独立的 DeBERTa-v3-base 研究模型。每份答卷由 61 个可解释的语言和写作质量特征来表示。

Four approaches - theory-driven composites, hierarchical feature groupings, exploratory factor analysis, and Random Forest surrogates - were compared in their ability to reproduce DeBERTa predictions. Random Forest surrogates produced the largest observed R2 values across all six prompt-by-trait conditions, with held-out R2 values from .81 to .95.

研究比较了四种方法——理论驱动的复合指标、分层特征分组、探索性因子分析和随机森林代理模型——在复现 DeBERTa 预测能力方面的表现。在所有六种“提示-特征”条件下，随机森林代理模型产生的 R2 值最高，留出数据的 R2 值在 .81 到 .95 之间。

SHAP analyses showed substantial overlap in high-importance features across traits, with 75% to 90% of the top 20 features shared. Trait differentiation occurred mainly through relative feature weighting: Expression showed greater importance for lexical and conceptual indicators, whereas Conventions showed greater importance for sentence-level and syntactic-control indicators.

SHAP 分析显示，不同特征之间的高重要性特征存在显著重叠，前 20 个特征中有 75% 到 90% 是共享的。特征间的区分主要通过相对特征权重实现：“表达”特征在词汇和概念指标上表现出更高的重要性，而“规范”特征在句子层面和句法控制指标上表现出更高的重要性。

Under researcher-defined mappings, rubric correspondence was 90%-95% for Expression and 12%-18% for Conventions. These percentages are descriptive and conditional on the mappings and available linguistic features. The findings support a fidelity-first framework for examining apparent scoring priorities while distinguishing post-hoc evidence about model behavior from direct explanation of transformer internals.

在研究者定义的映射下，评分标准的对应度在“表达”方面为 90%-95%，在“规范”方面为 12%-18%。这些百分比具有描述性，且取决于映射方式和可用的语言特征。研究结果支持一种“保真度优先”的框架，用于检查明显的评分优先级，同时将关于模型行为的事后证据与对 Transformer 内部机制的直接解释区分开来。
