# Tracking Diseases Cured by AI

Number of diseases cured by AI

AI 治愈的疾病数量

Zero. Not “almost one.” Not “one, if you count a scan.” No disease has been cured by a cure that AI discovered — no AI-discovered drug has even been approved yet.

零。不是“差不多一个”。也不是“如果你算上扫描结果的话算一个”。没有任何疾病是被 AI 发现的疗法所治愈的——目前甚至还没有任何一种 AI 发现的药物获得批准。

Snapshot as of Nov 27, 2026 — checked against FDA approvals through November 2026. This page does not poll live.

截至 2026 年 11 月 27 日的快照——已根据截至 2026 年 11 月的 FDA 批准情况进行核对。本页面不提供实时轮询。

The bar is not mysterious. It is the same bar every other drug has to clear. AI does not get a discount for being exciting in a keynote.

门槛并不神秘。它与其他所有药物必须达到的标准相同。AI 并不会因为在主题演讲中令人兴奋而获得优待。

If the number ever changes, it will almost certainly start here. None of these is there yet — here is exactly how far each one is.

如果这个数字发生变化，几乎肯定会从这里开始。目前还没有任何一个达到目标——以下是每一个项目的确切进展。

The furthest along by a clear margin, and the first drug where AI picked both the target (TNIK) and the molecule. Phase IIa in Nature Medicine (71 patients, 12 weeks) showed a +98.4 mL lung-function signal at the top dose. Phase III started in 2026 — 320 patients, 52 weeks. IPF has no cure today; even success here would first be a treatment, not yet a cure.

这是目前进展最快的一个，也是第一个由 AI 同时选定靶点（TNIK）和分子的药物。《自然-医学》发表的 IIa 期临床试验（71 名患者，12 周）显示，在最高剂量下，肺功能指标提升了 98.4 毫升。III 期临床试验于 2026 年启动，涉及 320 名患者，周期为 52 周。特发性肺纤维化（IPF）目前尚无治愈方法；即使该项目取得成功，也首先是一种治疗手段，而非治愈。

The nearest FDA decision: the FDA accepted its application with a target date in Q1 2027 after it beat an approved rival in Phase III. The catch for this counter: its roots are physics-based modelling from around 2016 against an already-known target, and psoriasis treatment is management, not a cure.

最接近的 FDA 决策：在 III 期临床试验中击败已获批的竞争对手后，FDA 已受理其申请，目标日期为 2027 年第一季度。该计数器的难点在于：其基础是 2016 年左右针对已知靶点的物理建模，且银屑病治疗属于管理范畴，而非治愈。

The loudest promise and the thinnest clinical record. Raised $2.1B in May 2026 on the strength of AlphaFold. As of November 2026, no Isomorphic molecule had been dosed in a human — first-in-human was targeted for the end of 2026.

承诺最响亮，但临床记录最薄弱。凭借 AlphaFold 的实力，于 2026 年 5 月筹集了 21 亿美元。截至 2026 年 11 月，没有任何 Isomorphic 的分子进入人体临床试验——首次人体试验的目标定在 2026 年底。
