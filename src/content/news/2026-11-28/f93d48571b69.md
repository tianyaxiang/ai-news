Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.33195 (cs) [Submitted on 16 Nov 2026] Title: Verified experience improves materials prediction with large language models Authors: Yiming Zhang, Koji Tsuda.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.33195 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 16 日] 标题：经核实的经验改进了大型语言模型的材料预测 作者：Yiming Zhang, Koji Tsuda。

Abstract: Materials discovery is an iterative loop: experiments and calculations verify predictions, and each outcome can inform the next. Yet most predictors are specialized single-task models fixed after training. Large language models have recently emerged as predictors across heterogeneous tasks but generally underperform specialized models, and retrieval-augmented variants discard their own outcomes. Benchmarks also score queries in isolation, leaving improvement from outcomes untested.

摘要：材料发现是一个迭代循环：实验和计算验证预测，每个结果都可以为下一个结果提供信息。然而，大多数预测器是训练后固定的专用单任务模型。大型语言模型最近已成为跨异构任务的预测器，但通常表现不如专用模型，且检索增强型变体往往会丢弃其自身的结果。基准测试也孤立地对查询进行评分，导致从结果中获得的改进未得到测试。

Here we construct MatMemBench, a sequential benchmark of 22 materials tasks that reveals outcomes only after prediction and tests online adaptation, memory retention and cross-task transfer. We present MatRecall, a framework that lets large language models store verified predictions, with evidence, outcome and error, as episodes and recall them without retraining.

在此，我们构建了 MatMemBench，这是一个包含 22 个材料任务的顺序基准测试，它仅在预测后才揭示结果，并测试在线适应性、记忆保留和跨任务迁移。我们提出了 MatRecall，这是一个让大型语言模型能够将经核实的预测（包括证据、结果和误差）作为片段存储，并在无需重新训练的情况下进行回溯的框架。

MatRecall improved median performance by 62% over a memory-free model, exceeded specialized models on 11 tasks, remained useful after feedback stopped and transferred between related tasks. Verified experience could become a reusable resource for self-improving discovery loops.

MatRecall 将中位数性能比无记忆模型提高了 62%，在 11 项任务中超过了专用模型，在反馈停止后仍然有效，并能在相关任务之间进行迁移。经核实的经验可能成为自我改进发现循环的可重用资源。
