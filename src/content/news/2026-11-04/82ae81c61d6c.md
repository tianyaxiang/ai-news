# All the news about The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake

GamingUpdated Today, Nov 4, 2026, 1:02 AM UTCAll the news about The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remakeby Verge StaffAhead of the release of Rockstar Games’ Grand Theft Auto VI, Nintendo is launching its own fall juggernaut: a Switch 2 remake of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.

在 Rockstar Games 的《侠盗猎车手 VI》发布前夕，任天堂即将推出其秋季的重磅产品：Switch 2 版本的《塞尔达传说：时之笛》重制版。

The remake, which is releasing 28 years after the launch of the original version on Nintendo 64, has a much more realistic visual style and some modern improvements like a jump button, a way to sprint, full voice acting, and a “Threads of Time” feature that helps you keep track of your progress. But while the game has a new coat of paint, it appears many elements will still remain faithful to the classic N64 release. Nintendo has also launched a special version of the Switch 2 and Switch 2 Pro Controller with Triforce imagery to celebrate the game.

这款重制版在任天堂 64 原版发布 28 年后推出，拥有更加写实的视觉风格，并进行了一些现代化改进，例如增加了跳跃键、冲刺功能、全程语音，以及帮助玩家追踪进度的“时间之线”（Threads of Time）功能。尽管游戏焕然一新，但许多元素似乎仍忠实于经典的 N64 原作。任天堂还推出了带有三角力量（Triforce）图案的 Switch 2 特别版主机和 Switch 2 Pro 手柄，以庆祝游戏发布。

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time remake is scheduled to release on November 5th. You can follow along with all of our coverage of the game right here.

《塞尔达传说：时之笛》重制版定于 11 月 5 日发售。您可以在此处关注我们对该游戏的所有报道。

With its upcoming remake for the Switch 2, Nintendo might have nailed the best thing about The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time: its timelessness. The 1998 3D action adventure game for the Nintendo 64 that pioneered so much — 3D graphics, movement, exploration, openness — has been reengineered with changes that make it feel more modern, and surprisingly terrifying at times. More than just sanding off its roughest edges, Nintendo also fitted Ocarina of Time with modern trappings to make it more welcoming to those who were introduced to Zelda through more modern releases, like Breath of the Wild. The remake has a new progression marker, called Threads of Time, that’s viewable when you pause. It also has Zelda Notes integration within the Nintendo Switch app to let you view game maps and other info on your phone. Oh, it also has a dedicated jump button.

任天堂不仅磨平了粗糙的边缘，还为《时之笛》配备了现代化的装饰，使其对那些通过《旷野之息》等现代作品接触塞尔达系列的玩家更加友好。重制版有一个新的进度标记，称为“时间之线”，可以在暂停时查看。它还集成了 Nintendo Switch 应用程序中的“塞尔达笔记”（Zelda Notes），让您可以在手机上查看游戏地图和其他信息。哦，它还有一个专门的跳跃键。
