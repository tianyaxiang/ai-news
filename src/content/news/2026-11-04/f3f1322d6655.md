# Death comes for XSLT

Death comes for XSLT Tuesday 3 Nov 2026 Last halloween, Google decided to kill XSLT. That was not a unilateral decision mind you, Google said “jump” and other browser vendors said “how high?”

XSLT 的末日即将来临，2026 年 11 月 3 日。去年万圣节，谷歌决定终结 XSLT。请注意，这并非单方面的决定，谷歌说“跳”，其他浏览器厂商则问“跳多高？”

In response, I published my Intent to Deprecate and Remove Google Chrome. A performative declaration of course, I don’t install spyware. Fearing my parody lacked seriousness, I created XSLT.RIP to ensure the developer world was informed of Google’s death note.

作为回应，我发布了“弃用并移除 Google Chrome”的意向声明。当然，这只是一种表演性的声明，我并不会安装间谍软件。担心我的讽刺不够严肃，我创建了 XSLT.RIP，以确保开发者世界知晓谷歌的这份“死亡通知书”。

This did the trick, although a few commenters believed it was satire. There is subtlety to XSLT.RIP. The website itself is built with XSLT and when Google kills XSLT they’ll kill my website.

这招奏效了，尽管一些评论者认为这只是讽刺。XSLT.RIP 别有深意：该网站本身就是用 XSLT 构建的，当谷歌杀死 XSLT 时，他们也就杀死了我的网站。

The web standards process and its stewards (Big Browser) used to adhere to a social contract. Backwards compatibility was a major design principle. Under no circumstances do you ever purposefully break the web. “Don’t break the web” “Don’t break the web” “Don’t break the web” Google decided to renege and break the web.

网络标准流程及其管理者（大浏览器厂商）过去一直遵守一项社会契约。向后兼容性曾是一项主要的设计原则。在任何情况下，你都不应故意破坏网络。“不要破坏网络”、“不要破坏网络”、“不要破坏网络”。谷歌决定背信弃义，破坏网络。

XSLT.RIP will be broken soon and I’ll sit back and watch Google break it. 17 November 2026 appears to be the date of execution. Some Chromium-based browsers have been running a man-in-the-middle attack by injecting propaganda into XSLT websites.

XSLT.RIP 很快就会崩溃，我将坐视谷歌将其摧毁。2026 年 11 月 17 日似乎就是执行日期。一些基于 Chromium 的浏览器一直在通过向 XSLT 网站注入宣传内容来实施中间人攻击。

This site uses XSLT; that functionality is being removed from this browser very soon. When that happens, this page will likely no longer display correctly. You might be able to install a browser extension that allows you to continue viewing it. Otherwise, you should contact the maintainer of the site for further information.
