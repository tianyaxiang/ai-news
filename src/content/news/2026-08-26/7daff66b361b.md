# Don't Wordle

**Don't Wordle** is a free daily word game. Like Wordle, you get six tries—but here the goal is to avoid guessing the hidden word. Use the clues from each guess (green for correct letter and position, yellow for correct letter wrong place, gray for letters not in the word) to eliminate possibilities. Your job is to keep as many valid words in play as you can without accidentally guessing the answer.

**Don't Wordle** 是一款免费的每日文字游戏。和 Wordle 一样，你有六次尝试机会——但这里的目标是避免猜中隐藏的单词。利用每次猜测后的线索（绿色代表字母和位置正确，黄色代表字母正确但位置错误，灰色代表单词中不包含该字母）来排除可能性。你的任务是在不意外猜中答案的前提下，尽可能多地保留有效的候选词。

Each day has one puzzle. You can use a limited number of undos to take back a guess if you get too close. Track your stats and share your results with friends.

每天有一个谜题。如果你不小心猜得太接近答案，可以使用有限次数的撤销功能来收回猜测。你还可以追踪自己的统计数据并与朋友分享结果。
