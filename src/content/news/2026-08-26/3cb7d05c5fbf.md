# Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold Review: Better, Not Best
# Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold 评测：有所提升，但非最佳

**The Good:** Good camera. Slightly improved design. IP68 water and dust resistance. Qi2 25W wireless charging. Slick AI features and software.
**优点：** 出色的摄像头。设计略有改进。支持 IP68 级防尘防水。支持 Qi2 25W 无线充电。流畅的 AI 功能与软件体验。

**The Bad:** Significantly weaker specs than other Pro models. Comparatively chunky. $100 more than last year’s 10 Pro Fold.
**缺点：** 硬件规格明显弱于其他 Pro 系列机型。机身相对厚重。价格比去年的 10 Pro Fold 高出 100 美元。

The Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold is a predictably iterative improvement over last year’s foldable, with slight upgrades to performance and the camera, but it comes with an unwelcome $100 price bump. The IP68 rating for water and dust resistance and the Qi2 25W support for faster magnetic charging are standouts in a category where Google is lagging, but it has never been known for its hardware. Software is where the company excels, and some folks will appreciate the unrivaled AI-infused software features.
Google Pixel 11 Pro Fold 是去年折叠屏机型的常规迭代升级，在性能和摄像头方面有小幅提升，但随之而来的是令人不悦的 100 美元涨价。IP68 级防尘防水和支持 Qi2 25W 快速磁吸充电是该机在 Google 相对落后的领域中表现出的亮点，但 Google 从来都不是以硬件见长。软件才是该公司的强项，一些用户会非常欣赏其无与伦比的 AI 驱动软件功能。

Google is good at elevating its devices beyond the spec sheet, but the 11 Pro Fold is perilously close to the Pixel 11 on paper, a phone that’s less than half its price. Beyond the big unfolding screen and customizable rear LEDs, little else justifies the “Pro” tag. With Samsung’s fresh Fold8 form factor and a folding iPhone on the horizon, I’m not convinced Google has done enough to make this the foldable you drop $1,900 on.
Google 擅长通过软件体验提升设备的价值，使其超越参数表本身，但从纸面参数来看，11 Pro Fold 与价格不到其一半的 Pixel 11 过于接近。除了那块巨大的折叠屏和可自定义的后置 LED 灯外，几乎没有其他理由支撑其“Pro”的称号。随着三星 Fold8 全新形态的推出以及折叠屏 iPhone 的蓄势待发，我不认为 Google 已经做出了足够的努力，让这款手机成为值得你花费 1,900 美元购买的折叠屏设备。

### Devil in the Details
### 细节魔鬼

The Pixel 11 Pro Fold doesn’t look different from the 10 Pro Fold, but Google has shaved off a fraction of a millimeter and 19 grams. Sadly, the 11 Pro Fold is still heavier and thicker (239 grams and 10.1 mm) than Samsung’s Galaxy Z Fold8 (201 grams and 9.7 mm), the Fold8 Ultra (215 grams and 8.9 mm), and the Honor Magic V6 (219 grams and 8.75 mm, not sold in the US). That makes it a little trickier to handle, though the horizontal camera bar means less rocking than Samsung’s phones when they lie flat.
Pixel 11 Pro Fold 在外观上与 10 Pro Fold 差别不大，但 Google 将其厚度减少了不到一毫米，重量减轻了 19 克。遗憾的是，11 Pro Fold 依然比三星 Galaxy Z Fold8（201 克，9.7 毫米）、Fold8 Ultra（215 克，8.9 毫米）以及荣耀 Magic V6（219 克，8.75 毫米，未在美国销售）更重、更厚。这使得它在握持时稍微有些不便，不过横向的摄像头模组设计意味着它平放在桌面上时，比三星手机更不容易晃动。

The double-camera module has had a makeover, so it looks less chunky, and the back is matte-textured glass that feels lovely. The Pixel 11 Pro Fold scores an impressive IP68 rating for water and dust resistance. That compares favorably to Samsung’s IP48 ratings, which means both can survive shallow submersion, but Google’s phone is dust-tight, whereas Samsung’s phones are at risk from fine dust and sand.
双摄模组经过了重新设计，看起来不再那么臃肿，背部采用磨砂质感玻璃，手感极佳。Pixel 11 Pro Fold 获得了令人印象深刻的 IP68 级防尘防水认证。相比之下，三星的 IP48 评级虽然也能应对浅水浸泡，但 Google 的手机具备完全防尘能力，而三星手机则面临细小灰尘和沙粒侵入的风险。

It’s a shame the Fold is only available in two colors: Obsidian, the color of my review unit, looks more like a dark matte gray than black, while Olive Green has a shinier gold frame.
令人遗憾的是，这款折叠屏手机仅提供两种颜色：我评测机型所用的曜石黑（Obsidian）看起来更像是哑光深灰色而非纯黑，而橄榄绿（Olive Green）则配有更闪亮的金色边框。

I’m not a fan of Google’s asymmetrical design. With the Pixel 11 Pro Fold closed, there’s a curved corner cutout, and when you open the phone, the two halves have slightly different thicknesses. All folding phones still have a detectable fold line in the middle of the screen when you open them, but Google’s is among the most noticeable. Some folks won’t notice or care, but I’m the kind of guy who goes upstairs to turn the light on so the downstairs switches aren’t misaligned.
我不喜欢 Google 的非对称设计。当 Pixel 11 Pro Fold 合上时，有一个弯曲的角落切口；而当你打开手机时，两半机身的厚度略有不同。所有折叠屏手机在展开时屏幕中间仍会有可见的折痕，但 Google 的折痕属于最明显的那一类。有些人可能不会注意到或不在意，但我是一个连楼下开关没对齐都要上楼去调好的人。

The fingerprint sensor in the power button always bugs me when I switch to a folding phone, because I have to pick it up to touch it and unlock the screen, rather than pressing on the screen like most phones. You do have face unlock with the Pixel 11 Pro Fold, and it works well provided there’s ample light, but it still doesn’t work in a dark room, which is still annoying.
每当我换用折叠屏手机时，电源键上的指纹传感器总是让我感到困扰，因为我必须拿起手机去触碰它才能解锁，而不是像大多数手机那样直接按压屏幕。Pixel 11 Pro Fold 虽然支持人脸解锁，且在光线充足的情况下表现良好，但在黑暗房间里依然无法使用，这确实很烦人。

You get two lovely, sharp OLED displays in the Pixel 11 Pro Fold: an 8-inch main screen and a 6.5-inch cover screen. Both push peak brightness to 3,600 nits, remaining legible in direct sunlight, and boast a variable refresh rate up to 120 Hz. They’re smooth, with rich colors, and I have nothing to complain about beyond that crease.
Pixel 11 Pro Fold 配备了两块精美且清晰的 OLED 显示屏：一块 8 英寸的主屏和一块 6.5 英寸的外屏。两者的峰值亮度均达到 3,600 尼特，在阳光直射下依然清晰可见，并支持最高 120Hz 的可变刷新率。屏幕显示流畅，色彩丰富，除了那道折痕外，我没什么可抱怨的。

### Pro Pretender
### “Pro”之名，名不副实

I’m not convinced the 11 Pro Fold earns the “Pro” in its title. It does have the new HiLight feature, a group of color LEDs in the camera module’s flash that light up different colors based on who is calling, or swirl through the rainbow while you chat to Gemini. It’s neat, if a little underbaked. It’s handy if you put your phone face down when you’re working or trying to relax, but it’s a very minor feature.
我不认为 11 Pro Fold 配得上“Pro”这个称号。它确实拥有新的 HiLight 功能，即摄像头模组闪光灯内的一组彩色 LED 灯，可以根据来电者显示不同颜色，或者在与 Gemini 对话时呈现彩虹般的旋转光效。这很酷，但略显稚嫩。如果你在工作或休息时习惯将手机正面朝下放置，这个功能会很方便，但它终究只是一个非常次要的功能。

All the Pixels get the new Tensor G6 processor, and the Fold has 16 GB of RAM paired with 256 GB, 512 GB, or 1 TB of storage. Switching from the Pixel 10 Pro XL, it was mostly smooth sailing. But it froze a couple of times in split-screen view in Gmail and after launching an app on the outer display, then unfolding the inner display.
所有 Pixel 机型都搭载了新的 Tensor G6 处理器，Fold 配备了 16GB 内存，以及 256GB、512GB 或 1TB 的存储空间。从 Pixel 10 Pro XL 换过来，整体体验还算顺畅。但在 Gmail 分屏视图下，以及在外屏启动应用后展开内屏时，手机曾出现过几次卡死。

The 11 Pro Fold actually has more in common with the regular Pixel 11, because it misses out on the high-end camera in its Pro siblings. Folding phones struggle to match flagships on camera specs because of form-factor limitations, and they must include two front-facing cameras (one for open and one for closed).
11 Pro Fold 实际上与普通版 Pixel 11 有更多共同点，因为它缺失了 Pro 系列兄弟机型上的高端摄像头配置。由于外形尺寸的限制，折叠屏手机很难在摄像头规格上与旗舰机抗衡，而且它们必须包含两颗前置摄像头（一颗用于展开状态，一颗用于闭合状态）。

You get the same 48-megapixel main camera and 10.8-megapixel telephoto for 5X optical zoom as on the Pixel 11, but the Fold drops to a 10.5-megapixel ultrawide. The main camera is excellent in a wide range of scenarios, and I used the telephoto lens a lot. The 5X zoom produces sharp, detailed shots, though things get a little fuzzy if you push up to the maximum digital zoom capability at 30X. The ultrawide is a very obvious weak spot, producing relatively grainy images, though it doubles as a macro lens for decent, if slightly distorted, close-ups. The 10-megapixel front-facing cameras are passable for selfies.
你得到的是与 Pixel 11 相同的 4800 万像素主摄和 1080 万像素 5 倍光学变焦长焦镜头，但 Fold 的超广角镜头降级为 1050 万像素。主摄像头在多种场景下表现出色，我经常使用长焦镜头。5 倍变焦拍摄的照片清晰且细节丰富，但如果推到 30 倍的最大数字变焦，画面会变得有些模糊。超广角镜头是一个非常明显的短板，成像相对粗糙，虽然它兼具微距功能，可以拍摄尚可但略有畸变的近景。1000 万像素的前置摄像头用于自拍尚可。

The 11 Pro and 11 Pro XL cameras are superior across the board (50-MP main, 48-MP ultrawide, 48-MP telephoto, and 42-MP front-facing). They also boast instant Night Sight, 120X digital zoom, and better video capture. That said, I took some great shots with the Pixel 11 Pro Fold; its camera results compare favorably to other foldables, and a good telephoto is more useful than an ultrawide.
11 Pro 和 11 Pro XL 的摄像头在各个方面都更胜一筹（5000 万像素主摄、4800 万像素超广角、4800 万像素长焦和 4200 万像素前置）。它们还拥有即时夜视、120 倍数字变焦和更好的视频录制能力。话虽如此，我还是用 Pixel 11 Pro Fold 拍出了一些很棒的照片；其成像效果在折叠屏手机中表现良好，而且一颗出色的长焦镜头比超广角镜头更有用。

我喜欢这些新的拍照小技巧：Camera Looks 允许你定义偏好的风格，设定对比度和色彩；Magic Capture 让你可以轻松捕捉活动中的视频和照片，而无需一直盯着取景器；Creator Suite 则会让创作者们感到欣喜……
