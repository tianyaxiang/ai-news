# The Witcher 3 is getting a remaster
# 《巫师 3》将推出重制版

CD Projekt Red is remastering The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt, the hit RPG that first launched in 2015, and the updated game will be available starting September 29th. The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Remastered will bring improvements to visuals and combat, a “reimagined” skill tree, and more, and it also includes the first two expansions, Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine.

CD Projekt Red 正在重制于 2015 年首次发行的热门 RPG 游戏《巫师 3：狂猎》，更新后的版本将于 9 月 29 日正式推出。《巫师 3：狂猎——重制版》将带来视觉效果和战斗系统的改进、“重构”的技能树以及更多内容，同时还将包含《石之心》和《血与酒》这两部最初的扩展包。

Remastered will be a free upgrade for players who own the base game on GOG, Steam, Epic Games Store, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X / S. With the launch of Remastered, The Witcher 3 will also be coming to the Nintendo Switch 2.

对于在 GOG、Steam、Epic Games Store、PlayStation 5 和 Xbox Series X / S 上拥有本体游戏的玩家，重制版将作为免费升级提供。随着重制版的发布，《巫师 3》也将登陆任天堂 Switch 2 平台。

This remastered version of The Witcher 3 (which follows a next-gen upgrade released in 2022) is the start of a big push for the franchise over the next few years. CDPR is set to launch a brand new Witcher 3 expansion, titled Songs of the Past, in 2027, and it’s aiming to release its next mainline Witcher game, The Witcher 4, in 2028.

《巫师 3》的这个重制版本（继 2022 年发布的次世代升级之后）标志着该系列在未来几年内重大推广计划的开始。CDPR 计划于 2027 年推出名为《往昔之歌》（Songs of the Past）的全新《巫师 3》扩展内容，并目标在 2028 年发布《巫师》系列的正统续作《巫师 4》。
