# The 4 Best Car Phone Mounts I’ve Tried (2026): Belkin, Andery, Andobil
# 我测试过的 4 款最佳车载手机支架（2026）：Belkin、Andery、Andobil

A car phone mount is no longer an option—it's a necessity. Nearly every US state restricts cell phone use while driving (we see you, Montana!), and most require full hands-free operation. The best car phone mounts offer an easy view of your phone's screen without blocking your view of the road, hold your phone securely, and keep vibration to a minimum.
车载手机支架不再是可有可无的配件，而是必需品。美国几乎每个州都限制驾驶时使用手机（蒙大拿州，我们看到你了！），且大多数州要求完全免提操作。最好的车载手机支架能让你在不遮挡视线的情况下轻松查看屏幕，稳固地固定手机，并将震动降至最低。

I've spent weeks and hundreds of miles testing car phone holders for iPhones and Android alike, on busy freeways and on potholed city roads. What I found is that the best car phone mounts aren't necessarily expensive or name brands: They're just smartly designed.
我花了数周时间，在繁忙的高速公路和坑洼的城市道路上行驶了数百英里，测试了适用于 iPhone 和安卓手机的车载支架。我发现，最好的车载支架不一定非要昂贵或出自大品牌，关键在于其巧妙的设计。

The best car phone mount for an iPhone is almost always a secure MagSafe mount. The one I keep in my car when I'm not testing other mounts is a sturdy, compact Andery suction mount ($36) that holds preternaturally fast to both my phone and dash but can also be mounted to a window. My favorite mechanical car phone holder for Android phones is a sturdy, versatile, ball-joint mount from Andobil ($30) that affixes tightly to a car's dash, windshield, or air vents.
iPhone 最好的车载支架几乎总是稳固的 MagSafe 支架。在不测试其他产品时，我车里一直用的是一款坚固、小巧的 Andery 吸盘支架（36 美元），它能异常牢固地吸附在手机和仪表盘上，也可以安装在车窗上。我最喜欢的安卓手机机械支架是 Andobil 的一款坚固且多功能的球头支架（30 美元），它可以紧紧固定在仪表盘、挡风玻璃或空调出风口上。

### Best Car Phone Mount for iPhones and Qi2 Phones
### 适用于 iPhone 和 Qi2 手机的最佳车载支架

**Andery MagSafe Car Phone Holder**
**Andery MagSafe 车载手机支架**

The world is full of bulky phone mounts that block your view of the road, inflexible phone mounts that only allow for horizontal or vertical mounting, weak suction cups, and kinda mid MagSafe attachments. And then there's this innovatively designed, wonderfully compact dash and window mount from Andery.
市面上充斥着各种笨重且遮挡视线的手机支架、只能横向或纵向安装的死板支架、吸力微弱的吸盘，以及表现平平的 MagSafe 配件。而 Andery 推出的这款设计创新、极其小巧的仪表盘及车窗支架则脱颖而出。

On flat surfaces, the nano-gel suction cup holds fast like Hodor at the door. The 22-magnet MagSafe ring allows airflow to avoid overheating, but it also holds stronger than most comparable Qi2 magnets. And unlike bulky plastic mounts, the Andery's aluminum-arm, stainless steel ring keeps a low profile and interferes minimally with one's line of sight. In fact, when affixed to my dash, this device doesn't block my view of the road at all. The ring rotates 360 degrees. The two-joint arm can either fold tightly to the mount or extend outward. No other mount I've tested combines all of these virtues.
在平坦表面上，纳米凝胶吸盘像“阿多（Hodor）守门”一样牢固。22 磁铁组成的 MagSafe 环既能保持空气流通以防过热，吸力也比大多数同类 Qi2 磁铁更强。与笨重的塑料支架不同，Andery 的铝合金支臂和不锈钢环设计低调，对视线的干扰极小。事实上，当它固定在我的仪表盘上时，完全不会遮挡路况视野。磁吸环可 360 度旋转，双关节支臂既能紧贴支架折叠，也能向外延伸。我测试过的其他支架中，没有一款能集齐这些优点。

But note you'll need a relatively flat surface to get a good bond with the suction cup. I've also seen some reports that the suction cup can lose adhesion over time if repeatedly re-mounted (and it's cussedly difficult to remove in the first place, though it leaves no residue when you do). Non-Qi2-compatible Android phones will need a MagSafe case to be compatible with this car phone holder. But if your phone has a magnet or a magnetic case, this is the simplest and most effective car phone mount I know, and it performs leagues better than other similarly designed car mounts like this one from Acer.
但请注意，你需要一个相对平坦的表面才能让吸盘获得良好的附着力。也有报告称，如果反复拆装，吸盘的粘性可能会随时间下降（而且它非常难拆卸，尽管拆下后不会留下残留物）。非 Qi2 兼容的安卓手机需要搭配 MagSafe 手机壳才能使用。但如果你的手机自带磁吸功能或配有磁吸壳，这是我所知最简单、最有效的车载支架，其性能远超 Acer 等品牌的同类设计。

### Best Universal Car Phone Mount
### 最佳通用型车载手机支架

**Andobil Vent, Windshield and Dash Car Phone Mount**
**Andobil 出风口、挡风玻璃及仪表盘车载手机支架**

Mechanical mounts like this one from Andobil—with holders that can be slid in sideways to securely grip the phone—are flexible for a broad range of phones and don't require MagSafe technology. But they're also mostly bulky, making them a less preferred option as a windshield or dashboard mount because this will block your field of vision.
像 Andobil 这种机械式支架——通过侧向滑动夹具来稳固手机——对各种手机的兼容性很强，且不需要 MagSafe 技术。但它们通常比较笨重，作为挡风玻璃或仪表盘支架时，往往会遮挡视线，因此不是首选。

Among all similar devices I've tested, this one from newer Chinese company Andobil is the most compact I've tested when not in use, while still offering a lot of flexibility. The nanogel suction cup on its dash mount is one of the most stable I've tested (almost aggressively stuck on, to be honest). And the extendable arm, while plastic, feels less cheap and breakable than other models. The kit also comes with an air vent mount, whose three-point design makes it among the most stable vent mounts I've tested and allows you to maneuver the phone so it does not block the vent.
在我测试过的所有同类产品中，这家新兴中国公司 Andobil 的产品在不使用时最为小巧，同时又提供了极高的灵活性。其仪表盘支架上的纳米凝胶吸盘是我测试过最稳定的（老实说，粘得甚至有点“激进”）。虽然支臂是塑料材质，但感觉比其他型号更结实，不易损坏。套装还附带一个出风口支架，其三点式设计使其成为我测试过最稳固的出风口支架之一，并允许你调整手机位置，以免遮挡出风口。

This mechanical car phone holder is hard to call aesthetic. It's essentially a pad with a gripper on the side. But the grips extend to accommodate even larger phones and are able to keep the phone in place even during vigorous shaking. (If you're wondering whether I place phones in these phone mounts, and then come at them like they're a shake weight? I do.) The release button's a little awkwardly placed, and Andobil's marketing about “military standards” is a little silly on a low-cost device made with thermoplastics. But after I watched other suction cups wilt in summer heat, this Andobil's still going strong.
这款机械支架很难称得上美观，本质上就是一个带侧边夹具的底座。但夹具可以伸展以容纳更大的手机，即使在剧烈晃动下也能保持手机稳固。（如果你好奇我是否会把手机放进支架然后像摇摆哑铃一样疯狂摇晃它？是的，我会。）释放按钮的位置有点尴尬，Andobil 关于“军工标准”的营销对于一款低成本热塑性塑料设备来说显得有些滑稽。但在目睹了其他吸盘在夏日高温下纷纷“缴械”后，这款 Andobil 依然表现强劲。
