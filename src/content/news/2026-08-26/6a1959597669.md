# Gemini in Chrome Adds Select from Screen for Faster Image and Page Analysis
# Chrome 版 Gemini 新增“屏幕选择”功能，实现更快速的图像与页面分析

Google has expanded Gemini in Chrome with a desktop workflow that lets users send a selected part of a web page directly to Gemini. Called Select from screen, the feature is designed for moments when a full page is not the relevant context: a user can draw a box around particular text, an image, or a mixed section of page content and ask Gemini to analyze or act on it in Chrome's side panel.
Google 已扩展了 Chrome 版 Gemini 的桌面工作流，允许用户将网页的选定部分直接发送给 Gemini。该功能被称为“屏幕选择”（Select from screen），专为那些无需整页上下文的场景而设计：用户可以框选特定的文本、图像或混合页面内容，并要求 Gemini 在 Chrome 侧边栏中对其进行分析或处理。

The change makes Gemini more closely embedded in everyday browser work. Rather than manually describing what is on a page or switching between tools, users can identify the exact on-screen material they want Gemini to consider. For teams that regularly research products, review creative assets, compare information, or work from web-based documents, that can make AI assistance more immediate. Its usefulness will still depend on whether Gemini in Chrome is enabled for the user and, for managed environments, how administrators configure access.
这一变化使 Gemini 更紧密地融入了日常浏览器工作。用户无需手动描述页面内容或在不同工具间切换，即可精准指定希望 Gemini 处理的屏幕素材。对于经常进行产品调研、审查创意资产、比较信息或处理网页文档的团队而言，这能让 AI 辅助变得更加即时。该功能的实用性仍取决于用户是否启用了 Chrome 版 Gemini，以及在受管理的环境中，管理员如何配置访问权限。

### How Select from screen works
### “屏幕选择”功能的工作原理

Google's official instructions for sharing specific parts of a screen with Gemini in Chrome describe a straightforward process. Users open the Gemini side panel in Chrome, choose Select from screen, then draw around the area they want to share. The chosen content is sent to Gemini as the basis for the next interaction. The important distinction is that the feature is not limited to a single content type. Google says the selected region can contain text and/or images. That gives users a more precise way to supply context from a web page without treating the entire page as the prompt.
Google 关于在 Chrome 版 Gemini 中共享屏幕特定部分的官方说明描述了一个简单的流程：用户打开 Chrome 中的 Gemini 侧边栏，选择“屏幕选择”，然后框选想要共享的区域。所选内容将作为下一次交互的基础发送给 Gemini。值得注意的是，该功能并不局限于单一内容类型。Google 表示，选定区域可以包含文本和/或图像。这为用户提供了一种更精确的方式来提供网页上下文，而无需将整个页面作为提示词。

### Why the workflow matters
### 为什么该工作流很重要

The value is context selection, not simply another way to open an AI chatbot. A marketing manager could isolate an image or page section when preparing a brief. A buyer could select product details while comparing suppliers. A support or operations employee could share a relevant portion of a web interface when seeking help interpreting what they see. These are examples of potential uses, not promises that Gemini will produce an accurate or complete answer in every case. Users should still review outputs before using them in customer-facing, financial, legal, or operational decisions. The browser feature reduces the friction of providing context, but it does not remove the need for human judgment.
其价值在于上下文的选择，而不仅仅是打开 AI 聊天机器人的另一种方式。营销经理在准备简报时可以截取图像或页面部分；采购人员在比较供应商时可以选中产品详情；支持或运营人员在寻求帮助以解读界面信息时，可以共享网页界面的相关部分。这些只是潜在用途的示例，并不保证 Gemini 在任何情况下都能提供准确或完整的答案。用户在将输出结果用于面向客户、财务、法律或运营决策之前，仍应进行审核。该浏览器功能降低了提供上下文的门槛，但并未消除对人类判断的需求。

### Practical ways to test it
### 实际测试方法

The most useful first test is a repeatable task with a clear expected outcome. For example, a team could select a product comparison area and ask Gemini to identify the differences shown, or select a visual and nearby copy to help generate a draft internal summary. This reveals whether the feature saves time in a real workflow rather than merely producing an interesting demonstration. Keep the evaluation narrow. Compare the time required to prepare a prompt manually with the time required to select the relevant region and refine Gemini's response. Also note when the selected content lacks the context needed for a reliable answer. A tightly cropped region may be efficient, but surrounding page information can sometimes change its meaning.
最有用的初步测试是针对具有明确预期结果的可重复任务。例如，团队可以选择产品比较区域并要求 Gemini 识别差异，或者选择一张图片及其附近的文案来帮助生成内部摘要草稿。这能揭示该功能是在实际工作流中节省了时间，还是仅仅产生了一个有趣的演示。评估范围要小。比较手动准备提示词所需的时间与选择相关区域并优化 Gemini 回复所需的时间。同时注意选定内容何时缺乏可靠回答所需的上下文。裁剪过紧的区域可能很高效，但周围的页面信息有时会改变其含义。
