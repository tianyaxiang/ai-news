# Topology of a Smile: Persistent Homology in Dental Imaging
# 微笑的拓扑学：牙科影像中的持久同调

**Abstract:** CBCT (Cone Beam Computed Tomography) scans provide detailed three-dimensional images, widely used in dentistry for diagnostic and treatment planning tasks. While invaluable, analyzing and documenting these scans is labor-intensive, prompting efforts to automate key steps like the classification and segmentation of anatomical structures to identify tooth types and associated pathologies.

**摘要：** 锥束计算机断层扫描（CBCT）提供了详细的三维图像，被广泛应用于牙科的诊断和治疗规划任务中。尽管这些扫描图像价值巨大，但对其进行分析和记录却非常耗时，这促使人们致力于自动化处理关键步骤，例如对解剖结构进行分类和分割，以识别牙齿类型及相关病理。

In this article, we propose an approach to automation that leverages persistent homology, a framework from topological data analysis that studies the shape of data by identifying features like connected components, holes, and voids across multiple scales. Persistent homology, together with a support vector machine, allows us to classify teeth in a CBCT scan and to perform diagnostics.

在本文中，我们提出了一种利用持久同调（persistent homology）的自动化方法。持久同调是拓扑数据分析中的一个框架，它通过识别多尺度下的连通分量、孔洞和空隙等特征来研究数据的形状。通过将持久同调与支持向量机（SVM）相结合，我们能够对 CBCT 扫描中的牙齿进行分类并执行诊断。

Our method advances the state of the art, reaching average accuracy scores of 97.67% for tooth-labeling and 96.77% for diagnostic tasks, outperforming a CNN trained on the same data with accuracy of 70.27% and 86.67%, respectively.

我们的方法提升了该领域的现有技术水平，在牙齿标注任务中达到了 97.67% 的平均准确率，在诊断任务中达到了 96.77% 的平均准确率，优于在相同数据上训练的卷积神经网络（CNN，其准确率分别为 70.27% 和 86.67%）。
