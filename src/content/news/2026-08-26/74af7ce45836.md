# 5 ways to upgrade your home decor with Google Search
# 利用 Google 搜索升级家居装饰的 5 种方法

From tracking down unique vintage finds to seeing how new furniture fits before you buy, here’s how Google Search can help transform your living space.
从寻找独特的复古好物，到在购买前预览新家具的摆放效果，Google 搜索可以助你轻松改造居住空间。

People turn to Google Search every day for discovery and inspiration, including when it comes to home decor: Searches for “home decor inspo” are up 300% in the past month, and both “bathroom inspo” and “living room inspo” are breakout searches in the same timeframe. Bold pops of color are in, too — this year's trending paint searches include teal and magenta.
人们每天都会通过 Google 搜索来获取发现与灵感，家居装饰也不例外：过去一个月里，“家居装饰灵感”（home decor inspo）的搜索量增长了 300%，“浴室灵感”和“客厅灵感”也成为了同期搜索的热门词汇。大胆的色彩搭配也正流行——今年热门的油漆搜索颜色包括青色和洋红色。

Getting inspired is the fun part. Making your vision reality is where people can get stuck. Here are five ways you can use Google Search tools to take action on your home decor vision board.
寻找灵感的过程固然有趣，但将构想变为现实往往会让人感到棘手。以下是利用 Google 搜索工具将你的家居装饰蓝图付诸实践的 5 种方法。

**1. Visualize furniture in your space.**
**1. 在空间中预览家具效果**

When you're eyeing a new couch or other large piece of furniture, it can be hard to know if the piece will actually fit your room both physically and aesthetically. AI Mode in Search can help you visualize it so you feel more confident about what will work in your space. Simply upload a photo of your room and ask something like, "Based on my living room, what kind of couch would you recommend? The wall is 84 inches wide." You can then see inspiration for different types of couches that would fit in that spot and follow up with a prompt like, "Can you show me what an emerald green velvet sofa could look like?" to see a mockup right in your space.
当你相中了一款新沙发或其他大型家具时，很难判断它在物理空间和审美上是否与房间匹配。搜索中的 AI 模式可以帮你进行可视化预览，让你更自信地做出选择。只需上传一张房间照片，并询问类似这样的问题：“根据我的客厅，你推荐什么样的沙发？墙面宽度为 84 英寸。”随后，你就能看到适合该位置的不同沙发款式灵感，并进一步追问：“能让我看看祖母绿丝绒沙发摆在这里的效果吗？”从而在空间中直接看到模拟图。

**2. Learn more and shop decor with Lens in Search.**
**2. 使用 Google Lens 搜索并购买装饰品**

This year, people are searching for vintage more than ever before, with “vintage braided rugs” and “vintage wood coffee table” as the top trending home furnishing items. If you're out and about and see a vintage decorative item you love, you can use Google Lens to learn more about it and find similar items online within your price range. Just take a picture of the item with the Google app on iOS or Android, and Lens will pull up links to similar items so you can bring that vintage style home.
今年，人们对复古风格的搜索热情空前高涨，“复古编织地毯”和“复古木质咖啡桌”成为了最热门的家居用品。如果你在逛街时看到心仪的复古装饰品，可以使用 Google Lens 了解详情，并在预算范围内在线寻找类似商品。只需使用 iOS 或 Android 版 Google App 对该物品拍照，Lens 就会为你提供类似商品的链接，让你将复古风格带回家。

**3. Uncover new decor finds with Circle to Search.**
**3. 通过“圈选搜索”（Circle to Search）发现新装饰**

Sometimes online scrolling leads to the best discoveries. With Circle to Search, you can track down the decor you love — from videos, social feeds, or web pages — without switching apps. Spot a social post featuring fish wallpaper (searches are up 140% in the past month!) that would look perfect in your home? Just long press the home button or navigation bar on the latest Pixel and Samsung Galaxy S26 devices, then circle the wallpaper on your screen to see similar options or ask a question about the product.
有时，在线浏览网页时会有意外收获。借助“圈选搜索”功能，你无需切换应用，即可从视频、社交动态或网页中追踪你喜爱的装饰品。在社交媒体上看到了一款鱼纹壁纸（过去一个月搜索量增长了 140%），觉得它非常适合你的家？只需长按最新款 Pixel 或三星 Galaxy S26 设备的主屏幕按钮或导航栏，然后圈选屏幕上的壁纸，即可查看类似选项或针对该产品进行提问。

**4. Tackle DIY projects with Search Live.**
**4. 使用 Search Live 攻克 DIY 项目**

Let’s say you’re one of the many people searching for floating shelves (a top-trending item this year) to put in your new studio apartment. They’re cute and practical — but once you buy them, you still have to install them. Search Live can help guide you through DIY projects step-by-step. To get started, just open the Google app and tap the Live icon under the search bar. You can start asking your questions out loud like you’re talking to a friend and enable video input to share visual context from your camera feed to get the job done right.
假设你也是众多正在为新公寓寻找浮动搁板（今年最热门的单品之一）的人之一。它们既美观又实用，但买回来后还需要安装。Search Live 可以一步步指导你完成 DIY 项目。只需打开 Google App，点击搜索栏下方的 Live 图标即可开始。你可以像和朋友聊天一样大声提问，并开启视频输入功能，通过摄像头分享实时画面，从而顺利完成安装工作。

**5. Get a great price.**
**5. 获取优惠价格**

Getting your home just-right can add up. Before you buy that funky lamp or harlequin wallpaper (both breakout searches in the past month), do a quick price check to make sure you’re getting a good deal. Just tap on any product listing to compare costs across stores. You can also expand the typical price drop down to view price history and get a sense of whether the current listing is high, low, or typical. Still not ready to buy? Tap “Track price” and you’ll get an alert when it falls into your budget.
打造理想的家可能会产生不小的开销。在购买那盏时髦的台灯或菱格纹壁纸（两者均为过去一个月的热门搜索）之前，先快速比价，确保买得划算。只需点击任何产品列表，即可比较不同商店的价格。你还可以展开“典型价格”下拉菜单查看价格历史记录，判断当前价格是偏高、偏低还是处于正常水平。还没准备好购买？点击“追踪价格”，当价格降至你的预算范围内时，你就会收到提醒。
