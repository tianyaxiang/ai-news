# Few-Shot Cross-Dataset Adaptation for Tuberculosis Detection Using DenseNet
# 基于 DenseNet 的肺结核检测少样本跨数据集自适应研究

**Abstract:** Tuberculosis (TB) is one of the most common and dangerous bacterial ailments. Every year, it causes a large number of deaths worldwide. Although many deep learning models can detect tuberculosis from chest X-rays quite accurately, severe domain shift across datasets makes the task challenging. Different imaging protocols, patient demographics, and equipment across domains make the task of generalization difficult. In real-world settings, a model may perform well on one dataset but show a noticeable drop in performance when tested on another.

**摘要：** 肺结核（TB）是最常见且危险的细菌性疾病之一，每年在全球范围内导致大量死亡。尽管许多深度学习模型能够相当准确地从胸部 X 光片中检测出肺结核，但数据集之间严重的领域偏移（domain shift）使得该任务极具挑战性。不同领域间成像协议、患者人口统计学特征以及医疗设备的差异，增加了模型泛化的难度。在实际应用中，模型可能在一个数据集上表现良好，但在测试另一个数据集时性能却出现显著下降。

In this work, we address this domain adaptation challenge through a few-shot scaling study. A controlled cross-dataset evaluation is presented in this paper using TBX11K as the source domain and the Mendeley TB dataset as the target domain. It is investigated how varying the number of target samples affects model performance under three training regimes: frozen backbone adaptation, full fine-tuning of a source-pretrained DenseNet121 model, and training from scratch.

在这项工作中，我们通过少样本缩放研究来应对这一领域自适应挑战。本文使用 TBX11K 作为源领域，Mendeley TB 数据集作为目标领域，进行了一项受控的跨数据集评估。研究探讨了在三种训练模式下，目标样本数量的变化如何影响模型性能：冻结主干网络自适应、对源预训练的 DenseNet121 模型进行全参数微调，以及从零开始训练。

The results indicate that the model can perform well even with limited data and can achieve 98.36% accuracy with just 75 labeled samples per class. The adaptation curves demonstrate how fine-tuning effectively mitigates domain shift. These findings establish full fine-tuning of pretrained models as a highly effective and practical strategy for mitigating domain shift in low-resource clinical deployment scenarios.

结果表明，即使在数据有限的情况下，模型也能表现良好，且在每个类别仅有 75 个标记样本时即可达到 98.36% 的准确率。自适应曲线展示了微调如何有效缓解领域偏移。这些发现确立了对预训练模型进行全参数微调是一种在资源受限的临床部署场景中缓解领域偏移的高效且实用的策略。
