# MNT Station - A modular, open hardware desktop computer and server
# MNT Station - 一款模块化、开源硬件台式机与服务器

MNT Station is a quiet and space-saving, future-proof open hardware computer for your desktop, homelab, or mounted on a wall as an edge device/router. It’s also a robust enclosure for your existing MNT Reform mainboard and can be turned into a standalone desktop PC, silent home NAS, or low-power entertainment center. As usual, this MNT Research device is fully open source hardware with all sources publicly available. As a modular, general-purpose computing platform, MNT Station is the most flexible MNT device yet designed!

MNT Station 是一款安静、节省空间且面向未来的开源硬件计算机，适用于桌面办公、家庭实验室，或作为边缘设备/路由器挂载在墙上。它同时也是您现有 MNT Reform 主板的坚固外壳，可以将其转变为独立的台式机、静音家庭 NAS 或低功耗娱乐中心。与往常一样，这款 MNT Research 设备是完全的开源硬件，所有源代码均公开可用。作为一款模块化的通用计算平台，MNT Station 是迄今为止设计最灵活的 MNT 设备！

### Your Next Desktop Computer, Router, or Server
### 您下一台台式机、路由器或服务器

Tired of your laptop taking up more space on your desk than it should? Of all the plugging and unplugging because you’ve been using it as a portable and as a stationary device? With MNT Station you get a machine that’s designed to be stationary while maintaining the energy efficiency of a laptop. Connect a monitor and a keyboard, hook it up with a USB Type-C cable for power delivery, and you’re good to go!

厌倦了笔记本电脑占用过多的桌面空间？厌倦了因为在便携和固定设备之间切换而频繁插拔线缆？MNT Station 为您提供了一台专为固定使用而设计，同时保持笔记本电脑能效比的机器。只需连接显示器和键盘，通过 USB Type-C 线缆供电，即可立即使用！

MNT Station has mounting holes on the back (standard VESA as well as keyhole slots) to give you the opportunity to install it on your wall, behind a cabinet, or on the back of a monitor. Its external antenna mounts ensures strong radio reception for Wi-Fi or SDRs, no matter where MNT Station is placed.

MNT Station 背面设有安装孔（标准 VESA 以及挂孔），让您可以将其安装在墙上、柜子后方或显示器背面。其外部天线接口确保了 Wi-Fi 或 SDR 设备无论放置在何处都能获得强大的无线信号接收能力。

Love tinkering with Docker containers, Tailnets, or Home Assistant? Add a flexible and uniquely open ARM-based server to your homelab with MNT Station. Test and develop for a broad range of ARM and other SoCs that fit the modular mainboard (including NXP, Amlogic, Qualcomm, Broadcom (Raspberry Pi) or even an AMD/Xilinx FPGA). And thanks to its native charging and battery monitoring technology, MNT Station has battery backup built-in, running for hours off of 8 LiFEPO4 cells, giving you ample time to shut down in case of a power outage. Or, underneath your TV set, MNT Station can pull duty as a streaming box and retro game console, thanks to its low-power design that doesn’t need ventilation.

喜欢折腾 Docker 容器、Tailnets 或 Home Assistant？通过 MNT Station 为您的家庭实验室添加一台灵活且独特的开源 ARM 服务器。您可以为适配该模块化主板的各种 ARM 及其他 SoC（包括 NXP、Amlogic、Qualcomm、Broadcom (树莓派) 甚至 AMD/Xilinx FPGA）进行测试和开发。得益于其原生的充电和电池监控技术，MNT Station 内置了电池备份，依靠 8 节 LiFePO4 电池可运行数小时，在断电时为您提供充足的关机时间。此外，得益于无需通风的低功耗设计，MNT Station 还可以放在电视机下方，充当流媒体盒子和复古游戏机。

### Features & Specifications
### 功能与规格

MNT Station is a versatile device: Upgrade your MNT Reform laptop to the latest mainboard version and use your previous mainboard with MNT Station. Or, build your own Station choosing the MNT Reform Mainboard 3.0, a processor module of your choice, the MNT Reform Standalone Keyboard, and more. It includes aluminum case parts, 1x electronic port cover with cable (JST PH 4-pin; JST PH 4-pin), 1x non-electronic port cover, and M2x5 mounting screws.

MNT Station 是一款多功能设备：您可以将 MNT Reform 笔记本电脑升级到最新的主板版本，并将旧主板用于 MNT Station。或者，您也可以选择 MNT Reform 3.0 主板、您心仪的处理器模块、MNT Reform 独立键盘等组件来组装属于您自己的 Station。它包含铝制外壳部件、1 个带线缆的电子接口盖（JST PH 4-pin）、1 个非电子接口盖以及 M2x5 安装螺丝。

### MNT Station case features
### MNT Station 外壳特性

*   Fully CNC milled enclosure from anodized, bead blasted aluminum
*   Open design, so you can 3D print or order new case parts yourself
*   Can lay flat or stand on the long edge, or be mounted to VESA arms/screens or walls
*   Compatible with all classic MNT Reform mainboard versions (2.0, 2.5, 3.0)
*   Electronic port cover with power button, ATTiny microcontroller, and a bar of 10 LEDs (controllable from Linux)
*   2x holes for SMA antenna panel mount connectors
*   2x wall mount keyhole slots in the back
*   VESA mounting holes in the back (75 x 75 mm and 100 x 100 mm)
*   Dimensions: 279 x 133 x 26 mm

*   采用阳极氧化喷砂铝材经 CNC 全铣削加工的外壳
*   开放式设计，您可以自行 3D 打印或订购新的外壳部件
*   可平放、侧立，或安装在 VESA 支架/显示器及墙面上
*   兼容所有经典 MNT Reform 主板版本（2.0、2.5、3.0）
*   电子接口盖配有电源按钮、ATTiny 微控制器和 10 个 LED 灯条（可通过 Linux 控制）
*   2 个用于 SMA 天线面板安装的接口孔
*   背面设有 2 个壁挂式挂孔
*   背面设有 VESA 安装孔（75 x 75 mm 和 100 x 100 mm）
*   尺寸：279 x 133 x 26 毫米
