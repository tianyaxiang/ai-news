# Apple introduces M6 and M5 Ultra
# 苹果发布 M6 与 M5 Ultra 芯片

**PRESS RELEASE August 25, 2026**
**新闻稿 2026年8月25日**

Apple introduces M6 and M5 Ultra for a big leap in performance and AI compute. M6, Apple’s first 2 nm chip, features a larger, more powerful 12-core CPU, 12-core GPU, and Dual 16-core Neural Engine, while M5 Ultra is Apple’s first quad-die architecture and its most powerful chip ever. M6 delivers a revolutionary leap in everyday performance and power efficiency. M5 Ultra unleashes unprecedented desktop-class power and massive unified memory bandwidth to conquer the most demanding projects.
苹果公司今日发布 M6 与 M5 Ultra 芯片，在性能与 AI 计算领域实现重大飞跃。M6 是苹果首款 2 纳米芯片，配备了更大、更强大的 12 核 CPU、12 核 GPU 以及双 16 核神经网络引擎；而 M5 Ultra 则是苹果首款采用四芯片（quad-die）架构的芯片，也是其迄今为止最强大的芯片。M6 在日常性能和能效方面实现了革命性的跨越，而 M5 Ultra 则释放了前所未有的桌面级性能与海量统一内存带宽，足以应对最严苛的项目需求。

**CUPERTINO, CALIFORNIA** Apple today debuted M6 in the new Mac mini and M5 Ultra in the new Mac Studio, providing an extraordinary leap in performance and AI capabilities. M6, Apple’s first state-of-the-art 2-nanometer chip, advances every compute block, delivering gains across every dimension of performance. The chip features a larger 12-core CPU complex with the world’s fastest CPU core, a larger 12-core GPU with Neural Accelerators, a Dual 16-core Neural Engine, and up to 170GB/s of unified memory bandwidth.
**加利福尼亚州库比蒂诺** 苹果公司今日在新款 Mac mini 中搭载 M6 芯片，并在新款 Mac Studio 中搭载 M5 Ultra 芯片，带来了性能与 AI 能力的非凡飞跃。M6 作为苹果首款顶尖的 2 纳米芯片，对每一个计算模块进行了升级，在性能的各个维度上均实现了提升。该芯片拥有更大的 12 核 CPU 集群，配备了全球最快的 CPU 核心、带有神经网络加速器的 12 核 GPU、双 16 核神经网络引擎，以及高达 170GB/s 的统一内存带宽。

M5 Ultra, the ultimate powerhouse for pro and AI workloads, uses next-generation UltraFusion technology to form a quad-die architecture for the first time in an M-series system on a chip (SoC). The chip includes an up-to-36-core CPU and up-to-80-core GPU with a massive 1.2TB/s of unified memory bandwidth, 50 percent more than M3 Ultra. With their advanced technologies, these SoCs deliver extraordinary compute with industry-leading power efficiency, empowering users to do even more on a desktop.
M5 Ultra 是专为专业人士和 AI 工作负载打造的终极动力源，它首次在 M 系列系统芯片（SoC）中利用新一代 UltraFusion 技术构建了四芯片架构。该芯片包含最高 36 核 CPU 和最高 80 核 GPU，并拥有高达 1.2TB/s 的海量统一内存带宽，比 M3 Ultra 提升了 50%。凭借这些先进技术，这些 SoC 提供了卓越的计算能力和行业领先的能效，助力用户在桌面端完成更多任务。

“Today, we’re debuting the next giant leap in performance and AI compute for Apple silicon with the incredibly advanced M6 and the most powerful M-series chip yet, M5 Ultra,” said Sri Santhanam, Apple’s vice president of Silicon Engineering Group. “Built using the cutting-edge 2 nm process, M6 combines a new CPU complex, two additional CPU and GPU cores, a Dual 16-core Neural Engine, and more unified memory bandwidth to power through workloads with amazing energy efficiency. And for the ultimate desktop performance and the ability to run massive AI models, M5 Ultra features a massive GPU, now with Neural Accelerators, and more unified memory bandwidth, pushing the boundaries of what a desktop can do.”
“今天，我们通过极其先进的 M6 和迄今为止最强大的 M 系列芯片 M5 Ultra，开启了 Apple Silicon 在性能和 AI 计算领域的下一个巨大飞跃，”苹果公司芯片工程副总裁 Sri Santhanam 表示。“M6 采用尖端的 2 纳米工艺制造，结合了全新的 CPU 集群、额外增加的 CPU 和 GPU 核心、双 16 核神经网络引擎以及更高的统一内存带宽，能够以惊人的能效处理繁重的工作负载。而为了实现极致的桌面性能并运行庞大的 AI 模型，M5 Ultra 配备了巨大的 GPU，现已加入神经网络加速器，并拥有更大的统一内存带宽，进一步拓展了桌面电脑的性能边界。”

**M6: Optimized Design and Enhanced Performance**
**M6：优化设计与性能增强**

Designed to power the workflows of everyday users, students, developers, AI hobbyists, and enterprises, M6 offers the ideal balance of performance, power efficiency, and on-device AI to effortlessly fly through daily tasks, coding, and creative projects. M6 is built using cutting-edge 2 nm process technology, packing greater transistor density into a smaller die for a major leap in performance and power efficiency.
M6 旨在为日常用户、学生、开发者、AI 爱好者和企业的工作流程提供动力，在性能、能效和端侧 AI 之间实现了理想平衡，能够轻松应对日常任务、编程和创意项目。M6 采用尖端的 2 纳米工艺技术制造，在更小的芯片面积内集成了更高的晶体管密度，从而在性能和能效上实现了重大飞跃。

M6 also introduces a Dual 16-core Neural Engine, providing up to 2x the peak compute over previous generations to make on-device AI workflows run even faster. System frameworks can automatically utilize both engines simultaneously, enabling applications to see faster model execution. Powered by the all-new 2 nm M6, Mac mini provides an incredibly fast and responsive experience for creative workflows in apps like Adobe Photoshop.
M6 还引入了双 16 核神经网络引擎，其峰值计算能力比前代产品提升高达 2 倍，使端侧 AI 工作流程运行速度更快。系统框架可以自动同时调用这两个引擎，从而使应用程序能够实现更快的模型执行速度。得益于全新的 2 纳米 M6 芯片，Mac mini 在 Adobe Photoshop 等创意应用中提供了极其快速且灵敏的体验。

M6 has a brand-new 12-core CPU complex — two more cores than M5 — that consists of 2 super cores, 4 performance cores, and 6 efficiency cores. It delivers the world’s fastest single-threaded performance and up to 1.2x faster multithreaded performance as compared to M5, and up to 2.4x faster than M1. The super cores blaze through single-threaded workloads, the performance cores use less power and join the super cores to run demanding multithreaded workloads, and the efficiency cores handle everyday background tasks — all with industry-leading performance per watt. As a result, demanding CPU tasks such as editing images, compiling code, indexing new files, and running agentic AI workloads are faster than ever.
M6 拥有全新的 12 核 CPU 集群（比 M5 多出两个核心），由 2 个超大核、4 个性能核和 6 个能效核组成。它提供了全球最快的单线程性能，多线程性能比 M5 快 1.2 倍，比 M1 快 2.4 倍。超大核可极速处理单线程工作负载，性能核在降低功耗的同时与超大核协作处理繁重的多线程任务，而能效核则负责处理日常后台任务——所有这些都具备行业领先的能效比。因此，图像编辑、代码编译、新文件索引以及运行智能体（Agentic）AI 工作负载等高负载 CPU 任务比以往任何时候都更快。

**Accelerated GPU and Faster Memory Bandwidth**
**加速的 GPU 与更快的内存带宽**

M6 features a 12-core GPU — two more cores than M5 — with a Neural Accelerator in each core. This design delivers a nearly 30 percent increase in peak GPU compute for AI compared to M5, and more than 8x compared to M1, enabling significantly faster prompt processing when interacting with on-device LLMs. M6 features a powerful, larger 12-core GPU that provides higher geometry rates and updated Dynamic Caching to deliver stunning visuals and fluid frame rates in demanding games like Mixtape.
M6 配备了 12 核 GPU（比 M5 多出两个核心），每个核心均内置神经网络加速器。与 M5 相比，该设计使 AI 的峰值 GPU 计算能力提升了近 30%，与 M1 相比则提升了 8 倍以上，从而在与端侧大语言模型（LLM）交互时，能显著加快提示词处理速度。M6 强大的 12 核 GPU 提供了更高的几何处理速率和更新的动态缓存技术，在《Mixtape》等高负载游戏中呈现出惊人的视觉效果和流畅的帧率。

In addition, M6 offers Apple’s latest advanced graphics capabilities, including updates to the shader core architecture, Dynamic Caching, and hardware-accelerated ray tracing. These technologies combine to deliver stunning visual realism, faster rendering, and higher frame rates for gaming. M6 also has 50 percent increased geometry rates for complex graphics. M6 supports up to 32GB of unified memory to multitask across demanding apps and run LLMs on device for secure and private agentic tasks. It also provides up to 170GB/s of unified memory bandwidth — a 10 percent increase over M5 and a 2.5x increase over M1. With a Dual 16-core Neural Engine, larger GPU with Neural Accelerators, and higher unified memory bandwidth, M6 enables developers to compile code, index files, and run multiple simulators in Xcode with incredible speed.
此外，M6 还提供了苹果最新的先进图形功能，包括着色器核心架构的更新、动态缓存以及硬件加速光线追踪。这些技术相结合，为游戏带来了震撼的视觉真实感、更快的渲染速度和更高的帧率。M6 在处理复杂图形时的几何处理速率也提升了 50%。M6 支持最高 32GB 统一内存，以便在处理高负载应用时进行多任务处理，并在本地运行大语言模型以执行安全私密的智能体任务。它还提供高达 170GB/s 的统一内存带宽，比 M5 提升 10%，比 M1 提升 2.5 倍。凭借双 16 核神经网络引擎、带有神经网络加速器的更大 GPU 以及更高的统一内存带宽，M6 使开发者能够以惊人的速度在 Xcode 中编译代码、索引文件并运行多个模拟器。

**M5 Ultra：专业工作流程的终极动力源**

M5 Ultra, Apple’s most powerful chip ever, is built for pros who need to speed through workloads that demand maximum CPU and GPU performance and unified memory bandwidth, such as complex 3D rendering, visual effects, scientific analysis, and running compute-intensive frontier AI models on device. Mac Studio with M5 Ultra delivers extreme performance and memory bandwidth to edit complex timelines with multiple streams of high-resolution video and effects in Adobe Premiere Pro. M5 Ultra uses UltraFusion to connect two dual-die M5 Max chips to form the quad-die architecture — a first for Apple silicon.
