# Dolly Parton has died

**Dolly Parton, country star, actor and philanthropist, dies aged 80.**
乡村音乐巨星、演员兼慈善家多莉·帕顿（Dolly Parton）去世，享年80岁。

**Songwriter of I Will Always Love You, Jolene and 9 to 5 also invested millions in child literacy, Covid-19 vaccine development and other causes.**
作为《I Will Always Love You》、《Jolene》和《9 to 5》的词曲作者，她还曾为儿童扫盲、新冠疫苗研发及其他事业投入了数百万美元。

Dolly Parton, one of the greatest singer-songwriters in country music who was also much cherished for her philanthropy, has died aged 80. Her death was announced by nephew Brian Seaver on Instagram. “I have imagined the heaviness of this moment but haven’t truly felt it until now,” he said. “It is an honour, an honour that is mixed with absolute pride and great sadness.”
多莉·帕顿是乡村音乐界最伟大的创作歌手之一，也因其慈善事业而备受爱戴，她已于80岁高龄去世。她的死讯由侄子布莱恩·西弗（Brian Seaver）在Instagram上宣布。他说：“我曾想象过这一刻的沉重，但直到现在才真正感受到。这是一种荣誉，一种夹杂着绝对自豪与巨大悲伤的荣誉。”

So gifted that she wrote two of the 20th century’s greatest songs – Jolene and I Will Always Love You – in a single day, Parton’s songwriting was characterised by its vivid storytelling, emotional acuity and melodic strength, all sung with strident clarity. From an eventual catalogue of hundreds of songs, she scored a record 25 US country No 1 singles and 47 albums in the country Top 10. She crossed over into the pop charts with 9 to 5, a US No 1 in 1980; balanced her music career with acclaimed acting, twice earning Golden Globe nominations for her roles; and her Imagination Library literacy scheme donated more than 150m books to children.
帕顿天赋异禀，曾在一日之内写下20世纪最伟大的两首歌曲——《Jolene》和《I Will Always Love You》。她的创作以生动的叙事、敏锐的情感和强劲的旋律为特色，演唱时嗓音清亮有力。在她创作的数百首歌曲中，有25首登上了美国乡村音乐排行榜榜首，创下纪录，并有47张专辑进入乡村音乐前十名。1980年，她凭借《9 to 5》跨界进入流行音乐排行榜并夺得美国榜首；她在音乐事业之余还投身演艺事业，备受赞誉，曾两次获得金球奖提名；此外，她的“想象力图书馆”（Imagination Library）扫盲计划已向儿童捐赠了超过1.5亿本书籍。

This was all done with considerable panache, rhinestoned glamour and towering hairdos, with her immortal assertion regarding her appearance – “it takes a lot of money to look this cheap” – the most quoted example of Parton’s ready wit.
这一切成就都伴随着她独特的风采、水钻装饰的魅力和高耸的发型。她关于自己外表的经典名言——“要看起来这么廉价，得花不少钱”——成为了帕顿机智幽默最常被引用的例子。

Parton was born in poverty in a one-room cabin in Tennessee, the fourth child to a mother who would have 12 children by the age of 35, and a father who was a farmer and construction worker. One of her best-loved songs, Coat of Many Colours, was about her mother creating patchwork clothes; Parton’s first guitar was cobbled together from a mandolin with bass guitar strings. Her first proper guitar was given to her aged eight by her uncle, and she went from singing in church, to appearing on local television aged 10, to cutting her first record aged 13. That year she appeared at the Grand Ole Opry, the concert hall in Nashville at the centre of country music, and her performance was introduced by Johnny Cash. “I’ve always believed things would go well, and I dreamed that they would even before I was in high school,” she later said. “I always wanted to be a star. It just seemed natural to me.”
帕顿出生于田纳西州一间贫困的单间小屋里，是家中的第四个孩子。她的母亲在35岁前生下了12个孩子，父亲是一名农民兼建筑工人。她最受欢迎的歌曲之一《Coat of Many Colours》讲述了母亲为她缝制拼布衣服的故事；帕顿的第一把吉他是用曼陀林和低音吉他弦拼凑而成的。她八岁时，叔叔送了她第一把真正的吉他。她从在教堂唱歌开始，10岁时登上当地电视节目，13岁时录制了第一张唱片。那一年，她在乡村音乐中心纳什维尔的音乐厅“大奥普里”（Grand Ole Opry）登台表演，并由约翰尼·卡什（Johnny Cash）介绍出场。“我一直相信事情会顺利，甚至在高中之前我就梦想着会这样，”她后来回忆道，“我一直想成为明星。这对我来说似乎是很自然的事。”
