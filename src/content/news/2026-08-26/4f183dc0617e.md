# Measuring Gender Representation in Animated Films
# 衡量动画电影中的性别代表性

**Abstract:** Animated films--often developed with an audience of children in mind--are an important vector for enculturation, and empirical work that has examined the representation of gender at scale in these films has largely focused on counting the gender composition of the cast rather than deploying a more fine-grained instrument (such as assessing the visibility of those characters in overall screentime).

**摘要：** 动画电影通常以儿童为受众群体，是文化熏陶的重要载体。此前针对这些电影中性别代表性的大规模实证研究，大多集中于统计演员阵容的性别构成，而非采用更精细的衡量工具（例如评估这些角色在总银幕时间中的可见度）。

In this work, we develop a computational pipeline for recognizing animated characters in these films, and use it to test several hypotheses about gender representation in a corpus of 224 popular animated movies.

在这项工作中，我们开发了一套用于识别动画电影中角色的计算流程，并利用它对 224 部热门动画电影语料库中的性别代表性假设进行了验证。

We find that while the overall representation of female characters in animated films largely tracks with those of live-action films (over the period 1980-2025), we see stark differences between the representation of human characters (much greater representation among women and girls) and non-humans (largely male).

我们发现，虽然动画电影中女性角色的整体代表性在很大程度上与真人电影（1980 年至 2025 年期间）保持一致，但人类角色（女性和女孩的代表性更高）与非人类角色（主要是男性）之间存在显著差异。

Contrary to past work on Disney, we do not see female characters declining in antagonist roles in animated films, and characters who are women and girls are much more likely to share scenes together than their live action contemporaneous counterparts.

与以往针对迪士尼的研究结论相反，我们并未观察到动画电影中女性反派角色的减少；此外，与同时期的真人电影相比，动画中的女性和女孩角色更有可能共同出现在同一场景中。
