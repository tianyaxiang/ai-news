# A New Towards Data Science: A Faster Site and a Brand-New Contributor Portal
# Towards Data Science 的新篇章：更快的网站与全新的投稿门户

**What's new for readers**
**读者有哪些新体验**

We've rebuilt towardsdatascience.com from the ground up, but most of the changes affect the site’s infrastructure. The main positive effect for you is that the new site is faster. We've also moved to our own platform and have full control over every detail you see — unlike before, where WordPress limited what we could do. This means that, going forward, we can ship improvements and new features quickly, and can respond to reader feedback faster, too.
我们从零开始重建了 towardsdatascience.com，但大部分变化集中在网站的基础设施上。对您而言，最直接的积极影响是新网站运行速度更快了。我们还迁移到了自主平台，能够完全掌控您所看到的每一个细节——这与以往受限于 WordPress 的情况大不相同。这意味着在未来，我们能够更迅速地发布改进和新功能，并更快地响应读者的反馈。

Accessing the site remains the same, and your account continues to work exactly as it did before, with the same login and email preferences.
访问网站的方式保持不变，您的账户也将像以前一样正常使用，登录方式和电子邮件偏好设置均保持原样。

One other thing that isn't changing: Our mission and editorial standards stay exactly the same. TDS remains the home for practitioner-written articles on data science, machine learning, and AI.
还有一点不会改变：我们的使命和编辑标准始终如一。TDS 依然是数据科学、机器学习和人工智能领域从业者撰写文章的家园。

**What's new for authors**
**作者有哪些新体验**

Alongside the new site, we're relaunching our dedicated contributor portal at write.insightmediagroup.io. This is our new, cleaner home for everything related to writing and publishing with TDS.
随着新网站的上线，我们重新推出了专属的投稿门户网站：write.insightmediagroup.io。这是我们全新的、更简洁的平台，涵盖了与在 TDS 写作和发布相关的一切事务。

Navigating our WordPress-based publishing platform could get clunky and frustrating, and we’re grateful that our authors were patient and open-minded enough to learn to use it anyway. We believe that uploading and formatting articles in 2026 should be easy and intuitive, and decided it was time to build a publishing experience that reflected that. The new portal is the result.
过去，使用基于 WordPress 的发布平台可能会显得笨重且令人沮丧，我们非常感谢作者们一直以来的耐心与包容。我们认为，在 2026 年，文章的上传与排版应当是简单且直观的，因此我们决定打造一种能够体现这一理念的发布体验。全新的门户网站正是这一努力的成果。

From the portal, you can write and submit your articles directly, and then follow them through every step of the review process.
通过该门户，您可以直接撰写并提交文章，并实时跟踪文章在审核流程中的每一个步骤。

Getting started is simple: Just log in at the portal with your existing credentials. Now that we fully control the platform, our work is really just starting. We have lots of ideas to explore and new features to deploy, and your feedback will help us shape what we launch next.
上手非常简单：只需使用您现有的凭据登录门户即可。现在我们已经完全掌控了平台，我们的工作才刚刚开始。我们有许多想法等待探索，也有许多新功能等待部署，您的反馈将帮助我们决定下一步的开发方向。

If you've been thinking about writing for TDS, there's never been a better time to start. Submit your first article here.
如果您一直考虑为 TDS 撰稿，现在正是最好的时机。点击此处提交您的第一篇文章。

**A work in progress**
**持续改进中**

We'll keep improving both the site and the portal in the weeks and months ahead. If you spot something that looks off, or have an idea that would make the experience better, we'd love to hear from you at publication@towardsdatascience.com.
在接下来的几周和几个月里，我们将持续改进网站和门户。如果您发现任何异常，或者有任何能提升体验的建议，欢迎通过 publication@towardsdatascience.com 与我们联系。

Thank you for reading, writing, and being part of this community.
感谢您的阅读、写作，并成为这个社区的一员。
