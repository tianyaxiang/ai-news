# Claude Cowork finally remembers what you told the app in chat
# Claude Cowork 终于能记住你在聊天中告诉过它的内容了

A change to Claude’s memory system will eliminate one of the most annoying things about using agents — the constant need to rebrief the AI on things it already knows. On Tuesday, Anthropic announced it’s merging the memory system used by chat and Claude Cowork, which means that Claude will always remember what it learned in one area, even when you’re engaging with it in another. Anthropic is also exposing what Claude has retained in its memory, allowing users to read, edit, or delete information on any topic.

Claude 记忆系统的更新将消除使用 AI 智能体时最令人烦恼的问题之一——即不断需要向 AI 重复它已经知道的信息。周二，Anthropic 宣布将聊天功能与 Claude Cowork 的记忆系统进行整合，这意味着 Claude 在一个场景中学到的内容，即使你在另一个场景中与它互动时，它也能始终记住。此外，Anthropic 还将向用户展示 Claude 记忆中保留的内容，允许用户阅读、编辑或删除任何主题的信息。

The update will improve the experience for Claude’s users as it will allow them to more seamlessly move between the research phase and taking action. Before, you may have developed ideas for projects over time while chatting with Claude, but when it came time to actually enact the changes, you’d have to start over by telling Cowork all the details. This was incredibly frustrating. The update makes Claude now feel like one continuous assistant, rather than two separate products under one roof.

此次更新将改善 Claude 用户的体验，使他们能够更无缝地在研究阶段和执行阶段之间切换。此前，你可能在与 Claude 的长期聊天中构思了项目方案，但当需要真正实施这些变更时，你不得不重新向 Cowork 详细说明所有细节。这非常令人沮丧。此次更新让 Claude 现在感觉像是一个连续的助手，而不是同一屋檐下的两个独立产品。

Anthropic offers an example of someone needing to draft an update for their manager or an agenda for a conference, where Cowork would already understand the references because of the past conversations about the matter at hand. For instance, it would know the headcount, city, and speakers at the conference, so you wouldn’t have to re-input that information.

Anthropic 举了一个例子：当用户需要为经理起草一份更新报告或为会议制定议程时，Cowork 会因为之前关于该事项的对话而直接理解其中的背景信息。例如，它会知道会议的人数、地点和演讲者，因此你无需再次输入这些信息。

In addition, Claude will now add topics to its memory as you chat, instead of summarizing a conversation when it ends. That makes it easier to move back and forth between chat and Cowork, as the new memories are more quickly updated and shared between the two experiences, even if the chat is ongoing.

此外，Claude 现在会在你聊天时实时将主题添加到记忆中，而不是等到对话结束时才进行总结。这使得在聊天和 Cowork 之间切换变得更加容易，因为新的记忆可以更快地在两种体验之间更新和共享，即使对话仍在进行中。

Anthropic says that, by default, Claude won’t store personal or sensitive information like your health data, race, ethnicity, religious beliefs, politics, gender identity, and more. However, users can opt to enable these things by toggling on “include sensitive topics in memory” if they prefer. The app will also notify users whenever a sensitive topic is saved in memory. However, the company says that Claude won’t ever save certain things, like government-issued ID and Social Security numbers, criminal history, immigration status, or other things that violate its acceptable use policy.

Anthropic 表示，默认情况下，Claude 不会存储个人或敏感信息，如健康数据、种族、民族、宗教信仰、政治倾向、性别认同等。不过，如果用户愿意，可以通过开启“在记忆中包含敏感主题”选项来启用这些功能。每当敏感主题被保存到记忆中时，应用程序也会通知用户。但该公司强调，Claude 永远不会保存某些特定信息，如政府签发的身份证件和社保号码、犯罪记录、移民身份或其他违反其可接受使用政策的内容。

The memory feature is enabled by default on Free, Pro, and Max plans across web, desktop, and mobile. On iOS and Android, users will need to update their app to the latest version.

该记忆功能在网页版、桌面版和移动版的 Free、Pro 和 Max 计划中默认开启。在 iOS 和 Android 设备上，用户需要将应用程序更新至最新版本。
