# Gamescom Opening Night Live 2026: The biggest announcements and trailers
# 2026 年科隆游戏展（Gamescom）开幕之夜：重磅公告与预告片汇总

The Geoff Keighley-hosted Opening Night Live event at Gamescom just wrapped up. Arguably the biggest announcement was the closer: a remastered version of The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt from CD Projekt Red that’s launching in September. But the show also featured a bunch of trailers for others games, including Gears of War: E-Day, Capcom’s upcoming Mega Man sidescroller, and Final Fantasy VII Revelation.

由 Geoff Keighley 主持的科隆游戏展“开幕之夜”（Opening Night Live）刚刚落下帷幕。本次活动最重磅的消息莫过于压轴环节：CD Projekt Red 宣布《巫师 3：狂猎》重制版将于 9 月正式推出。此外，展会还发布了多款游戏的预告片，包括《战争机器：E-Day》、《洛克人》系列新作，以及《最终幻想 VII：启示录》。

The event took place ahead of a potentially very big fall for the games industry. Grand Theft Auto VI is scheduled to release on November 19th, and the rest of the games industry is largely steering clear of GTA VI’s launch. That means the next several weeks are packed with major releases — and the calendar just got even busier with the Witcher 3 remaster. GTA VI wasn’t shown, but that’s not really a surprise: that game is set to have a reveal event of its own on Thursday.

本次活动正值游戏行业秋季大爆发前夕。《侠盗猎车手 6》（GTA VI）定于 11 月 19 日发售，游戏界其他厂商大多选择避开这一档期。这意味着接下来的几周将迎来大量重磅作品，而《巫师 3》重制版的加入让日程表变得更加拥挤。虽然本次活动未展示《GTA VI》，但这并不令人意外，因为该游戏已定于本周四举办专属发布会。

***

### The Witcher 3 is getting a remaster
### 《巫师 3》迎来重制版

CD Projekt Red is remastering The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt, the hit RPG that first launched in 2015, and the updated game will be available starting September 29th. The Witcher 3: Wild Hunt — Remastered will bring improvements to visuals and combat, a “reimagined” skill tree, and more, and it also includes the first two expansions, Hearts of Stone and Blood and Wine.

CD Projekt Red 正在对 2015 年发行的热门 RPG《巫师 3：狂猎》进行重制，更新后的版本将于 9 月 29 日上线。《巫师 3：狂猎——重制版》将带来视觉效果和战斗系统的改进、“重构”的技能树等内容，并包含《石之心》和《血与酒》两部扩展包。

Remastered will be a free upgrade for players who own the base game on GOG, Steam, Epic Games Store, PlayStation 5, and Xbox Series X / S. With the launch of Remastered, The Witches 3 will also be coming to the Nintendo Switch 2.

对于在 GOG、Steam、Epic Games Store、PlayStation 5 和 Xbox Series X/S 上拥有原版游戏的玩家，重制版将提供免费升级。随着重制版的发布，《巫师 3》也将正式登陆任天堂 Switch 2 平台。

***

### Watch out for Weapons.
### 小心“武器”（Weapons）

In a new trailer for Final Fantasy VII Revelation, Square Enix showed off footage of the gargantuan Ruby, Emerald, and Ultima Weapons. The game is set to launch in spring 2027 on Nintendo Switch 2, PC, PS5, and Xbox Series X / S.

在《最终幻想 VII：启示录》的新预告片中，Square Enix 展示了巨大的红宝石、绿宝石和终极武器（Weapons）的实机画面。该游戏定于 2027 年春季在 Switch 2、PC、PS5 和 Xbox Series X/S 上发售。

***

### The new game from the creator of Inscryption launches next year.
### 《邪恶冥刻》制作人的新作将于明年发布

Daniel Mullins’ bizarre-looking (complimentary) Pony Island 2: Panda Circus launches on April 27th, 2027. That gives you plenty of time to catch up on the original Pony Island.

Daniel Mullins 制作的画风诡异（褒义）的《小马岛 2：熊猫马戏团》（Pony Island 2: Panda Circus）将于 2027 年 4 月 27 日发售。这给了你充足的时间去补完初代《小马岛》。

***

### Path of Exile II’s 1.0 launch is set for December 11th.
### 《流放之路 2》1.0 版本定档 12 月 11 日

The dungeon crawler launched in early access in December 2024, and the 1.0 version is nearly here. When 1.0 hits, the game will go free to play.

这款地牢探险游戏于 2024 年 12 月开启抢先体验，目前 1.0 正式版即将到来。当 1.0 版本上线时，游戏将转为免费游玩模式。

### Building a city brick by brick.
### 一砖一瓦筑城

Lego Skylines is a new city builder from Cities: Skylines publisher Paradox Interactive, and it seems like it’s got a good mix of traditional city management and that signature Lego whimsy. This is one we at The Verge will be very excited to check out.

《乐高：天际线》（Lego Skylines）是《城市：天际线》发行商 Paradox Interactive 推出的全新城市建造游戏。它似乎很好地融合了传统的城市管理玩法与乐高标志性的奇思妙想。我们 The Verge 编辑部对此非常期待。

### Crazy Taxis.
### 疯狂出租车

Sega just showed off multiplayer for its Crazy Taxi reboot in a new trailer. It sure looks like Crazy Taxi — which isn’t a bad thing, IMO. The game is set to launch in 2027.

世嘉在最新的预告片中展示了《疯狂出租车》重启版的多人模式。在我看来，它看起来确实很有《疯狂出租车》的味道——这绝对是件好事。该游戏定于 2027 年发售。

### Mewgenics is coming to PS5, Switch 2, and Xbox Series X / S.
### 《Mewgenics》将登陆 PS5、Switch 2 和 Xbox Series X/S

The cat-themed strategy roguelike hits consoles on September 8th. It launched on PC earlier this year, and I highly recommend reading our interview with developer Edmund McMillen. Opening Night Live also featured a musical performance of a song from the game. It ruled.

这款以猫为主题的策略 Roguelike 游戏将于 9 月 8 日登陆主机平台。它已于今年早些时候在 PC 上发布，我强烈建议阅读我们对开发者 Edmund McMillen 的采访。开幕之夜活动中还表演了游戏中的一首曲目，非常棒。

### Proudly made in Ukraine.
### 乌克兰制造

Ukraine-based 4A Games showed off a short, gritty gameplay trailer for Metro 2039, its latest first-person shooter in the long-running series. It’ll debut February 2027 for the PlayStation 5, Xbox Series X/S, and on PC via Steam and the Epic Games Store.

总部位于乌克兰的 4A Games 展示了《地铁 2039》的简短且硬核的实机预告片，这是该长青系列最新的第一人称射击游戏。它将于 2027 年 2 月在 PS5、Xbox Series X/S 以及 PC（Steam 和 Epic Games Store）上首发。

### Agent 47… smoke weed every day.
### 47 号特工……每天都要嗨

The Doggfather comes to Hitman: World of Assassination. Noted gamer Snoop Dogg is hitting ’em in more ways than one as the next celebrity elusive target in “The Herbalist,” which you can play for free starting August 27th.

“狗爷”Snoop Dogg 加入《杀手：暗杀世界》。作为知名玩家，Snoop Dogg 将作为下一个名人“难以捉摸的目标”出现在“草药师”（The Herbalist）任务中，该任务将于 8 月 27 日起免费开放。

### Lots of gameplay from a new D&D game.
### 全新《龙与地下城》游戏实机演示

Unlike Baldur’s Gate 3, Warlock: Dungeons & Dragons is a more action-focused title — as shown in a new trailer, it appears to take inspiration from games like Jedi: Fallen Order and Control. It’s launching in 2027.

与《博德之门 3》不同，《术士：龙与地下城》（Warlock: Dungeons & Dragons）是一款更侧重动作的游戏。从新预告片来看，它似乎从《星球大战绝地：陨落的武士团》和《控制》等游戏中汲取了灵感。该作将于 2027 年发售。

### PUBG gets a little edgelord with it.
### 《绝地求生》变得有点“暗黑”了

PUBG: Ded.Net is a new multiplayer FPS with roguelite progression… and also a very in-your-face vibe. I love PUBG, but this announcement trailer was a lot to take in. We’ll see if it’s as satisfying to play Ded.Net as it was to get a chicken dinner back in the day.

《PUBG: Ded.Net》是一款带有 Roguelite 进阶要素的多人 FPS 游戏……风格非常激进。我喜欢《绝地求生》，但这个预告片的信息量实在太大。我们拭目以待，看看玩《Ded.Net》是否能像当年“大吉大利，今晚吃鸡”那样令人满足。
