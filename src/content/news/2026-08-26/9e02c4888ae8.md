# AI Visual Inspection for Garment Production
# 服装生产中的人工智能视觉检测

**Abstract:** The garment manufacturing industry is under increasing pressure to improve product quality, reduce costs, and accelerate digital transformation toward Industry 4.0. One of the most challenging quality-control activities is sewing-line inspection, where defects such as broken stitches and skipped stitches are difficult to detect consistently through manual inspection. Human-based inspection is often affected by fatigue, subjective judgement, and inconsistent performance, resulting in defect leakage, rework, and reduced production efficiency.

**摘要：** 服装制造业正面临着提高产品质量、降低成本以及加速向工业 4.0 数字化转型的巨大压力。缝纫线检测是质量控制中最具挑战性的环节之一，断线和跳针等缺陷很难通过人工检测实现一致性识别。人工检测往往受到疲劳、主观判断和表现不稳定的影响，导致缺陷漏检、返工以及生产效率下降。

This study presents the development and validation of an Artificial Intelligence (AI)-based visual inspection system for garment sewing-line quality control. The system utilizes Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) to detect sewing defects and was initially trained using black fabric and black sewing thread samples.

本研究提出并验证了一种用于服装缝纫线质量控制的人工智能（AI）视觉检测系统。该系统利用卷积神经网络（CNN）来检测缝纫缺陷，并最初使用黑色面料和黑色缝纫线样本进行了训练。

Experimental testing was conducted on black, red, dark green, light blue, silver, and fluorescent yellow fabrics. The results demonstrated successful detection of jump sewing-line defects on black, red, and dark green materials, while performance limitations were observed for broken sewing-line defects and fabrics with significantly different visual characteristics, including light blue, silver, and fluorescent yellow colours. These findings indicate that model accuracy is strongly influenced by the diversity of training data and the ability to generalize across different fabric and thread colours.

实验测试涵盖了黑色、红色、深绿色、浅蓝色、银色和荧光黄色面料。结果表明，该系统能够成功检测出黑色、红色和深绿色材料上的跳针缺陷；但在检测断线缺陷以及视觉特征差异显著的面料（如浅蓝色、银色和荧光黄色）时，表现出了一定的局限性。这些发现表明，模型的准确性在很大程度上受到训练数据多样性以及在不同面料和线色之间泛化能力的影响。
