# At just $8, you owe it to yourself to grab Inscryption
# 只需 8 美元，你真的应该入手《邪恶冥刻》(Inscryption)

If you watched the Gamescom 2026 announcements, you might have caught a trailer for the bizarre, frenetic Pony Island 2: Panda Circus, a game from Daniel Mullins. In case you haven’t heard of him, get a crash course with a fantastic discount on Inscryption. Humble is currently selling Steam versions of the secret-packed deckbuilder for $7.99, a sizable discount from its usual price of $20. It’s compatible with Windows, macOS, and Linux machines.

如果你观看了 2026 年科隆游戏展（Gamescom 2026）的发布会，可能已经注意到了 Daniel Mullins 那款怪诞且狂野的游戏《小马岛 2：熊猫马戏团》(Pony Island 2: Panda Circus) 的预告片。如果你还没听说过这位开发者，现在正是通过《邪恶冥刻》(Inscryption) 的超值折扣来补课的好机会。Humble 目前正在以 7.99 美元的价格出售这款充满秘密的卡牌构建游戏（Steam 版），相比其 20 美元的原价，折扣力度相当大。该游戏兼容 Windows、macOS 和 Linux 系统。

The game starts out as a fairly straightforward deckbuilding roguelike game with a dark atmosphere. Stick with it, and it’ll evolve, defying genres, breaking the fourth wall, and leading you through a twisted story with plenty of surprises. Part of the fun is discovering those twists yourself, so I won’t say anything else. It’s one of my personal favorite games, and it’s best to go in knowing as little as possible. Thankfully, taking the leap isn’t expensive at all.

这款游戏起初看起来是一款氛围阴暗、中规中矩的卡牌构建 Roguelike 游戏。但只要你坚持玩下去，它就会不断进化，打破类型限制，甚至打破“第四面墙”，带你进入一段充满惊喜的扭曲故事中。亲自发掘这些反转正是乐趣所在，所以我不会再多透露什么。这是我个人最喜欢的游戏之一，最好是在知之甚少的情况下开始体验。值得庆幸的是，入手的门槛一点也不高。
