# New Mac Studio with M5 Max and M5 Ultra
# 全新搭载 M5 Max 与 M5 Ultra 芯片的 Mac Studio

**PRESS RELEASE August 25, 2026**
**新闻稿 2026 年 8 月 25 日**

Apple introduces new Mac Studio with M5 Max and M5 Ultra — the ultimate desktop for on‑device AI and the most extreme pro workflows. Apple’s most powerful Mac raises the bar for local AI with up to 4.3x faster performance, more advanced graphics, up to 512GB of unified memory, and extensive connectivity. Apple unveils the new Mac Studio, the most powerful Mac ever, featuring M5 Max and M5 Ultra.
Apple 推出搭载 M5 Max 和 M5 Ultra 的全新 Mac Studio——这是专为端侧 AI 和极致专业工作流打造的终极台式机。作为 Apple 迄今最强大的 Mac，它将本地 AI 性能提升到了新高度，速度最高提升至 4.3 倍，配备更先进的图形处理能力、最高 512GB 统一内存以及极其丰富的连接性。Apple 正式发布了史上最强大的 Mac：全新 Mac Studio，搭载 M5 Max 和 M5 Ultra 芯片。

**CUPERTINO, CALIFORNIA** Apple today announced the new Mac Studio, featuring M5 Max and the all-new M5 Ultra, delivering a monumental leap in AI performance and even faster graphics for the most demanding pro workflows, all in its signature compact design that lives right on a user’s desk. Now featuring up to 4.3x faster AI performance, up to 2x faster storage, up to 1.8x faster graphics, and up to 1.3x faster CPU speed, along with higher memory bandwidth, Mac Studio empowers creatives, developers, AI researchers, data scientists, and more to push the boundaries of what they can do.
**加利福尼亚州库比蒂诺** Apple 今日宣布推出全新 Mac Studio，搭载 M5 Max 和全新的 M5 Ultra 芯片。这款产品在标志性的紧凑机身设计中，为最严苛的专业工作流带来了 AI 性能的巨大飞跃和更快的图形处理速度。凭借最高 4.3 倍的 AI 性能提升、最高 2 倍的存储速度、最高 1.8 倍的图形性能以及最高 1.3 倍的 CPU 速度，再加上更高的内存带宽，Mac Studio 将助力创意人士、开发者、AI 研究人员、数据科学家等突破创作边界。

Mac Studio with M5 Max features an 18-core CPU, an up-to-40-core GPU with Neural Accelerators built into each core, and up to 128GB of unified memory, accelerating complex pro and AI workloads. With the powerful M5 Ultra, Mac Studio scales up to a 36-core CPU, up to an 80-core GPU, and a staggering 512GB of unified memory, enabling users to run enormous LLMs entirely on device.
搭载 M5 Max 的 Mac Studio 配备 18 核 CPU、最高 40 核 GPU（每个核心均内置神经加速器）以及最高 128GB 统一内存，可加速复杂的专业和 AI 工作负载。而强大的 M5 Ultra 则将 Mac Studio 的性能提升至最高 36 核 CPU、最高 80 核 GPU 以及惊人的 512GB 统一内存，使用户能够在设备上直接运行超大规模语言模型（LLM）。

Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6 come to Mac Studio for the first time, while Thunderbolt 5 rounds out its extensive connectivity, so users can take advantage of blazing-fast external storage, PCIe expansion chassis, and powerful hub solutions for the most intense workloads. Thunderbolt 5 also enables multiple Mac Studio systems to be clustered, bringing up to 3x faster performance for distributed AI inference when compared to a single system. Together with Studio Display and Studio Display XDR, along with the power of macOS 27 and the next generation of Apple Intelligence, including Siri AI, it is the ultimate pro desktop.
Mac Studio 首次引入 Wi-Fi 7 和蓝牙 6，并配备 Thunderbolt 5 接口，完善了其丰富的连接性。用户可以利用极速外置存储、PCIe 扩展机箱和强大的集线器解决方案来应对最繁重的工作负载。Thunderbolt 5 还支持将多台 Mac Studio 系统集群，与单机相比，分布式 AI 推理性能最高可提升 3 倍。配合 Studio Display 和 Studio Display XDR，以及 macOS 27 和包括 Siri AI 在内的下一代 Apple Intelligence 的强大功能，它堪称终极专业台式机。

The new Mac Studio is available for pre-order starting today, with availability beginning September 22.
全新 Mac Studio 即日起开启预购，并将于 9 月 22 日正式发售。

“Mac Studio is the ultimate desktop for on-device AI and the world’s most demanding pro workflows, relied on by users for its tremendous performance and extensive pro connectivity, all in a quiet, compact design that sits right on your desk — and today, we’re pushing the boundaries even further,” said Johny Srouji, Apple’s chief hardware officer. “With the powerful M5 Max and the incredible capabilities of M5 Ultra, Mac Studio ushers in a new era of desktop computing, delivering huge performance gains for pro workloads and AI inference with frontier-class models. By integrating Neural Accelerators directly into the GPU and offering massive amounts of high-bandwidth unified memory, the new Mac Studio is our most powerful Mac ever.”
“Mac Studio 是端侧 AI 和全球最严苛专业工作流的终极台式机。用户信赖它是因为其卓越的性能和丰富的专业连接性，且这一切都集成在安静、紧凑的机身中，完美适配桌面环境。今天，我们进一步突破了极限，”Apple 硬件工程高级副总裁 Johny Srouji 表示，“凭借强大的 M5 Max 和 M5 Ultra 的惊人能力，Mac Studio 开启了台式计算的新纪元，为专业工作负载和前沿模型的 AI 推理带来了巨大的性能提升。通过将神经加速器直接集成到 GPU 中，并提供海量的高带宽统一内存，全新 Mac Studio 成为了我们史上最强大的 Mac。”

A Monumental Step for AI
AI 领域的里程碑

Mac Studio is at the forefront of high-performance AI computing. Now, with M5 Max and M5 Ultra — Apple’s most powerful silicon ever — it’s turbocharged, putting frontier-class AI models right on a user’s desk. Neural Accelerators in each GPU core deliver dramatically faster matrix multiplication. M5 Max features phenomenal on-device AI compute with up to 3.9x faster AI performance than the prior generation, speeding up prompt processing. With M5 Ultra, Mac Studio achieves up to 4.3x the peak AI compute performance of M3 Ultra and a staggering 9.8x more than M1 Ultra.
Mac Studio 处于高性能 AI 计算的最前沿。现在，搭载 Apple 史上最强芯片 M5 Max 和 M5 Ultra，它如虎添翼，将前沿 AI 模型带到了用户的桌面上。每个 GPU 核心中的神经加速器提供了显著更快的矩阵乘法运算。M5 Max 具备卓越的端侧 AI 计算能力，AI 性能比上一代提升最高 3.9 倍，大幅加快了提示词处理速度。搭载 M5 Ultra 的 Mac Studio，其峰值 AI 计算性能最高可达 M3 Ultra 的 4.3 倍，更是 M1 Ultra 的 9.8 倍。

Combined with up to 512GB of unified memory and 1.2TB/s of memory bandwidth, 50 percent higher than before, Mac Studio lets users run massive models entirely on device with complete privacy — without counting tokens or worrying about rising cloud costs. AI inference scales to entirely new levels with multiple Mac Studio systems. Users and teams looking to share AI compute can cluster multiple Mac Studio systems together using the built-in support for Thunderbolt 5 and RDMA (remote direct memory access). This creates a vast shared memory pool across systems, allowing users to load the largest and most demanding frontier-class open-weight models available today. A cluster of four Mac Studio systems delivers up to a remarkable 3x faster AI inference than a single system.
结合最高 512GB 的统一内存和 1.2TB/s 的内存带宽（比以往提升 50%），Mac Studio 让用户能够在完全保护隐私的前提下，在设备上运行超大规模模型，无需计算 Token 消耗，也无需担心云端成本上涨。通过多台 Mac Studio 系统，AI 推理能力可扩展至全新高度。需要共享 AI 计算资源的用户和团队，可以利用内置的 Thunderbolt 5 和 RDMA（远程直接内存访问）支持，将多台 Mac Studio 集群。这在系统间创建了一个巨大的共享内存池，使用户能够加载当今最大、最严苛的前沿开源模型。四台 Mac Studio 组成的集群，其 AI 推理速度比单机最高可提升 3 倍。

Mac Studio is also a powerful platform for the rich ecosystem of tools AI researchers and developers rely on every day, utilizing the advanced frameworks in macOS. Core AI is a brand-new framework for building, running, and deploying AI models on Apple silicon. It provides an architecture optimized for Apple silicon, including unified memory, CPU, GPU, and Neural Engine, allowing developers to deploy full-scale LLMs locally and bring their own custom models into their apps. MLX, Apple’s open-source machine learning framework optimized for Apple silicon, enables developers to run, train, and fine-tune models with exceptional efficiency on Mac.
Mac Studio 也是 AI 研究人员和开发者日常依赖的丰富工具生态的强大平台，充分利用了 macOS 中的先进框架。Core AI 是一个全新的框架，用于在 Apple 芯片上构建、运行和部署 AI 模型。它为 Apple 芯片（包括统一内存、CPU、GPU 和神经网络引擎）提供了优化架构，使开发者能够在本地部署全尺寸 LLM，并将自定义模型集成到应用中。MLX 是 Apple 为 Apple 芯片优化的开源机器学习框架，使开发者能够在 Mac 上以极高的效率运行、训练和微调模型。

In addition to these powerful frameworks, combined with Xcode and a robust ecosystem of AI tools and solutions, Mac Studio provides a complete, end-to-end platform for AI development — from experimentation and training to deployment. With the new Mac Studio, developers can experience faster build performance in Xcode and utilize on-device coding agents. With the new Mac Studio, users can experience up to 4.3x faster text-to-image performance in apps when compared to the previous generation.
除了这些强大的框架，结合 Xcode 以及稳健的 AI 工具和解决方案生态，Mac Studio 为 AI 开发提供了从实验、训练到部署的完整端到端平台。借助全新 Mac Studio，开发者可以在 Xcode 中体验更快的构建性能，并利用端侧编码助手。此外，用户在应用中进行文生图任务时，性能比上一代最高提升 4.3 倍。
