# Trump says it’s time to rebrand AI with a new name — and he’s also creating an AI Force
# 特朗普称是时候为人工智能改名了——他还将组建一支“人工智能部队”

President Donald Trump has responded in characteristic fashion to the recent debate around AI safety — by declaring that these concerns are part of a long line of hoaxes “generated by the Radical Left Dumocrats, for purposes of destroying our Country.”
唐纳德·特朗普总统以其一贯的风格回应了近期围绕人工智能（AI）安全的辩论——他宣称这些担忧是“激进左翼民主党人为了摧毁我们国家而制造的”一系列骗局的一部分。

On Saturday, Trump began his musings on AI by writing on his social network Truth Social that “many people think that the words ‘Artificial Intelligence’ are inaccurate, and very ineloquent, relative to AI, or Artificial Intelligence.” So he posted a poll where followers could vote on a new name for the technology — Superior Intelligence, Extreme Intelligence, or Supreme Intelligence. (The poll is still ongoing as of publication time)
周六，特朗普在社交网络“真实社交”（Truth Social）上发表了对人工智能的看法，称“许多人认为‘人工智能’（Artificial Intelligence）这个词相对于AI本身来说是不准确且非常不雅的”。因此，他发起了一项民意调查，让粉丝为这项技术投票选出一个新名称——“卓越智能”（Superior Intelligence）、“极端智能”（Extreme Intelligence）或“至高智能”（Supreme Intelligence）。（截至本文发布时，投票仍在进行中。）

A couple hours later, Trump followed up with a post claiming that attempts at “the decimation, or destruction, of AI” are one of “many” Democratic hoaxes, similar to “RUSSIA, RUSSIA, RUSSIA, UKRAINE, UKRAINE, UKRAINE, Global Warming, Impeachment Hoax #1, Impeachment Hoax #2, Men in Women’s Sports, [and] Transgender for Everyone.”
几小时后，特朗普又发布了一篇帖子，声称旨在“削弱或摧毁AI”的企图是民主党“众多”骗局之一，类似于“俄罗斯、俄罗斯、俄罗斯，乌克兰、乌克兰、乌克兰，全球变暖，弹劾骗局1号，弹劾骗局2号，跨性别者参加女子体育比赛，以及全民跨性别化”。

Trump then insisted that he “will not stand by and let this happen,” adding that this so-called hoax “began with an attack on our Data Centers, until people realized how wealthy and prestigious they were for the Communities in which they were built.” But after, in his telling, “the crazed Data Center attack has largely failed,” critics are “going straight at AI.”
特朗普随后坚称他“不会袖手旁观，任由这种情况发生”，并补充说，这个所谓的骗局“始于对我们数据中心的攻击，直到人们意识到这些数据中心对于它们所在的社区来说是多么富有和享有盛誉”。但他表示，在“疯狂的数据中心攻击基本失败”之后，批评者们现在“直接针对AI下手了”。

Trump did not offer any evidence to back up his claim that widespread suspicion of AI and data centers is not organic and sincere. (Both Republicans and Democrats have taken aim at data centers, and New York recently became the first state to halt permits for large projects.) The president’s post also echoes his comments at a golf tournament last weekend, when he said he’s open to “guardrails,” but also argued, “I think you have a lot of negative forces that are bringing it up that shouldn’t be bringing it up.”
特朗普并未提供任何证据来支持他的说法，即公众对AI和数据中心的广泛怀疑并非自发且真诚的。（共和党人和民主党人都曾将矛头指向数据中心，纽约州最近也成为第一个停止大型数据中心项目许可的州。）总统的帖子也呼应了他上周末在高尔夫球赛上的评论，当时他表示对“护栏”（监管措施）持开放态度，但也辩称：“我认为有很多不该提出这些问题的负面力量正在提出这些问题。”

On Saturday, Trump went on to say that he will “cherish [the AI industry,] help it, and watch over it, as it grows,” but he’s also forming an AI Force, similar to the Space Force that he established in his first term. “To that end, I will be announcing, in the near future, the AI ‘Czar’ — Only High I.Q. individuals need apply!” he added. The president did not say what the AI Force and AI czar’s duties will entail.
周六，特朗普进一步表示，他将“珍视（AI行业），帮助它，并在它成长过程中守护它”，但他同时也将组建一支“人工智能部队”，类似于他在第一任期内建立的“太空军”。他补充道：“为此，我将在不久的将来宣布人工智能‘沙皇’（负责人）的人选——只有高智商人士才能申请！”总统并未说明人工智能部队和人工智能沙皇的具体职责。

Venture capitalist David Sacks stepped down as Trump’s AI and crypto czar earlier this year, moving on to co-chair the President’s Council of Advisors on Science and Technology.
风险投资家大卫·萨克斯（David Sacks）今年早些时候卸任了特朗普的人工智能和加密货币沙皇一职，转而担任总统科学技术顾问委员会的联合主席。

The AI safety debate recently intensified after an AI researcher said he was quitting Anthropic over concerns that the leading AI companies “earnestly believe it could kill us all by the end of the decade” and are “gambling with our lives.” Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei subsequently released a plan to “pace the frontier,” which was seemingly endorsed by OpenAI CEO Sam Altman and SpaceX CEO Elon Musk.
近期，在一名AI研究人员因担忧领先的AI公司“真诚地相信AI可能在本世纪末杀死我们所有人”且在“拿我们的生命赌博”而宣布从Anthropic离职后，关于AI安全的辩论愈演愈烈。Anthropic首席执行官达里奥·阿莫代（Dario Amodei）随后发布了一项旨在“放缓前沿技术发展”的计划，该计划似乎得到了OpenAI首席执行官萨姆·奥特曼（Sam Altman）和SpaceX首席执行官埃隆·马斯克（Elon Musk）的支持。

Critics of the AI industry have suggested that many of these concerns around the technology’s supposed existential threat are a distraction from AI’s more immediate harms. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, meanwhile, called Trump on-stage during the All-In Summit (which Sacks co-hosts), agreed with the president that the AI backlash is a “hoax,” and insisted that “we’re not going to let” a slowdown happen.
AI行业的批评者认为，围绕该技术所谓“生存威胁”的许多担忧，实际上是为了转移人们对AI更直接危害的注意力。与此同时，英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋在“All-In峰会”（由萨克斯共同主持）期间与特朗普连线，他赞同总统关于AI抵制浪潮是“骗局”的观点，并坚称“我们绝不会让”行业放缓发生。
