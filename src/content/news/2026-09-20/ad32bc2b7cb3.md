# Join the WIRED World Fair in Miami on November 4
# 11月4日，欢迎参加在迈阿密举办的 WIRED 世界博览会

For more than a century, World’s Fairs have given people a first glimpse of what’s next: breakthroughs, ideas, and ways of living that would go on to reshape the everyday. WIRED will reimagine that tradition in Miami on November 4 with the WIRED World Fair.
一个多世纪以来，世界博览会一直让人们得以先睹为快，窥见未来的突破、理念以及将重塑日常生活的各种方式。11月4日，WIRED 将在迈阿密举办“WIRED 世界博览会”，以全新的方式重塑这一传统。

This one-day event brings the technologies, people, and cultural forces transforming our world off the page and into real life. At the Sacred Space in Miami’s Wynwood district, we’ll host conversations with luminaries from biotechnology, politics, artificial intelligence, and beyond.
这场为期一天的活动将把那些正在改变我们世界的科技、人物和文化力量从纸面带入现实生活。在迈阿密温伍德区（Wynwood）的“神圣空间”（Sacred Space），我们将与来自生物技术、政治、人工智能等领域的杰出人物进行对话。

The Onion will be there, and a researcher whose life’s work is decoding the language of dolphins. We’ll be screening the Musk documentary, and letting you experience the same training software astronauts use to get ready for the moon and Mars. There will also be robots.
《洋葱新闻》（The Onion）将亲临现场，还有一位致力于破译海豚语言的研究人员也将出席。我们将放映马斯克（Musk）的纪录片，并让你体验宇航员为登月和火星任务做准备时所使用的同款训练软件。当然，现场还会有机器人。

We’ll be announcing even more programming as we get closer to the date. For now, you can see the full list of speakers and events below—including immersive art installations from Marina Abramović and others—and find more information at the WIRED World Fair site.
随着活动日期的临近，我们将公布更多日程安排。目前，你可以查看下方完整的演讲者和活动列表（包括玛丽娜·阿布拉莫维奇等人的沉浸式艺术装置），并访问 WIRED 世界博览会官网获取更多信息。
