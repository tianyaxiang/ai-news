# The colorful, unique Hyte X50 PC case is $50 off
# 色彩鲜艳、独具匠心的 Hyte X50 机箱现直降 50 美元

The company’s Labor Day sale is still going strong through the weekend, and the Hyte X50 is one of my favorite deals.
该公司的劳动节促销活动将持续整个周末，而 Hyte X50 是我最心仪的优惠产品之一。

The Hyte X50 is a PC case that really stands out from the typical black box design, and it’s $50 off at the company’s site until September 21st, 2026, bringing the price down to $99.99.
Hyte X50 是一款与众不同的 PC 机箱，它打破了传统的“黑色方块”设计。在 2026 年 9 月 21 日之前，该机箱在官网直降 50 美元，售价仅为 99.99 美元。

This attractive case supports motherboards from ITX all the way up to the E-ATX form factors, and GPUs that are up to 430mm long, which should cover even the biggest options available.
这款外观精美的机箱支持从 ITX 到 E-ATX 等各种规格的主板，并能容纳长达 430 毫米的显卡，足以适配市面上绝大多数大型硬件。

The standout feature is a massive curved glass panel that spans the left face and top of the case, giving it a unique, eye-catching look. It’s easy to build in, supports a wide variety of components and cooling setups, and has a thoughtful, refined design.
其最显著的特点是覆盖了机箱左侧和顶部的巨大曲面玻璃面板，赋予了它独特且引人注目的外观。它易于装机，支持多种组件和散热方案，设计既周到又精致。

Unlike a lot of cases with big glass panels, the X50 has excellent airflow, with louvered fins along the back for ventilation, a porous mesh front, and plenty of spots for radiators and fans.
与许多采用大面积玻璃面板的机箱不同，X50 拥有出色的气流表现。它在背面设有百叶窗式散热鳍片，正面采用多孔网状设计，并为冷排和风扇预留了充足的安装位置。

It comes in a variety of bold colors, too, like the lavender-adjacent “taro milk” and trendy “matcha,” as well as more classic black and white options.
它还提供多种大胆的配色，例如接近薰衣草色的“芋泥奶茶色”和时尚的“抹茶色”，当然也有经典的黑白配色可选。

If you’d rather opt for something a little more subtle, the X50 Air swaps the glass panel for curved mesh, and is only $79.99 (usually $119.99) from Hyte.
如果你更倾向于低调的风格，X50 Air 版本将玻璃面板换成了曲面网孔面板，目前在 Hyte 官网售价仅为 79.99 美元（原价 119.99 美元）。
