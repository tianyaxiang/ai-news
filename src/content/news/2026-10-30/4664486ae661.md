# The Merchant Is Not the Customer

Recovery software has two audiences wearing one name. The merchant signs the contract, reads the reports, and pays the invoice. The merchant's customer receives the emails, taps the update link, and decides — in about four seconds, mid-scroll — whether this business handles problems like a professional or a collections agency.

恢复软件拥有两个受众，却共用一个名称。商家签署合同、阅读报告并支付发票。商家的客户则接收电子邮件、点击更新链接，并在滚动页面的四秒钟内决定——这家企业处理问题的方式是像专业人士，还是像收债机构。

Design for only one of them and the other quietly leaves. So every surface gets read twice. The dunning email is a merchant's recovery instrument and a stranger's first impression of how that merchant behaves under friction. The save offer is a retention metric and a moment of being cornered or being respected, depending on how the exit is drawn.

如果只为其中一方设计，另一方就会悄然离去。因此，每一个界面都会被审视两次。催款邮件既是商家的挽回工具，也是陌生人对该商家在面对摩擦时表现出的第一印象。挽留方案既是一个留存指标，也是一个决定客户是被逼入绝境还是受到尊重的时刻，这取决于退出机制的设计方式。

Even the report is two documents: proof of work for the merchant, and a tone-setter for how honestly the whole relationship will be run. Swipe Again is built in the merchant's branding for exactly this reason — the customer should never meet us at all. The recovery flow succeeds when the subscriber thinks their subscription company handled a hiccup gracefully.

即便是报告也包含两层含义：对商家而言是工作证明，对整体关系的诚信度而言则是基调设定。Swipe Again 正是出于这个原因采用了商家的品牌标识——客户根本不应察觉到我们的存在。当订阅用户认为他们的订阅公司优雅地处理了小故障时，挽回流程才算成功。

Invisible competence is the product. The merchant gets the revenue; their customer keeps the relationship; we keep the machinery out of the story entirely.

隐形的胜任力才是产品本身。商家获得了收入，客户维持了关系，而我们将后台机制完全排除在故事之外。
