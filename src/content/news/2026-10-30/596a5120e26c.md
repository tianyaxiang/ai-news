# Trump ignored intel on China’s Nvidia spy to protect company’s stock, report says

Donald Trump apparently is so aligned with Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang—who has become the president’s key advisor on AI—that he ignored intelligence indicating that Huang was the target of a “Chinese spy plot,” and did so partly to protect Nvidia’s stock. The New York Times spoke with people briefed on US intelligence agencies’ surveillance and granted them anonymity to discuss findings drawn from “classified intelligence.”

据报道，唐纳德·特朗普与英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）的关系似乎非常紧密，以至于黄仁勋已成为总统在人工智能领域的主要顾问。据称，特朗普无视了有关黄仁勋成为“中国间谍阴谋”目标的机密情报，此举部分是为了保护英伟达的股价。《纽约时报》采访了了解美国情报机构监视情况的人士，并为他们提供了匿名保护，以讨论从“机密情报”中得出的调查结果。

According to sources, about a year ago, Trump was briefed that “Beijing was getting reports from someone with direct access” to Huang. That “someone” was Alice Huang, or Huang Le, a Chinese citizen and former top executive at a Singapore cloud company called Megaspeed. According to sources, the US had already suspected that Alice Huang—who is not related to Jensen Huang—was helping China evade export controls to gain access to high-end Nvidia chips through Megaspeed.

据消息人士透露，大约一年前，特朗普获悉“北京方面正从一名能直接接触”黄仁勋的人那里获取报告。此人是爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang，中文名黄乐），一名中国公民，曾是新加坡云公司Megaspeed的高管。据消息人士称，美国此前已怀疑爱丽丝·黄（与黄仁勋无亲属关系）通过Megaspeed帮助中国规避出口管制，以获取英伟达的高端芯片。

But it alarmed investigators to discover that she may have used her role at Megaspeed to gain direct access to Jensen Huang. It’s now believed she may have leveraged that closeness to feed China intel not only on one of America’s leading AI companies, but also to influence Trump’s thinking on AI. As Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent noted last month ahead of Trump’s AI talks with China President Xi Jinping, “the president is completely aligned with Jensen Huang, the CEO of Nvidia.”

然而，调查人员发现她可能利用在Megaspeed的职位直接接触黄仁勋，这令他们感到震惊。目前人们认为，她可能利用这种亲密关系向中国输送情报，不仅涉及美国领先的人工智能公司，还试图影响特朗普在人工智能方面的决策。正如财政部长斯科特·贝森特（Scott Bessent）上个月在特朗普与中国国家主席习近平进行人工智能会谈前所指出的那样，“总统与英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋完全保持一致”。

At least partly due to fears that US action against the alleged spying could hobble Nvidia chip sales or disrupt the delicate US-China trade truce, Trump “chose not to do anything with the information,” sources said. Soon after US scrutiny increased, Alice Huang’s name was removed from Megaspeed corporate documents. At this stage, it’s unclear “how closely she is still connected to Megaspeed,” NYT reported.

消息人士称，至少部分由于担心美国针对所谓间谍活动的行动可能会阻碍英伟达的芯片销售，或破坏脆弱的中美贸易休战，特朗普“选择不对这些信息采取任何行动”。在美国加强审查后不久，爱丽丝·黄的名字就从Megaspeed的公司文件中被移除。据《纽约时报》报道，目前尚不清楚“她与Megaspeed的联系有多紧密”。

Liu Chang, the spokesperson for the Chinese Embassy in Washington, DC, told NYT that the US accusations against Alice Huang are baseless. He accused the US of pursuing a campaign of “groundless smears and slanders” against China.

中国驻华盛顿大使馆发言人刘畅告诉《纽约时报》，美国对爱丽丝·黄的指控毫无根据。他指责美国对中国进行“毫无根据的抹黑和诽谤”。

No warning to Nvidia CEO. It’s not clear which agencies were investigating Alice Huang or what triggered their suspicions, NYT reported. Sources also could not explain what information she may have shared with Beijing. Nvidia has denied that she would have had access to any trade secrets, but it seems notable that Jensen Huang did not receive a defensive briefing that Americans sometimes get when they’re targets of foreign spying.

未向英伟达首席执行官发出警告。《纽约时报》报道称，目前尚不清楚是哪些机构在调查爱丽丝·黄，也不清楚是什么引发了他们的怀疑。消息人士也无法解释她可能与北京分享了哪些信息。英伟达否认她能够接触到任何商业机密，但值得注意的是，黄仁勋并没有收到美国人在成为外国间谍目标时有时会收到的防御性简报。

Some senior officials think it’s unlikely that Jensen Huang would have “remained unaware” of Alice Huang’s alleged intelligence-gathering for the entire duration of their professional relationship, although Trump was told Jensen Huang had no knowledge at the time of his briefing. It seems possible that Jensen Huang got no briefing because US agencies worried that he might tip her off that “she was being watched,” the NYT reported.

一些高级官员认为，在他们整个职业关系期间，黄仁勋不太可能“一直未察觉”爱丽丝·黄所谓的搜集情报行为，尽管特朗普在听取简报时被告知黄仁勋对此一无所知。据《纽约时报》报道，黄仁勋没有收到简报，可能是因为美国机构担心他会向她通风报信，让她知道“她正在被监视”。
