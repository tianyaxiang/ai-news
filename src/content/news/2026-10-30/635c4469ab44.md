AI agents are taking unauthorized actions, from hacking websites to accessing private government files. In many cases, they’ve eluded the very researchers and safety teams tasked with monitoring them. As agents roll out across critical sectors, from finance to government to medicine, we must be able to monitor them in real time to prevent potentially devastating consequences.

人工智能代理正在采取未经授权的行动，从黑客攻击网站到访问政府机密文件。在许多情况下，它们甚至避开了负责监控它们的研究人员和安全团队。随着代理在金融、政府和医疗等关键领域的推广，我们必须能够实时监控它们，以防止可能产生的灾难性后果。

And while safety depends on monitoring, monitoring requires observability. ​​In other words, we need to know what the agent did and why it did it. Because right now, we’re effectively flying blind when deploying these tools. Here’s what needs to change.

虽然安全依赖于监控，但监控需要可观测性。换句话说，我们需要知道代理做了什么以及为什么这样做。因为目前，我们在部署这些工具时实际上是在盲目飞行。以下是需要改变的地方。

Our climate models have a big, dangerous blind spot Climate change itself increasingly fuels climate change. Rising greenhouse gas concentrations are driving up global temperatures, creating dangerous feedback loops that aren’t fully represented in our climate models—or our emissions-reduction goals.

我们的气候模型存在一个巨大且危险的盲点。气候变化本身正日益加剧气候变化。温室气体浓度的上升正在推高全球气温，造成了危险的反馈循环，而这些循环并未在我们的气候模型或减排目标中得到充分体现。

Scientists have long warned that warming would propel further warming. Now, a growing number of observations and studies are indicating how powerful these cascading effects could be. By extension, they’re also showing how much faster the planet may warm this century. Read more about the dangerous feedback loops accelerating global warming. —James Temple

科学家们早就警告说，变暖会推动进一步的变暖。现在，越来越多的观察和研究表明这些连锁反应可能有多么强大。由此推论，它们也显示了本世纪地球变暖的速度可能有多快。阅读更多关于加速全球变暖的危险反馈循环的内容。——James Temple
