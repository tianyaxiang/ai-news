# Phorge

Phorge is an open source, opinionated, community-driven platform for collaborating, managing, organizing and reviewing software development projects.

Phorge 是一个开源的、有主见的、社区驱动的平台，用于协作、管理、组织和审查软件开发项目。

Phorge is a community-maintained fork of Phabricator, which has been unmaintained since June 1st, 2021 (more info).

Phorge 是 Phabricator 的社区维护分支，后者自 2021 年 6 月 1 日起已停止维护（更多信息）。

You can learn more about the features of Phorge, and how to install Phorge. You can also browse questions and answers or ask a new question.

您可以了解更多关于 Phorge 的功能以及如何安装 Phorge。您还可以浏览问答或提出新问题。

Phorge is developed in Phorge! Explore the contributing docs and join the community to advance developments: we.Phorge.it

Phorge 本身就是使用 Phorge 开发的！探索贡献文档并加入社区以推动发展：we.Phorge.it
