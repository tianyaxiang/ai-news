The $5.8 billion joint venture between Rivian and Volkswagen Group is expanding to include AI assistants. Rivian is now licensing elements of its AI voice assistant technology to Volkswagen Group as part of the broader joint venture, according to the company’s third-quarter investor presentation released Thursday.

Rivian 与大众汽车集团之间价值 58 亿美元的合资企业正在扩大范围，将人工智能助手纳入其中。根据该公司周四发布的第三季度投资者报告，作为更广泛合资协议的一部分，Rivian 目前正在向大众汽车集团授权其人工智能语音助手技术的部分要素。

Rivian didn’t disclose the licensing fees, payment terms, or which Volkswagen vehicles will use the technology. However, the AI assistant, which recently rolled out to Rivian vehicles, could make its Volkswagen debut in the automaker’s low-cost EV called the ID EVERY1. The small four-door hatchback is slated to be the first VW vehicle to use Rivian’s software and electrical architecture under the joint venture established in 2024.

Rivian 并未披露许可费用、付款条款或哪些大众汽车车型将使用该技术。然而，这款最近在 Rivian 车辆上推出的 AI 助手，可能会在大众汽车的低成本电动汽车 ID EVERY1 上首次亮相。这款小型四门掀背车预计将成为 2024 年成立的合资企业下，首款使用 Rivian 软件和电气架构的大众汽车。

The joint venture between Rivian and VW Group has already expanded once before, at least financially. The companies initially announced a joint venture valued up to $5 billion, but within months VW upped its commitment to invest up to $5.8 billion in Rivian by 2027. The initial tie-up, formalized through an independent company that kicked off in November 2024, focused on bringing Rivian’s electrical architecture and software to Volkswagen’s next generation of vehicles.

Rivian 与大众集团之间的合资企业此前已经在财务层面进行过一次扩张。两家公司最初宣布的合资企业估值最高为 50 亿美元，但几个月内，大众汽车就将其对 Rivian 的投资承诺提高到了 2027 年前最高 58 亿美元。最初的合作通过一家于 2024 年 11 月启动的独立公司正式确立，重点是将 Rivian 的电气架构和软件引入大众汽车的下一代车型。

Rivian described the expansion to include elements of its AI assistant technology as “a proof point for the extensibility of the platform we’ve built in-house.” Rivian spent two years developing its AI assistant before showing it off last year. That development work continued longer than expected, with the assistant finally rolling out in May 2026 to its flagship R1S SUV, R1T pickup truck, and in October to its new midsize R2 SUV.

Rivian 将此次纳入 AI 助手技术要素的扩张描述为“我们内部构建的平台具有可扩展性的证明”。Rivian 在去年展示其 AI 助手之前，花费了两年时间进行开发。开发工作持续的时间比预期的要长，该助手最终于 2026 年 5 月在其旗舰车型 R1S SUV 和 R1T 皮卡上推出，并于 10 月在其新款中型 R2 SUV 上推出。

Rivian has big aspirations for its AI assistant, which allows drivers and passengers to interact with the vehicle and control certain functions. Rivian founder and CEO RJ Scaringe told TechCrunch onstage at Disrupt 2026 that the company has created its own in-house agentic framework that understands what action a person wants to take when they talk to the vehicle, and routes it to the right part of the car’s software. That capability could eventually allow Rivian vehicles to run AI agents created by other companies, including Anthropic.

Rivian 对其 AI 助手寄予厚望，该助手允许驾驶员和乘客与车辆进行交互并控制某些功能。Rivian 创始人兼首席执行官 RJ Scaringe 在 2026 年 Disrupt 大会上告诉 TechCrunch，公司已经创建了自己的内部代理框架，能够理解人们在与车辆交谈时想要采取的行动，并将其引导至汽车软件的相应部分。这种能力最终可能使 Rivian 车辆能够运行由其他公司（包括 Anthropic）创建的 AI 代理。

The in-house AI assistant program also fits into Rivian’s push to become more vertically integrated — a strategy the company emphasized in its third-quarter earnings report. “Our relentless focus on vertical integration remains a core competitive differentiator, allowing Rivian to seamlessly unify our hardware and software architecture to deliver significant value to our customers,” the company noted in its investor presentation.

内部 AI 助手项目也符合 Rivian 推动实现更高垂直整合的战略，这也是该公司在第三季度财报中强调的重点。公司在投资者报告中指出：“我们对垂直整合的不懈追求仍然是核心竞争差异化优势，使 Rivian 能够无缝统一我们的硬件和软件架构，从而为客户提供巨大价值。”

That strategy led to Rivian overhauling its flagship R1T truck and R1S SUV, changing everything from the battery pack and suspension system to the electrical architecture, sensor stack, and software user interface. Its new electric architecture system and compute platform slashed the amount of wiring used and reduced the total number of electronic control units, or computers, used to control the vehicle from 17 different ECUs to seven.

这一战略促使 Rivian 对其旗舰车型 R1T 皮卡和 R1S SUV 进行了全面改造，从电池组、悬挂系统到电气架构、传感器堆栈和软件用户界面，一切都发生了变化。其新的电气架构系统和计算平台大幅减少了布线用量，并将用于控制车辆的电子控制单元（ECU，即计算机）总数从 17 个减少到了 7 个。

That foundation, Rivian argued, would allow it to build vehicles that can handle the computing demands required for advanced software and automated driving. That technology caught the attention (and money) of VW, which has struggled to offer customers a modern software-centric vehicle found in competitors like Tesla.

Rivian 认为，这一基础将使其能够制造出能够处理高级软件和自动驾驶所需计算需求的车辆。这项技术引起了大众汽车的关注（并获得了投资），大众汽车一直难以像特斯拉等竞争对手那样，为客户提供现代化的软件中心型车辆。

Rivian made that vertical-integration case repeatedly in its investor presentation, noting that because it controls the entire technology stack, including compute, sensors, displays, lighting, and sound, “it can roll out rapid, vehicle-wide OTA updates that go well beyond the basics.”

Rivian 在其投资者报告中反复强调了垂直整合的案例，指出由于它控制着整个技术堆栈，包括计算、传感器、显示器、照明和声音，“它能够推出超越基础功能的快速、全车 OTA 更新。”
