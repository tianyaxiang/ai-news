# Patrick Stewart has died at age 86

帕特里克·斯图尔特（Patrick Stewart）去世，享年86岁。

Stewart was best known as Captain Jean-Luc Picard in Star Trek.

斯图尔特最为人所知的角色是《星际迷航》中的让-卢克·皮卡德船长。

by Emma Roth Oct 29, 2026, 8:10 PM UTC

作者：Emma Roth，2026年10月29日，世界标准时间晚上8:10。

Patrick Stewart, best known for his roles in Star Trek and X-Men, has died at age 86. A message posted to Stewart’s Instagram account says the actor died in his Los Angeles home on Wednesday “following a brief and poignant journey with Alzheimer’s Disease and Lewy Body Dementia.”

以《星际迷航》和《X战警》中的角色而闻名的帕特里克·斯图尔特去世，享年86岁。斯图尔特Instagram账号发布的一则消息称，这位演员于周三在洛杉矶家中去世，“此前他经历了一段短暂而令人心酸的与阿尔茨海默病和路易体痴呆症抗争的旅程。”

Born in 1940 in Mirfield, Yorkshire, England, Stewart began his acting career in theater. He eventually landed the role of Captain Jean-Luc Picard in Star Trek: The Next Generation, which ran from 1987 to 1994.

斯图尔特1940年出生于英国约克郡米尔菲尔德，他的演艺生涯始于戏剧。他最终获得了《星际迷航：下一代》中让-卢克·皮卡德船长的角色，该剧于1987年至1994年播出。

Stewart returned as the Star Trek captain in subsequent films, including Star Trek: Generations (1994), Star Trek: First Contact (1996), Star Trek: Insurrection (1998), and Star Trek: Nemesis (2002). Stewart also starred as Professor Charles Xavier in several X-Men movies. He was knighted by Queen Elizabeth II in 2010.

斯图尔特在随后的电影中回归饰演这位星际迷航船长，包括《星际迷航：日换星移》（1994）、《星际迷航：第一类接触》（1996）、《星际迷航：起义》（1998）和《星际迷航：复仇女神》（2002）。斯图尔特还在多部《X战警》电影中饰演了查尔斯·泽维尔教授。他于2010年被伊丽莎白二世女王授予爵士爵位。

“His sonorous voice and singular talent will forever be synonymous with indelible performances in every genre,” the message on Instagram says. “He explored the human condition in all its poetic complexity and brought hope not only to the far reaches of the Marvel Cinematic Universe, but into science fiction’s most iconic final frontier aboard the Starship Enterprise.”

Instagram上的消息称：“他那洪亮的声音和非凡的才华将永远与各个领域中不可磨灭的表演联系在一起。他探索了人类状况的所有诗意复杂性，不仅为漫威电影宇宙的遥远角落带来了希望，也为企业号星舰上科幻小说中最具标志性的终极边疆带来了希望。”
