Title: Go Minimal—the Ridge Wallet is 20 Percent Off Right Now

Everyday carry (EDC) enthusiasts and social media influencers have made the Ridge Wallet a favorite for its durability and minimalist design. But it’s not just hype. In her testing, WIRED reviewer Louryn Strampe gave it high marks, saying, “I can’t see myself using anything else,” even though she’s not a minimalist and didn’t fall for the “ridiculous” marketing. It’s also one of the wallets I use on vacation, when I just need to carry a few credit cards without drawing attention to the usual George Costanza bulge in my pocket.

日常携带（EDC）爱好者和社交媒体网红们因其耐用性和极简设计，将 Ridge 钱包捧为心头好。但这不仅仅是炒作。在测试中，WIRED 评论员 Louryn Strampe 给出了高分，称“我无法想象自己还会用其他钱包”，尽管她本人并非极简主义者，也没有被那些“荒谬”的营销所打动。这也是我度假时使用的钱包之一，因为我只需要带几张信用卡，而不必让口袋里出现像乔治·科斯坦扎（George Costanza）那样明显的鼓包。

Select Ridge Wallet styles are currently on sale for $76 (normally $95) as part of the brand's Friends & Family sale, which also has discounts on other Ridge accessories like pocket knives, power banks, luggage, trackers, and more. 作为该品牌“亲友特卖”活动的一部分，部分款式的 Ridge 钱包目前售价为 76 美元（原价 95 美元），此次活动还涵盖了其他 Ridge 配件的折扣，如折叠刀、充电宝、行李箱、追踪器等。如果这款钱包不适合你，Ridge 提供的 99 天无风险试用期可以为你办理退款。

As Strampe mentioned in her review, the Ridge Wallet will look unfamiliar to newbies. In fact, it may not feel user-friendly at first. The wallet acts like a sandwich for all your cards, using two aluminum pieces held together with screws and elastic bands. It holds up to 12 cards, but you can adjust the opening with the T-5 Torx screws and included screwdriver to fit exactly how many cards you need.

正如 Strampe 在评论中所述，Ridge 钱包对新手来说看起来很陌生。事实上，起初它可能让人感觉不太好用。这款钱包就像一个夹心饼干，由两块铝板通过螺丝和松紧带固定在一起。它最多可容纳 12 张卡片，但你可以使用 T-5 梅花螺丝刀（随附）调节开口，以精确适配你所需的卡片数量。

If you’re a minimalist, the Ridge Wallet can help you stay that way. For Strampe, she can’t imagine the Ridge replacing her purse—she still needs to carry her car keys and other necessities. But it does help her keep all her cards—credit, driver’s license, Costco—in one place without rummaging through her bag or using a bulkier wallet.

如果你是极简主义者，Ridge 钱包可以帮你保持这种生活方式。对 Strampe 而言，她无法想象 Ridge 能取代她的手提包——她仍需携带车钥匙和其他必需品。但它确实帮助她将所有卡片（信用卡、驾照、Costco 会员卡）集中放在一处，无需在包里翻找，也不必使用更笨重的钱包。

The Ridge Wallet is somewhat customizable. You can add a cash strap or clip on the outside; there’s even a money clip with a slot for an AirTag. There’s also a chain and D-ring attachment, and you can personalize your Ridge Wallet with colored screws.

Ridge 钱包具有一定的可定制性。你可以在外部添加现金绑带或夹子；甚至还有带有 AirTag 插槽的钞票夹。此外还有链条和 D 型环附件，你还可以通过彩色螺丝来个性化你的 Ridge 钱包。
