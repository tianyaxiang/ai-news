Just over a week ago, we released Granite Switch 4.1, showing that our modular Switch architecture lets you configure a language model with specialized, cache-efficient adapters — adding new expertise on a single endpoint. Granite Switch 4.1.1 is a patch release. The version number means what it means in software: fully backwards compatible with 4.1, no migration, incremental improvements under the hood.

就在一周多前，我们发布了 Granite Switch 4.1，展示了我们的模块化 Switch 架构如何让你能够通过专门的、缓存高效的适配器来配置语言模型，从而在单个端点上增加新的专业能力。Granite Switch 4.1.1 是一个补丁版本。其版本号的含义与软件领域一致：与 4.1 完全向后兼容，无需迁移，并在底层进行了增量改进。

Updated Granite Speech ASR model — multilingual speech-to-text across 10 languages with native punctuation and capitalization, so transcripts are ready for downstream models without a formatting pass. Updated Decision adapter in granitelib-core — featuring improved overall performance on the Jev Decision Index test set.

更新了 Granite Speech ASR 模型——支持 10 种语言的多语言语音转文字，具备原生标点和大小写功能，因此转录内容无需格式化处理即可直接供下游模型使用。更新了 granitelib-core 中的决策适配器——在 Jev Decision Index 测试集上实现了整体性能的提升。

While we are excited about these new features, what is notable is the speed and efficiency with which our teams could release this update. Apart from training the new ASR model and Decision adapter, nothing was recomputed: the base model is untouched. You compose the latest adapters you want and deploy.

虽然我们对这些新功能感到兴奋，但值得注意的是我们团队发布此更新的速度和效率。除了训练新的 ASR 模型和决策适配器外，没有任何内容被重新计算：基础模型保持不变。你只需组合所需的最新适配器并进行部署即可。

This pattern can power far more than patch releases to an IBM-provided model. We're working on tutorials and an Adapter SDK so developers and third-party providers can build their own adapters — iterate on versions, test features, and ship updates the way you ship software. More soon. Until then, test out our latest models, and try out the new multilingual and punctuation capabilities powering our Speech-to-Decisions demo.

这种模式所能支持的远不止 IBM 提供模型的补丁发布。我们正在开发教程和适配器 SDK，以便开发人员和第三方提供商能够构建自己的适配器——像发布软件一样迭代版本、测试功能并发布更新。更多信息即将发布。在此之前，请测试我们最新的模型，并尝试为我们的“语音转决策”演示提供支持的全新多语言和标点功能。
