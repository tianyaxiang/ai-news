# The director of Obsession put a new horror short on YouTube

《Obsession》的导演在 YouTube 上发布了一部新的恐怖短片。

You can watch Curry Barker’s ‘Mama Bear’ on YouTube right now. You can watch Curry Barker’s ‘Mama Bear’ on YouTube right now. by Jay PetersOct 29, 2026, 11:57 PM UTC.

你现在就可以在 YouTube 上观看 Curry Barker 的《Mama Bear》。作者：Jay Peters，2026 年 10 月 29 日，世界标准时间晚上 11:57。

Curry Barker, the director behind the hit horror film Obsession, just published a new horror short on YouTube. The short, titled “Mama Bear,” is nearly five minutes long. The video is pretty unnerving, and fair warning that it gets a little graphic, but it’s a fun one to watch if you’re looking for something scary ahead of Halloween on Saturday.

热门恐怖电影《Obsession》的导演 Curry Barker 刚刚在 YouTube 上发布了一部新的恐怖短片。这部名为《Mama Bear》的短片时长近五分钟。视频非常令人不安，且在此提醒，其中包含一些血腥画面，但如果你想在周六万圣节前找点恐怖的东西看，它会是一个不错的选择。

Barker wrote, directed, and edited “Mama Bear,” according to the description for the video, which was published on his that’s a bad idea YouTube channel. The description also has a note thanking Windows for “sponsoring this short film,” and Barker was featured in an Instagram video earlier this week with a Surface laptop.

根据视频描述，Barker 编写、执导并剪辑了《Mama Bear》，该视频发布在他的“that’s a bad idea”YouTube 频道上。描述中还特别感谢了 Windows 对这部短片的“赞助”，本周早些时候，Barker 还出现在一段展示 Surface 笔记本电脑的 Instagram 视频中。

Barker will follow Obsession with Anything but Ghosts, which debuts in May 2027. Horror is big business right now, with the film Backrooms, directed by YouTuber Kane Parsons, also proving to be a big box office success this year. Parsons debuted a new Backrooms YouTube episode in August.

继《Obsession》之后，Barker 将推出于 2027 年 5 月首映的《Anything but Ghosts》。恐怖题材目前是一门大生意，由 YouTuber Kane Parsons 执导的电影《Backrooms》今年也证明了其在票房上的巨大成功。Parsons 在 8 月份发布了《Backrooms》的最新 YouTube 剧集。
