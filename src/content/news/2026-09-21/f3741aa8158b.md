### Why models should never die

### FAQ
### 常见问题解答

**What is Pirate Face?**
A decentralized, peer-to-peer layer for sovereign AI. Open models from Hugging Face become checksum-verified torrents, held by a global swarm so they survive any single host taking them down. The goal is permanence for open-source AI.
**什么是 Pirate Face？**
这是一个用于主权人工智能的去中心化点对点层。来自 Hugging Face 的开源模型被转化为经过校验和验证的种子文件，由全球网络共同持有，从而确保即使单一主机下架，模型依然能够存活。其目标是实现开源人工智能的永久化。

**Do I need an account to use Pirate Face?**
No. You can browse, download, and seed without an account. Claiming is optional. A public claim reserves your Pirate Face handle and helps the network spread. Only your first eligible handle earns welcome points.
**使用 Pirate Face 需要账户吗？**
不需要。你可以在没有账户的情况下浏览、下载和做种。认领账户是可选的。公开认领可以保留你的 Pirate Face 昵称，并帮助网络传播。只有你第一个符合条件的昵称可以获得欢迎积分。

**Why use Hugging Face verification if the goal is independence from centralized hosting?**
Hugging Face verification confirms that someone claiming a creator’s handle controls the matching HF account or organization. It helps prevent impersonation and preserves attribution as models spread beyond their original host.
**既然目标是摆脱中心化托管，为什么还要使用 Hugging Face 验证？**
Hugging Face 验证旨在确认认领创作者昵称的人确实拥有对应的 HF 账户或组织权限。这有助于防止冒充，并在模型传播到原始托管平台之外时，保留创作者的归属权。
