# The hidden monopoly behind your TI graphing calculator

On *Version History*: Nilay Patel and Victoria Song recount their memories of their iconic and expensive calculators.
在《Version History》节目中：Nilay Patel 和 Victoria Song 回忆了他们对那些标志性且昂贵的计算器的记忆。

自 21 世纪初以来，同一款计算器就一直出现在大多数孩子的返校购物清单上，而德州仪器（Texas Instruments）计算器部门的市场主导地位甚至可以追溯到更早的年代。

In the next episode of *Version History*, David Pierce is joined by *The Verge*’s Nilay Patel and Victoria Song to tell the story of TI graphing calculators and how one company managed to take over the market and never let it go.
在下一期《Version History》中，David Pierce 将与 *The Verge* 的 Nilay Patel 和 Victoria Song 一起讲述 TI 图形计算器的故事，以及这家公司是如何成功占领市场并始终保持垄断地位的。
