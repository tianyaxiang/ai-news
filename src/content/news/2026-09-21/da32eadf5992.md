# RSA-896

Stephen A. Weis RSA-896 September 2026 RSA-896 is a RSA challenge number I factored with Claude on September 19, 2026. More details will follow.
RSA-896 是一个我在 2026 年 9 月 19 日与 Claude 合作分解的 RSA 挑战数。更多细节将在后续公布。

Briefly, Claude was used to port CADO-NFS to run on GPUs. It orchestrated running on a fleet of up to 2048 GPUs as a low-priority job during unused idle time between regular jobs. The computation ran over a 10-day period and performed about 30 GPU-years of compute time at Anthropic.
简而言之，我们利用 Claude 将 CADO-NFS 移植到 GPU 上运行。它协调了多达 2048 个 GPU 的集群，在常规任务之间的空闲时间以低优先级任务的形式进行计算。该计算过程持续了 10 天，在 Anthropic 消耗了约 30 个 GPU 年的算力。

This work did not meaningfully improve the runtime of the General Number Field Sieve (GNFS) algorithm. It does not impact the security of deployed RSA-2048 keys. However, it does demonstrate that RSA-1024 keys are vulnerable to many actors with data center-level fleets of GPUs.
这项工作并没有显著提升通用数域筛法（GNFS）算法的运行效率，也不会影响已部署的 RSA-2048 密钥的安全性。然而，它确实证明了 RSA-1024 密钥对于拥有数据中心级 GPU 集群的众多行为体而言是脆弱的。
