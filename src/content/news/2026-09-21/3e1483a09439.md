# Kojima Productions disputes reports the studio is in trouble
# 小岛制作室否认工作室陷入困境的传闻

It released a statement saying ‘the studio is in a healthy, profitable state.’
官方发布声明称：“工作室目前处于健康且盈利的状态。”

After it was announced that Sony was dropping Hideo Kojima’s *Physint*, and that instead the *Metal Gear Solid* creator’s latest title would be coming to Xbox, there were a flurry of rumors about the falling out. Bloomberg reported that the decision was driven by a number of factors, including missed deadlines, budget and profitability concerns, and its timed exclusivity.
在索尼宣布放弃小岛秀夫的《Physint》项目，并转而由这位《合金装备》系列的创作者将最新作品登陆 Xbox 平台后，关于双方关系破裂的传闻甚嚣尘上。据彭博社报道，这一决定是由多种因素驱动的，包括错过截止日期、预算与盈利担忧，以及游戏的限时独占协议。

In an interview with IGN, Kojima said that, considering the broader state of the industry, which has seen a number of studios close, he was worried about the future of Kojima Productions:
在接受 IGN 采访时，小岛秀夫表示，考虑到目前游戏行业整体状况不佳、多家工作室相继倒闭，他曾对小岛制作室的未来感到担忧：

“I know there’s a lot of news in the game industry that many studios are closing down, many titles are canceled, and I understand the situation in the industry as a whole. But I thought *Physint* was okay. That *Physint* was safe. So I was surprised … If we lose one contract, I thought I might have to cut all the people working on *Physint*, right? Or our studio could end up collapsing because we’ll run out of cashflow.”
“我知道游戏行业有很多工作室倒闭、许多项目被取消的消息，我也了解整个行业的现状。但我原本以为《Physint》没问题，以为它是安全的。所以我感到很惊讶……如果失去一份合同，我想我可能不得不裁掉所有负责《Physint》的员工，对吧？或者我们的工作室可能会因为现金流枯竭而倒闭。”

The company has now taken to social media to state that, despite reports about disappointing sales of *Death Stranding* and its sequel, and the loss of funding from Sony, it is “in a healthy, profitable state.” And that, moreover, its “relationship with PlayStation remains strong.”
目前，该公司已通过社交媒体发表声明，表示尽管有报道称《死亡搁浅》及其续作销量令人失望，且失去了索尼的资金支持，但工作室仍“处于健康且盈利的状态”。此外，他们还强调，公司与 PlayStation 的“关系依然稳固”。
