# Trump now says he wants to form an ‘AI Force’
# 特朗普称他想要组建一支“人工智能部队”

The president posted on Truth Social that he wanted to appoint an “AI czar” to lead a new “AI force.” He made the announcement amid growing calls from across the political spectrum and even within the industry to pump the brakes on AI development. He posted that his administration “will not in any way hinder or stifle the Growth of this incredible Industry. Rather, we will cherish it, help it, and watch over it, as it grows!”

总统在“真实社交”（Truth Social）平台上发文称，他希望任命一位“人工智能沙皇”来领导一支新的“人工智能部队”。他是在各政治派别甚至行业内部日益呼吁放缓人工智能发展步伐的背景下宣布这一消息的。他发文表示，他的政府“绝不会以任何方式阻碍或扼杀这个令人难以置信的行业的发展。相反，我们将珍视它、帮助它，并在它成长过程中守护它！”

In the rambling post, Trump said that data centers were “wealthy and prestigious” and claimed without evidence that they raised salaries, lowered taxes, and made for safer streets in the communities in which they were built. He also related the AI Force to his creation of the Space Force, which he claimed was a “tremendous SUCCESS.”

在这篇冗长的帖子中，特朗普称数据中心是“富有且享有盛誉的”，并声称（但未提供证据）数据中心提高了当地薪资、降低了税收，并使所在社区的街道更加安全。他还将这支“人工智能部队”与他创建的“太空军”联系起来，并称后者取得了“巨大的成功”。

The president gave no timeline or further details about the AI Force nor who might be tapped to lead it. Though he insisted that “Only High I.Q. individuals need apply!” for the position of AI Czar.

总统没有给出关于“人工智能部队”的时间表或更多细节，也没有说明谁可能被选中领导该部队。不过他坚持认为，只有“高智商人士”才有资格申请“人工智能沙皇”这一职位。
