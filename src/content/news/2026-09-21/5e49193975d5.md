One of the stickier problems for autonomous vehicle developers, regulators, and the public is determining when self-driving technology is safe enough for the road. So far, that question has largely fallen to industry to answer — companies that are applying their autonomous vehicle technology to self-driving trucks, robotaxis, and maybe someday personally owned passenger vehicles.
对于自动驾驶汽车开发商、监管机构和公众来说，一个棘手的问题是：如何确定自动驾驶技术何时才算足够安全，可以上路行驶。到目前为止，这个问题主要由行业自行回答——即那些将自动驾驶技术应用于自动驾驶卡车、自动驾驶出租车（Robotaxi），以及未来可能应用于私家乘用车的公司。

Today, there is no standardized test for judging how well an automated driving system performs in the real world. We require people to pass a driver’s test before getting a license, but there isn’t an equivalent test or system for autonomous vehicle technology. So how do we know whether an AV is a safe driver?
目前，还没有一套标准化的测试来评估自动驾驶系统在现实世界中的表现。我们要求人类在获得驾照前必须通过驾驶考试，但自动驾驶技术却没有相应的测试或系统。那么，我们如何判断一辆自动驾驶汽车是否是一名“安全驾驶员”呢？

The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration (NHTSA) says it’s working on an answer. Federal motor vehicle safety standards (FMVSS) already govern the vehicle automakers sell today. But those FMVSS rules weren’t designed for vehicles like the Tesla Cybercab and Zoox robotaxi, which lack traditional, and federally required, equipment like pedals, steering wheels, and design features like side mirrors and rearview mirrors.
美国国家公路交通安全管理局（NHTSA）表示，他们正在寻找答案。联邦机动车安全标准（FMVSS）已经规范了当今汽车制造商销售的车辆。但这些 FMVSS 规则并非为特斯拉 Cybercab 或 Zoox 自动驾驶出租车这类车辆设计，因为它们缺乏踏板、方向盘以及侧视镜和后视镜等联邦法律要求的传统设备和设计特征。

The NHTSA is working on a federal automated vehicle framework that it says will put safety at the foundation without stifling innovation, or as Administrator Jonathan Morrison put it this week, force the agency to play “whack-a-mole” with policy decisions or try to predict how the technology will evolve. That’s a tricky recipe to get right.
NHTSA 正在制定一套联邦自动驾驶汽车框架，该机构称这将以安全为基础，同时不会扼杀创新。正如局长乔纳森·莫里森（Jonathan Morrison）本周所言，这能避免该机构在政策决策上陷入“打地鼠”式的被动应对，或试图去预测技术将如何演变。要实现这一目标并不容易。

The agency took a notable step toward that goal this week by reaching an agreement with SAE to launch ASCEND, a government-industry initiative aimed at speeding up the development of data-driven AV performance standards.
本周，该机构朝着这一目标迈出了重要一步，与国际自动机工程师学会（SAE）达成协议，启动了 ASCEND 项目。这是一项旨在加速制定数据驱动型自动驾驶性能标准的政府与行业合作计划。

“Our top priority for this consortium is ADS competency, which will inform and accelerate a corresponding federal motor vehicle safety standard,” Morrison said at the AI Horizons Summit in Pittsburgh. “Ultimately, our ADS performance rulemaking will provide federal leadership in this space. By delivering an AV competency standard, we hope we’ll be able to address the burgeoning past patchwork of inconsistent state and local level requirements, and continue to encourage American innovation and promote the safe deployment and development of these vehicles on the road.”
“我们这个联盟的首要任务是自动驾驶系统（ADS）的能力评估，这将为相应的联邦机动车安全标准提供依据并加速其制定，”莫里森在匹兹堡举行的 AI Horizons 峰会上表示。“最终，我们的 ADS 性能规则制定将在该领域发挥联邦领导作用。通过提供自动驾驶能力标准，我们希望能够解决过去各州和地方层面不一致的零散要求，并继续鼓励美国创新，促进这些车辆在道路上的安全部署与发展。”

There is no timeline yet for when this consortium will produce ADS standards. But I bet the AV industry is already weighing in.
目前尚无该联盟何时出台 ADS 标准的时间表。但我敢肯定，自动驾驶行业已经开始参与其中了。

There are other publicly traded companies working on autonomous vehicle technology: Aurora and Kodiak, which are both focused on self-driving trucks; Rivian; and Tesla. And while Waymo is not (yet) publicly traded, its parent company Alphabet is. It’s the same story for Amazon-owned Zoox. But May Mobility, which operates autonomous Toyota Siennas in three locations, says it will be the first public company in the U.S. focused entirely on autonomous ride-hailing vehicles. Senior reporter Sean O’Kane notes this will also be a test of May Mobility’s “asset-light” and “partnership-first” approach to autonomy.
还有其他从事自动驾驶技术的上市公司：专注于自动驾驶卡车的 Aurora 和 Kodiak，以及 Rivian 和特斯拉。虽然 Waymo 尚未上市，但其母公司 Alphabet 已经上市。亚马逊旗下的 Zoox 也是如此。但 May Mobility 表示，它将成为美国第一家完全专注于自动驾驶网约车的上市公司，目前该公司在三个地区运营着自动驾驶丰田 Sienna。资深记者 Sean O’Kane 指出，这也将是对 May Mobility“轻资产”和“合作优先”自动驾驶模式的一次考验。
