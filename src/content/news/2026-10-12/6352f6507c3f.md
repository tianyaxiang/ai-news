Acer has released the Acer Googlebook 14, the inaugural 2-in-1 laptop in the new Googlebook device category. Powered by an octa-core Intel Core Ultra 5 325 processor, it features 16GB of LPDDR5X RAM, 512GB of NVMe storage, Wi-Fi 7, and Thunderbolt 4 connectivity, alongside a 14-inch OLED touch display and an estimated 16-hour battery life.

宏碁发布了 Acer Googlebook 14，这是全新 Googlebook 设备类别中的首款二合一笔记本电脑。它搭载八核 Intel Core Ultra 5 325 处理器，配备 16GB LPDDR5X 内存、512GB NVMe 存储空间、Wi-Fi 7 和 Thunderbolt 4 连接功能，并配有 14 英寸 OLED 触摸显示屏，预计续航时间为 16 小时。

Features and design The device features a 14-inch OLED touchscreen display with a resolution of 2.880 by 1.800 pixels. Inside, it is equipped with an eight-core Intel Core Ultra 5 325 processor, 16 gigabytes of LPDDR5X memory and 512 gigabytes of NVMe storage. Its body is made of magnesium-aluminum in Elemental Blue. It weighs about 1,14 kg and is 0,55 inches thick.

功能与设计：该设备配备 14 英寸 OLED 触摸屏，分辨率为 2880 x 1800 像素。内部搭载八核 Intel Core Ultra 5 325 处理器、16GB LPDDR5X 内存和 512GB NVMe 存储空间。机身采用镁铝合金材质，配色为元素蓝。重量约为 1.14 公斤，厚度为 0.55 英寸。

Connectivity and autonomy The laptop includes 10Gbps USB Type-C ports and a Thunderbolt 4 port, while it supports Wi-Fi 7 and Bluetooth 6. It also has a 1080p webcam with a physical privacy shutter, while its battery offers approximately 16 hours of autonomy.

连接性与续航：这款笔记本电脑包含 10Gbps USB Type-C 端口和 Thunderbolt 4 端口，并支持 Wi-Fi 7 和蓝牙 6。它还配备了带有物理隐私遮盖的 1080p 网络摄像头，电池续航时间约为 16 小时。
