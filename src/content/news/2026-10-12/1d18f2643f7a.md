What’s in a name. To date I’ve resisted the siren call of nominative determinism so I can’t answer to whether D-Bus is actually hell or not. I considered writing a D-Bus review as a joke, but then I decided to watch paint dry. I’ve never heard Linux nerds express any strong opinions on the topic (for which they’re prone to do; see Systemd, Wayland, etc.)

名字里有什么？迄今为止，我抵制住了“提名决定论”的诱惑，所以我无法回答 D-Bus 是否真的是地狱。我曾考虑写一篇 D-Bus 的评论作为笑话，但后来我决定还是去看墙上的油漆变干。我从未听过 Linux 极客们对这个话题表达过任何强烈的观点（尽管他们通常很喜欢这样做；参见 Systemd、Wayland 等）。

I did go down the Wikipedia rabbit hole of cognitive psychology and linguistics. There’s an interesting read on Rebracketing where a word like “Hamburger” becomes “ham + burger” which was altered from the origin: “Hamburg + er”. This is a form of Folk etymology where words can change due to unfamiliarity.

我确实钻进了认知心理学和语言学的维基百科兔子洞。关于“重切分”（Rebracketing）有一篇有趣的读物，比如“Hamburger”一词变成了“ham + burger”，而它原本的词源是“Hamburg + er”。这是一种民间词源学，词汇会因为陌生感而发生改变。

Etymology. My surname could simply come from “bushel”. An imperial unit of volume still favoured by fantasy novels, before the Trollocs attack and shit gets real. “Bushel” dates back to Middle English with origins in the Old French word “boissel”.

词源。我的姓氏可能仅仅源于“bushel”（蒲式耳）。这是一种英制体积单位，在巨魔攻击、事情变得严重之前的奇幻小说中仍然很受欢迎。“Bushel”可以追溯到中古英语，起源于古法语单词“boissel”。

At the risk of being part French, I’m inclined to believe an alternate theory that my name derives from “Bossall” a hamlet in North Yorkshire. But that would make me part Yorkshireman. I’m not sure which is worse.

冒着可能有一半法国血统的风险，我倾向于相信另一种理论，即我的名字源于北约克郡的一个小村庄“Bossall”。但这会让我有一半约克郡人的血统。我不确定哪种情况更糟。

It’s not a very common surname. If this Geneanet heat map has any credibility, the name favours the south of England. That might support the French theory? Liverpool has the highest number of Bushells though. Bushells is also an Australian brand of tea founded by tea dealer Alfred Bushell in 1883. Alfred was born in Worcestershire, England (pronounced: Wrcstrshr).

这不是一个很常见的姓氏。如果这个 Geneanet 热力图有可信度的话，这个名字在英格兰南部更常见。这或许支持了法国理论？不过利物浦的 Bushell 姓氏人数最多。Bushells 也是一个澳大利亚茶叶品牌，由茶叶商 Alfred Bushell 于1883年创立。Alfred 出生于英格兰的伍斯特郡（发音：Wrcstrshr）。

Interestingly, Wikipedia notes: After the Norman conquest of England; the Domesday Book noted in 1086 that in seven of the twelve hundreds covering Worcestershire, the Crown had no authority. The Domesday Book always references Bossall (the Yorkshire hamlet) as Boscele or Bosciale; names that are Anglo Saxon in origin.

有趣的是，维基百科指出：诺曼征服英格兰后，1086年的《末日审判书》记录到，在覆盖伍斯特郡的十二个百户区中，有七个王室没有管辖权。《末日审判书》总是将 Bossall（约克郡的小村庄）称为 Boscele 或 Bosciale；这些名字起源于盎格鲁-撒克逊语。

When I say “Bushell” over the phone my pronunciation is often mistaken for saying “Buchel”. Some sources say they’re from the same Anglo Saxon origin. These distribution maps for Bushell and Buchel cast doubt on that idea (if Forebears data is accurate). “Büchel” also has different origins if you stick an umlaut on it.

当我在电话里说“Bushell”时，我的发音经常被误认为是“Buchel”。一些资料称它们来自同一个盎格鲁-撒克逊起源。这些 Bushell 和 Buchel 的分布图对这一观点提出了质疑（如果 Forebears 的数据准确的话）。如果你在“Büchel”上加个变音符号，它也有不同的起源。

I suspect I’m dealing with multiple different names of Anglo Saxon and Norman origin. There are many spelling variations of “Bushell” and similar names in historic records (though few have survived). Most people were illiterate until recent decades so spelling variations are not surprising. It seems probable to me that whenever families adopted a spelling, a variation from the wrong lineage could’ve been written due to phonetic differences.

我怀疑我面对的是多个源自盎格鲁-撒克逊和诺曼的不同名字。在历史记录中，“Bushell”和类似名字有许多拼写变体（尽管留存下来的很少）。大多数人在近几十年之前都是文盲，所以拼写变体并不令人惊讶。在我看来，很可能每当家庭采用一种拼写时，由于语音差异，可能会写出错误血统的变体。

Even if I traced my ancestry back hundreds of years, I’d likely reach a moment when a scribe wrote “Bushell” in some form and my ancestor just shrugged because he couldn’t read. I started using it seriously once I had a design portfolio. If you’re curious you can see past versions at: legacy.dbushell.com.

域名里有什么？域名是一个奇怪的系统。它们是临时的 URI，被租用并最终不可避免地丢失。我花了一半的人生在 dbushell.com 上发布内容。当我有了设计作品集后，我开始认真使用它。如果你好奇，可以在 legacy.dbushell.com 查看过去的版本。
