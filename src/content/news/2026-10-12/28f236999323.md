The new Apple TV could have a built-in mic and a remote with a speaker.

新款 Apple TV 可能会内置麦克风，并配备带有扬声器的遥控器。

The overdue Apple TV refresh makes it easier to talk to Siri and find your remote.

姗姗来迟的 Apple TV 更新让与 Siri 对话以及寻找遥控器变得更加容易。

The latest rumors are that the new Apple TV will have a built-in microphone, while the upgraded remote will get a speaker. The mic would let users control the set-top box using Siri without having to speak into the remote or a connected HomePod device. That could also let the Apple TV serve as a simple smart home hub — controlling thermostats and locking doors — without the need to hunt for the remote.

最新的传闻称，新款 Apple TV 将内置麦克风，而升级后的遥控器将增加一个扬声器。麦克风可以让用户使用 Siri 控制机顶盒，而无需对着遥控器或连接的 HomePod 设备说话。这也可能让 Apple TV 充当一个简单的智能家居中心——控制恒温器和锁门——而无需到处寻找遥控器。

If you happen to misplace the remote, the new integrated speaker should make it easier to track down. It could play an audio alert to guide you to its location, whether it got carelessly kicked under the couch or a child mischievously hid it in a drawer.

如果你不小心弄丢了遥控器，新集成的扬声器应该能让你更容易找到它。它可以通过播放音频警报来引导你找到它的位置，无论它是被不小心踢到了沙发底下，还是被孩子调皮地藏在了抽屉里。

This is on top of the upgrade to an A19 chip that will power Siri AI. The Apple TV is expected to start shipping on October 30th, alongside the refreshed HomePod Mini.

除了这些功能外，设备还升级到了为 Siri AI 提供支持的 A19 芯片。预计 Apple TV 将于 10 月 30 日与新款 HomePod Mini 一起开始发货。
