### Nitter: Update Oct 10th seeking funding and legal help

On 24 August 2026 X Corp. sent cease and desist letters demanding a permanent takedown of Nitter instances and the project's repository. On 8 September 2026, they sent another, with the same demands and legal threats.

2026年8月24日，X Corp. 发送了停止侵权函，要求永久关闭 Nitter 实例及该项目的代码仓库。2026年9月8日，他们又发送了另一封信函，提出了相同的要求和法律威胁。

My September 7th update said the project would continue with legal representation in place. That was based on assurances I received and believed, which have since turned out to be untrue. I apologize for the misleading information.

我在9月7日的更新中曾表示，该项目将在有法律代理的情况下继续进行。那是基于我当时收到并相信的保证，但后来证明这些保证并不属实。对于误导性信息，我深表歉意。

Nitter currently has no legal counsel.

Nitter 目前没有法律顾问。
