### What to know about the landmark Warner Bros. Discovery sale

Earlier this year, the streaming and entertainment industry witnessed one of its most high-stakes megadeals ever, stunning industry observers. Not only is it historic in its size, but it is also predicted to disrupt Hollywood and the media business as we know it.

今年早些时候，流媒体和娱乐行业见证了史上风险最高的巨额交易之一，令业内观察人士感到震惊。这笔交易不仅规模具有历史意义，而且预计将颠覆我们所熟知的整个好莱坞和媒体行业。

After years of Warner Bros. Discovery (WBD) struggling under the weight of billions of dollars in debt, compounded by declining cable viewership and fierce competition from streaming platforms, the company has been considering major strategic changes, including selling its entertainment assets to one of its rivals.

在经历了多年数十亿美元债务的重压，加之有线电视收视率下滑以及来自流媒体平台的激烈竞争后，华纳兄弟探索公司（WBD）一直在考虑重大的战略调整，包括将其娱乐资产出售给竞争对手。

Several major players saw the potential in acquiring the media giant, and in December, Netflix announced it would acquire WBD’s studios and streaming for $82.7 billion. But in a surprise eleventh-hour move in late February, the David Ellison-run Paramount became the winner of this bidding war, offering $111 billion to acquire all of WBD’s assets, including its studios, HBO, streaming platforms, games, and TV networks such as CNN and HGTV.

几家主要参与者看到了收购这家媒体巨头的潜力。去年12月，Netflix宣布将以827亿美元收购WBD的制片厂和流媒体业务。但在2月下旬最后一刻的意外举动中，大卫·埃里森（David Ellison）掌舵的派拉蒙（Paramount）赢得了这场竞购战，出价1110亿美元收购WBD的所有资产，包括其制片厂、HBO、流媒体平台、游戏以及CNN和HGTV等电视网络。

Paramount was recently acquired by Ellison with significant support from his father, Larry Ellison — the Oracle chairman, world’s sixth-richest person, and major Trump donor. Paramount’s offer was approved by the U.S. Department of Justice (DOJ) in June. A federal judge then paused the deal after a lawsuit was filed on July 13 by a coalition of 12 state attorneys general. However, a judge approved it in late September, with the acquisition finally official as of October 6. Let’s break down exactly what happened and what could come next.

派拉蒙近期被埃里森收购，并得到了其父亲拉里·埃里森（Larry Ellison）的大力支持——拉里·埃里森是甲骨文公司董事长、全球第六大富豪，也是特朗普的主要捐款人。派拉蒙的报价于6月获得美国司法部（DOJ）批准。随后，在12个州的总检察长联盟于7月13日提起诉讼后，一名联邦法官暂停了该交易。然而，法官在9月下旬批准了该交易，收购最终于10月6日正式生效。让我们详细剖析一下到底发生了什么，以及接下来可能会发生什么。

What has happened so far? This all started back in October 2025 when Warner Bros. Discovery revealed it was exploring a potential sale after receiving unsolicited interest from several major players in the industry. The bidding process quickly became competitive, and Paramount and Comcast emerged as serious contenders, with Paramount initially viewed as the frontrunner.

目前为止发生了什么？这一切始于2025年10月，当时华纳兄弟探索公司在收到业内几家主要参与者的主动接洽后，透露正在探索潜在的出售事宜。竞标过程迅速变得激烈，派拉蒙和康卡斯特（Comcast）成为强有力的竞争者，派拉蒙最初被视为领跑者。

However, WBD’s board eventually determined that an offer from the streaming giant Netflix was the most attractive. Netflix offered $82.7 billion for just Warner’s film, television, and streaming assets. Thus began the bidding war. Paramount believed its bid of approximately $108 billion for all of Warner’s assets was superior to Netflix’s offer that focused on just the studios and streaming.

然而，WBD董事会最终认定流媒体巨头Netflix的报价最具吸引力。Netflix出价827亿美元，仅收购华纳的电影、电视和流媒体资产。竞购战由此开始。派拉蒙认为其以约1080亿美元收购华纳全部资产的报价，优于Netflix仅针对制片厂和流媒体的报价。

To sweeten its deal, Netflix amended its agreement in January to an all-cash offer at $27.75 per share of Warner Bros. Discovery, further reassuring investors and paving the way for the deal to proceed. Paramount persisted in its attempts to acquire WBD. Still, the Warner board repeatedly rejected its offers, citing concerns about Paramount’s heavy debt load and the increased risk associated with its proposal, including concern over the suite of investors bankrolling Paramount’s bid, which includes Saudi, Qatari, and Abu Dhabi sovereign wealth funds.

为了优化交易，Netflix在1月份修改了协议，提出以每股27.75美元的现金收购华纳兄弟探索公司的股份，进一步安抚了投资者并为交易的推进铺平了道路。派拉蒙坚持尝试收购WBD。尽管如此，华纳董事会多次拒绝了其报价，理由是担心派拉蒙沉重的债务负担及其提案带来的风险增加，包括对资助派拉蒙竞标的投资者组合（其中包括沙特、卡塔尔和阿布扎比的主权财富基金）的担忧。

The board noted that Paramount’s offer would have left the combined company burdened with $87 billion in debt, a risk they were unwilling to take at the time. In January, Paramount filed a lawsuit seeking more information about the Netflix deal. A month later, the company sought to sweeten its deal by announcing it would offer a $0.25 per share “ticking fee” to WBD shareholders for each quarter the deal fails to close by December 31, 2026.

董事会指出，派拉蒙的报价将使合并后的公司背负870亿美元的债务，这是他们当时不愿承担的风险。1月，派拉蒙提起诉讼，寻求了解更多关于Netflix交易的信息。一个月后，该公司宣布，如果交易在2026年12月31日前未能完成，将为WBD股东提供每季度每股0.25美元的“延期费”，以此优化其交易条件。

It also said it would pay the $2.8 billion breakup fee if Warner backs out of its deal with Netflix. Then, in a final attempt to secure a deal, Paramount increased its offer to $31 per share in February. This prompted the WBD board to prolong discussions with Paramount regarding a potential agreement, considering it as a superior offer. Netflix declined to increase its bid and withdrew from the negotiations.

该公司还表示，如果华纳退出与Netflix的交易，它将支付28亿美元的违约金。随后，为了达成交易，派拉蒙在2月将报价提高到每股31美元。这促使WBD董事会延长了与派拉蒙关于潜在协议的讨论，将其视为更优报价。Netflix拒绝提高报价并退出了谈判。

“The transaction we negotiated would have created shareholder value with a clear path to regulatory approval,” Netflix co-CEOs Ted Sarandos and Greg Peters said in a statement on February 26. “However, we’ve always been disciplined, and at the price required to match Paramount Skydance’s latest offer, the deal is no longer financially attractive, so we are declining to match the Paramount Skydance bid.”

“我们谈判达成的交易本可以创造股东价值，并拥有明确的监管审批路径，”Netflix联席首席执行官泰德·萨兰多斯（Ted Sarandos）和格雷格·彼得斯（Greg Peters）在2月26日的声明中表示。“然而，我们一直保持自律，以匹配派拉蒙天空之舞（Paramount Skydance）最新报价所需的价格，该交易在财务上已不再具有吸引力，因此我们拒绝匹配派拉蒙天空之舞的报价。”

In addition to the billions Paramount already holds in debt, the company is also set to assume the approximately $33 billion in debt Warner Bros. Discovery holds under the agreement. The deal will be backed by a $54 billion debt commitment from Bank of America, Merrill Lynch, Citi, and Apollo Global Management, as well as $45.7 billion in equity from Larry Ellison.

除了派拉蒙本身持有的数十亿美元债务外，根据协议，该公司还将承担华纳兄弟探索公司约330亿美元的债务。该交易将由美国银行、美林证券、花旗集团和阿波罗全球管理公司提供的540亿美元债务承诺，以及拉里·埃里森提供的457亿美元股权支持。

Regulatory hurdles and other concerns. In addition to the assumption of substantial debt posing a significant financial burden, Paramount faced several other hurdles in its deal with WBD. For one, Ellison has warned about significant job reductions that are expected in the near future. There have already been widespread concerns among critics about potential job losses and lower wages.

监管障碍及其他担忧。除了承担巨额债务带来的沉重财务负担外，派拉蒙在与WBD的交易中还面临其他几个障碍。首先，埃里森已经警告称，预计在不久的将来会进行大规模裁员。批评人士中已经出现了对潜在失业和工资降低的广泛担忧。

Ellison is also a controversial figure in the industry, and his ownership of CBS News has been seen as sympathetic and supportive of the administration of Donald Trump, of whom his father, Larry Ellison, is a major donor. Under Ellison’s ownership of Paramount, reporting critical of the administration has been shelved or received increased scrutiny from Ellison or his appointed head of CBS News, the conservative provocateur Bari Weiss.

埃里森在业内也是一位颇具争议的人物，他对比CBS新闻的所有权被视为对唐纳德·特朗普政府的同情和支持，而他的父亲拉里·埃里森正是特朗普的主要捐款人。在埃里森拥有派拉蒙期间，批评政府的报道被搁置，或受到了埃里森本人及其任命的CBS新闻负责人——保守派煽动者巴里·韦斯（Bari Weiss）的严格审查。

This has led to some concern among employees at Warner-owned CNN. Trump has personally sought concessions from news divisions critical of him, including a $16 million settlement from CBS, before his FCC would approve the Ellison takeover of Paramount. Before Netflix bowed out of the deal, Trump pressured the company to fire the former Biden White House official Susan Rice from its board.
