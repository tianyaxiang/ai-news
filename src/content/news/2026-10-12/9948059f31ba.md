# Prime Video unveils Blade Runner 2099 full trailer at NYCC

We got our first look at Prime Video’s new sci-fi series Blade Runner 2099 in July at San Diego Comic-Con. With the show’s premiere roughly six weeks away, the streamer unveiled the full trailer this weekend at New York Comic-Con. Co-stars Michelle Yeoh and Hunter Schafer (Euphoria) joined showrunner Silka Luisa (Shining Girls) onstage for the big reveal.

我们在七月的圣地亚哥国际动漫展上首次看到了 Prime Video 的新科幻剧集《银翼杀手 2099》。随着该剧首播还有大约六周的时间，流媒体平台在本周末的纽约动漫展上发布了完整预告片。主演杨紫琼和亨特·莎弗（《亢奋》）与剧集主管西尔卡·路易莎（《闪亮女孩》）一同登台，揭开了这部作品的神秘面纱。

As Luisa described the show during the NYCC panel Saturday night: We are 50 years after [Blade Runner] 2049 and, bad news, the worst has happened. The replicant uprising that has been bubbling up in the films has taken place and the humans have lost. So the creations have surpassed their creators. And the series, the big question it asks is, ‘What happens when our time as a species ends?’ 2099 is taking place at a junction point when one species is ending and a new one is rising.

正如路易莎在周六晚上的纽约动漫展小组讨论中所描述的那样：“故事发生在《银翼杀手 2049》的 50 年后，坏消息是，最糟糕的情况已经发生了。电影中酝酿已久的复制人起义已经爆发，人类战败了。造物主已被造物所超越。这部剧提出的核心问题是：‘当人类作为一个物种的时代终结时，会发生什么？’《2099》发生在一个物种走向终结、新物种正在崛起的交汇点。”

The first film, something I loved about it, was that it was looking at mortality on a micro level. Roy Batty and the other replicants were fighting for more life. This show asks that same question but on a macro level. Mankind—what happens when we realize our time is running out? And are we going to accept that loss? Can we hold on to our humanity during it?

“我喜欢第一部电影的地方在于，它从微观层面审视了死亡。罗伊·巴蒂和其他复制人在为争取更多生命而战。这部剧在宏观层面上提出了同样的问题。当人类意识到我们的时间所剩无几时，会发生什么？我们是否会接受这种失去？在此过程中，我们还能坚守人性吗？”

Yeoh stars as Olwen, the dying Replicant, while Schafer plays Cora. The cast also includes Hugo Hamlet as Buster Friendly, as well as Dmitri Abold, Lewis Gribben, Katelyn Rose Downey, Daniel Rigby, Johnny Harris, Amy Lennox, Sheila Atim, Matthew Needham, Tom Burke, and Maurizio Lombardi—all in as-yet-undisclosed roles. Prime Video will drop all eight episodes of Blade Runner 2099 on November 25, 2026.

杨紫琼饰演垂死的复制人奥尔温，莎弗饰演科拉。演员阵容还包括饰演巴斯特·弗兰德利的雨果·哈姆雷特，以及德米特里·阿博尔德、刘易斯·格里本、凯特琳·罗斯·唐尼、丹尼尔·里格比、约翰尼·哈里斯、艾米·伦诺克斯、希拉·阿蒂姆、马修·尼达姆、汤姆·伯克和毛里齐奥·隆巴迪——他们饰演的角色尚未公布。Prime Video 将于 2026 年 11 月 25 日一次性放出《银翼杀手 2099》的全部八集。

# Godzilla Minus Zero

We also got a new trailer for Godzilla Minus Zero, the sequel to director Takashi Yamazaki’s gloriously grounded kaiju epic, Godzilla Minus One, which became a major international hit and snagged an Oscar for best visual effects. Godzilla Minus One focused on kamikaze pilot Kōichi Shikishima, who struggled with survivor’s guilt after he failed to take action when Godzilla attacked the Japanese base on Odo Island in 1945.

我们还看到了《哥斯拉：负零》的新预告片，这是导演山崎贵执导的怪兽史诗巨作《哥斯拉-1.0》的续集。前作不仅在国际上大获成功，还斩获了奥斯卡最佳视觉效果奖。《哥斯拉-1.0》聚焦于神风特攻队飞行员敷岛浩一，他在 1945 年哥斯拉袭击大户岛日本基地时未能采取行动，此后一直饱受幸存者内疚感的折磨。

Kōichi was one of only two survivors and rebuilt a life with a woman named Noriko and the orphaned baby she rescued, Akiko. But then Godzilla re-emerged, and Kōichi resolved to die fighting the monster to atone for Odo Island的罪过。

敷岛是仅有的两名幸存者之一，他与一名叫典子的女子以及她救下的孤儿明子重建了生活。但随后哥斯拉再次出现，敷岛决定以死相搏，对抗这只怪兽，以赎回在大户岛犯下的过错。

Spoiler alert: Kōichi survived, thanks to Tachibana’s forgiveness and mechanical ingenuity. He reunited with Noriko, who had a strange black bruise on her neck. And we saw a chunk of Godzilla’s remains start to regenerate, leaving things open for the sequel. Per the official synopsis for Godzilla Minus Zero: War reduced Japan to zero, and Godzilla plunged it into minus. Two years have passed since then, and the country has faced agonizing struggles to achieve recovery and finally reclaim its daily life. Just as humanity managed to cling to a hard-won peace, a new threat strikes, crushing hope in an instant. There is no third time. Everything ends here.

剧透预警：多亏了橘健治的宽恕和机械天赋，敷岛活了下来。他与典子重逢，但典子的脖子上出现了一块奇怪的黑色淤青。我们还看到哥斯拉的一块残骸开始再生，为续集留下了悬念。根据《哥斯拉：负零》的官方剧情简介：战争将日本化为零，而哥斯拉将其推向了负数。自那时起已经过去了两年，国家在痛苦的挣扎中寻求复苏，并最终恢复了日常生活。就在人类好不容易守住来之不易的和平时，新的威胁袭来，瞬间粉碎了希望。没有第三次机会了。一切都将在此终结。

Most of the main cast from Godzilla Minus One are returning: Ryunosuke Kamiki as Kōichi; Minami Hamabe as his wife, Noriko; Sae Nagatani as Akiko; Hidetaka Yoshioka as Kenji Noda, director of the Large-Scale Disaster Response Bureau; Kuranosuke Sasaki as Seiji Akitsu, captain of the Shinsei Maru; Miou Tanaka as Tatsuo Hotta, former captain of the destroyer Yukikaze; Sakura Ando as Sumiko Ōta, Kōichi’s neighbor; and Yuki Yamada as Shirō Mizushima, a Shinsei Maru crewman. The cast also includes Min Tanaka as biologist Kanji Murakami and Masami Nagasawa as Aoyama. Godzilla Minus Zero hits theaters on November 3, 2026. Legendary Pictures plans to release Godzilla x Kong: Supernova—its Monsterverse sequel to 2024’s Godzilla x Kong: The New Empire—in 2027.

《哥斯拉-1.0》的大部分主要演员都将回归：神木隆之介饰演敷岛浩一；滨边美波饰演他的妻子典子；永谷咲恩饰演明子；吉冈秀隆饰演大规模灾害对策局局长野田健治；佐佐木藏之介饰演新生丸号船长秋津清治；田中美央饰演前雪风号驱逐舰舰长堀田辰雄；安藤樱饰演敷岛的邻居太田澄子；山田裕贵饰演新生丸号船员水岛四郎。演员阵容还包括饰演生物学家村上完治的田中泯和饰演青山的长泽雅美。《哥斯拉：负零》将于 2026 年 11 月 3 日上映。传奇影业计划在 2027 年发布《哥斯拉大战金刚：超新星》，这是其“怪兽宇宙”继 2024 年《哥斯拉大战金刚 2：帝国崛起》之后的续作。
