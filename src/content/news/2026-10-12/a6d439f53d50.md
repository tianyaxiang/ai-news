NASA dumped over 800GB of Artemis II data online

NASA 在线发布了超过 800GB 的“阿耳忒弥斯 2 号”（Artemis II）任务数据。

NASA’s treasure trove of images, audio, and video offers an unprecedented view inside a historic space mission.

NASA 提供的图像、音频和视频宝库，为人们深入了解这一历史性太空任务提供了前所未有的视角。

If you thought you’d seen all there is to see from the Artemis II mission, you’re wrong. This week NASA posted over 800GB worth of science data, images, and videos on the Artemis II: Sights & Sounds of Science site.

如果你认为自己已经看遍了“阿耳忒弥斯 2 号”任务的所有内容，那你就错了。本周，NASA 在“阿耳忒弥斯 2 号：科学的视觉与声音”（Artemis II: Sights & Sounds of Science）网站上发布了超过 800GB 的科学数据、图像和视频。

The archive includes over 11,000 photos and videos, over 8.5 hours of audio, and crew-annotated images, such as the one marking where they saw impact flashes on the dark side of the Moon. Together, they offer an unprecedented view inside a historic space mission.

该档案包含超过 11,000 张照片和视频、超过 8.5 小时的音频，以及由机组人员标注的图像，例如标记他们在月球背面观测到撞击闪光位置的图片。这些资料共同为人们深入了解这一历史性太空任务提供了前所未有的视角。

One of the more exciting reveals was footage of the Artemis II team telling the ground team back in Houston about its observation of impact flashes. Impact flashes have been seen before, but these ones on the dark side of the Moon could have been the faintest observed with the human eye, and everyone’s excitement is palpable in the clip.

其中比较令人兴奋的披露内容之一，是“阿耳忒弥斯 2 号”团队向休斯顿地面团队报告其观测到撞击闪光的视频片段。虽然此前也曾观测到过撞击闪光，但这些发生在月球背面的闪光可能是人类肉眼观测到的最微弱的闪光，视频中每个人的兴奋之情溢于言表。

Alongside the dump, NASA also released a trio of documents that help supplement the trove of unfiltered data. That includes the Artemis II Preliminary Lunar Science Report, the Artemis II Lunar Science Operations Report, and the Artemis II Lunar Science Data User Guide.

除了发布这些数据外，NASA 还发布了三份文件，以帮助补充这些未经处理的数据宝库。其中包括《阿耳忒弥斯 2 号初步月球科学报告》、《阿耳忒弥斯 2 号月球科学操作报告》以及《阿耳忒弥斯 2 号月球科学数据用户指南》。
