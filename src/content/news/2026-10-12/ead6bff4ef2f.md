Title: Will the HomeView be Apple’s first smart home success?

标题：HomeView 会是苹果在智能家居领域的首次成功吗？

In Brief Posted: 2:14 PM PDT · October 11, 2026 Image Credits: Brian Heater / TechCrunch Anthony Ha Will the HomeView be Apple’s first smart home success? Apple will announce a new smart home hub on Tuesday called the HomeView, according to Bloomberg’s Mark Gurman.

简讯发布时间：太平洋夏令时间 2026 年 10 月 11 日下午 2:14。图片来源：Brian Heater / TechCrunch，作者：Anthony Ha。HomeView 会是苹果在智能家居领域的首次成功吗？据彭博社的马克·古尔曼（Mark Gurman）称，苹果将于周二发布一款名为 HomeView 的新型智能家居中枢。

Over the past couple of years, we’ve seen multiple reports that Apple was working on a new smart home product, but now it seems the product has a name and a launch date. Gurman described the HomeView as a Siri-powered device with a display and an A18 chip, allowing customers to control their other smart home devices.

在过去几年里，我们已经看到多篇关于苹果正在研发新型智能家居产品的报道，但现在看来该产品已经有了名称和发布日期。古尔曼将 HomeView 描述为一款搭载 Siri、配备显示屏和 A18 芯片的设备，允许用户控制其其他的智能家居设备。

The HomeView will reportedly be available in two models — one designed for countertops and the other to be mounted on walls. This would be Apple’s most serious bet on the smart home to date, after it failed to get much traction for its HomeKit software framework or its HomePod smart speaker.

据报道，HomeView 将提供两种型号——一种专为台面设计，另一种则用于壁挂。在 HomeKit 软件框架和 HomePod 智能音箱未能获得广泛关注之后，这将是苹果迄今为止在智能家居领域最重大的押注。

Gurman reports that on Tuesday, Apple will also be unveiling updated versions of two other home devices, namely the Apple TV set-top box and the HomePod mini. Earlier this week, he said that Apple plans to announce a partnership with LG to create a smart lock, a thermostat, and a doorbell.

古尔曼报道称，苹果还将在周二发布另外两款家居设备的更新版本，即 Apple TV 机顶盒和 HomePod mini。本周早些时候，他表示苹果计划宣布与 LG 建立合作伙伴关系，共同开发智能锁、恒温器和门铃。
