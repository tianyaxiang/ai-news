### Why the LeapPad couldn’t survive the iPad
### 为什么 LeapPad 无法在 iPad 的冲击下生存

For multiple years, the hottest toy in the United States was a book. But not just any book: a smart book that could play sounds and respond to taps and help kids learn to read. It was the kind of gadget seemingly any parent could get behind, but it came with a high price — and some unexpected parenting questions.
多年来，美国最热门的玩具是一本书。但这可不是普通的书：这是一本能够播放声音、响应点击并帮助孩子学习阅读的智能书。这似乎是任何家长都会支持的那种小玩意，但它价格昂贵，同时也带来了一些意想不到的育儿问题。

On this episode of Version History, David Pierce is joined by The Verge’s Andru Marino and Jennifer Pattison Tuohy to explain how the LeapPad worked, why it was so enticing to both kids and adults, and why the company that made it could never quite follow it up.
在本期《版本历史》（Version History）节目中，大卫·皮尔斯（David Pierce）与 The Verge 的安德鲁·马里诺（Andru Marino）和詹妮弗·帕蒂森·图伊（Jennifer Pattison Tuohy）共同探讨了 LeapPad 的工作原理，它为何对儿童和成年人都有如此大的吸引力，以及为什么制造它的公司始终无法推出后续的成功产品。
