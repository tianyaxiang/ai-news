### 13 Best Gifts for Every Traveler Should Have (2026)

Save this story. Even the most seasoned globe-trotter likely finds traveling stressful. Between panic-packing, navigating cramped terminals, and living out of a suitcase, each phase of being away from home can be overstimulating. That’s why the best gifts for travelers are ones that focus on ease and peace of mind.

保存这篇文章。即使是经验最丰富的环球旅行者，也难免会感到旅行压力重重。从匆忙打包、穿梭于拥挤的航站楼，到过着行李箱里的生活，离家的每一个阶段都可能让人过度疲劳。因此，送给旅行者最好的礼物，往往是那些能带来轻松感和安心感的物品。

Jet-setters may think they have their travel routine down to a science, but the perfect gift can enhance any experience, whether it's a three-day business retreat or a two-week adventure across Europe. Below, find my favorite gifts for frequent travelers, from an essential suitcase set and a portable e-reader to comfortable compression socks and the perfect neck pillow.

经常飞行的旅客可能认为他们已经掌握了旅行的窍门，但一份完美的礼物可以提升任何体验，无论是三天的商务出差还是为期两周的欧洲探险。以下是我为常旅客挑选的最爱礼物，从必备的行李箱套装和便携式电子阅读器，到舒适的压力袜和完美的颈枕，应有尽有。

### A Matching Luggage Set: Quince Expandable Small Carry-On & Large Check-In Suitcase Set
Every traveler needs a luggage set that includes a checked case and a carry-on. Chances are, the jet-setter in your life already has one they love. But if they’re due for a refresh, this set from Quince is an excellent (and expandable) gift. The brand makes some of my favorite travel gear, with pieces that look remarkably similar to designs from premium brands—but at a fraction of the price.

### 行李箱套装：Quince 可扩展小型登机箱与大型托运箱套装
每位旅行者都需要一套包含托运箱和登机箱的行李套装。你生活中的常旅客可能已经有了心仪的行李箱，但如果他们需要更换，Quince 的这套产品是一个极佳的（且可扩展的）礼物选择。该品牌生产的一些旅行装备是我的最爱，其外观与高端品牌的设计非常相似，但价格却只有后者的一小部分。

### Vacation Reading: Amazon Kindle Paperwhite and Paperwhite Signature Edition (2024, 12th Generation)
For the bookworm who’s always traveling, the Kindle Paperwhite is the obvious gift. Reviewer Nena Farrell says the 12th-generation model has the highest contrast ratio of any Kindle and lasts up to three months on a single charge. Whether you're reading in direct sun or a dim plane cabin, its 7-inch display is crisp and easy on the eyes thanks to its warm backlighting.

### 假期阅读：亚马逊 Kindle Paperwhite 和 Paperwhite Signature Edition（2024，第 12 代）
对于总是奔波在路上的书虫来说，Kindle Paperwhite 是显而易见的礼物选择。评论员 Nena Farrell 表示，第 12 代机型拥有 Kindle 系列中最高的对比度，单次充电可使用长达三个月。无论是在直射阳光下还是在昏暗的机舱内阅读，其 7 英寸显示屏都清晰且护眼，这得益于其暖色调背光功能。

### Passport Control: Calpak Luka Zip Passport Wallet
If you’re traveling abroad—or just don’t yet have your Real ID—a travel wallet is a must. The puffy Luka from Calpak comes in 23 colors, so you’re sure to find an option that fits your giftee’s vibe. It’s got plenty of room for cash, cards, your passport, and other documents, and it zips securely for peace of mind.

### 护照管理：Calpak Luka 拉链护照包
如果你要出国旅行，或者还没有办理“真实身份”（Real ID），那么一个旅行钱包是必不可少的。Calpak 出品的这款蓬松的 Luka 护照包有 23 种颜色可选，你一定能找到符合收礼人风格的款式。它有足够的空间存放现金、卡片、护照和其他证件，并配有拉链，确保物品安全，让人倍感安心。

### Travel Organizer: Eagle Creek Pack-It Reveal Carry-On Set
Frequent travelers who don't use collapsible cubes when packing are struggling, and they don't even know it. Give them this travel tip: Eagle Creek's set is the best, made of durable fabric with compression and high-quality zipper pulls. Editor Kat Merck says there's just enough mesh for air circulation to prevent odor buildup or fabric degradation, and the bottom and back of the cubes are opaque for privacy.

### 旅行收纳：Eagle Creek Pack-It Reveal 登机收纳套装
那些打包时不使用可折叠收纳袋的常旅客其实是在自讨苦吃，而且他们自己还没意识到。送他们这个旅行小贴士：Eagle Creek 的套装是最好的，它由耐用面料制成，具有压缩功能和高质量的拉链头。编辑 Kat Merck 表示，网眼设计恰到好处，既能保证空气流通以防止异味积聚或面料损坏，收纳袋的底部和背面又是半透明的，以保护隐私。
