### The Best Travel Coffee Mug: Hydro Flask Coffee with Flex Sip Lid

**最佳旅行咖啡杯：Hydro Flask Coffee with Flex Sip Lid**

The Hydro Flask Coffee with Flex Sip Lid is my go-to travel mug. It’s low-maintenance and built like a tank, making it a forgiving choice for the clumsiest of sippers. The brand's powder coating can handle clammy hands, and the 16-ounce tumbler fits into most car cup holders.

Hydro Flask Coffee with Flex Sip Lid 是我首选的旅行杯。它维护简单，结构坚固如坦克，对于最笨手笨脚的人来说也是一个宽容的选择。该品牌的粉末涂层可以应对潮湿的手掌，16 盎司的杯身适合大多数汽车杯架。

It's also a multipurpose mug that isn't limited to housing just your steaming morning brew. Because Hydro Flask wide-mouth lids are conveniently interchangeable, you can swap in a straw lid for cold beverages (or anything else you'd like to drink). And if you purchase the travel mug directly from Hydro Flask, you can get it engraved with text or a graphic, in addition to picking from the 10-plus available color options.

它也是一个多功能杯子，不仅限于盛放你早晨冒着热气的咖啡。由于 Hydro Flask 的宽口盖可以方便地互换，你可以换上吸管盖来饮用冷饮（或任何你想喝的东西）。如果你直接从 Hydro Flask 购买，除了可以从 10 多种颜色中选择外，还可以刻上文字或图案。

### A Mug That Can Handle Your Mornings: Hydro Flask Mug

**能应付你早晨时光的杯子：Hydro Flask Mug**

Imagine sitting by a campfire under the stars, sipping hot cocoa, then waking up at sunrise to a cup of coffee by the lake. A handleless mug just doesn't do that scene justice. Hydro Flask's travel coffee mug brings that camp aesthetic to life. It also just feels great to hold in any setting—including for morning dog walks or while you get yourself ready for your day at home. The slider lid keeps out curious bugs and helps to slow the release of heat, but it’s not leakproof.

想象一下坐在星空下的篝火旁啜饮热可可，然后在日出时分在湖边喝上一杯咖啡。没有把手的杯子无法衬托这种场景。Hydro Flask 的旅行咖啡杯将这种露营美学带入了现实。无论是在什么环境下，比如早晨遛狗或在家准备开始新的一天时，握着它感觉都很棒。滑动盖可以防止虫子进入并有助于减缓热量散失，但它并不防漏。

I also appreciate the mug's stainless steel rim that's smooth to sip from sans lid. Just note that it’s not TPU powder-coated like most Hydro Flask bottles, so it's a bit less grippy and you’ll have to hand wash it.

我也很喜欢这个杯子的不锈钢杯口，不盖盖子时啜饮非常顺滑。请注意，它不像大多数 Hydro Flask 水瓶那样有 TPU 粉末涂层，所以抓握感稍差，而且必须手洗。

### Best Value: Owala SmoothSip Slider

**最佳性价比：Owala SmoothSip Slider**

If you’re a fan of the Owala FreeSip’s spout, then the lightweight SmoothSip Slider will not disappoint. Its triple-layer insulation helps to keep drinks hot for around six hours or cold for 24. The slider lid works fine for everyday use and didn't leak when I flipped it upside down. But I still wouldn’t toss it into a bag because of how easy it is to unlock. Alongside being available in fun colors, Owala also releases limited-edition designs you can shop. Also notable is that Owala bottles are lead-, BPA-, and phthalate-free.

如果你是 Owala FreeSip 壶嘴的粉丝，那么轻便的 SmoothSip Slider 不会让你失望。它的三层隔热层有助于保持饮品热度约 6 小时或冷度 24 小时。滑动盖在日常使用中表现良好，当我把它倒过来时也没有漏水。但我仍然不会把它扔进包里，因为它太容易解锁了。除了提供有趣的颜色外，Owala 还会发布限量版设计供你选购。值得注意的是，Owala 的瓶子不含铅、BPA 和邻苯二甲酸盐。

### Best Flippable: BrüMate Strova

**最佳翻转防漏：BrüMate Strova**

After an unfortunate entanglement with a coffee mug and my computer, there was a period of time when the only bar where everybody knew my name was the Genius Bar. Now, lid security is my top priority for any liquid I tote around. For that singular concern, BrüMate's cupholder-friendly Strova gets a W from me. The brand claims its patented BevLock lid is fully leakproof, and after putting the mug through its paces—flipping, shaking, and dropping it—before I even considered placing it in my purse to intermingle with my water-sensitive electronics, I came to trust the promise: I couldn't make a single drop of coffee slip through the closed-mouthpiece edges or perimeter of the lid.

在经历了一次咖啡杯与电脑的不幸纠缠后，有一段时间，我最常去的地方就是苹果天才吧。现在，杯盖的安全性是我携带任何液体时的首要考虑。出于这一核心关切，BrüMate 适合杯架的 Strova 赢得了我的认可。该品牌声称其专利的 BevLock 杯盖完全防漏，在经过翻转、摇晃和跌落等严苛测试后，我才敢把它放进包里与怕水的电子产品放在一起。我确实信任它的承诺：无论我怎么折腾，杯口边缘或盖子周围都没有漏出一滴咖啡。

Further, the mug's ceramic-lined interior both protects the temperature and taste of whatever you're drinking, and it comes in more than a dozen colors and designs any aesthete would be happy to show off. The compromise you'll have to make is that the Strova is a bit awkward to sip. The mouth opening is quite small, running in a thin, tight line, like an expressionless face, as opposed to larger, easier-to-sip options that might conjure more of an open-mouthed cackle. The construction means you have to tip the mug up past your nose to sip when it's half full or less. This may look a bit aggro while you're drinking, but it’s frankly a small price to pay for confidently avoiding spillage.

此外，杯子内部的陶瓷涂层既能保护饮品的温度和口感，而且它有十几种颜色和设计，任何审美讲究的人都会乐于展示。你需要做出的妥协是，Strova 喝起来有点别扭。它的开口很小，是一条细细的缝隙，像一张面无表情的脸，而不像那些开口更大、更容易啜饮的杯子。这种结构意味着当杯子剩下一半或更少时，你必须把杯子抬高过鼻子才能喝到。这看起来可能有点夸张，但坦率地说，为了确保不洒出咖啡，这只是一个小小的代价。
