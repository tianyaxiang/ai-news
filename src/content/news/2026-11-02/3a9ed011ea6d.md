### Binance resumes withdrawals following $6.3 billion breach

加密货币交易所币安（Binance）于周日早间宣布，将开始分阶段恢复提现功能。此前，黑客从币安钱包中窃取了价值 63 亿美元的加密货币，导致该公司冻结了所有充值和提现业务。

In its latest update, Binance said it has now covered 95% of the stolen assets, and it’s still working to buy the “remaining affected assets,” which are “less liquid in the market and take longer time to source at the required scale.” “Our commitment remains unchanged: Binance will bear the financial impact of the stolen funds,” the company added. “These losses will not be passed on to users.”

在最新的更新中，币安表示目前已覆盖了 95% 的被盗资产，并仍在努力购回“剩余受影响资产”，因为这些资产“在市场上的流动性较差，需要更长时间才能按所需规模筹集”。该公司补充道：“我们的承诺保持不变：币安将承担被盗资金带来的财务影响，这些损失不会转嫁给用户。”

As for the withdrawal freeze, Binance said it did not make the decision lightly, as it understands “how important reliable access to withdrawals is for our users.” Nonetheless, the company said this was a necessary “precaution while our teams worked to contain the incident loss, assess its impact, restore affected services, and complete security checks.”

关于提现冻结，币安表示做出这一决定并不容易，因为他们深知“可靠的提现渠道对用户而言有多重要”。尽管如此，该公司称这是一种必要的“预防措施，以便我们的团队能够控制损失、评估影响、恢复受影响的服务并完成安全检查”。

Binance will resume supporting withdrawals in phases, depending on network and asset type. As of Sunday morning, the company’s network status page showed a mix of normal, partially suspended, and fully suspended statuses — for example, Bitcoin withdrawals and deposits are still listed as partially suspended, while Ethereum deposits are normal and withdrawals are partially suspended.

币安将根据网络和资产类型，分阶段恢复提现支持。截至周日上午，该公司的网络状态页面显示各种状态并存，包括正常、部分暂停和完全暂停——例如，比特币的提现和充值仍显示为部分暂停，而以太坊的充值正常，提现则为部分暂停。

The company also said users should expect “temporary delays or longer processing times due to traffic volume.” “Resuming withdrawals is a major milestone, but our work continues,” Binance said. “We will continue strengthening our safeguards, completing the recovery process, and providing updates as progress continues.”

该公司还提醒用户，由于流量较大，可能会出现“暂时的延迟或处理时间延长”。币安表示：“恢复提现是一个重要的里程碑，但我们的工作仍在继续。我们将继续加强安全防护，完成恢复流程，并随着进展不断提供更新。”

Binance subsequently shared that between 6am and 3:30pm Coordinated Universal Time, it processed $4.6 billion in withdrawals and $1.0 billion in deposits, for a net outflow of $3.6 billion — 2.1% of its total assets under management. The company also said it will publish a more in-depth incident report when its investigation is complete.

随后，币安分享称，在协调世界时（UTC）上午 6 点至下午 3 点 30 分之间，平台处理了 46 亿美元的提现和 10 亿美元的充值，净流出额为 36 亿美元，占其资产管理总额的 2.1%。该公司还表示，将在调查结束后发布一份更深入的事件报告。

Binance previously blamed North Korea hackers for the breach. On Saturday, the FBI said that a North Korean group known as TraderTraitor was responsible, and that the group was converting the assets primarily into Ethereum, with further laundering likely.

币安此前曾指责朝鲜黑客实施了此次入侵。周六，美国联邦调查局（FBI）表示，一个名为“TraderTraitor”的朝鲜组织应对此负责，并称该组织正将资产主要转换为以太坊，后续可能还会进行洗钱活动。
