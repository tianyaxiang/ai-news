# 8 Best Space Heaters (2026): Tested, Measured, and Mistreated

The Best space heaters are winter’s rear guard, the last line of defense against goose bumps and cold feet. Though not a great way to heat a whole home, space heaters can be indispensable for a late-evening movie night, a workbench in a chilly garage, or the home’s odd corners that refuse to stay warm. And if you live in an apartment building where you don’t control the heat, they may be your only way to feel OK in January.

最好的空间加热器是冬天的后卫，是抵御鸡皮疙瘩和双脚冰冷的最后一道防线。虽然它们不是加热整个房屋的好方法，但对于深夜电影之夜、寒冷车库里的工作台，或者家里那些难以保持温暖的角落，空间加热器却是不可或缺的。如果你住在一栋无法控制暖气的公寓楼里，它们可能是你在1月份感到舒适的唯一途径。

Even so, these little machines do bring about serious safety and reliability concerns, along with a need to be able to parse dodgy and opaque marketing claims. (For example, no matter what the marketing specifies, large space heaters usually aren’t more powerful or more effective than small ones.) So we turned down the thermostat and tested dozens of space heaters to find the ones that’ll warm you up as quickly, quietly, and safely as possible.

即便如此，这些小机器确实带来了严重的安全和可靠性问题，同时也需要人们能够辨别那些不可靠且晦涩的营销宣传。（例如，无论营销文案如何说明，大型空间加热器通常并不比小型加热器更强大或更有效。）因此，我们调低了恒温器，测试了数十款空间加热器，以找到那些能让你尽可能快速、安静且安全地暖和起来的产品。

### Best Space Heater Overall: Dreo Whole Room Heater 714

### 最佳整体空间加热器：Dreo Whole Room Heater 714

This Dreo is the best answer to what I’ve been looking for in a space heater ever since I started testing them. I want simple things: the promise of safety, with reliable tip-over and overheat protections; quiet and effective functioning; and the ability to use the full capacity of an American outlet to heat quickly and evenly around the room. I like a remote control, but if I lose it, I still want to be able to use the heater. I also like precise digital-temperature control, with a thermostat that actually works. I also don’t want to hate how the thing looks, considering it’ll be with me all winter.

这款Dreo加热器是我自开始测试以来一直在寻找的最佳答案。我想要简单的东西：安全保障，包括可靠的倾倒和过热保护；安静且高效的运行；以及能够利用美式插座的全部容量，快速均匀地加热房间。我喜欢遥控器，但如果弄丢了，我仍然希望能够使用加热器。我还喜欢精确的数字温度控制，以及真正有效的恒温器。考虑到它将陪伴我整个冬天，我也不希望讨厌它的外观。

The Dreo Whole Room Heater 714 checks these boxes better than any other heater among the dozens I’ve tested in the past two years—with thoughtful design touches that make it possible to actually like the thing. It’s a nice modern update on the classic fan design, able to oscillate not just side-to-side but also up and down. The counter-spiral grill, similar to competitor Vornado’s models, creates good heat distribution around the room. And while it’s not silent, it’s still quieter than my refrigerator on high fan speeds.

在过去两年我测试过的几十款加热器中，Dreo Whole Room Heater 714比其他任何产品都更好地满足了这些要求，其周到的设计细节让人真正喜欢上它。它是对经典风扇设计的出色现代升级，不仅可以左右摆动，还可以上下摆动。其反向螺旋格栅与竞争对手Vornado的型号类似，能实现良好的室内热量分布。虽然它不是完全静音，但在高风速下仍然比我的冰箱还要安静。

### Best Quiet Space Heater: Vornado TAVH10 Space Heater

### 最佳静音空间加热器：Vornado TAVH10 Space Heater

Kansas-based fan and heater company Vornado offers a dizzying array of heaters that look visually similar: an oblong plastic box with a circular whorl on its face. This can make shopping a bit difficult, but there are good reasons the Vornado is wedded to this design. It’s wonderfully effective and quiet, with some of the most even heat distribution I’ve seen among any devices on the market. (The quietest and best fans available at any given time are often also named Vornado.)

总部位于堪萨斯的风扇和加热器公司Vornado提供了一系列外观相似、令人眼花缭乱的加热器：一个长方形的塑料盒，正面有一个圆形的涡流。这可能会让购物变得有些困难，但Vornado坚持这种设计是有充分理由的。它非常有效且安静，拥有我在市场上见过的所有设备中最均匀的热量分布。（在任何时候，最安静、最好的风扇通常也都是Vornado品牌。）
