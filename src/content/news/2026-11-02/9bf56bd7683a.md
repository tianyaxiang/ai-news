Dazzle AI CEO Marissa Mayer recently became the latest tech CEO to face criticism over seemingly out-of-touch comments extolling the usefulness of AI. In Mayer’s case, that involved asking an AI tool for help with a school-assigned description of her daughter. For OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, that’s included suggesting that parents create personalized podcasts for their kids’ drive to school and also declaring that he could not imagine parenting without ChatGPT. Meta CEO Mark Zuckerberg has even suggested that it will become increasingly acceptable to meet your social and romantic needs through AI.

Dazzle AI 首席执行官玛丽莎·梅耶尔（Marissa Mayer）最近成为最新一位因发表看似脱离现实、吹捧人工智能实用性的言论而受到批评的科技公司首席执行官。就梅耶尔而言，这涉及向人工智能工具寻求帮助，以完成学校布置的关于她女儿的描述。对于 OpenAI 首席执行官萨姆·奥特曼（Sam Altman）来说，这包括建议家长为孩子上学途中制作个性化播客，并宣称他无法想象没有 ChatGPT 的育儿生活。Meta 首席执行官马克·扎克伯格（Mark Zuckerberg）甚至暗示，通过人工智能满足社交和浪漫需求将变得越来越被接受。

This all led TechCrunch’s Amanda Silberling to wonder if AI executives are simply too out-of-touch to build products for normal people — a crucial question with Anthropic set to go public just as Wall Street begins losing its appetite for heavy AI spending. On the latest episode of the Equity podcast, Kirsten Korosec, Sean O’Kane, and I chatted with Amanda about why industry leaders can sometimes seem so tone deaf. Kirsten wondered if this is more of a marketing problem or a product problem. Amanda’s answer: “A little bit of both.”

这一切让 TechCrunch 的阿曼达·西尔伯林（Amanda Silberling）不禁怀疑，人工智能高管是否因为太脱离现实而无法为普通人打造产品——这是一个关键问题，因为 Anthropic 即将上市，而华尔街对人工智能巨额支出的兴趣正开始减退。在最新一期的《Equity》播客中，克里斯汀·科罗塞克（Kirsten Korosec）、肖恩·奥凯恩（Sean O’Kane）和我与阿曼达聊了聊为什么行业领袖有时看起来如此不接地气。克里斯汀想知道这更多是一个营销问题还是产品问题。阿曼达的回答是：“两者皆有。”

Anthony Ha: There’s a lot of societal skepticism about AI, this feeling that the people running these companies don’t have our best interests at heart, that they’re out of touch. And so, you would just think, “Maybe don’t make statements like that,” because of course, people are going to see them very skeptically. I think [CEOs] are trying to walk this delicate balance. On the one hand, there’s a tremendous financial incentive for them to make the case that you should use this technology in every aspect of your lives. But when you make that case, it actually [doesn’t paint] a very appealing picture. In some cases, it’s downright dystopian.

安东尼·哈（Anthony Ha）：社会对人工智能存在很多怀疑，人们觉得运营这些公司的人并没有把我们的最大利益放在心上，他们脱离了现实。所以，你会想，“也许不要发表那样的言论”，因为人们当然会非常怀疑地看待这些言论。我认为（首席执行官们）正试图走一条微妙的平衡线。一方面，他们有巨大的经济动机去证明你应该在生活的方方面面使用这项技术。但当你提出这种主张时，它实际上（并没有描绘出）一幅非常吸引人的图景。在某些情况下，这简直是反乌托邦式的。

Kirsten Korosec: Anthony, if they took your advice, we would have nothing to talk about on this show.
