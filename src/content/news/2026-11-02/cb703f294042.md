If you’ve done Fourier series approximations of functions, you had three reactions to today’s XKCD comic: First, of course, was a smile of recognition. Then came a “wait a minute” as you realized that the Gibbs phenomenon doesn’t get smaller as you add more terms to the Fourier expansion; it just concentrates itself at the corners: That ringing never goes away.
