# North Korea linked to massive Binance crypto heist

联邦调查局（FBI）表示，来自朝鲜的恶意行为者窃取了价值 63 亿美元的币安（Binance）客户加密货币。

The FBI says that malicious actors from North Korea stole $6.3 billion worth of Crypto from Binance customers.

联邦调查局表示，10 月 29 日针对币安的 63 亿美元巨额加密货币盗窃案幕后黑手是朝鲜。FBI 将此次活动标记为“TraderTraitor”，并称已观察到与此次黑客攻击有关联的行为者正在将代币转换为以太坊，并准备进一步洗钱。该机构几乎没有提供其他细节或证据。

The FBI says North Korea was behind the massive $6.3 billion crypto heist that hit Binance on October 29th. The FBI labeled the activity “TraderTraitor” and said that it had observed actors it has connected to hack converting the tokens to Ethereum and preparing for further laundering of the funds. The agency offered few other details or evidence.

声明称：朝鲜应对 60 亿美元以上的币安攻击负责。联邦调查局（FBI）发布信息称，朝鲜民主主义人民共和国（朝鲜）应对 2026 年 10 月 29 日左右从加密货币交易所币安窃取超过 60 亿美元虚拟资产的行为负责。FBI 将这种特定的朝鲜恶意网络活动称为“TraderTraitor”。

The statement says: North Korea Responsible for $6 Billion+ Binance Attack. The Federal Bureau of Investigation (FBI) is releasing information advising the Democratic People’s Republic of Korea (North Korea) was responsible for the theft of more than $6 billion USD in virtual assets from cryptocurrency exchange Binance, on or about October 29, 2026. The FBI refers to this specific North Korean malicious cyber activity as “TraderTraitor.”

FBI 与美国特勤局（USSS）和美国国税局刑事调查处（IRS-CI）的合作伙伴一起，观察到“TraderTraitor”行为者主要将窃取的资产转换为以太坊（ETH）。预计这些资产随后将通过去中心化金融（DeFi）和即时兑换服务进行进一步洗钱，为后续的洗钱阶段做准备。

The FBI, together with partners at the USSS and IRS-CI, has observed TraderTraitor actors converting stolen assets primarily into Ethereum (ETH). It is expected these assets will then be further laundered by converting them using DeFi and instant swap services as they prepare them for further laundering stages.

不出所料，币安在黑客攻击发生后立即冻结了客户提款，并于今日重新开放。

As expected, Binance reopened customer withdrawals today after freezing them in the immediate aftermath of the hack.
