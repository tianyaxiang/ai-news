Title: New Zelda and GTA represent a games industry that no longer exists

《新塞尔达》与《GTA》代表了一个已不复存在的游戏产业

The two biggest games of the year launch in November, offering some reprieve from a terrible year. by Andrew Webster Nov 1, 2026, 1:00 PM UTC

年度两款最重磅的游戏将于十一月发布，为这糟糕的一年带来了一丝慰藉。作者：Andrew Webster，2026年11月1日，协调世界时下午1:00。

How it started: The two biggest games of 2026 are set to launch just a few weeks apart in November. On the one side you have the behemoth that is Grand Theft Auto VI, an experience 13 years in the making, and the sequel to one of the best-selling games in history.

故事的开端：2026年最受瞩目的两款游戏定于十一月相隔仅几周发布。一边是巨作《侠盗猎车手6》（GTA VI），这款历经13年打造的作品是史上最畅销游戏之一的续作。

Its predecessor, GTA V, has sold a staggering 230 million copies since it launched in 2013, and was re-released across three generations of consoles. Developer Rockstar is a pioneer in the realm of open-world games, dating back to GTA III, and the scale and ambitions of those worlds have steadily grown since then.

其前作《GTA V》自2013年发布以来已售出惊人的2.3亿份，并跨越了三代主机平台重新发行。开发商Rockstar是开放世界游戏领域的先驱，这一传统可追溯至《GTA III》，自那以后，其游戏世界的规模与野心一直在稳步增长。

Analyst firm Circana estimates that 29 percent of gamers in the US intend to buy the game, with demand so large it could boost flagging console sales. “Many consumers who have yet to upgrade to a current-generation console may choose to purchase a PlayStation 5 or Xbox Series specifically to play the title,” analyst Mat Piscatella says in a statement.

分析公司Circana估计，29%的美国玩家打算购买这款游戏，巨大的需求甚至可能提振疲软的主机销量。分析师Mat Piscatella在声明中表示：“许多尚未升级到当前世代主机的消费者，可能会为了玩这款游戏而专门购买PlayStation 5或Xbox Series。”

The one exception is Nintendo. The company’s tentpole release for the holidays, and its next major exclusive for the Switch 2, is a lavish remake of The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time.

唯一的例外是任天堂。该公司为假期准备的重磅产品，也是其Switch 2的下一款主要独占游戏，是《塞尔达传说：时之笛》的豪华重制版。

These aren’t just two blockbuster games – they’re two series with the pull to make this a real cultural moment, akin to “Barbenheimer” but for video games. They’re the kind of launches that make it onto Saturday Night Live and have NBA players worried.

这两款游戏不仅仅是重磅大作，它们所属的系列拥有将此转化为真正文化时刻的影响力，类似于游戏界的“芭比海默”（Barbenheimer）。它们的发布足以登上《周六夜现场》，甚至让NBA球员们感到担忧。

How it’s going: These major November releases are something of a mirage, two outliers that stand out amid the wreckage that is the rest of the video game industry. While there have been plenty of major releases and great games this year, the main theme of 2026 has been just how dire things have become.

现状：十一月的这些重大发布宛如海市蜃楼，在满目疮痍的游戏产业中显得格格不入。尽管今年有不少大作和优秀游戏问世，但2026年的主旋律却是产业环境的极度恶化。

Here is just a snapshot of what the year has been like: Xbox is in the middle of a “reset” that involves laying off thousands of developers, shutting down some studios, and gutting others; Fortnite developer Epic cut more than 1,000 jobs due to a sharp downturn in the live-service games space, and it’s currently going all-in on vibe coding.

以下是今年状况的一个缩影：Xbox正处于“重置”之中，涉及裁减数千名开发人员、关闭部分工作室并削减其他部门；《堡垒之夜》开发商Epic因实时服务游戏领域的急剧下滑裁员超过1000人，目前正全力投入“氛围编程”（vibe coding）。

What happens next: Clearly, things are changing for the video game industry; they have to change. What that future looks like, though, remains unclear. The next generation of game consoles is being threatened by exorbitant prices, and all the while the rise of AI-powered tools is sending ripples across the industry.

未来会怎样：显然，游戏产业正在发生变化；它们必须改变。然而，未来究竟会是什么样子尚不清楚。下一代游戏主机正受到高昂价格的威胁，与此同时，人工智能工具的兴起正在整个行业引发连锁反应。
