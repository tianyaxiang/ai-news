# Adobe Premiere, One of the iPhone’s Best Video-Editing Apps, Is Now on Android
# Adobe Premiere：iPhone 上最出色的视频编辑应用之一，现已登陆 Android

Nearly a year after releasing Premiere for iPhone, Adobe is finally bringing its redesigned video-editing app to Android. The app has been in beta for several weeks but is now available as the official successor to Premiere Rush, which the company will stop supporting on September 30.
在 iPhone 版 Premiere 发布近一年后，Adobe 终于将其重新设计的视频编辑应用带到了 Android 平台。该应用此前已进行了数周的测试，现已正式发布，作为 Premiere Rush 的继任者。Adobe 将于 9 月 30 日停止对 Premiere Rush 的支持。

Eric Snowden, senior vice president of design at Adobe, tells WIRED the feedback for the iOS app has been “incredibly positive” (to his credit, it has a 4.8-star rating out of 5 on the App Store with more than 25,000 ratings). The goal with the Android version wasn't just to port the iPhone app experience, but to make it feel really native to Google's platform.
Adobe 设计高级副总裁 Eric Snowden 向《连线》（WIRED）杂志表示，iOS 版本的反馈“非常积极”（值得一提的是，它在 App Store 上拥有超过 25,000 条评分，平均分高达 4.8 星）。Android 版本的开发目标不仅仅是移植 iPhone 的应用体验，而是要让它在 Google 的平台上拥有真正的原生感。

That includes making sure the app works just as well on every kind of Android device out there, whether it's a Samsung foldable or a candy bar Google Pixel. Yes, when you open up a folding phone with the Adobe Premiere app, you can view your footage on the left and see the multi-track timeline on the right, maximizing space. Snowden suggests optimizing the app for Android foldables will help the company with the iPhone app for the upcoming iPhone Duo.
这包括确保该应用在各种 Android 设备上都能表现出色，无论是三星折叠屏手机还是直板式的 Google Pixel。没错，当你打开折叠屏手机使用 Adobe Premiere 时，可以在左侧查看素材，在右侧查看多轨时间轴，从而最大化利用屏幕空间。Snowden 表示，针对 Android 折叠屏手机的优化经验，也将有助于公司为未来可能出现的“iPhone Duo”（折叠屏 iPhone）开发应用。

And if you're wondering whether this app will work well on Google's new Googlebook laptop platform, Adobe says its apps—Acrobat, Photoshop, Lightroom, and Premiere—are optimized for the new laptops, meaning they'll take advantage of the larger screen, but can’t quite deliver the “desktop-grade” experience you’ll find on Windows or macOS.
如果你想知道这款应用在 Google 新的 Googlebook 笔记本平台上表现如何，Adobe 表示其系列应用（Acrobat、Photoshop、Lightroom 和 Premiere）已针对这些新笔记本进行了优化。这意味着它们可以利用更大的屏幕空间，但仍无法完全提供你在 Windows 或 macOS 上所能体验到的“桌面级”功能。

Equally important is ensuring the app runs on lower-power devices, though Premiere has a minimum requirement of 5 GB of RAM (phones will also need to run Android 13 or later).
同样重要的是确保应用能在低功耗设备上运行，尽管 Premiere 的最低内存要求为 5 GB（手机还需要运行 Android 13 或更高版本）。

“We wanted this to run on as many devices as possible, so the team spent a ton of time making sure that this is not just for flagship phones,” Snowden says. “This is for a really broad range of Android devices.”
“我们希望它能在尽可能多的设备上运行，因此团队花费了大量时间来确保它不仅仅适用于旗舰手机，”Snowden 说道，“它是为极其广泛的 Android 设备群体准备的。”

Launch the Adobe Premiere app and you will see all your footage from your camera roll on the home screen. From there, you can choose creator-made templates—ranging from lifestyle to beauty to templates for foodies—to get started on an edit, or start from scratch if you'd like.
启动 Adobe Premiere 应用后，你会在主屏幕上看到相册中的所有素材。你可以选择创作者制作的模板（涵盖生活方式、美妆到美食等多种风格）来开始编辑，或者如果你愿意，也可以从零开始创作。

It's easy to add your footage, then opt for the mini editor or the full editor. The former lets you easily trim or replace your footage, and even choose a filter to apply a specific color style to all of your clips. You can export directly to YouTube Shorts without leaving Premiere. The full editor gives you a multitrack timeline for more granular edits, like layering text, music, and effects.
添加素材非常简单，之后你可以选择“迷你编辑器”或“完整编辑器”。前者让你能轻松剪辑或替换素材，甚至可以选择滤镜为所有片段应用特定的色彩风格。你无需离开 Premiere 即可直接导出到 YouTube Shorts。完整编辑器则提供了多轨时间轴，支持更精细的编辑，例如添加文字、音乐和特效图层。

Several AI tools are on board, like Enhance Audio, which can boost the subject's voice and cut background noise. If you want to add a voice-over, you can speak into your phone's mic and see your footage in real time so it all matches up. Adobe says the app has Firefly-powered AI features, like Generative Fill, Image-to-Video, and Generate Sound Effects. However, these require generative credits, aka a subscription, if you run out of your monthly allotment.
应用内置了多项 AI 工具，例如“增强音频”（Enhance Audio），它可以提升主体人声并消除背景噪音。如果你想添加配音，可以通过手机麦克风录音，并实时查看素材以确保音画同步。Adobe 表示，该应用还具备由 Firefly 驱动的 AI 功能，如生成式填充、图生视频和生成音效。不过，如果用完了每月的免费额度，这些功能需要消耗生成积分，即需要订阅服务。

Some features are still “coming soon,” like the Continue Editing function so you can wrap up an edit on desktop after starting it on mobile, or support for Adobe Fonts.
一些功能仍处于“即将推出”阶段，例如“继续编辑”功能（让你在手机上开始编辑后，能在桌面端完成），以及对 Adobe Fonts 的支持。

Uniquely (for an Adobe product), you do not have to log in or subscribe to Adobe's creative suite to use Premiere on Android. Like the iOS version, the app is free to use.
独特的是（对于 Adobe 产品而言），你在 Android 上使用 Premiere 无需登录或订阅 Adobe Creative Suite。与 iOS 版本一样，该应用可以免费使用。

“What we're seeing with a lot of mobile apps right now is that there are these huge barriers to entry to using it for the first time,” Snowden says. “We really wanted to get away from that, and I think part of the accessibility and simplicity of the app—the onboarding process is part of that—and we just want to get people in and using it."
“我们目前看到许多移动应用在首次使用时存在巨大的准入门槛，”Snowden 说，“我们非常希望摆脱这一点。我认为该应用的易用性和简洁性——包括引导流程——都是其中的一部分，我们只是想让人们能够轻松上手并使用它。”
