# Google Expands Gemini Notebook With Syncing, Interactive Tools and a New Name
# 谷歌扩展 Gemini Notebook：引入同步功能、交互式工具及全新命名

Google is expanding Gemini Notebook, the product previously called NotebookLM, from a source-grounded research tool into a connected knowledge workspace across the Gemini app and NotebookLM. Official updates published in 2026 describe synced notebooks, real-time conversations, mobile audio recording and interactive overviews, while the product's July rebrand aligns it more closely with Gemini's wider app experience. A brief social signal about ongoing work does not identify a particular feature, release date or access plan. The more useful picture for teams comes from Google's published updates: Gemini Notebook is undergoing a broader product expansion, with availability and some benefits tied to subscription plans and phased rollouts rather than one newly specified capability.

谷歌正在将 Gemini Notebook（前身为 NotebookLM）从一个基于源文档的研究工具，扩展为一个跨 Gemini 应用和 NotebookLM 的互联知识工作空间。2026 年发布的官方更新介绍了同步笔记本、实时对话、移动端音频录制和交互式概览等功能，而 7 月份的品牌重塑使其与 Gemini 更广泛的应用体验保持了一致。虽然社交媒体上关于后续工作的简短预告并未指明具体功能、发布日期或访问计划，但从谷歌发布的更新中可以更清晰地看到：Gemini Notebook 正在经历更广泛的产品扩张，其可用性和部分权益与订阅计划及分阶段推广挂钩，而非单一的新功能发布。

What Google has confirmed Google introduced Notebooks in Gemini on April 8, 2026 as personal knowledge bases that can organize chats, files and projects. The company said notebooks sync between the Gemini app and NotebookLM, positioning them as a way to retain context across related work instead of repeatedly assembling the same source material in separate conversations. That distinction matters in practical use. A notebook can provide a defined place for project files and relevant Gemini chats, which may make it easier to return to research, planning or reference material without rebuilding the context each time. Google also connected the expansion to subscription plans and cross-app workflows, although the supplied research does not specify plan prices or a complete feature-by-plan breakdown.

谷歌已确认的内容：2026 年 4 月 8 日，谷歌在 Gemini 中引入了“笔记本”（Notebooks）功能，作为可以组织聊天记录、文件和项目的个人知识库。该公司表示，笔记本可在 Gemini 应用和 NotebookLM 之间同步，旨在作为一种在相关工作中保留上下文的方式，避免在不同的对话中重复整理相同的源材料。这种区别在实际使用中非常重要。笔记本可以为项目文件和相关的 Gemini 对话提供一个固定的存储空间，使用户无需每次都重新构建上下文，从而更轻松地查阅研究、规划或参考资料。谷歌还将此次扩展与订阅计划和跨应用工作流挂钩，尽管目前的研究资料并未详细说明计划价格或完整的按计划功能细分。

On July 16, 2026, Google confirmed that NotebookLM would become Gemini Notebook. According to the official Google Workspace update on the Gemini Notebook rename, the new branding would roll out across interfaces over the following weeks and required no administrator action. For existing users, the change is primarily one of product identity and interface branding, rather than a request to migrate notebooks manually.

2026 年 7 月 16 日，谷歌确认 NotebookLM 将更名为 Gemini Notebook。根据关于此次更名的 Google Workspace 官方更新，新品牌将在随后的几周内覆盖所有界面，且无需管理员进行任何操作。对于现有用户而言，此次变更主要是产品标识和界面品牌的调整，而非要求用户手动迁移笔记本。

Google's September 15, 2026 update then described several new tools for Gemini Notebook: Real-time conversations with notebooks, allowing users to interact with their notebook material in a live conversational format. Mobile audio recording, extending how users can capture material for use with notebooks. Interactive overviews, including quizzes, flashcards and video summaries. Student access offers, including a free year for eligible U.S. college students and free Google AI Plus in many markets. The September update is oriented heavily toward studying, but the underlying changes can also be relevant to work that depends on repeatedly interpreting a bounded collection of documents, notes and project material. That is an implication of the confirmed capabilities, not a separate Google promise about business use cases.

谷歌 2026 年 9 月 15 日的更新介绍了 Gemini Notebook 的几项新工具：与笔记本进行实时对话，允许用户以实时对话的形式与笔记本内容进行交互；移动端音频录制，扩展了用户捕获笔记本素材的方式；交互式概览，包括测验、抽认卡和视频摘要；学生访问优惠，包括为符合条件的美国大学生提供一年免费使用权，以及在许多市场提供免费的 Google AI Plus。9 月份的更新主要面向学习场景，但其底层的变化同样适用于那些依赖于反复解读特定文档、笔记和项目材料的工作。这是对已确认功能的推论，而非谷歌针对商业用例做出的单独承诺。

Why the cross-app notebook model matters The central development is not simply a name change. Google is connecting a notebook-based workspace with the Gemini app, so a notebook can serve as a continuing context layer for related chats and files. For a business team, that could be useful when people repeatedly consult the same policies, customer research, project briefs or planning material. The likely value is less context reconstruction. Instead of locating documents and restating the background for every new interaction, users can work from an organized notebook. Results will still depend on the material placed in the notebook and on how carefully users review generated output. Google's announcements establish the notebook and interaction features, but they do not promise accuracy for every use case or replace normal checks on important work. The interactive features also broaden Gemini Notebook beyond reading and static summaries. Quizzes, flashcards and video summaries are explicitly framed as study tools. Organizations should therefore distinguish between confirmed product capabilities and their own potential internal applications, such as training or onboarding. Those applications may be worth testing, but they are not announced as dedicated business features in the supplied materials.

跨应用笔记本模式为何重要？核心进展不仅仅是名称的变更。谷歌正在将基于笔记本的工作空间与 Gemini 应用连接起来，使笔记本能够作为相关聊天和文件的持续上下文层。对于商业团队来说，当员工需要反复查阅相同的政策、客户研究、项目简介或规划材料时，这一点非常有用。其潜在价值在于减少了上下文重建的工作量。用户无需在每次新交互时都去查找文档并重述背景，而是可以直接基于整理好的笔记本进行工作。最终结果仍取决于放入笔记本的材料以及用户对生成内容的审查程度。谷歌的公告确立了笔记本和交互功能，但并未承诺在所有用例中的准确性，也无法取代对重要工作的常规检查。这些交互功能还将 Gemini Notebook 的用途从阅读和静态摘要扩展开来。测验、抽认卡和视频摘要被明确定义为学习工具。因此，企业应区分已确认的产品功能与自身潜在的内部应用（如培训或入职引导）。这些应用值得测试，但它们并未在提供的材料中被宣布为专门的商业功能。

Rollout, access and what remains open Google describes rollout as phased, beginning on the web before mobile and broader geographic availability. The rename itself was expected to appear across interfaces in the weeks after the July announcement. Access to certain capabilities and offers is connected to subscription status, while the September student promotion has eligibility and market conditions. This means prospective users should not assume that every Gemini Notebook feature is available in every interface, country or plan at the same time. The verified research supports a direction of travel, but it does not provide a universal release schedule, a full pricing table or a feature matrix. Businesses comparing options should check the current Gemini and Workspace availability information for their account and region before designing a process around a particular capability.

推广、访问权限及未决事项：谷歌将推广描述为分阶段进行，首先在网页端推出，随后是移动端和更广泛的地理区域。更名本身预计在 7 月公告发布后的几周内出现在各个界面上。对某些功能和优惠的访问权限与订阅状态挂钩，而 9 月份的学生促销活动则有资格限制和市场条件。这意味着潜在用户不应假设每个 Gemini Notebook 功能都能在所有界面、国家或计划中同时使用。现有的研究证实了其发展方向，但并未提供通用的发布时间表、完整的价格表或功能矩阵。企业在比较方案时，应在围绕特定功能设计流程之前，先核实其账户和所在地区的 Gemini 及 Workspace 可用性信息。
