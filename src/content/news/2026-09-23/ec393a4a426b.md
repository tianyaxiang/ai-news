# Apple is reportedly working on a Whoop-like fitness tracker
# 据报道，苹果正在研发一款类似 Whoop 的健身追踪器

Apple is working on a new fitness tracker with a screenless design, similar to the one made by Whoop, according to a report from Bloomberg. A prototype of the device reportedly has “a thin fabric band with a sensor-equipped computing module.”
据彭博社报道，苹果正在研发一款采用无屏幕设计的新型健身追踪器，类似于 Whoop 推出的产品。据称，该设备的原型机由“一条带有传感器计算模块的轻薄织物表带”组成。

The device is in an early stage of development and likely wouldn’t arrive before 2028 if Apple decides to release it, Bloomberg reports. In addition to Whoop’s lineup of 5.0 bands, the company also sells MG — or “medical grade” — variants with a built-in EKG feature. The bands are designed to be worn continuously, as they offer more than 14 days of battery life.
彭博社指出，该设备目前处于研发初期，如果苹果决定发布，预计最早也要到 2028 年才会面世。除了 Whoop 的 5.0 系列手环外，该公司还销售带有内置心电图（EKG）功能的 MG（医疗级）版本。这些手环专为全天候佩戴而设计，续航时间超过 14 天。

As pointed out by Bloomberg, Apple has added several features already offered by Whoop to its new Apple Watch Series 12, including continuous heart rate tracking. Apple’s Health app also now has a “readiness score” used by Fitbit, Oura, and Whoop, which is supposed to tell you how “ready” you are to work out based on several factors, along with an estimate of your biological age.
正如彭博社所指出的，苹果已在其新款 Apple Watch Series 12 中加入了 Whoop 已经提供的多项功能，包括连续心率监测。苹果的“健康”应用现在也具备了 Fitbit、Oura 和 Whoop 所使用的“身体准备度评分”（readiness score），该功能旨在根据多种因素评估你的运动状态，并提供生理年龄估算。
