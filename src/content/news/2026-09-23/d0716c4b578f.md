# Rabbit’s new AI agent doesn’t need an R1 to run
# Rabbit 推出的全新 AI 智能体无需 R1 硬件即可运行

The startup’s AI agent can operate locally across Windows, Mac, and Linux devices.
这家初创公司的 AI 智能体可以在 Windows、Mac 和 Linux 设备上本地运行。

Rabbit, the company behind the underwhelming R1 device, is rolling out a standalone AI agent that you don’t need its hardware to use, as reported earlier by Wired. The startup says its new OS3 “agentic operating system” runs in the cloud but operates locally across Windows, Mac, and Linux devices.
据《连线》（Wired）此前报道，曾推出表现平平的 R1 设备的 Rabbit 公司，正在发布一款无需其硬件即可使用的独立 AI 智能体。这家初创公司表示，其全新的 OS3“智能体操作系统”虽然在云端运行，但可以在 Windows、Mac 和 Linux 设备上实现本地化操作。

According to Rabbit, you can add up to five devices to one account, along with your preferred AI models. OS3 will automatically determine the devices, files, apps, and AI models it needs to complete a task. You can also access OS3 through its dedicated desktop site, a paired messaging app like Telegram or iMessage, as well as on Rabbit’s R1 device.
据 Rabbit 称，一个账户最多可添加五台设备，并可配置你偏好的 AI 模型。OS3 会自动识别完成任务所需的设备、文件、应用程序和 AI 模型。你还可以通过其专属桌面网站、配对的即时通讯应用（如 Telegram 或 iMessage）以及 Rabbit 的 R1 设备来访问 OS3。

Though Rabbit founder Jesse Lyu tells Wired that the startup has stopped manufacturing R1 devices, it isn’t giving up on AI hardware entirely. It’s instead focusing on a new vibe-coding “cyberdeck” that will run OS3 when it launches within “months,” Wired reports.
尽管 Rabbit 创始人吕骋（Jesse Lyu）向《连线》透露，该公司已停止生产 R1 设备，但他们并未完全放弃 AI 硬件。据《连线》报道，该公司正专注于开发一款全新的“vibe-coding”风格“赛博甲板”（cyberdeck），该设备将在“数月内”发布，并运行 OS3 系统。

Rabbit claims its OS3 system won’t store, copy, use, or sell your data. It notes that it will still keep your chats and memories stemming from them on its servers. Other AI providers linked to OS3 will also handle your data under their own privacy rules.
Rabbit 声称其 OS3 系统不会存储、复制、使用或出售用户数据。但该公司指出，它仍会将用户的聊天记录及其衍生的记忆保存在服务器上。此外，与 OS3 关联的其他 AI 提供商也将根据其各自的隐私规则处理用户数据。
