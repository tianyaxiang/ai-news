# GoPro Mission 1 Pro ILS Review (2026): Cinema-Quality Action Cam
# GoPro Mission 1 Pro ILS 评测（2026）：电影级运动相机

**WIRED Review Summary:**
**WIRED 评测摘要：**

**GOOD:** Good selection of interchangeable Micro Four Thirds lenses. Outstanding image and video quality. Great cinematic footage from a GoPro. Solid battery life. Did I mention interchangeable lenses?
**优点：** 可选配多种 Micro Four Thirds (MFT) 可更换镜头。出色的图像和视频质量。能用 GoPro 拍出极佳的电影感画面。电池续航扎实。我提到过它支持更换镜头了吗？

**TIRED:** No electronic contacts means not all MFT lenses will work. No lens included. Manual focusing on the small screen is tough (you'll want a monitor).
**缺点：** 没有电子触点，意味着并非所有 MFT 镜头都能兼容。包装内不含镜头。在小屏幕上进行手动对焦很困难（你需要外接监视器）。

Don't let the GoPro in the name fool you, but its new Mission 1 Pro ILS (short for interchangeable lens system) isn't really an action camera. It's not even waterproof, and it uses potentially fragile interchangeable lenses. It's really a cinema camera crammed into the form factor of a GoPro.
别被 GoPro 这个名字误导了，其新款 Mission 1 Pro ILS（可更换镜头系统的缩写）实际上并不是一款运动相机。它甚至不防水，而且使用了相对脆弱的可更换镜头。它本质上是一台被塞进 GoPro 机身里的电影摄影机。

While the average action camera user probably won't want the ILS version of the Mission 1 (the standard Mission 1 Pro serves that need), professional filmmakers, creators, and camera nerds have been clamoring for something like this for years, and it's finally here. It isn't perfect—in fact, it has some serious shortcomings—but this initial ILS camera does what it is meant to do quite well and, with incremental improvements, could become a truly great, tiny 8K video powerhouse.
虽然普通运动相机用户可能并不需要 Mission 1 的 ILS 版本（标准版 Mission 1 Pro 已能满足需求），但专业电影制作人、创作者和相机发烧友多年来一直渴望这样的产品，现在它终于来了。它并不完美——事实上，它还有一些严重的缺陷——但这款初代的 ILS 相机很好地完成了它的使命，随着后续的改进，它有望成为一台真正强大且小巧的 8K 视频利器。

### What Is the Mission 1 Pro ILS?
### 什么是 Mission 1 Pro ILS？

The Mission 1 Pro ILS joins the Mission 1 and Mission 1 Pro to round out GoPro's high-end line of cinema-quality action cameras aimed at professionals. The major advantage over more consumer-oriented cameras like the GoPro Hero 13 or similar offerings from DJI and Insta360 is the large 1-inch (Type 1) sensor, which produces clearer, sharper video. The Mission 1 and Mission 1 Pro are still traditional action cameras, though, with a fixed ultrawide lens and rugged, waterproof bodies. Both deliver outstanding image quality, and the Mission 1 Pro is our favorite action camera on the market.
Mission 1 Pro ILS 与 Mission 1 和 Mission 1 Pro 一起，完善了 GoPro 面向专业人士的高端电影级运动相机产品线。与 GoPro Hero 13 或 DJI、Insta360 等类似消费级相机相比，它的主要优势在于配备了大型 1 英寸（Type 1）传感器，能产生更清晰、更锐利的视频。不过，Mission 1 和 Mission 1 Pro 仍属于传统运动相机，配备固定超广角镜头和坚固的防水机身。两者都提供出色的图像质量，而 Mission 1 Pro 是我们目前市场上最喜欢的运动相机。

The Mission 1 Pro ILS is a different beast in one important way: It has a Micro Four Thirds (MFT) lens mount. Yes, a GoPro with interchangeable lenses. Before you go thinking, “No one wants that,” consider that people have long clamored for this capability, and accessories already exist to modify the GoPro Hero Black with an interchangeable lens mount.
Mission 1 Pro ILS 在一个重要方面与众不同：它拥有 Micro Four Thirds (MFT) 卡口。没错，这是一台可以更换镜头的 GoPro。在你认为“没人想要这个”之前，请考虑一下人们长期以来对这一功能的呼声，而且市面上早已存在将 GoPro Hero Black 改装为可更换镜头卡口的配件。

But first, what's the same as the Mission 1 and Mission 1 Pro? Just about everything. The body is essentially the Mission 1 Pro with the lens removed and a mount added. It even weighs the same (207 grams, before you put a lens on the ILS). The screens, buttons, and controls are identical, so working with the camera will feel familiar to anyone who has used a GoPro.
首先，它与 Mission 1 和 Mission 1 Pro 有什么相同之处？几乎所有地方。它的机身本质上就是去掉了镜头并增加了卡口的 Mission 1 Pro。它的重量甚至是一样的（在未安装镜头前为 207 克）。屏幕、按钮和控制方式完全相同，因此任何使用过 GoPro 的人都会感到非常熟悉。

In terms of video recording, the specs are similar to the Mission 1, topping out at 8K/60 frames per second (fps), with all the same phenomenal slow-motion options, including 4K at 240 fps all the way up to 1080p at 960 fps. Probably the biggest physical difference between the ILS and the Pro (aside from lenses) is that the ILS is not waterproof.
在视频录制方面，其规格与 Mission 1 相似，最高支持 8K/60 帧（fps），并具备同样出色的慢动作选项，包括 4K/240 fps 甚至 1080p/960 fps。ILS 与 Pro 版本在物理上最大的区别（除了镜头外）可能就是 ILS 不防水。

### Why Would I Want to Change Lenses?
### 为什么要更换镜头？

Photographers and videographers know the benefits of interchangeable lenses in bigger cameras, giving them different focal perspectives and moods to suit the look they want. But why would someone want this capability in a small camera like a GoPro?
摄影师和摄像师都知道大尺寸相机中可更换镜头的优势，它们能提供不同的焦距视角和氛围，以满足所需的视觉效果。但为什么有人会希望在像 GoPro 这样的小型相机上拥有这种功能呢？

When videographers realized they could use smaller DSLRs (and even smartphones) for Hollywood productions, they've been employed as primary cameras for small-budget indie films or as secondary “cheaper” options to ultraexpensive cinema rigs for shooting in tight spaces, or that could be written off. In fact, even big camera companies like Sony, Nikon, and Canon have developed compact cinema cameras for such purposes.
当摄像师意识到他们可以使用更小的单反相机（甚至智能手机）进行好莱坞制作时，这些设备便开始被用作低成本独立电影的主力机，或者作为昂贵电影设备的“廉价”辅助机，用于在狭窄空间拍摄，或者作为可报废的备用机。事实上，索尼、尼康和佳能等大牌相机厂商也为此开发了紧凑型电影摄影机。

Which leads to two obvious use cases where the Mission 1 Pro ILS will shine. First, it's a great little crash camera for bigger productions (a crash camera is one that's disposable; imagine it installed in a car to capture an action sequence that might well destroy the camera in the process).
这引出了 Mission 1 Pro ILS 大放异彩的两个明显用例。首先，它是大型制作中绝佳的“碰撞相机”（crash camera，指一次性使用的相机；想象一下把它安装在车里拍摄动作戏，过程中相机很可能会被毁坏）。

Second, it could be a great B-cam for a wildlife shoot, for example, where something smaller like the ILS is more likely to be ignored by wildlife and get shots a bigger camera can't. I also think it could be a good low-cost option for solo or small crews who need to wear a video hat at times, like wedding photographers or small productions where a full cinema cam is likely out of budget.
其次，它可以作为野生动物拍摄的绝佳 B 机（辅助机），因为像 ILS 这样的小型设备更容易被野生动物忽略，从而拍到大型相机无法捕捉的镜头。我还认为，对于需要身兼数职的个人或小型团队来说，它是一个低成本的好选择，比如婚礼摄影师或预算不足以购买全套电影摄影机的小型制作组。

While smartphones (specifically the iPhone Pro) can now shoot fantastic high-resolution video, the Mission 1 Pro ILS' larger sensor, native lens mount, and even smaller size (minus the lens) give it an upper hand. And for WIRED readers, there's a more relatable use case: it's simply a fun camera to play with and experiment on.
虽然智能手机（特别是 iPhone Pro）现在可以拍摄出色的高分辨率视频，但 Mission 1 Pro ILS 更大的传感器、原生镜头卡口，以及更小的机身（不含镜头时）使其更具优势。对于 WIRED 的读者来说，还有一个更贴切的用途：它纯粹是一台适合把玩和实验的有趣相机。

MFT, also called Micro 4/3, lenses are cheap (for lenses), and if you include adapting vintage lenses, you open up all kinds of interesting shooting possibilities. I've known two directors of photography who both looked at lens selection as the most important decision. Whatever camera they were going to use was largely irrelevant because what mattered was the lens. In a sense, that is the appeal of the Mission 1 Pro ILS: It fades into the background and lets you focus on what the lens can do.
MFT（也称为 Micro 4/3）镜头相对便宜，如果算上转接老式镜头，你将开启各种有趣的拍摄可能性。我认识两位摄影指导，他们都认为镜头选择是最重要的决定。至于使用什么相机在很大程度上并不重要，因为关键在于镜头。从某种意义上说，这就是 Mission 1 Pro ILS 的魅力所在：它退居幕后，让你专注于镜头能实现的效果。

The great thing about MFT lenses is that they fit well with a small camera body like the Mission 1 Pro ILS. They're small, compact, and inexpensive—as Olympus (now OM System) and Panasonic, the creators of MFT, had intended when they created it as a foundation for mirrorless digital cameras. One thing to keep in mind, though, is that while the Mission 1 Pro ILS has a Micro Four Thirds mount, it still uses the same smaller, 1-inch sensor as the Mission 1 Pro. That means the crop factor is greater than it would be with an MFT sensor. When calculating the full-frame equivalence, it's about 2.7X with HyperSmooth (GoPro's digital image-stabilization tech) off and 3X with it on.
MFT 镜头的优点在于它们非常适合像 Mission 1 Pro ILS 这样的小型机身。它们小巧、紧凑且价格低廉——这正是 MFT 的创造者奥林巴斯（现为 OM System）和松下在将其作为无反相机基础时所设想的。不过需要记住的一点是，虽然 Mission 1 Pro ILS 拥有 Micro Four Thirds 卡口，但它使用的仍然是与 Mission 1 Pro 相同的较小 1 英寸传感器。这意味着它的裁切系数比使用 MFT 传感器时要大。在计算全画幅等效焦距时，关闭 HyperSmooth（GoPro 的数字图像稳定技术）时约为 2.7 倍，开启时约为 3 倍。
