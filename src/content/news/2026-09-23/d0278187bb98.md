# How to Claim Your Cut of Apple’s $250 Million Siri Settlement
# 如何领取苹果 2.5 亿美元 Siri 和解金的份额

In May, Apple agreed to pay out $250 million as part of a class action lawsuit over claims the company overhyped its Apple Intelligence tools, specifically the long-delayed Siri AI overhaul.
今年 5 月，苹果公司同意支付 2.5 亿美元，以了结一桩集体诉讼。该诉讼指控苹果公司过度宣传其“Apple Intelligence”工具，特别是迟迟未能推出的 Siri AI 重大升级。

Although the revamped Siri AI voice assistant is now available for owners of newer iPhones via iOS 27, you may still be eligible to claim cash back for past purchases. This settlement covers customers in the US who made their purchases from June 10, 2024 to March 29, 2025, and bought certain iPhone 15 or iPhone 16 models from Apple. If you qualify, you might receive up to $95 for each phone.
尽管新款 iPhone 用户现在已可通过 iOS 27 使用升级后的 Siri AI 语音助手，但你仍可能有资格就之前的购买行为申请现金赔偿。此次和解涵盖了在 2024 年 6 月 10 日至 2025 年 3 月 29 日期间，从苹果公司购买了特定 iPhone 15 或 iPhone 16 机型的美国客户。如果你符合条件，每部手机最高可获得 95 美元的赔偿。

This settlement revolves around Apple’s promotion of Siri features to sell iPhones. “I expected to receive a Siri Apple Intelligence feature and did not receive it,” reads part of the statement you have to sign when filling out the claim form. As part of the settlement, Apple denies any wrongdoing.
此次和解的核心在于苹果利用 Siri 功能作为卖点来推销 iPhone。在填写索赔表格时，你需要签署的一份声明中写道：“我曾期待获得 Siri 的 Apple Intelligence 功能，但并未收到。”作为和解协议的一部分，苹果否认存在任何不当行为。

“We resolved this matter to stay focused on doing what we do best, delivering the most innovative products and services to our users,” read part of Apple’s statement from May about the settlement.
苹果在 5 月份关于此次和解的声明中表示：“我们解决此事是为了专注于我们最擅长的事情，即为用户提供最具创新性的产品和服务。”

To file a claim, visit the dedicated website: smartphoneaisettlement.com. Click the blue button in the top-right corner of the page labeled File A Claim. First, you’ll be asked whether you have a Claim ID and PIN to enter. It’s not required for you to already have this info on hand. On the final page, you’ll need either your iPhone’s serial number or Apple Account ID on hand to file the claim.
如需提交索赔申请，请访问专用网站：smartphoneaisettlement.com。点击页面右上角标有“File A Claim”（提交索赔）的蓝色按钮。首先，系统会询问你是否有索赔 ID (Claim ID) 和 PIN 码。你无需预先准备这些信息。在最后一页，你需要准备好 iPhone 的序列号或 Apple 账户 ID 才能完成提交。

Not sure where your serial number is located? Open the Settings app, tap General, then choose About. Your serial number will be a string of letters and numbers near the top of the screen.
不知道序列号在哪里？打开“设置”应用，点击“通用”，然后选择“关于本机”。你的序列号将显示在屏幕顶部附近的一串字母和数字。

You can choose to receive the settlement money through PayPal, Venmo, an electronic bank transfer, or a paper check. If you have multiple devices and want to make claims for all of them, then you’ll need to fill out the form again for every eligible device. iPhone owners have until December 21 to make their claims.
你可以选择通过 PayPal、Venmo、电子银行转账或纸质支票接收和解金。如果你拥有多台设备并希望为所有设备申请赔偿，则需要为每台符合条件的设备重新填写表格。iPhone 用户需在 12 月 21 日之前提交申请。
