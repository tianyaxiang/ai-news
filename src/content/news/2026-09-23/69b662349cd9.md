# The Download: why AI’s latest breakthroughs and fears may be more hype than reality
# The Download：为什么人工智能的最新突破与担忧可能只是炒作大于现实

This is today's edition of The Download, our weekday newsletter that provides a daily dose of what's going on in the world of technology. Don’t be fooled by this summer of AI hype — Timnit Gebru, executive director of the Distributed AI Research Institute (DAIR), and Emily M. Bender, professor of linguistics at the University of Washington, argue that it’s been a busy few months for AI hype, with companies making breathless claims about hacking, mathematical breakthroughs, and the prospect of self-improving superintelligence.
这是今天的《The Download》，我们平日的通讯，为您提供每日科技界的动态。不要被这个人工智能炒作的夏天所迷惑——分布式人工智能研究所（DAIR）执行董事蒂姆尼特·格布鲁（Timnit Gebru）和华盛顿大学语言学教授艾米丽·M·本德（Emily M. Bender）认为，过去几个月人工智能的炒作非常频繁，各大公司纷纷就黑客攻击、数学突破以及自我进化超级智能的前景做出令人窒息的声明。

In all these cases, massive fanfare from the companies, presented as mea culpas in the hacking incidents, has been accompanied by intense press coverage. But once experts have had time to examine what happened, very different stories emerge. There’s a strong commercial incentive to overstate AI capabilities. The authors argue that the illusion of speed and urgency promulgated by the tech companies is also a ploy to misdirect policymakers and the public. Here’s why they say we must remain skeptical about AI hype.
在所有这些案例中，公司的大肆宣传（在黑客事件中表现为“认错”）都伴随着密集的媒体报道。但一旦专家们有时间审视所发生的事情，就会出现截然不同的故事。夸大人工智能能力存在强大的商业动机。作者认为，科技公司散布的这种速度感和紧迫感的假象，也是误导政策制定者和公众的一种策略。这就是为什么他们说我们必须对人工智能的炒作保持怀疑。

**How wind tech could help decarbonize cargo shipping**

Cargo shipping is responsible for about 3% of the world’s annual greenhouse-gas emissions, and the industry is under pressure to find alternatives to fossil fuels. Wind power is one option. In the Marshall Islands, where people have relied on wind-powered vessels for millennia, a new cargo sailboat inspired by traditional vessels made its maiden voyage last year. It could cut emissions by up to 80% compared with a fuel-powered cargo ship. See how engineers are bringing wind power back to cargo shipping. —Sofia Quaglia
货运航运约占全球年度温室气体排放量的 3%，该行业正面临寻找化石燃料替代品的压力。风能是一个选择。在马绍尔群岛，人们几千年来一直依赖风力驱动的船只，去年一艘受传统船只启发的新型货运帆船进行了首航。与燃料驱动的货船相比，它可以减少高达 80% 的排放。看看工程师们是如何将风能带回货运航运的。——索菲亚·夸利亚（Sofia Quaglia）
