# Seeing the City or Recognizing the Place? What Street-View Imagery Adds Beyond Existing Urban Data in VLM Urban Sensing
# 洞察城市还是识别地点？在视觉语言模型（VLM）城市感知中，街景图像相较于现有城市数据有何增量价值？

**Abstract:** Street-view imagery is increasingly used to infer urban attributes, but predictive accuracy alone does not reveal how much a photograph contributes beyond data already available for the same place. We compare image-based predictions with existing urban data across seven attributes from five public resources and three VLMs. The same urban units are evaluated using images, task context, nearby observations, and public records, while image replacements and conflicting records test source reliance.

**摘要：** 街景图像正越来越多地被用于推断城市属性，但仅凭预测准确率并不能揭示照片在现有城市数据之外究竟贡献了多少额外信息。我们对比了基于图像的预测与来自五个公共资源和三个视觉语言模型（VLM）的七项城市属性数据。研究通过图像、任务背景、周边观测和公共记录对相同的城市单元进行评估，并利用图像替换和冲突记录来测试模型对不同数据源的依赖程度。

Existing urban data matched or exceeded image-only models for road damage, curb ramps, and house price, while neighbouring official statistics nearly matched the best image result for population. Images were more informative for building type, building function, and low-rise floor count. For floor count, image advantage increased by 5.7 percentage points per doubling of distance to the nearest labelled building and declined for tall buildings whose rooflines often fell outside the frame.

在道路损坏、路缘坡道和房价方面，现有城市数据表现出与纯图像模型相当甚至更优的性能；而在人口统计方面，邻近地区的官方统计数据几乎达到了最佳图像模型的效果。相比之下，图像在建筑类型、建筑功能和低层建筑楼层数方面更具信息量。对于楼层数，距离最近标注建筑的距离每增加一倍，图像的优势就提升 5.7 个百分点；但对于屋顶线常超出画面范围的高层建筑，图像的优势则有所下降。

Models frequently followed conflicting records. OpenFACADES floor annotations were generated with OpenStreetMap floor values and showed the opposite height-dependent error pattern from image-only reruns. Street-view image value therefore depends on visual legibility and local data coverage. Comparing images with existing urban data can guide image collection and clarify the provenance of derived urban maps.

模型经常倾向于采信冲突的记录。OpenFACADES 的楼层标注是基于 OpenStreetMap 的楼层数值生成的，其表现出的高度相关误差模式与纯图像重测结果恰好相反。因此，街景图像的价值取决于视觉清晰度和本地数据的覆盖程度。将图像与现有城市数据进行对比，可以为图像采集提供指导，并厘清衍生城市地图的数据来源。
