# STATERA: Hidden Mass Estimation via Zero-Shot Sim-to-Real Kinematics using Frozen Temporal Tubelets
# STATERA：通过冻结时间管状体进行零样本模拟到现实运动学转换的隐藏质量估计

**Abstract:** Vision models pretrained for frame-level appearance often struggle to infer hidden physical properties from motion. We study center-of-mass (CoM) localization for opaque, asymmetric rigid bodies from short monocular videos, where surface cues and point tracking are unreliable under self-occlusion.

**摘要：** 针对帧级外观预训练的视觉模型，往往难以从运动中推断出隐藏的物理属性。我们研究了从短单目视频中对不透明、非对称刚体进行质心（CoM）定位的问题，在这种情况下，表面线索和点跟踪在自遮挡下往往不可靠。

We propose STATERA, which adapts a pretrained video backbone (V-JEPA) with mostly frozen weights and a lightweight temporal tubelet mixer to predict per-frame CoM heatmaps and trajectories. To support this task, we introduce the HiddenMass Benchmark, comprising 50K MuJoCo trajectories and a 63-sequence real-world test set with physically calibrated CoM ground truth.

我们提出了 STATERA，它通过适配一个预训练的视频骨干网络（V-JEPA，大部分权重冻结）和一个轻量级的时间管状体混合器（temporal tubelet mixer），来预测逐帧的质心热图和轨迹。为了支持这项任务，我们引入了 HiddenMass 基准测试，包含 5 万条 MuJoCo 轨迹以及一个包含 63 个序列的真实世界测试集，并提供了经过物理校准的质心真值。

In simulation, STATERA-50K-Sigma improves normalized CoM error from 41.7% (DINOv2) to 25.2%. In zero-shot sim-to-real transfer, we observe a fundamental trade-off in supervision: phase-aware targets can induce bimodal predictions, while phase-agnostic targets can collapse toward statistically safe centroids.

在模拟实验中，STATERA-50K-Sigma 将归一化质心误差从 41.7%（DINOv2）降低到了 25.2%。在零样本模拟到现实（sim-to-real）的迁移中，我们观察到监督学习中存在一个根本性的权衡：相位感知目标（phase-aware targets）可能导致双峰预测，而相位无关目标（phase-agnostic targets）则可能坍缩至统计学上的安全质心。

Nevertheless, our phase-aware STATERA-50K-Crescent is the only evaluated method that demonstrates consistent movement toward the true hidden offset. While this leads to a monocular vector overshoot artifact that marginally increases absolute Euclidean error compared to a static geometric centroid, it improves physics capture from 2.6% to 41.0%.

尽管如此，我们的相位感知模型 STATERA-50K-Crescent 是唯一被评估且表现出向真实隐藏偏移量持续移动的方法。虽然这会导致单目向量过冲伪影，使得绝对欧几里得误差相比静态几何质心略有增加，但它将物理捕捉能力从 2.6% 提升到了 41.0%。

These results suggest that frozen temporal representations can better separate inertial dynamics from visual geometry for hidden-parameter estimation.

这些结果表明，冻结的时间表征能够更好地将惯性动力学与视觉几何分离，从而实现隐藏参数的估计。
