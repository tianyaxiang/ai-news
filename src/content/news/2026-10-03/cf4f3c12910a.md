# DualCast: A Dual-Path Language Model for Bimodal Financial Time-Series Forecasting
# DualCast：用于双模态金融时间序列预测的双路径语言模型

**Abstract:** Financial time-series forecasting must capture price dynamics across heterogeneous assets while incorporating news available at prediction time. We introduce DualCast, a dual-path framework that extends a frozen language model with a discrete financial vocabulary.
**摘要：** 金融时间序列预测必须在捕捉不同资产价格动态的同时，整合预测时可获取的新闻信息。我们引入了 DualCast，这是一个双路径框架，通过离散的金融词汇表扩展了冻结的语言模型。

Each log-return patch is represented by a learned summary token and three residual shape tokens, preserving local drift and volatility while allowing shape patterns to be shared across assets. To improve codebook utilization, we develop adaptive frequency-equalizing residual vector quantization, which rebalances overloaded codewords without compromising reconstruction accuracy.
每个对数收益率片段（log-return patch）由一个学习到的摘要标记（summary token）和三个残差形状标记（residual shape tokens）表示，在保留局部漂移和波动性的同时，允许在不同资产间共享形状模式。为了提高码本利用率，我们开发了自适应频率均衡残差向量量化技术，在不影响重构精度的前提下重新平衡了过载的码字。

The fast path trains only the new financial-token embeddings and output heads on a frozen Qwen3-8B backbone. A toggleable LoRA adapter enables a slow path that conditions on the fast forecast and news available at the forecast origin to produce a revised prediction. The reviser is initialized by supervised fine-tuning and further optimized with a return-space group relative policy optimization objective that rewards improvements over the fast forecast.
快速路径仅在冻结的 Qwen3-8B 主干模型上训练新的金融标记嵌入和输出头。一个可切换的 LoRA 适配器开启了慢速路径，该路径基于快速预测结果以及预测起始点可获取的新闻信息，生成修正后的预测。修正器通过监督微调进行初始化，并利用收益空间组相对策略优化目标（return-space group relative policy optimization objective）进行进一步优化，该目标旨在奖励相对于快速预测的改进。

In zero-shot evaluations covering equities and energy prices at five-minute, daily, and weekly resolutions, the slow path achieves the lowest mean absolute percentage error among the compared methods in 8 of 12 dataset-horizon settings, including every longest-horizon setting. News ablations indicate additional gains in most tested settings, although their magnitude varies across markets. DualCast thus combines a fast numerical forecaster with an optional text-conditioned revision mechanism.
在涵盖五分钟、日线和周线分辨率的股票及能源价格零样本评估中，慢速路径在 12 种数据集-时间跨度设置中的 8 种里，实现了对比方法中最低的平均绝对百分比误差（MAPE），其中包括所有最长跨度的设置。新闻消融实验表明，在大多数测试设置中，新闻信息带来了额外的增益，尽管其幅度在不同市场间有所差异。因此，DualCast 将快速数值预测器与可选的文本条件修正机制结合在了一起。
