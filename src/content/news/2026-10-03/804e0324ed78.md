# Best Smart Cat Trackers of 2026: Fi Mini vs. Tractive
# 2026年最佳智能猫咪追踪器：Fi Mini 对比 Tractive

A smart cat tracker is like a wearable health-monitoring device, such as a smart ring, smartwatch, or fitness tracker, that helps people stay on top of key wellness markers—but for a cat. Why should humans have all the fun? In addition to live and historical GPS location tracking that helps any helicopter cat parent rest easy, the Fi Mini, Tractive Smart Cat Tracker, and Tractive Cat 6 Mini monitor your furry friend's health metrics, like activity and sleep. The collars also send alerts for changes in health patterns, such as when your pet is moving less or sleeping more than average, which can serve as early warning signs of potential health issues.

智能猫咪追踪器就像是为猫咪准备的可穿戴健康监测设备，类似于智能戒指、智能手表或健身追踪器，能帮助主人掌握宠物关键的健康指标。凭什么只有人类能享受这些科技？除了能让“直升机式家长”（过度操心的主人）安心的实时及历史 GPS 定位功能外，Fi Mini、Tractive Smart Cat Tracker 和 Tractive Cat 6 Mini 还能监测毛孩子的健康数据，如活动量和睡眠情况。这些项圈还会在健康模式发生变化时发送提醒，例如当你的宠物运动量减少或睡眠时间超过平均水平时，这可以作为潜在健康问题的早期预警信号。

For the better part of the year, I've been testing these trackers on my cat Basil, analyzing each device's location accuracy, sleep- and activity-tracking capabilities, and health summary metrics. I also considered practical factors, such as ease of use, product size, and battery life.

在过去大半年的时间里，我一直在我的猫 Basil 身上测试这些追踪器，分析每款设备的定位精度、睡眠和活动追踪能力以及健康摘要指标。我还考虑了实用性因素，例如易用性、产品尺寸和电池续航。

If you have a cat that also goes outdoors, these trackers are much more reliable and comprehensive than something like an AirTag because they determine positioning via satellite instead of the nearest Bluetooth signal. They'll also notify you if your cat leaves your designated “safe zone.”

如果你养的是一只会出门的猫，这些追踪器比 AirTag 之类的设备要可靠且全面得多，因为它们是通过卫星而非最近的蓝牙信号来确定位置的。如果你的猫离开了你设定的“安全区”，它们还会向你发送通知。

Ultimately, each smart tracker has its own strengths. The best model for you and your cat depends on your priorities—if you value long battery life, the Fi Mini ($99) is the best choice. If you want advanced health features, the Tractive Cat 6 Mini ($77) is the best smart collar option.

归根结底，每款智能追踪器都有各自的优势。哪款最适合你和你的猫，取决于你的优先需求——如果你看重超长续航，Fi Mini（99 美元）是最佳选择；如果你想要进阶的健康功能，Tractive Cat 6 Mini（77 美元）则是最好的智能项圈方案。

### Best Connected App: Tractive Smart Cat Tracker
### 最佳互联应用：Tractive Smart Cat Tracker

If tracking your cat's location is your top priority, Tractive (also called the Tractive Cat Mini) is the best option. Its historical heat maps show the areas your cat frequents, which is especially helpful for people with indoor–outdoor cats who roam large swaths of land.

如果追踪猫咪的位置是你的首要任务，Tractive（也称为 Tractive Cat Mini）是最佳选择。它的历史热力图可以显示猫咪经常出没的区域，这对于那些在广阔土地上游荡的“半放养”猫咪的主人来说特别有用。

However, note that all tested models rely on GPS for location tracking. This means that if the satellite signal is spotty—a common issue in rural areas and other settings—you may not know your cat's precise location. Some Reddit and Amazon reviewers complain that Tractive's location tracking is less accurate in areas with dense vegetation.

不过请注意，所有参与测试的型号都依赖 GPS 进行定位。这意味着如果卫星信号不稳定（在农村地区或其他环境下很常见），你可能无法获知猫咪的精确位置。一些 Reddit 和亚马逊的用户评论称，Tractive 在植被茂密的地区定位精度会下降。

In the Tractive app, after you create a profile, you'll get personalized daily activity goals for your cat based on statistics from other cats of similar age and weight. The app assigned Basil 120 to 230 minutes of daily activity and later reported that he was consistently more active than 70 percent of similar cats. I felt at peace knowing I'm keeping him on the move in my small apartment.

在 Tractive 应用中，创建档案后，系统会根据同龄和同体重猫咪的统计数据，为你提供个性化的每日活动目标。该应用给 Basil 分配的每日活动目标是 120 到 230 分钟，后来报告显示，他的活跃度始终超过了 70% 的同类猫咪。得知他在我的小公寓里保持着足够的运动量，我感到很安心。

### Best Battery Life, Charging, and Size: Fi Mini
### 最佳电池续航、充电与尺寸：Fi Mini

Testing these trackers made me realize the importance of basic features like battery life and ease of removing the tracker from the collar for charging. This is where the Fi Mini shines.

测试这些追踪器让我意识到电池续航和从项圈上拆卸充电的便捷性等基础功能有多重要。而这正是 Fi Mini 的闪光点。

Fi Mini takes about two hours to reach a full charge, which lasted about two weeks in my testing. Fi claims four weeks, but two weeks is much longer than the Tractive, which conked out at around five days on a full charge. While I didn't test this, Fi claims its tracker can hold a six- to eight-week charge with the Fi Base add-on.

Fi Mini 充满电大约需要两个小时，在我的测试中续航约为两周。Fi 官方宣称续航可达四周，但两周已经比 Tractive 长得多了，后者充满电后大约只能坚持五天。虽然我没有测试过，但 Fi 声称配合 Fi Base 配件使用，其追踪器可以实现六到八周的续航。

In addition to extending battery life by functioning as a connection source when your pet is at home, the Base also supposedly helps with location accuracy thanks to its Wi-Fi connection. One bonus for Fi users with an Apple Watch: You can add the Fi app to your watch to monitor your pet’s location and activity and enter “Lost Mode” without a phone, which is especially helpful if your cat has escaped while you are out.

除了在宠物在家时作为连接源以延长电池寿命外，Base 据称还能通过 Wi-Fi 连接辅助提高定位精度。对于拥有 Apple Watch 的 Fi 用户来说还有一个额外福利：你可以将 Fi 应用添加到手表上，无需手机即可监测宠物的实时位置和活动，并开启“丢失模式”，这在你不幸在户外弄丢猫咪时特别有用。
