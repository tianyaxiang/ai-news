# Conformal Adversarial Generative Ensemble (CAGE)

**Abstract:** Accurate time series forecasting is critical across various domains, yet traditional ensemble methods often suffer from the disproportionate influence of extreme forecasts. We introduce the Conformal Adversarial Generative Ensemble (CAGE), a novel framework that combines generative modeling, adversarial discrimination, and conformal prediction to enhance forecast reliability and accuracy.

**摘要：** 精确的时间序列预测在各个领域都至关重要，然而传统的集成方法往往会受到极端预测值不成比例的影响。我们引入了“共形对抗生成集成”（Conformal Adversarial Generative Ensemble，简称 CAGE），这是一种结合了生成建模、对抗判别和共形预测的新型框架，旨在提高预测的可靠性和准确性。

CAGE employs multiple generative models to produce initial forecasts, which are then evaluated by a discriminative component using conformal prediction techniques. P-values derived from nonconformity scores help dynamically adjust model weights, minimizing the impact of unreliable forecasts. This approach ensures that only the most credible predictions contribute to the final ensemble output.

CAGE 采用多个生成模型来产生初始预测，随后由一个使用共形预测技术的判别组件进行评估。通过非一致性得分（nonconformity scores）导出的 P 值有助于动态调整模型权重，从而最大限度地减少不可靠预测的影响。这种方法确保了只有最可信的预测结果才会对最终的集成输出产生贡献。

Our empirical and statistical analyses of time series data from New Zealand's milk collection and the global health data from the public owid-monkeypox dataset show that the CAGE outperforms traditional ensemble methods, especially in handling outliers and noisy data. By incorporating conformal prediction, CAGE delivers accurate and statistically rigorous forecasts, enhancing decision-making.

我们对来自新西兰牛奶采集的时间序列数据以及来自公共 owid-monkeypox 数据集的全球健康数据进行了实证和统计分析，结果表明 CAGE 的表现优于传统的集成方法，特别是在处理异常值和噪声数据方面。通过整合共形预测，CAGE 能够提供准确且具有统计严谨性的预测，从而增强决策能力。

We have demonstrated performance on two different datasets deliberately to showcase that the proposed method offers a versatile solution potentially applicable across finance, weather, and supply chain management.

我们特意在两个不同的数据集上展示了其性能，以证明该方法提供了一种多功能的解决方案，具有在金融、气象和供应链管理等领域广泛应用的潜力。
