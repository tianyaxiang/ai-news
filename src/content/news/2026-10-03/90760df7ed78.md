# Spatial Lifting for Dense Prediction
# 空间提升：用于密集预测的新方法

We present Spatial Lifting (SL), a novel methodology for dense prediction tasks. SL operates by lifting standard inputs, such as 2D images, into a higher-dimensional space and subsequently processing them using networks designed for that higher dimension, such as a 3D U-Net.

我们提出了空间提升（Spatial Lifting, SL），这是一种用于密集预测任务的新颖方法。SL 的工作原理是将标准输入（如 2D 图像）提升到更高维的空间，随后使用专为该高维空间设计的网络（如 3D U-Net）进行处理。

Counterintuitively, this dimensionality lifting allows us to achieve good performance on benchmark tasks compared to conventional approaches, while reducing inference costs and drastically lowering the number of model parameters.

反直觉的是，这种维度提升使我们能够在基准任务上获得优于传统方法的性能，同时降低了推理成本，并大幅减少了模型参数的数量。

The SL framework produces intrinsically structured outputs along the lifted dimension. This emergent structure facilitates dense supervision during training and enables single-forward-pass self-consistency-based quality and uncertainty estimation at test time.

SL 框架沿着提升后的维度产生具有内在结构性的输出。这种涌现出的结构不仅有助于训练过程中的密集监督，还支持在测试阶段通过单次前向传播进行基于自一致性的质量和不确定性评估。

Spatial Lifting introduces a simple and general modeling strategy that offers a promising path toward more efficient, accurate, and reliable deep networks for dense prediction tasks in vision.

空间提升引入了一种简单且通用的建模策略，为实现更高效、更准确、更可靠的视觉密集预测深度网络提供了一条有前景的路径。
