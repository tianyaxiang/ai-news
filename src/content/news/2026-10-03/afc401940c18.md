### A new contest pits competitors against each other in a race to biological youth —Jessica Hamzelou
### 一场让参赛者竞相追逐“生物学年轻化”的新比赛 ——Jessica Hamzelou

This week, I officially signed up for an unusual competition. One that rewards competitors for getting younger. I recently turned 40, and I don’t need reminding that both time and my chronological age only tick forward. But this game is focused on competitors’ biological ages, figures that are meant to provide a better way to measure the age-related health of our organs and bodies. Over six months, around 500 of us will try to reverse our biological age using a bunch of different measures. There’s even a leaderboard! But is it even possible to measure whether someone is getting younger? Read the full story on the controversy around biological age measurements and how I’ll test them myself. This story is from The Checkup, our weekly biotech newsletter. Sign up to receive it in your inbox every Thursday.
本周，我正式报名参加了一场不同寻常的比赛。这场比赛奖励那些变得更年轻的参赛者。我最近刚满 40 岁，不需要别人提醒我也知道，时间和我的生理年龄只会不断向前。但这场比赛关注的是参赛者的“生物学年龄”——这一指标旨在提供一种更好的方式来衡量我们器官和身体与年龄相关的健康状况。在接下来的六个月里，我们大约 500 人将尝试通过各种不同的措施来逆转我们的生物学年龄。甚至还有一个排行榜！但衡量一个人是否真的在变年轻，这可能吗？阅读关于生物学年龄测量争议的完整报道，以及我将如何亲自测试这些方法。本文来自我们的每周生物技术简报《The Checkup》。欢迎订阅，每周四即可在收件箱中查收。

### Opinion: Don’t be fooled—LLMs don’t reason —Thore Graepel
### 观点：别被骗了——大语言模型（LLM）并不具备推理能力 ——Thore Graepel

Ten years ago, I watched a program I helped build stun the world by beating Go champion Lee Sedol. AlphaGo won after making a move so strange that some commentators thought it was a programming glitch. It was AlphaGo’s powers of reasoning that made this creative choice—and these are powers that today’s AI lacks. This is why I recently left my position at Google DeepMind. I believe we need a fresh approach to machine reasoning, one that draws on AlphaGo’s architecture. Here’s why today’s AI doesn’t really reason—and what it would take to change that. Thore Graepel is chair of machine learning at University College London. He was a core member of the AlphaGo team at DeepMind.
十年前，我看着我参与构建的程序击败了围棋冠军李世石，震惊了世界。AlphaGo 在做出一步极其诡异的棋后获胜，以至于一些评论员认为那是程序故障。正是 AlphaGo 的推理能力做出了这一创造性的选择——而这正是当今人工智能所缺乏的能力。这就是我最近离开 Google DeepMind 的原因。我相信我们需要一种全新的机器推理方法，一种借鉴 AlphaGo 架构的方法。这就是为什么当今的人工智能并不真正具备推理能力，以及我们需要做些什么来改变这一点。Thore Graepel 是伦敦大学学院机器学习系主任，曾是 DeepMind AlphaGo 团队的核心成员。
