### Updates to Full Disk Access in macOS
### macOS “完全磁盘访问”权限更新

October 2, 2026
2026 年 10 月 2 日

We give developers powerful APIs to build incredible capabilities into their apps for Apple products, backed by a set of controls designed to protect users’ private data.
我们为开发者提供强大的 API，助力他们为 Apple 产品构建出色的应用功能，并辅以旨在保护用户隐私数据的一系列控制措施。

Full Disk Access largely sidesteps these controls in order to allow backup apps to function properly on the Mac.
“完全磁盘访问”权限在很大程度上绕过了这些控制措施，以确保备份类应用能在 Mac 上正常运行。

Some developers are using Full Disk Access in ways that could put users at risk, exposing everything on their systems—including files, mail, messages, and even browsing history—without users’ full knowledge and understanding.
一些开发者正以可能危及用户安全的方式使用“完全磁盘访问”权限，在用户未完全知情和理解的情况下，暴露其系统中的所有内容，包括文件、邮件、信息，甚至浏览记录。

For communication apps, this can also compromise the privacy of the people users are communicating with.
对于通讯类应用而言，这还可能损害与用户进行交流的其他人的隐私。

Going forward, we will introduce additional controls to ensure that users who genuinely wish to grant an app this extraordinary level of access can only do so with very explicit user action.
展望未来，我们将引入额外的控制措施，以确保只有在用户明确采取操作的情况下，才能授予应用这种极高级别的访问权限。

Addressing this is critical. As AI agents become increasingly capable and autonomous, the risks associated with this level of access will grow substantially.
解决这一问题至关重要。随着 AI 智能体变得日益强大且具备自主性，与此类访问权限相关的风险也将大幅增加。

We are committed to ensuring users clearly understand these risks before granting such access, so they can make informed decisions about their own data and privacy.
我们致力于确保用户在授予此类访问权限前能清晰了解相关风险，从而让他们能够就自身数据和隐私做出明智的决定。
