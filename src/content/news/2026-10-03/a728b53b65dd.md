# DSSR-3D: Decoupled Reasoning for View-Dependent Referring in 3D Gaussians
# DSSR-3D：用于 3D 高斯中视点依赖指代任务的解耦推理方法

**Abstract:** Recent advances in 3D Gaussian Splatting have enabled open-vocabulary and referring segmentation by distilling semantic knowledge from 2D foundation models into 3D representations.
**摘要：** 3D 高斯泼溅（3D Gaussian Splatting）的最新进展通过将 2D 基础模型的语义知识蒸馏到 3D 表示中，实现了开放词汇和指代分割。

However, existing referring fields embed language features in a globally view-invariant space, making them fundamentally unable to resolve observer-centric spatial relations (e.g., "to the left of") that depend on camera pose.
然而，现有的指代场将语言特征嵌入到一个全局视点不变的空间中，这使得它们从根本上无法解析依赖于相机位姿的观察者中心空间关系（例如“在……的左侧”）。

We propose DSSR-3D, an inference-time framework for view-dependent referring segmentation on continuous 3D Gaussian fields, formalized as two interfaces - pose-invariant semantic localization and pose-conditioned spatial reasoning - such that any pair of functions satisfying these constraints yields a valid instantiation, requiring no retraining of the underlying semantic field and no reliance on discrete geometric proxies such as bounding boxes.
我们提出了 DSSR-3D，这是一个用于连续 3D 高斯场上视点依赖指代分割的推理时框架。该框架形式化为两个接口——位姿不变的语义定位和位姿条件下的空间推理——使得任何满足这些约束的函数对都能产生有效的实例化，且无需对底层语义场进行重新训练，也不依赖于边界框等离散几何代理。

We instantiate the two interfaces with a temperature-sharpened softmax localization mechanism and a projection-based directional scoring function, fused via a lightweight, training-free step, and show they transfer zero-shot to structurally distinct semantic fields without adaptation.
我们通过温度锐化的 Softmax 定位机制和基于投影的方向评分函数实例化了这两个接口，并通过一个轻量级、无需训练的步骤进行融合，并证明了它们可以在无需适配的情况下零样本迁移到结构不同的语义场中。

We further propose ViewRef-GS, a benchmark isolating view-dependent segmentation on 3D Gaussian fields, evaluated jointly with an augmented Ref-LERF to provide a comprehensive testbed for viewpoint-dependent spatial grounding.
我们进一步提出了 ViewRef-GS，这是一个用于隔离 3D 高斯场上视点依赖分割的基准测试，并与增强版的 Ref-LERF 联合评估，为视点依赖的空间定位提供了一个全面的测试平台。

Experiments show consistent gains over existing 3DGS-based referring methods, with no additional training beyond the base semantic field.
实验表明，与现有的基于 3DGS 的指代方法相比，该方法在无需基础语义场之外的额外训练的情况下，取得了持续的性能提升。
