# Nacon’s new PS5 controller can mix audio from your phone and console
# Nacon 新款 PS5 手柄支持手机与主机音频混合

Nacon announced what the company is claiming is the world’s first officially licensed PlayStation 5 controller with a built-in screen for adjusting settings like joystick sensitivity or remapping buttons right on the gamepad. The Revolution 5 Unlimited’s screen can also be used to mix audio from multiple sources so while using a pair of headphones connected to the gamepad with a cable you can listen to music from your phone and sounds from the game you’re playing at the same time.

Nacon 发布了其宣称的全球首款获得官方授权的 PlayStation 5 手柄，该手柄内置了一块屏幕，可直接在手柄上调整摇杆灵敏度或重新映射按键等设置。Revolution 5 Unlimited 的屏幕还可用于混合来自多个来源的音频，因此当你通过线缆将耳机连接到手柄时，可以同时收听来自手机的音乐和正在进行的游戏音效。

The Revolution 5 Unlimited is available for preorder starting today for $229.99 through Nacon’s online store and is expected to ship sometime later this year. While it’s primarily designed for use with the PS5, the R5U features an asymmetrical stick layout similar to Xbox and Switch gamepads with a pair of drift-resistant tunneling magnetoresistance (TMR) thumbsticks. Both sticks can be customized by attaching alternate heads featuring different designs and the controller’s D-pad is swappable. You can use a classic four-way design or switch to an eight-way option that makes it easier to perform combo moves in fighting games.

Revolution 5 Unlimited 即日起在 Nacon 在线商店开启预购，售价 229.99 美元，预计将于今年晚些时候发货。虽然它主要为 PS5 设计，但 R5U 采用了类似于 Xbox 和 Switch 手柄的非对称摇杆布局，并配备了一对防漂移的隧道磁阻 (TMR) 摇杆。两个摇杆都可以通过更换不同设计的摇杆头进行定制，手柄的十字键也是可更换的。你可以使用经典的四向设计，也可以切换到八向选项，从而更轻松地在格斗游戏中施展连招。

Nacon even lets you adjust how balanced the controller feels with 10, 14, and 16 gram weights that can be inserted into the R5U’s non-slip grips.

Nacon 甚至允许你通过插入 10 克、14 克和 16 克的配重块来调整 R5U 防滑握把的平衡感。

On the top of the controller you’ll find a pair of shoulder buttons, a pair of customizable shortcut buttons, and a pair of triggers with adjustable travel that can be shortened to reduce reaction times in FPS games. There’s four additional customizable shortcut buttons on the back of the controller as well as a button used for quickly switching between multiple saved profiles.

在手柄顶部，你会发现一对肩键、一对可自定义的快捷键，以及一对行程可调的扳机键，通过缩短行程可以减少在 FPS 游戏中的反应时间。手柄背面还有四个额外的可自定义快捷键，以及一个用于在多个已保存配置文件之间快速切换的按钮。

The R5U is also compatible with PCs and Android devices and customizing its performance and functionality isn’t limited to its built-in screen. Nacon also offers a mobile app for iOS and Android that expands the customization options to include adjusting the thumbsticks’ response curves, tweaking EQ profiles for the audio sources, and modifying the color of the RGB lighting surrounding the right stick.

R5U 还兼容 PC 和 Android 设备，其性能和功能的自定义并不局限于内置屏幕。Nacon 还为 iOS 和 Android 提供了移动应用程序，扩展了自定义选项，包括调整摇杆响应曲线、微调音频源的均衡器 (EQ) 配置文件，以及修改右摇杆周围 RGB 灯光的颜色。
