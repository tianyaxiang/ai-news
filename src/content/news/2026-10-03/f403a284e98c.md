# Kinematic signatures of impairment: Detecting alcohol intoxication in e-scooter riders using sensor data and machine learning
# 损伤的运动学特征：利用传感器数据和机器学习检测电动滑板车骑行者的酒精中毒情况

**Abstract:** Alcohol intoxication is a leading contributor to fatal and severe-injured e-scooterist crashes. Current countermeasures, such as temporal restrictions or pre-ride cognitive screening, cannot continuously assess an e-scooterist's physical motor control or impairment in real time.

**摘要：** 酒精中毒是导致电动滑板车骑行者发生致命和重伤事故的主要原因。目前的应对措施，如时间限制或骑行前的认知筛查，无法实时持续评估骑行者的身体运动控制能力或损伤程度。

We conducted a controlled experiment in which 25 participants rode an instrumented e-scooter through a test track while sober and at two targeted blood alcohol concentration levels (0.05% and 0.08%). The e-scooter was instrumented with a six-axis inertial measurement unit (IMU), and throttle and brake lever position sensors, all sampled at 100 Hz.

我们进行了一项受控实验，25名参与者在清醒状态以及两个目标血液酒精浓度水平（0.05% 和 0.08%）下，骑行配备传感器的电动滑板车通过测试轨道。该电动滑板车配备了六轴惯性测量单元 (IMU) 以及油门和刹车杆位置传感器，所有数据均以 100 Hz 的频率进行采样。

Two complementary signal features were computed: normalised permutation entropy, which quantifies temporal complexity, and standard deviation, which quantifies signal amplitude. Repeated measures correlation identified seven kinematic features (all IMU and throttle signals) whose entropy decreased (p < 0.001) while standard deviation increased (p < 0.01) with increasing intoxication, indicating that intoxicated riders shift from continuous, low-amplitude micro-corrections to fewer, high-amplitude reactive corrections.

研究计算了两个互补的信号特征：用于量化时间复杂度的归一化排列熵，以及用于量化信号幅度的标准差。重复测量相关性分析确定了七个运动学特征（均为 IMU 和油门信号），随着中毒程度加深，这些特征的熵值下降 (p < 0.001)，而标准差增加 (p < 0.01)，这表明醉酒骑行者从持续的、低幅度的微调转向了较少但高幅度的反应性修正。

An entropy based multi-class logistic regression classifier, evaluated through leave-one-participant-out cross-validation, achieved 85% overall accuracy and a weighted one-vs-rest area under the receiver operating characteristic curve (AuROC) of 0.94, with a sober-vs-high AuROC of 1.00. Steering rate and lateral acceleration were the most important predictive features, indicating that alcohol induces a distinct collapse in lateral equilibrium during riding.

通过留一法交叉验证评估，基于熵的多类逻辑回归分类器实现了 85% 的总体准确率，加权一对多接收者操作特征曲线下面积 (AuROC) 为 0.94，其中清醒与高度醉酒状态对比的 AuROC 达到 1.00。转向率和横向加速度是最重要的预测特征，这表明酒精会导致骑行过程中横向平衡能力的明显丧失。

Ultimately, these results demonstrate that onboard kinematic sensing combined with entropy-based signal analysis can reliably distinguish sober from intoxicated e-scooter riding, providing a foundation for automatic intoxication detection systems that preserve mobility for sober riders.

最终，这些结果表明，车载运动学传感技术结合基于熵的信号分析，可以可靠地分辨清醒与醉酒的电动滑板车骑行状态，为开发既能检测醉酒又能保障清醒骑行者出行便利的自动检测系统奠定了基础。
