# A Data-Free Physics-Informed Neural Operator for Level-Set Interface Advection
# 一种用于水平集界面平流的无数据物理信息神经算子

**Abstract:** Operators for interfacial problems are trained on reference solutions produced by the solver they are intended to replace. This work develops a data-free physics-informed neural operator for level-set interface advection, in which the interface is the equation's unknown and the operator maps an initial interface to the full spatiotemporal trajectory under a prescribed flow.

**摘要：** 针对界面问题的算子通常基于其旨在替代的求解器所生成的参考解进行训练。本研究开发了一种用于水平集界面平流的无数据物理信息神经算子，其中界面是方程的未知量，算子将初始界面映射到规定流场下的完整时空轨迹。

Training uses only the transport residual and a geometric constraint; no reference solution enters the objective at any point. A spacetime Fourier backbone emits the entire trajectory in one pass, and the initial condition is imposed by construction rather than by penalty, which removes the competition between the anchoring term and the residual that otherwise arises when no solution data are available.

训练过程仅使用输运残差和几何约束；在任何阶段均不引入参考解。该模型采用时空傅里叶主干网络一次性输出完整轨迹，并通过构造方式而非惩罚项来施加初始条件，从而消除了在缺乏解数据时，锚定项与残差项之间产生的竞争。

Supervised and hybrid operators are trained under an identical architecture, family, budget and test set, and are reported throughout as baselines that quantify what refusing labels costs. On a reversed single vortex the data-free operator reaches 1.614 +/- 0.067% relative L2 error on 100 held-out initial interfaces against 0.369 +/- 0.035% for the supervised baseline, a factor of 4.4; on solid-body rotation the corresponding figures are 3.804 +/- 1.075% and 2.576 +/- 0.159%, a factor of 1.5.

监督学习算子和混合算子在相同的架构、模型族、计算预算和测试集下进行训练，并作为基准报告，以量化放弃标签所带来的代价。在反向单涡旋实验中，无数据算子在 100 个留出的初始界面上的相对 L2 误差为 1.614 +/- 0.067%，而监督学习基准为 0.369 +/- 0.035%，差距为 4.4 倍；在刚体旋转实验中，相应数据分别为 3.804 +/- 1.075% 和 2.576 +/- 0.159%，差距为 1.5 倍。

The two benchmarks rank the arms differently, and the difference is attributable to the eikonal constraint: the exact solution violates |grad phi| = 1 over 0.3% of the domain under rotation and 86.9% under the vortex. Where the constraint is valid the physics-trained operator conserves enclosed area 2.7 times better than the supervised baseline despite a larger field error, and a hybrid arm using eight reference solutions outperforms a supervised arm using sixteen.

这两个基准测试对各方法的排序不同，这种差异归因于程函（eikonal）约束：在旋转情况下，精确解在 0.3% 的域内违反了 |grad phi| = 1 的条件，而在涡旋情况下这一比例为 86.9%。在约束有效的区域，尽管物理训练算子的场误差较大，但其封闭面积的守恒性比监督学习基准好 2.7 倍；此外，使用 8 个参考解的混合模型表现优于使用 16 个参考解的监督模型。
