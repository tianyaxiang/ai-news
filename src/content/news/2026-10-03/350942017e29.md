# Cybertruck sales in free fall as Tesla reports mediocre sales for Q3
# 特斯拉第三季度财报表现平平，Cybertruck 销量大幅下滑

Tesla sold 486,532 electric vehicles during the third quarter of 2026. That’s a 2.1 percent decrease on the same quarter in 2025, when the company found homes for 497,099 EVs, and one might think that would be bad news for a company valued on the premise of near-constant growth. But Tesla stock is up this morning; the year-over-year numbers might look bad, but the company still exceeded analysts’ expectations of 456,600 cars, which would have been a larger 8 percent year-on-year decline.

特斯拉在 2026 年第三季度共售出 486,532 辆电动汽车。与 2025 年同期售出的 497,099 辆相比，下降了 2.1%。人们或许会认为，对于一家以“近乎持续增长”为前提进行估值的公司来说，这算是个坏消息。但特斯拉股价今早却有所上涨；尽管同比数据看起来不佳，但该公司仍超过了分析师此前预期的 456,600 辆（若按该预期计算，同比降幅将高达 8%）。

Total production at Tesla actually increased year over year; it built 464,391 vehicles—a 3.7 percent increase—457,387 of which were Models 3 and Y (a 4.9 percent increase year over year). The remaining 7,004 were other models, mostly Cybertrucks, with presumably some Semis and Cybercabs, as the Models S and X are now retired; this category saw a 39.8 percent decrease year over year.

特斯拉的总产量实际上实现了同比增长；其共生产了 464,391 辆汽车，增长了 3.7%。其中 457,387 辆为 Model 3 和 Model Y（同比增长 4.9%）。其余 7,004 辆为其他车型，主要是 Cybertruck，推测还包括部分 Semi 和 Cybercab，因为 Model S 和 Model X 现已停产；该类别产量同比下降了 39.8%。

But the company couldn’t sell all the cars it made in Q3. It was able to sell 478,237 Models 3 and Y, a 0.6 percent decrease compared to Q3 2025. The decline in other models was severe, dropping by 48 percent to 8,295 units in total.

然而，特斯拉并未能售出第三季度生产的所有车辆。其 Model 3 和 Model Y 的销量为 478,237 辆，较 2025 年第三季度下降了 0.6%。其他车型的销量下滑更为严重，总计下降了 48%，仅为 8,295 辆。

However, Tesla’s energy storage business had a much better three months. Between the beginning of July and the end of September, Tesla deployed 13.7 GWh of energy storage products, a 9.6 percent year-over-year increase. Tesla will publish its Q3 financial results on October 21.

不过，特斯拉的储能业务在过去三个月表现要好得多。从 7 月初到 9 月底，特斯拉部署了 13.7 GWh 的储能产品，同比增长 9.6%。特斯拉将于 10 月 21 日发布第三季度财务报告。
