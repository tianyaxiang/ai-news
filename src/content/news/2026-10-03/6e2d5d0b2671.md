# Reachability Is Not Generalization: Understanding Verb--Noun Decomposition in Assembly Action Recognition
# 可达性并非泛化：理解装配动作识别中的动词-名词分解

**Abstract:** Assembly actions are compositional: they combine a manipulation with a part or tool. In deployment, systems routinely encounter novel combinations of familiar components, yet an atomic action classifier assigns every unseen combination exactly zero probability by construction. The prevailing solution is verb--noun decomposition, which predicts components separately and recombines them to reach unseen actions. While widely adopted, how decomposition generalizes under compositional shift remains poorly understood.

**摘要：** 装配动作具有组合性：它们将一种操作与零件或工具相结合。在实际部署中，系统经常会遇到熟悉组件的新颖组合，然而原子动作分类器在构建时会将所有未见过的组合概率直接设为零。目前主流的解决方案是“动词-名词分解”，即分别预测组件并将其重新组合以实现对未见动作的识别。尽管该方法被广泛采用，但分解在组合偏移下的泛化机制仍知之甚少。

We present a systematic analysis of verb--noun decomposition across three assembly datasets (MECCANO, HAViD, and IMPACT). Although decomposition escapes the atomic ceiling, its generalization extends only partially beyond it. Unseen-composition performance remains strongly tied to the co-occurrence structure of the training data, indicating that much of the observed gain arises from interpolation within densely supported regions of the compositional space rather than from unconstrained recombination.

我们对三个装配数据集（MECCANO、HAViD 和 IMPACT）中的动词-名词分解进行了系统分析。虽然分解方法突破了原子分类的上限，但其泛化能力仅得到了部分提升。对未见组合的性能表现仍然与训练数据的共现结构紧密相关，这表明观察到的大部分增益来自于组合空间中高密度支持区域的插值，而非无约束的重新组合。

Across datasets, failures consistently concentrate on the larger-vocabulary component, and IMPACT's verb-heavy vocabulary reverses the bottleneck from nouns to verbs. We further show that shared-encoder training introduces component entanglement, encouraging reliance on co-occurrence patterns that transfer poorly to unseen compositions and trailing independent recombination by up to $6.0\times$ in harmonic mean.

在各个数据集中，失败案例始终集中在词汇量较大的组件上；而 IMPACT 数据集中以动词为主的词汇表将瓶颈从名词反转到了动词上。我们进一步证明，共享编码器训练引入了组件纠缠，促使模型依赖于那些难以迁移到未见组合的共现模式，其调和平均值比独立重组方法落后多达 6.0 倍。

Taken together, these findings explain why decomposition achieves only partial compositional generalization in practice. By identifying primitive support, vocabulary asymmetry, and component entanglement as connected sources of error, we provide a portable diagnostic framework for studying compositional recognition beyond aggregate accuracy.

综上所述，这些发现解释了为什么分解方法在实践中只能实现部分的组合泛化。通过将原始支持度、词汇不对称性和组件纠缠识别为相互关联的误差来源，我们提供了一个可移植的诊断框架，用于在总体准确率之外深入研究组合识别问题。
