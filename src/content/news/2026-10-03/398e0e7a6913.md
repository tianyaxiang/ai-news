# It’s not AI anymore, it’s ‘super intelligence’ (according to the White House)
# 不再是人工智能，而是“超级智能”（白宫如是说）

This week, the White House got nearly every major tech CEO in one room — Zuckerberg, Bezos, Musk, and Anthropic’s Dario Amodei among them — to sign an AI safety pledge that President Donald Trump called “morally binding.”
本周，白宫将几乎所有主流科技公司的首席执行官召集到一起——其中包括扎克伯格、贝索斯、马斯克以及 Anthropic 的达里奥·阿莫代（Dario Amodei）——签署了一份唐纳德·特朗普总统称之为“道德约束”的人工智能安全承诺。

Trump also signed an executive order officially rebranding AI as “super intelligence,” and meanwhile, Meta and OpenAI are putting friendlier faces on their AI products, even as the biggest money in AI still appears to be coming from the enterprise.
特朗普还签署了一项行政命令，正式将人工智能更名为“超级智能”。与此同时，尽管人工智能领域的大部分资金似乎仍来自企业端，但 Meta 和 OpenAI 正在为其人工智能产品打造更友好的形象。

On this episode of TechCrunch’s Equity podcast, Kirsten Korosec, Anthony Ha, and Sean O’Kane dig into this week’s AI moves, the ugly economics of consumer AI, plus the shifting IPO market and a handful of startup deals.
在本期 TechCrunch 的《Equity》播客中，Kirsten Korosec、Anthony Ha 和 Sean O’Kane 深入探讨了本周的人工智能动态、消费者人工智能背后残酷的经济学，以及不断变化的 IPO 市场和几笔初创企业交易。
