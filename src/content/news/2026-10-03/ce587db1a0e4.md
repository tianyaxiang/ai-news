# Domain generalization and synthetic data in object detection: the enabler, the probe, and the gap
# 目标检测中的域泛化与合成数据：赋能者、探测器与鸿沟

**Abstract:** Object detection models often experience performance degradation when deployed under distribution shifts, caused by for example changes in weather type, operational environment, or object appearance.
**摘要：** 目标检测模型在部署于分布偏移（distribution shifts）环境时，性能往往会下降，这通常是由天气类型、操作环境或物体外观的变化所引起的。

Domain Generalization (DG) aims to develop models that remain robust under such shifts and generalize well to unseen domains. DG research specifically focused on object detection models is scarce, although these models face additional challenges around localization and multi-scale representations.
域泛化（Domain Generalization, DG）旨在开发能够在上述偏移下保持鲁棒性，并能良好泛化到未见域的模型。尽管目标检测模型在定位和多尺度表示方面面临额外的挑战，但专门针对目标检测模型的 DG 研究仍然十分匮乏。

Synthetic data is a promising tool to support in DG, by enabling large-scale generation of diverse new samples. In this paper, we present an object detection-centric review of DG and examine the role of synthetic data from three complementary perspectives.
合成数据是支持 DG 的一种有前景的工具，它能够实现大规模多样化新样本的生成。在本文中，我们以目标检测为中心对 DG 进行了综述，并从三个互补的角度审视了合成数据的作用。

First, synthetic data acts as an enabler of DG through diversification and alignment strategies that aim to improve robustness to distribution shifts.
首先，合成数据通过旨在提高对分布偏移鲁棒性的多样化和对齐策略，充当了 DG 的赋能者。

Second, it serves as a probe that enables controlled experimentation to identify and understand failure modes.
其次，它充当了探测器，通过受控实验来识别和理解模型的失效模式。

Third, we discuss the synthetic-to-real gap, a particularly challenging form of domain shift that arises when models trained on synthetic imagery are deployed on real-world data.
第三，我们讨论了“合成到真实”（synthetic-to-real）的鸿沟，这是一种极具挑战性的域偏移形式，当在合成图像上训练的模型被部署到真实世界数据上时，就会出现这种问题。

Through reviewing these perspectives, we identify limitations of current DG approaches for object detection and argue that future research requires representation-aware methods that explicitly address both localization and classification under domain shift.
通过回顾这些视角，我们指出了当前目标检测 DG 方法的局限性，并提出未来的研究需要具备“表示感知”（representation-aware）的方法，以明确解决域偏移下的定位和分类问题。
