# Building AI that can work across the enterprise

SponsoredIn partnership withInfosys For years, enterprises have approached AI as a series of discrete experiments. Now, as AI agents begin to move into the workflows that run the business, that model is starting to look inadequate. Organizations moving from isolated pilots toward AI agents and autonomous workflows are confronting a deeper challenge: rethinking how data, business processes, governance, and people fit together, turning AI from a collection of tools into part of the enterprise operating model.

多年来，企业一直将人工智能视为一系列离散的实验。如今，随着人工智能代理开始进入企业的业务工作流程，这种模式已显得力不从心。那些正从孤立的试点项目转向人工智能代理和自主工作流程的组织，正面临着更深层次的挑战：重新思考数据、业务流程、治理和人员如何协同工作，从而将人工智能从一系列工具转变为企业运营模式的一部分。

For Thorsten Leiduck, SVP and global head of SAP Business AI Platform Ecosystem, that shift begins with treating AI as an enterprise platform rather than a collection of disconnected projects. “AI is absolutely dependent on the data foundation,” he says, arguing that companies need a connected, semantically rich layer that reflects their business before they can reliably deploy AI across processes. The stakes also change as AI moves into mission-critical workflows. For some everyday applications, “80% might be good enough,” he says, but in areas such as supply chains or financial close, that level of accuracy is “a very terrible result.”

对于 SAP 商业人工智能平台生态系统高级副总裁兼全球负责人 Thorsten Leiduck 而言，这种转变始于将人工智能视为一个企业平台，而非一系列互不关联的项目。他表示：“人工智能绝对依赖于数据基础。”他认为，企业在跨流程可靠地部署人工智能之前，需要一个能够反映其业务的、互联且语义丰富的数据层。随着人工智能进入关键任务工作流程，其风险也在发生变化。他指出，对于一些日常应用，“80% 的准确率可能就足够了”，但在供应链或财务结算等领域，这一准确率水平是“非常糟糕的结果”。

That foundation also determines which AI applications are worth pursuing. Satinder Pal Singh, associate VP North America for SAP BAIP and SAP AI Strategy at Infosys, frames AI agents as “digital coworkers” and recommends evaluating potential use cases through three lenses: impact, feasibility, and scalability. Companies should identify where an agent can move meaningful business metrics, determine whether their data and talent can support the use case, and then establish whether successful applications can be replicated. “A pilot that can't scale is just an experiment,” he says.

这一基础也决定了哪些人工智能应用值得追求。印孚瑟斯（Infosys）SAP BAIP 及 SAP 人工智能战略北美副总裁 Satinder Pal Singh 将人工智能代理定义为“数字同事”，并建议通过三个维度来评估潜在的用例：影响力、可行性和可扩展性。企业应确定代理可以在哪些方面推动有意义的业务指标，判断其数据和人才储备是否能支持该用例，然后确定成功的应用是否可以复制。他说：“无法扩展的试点项目仅仅是实验而已。”

Scaling also brings governance into sharper focus. Clear policies, access controls, monitoring, and oversight are necessary to ensure agents remain trustworthy and aligned with business requirements. “The absence of governance doesn't speed up AI adoption,” Singh says. “It creates a risk backlog” that can ultimately force a more disruptive stop and audit. Those considerations will become more important as AI agents take on more responsibilities as digital coworkers.

扩展也使治理问题变得更加突出。明确的政策、访问控制、监控和监督对于确保代理保持可信并符合业务需求至关重要。Singh 说：“缺乏治理并不会加速人工智能的采用，反而会造成风险积压，最终可能导致更具破坏性的停工和审计。”随着人工智能代理作为数字同事承担更多责任，这些考量将变得愈发重要。

Both executives see a future in which agentic systems coordinate work across applications, while customer-specific knowledge graphs and governed data become critical enterprise assets. For Singh, the longer-term opportunity is a shift from AI as a productivity tool to “AI as a decision-making partner,” helping leaders understand what is happening, anticipate what comes next, and determine how to respond. 两位高管都预见到了这样一个未来：代理系统将在各个应用程序之间协调工作，而特定于客户的知识图谱和受治理的数据将成为关键的企业资产。对于 Singh 而言，更长远的机遇在于从将人工智能视为生产力工具，转变为将其视为“决策合作伙伴”，帮助领导者了解正在发生的事情、预测未来趋势并确定应对方案。本期《商业实验室》（Business Lab）节目由印孚瑟斯合作制作。
