Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.17463 (cs) [Submitted on 12 Nov 2026] Title: Availability-Aware Historical Feedback Learning for Electric Arc Furnace Temperature and Chemical Composition Prediction Authors: Ahmed Shoyeb Raihan, Farzana Islam, Ashish Nimbarte, Imtiaz Ahmed.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.17463 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 12 日] 标题：用于电弧炉温度和化学成分预测的可用性感知历史反馈学习 作者：Ahmed Shoyeb Raihan, Farzana Islam, Ashish Nimbarte, Imtiaz Ahmed。

Abstract: Electric arc furnace (EAF) steelmaking plays an increasingly important role in recycled and lower-carbon steel production. However, variability in charged materials, energy input, and operating conditions makes accurate prediction of molten steel temperature and final chemical composition challenging. Intermittent or delayed measurements limit the information available for timely prediction.

摘要：电弧炉 (EAF) 炼钢在回收钢和低碳钢生产中发挥着越来越重要的作用。然而，装料、能源输入和操作条件的变化使得准确预测钢水温度和最终化学成分变得具有挑战性。间歇性或延迟的测量限制了可用于及时预测的信息。

This study develops an availability-aware historical-feedback learning framework for predicting EAF endpoint temperature and the final concentrations of twelve chemical elements. Eight industrial data sources are integrated into three progressively enriched predictor representations comprising 19 static, 39 temporal, and 47 history-informed features.

本研究开发了一种可用性感知历史反馈学习框架，用于预测电弧炉终点温度和十二种化学元素的最终浓度。八个工业数据源被整合为三种逐步丰富的预测器表示，包含 19 个静态特征、39 个时间特征和 47 个历史信息特征。

Historical outcomes are incorporated only when eligible under an assumed 60-minute measurement-release delay. Using 19,738 temperature heats and 3,341 chemistry heats, twelve tuned regression algorithms are evaluated through chronological forward validation. The proposed historical-feedback representation reduces root mean squared error (RMSE) in 147 of 156 matched algorithm-target comparisons.

仅在假设 60 分钟测量发布延迟的情况下，符合条件时才会纳入历史结果。使用 19,738 个温度炉次和 3,341 个化学成分炉次，通过时间序列前向验证评估了十二种经过调优的回归算法。所提出的历史反馈表示在 156 组匹配的算法-目标比较中，有 147 组降低了均方根误差 (RMSE)。

For temperature prediction, ExtraTrees achieves an RMSE of 12.290 degrees C, representing a 5.54 percent reduction compared with its temporal-only counterpart. Historical-feature ablation and feedback-delay sensitivity analyses demonstrate the importance of recent outcome information and its availability.

在温度预测方面，ExtraTrees 实现了 12.290 摄氏度的 RMSE，与其仅使用时间特征的对应模型相比降低了 5.54%。历史特征消融分析和反馈延迟敏感性分析证明了近期结果信息及其可用性的重要性。

In a separate retrospective later-period evaluation, lightweight adaptive residual correction reduces temperature RMSE from 12.502 to 12.275 degrees C without retraining the underlying model. Exploratory deep learning comparisons and target-specific feature-attribution analyses provide additional insights into predictive behavior. The results establish historical outcome availability and sequential feedback as important considerations for EAF endpoint prediction.

在另一项回顾性后期评估中，轻量级自适应残差校正将温度 RMSE 从 12.502 降低至 12.275 摄氏度，且无需重新训练底层模型。探索性的深度学习比较和针对特定目标的特征归因分析为预测行为提供了额外的见解。研究结果确立了历史结果的可用性和顺序反馈是电弧炉终点预测的重要考量因素。
