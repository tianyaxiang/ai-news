Abstract:Ensembles of neural networks have repeatedly been shown to outperform single networks. Ensemble theory attributes this gain to the accuracy of the individual members and the diversity among them. Mixture-of-experts models already combine many experts, but a single router decides how the experts are weighted for each input. Motivated by ensemble theory, we apply ensembling to the routing step: an ensemble of routers, trained jointly with the experts, proposes the expert weights.

摘要：神经网络集成已被反复证明优于单一网络。集成理论将这种增益归因于个体成员的准确性及其多样性。混合专家模型已经结合了许多专家，但单一的路由器决定了每个输入如何对专家进行加权。受集成理论的启发，我们将集成应用于路由步骤：一个与专家联合训练的路由器集成，负责提出专家权重。

We systematically evaluate this approach using underparameterized networks on two image-classification and two tabular-regression tasks. We analyse each experiment with a performance-diversity decomposition of the router ensemble, which, to our knowledge, has not previously been developed for routers. We report four findings. First, more routers lower the validation loss. Second, training routers to explicitly maximize expert diversity improves the mean performance on three of the four tasks. Third, explicitly maximizing router diversity has no effect on performance. Fourth, combining router weights with the arithmetic mean works better than combining them with the geometric mean. Routing benefits mildly from ensembling, but this benefit is offset by the computational cost of training additional routers.

我们使用欠参数化网络，在两个图像分类任务和两个表格回归任务上系统地评估了这种方法。我们通过路由器集成的性能-多样性分解来分析每个实验，据我们所知，这种方法此前尚未应用于路由器。我们报告了四个发现。第一，更多的路由器降低了验证损失。第二，训练路由器以显式最大化专家多样性，在四个任务中的三个上提高了平均性能。第三，显式最大化路由器多样性对性能没有影响。第四，将路由器权重与算术平均值结合比与几何平均值结合效果更好。路由从集成中获得了轻微的收益，但这种收益被训练额外路由器的计算成本所抵消。
