Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.17459 (cs) [Submitted on 12 Nov 2026] Title: Classification with Large-Step Gradient Descent: Unifying Self-Stabilization and Convergence Rates Authors: Salma Tarmoun, Lachlan Ewen MacDonald, Konstantinos Emmanouilidis, Ziqing Xu, René Vidal.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.17459 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 12 日] 标题：大步长梯度下降分类：统一自稳定与收敛速率 作者：Salma Tarmoun, Lachlan Ewen MacDonald, Konstantinos Emmanouilidis, Ziqing Xu, René Vidal。

Abstract: Neural networks are often trained with gradient descent (GD) using step sizes that exceed classical stability thresholds, a regime where standard convergence theory no longer applies. Despite exhibiting non-monotonic loss and oscillatory dynamics, this regime frequently leads to faster convergence and better generalization.

摘要：神经网络通常使用超过经典稳定性阈值的步长进行梯度下降（GD）训练，在这种机制下，标准收敛理论不再适用。尽管表现出非单调损失和振荡动力学，但这种机制往往能带来更快的收敛速度和更好的泛化能力。

Existing theoretical perspectives offer complementary but incomplete insights: one identifies a self-stabilization mechanism that drives GD toward flat regions but does not yield convergence rates; another derives rigorous rates for specific losses but does not explain the underlying stabilization mechanism; and a third unifies these views for overparameterized least squares, but relies on the existence of a minimizer manifold, which is absent in classification settings.

现有的理论视角提供了互补但不完整的见解：一种视角识别出一种将 GD 推向平坦区域的自稳定机制，但未能得出收敛速率；另一种视角为特定损失函数推导了严格的速率，但未能解释潜在的稳定机制；第三种视角在过参数化最小二乘法中统一了这些观点，但依赖于极小值流形的存在，而这在分类设置中并不存在。

In this work, we extend this unified geometric perspective to exponential-tail losses, including logistic and cross-entropy losses in separable linear classification, where the loss infimum is not attained at any finite parameter. We identify a curvature-defined reference subspace that replaces the role of the minimizer manifold, yielding a coupled dynamical system in which orthogonal oscillations are damped by parallel progress along directions of decreasing sharpness.

在这项工作中，我们将这种统一的几何视角扩展到指数尾损失函数，包括可分线性分类中的逻辑损失和交叉熵损失，其中损失下界在任何有限参数下都无法达到。我们确定了一个由曲率定义的参考子空间来取代极小值流形的作用，从而产生了一个耦合动力系统，其中正交振荡通过沿锐度递减方向的平行进展而得到抑制。

Under regularity conditions, which we verify for logistic and multiclass cross-entropy losses on symmetric data models, we derive explicit bounds for the iterates, not just the loss, covering both the unstable and stable regimes, and revealing a three-phase damping structure in the latter.

在对称数据模型上针对逻辑损失和多类交叉熵损失验证了正则性条件后，我们推导出了迭代值的显式界限，而不仅仅是损失的界限，涵盖了不稳定和稳定机制，并揭示了后者中存在的三阶段阻尼结构。

Our analysis further uncovers a transient implicit bias induced by large step sizes: although GD converges asymptotically to the max-margin direction, large steps can leave persistent orthogonal residuals and, in certain geometries, amplify them, affecting finite-time alignment with the max-margin direction.

我们的分析进一步揭示了大步长引起的瞬态隐式偏差：尽管 GD 渐近收敛于最大间隔方向，但大步长可能会留下持续的正交残差，并在某些几何结构中放大这些残差，从而影响与最大间隔方向的有限时间对齐。
