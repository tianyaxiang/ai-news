### Hacker behind 2022 GTA VI leaks pleads guilty

2022年《侠盗猎车手6》（GTA VI）泄密事件的黑客已认罪。

Arion Kurtaj, a member of the Lapsus$ cybercrime group who leaked 90 videos of Grand Theft Auto VI in 2022 and hacked other big companies, pleaded guilty on Monday, according to the UK’s Crown Prosecution Service (CPS).

据英国皇家检察署（CPS）称，Lapsus$网络犯罪组织成员Arion Kurtaj于周一认罪。他曾在2022年泄露了90段《侠盗猎车手6》的视频，并入侵了其他大型公司。

Kurtaj, who is now 21, hacked BT/EE sometime between August and November 2021, and starting in early November of that year, the company “noted a marked increase in unauthorised SIM swaps of their customers mobile phones.”

现年21岁的Kurtaj曾在2021年8月至11月期间入侵了BT/EE公司。从当年11月初开始，该公司“注意到其客户手机未经授权的SIM卡更换行为显著增加”。

He was charged for those offenses and moved to a hotel for his “own protection” in September 2022, but “breached his bail conditions to carry out further hacks on major companies using a mobile phone, the hotel TV’s Wi-Fi and a firestick,” including hacks on Rockstar Games, Uber, and Revolut, the CPS says.

CPS表示，他因这些罪行受到指控，并于2022年9月被转移到一家酒店以进行“自我保护”。但他“违反了保释条件，利用手机、酒店电视的Wi-Fi和电视棒对多家大公司进行了进一步的黑客攻击”，其中包括对Rockstar Games、Uber和Revolut的入侵。

He was sentenced to life in a secure hospital in December 2023 because of his autism, but in January, Kurtaj was deemed fit for trial and moved to prison, the BBC reports.

据英国广播公司（BBC）报道，他曾因患有自闭症于2023年12月被判处在安全医院接受终身监禁，但在今年1月，Kurtaj被认定适合受审并被转移至监狱。

Kurtaj is set to be sentenced on November 23rd. GTA VI will be released on November 19th.

Kurtaj将于11月23日被判刑。《侠盗猎车手6》将于11月19日正式发布。
