# Jensen Huang Is Gone. Now What?

Save this story. I don’t go into any given week at WIRED assuming I’ll be thinking much about extramarital affairs. But last week, they were the only thing on my mind.

保存这篇报道。我从未想过在《连线》（WIRED）工作的任何一周里，我会过多地思考婚外情问题。但上周，这成了我脑海中唯一的事情。

These thoughts, of course, were prompted by Jensen Huang. In the span of a few completely insane days, the billionaire CEO and cofounder of Nvidia went from being a globally influential Silicon Valley executive to a lesson in exactly what not to do if you want to keep your job, and the extraordinary power that comes with it. It’s a damn mess—for Huang and his family, sure, but also for Nvidia, the AI industry the company is instrumental in powering, and the entirety of the global economy.

当然，这些想法是由黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）引发的。在短短几天极其疯狂的时间里，这位英伟达（Nvidia）的亿万富翁首席执行官兼联合创始人，从一位全球具有影响力的硅谷高管，变成了一个反面教材——如果你想保住工作以及随之而来的巨大权力，绝对不能做这些事。这真是一团糟——对黄仁勋及其家人来说当然如此，对英伟达、该公司在其中发挥关键推动作用的人工智能行业，以及整个全球经济来说，也是如此。

WIRED’s senior correspondent Lauren Goode has spent years reporting on Nvidia and Huang, including interviewing the man himself on multiple occasions. She also spent last week breaking news about his downfall, despite being in Europe on vacation. (Sorry, Lauren. You can have those PTO days back.) So at the end of a very long week for WIRED’s newsroom, I called Lauren to talk about what she makes of Huang’s ouster, what it means for AI, and where this all goes next. We also made a bet about Huang’s future—one involving a meal he recently made semi-famous.

《连线》资深记者劳伦·古德（Lauren Goode）多年来一直报道英伟达和黄仁勋，包括多次亲自采访他。尽管上周她正在欧洲度假，但她还是花时间报道了黄仁勋下台的突发新闻。（抱歉，劳伦。那些休假日你可以补回来。）因此，在《连线》新闻编辑部度过漫长的一周后，我致电劳伦，谈论她对黄仁勋被罢免的看法、这对人工智能意味着什么，以及未来事态的发展。我们还就黄仁勋的未来打了个赌——涉及他最近让其小有名气的一顿饭。
