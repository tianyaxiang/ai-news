The latest numbers are in on how much energy hyperscale data centers use and the carbon pollution they create.

关于超大规模数据中心消耗多少能源以及它们所产生的碳污染，最新的数据已经出炉。

Fossil fuels are still the biggest source of electricity for hyperscale data centers in the US, a new paper published in the journal Nature Sustainability shows. They likely burn through more electricity collectively than many countries do, resulting in a serious amount of greenhouse gas emissions.

发表在《自然-可持续发展》期刊上的一篇新论文显示，化石燃料仍然是美国超大规模数据中心最大的电力来源。它们集体消耗的电力可能超过了许多国家，导致了大量的温室气体排放。

The study homes in on hyperscale facilities — the massive structures spanning millions of square feet that developers are racing to build in large part to support generative AI. Researchers say the findings show that there’s a lot of work left to do to cut down pollution stemming from AI’s reliance on fossil fuels.

这项研究聚焦于超大规模设施——即开发商竞相建造的、占地数百万平方英尺的庞大建筑，其主要目的是支持生成式人工智能。研究人员表示，研究结果表明，要减少人工智能对化石燃料的依赖所产生的污染，还有大量工作要做。

“We tend to discuss AI’s environmental footprint as though it were some fixed property of the technology, when in reality the same computing infrastructure can have dramatically different consequences depending on the electricity system supporting it,” Gianluca Guidi, lead author of the new study and a postdoctoral fellow in the Department of Biostatistics at Harvard T.H. Chan School of Public Health, says in an email to The Verge.

“我们倾向于讨论人工智能的环境足迹，就好像它是该技术的一种固定属性，但实际上，同样的计算基础设施根据其所依赖的电力系统，可能会产生截然不同的后果，”该项新研究的主要作者、哈佛大学陈曾熙公共卫生学院生物统计学系博士后研究员吉安卢卡·圭迪（Gianluca Guidi）在给《The Verge》的一封电子邮件中说道。

Altogether, the 403 hyperscale facilities likely consumed around 82 terawatt-hours of electricity that year, about 1.8 percent of all US electricity consumption. That’s even more than the amount of electricity that about 148 nations — a majority of the countries in the world including Austria, Israel, and Venezuela but excluding the largest economies including the US and China — individually burn through each year, according to John Sterman, director of the MIT System Dynamics group and MIT Sloan Sustainability Initiative.

麻省理工学院系统动力学小组及斯隆可持续发展倡议主任约翰·斯特曼（John Sterman）表示，总的来说，这403个超大规模设施在那一年可能消耗了约82太瓦时的电力，约占美国总用电量的1.8%。这甚至超过了世界上约148个国家（包括奥地利、以色列和委内瑞拉等大多数国家，但不包括美国和中国等最大经济体）每年各自消耗的电力总量。

Electricity demand from these data centers produced 25.7 million metric tons of CO2 emissions, comparable to the annual carbon footprint of Denmark, according to the research paper. Sterman says that exceeds the total carbon emissions of about 113 nations. That’s because about 54 percent of the electricity the hyperscale facilities consumed likely came from gas and coal.

根据该研究论文，这些数据中心的电力需求产生了2570万公吨的二氧化碳排放量，相当于丹麦一年的碳足迹。斯特曼表示，这超过了约113个国家的碳排放总量。这是因为超大规模设施所消耗的电力中，约有54%可能来自天然气和煤炭。

The study showed how big a difference the local electricity mix makes when it comes to the environmental impact of AI and data centers. Generating electricity in heavily coal-reliant Wyoming led to roughly seven times more carbon emissions per kilowatt-hour than in hydropower-rich Oregon.

该研究表明，在人工智能和数据中心的环境影响方面，当地的电力结构会产生巨大的差异。在严重依赖煤炭的怀俄明州发电，其每千瓦时的碳排放量大约是水力发电丰富的俄勒冈州的七倍。

Both You and Guidi also pointed out how problematic it is that researchers have to go through such great lengths to estimate energy consumption because data center operators won’t disclose that information. To that end, Guidi and his colleagues are using their research to build a website to track the environmental impact of individual data centers.

游（You）和圭迪（Guidi）都指出，由于数据中心运营商不愿披露相关信息，研究人员不得不费尽周折来估算能源消耗，这是一个严重的问题。为此，圭迪和他的同事们正在利用他们的研究成果建立一个网站，以追踪各个数据中心的环境影响。
