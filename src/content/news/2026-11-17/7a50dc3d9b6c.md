Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.17524 (cs) [Submitted on 12 Nov 2026] Title: Model Annealing: Data-Efficient Learning through Review and Consolidation Authors: Ali Mohammadi Ruzbahani, Rezvan Abouzadeh.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.17524 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 12 日] 标题：模型退火：通过回顾与整合实现数据高效学习 作者：Ali Mohammadi Ruzbahani, Rezvan Abouzadeh。

Abstract: A small labelled dataset is memorized long before the training budget runs out, and the one-hot labels that remain to be fitted then mostly sharpen confidence. Model annealing spends the rest of the budget on review and consolidation, relearning from the network's own knowledge.

摘要：小型标注数据集在训练预算耗尽前很久就被记住了，而剩余需要拟合的独热标签大多只是为了增强置信度。模型退火将剩余的预算用于回顾和整合，从网络自身的知识中进行再学习。

Training is divided into episodes whose learning rate is raised again and cooled, every cooled state joins a memory of past selves, and each new episode learns from the labels, from the tempered predictions of this memory, and from adversarial dreams sought where student and memory disagree most.

训练被划分为多个阶段，每个阶段的学习率会再次提高并冷却；每个冷却后的状态都会加入到一个包含过去自我的记忆库中，而每个新阶段的学习不仅来自标签，还来自该记忆库的温和预测，以及在学生模型与记忆库分歧最大处寻求的对抗性梦境。

The memory averages predictions rather than weights, and the deployed network is the last student, so inference costs nothing extra. Across CIFAR-100, Tiny-ImageNet and Imagewoof, four architecture families and 5% to 100% of the labels, model annealing raises the top-1 accuracy of the AdamW-trained ConvNeXt, ViT and MLP-Mixer over cross-entropy training in 28 of 33 configurations, by 1.3 points on average, and is on par in accuracy with snapshot distillation, the closest competitor.

记忆库对预测而非权重进行平均，且部署的网络即为最后一个学生模型，因此推理过程无需额外成本。在 CIFAR-100、Tiny-ImageNet 和 Imagewoof 数据集、四个架构系列以及 5% 到 100% 的标签比例下，模型退火在 33 种配置中的 28 种里，将 AdamW 训练的 ConvNeXt、ViT 和 MLP-Mixer 的 Top-1 准确率较交叉熵训练平均提高了 1.3 个百分点，且在准确率上与最接近的竞争对手——快照蒸馏（snapshot distillation）持平。
