Why It's Fun to Explore Have you ever wondered while reading Chinese history or classical poetry: "Where exactly on today's map are Jiankang (the Six Dynasties capital), Huangzhou (where Su Dongpo composed the Red Cliffs Rhapsody in exile), Languan Pass in the Qinling Mountains (where Han Yu halted his horse in the snow), or Zhuxianzhen (Yue Fei's northern campaign frontier)?" "How many names did Chang'an hold across two millennia, and what pivotal events unfolded on that soil?" "How did the realm evolve step by step—from the Partition of Jin and the Seven Warring States at the opening of Sima Guang's Zizhi Tongjian (403 BCE), Qin's unification campaigns, Emperor Wu of Han opening the Hexi Corridor, High Tang's Four Garrisons of Anxi, and Yuan's Xuanzhengyuan jurisdiction over Tibet, to Zeng Jize's recovery of Ili (1881 CE) and the 20th-century shift from the 'Begonia Leaf' (秋海棠) map to the 'Rooster' (雄鸡) map?"

为什么探索它很有趣？在阅读中国历史或古典诗词时，你是否曾好奇：“建康（六朝古都）、黄州（苏东坡被贬时创作《赤壁赋》之地）、秦岭蓝关（韩愈雪拥蓝关马不前之处）或朱仙镇（岳飞北伐前线）在今天的地图上究竟在哪里？”“长安在两千年间有过多少名字，那片土地上发生过哪些关键事件？”“疆域是如何一步步演变的——从司马光《资治通鉴》开篇的三家分晋与战国七雄、秦朝统一战争、汉武帝开辟河西走廊、盛唐安西四镇、元朝宣政院管辖西藏，到曾纪泽收复伊犁（1881年），以及20世纪从“秋海棠”地图到“雄鸡”地图的变迁？”

Chronicles of the Realm (jiangshan-map) brings together 2,400+ years of Chinese history across 18 historical periods into a single interactive parchment atlas—featuring multi-stage territorial and prefecture snapshots, ancient cities, strategic mountain passes, poetic and battlefield landmarks, chronological milestones, and reigning monarchs & imperial eras. No heavy print atlases required—just drag the timeline and click any city to travel through space and time.

《江山时序》（jiangshan-map）将中国两千四百多年的历史浓缩在18个历史时期，汇集成一张交互式的羊皮纸风格地图集。它包含多阶段的疆域与行政区划快照、古城、战略关隘、诗词与战场地标、历史里程碑，以及在位君主与年号。无需厚重的纸质地图，只需拖动时间轴并点击任意城市，即可穿梭于时空之中。

Feature 1: Scrub the Timeline to Watch Realms Rise & Fall, Track Monarchs, and Compare Ancient & Modern Borders Multi-Polity Confrontations on a Single Canvas: From the Seven Warring States (Qin, Chu, Qi, Yan, Zhao, Wei, Han) alongside Zhou, Yue, Ba-Shu, Zhongshan, and Yiqu, to the Three Kingdoms warlords (Yuan Shao, Cao Cao, Liu Bei, Sun Quan, Gongsun's Liaodong), Northern & Southern Dynasties, Five Dynasties & Ten Kingdoms, and Song, Liao, Western Xia, Dali, Jin, and the Mongols—all rendered side-by-side in high-contrast traditional parchment hues. Hover over any polity in the bottom-left legend to highlight its territory, or click to open that polity's profile (lifespan, capitals and a short history) and zoom to its territory.

功能一：拖动时间轴观察政权兴衰，追踪君主，对比古今疆界。单画布上的多政权对峙：从战国七雄（秦、楚、齐、燕、赵、魏、韩）及周、越、巴蜀、中山、义渠，到三国军阀（袁绍、曹操、刘备、孙权、公孙氏辽东）、南北朝、五代十国，以及宋、辽、西夏、大理、金、蒙古——所有政权均以高对比度的传统羊皮纸色调并列呈现。悬停在左下角图例中的任意政权即可高亮其疆域，点击则可打开该政权的简介（存续时间、都城及简史）并缩放至其领土。

Multi-Stage Territorial Evolution & Dashed "Ghost Boundary Diffs": At every major territorial milestone—such as Qin's conquest of Ba-Shu (316 BCE), Yan General Qin Kai's expansion into Liaodong (300 BCE), Ma Yuan's bronze pillars in Jiaozhi (43 CE), Sima Yi's campaign in Liaodong (238 CE), Li Jing's conquest of Tuyuhun (635 CE), the Four Garrisons of Anxi & Wang Xuance's campaign in Central India (648 CE), Yuan's Xuanzhengyuan rule over Tibet (1288 CE), Ming's Jiaozhi Province in Annam (1407 CE), the 1881 Treaty of Saint Petersburg recovering Ili, the 1898 foreign leased territories, the 1945 retrocession of Taiwan, and the 1946 transition from the "Begonia Leaf" (秋海棠) map to the "Rooster" (雄鸡) map—click any milestone or phase dot to switch maps and reveal the previous phase's dashed vermilion Ghost Boundary Diff so territorial gains and losses are immediately visible.

多阶段疆域演变与虚线“幽灵边界差”：在每一个重大疆域里程碑——如秦灭巴蜀（公元前316年）、燕将秦开拓东胡（公元前300年）、马援立铜柱于交趾（公元43年）、司马懿征辽东（公元238年）、李靖灭吐谷浑（公元635年）、安西四镇与王玄策出使天竺（公元648年）、元朝宣政院管辖西藏（公元1288年）、明朝交趾承宣布政使司（公元1407年）、1881年《中俄伊犁条约》收复伊犁、1898年列强租借地、1945年台湾光复，以及1946年从“秋海棠”地图到“雄鸡”地图的过渡——点击任意里程碑或阶段点，即可切换地图并显示前一阶段的朱红色虚线“幽灵边界差”，使疆域的得失一目了然。

Reigning Monarch on the Timeline: The bubble above the timeline names the monarch on the throne in the current year (e.g. 100 BCE · Western Han · Emp. Wu, 630 CE · Tang · Emp. Taizong, 1700 CE · Qing · Kangxi Emp. (Shengzu)); the Chinese interface also shows the regnal era year (唐太宗·贞观四年). Ancient Prefecture Hover Inspection & Modern Province Overlay: First-level administrative divisions (Commanderies, Circuits, Routes, and Provinces) are rendered with deduplicated Parchment Casing borders. Zoom in and hover over any prefecture (e.g., Ming Yingtian Prefecture or Three Kingdoms Jingzhou) to inspect its jurisdiction and modern provincial equivalents. In the top-right Layers menu, Provinces (on by default) draws today's provincial boundaries over the historical map, and Prov. Names adds their names.

时间轴上的在位君主：时间轴上方的气泡标注了当前年份的在位君主（例如：公元前100年·西汉·汉武帝；公元630年·唐·唐太宗；公元1700年·清·康熙帝（圣祖））；中文界面还会显示年号（如：唐太宗·贞观四年）。古地名悬停查看与现代省份叠加：一级行政区（郡、道、路、省）以去重后的羊皮纸边框渲染。放大并悬停在任意行政区（如明朝应天府或三国荆州）上，即可查看其管辖范围及对应的现代省份。在右上角的“图层”菜单中，开启“省份”（默认开启）可在历史地图上绘制现代省界，开启“省名”则可添加现代省份名称。

Dynamic River Courses, Ancient Lakes & Grand Engineering Works: As you scrub through time, historical shifts of the Yellow River, expanding and shrinking ancient lakes (Yunmeng Marsh, Lop Nur / Puchanghai), Warring States/Qin/Han/Ming Great Walls, Sui-Tang & Grand Canals, the Silk Road, and Zheng He's maritime routes evolve in lockstep with each era; ancient coastlines around the Bohai Gulf, northern Jiangsu, the Yangtze estuary, and the Pearl River Delta also advance and retreat by era. Zoomable Timeline: Drag the handles at either end of the timeline inward to zoom into one stretch of history (handy for short eras such as Qin, the Three Kingdoms, or the Five Dynasties); double-click the timeline to return to the full overview. Voice Narration: Turn on the narration toggle next to the play button and autoplay reads out each year and its events in the browser's built-in voice; it also follows a person's chronicle on autoplay (requires a browser with speech synthesis).
