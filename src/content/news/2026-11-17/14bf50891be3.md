计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.17438 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 12 日] 标题：实时扩散重光照作为神经传感器：弥合机器人感知的低光域差距 作者：Shengjie Xu, Alexander Gao, Anshul Rai, Dae Yeol Lee, Guan-Ming Su, Ming C. Lin。

Abstract: Low-light conditions severely degrade robotic perception: object detectors trained on daylight imagery are prone to failure under dark and adverse weather conditions that fall outside of their training distribution. Furthermore, from an efficiency standpoint, existing Low-Light Image Enhancement (LLIE) methods are designed for offline processing and are therefore impractical to integrate into live control loops.

摘要：低光照条件会严重降低机器人的感知能力：在日光图像上训练的目标检测器在超出其训练分布的黑暗和恶劣天气条件下容易失效。此外，从效率的角度来看，现有的低光图像增强（LLIE）方法是为离线处理而设计的，因此无法实际集成到实时控制回路中。

To address these issues, we propose to embed a Generative Neural Sensor within the control loop that treats illumination as a generative prior rather than a signal processing problem, enabling task-relevant semantic recovery. We further propose an Real-Time Perceptual Architecture that decouples high-latency semantic recovery from low-latency robotic control.

为了解决这些问题，我们建议在控制回路中嵌入一个生成式神经传感器，将光照视为一种生成先验而非信号处理问题，从而实现与任务相关的语义恢复。我们进一步提出了一种实时感知架构，将高延迟的语义恢复与低延迟的机器人控制解耦。

Rather than blocking the control loop, our system runs a large-scale image-to-image diffusion model in a background thread, and is activated only when a drop in detection confidence signals a perceptual uncertainty event, significantly reducing computational overhead.

我们的系统不会阻塞控制回路，而是在后台线程中运行大规模的图像到图像扩散模型，并且仅在检测置信度下降发出感知不确定性事件信号时才被激活，从而显著降低了计算开销。

Our method sets a new state-of-the-art performance on the ExDark low-light benchmark with 87.1% mAP, and under adverse weather conditions, significantly boosts detection rates over competing methods. On a navigation task in a closed-loop autonomous driving simulator (CARLA), we achieve a 100% lap completion rate, establishing a simulation-based proof-of-concept for integrating high-latency generative models with standard robotic control autonomy stacks.

我们的方法在 ExDark 低光基准测试中创下了 87.1% mAP 的最新性能记录，并且在恶劣天气条件下，其检测率显著高于竞争方法。在闭环自动驾驶模拟器（CARLA）的导航任务中，我们实现了 100% 的单圈完成率，为将高延迟生成模型与标准机器人控制自主堆栈集成提供了基于模拟的概念验证。
