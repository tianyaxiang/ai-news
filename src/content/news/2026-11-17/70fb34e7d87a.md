Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.17686 (cs) [Submitted on 13 Nov 2026] Title: What Do Multilingual Self-Supervised Speech Encoders Know About Sentence Meaning? Authors: Kevin Krahn, Eric Fosler-Lussier.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.17686 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 13 日] 标题：多语言自监督语音编码器对句子含义了解多少？作者：Kevin Krahn, Eric Fosler-Lussier。

Abstract: Self-supervised speech models (S3Ms) have been scaled up to thousands of languages and millions of hours of audio, yet little is known about how these models structure semantic knowledge across languages. In this work, we probe sentence-level semantics in massively multilingual S3Ms across 76 languages, using linear maps from frozen speech representations into a frozen text embedding space, evaluated by cross-modal retrieval.

摘要：自监督语音模型 (S3Ms) 已扩展至数千种语言和数百万小时的音频，但人们对这些模型如何跨语言构建语义知识知之甚少。在这项工作中，我们通过跨模态检索评估，使用从冻结语音表示到冻结文本嵌入空间的线性映射，探测了 76 种语言中大规模多语言 S3M 的句子级语义。

Sentence meaning is linearly accessible but highly uneven across languages and resource tiers. Cross-lingual probes can match language-specific probes on languages never seen in probe training, but per-model monolingual decodability does not predict transfer.

句子含义在语言和资源层级之间是线性可访问的，但分布极不均匀。跨语言探测可以在探测训练中从未见过的语言上匹配特定于语言的探测，但每个模型的单语可解码性并不能预测迁移效果。

Within the OmniASR wav2vec family, scaling from 300M--7B disproportionately benefits high-resource languages, and ASR tuning sharply improves semantic decodability while making representations more language-specific. However, semantic content is entangled with acoustics, inflating apparent performance on single-speaker corpora.

在 OmniASR wav2vec 系列中，从 300M 扩展到 7B 对高资源语言的收益不成比例，且 ASR 微调在显著提高语义可解码性的同时，使表示更具语言特异性。然而，语义内容与声学特征相互纠缠，夸大了在单说话人语料库上的表现。
