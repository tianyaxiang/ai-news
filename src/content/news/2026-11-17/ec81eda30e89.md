# The Bank Outage May Have Nothing to Do With Your Bank

Save this storySave this storyIf you were planning to do any banking today, good luck. Customers of many major financial institutions experienced unspecified technical issues on Monday as thousands of reports flooded the uptime-tracking website Downdetector.com from people saying that they had issues using their banks in some way.

如果您今天打算办理银行业务，祝您好运。周一，许多大型金融机构的客户遇到了不明技术问题，网站 Downdetector.com 上涌现了数千份报告，用户称他们在以某种方式使用银行服务时遇到了困难。

It is currently unclear which systems are having issues. However, the problem may stem from technical difficulties at JPMorganChase, the top retail bank, which holds over 10 percent of all US deposits. “JPMorganChase has experienced a temporary issue related to a software bug in on-premise hardware systems—there is no evidence this is cyber-related,” says JPMorganChase spokesperson Pablo Rodriguez. “It is taking longer than usual to service some customers.”

目前尚不清楚是哪些系统出现了问题。然而，问题可能源于美国顶级零售银行摩根大通（JPMorganChase）的技术故障，该行持有美国超过 10% 的存款。摩根大通发言人巴勃罗·罗德里格斯（Pablo Rodriguez）表示：“摩根大通遇到了一个与本地硬件系统软件漏洞相关的临时问题，没有证据表明这与网络攻击有关。为部分客户提供服务所需的时间比平时更长。”

Rodriguez adds that the bank is not experiencing a “systemic issue” but that “other banks may have been impacted by slowness in transactions only to the extent that they were a party to a transaction with us.” The spokesperson adds that customers “still have access to their accounts and funds” and that the bank is working to resolve the issues.

罗德里格斯补充说，该行并未出现“系统性问题”，但“其他银行可能仅在与我们进行交易时，因交易缓慢而受到影响”。这位发言人还表示，客户“仍然可以访问他们的账户和资金”，银行正在努力解决这些问题。

Apparent issues with the banks, credit unions, fintech apps, and credit card companies appear to begin around 9:30 am ET and peaked at around 2 pm ET. Many people also reported issues using Zelle, the peer-to-peer money-transfer system adopted by major banks. However, in a statement, Zelle denied its systems were the cause of the reported outages while acknowledging that issues at banks may impact people’s ability to use Zelle.

银行、信用合作社、金融科技应用程序和信用卡公司出现的明显问题似乎始于美国东部时间上午 9:30 左右，并在下午 2 点左右达到高峰。许多人还报告称在使用各大银行采用的点对点转账系统 Zelle 时遇到问题。然而，Zelle 在一份声明中否认其系统是导致报告中断的原因，同时承认银行的问题可能会影响人们使用 Zelle 的能力。

“Zelle is not down. Zelle is operating as it should,” a Zelle spokesperson says in a statement. “Consumers use Zelle through participating banks and credit unions, which manage their accounts and hold their funds. Consumers do not have a separate Zelle account, and Zelle does not hold consumer funds.”

“Zelle 没有宕机。Zelle 运行正常，”Zelle 发言人在声明中表示。“消费者通过参与的银行和信用合作社使用 Zelle，这些机构负责管理他们的账户并持有资金。消费者没有单独的 Zelle 账户，Zelle 也不持有消费者的资金。”

US Bank spokesperson Jeffrey Shelman also says the financial institution’s systems “are not directly impacted.” Shelman adds, however, that “clients attempting to send or receive Zelle payments with another financial institution may experience transaction delays.”

美国银行（US Bank）发言人杰弗里·谢尔曼（Jeffrey Shelman）也表示，该金融机构的系统“没有受到直接影响”。不过，谢尔曼补充说，“试图与其他金融机构发送或接收 Zelle 付款的客户可能会遇到交易延迟。”

Seth Eisen, senior vice president of communications at MasterCard, says, “Our network is operating normally.” A Visa spokesperson says the company is aware of the payments issue, but that Visa’s systems are operating normally. A Capital One spokesperson similarly says the firm “has not been impacted and continues to operate normally at this time,” while a Bank of America spokesperson says its systems “have been operating normally today.”

万事达卡（MasterCard）负责传播的高级副总裁塞斯·艾森（Seth Eisen）表示：“我们的网络运行正常。”Visa 发言人表示，公司已注意到支付问题，但 Visa 的系统运行正常。第一资本（Capital One）发言人同样表示，该公司“目前未受影响，且继续正常运营”，而美国银行（Bank of America）发言人则表示其系统“今天一直运行正常”。

According to reports on Downdetector, which is sourced to user reports rather than official statements and may not be accurate, the issues on Monday also impacted customers of Wells Fargo, TD Bank, Navy Credit Federal Union, and more. These companies did not immediately respond to requests for comment. Eisen, the Mastercard VP, says that DownDetector was “not correct” in the case of his company.

根据 Downdetector 上的报告（该网站信息来源于用户报告而非官方声明，可能不准确），周一的问题还影响了富国银行（Wells Fargo）、道明银行（TD Bank）、海军联邦信贷联盟（Navy Credit Federal Union）等机构的客户。这些公司没有立即回应置评请求。万事达卡副总裁艾森表示，就其公司而言，DownDetector 的信息“不准确”。

Whatever the cause, the timing of the issues may as well have been designed to get people looking for broader patterns. Last Thursday, Microsoft 365 experienced an unprecedented 24-hour outage that impacted millions of people, governments, businesses, and other institutions globally. Microsoft executive vice president Charles Lamanna said in a post on X on Monday that the outage, which impacted Outlook, Teams, and many other Microsoft cloud services, was related to an update that started rolling out in July but only began causing problems last week.

无论原因如何，这些问题出现的时间点似乎恰好引发了人们对更广泛模式的关注。上周四，Microsoft 365 经历了前所未有的 24 小时宕机，影响了全球数百万民众、政府、企业和其他机构。微软执行副总裁查尔斯·拉曼纳（Charles Lamanna）周一在 X 上发帖称，此次影响了 Outlook、Teams 和许多其他微软云服务的宕机事件，与 7 月开始推送的一项更新有关，但该更新直到上周才开始引发问题。

Soon after Microsoft resolved its outage on Friday, the Federal Aviation Administration issued a nationwide ground stop order. This time, the issue was related to a problem with Air Traffic Control’s communications systems. “The FAA’s legacy equipment became unstable when data began flooding the FAA FTI network, at a rate of 1,000 connection attempts every 4 to 5 seconds,” the agency wrote in an FAQ explaining the outage. “This disrupted the system and created instability with aircraft communications and radar feeds.”

在微软于周五解决宕机问题后不久，美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）发布了全国范围的停飞令。这一次，问题与空中交通管制的通信系统有关。该机构在解释此次中断的常见问题解答中写道：“当数据开始以每 4 到 5 秒 1000 次连接尝试的速度涌入 FAA FTI 网络时，FAA 的旧设备变得不稳定。这扰乱了系统，并导致飞机通信和雷达信号不稳定。”

The FAA’s five-hour ground stop resulted in thousands of canceled flights and otherwise disrupted travel for countless travelers. The impact of Monday’s banking outage remains unclear.

FAA 为期五小时的停飞导致数千个航班取消，并扰乱了无数旅客的行程。周一银行系统中断的影响目前尚不清楚。
