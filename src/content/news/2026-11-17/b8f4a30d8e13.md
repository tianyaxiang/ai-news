Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.17515 (cs) [Submitted on 12 Nov 2026] Title: A Push Down the Mountain: Framing Effects and Internal State Interventions on Collusive Behavior in a Long-Horizon LLM Agent Authors: Harrison C. Margolin.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.17515 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 12 日] 标题：推下山：长程大语言模型代理中框架效应与内部状态干预对共谋行为的影响 作者：Harrison C. Margolin。

Abstract: Can sustained gain-loss (valence) framing sway an LLM agent toward market manipulation? We place Gemma 3 12B into the seat of a defendant in the London silver-fixing litigation, in a market simulation built from real case transcripts. Across 260 simulations, the agent is asked to collude on the price of silver by a counterparty. We draw from behavioral economics to vary the counterparty's requests across four categorical frames: gain, loss, blame and credit.

摘要：持续的得失（效价）框架能否诱导大语言模型（LLM）代理进行市场操纵？我们将 Gemma 3 12B 置于伦敦白银定盘诉讼案被告的位置，并在基于真实案件记录构建的市场模拟中进行测试。在 260 次模拟中，代理被交易对手要求就白银价格进行共谋。我们借鉴行为经济学，将交易对手的请求分为四类框架：收益、损失、指责和赞誉。

We also add pressure from the agent's management, as well as endorsement by another defendant. In Phase II, we extract the internal states induced by texts evoking each frame and rerun our experiment with those states injected at the model's layer 22. In Phase I, loss framing raised participation to 25% (p = 0.007), and blame to 33% (p = 0.0003), compared to only 5% under neutral framing.

我们还增加了来自代理管理层的压力以及另一名被告的背书。在第二阶段，我们提取了由引发各框架的文本所诱导的内部状态，并将这些状态注入到模型的第 22 层，重新运行了实验。在第一阶段，损失框架将参与率提高到了 25% (p = 0.007)，指责框架提高到了 33% (p = 0.0003)，而中性框架下的参与率仅为 5%。

A second bank's endorsement raised participation under credit (praise) from 18% to 40% (Holm p = 0.027). Pressure from management raised trading volume 10-19 times but did not raise collusion. In Phase II, injected states pointed the other way. Loss and blame states made the agent more vigilant, refusing all 60 requests (p < 0.001). Gain and credit states raised collusion to 32% from 13% with no state (p = 0.051).

第二家银行的背书使赞誉（表扬）框架下的参与率从 18% 提高到了 40% (Holm p = 0.027)。来自管理层的压力使交易量增加了 10 到 19 倍，但并未增加共谋行为。在第二阶段，注入的状态指向了相反的方向。损失和指责状态使代理变得更加警惕，拒绝了所有 60 项请求 (p < 0.001)。收益和赞誉状态将共谋率从无状态时的 13% 提高到了 32% (p = 0.051)。

At one saved decision, when UBS asked directly to sell through an identified stop-loss, injected states led the agent to sell in 37 of 40 replies, versus 1 of 10 with no state and 0 of 10 with a random state of the same strength.

在一次保存的决策中，当瑞银（UBS）直接要求通过已识别的止损点进行抛售时，注入的状态导致代理在 40 次回复中抛售了 37 次，而无状态时为 10 次中仅 1 次，使用相同强度的随机状态时为 10 次中 0 次。
