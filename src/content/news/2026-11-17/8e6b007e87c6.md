Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.17671 (cs) [Submitted on 13 Nov 2026] Title: Provable Cross-Modal Transfer of Hierarchical Concept Structure in CLIP Embeddings Authors: Nghia Nguyen, Tianjiao Ding, Lachlan Ewen MacDonald, René Vidal.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.17671 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 13 日] 标题：CLIP 嵌入中层级概念结构的可证明跨模态迁移 作者：Nghia Nguyen, Tianjiao Ding, Lachlan Ewen MacDonald, René Vidal。

Abstract: Contrastive vision-language models such as CLIP are trained to align images and text, not to represent taxonomies. Yet empirical studies show that CLIP embeddings exhibit hierarchical structure aligned with the hierarchy of semantic concepts. A theoretical characterization of why such hierarchy emerges from a non-hierarchical objective remains open.

摘要：像 CLIP 这样的对比视觉-语言模型经过训练旨在对齐图像和文本，而非表示分类法。然而，实证研究表明，CLIP 嵌入表现出与语义概念层级相一致的层级结构。关于为何这种层级结构会从非层级目标中涌现，其理论表征仍是一个未解之谜。

In this paper, we formalize this phenomenon as Hierarchical Angular Separation (HAS), a geometric property whereby each non-leaf concept embedding is more similar to its descendants than to off-branch concepts. Empirically, we show that HAS holds approximately in raw images and randomly initialized CLIP image embeddings, while in text embeddings it holds approximately only after contrastive learning.

在本文中，我们将这一现象形式化为层级角度分离（HAS），这是一种几何属性，即每个非叶概念嵌入与其后代概念的相似度高于其与分支外概念的相似度。实证结果表明，HAS 在原始图像和随机初始化的 CLIP 图像嵌入中近似成立，而在文本嵌入中，仅在对比学习之后才近似成立。

To theoretically explain this asymmetry, we analyze an unconstrained features model in which the image embeddings are fixed and only the text embeddings are trained. At the global minimizer, the text-side HAS violation percentage at each node is at most the image-side HAS violation and near-violation percentage. The key mechanism is the hierarchical co-occurrence structure of image-text pairs, which biases text embeddings toward root-centered image prototypes.

为了从理论上解释这种不对称性，我们分析了一个无约束特征模型，其中图像嵌入是固定的，仅训练文本嵌入。在全局极小值处，每个节点处的文本侧 HAS 违规百分比最多等于图像侧的 HAS 违规和近违规百分比。其关键机制是图像-文本对的层级共现结构，它使文本嵌入偏向于以根节点为中心的图像原型。

For a linear contrastive loss, we derive a closed-form solution that makes this mechanism explicit. We extend the analysis to the InfoNCE loss and establish analogous guarantees under sufficient regularization. Synthetic experiments validate the theory and show that HAS is transferred even beyond the regimes covered by our analysis.

对于线性对比损失，我们推导出了一个使该机制明确的闭式解。我们将分析扩展到 InfoNCE 损失，并在充分正则化下建立了类似的保证。合成实验验证了该理论，并表明 HAS 甚至在超出我们分析范围的情况下也能实现迁移。
