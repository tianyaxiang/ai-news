# What's Next for Nvidia?

Nvidia’s board announced that Jensen Huang had resigned 33 years after he co-founded the company.

英伟达董事会宣布，黄仁勋在联合创立公司 33 年后已经辞职。

What happens to a company when a visionary CEO is gone? Most often innovation dies and the company coasts for years on momentum and its brand. Rarely does it regain its former glory.

当一位富有远见的首席执行官离职后，公司会发生什么？通常情况下，创新会随之消亡，公司仅靠惯性和品牌影响力维持多年。它很少能重现往日的辉煌。

That’s the danger facing Nvidia. Here’s what their board should be considering.

这就是英伟达面临的危险。以下是其董事会应该考虑的问题。

Jensen Huang reinvented Nvidia three times. In 2006 he launched CUDA, turning a graphics chip for PC gamers into a general-purpose computing platform – and funded it for years ahead of any revenue. In 2012, when researchers used Nvidia chips to win the ImageNet contest, he pointed the company to deep learning. In 2020, with the acquisition of Mellanox, Nvidia went from selling boards with GPUs to selling integrated racks that power AI datacenters.

黄仁勋曾三次重塑英伟达。2006 年，他推出了 CUDA，将一款面向 PC 游戏玩家的图形芯片转变为通用计算平台，并在产生任何收入之前为其提供了多年的资金支持。2012 年，当研究人员利用英伟达芯片赢得 ImageNet 竞赛时，他将公司引向了深度学习领域。2020 年，随着对 Mellanox 的收购，英伟达从销售带有 GPU 的电路板转型为销售驱动人工智能数据中心的集成机架。

These were not just product transitions, but radical business model transitions; new channels, new customers and new markets. Nvidia pivoted from a vendor of graphics boards, to a software platform, and then to systems company. It’s hard to imagine a CEO focused on executing an existing business model funding CUDA through those years.

这些不仅仅是产品转型，更是彻底的商业模式转型；涉及新的渠道、新的客户和新的市场。英伟达从图形板卡供应商转型为软件平台，进而转型为系统公司。很难想象一位专注于执行现有商业模式的首席执行官会在那些年里为 CUDA 提供资金。

Steve Jobs did the same at Apple, transforming it from a niche computer company into the most profitable company in the world. Between 2001 and 2008, Jobs reinvented the company three times.

史蒂夫·乔布斯在苹果公司也做了同样的事情，将其从一家小众电脑公司转变为世界上最赚钱的公司。在 2001 年至 2008 年间，乔布斯三次重塑了苹果公司。

Visionary CEOs are not “just” great at assuring world-class execution of a successful business model, they are also world-class innovators.

富有远见的首席执行官不仅擅长确保成功商业模式的世界级执行，他们本身也是世界级的创新者。

The best are agile and know how to pivot, making substantive changes to their business model while or before their market has shifted. The very best of them shape markets – they know how to create new markets by seeing opportunities before anyone else. They remain entrepreneurs.

最优秀的领导者具备敏捷性，懂得如何转型，在市场发生变化时或之前对商业模式进行实质性调整。其中最顶尖的人能够塑造市场——他们懂得如何在他人发现机会之前就看到机会，从而创造出新的市场。他们始终保持着创业者的精神。

Visionary CEOs don’t need someone else to demo the company’s key products for them. They deeply understand products, and they have their own coherent and consistent vision of where the industry/business models and customers are today, and where they need to take the company. They know who their customers are because they spend time talking to them. They use strategy committees and the exec staff for advice, but none of these CEOs pivot by committee.

富有远见的首席执行官不需要别人为他们演示公司的关键产品。他们深入了解产品，并对行业、商业模式和客户的现状以及公司未来的发展方向有着连贯且一致的愿景。他们知道自己的客户是谁，因为他们会花时间与客户交流。他们会利用战略委员会和高管团队提供建议，但这些首席执行官绝不会通过委员会来决定转型。

Microsoft and Apple are two examples of what happens when a visionary CEO is replaced by an execution CEO.

微软和苹果是当富有远见的首席执行官被执行型首席执行官取代后会发生什么的两个例子。

Microsoft and the Cost of Missing the Next Market
Microsoft entered the 21st century as the dominant software provider for anyone who interacted with a computing device. After running Microsoft for 25 years, Bill Gates handed the reins of Microsoft to Steve Ballmer in January 2000. Ballmer went on to run Microsoft for the next 14 years.

微软与错失下一个市场的代价
21 世纪初，微软是所有使用计算设备的人群中占据主导地位的软件提供商。在执掌微软 25 年后，比尔·盖茨于 2000 年 1 月将微软的帅印交给了史蒂夫·鲍尔默。鲍尔默随后执掌微软长达 14 年。

Ballmer organized the company around execution of its then current strengths – Windows and Office businesses. He tripled Microsoft’s sales to $78 billion and profits more than doubled from $9 billion to $22 billion, but those results hid missed opportunities that would reshape computing.

鲍尔默围绕公司当时的优势——Windows 和 Office 业务——来组织运营。他将微软的销售额翻了三倍，达到 780 亿美元，利润也从 90 亿美元翻了一倍多，达到 220 亿美元，但这些业绩掩盖了那些本可以重塑计算领域的错失机会。

Ballmer failed to understand and execute on the five most important technology trends of the 21st century: in search – losing to Google; in smartphones – losing to Apple; in mobile operating systems – losing to Google/Apple; in media – losing to Apple/Netflix; and in the cloud – losing to Amazon.

鲍尔默未能理解并执行 21 世纪最重要的五大技术趋势：在搜索领域输给了谷歌；在智能手机领域输给了苹果；在移动操作系统领域输给了谷歌/苹果；在媒体领域输给了苹果/Netflix；在云计算领域输给了亚马逊。

In early 2014, Satya Nadella took charge. Nadella got Microsoft organized around the cloud (Azure), freed the Office and Azure teams from Windows and killed the phone business. Nadella saved Microsoft from irrelevance. Today Microsoft is worth about $4 trillion. It is one of the few companies that regained its former glory, after its board replaced a salesman with a product and engineering insider.

2014 年初，萨提亚·纳德拉接管了公司。纳德拉将微软的组织架构围绕云计算（Azure）进行了重组，将 Office 和 Azure 团队从 Windows 的束缚中解放出来，并砍掉了手机业务。纳德拉将微软从边缘化中拯救了出来。如今，微软的市值约为 4 万亿美元。它是少数几家在董事会将销售型领导换成产品和工程内部人士后，重现往日辉煌的公司之一。

Innovation CEOs Hire Execution VPs
Successful visionary CEOs build an executive staff of world-class operating executives and technical leaders (and they unconsciously force out any world-class innovators from their direct reports). The problem is in a company driven by a visionary CEO; there is only one visionary.

创新型首席执行官聘用执行型副总裁
成功的远见型首席执行官会建立一支由世界级运营高管和技术领袖组成的执行团队（并且他们会下意识地排挤掉直接下属中任何世界级的创新者）。问题在于，在一家由远见型首席执行官驱动的公司里，只能有一位远见者。

Nvidia is the extreme case. Jensen had about 60 direct reports and discouraged one-on-one meetings. No long-term plans. Much like Jobs, he made himself the point where technical, product and business decisions came together. Replacing that role is harder than filling a box on an organization chart. He said he didn’t believe in succession planning.

英伟达就是一个极端的例子。黄仁勋大约有 60 名直接下属，且不鼓励一对一会议。没有长期计划。就像乔布斯一样，他让自己成为了技术、产品和商业决策的交汇点。取代这个角色比在组织结构图上填补一个空位要困难得多。他说他不相信继任计划。

When visionary founders depart (resignation, death, firing, etc.), their operating executives believe it’s their turn to run the company. At Microsoft, Bill Gates chose Steve Ballmer, and at Apple Steve Jobs chose Tim Cook. Jensen anointed no one.

当富有远见的创始人离职（辞职、去世、被解雇等）时，他们的运营高管会认为轮到自己来管理公司了。在微软，比尔·盖茨选择了史蒂夫·鲍尔默；在苹果，史蒂夫·乔布斯选择了蒂姆·库克。而黄仁勋没有指定任何人。

Once in charge, one of the first things these operations/execution CEOs do is to get rid of the chaos and turbulence in the organization. Execution CEOs value stability, process and repeatable execution. On one hand that’s great for predictability, but it often starts a creative death spiral – creative people start to leave, and other executors (without the innovation talent of the old leader) are put into more senior roles – hiring more process people, which in turn forces out the remaining creative talent. This culture shift ripples down from the top and what once felt like a company on a mission to change the world now feels like another job.

一旦掌权，这些运营/执行型首席执行官做的第一件事通常就是消除组织内的混乱和动荡。执行型首席执行官重视稳定性、流程和可重复的执行力。一方面，这对可预测性很有好处，但它往往会引发创造力的死亡螺旋——有创造力的人开始离开，而其他执行者（缺乏老领导的创新天赋）被提拔到更高级的职位，雇佣更多流程型人才，这反过来又迫使剩余的创造性人才离开。这种文化转变从高层向下蔓延，曾经感觉像是一家肩负改变世界使命的公司，现在感觉就像一份普通的工作。

(Amazon is a counterexample. Before succeeding Jeff Bezos, Andy Jassy had already founded and led AWS. He was an innovator who had built a new business inside the existing company.)

（亚马逊是一个反例。在接替杰夫·贝索斯之前，安迪·贾西已经创立并领导了 AWS。他是一位在现有公司内部建立起新业务的创新者。）

At Nvidia this step isn’t optional. No one else can run a company with 60 direct reports, so the next CEO’s first move will be to add layers of management.

在英伟达，这一步是不可避免的。没有其他人能管理拥有 60 名直接下属的公司，因此下一任首席执行官的第一步举措将是增加管理层级。
