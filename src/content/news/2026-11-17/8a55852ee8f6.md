# United Airlines system outage disrupts departures just days after the big ground stop

# 美联航系统故障导致航班延误，距离上次大规模停飞仅数日之隔

Less than a week after an issue with air traffic control communications and radar caused the FAA to halt takeoffs nationwide, United Airlines departing flights are being disrupted by an outage. At 6:13PM ET, United said the problem has been fixed:

在空中交通管制通信和雷达问题导致美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）在全国范围内暂停起飞不到一周后，美联航的离港航班再次受到系统故障的影响。美联航于美东时间下午 6:13 表示问题已得到解决：

Earlier today we experienced a technology-related issue that impacted some departing flights. The issue has been resolved. Please check the latest status of your flight at united.com or the United app.

今天早些时候，我们遇到了一项影响部分离港航班的技术问题。该问题现已解决。请通过 united.com 或美联航应用程序查看您的最新航班状态。

The outage is also coming after last week’s global Microsoft Outlook shutdown, and the great-bank-outage-that-wasn’t earlier Monday afternoon. According to posts on social media, a number of people onboard the planes have heard from their pilots that a systemwide IT outage for United has caused problems with a weight and balance calculator that has stopped them from getting clearance to take off.

此次故障发生在上周全球微软 Outlook 服务中断以及周一下午早些时候那场“虚惊一场”的银行系统大故障之后。据社交媒体上的帖子显示，多名机上乘客从飞行员处获悉，美联航全系统的 IT 故障导致重量与平衡计算器出现问题，从而无法获得起飞许可。

United tweeted earlier that “We are troubleshooting a technology issue affecting departing flights. There is no impact on flights in progress, or on United Express flights.”

美联航早些时候在推特上表示：“我们正在排查影响离港航班的技术问题。目前飞行中的航班以及美联航快运（United Express）航班未受影响。”

Update, November 16th: Added note that United says the problem has been resolved.

更新，11 月 16 日：补充说明美联航称问题已得到解决。
