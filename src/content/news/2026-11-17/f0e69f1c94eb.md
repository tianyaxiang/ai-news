# New Ask Maps features to help with holiday travel and prep

The holidays are a busy time between shopping for gifts, planning travel, and celebrating with loved ones. To help you stay one step ahead this season, we’re launching new Ask Maps features that make it easy to create itineraries, find available appointments, and search your lists in a snap so you can enjoy stress-free festivities.

假期是购物、规划旅行和与亲友庆祝的繁忙时段。为了帮助您在这个季节快人一步，我们推出了全新的 Ask Maps 功能，让您可以轻松创建行程、查找可用预约并快速搜索您的列表，从而享受无压力的节日时光。

Get on-the-go help with your holiday road trips. Now you can build step-by-step trip itineraries and get helpful, real-time details about your route right when you pull up directions. Use Ask Maps to plan a day trip for holiday shopping or something more complex — like a three-day road trip with stops for coastal hikes and oceanfront dining.

获取节日公路旅行的随行帮助。现在，您可以构建分步旅行行程，并在查看导航时直接获取有关路线的实用实时详情。使用 Ask Maps 规划节日购物的一日游，或更复杂的行程，例如包含沿海徒步和海滨餐饮站点的三日公路旅行。

You'll get a day-by-day schedule with insider tips and numbered map pins for each stop, making it easy to visualize the plan. Once you're happy with the itinerary, share it with friends and family, or export it to Google Docs on desktop. When you’re about to head out, use Ask Maps to pull up your itinerary or look for the “itinerary” badge in your chat history.

您将获得一份包含内部建议和每个站点编号地图图钉的每日日程表，从而轻松直观地查看计划。一旦您对行程满意，可以将其分享给亲友，或导出到桌面端的 Google Docs。准备出发时，使用 Ask Maps 调出您的行程，或在聊天记录中查找“行程”徽章。

Check off your holiday prep and let Maps find appointments for you. Holiday prep comes with a long list of to-dos. Now, with new agentic capabilities, you can choose to have Ask Maps find beauty, pet care, auto and home repair appointments online and call local businesses for you. Just ask, “Find me a 4 p.m. nail appointment near me.”

完成您的节日准备工作，让地图为您寻找预约。节日准备伴随着长长的待办事项清单。现在，借助新的代理功能，您可以选择让 Ask Maps 在线查找美容、宠物护理、汽车和房屋维修预约，并为您致电当地商家。只需询问：“帮我找一个下午 4 点附近的美甲预约。”

Show your guests the best spots on your lists. If you have friends visiting and want to show them around, now you can use Ask Maps to find saved places on your lists in seconds. Just ask, “Which spots saved in my lists have availability for five people in the next 30 minutes?” Ask Maps looks across your lists and checks live availability so you can pick a place and go.

向客人展示您列表中的最佳地点。如果有朋友来访并想带他们四处逛逛，现在您可以使用 Ask Maps 在几秒钟内找到列表中保存的地点。只需询问：“我列表中保存的哪些地点在未来 30 分钟内有适合五人的空位？”Ask Maps 会查看您的列表并检查实时可用性，以便您选择地点并出发。
