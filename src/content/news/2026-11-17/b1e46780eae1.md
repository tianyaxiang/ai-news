Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.17462 (cs) [Submitted on 12 Nov 2026] Title: Diffusion Tree Sampling for Inference Time Adaptation of Crystal Generative Models Authors: Daniel T. Levy, Vineet Jain, Tara Akhound-Sadegh, Sékou-Oumar Kaba, Siamak Ravanbakhsh.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.17462 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 12 日] 标题：用于晶体生成模型推理时间适应的扩散树采样 作者：Daniel T. Levy, Vineet Jain, Tara Akhound-Sadegh, Sékou-Oumar Kaba, Siamak Ravanbakhsh。

Abstract: Diffusion-based generative models have become strong priors over plausible crystal structures, but turning that prior into promising candidates requires steering toward several conflicting properties at once. Fine-tuning is expensive and produces a new model for every new objective, while differentiable guidance is not applicable to many real-world tasks.

摘要：基于扩散的生成模型已成为合理晶体结构的强先验，但要将该先验转化为有前景的候选结构，需要同时引导多个相互冲突的属性。微调成本高昂，且针对每个新目标都需要产生一个新模型，而可微引导并不适用于许多现实世界的任务。

We instead adapt these generative models at inference time using Diffusion Tree Sampling (DTS), a Monte Carlo tree search procedure over the denoising trajectory. We make two technical contributions on top of DTS that are particularly relevant for AI-for-science applications.

我们转而使用扩散树采样（DTS）在推理时对这些生成模型进行适应，这是一种针对去噪轨迹的蒙特卡洛树搜索过程。我们在 DTS 的基础上做出了两项对科学人工智能应用尤为重要的技术贡献。

First, we show how to use existing property-conditional generative models (e.g., models fine-tuned for classifier-free guidance) as guided proposals inside DTS, without distorting the target distribution. Second, we extend DTS to multi-objective sampling, so that a single search produces samples spanning an entire Pareto front.

首先，我们展示了如何将现有的属性条件生成模型（例如，针对无分类器引导进行微调的模型）用作 DTS 内部的引导建议，且不会扭曲目标分布。其次，我们将 DTS 扩展到多目标采样，使得单次搜索即可产生跨越整个帕累托前沿的样本。

Across these tasks, DTS-based inference-time adaptation successfully improves model outputs without modifying the models themselves.

在这些任务中，基于 DTS 的推理时间适应成功地改善了模型输出，而无需修改模型本身。
