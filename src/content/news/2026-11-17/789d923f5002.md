Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.17338 (cs) [Submitted on 30 Aug 2026] Title: When Should LLM Agents Forget? Memory Filtering Under Query-Critical Resolver Errors Authors: Samarth Vinayaka.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.17338 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 8 月 30 日] 标题：LLM 智能体何时应该遗忘？查询关键解析器错误下的记忆过滤 作者：Samarth Vinayaka。

Abstract: LLM memory systems often delete facts classified as stale. We show that this hard-filtering step can reduce downstream accuracy when resolver errors delete query-critical facts. Across three models, a controlled sensitivity intervention moves hard-drop from gains of +0.186 to +0.230 at perfect resolution to losses of -0.176 to -0.181 at the lowest tested reliability.

摘要：大语言模型（LLM）的记忆系统通常会删除被归类为陈旧的事实。我们证明，当解析器错误删除了查询关键事实时，这种硬过滤步骤会降低下游任务的准确性。在三个模型中，一项受控敏感度干预显示，在完美解析的情况下，硬删除带来的增益为 +0.186 到 +0.230，而在测试的最低可靠性下，则会导致 -0.176 到 -0.181 的损失。

Paired targeted interventions show that false-drop causes significantly greater degradation than false-keep, while a non-deleting baseline can still fail when incorrect labels steer generation. On a frozen 102/102 calibration/test split, a simple deployment-matched gate reaches 0.655 accuracy versus 0.641 for always hard-drop and 0.662 for test-informed oracle selection.

配对的目标干预显示，错误删除（false-drop）造成的性能下降明显大于错误保留（false-keep），而不删除的基准线在错误标签引导生成时仍可能失败。在固定的 102/102 校准/测试集划分上，一个简单的部署匹配门控达到了 0.655 的准确率，相比之下，始终硬删除的准确率为 0.641，而基于测试信息的预言机选择准确率为 0.662。

Global-accuracy crossovers require gold labels and are model- and task-specific diagnostics, not deployment thresholds. Deletion should instead be treated as cost-sensitive: validate query-critical false-drop risk on matched data, or default to label-free retention when such validation is unavailable.

全局准确率交叉点需要金标准标签，且属于模型和任务特定的诊断指标，而非部署阈值。删除操作应被视为成本敏感型：在匹配数据上验证查询关键的错误删除风险，或者在无法进行此类验证时，默认采用无标签保留策略。
