Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.17398 (cs) [Submitted on 12 Nov 2026] Title: Alignment with Reflect-Reasoning: Learning How To Follow Principles Authors: Henry Bell, Caroline Zhang, Dhaval Potdar, Mohammed Mobasserul Haque, Brandon Fain.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.17398 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 12 日] 标题：基于反射推理的对齐：学习如何遵循原则 作者：Henry Bell, Caroline Zhang, Dhaval Potdar, Mohammed Mobasserul Haque, Brandon Fain。

Abstract: The appropriate governing norms and principles for the alignment of Large Language Models (LLMs) often depend on cultural context and deployment application. However, most alignment techniques focus on training models to conform to human preferences or constitutional principles specified at training time and subsequently fixed. In practice, many deployers rely on system prompting to implement contextual norms and guardrails at inference time.

摘要：用于大语言模型（LLM）对齐的适当管理规范和原则通常取决于文化背景和部署应用。然而，大多数对齐技术侧重于训练模型以符合在训练时指定并随后固定的个人偏好或宪法原则。在实践中，许多部署者依赖系统提示词在推理时实施上下文规范和护栏。

Prior work has shown that post-generation reflective reasoning with critique and revision can improve conformance compared to simple system prompting, but such methods are limited to the reasoning abilities inherent in the trained model. We introduce Reflect-Reasoning, a training method that teaches models how to reason about alignment to principles, rather than simply which principles to follow.

先前的研究表明，与简单的系统提示词相比，带有批判和修订的生成后反射推理可以提高一致性，但此类方法受限于训练模型固有的推理能力。我们引入了“反射推理”（Reflect-Reasoning），这是一种训练方法，它教导模型如何推理原则的对齐，而不仅仅是遵循哪些原则。

Across five models, Reflect-Reasoning consistently outperforms both system prompting and explicit prompting strategies for in-context reflective reasoning. These improvements extend and generalize to entirely out-of-distribution domains of medical and financial principles. We additionally demonstrate that Reflect-Reasoning reduces the occurrence of rare but serious principle violations.

在五个模型中，反射推理在上下文反射推理方面始终优于系统提示词和显式提示策略。这些改进扩展并推广到了医疗和金融原则的完全分布外领域。我们还证明，反射推理减少了罕见但严重的原则违规行为的发生。

Finally, we show that Reflect-Reasoning does not erode the safety engineering of a model and is robust against jailbreaking attempts using adversarial constitutions. Overall, our results demonstrate that Reflect-Reasoning is a scalable method for training constitutional alignment as a generalizable reasoning capability, enabling models to adapt to novel principles and produce transparent reasoning traces without additional fine-tuning.

最后，我们展示了反射推理不会削弱模型的安全工程，并且对于使用对抗性宪法的越狱尝试具有鲁棒性。总的来说，我们的结果表明，反射推理是一种可扩展的方法，用于将宪法对齐训练为一种可泛化的推理能力，使模型能够适应新颖的原则，并在无需额外微调的情况下生成透明的推理轨迹。
