Title: Poetry 'resurrection' from LLMs

标题：大语言模型带来的诗歌“复活”

Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.17464 (cs) [Submitted on 12 Nov 2026] Title:Poetry 'resurrection' from LLMs Authors:Vincent Cellucci, Peter van der Putten View a PDF of the paper titled Poetry 'resurrection' from LLMs, by Vincent Cellucci and 1 other authors View PDF Abstract:In an era where linguistic creativity is challenged by algorithmic authorship, we pose a provocative question: can artificial intelligence (AI) write an infinite poem?

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.17464 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 12 日] 标题：大语言模型带来的诗歌“复活” 作者：Vincent Cellucci, Peter van der Putten 查看 Vincent Cellucci 及另一位作者撰写的论文《大语言模型带来的诗歌“复活”》的 PDF 版本。摘要：在一个语言创造力受到算法创作挑战的时代，我们提出了一个发人深省的问题：人工智能（AI）能写出一首无限的诗吗？

We set up two experiments to investigate the quantitative and qualitative output produced by AI utilizing couplets that evidence a poetic relationship between end words, the only variation in a strict anaphoric, litany structure. Our results show that while AI can generate interesting pairings, it still falls short of human-level creativity and understanding of poetic relationships.

我们进行了两项实验，旨在调查人工智能在利用对句（couplets）时产生的定量和定性输出，这些对句在严格的重复句首（anaphoric）和连祷（litany）结构中，仅在末尾词之间体现出诗意的关联。我们的结果表明，尽管人工智能可以生成有趣的搭配，但它在人类水平的创造力和对诗意关系的理解方面仍有欠缺。

We offer commentary and a brief survey of the field of computational poetry experiments, tracing back authorial intention and distinctions of 'natural' and 'artificial' poetry, as well as justifying why couplet form is suited to testing AI's poetic output. Experiment 1 relies on manual prompting and co-creation to extend the original poem, which is developed into an animated webpage installation and interactive artwork.

我们对计算诗歌实验领域进行了评述和简要调查，追溯了作者意图以及“自然”诗歌与“人工”诗歌的区别，并论证了为何对句形式适合测试人工智能的诗歌输出。实验 1 依赖于手动提示和共同创作来扩展原始诗歌，并将其开发为动画网页装置和交互式艺术作品。

Experiment 2 reliably achieves automated output reaching the 1000 benchmark via an alternative method of using an LLM in combination with semantic similarity, of which Method 2 demonstrates a higher capacity for semantic and quantitative poetic output. These experiments highlight the limitations of current LLMs' generation capacity at the word level but also suggest that poetry will also respond and revive by incorporating AI workflows.

实验 2 通过结合大语言模型与语义相似度的替代方法，可靠地实现了达到 1000 行基准的自动化输出，其中方法 2 在语义和定量诗歌输出方面表现出更高的能力。这些实验突显了当前大语言模型在词汇层面的生成能力局限，但也表明诗歌将通过整合人工智能工作流程而做出回应并实现复兴。
