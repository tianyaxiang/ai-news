Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.17754 (cs) [Submitted on 13 Nov 2026] Title: See Every Block Once: Variance-Reduced Post-Training for Masked Diffusion Language Models Authors: Yunzhen He, Hiroaki Yamagiwa, Hidetoshi Shimodaira.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.17754 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 13 日] 标题：See Every Block Once: Variance-Reduced Post-Training for Masked Diffusion Language Models（每个区块仅评估一次：掩码扩散语言模型的方差缩减后训练） 作者：Yunzhen He, Hiroaki Yamagiwa, Hidetoshi Shimodaira。

Abstract: Policy gradient methods are widely used to align large language model outputs with human preferences, but the log-likelihoods they require cannot be directly computed in Masked Diffusion Language Models (MDLMs). MDLM post-training therefore relies on a surrogate objective based on the Evidence Lower Bound (ELBO), and recent work estimates a blockwise variant of this objective via Monte Carlo methods to support blockwise generation.

摘要：策略梯度方法被广泛用于使大语言模型的输出与人类偏好对齐，但掩码扩散语言模型（MDLM）无法直接计算这些方法所需的对数似然。因此，MDLM 的后训练依赖于基于证据下界（ELBO）的代理目标，而近期研究通过蒙特卡洛方法估计该目标的区块化变体，以支持区块化生成。

However, existing estimation schemes randomly sample the block index, so they may evaluate the same block more than once and leave some blocks unevaluated. This introduces variance arising from block selection into the estimates.

然而，现有的估计方案随机采样区块索引，因此它们可能会多次评估同一个区块，并遗漏某些区块。这在估计中引入了由区块选择引起的方差。

In this work, we propose a full-block estimator that evaluates every block exactly once and averages the results. We theoretically show that the proposed estimator remains unbiased while eliminating the between-group variance arising from block selection, thereby achieving lower variance than the baseline for the same sample size.

在这项工作中，我们提出了一种全区块估计器，它对每个区块精确评估一次并对结果取平均值。我们在理论上证明，所提出的估计器在保持无偏性的同时，消除了由区块选择引起的组间方差，从而在相同样本量下实现了比基线更低的方差。

In experiments on five benchmarks spanning mathematics, planning, and coding, our method achieves the highest accuracy on four benchmarks and outperforms the random block sampling baseline by an average of 12.79 points.

在涵盖数学、规划和编码的五个基准测试实验中，我们的方法在四个基准测试中达到了最高准确率，并比随机区块采样基线平均高出 12.79 个百分点。
