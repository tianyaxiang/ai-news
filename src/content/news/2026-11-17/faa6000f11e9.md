Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.17586 (cs) [Submitted on 12 Nov 2026] Title: Where to Predict in Retinal OCT? Anatomy-Guided Target Selection for I-JEPA Authors: Yunchu Feng, Wai Tak Lau, Kaveri A. Thakoor.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.17586 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 12 日] 标题：视网膜 OCT 中应预测何处？I-JEPA 的解剖结构引导目标选择 作者：Yunchu Feng, Wai Tak Lau, Kaveri A. Thakoor。

Abstract: Self-supervised learning aims to learn meaningful representations from unlabeled data, potentially allowing models to capture subtle anatomical variations across patients and reduce the amount of labeled data needed. Image-based Joint-Embedding Predictive Architecture (I-JEPA) learns by predicting target-region representations from visible context rather than reconstructing pixels.

摘要：自监督学习旨在从无标签数据中学习有意义的表征，这可能使模型能够捕捉患者之间细微的解剖学差异，并减少对标注数据的需求。基于图像的联合嵌入预测架构（I-JEPA）通过从可见上下文中预测目标区域的表征来学习，而不是通过重建像素。

However, its uniform target placement does not account for the anatomical structure of retinal OCT, where retinal tissue occupies only part of each B-scan. We investigate anatomy-guided target selection using image intensity and predicted retinal segmentation while retaining the I-JEPA architecture and latent prediction objective.

然而，其统一的目标放置方式并未考虑视网膜 OCT 的解剖结构，在视网膜 OCT 中，视网膜组织仅占据每个 B-scan 的一部分。我们研究了使用图像强度和预测的视网膜分割进行解剖结构引导的目标选择，同时保留了 I-JEPA 架构和潜在预测目标。

We compare anatomy-guided rectangular targets, anatomy-shaped targets and coverage-constrained placement with uniform masking, and evaluate the learned representations using linear probing for glaucoma classification on FairVision. We found that rectangular targets guided by image intensity achieved the highest linear-probe AUC in our comparisons.

我们比较了解剖结构引导的矩形目标、解剖形状目标以及带有统一掩码的覆盖约束放置，并使用线性探测在 FairVision 上对青光眼分类的习得表征进行了评估。我们发现，在我们的比较中，由图像强度引导的矩形目标实现了最高的线性探测 AUC。

We show that anatomically meaningful target selection is promising for retinal OCT representation learning, while greater anatomical specificity did not consistently improve performance.

我们表明，具有解剖学意义的目标选择对于视网膜 OCT 表征学习具有前景，但更高的解剖学特异性并未持续提升性能。
