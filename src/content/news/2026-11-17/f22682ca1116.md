Abstract: Document-parsing metrics can give high scores to outputs that change important facts and penalize changes that preserve meaning. People can often judge these consequences more easily than they can design a metric to measure them. We introduce a method for developing parsing metrics from severity policies that specify how each error type should affect the score.

摘要：文档解析指标可能会给那些改变了重要事实的输出打出高分，反而惩罚那些保留了原意的修改。人们通常比设计衡量这些后果的指标更容易判断这些后果。我们引入了一种从严重性策略开发解析指标的方法，该策略规定了每种错误类型应如何影响分数。

We express these policies through test scenarios that specify how a metric should behave. We then optimize the metric implementations against these fixed expectations, using coding agents to refine comparison rules, error detectors and weights under human review. This makes stated human priorities an explicit objective of metric design.

我们通过指定指标应如何表现的测试场景来表达这些策略。然后，我们在人工审查下，利用编码代理来优化比较规则、错误检测器和权重，从而针对这些既定预期优化指标实现。这使得既定的人类优先级成为指标设计的明确目标。

We apply this method in ParseBench-2, a comprehensive document-parsing benchmark that combines broad capability coverage with metrics calibrated to error severity. It covers 4,317 pages across six dimensions of parsing quality: text, formatting, tables, charts, visual grounding, and forms.

我们将此方法应用于 ParseBench-2，这是一个综合性的文档解析基准测试，它结合了广泛的能力覆盖范围和针对错误严重性校准的指标。它涵盖了 4,317 页内容，涉及解析质量的六个维度：文本、格式、表格、图表、视觉定位和表单。

We use detailed annotations and targeted difficult cases to check for changed facts, broken structural relationships, and missing content. We take the lower of a parsing score that measures content recovery and a severity cap that penalizes detected errors, so recovering most of a page cannot hide a serious error.

我们使用详细的注释和针对性的困难案例来检查事实变更、结构关系破坏和内容缺失。我们取衡量内容恢复的解析分数与惩罚检测到错误的严重性上限两者中的较低者，因此恢复页面大部分内容无法掩盖严重的错误。

Across six severity test suites, our metrics more closely match the expert judgments expressed by the scenario targets than existing metrics and penalize at most 3.4% of the tested benign variations. We conduct a comprehensive evaluation spanning frontier vision-language models, specialized document parsers and open-weight systems, comparing their overall rankings, performance across parsing capabilities and tradeoffs between accuracy and cost.

在六个严重性测试套件中，我们的指标比现有指标更接近场景目标所表达的专家判断，并且最多仅惩罚 3.4% 的受测良性变体。我们进行了全面的评估，涵盖了前沿视觉语言模型、专业文档解析器和开放权重系统，比较了它们的整体排名、跨解析能力的性能以及准确性与成本之间的权衡。
