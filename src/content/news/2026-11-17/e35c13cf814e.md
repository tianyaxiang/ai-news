Tech leaders are convinced that Jensen Huang’s exit was a plot against him.

科技界领袖们确信黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）的离职是一场针对他的阴谋。

David Sacks, Tim Sweeney, and other big names in tech are raising questions about the circumstances surrounding Huang’s resignation.

大卫·萨克斯（David Sacks）、蒂姆·斯威尼（Tim Sweeney）以及其他科技界大腕们，正对黄仁勋辞职背后的情况提出质疑。

Jensen Huang’s stunning resignation from Nvidia after more than 33 years has sent shockwaves throughout the tech industry. Now prominent figures are calling for Huang’s reinstatement, and many are also hopping on board with a theory spreading throughout Silicon Valley: that the scandal is the result of a plot against the former CEO.

黄仁勋在英伟达任职超过33年后突然辞职，在整个科技行业引发了轩然大波。目前，多位知名人士呼吁让黄仁勋复职，许多人也开始认同硅谷流传的一种理论：这场丑闻是针对这位前首席执行官的阴谋。

At the center of this theory is the reporting from The New York Times that former Megaspeed executive Alice Huang, one of multiple women Huang is accused of having an affair with, is a Chinese spy. US agencies haven’t confirmed whether this is the case, and President Donald Trump claims he was unaware of any spy allegations.

这一理论的核心是《纽约时报》的报道，该报道称前Megaspeed高管爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang）——黄仁勋被指控有染的多名女性之一——是一名中国间谍。美国机构尚未证实这一情况，唐纳德·特朗普总统也声称他并不知晓任何有关间谍的指控。

David Sacks, a venture capitalist and former White House AI crypto advisor, has made it clear that he’s skeptical about The New York Times’ reporting. In a post on X, Sacks points to an article from CNN, which says US officials still can’t confirm that Alice Huang was a spy. “The spy narrative is collapsing already,” Sacks writes.

风险投资家、前白宫人工智能加密货币顾问大卫·萨克斯明确表示，他对《纽约时报》的报道持怀疑态度。萨克斯在X上发帖引用了CNN的一篇文章，称美国官员仍无法证实爱丽丝·黄是间谍。萨克斯写道：“间谍叙事已经开始崩塌了。”

Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney similarly expressed concern about how Huang’s resignation played out. In a reply to Sacks’ post about the CNN article, Sweeney said, “What?! So journalists with sketchy motives portrayed rumors from god-knows-who (lobbyists?) as facts sourced from intelligence officials and gaslit the NVIDIA board to push out the founder, CEO, and once-in-generation visionary?”

Epic Games首席执行官蒂姆·斯威尼同样对黄仁勋辞职的处理方式表示担忧。在回复萨克斯关于CNN文章的帖子时，斯威尼说道：“什么？！所以动机可疑的记者们把不知何人（说客？）传出的谣言，描绘成来自情报官员的事实，并对英伟达董事会进行煤气灯效应式的操纵，从而逼走了这位创始人、首席执行官和一代天才？”

SemiAnalysis, a research and consulting firm for the semiconductor and AI industry, posted a “conspiracy theory take on the situation,” speculating that Huang’s removal is a way to “remove opposition to an extremely hawkish China export control bill,” referring to the Remote Access Security Act (RASA).

半导体和人工智能行业研究咨询公司SemiAnalysis发布了一种“关于此局势的阴谋论观点”，推测罢免黄仁勋是为了“消除对一项极其强硬的对华出口管制法案的反对”，该法案指的是《远程访问安全法案》（RASA）。

More than 185,000 people, including Sweeney, Masad, Elon Musk, Uber co-founder Travis Kalanick, Y Combinator CEO Garry Tan, and Shopify CEO Tobias Lütke, have signed a “Stand with Jensen” letter, calling for his reinstatement as CEO. The letter similarly calls for a closer look into the circumstances surrounding Huang’s resignation.

包括斯威尼、马萨德（Masad）、埃隆·马斯克（Elon Musk）、Uber联合创始人特拉维斯·卡兰尼克（Travis Kalanick）、Y Combinator首席执行官加里·谭（Garry Tan）以及Shopify首席执行官托比亚斯·吕特克（Tobias Lütke）在内的超过18.5万人签署了一封“支持黄仁勋”（Stand with Jensen）的联名信，呼吁恢复其首席执行官职务。这封信同样要求对黄仁勋辞职背后的情况进行更深入的调查。
