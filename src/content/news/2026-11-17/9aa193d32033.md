Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.17573 (cs) [Submitted on 12 Nov 2026] Title: CamSpokenNLI: A Pilot Resource for Cross-Variety Transfer Evaluation in Cameroon Pidgin English and Camfranglais Authors: Tiani Pekins Ebika.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.17573 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 12 日] 标题：CamSpokenNLI：喀麦隆皮钦语与喀麦隆法语混合语跨变体迁移评估的试点资源 作者：Tiani Pekins Ebika。

Abstract: Cameroon Pidgin English (a West African Pidgin English variety) and Camfranglais are widely used in Cameroon, yet to our knowledge, neither has a dedicated spoken Natural Language Inference (NLI) resource. We introduce CamSpokenNLI, a pilot dataset of 114 scenario-matched spoken NLI items in each variety (228 in total), recorded by one native speaker from Buea.

摘要：喀麦隆皮钦语（一种西非皮钦英语变体）和喀麦隆法语混合语（Camfranglais）在喀麦隆被广泛使用，但据我们所知，两者均缺乏专门的口语自然语言推理（NLI）资源。我们推出了 CamSpokenNLI，这是一个试点数据集，包含每种变体各 114 个场景匹配的口语 NLI 条目（总计 228 个），由一名来自布埃亚的母语人士录制。

Each item pairs premise audio and text with a hypothesis, an NLI label, and English glosses. The author assigned labels, so the dataset is an exploratory resource rather than a mature benchmark. The NLI experiments use the reference text; the audio is evaluated separately.

每个条目都将前提音频和文本与假设、NLI 标签以及英语注释配对。标签由作者指定，因此该数据集是一个探索性资源，而非成熟的基准测试。NLI 实验使用参考文本；音频则单独进行评估。

XLM-RoBERTa baselines with five-fold item-level cross-validation reach 44.7% on Pidgin and 46.2% on Camfranglais (chance 33.3%). Still, neither is statistically distinguishable from hypothesis-only input, so hypothesis wording may account for much of the accuracy.

使用五折条目级交叉验证的 XLM-RoBERTa 基线在皮钦语上达到 44.7%，在喀麦隆法语混合语上达到 46.2%（随机概率为 33.3%）。尽管如此，两者在统计学上均与仅输入假设的情况无显著差异，因此假设的措辞可能占据了准确率的大部分来源。

Under disjoint-item cross-variety transfer, where a test item's counterpart is never seen in training, accuracy is 40.1% (Pidgin to Camfranglais) and 45.6% (Camfranglais to Pidgin). A counterpart-leak audit raises this to 60-64%, illustrating how counterpart leakage can overstate transfer.

在不相交条目的跨变体迁移设置下（即测试条目的对应项从未在训练中出现），准确率分别为 40.1%（从皮钦语到喀麦隆法语混合语）和 45.6%（从喀麦隆法语混合语到皮钦语）。一项针对对应项泄露的审计将此准确率提高到了 60-64%，这说明了对应项泄露如何导致迁移效果被高估。

Zero-shot Whisper transcription of the recordings performs poorly: the best word error rates are 66.6% for Pidgin and 45.5% for Camfranglais, and with automatic language identification, Whisper Large-v3 labels 93 of 114 Pidgin recordings as Yoruba or Swahili.

对录音进行的零样本 Whisper 转录表现不佳：皮钦语的最佳词错误率为 66.6%，喀麦隆法语混合语为 45.5%；在自动语言识别方面，Whisper Large-v3 将 114 个皮钦语录音中的 93 个标记为约鲁巴语或斯瓦希里语。

Together, the results suggest that such resources need disjoint-item evaluation and hypothesis-only controls, and that speech recognition must be adapted to these varieties before spoken NLI can be tested end to end.

总之，这些结果表明此类资源需要进行不相交条目评估和仅假设的对照实验，并且在能够进行端到端口语 NLI 测试之前，语音识别技术必须针对这些变体进行适配。
