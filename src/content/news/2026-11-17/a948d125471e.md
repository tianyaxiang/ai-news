# Sam Altman says he wants everyone ‘to have a lot of RAM’

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman is once again addressing his controversial comments on PC RAM purchases. “I want to be clear, I want people to have a lot of RAM, I think everybody should be able to buy a lot of RAM for their own computer,” Altman said today. “I think the home tinkering is great.”

OpenAI 首席执行官山姆·奥特曼（Sam Altman）再次回应了他关于个人电脑内存（RAM）购买的争议性言论。奥特曼今日表示：“我想明确一点，我希望人们拥有充足的内存，我认为每个人都应该能够为自己的电脑购买大量的内存。我认为家庭层面的技术折腾是非常棒的。”

The comments were made onstage at Semafor’s Silicon Valley & The World summit on Monday. Altman’s remarks on RAM stirred up controversy last month. In an appearance on the Cheeky Pint podcast, Altman said that it’s “quite terrible” for everyone to have RAM in personal computers rather than in data centers. He later followed up on those comments in posts on X earlier this month, saying, “i want you to have so much ram” and saying the original remarks had been “taken out of context.”

这些评论是在周一于 Semafor 举办的“硅谷与世界”峰会上发表的。奥特曼关于内存的言论在上个月引发了争议。在参加《Cheeky Pint》播客节目时，奥特曼曾表示，让每个人都在个人电脑中配置内存，而不是将其集中在数据中心，是“相当糟糕的”。本月初，他在 X 平台上发帖跟进此事，称“我希望你们拥有尽可能多的内存”，并表示最初的言论被“断章取义”了。

AI companies have been buying up the world’s memory supply for data centers, leading to ongoing price hikes on everything from phones to game consoles. Altman commented on supply chain issues, saying OpenAI has “been constrained by different things at different times,” like memory, rack assembly, data center capacity, or “some random component.”

人工智能公司一直在为数据中心抢购全球的内存供应，导致从手机到游戏机等各类产品的价格持续上涨。奥特曼在评论供应链问题时表示，OpenAI 在“不同时期受到不同因素的制约”，例如内存、机架组装、数据中心容量或“某些随机组件”。

Onstage today, Altman went on to say that “a few years ago we started saying the world needs to build a lot more memory supply chain in particular.” He acknowledged how RAM prices have soared over recent months, saying: “The world did not build out that much, and now we have seen DRAM prices — I don’t know exactly, but maybe they went up 5x, 7x, something like that. I think it would be much better if the world just had a lot of memory. That comment, which I think got misinterpreted, but there is a true part of it, which I will stand by is that, if we’re going to be in this memory supply shortage, then in some sense — whether or not we’ll decide to do it, because I think there are other tradeoffs like people having their own at-home inference — the optimal thing would be, can we share this memory and have it working 100 percent of the time rather than sometimes off in someone’s edge device? Again, optimal use of this limited supply. Maybe it’s not the reason to make the decision but that’s what I was trying to say.”

在今天的舞台上，奥特曼继续说道：“几年前我们就开始说，世界需要建立更多的内存供应链。”他承认内存价格在过去几个月里飙升，并表示：“世界并没有建设那么多产能，现在我们看到 DRAM 价格——我不确定具体数字，但可能上涨了 5 倍、7 倍左右。如果世界拥有充足的内存，情况会好得多。那条被误解的评论中，有一个我坚持的真实观点：如果我们正处于内存供应短缺之中，那么从某种意义上说——无论我们是否决定这样做，因为我认为还有其他权衡因素，比如人们在家里进行推理——最理想的情况是，我们能否共享这些内存，让它们 100% 的时间都在工作，而不是有时闲置在某个人的边缘设备上？再次强调，这是对有限供应的最优利用。也许这不是做决策的理由，但这正是我试图表达的意思。”

Altman also commented on Jensen Huang’s resignation as Nvidia’s CEO, saying he “doesn’t know enough” to say if Huang should be reinstated.

奥特曼还评论了黄仁勋辞去英伟达首席执行官一职的消息，称他“了解得不够多”，无法判断黄仁勋是否应该复职。
