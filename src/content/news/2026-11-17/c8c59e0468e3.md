Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.17495 (cs) [Submitted on 12 Nov 2026] Title: An Operationalization of Opaque Serial Depth Authors: Nathan Sheffield, Alek Westover, Lukas Finnveden, Alexa Pan, Julian Stastny, Ryan Greenblatt.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.17495 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 12 日] 标题：不透明序列深度的操作化 作者：Nathan Sheffield, Alek Westover, Lukas Finnveden, Alexa Pan, Julian Stastny, Ryan Greenblatt。

Abstract: Chain-of-thought (CoT) is currently a valuable tool for overseeing AI models, but some architectural shifts could significantly reduce CoT monitorability. We operationalize a measure that serves as a proxy for the amount of unverbalized serial cognition a model can perform, as a specific instantiation of the notion of opaque serial depth: roughly, the length of the longest path through a model's computation that does not pass through an interpretable bottleneck.

摘要：思维链（CoT）目前是监督人工智能模型的宝贵工具，但一些架构上的转变可能会显著降低 CoT 的可监控性。我们将一种度量指标操作化，作为模型能够执行的未言语化序列认知量的代理，这是不透明序列深度概念的具体实例化：大致而言，即模型计算过程中不经过可解释瓶颈的最长路径长度。

Our main contribution is a particular standard for what counts as such a bottleneck. We define natural-language-rooted (NL-rooted) nodes as nodes that were initialized from a pre-training prior to produce human-understandable outputs, whose output space has not been expanded beyond that prior, and whose outputs the model has not been modified to interpret as a different type of data.

我们的主要贡献是为衡量此类瓶颈设定了一个特定标准。我们将自然语言根源（NL-rooted）节点定义为：从预训练先验初始化以产生人类可理解输出的节点，其输出空间未超出该先验范围，且模型未被修改以将这些输出解释为其他类型的数据。

By construction, the resulting measure, NLS depth, can be adjudicated before training from an architecture, training recipe, and deployment configuration alone, and is insensitive to ordinary post-training such as prompting, distillation, and RL. We find that, across open-weight models, NLS depth correlates strongly with layer count, with some exceptions, and has grown slowly over recent years.

根据其构建方式，所得出的度量指标（NLS 深度）可以在训练前仅根据架构、训练方案和部署配置进行判定，并且对提示工程、蒸馏和强化学习等常规训练后处理不敏感。我们发现，在开源权重模型中，NLS 深度与层数高度相关（存在少数例外），且近年来增长缓慢。

It would likely grow slowly under continued scaling of standard transformers, while latent-reasoning architectures such as full-bandwidth transformers could increase it by a large amount. We work through how NLS depth applies to a range of existing and proposed architectures, discuss alternative operationalizations, and give a worked example bounding the depth of Gemma3-1B.

在标准 Transformer 模型持续扩展的情况下，它可能会缓慢增长，而全带宽 Transformer 等潜在推理架构可能会使其大幅增加。我们研究了 NLS 深度如何应用于一系列现有和提议的架构，讨论了替代的操作化方法，并给出了一个界定 Gemma3-1B 深度的实例。

We argue that AI developers should track and report the NLS depth of their models as a complement to direct measurements of CoT monitorability.

我们认为，人工智能开发者应跟踪并报告其模型的 NLS 深度，作为对 CoT 可监控性直接测量的补充。
