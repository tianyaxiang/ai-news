On Friday, a petition to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO of Nvidia began circulating on social media, and by Monday it listed some notable signatories, including Elon Musk and several influential Nvidia researchers.

本文最初发表于我们的 AI 每周通讯《算法》（The Algorithm）。如需第一时间在收件箱中获取此类报道，请在此注册。周五，一份要求恢复黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）英伟达首席执行官职务的请愿书开始在社交媒体上传播，到周一，该请愿书已列出了一些知名签署人，包括埃隆·马斯克（Elon Musk）和几位有影响力的英伟达研究人员。

But comb through the more than 115,000 names and you’ll notice a significant faction of Huang’s supporters—higher-ups from Hugging Face, a hub for sharing AI models, and venture capital firms Andreessen Horowitz and Sequoia Capital—come from a particular slice of the tech world. These names have a vested interest in the development of open-source AI, models that, unlike ChatGPT or Claude, can be downloaded and run independent of large tech companies.

但仔细查看这超过 11.5 万个名字，你会发现黄仁勋的支持者中有一个重要的派系——来自 AI 模型共享中心 Hugging Face 的高管，以及风险投资公司 Andreessen Horowitz 和红杉资本（Sequoia Capital）——他们来自科技界的特定领域。这些人对开源 AI 的发展有着既得利益，这些模型与 ChatGPT 或 Claude 不同，可以下载并在独立于大型科技公司的情况下运行。

Supporters of such models say that concentrating AI in the hands of a few powerful companies will leave everyone else worse off (a concern that wouldn’t sound out of place among the AI industry's critics on the left, if so many open-source advocates weren’t also venture capitalists who stand to profit when startups can build on cheaper AI without paying Big Tech).

此类模型的支持者认为，将 AI 集中在少数几家强大的公司手中会让其他人处于不利地位（如果不是因为许多开源倡导者同时也是风险投资家，且在初创公司能够利用更便宜的 AI 而无需向大型科技公司付费时获利，这种担忧在 AI 行业的左翼批评者中听起来并不会显得格格不入）。

Huang had become a sort of ringleader for those who defend open source to policymakers against concerns that such models come with no guardrails and cede AI leadership to China, where the most powerful and popular open-source models are created. They now want their most influential voice returned to his post.

黄仁勋已成为那些向政策制定者捍卫开源的人的领袖，以应对外界对开源模型缺乏护栏并将 AI 领导权拱手让给中国的担忧，而目前最强大、最受欢迎的开源模型正是在中国创造的。他们现在希望他们最有影响力的代言人重返岗位。

But it’s not just Huang’s extramarital affairs that threaten his (and Nvidia’s) credibility as an advocate for open-source AI. New research into China’s startups using Nvidia’s chips, and a new rule from the US Department of Commerce announced Monday, could deal a blow to both the movement’s reputation and its technical progress.

但不仅是黄仁勋的婚外情威胁到了他（以及英伟达）作为开源 AI 倡导者的信誉。针对中国初创公司使用英伟达芯片的新研究，以及美国商务部周一宣布的一项新规，可能会对该运动的声誉及其技术进步造成打击。

On Thursday, the day Huang resigned, ex-OpenAI researcher Will DePue published findings online suggesting that China’s AI companies had far more access to Nvidia’s advanced Blackwell chips than previously estimated. China is banned from accessing Nvidia’s most cutting-edge chips to help protect America’s AI lead. It’s unclear how they would have gained access to the chips.

周四，即黄仁勋辞职当天，前 OpenAI 研究员威尔·德普（Will DePue）在网上发布的研究结果显示，中国 AI 公司获得英伟达先进 Blackwell 芯片的渠道远比此前估计的要多。为了保护美国的 AI 领先地位，中国被禁止获取英伟达最尖端的芯片。目前尚不清楚他们是如何获得这些芯片的。

DePue claims the results were verified by senior researchers at OpenAI and Anthropic, but an independent analysis has not confirmed the results. (DePue did not respond to a request for an interview. Nvidia did not immediately respond to a request for comment.)

德普声称这些结果已由 OpenAI 和 Anthropic 的高级研究人员验证，但独立分析尚未证实这些结果。（德普未回应采访请求。英伟达未立即回应置评请求。）

The findings do not establish that Nvidia helped Chinese companies obtain the chips, and the broader problem of chip smuggling was already known to extend well beyond Nvidia’s control. But in suggesting that Nvidia may have profited from the rise of Chinese AI startups, whose models are increasingly being used by American companies instead of domestic alternatives, the findings do leave Huang in an awkward position.

这些发现并未证实英伟达帮助中国公司获取了芯片，而且芯片走私这一更广泛的问题早已被认为超出了英伟达的控制范围。但这些发现暗示英伟达可能从中国 AI 初创公司的崛起中获利，而这些公司的模型正越来越多地被美国公司使用以替代本土方案，这确实让黄仁勋处于尴尬境地。

He has championed open-source AI as a way to strengthen American technological leadership. “Without open source, startups can’t thrive, university researchers can’t do research, you can’t teach AI, scientists can’t use AI,” he said last December during a talk about helping the US win the AI race against China.

他曾倡导将开源 AI 作为加强美国技术领导地位的一种方式。去年 12 月，在谈到帮助美国赢得对华 AI 竞赛时，他曾表示：“没有开源，初创公司就无法蓬勃发展，大学研究人员就无法进行研究，你就无法教授 AI，科学家也无法使用 AI。”

It also makes the idea of reinstating Huang even more of a long shot. US Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent even said last week that Nvidia should not consider someone from inside the company to replace him. The new findings lower Huang’s chances further.

这也使得恢复黄仁勋职务的想法变得更加渺茫。美国财政部长斯科特·贝森特（Scott Bessent）上周甚至表示，英伟达不应考虑从公司内部选人来接替他。新的调查结果进一步降低了黄仁勋回归的可能性。

What happens next? Nvidia announced in September it would acquire Hugging Face for nearly $13 billion. Its support for open source also serves a straightforward financial interest: The more businesses that build their own models instead of forking over money to Anthropic and OpenAI, the more chips Nvidia can sell.

接下来会发生什么？英伟达在 9 月宣布将以近 130 亿美元收购 Hugging Face。它对开源的支持也服务于直接的财务利益：更多的企业建立自己的模型，而不是向 Anthropic 和 OpenAI 付费，英伟达就能卖出更多的芯片。

But a new rule from the US Department of Commerce could mean the models popular on Hugging Face will soon stop getting smarter. The rule aims to prevent Chinese companies from training models on Nvidia chips that they can’t own directly, but that they can access remotely in data centers in countries like Malaysia—a workaround that has helped China’s open-source models get so good.

但美国商务部的一项新规可能意味着 Hugging Face 上流行的模型很快将停止进化。该规定旨在防止中国公司利用其无法直接拥有、但可以通过马来西亚等国数据中心远程访问的英伟达芯片来训练模型——这正是帮助中国开源模型取得如此大进步的变通方法。

The open-source world rallied around Huang because of his influence on policymakers. That was important, since the rise of China’s open-source models has splintered some in Trump’s orbit. If Huang is out, who is next?

开源界团结在黄仁勋周围，是因为他在政策制定者中具有影响力。这一点很重要，因为中国开源模型的崛起已经在特朗普的圈子里造成了一些分歧。如果黄仁勋出局，谁会是下一个？
