title: "Rethinking Visual Reasoning in Text-to-Image Reward Modeling"

---

Reward models are a core component of modern text-to-image systems, serving as the objective for inference-time selection and RL-based post-training. A key prerequisite is visual understanding: to judge prompt adherence and ground aesthetic preferences, a reward model must bind attributes, resolve relations, and count reliably.

奖励模型是现代文生图系统的核心组件，作为推理时选择和基于强化学习的后训练目标。视觉理解是一个关键前提：为了判断提示词遵循程度并建立审美偏好，奖励模型必须能够绑定属性、解析关系并进行可靠的计数。

We find that widely-used reward models perform poorly on targeted visual reasoning evaluations. Even when starting from strong vision-language backbones, preference fine-tuning erodes these capabilities, and continual-learning baselines fail to mitigate a steep tradeoff between visual reasoning and preference accuracy.

我们发现，广泛使用的奖励模型在针对性的视觉推理评估中表现不佳。即使从强大的视觉-语言主干模型开始，偏好微调也会削弱这些能力，而持续学习基线方法也无法缓解视觉推理与偏好准确性之间严重的权衡问题。

We propose a data-centric intervention that injects visual reasoning supervision into preference learning by flipping the pairing axis. To complement conventional human preference data (paired images under a shared prompt), we convert existing visual reasoning datasets into preference pairs that share the same image but differ in prompt, and train on the mixture.

我们提出了一种以数据为中心的干预措施，通过翻转配对轴，将视觉推理监督注入到偏好学习中。为了补充传统的人类偏好数据（在共享提示词下的配对图像），我们将现有的视觉推理数据集转换为共享同一图像但提示词不同的偏好对，并对混合数据进行训练。

This yields a Pareto improvement in both visual understanding and preference prediction. Across inference-time best-of-n selection and RL post-training, the resulting reward model improves text-to-image generation on challenging compositional prompts (GenEval) by an average of 4.0% and 5.5% respectively, outperforming HPSv3 which was trained on 6x more preference pairs.

这在视觉理解和偏好预测方面均实现了帕累托改进。在推理时的“最佳之 n”选择和强化学习后训练中，所得的奖励模型在具有挑战性的组合提示词（GenEval）上的文生图生成效果平均分别提升了 4.0% 和 5.5%，优于使用 6 倍偏好对训练的 HPSv3。
