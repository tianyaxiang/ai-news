Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.17469 (cs) [Submitted on 12 Nov 2026] Title: Improved Regret via Variance-Aware Policy Optimization in Adversarial MDPs Authors: Ofir Schlisselberg, Alon Cohen, Yishay Mansour.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.17469 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 12 日] 标题：通过对抗性 MDP 中的方差感知策略优化改进遗憾值 作者：Ofir Schlisselberg, Alon Cohen, Yishay Mansour。

Abstract: We study episodic adversarial MDPs with unknown dynamics and full-information rewards and achieve regret with leading term $\widetilde O(\sqrt{H^3SAK})$. Here $H$ is the horizon, $S$ the total number of states across decision layers, $A$ the number of actions, and $K$ the number of episodes. This improves the two incomparable prior leading terms by factors $\sqrt{S/H}$ and $\sqrt{H^3}$, leaving a factor $\sqrt H$ to the lower-bound scale.

摘要：我们研究了具有未知动态和全信息奖励的片段式对抗性 MDP，并实现了以 $\widetilde O(\sqrt{H^3SAK})$ 为主项的遗憾值。其中 $H$ 为时间跨度，$S$ 为跨决策层的状态总数，$A$ 为动作数量，$K$ 为片段数量。这比之前两个不可比的主项分别改进了 $\sqrt{S/H}$ 和 $\sqrt{H^3}$ 倍，距离下界规模仅差一个 $\sqrt H$ 因子。

We give a reduction from unknown to known dynamics for \emph{downstream-dependent} algorithms, whose policy at step $h$ is computed only from information at steps $h+1$ and onward. This reduces the remaining leading-order gap to finding a downstream-dependent known-dynamics algorithm with $\widetilde O(H\sqrt K)$ regret, which would yield the tight $\widetilde O(H\sqrt{SAK})$ leading term for unknown dynamics.

我们针对“下游依赖”算法提出了一种从未知动态到已知动态的归约方法，该类算法在步骤 $h$ 的策略仅根据步骤 $h+1$ 及以后的信息计算得出。这使得剩余的主项差距转化为寻找一种具有 $\widetilde O(H\sqrt K)$ 遗憾值的下游依赖已知动态算法，从而为未知动态推导出紧致的 $\widetilde O(H\sqrt{SAK})$ 主项。

We instantiate the reduction with VOPO, a policy optimization (PO) algorithm. Its improved guarantees follow from a variance-aware analysis enabled by a novel bonus, which we call the Stability-Matching Bonus (SMB). VOPO achieves $\widetilde O(\sqrt{H^3K})$ regret under known dynamics with full information; its $\sqrt H$ gap to the target $H\sqrt K$ rate is inherited by our unknown-dynamics guarantee.

我们使用策略优化 (PO) 算法 VOPO 实现了这一归约。其改进的保证源于一种由新型奖励（我们称之为稳定性匹配奖励，SMB）支持的方差感知分析。VOPO 在已知动态和全信息条件下实现了 $\widetilde O(\sqrt{H^3K})$ 的遗憾值；其与目标 $H\sqrt K$ 速率之间 $\sqrt H$ 的差距被我们的未知动态保证所继承。

We also show that with known dynamics and trajectory bandit feedback, VOPO achieves $\widetilde O(\sqrt{H^2SAK})$ regret. Both known-dynamics guarantees improve the corresponding state-of-the-art PO bounds by a factor $\sqrt H$. A matching lower bound establishes that the full-information rate is tight in one parameter regime.

我们还证明了在已知动态和轨迹多臂老虎机反馈下，VOPO 可实现 $\widetilde O(\sqrt{H^2SAK})$ 的遗憾值。这两项已知动态保证均将相应的现有最优 PO 界限改进了 $\sqrt H$ 倍。一个匹配的下界证明了全信息速率在某种参数设定下是紧致的。
