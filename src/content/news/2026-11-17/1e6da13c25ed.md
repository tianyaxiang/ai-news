Small open-weight language models can run crowd decision-making (CDM) on an institution's own infrastructure, which keeps the checkpoint, the inference settings and the opinions under its control. Their judgements vary across repeated queries; whether repeating and aggregating them, often through the 2-tuple linguistic representation, settles the decision rather than the labels is untested.

小型开放权重语言模型可以在机构自己的基础设施上运行群体决策（CDM），从而将检查点、推理设置和意见保持在自身控制之下。它们的判断在重复查询中会有所不同；通过 2-元组语言表示法等方式重复并聚合这些判断是否能确定决策，而非仅仅确定标签，这一点尚未经过测试。

Five open-weight models of 8-36B total parameters on one consumer GPU and a hosted reference classify 474 TripAdvisor reviews of four London restaurants ten times each; three open-weight models repeat the protocol on 600 reviews of twelve Madrid restaurants. Continuous evaluations preserve resolution: snapping each review leaves 5 distinct values against 21-65, and snapping at every level at most two collective values.

五个总参数在 8-36B 之间、运行于单台消费级 GPU 上的开放权重模型以及一个托管参考模型，分别对四家伦敦餐厅的 474 条猫途鹰（TripAdvisor）评论进行了十次分类；其中三个开放权重模型对十二家马德里餐厅的 600 条评论重复了该协议。连续评估保留了分辨率：对每条评论进行截断会留下 5 个不同的值，而对比 21-65 个值，在每个层级进行截断最多只能留下两个集体值。

Sensitivity to the participants persists once run-to-run variability is reduced: in London, redrawing the reviewers keeps the winner on 47-81% of replicas against 86-100% for redrawing the runs. With simultaneous intervals over every pair of alternatives under crowd resampling, no system on either corpus names a single winner: in London every system keeps all four restaurants as candidates, and in Madrid the candidate sets hold five to eight of the twelve.

一旦运行间的变异性降低，对参与者的敏感性依然存在：在伦敦，重新抽取评论者使得获胜者在 47-81% 的副本中保持不变，而重新运行程序则为 86-100%。在群体重采样下，对每一对备选方案进行同步区间分析时，没有任何系统能在任何语料库中选出唯一的获胜者：在伦敦，每个系统都将所有四家餐厅保留为候选者；在马德里，候选集包含十二家中的五到八家。

Extra runs and output-level fusion stabilize the winner but do not remove that sensitivity, and the fusion does not beat its best member. The three larger open-weight models name the hosted reference's London winner and at least match its per-review correlation with the reviewers' stars, so small open-weight models can support local CDM, reported as candidate sets with margins rather than forced rankings.

额外的运行和输出级融合稳定了获胜者，但并未消除这种敏感性，且融合结果并未优于其最佳成员。三个较大的开放权重模型指出了托管参考模型在伦敦的获胜者，并至少在每条评论与评论者星级的相关性上与其持平，因此小型开放权重模型可以支持本地 CDM，以带有边际的候选集而非强制排名的方式进行报告。
