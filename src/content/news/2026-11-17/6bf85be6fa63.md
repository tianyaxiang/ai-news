# California sues OpenAI over rogue AI hacking incidents

加利福尼亚州就流氓人工智能黑客事件起诉 OpenAI

OpenAI lost control of its AI agents more than 80 times during testing, California’s AG claims.

加州总检察长声称，OpenAI 在测试过程中有 80 多次失去了对其人工智能代理的控制。

California is suing OpenAI after its AI agents broke out of their testing environment and hacked other companies, including Hugging Face. In a lawsuit filed Monday, Attorney General Rob Bonta accuses OpenAI of violating consumer safety law by knowingly developing a “dangerous product without proper oversight,” as reported earlier by Reuters.

在 OpenAI 的人工智能代理突破测试环境并入侵包括 Hugging Face 在内的其他公司后，加利福尼亚州对其提起了诉讼。据路透社早前报道，总检察长罗伯·邦塔（Rob Bonta）在周一提交的诉讼中指控 OpenAI 明知故犯，在“缺乏适当监管的情况下开发危险产品”，违反了消费者安全法。

The lawsuit claims the cyberattack on Hugging Face “was the predictable result of an internal culture at OpenAI that prioritizes growth and rapid model development over safety and accountability.” It alleges that OpenAI recently told the attorney general’s office that it “lost control” of its AI agents more than 80 times during testing.

诉讼称，针对 Hugging Face 的网络攻击“是 OpenAI 内部文化的可预见结果，这种文化将增长和快速模型开发置于安全和问责之上”。诉讼指控 OpenAI 最近向总检察长办公室承认，其在测试过程中有 80 多次“失去控制”了人工智能代理。

After revealing the rogue AI attack on Hugging Face in July, news of several other similar incidents emerged, including attacks on an Australian government website, a German wiki site, and the software package manager RubyGems. OpenAI’s agents also tried to “bruteforce” a United Nations website.

在 7 月份披露了针对 Hugging Face 的流氓人工智能攻击后，其他几起类似事件的消息也随之浮出水面，包括对澳大利亚政府网站、德国维基网站和软件包管理器 RubyGems 的攻击。OpenAI 的代理甚至试图“暴力破解”一个联合国网站。

“OpenAI’s actions escalated real-world cybersecurity risks, threatening the safety, health, and well-being of society,” Bonta says in the press release. “The company knowingly tested a dangerous product without proper oversight or safety procedures and misled the public about it — this disregard for public safety violated California law.”

“OpenAI 的行为加剧了现实世界的网络安全风险，威胁到了社会的公共安全、健康和福祉，”邦塔在新闻稿中表示。“该公司在没有适当监管或安全程序的情况下明知故犯地测试危险产品，并就此误导了公众——这种无视公共安全的行为违反了加州法律。”

OpenAI released its new cybersecurity AI model earlier than this month, saying that it’s less likely to go rogue and attack other platforms. It’s also giving safety evaluators at Apollo Research access to its AI systems as part of a pilot program that monitors models for misalignment.

OpenAI 本月早些时候发布了其新的网络安全人工智能模型，称其不太可能出现流氓行为并攻击其他平台。作为监测模型失调的试点项目的一部分，它还允许 Apollo Research 的安全评估人员访问其人工智能系统。

“Since the Hugging Face incident, we’ve undertaken an extensive and transparent effort to understand and address misaligned model activity,” OpenAI spokesperson Drew Pusateri says in an emailed statement to The Verge. “This lawsuit appears to single out OpenAI over a challenge that’s been identified by every major AI lab. We take misalignment seriously, and we have engaged — and will continue to engage — constructively with the Attorney General’s office to share information and advance strong, practical safety standards for the entire industry.”

“自 Hugging Face 事件以来，我们已经进行了广泛而透明的努力，以了解并解决模型失调活动，”OpenAI 发言人德鲁·普萨特里（Drew Pusateri）在给《The Verge》的电子邮件声明中表示。“这起诉讼似乎专门针对 OpenAI，而这一挑战是每个主要人工智能实验室都面临的问题。我们非常重视模型失调问题，并且已经并将继续与总检察长办公室进行建设性接触，以共享信息并为整个行业推进强有力且实用的安全标准。”
