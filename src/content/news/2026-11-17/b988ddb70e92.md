The stunning return of Starship from the skies to a launch tower in South Texas answered some questions about the future of SpaceX’s ambitious rocket program. Namely, it is now rapidly moving toward rapidly reusable launch. However, it raises other questions. For example, as Starship becomes fully reusable, when might humans fly on the vehicle? The short answer is pretty simple: If SpaceX prioritizes human spaceflight on Starship, it could happen reasonably quickly. The longer answer becomes more complicated and depends on what one means by human spaceflight.

星舰从高空返回并成功被南德克萨斯州的发射塔捕获，这令人惊叹的一幕回答了关于 SpaceX 雄心勃勃的火箭项目未来的一些问题。也就是说，它现在正迅速迈向快速可重复使用的发射。然而，这也引发了其他问题。例如，随着星舰变得完全可重复使用，人类何时可能乘坐该飞行器飞行？简短的回答很简单：如果 SpaceX 优先考虑星舰的载人航天，这可能会很快实现。更长的回答则更为复杂，取决于人们对“载人航天”的定义。

The first people to fly on Starship probably won’t launch from Earth, or land here. Rather, they will probably be NASA’s lunar astronauts. So there are different timelines for humans flying aboard Starship and landing on Earth. Given what is known publicly, and what Ars has heard privately, here are some educated guesses about all of this.

第一批乘坐星舰飞行的人可能不会从地球发射，也不会在地球着陆。相反，他们很可能是美国国家航空航天局（NASA）的登月宇航员。因此，人类乘坐星舰飞行和在地球着陆有着不同的时间表。根据公开信息以及 Ars 私下听到的消息，以下是对这一切的一些推测。

Past is prologue. The first thing we can say for sure is that all estimates of human spaceflight on Starship should be taken with a gigantic grain of salt, including those in this article. Putting humans on any space vehicle is a difficult and time-consuming task, even outside of the requirements that NASA would impose for its astronauts.

过去是序幕。我们可以肯定的一点是，所有关于星舰载人航天的估计都应持极度怀疑的态度，包括本文中的估计。将人类送上任何航天器都是一项艰巨且耗时的任务，即使不考虑 NASA 对其宇航员所施加的要求也是如此。

The original target for launching humans on Starship (then known as the Interplanetary Transport System) set a decade ago was 2024. That was for a Mars mission. Then, in 2018, SpaceX announced a lunar flyby mission on Starship with a target launch in 2023. This dearMoon mission was canceled in 2024. In 2022, SpaceX and Dennis Tito announced a lunar flyby mission later in the 2020s. More recently, according to sources, SpaceX has been telling individuals interested in launching on Starship that it could begin offering flights by 2029. I probably would not put too much stock in this date. All of this is to say, we cannot learn much from history other than to deeply discount any public launch dates on Starship for humans.

十年前，星舰（当时称为“行星际运输系统”）载人发射的最初目标定在 2024 年。那是针对火星任务的。随后，在 2018 年，SpaceX 宣布了一项星舰绕月飞行任务，目标发射时间为 2023 年。这项“亲爱的月球”（dearMoon）任务于 2024 年取消。2022 年，SpaceX 和丹尼斯·蒂托（Dennis Tito）宣布了一项 2020 年代后期的绕月飞行任务。最近，据消息人士称，SpaceX 一直在告诉那些有兴趣乘坐星舰发射的人，它可能在 2029 年开始提供飞行服务。我可能不会太看重这个日期。所有这些都说明，除了对任何公开的星舰载人发射日期持深度怀疑态度外，我们无法从历史中学到太多东西。

Artemis missions. Most likely, the first humans to fly on Starship in space will do so after launching from Earth inside the NASA Orion spacecraft. Once in orbit they will then dock with Starship before transiting to the Moon, landing there in Starship, launching from the Moon back to Orion, and then returning to Earth inside NASA’s spacecraft. NASA signed an agreement with SpaceX to develop Starship as this “Human Landing System” in 2021, with a lunar landing targeted for 2024. To no one’s surprise, this target has moved to 2028, and there remains considerable uncertainty about the viability of this date.

阿尔忒弥斯任务。最有可能的情况是，第一批在太空中乘坐星舰的人类，将是在从地球乘坐 NASA 的猎户座（Orion）航天器发射之后。一旦进入轨道，他们将与星舰对接，然后前往月球，乘坐星舰在月球着陆，从月球发射回猎户座，最后乘坐 NASA 的航天器返回地球。NASA 于 2021 年与 SpaceX 签署协议，开发星舰作为这种“人类着陆系统”，目标是在 2024 年实现登月。不出所料，这一目标已推迟至 2028 年，且该日期的可行性仍存在相当大的不确定性。

Nevertheless, the success of Saturday’s tower catch should help propel the Starship program toward a higher launch cadence, which is essential for lunar missions. It therefore seems plausible, but not certain, that humans could land on the Moon inside Starship before the end of this decade. Again, how soon SpaceX achieves this target will depend upon its prioritization of a Human Landing System, among its other major goals to accomplish with Starship. At this point, SpaceX appears to be more focused on revenue-generating activities such as launching Starlink, and eventually, Starmind data center satellites.

尽管如此，周六发射塔捕获的成功应有助于推动星舰项目实现更高的发射频率，这对登月任务至关重要。因此，人类在本十年结束前乘坐星舰登陆月球似乎是可能的，但并非确定。同样，SpaceX 多快能实现这一目标，将取决于它在星舰的其他主要目标中对“人类着陆系统”的优先排序。目前，SpaceX 似乎更专注于创收活动，例如发射星链（Starlink）卫星，以及最终发射 Starmind 数据中心卫星。

Human landings on Earth. Much has been made about the fact that Starship does not have a launch abort system—a feature of most human spacecraft that allows a crew vehicle to be rapidly pulled away from an exploding rocket. This may not matter should SpaceX conduct its own human spaceflight missions. Unlike NASA, which imposes detailed human-rating requirements on vehicles carrying its astronauts, the Federal Aviation Administration does not certify commercial spacecraft as safe for passengers, instead operating under a regulatory framework that emphasizes informed consent and protection of the uninvolved public.

人类在地球着陆。关于星舰没有发射中止系统——这是大多数载人航天器的一项功能，允许乘员舱从爆炸的火箭中迅速撤离——这一点引起了广泛讨论。如果 SpaceX 进行自己的载人航天任务，这可能并不重要。与 NASA 对搭载其宇航员的飞行器施加详细的载人认证要求不同，美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）并不认证商业航天器对乘客是否安全，而是根据强调知情同意和保护无关公众的监管框架进行运作。

Should SpaceX reach the point where it can launch Starship reliably, it will have the confidence it needs to press forward with human flights. Conceivably, this could happen within a few years. According to potential customers, SpaceX is developing variants of the Human Landing System Starship for human flights, potentially carrying a few dozen people to the Moon. And such a prospect is tantalizing, for the interior of Starship’s pressurized cabin is almost unimaginably large for a spacecraft. A single Starship lunar lander has a pressurized habitable volume greater than the comparable volume of the International Space Station. This would be extraordinary.

如果 SpaceX 达到能够可靠发射星舰的程度，它将有信心推进载人飞行。可以想象，这可能会在几年内发生。据潜在客户称，SpaceX 正在开发用于载人飞行的人类着陆系统星舰变体，可能搭载几十人前往月球。这种前景令人垂涎，因为星舰加压舱的内部空间对于航天器来说大得难以想象。单艘星舰月球着陆器的加压居住空间比国际空间站的相应体积还要大。这将是了不起的。

Yet it is not a simple step to go from the current lunar lander design to an Earth lander. NASA’s lunar lander will never need to experience the tremendous heating of atmospheric reentry or perform the dramatic flip maneuver and propulsive landing that Starship uses to return home. Certifying those capabilities for human passengers, particularly on a vehicle without a traditional launch escape system, is an entirely different challenge.

然而，从目前的月球着陆器设计过渡到地球着陆器并非易事。NASA 的月球着陆器永远不需要经历大气层再入时的巨大热量，也不需要执行星舰返回地球时所使用的那种戏剧性的翻转机动和动力着陆。为载人乘客认证这些能力，特别是在没有传统发射逃逸系统的飞行器上，是一个完全不同的挑战。

Moreover, building a commercial human spaceflight business on Starship would be time-consuming and would likely not be nearly as profitable as launching Starlink satellites into space. And the risk is far greater—spaceflight remains dangerous, and the loss of a human crew would be a major negative for a publicly traded company. On the other hand, losing dozens of Starlink satellites on an errant launch would be a blip. Despite Starship’s enormous theoretical capacity, SpaceX has also shown little interest in developing it as a crew transportation vehicle for commercial space stations in the near term.

此外，在星舰上建立商业载人航天业务将非常耗时，而且可能远不如发射星链卫星那样有利可图。而且风险要大得多——航天飞行仍然危险，损失载人机组对一家上市公司来说将是一个重大的负面影响。另一方面，在一次错误的发射中损失几十颗星链卫星只是一个小插曲。尽管星舰拥有巨大的理论运载能力，但 SpaceX 在短期内似乎对将其开发为商业空间站的乘员运输工具兴趣不大。

Several private space station operators have approached SpaceX about the possibility of Starship bringing astronauts to and from their stations. SpaceX indicated it was not interested.
