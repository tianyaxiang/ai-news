Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.17629 (cs) [Submitted on 13 Nov 2026] Title: Training-Free Acceleration of Multi-View 3D Transformers via Regime-Aware Attention Approximation Authors: Muhammed Izzet Sezer, Sevval Belkis Dikkaya.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.17629 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 13 日] 标题：通过区域感知注意力近似实现多视图 3D Transformer 的免训练加速 作者：Muhammed Izzet Sezer, Sevval Belkis Dikkaya。

Abstract: Multi-view 3D transformers such as VGGT reconstruct a scene from a set of unposed images in a single forward pass, predicting camera poses, depth and 3D points, but the cost of their global attention grows quadratically with the number of images. Accelerators borrowed from large language models assume that each token attends to only a few others; on these models they lose accuracy or gain little speed.

摘要：诸如 VGGT 之类的多视图 3D Transformer 通过单次前向传递从一组未标定图像中重建场景，预测相机位姿、深度和 3D 点，但其全局注意力的成本随图像数量呈二次方增长。从大型语言模型中借鉴的加速器假设每个标记仅关注其他少数标记；在这些模型上，它们会损失精度或几乎无法提升速度。

We study the global attention of three multi-view transformers on seven datasets and find that every layer plays one of three roles: it averages over the whole scene, matches points across many images, or anchors on the first image, whose coordinates the outputs use. Building on this analysis, we propose Regime-AWare ATTention Approximation (RAWATTA), a training-free method that runs attention in 8-bit precision and keeps 16-bit precision only in the layers where a worst-case calibration check requires it.

我们研究了七个数据集上三个多视图 Transformer 的全局注意力，发现每一层都扮演着三种角色之一：对整个场景进行平均、跨多幅图像匹配点，或锚定在第一幅图像上（输出使用该图像的坐标）。基于此分析，我们提出了区域感知注意力近似（RAWATTA），这是一种免训练方法，它以 8 位精度运行注意力，仅在最坏情况校准检查需要时才在特定层保持 16 位精度。

Its fast setting, RAWATTA-Fast, also approximates attention in a few averaging and anchoring layers, while matching layers and the first image keep all their keys. On a 12 GB GPU at 50 images, RAWATTA runs VGGT faster than the released code, mostly through our CUDA engine, and loses at most about one point of pose accuracy (AUC@3) on any dataset; RAWATTA-Fast is slightly faster and less accurate. Both fit 200 images, where the released code runs out of memory at 100.

其快速设置 RAWATTA-Fast 还在少数平均和锚定层中近似注意力，同时匹配层和第一幅图像保留其所有键。在 12 GB GPU 上处理 50 幅图像时，RAWATTA 运行 VGGT 的速度比发布的代码更快（主要通过我们的 CUDA 引擎实现），并且在任何数据集上最多损失约一个点的位姿精度（AUC@3）；RAWATTA-Fast 速度稍快但精度较低。两者均可处理 200 幅图像，而发布的代码在 100 幅图像时就会耗尽内存。
