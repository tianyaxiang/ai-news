Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.17695 (cs) [Submitted on 13 Nov 2026] Title: Open-World Scene Deocclusion Authors: K.M.S.J. Kumarasinghe, Neil A. Dodgson, Fang-Lue Zhang.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.17695 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 13 日] 标题：开放世界场景去遮挡 作者：K.M.S.J. Kumarasinghe, Neil A. Dodgson, Fang-Lue Zhang。

Abstract: Recovering the complete appearance of occluded objects from a single image is a fundamental problem for scene understanding and image editing. Real-world scenes are challenging due to clutter, complex spatial occlusions, and diverse object categories.

摘要：从单张图像中恢复被遮挡物体的完整外观是场景理解和图像编辑中的一个基本问题。由于杂乱、复杂的空间遮挡以及多样的物体类别，现实世界的场景极具挑战性。

Existing methods often struggle when occlusion involves background surfaces or objects that are not explicitly detected, and fail to resolve the cyclic occlusion dependencies that frequently occur in natural scenes.

当遮挡涉及背景表面或未被明确检测到的物体时，现有方法往往难以应对，且无法解决自然场景中频繁出现的循环遮挡依赖关系。

We introduce a training-free, open-world scene deocclusion framework that recovers the full amodal appearance of detected or user-selected objects from a single RGB image, decomposing the scene into depth-ordered RGBA layers that are geometrically plausible, semantically faithful, and visually consistent with the visible scene.

我们引入了一种无需训练的开放世界场景去遮挡框架，该框架能从单张 RGB 图像中恢复被检测到或用户选定物体的完整非模态外观，并将场景分解为按深度排序的 RGBA 图层，这些图层在几何上合理、语义上忠实，且与可见场景在视觉上保持一致。

Extensive experiments show that our approach improves scene-level deocclusion, producing coherent and realistic reconstructions in real-world settings. Code will be available on the project page.

大量实验表明，我们的方法改进了场景级的去遮挡效果，在现实场景中生成了连贯且逼真的重建结果。代码将在项目页面提供。
