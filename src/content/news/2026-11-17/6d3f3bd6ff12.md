Hugging Face and OpenRouter are teaming up to make open-weight models easy to run. Every major open-weight release is already being published on the Hugging Face Hub, and OpenRouter serves those models at scale, routing each request across 80+ providers based on live measurements of each one.

Hugging Face 与 OpenRouter 正在合作，旨在让开源权重模型（open-weight models）的运行变得更加简单。目前，每一个主要的开源权重模型发布都会在 Hugging Face Hub 上进行同步，而 OpenRouter 则负责大规模地提供这些模型，并根据对 80 多家服务商的实时监测数据，将每个请求智能路由到最合适的服务商。

Together, every open-weight model on the Hub that a provider serves can be called with your Hugging Face token, through the same API, with the best provider picked for each request. Today, we are switching Inference Providers from our own custom router to OpenRouter's.

现在，Hub 上所有由服务商支持的开源权重模型，都可以通过你的 Hugging Face Token 进行调用。通过统一的 API，系统会自动为每个请求选择最佳的服务商。今天，我们将推理服务（Inference Providers）从我们自有的路由系统切换到了 OpenRouter 的路由系统。

Requests made to language models now go through OpenRouter, which picks the best provider for each call based on live price, speed and reliability, and retries on another provider if the first one fails. Your code stays the same.

发送给语言模型的请求现在将通过 OpenRouter 进行处理。它会根据实时价格、速度和可靠性为每次调用选择最佳服务商，如果首次尝试失败，系统还会自动重试其他服务商。你的代码无需进行任何更改。

Run them with the tools you already use. The router supports OpenAI's Chat Completions and Responses APIs and Anthropic's Messages API for every chat model, whatever the provider behind it.

使用你现有的工具即可运行这些模型。无论底层服务商是谁，该路由系统都支持 OpenAI 的 Chat Completions 和 Responses API，以及 Anthropic 的 Messages API。

How open-weight models are used. On OpenRouter, open-weight models went from 26.8% of the tokens served in October 2025 to 71.7% at the end of September 2026, after peaking at 77.5% in July (OpenRouter, October 2026).

关于开源权重模型的使用情况：在 OpenRouter 上，开源权重模型所占的 Token 服务比例从 2025 年 10 月的 26.8% 上升到了 2026 年 9 月底的 71.7%，并在 7 月份达到了 77.5% 的峰值（OpenRouter，2026 年 10 月）。

Over the same period, weekly open-weight volume grew more than 60-fold, against almost tenfold for closed models. This week, the most used model on OpenRouter is an open-weight one, DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017, with 59.2 trillion tokens.

在同一时期，开源权重模型的每周使用量增长了 60 多倍，而闭源模型仅增长了近 10 倍。本周，OpenRouter 上使用量最大的模型是一款开源权重模型——DeepSeek V4.1 Flash 1017，其处理的 Token 总量达到了 59.2 万亿。

Open-weight models cost about four times less on average: $0.28 per million input tokens and $1.62 per million output tokens, against $1.27 and $6.36 for closed models. The best open-weight models still trail the best proprietary ones on the hardest benchmarks.

开源权重模型的平均成本大约是闭源模型的四分之一：每百万输入 Token 为 0.28 美元，每百万输出 Token 为 1.62 美元，而闭源模型则分别为 1.27 美元和 6.36 美元。不过，在最严苛的基准测试中，顶尖的开源权重模型仍落后于最强的闭源模型。

Routing powered by OpenRouter. OpenRouter serves more than 800 trillion tokens a month to over 10 million users, across 600+ models from 80+ providers. Its routing is based on live measurements of every provider across that traffic.

由 OpenRouter 提供支持的路由系统：OpenRouter 每月为超过 1000 万用户提供超过 800 万亿 Token 的服务，涵盖了来自 80 多家服务商的 600 多个模型。其路由机制基于对所有服务商流量的实时监测。

Pricing. Pricing doesn't change: you pay the provider's price on your Hugging Face bill, and Hugging Face doesn't add a markup on top of it. PRO users get $2 of credits every month, and Team and Enterprise organizations get $2 per seat.

关于定价：价格保持不变。你只需在 Hugging Face 账单中支付服务商的费用，Hugging Face 不会额外加价。PRO 用户每月可获得 2 美元的额度，团队和企业组织则按席位每月获得 2 美元额度。

Breaking change: custom provider keys. Requests to language models now go through OpenRouter and are billed to your Hugging Face account or organization, so you can no longer use your own API key for a provider with these models.

重大变更：自定义服务商密钥。发送给语言模型的请求现在通过 OpenRouter 处理，并计入你的 Hugging Face 账户或组织账单，因此你无法再针对这些模型使用自己的服务商 API 密钥。
