A humanoid robotics company backed and advised by President Donald Trump’s son, Eric Trump, has reportedly missed payroll and may be on the brink of collapse after a funding shortfall. The Silicon Valley startup, Foundation Future Industries, claims it is developing and testing robots for military roles on battlefields from Ukraine to Gaza.

一家由唐纳德·特朗普总统之子埃里克·特朗普支持并担任顾问的人形机器人公司，据报道因资金短缺未能发放工资，可能正处于崩溃边缘。这家位于硅谷的初创公司“未来工业基金会”（Foundation Future Industries）声称，他们正在开发并测试用于从乌克兰到加沙等战场军事任务的机器人。

Foundation failed to pay employees for the payroll period ending on October 30, anonymous sources told CNBC. The startup’s staff is especially angry because Foundation’s co-founder and CEO, Sankaet Pathak, supposedly borrowed $4.6 million in company funds to buy a luxury home overlooking San Francisco Bay about a year ago.

据匿名消息人士向美国消费者新闻与商业频道（CNBC）透露，该基金会未能支付截至10月30日的工资周期内的员工薪水。员工们感到格外愤怒，因为该公司的联合创始人兼首席执行官桑凯特·帕塔克（Sankaet Pathak）据称在大约一年前挪用了460万美元的公司资金，用于购买一栋俯瞰旧金山湾的豪宅。

This comes as Pathak and Foundation’s leadership are still looking to raise funding to support a $3 billion valuation, based on their promise to develop a humanoid robot ready for combat duty and capable of wielding various weapons. The company has demonstrated a humanoid robot named the Phantom MK-1 and is touting an upcoming robot called the Phantom MK-2.

与此同时，帕塔克和基金会的领导层仍在寻求融资，以支撑其30亿美元的估值。这一估值基于他们开发一款能够执行战斗任务并能操作各种武器的人形机器人的承诺。该公司此前展示了一款名为“幻影MK-1”（Phantom MK-1）的人形机器人，并正在宣传即将推出的“幻影MK-2”。

Foundation has touted its humanoid robots for roles in warehouses and other workplaces—but has courted media coverage by claiming it is developing humanoid robots for battlefield use. Now CNBC’s reporting based on “people with direct knowledge of the matter” indicates that insiders worry the startup could soon collapse without additional funding.

该基金会曾吹捧其人形机器人在仓库和其他工作场所的应用，但通过声称正在开发用于战场的机器人来博取媒体关注。目前，据CNBC援引“直接了解此事的人士”报道，内部人士担心，如果没有额外资金注入，这家初创公司可能很快就会倒闭。

Foundation’s business claims Eric Trump holds an equity stake in Foundation and was announced as its chief strategy advisor in March 2026, according to the Financial Times. Investment funds linked to both Eric Trump and Donald Trump Jr. are involved with multiple military technology companies that have received Pentagon loans and contracts worth more than $3.2 billion as of July 2026, according to The Washington Post.

据《金融时报》报道，该基金会的商业声明称，埃里克·特朗普持有该公司的股权，并于2026年3月被宣布担任其首席战略顾问。据《华盛顿邮报》报道，与埃里克·特朗普和小唐纳德·特朗普有关联的投资基金参与了多家军事技术公司，截至2026年7月，这些公司已获得总额超过32亿美元的五角大楼贷款和合同。

After Trump claimed that Foundation had “just secured a $24 million contract with the Pentagon” during the Fox Business interview in April, Sen. Elizabeth Warren (D-Mass.) responded by questioning whether the Pentagon is “just a cash machine from Trump’s kids now” in a social media post. “This looks like corruption in plain sight,” Warren wrote.

在4月份接受福克斯商业频道采访时，特朗普声称该基金会“刚刚获得了五角大楼一份2400万美元的合同”。对此，参议员伊丽莎白·沃伦（马萨诸塞州民主党人）在社交媒体上发文质疑五角大楼是否“现在只是特朗普孩子们的提款机”。沃伦写道：“这看起来就是赤裸裸的腐败。”

But publicly available evidence does not back up Trump’s claim about Foundation directly receiving US government funding. Federal spending records show that Foundation holds no publicly listed contracts, according to The San Francisco Standard. Instead, the company Boardwalk Robotics received four contracts worth $3.22 million prior to being acquired by Foundation in late 2024.

然而，公开证据并不支持特朗普关于该基金会直接获得美国政府资助的说法。据《旧金山标准报》报道，联邦支出记录显示，该基金会没有任何公开列出的合同。相反，是Boardwalk Robotics公司在2024年底被该基金会收购之前，获得了四份总价值322万美元的合同。

Meanwhile, the Institute for Human & Machine Cognition—a Florida-based nonprofit research institute and research partner to Boardwalk Robotics—secured a $24.8 million Navy contract in 2025, The San Francisco Standard reported. This was not the first time that Foundation’s leadership and investors have made unsupported statements. In 2024, General Motors pushed back on Foundation’s claims that it was about to get an investment from the Detroit automaker, according to CNBC.

与此同时，《旧金山标准报》报道称，位于佛罗里达州的非营利性研究机构、同时也是Boardwalk Robotics研究合作伙伴的“人类与机器认知研究所”（Institute for Human & Machine Cognition），在2025年获得了一份价值2480万美元的海军合同。这并非该基金会领导层和投资者首次发表未经证实的言论。据CNBC报道，2024年，通用汽车曾反驳了该基金会关于即将获得这家底特律汽车制造商投资的说法。

Shaky battlefield testing claims. On November 11, 2026, Mike LeBlanc, co-founder and president of Foundation Future Industries, told the Financial Times that the company was in talks with the Israeli military to provide humanoid robots for patrol and combat duties in Hamas tunnels beneath Gaza. He also described taking the company’s Phantom MK1 humanoid robot along to tour some of the tunnels.

关于战场测试的说法站不住脚。2026年11月11日，未来工业基金会联合创始人兼总裁迈克·勒布朗（Mike LeBlanc）告诉《金融时报》，公司正在与以色列军方商谈，为加沙地带哈马斯地道内的巡逻和战斗任务提供人形机器人。他还描述了自己带着公司的“幻影MK-1”人形机器人参观部分地道的情况。

However, the Israeli defense ministry declined to comment when the Financial Times reached out. LeBlanc also previously claimed that two of the company’s Phantom MK-1 units went to Ukraine for testing in frontline reconnaissance and logistics roles in February 2026. But Craig Smith, an independent journalist and host of the podcast Eye on AI, described tagging along during the delivery of the Phantom robots to Ukraine.

然而，当《金融时报》联系以色列国防部时，对方拒绝置评。勒布朗此前还声称，该公司两台“幻影MK-1”机器人于2026年2月前往乌克兰，用于前线侦察和后勤任务的测试。但独立记者、“Eye on AI”播客主持人克雷格·史密斯（Craig Smith）描述了他在“幻影”机器人运往乌克兰期间随行的情况。

He said that the robots never made it to Ukraine because they were stopped at the border with Poland. When questioned by Smith, LeBlanc only said that Foundation had conducted a pilot demonstration at an undisclosed location, without clarifying whether the robots ever reached Ukraine. LeBlanc also walked back a claim that Foundation had demonstrated its humanoid robots to the Ukrainian government organization Brave1.

他表示，这些机器人从未进入乌克兰，因为它们在波兰边境被拦下了。当被史密斯质问时，勒布朗仅表示该基金会在一个未公开的地点进行了试点演示，并未澄清机器人是否真的到达了乌克兰。勒布朗还收回了关于该基金会曾向乌克兰政府组织“Brave1”展示其人形机器人的说法。

Promotional videos showing Foundation’s humanoids have not provided evidence that the robots are ready for battlefield deployments. For example, a “mortar demo” video on the company website dated to May 2026 shows a person handing a mortar shell to one of the humanoid robots, which slowly lowers the shell over the muzzle of a mortar weapon before finally dropping it in.

展示该基金会人形机器人的宣传视频并未提供证据证明这些机器人已做好战场部署的准备。例如，公司网站上一段日期为2026年5月的“迫击炮演示”视频显示，一个人将一枚迫击炮弹递给其中一台人形机器人，机器人缓慢地将炮弹降低到迫击炮口上方，最后将其放入。

The real battlefield robots. Whatever happens to Foundation, humanoid robots remain largely unproven technology and have yet to fulfill company promises that they can become general-purpose robots handling many different tasks. Companies have just begun testing and deploying such robots in greater numbers within relatively controlled environments such as factories and warehouses.

真正的战场机器人。无论该基金会结局如何，人形机器人仍是一项很大程度上未经证实的技术，尚未实现公司承诺的成为能够处理多种任务的通用机器人。各公司才刚刚开始在工厂和仓库等相对受控的环境中，更大规模地测试和部署此类机器人。

Similarly, humanoid robots have not yet had any impact on the battlefield. Instead, pioneers in robotics warfare such as the Ukrainian military have focused on developing and deploying unmanned ground vehicles (UGVs)—robotic vehicles that roll along on wheels or tank-style caterpillar tracks.

同样，人形机器人尚未对战场产生任何影响。相反，乌克兰军队等机器人战争的先行者，专注于开发和部署无人地面车辆（UGV）——即依靠轮子或坦克式履带行进的机器人车辆。

While defending against the Russian invasion of Ukraine, the Ukrainian military has increasingly deployed more ground robots along with swarms of flying drones to make up for a shortfall in human soldiers compared to Russia. Such ground robots have typically been tasked with performing risky supply runs or evacuating wounded soldiers from frontline positions, but have also entered combat while wielding projectile weapons or even explosives when equipped as roving bombs. Most recently, Ukraine has deployed armed ground robots directly into combat through both.

在抵御俄罗斯对乌克兰的入侵时，乌克兰军队越来越多地部署地面机器人以及成群的飞行无人机，以弥补与俄罗斯相比在士兵人数上的不足。这些地面机器人通常被指派执行危险的补给任务，或从前线阵地撤离伤员，但也曾携带弹药武器进入战斗，甚至在被改装为巡回炸弹时携带爆炸物。最近，乌克兰已通过多种方式将武装地面机器人直接投入战斗。
