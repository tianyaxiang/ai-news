If your feed looks like mine, the lights go out tomorrow. November 17, 90 days, nationwide, maybe worldwide. There are countdown videos, a supposed Trump post telling people to stock three months of food, a Simpsons clip, and accounts telling you to pull your money out of the bank while you still can.

如果你的信息流和我的一样，明天灯就要熄灭了。11月17日，90天，全国范围，甚至可能是全球范围。网上充斥着倒计时视频、一条据称是特朗普发布的建议人们储备三个月食物的帖子、一段《辛普森一家》的片段，还有一些账户劝你趁现在赶紧把钱从银行取出来。

Here's what is actually scheduled for November 17: at 2:20 p.m. Eastern, FEMA and the FCC run a nationwide test of the Emergency Alert System. It plays on TV and radio for about a minute. It does not go to phones. If it has to be postponed, the backup date is December 3. (I used AI tools to help research and draft this post, and checked every claim against official statements, wire reports, and fact-checkers, linked or named below.)

11月17日实际安排的是：美国东部时间下午2:20，联邦紧急事务管理局（FEMA）和联邦通信委员会（FCC）将对紧急警报系统进行全国性测试。它会在电视和广播中播放约一分钟，不会发送到手机上。如果必须推迟，备用日期是12月3日。（我使用了人工智能工具来协助研究和起草这篇文章，并根据官方声明、通讯社报道和事实核查机构的信息核对了每一项主张，相关链接或名称见下文。）

So why does it feel like everything is falling apart? Because this week, a lot of things really did break. They just didn't break together. Where the 90-day blackout story came from The rumor is a collage. Each piece is real on its own. Glued together, they say something none of them say.

那么，为什么感觉一切都在分崩离析呢？因为本周确实有很多事情发生了故障，只是它们并非同时发生。关于90天停电的传言从何而来？这个谣言是一个拼贴画。每一部分单独来看都是真实的，但拼凑在一起后，它们所表达的内容却并非原意。

A Simpsons episode. The 2023 episode "It's a Blunderful Life" shows a blackout on November 17. It names no year, and the blackout in the episode is caused by a storm. Snopes points out that its "six days before Thanksgiving" detail fits 2023, not 2026, and France 24 notes the dates don't match.

《辛普森一家》的一集。2023年的剧集《It's a Blunderful Life》中展示了11月17日的一次停电。剧中没有提到年份，且停电是由风暴引起的。Snopes指出，其“感恩节前六天”的细节符合2023年而非2026年，法国24台也指出日期并不吻合。

A Pentagon program. The Defense Department's FORTRESS initiative is about making military bases able to run for 90 days without the public grid. It is about bases, not homes. Snopes covered it in October.

五角大楼的一个项目。国防部的“堡垒”（FORTRESS）倡议旨在使军事基地能够在没有公共电网的情况下运行90天。这是针对基地的，而不是针对家庭的。Snopes在10月份对此进行了报道。

Preparedness campaigns. The UK's "Be Ready" campaign and similar European advice tell households to keep 72 hours of supplies. That's ordinary storm advice, not a warning about a specific date.

应急准备宣传。英国的“做好准备”（Be Ready）运动和类似的欧洲建议告诉家庭储备72小时的物资。这是针对风暴的常规建议，而不是针对特定日期的警告。

A fake Trump post and fake videos. Lead Stories searched Trump's Truth Social archive and found no such post, and Snopes rated the videos fake: AI-altered clips that also pitched crypto tokens.

虚假的特朗普帖子和视频。Lead Stories搜索了特朗普在“真实社交”（Truth Social）上的存档，没有发现此类帖子；Snopes将这些视频评定为虚假：它们是经过人工智能篡改的片段，同时还在推销加密货币代币。

Crypto accounts. France 24 found crypto accounts claiming the test was cover for a cyberattack on banks, and some urged followers to pull their savings and buy crypto instead. If a post about a blackout ends with where to put your money, you've found the point of the post.

加密货币账户。法国24台发现一些加密货币账户声称测试是针对银行网络攻击的掩护，一些账户敦促追随者取出存款并购买加密货币。如果一篇关于停电的帖子最后落脚点在于如何投资你的钱，你就找到了这篇帖子的真实目的。

Snopes says it will formally rate the claim once November 17 has passed. PolitiFact, France 24, CNET, and others found no evidence of any planned blackout. In the UK, National Grid had to deny a separate rumor of a "Blackout Day" on November 19.

Snopes表示，一旦11月17日过去，他们将正式对这一说法进行评级。PolitiFact、法国24台、CNET等机构均未发现任何计划停电的证据。在英国，国家电网不得不辟谣了另一则关于11月19日“停电日”的传言。

What actually broke, day by day. Thursday, November 12: Microsoft 365. Outlook, Exchange, Teams, and other Microsoft 365 services failed for about a day across dozens of countries. Hospitals, courts, and governments lost email. In Germany, a hotel pool's chlorine alarm sent its warnings by email, the warning never arrived, and 15 guests were treated for exposure, according to the German broadcaster WDR.

实际上发生了什么故障，按天计算。11月12日星期四：Microsoft 365。Outlook、Exchange、Teams和其他Microsoft 365服务在数十个国家中断了约一天。医院、法院和政府部门失去了电子邮件功能。据德国广播公司WDR报道，在德国，一家酒店游泳池的氯气警报通过电子邮件发送警告，但警告从未送达，导致15名客人因接触氯气而接受治疗。

Microsoft's preliminary report blames a software defect introduced months earlier in a July fix. It stayed hidden until a traffic surge hit while nearly 14% of gateway capacity was offline. Microsoft said it was a technical issue, not a cyberattack. Its final report is due November 20.

微软的初步报告将原因归咎于几个月前在7月修复程序中引入的一个软件缺陷。它一直处于隐藏状态，直到在近14%的网关容量离线时遭遇流量激增才爆发。微软表示这是一个技术问题，而非网络攻击。其最终报告定于11月20日发布。

Friday, November 13: the FAA ground stop. At 3:54 p.m. Eastern, the FAA stopped departures nationwide "for reasons of safety" after radar and radio feeds became unstable at most of the country's en-route air traffic control centers. The stop lasted until about 8:15 p.m. FedScoop reported it was only the second nationwide ground stop since 2001.

11月13日星期五：联邦航空管理局（FAA）的停飞令。美国东部时间下午3:54，在全国大部分航路空中交通管制中心的雷达和无线电信号变得不稳定后，FAA出于“安全原因”在全国范围内停止了航班起飞。停飞一直持续到晚上8:15左右。据FedScoop报道，这是自2001年以来第二次全国性停飞。

The preliminary explanation is almost too small to believe. Contractors were replacing copper lines with fiber at an FAA site in El Dorado, Arkansas. A newly installed router began flooding the FAA's telecom network with connection attempts, and older equipment downstream couldn't cope. Isolating that router, and a second one in Amarillo, Texas, stabilized things.

初步解释几乎令人难以置信。承包商正在阿肯色州埃尔多拉多市的一个FAA站点将铜线更换为光纤。一台新安装的路由器开始向FAA的电信网络发送大量连接请求，导致下游的旧设备无法应对。隔离该路由器以及德克萨斯州阿马里洛的第二台路由器后，情况恢复了稳定。

Homeland Security Secretary Markwayne Mullin said there were no signs of an intrusion, while adding that investigators weren't formally ruling anything out. The FBI is examining the router as a precaution.

国土安全部部长马克韦恩·穆林表示没有入侵迹象，同时也补充说调查人员并未正式排除任何可能性。联邦调查局（FBI）正在检查该路由器以作预防。

Sunday, November 15: AWS. Amazon Web Services had about three hours of sign-in and connectivity problems in its Northern Virginia region early Sunday morning. It barely made the news. No sign of malicious activity was reported.

11月15日星期日：AWS。亚马逊网络服务（AWS）在周日清晨于北弗吉尼亚地区出现了约三个小时的登录和连接问题。这几乎没有成为新闻。没有报告显示存在恶意活动。

Monday, November 16: the banks. Chase customers saw slow and failed transactions online and at some branches for most of the day. JPMorgan blamed a software bug in an in-house hardware system and said it was "not a systemic issue." Customers at Bank of America and Wells Fargo noticed it too, because their transfers to and from Chase, including Zelle payments, got stuck. Zelle said its own service was not down.

11月16日星期一：银行。大通银行（Chase）的客户在当天大部分时间里发现线上和部分网点的交易缓慢或失败。摩根大通将原因归咎于内部硬件系统中的软件错误，并称这“不是系统性问题”。美国银行和富国银行的客户也注意到了这一点，因为他们与大通银行之间的转账（包括Zelle支付）卡住了。Zelle表示其自身服务并未中断。

Chase said it expected to resolve the issue by the end of the day, and systems were coming back online Monday afternoon. Monday, November 16: United Airlines. United's pilot-facing systems, including the one that gives pilots final aircraft weights for takeoff calculations, went down for roughly 90 minutes, according to the AP, delaying departures. United said there was no indication of a cyberattack.

大通银行表示预计在当天结束前解决问题，系统在周一下午开始恢复在线。11月16日星期一：联合航空。据美联社报道，联合航空面向飞行员的系统（包括为飞行员提供起飞计算所需最终飞机重量的系统）中断了约90分钟，导致航班延误。联合航空表示没有迹象表明发生了网络攻击。

Why five unrelated failures feel like one story. Think about the week you get a flat tire, the dishwasher leaks, and your phone screen cracks. It feels like the universe has it out for you. It doesn't. You have a lot of things that can break, each one breaks now and then, and sometimes three land in the same week.

为什么五起不相关的故障感觉像是一个故事？想想你轮胎漏气、洗碗机漏水、手机屏幕碎裂的那一周。感觉就像全世界都在针对你。其实不然。你有很多可能会坏的东西，每一件偶尔都会坏，有时三件坏事恰好发生在同一周。

Big systems work the same way. A useful sense of the base rate comes from an outage tracker my team keeps of major cloud provider outages: it averaged a bit under two a month this year, and this month's count was ordinary. What was unusual this week was that two genuinely rare events came back to back: one of Microsoft's worst email outages and a nationwide ground stop. After that, everything else got read through the same lens.

大型系统的运作方式也是如此。我团队维护的主要云服务商故障追踪器提供了一个有用的基准率：今年平均每月不到两次，而本月的故障次数属于正常范围。本周不寻常之处在于两起真正罕见的事件接连发生：微软最严重的电子邮件故障之一和全国性的停飞。在那之后，其他所有事情都被戴上了同样的有色眼镜来解读。
