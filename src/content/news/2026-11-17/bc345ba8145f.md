Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.17505 (cs) [Submitted on 12 Nov 2026] Title: Adversarial Tracking: Attacks on Image Sensors for Object Tracking in Autonomous Vehicles Authors: Han Wu, Alex Chan, Ekin Can Erkus, Tousif Rahman, Rishad Shafik.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.17505 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 12 日] 标题：对抗性追踪：针对自动驾驶车辆目标追踪中图像传感器的攻击 作者：Han Wu, Alex Chan, Ekin Can Erkus, Tousif Rahman, Rishad Shafik。

Abstract: Object tracking systems estimate the motion of surrounding vehicles by linking detected objects across video frames. In particular, Tracking-By-Detection (TBD) systems combine object detectors with motion prediction and data association to maintain object identities over time.

摘要：目标追踪系统通过在视频帧之间关联检测到的对象来估计周围车辆的运动。特别是，“检测即追踪”（TBD）系统将目标检测器与运动预测和数据关联相结合，以在时间推移中保持对象的身份。

However, neural network-based detectors are vulnerable to adversarial attacks that perturb input images, and their impact on downstream trajectory estimation is not fully characterized by detection accuracy alone. Therefore, this paper evaluates image-specific and image-agnostic attacks on YOLOv3 and YOLOv4 detectors paired with SORT, Deep SORT, Strong SORT, and OC-SORT tracking systems.

然而，基于神经网络的检测器容易受到扰动输入图像的对抗性攻击，且仅凭检测准确率无法完全表征其对下游轨迹估计的影响。因此，本文评估了针对 YOLOv3 和 YOLOv4 检测器（搭配 SORT、Deep SORT、Strong SORT 和 OC-SORT 追踪系统）的图像特定攻击和图像无关攻击。

The evaluation uses CARLA simulator and measures tracking quality through Multiple Object Tracking Accuracy (MOTA), Higher Order Tracking Accuracy (HOTA), and Identification F1 (IDF1). Under image-specific attacks, HOTA for YOLOv3-based trackers declines from 53.45-56.19% to 14.85-17.03%, while MOTA reaches -772.60% in the worst case.

评估使用 CARLA 模拟器，并通过多目标追踪准确度 (MOTA)、高阶追踪准确度 (HOTA) 和识别 F1 分数 (IDF1) 来衡量追踪质量。在图像特定攻击下，基于 YOLOv3 的追踪器的 HOTA 从 53.45-56.19% 下降至 14.85-17.03%，而 MOTA 在最坏情况下达到 -772.60%。

YOLOv4-based trackers exhibit greater robustness, while HOTA still declines to 25.06-26.86%. In addition, image-agnostic attacks can reduce the tracking accuracy using a single Universal Adversarial Perturbation (UAP), without generating a separate perturbation for individual frames.

基于 YOLOv4 的追踪器表现出更强的鲁棒性，但 HOTA 仍下降至 25.06-26.86%。此外，图像无关攻击可以使用单一的通用对抗扰动 (UAP) 来降低追踪准确度，而无需为单个帧生成单独的扰动。
