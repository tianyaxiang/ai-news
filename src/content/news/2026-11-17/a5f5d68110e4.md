No, not the rapper Coolio, but rather Dennis M. Moran. No dishonor intended for the real Coolio, the rapper, though arguably mine was harder. There I was, standing on the couch in my basement, pointing at the TV. Janet Reno is giving a press conference on CNN, vowing to bring these cyber terrorists to justice. My jaw is open, I’m laughing. I can’t believe it. She’s talking about my friend Dennis “Coolio” Moran, a 17-year-old.

不，不是那个说唱歌手 Coolio，而是丹尼斯·M·莫兰（Dennis M. Moran）。我无意冒犯真正的说唱歌手 Coolio，尽管可以说我认识的这位“更硬核”。当时我正站在地下室的沙发上，指着电视。珍妮特·雷诺正在 CNN 上召开新闻发布会，誓言要将这些网络恐怖分子绳之以法。我目瞪口呆，大笑不止。我简直不敢相信。她谈论的正是我的朋友，17 岁的丹尼斯·“Coolio”·莫兰。

But let’s back up. We were the #goonies on EFNet. We broke off from #doom, following our friend chunkk, the legendary Doom player (I still haven’t seen The Goonies movie though). Some of these people are still very close friends and at the time they were much closer friends than anyone I had IRL since they were computer elite and my local friends were all llamas. Above all, we were still very teenage and very dumb. They were fond of robotripping, since it was all the drugs teenagers could afford.

但让我们回顾一下。我们是 EFNet 上的 #goonies。我们从 #doom 频道脱离出来，跟随我们的朋友 chunkk，那位传奇的《毁灭战士》（Doom）玩家（尽管我至今还没看过《七宝奇谋》这部电影）。这些人中有些至今仍是我的挚友，而在当时，他们比我在现实生活中认识的任何人都要亲近，因为他们是计算机精英，而我当地的朋友都只是些“菜鸟”。最重要的是，我们当时还很年轻，也很愚蠢。他们喜欢滥用右美沙芬（robotripping），因为那是青少年唯一买得起的药物。

As a suburban kid from Queens from a loving supportive family I knew drugs were bad and would sit this stuff out. I had a job at a dialup ISP in Manhattan called nyct.net as a FreeBSD sys admin. I was moving up in the world. Others, not so much. The most extreme robotripper and psychonaut on #goonies was Coolio. He was also a lot of fun. He basically never spoke ill of anyone. Everyone loved him. Girls would fall for him just watching him say stuff on IRC over my shoulder.

作为一个来自皇后区、有着充满爱与支持的家庭的郊区孩子，我知道毒品不好，所以从不参与这些事。我在曼哈顿一家名为 nyct.net 的拨号 ISP 工作，担任 FreeBSD 系统管理员。我正在步步高升。其他人则不然。#goonies 里最极端的药物滥用者和迷幻药探索者是 Coolio。他也是个很有趣的人。他基本上从不说任何人的坏话。每个人都喜欢他。女孩们甚至只是通过我肩膀看他在 IRC 上聊天，就会被他吸引。

He was also the poorest friend I’ve ever had. He grew up in Wolfeboro, New Hampshire. His mom wasn’t in his life and his dad was, as far as I knew, a decent fellow, though they struggled to make ends meet and he had lived out of a tent for a period of his life. People mostly sort their friends and they get a picture of the world that’s very curated. Dennis (and indeed the rest of IRC) was my window out of my Queens NY bubble.

他也是我认识的最穷的朋友。他在新罕布什尔州的沃尔夫伯勒（Wolfeboro）长大。他的母亲不在他身边，据我所知，他的父亲是个正派的人，但他们生活拮据，他甚至有一段时间住在帐篷里。人们通常会筛选自己的朋友，从而获得一个经过精心修饰的世界观。丹尼斯（以及 IRC 上的其他人）是我走出纽约皇后区这个“泡沫”的窗口。

We did eventually hang out IRL. Dennis came to visit me in NYC and we went to Mindless Self Indulgence shows together, which were insanely fun. After one of them the (freakishly tall) lead singer Little Jimmy Urine was offering kisses for $5 (“VD free”). Dennis offered $20 over the heads of a bunch of clawing girls and he and Jimmy Urine made out. When he wasn’t talking about drugs or girls, he was talking about hacking.

我们最终在现实生活中见了面。丹尼斯来纽约看我，我们一起去看了 Mindless Self Indulgence 乐队的演出，那真是太有趣了。演出结束后，那位（高得离谱的）主唱 Little Jimmy Urine 以 5 美元的价格提供亲吻（“无性病”）。丹尼斯越过一群争抢的女孩，掏出 20 美元，结果他和 Jimmy Urine 接吻了。当他不谈论毒品或女孩时，他谈论的就是黑客技术。

We were all dabbling in computer stuff. We loved Doom and Quake and we had aspirations about becoming game developers. I bought a book on C and installed DJGPP and was trying my best to learn, but lost interest in games and pivoted to networks and coding on Linux. And also hacking. Though it had been out for years, I had just caught up with that Phrack article about Smashing the Stack for Fun and Profit and was curious about how to apply it.

我们当时都在涉猎计算机技术。我们热爱《毁灭战士》和《雷神之锤》（Quake），并立志成为游戏开发者。我买了一本 C 语言书，安装了 DJGPP，尽我所能去学习，但后来对游戏失去了兴趣，转而研究网络和 Linux 编程。当然还有黑客技术。尽管那篇关于“Smashing the Stack for Fun and Profit”的 Phrack 文章已经发表多年，但我当时才刚读到，并对如何应用它感到好奇。

Dennis was interested too, and we thought about how cool it’d be to write a PoC exploit together. Dennis found out about a BIND vulnerability with no PoC. We wrote one together. It worked! We were pumped. I thought we should promote internet health by publishing the PoC, since that was how you got people to fix bugs in those days. Dennis goes quiet for a few days. Then he comes back. Bro had been busy, he hacked into all of the vulnerable servers and recruited them into an insane botnet.

丹尼斯也很感兴趣，我们觉得一起编写一个概念验证（PoC）漏洞利用程序会很酷。丹尼斯发现了一个没有 PoC 的 BIND 漏洞。我们一起写了一个。成功了！我们兴奋不已。我以为我们应该通过发布 PoC 来促进互联网健康，因为在那个年代，这就是促使人们修复漏洞的方式。丹尼斯沉默了几天。然后他回来了。兄弟一直在忙，他入侵了所有存在漏洞的服务器，并将它们招募进了一个疯狂的僵尸网络。

I was shocked. WTF was he going to do with this? I thought we were just learning and there he goes hacking into half of the DNS servers on the internet! At least he patched BIND for them afterwards. Dennis reads about the CD Universe extortion attempt and sees a business idea. What if he combines his botnet with smurfing to do massive DDoS attacks? Then he could threaten to knock sites offline if they didn’t pay.

我惊呆了。他到底想干什么？我以为我们只是在学习，结果他竟然入侵了互联网上一半的 DNS 服务器！至少他事后为他们修补了 BIND。丹尼斯读到了关于 CD Universe 勒索事件的报道，并从中看到了一个“商机”。如果他将僵尸网络与 Smurf 攻击结合起来进行大规模 DDoS 攻击会怎样？然后他就可以威胁说，如果不付钱，就让网站下线。

I thought this sounded crazy but he insisted on trying. His reasoning was you can’t just cold email executives out of nowhere and try to extort them. You gotta build up a reputation first. So he flexed his muscle and took down Yahoo! He would say “watch this” and the sites would go down. First Yahoo, then buy.com and eBay, then etrade.com. He picked buy.com because it was the day of their IPO. What better way to show his power than ruin a company during the dot com bubble on their IPO day.

我觉得这听起来很疯狂，但他坚持要尝试。他的理由是，你不能平白无故地给高管发冷邮件去勒索他们。你必须先建立声誉。于是他展示了自己的实力，搞垮了雅虎！他会说“看着”，然后网站就瘫痪了。先是雅虎，然后是 buy.com 和 eBay，接着是 etrade.com。他选择 buy.com 是因为那天是他们的 IPO 日。还有什么比在互联网泡沫时期，在一家公司的 IPO 当天毁掉它更能展示力量的方式呢？

In our teenage chat room this all made sense. Like a lot of teenagers (and adults), Dennis thought wealth was often illegitimately acquired, and that it was only fair to take from the rich in the bullshit days of the dot com bubble. Thug life, kind of. Definitely worked on the girls, which was probably not healthy for him. To learn more about his views on wealth maybe we should go back to Wolfeboro.

在我们的青少年聊天室里，这一切都说得通。像许多青少年（以及成年人）一样，丹尼斯认为财富往往是通过非法手段获得的，在互联网泡沫的荒唐岁月里，劫富济贫是公平的。这算是一种“暴徒生活”吧。这招对女孩确实管用，但这对他来说可能并不健康。要了解他关于财富的观点，也许我们应该回到沃尔夫伯勒。

I visited twice, once while he was in jail and once just to hang out and party, and it was absolutely nothing like where I was from in Queens. According to Google it’s a small town of 6,000 known as being full of lakefront summer homes. The part I saw was very much not summer homes. It was houses in the sticks. The rich, such as Mitt Romney, were vacationing in his town while his family was, at times, living in a tent.

我曾去过那里两次，一次是在他入狱期间，另一次只是去聚会。那里和我来自的皇后区完全不同。根据谷歌搜索，这是一个拥有 6000 人口的小镇，以遍布湖畔度假屋而闻名。但我看到的地方绝非度假屋，而是乡下的破房子。像米特·罗姆尼（Mitt Romney）这样的富人在他的镇上度假，而他的家人有时却住在帐篷里。

Which brings us back to the basement. I’m standing on the couch, pointing at Janet Reno on the TV, laughing. She says they’ll find whoever did this. I know who did this. I know what he used, too, because I helped write it. Are we all going to jail?? Dennis wasn’t done though. He had previously rooted DNS servers and defaced dare.com and cwc.gov, but took the opportunity to deface rsa.com after the DDoS attacks. He did recruit his friends on IRC to help him write ad copy for the defacements. I am sad to

这又把我们带回了地下室。我站在沙发上，指着电视里的珍妮特·雷诺，大笑不止。她说他们会找到肇事者。我知道是谁干的。我也知道他用了什么，因为我参与了编写。我们都要坐牢了吗？？但丹尼斯还没收手。他之前曾获取过 DNS 服务器的 root 权限，并篡改了 dare.com 和 cwc.gov，但在 DDoS 攻击后，他又趁机篡改了 rsa.com。他确实招募了 IRC 上的朋友帮他撰写篡改页面的广告文案。我很遗憾……
