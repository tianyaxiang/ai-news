In *Get Huge*, the manosphere is here to make friends — with deadly consequences. *Get Huge: Looksmaxxing to Death* gets at the heart of how influencers are radicalizing impressionable men. By Charles Pulliam-Moore, Nov 16, 2026.

在《Get Huge》中，“男性圈”（manosphere）试图建立友谊，却带来了致命的后果。《Get Huge: Looksmaxxing to Death》直击网红如何让易受影响的男性走向激进化。作者：Charles Pulliam-Moore，2026年11月16日。

Before Clavicular and other manosphere streamers were introducing young guys to the concept of breaking their facial bones to become more attractive, there was an older generation of creators building massive followings with content focused on chemical biohacking. Were it not for steroid-obsessed influencers like Anthony “Tony Huge” Hughes launching YouTube channels over a decade ago, looksmaxxing might not have become such a prominent part of the digital media landscape in 2026.

在 Clavicular 和其他“男性圈”主播向年轻人介绍通过打断面骨来变帅的概念之前，老一代创作者早已通过专注于化学“生物黑客”的内容积累了大量粉丝。如果不是像安东尼·“托尼·休吉”·休斯（Anthony “Tony Huge” Hughes）这样痴迷于类固醇的网红在十多年前开设 YouTube 频道，那么“外貌最大化”（looksmaxxing）可能不会在 2026 年成为数字媒体领域如此显著的一部分。

Hughes is the gravitational center of *Get Huge: Looksmaxxing to Death* — Hulu’s new three-part docuseries from director Stacey Lee. The series is both a deep dive into Hughes’ strange life and a fascinating exploration of how perilous the creator economy can be in the age of manosphere and looksmaxxing influencers. Through Hughes’ ever-expanding orbit of creators, Lee is able to highlight how lonely, impressionable men are all too easily radicalized by what they see and post online.

休斯是 Hulu 三集纪录片《Get Huge: Looksmaxxing to Death》（由斯泰西·李执导）的核心人物。该系列既深入探讨了休斯离奇的生活，也引人入胜地揭示了在“男性圈”和“外貌最大化”网红时代，创作者经济可能有多么危险。通过休斯不断扩大的创作者圈子，导演李得以突显那些孤独、易受影响的男性是如何轻易地被他们在网上看到和发布的内容所激进化的。

*Get Huge* takes some time to touch on Hughes’ former life as a bankruptcy lawyer and supplement salesman who was investigated by the FDA for hawking unproduced performance-enhancing drugs. But most of the series takes place in Pattaya, Thailand, where Hughes spends his days producing content showcasing his extravagant life of nonstop partying and injecting substances that swell his muscles to huge proportions.

《Get Huge》花了一些时间回顾了休斯作为破产律师和补剂推销员的过去，他曾因兜售未经批准的增效药物而受到美国食品药品监督管理局（FDA）的调查。但该系列的大部分内容发生在泰国芭堤雅，休斯在那里每天制作内容，展示他那奢华的生活：不停地派对，并注射让肌肉膨胀到巨大比例的物质。

The men caught in Hughes’ aspirational fantasy aren’t just stateside fans who’ll never reach his muscle-y heights. He is also marketing himself to other established bodybuilding influencers who might be interested in joining his inner circle by following him to Thailand. These include a menagerie of biohacking / manosphere influencers who became vital parts of Hughes’ operation over the years.

陷入休斯这种“励志幻想”的男性不仅仅是那些永远无法达到他那种肌肉高度的美国粉丝。他还在向其他成熟的健美网红推销自己，吸引他们跟随他前往泰国，加入他的核心圈子。这些人包括一系列生物黑客和“男性圈”网红，他们多年来已成为休斯运作体系中的重要组成部分。

As *Get Huge* features clips of Hughes’ videos — the bulk of which are now only featured on Rumble and X following multiple account bans on YouTube and TikTok — you can see that he’s trying to sell his audience on his highly specific way of life. Hughes’ own videos make it seem like he’s living his dream surrounded by people who revere him. But with each interview, *Get Huge* makes it clear that for all of his posturing, Hughes has been living in something of a nightmare — one where many of his disciples have died under mysterious circumstances.

当《Get Huge》播放休斯的视频片段时（由于在 YouTube 和 TikTok 上多次被封号，这些视频目前大多仅在 Rumble 和 X 上发布），你可以看出他正试图向观众兜售他那种极其独特的生活方式。休斯自己的视频让他看起来像是生活在梦想中，被崇拜他的人所包围。但通过每一次采访，《Get Huge》都明确指出，尽管休斯摆出各种姿态，但他实际上生活在某种噩梦中——他的许多追随者都在神秘的情况下死亡了。

As these men explain why they were drawn to Hughes, you get the sense that looksmaxxing culture is a direct response to the widespread disaffection that followed the last decade of interconnected societal shifts. Hughes seems very much like an entrepreneur profiting from men’s loneliness by convincing them that their lives would be better if they were part of his community.

当这些人解释他们为何被休斯吸引时，你会感觉到“外貌最大化”文化是对过去十年社会变迁所引发的普遍不满的直接回应。休斯看起来非常像是一个利用男性孤独感获利的创业者，他通过说服这些人加入他的社区会让生活变得更好来牟利。

Part of what makes *Get Huge* such an unsettling watch is how relatively calm and collected Hughes and the other interviewees are as they begin to discuss how many people in their circle have died in recent years. Simply by letting these men speak frankly about their social dynamics, *Get Huge* reveals the manosphere as a space in which connections built on mogging and the objectification of women can quickly deteriorate into infighting and petty jealousy.

《Get Huge》之所以让人感到不安，部分原因在于休斯和其他受访者在讨论近年来圈内有多少人死亡时，表现得相对冷静和镇定。通过让这些人坦率地谈论他们的社交动态，《Get Huge》揭示了“男性圈”是一个建立在“压制他人”（mogging）和物化女性基础上的空间，这种关系很快就会恶化为内斗和琐碎的嫉妒。
