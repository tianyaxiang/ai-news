### What’s So Great About Digital Photo Frames?

### 数码相框到底好在哪里？

I've been testing digital photo frames for almost six years now and always sing their praises. These devices are designed to look like a regular photo frame, but with a screen inside that displays whichever photos you send to it. There's always an app involved to upload images, and you can also usually send photos over email, too. Both the email option and the app let you invite other family members to the frame, so multiple people can send images. It's perfect for families living in the same house or for long-distance family members who want to share their best pictures with Grandma.

我测试数码相框已经快六年了，一直对它们赞不绝口。这些设备的设计初衷是看起来像普通的相框，但内部配有一块屏幕，可以显示你发送给它的任何照片。通常需要通过应用程序上传图片，而且通常也可以通过电子邮件发送照片。电子邮件和应用程序选项都可以让你邀请其他家庭成员加入相框，这样多人都可以发送图片。这对于住在一起的家庭，或者想要与祖母分享精彩照片的异地亲人来说非常完美。

I also like that these devices are fairly intuitive and minimal. Digital photo frames simply connect to your Wi-Fi and display your photos. There are also options that can do multiple things if you want a multipurpose device in your home, but your frame doesn't have to do anything but show your photos.

我也喜欢这些设备相当直观且极简的特点。数码相框只需连接 Wi-Fi 即可显示你的照片。如果你想要一个多功能设备，市面上也有多种选择，但你的相框其实只需要负责展示照片就足够了。

### Is It Worth Buying a Digital Photo Frame?

### 数码相框值得买吗？

Do you have any photos on a digital device, like a smartphone, computer, or camera? Or does someone in your family have photos they could send to you or directly to the frame itself? Then yes, a digital photo frame is absolutely worth buying. If you have photos you take and want to see regularly or wish you had all your wedding photos on display instead of just the ones that fit on your wall, a digital photo frame will easily solve that for you.

你的数字设备（如智能手机、电脑或相机）里有照片吗？或者你的家人是否有可以发送给你或直接发送到相框的照片？如果是这样，那么数码相框绝对值得购买。如果你拍摄了一些想要经常查看的照片，或者希望展示所有的婚礼照片而不仅仅是挂在墙上的那几张，数码相框可以轻松帮你解决这些问题。

They're also great to purchase as a gift. I've given two different digital photo frames to my parents over the years and am currently helping my mother pick one out to gift my grandmother to enjoy photos in her nursing home. It's a great gift for young parents, too, who have camera rolls packed with photos of their new baby (or their fur baby!) and photo aficionados who will never have enough frames in their house for their work.

它们也非常适合作为礼物。多年来，我送给父母两个不同的数码相框，目前正在帮母亲挑选一个送给祖母，让她在疗养院里也能欣赏照片。对于那些相机胶卷里塞满了新生儿（或毛孩子！）照片的年轻父母，以及那些家里永远放不下作品的摄影爱好者来说，这都是一份很棒的礼物。
