AT&T on Friday accused Comcast of false advertising in a lawsuit challenging the marketing claim that Comcast’s cable broadband service and Wi-Fi is “fiber-powered.”

AT&T 周五在一项诉讼中指控康卡斯特（Comcast）进行虚假广告宣传，挑战其将康卡斯特的有线宽带服务和 Wi-Fi 称为“光纤驱动”（fiber-powered）的营销主张。

“This case challenges Comcast’s false advertising campaign for its residential Internet service, marketed under the Xfinity brand to consumers nationwide,” AT&T’s lawsuit said. “At the center of that campaign is Comcast’s claim that Xfinity Internet and its associated Wi-Fi service are ‘Fiber-Powered.’”

AT&T 的诉讼称：“本案旨在挑战康卡斯特针对其住宅互联网服务所开展的虚假广告宣传活动，该服务以 Xfinity 品牌向全国消费者进行营销。这场宣传活动的核心在于康卡斯特声称 Xfinity 互联网及其相关的 Wi-Fi 服务是‘光纤驱动’的。”

While Comcast has deployed plenty of fiber throughout the US, its cable Internet service makes the final connection to homes with coaxial cable. This results in upload speeds being far slower than download speeds. In contrast, fiber-to-the-home service can provide symmetrical upload and download speeds.

尽管康卡斯特在美国各地部署了大量光纤，但其有线互联网服务在连接到家庭的最后一段使用的是同轴电缆。这导致上传速度远低于下载速度。相比之下，光纤到户服务可以提供对称的上传和下载速度。

Comcast has deployed fiber-to-the-home in only small portions of its cable territory while advertising its service as fiber-powered in the majority of areas where cable makes the final connection.

康卡斯特仅在其有线电视覆盖区域的一小部分部署了光纤到户，却在绝大多数仍使用有线电缆进行最后连接的地区，将其服务宣传为“光纤驱动”。

AT&T’s lawsuit said that in describing its cable broadband as fiber-powered, Comcast “conveys to reasonable consumers the misleading impression that Xfinity Internet is a fiber-to-the-home service comparable to AT&T Fiber. In fact, the vast majority of Xfinity Internet runs over a hybrid fiber-coaxial (‘HFC’) network, where the critical ‘last mile’ connection to the customer’s home uses copper coaxial cable, not fiber.”

AT&T 的诉讼指出，康卡斯特在将其有线宽带描述为“光纤驱动”时，“向理性消费者传达了一种误导性印象，即 Xfinity 互联网是一种可与 AT&T 光纤相媲美的光纤到户服务。事实上，绝大多数 Xfinity 互联网运行在混合光纤同轴（HFC）网络上，其中连接到客户家庭的关键‘最后一英里’使用的是铜质同轴电缆，而非光纤。”

AT&T has its own history of ads that mislead customers about the nature of its network. It used to advertise 4G LTE service as “5GE” or “5G Evolution,” and displayed a 5GE network icon on 4G phones.

AT&T 自己也有过在网络性质上误导客户的广告历史。它曾将 4G LTE 服务宣传为“5GE”或“5G Evolution”，并在 4G 手机上显示 5GE 网络图标。

AT&T claimed that its 5G Evolution ads “served to educate customers about the billions of dollars AT&T has invested to give customers an outstanding experience,” but the BBB National Programs’ National Advertising Division found the term was likely to mislead consumers into thinking they had 5G connections.

AT&T 声称其 5G Evolution 广告“旨在让客户了解 AT&T 为提供卓越体验所投入的数十亿美元”，但 BBB 国家项目下的国家广告部门（NAD）认为，该术语很可能会误导消费者，使其认为自己拥有 5G 连接。

AT&T’s lawsuit said that Comcast’s fiber-powered claim “is literally false” because the cable firm’s HFC “architecture is fundamentally different from true fiber-to-the-home services like AT&T Fiber, which deliver fiber optic cables directly to the customer’s premises.”

AT&T 的诉讼称，康卡斯特的“光纤驱动”主张“在字面上是虚假的”，因为该有线电视公司的 HFC “架构与真正的光纤到户服务（如 AT&T Fiber）有着本质区别，后者将光缆直接铺设到客户场所。”

“Describing that service as ‘Fiber-Powered,’ ‘Powered by fiber,’ and ‘the most reliable, fiber-powered Wi-Fi’—particularly alongside Comcast’s own representations that fiber connections outperform ‘copper-based’ alternatives—is literally false or false by necessary implication,” AT&T wrote.

AT&T 写道：“将该服务描述为‘光纤驱动’、‘由光纤供电’以及‘最可靠的光纤驱动 Wi-Fi’——特别是在康卡斯特自身宣称光纤连接优于‘基于铜缆’的替代方案的情况下——在字面上是虚假的，或者在必然含义上是虚假的。”
