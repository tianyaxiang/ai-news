One flashpoint for the tech industry’s all-in push on large language models has been the sudden rush to turn investment money into hyperscale data centers. The rush hasn’t allowed anyone to plan how to supply the required electricity cleanly or with minimal impact on ratepayers. Instead, it has been difficult to even collect information on what’s being built.

科技行业在大型语言模型上全力投入的一个焦点，是近期将投资资金迅速转化为超大规模数据中心的浪潮。这股浪潮并未让任何人有时间规划如何以清洁方式或对纳税人影响最小的方式供应所需电力。相反，甚至连收集正在建设的项目信息都变得十分困难。

A new study has tallied energy use and estimated emissions for hyperscalers operating in the United States between May 2024 and April 2025. They weren’t able to differentiate between AI compute and more traditional data services, but it provides a clear point of reference to track moving forward.

一项新的研究统计了2024年5月至2025年4月期间在美国运营的超大规模数据中心的能源使用情况，并估算了其排放量。研究人员无法区分人工智能计算与更传统的服务，但这为未来的追踪提供了一个明确的参考点。

The team started with a list of 675 facilities from a commercial database based on news reports, company statements, and satellite imagery. They dropped any that didn’t have a reported electrical capacity or that they couldn’t verify was a suitably large building with the expected cooling and electrical connection equipment. That reduced the list to 403 data centers with an average electrical capacity of about 40 megawatts.

研究团队从一个商业数据库中获取了675个设施的名单，该数据库基于新闻报道、公司声明和卫星图像。他们剔除了那些没有报告电力容量，或者无法核实其是否为具备预期冷却和电力连接设备的足够大型建筑的设施。这使得名单缩减为403个数据中心，平均电力容量约为40兆瓦。

In a way, that makes this a very conservative estimate, since 272 facilities believed to be large data centers were left out. But if you’re interested in AI data centers, specifically, it’s also true that some of the facilities included may not fall in that category. That fact also introduces some uncertainty in the actual power usage of these data centers.

从某种程度上说，这是一个非常保守的估计，因为有272个被认为是大型数据中心的设施被排除在外。但如果你特别关注人工智能数据中心，事实也确实是，所包含的一些设施可能并不属于这一类别。这一事实也给这些数据中心的实际用电量带来了一些不确定性。

Relying on other research, they use a range—some conventional data centers run at an average of about 48 percent of capacity, while AI training loads have been running higher, at around 70 percent. Putting that together, the total power usage for the year by these 403 data centers was estimated at 68 to 99 terawatt-hours. That amounts to 1.5 to 2.2 percent of total US electricity use, on par with the total used by Wisconsin or Indiana.

研究人员参考了其他研究，使用了一个范围——一些传统数据中心的平均运行负荷约为容量的48%，而人工智能训练负载则较高，约为70%。综合来看，这403个数据中心全年的总用电量估计在68到99太瓦时之间。这相当于美国总用电量的1.5%到2.2%，与威斯康星州或印第安纳州的总用电量相当。

A recent Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory report estimated the total electricity use of all data centers of any kind in the US (minus crypto mining) at 76 terawatt-hours in 2018. In 2024, that number was up to 192 terawatt-hours. That means the hyperscale data centers identified by this study account for something like one-third to one-half of all data center energy use.

劳伦斯伯克利国家实验室最近的一份报告估计，2018年美国所有类型数据中心（不含加密货币挖矿）的总用电量为76太瓦时。到2024年，这一数字上升到了192太瓦时。这意味着本研究确定的超大规模数据中心约占所有数据中心能源使用量的三分之一到二分之一。

The researchers also wanted to estimate the greenhouse gas emissions associated with generating all that electricity. It’s fairly straightforward to get a reasonable number here—calculate the mix of generation in each data center’s region of the grid, and apply that CO2 per watt-hour rate to a data center’s electricity use. This yields 21 to 31 million metric tons of CO2 for the year. That’s around 0.5 percent of total US fossil fuel CO2 emissions.

研究人员还希望估算与产生这些电力相关的温室气体排放量。在这里得出一个合理的数字相当简单——计算每个数据中心所在电网区域的发电结构，并将该二氧化碳每瓦时排放率应用于数据中心的用电量。这得出的结果是全年排放2100万至3100万公吨二氧化碳。这约占美国化石燃料二氧化碳总排放量的0.5%。

These data centers aren’t located in areas of especially clean energy—their energy mix was only slightly cleaner than the US average. About 54 percent of the electricity was generated by fossil fuel power plants (dominantly natural gas), with 25 percent from renewables, and 21 percent from nuclear. This doesn’t include any “behind-the-meter” generation on-site, though.

这些数据中心并不位于清洁能源特别丰富的地区——它们的能源结构仅比美国平均水平略微清洁一些。约54%的电力由化石燃料发电厂（主要是天然气）产生，25%来自可再生能源，21%来自核能。不过，这并不包括现场的任何“表后”发电。

The xAI data center in Tennessee notably ran a fleet of natural gas turbines during this study’s time period, for example. Cleanview, which describes itself as a market intelligence company, has tracked a boom in on-site generation since early 2025, including 116 current or planned data centers. While this may not affect this study’s numbers much, it is becoming increasingly significant over time.

例如，田纳西州的xAI数据中心在研究期间显著运行了一组天然气涡轮机。自称市场情报公司的Cleanview追踪到，自2025年初以来，现场发电量激增，涉及116个现有或计划中的数据中心。虽然这可能不会对本研究的数据产生太大影响，但随着时间的推移，其重要性正日益凸显。

This matters because the types of generators being used are much less efficient than utility power plants, emitting 60 to 80 percent more greenhouse gases to produce the same amount of electricity. (Conversely, if a data center built solar generation instead, it could reduce the carbon footprint of its energy.) Although this study can’t pinpoint the energy use of AI data centers alone, repeating this analysis in subsequent years could reveal the growth related to the AI buildout—at least as long as that buildout lasts.

这一点很重要，因为所使用的发电机类型比公用事业发电厂效率低得多，产生相同电量所排放的温室气体要多出60%到80%。（相反，如果数据中心转而建设太阳能发电，则可以减少其能源的碳足迹。）尽管本研究无法单独确定人工智能数据中心的能源使用情况，但在后续年份重复此项分析可能会揭示与人工智能建设相关的增长——至少在建设持续期间是这样。
