Health officials in Pennsylvania and Wisconsin announced two more measles deaths late last week, bringing the national tally of confirmed measles deaths this year to 10. The total includes the deaths of at least four infants: two in Pennsylvania, one in New York, and one in Wisconsin.

宾夕法尼亚州和威斯康星州的卫生官员上周晚些时候宣布又有两例麻疹死亡病例，使今年全国确认的麻疹死亡总数达到 10 例。该总数包括至少四名婴儿的死亡：宾夕法尼亚州两例，纽约州一例，威斯康星州一例。

Wisconsin health officials said the latest measles death—the second for the state—was in a child. Pennsylvania’s health department did not reveal the age or other details of the latest death—the state’s seventh—besides revealing it was of an unvaccinated resident in Montour County. Overall, the seven deaths in Pennsylvania include two in Jefferson County, three in Lancaster County, and one in Mifflin, in addition to the new death in Montour. An eighth death was reported in Franklin County, but state health officials have not confirmed it.

威斯康星州卫生官员表示，最新的麻疹死亡病例（该州第二例）是一名儿童。宾夕法尼亚州卫生部门除了透露死者是蒙图尔县一名未接种疫苗的居民外，并未公布该州第七例死亡病例的年龄或其他细节。总体而言，宾夕法尼亚州的七例死亡病例包括杰斐逊县两例、兰开斯特县三例、米夫林县一例，以及蒙图尔县的新增死亡病例。富兰克林县也报告了第八例死亡，但州卫生官员尚未予以确认。

For all the states with deaths so far, health officials have noted that they’re the first deaths in decades. Multiple outbreaks are underway across the country, pushing the national tally of cases to nearly 5,500 across 47 states and jurisdictions, according to the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. Pennsylvania’s case count alone is over 1,500, and Wisconsin is reporting nearly 650.

对于迄今为止出现死亡病例的所有州，卫生官员指出，这些都是几十年来首次出现的死亡病例。据美国疾病控制与预防中心称，全国范围内正在发生多起疫情，使 47 个州和司法管辖区的全国病例总数达到近 5,500 例。仅宾夕法尼亚州的病例数就超过了 1,500 例，威斯康星州报告的病例数也接近 650 例。

With outbreaks that have been smoldering and flaring since last year, the US has reportedly lost its measles elimination status—as medical and public health experts have widely expected it would. A panel of experts for the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO), a regional branch of the World Health Organization, convened last week to review US measles data and assess its elimination status. The PAHO is set to announce its conclusion and the review’s other findings on Tuesday, November 17. But sources broke the news to The Wall Street Journal on Friday that the panel had found that the US had indeed lost its status.

由于疫情自去年以来一直处于潜伏和爆发状态，据报道，美国已经失去了麻疹消除状态——正如医学和公共卫生专家普遍预期的那样。泛美卫生组织（PAHO，世界卫生组织的一个区域分支机构）的一个专家小组上周召开会议，审查了美国的麻疹数据并评估了其消除状态。泛美卫生组织定于 11 月 17 日星期二宣布其结论和审查的其他结果。但消息人士周五向《华尔街日报》透露，该小组已认定美国确实失去了这一地位。

An elimination status is determined by international health authorities after a country has gone more than 12 months without continuous spread of a virus. The status is lost when epidemiological and laboratory data indicate that a chain of transmission involving a viral strain with an identical genotype and lineage has continued uninterrupted for 12 months or longer. The US earned its elimination status in 2000 after hard-fought vaccination campaigns over several decades.

消除状态是由国际卫生当局在某个国家超过 12 个月没有病毒持续传播后确定的。当流行病学和实验室数据表明涉及具有相同基因型和谱系的病毒株的传播链已连续 12 个月或更长时间未中断时，该状态即告丧失。美国在经过几十年的艰苦疫苗接种运动后，于 2000 年获得了消除状态。

But US vaccination rates have slipped below protective levels in recent years, fueled by the rise of anti-vaccine rhetoric and vaccine misinformation from fierce anti-vaccine advocates, including current US Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. As vaccination rates slipped, measles cases skyrocketed. The current case tally is the current case tally is the highest seen since 1991.

但近年来，由于反疫苗言论的兴起以及包括现任美国卫生部长小罗伯特·F·肯尼迪在内的激烈反疫苗倡导者散布的疫苗错误信息，美国的疫苗接种率已降至保护水平以下。随着疫苗接种率的下滑，麻疹病例激增。目前的病例总数是自 1991 年以来的最高水平。

In reporting the PAHO’s determination, the Journal quoted Noel Brewer, chair of an independent US committee who presented findings about the country’s measles outbreaks to PAHO, on the country’s lamentable status: “Runaway measles outbreaks in the United States, as well as the needless deaths, are all due to low measles vaccination coverage. We’ve gotten comfortable letting children be stopped by a deadly disease and die from it.”

在报道泛美卫生组织的决定时，《华尔街日报》引用了向该组织提交美国麻疹疫情调查结果的美国独立委员会主席诺埃尔·布鲁尔的话，谈到了该国令人遗憾的状况：“美国失控的麻疹疫情以及不必要的死亡，都是由于麻疹疫苗接种覆盖率低造成的。我们已经习惯于让孩子们被致命疾病阻断生命并因此死亡。”

The US Department of Health and Human Services did not immediately respond to requests to comment about the loss of the elimination status or to answer a question about how anti-vaccine rhetoric and misinformation, including that from Kennedy, has contributed. Medical and public health experts continue to sharply criticize Kennedy’s and the Trump administration’s poor handling of the ongoing severe outbreaks.

美国卫生与公众服务部没有立即回应关于失去消除状态的置评请求，也没有回答关于包括肯尼迪在内的反疫苗言论和错误信息如何导致这一结果的问题。医学和公共卫生专家继续严厉批评肯尼迪和特朗普政府对当前严重疫情的处理不当。

The CDC has not held press briefings in efforts to halt the vaccine-preventable illness and promote vaccination. Instead, Kennedy reportedly ordered the CDC to remove measles death data, and the CDC director declined to approve an urgent health alert about the life-threatening risks of measles during pregnancy.

美国疾控中心尚未举行新闻发布会，以努力遏制这种可通过疫苗预防的疾病并促进疫苗接种。相反，据报道，肯尼迪下令疾控中心删除麻疹死亡数据，而疾控中心主任拒绝批准发布关于孕期麻疹危及生命风险的紧急健康警报。
