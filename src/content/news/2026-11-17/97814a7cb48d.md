# Just 400 US Data Centers Use More Electricity Than Michigan

Just 400 hyperscale data centers connected in the US used as much electricity as the state of Michigan in a single year, new Harvard research has found.

哈佛大学的一项新研究发现，美国境内仅 400 个超大规模数据中心在一年内消耗的电力就相当于整个密歇根州的用电量。

The study, published Monday in Nature Sustainability, also finds that the majority of that electricity was generated using fossil fuels, and data center emissions are three times higher than they were before the AI boom.

这项周一发表在《自然-可持续发展》（Nature Sustainability）期刊上的研究还发现，这些电力大部分是由化石燃料产生的，且数据中心的排放量已达到人工智能热潮前水平的三倍。

Federal and state governments do not track data center use separate from the rest of the power sector, despite mounting projections that data centers are putting serious strain on the American power grid. Individual data centers’ power use is often considered proprietary business information, and data center operators and utilities do not have to disclose specific numbers to the public.

尽管越来越多的预测显示数据中心正给美国电网带来沉重负担，但联邦和州政府并未将数据中心的用电量从电力部门的其他部分中单独追踪。单个数据中心的用电量通常被视为商业机密，数据中心运营商和公用事业公司无需向公众披露具体数据。

The Harvard study specifically focuses on hyperscale data centers—the largest types of facilities, which have become flashpoints in community opposition across the country. While often used for AI inference or training, not all of the hyperscalers surveyed in this study are being used for AI.

哈佛大学的这项研究专门针对超大规模数据中心——这是规模最大的一类设施，已成为全国各地社区反对的焦点。虽然它们常用于人工智能的推理或训练，但本研究调查的超大规模数据中心并非全部用于人工智能。

“Hyperscalers have been growing fast, even before the AI boom,” says Jon Koomey, an energy researcher who was not a part of the study.

“即使在人工智能热潮之前，超大规模数据中心的发展速度就已经很快了，”未参与此项研究的能源研究员乔恩·库米（Jon Koomey）表示。

To assemble their dataset, the Harvard researchers put together a list of 403 hyperscale data centers around the country that were operational between May 2024 and April 2025 using a mix of industry research and satellite data. The team then modeled these facilities’ energy use in a variety of scenarios and calculated the energy mix of the regional grids to estimate individual facilities’ carbon emissions. Not all regions were created equal: Data centers in Virginia, Oregon, Ohio, and Iowa—the first of which is home to Data Center Alley—were responsible for more than 50 percent of the energy use in the study.

为了构建数据集，哈佛大学的研究人员结合行业研究和卫星数据，整理了一份 2024 年 5 月至 2025 年 4 月期间在美国境内运营的 403 个超大规模数据中心名单。随后，研究团队对这些设施在多种情境下的能源使用情况进行了建模，并计算了区域电网的能源结构，以估算各设施的碳排放量。并非所有地区的情况都相同：弗吉尼亚州、俄勒冈州、俄亥俄州和爱荷华州的数据中心占研究中能源使用量的 50% 以上，其中弗吉尼亚州还是“数据中心巷”（Data Center Alley）的所在地。

“Knowing for certain data centers how much they use over a year is a really useful thing,” says Koomey, who notes that a peer-reviewed study is more rigorous than some back-of-the envelope calculations. Koomey has been involved in a regular report from Lawrence Berkeley National Laboratory since 2007 that approximates the energy and water use of the industry. “Sometimes we know it, but sometimes it’s behind an NDA, and no one ever sees it.”

“确切了解数据中心一年的用电量是非常有用的，”库米说道。他指出，经过同行评审的研究比一些粗略的估算更为严谨。自 2007 年以来，库米一直参与劳伦斯伯克利国家实验室的一项定期报告，该报告旨在估算该行业的能源和水资源使用情况。“有时我们能掌握数据，但有时这些数据受到保密协议（NDA）的限制，无人能见。”

While based on recent data, the study may already be outdated—a reflection of how fast the data center industry is moving. The average size of a facility in the new study is 40 megawatts, which is relatively small compared to some of the data centers under development today. Developers are building campuses that are hundreds or even thousands of megawatts, and there are proposals for still larger data centers.

尽管该研究基于近期数据，但它可能已经过时了，这反映了数据中心行业的发展速度之快。新研究中设施的平均规模为 40 兆瓦，与当今正在开发的一些数据中心相比相对较小。开发商正在建设规模达数百甚至数千兆瓦的园区，且还有更大规模数据中心的提案。

“Some of the massive campuses being proposed today are substantially larger than the typical facility in our dataset,” says Gianluca Guidi, a postdoctoral research fellow at Harvard’s T. H. Chan School of Public Health and the lead author of the study.

“目前提出的一些大型园区规模远超我们数据集中的典型设施，”哈佛大学陈曾熙公共卫生学院博士后研究员、该研究的主要作者吉安卢卡·圭迪（Gianluca Guidi）表示。

The study also does not look at developers who choose to build power plants onsite, known as behind-the-meter power—a factor Koomey calls a “wild card” in accurately assessing total data center power use. Plans for behind-the-meter power generation have skyrocketed at proposed data center sites across the US over the past year, with as many as 189 gigawatts in the pipeline earmarked for data centers alone. Many of these plants rely increasingly on inefficient natural gas turbines. BloombergNEF estimated last week that if all of the behind-the-meter power plants in the development queue in the US in the US get built, it could increase overall US emissions by 10 percent.

该研究也没有考察那些选择在现场建设发电厂的开发商，即所谓的“表后电力”（behind-the-meter power）。库米称这一因素是准确评估数据中心总用电量的“变数”。过去一年里，美国各地拟建数据中心站点的“表后发电”计划激增，仅专门用于数据中心的规划容量就高达 189 吉瓦。其中许多电厂越来越依赖效率低下的天然气轮机。彭博新能源财经（BloombergNEF）上周估计，如果美国开发队列中所有的“表后电厂”都能建成，可能会使美国整体排放量增加 10%。

While the federal government hasn’t tracked data center energy use to date, that could change soon, and it could be a boon to researchers and the public. The Energy Information Agency, the arm of the federal government that tracks energy use across various sectors, conducted three pilot surveys of data center power use across 10 states this year. Those pilot surveys are now complete, EIA spokesperson Lee Tucker tells WIRED, and the agency is currently using them to design a potential voluntary survey for the sector.

虽然联邦政府迄今尚未追踪数据中心的能源使用情况，但这种情况可能很快会改变，这对研究人员和公众来说可能是一件好事。作为联邦政府负责追踪各行业能源使用情况的机构，能源信息署（EIA）今年在 10 个州对数据中心用电量进行了三次试点调查。EIA 发言人李·塔克（Lee Tucker）告诉《连线》（WIRED），这些试点调查现已完成，该机构目前正利用这些调查结果为该行业设计一项潜在的自愿性调查。
