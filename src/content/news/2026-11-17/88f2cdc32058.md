# Shareholders sue Intel, its CEO, and CFO after stock drops 28.6 percent in one day

Intel is being sued by shareholders, who are accusing the chipmaker of misleading them about the impact of the PC sales slump that contributed to a drop of 28.6 percent in the price of Intel’s stock on October 30. The lawsuit (PDF), filed on Friday in the San Jose, California, federal court, is seeking class-action certification and lists the City of Southfield Fire and Police Retirement System and “all others similarly situated” as plaintiffs.

英特尔正面临股东起诉，股东指控这家芯片制造商在个人电脑（PC）销售下滑的影响方面误导了投资者，这一下滑导致英特尔股价在10月30日下跌了28.6%。该诉讼于周五在加利福尼亚州圣何塞联邦法院提起，寻求集体诉讼认证，并将南菲尔德市消防与警察退休系统及“所有其他处于类似处境的人”列为原告。

In addition to Intel, Intel CEO Lip-Bu Tan and Intel’s EVP and CFO David Zinsner are listed as defendants. The plaintiffs claim that Intel made “materially false and misleading statements and omissions” between July 23 and October 29. They are seeking unspecified compensatory damages, plus legal and other associated fees, for shareholders during that period.

除英特尔外，英特尔首席执行官陈立武（Lip-Bu Tan）和英特尔执行副总裁兼首席财务官大卫·辛斯纳（David Zinsner）也被列为被告。原告声称，英特尔在7月23日至10月29日期间作出了“实质性虚假和误导性的陈述及遗漏”。他们正在为该期间的股东寻求未指明的赔偿金，以及法律和其他相关费用。

In the complaint, the shareholders argue that Intel misled them by failing to disclose: the full impact that the PC market sales slump would have on Intel’s third quarter and beyond; the impact of increasing PC component prices on PC sales for Intel’s OEM and distribution partners; that Intel was dealing with substrate supply constraints that would limit its ability to meet data center CPU demand; and “that any positive impacts to Intel’s business and financial results resulting from data center construction and other aspects of the AI boom were more than offset” by the PC sales slump.

在诉状中，股东们认为英特尔未能披露以下信息，从而误导了他们：PC市场销售下滑对英特尔第三季度及以后的全面影响；PC零部件价格上涨对英特尔原始设备制造商（OEM）和分销合作伙伴的PC销售的影响；英特尔正面临基板供应限制，这将限制其满足数据中心CPU需求的能力；以及“数据中心建设和人工智能热潮的其他方面对英特尔业务和财务业绩产生的任何积极影响，都被PC销售下滑所抵消”。

“[S]tatements regarding Intel’s business, operations, financial results, and ability to capitalize on the boom for AI-related infrastructure spending were materially false and misleading when made, and such statements did not accurately reflect Intel’s then-present business and prospects,” the law firm representing the plaintiffs, Robbins Geller Rudman & Dowd LLP, said in a statement on Friday.

代表原告的律师事务所Robbins Geller Rudman & Dowd LLP在周五的一份声明中表示：“关于英特尔业务、运营、财务业绩以及利用人工智能相关基础设施支出热潮的能力的陈述，在作出时是实质性虚假和误导性的，这些陈述并未准确反映英特尔当时的业务和前景。”

On October 29, Intel announced what the complaint calls “disastrous” fiscal Q3 earnings. During the quarter, revenue for Intel’s Client Computing and Physical AI Group (CCPG) business declined 5 percent year over year. At the time, Tan pointed to an “unprecedented memory crunch” and manufacturing supply shortages hurting the PC market.

10月29日，英特尔公布了诉状中所称的“灾难性”第三季度财报。该季度，英特尔客户端计算与物理人工智能集团（CCPG）业务的收入同比下降了5%。当时，陈立武指出，“前所未有的内存紧缺”和制造供应短缺正在损害PC市场。

The next day, Intel’s shares fell 28.6 percent in “the largest single-day price decline for Intel common stock in over 50 years,” the complaint notes. Intel hasn’t publicly commented on the lawsuit.

诉状指出，次日，英特尔股价下跌28.6%，这是“英特尔普通股50多年来单日最大跌幅”。英特尔尚未就此诉讼发表公开评论。
