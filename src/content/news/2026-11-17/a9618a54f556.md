Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.17563 (cs) [Submitted on 12 Nov 2026] Title: Benchmarks Don't Talk Back: Learning Through Self-Disagreement Authors: Soheil Abbasloo, Peng Cheng.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.17563 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 12 日] 标题：基准测试不会反驳：通过自我分歧进行学习 作者：Soheil Abbasloo, Peng Cheng。

Abstract: A benchmark usually measures a language model alone with a question, and deployment rarely looks like that: answers get contested, and models give up correct ones under counter-reasoning that is itself wrong. We show that what fails there is not a shortfall of accuracy but a capability in its own right -- knowing when to hold a position and when to yield to a better one -- which we call dialectical competence.

摘要：基准测试通常仅通过问题来衡量语言模型，而实际部署情况却并非如此：答案会受到质疑，模型会在本身错误的推论下放弃正确的答案。我们证明，这里失败的并非准确率的不足，而是一种独立的能力——即知道何时坚持立场，何时屈服于更好的立场——我们将其称为辩证能力。

It stands on first-answer accuracy without following from it: a model can be made materially better at answering and no better at this, and training for accuracy alone can drift against it. Nor can it be bought at inference, by self-reflection or by sampling more.

它建立在首答准确率的基础上，但并不直接源于此：一个模型可以在回答方面取得实质性进步，但在这种能力上却毫无提升，且仅针对准确率进行的训练可能会使其偏离这种能力。此外，这种能力也无法在推理阶段通过自我反思或增加采样来获得。

Taking a cue from the dialectical tradition in philosophy, in which productive opposition arises within a position rather than being imposed on it from outside, we hypothesize that this capability can be trained from opposition the model supplies itself. It states a position, generates the strongest case against that position -- its antithesis -- rules on whether the case stands, and is trained on that ruling, with no second model at training or at deployment.

借鉴哲学中的辩证传统，即富有成效的对立产生于立场内部而非从外部强加，我们假设这种能力可以通过模型自身提供的对立面来训练。模型陈述一个立场，生成针对该立场的最强论据（即其对立面），判定该论据是否成立，并根据该判定进行训练，在训练或部署过程中无需第二个模型。

We call this learning through Self-Disagreement: reinforcement learning from verifiable rewards throughout, where the same check that scores an answer against its reference scores the ruling too, so no reward model, no preference data and no human annotation enter anywhere.

我们将此称为通过“自我分歧”进行学习：即全程基于可验证奖励的强化学习，其中用于根据参考答案对回答进行评分的检查机制同样用于对判定进行评分，因此无需任何奖励模型、偏好数据或人工标注。

The capability is trained rather than elicited: weak in the base model, it arrives with the reward, is not paid for out of first-answer accuracy, and holds against challengers the model never trained against, including ones stronger than itself. The opposition a model needs in order to improve, it turns out, need not come from outside.

这种能力是训练出来的，而非诱导出来的：它在基础模型中很弱，随着奖励的引入而产生，并不以牺牲首答准确率为代价，并且能够抵御模型从未训练过的挑战者，包括比自身更强的挑战者。事实证明，模型改进所需的对立面并不一定来自外部。
