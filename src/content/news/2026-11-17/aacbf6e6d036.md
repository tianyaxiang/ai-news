# Many of Amazon’s Black Friday and Cyber Monday “deals” offered no savings

A blockbuster new investigation from Groundwork Collaborative exposes how Amazon shoppers may be getting hosed during the retail giant’s much-touted Black Friday and Cyber Monday sale events. The report, Deal or Decoy? Amazon’s Deceptive Discounts, examines products the retailer showcased as some the “best” discounts of the year to shoppers hunting for bargains during these sale events. A closer look at hundreds of these “deals” finds that Amazon frequently offered shoppers the same price, or in some cases a higher price, during recent holiday sale events than they could have paid just weeks earlier.

Groundwork Collaborative 的一项重磅调查揭露了亚马逊购物者在这一零售巨头大肆宣传的“黑色星期五”和“网络星期一”促销活动中可能遭受的欺骗。这份名为《交易还是诱饵？亚马逊的欺骗性折扣》的报告，审查了该零售商在这些促销活动中向寻求优惠的购物者展示的所谓“年度最佳”折扣产品。对数百个此类“交易”的深入研究发现，亚马逊在近期的假日促销活动中，经常向购物者提供与几周前相同的价格，甚至在某些情况下价格更高。

The report’s authors, Breyon Williams, Elizabeth Pancotti, and Lindsay Owens, find that nearly 1 in 3 of these Amazon “deals” offered no real savings – the exact same products were sold for the same price or less in the month before the sale. During Black Friday and Cyber Monday sales in the past two years, prices for nearly 1 in 10 products advertised as “deals” were higher than what customers could have paid for the products in the month leading up to the sales.

该报告的作者 Breyon Williams、Elizabeth Pancotti 和 Lindsay Owens 发现，近三分之一的亚马逊“交易”并没有带来真正的节省——完全相同的产品在促销前一个月以相同或更低的价格出售。在过去两年的“黑色星期五”和“网络星期一”促销活动中，近十分之一被宣传为“交易”的产品，其价格高于顾客在促销前一个月购买这些产品时可能支付的价格。

Groundwork’s analysis examined the prices of more than 350 products included on Amazon’s own deal pages for the Black Friday and Cyber Monday sale events in 2024 and 2025. These pages promote these deals as some of the “best” deals available. Amazon’s Black Friday 2025 deals page says, “With so many deals to explore, we’ve handpicked some of the best ones you’ll find.”

Groundwork 的分析审查了亚马逊 2024 年和 2025 年“黑色星期五”及“网络星期一”促销活动页面上 350 多种产品的价格。这些页面将这些交易宣传为“最佳”交易。亚马逊 2025 年“黑色星期五”交易页面写道：“有这么多交易可供探索，我们为您精选了一些您能找到的最佳交易。”

Groundwork Collaborative President and CEO and report co-author Lindsay Owens, shared the following reaction: “Millions of Americans wait all year for Black Friday and Cyber Monday to score the biggest bargains on stocking stuffers and household staples. Shoppers trust that the deals advertised to them deliver real savings because Amazon promotes them that way. But it turns out that these major sales aren’t all they’re cracked up to be. Instead, our investigation reveals that shoppers who wait for these major sale events may be playing rip-off roulette.

Groundwork Collaborative 总裁兼首席执行官、报告合著者 Lindsay Owens 分享了以下看法：“数百万美国人整年都在等待‘黑色星期五’和‘网络星期一’，以便在节日小礼品和家庭必需品上获得最大的优惠。购物者相信向他们宣传的交易能带来真正的节省，因为亚马逊就是这样宣传的。但事实证明，这些大型促销活动并不像人们吹嘘的那样。相反，我们的调查显示，等待这些大型促销活动的购物者可能是在玩‘宰客轮盘赌’。”

“Amazon dominates online shopping, and families count on its sale events to stretch their dollars. With the holiday shopping season upon us and prices continuing to rise, shoppers deserve to know they can trust every deal Amazon promises them. If Amazon can’t deliver that basic promise, lawmakers should hold them accountable.”

“亚马逊主导着在线购物，家庭依靠其促销活动来节省开支。随着假日购物季的到来，物价持续上涨，购物者理应知道他们可以信任亚马逊承诺的每一笔交易。如果亚马逊无法兑现这一基本承诺，立法者就应该追究他们的责任。”

Owens is the author of the recent book Gouged: The End of a Fair Price—And What That Means for Your Wallet, which exposes how corporations are sophisticated pricing schemes to overcharge consumers.

Owens 是近期出版的《被宰：公平价格的终结——以及这对你的钱包意味着什么》一书的作者，该书揭露了企业如何利用复杂的定价方案向消费者多收费。

ADDITIONAL BACKGROUND: The new report builds on Groundwork’s work exposing corporate pricing abuses and delivering results for American consumers.

背景补充：这份新报告建立在 Groundwork 揭露企业定价滥用行为并为美国消费者争取成果的工作基础之上。
