On the heels of the Nvidia scandal, the Trump administration is preparing to crack down on foreign data centers that are fueling China’s advancements in artificial intelligence by allowing remote access to otherwise restricted high-end chips necessary for AI training.

继英伟达（Nvidia）丑闻之后，特朗普政府正准备对外国数据中心采取打击行动。这些数据中心通过提供对受限高端人工智能芯片的远程访问，助推了中国在人工智能领域的发展。

In a summary of the rule change, the Commerce Department said it was closing a loophole that previously allowed foreign rivals like China, Russia, and Iran to bypass export curbs on certain advanced computer chips by instead renting access to those chips from data centers in Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand, and Brazil.

美国商务部在一份规则变更摘要中表示，此举旨在堵住一个漏洞。此前，中国、俄罗斯和伊朗等外国竞争对手通过租用印度尼西亚、马来西亚、新加坡、泰国和巴西数据中心的计算能力，绕过了对某些先进计算机芯片的出口限制。

US rivals could possibly use that access to train and run advanced AI models “capable of supporting military- and intelligence-related activities that could threaten US national security or foreign policy interests,” the Commerce Department said.

美国商务部称，美国竞争对手可能会利用这种访问权限来训练和运行先进的人工智能模型，这些模型“能够支持可能威胁美国国家安全或外交政策利益的军事和情报相关活动”。

Among threats, the US fears rivals could use advanced AI to develop “more effective weapons of mass destruction and advanced conventional weapons, coordinated cyber-espionage and targeted cyber-disruption, algorithmic theft, and weaponized disinformation.”

在诸多威胁中，美国担心竞争对手会利用先进人工智能开发“更有效的大规模杀伤性武器和先进常规武器、进行协同网络间谍活动和针对性网络破坏、算法窃取以及武器化的虚假信息”。

Now, the rules have been tightened up so much that the South China Morning Post reported that, “as a result, any advanced chips made for a Chinese firm are assumed to be restricted, and factories must ask the US government for a license before shipping them.”

目前，相关规则已大幅收紧。据《南华早报》报道，“因此，任何为中国公司制造的先进芯片都被推定为受限产品，工厂在发货前必须向美国政府申请许可证。”

The rule change takes effect 90 days after it’s posted to the Federal Register. Moving forward, companies headquartered in any of those countries deemed to “present a higher-than-normal risk of diversion” to China, Russia, and Iran must apply for a license with strict know-your-customer controls when transferring high volumes of advanced chips.

该规则变更将在发布于《联邦公报》90天后生效。此后，总部位于被认定为对中国、俄罗斯和伊朗存在“异常高转移风险”国家的公司，在转让大量先进芯片时，必须申请许可证并执行严格的“了解你的客户”（KYC）管控。

If a license is granted, companies will have to “perform robust due diligence,” submitting monthly reports on transactions that thoroughly detail all parties involved, as well as their parent companies, and the final destination for chips sold.

如果获得许可，公司必须“进行严格的尽职调查”，提交月度交易报告，详细说明所有相关方及其母公司，以及所售芯片的最终目的地。

In a press release Sunday, the Commerce Department described the rule change as “designed to ensure that existing restrictions on access to sensitive American technology cannot be circumvented through remote computing arrangements involving data centers in third countries.”

在周日的一份新闻稿中，商务部将此次规则变更描述为“旨在确保现有的敏感美国技术访问限制，不会通过涉及第三国数据中心的远程计算安排而被规避”。

An exception was carved out for companies providing computing power “exclusively used for commercial recommender systems.” The Department said it was acting to “prevent certain foreign actors from leveraging advanced computing power based on US technology to threaten US national security and foreign policy interests.”

规则中为提供“专门用于商业推荐系统”计算能力的公司设立了例外。商务部表示，此举是为了“防止某些外国行为体利用基于美国技术的先进计算能力来威胁美国的国家安全和外交政策利益”。

Without the intervention, officials fear that China could develop a military or economic edge, The New York Times reported. Trump officials clash on new rule.

据《纽约时报》报道，官员们担心，如果不进行干预，中国可能会在军事或经济上获得优势。特朗普政府官员在这一新规上产生了分歧。

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigned after admitting to an affair with a woman, Alice Huang, suspected of serving as a Chinese spy. Congress is probing if Alice Huang used her personal relationship with Jensen Huang to help her former company, Megaspeed, become one of Nvidia’s biggest customers, then used that position to smuggle Nvidia’s restricted AI chips into China.

英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋在承认与一名涉嫌担任中国间谍的女子爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang）有染后辞职。国会正在调查爱丽丝·黄是否利用她与黄仁勋的私人关系，帮助其前公司Megaspeed成为英伟达最大的客户之一，并利用这一地位将英伟达受限的人工智能芯片走私到中国。

China has denied that Alice Huang was a spy, and Megaspeed has denied that smuggling occurred, while launching its own internal probe. Megaspeed is a Singaporean data center firm whose primary business is cloud computing and AI infrastructure.

中国否认爱丽丝·黄是间谍，Megaspeed也否认发生了走私行为，并已启动内部调查。Megaspeed是一家新加坡数据中心公司，其主要业务是云计算和人工智能基础设施。

Prior to the rule change, selling remote access to AI servers to servers to customers in China was perfectly legal, NYT noted. But now, the Commerce Department has given firms like Megaspeed 90 days to either obtain licenses or face future export restrictions.

《纽约时报》指出，在规则变更之前，向中国客户出售人工智能服务器的远程访问权限是完全合法的。但现在，商务部已给予Megaspeed等公司90天的时间，要么获得许可证，要么面临未来的出口限制。

Whether or not to add more chip curbs has been a polarizing debate in the Trump administration. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent reportedly butted heads with Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick.

是否增加更多芯片限制在特朗普政府内部引发了极具争议的辩论。据报道，财政部长斯科特·贝森特（Scott Bessent）与商务部长霍华德·卢特尼克（Howard Lutnick）发生了冲突。

When the Megaspeed news broke, Bessent pushed for curbs, but Lutnick reportedly appeared “too cozy” with Nvidia to move. Instead, Lutnick initially favored looser controls that Nvidia claimed would ensure global dominance of US technology.

当Megaspeed的消息传出时，贝森特推动实施限制，但据报道，卢特尼克与英伟达的关系“过于亲密”，不愿采取行动。相反，卢特尼克最初倾向于更宽松的管控，英伟达声称这将确保美国技术在全球的统治地位。

(A Commerce Department spokesperson told NYT that Bessent has never confronted Lutnick over his handling of Megaspeed.) Any companies found violating the rules could face a “range of consequences,” the Commerce Department said after reportedly bending to Bessent’s pressure campaign.

（商务部发言人告诉《纽约时报》，贝森特从未就卢特尼克处理Megaspeed的方式与其对质。）据报道，在屈服于贝森特的施压后，商务部表示，任何被发现违反规则的公司都可能面临“一系列后果”。

Those include possibly facing new import restrictions, stricter license requirements, downgrading their status in the US export control regime, or other changes to their company’s or their base country’s entity-list designations.

这些后果包括可能面临新的进口限制、更严格的许可要求、在美国出口管制体系中被降级，或其公司及所在国的实体清单认定发生其他变化。

New rule may not be aggressively enforced. Although the summary of the rule change said that “Commerce will be actively monitoring for countries that tolerate evasion” of the anti-diversion policy, it’s unclear how aggressively the Commerce Department might enforce the rule, NYT noted.

新规可能不会被强力执行。尽管规则变更摘要称“商务部将积极监控那些纵容规避”反转移政策的国家，但《纽约时报》指出，尚不清楚商务部执行该规则的力度会有多大。

Bessent has publicly criticized Lutnick for failing to sanction Megaspeed, and NYT reported that ultimately, enforcement of the stricter rules—which Bessent helped draft—“would be up to the Commerce Department.”

贝森特曾公开批评卢特尼克未能制裁Megaspeed。《纽约时报》报道称，最终，由贝森特参与起草的更严格规则的执行“将取决于商务部”。

“The real story here should be: Did Nvidia know their client, and was there a robust effort at Nvidia to make sure that there was no leakage and that they were selling to what were obviously pass-through shell companies?” Bessent said on a CNBC appearance. “I think it’s important that anyone at the company there be held accountable for that.”

“这里真正的问题应该是：英伟达是否了解他们的客户？英伟达是否做出了强有力的努力来确保没有泄露，以及他们是否在向明显的空壳公司销售产品？”贝森特在接受CNBC采访时说。“我认为，公司里的任何人对此负责都很重要。”

There’s even a push to oust Lutnick over his position on chips, but The Guardian reported that Donald Trump confirmed he has no plans to replace the AI industry-friendly commerce secretary.

甚至有人推动罢免卢特尼克，原因是他对芯片问题的立场。但《卫报》报道称，唐纳德·特朗普证实他没有计划更换这位对人工智能行业友好的商务部长。

On Monday, Trump told reporters that Lutnick was right to delay blacklisting Megaspeed, because “if you didn’t delay it… you could have a blow-up with China.” At the press gaggle, Trump aligned with China in saying that there’s no evidence yet that Alice Huang was a spy.
