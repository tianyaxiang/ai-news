Title: ‘The show is starting soon. Please turn on your headphones.’

标题：“演出即将开始。请打开您的耳机。”

Auracast broadcasts are an integral part of the storytelling in this off-Broadway show about love, connections, and hearing loss.

Auracast 广播是这部关于爱、人际联系和听力损失的外百老汇演出中，叙事不可或缺的一部分。

Up a few concrete steps and through the front door of a 200-year-old row house in New York City, I follow a crowd of people into the 178-seat SoHo Playhouse. As we filter to our seats, ushers hand each person a black drawstring bag with a pair of wired headphones connected to a small Auracast receiver. Then, soon after the play begins, we’re instructed to put them on.

走上几级水泥台阶，穿过纽约市一栋拥有 200 年历史的联排别墅前门，我跟随人群进入了拥有 178 个座位的 SoHo 剧场。当我们陆续入座时，引座员递给每个人一个黑色抽绳袋，里面装着一副连接着小型 Auracast 接收器的有线耳机。随后，在演出开始后不久，我们被要求戴上它们。

The headphones are an integral storytelling tool of Love Me — a one-man show from the minds of stand-up comedian and former Saturday Night Live writer Drew Michael and director Prashanth Venkataramanujam. It’s a show about love and the bid for connection, told in five chapters about different relationships in Michael’s life, bound by the threads of his hearing loss and self-discovery.

耳机是《Love Me》这部单人剧不可或缺的叙事工具，该剧由单口喜剧演员兼前《周六夜现场》编剧德鲁·迈克尔（Drew Michael）与导演普拉尚特·文卡塔拉马努贾姆（Prashanth Venkataramanujam）共同构思。这是一部关于爱与寻求联系的剧作，通过五个章节讲述了迈克尔生活中的不同关系，并以他听力损失和自我发现的经历作为贯穿始终的线索。

Love Me’s experimental sound design is possible thanks to Auracast, a technology based on Bluetooth that can broadcast a high-quality audio stream to any number of nearby devices. As the show follows Michael from childhood through adulthood, sound designer Daniel Kluger uses the headphones in combination with the theater speakers to create experiences that wouldn’t be possible with a traditional setup.

《Love Me》实验性的声音设计得益于 Auracast，这是一种基于蓝牙的技术，能够向任意数量的附近设备广播高质量音频流。随着演出从迈克尔的童年讲述到成年，声音设计师丹尼尔·克鲁格（Daniel Kluger）将耳机与剧场扬声器结合使用，创造出传统设备无法实现的体验。

Tinnitus ringing and hearing test tones play in the music between sections as subtle clues as the show builds to Michael’s epiphany about his hearing loss. Kluger also explores the world through varying levels of sonic clarity. Sound playing through the theater’s speakers is sometimes intentionally muffled, which is then further dulled by the headphones, giving the audience a sense of what Michael is hearing in those moments.

在各章节之间的音乐中，耳鸣声和听力测试音作为微妙的线索穿插其中，随着剧情推进，迈克尔最终对自己的听力损失有了顿悟。克鲁格还通过不同程度的声音清晰度来探索这个世界。通过剧场扬声器播放的声音有时会被刻意调得模糊，随后又被耳机进一步削弱，让观众感受到迈克尔在那些时刻所听到的声音。

But one sound that stays clear and pristine is Michael’s inner monologue, heard in the headphones throughout the show. The inner monologue consists of both prerecorded tracks and the live feed from Michael’s microphone on stage as he talks back and forth with himself. “Any time you’re in my head — anything in the headphones is coming through clearly,” Michael tells me after the show. “[We’re] trying to also highlight that the appeal of the inner world is such that it’s the highest fidelity space for this person. It’s more comfortable.”

但有一种声音始终保持清晰纯净，那就是迈克尔在整场演出中通过耳机传出的内心独白。内心独白既包含预录音轨，也包含迈克尔在舞台上通过麦克风进行的实时对话，他在与自己进行交流。“每当你进入我的脑海时，耳机里的任何声音都会清晰地传达出来，”迈克尔在演出结束后告诉我，“我们还试图强调内心世界的吸引力，因为它对这个人来说是保真度最高的空间。它更舒适。”

The different sound sources and treatments of the sound can lead to intentionally confusing scenes for the audience. During one, where Michael is doing a stand-up set at a comedy club, three sounds compete for attention: The theater speakers play the crowd’s reactions (including a heckler whose voice is sometimes muffled and sometimes clear); his prerecorded inner monologue reacting to the crowd plays in the headphones; and on stage, Michael is performing the set, with his mic muffled so our focus is on his inner monologue.

不同的声源和声音处理方式可能会给观众带来刻意营造的困惑感。在其中一场戏里，迈克尔正在喜剧俱乐部表演单口相声，三种声音争夺着观众的注意力：剧场扬声器播放着观众的反应（包括一个声音时而模糊时而清晰的起哄者）；耳机里播放着他预录的、对观众反应做出回应的内心独白；而在舞台上，迈克尔正在表演，他的麦克风声音被调得模糊，从而将我们的注意力集中在他的内心独白上。

It’s one of the definitive moments where the audience feels the constant concentration necessary to parse what’s happening when sounds aren’t clear and become overwhelming. But the biggest impact comes during a climactic scene the sound design has been building toward, a realization that also incorporates the visuals of a series of Michael’s audiograms over the years. “I had this massive breakthrough about the whole history of what I’ve been doing — the cognitive labor, the invisible to myself labor that was affecting every single minute of my life,” Michael says. “That was a huge moment.”

这是最关键的时刻之一，观众能感受到当声音不清晰且变得令人难以承受时，解析正在发生的事情需要持续的专注。但最大的冲击力来自于声音设计一直铺垫的高潮场景，这一顿悟还结合了迈克尔多年来一系列听力图的视觉呈现。“我对自己的整个经历有了巨大的突破——那些认知劳动，那些我自己都未曾察觉、却影响了我生命中每一分钟的劳动，”迈克尔说，“那是一个巨大的时刻。”

That’s when the audience is instructed to remove the headphones we’ve been wearing for nearly the entire show. As Michael comes to the recognition of his relationship with his hearing loss and the clarity of mind that gives him, what we’ve become used to for the entire show’s experience — the headphones — are now gone, as is that literal weight from our heads. It’s an emotional moment, one that I’ve thought about multiple times since seeing the show.

就在那时，观众被要求摘下我们几乎戴了整场演出的耳机。当迈克尔意识到自己与听力损失的关系，并因此获得内心的清明时，我们整个演出过程中已经习惯的东西——耳机——现在消失了，我们头上的那种物理重量也随之而去。这是一个感人的时刻，自看完演出后，我曾多次回想起它。

It also leads into the final act, where Michael begins to understand his own Deaf identity. “The deafness is not the culprit, it’s the denial of it.” he tells me. “My hearing currently has never been worse, but I’ve never been more self-affirmed or self-actualized or felt happier in myself. It’s a misconception, I think, about deafness as a tragedy, deafness as a deficit, deafness as a burden, deafness as something that plagues a person.”

这也引出了最后一幕，迈克尔开始理解他自己的聋人身份。“耳聋不是罪魁祸首，否认它才是。”他告诉我，“我目前的听力从未如此糟糕，但我从未像现在这样自我肯定、自我实现，也从未感到如此快乐。我认为，将耳聋视为悲剧、视为缺陷、视为负担、视为折磨人的东西，这是一种误解。”

The ability of Auracast to broadcast an audio stream that any number of headphones can connect to is what made Love Me a possibility. Any Auracast headphones can connect to the audience broadcast — I brought along the Nothing Pro 1 to test the ability and connected to the audience broadcast channel in seconds — but I used the provided Auri receiver and Austrian Audio headset for the performance. The show’s sound was designed with those in mind, and I didn’t want the differing isolation of the Nothing headphones to affect the intent of the experience.

Auracast 能够广播音频流并让任意数量的耳机连接，这使得《Love Me》的演出成为可能。任何 Auracast 耳机都可以连接到观众广播频道——我带了 Nothing Pro 1 来测试这一功能，并在几秒钟内就连接上了——但我还是使用了演出提供的 Auri 接收器和 Austrian Audio 耳机。该剧的声音设计是针对这些设备进行的，我不想让 Nothing 耳机不同的隔音效果影响到体验的初衷。
