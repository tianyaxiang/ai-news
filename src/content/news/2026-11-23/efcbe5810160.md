How Tamagotchis got us hooked on digital pets

塔麻可吉（Tamagotchi）是如何让我们沉迷于电子宠物的

On Version History: Why millions of people signed up to feed, change, and play with a beeping little keychain.

在《版本历史》（Version History）节目中：为什么数百万人会注册去喂养、清理并与一个会发出哔哔声的小钥匙扣玩耍。

If you were a kid in the late 1990s, there’s a near-100 percent chance you either had a Tamagotchi or knew someone who did. The tiny digital pets came out of nowhere and became an overnight sensation, as millions of kids signed up to parent a crying, hungry, constantly dirty creature.

如果你是 20 世纪 90 年代末的孩子，那么你几乎百分之百拥有过塔麻可吉，或者认识拥有它的人。这些微小的电子宠物突然出现并一夜成名，数百万孩子开始“领养”这些会哭泣、饥饿且总是弄得很脏的生物。

Until they went to school, anyway, at which point their parents were given some new responsibilities they neither asked for nor wanted.

当然，直到孩子们去上学时，他们的父母便被迫承担起了一些他们既没要求也不想要的额外责任。

On this episode of Version History, we dig deep into the story of the Tamagotchi. That story has its 30th anniversary this week, even though the real craze only lasted a few years.

在本期《版本历史》中，我们深入探讨了塔麻可吉的故事。尽管真正的狂热只持续了几年，但本周恰逢该产品诞生 30 周年。

David Pierce is joined by The Verge’s Mia Sato and Victoria Song to discuss the origins of the Tamagotchi, the product’s many iterations, its incredible engagement tactics, and whether any of us could tolerate the Tama experience three decades later. (We… could not.)

David Pierce 与 The Verge 的 Mia Sato 和 Victoria Song 一起讨论了塔麻可吉的起源、产品的多次迭代、其令人难以置信的互动策略，以及我们中是否有人能在三十年后忍受这种体验。（我们……不能。）
