The blockchain remembers everything. Most users don't investigate enough. One of the biggest misconceptions in cryptocurrency is that a confirmed transaction is automatically a safe transaction. It isn't. A blockchain explorer can tell you: Which address sent the funds. Which address received them. When the transaction occurred. How much was transferred. Which smart contract or token was involved. But raw transaction data does not automatically explain the context.

区块链记录了一切，但大多数用户调查得不够深入。加密货币领域最大的误区之一是认为“已确认的交易”自动等同于“安全的交易”，事实并非如此。区块链浏览器可以告诉您：发送资金的地址、接收资金的地址、交易发生的时间、转账金额以及涉及的智能合约或代币。然而，原始交易数据并不能自动解释其背后的背景。

For example, consider this simplified transaction path: Wallet A → Wallet B → Wallet C → Your Wallet. Your immediate counterparty is Wallet C. However, Wallet B might be associated with a high-risk service, while Wallet A could have previously interacted with a known scam cluster. The important question is no longer simply: "Did I receive the USDT?" It becomes: "What is the risk associated with the funds I just received?" This distinction matters to exchanges, OTC desks, payment processors, merchants, and ordinary crypto users.

例如，考虑这条简化的交易路径：钱包 A → 钱包 B → 钱包 C → 您的钱包。您的直接交易对手是钱包 C。然而，钱包 B 可能与高风险服务相关联，而钱包 A 可能曾与已知的诈骗团伙有过交互。重要的问题不再仅仅是“我收到 USDT 了吗？”，而是变成了“我刚收到的资金存在什么风险？”这种区别对于交易所、场外交易（OTC）平台、支付处理商、商户和普通加密货币用户来说至关重要。

TRC20 transactions are widely used for payments, exchange deposits, international transfers, and peer-to-peer activity. That convenience comes with an important operational challenge: large transaction volumes can make manual investigation difficult. Three issues deserve particular attention.

为什么波场（TRON）上的 USDT 需要特别关注？USDT 存在于多个区块链网络中，但波场已成为稳定币转账的重要环境。TRC20 交易被广泛用于支付、交易所充值、国际转账和点对点活动。这种便利性带来了一个重要的运营挑战：巨大的交易量使得人工调查变得困难。有三个问题值得特别注意。

Indirect exposure: A wallet does not need to interact directly with a suspicious address to deserve further investigation. Risk can appear several transactions away. This is why transaction graph analysis is more useful than looking at a single incoming transfer. Counterparty risk: A wallet address is not a complete description of the person or organization controlling it. An address might be associated with an exchange, an individual, an OTC intermediary, or a service with an uncertain risk profile. Understanding the counterparty can be just as important as understanding the transaction itself.

间接风险暴露：钱包无需直接与可疑地址交互也可能需要进一步调查。风险可能出现在几笔交易之外，这就是为什么交易图谱分析比仅查看单笔转账更有用。交易对手风险：钱包地址并不能完整描述控制它的人或组织。一个地址可能与交易所、个人、场外交易中介或风险状况不明的服务相关联。了解交易对手与了解交易本身同样重要。

Cross-chain movement: Cryptocurrency does not necessarily remain on the network where it first appeared. Assets can move through bridges and other cross-chain infrastructure. Consequently, investigating only the latest transaction on one network may leave important parts of the history unexplored. What should a practical crypto AML check include? A useful screening process should go beyond a simple blacklist lookup. Here is a practical framework.

跨链移动：加密货币不一定会留在其最初出现的网络上。资产可以通过跨链桥和其他跨链基础设施进行转移。因此，仅调查单一网络上的最新交易可能会遗漏历史中重要的部分。实用的加密货币反洗钱检查应包含什么？有效的筛查流程应超越简单的黑名单查询。以下是一个实用的框架。

Step 1: Identify the network and token. Always establish which blockchain and token standard are involved. TRC20 USDT and ERC20 USDT should not be treated as interchangeable transaction records. Step 2: Check direct exposure. Compare the address against relevant sanctions information, known illicit wallet labels, fraud intelligence, and available freeze-list records. Step 3: Investigate transaction history. Look beyond the immediate counterparty. Review connected wallets and suspicious transaction patterns where reliable data is available.

第一步：识别网络和代币。始终确定涉及的区块链和代币标准。TRC20 USDT 和 ERC20 USDT 不应被视为可互换的交易记录。第二步：检查直接风险暴露。将地址与相关的制裁信息、已知的非法钱包标签、欺诈情报和可用的冻结名单记录进行比对。第三步：调查交易历史。超越直接交易对手，在有可靠数据的情况下，审查关联钱包和可疑的交易模式。

Step 4: Evaluate the risk in context. A connection to a high-risk address is a reason to investigate, not automatically proof that the wallet owner has committed wrongdoing. Consider the distance between transactions, the quality of the underlying intelligence, the amount involved, and the nature of the relationship. Step 5: Preserve the evidence. For businesses, an AML process should produce a reviewable record: the address, network, timestamp, findings, risk indicators, and decision. This is particularly important when compliance teams need to explain why a transaction was accepted, escalated, or rejected.

第四步：在背景中评估风险。与高风险地址的关联是调查的理由，而非钱包所有者已犯罪的自动证明。需考虑交易之间的距离、底层情报的质量、涉及金额以及关系的性质。第五步：保存证据。对于企业而言，反洗钱流程应生成可审查的记录：地址、网络、时间戳、调查结果、风险指标和决策。当合规团队需要解释为何接受、升级或拒绝某笔交易时，这一点尤为重要。

Risk scores are useful. Blind trust in them is not. A numerical risk score can make large-scale screening easier. For example, a 0–100 scale can help teams prioritize wallets that require attention. However, a score should never replace investigation. Two addresses with identical scores may have very different risk profiles. One might have an indirect connection to a suspicious service. Another might have a direct match against a sanctions designation. Those are not equivalent situations. Good AML workflows should explain why an address received a particular score, which sources contributed to the result, and what additional investigation is appropriate. The goal is not to produce a frightening number. The goal is to make a better-informed decision.

风险评分很有用，但盲目信任则不然。数值化的风险评分可以简化大规模筛查。例如，0-100 的评分标准可以帮助团队优先处理需要关注的钱包。然而，评分绝不能取代调查。两个评分相同的地址可能具有截然不同的风险状况：一个可能与可疑服务有间接联系，另一个可能直接匹配制裁名单。这些情况并不等同。良好的反洗钱工作流程应解释地址为何获得特定评分、哪些来源促成了该结果，以及需要进行何种额外调查。目标不是产生一个令人恐慌的数字，而是做出更明智的决策。
