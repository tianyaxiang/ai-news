Some of our favorite Switch 2 games are cheaper through Black Friday. Nintendo and several retailers are offering the best deals yet on a variety of Switch 2 games.

我们最喜爱的一些 Switch 2 游戏在“黑色星期五”期间价格更优惠。任天堂和多家零售商正在为各种 Switch 2 游戏提供迄今为止最优惠的价格。

Black Friday is a great time to expand your game library because it’s a chance to catch deals that aren’t normally happening throughout the year. That’s true for Nintendo Switch 2 games, as well. In addition to a $50 off sale on one of its console bundles, Nintendo is also knocking $20 off some of the Switch 2’s best titles.

“黑色星期五”是扩充游戏库的好时机，因为这是一个抓住全年难得一见的折扣机会。对于任天堂 Switch 2 游戏来说也是如此。除了其中一款主机套装优惠 50 美元外，任天堂还对其部分 Switch 2 热门游戏降价 20 美元。

Some of the most notable games include Donkey Kong Bananza, which is $49.99 (usually $69.99) at Amazon, Target, and Best Buy, and Super Mario Party Jamboree Plus Jamboree TV for $59.99 (usually $79.99) at Amazon, Walmart, and Best Buy.

其中最值得注意的游戏包括《Donkey Kong Bananza》，在亚马逊、塔吉特 (Target) 和百思买 (Best Buy) 的售价为 49.99 美元（通常为 69.99 美元）；以及《Super Mario Party Jamboree Plus Jamboree TV》，在亚马逊、沃尔玛和百思买的售价为 59.99 美元（通常为 79.99 美元）。

Amazon, Best Buy, Walmart, and GameStop are offering discounts on several more Switch 2 games, though their respective end times and availability varies. Nintendo shared that some game sales will end on November 29th. Even if deals dry up for physical games on your wishlist, digital game deals are happening through Nintendo’s eShop until 11:59 PM PT on Wednesday, December 2nd, 2026.

亚马逊、百思买、沃尔玛和 GameStop 正在为更多 Switch 2 游戏提供折扣，尽管它们各自的结束时间和库存情况各不相同。任天堂表示，部分游戏促销将于 11 月 29 日结束。即使你愿望清单上的实体游戏优惠活动结束，任天堂 eShop 的数字版游戏优惠仍将持续至太平洋时间 2026 年 12 月 2 日星期三晚上 11:59。
