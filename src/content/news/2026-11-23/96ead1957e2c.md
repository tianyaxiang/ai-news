# We haven’t seen the last of the major tech outages

我们还没有看到重大技术故障的终结。

Microsoft and the US government agree there are more outages to come.

微软和美国政府一致认为，未来还会有更多的故障发生。

Over the last week or so, we have seen a nationwide FAA ground stop, a multi-day Outlook outage that brought entire companies to a standstill, and a banking outage at JPMorgan Chase. But the consensus is that there are more to come.

在过去一周左右的时间里，我们目睹了美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）全国范围内的停飞、导致整个公司陷入瘫痪的 Outlook 多日故障，以及摩根大通的银行系统故障。但各方共识是，未来还会有更多此类事件发生。

Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton told CNN: “The American people should be prepared for a great deal of investment in upgrading our systems; they should be prepared for investment in cybersecurity, and they should be prepared that any time there is a step change in technology, a step change in both compute and applications, there will be bumps in the road.”

国家情报总监杰伊·克莱顿（Jay Clayton）告诉美国有线电视新闻网（CNN）：“美国人民应该做好准备，在升级系统方面进行大量投资；他们应该为网络安全投资做好准备，并且应该做好心理准备，即每当技术发生跨越式变革，无论是计算还是应用出现重大升级时，道路上总会出现坎坷。”

Microsoft President Brad Smith issued a similar warning about further outages on CBS News’ Face the Nation with Margaret Brennan, but sounded a much more ominous tone.

微软总裁布拉德·史密斯（Brad Smith）在哥伦比亚广播公司（CBS）新闻节目《面对国家》（Face the Nation）中与玛格丽特·布伦南（Margaret Brennan）对话时，就未来可能发生的故障发出了类似的警告，但语气要严峻得多。

“I think we have to be prepared, frankly, for the coming months because this isn’t the type of thing that just gets done in a couple of weeks … The whole information infrastructure of the world is in effect being upgraded right now.”

“坦率地说，我认为我们必须为接下来的几个月做好准备，因为这不是那种几周内就能解决的问题……实际上，全球整个信息基础设施目前正在进行升级。”

While the outages over the last week were believed to be caused by cyberattacks, Smith said that AI has made it easier to find flaws, “and then you can either use that knowledge to patch the code, or if you’re a bad actor, you can use that knowledge to try to hack into systems,” he said.

虽然过去一周的故障被认为是由网络攻击引起的，但史密斯表示，人工智能使得发现漏洞变得更加容易，“你可以利用这些知识来修补代码，或者如果你是恶意行为者，你也可以利用这些知识试图入侵系统，”他说。

The last few months have also seen an increase in attacks on government and commercial systems, including those at the USDA, JP Morgan, and supermarkets.

过去几个月里，针对政府和商业系统的攻击也有所增加，其中包括美国农业部（USDA）、摩根大通和各大超市的系统。

Whether outages are brought on by AI-assisted hackers or human error during the rush to patch an aging system, one thing seems certain: there will be more in the coming months.

无论故障是由人工智能辅助的黑客造成的，还是在匆忙修补老化系统时的人为错误导致的，有一点似乎是肯定的：在接下来的几个月里，此类事件还会更多。
