The problem with treating AML as a final checkpoint Imagine a typical cryptocurrency transaction. A user receives digital assets, exchanges them, and sends the resulting funds to another wallet. Everything appears normal from a technical perspective. The transaction is confirmed. The network has done its job. But blockchain confirmation and transaction risk assessment answer two completely different questions. Blockchain confirmation tells us that a transaction was successfully processed according to network rules. AML analysis helps investigate whether the associated wallet activity or transaction history contains indicators that deserve additional attention. These are not interchangeable concepts. A technically valid transaction is not automatically a low-risk transaction. This distinction becomes especially important as cryptocurrency businesses scale and transaction volumes increase.

将反洗钱视为最后一道关卡的弊端。想象一笔典型的加密货币交易：用户接收数字资产、进行兑换，并将所得资金发送到另一个钱包。从技术角度来看，一切似乎都很正常。交易已确认，网络已完成其工作。但区块链确认和交易风险评估回答的是两个完全不同的问题。区块链确认告诉我们交易已根据网络规则成功处理；而反洗钱分析则有助于调查相关的钱包活动或交易历史是否包含值得额外关注的指标。这些概念不可互换。一笔技术上有效的交易并不自动等同于低风险交易。随着加密货币业务规模的扩大和交易量的增加，这种区别变得尤为重要。

Why a single risk indicator is never the whole story One of the biggest mistakes in cryptocurrency risk management is relying on isolated indicators. A wallet may interact with multiple addresses. Those addresses may have different transaction histories, connections, and levels of exposure. An individual risk signal does not necessarily tell the entire story. For example: An indirect connection to a flagged address does not automatically establish wrongdoing. A transaction involving a high-risk service deserves contextual investigation. Historical exposure may require additional analysis rather than an immediate conclusion. Different blockchain networks can present different investigative challenges. Good AML analysis requires more than looking at one warning and making a final decision. It requires understanding what the available information actually means. This is where the quality of the assessment process becomes important.

为什么单一风险指标永远无法说明全部情况。加密货币风险管理中最大的错误之一是依赖孤立的指标。一个钱包可能与多个地址交互，而这些地址可能具有不同的交易历史、关联和风险敞口水平。单一的风险信号并不一定能说明全部情况。例如：与被标记地址的间接关联并不自动构成不当行为；涉及高风险服务的交易需要结合背景进行调查；历史风险敞口可能需要进一步分析，而不是立即下结论。不同的区块链网络可能带来不同的调查挑战。良好的反洗钱分析不仅仅是查看一个警告并做出最终决定，它需要理解现有信息的实际含义。这就是评估流程质量变得重要的地方。

Building an AML-aware transaction workflow Instead of treating AML checks as an emergency measure, crypto businesses can integrate them into their regular operational processes. A practical workflow can be divided into four stages. Identify the transaction Establish which wallet address, transaction, or asset movement needs to be assessed. The more precise the initial information, the more useful the subsequent analysis can be. Examine available risk indicators Review the available blockchain-related information and identify potential exposure, suspicious connections, or other relevant signals. The objective is not to assume that every unusual pattern represents criminal activity. It is to identify situations that may require further investigation. Interpret the results Risk indicators should be considered in context. A responsible assessment distinguishes between direct and indirect exposure, available evidence, and uncertainty. This is particularly important when making decisions that affect customers or business operations. Make an informed decision Depending on the findings and applicable business policies, the next step may involve proceeding, requesting additional information, conducting enhanced due diligence, or escalating the case for manual review. The important point is that decisions should be supported by an actual assessment process rather than assumptions.

构建具备反洗钱意识的交易工作流程。加密货币企业不应将反洗钱检查视为应急措施，而应将其整合到日常运营流程中。一个实用的工作流程可分为四个阶段：识别交易，确定需要评估的钱包地址、交易或资产变动。初始信息越精确，后续分析就越有用。检查可用风险指标，审查现有的区块链相关信息，并识别潜在的风险敞口、可疑关联或其他相关信号。其目的不是假设每个异常模式都代表犯罪活动，而是识别可能需要进一步调查的情况。解读结果，风险指标应在背景中进行考量。负责任的评估会区分直接和间接风险敞口、现有证据以及不确定性。这在做出影响客户或业务运营的决策时尤为重要。做出明智决策，根据调查结果和适用的业务政策，下一步可能涉及继续交易、要求提供更多信息、进行强化尽职调查或将案件升级进行人工审核。重点在于，决策应由实际的评估流程支持，而非基于假设。

The engineering principle: make risk assessment repeatable There is an important lesson here for developers building cryptocurrency products. A reliable system should not depend entirely on an employee remembering to investigate a suspicious transaction. Risk assessment should be considered when designing the workflow itself. This does not necessarily mean introducing complicated procedures into every operation. It means defining clear assessment points, documenting relevant findings, establishing escalation criteria, and ensuring that decisions can be reviewed when necessary. For example, a crypto exchange, payment processor, or OTC desk may have different operational requirements. However, they all benefit from having a consistent approach to identifying and evaluating potential transaction risks. Repeatability is particularly valuable when teams grow, transaction volumes increase, and operational responsibilities become distributed.

工程原则：使风险评估可重复。对于构建加密货币产品的开发人员来说，这里有一个重要的教训：一个可靠的系统不应完全依赖于员工是否记得去调查一笔可疑交易。在设计工作流程本身时就应考虑风险评估。这并不一定意味着在每项操作中引入复杂的程序，而是指定义明确的评估点、记录相关发现、建立升级标准，并确保在必要时可以对决策进行审查。例如，加密交易所、支付处理商或场外交易（OTC）柜台可能有不同的运营要求，但它们都受益于拥有一种识别和评估潜在交易风险的一致方法。当团队壮大、交易量增加且运营职责变得分散时，可重复性尤为宝贵。
