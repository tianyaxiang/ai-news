Title: Toyota, Nissan, and Honda to standardize parts to lower costs

Japan’s three largest automakers have agreed to begin standardizing vehicle parts in an effort to reduce costs, according to Automotive News. The outlet reports that Japan Automobile Manufacturers Association chairman Koji Sato, who is also the vice chairman of Toyota, announced the decision at a news conference in Tokyo this week.

据《汽车新闻》报道，日本三大汽车制造商已同意开始对汽车零部件进行标准化，以降低成本。该媒体报道称，日本汽车工业协会主席、同时也是丰田汽车副董事长的佐藤恒治在本周于东京举行的新闻发布会上宣布了这一决定。

In the short term, the standardization efforts will begin with unified quality criteria for steel, consistent protocols for plastics and resins, and a universal color-coding scheme for wiring harnesses. In the long term, the Japan Automobile Manufacturers Association has plans to move to more components that cover the entire car. The three areas were chosen as the first to be standardized because they are parts that are generally not noticed by consumers, and they shouldn’t have an impact on any brand’s individual identity. All three are also areas that account for significant production costs for the automakers, meaning they can have an immediate impact.

短期内，标准化工作将从统一钢材质量标准、统一塑料和树脂协议，以及统一线束颜色编码方案开始。从长远来看，日本汽车工业协会计划将标准化范围扩大到覆盖整车的更多零部件。选择这三个领域作为首批标准化对象，是因为它们通常不被消费者察觉，且不会影响任何品牌的个性化特征。这三个领域同时也占用了汽车制造商大量的生产成本，意味着它们能产生立竿见影的效果。

The first discussions of standardization are set to include Japan’s domestic automotive parts suppliers, though international suppliers will be brought into the fold in time. The first standardization efforts are set to go into place next year, according to the AN report.

据《汽车新闻》报道，首轮标准化讨论将涵盖日本国内的汽车零部件供应商，不过国际供应商也将适时加入。首批标准化工作预计将于明年实施。

The push to simplify and lower costs comes as Japan’s seven automakers, including Toyota, Nissan, Honda, Mazda, Subaru, Mitsubishi, and Suzuki, work to fend off the increasingly competitive list of Chinese automakers. The automakers are said to hope that they can channel the money saved by homogenizing into new innovations.

此次推动简化流程和降低成本的举措，正值包括丰田、日产、本田、马自达、斯巴鲁、三菱和铃木在内的日本七大汽车制造商努力抵御日益激烈的中国汽车制造商竞争之际。据悉，这些车企希望将通过同质化节省下来的资金投入到新的创新中。

“We all share this strong sense of crisis, creating a momentum for change,” Sato said. “Based upon our sense of a crisis, we decided to choose these three areas.”

“我们都有强烈的危机感，这为变革创造了动力，”佐藤表示，“基于这种危机感，我们决定选择这三个领域。”
