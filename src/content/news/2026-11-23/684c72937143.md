Delta flights in and out of Atlanta delayed due to system outage. Major delays for Delta come just a week after a nationwide groundstop.

由于系统故障，达美航空往返亚特兰大的航班延误。在全国性停飞事件发生仅一周后，达美航空再次遭遇重大延误。

All Delta flights in and out of Atlanta are facing significant delays on Sunday due to a problem with the system that calculates a plane’s weight and balance. Hartsfield-Jackson Atlanta International Airport is Delta’s largest hub and one of the busiest airports in the world. While only Delta is impacted at the moment, disruptions at Hartsfield-Jackson could potentially affect other airports and airlines if delays continue to pile up.

周日，由于计算飞机重量和平衡的系统出现问题，达美航空所有往返亚特兰大的航班都面临严重延误。哈茨菲尔德-杰克逊亚特兰大国际机场是达美航空最大的枢纽，也是世界上最繁忙的机场之一。虽然目前只有达美航空受到影响，但如果延误持续堆积，哈茨菲尔德-杰克逊机场的中断可能会波及其他机场和航空公司。

The issues with Delta’s systems in Atlanta come just one week after an FAA outage led to a nationwide groundstop and thousands of canceled flights. According to The New York Times: The system that went offline calculates the total weight of an airplane, including its airframe, fuel, passengers and their baggage. The system also allows pilots to determine the plane’s center of gravity and whether the weight is evenly distributed — data points that help ensure safe passage.

达美航空在亚特兰大的系统问题发生在一周前美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）故障导致全国停飞并造成数千次航班取消之后。据《纽约时报》报道：离线的系统用于计算飞机的总重量，包括机身、燃油、乘客及其行李。该系统还允许飞行员确定飞机的重心以及重量是否分布均匀——这些数据点有助于确保飞行安全。

“Delta teams are using manual workarounds this morning to safely get flights departed while work continues to restore automation,” the airline said in a statement on Sunday.

达美航空在周日的一份声明中表示：“达美航空团队今天上午正在使用手动变通方案，以确保航班安全起飞，同时相关工作仍在继续以恢复自动化系统。”
