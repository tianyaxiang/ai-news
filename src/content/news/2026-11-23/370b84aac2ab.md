After I published my review of the HomeView, Apple’s first smart display, y’all still had a ton of questions about how it works as a smart speaker. So, I spent a bunch more time testing its audio capabilities and support for third-party music apps — and found a few more frustrations along the way. Here are the answers to all your burning questions. Have more? I’ll answer them in the comments.

在我发布了苹果首款智能显示屏 HomeView 的评测后，大家对于它作为智能音箱的表现仍有许多疑问。因此，我花了更多时间测试了它的音频功能以及对第三方音乐应用的支持，并在过程中发现了一些令人沮丧的问题。以下是针对您所有迫切问题的解答。还有其他问题吗？我会在评论区回复。

How does the HomeView sound compared to the HomePod Mini, Sonos One, and HomePod? The HomeView isn’t a replacement for a Sonos One, a full-size HomePod, or a similar-size speaker. Those are much better speakers. In size and sound, the HomeView is closer to the HomePod Mini. I tested it alongside the Mini 2 (which sounds marginally better than the first-gen Mini) and found the tabletop HomeView mostly comparable, with these caveats: The HomeView has better bass and is slightly louder, but it’s not as clear and is less room-filling.

HomeView 与 HomePod Mini、Sonos One 和 HomePod 相比音质如何？HomeView 无法替代 Sonos One、全尺寸 HomePod 或同类尺寸的音箱，后者的音质要好得多。在尺寸和音质上，HomeView 更接近 HomePod Mini。我将它与 Mini 2（音质比第一代 Mini 略好）进行了对比测试，发现桌面版 HomeView 的表现基本相当，但有几点需要注意：HomeView 的低音更好，音量也稍大，但清晰度稍逊，且声音的覆盖感不如后者。

The difference comes down to the hardware. The Mini 2 has a single full-range driver and two passive radiators, whereas the tabletop HomeView has two drivers in the display, plus a woofer and two passive radiators in its base. While the woofer adds some oomph, the HomeView’s display, which sits over the speaker base, partly covers it, affecting the sound compared to the Mini. Apple added an accelerometer to the display because tilting it changes the sound, so HomeView adjusts its EQ in real time based on the angle. Still, the Mini, while not as loud, fills a room better, thanks partly to its 360-degree sound. (The HomeView supports Spatial Audio with Dolby Atmos, which the Mini 2 doesn’t.)

差异归根结底在于硬件。Mini 2 拥有一个全频驱动单元和两个被动辐射器，而桌面版 HomeView 在显示屏中内置了两个驱动单元，底座中还有一个低音单元和两个被动辐射器。虽然低音单元增加了一些力度，但位于音箱底座上方的显示屏部分遮挡了声音，影响了其与 Mini 相比的音质。苹果在显示屏中加入了加速度计，因为倾斜角度会改变声音，所以 HomeView 会根据角度实时调整均衡器。尽管如此，Mini 虽然音量没那么大，但得益于其 360 度音效，声音的覆盖感更好。（HomeView 支持杜比全景声的空间音频，而 Mini 2 不支持。）

Can you pair two HomeViews for stereo sound? Two HomeViews can’t be paired for stereo sound, as the HomePod Minis can. I use a stereo pair in my office, and they pack a decent punch in the small space. No. While you can group HomeViews with any other AirPlay speakers to play music out of them all at once, you can’t pair two for a stereo setup or pair a HomeView with a HomePod or Mini.

可以将两台 HomeView 配对实现立体声吗？两台 HomeView 无法像 HomePod Mini 那样配对实现立体声。我在办公室使用了一对立体声组合，它们在小空间里表现相当出色。答案是不行。虽然您可以将 HomeView 与其他 AirPlay 音箱组合在一起同时播放音乐，但无法将两台 HomeView 配对成立体声系统，也无法将 HomeView 与 HomePod 或 Mini 配对。

Can you AirPlay music/podcasts to the HomeView, and does audio handoff work? You can AirPlay to the HomeView as with any other AirPlay speaker, and audio handoff works the same as it does on HomePods. Tap your phone to the HomeView, and whatever is playing transfers to the speakers and the screen. Handoff also works in reverse, letting you send any content back to your phone. One addition with the HomeView: You can now hand off video.

可以将音乐/播客通过 AirPlay 投放到 HomeView 吗？音频接力功能是否有效？您可以像使用其他 AirPlay 音箱一样通过 AirPlay 将内容投放到 HomeView，音频接力功能的工作方式与 HomePod 相同。将手机靠近 HomeView，正在播放的内容就会转移到音箱和屏幕上。接力功能也支持反向操作，让您可以将任何内容传回手机。HomeView 的一个新增功能是：现在可以接力视频了。

Can you set a default speaker for the HomeView? HomeView doesn’t let you set a default speaker or group to use instead of its built-in speaker. However, you can play audio to any AirPlay speaker or group of speakers. I tested this on HomePods, Sonos speakers, and a Samsung TV, and it worked smoothly. For native apps (Apple Music, Podcasts, and Safari), the HomeView remembers.

可以为 HomeView 设置默认音箱吗？HomeView 不允许您设置默认音箱或组合来替代其内置扬声器。不过，您可以将音频播放到任何 AirPlay 音箱或音箱组合。我在 HomePod、Sonos 音箱和三星电视上进行了测试，运行非常顺畅。对于原生应用（Apple Music、播客和 Safari），HomeView 会记住您的设置。
