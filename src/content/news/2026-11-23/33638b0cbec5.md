The blacklist problem Traditional wallet screening often starts with a straightforward question: Does this address appear on a known sanctions or risk database? This is a reasonable first step. Sanctions screening is essential, and identifying direct matches can prevent serious compliance problems. But blockchain activity is rarely that simple. Imagine three wallets: Wallet A receives funds directly from a known suspicious address. Wallet B receives funds from Wallet A several transactions later. Wallet C interacts with Wallet B through an ordinary-looking payment. A basic blacklist check might identify Wallet A while returning no direct match for Wallets B and C. Does that mean B and C are automatically safe? Of course not. It means the screening process needs more context. This is where transaction graph analysis becomes particularly valuable.

黑名单问题。传统的钱包筛选通常从一个简单的问题开始：该地址是否出现在已知的制裁或风险数据库中？这是合理的第一步。制裁筛选至关重要，识别直接匹配项可以防止严重的合规问题。但区块链活动很少如此简单。想象三个钱包：钱包 A 直接从一个已知的可疑地址接收资金。钱包 B 在几次交易后从钱包 A 接收资金。钱包 C 通过一笔看起来普通的支付与钱包 B 发生交互。基础的黑名单检查可能会识别出钱包 A，但对钱包 B 和 C 却无法返回直接匹配结果。这是否意味着 B 和 C 就自动安全了？当然不是。这意味着筛选过程需要更多的背景信息。这就是交易图谱分析变得尤为重要的地方。

Blockchain transactions form a network, not a list One of the most important concepts in blockchain AML is transaction graph analysis. Instead of examining addresses independently, an analytical system examines relationships between them. Think of every wallet as a node and every transfer as a connection. This creates a network that can reveal patterns that individual transaction records might hide. For example: A wallet repeatedly receives funds from multiple high-risk counterparties. Several addresses transfer funds through a common intermediary. Funds move rapidly across multiple wallets. A cluster of addresses exhibits unusual transaction behavior. These patterns do not automatically establish criminal activity. However, they can provide useful indicators for further investigation. The distinction matters. A responsible AML process should identify potential exposure without automatically treating every connected wallet as guilty.

区块链交易构成的是网络，而非列表。区块链反洗钱中最重要的概念之一是交易图谱分析。分析系统不再孤立地检查地址，而是检查它们之间的关系。将每个钱包视为一个节点，将每笔转账视为一个连接。这创建了一个网络，可以揭示单个交易记录可能隐藏的模式。例如：一个钱包反复从多个高风险交易对手处接收资金；多个地址通过共同的中介转移资金；资金在多个钱包间快速移动；一组地址表现出异常的交易行为。这些模式并不自动构成犯罪活动，但它们可以为进一步调查提供有用的指标。这种区别很重要。负责任的反洗钱流程应当识别潜在的风险敞口，而不是自动将每一个关联的钱包都视为有罪。

Why stablecoins deserve special attention Stablecoins have become an important component of cryptocurrency infrastructure. They are used for trading, payments, transfers, settlements, and many other financial activities. USDT is particularly interesting because it operates across several blockchain networks. TRON's TRC20 ecosystem, for example, has become an important environment for USDT transfers. But there is an important technical consideration: The same token can have different transaction histories depending on the blockchain network. An AML system analyzing TRC20 USDT should understand TRC20 transfer activity rather than simply treating all activity on TRON as interchangeable. This distinction becomes important when investigating: Token-specific transaction flows. Wallet relationships. Transfer frequency. Counterparty exposure. Movement between addresses over time. A useful screening process needs to understand the asset and the network being investigated.

为何稳定币值得特别关注。稳定币已成为加密货币基础设施的重要组成部分。它们被用于交易、支付、转账、结算以及许多其他金融活动。USDT 尤为引人注目，因为它在多个区块链网络上运行。例如，波场的 TRC20 生态系统已成为 USDT 转账的重要环境。但有一个重要的技术考量：同一种代币在不同的区块链网络上可能拥有不同的交易历史。分析 TRC20 USDT 的反洗钱系统应当理解 TRC20 的转账活动，而不是简单地将波场上的所有活动视为可互换的。在调查以下内容时，这种区别至关重要：特定代币的交易流、钱包关系、转账频率、交易对手风险敞口、地址间随时间的资金移动。有效的筛选过程需要理解所调查的资产和网络。

Risk scores are useful, but they are not verdicts Let's talk about something that often gets misunderstood: AML risk scores. Many screening systems produce a numerical value intended to summarize the risk associated with an address. This can be useful. A compliance team handling thousands of transactions needs a way to prioritize investigations. However, a numerical score should never replace the underlying evidence. Consider two hypothetical wallets: Wallet Alpha One indirect connection to a high-risk address. No direct sanctions match. Otherwise ordinary transaction activity. Wallet Beta Direct exposure to a confirmed sanctions-listed address. Multiple suspicious counterparties. Repeated unusual transaction patterns. Both wallets deserve attention, but clearly not for the same reasons. A useful AML report should explain: Which risk indicators were identified? Were the connections direct or indirect? How many transaction hops separate the addresses? Which data sources produced the findings? How recent is the underlying information? Does the available evidence justify additional investigation? The objective is not to produce an impressive-looking number. It is to help someone make a better-informed decision.

风险评分很有用，但并非定论。让我们谈谈一个经常被误解的概念：反洗钱风险评分。许多筛选系统会生成一个数值，旨在总结与某个地址相关的风险。这可能很有用。一个处理数千笔交易的合规团队需要一种方法来确定调查的优先级。然而，数值评分绝不能取代底层证据。考虑两个假设的钱包：钱包 Alpha，与高风险地址存在一次间接关联，无直接制裁匹配，其余交易活动正常；钱包 Beta，直接接触已确认的受制裁地址，存在多个可疑交易对手，反复出现异常交易模式。两个钱包都值得关注，但原因显然不同。一份有用的反洗钱报告应当解释：识别出了哪些风险指标？关联是直接的还是间接的？地址之间相隔多少跳交易？调查结果来自哪些数据源？底层信息有多新？现有证据是否足以支持进一步调查？其目的不是为了产生一个看起来令人印象深刻的数字，而是为了帮助人们做出更明智的决策。

False positives are an operational problem There is another side to cryptocurrency screening that deserves more attention: false positives. A wallet may interact with a large exchange, a payment processor, or a service that also has exposure to higher-risk entities. That does not automatically make every customer of that service suspicious. Without sufficient context, overly aggressive screening can create unnecessary friction. For exchanges and payment platforms, this can mean: Delayed withdrawals. Additional manual investigations. Frustrated customers. Increased compliance workload. Poorer operational efficiency. The answer is not to make screening less strict. The answer is to make screening more informative. Risk indicators should be distinguishable from confirmed matches, and indirect exposure should be distinguishable from direct exposure. A good compliance workflow should also allow human review when automated findings are ambiguous.

误报是一个运营问题。加密货币筛选还有另一个值得关注的方面：误报。一个钱包可能与大型交易所、支付处理商或同样接触过高风险实体的服务商进行交互。这并不自动意味着该服务的所有客户都值得怀疑。如果没有足够的背景信息，过于激进的筛选会造成不必要的摩擦。对于交易所和支付平台而言，这意味着：提现延迟、额外的人工调查、客户不满、合规工作量增加、运营效率降低。解决办法不是降低筛选的严格程度，而是让筛选提供更多信息。风险指标应与确认的匹配项区分开来，间接风险敞口也应与直接风险敞口区分开来。良好的合规工作流程还应在自动化调查结果模糊时允许人工复核。
