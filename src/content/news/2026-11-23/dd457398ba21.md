Sheherazaad’s Khudgarzi is gorgeous, ‘genre-chaotic’ South Asian folk. The debut LP from Sheherazaad blends traditional Indian instruments with Western electronic flourishes and feminist narratives.

Sheherazaad 的《Khudgarzi》是一张华丽且“流派混乱”的南亚民谣专辑。这张由 Sheherazaad 推出的首张长篇专辑融合了传统的印度乐器、西方的电子元素以及女性主义叙事。

Sheherazaad started making ripples in the music world a few years ago with her EP Qasr, which was produced by Grammy Award winner Arooj Aftab. Now she’s back with Khudgarzi, her reverb-drenched folk-noir debut LP that’s primarily sung in Urdu.

几年前，Sheherazaad 凭借由格莱美奖得主 Arooj Aftab 制作的 EP《Qasr》在音乐界崭露头角。如今，她带着《Khudgarzi》回归，这是她首张充满混响效果的黑色民谣专辑，主要以乌尔都语演唱。

The instrumentation on Khudgarzi draws on traditional South Asian music, including Carnatic and Hindustani styles, but also dabbles in Arabic folk traditions, as well as modern Western electronic music. Trip-hop flourishes and Spaghetti Western guitar brush up against tanbur and ghatam. Sheherazaad’s stunning voice sits atop dripping percussion and seasick drones singing about the plight of “The Selfish Woman.”

《Khudgarzi》的配器借鉴了传统的南亚音乐，包括卡纳塔克音乐和印度斯坦音乐风格，同时也涉猎了阿拉伯民谣传统以及现代西方电子音乐。Trip-hop 的点缀和意大利西部片风格的吉他与坦布尔琴（tanbur）及陶罐鼓（ghatam）交织在一起。Sheherazaad 令人惊叹的嗓音置于滴答作响的打击乐和令人晕眩的持续音之上，吟唱着“自私女人”的困境。

For Sheherazaad, the character of the selfish woman isn’t the villain, it’s a charge leveled at women who didn’t conform to societal norms, “eccentric women whose desire for agency was often met with an awful end.” Even if you can’t understand most of the lyrics, the atmosphere tells the story clearly enough.

对 Sheherazaad 而言，“自私女人”这一角色并非反派，而是对那些不符合社会规范的女性的指控，即“那些渴望自主权却往往遭遇悲惨结局的古怪女性”。即使你听不懂大部分歌词，其营造的氛围也足以清晰地讲述这个故事。

From the barely simmering anger of “Registaan” to the bleak repetition of “Qatil” and the fantastical yearning of “Zeher,” Khudgarzi is clearly the work of a woman exploring what it means to be a woman, the lasting legacy of colonialism, and the perpetual threats of a patriarchal society.

从《Registaan》中隐约酝酿的愤怒，到《Qatil》中凄凉的重复，再到《Zeher》中奇幻的渴望，《Khudgarzi》显然是一位女性的作品，她在探索身为女性的意义、殖民主义的持久遗产，以及父权社会永恒的威胁。

Khudgarzi is alternately a funeral dirge and a celebration of femininity. Sheherazaad owes a lot of the album’s ability to navigate these various themes and moods to her impressive production work, top-notch mixing by J. Valleau, and the cast of over 30 “genre-chaotic” virtuosic musicians including Ria Modak, Sarathy Korwar, Jay Chakravorty, Shunya, and Qasim Naqvi.

《Khudgarzi》时而如葬礼挽歌，时而又是对女性气质的赞美。这张专辑之所以能够驾驭这些多样的主题和情绪，很大程度上归功于 Sheherazaad 出色的制作、J. Valleau 顶级的混音，以及包括 Ria Modak、Sarathy Korwar、Jay Chakravorty、Shunya 和 Qasim Naqvi 在内的 30 多位“流派混乱”的演奏家。

The instrumentation never distracts, though. It’s not showy, and all emulsifies around Sheherazaad’s vocals in a shawl that feels equal parts comforting and oppressive. Khudgarzi is available now on Bandcamp and most major streaming platforms, including YouTube Music, Spotify, Apple Music, Deezer, and Qobuz.

不过，配器从未喧宾夺主。它并不浮夸，所有元素都围绕着 Sheherazaad 的人声融合在一起，形成了一种既令人慰藉又令人压抑的氛围。目前，《Khudgarzi》已在 Bandcamp 及大多数主流流媒体平台上线，包括 YouTube Music、Spotify、Apple Music、Deezer 和 Qobuz。
