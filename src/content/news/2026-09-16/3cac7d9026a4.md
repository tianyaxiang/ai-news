# Occlusal Geometry in Closed Form for Orthodontic Report Generation
# 用于正畸报告生成的闭式咬合几何分析

**Abstract:** Orthodontic report generation from intraoral data is normally cast as multimodal captioning, yet the released Bite2Text scan pairs are supplied already registered in occlusion, which makes several core occlusal quantities directly measurable rather than inferable. 

**摘要：** 从口腔内数据生成正畸报告通常被视为多模态字幕生成任务，然而，已发布的 Bite2Text 扫描对在提供时已完成了咬合配准，这使得若干核心咬合量可以直接测量，而无需进行推断。

The system reported here exploits that property: an anatomical frame is recovered per case from arch taper and arch closure instead of the stated RAS convention, which does not hold across the release, and each arch is reduced to an occlusal ridge profile in arch-angle coordinates yielding overbite, overjet, midline deviation, transverse overlap, crossbite extent, cusp interdigitation lag, and the occlusal curves in closed form. 

本文报告的系统利用了这一特性：针对每个病例，系统通过牙弓锥度和牙弓闭合度来恢复解剖坐标系，而非使用在整个数据集中并不通用的 RAS 约定。每个牙弓被简化为牙弓角度坐标下的咬合脊轮廓，从而以闭式形式得出覆合、覆盖、中线偏移、横向重叠、反颌程度、牙尖交错滞后以及咬合曲线。

Gradient boosting maps 31 such measurements onto 13 template fields, a field being predicted only where patient-level cross-validation beats its own majority baseline, and a deterministic renderer emits the corpus six-part narrative; a ConvNeXt-Tiny classifier over the five standardised photographic views is fused per field, raising mean field accuracy from 0.601 to 0.683. 

梯度提升树（Gradient boosting）将 31 个此类测量值映射到 13 个模板字段中；只有当患者层面的交叉验证结果优于其多数基准时，才会对该字段进行预测。随后，确定性渲染器生成包含六部分的语料叙述。此外，系统融合了针对五种标准化摄影视图的 ConvNeXt-Tiny 分类器，将平均字段准确率从 0.601 提升至 0.683。

Reimplementation of the challenge evaluator shows that its BLEU-4 and METEOR are local variants whose F-mean weights recall nine to one, that two clinicians agree on 47 percent of findings for the same patient, and that a constant report consequently outscores a genuine second clinician report by 0.165 captioning. 

对挑战评估器的重新实现表明，其 BLEU-4 和 METEOR 指标属于局部变体，其 F-均值权重在召回率上的比例为 9:1。研究还发现，两名临床医生对同一患者的诊断结果一致性仅为 47%，因此，一份恒定的报告在字幕生成得分上比真实的第二位临床医生报告高出 0.165。

Held-out scores reach BLEU-4 0.458 and METEOR 0.677 against intraoral scan references and 0.278 and 0.507 against photograph references, and the submitted system placed third in the ODIN 2026 Bite2Text test phase at 0.2680 and 0.4629, within 0.022 BLEU-4 of first, running on CPU in under ten seconds per case. 

在留出集（Held-out）测试中，针对口腔内扫描参考数据的 BLEU-4 和 METEOR 分数分别达到 0.458 和 0.677，针对照片参考数据的分数分别为 0.278 和 0.507。该系统在 ODIN 2026 Bite2Text 测试阶段中排名第三（得分 0.2680 和 0.4629），与第一名仅相差 0.022 BLEU-4，且在 CPU 上运行每个病例耗时不到十秒。
