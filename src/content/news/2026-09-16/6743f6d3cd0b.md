# Diagnosing Faults in Reinforcement Learning Simulators and World Models with Canonical Polynomial Invariants
# 使用规范多项式不变量诊断强化学习模拟器与世界模型中的故障

**Abstract:** A large literature builds physical structure into learned dynamics on the premise that models respecting the underlying physics predict better. We test that premise using exact polynomial invariants recovered from trajectories and canonicalised as reduced Gröbner bases over $\mathbb{Q}$. 

**摘要：** 现有大量文献基于“遵循底层物理规律的模型预测效果更好”这一前提，将物理结构构建到学习到的动力学中。我们使用从轨迹中恢复并规范化为 $\mathbb{Q}$ 上约简 Gröbner 基的精确多项式不变量，对这一前提进行了测试。

On Acrobot, exactness provides little benefit for prediction: a consistency regulariser reduces algebraic residual while leaving rollout fidelity essentially unchanged, and a shaping potential recovered from a system with a 100% mass error accelerates learning as effectively as the correct potential. 

在 Acrobot 环境中，精确性对预测几乎没有帮助：一致性正则化器虽然降低了代数残差，但基本没有改变展开（rollout）的保真度；此外，从质量误差为 100% 的系统中恢复出的塑形势（shaping potential）在加速学习方面的效果与正确的势函数相当。

Exact canonical invariants instead prove valuable for diagnosis. We develop two procedures: screening, which identifies the violated physical constraint, and attribution, which recovers the faulty invariant and identifies the responsible physical parameter. To enable this, we introduce normal-form deflation and quotient-space recovery. 

相反，精确的规范不变量在故障诊断中证明了其价值。我们开发了两种程序：一是“筛选”（screening），用于识别被违反的物理约束；二是“归因”（attribution），用于恢复故障不变量并确定导致问题的物理参数。为了实现这一点，我们引入了范式降维（normal-form deflation）和商空间恢复（quotient-space recovery）。

Across fifteen injected faults, screening localises every broken constraint with no false alarms, whereas observation-space baselines do not localise any; attribution recovers the responsible parameter on all seven parameter faults. Paired difference tests detect all faults, showing that the advantage is localisation rather than detection. 

在十五个注入故障的测试中，筛选程序定位了每一个被破坏的约束且零误报，而观测空间的基准方法则无法定位任何故障；归因程序在所有七个参数故障中均成功恢复了相关参数。配对差异检验检测出了所有故障，这表明该方法的优势在于定位而非检测。

Perturbing reference generators by $10^{-4}$ preserves 14--15/15 localisations, showing that screening does not require exactness, whereas ideal-equality decisions distinguish perturbations of only $10^{-12}$, showing that exactness is required for algebraic comparison. 

将参考生成器扰动 $10^{-4}$ 后，仍能保持 14-15/15 的定位成功率，这表明筛选过程不需要绝对的精确性；而理想等式判定仅能区分 $10^{-12}$ 级别的扰动，这说明代数比较确实需要精确性。

Applied to 350 release pairs across eleven RL environments, the diagnostic finds no evidence of changed simulator dynamics, instead revealing properties of the benchmark implementations themselves.

该诊断方法应用于十一个强化学习环境中的 350 个版本对，结果未发现模拟器动力学发生改变的证据，反而揭示了基准测试实现本身的一些特性。
