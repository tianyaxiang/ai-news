# Fujifilm’s Instax Pal 2 is a tiny digital camera that may not disappoint
# 富士胶片 Instax Pal 2：一款或许不会让你失望的迷你数码相机

Fujifilm announced a follow-up to its tiny and quirky Instax Pal camera that debuted three years ago bundled with an instant printer. While the original Pal was a palm-sized camera with a round design that lacked a screen or viewfinder, the new Instax Pal 2 includes both in a design that looks like a miniaturized analog film camera. It’s also reminiscent of trendy pocket-friendly cameras like the Kodak Charmera, but with specs that may actually produce decent shots.

富士胶片发布了其三年前随拍立得打印机一同推出的迷你奇趣相机 Instax Pal 的后续机型。初代 Pal 是一款圆润的掌心大小相机，既没有屏幕也没有取景器；而新款 Instax Pal 2 则两者兼备，外观看起来就像一台微缩版的胶片相机。它也让人联想到柯达 Charmera 等流行的口袋相机，但其规格或许真的能拍出不错的照片。

In addition to an optical viewfinder, the Pal 2 has a 1.3-inch LCD screen on top for framing, reviewing shots, or applying image effects.

除了光学取景器外，Pal 2 的顶部还配备了一块 1.3 英寸的 LCD 屏幕，用于取景、回看照片或应用图像特效。

The Instax Pal 2 will launch in the US later this month for $169.95, according to PetaPixel, and uses a ⅓.06-inch sensor paired with an f/2.2 28mm lens to capture images up to 10-megapixels in size. That’s a big step up over the Kodak Charmera’s 1.6-megapixel sensor. The Pal 2 has an optical viewfinder and a 1.3-inch LCD screen on top for framing shots and is equipped with autofocus capabilities including optional face detection plus adjustable ISO, shutter speed, and exposure compensation.

据 PetaPixel 报道，Instax Pal 2 将于本月晚些时候在美国上市，售价为 169.95 美元。它采用了一枚 1/3.06 英寸传感器，搭配 f/2.2 28mm 镜头，可拍摄最高 1000 万像素的照片。这比柯达 Charmera 的 160 万像素传感器有了巨大的提升。Pal 2 配备了光学取景器和顶部的 1.3 英寸 LCD 屏幕用于取景，并具备自动对焦功能（包括可选的人脸检测），以及可调节的 ISO、快门速度和曝光补偿。

Photos can be wirelessly transferred to a phone or instant printer over Bluetooth, but the Pal 2 also includes a slide-out USB-C port for connecting to mobile devices.

照片可以通过蓝牙无线传输到手机或拍立得打印机，但 Pal 2 还内置了一个可滑出的 USB-C 接口，用于连接移动设备。

Atop the Instax Pal 2 you’ll find a shutter button, a joystick, and a functional lever similar to what old film cameras used to advance to the next exposure. The camera pairs with Fujifilm’s mobile app where you can select and download image effects to the Pal 2 or upload images to your phone for printing using a wireless Bluetooth connection or a slide-out USB-C port. The camera also features a flash, a tripod mount, a self-timer, and a rechargeable battery good for up to three hours of use, Fujifilm claims.

在 Instax Pal 2 的顶部，你可以找到快门按钮、摇杆以及一个功能性拨杆，类似于老式胶片相机用于卷片的过片杆。该相机可与富士胶片的移动应用程序配对，用户可以在应用中选择并下载图像特效到 Pal 2，或通过蓝牙无线连接及滑出式 USB-C 接口将照片上传到手机进行打印。据富士胶片称，该相机还配备了闪光灯、三脚架接口、自拍定时器，以及续航可达三小时的可充电电池。
