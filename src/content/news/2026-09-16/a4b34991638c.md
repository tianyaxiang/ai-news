### Let's make quality the norm again
### 让我们让“高质量”再次成为常态

A more circular economy is not only important for the environment. It can also strengthen consumer rights and improve societal resilience.
循环经济不仅对环境至关重要，它还能加强消费者权益并提高社会韧性。

In this report, we show how consumer policy can make circular choices easier, safer and more attractive for consumers.
在本报告中，我们展示了消费者政策如何能让循环消费的选择变得更简单、更安全且更具吸引力。
