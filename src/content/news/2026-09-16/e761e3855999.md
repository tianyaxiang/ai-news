# LLMs or Naive Bayes? Old Gems or New Ways
# 大语言模型还是朴素贝叶斯？旧宝石还是新路径

**Abstract:** Large language models (LLMs) prompt a recurring question in research computing: should classical methods like Naive Bayes (NB) be retired? We benchmark Complement Naive Bayes against zero-shot and few-shot LLMs spanning four model families and a 37x range in scale (27B to a 1T-parameter mixture-of-experts) across text classification tasks.

**摘要：** 大语言模型（LLMs）在研究计算领域引发了一个反复出现的问题：像朴素贝叶斯（NB）这样的经典方法是否应该被淘汰？我们针对文本分类任务，将补集朴素贝叶斯（Complement Naive Bayes）与涵盖四个模型家族、规模跨度达 37 倍（从 270 亿参数到 1 万亿参数的混合专家模型）的零样本（zero-shot）和少样本（few-shot）LLM 进行了基准测试。

LLMs dominate only in zero-data regimes (98.0% vs 88.2% on Amazon Polarity sentiment), and even that win is contamination-prone: on a low-contamination sentiment task NB beats the zero-shot LLM (81.7% vs 73.0%). However, once labeled data is available (e.g., AG News), NB reaches 89.1% accuracy, statistically indistinguishable from the zero-shot 27B LLM (89.0%) and better than the 397B frontier model (84.8%), at thousands of samples/sec on a commodity CPU.

LLM 仅在零数据（zero-data）场景下占据优势（在 Amazon Polarity 情感分析任务中为 98.0% 对 88.2%），且即便如此，该优势也容易受到数据污染的影响：在低污染的情感任务中，NB 击败了零样本 LLM（81.7% 对 73.0%）。然而，一旦有标注数据可用（例如 AG News 数据集），NB 的准确率可达 89.1%，在统计学上与 270 亿参数的零样本 LLM（89.0%）无异，且优于 3970 亿参数的前沿模型（84.8%），同时在普通 CPU 上能达到每秒处理数千个样本的吞吐量。

Fine-tuned DistilBERT reaches 90.6% but at far lower throughput than NB at batch size 1 (Table 2). Our measured GPU throughput analysis shows small-LLM batched inference is 40-486x slower than NB CPU inference (the multiplier depends strongly on the host CPU), exposing a structural gap bounded by memory bandwidth, with roughly two orders of magnitude lower energy per sample.

经过微调的 DistilBERT 准确率达到了 90.6%，但在批处理大小为 1 时，其吞吐量远低于 NB（见表 2）。我们对 GPU 吞吐量的分析表明，小型 LLM 的批处理推理速度比 NB 的 CPU 推理慢 40 到 486 倍（倍数很大程度上取决于宿主 CPU），这揭示了受限于内存带宽的结构性差距，且每个样本的能耗降低了约两个数量级。

For resource-constrained HPC practitioners performing text classification with labeled data, NB remains the optimal choice. We show the decision line is task-dependent (NB reaches LLM parity around $N \sim 10^4$ labels for topic classification, while zero-data sentiment favors the LLM at all N tested) and provide a Kubernetes Helm operator that automates model selection using configurable thresholds and verifiable Prometheus metrics.

对于在资源受限的高性能计算（HPC）环境下进行文本分类并拥有标注数据的从业者来说，NB 仍然是最佳选择。我们展示了决策界限取决于具体任务（在主题分类中，当标注样本 $N \sim 10^4$ 时，NB 可达到与 LLM 持平的水平；而在零数据情感分析中，无论测试何种 $N$ 值，LLM 都更具优势），并提供了一个 Kubernetes Helm 操作器，通过可配置的阈值和可验证的 Prometheus 指标实现模型选择的自动化。
