# One of the best 3D printers for beginners is almost $100 off
# 最适合初学者的 3D 打印机之一，现优惠近 100 美元

If the high price tag of premium 3D printers has kept you from exploring the hobby, this deal is a great entry point. Elegoo has its Centauri Carbon 2 Combo on sale for $369, almost $100 off its usual price of $449. This capable 3D printer can handle four filament rolls at once, with a fully enclosed printing area, auto-leveling bed, and big touchscreen for easy project management. It’s altogether a very capable 3D printer for beginners, or just anyone who doesn’t want to spend a lot of time tinkering with settings just to print occasional trinkets and brackets.

如果高端 3D 打印机的高昂价格让你望而却步，那么这次促销是一个绝佳的入门机会。Elegoo 的 Centauri Carbon 2 Combo 目前售价为 369 美元，比 449 美元的原价优惠了近 100 美元。这款性能出色的 3D 打印机可同时处理四卷耗材，配备全封闭打印空间、自动调平底板以及用于轻松管理项目的大尺寸触摸屏。对于初学者，或者那些不想花费大量时间调试设置、只想偶尔打印一些小物件或支架的用户来说，这是一款非常称手的 3D 打印机。

The Centauri Carbon 2 is fast and capable right out of the box, though first-time setup is a little involved (I found it tough to get the upper shield and filament hub to fit together, for example). But I really like its fully-enclosed build area, which results in consistent prints, and the auto-leveling bed checks that everything is locked in the right place before each print. It’s worth noting that the filament rolls are stored externally, so if you’re in a particularly humid environment and don’t print often, you may want to remove them between uses.

Centauri Carbon 2 开箱即用，速度快且性能强劲，尽管首次设置过程稍显繁琐（例如，我发现安装上护罩和耗材集线器时有些费劲）。但我非常喜欢它的全封闭打印空间，这能带来更稳定的打印效果，而且自动调平底板会在每次打印前确保一切就绪。值得注意的是，耗材卷是存放在外部的，因此如果你所处的环境比较潮湿且不常打印，建议在不使用时将耗材取下。
