# How The Oregon Trail became a generational icon
# 《俄勒冈之旅》如何成为一代人的标志

On Version History: The game’s designer tells us how the brutal edutainment adventure came together.
在《Version History》节目中：这款残酷的教育娱乐冒险游戏是如何诞生的，游戏设计师亲自向我们讲述。

Whether you played it in the back of your classroom or on your computer at home, if you were a kid in the last 30 years you almost certainly grew up playing The Oregon Trail.
无论你是在教室后排还是在家里电脑上玩过它，如果你是过去 30 年里成长起来的孩子，你几乎肯定玩过《俄勒冈之旅》（The Oregon Trail）。

On the season 5 premiere of Version History, our fearless wagon leader David Pierce is joined by gaming journalist Chris Grant and Philip Bouchard, the designer and team leader of the game’s most iconic version. They talk about the academic origins of The Oregon Trail, the game’s complicated legacy, and whether anyone ever really died of dysentery.
在《Version History》第五季的首播集里，我们无畏的“领队” David Pierce 邀请了游戏记者 Chris Grant 以及该游戏最经典版本的首席设计师兼团队负责人 Philip Bouchard。他们探讨了《俄勒冈之旅》的学术起源、该游戏复杂的历史遗产，以及是否真的有人死于痢疾。

This is the first episode of the fifth season of Version History, and this time we’re going back to school. In the weeks to come, we’re taking on the iconic TI-84 graphing calculator, One Laptop Per Child, Chromebook, LeapPad, and the overhead projector.
这是《Version History》第五季的第一集，这一次我们将重返校园。在接下来的几周里，我们将深入探讨标志性的 TI-84 图形计算器、“每个孩子一台笔记本电脑”（OLPC）计划、Chromebook、LeapPad 以及投影仪。
