# Part Grounding, Not Action Knowledge: Locating the Bottleneck in VLM Affordance Prediction
# 部件定位而非动作知识：定位视觉语言模型（VLM）可供性预测的瓶颈

**Abstract:** Benchmarks agree that vision-language models reason poorly about low-level manipulation, but an aggregate accuracy score does not say which step fails. We separate two steps that affordance questions conflate: identifying which part of an object to act on, and knowing what action that part requires.

**摘要：** 现有的基准测试一致认为，视觉语言模型（VLM）在处理底层操作任务时的推理能力较差，但总体的准确率得分并不能揭示具体是哪一步出现了问题。我们将可供性（affordance）问题中混淆的两个步骤进行了拆分：一是识别物体上需要操作的部件，二是了解该部件所需的动作。

Across 19 articulated objects we asked eight models, spanning three developers, what motion a robot should apply. Under an open prompt, push was produced once in 64 evaluations where it was correct, despite being correct for 8 of 19 objects and appearing in the offered label set every time. Inspecting the outputs showed why: models described a different part than the one being scored, e.g. explaining how to pick up a camera rather than press its button.

我们针对 19 个铰接式物体，询问了来自三家开发商的八个模型，机器人应该执行什么样的动作。在开放式提示下，在 64 次评估中，“推”（push）这一动作仅在正确的情况下被生成了一次，尽管它在 19 个物体中有 8 个是正确动作，且每次都出现在提供的标签集中。检查输出结果揭示了原因：模型描述的部件与评分标准中的部件不一致，例如，模型解释的是如何拿起相机，而不是如何按下相机的按钮。

Naming the target part raises action accuracy by 0.32 to 0.63 for every model, from 0.158-0.474 to 0.684-0.947, and push recall from 0-1/8 to 7-8/8. No model beats a constant answer that ignores the image under the open prompt; once the part is named, all eight do. Asked to describe the same part in free prose with no label set, models produce pressing language for 6 to 8 of 8.

明确指定目标部件后，每个模型的动作准确率提高了 0.32 到 0.63，从 0.158-0.474 提升至 0.684-0.947，而“推”动作的召回率从 0-1/8 提升至 7-8/8。在开放式提示下，没有任何模型能胜过忽略图像的“常量回答”；但一旦指定了部件，所有八个模型都能胜出。当要求模型在没有标签集的情况下用自由文本描述同一部件时，8 个模型中有 6 到 8 个能生成关于“按压”的描述语言。

These results are hard to reconcile with missing action knowledge, and instead point to part grounding as the dominant bottleneck, a pattern that holds across all three model families and does not diminish with capability. Naming the part supplies the grounding variable, so this bounds what a perfect part detector would offer rather than demonstrating a general model of mechanics.

这些结果很难用“缺乏动作知识”来解释，反而表明“部件定位”才是主要的瓶颈。这种模式在所有三个模型系列中都存在，且不会随着模型能力的提升而减弱。指定部件提供了定位变量，因此这界定了完美部件检测器所能提供的上限，而非证明了模型具备通用的力学模型。

Two supporting results agree: on real photographs only three of eight models localize grasp points better than a constant baseline, and on rendered objects none do. We also document two measurement errors of our own, a threshold that let a constant baseline score 0.929 and a labelling rule wrong on 4 of 19 objects, both caught only by testing our numbers against trivial alternatives.

两项辅助结果也印证了这一点：在真实照片上，八个模型中只有三个在定位抓取点方面优于常量基准，而在渲染物体上，没有一个模型能做到。我们还记录了我们自身存在的两个测量误差：一个阈值设置导致常量基准得分达到 0.929，以及一个在 19 个物体中有 4 个标注错误的规则；这两个错误都是通过将我们的数据与简单替代方案进行对比才发现的。
