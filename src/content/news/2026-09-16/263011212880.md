### An Update on Wayback Machine Access
### 关于 Wayback Machine 访问情况的更新

We’ve heard you: “Fix the Wayback Machine!” Here’s what’s going on.
我们听到了你们的声音：“修复 Wayback Machine！”以下是目前的情况。

The Internet Archive’s Wayback Machine has been hit by waves of high-volume automated traffic, and we’ve put protections in place to keep the service running.
互联网档案馆（Internet Archive）的 Wayback Machine 近期遭受了多波高频自动化流量的攻击，为了维持服务正常运行，我们已经采取了相应的保护措施。

One recent change: we rewrote the message you see when a request is blocked with a 429 error — the HTTP code that means “too many requests.”
最近的一项调整是：我们重写了当请求因 429 错误（即表示“请求过多”的 HTTP 代码）被拦截时所显示的提示信息。

Those protections sometimes catch real people by mistake. If that’s happened to you, we’re sorry, and we appreciate your patience while we work to reduce the errors.
这些保护措施有时会误伤真实用户。如果这给您带来了困扰，我们深表歉意，并感谢您在我们努力减少此类错误期间所给予的耐心。

We’re getting better at telling abusive bots apart from the people who depend on the Wayback Machine every day.
我们正在不断改进，以更好地将恶意机器人与每天依赖 Wayback Machine 的真实用户区分开来。

If you think you were blocked in error, email info@archive.org with your operating system, browser, and IP address, and we’ll look into it.
如果您认为自己被误拦截，请将您的操作系统、浏览器和 IP 地址发送邮件至 info@archive.org，我们将对此进行调查。
