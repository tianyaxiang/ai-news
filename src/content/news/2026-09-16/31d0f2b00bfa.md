# Don't Just Look, Intervene: Perturbation Based Region Labeling for VQA Images
# 不只是观察，更要干预：基于扰动的视觉问答（VQA）图像区域标注方法

**Abstract:** Vision Language Models (VLMs) should rely on visual evidence that directly determines the correct answer, but supervision for grounding visual reasoning is often expensive to obtain manually or tied to dataset-specific annotation primitives. We instead introduce model-causal visual evidence as an annotation target, defined as the set of image regions whose counterfactual intervention changes a model's answer distribution for a given image-question pair.

**摘要：** 视觉语言模型（VLM）应当依赖于直接决定正确答案的视觉证据，但获取用于视觉推理基础（Grounding）的监督信号往往成本高昂，且通常与特定数据集的标注原语绑定。为此，我们引入了“模型因果视觉证据”作为标注目标，将其定义为一组图像区域，当对这些区域进行反事实干预时，会改变模型针对给定图文对的答案分布。

Based on this principle, we introduce Counterfactual Search for Grounding Regions (CSGR). CSGR is a scalable pipeline that proposes candidate regions, perturbs them, measures their effect on answer sensitivity, and aggregates this evidence across multiple judges to approximate answer-critical regions in VQA data.

基于这一原则，我们提出了“反事实基础区域搜索”（CSGR）。CSGR 是一个可扩展的流水线，它能够提出候选区域、对区域进行扰动、测量其对答案敏感度的影响，并通过多个判别器汇总这些证据，从而近似得出 VQA 数据中对答案至关重要的区域。

To assess whether CSGR annotations contain a useful supervision signal, we plug them into three existing grounding-aware training routines: attention steering, Visual CoT finetuning, and latent visual reasoning. These experiments test whether the proposed annotation scheme can provide a useful supervision signal across multiple ways of consuming region labels, rather than introducing a new way of using them.

为了评估 CSGR 标注是否包含有效的监督信号，我们将其接入三种现有的基础感知训练流程中：注意力引导（Attention Steering）、视觉思维链（Visual CoT）微调以及潜在视觉推理。这些实验旨在测试所提出的标注方案是否能在多种区域标签使用方式下提供有效的监督信号，而非仅仅引入一种新的使用方法。

Across competing automatic region-labeling mechanisms, CSGR annotations provide the most consistent gains over Cross Entropy-only finetuning in both in-domain and out-of-domain evaluations, indicating that the proposed labeling scheme captures useful region-level information.

在与现有的自动区域标注机制对比中，CSGR 标注在域内（in-domain）和域外（out-of-domain）评估中，相比仅使用交叉熵（Cross Entropy）的微调，均提供了最一致的性能提升，这表明该标注方案成功捕捉到了有用的区域级信息。
