### AI agents blew the whistle on their cheating colleagues
### AI 智能体举报了“作弊”的同伴

A group of AI agents asked to solve a series of math problems split into rival factions—when some cheated, others tried to stop them. That whistleblowing behavior, seen for the first time in a recent experiment run by Google DeepMind, could have implications for alignment researchers trying to keep swarms of autonomous AI agents in line. The experiment offers a glimpse of how AI agents might police one another. But it also shows how quickly things can go off the rails when they’re left to interact on their own. Find out what happens when AI agents start enforcing their own rules. —Amit Katwala
在一项要求解决一系列数学问题的实验中，一组 AI 智能体分裂成了对立阵营——当一些智能体作弊时，另一些则试图阻止它们。这种“吹哨”行为在 Google DeepMind 最近的一项实验中首次被观察到，这可能对致力于约束自主 AI 智能体群体的对齐研究人员产生影响。该实验让我们得以一窥 AI 智能体如何相互监督，但也揭示了当它们被放任自流时，事情会以多快的速度失控。了解当 AI 智能体开始执行自己的规则时会发生什么。——Amit Katwala

### Donated livers can be made biologically younger
### 捐赠的肝脏可以实现“生物学年轻化”

Once an organ is removed from a donor’s body, the clock starts ticking. Surgeons usually flush it with a preservative solution, bag it and put it on ice, where it immediately starts to degrade. The team has a matter of hours to get it into a recipient’s body. But there’s another option: machines that pump donated organs with nutrients and remove waste products, essentially giving them a chance to be back in a body. Now, scientists have found that livers kept on these systems seem to get younger, at least at a molecular level. The finding could help explain why organs kept on these machines tend to do better after transplantation. It could also lead to new ways to test the health of donated organs and potentially repair ones that might otherwise be discarded. Here’s what scientists discovered about making donated livers biologically younger. —Jessica Hamzelou
一旦器官从捐赠者体内取出，倒计时就开始了。外科医生通常会用保存液冲洗器官，将其装袋并置于冰上，此时器官便开始退化。医疗团队只有几个小时的时间将其移植到受体体内。但现在有了另一种选择：利用机器为捐赠器官泵入营养物质并清除代谢废物，这实际上给了器官“重获新生”的机会。科学家们发现，在这些系统上维持的肝脏似乎变得更年轻了，至少在分子水平上是这样。这一发现有助于解释为什么在这些机器上维持的器官在移植后表现更好。它还可能带来测试捐赠器官健康状况的新方法，并有望修复那些原本会被丢弃的器官。了解科学家们关于如何让捐赠肝脏实现生物学年轻化的发现。——Jessica Hamzelou
