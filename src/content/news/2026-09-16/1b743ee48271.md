# Personalized and Explainable Blood Pressure Estimation from PPG via Hybrid CNN--Morphological Features
# 基于混合 CNN 与形态学特征的个性化且可解释的 PPG 血压估计

Continuous cuffless blood pressure (BP) monitoring using photoplethysmography (PPG) offers a promising solution for personalized healthcare. However, existing methods have two major limitations. Handcrafted feature-based approaches rely on precise fiducial point detection and are limited to short-term analysis, while deep learning models, despite their accuracy, often operate as black boxes with limited physiological interpretability.

利用光电容积脉搏波（PPG）进行连续无袖带血压（BP）监测，为个性化医疗提供了一种极具前景的解决方案。然而，现有方法存在两个主要局限性：基于手工特征的方法依赖于精确的基准点检测，且仅限于短期分析；而深度学习模型尽管精度较高，但往往表现为“黑盒”，缺乏生理学上的可解释性。

To address these challenges, we propose a physiology-guided hybrid framework for personalized BP estimation that couples a convolutional neural network (CNN) branch capturing global and local waveform dynamics with a morphology-prior branch that explicitly encodes person-specific vascular characteristics. By embedding a morphology-based feature set that explicitly encodes individual vascular characteristics, the proposed framework enhances personalization and reduces dependence on large-scale training datasets.

为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了一种生理学引导的混合框架，用于个性化血压估计。该框架结合了两个分支：一个是捕捉全局和局部波形动态的卷积神经网络（CNN）分支，另一个是显式编码个人特定血管特征的形态学先验分支。通过嵌入能够明确编码个体血管特征的形态学特征集，该框架增强了个性化能力，并降低了对大规模训练数据集的依赖。

Evaluated on a subset of the MIMIC-III database under a subject-specific (personalized) testing protocol, the proposed personalized physiology-guided hybrid approach achieved mean absolute errors (MAEs) of 3.77 mmHg for systolic BP and 2.36 mmHg for diastolic BP, corresponding to relative improvements of 43.7% and 32.4% over a subject-specific (personalized) CNN-only baseline. SHAP-based analysis confirmed that the introduced morphology-prior features align with individual vascular characteristics, reinforcing per-subject interpretability. These findings highlight the potential of personalized, physiology-guided hybrid learning with novel morphological descriptors for accurate and explainable BP monitoring in real-world settings.

在 MIMIC-III 数据库的子集上，通过受试者特定（个性化）测试协议进行评估，所提出的个性化生理学引导混合方法在收缩压（SBP）和舒张压（DBP）上的平均绝对误差（MAE）分别为 3.77 mmHg 和 2.36 mmHg，相较于仅使用 CNN 的个性化基准模型，分别提升了 43.7% 和 32.4%。基于 SHAP 的分析证实，引入的形态学先验特征与个体血管特征相吻合，从而增强了针对每个受试者的可解释性。这些研究结果凸显了结合新型形态学描述符的个性化生理学引导混合学习在现实场景中实现准确且可解释的血压监测的潜力。
