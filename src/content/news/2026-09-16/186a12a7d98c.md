# What Does the Encoder Actually Decide? A Controlled Comparison of Vision Backbones on Joint Tree Segmentation and Stereo Depth
# 编码器到底决定了什么？关于联合树木分割与立体深度估计中视觉骨干网络的受控对比研究

**Abstract:** A robot pruning trees needs two facts per pixel: whether it belongs to a tree, and its distance. Both are usually obtained via task heads attached to a vision backbone chosen by reputation rather than measurement. Holding dataset, decoders, losses, schedule, and evaluation fixed, we ask: how much does the encoder choice change joint semantic segmentation and stereo depth on thin vegetation?

**摘要：** 机器人进行树木修剪时，每个像素点需要两个关键信息：该像素是否属于树木，以及其距离。这两项信息通常通过连接在视觉骨干网络（Vision Backbone）上的任务头（Task Heads）获得，而骨干网络的选择往往基于声誉而非实测数据。在保持数据集、解码器、损失函数、训练计划和评估标准不变的前提下，我们提出了一个问题：在稀疏植被场景下，编码器的选择对联合语义分割和立体深度估计的影响究竟有多大？

We build a hard parameter-sharing network with one encoder feeding both branches, swapping only the encoder without downstream retuning. We evaluate [N] encoders across [M] architecture families (CNNs, transformers, hybrids, MLP-mixers, state-space models) near a ~25M budget, trained from scratch. Depth is evaluated on tree pixels only; segmentation uses boundary F1 and background IoU to prevent "label-everything-tree" shortcuts.

我们构建了一个硬参数共享网络，由一个编码器同时为两个分支提供特征，并在不进行下游微调的情况下仅替换编码器。我们评估了涵盖 [M] 种架构家族（包括 CNN、Transformer、混合架构、MLP-mixer 和状态空间模型）的 [N] 个编码器，参数量均控制在 2500 万左右，并从零开始训练。深度评估仅针对树木像素；分割评估则使用边界 F1 分数和背景 IoU，以防止模型出现“将所有像素标记为树木”的捷径行为。

Three findings stand out. First, the strongest encoders are convolutional and hybrid, not transformers: [BestEncoder] leads with [MIoU] segmentation mIoU and [Delta] depth $\delta_1$, while [X] of [Y] plain vision transformers collapse when trained from scratch. Second, parameter count does not predict quality --- [SmallEncoder] at only [P]M parameters outranks models two orders of magnitude larger. Third, segmentation and depth rankings agree strongly (Spearman $\rho$ = [RhoValue]), showing no task conflict. Finally, [K] of [N] encoders collapse to degenerate all-tree segmentation --- exposed by boundary F1 but hidden by region IoU.

研究得出三个显著结论。首先，表现最强的编码器是卷积网络和混合架构，而非 Transformer：[BestEncoder] 在分割 mIoU 和深度 $\delta_1$ 指标上均处于领先地位，而 [Y] 个纯视觉 Transformer 中有 [X] 个在从零训练时出现了崩溃。其次，参数量并不能预测模型质量——参数量仅为 [P]M 的 [SmallEncoder] 表现优于参数量大两个数量级的模型。第三，分割和深度的排名高度一致（Spearman $\rho$ = [RhoValue]），表明两者之间不存在任务冲突。最后，[N] 个编码器中有 [K] 个退化为“全树分割”的无效状态——这一问题通过边界 F1 分数可以发现，但会被区域 IoU 指标所掩盖。
