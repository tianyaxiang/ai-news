# Abstract-LoRA: Unlocking Single-Image Style Transfer through Targeted U-Net Block Training
# Abstract-LoRA：通过针对性 U-Net 模块训练解锁单图风格迁移

**Abstract:** Diffusion models represent one of the most advanced paradigms in generative modeling. Leveraging their development, a growing number of style transfer methods based on diffusion models have been proposed. However, among these methods, multi-image style transfer approaches that require at least five to ten style examples tend to achieve more satisfactory results. Single-image methods, by contrast, often struggle with either insufficient content preservation or inadequate style fidelity. This greatly limits style extraction from scarce artworks and undermines their artistic value.

**摘要：** 扩散模型代表了生成式建模中最先进的范式之一。随着其发展，越来越多的基于扩散模型的风格迁移方法被提出。然而，在这些方法中，需要至少五到十个风格样本的多图风格迁移方法往往能取得更令人满意的结果。相比之下，单图方法往往在内容保留不足或风格保真度不够方面存在困难。这极大地限制了从稀缺艺术品中提取风格的能力，并削弱了它们的艺术价值。

To address this, we propose Abstract-LoRA, a method that pushes the boundaries of single-image style transfer through lightweight LoRA training on specific U-Net blocks in diffusion models. Specifically, our work is inspired by B-LoRA, a style transfer method that achieves basic style-content disentanglement by training specific U-Net blocks. However, it suffers from a critical limitation: the inability to capture complex backgrounds.

为了解决这一问题，我们提出了 Abstract-LoRA，这是一种通过对扩散模型中特定 U-Net 模块进行轻量级 LoRA 训练，从而突破单图风格迁移边界的方法。具体而言，我们的工作受到 B-LoRA 的启发，这是一种通过训练特定 U-Net 模块来实现基本风格与内容解耦的风格迁移方法。然而，它存在一个关键局限：无法捕捉复杂的背景。

Building upon B-LoRA, our method conducts a more refined analysis of U-Net blocks, employing additional U-Net blocks and clustering-based abstraction of style images to better disentangle and balance style and content. Extensive experiments demonstrate that our proposed method not only generates visually more harmonious and satisfying artistic images but also quantitatively improves the preservation of both style and content in the final outputs.

在 B-LoRA 的基础上，我们的方法对 U-Net 模块进行了更精细的分析，采用了额外的 U-Net 模块以及基于聚类的风格图像抽象，以更好地解耦并平衡风格与内容。大量实验表明，我们提出的方法不仅能生成视觉上更和谐、更令人满意的艺术图像，而且在定量指标上提升了最终输出中风格与内容的保留效果。
