# Land Art as a Big-Data Climate Sensor
# 大地艺术作为大数据气候传感器

**Abstract:** Robert Smithson's 1970 land artwork *Spiral Jetty*, located in the north arm of Utah's Great Salt Lake, has alternated between submergence and exposure during severe lake decline. We analyze 1,744 co-registered Landsat 4-9 and Sentinel-2 image chips spanning every year and calendar month from 1984 to 2025.

**摘要：** 罗伯特·史密森（Robert Smithson）1970年的大地艺术作品《螺旋防波堤》（Spiral Jetty）位于犹他州大盐湖北岸，在湖泊水位严重下降的过程中，该作品在被淹没和露出水面之间交替变化。我们分析了1984年至2025年间，涵盖每年及每月共1,744张经过配准的Landsat 4-9和Sentinel-2影像切片。

A 14-feature complexity signature combines Shannon entropy, multiscale permutation entropy, fractal dimension, lacunarity, gray-level co-occurrence texture, intensity statistics, and ImageNet-pretrained ResNet50 features. These measurements are compared with a 42-year monthly climate and hydrology panel from NASA GISTEMP, USGS NWIS, Open-Meteo, and the Global Carbon Budget.

一个包含14个特征的复杂性签名结合了香农熵（Shannon entropy）、多尺度排列熵、分形维数、空隙度（lacunarity）、灰度共生矩阵纹理、强度统计以及ImageNet预训练的ResNet50特征。这些测量数据与来自NASA GISTEMP、USGS NWIS、Open-Meteo和全球碳预算（Global Carbon Budget）的42年气候与水文月度面板数据进行了对比。

Bootstrap analysis shows that Shannon entropy is a weak proxy and does not support an earlier small-sample claim of positive correlation with global temperature. By contrast, coarse-scale permutation entropy and mean intensity track lake elevation strongly, with Spearman correlations of 0.85 to 0.88 and 95 percent confidence intervals excluding zero.

自助法（Bootstrap）分析表明，香农熵是一个较弱的代理指标，并不支持此前小样本研究中提出的其与全球气温呈正相关的结论。相比之下，粗尺度排列熵和平均强度能强有力地追踪湖泊水位，其斯皮尔曼相关系数（Spearman correlations）在0.85至0.88之间，且95%的置信区间不包含零。

The third principal component of the ResNet50 embeddings emerges without supervision as an AI climate axis, correlating 0.86 with cumulative CO2 and -0.83 with lake elevation. Image complexity leads lake stage by about three years, with Pearson r = 0.58 at lag +3 and a 95 percent confidence interval of 0.40 to 0.73.

ResNet50嵌入的第三主成分在无监督的情况下显现为一个AI气候轴，其与累积二氧化碳的相关系数为0.86，与湖泊水位的相关系数为-0.83。图像复杂性领先于湖泊水位约三年，在滞后3期时皮尔逊相关系数（Pearson r）为0.58，95%置信区间为0.40至0.73。

STL decomposition reveals a non-monotonic trend that rises from 1984 to 2015 and declines sharply thereafter as the lake approaches record-low elevations. Partial correlations controlling for month and sensor confirm robustness to seasonal and sensor effects. These results refine the art-as-thermometer metaphor into an art-as-leading-indicator-of-hydrological-state interpretation. The dataset, feature pipeline, and analysis code are released as a public benchmark.

STL分解揭示了一种非单调趋势：从1984年到2015年呈上升趋势，此后随着湖泊水位接近历史最低点而急剧下降。控制了月份和传感器变量后的偏相关分析证实了该结果对季节和传感器效应的稳健性。这些结果将“艺术作为温度计”的隐喻升华为“艺术作为水文状态领先指标”的解读。数据集、特征流水线和分析代码已作为公共基准发布。
