# GTA VI: all the news on Rockstar’s next entry in the Grand Theft Auto series
# 《GTA VI》：关于 Rockstar 下一部《侠盗猎车手》系列作品的所有新闻

It’s been over a decade and two console generations since GTA V came out, and its sequel is still a work in progress. GTA VI has faced multiple delays, with developer Rockstar Games bumping back its predicted release date twice, first to May 26th, 2026, then again to November 19th, 2026, a full year after its originally planned release window of fall 2025, and facing accusations of union busting after firing more than 30 employees.

距离《GTA V》发布已经过去了十多年，跨越了两个主机世代，但其续作仍在开发中。《GTA VI》经历了多次延期，开发商 Rockstar Games 将其预计发布日期推迟了两次：先是推迟到 2026 年 5 月 26 日，随后又推迟到 2026 年 11 月 19 日。这比原计划 2025 年秋季的发布窗口整整晚了一年，且在解雇了 30 多名员工后，该公司还面临着破坏工会的指控。

Now, preorders have opened for the digital code-only game, offering the $79.99 standard edition and $99.99 Ultimate Edition on both PS5 and Xbox Series X / S.

目前，这款仅提供数字兑换码的游戏已开启预购，PS5 和 Xbox Series X/S 平台的标准版售价为 79.99 美元，终极版售价为 99.99 美元。

Early official glimpses offered a look at the game’s protagonists, Jason and Lucia, as the game will follow the pair around Vice City as they attempt to navigate a criminal conspiracy after a job goes south. After leaks of early builds a few years ago, a new “CyberLeek” series of posts have popped up, showing sequences of the game just days ahead of Rockstar’s planned Netflix-exclusive (for six hours) event on August 27th.

早期的官方预览展示了游戏的主角 Jason 和 Lucia。游戏将跟随这对搭档在罪恶都市（Vice City）中展开冒险，讲述他们在一次行动失败后，试图应对一场犯罪阴谋的故事。继几年前早期版本泄露后，近期又出现了一系列名为“CyberLeek”的帖子，在 Rockstar 计划于 8 月 27 日举办的 Netflix 独家（限时六小时）活动前夕，提前曝光了游戏片段。

### Grand Theft Auto VI exists in its own universe / 《侠盗猎车手 VI》存在于其独立的宇宙中

Four years ago, fans got a long-awaited look at Grand Theft Auto VI with a huge cache of videos that showed off Rockstar’s latest open-world epic. There were 90 videos in total, providing a glimpse at the next-generation take on Vice City, introducing the game’s new dual protagonists, and generally showing off the crime-fueled mayhem the series is known for. The twist? This wasn’t some official reveal. The videos were part of a massive leak in September 2022 that ultimately ended with a life sentence in a secure hospital for the hacker, and they dropped months before Rockstar properly unveiled GTA VI to the world with its debut trailer.

四年前，粉丝们通过大量泄露的视频终于看到了期待已久的《侠盗猎车手 VI》，这些视频展示了 Rockstar 最新的开放世界史诗巨作。总计 90 段视频展示了次世代罪恶都市的面貌，介绍了游戏的新双主角，并展现了该系列标志性的犯罪狂欢。令人意外的是，这并非官方发布。这些视频源于 2022 年 9 月的一次大规模泄露事件，最终导致黑客被判处在安全医院终身监禁。而这些视频泄露的时间，比 Rockstar 通过首支预告片正式向全球公布《GTA VI》还要早几个月。

### Microsoft and Discord subpoenaed over GTA VI gameplay leaks / 微软与 Discord 因《GTA VI》游戏泄露被传唤

Following several apparent video leaks of Grand Theft Auto VI, Take-Two Interactive has subpoenaed Microsoft and Discord over content that “infringes copyrights” held for the game, Kotaku reports. In the subpoenas, filed on Thursday, Take-Two says copyrighted material includes “audiovisual content, artwork, images, dialogue, or other creative elements” and it is looking to identify “alleged infringers at issue.” The subpoenas give Microsoft and Discord until September 4th to produce the requested information.

据 Kotaku 报道，在《侠盗猎车手 VI》发生多起明显的视频泄露事件后，Take-Two Interactive 已向微软和 Discord 发出传票，要求调查涉及“侵犯版权”的内容。在周四提交的传票中，Take-Two 表示受版权保护的材料包括“视听内容、艺术作品、图像、对话或其他创意元素”，并寻求识别“涉嫌侵权者”。传票要求微软和 Discord 在 9 月 4 日前提供所要求的信息。

The leaked clips have been watermarked by a person or group going by “CyberLeek,” and they have shown what appears to be GTA VI protagonist Jason Duval (one of two protagonists) doing various activities, including driving, flying an airplane, using a taser, and attacking somebody with a wrench. In one clip, the player spells “LEEK” by shooting holes in a wall.

泄露的片段带有自称“CyberLeek”的个人或团体的水印，视频中似乎展示了《GTA VI》主角 Jason Duval（双主角之一）进行各种活动，包括驾驶、驾驶飞机、使用电击枪以及用扳手攻击他人。在其中一个片段中，玩家通过在墙上射击孔洞拼出了“LEEK”字样。

### A GTA VI leaker seemingly has a playable build / 《GTA VI》泄露者似乎拥有可玩版本

A new clip shared by the person or group identifying themselves as “CyberLeek” shows Jason Duval, one of the game’s protagonists, shooting the word “LEEK” into a wall, which suggests an actual copy of the game has escaped Rockstar Games’ containment. It’s the latest of several leaks ahead of GTA VI’s launch on November 19th.

自称“CyberLeek”的个人或团体分享的一段新视频显示，游戏主角之一 Jason Duval 在墙上射出了“LEEK”字样，这表明游戏的实际版本已经从 Rockstar Games 的管控中流出。这是《GTA VI》在 11 月 19 日发布前发生的最新一起泄露事件。

### GTA VI keeps leaking ahead of its gameplay premiere / 《GTA VI》在游戏首映前持续泄露

Clips of what appears to be Grand Theft Auto VI have hit the internet, possibly spoiling aspects of the game ahead of Rockstar Games’ deep dive debuting on Netflix next week and its long-awaited launch in November. I’ve seen three clips seemingly showing off the game, though two have been pulled from the site I saw them on “due to a copyright claim.” The first clip (now removed) was a quiet scene that mostly focused on Jason Duval, one the game’s two protagonists, shooting a basketball into a hoop. Another clip (also removed) featured more of the crime-focused gameplay you’d expect from GTA, including Duval hitting a person with a wrench, and also showed the return of a six-star Wanted system.

疑似《侠盗猎车手 VI》的片段已在互联网上传播，这可能会在 Rockstar Games 下周于 Netflix 首播深度演示以及 11 月正式发布前，剧透游戏的部分内容。我看到了三个似乎展示了游戏的片段，尽管其中两个已因“版权声明”从我观看的网站上被撤下。第一个片段（现已删除）是一个安静的场景，主要聚焦于游戏双主角之一的 Jason Duval 投篮。另一个片段（也已删除）展示了更多《GTA》标志性的犯罪玩法，包括 Duval 用扳手击打他人，并展示了六星通缉系统的回归。

### GTA VI preorders have gotten off to an “exceptional start.” / 《GTA VI》预购取得“异常出色的开局”

“I can say that the level of pre-orders is unprecedented and astonishing, and we’re very grateful for that,” Take-Two CEO Strauss Zelnick said on Friday’s earnings call, according to Variety. An “extended look” at GTA VI will stream on August 27th. GTA VI also won’t launch on a physical disc. On the call, Zelnick said that Take-Two’s business is already “well over 90 percent digitally distributed,” according to Bloomberg’s Jason Schreier.

据《综艺》杂志报道，Take-Two 首席执行官 Strauss Zelnick 在周五的财报电话会议上表示：“我可以肯定地说，预购水平是前所未有的，令人震惊，我们对此深表感激。”《GTA VI》的“加长版预览”将于 8 月 27 日播出。《GTA VI》也不会发行实体光盘。据彭博社的 Jason Schreier 报道，Zelnick 在电话会议上表示，Take-Two 的业务目前已有“超过 90% 是通过数字渠道分发的”。
