# Survival Analysis and the Cox Proportional Hazards Model: A Beginner-Friendly Guide
# 生存分析与 Cox 比例风险模型：初学者指南

Data Science Survival Analysis and the Cox Proportional Hazards Model: A Beginner-Friendly Guide From Kaplan-Meier curves to hazard ratios with runnable Python Code throughout.
数据科学生存分析与 Cox 比例风险模型：从 Kaplan-Meier 曲线到风险比的初学者指南，内含可运行的 Python 代码。

The question ordinary regression can’t answer. Here is a dataset. 432 people were released from prison and were followed for one year. They were being watched for a single event: re-arrest. Some were re-arrested in week 8, some in week 30. Most (318 of them) reached the end of the study having never been re-arrested at all.
普通回归无法回答的问题。假设有一个数据集：432 名刑满释放人员被跟踪观察了一年。观察的目标是单一事件：重新被捕。有些人是在第 8 周被捕的，有些人是在第 30 周。大多数人（318 人）在研究结束时从未被重新逮捕过。

The simple question that was being asked in this study was how long until someone re-offends? We cannot just average the arrest times. Three-quarters of the people never got arrested, so they have no arrest time to average. We cannot throw those people away either. Because if we only analyse the ones who failed, we will end up concluding that everyone eventually re-offends, which is both false and grim.
这项研究提出的简单问题是：一个人需要多久才会再次犯罪？我们不能简单地对被捕时间取平均值。因为四分之三的人从未被捕，所以他们没有可供平均的被捕时间。我们也不能直接剔除这些人，因为如果我们只分析那些“失败”（再次被捕）的样本，最终会得出“每个人最终都会再次犯罪”的结论，这既不符合事实，也过于悲观。

And we also cannot code the survivors as arrested after week 52, because they weren’t. They were either arrested at some week after the 52nd week, that we never got to see, or maybe never. That last situation, which is we know someone lasted at least this long, but we do not know how long in total, is called censoring. It is the entire reason survival analysis exists as its own field.
我们也不能将幸存者标记为在第 52 周后被捕，因为事实并非如此。他们可能是在第 52 周之后的某个时间被捕（但我们未观察到），或者永远不会被捕。最后这种“我们知道某人至少坚持了这么久，但不知道总共持续了多久”的情况被称为“删失”（Censoring）。这正是生存分析作为一个独立领域存在的原因。

Ordinary linear regression has no way to represent “the answer is at least 52.” It wants a number. Survival analysis is the set of tools built for the case where, for a big chunk of our data, the clock was still running when we stopped watching.
普通线性回归无法表示“答案至少是 52”这种概念，它需要一个确定的数值。生存分析是一套专门为以下情况构建的工具：在数据的大部分样本中，当我们停止观察时，计时器仍在运行。

### Three Core Concepts in Survival Analysis
### 生存分析的三个核心概念

Almost everything in survival analysis is built from three objects as detailed below.
生存分析中的几乎所有内容都建立在以下三个要素之上：

1. **The event and the time:** Let’s pick one well-defined event. It can be death, re-arrest, machine failure, subscription cancellation or a loan default. For each subject, we record two things: how long they were observed (duration), and whether the observation ended because the event happened or because we stopped watching (event indicator, binary 1 or 0).
1. **事件与时间：** 让我们选择一个定义明确的事件。它可以是死亡、重新被捕、机器故障、订阅取消或贷款违约。对于每个主体，我们记录两件事：观察时长（duration），以及观察结束是因为事件发生还是因为我们停止了观察（事件指标，二进制 1 或 0）。

2. **The survival function, S(t):** This is the probability that a subject makes it past time t without the event. It starts at 1 (at time zero) and decays toward 0 over time. For example, “Survival at 12 months is 0.7” means 70% are expected to still be event-free at a year.
2. **生存函数 S(t)：** 这是主体在时间 t 之前未发生事件的概率。它从 1（时间为零时）开始，并随时间推移衰减至 0。例如，“12 个月的生存率为 0.7”意味着预计 70% 的人在一年后仍未发生该事件。

3. **The hazard function, h(t):** The hazard is the instantaneous rate of the event at time t, given that we have survived up to t. Informally, we can say it like this: Of the people who’ve made it this far, what fraction fail right now?
3. **风险函数 h(t)：** 风险是指在已知存活到时间 t 的前提下，在时间 t 发生事件的瞬时速率。通俗地说：在坚持到这一刻的人群中，有多少比例的人现在“失败”了？

The distinction between S(t) and h(t) matters. Survival is cumulative; hazard is momentary. Our overall probability of still being alive at 80 is low (survival is small), but our hazard at the exact instant we turn 80 is a different quantity entirely. Survival is how much water is left in the tank. Hazard, on the other hand, is how fast it is draining right now.
S(t) 和 h(t) 之间的区别很重要。生存是累积的，而风险是瞬时的。我们 80 岁时仍然存活的总概率很低（生存率小），但我们在 80 岁那一刻的风险是完全不同的概念。生存率就像水箱里还剩多少水，而风险则是水箱当前流失的速度。

### Kaplan-Meier: a survival curve with no assumptions
### Kaplan-Meier：无需假设的生存曲线

Before we model anything, we can estimate S(t) directly from data. The Kaplan-Meier estimator (Kaplan & Meier, 1958) does this without assuming any particular shape of the curve. The idea is very simple. Let’s walk forward in time. At each moment where an event actually happens, we look at how many people were still at risk just before and how many failed. Multiply together those “fraction who survived this instant” numbers as we go.
在进行任何建模之前，我们可以直接从数据中估计 S(t)。Kaplan-Meier 估计量（Kaplan & Meier, 1958）在不假设曲线形状的情况下完成此操作。其思想非常简单：随着时间推移，在每个事件实际发生的时刻，我们观察在此之前有多少人处于风险中，以及有多少人“失败”了。随着时间推进，将这些“在此瞬间存活的比例”相乘即可。

Censored people contribute to the at-risk count right up until they leave, and then quietly drop out without ever causing a downward step.
被删失的个体在离开前一直计入风险人群，随后悄然退出，不会导致曲线出现下降阶梯。
