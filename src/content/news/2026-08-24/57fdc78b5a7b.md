# Parse the Folder, Not Just the PDFs: The Relational Tables RAG Needs on a Case File
# 解析文件夹，而不仅仅是 PDF：案例档案中 RAG 所需的关系表

Large Language Model Parse the Folder, Not Just the PDFs: The Relational Tables RAG Needs on a Case File Enterprise Document Intelligence [Vol.1 #14D] – The index lists what the case type demands before any folder is opened, and the two questions worth building for are not retrieval questions at all.
大型语言模型解析文件夹，而不仅仅是 PDF：案例档案中 RAG 所需的关系表。企业文档智能 [第 1 卷 #14D] —— 索引在打开任何文件夹之前就列出了案例类型所需的内容，而值得构建的两个问题根本不是检索问题。

Context windows keep getting longer, and the advice that comes with them is simple: stop retrieving, put the whole thing in the prompt. On a case file that advice nearly works. Eleven PDFs about one insurance claim come to sixty-odd pages, which fits inside a 200,000-token window several times over.
上下文窗口越来越长，随之而来的建议很简单：停止检索，把所有内容都放进提示词（Prompt）里。对于案例档案来说，这个建议几乎是有效的。关于一份保险索赔的 11 个 PDF 文件总共约 60 多页，可以多次放入 200,000 token 的窗口中。

It still misses the two questions the person handling the claim has. Is the second repair invoice in the folder at all, and does the date of loss on the claim form match the one in the adjuster’s report? The reason is in the shape of the thing. A case file is a bundle of unlike documents about one entity, and the unit of work is the bundle.
但它仍然无法回答处理索赔的人员所关心的两个问题：文件夹里到底有没有第二张维修发票？索赔表上的损失日期是否与理算员报告中的日期一致？原因在于事物的形态。案例档案是关于同一个实体的一堆不同文档的集合，工作的基本单位是这个“包”。

A claim, a credit application, a medical record, a hiring file. The contract, the certificate, the photographs, the expert’s report, the letters: nothing in there resembles anything else, and the answer comes from reading across all of it. So neither question is a search. The first is about a document that does not exist, and no amount of context holds it. The second needs one value out of each of two files, compared.
索赔申请、信贷申请、医疗记录、招聘档案。合同、证书、照片、专家报告、信件：其中没有任何东西是相似的，答案来自于对所有内容的通读。因此，这两个问题都不是搜索问题。第一个问题关于一个不存在的文档，无论多少上下文都无法包含它。第二个问题需要从两个文件中各提取一个值进行比较。

Models read the middle of a long input less reliably than the ends, and that has been measured. In a bundle of eleven, both values are in the middle. This article works through what that changes: what separates a case file from a collection that happens to be small; the list of pieces a case type expects, which indexes what should be there rather than what is; why a missing document is a first-class answer, and what it takes to produce one; the checks that compare one piece against another, and the typed values they need; first the shape of the answer, which is the state of the case rather than a passage; why this shape has the least prior art of the three, and how to evaluate it anyway.
模型读取长输入中间部分的可信度低于两端，这一点已经过测量。在 11 个文件的集合中，这两个值都位于中间。本文将探讨这带来的变化：是什么将案例档案与仅仅是“数量较少”的集合区分开来；案例类型所预期的组件列表，它索引的是“应该存在什么”而非“实际存在什么”；为什么缺失文档是一个一等重要的答案，以及如何生成它；比较不同组件的检查机制，以及它们所需的类型化值；首先是答案的形态，它是案例的状态而非一段文本；为什么这种形态在三种形态中先例最少，以及如何对其进行评估。

This article is part of Part IV of Enterprise Document Intelligence, a series that builds an enterprise RAG system from four bricks. Part IV is where the input stops being a file and becomes a folder, and this is the folder that behaves like one object rather than like a collection.
本文是《企业文档智能》第四部分的一部分，该系列旨在用四块基石构建企业级 RAG 系统。第四部分标志着输入从“文件”转变为“文件夹”，而这里的文件夹表现为一个单一对象，而不是一个简单的集合。

### 1. A bundle is not a small corpus
### 1. “包”不是小型语料库

A fire damages a joinery workshop on 12 March. Over the next six weeks the broker’s folder for that claim fills up with eleven PDFs: the claim form, the policy schedule in force that month, a certificate of premium payment, the loss adjuster’s report, two repair quotes, one repair invoice, a set of damage photographs, two letters, and a vehicle schedule that belongs to a different policy and was filed here by mistake.
3 月 12 日，一场火灾损坏了一家细木工车间。在接下来的六周里，经纪人关于该索赔的文件夹中填入了 11 个 PDF 文件：索赔表、当月生效的保单明细表、保费支付凭证、理算员报告、两份维修报价单、一份维修发票、一套损坏照片、两封信件，以及一份属于另一份保单、被误存入此处的车辆明细表。

Nothing in that list resembles anything else in it. The photographs carry no text. The adjuster’s report is prose. The quotes are tables. The claim form is boxes with values typed into them. Ask the handler what she does first and she does not search. She runs down a list she knows by heart and ticks off what is there.
清单中的任何内容都与其他内容毫无相似之处。照片不包含文字。理算员报告是散文体。报价单是表格。索赔表是带有填入值的方框。问问处理人员她首先做什么，她不会去搜索。她会对照烂熟于心的清单，勾选出已有的内容。

Fire claim, so the fire brigade report is required, and it is not in the folder. Two quotes accepted, so two invoices should follow, and only one has. That is a couple of minutes of work, and no document has been read yet. Only after that does she read. The date of loss on the claim form says 12 March. The adjuster writes that he inspected the site on 3 March. One of those two is wrong, and until somebody says which, the file does not move.
火灾索赔，因此需要消防队报告，但文件夹里没有。两份报价单被接受，因此应该有两张发票，但只有一张。这只需要几分钟的工作，而且此时还没有阅读任何文档。只有在那之后她才会开始阅读。索赔表上的损失日期写的是 3 月 12 日。理算员写道他于 3 月 3 日视察了现场。这两者中有一个是错的，在有人指出错误之前，档案无法推进。
