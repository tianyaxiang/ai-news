# RoboTrack: Point tracking for real-world robotic manipulation

在现实世界中部署机器人需要整个交互过程中的可靠感知。为了拾取物体，机器人需要跟踪其夹具和物体移动时的位置。在实验室中，操作移液管的机器人必须在将其与试管口对齐时持续跟踪移液管尖端。当附近物体遮挡视野、夹具遮挡被操作物体或视角发生变化时，这些任务会变得更加困难。

Deploying robots in real-world settings requires reliable perception throughout an interaction. To pick something up, a robot needs to follow the positions of its gripper and the object as they move. In a laboratory, a robot handling a pipette must keep track of its tip while aligning it with the opening of a tube. These tasks become harder when nearby objects clutter the view, the gripper obscures the object being handled, or the viewing angle changes.

帮助机器人跟踪这些交互的一种方法是追踪它们所操作物体上的特定位置。这就是点跟踪（point tracking）背后的理念。给定视频帧中的一个位置，跟踪器会估计该点在后续帧中的出现位置，包括它被遮挡时以及重新出现的位置。这些轨迹可以帮助机器人从演示中学习、规划和引导运动，并模拟物体对动作的反应。

One way to help robots follow these interactions is to track specific locations on the objects they handle. This is the idea behind point tracking. Given a location in one video frame, a tracker estimates where that same point appears in subsequent frames, when it is occluded, and where it reappears. These trajectories can help robots learn from demonstrations, plan and guide movements, and model how objects respond to actions.

机器人系统通常依赖现成的跟踪器来自动标注视频中的运动并在执行过程中提供反馈。目前的跟踪器在既定的机器人基准测试中得分很高，这表明它们可能已准备好在现实系统中使用。我们希望验证这些结果是否在机器人实践中遇到的各种场景、视角和物体交互中依然成立。

Robotics systems often rely on off-the-shelf trackers to automatically annotate motion in videos and provide feedback during execution. And current trackers score well on established robotics benchmarks, suggesting they could be ready for wider use in real-world systems. We wanted to see whether those results hold up across the varied scenes, viewpoints, and object interactions robots encounter in practice.

我们推出了 RoboTrack，这是一个用于机器人操作中点跟踪的基准测试和训练数据套件。RoboTrack-Bench 包含来自 DROID、Ego-Exo4D、MolmoAct 2 和 EgoVerse 的 307 个经人工标注的视频，涵盖了不同的机器人和人手、杂乱的场景以及第一和第三人称视角。为了研究训练数据如何影响这些环境下的跟踪效果，我们还扩展了 Ai2 的机器人学习模拟生态系统 MolmoSpaces，以从模拟的机器人操作轨迹中生成密集点轨迹。该流程产生了 RoboTrack-15M，这是一个针对机器人操作的新型合成训练数据集。

We introduce RoboTrack, a benchmark and training data suite for point tracking in robotic manipulation. RoboTrack-Bench contains 307 human-annotated videos from DROID, Ego-Exo4D, MolmoAct 2, and EgoVerse, covering different robots and human hands, cluttered scenes, and first- and third-person viewpoints. To study how training data affects tracking in these settings, we also extend MolmoSpaces, Ai2’s simulation ecosystem for robot learning, to generate dense point tracks from simulated robot manipulation trajectories. This pipeline produces RoboTrack-15M, a new synthetic training dataset targeted toward robotic manipulation.

我们的评估显示，领先的跟踪器在 RoboTrack-Bench 上的得分远低于现有的机器人基准测试。将 RoboTrack-15M 添加到现有跟踪器的训练中可以提高其在真实操作视频中的表现，且这种增益延伸到了其他跟踪基准测试中。我们将这些成果应用到真实机器人上，利用由此产生的跟踪器来辅助实验室任务中的移液管操作。

Our evaluations show that leading trackers score much lower on RoboTrack-Bench than on existing robotics benchmarks. Adding RoboTrack-15M to an existing tracker’s training improves performance on real manipulation video, with gains extending to other tracking benchmarks. We bring these pieces together on a real robot, using the resulting tracker to help guide pipette handling in wet-lab tasks.

今天，我们发布了 RoboTrack 的两个组件以及两个新的点跟踪模型：RoboTracker 和 RoboTracker-Warp。RoboTracker 是使用我们的技术和数据微调后的 AllTracker 模型，而 RoboTracker-Warp 则基于 CoWTracker 训练。与各自发布的基准模型相比，这两个模型在三个机器人基准测试中的平均 Jaccard (AJ) 指数均有所提高，其中 RoboTracker-Warp 在我们评估的五个基准测试中的四个上达到了最先进的性能。

Today, we’re releasing both RoboTrack components and two new point tracking models: RoboTracker and RoboTracker-Warp. RoboTracker is a fine-tuned AllTracker model using our techniques and data, while RoboTracker-Warp is trained from CoWTracker. Both models improve average AJ across the three robotics benchmarks compared with their respective released baselines, and RoboTracker-Warp achieves state-of-the-art performance on four out of the five benchmarks we evaluated.

我们评估了四种领先的跟踪器——CoTracker3、BootsTAPNext、AllTracker 和 CoWTracker——在 RoboTrack-Bench 和其他四个基准测试上的表现。我们报告了平均 Jaccard (AJ) 指数，该指数衡量跟踪准确性和可见性预测；分数越高越好。RoboTrack-Bench 比现有的机器人基准测试更难。我们评估的每一个最终跟踪器在 RoboTrack-Bench 上的得分都低于 RoboTAP（另一个真实视频机器人基准测试），分数下降了 3.0 到 6.6 个 AJ 点。

We evaluated four leading trackers – CoTracker3, BootsTAPNext, AllTracker, and CoWTracker – on RoboTrack-Bench and four other benchmarks: RoboTAP (real-world tabletop manipulation), RGB-Stacking (simulated manipulation), and DAVIS and Kinetics (general video). We report Average Jaccard (AJ), which measures tracking accuracy and visibility prediction; higher scores are better. RoboTrack-Bench is harder than existing robot benchmarks. Every final tracker we evaluated scores lower on RoboTrack-Bench than on RoboTAP, the only other real-video robotics benchmark. Their score drops by 3.0 to 6.6 AJ.
