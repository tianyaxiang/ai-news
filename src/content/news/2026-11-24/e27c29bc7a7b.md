# Nvidia Reinstates Jensen Huang as CEO

Jensen Huang was reinstated as CEO of Nvidia, the board announced in a statement. The Nvidia board said it had input from senior levels of the U.S. government. Huang resigned as CEO on Nov. 13 following reports that he engaged in several extramarital affairs, including one with an alleged Chinese spy. Huang disclosed one affair in a note to employees.

英伟达董事会发表声明，宣布黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）已复职担任首席执行官。英伟达董事会表示，此举参考了美国政府高层的意见。此前，黄仁勋因被曝出多起婚外情（其中包括与一名涉嫌为中国间谍的女子有染）而于11月13日辞去首席执行官职务。黄仁勋曾在给员工的信中披露过其中一段婚外情。

Nvidia on Monday announced Jensen Huang has been reinstated as CEO, effective immediately, a dramatic twist in a scandal that hammered the chipmaker's stock price and rocked the tech and artificial intelligence industries. The board of directors said it unanimously voted to reinstate Huang following "a series of discussions and meetings" with him. The board added that it "received input from senior levels of the United States government" and that it is confident Huang's reinstatement "poses no national security risk."

英伟达周一宣布，黄仁勋即刻复职担任首席执行官。这一戏剧性的转折发生在一场丑闻之后，该丑闻曾重创这家芯片制造商的股价，并震动了科技和人工智能行业。董事会表示，在与黄仁勋进行“一系列讨论和会议”后，一致投票决定让他复职。董事会补充称，他们“收到了美国政府高层的意见”，并确信黄仁勋的复职“不会构成国家安全风险”。

President Donald Trump told CNBC on Monday that he had been speaking to Nvidia's board "directly and indirectly," and that Huang "did nothing wrong." Trump said he talked to Huang on Sunday and asked him to promise to "work hard." Huang resigned from his role as chief executive earlier this month after a series of shocking reports about multiple extramarital affairs, including one with an alleged Chinese spy.

唐纳德·特朗普总统周一告诉CNBC，他曾“直接和间接地”与英伟达董事会进行过沟通，并称黄仁勋“没有做错任何事”。特朗普表示，他周日与黄仁勋进行了交谈，并要求他承诺“努力工作”。本月早些时候，在有关其多起婚外情（包括与一名涉嫌为中国间谍的女子有染）的系列惊人报道传出后，黄仁勋辞去了首席执行官一职。

The New York Times reported on Sunday that the woman, Alice Huang, was in contact with an officer of China's Ministry of State Security during at least part of the time that she was engaging in a relationship with Huang. Shares of Nvidia closed up 31% following Huang's reinstatement. The stock had shed nearly 33% from the time Huang admitted to an extramarital affair on Nov. 8. The stock closed at $186.64 on Nov. 6.

《纽约时报》周日报道称，该女子爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang）在与黄仁勋交往的部分时间里，曾与中国国家安全部的一名官员有过接触。在黄仁勋复职后，英伟达股价收盘上涨31%。自黄仁勋11月8日承认婚外情以来，该股已下跌近33%。该股11月6日的收盘价为186.64美元。

"I am honored to return to NVIDIA and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a new industrial revolution," Huang said in a statement. "I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment. I want to thank the NVIDIA Board for its confidence and deliberation through this process."

黄仁勋在声明中表示：“我很荣幸能回到英伟达，在新的工业革命开启之际，领导这家世界上最重要的科技公司之一。我怀着最谦卑的心情和全新的承诺承担起这一责任。我要感谢英伟达董事会在这一过程中给予的信任和审慎考虑。”
