# Nvidia Is Powering Brain-Computer Interfaces Now

A Michigan woman named Mary has a chip in her brain roughly the size of a dime. Though she struggles to speak due to motor neuron disease, the implant allows her to express her thoughts through a synthesized voice on a computer.

一位名叫玛丽的密歇根州女性大脑中植入了一枚大约一角硬币大小的芯片。尽管她因运动神经元疾病而难以说话，但这种植入物使她能够通过计算机上的合成语音表达自己的想法。

Made by brain-computer interface company Paradromics, the implant is outwardly invisible, but the speech decoding requires a cart full of electronics. A year from now, however, that cart will be transformed into a small box that can sit on her wheelchair, thanks to technology from Nvidia. Eventually, those electronics will be so small they’ll fit in a phone, says Paradromics CEO and founder Matt Angle.

该植入物由脑机接口公司 Paradromics 制造，从外观上看是隐形的，但语音解码过程需要一整车电子设备。然而，得益于英伟达（Nvidia）的技术，一年后，这辆车将被改造成一个可以放在她轮椅上的小盒子。Paradromics 首席执行官兼创始人马特·安格尔（Matt Angle）表示，最终这些电子设备将小到可以装进手机里。

Today, Austin-based Paradromics announced a partnership with the AI chipmaker to use Nvidia’s hardware and software to process its brain data. Nvidia is facing a major leadership shakeup and federal scrutiny following longtime CEO Jensen Huang’s resignation over an affair with an alleged Chinese spy.

今天，总部位于奥斯汀的 Paradromics 宣布与这家人工智能芯片制造商建立合作伙伴关系，利用英伟达的硬件和软件来处理其大脑数据。在长期担任首席执行官的黄仁勋因与一名据称是中国间谍的人员有染而辞职后，英伟达正面临重大的领导层变动和联邦审查。

Though Mary has lost much of the use of her speaking muscles, neurons in her brain still fire when she attempts to speak. The Paradromics implant uses more than 400 embedded electrodes to capture those signals, and a computer uses artificial intelligence to make inferences about what she’s trying to say. The words appear on a screen, and the computer speaks the words for her.

尽管玛丽已经失去了大部分说话肌肉的使用能力，但当她尝试说话时，她大脑中的神经元仍然会放电。Paradromics 的植入物使用 400 多个嵌入式电极来捕捉这些信号，计算机则利用人工智能对她试图表达的内容进行推断。文字会出现在屏幕上，计算机随后会替她读出这些话。

That decoding process takes an incredible amount of computing power. The cart of electronics has to be plugged into an electrical outlet, and Mary must be within range of the cart in order to use her brain-computer interface.

这一解码过程需要惊人的计算能力。那辆电子设备车必须插在电源插座上，而且玛丽必须在车的范围内才能使用她的脑机接口。

“We're producing a huge amount of data that has a lot of value, but we need to translate that neural data into applications in order to make this a really effective platform,” Angle says. It’s why he calls brain-computer interfaces the ultimate case for edge computing, a distributed computing model that processes and stores data in physical devices close to where it is generated rather than sending it to a distant cloud or data center.

“我们正在产生大量极具价值的数据，但我们需要将这些神经数据转化为应用程序，才能使其成为一个真正有效的平台，”安格尔说。这就是他将脑机接口称为边缘计算终极案例的原因，边缘计算是一种分布式计算模型，它在数据生成地附近的物理设备中处理和存储数据，而不是将其发送到遥远的云端或数据中心。

“We want to be able to do it through increasingly smaller and lower-power devices, and the people leading in that area are Nvidia,” he says.

“我们希望能够通过越来越小、功耗越来越低的设备来实现这一目标，而该领域的领导者正是英伟达，”他说。

Making brain-computer interfaces practical and portable for patients is a major challenge for the field. While these setups have become far more streamlined since their early days in academic labs, they still require lots of external electronics. Shrinking them down and reducing their power requirements would allow patients to be more mobile with these devices, using them wherever they go.

使脑机接口对患者而言既实用又便携是该领域的一大挑战。虽然这些装置自学术实验室的早期阶段以来已经精简了许多，但它们仍然需要大量的外部电子设备。缩小它们的体积并降低功耗要求，将使患者能够更灵活地携带这些设备，随时随地使用它们。

Paradromics plans to do that by using Nvidia’s Jetson, a low-powered computing module that will allow speech decoding to be done in a small device close to the user. It will also use Holoscan, Nvidia’s AI sensor processing software, to allow users to interact with computers and other devices with low lag time. Brain-computer interfaces will need to work alongside other sensors, such as cameras and microphones, and Holoscan will help process these different data streams together so applications can respond to both neural signals and the context around them.

Paradromics 计划通过使用英伟达的 Jetson 来实现这一目标，这是一种低功耗计算模块，允许在靠近用户的微型设备中进行语音解码。它还将使用英伟达的人工智能传感器处理软件 Holoscan，使用户能够以低延迟与计算机和其他设备进行交互。脑机接口需要与其他传感器（如摄像头和麦克风）协同工作，而 Holoscan 将有助于整合处理这些不同的数据流，使应用程序能够同时响应神经信号及其周围的环境背景。

“Brain-computer interfaces must translate complex neural signals into meaningful actions in real time. Even small delays can make communication feel unnatural,” David Niewolny, Nvidia’s director of business development for health care and medical, tells WIRED via email. “Neural signals also change over time, requiring AI that can continuously adapt to the individual.” Nvidia’s technology processes neural signals and runs AI models in real time.

“脑机接口必须实时将复杂的神经信号转化为有意义的行动。即使是微小的延迟也会让交流感觉不自然，”英伟达医疗保健与医疗业务发展总监大卫·尼沃尼（David Niewolny）通过电子邮件告诉《连线》（WIRED）杂志。“神经信号也会随时间变化，因此需要能够持续适应个人的 AI。”英伟达的技术可以实时处理神经信号并运行人工智能模型。

Reducing lag time is important for a brain-computer interface with a speech function so that users can have naturally flowing conversation without delays.

对于具有语音功能的脑机接口来说，减少延迟非常重要，这样用户才能进行自然流畅、没有停顿的对话。

Through the partnership, Paradromics will also be able to integrate Nvidia’s foundation models into its brain-computer interface. Vikash Gilja, chief scientific officer at Paradromics, says AI models could help make synthesized voices more expressive and human-like. Also, if these models are running locally on Nvidia hardware, it means users won’t have to be connected to the internet in order to use their devices.

通过此次合作，Paradromics 还将能够把英伟达的基础模型集成到其脑机接口中。Paradromics 首席科学官维卡什·吉尔贾（Vikash Gilja）表示，人工智能模型有助于使合成语音更具表现力和人情味。此外，如果这些模型在英伟达硬件上本地运行，意味着用户无需连接互联网即可使用他们的设备。

Niewolny says he sees “tremendous potential for AI to expand human capability.” Nvidia is working with other players in the brain-computer interface space, including Synchron. The chipmaker will not have access to brain data through these partnerships, he says.

尼沃尼表示，他看到了“人工智能在扩展人类能力方面的巨大潜力”。英伟达正在与脑机接口领域的其他参与者合作，包括 Synchron。他表示，这家芯片制造商不会通过这些合作关系获取大脑数据。
