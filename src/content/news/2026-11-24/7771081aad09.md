### Tracking partial payments in Excel: Amount Paid = Remaining

Customers rarely pay everything at once. A customer pays half today and the rest next week; a supplier gets a deposit now and the balance after delivery. If your sheet only records "paid" or "not paid", those in-between amounts disappear — and month-end totals quietly go wrong.

客户很少会一次性付清所有款项。客户今天支付一半，下周支付剩余部分；供应商现在收到定金，交货后再收取余款。如果你的表格只记录“已付”或“未付”，那些中间金额就会消失，导致月末总计悄然出错。

The fix is three columns per item: Amount, Paid, Remaining. Remaining is one formula: Amount − Paid. When a customer pays part, type the amount into Paid and Remaining updates by itself. A Status column then compares the payment due date with one reference date, so each item sorts itself into done, payment due, payment due soon, or payment not yet due.

解决方法是为每个项目设置三列：金额、已付、剩余。剩余额只需一个公式：金额 − 已付。当客户支付部分款项时，在“已付”列输入金额，“剩余”列会自动更新。随后，通过“状态”列将付款到期日与一个参考日期进行对比，这样每个项目就会自动归类为已完成、付款到期、即将到期或尚未到期。

In my November 2026 sample — 40 items, reference date 12 Nov 2026 — the sheet splits into 11 done, 5 payment due, 14 payment due soon, 10 payment not yet due. Receivables 33,140,000 VND, payables 29,170,000 VND, remaining 37,980,000 VND. The totals on the Overview sheet read straight from the Remaining column, so partial payments never get lost.

在我 2026 年 11 月的样本中（共 40 个项目，参考日期为 2026 年 11 月 12 日），表格显示：11 个已完成，5 个付款到期，14 个即将到期，10 个尚未到期。应收账款为 33,140,000 越南盾，应付账款为 29,170,000 越南盾，剩余金额为 37,980,000 越南盾。“概览”表中的总计直接读取自“剩余”列，因此部分付款永远不会丢失。

Two habits keep it honest: record the payment on the day it arrives, not at month-end; and keep one reference date for the whole sheet, so changing it updates every status at once.
