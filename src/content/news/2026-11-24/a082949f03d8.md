# Richpanel helps companies automate 50% of support in 30 days

Richpanel 帮助企业在 30 天内实现 50% 的支持自动化。

November 23, 2026 Startup. Richpanel uses GPT‑6 Astra and GPT‑6.1 Sol to equip its AI support agents with tools and procedures, and GPT‑6 Luna for their conversations. Start building with OpenAI. Company size: Startup. Region: Asia-Pacific & Oceania. Industry: Technology. Products: API. Share.

2026 年 11 月 23 日，初创公司。Richpanel 使用 GPT-6 Astra 和 GPT-6.1 Sol 为其 AI 支持代理配备工具和流程，并使用 GPT-6 Luna 进行对话。开始使用 OpenAI 构建。公司规模：初创公司。地区：亚太及大洋洲。行业：科技。产品：API。分享。

Beyond knowing how to answer a customer’s request, AI support agents need to be able to execute a solution. And for that, they need access to the right tools and instructions. According to Richpanel CEO Amit RG, human support agents can use 20 or 30 tools to serve a customer, and the procedures they follow may never have been written down.

除了知道如何回答客户的请求外，AI 支持代理还需要能够执行解决方案。为此，他们需要访问正确的工具和说明。据 Richpanel 首席执行官 Amit RG 称，人工支持代理可能需要使用 20 到 30 种工具来服务客户，而他们遵循的流程可能从未被书面记录下来。

Richpanel solves that using OpenAI models to both set up the tools and procedures AI support agents need to act and handle customer conversations. “The intelligence is no longer the bottleneck. It’s everything around it.”—Amit RG, CEO, Richpanel.

Richpanel 使用 OpenAI 模型来解决这一问题，既能设置 AI 支持代理行动所需的工具和流程，又能处理客户对话。“智能不再是瓶颈。瓶颈在于围绕智能的一切。”——Amit RG，Richpanel 首席执行官。

Building the tools and procedures for AI support. Deploying a support agent often requires a forward-deployed engineer to connect the agent to a customer’s systems and define how it should work. Amit saw that many companies didn’t have engineers to spare and asked himself, “What if that forward-deployed engineer itself was an AI agent?”

构建 AI 支持的工具和流程。部署支持代理通常需要一名驻场工程师将代理连接到客户的系统并定义其工作方式。Amit 发现许多公司没有多余的工程师，于是问自己：“如果那名驻场工程师本身就是一个 AI 代理会怎样？”

So Richpanel built it: an AI manager to handle agent setup. Customers began calling it Boss AI. It writes procedures and defines how Richpanel’s frontline support agent, copilot, and quality-assurance agent call the tools they need. Richpanel uses GPT‑6 Astra and GPT‑6.1 Sol for Boss AI, prioritizing accuracy when building the tools and procedures its other agents will follow.

于是 Richpanel 构建了它：一个用于处理代理设置的 AI 管理器。客户开始称其为“Boss AI”。它负责编写流程，并定义 Richpanel 的一线支持代理、副驾驶（copilot）和质量保证代理如何调用所需的工具。Richpanel 为 Boss AI 使用了 GPT-6 Astra 和 GPT-6.1 Sol，在构建其他代理将遵循的工具和流程时，优先考虑准确性。

Balancing intelligence and cost across support operations. Richpanel designs the frontline agent to handle hundreds of thousands of conversations, making the cost of each interaction an important part of model selection. It uses GPT‑6 Luna and other OpenAI models for that work. Amit says Richpanel takes the same approach to its copilot and quality-assurance agents.

在支持运营中平衡智能与成本。Richpanel 设计的一线代理旨在处理数十万次对话，这使得每次交互的成本成为模型选择的重要组成部分。它为此使用了 GPT-6 Luna 和其他 OpenAI 模型。Amit 表示，Richpanel 对其副驾驶和质量保证代理采取了同样的方法。

“What matters is a balance of cost and intelligence,” Amit says. Richpanel’s customers may already use low-cost outsourced support. Automating their operations has to make financial sense against that starting point. Amit credits OpenAI with making models available that can handle long context and longer tasks while remaining cost efficient.

“重要的是成本与智能之间的平衡，”Amit 说。Richpanel 的客户可能已经在使用低成本的外包支持。自动化其运营必须在这一起点上具有财务意义。Amit 将功劳归于 OpenAI，因为他们提供的模型能够处理长上下文和更长的任务，同时保持成本效益。

Richpanel can use deeper reasoning to prepare its support agents and more economical models to run the conversations that follow. “OpenAI models can run these long-context, longer-horizon tasks while still being very cost efficient.”—Amit RG, CEO, Richpanel.

Richpanel 可以利用更深层的推理来准备其支持代理，并使用更经济的模型来运行随后的对话。“OpenAI 模型可以运行这些长上下文、长周期的任务，同时仍然非常具有成本效益。”——Amit RG，Richpanel 首席执行官。

Increasing contact center automation and customer satisfaction. At the time of the October 2026 interview, Amit said Richpanel had completed approximately 300 AI customer-support deployments over the previous six months.

提高联络中心自动化程度和客户满意度。在 2026 年 10 月的采访时，Amit 表示 Richpanel 在过去六个月中完成了大约 300 次 AI 客户支持部署。

The company guarantees customers a 50% automation rate within the first 30 days or their money back. Amit says it met that commitment across all of those deployments, adding, “In each of the cases, the AI is beating human support agents when it comes to customer satisfaction.”

该公司向客户保证在最初 30 天内实现 50% 的自动化率，否则退款。Amit 表示，它在所有这些部署中都兑现了这一承诺，并补充道：“在每一个案例中，AI 在客户满意度方面都超过了人工支持代理。”
