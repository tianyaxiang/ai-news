# Amazon fights Delivery Protection Act by putting stickers on its packages

Amazon is placing a sticker on some of its delivery boxes to urge customers to oppose a New York City bill. This weekend, some Amazon shoppers reported getting a delivery from Amazon with a sticker on it that reads: “NYC Council could add up to $5 per order. Act now to protect low prices.” The sticker reportedly includes a QR code that leads to a link for contacting NYC Councilmembers. The sticker encourages the package’s recipient to “Scan to tell your council member: no increases for New Yorkers.”

亚马逊正在其部分快递包裹上张贴贴纸，敦促客户反对纽约市的一项法案。本周末，一些亚马逊购物者反映收到的包裹上贴着一张贴纸，上面写着：“纽约市议会可能会为每笔订单增加高达 5 美元的费用。立即行动，保护低价。”据报道，该贴纸包含一个二维码，链接到联系纽约市议员的页面。贴纸鼓励包裹接收者“扫描并告诉你的议员：不要增加纽约人的负担。”

Amazon is looking to stoke opposition to Introduction 0518-2026 (518), also known as the Delivery Protection Act. The proposed law would require Amazon and other companies with last-mile delivery facilities in the city, such as FedEx, to directly employ third-party contractors Amazon uses for making deliveries. Amazon previously sent mailers to NYC residents regarding a new headquarters (which was subsequently scrapped), but political messaging on packages is unprecedented for Amazon, which hasn’t used its delivery boxes to lobby for political causes before.

亚马逊正试图煽动对 0518-2026 号提案（即《快递保护法》）的反对。该拟议法律将要求亚马逊及其他在市内设有“最后一公里”配送设施的公司（如联邦快递）直接雇佣亚马逊用于配送的第三方承包商。亚马逊此前曾向纽约市居民邮寄过关于新总部的宣传材料（该计划随后被取消），但在包裹上发布政治信息对亚马逊来说尚属首次，该公司此前从未利用其快递箱进行政治游说。

Last week, Amazon made another uncharacteristic move by showing NYC shoppers a homepage banner ad—which I see when logging in to Amazon as of this writing—and an in-app pop-up that also urges opposition to the bill. The banner and pop-up include a link to a blog where Amazon says the law would force it to consider moving operations outside of NYC and lead to “slower and more expensive delivery for millions of customers in New York City, while also threatening small businesses and their thousands of employees.” Mayor Zohran Mamdani and the Amazon division of the International Brotherhood of Teamsters, however, support the bill.

上周，亚马逊采取了另一项不同寻常的举措，向纽约市的购物者展示了主页横幅广告（截至本文撰写时，我登录亚马逊时仍能看到）以及应用内弹窗，同样敦促反对该法案。横幅和弹窗中包含一个指向博客的链接，亚马逊在博客中称，该法律将迫使其考虑将业务迁出纽约市，并导致“数百万纽约市客户的配送速度变慢且成本更高，同时还会威胁到小企业及其数千名员工”。然而，市议员佐兰·曼达尼（Zohran Mamdani）和国际卡车司机工会（International Brotherhood of Teamsters）的亚马逊部门则支持该法案。

By using its packages, website, and app to advocate against the bill, Amazon hopes to leverage its large customer base to push a political ideal. This raises concerns about whether it’s presenting the debate fairly, the appropriateness of using an e-commerce platform for political purposes, and whether we’ll see more political messaging while shopping with Amazon or other companies in the future. As it stands, some shoppers are already put off by Amazon’s lobbying. “Something about this just feels off to me,” a Reddit user who apparently received a package with the sticker wrote.

通过利用其包裹、网站和应用程序来反对该法案，亚马逊希望利用其庞大的客户群来推动某种政治理念。这引发了人们的担忧：亚马逊是否在公平地呈现这场辩论？利用电子商务平台进行政治宣传是否得当？以及未来我们在亚马逊或其他公司购物时是否会看到更多的政治信息？目前，一些购物者已经对亚马逊的游说感到反感。一位显然收到了贴有该贴纸包裹的 Reddit 用户写道：“这让我感觉有些不对劲。”

In a statement to CNBC last week, Amazon spokesperson Kelly Nantel said the company is using the homepage and in-app messages to be “transparent with our customers” about what the proposed law “would cause them if it passes as written.” As of Friday, Amazon shoppers had sent over 60,000 emails to NYC Councilmembers opposing the bill, NBC New York reported. The City Council’s Committee on Consumer and Worker Protection will decide whether to advance the bill to the entire council. The date for that vote hasn’t been set yet.

在上周给 CNBC 的一份声明中，亚马逊发言人凯利·南特尔（Kelly Nantel）表示，公司利用主页和应用内消息是为了“对客户保持透明”，告知他们如果该法案按原样通过，“会给他们带来什么后果”。据 NBC 纽约频道报道，截至周五，亚马逊购物者已向纽约市议员发送了超过 6 万封反对该法案的电子邮件。市议会的消费者和工人保护委员会将决定是否将该法案提交至全体议会。投票日期尚未确定。
