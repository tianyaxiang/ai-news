Welcome to elite impunity, Jensen Huang. Consequences are for the poors. By Elizabeth Lopatto, Nov 23, 2026.

欢迎来到精英豁免权时代，黄仁勋。后果是留给穷人的。伊丽莎白·洛帕托（Elizabeth Lopatto）于2026年11月23日报道。

I suppose I must issue a hearty congratulations to Jensen Huang. Huang is CEO of Nvidia again, to the surprise of basically everyone except President Donald Trump and Vice President JD Vance, who made sure it happened.

我想我必须向黄仁勋表示衷心的祝贺。黄仁勋再次担任英伟达首席执行官，这让除了唐纳德·特朗普总统和JD·万斯副总统之外的所有人都感到惊讶，正是他们确保了这一切的发生。

Now, those of us who had some kind of basic education on what a “free market” is might find it objectionable to have the American president strong-arming a major American company. But our billionaire tech overlords care about the “free market” exactly as much as they care about “free speech,” which is to say not at all.

现在，我们这些受过关于什么是“自由市场”基本教育的人，可能会觉得美国总统强迫一家大型美国公司行事令人反感。但我们的亿万富翁科技巨头们对“自由市场”的关心程度，与他们对“言论自由”的关心程度完全一样，也就是说，他们根本不在乎。

Watching Silicon Valley cheer for what is, effectively, a sign of a command economy has certainly been bracing. But then, why would they give a shit? Their only real beliefs are that they should remain rich and above the law. Jensen may not have exchanged emails with Jeffrey Epstein, but he’s squarely at the Epstein table now.

看着硅谷为这种实际上是指令性经济的迹象欢呼，确实令人警醒。但话说回来，他们为什么要介意呢？他们唯一真正的信仰就是他们应该保持富有并凌驾于法律之上。黄仁勋可能没有和杰弗里·爱泼斯坦交换过电子邮件，但他现在已经稳稳地坐在了爱泼斯坦的桌旁。

I have argued here, in the past, that the Epstein case laid bare the real politics of the revanchist American right wing: total elite impunity. Well, here it is again! If you are close enough with Trump, it doesn’t matter if you may have jeopardized national security by fucking an alleged Chinese spy. You won’t even be investigated for it before the American president calls in to your board of directors’ meeting to demand you be reinstated.

我过去曾在这里论证过，爱泼斯坦案揭露了美国复仇主义右翼的真实政治：彻底的精英豁免权。好吧，现在它又出现了！如果你与特朗普关系足够密切，那么即使你因为与一名涉嫌中国间谍的人发生关系而危及国家安全，也无关紧要。在美国总统打电话给你的董事会要求让你复职之前，你甚至不会因此受到调查。

Just so we can be absolutely clear about what transpired at Nvidia, let’s quickly go through the facts as we know them. A Chinese citizen named Alice Huang acquired the company she renamed Megaspeed, the Singaporean data firm at the center of this inquiry, in 2023. At some point, Huang met Huang, and the two were seen together at a dinner that December. From 2024 to 2025, Megaspeed received more than 200 Nvidia shipments.

为了让我们清楚地了解英伟达发生了什么，让我们快速梳理一下已知的事实。一名叫爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang）的中国公民在2023年收购了一家公司，并将其更名为Megaspeed，即本次调查核心的那家新加坡数据公司。在某个时间点，黄仁勋与爱丽丝·黄相识，两人在当年12月的一次晚宴上被看到在一起。从2024年到2025年，Megaspeed收到了超过200批次的英伟达货物。

This level of open corruption is genuinely impressive, and not just because Huang is CEO of Nvidia again. Almost in passing, Trump noted that he had “protected” Musk in his first term from “a certain incident [that] took place, a very tough incident.” In his own inimitable style, Trump told Jim Cramer, “You wouldn’t have Elon if it wasn’t for me.” Surely there is no possible downside when the CEOs of two of the biggest corporations on Earth owe their jobs to Trump personally.

这种程度的公开腐败确实令人印象深刻，不仅仅是因为黄仁勋再次担任英伟达首席执行官。特朗普几乎是顺口提到，他在第一任期内曾“保护”马斯克免受“某起事件的影响，那是一起非常棘手的事件”。特朗普以其独特的风格告诉吉姆·克莱默：“如果没有我，你就不会有埃隆。”当全球两家最大公司的首席执行官的职位都归功于特朗普个人时，肯定不会有什么负面影响。
