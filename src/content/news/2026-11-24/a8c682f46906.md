Ikke did all of the real heavy lifting, but it takes a community to keep a distribution together. Everything, and I mean everything, is kept together by volunteers lending their time and talent with no expectation that there will be a reward for the effort. We do it because we care for the work being done. Whether it's a critical service enabling a community of tens of thousands to distribute hundreds of petabytes of data that we call "Alpine", or simply enabling a small piece of software to work for personal desires. All of it happens because someone cares enough to do the work.

I love this so much, frankly. It's absolutely flabbergasting what we as a community can do with our spare time. Wiki.alpinelinux.org has been languishing a bit. Not in the sense that it wasn't used; it gets tons of traffic and contains a treasure trove of useful information that people depend on and are excited to contribute to. But between keeping the infrastructure afloat, migrating off of Equinix to new sponsored compute, and mitigating the constant wave of problems, from scale to RCE, that plague a small engineering team trying to serve a massive consumer base, well, it's no wonder that once alice was no longer contributing, the infrastructure languished a bit. This wasn't intentional, nor was it bad. There simply wasn't spare time to contribute to it.

Wiki.alpinelinux.org 此前一直处于停滞状态。这并不是说它没人用；它拥有巨大的流量，并包含着人们所依赖且乐于贡献的宝贵信息。但在维持基础设施运行、从 Equinix 迁移到新的赞助计算资源，以及应对从规模扩展到 RCE 等不断涌现的问题（这些问题困扰着试图服务庞大用户群的小型工程团队）之间，难怪一旦 alice 不再贡献，基础设施就会变得有些荒废。这并非有意为之，也不是坏事，只是确实没有多余的时间去维护它。

Everyone has a unique flair to how they deploy something. Enterprise admins end up deploying things in a real textbook manner, and when you deal with small businesses it's usually a hodgepodge of "just get it done". I haven't had a ton of opportunities to spelunk FOSS infrastructure though! What I found was interesting in its own right, mostly because I don't think I'd have deployed the service this way. What ikke told me was spot on: MediaWiki was a git checkout with extensions and skins vendored as submodules inside the repo.

每个人在部署服务时都有独特的风格。企业管理员倾向于以教科书般的方式部署，而处理小型企业时，通常是“先搞定再说”的大杂烩。不过，我并没有太多机会去探索开源基础设施！我所发现的东西本身就很有趣，主要是因为我不认为我会用这种方式部署该服务。ikke 告诉我的完全正确：MediaWiki 是一个 git checkout，其中的扩展和皮肤作为子模块包含在仓库中。

You could slot in new modules using composer, and as long as you had an appropriate underlying PHP version to work with, you were golden. But that was then mixed with actual wiki data, like images, all of it lumped together in /var/www. Very simple, but not obvious if you'd not dealt with it before. When I got into the server, MediaWiki 1.40.0 was running on PHP 8.2 on top of Alpine 3.20, but the LocalSettings.php that controlled the wiki was generated under 1.25.3.

你可以使用 composer 插入新模块，只要底层 PHP 版本合适，一切就没问题。但它与实际的 wiki 数据（如图片）混合在一起，全部堆在 /var/www 中。非常简单，但如果你以前没处理过，就不会那么直观。当我进入服务器时，MediaWiki 1.40.0 运行在 Alpine 3.20 上的 PHP 8.2 环境中，但控制 wiki 的 LocalSettings.php 是在 1.25.3 版本下生成的。

Just to give you a sense, 1.40.0 is from July 2023, and 1.25.3 is from October of 2015! It's really neat to get a sense for how long something was tended for.
