Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.26756 (cs) [Submitted on 19 Nov 2026] Title: Reinforcement Learning Unlearns Corrupted SFT Reasoning Authors: Anika Misra, Shreyas Vinod, Huteng Dai.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.26756 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 19 日] 标题：强化学习消除受损的 SFT 推理 作者：Anika Misra, Shreyas Vinod, Huteng Dai。

Abstract: We show that reinforcement learning (RL) in reasoning tasks can succeed even when the supervised fine-tuning (SFT) data is severely corrupted. In a mathematical reasoning dataset (GSM8K), we systematically corrupt training trajectories across 361 configurations by injecting realistic error types, varying error locations, trace-answer consistency, and corruption ratios up to 100%.

摘要：我们展示了在推理任务中，即使监督微调（SFT）数据受到严重破坏，强化学习（RL）仍能取得成功。在一个数学推理数据集（GSM8K）中，我们通过注入现实的错误类型、改变错误位置、调整轨迹与答案的一致性以及高达 100% 的破坏比例，在 361 种配置下系统地破坏了训练轨迹。

While aggregated SFT-only performance steadily deteriorates under corruption (dropping from 43.61% to 13.80%), Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) post-training consistently restores model accuracy back to gold-baseline levels (approximately 60%).

虽然仅使用 SFT 的聚合性能在破坏下稳步恶化（从 43.61% 下降至 13.80%），但组相对策略优化（GRPO）训练后能始终将模型准确率恢复至黄金基准水平（约 60%）。

Crucially, initializing RL with as few as N = 8 minimal, incorrect, three-line "form-only" traces boosts post-RL performance to 69.30%, outperforming standard gold SFT initialization (60.71%) and GRPO without SFT (35.00%).

至关重要的是，仅使用 N = 8 条极简、错误且仅包含三行的“仅格式”轨迹来初始化 RL，就能将 RL 后的性能提升至 69.30%，优于标准的黄金 SFT 初始化（60.71%）和不带 SFT 的 GRPO（35.00%）。

Further, we show that SFT accuracy is largely uncorrelated with post-RL accuracy (r approximately 0), demonstrating a fundamental asymmetry in post-training: SFT primarily establishes structural layout (form), while RL independently discovers functional reasoning (function).

此外，我们证明了 SFT 准确率与 RL 后准确率在很大程度上是不相关的（r 约为 0），这揭示了训练后阶段的一个基本不对称性：SFT 主要建立结构布局（形式），而 RL 则独立发现功能性推理（功能）。
