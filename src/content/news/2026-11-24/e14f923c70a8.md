Western intelligence officials worry that Russia could launch a nuclear-armed satellite into orbit before the end of the year, suggesting the country’s development of a satellite-killing weapon of mass destruction is more advanced than previously thought, German media reported Monday.

据德国媒体周一报道，西方情报官员担心俄罗斯可能在今年年底前将一颗核武装卫星送入轨道，这表明该国在开发用于摧毁卫星的大规模杀伤性武器方面，其进展比此前预想的更为先进。

The orbiting nuclear weapon would threaten thousands of satellites in low-Earth orbit, including military and commercial communications networks like Starshield and Starlink. A nuclear-armed satellite would also put at risk Western military surveillance satellites and human spaceflight platforms like the International Space Station. A nuclear detonation in orbit would be indiscriminate, also knocking out Russia’s own spacecraft and Chinese satellites.

这种轨道核武器将威胁到近地轨道上的数千颗卫星，包括“星盾”（Starshield）和“星链”（Starlink）等军事及商业通信网络。核武装卫星还将使西方军事监视卫星和国际空间站等载人航天平台面临风险。在轨道上进行核爆炸是不加区分的，也会摧毁俄罗斯自己的航天器以及中国卫星。

US lawmakers briefed on government intelligence first disclosed Russia’s ambition to deploy a space-based nuclear weapon in early 2024. At the time, US officials said Russia had launched a test satellite in 2022 to test the effects of radiation on space hardware and pave the way for a future nuclear-armed craft, but that Russia had not yet sent a nuclear weapon into orbit.

2024年初，听取了政府情报简报的美国议员首次披露了俄罗斯部署天基核武器的意图。当时，美国官员表示，俄罗斯曾在2022年发射过一颗测试卫星，旨在测试辐射对太空硬件的影响，并为未来的核武装航天器铺平道路，但俄罗斯当时尚未将核武器送入轨道。

Reporting from three German news outlets—public broadcasters NDR and WDR and the Munich-based newspaper Süddeutsche Zeitung—indicates Russia’s launch of a nuclear-armed satellite could be imminent. The report says Western intelligence agencies, including the German Armed Forces and Federal Intelligence Service, believe Russia is preparing to deploy the weapon in space this year. Several NATO countries have exchanged their findings on the matter.

来自三家德国新闻机构——公共广播公司NDR和WDR以及总部位于慕尼黑的《南德意志报》——的报道显示，俄罗斯发射核武装卫星可能迫在眉睫。报道称，包括德国联邦国防军和联邦情报局在内的西方情报机构认为，俄罗斯正准备在今年将该武器部署到太空。几个北约国家已经就此事交换了调查结果。

The nuclear-armed satellite, designated Sputnik-S, has been in storage in the greater Moscow area for years, the German news report said. German Space Command believes the satellite could be launched as early as December. Once in orbit, a nuclear weapon could loiter for months or years, ready for detonation on the command of Russian military forces.

据德国新闻报道，这颗代号为“斯普特尼克-S”（Sputnik-S）的核武装卫星已在大莫斯科地区储存多年。德国太空司令部认为，该卫星最早可能在12月发射。一旦进入轨道，核武器可以在太空中徘徊数月或数年，随时准备在俄罗斯军队的指令下引爆。

The Kremlin dismissed the report. “Given the number of qualifiers like ‘allegedly’ and ‘possibly’—not to mention the fact that the claims are attributed to some unspecified source—they are hardly worth taking seriously,” said Dmitry Peskov, spokesperson for Russian President Vladimir Putin.

克里姆林宫驳斥了这一报道。俄罗斯总统普京的发言人德米特里·佩斯科夫表示：“考虑到报道中使用了大量诸如‘据称’和‘可能’之类的限定词，更不用说这些说法还归咎于某些未指明的来源，它们根本不值得认真对待。”

If it goes ahead, the launch would violate the 1967 Outer Space Treaty, which prohibits the deployment of weapons of mass destruction in space. Russia is a party to the treaty, but the Russian government vetoed a draft UN Security Council resolution in 2024 seeking to reaffirm the prohibition on weapons of mass destruction in space.

如果发射成行，将违反1967年的《外层空间条约》，该条约禁止在太空部署大规模杀伤性武器。俄罗斯是该条约的缔约国，但俄罗斯政府在2024年否决了一项旨在重申禁止在太空部署大规模杀伤性武器的联合国安理会决议草案。

German Chancellor Friedrich Merz did not address the timing of the potential launch when asked about it at a press conference Monday. “The news that Russia is planning to arm space with nuclear weapons is not new, but that doesn’t make it any less shocking,” Merz said. “It shows the extent to which Russia is apparently prepared to escalate the situation.”

德国总理弗里德里希·默茨周一在新闻发布会上被问及此事时，并未谈及潜在发射的时间。默茨说：“俄罗斯计划在太空部署核武器的消息并不新鲜，但这并没有减少其带来的震惊。这表明俄罗斯显然已准备好将局势升级到何种程度。”

The German news reports published Monday come a month after the US military’s Defense Intelligence Agency highlighted the threat of a Russian nuclear-armed satellite. “Russia is developing a satellite designed to carry a nuclear weapon despite its obligation to comply with the 1967 Outer Space Treaty,” the DIA said in a public report on challenges to security in space.

周一发表的德国新闻报道是在美国军方国防情报局强调俄罗斯核武装卫星威胁一个月后发布的。国防情报局在一份关于太空安全挑战的公开报告中称：“尽管有义务遵守1967年的《外层空间条约》，俄罗斯仍在开发一种旨在携带核武器的卫星。”

“A space-based capability such as this could cause rapid and sudden loss or degradation of thousands of satellites operated by countries and companies around the world, as well as to the vital communications, navigation, scientific, meteorological, commercial, and national security services they provide. Beyond its immediate effects, a nuclear detonation in space would also leave lingering radiation belts, which could persist for years and damage satellites that initially were only temporarily disrupted,” the DIA said.

国防情报局表示：“这种天基能力可能导致世界各国和公司运营的数千颗卫星迅速且突然地损毁或性能下降，并影响它们所提供的关键通信、导航、科学、气象、商业和国家安全服务。除了直接影响外，太空核爆炸还会留下持久的辐射带，这些辐射带可能会持续数年，并损坏最初仅受到暂时干扰的卫星。”

The Pentagon increasingly relies upon satellites to support military operations. US forces have used satellites for navigation, strategic communications, missile warning, and surveillance for decades. The US military is now launching satellites to support real-time targeting of missiles, aircraft, and land and sea vehicles, incorporating space platforms into a wider array of offensive and defensive operations.

五角大楼越来越依赖卫星来支持军事行动。几十年来，美军一直利用卫星进行导航、战略通信、导弹预警和监视。美军目前正在发射卫星，以支持对导弹、飞机以及陆海车辆的实时瞄准，并将太空平台纳入更广泛的攻防行动中。

In September, the Pentagon confirmed the Space Force has placed unspecified weapons in orbit. There is no international ban on conventional weapons in space. Leaders in China, which has the second-highest number of operational satellites, have not made any public statements on Russia’s nuclear-armed satellite program. China would be most impacted by a nuclear detonation in orbit after the United States.

9月，五角大楼证实太空军已将未指明的武器送入轨道。目前国际上并没有禁止在太空部署常规武器。中国拥有仅次于美国的在轨运行卫星数量，但中国领导人尚未就俄罗斯的核武装卫星计划发表任何公开声明。在遭受轨道核爆炸影响方面，中国将是继美国之后受影响最大的国家。

Russia has comparatively fewer satellites in orbit, and its military spending in recent years has focused on fighting the war in Ukraine rather than modernizing its space program. If Russia does launch a nuclear-armed satellite, it would be an escalation of the country’s already-bellicose behavior in orbit.

俄罗斯在轨卫星数量相对较少，近年来其军费开支主要集中在乌克兰战争上，而非太空项目的现代化。如果俄罗斯确实发射了核武装卫星，这将是该国在轨道上本已好战行为的升级。

Russia has tested what US officials say is a kinetic anti-satellite weapon and is now operationalizing the system, according to a top US Space Force general. The weapon works by firing projectiles from one satellite toward another. Russia has also launched military satellites into orbits near the US government’s most sophisticated Keyhole-class spy satellites.

据一位美国太空军高级将领称，俄罗斯已经测试了美国官员所说的动能反卫星武器，目前正在将该系统投入使用。该武器的工作原理是从一颗卫星向另一颗卫星发射弹丸。俄罗斯还将军事卫星发射到了靠近美国政府最先进的“锁眼”（Keyhole）级间谍卫星的轨道上。

Last month, a Russian satellite approached very near a commercial radar reconnaissance satellite owned by Finnish company ICEYE. The Russian spacecraft flew within “tens of meters” of the ICEYE satellite, which provides surveillance imagery to the Ukrainian military, before backing away, a US official said. The kinds of weapons Russia has demonstrated so far would take out only a small number of satellites. A proliferated constellation like Starlink or the Pentagon’s new airborne and ground targeting satellites could absorb such an attack.

上个月，一颗俄罗斯卫星非常接近芬兰ICEYE公司拥有的一颗商业雷达侦察卫星。一位美国官员表示，这艘俄罗斯航天器在撤离前，飞到了距离为乌克兰军队提供监视图像的ICEYE卫星“几十米”范围内。俄罗斯迄今展示的武器类型只能摧毁少量卫星。像“星链”或五角大楼新的机载和地面瞄准卫星这样的大规模卫星星座，能够承受此类攻击。
