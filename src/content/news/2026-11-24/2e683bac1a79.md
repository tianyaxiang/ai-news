Trump engineered a risky comeback for Jensen Huang. Trump got his wish to reinstate Jensen Huang at the helm of Nvidia — but investigations are still looming.

特朗普为黄仁勋策划了一场充满风险的回归。特朗普如愿以偿地让黄仁勋重掌英伟达，但相关调查依然阴云密布。

After Nvidia’s board accepted Jensen Huang’s resignation as CEO, following reports that Huang had an affair with an alleged Chinese spy, President Donald Trump, too, appeared to submit to the new reality. Appearing on Stephen A. Smith’s radio show shortly after the news came out, Trump said that Huang would “end up on his feet.”

在英伟达董事会接受黄仁勋辞去首席执行官职务（此前有报道称黄仁勋与一名涉嫌为中国间谍的女子有染）后，唐纳德·特朗普总统似乎也接受了这一新现实。在新闻传出后不久，特朗普在斯蒂芬·A·史密斯的广播节目中表示，黄仁勋“最终会站稳脚跟”。

“You know the expression, anybody can be replaced,” Trump said, floating Apple’s ex-CEO Tim Cook as a potential CEO candidate. It didn’t take long for Trump to begin softening his approach, making it clear he’d welcome Huang back into the role of CEO even if Nvidia’s own board wasn’t ready to do so.

“你知道那句老话，任何人都是可以被取代的，”特朗普说道，并提出苹果前首席执行官蒂姆·库克是潜在的继任人选。没过多久，特朗普就开始软化态度，明确表示即使英伟达董事会尚未准备好，他也欢迎黄仁勋重返首席执行官职位。

Behind the scenes, the Trump administration was working to grease the landing for Huang’s return. And soon, Nvidia’s board changed its tune: Barely a week after insisting they all agreed to shut the door on Huang’s return, they voted unanimously to reinstate him as CEO, president, and board director.

在幕后，特朗普政府正致力于为黄仁勋的回归铺平道路。很快，英伟达董事会改变了口径：在坚称一致同意拒绝黄仁勋回归仅一周后，他们便全票通过决议，恢复其首席执行官、总裁及董事会成员的职务。

“There’s nothing wrong with a comeback,” Trump said in a celebratory call to CNBC. Trump then posted an image on Truth Social of Huang transposed onto Trump’s own body in the infamous photo of him raising his fist after being shot at a campaign rally. In his raised fist, Huang-on-Trump’s-body is clutching a scarlet bra, holding it high in the air.

“回归并没有什么错，”特朗普在给CNBC的庆祝电话中说道。随后，特朗普在“真实社交”（Truth Social）平台上发布了一张图片，将黄仁勋的头像合成到了他自己在竞选集会上遇刺后举拳的照片上。在合成图中，那只高举的拳头里紧握着一件深红色的胸罩，高高地举在空中。

Even after a decisive blow to the president’s power as Democrats swept the midterm elections, Trump appears to maintain his position as a corporate kingmaker. Trump said he spoke with Huang the night prior to his reinstatement and had been in communication with the board both “directly and indirectly.”

尽管民主党在期中选举中大获全胜，对总统权力造成了决定性打击，但特朗普似乎仍保持着他作为企业“造王者”的地位。特朗普表示，他在黄仁勋复职的前一天晚上与其通过话，并一直与董事会保持着“直接和间接”的沟通。

White House communications director Steven Cheung said in a statement that “President Trump was totally involved” in the board’s decision — Trump decided near the end of last week that he wanted to lead efforts to get Huang reappointed, per The New York Times, so he asked Vice President JD Vance to act as a liaison between Huang and Nvidia’s board, providing reassurances that the move wouldn’t pose a security risk.

白宫通讯联络主任史蒂文·张在一份声明中表示，“特朗普总统完全参与了”董事会的决定。据《纽约时报》报道，特朗普在上周末决定亲自领导黄仁勋的复职工作，因此他要求副总统万斯担任黄仁勋与英伟达董事会之间的联络人，并保证此举不会构成安全风险。
