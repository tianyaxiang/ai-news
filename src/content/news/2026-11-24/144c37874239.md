Las Vegas welcomed Formula 1 to its streets this past weekend. And for the first time since the event’s inception in 2023, the Grand Prix delivered on a long-promised spectacle. The racing was closer to an IndyCar oval race than anything F1 fans will have seen since slipstreaming was the order of the day at places like Monza and Hockenheim, back when humans were still landing on the Moon and downforce had yet to fundamentally change racing.

上周末，拉斯维加斯迎来了世界一级方程式锦标赛（F1）。自2023年该赛事创办以来，这届大奖赛首次兑现了长期承诺的精彩场面。比赛过程更像是一场印地赛车（IndyCar）椭圆赛道竞逐，这是F1车迷自蒙扎和霍根海姆赛道“尾流效应”时代以来从未见过的景象——那时人类还在登月，下压力也尚未从根本上改变赛车运动。

The Vegas street circuit—the one that looks like an upside-down pig—is an extreme outlier on the calendar. It has plenty of long straights, so this year’s cars still suffer from energy starvation throughout the lap, as the inadequate size of their lithium-ion hybrid batteries means they become depleted very quickly, leaving the cars on just over half power with their V6 engines operating without the benefit of the hybrid’s electric motor.

拉斯维加斯街道赛道——看起来像一只倒过来的猪——是赛历中一个极端的异类。赛道拥有许多长直道，因此今年的赛车在整圈行驶中仍会遭遇能量匮乏，因为其锂离子混合动力电池容量不足，导致电量消耗极快，使得赛车在失去混合动力电机辅助的情况下，仅能以刚过半的功率驱动V6引擎。

But the 17 turns are mostly all low speed, so there’s no risk of seeing the cars driving demoralizingly slowly through corners that are supposed to test one’s mettle. And because it takes place at night, in the desert, in November, it’s cold—much, much colder than anywhere else the sport will visit all year. This coming weekend in the Middle East, track temperatures may well exceed 122° F (50° C); on Saturday night in Nevada the track was a mere 59° F (15° C).

但赛道的17个弯道大多是低速弯，因此不必担心赛车在考验车手胆识的弯道中以令人沮丧的低速行驶。由于比赛在11月的沙漠之夜举行，天气非常寒冷——比该项赛事全年造访的任何地方都要冷得多。即将到来的中东站比赛，赛道温度很可能会超过50°C（122°F）；而在内华达州的周六晚上，赛道温度仅为15°C（59°F）。

Combined with the concrete streets, that spells low grip, but there is an added benefit—virtually no tire wear or overheating. FP1 was completely rained out. A few drivers completed installation laps, but the risk of a crash was far too high.

结合混凝土街道的特性，这意味着抓地力较低，但也有一个额外的好处——几乎没有轮胎磨损或过热问题。第一次自由练习赛（FP1）因雨完全取消。几位车手完成了安装圈，但发生碰撞的风险实在太高。

And finally, there’s the slipstream. This was far more powerful than in years past, thanks to the new aerodynamic regulations. We saw this play out in qualifying, which took place on Friday night, when Mercedes’ Kimi Antonelli snatched pole position by being the last man on track and benefiting as the cars ahead cleared the air out of his way.

最后是尾流效应。得益于新的空气动力学规则，今年的尾流效应比往年强大得多。我们在周五晚上的排位赛中就看到了这一点：梅赛德斯车队的基米·安东内利（Kimi Antonelli）作为最后一位出场的车手，受益于前方车辆清理出的气流，成功夺得了杆位。

Conversely, the Ferrari duo of Lewis Hamilton and Charles Leclerc had a torrid time by not working together—a preview for the following night, as we’ll see… Antonelli’s pole was quickly forgotten, though, as the new World Champion would only start sixth due to a grid penalty for using an engine component outside his allowance.

相反，法拉利车队的刘易斯·汉密尔顿（Lewis Hamilton）和查尔斯·勒克莱尔（Charles Leclerc）因未能协同作战而度过了一段艰难时光——这正如我们随后将看到的，是次日正赛的前奏……不过，安东内利的杆位很快被人们遗忘，因为这位新科世界冠军因使用超出配额的引擎部件而受到罚退，只能从第六位起步。

Because of the penalty, Red Bull’s Max Verstappen was on pole, with Mercedes’ George Russell next to him. To say there is little love lost between these two drivers would be to undersell the depths of animosity in that relationship. This was evident in FP3, when the pair clashed on track in what looked like a repeat of the Verstappen/Liam Lawson incident in Mexico. This time, no penalties were handed out.

由于罚退，红牛车队的马克斯·维斯塔潘（Max Verstappen）获得杆位，梅赛德斯车队的乔治·拉塞尔（George Russell）紧随其后。说这两位车手之间没什么交情，都低估了他们关系中敌意的深度。这一点在第三次自由练习赛（FP3）中表现得尤为明显，两人在赛道上发生了碰撞，看起来就像是维斯塔潘与利亚姆·劳森（Liam Lawson）在墨西哥站事故的重演。这一次，没有开出任何罚单。

“He does this week in, week out with many drivers, and everybody accepts it, and I’m not just going to accept somebody to screw me over like this and walk around like they’re bigger and better than everyone else. Somebody’s got to stand up to that guy,” Russell said on Friday.

“他每周都对许多车手这样做，每个人都接受了，但我不会接受有人这样坑我，还表现得好像自己比所有人都高人一等。总得有人站出来对抗那个家伙，”拉塞尔在周五说道。

That set up the prospect of a lap 1, turn 1 coming together, but instead the start of the race was remarkably clean. But not boring. Within a lap, Russell had passed Verstappen for the lead on a straight, before the Red Bull reclaimed the position going into turn 17 on the last corner. This set the tone for things to come, with the Mercedes repeatedly getting past the Red Bull at turn 14, only for Verstappen to regain the lead through sheer commitment, taking back his lead in the corners.

这让人预感到第一圈第一弯可能会发生碰撞，但比赛起步却异常干净。不过并不无聊。不到一圈，拉塞尔就在直道上超越维斯塔潘取得领先，随后红牛车手在最后一个弯道（17号弯）夺回了位置。这为接下来的比赛定下了基调：梅赛德斯多次在14号弯超越红牛，但维斯塔潘凭借纯粹的拼劲，在弯道中夺回了领先优势。

Don’t be tempted to call this yo-yo racing; it was nothing like the races we saw early in the season, when everyone was still trying to understand their hybrid deployment and regeneration strategies. Like an oval race, knowing when to try to move up through the pack was vital; choosing it wrong meant losing multiple positions, not just one.

不要试图称之为“溜溜球式”比赛；这与我们在赛季初看到的比赛完全不同，那时大家还在努力理解各自的混合动力部署和能量回收策略。就像椭圆赛道比赛一样，知道何时尝试在车阵中超车至关重要；选择错误意味着会损失多个位置，而不仅仅是一个。

But like an oval, it was also possible to work one’s way up through the pack. The second Red Bull of Isack Hadjar looked more competitive than at any other time this year, and, had the two teammates worked together, they might have been able to leapfrog each other to break free of the pack—a tactic we see used to good effect on oval races.

但像椭圆赛道一样，在车阵中逐步提升名次也是可能的。红牛的第二位车手伊萨克·哈贾尔（Isack Hadjar）看起来比今年任何时候都更具竞争力。如果两位队友能够协同作战，他们或许能够通过交替领先来摆脱车阵——这是我们在椭圆赛道比赛中经常看到的有效战术。

Whether Verstappen’s temperament would allow him to let his teammate past even temporarily remains unknown (I have my doubts), but Red Bull never tried it, instead ordering Hadjar to hold position, leaving him vulnerable to attack from behind. His day was ruined when his front wing exploded on lap 25, dropping him from third to dead last.

维斯塔潘的脾气是否允许他暂时让队友通过仍未可知（我对此表示怀疑），但红牛从未尝试过，反而命令哈贾尔保持位置，这使他容易受到后方的攻击。第25圈他的前翼爆裂，名次从第三跌至最后，他的一天也就此毁了。

So Verstappen never broke free of the chasing pack, with five or six cars covered by as little as 2.5 seconds for much of the time. Again and again Russell took the lead briefly but lost it again to Verstappen, who worried over the radio that the Mercedes team had figured out his strategy and was going to be able to defeat it on the last lap. “I think in the beginning we had the deployment immediately correct, but of course, people are learning and watching what you’re doing. At one point, I couldn’t get back in.”

因此，维斯塔潘始终未能摆脱追赶车阵，五六辆车在大部分时间里差距仅在2.5秒以内。拉塞尔一次又一次短暂取得领先，但又被维斯塔潘夺回。维斯塔潘在无线电中担心梅赛德斯车队已经看穿了他的策略，并可能在最后一圈击败他。“我认为起初我们的部署是正确的，但当然，人们在学习并观察你的动作。有一刻，我无法再回到领先位置。”
