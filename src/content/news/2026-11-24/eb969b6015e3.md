Why using rocks for carbon removal could eventually reduce crop growth. Google, Microsoft, and others are investing hundreds of millions of dollars in startups spreading crushed rock across farms to capture carbon from the air. The approach, known as enhanced rock weathering, could also improve soil quality and boost crop yields. But new research raises serious questions about how much material can be added to farmland before it starts reducing yields, MIT Technology Review can exclusively report. The findings suggest the carbon removal potential could be far smaller than previously estimated. Here’s why major concerns are emerging about enhanced rock weathering. —James Temple
