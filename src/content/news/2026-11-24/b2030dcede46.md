# How to Win Big Betting on Jensen Huang? Listen to Trump

Save this storySave this storyJust days ago, Jensen Huang’s chances of returning as Nvidia CEO looked increasingly slim, at least according to prediction markets. Earlier this month, Huang resigned from the AI hardware giant following reports of multiple extramarital affairs, including one with a woman alleged to have ties to Chinese intelligence services. As more reports emerged about Huang’s alleged bad behavior, Nvidia’s board insisted it had no plans to reinstate him. By early Sunday morning on the East Coast, traders on Polymarket’s US platform put his chances of returning at just 10 percent. But by Monday, Huang was back in charge.

就在几天前，根据预测市场的说法，黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）重返英伟达（Nvidia）担任首席执行官的可能性看起来越来越渺茫。本月早些时候，在多起婚外情报道曝光后，黄仁勋辞去了这家人工智能硬件巨头的职务，其中一名女性被指与中国情报部门有关联。随着更多关于黄仁勋所谓不当行为的报道出现，英伟达董事会坚称没有计划让他复职。到美国东部时间周日清晨，Polymarket美国平台上的交易员认为他回归的可能性仅为10%。但到了周一，黄仁勋已重掌大权。

The swings in Huang’s odds on prediction markets offer a window into how his unlikely comeback unfolded and who saw it coming. The New York Times reported that President Trump played a crucial role in convincing the board to bring Huang back. Traders who paid attention to what Trump and his allies wanted appear to have fared much better than those who did not.

黄仁勋在预测市场赔率的波动，为人们了解他那不可思议的回归过程以及谁预见到了这一结果提供了一个窗口。《纽约时报》报道称，特朗普总统在说服董事会请回黄仁勋的过程中发挥了关键作用。那些关注特朗普及其盟友意图的交易员，似乎比那些不关注的人获利丰厚得多。

On Sunday evening in Washington, Huang’s odds on Polymarket US jumped 6 percentage points in just 40 minutes after Trump published a Truth Social missive calling for Huang’s reinstatement. The president said he wanted to help revive the stock market, which had plunged following Huang’s scandal, a sign of Nvidia’s enormous influence over the AI industry and broader US economy.

周日晚上，在华盛顿，特朗普在“真实社交”（Truth Social）上发布消息呼吁黄仁勋复职后，黄仁勋在Polymarket美国平台上的赔率在短短40分钟内跃升了6个百分点。总统表示，他希望帮助提振股市，此前股市因黄仁勋的丑闻而暴跌，这显示了英伟达对人工智能行业及更广泛的美国经济的巨大影响力。

According to the prediction market intelligence firm Betting Intelligence, the odds on both Kalshi and Polymarket largely tracked with public signals about the likelihood of Huang’s return. They rose when Huang said he was open to returning, fell when the board dismissed the possibility, and climbed again when Trump threw his support behind the former CEO.

据预测市场情报公司Betting Intelligence称，Kalshi和Polymarket上的赔率在很大程度上追踪了有关黄仁勋回归可能性的公开信号。当黄仁勋表示愿意回归时，赔率上升；当董事会排除这种可能性时，赔率下降；而当特朗普对这位前首席执行官表示支持时，赔率再次攀升。

But Betting Intelligence and the prediction market forensics firm MSR Decode also identified clusters of trades that did not appear to be driven by news or public commentary. Kalshi and Polymarket declined to comment on whether they are currently investigating potential insider trading in markets related to Huang’s return.

但Betting Intelligence和预测市场取证公司MSR Decode也发现了一些似乎并非由新闻或公众评论驱动的交易集群。Kalshi和Polymarket拒绝就其目前是否正在调查与黄仁勋回归相关的市场中潜在的内幕交易发表评论。

Despite these anomalies, Betting Intelligence CEO Dan Zimmermann says the Huang-related markets showed fewer signs of potentially suspicious activity than usual. “The group that could have known the board’s decision in advance was small. So the market was mostly headline chasers and long-shot buyers,” he says.

尽管存在这些异常情况，Betting Intelligence首席执行官丹·齐默尔曼（Dan Zimmermann）表示，与黄仁勋相关的市场显示出的潜在可疑活动迹象比平时要少。他说：“能够提前知道董事会决定的人群很小。所以市场主要是由追逐头条新闻的人和押注冷门的人组成的。”

For traders who profited from Huang’s comeback, the lesson is clear: Don’t discount Trump.

对于从黄仁勋回归中获利的交易员来说，教训很明确：不要低估特朗普。

Isaac Rose-Berman, a research and policy fellow at the American Institute for Boys & Men and a professional sports bettor, says he made approximately $10,000 wagering on Huang’s return on Kalshi. He placed his trade after the Nvidia chief said on X that he was open to reclaiming his old job. Rose-Berman says that he figured the board would consider Huang’s return if it looked likely to improve the company’s stock performance. (Never mind the concerns about national security or Huang allegedly threatening employees that he would “pee in your brain.”)

美国男孩与男人研究所（American Institute for Boys & Men）的研究与政策研究员、职业体育博彩玩家艾萨克·罗斯-伯曼（Isaac Rose-Berman）表示，他在Kalshi上押注黄仁勋回归，赚取了约1万美元。他在英伟达首席执行官在X上表示愿意重回旧职后进行了交易。罗斯-伯曼说，他认为如果黄仁勋的回归看起来能改善公司股价表现，董事会就会考虑。（至于有关国家安全的担忧，或者黄仁勋据称威胁员工要“尿在你们脑子里”的言论，他并不在意。）

“It’s 2026 and Trump is president, scandal schmandal,” Rose-Berman says. “Should’ve bet more.”

“现在是2026年，特朗普是总统，什么丑闻不丑闻的，”罗斯-伯曼说。“我当时应该多押点。”

A pseudonymous prediction market trader known as Domer tells WIRED he cleared approximately $600,000 by placing an early wager last week on Huang’s return. He wasn’t focused explicitly on Trump’s desires, but he says his understanding of power dynamics ultimately guided his approach. “Boards can be very malleable,” he tells WIRED.

一位化名为Domer的预测市场交易员告诉《连线》（WIRED），他上周通过对黄仁勋的回归进行早期押注，获利约60万美元。他并没有明确关注特朗普的意愿，但他表示，他对权力动态的理解最终指导了他的操作。他告诉《连线》：“董事会可能是非常容易被左右的。”

Other prediction market “sharps” say that commentary from Trumpworld was the strongest signal available about Huang’s return. As some bettors noted, there was a notable opportunity for profit after Trump suggested to a PBS NewsHour reporter that Huang would be back in power soon, but before Nvidia officially announced his reinstatement. “The key was looking at Trump’s support and the recent performance of the stock market,” says Kalshi trader Caleb Davies.

其他预测市场的“高手”表示，来自特朗普阵营的评论是关于黄仁勋回归最强有力的信号。正如一些博彩者所指出的，在特朗普向PBS新闻一小时（PBS NewsHour）记者暗示黄仁勋很快会重掌大权之后，但在英伟达正式宣布他复职之前，存在一个显著的获利机会。Kalshi交易员凯莱布·戴维斯（Caleb Davies）说：“关键在于关注特朗普的支持以及股市近期的表现。”

Aaron Courtney, a data analyst and cofounder of a prediction market analytics firm, says he took a loss on his Huang position. Looking back, he says his mistake was not paying attention to what Trump and other administration officials said over the weekend. “Trump saying he hoped Nvidia would bring Jensen back would be the clearest signal,” he says.

数据分析师兼一家预测市场分析公司的联合创始人亚伦·考特尼（Aaron Courtney）表示，他在黄仁勋的仓位上亏损了。回顾过去，他说他的错误是没有关注特朗普和其他政府官员在周末说了什么。他说：“特朗普说他希望英伟达能请回黄仁勋，这会是最明确的信号。”
