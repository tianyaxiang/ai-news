Abstract: We investigate whether the geometric structure of neural representations can provide a sufficient learning signal for improving model behavior without any supervision on model outputs. First, we fine-tune Llama-3.1-8B to answer questions such as "What month is four months after June?" by supervising internal representations of the input month (June = 6) and offset (4), which are encoded with Fourier harmonics.

摘要：我们研究了神经表征的几何结构是否能在不对模型输出进行任何监督的情况下，为改善模型行为提供足够的学习信号。首先，我们对 Llama-3.1-8B 进行微调，使其能够回答诸如“六月之后的四个月是几月？”之类的问题，方法是监督输入月份（六月 = 6）和偏移量（4）的内部表征，这些表征通过傅里叶谐波进行编码。

Second, we fine-tune the model to predict line breaks in text wrapped to a fixed width by supervising an internal representation of the number of characters since the preceding newline, which is represented by a low-dimensional helical structure. In both tasks, we supervise a low-dimensional subspace of the residual stream using an idealized target structure, and find that internal geometric supervision achieves similar performance to standard fine-tuning (despite no supervision on outputs).

其次，我们通过监督自上一个换行符以来的字符数的内部表征（该表征由低维螺旋结构表示），对模型进行微调，以预测固定宽度文本中的换行符。在这两项任务中，我们使用理想化的目标结构来监督残差流的低维子空间，并发现内部几何监督实现了与标准微调相似的性能（尽管没有对输出进行监督）。

Moreover, we find that simply patching in an ideal geometry is not sufficient to recover fine-tuning performance, and thus demonstrate geometric supervision provides beneficial effects beyond the representations it targets. Together, these findings show how mechanistic understanding of neural geometry can be leveraged to shape the behavior of neural networks.

此外，我们发现仅仅修补理想的几何结构不足以恢复微调性能，因此证明了几何监督在它所针对的表征之外还提供了有益的影响。总之，这些发现展示了如何利用对神经几何的机制理解来塑造神经网络的行为。
