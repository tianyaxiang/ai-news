Nvidia’s board of directors has voted to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO and chairman of the board, just 11 days after he resigned in the wake of multiple scandals.

英伟达董事会投票决定恢复黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）的首席执行官及董事会主席职务，此时距离他因多项丑闻辞职仅过去了11天。

The reinstatement, announced Monday morning, is “effective immediately” and was based in part on “input from senior levels of the United States government” that assured the board members that “Mr. Huang’s reinstatement poses no national security risk.”

这项于周一上午宣布的复职决定“立即生效”，部分基于“美国政府高层的意见”，这些意见向董事会成员保证“黄先生的复职不会构成任何国家安全风险”。

That assurance comes despite Huang’s admitted affair with a woman who, US intelligence sources believe, was working as a Chinese spy and allegations that Nvidia had subverted export controls meant to keep Nvidia chips from reaching China.

尽管黄仁勋承认与一名被美国情报来源认为是中国间谍的女性有染，且有指控称英伟达规避了旨在阻止其芯片流入中国的出口管制，但政府仍给出了上述保证。

It also comes despite multiple developing investigations from multiple different government departments into those and other alleged national security, accounting, and corporate governance failures.

此外，尽管多个政府部门正在对上述问题以及其他涉嫌国家安全、会计和公司治理失职的行为进行多项调查，该决定依然得以做出。

Huang had been seeking a return to Nvidia since shortly after his resignation, reportedly forming a war room to strategize how best to bring it to pass.

黄仁勋自辞职后不久便一直寻求重返英伟达，据报道，他组建了一个“作战室”来策划如何最好地实现这一目标。

Last week, Huang posted on social media that “if the Board of Directors decides that my return is in the best interest of our shareholders, our employees, the company, and America, I would be honored to answer these calls and return as CEO.”

上周，黄仁勋在社交媒体上发文称：“如果董事会认为我的回归符合股东、员工、公司以及美国的最佳利益，我很荣幸能响应这些呼吁并重返首席执行官岗位。”

President Trump has also been a major backer of Huang’s return to Nvidia. “I hope the Board of Nvidia rehires Jensen Huang,” he wrote on Truth Social last week. “It would be the best thing for the Company, and also, for the U.S.A.”

特朗普总统也是黄仁勋重返英伟达的主要支持者。他上周在“真实社交”（Truth Social）上写道：“我希望英伟达董事会重新聘用黄仁勋。这对公司乃至美国来说都是最好的事情。”

In a social media post immediately following the reinstatement, Trump called it “the correct and proper decision” and confirmed that “the United States Government has no reason to believe that Jensen’s return to NVIDIA poses any risk to National Security.”

在复职消息宣布后，特朗普立即在社交媒体上发文称这是“正确且恰当的决定”，并确认“美国政府没有理由认为黄仁勋重返英伟达会对国家安全构成任何风险”。

Appearing on CNBC Monday morning, Trump said that he had spoken to the Nvidia board “directly and indirectly” about Huang’s reinstatement, and that Huang “did nothing wrong.”

周一上午，特朗普在接受CNBC采访时表示，他曾就黄仁勋的复职与英伟达董事会进行过“直接和间接”的沟通，并称黄仁勋“没有做错任何事”。

Losing “the brainpower of someone like [Huang]” would be “a much bigger threat” than anything related to his alleged interactions with China, Trump said. “We’ve done a very thorough investigation and the investigation showed that… he had bad judgement having to do with somewhat unrelated incidents,” Trump said. “You have to protect our geniuses and there aren’t enough of them.”

特朗普表示，失去“像（黄仁勋）这样的人的智力”比他与中国所谓的互动所带来的任何威胁都要“大得多”。特朗普说：“我们进行了非常彻底的调查，调查显示……他在一些不相关的事情上判断失误。你必须保护我们的天才，因为他们还不够多。”

Huang’s tenure at Nvidia dates back to the company’s founding in 1993, when he and three cofounders were focused on the then-nascent field of GPU hardware aimed at PC gamers.

黄仁勋在英伟达的任期可以追溯到1993年公司成立之时，当时他和三位联合创始人专注于面向PC游戏玩家的GPU硬件这一当时尚处于萌芽阶段的领域。

In the decades since, the highly parallel cores in those GPUs have been embraced first by cryptocurrency miners and then AI companies, propelling the company to its position as the most valuable in the world at its October peak.

在此后的几十年里，这些GPU中的高度并行核心首先受到加密货币矿工的青睐，随后又被人工智能公司采用，推动该公司在10月份达到顶峰时成为全球市值最高的公司。

Nvidia stock—the price of which had sunk roughly 25 percent since Huang’s resignation was announced and nearly 50 percent since its peak—was up about 30 percent in early morning trading in the immediate aftermath of Huang’s reinstatement announcement.

英伟达的股价在黄仁勋辞职消息宣布后下跌了约25%，较峰值下跌了近50%；而在黄仁勋复职消息宣布后，其股价在早盘交易中上涨了约30%。

Huang’s return echoes, in some ways, that of OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, who was fired almost exactly three years ago after the board said it “no longer has confidence in his ability to continue leading OpenAI.”

黄仁勋的回归在某些方面与OpenAI首席执行官萨姆·奥特曼（Sam Altman）的经历相呼应。奥特曼在几乎整整三年前被解雇，当时董事会称其“不再有信心让他继续领导OpenAI”。

Two weeks later, Altman was back at the helm after many employees threatened a mass revolt and departure if he wasn’t brought back.

两周后，在许多员工威胁称如果不让他回来就集体辞职的情况下，奥特曼重掌大权。

In Huang’s case, the revolt following his departure came not from employees but from fellow titans of the tech industry. Executives including Altman, OpenAI President Greg Brockman, xAI’s Elon Musk and Anduril’s Palmer Luckey are among the prominent industry figures who have publicly offered him support in the days since his resignation.

在黄仁勋的案例中，他离职后引发的“反抗”并非来自员工，而是来自科技行业的其他巨头。包括奥特曼、OpenAI总裁格雷格·布罗克曼（Greg Brockman）、xAI的埃隆·马斯克（Elon Musk）和Anduril的帕尔默·拉基（Palmer Luckey）在内的多位高管，都是在他辞职后公开表示支持他的知名行业人士。

In its statement, the Nvidia board reiterated that it has retained independent counsel and formed a committee of independent directors to investigate what it calls Huang’s targeting by “a foreign intelligence effort.”

英伟达董事会在声明中重申，已聘请独立法律顾问并成立了一个由独立董事组成的委员会，以调查其所谓的黄仁勋遭到“外国情报机构”针对的事件。

The company has also formed a National Security and Export Control Committee to “oversee trade compliance and national security risks,” and is seeking to hire a new chief compliance officer.

该公司还成立了一个国家安全与出口管制委员会，以“监督贸易合规和国家安全风险”，并正在寻求聘请一位新的首席合规官。
