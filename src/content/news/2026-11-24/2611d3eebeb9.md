### Sneakernet

NOTICE: Sneakernet is a work in progress. The documentation makes some promises that are not reflected in the current state of affairs. Links to to-be-written parts of the documentation are indicated in red.

注意：Sneakernet 尚处于开发阶段。目前的文档做出了一些尚未在当前版本中实现的承诺。文档中尚未编写部分的链接以红色标出。

Sneakernet is a system for securely sending digital messages to other people without using the Internet. Instead, messages are automatically passed on to other Sneakernet users on your local network – at your hackerspace, an event or conference, your local cafe, etc — and then physically carried to their destination on your “sneakers”, where they will be automatically delivered to any recipients on the same network (or handed over to be carried further on by someone else).

Sneakernet 是一个无需使用互联网即可安全地向他人发送数字消息的系统。相反，消息会自动传递给您本地网络中的其他 Sneakernet 用户（例如在您的黑客空间、活动或会议现场、当地咖啡馆等），然后通过您的“运动鞋”物理携带至目的地，并在那里自动发送给同一网络中的任何接收者（或转交给他人继续携带）。

Sneakernet encourages slow, thoughtful participation in community and face-to-face meetings with the users who will carry your messages onwards to their destination, and entrust their own messages to you. ⇒ Head to Getting started to set up Sneakernet on your device.

Sneakernet 鼓励人们以缓慢、深思熟虑的方式参与社区活动，并与那些将把您的消息带往目的地、同时也将他们的消息托付给您的用户进行面对面的交流。⇒ 前往“入门指南”以在您的设备上设置 Sneakernet。

Many applications can be built on top of sneakernet’s general-purpose messaging system, provided that they are tolerant of high latency and out-of-order messages. Some examples include: E-Mail (sneakermail), Message boards/news groups (sneakernews), File sharing (sneakershare), And many more!

只要应用程序能够容忍高延迟和乱序消息，就可以在 Sneakernet 的通用消息系统之上构建许多应用。一些示例包括：电子邮件 (sneakermail)、留言板/新闻组 (sneakernews)、文件共享 (sneakershare) 以及更多其他应用！

Building your own sneakernet applications is easy, too, in any programming language – see Building applications for more details. All messages are authenticated and all private messages are also encrypted. People who carry your messages along cannot tamper with them and cannot read your private messages. See Security for more details. Back ↩︎

使用任何编程语言构建您自己的 Sneakernet 应用程序都很简单——有关详细信息，请参阅“构建应用程序”。所有消息都经过身份验证，所有私人消息也都经过加密。携带您消息的人无法篡改这些消息，也无法阅读您的私人消息。有关详细信息，请参阅“安全性”。返回 ↩︎
