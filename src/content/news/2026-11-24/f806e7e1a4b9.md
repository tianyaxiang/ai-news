Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.26809 (cs) [Submitted on 19 Nov 2026] Title: Search From the Start: Search-Native Pretraining for Language Models Authors: Kaustubh Ponkshe, Gyung Hyun Je, Stefan Taga, Colin Raffel, Martin Jaggi.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.26809 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 19 日] 标题：从头开始搜索：语言模型的搜索原生预训练 作者：Kaustubh Ponkshe, Gyung Hyun Je, Stefan Taga, Colin Raffel, Martin Jaggi。

Abstract: Modern language models (LMs) are increasingly deployed with access to search tools at inference. Yet pretraining remains predominantly focused on predicting static text, with search capabilities typically introduced during post-training. We propose Search-Native Pretraining as a practical route to teach search capability during pretraining from ordinary text corpora.

摘要：现代语言模型 (LM) 在推理时越来越多地被部署并接入搜索工具。然而，预训练仍然主要集中在预测静态文本上，搜索能力通常是在训练后阶段引入的。我们提出了“搜索原生预训练”，作为一种从普通文本语料库中在预训练期间教授搜索能力的实用途径。

Specifically, we augment pretraining corpus at scale with web-search queries and retrieve web response using a local index. Training on this augmented corpus teaches models to generate search calls and incorporate retrieved evidence during language modeling.

具体而言，我们利用网络搜索查询大规模扩充预训练语料库，并使用本地索引检索网络响应。在这一扩充语料库上进行训练，可以教会模型在语言建模过程中生成搜索调用并整合检索到的证据。

Across Wikipedia and DCLM-Edu corpora and 360M to 3.3B parameter scales, search-pretrained models ("QueryLM") show improved performance on knowledge-intensive tasks and higher steerability toward the retrieved evidence. Crucially, their advantage persists after supervised fine-tuning of both search-pretrained and conventionally pretrained models, indicating that the tested post-training procedures do not fully recover the capabilities developed during pretraining.

在 Wikipedia 和 DCLM-Edu 语料库以及 360M 到 3.3B 参数规模下，搜索预训练模型（“QueryLM”）在知识密集型任务中表现出更好的性能，并且对检索到的证据具有更高的可控性。至关重要的是，这种优势在对搜索预训练模型和传统预训练模型进行监督微调后依然存在，这表明所测试的训练后程序无法完全恢复预训练期间所形成的能力。

Throughout our experiments, we find that a 1.4B QueryLM consistently beats or matches the performance of a FLOP-matched 3.3B baseline model across all knowledge intensive tasks. These results establish search-pretraining as a practical way to develop foundational search capabilities during pretraining. We release the training artifacts, including model checkpoints and data.

在我们的整个实验中，我们发现 1.4B 的 QueryLM 在所有知识密集型任务中始终优于或等同于 FLOP 匹配的 3.3B 基准模型的性能。这些结果确立了搜索预训练作为在预训练期间开发基础搜索能力的实用方法。我们发布了训练产物，包括模型检查点和数据。
