### Great Parenting Gadgets to Gift
### 值得赠送的育儿小工具

It’s easy to remember to register for things like the car seat and baby clothes. But there are a lot of small gadgets that make parenting a little easier, things that new parents don’t realize they might love using. Here are some of the favorites from the parents at WIRED.

人们很容易记得去登记购买汽车安全座椅和婴儿服装等必需品。但还有许多能让育儿变得更轻松的小工具，是新手父母们还没意识到自己会爱上的东西。以下是《连线》编辑部父母们最喜爱的一些产品。

**Qunlions Life Electric Baby Nail Trimmer**
Baby nails are sharp, but it’s also easy to clip their tiny nails too far. The solution? An electric nail trimmer. No more scratches on Mom's and Dad's or baby’s new, beautiful faces. Plus it can be less scary for parents than wielding traditional nail clippers near baby’s tiny fingers.

**Qunlions Life 电动婴儿指甲修剪器**
婴儿的指甲很锋利，但用指甲剪很容易剪得太深。解决办法是什么？一个电动指甲修剪器。这样就不会再抓伤爸爸妈妈或宝宝那娇嫩漂亮的脸蛋了。此外，对于父母来说，这比在宝宝细小的手指旁挥舞传统的指甲剪要少一些心理负担。

**Munchkin Mini Portable UV-C Sterilizer**
This was a gift from a friend that wasn’t actually on my registry, but I used it more than so many things I did register for. Pacifiers fall all the time and get grimy instantly. I kept this portable UV sterilizer hooked to my diaper bag, and loved popping a pacifier in after drops (after wiping it down with a wet wipe first).

**Munchkin 便携式迷你紫外线消毒器**
这是朋友送的一份礼物，其实并不在我的登记清单上，但我使用它的频率比清单上很多东西都要高。安抚奶嘴总是掉在地上，瞬间就会变脏。我把这个便携式紫外线消毒器挂在尿布包上，每次奶嘴掉落后（先用湿巾擦拭一下），我都很喜欢把它放进去消毒。

**Hatch Baby Sound Machine**
The Hatch is my most-used item in the last four years. It was easy to set up on the fly when I came home from the hospital, and I love that I can set up a few scenes in the app to flip through depending on whether I was nursing at three a.m. or putting my son to sleep. The Hatch Baby is now in his room and keeps him asleep each night with its music, while I have the Hatch Restore for myself.

**Hatch Baby 婴儿安抚音响**
Hatch 是我过去四年中使用频率最高的物品。当我从医院回家时，它非常容易设置。我喜欢在应用程序中设置几种场景，根据我是凌晨三点喂奶还是哄儿子睡觉来切换。现在 Hatch Baby 放在他的房间里，每晚用音乐陪伴他入睡，而我自己则用 Hatch Restore。

**Grownsy EaseClean Bottle Washer Pro**
Everyone on TikTok might talk about the MomCozy washer, but the best baby bottle washer WIRED reviewer Nicole Kinning found actually comes from Grownsy. It's quiet, has a deep interior cavity to hold tons of bottle and pump parts, and has multiple modes and combinations you can customize for the perfect cleaning session. It circulates HEPA-filtered air to keep bottles sterile after washing.

**Grownsy EaseClean 奶瓶清洗机 Pro**
TikTok 上可能都在讨论 MomCozy 清洗机，但《连线》评测员 Nicole Kinning 发现最好的奶瓶清洗机其实来自 Grownsy。它运行安静，内部空间深，可以容纳大量的奶瓶和吸奶器配件，并拥有多种模式和组合，你可以自定义以获得完美的清洁效果。它还能循环 HEPA 过滤空气，确保奶瓶清洗后保持无菌。
