# AI data centers throw another wrench into chaotic UN climate talks

Global climate negotiations fell apart as delegates tried to tackle AI’s growing carbon footprint.

全球气候谈判在代表们试图解决人工智能日益增长的碳足迹问题时陷入了僵局。

The clean energy revolution hinges on electrifying the entire global economy just as power-hungry data centers are surging. It’s a challenge delegates from all over the world had to grapple with during the United Nations climate conference, called COP31, that came to a chaotic and unsatisfying close in Antalya, Turkey, on Sunday.

清洁能源革命的关键在于实现全球经济的全面电气化，而此时耗电量巨大的数据中心正处于激增状态。在土耳其安塔利亚举行的联合国气候变化大会（COP31）于周日以混乱且令人不满的方式闭幕，来自世界各地的代表们不得不应对这一挑战。

Each year, attendees try to make good on the commitment to stop climate change as part of the 2015 Paris climate accord. Preventing extreme weather from growing more terrifying, coastal communities from flooding, and droughts from wrecking food and water systems requires slashing greenhouse gas emissions. But in order to power homes or cars with solar and wind instead of oil and gas, they need to go electric.

每年，与会者都试图履行作为 2015 年《巴黎协定》一部分的应对气候变化承诺。为了防止极端天气变得更加可怕、沿海社区遭受洪水侵袭，以及干旱破坏粮食和水系统，必须大幅削减温室气体排放。但为了用太阳能和风能代替石油和天然气来为家庭或汽车供电，他们必须实现电气化。

“Every climate minister in the world is thinking about this”

“全世界的每一位气候部长都在思考这个问题”

To that end, one of the most anticipated outcomes of this year’s negotiations was a deal to raise electricity’s share of global energy consumption to 35 percent by 2035, compared to about 20 percent today. That becomes harder to do, however, when data centers are causing electricity demand to skyrocket. That made the rush of new AI data centers a climate issue alongside talks on how to transition from fossil fuels to renewable energy.

为此，今年谈判中最受期待的成果之一是一项协议，即到 2035 年将电力在全球能源消费中的占比提高到 35%，而目前约为 20%。然而，当数据中心导致电力需求激增时，这一目标变得更加难以实现。这使得人工智能数据中心的涌现，与如何从化石燃料向可再生能源转型的问题一道，成为了一个气候议题。

“Every climate minister in the world is thinking about this, and it will inevitably come up in the discussions. It’s part of the broader energy transformation,” Chris Bowen, Australia’s minister for climate change and energy and the president of COP31 climate negotiations, told Politico in an interview at the start of the COP31 summit.

澳大利亚气候变化与能源部长、COP31 气候谈判主席克里斯·鲍文（Chris Bowen）在 COP31 峰会开始时接受《政治报》（Politico）采访时表示：“全世界的每一位气候部长都在思考这个问题，它不可避免地会出现在讨论中。这是更广泛的能源转型的一部分。”

Despite becoming the longest COP yet, with debates lasting two days longer than scheduled, official negotiations fell apart spectacularly. Delegates ultimately failed to garner enough support to pass the “pact” they’d been bargaining on for two weeks. The EU, UK, and several smaller nations most vulnerable to climate change rejected the deal that they saw as too weak.

尽管这次会议成为了有史以来最长的一次气候大会，辩论时间比原计划延长了两天，但官方谈判还是以惨败告终。代表们最终未能获得足够的支持来通过他们已经商讨了两周的“公约”。欧盟、英国以及几个最易受气候变化影响的小国拒绝了该协议，认为其力度太弱。

The pact omitted the term “fossil fuels” despite many nations calling for stronger commitments to phase out oil, coal, and gas. And the US, the epicenter of the data center buildout and the biggest climate polluter historically, was a no-show after the pro-fossil-fuel Trump administration pulled the country out of the Paris accord (twice).

尽管许多国家呼吁做出更强有力的承诺以逐步淘汰石油、煤炭和天然气，但该公约却删除了“化石燃料”一词。而作为数据中心建设中心和历史上最大的气候污染国，美国在支持化石燃料的特朗普政府两次退出《巴黎协定》后，并未出席此次会议。

With official negotiations falling apart, and countries only making voluntary commitments on AI and electrification, some environmental and humanitarian groups called this year “the worst COP” ever.

由于官方谈判破裂，且各国仅在人工智能和电气化方面做出了自愿承诺，一些环保和人道主义团体称今年是“最糟糕的一次气候大会”。

To meet the Paris goals, nations will need to cut their greenhouse gas emissions roughly in half by 2030 before reaching net zero by the middle of the century. Many tech companies made similar commitments in recent years, including Google, Microsoft, and Meta. But thanks to the AI boom, those goals have fallen by the wayside.

为了实现《巴黎协定》的目标，各国需要在 2030 年前将温室气体排放量减少约一半，并在本世纪中叶实现净零排放。近年来，包括谷歌、微软和 Meta 在内的许多科技公司也做出了类似的承诺。但由于人工智能的繁荣，这些目标已被抛诸脑后。

Meta is “re-evaluating” its timeline to reach net zero emissions as it focuses on AI, the company said in its annual sustainability report on Thursday. Meta reported a 45 percent year-over-year increase in climate pollution. Google’s and Microsoft’s carbon footprints are also growing despite climate commitments the companies have made. Data centers’ electricity consumption is forecast to double by 2030, according to a forecast by the International Energy Agency.

Meta 在周四发布的年度可持续发展报告中表示，由于专注于人工智能，公司正在“重新评估”其实现净零排放的时间表。Meta 报告称，其气候污染同比增加了 45%。尽管谷歌和微软做出了气候承诺，但它们的碳足迹也在增长。根据国际能源署的预测，到 2030 年，数据中心的电力消耗预计将翻一番。

“That is a huge climate risk: rising power demand from data centres is not yet sustainable, and an AI boom cannot be allowed to become a fossil fuel boom,” Bruce Douglas, CEO of the Global Renewables Alliance, tells The Verge in an email. Data centers were “a key topic” at COP31, according to Douglas, whose organization cohosted a panel on the challenges during the event.

全球可再生能源联盟首席执行官布鲁斯·道格拉斯（Bruce Douglas）在给《The Verge》的一封电子邮件中表示：“这是一个巨大的气候风险：数据中心不断增长的电力需求目前尚不可持续，不能让人工智能的繁荣演变成化石燃料的繁荣。”据道格拉斯称，数据中心是 COP31 的“一个关键议题”，他的组织在会议期间共同举办了一个关于相关挑战的小组讨论。

In the US, the country with the most data centers, power sector emissions could increase 42 percent by 2030 as energy companies build more fossil fuel infrastructure to meet data center electricity demand, according to a recent analysis by the Sierra Club. Already, hyperscale data centers in the US create more climate pollution than the country of Denmark, a separate study by Harvard researchers recently found.

根据塞拉俱乐部（Sierra Club）最近的一项分析，在美国这个拥有最多数据中心的国家，随着能源公司建设更多的化石燃料基础设施以满足数据中心的电力需求，到 2030 年电力行业的排放量可能会增加 42%。哈佛大学研究人员最近的一项独立研究发现，美国超大规模数据中心产生的气候污染已经超过了丹麦整个国家。

To stop data centers from creating more pollution, they would need to be powered by additional new sources of renewable energy, Douglas says. He adds that data centers are currently competing with everyone else for grid connections, finance, and electricity generation capacity — and against arguably more essential tasks like heating and air conditioning, as well as electrifying transportation.

道格拉斯表示，为了阻止数据中心产生更多的污染，它们需要由额外的新可再生能源供电。他补充说，数据中心目前正在与所有人争夺电网连接、资金和发电能力，并且与供暖、空调以及交通电气化等可以说更重要的任务形成了竞争。

Many tech companies match their electricity use with renewable energy purchases. But unless they’re helping to build new sources of green energy to meet their added electricity demand, they might just end up hogging limited renewable energy capacity. And if more fossil fuels make up the difference, then greenhouse gas emissions continue to rise overall.

许多科技公司通过购买可再生能源来抵消其用电量。但除非他们帮助建设新的绿色能源以满足其增加的电力需求，否则他们最终可能只是在占用有限的可再生能源容量。如果用更多的化石燃料来弥补差额，那么温室气体排放总量就会持续上升。

“Should new green electrons be used for air-conditioning, electrification or data centres?”

“新的绿色电力应该用于空调、电气化还是数据中心？”

“In many cases, datacentres are outbidding other consumers to get speed to power,” Douglas says. “Should new green electrons be used for air-conditioning, electrification or data centres?”

“在许多情况下，数据中心为了快速获得电力而以高价竞标，超过了其他消费者，”道格拉斯说。“新的绿色电力应该用于空调、电气化还是数据中心？”
