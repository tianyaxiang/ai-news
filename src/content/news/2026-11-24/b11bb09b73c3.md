# Jensen Huang Is Back as Nvidia CEO

Jensen Huang is stepping back into his role as chief executive of Nvidia effective immediately. The stunning reversal comes 11 days after Huang resigned as CEO from the company he cofounded 33 years ago and after multiple declarations from the board of directors that it had no intention of reinstating him. On Monday, they took a conciliatory tone.

黄仁勋即刻起重新担任英伟达首席执行官。这一惊人的反转发生在他辞去自己33年前联合创立的公司CEO职务11天后，此前董事会曾多次声明无意让他复职。周一，董事会采取了和解的态度。

“The Board believes Jensen is the right person to lead Nvidia and that his reinstatement is in the best interests of our stockholders, employees, partners, and customers,” said recently appointed board chair Harvey Jones in a press release announcing the decision. In that announcement the board said it was “satisfied with respect to Mr. Huang’s candor and ability to lead Nvidia” and that it had “received input from senior levels of the United States government” on the way to its decision.

“董事会认为黄仁勋是领导英伟达的合适人选，他的复职符合我们股东、员工、合作伙伴和客户的最大利益，”新任董事会主席哈维·琼斯在宣布该决定的新闻稿中表示。在公告中，董事会称其“对黄先生的坦诚和领导英伟达的能力感到满意”，并表示在做出决定过程中“收到了来自美国政府高层的意见”。

On Monday morning, President Donald Trump posted that the Nvidia board “has made the correct and proper decision to return Jensen Huang to the position of CEO.” “I am honored to return to Nvidia and lead one of the most consequential technology companies in the world at the beginning of a new industrial revolution,” said Huang in a statement. “I take this responsibility with the greatest humility and renewed commitment.”

周一上午，唐纳德·特朗普总统发文称，英伟达董事会“做出了让黄仁勋重返CEO职位的正确且恰当的决定”。黄仁勋在一份声明中表示：“我很荣幸能回到英伟达，在新的工业革命开端之际领导这家世界上最重要的科技公司之一。我怀着最大的谦卑和全新的承诺承担起这一责任。”

Huang resigned as Nvidia’s CEO on November 12, and longtime chief financial officer Colette Kress was installed as his interim replacement. Five days later, Huang publicly announced that he was open to resuming his role as chief executive, noting the “outpouring of calls” from “employees, investors, business partners, and customers” advocating for his return. “If the board of directors decides that my return is in the best interest of our shareholders, our employees, the company, and America, I would be honored to answer these calls and return as CEO,” Huang wrote.

黄仁勋于11月12日辞去英伟达CEO职务，长期担任首席财务官的科莱特·克雷斯被任命为临时继任者。五天后，黄仁勋公开宣布愿意恢复其首席执行官职务，并指出“员工、投资者、商业合作伙伴和客户”纷纷呼吁他回归。黄仁勋写道：“如果董事会认为我的回归符合股东、员工、公司和美国的最佳利益，我很荣幸能响应这些呼吁并重返CEO岗位。”

The day Huang resigned, many Nvidia employees spoke up in his support. As reported by WIRED, employees left more than 1,600 comments on an internal “suggestion box” post in which they urged the company’s board to reinstall him as CEO. In the days following, a public petition calling for Huang’s reinstatement began circulating. At the time of writing, it has amassed more than 200,000 online signatures, the majority of whom self-identified as Nvidia shareholders. Tech elites like Elon Musk and Y Combinator CEO Garry Tan have claimed that they were going to sign the petition or had signed it.

黄仁勋辞职当天，许多英伟达员工发声支持他。据《连线》报道，员工在内部“意见箱”帖子下留下了超过1600条评论，敦促公司董事会让他复职。随后的几天里，一份呼吁黄仁勋复职的公开请愿书开始流传。截至撰稿时，该请愿书已收集了超过20万个在线签名，其中大多数人自称是英伟达股东。埃隆·马斯克和Y Combinator首席执行官加里·谭等科技精英声称他们已经或准备签署该请愿书。

Huang’s reinstatement marks a total reversal from the public stance of Nvidia’s board of directors, which said during the company’s November 17 earnings call that it “does not intend” to reinstate him as CEO. WIRED has further reported that the board was alarmed by what one source described as Huang’s “lying” over the last year or so. President Donald Trump, who developed a close working relationship with Huang during his second administration, had indicated openness to Huang returning as CEO. Trump told reporters on November 16 that he “would not mind” if Huang returned as chief executive, adding that he believed he had “learned a big lesson.”

黄仁勋的复职标志着英伟达董事会公开立场的彻底反转，该董事会在11月17日的财报电话会议上曾表示“无意”让他复职。据《连线》进一步报道，董事会对一位消息人士所称的黄仁勋在过去一年左右的“撒谎”行为感到震惊。在第二任期内与黄仁勋建立了密切工作关系的唐纳德·特朗普总统曾表示，对黄仁勋重返CEO职位持开放态度。特朗普在11月16日告诉记者，如果黄仁勋回归担任首席执行官，他“不会介意”，并补充说他相信黄仁勋已经“吸取了深刻的教训”。

Huang’s resignation followed days of scrutiny over several extramarital romantic relationships: one with Chinese businesswoman Alice Huang, who has no known relation to Jensen, one with an Nvidia employee who directly reported to Huang, and one with a different Nvidia employee. Huang’s relationship with Alice became the subject of particularly intense focus on November 6, when The New York Times and Financial Times reported that US intelligence officials suspected that Alice may be an asset of the Chinese Ministry of State Security, both citing an intelligence briefing that circulated among top Trump administration officials last year.

黄仁勋的辞职是在几段婚外情受到数日审查后发生的：一段是与中国女商人爱丽丝·黄（与黄仁勋无已知亲属关系），一段是与直接向黄仁勋汇报的英伟达员工，还有一段是与另一名英伟达员工。11月6日，当《纽约时报》和《金融时报》报道称美国情报官员怀疑爱丽丝可能是中国国家安全部的资产时，黄仁勋与爱丽丝的关系成为了特别关注的焦点，两家媒体均援引了去年在特朗普政府高层官员中流传的一份情报简报。

In the days since, some reports and Trump administration officials have questioned the accuracy of that intelligence. The Chinese government has consistently rejected claims that Alice was one of its spies. Alice was the chief executive of Megaspeed, a Singapore-based AI company that’s been accused of helping Chinese tech companies use Nvidia’s most advanced chips at overseas locations, effectively sidestepping US export restrictions. Alice has also reportedly worked as a television reporter, at HSBC’s Hong Kong-based private banking arm, and at an investment bank backed by the Chinese state.

此后的几天里，一些报道和特朗普政府官员对该情报的准确性提出了质疑。中国政府一贯否认爱丽丝是其间谍的说法。爱丽丝曾是总部位于新加坡的AI公司Megaspeed的首席执行官，该公司被指控帮助中国科技公司在海外使用英伟达最先进的芯片，从而有效地规避了美国的出口限制。据报道，爱丽丝还曾担任电视记者，在汇丰银行香港私人银行部门工作，并曾在一家由中国政府支持的投资银行任职。

Huang cofounded Nvidia in 1993 with Chris Malachowsky and Curtis Priem, purportedly with the mission of bringing realistic 3D graphics to personal computers. For many years, the company was primarily known within the gaming community. It didn’t become a household name until the recent AI boom, when its graphics processing units (GPUs) became a crucial piece of infrastructure for powering advanced AI models. Nvidia controlled about 90 percent of the global GPU market as of September 2026. The year prior, it became the first company to be valued at more than $4 trillion, though its stock price has been in precipitous decline since Huang’s resignation.

黄仁勋于1993年与克里斯·马拉乔斯基和柯蒂斯·普里姆共同创立了英伟达，据称其使命是将逼真的3D图形带入个人电脑。多年来，该公司主要在游戏界广为人知。直到最近的AI热潮，其图形处理器（GPU）成为驱动先进AI模型的关键基础设施，它才变得家喻户晓。截至2026年9月，英伟达控制了全球约90%的GPU市场。前一年，它成为首家估值超过4万亿美元的公司，尽管自黄仁勋辞职以来，其股价一直在急剧下跌。
