One of Apple’s biggest shows is sticking around for at least one more season. The streamer announced today that Ted Lasso has been renewed for a fifth season on Apple TV, after what the company calls a “record-breaking season four debut.” There are currently no details about the new season nor when it might debut.

Apple 最重磅的剧集之一将至少再续订一季。该流媒体平台今日宣布，《泰德·拉索》已获 Apple TV 第五季续订，此前该公司称其第四季首播“打破了纪录”。目前尚无关于新一季的细节，也未公布首播时间。

Ted Lasso has proven to be one of the tentpole series for Apple TV, and it returned in 2026 after a three-year hiatus. While the season 3 finale seemed to mark the end for the show, season 4 introduced a new cast of characters with head coach Ted — played by Jason Sudeikis, who also just appeared as a voice actor in Grand Theft Auto VI — managing a new professional women’s team.

《泰德·拉索》已被证明是 Apple TV 的支柱剧集之一，该剧在中断三年后于 2026 年回归。虽然第三季大结局似乎标志着该剧的终结，但第四季引入了一批新角色，由杰森·苏戴奇斯（Jason Sudeikis，他刚在《侠盗猎车手 VI》中担任配音演员）饰演的主教练泰德开始执教一支新的职业女子球队。

The news comes amidst a handful of other recent renewals from Apple — including a second season of Maximum Pleasure Guaranteed and multiple new seasons of Slow Horses — in what is turning out to be a strong year for the streaming service, as it attempts to reach a larger audience.

此消息发布之际，Apple 最近还续订了其他几部剧集，包括《Maximum Pleasure Guaranteed》第二季以及《流人》（Slow Horses）的多部新季。对于该流媒体服务而言，今年表现强劲，正努力触达更广泛的受众。
