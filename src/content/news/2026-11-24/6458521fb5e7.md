# Submit Your Questions: Will Anyone Keep You Safe From AI?

Save this storySave this storyThe AI safety conversation, as Lauren Goode put it, has lost the plot. Will the technology really kill us all? Should each one of us be worried about cyberattacks by rogue agents? Will it bring on total societal collapse? Though there’s room for disagreement about which catastrophe is most salient, experts are aligned on the idea that society is, for one reason or another, in real trouble.

正如劳伦·古德（Lauren Goode）所言，关于人工智能安全的讨论已经偏离了重点。这项技术真的会终结我们所有人吗？我们每个人都应该担心流氓代理发起的网络攻击吗？它会带来彻底的社会崩溃吗？尽管对于哪种灾难最为突出尚存争议，但专家们一致认为，无论出于何种原因，社会确实正处于真正的困境之中。

This begs one big question: What are industry leaders doing to mitigate the danger they’ve unleashed? Though a pause in training models, a data center moratorium, and other regulation has been proposed, the industry hasn’t exactly made changes that are putting people at ease around AI safety. Meanwhile, critical infrastructure seems more and more vulnerable every day. We’re gathering our Backchannel team, who will deploy their expertise on the ins and outs of Big Tech and AI safety, to answer all your questions.

这引出了一个重大问题：行业领袖们正在采取什么措施来减轻他们所释放的危险？尽管有人提议暂停模型训练、对数据中心实施暂停令以及其他监管措施，但该行业并未做出真正让人们对人工智能安全感到放心的改变。与此同时，关键基础设施似乎每天都变得越来越脆弱。我们将召集我们的 Backchannel 团队，他们将运用在大型科技公司和人工智能安全方面的专业知识，来回答你们的所有问题。
