It was the most simple of questions. On Monday afternoon, during a NASA news conference about the International Space Station, Space News reporter Jeff Foust asked about an upcoming launch of a Russian spacecraft. “It’s my understanding there’s another cargo mission going to the ISS this week, a Russian Progress,” Foust said. “Can you confirm that, and give the date and time that will launch?”

这是一个最简单的问题。周一下午，在NASA关于国际空间站的新闻发布会上，《太空新闻》记者杰夫·福斯特（Jeff Foust）询问了俄罗斯航天器即将进行的发射任务。“据我了解，本周还有一次前往国际空间站的货运任务，是一艘俄罗斯‘进步号’飞船，”福斯特说，“你能确认一下吗，并提供发射的日期和时间？”

This Progress MS-36 cargo launch, mind you, is presently scheduled for fewer than 16 hours from now, at 10:13 UTC (05:13 am ET) on Tuesday. It is going to a space station co-operated by the US space agency. But no, NASA would not provide this basic information about the upcoming Progress launch.

请注意，这次“进步号”MS-36货运发射目前定于不到16小时后，即周二协调世界时10:13（美东时间凌晨5:13）进行。它将前往一个由美国航天局共同运营的空间站。但NASA拒绝提供有关此次“进步号”发射的基本信息。

“Regarding the Progress launch, I would ask you to please reach out to Roscosmos directly for any questions tied to that vehicle,” said the senior NASA official on the call, Gaurang Patel, operations integration manager for NASA’s Low Earth Orbit Program. Effectively, this is a “Go f- yourself” response from NASA. Roscosmos typically does not reply to Western media outlets, and everyone knows this.

“关于‘进步号’的发射，如果有任何与该航天器相关的问题，请直接联系俄罗斯国家航天集团（Roscosmos），”通话中的NASA高级官员、NASA近地轨道项目运营整合经理高朗·帕特尔（Gaurang Patel）表示。实际上，这等同于NASA在说“滚一边去”。众所周知，俄罗斯国家航天集团通常不会回复西方媒体。

So why is a bog-standard Progress launch suddenly a state secret? It seems to be a recent policy change, because, historically, NASA has televised every one of these cargo flights from Russia’s main spaceport in Baikonur, Kazakhstan. But there is no information about the upcoming launch on NASA’s television schedule for upcoming “live” events.

那么，为什么一次平平无奇的“进步号”发射突然变成了国家机密？这似乎是最近的一项政策变化，因为从历史上看，NASA曾转播过每一次从哈萨克斯坦拜科努尔俄罗斯主要航天发射场起飞的货运航班。但在NASA即将进行的“直播”活动时间表中，没有任何关于此次发射的信息。

As recently as September, NASA provided live television coverage of Progress launches from Baikonur. It even issued a media advisory promising to update its broadcast schedule once Roscosmos provided launch details. Two months later, NASA officials apparently are unwilling to confirm that another Progress launch is taking place.

就在今年9月，NASA还对从拜科努尔发射的“进步号”进行了电视直播。它甚至发布了一份媒体公告，承诺一旦俄罗斯国家航天集团提供发射细节，就会更新其广播时间表。两个月后，NASA官员显然不愿确认另一次“进步号”发射正在进行。

Who made this decision? The question is whether this decision was made by NASA or was a request from Roscosmos, and, ultimately, whether any of this matters. We can make some educated guesses. It is probably safe to surmise that the decision here was originally made by state-sponsored Russian space corporation Roscosmos.

是谁做出了这个决定？问题在于这是NASA的决定，还是俄罗斯国家航天集团的要求，以及归根结底，这一切是否重要。我们可以做出一些有根据的猜测。可以肯定的是，这一决定最初是由俄罗斯国家航天集团做出的。

The company has not been advertising the preparation of the Progress spacecraft as it customarily does, some local sources say. Russian space reporter Anatoly Zak confirms that Roscosmos has “dramatically curtailed” its coverage of all launch campaigns in recent months. There is no mention of the launch on Roscosmos’ Telegram channel.

一些当地消息来源称，该公司并没有像往常一样宣传“进步号”航天器的准备工作。俄罗斯航天记者阿纳托利·扎克（Anatoly Zak）证实，俄罗斯国家航天集团近几个月来“大幅削减”了对所有发射活动的报道。在俄罗斯国家航天集团的Telegram频道上，也没有提及此次发射。

So why is Roscosmos doing this? There are some novel aspects of this flight. Among the cargo being brought to the space station is a robot that was created to assist spacewalkers during extravehicular activities. Operated by humans, the human-like “Teledroid” is designed to operate in a vacuum, exposed to radiation. Surely nothing will go wrong with this.

那么，俄罗斯国家航天集团为什么要这样做？这次飞行有一些新颖之处。运往空间站的货物中包括一个旨在协助宇航员进行舱外活动的机器人。这个由人类操作、类人型的“远程机器人”（Teledroid）被设计用于在真空中运行并暴露在辐射下。当然，这肯定不会出什么问题。

This is also the first launch of a Progress spacecraft since NASA and Roscosmos agreed that the leaky PrK module on board the space station would be decommissioned. Zak reports that, as a result, the Progress spacecraft will first dock to the Poisk module, where it will be unloaded. It will then be moved from there to the docking port attached to the PrK module to provide propulsion for the space station.

这也是自NASA和俄罗斯国家航天集团同意将空间站上漏气的PrK舱退役以来的首次“进步号”发射。扎克报道称，因此，“进步号”航天器将首先与“搜索号”（Poisk）模块对接并卸货。随后，它将从那里移动到连接在PrK舱上的对接端口，为空间站提供推进力。

Docked there, the ship’s propulsion system is better aligned with the station’s main axis. But none of this likely is the cause of secrecy surrounding Tuesday’s Progress launch. Rather, more likely it is due to Russia’s ongoing war against Ukraine. Worried about Ukrainian drones?

停靠在那里时，飞船的推进系统能更好地与空间站的主轴对齐。但这些都不太可能是周二“进步号”发射保密的原因。相反，更有可能是因为俄罗斯正在进行的对乌克兰的战争。担心乌克兰的无人机吗？

Ars reported earlier this year that, after a spaceport in northern Russia came under attack from drones on multiple occasions, the Russian government put a tighter lid on information about its launches from Plesetsk. It now seems likely that Russia has extended this secrecy policy to other missions at other spaceports.

《Ars Technica》今年早些时候报道称，在俄罗斯北部的一个航天发射场多次遭到无人机袭击后，俄罗斯政府加强了对普列谢茨克（Plesetsk）发射信息的管控。现在看来，俄罗斯很可能已将这一保密政策扩展到了其他航天发射场的其他任务中。

For example, earlier this month, Russia launched a Soyuz rocket from the Vostochny Cosmodrome without publicly announcing its precise launch date and time beforehand. Roscosmos confirmed the mission only after liftoff, and independent observers had to rely on local government notices to determine when the launch would occur.

例如，本月初，俄罗斯从东方航天发射场发射了一枚联盟号火箭，但并未提前公开宣布其精确的发射日期和时间。俄罗斯国家航天集团仅在升空后才确认了此次任务，而独立观察员不得不依靠当地政府的通知来确定发射时间。

Through all of the geopolitical turmoil on Earth, NASA has sought to maintain a good working relationship with Russia. Most likely, then, NASA has gone along with Russia’s secrecy policy for this week’s cargo launch—absurd though it appears in a culture of transparency.

尽管地球上地缘政治动荡不安，但NASA一直试图与俄罗斯保持良好的工作关系。因此，最有可能的情况是，NASA配合了俄罗斯对本周货运发射的保密政策——尽管在透明文化中这看起来很荒谬。

Nevertheless, this kind of secrecy would seemingly be untenable with a crewed launch involving NASA astronauts. The next test of this will come in March 2027, when the Soyuz MS-30 mission carrying three astronauts, including NASA’s Deniz Burnham, is due to lift off from Baikonur. We’ll be watching.

然而，对于涉及NASA宇航员的载人发射任务，这种保密做法似乎是站不住脚的。下一次考验将在2027年3月到来，届时搭载三名宇航员（包括NASA的丹妮兹·伯纳姆）的联盟号MS-30任务预计将从拜科努尔升空。我们将拭目以待。
