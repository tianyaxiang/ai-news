On Monday, Virginia and Oregon became the first states to sue SpaceX, alleging that Grok continues to sexualize, unchecked, real photos of mostly women and children. SpaceX acquired xAI in February and now must handle the ongoing reckoning over the controversial rollout of Grok’s “spicy mode”—a model feature Musk hyped but was soon found to generate a flood of non-consensual intimate imagery (NCII) and child sex abuse images (CSAM).

周一，弗吉尼亚州和俄勒冈州成为首批起诉 SpaceX 的州，指控 Grok 在无人监管的情况下，持续将主要是妇女和儿童的真实照片色情化。SpaceX 于二月收购了 xAI，现在必须应对因 Grok“辣味模式”（spicy mode）发布而引发的持续争议——马斯克曾大肆宣传这一模型功能，但很快被发现会生成大量非自愿亲密图像（NCII）和儿童性虐待图像（CSAM）。

Initial safeguards were so lax that Grok sometimes generated nude images without being asked. Although X Safety and xAI (now known as SpaceXSI) were silent at first, Musk’s companies eventually apologized and claimed that additional safeguards were implemented and policies redesigned to block harmful outputs. However, state attorneys general alleged that those statements were “misleading” and that SpaceXSI instead quietly “embraced” sexualized content to seize more AI market share, as Grok continues to generate NCII and CSAM today.

最初的安全防护措施非常松懈，以至于 Grok 有时会在未经要求的情况下生成裸体图像。尽管 X Safety 和 xAI（现称为 SpaceXSI）起初保持沉默，但马斯克的公司最终道歉，并声称已实施额外的防护措施并重新设计了政策以阻止有害输出。然而，州总检察长指控这些声明具有“误导性”，并称 SpaceXSI 反而悄悄地“拥抱”色情内容以抢占更多人工智能市场份额，因为 Grok 至今仍在生成 NCII 和 CSAM。

Without intervention, kids in Virginia and Oregon risk a lifetime of distress, as Grok images will likely be impossible to remove online. “Once created and shared,” Grok-generated CSAM and NCII “can never be fully erased, and it becomes a permanent record of the child’s abuse that may resurface for the rest of the child’s life,” Virginia Attorney General Jay Jones’ complaint said. “Virginia has a profound interest in protecting its children from this harm.”

如果不加干预，弗吉尼亚州和俄勒冈州的儿童将面临终身的痛苦，因为 Grok 生成的图像很可能无法从网上彻底删除。弗吉尼亚州总检察长杰伊·琼斯（Jay Jones）的诉状称：“一旦创建并分享，Grok 生成的 CSAM 和 NCII 就永远无法完全抹除，它将成为儿童受虐待的永久记录，并可能在孩子余生中再次出现。弗吉尼亚州在保护其儿童免受这种伤害方面有着深切的利益。”

Millions of residents were allegedly harmed, as the attorneys general seek to protect every Grok or X user in their state. Many Grok victims will likely suffer from chronic anxiety, depression, and PTSD, as well as reputational harm, as some viewers may not realize the images are AI-generated, the states alleged. Further—due to Grok’s integration with the social media platform X—users as young as 13 may have been exposed to the AI content and possibly re-traumatized if they, too, are victims of sexual abuse, the states alleged.

据称有数百万居民受到伤害，总检察长们正寻求保护其州内的每一位 Grok 或 X 用户。各州指控称，许多 Grok 的受害者可能会遭受慢性焦虑、抑郁和创伤后应激障碍（PTSD），以及名誉受损，因为一些观众可能没有意识到这些图像是人工智能生成的。此外，由于 Grok 与社交媒体平台 X 的整合，年仅 13 岁的用户也可能接触到这些人工智能内容，如果他们本身也是性虐待的受害者，还可能遭受二次创伤。

“xAI not only knowingly endangered kids and our communities, but they also lied to our faces about non-existent safety measures,” Oregon Attorney General Dan Rayfield said in a press release. States alleged that xAI could have conducted rigorous red-teaming to block outputs before the new feature launched. But instead, the X Safety team responsible for Grok red-teaming was reduced to two or three team members, states alleged.

俄勒冈州总检察长丹·雷菲尔德（Dan Rayfield）在一份新闻稿中表示：“xAI 不仅明知故犯地危及儿童和我们的社区，还就根本不存在的安全措施当面撒谎。”各州指控称，xAI 本可以在新功能发布前进行严格的红队测试以阻止有害输出。但各州称，负责 Grok 红队测试的 X Safety 团队反而被缩减至两到三名成员。

Overall, rather than use industry-standard safeguards, Musk’s firms allegedly put profits over women’s and children’s safety, states alleged, while complaining that SpaceXSI won’t even publicly identify “the officers responsible for safety governance.” “Industry standard requires not only building guardrails but also vigilantly monitoring whether they hold,” states alleged. SpaceXSI is doing neither effectively, states alleged.

总的来说，各州指控称，马斯克的公司没有使用行业标准的防护措施，而是将利润置于妇女和儿童的安全之上，并抱怨 SpaceXSI 甚至不愿公开披露“负责安全治理的官员”。各州称：“行业标准不仅要求建立护栏，还要求警惕地监控这些护栏是否有效。”各州指控称，SpaceXSI 在这两方面都没有做到位。

Asked for comment, SpaceXSI shared an X Safety post on X, which repeated claims that the company has a “zero-tolerance” policy for “any forms of child sexual exploitation.” X Safety said that the company “acted promptly to tighten” Grok protections, which are supposedly “designed to refuse any request to generate explicit images of minors or real people.”

在被要求置评时，SpaceXSI 在 X 上分享了一篇 X Safety 的帖子，重申了该公司对“任何形式的儿童性剥削”采取“零容忍”政策的声明。X Safety 表示，公司“迅速采取行动加强”了 Grok 的保护措施，这些措施据称“旨在拒绝任何生成未成年人或真实人物露骨图像的请求”。

That statement also claimed that X Safety continually tests and adjusts protections when bad actors find ways to avoid filters, while relying on “sophisticated classifiers that screen every prompt” to catch harmful outputs “before an image is generated and check every output after.” Although the statement did not mention whether xAI sues users who break Grok rules to generate CSAM and NCII, it emphasized that xAI reports harmful content generated to police.

该声明还声称，当不良行为者找到规避过滤器的方法时，X Safety 会持续测试和调整保护措施，同时依靠“复杂的分类器筛选每一个提示词”，以便在“图像生成前捕捉有害输出，并在生成后检查每一个输出”。尽管该声明未提及 xAI 是否会起诉违反 Grok 规则以生成 CSAM 和 NCII 的用户，但它强调 xAI 会向警方报告所生成的有害内容。

“When someone tries to break our safeguards and generate this content, we take appropriate measures, including actioning accounts and notifying law enforcement,” X Safety said. “Attempting to create this material is a serious crime, and the individuals who do it should be prosecuted to the fullest extent of the law. We will continue to work with the authorities via all available means to pursue these bad actors.”

X Safety 表示：“当有人试图破坏我们的防护措施并生成此类内容时，我们会采取适当措施，包括处理账户和通知执法部门。试图制作这些材料是严重的犯罪行为，相关个人应受到法律的全面制裁。我们将继续通过一切可用手段与当局合作，追究这些不良行为者的责任。”

However, state attorneys general alleged that reports from independent researchers and news outlets this year have shown that Grok is still generating NCII and CSAM. They noted that the chatbot “even advises users how to circumvent its own limited moderation measures to generate those images.” In some cases, a Virginia state probe found that Grok complied “with a user request to draft a prompt designed to avoid moderation.”

然而，州总检察长们指控称，今年独立研究人员和新闻媒体的报告显示，Grok 仍在生成 NCII 和 CSAM。他们指出，该聊天机器人“甚至会建议用户如何规避其自身有限的审核措施来生成这些图像”。在某些情况下，弗吉尼亚州的一项调查发现，Grok 顺从了“用户起草旨在规避审核的提示词的请求”。

So, even when filters work, the chatbot helps determined users immediately get around them, states alleged. Nothing xAI did put a dent in the harmful outputs, least of all putting the feature behind a paywall, states alleged, which only ensured maximum profits. A troubled New York City Council speaker, Julie Menin, confronted SpaceX this October with proof that attempts to digitally undress her own photo still worked for free Grok users, states alleged.

因此，各州指控称，即使过滤器起作用，聊天机器人也会帮助坚定的用户立即绕过它们。各州称，xAI 所做的一切都没有减少有害输出，尤其是将该功能设为付费墙，这只会确保其利润最大化。各州指控称，今年十月，一位困扰的纽约市议会议长朱莉·梅宁（Julie Menin）向 SpaceX 出示证据，证明针对她本人照片的数字脱衣尝试对免费的 Grok 用户仍然有效。

Concluding its probe, Virginia found that moderation remains “inconsistent and ineffective,” with Grok sometimes carrying out prompts “that it later blocked.” In its complaint, Virginia alleged that Grok protections “were weak, ineffective, sparsely enforced, and easily circumvented.” Oregon agreed that rather than act to protect users, SpaceX entities repeatedly made statements “with the intent to deceive consumers” that Grok was safe.

弗吉尼亚州在结束调查时发现，审核工作仍然“不一致且无效”，Grok 有时会执行“随后又被其拦截”的提示词。弗吉尼亚州在诉状中指控，Grok 的保护措施“薄弱、无效、执行力差且容易被规避”。俄勒冈州也表示，SpaceX 实体不仅没有采取行动保护用户，反而多次发表“意在欺骗消费者”的声明，声称 Grok 是安全的。

Most egregiously to state attorneys general, in January, Elon Musk denied any harm to real people, claiming that Grok is only “supposed [to] allow upper body nudity of imaginary adult humans (not real ones) and only generated nudity of fake people (not real ones).” “Contrary to Musk’s representation,” Grok “makes the same sexualizing edits to photographs of real, identifiable people,” states warned.

对于州总检察长们来说，最恶劣的是，埃隆·马斯克在今年一月否认了对真实人物造成的任何伤害，声称 Grok 只是“应该允许虚构成年人类（而非真实人类）的上半身裸露，且仅生成虚构人物（而非真实人物）的裸体”。各州警告称：“与马斯克的陈述相反，Grok 对真实、可识别人物的照片进行了同样的色情化编辑。”
