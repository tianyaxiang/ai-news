# A more tailored Google Search that’s uniquely yours

A more tailored Google Search that’s uniquely yours. Nov 23, 2026. Get more helpful Search responses by securely opting in to connect Google Wallet, Drive, and the Gemini app to Personal Intelligence in AI Mode.

为您打造更具个性化的 Google 搜索。2026 年 11 月 23 日。通过安全授权将 Google 钱包、云端硬盘和 Gemini 应用连接到 AI 模式下的“个人智能”（Personal Intelligence），即可获得更有帮助的搜索响应。

The most helpful search experience brings together the richness of the world’s information with insights uniquely relevant to you. Today, Search is getting even more tailored with the ability to connect more of your apps to Personal Intelligence — and we're introducing more personally relevant responses, so it's easier to find just what you need.

最有帮助的搜索体验将世界信息的丰富性与对您而言独特的见解结合在一起。今天，搜索功能变得更加个性化，您可以将更多应用连接到“个人智能”，我们还引入了更多与您个人相关的响应，让您更容易找到所需内容。

Starting to roll out today, you can opt in to securely connect Google Wallet to Personal Intelligence in AI Mode, with support for Drive and the Gemini app following in the coming weeks — bringing context from your spending, documents, and chats directly to Search. These apps join Gmail, Calendar, and Photos in delivering even more tailored responses with your personal context.

从今天开始，您可以选择安全地将 Google 钱包连接到 AI 模式下的“个人智能”，对云端硬盘和 Gemini 应用的支持也将在未来几周内推出，从而将您的消费、文档和聊天记录中的背景信息直接带入搜索。这些应用将与 Gmail、日历和相册一起，利用您的个人背景信息提供更具针对性的响应。

Get a clearer picture of your spending with Google Wallet: By connecting Google Wallet to Personal Intelligence in both AI Mode and the Gemini app, you can map out your financial milestones and identify actionable ways to trim everyday spending. You can also maximize credit card benefits — like optimizing cash-back categories, travel perks, and reward points — based on your cards linked via Wallet. If you choose to connect your Wallet, AI Mode can only read information from your accounts; it can’t move, manage, or invest your money.

通过 Google 钱包更清晰地了解您的支出：通过将 Google 钱包连接到 AI 模式和 Gemini 应用中的“个人智能”，您可以规划财务里程碑，并找到减少日常开支的可行方法。您还可以根据通过钱包关联的卡片，最大化信用卡权益，例如优化现金返还类别、旅行福利和奖励积分。如果您选择连接钱包，AI 模式只能读取您账户中的信息；它无法转移、管理或投资您的资金。

Stay in your flow with Google Drive: Sometimes the important information that you need to get started on a project or make a big decision can be scattered across your docs, sheets, or PDFs. With Google Drive connected in Personal Intelligence, you can get uniquely helpful responses that take all of that context into account and help you get started, like a personalized preparation plan ahead of tax season.

利用 Google 云端硬盘保持工作流：有时，您开始项目或做出重大决策所需的重要信息可能分散在您的文档、表格或 PDF 中。通过在“个人智能”中连接 Google 云端硬盘，您可以获得考虑到所有这些背景信息的独特帮助响应，并帮助您开始工作，例如在报税季前制定个性化的准备计划。

Connect the dots between the Gemini app and Search: In the coming weeks you can easily share context across the Gemini app and AI Mode in Search. So if you explore an idea in the Gemini app, AI Mode will have that foundation to build on, and vice versa. While you could already opt in to connect your Search history to Personal Intelligence in the Gemini app, soon you can also opt in to securely connect your Gemini app to Personal Intelligence in AI Mode.

连接 Gemini 应用与搜索：在未来几周内，您可以轻松地在 Gemini 应用和搜索的 AI 模式之间共享背景信息。因此，如果您在 Gemini 应用中探索了一个想法，AI 模式将以此为基础进行构建，反之亦然。虽然您之前已经可以选择将搜索历史记录连接到 Gemini 应用中的“个人智能”，但很快您也可以选择安全地将 Gemini 应用连接到 AI 模式下的“个人智能”。

To connect your apps to Personal Intelligence in AI Mode, open Search Personalization, select Connected Apps, and then choose the apps you want to connect. You choose if and when you opt in, and you can turn those connections on or off at any time. You’re always in control of your personalization settings, and soon you’ll also be able to view the context AI Mode uses via the “Personalization” icon below any tailored response.

要将应用连接到 AI 模式下的“个人智能”，请打开“搜索个性化”，选择“已连接的应用”，然后选择您想要连接的应用。您可以自行决定是否以及何时加入，并且可以随时开启或关闭这些连接。您始终掌控着自己的个性化设置，很快您还可以通过任何个性化响应下方的“个性化”图标查看 AI 模式所使用的背景信息。
