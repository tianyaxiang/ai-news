Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.26747 (cs) [Submitted on 19 Nov 2026] Title: SkillPrivus: Uncovering PRIVacy of USe Harms in Agentic Skills Authors: Mariana Fernandez-Espinosa, Ivoline Ngong, Keerthiram Murugesan, Karthikeyan Natesan Ramamurthy.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.26747 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 19 日] 标题：SkillPrivus：揭示智能体技能中的使用隐私危害 作者：Mariana Fernandez-Espinosa, Ivoline Ngong, Keerthiram Murugesan, Karthikeyan Natesan Ramamurthy。

Abstract: Agent skills extend AI assistants through installable instructions and code that can access users' files, accounts, and data. Even when performing their advertised tasks, skills may infer sensitive attributes, retain unnecessary information, or disclose data to unexpected recipients.

摘要：智能体技能通过可安装的指令和代码扩展了 AI 助手的功能，这些指令和代码可以访问用户的文档、账户和数据。即使在执行其宣传的任务时，这些技能也可能推断出敏感属性、保留不必要的信息，或将数据泄露给非预期的接收者。

We study these potential privacy-of-use harms through SkillPrivus, a static audit framework that analyzes skills before installation without executing them. SkillPrivus extracts data operations from code and natural-language instructions, grounds them in source evidence, and adapts Contextual Integrity to assess their appropriateness.

我们通过 SkillPrivus 研究了这些潜在的使用隐私危害，这是一个在安装前分析技能而不执行它们的静态审计框架。SkillPrivus 从代码和自然语言指令中提取数据操作，将其建立在源证据的基础上，并采用“语境完整性”（Contextual Integrity）来评估其适当性。

Applied to 726 real skills from public marketplaces, 563 of which handle personal data, SkillPrivus flags at least one potential violation in 307 of these skills (54.5%), and its judgments agree with three blind human annotators on 83.2% of resolvable moves.

在应用于来自公共市场的 726 个真实技能（其中 563 个处理个人数据）时，SkillPrivus 在其中的 307 个技能（54.5%）中标记了至少一个潜在违规行为，其判断与三名盲测人类标注员在 83.2% 的可判定操作上达成一致。

The flagged behaviors include sensitive inferences, information use beyond the stated task, and disclosures to unexpected recipients, suggesting that privacy harms can arise from ordinary skill functionality.

被标记的行为包括敏感推断、超出既定任务的信息使用以及向非预期接收者披露数据，这表明隐私危害可能源于普通的技能功能。
