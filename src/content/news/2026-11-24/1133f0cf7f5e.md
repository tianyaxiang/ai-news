“It costs what it costs” is a mindset I’ve found useful when encountering a sizable, but highly necessary or desired, expense. The sentiment encourages me to accept the price because that’s the accepted value of the gadget, app, or service that I really need or want. But with surveillance pricing, that comfort is no longer a given. I could end up paying more for my TV because of where I am or more for a software subscription because of my age.

“该花多少钱就花多少钱”是我在面对一笔数额巨大但又非常必要或渴望的开支时，觉得很有用的一种心态。这种情绪鼓励我接受价格，因为那是我真正需要或想要的设备、应用程序或服务的公认价值。但有了监控定价（surveillance pricing），这种心理安慰就不再理所当然了。我可能会因为所处的位置而为电视支付更多费用，或者因为年龄的原因而为软件订阅支付更多费用。

Surveillance pricing leverages data collected from our web history, smart devices, and apps with the goal of getting us to pay more. That obfuscates the value of products, challenges smart shopping and budgets, and makes it harder for people to get products and services at a fair price.

监控定价利用从我们的网络历史记录、智能设备和应用程序中收集的数据，目的是让我们支付更多费用。这模糊了产品的价值，挑战了精明购物和预算规划，并使人们更难以公平的价格获得产品和服务。

Surveillance pricing, according to the nonprofit Electronic Privacy Information Center (EPIC), is an algorithmic pricing practice that charges individuals or groups of individuals different prices “for the same goods or services.” “The goal of surveillance pricing is to infer the highest price that the customer is willing to pay, based on consumers’ personal data and other market data,” EPIC explains.

根据非营利组织电子隐私信息中心（EPIC）的说法，监控定价是一种算法定价实践，即对个人或群体“针对相同的商品或服务”收取不同的价格。EPIC解释说：“监控定价的目标是根据消费者的个人数据和其他市场数据，推断出客户愿意支付的最高价格。”

Dynamic pricing, or surge pricing, differs from surveillance pricing in that, instead of relying on consumer data, it uses changing market trends, like supply and demand, inventory levels, and competitor pricing, to set prices. While dynamic pricing may charge you more for an Uber ride when it’s very busy, surveillance pricing can feel less reasonable.

动态定价（或称激增定价）与监控定价的不同之处在于，它不依赖消费者数据，而是利用供需、库存水平和竞争对手定价等不断变化的市场趋势来设定价格。虽然动态定价可能会在非常繁忙时向你收取更高的优步车费，但监控定价可能会让人觉得更不合理。

ZipRecruiter was one of the first companies to leverage surveillance pricing as we think of it today. In 2018, its profits increased 84 percent by using surveillance pricing versus charging every customer $99. Surveillance pricing is still somewhat nascent, but its potential lucrativeness and the growing availability of user data suggest it may become more common.

ZipRecruiter 是最早利用我们今天所理解的监控定价的公司之一。2018 年，通过使用监控定价而非向每位客户收取 99 美元，其利润增长了 84%。监控定价目前仍处于萌芽阶段，但其潜在的盈利能力和用户数据的日益普及表明，它可能会变得更加普遍。

Surveillance pricing makes it hard for shoppers to get the best, or even fair, prices and makes it difficult to assess the true value of products. There are also broader implications that go beyond how consumers feel about it. A 2025 report from the American Economic Liberties Project, “Prohibiting Surveillance Prices and Wages” (PDF), says surveillance pricing relies on biased algorithms: Algorithms often rely on data that reflect historical and ongoing biases, embedding these prejudices into wage- and price-setting systems and perpetuating them.

监控定价使购物者难以获得最优甚至公平的价格，也难以评估产品的真实价值。此外，它还有超出消费者感受范围的更广泛影响。美国经济自由项目（American Economic Liberties Project）2025 年的一份报告《禁止监控价格和工资》（PDF）指出，监控定价依赖于有偏见的算法：算法通常依赖于反映历史和当前偏见的数据，将这些偏见嵌入到工资和价格设定系统中，并使之长期存在。
