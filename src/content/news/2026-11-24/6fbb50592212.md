# AISCARE

I Built Something I Never Expected To Build I started using AI because I was curious. Everyone seemed to have an opinion about it, so I decided to see for myself. At first I was just experimenting with One or two ideas led to another, and before I knew it I was working on Jupiter TV, a project that lets people stream and organise video in a smarter way.

我构建了一些我从未预料到会构建的东西。我开始使用人工智能是因为我感到好奇。每个人似乎对此都有自己的看法，所以我决定亲自一探究竟。起初，我只是在进行实验，一个或两个想法引出了另一个，不知不觉中，我已经在开发 Jupiter TV 了，这是一个让人们能以更智能的方式流式传输和组织视频的项目。

The project wasn't the difficult part. The difficult part was sticking with it when things didn't work. Most of what I learned came from trying things, getting stuck, fixing problems and starting again. Some days felt like progress. Other days felt like going backwards.

项目本身并不是最困难的部分。最困难的是在事情进展不顺时坚持下去。我学到的大部分东西都来自于尝试、遇到瓶颈、解决问题并重新开始。有些日子感觉是在进步，而另一些日子则感觉是在倒退。

What kept me going was curiosity. I wanted to know what would happen if I kept building. Looking back, AI didn't magically create anything for me. It gave me the confidence to try things I wouldn't have attempted before. That's the biggest thing I've gained from this journey.

让我坚持下去的是好奇心。我想知道如果我继续构建会发生什么。回首往事，人工智能并没有为我神奇地创造出任何东西。它给了我尝试以前不敢尝试的事物的信心。这是我从这段旅程中获得的最大收获。

Not the project itself. The belief that I can learn new things by simply having a go. And it all started because I was curious enough to start asking questions.

并非项目本身，而是那种相信只要去尝试就能学到新东西的信念。而这一切的开始，仅仅是因为我足够好奇，从而开始提出问题。
