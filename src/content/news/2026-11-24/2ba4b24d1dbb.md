# COP31 ends in failure as countries split over fossil fuels, climate finance

ANTALYA, Turkey—By the time the United Nations COP31 climate talks ended Sunday afternoon, the conference center resembled a going-out-of-business sale at a pawnshop for broken dreams. As cleaning crews cleared away the debris left by more than 100,000 participants, Murat Kurum, president of the Conference of the Parties and Turkey’s minister of environment, urbanization, and climate change, tried to tie together disparate parts of a draft climate deal with a shiny ribbon of multilateralism.

土耳其安塔利亚——当联合国COP31气候谈判于周日下午结束时，会议中心看起来就像一家破产当铺的清仓甩卖现场，到处都是破碎的梦想。当清洁人员清理掉超过10万名与会者留下的垃圾时，缔约方会议主席、土耳其环境、城市化和气候变化部长穆拉特·库鲁姆（Murat Kurum）试图用多边主义的华丽丝带将气候协议草案中零散的部分串联起来。

Many countries were not buying it. For the first time since 2009, the conference ended without consensus on a cover decision, the concluding statement that typically signals global solidarity on how to confront the worsening climate crisis. Countries did adopt several decisions, including a program to help workers and communities transition away from fossil fuels, and a plan to assess progress on climate adaptation.

许多国家并不买账。自2009年以来，本次会议首次在没有就“封面决定”（cover decision）达成共识的情况下结束。该总结性声明通常标志着全球在如何应对日益恶化的气候危机方面达成团结。各国确实通过了几项决定，包括一项帮助工人和社区摆脱化石燃料的计划，以及一项评估气候适应进展的计划。

The failure to agree on an overarching conference statement reflected deep divisions over whether the agreement should mention fossil fuels and carbon capture, as well as how much climate financing developed countries should provide to developing nations hit by costly climate disasters. The European Union, as well as several Latin American countries and Pacific Island states, said they would not accept the proposed text from the conference leaders.

未能就总体会议声明达成一致，反映出各方在协议是否应提及化石燃料和碳捕获，以及发达国家应向遭受昂贵气候灾害的发展中国家提供多少气候融资等问题上存在严重分歧。欧盟以及几个拉美国家和太平洋岛国表示，他们不会接受会议领导人提出的文本。

Cover decisions at the annual conferences usually focus on new steps or language that could reinforce international efforts to slow global warming. A central aim at this meeting was to set quantifiable but voluntary global goals for the widespread electrification of economies, and to increase funding from developed countries to help developing countries adapt to climate impacts.

年度会议上的“封面决定”通常侧重于能够加强国际社会减缓全球变暖努力的新举措或措辞。本次会议的一个核心目标是为经济的广泛电气化设定可量化但自愿的全球目标，并增加发达国家的资金投入，以帮助发展中国家适应气候影响。

In 2022, the UN conference in Egypt agreed to establish a loss and damage fund to help vulnerable countries pay for irreversible climate impacts. A year later in Dubai, delegates from more than 190 countries agreed, for the first time, that a “just and orderly” transition away from fossil fuels in energy systems is needed to slow global warming. Since then, international collaboration on climate issues has declined amid increased conflict, rising oil and gas prices and economic insecurity.

2022年，在埃及举行的联合国会议同意设立“损失与损害”基金，以帮助脆弱国家支付不可逆转的气候影响所带来的费用。一年后在迪拜，来自190多个国家的代表首次达成一致，认为需要对能源系统进行“公正且有序”的转型，以摆脱化石燃料，从而减缓全球变暖。自那以后，在冲突加剧、油气价格上涨和经济不稳定的背景下，国际社会在气候问题上的合作有所下降。

The talks in Turkey ran two days beyond the scheduled end without reaching broad agreement on potential next steps, an outcome that won’t kill the global effort but is widely viewed as a serious setback. But compromise for the sake of consensus is not enough, said Kathy Jetn̄il-Kijiner, a climate envoy for the Marshall Islands Ministry of the Environment. “We will not be forced to accept false solutions,” she said. “We will not compromise our future to benefit fossil fuel interests.”

土耳其的谈判比原定结束时间延长了两天，但仍未就潜在的后续步骤达成广泛共识。这一结果虽然不会终结全球性的努力，但被广泛视为一次严重的挫折。然而，马绍尔群岛环境部气候特使凯西·杰特尼尔-基吉纳（Kathy Jetn̄il-Kijiner）表示，为了达成共识而妥协是不够的。“我们不会被迫接受虚假的解决方案，”她说，“我们不会为了化石燃料利益而牺牲我们的未来。”

As written, she added, the Antalya Pact undermines her country’s security and threatens its future. “People are dying, seas are rising, and ecosystems are collapsing,” Jetn̄il-Kijiner said. “Are we so divorced from reality that we take backward steps, even as overshoot threatens to overtake us? To accept this deal would be a betrayal to the Marshallese people.”

她补充说，按照目前的写法，《安塔利亚公约》损害了她国家的安全并威胁到其未来。“人们正在死去，海平面正在上升，生态系统正在崩溃，”杰特尼尔-基吉纳说，“我们是否已经脱离现实到这种地步，以至于在气候超标威胁着我们的时候还要倒退？接受这份协议将是对马绍尔人民的背叛。”

It’s not the first time Pacific Island nations threatened by rising seas have objected to the outcome at one of the United Nations’ annual conferences. During the 2023 UN talks in Dubai, a final text was adopted even though a delegation representing 39 small island developing states objected because they were still outside the plenary meeting, trying to coordinate their position. But in Antalya, the divide ran much deeper.

这并非受海平面上升威胁的太平洋岛国首次对联合国年度会议的结果提出异议。在2023年迪拜举行的联合国谈判中，尽管代表39个小岛屿发展中国家的代表团因当时仍在全体会议外协调立场而表示反对，但最终文本还是获得了通过。但在安塔利亚，分歧要深刻得多。

The economically powerful and politically influential European Union, representing 28 member countries, also rejected the proposed agreement. Ireland’s climate, energy, and environment minister, Darragh O’Brien, said the European countries formally objected to the cover decision because it fell short of what the global climate crisis demands. “We will not endorse a decision that does not even mention fossil fuels, the key driver of climate change, and not accept a COP that greenwashes fossil fuels,” said O’Brien, speaking on behalf of the EU during Ireland’s rotating presidency.

代表28个成员国、在经济和政治上具有影响力的欧盟也拒绝了该拟议协议。爱尔兰气候、能源和环境部长达拉·奥布莱恩（Darragh O’Brien）表示，欧洲国家正式反对该“封面决定”，因为它未能达到全球气候危机的要求。在爱尔兰担任欧盟轮值主席国期间，奥布莱恩代表欧盟发言称：“我们不会支持一项甚至不提及气候变化关键驱动因素——化石燃料的决定，也不会接受一个为化石燃料进行‘漂绿’的缔约方会议。”

Egypt and Brazil, hosts of the UN climate talks in 2023 and 2025, also criticized the text proposed by COP31 president Kurum of Turkey and Chris Bowen of Australia, president of the conference negotiations. Liliam Beatris Chagas de Moura, director for climate at Brazil’s Ministry of Foreign Affairs, said she was disappointed that this year’s conference did not sustain the momentum from last year’s talks in her country.

2023年和2025年联合国气候谈判的主办国埃及和巴西也批评了由COP31主席、土耳其人库鲁姆和会议谈判主席、澳大利亚人克里斯·鲍文（Chris Bowen）提出的文本。巴西外交部气候司司长莉莉安·比阿特丽斯·查加斯·德·莫拉（Liliam Beatris Chagas de Moura）表示，她对今年的会议未能保持去年在她国家举行谈判时的势头感到失望。

Mohammad Ayoub, a climate negotiator with the Saudi Arabian energy ministry, said the 22-member League of Arab States supported the proposed Antalya Pact. He criticized developed countries for using up most of the global carbon budget, the amount of fossil fuels that can be burned without overheating the planet, and then asking developing countries to give up the very energy sources that fueled their own growth.

沙特阿拉伯能源部气候谈判代表穆罕默德·阿尤布（Mohammad Ayoub）表示，由22个成员国组成的阿拉伯国家联盟支持拟议的《安塔利亚公约》。他批评发达国家耗尽了全球大部分碳预算（即在不使地球过热的情况下可以燃烧的化石燃料总量），然后却要求发展中国家放弃那些曾推动其自身增长的能源。

Ayoub condemned some countries’ opposition to including references to carbon dioxide removal and carbon capture and storage in the pact, calling their resistance to those technologies unscientific. Carbon capture and storage are not close to being deployed fast enough “to keep 1.5 degrees within reach,” he said, referring to the long-term goal of limiting the average全球 temperature increase established by the 2015 Paris climate agreement. “We believe that climate change requires all solutions on the table, and that countries select from these solutions depending on their national circumstances.”

阿尤布谴责一些国家反对在公约中提及二氧化碳清除以及碳捕获与封存技术，称他们对这些技术的抵制是不科学的。他提到2015年《巴黎协定》设定的将全球平均气温升幅限制在1.5摄氏度以内的长期目标时表示，碳捕获与封存技术的部署速度远不足以“实现1.5摄氏度的目标”。“我们认为，气候变化需要将所有解决方案摆在桌面上，各国应根据自身国情从中进行选择。”

UN Secretary-General António Guterres said he was “deeply disappointed” that the talks ended without consensus on an overarching agreement. “But this setback does not change the facts,” he said. “Fossil fuels are driving the climate crisis. We can’t put out the fire while pouring fuel on it.”

联合国秘书长古特雷斯表示，他对谈判在未能就总体协议达成共识的情况下结束感到“深感失望”。“但这次挫折并没有改变事实，”他说，“化石燃料正在驱动气候危机。我们不能在往火上浇油的同时又试图灭火。”
