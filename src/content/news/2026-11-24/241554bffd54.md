**EXECUTIVE SUMMARY** Tech companies are investing a lot of hope and money in rocks. Google, Microsoft, and others have committed hundreds of millions of dollars to get startups to spread crushed basalt, olivine, or other alkaline materials across farms, in an effort to capture carbon from the air and lock it away.

**执行摘要** 科技公司正将大量的希望和资金投入到岩石上。谷歌、微软及其他公司已投入数亿美元，支持初创企业在农田上铺设粉碎的玄武岩、橄榄石或其他碱性材料，旨在从空气中捕获二氧化碳并将其封存。

A number of influential models have found that this approach, known as enhanced rock weathering (ERW), could eventually remove 3 billion to 8 billion metric tons of carbon dioxide per year, helping to slow or one day even reverse global warming. An additional promise is that these applications of rock could help farmers by improving the quality of their soil.

一些具有影响力的模型发现，这种被称为“增强岩石风化”（ERW）的方法最终每年可清除 30 亿至 80 亿公吨二氧化碳，有助于减缓甚至在未来扭转全球变暖。另一个承诺是，这些岩石的应用可以通过改善土壤质量来帮助农民。

But a pair of preprint studies set for release today raise serious questions about how much material could be added to that soil before it may actually reduce the crop yields that are essential to farmers’ livelihoods, MIT Technology Review can exclusively report. Cutting back on the rock applications to prevent these negative effects would shrink the method’s carbon dioxide removal capacity, likely capping it at less than a billion tons per year.

但据《麻省理工科技评论》独家报道，今天发布的两项预印本研究提出了严重质疑：在土壤中添加多少材料才不会导致农作物减产，从而影响农民的生计？为了防止这些负面影响而减少岩石的使用量，将削弱该方法的二氧化碳清除能力，其上限可能降至每年不到 10 亿吨。

The analyses were led by researchers at CarbonPlan, a San Francisco nonprofit known for rigorous scientific analysis of carbon removal methods. These concerns come on top of a series of scientific questions about how to accurately estimate the carbon removal achieved through this approach and the possibility that some projects have overstated it, which researchers at CarbonPlan and elsewhere have raised in recent months and years.

这些分析由旧金山非营利组织 CarbonPlan 的研究人员领导，该组织以对碳清除方法进行严谨的科学分析而闻名。除了这些担忧之外，CarbonPlan 及其他机构的研究人员在近几个月和几年里还提出了一系列科学问题，包括如何准确评估通过该方法实现的碳清除量，以及某些项目是否存在夸大其词的可能性。

“There’s still a heck of a lot we need to learn about ERW,” says Tyler Kukla, a research scientist at CarbonPlan and lead author of the new reports. “But a real tension, to me, exists between this massive amount of uncertainty and the big questions we have about whether or not this works, sitting alongside tremendous efforts to scale it up really quickly.”

“关于 ERW，我们还有很多需要了解的地方，”CarbonPlan 的研究科学家、新报告的主要作者泰勒·库克拉（Tyler Kukla）表示，“但在我看来，这种巨大的不确定性以及关于它是否有效的重大疑问，与目前快速扩大规模的巨大努力之间，存在着真正的矛盾。”

**How it works** There is no scientific dispute that certain rocks and minerals help to sequester carbon. Rain captures carbon dioxide molecules as it falls through the atmosphere, forming carbonic acid. When it lands on exposed silicate materials, that weak acid dissolves or chemically “weathers” the rocks.

**它是如何运作的** 科学界对于某些岩石和矿物有助于封存碳这一点并无争议。雨水在穿过大气层时会捕获二氧化碳分子，形成碳酸。当雨水落在暴露的硅酸盐物质上时，这种弱酸会溶解或在化学上“风化”岩石。

The resulting calcium, magnesium, and carbon (in the form of bicarbonate) then travel on a long journey through creeks, streams, and rivers until they reach the ocean, where corals, mollusks, and other marine organisms may eventually use them to form calcium carbonate shells. As they die, those shells fall to the seafloor, combine with sediments, and form limestone rock that locks away the carbon.

由此产生的钙、镁和碳（以碳酸氢盐形式存在）随后经过溪流、河流的长途跋涉到达海洋，珊瑚、软体动物和其他海洋生物最终可能利用它们形成碳酸钙外壳。当这些生物死亡时，外壳会落入海底，与沉积物结合，形成封存碳的石灰岩。

If you thought that explanation was long, just imagine how long the process takes to unfold in real life! It’s not called the slow carbon cycle for nothing. “Enhanced” rock weathering promises to accelerate the process. Grinding up the rocks and spreading them across fields creates much more surface area for carbonic acid to react with—and that should ultimately direct more carbon into the oceans.

如果你觉得这个解释很长，试想一下现实生活中这个过程需要多长时间！它被称为“慢碳循环”并非没有道理。“增强”岩石风化旨在加速这一过程。将岩石粉碎并铺撒在田间，可以为碳酸提供更大的反应表面积，从而最终将更多的碳引入海洋。

ERW doesn’t require as much energy or infrastructure as other carbon dioxide removal options like direct-air-capture plants, and at least in the early rollout stages it can largely rely on waste material from rock mining operations. But the big selling point is that there can be an economic benefit to doing it; it’s not just a cost like other forms of carbon removal.

与其他二氧化碳清除方案（如直接空气捕获工厂）相比，ERW 不需要那么多的能源或基础设施，并且至少在推广初期，它可以在很大程度上依赖采矿作业产生的废料。但其最大的卖点在于它能带来经济效益；它不像其他形式的碳清除那样仅仅是一种成本。

Added to agricultural soils, these sorts of rocks can increase nutrients, reduce acidity, and improve water retention, helping to boost crop yields (at least up to a point). Indeed, many farmers already routinely add crushed-up limestone to their soil to counteract the increased acidity caused by fertilizer use and other agricultural practices.

将这类岩石添加到农业土壤中，可以增加养分、降低酸度并改善保水性，从而有助于提高农作物产量（至少在一定程度上）。事实上，许多农民已经习惯在土壤中添加粉碎的石灰岩，以抵消因使用化肥和其他农业实践而导致的酸度增加。

So carrying out enhanced rock weathering could merely be a matter of switching to other types of rock with the potential to lock away more carbon (about twice as much as limestone). “It’s something the Earth has been doing for billions of years,” says Mary Yap, chief executive and cofounder of Lithos Carbon, an ERW startup. “It can scale. We know it works kinetically.”

因此，实施增强岩石风化可能仅仅是改用其他类型的岩石，这些岩石具有封存更多碳（约为石灰岩的两倍）的潜力。“这是地球几十亿年来一直在做的事情，”ERW 初创公司 Lithos Carbon 的首席执行官兼联合创始人玛丽·叶（Mary Yap）说，“它可以规模化。我们知道它在动力学上是有效的。”

“If we can figure out where in the world are the best places to do this and then make that happen,” she adds, “it can help both the communities and also help our atmosphere.” Enhanced rock weathering could be of particular benefit to farmers who haven’t been able to manage their soil acidity, including many subsistence farmers in poorer parts of the world, says Tim Jesper Suhrhoff, a postdoctoral fellow at the Yale Center for Natural Carbon Capture.

“如果我们能找出世界上最适合进行这项工作的地方并付诸实践，”她补充道，“它既能帮助当地社区，也能改善我们的大气环境。”耶鲁大学自然碳捕获中心博士后研究员蒂姆·杰斯珀·苏尔霍夫（Tim Jesper Suhrhoff）表示，增强岩石风化对那些无法管理土壤酸度的农民尤其有益，包括世界贫困地区的许多自给自足的农民。

One recent study found that ERW boosted the median yields of smallholder farmers in India by more than 20%. “That is huge for subsistence farmers,” he says.

最近的一项研究发现，ERW 使印度小农户的产量中位数提高了 20% 以上。“这对自给自足的农民来说意义重大，”他说。

**Agricultural limits** But the new findings, which have not yet been peer-reviewed, suggest there could be a steep trade-off, at a certain point, between benefits for farmers and benefits for the climate. A number of modeling studies that inform UN climate reports and national climate efforts have estimated that adding crushed rock to agricultural land could store away four to 12 tons of carbon dioxide per hectare per year (though some estimate lower levels).

**农业限制** 但这些尚未经过同行评审的新发现表明，在一定程度上，农民的利益与气候利益之间可能存在巨大的权衡。一些为联合国气候报告和国家气候工作提供参考的建模研究估计，在农田上添加碎石每年每公顷可封存 4 至 12 吨二氧化碳（尽管有些估计值较低）。

But the authors state in one of the new papers that these findings don’t account for the ways that significant levels of enhanced rock weathering will alter the pH of agricultural soil over time. Initially it should merely reduce the soil acidity, but adding too much of these materials would eventually push up the pH beyond neutral and into alkaline territory.

但作者在其中一篇新论文中指出，这些研究结果没有考虑到大规模的增强岩石风化会随时间推移改变农业土壤的 pH 值。最初，它可能只是降低土壤酸度，但添加过多的此类材料最终会将 pH 值推高至中性以上，进入碱性范围。

That could cause micronutrient deficiencies, disease susceptibility, and other problems for certain crops, potentially reducing yields—and, by extension, food supplies and farm revenue. That would likely force farmers to halt the practice, at least until soil pH returns to favorable levels. The analysis found that preventing the more extreme effects of soil alkalization could limit ERW’s total potential to around 600 million tons of carbon dioxide.

这可能会导致某些农作物出现微量元素缺乏、易感疾病及其他问题，从而可能降低产量，进而影响粮食供应和农业收入。这很可能会迫使农民停止这种做法，至少要等到土壤 pH 值恢复到适宜水平。分析发现，为了防止土壤碱化的极端影响，ERW 的总潜力可能会被限制在每年约 6 亿吨二氧化碳左右。
