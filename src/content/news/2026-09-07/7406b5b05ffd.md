# Nitter and XCancel resume service after legal advice
# 在获得法律建议后，Nitter 与 XCancel 恢复服务

On 24 August 2026, cease and desist letters were sent by X Corp. demanding a permanent takedown of Nitter instances and the project's repository.
2026 年 8 月 24 日，X Corp. 发出停止侵权函（Cease and Desist），要求永久关闭 Nitter 实例及该项目的代码仓库。

**UPDATE:** Following legal advice, the Nitter project will continue. More details will be announced soon.
**更新：** 在获得法律建议后，Nitter 项目将继续运营。更多细节将于近期公布。

A free and open source alternative Twitter front-end focused on privacy and performance. Inspired by the Invidious project.
这是一个专注于隐私和性能的免费开源 Twitter 前端替代方案，灵感源自 Invidious 项目。

The project features no JavaScript requirements, zero ads, and ensures all requests go through the backend so the client never talks to Twitter directly. This prevents Twitter from tracking your IP or JavaScript fingerprint.
该项目无需 JavaScript，没有任何广告，并确保所有请求均通过后端处理，客户端不会直接与 Twitter 通信。这有效防止了 Twitter 追踪用户的 IP 地址或 JavaScript 指纹。

Additionally, Nitter uses Twitter's unofficial API, which requires no developer account, and remains lightweight compared to the official platform. It also supports RSS feeds, themes, and mobile-responsive design, while being licensed under AGPLv3 to prevent proprietary instances.
此外，Nitter 使用无需开发者账号的 Twitter 非官方 API，且相比官方平台更加轻量。它还支持 RSS 订阅、主题切换和移动端响应式设计，并采用 AGPLv3 协议授权，以禁止私有化实例。

For legal inquiries and DMCA requests, the project has directed contacts to legal@poast.org.
针对法律咨询及 DMCA（数字千年版权法）请求，该项目已指定联系邮箱为 legal@poast.org。
