# An Amazon cargo plane crashed at Miami International Airport
# 一架亚马逊货运飞机在迈阿密国际机场坠毁

An Amazon cargo plane crashed at Miami International Airport on September 06, 2026.
2026年9月6日，一架亚马逊货运飞机在迈阿密国际机场坠毁。

A plane bearing an Amazon logo overran the runway at Miami International Airport on Sunday during landing, crashing into vehicles and resulting in multiple injuries. The extent of the damage or the seriousness of the injuries was not clear at the time of publication.
周日，一架印有亚马逊标志的飞机在迈阿密国际机场降落时冲出跑道，撞上了多辆汽车，并造成多人受伤。截至本文发布时，受损程度及伤者伤势尚不明确。

In the aftermath of the incident, the FAA released a statement saying: 21 Air Flight 7598 overran the runway after landing at Miami International Airport around 2 p.m. local time on Sunday, Sept. 6. The Boeing 767-300 cargo aircraft departed from Luis Muñoz Marín International Airport in San Juan, Puerto Rico.
事故发生后，美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）发表声明称：9月6日周日当地时间下午2点左右，21 Air 7598号航班在迈阿密国际机场降落后冲出跑道。该波音767-300货机此前从波多黎各圣胡安的路易斯·穆尼奥斯·马林国际机场起飞。

As a result, flights at the airport were grounded and over 60 Miami-Dade Fire Rescue units were deployed to the scene. When MDFR arrived, the plane was on fire and spewing heavy smoke, according to reports. Video from the scene posted by NBC 6 clearly shows the Amazon logo on the plane’s tail and thick smoke rising from the scene where the plane came to a stop on the other side of the road from the airport.
受此影响，机场航班被迫停飞，超过60个迈阿密-戴德县消防救援单位被部署到现场。据报道，当消防救援人员抵达时，飞机已经起火并冒出浓烟。NBC 6发布的现场视频清晰地显示了飞机尾部的亚马逊标志，以及飞机在机场路对面停下后现场升起的滚滚浓烟。

Amazon spokesperson Kelly Nantel told The Verge: “We can confirm that an Amazon Air plane operated by 21 Air experienced an incident while attempting to land at Miami International Airport today. This is a fast-moving situation and we’re still gathering details. We’re working closely with local authorities and officials to understand exactly what happened. Right now, our absolute priority is the safety, well-being, and care of everyone involved. We’re doing everything we can to support those affected.”
亚马逊发言人凯利·南特尔（Kelly Nantel）向《The Verge》表示：“我们可以确认，一架由21 Air运营的亚马逊航空飞机今日在尝试降落迈阿密国际机场时发生了事故。目前情况正在迅速发展，我们仍在收集细节。我们正与当地政府和官员密切合作，以了解事故的具体经过。眼下，我们的首要任务是确保所有相关人员的安全、健康和照护。我们正在尽一切努力支持受影响的人员。”
