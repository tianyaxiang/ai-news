Depending on which corner of social media you frequent, the Tesla Cybercab event was either proof that the company is a robotaxi leader delivering the future or an overhyped affair with little substance. Whatever your view, Wall Street wasn’t impressed by the Cybercab event in Austin, which was unusually quiet and lacked the festival-like atmosphere of past Tesla events. CEO Elon Musk didn’t even attend — a notable and puzzling absence, especially considering he has spent years arguing that Tesla’s future — and its value — hinges on robotics and AI.
根据您常逛的社交媒体圈子，特斯拉的 Cybercab 发布会要么被视为该公司作为自动驾驶出租车领军者正在交付未来的证明，要么被视为一场缺乏实质内容、过度炒作的活动。无论您的观点如何，华尔街对这场在奥斯汀举行的 Cybercab 发布会并不买账，现场异常安静，缺乏特斯拉以往活动中那种节日般的氛围。首席执行官埃隆·马斯克甚至没有出席——这是一个引人注目且令人困惑的缺席，尤其是考虑到他多年来一直坚称特斯拉的未来及其价值取决于机器人技术和人工智能。

As senior reporter Sean O’Kane wrote before the event, “The Cybercab is Tesla’s fork in the road. This is either the moment the company begins to transform urban transportation as we know it, or the moment that reveals that, despite its many efforts, Tesla really is just an automaker after all.” The event did little to boost confidence, but I don’t put much stock in events or demos. The real test is whether Tesla can launch a robotaxi service safely and at scale. For now, there is no scale, at least compared with Waymo. (As of today, Tesla has registered 45 Cybercabs in Texas, according to the state’s automated vehicle tracker.)
正如资深记者 Sean O’Kane 在活动前所写：“Cybercab 是特斯拉的十字路口。这要么是该公司开始改变我们所知的城市交通的时刻，要么是揭示出尽管特斯拉做出了诸多努力，但它终究只是一家汽车制造商的时刻。”这次活动并没有起到多少提振信心的作用，但我并不太看重活动或演示。真正的考验在于特斯拉能否安全且大规模地推出自动驾驶出租车服务。目前，至少与 Waymo 相比，特斯拉还没有实现规模化。（据德克萨斯州自动驾驶车辆追踪系统显示，截至今日，特斯拉在该州仅注册了 45 辆 Cybercab。）

Another wrinkle emerged early Friday morning. The National Highway Traffic Safety Administration opened an investigation into Tesla just hours after the company put its first Cybercabs, which lack a steering wheel and pedals, on Austin streets. Federal vehicle safety regulations require manual controls like brake pedals, though the Department of Transportation recently proposed removing those requirements for vehicles that are designed to drive themselves. Tesla chose to self-certify, the standard route taken by automakers for traditional human-driven vehicles. The NHTSA responded by tapping the brakes, although Tesla may choose to operate even as the investigation plays out. To be clear, there is precedent for the NHTSA’s action, and you can read all about it here.
周五清晨又出现了一个波折。在特斯拉将首批没有方向盘和踏板的 Cybercab 投放至奥斯汀街头仅数小时后，美国国家公路交通安全管理局（NHTSA）就对特斯拉展开了调查。联邦车辆安全法规要求车辆必须配备刹车踏板等手动控制装置，尽管交通部最近提议取消针对自动驾驶车辆的这些要求。特斯拉选择了自我认证，这是汽车制造商为传统人工驾驶车辆采取的标准途径。NHTSA 对此采取了制动措施，尽管特斯拉可能会选择在调查期间继续运营。需要明确的是，NHTSA 的行动是有先例的，您可以在此处阅读相关详情。
