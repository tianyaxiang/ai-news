# The Best Gaming Chairs (2026): Are They Worth It?
# 2026 年最佳电竞椅：它们值得买吗？

The best gaming chairs will be different from person to person. It boils down to the one that you prefer based on your own priorities and sitting styles, but gaming chairs can sometimes fall into the trap of focusing on gamer aesthetics and gimmicks over comfort. There are, however, a few that avoid this pitfall. As I continue the hunt for gaming chairs that don't suck, here are a few picks I've tested that are a good place to start.
每个人心目中的最佳电竞椅各不相同。这归根结底取决于你根据个人需求和坐姿偏好所做出的选择。然而，电竞椅有时会陷入一种误区，即过度追求游戏美学和噱头，而忽略了舒适度。不过，还是有几款产品避开了这个陷阱。随着我不断寻找那些“不坑人”的电竞椅，以下是我测试过的一些值得参考的型号。

Whether you get in some gaming sessions between work and the gym, or you play from 5 on Friday to 11 on Sunday, we've got a recommendation for you here.
无论你是想在工作和健身之余玩会儿游戏，还是从周五下午 5 点一直玩到周日晚上 11 点，我们都能为你提供合适的推荐。

### Best Gaming Chair for Most People: Branch Ergonomic Chair Pro: SEGA Edition
### 大多数人的最佳选择：Branch Ergonomic Chair Pro: SEGA 联名版

The Branch Ergonomic Chair Pro is our favorite office chair, and it's also my favorite gaming chair. As it turns out, a comfortable and reliable design is ideal regardless of the use case. The real “gaming” piece boils down to aesthetics. And now, this chair is available in three different Sega-branded styles, all of which are super comfortable and adjustable. Assembly wasn't too difficult, and the chair isn't too fiddly to get dialed in, though there definitely is a learning curve when it comes to all the different knobs.
Branch Ergonomic Chair Pro 是我们最喜欢的办公椅，也是我最喜欢的电竞椅。事实证明，无论用途如何，舒适且可靠的设计都是理想之选。这款椅子真正的“电竞”属性在于其外观设计。现在，它推出了三种不同的世嘉（Sega）联名款式，每一款都非常舒适且可调节。组装并不困难，调节起来也不算太繁琐，尽管面对各种旋钮时确实需要一点学习成本。

Each of the designs is both fun and attractive, and the chair offers incredible value for the price. The Sonic version has a mesh back, while the Sega Genesis and Sega Dreamcast versions have leather backs. I didn't find the leather-backed version that I tested to be too sweaty. All the little details of the designs pop, and it's hard to find a better gaming chair for the money.
每一款设计都既有趣又美观，而且这款椅子在价格上极具性价比。索尼克（Sonic）版本采用网面靠背，而世嘉 Genesis 和世嘉 Dreamcast 版本则采用皮革靠背。我测试的皮革版本并没有让我感到闷热。设计中的所有小细节都非常出彩，在这个价位很难找到比它更好的电竞椅了。

### Best Gaming Chair for Any Sitting Style: Razer Iskur V2 NewGen
### 适合各种坐姿的最佳电竞椅：Razer Iskur V2 NewGen

The Razer Iskur V2 Newgen is comfortable and stylish. It definitely feels like a “gaming” chair more than an office chair, with thick foam padding and an aesthetic that serves more “Twitch streamer” than “white-collar employee.” It isn't as breathable as I want it to be, and while the phase-change material keeps me relatively cool, it does get a little warm during long gaming sessions, especially if my house is already hot.
Razer Iskur V2 Newgen 既舒适又时尚。它给人的感觉绝对更像是一把“电竞椅”而非办公椅，厚实的泡沫填充物和外观设计更符合“Twitch 主播”的风格，而非“白领员工”。它的透气性不如我预期的那么好，虽然相变材料能让我保持相对凉爽，但在长时间的游戏过程中，它确实会变得有些温热，尤其是在室内本身就很热的情况下。

What I really love about this chair is its adaptive lumbar support, which can be dialed in using a knob on the side, and its back tilt and wide seat. I can sit cross-legged or put up one foot with no issues. I wish the seat were a little deeper, though, and the pillow-like headrest is admittedly silly.
我真正喜欢这款椅子的地方在于它的自适应腰部支撑（可以通过侧面的旋钮调节）、后仰功能以及宽大的座垫。我可以毫无压力地盘腿坐或翘起一只脚。不过，我希望座垫能再深一点，而且那个枕头式的头枕确实有点滑稽。

### The Upgrade Pick: Herman Miller Embody Gaming Chair
### 进阶之选：Herman Miller Embody 电竞椅

The Embody is taking me a long time to get used to. After my week of testing, it feels a bit slippery and stiff. It even creaks here and there. I try not to read other testimonials about products before I form my own opinions, but for a chair this expensive, I thought I must have been missing something. Many people across the Internet say that this chair requires a break-in period, so I'm trusting the process.
我花了好长时间才适应 Embody。经过一周的测试，它感觉有点滑且硬，甚至偶尔还会发出吱吱声。在形成自己的观点之前，我尽量不去阅读其他关于产品的评价，但对于一把如此昂贵的椅子，我觉得我肯定错过了什么。互联网上有许多人表示这把椅子需要一个磨合期，所以我选择相信这个过程。

WIRED's own Julian Chokkattu tells me that it'll be worth my patience. And I can objectively see how it could be a great chair, with eye-catching aesthetics, memory foam cushions with copper infusion to keep you cool, and active support points to accommodate however you like to sit.
WIRED 的同事 Julian Chokkattu 告诉我，我的耐心是值得的。客观来看，它确实可能是一把伟大的椅子：引人注目的美学设计、注入铜离子的记忆棉坐垫以保持凉爽，以及能够适应你任何坐姿的主动支撑点。
