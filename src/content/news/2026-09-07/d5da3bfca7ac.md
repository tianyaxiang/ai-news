# A/I shuts down – Stay human
# A/I 关停——保持人性

A/I shuts down - Stay human
Sep 6, 2026

A/I 关停——保持人性
2026年9月6日

The day we discovered we had been designated a global terrorist organization, we promised that we would try to resist as long as this was possible, that we would not back down as long as we could see options to stand our ground. For years, we have proudly maintained and defended a free, privacy-friendly, autonomous and politically committed infrastructure. “Stay human” is not an empty phrase to us.

当我们发现自己被定性为全球恐怖组织的那一天，我们承诺过，只要有可能，我们就会尝试抵抗；只要还有坚持下去的选择，我们就绝不退缩。多年来，我们自豪地维护并捍卫着一个自由、保护隐私、自主且具有政治担当的基础设施。“保持人性”对我们来说绝非空话。

It means, above all, that we have a responsibility to protect our users, those who build the human networks we rely, and for the communities who flooded us with messages of support and solidarity.

它首先意味着，我们有责任保护我们的用户，保护那些构建了我们所依赖的人际网络的人们，以及那些向我们涌来支持与声援信息的社区。

We cannot engage in a fight or expose ourselves to manipulations that threaten the lives of these people and their loved ones, leaving them at the mercy of autocrats, fascists and agencies that take extra-legal courses of action. The possibility that our work may cause legal and financial consequences to those who are close to us - or even only have something to do with us - leaves us no choice.

我们无法参与一场斗争，或让自己暴露在威胁这些人及其亲人生命的操纵之下，让他们任由独裁者、法西斯分子以及采取法外手段的机构摆布。我们的工作可能会给那些与我们亲近的人——甚至仅仅是与我们有关联的人——带来法律和经济后果，这种可能性让我们别无选择。

A/I is shutting down. The Autistici/Inventati collective is shutting down and will soon discontinue all of the services we provide.

A/I 即将关停。Autistici/Inventati 集体即将解散，并将很快停止我们提供的所有服务。

There is no easy way to announce or explain this. Every day we stayed online after August 26, 2026, has been a victory, but now we are forced to stop.

宣布或解释这一切绝非易事。自2026年8月26日以来，我们在线的每一天都是一场胜利，但现在我们被迫停止了。

None of us holds heroic gestures and martyrs in high esteem, therefore we will demand no sacrifices. Not from us, not from anyone else. In this political climate, continuing to offer our services endangers our users and anyone who is part of our communities. In a world where allegations are disconnected from reality, we can only expect repression to be increasingly disproportionate. Under these circumstances, we are no longer able to maintain our original mission - offering secure and non-commercial digital tools.

我们中没有人推崇英雄主义姿态或殉道者，因此我们不会要求任何牺牲。无论是对我们自己，还是对其他人。在当前的政治气候下，继续提供我们的服务会危及我们的用户以及我们社区中的任何人。在一个指控与现实脱节的世界里，我们只能预见镇压会变得越来越不成比例。在这种情况下，我们已无法维持最初的使命——提供安全且非商业的数字工具。

These 25 years have been amazing. It’s been an “incredible ride”.

这25年是美妙的。这是一段“不可思议的旅程”。

Now turn off your PCs, get out of your homes, struggle, hug each other and keep on smiling. We are stopping, but the Resistance and ideas do not stop. Stay human.

现在，关掉你们的电脑，走出家门，去抗争，去拥抱彼此，并保持微笑。我们停下了，但抵抗和思想不会停止。保持人性。

We will soon send instructions on how to back up the content of your blogs, mailboxes and websites, along with more technical recommendations. But keep in mind that, just as the autistici.org domain was made unreachable without previous notice, in the next few days we might suffer similar issues that we may not be able to predict.

我们将很快发送关于如何备份博客、邮箱和网站内容的说明，以及更多的技术建议。但请记住，正如 autistici.org 域名在没有事先通知的情况下被切断访问一样，在接下来的几天里，我们可能会遭遇类似且无法预料的问题。
