# The Fairphone 6 Plus is the midrange phone we desperately needed
# Fairphone 6 Plus：我们迫切需要的中端手机

The Fairphone 6 Plus feels like an extremely average midrange Android phone and I couldn’t be more thrilled.
Fairphone 6 Plus 给人的感觉就是一款极其普通的中端安卓手机，而我对此感到无比兴奋。

The mission has always been admirable. Fairphone seeks out ethically sourced materials and offers a high degree of repairability for its devices. But the phones themselves required a lot of sacrifices, like putting up with underpowered processors and bad cameras, in order to embrace the sustainability mission. The latest generations of Fairphone have inched toward closing that gap, and the 6 Plus achieves two huge milestones: It feels very much like using a middling Android phone, and it’s actually on sale in the US.
Fairphone 的使命一直令人钦佩。他们致力于寻找符合道德标准的原材料，并为设备提供极高的可维修性。但为了践行可持续发展的使命，这些手机本身往往需要做出许多牺牲，比如忍受性能不足的处理器和糟糕的摄像头。最新几代的 Fairphone 正在逐步缩小这一差距，而 6 Plus 则实现了两个重要的里程碑：它用起来感觉就像一款普通的中端安卓手机，而且它终于在美国正式发售了。

The 6 Plus is a light update to last year’s Fairphone 6, which my colleague Dominic Preston reviewed and found very easy to recommend. The 6 Plus builds on it with a newer midrange Snapdragon chipset and a new color treatment. Fairphone says it’s cobalt; we know it around these parts as blurple. Otherwise, the phone is basically the same, and the replacement parts sold by Fairphone work interchangeably with both models.
6 Plus 是对去年 Fairphone 6 的小幅升级，我的同事 Dominic Preston 在评测后认为它非常值得推荐。6 Plus 在此基础上采用了更新的中端骁龙芯片组和全新的配色。Fairphone 称其为“钴蓝色”（cobalt），但在我们看来，这更像是一种蓝紫色（blurple）。除此之外，这款手机基本保持不变，Fairphone 出售的替换零件也适用于这两个型号。

But above all, this phone is just fine. It runs about as fast as your typical mid-tier Razr or A-series Pixel. It offers a pleasantly close-to-stock version of Android free of bloat or gimmicky AI features. It’s just a phone. And that’s great.
最重要的是，这款手机表现得恰到好处。它的运行速度与典型的中端 Razr 或 A 系列 Pixel 相当。它提供了一个令人愉悦的、接近原生的安卓系统，没有臃肿软件或花哨的 AI 功能。它就是一部纯粹的手机，这非常好。

I know, calling this a midrange phone when it’s priced at $649 might feel like a stretch. At best, it’s at the very top of the midrange class. But RAMageddon has done a real number on phone prices over the past year, and it’s not over yet. Given that the phone comes with 12GB of RAM, a five-year warranty, and up to six OS upgrades, that price feels more manageable.
我知道，定价 649 美元却称其为中端手机可能有些牵强。充其量，它处于中端机型的顶端。但过去一年里，“内存危机”（RAMageddon）对手机价格造成了严重影响，而且这种影响尚未结束。考虑到这款手机配备了 12GB 内存、五年保修以及多达六次的操作系统升级，这个价格感觉还是可以接受的。

Also, you don’t need to add AppleCare or whatever to that price. You are the AppleCare. The 6 Plus has 12 user-replaceable parts including the battery, screen, USB-C port, and all three cameras. The repairs are extremely beginner-friendly — if you can use the tiny included screwdriver and follow some easy-to-parse directions, you won’t have a problem.
此外，你不需要在这个价格之外额外购买 AppleCare 之类的服务。你自己就是 AppleCare。6 Plus 有 12 个可由用户更换的部件，包括电池、屏幕、USB-C 接口和全部三个摄像头。维修过程对新手非常友好——只要你会使用附带的小螺丝刀并遵循一些易于理解的说明，就不会有任何问题。

The price of these parts compared to the cost of repairing a Google Pixel or Samsung Galaxy phone range from a pretty good deal to a hell of a good deal. Without an extended warranty of some kind, a battery replacement would typically cost in the ballpark of $100 to $180; Fairphone charges $39.99 for a new battery. A screen repair ranges from $100 to nearly $300; a new display from Fairphone costs $89.99. Add in the entirely reasonable cost of 30 minutes of your own labor (if that), and the long-term value of this phone starts looking pretty good.
与维修 Google Pixel 或三星 Galaxy 手机的成本相比，这些零件的价格非常划算，甚至可以说是超值。在没有延长保修的情况下，更换电池通常需要 100 到 180 美元；而 Fairphone 的新电池仅需 39.99 美元。屏幕维修费用通常在 100 到近 300 美元之间；而 Fairphone 的新显示屏仅需 89.99 美元。再加上你投入的 30 分钟劳动成本（如果算上的话），这款手机的长期价值看起来相当不错。

Knowing how easy it is to replace parts of this phone as it ages actually required me to think a bit differently while I was using it. Battery life is just okay. I usually got through most days of light-to-moderate use with 60 percent left at bedtime. I did turn on every battery-draining feature I could think of, including the always-on display, higher screen refresh rate, and light mode. Those are all off by default, and probably for good reason. Normally I’d be pretty concerned about what that battery performance would look like in a few years.
知道这款手机随着老化更换零件是多么容易，这确实让我在使用时有了不同的思考。电池续航表现尚可。在轻度到中度使用的情况下，我通常在睡前还能剩下 60% 的电量。我确实开启了所有我能想到的耗电功能，包括息屏显示、更高的屏幕刷新率和浅色模式。这些功能默认都是关闭的，这可能是有原因的。通常情况下，我会非常担心几年后电池性能会变成什么样。

But there’s an easy fix: Just swap the battery yourself. No trip to the repair shop, no time away from your phone. Most phones would benefit from a new battery after a few years of continuous use anyway; at least with this one you can do it yourself and pay a lot less.
但这里有一个简单的解决方法：自己更换电池。无需跑维修店，也不用忍受没有手机的日子。大多数手机在连续使用几年后，更换新电池都会有很大改善；至少在这款手机上，你可以自己动手，而且花费要少得多。

The 6 Plus comes with way fewer compromises than early versions of the Fairphone did. The first iterations came with outdated designs, poor-quality cameras, and previous-gen displays. Things have steadily improved, but there are still some tradeoffs hanging around. The phone is rated IP55, which equates to mostly dust-resistant and mostly water-resistant, but not fully protected. Sealing up the phone completely and still offering easy repairability would be difficult, maybe impossible. That’s bad news, item one.
6 Plus 比早期的 Fairphone 版本做出的妥协要少得多。最初的版本设计过时、摄像头质量差、显示屏也是上一代的产品。情况一直在稳步改善，但仍存在一些权衡。这款手机的防护等级为 IP55，意味着它具备基本的防尘防水能力，但无法完全防护。要将手机完全密封同时又保持易于维修，这很困难，甚至可能是不可能的。这是第一个坏消息。

Item two: no wireless charging, and likewise no ring of magnets a la Qi2 or MagSafe. I’m growing partial to an easily removable PopSocket on my phone, so I missed having that option here. Moving along: The screen is thoroughly decent, though it doesn’t get quite as bright in daylight as some of the nicest ones out there. The speakers are tinny. And even after more than a week with this phone, I still haven’t learned the muscle memory of finding the wake button / fingerprint scanner without looking, since it sits basically flush with the side of the phone.
第二个坏消息：没有无线充电，也没有类似 Qi2 或 MagSafe 的磁吸环。我越来越喜欢在手机上使用易于拆卸的 PopSocket 气囊支架，所以在这里我感到有些遗憾。继续说：屏幕表现相当不错，尽管在日光下不如市面上一些顶级屏幕那么亮。扬声器声音单薄。即使使用了一周多，我仍然没有形成无需看就能找到唤醒键/指纹扫描仪的肌肉记忆，因为它基本上与手机侧面齐平。
