### Best Value: Everlane ReNew Transit Backpack
### 最佳性价比：Everlane ReNew Transit Backpack

I took this backpack on a weeklong work trip, where I bitched about it endlessly to everyone who would listen. However, I must fairly and objectively conclude that this bag offers tremendous value for the money. It's a light, attractive, rucksack-style backpack that's sleek and understated enough for work, and it's under $120.
我带着这款背包进行了一次为期一周的工作差旅，期间我向所有愿意听的人不停地抱怨它。然而，我必须公正客观地得出结论：这款包的性价比极高。它是一款轻便、美观的帆布背包，外观简洁低调，非常适合工作场合，且价格不到120美元。

The 27-liter capacity accommodated enough gear for a full day's worth of travel, from a scarf for the plane to a water bottle, snacks, and other in-flight accouterments. It has a slim profile and lies close to your back, so it's not hard to carry and won't swing into people on the subway. The pockets are very minimal—just an exterior laptop pocket, an interior tablet sleeve, an interior zip pocket, and two large catch-all pockets—but they work if you already put your belongings in pouches anyway.
其27升的容量足以容纳一整天旅行所需的装备，从飞机上用的围巾到水壶、零食以及其他飞行必备品。它外形纤薄且贴合背部，因此背负轻松，在地铁里也不会晃动撞到人。口袋设计非常极简——只有一个外部笔记本电脑隔层、一个内部平板电脑套、一个内部拉链袋和两个大号收纳袋——但如果你习惯将物品分类装在小袋子里，这些空间完全够用。

Certain design elements just felt cheap. I didn't like the curved zipper that gives you access to the main compartment—zippers that don't run straight are doomed to get stuck. One of the catch-all pockets is annoyingly located on the cover flap, where anything inside will flap and bang around. The luggage pass-through is a bare piece of webbing. However, it all works: it's light, affordable, and looks nice. The fabric is also water-resistant, so you can wear it in the rain! For most people, that's all that really matters. —Adrienne So
某些设计元素确实显得廉价。我不喜欢那个通往主隔层的弧形拉链——不平直的拉链注定会卡住。其中一个收纳袋的位置设计得很烦人，位于顶盖上，里面的东西会随着背包晃动而乱撞。行李箱固定带只是一条简单的织带。然而，它整体表现不错：轻便、实惠且外观好看。面料也具备防水功能，所以下雨天也能背！对大多数人来说，这才是最重要的。——Adrienne So

### Best Laptop Bag for Commuting: Thule Paramount Daypack Lite 20L
### 最佳通勤笔记本电脑包：Thule Paramount Daypack Lite 20L

Thule's newest addition to its already solid backpack lineup was made specifically for schlepping gear to and from work on a daily basis, and it shows. The silhouette is sleek and aerodynamic, making it suitable for bike commuting (there's even a loop on the front to attach a bike light), and it's just big enough for a laptop, several notebooks, and maybe a jacket without feeling too big or bulky. The water bottle holder on the side is stretchy for a variety of sizes.
Thule在其稳健的背包产品线中新增的这款产品，是专门为日常通勤携带装备而设计的，这一点显而易见。其轮廓简洁且符合空气动力学，非常适合骑行通勤（正面甚至有一个挂载自行车灯的环），容量刚好能装下笔记本电脑、几本笔记本和一件外套，而不会显得过大或臃肿。侧面的水壶袋具有弹性，可适配各种尺寸的水壶。

I also love the amount of organization inside for smaller things like pens and notebooks (the flap folds down for access to the interior, too, so you can access these things next to your desk), as well as the hidden yet easily accessible small pocket under the flap for wallet, keys, and phone. It's not the roomiest pack, so you're not carrying gym gear in this or taking it on an overnight trip, but if you're just going to and from the office with basics and a laptop, this is the most well-engineered option I've tried.
我也很喜欢它内部针对笔和笔记本等小物件的收纳设计（翻盖可以向下折叠以方便取用内部物品，这样你在办公桌旁就能轻松拿取），以及翻盖下隐藏但易于取用的钱包、钥匙和手机小口袋。它不是空间最大的背包，所以你无法用它装健身装备或进行过夜旅行，但如果你只是带着基本物品和笔记本电脑往返办公室，这是我试过设计最精良的选择。

### Best Laptop Bag for Travel: Aer Travel Pack 3
### 最佳旅行笔记本电脑包：Aer Travel Pack 3

Aer has a lock on the tech-minimalist aesthetic with its no-frills, black Cordura bags; this is the larger version of the popular City Pack. Though there is a newer version of the Travel Pack—the Travel Pack 4 is supposedly more streamlined—my husband and I have both been using this bag constantly for two years and have zero complaints, which is rare. It's also still in pristine condition, despite going on at least 10 work trips between the two of us.
Aer凭借其简约、黑色的Cordura面料包袋，牢牢占据了科技极简美学市场；这是广受欢迎的City Pack的大号版本。虽然Travel Pack已经有了更新版本（据说Travel Pack 4更精简），但我丈夫和我已经连续使用这款包两年了，没有任何抱怨，这非常难得。尽管我们两人加起来至少带着它出差了10次，但它依然保持着完好的状态。

Any backpack need you might have, the Travel Pack can probably cover it, from hiking to one-bag travel. The front zip pocket has built-in organization for all your pens, cords, and notebooks, and the main compartment opens clamshell-style for packing cubes or gym gear. It covers all the bases without feeling too fiddly. However, note it's not the lightest pack and would likely be frustratingly bulky for commuters.
无论你有什么样的背包需求，Travel Pack几乎都能满足，从徒步旅行到“一包走天下”的旅行。前置拉链袋内置了收纳空间，可存放所有的笔、数据线和笔记本，主隔层采用翻盖式开启，方便放入收纳袋或健身装备。它功能全面且不会显得过于繁琐。不过请注意，它不是最轻的背包，对于通勤者来说可能会显得过于笨重。
