# Apple’s four-pack of second-gen AirTags is $20 off
# 苹果第二代 AirTag 四件套优惠 20 美元

Apple’s four-pack of second-generation AirTags is down to $79 (originally $99) at Amazon and at Target, which is the bundle’s lowest price yet. You can get an AirTag for $24 right now piecemeal, but getting four together as a set is a way to save even more. Why pay $96 for four when you can save $17? It felt like we had to wait longer for the cost to fall this much for first-gen AirTags, so it’s a little surprising to see the newer, better model available for less.

苹果第二代 AirTag 四件套目前在亚马逊和塔吉特（Target）的售价降至 79 美元（原价 99 美元），这是该套装迄今为止的最低价格。虽然你现在可以以 24 美元的单价购买单个 AirTag，但购买四件套能节省更多。既然能省下 17 美元，何必花 96 美元买四个呢？回想第一代 AirTag，我们似乎等了很久价格才降到这个程度，因此看到更新、更好的型号以更低的价格出售，确实令人有些惊喜。

Apple’s latest AirTags features an upgraded ultra-wideband chip for more precise location tracking, a noticeably louder speaker, and an expanded Bluetooth range.

苹果最新的 AirTag 配备了升级后的超宽带芯片，可实现更精确的定位追踪，扬声器声音明显更大，蓝牙覆盖范围也更广。

The second-generation AirTag looks identical to the first-generation AirTag. All of its upgrades are inside, and primarily include spec improvements to let you more easily find it. It includes boosted speaker volume, longer range, and overall better tracking capabilities. My colleague Victoria Song had a much easier time tracking the latest AirTag on different floors compared to the older model.

第二代 AirTag 的外观与第一代完全相同。所有的升级都在内部，主要包括规格上的改进，让你能更轻松地找到它。这些改进包括增强的扬声器音量、更长的传输距离以及整体更好的追踪能力。我的同事 Victoria Song 表示，与旧型号相比，追踪最新款 AirTag 在不同楼层间的表现要轻松得多。
