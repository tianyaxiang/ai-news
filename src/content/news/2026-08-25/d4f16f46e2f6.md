# De-Googled GrapheneOS is coming to Motorola’s foldables next year
# 去谷歌化的 GrapheneOS 明年将登陆摩托罗拉折叠屏手机

GrapheneOS, an open source version of Android that prioritizes security and privacy, has detailed its plans for supporting Motorola smartphones. Official support is set to arrive next year, starting with traditional flagships, before rolling out to Motorola’s foldable phones and perhaps cheaper models, eventually.

GrapheneOS 是一款优先考虑安全和隐私的开源版 Android 系统，该项目近日详细介绍了其支持摩托罗拉智能手机的计划。官方支持预计将于明年到来，首先从传统的旗舰机型开始，随后逐步扩展到摩托罗拉的折叠屏手机，并最终可能覆盖更便宜的机型。

In a Mastodon thread, the GrapheneOS Foundation announced that it will initially support a Motorola slab flagship phone, but will later support both Razr Fold and Razr Ultra foldables — the latter of which would be the first flip phone to officially run GrapheneOS.

GrapheneOS 基金会在 Mastodon 的帖子中宣布，他们将首先支持一款摩托罗拉直板旗舰手机，随后将支持 Razr Fold 和 Razr Ultra 折叠屏系列——后者将成为首款正式运行 GrapheneOS 的翻盖式折叠屏手机。

Graphene has always had stringent security standards when it comes to hardware. The organization said that this year’s Motorola Signature and Razr handsets are “very close to meeting our requirements,” but lack mature support for hardware memory tagging and secure element integration. The organization expects the next generation of Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite chips to meet its standards, which is why it’s targeting Motorola’s 2027 phones for initial support.

Graphene 在硬件方面一直有着严格的安全标准。该组织表示，今年的摩托罗拉 Signature 和 Razr 系列手机“非常接近我们的要求”，但缺乏对硬件内存标记（memory tagging）和安全元件集成的成熟支持。该组织预计下一代高通骁龙 8 Elite 芯片将能满足其标准，这也是他们将摩托罗拉 2027 年的机型作为首批支持目标的原因。

Earlier this month Graphene confirmed that its initial Motorola devices would be flagships that would “be higher end hardware than Pixels at a higher price.” More affordable devices are on the roadmap, but would require better security features from Qualcomm for its cheaper chips, and for Motorola to invest in seven years of software support for those phones — this year’s $499.99 Moto G Stylus only promised two major OS upgrades and three years of security updates.

本月初，Graphene 确认其首批支持的摩托罗拉设备将是旗舰机型，这些机型“硬件规格比 Pixel 更高，价格也更贵”。更实惠的设备也在路线图中，但这需要高通为其廉价芯片提供更好的安全功能，并要求摩托罗拉为这些手机投入七年的软件支持——今年售价 499.99 美元的 Moto G Stylus 仅承诺了两次重大操作系统升级和三年的安全更新。

Motorola first announced its collaboration with Graphene in March, saying that the two organizations would “work to strengthen smartphone security.” Until now, Graphene has only officially supported Google’s Pixel phones. That support is ongoing, and the organization confirmed it has plans to add support for the Pixel 11 series in the future. However, it noted that changes to the Android Open Source Project’s support for Pixel phones mean they are now “harder to properly support than many other devices.” In contrast, it said “Motorola is doing a large portion of the work of porting GrapheneOS to their devices.”

摩托罗拉于今年 3 月首次宣布与 Graphene 合作，称双方将“共同致力于加强智能手机的安全性”。到目前为止，Graphene 仅正式支持谷歌的 Pixel 手机。该支持工作仍在继续，且该组织确认计划在未来增加对 Pixel 11 系列的支持。不过，他们指出，Android 开源项目（AOSP）对 Pixel 手机支持方式的改变，意味着这些设备现在“比许多其他设备更难进行妥善适配”。相比之下，他们表示“摩托罗拉正在承担将 GrapheneOS 移植到其设备上的大部分工作”。
