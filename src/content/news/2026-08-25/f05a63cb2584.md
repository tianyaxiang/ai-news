# Can an LLM Forget the Right Things?
# 大语言模型能学会“适时遗忘”吗？

Large Language Models Can an LLM Forget the Right Things? A hand-written CUDA inference runtime for Vision-Language-Action robots that decides what to remember, what to forget, and when it's simply too late to think. Anubhab Banerjee Aug 24, 2026 23 min read Share Every reasoning cycle in this runtime has exactly 33 milliseconds to finish — or it doesn’t start at all.

大语言模型能学会“适时遗忘”吗？这是一个为视觉-语言-动作（VLA）机器人编写的手写 CUDA 推理运行时，它能决定什么该记住、什么该遗忘，以及何时该果断放弃思考。作者：Anubhab Banerjee，2026年8月24日，阅读时长23分钟。该运行时中的每一个推理周期必须在 33 毫秒内完成，否则根本不会启动。

### Key takeaways
### 核心要点

The problem: point a normal chat-oriented LLM runtime at a live robot camera and three things break at once: (i) VRAM overflows from a vision stream that never ends, (ii) control-loop deadlines get missed silently, and (iii) a 60Hz camera easily outpaces a much slower reasoning step.

问题所在：如果将普通的聊天型 LLM 运行时直接接入实时机器人摄像头，会同时出现三个问题：(i) 永不停歇的视觉流导致显存（VRAM）溢出；(ii) 控制循环的截止时间被悄无声息地错过；(iii) 60Hz 的摄像头处理速度远超缓慢的推理步骤。

The fix: an admission controller that estimates a reasoning chunk’s cost with an online exponential moving average and refuses to even start it rather than gamble on the 33ms deadline; a KV cache that evicts the most redundant frame, by cosine similarity, instead of automatically selecting the oldest one; a lock-free double buffer so perception never blocks on a stale backlog.

解决方案：引入一个准入控制器，通过在线指数移动平均值估算推理任务的成本，若无法保证在 33ms 内完成，则拒绝启动任务，而非盲目尝试；KV 缓存不再自动剔除最旧的帧，而是根据余弦相似度剔除最冗余的帧；采用无锁双缓冲机制，确保感知过程不会因陈旧的积压数据而阻塞。

The engine room: the entire transformer for Qwen2.5-Coder-1.5B-Instruct: RMSNorm, RoPE, grouped-query attention, SwiGLU — is hand-written in CUDA, no cuBLAS, no libtorch; validated against a real HuggingFace forward pass at ≥0.999 cosine similarity.

核心引擎：Qwen2.5-Coder-1.5B-Instruct 的整个 Transformer 架构（包括 RMSNorm、RoPE、分组查询注意力机制、SwiGLU）均使用 CUDA 手写，未使用 cuBLAS 或 libtorch；经验证，其与 HuggingFace 的实际前向传播结果余弦相似度 ≥0.999。

The honest part: this post is an architecture, not a benchmark. Built on a single cloud NVIDIA Hopper GPU (sm_90), the 8GB VRAM ceiling is a modeled budget in the code, not a number measured on a Jetson or any other physical edge board.

坦诚地说：本文介绍的是一种架构，而非基准测试。该系统构建于单块云端 NVIDIA Hopper GPU (sm_90) 之上，代码中设定的 8GB 显存上限是模型设计的预算，而非在 Jetson 或其他物理边缘设备上实测的数据。

TL;DR: Point a normal LLM server at a live robot camera and it will happily flood the VRAM, blow through the control-loop deadlines, and choke on a camera that’s faster than its own brain. vla-edge-backend is a hand-written CUDA runtime that refuses reasoning it can’t finish in 33ms, evicts KV cache by ‘meaning’ instead of just age, and never lets perception block on the reasoning.

简而言之：将普通的 LLM 服务器接入机器人摄像头，它会迅速填满显存、错过控制循环的截止时间，并因摄像头速度快于其“大脑”处理速度而陷入瘫痪。vla-edge-backend 是一个手写的 CUDA 运行时，它会拒绝无法在 33ms 内完成的推理任务，根据“语义”而非时间顺序剔除 KV 缓存，并确保感知过程永远不会被推理任务阻塞。

### 1. The clock that never stops
### 1. 永不停歇的时钟

Somewhere inside this project, a camera produces a fresh frame every 16.7 milliseconds. It doesn’t care whether anything downstream is ready. It doesn’t pause, it doesn’t retry, and it definitely doesn’t file a support ticket. It just keeps going, 60 times a second, forever, because that’s what a camera bolted to a robot does.

在这个项目中，摄像头每 16.7 毫秒就会产生一帧新画面。它不在乎下游是否准备就绪。它不会暂停，不会重试，更不会提交工单。它只是每秒 60 次、永不停歇地运行，因为这就是安装在机器人上的摄像头的工作方式。

Meanwhile, a small language model wired to a vision encoder has a much harder job: grab the freshest frame it can get, decide what to do about it, and finish that decision inside a 33-millisecond window. Not “usually”, not “on a good day”. Thirty-three milliseconds, for reference, is faster than just the closing half of a single human blink, eyelids take about 50 to 100 milliseconds to snap shut. The robot doesn’t get to blink and think about it.

与此同时，连接到视觉编码器的小型语言模型面临着更艰巨的任务：获取最新的帧，决定如何处理，并在 33 毫秒内完成决策。不是“通常情况下”，也不是“在状态好的时候”。作为参考，33 毫秒比人类眨眼闭合过程的一半还要快（人类眨眼闭合大约需要 50 到 100 毫秒）。机器人没有时间眨眼思考。

That number isn’t a vibe. It’s a compile-time constant sitting at the top of the codebase, in include/physical_ai/common.hpp:
`constexpr double DEADLINE_MS = 33.0;`
`constexpr double SAFETY_MARGIN_MS = 2.0;`

这个数字并非凭感觉设定，而是代码库顶部的编译时常量，位于 `include/physical_ai/common.hpp` 中：
`constexpr double DEADLINE_MS = 33.0;`
`constexpr double SAFETY_MARGIN_MS = 2.0;`

Every other piece of this runtime exists to either hit that number or fail loudly and on purpose instead of quietly missing it — which, if you’ve ever worked with a deadline-blind system, is already a personality upgrade.

该运行时的其他所有部分，要么是为了达成这个目标，要么是为了在无法达成时主动且明确地报错，而不是悄无声息地错过——如果你曾处理过对截止时间“视而不见”的系统，就会明白这是一种巨大的进步。
