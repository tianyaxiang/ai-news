# Netflix reportedly considers opening its app to other streamers
# 据报道，Netflix 考虑向其他流媒体平台开放其应用程序

Netflix executives have considered making third-party streaming services available within its app, according to a report from The New York Times. The recent discussions reportedly centered around bringing Peacock and Fox One to Netflix, though it’s unclear whether the streaming giant would sell subscriptions to the other services or add their content to its app.

据《纽约时报》报道，Netflix 高管已考虑在其应用程序内提供第三方流媒体服务。据悉，近期的讨论主要围绕将 Peacock 和 Fox One 引入 Netflix 展开，但目前尚不清楚这家流媒体巨头是会销售其他服务的订阅，还是直接将其内容整合进自己的应用中。

While Amazon’s Prime Video and Roku have long sold subscriptions to rival services, the move would be a major change for Netflix, which has only joined bundles with other streamers. YouTube — Netflix’s biggest rival — will also include access to Peacock as part of its Premium subscription next year.

虽然亚马逊的 Prime Video 和 Roku 长期以来一直在销售竞争对手服务的订阅，但对于 Netflix 而言，此举将是一个重大转变，因为该公司此前仅参与过与其他流媒体平台的捆绑销售。Netflix 最大的竞争对手 YouTube 也将在明年把 Peacock 的访问权限纳入其 Premium 订阅服务中。

Netflix currently has no “imminent deal” planned, The New York Times reports.

据《纽约时报》报道，Netflix 目前尚无“迫在眉睫的交易”计划。

But Netflix has already begun dabbling in third-party streaming partnerships in France, where it folded live channels and streaming content from local broadcaster TF1 into its app in June. When asked about this partnership during a July earnings call, Netflix co-CEO Greg Peters said the company is seeing “promising” results. “If we see additional deals that similarly serve our members that work for our partner, that work for us, we’ll certainly consider them,” Peters said. Netflix declined to comment.

但 Netflix 已经在法国开始尝试第三方流媒体合作。今年 6 月，该公司将当地广播公司 TF1 的直播频道和流媒体内容整合到了其应用程序中。在 7 月的财报电话会议上，当被问及此次合作时，Netflix 联席首席执行官格雷格·彼得斯（Greg Peters）表示，公司看到了“令人鼓舞”的结果。“如果我们看到其他类似的交易，既能服务于我们的会员，又能让合作伙伴和我们双方受益，我们肯定会考虑，”彼得斯说道。Netflix 对此拒绝置评。
