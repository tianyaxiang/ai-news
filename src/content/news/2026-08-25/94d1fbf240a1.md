I've tested quite a few headsets, and I have yet to find one I like more than this one. The battery lasts for around 35 hours for me. The mic sounds clear. The ear cups are supremely comfortable, and the whole headset is lightweight. My only gripe is that the pop cover on the mic seems to come off easily, but that also might just be user error. (I shake my head a lot while gaming due to my teammates—not at all my own actions.)

我测试过不少耳机，但目前还没发现比这款更让我满意的。对我来说，它的续航时间约为 35 小时。麦克风音质清晰，耳罩极其舒适，整个耳机也非常轻便。我唯一的抱怨是麦克风上的防喷罩似乎很容易掉，但这可能只是我个人的操作问题。（因为队友的原因，我在玩游戏时经常摇头——绝对不是我自己的问题。）
