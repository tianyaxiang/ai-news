### Kindle Cases and Covers / Kindle 保护壳与保护套

An easy must-have for any Kindle reader is a case or cover to protect your e-reader. Here are some of my favorites that I've tried on my various Kindles.
对于任何 Kindle 用户来说，保护壳或保护套是必不可少的。以下是我在不同 Kindle 设备上试用过的一些心头好。

**CoBak Clear Case ($19 $8)**
This clear case from CoBak is nice and thick and has reinforced corners—perfect for if you're a chronic dropper.
这款来自 CoBak 的透明保护壳厚实且边角经过加固，非常适合那些经常不小心摔设备的人。

**Fintie Shell Kindle Case ($29 $20)**
If you want something tough to protect your Kindle that isn't bulky, I like this sleek case. It has a hard exterior shell with a soft interior to protect your Kindle Paperwhite or Colorsoft.
如果你想要一款既坚固又能保护 Kindle 又不显笨重的产品，我推荐这款轻薄的保护壳。它拥有坚硬的外壳和柔软的内衬，能很好地保护你的 Kindle Paperwhite 或 Colorsoft。

**PopSockets Kindle Paperwhite Case ($40)**
PopSockets has a nice range of Kindle cases that add a MagSafe ring for its grips. The Dragon Sterling is my current favorite design, but PopSockets has a ton to choose from.
PopSockets 提供了一系列带有 MagSafe 磁吸环的 Kindle 保护壳，方便安装其气囊支架。Dragon Sterling（龙之纯银）是我目前最喜欢的款式，但 PopSockets 还有海量选择。

**Owkey Magnetic Kindle Case ($30 $26)**
If you like the idea of a case that includes a MagSafe ring but want the ring toward the middle of the device, or just want a more minimalist design, this simple case works perfectly.
如果你喜欢带 MagSafe 磁吸环的保护壳，但希望磁吸环位于设备中部，或者只是想要更极简的设计，这款简单的保护壳非常合适。

### Kindle Straps and Grips / Kindle 绑带与握把

Looking for an easier way to hold your Kindle? Add these straps and grips right onto the Kindle or the case.
想找一种更轻松的握持 Kindle 的方式吗？将这些绑带和握把直接加装在 Kindle 或保护壳上吧。

**Strapsicle E-Reader Hand Strap ($20)**
These silicone straps are super simple to use: Just pull them over opposite corners and you're done. No sticky adhesive, no magnets, just simple straps.
这些硅胶绑带使用起来超级简单：只需将其套在对角线上即可。无需粘胶，无需磁铁，就是简单的绑带。

**CoBak Secure Hand Strap ($5)**
This strap is similar to the Strapsicle, but it has a softer feel and can stretch farther. It's both affordable and super comfortable.
这款绑带与 Strapsicle 类似，但手感更柔软，拉伸性更好。它既实惠又非常舒适。

**PopSockets Nightshade PopGrip ($30 $26)**
PopSockets makes a huge range of PopGrips that can attach with either a MagSafe adapter or an adhesive. My go-to is the Nightshade PopGrip. The colors are like an oil slick kaleidoscope.
PopSockets 生产了大量可以通过 MagSafe 适配器或背胶安装的气囊支架。我最常用的是 Nightshade 系列，它的颜色就像油膜万花筒一样绚丽。

This holder works for Kindles and tablets alike. The clamp base lets you attach it to tables and furniture, and it's easy to position in front of or even above you if you want to lie down and read.
这款支架适用于 Kindle 和平板电脑。夹式底座可以让你将其固定在桌子或家具上，如果你想躺着阅读，它很容易调整到你面前甚至头顶上方的位置。
