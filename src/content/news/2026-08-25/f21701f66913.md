# GM vehicles under federal scrutiny after hundreds of reports
# 通用汽车因数百起报告面临联邦审查

The federal government is expanding a safety investigation into a number of vehicles manufactured by General Motors after receiving hundreds of reports of brake failure. The problem affects more than 1.1 million cars, including many of GM’s electric vehicles, as well as ones it has built for Honda and Acura.
在收到数百起刹车失灵的报告后，美国联邦政府正在扩大对通用汽车（General Motors）旗下多款车型的安全调查。该问题影响了超过 110 万辆汽车，其中包括通用汽车旗下的多款电动汽车，以及为本田（Honda）和讴歌（Acura）代工生产的车型。

The problem is with the brake-by-wire system (which GM calls eBoost), which can suffer a spindle fracture while driving. GM says that in the event of that happening, the car should still retain a number of critical safety features—antilock brakes, traction control, and stability control—at least until it comes to a complete stop, at which point they become inoperable.
问题出在线控刹车系统（通用汽车称之为 eBoost）上，该系统在行驶过程中可能会发生主轴断裂。通用汽车表示，如果发生这种情况，车辆仍应保留多项关键安全功能——如防抱死制动系统（ABS）、牵引力控制系统和稳定性控制系统——至少在车辆完全停止前有效，但在停车后这些功能将失效。

But NHTSA’s ODI says that it has received many reports that contradict this behavior and allege that eBoost failures are followed by an immediate loss of braking, which obviously poses a safety risk. Indeed, NHTSA has received 227 incident reports, a number that grows to 745 once you include the hundreds of occurrences reported to GM. Twenty-two cases resulted in crashes, and while six people have been injured in five crashes, thankfully there have been no fatal incidents.
然而，美国国家公路交通安全管理局（NHTSA）下属的缺陷调查办公室（ODI）表示，他们收到了许多与此说法相矛盾的报告，称 eBoost 失效后会导致刹车立即失灵，这显然构成了安全风险。事实上，NHTSA 已收到 227 起事故报告，如果加上通用汽车收到的数百起相关案例，这一数字已增至 745 起。其中 22 起案例导致了碰撞事故，虽然有 5 起事故造成 6 人受伤，但所幸目前尚未出现死亡事故。

NHTSA says that the following vehicles are equipped with eBoost: “2023-2026 MY Cadillac Lyriq, Chevrolet Colorado, and GMC Canyon; 2024-2026 MY Buick Enclave, Envision; Chevrolet Blazer EV, Equinox EV, Traverse; Cruise Origin; and GMC Acadia; 2025-2026 MY Cadillac Celestiq and Optiq. ODI is also aware of 2024-2026 MY Acura ZDX and 2024-2026 MY Honda Prologue vehicles that were built with this system.”
NHTSA 指出，以下车型配备了 eBoost 系统：“2023-2026 款凯迪拉克 Lyriq、雪佛兰 Colorado 和 GMC Canyon；2024-2026 款别克昂科雷（Enclave）、昂科威（Envision）；雪佛兰 Blazer EV、Equinox EV、Traverse；Cruise Origin；以及 GMC Acadia；2025-2026 款凯迪拉克 Celestiq 和 Optiq。ODI 同时获悉，2024-2026 款讴歌 ZDX 和 2024-2026 款本田 Prologue 也采用了该系统。”
