### Best Overall: XGIMI Titan Noir Max
### 最佳综合表现：极米 (XGIMI) Titan Noir Max

Picking the best projector was not easy, especially since there's so much competition. The Xgimi Titan Noir Max won me over because of the dynamic, self-adjusting iris. As you watch a movie or show, the projector adjusts to allow more light in (for bright scenes) or closes to reduce light (for dark scenes). For example, during a nighttime sequence in the movie Hoppers on Disney+, the iris will close to make the blacks and grays look dark and richly detailed. That feature alone sets the Titan Noir Max apart.

挑选最佳投影仪并非易事，尤其是在竞争如此激烈的情况下。极米 Titan Noir Max 凭借其动态自适应光圈赢得了我的青睐。当你观看电影或节目时，投影仪会自动调节以允许更多光线进入（针对明亮场景），或关闭光圈以减少光线（针对暗部场景）。例如，在 Disney+ 电影《Hoppers》的夜间片段中，光圈会自动收缩，使黑色和灰色看起来深邃且细节丰富。仅凭这一功能，Titan Noir Max 就足以脱颖而出。

### Best for Gaming: Hisense XR10 4K Smart Projector
### 最佳游戏投影仪：海信 (Hisense) XR10 4K 智能投影仪

I’ll never forget the first time I played the game 007 First Light projected on a wall in my house using the Hisense XR10. An ocean scene looked bright blue and realistic, thanks to the excellent color processing on this projector. I felt very little lag using the low-latency mode, and the built-in speakers (the best I've tested on a projector) never felt lacking.

我永远不会忘记第一次使用海信 XR10 将游戏《007：第一道光》(007 First Light) 投射到墙上时的情景。得益于该投影仪出色的色彩处理能力，海洋场景看起来明亮、湛蓝且逼真。在使用低延迟模式时，我几乎感觉不到任何延迟，而且其内置扬声器（我测试过的投影仪中表现最好的）音质也从未让我失望。

### Best for Movies: Leica Cine Play 1
### 最佳电影投影仪：徕卡 (Leica) Cine Play 1

Leica has a rich history making cameras, and this Leica Cine Play 1 projector is a showcase of that superior craftsmanship. The Leica Summicron lens renders ultra-realistic video with extreme clarity and exceptional color accuracy. I watched Hoppers in a windowless testing room, and the furry creatures all looked stunningly realistic—you could even see individual strands of fur cleanly.

徕卡在相机制造方面拥有悠久的历史，而这款徕卡 Cine Play 1 投影仪正是其卓越工艺的体现。徕卡 Summicron 镜头以极高的清晰度和出色的色彩准确度呈现出超逼真的视频画面。我在一个无窗的测试室里观看了《Hoppers》，那些毛茸茸的生物看起来极其真实——你甚至能清晰地看到每一根毛发。
