# The Best Multiuse Air Purifier for Home in 2026: BlueAir, Rabbit Air, Dreame
# 2026年家用多功能空气净化器推荐：BlueAir、Rabbit Air、Dreame

If you're tight on space or just want a more aesthetically pleasing machine, the best air purifier for home depends on your needs. If you live in a small apartment like me, you may need to have a multifunctional air purifier for a practical reason: space. These compact, dual-purpose machines are useful if you are tight on square footage or don't want to shell out the money for two separate appliances. Or maybe you're aesthetically driven and want something that looks cool in your space rather than a clunky, large air purifier that can be an eyesore.
如果你空间有限，或者只是想要一台外观更美观的机器，那么最适合家用的空气净化器取决于你的具体需求。如果你像我一样住在小公寓里，出于空间考虑，你可能需要一台多功能空气净化器。这些紧凑的双用途机器非常实用，特别是在你居住面积狭小，或者不想花钱购买两台独立设备的情况下。又或者，你是一个注重审美的人，想要一件看起来很酷的家居装饰，而不是一台笨重、碍眼的空气净化器。

Regardless of the reason, these multiuse air purifiers look good, save space, and combine appliances into a single, energy-efficient device.
无论出于何种原因，这些多功能空气净化器不仅外观精美、节省空间，还将多种家电功能整合进了一台节能设备中。

### Best Air Purifier That's Also a Fan: Dreo Air Purifier Tower Fan MC710S
### 最佳空气净化风扇：Dreo Air Purifier Tower Fan MC710S

This Dreo offers 99.97 percent HEPA filtration and an air quality sensor. You can control it with a remote, the Dreo app, Google Home, and Amazon Alexa. Best of all, the fan and purifier can operate independently, in case you need just one or the other or want to extend the filter lifespan (replacements run around $40). The 12-month warranty isn’t the greatest, but the device has been trouble-free for nearly two years in our long-term tests. If you're in the market for a fan and purifier in one, this model is definitely worth a look. —Kat Merck
这款 Dreo 提供 99.97% 的 HEPA 过滤效果，并配有空气质量传感器。你可以通过遥控器、Dreo App、Google Home 和 Amazon Alexa 对其进行控制。最棒的是，风扇和净化器可以独立运行，以备你只需要其中一项功能，或者想要延长滤网寿命（更换滤网费用约为 40 美元）。虽然 12 个月的保修期不算长，但在我们的长期测试中，该设备在近两年内运行良好，没有出现任何问题。如果你正在寻找一款集风扇与净化器于一体的产品，这款机型绝对值得考虑。——Kat Merck

### Best Air Purifier That's Also a Heater: Blueair ComfortPure 3-in-1 Air Purifier
### 最佳空气净化暖风机：Blueair ComfortPure 3-in-1 Air Purifier

Blueair’s small-room model is a little under 2 feet tall and has built-in heating and cooling. Blueair’s app is easy to use, and I preferred it rather than the actual control panel. The panel did not always illuminate when pressed, making the buttons difficult to use. The app also has more options, like controlling the oscillation speed and angle. Blueair uses both mechanical HEPA filtration and electrostatic technology to capture pollutants in the air. Its CARB certification is listed as electronic, unlike HEPA-only air purifiers that are listed as mechanical.
Blueair 的这款小空间机型高度不到 2 英尺，内置了加热和冷却功能。Blueair 的 App 非常好用，我个人比使用机身上的控制面板更喜欢用 App。控制面板在按下时并不总是会亮起，导致按键操作有些困难。App 则提供了更多选项，例如控制摆动速度和角度。Blueair 同时使用机械 HEPA 过滤和静电技术来捕捉空气中的污染物。其 CARB 认证被列为电子类，这与仅使用 HEPA 过滤的机械类空气净化器有所不同。

The ComfortPure 3-in-1 also has an air quality index indicator light that responds to the purifier's built-in sensor to measure particulate matter (PM) 2.5. The easy-to-miss sliver of light glows blue for excellent, green for good, yellow for moderate, orange for polluted, and red for very polluted. It has a control panel lock and filter change indicator light. I also appreciate the cloth handle that makes moving the Blueair easy. —Lisa Wood Shapiro
ComfortPure 3-in-1 还配有一个空气质量指数指示灯，它会根据内置传感器测量到的颗粒物 (PM) 2.5 数值进行反馈。那条容易被忽略的细长灯带会根据空气质量变色：蓝色代表优，绿色代表良，黄色代表中度污染，橙色代表污染，红色代表重度污染。它还具备控制面板锁定功能和滤网更换指示灯。我个人很喜欢它的布艺提手，这让移动 Blueair 变得非常轻松。——Lisa Wood Shapiro

### 最佳空气净化吸尘器：EyeVac Air 2-in-1 Automatic Dustpan & HEPA Air Purifier

The Eye-Vac Air combines an air purifier with a touchless vacuum. Sweep debris up to the stationary unit, and it'll suck it up. It has two sets of HEPA and activated carbon filters pulling in air from both sides of the purifier. Its filter placement lets you position the Eye-Vac flush against the wall, which is especially useful in kitchen settings. The air filter can clean the air effectively, achieving five air exchanges per hour in a 120-square-foot space. While the large control panel is easy to read, the air purifier and vacuum offer only two options: manual or automatic. Indicator lights show when the bagless vacuum canister is full and when the air purifier filters need replacing. There are three fan speeds, but there are no color-coded indicator lights that correspond to air quality. Instead, three blue bars indicate the fan setting. —Lisa Wood Shapiro
Eye-Vac Air 将空气净化器与感应式吸尘器结合在一起。只需将灰尘扫到这个固定装置前，它就会自动吸入。它配有两组 HEPA 和活性炭滤网，从净化器两侧吸入空气。其滤网布局设计允许你将 Eye-Vac 紧贴墙壁放置，这在厨房环境中尤为实用。该空气净化器能有效清洁空气，在 120 平方英尺的空间内每小时可完成五次空气交换。虽然大尺寸控制面板易于读取，但空气净化和吸尘功能仅提供手动或自动两种模式。指示灯会显示无尘袋吸尘盒是否已满，以及何时需要更换空气净化滤网。它有三种风速，但没有对应空气质量的彩色指示灯，而是通过三条蓝色横杠来显示风速档位。——Lisa Wood Shapiro
