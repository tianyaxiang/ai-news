### Kids outlearn AI—and we still don’t know why
### 孩子们的学习能力超越 AI——而我们至今仍不知原因

Teaching a computer to use human language requires an inhuman amount of data. An LLM can easily churn through a hundred thousand times more words than a person will experience in the process of mastering their mother tongue. This yawning divide between children and machines is called the data efficiency gap. It raises a tantalizing question for cognitive scientists and a challenge for the architects of AI models: how can children still outperform the most linguistically sophisticated machines ever built?
教会计算机使用人类语言需要海量的数据。一个大语言模型（LLM）处理的词汇量，可以轻松达到一个人掌握母语过程中所接触词汇量的十万倍。这种孩子与机器之间巨大的鸿沟被称为“数据效率差距”。这对认知科学家提出了一个诱人的问题，也对 AI 模型架构师提出了挑战：为什么孩子们依然能胜过迄今为止最复杂的语言机器？

Kids show that it could be possible to learn more with less. Far less. By reverse-engineering the way they learn, scientists hope to create more data-efficient AI models—and perhaps settle enduring questions about language and children’s developing minds. Here’s how researchers are trying to close the data gap. —Elise Cutts
孩子们证明了用更少的数据学习更多知识是可能的。少得多。通过对他们的学习方式进行逆向工程，科学家们希望创造出数据效率更高的 AI 模型，并或许能解决关于语言和儿童心智发展的长期疑问。以下是研究人员如何尝试缩小这一数据差距的方法。——Elise Cutts

### Job titles of the future: space travel agent
### 未来的职位：太空旅行代理人

As co-owner of a luxury lifestyle firm, Roman Chiporukha has long turned wild vacation dreams into reality. In 2018, he got a phone call that would open up a new frontier: Axiom Space wanted to find citizen explorers willing to pay $50 million each to join the first fully private mission to the International Space Station (ISS), slated for April 2022. This showed Chiporukha that the sky was no longer the limit; it was the market. He successfully signed up a private astronaut and then launched SpaceVIP in 2021 to offer celestial experiences that mix culture, science, and purpose. Find out what it takes to become the Expedia of the cosmos. —Linda Childers
作为一家奢华生活方式公司的共同所有者，Roman Chiporukha 长期以来一直致力于将疯狂的度假梦想变为现实。2018 年，他接到的一通电话开启了一个新领域：Axiom Space 想要寻找愿意支付 5000 万美元加入首个完全私人国际空间站（ISS）任务的平民探险家，该任务定于 2022 年 4 月进行。这让 Chiporukha 意识到，天空不再是极限，而是市场。他成功签下了一名私人宇航员，并于 2021 年创办了 SpaceVIP，提供融合文化、科学和使命感的太空体验。了解如何成为宇宙中的 Expedia。——Linda Childers
