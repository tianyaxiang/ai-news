# ESPN streaming plans are getting more expensive
# ESPN 流媒体订阅计划即将涨价

Starting September 17th, a subscription to ESPN Unlimited will cost $31.99 per month.
从 9 月 17 日起，ESPN Unlimited 订阅服务的月费将调整为 31.99 美元。

ESPN is hiking the price of its subscription on September 17th, a change that will also impact its bundles with Disney Plus. In a support page spotted earlier by Sports Media Watch, ESPN says its ad-supported Select membership will cost $13.99 instead of $12.99 / month, while its Unlimited plan will rise to $31.99 from $29.99 / month.
ESPN 将于 9 月 17 日上调其订阅价格，此次调整也将影响其与 Disney Plus 的捆绑套餐。据 Sports Media Watch 此前发现的支持页面显示，ESPN 的含广告 Select 会员月费将从 12.99 美元涨至 13.99 美元，而 Unlimited 计划的月费将从 29.99 美元涨至 31.99 美元。

The annual ESPN Select plan is also going to $139.99 from $129.99 / year, and the annual Unlimited tier is increasing to $319.99 from $299.99 / year. Meanwhile, Disney Plus bundles with ESPN Select are going up in price as well. Here’s a rundown of the changes:
ESPN Select 年度计划的价格也将从每年 129.99 美元涨至 139.99 美元，年度 Unlimited 计划则从每年 299.99 美元涨至 319.99 美元。与此同时，包含 ESPN Select 的 Disney Plus 捆绑套餐价格也在上涨。以下是具体变动清单：

*   Disney Plus (with ads), Hulu (with ads), and ESPN Select: $21.99 / month, up from $19.99 / month.
    Disney Plus（含广告）、Hulu（含广告）和 ESPN Select：月费从 19.99 美元涨至 21.99 美元。
*   Disney Plus (ad-free), Hulu (ad-free), ESPN Select: $32.99 / month, up from $29.99.
    Disney Plus（无广告）、Hulu（无广告）和 ESPN Select：月费从 29.99 美元涨至 32.99 美元。
*   Legacy Disney Plus (ad-free), Hulu (ad-free), and ESPN Select: $27.99 / month, up from $24.99 / month.
    旧版 Disney Plus（无广告）、Hulu（无广告）和 ESPN Select：月费从 24.99 美元涨至 27.99 美元。

Disney Plus bundles with ESPN Unlimited aren’t affected. Last week, Axios reported that about 80 percent of Unlimited subscribers signed up through the bundle with Disney Plus and Hulu.
包含 ESPN Unlimited 的 Disney Plus 捆绑套餐不受影响。Axios 上周报道称，约 80% 的 Unlimited 订阅用户是通过 Disney Plus 和 Hulu 的捆绑套餐进行订阅的。

Though Disney last raised prices across its bundles last year, this is the first increase for ESPN, which rolled out its standalone streaming subscription last August. As part of the launch, ESPN updated its mobile app with a TikTok-like feed of videos and a personalized version of SportsCenter. ESPN also launched multiview in its app on major smart TV platforms.
尽管迪士尼去年曾上调过捆绑套餐的价格，但这是 ESPN 自去年 8 月推出独立流媒体订阅服务以来的首次涨价。作为发布的一部分，ESPN 更新了其移动应用程序，增加了类似 TikTok 的视频流和个性化版本的 SportsCenter。此外，ESPN 还在各大智能电视平台的应用程序中推出了多视角观看功能。
