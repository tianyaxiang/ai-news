# Raspberry Pi shares its official tutorial for making a cyberdeck
# Raspberry Pi 发布了制作“赛博甲板”（Cyberdeck）的官方教程

Raspberry Pi’s head of social, Ashley Whittaker, acknowledged the cyberdeck trend today, saying “we haven’t been able to get away from cyberdecks this year.” Tiny portable computers made out of things like purses, jewelry boxes, and other thrifted or recycled parts have gone viral on TikTok and other social media platforms over the past year, and Raspberry Pi now has its own guide to building one of the homemade computers.

Raspberry Pi 社交媒体负责人 Ashley Whittaker 今天回应了这一“赛博甲板”趋势，称“今年我们简直无法避开赛博甲板的话题”。过去一年里，由手提包、珠宝盒以及其他二手或回收零件制成的微型便携式电脑在 TikTok 等社交媒体平台上走红，而 Raspberry Pi 现在也推出了自己的自制电脑构建指南。

Raspberry Pi has also increased its prices multiple times this year due to rising costs for RAM and other components, which could be another reason to support this growing market of active and would-be hobbyists. The 16GB version of the Raspberry Pi 5 included in the company’s official cyberdeck guide got a $100 price hike in April, nearly as much as the board’s original $120 price.

由于内存及其他组件成本上涨，Raspberry Pi 今年已多次上调价格，这或许也是支持这一日益增长的爱好者市场的另一个原因。公司官方赛博甲板指南中使用的 16GB 版本 Raspberry Pi 5 在四月份涨价了 100 美元，涨幅几乎相当于该主板最初 120 美元的售价。

Raspberry Pi’s cyberdeck guide includes a parts list and detailed assembly instructions, but sticks to a fairly simple design using a plain black Pelican-style case. As content creators are proving, though, you can make a cyberdeck out of just about anything. Annike Tan, whose cyberdecks have attracted millions of views on TikTok, continues to upgrade her “mermaid” purse cyberdeck with new design details and even added a USB hub.

Raspberry Pi 的赛博甲板指南包含了零件清单和详细的组装说明，但其设计相对简洁，使用的是一个普通的黑色派力肯（Pelican）风格外壳。不过，正如内容创作者们所证明的那样，你可以用几乎任何东西来制作赛博甲板。Annike Tan 在 TikTok 上凭借其赛博甲板作品获得了数百万次观看，她不断为自己的“美人鱼”手提包赛博甲板添加新的设计细节，甚至还加装了一个 USB 集线器。
