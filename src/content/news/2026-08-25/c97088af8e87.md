# 10 Positions for Enterprise RAG That Mainstream Tutorials Get Wrong
# 企业级 RAG 的 10 个立场：主流教程错在哪里

Large Language Model 10 Positions for Enterprise RAG That Mainstream Tutorials Get Wrong Enterprise Document Intelligence [Vol.1 #M3] – The ten positions the series argues from, and the map of every article that argues them.
大型语言模型：企业级 RAG 的 10 个立场——主流教程错在哪里。企业文档智能 [第1卷 #M3] —— 本系列文章所持的十个立场，以及每篇文章的论证导图。

This series argues a specific way to build enterprise RAG, and it disagrees with a lot of standard practice along the way. RAG is not machine learning; embeddings are not magic; a chunk-size sweep optimizes the wrong thing; the answer schema matters more than the model. None of these are neutral, and each one changes what you build.
本系列文章主张一种构建企业级 RAG 的特定方式，并在此过程中对许多标准做法提出了异议。RAG 不是机器学习；嵌入（Embeddings）并非魔法；对分块大小（chunk-size）进行穷举优化是方向错误；答案模式（answer schema）比模型本身更重要。这些观点绝非中立，每一个都会改变你构建系统的方式。

The standard RAG tutorial reads the same everywhere. Chunk the documents, push the chunks into a vector store, embed the question, retrieve top-k by cosine similarity, optionally rerank, send the hits to an LLM. Vendor decks, framework quickstarts, and conference talks all repeat it. The pattern works on hello-world examples (a Wikipedia paragraph, a short PDF, a curated FAQ) and starts wobbling the day a real enterprise document hits it.
标准的 RAG 教程在任何地方看起来都一样：对文档进行分块，将分块推入向量数据库，对问题进行嵌入，通过余弦相似度检索 Top-k，可选地进行重排序（rerank），最后将命中结果发送给大模型。厂商演示文稿、框架快速入门和会议演讲都在重复这一套。这种模式在“Hello World”级别的示例（如维基百科段落、简短 PDF、精选 FAQ）上运行良好，但一旦遇到真实的商业文档，它就开始摇摇欲坠。

### 1. Vector stores are a fallback, not a foundation
### 1. 向量数据库是后备方案，而非基础

The default tutorial wires a vector store as the entry point of the pipeline. Everything goes through cosine similarity, then a reranker patches what cosine got wrong. The series inverts this. Structure-first retrieval (the document’s declared TOC, the corpus index, expert keywords) handles the bulk of real questions on enterprise corpora. Embeddings come in as a safety net for the residual cases (paraphrase, cross-language, internal acronyms), not as the default first stage.
默认教程将向量数据库作为流水线的入口。所有内容都经过余弦相似度计算，然后由重排序器来修补余弦相似度出错的地方。本系列则颠倒了这一逻辑。结构优先的检索（文档声明的目录、语料库索引、专家关键词）能够处理企业语料库中绝大多数的实际问题。嵌入仅作为处理剩余情况（如改写、跨语言、内部缩写）的安全网，而不是默认的第一阶段。

### 2. Expert dictionaries beat better embedding models
### 2. 专家词典优于更强的嵌入模型

The synonym problem is the one embeddings are supposed to solve. Premium matches cost. Termination matches cancellation. Franchise matches deductible. In practice, a concept_keywords_df table maintained by domain experts (the satellite table built in Article 6) solves the synonym problem more reliably than an off-the-shelf embedding model, and usually a fine-tuned one, for the vocabulary the experts know.
同义词问题本应由嵌入模型来解决。例如，“Premium”（保费）对应“cost”（成本），“Termination”（终止）对应“cancellation”（取消），“Franchise”（免赔额）对应“deductible”（免赔额）。但在实践中，由领域专家维护的 `concept_keywords_df` 表（第 6 篇文章中构建的辅助表）在处理专家熟悉的词汇时，比现成的嵌入模型（通常也包括微调后的模型）更可靠地解决了同义词问题。

### 3. Rerankers are a secondary tool, not a primary stage
### 3. 重排序器是辅助工具，而非主要阶段

Cross-encoder rerankers have a legitimate place in the literature. They sit between cheap embedding similarity (high recall, fuzzy precision) and expensive LLM judgment (precise but slow).
交叉编码器（Cross-encoder）重排序器在文献中占有一席之地。它们位于廉价的嵌入相似度（高召回率、模糊精度）和昂贵的大模型判断（精确但缓慢）之间。
