# Apply now to host a Side Event at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026
# 立即申请成为 TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 的周边活动主办方

We’re getting close to TechCrunch Disrupt 2026! In less than two months, more than 10,000 innovators, founders, investors, and industry leaders will gather in San Francisco. You can meet up with that community the week of TechCrunch Disrupt, which runs from October 13-15. With our Disrupt Side Events program, you can bring people together anytime from October 10-16 for a custom event that gets promoted to Disrupt’s attendees. But time is running out — our deadline to apply for Side Events is September 4, so act now!

TechCrunch Disrupt 2026 即将到来！不到两个月后，超过 10,000 名创新者、创始人、投资者和行业领袖将齐聚旧金山。在 10 月 13 日至 15 日 TechCrunch Disrupt 大会举办期间，您将有机会与这一群体会面。通过我们的“Disrupt 周边活动”（Side Events）计划，您可以在 10 月 10 日至 16 日期间举办定制活动，并将活动推广给所有 Disrupt 的参会者。但时间紧迫——周边活动的申请截止日期为 9 月 4 日，请立即行动！

What are the benefits of a Side Event? Hosting a Side Event puts you in control of how your community meets up, placing your brand front and center with the people who matter most. Whether you’re a VC looking to spark conversations, a company ready to showcase breakthrough innovation, or a community builder with something to say, this is your moment to: Engage directly with thousands of the sharpest minds in tech. Build authentic relationships with potential partners, investors, and collaborators. Own your narrative and showcase your expertise on your terms. Generate buzz that extends far beyond the event itself. Approved Side Events are all promoted to Disrupt attendees and the rest of our audiences through the official Side Events calendar and marketing channels.

举办周边活动有什么好处？举办周边活动让您可以掌控社区聚会的方式，将您的品牌置于最重要人群的视线中心。无论您是希望激发讨论的风险投资人、准备展示突破性创新的公司，还是有话要说的社区建设者，现在都是您的绝佳机会：与数千名科技界最敏锐的头脑直接互动；与潜在合作伙伴、投资者和合作者建立真实的联系；掌控您的叙事方式，按自己的方式展示专业知识；产生远超活动本身的轰动效应。所有获批的周边活动都将通过官方周边活动日历和营销渠道，向 Disrupt 参会者及我们的其他受众进行推广。

What Side Events can you host? Anything that aligns with your vision is likely to be approved. Everything from industry roundtables and VC office hours to deep-dive workshops to intimate dinners and casual networking happy hours have succeeded at past Disrupt events. The format is yours to design — we just ask that you bring the energy and make sure it’s open to both Disrupt attendees and the broader public. We recommend hosting events that take place in the evenings during Disrupt from October 13-15, as there’s less competition and more post-conference excitement to fuel meaningful conversations with undivided audiences.

您可以举办什么样的周边活动？任何符合您愿景的活动都有可能获得批准。从行业圆桌会议、VC 办公时间（Office Hours）、深度研讨会，到私密晚宴和休闲社交酒会，这些形式在往届 Disrupt 活动中都取得了成功。活动形式由您设计——我们只要求您充满活力，并确保活动同时向 Disrupt 参会者和广大公众开放。我们建议在 10 月 13 日至 15 日 Disrupt 大会期间的晚上举办活动，因为此时竞争较小，且会议后的兴奋感更有助于在专注的受众中推动有意义的对话。

How can I apply for a Side Event? You can look at the application form here, but it’s helpful to have a few things locked in before starting the application process: Have a venue selected in the Bay Area — this is all about in-person experiences. Have a title, description, and venue prepared for the event. Double-check the Side Events Host Guide and the Side Event Host T&Cs. If you have additional questions, we’re here to help! Just email our Side Events team and we’ll follow up to help. We prioritize events that provide meaningful value to founders, investors, and the startup community, but aside from that, you know your community and ideal audience the best! There’s zero cost to apply and participate, so let’s hear your ideas.

如何申请举办周边活动？您可以在此处查看申请表，但在开始申请流程前，最好先确定以下几点：在湾区选定场地——这关乎线下的面对面体验；准备好活动的标题、描述和场地信息；仔细阅读《周边活动主办方指南》和《周边活动主办方条款与细则》。如果您还有其他问题，我们随时为您提供帮助！只需发送电子邮件给我们的周边活动团队，我们将跟进协助。我们优先考虑能为创始人、投资者和创业社区提供实质价值的活动，除此之外，您最了解您的社区和理想受众！申请和参与均无需任何费用，期待听到您的想法。
