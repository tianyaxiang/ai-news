### AI didn’t replace my design process. It changed how fast I could execute it.
### AI 没有取代我的设计流程，它改变的是我的执行速度。

AI didn’t replace my design process. It changed how fast I could execute it. I’ve been experimenting with AI tools in my UI/UX workflow, and one thing has become clear: AI is great at generating ideas.
AI 没有取代我的设计流程，它改变的是我的执行速度。我一直在 UI/UX 工作流中尝试各种 AI 工具，有一点变得非常明确：AI 非常擅长生成创意。

But knowing what to build, what to remove, and why a user should care is still the designer’s job. My workflow has started looking something like: PRD → User flows → Wireframes → AI-assisted exploration → UI design → Prototype → Testing.
但决定构建什么、删减什么，以及用户为何要在意这些，依然是设计师的工作。我的工作流现在看起来大致是这样的：产品需求文档 (PRD) → 用户流程 → 线框图 → AI 辅助探索 → UI 设计 → 原型制作 → 测试。

The biggest win isn’t “AI designed this for me.” It’s moving from an idea to something tangible much faster. The tools are getting better every month.
最大的收获并不是“AI 为我完成了设计”，而是从想法到具体产出的速度大幅提升了。这些工具每个月都在变得更好。

The interesting part is figuring out how designers can actually use them without losing the thinking behind the work. I’m still experimenting. What’s your experience been like using AI in your design or development workflow?
有趣的部分在于，我们要探索设计师如何在不丢失作品背后思考逻辑的前提下，真正利用好这些工具。我仍在尝试中。你在设计或开发工作流中使用 AI 的体验如何？
