# Waymo pulls over, calls cops on riders with a ghost gun
# Waymo 自动驾驶出租车靠边停车，举报乘客携带“幽灵枪”

Your robotaxi can and will snitch on you.
你的自动驾驶出租车不仅能举报你，而且真的会这么做。

Two people were arrested in San Francisco while riding around in a Waymo robotaxi after the cab pulled over and called the cops on them. The riders were juveniles in possession of a loaded AR-style ghost gun and were taken to a juvenile hall. While the police report did not specify who was operating the autonomous car, Waymo later confirmed it was one of its vehicles to the Los Angeles Times.
两名乘客在旧金山乘坐 Waymo 自动驾驶出租车时被捕，原因是车辆在行驶途中靠边停车并报了警。这两名乘客均为未成年人，且随身携带了一把已上膛的 AR 风格“幽灵枪”（无序列号自制枪支），随后被送往少管所。虽然警方报告中未明确说明该自动驾驶车辆的运营商，但 Waymo 随后向《洛杉矶时报》证实，该车确实属于其车队。

A spokesperson told the LA Times that a “violation of our terms of service involving a firearm” was detected. At which point the car pulled over and alerted the authorities.
Waymo 发言人告诉《洛杉矶时报》，系统检测到了“违反我们服务条款中关于枪支使用的规定”。随后，车辆便自动靠边停车并通知了相关部门。

Earlier this year, a Waymo faked having mechanical problems to pull over when two passengers were drinking alcohol and firing a toy gun out of the back of the car. In that instance, the riders Waymo snitched on were also juveniles. So let this be a lesson to teens: if you’re gonna do dumb stuff, don’t do it in the back of a robotaxi.
今年早些时候，当两名乘客在车内饮酒并向窗外发射玩具枪时，一辆 Waymo 自动驾驶出租车曾伪装成发生机械故障而靠边停车。在那起事件中，被 Waymo 举报的乘客同样是未成年人。所以，这给青少年们上了一课：如果你想做蠢事，千万别在自动驾驶出租车的后座上做。
