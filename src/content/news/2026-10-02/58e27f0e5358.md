# Marvel releases one last VisionQuest trailer
# 漫威发布《幻视探寻》（VisionQuest）最终预告片

With its premiere just two weeks away, Marvel Studios has dropped one last trailer for the limited series, *VisionQuest*, which will complete the trilogy begun with *WandaVision* and *Agatha All Along*. (Spoilers for *WandaVision* and *Agatha All Along* below.)
距离首播仅剩两周，漫威影业发布了限定剧集《幻视探寻》（VisionQuest）的最后一部预告片。该剧将为始于《旺达幻视》和《阿加莎：无所不在》的三部曲画上句号。（以下内容包含《旺达幻视》和《阿加莎：无所不在》的剧透。）

As previously reported, Marvel’s streaming TV portfolio has kind of been all over the place: a handful of hits, a few misses, and a bunch of middling offerings. One standout was *WandaVision*, followed by an excellent sequel (of sorts), *Agatha All Along*. *VisionQuest* will complete the trilogy.
正如之前报道的那样，漫威的流媒体电视作品表现参差不齐：有几部佳作，也有几部失利之作，还有不少平庸之作。其中，《旺达幻视》表现突出，随后是其（某种意义上的）优秀续作《阿加莎：无所不在》。而《幻视探寻》将完成这一三部曲。

Vision (Paul Bettany) died in *Infinity War*, but *WandaVision* resurrected an all-white version whose memories were restored in the finale. We finally get to learn what happened afterward.
幻视（保罗·贝坦尼饰）在《复仇者联盟3：无限战争》中牺牲，但《旺达幻视》复活了一个全白色的幻视，并在大结局中恢复了他的记忆。我们终于可以了解在那之后发生了什么。

Per the official premise: Vision, rebooted and having escaped from those who sought to weaponize him, has been in hiding. Searching for new meaning, he consults the AI personas embedded in his programming, including F.R.I.D.A.Y., E.D.I.T.H., J.A.R.V.I.S., and the infamous Ultron. His discreet existence ends when a bounty placed on his head thrusts him on the run with Thomas Shepard, a mysterious boy who may be Vision’s son, reincarnate. As Vision evades capture, he must confront his nature, resist Ultron’s influence, and unravel the enigma that is his young companion if he’s to survive.
根据官方剧情简介：幻视在重启并逃离那些试图将他武器化的人后，一直处于隐居状态。为了寻找新的意义，他咨询了植入其程序中的人工智能人格，包括星期五（F.R.I.D.A.Y.）、伊迪丝（E.D.I.T.H.）、贾维斯（J.A.R.V.I.S.）以及臭名昭著的奥创（Ultron）。当有人对他发出悬赏令，迫使他与托马斯·谢泼德（Thomas Shepard）——一个可能是幻视转世儿子的神秘男孩——一起逃亡时，他低调的生活宣告结束。在躲避追捕的过程中，幻视必须直面自己的本质，抵御奥创的影响，并解开这位年轻同伴身上的谜团，才能生存下去。

Bettany is returning as Vision, along with James Spader as Ultron. *Agatha All Along* gave us a new Billy Maximoff, whose soul took over the dying body of another teen as Wanda’s spell unraveled at the end of *WandaVision*. Agatha helped Billy track down the soul of his twin, Tommy (aka Speed), and placed him into the body of a teen boy who drowned, Thomas Shepherd, both played here by Ruaridh Mollica.
贝坦尼回归饰演幻视，詹姆斯·斯派德回归饰演奥创。《阿加莎：无所不在》为我们带来了新的比利·马克西莫夫（Billy Maximoff），在《旺达幻视》结尾旺达的咒语失效时，他的灵魂占据了另一名垂死少年的身体。阿加莎帮助比利找到了他双胞胎兄弟汤米（Tommy，又名“快银”）的灵魂，并将他安置在了一名溺水少年的身体里，即托马斯·谢泼德（Thomas Shepherd），这两个角色均由鲁阿里·莫利卡（Ruaridh Mollica）饰演。

The cast also includes Todd Stashwick as a bounty hunter named Paradin; T’Nia Miller as a vengeful robot named Jocasta; Emily Hampshire as EDITH, aka Tony Stark’s augmented reality security and defense system; Orla Brady as FRIDAY, Stark’s replacement AI system for JARVIS; and James D’Arcy as JARVIS. Henry Lewis and Jonathan Sayer play DUME and U, respectively, two of Stark’s robot workshop assistants. In addition, Lauren Morais plays Lisa Molinari; Diane Morgan plays an associate of Paladin; Cristian Lavin plays a mercenary; and Mary McDonnell will appear in an as-yet-undisclosed role. And Faran Tahir will be back as Raza, leader of the Ten Rings, who kidnapped Tony Stark in 2008’s *Iron Man*.
演员阵容还包括：托德·斯塔什维克（Todd Stashwick）饰演赏金猎人帕拉丁（Paradin）；蒂尼亚·米勒（T’Nia Miller）饰演复仇机器人乔卡斯塔（Jocasta）；艾米莉·汉普希尔（Emily Hampshire）饰演伊迪丝（EDITH），即托尼·斯塔克的增强现实安全防御系统；奥拉·布莱迪（Orla Brady）饰演星期五（FRIDAY），斯塔克在贾维斯之后的替代AI系统；詹姆斯·达西（James D’Arcy）饰演贾维斯。亨利·刘易斯（Henry Lewis）和乔纳森·塞耶（Jonathan Sayer）分别饰演DUME和U，这是斯塔克机器人工作室的两个助手。此外，劳伦·莫莱斯（Lauren Morais）饰演丽莎·莫利纳里（Lisa Molinari）；黛安·摩根（Diane Morgan）饰演帕拉丁的同伙；克里斯蒂安·拉文（Cristian Lavin）饰演雇佣兵；玛丽·麦克唐纳（Mary McDonnell）将出演一个尚未公开的角色。法兰·塔希尔（Faran Tahir）也将回归饰演拉扎（Raza），即2008年《钢铁侠》中绑架托尼·斯塔克的“十戒帮”首领。

Marvel unveiled the first trailer at D23 in August, introducing Vision’s existential crisis and the primary characters inhabiting his interior mindscape. This new trailer opens with Vision (in human form) entering a reception area and announcing that he’s there “for the trap.” A gunfight ensues before we return to Vision’s interior world. “[The original] Vision was a hero,” Vision says. “An Avenger. I have his memories, but not his virtue.”
漫威在八月的D23博览会上发布了首支预告片，介绍了幻视的生存危机以及居住在他内心思维空间的主要角色。这支新预告片以幻视（人类形态）进入接待区并宣布他是“为了陷阱而来”开场。随后爆发了枪战，镜头又回到了幻视的内心世界。“（最初的）幻视是一位英雄，”幻视说道，“一位复仇者。我拥有他的记忆，却没有他的美德。”

The “impossibly cruel and callous” Ultron seeks to exploit Vision’s confusion, using Tommy as leverage. “Free me, and save your son,” Ultron demands, all so he can “finish what I started all those years ago.” (Fans will recall that in *Age of Ultron*, the newly sentient AI wanted to wipe out humanity in order to save Earth. We doubt his intentions have changed much.)
“极其残忍冷酷”的奥创试图利用幻视的困惑，以汤米作为筹码。“释放我，救你的儿子，”奥创要求道，这一切都是为了让他能“完成我多年前开始的事情”。（粉丝们会记得，在《复仇者联盟2：奥创纪元》中，这个新觉醒的AI想要消灭人类以拯救地球。我们怀疑他的意图并没有太大改变。）

*VisionQuest* premieres October 14 on Disney+.
《幻视探寻》将于10月14日在Disney+首播。
