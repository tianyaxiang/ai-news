An AI “mind-reading” tool can reconstruct what you’re looking at based on a brain scan. A new AI tool can guess what you’re looking at just by analyzing your brain scans—and recreate that image with remarkable precision. It can go the other way too, and predict a person’s brain activity based on what they’re looking at. The researchers hope the “mindreading” tool could reveal more about how the brain works. It could also help locked-in people communicate and, incredibly, maybe even allow scientists to recreate the content of dreams. But other scientists warn that a similar approach could reveal people’s inner thoughts and mental imagery, potentially without their consent. Here’s how AI is getting closer to reading images from the brain. —Jessica Hamzelou
一款AI“读心”工具可以通过脑部扫描重建你所看到的画面。一种新的AI工具仅通过分析脑部扫描结果，就能猜出你正在看什么，并以惊人的精度重现该图像。它也可以反向操作，根据人所看到的画面来预测其大脑活动。研究人员希望这种“读心”工具能揭示更多关于大脑运作的奥秘。它还可以帮助“闭锁综合征”患者进行交流，甚至令人难以置信地让科学家重现梦境内容。但其他科学家警告称，类似的方法可能会在未经本人同意的情况下泄露人们的内心想法和心理意象。以下是AI如何一步步实现从大脑中读取图像的。——Jessica Hamzelou

How smaller, distributed batteries could help the grid. Large, utility-scale battery installations are coming online quickly around the world. But there’s been pushback from communities, especially population-dense ones like NYC, because of concerns about relatively rare but high-profile battery fires. Faced with those obstacles, some startups are getting creative, finding ways to use relatively small batteries in unexpected places, from induction stovetops to food carts. By deploying these more consumer-focused batteries, the companies are helping people directly, cutting pollution or power bills. These units are small on their own, but could add up to a lot of capacity. And as batteries get cheaper, they could find their way into more corners of our lives. Here’s why batteries could help ease pressure on the grid. —Casey Crownhart
小型分布式电池如何助力电网。大型公用事业规模的电池设施正在全球范围内迅速上线。但由于对相对罕见但备受关注的电池火灾的担忧，社区（尤其是像纽约市这样人口稠密的地区）对此产生了抵触情绪。面对这些障碍，一些初创公司正在发挥创意，寻找在意想不到的地方使用小型电池的方法，从电磁炉到餐车。通过部署这些更贴近消费者的电池，这些公司正在直接帮助人们减少污染或降低电费。这些装置本身虽小，但累积起来却能提供巨大的容量。随着电池成本的降低，它们可能会进入我们生活的更多角落。以下是电池为何能帮助缓解电网压力的原因。——Casey Crownhart
