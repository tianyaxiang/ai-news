### The founders Disrupt empowers
### Disrupt 赋能的创始人类型

Disrupt works a bit differently, depending on where you are in your founding journey, with different tracks you can follow to make the most of the three days:
根据你所处的创业阶段，Disrupt 的参与方式略有不同，你可以通过以下路径充分利用这三天的时间：

**Pre-seed and idea stages**
**种子轮前及构思阶段**
You’re here to pressure-test your thesis, meet your first angel or pre-seed investors, and figure out who else is building in your space. Prioritize the Builders Stage, the Startup Battlefield semifinalist pitches, and the Expo Hall, along with roundtables tied to your industry and conversations you’ll want to learn more from.
你来到这里是为了压力测试你的创业构想，结识你的第一批天使投资人或种子轮投资人，并了解谁在你的领域内进行开发。请优先关注“建设者舞台”（Builders Stage）、“创业战场”半决赛路演以及展厅，同时参加与你行业相关的圆桌会议，并参与你感兴趣的深度对话。

**Early revenue or actively raising**
**早期营收或积极融资阶段**
You’re here for capital and validation above all else, and your time is valuable. Prioritize our investor-matchmaking tools and the connections that come with your Founder Pass, including the networking tools in our app. You also get access to the Deal Flow Café, where we foster one-on-one conversations between founders and investors. And when your schedule isn’t packed with dealmaking, learn from VC perspectives and fundraising-focused sessions at the Builders Stage.
你来到这里主要是为了寻求资本和市场验证，你的时间非常宝贵。请优先使用我们的投资人匹配工具，并利用“创始人通行证”（Founder Pass）带来的连接资源，包括我们 App 中的社交工具。你还可以进入“交易流咖啡馆”（Deal Flow Café），我们在那里促进创始人与投资人之间的一对一交流。当你的日程表没有排满交易洽谈时，可以在“建设者舞台”学习 VC 的观点和融资相关的专题会议。

**Scaling, hiring, in search of partnerships**
**扩张、招聘及寻求合作伙伴阶段**
You’re here in search of talent, strategic partners, and market visibility. Those same networking tools and opportunities can help you meet potential hires and partners and deepen those connections or make new ones. Disrupt’s Side Events aren’t to be missed — there’s one for every industry, interest, or post-event vibe you could be looking to find.
你来到这里是为了寻找人才、战略合作伙伴和市场曝光度。同样的社交工具和机会可以帮助你结识潜在的雇员和合作伙伴，加深现有联系或建立新的人脉。Disrupt 的周边活动不容错过——无论你关注哪个行业、兴趣点或想要什么样的社交氛围，总有一款适合你。

### Startup Battlefield: The most iconic pitch competition
### 创业战场：最具标志性的路演竞赛

The Startup Battlefield winner’s reveal packs the Disrupt stage and is each year’s capstone event. But it’s the lead-up to that $100,000 check and the deluge of attention the winner receives that’s the most valuable for founders attending Disrupt. The 200 startups chosen on August 20 to compete go through a rigorous prep process and get the opportunity to pitch onstage in front of top-tier VCs and the Disrupt crowds. You’ll also get to watch those pitches, take notes, learn from the peaks and valleys, and potentially meet the teams behind the ideas and innovations.
“创业战场”的获胜者揭晓仪式总是让 Disrupt 的舞台座无虚席，也是每年大会的压轴戏。但对于参会的创始人来说，通往那 10 万美元奖金的过程以及获胜者所获得的巨大关注度，才是最有价值的部分。8 月 20 日选出的 200 家参赛初创公司将经历严格的准备过程，并有机会在顶级 VC 和 Disrupt 观众面前登台路演。你也可以观看这些路演，做笔记，从他们的成功与挫折中学习，并有机会结识这些创意和创新背后的团队。

### The founder-focused programming
### 聚焦创始人的议程设置

Disrupt 2026 spans several stages, each built around a different founder need:
Disrupt 2026 设有多个舞台，每个舞台都围绕创始人的不同需求而设：

*   **Disrupt Stage:** The biggest conversations in technology, featuring the founders, CEOs, and industry leaders defining what’s next. These are the conversations you’ll be talking about long after Disrupt ends.
    **Disrupt 舞台：** 科技界最重磅的对话，汇聚了定义未来的创始人、CEO 和行业领袖。这些对话将让你在 Disrupt 结束后依然回味无穷。
*   **Builders Stage:** The tactical playbook for building a startup. From fundraising and hiring to GTM, product, and scaling, every session is designed to give founders practical advice they can put to work immediately.
    **建设者舞台：** 创业实战手册。从融资、招聘到市场进入策略（GTM）、产品和扩张，每一场会议都旨在为创始人提供可以立即付诸实践的实用建议。
*   **AI Stage:** AI isn’t the future — it’s the present. Learn how the fastest-growing startups are building, deploying, and monetizing AI, with practical lessons on agents, models, infrastructure.
    **AI 舞台：** AI 不是未来，而是现在。了解增长最快的初创公司如何构建、部署和实现 AI 变现，获取关于智能体（Agents）、模型和基础设施的实战经验。
