# Trump’s ‘Morally Binding’ AI ‘Accord,’ the Rise of AI Agents, and Extremists on the Ballot
# 特朗普的“道德约束”人工智能“协议”、AI 代理的兴起以及选票上的极端分子

This week on Uncanny Valley, Brian Barrett, Zoë Schiffer, and Leah Feiger discuss the “morally binding” AI accord that tech executives signed following a meeting with Donald Trump. They’ve agreed that AI labs will regulate themselves—should work out great. Plus, the team digs into OpenAI’s Developer Day, where CEO Sam Altman introduced AI agents for normies, otherwise known as Dots; how AI agents are coming to workplaces near you; and a quick rundown of the most extremist candidates running for office.

在本周的《恐怖谷》（Uncanny Valley）节目中，Brian Barrett、Zoë Schiffer 和 Leah Feiger 讨论了科技高管在与唐纳德·特朗普会面后签署的“道德约束”人工智能协议。他们一致同意由人工智能实验室进行自我监管——这听起来效果应该会很棒。此外，团队还深入探讨了 OpenAI 的开发者日，首席执行官山姆·奥特曼（Sam Altman）在会上为普通用户推出了 AI 代理（即 Dots）；探讨了 AI 代理如何进入你的工作场所；并简要盘点了正在竞选公职的最极端候选人。
