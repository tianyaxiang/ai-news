# 吐槽 Android 开发者验证计划 (Fuck Android Developer Verification Program)

**ivx 👾💀@0xcrypto**
Previously, I started a new Play Store account because I wanted to make video games for Android. But I never got time to finish my game. As a result, my personal play store account was closed by @Google because of inactivity. Now, I developed a new app, let's publish it on play store. But I cannot restore my old account. The message now says "To start publishing apps on Google Play, create a new account." but I haven't found any method to create a new play console account yet either.

之前，我注册了一个新的 Play Store 账号，因为我想为 Android 开发游戏。但我一直没时间完成我的游戏。结果，我的个人 Play Store 账号因为长期不活跃被 @Google 给注销了。现在，我开发了一个新应用，想把它发布到 Play Store 上。但我无法恢复旧账号。现在的提示信息显示“要开始在 Google Play 上发布应用，请创建一个新账号”，但我甚至找不到任何创建新 Play Console 账号的方法。

Okay no problem, I don't need a play store account just to publish apps, I could maybe ship an APK via @fdroidorg . But wait, what's this? To distribute my APK in the future, I must sign up for Android Developer Verification Program? Okay it should be just a sign up then. No big deal. But wait, this Android Developer Verification Program costs 25$? I just wanna publish my little app for free. I am not even making money out of it. Why should I be paying this tax to Google?

好吧，没问题，我不需要 Play Store 账号也能发布应用，我或许可以通过 @fdroidorg 分发 APK。但等等，这是什么？未来要分发我的 APK，我必须注册“Android 开发者验证计划”？好吧，那应该只是个注册流程，没什么大不了的。但等等，这个 Android 开发者验证计划要收 25 美元？我只是想免费发布我的小应用而已。我甚至都没从中赚钱。我为什么要向 Google 缴纳这笔“税”？

Okay 25$ isn't a big amount, I can pay. But what's this, I need to tell them who I am as well? WTF is this shit! Okay how about I will just share a development build with my friends. Tf? Even for the users below 20, I must register with the techno overlord? Sure there is no 25$ for 20 or less, but I still need to have a verified payments profile and must tell them my opinion about publishing on android? Tell me @Google, can I write "Fuck you" there? or should I be a lickaboot loyal little rule-follower and write a love letter there?

好吧，25 美元不是什么大数目，我付得起。但这是什么鬼，我还需要向他们证明我的身份？这到底是什么狗屁玩意儿！好吧，那我就只把开发版本分享给朋友总行了吧？搞什么？即使是针对 20 人以下的用户，我也必须向这位“科技霸主”注册？确实，20 人以下不需要交 25 美元，但我仍然需要有一个经过验证的支付资料，还必须告诉他们我对在 Android 上发布应用的看法？告诉我 @Google，我能在那里写“去你的”吗？还是说我应该做一个唯唯诺诺、忠诚守规的小奴才，给他们写一封情书？

**Suraj Verma 🇮🇳@vermaizer**
I don't really have an issue with the $25 or even ID verification if someone wants to distribute apps at scale. $25 isn't going to stop an actual scammer anyway, but verification can at least make repeated abuse harder. What I don't like is Google becoming the gatekeeper.

如果有人想大规模分发应用，我其实并不介意 25 美元的费用，甚至也不介意身份验证。反正 25 美元也挡不住真正的骗子，但验证至少能增加重复滥用的难度。我不喜欢的是 Google 正在成为（应用分发的）守门人。
