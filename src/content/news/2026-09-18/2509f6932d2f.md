# Here’s What the AI Apocalypse Could Look Like
# 人工智能末日可能是什么样子

The debate of whether AI can kill us has been everywhere lately, but what would an actual AI catastrophe look like? This week, Brian Barrett, Zoë Schiffer, and Leah Feiger break down three real-world scenarios experts worry the most about—including hacked water supplies, bioweapons, and autonomous robots.
关于人工智能是否会终结人类的争论最近无处不在，但真正的人工智能灾难会是什么样子呢？本周，Brian Barrett、Zoë Schiffer 和 Leah Feiger 剖析了专家们最担心的三种现实场景，包括供水系统被黑、生物武器以及自动机器人。

We also discuss the highlights of Salesforce’s annual conference, where OpenAI’s Sam Altman and Anthropic’s Dario Amodei defended their positions on AI safety. Plus, Leah tells us how this backlash against AI has become a rare bipartisan flash point, to the point that Bernie Sanders and Steve Bannon shared a stage over it this week.
我们还讨论了 Salesforce 年度大会的亮点，OpenAI 的 Sam Altman 和 Anthropic 的 Dario Amodei 在会上为他们的人工智能安全立场进行了辩护。此外，Leah 还告诉我们，这种针对人工智能的抵制情绪是如何成为一个罕见的跨党派焦点，以至于伯尼·桑德斯（Bernie Sanders）和史蒂夫·班农（Steve Bannon）本周甚至因此同台讨论。
