# Geometry-Driven Shadow Harmonisation for Composited Faces: A Multiplicative, Albedo-Preserving Relighting Pipeline
# 基于几何驱动的合成人脸阴影协调：一种乘法、保留反照率的重光照流水线

**Abstract:** Face swapping and face compositing pipelines routinely produce a face that is geometrically well aligned but photometrically implausible: the donor face carries flat, near-frontal studio illumination while the host body and background carry directional scene light. Most existing remedies re-synthesise the face through colour transfer, neural relighting, or inverse rendering, and therefore risk altering identity, skin tone, and texture.

**摘要：** 人脸交换和人脸合成流水线通常能生成几何对齐良好但光度不合理的人脸：源人脸带有平坦、近乎正面的摄影棚照明，而宿主身体和背景则带有方向性的场景光。大多数现有的补救措施通过色彩转换、神经重光照或逆向渲染来重新合成人脸，因此存在改变身份、肤色和纹理的风险。

We present a conservative alternative: geometry-driven form-shadow injection. The pipeline never repaints the face. It estimates a per-pixel gain field $g\in[g_{\min},1]$ from a rasterised 3D face proxy and multiplies it channel-uniformly onto linear RGB, so the operator can only darken and cannot shift chromaticity. A dense landmark mesh is rasterised into a depth buffer, from which we derive surface normals, a cavity term, and screen-space cast shadows.

我们提出了一种保守的替代方案：几何驱动的形态阴影注入。该流水线从不重绘人脸。它通过光栅化的 3D 人脸代理估计一个像素级的增益场 $g\in[g_{\min},1]$，并将其通道统一地乘以线性 RGB，因此该算子只能变暗，不能改变色度。一个密集的特征点网格被光栅化到深度缓冲区中，我们从中推导出表面法线、空腔项和屏幕空间投影。

Key-light direction is estimated from host-side cues (body, background, hair halo); on-face cues are downweighted because they recover the donor's lighting. Shadow magnitude is not matched to the host: it is set by a three-parameter transfer $(\tau,\sigma,g_{\min})$. The shading field is divided by its 75th percentile over skin, then gated, scaled, clamped, smoothed, and re-clipped inside a feathered, skin-gated face mask.

主光源方向根据宿主侧的线索（身体、背景、头发光晕）进行估计；人脸上的线索权重被降低，因为它们反映的是源人脸的照明。阴影强度不与宿主匹配，而是由三参数转换 $(\tau,\sigma,g_{\min})$ 设置。着色场除以其在皮肤区域的第 75 百分位数，然后在羽化且经过皮肤门控的人脸掩模内进行门控、缩放、钳制、平滑和重新裁剪。

On an analytic face heightfield, the default $(\tau,\sigma,g_{\min})=(0.90,0.45,0.82)$ modifies 56.5% of face pixels with mean gain 0.938 (0.890 on modified pixels) and drives 3.4% of pixels to the floor. Hue invariance is a corollary of the operator. We analyse the transfer in closed form, ablate its parameters, and discuss failure modes of a monotone, darkening-only formulation, including double-shadowing of non-flat donors.

在解析人脸高度场上，默认参数 $(\tau,\sigma,g_{\min})=(0.90,0.45,0.82)$ 修改了 56.5% 的人脸像素，平均增益为 0.938（在被修改像素上为 0.890），并将 3.4% 的像素驱动至下限。色调不变性是该算子的一个推论。我们以闭式形式分析了该转换，对其参数进行了消融研究，并讨论了单调、仅变暗公式的失效模式，包括非平坦源人脸的双重阴影问题。
