# A Heisenberg Lift Descriptor for Order Sensitive Online Handwriting Recognition
# 用于顺序敏感在线手写识别的海森堡提升描述符

**Abstract:** Online handwriting recognition systems typically represent pen trajectories through fixed-length Euclidean shape descriptors that capture the spatial outline of each stroke, but are insensitive to the order in which that outline is produced. Two strokes that trace the same region of the plane in opposite directions are indistinguishable to any such order-blind representation, yet their traversal directions may carry decisive class information in characters where loop orientation and stroke sequencing matter.

**摘要：** 在线手写识别系统通常通过定长的欧几里得形状描述符来表示笔迹轨迹，这些描述符能够捕捉每一笔画的空间轮廓，但对轮廓产生的顺序不敏感。对于任何此类忽略顺序的表示方法而言，两个在平面上以相反方向描绘同一区域的笔画是无法区分的；然而，在循环方向和笔画顺序至关重要的字符中，其遍历方向可能携带决定性的分类信息。

This paper introduces a Heisenberg-lift framework that addresses this gap through a compact, interpretable, order-sensitive augmentation for online pen-trajectory features. The simplest instance is the terminal signed area, a single parameter-free scalar appended to an existing Euclidean descriptor at negligible computational cost. Evaluated on two standard online handwriting benchmarks, this one-scalar addition, consistently raises classifier accuracy over the Euclidean baseline.

本文引入了一种海森堡提升（Heisenberg-lift）框架，通过为在线笔迹轨迹特征提供一种紧凑、可解释且对顺序敏感的增强方法，解决了这一缺陷。其最简单的实例是“末端有向面积”（terminal signed area），这是一个无需参数的标量，可以以极低的计算成本附加到现有的欧几里得描述符上。在两个标准的在线手写基准测试中，这一标量的加入始终能提高分类器相对于欧几里得基准的准确率。

On the hardest character pair in our study, the letters o and y, the signed area alone achieves perfect separation while the Euclidean baseline falls short. The advantage grows further under additive coordinate noise, a practically relevant degradation in pen-trajectory data. A richer fifteen-dimensional extension, derived from a noncommutative Heisenberg-group subdivision scheme, provides additional gains in noisy and loop-structured conditions.

在我们研究中最难区分的字符对“o”和“y”上，仅使用有向面积就能实现完美区分，而欧几里得基准则表现不佳。在存在加性坐标噪声（笔迹轨迹数据中一种实际常见的退化情况）的情况下，这一优势进一步扩大。通过非交换海森堡群细分方案导出的更丰富的十五维扩展，在噪声和循环结构条件下提供了额外的性能提升。

Dimension-matched statistical controls confirm that all improvements reflect geometric information rather than feature-count inflation. The resulting descriptor is lightweight, closed-form, and directly interpretable, making it a practical augmentation for online handwriting and related document-trajectory classification pipelines in which the direction of stroke execution carries discriminative information.

维度匹配的统计对照实验证实，所有的改进都反映了几何信息，而非特征数量膨胀的结果。由此产生的描述符轻量、闭式且具有直接的可解释性，使其成为在线手写及相关文档轨迹分类流程的实用增强工具，特别是在笔画执行方向携带判别信息的场景中。
