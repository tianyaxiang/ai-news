### Title: What is the programming job market like in 2026? Is it still worth investing in the field and pursuing a career opportunity?
### 标题：2026 年的编程就业市场现状如何？现在投入该领域并寻求职业机会还值得吗？

Hi everyone, I’d like to hear the opinions of those already in the field or those trying to break into it. I’m a Computer Science student, and I’ve been trying to land my first opportunity as a developer, but so far, I haven't succeeded.
大家好，我想听听已经在职场的前辈，或者同样正在尝试入行的人的看法。我是一名计算机科学专业的学生，一直试图获得第一份开发工作机会，但到目前为止还没有成功。

Throughout this time, I’ve kept studying and trying to improve. Currently, I’m focusing on Python, data structures, and databases, and I’m starting to dive deeper into the Spring ecosystem. I also try to build projects to put what I learn into practice, but the biggest challenge remains getting that first professional experience.
在这段时间里，我一直在不断学习和提升自己。目前，我专注于 Python、数据结构和数据库，并开始深入研究 Spring 生态系统。我也尝试通过构建项目来实践所学知识，但最大的挑战依然是获得那份最初的职业经验。

What discourages me the most is looking at current Junior Developer job listings. Many seem to require an absurd amount of knowledge: years of experience, Java, Spring Boot, Docker, cloud, microservices, testing, CI/CD, advanced English, and various other technologies. Sometimes it feels like the "Junior" role is closer to a Mid-level position.
最让我感到沮丧的是查看当前的初级开发人员招聘信息。许多职位似乎要求掌握极其繁杂的知识：多年的工作经验、Java、Spring Boot、Docker、云服务、微服务、测试、CI/CD、高级英语以及各种其他技术。有时我觉得，“初级”职位的要求更像是中级岗位的标准。
