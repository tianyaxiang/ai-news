# OpenAI Astra for Law Brings GPT-6 Astra to Legal Research and Workflow Building
# OpenAI 推出 Astra for Law，将 GPT-6 Astra 引入法律研究与工作流构建

OpenAI has announced Astra for Law, a legal AI foundation that combines its GPT-6 Astra flagship model with a legal-specific toolkit for law firms and legal technology companies. The initial rollout is limited to selected firms through OpenAI’s Trusted Access Program in ChatGPT and Codex, while API access is described as coming soon. The product is designed to support legal research, writing, deal preparation and broader legal workflows while keeping professional judgment and client confidentiality central to its use.

OpenAI 宣布推出 Astra for Law，这是一个法律 AI 基础平台，将旗舰模型 GPT-6 Astra 与专为律师事务所和法律科技公司设计的法律工具包相结合。初期部署仅限于通过 OpenAI 的“可信访问计划”（Trusted Access Program）在 ChatGPT 和 Codex 中向部分律所开放，API 访问权限预计将于近期推出。该产品旨在支持法律研究、文书写作、交易准备及更广泛的法律工作流，同时确保专业判断和客户保密性始终处于核心地位。

According to OpenAI’s official Astra for Law announcement, the offering will appear as GPT-6 Astra Law in the model picker and as gpt-6-astra-law in the API. The launch matters because it packages a frontier model with legal retrieval, domain instructions and controls rather than leaving firms and software providers to assemble those elements independently.

根据 OpenAI 关于 Astra for Law 的官方公告，该产品在模型选择器中将显示为“GPT-6 Astra Law”，在 API 中则标识为“gpt-6-astra-law”。此次发布意义重大，因为它将前沿模型与法律检索、领域指令及控制功能打包在一起，无需律所和软件供应商自行整合这些要素。

### What OpenAI Astra for Law includes
### OpenAI Astra for Law 包含的内容

At the core of Astra for Law is GPT-6 Astra, which OpenAI describes as its latest flagship model. The legal version adds a toolkit intended for legal analysis and legal writing, including specialized instructions and a legal search index. That index can search US case law, statutes, regulations and court rules across a corpus of more than 230 million URLs. OpenAI says the index leverages CourtListener data to cover a substantial share of published US precedential law. For legal users, this is an important distinction from a general-purpose model that may help draft or summarize text but does not include a dedicated legal retrieval layer. Research quality still depends on the underlying sources, the matter at hand and lawyer review, but the index is intended to make authoritative legal material more accessible within an AI-assisted workflow.

Astra for Law 的核心是 GPT-6 Astra，OpenAI 将其描述为最新的旗舰模型。法律版本增加了一个旨在进行法律分析和法律写作的工具包，包括专业指令和法律搜索索引。该索引可以搜索超过 2.3 亿个 URL 语料库中的美国判例法、法规、条例和法院规则。OpenAI 表示，该索引利用 CourtListener 数据，覆盖了已发表的美国判例法中的很大一部分。对于法律用户而言，这与通用模型有重要区别——通用模型或许能协助起草或总结文本，但不包含专门的法律检索层。研究质量仍取决于底层来源、具体案件及律师的审查，但该索引旨在使权威法律资料在 AI 辅助工作流中更易于获取。

The announced toolkit and rollout include:
* A legal search index for US case law, statutes, regulations and court rules.
* Specialized instructions for legal analysis and writing.
* Legal-grade trust and controls, according to OpenAI.
* A Trusted Access Program for eligible law firms.
* Zero Data Retention on API access for eligible law firms.
* Plugins and integrations that connect ChatGPT with established legal software.

此次宣布的工具包和部署内容包括：
* 用于美国判例法、法规、条例和法院规则的法律搜索索引。
* 针对法律分析和写作的专业指令。
* 据 OpenAI 称，具备法律级的信任与控制功能。
* 面向符合条件的律师事务所的“可信访问计划”。
* 面向符合条件的律师事务所提供 API 访问的“零数据保留”政策。
* 将 ChatGPT 与现有法律软件连接的插件和集成功能。

### Access, data controls and unanswered pricing questions
### 访问权限、数据控制及未明确的定价问题

The launch is not a general availability announcement for every legal organization. OpenAI says initial deployment will go to selected law firms through Trusted Access, a program for eligible firms. This staged approach is paired with ongoing collaboration between OpenAI, law firms and legal technology partners as the company evolves the product’s capabilities and controls. For firms handling sensitive client information, OpenAI’s stated Zero Data Retention commitment for eligible law firms using API access is a notable part of the proposition. However, the announcement does not provide public pricing, a broad API launch date or detailed eligibility requirements for Trusted Access. Organizations evaluating the platform will need to watch for those operational details before making implementation decisions.

此次发布并非针对所有法律机构的全面公开发布。OpenAI 表示，初期部署将通过“可信访问计划”提供给选定的律师事务所。这种分阶段的方法伴随着 OpenAI 与律所及法律科技合作伙伴之间的持续协作，以不断优化产品的功能和控制手段。对于处理敏感客户信息的律所而言，OpenAI 对使用 API 访问的合格律所做出的“零数据保留”承诺是该方案中值得注意的一部分。然而，公告并未提供公开定价、广泛的 API 发布日期或“可信访问计划”的详细资格要求。评估该平台的机构在做出实施决策前，需密切关注这些运营细节。

### A plugin ecosystem for existing legal workflows
### 现有法律工作流的插件生态系统

OpenAI is positioning Astra for Law as a foundation for both direct use and product development. It highlights 26 partner-built plugins that connect ChatGPT to legal tools, including Relativity, Clio and HighQ/CoCounsel Legal through Thomson Reuters. The company also cites work with firms and vendors including Sullivan & Cromwell, Harvey, Thomson Reuters and Latham & Watkins. This ecosystem focus is significant because useful legal AI rarely operates as an isolated chat interface. Legal teams work across matter files, document review systems, practice-management platforms and research sources. Integrations can make AI more relevant to an existing workflow, although the announcement does not specify which plugin capabilities are available to which firms or how individual integrations will be configured.

OpenAI 将 Astra for Law 定位为直接使用和产品开发的基础。它重点介绍了 26 个由合作伙伴构建的插件，这些插件将 ChatGPT 与 Relativity、Clio 以及汤森路透旗下的 HighQ/CoCounsel Legal 等法律工具连接起来。该公司还提到了与 Sullivan & Cromwell、Harvey、汤森路透和 Latham & Watkins 等律所及供应商的合作。这种对生态系统的关注至关重要，因为实用的法律 AI 很少作为孤立的聊天界面运行。法律团队的工作涉及案件档案、文档审查系统、律所管理平台和研究资源。集成可以使 AI 与现有工作流更紧密地结合，尽管公告并未明确哪些插件功能适用于哪些律所，或具体的集成将如何配置。

OpenAI also says API customers such as Harvey and Legora will be able to build on Astra for Law. That creates a path for legal software companies to incorporate the model and legal toolkit into their own products. OpenAI’s stated plan to maintain the legal configuration could reduce the amount of model setup required from builders, allowing them to focus on their product experience and workflow design.

OpenAI 还表示，Harvey 和 Legora 等 API 客户将能够在 Astra for Law 的基础上进行开发。这为法律软件公司将该模型和法律工具包整合到其自身产品中开辟了路径。OpenAI 计划维护法律配置，这可以减少开发者所需的模型设置工作量，使他们能够专注于产品体验和工作流设计。

### What the rollout could mean in practice
### 此次发布在实践中的意义

The practical promise is faster access to relevant legal material and more capable assistance with research, drafting and deal preparation. A lawyer might use a legal AI workflow to locate authorities, organize a research trail, create an initial document outline or prepare a matter-specific summary. The value is not simply producing text faster. It is reducing the time spent moving between systems and assembling first-pass work that professionals can assess and refine.

其实际价值在于能够更快地获取相关法律资料，并在研究、起草和交易准备方面提供更强大的辅助。律师可以使用法律 AI 工作流来查找权威依据、整理研究路径、创建初步文档大纲或准备特定案件的摘要。其价值不仅仅在于更快地生成文本，而在于减少在不同系统间切换的时间，并汇总出可供专业人士评估和完善的初步工作成果。

The limits matter as much as the potential. Astra for Law does not remove the need to validate authorities, assess factual context or apply legal judgment. OpenAI itself frames the initiative around preserving professional judgment and client confidentiality. The initial limited access also means firms should not assume immediate availability, public pricing or standardized deployment options. For legal technology providers, the launch signals a more specialized layer on top of a general model. The combination of GPT-6 Astra, legal search, instructions and planned API access may make it easier to develop legal-focused applications, but product teams will still need to determine how the model fits their data, user experience and review processes.

局限性与潜力同样重要。Astra for Law 并不能免除验证权威依据、评估事实背景或运用法律判断的必要性。OpenAI 本身也将该计划的核心定位于维护专业判断和客户保密性。初期有限的访问权限也意味着律所不应假设其已全面可用、拥有公开定价或标准化的部署选项。对于法律科技提供商而言，此次发布标志着在通用模型之上出现了一个更专业的层级。GPT-6 Astra、法律搜索、指令及计划中的 API 访问权限的结合，可能会使开发法律导向的应用变得更容易，但产品团队仍需确定该模型如何适配其数据、用户体验和审查流程。
