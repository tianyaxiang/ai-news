# When the Gradient Sees Rank: Provable Necessity, Causal Recruitment, and Composition in Trained Matrix Memories

**当梯度“看见”秩：训练矩阵记忆中的可证明必要性、因果招募与组合**

***

**Abstract:** Can gradient-based training learn the rank needed to store and compose associations in a matrix memory? In our earlier study, we used a matrix-augmented reasoner on a task that admits a rank-1 solution, leaving this question open. We train matrix memories on $K$ fresh key-value bindings whose exact linear recovery requires $\mathrm{rank}(Z) \geq K$. A fixed linear readout queries a single matrix state without access to the original bindings.

**摘要：** 基于梯度的训练能否学习到在矩阵记忆中存储和组合关联所需的秩？在我们之前的研究中，我们使用了一个矩阵增强推理机来处理一个允许秩为 1 的解的任务，从而将这个问题留作悬而未决。我们对 $K$ 个新的键值绑定训练矩阵记忆，其精确的线性恢复要求 $\mathrm{rank}(Z) \geq K$。一个固定的线性读出器在无法访问原始绑定的情况下查询单个矩阵状态。

***

Experiments measure recovery by cosine similarity greater than 0.9, a threshold distinct from mathematical equality. Learned effective rank increases with $K$ across the tested grid (Spearman $\rho = 1.0$ at $d = 16$). Training-time rank caps produce a recovery transition near $k = K$: at $d = 8$, $K = 4$, rank 3 gives at most 0.0004 recovery and rank 4 gives 0.97.

实验通过余弦相似度大于 0.9 来衡量恢复情况，这是一个不同于数学相等的阈值。在测试网格中，学习到的有效秩随 $K$ 的增加而增加（在 $d = 16$ 时，Spearman $\rho = 1.0$）。训练时的秩上限在 $k = K$ 附近产生了恢复转换：在 $d = 8, K = 4$ 时，秩 3 最多提供 0.0004 的恢复率，而秩 4 则达到 0.97。

***

Four of five seeds retain at least 0.9996 recovery through 21-fold self-application of the trained operator. On the entity subspace, the learned operator has effective rank close to $K$ and approximates the ideal cycle. For the single converged seed capped below $K$, a calculation using the entity-subspace operator and ideal cycle predicts the measured cosine within 0.008 through seven applications. Extending training resolves several initial failures, but recovery still declines at larger matrix dimensions with encoder width fixed.

在五个随机种子中，有四个在经过 21 次训练算子的自应用后，仍保持至少 0.9996 的恢复率。在实体子空间上，学习到的算子具有接近 $K$ 的有效秩，并近似于理想循环。对于单个收敛于 $K$ 以下的种子，使用实体子空间算子和理想循环进行的计算，在七次应用中预测的余弦值误差在 0.008 以内。延长训练时间可以解决一些初始失败，但在编码器宽度固定的情况下，随着矩阵维度的增大，恢复率仍然会下降。
