# I built a digital city where brands can own the advertising space
# 我构建了一座数字城市，品牌可以在这里拥有广告位

Most internet advertising still looks the same. Banners. Sponsored cards. Promoted posts. Rectangles everywhere. I wanted to try something different: What if advertising was a place instead of a box? So I built Swishtown.
目前的互联网广告看起来大同小异：横幅、赞助卡片、推广帖，到处都是矩形框。我想尝试一些不同的东西：如果广告不再是一个“盒子”，而是一个“场所”会怎样？于是，我构建了 Swishtown。

Swishtown is a fictional digital city where buildings, shops, roads, parks, and other locations can become advertising real estate. A brand can take over a location, place its logo there, and turn that part of the city into a clickable destination. Instead of putting an ad next to the experience, the ad becomes part of the experience itself.
Swishtown 是一座虚构的数字城市，其中的建筑、商店、道路、公园和其他地点都可以成为广告地产。品牌可以接管某个地点，放置其 Logo，并将城市的那一部分变成一个可点击的目标页面。广告不再是依附于体验之外的附属品，而是成为了体验本身的一部分。

### How it works
### 运作方式

A visitor explores the city and clicks on a location. Each location has its own price and status. If a company takes over that space, its branding appears directly on that part of the city. The logo becomes clickable and can redirect people to: a startup, a product page, a company website, a campaign, basically any destination the advertiser wants.
访客在城市中探索并点击某个地点。每个地点都有自己的价格和状态。如果一家公司接管了该空间，其品牌形象会直接出现在城市的那一部分。Logo 可以点击，并能将用户重定向到：初创公司页面、产品页面、公司官网、营销活动页面，基本上是广告主想要的任何目标地址。

The interesting part is that these spaces are not necessarily permanent. Some locations can later become available again if another advertiser is willing to pay more for the same space. So the city is designed to stay dynamic instead of becoming a static directory of logos.
有趣的是，这些空间并不一定是永久的。如果另一个广告主愿意为同一空间支付更高的费用，某些地点可能会重新开放。因此，这座城市的设计初衷是保持动态，而不是成为一个静态的 Logo 目录。

### Why I built it
### 构建初衷

I kept thinking about how predictable online advertising has become. Most users have trained themselves to ignore banner-like elements almost automatically. So I wanted to test a different idea: Can advertising itself become something people want to explore? Instead of: content-ad-content-ad-content, I wanted something closer to: the environment itself becomes the advertising surface.
我一直在思考在线广告变得多么“可预测”。大多数用户已经习惯性地自动忽略横幅广告。因此，我想测试一个不同的想法：广告本身能否成为人们想要探索的对象？与其采用“内容-广告-内容-广告”的模式，我更希望实现：环境本身成为广告的载体。

Think of it like digital real estate. A skyscraper has more visual presence than a tiny shop. A central location feels different from a remote location. A road, park, tower, or landmark can each have a different kind of value. That makes the advertising inventory feel more tangible.
把它想象成数字房地产。摩天大楼比小商店具有更强的视觉存在感。中心地段与偏远地段的感觉截然不同。道路、公园、塔楼或地标建筑都可以拥有不同类型的价值。这使得广告库存变得更加具体可感。

### The product
### 产品功能

Swishtown currently includes: an explorable digital city, multiple city visual modes, clickable properties, brand placements, purchase flows, payment handling, analytics, session tracking, uptime monitoring, and performance monitoring. I also built the launch infrastructure around it so I can understand how people actually use the city after they arrive.
Swishtown 目前包括：可探索的数字城市、多种城市视觉模式、可点击的属性、品牌植入、购买流程、支付处理、数据分析、会话跟踪、正常运行时间监控以及性能监控。我还围绕它构建了发布基础设施，以便了解人们在进入城市后的实际使用行为。

### What I am testing now
### 目前的测试重点

The biggest question is not really technical. It is this: Will people actually care about advertising space more if it feels like a world instead of a banner? That is what I am trying to validate now. The first version is live: [swishplay.in](https://swishplay.in)
最大的问题其实不在技术层面，而在于：如果广告空间感觉像是一个世界，而不是一个横幅，人们是否会更关注它？这就是我目前试图验证的内容。第一个版本已经上线：[swishplay.in](https://swishplay.in)

I would especially love feedback from developers, founders, marketers, and product people. What would make a digital city like this more interesting to explore?
我特别期待开发者、创始人、营销人员和产品经理的反馈。什么样的设计会让这样的数字城市更具探索价值？
