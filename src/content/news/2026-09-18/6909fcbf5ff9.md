### Where Grokking Happens: Distributed Utility and Fourier Recoding Without a Module Switch
### “顿悟”发生在哪里：分布式效用与无需模块切换的傅里叶重编码

**Abstract:** Where in a Transformer is the change from memorization to generalization functionally expressed? We introduce Transition Games--behavior-aligned exact activation games with paired non-generalizing controls--and find distributed utility gain with a prospective block-0 attention bias; selected degree-two modes account for 67--92% of its addition contrast across replacement games, and a disjoint exact path study confirms that block-1 MLP mediates more of their effect than all other tested downstream paths in 12/12 pairs.

**摘要：** 在 Transformer 模型中，从“记忆”到“泛化”的转变在功能上是如何体现的？我们引入了“转换博弈”（Transition Games）——即一种行为对齐的精确激活博弈，并配有非泛化对照组。研究发现，分布式效用的提升与前瞻性的第 0 层注意力偏差（block-0 attention bias）有关；选定的二阶模态（degree-two modes）在替换博弈中贡献了 67%–92% 的加法对比度。此外，一项不相交的精确路径研究证实，在所有 12/12 的测试对中，第 1 层 MLP（多层感知机）比其他所有下游路径更能中介这些效应。

The sharper "MLP memorizes, attention generalizes" prediction instead reverses (-.331 bits/example at the memory anchor; 0/12 in the predicted direction), while routing onset, global rank collapse, and a prime-invariant architecture ridge also fail, identifying grokking here as spectral recoding of an existing distributed circuit rather than a module switch.

此前关于“MLP 负责记忆，注意力机制负责泛化”的尖锐预测反而被推翻了（在记忆锚点处为 -0.331 比特/样本；在预测方向上的成功率为 0/12）。同时，路由启动、全局秩坍缩以及素数不变架构脊（prime-invariant architecture ridge）等假设也未能成立。这表明，“顿悟”（grokking）现象在此处表现为现有分布式电路的频谱重编码（spectral recoding），而非模块切换。
