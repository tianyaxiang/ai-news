# Stop Asking Which Agentic Coding Methodology to Use
# 别再纠结该用哪种智能体编程方法论了

The prevailing question in AI-assisted development—"Which methodology should I use?"—is fundamentally flawed. Superpowers, BMAD, Compound Engineering, OpenSpec, SpecKit, GSD, Ralph. The ecosystem is flooded with systems claiming to run your AI agents. The thesis here is simple: There is no universal winner. Each framework orchestrates a distinct layer of agentic development. Stop searching for a silver bullet. Build a stack.

在 AI 辅助开发领域，一个普遍存在的问题是：“我应该使用哪种方法论？”——这从根本上就是错误的。Superpowers、BMAD、Compound Engineering、OpenSpec、SpecKit、GSD、Ralph……整个生态系统充斥着各种声称能驱动你 AI 智能体的系统。本文的核心观点很简单：没有所谓的“万能赢家”。每个框架都负责智能体开发中不同的层面。停止寻找银弹吧，去构建属于你的技术栈。

### 1. Execution vs. Intent: Superpowers vs. BMAD
### 1. 执行与意图：Superpowers 对比 BMAD

The most critical comparison dictates your starting vector. Superpowers (Pure Engineering): Operates as a demanding senior engineer. It enforces design, planning, strict test-driven development, and rigorous review. Default to this for daily coding, solo development, and pure implementation tasks. BMAD (Product Intent): Operates as a complete product organization—analysts, PMs, architects, and readiness gates. Deploy this when product intent remains uncertain or stakeholder complexity is high.

最关键的对比决定了你的切入点。Superpowers（纯工程化）：其运作方式如同严苛的高级工程师，强制执行设计、规划、严格的测试驱动开发（TDD）以及严谨的代码审查。在日常编码、独立开发和纯实现任务中，优先选择此方案。BMAD（产品意图）：其运作方式如同一个完整的产品组织——包含分析师、产品经理、架构师和就绪门控。当产品意图尚不明确或利益相关者关系复杂时，请部署此方案。

### 2. Context Retention: OpenSpec vs. SpecKit
### 2. 上下文留存：OpenSpec 对比 SpecKit

Both frameworks combat context drift by anchoring requirements in versioned files. OpenSpec: Optimized for change and brownfield execution. It is the durable change contract for everyday work on real, existing production codebases. SpecKit: Optimized for governance. It dictates a constitution, traceability, and audit trails. Reserve for heavily regulated environments.

这两个框架都通过将需求锚定在版本化文件中来对抗上下文漂移。OpenSpec：针对变更和存量代码（Brownfield）执行进行了优化。它是处理真实、现有生产代码库日常工作的持久变更契约。SpecKit：针对治理进行了优化。它规定了章程、可追溯性和审计追踪。仅在受严格监管的环境中使用。

### 3. The Autonomy Layer: GSD vs. Ralph
### 3. 自主层：GSD 对比 Ralph

Tread carefully here. GSD: Designed to safeguard structured projects across context windows via requirements, roadmaps, and verification. (Note: The original repository was archived in June 2026. Use only for long unattended runs once a stable fork emerges.) Ralph: A continuous loop (pick an item, implement a test, repeat). Warning: Without impenetrable tests, Ralph will happily burn tokens iterating on bad decisions.

此处需谨慎行事。GSD：旨在通过需求、路线图和验证，在上下文窗口中保护结构化项目。（注：原始仓库已于 2026 年 6 月归档。请仅在出现稳定的分支版本后，将其用于长时间的无人值守运行。）Ralph：一个连续循环（挑选任务、实现测试、重复）。警告：如果没有坚不可摧的测试，Ralph 会很乐意在错误的决策上反复迭代，从而耗尽你的 Token。

### 4. The Meta-Layer: Compound Engineering
### 4. 元层：复合工程（Compound Engineering）

Compound Engineering shifts the focus from "How do we build this correctly?" to "How does this work make the next task frictionless?" Every feature delivery concludes by recording lessons for agents to reuse. If your role leans closer to founder than individual contributor, this is your core methodology.

复合工程将关注点从“我们如何正确构建它？”转移到了“这项工作如何让下一个任务变得顺畅无阻？”。每次功能交付结束时，都会记录下经验教训供智能体复用。如果你的角色更偏向于创始人而非独立贡献者，这就是你的核心方法论。

### The Winning Synthesis
### 获胜的综合方案

Do not install everything. Orchestrate the right sequence based on the situation: BMAD to define requirements when product intent is ambiguous. OpenSpec to establish a durable change contract in the codebase. Superpowers to drive implementation and rigorous verification. Compound Engineering to extract and record lessons post-delivery. I personally would start with BMAD as a higher level harness, and combine it with OpenSpec to drive quality of implementation to the desired level.

不要试图安装所有东西。根据具体情况编排正确的顺序：当产品意图模糊时，使用 BMAD 定义需求；使用 OpenSpec 在代码库中建立持久的变更契约；使用 Superpowers 驱动实现和严谨的验证；使用复合工程在交付后提取并记录经验教训。我个人会以 BMAD 作为更高层级的约束，并结合 OpenSpec，将实现质量提升到理想水平。
