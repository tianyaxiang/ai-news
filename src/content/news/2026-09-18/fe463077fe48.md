# Beyond Performance Metrics: Uncertainty Mapping of Label Ambiguity in Fazekas Score Prediction
# 超越性能指标：Fazekas 评分预测中标签模糊性的不确定性映射

**Abstract:** Reference labels used to train medical image classification models are not always as certain as they may appear, and this uncertainty has implications on performance metrics. In this study, we propose a framework to analyze model performance for periventricular Fazekas score prediction that goes beyond conventional metrics. 

**摘要：** 用于训练医学图像分类模型的参考标签并不总是像看起来那样确定，这种不确定性会对性能指标产生影响。在本研究中，我们提出了一个框架，用于分析脑室周围 Fazekas 评分预测的模型性能，该框架超越了传统的评估指标。

The Fazekas score is an ordinal visual rating scale used to assess the severity of white matter hyperintensities and is known to be affected by inter-rater variability. While the best Fazekas score prediction model achieved a Matthews correlation coefficient (MCC) of 0.70, performance varied across data splits and loss functions, making interpretation of model capabilities difficult. 

Fazekas 评分是一种用于评估白质高信号严重程度的序数视觉评分量表，众所周知，它会受到评估者间差异的影响。虽然表现最好的 Fazekas 评分预测模型达到了 0.70 的马修斯相关系数 (MCC)，但其性能在不同的数据划分和损失函数下存在差异，这使得对模型能力的解读变得困难。

Rather than interpreting epistemic uncertainty of a model's prediction as an isolated scalar value, our approach of uncertainty mapping relates uncertainty to its position within the learned feature representation. This highlights regions of class-boundary transitions where cases appear more ambiguous and misclassifications are more likely. 

我们的不确定性映射方法不再将模型预测的认知不确定性解释为孤立的标量值，而是将不确定性与其在学习到的特征表示中的位置联系起来。这突出了类别边界转换的区域，在这些区域中，病例显得更加模糊，且更容易出现误分类。

It also identifies potential label disagreement, including low-uncertainty misclassified cases that expert review found to be inconsistent with the original reference Fazekas score. Therefore, uncertainty mapping allows model behaviour to be examined in relation to class separation and potential model-label disagreement. 

该方法还识别了潜在的标签分歧，包括那些经专家审查发现与原始参考 Fazekas 评分不一致的低不确定性误分类病例。因此，不确定性映射允许我们结合类别分离情况和潜在的模型-标签分歧来检查模型行为。

Loss function choice also influenced the uncertainty profile, with some models showing clearer class separation and more localized uncertainty in ambiguous regions than others. These findings suggest that uncertainty mapping for Fazekas score predictions can support model interpretation and targeted dataset review when reference labels are affected by ambiguity/inter-rater variability.

损失函数的选择也会影响不确定性分布，一些模型在模糊区域表现出比其他模型更清晰的类别分离和更局部化的不确定性。这些发现表明，当参考标签受到模糊性或评估者间差异的影响时，针对 Fazekas 评分预测的不确定性映射可以支持模型解读和有针对性的数据集审查。
