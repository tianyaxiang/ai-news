# Selective Prediction and Uncertainty-Aware Referral for Pap Smear Classification
# 巴氏涂片分类中的选择性预测与不确定性感知转诊

**Abstract:** Deep learning models for cervical cytology are almost always evaluated as if every prediction must be acted upon, yet a screening system deployed alongside a cytopathologist need not classify every slide: it can defer the cases it is least certain about. Evaluating such a system requires asking not only how often it is correct, but whether its confidence ranks its errors to the bottom.

**摘要：** 宫颈细胞学的深度学习模型在评估时，几乎总是假设每一个预测都必须被采纳。然而，与细胞病理学家协同工作的筛查系统并不需要对每一张切片进行分类：它可以将那些最不确定的病例进行转诊（defer）。评估此类系统不仅需要考察其准确率，还需要考察其置信度是否能将错误预测排在末位。

This paper studies selective prediction and uncertainty-aware referral on the Herlev Pap smear dataset under a binary Normal-versus-Abnormal formulation. Two lightweight transformer backbones (Swin-Tiny, TinyViT-5M) are fine-tuned on Herlev from ImageNet-pretrained weights with weighted random sampling, calibrated by post-hoc temperature scaling fit on a held-out calibration subset, and compared against a soft-voting ensemble of both models.

本文在 Herlev 巴氏涂片数据集上，针对“正常 vs. 异常”的二分类任务，研究了选择性预测和不确定性感知转诊。研究使用了两个轻量级 Transformer 主干网络（Swin-Tiny 和 TinyViT-5M），通过加权随机采样在 Herlev 数据集上对 ImageNet 预训练权重进行微调，并利用在留出校准子集上拟合的后验温度缩放（post-hoc temperature scaling）进行校准，最后将其与两者的软投票集成模型进行了对比。

Discrimination is reported alongside expected calibration error (ECE) and, as the primary endpoint, the area under the risk-coverage curve (AURC). No statistically significant difference was detected between the two configurations in accuracy or macro-F1, yet the ensemble halves AURC (0.0022 vs. 0.0045, a 51.8% reduction, lower in all five folds) and extends the coverage at which zero errors are made from 18.3% to 72.8% of the pooled test predictions.

研究报告了判别能力、预期校准误差（ECE）以及作为主要终点的风险覆盖曲线下面积（AURC）。结果显示，两种配置在准确率或宏观 F1 分数上没有统计学上的显著差异，但集成模型将 AURC 减半（从 0.0045 降至 0.0022，降低了 51.8%，且在所有五个折叠中均表现更优），并将零错误覆盖率从汇总测试预测的 18.3% 提升至 72.8%。

The same ensemble is nonetheless worse calibrated in absolute terms (ECE 0.0339 vs. 0.0247) and produces more false negatives (14 vs. 10). These results separate two properties that are frequently conflated: the ability to rank predictions by trustworthiness, and the accuracy of the confidence values themselves. Ensembling improves the former while degrading the latter, and the former directly governs the observed risk-coverage tradeoff, whereas the latter governs the interpretation of the reported confidence values.

尽管如此，该集成模型在绝对校准方面表现较差（ECE 为 0.0339 vs. 0.0247），且产生的假阴性更多（14 vs. 10）。这些结果区分了两个经常被混淆的属性：根据可信度对预测进行排序的能力，以及置信度值本身的准确性。集成学习提升了前者，却削弱了后者；前者直接决定了观察到的风险-覆盖权衡，而后者则决定了对所报告置信度值的解读。
