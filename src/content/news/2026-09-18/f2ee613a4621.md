# Adaptive Interpolatory Curve Subdivision with Learned Local Angles
# 基于学习局部角度的自适应插值曲线细分

**Abstract:** Curve subdivision is pivotal in computer graphics for generating smooth geometric objects from control polygons. Interpolatory subdivision is especially attractive because the refined curve is guaranteed to pass through the designer's control points. Classical four-point and six-point schemes preserve this property, but their behaviour is governed by a single global tension parameter, limiting their ability to adapt across flat regions, sharp turns and varying local geometries.

**摘要：** 曲线细分在计算机图形学中对于从控制多边形生成平滑几何对象至关重要。插值细分因其能确保细分后的曲线通过设计者的控制点而极具吸引力。经典的四点和六点细分方案虽然保留了这一特性，但其行为受单一全局张力参数的控制，限制了它们在平坦区域、急转弯和不同局部几何形状之间的适应能力。

We introduce an adaptive local-angle formulation that keeps the interpolatory structure intact while learning how each new vertex should be inserted. A compact edge-wise predictor assigns one insertion angle per edge, while the original vertices are copied exactly at every refinement level. Interpolation is therefore a structural property of the operator and does not depend on the trained weights.

我们引入了一种自适应局部角度公式，在保持插值结构完整的同时，学习如何插入每个新顶点。一个紧凑的边级预测器为每条边分配一个插入角度，而原始顶点在每个细分级别都被精确复制。因此，插值是算子的一种结构属性，并不依赖于训练权重。

The same predictor is used with geometry-specific geodesic primitives on the Euclidean plane, the two-sphere and the Poincaré disk. Under a matched-density evaluation protocol, the method reduces nearest-neighbour error by factors of five to seventeen over the best validation-tuned fixed-tension baseline, and by about 1.8 over centripetal Catmull-Rom in the Euclidean case. It also substantially reduces bending energy and tangent roughness, while remaining competitive with separately trained per-geometry models.

该预测器同样适用于欧几里得平面、二维球面和庞加莱圆盘上的特定几何测地线基元。在匹配密度评估协议下，该方法在欧几里得情形下，将最近邻误差较最佳验证调整后的固定张力基准降低了 5 到 17 倍，较向心 Catmull-Rom 样条降低了约 1.8 倍。此外，它还显著降低了弯曲能量和切线粗糙度，同时在与针对特定几何形状单独训练的模型相比时，仍保持了极强的竞争力。
