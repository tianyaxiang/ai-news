# The clock is running out for America’s most contentious spying law

立法会议结束前几周，一些国会议员正忙于重新授权一项极具争议的无证窃听权，可能将其附加到必须通过的军事开支法案中。参议员迈克·朗兹（共和党，南达科他州）告诉《政客》杂志，《外国情报监视法》（FISA）第702条可能会被附加到《国防授权法案》中。这是围绕一项尚未正式失效的主要间谍规则所进行的艰苦斗争中的最新进展。

Weeks before the end of the legislative session, some members of Congress are scrambling to reauthorize a controversial warrantless wiretapping authority, possibly by attaching it to a must-pass military spending bill. Sen. Mike Rounds (R-SD) told Politico that Section 702 of the Foreign Intelligence Surveillance Act (FISA) could be attached to the National Defense Authorization Act. It’s the latest in a grueling fight over a major spying rule that hasn’t technically lapsed.

“我们没有两周时间专门处理FISA，”参议院情报特别委员会成员朗兹说，“但如果把它修正到NDAA中，你就可以推动它并及时完成。”

“We don’t have two weeks to work just on FISA,” said Rounds, a member of the Senate Select Committee on Intelligence. “But if it’s amended onto the NDAA then you can move it forward and get that done in time.”

FISA允许在所谓的国家安全理由下，对某些外国人的通信进行无证监视。部分由于FISA的范围广泛，国会每隔几年就会重新审视它，既是为了重新授权该权力，也是为了对其进行改革。上一次同时实现这两点的努力失败了。尽管受到唐纳德·特朗普总统的压力，国会还是在夏天未能重新授权FISA，部分原因是领导层不允许对任何修正案——特别是限制其广泛范围的尝试——进行辩论或提出。

FISA allows for the warrantless surveillance of certain foreigners’ communications, ostensibly on national security grounds. In part because of FISA’s broad scope, Congress revisits it every few years, both to reauthorize the authority and to reform it. The last effort to do both failed. Despite pressure from President Donald Trump, Congress failed to reauthorize FISA over the summer, in part because leadership wouldn’t allow any amendments — particularly attempts to limit its sweeping scope — to be debated or introduced.

但监视权力实际上并没有失效，因为FISA法院在3月份纠正了第702条下的监视行为，这意味着监视国家可以照常运行四个月。那么为什么要这么紧急呢？如果你还没听说，新一届国会即将上任——这一届国会更不可能在没有某种形式改革的情况下，给予特朗普看似无限的间谍权力。

But the surveillance authority didn’t actually lapse because the FISA court rectified surveillance under Section 702 back in March, meaning the surveillance state can keep chugging along as is for another four months. So why the urgency? If you haven’t heard, there’s about to be a new Congress in town — one that is even less likely to give Trump seemingly unlimited spying powers without some semblance of reform.

对FISA的支持和反对并不是一个明确的党派问题。上一次，由众议院议长迈克·约翰逊（共和党，路易斯安那州）和众议院情报常设特别委员会高级成员吉姆·海姆斯（民主党，康涅狄格州）领导的两党联盟发起了“干净”重新授权FISA的运动。他们的努力主要因为自由核心小组的抵制而失败，该小组的隐私担忧与自由派议员的担忧相呼应。FISA的批评者想要的是改革，而不是废除。他们的目标是增加情报机构使用该法规的难度，该法规本意是针对外国目标的，但却被用来获取美国人的通信。

Support for — and opposition to — FISA isn’t a clear-cut partisan issue. Last time around, a bipartisan coalition led by House Speaker Mike Johnson (R-LA) and Rep. Jim Himes (D-CT), the ranking member of the House Permanent Select Committee on Intelligence, led the campaign for a “clean” reauthorization of FISA. Their efforts were defeated largely because of holdouts from the Freedom Caucus, whose privacy concerns echoed those of liberal lawmakers. FISA’s critics wanted reform, not abolition. Their goal was to make it harder for intelligence agencies to use the statute, which is intended for foreign targets, to access Americans’ communications.

“我认为这是监视鹰派的孤注一掷，试图强行通过他们一直想做的事情，”政府监督项目宪法项目高级政策顾问唐·贝尔告诉《The Verge》。“这是每个人都知道即将发生的事情。已经有几个月的时间来做这件事了，我不认为有人说完全不要重新授权。争论的焦点在于这是一个极其强大的监视工具，需要有护栏来保护人们的宪法权利。”

“I think it’s a Hail Mary by surveillance hawks to try to ram through what they wanted to do the entire time,” Don Bell, senior policy counsel at the Constitution Project of the Project on Government Oversight, told The Verge. “This is something everyone knew was coming down the pike. There have been months to do this, and I don’t think anyone is saying don’t reauthorize at all. The argument is this is an incredibly powerful surveillance tool, and there needs to be guardrails in place to protect people’s constitutional rights.”
