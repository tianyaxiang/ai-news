The 2020 Oregon wildfires burned over 1 million acres, destroyed thousands of structures, and killed 11 people. Smoke from the fires spread across the entire region; this “smoke taint” adversely affected not only local wine production, but also hops grown for beer brewing. Since the Pacific Northwest produces more than 95 percent of US hops and about 40 percent of the global supply, the potential economic impact is significant.

2020年俄勒冈州的野火烧毁了超过100万英亩的土地，摧毁了数千座建筑物，并造成11人死亡。火灾产生的烟雾蔓延至整个地区；这种“烟熏味”不仅对当地的葡萄酒生产产生了不利影响，也影响了用于啤酒酿造的啤酒花。由于太平洋西北地区生产了美国95%以上以及全球约40%的啤酒花，其潜在的经济影响十分巨大。

That’s why Oregon State University (OSU) scientists have been studying how brewers could offset the negative impacts of smoke taint while still incorporating affected hops in their brews. Most recently, they found that it all depends on the quantity of tainted hops that are included and when hops are added during the brewing process, according to a new paper published in the Journal of the American Society of Brewing Chemists.

正因如此，俄勒冈州立大学（OSU）的科学家们一直在研究酿酒商如何在将受影响的啤酒花纳入酿造过程的同时，抵消烟熏味带来的负面影响。根据发表在《美国酿酒化学家协会杂志》上的一篇新论文，他们最近发现，这完全取决于所添加受污染啤酒花的数量以及在酿造过程中添加啤酒花的时间。

As previously reported, the chemistry of brewing beer is a very active area of research. For instance, in 2018, OSU chemist Thomas Shellhammer—who also co-authored this latest paper—identified the growing popularity of “dry hopping” (adding hops after fermentation, common for pale ales) among craft brewers as the likely culprit of the exploding beer bottle phenomenon.

正如之前报道的那样，啤酒酿造化学是一个非常活跃的研究领域。例如，2018年，俄勒冈州立大学的化学家托马斯·谢尔哈默（Thomas Shellhammer）——他也是这篇最新论文的合著者——指出，精酿啤酒商中日益流行的“干投啤酒花”（在发酵后添加啤酒花，常见于淡色艾尔啤酒）很可能是导致啤酒瓶爆炸现象的罪魁祸首。

All beer contains hops, a key flavoring agent that also imparts useful antimicrobial properties. (Beer spoils quickly without them.) Brewers mash and steep grain in hot water, which converts all that starch into sugars. This is traditionally the stage where hops are added to the liquid extract (wort) and boiled to give the beer that hint of bitterness. During the boiling process, a portion of the resins (alpha acids) in hops isomerize into iso-alpha acids. That chemical rearrangement of the molecules is what produces bitterness.

所有的啤酒都含有啤酒花，这是一种关键的调味剂，同时也赋予了啤酒有用的抗菌特性。（没有啤酒花，啤酒很快就会变质。）酿酒商将谷物在热水中浸泡和糖化，将淀粉转化为糖。传统上，这是将啤酒花添加到液体提取物（麦汁）中并煮沸的阶段，以赋予啤酒那种苦味。在煮沸过程中，啤酒花中的部分树脂（α-酸）异构化为异-α-酸。这种分子的化学重排产生了苦味。

Yeast is then added to trigger fermentation, turning the sugars into alcohol. Far from being the chemically inert ingredient brewers have long assumed, hops contain starch enzymes that, under certain conditions, can boost their activity to such a great extent that excessive re-fermentation occurs (“hop-creep”) after bottling. Dry-hopped beers produce significantly higher CO2 levels and packaging pressures, because there is much more re-fermentation going on than usual. And sometimes that pressure builds until the bottle explodes.

随后加入酵母以触发发酵，将糖转化为酒精。啤酒花远非酿酒商长期以来认为的那种化学惰性成分，它含有淀粉酶，在特定条件下，这些酶的活性会大幅增强，导致装瓶后出现过度的二次发酵（即“啤酒花蠕变”）。干投啤酒花的啤酒会产生明显更高的二氧化碳水平和包装压力，因为其发生的二次发酵比通常情况要多得多。有时这种压力会不断积聚，直到瓶子爆炸。

In the wake of the 2020 wildfires, Shellhammer turned his attention to smoke-tainted hops. The primary culprit is volatile phenols (VP), which are produced when the lignin in plant cells is exposed to the high heat of a wildfire. Earlier this year, the OSU team discovered that smoke exposure while the hops are drying increased VP uptake and substantially altered the hops’ aroma; those with higher levels took on meaty, smoky, and artificial BBQ notes; medicinal flavors have also been reported.

2020年野火过后，谢尔哈默将注意力转向了受烟熏污染的啤酒花。主要的罪魁祸首是挥发性酚类物质（VP），当植物细胞中的木质素暴露在野火的高温下时，就会产生这种物质。今年早些时候，俄勒冈州立大学的研究小组发现，啤酒花在干燥过程中接触烟雾会增加VP的吸收，并显著改变啤酒花的香气；VP含量较高的啤酒花带有肉味、烟熏味和人工烧烤味；也有报道称出现了药味。

Those notes were so strong that they overpowered more desirable beery qualities, like citrus notes or aroma intensity. For this latest project, Shellhammer worked with graduate student Cade Jobe to harvest Idaho-grown Cascade hops. Then they “smoked” the hops in kilns using wood pellets injected into the air intake systems, then dried them. They also harvested and dried non-smoked hops as a control.

这些气味非常强烈，以至于掩盖了更受欢迎的啤酒品质，如柑橘味或香气强度。在最新的项目中，谢尔哈默与研究生凯德·乔布（Cade Jobe）合作，收获了爱达荷州种植的卡斯卡特（Cascade）啤酒花。然后，他们通过向进气系统注入木屑颗粒，在窑炉中对啤酒花进行“烟熏”，随后将其干燥。他们还收获并干燥了未受烟熏的啤酒花作为对照。

Next, Shellhammer and Jobe brewed several batches of beer, blending smoke-tainted hops with untainted hops at different levels (25, 50, and 75 percent), along with beer brewed using only tainted hops and beer brewed with no tainted hops. They did this using both a dry-hopping process and one where they added hops during wort boiling. They measured the VP concentrations of each batch of beer with gas chromatography. Finally, a panel of 24 blind tasters evaluated each beer.

接下来，谢尔哈默和乔布酿造了几批啤酒，将受烟熏污染的啤酒花与未受污染的啤酒花以不同比例（25%、50%和75%）混合，同时还酿造了仅使用受污染啤酒花的啤酒和完全不含受污染啤酒花的啤酒。他们分别采用了干投啤酒花工艺和在麦汁煮沸过程中添加啤酒花的工艺。他们利用气相色谱法测量了每批啤酒中的VP浓度。最后，由24名盲测员组成的评审团对每种啤酒进行了评估。

Shellhammer and Jobe found that dry-hopping with smoke-tainted hops resulted in increased VP concentrations; the more tainted hops were included, the higher those concentrations. The tasters reported more intense smoky aromas in those beers as well as meaty, smoky, medicinal, and artificial BBQ flavors. By contrast, when tainted hops were added during the boil, the resulting beer was pretty much indistinguishable from beers brewed with no smoke-tainted hops, even though the former still showed the same increases in VP concentrations.

谢尔哈默和乔布发现，使用受烟熏污染的啤酒花进行干投会导致VP浓度升高；受污染啤酒花的比例越高，浓度就越高。品鉴员报告称，这些啤酒具有更强烈的烟熏香气，以及肉味、烟熏味、药味和人工烧烤味。相比之下，当在煮沸过程中添加受污染的啤酒花时，酿造出的啤酒与未使用受污染啤酒花的啤酒几乎无法区分，尽管前者在VP浓度上同样有所增加。

There was no discernible difference when hops were added during the wort boiling stage. The authors suggest brewers could blend smoke-tainted hops with untainted batches when dry-hopping, keeping the smoke-tainted hops to less than 50 percent. This would still allow brewers to use tainted batches of hops, reducing the economic impact of wildfires on beer production.

当在麦汁煮沸阶段添加啤酒花时，没有明显的区别。作者建议，酿酒商在进行干投时，可以将受烟熏污染的啤酒花与未受污染的批次混合，并将受污染啤酒花的比例控制在50%以下。这仍然允许酿酒商使用受污染的啤酒花批次，从而减少野火对啤酒生产的经济影响。

“Hop growers and brewers are looking for guidance on whether and how to use smoke-affected hops,” said Jobe. “This research begins to provide some practical information to help hop growers and brewers understand how smoky hops impact beer so that they can make the best decisions.”

“啤酒花种植者和酿酒商正在寻求关于是否以及如何使用受烟雾影响的啤酒花的指导，”乔布说。“这项研究开始提供一些实用的信息，帮助种植者和酿酒商了解烟熏啤酒花如何影响啤酒，以便他们能够做出最佳决策。”

There’s still more research to be done to confirm the precise ratios and to replicate these initial findings. Future research should also explore the impact of hops exposed to smoke under field conditions, when the plant is still metabolically active, along with consumer studies to evaluate whether beer drinkers more generally will find the flavors of beers made with blended hops (tainted and untainted) acceptable.

仍需进行更多研究以确认精确的比例并复制这些初步发现。未来的研究还应探讨在田间条件下（即植物仍处于代谢活跃期时）暴露于烟雾中的啤酒花的影响，并进行消费者研究，以评估更广泛的啤酒饮用者是否能接受使用混合啤酒花（受污染和未受污染）酿造的啤酒风味。

The findings may not extend to wine production, however, since the fermentation processes are so different, per the authors. And grapes comprise almost all the raw materials in wine, while hops are a smaller fraction of beer brewing ingredients.

然而，据作者称，这些发现可能无法推广到葡萄酒生产中，因为两者的发酵过程差异巨大。此外，葡萄构成了葡萄酒几乎所有的原材料，而啤酒花只是啤酒酿造原料中的一小部分。
