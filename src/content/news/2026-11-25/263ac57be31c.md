An Amazon delivery drone that clipped a fiber-optic line before crashing onto a garage roof is now the subject of an investigation by the US Federal Aviation Administration. It’s the latest delivery drone mishap caught on video as Amazon and other companies are ramping up such aerial delivery operations across the United States.

一架亚马逊送货无人机在撞上一根光纤线路后坠毁在车库屋顶上，目前正受到美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）的调查。随着亚马逊和其他公司在美国各地扩大此类空中配送业务，这是最新一起被视频记录下来的送货无人机事故。

The November 20 incident began with Jim Brennan and his wife, residents of Huntington Woods, Michigan, placing an order for shampoo and being surprised by a notification about the package arriving by drone within seven minutes, according to WXYZ 7 News Detroit. After the drone dropped the package into Brennan’s backyard, it lost a propeller when it caught on fiber-optic cable and floundered sideways before getting pinned between the neighbor’s garage roof and some power lines. “Like a sick bird, it tried to go up, it tried to get out of here,” Brennan told WXYZ 7.

据底特律 WXYZ 7 新闻报道，这起发生在 11 月 20 日的事件始于密歇根州亨廷顿伍兹的居民吉姆·布伦南（Jim Brennan）和他的妻子订购了洗发水，并惊讶地收到通知称包裹将在七分钟内由无人机送达。无人机将包裹投放到布伦南的后院后，因挂到光纤电缆而掉落了一个螺旋桨，随后侧向挣扎，最终被卡在邻居的车库屋顶和几根电线之间。“它就像一只生病的鸟，试图飞起来，试图离开这里，”布伦南告诉 WXYZ 7。

Amazon Prime Air currently operates a fleet of battery-powered MK30 drones that can deliver packages weighing up to five pounds and have onboard sense-and-avoid technology to avoid obstacles. However, the drone clearly failed to avoid the thin fiber-optic cable in this case—so hopefully the FAA investigation would take that into account along with Amazon delivery drone procedures.

亚马逊 Prime Air 目前运营着一支电池驱动的 MK30 无人机机队，可以运送重达 5 磅的包裹，并配备了用于避障的机载感知与规避技术。然而，这架无人机显然没能避开那根细细的光纤电缆，因此希望 FAA 的调查能将这一点以及亚马逊的送货无人机操作流程纳入考量。

The FAA preliminary accident and incident notification briefly describes the incident as “UAS struck a fiber cable, a residential garage, and became entangled in power lines during takeoff.” That notice was posted on November 23. The National Transportation Safety Board is also gathering information about the incident but has not yet decided whether to open its own probe, according to CNBC. That US government agency holds primary responsibility for investigating aviation accidents and other transportation incidents.

FAA 的初步事故和事件通知简要描述称：“无人机系统（UAS）在起飞过程中撞击了光纤电缆和住宅车库，并缠绕在电线上。”该通知于 11 月 23 日发布。据 CNBC 报道，美国国家运输安全委员会（NTSB）也在收集有关该事件的信息，但尚未决定是否展开独立调查。该美国政府机构主要负责调查航空事故及其他交通事件。

Logan Coomes, the neighbor whose garage was struck by the drone, described an Amazon Prime Air team arriving within 10 minutes and coordinating with Detroit Edison workers to eventually remove the drone by early evening, in an interview with WXYZ 7. The Amazon Prime Air team also reportedly told Coomes that the company would help with repairs to his garage roof, and gave the homeowner a $100 gift card on the spot.

在接受 WXYZ 7 采访时，车库被无人机撞击的邻居洛根·库姆斯（Logan Coomes）描述称，亚马逊 Prime Air 团队在 10 分钟内赶到现场，并与底特律爱迪生电力公司的工人协调，最终在傍晚时分移除了无人机。据报道，亚马逊 Prime Air 团队还告诉库姆斯，公司将协助维修他的车库屋顶，并当场给了这位房主一张 100 美元的礼品卡。

Amazon claimed that this was the first such delivery drone crash from its operations near Detroit. However, this would also add to the handful of Amazon drone incidents already under investigation by the US government. In October 2025, two Amazon delivery drones crashed into a construction crane in Tolleson, Arizona, while another delivery drone downed an Internet cable in Waco, Texas, the following month. The FAA has also been investigating an Amazon Prime Air drone crash involving an apartment building in Richardson, Texas, that occurred in February 2026.

亚马逊声称这是其在底特律附近运营中首次发生此类送货无人机坠毁事件。然而，这也增加了美国政府目前正在调查的几起亚马逊无人机事故的数量。2025 年 10 月，两架亚马逊送货无人机在亚利桑那州托勒森撞上了一台建筑起重机，次月，另一架送货无人机在德克萨斯州韦科撞断了一根互联网电缆。FAA 此前也一直在调查 2026 年 2 月发生在德克萨斯州理查森的一起涉及公寓楼的亚马逊 Prime Air 无人机坠毁事件。

In response to the latest crash in Michigan, an Amazon spokesperson described Prime Air drones as making nearly 6,000 flights per day and already having delivered “hundreds of thousands of packages this year” in a statement shared with CNN. Amazon has been growing its Prime Air service with the goal of having delivery drones reach nearly 500 cities and towns across the United States by the end of 2026.

针对密歇根州发生的最新坠机事件，亚马逊发言人在分享给 CNN 的一份声明中表示，Prime Air 无人机每天飞行近 6,000 次，今年已经运送了“数十万个包裹”。亚马逊一直在扩大其 Prime Air 服务，目标是在 2026 年底前让送货无人机覆盖美国近 500 个城镇。

The company has described delivery drones as enabling faster deliveries through potentially lower-emission and lower-cost operations compared with relying solely on ground vehicles. But the growth of delivery drone services has also drawn noise complaints from people living in neighborhoods where drones operate. As Sean Gallagher has written for Ars, the constant buzz of drones could even trigger post-traumatic stress disorder or other anxiety-related disorders—especially for people who have directly or indirectly experienced some aspect of modern drone warfare.

该公司称，与仅依靠地面车辆相比，送货无人机能够通过潜在的低排放和低成本运营实现更快的配送。但送货无人机服务的增长也引发了无人机运营区域居民的噪音投诉。正如肖恩·加拉格尔（Sean Gallagher）为 Ars 所写的那样，无人机持续的嗡嗡声甚至可能引发创伤后应激障碍或其他与焦虑相关的疾病——尤其是对于那些直接或间接经历过现代无人机战争某些方面的人来说。
