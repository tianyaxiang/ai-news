Two of NASA’s four missions operating at Mars have suspended science operations after encountering separate technical issues in recent weeks. The missions affected by the outages—hopefully temporary—are NASA’s Perseverance rover and Odyssey orbiter. Both are operating beyond their original design lifetimes. Perseverance is in its sixth year exploring the surface of Mars, having collected rock and soil samples for eventual return to Earth. The rover is now studying the rim of a crater that once contained an ancient lake of liquid water. Odyssey arrived in orbit around Mars in 2001, and is the longest-lived Mars mission in history.

在过去几周里，美国国家航空航天局（NASA）在火星上运行的四个任务中有两个因遇到各自的技术问题而暂停了科学作业。受此次中断影响的任务包括“毅力号”火星车和“奥德赛”轨道飞行器，希望这只是暂时的。这两个任务的运行时间都已经超过了最初的设计寿命。“毅力号”在火星表面探索已进入第六个年头，并收集了岩石和土壤样本，以备将来送回地球。该火星车目前正在研究一个曾经拥有古老液态水湖泊的陨石坑边缘。“奥德赛”号于2001年抵达火星轨道，是历史上寿命最长的火星任务。

The problem on Perseverance stems from the rover’s communications system. The rover has two redundant UHF radios for high-rate communications with a fleet of orbiters that relay data to Earth. A separate X-band system is available for direct-to-Earth communications at a lower data rate. One of the rover’s two UHF radios was unresponsive when it powered up on October 30, triggering an automatic switch to the backup radio and placing the rover in safe mode. Ground teams at NASA’s Jet Propulsion Laboratory in California later discovered the backup UHF radio was transmitting at about half of its expected power output, NASA said in a statement last week.

“毅力号”的问题源于其通信系统。该火星车拥有两台冗余的超高频（UHF）无线电，用于与向地球中继数据的轨道飞行器群进行高速通信。此外，还有一个独立的X波段系统可用于以较低的数据速率直接与地球通信。10月30日，“毅力号”在启动时，两台UHF无线电中的一台没有响应，触发了自动切换到备用无线电的机制，并使火星车进入了安全模式。NASA在上周的一份声明中表示，位于加州的喷气推进实验室（JPL）的地面团队后来发现，备用UHF无线电的发射功率仅为预期的一半左右。

The situation with the Odyssey orbiter seems more dire. One of the four reaction wheels used for pointing the spacecraft stopped spinning in early November, leaving Odyssey with two good wheels after the first one failed in 2013. The orbiter needs at least three wheels to keep it oriented correctly. “Without the three reaction wheels, the spacecraft must rely solely on its hydrazine thrusters to remain oriented,” NASA said. The orbiter requires stable pointing to turn its solar arrays toward the Sun and its science instruments toward Mars.

“奥德赛”轨道飞行器的情况似乎更为严峻。11月初，用于调整航天器指向的四个反作用轮中的一个停止了转动，继2013年第一个反作用轮失效后，这使得“奥德赛”号目前只剩下两个完好的轮子。该轨道飞行器至少需要三个轮子才能保持正确的姿态。NASA表示：“如果没有这三个反作用轮，航天器必须完全依靠其联氨推进器来保持姿态。”该轨道飞行器需要稳定的指向，以便将其太阳能电池阵列对准太阳，并将科学仪器对准火星。

Odyssey is one of four orbiters NASA uses to relay data between Earth and the Perseverance and Curiosity rovers on the Martian surface. Curiosity is still driving and collecting data after 14 years on Mars. The other three relay craft are NASA’s Mars Reconnaissance Orbiter, which reached the red planet in 2006, the European Space Agency’s 10-year-old ExoMars Trace Gas Orbiter, and ESA’s 23-year-old Mars Express spacecraft. NASA lost contact with its MAVEN orbiter last December after 11 years of operations at Mars when the spacecraft suddenly spun out of control.

“奥德赛”号是NASA用于在地球与火星表面的“毅力号”和“好奇号”火星车之间中继数据的四颗轨道飞行器之一。“好奇号”在火星上运行14年后，仍在行驶并收集数据。另外三颗中继飞行器分别是2006年抵达火星的NASA“火星勘测轨道飞行器”、欧洲航天局运行10年的“ExoMars微量气体轨道飞行器”，以及欧空局运行23年的“火星快车”号。去年12月，NASA的MAVEN轨道飞行器在火星运行11年后，因航天器突然失控旋转而失去联系。

NASA is preparing to send reinforcements to renew the agency’s presence at Mars. In September, NASA awarded Blue Origin a $700 million contract for the Mars Telecommunications Network (MTN), an orbiter dedicated to providing high-bandwidth connectivity between Earth and other missions operating on and around the red planet. NASA wanted Blue Origin to complete the telecom orbiter by the end of 2028 and have it operational at Mars in 2030. But the Mars Telecommunications Network is on hold after Rocket Lab, which lost its bid to win the MTN contract, filed a protest with the Government Accountability Office.

NASA正准备派遣增援力量，以更新其在火星的存在。9月，NASA授予蓝色起源公司一份价值7亿美元的合同，用于建设“火星电信网络”（MTN），这是一颗专门用于在地球与在火星及其周围运行的其他任务之间提供高带宽连接的轨道飞行器。NASA希望蓝色起源公司在2028年底前完成该电信轨道飞行器，并于2030年在火星投入使用。但由于在MTN合同竞标中落败的火箭实验室公司向政府问责局（GAO）提出抗议，该项目目前处于搁置状态。
