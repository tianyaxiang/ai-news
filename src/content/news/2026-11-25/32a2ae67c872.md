# 11 Things Jensen Huang Actually Said at His First All-Hands Back at Nvidia

Jensen Huang is going to therapy for 90 minutes a day and rethinking his management style, and he says that violating Nvidia’s code of conduct isn’t necessarily a fireable offense. That’s just some of what the newly returned Nvidia CEO told staff during his first all-hands meeting on Tuesday.

黄仁勋每天进行90分钟的心理治疗，并正在重新思考他的管理风格。他表示，违反英伟达的行为准则并不一定会被解雇。这只是这位刚回归的英伟达首席执行官在周二的首次全员大会上对员工所说的一部分内容。

Here are some of the most striking things Huang said during the meeting, according to remarks reviewed by WIRED. Nvidia did not immediately respond to a request for comment.

根据《连线》（WIRED）审阅的发言记录，以下是黄仁勋在会议期间提到的一些最引人注目的言论。英伟达未立即回应置评请求。

On self-improvement: “I go to therapy every day for an hour and a half. And it’s been helping me a lot.”

关于自我提升：“我每天进行一个半小时的心理治疗，这对我帮助很大。”

On accountability: “There are rumors on the internet that apparently I’m a tech bro, and therefore I’m back. Apparently, I’m a tech bro, and because I wear a super nice jacket, that I’m above the law, and I’m above the code of conduct. That is not true. It won’t be tolerated, and it wasn’t tolerated here.”

关于问责制：“互联网上有传言说我显然是个‘科技兄弟’（tech bro），所以我才回归。显然，因为我穿了一件很棒的夹克，我就凌驾于法律之上，凌驾于行为准则之上。这不是真的。这种行为不会被容忍，过去在这里也没有被容忍过。”

“If one of you were to violate a code of conduct, what does that mean? If you were ever to violate a code of conduct, it is not appropriate. I just wanted you to know, it is not right, it's not appropriate, it won’t be tolerated. But ‘it won’t be tolerated’ does not mean the moment you violate a code of conduct, we walk you out the door. Facts, circumstances, a lot of assessments. And your management team will still make the decision. That is what is in the best interest of the company and its employees, with all the facts and circumstances.”

“如果你们中的某个人违反了行为准则，这意味着什么？如果你违反了行为准则，这是不合适的。我只是想让你们知道，这不对，不合适，也不会被容忍。但‘不会被容忍’并不意味着一旦你违反了准则，我们就会立刻让你走人。这需要考量事实、环境以及大量的评估。你的管理团队仍会做出决定。在考量所有事实和情况后，这才是符合公司及其员工最大利益的做法。”

On his management style: “The intensity I bring elevates everybody else. But I can go too far. When I go too far, it hurts people, it belittles them, it disempowers them. And that’s not right. And so I need to go figure out how I’m going to bring the intensity, the crazy intensity, to the field.”

关于他的管理风格：“我带来的强度提升了其他所有人的水平。但我可能会做得过火。当我做得过火时，会伤害到他人，贬低他们，削弱他们的能力。这不对。所以我需要去思考如何将这种强度，这种疯狂的强度，带到实际工作中去。”

On women at Nvidia: “I believe we need to create and continue to sustain a safe environment for all of our women. And if I did anything that put that in jeopardy, I cannot tell you how sad I am, and I’ll do everything I can to fix it. We will not tolerate that.”

关于英伟达的女性员工：“我相信我们需要为所有的女性员工创造并持续维持一个安全的环境。如果我做了任何危及这一点的事情，我无法表达我有多难过，我会尽我所能去弥补。我们绝不容忍这种情况。”

On succession: “All day long, this company can run autonomously, and you saw it. I promise you, whether I’m here or not, this company is wired up to execute, execute, and execute, and deliver on financial results.”

关于继任：“这家公司整天都可以自主运行，你们也看到了。我向你们保证，无论我是否在这里，这家公司都已经准备好去执行、执行、再执行，并实现财务业绩。”

“I also didn’t believe in succession planning. Let that sink in. I don’t believe in succession planning. I think it’s flawed. The idea of succession planning is not flawed. What people think succession planning means is flawed.”

“我也不相信继任计划。请仔细想想。我不相信继任计划。我认为它是有缺陷的。继任计划这个概念本身没有错，但人们对继任计划含义的理解是有缺陷的。”

“There’s a reason why we don’t have succession planning; it’s not because we are unaware of the word. I’m the longest-running tech CEO in history.”

“我们没有继任计划是有原因的；并不是因为我们不知道这个词。我是历史上任职时间最长的科技公司首席执行官。”

On politics: “There [were] also rumors that apparently I’m back because the highest levels of our government wanted me back. That is also completely untrue. Our board of directors performed their duty as they should have.”

关于政治：“还有传言说我回归是因为我们政府的最高层希望我回来。这也是完全不真实的。我们的董事会履行了他们应尽的职责。”

“It is absolutely true that President Trump really, really loves our company,” Huang told employees. “He gloats about Nvidia. You know he does. But there’s a question about politics. Are we now political? Are we partisan? I wanna be very clear with you. President Trump is the United States president. He is my president. And I’ll do everything I can to help this president be informed about how he could make decisions that are in the best interest of our country … We have to do it, and whoever the next president is will be my president, and I will do exactly the same.”

“特朗普总统确实非常、非常喜欢我们公司，”黄仁勋告诉员工。“他以英伟达为傲。你们知道他会的。但这里有一个关于政治的问题。我们现在变得政治化了吗？我们有党派倾向吗？我想向你们明确一点。特朗普总统是美国总统。他是我的总统。我会尽我所能帮助这位总统了解如何做出符合我们国家最大利益的决策……我们必须这样做，无论下一任总统是谁，他都将是我的总统，我也会做同样的事情。”

“Don’t think for a second that politics ever plays a role in this company, not even a little bit. We are the most apolitical company. I don’t love that we talk about politics here. Take it home. I love that we talk about work here.”

“千万不要以为政治在这家公司发挥了任何作用，哪怕是一点点。我们是最不关心政治的公司。我不喜欢我们在这里谈论政治。把这些带回家吧。我喜欢我们在这里谈论工作。”
