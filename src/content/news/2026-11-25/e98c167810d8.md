The best espresso machines for home must thread a delicate needle. Espresso is forceful and finicky, and a little intimidating, and yet so essential that astronauts might take it into orbit. A home espresso machine remains the most intense and expensive device I regularly operate before my first cup of coffee.

家用最佳意式咖啡机必须在微妙的平衡中寻找出路。意式浓缩咖啡制作过程既强力又挑剔，甚至让人有些望而生畏，但它又是如此重要，以至于宇航员都可能将其带入轨道。对我而言，家用咖啡机依然是我在喝第一杯咖啡前操作的最复杂、最昂贵的设备。

I tend to favor devices that let technology do much of the work and don’t make you join a cult of engineers before your first cup. And so my top-rated espresso machine, the Fellow Series 1, earned its place on WIRED’s counter by combining ease of use with a solid build, consistent temperature, and rare control over pressure—an industry-beating mix of nerdiness and hand-holding friendliness. For a more classic, all-analog build, we offer the compact, stylish Diletta Mio.

我倾向于那些能让技术代劳大部分工作，且无需你在喝第一杯咖啡前先成为“工程师”的设备。因此，我评级最高的 Fellow Series 1 咖啡机凭借其易用性、坚固的结构、稳定的温度以及罕见的压力控制功能，在 WIRED 的测试台占据了一席之地——它完美融合了极客精神与贴心的易用性，在行业内首屈一指。若追求更经典、全模拟的结构，我们推荐紧凑且时尚的 Diletta Mio。

But I know not everyone wants to also buy a good coffee grinder or spend four figures for their morning cup. For those who want milk-based drinks and a built-in grinder, I recommend the top-selling Breville Barista Express as the Goldilocks machine balancing performance and cost. For an entry-level device, I pretty much always recommend the straight-ahead Breville Bambino, among the lowest-cost machines with PID-controlled temperature.

但我知道并非每个人都想额外购买磨豆机或为早晨的一杯咖啡花费四位数。对于那些想要制作奶咖且需要内置磨豆机的用户，我推荐销量领先的 Breville Barista Express，它是平衡性能与成本的“金发姑娘”式选择。对于入门级设备，我几乎总是推荐简单直接的 Breville Bambino，它是具备 PID 温度控制功能且价格最低的机型之一。

San Francisco-based Fellow is almost certainly the most innovative and tech-forward coffee machine company of the moment. And rarely has an espresso machine so swiftly changed what I want from the best espresso makers as the Fellow Series 1, which started shipping in early 2026. Sure, the temperature control is admirable, with a patented heating technology that threads the needle between the fast heating of a thermoblock and the stability of a boiler. Automatic milk frothing is about as good and as fast as it gets. The dial and LED-screen controls are intuitive. And the device is compact, conserving both counter space and airspace.

总部位于旧金山的 Fellow 无疑是当下最具创新精神和技术前瞻性的咖啡机公司。很少有咖啡机能像 2026 年初开始发货的 Fellow Series 1 那样，如此迅速地改变我对顶级意式咖啡机的期待。它的温度控制令人赞叹，其专利加热技术在加热块的快速加热与锅炉的稳定性之间找到了完美的平衡。自动打奶泡功能既出色又迅速。旋钮和 LED 屏幕控制直观易用。此外，该设备结构紧凑，节省了台面空间和上方空间。

But the real game-changing addition with this machine is the way Fellow has introduced tight pressure control and shot profiles to the non-geek home espresso landscape. Almost every other espresso machine on the market just sorta aims at 9 bars of pressure and shrugs. The Series 1 offers profiles for light, medium, or dark roast that change pressure based on what's right for each bean—keeping dark roasts classic but pulling longer shots and dialing back pressure on light-roast beans. Other profiles, like turbo shots or “Modern Arc,” cater to the modern yen for espresso with less bitterness.

但这款机器真正改变游戏规则的地方在于，Fellow 将精准的压力控制和萃取方案引入了非极客的家用咖啡领域。市面上几乎所有其他咖啡机都只是瞄准 9 巴压力，然后就草草了事。Series 1 针对浅度、中度或深度烘焙提供了不同的方案，根据每种豆子的特性调整压力——在保持深度烘焙经典口感的同时，通过延长萃取时间并降低压力来处理浅度烘焙豆。其他方案，如“涡轮萃取”或“现代弧线”，则迎合了现代人对低苦味意式浓缩咖啡的追求。

After testing for weeks, I'm sold. You'd pay at least double to find this feature on any other espresso machine. And no device lets you play with pressure as easily as the Fellow, even before a planned firmware update that'll allow you to sync profiles through Fellow's phone app. The app remains mostly a placeholder for now, offering tutorials and education but TBD functionality. But Fellow's track record building out the app on my top-pick drip coffee machine, the Fellow Aiden, gives me confidence this is a temporary blip. Volumetric controls still need tweaking, and the removable water reservoir can only be removed straight up from the device, requiring significant clearance.

经过数周的测试，我被彻底折服了。在其他任何咖啡机上，你至少要花双倍的价格才能获得这些功能。没有哪款设备能像 Fellow 这样让你轻松玩转压力，即便是在计划中的固件更新（允许通过手机 App 同步方案）发布之前。目前该 App 主要还是个占位符，提供教程和知识，但功能尚待完善。不过，鉴于 Fellow 在我首选的滴滤咖啡机 Fellow Aiden 上展现出的 App 开发能力，我相信这只是暂时的不足。容量控制仍需微调，且可拆卸水箱只能从设备上方垂直取出，需要预留足够的空间。

But even with these early quirks, the Fellow Series 1 remains the most impressive espresso machine I've tested in the past couple of years. And it's changed what I want from espresso machines in the future. The Series 1 might already be the most-hyped machine of 2026. But this time, the hype is warranted.

尽管存在这些早期的小瑕疵，Fellow Series 1 依然是我过去几年测试过的最令人印象深刻的意式咖啡机。它改变了我对未来咖啡机的期待。Series 1 可能已经是 2026 年最受热捧的机器，但这一次，这种热捧是名副其实的。
