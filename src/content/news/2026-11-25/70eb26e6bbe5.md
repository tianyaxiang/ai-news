# Microsoft's historic cloud outage exposed an engineering “fog of war”

According to internal memos reviewed by Reuters, Microsoft has declared a “Code Red” overhaul of its engineering response practices after the November 12 outage that lost access to Exchange Online, Teams, and Outlook for users worldwide. Per the memos, Microsoft 365 Core President Jason Henderson described the outage as having the most customer impact in the history of Exchange Online.

根据路透社查阅的内部备忘录，在 11 月 12 日导致全球用户无法访问 Exchange Online、Teams 和 Outlook 的宕机事件后，微软宣布对其工程响应流程进行“红色代码”级别的全面改革。根据备忘录，微软 365 核心业务总裁 Jason Henderson 称，此次宕机是 Exchange Online 历史上对客户影响最大的一次事件。

In a separate memo, Henderson wrote that an urgent production fix in a recent incident took five to six days to reach worldwide saturation. Microsoft’s target for its most urgent security fixes is to move from detection to safe worldwide deployment in one hour. “Needless to say, we aren’t here yet,” Henderson wrote.

在另一份备忘录中，Henderson 写道，近期一次事件中的紧急生产修复程序花费了五到六天时间才在全球范围内完成部署。微软针对最紧急安全修复的目标是实现从检测到全球安全部署仅需一小时。Henderson 写道：“不用说，我们目前还未达到这一目标。”

The memos, which Reuters reviewed but Microsoft has not published, outline the response to the November 12 disruption. The company’s post-incident report, dated November 20, attributed the downtime to an undetected Windows scalability defect introduced in July, which caused lock contention in the gateway processes. A Teams traffic failover then dumped additional load onto infrastructure already under stress, as maintenance had left nearly 14 percent of gateway server capacity offline at the time of the incident.

这些路透社查阅但微软尚未公布的备忘录概述了对 11 月 12 日中断事件的响应情况。该公司日期为 11 月 20 日的事后报告将此次停机归咎于 7 月引入的一个未被发现的 Windows 可扩展性缺陷，该缺陷导致了网关进程中的锁竞争。随后，Teams 的流量故障转移将额外负载倾倒到了本已处于压力下的基础设施上，因为在事件发生时，维护工作已导致近 14% 的网关服务器容量处于离线状态。

Amanda Silver, a corporate vice president overseeing Microsoft 365 Core and Work IQ, wrote that engineers had to contend with a “fog of war” as the outage unfolded. According to Silver, responders exchanged more than 11,000 messages across 11 Teams chats, leaving diagnostic data scattered, and a full view of the incident fractured. Silver’s memo also quotes Charles Lamanna, executive vice president of Copilot, Agents and Platform, telling a Code Red review that the affected component had accumulated “roughly ten years’ worth of acceptable failures.”

负责微软 365 核心业务和 Work IQ 的公司副总裁 Amanda Silver 写道，在宕机发生时，工程师们不得不面对“战争迷雾”。据 Silver 称，响应人员在 11 个 Teams 聊天群组中交换了超过 11,000 条消息，导致诊断数据分散，对事件的整体把控支离破碎。Silver 的备忘录还引用了 Copilot、代理和平台执行副总裁 Charles Lamanna 在一次“红色代码”审查会议上的话，称受影响的组件已经积累了“大约十年份的可接受故障”。

Microsoft has said the outage was not related to any cyberattack or malicious activity, and the defects were not caused by AI tooling. As part of the Code Red overhaul, Silver’s memo outlines five workstreams focused on load shedding, geographic traffic partitioning, and faster recovery mechanisms. According to Reuters, Microsoft plans to improve its incident response and patching timelines by deploying its own AI agents to help engineers diagnose system health data more quickly.

微软表示，此次宕机与任何网络攻击或恶意活动无关，且这些缺陷并非由人工智能工具引起。作为“红色代码”改革的一部分，Silver 的备忘录概述了五个工作流，重点关注负载削减、地理流量分区和更快的恢复机制。据路透社报道，微软计划通过部署自己的人工智能代理来帮助工程师更快地诊断系统健康数据，从而改善其事件响应和补丁修复时间表。

Henderson said that he will hold daily meetings with the Substrate leadership team, which oversees the engineering team responsible for Microsoft 365 services. Microsoft has set targets of one-hour worldwide resolution in major incidents and a one-hour deployment window for urgent security fixes. It hasn’t said when it hopes to meet them, or how future response efforts will avoid 11 separate Teams chats and the “fog of war.”

Henderson 表示，他将与负责微软 365 服务的工程团队的 Substrate 领导团队举行每日会议。微软已设定了重大事件一小时内全球解决以及紧急安全修复一小时内部署完成的目标。该公司尚未说明希望何时实现这些目标，也未说明未来的响应工作将如何避免出现 11 个独立 Teams 聊天群组和“战争迷雾”的情况。
