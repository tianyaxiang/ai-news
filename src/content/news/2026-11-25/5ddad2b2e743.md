This post is about one design question: if the player takes their hands off after pressing FIGHT, what makes the result feel fair? A note on how it was made: the game was built with AI coding tools, directed and checked by our team at Ant Games. It is free, there is no pay to win, there is no sign-up, and it plays in the browser with nothing to install.

在浏览器中免费游玩《WARSHIP WORKSHOP》。这是一款 3D 太空自动战斗游戏：你用零件组装一艘飞船，按下“战斗”（FIGHT）按钮，然后看着它独自与 AI 对手交战。本文探讨了一个设计问题：如果玩家在按下“战斗”后就撒手不管，是什么让结果显得公平？关于制作过程的说明：该游戏由 Ant Games 团队指导并检查，使用 AI 编码工具构建。游戏完全免费，没有“付费获胜”机制，无需注册，直接在浏览器中运行，无需安装。

The problem with "the computer fought for me" In an auto battler the player decides nothing during the fight. That is the appeal, and also the risk. If a ship loses and the player cannot say why, the game feels like a slot machine. So we set one goal: every loss must be explainable from the build alone. We did that with four choices: a short list of rules, a visible limit on both sides, a fight you can take apart, and rivals built to your budget.

“电脑替我战斗”的问题：在自动战斗游戏中，玩家在战斗过程中无法做出任何决定。这既是它的魅力所在，也是它的风险所在。如果飞船输了而玩家说不出原因，游戏就会感觉像是一台老虎机。因此，我们设定了一个目标：每一次失败都必须能仅从飞船的构造中找到解释。我们通过四个选择实现了这一点：简短的规则列表、双方可见的限制、可拆解的战斗过程，以及根据你的预算构建的对手。

Choice 1: three rules the player can say out loud The whole fight rule set fits in three sentences: A ship with no reactor is out. A ship with no working gun is out. At 90 seconds, the less damaged ship wins. A player can check a build against that before pressing FIGHT. The first two rules catch the classic mistake (a gorgeous ship that cannot shoot), and the third rule means a fight always ends. Nothing in the rules asks for a hidden stat.

选择 1：玩家可以大声说出的三条规则。整套战斗规则可以用三句话概括：没有反应堆的飞船出局；没有可用火炮的飞船出局；90 秒时，受损较轻的飞船获胜。玩家可以在按下“战斗”前根据这些规则检查自己的构造。前两条规则可以规避经典错误（一艘华丽但无法射击的飞船），第三条规则确保战斗总会结束。规则中没有任何隐藏属性的要求。

Choice 2: two bars, shown before the fight Free-form building needs limits, or the best ship is just the biggest one. Two bars do the job: BUDGET and POWER. You stay inside both, and the workshop shows them while you build. Because both sides are held to a budget, the fight tests the design, not the size of the wallet. The game also never sells anything. Credits come from fighting and unlock parts and hulls, so a stronger option is something you earn by playing, not something you buy.

选择 2：战斗前显示的两个进度条。自由建造需要限制，否则最强的飞船只会是最大的那一个。两个进度条起到了作用：预算（BUDGET）和动力（POWER）。你必须保持在两者范围内，工作坊会在你建造时显示它们。由于双方都受到预算限制，战斗考验的是设计，而不是钱包的大小。此外，游戏从不出售任何东西。积分来自战斗，用于解锁零件和船体，因此更强的选项是通过游玩获得的，而不是买来的。

Choice 3: rivals built to your budget, with the line shown Before each fight you can look at the enemy ship and its budget line, then build a ship that can beat it. The AI rivals are built to your budget, and that line is on every match. This matters for fairness in two ways. If a loss comes from being outspent, you can see that on the line. If it comes from a poor layout, you know that too, and layout is the thing you control. Six ranked tiers run from a budget of 440 up to 5,200, so the same idea scales from small ships to large ones.

选择 3：根据你的预算构建的对手，并显示预算线。在每场战斗前，你可以查看敌方飞船及其预算线，然后建造一艘能击败它的飞船。AI 对手是根据你的预算构建的，每场比赛都会显示这条线。这在两个方面对公平性至关重要：如果失败是因为预算不足，你可以在线上看出来；如果是因为布局不佳，你也能知道，而布局正是你可以控制的部分。六个排名等级的预算从 440 到 5,200 不等，因此同样的理念适用于从小飞船到大飞船的各种情况。

Choice 4: a fight you can take apart A fair result is one you can inspect. So the fight is not a movie: Pause at any time and step through every part on both ships. Replay any fight after it ends, to see the moment it turned. LAST FIGHT HITS shows where your ship was hit, so you know what to armour. Speed keys (1, 2 and 4) let you run a fight faster or slower; a fight lasts at most 90 seconds. A live flight course line in the workshop shows where your ship will go before it does. Together these turn a loss into a to-do list: move the engine, add armour on the side that was hit, or turn a gun so it has a clear shot.

选择 4：可拆解的战斗过程。公平的结果是可以检查的结果。所以战斗不是电影：你可以随时暂停并逐一查看双方飞船的每个零件。战斗结束后可以重播，查看局势扭转的瞬间。“最后战斗命中”（LAST FIGHT HITS）功能会显示你的飞船哪里被击中，让你知道该加固哪里。速度键（1、2 和 4）可以让你加快或减慢战斗速度；战斗最多持续 90 秒。工作坊中的实时飞行路线显示了飞船在移动前的轨迹。这些功能将失败变成了一份待办事项清单：移动引擎、在被击中的一侧增加装甲，或者调整火炮角度以获得清晰的射击视野。

What the player is allowed to decide It helps to be strict about this. Before the fight, the player decides everything: the hull outline (SHAPE mode lets you paint or cut cells, with mirror and undo), which of 500 parts go on it, and which way guns, shields and engines point. After FIGHT, they decide nothing. Because the line is that clear, any win or loss belongs to the build, and the player knows exactly what to change. AUTO fights let you run builds with parts you already own, without playing each match by hand, which is a quick way to test a change. You can save up to 30 ships per tier in MY SHIPS and compare versions.

玩家可以决定的内容：对此保持严格是有帮助的。在战斗前，玩家决定一切：船体轮廓（SHAPE 模式允许你绘制或切割单元格，并支持镜像和撤销）、500 个零件中哪些被使用，以及火炮、护盾和引擎的朝向。按下“战斗”后，玩家不再做任何决定。由于界限如此清晰，任何胜负都归因于构造，玩家确切地知道该修改什么。“自动战斗”（AUTO fights）允许你使用已拥有的零件运行构造，无需手动操作每场比赛，这是测试修改的快捷方式。你可以在“我的飞船”（MY SHIPS）中为每个等级保存最多 30 艘飞船并比较不同版本。

What we would tell another auto battler maker Keep the rules short enough to say in three sentences. Show the limits on both sides before the fight, not after it. Give the loser tools to read the fight: pause, step, replay, and a record of hits. Sell nothing that changes the result. Let the player earn options by playing. An auto battler does not need the player to act in the fight. It needs the player to believe the fight was decided by their choices, and every piece above exists for that.

我们会给其他自动战斗游戏制作者的建议：保持规则简短，三句话内能说完。在战斗前而不是战斗后显示双方的限制。给失败者提供解读战斗的工具：暂停、单步、重播和命中记录。不要出售任何会改变结果的东西。让玩家通过游玩获得选项。自动战斗游戏不需要玩家在战斗中操作。它需要玩家相信战斗是由他们的选择决定的，而上述所有内容都是为了实现这一点。
