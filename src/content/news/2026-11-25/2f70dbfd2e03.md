# The Verge’s 2026 gift guide for dads

《The Verge》2026 年父亲节礼物指南

This year, why not get Dad a gift or two he won’t expect? Gone are the days when you could just buy your father a random tie or some leather-clad alcohol vessel or a shaving kit. Dads want — nay, deserve — something memorable. Luckily for you, it’s not that hard to find something special that he’ll approve of, and we’re here to guide you.

今年，何不送给父亲一两件他意想不到的礼物呢？那种随手买条领带、皮质酒具或剃须套装的日子已经一去不复返了。父亲们想要——甚至可以说值得拥有——一些令人难忘的东西。幸运的是，找到一件他会认可的特别礼物并不难，而我们正是来为你提供指南的。

We’ve curated this year’s gift list with the modern father in mind, whether he’s a gamer, creator, chef, or simply a gadget lover. There’s a lot in here we think he’ll like, from noise-canceling headphones like the Sonos Ace Ultra and an autonomous drone to a brilliant QD-OLED monitor and a nifty light that’s perfect for illuminating his next campsite. Whatever your dad is into, we’ve got you covered.

我们为今年的礼物清单注入了“现代父亲”的理念，无论他是游戏玩家、创作者、厨师，还是单纯的数码爱好者。我们相信这里有很多他会喜欢的东西，从 Sonos Ace Ultra 等降噪耳机和自动无人机，到出色的 QD-OLED 显示器，以及非常适合照亮他下一次露营地的精巧灯具。无论你的父亲喜欢什么，我们都能满足你的需求。

Dyson PencilVac Fluffycones: Cleaning gadgets like Dyson’s PencilVac FluffyCones are never cheap, but they’re often worth the cost and a pleasure to use. The lightweight, broom-replacing stick vacuum doesn’t do carpets, but its rolling bars pack incredible cleaning power for touching up hard floors, including stairs.

Dyson PencilVac Fluffycones：像 Dyson PencilVac FluffyCones 这样的清洁小工具从来都不便宜，但它们通常物有所值，使用起来也是一种享受。这款轻便的吸尘器可以替代扫帚，虽然不适用于地毯，但其滚轴在清理硬质地板（包括楼梯）时具有令人难以置信的清洁能力。

Vampire Tools Multipurpose Screw Extractor Pliers (6.25-inch): Give the gift that fixes past home improvement transgressions. These handy Japanese-made pliers from Vampire Tools have one fundamental purpose — to remove stripped screws — and they’re damn good at it. The best part? Watching Dad’s face light up seconds before he sprints to the garage for them.

Vampire Tools 多功能螺丝取出钳（6.25 英寸）：送上一份能修复过去家居装修失误的礼物。这款由 Vampire Tools 出品的日本制造实用钳子有一个基本用途——取出滑丝的螺丝——而且效果极佳。最棒的部分是什么？看着父亲在冲向车库去使用它们之前，脸上露出的兴奋神情。

Peak Design City Backpack (15L): Who would have thought a simple hook could make roaming the city with a backpack so much more convenient? After testing Peak Design’s well-designed bags, we’re convinced the clever addition is a game-changer for anyone who doesn’t want to leave their bag on the filthy ground.

Peak Design 城市背包（15L）：谁能想到一个简单的挂钩就能让背着背包在城市中漫步变得如此方便？在测试了 Peak Design 设计精良的包袋后，我们确信这个巧妙的附加设计对于任何不想把包放在肮脏地面上的人来说，都是一种颠覆性的改变。

Sharge Disk Pro 2 SSD / HDMI dock: Give your father a little nostalgia and a lot of convenience in one package with this stylish USB-C dock and SSD combo gadget. The cassette-shaped Sharge Disk Pro 2 works as a hub with support for HDMI at 4K, SD and microSD card slots, USB-A and USB-C ports, along with up to 8TB of storage stored within.

Sharge Disk Pro 2 SSD / HDMI 扩展坞：这款时尚的 USB-C 扩展坞和 SSD 组合设备，能为你的父亲带来一丝怀旧感和极大的便利。磁带形状的 Sharge Disk Pro 2 可作为集线器使用，支持 4K HDMI 输出、SD 和 microSD 卡槽、USB-A 和 USB-C 端口，内部还可存储高达 8TB 的数据。

NotNeutral Vero Cortado Glass: It’s time to help Dad ditch his collection of plastic reusable cups, which have clearly seen better days. NotNeutral’s hand-pressed, timeless tumbler offers ’70s vibes with a modern twist, making it the perfect vessel for whatever Dad twists into his favorite drink.

NotNeutral Vero Cortado 玻璃杯：是时候帮父亲扔掉他那些显然已经破旧不堪的塑料可重复使用杯子了。NotNeutral 手工压制的经典平底杯既有 70 年代的复古感，又不失现代气息，是父亲调制他最爱饮品的完美容器。

Belkin Wireless HDMI Display Adapter: For those who love travel but hate the constraints of a hotel TV, Belkin’s ConnectAir is a slick solution. Plug the transmitter into any device with a USB-C video-out port and the receiver into the TV’s HDMI port, and voilà, you’re easily (and wirelessly) connected.

Belkin 无线 HDMI 显示适配器：对于那些热爱旅行却讨厌酒店电视限制的人来说，Belkin 的 ConnectAir 是一个绝佳的解决方案。将发射器插入任何带有 USB-C 视频输出端口的设备，并将接收器插入电视的 HDMI 端口，瞧，你就能轻松实现无线连接。

Traditional Kintsugi Repair Kit: Have you broken something fragile that seems impossible to mend? Maybe all you need is a Kintsugi Repair Kit, which provides the Japanese method and a special lacquer to repair and restore broken ceramics to good health. The resulting golden scar is merely to remind us that rebuilding is possible.

传统金缮修复套装：你是否打碎了什么看起来无法修复的易碎品？也许你只需要一套金缮修复套装，它提供了日本传统的修复方法和特殊漆料，可以修复并还原破碎的陶瓷。由此产生的金色裂痕只是在提醒我们，重建是有可能的。

Werewolf VFLEX Starter Kit Lite: Werewolf’s affordable dongle kit provides an almost magical way to power nearly any DC device from a USB-C charger or portable battery. Program it from a mobile app, connect it to your old gear, and everything from a PC monitor to an original Game Boy can accept USB-C charging.

Werewolf VFLEX 入门套装 Lite：Werewolf 实惠的加密狗套件提供了一种近乎神奇的方法，可以通过 USB-C 充电器或移动电源为几乎任何直流设备供电。通过移动应用程序进行编程，将其连接到你的旧设备上，从电脑显示器到初代 Game Boy，一切都可以支持 USB-C 充电。

Nomad Stratos Band: If your father already owns an Apple Watch, the Nomad Stratos is a foolproof upgrade idea. The refined watch band features titanium links with a rubbery interior for durable comfort, and it comes in an array of subtle color options that make it as suitable for the office as it is for the slopes.

Nomad Stratos 表带：如果你的父亲已经拥有 Apple Watch，Nomad Stratos 是一个绝不出错的升级方案。这款精致的表带采用钛金属链节，内部配有橡胶衬里，既耐用又舒适，并提供多种低调的颜色选择，使其既适合办公室佩戴，也适合滑雪时使用。

Trond Flat Extension Cord: If you’re after a cheap, useful stocking stuffer, Trond’s reliable three-prong extension cord is a practical pick that’s available in leftward or rightward configurations to fit nearly any outlet. Dad will probably say, “Oh, I’ve always wanted one of these,” and this time he’ll mean it.

Trond 扁平延长线：如果你在寻找一份便宜又实用的圣诞袜礼物，Trond 可靠的三孔延长线是一个实用的选择，它有向左或向右的配置，几乎可以适配任何插座。父亲可能会说：“哦，我一直想要一个这样的东西”，而这一次，他是认真的。

Camp Snap CS-Pro: The CS-Pro will make your dad feel like a private eye in the early ’80s. The retro-inspired digital camera serves up 16-megapixel stills and offers four filters accessible from its tactile dial, plus USB-C charging, a high-quality Xenon flash, and a 4GB SD card for plenty of vintage photo fun.

Camp Snap CS-Pro：CS-Pro 会让你的父亲感觉自己像是 80 年代初的私家侦探。这款复古风格的数码相机可拍摄 1600 万像素的照片，通过触感拨盘可使用四种滤镜，此外还具备 USB-C 充电、高质量氙气闪光灯和 4GB SD 卡，带来满满的复古摄影乐趣。

Antigravity A1 drone: Antigravity’s A1 is the ultimate gadget for the dad who dreams of owning the sky. The compact drone comes with specialized goggles that will make him feel like he’s in the cockpit, and it records always-on, 360-degree 8K video. It’s an exhilarating flight experience and an excellent creator’s tool in one.

Antigravity A1 无人机：对于梦想拥有天空的父亲来说，Antigravity 的 A1 是终极装备。这款紧凑型无人机配有专用护目镜，让他感觉仿佛置身于驾驶舱中，并能录制全天候的 360 度 8K 视频。它既是一种令人振奋的飞行体验，也是一款出色的创作工具。
