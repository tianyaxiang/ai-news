# Data centers are getting their own natural gas power plants

EXECUTIVE SUMMARY One of the biggest roadblocks to starting up a data center is getting approval to connect to the electric grid—a wait that could last as long as a decade. Some developers aren’t willing to wait and are instead opting to build their own off-grid, or behind-the-meter, gas power plants to fuel the AI boom.

执行摘要：启动数据中心面临的最大障碍之一是获得连接电网的批准，这一等待过程可能长达十年。一些开发商不愿等待，转而选择建造自己的离网或“表后”（behind-the-meter）天然气发电厂，以推动人工智能热潮。

xAI’s Memphis campus has famously used dozens of natural gas turbines, some of them without permits, as far back as 2024. At the time, that facility was a rarity, and it faced scrutiny from regulators. It remains the subject of ongoing legal battles. But the strategy of going off-grid for power has since become a lot more common.

xAI 的孟菲斯园区早在 2024 年就因使用数十台天然气涡轮机而闻名，其中一些甚至未经许可。当时，该设施非常罕见，并面临监管机构的审查。它至今仍是法律诉讼的对象。但此后，这种离网供电的策略已变得普遍得多。

As of October 2026, data center developers have announced plans for more than 100 behind-the-meter gas power plants at facilities in the US, according to data from Cleanview, a market intelligence company. The facilities have a combined planned capacity of over 115 gigawatts, more than double the peak power demand of California. The vast majority of these announcements have come since the beginning of 2025.

根据市场情报公司 Cleanview 的数据，截至 2026 年 10 月，数据中心开发商已宣布在美国各地的设施中建设 100 多座“表后”天然气发电厂。这些设施的总规划容量超过 115 吉瓦，是加利福尼亚州峰值电力需求的两倍多。绝大多数此类公告都是在 2025 年初之后发布的。

It’s possible that not all these facilities will be built; only a small fraction are under construction or operating today. But there’s a growing trend of large data centers looking to build their own power supply, and this could shape the grid—and its climate impacts—for decades to come.

这些设施未必都能建成；目前只有一小部分正在建设或运营中。但大型数据中心寻求建立自有电力供应的趋势日益明显，这可能会在未来几十年内影响电网及其气候影响。

Building your own power plant is generally more expensive than just hooking up to the grid, but the AI rush has made developers willing to pay a significant premium to get online faster. “The biggest motivator for on-site power right now from data centers is speed,” says Musfika Mishi, an associate at BloombergNEF, an energy consultancy.

建造自己的发电厂通常比直接接入电网更昂贵，但人工智能热潮使开发商愿意支付高额溢价以更快上线。能源咨询公司彭博新能源财经（BloombergNEF）的助理 Musfika Mishi 表示：“目前数据中心对现场供电的最大驱动力是速度。”

When developers want to build a new industrial facility—like a data center—that requires a lot of electricity, they need to request a grid connection from a local utility or regional grid operator. That request kicks off a yearslong process in which grid planners study the existing grid to understand what would need to be built to safely deliver power to the facility without compromising reliability.

当开发商想要建造一个需要大量电力的新工业设施（如数据中心）时，他们需要向当地公用事业公司或区域电网运营商申请电网连接。该请求会启动一个长达数年的流程，电网规划者在此期间研究现有电网，以了解需要建设什么，才能在不影响可靠性的情况下安全地向设施输送电力。

Upgrades might be required, like building new transmission lines or larger transformers, and the developer might be on the hook to help pay for them. Rather than waiting for that process to play out, developers can opt to instead build their own power plants. These facilities are referred to as “behind the meter” because they sit on the customer’s side of a utility meter—supplying the building directly, rather than relying on a utility via a grid hookup.

可能需要进行升级，例如建设新的输电线路或更大的变压器，而开发商可能需要承担部分费用。与其等待这一过程完成，开发商可以选择建造自己的发电厂。这些设施被称为“表后”，因为它们位于公用事业电表的用户侧，直接为建筑物供电，而不是通过电网连接依赖公用事业公司。

“This shift in the data center developers’ power strategies towards off-grid gas raises some important questions about how data centers will be powered going forward,” says Brendan Pierpont, director of electricity at the think tank Energy Innovation. Behind-the-meter doesn’t always mean being totally disconnected from the grid, however. Some facilities may build such plants as a temporary measure, eventually connecting to the grid and tapping their own power plants only as backup.

智库 Energy Innovation 的电力总监 Brendan Pierpont 表示：“数据中心开发商在电力策略上转向离网天然气，引发了一些关于未来数据中心如何供电的重要问题。”然而，“表后”并不总是意味着与电网完全断开。一些设施可能将此类电厂作为临时措施，最终连接到电网，仅将自有电厂作为备用。

“It’s really just a very expensive way to bridge that maybe five-to-seven-year timeline gap until these data centers can get a grid connection,” Pierpont says. Even if gas plants are pitched as a near-term solution, building new fossil fuel infrastructure could lead to longer-term effects, since an operator might choose to continue running plants once they’ve spent money building them.

Pierpont 说：“这实际上只是一种非常昂贵的方式，用来填补这些数据中心获得电网连接前可能长达五到七年的时间缺口。”即使天然气发电厂被宣传为短期解决方案，建设新的化石燃料基础设施也可能导致更长期的影响，因为运营商一旦投入资金建设，可能会选择继续运行这些电厂。

“Even though data centers say that it’s a grid-bridging solution, the probability that it is going to be in use much longer is a real risk, and that leads to fossil fuel lock-in,” Mishi says. The on-site gas rush could have major implications for greenhouse-gas emissions. Today, the US grid is still reliant on fossil fuels, but nuclear and renewables together make up about 40% of electricity generation.

Mishi 说：“尽管数据中心声称这是一种电网过渡方案，但它被长期使用的可能性是一个真正的风险，这会导致化石燃料锁定。”现场天然气热潮可能对温室气体排放产生重大影响。如今，美国电网仍然依赖化石燃料，但核能和可再生能源合计占发电量的 40% 左右。

That means making a grid connection is generally a lower-emissions option than relying entirely on an on-site gas plant. (That’s not the case everywhere—India, for example, is expecting to see a data center boom in the next decade, and its grid is largely served by emissions-intensive coal-fired power plants today.)

这意味着，与完全依赖现场天然气发电厂相比，接入电网通常是排放量较低的选择。（并非所有地方都是如此——例如，印度预计在未来十年内将迎来数据中心热潮，而其电网目前主要由排放密集的燃煤电厂提供电力。）

Utilities are already planning to build more natural gas power plants on the grid to meet rising electricity demand. Equipment manufacturers are struggling to keep up with the surge, and it’s pushing developers of behind-the-meter plants toward less-efficient alternatives. Today, large natural gas plants generally use combined-cycle turbines. These capture heat to generate additional electricity, making them more efficient than single-cycle turbines, which are generally cheaper to build.

公用事业公司已经在计划在电网上建设更多的天然气发电厂，以满足不断增长的电力需求。设备制造商难以跟上这一激增的需求，这正迫使“表后”电厂的开发商转向效率较低的替代方案。如今，大型天然气发电厂通常使用联合循环涡轮机。它们捕获热量以产生额外的电力，使其比通常建造更便宜的单循环涡轮机效率更高。

Very few of the planned behind-the-meter facilities, however, will use combined-cycle turbines, Mishi says. That means these off-grid plants will likely produce more greenhouse-gas emissions than even other natural gas resources. BloombergNEF is also tracking on-site natural gas plants at data centers, finding 181 gigawatts’ worth of announced projects globally, with 90% of that capacity designated for the US.

然而，Mishi 表示，计划中的“表后”设施中很少有使用联合循环涡轮机的。这意味着这些离网电厂产生的温室气体排放量可能比其他天然气资源还要多。彭博新能源财经也在追踪数据中心的现场天然气发电厂，发现全球已宣布的项目容量为 181 吉瓦，其中 90% 的容量指定用于美国。

If all planned behind-the-meter gas plants for data centers in the US go ahead, they could emit 345 million metric tons of carbon dioxide equivalent each year by 2035, according to a BloombergNEF report. That’s 152 million metric tons more than if those data centers pulled power from the US grid—a difference greater than the total annual emissions of the Netherlands.

根据彭博新能源财经的一份报告，如果美国所有计划中的数据中心“表后”天然气发电厂都得以实施，到 2035 年，它们每年可能排放 3.45 亿公吨二氧化碳当量。这比这些数据中心从美国电网获取电力多出 1.52 亿公吨，这一差额超过了荷兰全年的总排放量。

And while it might seem logical that data centers bringing their own power could ease communities’ energy bills, those facilities going off-grid doesn’t protect prices for everyone else, says Matthias Fripp, director of global policy research at Energy Innovation. More demand for natural gas could push its price higher, he explains, which could in turn raise rates for the roughly 60% of American households that use natural gas for heating and cooking. 虽然数据中心自带电力似乎可以减轻社区的能源账单，但 Energy Innovation 全球政策研究总监 Matthias Fripp 表示，这些设施离网并不能保护其他人的价格。他解释说，对天然气的需求增加可能会推高其价格，进而可能提高约 60% 使用天然气进行供暖和烹饪的美国家庭的费用。这种影响也可能波及电价。关于有多少...仍然存在很大的不确定性。
