# Jensen Huang’s Return Shows Nvidia Is Too Big to Police

Nvidia’s decision to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO signals that the chipmaker may have grown too important to the stock market for the US government to hold it accountable for national security lapses, intelligence and export-control experts warn WIRED. The move also undercuts the warnings Washington routinely issues to American companies and foreign governments about Beijing’s pursuit of US technology, they say.

英伟达决定恢复黄仁勋的首席执行官职务，情报和出口管制专家向《连线》杂志警告称，这表明该芯片制造商对股市的重要性可能已经大到让美国政府无法因其国家安全失误而追究其责任。他们表示，此举还削弱了华盛顿经常向美国公司和外国政府发出的关于北京寻求获取美国技术的警告。

Nvidia’s board brought back Huang on Monday, 11 days after he resigned amid revelations of extramarital affairs—one of them with Alice Huang, a woman US intelligence suspects has ties to China’s Ministry of State Security. The board said input from “senior levels of the United States government” left it confident that his return poses no national security risk.

英伟达董事会于周一让黄仁勋复职，此前他因婚外情曝光而辞职仅11天。其中一段婚外情对象是爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang），美国情报部门怀疑此人与中国国家安全部有联系。董事会表示，来自“美国政府高层”的意见使其确信，他的回归不会构成国家安全风险。

President Donald Trump told The Wall Street Journal he lobbied the board directly and through intermediaries, and that Nvidia’s falling share price helped his case. The board acted before its own investigation of Huang had concluded, and while the Justice Department is pursuing a criminal probe of Megaspeed—a Singapore company Alice Huang once ran—over the alleged diversion of Nvidia chips to China. Alice Huang is also under investigation, according to director of national intelligence Jay Clayton.

唐纳德·特朗普总统告诉《华尔街日报》，他曾直接并透过中间人游说董事会，而英伟达股价的下跌助推了他的主张。董事会在其针对黄仁勋的内部调查结束前就采取了行动，而此时司法部正在对新加坡公司Megaspeed进行刑事调查，该公司曾由爱丽丝·黄经营，涉嫌将英伟达芯片转移至中国。据国家情报总监杰伊·克莱顿称，爱丽丝·黄本人也正在接受调查。

The experts tell WIRED they worry more about the precedent Huang’s return sets than about Huang himself. Some say the scrutiny now on him makes further compromise unlikely. But Beijing may already have gained insight into Nvidia’s chip designs and its private conversations with the White House and Congress, or, through Megaspeed, obtained the chips themselves, they say, and no one has publicly established what, if anything, it got.

专家们告诉《连线》，他们担心的不仅仅是黄仁勋本人，更是他回归所开创的先例。一些人表示，目前对他进行的审查使得他不太可能再做出进一步的妥协。但他们指出，北京可能已经洞察了英伟达的芯片设计及其与白宫和国会的私下对话，或者通过Megaspeed获得了芯片本身，而目前尚无人公开证实北京到底获得了什么（如果有的话）。

Several want to know who gave the board its assurances and on what basis, and whether the government ran a counterintelligence damage assessment or simply made a political judgment. They also say the administration has repeatedly failed to enforce its own export controls, and that when intelligence concerns appear to yield to market pressure, US warnings about China start to sound negotiable, to Beijing’s benefit.

许多人想知道是谁向董事会提供了保证，依据是什么，以及政府是进行了反情报损害评估，还是仅仅做出了政治判断。他们还表示，本届政府一再未能执行其自身的出口管制，当情报担忧似乎向市场压力屈服时，美国关于中国的警告听起来就开始变得可以商量，这让北京从中受益。

“If intelligence assessments are seen to be overridden, selectively interpreted, or publicly dismissed for political or commercial reasons, this could undermine long-term confidence in the independence of national security institutions,” says Tahlia Peterson, a cyber and technology research fellow at the Royal Services Institute, a London defense think tank. “That would be damaging to US national security, and potentially advantageous to Beijing.”

“如果情报评估被视为因政治或商业原因而被推翻、选择性解读或公开驳回，这可能会破坏人们对国家安全机构独立性的长期信心，”伦敦国防智库皇家联合服务研究所的网络与技术研究员塔利亚·彼得森表示。“这将损害美国的国家安全，并可能对北京有利。”

Advisers briefed Trump at least twice on Alice Huang’s links to Chinese intelligence before the affair became public, and both times he played down their concerns, The New York Times reported Sunday. When the Journal asked him on Monday about Republican lawmakers who say Huang’s return endangers national security, Trump pointed to Nvidia’s rebounding stock.

据《纽约时报》周日报道，在婚外情曝光前，顾问们至少两次向特朗普简要介绍了爱丽丝·黄与中国情报部门的联系，但他两次都淡化了他们的担忧。当《华尔街日报》周一问及共和党议员称黄仁勋的回归危及国家安全时，特朗普指出了英伟达反弹的股价。

“Some tech companies have become so big—and so important to the stock market—that Washington is reluctant to hold them accountable for national security lapses,” says Edward Fishman, director of the Council on Foreign Relations’ Maurice R. Greenberg Center for Geoeconomics.

“一些科技公司已经变得如此庞大，对股市如此重要，以至于华盛顿不愿因其国家安全失误而追究它们的责任，”外交关系协会莫里斯·R·格林伯格地缘经济中心主任爱德华·菲什曼表示。

Fishman sees a parallel in Wall Street’s slow reckoning with sanctions. “It took years of aggressive enforcement to get banks to take US sanctions compliance seriously,” he says. “We haven’t seen anything comparable in the tech sector, which helps explain why US export controls remain so weak and leaky.” Reinstating Huang on the administration's assurances before Nvidia’s own investigation has concluded “does not inspire confidence that meaningful enforcement is coming,” he says.

菲什曼认为这与华尔街在制裁问题上的缓慢清算有相似之处。“花了数年时间的强力执法，才让银行认真对待美国的制裁合规，”他说。“我们在科技领域还没有看到类似的情况，这解释了为什么美国的出口管制仍然如此薄弱且漏洞百出。”他表示，在英伟达内部调查结束前，仅凭政府的保证就让黄仁勋复职，“并不能让人相信有效的执法即将到来。”

Emily Kilcrease, a former deputy assistant US trade representative who now directs the Energy, Economics, and Security program at the Center for a New American Security, says the administration’s record gives little reason to take those assurances at face value. “We cannot rely on the administration’s judgment, as it has repeatedly failed to enforce export controls over the past year,” she says.

曾任美国贸易代表副助理、现任新美国安全中心能源、经济与安全项目主任的艾米丽·基尔克里斯表示，本届政府的记录让人没有理由轻信这些保证。“我们不能依赖政府的判断，因为它在过去一年里一再未能执行出口管制，”她说。

On Monday, the Commerce Department added Megaspeed to its trade blacklist, and the administration has recently moved to tighten rules on China’s AI industry. But Kilcrease says it acted only under pressure. “Given the poor track record on export controls and their broader accommodationist stance toward China, it’s fair to ask whether they are willing to really enforce the new controls,” she says.

周一，商务部将Megaspeed列入了贸易黑名单，政府最近也采取行动收紧了针对中国人工智能行业的规则。但基尔克里斯表示，这是在压力下才采取的行动。“鉴于在出口管制方面糟糕的记录以及对华更广泛的迁就立场，质疑他们是否愿意真正执行新的管制是合理的，”她说。

The board’s assurances appear to have come mostly from the Justice Department. Attorney general Todd Blanche allegedly told Trump and vice president JD Vance on Saturday that Huang was neither a target nor a subject of any department investigation, and that the department had no evidence he had broken the law or posed a national security risk, the Journal reports. Vance then reportedly sought assurances for the board that the administration did not consider Huang a threat and that he would not face criminal charges. Trump told the Times he also consulted Clayton before pushing for Huang’s return. (“I can’t do this and then all of a sudden have somebody get indicted,” he said.)

据《华尔街日报》报道，董事会的保证似乎主要来自司法部。据称，司法部长托德·布兰奇周六告诉特朗普和副总统JD·万斯，黄仁勋既不是司法部任何调查的目标，也不是调查对象，司法部也没有证据表明他触犯了法律或构成国家安全风险。据报道，万斯随后为董事会寻求保证，即政府不认为黄仁勋构成威胁，他也不会面临刑事指控。特朗普告诉《纽约时报》，他在推动黄仁勋回归之前也咨询了克莱顿。（“我不能做完这件事，然后突然有人被起诉，”他说。）

Nvidia did not immediately respond to WIRED’s request for comment.

英伟达没有立即回应《连线》杂志的置评请求。

Whether Huang broke any laws is a separate question from whether Beijing gained anything through him. “Did [the assurances] reflect a counterintelligence damage assessment by the intelligence community or the FBI, or were they political in nature?” says Jessica Brandt, a senior fellow for technology and national security at the Council on Foreign Relations who previously directed the Foreign Malign Influence Center at the Office of the Director of National Intelligence. “Lawmakers should press on that.”

黄仁勋是否触犯法律，与北京是否通过他获得了什么利益是两个独立的问题。“（这些保证）是否反映了情报界或联邦调查局的反情报损害评估，还是本质上是政治性的？”外交关系协会技术与国家安全高级研究员、曾任国家情报总监办公室外国恶意影响中心主任的杰西卡·勃兰特表示。“立法者应该就此施压。”
