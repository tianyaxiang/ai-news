# US financial crimes bureau discloses ‘cybersecurity incident’

美国金融犯罪执法局披露“网络安全事件”

The Financial Crimes Enforcement Network (FinCEN) disclosed on Tuesday that it’s investigating a “cybersecurity incident.” The bureau, which is part of the Treasury Department, said it’s looking into an “incident that resulted in unauthorized access to a limited subset of its systems.” It hasn’t offered any information about the full scope or source of the breach, but FinCEN has taken some systems offline as a precaution.

美国金融犯罪执法局（FinCEN）周二披露，该机构正在调查一起“网络安全事件”。隶属于美国财政部的该局表示，正在调查一起“导致未经授权访问其部分系统”的事件。目前尚未提供有关此次泄露事件的全部范围或来源的信息，但作为预防措施，FinCEN 已将部分系统下线。

The news comes hours after The New York Times reported that the Central Intelligence Agency and Office of the Director of National Security discovered a breach of an open source intelligence system, exposing unclassified information. The Department of Defense, whose National Geospatial-Intelligence Agency was also reportedly impacted by the breach, declined to comment. The CIA and ODNI did not immediately respond to requests for comment. It’s unclear if the FinCEN breach is related.

在此消息传出数小时前，《纽约时报》报道称，中央情报局（CIA）和国家情报总监办公室（ODNI）发现了一个开源情报系统的泄露事件，导致非机密信息外泄。据报道，美国国防部下属的国家地理空间情报局也受到了此次泄露事件的影响，但国防部拒绝置评。中央情报局和国家情报总监办公室未立即回应置评请求。目前尚不清楚 FinCEN 的泄露事件是否与此有关。

FinCEN’s role is to protect the financial system from illegal activity, including by combatting money laundering and terrorism financing. The bureau maintains financial transaction data and distributes it for law enforcement purposes. FinCEN also houses reports of suspicious activity that financial institutions file as part of the Bank Secrecy Act.

FinCEN 的职责是保护金融系统免受非法活动的侵害，包括打击洗钱和恐怖主义融资。该局负责维护金融交易数据，并将其分发用于执法目的。FinCEN 还保存着金融机构根据《银行保密法》提交的可疑活动报告。

FinCEN says in its alert that banks can still file reports about suspicious activity online, but some services may not be immediately available. Filers that need to expedite their reports in the meantime can still call the Financial Institutions Hotline, which it says was not impacted by the disruption.

FinCEN 在其警报中表示，银行仍可以在线提交可疑活动报告，但部分服务可能无法立即使用。在此期间需要加急报告的提交者仍可拨打金融机构热线，该局称热线未受到此次中断的影响。
