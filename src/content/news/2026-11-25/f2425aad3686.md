Kalshi made the “difficult decision” to pause operations in Utah after losing a court fight to block the state’s ongoing enforcement actions under Utah gambling laws, Kalshi spokesperson Jacki McGavick said Monday. It seems Kalshi’s legal troubles in Utah have become too much for the prediction market platform to gamble on continued operations there.

Kalshi 发言人 Jacki McGavick 周一表示，在输掉了一场旨在阻止该州根据犹他州博彩法采取持续执法行动的法庭诉讼后，Kalshi 做出了暂停在犹他州运营的“艰难决定”。看来 Kalshi 在犹他州面临的法律麻烦已经让这个预测市场平台无法再冒险继续在那里运营。

In Utah, several lawsuits are still playing out, and Kalshi also lost a bid to persuade a federal appeals court to block those cases until the appeal is resolved. Kalshi also wasn’t saved by an emergency motion from the Commodity Futures Trading Commission—which argues that the CFTC has exclusive jurisdiction over prediction market trading. At this time, the federal district court has not granted that motion to block Utah’s enforcement actions, either.

在犹他州，多起诉讼仍在进行中，Kalshi 此前试图说服联邦上诉法院在裁决上诉前阻止这些案件的努力也以失败告终。美国商品期货交易委员会（CFTC）曾提出紧急动议，主张其对预测市场交易拥有专属管辖权，但这也没能拯救 Kalshi。目前，联邦地方法院也尚未批准该阻止犹他州执法行动的动议。

“This is an unfortunate outcome for the more than 177,000 Utahns who use Kalshi,” McGavick said. “We will continue to fight this in court, and we look forward to returning to Utah once our legal position is vindicated.”

“对于超过 177,000 名使用 Kalshi 的犹他州居民来说，这是一个不幸的结果，”McGavick 说。“我们将继续在法庭上抗争，并期待在我们的法律立场得到证明后重返犹他州。”

Utah’s attorney general, Derek Brown, had filed both civil and criminal charges against Kalshi, as he defended the state’s 130-year ban on gambling. In a press release discussing the initial lawsuit, he accused Kalshi of running “an online casino” that violates the state’s constitution. “Not only do our state laws ban gambling but Utah is one of only two states where gambling is also prohibited under the state constitution,” Brown said. “For 130 years, Utah has been clear: gambling has no place here, and we will enforce our laws.”

犹他州总检察长 Derek Brown 在捍卫该州 130 年来的博彩禁令时，对 Kalshi 提起了民事和刑事诉讼。在讨论最初诉讼的新闻稿中，他指责 Kalshi 经营着一家违反州宪法的“在线赌场”。Brown 表示：“我们的州法律不仅禁止博彩，而且犹他州是仅有的两个在州宪法中也禁止博彩的州之一。130 年来，犹他州的立场一直很明确：博彩在这里没有立足之地，我们将执行我们的法律。”

Late Monday, a major Utah-area news outlet, KSL.com, reported that Brown was prepared to jail Kalshi’s co-founders, who had been summoned to be booked on 14 charges on December 5. He defended those plans, saying, “There is a question I have seen from people asking, ‘Well, do we modify this booking process if someone is a very wealthy and well-known tech billionaire?’ and the answer is ‘No, the process is the process; we don’t modify it based on who you are.’”

周一晚间，犹他州主要新闻媒体 KSL.com 报道称，Brown 准备将 Kalshi 的联合创始人关进监狱，他们已被传唤于 12 月 5 日就 14 项指控进行登记。他为这些计划辩护称：“我看到有人问，‘如果某人是非常富有且知名的科技亿万富翁，我们是否会修改这个登记程序？’答案是‘不，程序就是程序；我们不会根据你的身份来修改它。’”

However, on Tuesday, Utah decided to drop the criminal charges against Kalshi, McGavick confirmed to Ars. In a statement on X, Kalshi general counsel Yaakov Roth said, “We are pleased that Utah has chosen to dismiss criminal charges against Kalshi, while proceeding with civil litigation. Good faith jurisdictional disagreements should never be criminalized. We stand ready to work with State AGs on reasonable interim measures until courts resolve the dispute over who regulates prediction markets.”

然而，McGavick 向 Ars 证实，犹他州周二决定撤销对 Kalshi 的刑事指控。Kalshi 总法律顾问 Yaakov Roth 在 X 上发表声明称：“我们很高兴犹他州选择撤销对 Kalshi 的刑事指控，同时继续进行民事诉讼。善意的管辖权分歧绝不应被定为刑事犯罪。我们随时准备与各州总检察长合作，采取合理的临时措施，直到法院解决关于谁来监管预测市场的争议。”

In a press release, Brown explained that the charges were dropped specifically due to the pause. “Kalshi said it would geofence Utah, which blocks access to its site in the state,” the press release said. “The company also committed to monitor for Utah-based IP addresses trying to reach its site. The Office then reevaluated its position and moved to dismiss the criminal case against the company and the individual defendants.”

Brown 在一份新闻稿中解释说，撤销指控正是因为该平台的暂停运营。“Kalshi 表示将对犹他州进行地理围栏限制，从而阻止该州用户访问其网站，”新闻稿称。“该公司还承诺监控试图访问其网站的犹他州 IP 地址。随后，办公室重新评估了立场，并申请撤销针对该公司及个人被告的刑事案件。”

Brown threatened that the charges would be raised again if Kalshi tried to renew operations. “Utah does not allow gambling, and prediction markets are gambling,” Brown said. “Kalshi has pulled out of Utah, and if it returns, so will the charges. Every company in this space should take note. If they promote, support, or enable gambling, we will hold them accountable.”

Brown 威胁称，如果 Kalshi 试图恢复运营，指控将再次被提起。“犹他州不允许博彩，而预测市场就是博彩，”Brown 说。“Kalshi 已经撤出了犹他州，如果它回来，指控也会回来。该领域的每一家公司都应引以为戒。如果他们推广、支持或促成博彩，我们将追究他们的责任。”

Discussion of the Kalshi pause in a Utah sub-Reddit found some Redditors skeptical of the state’s cases as potentially overreaching. However, many top commenters seemed pleased with the enforcement efforts, suggesting that Utah may have bipartisan support among some residents. One commenter cheered on the attorney general’s efforts as “the one item in the Utah GOP’s agenda I can get behind,” while another agreed that “prediction markets are incredibly toxic and are manipulated against ordinary people.”

在犹他州的一个 Reddit 子版块中，关于 Kalshi 暂停运营的讨论显示，一些用户怀疑该州的诉讼可能存在越权行为。然而，许多热门评论者似乎对执法行动感到满意，这表明犹他州的部分居民可能对此持两党支持态度。一位评论者为总检察长的努力喝彩，称其为“犹他州共和党议程中我唯一能支持的项目”，而另一位评论者则同意“预测市场极其有害，且被操纵以损害普通人的利益”。

Many complained that Kalshi ads flooded their social media feeds. Some suggested the state should focus on banning advertising of prediction markets, while potentially still allowing adults to trade on event contracts if they want to throw their “money away.” Fears of insider trading were also raised. One commenter laid out why prediction markets seem suspiciously designed to promote insider trading and other market manipulation: “I’m pretty libertarian and hate banning things. But gambling and prediction markets are a scourge. Even the term ‘prediction market’ is a misnomer. Because ultimately when people with big dollars get involved it creates a financial incentive for them to make fucked up things happen in the real world. Think about it, decades ago we understood that sports betting was a problem because it corrupted sports—something that ultimately is pretty meaningless. Then we got all these people betting on the stock market with puts and it created huge incentives to cause havoc with markets. Now if people are betting big money on politics and wars and everything else, it just means people with influence can make money by making improbable/unexpected things happen.”

许多人抱怨 Kalshi 的广告充斥着他们的社交媒体信息流。一些人建议该州应专注于禁止预测市场广告，同时如果成年人想“扔掉钱”，或许仍可允许他们交易事件合约。人们还提出了对内幕交易的担忧。一位评论者阐述了为什么预测市场看起来像是为了促进内幕交易和其他市场操纵而设计的：“我相当自由意志主义，讨厌禁止任何事情。但博彩和预测市场是祸害。甚至‘预测市场’这个词也是用词不当。因为最终，当拥有巨额资金的人参与进来时，这会产生一种经济激励，促使他们在现实世界中制造糟糕的事情。想想看，几十年前我们明白体育博彩是个问题，因为它腐蚀了体育——这最终是相当无意义的。后来我们看到所有人都在股市上通过看跌期权进行赌博，这产生了巨大的动机去扰乱市场。现在，如果人们在政治、战争和其他一切事情上投入巨资，这意味着有影响力的人可以通过制造不可能或意想不到的事情来赚钱。”

One commenter joked, “I wonder how Kashi would have reacted if the judge in charge of their case had ‘traded’ $500,000 on the event of them losing the case. Not a problem at all, right?”

一位评论者开玩笑说：“我想知道，如果负责他们案件的法官在他们败诉这件事上‘交易’了 50 万美元，Kalshi 会作何反应。完全没问题，对吧？”
