It wasn’t until I was stuck in traffic on the way back from a run up and down some canyon roads that I realized just how low Porsche’s upcoming all-electric 718 Boxster truly is. I was idly looking around at the other cars, doing the math on the EV adoption rate in this small sample of Los Angeles gridlock (roughly 50 percent, impressively) when I noticed that I was eye-level with a Tesla Model Y door handle.

直到我在峡谷道路上往返行驶、被困在回程的交通流中时，我才真正意识到保时捷即将推出的纯电动 718 Boxster 到底有多低。我漫不经心地环顾四周的其他车辆，计算着洛杉矶这段拥堵路段中电动汽车的普及率（令人印象深刻，大约为 50%），这时我注意到我的视线与一辆特斯拉 Model Y 的门把手齐平。

That’s not particularly low for a sports car, but it’s practically unheard of for an EV. Nearly all modern electric cars are built atop a skateboard-style battery pack, a stack of cells that elevates the seating position by a good 3 inches. In these SUV-heady days, that’s usually not a problem, possibly even an advantage. But, in a car like this, the closer you can get your keister to the road, the greater the sensation of speed.

对于跑车来说，这并不算特别低，但对于电动汽车而言，这几乎是闻所未闻的。几乎所有现代电动汽车都建立在滑板式电池组之上，这种电池堆叠会将座椅位置抬高足足 3 英寸。在如今这个 SUV 风靡的时代，这通常不是问题，甚至可能是一种优势。但对于这样的车来说，你的臀部离地面越近，速度感就越强烈。

So, for the company’s first all-electric sports car, the 718 Boxster that will be fully revealed next year, Porsche engineers put the battery pack behind the driver. That powers a single motor spinning the rear wheels and creates a driving experience that both honors the simple joy of motoring in a mid-engined Boxster while pushing the 718 to new heights of power and performance. Mid-engined? Mid-batteried.

因此，对于该公司明年将全面亮相的首款纯电动跑车 718 Boxster，保时捷工程师将电池组放置在驾驶员身后。它为驱动后轮的单电机供电，创造出一种既能致敬中置引擎 Boxster 驾驶乐趣，又能将 718 的动力和性能推向新高度的驾驶体验。中置引擎？不，是中置电池。

That EV battery layout, stacked behind the two-seat cabin rather than sandwiched below it, isn’t novel. It’s the same setup that Tesla used on the original Roadster back in 2008. Back then it was done for cost reasons as much as anything, enabling Tesla to repurpose a Lotus chassis meant to hold an internal combustion engine in the middle.

这种将电动汽车电池组堆叠在双座驾驶舱后方而非夹在下方的布局并不新颖。这与特斯拉在 2008 年最初的 Roadster 上使用的设置相同。当时这样做主要是出于成本考虑，使特斯拉能够重新利用原本用于容纳中置内燃机的路特斯底盘。

For Porsche’s new Boxster (and the Audi Concept C, which is built on the same platform), that pack enables the low seating position and also delivers a rearward weight bias (40:60 front/rear) that’s in keeping with the last-generation 718 Boxster, the internal combustion model killed by EU cybersecurity regulations last year.

对于保时捷的新款 Boxster（以及基于同一平台打造的奥迪 Concept C）来说，这种电池组布局实现了低矮的座椅位置，并提供了与上一代 718 Boxster 保持一致的后向重量分配（前/后 40:60），而上一代内燃机车型已于去年因欧盟网络安全法规而停产。

Porsche isn’t saying how big the new roadster’s battery is in terms of charge capacity, only that its size was chosen to balance range and weight. We also don’t have an idea of rated range, charging speed, nor battery composition, but I can say the battery pack is based on an 800 V architecture like the electric Macan, so hopefully we can expect charging performance that at least matches that SUV’s 270 kW.

保时捷尚未透露这款新敞篷跑车电池的充电容量，仅表示其尺寸的选择是为了平衡续航里程和重量。我们目前也不清楚额定续航里程、充电速度或电池成分，但我可以确定该电池组与电动 Macan 一样基于 800V 架构，因此我们有望期待其充电性能至少能达到该 SUV 的 270 kW 水平。

The new Boxster also shares a motor with the Macan. Porsche is not quoting any specs yet, but it puts out about 500 hp (370 kW) to the rear wheels on the Macan Turbo. You can place your bets on whether the Boxster application will result in more or less power. I would take the over, but regardless, it’s enough to sprint this electric roadster to 62 mph (100 km/h) in under 3.0 seconds, making it the quickest Boxster ever.

新款 Boxster 还与 Macan 共用电机。保时捷尚未公布任何规格，但在 Macan Turbo 上，它能向后轮输出约 500 马力（370 kW）的动力。你可以猜猜 Boxster 的应用会带来更多还是更少的动力。我倾向于认为会更多，但无论如何，这足以让这款电动敞篷跑车在 3 秒内从静止加速到 62 英里/小时（100 公里/小时），使其成为有史以来最快的 Boxster。

But it’s also the largest and heaviest. I was told the new Boxster’s curb weight will be somewhere in the ballpark of a current-generation 911, so in the region of 3,300 to 3,700 pounds (roughly 1,500 to 1,700 kg), meaning it has gained at least a few hundred pounds. That’s light for an EV but heavy for a roadster. Its wheelbase has grown accordingly. You can absolutely feel that on the road.

但它也是最大、最重的一款。我被告知新款 Boxster 的整备质量将与现款 911 相当，大约在 3,300 到 3,700 磅（约 1,500 到 1,700 公斤）之间，这意味着它至少增重了几百磅。对于电动汽车来说这很轻，但对于敞篷跑车来说却很重。其轴距也相应增加。你在路上绝对能感觉到这一点。

The 718 just doesn’t have the same buzzy eagerness as the latest 718 Boxster or Cayman. But that doesn’t mean it’s less fun to drive. In fact, I think most people would say it’s much more fun. Nearly doubling the old car’s base power output will do that.

这款 718 确实没有最新款 718 Boxster 或 Cayman 那种充满活力的急切感。但这并不意味着它的驾驶乐趣减少了。事实上，我认为大多数人会说它更有趣。将旧款车型的基础动力输出几乎翻倍，自然会带来这种效果。

This car, though, is about a lot more than acceleration. It still turns and handles incredibly well, borrowing much of its suspension, steering, and even chassis from the current-generation 911. Few would call a 911 a boring car, and the 718 is a step more aggressive even than that coupe. That’s in part thanks to the response afforded by the electric powertrain, which isn’t saddled by the lazy throttle application caused by turbochargers and modern emission regulations.

然而，这款车不仅仅在于加速。它的转向和操控依然非常出色，借鉴了现款 911 的大部分悬挂、转向甚至底盘技术。很少有人会说 911 是一辆无聊的车，而 718 甚至比那款轿跑车还要激进一些。这在一定程度上要归功于电动动力系统所提供的响应速度，它不会受到涡轮增压器和现代排放法规所导致的油门迟滞的影响。

It’s sharper even than the latest 911 GTS, which required a trick electrically assisted turbocharger plus a battery and electric motor to bring the EV feel to a car driven by internal combustion.

它甚至比最新的 911 GTS 还要敏锐，后者需要通过巧妙的电动辅助涡轮增压器加上电池和电机，才能为内燃机驱动的汽车带来电动汽车的驾驶感受。

To bring some of the charm of internal combustion to an EV, Porsche followed in the footsteps of the Hyundai Ioniq 5N by adding synthesized shifting and engine noises to the mix. Many cringe at the thought of an EV wailing unnecessarily, but having driven many battery-powered machines that make gas-powered noises, I can say with assurance that they always dial up the fun factor. What they often lack is authenticity.

为了给电动汽车带来一些内燃机的魅力，保时捷效仿现代 Ioniq 5N，加入了合成换挡和引擎声浪。许多人对电动汽车发出不必要的轰鸣声感到反感，但驾驶过许多能发出燃油车声浪的电池驱动机器后，我可以肯定地说，它们总是能提升驾驶乐趣。它们通常缺乏的是真实感。

Porsche sampled the sound of what its engineers determined was the sweetest-sounding 718: the 981-generation Boxster GTS. The new 718 Boxster replicates both that car’s 3.4 L flat-six and its seven-speed dual-clutch transmission, controlled by the paddles behind the steering wheel. Enable the system, which Porsche calls E-Shift, then put the Boxster into launch control, and you’re in for a bit of a show: The car not only sounds like it’s bouncing off of a rev limiter, but the car itself actually shakes.

保时捷采样了其工程师认为声浪最悦耳的 718 车型：981 代 Boxster GTS。新款 718 Boxster 复制了那款车的 3.4 升水平对置六缸引擎及其七速双离合变速箱，并通过方向盘后的拨片进行控制。启用保时捷称为 E-Shift 的系统，然后将 Boxster 置于弹射起步模式，你将欣赏到一场表演：这辆车不仅听起来像是在触碰转速限制器，而且车身本身也会产生震动。

It does this by pulsing the electric motor, vibrating in much the same way that the original Harley-Davidson Livewire did. Lift off the brake and the Boxster blasts forward, again doing the sprint to 60 in less than 3 seconds. All the while, the pretend engine is screaming and running through its mock gears, either automatically or at your command with the paddles. In manual mode, the car will hit that artificial rev limiter and abruptly stop accelerating.

它通过脉冲电机来实现这一点，震动方式与最初的哈雷戴维森 Livewire 非常相似。松开刹车，Boxster 便会向前冲刺，再次在 3 秒内完成 0-60 英里/小时的加速。在此过程中，模拟引擎会发出尖叫声，并自动或通过拨片手动切换模拟档位。在手动模式下，车辆会触及那个虚构的转速限制器并突然停止加速。
