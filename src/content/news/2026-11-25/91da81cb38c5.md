Most streaming music providers have opted to label AI-generated content on their platforms, and until now, Tidal was one of them. However, the company is about to take a harder stance. Tidal announced that in the coming weeks, it will fully ban AI music because of the harm it does to human artists.

大多数流媒体音乐提供商此前选择在其平台上标注人工智能生成的内容，而 Tidal 此前也是其中之一。然而，该公司即将采取更强硬的立场。Tidal 宣布，由于人工智能对人类艺术家造成的伤害，它将在未来几周内全面禁止人工智能音乐。

Tidal debuted its initial AI policy in July 2026, which included labeling and demonetizing all songs created entirely with AI. That wasn’t enough to stem the tide of AI slop, though. Over the past 90 days, Tidal reports that 43 percent of new uploads were completely AI-generated. On November 6, new uploads to Tidal hit a whopping 60 percent AI-generated.

Tidal 于 2026 年 7 月首次推出了人工智能政策，其中包括对所有完全由人工智能创作的歌曲进行标注并取消其获利资格。然而，这不足以遏制人工智能垃圾内容的泛滥。据 Tidal 报告，在过去 90 天里，43% 的新上传内容完全由人工智能生成。11 月 6 日，Tidal 的新上传内容中人工智能生成的比例高达 60%。

As things stand, Tidal hosts 27 million tracks labeled as AI, all of which were obviously added in the past couple of years. That accounts for 6.3 percent of Tidal’s total catalog. On December 9, Tidal will delete all those songs. The streamer notes this will have essentially no negative impact, because 96 percent of AI-labeled tracks got zero plays in the past 90 days.

目前，Tidal 平台上托管了 2700 万首被标记为人工智能生成的曲目，这些曲目显然都是在过去几年中添加的。这占 Tidal 总曲库的 6.3%。12 月 9 日，Tidal 将删除所有这些歌曲。该流媒体平台指出，此举基本上不会产生负面影响，因为 96% 被标记为人工智能的曲目在过去 90 天内播放量为零。

Throughout its announcement, Tidal is careful to stipulate that it’s only talking about music that has been fully created by AI. Artists can still use AI tools as long as the final product contains human elements.

在整个公告中，Tidal 特别声明，其禁令仅针对完全由人工智能创作的音乐。只要最终产品包含人类创作元素，艺术家仍然可以使用人工智能工具。

Some people don’t particularly mind AI music, but the team at Tidal has come to believe the harm to artists is too great. AI models can pump out random noises faster than humans can create things of real value, which can mislead listeners and siphon royalty payments away from creators. There’s also an issue with AI uploaders who are actively attempting to steal royalty payments. Tidal says it has already removed about 66,000 tracks from its service for impersonating artists, which was against its AI policy even before this update.

有些人并不介意人工智能音乐，但 Tidal 团队认为这对艺术家造成的伤害太大。人工智能模型产生随机噪音的速度比人类创造真正有价值作品的速度要快，这可能会误导听众并从创作者手中窃取版税。此外，还有一些人工智能上传者正试图窃取版税。Tidal 表示，由于冒充艺术家，它已经从服务中删除了约 66,000 首曲目，这在本次更新之前就已经违反了其人工智能政策。

As of December 9, individual uploaders and distributors will no longer be allowed to add AI music to Tidal. However, they could lie, couldn’t they? Tidal hasn’t specified how it detects AI music, but it will probably rely on watermarking. Almost every AI song on Tidal (94 percent) comes from Suno, which added watermarking earlier this year. Tidal also cites Google Lyria as a source of AI tunes, which has SynthID labels embedded in the waveform.

从 12 月 9 日起，个人上传者和分销商将不再被允许向 Tidal 添加人工智能音乐。然而，他们可能会撒谎，不是吗？Tidal 没有具体说明它如何检测人工智能音乐，但它可能会依赖水印技术。Tidal 上几乎每一首人工智能歌曲（94%）都来自 Suno，该公司在今年早些时候增加了水印功能。Tidal 还提到 Google Lyria 是人工智能音乐的来源之一，其波形中嵌入了 SynthID 标签。

That will take care of most AI music, but this approach won’t detect everything. There are open models for music generation that you can run locally on a GPU, and some cloud-based providers aren’t bothering to watermark their outputs. Even if Tidal is using a separate detection system, new models may be able to evade it—it’s always going to be a cat-and-mouse game.

这可以处理掉大部分人工智能音乐，但这种方法无法检测到所有内容。目前存在可以在 GPU 上本地运行的开源音乐生成模型，而且一些基于云的提供商并不打算为其输出内容添加水印。即使 Tidal 使用了独立的检测系统，新的模型也可能绕过它——这永远是一场猫鼠游戏。

The people directing robots to make these songs will have plenty of other options after Tidal blocks AI. Spotify, YouTube Music, Deezer, and Apple Music all allow AI music on their platforms with labeling. Spotify has removed millions of AI tracks from its collection based on user feedback. Other streamers haven’t specified how much slop (if any) they’ve cleared out.

在 Tidal 封杀人工智能音乐后，那些指挥机器人制作这些歌曲的人还有很多其他选择。Spotify、YouTube Music、Deezer 和 Apple Music 都允许在其平台上发布带有标注的人工智能音乐。Spotify 已根据用户反馈从其曲库中删除了数百万首人工智能曲目。其他流媒体平台尚未说明它们清理了多少（如果有的话）垃圾内容。
