### VP ≠ VNP over Every Field

VP ≠ VNP over every field. For every field, circuits for the Hamiltonian cycle polynomial, and for the permanent outside characteristic 2, need more than m^(c (log m)^(1/3)) gates for all large m. The same holds for approximating circuits. So VP is a proper subset of VNP, and VNP is not contained in the closure of VP. This does not settle P vs NP.

对于任意域，哈密顿回路多项式以及特征 2 以外的积和式（permanent）的电路，在所有足够大的 m 下，都需要超过 m^(c (log m)^(1/3)) 个门。近似电路的情况亦是如此。因此，VP 是 VNP 的真子集，且 VNP 不包含在 VP 的闭包中。这并未解决 P 与 NP 的问题。

The result was discovered by an internal research Claude model. This folder holds the paper and its Lean 4 formalization. Both were written by AI models, and no human mathematician has checked the proof. Research artifact. Not maintained and not accepting contributions.

该结果由 Claude 内部研究模型发现。此文件夹包含论文及其 Lean 4 形式化验证。两者均由 AI 模型编写，且未经任何人类数学家核实证明。此为研究产物，不进行维护，也不接受贡献。
