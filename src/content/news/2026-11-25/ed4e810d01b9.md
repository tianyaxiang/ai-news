# Anthropic breaks below $83 IPO price

Anthropic PBC shares traded below their $83 initial public offering price for the first time, wiping out gains for investors who piled into the artificial intelligence lab’s record-setting debut last week.

Anthropic PBC 的股价首次跌破其 83 美元的首次公开募股（IPO）价格，这使得上周涌入这家人工智能实验室创纪录首秀的投资者收益化为乌有。

The stock fell 6.5% to close at $81.91 Tuesday, more than 1% below its IPO price, amid a broader pullback in equity markets which had soared to begin the week. Anthropic first broke the IPO price in pre-market trading, dropping as low as $81.28. The company has a market capitalization of about $1.5 trillion at Tuesday’s closing price.

周二，该股下跌 6.5%，收于 81.91 美元，较其 IPO 价格低出 1% 以上。此前，股市在周初曾大幅上涨，但随后出现了普遍回调。Anthropic 在盘前交易中首次跌破 IPO 价格，最低曾触及 81.28 美元。按周二收盘价计算，该公司的市值约为 1.5 万亿美元。
