Data centers are getting their own natural gas power plants. One of the biggest obstacles to building a data center is getting approval to connect to the grid, a wait that could last as long as a decade. Some developers won’t wait and are instead building their own off-grid gas power plants to fuel the AI boom. As of October 2026, developers had announced plans for more than 100 such gas power plants at data centers in the US. But bypassing the grid brings its own problems, from worsening climate emissions to potentially higher energy bills for everyone else. Find out how the push for gas power could shape the grid—and the climate—for decades. —Casey Crownhart

A legendary philosopher isn’t buying Anthropic’s consciousness argument. Before going public last week, Anthropic named the possibility of AI consciousness as a risk factor for investors, warning that it might introduce new rules to protect its models. But that stance came under fire from an inconvenient source: one of the philosophers who helped shape our understanding of consciousness in the first place. Thomas Nagel, best known for his essay about what it's like to be a bat, was blunt. “Here are some of the smartest people on the planet being completely idiotic,” he said. He argued that producing language isn't enough to establish consciousness. But other philosophers disagree. Here's why language has become central to the debate over conscious AI. —James O'Donnell
