The Scientific Crown. Red-light therapy didn’t start as a treatment for antiaging and recovery. It actually came about as an accidental solution for hair loss in the 1960s, when Hungarian scientist Endre Mester was researching whether exposure to red light could treat cancer in hairless mice. Imagine his surprise when the mice started growing hair.

科学之冠。红光疗法最初并非作为抗衰老和恢复的治疗手段。它实际上是在 20 世纪 60 年代作为脱发的意外解决方案出现的，当时匈牙利科学家 Endre Mester 正在研究红光照射是否能治疗无毛小鼠的癌症。想象一下，当小鼠开始长出毛发时，他有多么惊讶。

Red-light therapy has come a long way since then. According to Rahman, red-light wavelengths are thought to activate the mitochondria in skin cells, which may encourage an uptick in the production of adenosine triphosphate (ATP) to promote new growth. Red-light therapy may also improve blood flow to hair follicles.

从那时起，红光疗法已经取得了长足的进步。据 Rahman 称，红光波长被认为可以激活皮肤细胞中的线粒体，这可能会促进三磷酸腺苷（ATP）的产生，从而促进新发生长。红光疗法还可能改善毛囊的血液循环。

Importantly, iRestore's devices are FDA-cleared—a safety designation from the US Food and Drug Administration indicating that a product is “substantially equivalent” to another legally marketed device. There are three hair-growth devices in iRestore's lineup: the entry-level Essential ($425), the mid-tier Professional ($799), and the Elite ($1,699). Each contains iRestore’s proprietary Lumitech technology, which combines LED lights and medical-grade lasers. These laser diodes, called Vixo lasers, operate in the range of 655 to 680 nanometers (nm).

重要的是，iRestore 的设备已获得 FDA 认证——这是美国食品药品监督管理局的一种安全认定，表明该产品与另一种合法上市的设备“实质等同”。iRestore 的产品系列中有三款生发设备：入门级的 Essential（425 美元）、中端的 Professional（799 美元）和高端的 Elite（1,699 美元）。每款产品都包含 iRestore 专有的 Lumitech 技术，该技术结合了 LED 灯和医用级激光。这些被称为 Vixo 激光的激光二极管工作在 655 到 680 纳米（nm）的范围内。

The idea is that this dynamic duo of lights and lasers can penetrate deeper into the skin than ordinary red light with lasers, while the LEDs ensure light is distributed across the scalp for maximum efficacy. Together, they reach a maximum of 2,500 milliwatts (mW) in the Elite, compared to the Professional's 1,410 mW and Essential's 600 mW.

其理念是，这种灯光与激光的动态组合比普通的红光激光能更深入地渗透到皮肤中，同时 LED 灯确保光线分布在整个头皮上，以达到最大功效。在 Elite 型号中，它们共同达到最高 2,500 毫瓦（mW）的功率，而 Professional 为 1,410 mW，Essential 为 600 mW。

The Elite is iRestore’s top-of-the-line offering and has the most LEDs of the three laser hair-growth systems, with 500 diodes in total—that's 300 lasers and 200 LEDs. The Essential has 51 lasers and 69 LEDs for a total of 120 diodes, while the iRestore Professional has 82 lasers and 200 LEDs for a total of 282 diodes. The Elite was also designed to cover more of the scalp than the other two models and requires 12 minutes of daily use, while the Professional and Essential require 25 minutes every other day.

Elite 是 iRestore 的顶级产品，在三款激光生发系统中拥有最多的 LED 灯，共有 500 个二极管——即 300 个激光器和 200 个 LED 灯。Essential 有 51 个激光器和 69 个 LED 灯，共 120 个二极管；而 iRestore Professional 有 82 个激光器和 200 个 LED 灯，共 282 个二极管。Elite 的设计覆盖范围也比其他两款型号更广，每天需要使用 12 分钟，而 Professional 和 Essential 则需要每隔一天使用 25 分钟。

If price is not a factor, the Elite is the no-brainer choice due to its higher-concentrated light therapy, shorter use time, and potentially faster results.

如果价格不是考虑因素，Elite 是不二之选，因为它具有更高浓度的光疗效果、更短的使用时间以及可能更快的见效速度。
