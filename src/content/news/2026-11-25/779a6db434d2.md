### How Gemini is changing the game for Golden State's organization and fans.

Gemini 如何改变金州勇士队组织及其球迷的游戏规则。

Nov 24, 2026 Today, we’re expanding our partnership with Golden State — making Google Gemini the Official AI Assistant Partner and Official Generative AI & Enterprise LLM Partner of the Warriors, the 2026 WNBA Champion Valkyries, and Chase Center.

2026 年 11 月 24 日，我们正在扩大与金州勇士队的合作伙伴关系，使 Google Gemini 成为勇士队、2026 年 WNBA 冠军 Valkyries 队以及大通中心（Chase Center）的官方 AI 助手合作伙伴，以及官方生成式 AI 和企业级大语言模型合作伙伴。

The collaboration integrates Gemini across the entire franchise ecosystem. For fans, that means a more interactive game-day experience, both inside Chase Center via interactive videoboard features, and across digital channels for fans following from home.

此次合作将 Gemini 整合到整个特许经营生态系统中。对于球迷而言，这意味着更具互动性的比赛日体验，既包括在大通中心内通过互动视频大屏实现的功能，也包括为在家观赛的球迷提供的数字渠道体验。

And behind the scenes, Golden State is deploying Gemini for business to build custom AI agents, helping their teams work smarter across both business and basketball operations.

在幕后，金州勇士队正在部署 Gemini for business 以构建自定义 AI 代理，帮助其团队在商业和篮球运营方面更智能地工作。

As a Founding Partner of Chase Center since 2019, Golden State has used our gen AI tools to help their operations run smoother, and this next chapter brings the best of Gemini to the team and fans alike.

作为自 2019 年以来大通中心的创始合作伙伴，金州勇士队一直使用我们的生成式 AI 工具来帮助其运营更加顺畅，而这一新篇章将为球队和球迷带来 Gemini 的最佳体验。
