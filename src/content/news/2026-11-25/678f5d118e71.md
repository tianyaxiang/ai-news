Expect travel headaches from coast to coast this week. Several rounds of disruptive weather sweep are likely to cause delays at airports and make for challenging driving conditions just in time for the Thanksgiving long weekend.

本周，全美各地的旅客都将面临出行难题。几轮恶劣天气预计将导致机场延误，并使感恩节长周末的道路交通状况变得严峻。

A very active pattern draped across North America will keep the storm train running in the days ahead. Potent systems rolling into the West Coast will bring disruptions from Seattle to San Francisco, where high tides could swamp airport runways. East of the Rockies, a major coastal storm expected to develop on Wednesday will generate widespread heavy rain and winds so gusty there’s a “significant chance” the Macy’s Thanksgiving Day Parade could be balloonless for the first time in 55 years. The same system will also deliver the season’s first measurable snow in some areas.

北美上空活跃的天气系统将使未来几天风暴不断。强劲的天气系统正向西海岸移动，将给从西雅图到旧金山的地区带来干扰，其中高潮位可能会淹没机场跑道。在落基山脉以东，预计周三形成的一场大型沿海风暴将带来大范围强降雨和强风，梅西感恩节大游行有“很大可能”55年来首次无法放飞气球。同一系统还将在部分地区带来本季首场可测量的降雪。

The stormy setup arrives at the peak travel time of the year in the US. The Transportation Security Administration screened 3.1 million passengers to close out the long weekend in 2025, marking the agency’s highest passenger volume on record. And with the extreme weather this week is set to sweep across some of the country’s biggest travel hubs, it could upend the best-laid travel plans.

这种风暴天气恰逢美国一年中的出行高峰期。2025年长周末结束时，美国运输安全管理局安检了310万名旅客，创下了该机构有记录以来的最高客流量。由于本周极端天气将席卷全国一些最大的交通枢纽，这可能会打乱人们精心制定的出行计划。

West Coast Storms: A persistent series of low-pressure systems will drag waves of heavy precipitation into the Pacific Northwest throughout the week, making delays and the occasional cancellation likely for flyers in Seattle and Portland.

西海岸风暴：一系列持续的低压系统将在整个一周内给太平洋西北地区带来多波强降水，西雅图和波特兰的航班很可能因此延误，甚至偶尔取消。

The real risk for delays begins to pile up when rain and gusty winds follow a cold front into northern California on Tuesday night. Though the worst of the rain will likely clear out in time for the bulk of Wednesday’s travel, flooding is still a major concern for low-lying highways and parts of San Francisco International Airport.

当周二晚间冷锋伴随降雨和阵风进入北加州时，延误风险将真正开始增加。尽管最严重的降雨可能会在周三大部分出行时间前结束，但低洼公路和旧金山国际机场部分地区的洪水问题仍令人担忧。

Forecasters expect a near-record king tide—that is, one of the highest tides of the year—on Tuesday and Wednesday. King tides are a normal occurrence, but this year’s will be exacerbated by record-high sea levels driven by the Super El Niño. The climate phenomenon has pushed warm water toward the coast, which takes up more space than cold water.

气象预报员预计周二和周三将出现接近纪录的“帝王潮”，即一年中最高潮汐之一。帝王潮是正常现象，但今年的情况将因超级厄尔尼诺现象导致的海平面创纪录升高而加剧。这种气候现象将温暖的海水推向海岸，而暖水比冷水占据的空间更大。

Monday’s forecast from the National Weather Service called for a high tide of 8.5 feet at the San Francisco gauge on Wednesday morning, which is just shy of the all-time record of 8.66 feet set in January 1983, another Super El Niño year.

美国国家气象局周一的预报显示，周三上午旧金山测潮站的潮位将达到8.5英尺，仅略低于1983年1月（另一个超级厄尔尼诺年）创下的8.66英尺的历史纪录。

East Coast Nor’easter Risk: Farther east, the atmospheric ingredients are lining up for a sprawling nor’easter to develop over the Southeast by Wednesday and move north through New England by the end of the week.

东海岸东北风暴风险：在更东部地区，大气条件正促使一场大规模的东北风暴于周三在东南部形成，并将在周末前向北移动穿过新英格兰地区。

Widespread heavy rainfall will accompany the storm northward, potentially worsening highway traffic as commuters and vacationers alike navigate slick roads in gloomy conditions. Gusty winds will reduce visibility during heavy rainfall. Forecasters expect several inches of rain along the East Coast, especially along the Interstate 95 corridor between South Carolina and New York.

大范围强降雨将伴随风暴北上，通勤者和度假者在阴沉天气下行驶在湿滑路面上，公路交通状况可能恶化。强风将在大雨期间降低能见度。预报员预计东海岸将有数英寸降雨，特别是南卡罗来纳州到纽约州之间的95号州际公路沿线。

Airline delays are almost certain given the dreaded mix of precipitation, low clouds, and stiff winds. The greatest impacts are likely in Charlotte and Raleigh in North Carolina on Wednesday, followed by Washington, DC, Philadelphia, New York, and Boston on Thursday.

考虑到降水、低云和强风的糟糕组合，航空公司延误几乎是肯定的。周三受影响最大的可能是北卡罗来纳州的夏洛特和罗利，周四则是华盛顿特区、费城、纽约和波士顿。

All these cities are home to major hubs for various airlines. Foul conditions at one of those airports can have ripple effects that lead to delayed or canceled flights down the line, including in cities where the weather is just fine. This may be the most impactful Thanksgiving weather since a nor’easter rolled up the East Coast on the Wednesday and Thursday of the holiday weekend in 2014.

这些城市都是各大航空公司的主要枢纽。其中一个机场的恶劣天气会产生连锁反应，导致后续航班延误或取消，即使在天气良好的城市也不例外。这可能是自2014年感恩节周末周三和周四东北风暴席卷东海岸以来，对感恩节影响最大的一次天气事件。

That storm hit the major hubs with high winds and heavy rain, as well as up to a foot of wet snow across much of the interior Northeast. The event led to thousands of cancelled and delayed flights. (The US also got a taste of travel trouble earlier this month when a Federal Aviation Administration ground stop left travelers stranded.)

那场风暴以强风和大雨袭击了主要枢纽，并在东北部内陆大部分地区带来了深达一英尺的湿雪。该事件导致数千个航班取消和延误。（本月早些时候，美国联邦航空管理局的地面停飞令导致旅客滞留，美国也经历了一次出行困境。）

The conditions could get so dire, that the Macy’s parade could go on but sans balloons. The parade will ground its balloons if there are sustained winds of 24 mph or gusts of 35 mph. The National Weather Service issued a high wind watch for New York City beginning Thursday morning, with winds of 25 to 35 mph with gusts up to 60 mph possible, though when the nor’easter reaches New York will determine if the winds cross that crucial threshold during the parade.

情况可能会变得非常糟糕，以至于梅西游行可能在没有气球的情况下继续进行。如果持续风速达到每小时24英里或阵风达到每小时35英里，游行将取消气球飞行。国家气象局已对纽约市发布了从周四上午开始的大风预警，风速可能达到每小时25至35英里，阵风可达每小时60英里，但东北风暴到达纽约的时间将决定风速是否会在游行期间超过这一关键阈值。

Cold air wrapping into the western side of the same storm system could lead to the first measurable snow of the season throughout portions of the Midwest. Computer models are still a bit wishy-washy on exactly where and how much snow will fall, as the rain-snow line will depend on the precise track of the storm. As of Monday, the general consensus for a few inches of wet snow appears to focus on the St. Louis metro area east into central Illinois.

冷空气卷入同一风暴系统的西侧，可能导致中西部部分地区出现本季首场可测量的降雪。计算机模型对于降雪的具体位置和降雪量仍存在不确定性，因为雨雪分界线将取决于风暴的确切路径。截至周一，普遍共识认为几英寸的湿雪可能集中在圣路易斯都会区向东至伊利诺伊州中部一带。

There’s some good news for travelers in the eastern US, at least: The nor’easter is expected to clear out by Saturday morning, which should hopefully reduce any snarls for travelers headed home on Sunday.

至少对于美国东部的旅客来说，有一个好消息：东北风暴预计将在周六上午消散，这有望减少周日返程旅客遇到的交通混乱。
