A European Ariane 6 launcher propelled 36 satellites into orbit for the Amazon Leo broadband constellation Tuesday, continuing the network’s build-out after 40 satellites were lost earlier this month during a failure of United Launch Alliance’s Vulcan rocket.

周二，一枚欧洲阿丽亚娜6号运载火箭将36颗卫星送入轨道，用于亚马逊Leo宽带星座项目。此前，联合发射联盟（ULA）的火神火箭在本月初发生故障，导致40颗卫星损失，此次发射延续了该网络的建设进程。

The launch was an important rebound for Amazon, bringing the Amazon Leo fleet to 432 satellites as the company seeks to fill the void left by the grounding of ULA’s Vulcan rocket, Amazon’s primary launch provider. Now, an Amazon official tells Ars it is accelerating several Falcon 9 launches the company has purchased from SpaceX, which also owns the Starlink satellite network that Amazon seeks to dethrone.

这次发射对亚马逊来说是一次重要的反弹，使亚马逊Leo卫星群的规模达到432颗。该公司正试图填补其主要发射服务商ULA火神火箭停飞后留下的空白。目前，一位亚马逊官员向Ars透露，公司正在加速推进此前从SpaceX购买的数次猎鹰9号发射任务；而SpaceX正是亚马逊试图超越的星链卫星网络的所有者。

The next Falcon 9 launch for Amazon Leo is now planned for December, Amazon said, moved forward from its previous launch date next year. Several more Falcon 9s are scheduled to launch Amazon Leo satellites in rapid succession after the December flight.

亚马逊表示，下一次为亚马逊Leo进行的猎鹰9号发射计划于12月进行，较原定明年的发射日期有所提前。在12月的飞行任务之后，还有数枚猎鹰9号火箭计划在短时间内连续发射亚马逊Leo卫星。

Amazon cast a wide net when it started signing deals for launch services five years ago, securing rides on 108 flights with every available medium- or heavy-lift rocket in the West to place a fleet of 3,232 satellites into low-Earth orbit. As of Tuesday, Amazon and its launch partners have completed 17 of those flights, with one mission failure.

五年前，当亚马逊开始签署发射服务协议时，其范围非常广泛，通过与西方所有可用的中型或重型运载火箭签署108次发射合同，旨在将3232颗卫星送入近地轨道。截至周二，亚马逊及其发射合作伙伴已完成了其中的17次飞行，其中有一次任务失败。

Amazon executives initially bought up capacity from US-based United Launch Alliance, Europe’s Arianespace, and Blue Origin. Nearly all of those launch contracts depended on rockets still in development: ULA’s Vulcan, Europe’s Ariane 6, and Blue Origin’s New Glenn. Each launch vehicle faced development delays, as all new rockets do.

亚马逊高管最初购买了来自美国联合发射联盟、欧洲阿丽亚娜航天公司和蓝色起源公司的运载能力。几乎所有这些发射合同都依赖于当时仍在研发中的火箭：ULA的火神火箭、欧洲的阿丽亚娜6号以及蓝色起源的新格伦火箭。正如所有新型火箭一样，每种运载工具都面临着研发延误的问题。

New Glenn has been grounded since May after an explosion on Blue Origin’s launch pad in Florida. ULA’s Vulcan will be sidelined for months after misfiring during a mission with Amazon satellites on November 15.

自5月佛罗里达州蓝色起源发射台发生爆炸以来，新格伦火箭一直处于停飞状态。ULA的火神火箭在11月15日执行搭载亚马逊卫星的任务时发生故障，预计将停飞数月。

Amazon started booking launches in 2023 with SpaceX. Falcon 9 successfully launched Amazon’s first three missions last year, each with 24 satellites. Amazon soon reserved 10 more flights on SpaceX’s Falcon 9 rocket as delays mounted with Amazon Leo’s other launch providers, especially ULA and Blue Origin.

亚马逊于2023年开始预订SpaceX的发射服务。去年，猎鹰9号成功执行了亚马逊的前三次任务，每次搭载24颗卫星。随着亚马逊Leo的其他发射服务商（尤其是ULA和蓝色起源）延误加剧，亚马逊很快又预订了10次SpaceX猎鹰9号的飞行任务。

The relationship between Amazon and SpaceX is a peculiar one. Amazon aims to catch up to SpaceX’s Starlink network, now boasting more than 11,000 satellites, to bring competition to the satellite broadband market. Federal regulators approved Amazon Leo’s second-generation constellation earlier this year, an addition that will bring the fleet to more than 7,000 satellites in the 2030s.

亚马逊与SpaceX之间的关系十分微妙。亚马逊旨在追赶拥有超过11000颗卫星的SpaceX星链网络，从而为卫星宽带市场带来竞争。联邦监管机构今年早些时候批准了亚马逊Leo的第二代星座计划，这将使该卫星群在2030年代达到7000多颗卫星的规模。

Amazon also plans a direct-to-device service, joining SpaceX and others in the pursuit of consumer demand for mobile connectivity. But Starlink is far ahead. SpaceX has repurposed the Starlink satellite design for use on several US military programs, and Starlink launches on Falcon 9 are winding down as SpaceX’s Starship enters service.

亚马逊还计划提供直连设备服务，加入SpaceX及其他公司的行列，以满足消费者对移动连接的需求。但星链目前遥遥领先。SpaceX已将星链卫星设计重新用于多个美国军事项目，随着SpaceX星舰投入使用，猎鹰9号上的星链发射任务正在逐渐减少。

Starship is capable of launching heavier, more powerful Starlink satellites, a culmination that will only widen the gap with Amazon and other competitors in the satellite Internet business. Starlink’s move to Starship opens up launch slots on Falcon 9, allowing Amazon to accelerate its launches with SpaceX in the near term.

星舰能够发射更重、功能更强大的星链卫星，这一成果只会进一步拉大与亚马逊及卫星互联网业务中其他竞争对手的差距。星链转向星舰发射为猎鹰9号腾出了发射位，使亚马逊能够在短期内加速其与SpaceX的发射计划。

But SpaceX doesn’t plan to keep Falcon 9 around for long. The company has reportedly stopped accepting commercial Falcon 9 bookings after 2028.

但SpaceX并不打算长期保留猎鹰9号。据报道，该公司已停止接受2028年之后的商业猎鹰9号预订。

That means Amazon Leo needs its other launch providers to step up. Arianespace is doing so, with four successful Ariane 6 flights for Amazon this year. Amazon reserved six additional flights with Arianespace in September as Ariane 6 proved its mettle.

这意味着亚马逊Leo需要其其他发射服务商加大力度。阿丽亚娜航天公司正在这样做，今年已为亚马逊成功执行了四次阿丽亚娜6号飞行任务。随着阿丽亚娜6号证明了其实力，亚马逊在9月份又向阿丽亚娜航天公司预订了六次飞行任务。

But there are questions about Europe’s ability to ramp up production of Ariane 6 rockets, which are single-use and not reusable like Falcon 9 and New Glenn. Europe’s rocket factories are sized to support a launch cadence of up to nine or 10 Ariane 6 flights per year. Arianespace will likely reach six Ariane 6 flights this year, with one more remaining before the end of December.

但人们对欧洲提高阿丽亚娜6号火箭产量的能力存有疑问，该火箭是一次性的，不像猎鹰9号和新格伦火箭那样可重复使用。欧洲火箭工厂的规模支持每年最多9到10次阿丽亚娜6号的发射频率。阿丽亚娜航天公司今年可能会完成6次阿丽亚娜6号发射，12月底前还有一次任务。

European taxpayers paid for development of the Ariane 6 and the industrial capacity to build the rockets, and they continue to subsidize each Ariane 6 flight, whether it carries a European government payload or commercial satellites for a large American corporation like Amazon.

欧洲纳税人为阿丽亚娜6号的研发及其制造火箭的工业能力支付了费用，并且他们继续补贴每一次阿丽亚娜6号的飞行，无论其搭载的是欧洲政府的有效载荷，还是像亚马逊这样的大型美国公司的商业卫星。

European government ministers are mulling proposals to increase Ariane 6 production to meet surging demand from commercial users and Europe’s internal space programs. A decision could come next month, but a ramp-up in launch cadence won’t happen overnight.

欧洲政府部长们正在考虑增加阿丽亚娜6号产量的提案，以满足商业用户和欧洲内部太空项目激增的需求。相关决定可能会在下个月做出，但发射频率的提升不会一蹴而就。

Amazon paid United Launch Alliance hundreds of millions of dollars for expansion at Cape Canaveral, Florida, to prepare for as many as 25 Vulcan launches per year. Such a launch cadence is a long way off for ULA, which has now launched Vulcan five times (with one failure) in its three years of service.

亚马逊向联合发射联盟支付了数亿美元，用于佛罗里达州卡纳维拉尔角的扩建，以准备每年多达25次的火神火箭发射。对于在三年服役期内仅发射了五次（其中一次失败）的ULA来说，这样的发射频率还遥不可及。

Blue Origin, owned by Amazon founder Jeff Bezos, could resume New Glenn flights early next year after completing a launch pad rebuild. It is not clear when Amazon might begin launching on New Glenn, but finding slots in the rocket’s launch schedule could be difficult as Blue Origin prioritizes work for NASA’s Artemis lunar program.

由亚马逊创始人杰夫·贝索斯拥有的蓝色起源公司，在完成发射台重建后，可能会在明年初恢复新格伦火箭的飞行。目前尚不清楚亚马逊何时开始使用新格伦火箭进行发射，但由于蓝色起源优先考虑美国国家航空航天局（NASA）的阿尔忒弥斯登月计划，在该火箭的发射计划中寻找空档可能会很困难。

In the meantime, SpaceX will offer Amazon its most assured rides to space. Falcon 9s can launch from Cape Canaveral every few days, and Amazon is ready to tap into that capability with hundreds of satellites in storage waiting to go to orbit.

在此期间，SpaceX将为亚马逊提供最可靠的太空发射服务。猎鹰9号每隔几天就能从卡纳维拉尔角发射一次，而亚马逊已准备好利用这一能力，其仓库中还有数百颗卫星等待进入轨道。
