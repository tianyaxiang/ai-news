Last week, international health authorities formally announced that the US had lost its measles-elimination status, meaning that the virus is once again endemic. But the official determination is a bit like getting the technical readout of a cracked dam while the country is already feet under water. The national tally of measles cases is nearing 6,000 for the year, the highest count since 1991. Transmission is widespread, with 47 states and jurisdictions reporting cases. There have been 43 outbreaks this year, which account for 96 percent of the cases. Four states have case counts above 500, including Pennsylvania, which leads with 1,638 cases as of November 23. Nearly 650 people have been hospitalized nationwide. As of today, 14 people have died.

上周，国际卫生当局正式宣布美国已失去麻疹消除状态，这意味着该病毒再次成为地方性流行病。但这一官方认定就像是在国家已经深陷水深火热之时，才拿到大坝破裂的技术读数。全国麻疹病例数今年已接近 6000 例，为 1991 年以来的最高值。病毒传播范围广泛，共有 47 个州和司法管辖区报告了病例。今年共发生了 43 起疫情，占病例总数的 96%。有四个州的病例数超过 500 例，其中宾夕法尼亚州截至 11 月 23 日以 1638 例位居榜首。全国范围内已有近 650 人住院治疗。截至今日，已有 14 人死亡。

Deaths and transmission: On November 23, Pennsylvania’s health department announced its ninth confirmed death, an unvaccinated resident of Centre County. On November 24, Wisconsin reported its third measles death, an adult in Lafayette County. The report follows two other deaths in the state, an infant and a child. New York has also reported a death, which was of a 7-month-old baby. On November 20, Virginia reported the death of a child between the age of 0 and 4. The virus isn’t slowing down. Measles is the most contagious virus known; it can linger in the air for up to two hours. Up to 90 percent of people who are not immune will fall ill after an exposure. People are considered immune if they’ve been vaccinated or previously infected.

死亡与传播：11 月 23 日，宾夕法尼亚州卫生部门宣布了该州第九例确诊死亡病例，死者为中央县一名未接种疫苗的居民。11 月 24 日，威斯康星州报告了该州第三例麻疹死亡病例，死者为拉斐特县的一名成年人。此前该州已报告了两例死亡病例，分别为一名婴儿和一名儿童。纽约州也报告了一例死亡病例，死者为一名 7 个月大的婴儿。11 月 20 日，弗吉尼亚州报告了一名 0 至 4 岁儿童的死亡。病毒传播并未放缓。麻疹是已知传染性最强的病毒；它可以在空气中停留长达两个小时。多达 90% 的非免疫人群在接触后会发病。如果人们接种过疫苗或曾被感染，则被视为具有免疫力。

With slipping vaccination rates nationwide, the number of susceptible people is only growing. To keep measles from getting a foothold in a community, vaccination rates need to be 95 percent or higher. According to the latest data, only 92.4 percent of US kindergartners were vaccinated with the measles, mumps, and rubella (MMR) vaccine in the 2025–2026 school year. Only 10 states have MMR vaccination rates at or above the 95 percent threshold. But even in those states, there are pockets and communities that are undervaccinated. In Pennsylvania, which has the record for the largest measles outbreak this year so far, the epidemic curve appears to be past the peak. But Wisconsin seems to be in the thick of its outbreak. As of today, the state case count hit 920, with 88 cases reported since November 20. The health department has tallied 418 cases since the start of the month.

随着全国疫苗接种率的下滑，易感人群的数量只会不断增加。为了防止麻疹在社区中立足，疫苗接种率需要达到 95% 或更高。根据最新数据，在 2025-2026 学年，美国仅有 92.4% 的幼儿园儿童接种了麻疹、腮腺炎和风疹（MMR）疫苗。只有 10 个州的 MMR 疫苗接种率达到或超过了 95% 的门槛。但即使在这些州，也存在疫苗接种不足的局部地区和社区。在今年迄今为止麻疹疫情最严重的宾夕法尼亚州，疫情曲线似乎已过峰值。但威斯康星州似乎正处于疫情高峰期。截至今日，该州病例数已达 920 例，自 11 月 20 日以来报告了 88 例。卫生部门统计显示，自本月初以来已记录了 418 例病例。

Measles infections: In most cases, measles causes high fever, a telltale rash, cough, runny nose, and watery eyes. But some develop complications, which can be severe and even life-threatening, including pneumonia and brain swelling, which can result in intellectual disabilities and hearing loss. In pregnant people, measles can cause more severe illness and pregnancy complications, including premature birth, low birth weight, and congenital measles. After an acute infection, every person infected faces a long immune recovery in which they are newly vulnerable to a slew of other diseases. The measles virus specifically infects and destroys memory immune cells, which hold molecular records of germs the immune system has previously encountered and defeated.

麻疹感染：在大多数情况下，麻疹会导致高烧、典型的皮疹、咳嗽、流鼻涕和流泪。但有些人会出现并发症，这些并发症可能很严重甚至危及生命，包括肺炎和脑肿胀，这可能导致智力障碍和听力丧失。对于孕妇，麻疹可能导致更严重的疾病和妊娠并发症，包括早产、低出生体重和先天性麻疹。急性感染后，每位感染者都面临漫长的免疫恢复期，期间他们对一系列其他疾病变得更加脆弱。麻疹病毒会专门感染并破坏记忆免疫细胞，这些细胞保存着免疫系统先前遇到并击败过的病原体的分子记录。

In other words, the immunity built up over the person’s life gets wiped, a phenomenon called immune amnesia. A study in 2019 estimated that the measles virus erases up to 73 percent of a person’s antibody repertoire, which would otherwise defend against infections and reinfections. To regain protection, people must be exposed to the germs again, either through reinfections or repeat vaccinations. People who appear to recover are also at risk of subacute sclerosing panencephalitis (SSPE), a rare but devastating condition in which the virus remains in the central nervous system, striking years later to cause a progressive, fatal neurological disease.

换句话说，一个人一生中建立的免疫力会被抹去，这种现象被称为“免疫失忆”。2019 年的一项研究估计，麻疹病毒会抹去一个人多达 73% 的抗体库，而这些抗体本可以抵御感染和再感染。为了重新获得保护，人们必须再次接触这些病原体，无论是通过再次感染还是重复接种疫苗。即使看起来已经康复的人，也面临亚急性硬化性全脑炎（SSPE）的风险，这是一种罕见但具有破坏性的疾病，病毒会残留在中枢神经系统中，多年后发作，导致进行性的、致命的神经系统疾病。
