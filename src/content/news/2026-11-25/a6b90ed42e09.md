# Jensen Huang reportedly tells Nvidia staff it’s ‘not true’ Trump got him rehired

据报道，英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋在重返公司后的首次全员大会上向员工道歉，并试图与“白宫和科技领袖帮助他复职”的说法撇清关系。这一消息来自彭博社、《纽约时报》以及《The Handbasket》记者玛丽莎·卡巴斯（Marisa Kabas）的报道。

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang apologized to staff during his first all-hands since returning to the company, while attempting to distance himself from the narrative that the White House and tech leaders helped him get his job back, according to reports from Bloomberg, The New York Times, and The Handbasket’s Marisa Kabas.

在会议期间，据报道黄仁勋称唐纳德·特朗普总统直接参与其复职的说法——这一观点由特朗普本人公开宣扬——是“完全不真实的”。据卡巴斯报道，他补充说：“千万别以为政治在公司中扮演了任何角色。我们是这家公司最不讲政治的。”

During the meeting, Huang reportedly called claims that President Donald Trump was directly involved in his reinstatement — an idea publicly promoted by Trump himself — “completely untrue.” He added, “Don’t think for a second politics played a role in the company. We are the most apolitical company,” according to Kabas.

除了试图否认特朗普的参与，据报道黄仁勋还表示，他的回归并非因为他是“科技兄弟”（tech bro），也不是因为他“凌驾于法律之上”或“凌驾于行为准则之上”。据卡巴斯报道，他补充说：“人类历史上没有比这更扭曲的故事了。”相反，据《纽约时报》报道，黄仁勋表示英伟达董事会“评估了整个情况”，同时获得了政府的保证，即他不会构成国家安全威胁。

Along with attempting to dismiss Trump’s involvement, Huang reportedly said he also didn’t return because he was a “tech bro” who was “above the law” or “above the code of conduct.” He added that, “no story has been more tortured than this one in human history,” Kabas reports. Instead, Huang said the Nvidia board “evaluated the entire situation,” while receiving assurance from the government that he posed no national security threat, according to the NYT.

黄仁勋于11月12日辞去英伟达首席执行官职务，此前有报道称他与一名据称是中国间谍的人员以及两名员工（包括医疗保健副总裁金伯利·鲍威尔）有染。周一，英伟达宣布已恢复黄仁勋首席执行官、总裁和董事会成员的职务——白宫称特朗普对此事“完全参与”。特朗普也证实，他与副总统万斯及其他官员合作促成了黄仁勋的回归。包括特斯拉首席执行官埃隆·马斯克、Epic Games首席执行官蒂姆·斯威尼以及前白宫人工智能主管大卫·萨克斯在内的一批科技领袖也支持黄仁勋的回归。

Huang resigned as CEO of Nvidia on November 12th, following reports of affairs with an alleged Chinese spy and two employees, including vice president of healthcare Kimberly Powell. On Monday, Nvidia announced that it had reinstated Huang as CEO, president, and member of the board — a decision the White House said Trump was “totally involved in.” Trump also confirmed that he worked with Vice President JD Vance and other officials to bring Huang back. A swath of tech leaders, including Tesla CEO Elon Musk, Epic Games CEO Tim Sweeney, and former White House AI czar David Sacks, were also in favor of Huang’s return.

在“真实社交”（Truth Social）平台上的一篇帖子中，特朗普表示政府“没有理由相信”黄仁勋的回归会对国家安全构成风险，并补充说美国司法部长“没有证据”表明他参与了“非法行为”。据路透社报道，英伟达目前仍面临对其会计实务的调查。该公司也在针对有关黄仁勋成为中国间谍阴谋目标的报道进行内部调查。黄仁勋定于12月1日出席国会听证会，回答有关国家安全担忧的问题，民主党人已经计划就他的回归以及特朗普的参与向黄仁勋施压。

In a post on Truth Social, Trump said that the government “has no reason to believe” Huang’s return poses a risk to national security, adding that the US attorney general “does not possess evidence” that he engaged in “unlawful conduct.” Nvidia is still facing an investigation over its accounting practices, according to Reuters. The company is also carrying out its own internal investigation surrounding reports that Huang was the target of a Chinese spy plot. Huang is set to testify before Congress on December 1st to answer questions about national security concerns, and Democrats are already planning to press Huang about his return, as well as Trump’s involvement.

尽管经历了这一切，据报道黄仁勋仍表示他“不相信继任计划”。据彭博社报道，他还承认自己违反了英伟达的行为准则，并补充说他正在每天接受心理治疗。“我犯了一些可怕的错误，”据《纽约时报》报道，黄仁勋说。“我背叛了洛里，我伤害了我的孩子们，当然，我也伤害了你们所有人。”

Even after all this, Huang reportedly said he “doesn’t believe in succession planning.” He also acknowledged that he violated Nvidia’s code of conduct, adding that he is attending therapy daily, Bloomberg reports. “I made some terrible mistakes,” Huang said, according to the NYT. “I was unfaithful to Lori, and I hurt my kids and of course, I hurt all of you.”
