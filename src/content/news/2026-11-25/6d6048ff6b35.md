# Why there are still no non-WebKit browsers on iOS

Mozilla Firefox on iPhone is growing fast across the world. This is especially true in the EU since 2024, when the EU Digital Markets Act (DMA) started requiring Apple to let people choose their default browser; millions have picked Firefox, and daily use on iOS has doubled. People want to choose the browser on their phone.

Mozilla Firefox 在 iPhone 上的全球使用量增长迅速。自 2024 年欧盟《数字市场法案》（DMA）开始要求苹果允许用户选择默认浏览器以来，这一趋势在欧盟尤为明显；数以百万计的用户选择了 Firefox，其在 iOS 上的日活跃用户数翻了一番。人们希望能够自主选择手机上的浏览器。

But there remains an important limitation to what is available on iPhones: Apple forces all browsers on iOS to be built on Apple’s own browser engine. This has meant that companies like Mozilla cannot easily bring the full range of Firefox features and functionality to iPhone users because many of these are built within our browser engine, Gecko. iPhones and iPads are unique in having this restriction. On all other major operating systems, including Apple’s macOS, people can access the Gecko-powered version of Firefox.

然而，iPhone 上可用的浏览器仍存在一个重要的局限性：苹果强制要求 iOS 上的所有浏览器必须基于苹果自家的浏览器引擎构建。这意味着像 Mozilla 这样的公司无法轻易地为 iPhone 用户提供 Firefox 的全部功能，因为其中许多功能是基于我们的浏览器引擎 Gecko 构建的。iPhone 和 iPad 是唯一有此限制的设备。在包括苹果 macOS 在内的所有其他主流操作系统上，用户都可以使用基于 Gecko 的 Firefox 版本。

As a result of the DMA, Apple began allowing alternative browser engines on iOS in the EU. It published a set of conditions that companies like Mozilla must comply with to bring their own browser engine. At the time we said Apple’s rules made it “as painful as possible” for alternatives like Gecko. In the meantime, Japan also introduced new rules requiring alternative browser engines on iOS. Apple applied very similar conditions in Japan to those in the EU.

受 DMA 影响，苹果开始允许在欧盟地区的 iOS 上使用替代浏览器引擎。苹果发布了一系列条件，要求像 Mozilla 这样的公司必须遵守才能引入自己的浏览器引擎。当时我们曾表示，苹果的规则让 Gecko 等替代方案的实施变得“尽可能痛苦”。与此同时，日本也出台了新规，要求 iOS 支持替代浏览器引擎。苹果在日本实施的条件与在欧盟实施的条件非常相似。

Nearly three years since Apple introduced these conditions in the EU and almost a year since Japan, not one developer has been able to launch a non-WebKit browser on iOS. The reason is simple: Apple’s conditions remain unworkable. The real test of any rule is whether it delivers the change it was meant to achieve in practice. Compliance on paper alone falls short of that test.

距离苹果在欧盟引入这些条件已过去近三年，在日本引入也已近一年，但还没有任何开发者能够在 iOS 上推出非 WebKit 浏览器。原因很简单：苹果的条件仍然不可行。任何规则的真正考验在于它在实践中是否实现了预期的变革。仅在纸面上合规是无法通过这一考验的。

Since the DMA came into effect we’ve been working behind the scenes to test and improve Apple’s conditions so that we can bring Gecko to iOS users. Those efforts have so far largely failed. One of the conditions requires us not to disclose non-public information related to the project, but here’s an example of what we are up against: to use Gecko, we can’t simply update the Firefox app people already use and love. We’d have to build a whole separate app, a second Firefox.

自 DMA 生效以来，我们一直在幕后努力测试和改进苹果的条件，以便将 Gecko 带给 iOS 用户。但这些努力目前大多以失败告终。其中一个条件要求我们不得披露与项目相关的非公开信息，但以下是我们所面临困境的一个例子：为了使用 Gecko，我们不能简单地更新人们已经使用并喜爱的 Firefox 应用。我们必须构建一个完全独立的应用程序，即第二个 Firefox。

Then we’d have to ask every Firefox user to download it, move their bookmarks, passwords and history across, and manually switch over, step by confusing step. That’s a lot to ask of people who already made their choice. A significant proportion of people would understandably give up, including many who picked Firefox from the EU’s choice screen. The conditions Apple imposes, including a level of user friction Apple would never accept for its own apps, mean that the open door feels more like a trap door.

然后，我们必须要求每一位 Firefox 用户下载它，迁移他们的书签、密码和历史记录，并一步步地手动切换，过程十分繁琐。对于已经做出选择的用户来说，这要求太高了。相当一部分人理所当然地会选择放弃，包括许多从欧盟选择界面中挑选 Firefox 的用户。苹果施加的这些条件，包括苹果绝不会在其自家应用中接受的用户摩擦，意味着这扇“敞开的门”更像是一个陷阱。

Firefox users on iPhone chose an independent browser. They should get everything that comes with it, including Gecko, the last major browser engine not developed by a big tech company. We’ll keep pushing for a real level playing field, not an opening that drops independent browsers through the floor.

iPhone 上的 Firefox 用户选择了独立的浏览器。他们理应获得该浏览器所带来的一切，包括 Gecko——这是最后一个非大型科技公司开发的重大浏览器引擎。我们将继续推动实现真正的公平竞争环境，而不是一个让独立浏览器陷入困境的虚假开放。
