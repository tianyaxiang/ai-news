# Raising the stakes for enterprise networks with AI modernization

企业网络正承受着巨大的压力。随着各公司竞相部署人工智能，这项技术正迅速成为网络需求的最大来源。

Now, agentic AI is adding to the load. Tasks conducted by AI agents generate an estimated 450% more network traffic than the same tasks done by human workers.

如今，代理式人工智能（Agentic AI）进一步加重了负担。据估计，由人工智能代理执行的任务所产生的网络流量比人类员工执行相同任务时多出 450%。

As AI inference shifts from hyperscale training clouds—the huge centralized data centers where raw AI models ingest vast datasets and learn—to real-time, latency-sensitive applications, the need for on-premises or near-premises infrastructure grows.

随着人工智能推理从超大规模训练云（即原始人工智能模型摄取海量数据集并进行学习的庞大集中式数据中心）转向实时、对延迟敏感的应用，对本地或近场基础设施的需求日益增长。

As a result, the gap between enterprise networks that are ready to support AI and those that are not is widening. As few as half of enterprises believe their current networks can handle AI’s bandwidth and latency demands, according to a recent study.

因此，能够支持人工智能的企业网络与无法支持的企业网络之间的差距正在拉大。根据最近的一项研究，只有不到一半的企业认为其现有网络能够满足人工智能对带宽和延迟的需求。

本报告探讨了将网络现代化视为一项持续战略而非一次性项目，如何帮助组织在面对颠覆和不确定性时进行调整。报告的主要发现包括：

AI may force companies to shift from a cloud-first approach to routing workloads. Training AI requires server-to-server data center architectures, while inference calls for low-latency connectivity close to users. Enterprises may need to fundamentally reconsider their network architecture to meet these requirements.

人工智能可能会迫使企业改变以云优先的负载路由方式。人工智能训练需要服务器到服务器的数据中心架构，而推理则需要靠近用户的低延迟连接。企业可能需要从根本上重新考虑其网络架构以满足这些要求。

AI inference is pushing infrastructure back on premises. Inference typically requires real-time processing and is sensitive to latency, making hosting such capabilities on distant hyperscale clouds neither economical nor practical.

人工智能推理正在将基础设施推回本地。推理通常需要实时处理且对延迟敏感，这使得在遥远的超大规模云上托管此类功能既不经济也不切实际。

Inconsistent latency can lead to AI model failures, driving up queue wait times, saturating GPUs loads, and causing bottlenecks in data flows. Large enterprises may need on-premises or near-premises inference, which reverses years of cloud-first consolidation and forces new approaches to data center architecture, connectivity, and physical infrastructure.

不稳定的延迟可能导致人工智能模型故障，增加队列等待时间，使 GPU 负载饱和，并导致数据流瓶颈。大型企业可能需要本地或近场推理，这扭转了多年来以云优先的整合趋势，并迫使企业在数据中心架构、连接性和物理基础设施方面采取新方法。

AI workloads are stressing physical network capacity in unexpected ways. The traffic increase from agentic AI, combined with inference moving on-premises, is exposing bandwidth bottlenecks in legacy underlay infrastructure.

人工智能工作负载正以意想不到的方式给物理网络容量带来压力。代理式人工智能带来的流量增长，加上推理向本地迁移，正暴露出传统底层基础设施中的带宽瓶颈。

Network modernization is an ongoing project that requires a holistic approach, allowing companies to adapt with ease. A combination of strong architectural governance, reusable design templates, and retained internal expertise supports organizations in adapting to disruptive events without rebuilding their infrastructure from scratch each time.

网络现代化是一项需要整体方法的持续性项目，旨在让企业能够轻松适应变化。强大的架构治理、可重复使用的设计模板以及保留的内部专业知识相结合，支持组织在不每次从零开始重建基础设施的情况下适应颠覆性事件。

Such events could include mergers and acquisitions, changing vendors, or technology upgrades. NaaS and AI-driven observability can improve efficiency and reduce complexity in network operations.

此类事件可能包括并购、更换供应商或技术升级。网络即服务（NaaS）和人工智能驱动的可观测性可以提高效率并降低网络运营的复杂性。

Network-as-a-service (NaaS) and AI-driven observability can offer companies benefits like faster, more flexible provisioning and lower costs. Data sovereignty and sovereign AI will create infrastructure challenges for global enterprises.

网络即服务（NaaS）和人工智能驱动的可观测性可以为企业提供更快速、更灵活的配置和更低的成本等优势。数据主权和主权人工智能将为全球企业带来基础设施挑战。

Regulators worldwide are passing laws requiring data and AI processing to remain within national borders. This conflicts with how multinational companies currently operate, requiring the build-out of sovereign-capable infrastructure for each geography, which will require significant new investment and complicate operations.

全球监管机构正在通过法律，要求数据和人工智能处理必须保留在国界之内。这与跨国公司目前的运营方式相冲突，要求为每个地区建立具备主权能力的基础设施，这将需要大量新的投资并使运营复杂化。
