Last week, news broke that a mathematician had used AI to help crack one of the biggest outstanding problems in math: a proof of the Riemann hypothesis. The news follows several other major breakthroughs in math that have either been enabled by or managed entirely by large language model-based systems, a development that has left the entire field of mathematics in a state of upset. It’s too early to say much about the proof itself—mathematicians are still in the process of digesting it. But we can say a little about how the proof came about, and what it tells us about the state of math.

上周有消息称，一位数学家利用人工智能协助破解了数学界最大的未解难题之一：黎曼猜想的证明。此前，数学领域已经出现了多项由大语言模型系统辅助或完全主导的重大突破，这一发展使整个数学界陷入了不安。现在评价该证明本身还为时过早，数学家们仍在消化这一成果。但我们可以简要探讨该证明是如何产生的，以及它揭示了数学领域的何种现状。

Tackling Riemann: The Riemann hypothesis dates from the mid-1800s and was formulated by (you will be surprised to learn) Bernhard Riemann. There’s not an obvious way to describe the details of the actual hypothesis simply, but it has implications for the distribution of prime numbers. This gives it significance for areas ranging from number theory to cryptography. As Wikipedia accurately states, “It is considered by pure mathematicians to be one of the most important problems in the field.”

攻克黎曼猜想：黎曼猜想源于19世纪中期，由伯恩哈德·黎曼（你可能会感到惊讶）提出。虽然很难用简单的方式描述该猜想的具体细节，但它对素数的分布具有深远影响。这使得它在从数论到密码学的各个领域都具有重要意义。正如维基百科准确指出的那样，“它被纯数学家认为是该领域最重要的难题之一。”

The proof of the hypothesis was announced by a mathematician named Levent Alpöge, who has been working at Anthropic, and, not surprisingly, used Claude to generate it. His announcement is written in a somewhat florid style (example: “one of which was already new and shocking, making very simple one of the shadows of Riemann over which we mathematicians felt most triumph”), and he has apparently dropped some hints that some of it was meant in jest. But it does make clear that this proof was a longstanding interest of Alpöge’s, and he had some insights into how to connect different areas of math to reach the final proof.

该猜想的证明由曾在Anthropic工作的数学家Levent Alpöge宣布，不出所料，他使用了Claude来生成证明过程。他的公告文风略显华丽（例如：“其中一个已经是新的且令人震惊的，它简化了黎曼阴影中的一个，而我们数学家曾对此感到无比胜利”），并且他显然暗示其中部分内容带有戏谑成分。但可以明确的是，该证明是Alpöge长期关注的课题，他对于如何连接数学的不同领域以达成最终证明有着独到的见解。

The process seems to have involved getting the AI started with handling a simplified “toy” form of some of Alpöge’s insights and then directing it to expand what it found there to the full scope of the Riemann hypothesis. The proof itself comes in two different forms, and the difference between them gets at the heart of why AI usage in math has been controversial. One is a rigorous description of the proof using a programming language named Lean that has allowed the rapid verification that the proof is valid. The second is a description of the logic behind it, which may ultimately be the key for mathematics as a field to benefit from this proof.

这一过程似乎是先让AI处理Alpöge见解中的简化“玩具”模型，然后引导它将所发现的结果扩展到黎曼猜想的全部范畴。证明本身以两种不同的形式呈现，两者之间的差异触及了AI在数学领域应用引发争议的核心。一种是使用名为Lean的编程语言对证明进行的严谨描述，这使得快速验证证明的有效性成为可能。第二种是其背后的逻辑描述，这最终可能是数学领域从该证明中获益的关键。

Proof factories: It’s important to emphasize that, so far at least, AI has been solving problems that humans previously recognized as significant. That significance is typically ascribed to mathematical ideas because of their implications or utility—they either tell us something about areas of math that people care about, or they keep popping up in problems that mathematicians are working on. So far, there are no indications that AI can recognize or formulate interesting and important unresolved problems on its own.

证明工厂：需要强调的是，至少到目前为止，AI解决的都是人类此前认为具有重要意义的问题。这种重要性通常归因于数学思想的影响力或实用性——它们要么揭示了人们关心的数学领域的相关信息，要么不断出现在数学家正在研究的问题中。目前没有任何迹象表明AI能够独立识别或提出有趣且重要的未解难题。

But AI has tackled many problems humans have considered significant with impressive ease. Trained on a body of material that included a lot of advanced math, the software has provided both formal proofs as well as counterexamples that have clearly demonstrated that some proposals are incorrect. When it comes to proofs, AI might have some advantages over people. For starters, it’s not specialized in specific fields of math and can potentially pull together tools from a variety of disciplines. AI also pays no cost (either in terms of career or emotional soundness) from going down the wrong track, giving up, and starting over.

但AI确实以令人印象深刻的轻松方式解决了许多人类认为重要的问题。在包含大量高等数学的资料库中训练后，该软件不仅提供了形式化证明，还提供了明确证明某些命题不成立的反例。在证明方面，AI可能比人类具有一些优势。首先，它不局限于特定的数学领域，能够整合来自多个学科的工具。此外，AI在走错路、放弃并重新开始时无需付出任何代价（无论是职业生涯还是心理健康方面）。

AI models have also had one of their biggest limitations removed via humanity’s development of Lean, a computer language that allows mathematical proofs to be expressed in a way that can be readily verified. Due to their tendency to hallucinate, a description of a proof generated by an AI would still require extensive human validation before it could be accepted, a task that might radically shift the effort/reward ratio of using AI models. But by instructing an AI to include the necessary Lean code as part of its output, verification has become straightforward.

随着人类开发出Lean语言，AI模型的一大局限性已被消除，这种计算机语言使数学证明能够以易于验证的方式表达。由于AI倾向于产生“幻觉”，其生成的证明描述在被接受前仍需经过大量的人工验证，这项任务可能会彻底改变使用AI模型的投入产出比。但通过指示AI将必要的Lean代码作为输出的一部分，验证过程变得简单直接。

The unfortunate aspect of this for mathematics as a field is that it’s often the process of working through the reasoning behind a proof, rather than the step-by-step logic, that advances the field as a whole.

对于数学领域而言，不幸的是，推动整个学科进步的往往是推导证明背后逻辑的过程，而非仅仅是逻辑步骤本身。

Deriving worries: Mathematicians have expressed their fears about this aspect of AI usage in a formal open letter. We’ll try to connect those fears to the recent announcement. Before AI, proofs were typically worked through by people who recognized potential pathways connecting disparate fields of math and spent years finding ways to formalize those intuitions and validate that the math worked. It wasn’t necessarily an efficient use of time if the only output was the proof itself. But that was rarely the case.

衍生担忧：数学家们在一封正式的公开信中表达了对AI应用这一方面的担忧。我们将尝试把这些担忧与最近的公告联系起来。在AI出现之前，证明通常由那些能够识别连接不同数学领域潜在路径的人来完成，他们花费数年时间寻找方法将这些直觉形式化，并验证数学推导是否成立。如果唯一的产出仅仅是证明本身，这未必是时间的有效利用。但事实很少如此。

By finding the connections between problems, this work helped many other people see new ways of tackling whatever issues they were wrestling with. Mathematicians often developed new tools, found shortcuts, or stumbled across related problems while working through their proofs, enriching math as a whole. In other words, the proof itself was often less important for math than the process of generating it.

通过寻找问题之间的联系，这项工作帮助许多其他人找到了解决他们所面临问题的新途径。数学家在推导证明的过程中，往往会开发出新工具、找到捷径或偶然发现相关问题，从而丰富了整个数学领域。换句话说，对于数学而言，证明本身往往不如生成证明的过程重要。

By contrast, the internal workings of large neural networks are notoriously opaque. Asking an AI to explain its reasoning runs into the same problems that asking it anything else does: Since the software has only been selected for producing decent-sounding text, it’s likely to make something up that sounds good when asked about its own internal processes.
