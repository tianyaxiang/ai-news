The US Army is scrambling to contain any potential lead pollution that could spread if an old stockpile of explosives blows up.

美国陆军正忙于控制如果旧炸药库存发生爆炸可能扩散的潜在铅污染。

The US Army is moving ahead with plans to detonate the nation’s stockpile of lead azide at an ammunition plant in Middletown, Iowa, at approximately 9:00AM CST on Wednesday.

美国陆军正按计划推进，将于周三上午9点左右（中部标准时间）在爱荷华州米德尔敦的一家弹药厂引爆全国的叠氮化铅库存。

There are 68 drums holding about 9,700 pounds of lead azide, an unstable chemical compound created to detonate munitions like grenades. The US started using lead azide as a primary explosive almost a century ago before “moving away” from it about 15 years ago because of how difficult the material is to store and transport.

共有68个桶装有约9700磅的叠氮化铅，这是一种用于引爆手榴弹等弹药的不稳定化合物。美国在大约一个世纪前开始使用叠氮化铅作为主要炸药，但在约15年前因其储存和运输难度大而“弃用”了该材料。

Some of the material on site in Iowa is more than 60 years old. The material can crystallize as it degrades over time, becoming unstable enough to explode even at ambient temperatures.

爱荷华州现场的一些材料已有60多年的历史。随着时间的推移，这些材料会发生降解并结晶，变得极不稳定，甚至在环境温度下也会发生爆炸。

The chemical was stored in liquid form, embedded in sawdust and burlap bags inside the drums. Water and ethanol were also added to the containers to keep the lead azide from freezing in the winter. But during a March inspection, ATF noticed “significant crystallization on the exterior of the barrels.”

这种化学品以液体形式储存，嵌入桶内的锯末和粗麻布袋中。容器中还添加了水和乙醇，以防止叠氮化铅在冬季结冰。但在3月份的一次检查中，美国烟酒枪炮及爆炸物管理局（ATF）注意到“桶外部有明显的结晶”。

Officials have been trying to figure out what to do with the crystallized lead azide since that alarming March inspection. The Army initially hired a contractor to safely dissolve the chemicals but canceled the contract earlier this year because crystallization on the outside of the barrels made it too risky to open the lids.

自3月份那次令人担忧的检查以来，官员们一直在设法处理这些结晶的叠氮化铅。陆军最初聘请了一家承包商来安全溶解这些化学品，但今年早些时候取消了合同，因为桶外的结晶使得打开桶盖的风险过高。

Compared to an uncontrolled, spontaneous explosion, a controlled detonation is supposed to limit the amount of lead dust unleashed on surrounding communities and the environment. Still, the dust plume could reach as far as 100 miles away from the site.

与不受控制的自发爆炸相比，受控引爆旨在限制释放到周边社区和环境中的铅尘量。尽管如此，尘埃云仍可能扩散到距离现场100英里远的地方。

The Iowa Department of Health and Human Services (Iowa HHS) recommends that anyone in potentially affected areas stay indoors and keep windows shut for 24 to 48 hours after the detonation.

爱荷华州卫生与公众服务部（Iowa HHS）建议，潜在受影响地区的居民在引爆后24至48小时内留在室内并关闭窗户。
