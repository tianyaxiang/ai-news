### How Intentful uses dots to make more time for growth

**Intentful 如何利用“点”（dots）来腾出更多时间实现增长**

2026 年 11 月 24 日。Intentful 如何利用“点”来腾出更多时间实现增长。一位小企业创始人如何通过她的“点”来协助研究和提案，同时接收关键信息提醒并解决问题。探索面向小型企业的 ChatGPT。公司规模：中小企业。地区：北美。行业：服务业。产品：ChatGPT。分享。

Marina Petrova is CEO and cofounder of Intentful, a lean team that helps brands make their marketing interactive, with products such as ads that answer customers’ questions in real time and tools that make website information readable by AI. The company also advises and trains marketers on using AI.

Marina Petrova 是 Intentful 的首席执行官兼联合创始人。这是一个精简的团队，致力于帮助品牌实现营销互动，其产品包括能实时回答客户问题的广告，以及能让网站信息被人工智能读取的工具。该公司还为营销人员提供人工智能使用方面的咨询和培训。

In travel and tourism, Intentful works with destination organizations and local businesses such as hotels, restaurants, and attractions. A custom proposal is part of how Marina wins their business, showing prospects what’s possible and what it would take to deliver.

在旅游业领域，Intentful 与目的地组织及酒店、餐厅和景点等当地企业合作。定制化提案是 Marina 赢得业务的方式之一，它向潜在客户展示了各种可能性以及交付所需的工作内容。

To keep Intentful growing, she needed more room for that strategic work, but she was spending too much time on research, hunting for files, and formatting decks. Her dot changed all that, taking on tasks and meeting Marina’s needs before she even had to ask. “My dot makes me feel like I gained a new superpower.”—Marina Petrova, CEO and cofounder, Intentful.

为了保持 Intentful 的增长，她需要更多空间来进行战略性工作，但她却花费了太多时间在研究、查找文件和格式化演示文稿上。她的“点”改变了这一切，它主动承担任务，并在 Marina 提出要求之前就满足了她的需求。“我的‘点’让我感觉自己获得了一种新的超能力。”——Marina Petrova，Intentful 首席执行官兼联合创始人。

From 300 websites to a proposal in hours instead of days. For one of Intentful’s tourism clients, Marina wanted to propose a package of Intentful’s AI services for hundreds of local businesses in the destination. Before she could price the package, she needed to work out which businesses’ websites the package could cover and what Intentful had charged other clients for similar work.

从 300 个网站到一份提案，耗时从几天缩短至几小时。对于 Intentful 的一家旅游业客户，Marina 想要为该目的地数百家当地企业提议一套 Intentful 的人工智能服务方案。在为该方案定价之前，她需要确定该方案能覆盖哪些企业的网站，以及 Intentful 此前为类似工作向其他客户收取了多少费用。

She brought in her dot, which she named Aria, to help her get from research to a proposal she could put her name on in a few hours instead of several days. First, Marina gave Aria a spreadsheet of URLs and criteria for deciding which sites to include. For example, a site needed a readable sitemap listing no more than 3,000 pages. “My dot looked through about 300 website sitemaps, helped me figure out which sites to include in a new offer for local businesses, and pulled pricing from past agreements.”—Marina Petrova, CEO and cofounder, Intentful.

她启用了名为 Aria 的“点”，帮助她在几小时内（而非几天内）完成从研究到提案的全过程，并最终签署自己的名字。首先，Marina 给 Aria 提供了一份包含网址的电子表格，以及决定纳入哪些网站的标准。例如，网站必须拥有可读取的网站地图，且页面数量不超过 3,000 个。“我的‘点’浏览了大约 300 个网站地图，帮我确定了哪些网站应包含在面向当地企业的新方案中，并从过去的协议中提取了定价信息。”——Marina Petrova，Intentful 首席执行官兼联合创始人。

Next, Aria helped Marina research pricing. Through ChatGPT plugins, Aria reviewed Intentful’s sales data, including past engagements and discussions in HubSpot. It surfaced pricing from earlier agreements and context from Intentful’s plans for the following year.

接下来，Aria 帮助 Marina 研究定价。通过 ChatGPT 插件，Aria 审查了 Intentful 的销售数据，包括过去的业务往来和 HubSpot 中的讨论记录。它从早期的协议中提取了定价信息，并结合了 Intentful 来年的计划背景。

Using that data, Marina and Aria then built a pricing model: page counts determined the pricing tier, and previous customer agreements also informed the price. “We worked through a custom pricing model together, and then Aria turned it into a one-page proposal I reviewed and edited,” said Marina.

利用这些数据，Marina 和 Aria 构建了一个定价模型：页面数量决定定价层级，之前的客户协议也为价格提供了参考。“我们共同制定了一个定制化的定价模型，然后 Aria 将其转化为一份单页提案，我对其进行了审阅和编辑，”Marina 说道。

When drafting proposals, Aria knows to use Intentful’s templates from Google Drive. Marina can also point Aria to an earlier proposal as a reference, so the draft follows Intentful’s style from the start. “I need to say what needs to be in a proposal, but I don’t need to spend an hour figuring out its layout. My dot handles that,” Marina says.

在起草提案时，Aria 知道使用 Google Drive 中 Intentful 的模板。Marina 还可以指定一份之前的提案作为参考，这样草稿从一开始就符合 Intentful 的风格。“我只需要说明提案中需要包含什么，而不必花一小时去设计排版。我的‘点’会处理这些，”Marina 说。

Aria can also spot things Marina hadn’t thought to check. When Marina asked Aria to reuse pricing from an earlier proposal, it noticed that the original renewal date didn’t match the timing she remembered. Aria flagged the discrepancy and left the date out of the new draft.

Aria 还能发现 Marina 未曾想到要检查的问题。当 Marina 要求 Aria 复用之前提案中的定价时，它注意到原始续约日期与她记忆中的时间不符。Aria 标记了这一差异，并在新草稿中删除了该日期。

Staying ahead of messages, tasks, and team updates. Marina uses the Gmail and Slack plugins in ChatGPT to catch up on conversations, find open tasks, and see where the team needs her. Aria checks for messages that need Marina’s attention. While Marina was offline, Aria spotted a reply she had been waiting for and let her know it included an invitation for a call that morning.

保持对消息、任务和团队更新的掌控。Marina 使用 ChatGPT 中的 Gmail 和 Slack 插件来跟进对话、查找待办任务，并了解团队在哪些方面需要她的支持。Aria 会检查需要 Marina 注意的消息。当 Marina 离线时，Aria 发现了她一直在等待的回复，并告知她其中包含当天上午的通话邀请。

Marina also uses Aria to talk through ideas, find conversations, check an account’s history in the CRM, or open and assign tasks in Asana. She often briefs Aria by phone while she travels. “On a long drive, I call my dot and talk through what needs to get done. We chat through updates for client proposals, and my dot already knows the structure, the formatting, and what needs to be done.”—Marina Petrova, CEO and cofounder, Intentful.

Marina 还利用 Aria 来探讨想法、查找对话、查看 CRM 中的账户历史记录，或在 Asana 中创建并分配任务。她在旅行时经常通过电话向 Aria 简要说明情况。“在长途驾驶时，我会给我的‘点’打电话，讨论需要完成的工作。我们交流客户提案的更新内容，而我的‘点’已经了解结构、格式以及需要完成的事项。”——Marina Petrova，Intentful 首席执行官兼联合创始人。

Marina loves that her dot works in the tools she already uses. When Aria writes a draft, Marina can leave comments in Google Docs exactly where she wants changes. On longer documents, she asks Aria to use suggesting mode so she can see what changed.

Marina 很喜欢她的“点”能在她现有的工具中工作。当 Aria 撰写草稿时，Marina 可以在 Google Docs 中她想要修改的地方直接留下评论。对于较长的文档，她会要求 Aria 使用建议模式，以便她查看修改内容。

Aria also keeps up with changes in shared work without being asked. For a conference presentation, Marina created the deck and a colleague added two slides. “Aria noticed the new slides, updated the speaker notes without being asked, and let me know,” Marina says.

Aria 还能在无需提醒的情况下跟进共享工作中的变更。在一次会议演示中，Marina 创建了演示文稿，一位同事添加了两张幻灯片。“Aria 注意到了新幻灯片，在没被要求的情况下更新了演讲者备注，并通知了我，”Marina 说。

Marina chooses which information Aria can access and always reviews outgoing email. She can keep work moving without handing over the final call.

Marina 可以选择 Aria 能访问哪些信息，并始终会对发出的电子邮件进行审核。她可以在不交出最终决策权的情况下保持工作进度。

Using dots to teach others about AI. When Marina prepared an AI 101 session on agents, she used Aria to help build the lesson. The process was itself an example of what she wanted attendees to learn: delegate a real task, give the agent enough context, challenge the result, and stay in control of what goes out.

利用“点”向他人传授人工智能知识。当 Marina 准备关于智能体（agents）的 AI 101 课程时，她利用 Aria 来协助构建课程内容。这个过程本身就是她希望学员学习的范例：委派真实任务、为智能体提供足够的背景信息、质疑结果，并掌控最终输出的内容。

Aria’s support continued even after the class. After a flight, Marina saw a participant’s request for access to a training file. When she checked with Aria, it had already shared the file. And after a session for 50 marketers, Aria noticed that a software glitch was preventing participants from opening the recording. It flagged the access issue and offered to fix it before the link was shared. “With my dot, I can focus on strategic things. I can focus on ideas and growth.”—Marina Petrova, CEO and cofounder, Intentful.

Aria 的支持在课后仍在继续。下飞机后，Marina 看到一位学员请求访问培训文件。当她向 Aria 核实时，它已经共享了该文件。在为 50 名营销人员举办课程后，Aria 注意到一个软件故障导致学员无法打开录像。它标记了访问问题，并在链接共享前主动提出修复。“有了我的‘点’，我可以专注于战略性事务。我可以专注于创意和增长。”——Marina Petrova，Intentful 首席执行官兼联合创始人。
