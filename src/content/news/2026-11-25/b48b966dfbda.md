TL;DR I made a romhack which implements the most common urban legends from the gen 1 Pokemon games. Catching Mew under the truck Including: Spam A to increase catch chance Magikarp splash has a 1/256 chance to 1-shot KO Talk to Bill 100 times and you can go to his garden You can catch all three starters in Bill's garden You can catch Pikablu in Bill's garden You can battle Professor Oak after completing the Pokedex ... and of course, Mew is under the truck

简而言之，我制作了一个 ROM 改版，实现了第一代宝可梦游戏中最常见的都市传说。包括在卡车底下捕捉梦幻：连按 A 键增加捕捉几率；鲤鱼王的“跃起”有 1/256 的几率一击必杀；和正辉对话 100 次就能进入他的花园；可以在正辉的花园里捕捉所有三只御三家；可以在正辉的花园里捕捉皮卡蓝；集齐图鉴后可以挑战大木博士……当然，梦幻就在卡车底下。

How do I actually get to the truck tho? The old fashioned way! I did not make the actual getting to the truck any easier. You have to either trade for a Pokemon that knows CUT, to avoid entering the S.S. Anne. Or, you can enter the ship and collect the CUT HM as usual, then backtrack to a trainer on the ship that you have not already defeated, battle them, and let them win. This will teleport you back to the nearest Pokecentre without triggering the S.S. Anne to leave.

那么我到底该怎么去卡车那里呢？用老办法！我并没有让到达卡车的过程变得更容易。你必须要么通过交换获得一只会“居合斩”的宝可梦，从而避免进入圣特安努号；要么像往常一样进入船只并收集“居合斩”秘传机，然后折返回船上找一个还没打过的训练家，在对战中故意输掉。这会把你传送回最近的宝可梦中心，且不会触发圣特安努号离开的剧情。
