# Two Agencies Wrote "Top Agencies" Lists — One Put Itself at #1

A reader question sent us down a rabbit hole worth sharing: when agencies write their own "top agencies in the UK" listicles — which, as we found earlier this week, is how the UK's list layer works — do those lists read like editorial or like service pages wearing a list costume?

一位读者的提问让我们陷入了一个值得分享的深究之中：当代理机构撰写自己的“英国顶级代理机构”列表文章时——正如我们本周早些时候发现的那样，这就是英国列表层面的运作方式——这些列表读起来像是编辑内容，还是披着列表外衣的服务页面？

Four agency-written roundups rank on page one for "content marketing agency" (UK). We opened two of them — the sharpest contrast pair — and checked where the author's own agency sits. Munro's "Top 12 Content Marketing Agencies": Munro Agency is entry #1. The section reads like a service page — Glasgow-based, what they position content as — with competitors filling the remaining slots. Ranking-as-packaging.

有四篇由代理机构撰写的综述文章在“内容营销代理机构”（英国）的搜索结果首页排名。我们打开了其中两篇——对比最鲜明的一对——并查看了作者所属机构的位置。Munro 的“12 大内容营销代理机构”：Munro Agency 位列第一。该部分读起来就像一个服务页面——介绍了其位于格拉斯哥的总部以及他们对内容的定位——而竞争对手则填补了其余位置。这是一种“包装式排名”。

Koozai's "Best Content Marketing Agencies in the UK": Koozai is entry #8 of 20, and the list runs alphabetically — Brafton, Cheshire Cat, CopyHouse, Found, Honest London, inBeat, Komodo… Before any agency appears, the post documents its evaluation criteria: strategy quality and research depth, editorial standards, measurement and reporting. Editorial-as-marketing.

Koozai 的“英国最佳内容营销代理机构”：Koozai 在 20 家机构中排名第八，且列表按字母顺序排列——Brafton、Cheshire Cat、CopyHouse、Found、Honest London、inBeat、Komodo……在任何代理机构出现之前，该文章记录了其评估标准：策略质量与研究深度、编辑标准、衡量与报告。这是一种“营销式编辑”。

Same SERP, same tactic ("agencies write the lists"), opposite integrity. And from the outside, the tell is cheap to check: sort order. Alphabetical with stated criteria reads as editorial. Self at the top reads as packaging.

同样的搜索结果页面，同样的策略（“代理机构撰写列表”），却有着截然不同的诚信度。从外部来看，辨别的方法很简单：排序方式。按字母顺序排列并附带明确标准的内容读起来像是编辑文章。将自己排在首位则读起来像是包装宣传。

Why this matters beyond trivia: if you're an agency deciding whether to invest in a listicle, the Koozai shape — criteria first, alphabetical order, yourself mid-list — is the version a skeptical reader can verify. If you're a buyer skimming "top agencies" posts, scroll to the order before you scroll to the names.

这不仅仅是琐事，其重要性在于：如果你是一家正在决定是否要投资撰写列表文章的代理机构，Koozai 的模式——先列出标准、按字母顺序排列、将自己放在列表中间——是持怀疑态度的读者可以验证的版本。如果你是一位浏览“顶级代理机构”文章的买家，在看名字之前，请先看看排序方式。

(Background: we make a marketing-data tool, and both lists are archived as pulled — any roundup in your category can be checked the same way.) Next time a "top 12" post crosses your feed: glance at slot #1 before trusting slots #2-12.
