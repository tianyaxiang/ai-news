Pikmin 4 is $20 off ahead of its $10 Nintendo Switch 2 update.

《皮克敏 4》在 10 美元的任天堂 Switch 2 更新发布前降价 20 美元。

The base game is only $40, and the upgrade to the Switch 2 Edition will only cost $10, saving you $20 total.

基础游戏仅需 40 美元，升级到 Switch 2 版本仅需 10 美元，总共可节省 20 美元。

If you’re wondering what video game to play this weekend, my colleague Sheena Vasani spotted a discounted Nintendo Switch title with instant digital delivery. Walmart has the digital version of Pikmin 4 for Switch discounted to $39.88, just over $20 lower than its usual price.

如果你正在考虑这个周末玩什么电子游戏，我的同事 Sheena Vasani 发现了一款提供即时数字交付的打折任天堂 Switch 游戏。沃尔玛目前将 Switch 版《皮克敏 4》的数字版价格降至 39.88 美元，比其通常价格低了 20 多美元。

This version of the game follows the same puzzle formula as previous titles in the series, but adds in a dog companion for an extra layer of complexity and action, and dives deeper into the dark undertones previous games hinted at.

该版本的游戏沿用了该系列前作相同的解谜模式，但增加了一位狗狗同伴，增添了额外的复杂性和动作元素，并深入探讨了前作中曾暗示过的黑暗基调。

Nintendo recently announced a Switch 2 version of Pikmin 4 will launch on November 12th, 2026. That version features polished visuals, adds in voice commands for your dog companion, plus a new mode called Dandori Academy that adds extra challenges over time.

任天堂最近宣布，《皮克敏 4》的 Switch 2 版本将于 2026 年 11 月 12 日发布。该版本具有更精致的视觉效果，增加了狗狗同伴的语音指令，并新增了一个名为“Dandori Academy”的模式，该模式会随时间推移增加额外的挑战。

You’ll be able to upgrade to that version for just $10, saving you $20 total over buying the $69.99 Switch 2 Edition outright.

你只需支付 10 美元即可升级到该版本，相比直接购买 69.99 美元的 Switch 2 版本，总共可节省 20 美元。
