Cross/The Washington Post/Bloomberg / Getty Images Anthony Ha TikTok permanently bans ‘Musk’ documentary advertising account A four-hour documentary about Elon Musk continues to meet resistance from social media platforms, with TikTok banning the film’s advertising account.

简讯发布于太平洋夏令时间 2026 年 10 月 17 日下午 1:55。图片来源：Tierney L. Cross/华盛顿邮报/彭博社/盖蒂图片社。安东尼·哈（Anthony Ha）报道：TikTok 永久封禁了“马斯克”纪录片的广告账户。一部关于埃隆·马斯克的四小时纪录片持续在社交媒体平台上遭遇阻力，TikTok 封禁了该片的广告账户。

According to a story in The Hollywood Reporter, the documentary’s distributor Bleecker Street was recently notified by TikTok that the account had received a “permanent suspension” due to its political content. TechCrunch has reached out to TikTok for comment.

据《好莱坞报道》的一篇报道称，该纪录片的发行商 Bleecker Street 最近收到 TikTok 的通知，称其账户因政治内容被“永久封禁”。TechCrunch 已联系 TikTok 请求置评。

Ads promoting the trailer for “Musk” — directed by Oscar-winner filmmaker Alex Gibney — were initially rejected by Meta and YouTube as well, though those platforms eventually allowed the ad, with Meta blaming the rejection on “an error.” Gibney recently confirmed that the film’s international distributor Universal had wavered in its plans to release “Musk,” though it’s now moving forward. X, which Musk owns, has refused to carry ads for “Musk.”

由奥斯卡获奖导演亚历克斯·吉布尼（Alex Gibney）执导的“马斯克”预告片广告最初也被 Meta 和 YouTube 拒绝，尽管这些平台最终允许了广告投放，Meta 将拒绝原因归咎于“失误”。吉布尼最近证实，该片的国际发行商环球影业曾对其发行“马斯克”的计划犹豫不决，但目前该计划正在推进中。马斯克拥有的 X 平台则拒绝为“马斯克”投放广告。

TikTok’s U.S. operations are partly owned by Larry Ellison — who, like Musk, has been a notable supporter of President Donald Trump. “Musk” opened in just four theaters last weekend and is now rolling out more broadly. Next year, it’s supposed to stream on HBO — now part of Paramount/Skydance and led by Ellison’s son David.

TikTok 的美国业务由拉里·埃里森（Larry Ellison）部分持有，他与马斯克一样，是唐纳德·特朗普总统的重要支持者。“马斯克”上周末仅在四家影院上映，目前正扩大放映范围。明年，该片预计将在 HBO 上线——HBO 现隶属于派拉蒙/Skydance，由埃里森的儿子大卫（David）领导。
