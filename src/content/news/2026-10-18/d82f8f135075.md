New: decision models, powered by Ollaya. PS5LM now runs Jev-like decision models (d1, Clef, OpenJev and more) on the GPU and answers the same /v1/systemone API as Ollaya: typed decisions with calibrated probabilities, in a fraction of a second.

新功能：由 Ollaya 提供支持的决策模型。PS5LM 现在可以在 GPU 上运行类似 Jev 的决策模型（d1、Clef、OpenJev 等），并响应与 Ollaya 相同的 /v1/systemone API：在几分之一秒内提供带有校准概率的类型化决策。

PS5LM is for anyone with a jailbroken PS5 who wants a private model server at home. It loads a GGUF model onto the console's GPU and serves it to your laptop, your phone and your coding tools. It needs no PC while it runs, no cloud and no account.

PS5LM 专为拥有越狱 PS5 并希望在家中搭建私有模型服务器的用户设计。它将 GGUF 模型加载到主机的 GPU 上，并为您的笔记本电脑、手机和编程工具提供服务。运行过程中无需 PC、云端支持或账户。

Ollaya runs open decision models on a PC the way Ollama runs language models. PS5LM brings the same idea to the console: a decision model reads a state and answers typed questions in one forward pass, with no text generated.

Ollaya 在 PC 上运行开放决策模型的方式与 Ollama 运行语言模型的方式相同。PS5LM 将这一理念引入了主机：决策模型通过一次前向传递读取状态并回答类型化问题，无需生成文本。

The Suggested list has d1 Omni 600M, d1 3B and Clef Flash. Any GGUF that carries llama.cpp's decision metadata loads the same way, and the app labels it a decision model.

建议列表中包含 d1 Omni 600M、d1 3B 和 Clef Flash。任何带有 llama.cpp 决策元数据的 GGUF 模型均可按相同方式加载，应用会将其标记为决策模型。
