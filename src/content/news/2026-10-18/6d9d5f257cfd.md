# Reverse-engineer a creator's style: an AI persona and style guide from their latest videos

Disclosure: I build TubeText. This post uses our Video Breakdown AI Actor on Apify. The output below is from a run I did today. When you study a creator, the useful question isn't "what is this video about?" but "what does this person do every time?": which hooks they open with, how fast they cut, what they sell, how they end. Answering that means watching a dozen videos with a notepad. Here's how I get a first draft of that notepad from one API call: a persona and style guide built from a creator's latest videos, using Video Breakdown AI.

披露：我开发了 TubeText。本文使用了我们在 Apify 上运行的 Video Breakdown AI Actor。以下输出是我今天运行的结果。当你研究一位创作者时，有用的问题不是“这个视频讲了什么？”，而是“这个人每次都做了什么？”：他们以什么样的钩子开头，剪辑速度有多快，他们推销什么，以及他们如何结尾。要回答这些问题，意味着要拿着笔记本观看十几部视频。以下是我如何通过一次 API 调用获得该笔记本初稿的方法：使用 Video Breakdown AI，根据创作者的最新视频构建人物画像和风格指南。

One call: a channel in, a style guide out
curl -X POST "https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/tubetext~video-breakdown-ai/run-sync-get-dataset-items?token=$APIFY_TOKEN" \
-H 'Content-Type: application/json' \
-d '{"urls":["https://www.youtube.com/@mkbhd/shorts"],"maxVideosPerSource":5,"accountSummary":true}'

一次调用：输入频道，输出风格指南
curl -X POST "https://api.apify.com/v2/acts/tubetext~video-breakdown-ai/run-sync-get-dataset-items?token=$APIFY_TOKEN" \

-d '{"urls":["https://www.youtube.com/@mkbhd/shorts"],"maxVideosPerSource":5,"accountSummary":true}'

It also returns a persona, tone and voice, on-screen text style, products and brands, posting cadence, and a style guide of 5 to 8 rules. Three of the seven it wrote for this channel:
"Start with an immediate visual or auditory hook that defies expectations."
"Prioritize 'show, don't tell' by using high-quality B-roll to demonstrate features."
"Keep on-screen text minimal, using it only for critical data points or emphasis."

它还会返回人物画像、语气和语调、屏幕文字风格、产品和品牌、发布频率，以及包含 5 到 8 条规则的风格指南。它为该频道撰写的七条规则中的三条如下：
“以打破预期的即时视觉或听觉钩子开头。”
“通过使用高质量的 B-roll（辅助镜头）来展示功能，优先考虑‘展示而非讲述’。”
“保持屏幕文字简洁，仅用于关键数据点或强调。”

Two things I find more useful than the prose:
The hook mix is countable. 3 of 5 Shorts open on a visual surprise and 2 on a bold spoken claim. Run it on three competitors and you can compare those shares directly.
The medians give you a brief. About 66 seconds, about 10 cuts a minute, 11 shots. That's a concrete target for an editor, not "make it punchy".

比起文字描述，我发现以下两点更有用：
钩子组合是可以量化的。5 个 Shorts 中有 3 个以视觉惊喜开头，2 个以大胆的口头断言开头。在三个竞争对手身上运行此工具，你可以直接比较这些比例。
中位数为你提供了一个简报。大约 66 秒，每分钟约 10 次剪辑，11 个镜头。这对剪辑师来说是一个具体的目标，而不是“让它更有冲击力”这种模糊的要求。

Caveats
5 videos is a small sample. The shares (60%, 40%) are counts, not statistics. Use 10 to 20 videos for anything you'll act on. The persona and style guide are a model's reading of the videos. Treat them as a first draft to check against the per-video rows, not as fact. Instagram profiles often hide view counts. When that happens, the summary says so instead of guessing what performs best.
