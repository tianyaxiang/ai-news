### What Is PPFD?

### 什么是 PPFD？

Photosynthetic photon flux density, or PPFD, expressed in micromoles per square meter per second (μmol/m²/s), is a measurement of how much light reaches your plant that's usable for photosynthesis. In general, the higher the plant's energy needs, such as producing a flower or a fruit, the higher the PPFD number you want. Though different lights have different height requirements given the plants they're made for, for standardization purposes, I measured the PPFD of each grow light at 18 inches with the Photone app and a light diffuser. Keep in mind these measurements are general and approximate.

光合光子通量密度（PPFD），以微摩尔每平方米每秒（μmol/m²/s）为单位，是衡量到达植物且可用于光合作用的光照量的指标。通常情况下，植物的能量需求越高（例如开花或结果），你所需要的 PPFD 数值就越高。虽然不同的灯具根据其适用的植物有不同的高度要求，但为了标准化，我使用 Photone 应用程序和光扩散器在 18 英寸处测量了每种生长灯的 PPFD。请记住，这些测量结果是通用的近似值。

### How I Test Grow Lights

### 我是如何测试生长灯的

Some of these grow lights I bought myself, with my own money, before I even began working at WIRED. Others were owned by staffers and recommended to me. I also have press samples from other guides I worked on, which I liked so much I ended up keeping around.

其中一些生长灯是我在加入《连线》之前自费购买的。另一些则是员工自有的并推荐给我的。我还有一些来自我参与制作的其他指南的媒体样品，因为非常喜欢，最终留了下来。

For the traditional grow lights, I tested them at home under normal-life conditions. I put them where they'd fit, tested their timers, and noted whether or not they were dimmable. I also measured each light's PPFD (see above) and noted whether certain lights could be daisy-chained for more extensive coverage.

对于传统的生长灯，我在家中正常的居住条件下进行了测试。我将它们放置在合适的位置，测试了定时器，并记录了它们是否可调光。我还测量了每盏灯的 PPFD（见上文），并记录了某些灯具是否可以串联以实现更广泛的覆盖。
