# How to watch Apple’s foldable iPhone announcement
# 如何观看苹果折叠屏 iPhone 发布会

Apple will host its annual iPhone event on Wednesday, September 9 at 10 a.m. PDT, and it’s expected to be a big one. All signs point to Apple’s long-awaited entrance into the foldable smartphone market, finally shipping this kind of hardware about seven years after Samsung did.

苹果将于太平洋夏令时间 9 月 9 日星期三上午 10 点举办年度 iPhone 发布会，预计这将是一场重磅活动。种种迹象表明，苹果终于要进军期待已久的折叠屏智能手机市场，在三星推出此类硬件约七年后，苹果也将正式发布其折叠屏产品。

Apple has a YouTube stream prepared for the “Surprise and shine” event, and you can set reminder notifications within YouTube to make sure you don’t miss it (or you can bookmark this page). You can also watch the livestream on Apple’s own events site.

苹果已为这场名为“Surprise and shine”（惊喜与闪耀）的活动准备了 YouTube 直播，你可以在 YouTube 上设置提醒通知，确保不会错过（或者你也可以收藏此页面）。你也可以在苹果官方活动网站上观看直播。

Aside from the foldable iPhone Ultra, Apple is also expected to announce the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max models, for people who want an upgrade but don’t see the appeal of the foldable, or who don’t want to pay the rumored $2,000 price for the iPhone Ultra. The standard iPhone 18 model isn’t expected to launch until next year.

除了折叠屏 iPhone Ultra 外，预计苹果还将发布 iPhone 18 Pro 和 iPhone 18 Pro Max 机型，以满足那些想要升级手机但对折叠屏不感兴趣，或者不想支付传闻中 iPhone Ultra 高达 2000 美元售价的用户。标准版 iPhone 18 预计要到明年才会发布。

Apple may also release new hardware like an updated HomePod, camera-enabled AirPods, and new Apple Watches. With new Apple CEO John Ternus at the helm, this will be Apple’s first major product announcement since longtime CEO Tim Cook stepped down.

苹果还可能发布其他新硬件，例如更新版的 HomePod、配备摄像头的 AirPods 以及新款 Apple Watch。随着新任首席执行官 John Ternus 上任，这将是自长期担任 CEO 的蒂姆·库克（Tim Cook）卸任以来，苹果举办的首场重大产品发布会。
