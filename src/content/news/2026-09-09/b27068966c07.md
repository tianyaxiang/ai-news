# Godzilla Minus Zero IMAX trailer teases King Ghidorah
# 《哥斯拉：负零》IMAX预告片暗示王者基多拉登场

One of the cinematic highlights of 2023 was director Takashi Yamazaki’s gloriously grounded kaiju epic, *Godzilla Minus One*, which became a major international hit and snagged an Oscar for best visual effects. Yamazaki is back this fall with the hotly anticipated sequel, *Godzilla Minus Zero*. Toho just dropped the full IMAX trailer, featuring not just our beloved Godzilla, but also a distinctive roar that teases the appearance of none other than the three-headed King Ghidorah.
2023 年的电影亮点之一，是导演山崎贵执导的怪兽史诗巨作《哥斯拉-1.0》（Godzilla Minus One）。该片不仅在全球范围内大获成功，还斩获了奥斯卡最佳视觉效果奖。今年秋季，山崎贵携备受期待的续集《哥斯拉：负零》（Godzilla Minus Zero）回归。东宝公司刚刚发布了完整的 IMAX 预告片，其中不仅出现了我们挚爱的哥斯拉，还伴随着一声独特的咆哮，暗示了三头怪兽“王者基多拉”的登场。

(Spoilers for the 2023 film below.) *Godzilla Minus One* focused on kamikaze pilot Kōichi Shikishima, who struggled with survivor’s guilt after he failed to take action when Godzilla attacked the Japanese base on Odo Island in 1945. Knocked unconscious, Kōichi woke to find he was one of only two survivors—and the other, a mechanic named Tachibana, was furious about it. His parents and many others were killed in the World War II bombing of Tokyo. Kōichi rebuilt a life with a woman named Noriko and the orphaned baby she rescued, Akiko. But then Godzilla re-emerged, and Kōichi resolved to die fighting the monster to atone for Odo Island.
（以下内容包含 2023 年前作剧透。）《哥斯拉-1.0》讲述了神风特攻队飞行员敷岛浩一的故事。1945 年，当哥斯拉袭击大户岛的日本基地时，敷岛因未能采取行动而陷入幸存者内疚中。他在昏迷中醒来，发现自己是仅有的两名幸存者之一，而另一位幸存者——机械师橘宗作对此感到愤怒。敷岛的父母和其他许多人在二战东京大轰炸中丧生。后来，敷岛与一名叫典子的女子以及她救下的孤儿明子重建了生活。但随后哥斯拉再次出现，敷岛决定以死相搏，以此为大户岛的失职赎罪。

Spoiler alert: Kōichi survived, thanks to Tachibana’s forgiveness and mechanical ingenuity. He reunited with Noriko, who had a strange black bruise on her neck. And we saw a chunk of Godzilla’s remains start to regenerate, leaving things open for a sequel. And now it’s here.
剧透预警：多亏了橘宗作的宽恕和机械天赋，敷岛最终幸存了下来。他与典子重逢，但典子的脖子上出现了一块奇怪的黑色淤青。同时，我们看到哥斯拉的一块残骸开始再生，为续集留下了伏笔。现在，续集终于来了。

Per the official synopsis for *Godzilla Minus Zero*: War reduced Japan to zero, and Godzilla plunged it into minus. Two years have passed since then, and the country has faced agonizing struggles to achieve recovery and finally reclaim its daily life. Just as humanity managed to cling to a hard-won peace, a new threat strikes, crushing hope in an instant. There is no third time. Everything ends here.
根据《哥斯拉：负零》的官方剧情简介：战争将日本化为零，而哥斯拉将其推向了负数。两年过去了，这个国家在痛苦的挣扎中寻求复苏，终于找回了日常生活。就在人类好不容易守住来之不易的和平时，新的威胁袭来，瞬间粉碎了希望。没有第三次机会了，一切都将在此终结。

Most of the main cast from *Godzilla Minus One* are returning: Ryunosuke Kamiki as Kōichi; Minami Hamabe as his wife, Noriko; Sae Nagatani as Akiko; Hidetaka Yoshioka as Kenji Noda, director of the Large-Scale Disaster Response Bureau; Kuranosuke Sasaki as Seiji Akitsu, captain of the Shinsei Maru; Miou Tanaka as Tatsuo Hotta, former captain of the destroyer Yukikaze; Sakura Ando as Sumiko Ōta, Kōichi’s neighbor; and Yuki Yamada as Shirō Mizushima, a Shinsei Maru crewman. The cast also includes Min Tanaka as biologist Kanji Murakami and Masami Nagasawa as Aoyama.
《哥斯拉-1.0》的大部分主演回归：神木隆之介饰演敷岛浩一；滨边美波饰演他的妻子典子；永谷咲笑饰演明子；吉冈秀隆饰演大型灾害对策局局长野田健治；佐佐木藏之介饰演“新生丸”号船长秋津清治；田中美央饰演驱逐舰“雪风”号前船长堀田辰雄；安藤樱饰演敷岛的邻居太田澄子；山田裕贵饰演“新生丸”号船员水岛四郎。演员阵容还包括饰演生物学家村上勘治的田中泯，以及饰演青山的长泽雅美。

*Godzilla Minus Zero* stomps into theaters in Japan on November 3 and in North America on November 6. The film will have its official world premiere later this month at the New York Film Festival. For those who can’t get enough of Godzilla, Legendary Pictures plans to release *Godzilla x Kong: Supernova*—its Monsterverse sequel to 2024’s *Godzilla x Kong: The New Empire*—in 2027.
《哥斯拉：负零》将于 11 月 3 日在日本影院上映，11 月 6 日在北美上映。该片将于本月晚些时候在纽约电影节举行全球首映。对于那些对哥斯拉意犹未尽的影迷，传奇影业计划在 2027 年推出《哥斯拉大战金刚：超新星》（Godzilla x Kong: Supernova），这是 2024 年《哥斯拉大战金刚 2：帝国崛起》的怪兽宇宙续集。
