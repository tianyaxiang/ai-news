# Razer’s ANC-equipped gaming headset for PS5 and Xbox is almost $100 off
# 雷蛇（Razer）配备主动降噪功能的 PS5 和 Xbox 游戏耳机直降近 100 美元

The BlackShark V3 Pro gaming headset features a clear microphone, ANC, and crisp spatial audio.
BlackShark V3 Pro 游戏耳机配备了清晰的麦克风、主动降噪（ANC）功能以及清脆的空间音频。

It’s rare to find high-end features like active noise cancellation in a gaming headset under $200, so we’re thrilled that the Razer BlackShark V3 Pro is on sale at Woot for $164.99.
在 200 美元以下的游戏耳机中，很难找到像主动降噪这样高端的功能，因此我们很高兴看到 Razer BlackShark V3 Pro 目前在 Woot 网站上以 164.99 美元的价格促销。

There are specific models marketed to Xbox and PlayStation, but the Xbox version is the best choice because it also supports PlayStation, PC, Nintendo Switch and mobile. At just $35 more than the current sale price for the BlackShark V2 Pro, the newer headset adds ANC and simultaneous audio between the 2.4GHz and Bluetooth connections, although you only get a 90-day Woot warranty with this deal.
虽然市面上有针对 Xbox 和 PlayStation 分别销售的特定型号，但 Xbox 版本是最佳选择，因为它同时也支持 PlayStation、PC、任天堂 Switch 和移动设备。这款较新的耳机仅比目前促销中的 BlackShark V2 Pro 贵 35 美元，却增加了主动降噪功能以及 2.4GHz 和蓝牙连接的同步音频功能，不过需要注意的是，此优惠仅提供 90 天的 Woot 保修。

The BlackShark V3 Pro’s main connection mode is with its low latency 2.4GHz transmitter, which plugs into your console or PC via USB. While the headset is somewhat bulky, it can double as headphones you can take on the go thanks to its removable boom mic and Bluetooth 5.3 support. Plus, its noise cancellation can drown out sounds from your commute.
BlackShark V3 Pro 的主要连接方式是通过低延迟的 2.4GHz 发射器，该发射器可通过 USB 插入游戏主机或 PC。虽然这款耳机体积稍显笨重，但得益于其可拆卸的麦克风杆和蓝牙 5.3 支持，它也可以作为便携式耳机使用。此外，它的降噪功能还能有效隔绝通勤途中的噪音。

Fans of Riot’s soon-to-be-retired fighting game, 2XKO, can save an extra $15 for a special edition version of the BlackShark V3 Pro that’s $149.99.
Riot 即将停运的格斗游戏《2XKO》的粉丝们，还可以以 149.99 美元的价格购买特别版 BlackShark V3 Pro，额外节省 15 美元。
