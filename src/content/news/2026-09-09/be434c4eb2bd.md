### Welcome to the spiderverse, a world measured through webs
### 欢迎来到“蜘蛛宇宙”，一个通过蛛网来衡量的世界

Counting the creatures around us is critical for conservation, but it’s often a laborious, costly process that still leaves gaps. Environmental DNA, or eDNA, offers a promising alternative by analyzing genetic material shed by living things. Recently, spiderwebs have emerged as an eDNA goldmine, as they trap material from their arachnid creators, their prey, and bio-detritus like saliva and pollen from nearby plants and animals. Studies found no passive tool matched spiderwebs’ ability to ID vertebrates. Find out how spiderwebs are unlocking better ways to measure nature. —Stephen Ornes
统计我们周围的生物对于保护工作至关重要，但这通常是一个费力且昂贵的过程，且往往存在遗漏。环境 DNA（eDNA）通过分析生物体脱落的遗传物质，提供了一种有前景的替代方案。最近，蛛网已成为 eDNA 的金矿，因为它们能捕获来自蜘蛛本身、猎物以及唾液、花粉等生物碎屑。研究发现，没有任何被动工具能像蛛网那样精准地识别脊椎动物。了解蛛网如何开启衡量自然的新方法。——Stephen Ornes
