# Nike’s new air sole technology can mix soft and firm cushioning

耐克全新的气垫技术能够混合软硬不同的缓冲效果。

The first shoes with Nike’s new Tuned Air technology will be available for runners starting in late 2027.

首批搭载耐克全新 Tuned Air 技术的跑鞋将于 2027 年末面向跑者发售。

In 2024, Nike announced its Air Max 1000 that were manufactured using a 3D-printing process resulting in a shoe that had a uniform silhouette but varying areas of support and flexibility throughout. The company is now doing something similar with its iconic air-filled soles using its new Tuned Air technology. While previously the level of cushioning in Nike Air shoes was uniform across the entire sole, a new manufacturing process can produce multiple air pockets with varying levels of cushioning and stability.

2024 年，耐克发布了 Air Max 1000，该鞋款采用 3D 打印工艺制造，在保持统一外观的同时，实现了不同区域支撑性和灵活性的差异化。如今，该公司正利用全新的 Tuned Air 技术，对其标志性的充气鞋底进行类似的改进。此前，耐克气垫鞋的缓冲水平在整个鞋底是均匀的，而新的制造工艺可以生产出具有不同缓冲和稳定性水平的多个气囊。

Nike has been putting air pockets into its shoes for nearly 50 years as a way to increase comfort and cushioning while making them lighter. Its air pockets have even found their way into apparel as a way to improve insulation by trapping body heat, but Nike plans to use its new Tuned Air technology as a way to customize the cushioning experience in its shoes and tailor them to specific athletes and sports. The first shoes featuring the technology are expected to arrive in late 2027 and will be targeted at runners.

近 50 年来，耐克一直将气囊植入鞋履中，以增加舒适度和缓冲性，同时减轻重量。其气囊甚至被应用到服装中，通过锁住身体热量来提高保暖性。但耐克计划利用全新的 Tuned Air 技术，定制鞋履的缓冲体验，并针对特定运动员和运动项目进行调整。首批采用该技术的鞋款预计将于 2027 年末推出，目标群体为跑者。

While one of Nike’s earliest successes was track shoes with a grip design inspired by a waffle iron, the company credits the “vibrant colors on magazine covers” as the inspiration for its latest shoe technology. A Tuned Air sole is manufactured using a “proprietary digital print and selective lamination process” where an ink is applied in specific patterns that define where the layers bond to each other. This process can be used to create internal pockets in an unlimited combination of shapes and sizes, and by fine-tuning how much air is in each of those pockets, some parts of the sole can feel firmer while others feel softer.

虽然耐克早期的成功之一是受华夫饼机启发而设计出抓地力设计的田径鞋，但该公司将最新鞋履技术的灵感归功于“杂志封面上鲜艳的色彩”。Tuned Air 鞋底采用“专有的数字打印和选择性层压工艺”制造，通过以特定图案涂抹油墨，从而确定各层之间的粘合位置。该工艺可用于制造形状和尺寸组合无限的内部气囊，通过微调每个气囊中的空气量，可以使鞋底某些部位感觉更硬，而另一些部位则更软。

The Tuned Air technology will not only allow Nike to highly customize the fit and feel of air sole shoes for different sports, it will also help keep the benefits of the custom tuning consistent across different sizes. The new manufacturing process will also help reduce the amount of time it takes Nike to go from a concept for a new shoe to a final design ready for production. “Prototypes that once took three weeks can now be made in three days,” the company claims in a release today. That gives Nike’s designers more time to refine new shoes, and more opportunity to experiment.

Tuned Air 技术不仅能让耐克针对不同运动高度定制气垫鞋的贴合度和脚感，还有助于在不同尺码中保持定制调节的优势。新的制造工艺还将缩短耐克从新鞋概念到最终生产设计所需的时间。该公司在今日发布的一份声明中称：“曾经需要三周才能完成的原型，现在只需三天即可制成。”这为耐克的设计师提供了更多时间来打磨新鞋，并创造了更多实验机会。
