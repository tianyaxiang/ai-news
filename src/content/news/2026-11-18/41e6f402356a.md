People who say they were groomed on Roblox testified in front of a Congressional subcommittee Tuesday about safety risks associated with the popular gaming platform frequented by kids and teenagers.

周二，一些声称在 Roblox 上遭到诱骗的人在国会小组委员会前作证，讲述了与这个深受儿童和青少年欢迎的游戏平台相关的安全风险。

“Roblox is one of the most popular gaming platforms in the world,” Republican senator Josh Hawley said in his opening remarks. “If your children are on Roblox they are being exposed in all likelihood to sexual predators … to sexually exploitative content, left, right, and center.”

共和党参议员乔什·霍利（Josh Hawley）在开场白中说：“Roblox 是世界上最受欢迎的游戏平台之一。如果你的孩子在使用 Roblox，他们极有可能接触到性掠夺者……接触到随处可见的性剥削内容。”

The hearing was intended to address the bipartisan legislation introduced by Hawley and Democratic senator Dick Durbin, coauthors of the No Forced Arbitration for Child Sexual Exploitation and Cyber Injuries Act, which would allow families of victims to sue Roblox directly instead of being forced to settle their claims privately in arbitration, as the platform’s terms of service currently dictate.

此次听证会旨在讨论由霍利和民主党参议员迪克·德宾（Dick Durbin）提出的两党立法。他们是《禁止针对儿童性剥削和网络伤害进行强制仲裁法案》的共同提案人，该法案将允许受害者家庭直接起诉 Roblox，而不是像该平台目前的服务条款所规定的那样，被迫在私下仲裁中解决索赔问题。

The process of forced arbitration “strips consumers and workers of their rights,” testified Myriam Gilles, a law professor at Northwestern University who is an expert on forced arbitration. “I never expected to come before this body to talk about its use against 9-year-olds, but that’s where we are.”

西北大学法学教授、强制仲裁专家米里亚姆·吉尔斯（Myriam Gilles）作证称，强制仲裁程序“剥夺了消费者和工人的权利”。她说：“我从未想过会来到这个机构讨论它如何被用于 9 岁的孩子，但这就是我们现在的处境。”

Roblox is currently facing nearly 200 lawsuits from families who allege that their children were groomed by sexual predators posing as children on the platform. According to Hawley, Roblox CEO David Baszucki was invited to testify at the hearing but declined.

Roblox 目前面临近 200 起诉讼，这些家庭指控他们的孩子在平台上被伪装成儿童的性掠夺者诱骗。据霍利称，Roblox 首席执行官大卫·巴祖基（David Baszucki）受邀出席听证会作证，但遭到了拒绝。

“He may think he’s silent, but in fact he’s testifying, and what he’s testifying to is his commitment to money over people,” Hawley said.

霍利说：“他可能认为自己保持沉默，但事实上他正在作证，而他所证明的是他将金钱置于人之上。”

In a statement, Sara Kanawati Stephany, a senior manager for US policy communications at Roblox, told WIRED, “Safety has been central to Roblox since we launched. We require age checks for chat, place users under 16 in age-based accounts with protective defaults, and block images and video in chat. A legal position about arbitration is not a measure of a company’s safety record, and a hearing built around it fails to advance safety. Protecting children online takes stronger protections, better tools, and consistent standards that apply everywhere kids spend time online.”

Roblox 美国政策传播高级经理萨拉·卡纳瓦蒂·斯蒂芬妮（Sara Kanawati Stephany）在给《连线》（WIRED）的一份声明中表示：“自我们推出以来，安全一直是 Roblox 的核心。我们要求聊天进行年龄验证，将 16 岁以下的用户置于具有保护性默认设置的年龄账户中，并屏蔽聊天中的图像和视频。关于仲裁的法律立场并不能衡量一家公司的安全记录，围绕它展开的听证会也无法促进安全。保护在线儿童需要更强有力的保护措施、更好的工具以及适用于儿童在线活动所有场所的一致标准。”

Roblox has previously argued that users waive their right to sue in open court in the platform’s terms of agreement. According to Roblox’s own data, 35 percent of its age-checked daily active users are under the age of 13—too young to fully comprehend the terms of the agreement, witnesses argued.

Roblox 此前曾辩称，用户在平台的协议条款中放弃了在公开法庭起诉的权利。证人们指出，根据 Roblox 自己的数据，其经过年龄验证的每日活跃用户中有 35% 在 13 岁以下——他们太小了，无法完全理解协议条款。

“There is no way an 8-year-old could read those terms and then sign away their right to a moment in court, effectively silencing themselves,” Republican senator Katie Britt said after being presented with a copy of the multi-page agreement.

共和党参议员凯蒂·布里特（Katie Britt）在看到一份多页协议副本后表示：“一个 8 岁的孩子不可能读懂这些条款，然后签署放弃他们出庭权利的文件，这实际上是让他们自己噤声。”

Two of those who testified before Congress were alleged survivors of grooming on the platform. One was Michael Schlep, a content creator who has advocated for more stringent child safety measures on the platform.

在国会作证的两人是该平台诱骗行为的所谓幸存者。其中一位是迈克尔·施莱普（Michael Schlep），他是一位内容创作者，一直倡导在该平台上采取更严格的儿童安全措施。

Schlep testified that when he was 12, he met a game developer on the platform who befriended him in a public chatroom, then moved to private chats. He alleges he was exposed to “violent pornography, cartel beheadings, and extreme racist ideologies” that he says was a result of his communication with this adult man, and that his abuser exposed his private information, leading to a suicide attempt. He currently runs a YouTube channel exposing alleged Roblox child predators and criticizing the platform for failing to protect children, which has led to Roblox hitting him with a cease-and-desist.

施莱普作证说，他 12 岁时在平台上遇到了一位游戏开发者，对方在公共聊天室与他结为朋友，随后转入私聊。他声称自己接触到了“暴力色情、卡特尔斩首视频和极端种族主义意识形态”，他说这是他与这名成年男子交流的结果，而且施虐者还泄露了他的私人信息，导致他试图自杀。他目前经营着一个 YouTube 频道，揭露 Roblox 上涉嫌诱骗儿童的掠夺者，并批评该平台未能保护儿童，这导致 Roblox 向他发出了停止侵权通知。

“Roblox must be held accountable publicly,” Schlep testified. “They will not voluntarily act to protect children.”

“Roblox 必须公开承担责任，”施莱普作证说。“他们不会主动采取行动保护儿童。”

The hearing also featured testimony from a Marine Corps veteran identified as Woody Doe, who alleged that he was groomed, then sexually assaulted by an adult man he met on Roblox when he was 11 years old. “For years, I carried the shame of what happened,” he testified. He now has a lawsuit pending against Roblox for allegedly failing to warn parents and children of the dangers of the platform.

听证会还听取了一位被称为伍迪·多伊（Woody Doe）的海军陆战队退伍军人的证词，他声称自己在 11 岁时在 Roblox 上结识了一名成年男子，随后遭到诱骗和性侵犯。“多年来，我一直背负着那件事带来的耻辱，”他作证说。他目前正在对 Roblox 提起诉讼，指控其未能向家长和儿童警告该平台的危险。

“I cannot go back and protect the 11-year-old boy I once was, but I can speak for him today … and prevent any other children from ever experiencing the same pain,” he said.

他说：“我无法回到过去保护曾经那个 11 岁的男孩，但我今天可以为他发声……并防止其他任何孩子经历同样的痛苦。”

Roblox is a massive gaming platform, with about 138 million daily active users according to recent company data. As of 2025, 39.7 million daily active users were under the age of 13.

根据公司最近的数据，Roblox 是一个庞大的游戏平台，拥有约 1.38 亿每日活跃用户。截至 2025 年，有 3970 万每日活跃用户在 13 岁以下。

In response to the litigation and to intense media scrutiny, Roblox has adopted what it says are more stringent child-safety measures, including AI-assisted face recognition to estimate a user’s age. But Khari James, senior legal counsel at the National Center on Sexual Exploitation said face-recognition technology isn’t always accurate, and predators could potentially purchase verified accounts to continue to prey on children. Doing face verifications just once, she argues, gives predators “complete access to all the children on Roblox.”

为了应对诉讼和媒体的强烈关注，Roblox 采取了其所称的更严格的儿童安全措施，包括使用人工智能辅助的人脸识别来估算用户的年龄。但国家性剥削中心（National Center on Sexual Exploitation）的高级法律顾问卡里·詹姆斯（Khari James）表示，人脸识别技术并不总是准确的，掠夺者可能会购买经过验证的账户，继续对儿童进行侵害。她认为，仅进行一次人脸验证，就让掠夺者“完全接触到了 Roblox 上的所有儿童”。

“Roblox may be a virtual world, but the children are real,” James said during her testimony. “The predators are real. The addiction is real. The exploitation is real, and the consequences are real. Children should never have to enter a game marketed to them and wonder whether the person on the other side of the screen is there to play with them or prey on them.”

“Roblox 也许是一个虚拟世界，但孩子们是真实的，”詹姆斯在作证时说。“掠夺者是真实的。成瘾是真实的。剥削是真实的，后果也是真实的。孩子们不应该在进入一款面向他们的游戏时，还要怀疑屏幕另一端的人是来和他们一起玩，还是来猎捕他们的。”
