Harvey C. Jones said the company would not be addressing or taking questions ‘regarding the circumstances of Jensen’s resignation.’

Harvey C. Jones 表示，公司将不会回应或接受任何关于“黄仁勋辞职情况”的提问。

Nvidia’s quarterly earnings call on Tuesday was its first with CFO and interim CEO Colette Kress at the helm alongside the company’s first board chair, Harvey C. Jones. The leadership change followed Jensen Huang’s resignation last week after reports he had multiple affairs – including one with former Megaspeed executive and alleged Chinese asset Alice Huang and one with a direct report, Kimberly Powell.

英伟达周二举行的季度财报电话会议，是首席财务官兼临时首席执行官 Colette Kress 与公司首任董事会主席 Harvey C. Jones 共同主持的首次会议。此前，黄仁勋因被曝出多段婚外情而于上周辞职，其中包括与前 Megaspeed 高管、被指控为中国资产的 Alice Huang 的关系，以及与下属 Kimberly Powell 的关系。

Ahead of Tuesday’s earnings, Huang said he would be willing to return as CEO. However, during the company’s earnings call, Jones stated that “the Nvidia board unanimously accepted [Huang’s] resignation and does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO.”

在周二的财报发布前，黄仁勋曾表示愿意重返首席执行官职位。然而，在公司的财报电话会议上，Jones 表示：“英伟达董事会一致接受了（黄仁勋的）辞职，并不打算让黄仁勋复职担任首席执行官。”

As Chairman of NVIDIA’s Board of Directors, I want to say a few words on the Board’s behalf. NVIDIA is an exceptionally strong company. Its strength comes from its extraordinary people, groundbreaking technology, and exemplary track record of execution. Our financial performance speaks for itself.

作为英伟达董事会主席，我谨代表董事会说几句话。英伟达是一家实力极其雄厚的公司。其力量源于我们非凡的员工、开创性的技术以及堪称典范的执行记录。我们的财务表现足以说明一切。

Kress responded to a question about the company’s leadership transition, saying “the most important thing that we think through right now is our employees.” She added, “From a business perspective, it’s business as usual.”

Kress 在回答有关公司领导层变动的问题时表示：“我们目前最关心的就是我们的员工。”她补充道：“从业务角度来看，一切照常进行。”

Jones also gave an update on Nvidia’s search for a new permanent CEO: From the board’s point of view, we take the search very seriously. We’re going to look into internal candidates as well as external candidates. The Board appreciates that we’re living in a world where the advanced silicon technologies and national security dynamics are making major changes in our marketplace.

Jones 还介绍了英伟达寻找新任常务首席执行官的进展：从董事会的角度来看，我们非常重视这次搜寻工作。我们将考察内部候选人以及外部候选人。董事会意识到，我们所处的世界中，先进的芯片技术和国家安全动态正在给我们的市场带来重大变革。
