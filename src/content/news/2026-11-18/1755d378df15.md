Nvidia’s Q3 revenue has more than doubled, with income from data centers up 110 percent from last year, to $107.6 billion.

英伟达第三季度营收增长了一倍多，其中数据中心收入较去年增长了 110%，达到 1076 亿美元。

Interim Nvidia CEO Colette Kress announced the company’s Q3 earnings on Tuesday, without initially mentioning former CEO Jensen Huang or the details of his departure, or possible attempt at a return. On the earnings call accompanying the release, board chair Harvey Jones kicked things off by saying the board “does not intend to reinstate Huang as CEO,” and that “we will not be addressing or taking questions on today’s call regarding the circumstances of Jensen’s resignation and the ongoing investigation,” before Kress started with her comments on the results.

英伟达临时首席执行官科莱特·克雷斯（Colette Kress）周二公布了公司第三季度财报，起初并未提及前首席执行官黄仁勋，也未提及他离职的细节或其可能试图回归的消息。在随后的财报电话会议上，董事会主席哈维·琼斯（Harvey Jones）首先表示，董事会“无意恢复黄仁勋的首席执行官职务”，并称“我们今天不会在电话会议上讨论或回答有关黄仁勋辞职情况及正在进行的调查的问题”，随后克雷斯才开始对财报结果发表评论。

As it predicted in the last financial update, Nvidia is now on the list of companies that have pulled in over $100 billion in a single quarter, with revenues of $115.8 billion, up 103 percent from the same period last year and higher than its expectation of $108 billion. This quarter’s figures include data center revenues of $107.6 billion, which were up 110 percent, and edge computing (which includes the consumer gaming PC business and devices like the new RTX Spark laptops) revenue of $8.2 billion, up 42 percent.

正如在上次财务更新中所预测的那样，英伟达现已跻身单季度营收超过 1000 亿美元的公司行列，其营收达到 1158 亿美元，较去年同期增长 103%，高于此前 1080 亿美元的预期。本季度数据包括 1076 亿美元的数据中心收入（增长 110%），以及 82 亿美元的边缘计算收入（增长 42%，其中包括消费级游戏 PC 业务和新款 RTX Spark 笔记本电脑等设备）。

The company reported $65.3 billion in profit, a new record. “Our momentum is real. NVIDIA is an exceptional technology leader, powered by the relentless innovation of our people,” said Colette Kress, interim CEO and executive vice president and chief financial officer of NVIDIA. “Vera Rubin is off to an explosive start, our roadmap is on track and our ambition has never been greater.”

该公司报告利润为 653 亿美元，创下新纪录。“我们的增长势头是真实的。英伟达是一家卓越的技术领导者，由我们员工的不懈创新所驱动，”英伟达临时首席执行官兼执行副总裁兼首席财务官科莱特·克雷斯表示。“Vera Rubin（架构）开局火爆，我们的路线图进展顺利，我们的雄心壮志从未如此宏大。”

In the fourth quarter, Nvidia expects revenues of $130 billion.

英伟达预计第四季度营收将达到 1300 亿美元。
