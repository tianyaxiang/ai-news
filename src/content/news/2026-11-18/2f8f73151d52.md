Opening the keynote at its Ignite event, Microsoft CEO Satya Nadella outlined the company’s vision for its new “frontier ecosystem,” announcing Tuesday the general availability of Word, Excel, and PowerPoint inside Copilot, which he described as “the new OS for work.”

在 Ignite 大会的开幕演讲中，微软首席执行官萨提亚·纳德拉（Satya Nadella）概述了该公司对其全新“前沿生态系统”的愿景，并于周二宣布 Word、Excel 和 PowerPoint 已在 Copilot 中全面可用，他将其描述为“工作的新操作系统”。

The announcement is a striking inversion of how we’ve traditionally used software. Rather than an app like Word being the attraction and Clippy the assistant to help you use it, Microsoft envisions the new Copilot integrations will see agents driving things, with the software packages riding shotgun.

这一公告标志着我们传统使用软件方式的惊人逆转。微软设想的全新 Copilot 集成将由智能体（Agent）主导工作，而软件应用则退居辅助地位，这与过去以 Word 等应用为核心、Clippy 仅作为辅助工具的模式截然不同。

Applications will become plugin components of the agent’s work, backed by an organizational knowledge layer that draws context from emails, meetings, calendars, chats, and files. Users will now be able to create documents, spreadsheets, and presentations directly in Copilot, with the resulting files remaining editable in their respective Office applications.

应用程序将成为智能体工作中的插件组件，并由一个从电子邮件、会议、日历、聊天记录和文件中提取上下文的组织知识层提供支持。用户现在可以直接在 Copilot 中创建文档、电子表格和演示文稿，生成的文档仍可在各自的 Office 应用程序中进行编辑。

Copilot Autopilot (not to be confused with Windows Autopilot) is a “persistent, proactive agent for personal productivity” according to Microsoft. In a nutshell, Autopilot is a cloud-based autonomous AI agent that users can delegate responsibilities to, not just single tasks.

根据微软的说法，Copilot Autopilot（不要与 Windows Autopilot 混淆）是一个“用于个人生产力的持久、主动的智能体”。简而言之，Autopilot 是一个基于云的自主 AI 智能体，用户可以将责任（而不仅仅是单一任务）委托给它。

Trust, the third pillar of Microsoft’s new frontier ecosystem, was something that Nadella continued returning to during his presentation, and not in a way that some might expect. “We find ourselves in a place where alignment is elusive. Mechanistic understanding of these models is elusive. The question is: How can you even trust all this powerful technology? The reality is, by definition, you can’t,” he said.

信任是微软新前沿生态系统的第三大支柱，纳德拉在演讲中不断提及这一点，但方式可能出乎某些人的意料。“我们发现自己处于一个对齐（Alignment）难以实现的境地。对这些模型的机制性理解也难以捉摸。问题在于：你如何能信任所有这些强大的技术？现实是，从定义上讲，你无法信任，”他说。

In a departure from the norms of “AI is magic” corporate hype sentiment, Nadella conceded that the internal workings of AI thought processes remain difficult to understand, and models cannot be trusted to govern themselves, regardless of whether their answers are accurate.

与“AI 是魔法”这种企业炒作的常态不同，纳德拉承认 AI 思维过程的内部运作仍然难以理解，且无论模型给出的答案是否准确，都不能指望它们进行自我管理。

The promised agent controls and admission that AI systems can’t always be trusted are meaningful, but Microsoft is asking businesses and users to place their trust in the company’s own safeguards instead, though the effectiveness of those safeguards against mistakes, rogue actions, or data exposure will still have to be demonstrated.

所承诺的智能体控制措施以及对 AI 系统并非总是可信的承认具有重要意义，但微软要求企业和用户转而信任该公司自身的保障措施，尽管这些措施在应对错误、违规操作或数据泄露方面的有效性仍有待验证。

Along with the guardrails, Microsoft announced what it calls the “AI Transparency Initiative.” The effort has partnered with 40-plus major companies, including Adobe and Mastercard, to develop open, industrywide standards for AI governance and security.

除了这些护栏措施外，微软还宣布了所谓的“AI 透明度倡议”。该计划已与包括 Adobe 和万事达卡在内的 40 多家大型公司合作，旨在为 AI 治理和安全制定开放的行业标准。

Along with the other announced additions, Microsoft also announced expanding capabilities for Agent 365, its existing control plane for managing AI agents across enterprise environments. The new agent sheriff extends existing identity, security, and management systems to AI agents.

除了上述新增功能外，微软还宣布扩展 Agent 365 的功能，这是其用于管理企业环境中 AI 智能体的现有控制平面。这个新的“智能体警长”将现有的身份、安全和管理系统扩展到了 AI 智能体上。
