Mobile game localization is not just swapping strings. UI, story, art, audio, store listing and legal text all have to feel native, and the work does not end at launch because live games keep shipping content. Here is a pipeline that treats localization as part of the build, with the bugs each stage is meant to catch. What actually needs localizing User interface, narrative text, visual assets (icons, banners, costumes), audio and voice-over, app store metadata, in-game offers, and legal and support content. Missing any one can produce bad reviews or a poor launch in that market.

移动游戏本地化不仅仅是替换字符串。用户界面（UI）、故事、美术、音频、商店列表和法律文本都必须让人感觉像是原生内容，而且工作并不会在发布时结束，因为在线游戏会持续发布新内容。以下是一个将本地化视为构建一部分的流程，以及每个阶段旨在预防的错误。真正需要本地化的内容包括：用户界面、叙事文本、视觉资产（图标、横幅、服装）、音频和配音、应用商店元数据、游戏内优惠，以及法律和支持内容。遗漏其中任何一项都可能导致差评或在当地市场发布失败。

The eight stages Plan. Pick target languages and launch priorities, estimate word count and voice-over volume, map the schedule to sprints, and prepare a localization kit with a style guide, glossary, screenshots and reference video. Internationalize. Support Unicode, leave extra UI room, choose fonts that cover Arabic, Cyrillic and Vietnamese, and move text out of code so strings are not hard-coded. Export source text. Export to CSV, JSON, XML, XLIFF or PO, connect to a translation management system, and keep naming consistent so updates do not conflict. Translate and localize audio. Work in batches with CAT tools, translation memory and a termbase. Adapt tone, humor and references rather than translating literally. Integrate. Import translated text and audio into the build with an automated pipeline, and run quick checks for broken tags and placeholders. Linguistic QA. Native testers play the build and check UI text, dialogue, missions and tutorials for overflow, font problems, mistranslations and tone. Mid-size titles often spend 8 to 12 weeks here. Compliance and submission. Match store and platform guidelines and official terminology. Release and update. Repeat a short translate, integrate and QA loop for patches, events and downloadable content.

八个阶段：规划。选择目标语言和发布优先级，估算字数和配音量，将时间表映射到冲刺阶段，并准备包含风格指南、术语表、截图和参考视频的本地化工具包。国际化。支持 Unicode，留出额外的 UI 空间，选择涵盖阿拉伯语、西里尔字母和越南语的字体，并将文本移出代码，以免字符串被硬编码。导出源文本。导出为 CSV、JSON、XML、XLIFF 或 PO 格式，连接到翻译管理系统，并保持命名一致，以免更新时发生冲突。翻译和本地化音频。使用计算机辅助翻译（CAT）工具、翻译记忆库和术语库进行批量工作。调整语气、幽默感和引用，而不是进行字面翻译。集成。通过自动化流程将翻译后的文本和音频导入构建版本，并对损坏的标签和占位符进行快速检查。语言质量保证（Linguistic QA）。母语测试人员试玩构建版本，检查 UI 文本、对话、任务和教程是否存在溢出、字体问题、翻译错误和语气不当。中型游戏通常需要在此阶段花费 8 到 12 周。合规与提交。符合商店和平台的准则以及官方术语。发布与更新。针对补丁、活动和可下载内容，重复进行简短的翻译、集成和质量保证循环。

Three bugs worth designing against Strings without context. A single word such as "Play" or "Charge" can mean several things. Give strings descriptions, screenshots or video, group them by screen or feature, and use in-context testers. Text expansion. German text typically runs 20 to 35 percent longer than English, so build flexible containers with a safety margin and use pseudo-localization early, before the first export. Chinese, Japanese and Korean strings use fewer characters but need larger font sizes and taller lines to stay legible. Hard-coded text. Strings embedded in code or images never reach translators. Externalize them before the localization pipeline begins.

值得在设计时预防的三个错误：缺乏上下文的字符串。像“Play”或“Charge”这样的单个单词可能有多种含义。为字符串提供描述、截图或视频，按屏幕或功能对它们进行分组，并使用上下文测试人员。文本扩展。德语文本通常比英语长 20% 到 35%，因此要构建带有安全边距的灵活容器，并在首次导出前尽早使用伪本地化。中、日、韩语字符串使用的字符较少，但需要更大的字号和更高的行距才能保持可读性。硬编码文本。嵌入在代码或图像中的字符串永远无法传达给译员。在本地化流程开始前将它们外部化。

Why teams bother Surveys cited in the source material say 76 percent of consumers prefer buying in their native language, and over 90 percent of players in Taiwan, Korea, China and Japan choose localized games over English ones. Genshin Impact, localized into 13 languages with four voice-over options, earned over $4 billion, with about 45 percent from outside China. At the other extreme, Arena of Valor, a stripped-down international version of Honor of Kings, earned about $71 million against the original's reported $15 billion.
