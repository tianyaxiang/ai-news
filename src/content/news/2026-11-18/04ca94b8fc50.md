It can be hard to use Google products—or even the Internet itself—without coming into contact with Gemini AI. That is, of course, by design. The more you use Gemini, the more data Google collects to train and improve its generative AI chatbot. The options to limit training on your data have been scant, but Google is finally adding more controls to the Gemini app.

在不接触 Gemini AI 的情况下使用谷歌产品，甚至仅仅是使用互联网，都变得越来越困难。当然，这是刻意为之的。你使用 Gemini 的次数越多，谷歌收集用于训练和改进其生成式 AI 聊天机器人的数据就越多。此前限制使用你的数据进行训练的选项寥寥无几，但谷歌终于在 Gemini 应用中增加了更多控制功能。

The change currently rolling out to Gemini will separate your chat history from training, which allows you to retain basic AI functionality without handing Google quite so much control over your data. Prior to this change, the only way to opt out of contributing your chat contents (including attachments) for training was to turn off your history. That made it impossible to go back and pick up a chat down the road.

目前正在向 Gemini 推出的这项变更将把你的聊天记录与训练数据分离开来，这使你能够在保留基本 AI 功能的同时，不必将过多的数据控制权交给谷歌。在此项变更之前，若想拒绝贡献聊天内容（包括附件）用于训练，唯一的办法就是关闭历史记录。但这会导致你无法在日后回顾或继续之前的对话。

As the new settings roll out, users will see separate toggles for model training and chat history. The history setting will continue to support an adjustable limit, but you will no longer have to change it to stop Google from training on your Gemini usage. Meanwhile, AI training will be an opt-out situation. You’ll need to find the toggle to block Google from using your chats for AI training. It’s under Activity in the Gemini settings.

随着新设置的推出，用户将看到模型训练和聊天记录的独立开关。历史记录设置将继续支持可调节的限制，但你不再需要通过更改此设置来阻止谷歌利用你的 Gemini 使用情况进行训练。与此同时，AI 训练将采用“选择退出”机制。你需要找到相应的开关来阻止谷歌将你的聊天内容用于 AI 训练，该选项位于 Gemini 设置的“活动”中。

You can also access the menu from your Google account under Data and Privacy > My Activity > Other activity > Gemini Apps Activity. Importantly, this only applies to future interactions with Gemini. If you have previously fed any data into Google’s AI, that’s already part of the training data. That can include your inputs, Gemini’s responses, uploaded files, tool calls, your location, and data from connected apps.

你也可以通过谷歌账户中的“数据和隐私” > “我的活动” > “其他活动” > “Gemini 应用活动”来访问该菜单。重要的是，这仅适用于未来与 Gemini 的交互。如果你之前曾向谷歌的 AI 输入过任何数据，这些数据已经成为了训练数据的一部分。这可能包括你的输入内容、Gemini 的回复、上传的文件、工具调用、你的位置以及来自关联应用的数据。

Google says it may retain this data for up to three years to improve its models. The company has previously noted it attempts to filter personal information out of the Gemini activity history, but that isn’t a guarantee. Regardless of your chosen activity settings, you can always manually remove anything you don’t want saved on the same page with the history and training toggles. However, reviewed chats that are already part of the training data won’t be retroactively deleted.

谷歌表示，为了改进模型，它可能会保留这些数据长达三年。该公司此前曾指出，它会尝试从 Gemini 活动记录中过滤掉个人信息，但这并不能得到保证。无论你选择何种活动设置，你始终可以在包含历史记录和训练开关的同一页面上手动删除任何你不希望保存的内容。然而，已经作为训练数据一部分的聊天记录将不会被追溯删除。

Opting out of training doesn’t mean Google won’t have your data, either. Google may use this data in other ways for up to three years—it just won’t add it to AI-training data sets. If you want to use Gemini and not retain any data, your options are actually a bit more limited now. Currently, if you disable history, Google still retains that data for 72 hours (and 24 hours for the user). Once the updated page is live, this duration will change to two months.

选择退出训练并不意味着谷歌就不会拥有你的数据。谷歌仍可能在长达三年的时间内以其他方式使用这些数据，只是不会将其加入到 AI 训练数据集之中。如果你想使用 Gemini 且不保留任何数据，你的选择实际上现在变得更加有限了。目前，如果你禁用历史记录，谷歌仍会保留这些数据 72 小时（用户端为 24 小时）。一旦更新后的页面上线，这一期限将变为两个月。

So you can access that data for longer, but so can Google. The company says this is to “respond to you, process your feedback, and protect Google, its users, and the public.” Essentially, Google will be more able to identify nefarious AI usage even if someone disables history. Even with the longer chat retention, the ability to control AI training is still a welcome privacy improvement. Google says the change is rolling out globally, but it may take a few weeks to show up for everyone.

因此，你可以更长时间地访问这些数据，但谷歌也可以。该公司表示，这是为了“响应你、处理你的反馈，并保护谷歌、其用户及公众”。本质上，即使有人禁用了历史记录，谷歌也将更有能力识别恶意的 AI 使用行为。尽管聊天记录保留时间延长了，但能够控制 AI 训练仍然是一项受欢迎的隐私改进。谷歌表示该变更正在全球范围内推出，但可能需要几周时间才能覆盖所有用户。
