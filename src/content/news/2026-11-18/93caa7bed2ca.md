Follow The National Security Agency closed its campus to most employees on Tuesday night, reverting to essential personnel only as it works to bring its classified internal communications network back online, according to a voicemail inbox used by NSA employees and a source familiar with the matter.

2010年，位于马里兰州米德堡的美国国家安全局（NSA）总部。据NSA员工使用的语音信箱和一位知情人士透露，国家安全局于周二晚间对大多数员工关闭了园区，仅保留必要人员，以努力恢复其机密内部通信网络的运行。

The outage of the highly classified network was first reported by CNN, citing six people familiar with the matter. The move to close its campus came just hours after the NSA told employees they could return to work as normal. It is not clear what prompted the reversal. The agency had staggered employee arrivals earlier in the day as it worked to address the issue. The network, called NSANet, allows employees to communicate classified information internally. At this point there is no evidence of a cyberattack, sources said.

这一高度机密网络的故障最初由美国有线电视新闻网（CNN）援引六名知情人士的消息报道。在关闭园区的决定做出前几个小时，NSA刚刚通知员工可以正常返岗。目前尚不清楚是什么原因导致了这一反转。当天早些时候，该机构曾采取错峰上班的方式来应对这一问题。该网络被称为“NSANet”，允许员工在内部交流机密信息。消息人士称，目前没有证据表明这是一次网络攻击。

“The National Security Agency experienced temporary degradation during scheduled routine maintenance,” the agency said in a statement on Tuesday afternoon. “While some internal systems were affected, mission-critical capabilities remain fully operational and our ability to defend national security continues. NSA’s infrastructure is designed with multiple layers of redundancy to maintain operations during planned maintenance or unexpected events.” A source familiar with the matter told CNN that restoration of the network was ongoing but not yet complete.

“国家安全局在例行维护期间经历了暂时的性能下降，”该机构在周二下午的一份声明中表示。“虽然部分内部系统受到影响，但关键任务能力保持完全正常，我们捍卫国家安全的能力依然存在。NSA的基础设施设计有多层冗余，以确保在计划内维护或突发事件期间维持运营。”一位知情人士告诉CNN，网络的恢复工作正在进行中，但尚未完成。

Director of National Intelligence Jay Clayton said later Tuesday afternoon that the NSA outage was the result of the agency applying a software patch to address major software vulnerabilities that were discovered last month. “The patching that has gone on following that has caused some of these systems to not come back up when you patch them as expected,” Clayton said on Fox Business. “That is that is what has happened at the NSA today.”

国家情报总监杰伊·克莱顿（Jay Clayton）在周二下午晚些时候表示，NSA的故障是该机构为修复上个月发现的重大软件漏洞而应用软件补丁的结果。“此后进行的补丁修复导致其中一些系统在修补后未能如预期般恢复运行，”克莱顿在福克斯商业频道上说。“这就是今天NSA发生的情况。”

The vulnerabilities Clayton were referring to are in PHP, a programming language that powers about 70% of the world’s websites. AI tools helped researchers exploit them, setting off a scramble by government agencies and corporations around the world to address the issue in recent weeks. The NSA itself urged organizations to apply software patches. The flaws in PHP could allow hackers to break into websites and read memory that could contain sensitive information, like passwords.

克莱顿所指的漏洞存在于PHP中，这是一种支撑着全球约70%网站的编程语言。人工智能工具帮助研究人员利用了这些漏洞，引发了全球政府机构和企业在最近几周争相解决这一问题。NSA本身也敦促各组织应用软件补丁。PHP中的缺陷可能允许黑客入侵网站并读取可能包含密码等敏感信息的内存。

Clayton said there is no sign that any of the recent tech outages, including that affecting the NSA, were the result of a cyberattack. A current and a former US official said an outage at NSANet would not impact the agency’s ability to communicate urgent intelligence to President Donald Trump while he is traveling in Asia on Air Force One. Both sources described multiple redundancies and backup networks that the government could use to communicate securely with the president who is currently in the Philippines before heading to China tomorrow for talks with Chinese leader Xi Jinping and Russian President Vladimir Putin.

克莱顿表示，没有迹象表明最近发生的任何技术故障（包括影响NSA的故障）是网络攻击的结果。一位现任和一位前任美国官员表示，NSANet的故障不会影响该机构在唐纳德·特朗普总统乘坐“空军一号”在亚洲旅行期间向其传达紧急情报的能力。两位消息人士都描述了政府可以用来与总统进行安全通信的多重冗余和备份网络，总统目前在菲律宾，明天将前往中国与中国领导人习近平和俄罗斯总统弗拉基米尔·普京进行会谈。

NSANet is “more of a network of networks, and it’s unlikely that a full outage exists given the many redundancies in place,” a former senior NSA official told CNN. “It could impact analysis and reporting, but for highest priority requirements they would find a way to work those. So it is alarming, but likely not crippling.”

一位NSA前高级官员告诉CNN，NSANet“更像是一个网络集合体，考虑到现有的多重冗余，不太可能出现全面瘫痪。”“这可能会影响分析和报告，但对于最高优先级的需求，他们会找到解决办法。所以这令人担忧，但不太可能造成瘫痪。”

A voicemail inbox recording for NSA employees on Tuesday morning described the situation as a “code yellow,” the second-highest in a three-level system that governs whether NSA employees report for work at the agency’s Fort Meade, Maryland, headquarters or not. The recording told non-emergency personnel to arrive for work in a staggered manner and for emergency personnel to arrive as usual. The New York Times first reported that detail.

周二上午，NSA员工的语音信箱录音将情况描述为“黄色代码”，这是管理NSA员工是否前往马里兰州米德堡总部上班的三级系统中的第二高级别。录音通知非紧急人员错峰上班，紧急人员照常上班。《纽约时报》率先报道了这一细节。

Sometime Tuesday evening, the inbox reverted to “code green,” instructing employees to report to work as scheduled. It was not immediately clear whether the outage was resolved. But later Tuesday night, the inbox moved to a “code orange,” closing the campus for most employees until further notice, according to the inbox recording and source familiar with the matter.

周二晚些时候，语音信箱恢复为“绿色代码”，指示员工按计划上班。目前尚不清楚故障是否已解决。但据语音信箱录音和知情人士透露，周二深夜，信箱转为“橙色代码”，通知大多数员工关闭园区，直至另行通知。

The outage at America’s most powerful intelligence agency comes on the heels of multiple major tech glitches affecting airlines and other corporations around the country. All flights nationwide were grounded for hours on Friday after a massive systems failure, leading to thousands of flights being delayed or canceled. The Federal Aviation Administration traced the problem to a single router in Arkansas. Meanwhile, a massive outage last week affecting Microsoft products left people around the world unable to access Outlook email and Team meetings.

美国最强大的情报机构发生的这次故障，紧随全国范围内影响航空公司和其他企业的多次重大技术故障之后。周五，由于大规模系统故障，全国所有航班停飞数小时，导致数千个航班延误或取消。美国联邦航空管理局将问题追溯到阿肯色州的一台路由器。与此同时，上周影响微软产品的大规模故障导致全球用户无法访问Outlook电子邮件和Teams会议。

And in recent weeks banks and other services have experienced outages, on Monday thousands of Chase Bank customers reported they had trouble using their online accounts. There is no indication that the NSA, FAA or Microsoft outages were caused by a cyberattack. But they are stark examples of how tech failures can roil markets and potentially threaten national security.

最近几周，银行和其他服务也经历了故障，周一有数千名大通银行客户报告称在使用在线账户时遇到困难。没有迹象表明NSA、FAA或微软的故障是由网络攻击引起的。但这些都是技术故障如何扰乱市场并可能威胁国家安全的鲜明例子。

The US shares intelligence with a group of countries in what is known as the Five Eyes intelligence-sharing alliance, which includes the US, UK, New Zealand, Australia and Canada. That intelligence sharing system has not been impacted as a result of the NSANet outage, according to a source from one of the countries and a former US intelligence official.

美国与一组国家共享情报，即所谓的“五眼联盟”情报共享联盟，成员包括美国、英国、新西兰、澳大利亚和加拿大。据其中一个国家的消息人士和一位美国前情报官员称，该情报共享系统并未受到NSANet故障的影响。

Tuesday’s outage was not the first time the NSA’s internal computer networks have suffered significant disruption. In 2000, a tech failure at the agency downed computers for nearly three days and interrupted the processing of intelligence at Fort Meade, an agency spokesperson said at the time. 周二的故障并非NSA内部计算机网络首次遭受重大中断。该机构的一位发言人当时表示，2000年，该机构的一次技术故障导致计算机瘫痪了近三天，并中断了米德堡的情报处理工作。本文已更新了更多信息。戴维斯·温基（Davis Winkie）在CNN的工作由合作伙伴支持。
