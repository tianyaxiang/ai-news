A Steve Jobs Core Animation demo from WWDC 2007 popped up in my feed and it stopped me cold. A live wall of playing videos, instant zoom, instant tag search, zero glitches, on 2007 hardware.

一段史蒂夫·乔布斯在 2007 年全球开发者大会（WWDC）上演示 Core Animation 的视频出现在我的信息流中，让我瞬间驻足。在 2007 年的硬件设备上，它实现了实时播放视频墙、即时缩放、即时标签搜索，且毫无卡顿。

Then I clicked around Sequoia for thirty seconds and watched panels load in slowly. Tahoe is worse. I am still holding out on Sequoia and it is barely better.

随后，我在 macOS Sequoia 系统中操作了三十秒，眼看着面板缓慢加载。Tahoe（macOS 15.1）的情况更糟。我至今仍对 Sequoia 持保留意见，它的表现也仅仅是稍好一点点。

I want to be wrong about this. Tell me where Apple software still feels like 2007 to you. Keynote footage: Apple WWDC 2007.

我希望我的看法是错的。请告诉我，在哪些方面你觉得苹果的软件依然保持着 2007 年的水准。主题演讲素材来源：Apple WWDC 2007。
