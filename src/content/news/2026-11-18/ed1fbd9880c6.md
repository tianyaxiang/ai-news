Nvidia says it does not intend to reinstate Jensen Huang as CEO. The board said it would not take additional questions on the call about Huang’s departure.

英伟达表示无意让黄仁勋复职担任首席执行官。董事会表示，在电话会议上不会就黄仁勋的离职事宜接受额外提问。

Starting Nvidia’s quarterly earnings call on Tuesday, new board chair Harvey Jones said that when former CEO Jensen Huang resigned, “the Nvidia board unanimously accepted his resignation and does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO.”

在周二英伟达季度财报电话会议开始时，新任董事会主席哈维·琼斯（Harvey Jones）表示，在前首席执行官黄仁勋辞职时，“英伟达董事会一致接受了他的辞呈，并无意让黄仁勋复职担任首席执行官。”

Jones added, “We will not be addressing or taking questions on today’s call regarding the circumstances of Jensen’s resignation and the ongoing investigation.”

琼斯补充道：“我们不会在今天的电话会议上讨论或回答有关黄仁勋辞职情况及正在进行的调查的问题。”

Huang had publicly floated the idea of returning earlier on Tuesday. In a statement posted to X, he wrote that he “did not live up to the standards expected of everyone” at the company, that he regretted his mistakes, and that he would cooperate with the Nvidia board’s investigation into his actions.

黄仁勋周二早些时候曾公开表达过回归的想法。他在 X 上发布的一份声明中写道，他“没有达到公司对每个人的期望标准”，他对自己的错误感到后悔，并将配合英伟达董事会对其实际行为的调查。

He added that he’d be willing to return to the chief executive role if offered it, though a day earlier, the Nvidia board reportedly said it was not considering bringing him back. “NVIDIA is a once-in-a-generation company,” Huang wrote. “If the Board of Directors decides that my return is in the best interest of our shareholders, our employees, the company, and America, I would be honored to answer these calls and return as CEO.”

他补充说，如果获得邀请，他愿意重返首席执行官职位，尽管据报道英伟达董事会前一天曾表示不考虑让他回归。“英伟达是一家时代性的公司，”黄仁勋写道，“如果董事会认为我的回归符合股东、员工、公司和美国的最佳利益，我很荣幸能响应这些呼吁并重返首席执行官岗位。”

But since his November 12th resignation, Huang remains entangled in scandal. He’s reportedly had three affairs, two with subordinates at Nvidia, one with an alleged Chinese intelligence asset suspected of facilitating illicit chip sales. (Huang has pushed against restrictions on Nvidia chip sales in China.)

但自 11 月 12 日辞职以来，黄仁勋仍深陷丑闻。据报道，他有三段婚外情，其中两段涉及英伟达的下属，另一段涉及一名被怀疑协助非法芯片销售的所谓中国情报人员。（黄仁勋曾反对对英伟达在华芯片销售的限制。）

He’s scheduled to testify before Congress on December 1st, and an internal company investigation is ongoing.

他定于 12 月 1 日在国会作证，公司内部调查也正在进行中。
