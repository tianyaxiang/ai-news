If your emails, calendar, passwords, photos, files, and payment cards are already in the Google ecosystem, Gemini is your best choice as a personal assistant because this sensitive data already lives at Google securely, and you don’t have to send it elsewhere. Here’s how I’m using it. Recently, our minivan’s check-engine light came on. Ugh. I pulled out Gemini and told it, "Our minivan is overdue for a service appointment. Please call the two nearest dealerships and figure out how to get an appointment early Friday morning over the next month or so."

Because Gemini already knows we drive a 2019 Honda Odyssey and where we live through Personal Intelligence, it found the nearest shops on Google Maps and called both at once.
