# Instagram is helping creators crack down on copycat videos

Instagram 正在帮助创作者打击抄袭视频。

Meta will let creators decide to hide copies of their videos flagged by a new ‘content protection’ feature.

Meta 将允许创作者决定是否隐藏被一项新的“内容保护”功能标记出的视频副本。

Instagram is beginning to roll out a “content protection” feature that lets creators see and review copies of their videos posted to the platform.

Instagram 正开始推出一项“内容保护”功能，让创作者能够查看并审核发布在平台上的视频副本。

“For eligible creators who enroll, content protection is a tool that automatically looks out for visual matches of your original videos, alerts you when we find a match across Instagram and Facebook, and lets you decide what happens next,” Meta says in a blog post. Meta launched a similar tool for Facebook last year to help users “safeguard” their Reels and “maintain control” over their creative work.

Meta 在一篇博客文章中表示：“对于加入该计划的符合条件的创作者，内容保护工具会自动查找与您原创视频视觉匹配的内容，在 Instagram 和 Facebook 上发现匹配项时向您发出提醒，并让您决定后续操作。”Meta 去年为 Facebook 推出了类似的工具，旨在帮助用户“保护”他们的 Reels 短视频并“保持对”其创作成果的控制。

When an Instagram creator is notified of a matched video, they can see how many views the match has and decide if they want to track the video, hide it from everyone on Instagram and Facebook, add a credit link, or release their claim on the match.

当 Instagram 创作者收到匹配视频的通知时，他们可以查看该匹配视频的观看次数，并决定是否要追踪该视频、将其在 Instagram 和 Facebook 上对所有人隐藏、添加来源链接，或放弃对该匹配项的主张。

After a creator enrolls, every “original video” they post on Instagram is protected, and creators can retroactively protect older videos manually. Creators can also put accounts on an allow list that lets them use their videos.

创作者加入后，他们在 Instagram 上发布的每一个“原创视频”都会受到保护，创作者还可以手动追溯保护旧视频。创作者还可以将某些账户加入白名单，允许其使用自己的视频。

In addition, Instagram will start flagging AI-generated videos that “match the spoken words” in a creator’s original video but have visuals replaced with AI. Initially, Instagram will test this with a “small group of creators” in “the coming weeks.” The platform says it’s exploring ways to limit the reach of content and profiles that “use AI tools to post this type of content repeatedly.”

此外，Instagram 将开始标记那些“口述内容与创作者原创视频相符”但视觉画面被 AI 替换的 AI 生成视频。最初，Instagram 将在“未来几周内”向“一小部分创作者”测试此功能。该平台表示，正在探索限制那些“重复使用 AI 工具发布此类内容”的账号和内容的影响力。
