# Yet another satellite company is jockeying for a rocket—but this one makes sense

芬兰卫星开发商 ICEYE 周二宣布，正寻求以超过 22 亿美元的价格收购意大利火箭制造商 Avio，此举旨在打造欧洲首家真正垂直整合的端到端太空公司。

The announcement came as an all-cash voluntary tender offer from an Italian subsidiary of ICEYE to Avio’s shareholders, led by the Italian defense conglomerate Leonardo. Avio said its board of directors “has taken note of the notice” from ICEYE and is considering the purchase offer.

Avio 制造的 Vega C 火箭是一款固体燃料运载火箭，旨在将重达 3.3 公吨（7,300 磅）的有效载荷送入近地轨道。自 2022 年以来，Vega C 在 8 次尝试中成功飞行了 7 次。它是 Vega 火箭早期版本的后续型号，该版本自 2012 年首次亮相以来，创下了 22 次发射 20 次成功的记录。

ICEYE is, by far, the most successful space startup in Europe, where access to private capital, regulatory barriers, and fragmented markets often hinder startup growth, according to a recent report from the Organization for Economic Cooperation and Development. ICEYE’s valuation stands at more than $12 billion after the company’s most recent fundraising round in June.

在追求主权方面，ICEYE 在一份声明中表示，希望与 Avio 联手，以满足欧洲对“主权太空和防御能力”的需求。ICEYE 制造并运营自己的雷达监视卫星，并向商业客户和各国政府出售数据，其中包括利用 ICEYE 图像进行目标定位和追踪俄罗斯军队动向的乌克兰。

“Governments need control of their critical space infrastructure, and Europe needs it most,” said Rafal Modrzewski, CEO and co-founder of ICEYE. “Avio has built the launch and propulsion capability Europe relies on. With ICEYE’s long-term capital behind it, we can integrate the supply chain, grow output and strengthen the European programs that already depend on it. Together we can build, launch and operate sovereign space systems end-to-end, made in Europe for governments across the world.”

Avio 负责监管生产 Vega C 火箭的欧洲公司财团，并在其位于罗马附近的工厂进行 Vega C 火箭级的总装。在 Vega 项目的大部分历史中，Avio 将火箭交给法国发射服务提供商 Arianespace，由后者负责法属圭亚那的营销、销售和发射日运营。Avio 最近接管了这些职责，将整个 Vega C 的运营纳入内部。

ICEYE has built and launched 84 radar reconnaissance satellites, providing routine overhead imagery of locations around the world, even during darkness or cloud cover. In addition to its commercial satellite fleet, ICEYE has sold turnkey satellites directly to national governments, including Finland, Poland, Germany, Sweden, Portugal, Greece, and the Netherlands.

上个月，ICEYE 宣布与另一家芬兰公司诺基亚建立合作伙伴关系，开发一个近地轨道卫星星座，以支持欧洲的连接需求，特别是在国防和安全领域。乌克兰国防部宣布了一项初步协议，称打算与 ICEYE 和诺基亚就该星座进行合作，此前几天乌克兰报告称其对 SpaceX 提供的 Starlink 和 Starshield 宽带服务的访问受到新的限制。

“Modern warfare has proven that reliable communications are a matter of national security,” Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said. “We must all rely on capabilities that we can control.”

在 ICEYE 和诺基亚能够开始部署其星座之前，还有很长的路要走，但他们的目标是从 2028 年开始每年发射 50 颗卫星。这些公司尚未披露该网络吞吐量和延迟的预期数据，也没有披露全球或区域覆盖所需的卫星总数。根据高度的不同，这可能需要数百或数千颗卫星。SpaceX 目前在其 Starlink 星座中拥有超过 11,000 颗运行中的卫星。

What is clear, however, is that ICEYE’s future radar and broadband satellites will need rides to space, and Avio’s Vega C rocket needs business. The primary customer for Vega C is the European Space Agency, and the rest of the rocket’s future schedule is largely filled with satellites from European national governments. Vega C’s purely commercial business line is thin.

通过拟议的交易，ICEYE 表示将获得更多发射服务渠道，而 Avio 将获得投资以提高产量并增加 Vega 火箭系列的飞行频率，该系列目前的平均发射频率不到每年三次。

The most recent data indicates a dedicated Vega C launch sells for approximately $55 million, just shy of the commercial price for a much more powerful dedicated SpaceX Falcon 9. Nearly all of ICEYE’s satellites, except for the first view demo spacecraft, have launched on Falcon 9 rideshare missions, which SpaceX intends to phase out after 2028. SpaceX sold rideshare launch slots for satellites of ICEYE’s size and mass for about $700,000. After accounting for potential savings after ICEYE’s potential purchase of Avio, an ICEYE satellite might be able to launch on Vega C for two to three times that price.

Avio 和欧洲航天局正在共同资助开发新版本的 Vega，即 Vega E，它用一种新的高性能甲烷燃料上面级取代了 Vega C 的顶部。欧洲航天局和 Avio 去年还签署了一份价值近 5000 万美元的合同，开始设计一种在概念和外观上类似于 SpaceX 星舰但规模小得多的可重复使用上面级。其想法是完成可重复使用上面级的早期设计工作，作为 Vega E 的潜在替代品。
