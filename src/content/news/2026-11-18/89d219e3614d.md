# iFixit’s Apple HomeView teardown reveals a device that will be difficult to repair

iFixit 对 Apple HomeView 的拆解显示，该设备将难以维修。苹果在设计可维修设备方面所取得的进展似乎在 HomeView 上被放弃了。

Apple’s inroads on designing repairable devices seems to have been abandoned with the HomeView. After finding recent versions of Apple’s iPads and HomePods much easier to disassemble for battery replacements or other repairs, iFixit assumed it would be the same story for Apple’s new HomeView hub that looks like a seven-inch tablet stuck to a smart speaker.

在发现近期版本的 iPad 和 HomePod 在更换电池或其他维修时更容易拆解后，iFixit 原本以为苹果这款看起来像是一个七英寸平板电脑粘在智能音箱上的新 HomeView 集线器也会如此。然而现实却大相径庭，当 iFixit 的专业技术人员拆开 HomeView 时，他们“扯断了一个连接器，将一个扬声器掰成两半，并对那块可怜的逻辑板施加了超出我们承受范围的压力”。

The reality was much different and by the time they got the HomeView apart iFixit’s skilled techs had “ripped a connector, snapped a speaker in two, and put more force on the poor logic board than any of us were comfortable with.” The tablet section of the HomeView proved to be the bigger challenge to disassemble as a result of some questionable decisions with Apple’s design.

由于苹果设计中的一些令人质疑的决定，HomeView 的平板电脑部分被证明是拆解过程中更大的挑战。iFixit 表示，用于固定显示屏的胶水“比我们最近拆解的任何苹果产品都要牢固”。虽然苹果将 HomeView 的整个铝制底盘变成散热器的决定很巧妙，但隔热罩与设备逻辑板之间的热粘合使得将两者分离成为一项“极其冒险的操作”。

The glue used to secure the display in place “held on harder than on any Apple product we’ve opened recently,” iFixit says. And while Apple’s decision to turn the HomeView’s entire aluminum chassis into a heat sink was clever, the thermal bond between a heat shield and the device’s logic board made separating the two a “remarkably risky operation.” While these design decisions possibly streamlined assembly at a factory, the extensive use of glue makes servicing the HomeView incredibly difficult.

虽然这些设计决定可能简化了工厂的组装流程，但大量胶水的使用使得维修 HomeView 变得异常困难。在随附的拆解视频中，iFixit 还揭示了壁挂式 HomeView 中的扬声器驱动单元比桌面版小得多。尽管两个版本的售价均为 299 美元，但考虑到需要额外购买不包含在内的支架，壁挂版的价格实际上更高，这让 iFixit 感到消费者在花费更多金钱的同时却获得了更低的性能。

In an accompanying teardown video iFixit also reveals how much smaller the speaker drivers are in the wall-mounted version of the HomeView versus the tabletop option. Although both versions are priced at $299, the wall version costs a little more when you factor in the required mount that’s not included, leaving iFixit feeling that you’re getting less performance while spending more money.
