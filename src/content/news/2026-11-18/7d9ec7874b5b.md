# VSCO Is Gunning for Adobe Lightroom With Its New Desktop App

Save this storySave this storyNew photographers looking for inspiration often start with an app like VSCO. The popular mobile app, launched more than a decade ago, is known for one-click presets that emulate film and make it easy to elevate a photograph before posting it to social media. But as newbie shutterbugs level up their craft, they often graduate to an app like Adobe Lightroom and its more professional tool set.

寻找灵感的摄影新手通常会从 VSCO 这类应用开始。这款十多年前推出的热门移动应用以“一键预设”而闻名，它能模拟胶片质感，让用户在将照片发布到社交媒体前轻松提升画质。然而，随着摄影新手们不断精进技艺，他们往往会转向 Adobe Lightroom 等功能更专业的工具。

VSCO's pitch is simple: Keep photographers in the ecosystem by building a desktop app to rival Lightroom. On Tuesday, the company announced that its Studio Pro Desktop app is officially landing on macOS, following the launch of Studio Pro on iOS earlier this year. Neither version is available on Android or Windows, though the San Francisco–based company plans to port the apps in the future.

VSCO 的策略很简单：通过打造一款能与 Lightroom 抗衡的桌面应用，将摄影师留在其生态系统中。周二，该公司宣布其 Studio Pro 桌面版应用正式登陆 macOS，此前该应用的 iOS 版本已于今年早些时候发布。目前这两个版本均不支持 Android 或 Windows 系统，不过这家总部位于旧金山的初创公司计划在未来进行移植。

“We're helping shape these folks in their formative years,” VSCO CEO Eric Wittman tells WIRED. “We've got to keep giving them more bricks to build upon their own personal, creative journey—not just their creative journey, but also help them build a career.”

“我们正在帮助这些人在成长期塑造自我，”VSCO 首席执行官 Eric Wittman 在接受《连线》杂志采访时表示，“我们必须不断为他们提供更多的基石，以构建他们个人的创作之旅——不仅是创作之旅，还要帮助他们建立职业生涯。”

The desktop client is one part of VSCO's growing portfolio that caters to full-time photographers. Subscribing to VSCO One, for example, nets you access to Studio Pro, additional presets, and VSCO Workspace—a customer relationship management tool for handling contracts, booking, and scheduling. It also includes VSCO Galleries to send finished work directly to clients and VSCO Sites, a portfolio website builder to find new clients. All of these apps and services launched in the past year.

这款桌面客户端是 VSCO 不断扩大的全职摄影师服务组合的一部分。例如，订阅 VSCO One 即可使用 Studio Pro、额外的预设以及 VSCO Workspace——这是一个用于处理合同、预订和日程安排的客户关系管理工具。此外，它还包括用于将成品直接发送给客户的 VSCO Galleries，以及用于寻找新客户的作品集网站构建工具 VSCO Sites。所有这些应用和服务均于去年推出。

Access to the broad suite costs $500 a year, or you can get the Studio Pro mobile and desktop apps alone with a VSCO Pro membership for $5 per month ($60 annually). There is also a free tier, but some tools are locked behind the subscription. (Naturally, the original VSCO app is still around for enthusiasts.)

访问全套服务每年需花费 500 美元，或者您也可以通过每月 5 美元（每年 60 美元）的 VSCO Pro 会员资格单独获取 Studio Pro 移动版和桌面版应用。该服务还提供免费层级，但部分工具需要订阅才能使用。（当然，最初的 VSCO 应用依然面向爱好者开放。）

The Studio Pro Desktop app is speedy, features more than 200 presets, and supports RAW editing for 20 camera brands. Batch editing is the highlight, letting photographers edit up to 500 images simultaneously to speed up workflows while preserving the original files. Previews are rendered in real time, and at the end, users can export the final images directly to VSCO Galleries (or wherever they'd like).

Studio Pro 桌面版应用运行速度快，拥有 200 多种预设，并支持 20 个相机品牌的 RAW 格式编辑。批量编辑是其亮点，允许摄影师同时编辑多达 500 张照片，在保留原始文件的同时加快工作流程。预览可实时渲染，用户最终可以将成品直接导出到 VSCO Galleries（或任何他们想要的地方）。

I've been trying the app for a few weeks, and it is snappy. It loads RAW files quickly, and it even supports keyboard shortcuts. The sheer breadth of presets is also really nice, even if I tend to tweak colors myself. But there's a catch. If you're like me and coming from Lightroom, you'll miss a few features that Studio Pro Desktop doesn't offer yet. For example, Masking, Curves, and the Erase function (aka the spot removal tool) are “coming soon.”

我已经试用了这款应用几周，它反应非常灵敏。它能快速加载 RAW 文件，甚至支持键盘快捷键。尽管我倾向于自己调整颜色，但其丰富的预设确实非常出色。不过，这里有个问题。如果你像我一样是从 Lightroom 转过来的，你会发现 Studio Pro 桌面版缺少一些功能。例如，遮罩（Masking）、曲线（Curves）和擦除功能（即污点修复工具）目前都显示为“即将推出”。

“We want to be simple, intentionally, but we want to be powerful with our tools,” says Kyle Hale, VSCO's senior product manager. He says the app will feature around 70 percent of what people need for desktop editing, with the rest coming through regular software updates. While he doesn't expect full parity with a competitor like Lightroom by the first quarter of 2027, Studio Pro Desktop will have “the meat of what those programs offer.”

“我们有意保持简洁，但同时也希望我们的工具功能强大，”VSCO 高级产品经理 Kyle Hale 表示。他称，该应用目前涵盖了用户桌面编辑需求的大约 70%，其余功能将通过定期的软件更新逐步添加。虽然他预计到 2027 年第一季度仍无法与 Lightroom 等竞争对手完全对等，但 Studio Pro 桌面版将具备“这些程序所提供的核心功能”。

Companies like Adobe have heavily leaned into generative AI in their creative editing tools. While VSCO uses AI and machine-learning models in some tools like the upcoming Erase and retouch tools, the company says it doesn't train its own models on anything that people post on the VSCO app. VSCO also relies on some third-party models, like those from Black Forest Labs, but it has agreements in place that third parties cannot train on its user data.

像 Adobe 这样的公司已在其创意编辑工具中大量投入生成式 AI。虽然 VSCO 在即将推出的“擦除”和“修图”等部分工具中使用了 AI 和机器学习模型，但该公司表示，它不会利用用户在 VSCO 应用上发布的内容来训练自己的模型。VSCO 也依赖一些第三方模型（如来自 Black Forest Labs 的模型），但已签署协议，禁止第三方利用其用户数据进行训练。

“I don't think all AI is bad,” Wittman says. “But to be very, very clear: Generative AI, or if you're replacing the photographer—like what you're seeing with what Meta is doing, what Adobe's doing, like almost every other company is doing—we are not doing that. We stand by and champion photographers first and foremost, and that's not going to change.”

“我不认为所有的 AI 都是坏事，”Wittman 说，“但必须非常明确的是：生成式 AI，或者说取代摄影师的行为——就像你看到的 Meta、Adobe 以及几乎所有其他公司正在做的那样——我们不会这样做。我们始终支持并捍卫摄影师的利益，这一点永远不会改变。”
