Threat Intelligence Report | November 2026 From espionage campaigns and cloud credential theft to supply-chain compromise: how an autonomous penetration-testing framework is changing the speed, scale, and economics of cyber operations.

Executive Summary A new generation of cyber operations is emerging — not because attackers have suddenly discovered new vulnerabilities, but because they can now automate the process of finding, validating, and exploiting weaknesses across multiple environments. At the center of this development is ARTEX, an open-source, agentic AI penetration-testing framework originally designed for authorized security assessments.

执行摘要：新一代网络行动正在兴起——这并非因为攻击者突然发现了新的漏洞，而是因为他们现在可以自动化地在多个环境中查找、验证和利用弱点。这一发展的核心是 ARTEX，这是一个最初为授权安全评估而设计的开源代理式 AI 渗透测试框架。

Released in July 2026, ARTEX uses a planner-and-worker architecture to translate objectives into tasks, delegate operations to specialized agents, collect findings, and maintain shared operational knowledge. Threat intelligence reporting now connects ARTEX to campaigns involving Chinese-nexus espionage groups, financially motivated operators, and other malicious actors.

ARTEX 发布于 2026 年 7 月，它使用规划者与执行者架构将目标转化为任务，将操作委派给专业代理，收集调查结果，并维护共享的操作知识。目前的威胁情报报告已将 ARTEX 与涉及中国背景的间谍组织、以经济利益为动机的操作者以及其他恶意行为者的行动联系起来。

The FBI’s November 13, 2026 FLASH advisory warns that ARTEX has been weaponized in intrusions targeting organizations across financial services, government, technology, defense, semiconductor, energy, healthcare, manufacturing, telecommunications, and legal sectors. Some intrusions progressed from initial targeting to privileged access in under three hours.

The attacker does not necessarily need a sophisticated zero-day. It may only need one exposed credential, one overlooked application, or one excessive permission — and enough automation to turn that foothold into a chain of opportunities.

攻击者不一定需要复杂的零日漏洞。他们可能只需要一个暴露的凭据、一个被忽视的应用程序或一个过度的权限，再加上足够的自动化手段，就能将该立足点转化为一系列机会。

What Is ARTEX? ARTEX is an LLM-driven agentic penetration-testing framework designed to coordinate complex security-testing operations. Unlike a conventional scanner that executes a fixed set of checks, ARTEX uses an AI-driven planning process to determine what should happen next based on the information gathered during an operation.

什么是 ARTEX？ARTEX 是一个由大语言模型（LLM）驱动的代理式渗透测试框架，旨在协调复杂的安全测试操作。与执行固定检查集的传统扫描器不同，ARTEX 使用 AI 驱动的规划流程，根据操作期间收集的信息来确定下一步行动。

Its architecture generally revolves around three concepts: Planner: Receives the mission objective, develops a task sequence, and determines which activities should be performed. Worker agents: Execute delegated tasks, use security tools, collect results, and return findings to the shared operational environment. Shared knowledge and orchestration: Retains discovered assets, findings, task progress, and relationships so subsequent agents can continue from previous results.

其架构通常围绕三个概念：规划者：接收任务目标，制定任务序列，并确定应执行哪些活动。执行代理：执行委派任务，使用安全工具，收集结果，并将调查结果返回到共享操作环境。共享知识与编排：保留已发现的资产、调查结果、任务进度和关联关系，以便后续代理可以基于先前的结果继续工作。

This architecture can support activities such as: Target and asset discovery. Service and application enumeration. Vulnerability assessment. Credential and secret discovery. Authentication testing. Cloud permission analysis. Privilege escalation opportunities. Lateral movement planning. Post-exploitation discovery.

该架构可支持以下活动：目标与资产发现、服务与应用程序枚举、漏洞评估、凭据与机密发现、身份验证测试、云权限分析、特权提升机会、横向移动规划、利用后发现。

The important distinction is that ARTEX is not simply an AI chatbot suggesting commands. It is an orchestration framework designed to coordinate multiple operations and preserve their results. That makes it attractive to legitimate penetration testers — and potentially useful to attackers who want to scale operations without manually directing every individual step.

重要的区别在于，ARTEX 不仅仅是一个建议命令的 AI 聊天机器人。它是一个旨在协调多个操作并保存其结果的编排框架。这使得它对合法的渗透测试人员具有吸引力，同时也可能对那些希望在不手动指导每一步的情况下扩展操作的攻击者有用。

Why agentic architecture changes the threat Traditional security tools often perform predefined checks. Agentic systems can use the results of one activity to decide which activity should follow. For example: An agent discovers an exposed application. Another agent identifies an authentication weakness. A subsequent task discovers an accessible credential. The credential is validated against another service. New access reveals additional infrastructure and secrets. The planner assigns further tasks based on the newly discovered environment. Each step can create new opportunities for the next step. The result is a potentially self-reinforcing operational cycle: discovery creates access, access creates additional discovery, and additional discovery creates more opportunities for access. This is the foundation of the ARTEX threat.

为什么代理式架构会改变威胁？传统的安全工具通常执行预定义的检查。而代理式系统可以使用一项活动的结果来决定接下来应执行哪项活动。例如：一个代理发现了一个暴露的应用程序。另一个代理识别出了身份验证弱点。随后的任务发现了一个可访问的凭据。该凭据在另一个服务上得到验证。新的访问权限揭示了更多的基础设施和机密。规划者根据新发现的环境分配进一步的任务。每一步都可以为下一步创造新的机会。其结果是一个潜在的自我强化的操作循环：发现创造访问，访问创造额外的发现，而额外的发现又创造了更多的访问机会。这就是 ARTEX 威胁的基础。

Public reporting identifies several different operators and motivations, including espionage, financially motivated credential theft, and cryptocurrency-related activity. It is important to distinguish confirmed reporting from broader attribution assessments. The presence of ARTEX does not independently establish that an attack originated from China or that it was directed by a government. 中国背景的威胁行为者正在采用 ARTEX。新兴的 ARTEX 生态系统并不与单一的威胁组织相关联。公开报告识别出了几种不同的操作者和动机，包括间谍活动、以经济利益为动机的凭据窃取以及与加密货币相关的活动。区分已证实的报告与更广泛的归因评估非常重要。ARTEX 的存在并不能独立证明攻击源自中国或由政府指挥。尽管如此，多项调查已确认有中国背景的行为者正在使用该框架。
