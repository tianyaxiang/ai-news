Firaxis Games, the studio behind the Civilization and XCOM franchises of computer strategy games, last week laid off about a third of its staff, totaling around 60 people. That’s according to a recent report in Bloomberg. The report cites people “familiar with the cuts,” saying that the flagship game the studio is currently working on, a new entry in the XCOM series, “has faced development struggles and missed a significant milestone.” The departures also include the studio head, Heather Hazen.

《文明》与《幽浮》系列电脑策略游戏开发商 Firaxis Games 上周裁减了约三分之一的员工，总计约 60 人。据彭博社近期报道，知情人士透露，该工作室目前正在开发的旗舰游戏——《幽浮》系列新作——“面临开发困境，且错过了一个重要里程碑”。此次离职人员中还包括工作室负责人 Heather Hazen。

A Firaxis spokesperson released the following statement: Firaxis is restructuring and recentering around the studio’s core strength—designing world class strategy games that have set the industry standard for the genre for the last 30 years. As part of these changes, the size of the studio is being reduced to focus on design, technology and the player experience, which means saying goodbye to talented colleagues who helped build Firaxis. These decisions are never easy, but they’re essential to build the next chapter of Firaxis.

Firaxis 发言人发表声明称：“Firaxis 正在进行重组，并将重心重新聚焦于工作室的核心优势——设计过去 30 年来为该类型游戏树立行业标杆的世界级策略游戏。作为变革的一部分，工作室规模正在缩减，以专注于设计、技术和玩家体验，这意味着我们要告别那些曾帮助建立 Firaxis 的优秀同事。这些决定绝非易事，但对于开启 Firaxis 的下一个篇章至关重要。”

The storied studio has had a rough run of things of late. Its most recent game, Civilization VII, launched to solid enough sales but divided players in terms of reception. Players have long criticized new Civilization titles for being barebones compared to immediate predecessors that have seen multiple post-launch updates and expansions, but the response was sharper this time. The game has found more fans in recent months, but a portion of the Civilization audience will likely never fully warm to it due to its ages system—a design choice that partially solved many long-standing pacing problems, but at the cost of altering the core experience.

这家久负盛名的工作室近来境况不佳。其最新作品《文明 VII》发布后销量尚可，但玩家口碑两极分化。玩家长期以来一直批评《文明》系列新作相比于经过多次更新和扩展的前作显得内容匮乏，而这一次的批评声浪尤为尖锐。尽管近几个月来该游戏吸引了更多粉丝，但部分《文明》受众可能永远无法完全接受它，原因在于其“时代系统”——这一设计选择虽然部分解决了长期存在的节奏问题，但代价是改变了核心体验。

Before that, Firaxis worked on Marvel’s Midnight Suns, which had a more positive reception in terms of sentiment but does not seem to have sold as many units as the company had hoped. Now, with the new XCOM title facing challenges, that’s three troubled games in a row. To top it off, most of Firaxis’ direct competitors in the strategy game market are much leaner and not beholden to a major publisher. (The publisher for the Civilization franchise is 2K Games, which lives under the same company that publishes the soon-to-launch Grand Theft Auto VI.)

在此之前，Firaxis 开发了《漫威暗夜之子》，虽然口碑较好，但销量似乎未达公司预期。如今，随着《幽浮》新作面临挑战，这已经是连续三款游戏陷入困境。更糟糕的是，Firaxis 在策略游戏市场的大多数直接竞争对手规模更小，且不受大型发行商的束缚。（《文明》系列的发行商是 2K Games，该公司与即将发行的《侠盗猎车手 VI》属于同一母公司。）

The industry at large has seen widespread layoffs and studio closures over the past few years. There’s no single reason for that; rather, there are numerous factors, including contraction after COVID-era over-investment, increased competition, changes in leadership culture at major publishers, and larger economic factors beyond the scope of just the games industry.

过去几年，整个行业经历了大规模的裁员和工作室倒闭。这并非单一原因所致，而是多种因素共同作用的结果，包括疫情期间过度投资后的收缩、竞争加剧、大型发行商领导文化的变化，以及超出游戏行业范畴的宏观经济因素。
