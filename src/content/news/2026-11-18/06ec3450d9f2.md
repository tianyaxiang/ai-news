The MacBook Neo is receiving a rare $50 discount ahead of Black Friday.

MacBook Neo 在黑色星期五前迎来了罕见的 50 美元折扣。

While $50 might not feel like much, it brings the laptop closer to its original launch price.

虽然 50 美元可能看起来不多，但这让这款笔记本电脑的价格更接近其最初的发布价。

If you’re looking for a new laptop or a gift for the student in your life, you can save $50 on the MacBook Neo at several retailers, in both storage sizes and all four colors. The 256GB model is currently discounted to $649 (Amazon, Best Buy, Target), while the 512GB model with Touch ID is down to $749 (Amazon, Best Buy, Target).

如果您正在寻找一台新笔记本电脑或想为学生挑选礼物，您可以在多家零售商处以优惠 50 美元的价格购买 MacBook Neo，该优惠适用于所有存储版本和四种颜色。256GB 型号目前折扣价为 649 美元（亚马逊、百思买、塔吉特），而带有触控 ID 的 512GB 型号则降至 749 美元（亚马逊、百思买、塔吉特）。

None of the retailers currently list an end date for the discount, which is the biggest price drop we’ve seen since Apple raised the laptop’s MSRP by $100 in July in response to the ongoing RAM crisis.

目前没有任何零售商列出此折扣的结束日期，这是自苹果公司在 7 月份因持续的内存危机将该笔记本电脑的建议零售价提高 100 美元以来，我们所见过的最大降价幅度。

Apple’s budget-friendly MacBook leverages the A18 Pro processor, a chip originally found in the iPhone 16 Pro, to keep costs down. Both the 256GB and 512GB models feature 8GB of memory, and my colleague Antonio G. Di Benedetto was impressed by the vivid 13-inch screen and speakers, the latter of which are better than those in many cheap (and even midrange) Windows laptops.

苹果这款经济实惠的 MacBook 利用了最初用于 iPhone 16 Pro 的 A18 Pro 处理器来降低成本。256GB 和 512GB 型号均配备 8GB 内存，我的同事 Antonio G. Di Benedetto 对其生动的 13 英寸屏幕和扬声器印象深刻，后者的表现优于许多廉价（甚至中端）Windows 笔记本电脑。

The Neo isn’t super performant, especially in terms of storage speed and total memory, but it’s great for everyday tasks, casual use, and students.

Neo 的性能并非极其强劲，尤其是在存储速度和总内存方面，但它非常适合日常任务、休闲使用和学生群体。
