You might be excused for forgetting about the existence of 3 Body Problem, Netflix’s heady sci-fi series adapted from the award-winning novel The Three-Body Problem by Liu Cixin, the first book in his Remembrance of Earth’s Past trilogy. After all, it debuted in March 2024. But it was an exceptionally well-done, thematically rich series, and we’re finally getting the second of a planned three seasons in January. Netflix just dropped the first teaser. (Spoilers for S1 below.)

如果你已经忘记了《三体》（3 Body Problem）这部剧的存在，那也情有可原。这是 Netflix 改编自刘慈欣获奖小说《三体》（“地球往事”三部曲的第一部）的一部烧脑科幻剧集。毕竟，它是在 2024 年 3 月首播的。但这是一部制作精良、主题丰富的剧集，我们终于要在 1 月迎来计划中三季里的第二季了。Netflix 刚刚发布了首支预告片。（下文包含第一季剧透。）

Created by David Benioff, D.B. Weiss (Game of Thrones), and Alexander Woo (True Blood), the first season followed two primary timelines. In the earlier one, a young Chinese astrophysicist, Ye Wenjie (Rosalind Chao), witnessed her father being beaten to death by Red Guards during the Cultural Revolution. Ye figured out a means of sending an interstellar message to possible extraterrestrial civilizations, receiving a response from a planet called Trisolaris. The planet had three suns, which wreaked havoc on Trisolaris via unpredictable “chaotic periods”—hence the novel’s title, which refers to a classic problem in celestial mechanics.

第一季由大卫·贝尼奥夫、D·B·威斯（《权力的游戏》）和亚历山大·伍（《真爱如血》）创作，讲述了两条主要的时间线。在较早的时间线中，年轻的中国天体物理学家叶文洁（赵家玲饰）目睹了父亲在文化大革命期间被红卫兵殴打致死。叶文洁找到了一种向潜在的外星文明发送星际信息的方法，并收到了来自三体行星的回应。这颗行星有三个太阳，由于不可预测的“乱纪元”而给三体世界带来了灾难——这正是小说标题的由来，指的是天体力学中的一个经典问题。

Despite being warned that the aliens intended to invade and conquer Earth, Ye responded to the message and invited them to do so, bitter and disillusioned by the state of the world. She eventually became an established professor (Zine Tseng played the older version). In the present day, the consequences of Ye’s fateful decision began to play out, beginning with a series of mysterious suicides by scientists, many of whom had possessed a highly advanced VR gaming headset. Among the dead: a particle physicist named Vera Ye (daughter of Ye Wenjie).

尽管被警告外星人意图入侵并征服地球，但叶文洁因对世界现状感到痛苦和幻灭，还是回复了信息并邀请他们到来。她最终成为了一名资深教授（曾靖饰）。在现代，叶文洁这一决定性选择的后果开始显现，首先是一系列科学家神秘自杀事件，其中许多人曾拥有过一套高度先进的虚拟现实游戏头显。死者中包括一位名叫叶薇（叶文洁的女儿）的粒子物理学家。

Five of her former students (known as the Oxford Five) joined forces to figure out what was going on, eventually finding themselves caught in a global fight to save Earth from the invading Trisolarians (or San-Ti). Another human cult faction was just as intent on enabling the San-Ti invasion, believing they would be spared as a reward, while Ye Wenjie still believed the entire human race deserved to be annihilated. The second season will adapt the second novel in the series, The Dark Forest, with the third book, Death’s End, serving as source material for the third season.

她的五名学生（被称为“牛津五人组”）联手调查真相，最终发现自己卷入了一场拯救地球免受三体人（或称“三体”）入侵的全球战争。另一个人类邪教派系则一心促成三体人的入侵，认为他们会因此获得奖赏而幸免于难，而叶文洁依然认为全人类都应该被毁灭。第二季将改编该系列的第二部小说《黑暗森林》，第三部小说《死神永生》将作为第三季的素材。

Per the official S2 synopsis: “As the alien invasion nears, humanity prepares to meet them—on Earth and beyond. While a space fleet embarks on a mission that spans centuries, a secret locked inside of one man’s mind might be humanity’s only hope for survival.”

根据第二季的官方剧情简介：“随着外星入侵临近，人类正准备在地球内外迎接他们。当一支太空舰队踏上跨越世纪的征程时，锁在一个人脑海中的秘密，或许是人类生存的唯一希望。”

We lost two members of the Oxford Five in S1: Jack Rooney (John Bradley), killed by cult member Tatiana (Marlo Kelly); and physics teacher Will Downing (Alex Sharp), who was dying of cancer and chose to euthanize himself and donate his cryogenically frozen brain to a bold effort to stave off the invasion. (While the probe carrying Will’s brain veered off-course in the season finale, the original plan was for the San-Ti to use the brain to reconstruct his body, so some version of Will could show up again.)

我们在第一季中失去了“牛津五人组”的两名成员：被邪教成员塔蒂亚娜（玛洛·凯利饰）杀害的杰克·鲁尼（约翰·布莱德利饰）；以及身患绝症、选择安乐死并将冷冻大脑捐献给一项旨在抵御入侵的大胆计划的物理老师威尔·唐宁（艾利克斯·夏普饰）。（虽然在第一季结尾，携带威尔大脑的探测器偏离了航线，但最初的计划是让三体人利用大脑重建他的身体，因此某种形式的威尔可能会再次出现。）

Kelly’s Tatiana is returning for S2, along with surviving Oxford Five members Jin Cheng (Jess Hong), Saul Durand (Jovan Adepo), and Auggie Salazar (Eiza Gonzalez), whose pioneering nanofibers were memorably used in S1 to slice through a ship in the Panama Canal, killing all the pro-alien cult members on board in gruesome fashion. Also returning are Liam Cunningham as Thomas Wade; Benedict Wong as MI6 agent Clarence “Da” Shi; Sea Shimooka as Sophon, an avatar in the show’s mysterious VR game; and Saamer Usmani as Jin’s Royal Navy officer boyfriend Prithviraj Varma.

凯利的塔蒂亚娜将回归第二季，幸存的“牛津五人组”成员程心（洪连娜饰）、索尔·杜兰德（约翰·艾德坡饰）和奥吉·萨拉查（艾莎·冈萨雷斯饰）也将回归。奥吉的开创性纳米纤维曾在第一季中令人难忘地切开了巴拿马运河上的一艘船，以残酷的方式杀死了船上所有支持外星人的邪教成员。其他回归的演员还包括饰演托马斯·韦德的利亚姆·坎宁安；饰演军情六处特工史强（大史）的本尼迪克特·王；饰演剧中神秘 VR 游戏化身“智子”的希·下冈；以及饰演程心皇家海军军官男友普里特维拉杰·瓦尔马的萨默·乌斯马尼。

New cast members include Claudia Doumit as Captain Van Rijn and Ellie de Lange as Ayla. In addition, Alfie Allen, David Yip, and Jordan Sunshine will appear in as-yet-undisclosed guest roles. The second season of 3 Body Problem drops on Netflix on January 14, 2027.

新加入的演员包括饰演范·里恩船长的克劳迪娅·多米特和饰演艾拉的艾莉·德·兰格。此外，阿尔菲·艾伦、大卫·叶和乔丹·桑夏恩将以尚未公开的客串角色身份出现。《三体》第二季将于 2027 年 1 月 14 日在 Netflix 上线。
