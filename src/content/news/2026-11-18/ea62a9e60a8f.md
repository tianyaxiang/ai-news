Jeff Bezos says he was ‘heavily involved’ in picking the new James Bond.

杰夫·贝索斯称他“深度参与”了新任詹姆斯·邦德的选角工作。

Bezos said that he was immediately drawn to Robert Pattinson’s performance during screen tests.

贝索斯表示，他在试镜过程中立刻被罗伯特·帕丁森的表现所吸引。

Robert Pattinson’s casting as James Bond made enough sense given his recent work with director Denis Villeneuve, who’s making the franchise’s next film over at Amazon. But it turns out that Amazon founder Jeff Bezos played a significant role in making the final decision, too.

考虑到罗伯特·帕丁森近期与导演丹尼斯·维伦纽瓦的合作，他被选为詹姆斯·邦德在情理之中，而维伦纽瓦目前正在亚马逊制作该系列的下一部电影。但事实证明，亚马逊创始人杰夫·贝索斯在最终决策中也发挥了重要作用。

During Semafor’s Silicon Valley and the World conference today, Bezos said that he was “heavily involved” in the process of casting the newest 007: We ran a really tight process and we made the final decision on Friday. There was a big group initially, and we down-selected to five finalists. Then we did real old-fashioned screen tests of those five finalists, and it was really interesting — full costume, real sets, other actors. We did three different scenes with each of the five finalists, and then we got together on Friday to spend a couple of hours looking at all those screening tests, and reviewing all of that. And all of the actors were extraordinarily talented.

在今天举行的 Semafor“硅谷与世界”大会上，贝索斯表示他“深度参与”了最新一任 007 的选角过程：“我们执行了一个非常严密的流程，并在周五做出了最终决定。最初候选人很多，我们筛选出了五名决赛入围者。随后，我们对这五人进行了非常传统的试镜，过程非常有趣——全套服装、真实布景，还有其他演员配合。我们与每位入围者拍摄了三个不同的场景，然后在周五聚在一起，花了几个小时观看并评估了所有试镜片段。所有的演员都非常有才华。”

By the time we got to this list of five, they were all amazing, but there was something special about Rob Pattinson and his performances. It was kind of immediately undebated. Denis — the director — we all sort of gravitated to Rob. By the way, initially, I would say we were slightly biased a little against him, just because he’s already so famous. We sort of thought, ‘maybe we should have somebody a little more unknown.’ But in the screen test, it was so obvious. He is James Bond.

“当我们确定这五人名单时，他们都很出色，但罗伯·帕丁森和他的表演有一些特别之处。这几乎是毫无争议的。丹尼斯（导演）和我们所有人都倾向于罗伯。顺便说一下，起初我们对他有一点偏见，仅仅因为他已经太出名了。我们当时想，‘也许我们应该找一个名气没那么大的人。’但在试镜中，结果显而易见。他就是詹姆斯·邦德。”

It’s not exactly surprising that a studio leader would want to have a say in casting the new face for one of their most lucrative franchises. But with Bezos being so hands-on, one can only image what this new era of Bond films will look and feel like once Amazon starts releasing them in theaters.

制片厂负责人想要在他们最赚钱的系列电影之一中对新面孔的选角拥有发言权，这并不令人惊讶。但鉴于贝索斯如此亲力亲为，人们不禁会想象，一旦亚马逊开始在影院发行这些电影，新时代的邦德电影将会呈现出怎样的面貌和质感。
