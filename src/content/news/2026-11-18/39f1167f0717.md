# Jensen Huang Says He’s Open to Coming Back to Nvidia

Jensen Huang said that he is open to returning as chief executive of Nvidia, five days after he resigned from the company. In a statement published Tuesday morning on X, Huang said that since stepping down he has received an “outpouring of calls from Nvidia’s employees, investors, business partners, and customers for me to return as CEO.”

黄仁勋表示，在辞去英伟达首席执行官一职五天后，他愿意重返公司。周二上午，黄仁勋在 X 上发表声明称，自卸任以来，他收到了“来自英伟达员工、投资者、商业伙伴和客户的如潮呼声，要求我重返首席执行官岗位。”

“If the board of directors decides that my return is in the best interest of our shareholders, our employees, the company, and America, I would be honored to answer these calls and return as CEO,” Huang wrote. Huang also thanked people who showed “kindness” to him and his family and acknowledged “the love and support of friends, industry colleagues, and people I've never met.” He reiterated his love for America and Nvidia and his remorse for his unfaithfulness to his wife, which he also expressed in a company-wide email sent last week.

“如果董事会认为我的回归符合股东、员工、公司和美国的最佳利益，我将很荣幸响应这些呼声并重返首席执行官岗位，”黄仁勋写道。他还感谢了对他及其家人表现出“善意”的人们，并对“朋友、行业同仁以及我从未谋面之人的关爱与支持”表示感谢。他重申了自己对美国和英伟达的热爱，并对自己对妻子的不忠表示悔恨，这与他上周发送给全公司的电子邮件中所表达的内容一致。

Huang resigned four days after Nvidia launched an internal investigation into an affair he had admitted to having with Chinese businesswoman Alice Huang, who has no known relation to Jensen. Huang reportedly misled the company’s general counsel and chief marketing officer about the affair with Alice.

在英伟达对其承认与中国女商人黄乐（Alice Huang，与黄仁勋无已知亲属关系）发生婚外情展开内部调查四天后，黄仁勋辞职。据报道，黄仁勋曾就与黄乐的婚外情一事误导了公司的总法律顾问和首席营销官。

The day before Huang stepped down, The New York Times reported that he'd had a second affair with one of his direct reports at Nvidia. After his resignation, Reuters reported on an alleged third affair with another Nvidia employee. The company’s code of conduct discourages "personal relationships" that "may create a conflict of interest."

在黄仁勋卸任的前一天，《纽约时报》报道称他与英伟达的一名直属下属发生了第二次婚外情。在他辞职后，路透社报道了据称他与另一名英伟达员工的第三段婚外情。公司的行为准则不鼓励可能“造成利益冲突”的“个人关系”。

Colette Kress, Nvidia’s longtime chief financial officer, was installed as interim CEO upon Huang’s resignation.

在黄仁勋辞职后，英伟达长期担任首席财务官的科莱特·克雷斯（Colette Kress）被任命为临时首席执行官。

The New York Times first reported Jensen’s association with Alice, also known as Huang Le, in October 2025. On November 6 of this year, citing an intelligence memo that reportedly circulated among Trump administration officials last spring, The New York Times and the Financial Times reported that intelligence officials believed Alice may be an asset of China’s Ministry of State Security. Trump administration officials, including tech executive and White House Science and Technology advisory council cochair David Sacks, have questioned whether Alice was associated with the MSS. The Chinese government has rejected claims that Alice spied for the nation.

《纽约时报》于 2025 年 10 月首次报道了黄仁勋与黄乐（Alice Huang）的交往。今年 11 月 6 日，《纽约时报》和《金融时报》援引一份据称去年春天在特朗普政府官员间传阅的情报备忘录报道称，情报官员认为黄乐可能是中国国家安全部的资产。包括科技高管兼白宫科技咨询委员会联合主席大卫·萨克斯（David Sacks）在内的特朗普政府官员曾质疑黄乐是否与国安部有关联。中国政府否认了黄乐为国家从事间谍活动的说法。

WIRED reported last week that on the same day Huang resigned, Nvidia employees began using an existing petition to the board to call for his reinstatement. It gained more than 1,600 comments in just a few hours.

《连线》杂志上周报道称，在黄仁勋辞职的当天，英伟达员工就开始利用一份现有的致董事会请愿书呼吁让他复职。该请愿书在短短几个小时内就获得了超过 1,600 条评论。

A separate, public petition titled “Stand With Jensen” similarly calls for the executive’s return. At the time of writing, it has more than 193,000 online signatures, including more than 11,000 self-identified current and former Nvidia employees and more than 100,000 self-identified shareholders. On Friday, Elon Musk said that he was going to sign the petition, and Y Combinator CEO Garry Tan claimed that he had signed.

另一份名为“支持黄仁勋”（Stand With Jensen）的公开请愿书也同样呼吁这位高管回归。截至撰稿时，该请愿书已获得超过 193,000 个在线签名，其中包括超过 11,000 名自称是英伟达现任和前任员工的人，以及超过 100,000 名自称是股东的人。周五，埃隆·马斯克（Elon Musk）表示他将签署该请愿书，Y Combinator 首席执行官加里·谭（Garry Tan）则声称他已经签署。

President Donald Trump told reporters on Monday that he “would not mind” if Huang returned as CEO, adding that he believed Huang had “learned a big lesson.”

唐纳德·特朗普总统周一告诉记者，如果黄仁勋重返首席执行官岗位，他“不会介意”，并补充说他相信黄仁勋已经“吸取了深刻的教训”。

Nvidia’s board of directors, however, reportedly said on Monday that it was not considering reinstating Huang as CEO. On Tuesday, the board announced that it had established a National Security and Export Control Oversight Committee “for board-level oversight of the company’s national security policies, export control compliance and global trade risk management.”

然而，据报道，英伟达董事会周一表示，并未考虑让黄仁勋复职担任首席执行官。周二，董事会宣布成立国家安全与出口管制监督委员会，“旨在从董事会层面监督公司的国家安全政策、出口管制合规性以及全球贸易风险管理。”
