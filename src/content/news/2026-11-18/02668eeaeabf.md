United Launch Alliance (ULA) confirmed Tuesday morning that its return-to-flight launch of the Vulcan rocket began failing during the initial stages of the mission. “While we appear to have lost telemetry and the mission at approximately 10 minutes into flight, early indications are that just after liftoff, the launch vehicle experienced low booster performance, resulting in stage separation occurring when the vehicle was low in altitude and below minimal velocity to reach orbit,” the company said.

联合发射联盟（ULA）周二上午证实，其“火神”火箭的复飞任务在飞行初期就开始出现故障。该公司表示：“虽然我们似乎在飞行约10分钟后失去了遥测信号和任务控制，但早期迹象表明，在起飞后不久，运载火箭的助推器性能较低，导致级间分离发生时，火箭高度较低且速度低于进入轨道所需的最低速度。”

The launch company also confirmed reporting by Ars that the Centaur V upper stage and the solid rocket boosters performed nominally during the flight. Critically, ULA did not provide any specific information about the “low booster performance.” This matters greatly because the core stage of the Vulcan rocket is powered by two BE-4 engines, which are provided by Blue Origin.

该发射公司还证实了Ars的报道，即“半人马座V”上面级和固体火箭助推器在飞行过程中表现正常。关键在于，ULA并未提供关于“助推器性能低下”的具体信息。这一点至关重要，因为“火神”火箭的核心级由蓝色起源公司提供的两台BE-4发动机驱动。

That company’s own New Glenn rocket uses a similar but slightly more powerful version of the BE-4 engine. And New Glenn experienced its own catastrophic failure in May during a ground test, a failure that later was attributed to a main oxygen valve on one of the BE-4 engines.

蓝色起源公司自己的“新格伦”火箭使用的是BE-4发动机的类似但功率稍大的版本。“新格伦”火箭曾在5月份的一次地面测试中遭遇灾难性故障，该故障后来被归因于其中一台BE-4发动机的主氧阀。

Information about the Vulcan failure is being closely held by ULA and Blue Origin, who have only acknowledged that they’re working together to investigate the cause. According to two sources, the investigation is “incredibly complicated” and remains in the early stages. However, according to preliminary data, two independent sources indicated to Ars that the problem is currently believed to have originated on the launch vehicle side rather than in the engines themselves.

关于“火神”故障的信息正由ULA和蓝色起源公司严格保密，他们仅承认双方正在合作调查原因。据两位消息人士透露，调查“极其复杂”，目前仍处于早期阶段。然而，根据初步数据，两个独立消息源向Ars表示，目前认为问题出在运载火箭本身，而非发动机。

The issue may be related to systems that feed methane and liquid oxygen propellant to the engines. ULA engineers are looking at the possibility that one of the two BE-4 engines started producing lower thrust shortly after liftoff and never ramped back up to full throttle for the remainder of the burn.

该问题可能与向发动机输送甲烷和液氧推进剂的系统有关。ULA的工程师正在研究一种可能性，即两台BE-4发动机中的一台在起飞后不久推力下降，并在剩余的燃烧过程中未能恢复到全油门状态。

Video observations from high-zoom tracking cameras appeared to show one engine slightly dimmer than the other after Vulcan jettisoned its solid rocket boosters nearly two minutes into flight. An asymmetric shutdown of the BE-4 engines could also have contributed to the sudden attitude motion indicated by ULA’s telemetry-driven real-time animation near stage separation.

高倍变焦跟踪摄像头的视频观测显示，在“火神”火箭飞行近两分钟抛掉固体火箭助推器后，其中一台发动机看起来比另一台稍暗。BE-4发动机的不对称关机也可能导致了ULA遥测实时动画中显示的级间分离附近的姿态突变。

The Federal Aviation Administration is overseeing the investigation, which is being led by ULA. Again, this information is preliminary, and no definitive conclusions can or should be drawn.

美国联邦航空管理局正在监督此次调查，调查由ULA主导。再次强调，这些信息仅为初步数据，不能也不应得出任何最终结论。

This was the fifth flight of the Vulcan rocket since its debut in January 2024. Two of the previous flights have experienced issues with the nozzles on its solid rocket boosters, but in both cases, the rocket was able to compensate for the reduced and asymmetric thrust and reach its intended orbit. The second nozzle issue caused Vulcan to stand down for nine months.

这是“火神”火箭自2024年1月首飞以来的第五次飞行。之前的两次飞行中，其固体火箭助推器的喷管曾出现问题，但在两次事故中，火箭都成功补偿了推力的降低和不对称，并到达了预定轨道。第二次喷管问题导致“火神”停飞了九个月。

This latest setback is likely to sideline Vulcan for at least half a year, and probably longer. Most impacted will be the US Space Force, which has national security payloads waiting for a ride to space on Vulcan, and Amazon, which had more than a dozen launches of its Amazon Leo constellation on the manifest for 2027. It would exacerbate a worsening launch crisis in the United States.

这次最新的挫折可能会使“火神”火箭至少停飞半年，甚至更久。受影响最大的是美国太空军，其国家安全载荷正等待由“火神”火箭送入太空；此外还有亚马逊公司，其2027年的发射清单上有十多次“柯伊伯”星座卫星的发射任务。这将加剧美国日益严重的发射危机。

There are potentially serious implications for Blue Origin as well, which is seeking to return New Glenn to flight as early as January. Last Tuesday, the company said a modified BE-4 main oxygen valve had successfully completed qualification testing. “Our modified valve has already been incorporated into engines slated for upcoming flights, and we are now rolling it out across the remaining fleet,” the company said. “We expect to complete the anomaly investigation later this month.”

这对蓝色起源公司也可能产生严重影响，该公司正寻求最早在1月让“新格伦”火箭复飞。上周二，该公司表示，改进后的BE-4主氧阀已成功通过鉴定测试。公司称：“我们改进后的阀门已经安装到计划用于后续飞行的发动机中，目前正在其余机队中推广。我们预计将在本月晚些时候完成异常调查。”

If Vulcan’s failure were ultimately due to a problem with the BE-4 engine, it would certainly delay the New Glenn rocket’s return to flight. This would be a setback not just for Blue Origin but for NASA, which is counting on the super heavy lift rocket to do much of the heavy lifting for its Artemis Program and Moon Base.

如果“火神”的故障最终被证实是由BE-4发动机问题引起的，那无疑将推迟“新格伦”火箭的复飞。这对蓝色起源公司来说是一个挫折，对美国国家航空航天局（NASA）而言也是如此，因为NASA正指望这款超重型运载火箭为其“阿尔忒弥斯”计划和月球基地承担大部分重型运输任务。
