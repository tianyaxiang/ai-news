EXECUTIVE SUMMARY The US Southwest generated 28% less electricity via hydropower between April and August than the 10-year average, according to new data from the US Energy Information Administration. Most hydropower plants in the US rely on dams to hold back water, creating reservoirs. The water can be released through turbines, generating electricity on demand. But an extreme, prolonged drought in the Colorado River Basin has resulted in dangerously low water levels in the Southwest’s two largest reservoirs, affecting the availability of this power source.

根据美国能源信息署的最新数据，今年4月至8月期间，美国西南部通过水力发电产生的电力比10年平均水平低了28%。美国大多数水电站依靠大坝蓄水形成水库，通过释放水流带动涡轮机按需发电。然而，科罗拉多河流域极端且持续的干旱导致西南部两个最大的水库水位降至危险低位，影响了这一能源的供应。

Residents and farmers in the US Southwest, a region that includes Arizona, New Mexico, and parts of Nevada and California, rely on water that melts in the spring from snowpack in the mountains and is slowly allowed to flow south via dam releases. Snowfall in the Rocky Mountains provides about 85% of the annual flow of the Colorado River. And in recent years, the river has seen record-low flows.

美国西南部（包括亚利桑那州、新墨西哥州以及内华达州和加利福尼亚州的部分地区）的居民和农民，依赖于春季山区积雪融化并通过大坝放水缓慢流向南方的水源。落基山脉的降雪提供了科罗拉多河约85%的年径流量，而近年来该河流的流量已降至历史最低水平。

Two reservoirs along the Colorado River, Lake Powell and Lake Mead, have struggled to stay above the minimum level required for hydropower plants. At the end of September, Lake Powell’s depth was 3,518 feet, less than 1% above the level required to run the water through turbines that produce electricity at the Glen Canyon power plant. The Hoover Dam power plant requires 950 feet in Lake Mead, and that reservoir was 9% above the minimum in September. Together, the two plants produced nearly seven terawatt-hours of electricity in 2025, enough to serve over 2 million people.

科罗拉多河沿岸的两个水库——鲍威尔湖和米德湖，一直难以维持在水电站所需的最低水位之上。9月底，鲍威尔湖的水位为3518英尺，仅比格伦峡谷水电站涡轮机发电所需的最低水位高出不到1%。胡佛大坝水电站要求米德湖水位达到950英尺，9月份该水库水位比最低要求高出9%。这两座电站2025年共生产了近7太瓦时的电力，足以供应超过200万人使用。

Low water levels have been an ongoing struggle for the region, which has been in a drought for 27 years. Meanwhile, climate change is continuing to shift rainfall and snowfall patterns across the region and around the world. The effects of climate change on hydropower aren’t entirely straightforward globally—some regions will see more rain and snow because of shifting weather patterns, while others are likely to see less. But it’s playing a role for hydropower availability, according to one 2022 review study. Rising temperatures bring more droughts, warmer winters means less snow and ice to fill the reservoirs, and more variability in precipitation can lead to alternating floods and droughts, making the power source less reliable.

低水位一直是该地区长期面临的困境，这里已经持续干旱了27年。与此同时，气候变化继续改变着该地区乃至全球的雨雪模式。气候变化对全球水力发电的影响并不完全一致——由于天气模式的转变，一些地区可能会出现更多的雨雪，而另一些地区则可能减少。但根据2022年的一项综述研究，气候变化确实影响了水力发电的可用性。气温升高带来更多干旱，暖冬意味着积雪和冰川减少，无法补充水库，而降水量的波动性增加可能导致洪水与干旱交替出现，使得这一能源变得不再可靠。

Hydropower production is likely to be at its lowest level in over a decade in 2026 and tick even lower in 2027, according to projections from the EIA. El Niño could provide some temporary relief. Sometimes this weather pattern can mean cooler, wetter conditions for the southern US. Heavy rains brought floods to the region in October and early November, and the water levels in Lake Powell rose more than 20 feet from their mid-September low. But the water is still quite low, and it’ll take much more to help reservoirs recover from decades of drought conditions.

根据美国能源信息署的预测，2026年的水力发电量可能会降至十多年来的最低水平，并在2027年进一步下降。厄尔尼诺现象可能会提供一些暂时的缓解，因为这种天气模式有时意味着美国南部地区气候更凉爽、更潮湿。10月和11月初的强降雨在该地区引发了洪水，鲍威尔湖的水位从9月中旬的低点上升了20多英尺。但水位仍然很低，水库要从几十年的干旱状态中恢复还需要更多的降水。

Even as a changing climate complicates the availability of hydropower, the decline in capacity could push greenhouse-gas emissions higher. In 2023, energy-related greenhouse-gas emissions rose by 1.1% globally. A worldwide shortfall in hydropower generation accounted for about 40% of that rise, according to a report from the International Energy Agency.

尽管气候变化使水力发电的可用性变得复杂，但发电能力的下降可能会推高温室气体排放。根据国际能源署的一份报告，2023年全球与能源相关的温室气体排放量增长了1.1%，其中全球水力发电量的不足约占这一增长的40%。
