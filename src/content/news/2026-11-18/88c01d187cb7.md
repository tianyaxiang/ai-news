On Monday, large trucks began circling the streets of Washington, DC, carrying billboards urging Congress to investigate the Trump administration’s ties with Nvidia’s disgraced CEO Jensen Huang. “Jensen was in bed with a spy from China,” the billboards created by the Tech Oversight Project (TOP) alleged. “Trump was in bed with Jensen.”

周一，大型卡车开始在华盛顿特区的街道上巡回，车上悬挂的广告牌敦促国会调查特朗普政府与英伟达（Nvidia）名誉扫地的首席执行官黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）之间的关系。由科技监督项目（Tech Oversight Project, TOP）制作的广告牌声称：“黄仁勋与一名中国间谍有染”，“特朗普与黄仁勋有染”。

TOP is a nonprofit that describes itself as “dedicated to reining in Big Tech.” Currently, Trump is supporting a campaign to bring back Huang as Nvidia’s CEO—despite many Republicans calling it a “horrible idea.” Breaking his silence, Huang issued a statement Tuesday saying he would be “honored” to return, but the board said reinstatement is not under consideration as an internal investigation into US spying claims remains ongoing.

TOP是一个非营利组织，自称“致力于约束大型科技公司”。目前，尽管许多共和党人称这是一个“糟糕的主意”，但特朗普仍支持让黄仁勋重返英伟达担任首席执行官的行动。黄仁勋打破沉默，于周二发表声明称，他将“很荣幸”回归，但董事会表示，由于针对美国间谍指控的内部调查仍在进行中，目前不考虑恢复其职务。

Huang used the moment to say that his interests align with the US’s, saying: I came to the United States 53 years ago, and becoming an American citizen is one of the greatest gifts and proudest moments of my life. Nvidia has been my life’s work. Building it into the company it is today alongside my colleagues has been a privilege and an honor. Nowhere but in America could a company like Nvidia be possible. I love our country, and I love Nvidia.

黄仁勋借此机会表示，他的利益与美国一致，他说道：“我53年前来到美国，成为一名美国公民是我一生中收到的最伟大的礼物之一，也是我最自豪的时刻。英伟达是我毕生的事业。与同事们一起将其打造成为今天的公司，是一种荣幸。除了美国，没有其他地方能诞生像英伟达这样的公司。我爱我们的国家，我也爱英伟达。”

Trump may have learned as early as this spring that Huang was potentially the target of Chinese espionage, but the president claimed recently that he didn’t know Huang was potentially exposed to a Chinese spy as he was becoming a prominent part of Trump’s inner circle, CNN reported. If Trump wasn’t adequately briefed, it raises question about whether the White House acted with “sufficient urgency” to address a serious espionage threat, CNN said.

据CNN报道，特朗普可能早在今年春天就获悉黄仁勋可能是中国间谍活动的目标，但这位总统最近声称，当黄仁勋成为他核心圈子的重要成员时，他并不知道黄仁勋可能接触过中国间谍。CNN称，如果特朗普没有得到充分的简报，这就引发了白宫在应对严重间谍威胁时是否采取了“足够紧迫”行动的质疑。

But if Trump is deflecting, an even “graver question” will emerge about why Trump didn’t act on information and instead continued to keep Huang close, CNN said. Instead of distancing himself from the Nvidia CEO, Trump invited Huang to sit at the head table during a state dinner with China’s president Xi Jinping, and last month, Trump awarded Huang the National Medal of Science, the nation’s highest scientific honor, for his AI achievements.

但CNN表示，如果特朗普是在推卸责任，那么一个更“严重的问题”将会出现：为什么特朗普没有根据情报采取行动，反而继续与黄仁勋保持密切关系？特朗普不仅没有与这位英伟达首席执行官保持距离，反而邀请黄仁勋在与中国国家主席习近平举行的国宴上坐在主桌，并且上个月，特朗普还因黄仁勋在人工智能领域的成就，授予了他美国最高科学荣誉——国家科学奖章。

“Not only has he continued to do nothing, he’s invited that very CEO into the White House and on state visits to China with him,” Sacha Haworth, TOP’s founder and CEO, told Ars. “And the question we all have to ask is ‘why on Earth would he do that?’”

“他不仅继续无所作为，还邀请这位首席执行官进入白宫，并随他一起对中国进行国事访问，”TOP的创始人兼首席执行官萨莎·霍沃斯（Sacha Haworth）告诉Ars，“我们所有人都必须问的问题是，‘他到底为什么要这样做？’”

Trump officials accused of “corruption” What Trump and Nvidia knew—and when—have become central questions for some Republicans, as well as top Democrats who plan to investigate the scandal when they assume control of key committees in Congress next year. Andrew Yoon, head of research for a nonprofit called CivAI, which warns the public of dangers from AI, told Ars that Huang doesn’t need to be compromised by China to have endangered the US. Prioritizing boosting profits over national security alone is “pretty concerning.”

特朗普官员被指控“腐败”。特朗普和英伟达知道什么、何时知道，已成为一些共和党人以及计划在明年接管国会关键委员会后调查此丑闻的民主党高层关注的核心问题。警告公众防范人工智能危险的非营利组织CivAI的研究主管安德鲁·尹（Andrew Yoon）告诉Ars，黄仁勋并不需要被中国收买才能危及美国。仅仅将提高利润置于国家安全之上就“非常令人担忧”。

“I think the most notable thing here is that Jensen Huang has been at the absolute forefront of lobbying to be able to sell chips to China,” Yoon said. “He has leveraged his very deep relationships with government, especially President Trump, in order to be able to sell as many Nvidia chips to China as he can get away with. If he could be selling Blackwell chips to China, I’m sure he would be.”

“我认为这里最值得注意的一点是，黄仁勋一直处于游说向中国出售芯片的最前沿，”尹说，“他利用自己与政府，特别是与特朗普总统的深厚关系，尽可能多地向中国出售英伟达芯片。如果他能向中国出售Blackwell芯片，我相信他一定会这么做。”

Yoon noted that evidence suggests some Chinese AI models have been trained on Blackwell chips. Even with China’s push to prioritize domestic chipmakers like Huawei, Nvidia chips remain “indispensable” to China’s frontier AI goals, Yoon said. And national security experts fear that access to high-end chips will help China advance its military AI or gain economic ground.

尹指出，有证据表明一些中国人工智能模型是在Blackwell芯片上训练的。尹表示，即使中国推动优先考虑华为等国内芯片制造商，英伟达芯片对中国的尖端人工智能目标仍然“不可或缺”。国家安全专家担心，获得高端芯片将帮助中国推进其军事人工智能或在经济上取得优势。

The White House isn’t commenting on Trump’s relationship with Huang. Among the lawmakers accusing the Trump administration of “corruption” is Sen. Richard Blumenthal (D-Conn.), The Wall Street Journal reported. He has already asked the White House to “preserve all communications” between Trump and Huang. Other lawmakers are “seeking more information about what Trump knew about the relationship between Jensen Huang and Alice Huang,” the former Megaspeed CEO whom Huang admitted to having an affair with.

白宫没有对特朗普与黄仁勋的关系发表评论。据《华尔街日报》报道，指责特朗普政府“腐败”的议员包括参议员理查德·布卢门撒尔（Richard Blumenthal，民主党，康涅狄格州）。他已要求白宫“保留特朗普与黄仁勋之间的所有通讯记录”。其他议员正在“寻求更多信息，了解特朗普对黄仁勋与爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang）之间关系的了解程度”，爱丽丝·黄是前Megaspeed首席执行官，黄仁勋已承认与她有染。

They also want to know more about “the administration’s failure to stop artificial-intelligence chips from going to China,” WSJ reported, and Nvidia’s reportedly $25 million donation to the White House ballroom. “It is not just an intelligence issue; it’s also a potential corruption issue,” Blumenthal said. “There needs to be a really penetrating, comprehensive investigation of all the possible compromises of our intelligence that may have occurred, but also… that Donald Trump is so high on the head of Nvidia, and now is potentially linked to intelligence compromises.”

据《华尔街日报》报道，他们还想了解更多关于“政府未能阻止人工智能芯片流向中国”的情况，以及英伟达据称向白宫宴会厅捐赠的2500万美元。“这不仅仅是一个情报问题，也是一个潜在的腐败问题，”布卢门撒尔说，“需要对所有可能发生的情报泄露进行深入、全面的调查，而且……唐纳德·特朗普如此推崇英伟达的负责人，现在又可能与情报泄露有关。”

Trump officials profit off Nvidia policies Trump has profited off Nvidia’s success while in office, buying “millions of dollars worth of Nvidia shares in his personal brokerage accounts since returning to office in 2025,” the WSJ reported. The White House maintains that the president has no role in managing his stock portfolio. Other officials advising Trump on AI also benefited from relaxing export curbs, TOP said.

特朗普官员从英伟达政策中获利。据《华尔街日报》报道，特朗普在任期间从英伟达的成功中获利，自2025年重返办公室以来，他在个人经纪账户中购买了“价值数百万美元的英伟达股票”。白宫坚称总统不参与管理其股票投资组合。TOP表示，其他为特朗普提供人工智能建议的官员也从放宽出口限制中获益。

On Tuesday, the group issued a report summarizing what the public knows about potential conflicts of interest of two Trump officials: former White House AI Czar David Sacks and Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick. Haworth, TOP’s founder, told Ars she’s concerned that Sacks and Lutnick abused their power to convince Trump to let Nvidia sell cutting-edge technology to China, while standing to “gain astronomical amounts of money.”

周二，该组织发布了一份报告，总结了公众所知的两名特朗普官员的潜在利益冲突：前白宫人工智能沙皇大卫·萨克斯（David Sacks）和商务部长霍华德·卢特尼克（Howard Lutnick）。TOP创始人霍沃斯告诉Ars，她担心萨克斯和卢特尼克滥用职权说服特朗普允许英伟达向中国出售尖端技术，同时他们自己也可能“获得天文数字的收益”。

“The public is outraged by Nvidia’s CEO for compromising our national security, but we didn’t get here because of Jensen Huang’s missteps alone,” Haworth said. If “Nvidia was knowingly doing business with companies tied to illegal chip sales and smuggling with C

“公众对英伟达首席执行官损害我们国家安全的行为感到愤怒，但我们走到这一步不仅仅是因为黄仁勋的失误，”霍沃斯说。如果“英伟达明知故犯，与那些涉及非法芯片销售和走私的公司进行交易……”
