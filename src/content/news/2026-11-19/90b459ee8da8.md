Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.21766 (cs) [Submitted on 16 Nov 2026] Title: Margin Dynamics for Large Language Model Alignment Authors: Xingzi Xu, Mehmet Saygin Seyfioglu, Karim Bouyarmane.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.21766 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 16 日] 标题：大语言模型对齐的边界动力学 作者：Xingzi Xu, Mehmet Saygin Seyfioglu, Karim Bouyarmane。

Abstract: Practitioners choose among LLM alignment methods largely by trial and error. We provide a theoretical foundation: each pairwise method defines a vector field on the probability simplex that governs how the model redistributes mass between preferred and dispreferred completions.

摘要：从业者在选择大语言模型（LLM）对齐方法时，很大程度上依赖于试错法。我们提供了一个理论基础：每种成对方法都在概率单纯形上定义了一个向量场，该向量场控制着模型如何在偏好和非偏好的补全结果之间重新分配概率质量。

Projecting onto a single preferred/dispreferred pair reduces this to a one-dimensional margin ODE with a closed-form solution. These solutions reveal that DPO margins grow without bound (logarithmically as $O(\log t)$ in the gradient-flow limit) while IPO margins converge to a finite $1/(2\tau)$.

通过投影到单个偏好/非偏好对，可以将此问题简化为一个具有闭式解的一维边界常微分方程（ODE）。这些解表明，DPO 边界无限制地增长（在梯度流极限下呈对数增长 $O(\log t)$），而 IPO 边界则收敛于有限值 $1/(2\tau)$。

That label noise induces a qualitative phase transition in DPO (unbounded $\to$ bounded) but affects IPO only quantitatively; and that gradient concentration under asymmetric weighting governs trainability at scale. We confirm all three predictions across five model families spanning four benchmarks.

标签噪声在 DPO 中引起了定性的相变（从无界变为有界），但对 IPO 的影响仅限于定量层面；此外，非对称加权下的梯度集中度决定了在大规模训练下的可训练性。我们在涵盖四个基准测试的五个模型系列中证实了这三个预测。

The closed-form solutions also enable principled method design: we prove that adding any label smoothing $\varepsilon > 0$ to SimPO creates a finite margin attractor at $\Delta^* = \beta^{-1}\log((1-\varepsilon)/\varepsilon)$, while $\varepsilon = 0$ (standard SimPO) has no attractor.

这些闭式解也使得原则性的方法设计成为可能：我们证明了在 SimPO 中添加任何标签平滑 $\varepsilon > 0$ 都会在 $\Delta^* = \beta^{-1}\log((1-\varepsilon)/\varepsilon)$ 处创建一个有限边界吸引子，而 $\varepsilon = 0$（标准 SimPO）则没有吸引子。

We test this across three models (8B-9B) and confirm the prediction: the theory-derived configuration (TPO-S, $\varepsilon = 0.05$) is competitive with the strongest baselines, attaining the highest mean IFEval score among the compared methods on all three, validating that the predicted finite attractor is a usable operating point rather than proposing a new state-of-the-art loss.

我们在三个模型（8B-9B）上进行了测试并证实了该预测：理论导出的配置（TPO-S, $\varepsilon = 0.05$）与最强的基准方法具有竞争力，在所有三个模型上均获得了比较方法中最高的平均 IFEval 分数，这验证了所预测的有限吸引子是一个可用的操作点，而非提出一种新的最先进损失函数。
