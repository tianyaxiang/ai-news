Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.21680 (cs) [Submitted on 15 Nov 2026] Title: Edge-Adaptive Hiera-Seg Model for Building-Integrated Photovoltaic Power Forecasting Authors: Na Yu.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.21680 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 15 日] 标题：用于建筑集成光伏发电预测的边缘自适应 Hiera-Seg 模型，作者：Na Yu。

Abstract: Building integrated photovoltaic power forecasting is challenging because facade orientation, rooftop geometry, nearby buildings, vegetation, cloud movement, module temperature, inverter status and edge device reliability jointly affect power generation. This paper proposes an edge-adaptive HieraSeg model for short term BIPV power forecasting with multi-orientation visual-irradiance fusion.

摘要：建筑集成光伏（BIPV）发电预测极具挑战性，因为立面朝向、屋顶几何形状、周边建筑、植被、云层移动、组件温度、逆变器状态以及边缘设备的可靠性共同影响着发电量。本文提出了一种边缘自适应 HieraSeg 模型，用于结合多朝向视觉与辐照度融合的短期 BIPV 发电预测。

RGB facade images, optional thermal cues, irradiance measurements, module temperature, facade orientation, rooftop masks, shading labels, sky condition, historical power and edge device state are aligned into synchronous forecasting windows. HieraSeg extracts hierarchical regions from facade, rooftop, sky, shadow and background images, while the edge-adaptive module changes feature weights under missing frames, latency shifts and local shading changes.

RGB 立面图像、可选的热感提示、辐照度测量值、组件温度、立面朝向、屋顶掩码、遮挡标签、天空状况、历史功率和边缘设备状态被对齐到同步预测窗口中。HieraSeg 从立面、屋顶、天空、阴影和背景图像中提取分层区域，而边缘自适应模块则在出现丢帧、延迟偏移和局部遮挡变化时调整特征权重。

The segmentation branch identifies occluded module areas and separates facade, rooftop, sky and background evidence before the forecasting head estimates future BIPV output. Experiments are designed on a simulated multibuilding BIPV dataset with unseen building and unseen orientation tests. The model reports predicted power, confidence interval, dominant evidence, occlusion contribution, orientation contribution and energy-management review priority.

分割分支在预测头估计未来 BIPV 输出之前，识别被遮挡的组件区域并分离立面、屋顶、天空和背景证据。实验是在一个模拟的多建筑 BIPV 数据集上设计的，并进行了未见建筑和未见朝向的测试。该模型报告预测功率、置信区间、主导证据、遮挡贡献、朝向贡献以及能源管理审查优先级。

This output is intended to show whether the forecast is driven by irradiance change, visible shading, orientation response, stale edge information or recent power history. The framework supports local forecasting at edge controllers with limited communication delay and computing resources, linking visual occlusion, irradiance dynamics and power history to traceable forecasting results for building energy management, microgrid scheduling and BIPV operation planning.

该输出旨在展示预测是由辐照度变化、可见遮挡、朝向响应、陈旧的边缘信息还是近期功率历史所驱动。该框架支持在通信延迟和计算资源有限的边缘控制器上进行本地预测，将视觉遮挡、辐照度动态和功率历史与可追溯的预测结果联系起来，用于建筑能源管理、微电网调度和 BIPV 运行规划。
