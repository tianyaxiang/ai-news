title: "Chain-of-Thought Controllability Can Reduce Chain-of-Thought Monitorability"
originalUrl: "https://arxiv.org/abs/2611.21799"

---

Chain-of-thought (CoT) monitoring is a promising tool for detecting hidden goals, off-task behavior, and other failures in AI agents. One risk is that models could deliberately shape their reasoning to make it opaque to monitors. We stress-test this risk through persona-control training: supervised fine-tuning on mathematical reasoning traces rewritten in the voices of diverse personas.

思维链（CoT）监控是检测人工智能代理中隐藏目标、偏离任务行为及其他故障的一种有前景的工具。一个风险是模型可能会刻意塑造其推理过程，使其对监控器而言变得不透明。我们通过角色控制训练对这一风险进行了压力测试：即在以不同角色口吻重写的数学推理轨迹上进行监督微调。

This induces a broader ability to control CoT, even though training contains no monitor-evasion examples. In covert side-task evaluations, traces in which a gpt-oss-20b model organism completes the hidden side task contain less monitorable evidence than the base model's. Averaged across evaluations and CoT lengths, weighted by side-task-success counts, the CoT monitorability metric g-mean$^2$ falls from 0.94 for the base model to 0.68 for the model organism without a hiding instruction, and to 0.22 when explicitly instructed to hide.

这诱导产生了一种更广泛的控制思维链的能力，尽管训练中并不包含任何规避监控的示例。在隐蔽的侧任务评估中，gpt-oss-20b 模型有机体完成隐藏侧任务的轨迹所包含的可监控证据，少于基础模型。在各项评估和思维链长度的平均值中，经侧任务成功计数加权后，思维链可监控性指标 g-mean$^2$ 从基础模型的 0.94 下降到未接收隐藏指令的模型有机体的 0.68，而在明确指示隐藏时则降至 0.22。

In intervention evaluations, explicit hiding often drives monitorability near zero. The effect extends to a Kimi-K2.6 model organism in agentic environments, though hidden objectives requiring extended reasoning or longer trajectories remain more monitorable than simple single-step side tasks. We further discover that targeted transparency prompts can substantially recover monitorability, sometimes surpassing the base model. These model organisms provide a testbed for studying monitorability failures and potential mitigations.

在干预评估中，明确的隐藏行为通常会将可监控性推向接近于零的水平。这种效应延伸到了代理环境中的 Kimi-K2.6 模型有机体，尽管需要扩展推理或更长轨迹的隐藏目标，相比简单的单步侧任务仍然更具可监控性。我们进一步发现，针对性的透明度提示可以大幅恢复可监控性，有时甚至超过基础模型。这些模型有机体为研究可监控性故障及潜在的缓解措施提供了一个测试平台。
