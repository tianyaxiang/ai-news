Abstract:Compressing 3D shapes is important for the efficiency of many applications, yet existing methods either cannot leverage dataset-level statistics or use a fixed compression ratio for all shapes. We propose a learned variable-length compression method that exploits structure across a training dataset while adapting compressed representation size to individual shape complexity using only fast feed-forward inference. Our method operates on general latent representations and improves rate-distortion for both meshes and signed distance fields. Code is available at this https URL.

摘要：压缩 3D 形状对于许多应用的效率至关重要，然而现有方法要么无法利用数据集层面的统计信息，要么对所有形状使用固定的压缩比。我们提出了一种学习型变长压缩方法，该方法在利用训练数据集结构的同时，仅通过快速前馈推理即可根据单个形状的复杂性调整压缩表示的大小。我们的方法适用于通用潜在表示，并改善了网格和符号距离场的速率-失真性能。代码可在该 HTTPS 链接获取。
