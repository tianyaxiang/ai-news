# Does Nvidia Got This? Interim CEO Colette Kress Says It’s Got This

Nvidia is going through it. The chipmaker’s iconic leader just resigned due to an international scandal, its stock has taken a hit, and it’s in a precarious position as it tries to maintain its dominance in the AI era.

英伟达正经历着动荡。这家芯片制造商的标志性领导人因一起国际丑闻刚刚辞职，其股价受到冲击，在试图维持其人工智能时代的主导地位时，公司正处于岌岌可危的境地。

Following Jensen Huang’s ouster, the company’s interim CEO and still-CFO, Colette Kress, has been tasked with stabilizing the deck. So far, Kress’ public-facing duties have included running the company’s third-quarter earnings call and hosting an all-hands meeting for Nvidia’s money-hungry workforce. And as part of her calming campaign, Kress has also been deploying a catchphrase that sounds like it was taken straight from the draft files of a summer of ’24 Democratic campaign strategist: “We’ve got this.”

在黄仁勋被免职后，公司临时首席执行官兼现任首席财务官科莱特·克雷斯（Colette Kress）被委以稳定局势的重任。到目前为止，克雷斯的公开职责包括主持公司的第三季度财报电话会议，以及为英伟达渴望金钱的员工主持全员大会。作为她安抚行动的一部分，克雷斯还一直在使用一句口号，听起来就像是直接从2024年夏季民主党竞选策略师的草稿文件中摘录出来的：“我们能搞定（We’ve got this）。”

By WIRED’s count, Kress has publicly used the phrase three times in the past 24 hours, often putting emphasis or a little bit of zhuzh in it:

据《连线》（WIRED）统计，克雷斯在过去24小时内公开使用了三次这句话，通常还会加上重音或进行一点润色：

Tuesday’s earnings call: “Our momentum is real. Our demand is accelerating. Our supply is growing. Our customers and our partners are with us, and employees are committed. We’ve got this.”

周二的财报电话会议：“我们的势头是真实的。我们的需求正在加速。我们的供应正在增长。我们的客户和合作伙伴与我们站在一起，员工们也充满干劲。我们能搞定。”

Wednesday’s all-hands meeting: “Our ambition will not change. Our passion to innovate will not change. Our commitment to customers, partners, and shareholders will not change. We thank you all for the great quarter that we had. Just remember, we’ve got this.”

周三的全员大会：“我们的雄心不会改变。我们创新的热情不会改变。我们对客户、合作伙伴和股东的承诺不会改变。感谢大家度过了一个伟大的季度。请记住，我们能搞定。”

Wednesday’s all-hands meeting, again: Kress ended Wednesday’s meeting by comparing Nvidia’s employees to “90,000 people packed into the sweatiest, hottest” University of Florida football game, all singing together. “It is impressive to see them all together, all in harmony singing that song … Do you know what? We’re gonna hear that song, guys. And I believe in all of you that you will help sing it. But Nvidia, we’ve got this.”

周三的全员大会（续）：克雷斯在周三会议结束时，将英伟达的员工比作“90,000人挤在最闷热、最火爆的”佛罗里达大学橄榄球赛场上，大家齐声歌唱。“看到他们聚在一起，和谐地唱着那首歌，真是令人印象深刻……你知道吗？伙计们，我们会听到那首歌的。我相信你们所有人都会参与合唱。但是英伟达，我们能搞定。”

Kress’ new catchphrase perhaps isn’t the most inspiring; companies that got this usually don’t have to remind people how much they got this twice in one meeting. It’s a collectivist’s version of “You got this,” a slightly less corny version of “Yes we can,” a saying that may well pose more questions than it answers. (We got … what?)

克雷斯的新口号或许并不是最鼓舞人心的；真正“搞得定”的公司通常不需要在一次会议中两次提醒人们他们有多“搞得定”。这是“你能行（You got this）”的集体主义版本，是“是的，我们能（Yes we can）”稍微不那么老套的版本，这句口号可能带来的疑问比它回答的问题还要多。（我们搞定……什么？）

Nvidia investors, or any investor, might not have fist-pumped and said “We’ve got this!” during a quick portfolio check these past 10 days. Anyone who has had the slightest hint of knowledge that Nvidia’s chips were finding their way into China is unlikely to be in a “We totally got this” mood at the moment.

英伟达的投资者，或者任何投资者，在过去10天里快速查看投资组合时，可能都不会挥拳高呼“我们能搞定！”。任何稍微了解英伟达芯片正流入中国市场的人，此刻都不太可能处于“我们完全搞得定”的心态中。

Still, some Nvidia employees appear to be inspired by Kress’ mantra. “Damn right we got this,” Aaron Erickson, Nvidia’s head of NemoClaw engineering, posted on X on Wednesday. Vega Shah, who works in portfolio management for Nvidia’s venture capital arm, shared Erickson’s post and echoed the phrase, adding her own rocket ship emoji to the post.

尽管如此，一些英伟达员工似乎受到了克雷斯口号的鼓舞。英伟达NemoClaw工程主管亚伦·埃里克森（Aaron Erickson）周三在X上发帖称：“没错，我们能搞定。”在英伟达风险投资部门从事投资组合管理的维加·沙阿（Vega Shah）转发了埃里克森的帖子并附和了这句话，还加上了她自己的火箭表情符号。

And yesterday, Nvidia board chair Harvey Jones said the board did not intend to reinstate Huang, despite some calls on social media and within the company to put Huang back in the top spot. Which means that Kress, for the moment, has … got this.

昨天，英伟达董事会主席哈维·琼斯（Harvey Jones）表示，尽管社交媒体和公司内部有一些要求让黄仁勋重回最高职位的呼声，但董事会无意恢复黄仁勋的职务。这意味着，目前为止，克雷斯……确实“搞定了”。
