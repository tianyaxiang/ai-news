Title: CrisisBench: an evaluation suite for crisis counseling AI

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.21622 (cs) [2026年9月11日提交] 标题：CrisisBench：一个用于危机咨询人工智能的评估套件 作者：Akshay Swaminathan, Filip Ramazan, Sharang Phadke, Kevina Wang, Ivan Lopez, Shaked Peleg Azzam, Gloria Ye, Chastin Chung, William Wang, Stephanie Stoll, Ivy Pham, Rebecca Hurwitz, Shreya Shah, Samuel Chuang, Divyanjali Verma, Abhay John, Ehsan Adeli, Nigam H. Shah

Abstract: Background: Crisis helplines are essential to mental health systems but are often understaffed and underfunded. LLMs could support counselors, but existing benchmarks do not capture the escalation, risk assessment, and de-escalation central to crisis conversations, while privacy restrictions limit use of real transcripts.

摘要：背景：危机求助热线对心理健康系统至关重要，但往往人手不足且资金匮乏。大语言模型（LLM）可以为咨询师提供支持，但现有的基准测试无法捕捉危机对话中核心的升级、风险评估和降级过程，同时隐私限制也限制了真实对话记录的使用。

Methods: We present CrisisBench, a privacy-preserving suite for single- and multi-turn crisis counseling evaluation. It includes 1,085 single-turn tasks from 200 de-identified conversations and 1,185 patient LLMs derived from hotline metadata. Patient models and a 23-item LLM judge were calibrated against expert counselors.

方法：我们提出了 CrisisBench，这是一个用于单轮和多轮危机咨询评估的隐私保护套件。它包含来自200个去标识化对话的1,085个单轮任务，以及源自热线元数据的1,185个患者大语言模型。患者模型和一个包含23个项目的LLM评判员均根据专家咨询师进行了校准。

Results: We evaluated five GPT models plus draft-then-edit and retrieval-augmented generation. All systems underperformed human counselors on excessive empathy and questioning in single-turn evaluation. The rubric achieved human-human Krippendorff's $\alpha$ = 0.51 and LLM-human $\alpha$ = 0.54 (Pearson r = 0.80).

结果：我们评估了五个GPT模型以及“先草拟后编辑”和“检索增强生成”方法。在单轮评估中，所有系统在过度共情和提问方面均表现不如人类咨询师。该评分标准实现了人与人之间 Krippendorff's $\alpha$ = 0.51 以及 LLM 与人之间 $\alpha$ = 0.54（皮尔逊相关系数 r = 0.80）的一致性。

In multi-turn evaluation, retrieval-augmented generation scored highest overall (0.961), with all systems within 0.058. Microskills and style/tone were near ceiling, while productivity and safety/risk accounted for most between-system variation. Counselors rated patient LLMs realistic (86%) and useful (92%).

在多轮评估中，检索增强生成方法的总分最高（0.961），所有系统的得分差距在0.058以内。微技能和风格/语调接近上限，而生产力和安全性/风险则构成了系统间差异的主要来源。咨询师认为患者大语言模型表现真实（86%）且有用（92%）。

Conclusion: CrisisBench enables scalable, privacy-preserving evaluation of crisis counseling. Current systems perform strongly on core structural competencies but still diverge from experts on important response-level failure modes. We release the rubrics, judge prompts, and evaluation tasks.

结论：CrisisBench 实现了可扩展且保护隐私的危机咨询评估。目前的系统在核心结构能力上表现强劲，但在重要的响应级故障模式上仍与专家存在差异。我们发布了评分标准、评判员提示词和评估任务。
