# Inside Deutsche Bank

A major global investigation revealing the inner workings of the financial giant. The leak of 15+ million files lays bare the bank's flawed culture, systemic blind spots and pursuit of profit over compliance.

一项重大的全球调查揭示了这家金融巨头的内部运作。超过 1500 万份文件的泄露，暴露了该银行存在缺陷的企业文化、系统性盲点以及重利润轻合规的经营方针。

The Inside Deutsche Bank files span some of the bank’s most sensitive functions and activities: anti-financial crime, sanctions monitoring, internal auditing and the units responsible for deciding whether risky clients stay or go.

“深入德意志银行”（Inside Deutsche Bank）文件涵盖了该银行一些最敏感的职能和活动：反金融犯罪、制裁监控、内部审计，以及负责决定是否保留高风险客户的部门。

Leaked documents reveal how dealings with royal dynasty blurred traditional boundaries between investor, client and counterparty.

泄露的文件揭示了与皇室王朝的交易如何模糊了投资者、客户和交易对手之间的传统界限。
