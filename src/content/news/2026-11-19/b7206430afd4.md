# AI is killing All About Berlin

Psst! There is an update to this post.
嘘！这篇文章有更新。

This is the traffic to All About Berlin, the website that I run since 2017, and live from since 2020. After 7 years of growth, it has lost 70% 75% of its traffic to Google AI Overviews.
这是 All About Berlin 的流量情况，这个网站我从 2017 年开始运营，并自 2020 年起以此为生。在经历了 7 年的增长后，由于谷歌的 AI 概览（AI Overviews），它已经损失了 70% 到 75% 的流量。

When you Googled something, you used to get a link to my website. Now you get a page of ads, then an AI-generated answer trained on my content, then finally a link to my website. This has a devastating impact on traffic.
过去当你用谷歌搜索时，你会得到指向我网站的链接。现在你看到的是一整页广告，接着是基于我的内容训练出来的 AI 生成答案，最后才是一个指向我网站的链接。这对流量造成了毁灭性的影响。

It’s hard to fund my work with only a quarter of my audience. Soon, I will need another way to pay my rent. Instead of writing new guides, I already spend my days preparing for that future.
仅靠四分之一的受众很难维持我的工作。很快，我就需要寻找其他方式来支付房租了。与其撰写新的指南，我现在的日子都花在为那个未来做准备上。

It’s not just about the money. I have poured 8 years of my life into making something good and useful. Strangers’ faces light up when they learn that I run All About Berlin. Local and federal government offices refer people to my guides. The Berlin immigration office uses my data about themselves. It inspired two government portals and a few similar websites in other cities.
这不仅仅是钱的问题。我投入了 8 年的生命去创造一些有益且有用的东西。当陌生人得知我运营着 All About Berlin 时，他们的脸上会露出光彩。地方和联邦政府办公室会将人们引向我的指南。柏林移民局甚至使用我关于他们自己的数据。它还启发了两个政府门户网站以及其他城市的一些类似网站。

If my work just get mixed into an AI-generated answer, I lose my voice and my audience. My carefully chosen words lose their meaning. No one shakes my hand and buys me a beer. No one tells me how their citizenship application went.
如果我的作品只是被混入 AI 生成的答案中，我就失去了自己的声音和受众。我精心挑选的文字失去了意义。没有人会再与我握手并请我喝啤酒，也没有人会再告诉我他们的公民申请进展如何。

This is not an isolated case. Many website owners have reached out in private to commiserate. Some have paywalled their work. Others have given up and moved on. They keep the websites online, but they no longer update them. It’s just not worth it.
这并非个例。许多网站所有者私下联系我表示同情。有些人对他们的作品设置了付费墙。另一些人则选择了放弃并转行。他们虽然保持网站在线，但不再进行更新。这根本不值得。

It will get worse. Last week, Google has made two announcements: AI-generated answers will replace search results entirely, and they have ads in them. This is the future of the internet. No websites, just a chat box with ads.
情况还会恶化。上周，谷歌发布了两项公告：AI 生成的答案将完全取代搜索结果，并且其中会包含广告。这就是互联网的未来。没有网站，只有一个带有广告的聊天框。

I don’t know where All About Berlin fits in that future.
我不知道 All About Berlin 在那个未来中还有什么位置。

这个话题也在 Mastodon、Hacker News、LinkedIn 和 Twitter 上进行了讨论。如果你也受到了影响，请联系我。让我知道你的近况。你并不是独自面对这一切。
