Layerwise learning-rate scaling assigns different learning rates to different parts of a network. Although established in fine-tuning pretrained models, its effects in vision networks trained from scratch are commonly assessed by training each candidate recipe.

分层学习率缩放为网络的不同部分分配不同的学习率。尽管在微调预训练模型中已确立，但在从头开始训练的视觉网络中，其效果通常通过训练每个候选方案来评估。

We show that, in this setting, the rate-related component of a layerwise strategy is well summarized by an effective learning rate computable directly from its multiplier profile. Across matched-control comparisons, changes in test accuracy closely track changes in training loss (r = -0.996), which supports an optimization-progress explanation of the short-budget rate effect.

我们证明，在这种设置下，分层策略中与速率相关的部分可以通过直接从其乘数配置文件计算出的有效学习率来很好地概括。在匹配对照比较中，测试准确率的变化与训练损失的变化密切相关（r = -0.996），这支持了对短预算速率效应的优化进度解释。

Increasing the untuned base learning rate tenfold shifts the rate-matched contrast upward in all six tested architectures (p = 0.016) and increases the number of architectures in which a published discriminative schedule, excluded from model fitting, outperforms the uniform control from two to five of six (p = 0.031).

将未调整的基础学习率提高十倍，在所有六种测试架构中都使速率匹配的对比度向上移动（p = 0.016），并增加了在模型拟合之外发布的判别式调度优于统一对照的架构数量，从六个中的两个增加到五个（p = 0.031）。

Within the evaluated strategy space, a parameter-weighted power mean with exponent 0.35 (95% confidence interval [0.20, 0.50]) accounts for 42.6% of within-architecture variance, compared with 36.4% for the arithmetic mean. Ranking candidates by this scalar without training improves the selected candidate's mean percentile rank from the 26th to the 10th and reduces its mean accuracy shortfall relative to the best candidate from 3.70 to 1.12 points.

在评估的策略空间内，指数为 0.35（95% 置信区间 [0.20, 0.50]）的参数加权幂平均值解释了 42.6% 的架构内方差，而算术平均值为 36.4%。在不进行训练的情况下，通过该标量对候选者进行排名，可将所选候选者的平均百分位排名从第 26 位提高到第 10 位，并将其相对于最佳候选者的平均准确率差距从 3.70 个百分点缩小到 1.12 个百分点。

The rate-related effect decays as the training budget increases; relative to tuned baselines, the overall strategy effect falls within the prespecified practical-equivalence margin in two of three architectures. A separate grouping-related contrast can persist at long budgets, although its direction and magnitude depend on the architecture.

随着训练预算的增加，与速率相关的效应会减弱；相对于调整后的基线，整体策略效应在三分之二的架构中落入预先指定的等效性范围内。一个单独的与分组相关的对比在长预算下可能会持续存在，尽管其方向和幅度取决于架构。
