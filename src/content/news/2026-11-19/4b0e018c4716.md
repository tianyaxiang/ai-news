Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.21832 (cs) [Submitted on 16 Nov 2026] Title: Auditors Struggle to Catch Sabotage In End-to-End Transcripts of AI Safety Research Authors: Keshav Shenoy, Anthony Zang, Eric Gan, Jacqueline Tay, Amy Ngo, Sebastian Prasanna, Julian Stastny, Vivek Hebbar.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.21832 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 16 日] 标题：审计人员难以发现人工智能安全研究端到端记录中的破坏行为 作者：Keshav Shenoy, Anthony Zang, Eric Gan, Jacqueline Tay, Amy Ngo, Sebastian Prasanna, Julian Stastny, Vivek Hebbar。

Abstract: As AI systems are increasingly used to automate AI R&D, misaligned systems could sabotage research intended to improve model safety and alignment. To stress-test defenses against such sabotage, we use a human expert as a proxy saboteur on AI safety research. We conduct a red-team/blue-team game where a human researcher runs AI safety experiments from scratch and auditors try to tell which were sabotaged and how.

摘要：随着人工智能系统越来越多地被用于自动化人工智能研发，失调的系统可能会破坏旨在提高模型安全性和对齐性的研究。为了压力测试针对此类破坏的防御措施，我们使用人类专家作为人工智能安全研究中的代理破坏者。我们进行了一场红队/蓝队博弈，其中人类研究员从零开始运行人工智能安全实验，而审计人员则试图辨别哪些实验遭到了破坏以及破坏方式。

Given access to the full research transcript and the ability to run experiments, human auditors mostly fail to distinguish sabotaged from benign research: they achieve an AUROC of 0.59, not significantly better than chance, and fully identify the specific sabotage in only 17% of cases. Despite not being allowed to run experiments, the best frontier models outperform human auditors (Fable 5.1: AUROC 0.73, 32% full identification) within noise and weaker models perform comparably.

在获得完整研究记录并有权运行实验的情况下，人类审计人员大多无法区分被破坏的研究和良性研究：他们的 AUROC 为 0.59，并不比随机猜测好多少，且仅在 17% 的案例中完全识别出了具体的破坏行为。尽管不被允许运行实验，但最优秀的前沿模型在误差范围内优于人类审计人员（Fable 5.1：AUROC 0.73，32% 的完全识别率），而较弱的模型表现相当。

When the saboteur chooses which experiments are attacked, rather than being required to sabotage every question given, sabotage identification drops further. Our results suggest that research sabotage by expert-level agents is difficult to detect with current auditing methods and provide a new setting for studying research sabotage.

当破坏者可以选择攻击哪些实验，而不是被要求破坏给出的每一个问题时，破坏识别率会进一步下降。我们的结果表明，专家级智能体进行的科研破坏很难通过当前的审计方法检测出来，并为研究科研破坏提供了一个新的环境。
