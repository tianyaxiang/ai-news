The Gemini app is now available on lower-memory Android devices. Angus Logan Builder, Gemini App Nov 18, 2026 For hundreds of millions of people globally, a lower-memory Android device is their everyday connection to the internet, education, and business.

Gemini 应用程序现已在内存较低的 Android 设备上推出。Angus Logan Builder，Gemini App，2026 年 11 月 18 日。对于全球数以亿计的用户来说，内存较低的 Android 设备是他们连接互联网、获取教育资源和处理日常事务的主要工具。

Until recently, hardware limits meant those phones only had basic voice commands. Now, we’re bringing the Gemini app to eligible Android (Go edition) devices, putting Google’s personal AI assistant in the hands of hundreds of millions more people.

直到最近，受限于硬件性能，这些手机仅能使用基本的语音指令。现在，我们将 Gemini 应用程序引入符合条件的 Android (Go edition) 设备，让数以亿计的更多用户能够使用谷歌的个人 AI 助手。

We re-architected the app so the core Gemini experience runs smoothly on hardware with less memory. Instead of typing out long prompts, you can talk things through or practice for an interview out loud in more than 45 languages with Gemini Live, point your camera at notes or forms to learn and translate, or create custom images with Nano Banana — like a flyer for a weekend bakery sale.

我们对该应用程序进行了重新架构，以确保 Gemini 的核心体验能够在内存较小的硬件上流畅运行。你无需输入冗长的提示词，可以通过 Gemini Live 用 45 种以上的语言进行口头交流或练习面试，将摄像头对准笔记或表格进行学习和翻译，或者使用 Nano Banana 创建自定义图像，例如制作一张周末烘焙义卖的传单。

To get started, download the Gemini app on Google Play, press and hold the power button on supported phones, or look for it preloaded on upcoming devices from partners like Transsion.

若要开始使用，请在 Google Play 下载 Gemini 应用程序，在受支持的手机上长按电源键，或者留意传音 (Transsion) 等合作伙伴即将推出的预装该应用的设备。
