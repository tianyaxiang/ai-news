International health authorities formally announced on Tuesday that the US has lost its measles elimination status, a hard-fought public health triumph won in 2000 after decades-long vaccination efforts. The loss had been widely expected by public health experts for months, and the decision was leaked by the press last week. Still, having the designation officially stripped stings.

国际卫生当局周二正式宣布，美国已失去麻疹消除状态。这是美国在经过数十年的疫苗接种努力后，于2000年取得的一项来之不易的公共卫生胜利。公共卫生专家几个月来一直普遍预料到这一结果，且该决定已于上周被媒体泄露。尽管如此，这一称号被正式剥夺仍然令人感到刺痛。

After the announcement Tuesday, public health experts, doctors, and political leaders decried the dramatic backslide for the US, calling it “shameful” and a “national embarrassment.” News articles point to the staggering vertical rise of measles cases this year, currently over 5,400 cases, more than any other year since 1991. An explosive measles outbreak in Pennsylvania accounts for over 1,500 of those cases. There have been 10 confirmed deaths across three states, several deaths just in the last week.

周二公告发布后，公共卫生专家、医生和政治领导人纷纷谴责美国出现的这种剧烈倒退，称其为“可耻”和“国家耻辱”。新闻报道指出，今年麻疹病例呈惊人的垂直上升趋势，目前已超过5400例，为1991年以来的最高值。宾夕法尼亚州爆发的麻疹疫情就占了其中1500多例。目前已有三个州确认了10例死亡病例，仅上周就发生了数起死亡事件。

But the measles data that caused the US to lose its status—the bits of it that are public at least—paint a darker picture of where the US is in its rematch with measles, the most contagious virus known on Earth. For one thing, almost none of the data from this year—the cases, the explosive Pennsylvania outbreak, the deaths—factored in to the loss. It was lost earlier. What’s happening now is a runaway epidemic.

然而，导致美国失去该地位的麻疹数据（至少是已公开的部分）描绘了一幅更阴暗的图景，反映了美国在与这种地球上已知传染性最强的病毒进行“重赛”时的处境。首先，今年几乎没有任何数据——包括病例、宾夕法尼亚州爆发的疫情以及死亡人数——被计入此次地位丧失的考量中。该地位在此之前就已经丧失了。现在发生的是一场失控的流行病。

The US measles elimination status is a designation granted by the Pan American Health Organization (PAHO), which is a regional division of the World Health Organization. Last week a committee of experts assembled by the PAHO, called the RVC, met to review measles data for countries across the region, including the US. (RVC is short for the Measles, Rubella and Congenital Rubella Syndrome Elimination Regional Monitoring and Re-Verification Commission.)

美国的麻疹消除状态是由泛美卫生组织（PAHO）授予的称号，该组织是世界卫生组织的一个区域分支机构。上周，由泛美卫生组织召集的一个专家委员会（称为RVC）开会审查了包括美国在内的该地区各国的麻疹数据。（RVC是“麻疹、风疹和先天性风疹综合征消除区域监测与再核查委员会”的缩写。）

For their review of the US data, the RVC heard from an independent committee of experts from the US, called the NSC, which presented measles data collected from January 2025 onward. (The NSC is short for the US National Sustainability Committee for the Elimination of Measles, Rubella, and Congenital Rubella Syndrome.) According to the PAHO, an elimination status is lost when epidemiological and laboratory data shows that a single viral lineage has spread continuously for at least 12 months within a country.

在审查美国数据时，RVC听取了来自美国的一个独立专家委员会（称为NSC）的报告，该委员会提交了自2025年1月起收集的麻疹数据。（NSC是“美国麻疹、风疹和先天性风疹综合征消除国家可持续性委员会”的缩写。）根据泛美卫生组织的说法，当流行病学和实验室数据表明单一病毒谱系在一个国家内持续传播至少12个月时，消除状态即告丧失。

At this point, the virus is considered to be reestablished or endemic. So, to withdraw the US elimination status, the RVC needed to see evidence that a single introduction of a measles virus led to a 12-month-long chain of cases, which requires a lot of data. The full set of data the NSC presented to the RVC is private for now. The US Centers for Disease Control and Prevention needs to review and approve such data for public release, which can be a lengthy process that has not yet occurred.

此时，该病毒被认为已重新建立或成为地方性流行病。因此，要撤销美国的消除状态，RVC需要看到证据，证明单一的麻疹病毒输入导致了长达12个月的病例链，这需要大量数据。NSC提交给RVC的全部数据目前仍是私密的。美国疾病控制与预防中心需要审查并批准这些数据才能公开发布，这可能是一个漫长的过程，目前尚未完成。

But Ars Technica spoke with the chair of the NSC, Noel Brewer, who tried to talk through the pieces of data in the NSC report to the PAHO that he could discuss—these are pieces that have already been made public at some point by others, such as by state health officials or researchers. Brewer said the NSC presented the PAHO with genetic data spanning January 2025 to January 2026, and also epidemiological data that extended to June 2026.

但Ars Technica采访了NSC主席诺埃尔·布鲁尔（Noel Brewer），他试图梳理NSC提交给泛美卫生组织报告中他可以讨论的数据片段——这些片段是此前已由州卫生官员或研究人员等公开过的信息。布鲁尔表示，NSC向泛美卫生组织提交了涵盖2025年1月至2026年1月期间的遗传数据，以及延伸至2026年6月的流行病学数据。

In a press briefing Tuesday, officials for the PAHO made clear that only some of the data presented by NSC factored into its assessment of the US elimination status. The RVC only looked at the reporting period between January 2025 and January 2026, which doesn’t include the massive outbreak ongoing in Pennsylvania, which began in April. “The number of cases in Pennsylvania did not actually contribute to the decision that was made by the Reverification Commission,” Rhonda Sealey-Thomas, PAHO’s assistant director, explicitly told reporters Tuesday.

在周二的新闻发布会上，泛美卫生组织的官员明确表示，NSC提交的数据中只有一部分被纳入了对美国消除状态的评估。RVC仅审查了2025年1月至2026年1月期间的报告，其中不包括4月份开始并在宾夕法尼亚州持续的大规模疫情。泛美卫生组织助理主任朗达·西利-托马斯（Rhonda Sealey-Thomas）周二明确告诉记者：“宾夕法尼亚州的病例数实际上并未对再核查委员会做出的决定产生影响。”

For 2025, the US reported 2,289 cases nationally. Still, there was no ambiguity about the US losing its status; “multiple lines of data show clearly that the US has sustained transmission of measles,” Brewer told Ars. Brewer, who is also the Gillings Distinguished Professor in Public Health at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill, confirmed that the beginning of the end of US elimination was January 20, 2025, in Texas—that date was included in the letter PAHO sent to the US informing it of the loss of elimination status.

2025年，美国全国报告了2289例病例。尽管如此，美国失去该地位已毫无疑问；布鲁尔告诉Ars：“多条数据线清楚地表明，美国存在持续的麻疹传播。”布鲁尔同时也是北卡罗来纳大学教堂山分校的吉林斯公共卫生杰出教授，他证实美国消除状态终结的起点是2025年1月20日的德克萨斯州——这一日期被包含在泛美卫生组织发给美国告知其失去消除状态的信函中。

That Texas outbreak was declared over on August 18, 2025, after 762 cases were confirmed in the state. But the virus carried on elsewhere and is still simmering. “Since January 20, 2025, the US, Canada, and Mexico have been passing measles cases back and forth across borders,” Brewer said, and the national data shows multiple, “genetically distinct” measles introductions into the US. “However, there is one genomic lineage that predominates across the cases and ties many of them, the majority of them, together,” he said.

那场德克萨斯州的疫情在确认了762例病例后，于2025年8月18日宣布结束。但病毒在其他地方继续传播，且仍在酝酿之中。布鲁尔说：“自2025年1月20日以来，美国、加拿大和墨西哥一直在边境之间相互传播麻疹病例。”国家数据显示，有多种“遗传上不同”的麻疹病毒输入美国。他说：“然而，有一个基因组谱系在这些病例中占主导地位，并将其中许多（大部分）病例联系在了一起。”

For people closely tracking the US measles status, a key question has been whether the big outbreaks that followed the initial one in Texas were clearly linked. If they were, it would be immediately obvious that the US had lost the elimination status; but if not, and the outbreaks were driven by different virus introductions, the US could have kept its measles-free status based on the technicality of how the status is defined. Data that might hold the answer to this question has been elusive. The measles strain in Texas, D8-9171, appeared similar to the viruses seen subsequently in big outbreaks in Arizona and Utah. But the strain was also similar to those from Mexico and Canada. 对于密切关注美国麻疹状况的人来说，一个关键问题是，德克萨斯州最初疫情之后的大规模爆发是否有着明确的关联。如果有关联，那么美国失去消除状态将显而易见；但如果不是，且疫情是由不同的病毒输入引起的，那么根据该状态定义的具体技术细节，美国本可以保持其无麻疹状态。可能包含此问题答案的数据一直难以获得。德克萨斯州的麻疹毒株D8-9171看起来与随后在亚利桑那州和犹他州大规模疫情中发现的病毒相似。但该毒株也与来自墨西哥和加拿大的毒株相似。通常情况下，麻疹病毒之间可能存在相对较小的遗传变化，仅凭遗传数据并不能被泛美卫生组织用于评估消除情况。
