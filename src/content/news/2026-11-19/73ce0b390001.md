A Ukrainian barrage of missiles and drones collapsed a crucial segment of Russian President Vladimir Putin’s prized Kerch Strait Bridge—and Ukraine’s military pulled off the strike deep within Russian-occupied territory without relying on the military-grade version of Elon Musk’s Starlink satellite communications to guide the missiles in.

乌克兰的一连串导弹和无人机袭击摧毁了俄罗斯总统弗拉基米尔·普京引以为傲的刻赤海峡大桥的关键部分。乌克兰军方在深入俄罗斯占领区的腹地执行此次打击时，并未依赖埃隆·马斯克的“星链”（Starlink）卫星通信系统的军事版本来引导导弹。

On the nights of November 15 and November 16, coordinated waves of Ukrainian drones and missiles caused enough damage to collapse one of two steel arches supporting the Kerch bridge’s roadway for automobile traffic, according to an Institute for the Study of War update. Russian sources claimed several hundred Ukrainian drones first overwhelmed Russian air defenses to pave the way for remote-controlled missile strikes focused on the bridge, with the latter prominently including Ukraine’s homegrown Flamingo cruise missile.

根据战争研究所（Institute for the Study of War）的最新消息，11月15日和16日晚，乌克兰协调一致的无人机和导弹波次造成了足够的破坏，导致支撑刻赤大桥汽车通行路面的两座钢拱桥之一坍塌。俄罗斯消息来源称，数百架乌克兰无人机首先压制了俄罗斯的防空系统，为针对大桥的远程遥控导弹打击铺平了道路，其中后者显著包括乌克兰国产的“火烈鸟”（Flamingo）巡航导弹。

The Ukrainian operation adapted a Russian battlefield innovation by using drones equipped with mesh radios to create an airborne communications network, according to the Financial Times. Each networked drone acts as a relay node to extend the communication link between human operators and remotely piloted missiles and strike drones. Russia began using the technique to support long-range drone and missile strikes on Ukrainian cities.

据《金融时报》报道，乌克兰的此次行动借鉴了俄罗斯的战场创新，利用配备网状无线电的无人机建立了一个机载通信网络。每架联网的无人机都充当一个中继节点，以延长人类操作员与远程驾驶导弹及攻击无人机之间的通信链路。俄罗斯此前已开始使用该技术来支持对乌克兰城市的远程无人机和导弹袭击。

During the Ukrainian attacks on the Kerch Strait Bridge, the flying mesh network allowed operators located more than 250 kilometers away (155 miles) to remotely guide missiles and drones in evading Russian air defenses while also homing in on the bridge’s weakest structural points, according to the Financial Times. Ukraine’s takedown of the bridge segment was preceded by weeks of preparatory strikes that systematically destroyed or degraded Russian air defense weapons, radars and electronic warfare equipment arrayed to protect the bridge.

据《金融时报》报道，在乌克兰对刻赤海峡大桥的袭击中，这种飞行网状网络使位于250公里（155英里）外的操作员能够远程引导导弹和无人机规避俄罗斯的防空系统，同时锁定大桥结构上最薄弱的点。在乌克兰摧毁该桥梁部分之前，曾进行了数周的准备性打击，系统性地摧毁或削弱了部署在桥梁周围的俄罗斯防空武器、雷达和电子战设备。

The November 15 night attack on the bridge also coincided with the largest Ukrainian overnight drone attack on Russia since the start of the full-scale war, Reuters reported. Notably, Ukraine conducted the coordinated strike with primarily Ukrainian developed or adapted technologies. The Financial Times reported that “Ukrainian units involved in the operation deliberately avoided using Starshield, the military version of Musk’s Starlink satellite network,” because of recent US restrictions that prevent Ukraine from using Starshield on drones or anything else traveling more than 100 miles per hour (161 kilometers per hour).

据路透社报道，11月15日晚对大桥的袭击恰逢自全面战争爆发以来乌克兰对俄罗斯发动的最大规模夜间无人机袭击。值得注意的是，乌克兰此次协调打击主要使用了乌克兰自主研发或改装的技术。《金融时报》报道称，参与行动的乌克兰部队“刻意避免使用马斯克星链卫星网络的军事版本——星盾（Starshield）”，这是因为美国近期实施了限制，禁止乌克兰在时速超过100英里（161公里）的无人机或其他设备上使用星盾。

Ukrainian President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said that the latest US-imposed restrictions on Starshield have not yet been lifted, although SpaceX denied that the restrictions had returned. Zelenskyy described the speed limit as interfering with some Ukrainian drone interceptor technologies that were designed to defend against Russian drones attacking Ukrainian cities and infrastructure. However, the Ukrainian strikes on the Kerch Strait Bridge provide yet another example of how the Ukrainian military and defense technology companies have been learning to fight without relying on Starlink.

乌克兰总统弗拉基米尔·泽连斯基表示，美国对“星盾”实施的最新限制尚未解除，尽管SpaceX否认这些限制已经恢复。泽连斯基称，这一速度限制干扰了乌克兰的一些无人机拦截技术，而这些技术旨在防御俄罗斯对乌克兰城市和基础设施的无人机袭击。然而，乌克兰对刻赤海峡大桥的打击再次证明，乌克兰军方和国防科技公司正在学习如何在不依赖“星链”的情况下进行战斗。

From Russia’s view, the only glimmer of good news may be that the bridge’s railway section was only moderately damaged. The Ukrainian newspaper Kyiv Independent pointed to Russian news and video sources indicating that trains appeared to be running again on the bridge after the strikes. But the severe damage to the bridge’s arch and roadway section cuts off a crucial transportation and supply link between Russia and the Russian-occupied Crimean peninsula of Ukraine.

从俄罗斯的角度来看，唯一的一丝好消息可能是大桥的铁路部分仅受到中度损坏。《基辅独立报》援引俄罗斯新闻和视频来源指出，袭击发生后，火车似乎已在大桥上恢复运行。但大桥拱门和路面部分的严重损坏切断了俄罗斯与乌克兰被占领的克里米亚半岛之间至关重要的交通和补给线。

The Institute for the Study of War assesses that the “destruction of the bridge” will complicate Russia’s ability to maintain its occupation and military basing within the peninsula. The other overland supply route between Russia and Crimea involves highways running through Russian-occupied southern Ukraine. But Ukrainian forces have already been striking such highway infrastructure and the Russian supply trucks running along the exposed route.

战争研究所评估认为，“大桥的破坏”将使俄罗斯维持其在半岛上的占领和军事基地的能力变得复杂化。俄罗斯与克里米亚之间的另一条陆路补给线涉及穿过俄罗斯占领的乌克兰南部的公路。但乌克兰军队已经开始打击这些公路基础设施以及沿该暴露路线行驶的俄罗斯补给卡车。

Beyond the physical damage to the bridge, such attacks represent a symbolic blow to Putin’s ambition of holding onto the Crimean peninsula. After Russian forces seized Crimea from Ukraine in 2014, Russia based much of its Black Sea Fleet at the naval base in Sevastopol and later used the peninsula as a logistics hub for its full-scale invasion of Ukraine in February 2022. The $3.5 billion Kerch Strait Bridge was built as a physical link to cement Russia’s claim over Crimea.

除了对大桥造成的物理破坏外，此类袭击对普京占领克里米亚半岛的野心构成了象征性的打击。2014年俄罗斯军队从乌克兰手中夺取克里米亚后，俄罗斯将其黑海舰队的大部分驻扎在塞瓦斯托波尔海军基地，并随后将该半岛用作2022年2月全面入侵乌克兰的后勤枢纽。耗资35亿美元的刻赤海峡大桥是作为巩固俄罗斯对克里米亚主权主张的物理纽带而建造的。
