### ID.me terms required for IRS sign-in

Taxpayers who sign in to Internal Revenue Service (IRS) online services with ID.me, a private-sector sign-in provider, use it under ID.me's own Terms of Service, which send disputes outside small claims court to a neutral arbitrator instead of a judge or jury unless the user opts out within 30 days of accepting them. ID.me's Privacy Policy says it "may use information for marketing purposes" & may transfer information in a sale, merger or other corporate transaction, though it says information from state or federal government identity verification is not used for marketing for ID.me Shop, its shopping-discount site, unless the user consents or meets another listed exception.

使用 ID.me（一家私营部门登录提供商）登录美国国税局 (IRS) 在线服务的纳税人，必须遵守 ID.me 自己的服务条款。该条款规定，除非用户在接受条款后的 30 天内选择退出，否则争议将提交给中立的仲裁员，而非由法官或陪审团裁决。ID.me 的隐私政策称其“可能将信息用于营销目的”，并可能在出售、合并或其他企业交易中转移信息；不过，该政策也指出，除非用户同意或符合其他列出的例外情况，否则来自州或联邦政府身份验证的信息不会用于其购物折扣网站 ID.me Shop 的营销。

Login.gov, which the U.S. government maintains, also works for IRS sign-in, & the IRS's Direct Pay service takes bank-account tax payments with "No sign-in required". ID.me replaced the traditional login for Treasury's savings-bond site TreasuryDirect on October 28, 2026, & on October 29, 2026 senators Ron Wyden & Elizabeth Warren asked Treasury & the IRS to explain why they continue to use ID.me "instead of the government-run Login.gov platform".

由美国政府维护的 Login.gov 也可用于 IRS 登录，而 IRS 的“直接支付”(Direct Pay) 服务在处理银行账户缴税时则“无需登录”。2026 年 10 月 28 日，ID.me 取代了财政部储蓄债券网站 TreasuryDirect 的传统登录方式。2026 年 10 月 29 日，参议员罗恩·怀登 (Ron Wyden) 和伊丽莎白·沃伦 (Elizabeth Warren) 要求财政部和 IRS 解释为何继续使用 ID.me，“而不是政府运营的 Login.gov 平台”。

The IRS describes ID.me as "a private sector sign-in provider for secure access to government and non-government websites". ID.me's Terms of Service say the company provides "a level of assurance to businesses and government agencies" that a user's identity and attributes are verified "based upon widely-recognized standards", & that "ID.me does not charge members any fees for registering and/or using the Service or Website."

IRS 将 ID.me 描述为“用于安全访问政府和非政府网站的私营部门登录提供商”。ID.me 的服务条款称，该公司为企业和政府机构提供“一定程度的保证”，即用户的身份和属性是“基于公认标准”进行验证的，并且“ID.me 不向会员收取注册和/或使用本服务或网站的任何费用”。

The IRS sign-in page says taxpayers "need an ID.me or Login.gov account" to access tax information online. The IRS says it uses these sign-in providers "to protect your privacy and data through identity verification". One account covers the IRS online services: "Individual Online Account, Business Tax Account, Tax Pro Account, e‑Services and more". ID.me's Privacy Policy says some partners, "such as the IRS", may require identity verification "to a higher level of certainty" under National Institute of Standards and Technology guidelines.

IRS 登录页面显示，纳税人“需要一个 ID.me 或 Login.gov 账户”才能在线访问税务信息。IRS 表示，它使用这些登录提供商是为了“通过身份验证来保护您的隐私和数据”。一个账户即可覆盖多项 IRS 在线服务，包括“个人在线账户、企业税务账户、税务专业人员账户、电子服务等”。ID.me 的隐私政策称，某些合作伙伴（如 IRS）可能会根据美国国家标准与技术研究院 (NIST) 的指导方针，要求“更高确定性”的身份验证。

Treasury's savings-bond site TreasuryDirect says it uses ID.me because it "aligns with Treasury security requirements and those used by the Internal Revenue Service". The IRS said on December 7, 2021 that it was "using ID.me to provide verification services". It announced on February 7, 2022 that it would "transition away from using a third-party service for facial recognition", & said on February 21, 2022 that taxpayers could still choose biometric verification "through ID.me’s self-assistance tool".

财政部的储蓄债券网站 TreasuryDirect 表示，使用 ID.me 是因为它“符合财政部的安全要求以及国税局使用的安全要求”。IRS 在 2021 年 12 月 7 日表示，它正在“使用 ID.me 提供验证服务”。2022 年 2 月 7 日，IRS 宣布将“不再使用第三方服务进行人脸识别”，并于 2022 年 2 月 21 日表示，纳税人仍可通过“ID.me 的自助工具”选择生物识别验证。

A user accepts ID.me's Terms of Service, with the Credential Policy made available at that time, by selecting "I agree" or "taking another affirmative action clearly identified as acceptance when these Terms are presented to you". The Terms say ID.me's Credential Policy "gives further details of how we accomplish our identity proofing". The Terms say the company keeps a wallet for each user "that contains your identity and group affiliation information". ID.me's personal-information obligations are governed "at all times" by the Credential Policy & Privacy Policy.

用户通过选择“我同意”或“在向您展示这些条款时采取其他明确标识为接受的肯定行动”，即表示接受 ID.me 的服务条款及当时提供的凭证政策。条款称，ID.me 的凭证政策“详细说明了我们如何完成身份验证”。条款还提到，公司为每位用户保留一个钱包，“其中包含您的身份和团体隶属信息”。ID.me 对个人信息的义务“始终”受凭证政策和隐私政策的约束。

A taxpayer emailed consumer advocate Louis Rossmann a screenshot of the "UPDATED TERMS & CONDITIONS" screen the taxpayer said they saw while signing in to IRS.gov through ID.me, showing ID.me & IRS logos and one button, "Accept Terms & Conditions".

一位纳税人向消费者权益倡导者路易斯·罗斯曼 (Louis Rossmann) 发送了一张“更新后的条款与条件”屏幕截图。该纳税人称，这是他们在通过 ID.me 登录 IRS.gov 时看到的界面，上面显示了 ID.me 和 IRS 的徽标，以及一个“接受条款与条件”的按钮。

ID.me's Privacy Policy dated November 2, 2026 says by using the platform "you agree that ID.me. determines how personal information may be used" for the user's wallet. The policy's May 7, 2026 text lacked it.

2026 年 11 月 2 日的 ID.me 隐私政策称，通过使用该平台，“您同意由 ID.me 决定如何使用”用户钱包中的个人信息。该政策 2026 年 5 月 7 日的版本中并未包含此内容。

The current Terms, Version 10.1.0 dated November 13, 2026, count continued use after revised Terms take effect as "acceptance of those changes", "Unless we expressly state otherwise"; a user who disagrees may stop using the Service & close the wallet first. Version 10.0.3, dated March 3, 2023, said continuing use after notice "will indicate your agreement to all changes", "Unless otherwise noted by us". The Credential Policy, Version 10.1, published July 1, 2024, requires users to reaffirm acceptance of "the prevailing Terms of Service, Privacy and other Policies and any other required agreements" "at least once every 5 years" or face credential suspension.

当前的 10.1.0 版本条款（日期为 2026 年 11 月 13 日）规定，除非另有明确说明，否则在修订后的条款生效后继续使用即视为“接受这些变更”；不同意的用户可以停止使用该服务并先关闭钱包。2023 年 3 月 3 日的 10.0.3 版本曾称，除非另有说明，否则在收到通知后继续使用“将表明您同意所有变更”。2024 年 7 月 1 日发布的 10.1 版本凭证政策要求用户“至少每 5 年”重新确认接受“现行的服务条款、隐私政策及其他政策以及任何其他必要协议”，否则将面临凭证被暂停的风险。

Public radio program Marketplace reported that Nancy Kim, a professor emerita at Illinois Tech's Chicago-Kent College of Law, said she found nothing reassuring in ID.me's terms, pointing out that although they state ID.me won't sell data, ID.me can change them.

公共广播节目 Marketplace 报道称，伊利诺伊理工大学芝加哥肯特法学院名誉教授南希·金 (Nancy Kim) 表示，她在 ID.me 的条款中没有发现任何令人放心的地方，并指出尽管条款声明 ID.me 不会出售数据，但 ID.me 可以随时更改这些条款。

ID.me's Privacy Policy says: We may use information for marketing purposes. We may use your information to send promotional messages and newsletters via email, or otherwise alert you to products or Services we think might be of interest to you, including for ID.me Shop.

ID.me 的隐私政策称：我们可能会将信息用于营销目的。我们可能会使用您的信息通过电子邮件发送促销信息和时事通讯，或以其他方式提醒您注意我们认为您可能感兴趣的产品或服务，包括 ID.me Shop 的相关内容。

ID.me Shop, a page of deals & discounts, says it "is part of the Amazon Associates program and other affiliate programs" & "may earn money when you buy products through links on Shop".

ID.me Shop 是一个提供交易和折扣的页面，它表示自己“是亚马逊联盟计划和其他附属计划的一部分”，并且“当您通过 Shop 上的链接购买产品时，可能会赚取佣金”。

The policy also says: We may share your information in connection with a corporate transaction. We may disclose or transfer your information as part of, or during negotiations for, any purchase, sale, lease, merger, or any other type of acquisition, disposal, or financing involving our brands.

该政策还称：我们可能会在企业交易中共享您的信息。作为涉及我们品牌的任何购买、出售、租赁、合并或任何其他类型的收购、处置或融资的一部分，或在相关谈判期间，我们可能会披露或转移您的信息。

A capitalized notice atop ID.me's Terms of Service says they contain "A MUTUAL ARBITRATION PROVISION, JURY TRIAL WAIVER, AND CLASS-ACTION WAIVER" & that users may opt out "WITHIN THIRTY (30) DAYS OF ACCEPTING THESE TERMS". Section 20 covers disputes "arising out of or relating to" the Terms, the Privacy Policy, the website or the service. A dispute not resolved within 45 calendar days after the notice of dispute is received goes to arbitration, where "a neutral arbitrator will resolve all Disputes"; both sides give up suing before a judge or

ID.me 服务条款顶部的一则大写通知称，条款包含“相互仲裁条款、陪审团审判豁免和集体诉讼豁免”，且用户可以在“接受这些条款后的三十 (30) 天内”选择退出。第 20 节涵盖了“因”条款、隐私政策、网站或服务“引起或与之相关”的争议。如果在收到争议通知后的 45 个日历日内未能解决争议，则将进入仲裁程序，由“中立的仲裁员解决所有争议”；双方均放弃在法官面前提起诉讼的权利。
