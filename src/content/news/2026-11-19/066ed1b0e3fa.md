Trump’s latest land grab is an AI hub in the Philippines. Facing opposition at home, Trump is eyeing the former US colony to build chip fabs and data centers.

特朗普最新的圈地行动是菲律宾的一个人工智能中心。由于在国内面临阻力，特朗普正着眼于这个前美国殖民地，计划在那里建设芯片工厂和数据中心。

President Donald Trump was greeted by no less than five beauty queens when he landed in the Philippines on Tuesday, according to local news outlet Rappler. As he stepped onto the tarmac, a child handed him small US and Philippine flags. A band played “YMCA,” a staple at Trump rallies, as more girls in traditional bell-sleeved blouses and young men in wide-brimmed hats danced to the song.

据当地新闻媒体 Rappler 报道，唐纳德·特朗普总统周二抵达菲律宾时，受到了不少于五位选美皇后的迎接。当他走下停机坪时，一个孩子递给他美国和菲律宾的小国旗。一支乐队演奏了特朗普集会上的常备曲目《YMCA》，与此同时，更多身穿传统钟形袖衬衫的女孩和戴着宽檐帽的年轻男子随着音乐起舞。

Outside the airport, hundreds of protesters packed streets bearing signs — and at least one prop casket — saying “Trump out.” Others say “Reject Pax Silica,” the name for the US-led international initiative to secure chips and critical minerals used in the hardware fueling the AI boom.

在机场外，数百名抗议者挤满了街道，他们举着标语——其中至少有一个道具棺材——上面写着“特朗普滚出去”。其他人则喊着“拒绝‘硅和平’（Pax Silica）”，这是美国主导的一项国际倡议的名称，旨在确保人工智能繁荣所需的硬件芯片和关键矿产的供应。

“Pax” comes from the Latin word for “peace, stability, and long-term prosperity,” the Pax Silica website proudly says. Yet the unspoken question — peace and prosperity for whom? — explains the whiplash between the Philippine government’s warm welcome of Trump and the public fury raging outside.

“Pax”一词源自拉丁语，意为“和平、稳定和长期繁荣”，Pax Silica 网站自豪地宣称。然而，一个未被言明的问题——究竟是谁的和平与繁荣？——解释了菲律宾政府对特朗普的热烈欢迎与外界公众愤怒之间的巨大反差。

Trump says he wants to “win AI” — whatever that means. As such, the US needs data centers full of GPUs to do it. With Americans increasingly opposed to data centers and chip factories being built in their communities, the US is now recruiting less affluent nations to take up some of the slack.

特朗普声称他想要“赢得人工智能”——无论这意味着什么。因此，美国需要充满 GPU 的数据中心来实现这一目标。随着美国人越来越反对在他们的社区建设数据中心和芯片工厂，美国现在正招募较不富裕的国家来分担部分压力。

Lawmakers from both the US and the Philippines are promising a slice of the riches that advances in generative AI are supposed to create. And that’s supposed to be worth the land razed, homes destroyed, and natural resources lost in the name of technological progress. In the Philippines, that’s taking the shape of a proposed 1,620-hectare (4,000 acres) hub for chip factories, processing plants for critical minerals, and data centers.

美国和菲律宾的立法者都承诺，生成式人工智能的进步将带来财富，并承诺分享其中的一部分。而这被认为足以抵消以技术进步之名所造成的土地夷平、家园破坏和自然资源流失。在菲律宾，这表现为一个拟议中的 1620 公顷（4000 英亩）的中心，用于建设芯片工厂、关键矿产加工厂和数据中心。
