# Instagram now lets you edit your stories after you post them

Instagram 现在允许你在发布快拍（Stories）后对其进行编辑。

You can edit the text, music, stickers, and drawings in your Stories for up to 15 minutes after sharing.

在分享快拍后的 15 分钟内，你可以编辑其中的文字、音乐、贴纸和涂鸦。

Instagram’s latest update will allow you to edit your Stories without losing your views, likes, or replies. You can now edit the text, music, stickers, and drawings for up to 15 minutes after sharing your Story, Instagram head Adam Mosseri has announced.

Instagram 的最新更新将允许你在不丢失浏览量、点赞或回复的情况下编辑快拍。Instagram 负责人 Adam Mosseri 宣布，现在你可以在分享快拍后的 15 分钟内编辑其中的文字、音乐、贴纸和涂鸦。

Reverse engineer Alessandro Paluzzi first spotted Instagram working on this feature in April, but now it’s official. Previously, you had to delete and repost your Story to fix a typo or add a song that you forgot to include.

逆向工程师 Alessandro Paluzzi 最早在四月份就发现了 Instagram 正在开发此功能，但现在它已正式发布。此前，如果你想修正错别字或添加遗漏的歌曲，必须删除并重新发布快拍。

You can edit your post by opening your Story and selecting the three-dot menu in the bottom-right corner. From there, tap “Edit Story” to start making changes.

你可以通过打开快拍并选择右下角的三点菜单来编辑你的帖子。点击“编辑快拍”（Edit Story）即可开始修改。

In addition to editable Stories, Instagram announced that it’s launching new fonts, colors, and shapes for stickers, as well as presenting them on sticker sheets that are supposed to be easier to browse. The platform is also making changes to the way you select multiple photos and adding new blur, colors, and gradient background options.

除了可编辑的快拍功能外，Instagram 还宣布将推出新的字体、颜色和贴纸形状，并以更易于浏览的贴纸页形式呈现。该平台还改进了多选照片的方式，并增加了新的模糊、颜色和渐变背景选项。
