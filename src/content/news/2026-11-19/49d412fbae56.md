Interactive video world models serve as simulators for embodied agents: from a starting image and an action trajectory, they generate the views the agent would see. The starting image fixes how high the camera is, for example 0.15 m for a floor robot or 2.2 m for an overhead camera, and a faithful simulator must keep this height as the camera moves.

交互式视频世界模型充当具身智能体的模拟器：从起始图像和动作轨迹出发，它们生成智能体所能看到的视角。起始图像确定了摄像机的高度，例如地面机器人的 0.15 米或高空摄像机的 2.2 米，而一个忠实的模拟器必须在摄像机移动时保持这一高度。

We show that six recent open-weight models fail to do so: as motion replaces the starting view with generated content, they move the camera toward human eye level, a failure we call camera-height regression. Our benchmark, EyeHeight, pairs six rendered elevations (0.15 m to 2.2 m) and four calibrated real-world rig heights with scale-free multi-view height estimation.

我们展示了六个近期的开源权重模型未能做到这一点：随着运动将起始视图替换为生成内容，它们会将摄像机移向人类视线高度，我们将这种失败称为“摄像机高度回归”。我们的基准测试 EyeHeight 将六种渲染高度（0.15 米至 2.2 米）和四种校准的真实世界装置高度与无尺度多视图高度估计相结合。

All six models lose their initial height during a $180^\circ$ turn-and-walk rollout: initialized at 0.15 m, only 0-20% of clips end within $1.3\times$ of the starting height as inferred viewpoints converge to human heights of 1.1 m to 1.5 m, whereas ground-truth renders remain within a few percent.

所有六个模型在 $180^\circ$ 转弯行走的过程中都丢失了初始高度：在 0.15 米处初始化时，只有 0-20% 的片段最终高度在起始高度的 $1.3$ 倍以内，因为推断出的视角会收敛到 1.1 米至 1.5 米的人类高度，而地面实况渲染则保持在几个百分点的误差范围内。

In Matrix-Game models, homography-tracked floor motion stays consistent with constant elevation during rotation even as newly outpainted room geometry yields a higher reconstructed viewpoint. Regression grows with rotation angle, begins while the start frame remains in context, and largely reverses in SANA-WM when a $360^\circ$ turn restores the initial view.

在 Matrix-Game 模型中，单应性跟踪的地面运动在旋转过程中与恒定高度保持一致，即使新绘制的房间几何结构产生了更高的重建视角。回归现象随旋转角度增加，在起始帧仍处于上下文时就开始出现，并且在 SANA-WM 中，当 $360^\circ$ 转弯恢复初始视图时，该现象在很大程度上会逆转。

We trace this pull to human-centric video priors in which floor-level views are nearly absent: in SANA-WM, textual height cues and dense floor-disparity maps do not prevent it, whereas classifier-free text guidance during the first half of denoising strengthens it. Combining unguided sampling with height-balanced fine-tuning recovers more retention but costs 5 MUSIQ points, a loss fixed during those same early steps. Viewpoint drift alone shifts the paths predicted by visual navigation policies.

我们将这种偏差归因于以人为中心的视频先验，其中几乎不存在地面视角的图像：在 SANA-WM 中，文本高度提示和密集的地板视差图无法阻止它，而去噪前半段的无分类器文本引导反而加强了它。将无引导采样与高度平衡微调相结合可以恢复更多的保持力，但会损失 5 个 MUSIQ 分数，这种损失在相同的早期步骤中就已经确定。仅视角漂移就会改变视觉导航策略所预测的路径。
