# Absolute Batman can save DC Studios

EntertainmentComicsFilmAbsolute Batman can save DC StudiosSkydance’s cinematic superhero universe could get back on track through DC’s progressive Absolute Universe comics.by Charles Pulliam-MooreNov 18, 2026, 9:20 PM UTC Image: DC Comics / Daniel Warren Johnson, Mike Spicer, Clayton Cowles Charles Pulliam-Moore is a reporter focusing on film, TV, and pop culture. Before The Verge, he wrote about comic books, labor, race, and more at io9 and Gizmodo for almost five years.

随着 Skydance 收购华纳兄弟探索公司（WBD），詹姆斯·古恩（James Gunn）和彼得·萨夫兰（Peter Safran）不再担任 DC 工作室的联席负责人，围绕去年《超人》等电影构建的电影宇宙未来变得扑朔迷离。虽然古恩的《明日之人》（Man of Tomorrow）长片可能仍会上映，但 DC 工作室新任首席执行官杰里米·拉查姆（Jeremy Latcham）似乎打算推翻古恩对 DC 宇宙的宏大愿景。古恩此前一直在通过“和平使者”、“女超人”和“泥面人”等相对非主流的角色来充实 DC 宇宙，但据报道，拉查姆已收到 Skydance 首席执行官大卫·埃里森（David Ellison）的指令，要求加快推出以神奇女侠和绿灯侠等更受欢迎的英雄为核心的新版正义联盟。

With James Gunn and Peter Safran out as the co-heads of DC Studios following Skydance’s acquisition of Warner Bros. Discovery, it’s unclear what will become of the cinematic universe that was building around movies like last year’s Superman. Though Gunn’s Man of Tomorrow feature might still come out, it seems like his larger vision for the DC Universe may be tossed out by DC Studios’ newly appointed CEO, Jeremy Latcham. Gunn was taking his time fleshing out the DCU with relatively less high-profile characters like Peacemaker, Supergirl, and Clayface. But Latcham has reportedly received a mandate from Skydance CEO David Ellison to fast-track a new version of the Justice League featuring more popular heroes like Wonder Woman and the Green Lantern.

这种优先级的剧烈转变可能会改变未来几年 DC 宇宙的面貌，因为 Skydance 正试图将其漫画知识产权转化为项目，以帮助公司摆脱收购 WBD 后背负的 800 亿美元债务。从商业角度来看，埃里森想要推出一线超级英雄的直觉可能是正确的。大多数 DC 电影的普通观众对“三巨头”的故事比对大猩猩格鲁德（Gorilla Grodd）等冷门角色更感兴趣。但在经历了两次不温不火的 DC 电影互联宇宙尝试后，观众可能对又一次彻底的重启并没有太大胃口。

This drastic shift in priorities will likely change what the DCU looks like in the coming years as Skydance attempts to parlay its comics IP into projects that can help it get on top of the $80 billion of debt the company is now responsible for after acquiring WBD. Ellison’s instinct to whip out the A-list superheroes is probably right from a business perspective. Most casual DC movie fans are more interested in stories about the Trinity than obscure characters like Gorilla Grodd. But after two iffy attempts at creating interconnected DC movie franchises, that audience might not have much of an appetite for yet another full-on reboot.

DC 工作室不应从零开始，而应保留古恩和萨夫兰任期内行之有效的部分，例如将超人塑造成一个拥有坚定道德准则的富有同情心的人。但如果工作室真的想让这个系列朝着令人兴奋的新方向发展，就应该考虑借鉴 DC 近期一些最成功漫画的精髓。尽管 WBD 的合并促使 Skydance 上个月开始裁员近 2000 人，但作为交易一部分被 Skydance 接管的 DC 漫画公司据称并未受到影响，因为该出版部门的业务表现出人意料地好。这种成功很大程度上归功于 DC“绝对宇宙”（Absolute Universe）系列漫画的强劲销量。

Rather than starting from scratch, DC Studios should probably keep some of the things that worked well during Gunn and Safran’s tenure, like presenting Superman as a compassionate person with a strong moral compass. But if the studio really wants to take this franchise in a new direction that gets people excited, it should also be thinking about tapping into the energy of some of DC’s most successful recent comics. Though the WBD merger prompted Skydance to begin laying off almost 2,000 employees last month, DC Comics — which Skydance gained control of as part of the deal — was reportedly left unscathed because the publishing arm’s business has been performing surprisingly well. Much of that success seems to have been driven by strong sales of comics created by DC’s Absolute Universe imprint.

2024 年推出的“绝对宇宙”漫画将 DC 的英雄和反派重新想象为与其经典形象截然不同的版本。绝对版神奇女侠是在地狱中长大的女巫，绝对版超人作为青少年首次来到地球，并记得他在氪星的童年，而绝对版蝙蝠侠则是一位工人阶级的土木工程师，而非出生于巨额财富之家的社会名流。这些绝对版角色的世界观和执行正义的方式都与主流宇宙的对应角色截然不同。尽管这些漫画与既定正典有所偏离，但它们都捕捉到了使这些核心角色成为 DC 品牌标志性支柱的精髓。

Launched in 2024, the Absolute Universe comics reimagine DC’s heroes and villains as wildly different versions of their classic selves. Absolute Wonder Woman is a witch raised in Hell, Absolute Superman first arrives on Earth as a teenager who remembers his childhood on Krypton, and Absolute Batman is a working-class civil engineer rather than a socialite born into immense wealth. The Absolute characters’ worldviews and approaches to enacting justice are all distinct from their mainstream universe counterparts. But as much as the Absolute comics diverge from established canon, they each capture the essence of what makes their central characters iconic pillars of the DC brand.

除了神奇女侠之外，基于经典形象的 DC 角色电影改编已经太多了，以至于华纳兄弟似乎陷入了一种陈规。多年来，大银幕上超人和蝙蝠侠的各种迭代在风格上略有不同，但总的来说，它们都给人一种工作室不愿尝试新事物的印象。绝对版蝙蝠侠之所以引人注目，是因为它颠覆了定义大多数经典蝙蝠侠漫画的既定权力结构。

Wonder Woman aside, there have been so many cinematic portrayals of DC characters based on their classic selves that it has often seemed like Warner Bros. was stuck in a kind of rut. There have been slight stylistic differences between the various big-screen iterations of Superman and Batman over the years, but for the most part, they have all come across as the products of a studio that’s averse to trying new things. Absolute Batman is compelling because it inverts the established power structures that define most classic Batman comics.

这就是为什么马特·里夫斯（Matt Reeves）的《蝙蝠侠》（The Batman）在 2022 年上映时让人感到耳目一新的部分原因——它没有费心去重述布鲁斯·韦恩悲惨的起源故事。罗伯特·帕丁森（Robert Pattinson）将在《蝙蝠侠 2》中继续饰演这位黑暗骑士，但目前尚不清楚该版本的角色是否会对 DC 宇宙的未来产生任何影响。

That’s part of what made Matt Reeves’ The Batman— which didn’t bother to rehash Bruce Wayne’s tragic origin story — feel somewhat like a breath of fresh air when it debuted in 2022. Robert Pattinson will reprise his role as the Dark Knight in The Batman Part II, but it’s not really clear whether that version of the character will have any bearing on the DCU’s future.

由于里夫斯的蝙蝠侠电影存在于其独立的宇宙中（类似于托德·菲利普斯的《小丑》系列），且帕丁森在被选为亚马逊新任詹姆斯·邦德后想必会很忙，因此该版本的角色不太可能被纳入 DC 宇宙。不过，埃里森显然打算推出更多以蝙蝠侠为主角的项目，如果 DC 工作室能从这位英雄的“绝对版”化身中汲取灵感，或许能讲述一个引人入胜的故事。绝对版蝙蝠侠之所以如此引人入胜，是因为它颠覆了定义大多数经典蝙蝠侠漫画的既定权力结构。在那些书中，布鲁斯·韦恩是哥谭市边缘化的底层阶级，而小丑则是富有的精英，他的邪恶通过他操纵各种行业的方式凸显出来。

Between Reeves’ Batman films existing in their own universe (similar to Todd Phillips’ Joker features) and Pattinson presumably being busy now that he’s been cast as Amazon’s new James Bond, it doesn’t seem likely that that version of the character will be incorporated into the DCU. Ellison obviously intends to put out more projects featuring Batman, though, and DC Studios could tell a riveting story if it took some cues from the hero’s Absolute incarnation. What makes Absolute Batman such a compelling series is the way it inverts the established power structures that define most classic Batman comics. In those books, Bruce Wayne is part of Gotham’s marginalized underclass, while the Joker is a moneyed elite whose villainy is highlighted in the way he manipulates various industries.

这种颠覆使绝对版蝙蝠侠能够以一种与他誓言保护的普通民众有着更深联系的义警身份行事。毒藤女和贝恩等可怕的超级反派仍然是主要威胁，但漫画也花时间让绝对版蝙蝠侠对抗更普通的邪恶，例如被痴迷于恐吓少数族裔的白人民族主义者所渗透的腐败警察部门。绝对版蝙蝠侠的进步政治色彩可能与埃里森的个人情感不符，而漫画的残酷性也可能与拉查姆的方法不一致。

This inversion puts Absolute Batman in a position to operate as a vigilante with a much deeper connection to the ordinary people he has sworn to protect. Monstrous supervillains like Poison Ivy and Bane are still major threats, but the comics also spend time pitting Absolute Batman against more ordinary evils like corrupt police departments infested with white nationalists fixated on terrorizing minorities.
