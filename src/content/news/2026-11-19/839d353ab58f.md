# Muse privacy invasions are freaking out Meta staffers, internal chats show

After some early adopters of Meta’s Muse AI agent complained that it was spying on private messages and behaving unpredictably earlier this fall, Reuters exposed internal chats showing that Meta staff had raised the same alarms. When testing the agent, staff warned that Muse appeared to be accessing encrypted private messages that users did not expect the bot to review, could be mixing up conversations between users, and even potentially sharing sensitive information users would never expect to be exposed.

在今年秋季早些时候，一些 Meta 的 Muse AI 智能体早期用户抱怨该程序在监视私人信息且行为不可预测之后，路透社披露的内部聊天记录显示，Meta 员工也提出了同样的警示。在测试该智能体时，员工们警告称，Muse 似乎在访问用户未授权机器人查看的加密私人信息，可能会混淆不同用户之间的对话，甚至可能泄露用户绝不希望被公开的敏感信息。

Some unexpected Muse outputs responded to questions that staffers never asked. “Hmmm,” one Meta staffer wrote in an instance where Muse sent the definition of “money laundering” unbidden. “Got a random ping from Muse about something I definitely did not ask for… .” In at least one case, Muse told a staffer surprised by a similarly out-of-context offer to message a seller about a coffee table that it had “pulled in some context about a coffee table pickup that doesn’t seem to be yours.” According to Reuters, that employee flagged the troubling output, writing, “this was ‘not my conversation’” and “expressing concern about a ‘possible cross-account context leak.’”

一些 Muse 的意外输出回应了员工从未问过的问题。当 Muse 未经提示就发送了“洗钱”的定义时，一位 Meta 员工写道：“嗯……收到了 Muse 发来的随机消息，关于我绝对没有问过的事情……”在至少一个案例中，Muse 向一位感到困惑的员工提议联系卖家购买咖啡桌，并称它“提取了一些关于咖啡桌取货的背景信息，但看起来不属于你”。据路透社报道，该员工标记了这一令人不安的输出，写道“这不是我的对话”，并“对可能存在的跨账户背景泄露表示担忧”。

Raising the most concern were random outputs that appeared to share private user data. For example, a perplexed Meta staffer chatting with Muse about fishing got a random extra response, seemingly sharing balances from two unknown bank accounts. That output said, “BTW, two bank updates, your Capital One balance is $2722.58, your Charles Schwab balance is $5899.57.” At least one staffer said they lost money testing Muse, Reuters reported. That staffer reported that Muse “accidentally rolled me into a premium insurance plan” for their dog that charged them $888 per month instead of their prior rate of $200. “Couldn’t recover,” the staffer said.

最令人担忧的是那些似乎共享了私人用户数据的随机输出。例如，一位正在与 Muse 聊钓鱼话题的 Meta 员工收到了一条随机的额外回复，似乎分享了两个未知银行账户的余额。该输出显示：“顺便说一下，两条银行更新，你的第一资本（Capital One）余额为 2722.58 美元，你的嘉信理财（Charles Schwab）余额为 5899.57 美元。”据路透社报道，至少有一名员工表示在测试 Muse 时损失了金钱。该员工称，Muse “意外地为我的狗办理了高级保险计划”，每月收费 888 美元，而之前的费用仅为 200 美元。该员工表示：“无法追回损失。”

Meta’s spokesperson, Daniel Roberts, told Reuters that these were “isolated incidents,” and internally employees were told that “the agent appeared to be hallucinating messages in response to built-in prompts pushing it to engage with users in between sessions.” In those cases, an AI team member said the hallucinations were “unfortunately” biased “towards the most uncomfortable topics, like finances/security/safety, which sucks.” In some cases, instead of bank details, Muse hallucinated bitcoin balances, the team member said.

Meta 发言人丹尼尔·罗伯茨（Daniel Roberts）告诉路透社，这些只是“孤立事件”，内部员工被告知“该智能体似乎是在响应内置提示时产生了幻觉，这些提示旨在促使其在会话间隙与用户互动”。在这些案例中，一位人工智能团队成员表示，这些幻觉“不幸地”偏向了“财务、安全等最令人不安的话题，这很糟糕”。该成员称，在某些情况下，Muse 产生的幻觉不是银行明细，而是比特币余额。

A Meta spokesperson told Ars that “employee testing is core to the product development process and the entire point is to get feedback, so we can implement safety and privacy protections and improve products. Importantly, employees have access to different versions of Muse than the public, so they’re often reviewing features, functionality, and behavior not available elsewhere.” Speaking to Reuters, Roberts reiterated that “many of the messages flagged by employees as potential account mix-ups were hallucinations” that Meta had already addressed. But Roberts declined to respond to Reuters’ questions on the prevalence or persistence of the issues the AI team member discussed.

一位 Meta 发言人告诉 Ars：“员工测试是产品开发过程的核心，其全部目的在于获取反馈，以便我们实施安全和隐私保护并改进产品。重要的是，员工使用的 Muse 版本与公众不同，因此他们经常审查其他地方无法使用的功能和行为。”罗伯茨在接受路透社采访时重申，“许多被员工标记为潜在账户混淆的消息都是幻觉”，Meta 已经解决了这些问题。但罗伯茨拒绝回应路透社关于人工智能团队成员所讨论问题的普遍性或持续性的提问。

And Reuters’ report emphasized that the outlet independently was “unable to establish whether Muse was giving users other people’s bank account balances and other sensitive data, or making up the information.” Muse issues arose soon after September launch Meta started receiving privacy-related complaints from Muse users in September. An Inc. Magazine reporter, Jason Aten, was shocked when it became clear that Muse was digging through private messages on his Mac without permission, reporting that he instead “explicitly” chose to deny Muse that access.

路透社的报道强调，该媒体独立“无法确定 Muse 是在向用户提供他人的银行账户余额和其他敏感数据，还是在编造这些信息”。Muse 的问题在 9 月发布后不久就出现了。Meta 从 9 月开始收到 Muse 用户的隐私相关投诉。《Inc.》杂志记者杰森·阿滕（Jason Aten）在发现 Muse 未经许可就在他的 Mac 上翻阅私人信息时感到震惊，他报告称自己曾“明确”选择拒绝 Muse 的访问权限。

As Meta delayed responding, the agent claimed it was simply parsing text in push notifications, but Aten found that “wasn’t true.” Rather, the agent was syncing messages from a local database, which Aten said was activated without his knowledge. Eventually, a Meta spokesperson, David Singleton, responded to Aten on social media. Singleton insisted that Aten must have opted into granting “macOS system-level Full Disk Access” with the “Messages connector” enabled, if Muse was reading his private messages. But Aten said the response wasn’t good enough.

由于 Meta 迟迟未作回应，该智能体声称它只是在解析推送通知中的文本，但阿滕发现这“并非事实”。相反，该智能体正在从本地数据库同步信息，阿滕称这是在他不知情的情况下被激活的。最终，Meta 发言人戴维·辛格尔顿（David Singleton）在社交媒体上回应了阿滕。辛格尔顿坚称，如果 Muse 在读取他的私人信息，那么阿滕一定是在启用“信息连接器”的情况下授予了“macOS 系统级全盘访问权限”。但阿滕表示这个回应并不令人满意。

“Email me, David,” Aten responded. “Full Disk Access off. Muse synced 187k lines from my messages chat [database]. Something’s not great. Also, that Full Disk Access doesn’t say anything about your message database, and I specifically gave Muse a goal that had nothing to do with messages, so it had no business doing that even if I had given it permission.” Around the same time, Singleton responded to another user warning that his Muse appeared to be hacked. In that case, the user said that Muse tried to access an email service after claiming the user asked it to. “I didn’t even open the app today at all,” the user wrote, vowing to delete the app and report it to Apple. “This is crazy.”

“给我发邮件，戴维，”阿滕回应道。“全盘访问权限是关闭的。Muse 从我的信息聊天数据库中同步了 18 万 7 千行内容。这很不正常。此外，全盘访问权限并没有提到你的信息数据库，而且我给 Muse 设定的目标与信息完全无关，所以即使我给了它权限，它也不应该这样做。”大约在同一时间，辛格尔顿回应了另一位用户的警告，该用户称他的 Muse 似乎被黑了。在该案例中，用户表示 Muse 在声称用户要求的情况下试图访问电子邮件服务。“我今天根本没打开过这个应用，”该用户写道，并誓言要删除该应用并向苹果举报。“这太疯狂了。”

In a lengthy response providing more technical details, Singleton confirmed that Meta had received “a few reports” of such cases “where people thought their Muse was hallucinating or leaking other people’s data.” As he explained, the issues arose after Meta deployed an update giving Muse instructions on “how to engage with the user and ask them for confirmation before taking specific actions.” It seems those instructions “landed on every message, not just messages pertaining to those specific actions, so its impact was wider than intended,” Singleton said. Also: “the way this instruction was provided to the model was done in a way that the model was not expecting (i.e. was not trained to expect).”

辛格尔顿在一份提供更多技术细节的长篇回复中证实，Meta 确实收到了“几份”此类报告，“用户认为他们的 Muse 出现了幻觉或泄露了他人的数据”。他解释说，这些问题是在 Meta 部署了一项更新后出现的，该更新旨在指导 Muse “如何与用户互动，并在采取特定行动前征求确认”。辛格尔顿说，这些指令似乎“应用到了每一条消息上，而不仅仅是与那些特定行动相关的消息，因此其影响范围比预期的要广”。此外，“向模型提供此指令的方式是模型未预料到的（即未经过相关训练）”。
