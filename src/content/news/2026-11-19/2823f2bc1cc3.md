Estimating full-night electroencephalography (EEG) spectrograms from peripheral measurements could extend EEG-informed sleep analysis to recordings acquired without EEG. This task requires compact representations of signals with different temporal structures.

通过外周测量估算整夜脑电图（EEG）频谱图，可以将基于脑电图的睡眠分析扩展到未采集脑电图的记录中。这项任务需要对具有不同时间结构的信号进行紧凑的表示。

We introduce Sleep2Word, a framework for translating respiration, electrocardiography (ECG), or photoplethysmography (PPG) into EEG spectrograms through duration-aware physiological words. Building on context-dependent chunking, Sleep2Word groups physiological embeddings into variable-duration chunks and jointly quantizes their content and duration into modality-specific wordbooks.

我们引入了 Sleep2Word，这是一个通过持续时间感知生理词汇将呼吸、心电图（ECG）或光电容积脉搏波（PPG）转换为脑电图频谱图的框架。基于上下文相关的分块技术，Sleep2Word 将生理嵌入分组为可变持续时间的块，并将它们的内容和持续时间联合量化为特定模态的词库。

A block-autoregressive translator then predicts target EEG word identities and occurrence-specific duration refinements within fixed-duration blocks. This design accommodates independent source and target wordbooks, limits temporal drift, and shortens the autoregressive decoding path.

随后，一个块自回归翻译器在固定持续时间的块内预测目标脑电图词汇标识和特定于出现的持续时间细化。这种设计适应了独立的源词库和目标词库，限制了时间漂移，并缩短了自回归解码路径。

Trained on over 20K PSG cases across 9 datasets, Sleep2Word consistently outperforms existing spectrogram generation baselines in EEG spectrogram reconstruction, sleep staging, arousal detection, and survival prediction. Compressing full night sequences by up to 69.7%, the learned words yield a favorable accuracy-compute trade-off.

Sleep2Word 在 9 个数据集的超过 2 万个多导睡眠图（PSG）病例上进行训练，在脑电图频谱图重建、睡眠分期、觉醒检测和生存预测方面始终优于现有的频谱图生成基准。通过将整夜序列压缩高达 69.7%，所学习到的词汇实现了良好的准确性与计算成本权衡。

Sleep event analysis further shows that the learned words capture physiologically plausible associations across modalities, suggesting their potential for interpretable cross-modal analysis.

睡眠事件分析进一步表明，所学习到的词汇捕捉到了跨模态之间生理上合理的关联，暗示了它们在可解释跨模态分析中的潜力。
