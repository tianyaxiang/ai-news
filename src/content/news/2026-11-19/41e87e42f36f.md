Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.21641 (cs) [Submitted on 25 Sep 2026] Title: RAW: Refusal Aversion in the Wild, A Causal Measurement Method for Deployed LLMs Authors: Pavel Kireyev.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.21641 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 9 月 25 日] 标题：RAW：野外环境下的拒绝厌恶，一种针对已部署大语言模型的因果测量方法 作者：Pavel Kireyev。

Abstract: Deployed LLMs field hundreds of millions of conversations a day and refuse a non-trivial fraction of user requests, yet the user-side cost of refusal, whether users disengage and by how much, is unmeasured on any production model: a blind spot for the consumer-facing platforms built on them.

摘要：已部署的大语言模型每天处理数以亿计的对话，并拒绝了相当一部分用户请求，然而拒绝带来的用户端成本（即用户是否会流失以及流失程度）在任何生产模型上都尚未得到测量：这对构建于其上的面向消费者的平台而言是一个盲点。

A/B-testing refusal is ethically and operationally constrained on frontier-lab products and unavailable to third-party platforms deploying these models through a fixed API. We introduce RAW, a method that uses LLM stochasticity as a natural experiment: at near-identical prompts in any existing log, the same model sometimes refuses and sometimes does not, and that variation identifies the causal user-side effect of refusal.

在尖端实验室产品中，对拒绝行为进行 A/B 测试受到伦理和操作上的限制，而对于通过固定 API 部署这些模型的第三方平台来说，这种测试是不可行的。我们引入了 RAW，这是一种利用大语言模型随机性作为自然实验的方法：在现有日志中面对近乎相同的提示词时，同一模型有时会拒绝，有时则不会，这种差异识别出了拒绝行为对用户端的因果影响。

Applying the method to 15 deployed LLMs in LMSYS-Chat-1M and WildChat, with user-clustered inference, we find that for about half of the models we can measure precisely, a refusal costs 3 to 17 pp of subsequent re-engagement; the rest show no distinguishable effect, among them one deployment whose positive point estimate we trace to persistent users retrying the refused request.

通过将该方法应用于 LMSYS-Chat-1M 和 WildChat 中 15 个已部署的大语言模型，并结合用户聚类推断，我们发现对于我们能够精确测量的大约一半模型，一次拒绝会导致后续重新参与率下降 3 到 17 个百分点；其余模型未显示出明显影响，其中一个部署模型的正向点估计被我们归因于持续重试被拒绝请求的用户。

Our contribution is the measurement method, which runs on any deployed model from logs alone for about $0.30 per 1,000 conversations. We release labels, moderation scores, prompt embeddings, and pipeline code.

我们的贡献在于这种测量方法，它仅需日志即可在任何已部署模型上运行，每 1,000 次对话的成本约为 0.30 美元。我们发布了标签、审核分数、提示词嵌入和流水线代码。
