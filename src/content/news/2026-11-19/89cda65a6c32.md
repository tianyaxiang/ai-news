Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.21649 (cs) [Submitted on 15 Oct 2026] Title: Literary Language Mashup: Curating Fictions with Large Language Models Authors: Manzanarez Gerardo Aleman, Monroy Raul, Garcia Flores Jorge, Calvo Hiram.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.21649 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 10 月 15 日] 标题：文学语言大杂烩：利用大型语言模型策划小说 作者：Manzanarez Gerardo Aleman, Monroy Raul, Garcia Flores Jorge, Calvo Hiram。

Abstract: In this paper, we propose an alternative approach to human evaluation of literary fictions by employing LLMs themselves as evaluators within the GrAImes framework. We apply this methodology to assess human-written and AI-generated microfictions in Spanish, to five PhD professors in literature and sixteen literary enthusiasts, and to short stories in both Spanish and English.

摘要：在本文中，我们提出了一种替代人类评估文学小说的方法，即在 GrAImes 框架内聘请大语言模型（LLM）本身作为评估者。我们将此方法应用于评估西班牙语的人类创作和人工智能生成的微型小说，评估者包括五位文学博士教授和十六位文学爱好者，并将其应用于西班牙语和英语的短篇小说。

By comparing the evaluations performed by LLMs with those of human judges, we examine the degree of alignment and divergence between both perspectives, thereby assessing the feasibility of LLMs as auxiliary literary evaluators. Our analysis focuses on the alignment of responses from LLMs with those of human evaluators, providing insights into the potential of LLMs in literary assessment.

通过比较大语言模型与人类评委的评估结果，我们考察了两种视角之间的一致性和差异程度，从而评估了大语言模型作为辅助文学评估者的可行性。我们的分析重点在于大语言模型与人类评估者反馈的一致性，为大语言模型在文学评估中的潜力提供了见解。

The conducted experiments reveal that while LLMs cannot be regarded as substitutes for human judges in the evaluation of literary microfictions and short stories, with a Krippendorff's alpha reliability coefficient less than 0.66, they can serve as a valuable tool that offers an initial perspective on the editorial quality of the texts in question.

实验结果表明，虽然大语言模型在评估文学微型小说和短篇小说时不能被视为人类评委的替代品（其 Krippendorff's alpha 信度系数小于 0.66），但它们可以作为一种有价值的工具，为所涉文本的编辑质量提供初步视角。

Overall, this study contributes to the ongoing discourse on the role of artificial intelligence in literature, underlining both its methodological constraints and its potential as a complementary resource for literary evaluation.

总的来说，这项研究为关于人工智能在文学中作用的持续讨论做出了贡献，强调了其方法论上的局限性以及作为文学评估补充资源的潜力。
