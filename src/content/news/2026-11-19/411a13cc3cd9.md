In the span of less than 24 hours, two Trump administration nominees have suffered sudden blows—Trump’s pick to lead the Food and Drug Administration and his pick to lead the National Science Foundation. Late Tuesday evening, Axios reported that the White House had abruptly withdrawn its controversial nomination of Heidi Overton to lead the FDA.

在不到24小时的时间里，特朗普政府的两名提名人选遭遇了突如其来的打击——分别是特朗普提名的美国食品药品监督管理局（FDA）局长人选和国家科学基金会（NSF）负责人选。周二深夜，Axios报道称，白宫已突然撤回了对海蒂·奥弗顿（Heidi Overton）担任FDA局长的争议性提名。

Overton, a top conservative domestic policy aide in the White House, has drawn criticism and skepticism from Republicans and Democrats alike over her stances on vaccines, abortion, vapes, and birth control. Overton opposes abortion—though perhaps not strongly enough for some Republicans—and supported Trump and Kennedy’s decried anti-vaccine policies and statements, vexing Democrats.

奥弗顿是白宫的一名高级保守派国内政策助手，她因在疫苗、堕胎、电子烟和节育问题上的立场，招致了共和党和民主党双方的批评与质疑。奥弗顿反对堕胎——尽管在一些共和党人看来力度还不够——并且支持特朗普和肯尼迪备受诟病的疫苗怀疑论政策和言论，这令民主党人感到恼火。

In her confirmation hearing in September, Overton consistently stonewalled senators on every topic, refusing to directly answer even softball questions from friendly Republicans, leaving lawmakers on both sides of the aisle openly frustrated. The questions she dodged included those on how she would handle the FDA’s current safety review of the abortion pill, mifepristone, which extensive data has established is extremely safe.

在9月份的确认听证会上，奥弗顿在所有议题上都不断敷衍参议员，甚至拒绝直接回答来自友好共和党人的简单问题，这让两党的议员都感到公开的沮丧。她回避的问题包括她将如何处理FDA目前对堕胎药米非司酮（mifepristone）的安全审查，而大量数据已证实该药极其安全。

She also wouldn’t say whether she agreed with Trump when he falsely claimed the measles vaccine is “quite lethal” or that children are given vaccine doses the “size of a soda bottle.” Overton repeatedly refused to answer whether Trump could ever be wrong. Axios quoted a senior Trump administration official as saying that Overton’s nomination was dropped because she “lacks the support on the Hill to move forward with her confirmation.”

她也不愿说明是否同意特朗普关于麻疹疫苗“相当致命”或儿童接种的疫苗剂量“像汽水瓶那么大”的虚假说法。奥弗顿多次拒绝回答特朗普是否会犯错。Axios援引一位特朗普政府高级官员的话称，奥弗顿的提名被撤回是因为她“在国会缺乏推进确认程序所需的支持”。

The Senate’s Health, Education, Labor and Pensions (HELP) Committee, chaired by Bill Cassidy (R-La.), was scheduled to vote on Overton’s nomination Wednesday. In a social media post Tuesday night, after her nomination was withdrawn, Cassidy said he believed she had a “viable confirmation path.” But he said the nomination was “abandoned” after some anti-abortion senators sought commitments from her that they couldn’t get.

由比尔·卡西迪（Bill Cassidy，路易斯安那州共和党人）担任主席的参议院卫生、教育、劳工与养老金委员会（HELP）原定于周三对奥弗顿的提名进行投票。在周二晚提名被撤回后，卡西迪在社交媒体上发文称，他认为她原本有“可行的确认途径”。但他表示，在一些反堕胎参议员寻求她无法做出的承诺后，该提名被“放弃”了。

“The Administration withdrew Dr. Overton rather than make such commitments,” Cassidy said. “Unfortunately, with the limited time left, confirming a permanent FDA Commissioner will fall to Senate Democrats,” Cassidy noted.

“政府撤回了奥弗顿博士的提名，而不是做出这些承诺，”卡西迪说。“不幸的是，由于剩下的时间有限，确认一名常任FDA局长的任务将落到参议院民主党人身上，”卡西迪指出。

After Overton’s last-minute withdrawal, the HELP committee’s agenda for Wednesday was updated again this morning to scrap a scheduled vote on Jim O’Neill, who was Trump’s nominee to run the NSF. O’Neill has been criticized for lacking the science experience needed to run the foundation. In the past, O’Neill worked as a Silicon Valley investor and entrepreneur and became a close ally of Peter Thiel.

在奥弗顿最后一刻被撤回提名后，HELP委员会周三的议程于今早再次更新，取消了原定对吉姆·奥尼尔（Jim O’Neill）的投票，他是特朗普提名的NSF负责人选。奥尼尔因缺乏管理该基金会所需的科学经验而受到批评。过去，奥尼尔曾是一名硅谷投资者和企业家，并成为彼得·蒂尔（Peter Thiel）的亲密盟友。

But under Trump, O’Neill has been the deputy secretary of the Department of Health and Human Services and served as acting director of the Centers for Disease Control and Prevention. O’Neill took over the latter role after the former director, Susan Monarez, was ousted from it less than a month after her Senate confirmation. She later told Senators Kennedy fired her for refusing to blindly approve vaccine recommendations from his anti-vaccine advisors.

但在特朗普政府下，奥尼尔曾担任卫生与公众服务部副部长，并担任过疾病控制与预防中心（CDC）的代理主任。奥尼尔接任该职位是在前任主任苏珊·莫纳雷斯（Susan Monarez）在参议院确认后不到一个月就被解职之后。她后来告诉参议员，肯尼迪解雇她是因为她拒绝盲目批准其反疫苗顾问提出的疫苗建议。

In remarks this morning, Cassidy said that O’Neill “did not do enough to stand up for science and against irresponsible decisions and statements from [Health] Secretary [Robert F.] Kennedy [Jr.].” As an example, Cassidy specifically referenced O’Neill’s role in the firing of Monarez. “Several members of the [HELP] committee had concerns regarding this,” Cassidy said. “This time requires leadership and fortitude and standing up for what is right,” Cassidy added.

在今早的讲话中，卡西迪表示，奥尼尔“在捍卫科学以及反对[卫生部长罗伯特·F·]肯尼迪[小]不负责任的决定和言论方面做得不够。”作为例子，卡西迪特别提到了奥尼尔在解雇莫纳雷斯一事中的角色。“[HELP]委员会的几名成员对此表示担忧，”卡西迪说。“这个时代需要领导力、坚毅以及坚持正确的事，”卡西迪补充道。

The NSF has been hard hit by the Trump administration and actions by Elon Musk’s DOGE. The NSF has been forced to cancel hundreds of research grants and taken brutal cuts to its budget and staff in the past two years. In April, the Trump administration also terminated all members of the board that steers the NSF, called the National Science Board.

NSF受到了特朗普政府和埃隆·马斯克（Elon Musk）的“政府效率部”（DOGE）行动的沉重打击。在过去两年中，NSF被迫取消了数百项研究资助，并遭受了预算和人员的残酷削减。今年4月，特朗普政府还解雇了指导NSF的董事会——国家科学委员会的所有成员。

Former members feared that it would make way for O’Neill to stack the board with political allies, pointing to how Trump filled the President’s Council of Advisors on Science and Technology with tech billionaires—including leaders of Meta, Google, Oracle, and Nvidia—instead of top scientists and researchers.

前成员们担心，这将为奥尼尔用政治盟友填补董事会铺平道路，他们指出特朗普是如何用科技亿万富翁——包括Meta、谷歌、甲骨文和英伟达的领导人——而不是顶尖科学家和研究人员来填补总统科学技术顾问委员会的。
