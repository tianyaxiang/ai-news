The EgoLink Challenge at ACM Multimedia 2026 benchmarks egocentric multimodal intelligence linking first-person perception, social reasoning, and interactive action. Unlike benchmarks centered on visible objects and activities, EgoLink evaluates whether models can interpret emotional and social cues, resolve visually grounded references, and convert user requests into verified tool-mediated actions.

ACM Multimedia 2026 的 EgoLink 挑战赛旨在对以第一人称视角感知、社会推理和交互动作为核心的以自我为中心的多模态智能进行基准测试。与侧重于可见物体和活动的基准测试不同，EgoLink 评估模型是否能够解读情感和社会线索、解析视觉基础引用，并将用户请求转换为经过验证的工具辅助操作。

Its two tracks cover social reasoning in egocentric video and interactive agents that complete service tasks through dialogue, tool use, and closed-loop execution. Together, they test whether perception, reasoning, and decision-making remain coupled under both offline question answering and state-changing interaction.

其两个赛道涵盖了以自我为中心视频中的社会推理，以及通过对话、工具使用和闭环执行来完成服务任务的交互式智能体。这些赛道共同测试了在离线问答和状态改变交互两种情况下，感知、推理和决策是否能够保持耦合。

We highlight a multi-stage Track 1 data pipeline for multiple-choice question (MCQ) generation, chain-of-thought generation, automatic quality grading, and video-contribution filtering. We summarize the task design, evaluation metrics, rules, leaderboards, and representative solutions.

我们重点介绍了赛道 1 的多阶段数据流水线，该流水线用于多项选择题（MCQ）生成、思维链生成、自动质量评分和视频贡献过滤。我们总结了任务设计、评估指标、规则、排行榜和代表性解决方案。

The benchmark combines objective answer scoring with process- and result-based evaluation, enabling analysis of both final correctness and execution quality. The best Track 1 system achieved 0.9263 accuracy, while the best Track 2 system achieved 0.844 joint success and 0.916 micro accuracy.

该基准测试将客观答案评分与基于过程和结果的评估相结合，从而能够同时分析最终正确性和执行质量。赛道 1 的最佳系统达到了 0.9263 的准确率，而赛道 2 的最佳系统达到了 0.844 的联合成功率和 0.916 的微观准确率。

Participant reports reveal a shared principle: strong systems make intermediate evidence explicit through candidate verification, option-robust reasoning, scenario-aware planning, tool canonicalization, and state-aware correction before answering or acting. These findings highlight the importance of process verification and evidence-grounded reasoning for reliable egocentric multimodal intelligence.

参与者的报告揭示了一个共同原则：强大的系统在回答或行动之前，通过候选验证、选项稳健推理、场景感知规划、工具规范化和状态感知校正，使中间证据变得明确。这些发现强调了过程验证和基于证据的推理对于可靠的以自我为中心的多模态智能的重要性。
