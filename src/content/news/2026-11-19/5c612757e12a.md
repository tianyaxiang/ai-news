Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.21682 (cs) [Submitted on 15 Nov 2026] Title: NEBench R1: a Reasoning Benchmark for Evaluating Nuclear Domain Competence in Large Language Models Authors: Liyan Wang, Longze Li, Mohamed Hassan, Isshu Lee, Dajie Sun, Ruimin Feng, Aleksandar Vakanski.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.21682 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 15 日] 标题：NEBench R1：用于评估大型语言模型核领域能力的推理基准。作者：Liyan Wang, Longze Li, Mohamed Hassan, Isshu Lee, Dajie Sun, Ruimin Feng, Aleksandar Vakanski。

Abstract: Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly being explored for knowledge-intensive and safety-critical applications in nuclear engineering. However, existing nuclear-domain benchmarks provide limited content coverage or focus primarily on answer accuracy without systematically evaluating the quality of the underlying reasoning.

摘要：大型语言模型（LLMs）正越来越多地被探索用于核工程中知识密集型和安全关键型应用。然而，现有的核领域基准测试内容覆盖范围有限，或者主要关注答案准确性，而没有系统地评估底层推理的质量。

This study introduces NEBench R1, a reasoning-oriented benchmark comprising 7,546 unique multiple-choice questions extracted from U.S. Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) reactor operator licensing examinations covering 18 nuclear facilities. Each question is classified according to the NRC Knowledge and Abilities catalogs and accompanied by an official explanation serving as a reference for reasoning evaluation.

本研究引入了 NEBench R1，这是一个面向推理的基准测试，包含从美国核管理委员会（NRC）涵盖 18 个核设施的反应堆操作员执照考试中提取的 7,546 个独特的多项选择题。每个问题都根据 NRC 的知识和能力目录进行分类，并附有作为推理评估参考的官方解释。

Six LLMs were evaluated, with overall accuracy ranging from 34.0% to 73.9% across three distinct performance tiers. To assess reasoning quality, an LLM-based judge evaluated correctly answered responses using a multidimensional rubric. Although responses generally demonstrated strong problem comprehension, they scored lower in factual and procedural accuracy and distractor analysis, indicating that correct answers were not necessarily supported by technically sound justifications.

研究评估了六个 LLM，在三个不同的性能层级中，总体准确率从 34.0% 到 73.9% 不等。为了评估推理质量，一个基于 LLM 的评判员使用多维评分标准对回答正确的响应进行了评估。尽管回答通常表现出较强的题目理解能力，但在事实和程序准确性以及干扰项分析方面得分较低，这表明正确的答案并不一定有技术上合理的论据支持。

Response clarity was also weakly indicative of technical quality. Furthermore, longer reasoning traces were associated with lower accuracy in both frontier reasoning models, suggesting that reasoning length may reflect question difficulty rather than improved problem-solving performance. Reasoning-token consumption may therefore provide an additional signal for identifying potentially unreliable responses.

回答的清晰度对技术质量的指示作用也很弱。此外，在两个前沿推理模型中，较长的推理轨迹与较低的准确率相关，这表明推理长度可能反映了问题的难度，而不是问题解决能力的提高。因此，推理标记（token）的消耗量可能为识别潜在不可靠的回答提供了一个额外的信号。

NEBench R1 establishes a benchmark for evaluating both answer accuracy and reasoning quality, providing a foundation for assessing the domain competence and reliability of LLMs in nuclear engineering.

NEBench R1 建立了一个用于评估答案准确性和推理质量的基准，为评估 LLM 在核工程领域的专业能力和可靠性奠定了基础。
