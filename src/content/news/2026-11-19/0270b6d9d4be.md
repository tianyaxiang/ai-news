Title: Language Models Can Rewrite Their Own Weights

标题：语言模型可以重写自身的权重

A language model is trained once and frozen. Making it better takes another training run, or a prompt, a harness or extra computation at inference that is gone once the task ends. We show a third way: to our knowledge for the first time, a language model rewrites its own weights by its own reasoning and becomes better, with no gradients and no training.

语言模型在训练一次后即被冻结。要使其性能提升，通常需要进行另一次训练，或者在推理时使用提示词、辅助工具或额外的计算，而这些在任务结束后即失效。我们展示了第三种方式：据我们所知，这是首次实现语言模型通过自身的推理来重写其权重，从而在无需梯度和额外训练的情况下实现性能提升。

We call this self-rewriting: the model is its own optimizer. In earlier work, a frontier model edited a much smaller one; here an open 27B model, given its weights, its graded answers and the ability to run itself, diagnoses its own failure and rewrites the weights behind it. It surpasses itself: its own edit makes it 6.6 points more accurate on problems it never saw.

我们将此称为“自我重写”：模型即其自身的优化器。在早期的研究中，前沿模型曾编辑过规模小得多的模型；而在这里，一个开源的 27B 模型在获得其权重、评分答案以及运行自身的能力后，能够诊断出自身的失败并重写其背后的权重。它超越了自身：通过自身的编辑，它在从未见过的难题上准确率提高了 6.6 个百分点。

It keeps getting better: it tests its edits on practice problems and submits only what works, each edit improving on the last, and over eight consecutive sessions its accuracy only rises. It repairs a damaged copy of itself, like a broken robot that fixes itself without being rebuilt: the copy solves almost nothing, yet with no retraining and without ever reading the original weights, the model brings it back to over 80% of the original accuracy on image tasks it never saw.

它不断进步：它在练习题上测试其编辑结果，并仅提交有效的部分，每一次编辑都比上一次有所改进，在连续八次会话中，其准确率持续上升。它修复了一个受损的自身副本，就像一个无需重建就能自我修复的故障机器人：该副本原本几乎无法解决任何问题，但在没有重新训练且从未读取原始权重的情况下，模型使其在从未见过的图像任务上恢复到了原始准确率的 80% 以上。

And the ability emerges with scale: in one model family, the smaller models make no edit or break themselves, and only the largest improves and repairs itself. Unlike test-time scaling, self-rewriting writes its gain into the weights, at no cost per answer; unlike training, it runs only forward passes, as serving does. The wall between training and inference is a limit of our tools, not a property of intelligence.

这种能力随着规模的扩大而显现：在同一个模型系列中，较小的模型无法进行编辑或导致自身损坏，只有最大的模型能够实现自我改进和修复。与测试时扩展（test-time scaling）不同，自我重写将增益写入权重，且不会增加每个答案的成本；与训练不同，它仅运行前向传播，就像服务部署时一样。训练与推理之间的壁垒是我们工具的局限，而非智能的属性。

We believe self-rewriting is a necessary step toward recursive self-improvement: a model that, like a human, does not separate training from inference could keep upgrading itself for life, with no larger model, no harness or prompt engineering, and no pre- or post-training.

我们认为，自我重写是迈向递归自我改进的必要步骤：一个像人类一样不将训练与推理分开的模型，可以在无需更大模型、无需辅助工具或提示工程、也无需预训练或后训练的情况下，终身不断地自我升级。
