# Anthropic prices initial public offering

Today Anthropic, PBC priced its initial public offering of its Class A common stock at $83.00 per share. The offering consists of 980,000,000 shares of Class A common stock, 956,351,354 of which are being sold by Anthropic and 23,648,646 of which are being sold by certain selling stockholders. Anthropic will not receive any proceeds from the sale of shares by the selling stockholders.

今天，Anthropic, PBC 将其 A 类普通股的首次公开募股价格定为每股 83.00 美元。此次发行包括 9 亿 8 千万股 A 类普通股，其中 956,351,354 股由 Anthropic 出售，23,648,646 股由部分售股股东出售。Anthropic 将不会从售股股东的股票出售中获得任何收益。

The shares are expected to begin trading on the Nasdaq Global Select Market on November 19, 2026 under the symbol “ANT.” The offering is expected to close on November 20, 2026, subject to the satisfaction of customary closing conditions. In addition, Anthropic has granted the underwriters a 30-day option to purchase up to an additional 147,000,000 shares of its Class A common stock.

这些股票预计将于 2026 年 11 月 19 日在纳斯达克全球精选市场开始交易，股票代码为“ANT”。此次发行预计将于 2026 年 11 月 20 日完成，具体取决于惯例成交条件的满足情况。此外，Anthropic 已授予承销商 30 天的选择权，可额外购买最多 1 亿 4 千 7 百万股其 A 类普通股。
