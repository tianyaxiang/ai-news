Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.21706 (cs) [Submitted on 16 Nov 2026] Title: Video recordings and play behaviour annotations of group-housed dairy calves on two Dutch farms Authors: Haiyu Yang, Heidi Lesscher, Enhong Liu, Miel Hostens.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.21706 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 16 日] 标题：荷兰两个农场群养奶牛犊的视频记录与游戏行为标注 作者：Haiyu Yang, Heidi Lesscher, Enhong Liu, Miel Hostens。

Abstract: Play is used as an indicator of positive welfare in dairy calves, but openly available video of group-housed calves with individual behaviour annotations is scarce. We describe video from four camera deployments of about one week each in group pens of dairy calves on two farms in the Netherlands: a research and teaching dairy farm (2024 and 2025) and a commercial dairy farm (2024).

摘要：游戏行为被用作奶牛犊积极福利的指标，但公开可用的、带有个体行为标注的群养奶牛犊视频却很少。我们描述了在荷兰两个农场（一个研究与教学奶牛场（2024年和2025年）和一个商业奶牛场（2024年））的奶牛犊群养圈中，四次每次约一周的摄像机部署所拍摄的视频。

The recordings comprise 1,173 video files spanning 651 h. For eight observation days, trained observers coded calf behaviour continuously in Noldus The Observer XT with an 18-item ethogram of play and other behaviours, covering 104 h of video, 90 h of which have every calf in the pen coded.

这些记录包含 1,173 个视频文件，总时长 651 小时。在八个观察日中，训练有素的观察员使用 Noldus The Observer XT 软件，根据包含 18 项游戏及其他行为的动物行为谱，对牛犊行为进行了连续编码，涵盖了 104 小时的视频，其中 90 小时的视频对圈内每头牛犊都进行了编码。

The release also contains 1.1 million decoded frames from seven annotated sessions, a per-frame timestamp read by optical character recognition from the camera clock burned into each frame, per-calf segmentation tracks and per-calf frame-level play labels. The data support studies of calf behaviour and welfare and the development and evaluation of automated behaviour recognition.

此次发布还包含来自七个标注会话的 110 万帧解码图像、通过光学字符识别从每帧嵌入的摄像机时钟读取的逐帧时间戳、每头牛犊的分割轨迹以及每头牛犊的帧级游戏标签。这些数据支持对牛犊行为和福利的研究，以及自动化行为识别的开发与评估。
