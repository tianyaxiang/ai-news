The computer gaming world hasn’t heard much from Will Wright (SimCity, The Sims) since the underwhelming release of Spore in 2008 and Wright’s subsequent departure from Maxis owner EA. Now, well over 15 years later, Wright is starting to promote his next digital simulation, which will use generative AI technology to craft a game-like experience from players’ memories.

自 2008 年《孢子》(Spore) 发布反响平平以及威尔·赖特 (Will Wright) 随后离开 Maxis 母公司 EA 以来，电脑游戏界就很少听到他的消息了（他曾开发过《模拟城市》和《模拟人生》）。如今，时隔 15 年多，赖特开始宣传他的下一个数字模拟项目，该项目将利用生成式人工智能技术，根据玩家的记忆构建出一种类似游戏的体验。

MyMems, which is slated to release next year, differentiates itself from Wright’s previous projects by “turn[ing] the simulation inward,” as Wright’s Gallium Studios and partner Artificial Agency put it in a press release this morning. The game will mine memories provided by the player to create digital people that can then form teams to solve puzzles in the game world.

计划于明年发布的 MyMems 与赖特之前的项目有所不同，正如赖特的 Gallium Studios 及其合作伙伴 Artificial Agency 在今天上午的新闻稿中所述，该游戏将“向内转向模拟”。游戏将挖掘玩家提供的记忆，创造出数字人物，然后这些人物可以组成团队，在游戏世界中解决谜题。

“If my Grandmother ended up in a Grand Slam match against Serena Williams, who would win?” Wright says by way of example in the release. “If I had to build a bridge across a short chasm, which three people from my life would I choose to help me?” In an accompanying announcement video, Wright talks about how the “plausible behavior” in a game like The Sims still didn’t effectively capture the nuances of people he encounters in real life.

“如果我的祖母参加大满贯比赛对阵塞雷娜·威廉姆斯，谁会赢？”赖特在新闻稿中举例说道。“如果我必须在一条短峡谷上架一座桥，我会选择我生命中的哪三个人来帮助我？”在随附的公告视频中，赖特谈到像《模拟人生》这类游戏中的“合理行为”仍然无法有效捕捉他在现实生活中遇到的人的细微差别。

“It was nowhere near them understanding my claustrophobia or why my sister and I fought so much… We’re actually taking parts of your life, your memories, your experiences, people you know, and turning that into a game that’s very personal.”

“它们远不能理解我的幽闭恐惧症，或者我和姐姐为什么吵架那么多……我们实际上是在提取你生活的一部分、你的记忆、你的经历、你认识的人，并将其转化为一款非常个性化的游戏。”

Wright said MyMems will be able to extract generalized themes from your memories into self-contained areas that Wright compared to the various parts in a theme park: Sports, vacations, injuries, memories, and deaths could all be the basis for their own zones to play in. Players will also have the option to create worlds based on shared memories with family, friends, and co-workers, for example.

赖特表示，MyMems 能够从你的记忆中提取概括性的主题，并将其转化为独立的区域，赖特将其比作主题公园里的不同部分：运动、假期、伤痛、记忆和死亡都可以成为各自游戏区域的基础。例如，玩家还可以选择基于与家人、朋友和同事的共同记忆来创建世界。

Since this is 2026, it shouldn’t be surprising that MyMems is making heavy use of generative AI (with a “proprietary behavior engine” on top) to transform your typed-up memories into simulated locales and characters. In the announcement video, Wright praises the “entirely open-ended” AI systems Gallium is using “to extract from the stories and whatnot based upon the memories you put into the game.”

鉴于现在是 2026 年，MyMems 大量使用生成式人工智能（外加一个“专有行为引擎”）将你输入的记忆转化为模拟场景和角色，这并不令人意外。在公告视频中，赖特称赞了 Gallium 正在使用的“完全开放式”人工智能系统，该系统“能够根据你输入游戏中的记忆，从故事等内容中进行提取”。

But Wright’s focus on mining memories for simulation content predates the current generative AI boom. What has now become MyMems started off as Proxi, a mobile simulation that was first announced in 2018 as “a game of self-discovery, a game where we actually uncover the hidden you.”

但赖特对挖掘记忆以获取模拟内容的关注，早于当前的生成式人工智能热潮。如今的 MyMems 最初名为 Proxi，这是一款移动模拟游戏，于 2018 年首次宣布，被称为“一款自我发现的游戏，一款我们真正揭示隐藏的你的游戏”。

But after years of pre-production work (as outlined in an excellent Vulture piece earlier this year), Wright’s Gallium Studios finally ran out of funding in late 2024, leading to mass layoffs for the small team and an uncertain future for the project. Now the idea has been revived thanks in large part to a partnership with AI middleware firm Artificial Agency.

但在经过多年的前期制作工作后（正如今年早些时候 Vulture 的一篇优秀文章所概述的那样），赖特的 Gallium Studios 在 2024 年底耗尽了资金，导致该小团队大规模裁员，项目的未来也变得不确定。现在，得益于与人工智能中间件公司 Artificial Agency 的合作，这个想法在很大程度上得以复兴。

Until now, that company has mainly been focused on agentic systems that can create “virtual players” to playtest a game or serve as a partner for human players. Now, Artificial Agency’s marketing says the company’s AI tool will be used to create “a personal behavior model and a knowledge graph” from players’ memories, drawing some unsettling comparisons to big tech surveillance.

到目前为止，该公司主要专注于能够创建“虚拟玩家”的代理系统，用于测试游戏或作为人类玩家的合作伙伴。现在，Artificial Agency 的营销宣传称，该公司的人工智能工具将用于从玩家的记忆中创建“个人行为模型和知识图谱”，这引发了一些关于大型科技公司监控的令人不安的比较。

“Meta knows what you click,” the company writes. “Netflix knows what you watch. Nobody knows what you remember. MemDNA learns from your memories locally and only with your permission.”

“Meta 知道你点击了什么，”该公司写道。“Netflix 知道你在看什么。但没人知道你记得什么。MemDNA 仅在获得你许可的情况下，在本地从你的记忆中进行学习。”

Wright said the partnership with Artificial Agency will let Gallium benefit from “off-the-shelf” AI solutions without having to “become an AI research company” in its own right. He also said the collaboration will help limit costs by providing a cheaper option than the “really expensive” frontier models that might be overkill for a project like this.

赖特表示，与 Artificial Agency 的合作将使 Gallium 受益于“现成”的人工智能解决方案，而不必亲自“成为一家人工智能研究公司”。他还表示，这种合作将提供比“非常昂贵”的前沿模型更便宜的选择，从而有助于控制成本，因为那些前沿模型对于这样的项目来说可能有些大材小用。

“Right now AI has opened a vast new design area and I’m enjoying, you know, exploring that as a designer,” Wright said.

“目前，人工智能开辟了一个广阔的新设计领域，作为一名设计师，我很享受探索它的过程，”赖特说道。
