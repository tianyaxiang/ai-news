# The leaked Lenovo Legion Go Beam could be the affordable handheld we need

泄露的联想 Legion Go Beam 可能是我们所需要的经济型掌机。

It looks comparatively cheap — and that might be a good thing in this economy.

它看起来相对便宜——在当前的经济环境下，这可能是一件好事。

For a while there, it seemed like Lenovo had given up on selling handheld gaming PCs, hiking the price of its Legion Go 2 and Legion Go S far higher than other RAM-starved competitors. A decent one can easily cost $1,000 if not $2,000 now. But it looks like Lenovo might be about to announce a relatively cheap handheld once again— the Legion Go Beam.

曾有一段时间，联想似乎放弃了销售掌上游戏电脑，将其 Legion Go 2 和 Legion Go S 的价格抬高到远超其他内存受限的竞争对手。如今，一台像样的设备价格很容易达到 1000 美元甚至 2000 美元。但看起来联想可能即将再次发布一款相对便宜的掌机——Legion Go Beam。

Windows Latest has leaked images of the handheld, and there’s no question it’s designed to be a value alternative to even the company’s least expensive Legion Go S variant. While it’s got the same big stippled grips and triggers for comfort, there’s only one USB-C port on the top instead of two, a comparatively cheap-looking D-pad, no touchpad, and only a single tiny intake and exhaust for its fan.

Windows Latest 泄露了该掌机的图片，毫无疑问，它的设计初衷是作为一种高性价比的替代品，甚至比该公司最便宜的 Legion Go S 版本还要实惠。虽然它保留了同样舒适的大型点状握把和扳机键，但顶部只有一个 USB-C 接口（而非两个），配备了看起来相对廉价的十字键，没有触摸板，且风扇只有一个微小的进气口和排气口。

It still appears to have an SD card slot and a 3.5mm headphone jack, alongside one USB-C port. There’s no SteamOS logo where that would go, so it likely runs Windows instead… but then there’s that name, “Beam.” Perhaps you’ll “beam” your games to it because it’s a streaming and cloud gaming handheld, instead of one that primarily plays games on its own chip? Maybe that’s why it doesn’t need as much airflow as previous handhelds?

它似乎仍然保留了 SD 卡槽和 3.5 毫米耳机插孔，以及一个 USB-C 接口。在原本应有 SteamOS 标志的地方并没有该标志，所以它很可能运行 Windows 系统……但随后是那个名字，“Beam”（光束）。也许你会将游戏“投射”（beam）到它上面，因为它是一款流媒体和云游戏掌机，而不是主要依靠自身芯片运行游戏的设备？这或许就是它不需要像以往掌机那样大风量的原因？

I won’t begin to speculate on the Legion Go Beam’s price, except to say that “cheap” may be relative. The Legion Go S was originally supposed to cost $499, but wound up costing far more except on the occasional sale. The Legion Go 2 was supposed to start at $1,099 but wound up costing $1,500 and up after RAMageddon. Streaming-first handhelds have been far cheaper, though, like the $249 Sony PlayStation Portal.

我不会去猜测 Legion Go Beam 的价格，只能说“便宜”可能是相对的。Legion Go S 最初定价为 499 美元，但除了偶尔的促销活动外，最终售价要高得多。Legion Go 2 原定起售价为 1099 美元，但在“内存灾难”之后，最终售价涨到了 1500 美元及以上。不过，以流媒体为主的掌机要便宜得多，比如售价 249 美元的索尼 PlayStation Portal。

Lenovo has a history of announcing its new gaming devices at CES in January each year, including previous Legion Go handhelds, so I’m expecting to see an official announcement of the Lenovo Legion Go Beam then. Even if so, that doesn’t mean it’ll be available in January — there’s often a many-month lag.

联想有在每年一月的 CES 上发布新款游戏设备的传统，包括之前的 Legion Go 掌机，所以我预计届时会看到联想 Legion Go Beam 的正式发布。即便如此，也不意味着它会在一月份上市——通常会有几个月的滞后期。
