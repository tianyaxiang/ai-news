# AdaFlowLite: Scalable and Non-blocking Inference on Asynchronous Mobile Data

Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.21691 (cs) [Submitted on 16 Nov 2026] Title: AdaFlowLite: Scalable and Non-blocking Inference on Asynchronous Mobile Data Authors: Sicong Liu, Fengmin Wu, Yuan Gao, Bin Guo, Zimu Zhou, Hongkai Wen, Zhiwen Yu.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.21691 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 16 日] 标题：AdaFlowLite：异步移动数据上的可扩展且非阻塞推理 作者：Sicong Liu, Fengmin Wu, Yuan Gao, Bin Guo, Zimu Zhou, Hongkai Wen, Zhiwen Yu。

Abstract: The rise of mobile devices equipped with numerous sensors, such as LiDAR and cameras, has driven the adoption of multi-modal deep intelligence for distributed sensing tasks, such as smart cabins and driving assistance. However, the arrival time of mobile sensory data vary due to modality size and network dynamics, which can lead to delays (if waiting for slow data) or accuracy decline (if inference proceeds without waiting).

摘要：配备激光雷达和摄像头等多种传感器的移动设备的兴起，推动了多模态深度智能在智能座舱和驾驶辅助等分布式感知任务中的应用。然而，由于模态大小和网络动态的影响，移动感知数据的到达时间各不相同，这可能导致延迟（如果等待缓慢的数据）或准确性下降（如果不等待直接进行推理）。

Moreover, the diversity and dynamic nature of mobile systems exacerbate this challenge. In response, we present a shift to opportunistic inference for asynchronous distributed multi-modal data, enabling inference as soon as partial data arrives. While existing methods focus on optimizing modality consistency and complementarity, known as modal affinity, they lack a computational approach to control this affinity in open-world mobile environments.

此外，移动系统的多样性和动态性加剧了这一挑战。为此，我们提出了一种针对异步分布式多模态数据的“机会主义”推理转变，即在部分数据到达时立即进行推理。虽然现有方法侧重于优化模态一致性和互补性（即模态亲和力），但它们缺乏一种在开放世界移动环境中控制这种亲和力的计算方法。

\sysname pioneers the formulation of structured cross-modality affinity in mobile contexts using a hierarchical analysis-based normalized matrix. This approach accommodates the diversity and dynamics of modalities, generalizing across different types and numbers of inputs. Employing an multi-modal lightweight Swin Transformer (MMLST), \sysname facilitates real-time and flexible data imputation, adapting to various modalities and downstream tasks without retraining.

\sysname 开创性地利用基于层次分析的归一化矩阵，在移动环境中构建了结构化的跨模态亲和力。该方法适应了模态的多样性和动态性，能够推广到不同类型和数量的输入。通过采用多模态轻量级 Swin Transformer (MMLST)，\sysname 实现了实时且灵活的数据填充，无需重新训练即可适应各种模态和下游任务。

Experiments show that \sysname significantly reduces inference latency by up to 80.4\% and enhances accuracy by up to 62.1\%, while achieving nearly a 50\% reduction in energy consumption, outperforming status quo approaches. Also, this method can enhance LLM performance to preprocess asynchronous data.

实验表明，\sysname 将推理延迟显著降低了高达 80.4%，并将准确率提高了高达 62.1%，同时实现了近 50% 的能耗降低，优于现有技术方案。此外，该方法还可以增强大语言模型（LLM）预处理异步数据的性能。
