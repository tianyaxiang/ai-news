Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.21648 (cs) [Submitted on 14 Oct 2026] Title: Can an LLM dream? Testing the overfitted brain hypothesis on an agent with text memory Authors: Eric Zou.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.21648 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 10 月 14 日] 标题：大语言模型会做梦吗？在具有文本记忆的智能体上测试过拟合大脑假说 作者：Eric Zou。

Abstract: Hoel (2021) proposed that dreams distort replayed experience so that the brain does not overfit to recent events. We test this hypothesis on an LLM agent (Claude Opus 5.5) that stores what it learns in a text memory and rewrites that memory each night from the day's conversation transcript.

摘要：Hoel (2021) 提出，梦境会扭曲重现的经历，从而使大脑不会对近期事件产生过拟合。我们在一个大语言模型智能体（Claude Opus 5.5）上测试了这一假说，该智能体将其所学内容存储在文本记忆中，并每天晚上根据当天的对话记录重写该记忆。

The agent goes through ten simulated days of conversation built from LongMemEval. We corrupt 5, 10, 20 or 40 percent of the transcript's turns before each nightly rewrite in one of two ways. Detail corruption changes one or two details in a turn to plausible wrong ones, or replaces the turn with one by the same speaker from an earlier day.

该智能体经历了基于 LongMemEval 构建的十天模拟对话。在每晚重写之前，我们以两种方式之一破坏了对话记录中 5%、10%、20% 或 40% 的轮次。细节破坏将轮次中的一两个细节更改为看似合理但错误的细节，或者用同一说话者在更早日期的一轮对话替换当前轮次。

Random-text corruption replaces half of the selected turns with a passage of Moby-Dick and inserts Moby-Dick words into the other half. A size limit on the memory, rising from 19,500 to 117,000 tokens over ten nights, set from human free-recall data, forces it to forget.

随机文本破坏将选定轮次的一半替换为《白鲸记》的段落，并将《白鲸记》中的词汇插入到另一半中。记忆的大小限制（在十天内从 19,500 个标记增加到 117,000 个标记，根据人类自由回忆数据设定）迫使其进行遗忘。

We learned that corruption did not help the agent apply what it learned in earlier conversations to new tasks in any of 30 runs (ten conditions, three different conversation histories). Replacing details in 40 percent of turns lowered recall from 76 to 60 percent.

我们发现，在 30 次运行（十种条件，三种不同的对话历史）中，破坏并没有帮助智能体将早期对话中学到的内容应用到新任务中。替换 40% 轮次中的细节使回忆率从 76% 降低到了 60%。

Random text had no consistent effect on recall, with a paired difference of 0 ± 2 points at 40 percent, because the model recognized it as corrupted and left it out of memory. Replaced details were stored as fact even when another turn held the correct value, and the agent answered 40 percent of those questions correctly, compared with 75 percent without corruption.

随机文本对回忆没有一致的影响，在 40% 的破坏率下配对差异为 0 ± 2 个百分点，因为模型将其识别为已损坏并将其排除在记忆之外。即使在另一轮对话包含正确值的情况下，被替换的细节仍被作为事实存储，智能体对这些问题的回答正确率为 40%，而未受破坏时的正确率为 75%。

These results suggest that a pretrained model can separate corrupted text from real events, so a fair test needs a learner that cannot make this separation as easily, such as a model fine-tuned on the corrupted days or an agent whose days take place in an invented world.

这些结果表明，预训练模型能够将损坏的文本与真实事件区分开来，因此公平的测试需要一个无法轻易做出这种区分的学习者，例如在受损日期上进行微调的模型，或者其日期发生在虚构世界中的智能体。
