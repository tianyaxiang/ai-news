The Trump administration ordered prediction market Kalshi to stop offering event contracts that pay out based on whether President Trump will say a specific word before a particular date. Kalshi halted trading in the contracts while the US Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC) conducts a review into whether the Trump mention contracts violate federal law.

特朗普政府已下令预测市场平台 Kalshi 停止提供基于特朗普总统是否会在特定日期前说出特定词汇而进行结算的合约。在商品期货交易委员会（CFTC）审查此类“特朗普提及”合约是否违反联邦法律期间，Kalshi 已暂停了相关交易。

CNBC reported yesterday that “Kalshi quietly paused trading in a handful of ‘mention’ contracts tied to President Donald Trump at the end of last week at the request of the CFTC.” Kalshi told media outlets that it is complying with the CFTC order. The markets taken down by Kalshi allowed users to wager on whether Trump would say certain words, including nicknames and the names of countries and companies.

CNBC 昨日报道称，“应 CFTC 的要求，Kalshi 在上周末悄然暂停了少数与唐纳德·特朗普总统相关的‘提及’合约交易。”Kalshi 向媒体表示，公司正在遵守 CFTC 的命令。此前，Kalshi 下架的这些市场允许用户押注特朗普是否会说出某些词汇，包括绰号以及国家和公司的名称。

A similar expired event contract that can still be viewed on Kalshi’s website asked whether Trump would use the nicknames “Marjorie Traitor Greene,” “Sleepy Joe,” and “Whack Job” or “Wack Job” before April 2026. The CFTC order issued to Kalshi last week applies broadly to “the TRUMPSAY contract series.” The event contracts in this series include trades in the format, “Will [word] be said by Donald Trump after Issuance and before [date]?”

在 Kalshi 网站上仍可查看的一个类似的已过期合约，曾询问特朗普在 2026 年 4 月之前是否会使用“Marjorie Traitor Greene”、“瞌睡乔”（Sleepy Joe）以及“疯子”（Whack Job 或 Wack Job）等绰号。CFTC 上周向 Kalshi 发出的命令广泛适用于“TRUMPSAY 合约系列”。该系列中的合约包括格式为“唐纳德·特朗普是否会在发行后至 [日期] 前说出 [词汇]？”的交易。

The CFTC order also applied to a specific event contract that asks, “What nicknames will Trump say before 2027?” The CFTC order came several months after the federal agency settled charges against a Trump teleprompter aide who made over $100,000 in Kalshi wagers on what Trump would say in prepared remarks.

CFTC 的命令还适用于一个询问“特朗普在 2027 年前会说出哪些绰号？”的特定合约。在此之前几个月，该联邦机构刚刚与一名特朗普的提词器助手达成和解，该助手曾通过押注特朗普在准备好的演讲稿中会说什么，在 Kalshi 获利超过 10 万美元。

On August 28, the CFTC required the man, Gabriel Perez, to disgorge profits of $107,539.02 and pay a civil penalty of $65,000. “In his position, Perez had access to presidential speeches prior to those speeches being delivered and Perez misappropriated that information—in breach of his duty of trust and confidence—to trade presidential mention market contracts, generating over $107,500 in profits,” the CFTC said at the time.

8 月 28 日，CFTC 要求此人加布里埃尔·佩雷斯（Gabriel Perez）上缴 107,539.02 美元的利润，并支付 65,000 美元的民事罚款。CFTC 当时表示：“佩雷斯利用其职务之便，在总统演讲发表前接触到了演讲内容，并滥用了这些信息——违反了其信任和保密义务——进行总统提及市场的合约交易，获利超过 10.75 万美元。”

Senate Democrats recently said the White House hasn’t taken meaningful action against insider trading, “all while the president and his family stand to personally profit from their participation in prediction markets.” Prediction markets have flourished under the Trump administration’s permissive regulatory approach.

参议院民主党人最近表示，白宫尚未针对内幕交易采取有意义的行动，“而总统及其家人却有望从参与预测市场中获得个人利益。”在特朗普政府宽松的监管方针下，预测市场蓬勃发展。

The CFTC has allowed prediction markets to offer sports gambling nationwide and filed lawsuits against states that attempt to regulate Kalshi under their gambling laws. Kalshi recently dropped Donald Trump Jr. as an advisor, saying it made the decision because Trump Jr.’s venture capital firm increased its investment in its competitor, Polymarket.

CFTC 允许预测市场在全国范围内提供体育博彩，并对试图根据自身博彩法监管 Kalshi 的州提起了诉讼。Kalshi 最近解除了小唐纳德·特朗普的顾问职务，称做出这一决定是因为小特朗普的风险投资公司增加了对竞争对手 Polymarket 的投资。

Kalshi CEO Tarek Mansour also acknowledged that the “optics” of Trump Jr.’s advisory role weren’t good for Kalshi. Trump Jr. still stands to benefit from Kalshi’s success, because he was given equity shares in the company. The CFTC has said that mention markets are easily manipulated but reviews them on a case-by-case basis instead of prohibiting them.

Kalshi 首席执行官塔雷克·曼苏尔（Tarek Mansour）也承认，小特朗普担任顾问角色的“观感”对 Kalshi 不利。小特朗普仍有望从 Kalshi 的成功中获益，因为他持有该公司股份。CFTC 曾表示，提及市场很容易被操纵，但目前采取的是逐案审查而非直接禁止的策略。

Prediction markets can “self-certify” that any new event contract complies with US law and start offering it without waiting for the CFTC’s permission, but the CFTC can halt trading afterward based on reviews of specific contracts. The Commodity Exchange Act (CEA) prohibits the listing of contracts that are readily susceptible to manipulation.

预测市场可以“自我证明”任何新的事件合约符合美国法律，并在无需等待 CFTC 许可的情况下开始提供交易，但 CFTC 随后可以根据对特定合约的审查叫停交易。《商品交易法》（CEA）禁止上市容易受到操纵的合约。

CFTC staff issued a September advisory about the legality of markets on what people will say, and whether they will attend an event or interact with another person. The advisory said these contracts may be viewed as “presumptively readily susceptible to manipulation,” because they “may be controlled by a single individual, a small group of individuals, or persons with access to or influence over the individual whose words, attendance, or interaction determines settlement.”

CFTC 工作人员在 9 月发布了一份关于此类市场合法性的咨询意见，涉及人们会说什么、是否会参加某项活动或与他人互动等市场。该咨询意见称，这些合约可能被视为“推定容易受到操纵”，因为它们“可能受到个人、一小群人，或能够接触到或影响那些决定结算结果的言论、出席情况或互动的人的控制”。

The CFTC said this type of contract requires measures “to prevent manipulation and misappropriation of material non-public information,” and that Kalshi failed to comply with the CFTC staff advisory. About “a week after the Staff Advisory was released, Kalshi listed the Contract [on nicknames] for trading without amending the [TRUMPSAY] Contract Series’ self-certification (or submitting a new self-certification) to make the ‘heightened showing’ of non-manipulability required by the Staff Advisory,” the order said.

CFTC 表示，此类合约需要采取措施“防止操纵和滥用重大非公开信息”，而 Kalshi 未能遵守 CFTC 的工作人员咨询意见。命令称，在“工作人员咨询意见发布约一周后，Kalshi 上线了 [关于绰号的] 合约进行交易，且未修改 [TRUMPSAY] 合约系列的自我证明（或提交新的自我证明），以满足咨询意见所要求的关于‘非操纵性’的‘更高证明标准’。”

The CFTC said it will conduct “a proceeding to determine whether the certification of the Contracts was false—that is, whether the Contracts comply with the Act, including the Core Principles, and Commission regulations thereunder as Kalshi has certified.” So far, “Kalshi has not adequately demonstrated that the Contracts are not readily susceptible to manipulation” as required by law, the order said.

CFTC 表示，将进行“一项程序，以确定合约的证明是否虚假——即合约是否如 Kalshi 所证明的那样，符合《商品交易法》、核心原则及委员会的相关法规。”命令称，到目前为止，“Kalshi 尚未充分证明这些合约不易受到操纵”，而这是法律所要求的。

“Given the available supporting evidence, including that Kalshi has not attempted to establish that the Contracts are non-manipulable with respect to the Staff Advisory’s guidance, the Commission presumptively believes the certification is false,” the order said. The CFTC order is not a determination on whether the Trump mention markets can meet the standard of not being readily susceptible to manipulation.

命令指出：“鉴于现有的支持证据，包括 Kalshi 未尝试根据工作人员的指导意见证明合约不可被操纵，委员会推定该证明是虚假的。”CFTC 的命令并非判定“特朗普提及”市场是否能够达到不易受操纵的标准。

Rather, it is a determination that Kalshi has so far failed to show that the contracts are not easily manipulated. The order “resolves only that, based on the current record, Kalshi has not demonstrated that the Contracts as certified comply with the Act and that the Contracts should not trade while the Commission completes its review,” the CFTC said.

相反，这只是判定 Kalshi 迄今未能证明这些合约不易被操纵。CFTC 表示，该命令“仅解决了一个问题，即根据目前的记录，Kalshi 未能证明其所证明的合约符合《商品交易法》，且在委员会完成审查期间，这些合约不应进行交易。”

We contacted Kalshi and will update this article if it provides a response. Mansour defended the Trump mention contracts at an Axios event on Tuesday, according to Bloomberg. “What the president does say moves markets,” Mansour said.

我们已联系 Kalshi，如果收到回复，我们将更新本文。据彭博社报道，曼苏尔周二在 Axios 的一场活动中为“特朗普提及”合约进行了辩护。曼苏尔表示：“总统所说的话确实会影响市场。”
