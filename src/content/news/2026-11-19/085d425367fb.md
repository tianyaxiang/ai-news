计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.21655 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 10 月 27 日] 标题：基于注意力残差的深度感知激活引导 作者：Shilong Zhao（独立研究员）。

Abstract: Activation steering is a simple, training-free way to modulate LLM behavior, yet existing methods typically inject vectors at a single layer and lack mechanisms for depth-aware propagation or internal verification. We combine two recent ideas - Attention Residuals (AttnRes) providing learnable cross-layer pathways, and Jacobian-lens (J-space) concept readout - into a lightweight depth-aware steering pipeline.

摘要：激活引导是一种简单且无需训练的调节大语言模型（LLM）行为的方法，但现有方法通常在单一层注入向量，且缺乏深度感知传播或内部验证机制。我们将两个近期的概念相结合——提供可学习跨层路径的注意力残差（AttnRes）以及雅可比透镜（J-space）概念读取——构建了一个轻量级的深度感知引导流水线。

On two small open models (Qwen3-0.6B and Qwen3.5-0.8B), distributing a persona vector according to AttnRes-style depth weights yields stronger internal alignment (deeper-layer concept cosine and rank, p<0.0001) and stronger behavioral shifts than single-layer injection. The effect is sign-specific: reverse vectors invert all six metrics, random directions degrade alignment (p<0.0001), and a short LoRA fine-tune erases the imprint.

在两个小型开源模型（Qwen3-0.6B 和 Qwen3.5-0.8B）上，根据 AttnRes 风格的深度权重分配角色向量，比单层注入产生了更强的内部对齐（深层概念余弦相似度和秩，p<0.0001）以及更显著的行为转变。这种效应具有符号特异性：反向向量会使所有六项指标反转，随机方向会降低对齐度（p<0.0001），而短时间的 LoRA 微调则会抹除这种印记。

Injection rewires attention strictly downstream of the injection point (upstream JS = 0.00000). Three practical findings follow. First, a layer-wise geometric decomposition of cosine readouts: at the injection layer 123% of the increase is arithmetic, while 5-11 layers downstream 100% is dynamical. Second, an intensity window: effects are monotone over 0.3-0.7, then generation collapses (617 to 306 to 25 chars). Third, metric discipline: keyword-rule metrics fail LLM-judge validation (kappa <= 0.09), and two rule-based conclusions were retracted.

注入操作严格地重构了注入点下游的注意力（上游 JS = 0.00000）。研究得出三个实践发现。首先，余弦读取的逐层几何分解：在注入层，123% 的增长是算术性的，而在下游 5-11 层，100% 是动态性的。其次，强度窗口：效应在 0.3-0.7 范围内呈单调变化，随后生成能力崩溃（字符数从 617 降至 306 再降至 25）。第三，指标准则：关键词规则指标未能通过 LLM 判别验证（kappa <= 0.09），且两项基于规则的结论已被撤回。

Training the depth weights on a steering objective (200 steps, frozen backbone) raises final alignment 10x on held-out prompts. Cross-persona vectors retain 96-99% of the effect: specificity holds against random, not semantic, directions. We contribute a practical, low-overhead depth-aware steering method with a careful evaluation protocol. Code and data are released.

在引导目标上训练深度权重（200 步，冻结主干网络）使保留提示词上的最终对齐度提高了 10 倍。跨角色向量保留了 96-99% 的效应：特异性在面对随机方向时成立，但在语义方向上不成立。我们贡献了一种实用、低开销的深度感知引导方法，并制定了严谨的评估协议。代码和数据已发布。
