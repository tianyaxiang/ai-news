Senate commerce committee Chair Ted Cruz (R-TX) began a meeting Wednesday meant to confirm President Donald Trump’s nominee to the Federal Communications Commission with a lopsided dais. To his right sat all 14 Republican members of the panel, including Senate Majority Leader John Thune (R-SD). To his left were about a dozen empty chairs — every Democratic member of the panel was absent.

参议院商务委员会主席泰德·克鲁兹（共和党，得克萨斯州）周三主持了一场旨在确认唐纳德·特朗普总统提名的联邦通信委员会（FCC）委员人选的会议，但现场席位分布极不平衡。他的右侧坐着委员会全部14名共和党成员，包括参议院多数党领袖约翰·图恩（共和党，南达科他州）。而他的左侧则有大约十几把空椅子——所有民主党成员均缺席了会议。

Cruz said he’d learned the night before that Democrats intended to boycott the vote, though Democrats say they’d repeatedly raised their concerns about advancing a Republican FCC nominee without a Democratic one. “The administration has shown it’s willing to move Democrats,” Cruz said, pointing to Senate votes scheduled on bipartisan pairs of nominations to the Commodity Futures Trading Commission and the Securities and Exchange Commission.

克鲁兹表示，他前一天晚上得知民主党人打算抵制投票，尽管民主党人称他们曾多次提出担忧，即在没有民主党提名人的情况下推进共和党FCC提名人选是不妥的。克鲁兹指出，参议院已安排对商品期货交易委员会和证券交易委员会的两党提名人选进行投票，并称“本届政府已表现出愿意推进民主党人选的意愿”。

But in statements and comments to reporters outside the committee room, Democrats said that the process has been far from normal. Senate committees often vote on nominations to historically independent agencies in bipartisan pairs. Confirming Danielle Thumann Severs, senior counsel to FCC Chair Brendan Carr, to become a commissioner at the agency would put the FCC at four total members — three Republicans and one Democrat, Anna Gomez.

但在委员会会议室外发表的声明和评论中，民主党人表示这一过程远非正常。参议院委员会通常以两党配对的方式对历史上独立机构的提名进行投票。确认FCC主席布伦丹·卡尔的高级法律顾问丹妮尔·图曼·西弗斯担任该机构委员，将使FCC的总人数达到四人——三名共和党人和一名民主党人安娜·戈麦斯。

Democrats asked for Thumann’s confirmation vote to be withheld until her nomination could be paired with a Democratic nomination. One way to do that would be to renominate Gomez. But so far, the president has not offered any Democratic nominees for the commission. Democrats said they made their concerns clear to Cruz, and added, “This is not a problem we created, but a problem that the White House can solve.”

民主党人要求推迟对图曼的确认投票，直到她的提名能与一名民主党提名人选配对。实现这一目标的一种方式是重新提名戈麦斯。但到目前为止，总统尚未提供任何民主党籍的委员会提名人选。民主党人表示，他们已向克鲁兹明确表达了担忧，并补充说：“这不是我们制造的问题，而是白宫可以解决的问题。”

In addition to the procedural concerns, Democrats worry about Thumann’s work as an adviser to Carr, and whether she was truthful about the matters she worked on. In a letter asking Cruz to delay her confirmation vote, Sen. Ben Ray Luján (D-NM) detailed his concerns. At the November 10th hearing where Thumann answered committee members’ questions, she testified that she had “not worked with the Chairman on any media-related issues,” Luján noted in the letter.

除了程序上的担忧外，民主党人还对图曼作为卡尔顾问的工作，以及她在所负责事务上是否诚实表示怀疑。参议员本·雷·卢扬（民主党，新墨西哥州）在致克鲁兹要求推迟确认投票的信中详细说明了他的担忧。卢扬在信中指出，在11月10日的听证会上，图曼在回答委员会成员提问时曾作证称，她“从未与主席在任何媒体相关议题上进行过合作”。

But documents obtained via a Freedom of Information Act request show that Thumann was “looped in” on various media matters, and “helped draft Skydance’s commitments that facilitated the FCC’s approval of the Skydance-Paramount merger,” Luján wrote. “She also played a role in processing informal complaints related to CBS 60 Minutes alleged news distortion and she apparently orchestrated the Commission’s response to consumers regarding the Jimmy Kimmel controversy.”

但卢扬写道，通过《信息自由法》请求获得的文件显示，图曼曾“参与”了多项媒体事务，并“协助起草了Skydance的承诺，从而促成了FCC对Skydance与派拉蒙合并案的批准”。“她还在处理有关CBS《60分钟》涉嫌新闻歪曲的非正式投诉中发挥了作用，并且显然策划了委员会针对吉米·坎摩尔争议对消费者的回应。”

Speaking to reporters outside of the hearing room ahead of the meeting, Luján accused Thumann of lying to the committee. “The questions that I have is if she was lying about this, what else is she lying about and what else is she hiding?” he said. Luján said he wants both Thumann and Carr to appear before the committee by the end of the year to answer lawmakers’ questions.

在会议开始前，卢扬在听证室外对记者表示，指责图曼向委员会撒谎。“我的问题是，如果她在这一点上撒了谎，她还在哪些方面撒了谎，又在隐瞒什么？”他说。卢扬表示，他希望图曼和卡尔都能在年底前出席委员会会议，回答议员们的提问。
