# Time-Series Models in the Streamhouse: A New Frontier and Use Cases

回到文章：流式仓库中的时间序列模型：新前沿与用例。企业文章，发布于 2026 年 11 月 18 日。点赞 18 +12。作者：Nicholas Fuller, Sean Falconer, Ayhan Sebin, Pankaj Dayama, Roman Vaculin, Bilge Zeren Aksu, Julian Payne, Mountu Jinwala, Wesley M. Gifford, Chandra Reddy, Vijay Ekambaram, Arindam Jati, Robert Metzger。

Real-time forecasting, anomaly detection, and optimization, powered by IBM time-series models with more than 50 million downloads. Now available natively on Confluent, in the cloud and on premises, running on CPUs. Every business generates a continuous record of change: a machine reading, a payment, a sale, an order, or a change in inventory. These observations form time series, and they carry signals that can help teams anticipate what happens next.

由下载量超过 5000 万次的 IBM 时间序列模型驱动的实时预测、异常检测和优化功能，现已在 Confluent 上原生可用，支持云端和本地部署，并可在 CPU 上运行。每家企业都会产生持续的变更记录：机器读数、付款、销售、订单或库存变化。这些观测结果构成了时间序列，其中携带的信号可以帮助团队预测未来。

The challenge is turning those signals into decisions while they still matter. A forecast delivered after a production run, a stockout, or a payment authorization may be accurate, but too late to influence the outcome. IBM and Confluent are bringing time-series foundation models closer to the data as it flows. With Apache Flink, teams can apply forecasting, anomaly detection and semantic intelligence to streaming data using SQL, making model outputs available to the applications and people responsible for the next action.

挑战在于如何在这些信号尚具价值时将其转化为决策。在生产运行、缺货或支付授权之后才交付的预测，即使准确，也已无法影响结果。IBM 和 Confluent 正在将时间序列基础模型引入数据流动的过程中。借助 Apache Flink，团队可以使用 SQL 对流数据应用预测、异常检测和语义智能，从而使模型输出可供负责后续操作的应用程序和人员使用。

Foundation models have changed how organizations work with text, images, and documents. Time-series foundation models extend a similar idea to numerical data by learning general patterns from diverse historical data and then applying that knowledge to new series and tasks. The models can provide a starting point without requiring teams to train a separate model for every series. Depending on the task and deployment, teams can also fine-tune a model or combine its outputs with other data and business logic.

基础模型改变了组织处理文本、图像和文档的方式。时间序列基础模型将类似的理念扩展到数值数据，通过从多样的历史数据中学习通用模式，并将这些知识应用于新的序列和任务。这些模型可以提供一个起点，而无需团队为每个序列训练单独的模型。根据任务和部署情况，团队还可以微调模型或将其输出与其他数据和业务逻辑相结合。

The models can support a range of mission-critical use cases, from helping keep production lines in spec and detecting unusual store activity to improving product-level planning. They can also provide signals to assess card payments and forecast trading volume to help place large orders. These building blocks can be applied across manufacturing, retail, banking, and airlines, bringing insights closer to where decisions are made. 这些模型可以支持一系列关键任务用例，从帮助保持生产线符合规范、检测异常的商店活动，到改进产品级规划。它们还可以提供信号来评估信用卡支付并预测交易量，以帮助下达大额订单。这些构建模块可应用于制造、零售、银行和航空业，使洞察力更接近决策点。了解时间序列和实时上下文如何结合以创造价值：流式仓库（Streamhouse）：代理时代的实时数据与智能。

Businesses operate continuously. Orders are updated, payments arrive, shipments are delayed, and inventory changes throughout the day. Yet the information behind these events often sits across systems that update at different times or rely on periodic batch processes. When applications and AI agents act on outdated or inconsistent information, they risk making decisions that no longer reflect what is happening. A Streamhouse addresses this by continuously capturing, processing, governing, and serving current business context.

企业在持续运营。订单更新、付款到达、货物延迟，库存全天都在变化。然而，这些事件背后的信息往往分散在不同时间更新或依赖定期批处理的系统中。当应用程序和 AI 代理基于过时或不一致的信息采取行动时，它们可能会做出不再反映当前实际情况的决策。流式仓库通过持续捕获、处理、治理和提供当前的业务上下文来解决这一问题。

Confluent’s Data Streaming Platform brings together five capabilities—connect, stream, process, govern, and query—to make that context available to applications, analytics, and agents. Apache Flink transforms and enriches data as it flows, while Lightning Tables provide low-latency access to the current state. Figure 2. Confluent 的数据流平台汇集了五项能力——连接、流式传输、处理、治理和查询——使这些上下文可供应用程序、分析和代理使用。Apache Flink 在数据流动时对其进行转换和丰富，而 Lightning Tables 则提供对当前状态的低延迟访问。图 2：Confluent 上的流式仓库：从现有数据源到代理、应用程序、分析和湖仓在任何地方都能使用的当前业务上下文。

Time-series models add predictive power to this architecture. Flink can prepare the data, invoke forecasting and anomaly detection, and make the results available alongside the events that produced them. This approach can help teams in four ways: -Scale model development. A pretrained model can provide a common starting point across many time series, reducing the need to develop and maintain a separate model for each one. -Refresh predictions as data changes. Forecasts can incorporate recent observations as part of a streaming workflow, subject to the model’s requirements and the pipeline’s configuration. -Put signals closer to decisions. Forecasts and anomalies can be routed to operational applications sooner, where teams can combine them with business context, rules, and human judgment. -Run compact models on CPUs. The models are small enough to run on standard CPUs alongside the streaming pipeline, in the cloud or on premises, which can reduce the need for dedicated GPU infrastructure and simplify deployment.

这种方法可以在四个方面帮助团队：-扩展模型开发。预训练模型可以为许多时间序列提供共同的起点，减少为每个序列开发和维护单独模型的需求。-随数据变化刷新预测。预测可以将最近的观测结果作为流式工作流的一部分纳入，具体取决于模型的要求和管道的配置。-将信号置于更接近决策的位置。预测和异常可以更快地路由到操作应用程序，团队可以在那里将它们与业务上下文、规则和人类判断相结合。-在 CPU 上运行紧凑模型。这些模型足够小，可以在云端或本地与流式管道一起在标准 CPU 上运行，这可以减少对专用 GPU 基础设施的需求并简化部署。
