With just a few weeks remaining, lawmakers are trying to pass new protections for victims of online harm before the end of 2026, targeting deepfake creators and online platforms that block users from filing lawsuits.

随着2026年仅剩几周时间，立法者正试图在年底前通过针对网络伤害受害者的新保护措施，目标直指深度伪造（deepfake）创作者以及那些阻止用户提起诉讼的在线平台。

On Tuesday, members of a Senate Judiciary subcommittee hosted a hearing calling for the end of forced arbitration for online harms. Their immediate target was Roblox, which they accused of evading legal complaints about children allegedly groomed on the platform by enforcing its own forced arbitration clause against users who agreed to the terms as minors, in some cases even under the age of 10.

周二，参议院司法小组委员会的成员举行了一场听证会，呼吁终止针对网络伤害的强制仲裁。他们直接针对的目标是Roblox，指责该公司通过对其用户（包括一些未成年时同意条款、甚至在10岁以下的用户）强制执行仲裁条款，来规避有关儿童在平台上被诱骗的法律投诉。

Separately, a few blocks away, Reps. Alexandria Ocasio-Cortez (D-NY) and Laurel Lee (R-FL) brought hotel heiress Paris Hilton to Capitol Hill to advocate for the Defiance Act, which would let victims of nonconsensual intimate deepfakes sue their creators.

与此同时，在几个街区外，众议员亚历山大·奥卡西奥-科尔特斯（民主党，纽约州）和劳雷尔·李（共和党，佛罗里达州）将酒店女继承人帕里斯·希尔顿带到了国会山，以倡导《Defiance法案》。该法案将允许非自愿亲密深度伪造内容的受害者起诉其创作者。

“The best thing we can do in terms of enforcement is we can open up the courthouse doors,” Sen. Josh Hawley (R-MO), who chairs the Judiciary subcommittee, told reporters after the Roblox hearing. “The more I see these companies evade regulations, the more I think there is nothing as powerful as a jury.”

“在执法方面，我们能做的最好的事情就是敞开法院的大门，”担任司法小组委员会主席的参议员乔什·霍利（共和党，密苏里州）在Roblox听证会后告诉记者。“我越是看到这些公司规避监管，就越觉得没有什么比陪审团更有力量了。”

Roblox has denied that it’s trying to dodge accountability. “Safety has been central to Roblox since we launched. We require age checks for chat, place users under 16 in age-based accounts with protective defaults, and block images and video in chat,” Roblox’s global head of public policy Nicky Jackson Colaço said in a statement.

Roblox否认其试图逃避责任。Roblox全球公共政策主管尼基·杰克逊·科拉索在一份声明中表示：“自我们推出以来，安全一直是Roblox的核心。我们要求聊天进行年龄验证，将16岁以下的用户置于具有保护性默认设置的年龄分级账户中，并屏蔽聊天中的图片和视频。”

Congress has only a little more than three more weeks left in session before the end of the year, and a new Democratic majority will be sworn in come January. Republicans are expected to do their best to push through President Donald Trump’s nominees for judgeships and executive branch posts like FCC commissioner in the lame-duck session, as well as try to pass year-end bills like the National Defense Authorization Act, which often includes a range of other policy items.

国会在年底前仅剩下三周多的会期，而新的民主党多数派将于一月宣誓就职。预计共和党人将尽最大努力在“跛脚鸭”会期内推动唐纳德·特朗普总统提名的法官人选及联邦通信委员会（FCC）委员等行政部门职位，并试图通过《国防授权法案》等年终法案，这些法案通常包含一系列其他政策项目。

Litigation has become one of the more successful ways for survivors of online harm to seek change. Congress has failed to pass many prominent child safety, privacy, and consumer protection bills, though it has begun to tackle the deepfake issue through the Take It Down Act.

诉讼已成为网络伤害幸存者寻求变革的较成功途径之一。尽管国会已开始通过《Take It Down法案》着手解决深度伪造问题，但未能通过许多重要的儿童安全、隐私和消费者保护法案。

At the Roblox hearing, Northwestern University law professor Myriam Gilles, an expert on forced arbitration, said she’s testified before Congress many times on this topic, but “never expected to come before this body to talk about its use against 9 year olds.” Passing either proposal, she said, would have a profound impact. “You will be amazed by how quickly the company will change its behavior once it is sued in public court.”

在Roblox听证会上，西北大学法学教授、强制仲裁专家米里亚姆·吉尔斯表示，她曾多次就此话题在国会作证，但“从未想到会来到这里讨论它如何被用于针对9岁的孩子”。她说，通过任何一项提案都将产生深远影响。“一旦公司在公开法庭被起诉，你会惊讶于它改变行为的速度有多快。”
