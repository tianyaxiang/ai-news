# The ‘GTA 6’ Work Blackout Is Here

Save this storySomewhere out there, right at this moment, a couple is probably still negotiating the final terms of a contract that permits one partner to play Grand Theft Auto VI around the clock for an entire week following its Thursday release. Adults who grew up on the felony-based video game franchise are taking steps to secure sick days—and some, no doubt, are granting their own kids permission to stay home from school (despite warnings from their teachers). Streamers on Twitch and YouTube stand ready to conquer the enhanced and expanded virtual domain of Vice City, seeking out every last Easter egg.

此时此刻，在某个地方，一对情侣可能还在商定一份合同的最终条款，允许其中一方在周四游戏发布后的整整一周内全天候畅玩《侠盗猎车手 6》（Grand Theft Auto VI）。那些伴随着这个以犯罪为主题的游戏系列长大的成年人们，正纷纷设法请病假——毫无疑问，有些人甚至允许自己的孩子逃学在家（尽管老师们发出了警告）。Twitch 和 YouTube 上的主播们也已蓄势待发，准备征服罪恶都市（Vice City）那个增强且广阔的虚拟领域，搜寻每一个彩蛋。

So don’t be surprised if a console owner in your life is curiously unavailable between now and Thanksgiving. The 13-year-wait for this latest installment of the series has felt interminable enough to inspire a meme used in reaction to any surprising or unprecedented news: “We got [fill in the blank] before GTA VI.”

所以，如果你身边有主机玩家在接下来到感恩节这段时间里“离奇失踪”，请不要感到惊讶。为了等待该系列的最新作，人们已经苦熬了 13 年，这段时间漫长到催生了一个网络梗，用来回应任何令人惊讶或前所未有的新闻：“在《GTA 6》发布之前，我们竟然先等到了[填空]。”

Especially within the tech and gaming industries, the expectation is that many developers won’t be clocking in on Thursday or Friday. (GTA VI officially launches for PlayStation 5 and Xbox Series X|S at midnight local time on Thursday, November 19, according to publisher Rockstar Games.)

特别是在科技和游戏行业，人们普遍预计许多开发人员周四和周五都不会打卡上班。（据发行商 Rockstar Games 称，《GTA 6》将于 11 月 19 日周四当地时间午夜正式登陆 PlayStation 5 和 Xbox Series X|S。）

“I’m not going to question whether folks are really sick on their sick days or how they spend a vacation day,” says Mike, an executive of a gaming studio with more than a thousand employees, who requested a pseudonym to avoid damaging professional relationships within the industry. “I want them to spend time engaging with the medium we love, and there’s no question that playing lots of video games helps you make video games.”

“我不会去质疑员工请病假时是否真的生病了，也不会去管他们怎么度过假期，”一家拥有上千名员工的游戏工作室高管迈克（Mike）说道。为了避免损害行业内的职业关系，他要求使用化名。“我希望他们花时间去接触我们所热爱的媒介，毫无疑问，多玩游戏有助于你制作游戏。”

In October, the HR software company BambooHR concluded through data analysis and a survey of salaried workers that the PTO request rate for November 19 is nearly 13 percent higher than equivalent days in past years, while Xbox is running a promotional campaign in which gamers can generate notes from a Dr. Xbox (DJ Khaled in glasses and a lab coat) claiming that they have a “Vitamin G” deficiency and are unable to work.

今年 10 月，人力资源软件公司 BambooHR 通过数据分析和对受薪员工的调查得出结论：11 月 19 日的请假申请率比往年同期高出近 13%。与此同时，Xbox 正在开展一项促销活动，玩家可以生成一份来自“Xbox 博士”（由戴着眼镜、身穿白大褂的 DJ Khaled 扮演）的病假条，声称他们患有“G 维生素”缺乏症，无法工作。

A few companies are getting ahead of the productivity slump; the hardware and software manufacturer Logitech, for example, announced earlier this month that all 7,000 employees would be granted a paid day off for gaming, to be scheduled any time within a year or so. In announcing the policy, the company cited internal studies on the ways that gaming may potentially improve job performance and mental health.

一些公司正在提前应对生产力下滑的问题；例如，硬件和软件制造商罗技（Logitech）本月初宣布，其 7000 名员工都将获得一天的带薪游戏假，可在一年左右的时间内随时安排。在宣布该政策时，该公司引用了内部研究，指出游戏可能有助于提高工作表现和心理健康。

Riot Games, the publisher behind League of Legends and Valorant, has likewise made arrangements for the blockbuster event. “We have an open PTO policy, and we’ve seen leaders encourage their teams to enjoy the first day of release,” Joe Hixson, Riot Games vice president of communications, tells WIRED. “On our main campus, we'll be celebrating with a special-themed lunch menu and snacks that pay homage to the game. We also expect to see people firing it up in our console rooms during breaks and after hours.”

《英雄联盟》和《无畏契约》的发行商拳头游戏（Riot Games）也为这场大作发布做了安排。“我们有开放的请假政策，我们看到领导者鼓励团队去享受游戏发布的第一天，”拳头游戏传播副总裁乔·希克森（Joe Hixson）告诉《连线》（WIRED）杂志。“在我们的主园区，我们将通过特别主题的午餐菜单和向游戏致敬的零食来庆祝。我们还预计在休息时间和下班后，会看到员工在我们的主机室里开玩。”

Riot additionally offers the Play Fund, a stipend of $25 a month for employees to spend on gaming titles across platforms and genres. “It makes us better developers by deepening our understanding of what makes a great player experience,” Hixson says.

此外，拳头游戏还提供“游戏基金”（Play Fund），每月为员工提供 25 美元的津贴，用于购买跨平台和跨类型的游戏。“这加深了我们对什么是优秀玩家体验的理解，从而使我们成为更好的开发者，”希克森说。

Mike tells WIRED that on in-office workdays, the company has a typical absentee rate of 5 to 7 percent due to vacations, illness, travel, and the like. He estimates that on Thursday, it will be more like 10 or 12 percent. “Now, on the Friday, will there be some number of employees who are ‘working’ when they’re at home but also suspiciously slow to answer messages while they have big chunks of their calendar blocked out for ‘deep individual work?’” says the studio leader. “Yeah, probably.”

迈克告诉《连线》，在办公室工作日，由于休假、疾病、出差等原因，公司通常的缺勤率为 5% 到 7%。他估计周四这一比例将达到 10% 或 12%。“那么，到了周五，会不会有一些员工在家‘工作’，但回复消息的速度却慢得可疑，同时他们的日程表上还留出了大块时间用于‘深度个人工作’？”这位工作室负责人说，“是的，很可能会有。”

But it’s hardly unusual in the business for people to select their days off to accommodate a major game release, Mike says. In 2023, he was out for three days to play The Legend of Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom, which he recalls as “paradise.”

但迈克表示，在这一行业，人们为了配合大型游戏发布而选择休假并不罕见。2023 年，他曾请假三天去玩《塞尔达传说：王国之泪》，他回忆那段时光简直是“天堂”。

That said, he notes that the next leap forward for GTA is no ordinary affair. The hype for this franchise, he explains, “breaks containment,” reaching people who have not bought a new title in years.

话虽如此，他指出《GTA》的下一次飞跃绝非寻常之事。他解释说，该系列的热度已经“突破了圈层”，触及了那些多年未曾购买过新游戏的人群。

That’s why even companies without ties to gaming culture have been making allowances for so-called Grand Theft Auto Flu. On Monday, the social team for the luxury real estate firm Serhant posted a video of CEO Ryan Serhant (also the host of the Netflix reality show Owning Manhattan) agreeing with an employee that if the clip garnered 400,000 likes, the office would close for GTA day. “You’ll never get that,” Serhant predicted.

正因如此，即使是与游戏文化毫无关联的公司，也开始为所谓的“侠盗猎车手流感”做出让步。周一，豪华房地产公司 Serhant 的社交团队发布了一段视频，视频中首席执行官瑞安·瑟汉特（Ryan Serhant，也是 Netflix 真人秀节目《拥有曼哈顿》的主持人）与一名员工达成协议：如果视频获得 40 万个赞，办公室就为《GTA》发布日放假一天。“你们永远也达不到那个数字，”瑟汉特预测道。

The post picked up 1.4 million likes within 24 hours, prompting an update on Tuesday: The firm would honor Serhant’s promise. “We are big supporters of our team, their passions both in and out of real estate, and all things culture and tech,” Serhant said in a statement to WIRED, noting that “13 years of anticipation deserves a day off.” The company announced a floating holiday to staff on Wednesday, to be used any time before the end of 2026.

该帖子在 24 小时内获得了 140 万个赞，促使公司在周二发布了更新：公司将兑现瑟汉特的承诺。“我们非常支持我们的团队，支持他们在房地产领域内外的热情，以及所有文化和科技相关的事物，”瑟汉特在给《连线》的声明中说道，并指出“13 年的期待值得放一天假。”该公司周三向员工宣布了一个浮动假期，可在 2026 年底前的任何时间使用。

Also on Wednesday, Arizona Beverages announced a sweepstakes for social media followers who may have felt they couldn’t afford to miss a work shift. Dropping a PTO request for GTA day in the comment section, the company said, would enter that person for a chance to win $500 to cover their lost hours.

同样在周三，亚利桑那饮料公司（Arizona Beverages）为那些觉得无法错过工作班次的社交媒体粉丝宣布了一项抽奖活动。该公司表示，在评论区提交一份《GTA》发布日的请假申请，就有机会赢取 500 美元，以补偿他们损失的工时。

With this level of fanfare, nearly 16 million preorders bringing in revenue of $1.5 billion, and expectations of 45 million sales in its launch quarter, GTA VI is poised to be the most significant entertainment event of the year and the biggest video game of all time. It’s little wonder, then, that some have warned about the global economy slowing down this week as fans ignore their email inboxes and Slack messages to indulge in some extended role-play as streetwise criminals. One economist has estimated that the GTA release could cost the US economy $1 billion in lost productivity.

凭借如此高的热度、近 1600 万份预购带来的 15 亿美元收入，以及首季度 4500 万份的销量预期，《GTA 6》注定将成为年度最重要的娱乐盛事和史上最伟大的电子游戏。因此，难怪有人警告称，本周全球经济可能会放缓，因为粉丝们会无视电子邮件和 Slack 消息，沉浸在扮演街头罪犯的长时间角色扮演中。一位经济学家估计，《GTA》的发布可能会导致美国经济损失 10 亿美元的生产力。
