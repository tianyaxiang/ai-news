Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.21812 (cs) [Submitted on 16 Nov 2026] Title: Sketch, Don't Paint: Allocating Test-Time Compute in Generative Visual Reasoning Authors: Sheng Lu, Guangpin Tao, Tong Lu, Ying Tai.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.21812 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 16 日] 标题：草图而非绘图：生成式视觉推理中的测试时计算分配 作者：Sheng Lu, Guangpin Tao, Tong Lu, Ying Tai。

Abstract: Multimodal models increasingly reason by synthesizing pixels, sketching maze paths within interleaved chains of thought or rolling out video solutions frame by frame. Each visual thought runs the full diffusion schedule inherited from photorealistic synthesis, so iterative denoising dominates inference cost.

摘要：多模态模型越来越多地通过合成像素进行推理，例如在交错的思维链中勾勒迷宫路径，或逐帧展开视频解决方案。每个视觉思维过程都运行继承自照片级真实感合成的完整扩散调度，因此迭代去噪占据了推理成本的主要部分。

We study how to allocate test-time compute along two axes: when to render visual thoughts rather than thinking in text or answering directly, and how many denoising steps each thought requires. Controlled interventions that vary only how the thought is rendered uncover three regimes.

我们研究了如何沿两个维度分配测试时计算：何时渲染视觉思维而非进行文本思考或直接回答，以及每个思维过程需要多少去噪步骤。通过仅改变思维渲染方式的受控干预，我们发现了三种模式。

Causal thoughts carry the plan and become legible early: an 8-step sketch nearly matches a 50-step render in accuracy, commits to the same plan as often as two independent full renders agree, and in video models mostly fails by stalling short of the goal, so the rollout abstains.

因果思维承载着计划并能较早显现：8 步草图在准确性上几乎与 50 步渲染相当，其对计划的确定性与两次独立完整渲染的一致性相当；在视频模型中，它主要因在目标前停滞而失败，因此展开过程会选择放弃。

Decorative thoughts can be omitted without loss, whereas harmful thoughts lower accuracy. Because the regime depends on both model and task, always drawing is suboptimal.

装饰性思维可以被省略而不造成损失，而有害的思维则会降低准确性。由于这种模式取决于模型和任务，因此总是进行绘图并非最优选择。

Across 14 model-benchmark pairs of three interleaved reasoners, answering directly, without any thinking, wins 9 pairs, and choosing among direct, text-only, and visual tiers from ten labeled examples per pair raises mean accuracy from 60.7% (always drawing) to 65.1%.

在三个交错推理器的 14 个模型-基准对中，不进行任何思考直接回答在 9 个对中胜出；通过每对十个标记示例在直接回答、纯文本和视觉层级之间进行选择，平均准确率从 60.7%（总是绘图）提高到了 65.1%。

Where drawing pays, we propose sketch-and-vote, which samples 8-step sketches (a step count chosen without labels) from independent noises until one answer reaches three votes. With this one setting, sketch-and-vote beats full rendering with fewer denoising steps on an image reasoner and two video reasoners, by 8.6 points on VSP spatial planning and by 9.6 and 22.1 points on mazes.

在绘图有益的情况下，我们提出了“草图投票法”（sketch-and-vote），即从独立噪声中采样 8 步草图（无需标签即可选择步数），直到某个答案获得三票。通过这一设置，草图投票法在图像推理器和两个视频推理器上，以更少的去噪步骤击败了完整渲染，在 VSP 空间规划任务上提升了 8.6 个百分点，在迷宫任务上分别提升了 9.6 和 22.1 个百分点。
