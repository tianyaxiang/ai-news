Best Buy is selling Blu-rays directly through its website and in physical stores again after exiting Blu-ray and DVD sales in 2023. The Best Buy website’s movie section currently lists 15 Blu-rays (including one two-pack) that are “sold & shipped by Best Buy.”

在 2023 年退出蓝光和 DVD 销售业务后，百思买（Best Buy）再次通过其网站和实体店直接销售蓝光光盘。百思买网站的电影板块目前列出了 15 款由“百思买销售并配送”的蓝光光盘（包括一套双碟装）。

New titles available include The Odyssey (2026), Project Hail Mary (2026), Supergirl (2026), Super Mario Galaxy (2026), and Obsession (2025). Best Buy is also selling some older titles on Blu-ray, including It’s a Wonderful Life (1943), Saving Private Ryan (1998), and The Godfather (1972).

新上架的影片包括《奥德赛》（2026）、《挽救计划》（2026）、《女超人》（2026）、《超级马力欧银河》（2026）和《痴迷》（2025）。百思买同时也销售一些较老的蓝光影片，包括《生活多美好》（1943）、《拯救大兵瑞恩》（1998）和《教父》（1972）。

Most of the Blu-rays are available for purchase online and can be delivered to an address or picked up at a Best Buy store within a few days. As of this writing, The Odyssey appears to be in stock at Best Buy stores, as it’s currently listed as available for pickup within as little as an hour. Ars Technica reached out to Best Buy to ask whether customers can expect the store to continue selling Blu-rays moving forward.

大多数蓝光光盘均可在线购买，并可在几天内送货上门或在百思买门店自提。截至本文撰写时，《奥德赛》似乎在百思买门店有现货，页面显示最快一小时内即可到店自取。Ars Technica 已联系百思买，询问消费者是否可以期待该店未来继续销售蓝光光盘。

In October 2023, Best Buy announced that it would stop selling Blu-rays and DVDs online and in stores starting in 2024. “To state the obvious, the way we watch movies and TV shows is much different today than it was decades ago. Making this change gives us more space and opportunity to bring customers new and innovative tech for them to explore, discover and enjoy,” a Best Buy spokesperson said in a statement issued at the time.

2023 年 10 月，百思买宣布将从 2024 年起停止在线上和实体店销售蓝光和 DVD。百思买发言人在当时发表的一份声明中表示：“显而易见，我们今天观看电影和电视节目的方式与几十年前大不相同。做出这一改变为我们提供了更多的空间和机会，让我们能够为客户带来新的创新技术，供他们探索、发现和享受。”

Last year, the Best Buy website started selling Blu-rays and DVDs from third parties via its online marketplace. Numerous other companies have reduced or ended their Blu-ray and/or DVD businesses in recent years. Target, for instance, only sells the discs in stores during special occasions (like holidays or for big releases) now. Disney outsourced its DVD and Blu-ray business to Sony, which subsequently stopped selling recordable Blu-rays to people, and DVD rental company Redbox went out of business.

去年，百思买网站开始通过其在线市场销售第三方提供的蓝光和 DVD。近年来，许多其他公司也缩减或终止了其蓝光和/或 DVD 业务。例如，塔吉特（Target）现在仅在特殊场合（如节假日或大片上映时）才在店内销售这些光盘。迪士尼将其 DVD 和蓝光业务外包给了索尼，而索尼随后停止向个人销售可刻录蓝光光盘，DVD 租赁公司 Redbox 也已倒闭。

That said, physical media, including Blu-rays and DVDs, still have a place in many people’s homes. Because digital copies of movies and shows can be plucked from online platforms and people’s libraries that were previously “purchased” (but truly only rented) without warning, enthusiasts continue relying on physical media. Enthusiasts also opt for physical copies of movies and shows for better picture and/or sound quality than what’s available digitally, as well as for bragging rights.

尽管如此，包括蓝光和 DVD 在内的实体媒体在许多人的家中仍有一席之地。由于电影和节目的数字副本可能会在没有预警的情况下从在线平台和人们的库中被移除（尽管之前是“购买”的，但实际上只是租赁），爱好者们依然依赖实体媒体。爱好者们选择实体版电影和节目，也是为了获得比数字版更好的画质和/或音质，以及出于炫耀的心理。

Overall, the sales of Blu-rays and DVDs in the US have been steadily declining, and annual consumer spending dropped 9.3 percent from 2024 to 2025, according to the Digital Entertainment Group (DEG). During this same time, however, consumer spending on 4K Blu-ray discs specifically increased 12 percent year over year, per DEG.

根据数字娱乐集团（DEG）的数据，总体而言，美国的蓝光和 DVD 销量一直在稳步下降，2024 年至 2025 年间的年度消费者支出下降了 9.3%。然而，据 DEG 统计，在同一时期，消费者在 4K 蓝光光盘上的支出却同比增长了 12%。

A resurgence in Blu-ray sales has been associated with the growth of collectible SteelBooks, which are Blu-rays sold in collectible steel boxes, and Gen Z, which, in some cases, are even turning to video rental stores for Blu-rays and DVDs. “We’re seeing a lot of evidence, including at the Criterion Mobile Closet, that more young people are thinking about physical media in a different way,” Peter Becker, president of Criterion, which sells Blu-rays and DVDs online, told the Los Angeles Times in February. “In an age where so much is available to us on demand, it becomes increasingly important to us.”

蓝光销量的回升与收藏版铁盒（SteelBooks，即装在可收藏金属盒中的蓝光光盘）的增长有关，同时也与 Z 世代有关，在某些情况下，他们甚至开始转向视频租赁店寻找蓝光和 DVD。“我们看到了很多证据，包括在 Criterion 移动衣橱活动中，越来越多的年轻人开始以不同的方式看待实体媒体，”在线销售蓝光和 DVD 的 Criterion 公司总裁彼得·贝克（Peter Becker）在二月份告诉《洛杉矶时报》。“在一个我们可以随时随地点播如此多内容的时代，它对我们来说变得越来越重要。”
