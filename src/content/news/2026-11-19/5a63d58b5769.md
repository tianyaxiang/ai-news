Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.21946 (cs) [Submitted on 16 Nov 2026] Title: Conserved Verdicts, Drifting Commitments: Identifying Harmful Overthinking Authors: Jianghong Zhou, Hao Rong, Chen Lin.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.21946 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 16 日] 标题：保守的结论，漂移的承诺：识别有害的过度思考 作者：Jianghong Zhou, Hao Rong, Chen Lin。

Abstract: A reasoning model that has reached a correct answer can lose it by continuing to think. Deciding whether to stop means comparing two probabilities at the same point of a chain: that the answer the model would give if stopped now is correct (its commitment), and that the answer it will finish with is correct (its verdict).

摘要：一个已经得出正确答案的推理模型可能会因为继续思考而丢失该答案。决定是否停止意味着在推理链的同一点比较两个概率：模型如果现在停止所给出的答案是正确的概率（其“承诺”），以及它最终完成时的答案是正确的概率（其“结论”）。

We show that the verdict alone, which is what final-correctness probes estimate, cannot make this comparison. As a forecast of a fixed outcome it has no predictable drift, whether continuing helps or hurts. The commitment-verdict gap equals the expected remaining change in answer quality and the accuracy gained by stopping.

我们证明，仅凭结论（即最终正确性探测器所估计的内容）无法进行这种比较。作为对固定结果的预测，它没有可预测的漂移，无论继续思考是有益还是有害。承诺与结论之间的差距等于答案质量的预期剩余变化以及通过停止所获得的准确率提升。

A second identity splits any accuracy-versus-budget curve into answer change within chains and change in which chains are still running. We measure both on MATH-500. At all 379 points where DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B holds a correct answer, selected without regard to how its chain ends, continuing loses 3.6 points on average (95% CI [2.2, 5.2]), and the loss concentrates in a few problems.

第二个恒等式将任何“准确率与预算”曲线拆分为推理链内部的答案变化，以及哪些推理链仍在运行的变化。我们在 MATH-500 上测量了这两者。在 DeepSeek-R1-Distill-Qwen-1.5B 持有正确答案的所有 379 个点上（无论其推理链如何结束），继续思考平均会损失 3.6 个百分点（95% 置信区间 [2.2, 5.2]），且这种损失集中在少数问题上。

In six reasoning models from three families, on MATH-500 and OlympiadBench and with budgets up to 16,384 tokens, declining accuracy curves come from which chains remain, while the chains still reasoning keep or improve their answers. An oracle stopping rule gains at most +1.8 and +2.5 points at 1.5B and 7B. The framework says what to measure before building a stopping rule.

在来自三个系列的六个推理模型中，在 MATH-500 和 OlympiadBench 上，且预算高达 16,384 个 token 的情况下，准确率曲线的下降源于哪些推理链被保留了下来，而仍在进行推理的链则保持或改善了它们的答案。一个预言式的停止规则在 1.5B 和 7B 模型上最多分别获得 +1.8 和 +2.5 个百分点的提升。该框架指出了在构建停止规则之前需要测量的内容。
