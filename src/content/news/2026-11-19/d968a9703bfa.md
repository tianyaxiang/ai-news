The streaming wars, or the battle to be the top streaming company, are ongoing, according to Ted Sarandos, a co-CEO of what many would consider today’s frontrunner. When it comes to subscribers, no streaming service has more than Netflix, which reported having over 325 million subscribers as of January. But in an interview with Bloomberg published today, Sarandos noted that people are still spending significant time watching traditional broadcast TV.

根据目前被许多人视为行业领跑者的 Netflix 联席首席执行官泰德·萨兰多斯（Ted Sarandos）的说法，流媒体大战，即争夺顶级流媒体公司的战斗，仍在持续进行中。在订阅用户方面，没有任何流媒体服务能超过 Netflix，该公司报告称截至 1 月份拥有超过 3.25 亿订阅用户。但在今天发表的彭博社采访中，萨兰多斯指出，人们仍然花费大量时间观看传统的广播电视。

“The streaming wars are continuing as we go. Half of television engagement in the United States is still linear, old television,” he said, without specifying the source of the statistic. According to analysis from Nielsen, streaming represented 49.6 percent of TV viewing in August, with 36.4 percent coming from broadcast and cable. That still suggests a sizable amount of viewing time for streaming providers to target.

“流媒体大战仍在继续。在美国，一半的电视参与度仍然是线性的、传统的电视节目，”他说，但没有说明该统计数据的来源。根据尼尔森（Nielsen）的分析，8 月份流媒体占电视观看量的 49.6%，而广播和有线电视占 36.4%。这仍然表明流媒体提供商还有相当大的观看时间份额可以争取。

Netflix wants more hits. As Netflix seeks to strengthen its lead against rivals like YouTube and Skydance, there’s extra pressure for it to deliver hit shows and movies. During the Peak TV era, Netflix drove subscribers by offering exclusive hit shows and movies, like House of Cards, Orange Is the New Black, and Beasts of No Nation (2015). Like many of its rivals, Netflix’s reputation for consistently delivering must-watch series and movies has weakened in the 2020s as streaming customers have become inundated with content and struggle to navigate the vast streaming landscape to find relevant content.

Netflix 需要更多热门作品。随着 Netflix 寻求加强其对 YouTube 和 Skydance 等竞争对手的领先地位，它面临着推出热门剧集和电影的额外压力。在“电视巅峰”时代，Netflix 通过提供《纸牌屋》、《女子监狱》和《无境之兽》（2015）等独家热门影视作品来吸引订阅用户。与许多竞争对手一样，Netflix 在 2020 年代持续提供必看剧集和电影的声誉有所减弱，因为流媒体用户已被海量内容淹没，难以在广阔的流媒体环境中找到相关内容。

Sarandos said: I think as Netflix has grown and matured and the business has grown and matured around us, that hit rate really matters. And you’ve got to get ahead of ‘are we on a path to continue [and] maintain that hit rate in the US particularly, but everywhere in the world?’ And I’d say the business is working everywhere in the world, including the US, but we should be doing better in the US than we were. And we’ve got to really focus on our ability to do that.

萨兰多斯表示：“我认为，随着 Netflix 的成长和成熟，以及我们周围业务的成长和成熟，热门作品的命中率确实很重要。你必须提前考虑‘我们是否走在继续并保持这种命中率的道路上，特别是在美国，以及世界各地？’我想说，我们的业务在世界各地（包括美国）都很有效，但我们在美国的表现应该比过去更好。我们必须真正专注于实现这一目标的能力。”

The CEO said that Netflix is focusing on “the ambition of storytelling” rather than “chasing everything that people just happen to spend five seconds on television on” or relying on AI-generated content. “I think what’s going to happen is that people will desire more ambitious, more accomplished storytelling in a world that is flooded with slop,” Sarandos said.

这位首席执行官表示，Netflix 专注于“叙事的雄心”，而不是“追逐人们在电视上偶然停留五秒钟的任何内容”，也不依赖人工智能生成的内容。萨兰多斯说：“我认为未来会发生的是，在一个充斥着劣质内容的世界里，人们将渴望更具雄心、更出色的叙事作品。”

Netflix has used generative AI-based workflows for various production tasks, like creating crowds or historical battle scenes, for hundreds of titles. This has helped Netflix save money while it increased the amount it spends on content this year by about 10 percent. When it comes to streaming users struggling to find something to watch, Sarandos acknowledged that Netflix sometimes “make[s] mistakes about how we present new things” and can “bury things that you might be looking for under new things that you’re not coming for.”

Netflix 已经在数百部作品中将基于生成式人工智能的工作流程用于各种制作任务，例如创建人群或历史战争场景。这帮助 Netflix 节省了资金，同时将其今年的内容支出增加了约 10%。当谈到流媒体用户难以找到想看的内容时，萨兰多斯承认，Netflix 有时“在呈现新事物的方式上犯了错误”，并且可能会“把你可能正在寻找的内容埋没在你不感兴趣的新内容之下”。

He pointed to AI as a tool for content discovery in addition to aiding in the pre-production and post-production of content. Netflix is also being more open toward theater releases than it has been historically. That change was on the forefront when Netflix was trying to buy Warner Bros. Discovery’s (WBD’s) movie studios business (as well as its streaming business) and convince critics that the acquisition wouldn’t mean fewer theater releases.

他指出，人工智能不仅可以辅助内容的预制作和后期制作，还可以作为内容发现的工具。Netflix 对院线发行的态度也比以往更加开放。当 Netflix 试图收购华纳兄弟探索公司（WBD）的电影制片业务（以及其流媒体业务）并试图说服评论家此次收购并不意味着减少院线发行时，这种变化就已显现出来。

Although Netflix pulled out of the deal, it’s still eying an increase in theater releases as an opportunity. During the interview, Sarandos noted that the sequel to Netflix’s most popular movie of all time, KPop Demon Hunters (2025), “will be huge in theaters and big on Netflix.” It has also said that it will put movies in theaters for longer and recently extended the scheduled theater release for Narnia: The Magician’s Nephew from two weeks to seven.

尽管 Netflix 退出了这笔交易，但它仍将增加院线发行视为一个机会。在采访中，萨兰多斯指出，Netflix 史上最受欢迎电影的续集《KPop 恶魔猎手》（2025）“在影院将会非常火爆，在 Netflix 上也会大受欢迎”。该公司还表示，将延长电影在影院的上映时间，最近已将《纳尼亚传奇：魔法师的外甥》的预定院线发行时间从两周延长到了七周。
