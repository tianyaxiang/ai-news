Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.21647 (cs) [Submitted on 14 Oct 2026] Title: FieldGuide: A Corpus Map for Token-Efficient Agentic Document Exploration Authors: Nick Hagar.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.21647 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 10 月 14 日] 标题：FieldGuide：一种用于高效标记代理文档探索的语料库地图 作者：Nick Hagar。

Abstract: Generative AI agents can help journalists explore large document collections. But exploratory workflows can require substantial token use and time, especially when the task is broad and the relevant subquestions are not yet known. This paper introduces FieldGuide, an agent-facing corpus map that organizes a document collection into a topic hierarchy and exposes tools for corpus orientation, scoped search, and bounded reading.

摘要：生成式人工智能代理可以帮助记者探索大型文档集合。但探索性工作流程可能需要大量的标记使用和时间，尤其是在任务广泛且尚未明确相关子问题的情况下。本文介绍了 FieldGuide，这是一种面向代理的语料库地图，它将文档集合组织成主题层次结构，并提供了用于语料库定位、范围搜索和有限阅读的工具。

I evaluate FieldGuide on five journalistic document-exploration tasks using 38 Chicago Police Department public-records documents, comparing it with raw-file access and retrieval-augmented generation. Across the five tasks, FieldGuide used 27% fewer tokens and 41% less elapsed time than raw-file access; relative to RAG, it used 38% fewer tokens and 21% less elapsed time.

我使用 38 份芝加哥警察局的公共记录文档，在五项新闻文档探索任务中评估了 FieldGuide，并将其与原始文件访问和检索增强生成（RAG）进行了比较。在五项任务中，与原始文件访问相比，FieldGuide 的标记使用量减少了 27%，耗时减少了 41%；与 RAG 相比，其标记使用量减少了 38%，耗时减少了 21%。

An accuracy audit identified four minor issues in FieldGuide's responses, comparable to the other conditions. In blind ratings of generated reporting leads, FieldGuide's mean ratings were higher than RAG's and lower than the raw-file condition's. These descriptive results suggest that corpus orientation may reduce runtime token use and elapsed time without severe accuracy failures.

准确性审计在 FieldGuide 的回复中发现了四个小问题，与其他条件相当。在对生成的报道线索进行的盲评中，FieldGuide 的平均评分高于 RAG，低于原始文件条件。这些描述性结果表明，语料库定位可以在不出现严重准确性故障的情况下减少运行时标记使用和耗时。

They also motivate further study of whether selective access limits broad, cross-document discovery. Comments: Subjects: Computation and Language (cs.CL); Multiagent Systems (cs.MA) Cite as: arXiv:2611.21647 [cs.CL].
