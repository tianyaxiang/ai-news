Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.21698 (cs) [Submitted on 16 Nov 2026] Title: FACE: Flow-Guided Angular Channel Estimation Framework for mmWave MIMO Systems Authors: Samitha Gunarathne, Eslam Eldeeb, Nurul Huda Mahmood, Matti Latva-aho.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.21698 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 16 日] 标题：FACE：面向毫米波 MIMO 系统的流引导角度信道估计框架 作者：Samitha Gunarathne, Eslam Eldeeb, Nurul Huda Mahmood, Matti Latva-aho。

Abstract: Acquiring accurate channel state information (CSI) is essential for millimeter-wave (mmWave) multi-input multi-output (MIMO) systems. However, it remains challenging due to high-dimensional channels, limited pilot observations, and measurement noise. To address these challenges, we propose flow-guided angular channel estimation (FACE), a novel channel estimation framework based on flow matching, an emerging generative modeling paradigm that learns continuous transport dynamics between probability distributions.

摘要：获取准确的信道状态信息 (CSI) 对于毫米波 (mmWave) 多输入多输出 (MIMO) 系统至关重要。然而，由于高维信道、有限的导频观测以及测量噪声，这仍然具有挑战性。为了应对这些挑战，我们提出了流引导角度信道估计 (FACE)，这是一种基于流匹配的新型信道估计框架。流匹配是一种新兴的生成建模范式，用于学习概率分布之间的连续传输动力学。

FACE exploits the inherent angular-domain sparsity of mmWave channels and incorporates the learned flow matching model as a generative channel prior. Numerical results show that FACE consistently achieves higher channel estimation accuracy than both conventional estimators and diffusion-based generative estimator across diverse channel and system configurations.

FACE 利用了毫米波信道固有的角度域稀疏性，并将学习到的流匹配模型作为生成式信道先验。数值结果表明，在各种信道和系统配置下，FACE 的信道估计精度始终高于传统估计器和基于扩散的生成式估计器。

Moreover, FACE maintains high estimation accuracy with fewer pilot observations and achieves an average achievable rate close to the perfect-CSI benchmark. Compared with diffusion-based estimation, FACE requires substantially fewer sampling steps while achieving superior or comparable estimation accuracy, reducing inference time and computational cost.

此外，FACE 在较少导频观测的情况下仍能保持较高的估计精度，并实现了接近完美 CSI 基准的平均可达速率。与基于扩散的估计相比，FACE 在实现更优或相当的估计精度的同时，所需的采样步骤显著减少，从而降低了推理时间和计算成本。

Overall, the proposed framework demonstrates the potential of flow matching for accurate, pilot-efficient, and computationally efficient mmWave MIMO channel estimation.

总体而言，所提出的框架展示了流匹配在实现准确、导频高效且计算高效的毫米波 MIMO 信道估计方面的潜力。
