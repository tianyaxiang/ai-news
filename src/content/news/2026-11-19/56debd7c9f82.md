Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.21701 (cs) [Submitted on 16 Nov 2026] Title: Hybrid Quantum Selection of Learning Rates in Language-Model Training: Termination Criteria and an Interaction-Guided Schedule Oracle Authors: Wei-Che Hung.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.21701 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 16 日] 标题：语言模型训练中学习率的混合量子选择：终止准则与交互引导的调度预言机 作者：Wei-Che Hung。

Abstract: Learning-rate choices strongly affect language-model fine-tuning, but the best joint profile across layer groups is generally unknown. This paper studies quantum minimum finding for layer-group learning-rate selection within a hybrid classical-quantum training framework. It combines Durr-Hoyer minimum finding with BBHT search and compares fixed-budget and confidence-based stopping policies using a lookup oracle built from measured profile scores at fixed training checkpoints.

摘要：学习率的选择对语言模型的微调有很大影响，但跨层组的最佳联合配置通常是未知的。本文研究了在混合经典-量子训练框架内，用于层组学习率选择的量子最小值查找。它结合了 Durr-Hoyer 最小值查找与 BBHT 搜索，并使用基于固定训练检查点测得的配置分数构建的查找预言机，比较了固定预算和基于置信度的停止策略。

The paper then introduces an Interaction-Guided Schedule Oracle that scores and marks joint schedules in superposition without a precomputed score table. Classical one-step loss measurements supply the main, pairwise and three-way interaction effects between layer groups. The circuit adds the effects for each schedule, compares the resulting score with the current baseline score, and marks improving schedules for amplitude amplification.

随后，本文引入了一种交互引导的调度预言机，它无需预先计算的分数表，即可在叠加态中对联合调度进行评分和标记。经典的单步损失测量提供了层组之间的主要、成对和三向交互效应。电路将每个调度的效应相加，将所得分数与当前基准分数进行比较，并标记出用于振幅放大的改进调度。

Measured candidates are checked classically before acceptance. Uniform-rate training supplies the initial baseline; using schedules from classical adaptive methods as baselines for joint refinement is left for future work. In closed-loop fine-tuning of a 1.5-billion-parameter language model, each selected schedule drives the next 200 classical training steps. Benchmark accuracies lie within the range observed across five uniform-rate seeds.

测量出的候选方案在被采纳前会经过经典方式的校验。统一速率训练提供了初始基准；将经典自适应方法的调度作为联合优化的基准留待未来研究。在对一个 15 亿参数的语言模型进行闭环微调时，每个选定的调度驱动接下来的 200 个经典训练步骤。基准测试准确率处于五个统一速率种子所观察到的范围内。

The results give a working example of how quantum superposition can support adaptive learning-rate selection during training; the remaining quantum limitations are discussed in the paper.

研究结果提供了一个工作示例，展示了量子叠加如何支持训练过程中的自适应学习率选择；文中还讨论了尚存的量子局限性。
