Black Friday is a great time to save on a new OLED television for your winter movie nights. Though, it turns out that you can already get a great deal on the 65-inch Sony Bravia 8 II, one of our favorite OLED televisions. It’s currently discounted by $1,100 at various retailers, including Amazon, Best Buy, and directly from Sony. That discount brings the price under $2,200, the lowest we’ve seen. You can also save $800 on the 55-inch model, which is typically $2,599.99 (Amazon, Best Buy, Sony), but now costs just under $1,800.

黑色星期五是为您冬日电影之夜选购新款 OLED 电视并节省开支的好时机。不过，事实证明，您现在已经可以以优惠价格买到我们最喜爱的 OLED 电视之一——65 英寸的索尼 Bravia 8 II。目前，包括亚马逊、百思买和索尼官网在内的多家零售商均提供 1100 美元的折扣。这一折扣使价格降至 2200 美元以下，是我们见过的最低价。您还可以在 55 英寸型号上节省 800 美元，该型号通常售价为 2599.99 美元（亚马逊、百思买、索尼），但现在售价不到 1800 美元。

The Bravia 8 II was chosen as the “king of TV” by a panel of judges (including The Verge’s own Nilay Patel) in Value Electronics’ 2025 shootout. The OLED TV has excellent color accuracy, a bright panel, and a 120Hz refresh rate.

Bravia 8 II 在 Value Electronics 举办的 2025 年电视评测中被评审团（包括 The Verge 自己的 Nilay Patel）选为“电视之王”。这款 OLED 电视具有出色的色彩准确度、明亮的面板和 120Hz 的刷新率。

My colleague John Higgins praised the Bravia 8 II for its excellent image quality and engaging picture, though those positive traits are consistent with almost all OLED TVs. What sets this one apart is its impressive image processing, the bold colors from its quantum dot-equipped OLED panel, and that it looks great right out of the box (although he recommends using Professional mode). The screen was awarded the top spot at 2025’s Value Electronics TV Shootout, beating out similar offerings from competitors like Panasonic, LG, and Samsung.

我的同事 John Higgins 称赞 Bravia 8 II 拥有出色的画质和引人入胜的图像，尽管这些优点几乎所有 OLED 电视都具备。这款电视的独特之处在于其令人印象深刻的图像处理能力、配备量子点 OLED 面板所带来的大胆色彩，以及开箱即用的出色显示效果（尽管他建议使用“专业”模式）。该屏幕在 2025 年 Value Electronics 电视评测中荣获榜首，击败了来自松下、LG 和三星等竞争对手的同类产品。

For gaming, the panel can reach up to 120Hz at 4K, with both variable refresh rate and automatic low-latency mode to ensure that your content looks and feels good to play. The connectivity includes two HDMI 2.1 (one with eARC), and two HDMI 2.0 ports, giving you plenty of inputs for consoles and a Blu-ray player, among other gadgets. We spotted some color fringing of certain colors in dark scenes, and lamented the lack of a but it’s an otherwise excellent screen we can easily recommend.

在游戏方面，该面板在 4K 分辨率下最高可达 120Hz，并具备可变刷新率和自动低延迟模式，以确保您的内容看起来和玩起来都很棒。连接功能包括两个 HDMI 2.1 接口（其中一个支持 eARC）和两个 HDMI 2.0 接口，为您连接游戏机、蓝光播放器及其他设备提供了充足的输入端口。我们在暗场景中发现某些颜色存在色彩边缘问题，并对其缺少某项功能感到遗憾，但除此之外，这是一款我们可以轻松推荐的出色屏幕。
