# Nvidia’s rise, visualized

TechAIReportNvidia’s rise, visualizedWhat’s next for Nvidia? Whatever doesn’t rock the mind-bogglingly profitable boat.by Sean HollisterNov 18, 2026, 7:30 PM UTC

英伟达的崛起，可视化呈现。英伟达的下一步是什么？任何不会动摇其惊人盈利能力的事情。作者：Sean Hollister，2026年11月18日，世界标准时间下午7:30。

If someone handed you $8,300 every second, what would you do? That’s how much pure profit Nvidia now makes. By the time you finish reading this paragraph, Nvidia will likely have cleared $45,000 and change. It’s easy to look at Nvidia, a company that just lost its visionary founder CEO due to a series of scandals, and think that the company and maybe the entire AI industry are in terrible trouble. Perhaps so?

如果有人每秒钟给你8300美元，你会怎么做？这就是英伟达目前获得的纯利润。当你读完这段话时，英伟达可能已经赚了超过45000美元。人们很容易认为，这家刚刚因一系列丑闻失去其富有远见的创始人兼首席执行官的公司，以及整个AI行业，正处于巨大的麻烦之中。也许确实如此？

But I want you to understand: Nvidia isn’t your average semi-successful company that might tempt a new CEO to fix things that are broken. It is a company so staggeringly, mind-bogglingly successful it’ll do everything in its power not to rock the boat. There is so much money at stake.

但我希望你明白：英伟达并不是那种普通的半成功企业，可能会诱使新任首席执行官去修复破损之处。它是一家如此令人震惊、如此不可思议的成功公司，以至于它会竭尽全力不去动摇这艘船。因为涉及的资金实在太庞大了。

It’s so much money that when I heard about Jensen Huang’s resignation, my first thought was: I need a way to visualize this jaw-dropping amount of money for you. I want to show you that Nvidia has risen so far, so fast, that rival chipmakers AMD, Intel, and Qualcomm barely qualify as competition now.

这笔钱多到当我听说黄仁勋辞职时，我的第一个想法是：我需要一种方式为你直观地展示这笔令人瞠目结舌的巨款。我想向你展示，英伟达崛起得如此之高、如此之快，以至于竞争对手AMD、英特尔和高通现在几乎算不上是竞争对手了。

Yes, Jensen may have set a trillion dollars of Nvidia’s market cap on fire — from around $5 trillion at the start of November to “only” $3.6 trillion now — but the smoldering company is raking in $700 million in profit a day on average.

是的，黄仁勋可能让英伟达的市值蒸发了一万亿美元——从11月初的约5万亿美元降至现在的“仅”3.6万亿美元——但这家处于风口浪尖的公司平均每天仍能赚取7亿美元的利润。

Put another way: Nvidia could give a $1 million bonus to every one of its 42,000 employees, every three months, without running out of cash. Or, you know, Nvidia could end world hunger or provide clean water for everyone, now that it’s making over $200 billion in pure profit a year.

换句话说：英伟达每三个月可以给其42,000名员工每人发放100万美元的奖金，而不会耗尽现金。或者，你知道，鉴于它每年赚取超过2000亿美元的纯利润，英伟达本可以终结世界饥饿或为每个人提供清洁用水。

On its latest earnings call, the first since cofounder Jensen Huang’s resignation, interim CEO and CFO Colette Kress reinforced the idea that Nvidia expects the good times to keep on rolling. Asked how things might change under new management, she suggested that they wouldn’t: “From a business perspective, it’s business as usual,” she said.

在联合创始人黄仁勋辞职后的首次财报电话会议上，临时首席执行官兼首席财务官科莱特·克雷斯（Colette Kress）强调，英伟达预计好日子将持续下去。当被问及在新管理层下情况会有何变化时，她表示不会有变化：“从商业角度来看，一切照旧，”她说。

Nvidia claims it’ll rake in even more money next quarter, with projected revenue of $130 billion. Profit may be proportionally smaller since gross margins are estimated to drop to 71–72 percent (compared to nearly 75 percent), but Kress said Nvidia intends to get them up as high as 73 percent next year. In other words: This astounding gravy train isn’t going anywhere. At least that’s the plan.

英伟达声称下个季度将赚取更多资金，预计营收为1300亿美元。由于毛利率预计将降至71%至72%（此前接近75%），利润比例可能会有所下降，但克雷斯表示，英伟达计划明年将其提高到73%。换句话说：这列惊人的财富快车不会停下。至少计划是这样的。
