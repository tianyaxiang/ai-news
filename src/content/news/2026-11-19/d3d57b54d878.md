Last year marked the 40th anniversary of Phrack so we went big. We published our anniversary launch release in print at Defcon 33, HOPE, WHY25 and some additional variations at several conferences around the world including AlligatorCon, B-Sides Canberra, Out of the Box, Datenspuren, DistrictCon, OffensiveCon, Recon, and Toorcamp. We included a CTF with exploit challenges and had puzzles and easter eggs included in the zine. We threw parties and had talks at Defcon and WHY25 to celebrate this milestone.

去年是 Phrack 创刊 40 周年，所以我们搞了个大动作。我们在 Defcon 33、HOPE、WHY25 等会议上发布了周年纪念印刷版，并在全球多个会议上推出了不同版本，包括 AlligatorCon、B-Sides Canberra、Out of the Box、Datenspuren、DistrictCon、OffensiveCon、Recon 和 Toorcamp。我们在杂志中加入了包含漏洞利用挑战的 CTF，以及各种谜题和彩蛋。我们还在 Defcon 和 WHY25 举办了派对和讲座，以庆祝这一里程碑。

More importantly, we got new research into people's hands. The point of the relaunch was to get people reading, researching, writing, and contributing again. We wanted the next person picking up Phrack to find something that made them sit down at a computer and start pulling a system apart.

更重要的是，我们将新的研究成果交到了大家手中。重启的意义在于让人们重新开始阅读、研究、写作和贡献。我们希望下一个拿起 Phrack 的人能从中发现一些东西，促使他们坐到电脑前，开始拆解系统。

We owe a huge thanks to the authors, editors, artists, publishing partners, donors, and staff who made it happen. Writing the paper, working through revisions, putting the print editions together, paying the bills, and getting boxes of zines to the right places all take time and effort. Most of that work is invisible when you are holding the finished issue. We appreciate everyone who did it.

我们要向促成这一切的作者、编辑、艺术家、出版合作伙伴、捐赠者和工作人员表示衷心的感谢。撰写论文、反复修改、制作印刷版、支付账单以及将一箱箱杂志送到指定地点，都需要时间和精力。当你拿到成品杂志时，大部分工作都是隐形的。我们感谢每一位为此付出的人。

And to the readers: thank you for giving a shit. Seeing someone get excited about an article, connect it to something they are working on, or realize that they could contribute something themselves makes the effort worthwhile. That exchange is how this thing keeps going. The relaunch worked, and this issue is the follow-through.

对于读者们：感谢你们的关注。看到有人对一篇文章感到兴奋，将其与自己的工作联系起来，或者意识到自己也能做出贡献，这让所有的努力都变得值得。这种交流正是我们持续前行的动力。重启计划成功了，而这一期就是后续的成果。

This is the third release curated by the new staff, and we are not slowing down. This issue is larger than last year's, with 23 main papers and eight additional articles in Line Noise. The subjects range from exploitation and reverse engineering to machine learning, hardware, and the history and culture that grew around this work.

这是新团队策划的第三期发布，我们不会放慢脚步。本期比去年规模更大，包含 23 篇主论文和 8 篇“Line Noise”栏目的额外文章。主题涵盖了漏洞利用、逆向工程、机器学习、硬件，以及围绕这些工作发展起来的历史和文化。

We have container escapes through the vDSO, use-after-free exploitation, QNX userland shellcode, Linux kernel research, and a Chromium renderer sandbox escape through Skia. Elsewhere, the issue takes on Windows smart card architecture, static taint analysis in Binary Ninja, and autonomous underwater vehicles. There is also work examining backdoors in machine learning and what it means to trust an AI system. One paper takes us from PhrackCTF to two WinFSP zero-days, which is a pretty good argument for continuing to put challenges in a zine.

我们有通过 vDSO 实现的容器逃逸、释放后利用（use-after-free）、QNX 用户态 shellcode、Linux 内核研究，以及通过 Skia 实现的 Chromium 渲染器沙箱逃逸。此外，本期还探讨了 Windows 智能卡架构、Binary Ninja 中的静态污点分析以及自主水下航行器。还有关于机器学习后门以及信任 AI 系统意味着什么的研究。其中一篇论文带我们从 PhrackCTF 一路挖掘到两个 WinFSP 零日漏洞，这充分证明了在杂志中保留挑战环节的必要性。

We also have four more articles in Phrack World History, including the story of the infamous SSH1 CRC32 vulnerability featured in The Matrix Reloaded as well as stories about blue boxing free phone calls, a confession of a hacker who tried to backdoor Linux kernel in 2003, and an early discovery of botnets for hire. Our Prophile features Halvar Flake, whose contributions to reverse engineering and security research are legendary.

我们还在“Phrack 世界历史”栏目中增加了四篇文章，包括《黑客帝国 2：重装上阵》中提到的臭名昭著的 SSH1 CRC32 漏洞故事，以及关于蓝盒免费电话、2003 年一名试图在 Linux 内核中植入后门的黑客的自白，以及早期雇佣型僵尸网络的发现。我们的“人物专栏”（Prophile）介绍了 Halvar Flake，他在逆向工程和安全研究方面的贡献堪称传奇。

In 2026, we find ourselves in a time of rapid technological change. It is hard to say what vulnerability research or red teaming will look like in another five years. AI has moved well beyond completing a line of code into agentic systems capable of analyzing source code and reverse engineering binaries to find and exploit vulnerabilities.

2026 年，我们正处于技术飞速变革的时代。很难说五年后的漏洞研究或红队测试会是什么样子。人工智能已经远远超越了补全代码行的阶段，进化为能够分析源代码、逆向二进制文件以发现并利用漏洞的智能体系统。

We can be sure that the future will require us to continue to explore new ideas, create new tools, and push the boundaries of what is possible in the face of new challenges and opportunities created by the advancement of AI and automation systems. As the effort required to put ideas into practice falls, creativity and human intuition forged through experience is valued even higher.

我们可以确定的是，未来需要我们继续探索新想法、创造新工具，并在人工智能和自动化系统进步所带来的新挑战与机遇面前，不断突破可能的边界。随着将想法付诸实践所需的门槛降低，通过经验磨练出的创造力和人类直觉将变得更加宝贵。

We hope that this release of Phrack will inspire new ideas, discussions, and collaborations that will keep the human side of hacking a critical element in shaping the future of technology and society. We will continue to do our part to support the community and provide a platform for sharing knowledge and ideas. We look forward to seeing what the future holds and we are excited to be a part of it.

我们希望这一期 Phrack 能激发新的想法、讨论和合作，使黑客精神中的人文一面成为塑造技术与社会未来的关键要素。我们将继续尽己所能支持社区，并提供一个分享知识和想法的平台。我们期待看到未来会发生什么，并为能参与其中感到兴奋。

Read the papers, try the ideas, and challenge the conclusions. When you find something worth sharing, write it up and send it in. We have another issue to put together, and some of you should be in it!

阅读这些论文，尝试其中的想法，并挑战其结论。当你发现值得分享的内容时，请写下来并发送给我们。我们还要筹备下一期，你们中的一些人应该出现在其中！

To those who have supported us the past three years helping to restore vitality to Phrack, we thank you.

感谢过去三年支持我们、帮助 Phrack 恢复活力的所有人。
