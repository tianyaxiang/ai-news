### Even His Own Officials Don’t Think Trump’s China Trip Will Lead to Progress on AI

在特朗普总统周四与习近平会晤之前，政府官员私下告诉《内环》（Inner Loop），他们认为在缓解人工智能安全和间谍活动担忧方面几乎没有取得进展的空间。

As he departed the White House, Trump left it an open question whether he would press Xi on Chinese interference in AI or the Nvidia scandal, as part of what officials say is an effort to not antagonize China during the summit.

但官员们表示，前英伟达（Nvidia）首席执行官黄仁勋与Megaspeed高管、涉嫌为中国情报机构工作的爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang）之间的绯闻所引发的后果，使得全球两大经济体围绕人工智能发展的任何谈判都变得充满变数。

Back in September, the Trump administration moved to set up an AI incident hotline between Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent and Chinese vice premier He Lifeng to account for situations just like this one. Unfortunately, the communication channel remains at the concept stage and is not operational.

国务卿马尔科·鲁比奥（Marco Rubio）上周告诉记者，自热线宣布以来，美中两国政府已在“技术层面”进行了人工智能安全会谈，但他承认这“仍处于进展中”。

Trump officials have previously discussed putting together a framework that lays out criteria for when each country is obligated to alert the other and enforcement measures to ensure it doesn't only become the US alerting the Chinese.

官员们表示，进展缓慢的部分原因是双方对人工智能最大的风险存在不同看法，而且两国在公开事故方面的动机也各不相同。

In the US, Trump administration officials and tech leaders have been most worried about AI models becoming autonomous and misaligned, where they can operate beyond the bounds of human oversight.

但官员们表示，在中国，人们普遍认为政府官员更担心先进的开源模型被国内的批评者和异见人士利用，从而破坏中国共产党的统治。

In an added complication, the Chinese government has appeared interested in using frontier AI models to gather intelligence domestically and abroad, and would be unlikely to report situations where their models went rogue, officials say.

官员们告诉《内环》，对相互间谍活动的怀疑也加剧了对任何人工智能相关谈判的不信任。中国一直认为美国试图阻碍其人工智能发展——甚至在英伟达丑闻发生之前就是如此。

China has forcefully bristled at allegations that Alice Huang was recruited by the Chinese intelligence agency, calling them “groundless” and a “smear.”

尽管特朗普一直寻求与中国建立更友好的经济关系，但本届政府本周宣布，将推进一项新规定，限制中国远程访问外国数据中心内美国制造的人工智能芯片的能力。

It marked a direct intervention by Trump in response to the alleged role of Megaspeed in the Nvidia scandal and criticism that a firm with ties to China had gained access to advanced AI chips. The rule could also prevent similar companies that might have their corporate parents in China from doing business with Nvidia, WIRED has previously reported.
