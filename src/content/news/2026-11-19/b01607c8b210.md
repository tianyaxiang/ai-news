Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.21644 (cs) [Submitted on 14 Oct 2026] Title: MInfoVisQA: A Multilingual Benchmark for Information-Rich Visual Question Answering Authors: Changjiang Jiang, Jiang Zhou, Xinbin Yuan, Fan Zhang, Tao Feng, Zhuohan Xie.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.21644 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 10 月 14 日] 标题：MInfoVisQA：一个用于信息丰富型视觉问答的多语言基准测试。作者：Changjiang Jiang, Jiang Zhou, Xinbin Yuan, Fan Zhang, Tao Feng, Zhuohan Xie。

Abstract: Multimodal large language models (MLLMs) excel in vision-language tasks; yet, whether they maintain consistent understanding of information-rich visuals (InfoVis) when the surface language changes remains unclear. To quantify this capability, we introduce MInfoVisQA, a parallel benchmark with 8,960 QA configurations derived from 128 seed instances across 24 languages.

摘要：多模态大语言模型（MLLMs）在视觉-语言任务中表现出色；然而，当表面语言发生变化时，它们是否能保持对信息丰富型视觉内容（InfoVis）的一致理解仍不清楚。为了量化这一能力，我们引入了 MInfoVisQA，这是一个包含 8,960 个问答配置的并行基准测试，源自 24 种语言中的 128 个种子实例。

We construct seed-matched, language-parallel visuals and QA pairs by disentangling numerical-structural data from text and programmatically synchronizing translated labels. We evaluate 25 MLLMs under two settings: Language-Aligned QA (LQA, matching visual-text languages) and Cross-Language QA (XQA, fixed QA language with varying visual languages).

我们通过将数值结构数据与文本分离，并以编程方式同步翻译后的标签，构建了种子匹配、语言平行的视觉内容和问答对。我们在两种设置下评估了 25 个 MLLM：语言对齐问答（LQA，匹配视觉-文本语言）和跨语言问答（XQA，固定问答语言但视觉语言各异）。

The results show that aggregate accuracy masks substantial robustness gaps: two models with comparable mean LQA accuracy (80.4%) exhibit performance spreads of 7.8 and 20.3 percentage points across languages. Moreover, all 25 models score lower with non-English visuals under fixed English QA, underscoring the need for cross-language sensitivity metrics.

结果表明，总体准确率掩盖了巨大的鲁棒性差距：两个平均 LQA 准确率相当（80.4%）的模型在不同语言间的表现差异分别为 7.8 和 20.3 个百分点。此外，所有 25 个模型在固定英语问答下处理非英语视觉内容时得分均较低，这凸显了对跨语言敏感度指标的需求。
