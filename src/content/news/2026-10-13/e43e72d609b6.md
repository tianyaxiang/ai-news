A common failure mode in corporate e-learning is optimising for completion rather than competence. Employees click through slides, pass a short quiz, and the LMS records that training happened. That proves attendance; it does not necessarily prove that somebody can make the right decision when the real situation appears.

企业在线学习中一个常见的失败模式是追求“完成率”而非“胜任力”。员工点击幻灯片，通过简单的测验，学习管理系统（LMS）便记录下培训已完成。这只能证明出勤，却未必能证明当真实情况出现时，员工能够做出正确的决策。

The problem is often not the employee. Some courses have been designed primarily to demonstrate completion rather than assess how learning is applied. When the primary goal is "prove that employees saw this content," the result can be passive learning with little opportunity to practise.

问题往往不在员工身上。有些课程的设计初衷主要是为了证明培训已完成，而非评估学习成果如何应用。当主要目标是“证明员工看过这些内容”时，结果往往是缺乏实践机会的被动式学习。

Game-based learning can offer a different approach. Instead of asking employees to absorb information, it asks them to make decisions, face consequences, and adapt their behaviour - the same cognitive processes that drive real-world performance. This post focuses on game-based learning through interactive scenarios.

基于游戏的学习可以提供一种不同的方法。它不再要求员工被动吸收信息，而是要求他们做出决策、面对后果并调整行为——这与驱动现实世界绩效的认知过程如出一辙。本文重点探讨通过交互式场景进行的基于游戏的学习。

Gamification can add elements such as points or rewards to existing training, while game-based learning uses the activity itself to practise decisions. Both can have a role, but they are not the same thing.

游戏化可以在现有培训中加入积分或奖励等元素，而基于游戏的学习则是利用活动本身来练习决策。两者皆有其作用，但它们并非同一回事。

Where Traditional E-Learning Can Fall Short: Before examining the solution, it is worth understanding exactly why the current approach underperforms. The Passive Consumption Problem: Traditional e-learning often follows a pattern: read content, watch a video, answer multiple-choice questions. That can be useful for checking recognition, but many workplace tasks require recall, judgement, and application under imperfect conditions. If the training never asks learners to practise those decisions, the assessment may be measuring the wrong thing.

传统在线学习的不足之处：在探讨解决方案之前，有必要弄清楚为什么当前的方法表现不佳。被动消费问题：传统在线学习通常遵循一种模式：阅读内容、观看视频、回答多项选择题。这对于检查认知能力可能有用，但许多工作任务需要在不完美的情况下进行回忆、判断和应用。如果培训从未要求学习者练习这些决策，那么评估可能衡量的是错误的东西。

The Compliance Trap: When training exists primarily to satisfy regulatory requirements, the incentive structure is backwards. The organisation wants proof of completion, not proof of competence. Employees learn that the fastest path through is to click "Next" repeatedly, skim the quiz questions, and move on. The LMS records a completion. Everybody is satisfied, and nobody has learned anything.

合规陷阱：当培训的存在主要是为了满足监管要求时，激励结构就是本末倒置的。组织想要的是完成证明，而非胜任力证明。员工学会了最快的通关路径就是不断点击“下一步”，浏览测验题目，然后继续。LMS 记录了完成情况。每个人都很满意，但没人学到任何东西。

The Context Gap: Real workplace decisions happen under pressure, with incomplete information, and with consequences for getting it wrong. A multiple-choice question about data protection policy bears no resemblance to the actual moment when an employee must decide whether to share a file externally. Without contextual practice, knowledge remains abstract and inapplicable.

情境鸿沟：现实中的工作决策是在压力下、信息不完整且决策失误需承担后果的情况下做出的。关于数据保护政策的多项选择题，与员工必须决定是否对外共享文件的那一刻毫无相似之处。没有情境化的练习，知识就依然是抽象且无法应用的。

What Game-Based Corporate Training Actually Looks Like: Badges and leaderboards are examples of gamification, but they cannot replace useful practice on their own. For the situations discussed here, we mean designing game-based training around interactive decision-making.

基于游戏的企业培训究竟是什么样子的：徽章和排行榜是游戏化的例子，但它们本身无法取代有效的练习。对于此处讨论的情况，我们指的是围绕交互式决策来设计基于游戏的培训。

Scenario-Based Simulations: The most effective corporate training games place employees inside realistic workplace scenarios and ask them to make decisions. Consider these examples: Compliance training: Instead of reading about data handling policies, the employee manages a simulated inbox where they must identify phishing attempts, classify documents by sensitivity, and decide when to escalate a potential breach. Wrong decisions trigger realistic consequences - a simulated data leak, a regulatory inquiry, a news headline.

基于场景的模拟：最有效的企业培训游戏将员工置于真实的工作场景中，并要求他们做出决策。考虑以下例子：合规培训：员工不再是阅读数据处理政策，而是管理一个模拟收件箱，他们必须识别网络钓鱼企图、对文档的敏感度进行分类，并决定何时上报潜在的违规行为。错误的决策会引发真实的后果——模拟数据泄露、监管调查、新闻头条。

Customer service training: The employee handles a series of increasingly difficult customer interactions, choosing from dialogue options and observing how the conversation unfolds based on their choices. Safety training: Workers navigate a virtual environment identifying hazards, choosing corrective actions, and managing time pressure - simulating the cognitive load of a real work environment.

客户服务培训：员工处理一系列难度递增的客户互动，从对话选项中进行选择，并观察对话如何根据他们的选择展开。安全培训：员工在虚拟环境中导航，识别危险、选择纠正措施并管理时间压力——模拟真实工作环境的认知负荷。

Onboarding: New hires explore a simulated version of company systems and processes, completing tasks that mirror their actual first-week responsibilities. In each case, the employee is actively engaging with the material rather than passively observing it. This is the critical distinction.

入职培训：新员工探索公司系统和流程的模拟版本，完成反映他们入职第一周实际职责的任务。在每种情况下，员工都在积极参与材料，而不是被动观察。这是关键的区别所在。

Branching Decision Trees: Linear training presents the same content to everyone in the same order. Game-based training adapts based on the learner's choices. A branching decision tree allows: Employees who demonstrate competence to progress quickly through familiar material; Employees who struggle with specific topics to receive additional practice in those areas; Different learning paths based on role, department, or experience level; Multiple outcomes that show how different decisions lead to different results. This approach respects the employee's time while ensuring that everyone achieves the required level of competence.

分支决策树：线性培训以相同的顺序向所有人呈现相同的内容。基于游戏的培训则根据学习者的选择进行调整。分支决策树允许：表现出胜任力的员工快速通过熟悉的内容；在特定主题上遇到困难的员工在这些领域获得额外练习；根据角色、部门或经验水平提供不同的学习路径；展示不同决策如何导致不同结果的多种结局。这种方法既尊重了员工的时间，又确保每个人都能达到所需的胜任水平。

Decision-Quality Scoring: Traditional training uses binary pass/fail assessment. You either answered the question correctly or you did not. Game-based training can be designed to record more aspects of decisions, where appropriate: Speed: How quickly did the employee identify the correct course of action? Accuracy: Did they make the right call? Reasoning, if captured: Can learners explain their choice, or does the scenario record enough steps to analyse the route they took? Consistency: Do they make the same types of errors repeatedly? Improvement: How does performance change across repeated scenarios? This may reveal patterns that one score misses, although collecting extra data is only worthwhile if someone can act on it.

决策质量评分：传统培训使用二元制的通过/不通过评估。你要么答对了问题，要么没有。基于游戏的培训可以设计为在适当的情况下记录决策的更多方面：速度：员工识别正确行动方案的速度有多快？准确性：他们是否做出了正确的判断？推理（如果已捕获）：学习者能否解释他们的选择，或者场景是否记录了足够的步骤来分析他们所采取的路径？一致性：他们是否反复犯同类型的错误？改进：在重复的场景中表现有何变化？这可能会揭示单一分数所遗漏的模式，尽管收集额外数据只有在有人能据此采取行动时才有意义。

Key Takeaway: Game-based training can give learners opportunities to practise decisions in realistic scenarios. The game mechanic is not the point - the decision-making practice is the point.

关键要点：基于游戏的培训可以为学习者提供在真实场景中练习决策的机会。游戏机制本身不是重点——决策练习才是重点。

Measurable Outcomes: What L&D Teams Should Measure: The case for scenario-based training should rest on results that can be measured against an existing approach, not on an assumption that it will always be more engaging or effective. Completion Rates: Completion rate is worth measuring, but it should be measured against your existing course rather than against a generic benchmark. If a scenario-based module improves completion, that is useful; if completion rises but decision quality does not, the redesign...

可衡量的成果：学习与发展（L&D）团队应该衡量什么：基于场景的培训的理由应建立在可与现有方法进行比较的结果之上，而不是假设它总是更具吸引力或更有效。完成率：完成率值得衡量，但应将其与现有课程进行比较，而不是与通用基准进行比较。如果基于场景的模块提高了完成率，那是有用的；如果完成率上升但决策质量没有提高，那么重新设计……
