# Fitbit Edge is Google’s middle ground between Fitbit Air and Pixel Watch

Google’s Fitbit lineup expanded on Monday with the introduction of the Fitbit Edge, a new wearable that’s positioned between the Fitbit Air and Pixel Watch. Priced at $179.99, it combines the Fitbit Air’s slim band with an OLED display for checking map directions, notifications, weather, and music controls.

周一，谷歌的 Fitbit 产品线迎来了一款新产品——Fitbit Edge。这款全新的可穿戴设备定位介于 Fitbit Air 和 Pixel Watch 之间。其售价为 179.99 美元，结合了 Fitbit Air 的纤细表带与 OLED 显示屏，可用于查看地图导航、通知、天气和音乐控制。

Like the Pixel Watch and Fitbit Air, the Edge works with Google Health Coach, powered by Gemini. (You’ll need a Google Health Premium subscription to use it.) The coach can use your fitness, health, and medical information to put together weekly routines and workout plans, then adjust them around your schedule. It also comes with Google’s Health Guardian feature, which is designed to spot notable changes in health measurements over time, including indicators related to blood pressure and insulin resistance. (The company announced these features at the Pixel Watch 5 launch in August.)

与 Pixel Watch 和 Fitbit Air 一样，Edge 支持由 Gemini 提供支持的 Google Health Coach（使用该功能需要订阅 Google Health Premium）。该教练可以利用你的健身、健康和医疗信息来制定每周的日常安排和锻炼计划，并根据你的日程进行调整。它还配备了谷歌的 Health Guardian 功能，旨在发现健康指标随时间推移产生的显著变化，包括与血压和胰岛素抵抗相关的指标。（该公司在 8 月份的 Pixel Watch 5 发布会上宣布了这些功能。）

Image Credits:Google Google says the Fitbit Edge can automatically recognize and log up to 100 activities, including runs, bike rides, and walks. The company explains it uses context from your movement to work out what you’re doing and add it to your weekly goals. Plus, the device supports more than 50 exercise modes and can offer on-screen prompts during workouts, such as a nudge to push harder or take a breather.

图片来源：Google。谷歌表示，Fitbit Edge 可以自动识别并记录多达 100 种活动，包括跑步、骑行和步行。该公司解释称，它会利用你的运动背景来判断你正在进行的活动，并将其计入你的每周目标。此外，该设备支持超过 50 种运动模式，并能在锻炼过程中提供屏幕提示，例如提醒你加大强度或休息一下。

The Fitbit Edge’s built-in GPS is 40% more accurate than the previous generation, the company claims, which should make it easier to track outdoor runs. Google says sleep-stage detection is also 15% more accurate than on earlier generations, helping the device provide better sleep summaries and coaching tips. Meanwhile, the battery is rated for up to seven days on a charge, and the wearable is water-resistant to 50 meters.

该公司声称，Fitbit Edge 的内置 GPS 比上一代产品准确度提高了 40%，这应该能让户外跑步的追踪变得更加容易。谷歌表示，睡眠阶段检测的准确度也比早期版本提高了 15%，有助于设备提供更好的睡眠总结和指导建议。同时，该设备的电池续航时间最长可达七天，并具备 50 米防水功能。

The Fitbit Edge is available to preorder through the Google Store, with store availability set for October 27. Color choices include Obsidian, Fog, Olive, and Canyon. There’s also a Stephen Curry Special Edition, priced at $219.99.

Fitbit Edge 现已在 Google Store 开启预购，并将于 10 月 27 日正式上架。颜色选择包括黑曜石色（Obsidian）、雾灰色（Fog）、橄榄色（Olive）和峡谷色（Canyon）。此外还有一款斯蒂芬·库里特别版，售价为 219.99 美元。
