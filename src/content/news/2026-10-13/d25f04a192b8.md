# The OnePlus 16 is the company’s most interesting phone in years

# OnePlus 16 是该公司多年来最令人感兴趣的手机

Today the OnePlus 16 launched in China, the company’s first flagship phone since quitting both the US and European markets this summer. As luck would have it, it also looks like the company’s best phone in some time.

今天，OnePlus 16 在中国发布，这是该公司自今年夏天退出美国和欧洲市场以来的首款旗舰手机。巧合的是，它看起来也是该公司一段时间以来最好的手机。

Like most OnePlus phones, the 16 is positioned around performance, with a Snapdragon 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 chipset, and up to 1TB of storage and 16GB of RAM. That’s backed up by an enormous 9,000mAh silicon-carbon battery, one of the biggest seen in any Android flagship yet. 100W wired and 50W wireless charging complete the power setup.

与大多数 OnePlus 手机一样，16 的定位围绕性能展开，搭载骁龙 8 Elite Extreme Gen 6 芯片组，最高配备 1TB 存储空间和 16GB 内存。此外，它还配备了一块巨大的 9,000mAh 硅碳电池，是目前安卓旗舰机中最大的电池之一。100W 有线充电和 50W 无线充电完善了其电源配置。

On the display front, the phone jumps to a 185Hz OLED panel for the first time. That’ll not only be used for 185fps gaming in compatible titles, but can also run at up to 165Hz throughout the system UI — a first for high refresh rate panels like this.

在显示屏方面，该手机首次采用了 185Hz OLED 面板。这不仅可用于兼容游戏中的 185fps 游戏体验，还可以在整个系统 UI 中以高达 165Hz 的频率运行——这在同类高刷新率面板中尚属首次。

Even the cameras, not usually OnePlus’ strong suit, have had a meaningful upgrade. The main camera has a higher resolution (200 megapixels), larger sensor (1/1.4-inch-type), and faster aperture (f/1.4) than the OnePlus 15’s. That should help, given we found that phone’s camera had frustrating “imitations in low light.” 50-megapixel ultrawide and telephoto cameras round out the rear trio, and the latter also enjoys a big bump to a 1/1.95-inch sensor.

即使是通常并非 OnePlus 强项的摄像头，也进行了有意义的升级。主摄像头拥有比 OnePlus 15 更高的分辨率（2 亿像素）、更大的传感器（1/1.4 英寸型）和更快的快门（f/1.4）。考虑到我们曾发现该手机的摄像头在“弱光环境下存在令人沮丧的局限性”，这应该会有所帮助。5000 万像素的超广角和长焦摄像头构成了后置三摄组合，后者也升级到了 1/1.95 英寸的大传感器。

There’s a design flourish too, with the phone’s silver finish including LED lighting that features a big “16” and what the company claims is a set of galactic coordinates. While the shapes of the lights are fixed, they’ll reportedly flash with different rhythms to notify you of calls, messages, battery level, and more.

手机在设计上也有亮点，其银色机身包含 LED 灯效，呈现出一个巨大的“16”字样以及公司所称的一组银河坐标。虽然灯光的形状是固定的，但据报道它们会以不同的节奏闪烁，以通知用户来电、消息、电量等信息。

The OnePlus 16 is available in China starting from ¥5,699 (about $850). With OnePlus no longer in either the US or Europe, there’s no hope of a launch for the 16 in either market.

OnePlus 16 在中国的起售价为 5,699 元人民币（约合 850 美元）。由于 OnePlus 已不再涉足美国或欧洲市场，该机型在这两个市场推出的希望渺茫。
