Google’s screenless Fitbit Air made a splash, but it’s been three years since the company updated its display-equipped Charge 6. That older tracker is showing its age, lacking newer automatic workout detection, deeper health metrics, and an IP68 rating. That's where the new Fitbit Edge comes in.

谷歌无屏幕版 Fitbit Air 虽然引起了轰动，但该公司距离上次更新配备显示屏的 Charge 6 已经过去了三年。那款旧型号追踪器已显老态，缺乏更新的自动锻炼检测、更深入的健康指标以及 IP68 级防护。而新款 Fitbit Edge 正是为了解决这些问题而生。

Announced on Monday, it is the successor fitness tracker fans with a screen preference have been waiting for. It sits in a sweet spot at $180—not as costly as a Google Pixel Watch but offering a similar experience in a slimmer, pill-shaped design. And unlike the Pixel Watch, it's compatible with Android and iPhones via the Google Health app. Preorders are available now, and it goes on sale on October 27.

该产品于周一发布，是那些偏好屏幕的粉丝们期待已久的后续健身追踪器。它定价 180 美元，处于一个理想的价位——虽然不像 Google Pixel Watch 那么昂贵，但以更纤薄的药丸状设计提供了类似的体验。与 Pixel Watch 不同的是，它通过 Google Health 应用程序兼容安卓和 iPhone。目前已开启预订，并将于 10 月 27 日正式发售。

The Edge has a user-replaceable battery, a Fitbit first, aligning with Google’s efforts to make its Pixel Watch more repairable. (Likely also due to new battery regulations set to take effect next year in the European Union.) Repair options will vary by region and availability.

Edge 配备了用户可更换电池，这是 Fitbit 的首次尝试，与谷歌让 Pixel Watch 更易于维修的努力保持一致。（这也可能是由于欧盟明年即将实施的新电池法规。）维修选项将因地区和可用性而异。

If you find the Pixel Watch’s round face too bulky, the Edge's capsule-shaped alternative might suit smaller wrists better. If the Charge 6’s 1.04-inch display was too small, the Edge upgrades it to a larger 1.34-inch screen. The difference is noticeable, and that extra real estate could make a real impact when glancing at your metrics mid-workout.

如果您觉得 Pixel Watch 的圆形表盘太笨重，Edge 的胶囊状设计可能更适合较细的手腕。如果 Charge 6 的 1.04 英寸显示屏太小，Edge 将其升级为更大的 1.34 英寸屏幕。这种差异显而易见，额外的显示空间在锻炼过程中查看指标时可能会产生实际影响。

The Edge borrows a few more attributes from the Pixel Watch, including an altimeter that uses atmospheric pressure to measure elevation changes. That means it can record elevation gain and loss during outdoor activities, track floors climbed, and add more context to your outdoor stats. It also has a far-field skin temperature sensor, replacing the standard device temperature sensor found in other Fitbits. By bringing some of the Pixel Watch’s more advanced hardware to a fitness-first wearable, Google is giving the Edge a compelling middle-ground position in its lineup at a Fitbit-friendly price.

Edge 从 Pixel Watch 借鉴了更多特性，包括一个利用大气压测量海拔变化的高度计。这意味着它可以记录户外活动中的海拔升降、追踪爬楼层数，并为您的户外统计数据增加更多背景信息。它还配备了远场皮肤温度传感器，取代了其他 Fitbit 设备中常见的标准设备温度传感器。通过将 Pixel Watch 的一些更先进硬件引入这款以健身为主的可穿戴设备，谷歌以 Fitbit 友好的价格为 Edge 在其产品阵容中确立了一个极具吸引力的中间定位。

The Edge inherits the Fitbit Air’s improvements while restoring several Charge 6 features the screenless tracker lacks, including built-in GPS and electrical sensors that work with the electrocardiogram (ECG) app. You can also control music playback on a connected Bluetooth device, as you could with the Charge 6, and Google Maps turn-by-turn directions will arrive later this year.

Edge 继承了 Fitbit Air 的改进，同时恢复了无屏幕追踪器所缺乏的几项 Charge 6 功能，包括内置 GPS 和可配合心电图 (ECG) 应用程序工作的电子传感器。您还可以像使用 Charge 6 一样控制已连接蓝牙设备的音乐播放，而谷歌地图的逐向导航功能将于今年晚些时候推出。

Google also announced more advanced automatic activity tracking for the Edge, using Gemini Intelligence to identify up to 100 workout types. It can track more than 50 exercise modes, up from 40 on its predecessor. Google will also roll out these features to the Pixel Watch 5 and Air. If they work as promised, it means users won't have to log every activity manually.

谷歌还宣布为 Edge 提供更先进的自动活动追踪功能，利用 Gemini 智能识别多达 100 种锻炼类型。它可以追踪超过 50 种运动模式，高于其前代产品的 40 种。谷歌还将向 Pixel Watch 5 和 Air 推送这些功能。如果它们能如承诺般运作，这意味着用户不必手动记录每一项活动。

With a Google Health Premium subscription, the Edge can track blood pressure trends without manual calibration and insulin resistance trends without requiring a blood draw. Google first announced these capabilities alongside the Pixel Watch 5's debut, but they only became available at the end of September. They’re also available on the Air, keeping Google’s screenless tracker up to date as its lineup expands.

通过 Google Health Premium 订阅，Edge 可以在无需手动校准的情况下追踪血压趋势，并在无需抽血的情况下追踪胰岛素抵抗趋势。谷歌在 Pixel Watch 5 发布时首次宣布了这些功能，但它们直到 9 月底才正式可用。这些功能也适用于 Air，确保了谷歌的无屏幕追踪器随着产品线的扩展而保持最新状态。

The seven-day battery life remains unchanged, but it’s one of the Edge’s biggest advantages over the Pixel Watch. Like the Air, it takes about 90 minutes to fully charge, which is half an hour less than the Charge 6.

七天的电池续航时间保持不变，但这是 Edge 相比 Pixel Watch 的最大优势之一。与 Air 一样，它充满电大约需要 90 分钟，比 Charge 6 快了半小时。

One odd omission is a near-field communication (NFC) sensor, which means the Edge doesn’t support contactless payments through Google Wallet. That’s a downgrade from the Charge 6 and a strange function to lose on a fitness tracker that Google is pitching as a step up from its basic Fitbits.

一个奇怪的缺失是近场通信 (NFC) 传感器，这意味着 Edge 不支持通过 Google Wallet 进行非接触式支付。这相比 Charge 6 是一种倒退，对于一款谷歌宣称比基础款 Fitbit 更进一步的健身追踪器来说，失去这项功能显得非常奇怪。

The Edge introduces new digital and analog clock faces, and the Canyon band color returns from the Pixel Watch 5, alongside five other band styles (Active Band, Active Sport Band, Performance Loop Band, Magnet Band, and Metal Links Band). The Stephen Curry collaboration ($220), which also appeared with the Fitbit Air and Pixel Watch 5, is back in the same brown-orange color.

Edge 引入了新的数字和模拟表盘，Pixel Watch 5 上的 Canyon 表带颜色也回归了，此外还有其他五种表带款式（Active Band、Active Sport Band、Performance Loop Band、Magnet Band 和 Metal Links Band）。与 Fitbit Air 和 Pixel Watch 5 合作推出的斯蒂芬·库里联名款（220 美元）也以同样的棕橙色回归。

Alongside the Edge, Google is also giving the screenless Fitbit Air a visual upgrade with a new Metal Mesh band. It’s an even dressier strap than the polyurethane Elevated Modern Band. The Air may have initially been designed to disappear on your wrist, but the new band makes it a statement accessory.

除了 Edge，谷歌还为无屏幕的 Fitbit Air 提供了视觉升级，配备了全新的金属网状表带。这比聚氨酯材质的 Elevated Modern 表带看起来更显档次。Air 最初的设计初衷可能是让它在手腕上“隐形”，但这款新表带让它成为了一款彰显个性的配饰。

If you’re all in on the screenless Fitbit Air, the Edge won’t change your mind. But if you’re not ready to give up a display, the Edge might be a compelling and affordable alternative for both iPhone and Android users. Stay tuned for our review.

如果您已经完全适应了无屏幕的 Fitbit Air，Edge 可能不会改变您的想法。但如果您还没准备好放弃显示屏，那么对于 iPhone 和安卓用户来说，Edge 可能是一个极具吸引力且经济实惠的选择。敬请期待我们的评测。
