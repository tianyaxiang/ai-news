# French supermarket chains say cyberattack forced refrigerator shutdown

Intermarche was one of the groups affected — Gabriel BOUYS / AFP Major French supermarket chains on Monday said they were hit by a cyberattack that caused their refrigerators to shoot up in temperature in hundreds of stores, forcing spoiled food to be thrown away and raising concerns about food security.

Intermarche 是受影响的集团之一——Gabriel BOUYS / 法新社。法国各大连锁超市周一表示，他们遭到了一次网络攻击，导致数百家门店的冰箱温度升高，迫使变质食品被丢弃，并引发了对食品安全的担忧。

Top retailers E. Leclerc, Intermarche and Cooperative U confirmed to AFP that they suffered a cyberattack that Cooperative U's chief executive described as "unprecedented". "Food security requires more attention at all levels," said the EU's food and agriculture commissioner Christophe Hansen on X, condemning the attack and saying such "exploitation of food" was now a "reality".

顶级零售商 E. Leclerc、Intermarche 和 Cooperative U 向法新社证实，他们遭受了一次网络攻击，Cooperative U 的首席执行官将其描述为“史无前例”。欧盟食品和农业专员克里斯托弗·汉森（Christophe Hansen）在 X 上谴责了这次袭击，并表示这种“对食品的利用”现在已成为一种“现实”，并补充说“食品安全需要在各个层面得到更多关注”。

At least 300 shops of Intermarche or its Netto brand were targeted, along with over 100 Cooperative U outlets, and several E. Leclerc stores, the retailers told AFP in separate statements. Paris prosecutors confirmed the attack, which reportedly also hit other major chains, and also indicated it had sparked fires.

零售商在单独的声明中告诉法新社，至少有 300 家 Intermarche 或其 Netto 品牌的门店、100 多家 Cooperative U 网点以及几家 E. Leclerc 门店成为攻击目标。巴黎检察官证实了此次袭击，据报道该袭击还波及了其他主要连锁店，并指出袭击还引发了火灾。

BFM TV said hackers "managed to remotely trigger the forced defrosting of the refrigeration systems in numerous stores". Those branches that were affected will have to "throw away all of the products" that are fresh and frozen, Intermarche told AFP.

BFM 电视台称，黑客“成功远程触发了多家门店制冷系统的强制除霜”。Intermarche 告诉法新社，受影响的分店将不得不“丢弃所有”新鲜和冷冻产品。

Specialist French retail sector news website jebosseengrandedistribution.fr said other retail giants Carrefour Market and Monoprix were affected but the companies have yet to confirm this. E. Leclerc confirmed to AFP "that a cyberattack affected one of its refrigeration equipment suppliers," resulting in "repercussions on the operation of refrigeration systems in several stores".

法国零售业专业新闻网站 jebosseengrandedistribution.fr 称，其他零售巨头家乐福超市（Carrefour Market）和 Monoprix 也受到了影响，但这些公司尚未证实这一点。E. Leclerc 向法新社证实，“一次网络攻击影响了其制冷设备供应商之一”，导致“对几家门店制冷系统的运行产生了影响”。

A source close to the issue said refrigeration control systems from Danish manufacturer Danfoss were targeted. Danfoss told AFP in a statement that most of the affected equipment was "legacy" products which "no longer receive software or security updates".

一位知情人士表示，丹麦制造商丹佛斯（Danfoss）的制冷控制系统成为了攻击目标。丹佛斯在声明中告诉法新社，大多数受影响的设备都是“遗留”产品，这些产品“不再接收软件或安全更新”。

It said its AK-SM800 and AK-SC355 fridge regulator systems were notably affected, with a version of the former using "outdated firmware". It added that it had long advised supermarkets to update from those models. Danfoss gave no figures of how many systems were affected.

该公司表示，其 AK-SM800 和 AK-SC355 冰箱调节器系统受影响尤为严重，其中前者的一个版本使用了“过时的固件”。它补充说，长期以来一直建议超市升级这些型号。丹佛斯没有提供受影响系统数量的具体数据。

A person close to French Prime Minister Sebastien Lecornu said France's cyber security agency had already identified "a vulnerability in software supplied by a Danish company and has contacted the vendor". "It is liaising with its European counterparts, as other countries may be affected," and is seeking "to identify other French companies involved in order to alert them" and "prevent the spread", the source added.

一位接近法国总理塞巴斯蒂安·勒科尔尼（Sebastien Lecornu）的人士表示，法国网络安全机构已经确定了“丹麦公司提供的软件中存在漏洞，并已联系了供应商”。该消息人士补充说，该机构“正在与其欧洲同行联络，因为其他国家也可能受到影响”，并正在寻求“识别其他涉及的法国公司以便提醒他们”并“防止扩散”。

The chief executive of Cooperative U, Dominique Schelcher, described the attack as "unprecedented" and confirmed the cyberattack had affected refrigeration systems since Sunday night. "Targeting equipment essential to the preservation of fresh products and to the food supply of the French people is extremely serious and is unprecedented. It is unacceptable," he wrote on X.

Cooperative U 首席执行官多米尼克·谢尔彻（Dominique Schelcher）将此次袭击描述为“史无前例”，并证实网络攻击自周日晚上以来一直影响着制冷系统。他在 X 上写道：“针对对新鲜产品保存和法国人民食品供应至关重要的设备进行攻击，这是极其严重的，也是史无前例的。这是不可接受的。”

He added that the company's teams were "fully mobilised" to restore the situation as quickly as possible. "An investigation will be necessary to shed full light on this attack," he added. The office of the Paris prosecutor confirmed to AFP that an investigation has been opened into "the large-scale cyberattack affecting the refrigerators of several major food retail chains across France".

他补充说，公司团队已“全面动员”，以尽快恢复正常状况。他补充道：“有必要进行调查，以彻底查清这次袭击。”巴黎检察官办公室向法新社证实，已针对“影响法国多家主要食品零售连锁店冰箱的大规模网络攻击”展开调查。

The probe will help "identify the victims and establish the link with the damage they reported in terms of foodstuff losses and the outbreak of fires", the prosecutor's office said. Intermarche condemned the food waste and financial losses resulting from the attack, without providing figures.

检察官办公室表示，此次调查将有助于“确定受害者，并建立与他们报告的食品损失和火灾爆发等损害之间的联系”。Intermarche 谴责了袭击造成的食品浪费和经济损失，但未提供具体数字。

BFM TV said temperatures were "altered remotely, resulting in the destruction of stocks of fresh and frozen products". The incident comes against the background of growing concern over the potential havoc that can be caused in Europe in all sectors by malicious actors carrying out cyberattacks.

BFM 电视台称，温度被“远程篡改，导致新鲜和冷冻产品库存被毁”。此次事件发生的背景是，人们越来越担心恶意行为者进行网络攻击可能会在欧洲各行业造成潜在的破坏。

President Emmanuel Macron, a strong supporter of Ukraine in its fight against the Russian invasion, last month ordered measures to protect France's critical infrastructure from Russian "hybrid attacks". Industrial equipment such as refrigeration units, long isolated from the internet, are now connected via sensors or remote monitoring systems, Antoine Hautin of cybersecurity consultancy Almond told AFP.

作为乌克兰抗击俄罗斯入侵的坚定支持者，法国总统埃马纽埃尔·马克龙上个月下令采取措施，保护法国关键基础设施免受俄罗斯“混合攻击”。网络安全咨询公司 Almond 的安托万·奥坦（Antoine Hautin）告诉法新社，长期与互联网隔离的制冷装置等工业设备，现在通过传感器或远程监控系统连接起来。

By likely affecting hundreds of units simultaneously, the attack demonstrated "a certain level of sophistication," he added.

他补充说，这次袭击可能同时影响了数百台设备，展示了“一定程度的复杂性”。
