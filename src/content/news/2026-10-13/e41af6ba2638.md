Meta has some Wonder Full new AI models. Meta says the models compete with the industry’s best and will soon power its Muse agent.

Meta 推出了一些名为“Wonder Full”的全新人工智能模型。Meta 表示，这些模型足以与业内顶尖产品竞争，并将很快为其 Muse 智能体提供支持。

Meta is making up for lost time in the AI race. After launching Muse Spark, the first model from its revamped Superintelligence Labs division, and buzzy AI agent Muse, it’s now launching two new models it claims can go toe-to-toe with the industry’s best.

Meta 正在人工智能竞赛中弥补失去的时间。继推出其重组后的超级智能实验室（Superintelligence Labs）的首个模型 Muse Spark 以及备受关注的 Muse 智能体之后，该公司现在又发布了两款新模型，并声称它们能够与业内最优秀的产品一较高下。

The new models, Muse Wonder and Muse Wonder Full, are available for Muse Code subscribers starting today. Wonder can also be accessed through the Meta Model API, Meta said.

这两款新模型 Muse Wonder 和 Muse Wonder Full 从今天起向 Muse Code 订阅用户开放。Meta 表示，用户也可以通过 Meta Model API 访问 Wonder。

Meta says it eventually plans to power its Muse AI agent with Wonder, starting with subscribers, and will develop specialized models it “can bring to everyone using Muse.” It hasn’t shared specifics or provided a timeline for either.

Meta 表示，最终计划利用 Wonder 为其 Muse AI 智能体提供支持，并将首先从订阅用户开始，同时还将开发能够“带给所有 Muse 用户”的专用模型。该公司尚未分享具体细节或提供相关时间表。

The company says its new models can rival leading models from OpenAI and Anthropic, including GPT-6 Astra, Claude Opus 5.5, and Claude Fable 5.1, pointing to results from internal testing on several industry benchmarks. It’s unclear how they stack up against Google’s Gemini 4 Argon, whose capabilities are still coming into focus.

该公司称，其新模型可以与 OpenAI 和 Anthropic 的领先模型（包括 GPT-6 Astra、Claude Opus 5.5 和 Claude Fable 5.1）相媲美，并指出了在多项行业基准测试中的内部测试结果。目前尚不清楚它们与谷歌 Gemini 4 Argon 相比表现如何，后者的能力仍有待进一步明确。

The launches are part of Meta’s aggressive push to reestablish itself as a major force in AI. The company has poured billions into talent and infrastructure and has been steadily expanding its AI lineup, including with image model Muse Image and a heavy focus on bringing useful AI agents to the masses through Muse.

此次发布是 Meta 积极推动自身重回人工智能领域主要力量地位的一部分。该公司已在人才和基础设施方面投入了数十亿美元，并一直在稳步扩大其人工智能产品阵容，包括图像模型 Muse Image，并重点致力于通过 Muse 将实用的人工智能智能体带给大众。
