# No Man’s Sky is coming to the iPhone and iPad

《无人深空》（No Man’s Sky）即将登陆 iPhone 和 iPad。

A decade later, and the No Man’s Sky updates just keep coming.

十年过去了，《无人深空》的更新依然源源不断。

The No Man’s Sky universe is about to get a little bit smaller — but in a good way. Hello Games announced that its long-running sci-fi exploration game will be coming to iOS, with versions for both the iPhone and iPad available starting on October 28th.

《无人深空》的宇宙即将变得“小”一点——但这并非坏事。Hello Games 宣布，这款长盛不衰的科幻探索游戏即将登陆 iOS 平台，iPhone 和 iPad 版本将于 10 月 28 日正式推出。

According to Hello’s Sean Murray, the mobile version of the game is essentially the full No Man’s Sky experience. “I guess I should say this is not No Man’s Sky Lite,” he wrote in a blog post. It includes all of the game’s many, many updates that have come out since launch — like the gigantic Voyagers expansion from last year — and the only major features missing will be multiplayer and the planetary settlements.

据 Hello Games 的肖恩·默里（Sean Murray）称，移动版游戏本质上就是完整的《无人深空》体验。他在博客文章中写道：“我想我应该说明，这不是《无人深空》的简化版。”它包含了自游戏发布以来推出的所有海量更新——比如去年的大型扩展包《旅行者》（Voyagers）——唯一缺失的主要功能是多人游戏和行星定居点。

Murray likens the iOS version to when the game initially launched on the original Switch. “On Switch we have listened to players and built from there, including with a much expanded version on Switch 2,” he says. “Based on feedback and player numbers we will support iOS in a similar way.”

默里将 iOS 版本比作游戏最初在初代 Switch 上发布时的情景。他说：“在 Switch 上，我们倾听了玩家的意见并在此基础上进行构建，包括在 Switch 2 上推出的扩展版本。我们将根据反馈和玩家数量，以类似的方式支持 iOS。”

The game will be playable on the iPhone 11 and 3rd generation iPad Air and above, and the studio says that the download comes in at a surprisingly scant 5GB. It also has newly-designed touch controls, as well as support for controllers, screen casting, and mouse-and-keyboard on the iPad.

该游戏可在 iPhone 11 和第三代 iPad Air 及更高机型上运行，工作室表示其下载大小仅为令人惊讶的 5GB。它还配备了全新设计的触控操作，并支持手柄、投屏以及 iPad 上的鼠标和键盘操作。

Murray also notes that “Perhaps most importantly we’ve put tons of work into battery life and thermodynamics, and we’re super proud that No Man’s Sky can be enjoyed for really long comfortable play sessions.” It’ll be “free to try” at launch, though it’s not clear how much the full version will cost.

默里还指出：“或许最重要的是，我们在电池续航和散热方面投入了大量工作，我们非常自豪《无人深空》能够支持长时间舒适的游戏体验。”游戏发布时将提供“免费试玩”，但目前尚不清楚完整版的价格。

It’s now been a decade since No Man’s Sky first launched, and the game has changed dramatically since its debut. iOS will be joining a huge list of supported platforms, including Mac, PC, and essentially every modern console, and there’s also a VR version available. Just as significant, Hello has continued to keep the game vibrant with a constant stream of free expansions, including the recent Cosmos update in September, which fleshed out the vast expanse of space with more things to explore.

距离《无人深空》首次发布已经过去十年了，这款游戏自问世以来发生了翻天覆地的变化。iOS 将加入庞大的支持平台列表，其中包括 Mac、PC 以及几乎所有现代游戏主机，此外还有 VR 版本。同样重要的是，Hello Games 通过持续不断的免费扩展内容保持了游戏的活力，包括最近 9 月份推出的《宇宙》（Cosmos）更新，它为广袤的太空增添了更多可供探索的内容。
