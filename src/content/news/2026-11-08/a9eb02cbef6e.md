In October, Amazon announced its refreshed Kindle lineup, its first significant design overhaul in years. The highlights included a new edge-to-edge design that eliminated traditional raised bezels, thinner and lighter Paperwhite and Colorsoft models with better displays, and page-turning accessories like a Bluetooth remote and a magnetic cover with page-turn buttons.

10月，亚马逊宣布了其焕然一新的 Kindle 产品阵容，这是多年来首次重大的设计革新。亮点包括消除了传统凸起边框的全新全面屏设计、显示效果更佳且更轻薄的 Paperwhite 和 Colorsoft 型号，以及蓝牙遥控器和带有翻页按钮的磁吸保护套等翻页配件。

However, given the impact of the global memory-chip shortage driven by the AI boom, the new Kindles also debuted with pricing that had increased by $40 to $50 across the lineup. That makes it more difficult for the average consumer to consider buying one of the more premium devices. For that reason, the Kindle I most wanted to get my hands on was the 6-inch entry-level device, which starts at $169.99 for the ad-free version. With the price hikes, this is the Kindle that will remain the most affordable to new e-reader buyers.

然而，受人工智能热潮引发的全球存储芯片短缺影响，新款 Kindle 全系产品的价格上涨了 40 到 50 美元。这使得普通消费者更难考虑购买其中更高端的设备。因此，我最想上手体验的是 6 英寸入门级设备，其无广告版本的起售价为 169.99 美元。在价格普遍上涨的情况下，这款 Kindle 依然是电子书新用户最能负担得起的选择。

The question I weighed was whether or not the basic Kindle is even worth buying at this price when there are arguably better Kindles on the market. The answer, after a couple of weeks of testing, is yes — if you can live without the bells and whistles and prefer a smaller, lighter device. On the 6-inch Kindle, there’s no warm light to reduce eye strain, no color display, no auto-adjusting brightness, no option to use the page-turn cover, and it’s not waterproof. Obviously, because of its size, the screen is smaller, as is the battery. Battery life is 6 weeks compared with the Paperwhite’s 12 weeks or Colorsoft’s 8 weeks.

我权衡的问题是，在市场上已有更好选择的情况下，基础款 Kindle 是否还值得以这个价格购买。经过几周的测试，答案是肯定的——前提是你不需要那些花哨的功能，并且更喜欢小巧轻便的设备。6 英寸的 Kindle 没有用于减轻视疲劳的暖光灯、没有彩色显示屏、没有自动亮度调节、无法使用翻页保护套，也不防水。显然，由于尺寸原因，它的屏幕和电池都更小。其电池续航时间为 6 周，而 Paperwhite 为 12 周，Colorsoft 为 8 周。

Still, even this basic Kindle has seen improvements of its own, mostly from a physical design perspective. The plastic 2026 Kindle is 11% lighter and 14% thinner than the 2024 model, weighing 140g versus 158g and measuring 6.85mm thick versus 8mm. While this seems like a small change, the 18-gram difference is noticeable and particularly welcome for a device you’re likely to carry in your purse or bag. Plus, it makes it easier to hold for hours on end when you’re really binging your way through a book.

尽管如此，这款基础款 Kindle 本身也有所改进，主要体现在物理设计方面。塑料材质的 2026 款 Kindle 比 2024 款轻了 11%，薄了 14%，重量从 158 克降至 140 克，厚度从 8 毫米减至 6.85 毫米。虽然这看起来变化不大，但 18 克的重量差异非常明显，对于经常放在钱包或包里携带的设备来说尤其受欢迎。此外，当你沉浸在书中长时间阅读时，它也更容易握持。

And despite being the “starter” Kindle, the 6-inch basic device still received the new flush-front screen update. This makes it more comfortable to hold in your hand, especially for those long reading sessions. Amazon explained that you don’t have to worry about no longer having a bezel to rest your thumb upon, either, as the new Kindles have been updated to detect a dead zone in the capacitive touch that will readjust to account for the fact that your finger is there.

尽管是“入门级”Kindle，这款 6 英寸基础设备依然获得了全新的纯平屏幕更新。这使得握持手感更舒适，特别是在长时间阅读时。亚马逊解释说，你无需担心没有边框来放置拇指，因为新款 Kindle 已经更新，可以检测电容触控中的盲区，并根据手指的位置进行重新调整。

In terms of the software, the new basic Kindle is a bit snappier than its 2024 predecessor. This is most noticeable when opening books, which Amazon claims to be 30% faster. If you’re coming from the 2019 Kindle Basic, the 2026 version is an even bigger upgrade, as you gain USB-C, more storage (from 4GB to 8GB to now 16GB or 32GB), the new flush screen, and the screen resolution jumps from 167 ppi to 300 ppi. It’s faster, thinner, and lighter, too, and the battery lasts weeks longer.

在软件方面，新款基础款 Kindle 比 2024 款前代产品更灵敏。这一点在打开书籍时最为明显，亚马逊称其速度提升了 30%。如果你是从 2019 款基础版 Kindle 升级而来，2026 款的提升幅度更大，你将获得 USB-C 接口、更大的存储空间（从 4GB/8GB 提升至现在的 16GB 或 32GB）、全新的纯平屏幕，屏幕分辨率也从 167 ppi 跃升至 300 ppi。它更快、更薄、更轻，电池续航也延长了数周。

Another perk about the basic Kindle beyond its small size (this thing fits in your jeans’ back pocket!), is that you can now pay a little more for an upgraded version with an aluminum backing instead of plastic and more storage. The new $189.99 aluminum option comes in Seagrass and Alpine Sky hues, and ships with 32GB of storage. Meanwhile, the $169.99 plastic model offers 16GB of storage. However, even if you go for the less expensive option, you can at least now pick a fun color other than black (aka Graphite), as Amazon has introduced a cheery purple option called Ube.

除了小巧的尺寸（它甚至能塞进牛仔裤后兜！）之外，基础款 Kindle 的另一个优点是，你可以多花一点钱购买升级版，它采用铝制背壳而非塑料，并拥有更大的存储空间。售价 189.99 美元的新款铝制版本有海草绿和高山蓝两种配色，并配备 32GB 存储空间。同时，169.99 美元的塑料版本提供 16GB 存储空间。即便你选择较便宜的版本，至少现在除了黑色（即石墨色）之外，还可以选择一种有趣的颜色，因为亚马逊推出了一种名为“Ube”的欢快紫色。

The big thing to consider here is whether you want to spend this much on a Kindle that lacks a warm light. While this is something of a personal preference, for some readers, no warm light is a dealbreaker. The warm light shifts the color tone of the Kindle’s display from a cooler bluish-white to a softer amber glow, which is meant to help reduce eye strain and improve reading conditions in low light or at night.

这里需要考虑的重要一点是，你是否愿意花这么多钱买一台没有暖光灯的 Kindle。虽然这在一定程度上是个人偏好，但对一些读者来说，没有暖光灯是无法接受的。暖光灯可以将 Kindle 显示屏的色调从较冷的蓝白色转变为更柔和的琥珀色光，旨在帮助减轻视疲劳并改善弱光环境或夜间的阅读条件。
