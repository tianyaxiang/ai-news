This post discusses a public METR report on AI R&D acceleration at Anthropic. I did not work on this project or see the unredacted report. All opinions are my own and do not represent METR or the report authors. Thanks to Ryan Greenblatt for discussion, feedback, and help with modeling, and Daniel Kokotajlo, Eli Lifland, and Hjalmar Wjik for comments.

本文讨论了一份关于 Anthropic 公司人工智能研发加速的公开 METR 报告。我并未参与该项目，也未见过未经删减的报告。文中所有观点仅代表我个人，不代表 METR 或报告作者。感谢 Ryan Greenblatt 在讨论、反馈和建模方面提供的帮助，以及 Daniel Kokotajlo、Eli Lifland 和 Hjalmar Wjik 提供的评论。

Earlier this week, Anthropic stated in their S1 that they “may imminently meet” the threshold of “dramatically accelerated progress due to AI” as defined in their Responsible Scaling Policy. In their latest RSP (effective July 2026), say this threshold will be met when: They observe or expect overall capabilities progress to be going twice as fast as the fastest sustained rate before AI was contributing much to AI R&D. This is plausibly attributable to AI automating AI R&D, rather than acceleration in compute or other inputs.

本周早些时候，Anthropic 在其 S1 文件中表示，他们“可能即将达到”其《负责任扩展政策》（RSP）中定义的“因人工智能而实现大幅加速进展”的阈值。在他们最新的 RSP（2026 年 7 月生效）中，该阈值的定义是：观察到或预期整体能力进展速度达到人工智能对研发贡献显著之前最快持续速度的两倍。这很可能是由于人工智能实现了研发自动化，而非算力或其他投入的加速所致。

For the last few months, some of my colleagues at METR have been working on a deep-access investigation to understand how much Anthropic is being accelerated by its AI systems. From my reading of their report and Anthropic’s RSP, it looks like the “dramatic acceleration” threshold has already been crossed.

过去几个月里，我在 METR 的一些同事一直在进行一项深度调查，旨在了解 Anthropic 的人工智能系统在多大程度上加速了其研发进程。根据我对他们报告和 Anthropic RSP 的解读，该“大幅加速”的阈值似乎已经被突破。

The report authors found that: Between October 2025 and October 2026, capabilities progress at Anthropic has been going about twice as fast as it had been over the previous twelve months. The measurement they report is an internal version of the Epoch Capabilities Index called AECI; a wide range of other supporting evidence for acceleration has been redacted. They said that this redacted evidence was "a majority of the evidence [they] found most informative."

报告作者发现：2025 年 10 月至 2026 年 10 月期间，Anthropic 的能力进展速度约为前十二个月的两倍。他们报告中使用的衡量标准是 Epoch 能力指数的内部版本，称为 AECI；其他大量支持加速的证据已被删减。他们表示，这些被删减的证据是“他们认为最有参考价值的证据中的大部分”。

While the pace may have initially started picking up for a mix of reasons, AI automation is probably the large majority of the reason why capabilities are still improving at this faster pace today. Their reasons for believing this have been redacted.

虽然最初的提速可能是由多种原因共同促成的，但人工智能自动化很可能是目前能力仍能保持这种较快提升速度的主要原因。他们得出这一结论的理由已被删减。

This is a much bigger deal than it might sound at first. I think this report is the most important update we have gotten about AI risk in the last couple of years — much bigger than the Hugging Face attack. Naively, it points toward much shorter timelines than most external researchers (including the AI 2027 team) have projected.

这比听起来要严重得多。我认为这份报告是我们过去几年中收到的关于人工智能风险的最重要更新——其重要性远超 Hugging Face 攻击事件。简单来看，它指向的时间线比大多数外部研究人员（包括 AI 2027 团队）所预测的要短得多。

Importantly, if AI is roughly doubling capabilities progress overall, it must be speeding up human researchers by much more than that.

重要的是，如果人工智能使整体能力进展速度翻了一番，那么它对人类研究人员的加速作用必然远不止于此。

Researchers work on improving algorithms and architectures; Anthropic has sometimes referred to these innovations as “compute multipliers.” Overall AI capabilities (“effective compute”) is the product of compute multipliers and training compute. Because the compute multipliers are only one input into capabilities, this means that if AI capabilities are advancing twice as fast overall because of software progress, the rate of growth in compute multipliers would need to be more than twice as fast: perhaps ~2.5-3x faster based on reasonable assumptions. Since there’s uncertainty about the size of speedup and how much of it comes from AI, I’ll go with ~2-3x.

研究人员致力于改进算法和架构；Anthropic 有时将这些创新称为“算力乘数”。整体人工智能能力（“有效算力”）是算力乘数与训练算力的乘积。由于算力乘数只是能力的投入之一，这意味着如果人工智能能力因软件进步而整体提升了两倍，那么算力乘数的增长率就需要超过两倍：基于合理的假设，可能需要快约 2.5 到 3 倍。由于对加速幅度和人工智能贡献占比存在不确定性，我将其设定为约 2 到 3 倍。

Similarly, researcher labor is only one input to discovering these compute multipliers: they need to test their ideas, which requires compute for experiments. If we assume a very simple Cobb-Douglas model where experiment compute and labor are similarly important, that means the uplift to labor roughly needs to be the square of the acceleration in compute multipliers. This would imply that we would need a (~2-3x)^2 = ~4-9x speed up in labor to make overall AI capabilities go twice as fast.

同样，研究人员的劳动只是发现这些算力乘数的投入之一：他们需要测试自己的想法，这需要实验算力。如果我们假设一个非常简单的柯布-道格拉斯模型，即实验算力和劳动力同样重要，那么这意味着劳动力的提升幅度大致需要是算力乘数加速幅度的平方。这意味着我们需要约 4 到 9 倍的劳动力加速，才能使整体人工智能能力提升两倍。

However, once capabilities start speeding up significantly, this also means that low-hanging fruit is plucked at a correspondingly faster rate. Each new compute multiplier takes more and more work to discover and implement (i.e., “ideas are getting harder to find”). Before there was much acceleration from AI, labor roughly grew at some roughly fixed exponential rate from hiring more humans. Combined with growth in compute, this compensated for diminishing returns enough to maintain the baseline rate of capabilities progress. But to maintain a new faster rate of capabilities progress without scaling compute faster, we would need a new faster rate of growth in overall labor.

然而，一旦能力开始显著加速，也意味着“低垂的果实”被摘取的速度相应加快。每一个新的算力乘数的发现和实现都需要更多的工作（即“想法越来越难找”）。在人工智能带来显著加速之前，劳动力大致通过雇佣更多人类以固定的指数速度增长。结合算力的增长，这足以抵消收益递减，从而维持能力进展的基准速度。但若要在不进一步扩大算力规模的情况下维持新的、更快的进展速度，我们就需要整体劳动力以更快的速度增长。

Since the human labor force is stuck growing at more or less the old rate, the uplift from AI would need to get larger and larger over time just to sustain capabilities progress at its new rate. That means the uplift today, after several months of accelerated progress from AI, needs to be even higher than ~4-9x, the estimate for what it would take to double progress at the start: maybe the necessary labor uplift is ~10x. This is very uncertain, and substantially more or less is plausible. But it lines up surprisingly well with informal self-reports from Anthropic staff, who say they are now getting a ~12x uplift from the use of internal models.

由于人类劳动力的增长速度基本保持不变，人工智能带来的提升幅度必须随着时间的推移越来越大，才能维持新的进展速度。这意味着在经历了几个月的人工智能加速后，今天的提升幅度需要高于最初估计的 4 到 9 倍：所需的劳动力提升可能达到约 10 倍。这存在很大的不确定性，实际情况可能会高出或低于此数值。但这与 Anthropic 员工的非正式自述非常吻合，他们表示目前通过使用内部模型获得了约 12 倍的效率提升。

When you’re picturing a 10x uplift, you should not be imagining that every researcher is now a team of ten researchers — as anyone who has ever done anything knows, a team of ten does not get ten times as much valuable work done per hour as one person. Instead, it’s as if every researcher at Anthropic is in a time warp bubble where they experience ten days for every day that passes in the outside world. As if AI lets them think, read, speak, type, and do everything else ten times faster. Anthropic probably wouldn’t have been able to speed up its research this much by hiring any number of people.

当你想象 10 倍的提升时，不要认为每个研究人员现在都变成了一个十人团队——任何有工作经验的人都知道，十人团队每小时完成的有价值工作量并不等于一个人的十倍。相反，这就像 Anthropic 的每位研究人员都处于一个时间扭曲的泡泡中，外界过去一天，他们却经历了十天。就好像人工智能让他们思考、阅读、说话、打字以及做其他所有事情的速度都快了十倍。Anthropic 可能无法通过雇佣任何数量的人员来实现如此程度的研发加速。

A reasonable default expectation is that as AI capabilities continue to improve, AI researchers will be sped up more and more from automation: if today it takes them a month to get as much work done as they used to in a year, soon it will take a week, and then perhaps a day. If this increasing labor uplift is strong enough to overcome diminishing returns, then in the absence of other bottlenecks it would translate into even larger speed ups in the rate of AI progress: progress will start going 4x.

一个合理的默认预期是，随着人工智能能力的持续提升，人工智能研究人员将因自动化而获得越来越大的加速：如果今天他们用一个月完成过去一年的工作量，很快就会缩短到一周，然后可能是一天。如果这种不断增长的劳动力提升幅度足以克服收益递减，那么在没有其他瓶颈的情况下，这将转化为人工智能进展速度的更大提升：进展速度将开始达到 4 倍。
