你如何应对网络上人工智能带来的所有负面影响？☶ ask web 作者：tmcb 2026-11-07 03:47:24 | 53 条评论 | +62, 4 条离题

It seems to me that the AI hype killed the web for good, but not for the reasons we immediately associate with it. Search engines now rank links full of slop at the top, which ironically make LLM summaries acceptable, not because they are better than what we had before (they are not), but because they at least mention the good links which are now buried at the bottom of the page.

在我看来，人工智能的炒作彻底毁掉了网络，但原因并非我们通常联想到的那些。搜索引擎现在将充斥着垃圾内容的链接排在首位，讽刺的是，这反而让大语言模型的摘要变得可以接受了——并非因为它们比以前的内容更好（事实并非如此），而是因为它们至少提到了那些现在被埋在页面底部的优质链接。

Also, they've been rate limiting ordinary users like crazy: more often than I'd like to, Google and DuckDuckGo now either show me either an error message or tell me to wait a bit. Sometimes I waste at least a minute refreshing the page and waiting for a result. I also can't browse GitHub freely without being logged in. Anubis is now everywhere, occasionally blocking me. I feel like my productivity has actually gone down thanks to all of that.

此外，它们对普通用户进行了疯狂的速率限制：Google 和 DuckDuckGo 现在经常向我显示错误信息或提示我稍后再试，这种情况比我预想的要频繁得多。有时我不得不浪费至少一分钟来刷新页面以等待结果。而且，不登录我就无法自由浏览 GitHub。Anubis 现在无处不在，偶尔还会拦截我。我觉得由于这一切，我的工作效率实际上已经下降了。

Edit: I see that most of the replies here are suggesting relying less on the web and going after more fundamental resources, which are great suggestions, don’t get me wrong! But a book from 5 years ago will not help you understand why your custom firmware doesn’t boot in your 1-year old mobile chipset. A existing bug report on the web probably will. Not quite my predicament at the moment, but something very similar for which the web seemed to work just fine before the AI craze.

编辑：我看到这里的回复大多建议减少对网络的依赖，转而寻求更基础的资源，这些建议很好，别误会我的意思！但是，一本五年前的书无法帮你理解为什么你的自定义固件无法在你一年前的移动芯片组上启动。而网络上现有的错误报告或许可以。虽然这并非我目前面临的困境，但情况非常相似，而在人工智能热潮之前，网络在处理这类问题时表现得相当不错。
