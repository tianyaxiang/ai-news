Crusoe, an eight-year-old, Denver-based data center developer recently valued at $30.9 billion, confirmed Friday it has sold an unfinished data center project in southeastern Wyoming to Google.

Crusoe 是一家总部位于丹佛、成立八年的数据中心开发商，近期估值达到 309 亿美元。该公司周五证实，已将其位于怀俄明州东南部的一个未完工数据中心项目出售给了谷歌。

Google, which Crusoe had tried and failed to land as a customer for the project, is taking over the development after reportedly raising concerns about its cost and construction timeline under Crusoe’s management.

谷歌曾是 Crusoe 试图争取但未果的该项目客户。据报道，谷歌在对 Crusoe 管理下的成本和施工进度表示担忧后，接手了该项目的开发工作。

Crusoe initially announced the project last year with Blackstone-backed infrastructure company Tallgrass, planning to eventually scale it to 10 gigawatts of power capacity.

Crusoe 最初于去年与黑石集团支持的基础设施公司 Tallgrass 共同宣布了该项目，计划最终将其电力容量扩展至 10 吉瓦。

Google did not disclose how much it paid Crusoe for the unfinished project, but it announced plans to invest $17 billion to develop the data center campus.

谷歌并未披露为该未完工项目向 Crusoe 支付了多少费用，但宣布计划投资 170 亿美元来开发该数据中心园区。

The deal highlights how difficult it can be for independent data center developers to deliver massive projects on time and within budget, given the challenges of securing equipment and power. Growing opposition from local communities can further complicate development.

这笔交易凸显了独立数据中心开发商在确保设备和电力方面面临挑战时，按时且在预算内交付大型项目是多么困难。来自当地社区日益增长的反对声音可能会使开发工作进一步复杂化。

Despite taking over the Wyoming facility, Google hasn’t walked away from working with Crusoe entirely. Crusoe is developing another data center for Google near Amarillo, Texas.

尽管接管了怀俄明州的设施，但谷歌并未完全停止与 Crusoe 的合作。Crusoe 目前正在德克萨斯州阿马里洛附近为谷歌开发另一个数据中心。

The deal to sell the site to Google was first reported by Bloomberg in June, but the Wyoming setback didn’t stop Crusoe from raising a $3.9 billion round in September.

将该地块出售给谷歌的交易最早由彭博社在 6 月报道，但怀俄明州的挫折并未阻止 Crusoe 在 9 月完成 39 亿美元的融资。

Crusoe makes money by leasing data center space to customers that bring their own GPUs, by renting out its own GPUs, and by selling compute power used to run AI models, known as inference.

Crusoe 的盈利模式包括：向自带 GPU 的客户出租数据中心空间、出租其自有的 GPU，以及出售用于运行 AI 模型（即推理）的计算能力。

Crusoe’s largest projects serve some of the biggest names in AI. Its campus in Abilene, Texas, is being built in phases for Oracle and OpenAI, and the company is also constructing a separate 900-megawatt campus next door for Microsoft, with its first building expected to come online in mid-2027.

Crusoe 的大型项目服务于 AI 领域的一些巨头。其位于德克萨斯州阿比林的园区正在为甲骨文（Oracle）和 OpenAI 分阶段建设；该公司还在隔壁为微软建设一个独立的 900 兆瓦园区，预计首栋建筑将于 2027 年年中投入使用。

Meta is also a customer, and Bloomberg reported that the trading firm Jane Street signed a $13 billion, five-year cloud deal with Crusoe.

Meta 也是其客户之一，据彭博社报道，贸易公司 Jane Street 与 Crusoe 签署了一份价值 130 亿美元、为期五年的云服务协议。

Crusoe was founded by Chase Lochmiller, now its CEO, and Cully Cavness, who started it as a cryptocurrency-mining business powered by natural gas that would otherwise have been burned off.

Crusoe 由现任首席执行官 Chase Lochmiller 和 Cully Cavness 共同创立。公司最初是一家加密货币挖矿企业，利用本会被废弃燃烧的天然气进行发电。

In September, alongside its $3.9 billion funding round, the company added three board members, including JB Straubel, the Redwood Materials founder and Tesla board member who invested in Crusoe in 2021. Crusoe later became the first customer of Straubel’s energy-storage business.

9 月，在完成 39 亿美元融资的同时，该公司增加了三名董事会成员，其中包括 Redwood Materials 创始人兼特斯拉董事会成员 JB Straubel，他曾在 2021 年投资过 Crusoe。此后，Crusoe 成为了 Straubel 储能业务的首位客户。
