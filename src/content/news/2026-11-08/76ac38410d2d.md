An Estonian newspaper recently revealed that a British soldier disobeyed a direct order to remove the SIM card from his cellphone before being deployed to a part of Estonia, approximately 90 miles west of the Russian border. As a result of his refusal to engage in this element of operational security (opsec), the soldier, Daniel Barton-Kennedy, had his phone hacked, and nude photos of himself were stolen and used as blackmail against him.

一家爱沙尼亚报纸最近披露，一名英国士兵在被派往距离俄罗斯边境约90英里的爱沙尼亚某地之前，违抗了移除手机SIM卡的直接命令。由于他拒绝执行这一作战安全（opsec）要素，这名士兵丹尼尔·巴顿-肯尼迪（Daniel Barton-Kennedy）的手机遭到黑客攻击，其裸照被窃取并被用于对他进行勒索。

Eventually, Barton-Kennedy was court-martialed for disobeying a lawful command, pled guilty, was reduced in rank, and ordered to serve 10 months in detention. According to a 21-page British appeals court document shared with Ars Technica by Eesti Ekspress, the Estonian newspaper that first reported on this saga, the story begins in January 2026.

最终，巴顿-肯尼迪因违抗合法命令而受到军事法庭审判，他认罪后被降职，并被判处10个月监禁。根据爱沙尼亚报纸《Eesti Ekspress》（该事件的首发媒体）与Ars Technica分享的一份21页的英国上诉法院文件，此事始于2026年1月。

At the time, Barton-Kennedy was a corporal, with over seven years of service, serving in the 1st Battalion of the Duke of Lancaster Regiment of the British Army. His unit was deployed to participate in “Exercise Eastern Warrior” in Tapa, Estonia, roughly halfway between Tallinn, the capital city, and the Estonia-Russia border. While in the United Kingdom, on January 7, Barton-Kennedy, along with other members of his unit, were briefed on their orders and given specific instructions to “remove SIM cards and switch off location settings and to use a trusted virtual private network on all personal electronic devices.”

当时，巴顿-肯尼迪是一名服役超过七年的下士，隶属于英国陆军兰开斯特公爵团第1营。他的部队被派往爱沙尼亚塔帕参加“东方勇士”演习，该地大致位于首都塔林与爱沙尼亚-俄罗斯边境的中间位置。1月7日，当还在英国时，巴顿-肯尼迪与其部队的其他成员一起听取了命令简报，并收到了具体指示，要求“移除SIM卡、关闭定位设置，并在所有个人电子设备上使用受信任的虚拟专用网络（VPN）”。

Once the unit arrived in Estonia, days later, they were given further briefings and reminders of this policy. Despite this, Barton-Kennedy ignored the instruction. On January 19, Barton-Kennedy informed his platoon commander that he had received a message “from an unknown party who had acquired nude photographs of him from the photo section of his mobile phone and had identified him on social media.”

几天后，当部队抵达爱沙尼亚时，他们再次接受了简报并被提醒了这一政策。尽管如此，巴顿-肯尼迪还是无视了这些指示。1月19日，巴顿-肯尼迪告知他的排长，他收到了一条消息，“来自一个未知方，对方从他手机的照片库中获取了他的裸照，并在社交媒体上认出了他。”

Barton-Kennedy initially acquiesced to paying the attacker 100 British pounds ($133) but balked when he was asked to pay 250 British pounds ($333), and only then informed his platoon commander what had happened. Eventually, Barton-Kennedy was court-martialed and pled guilty at a hearing on July 30. During that process, according to the court filing, Barton-Kennedy understood the orders that were given to him at the time, but asked the court to “consider the personal circumstances” that he faced at the time.

巴顿-肯尼迪最初同意向攻击者支付100英镑（133美元），但在对方要求支付250英镑（333美元）时拒绝了，直到那时他才将发生的事情告知排长。最终，巴顿-肯尼迪在7月30日的听证会上接受了军事法庭审判并认罪。根据法庭文件，在审判过程中，巴顿-肯尼迪表示他当时理解所接到的命令，但请求法庭“考虑他当时面临的个人情况”。

Without his phone, he told the court, he could not reach his daughter on her birthday, nor his pregnant partner. In a written statement, Barton-Kennedy said he was stunned when he received the first blackmail message. “Despite still being in a state of shock and uncertainty, I recognised that I had a duty to report the compromise without any further delay,” he wrote. Additionally, he said that he “deeply regrets” the incident and added that serving in the British Army “has been one of the greatest privileges of my life.”

他告诉法庭，没有手机，他就无法在女儿生日时联系她，也无法联系他怀孕的伴侣。在一份书面声明中，巴顿-肯尼迪表示，当他收到第一条勒索信息时感到震惊。“尽管仍处于震惊和不确定的状态，但我意识到我有责任毫不拖延地报告这一泄密事件，”他写道。此外，他表示对该事件“深感遗憾”，并补充说在英国陆军服役“是我一生中最大的荣幸之一”。

Barton-Kennedy appealed the sentence, which was denied in August 2026. In response to an inquiry for comment, Lt. Col. EP Mackie, a British Army spokesperson, told Ars in a statement that the security measures imposed are meant to protect the safety of military personnel. “Any breaches of Army security measures that might compromise personal safeguarding or operational security will be dealt with swiftly, and offending personnel can expect uncompromising disciplinary action,” he wrote.

巴顿-肯尼迪对判决提出上诉，但于2026年8月被驳回。在回应置评请求时，英国陆军发言人EP·麦基（EP Mackie）中校在声明中告诉Ars，所实施的安全措施旨在保护军事人员的安全。“任何可能危及个人安全或作战安全的违反陆军安全措施的行为都将得到迅速处理，违规人员将面临毫不妥协的纪律处分，”他写道。
