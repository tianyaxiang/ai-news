# From the front row to the hot seat — what the midterms mean for Big Tech

Right before the midterm elections, OpenAI hired Jessica Schumer, daughter of Democratic Sen. Chuck Schumer. The company also said it would start calling its data centers “compute campuses.” For the past two years, many of Silicon Valley’s biggest names have built closer ties with President Donald Trump and his administration. Several attended White House events, some companies scaled back diversity, equity and inclusion programs, and some executives donated millions to super PACs supporting his campaign and allied candidates. Administration policies also benefited the industry, including tax breaks, the pausing of some federal investigations, and looser AI regulation. Meanwhile, Meta faced lawsuits and billion-dollar fines, and OpenAI faced suits alleging its technology harmed users. Now, with the midterms shifting the balance of power in Washington, those same companies are working to rebuild ties with Democrats, who have pledged to tighten oversight of AI. How far that gets them may depend less on image-polishing than on where the real fights are happening: in state capitols, in communities deciding whether to host data centers, and, as we’ve seen this past week, in the markets watching the AI boom.

在中期选举前夕，OpenAI 聘请了民主党参议员查克·舒默（Chuck Schumer）的女儿杰西卡·舒默（Jessica Schumer）。该公司还表示，将开始把其数据中心称为“计算园区”。在过去两年中，硅谷许多知名企业与唐纳德·特朗普总统及其政府建立了更紧密的联系。一些公司高管参加了白宫活动，部分公司缩减了多元化、公平和包容性项目，还有一些高管向支持特朗普竞选及其盟友候选人的超级政治行动委员会捐赠了数百万美元。政府的政策也使该行业受益，包括税收减免、暂停部分联邦调查以及更宽松的人工智能监管。与此同时，Meta 面临诉讼和数十亿美元的罚款，OpenAI 也面临指控其技术伤害用户的诉讼。现在，随着中期选举改变了华盛顿的权力平衡，这些公司正努力重建与民主党人的关系，而民主党人已承诺加强对人工智能的监管。他们能走多远，可能不取决于形象包装，而取决于真正的战场在哪里：在州议会、在决定是否接纳数据中心的社区，以及正如我们过去一周所见，在关注人工智能热潮的市场中。

J.B. Branch, director of federal AI governance and technology policy at Public Citizen, a nonprofit advocacy group that describes itself as nonpartisan, told me that the tech industry “realizes they have an optics problem.” He characterized moves like the Schumer hire and the rebranding as “PR washing.” He expects more of it, too, pointing to OpenAI’s partnership with the American Federation of Teachers, announced last year, as well as Anthropic’s newly launched Claude Corps and Meta’s America’s Workforce Academy. “I would imagine more of that sort of investment in these corporate, private-public partnerships,” he said. These initiatives didn’t spring up overnight. Meta and other tech companies reportedly began looking for ways to strengthen their ties to Democrats as Democratic gains in the midterms looked more likely. Reached for comment about the midterm elections’ impact on Meta, a company spokesperson pointed TechCrunch to remarks that Meta President Dina Powell McCormick made this week at WSJ Tech. “You have to work with the government that the American people elect,” McCormick had said. “I have seen it across the board that you have to be bipartisan, and you want to be, because you want buy-in on the things that you’re trying to do.”

非营利倡导组织“公共公民”（Public Citizen，自称为无党派组织）的联邦人工智能治理与技术政策主任 J.B. Branch 告诉我，科技行业“意识到他们存在形象问题”。他将聘请舒默之女和品牌重塑等举措称为“公关洗白”。他预计未来会有更多此类动作，并指出了 OpenAI 去年宣布与美国教师联合会（American Federation of Teachers）的合作，以及 Anthropic 新推出的 Claude Corps 和 Meta 的“美国劳动力学院”（America’s Workforce Academy）。他说：“我预计会有更多此类对企业与公私合作伙伴关系的投资。”这些举措并非一夜之间出现。据报道，随着民主党在中期选举中获胜的可能性增加，Meta 和其他科技公司开始寻求加强与民主党人联系的方法。在被问及中期选举对 Meta 的影响时，该公司发言人向 TechCrunch 指出了 Meta 总裁迪娜·鲍威尔·麦考密克（Dina Powell McCormick）本周在《华尔街日报》科技会议上的言论。麦考密克曾表示：“你必须与美国人民选出的政府合作。我看到的情况是，你必须保持两党立场，而且你也愿意这样做，因为你需要为你试图做的事情争取支持。”

Not everyone expects them to work. Alice Marwick, director of research at the tech policy nonprofit Data & Society, told me she expects Big Tech to try to pivot back toward the middle, “if not the left. But it’s a little too late,” she said. “Tech companies should be asking themselves how their products can make people’s lives better, because right now, it doesn’t seem like they are.” If Democrats follow through on pledges to tighten oversight of AI and Big Tech, the industry could face a tougher environment. Data center moratoriums are gaining traction, and a wave of AI regulation bills awaits drafting. At the same event, Powell McCormick said Meta wants to show that communities welcoming its investments will see “an enormous amount of benefit,” while those that don’t want them “will of course have the right to stop them.” Still, the next two years could bring political gridlock in Washington. Aryan Ranjan, founder of Baraza, a political campaign management software company, told me most action will likely happen at the state level, since states have already sued tech companies and passed regulations. “A coalition built on newer, working-class voters is likely to push Democrats to frame AI around electricity bills, data-center siting and jobs rather than abstract AI safety,” he said of what to expect at the federal level.

并非所有人都认为这些努力会奏效。科技政策非营利组织 Data & Society 的研究主任爱丽丝·马威克（Alice Marwick）告诉我，她预计大型科技公司会试图向中间派靠拢，“如果不是向左派靠拢的话。但这有点太晚了，”她说。“科技公司应该问问自己，他们的产品如何能让人们的生活变得更好，因为目前看来，事实并非如此。”如果民主党人兑现加强对人工智能和大型科技公司监管的承诺，该行业可能会面临更严峻的环境。数据中心暂停令正受到关注，一系列人工智能监管法案正等待起草。在同一活动中，鲍威尔·麦考密克表示，Meta 希望表明，欢迎其投资的社区将看到“巨大的利益”，而那些不希望其投资的社区“当然有权阻止它们”。尽管如此，未来两年华盛顿可能会出现政治僵局。政治竞选管理软件公司 Baraza 的创始人雅利安·兰詹（Aryan Ranjan）告诉我，大多数行动可能会发生在州一级，因为各州已经起诉了科技公司并出台了法规。他在谈到联邦层面的预期时说：“一个由较新的工人阶级选民组成的联盟，可能会推动民主党人将人工智能问题聚焦于电费、数据中心选址和就业，而不是抽象的人工智能安全。”

Last December, the Trump administration signed an executive order creating the AI Litigation Task Force, a federal group meant to challenge state AI laws. The administration has argued that U.S. AI companies “must be free to innovate without cumbersome regulations.” Many states have nonetheless passed their own laws, including measures on chatbots and deepfakes. It also remains to be seen how President Trump will respond, including whether he’ll sign more AI executive orders or veto bills that could be seen as anti-AI. Clearly, voters want stronger guardrails around the technology. In a statement to TechCrunch, a White House official said: “American leadership in super intelligence is essential for our national security, economic prosperity, and the quality of life of the American people. President Trump is committed to ensuring America continues to lead the world in super intelligence while protecting American consumers. As the President has said, he’s willing to work with anyone to continue delivering victories for hardworking Americans.”

去年 12 月，特朗普政府签署了一项行政命令，成立了人工智能诉讼特别工作组（AI Litigation Task Force），这是一个旨在挑战各州人工智能法律的联邦机构。政府辩称，美国人工智能公司“必须能够在没有繁琐监管的情况下自由创新”。尽管如此，许多州还是通过了自己的法律，包括针对聊天机器人和深度伪造（deepfakes）的措施。特朗普总统将如何应对还有待观察，包括他是否会签署更多人工智能行政命令，或否决可能被视为反人工智能的法案。显然，选民希望为这项技术设置更强的护栏。白宫官员在给 TechCrunch 的一份声明中表示：“美国在超级智能领域的领导地位对于我们的国家安全、经济繁荣和美国人民的生活质量至关重要。特朗普总统致力于确保美国继续在超级智能领域引领世界，同时保护美国消费者。正如总统所说，他愿意与任何人合作，继续为勤劳的美国人民带来胜利。”

Branch argued that Trump’s political fortunes are now tied to the AI industry. On Wednesday, the day after the midterms, a trillion-dollar market sell-off spooked investors, some are whom are reportedly now reconsidering Anthropic’s reported trillion-dollar IPO. “There’s a very strong likelihood that there will be an AI bubble pop, and it’ll happen on Trump’s hands,” Branch said, predicting a possible market collapse. “Does he want to sink or swim along with the AI industry?” For now, the rules companies face may vary by location. “For the next two years, a company’s AI rules may depend more on where it operates rather than on anything Washington passes,” Ranjan said.

Branch 认为，特朗普的政治命运现在与人工智能行业捆绑在一起。周三，即中期选举后的第二天，一场万亿美元的市场抛售惊吓了投资者，据报道，其中一些人现在正在重新考虑 Anthropic 传闻中的万亿美元首次公开募股（IPO）。“人工智能泡沫破裂的可能性非常大，而且会发生在特朗普任期内，”Branch 预测市场可能崩溃时说道。“他是想与人工智能行业共沉浮，还是想脱身？”目前，公司面临的规则可能因地点而异。Ranjan 说：“在接下来的两年里，一家公司的人工智能规则可能更多地取决于其运营地点，而不是华盛顿通过的任何法案。”
