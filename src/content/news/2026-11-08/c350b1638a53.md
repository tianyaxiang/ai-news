# AI broke the cybersecurity world. What’s next?

PodcastsAITechAI broke the cybersecurity world. What’s next?Going inside the security ‘dark ages’ with Alex Stamos.by Nilay PatelNov 7, 2026, 3:00 PM UTCNilay Patel is editor-in-chief of The Verge, host of the Decoder podcast, and co-host of The Vergecast.

Both private actors and also state actors, like Russia or North Korea, are breaking into systems all over thanks to AI helping them both detect and exploit vulnerabilities all at the same time. It’s got a real frontier feel to it, Alex notes, just like the ’90s, when the whole idea of an always-online world was brand-new.

无论是私人行为者还是俄罗斯或朝鲜等国家行为者，都在人工智能的帮助下入侵世界各地的系统，因为人工智能能同时帮助他们发现并利用漏洞。亚历克斯指出，这有一种真正的“前沿”感，就像90年代那样，当时“全时在线”世界的概念还是全新的。

That much disruption means a lot of places where bad guys can get in while everyone else is left scrambling. Alex isn’t a doomer in the long run, but he does think we’re in for a “dark age” while we try to figure out how on earth to adapt to the new era.

如此巨大的破坏意味着坏人有许多可乘之机，而其他人则只能手忙脚乱。从长远来看，亚历克斯并非悲观主义者，但他确实认为，在我们设法适应这个新时代的过程中，我们将经历一个“黑暗时代”。

Hey, thanks for having me, man. I’m excited to talk to you. It’s been a long time since you and I chatted in what you might call the “content moderation era” of internet policy discourse. I feel like you and I talked a lot about how all that was working, about election security, all those sorts of things.

嘿，伙计，谢谢你邀请我。我很高兴能和你交谈。距离我们上次在所谓的互联网政策讨论的“内容审核时代”聊天已经过去很久了。我觉得我们当时讨论了很多关于这一切是如何运作的，关于选举安全，以及所有这类事情。

We’re in a new era of big problems on the internet, particularly around cyber defense, cybersecurity. You’ve been thinking about this stuff a lot. You referred to the past few weeks as “security awareness month.” Tell me what’s going on. Just give me the lay of land right now.

我们现在处于互联网重大问题的新时代，特别是在网络防御和网络安全方面。你一直在深入思考这些问题。你把过去几周称为“安全意识月”。告诉我发生了什么。请简要介绍一下目前的形势。

October is traditionally security awareness month. Lots of companies run events and deal both internally and then tell people, “Change your password, use multiple passwords, use a password manager.” What has happened over the last month is we’ve had a couple of these big bugs, PHP, Node and such. What’s been less discussed is a huge number of companies have been breached.

十月传统上是安全意识月。许多公司会举办活动，在内部处理相关事宜，并告诉员工：“更改密码，使用多个密码，使用密码管理器。”过去一个月发生的事情是，我们遇到了一些重大漏洞，比如 PHP、Node 等。但讨论较少的是，有大量公司遭到了入侵。

You have a series of AI-related companies, the neoclouds that have been breached in succession. You have the Binance hack, which is the largest heist of anything in human history. You have the Cursor incident where one of the things that’s going on here is these hacks are being chained together. From what we know right now, one of the companies that was broken into was Fireworks, which is a prominent provider of AI services to a number of companies.

有一系列与人工智能相关的公司，即所谓的“新云”公司，相继遭到入侵。还有币安（Binance）黑客事件，这是人类历史上规模最大的盗窃案。还有 Cursor 事件，其中发生的情况之一是这些黑客攻击被串联在了一起。据我们目前所知，被入侵的公司之一是 Fireworks，它是多家人工智能服务的重要提供商。

From the Fireworks hack, API keys were stolen that allowed people to break into Cursor. Once they got into Cursor, they then pushed a bad plugin, which went to 5,000 more companies and that caused 5,000 potential breaches. It’s just this kind of permanent continuous breach that is going on throughout the tech industry.

通过对 Fireworks 的黑客攻击，API 密钥被盗，这使得攻击者能够入侵 Cursor。一旦进入 Cursor，他们就推送了一个恶意插件，该插件传播到了另外 5,000 家公司，导致了 5,000 起潜在的违规事件。这就是整个科技行业正在发生的这种永久性的、持续性的违规行为。

A CISO friend of mine said to a bunch of us, “Is anybody else tired?” And everybody said yes, because effectively we have been at DEFCON 2 for all of October and now going into November.

我的一位首席信息安全官（CISO）朋友对我们一群人说：“还有人感到疲惫吗？”每个人都回答说是，因为实际上整个十月以及现在进入十一月，我们一直处于“二级防御状态”（DEFCON 2）。

I’m going to ask you some really dumb questions here. Who is behind all these attacks? Is it Russia? Is it North Korea? Do we know?

我要问你一些非常愚蠢的问题。谁是所有这些攻击的幕后黑手？是俄罗斯吗？是朝鲜吗？我们知道吗？

It’s a variety of different groups. It is not just because adversaries have decided, but what has happened is, from my perspective, we are at the start of the takeoff of adversary capability enabled by AI, that the open-weight models have now gotten to the point at which you can automate most of the kill chain.

这是各种不同的组织。这不仅仅是因为对手做出了决定，而是从我的角度来看，我们正处于人工智能赋能的对手能力起飞的初期，开源权重模型现在已经发展到可以自动化大部分“杀伤链”（kill chain）的地步。

This “kill chain” term is something we stole from the military. In the military, they mean to actually kill something. They’re the steps you do to blow something up. In cyber, the kill chain is the things you do to have an effect on a target. So to steal information, to turn something off, to steal money, to steal Bitcoin.

这个“杀伤链”术语是我们从军队那里借用的。在军事上，它们指的是真正杀死某物。它们是你为了炸毁某物而采取的步骤。在网络领域，杀伤链是你为了对目标产生影响而做的事情。比如窃取信息、关闭某物、窃取金钱、窃取比特币。

Traditionally, you do research upfront, you look at a piece of software, you find a vulnerability, you write exploits, and you do that all way ahead of time. And then you have a human being go and take that stuff you built and deploy it against either one target — because maybe you’re a government and you’re going against Lockheed Martin or you’re going against another government or you’re a ransomware actor — and you take all that work you did and you deploy it against hundreds or thousands of targets really fast.

传统上，你会提前进行研究，查看软件，发现漏洞，编写漏洞利用程序，所有这些都是提前完成的。然后，你会让人去拿你构建的东西，并将其部署到一个目标上——因为你可能是一个政府，正在针对洛克希德·马丁公司，或者针对另一个政府，或者你是一个勒索软件攻击者——你把你所做的一切工作拿出来，非常迅速地将其部署到成百上千个目标上。

Now with AI, what’s happening is this kill chain has inverted where you can run an AI system that just goes hog wild and it looks at the targets and it finds the bugs in real time and it writes the exploits in real time and it breaks into these companies. One of the things that’s happening is that these systems are going and they’re so good at finding bugs that they are finding the bugs in real time and writing the exploits in real time.

现在有了人工智能，发生的情况是这个杀伤链被颠倒了，你可以运行一个人工智能系统，它会疯狂地工作，实时查看目标，实时发现漏洞，实时编写漏洞利用程序，并入侵这些公司。正在发生的事情之一是，这些系统非常擅长发现漏洞，以至于它们能够实时发现漏洞并实时编写漏洞利用程序。
