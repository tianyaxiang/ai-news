# The end of Microsoft Research's AI Frontiers group

In 2023 after the ChatGPT release, it was clear to me that research needed to be more organized to succeed than was typical within Microsoft Research. The amount of support and focus which other research groups were applying was great enough that even with a clever idea pursued by one person it was likely to arrive late and unconvincing because one person could not move fast enough and the baselines which one person could assemble just were not strong enough to really show that an idea was worth taking flight.

2023年ChatGPT发布后，我很清楚，研究工作若想取得成功，其组织化程度必须高于微软研究院（MSR）以往的水平。其他研究团队所投入的支持和专注度非常高，以至于即便是一个人提出的巧妙想法，也往往因为个人行动不够迅速，或者所构建的基准模型不够强大，无法证明该想法值得进一步发展，从而导致成果发布滞后且缺乏说服力。

Several other folks thought similarly and we organized to create the AI Frontiers sub-organization within MSR. In AI Frontiers, we actively tried to organize coherent research efforts. This was not precisely “top down” or “bottom up”, but rather mostly somewhere in the messy middle. There were disagreements of course, and resolving them was sometimes quite challenging. Compute availability was also an often-severe bottleneck. But at a high level I was under the general impression that the organization was succeeding in doing pioneering things, particularly given the small size of AIF (~50 people) and relative to what we could do previously.

其他几位同事也有类似的想法，于是我们组织成立了MSR内部的AI Frontiers（AIF）子部门。在AIF，我们积极尝试组织连贯的研究工作。这并非严格意义上的“自上而下”或“自下而上”，而是处于两者之间混乱的中间地带。当然，我们之间存在分歧，解决这些分歧有时极具挑战性。计算资源的可用性也常常是一个严重的瓶颈。但从宏观层面来看，我的总体印象是该组织在开创性工作上取得了成功，特别是考虑到AIF规模较小（约50人）且相对于我们之前所能做的工作而言。

Much of the work of AIF was figuring out how to make agents work, for example the Autogen orchestration framework and the Fara computer use agent. In NYC however, several of us were interested in developing new fundamental capabilities such as the Next Latent objective which enables extrapolation, Dion3 which creates an optimized/improved Muon, or the Full Bandwidth Transformer which efficiently makes models function substantially more powerfully via recursion. We also actively tried to share with the rest of MSR. For example, AIF decided to develop a monorepo that was shared by default with all of Microsoft Research.

AIF的大部分工作是探索如何让智能体（agents）发挥作用，例如Autogen编排框架和Fara计算机使用智能体。然而，在纽约，我们几个人对开发新的基础能力很感兴趣，例如支持外推的Next Latent目标、创建优化/改进版Muon的Dion3，或者通过递归高效提升模型性能的Full Bandwidth Transformer。我们也积极尝试与MSR的其他部门分享成果。例如，AIF决定开发一个默认与整个微软研究院共享的单一代码库（monorepo）。

In this monorepo for example was the proven-capable edge scale pretraining codebase I talked about in the COLM keynote last month. This might seem like a minor detail, but the amount effort required to create a well-formed model creation pipeline is substantial and I’m aware of at least 4 efforts (the other 3 not in public-within-MSR repos) trying to develop model building pipelines. In early September we were reorged into Microsoft AI. On the surface this could make sense, because putting two different AI organizations together creates opportunities for better concentration of effort, and there were places where AIF was ahead of MAI and of course vice-versa (MAI is much larger).

例如，这个单一代码库中包含了我在上个月COLM主题演讲中提到的、经过验证的边缘规模预训练代码库。这看起来可能是一个微小的细节，但创建一个完善的模型构建流水线所需的工作量是巨大的，据我所知，至少有4个团队（其他3个不在MSR内部的公共代码库中）正在尝试开发模型构建流水线。9月初，我们被重组到了微软AI（MAI）。从表面上看，这似乎合情合理，因为将两个不同的AI组织合并可以创造机会，更好地集中力量，而且AIF在某些方面领先于MAI，反之亦然（MAI规模大得多）。

In practice, there are substantial issues because the philosophy of the ex-Inflection leadership of MAI is incompatible. The core philosophy there is scaling, and aggressively not research. This might shock some folks who naturally think that the way you make better models is via research but there is plenty of evidence that bigger models are better models as far as the quality of the predictions they make and from a business perspective returns to scale are far more predictable than returns to research. In a scaling-only environment of course the primary effort is on getting/making/refining data, which is quite a step away from what we were working on.

在实践中，这存在重大问题，因为MAI前Inflection领导层的理念与我们不兼容。他们的核心理念是规模化，而非研究。这可能会让一些人感到震惊，因为他们自然认为提升模型性能的方法是通过研究，但有大量证据表明，就预测质量而言，更大的模型就是更好的模型；从商业角度来看，规模化带来的回报比研究带来的回报更具可预测性。在纯规模化的环境中，主要的精力自然是获取、制作和优化数据，这与我们当时的工作相去甚远。

Aside from the incompatible focus, there was plenty of evidence that they just did not really want us. It’s a tough situation to find yourself in. I consider the scaling-only focus to be both misdirected and not fun. It is an understandable failure mode—scaling is straightforward and it’s common for people to give up on a tradeoff between multiple competing ideals (like innovation and size) by short-sightedly adopting one in totality and dropping the other. But returns to research are real and significant.

除了关注点不兼容之外，还有大量证据表明他们并不真正需要我们。身处这种境地非常艰难。我认为纯规模化的关注点既是误导性的，也毫无乐趣可言。这是一种可以理解的失败模式——规模化很简单，人们往往会因为短视，通过完全采纳其中一个而放弃另一个，从而放弃在多个相互竞争的理想（如创新和规模）之间进行权衡。但研究的回报是真实且显著的。

For example, the Bytedance experiments suggest a variant of the Full Bandwidth Transformer might be comparable to a model of twice the size, implying significant savings in training and inference for the same capability. Do the math—how much is it worth to halve training and inference cost given that the industry has investments approaching a trillion dollars? So I situated my folks as best I could and left. I am quite fortunate in that there are many other people in the world who are capable of seeing the value of research and innovation which is my intrinsic center of interest. Every other founding AIF leadership team member seems to have come to a similar conclusion.

例如，字节跳动的实验表明，Full Bandwidth Transformer的一个变体可能与两倍大小的模型相当，这意味着在实现相同能力的情况下，可以显著节省训练和推理成本。算算账吧——考虑到行业投资接近一万亿美元，将训练和推理成本减半价值几何？因此，我尽我所能安置了我的团队成员，然后离开了。我很幸运，世界上还有许多其他人能够看到研究和创新的价值，而这正是我的核心兴趣所在。AIF的其他创始领导团队成员似乎也得出了类似的结论。

This does leave a question: Why not go back to MSR? I chatted with folks a bit but could not get away from two serious obstacles. The first is that I am just not comfortable being at a company where the research I do has no path to real use. I could not get innovation into use (or even seriously considered) when I was in MAI, so the prospects for success from MSR seem quite small. The second is that I’m afraid there’s a breakdown of the basic deal in MSR: do good work and good things happen. As far as I can tell we did good work and were non-consensually reorged away.

这留下了一个问题：为什么不回到MSR？我和一些人聊了聊，但无法摆脱两个严重的障碍。首先，我不愿意待在一家我所做的研究无法转化为实际应用的公司。当我在MAI时，我无法将创新投入使用（甚至无法得到认真考虑），因此在MSR取得成功的希望似乎非常渺茫。其次，我担心MSR的基本契约已经破裂：做好工作，好事就会发生。据我所知，我们做了出色的工作，却在违背意愿的情况下被重组掉了。

Unfortunately, I would not be surprised to see that kind of thing continue to happen until MSR leadership develops a will to effectively organize to meet the moment. So AI Frontiers is over. I’m sorry to say that there will not be internships with the folks there in the coming year—I enjoyed working with interns on many occasions. And my best wishes for the folks I leave behind. It is not hard to imagine a Microsoft of the future which becomes attractive for research again if the people in control can develop a little bit more wisdom.

遗憾的是，如果MSR领导层不拿出意愿来有效地组织以应对当前的形势，我不会对这种事情继续发生感到惊讶。所以，AI Frontiers结束了。很抱歉，明年那里将不会有实习机会——我曾多次享受与实习生共事的时光。我向留下的同事们致以最美好的祝愿。如果掌权者能多一点智慧，不难想象未来的微软会再次成为吸引研究的地方。
