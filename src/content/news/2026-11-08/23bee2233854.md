Sequoia this week announced a broad overhaul in a memo posted to X, a move that includes closing its London office and shutting down its scout program. Observers warn that it would be a mistake to read too much into the London closure, since the British capital has a thriving AI scene. On the other hand, the decision to end scouts says more about where venture investing is headed.

红杉资本本周在 X 上发布的一份备忘录中宣布了一项重大调整，其中包括关闭其伦敦办事处并终止其“星探”（scout）计划。观察人士警告称，过度解读伦敦办事处的关闭是一个错误，因为英国首都拥有蓬勃发展的人工智能产业。另一方面，终止星探计划的决定则更多地反映了风险投资的发展方向。

That’s partly because 17 years ago, Sequoia pioneered the scouts model, in which select founders and operators write checks on the firm’s behalf. Amid increased competition for deal flow, many VC firms followed suit. But a consensus now seems to be emerging that the model has reached its limits in its current form.

这在一定程度上是因为 17 年前，红杉资本开创了“星探”模式，即由精选的创始人和运营者代表公司进行投资。在交易流竞争日益激烈的情况下，许多风投公司纷纷效仿。但目前似乎已达成共识，即这种模式在其现有形式下已达到极限。

Not everyone was sold on the model in the first place. “They pioneered the scout program but [to be honest] it never made sense to me!” VC Sheel Mohnot wrote on X this week. “The scouts themselves don’t really care that much about the investments, you can still miss (like they did with Uber), not sure what it gets you. I suspect other firms will shutter theirs too,” he added.

并非所有人都从一开始就认可这种模式。风投人士 Sheel Mohnot 本周在 X 上写道：“他们开创了星探计划，但（老实说）我一直觉得这毫无意义！星探们自己并不太关心投资，你依然会错过机会（就像他们错过 Uber 一样），我不确定这能带来什么。我怀疑其他公司也会关闭他们的星探计划。”

To be clear, Sequoia didn’t make a case against scouts in the memo. Instead, it framed its decision as part of a broader need for more focus. But its goal of “putting more energy behind fewer people and things” is likely to resonate with others who’ve questioned the ‘spray and pray’ approach to scouting.

需要明确的是，红杉资本在备忘录中并未针对星探提出反对意见。相反，它将这一决定描述为更广泛的专注需求的一部分。但其“将更多精力投入到更少的人和事上”的目标，很可能会引起那些质疑“广撒网”式星探策略的人的共鸣。

Rival firm Andreessen Horowitz (a16z), for instance, rolled out a global scout network aimed at spotting opportunities in the U.S. and also in smaller European hubs. But a16z partner Gabriel Vasquez told TechCrunch earlier this year that the firm had revamped its program to double down “on the best people.” Under this new approach, Vasquez explained, scouts get more resources but are also expected to be more active, such as by co-hosting dinners with the firm. “They get more budget, but we have reduced the number,” he said.

例如，竞争对手 Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) 曾推出一个全球星探网络，旨在发掘美国及欧洲小型中心城市的投资机会。但 a16z 合伙人 Gabriel Vasquez 今年早些时候告诉 TechCrunch，该公司已对其计划进行了改革，以专注于“最优秀的人才”。Vasquez 解释说，在这种新模式下，星探们获得了更多资源，但也需要更加活跃，例如与公司共同举办晚宴。“他们的预算增加了，但我们减少了人数，”他说。

This also seems to indicate that it is VC firms that have been driving an overhaul of the model. For founders, being a scout is attractive: it brings them closer to firms they may want checks from someday, or even a partner role with, and being seen as someone who can write a check can raise their standing among other founders.

这也似乎表明，正是风投公司在推动这一模式的改革。对于创始人来说，成为一名星探很有吸引力：这使他们更接近那些未来可能希望获得投资的公司，甚至可能获得合伙人职位；而被视为有能力进行投资的人，也能提升他们在其他创始人中的地位。

Some scouts will undoubtedly mourn the end of Sequoia’s program. Among them is Station F boss Roxanne Varza, who wrote on LinkedIn that she was “sad” to hear that the program was getting cut off, and “hope[d] that other funds [w]on’t follow suit.” Varza, a former Sequoia scout from 2020 to 2025, told TechCrunch that she had no information beyond what has been shared publicly, but observed that becoming a scout had often been a stepping stone for aspiring investment managers.

一些星探无疑会为红杉计划的终结感到惋惜。其中包括 Station F 的负责人 Roxanne Varza，她在 LinkedIn 上写道，听到该计划被取消她感到“难过”，并“希望其他基金不会效仿”。Varza 曾于 2020 年至 2025 年担任红杉星探，她告诉 TechCrunch，除了公开信息外她并无其他消息，但她观察到，成为星探往往是有抱负的投资经理的垫脚石。

“I cannot name all of the incredible scouts that have gone on to join or build funds, but there are quite a few. And the scout programs may have helped them get there — allowed them to build an investment track record, allowed them to build relationships with various funds directly, and more,” she wrote to TechCrunch. According to Varza, the program’s impact went beyond the individual level, instead helping the entire ecosystem become more mature. “I believe they have helped to foster the investment culture and early-stage investment landscape that we now see in Europe, for example,” Varza said.

“我无法列举所有那些后来加入或创立基金的出色星探，但确实有不少人。星探计划可能帮助他们实现了这一目标——让他们建立了投资记录，让他们能够直接与各家基金建立联系，等等，”她在给 TechCrunch 的信中写道。Varza 认为，该计划的影响超越了个人层面，有助于整个生态系统变得更加成熟。“我相信他们帮助培育了我们现在在欧洲看到的投资文化和早期投资环境，”Varza 说。

While others may agree with Varza about the role these programs have played, they may still have run their course for two reasons. First, seasoned operators may get more financial upside as angels. When it’s their own money, they get to keep the returns, too. Second, leading VC firms are now big and well-known enough that the best founders tend to come to them, so they worry less about missing deals, except at the very earliest stages or in niches and geographies where they have less reach.

虽然其他人可能同意 Varza 关于这些计划所发挥作用的看法，但由于两个原因，这些计划可能已经完成了其历史使命。首先，经验丰富的运营者作为天使投资人可能获得更多的财务收益。当使用自己的资金时，他们也能保留投资回报。其次，领先的风投公司现在规模足够大且知名度足够高，最优秀的创始人往往会主动找上门来，因此他们不太担心错过交易，除非是在极早期阶段，或者在他们触及范围较小的利基市场和地理区域。

Still, the pull of those early and niche deals is why scouts programs are much more likely to get revamped than to disappear entirely. “While Sequoia may be cutting the program, venture partner-style deals are likely to continue,” said Varza in her email to TechCrunch. “Scouts was just a way to scale the format. I don’t imagine Sequoia would end all forms of venture partners, but they are full of surprises.”

尽管如此，这些早期和利基交易的吸引力正是星探计划更有可能被改革而非完全消失的原因。“虽然红杉可能会削减该计划，但风险合伙人式的交易很可能会继续存在，”Varza 在给 TechCrunch 的邮件中说。“星探只是扩展这种模式的一种方式。我不认为红杉会终止所有形式的风险合伙人合作，但他们总是充满惊喜。”
