# This Totally Free App Can Free Up Space on Your Mac

Save this storySave this storyYour Mac's hard drive is full ... again. What's taking up all that space? Endless applications promise to answer this question and help you free up space, most with a subscription. I've tested many over the years, and few are easier to recommend than Purge.

保存这篇文章。你的 Mac 硬盘又满了……到底是什么占用了这么多空间？无数应用程序承诺能回答这个问题并帮你腾出空间，但大多数都需要订阅。多年来我测试过很多软件，其中很少有比 Purge 更值得推荐的了。

This totally free, open source app gives you a quick breakdown of what's taking up space on your hard drive and offers a few specific ways to free it up. Even better: There are no ads or upsells. Purge can even delete Apple Intelligence's offline models from macOS 27, which could free up over 10 GB.

这款完全免费的开源应用程序可以让你快速了解硬盘空间的占用情况，并提供几种具体的清理方法。更棒的是：它没有任何广告或推销。Purge 甚至可以从 macOS 27 中删除 Apple Intelligence 的离线模型，这能腾出超过 10 GB 的空间。

But even if you want to keep Apple's AI features, you can still clear up plenty of space. Your Mac, like any computer, tends to build up clutter over time. Sure, you might have forgotten about the games, images, and videos you downloaded, but caches also take up space. These are files downloaded and saved to speed things up, which can be useful. The problem is they can stick around longer than necessary and take up space. And then there are caches and settings left behind by apps you uninstalled ages ago—they're still there, eating up precious bytes.

即便你想保留苹果的 AI 功能，依然可以清理出大量空间。和任何电脑一样，Mac 随着使用时间的推移往往会堆积垃圾文件。当然，你可能已经忘记了下载的游戏、图片和视频，但缓存也会占用空间。这些文件是为了加速运行而下载保存的，本是有用的，但问题在于它们往往会滞留过久并占用空间。此外，还有你很久以前卸载的应用程序留下的缓存和设置——它们依然存在，蚕食着宝贵的存储空间。

Purge is designed to help you clean all that out, but it can do more. It's built around lists of files known to be safe to purge. There's also a list of things it will never touch, including crucial system files and your own documents. Finally, Purge sends files to the Trash instead of deleting them outright, so you can always recover them if something breaks.

Purge 的设计初衷就是帮你清理这些垃圾，但它的功能远不止于此。它基于一份已知可安全清理的文件列表进行工作。同时，它也有一份绝不会触碰的文件清单，包括关键的系统文件和你的个人文档。最后，Purge 会将文件移至废纸篓而非直接删除，因此如果出现问题，你随时可以恢复。

Courtesy of Justin PotTo get started, download Purge and install it. Open the application, and it will show you what's taking up space on your Mac.

图片来源：Justin Pot。开始使用前，请下载并安装 Purge。打开应用程序，它会向你展示 Mac 上占用空间的内容。

If you want, click the Clean Safe Items button, and the app will handle it, assuming you trust it. I recommend paying a bit more attention, though. First, delete some caches. You'll see a list of caches, all of which are safe to purge periodically.

如果你愿意，可以点击“清理安全项目”（Clean Safe Items）按钮，只要你信任它，应用就会自动处理。不过，我建议你多留意一下。首先，删除一些缓存。你会看到一个缓存列表，它们都可以定期安全清理。

You'll also see Apple's Aerial screen savers here. These videos are very cool, but take up a lot of space—delete them if you, like most 21st-century people, don't use a screen saver.

你还会在这里看到苹果的“航拍”屏幕保护程序。这些视频非常酷，但占用了大量空间——如果你像大多数 21 世纪的人一样不使用屏保，那就把它们删掉吧。

There's also a Dev Tools section, which isn't relevant for most people. If you use Homebrew, though, don't skip this—it's an easy way to delete cached installation files you don't need anymore.

这里还有一个“开发工具”（Dev Tools）部分，对大多数人来说没用。但如果你使用 Homebrew，千万别跳过这一步——这是删除不再需要的缓存安装文件的简便方法。

The next tool shows you only the largest files on your computer, likely videos or other large media. I ended up moving a few things I'd forgotten about off my laptop—I bet you'll find something, too.

下一个工具只显示你电脑上最大的文件，通常是视频或其他大型媒体文件。我最终把一些自己都忘了的文件从笔记本电脑上移走了——我敢打赌你也能找到一些。

Up next is a tool for uninstalling apps. Note that this doesn't just remove the application—it also removes every file associated with it, including your settings, caches, and anything the application downloaded. Deleting applications in Finder doesn't do this, meaning you might leave all kinds of files behind.

接下来是一个卸载应用程序的工具。请注意，它不仅会移除应用程序本身，还会移除与之关联的所有文件，包括你的设置、缓存以及应用程序下载的任何内容。在访达（Finder）中删除应用无法做到这一点，这意味着你可能会留下各种残留文件。

Speaking of that, there's also a tool that shows leftover data from applications you've already deleted. In my case, it was several gigabytes, all basically wasted. I highly recommend checking this section and deleting anything large that you're confident you won't use again.

说到这里，还有一个工具可以显示你已经删除的应用程序留下的数据。就我而言，这占用了好几个 GB，基本都是浪费。我强烈建议检查此部分，并删除任何你确定以后不会再用的大型文件。

Finally, so long as we're talking about things you're not using, there's Apple Intelligence. Apple's most recent operating systems come preloaded with offline AI models, which power things like notification summaries, writing suggestions, and the new AI-powered Siri. You might like and use these features, but if not, Apple removed the toggle in macOS 27 that lets you disable them altogether.

最后，既然提到了你没在用的东西，那就不得不说 Apple Intelligence。苹果最新的操作系统预装了离线 AI 模型，用于支持通知摘要、写作建议和全新的 AI Siri 等功能。你可能喜欢并使用这些功能，但如果不用，苹果在 macOS 27 中移除了彻底禁用它们的开关。

That's why Purge has one final tool that can completely remove the Apple Intelligence models. Click it, and you'll see a list of installed models above a delete button. Click the button, and Purge will delete the models and prevent macOS from reinstalling them.

这就是为什么 Purge 提供了最后一个工具，可以彻底移除 Apple Intelligence 模型。点击它，你会看到已安装模型的列表以及下方的删除按钮。点击按钮，Purge 就会删除这些模型并阻止 macOS 重新安装它们。

These features are why I plan on keeping Purge around. It's a great way to see what's taking up space on your drive and then do something about it.

这些功能就是我打算保留 Purge 的原因。它是查看硬盘空间占用情况并采取行动的绝佳方式。
