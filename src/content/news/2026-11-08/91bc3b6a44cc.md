简讯发布于：太平洋标准时间 2026 年 11 月 7 日上午 9:24 图片来源：Didem Mente/Anadolu / Getty Images 作者：Anthony Ha，币安首席财务官在 63 亿美元黑客攻击事件后离职，称将“寻求新的事业”。

Binance’s chief financial officer Kaiser Ng is leaving the cryptocurrency exchange. According to Bloomberg, co-CEO Yi He said in a statement that Ng’s departure will allow him to “pursue a new venture after more than five years at Binance,” and that a transition plan has been “implemented over the last half year.”

币安首席财务官 Kaiser Ng 即将离开这家加密货币交易所。据彭博社报道，联席首席执行官何一在一份声明中表示，Ng 的离职是为了让他“在币安工作五年多后去寻求新的事业”，并称过渡计划已在“过去半年内实施”。

The announcement comes barely more than a week after hackers stole $6.3 billion worth of cryptocurrency from Binance users. The company froze withdrawals for several days; after accounts were unfrozen, users withdrew a net $3.6 billion in the first few hours.

此次公告发布距离黑客从币安用户处窃取价值 63 亿美元的加密货币仅过去了一周多。该公司冻结了提现功能数日；在账户解冻后的最初几个小时内，用户净提现了 36 亿美元。

Binance and the FBI blamed a North Korean group called TraderTraitor for the hack. The company also said that it would cover all losses, promising, “Binance will bear the financial impact of the stolen funds. These losses will not be passed on to users.” Ng will be replaced as CFO by Justin Yek.

币安和美国联邦调查局（FBI）将此次黑客攻击归咎于一个名为 TraderTraitor 的朝鲜组织。该公司还表示将承担所有损失，并承诺：“币安将承担被盗资金带来的财务影响。这些损失不会转嫁给用户。” Justin Yek 将接替 Ng 出任首席财务官。
