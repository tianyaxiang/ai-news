Discogs’ Kevin Lewandowski can’t live without his AeroPress. The founder of Discogs talks coffee, Rez, and (of course) records. By Terrence O'Brien, Nov 7, 2026, 4:00 PM UTC.

Discogs 的 Kevin Lewandowski 离不开他的 AeroPress。Discogs 的创始人畅谈咖啡、游戏《Rez》以及（当然）唱片。作者：Terrence O'Brien，2026 年 11 月 7 日，世界标准时间下午 4:00。

Twenty-five years ago, Kevin Lewandowski founded Discogs, arguably the destination online for obsessive vinyl collectors and music nerds of all stripes. It is a marketplace for buying and selling records, obviously, but it’s also become so much more.

二十五年前，Kevin Lewandowski 创立了 Discogs，这可以说是各类黑胶唱片收藏狂热者和音乐极客的在线首选目的地。显而易见，它是一个买卖唱片的市场，但它已经不仅仅于此。

It’s a shockingly comprehensive database that covers not just albums and singles, but all the various versions and pressings. It’s a really fun rabbit hole to disappear down when you’re trying to figure out what obscure pressing of Ministry’s The Mind is a Terrible Thing to Taste or Psalm 69 you had in high school.

它是一个令人震惊的综合数据库，不仅涵盖了专辑和单曲，还包括了所有不同的版本和压片。当你试图找出高中时拥有的 Ministry 乐队《The Mind is a Terrible Thing to Taste》或《Psalm 69》的某种冷门压片时，深入研究这个数据库会是一件非常有趣的事情。

In the years since it debuted, Discogs has grown to be much more than just a site where audiophiles go to trade rare first pressings of Beach Boys records. There are forums, groups, user-created lists, and more. It’s part eBay, part Reddit, and IMDB — just specifically for music.

自问世以来的这些年里，Discogs 的发展早已超越了发烧友交易海滩男孩乐队（Beach Boys）珍稀首版唱片的网站。它现在拥有论坛、群组、用户创建的列表等等。它既像 eBay，又像 Reddit 和 IMDB——只不过是专门针对音乐的。

With more and more people turning their back on the algorithm and rediscovering physical media, now seemed like a perfect time to check in with Lewandowski. What is your most indispensable tool? My AeroPress. I use it multiple times a day. In fact, I just made a coffee with it [sips coffee].

随着越来越多的人背离算法并重新发现实体媒体，现在似乎是采访 Lewandowski 的绝佳时机。你最不可或缺的工具是什么？我的 AeroPress。我每天用它好几次。事实上，我刚用它冲了一杯咖啡 [喝了一口咖啡]。

How many tabs do you have open right now? Probably around 30 on my work computer. My personal computer is worse — probably 40 or 50. Which social media platform do you use the most? I look at X and Instagram occasionally, but I don’t really post.

你现在打开了多少个标签页？工作电脑上大概有 30 个左右。个人电脑更糟糕——大概 40 或 50 个。你最常使用哪个社交媒体平台？我偶尔会看 X 和 Instagram，但我并不怎么发帖。

What is your favorite gadget you’ve ever owned? It’s not really a gadget, but I like to tinker. Lately I’ve been playing around with Home Assistant software and built my own setup to control things like the garage door and outdoor lights. So I guess my favorite gadget is one I made myself.

你拥有过的最喜欢的电子产品是什么？它算不上什么电子产品，但我喜欢折腾。最近我一直在研究 Home Assistant 软件，并搭建了自己的系统来控制车库门和户外灯光等设备。所以我猜我最喜欢的电子产品是我自己制作的那个。

What is one thing you wish you had created? Probably a game like Rez, which came out around 2000. It’s a rhythm game where you’re almost controlling the music as you play, about as close to DJing as a video game can get. I’ve actually had an idea for my own version with a similar concept. It’s still just an idea, though.

你希望自己创造的一样东西是什么？大概是像 2000 年左右推出的《Rez》那样的游戏。这是一款节奏游戏，你在玩的时候几乎是在控制音乐，这在电子游戏中已经非常接近打碟（DJing）了。我其实有一个关于自己版本类似概念的想法。不过，那还只是个想法。

What do you do when you need to focus? I’ll either clear everything off my desk or change locations completely. Go to a coffee shop or sit outside. Sometimes I just need a different environment. What’s the last piece of physical media you bought? Records. I was at Mint Gallery in Portland last week and picked up four.

当你需要专注时会做什么？我会清理桌面上所有的东西，或者彻底换个地方。去咖啡店或者坐在户外。有时我只是需要一个不同的环境。你最后购买的实体媒体是什么？唱片。上周我在波特兰的 Mint Gallery 买了四张。

I still really like going into a record store and finding something to bring home. And I also recently picked up three reissues from the Moodsville / Acoustic Sounds series. What would the tagline for your biopic be? The Secret Weapon. What’s the last GIF or meme you used? I don’t use memes very often, but one I was kind of proud of was putting a Discogs screen into a North Korean war room photo. I was advised that maybe it wasn’t one for the company chat.

我仍然非常喜欢走进唱片店，寻找可以带回家的东西。最近我还买了 Moodsville / Acoustic Sounds 系列的三张再版唱片。你传记片的标语会是什么？秘密武器。你最后使用的 GIF 或表情包是什么？我不常使用表情包，但我比较自豪的一个是将 Discogs 的屏幕画面放进了一张朝鲜作战室的照片里。有人建议我，这可能不太适合发在公司聊天群里。
