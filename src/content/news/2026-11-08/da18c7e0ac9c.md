# Good riddance, Cybertruck!

TransportationPolicyTechGood riddance, Cybertruck!We’ll always have our comically oversized windshield wiper.We’ll always have our comically oversized windshield wiper.by Andrew J. HawkinsNov 7, 2026, 2:40 PM UTC

再见，Cybertruck！我们永远都会记得那滑稽的超大号雨刷器。作者：Andrew J. Hawkins，2026年11月7日，世界标准时间下午2:40。

I’m calling it: time of death, November 6th, 1:30 AM, by tweet. Elon Musk’s declaration Friday that Tesla would be “winding down” production of the Cybertruck next year to make room for the Cybercab was fascinating for a number of reasons. First, it was a humiliating way to kill a vehicle that was once hailed as the pinnacle of Tesla’s engineering — faster than a Porsche 911, more powerful than an F-150, able to leap over the Model Y as a singular representation of Musk’s Blade Runner-inspired designs for the future.

我宣布：死亡时间为11月6日凌晨1:30，通过推文发布。埃隆·马斯克周五宣布，特斯拉明年将“逐步停止”Cybertruck的生产，以便为Cybercab腾出空间，这一声明在多个方面都令人着迷。首先，这是一种羞辱性的方式，终结了一款曾经被誉为特斯拉工程巅峰的车型——它比保时捷911更快，比F-150更强劲，作为马斯克受《银翼杀手》启发设计的未来代表，它甚至超越了Model Y。

“I think the Tesla Cybertruck is our best product ever,” he declared during a 2019 earnings call. (He prefaced the statement by saying, “This could be totally wrong. I could be totally out to lunch here.” It would appear so!) The fact that Musk would just quietly put an end to the Cybertruck without any fanfare was a sign of how far it had fallen, not just in the eyes of the public, but in the eyes of its creator, the man most responsible for brute-forcing this low-emission, low-poly technological nightmare into the world.

“我认为特斯拉Cybertruck是我们有史以来最好的产品，”他在2019年的一次财报电话会议上宣称。（他在声明前曾说：“这可能完全是错的。我可能完全搞错了。”事实证明确实如此！）马斯克竟然在没有任何大张旗鼓的情况下悄悄终结了Cybertruck，这表明它不仅在公众眼中，甚至在它的创造者——那个强行将这个低排放、低多边形技术噩梦推向世界的人——眼中，都已经跌落到了何种地步。

Tesla actually thought the Cybertruck would be a huge hit, targeting production and demand of 250,000 units per year. To date, the company has only sold 70,000 units — total. Musk’s other company, SpaceX, ended up being one of the largest single purchasers of Cybertrucks to absorb unsold inventory. Musk had to become his own customer to make the Cybertruck look better on paper.

特斯拉曾认为Cybertruck会大获成功，目标是年产量和需求量达到25万辆。迄今为止，该公司总共只售出了7万辆。马斯克的另一家公司SpaceX最终成为了Cybertruck最大的单一买家之一，以消化未售出的库存。马斯克不得不成为自己的客户，才能让Cybertruck在账面上看起来好看一些。

But the death of the Cybertruck is even more noteworthy given the timing of Musk’s tweet, coming just three days after his epic flop at the ballot box. Musk poured over $445 million into Republican races, with most of his money targeting his home turf of Texas — headquarters to both Tesla and SpaceX. Between funnels of $168 million to super PACs backing Republican Senate candidate Ken Paxton and nearly $35 million redirected from his own America PAC, Musk dropped more than $200 million on the Lone Star State alone.

但考虑到马斯克发布推文的时间点，Cybertruck的消亡更值得注意，因为这距离他在投票箱前的惨败仅过了三天。马斯克向共和党的竞选活动投入了超过4.45亿美元，其中大部分资金针对的是他的大本营德克萨斯州——特斯拉和SpaceX的总部所在地。通过向支持共和党参议院候选人肯·帕克斯顿的超级政治行动委员会投入1.68亿美元，以及从他自己的“美国政治行动委员会”（America PAC）转出的近3500万美元，马斯克仅在“孤星之州”就砸下了超过2亿美元。

All that money, and zero return. Democrat James Talarico crushed Paxton by 12 points to break a 38-year Democratic Senate drought, while Gina Hinojosa ousted Greg Abbott from the governor’s mansion. Texas turned blue, despite the best efforts of the world’s richest man — or perhaps because of them?

投入了这么多钱，却毫无回报。民主党人詹姆斯·塔拉里科以12个百分点的优势击败了帕克斯顿，打破了民主党在参议院38年的沉寂，而吉娜·伊诺霍萨则将格雷格·阿博特赶出了州长官邸。尽管这位世界首富竭尽全力——或者说正是因为他的努力——德克萨斯州还是变蓝了。

Americans are fed up: they don’t want Musk meddling in their government, and they especially don’t want his stainless-steel monument to his own vanity and utter refusal to accept human feedback. The Cybertruck was one of the most epic failures in modern automotive history. While it succeeded as a viral PR spectacle and initially became a top-selling premium electric pickup in early 2024, it ultimately collapsed under the weight of Musk’s own hubris.

美国人受够了：他们不希望马斯克干预他们的政府，更不希望看到他那座象征着虚荣心、完全拒绝接受人类反馈的不锈钢纪念碑。Cybertruck是现代汽车史上最史诗级的失败之一。虽然它作为一场病毒式的公关奇观取得了成功，并在2024年初一度成为最畅销的高端电动皮卡，但最终还是在马斯克傲慢的重压下崩溃了。

The reasons for the Cybertruck’s failures are almost too many to enumerate: too expensive, not as much range or towing power as promised, too many recalls, and, well, it looked the way it did. But when Musk outed himself as a Trump supporter, forever tying himself to Trump’s MAGA brand, the Cybertruck went with him. Electric vehicles are typically associated with a style of coastal progressivism, but the Cybertruck crossed the aisle to become a physical avatar of Trump’s ugly brand of politics.

Cybertruck失败的原因多到数不胜数：太贵、续航里程和牵引力不如承诺的那样、召回次数太多，而且，嗯，它的外观就是那样。但当马斯克公开自己是特朗普的支持者，并将自己永远与特朗普的“让美国再次伟大”（MAGA）品牌捆绑在一起时，Cybertruck也随之沉沦。电动汽车通常与沿海地区的进步主义风格联系在一起，但Cybertruck却跨越了党派界限，成为了特朗普那种丑陋政治品牌的实体化身。

And more so than any other car in modern history, the Cybertruck was inseparable from its creator. By the time Musk threw hundreds of millions of dollars into Trump’s campaign, launched the America PAC, and took a prominent position in Trump’s administration as the head of the disastrous DOGE project, owning a Cybertruck had become a rolling liability. People who owned a Model 3 or Model Y could credibly claim they bought it before “Elon went crazy,” as the bumper stickers would later declare — a Cybertruck owner could make no such claim.

而且，Cybertruck比现代史上任何其他汽车都更与其创造者密不可分。当马斯克向特朗普的竞选活动投入数亿美元、发起“美国政治行动委员会”，并在特朗普政府中担任灾难性的DOGE项目负责人时，拥有一辆Cybertruck已经成为了一种移动的负债。拥有Model 3或Model Y的人可以理直气壮地说，他们是在“埃隆变疯”之前买的（正如后来的保险杠贴纸所宣称的那样）——而Cybertruck的车主则无法做出这样的辩解。

To be sure, most people who bought Cybertrucks were not interested in distancing themselves from Musk’s brand of trollish politics. A big part of MAGA culture is owning the libs. And because liberals, car purists, and environmentalists couldn’t stand the Cybertruck — mocking everything from its sharp edges to its build quality — buying one became a way to get under their skin. Before long, people were slapping Trump wraps on them, flying flags from the truck beds, and turning them into props for conservative rallies.

当然，大多数购买Cybertruck的人并不想与马斯克那种挑衅性的政治品牌划清界限。MAGA文化的一个重要部分就是“羞辱自由派”。正因为自由派、汽车纯粹主义者和环保主义者无法忍受Cybertruck——嘲笑它从锋利边缘到制造质量的一切——购买它就成了一种激怒他们的方式。没过多久，人们就开始在车身上贴上特朗普的贴纸，在车斗上插上旗帜，并将其变成保守派集会的道具。

But vibes shift, as they always do. Edgelords lose their edge, and voters become weary and wary of efforts to divide them. The blue tsunami was a historic rejection of Trump and Musk’s brand of right-wing grievance politics. When the spectacle wore off, the bluster stopped feeling like rebellion and started looking like a shit way to run a government. In a single election, the country said it was done.

但风向总会转变。那些“边缘领主”失去了锋芒，选民们也对分裂他们的企图感到厌倦和警惕。“蓝色浪潮”是对特朗普和马斯克那种右翼不满政治的历史性拒绝。当奇观褪去，虚张声势不再像是一种反叛，而开始看起来像是治理政府的一种糟糕方式。在一次选举中，这个国家宣告了这一切的终结。

The results on Tuesday also rendered the Cybertruck an instant relic. The election stripped away the sheen of inevitability that once insulated both the billionaire and his pet project. It’s a relief, but a peculiar one at that. Has there ever been a vehicle so desperate to look like the future, only to become an immediate symbol of the past?

周二的选举结果也让Cybertruck瞬间成为了遗迹。这场选举剥去了曾经保护这位亿万富翁及其宠儿项目的那层“不可避免”的光环。这是一种解脱，但却是一种奇特的解脱。是否曾经有过这样一辆车，如此渴望看起来像未来，结果却立即成为了过去的象征？
