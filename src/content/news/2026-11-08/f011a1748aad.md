### Megaspeed exec was recruited to spy on Nvidia CEO, according to US intelligence memo

根据美国情报备忘录，Megaspeed 高管曾被招募以监视英伟达首席执行官。

A memo circulated earlier this year claimed that technology executive Alice Huang was recruited by China’s Ministry of State Security to spy on Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang, according to multiple media reports. The New York Times, which had previously said Nvidia’s chief executive had been the target of Chinese intelligence, was the first to report on the memo, which was reportedly based on U.S. intelligence.

据多家媒体报道，今年早些时候流传的一份备忘录称，科技高管爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang）曾被中国国家安全部招募，以监视英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）。《纽约时报》此前曾报道称英伟达首席执行官一直是其中国情报工作的目标，该报也是首家报道这份备忘录的媒体，据称该备忘录基于美国情报。

The Financial Times subsequently reported that the memo also claimed Jensen Huang had a romantic relationship with Alice Huang, an executive at a Singapore-based company called Megaspeed. An Nvidia spokesperson told the NYT that Jensen Huang’s interactions with Alice Huang were “not unusual,” and that the company does not share classified information with its customers.

《金融时报》随后报道称，该备忘录还声称黄仁勋与爱丽丝·黄存在恋爱关系，后者是一家名为 Megaspeed 的新加坡公司的高管。英伟达发言人告诉《纽约时报》，黄仁勋与爱丽丝·黄的互动“并不罕见”，并表示该公司不会与客户分享机密信息。

A separate NYT report examined New Matrix, a Chinese company authorized by the U.S. Commerce Department to buy advanced Nvidia chips. But at the office address listed by New Matrix, reporters only found “a hollow gray shell,” raising concerns that it could be a front for a blacklisted private equity firm. The Nvidia spokesperson said the company sells “the first, best and most of our technology in America.”

《纽约时报》的另一篇报道调查了 New Matrix，这是一家获得美国商务部授权购买英伟达先进芯片的中国公司。但在 New Matrix 列出的办公地址，记者只发现了一个“空洞的灰色外壳”，这引发了人们对其可能是被列入黑名单的私募股权公司幌子的担忧。英伟达发言人表示，该公司“将我们最先进、最好的技术首先且主要供应给美国”。
