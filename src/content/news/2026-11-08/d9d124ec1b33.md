Hi, friends! Welcome to Installer No. 151, your guide to the best and Verge-iest stuff in the world. (If you’re new here, welcome, get psyched because it’s officially Bad Holiday Movie Season, and also you can read all the old editions at the Installer homepage.)

朋友们好！欢迎来到第 151 期“安装者”栏目，这是你了解世界上最棒、最符合 The Verge 风格事物的指南。（如果你是新读者，欢迎加入，请做好准备，因为现在正式进入了“烂节日电影季”，你也可以在“安装者”主页阅读所有往期内容。）

This week, I’ve been reading about Andrew Garfield and GTA VI and sexy joke thieves, finally actually watching The Sopranos for the first time (I know, I know), mourning the end of Salon, reinstalling the new and extremely improved Notion Calendar, testing the new Keurig Alta coffee machine, reviewing the iPad Mini and the touchscreen MacBook Pro, and really thinking about just throwing it all away and buying an Enough Phone.

本周，我一直在阅读关于安德鲁·加菲尔德、《GTA VI》以及性感笑话窃贼的内容，终于第一次开始看《黑道家族》（我知道，我知道），哀悼 Salon 的终结，重新安装了经过极大改进的 Notion Calendar，测试了新的 Keurig Alta 咖啡机，评测了 iPad Mini 和触屏版 MacBook Pro，并且真的在考虑抛弃这一切，去买一部 Enough Phone。

I also have for you the long-awaited return of a classic Zelda game, a monster movie worth the trek to the theater, a couple of great new phone accessories, awesome retro headphones, and more. Let’s do it.

我还为你带来了备受期待的经典《塞尔达传说》游戏的回归、一部值得去电影院观看的怪兽电影、几款很棒的新手机配件、超酷的复古耳机等等。让我们开始吧。

The Legend of Zelda: Ocarina of Time. I have a confession to make: I’ve never played this game all the way through. Not sure how! Just missed it. But seeing my friends and coworkers fall back in love with Hyrule has convinced me to get all the way into the remake. I genuinely can’t wait.

《塞尔达传说：时之笛》。我得坦白一件事：我从来没有完整地玩过这款游戏。不知道是怎么错过的！就是没玩到。但看到我的朋友和同事们重新爱上海拉鲁大陆，这说服了我一定要深入体验这款重制版。我真的迫不及待了。

Godzilla Minus Zero. Three years ago, Godzilla Minus One was a big hit and a surprisingly great movie. The sequel came with much higher expectations, but everyone seems to agree it’s a deserving follow-up. I’m not generally one for monster movies, but this series really does it for me.

《哥斯拉：负零》。三年前，《哥斯拉-1.0》大获成功，是一部令人惊喜的佳作。续集的期望值更高，但大家似乎都认为它是一部当之无愧的续作。我通常不太看怪兽电影，但这个系列确实很合我的胃口。

The Moft Dynamic Folio Case. Here’s one for all you iPhone Duo buyers. Moft’s cases are a little complicated to figure out, but really cleverly designed to make your phone even more useful. I’m guessing this is going to be a lot better than Apple’s weird case / kickstand thing.

Moft 动态对开保护壳。这是给所有 iPhone Duo 买家准备的。Moft 的保护壳使用起来稍微有点复杂，但设计非常巧妙，能让你的手机变得更加实用。我猜这会比苹果那个奇怪的保护壳/支架组合好用得多。

The Motorola Signature 27. This is just a pure tour de force of a smartphone spec sheet. Monster camera; brightest screen; giant battery. Also very cool to see a flagship phone with support for GrapheneOS, for the less Google-inclined among us. It’s expensive, and only shipping… someday… but I just like that phones like this exist.

摩托罗拉 Signature 27。这简直是智能手机规格表中的巅峰之作。怪兽级的摄像头、最亮的屏幕、超大容量电池。此外，看到一款旗舰手机支持 GrapheneOS 也很酷，这对于我们中不太喜欢谷歌的人来说是个好消息。它很贵，而且还要……等很久才能发货……但我就是喜欢这种手机的存在。
