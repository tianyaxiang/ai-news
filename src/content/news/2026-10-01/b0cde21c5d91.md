本季度的年度故障率（AFR）为 1.73%，是相当长一段时间以来的最高值。我们稍后会讨论其原因，但首先，以下是各季度故障率的对比跟踪。

像往常一样，让我们列出“荣誉榜”。以下硬盘实现了零故障：
* Seagate ST8000NM000A (8TB)
* Seagate ST12000NM000J (12TB)
* Seagate ST14000NM000J (14TB)
以下硬盘仅出现一次故障：
* Seagate ST16000NM000J (16TB)
各位，这里没有笔误：希捷（Seagate）实现了大获全胜。

Out with the old. This quarter, we’ve seen two drives fully retire, the 4TB HGST HMS5C4040BLE640 at nearly nine years old total; and, the 8TB HGST HUH728080ALE600 at nearly eight years old. But where is the new? Just like last quarter, we have no new drive models—a curiosity, especially to have that happen two quarters in a row. 

辞旧迎新。本季度，我们看到两款硬盘完全退役：总服役期近九年的 4TB HGST HMS5C4040BLE640，以及服役近八年的 8TB HGST HUH728080ALE600。但新硬盘在哪里呢？和上季度一样，我们没有引入新的硬盘型号——这很令人好奇，尤其是连续两个季度出现这种情况。

But, also like last quarter, that doesn’t mean no new deployments. While most drive models’ counts stayed more or less the same (plus or minus replacements), we once again see deployment concentrated in the 20TB+ drives.

不过，也和上季度一样，这并不意味着没有新的部署。虽然大多数硬盘型号的数量基本保持不变（仅有少量更换），但我们再次看到部署集中在 20TB 以上的硬盘上。

### What’s going on with the high-end outliers?
### 高端异常值是怎么回事？

This quarter we had three outliers, which, according to a quartile analysis, defines an outlier as an AFR above 6.95%. Those drives are:
* HGST HUH721212ALN604 (12TB)—7.63%
* Seagate ST10000NM0086 (10TB)—9.33%
* Seagate ST14000NM0138 (14TB)—8.26%

本季度我们出现了三个异常值。根据四分位数分析，异常值定义为 AFR 高于 6.95% 的型号。这些硬盘包括：
* HGST HUH721212ALN604 (12TB) — 7.63%
* Seagate ST10000NM0086 (10TB) — 9.33%
* Seagate ST14000NM0138 (14TB) — 8.26%

Each of these drives have some pretty clear reasons for high failure rates. The HGST drive is nearly seven years old, and the 10TB Seagate was nearly 8.5 years old. Meanwhile, both the Seagate drives have a pretty low drive count—the 10TB drive only has 965; whereas the 14TB has 1,235. That means that while they only had 22 and 25 failures (respectively) their AFRs were quite high.

这些硬盘出现高故障率的原因都很明确。那款 HGST 硬盘已服役近七年，而 10TB 的希捷硬盘已服役近 8.5 年。同时，两款希捷硬盘的样本数量都相当少——10TB 型号仅有 965 块，而 14TB 型号有 1,235 块。这意味着尽管它们分别仅有 22 次和 25 次故障，但其 AFR 却非常高。

It’s worth noting there were a total of seven drives that still had an AFR of >3.0%. While we’ve just determined those aren’t outliers, that’s still quite a few high AFRs compared to other quarters—that means that 10 out 31 drives had an AFR higher than 3.0%. 

值得注意的是，总共有七款硬盘的 AFR 超过了 3.0%。虽然我们刚才确定它们不属于异常值，但与其他季度相比，这仍然是相当多的高 AFR 记录——这意味着 31 款硬盘中有 10 款的 AFR 高于 3.0%。

I haven’t done a full analysis of how rare it is to have our distribution of >3.0% skew that high, but a casual look at the last few quarters show it to be skewed towards the higher end of the spectrum. That will, of course, bring up our average. Something to keep an eye on as we move forward.

我还没有对这种 >3.0% 的分布偏高现象的罕见程度进行全面分析，但粗略观察过去几个季度，数据确实向高位倾斜。这当然会拉高我们的平均值。这是我们在未来需要密切关注的问题。
