# Valor, Atreides, and Sequoia back AI startup Flow Engineering at $750M valuation
# Valor、Atreides 和 Sequoia 投资 AI 初创公司 Flow Engineering，估值达 7.5 亿美元

Flow Engineering, a startup that offers AI tools for hardware design, has raised a $50 million Series B round at a $750 million valuation from some big-name investors, the company announced on Wednesday.
Flow Engineering 是一家为硬件设计提供 AI 工具的初创公司。该公司周三宣布，已完成 5000 万美元的 B 轮融资，估值达到 7.5 亿美元，投资方包括多家知名机构。

The round was co-led by Antonio Gracias of Valar Equity Partners (best known for its investments in Elon Musk’s companies, particularly SpaceX) and Gavin Baker of Atreides Management (a hedge fund that has also backed Musk’s companies and others like AI chipmaker Cerebras).
本轮融资由 Valar Equity Partners 的 Antonio Gracias（以投资埃隆·马斯克的公司，尤其是 SpaceX 而闻名）和 Atreides Management 的 Gavin Baker（该对冲基金也曾投资马斯克的公司以及 AI 芯片制造商 Cerebras 等）共同领投。

Sequoia Capital, which led Flow’s Series A round last October, also participated, as did former Sequoia partner Roelof Botha, who invested as an individual investor. Botha has joined Flow’s board, too.
去年 10 月领投 Flow A 轮融资的红杉资本（Sequoia Capital）也参与了本轮投资，前红杉合伙人 Roelof Botha 也以个人投资者身份参投。此外，Botha 已加入 Flow 的董事会。

Three-year-old, San Francisco-based Flow is tackling the difficulties of hardware design by offering AI agents that automatically align CAD drawings with product requirements, simulation results, and other testing.
总部位于旧金山、成立三年的 Flow 致力于解决硬件设计的难题，其提供的 AI 智能体能够自动将 CAD 图纸与产品需求、仿真结果及其他测试数据进行对齐。

It names Anduril, Rivian, Joby Aviation, General Motors PPU (a joint venture between General Motors and TWG Motorsports), RV Tech (a Rivian and Volkswagen joint venture), Stoke Space, and others, as customers.
该公司的客户包括 Anduril、Rivian、Joby Aviation、通用汽车 PPU（通用汽车与 TWG Motorsports 的合资企业）、RV Tech（Rivian 与大众汽车的合资企业）、Stoke Space 等。
