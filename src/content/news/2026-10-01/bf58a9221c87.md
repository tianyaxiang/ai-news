# The new and huger Paramount has a new co-CEO
# 全新且规模更庞大的派拉蒙迎来新任联席首席执行官

Mattel chairman and CEO Ynon Kreiz is joining Paramount to help David Ellison run the company.
美泰（Mattel）董事长兼首席执行官伊农·克雷兹（Ynon Kreiz）将加入派拉蒙，协助大卫·埃里森（David Ellison）共同管理公司。

Paramount is appointing a new co-CEO ahead of the close of its $110 billion merger with Warner Bros. Discovery. Ynon Kreiz, previously Mattel’s chairman and CEO, will be joining Paramount to lead alongside chairman and CEO David Ellison.
在与华纳兄弟探索公司（Warner Bros. Discovery）价值 1100 亿美元的合并案完成之前，派拉蒙正在任命一位新的联席首席执行官。此前担任美泰董事长兼首席执行官的伊农·克雷兹将加入派拉蒙，与董事长兼首席执行官大卫·埃里森共同领导公司。

According to Paramount, “Ellison will focus on the company’s long-term strategy, creative vision and direction, including its talent relationships, strategic partnerships, technology and capital allocation. Kreiz will focus on the company’s day-to-day management and integration of the combined businesses.” The combined Paramount and Warner Bros. Discovery will “report jointly” to Ellison and Kreiz.
据派拉蒙称：“埃里森将专注于公司的长期战略、创意愿景和发展方向，包括人才关系、战略合作伙伴关系、技术和资本配置。克雷兹将专注于公司的日常管理以及合并后业务的整合。”合并后的派拉蒙与华纳兄弟探索公司将向埃里森和克雷兹“共同汇报”。

Kreiz joined Mattel in 2018. While he was leading the company, it launched the hit Barbie film in partnership with Warner Bros. Pictures. His first day at Paramount is October 5th.
克雷兹于 2018 年加入美泰。在他领导公司期间，美泰与华纳兄弟影业合作推出了热门电影《芭比》。他将于 10 月 5 日正式入职派拉蒙。

Today, a judge also approved Paramount’s recently-announced settlement in a lawsuit with several states that had been blocking the Warner Bros. Discovery merger, according to Reuters.
据路透社报道，今天，一位法官还批准了派拉蒙最近宣布的一项和解协议，以解决此前多个州针对华纳兄弟探索公司合并案所提起的诉讼。

On Tuesday, Paramount’s streaming chief, Cindy Holland, announced that she would be stepping down, and Variety reports that HBO boss Casey Bloys is “poised” to take over both Paramount Plus and HBO.
周二，派拉蒙流媒体业务负责人辛迪·霍兰德（Cindy Holland）宣布辞职。据《综艺》（Variety）报道，HBO 负责人凯西·布洛伊斯（Casey Bloys）已“准备好”接管派拉蒙 Plus 和 HBO 的业务。
