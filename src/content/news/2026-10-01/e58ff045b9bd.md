# Neon sticks it to A24 by announcing a Creative Commons SCP Foundation movie
# Neon 宣布制作一部基于知识共享协议的 SCP 基金会电影，以此回击 A24

A24 pissed off one of the internet’s largest horror communities when it announced it was working on an SCP Foundation film, but apparently hadn’t bothered to contact the SCP Wiki team, nor did it commit to releasing it under a Creative Commons license as required. Seeing an easy PR win, Neon is cashing in by announcing it’s making its own SCP movie. But unlike A24, it proactively reached out to the SCP Foundation Wiki team and has pledged to release it under the Creative Commons ShareAlike (CC BY-SA) license.

A24 在宣布制作 SCP 基金会电影时激怒了互联网上最大的恐怖题材社区之一，因为他们显然没有联系 SCP 维基团队，也没有承诺按照要求以知识共享（Creative Commons）协议发布该作品。Neon 看到了一个轻松赢得公关胜利的机会，随即宣布他们也要制作自己的 SCP 电影。但与 A24 不同的是，Neon 主动联系了 SCP 基金会维基团队，并承诺将根据知识共享署名-相同方式共享（CC BY-SA）协议发布该电影。

This is not an “official” SCP film. None of the wiki’s staff is involved in the writing or production, it is not receiving compensation, and the team’s official position is not to endorse such projects.

这并非一部“官方”SCP 电影。维基的任何工作人员都没有参与其编剧或制作，他们也没有获得任何报酬，且团队的官方立场是不为此类项目背书。

Other than the licensing details, there is almost no information about the upcoming film. Neon says it will be released theatrically in 2027, suggesting that this will be a quick turnaround, potentially low-budget affair. That’s not shocking considering the challenges of making a profit on a film made under a ShareAlike license.

除了授权细节外，关于这部即将上映的电影几乎没有任何信息。Neon 表示该片将于 2027 年在影院上映，这表明该项目制作周期较短，且可能是一部低成本电影。考虑到在“相同方式共享”协议下制作电影所面临的盈利挑战，这一点并不令人意外。

In a statement, the SCP Foundation Wiki admin team said that, “The staff of the SCP Foundation Wiki and our community appreciate NEON’s excellent communication and commitment to following the requirements of our license as well as the spirit of collaboration at the core of the SCP Foundation project.”

SCP 基金会维基管理团队在一份声明中表示：“SCP 基金会维基的工作人员和我们的社区非常感谢 NEON 出色的沟通，以及他们对遵守我们授权协议要求的承诺，同时也感谢他们对 SCP 基金会项目核心协作精神的尊重。”
