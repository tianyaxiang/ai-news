### A New E-Reader / 全新电子阅读器

When I got back into reading, I fell in love with ebooks and getting free copies from the library that I would read on my Libby app. That year, for my birthday, I was gifted a Kindle from two different people, and I’ve never looked back. Whether they’re a new reader looking for a better way to read or one who’s complaining about how heavy their books are, an e-reader makes for a great solution (and you can still get those free library books on it!).
当我重新开始阅读时，我爱上了电子书，并喜欢通过 Libby 应用程序阅读从图书馆借来的免费副本。那年过生日，我收到了两个人送的 Kindle，从此便一发不可收拾。无论他们是寻找更好阅读方式的新手，还是抱怨纸质书太重的读者，电子阅读器都是一个绝佳的解决方案（而且你仍然可以在上面阅读那些免费的图书馆书籍！）。

**Kobo Libra Colour ($260 Amazon)**
If your giftee has an e-reader but wants the option to use color, the Kobo Libra Colour is the best option. It lets them use a range of colors as they read, and you can add on a stylus to turn it into a digital notebook. It’s got page-turner buttons, which are great and something you won’t find on Kindle.
如果你的赠礼对象已经有电子阅读器但想要彩色显示功能，Kobo Libra Colour 是最佳选择。它允许用户在阅读时使用多种色彩，你还可以加购一支触控笔将其变成数字笔记本。它配有翻页键，这非常棒，也是你在 Kindle 上找不到的功能。

**Kobo Clara Colour ($180 Amazon)**
This e-reader from Kobo is the same price as the Kindle Paperwhite, but it comes with a color screen. It’s a smaller 6-inch size like the base Kindle, and I love how lightweight it is to hold. It’s a great budget color e-reader if you don’t want the Libra’s stylus and notebook feature.
这款来自 Kobo 的电子阅读器与 Kindle Paperwhite 价格相同，但它配备了彩色屏幕。它和基础款 Kindle 一样是 6 英寸的小巧尺寸，我非常喜欢它轻盈的手感。如果你不需要 Libra 的触控笔和笔记本功能，这是一款很棒的入门级彩色电子阅读器。

**Amazon Kindle Paperwhite (2024, 12th Generation) ($200 Amazon)**
If they don’t have a Kindle, the 12th-edition Paperwhite is our favorite one. It’s got a warm front light and great battery life, and it comes in a fun pink color. Upgrade to Signature for an auto-adjusting light and more storage (aka more books!).
如果他们还没有 Kindle，第 12 代 Paperwhite 是我们的首选。它拥有暖色调前光和出色的电池续航，还有一款有趣的粉色可选。升级到 Signature 版本即可获得自动调节光线功能和更大的存储空间（意味着可以装更多书！）。

**Amazon Kindle Scribe (2nd Gen, 2024) ($400 Amazon)**
The Kindle Scribe 2 is a great e-reader and digital notebook in one. It’s larger than the Libra Color, making it easy to write and draw on, but the downside is it doesn’t have a color screen. There’s a color model coming out this month, though, so keep an eye out to see if we recommend it in our Best Kindles guide.
Kindle Scribe 2 是一款集电子阅读器和数字笔记本于一身的优秀设备。它比 Libra Color 更大，书写和绘图都很方便，但缺点是没有彩色屏幕。不过，本月会有一款彩色型号发布，请密切关注我们的“最佳 Kindle”指南，看看我们是否会推荐它。

### E-Reader Accessories / 电子阅读器配件

From cute cases to handy straps that make reading easier, there’s no lack of fun options to add to a Kindle or other e-readers.
从可爱的保护壳到让阅读更轻松的实用绑带，为 Kindle 或其他电子阅读器增添趣味的选择应有尽有。

**PopSockets Kindle Paperwhite Case ($40 PopSockets)**
PopSockets has a really beautiful collection of Kindle cases that are not only easy on the eyes, but have a MagSafe ring so you can easily pop on a PopSockets grip. My current favorite is the Dragon Sterling design, which always gets me compliments when I’m reading on the go and makes for a great gift for a fantasy reader.
PopSockets 推出了一系列非常精美的 Kindle 保护壳，不仅外观养眼，还内置了 MagSafe 磁吸环，方便你轻松安装 PopSockets 气囊支架。我目前最喜欢的是 Dragon Sterling（龙之纯银）设计，每次我在外阅读时总能收到赞美，对于奇幻小说读者来说，这绝对是一份很棒的礼物。

**SleepCover ($30 Kobo)**
This handy case not only looks nice, but the lines on the cover actually allow it to fold and prop the Kobo up while you read it. So handy! It comes in cute colors and is available for multiple Kobo devices, including the Clara and Libra.
这款实用的保护壳不仅外观精美，盖子上的折痕设计还能让它折叠起来，在阅读时支撑起 Kobo。非常方便！它有多种可爱的颜色，适用于包括 Clara 和 Libra 在内的多款 Kobo 设备。

**PopSockets Heart of Silver MagSafe PopGrip ($40 PopSockets)**
This is my favorite PopGrip. It’s big enough that my small hands can comfortably reach it on the 7-inch Paperwhite and Colorsoft, and it is both cool to the touch and to look at.
这是我最喜欢的 PopGrip 气囊支架。它足够大，即使是我这样的小手也能在 7 英寸的 Paperwhite 和 Colorsoft 上舒适地握持，而且它摸起来和看起来都非常酷。

**Strapsicle E-Reader Hand Strap ($20 Strapsicle)**
These straps from Strapsicle make it really easy to hold up an e-reader. They’re easy to attach, and I even find that just using one of the two straps secures it. The limited-edition neon collection is super fun and worth shopping before it runs out.
来自 Strapsicle 的这些绑带让握持电子阅读器变得非常轻松。它们安装简单，我甚至发现只用两条绑带中的一条就能稳固设备。限量版的霓虹系列非常有趣，值得在售罄前入手。
