# Tesla takes on $30B in credit as it approaches unprofitability
# 特斯拉在接近亏损之际获得 300亿美元信贷额度

Tesla opened $30 billion in lines of credit according to a regulatory filing today. The loans come as company profits have dropped in recent years, and as Tesla guides for more spending in upcoming quarters.
根据今日提交的监管文件，特斯拉已开启了 300 亿美元的信贷额度。随着公司近年来利润下滑，且特斯拉预计未来几个季度将增加支出，这些贷款应运而生。

For its first decade and a half of operation, Tesla sales and revenue basically did nothing but increase. The company grew and grew, at a rapid rate, and justified basically any money spent on chasing more growth with what seemed like near-infinite demand as it scaled. That lasted until 2024, when Tesla went from 38% growth the prior year to a 1% drop in 2024. Since then, profitability has been rough for the company, with it having to book some questionable one-time profits in order to eke out profitability. Even with that, profits have remained low.
在运营的前十五年里，特斯拉的销售额和营收基本保持持续增长。公司以极快的速度不断扩张，并以规模化过程中看似无限的需求，为追求增长而投入的任何资金提供了合理性。这种情况一直持续到 2024 年，当时特斯拉的增长率从前一年的 38% 跌至 2024 年的 1% 下滑。自那以后，公司的盈利之路变得艰难，不得不通过计入一些存疑的一次性利润来勉强维持盈利。即便如此，利润依然处于低位。

One downward pressure on profits has been Tesla’s increased amount of capital expenditures as it chases various pie-in-the-sky ideas like cars without steering wheels, cars that don’t gain any performance benefit from flying, and the promise of one man controlling a trillion-dollar robot army. CapEx more than doubled in the last quarter, and Tesla says that it will remain high in the current environment of extremely high spending by tech companies. The company expects to spend a total of $25 billion in 2026 (up from $8.5 billion in 2025), and analysts expect similar CapEx in 2027.
利润下滑的压力之一在于特斯拉资本支出的增加，因为它在追逐各种空中楼阁般的构想，例如没有方向盘的汽车、飞行性能并无提升的汽车，以及一人掌控万亿级机器人大军的承诺。上个季度资本支出翻了一番多，特斯拉表示，在当前科技公司支出极高的环境下，这一支出水平仍将保持高位。公司预计 2026 年总支出将达到 250 亿美元（2025 年为 85 亿美元），分析师预计 2027 年的资本支出也将维持在类似水平。

So, given falling profits and increasing capital expenditures, Tesla is now taking out a big loan to fill the gap. Today’s regulatory filing shows that Tesla has taken out a total of $30 billion in loan facilities from Citi and Wells Fargo. The loans come in terms between one and five years, and replace a previous $5 billion credit line which Tesla had previously filed for (but had no current debt from). The company says it doesn’t expect to have to draw from the credit line in 2026, but the end of 2026 is only three months away, and a lot of spending is planned for next year.
因此，鉴于利润下降和资本支出增加，特斯拉现在通过大额贷款来填补缺口。今日的监管文件显示，特斯拉已从花旗银行和富国银行获得了总计 300 亿美元的贷款额度。这些贷款期限为一到五年不等，取代了特斯拉此前申请的 50 亿美元信贷额度（目前并无未偿债务）。公司表示预计 2026 年无需动用该信贷额度，但 2026 年底仅剩三个月，且明年已计划了大量支出。

The loan amounts for quite a lot of cash, equivalent to about a quarter worth of Tesla’s revenues at current revenue levels. But compared to Tesla’s profits, which have been razor-thin in the few hundreds of millions in the last few quarters, it’s a much bigger chunk of money. Most worryingly, last quarter, Tesla was cash flow negative for the first time since Q1 2024, when it experienced a massive drop in sales. Prior to that, Tesla had been cash flow positive for years running.
这笔贷款金额巨大，相当于特斯拉当前营收水平下约一个季度的收入。但与特斯拉过去几个季度仅数亿美元、薄如蝉翼的利润相比，这笔钱的规模要大得多。最令人担忧的是，上个季度特斯拉出现了自 2024 年第一季度销量大幅下滑以来的首次现金流为负。在此之前，特斯拉已连续多年保持现金流为正。

**Top comment by Larry Lasareth:**
The $20B term loan is the real tell. It self-terminates: drops to $10B after 12 months, $5B after 15 months, gone at 18 months if undrawn. That's not a "just in case" backstop — that's a use-it-or-lose-it structure. It means Tesla is signaling it expects to draw within 18 months, or the banks are hedging against Tesla not needing it. Either way, the credit is being treated as a deployment tool, not a safety net.

**热门评论（Larry Lasareth）：**
这笔 200 亿美元的定期贷款才是真正的信号。它具有自动终止机制：12 个月后降至 100 亿美元，15 个月后降至 50 亿美元，如果未动用，18 个月后将失效。这绝非“以防万一”的后备方案，而是一种“不用就作废”的结构。这意味着特斯拉在暗示它预计会在 18 个月内动用这笔资金，或者银行在对冲特斯拉不需要这笔钱的风险。无论哪种情况，这笔信贷都被视为一种部署工具，而非安全网。

So, despite Tesla having some $43 billion in cash in the bank as of its last quarterly report, it felt the need to take out a rather large loan to finance its operating activities – automotive is a pretty money-intensive business, after all – and to pay for its ventures into various product ideas that may not produce revenue anytime soon. Tesla has had little to show for its increased CapEx so far. While it is rolling out three products this month (er, well, one has been delayed), all three of those products were announced a decade ago in some form or another. One of them still doesn’t work (Cybercab), one has been slow to ramp (Semi), and one seems to exist just to sell investors on SpaceX merger hype (Roadster). Between those and Tesla’s other promised future products, it’s going to cost a lot to fund all these ideas. It remains to be seen whether or when any of them pan out – and whether Tesla will need more money to finance them beyond this new $30B loan.
因此，尽管根据上一份季度报告，特斯拉银行账户中仍有约 430 亿美元现金，但它仍感到有必要申请一笔巨额贷款来资助其运营活动——毕竟汽车行业是一个非常烧钱的行业——并为其各种短期内可能无法产生收入的产品构想买单。到目前为止，特斯拉增加的资本支出几乎没有带来什么成果。虽然本月它推出了三款产品（呃，好吧，其中一款已经推迟），但这三款产品早在十年前就以某种形式宣布过了。其中一款仍无法正常工作（Cybercab），一款产能爬坡缓慢（Semi），而另一款似乎只是为了向投资者兜售 SpaceX 合并的炒作（Roadster）。在这些产品以及特斯拉承诺的其他未来产品之间，资助所有这些构想将耗资巨大。这些项目是否或何时能成功，以及特斯拉在 300 亿美元贷款之外是否还需要更多资金来支持它们，仍有待观察。
