# EDG C++ front-end goes public
# EDG C++ 前端正式开源

01 Fiscal sponsorship: Receive and manage donations on behalf of EDG.
01 财政资助：代表 EDG 接收并管理捐款。

02 Technical continuity: Sustain the engineering quality EDG users depend on.
02 技术连续性：维持 EDG 用户所依赖的工程质量。

03 Domain expertise: C++ compiler front-end knowledge, from the inside.
03 领域专业知识：来自内部的 C++ 编译器前端知识。

Track 1 Community contributions: Bug fixes and new features from EDG users, submitted as pull requests. Reviewers, who are initially experienced EDG developers, evaluate the changes. Once approved, these changes are merged into the EDG codebase.
轨道 1 社区贡献：来自 EDG 用户的错误修复和新功能，通过 Pull Request 提交。最初由经验丰富的 EDG 开发人员担任审核员，对变更进行评估。一旦获得批准，这些变更将被合并到 EDG 代码库中。

Track 2 Maintenance: always open: Bug fixes and maintenance by the Alliance's EDG developers are published immediately.
轨道 2 维护：始终开放：由联盟的 EDG 开发人员进行的错误修复和维护工作将立即发布。

Track 3 Features: collectively funded: To support the EDG community, the Alliance will provide a way for larger features to be developed and funded by the community. In this way, organizations can support the development of larger features financially rather than committing their developers to the development of these features. Once a feature is funded, it is released to everyone at the same time.
轨道 3 功能：集体资助：为了支持 EDG 社区，联盟将提供一种由社区开发和资助大型功能的方式。通过这种方式，组织可以从财务上支持大型功能的开发，而不必投入自己的开发人员来参与这些功能的开发。一旦某个功能获得资助，它将同时向所有人发布。
