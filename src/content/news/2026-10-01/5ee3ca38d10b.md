# Elon Musk’s Grokipedia has a ‘newly refreshed’ design
# 埃隆·马斯克的 Grokipedia 迎来“焕新”设计

The AI-powered Grokipedia, which recently started updating again after months without edits, just got some visual updates.
由人工智能驱动的 Grokipedia 在沉寂数月后近期恢复了更新，并刚刚进行了一些视觉上的调整。

Grokipedia, SpaceXAI’s AI-powered competitor to Wikipedia, recently started incorporating edits again, and today, it got some design tweaks as part of a v0.3 update, including a new logo and refreshes to its homepage and live edits page. SpaceXAI head of design Benji Taylor calls it a “newly refreshed Grokipedia.”
Grokipedia 是 SpaceXAI 推出的维基百科（Wikipedia）人工智能竞争对手。该平台近期恢复了编辑更新，并于今日作为 v0.3 版本更新的一部分进行了一些设计调整，包括全新的 Logo 以及主页和实时编辑页面的焕新。SpaceXAI 设计主管 Benji Taylor 将其称为“焕然一新的 Grokipedia”。

Grokipedia’s old homepage was pretty much just a logo and a search bar, but the updated homepage includes a list of featured articles, a list of the most-read articles, and a tracker showing the “latest edits.” The new featured list showcases digital representations of books that you can spin around, and the most read section also uses the book metaphor with a horizontally-scrolling shelf of “book” spines. On the new live edits page, it’s easier to see more of the recent changes at once.
Grokipedia 的旧版主页基本上只有一个 Logo 和一个搜索栏，但更新后的主页增加了精选文章列表、阅读量最高文章列表，以及一个显示“最新编辑”的追踪器。新的精选列表展示了可以旋转的数字书籍模型，阅读量最高的部分也采用了书籍隐喻，通过一个可横向滚动的“书脊”架来呈现。在新的实时编辑页面上，用户可以更轻松地一次性查看更多近期变动。

Article pages have a few new design tweaks, too, including a nicer-looking format for the tables at the top of articles with key facts and smaller spacing between lines of text. Otherwise, at least from what I can tell, articles themselves look largely the same.
文章页面也进行了一些新的设计调整，包括优化了文章顶部关键事实表格的格式，并缩小了行间距。除此之外，据我观察，文章本身的内容布局看起来基本没有变化。

The site initially launched in v0.1 in late October 2025 with AI-cloned pages from Wikipedia, and a v0.2 update arrived a month later. But earlier this year, it appeared that Grokipedia stopped making updates altogether, with Lawfare publishing a report in August where it said that “As far as we can tell, no entry has changed in more than three months.” Given these recent updates, it appears there is some interest inside SpaceXAI toward Grokipedia once again, but the company didn’t immediately reply to a request for comment.
该网站最初于 2025 年 10 月下旬以 v0.1 版本上线，内容由人工智能克隆自维基百科，一个月后推出了 v0.2 更新。但今年早些时候，Grokipedia 似乎完全停止了更新。Lawfare 在 8 月发布的一份报告中称：“据我们所知，超过三个月没有任何条目发生过变动。”鉴于最近的这些更新，SpaceXAI 内部似乎重新对 Grokipedia 产生了兴趣，但该公司并未立即回复置评请求。
