# RSS Feeds for Last.fm
# Last.fm 的 RSS 订阅源

Last.fm RSS Feeds So you we're looking for the RSS Feed of Last.fm, and then discovered they were gone! Well, the solution is right here, all you have to do is tell me your username and i'll give you four URLs, one for you recent tracks, one for your loved ones, one for your top tracks and one for your top artists (the last two with an optional period, defaults to 1 month).

你是否在寻找 Last.fm 的 RSS 订阅源，却发现它们已经消失了？别担心，解决方案就在这里。你只需要告诉我你的用户名，我就会为你提供四个 URL：分别对应你最近收听的曲目、你喜爱的曲目、你的热门曲目以及你的热门艺人（后两者支持可选的时间周期，默认为 1 个月）。

also new: images! if last.fm adds something other than a default image, it's large version is added as an enclosure 2025: Added support for recommended tracks. This comes bot in RSS as Soundixx-compatible JSON format.

此外还有新功能：图片支持！如果 Last.fm 添加了非默认图片，其大图版本将作为附件包含在内。2025 年更新：增加了对推荐曲目的支持。该功能同时提供 RSS 格式和兼容 Soundixx 的 JSON 格式。
