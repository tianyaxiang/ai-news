# The Best Gaming Routers (2026): Tested By a Family of Gamers
# 2026年最佳游戏路由器：由游戏玩家家庭实测

Few things are more frustrating than packet loss or high ping causing in-game lag. It always seems to happen at the worst possible moment, when you’re about to snipe an unsuspecting opponent or screech into a hairpin bend. While there are other potential causes, your substandard router could be doing you dirty. The best gaming routers promise rock solid connections and can usually prioritize game-related traffic to reduce the risk of issues. They also frequently feature fancy designs and customizable lighting to fit your gaming room.

没有什么比丢包或高延迟导致的游戏卡顿更让人沮丧的了。这种情况似乎总是在最糟糕的时刻发生——比如当你正准备狙击毫无防备的对手，或是高速冲进发夹弯时。虽然造成卡顿的原因有很多，但你那性能不佳的路由器很可能就是罪魁祸首。最好的游戏路由器能提供极其稳定的连接，通常还能优先处理游戏流量，从而降低出现问题的风险。此外，它们往往拥有炫酷的外观和可定制的灯效，能完美融入你的游戏室。

Now, you don’t necessarily need a special gaming router. A good router is a good router, and you’ll find tons of them in my guides to the Best Routers and Best Mesh Systems. You can also get a better connection simply by running an Ethernet cable from your router to your gaming PC. That said, I have tested several gaming routers, and my top pick is the fastest router I have ever tested.

其实，你并不一定非要买专门的游戏路由器。一款优秀的路由器就是好路由器，你可以在我的《最佳路由器》和《最佳Mesh系统》指南中找到很多选择。此外，只需从路由器拉一根以太网线连接到你的游戏PC，也能获得更好的连接效果。话虽如此，我测试过几款游戏路由器，而我的首选是迄今为止我测试过速度最快的一款。

### Best Asus Gaming Routers
### 最佳华硕游戏路由器

The brand I recommend as the best for gamers is Asus. The Taiwanese company has a long gaming history and dedicated sub-brands like Republic of Gamers (ROG). It produces fast, highly configurable routers with free security software and many gaming extras. Ports are usually generous, there’s no need for a subscription, and you can combine different Asus routers that support AiMesh into a mesh system to extend your coverage. The potential downsides are the relatively high prices, occasionally buggy firmware, and the need to dig into configuration settings to get the best performance.

我向玩家推荐的最佳品牌是华硕。这家台湾公司拥有悠久的游戏历史，并拥有“玩家国度”（ROG）等专属子品牌。它生产的路由器速度快、可配置性高，并附带免费的安全软件和许多游戏增强功能。接口通常非常丰富，无需订阅服务，你还可以将支持AiMesh的不同华硕路由器组合成Mesh系统，以扩大覆盖范围。潜在的缺点是价格相对较高，固件偶尔会有Bug，且需要深入研究配置设置才能获得最佳性能。

### Best Gaming Router: Asus ROG Rapture GT-BE98 Pro
### 最佳游戏路由器：华硕 ROG Rapture GT-BE98 Pro

Topping my testing charts, the Asus ROG Rapture GT-BE98 Pro is a supremely capable, quad-band Wi-Fi 7 gaming router. In terms of raw speed, this is the best router I have tested to date. That alone makes it feel worthy of the “gimmick” of being designed explicitly for gaming. It is very similar to the RT-BE96U in my regular router guide, but with the ROG model, Asus has added a second 6-GHz band on top of the existing 2.4-GHz, 5-GHz, and 6-GHz bands. This massively increases the available bandwidth, affording you plenty of space for multiple gaming streams, or giving you an extra band for wireless backhaul if you decide to set up a mesh using Asus’ AiMesh connectivity.

在我的测试排行榜中，华硕 ROG Rapture GT-BE98 Pro 是一款性能极其强悍的四频 Wi-Fi 7 游戏路由器。就原始速度而言，这是我迄今为止测试过的最强路由器。仅凭这一点，它就配得上“专为游戏设计”的称号。它与我常规路由器指南中的 RT-BE96U 非常相似，但 ROG 型号在现有的 2.4GHz、5GHz 和 6GHz 频段基础上，额外增加了一个 6GHz 频段。这极大地增加了可用带宽，为你提供了充足的空间来处理多个游戏流，或者如果你决定使用华硕的 AiMesh 连接组建 Mesh 网络，它还能为你提供一个额外的无线回程频段。

It also has an extra 10-Gbps LAN port, giving you one 10-Gbps WAN/LAN, two 10-Gbps LAN, four Gigabit LAN, one USB 3.2, and a USB 2.0 port. The USB ports come in handy for NAS devices. You must pay extra for these additions, so if you don’t have multiple gaming streams or a NAS, you can save money with the RT-BE96U and still get great performance.

它还额外配备了一个 10Gbps LAN 接口，总共提供一个 10Gbps WAN/LAN 接口、两个 10Gbps LAN 接口、四个千兆 LAN 接口、一个 USB 3.2 接口和一个 USB 2.0 接口。USB 接口对于连接 NAS 设备非常有用。你需要为这些额外功能支付溢价，所以如果你没有多个游戏流需求或 NAS 设备，选择 RT-BE96U 可以省钱，同时依然能获得出色的性能。

This is also easily the biggest router I have ever tested at nearly 14 inches across, with eight huge antennas, and it weighs 4.4 pounds. It is far from subtle, won’t blend in, and could prove tricky to place due to its size. Because it’s a gaming router, Asus has included Aura RGB lighting, so you can pick the colored lighting you want and make it a feature.

这也是我测试过的体积最大的路由器，宽度接近 14 英寸（约 35 厘米），配有八根巨大的天线，重量达 4.4 磅（约 2 公斤）。它非常显眼，无法低调隐藏，且由于体积庞大，摆放位置可能会比较棘手。作为一款游戏路由器，华硕加入了 Aura RGB 灯效，你可以选择自己喜欢的颜色，将其作为房间的一大亮点。

### Asus ROG Strix GS-BE12000
### 华硕 ROG Strix GS-BE12000

Asus ROG Strix GS-BE12000 for $400: If you don’t want to spend as much, but you’re set on a tri-band Wi-Fi 7 router, and you want lots of ports, this model could make sense. I found it stable and seriously speedy on the 6-GHz and 5-GHz bands. It boasts 320 MHz channels on the 6-GHz band, Multi-Link Operation (MLO), and 4K-QAM. It has a 2.5 Gbps WAN and seven 2.5 Gbps LAN ports, two of which are dedicated gaming ports, and a USB 3.2 Gen 1 port. You get mostly the same excellent software and gaming options as my top pick.

华硕 ROG Strix GS-BE12000（售价 400 美元）：如果你不想花那么多钱，但又执着于三频 Wi-Fi 7 路由器，并且需要大量接口，那么这款型号很适合你。我发现它在 6GHz 和 5GHz 频段上非常稳定且速度极快。它支持 6GHz 频段下的 320MHz 信道、多链路操作 (MLO) 和 4K-QAM。它拥有一个 2.5Gbps WAN 接口和七个 2.5Gbps LAN 接口（其中两个是专用游戏接口），以及一个 USB 3.2 Gen 1 接口。你几乎可以获得与我首选型号同样出色的软件和游戏功能。
