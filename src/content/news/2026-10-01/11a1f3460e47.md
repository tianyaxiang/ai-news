# Here’s what AI leaders are saying about Trump’s new safety plan
# AI 领袖们如何看待特朗普的新安全计划

After hosting a meal with Big Tech leaders on Tuesday, President Donald Trump responded to journalist questions about his artificial intelligence announcements in typical fashion. He said his previous claims regarding fears around AI safety being a “hoax” cooked up by Democrats no longer apply now that he’s renamed the technology to “Super Intelligence.” He brushed off concerns that the government wasn’t enforcing enough AI guardrails because US companies are “dominating China.”
周二，唐纳德·特朗普总统在与科技巨头领袖共进午餐后，以其典型的方式回应了记者关于其人工智能公告的提问。他表示，此前他声称对人工智能安全的担忧是民主党人编造的“骗局”，但现在他已将该技术更名为“超级智能”（Super Intelligence），因此之前的说法不再适用。他驳回了关于政府未能实施足够人工智能护栏的担忧，理由是美国公司正在“主导中国”。

And instead of ushering in federal AI regulations, Trump now has “the smartest people in the world watching over each other.”
特朗普没有引入联邦人工智能法规，而是让“世界上最聪明的人互相监督”。

The people in question here are Google’s Sundar Pichai, Anthropic’s Dario Amodei, Meta’s Mark Zuckerberg, OpenAI’s Greg Brockman, XAI’s Elon Musk, and Nvidia’s Jensen Huang. These tech leaders have signed the Joint Commitment on Frontier Responsibilities: a “morally binding” safety pledge that requires their companies to self-regulate their own AI technology with common sense guidelines and, seemingly, no ramifications for violating the agreement.
这里提到的这些人包括谷歌的桑达尔·皮查伊（Sundar Pichai）、Anthropic的达里奥·阿莫代（Dario Amodei）、Meta的马克·扎克伯格（Mark Zuckerberg）、OpenAI的格雷格·布罗克曼（Greg Brockman）、XAI的埃隆·马斯克（Elon Musk）以及英伟达的黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）。这些科技领袖签署了《前沿责任联合承诺》（Joint Commitment on Frontier Responsibilities）：这是一项“道德约束”的安全承诺，要求各公司以常识性准则对其人工智能技术进行自我监管，且违反该协议似乎不会产生任何后果。

All six of the signees provided press commentary at Trump’s behest, praising the self-policing plans as a positive step toward advancing AI technology, creating jobs, and dispersing negative public opinion around AI safety.
在特朗普的要求下，所有六位签署人均发表了新闻评论，称赞这些自我监管计划是推动人工智能技术发展、创造就业机会以及消除公众对人工智能安全负面舆论的积极一步。

### Mark Zuckerberg:
### 马克·扎克伯格：

“I can kind of summarize what I think we all signed today, which is the White House accord on developing this technology safely. The basic idea is that we want to give the American people and our customers confidence that the technology works in the way we intend[...]”
“我可以总结一下我认为我们今天签署的内容，即白宫关于安全开发该技术的协议。其基本理念是，我们希望让美国人民和我们的客户相信，这项技术正按照我们预想的方式运行……”

“The idea isn’t that this is the only thing that we will ever do. It is that this is a start and an accord that the whole industry could come to and I was very proud to have that discussion with a number of our colleagues who I think are running great companies. It was sort of a historic conversation. I don’t think anything like this has come before and I think this is a meaningful step forward.”
“这并不是说这是我们唯一要做的事情。这是一个开始，也是整个行业都能达成的协议。我非常自豪能与几位我认为经营着伟大公司的同事进行讨论。这可以说是一次历史性的对话。我认为此前从未有过类似的事情，我认为这是一个有意义的进步。”

### Greg Brockman:
### 格雷格·布罗克曼：

“We build artificial intelligence to benefit everyone. We think it can lift everyone up. We see a whole renaissance of small businesses, scientific discoveries really starting to happen, drug development, really being able to improve everyone’s lives. And we think that these accords and under President Trump’s leadership, everyone coming together to agree on how to move forward safely, I think that’s just so important because we’re doing this in order to benefit people in the world.”
“我们构建人工智能是为了造福每个人。我们认为它可以提升所有人。我们看到了小型企业的全面复兴，科学发现真正开始涌现，药物开发也真正能够改善每个人的生活。我们认为，在特朗普总统的领导下，这些协议以及大家齐心协力就如何安全地向前迈进达成共识，我认为这非常重要，因为我们这样做是为了造福世界人民。”

### Jensen Huang:
### 黄仁勋：

“This is the largest industrial revolution in history. And we’re going through the single largest infrastructure buildout in human history. It’s creating a million jobs here in the United States[...]”
“这是历史上最大的工业革命。我们正在经历人类历史上规模最大的基础设施建设。它正在美国创造一百万个就业岗位……”

“There’s no conflict between innovation, technology, and safety. But we are going to have to create new technologies to advance the capabilities of AI, but we’re also creating new technologies to advance the safety of AI. All of this technology is being developed by the industry today. You see behind me just an incredible number of leaders in our industry, moving together as one, and we’re going to advance this technology that is so consequential to humanity, so consequential to the future prosperity of our nation. And we’re going to do it responsibly and safely.”
“创新、技术与安全之间并不存在冲突。但我们必须创造新技术来提升人工智能的能力，同时我们也在创造新技术来提升人工智能的安全性。所有这些技术目前都由业界在开发。你们看到我身后有这么多我们行业的领袖，大家团结一致，我们将推动这项对人类至关重要、对我们国家未来繁荣至关重要的技术。我们将以负责任和安全的方式来完成它。”

### Sundar Pichai:
### 桑达尔·皮查伊：

“[We’re] really proud to be here today as an industry. I think this moment is historic and consequential, and I think there are extraordinary benefits to this technology. You can see it in the GDP growth we see. We have to make sure we are addressing the risks and the President has asked us as an industry to step up and we want to make sure we’re putting out products safely.”
“作为行业代表，我们今天能来到这里感到非常自豪。我认为这一时刻具有历史意义且影响深远，我认为这项技术有着非凡的益处。从我们看到的GDP增长中就能体现出来。我们必须确保应对好风险，总统已要求我们作为一个行业挺身而出，我们希望确保我们发布的产品是安全的。”

People speak about medical benefits, I think maybe it’s worth talking a bit more about that. When you have robots and you have super intelligence, it means that everyone in the world can have better medical care than anyone here, including me. And wouldn’t that be wonderful?”
“我认为值得强调人工智能——呃，超级智能（SI），抱歉——的积极益处。我认为最可能的结果是一个富足的时代，我们不仅拥有全民基本收入，还拥有全民高收入。人们谈论医疗益处，我认为也许值得多谈谈这一点。当你拥有机器人和超级智能时，这意味着世界上每个人都能获得比在座任何人都更好的医疗护理，包括我在内。那难道不美妙吗？”
