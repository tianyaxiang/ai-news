# Sony cancels live-service shooter Fairgames

Fairgames is PlayStation’s latest cancellation as it steps back from many of its live service efforts.

《Fairgames》是 PlayStation 在缩减多项实时服务游戏计划后，最新取消的一款作品。

Sony has canceled Fairgames, a heist game announced in 2023, according to Bloomberg. Fairgames had once been part of Sony’s big planned push for PlayStation live service games, but the company ended up canceling some planned live service titles, including in the God of War and The Last of Us franchises, and it shut down Concord shortly after its failed launch.

据彭博社报道，索尼已取消了于 2023 年公布的抢劫题材游戏《Fairgames》。《Fairgames》曾是索尼大力推动 PlayStation 实时服务游戏计划的一部分，但该公司最终取消了部分已规划的实时服务游戏，其中包括《战神》和《最后生还者》系列的相关项目，并在《星鸣特攻》（Concord）发布失败后将其关闭。

The Fairgames cancellation won’t result in job cuts, and developer Haven Studios will now support other PlayStation studios, Bloomberg reports. The game was canceled following a recent test where “player feedback and retention results were mixed and fell short of the goals we had set,” according to part of an executive memo published by Bloomberg, which also cited “challenging market conditions in the PVP shooter genre.” Sony acquired Haven Studios in 2022.

据彭博社报道，此次《Fairgames》的取消不会导致裁员，开发商 Haven Studios 今后将转为支持其他 PlayStation 工作室。根据彭博社公布的一份高管备忘录显示，该游戏是在近期测试后被取消的，原因是“玩家反馈和留存率表现不一，未达到我们设定的目标”，备忘录中还提到了“PVP 射击游戏市场环境充满挑战”。索尼于 2022 年收购了 Haven Studios。

Sony’s live service efforts have seen some success with Helldivers 2, which is also available on Xbox. Bungie’s Marathon, which launched this year, has had a rough start, but today the studio showed off some new features coming in a December update, including a new co-op PvE roguelite mode and a PvP deathmatch mode. Sony is also working on a live service Horizon spinoff, but recently rebooted it.

索尼在实时服务领域的努力凭借《绝地潜兵 2》（Helldivers 2）取得了一定成功，该游戏也已登陆 Xbox 平台。Bungie 今年推出的《马拉松》（Marathon）开局并不顺利，但该工作室今日展示了 12 月更新中即将加入的一些新功能，包括全新的合作 PvE Roguelite 模式和 PvP 死斗模式。此外，索尼正在开发一款《地平线》系列的实时服务衍生作品，但近期已对其进行了重启。
