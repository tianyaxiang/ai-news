Nancy Pelosi’s replacement is ready to regulate AI —Grace Huckins. As a California state senator, Scott Wiener authored legislation aimed at curbing the risks of advanced AI. Now, after winning a tight congressional race for Nancy Pelosi’s open San Francisco seat, he could play a key role in setting federal AI policy. I spoke with Wiener only a few hours after he had been declared the victor. But he was already thinking about AI risk: during our 20-minute conversation, he used the phrase “recursive self-improvement” half a dozen times. “We need to slow this down,” he told me. “Because it’s moving very quickly, and we could sleepwalk into a disaster.” Still, he describes himself as only “cautiously optimistic” about Congress’s ability to rein in AI. Here’s what he plans to do about it.

The backlash about data centers might really just be about electricity —James O’Donnell. As voters went to the polls across the US last week, I was in Ashburn, Virginia, part of what’s known as Data Center Alley—the world’s largest data center hub. It’s also where the backlash sweeping the country arguably first began to take shape. Big Tech has framed the data center uproar as a culture war over progress. But a few days in Ashburn convinced me the real fault line may be electricity: drawing it from the grid or generating it onsite provokes different forms of blowback. The industry can improve its image, but it can’t escape the consequences of its enormous appetite for electricity. Find out why.

围绕数据中心的强烈反对可能仅仅是因为电力问题——詹姆斯·奥唐奈（James O’Donnell）。上周美国各地选民前往投票站时，我身处弗吉尼亚州的阿什本，那里被称为“数据中心巷”，是全球最大的数据中心枢纽。这也是席卷全国的反对浪潮可以说最初形成的地方。大型科技公司将数据中心的骚乱描述为一场关于进步的文化战争。但在阿什本待了几天后，我确信真正的分歧点可能是电力：从电网获取电力或在现场发电会引发不同形式的抵制。该行业可以改善其形象，但无法逃避其对电力巨大需求所带来的后果。了解原因。

One More Thing: China wants to restore the sea with high-tech marine ranches. It even has a seven-story visitor center shaped like a cartoon starfish. But its main purpose is to breed fish and release them into the ocean. Genghai is part of China's ambitious marine ranching program. Since 2015, the country has built 169 demonstration ranches and released at least 167 billion juvenile fish and shellfish. But the approach has also proved controversial. Dive into China's divisive plans to restore the seas with marine ranches. —Matthew Ponsford
