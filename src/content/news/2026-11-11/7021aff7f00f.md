Amazon is betting that many Ring customers will also want an AI-powered security drone and robot patrolling their homes or offices. But the company’s upcoming Ring Robotic products are also certain to intensify privacy concerns previously raised around a prior Amazon attempt to popularize a flying security camera.

亚马逊押注许多 Ring 用户也会希望拥有人工智能驱动的安全无人机和机器人，在他们的家庭或办公室中进行巡逻。但该公司即将推出的 Ring 机器人产品，肯定也会加剧此前围绕亚马逊推广飞行安全摄像头的尝试所引发的隐私担忧。

The Ring Rover Cam and Ring Drone Cam are expected to reach customers starting at the end of 2026 and in early 2027, respectively, according to an Amazon announcement posted on November 10. Amazon describes the devices as relying on an AI Pilot system that uses each device’s cameras along with “specialized vision models, on-device sensors and cloud processing” to plan patrol routes while autonomously navigating around obstacles such as furniture.

根据亚马逊 11 月 10 日发布的公告，Ring Rover Cam 和 Ring Drone Cam 预计将分别于 2026 年底和 2027 年初送达客户手中。亚马逊称，这些设备依赖于一套“AI 领航”（AI Pilot）系统，该系统利用每台设备的摄像头，结合“专业视觉模型、设备传感器和云处理”来规划巡逻路线，同时在家具等障碍物周围进行自主导航。

Both devices also use that AI Pilot system to digitally map the floor plan of the customer’s home or business. This setup process occurs after the customer carries the Ring drone through their indoor spaces or allows the Ring Rover to do some autonomous exploration.

这两款设备还使用该“AI 领航”系统对客户家庭或企业的平面图进行数字化测绘。这一设置过程是在客户携带 Ring 无人机穿过室内空间，或允许 Ring Rover 进行自主探索之后进行的。

The AI Pilot can supposedly use that guided- or self-tour information to identify specific rooms and suggest patrol points near side doors, windows, or even sensitive locations such as a cash register in the case of a business. However, flying and roving cameras with AI-enabled features could introduce new privacy risks for homes and businesses, on top of existing concerns involving fixed Ring cameras installed in room corners or as doorbell cameras.

据称，“AI 领航”系统可以利用这些引导式或自主巡视的信息来识别特定房间，并建议在侧门、窗户附近，甚至在企业环境下的收银机等敏感位置设置巡逻点。然而，具备人工智能功能的飞行和移动摄像头，除了现有的关于安装在房间角落或作为门铃摄像头的固定式 Ring 摄像头的担忧外，还可能为家庭和企业带来新的隐私风险。

Return of the Ring drone. Amazon’s Ring Drone appears to be a direct successor to the company’s Ring Always Home Cam that was only temporarily available for purchase by invitation as a “Day 1 Edition” product in September 2021—even having the same starting price of $250. But the Ring Drone still requires authorization from the Federal Communications Commission before going on sale.

Ring 无人机的回归。亚马逊的 Ring 无人机似乎是该公司 Ring Always Home Cam 的直接继任者，后者仅在 2021 年 9 月作为“第一天版本”（Day 1 Edition）产品通过邀请方式短暂发售过，甚至起售价也相同，均为 250 美元。但 Ring 无人机在上市前仍需获得美国联邦通信委员会（FCC）的授权。

Assuming that comes through, Amazon aims to make the drone available on January 27, 2027, with customers able to register for email updates through the product page. The drone can supposedly follow a set route and schedule for customers who subscribe to the Amazon Ring Protect plan. It can automatically fly along those set routes when responding to a notification from a Ring camera, sensor, or alarm.

假设授权获批，亚马逊计划于 2027 年 1 月 27 日推出该无人机，客户可以通过产品页面注册以获取电子邮件更新。据称，订阅了 Amazon Ring Protect 计划的客户可以让无人机按照设定的路线和时间表运行。当响应来自 Ring 摄像头、传感器或警报器的通知时，它可以自动沿这些设定路线飞行。

Onboard camera capability includes a 148-degree field of view and 360-degree rotation with a 2K video resolution. Two LED lights also activate in low-light conditions to help illuminate the scene for the drone video camera. However, the drone is limited to just 8 minutes of flight time when operating on a “new, fully-charged battery under controlled conditions” according to the product description. It comes with a docking station for charging.

机载摄像头功能包括 148 度视野和 360 度旋转，视频分辨率为 2K。在光线不足的情况下，两个 LED 灯也会自动开启，帮助无人机摄像头照亮场景。然而，根据产品描述，该无人机在“受控条件下使用全新、充满电的电池”时，飞行时间仅限于 8 分钟。它配有一个用于充电的基站。

Another caveat in the product description is that the Ring Drone Cam may experience degraded performance when navigating in environments with reflective surfaces such as “polished marble or wet tile,” blank matte walls without any features, or transparent surfaces like frameless glass and mirrors. Still, Amazon touts the drone as providing an “autonomous way to check on your home that has never existed before,” whether the customer is away or at home. The Ring Drone can be controlled directly from the Alexa Home Map interactive map that shows all of a customer’s Amazon-linked devices.

产品描述中的另一个注意事项是，当在具有“抛光大理石或湿瓷砖”等反射表面、没有任何特征的空白哑光墙壁，或无框玻璃和镜子等透明表面的环境中导航时，Ring Drone Cam 的性能可能会下降。尽管如此，亚马逊仍吹捧该无人机提供了一种“前所未有的自主查看家庭的方式”，无论客户是在外还是在家。Ring 无人机可以直接通过 Alexa Home Map 交互式地图进行控制，该地图显示了客户所有与亚马逊关联的设备。

Roaming robot. Meanwhile, Amazon has pitched the Ring Rover Cam priced at $1,000 as “an autonomous security robot designed for business.” This product is already available for preorder for those so inclined and scheduled to start shipping to customers on December 16, 2026. The company described the Ring Rover as being inspired by customer feedback involving another invite-only beta product, the Amazon Astro robot designed for home monitoring.

移动机器人。与此同时，亚马逊将售价 1,000 美元的 Ring Rover Cam 推销为“专为企业设计的自主安全机器人”。该产品现已开放预购，计划于 2026 年 12 月 16 日开始向客户发货。该公司称，Ring Rover 的灵感来自于客户对另一款仅限受邀参与测试的产品——专为家庭监控设计的 Amazon Astro 机器人的反馈。

The Ring Rover can livestream and record in up to 4K video resolution when patrolling or while doing a 360-degree scan at a designated location. Videos triggered by doorbell rings, motion events and “Live View” events are stored in the cloud for sharing and replaying for up to 180 days. Like the Ring Drone, the Ring Rover can automatically respond to alerts from connected Ring devices by following its preprogrammed patrol points.

Ring Rover 在巡逻或在指定地点进行 360 度扫描时，可以进行最高 4K 分辨率的视频直播和录制。由门铃响铃、运动事件和“实时查看”（Live View）事件触发的视频会存储在云端，可供分享和回放，保存期限最长为 180 天。与 Ring 无人机一样，Ring Rover 可以通过遵循预设的巡逻点，自动响应来自已连接的 Ring 设备的警报。

But the wheeled robot is only designed to operate within a single-floor, indoor environment because it lacks stair-climbing capability. Amazon’s product description also warns that the Rover is not compatible with “curved edges, sunken areas, flooring transitions greater than 1.375 inches (3.5 centimeters), and black glossy floors.” To help compensate for its ground-based view, the Ring Rover comes with an automatically adjustable mast to peer over counters, shelving and other obstacles.

但这款轮式机器人仅设计用于单层室内环境，因为它不具备爬楼梯的能力。亚马逊的产品描述还警告称，Rover 不适用于“弧形边缘、下沉区域、大于 1.375 英寸（3.5 厘米）的地板过渡处以及黑色光面地板”。为了弥补其地面视角的不足，Ring Rover 配备了一个可自动调节的桅杆，以便越过柜台、货架和其他障碍物进行观察。

It can supposedly cover up to 5,000 square feet (465 square meters), according to Amazon’s product description. The Ring Rover also comes with additional security-related features such as a siren and light tower to loudly announce its presence, along with a microphone for two-way audio communication if the customer wants to address someone near the robot.

根据亚马逊的产品描述，它据称可以覆盖多达 5,000 平方英尺（465 平方米）的区域。Ring Rover 还配备了其他与安全相关的功能，例如警报器和灯塔，可以大声宣告其存在，并配有麦克风，如果客户想与机器人附近的人交谈，可以进行双向音频通信。

Privacy concerns rising. Amazon touts the new Ring Robotics products as offering “privacy and security without compromise,” with features such as LED indicators and screen notifications informing customers when video and audio recordings are active. But Ring, as an Amazon subsidiary, has been previously implicated in multiple privacy and security scandals.

隐私担忧加剧。亚马逊吹捧新的 Ring 机器人产品提供了“毫不妥协的隐私和安全”，并具备 LED 指示灯和屏幕通知等功能，在视频和音频录制处于活动状态时通知客户。但作为亚马逊的子公司，Ring 此前曾卷入多起隐私和安全丑闻。

Ring was previously charged in a 2023 Federal Trade Commission investigation with allowing employees and contractors to watch Ring video recordings of customers’ private spaces. The FTC also described Ring as having lackluster safeguards that allowed hackers to access 55,000 US customers’ accounts.
