David Turturean, a recent MIT graduate and a member of the technical staff at the startup lab Physical Superintelligence (PSI), used early access to Anthropic’s Fable 5.2 model to solve an 18-year-old problem about quantum computing. “I’m totally accepting the idea that this might be the last paper I ever put out,” Turturean, who deferred plans to pursue a master’s at MIT’s prestigious computer science and AI lab to join PSI, told TechCrunch. “In a few months, like, why would you even put out a paper?”

麻省理工学院应届毕业生、初创实验室 Physical Superintelligence (PSI) 的技术人员 David Turturean 利用 Anthropic 公司 Fable 5.2 模型的早期访问权限，解决了一个困扰量子计算领域 18 年的问题。“我完全接受这可能是我发表的最后一篇论文，”Turturean 对 TechCrunch 表示。他此前推迟了在麻省理工学院著名计算机科学与人工智能实验室攻读硕士学位的计划，转而加入 PSI。“几个月后，谁还会去发表论文呢？”

The problem in question, the Aaronson-Ambainis conjecture, concerns the ability of quantum computers to solve problems faster than classical ones. Scott Aaronson, one of the two researchers who posed the problem, writes that he doesn’t quite understand the proof but that he believes it’s correct because it was autoformalized in Lean, a coding language for math, and explained by a team of top researchers with whom PSI shared the solution.

该问题即 Aaronson-Ambainis 猜想，涉及量子计算机解决问题的速度是否快于经典计算机。该问题的提出者之一 Scott Aaronson 写道，他虽然不太理解这个证明过程，但相信它是正确的，因为该证明是在数学编码语言 Lean 中自动形式化的，并由 PSI 分享解决方案的一组顶尖研究人员进行了讲解。

The solution appears to close the door on one avenue quantum computers could follow to solve certain difficult problems at high speed, but not all of them. “It’s a negative result that allows people to know, okay, we’re no longer looking there,” Turturean said. More broadly, it reflects the upheaval in the scientific world caused by AI problem-solving.

这一解决方案似乎堵死了量子计算机高速解决某些难题的一条路径，但并非全部。Turturean 说：“这是一个否定性结果，它让人们知道，好吧，我们不必再往那个方向看了。”从更广泛的意义上讲，这反映了人工智能解决问题给科学界带来的剧变。

“A proof of full [Aaronson-Ambainis conjecture] was one of the last remaining open problems in quantum query complexity that everyone seemed to agree was centrally important,” Aaronson, who described himself feeling satisfied, awestruck, disappointed, and fatigued by the news, wrote. “I was surprised, honestly, to see it missing from OpenAI’s Mathocalypse dump a few weeks ago. Surely they tried it, so was it really so much harder than all the problems that were solved?”

“对 [Aaronson-Ambainis 猜想] 的完整证明是量子查询复杂度领域最后剩下的未解难题之一，每个人似乎都认为它至关重要，”Aaronson 写道。他形容自己对这一消息感到既满足又敬畏，既失望又疲惫。“老实说，我很惊讶几周前 OpenAI 的‘数学启示录’（Mathocalypse）发布中竟然没有它。他们肯定尝试过，难道它真的比所有那些被解决的问题难得多吗？”

That’s a point in favor of PSI’s model, which is to solve scientific problems with specialized model harnesses and scaffolding. “An effective kind of research system should have lots of things related to harnesses, lots related to tools, memory systems, simulation infrastructure,” PSI CEO Matt Pines told TechCrunch. “You want to be able to really go after a problem at breadth and depth, and be able to consider a wide range of potential alternatives.”

这一点支持了 PSI 的模式，即利用专门的模型工具和框架来解决科学问题。“一个有效的科研系统应该包含许多与工具、记忆系统和模拟基础设施相关的内容，”PSI 首席执行官 Matt Pines 对 TechCrunch 说。“你希望能够真正从广度和深度上去攻克一个问题，并能够考虑广泛的潜在替代方案。”

For now, the company’s revenue comes from using its tools to optimize data centers, simulating the variables of heat, power, water, sound — “where the performance of that site as well as its impact on the community are a direct function of its physics.” The long play, however, is converting theoretical physics breakthroughs into actual technologies.

目前，该公司的收入来自利用其工具优化数据中心，模拟热量、电力、水和声音等变量——“在该领域，站点的性能及其对社区的影响是其物理特性的直接函数。”然而，其长远目标是将理论物理学的突破转化为实际技术。

“Six to nine months ago, when I was talking to investors, like I had to sort of stretch to validate that conditional, right? Like, ‘no, no, no, trust me, pretty soon, theoretical physics is going to be mostly the domain of machines,’” Pines said. “And it’s not there yet, but it’s on its way.”

“六到九个月前，当我和投资者交谈时，我不得不费力去验证那个条件，对吧？比如，‘不，不，不，相信我，很快，理论物理学将主要成为机器的领域，’”Pines 说。“虽然现在还没到那一步，但已经在路上了。”

Still, while AI has claimed important solutions in fields like mathematics and computer science where it’s possible to express them in validated code, doing so in less abstracted disciplines like the life sciences or physics has proven more difficult. Last week, the startup Discovered Materials released a $1 million grand challenge for researchers using AI to understand problems like superconductivity and heat dissipation.

尽管如此，虽然人工智能在数学和计算机科学等可以通过验证代码表达的领域取得了重要成果，但在生命科学或物理学等抽象程度较低的学科中，这样做已被证明更为困难。上周，初创公司 Discovered Materials 发起了一项 100 万美元的重大挑战，旨在鼓励研究人员利用人工智能来理解超导和散热等问题。

“At the end of the day, physics is different than math because it’s the real world … nature gets a vote in your models of reality,” Pines said. He said the startup is focused on understanding how to take computational results and validate them with experiments in the most efficient way possible. “If it’s a true fact of nature, that’s a trick that you can exploit,” Pines said. “You could build a device out of it, right? The AI suggests an idea; it allows you to do something in the world you weren’t able to do before … and so each of those are kind of loops that PSI is building.”

“归根结底，物理学与数学不同，因为它涉及现实世界……大自然会对你的现实模型进行投票，”Pines 说。他表示，该初创公司正专注于研究如何以最高效的方式获取计算结果并通过实验进行验证。“如果这是大自然的一个真实事实，那这就是你可以利用的诀窍，”Pines 说。“你可以据此制造出一台设备，对吧？人工智能提出了一个想法；它让你能够在这个世界上做以前做不到的事情……因此，这些都是 PSI 正在构建的循环。”

As “neolabs for science” become a pathway for potentially groundbreaking science, the participants are still figuring out what the standards are for this new world. Because PSI had to hold its result until Anthropic’s model was nearly ready for release, they were able to share it with relevant researchers to better understand it.

随着“科学新实验室”成为潜在突破性科学研究的途径，参与者们仍在摸索这个新世界的标准。由于 PSI 必须在 Anthropic 的模型准备好发布之前保留其结果，他们得以将其与相关研究人员分享，以便更好地理解它。

“As an academic, we wanted to play our role in doing things the right way,” Jim Halverson, a professor at Northeastern University who advises PSI, said. He’ll be attending a summit at MIT next week where leading physicists will try to hash out a response to the new pace of science. “Best practices are still emerging … We figured that transparency is a good way to go, and contacting experts is a good way to go.”

“作为一名学者，我们希望以正确的方式发挥我们的作用，”担任 PSI 顾问的东北大学教授 Jim Halverson 说。他下周将参加在麻省理工学院举行的一次峰会，届时顶尖物理学家将尝试商讨如何应对科学发展的新节奏。“最佳实践仍在涌现……我们认为透明度是一个好的方向，联系专家也是一个好的方向。”

“We had some time to sit on it … and it was the first time that I actually took one of my AI-elicited solutions on print, and I read through it, and I was like, okay, how can I make this … be presented more naturally for a human?” Turturean, who said the academic community will expect him to be able to explain the result, told TechCrunch. “And I think it also made me realize, oh my God, the weight of what AI can do.”

“我们有一些时间来沉淀它……这是我第一次真正把我的人工智能导出的解决方案打印出来，我通读了一遍，然后我在想，好吧，我该如何让它……以一种对人类更自然的方式呈现出来？”Turturean 对 TechCrunch 说，他表示学术界会期望他能够解释这一结果。“我认为这也让我意识到，天哪，人工智能所能做的事情的分量。”
