Nuclear startup X-energy announced Monday that it had been given the go-ahead from federal officials to build its first four commercial reactors. The Nuclear Regulatory Commission (NRC) approved the construction permit application for four of X-energy’s Xe-100 reactors at a Dow chemical plant in Texas. Each reactor will be capable of generating 80 megawatts of electricity and 200 megawatts of heat. X-energy says the approval is the first for an advanced nuclear reactor at an industrial site in the United States.

核能初创公司 X-energy 周一宣布，已获得联邦官员批准，将建造其首批四座商用反应堆。美国核管理委员会（NRC）批准了在德克萨斯州一家陶氏化学工厂建造四座 X-energy Xe-100 反应堆的施工许可申请。每座反应堆将能够产生 80 兆瓦的电力和 200 兆瓦的热能。X-energy 表示，这是美国工业场所首次获准建设先进核反应堆。

The U.S. nuclear industry has pinned its hopes on small modular reactors — or SMRs — like those being developed by X-energy. By shrinking the reactor, proponents say it will speed construction and reduce costs, though those advantages have yet to be proven. No SMR is currently in operation in the U.S., and X-energy says Dow’s reactors won’t be operational until the early 2030s.

美国核工业将希望寄托在像 X-energy 正在开发的那种小型模块化反应堆（SMR）上。支持者称，通过缩小反应堆体积，可以加快施工速度并降低成本，尽管这些优势尚未得到证实。目前美国尚无投入运营的 SMR，X-energy 表示，陶氏化学的反应堆要到 2030 年代初才能投入运营。

X-energy claims that its reactor design, which flows helium gas over uranium-containing fuel pellets, is safer than many existing reactors, but the Union of Concerned Scientists questioned the claim and the speed with which X-energy received the permit. “The NRC only accomplished this feat by pushing consideration of critical safety questions to a later date—issues the NRC should’ve fully resolved before green lighting construction,” Edwin Lyman, director of nuclear power safety for the organization, said in a statement.

X-energy 声称其反应堆设计（通过含铀燃料颗粒输送氦气）比许多现有反应堆更安全，但忧思科学家联盟对这一说法以及 X-energy 获得许可的速度提出了质疑。该组织核电安全主任埃德温·莱曼（Edwin Lyman）在一份声明中表示：“NRC 只有通过将关键安全问题的考量推迟到以后，才实现了这一壮举——这些问题本应在批准施工前就得到彻底解决。”

The nuclear industry has benefited from strong interest from hyperscalers and data center developers, who are desperate for any and all sources of electricity. Amazon’s Climate Pledge Fund led X-energy’s $500 million Series C-1, and the big tech company has also committed to buying 5 gigawatts of capacity from the nuclear startup. X-energy rode that wave of excitement earlier this year when its April initial public offering raised $1 billion.

核工业受益于超大规模企业和数据中心开发商的浓厚兴趣，他们迫切需要各种电力来源。亚马逊的气候承诺基金领投了 X-energy 5 亿美元的 C-1 轮融资，这家科技巨头还承诺从该核能初创公司购买 5 吉瓦的电力容量。今年早些时候，X-energy 乘着这股热潮，在 4 月份的首次公开募股中筹集了 10 亿美元。

But since those days, investors’ optimism has tempered. The hesitancy isn’t surprising: Nuclear power plants are expensive to build and can take years to complete. Eventually, X-energy hopes to rein in both time and cost, but its first reactors are likely to be pricey. The new permit approval gave X-energy a small jolt yesterday, though it was only enough to recover last week’s losses. The company’s shares are trading at less than half their initial offering price.

但自那时起，投资者的乐观情绪有所减弱。这种犹豫并不令人意外：核电站建设成本高昂，且可能需要数年时间才能完工。X-energy 希望最终能控制时间和成本，但其首批反应堆很可能价格不菲。新的许可批准昨日给 X-energy 带来了一点提振，但这仅足以弥补上周的损失。该公司目前的股价不到其首次发行价的一半。
