# Google is making Pixel HiLight slightly more useful

Google 正在让 Pixel HiLight 变得稍微实用一些。

The November 2026 Pixel Drop includes a small improvement for the HiLight light and a bunch of software updates.

2026 年 11 月的 Pixel Drop 更新包含对 HiLight 灯的一项小改进以及一系列软件更新。

Google is adding a new feature to its underwhelming HiLight light on the back of Pixel 11 Pro phones: As part of the November 2026 Pixel Drop, the light can light up when you get Google Messages texts from your favorite contacts.

Google 正在为其 Pixel 11 Pro 手机背面表现平平的 HiLight 灯增加一项新功能：作为 2026 年 11 月 Pixel Drop 更新的一部分，当您收到来自常用联系人的 Google Messages 短信时，该灯可以亮起。

Sure, it’s a small addition to the disappointing ways you could use HiLight at the Pixel 11 Pro’s August launch, which included glowing when you used Gemini and lighting up for calls from your favorite contacts. But it’s something!

当然，这只是对 Pixel 11 Pro 八月发布时 HiLight 令人失望的使用方式的一点小补充，当时的功能仅包括使用 Gemini 时发光以及收到常用联系人来电时亮起。但总比没有好！

Fortunately, that’s not everything included in the Pixel Drop, as a bunch of other new features are coming to a wider range of Pixel devices. The “Create My Widget” feature to build a custom widget with a prompt is rolling out to Pixel 11 and Pixel 10 phones.

幸运的是，Pixel Drop 包含的内容不止于此，还有许多其他新功能正推广到更广泛的 Pixel 设备上。通过提示词构建自定义小部件的“Create My Widget”功能正在向 Pixel 11 和 Pixel 10 手机推送。

Google is bringing Gemini Intelligence features to Pixel 10 devices, including the Rambler transcription tool. Users with Pixel 11 and Pixel 10 phones will be able to have Gemini make calls on your behalf to help with tasks like making reservations or checking inventory.

Google 正在将 Gemini 智能功能引入 Pixel 10 设备，包括 Rambler 转录工具。拥有 Pixel 11 和 Pixel 10 手机的用户将能够让 Gemini 代替他们拨打电话，以协助完成预订或查询库存等任务。

If you have a Pixel Watch 3 or newer, you’ll be able to see step-by-step instructions during workouts. And users on Pixel 6A phones and newer will be able to take advantage of a new “Family Hub” spot in settings that consolidates family management controls.

如果您拥有 Pixel Watch 3 或更新机型，则可以在锻炼期间查看分步指导。此外，Pixel 6A 及更新机型的用户将能够利用设置中新增的“家庭中心”（Family Hub）功能，该功能整合了家庭管理控制选项。

Google is also announcing today that its Pixel Tag item tracker is now available. It was originally announced in August.

Google 今天还宣布其 Pixel Tag 物品追踪器现已上市。该产品最初于八月发布。
