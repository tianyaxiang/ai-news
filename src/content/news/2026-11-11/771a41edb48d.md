The VR Disney Plus app on Meta Quest now has 3D movies.

Meta Quest 上的 Disney Plus VR 应用程序现已支持 3D 电影。

With the updated Disney Plus Quest app, you’ll be able to stream more than 60 3D movies and watch films in immersive virtual environments.

通过更新后的 Disney Plus Quest 应用程序，您将能够在线观看 60 多部 3D 电影，并在沉浸式虚拟环境中欣赏影片。

Disney is bringing more than 60 3D movies to the Disney Plus app on Meta Quest. The movies will be available to Disney Plus subscribers as part of an updated Disney Plus app that’s rolling out today.

迪士尼正在将 60 多部 3D 电影引入 Meta Quest 上的 Disney Plus 应用程序。作为今天推出的更新版 Disney Plus 应用程序的一部分，这些电影将向 Disney Plus 订阅用户开放。

The 3D movies you can check out include The Avengers and Black Panther, Meta says. But the updated Quest app doesn’t just offer 3D movies; you’ll also be able to see some films in themed, immersive virtual environments, like a Tatooine environment when watching Star Wars: A New Hope or a Scare Floor one for Monsters, Inc.

Meta 表示，您可以观看的 3D 电影包括《复仇者联盟》和《黑豹》。但更新后的 Quest 应用程序不仅提供 3D 电影；您还可以在主题沉浸式虚拟环境中观看某些影片，例如在观看《星球大战：新希望》时体验塔图因环境，或在观看《怪兽电力公司》时体验惊吓楼层环境。

I got to see an Avengers-themed immersive environment for myself while trying Meta’s forthcoming VR Glasses in September, and while it was novel for the few minutes I tried it, I wasn’t sure if it would be good during an entire movie.

九月份在试用 Meta 即将推出的 VR 眼镜时，我亲自体验了复仇者联盟主题的沉浸式环境。虽然在我试用的那几分钟里感觉很新奇，但我并不确定在观看整部电影时这种体验是否依然良好。

The Disney Plus app on the VR Glasses, which are set to release sometime in spring 2027, will also let you watch IMAX Enhanced movies, including Avengers: Endgame, Lightyear, and Tron: Ares. The VR Glasses will also be a place to watch 3D movies from Lionsgate.

这款 VR 眼镜预计将于 2027 年春季发布，其上的 Disney Plus 应用程序还将允许用户观看 IMAX Enhanced 电影，包括《复仇者联盟 4：终局之战》、《光年正传》和《创：战神》。该 VR 眼镜也将成为观看狮门影业 3D 电影的平台。
