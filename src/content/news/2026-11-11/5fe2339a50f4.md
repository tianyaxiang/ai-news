OpenAI has acquired an AI travel agent called Odessia, according to Odessia’s founder, Francis Davidson. Davidson says in a post announcing the deal that he’s now working for OpenAI, helping the AI lab with the future of travel. Terms of the deal were not disclosed, but this looks like an acqui-hire.

据 Odessia 创始人弗朗西斯·戴维森（Francis Davidson）称，OpenAI 已收购了一家名为 Odessia 的人工智能旅行代理公司。戴维森在宣布该交易的帖子中表示，他目前已加入 OpenAI，协助该人工智能实验室探索旅行的未来。交易条款未予披露，但这看起来是一次人才收购（acqui-hire）。

Davidson’s announcement focused largely on how he built his AI travel agent Odessia by directing teams of AI agents. The year-old Odessia raised a $6 million pre-seed round led by Sequoia and launched its AI travel agent around five months ago. Davidson says that it had helped early adopters plan millions of dollars’ worth of travel, but the tool has already been shut down.

戴维森的公告主要聚焦于他如何通过指挥人工智能代理团队来构建 Odessia。成立仅一年的 Odessia 此前曾获得由红杉资本领投的 600 万美元种子轮融资，并于约五个月前推出了其人工智能旅行代理工具。戴维森表示，该工具已帮助早期用户规划了价值数百万美元的行程，但目前该工具已被关闭。

An OpenAI spokesperson confirmed the hire to TechCrunch. “We’re thrilled to welcome Francis to OpenAI. Francis is a proven founder who has built multiple businesses to help people travel, and we’re excited he’s chosen to build with us.” However the AI lab has not announced new travel-specific products or plans. It does, however, count such companies as Bookings.com, Expedia, and KAYAK as customers or partners.

OpenAI 发言人向 TechCrunch 证实了此次聘用。“我们很高兴欢迎弗朗西斯加入 OpenAI。弗朗西斯是一位久经考验的创始人，曾创立多家企业以帮助人们旅行，我们很高兴他选择与我们共同构建未来。”然而，该人工智能实验室尚未宣布任何针对旅行的新产品或计划。不过，该公司确实将 Bookings.com、Expedia 和 KAYAK 等公司视为客户或合作伙伴。

Travel is also one of the main areas that people are using personal AI assistants to help with. And, back in July 2025, when OpenAI launched its ChatGPT agent, it mentioned travel as one of its potential uses. OpenAI’s spokesperson didn’t have more to share on its plans for travel-related products, except to say that travel is one of the areas that chatbot users frequently ask about.

旅行也是人们使用个人人工智能助手的主要领域之一。早在 2025 年 7 月，当 OpenAI 发布其 ChatGPT 代理时，就曾提到旅行是其潜在用途之一。OpenAI 发言人未透露更多关于旅行相关产品的计划，仅表示旅行是聊天机器人用户经常咨询的领域之一。

“1.2 billion people turn to ChatGPT each week for help with everyday tasks — from understanding their health, figuring out what to make for dinner, building a budget, and even planning a trip. We see a real opportunity to make these experiences even more useful,” the spokesperson said.

“每周有 12 亿人使用 ChatGPT 来处理日常任务——从了解健康状况、决定晚餐吃什么、制定预算，甚至规划行程。我们看到了让这些体验变得更加实用的真正机会，”该发言人说道。

Acquiring Odessia is interesting for another reason. Davidson is well known in the travel industry after he founded a travel startup called Sonder as a college student in 2014. It was sort of like Airbnb, but it focused on short-term leasing of upscale apartments in major cities worldwide. The pandemic badly hurt it, and as it climbed out of that rut, it went public via a SPAC but didn’t raise as much money as it needed, Davidson later said in a post announcing his resignation as CEO in June 2025.

收购 Odessia 的另一个看点在于戴维森本人。他在 2014 年还是大学生时就创立了旅行初创公司 Sonder，因此在旅游业颇有名气。Sonder 类似于 Airbnb，但专注于全球主要城市高档公寓的短期租赁。疫情对其造成了严重打击，尽管后来有所好转，但通过 SPAC 上市时并未筹集到足够的资金，戴维森在 2025 年 6 月宣布辞去首席执行官职务的帖子中如是说道。

When a key partnership with Marriott fell through, the company went into bankruptcy and wound down. The bankruptcy trustee sold Sonder’s name, trademarks, and website domain in 2026 to TravelAI, and that website operates as a curated guide to the kind of travel rental properties that Sonder was once known for. Prior to its downfall, Davidson had built Sonder into a unicorn with $143 million in revenues.

当与万豪（Marriott）的关键合作破裂后，该公司陷入破产并最终清算。破产受托人在 2026 年将 Sonder 的名称、商标和网站域名出售给了 TravelAI，该网站目前作为 Sonder 曾经闻名的那种旅行租赁物业的精选指南运营。在倒闭之前，戴维森曾将 Sonder 打造成一家营收达 1.43 亿美元的独角兽企业。

While Sonder was not, ultimately, a success story, Davidson is the kind of battle-scarred founder that Silicon Valley often values. So we’ll have to wait and see what OpenAI has in store now with travel, now that he’s joined the company.

虽然 Sonder 最终并非一个成功案例，但戴维森属于硅谷通常看重的那种身经百战的创始人。因此，随着他的加入，我们只能拭目以待 OpenAI 在旅行领域会有什么动作。
