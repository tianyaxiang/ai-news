Google Cloud launches Gemini for healthcare. Nov 10, 2026 Google Cloud has announced Gemini for healthcare, an AI solution engineered to unify fragmented clinical, operational, and administrative systems.

Google Cloud 推出面向医疗行业的 Gemini。2026 年 11 月 10 日，Google Cloud 宣布推出 Gemini for healthcare，这是一款旨在统一分散的临床、运营和管理系统的 AI 解决方案。

Moving beyond simple information retrieval, it enables healthcare teams to turn siloed data into coordinated action.

它超越了简单的信息检索，使医疗团队能够将孤立的数据转化为协调一致的行动。

Clinicians lose nearly 28 hours each week to paperwork and administrative tasks, fueling burnout and straining budgets. By integrating specialized skills and data connectors with existing third-party systems, the platform brings together critical information from electronic health records, billing platforms, clinical guidelines, and Medicare datasets.

临床医生每周在文书工作和行政任务上花费近 28 小时，这加剧了职业倦怠并使预算紧张。通过将专业技能和数据连接器与现有的第三方系统集成，该平台汇集了来自电子健康记录、计费平台、临床指南和医疗保险数据集的关键信息。

Gemini for healthcare reasons across this context to orchestrate and execute complex, multi-step workflows — such as medical records synthesis and prior authorization review — within defined guardrails and with appropriate human oversight.

Gemini for healthcare 在此背景下进行推理，以在既定的护栏内并在适当的人工监督下，编排和执行复杂的多步骤工作流程，例如病历综合和预先授权审查。

Initially available in preview alongside launch partners including HCA Healthcare, Highmark Health, and Northwell Health, it resolves bottlenecks and reclaims valuable staff time for patient care. Learn more on the Cloud blog.

该产品最初与包括 HCA Healthcare、Highmark Health 和 Northwell Health 在内的发布合作伙伴一起提供预览版，旨在解决瓶颈问题，并为患者护理争取宝贵的员工时间。欲了解更多信息，请访问 Cloud 博客。
