Google has announced a “super network” upgrade for its Fi cell service. For the past few years, Google Fi has operated exclusively on the T-Mobile network, but now it’s adding support for a second 5G network. Oddly, Google refuses to say which one.

谷歌宣布为其 Fi 移动服务进行“超级网络”升级。过去几年里，Google Fi 一直仅在 T-Mobile 网络上运行，但现在它增加了对第二个 5G 网络的支持。奇怪的是，谷歌拒绝透露具体是哪一个。

Google’s MVNO (mobile virtual network operator) launched in 2015 as Project Fi with support for both T-Mobile and Sprint service. The service initially only worked on Google’s Nexus phones, which would switch between networks based on signal strength. After T-Mobile absorbed Sprint, Fi switched to being a single-network service in the US while continuing to hop between networks for international travel.

谷歌的移动虚拟运营商（MVNO）于 2015 年以 Project Fi 的名义推出，当时支持 T-Mobile 和 Sprint 两家网络。该服务最初仅适用于谷歌的 Nexus 手机，手机会根据信号强度在不同网络间切换。在 T-Mobile 收购 Sprint 后，Fi 在美国转变为单一网络服务，但在国际漫游时仍会切换网络。

With the super network, Google Fi is again a dual-carrier service stateside. Phones connected to Fi will be able to hop between T-Mobile 5G and a different 5G network to provide the best possible connectivity. Google’s blog post announcing the change conspicuously doesn’t mention either network, saying only that it supports “two of America’s best 5G cellular networks.”

随着“超级网络”的推出，Google Fi 在美国再次成为双运营商服务。连接到 Fi 的手机将能够在 T-Mobile 5G 和另一个 5G 网络之间切换，以提供最佳的连接体验。谷歌宣布这一变化的博客文章明显没有提及任何一家网络，仅表示它支持“美国两家最好的 5G 蜂窝网络”。

We know one of them is T-Mobile, and the other must be either AT&T or Verizon. When reached for comment, a Google spokesperson declined to name the second network, which is odd. Looking at the Fi coverage map, my best guess for the super network partner is Verizon. The shape of unserved regions is a closer match than AT&T, but carriers do tend to fudge the fine details of these maps.

我们知道其中一家是 T-Mobile，另一家必然是 AT&T 或 Verizon。当被联系置评时，谷歌发言人拒绝透露第二个网络的名称，这很奇怪。查看 Fi 的覆盖地图，我猜测“超级网络”的合作伙伴最可能是 Verizon。未覆盖区域的形状与 Verizon 更为吻合，尽管运营商往往会模糊这些地图的细节。

MVNOs don’t always advertise which network they use, and that information is getting harder to find in FAQs and support pages. Google’s Fi FAQ no longer even confirms T-Mobile as a network partner. Google used to be very upfront about its partnership with T-Mobile, pointing out that, unlike most MVNOs, it gets top prioritization on T-Mobile’s towers.

移动虚拟运营商并不总是宣传他们使用的网络，而且这些信息在常见问题解答和支持页面中也越来越难找到。Google Fi 的常见问题解答甚至不再确认 T-Mobile 是其网络合作伙伴。谷歌过去曾非常坦诚地说明其与 T-Mobile 的合作关系，并指出与大多数移动虚拟运营商不同，它在 T-Mobile 的基站上享有最高优先级。

Network information like this is really something consumers should have when shopping for plans—you probably know which carriers work best where you spend your time. There are a few caveats in the super network rollout beyond the mysterious new partner. Currently, network switching is only supported on the Pixel 9, Pixel 10, and Pixel 11 series. Other devices will get access in 2027.

此类网络信息确实是消费者在选购套餐时应该了解的——你可能知道哪些运营商在你常去的地方信号最好。除了神秘的新合作伙伴外，此次“超级网络”的推出还有一些注意事项。目前，网络切换仅支持 Pixel 9、Pixel 10 和 Pixel 11 系列手机。其他设备将在 2027 年获得支持。

You also won’t be able to enjoy traditional dual-SIM functionality if you want Fi to switch networks. This will only work if you have Fi configured as an eSIM with all non-Fi SIMs disabled. If you meet those requirements, super network support is rolling out now at no additional cost on Unlimited Premium and Flexible plans.

如果你希望 Fi 切换网络，你将无法使用传统的双卡双待功能。只有在将 Fi 配置为 eSIM 且禁用所有非 Fi SIM 卡的情况下，此功能才能正常工作。如果你满足这些要求，超级网络支持目前已在 Unlimited Premium 和 Flexible 套餐中免费推出。
