Short answer: publish each kitchen-order transition to the location's channel, and make every kiosk refetch the full order board after it reconnects. That split is the important design choice. Transitions are small and frequent; a complete board is large and comparatively rare. Treating every display update as a full snapshot makes normal traffic expensive, while treating a reconnect as if it had received every missed event leaves a visible hole in the board.

简短的回答是：将每个厨房订单的变更发布到该地点的频道，并让每个自助服务终端在重新连接后重新获取完整的订单看板。这种拆分是重要的设计选择。变更（Transitions）规模小且频繁；而完整的看板规模大且相对罕见。将每次显示更新都视为完整快照会使正常流量变得昂贵，而将重新连接视为已接收所有错过的事件，则会在看板上留下明显的漏洞。

The delivery guarantee is therefore bounded, not magical: live subscribers receive transitions, and a display that loses its stream restores authority by reading the current board. Give kiosks subscribe-only tokens. Keep publishing and board reads on the backend. A kitchen screen can look healthy while missing the one transition that changed an order from preparing to ready. A green connection indicator does not answer that question. I model the workflow as two paths. The order service commits a transition and publishes a small event to location/{locationID}. A kiosk applies events while connected. On reconnect, it fetches the full board, replaces its local state, records the new revision, and only then resumes incremental updates.

厨房屏幕可能看起来正常，但却错过了将订单从“准备中”变为“已就绪”的那一个变更。绿色的连接指示灯无法回答这个问题。我将工作流程建模为两条路径。订单服务提交变更并向 location/{locationID} 发布一个小事件。自助服务终端在连接时应用事件。重新连接时，它会获取完整看板，替换本地状态，记录新版本，然后才恢复增量更新。

The fetch is the recovery boundary. This is the invariant I want in a postmortem: an event accelerates convergence; the board snapshot establishes truth. It also gives the on-call engineer something concrete to inspect instead of trusting a dashboard that says “connected.” There is a practical wrinkle. A display should not hold a credential that can publish, issue tokens, or read other locations. The backend issues a subscribe-only token scoped to the location channel.

获取操作是恢复边界。这是我在事后分析中想要的不变量：事件加速收敛；看板快照确立事实。它还为值班工程师提供了一些具体的可检查内容，而不是盲目信任显示“已连接”的仪表板。这里有一个实际的难点。显示屏不应持有可以发布、发放令牌或读取其他地点的凭据。后端会发放一个仅限于该地点频道的订阅令牌。

The kiosk receives no order-writing authority, even if its browser process is inspected. I first assumed the hard part would be fan-out. It is usually the gap between fan-out and recovery: a reconnect can look successful before the local board is current. Why is reconnect recovery more important than perfect fan-out? Because the two workloads have different shapes. A lunch rush can produce many small order transitions, but the board itself is much larger and rarely changes as a whole.

即使其浏览器进程被检查，自助服务终端也不会获得订单写入权限。我最初以为难点在于扇出（fan-out）。通常难点在于扇出与恢复之间的差距：在本地看板更新之前，重新连接可能看起来是成功的。为什么重新连接恢复比完美的扇出更重要？因为这两种工作负载的形态不同。午餐高峰期会产生许多小的订单变更，但看板本身规模大得多，且很少整体发生变化。

Publishing the transition keeps fan-out proportional to the change. Refetching after a gap keeps correctness proportional to the current board, rather than requiring the client to reconstruct an unbounded event history. That choice does not mean event delivery is unimportant. It means the system has a recovery contract when delivery is interrupted. A publish retry needs an idempotency key, and the consumer needs to tolerate a transition that is repeated or arrives after a snapshot.

发布变更使扇出与变化成正比。在中断后重新获取使正确性与当前看板保持一致，而不是要求客户端重建无限的事件历史。这个选择并不意味着事件交付不重要。它意味着当交付中断时，系统拥有恢复契约。发布重试需要幂等键，且消费者需要容忍重复的或在快照之后到达的变更。

The order transition must be safe to apply against the board revision the kiosk currently holds; if it is not, the kiosk refetches. Here is the narrow backend path I would keep near the order transaction. It publishes after the state change is accepted, uses a stable transition identifier for retries, and surfaces the response instead of declaring victory on any HTTP status.

订单变更必须能够安全地应用于自助服务终端当前持有的看板版本；如果不能，终端将重新获取。这是我建议在订单事务附近保留的精简后端路径。它在状态变更被接受后进行发布，使用稳定的变更标识符进行重试，并呈现响应结果，而不是在任何 HTTP 状态下就宣布成功。
