Earlier this year, we announced Gemini Intelligence, and this summer devices started rolling out — from the Pixel 11 to the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8 Ultra, Z Fold8, Z Flip8 and S26 series. 2 Today, we’re introducing new features built for Gemini Intelligence, and expanding capabilities to more of Android’s most advanced devices. 3 Here’s a look at what’s new.

Alongside this expansion, we’re bringing some of our favorite capabilities to more devices and regions, helping handle the busywork so you can focus on what matters. Rambler 4 — which turns spoken thoughts into clean, organized text in real-time — is now available on Pixel 10, Pixel 10 Pro, Pixel 10, Pro XL and Pixel 10 Pro Fold, as well as the new motorola signature 27. And task automation 5 — which lets you ask Gemini to handle logistics like ordering food, hailing rides, or booking reservations across your favorite apps — is expanding to the UK, India, and Japan.

Build custom widgets with a prompt. Create My Widget 6, which is rolling out over the next few weeks, lets you design a personalized widget with a simple description in your own words — no coding required. Whether you want a dashboard for your upcoming trip, a monthly spending tracker, or a race day countdown, Create My Widget can pull in information from Gmail, Calendar, and Search to create a custom widget just for you, giving you the information you want at a glance on your homescreen.

通过提示词构建自定义小部件。Create My Widget 6 将在未来几周内推出，它允许您用简单的语言描述来设计个性化小部件，无需任何编程。无论您是想要一个即将到来的旅行仪表板、月度支出跟踪器，还是比赛日倒计时，Create My Widget 都能从 Gmail、日历和搜索中提取信息，为您创建专属的自定义小部件，让您在主屏幕上一目了然地获取所需信息。

Get personalized tech support with Device Assistance. Device Assistance 7 helps you control, change, or fix your device by just asking your phone for help. Ask Gemini to clear up space on your device, check which apps can access your location, or mute distractions while making sure important calls still come through. Gemini deeply understands your device so it can act as your personal device expert.

通过设备助手获取个性化技术支持。设备助手 7 只需您向手机寻求帮助，即可协助您控制、更改或修复设备。您可以要求 Gemini 清理设备空间、检查哪些应用程序可以访问您的位置，或在静音干扰的同时确保重要来电仍能接通。Gemini 对您的设备有深入了解，因此可以充当您的个人设备专家。

Experience proactive help across a range of screens. Gemini Intelligence is designed to support you across whatever screen you’re using. Last month we launched Googlebook, the first laptop designed for Gemini Intelligence. And if you have a Pixel phone with Gemini Intelligence, it will soon bring proactive suggestions to Android Auto — connecting the dots across your apps to surface helpful, personalized suggestions 8 when you need them. For example, if your friend texts you to ask where you are meeting for a pilates class, Gemini Intelligence on Android Auto can find your confirmation email and instantly suggest sending the address with a single tap.
