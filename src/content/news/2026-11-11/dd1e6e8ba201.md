Kirt McMaster knows that his new operating system—Kora—won’t be for everyone, and he’s fine with that. Kora, announced on Tuesday, is designed as a desktop alternative to Windows or macOS for “the AI native, not mass market,” he says. McMaster, the company’s CEO and cofounder—who also cofounded Boost Mobile—likens his strategy to how Steve Jobs targeted creators when overhauling desktop publishing with the Mac.

Kirt McMaster 深知他的新操作系统 Kora 并不会适合所有人，而他对此并不介意。Kora 于周二发布，旨在成为 Windows 或 macOS 的桌面替代品，其目标群体是“AI 原生用户，而非大众市场”。作为公司首席执行官兼联合创始人（他也是 Boost Mobile 的联合创始人），McMaster 将其战略比作史蒂夫·乔布斯当年通过 Mac 彻底改变桌面出版时，将目标锁定在创作者身上的做法。

This “next-generation” desktop OS can integrate context from user interactions across multiple AI tools. Targeted to creators and developers, Kora’s central idea is to bring AI models together at the system level and help power users interact more seamlessly with multiple providers, rather than siloing everything off into desktop apps and browser tabs. It’s scheduled to launch sometime in 2027.

这款“下一代”桌面操作系统能够整合来自多个 AI 工具的用户交互上下文。Kora 的核心理念是面向创作者和开发者，在系统层面汇集各种 AI 模型，帮助高级用户更无缝地与多个服务提供商进行交互，而不是将一切孤立在桌面应用程序和浏览器标签页中。该系统计划于 2027 年某个时间发布。

By tapping into your data and context at a machine level, Kora could streamline some of the current speed bumps of using AI tools on a desktop. “All your context, all your memory is on your machine,” says Franck De Girolami, one of Kora’s cofounders. He gives the example of someone designing a project with an OpenAI model inside Kora’s interface, switching to Anthropic’s tools to deploy code, and leaning on a local model to save money on AI tokens.

通过在机器层面利用用户的数据和上下文，Kora 可以简化目前在桌面上使用 AI 工具时遇到的一些障碍。“你所有的上下文和记忆都存储在你的机器上，”Kora 的联合创始人之一 Franck De Girolami 说道。他举例说，用户可以在 Kora 的界面内使用 OpenAI 模型设计项目，切换到 Anthropic 的工具部署代码，并依靠本地模型来节省 AI 代币费用。

Kora is a standalone operating system built on the Linux kernel that runs directly on hardware. The startup has partnered with Qualcomm to bring Kora to Snapdragon PCs next year, but it also supports x86-64 architecture, so you can likely install it on current Intel- or AMD-based PCs.

Kora 是一个基于 Linux 内核构建的独立操作系统，可直接在硬件上运行。该初创公司已与高通合作，计划明年将 Kora 引入骁龙 PC，同时它也支持 x86-64 架构，因此用户很可能可以在当前的 Intel 或 AMD 架构 PC 上安装它。

To bridge the software gap, built-in compatibility layers will allow users to run standard Windows apps. The company is also developing its own native apps for basics like contacts, calendar, and email, alongside a software development kit (SDK) for third-party developers. Along the bottom of the home screen is a dock, as you would expect on a desktop OS. This is where users can switch between email, web browsing, and launching games.

为了弥补软件差距，内置的兼容层将允许用户运行标准的 Windows 应用程序。该公司还在开发用于联系人、日历和电子邮件等基础功能的原生应用，并为第三方开发者提供软件开发工具包 (SDK)。主屏幕底部设有停靠栏，正如人们在桌面操作系统中所期望的那样。用户可以在此切换电子邮件、网页浏览和启动游戏。

The OS is designed around what the startup calls a "neural file system.” Based on example screenshots, the user experience may feel like messaging a chatbot or AI agent. File data is indexed by meaning so you can ask questions about the broad context of files by typing into an OS search bar, without necessarily looking up specific file names. For example, you could search for a business contract saved somewhere on your device based on a small detail you remember.

该操作系统围绕初创公司所谓的“神经文件系统”进行设计。根据示例截图，用户体验可能类似于与聊天机器人或 AI 代理进行对话。文件数据按含义进行索引，因此你可以通过在系统搜索栏中输入内容来询问有关文件广泛上下文的问题，而不必非要查找特定的文件名。例如，你可以根据记忆中的一个小细节，搜索保存在设备某处的商业合同。

Kora grew out of McMaster’s startup Playtron, which launched its own gaming OS, GameOS, last year. GameOS is primarily built to run on portable handhelds, like a Steam Deck, with PC support as well. That architecture now lives inside Kora as GameFrame, letting users run PC games—provided their hardware can keep up.

Kora 源自 McMaster 的初创公司 Playtron，该公司去年推出了自己的游戏操作系统 GameOS。GameOS 主要为 Steam Deck 等便携式掌机打造，同时也支持 PC。该架构现在作为 GameFrame 存在于 Kora 中，只要硬件性能跟得上，用户就可以运行 PC 游戏。

The broader pitch caters to PC gamers and power users. “That’s always our thing—next-generation creators who are using these tools daily to build new applications and services, but they want everything else that they can get from a Windows PC or what’s currently available on macOS,” McMaster says.

其更广泛的宣传目标是 PC 游戏玩家和高级用户。McMaster 表示：“这始终是我们的核心——那些每天使用这些工具构建新应用和服务的下一代创作者，他们同时也希望获得 Windows PC 或 macOS 目前所能提供的所有其他功能。”

Taking on the giants is difficult, especially with a team of under 100 people, but Kora’s leadership knows a thing or two about launching operating systems. Kora’s three founders—McMaster, De Girolami, and John Lagerling—each bring their own expertise. McMaster cofounded and helped lead Cyanogen, an open source Android fork that gained millions of users before shutting down around a decade ago. De Girolami helped develop Playtron’s GameOS. Lagerling previously worked on Android at Google.

挑战巨头并非易事，尤其是在团队人数不足 100 人的情况下，但 Kora 的领导层对发布操作系统并不陌生。Kora 的三位创始人——McMaster、De Girolami 和 John Lagerling——各自带来了自己的专业知识。McMaster 曾共同创立并领导过 Cyanogen，这是一个开源的 Android 分支，在约十年前关闭前曾拥有数百万用户。De Girolami 曾参与开发 Playtron 的 GameOS。Lagerling 此前曾在谷歌负责 Android 相关工作。

Kora isn’t the first to rethink the desktop operating system in the AI age. Google launched its Googlebook OS and compatible laptops in October, designed around Gemini AI. Even so, McMaster doesn’t see that OS and its Android support as satisfying power users who want to run AI models that require serious chip power. The company is optimizing Kora for desktop configurations with at least 64 gigabytes of RAM, which is twice the memory capacity of the top Googlebook.

Kora 并不是第一个在 AI 时代重新思考桌面操作系统的产品。谷歌在 10 月推出了围绕 Gemini AI 设计的 Googlebook OS 及其兼容笔记本电脑。即便如此，McMaster 认为该系统及其对 Android 的支持并不能满足那些想要运行需要强大芯片算力的 AI 模型的高级用户。该公司正在针对至少配备 64GB 内存的桌面配置优化 Kora，这相当于顶级 Googlebook 内存容量的两倍。

“We can evolve much faster than macOS and Windows,” McMaster says. “When you’re a billion-user incumbent, it’s impossible to tip the boat, man. It’s just really hard to make big changes. Over time, there’s this event horizon where building a new kind of OS for intelligence can become something very compelling.”

“我们可以比 macOS 和 Windows 进化得快得多，”McMaster 说。“当你是一个拥有十亿用户的既有巨头时，想要掉头是不可能的。做出重大改变真的很难。随着时间的推移，会出现一个临界点，届时构建一种全新的智能操作系统将变得非常有吸引力。”
