# Seagate's new hybrid drive raises some old questions

Seagate doesn’t think hybrid drives are done just yet. On Tuesday, the company announced the launch of the new FireCuda Forge line of storage drives. The new drives combine the high capacity of a traditional plattered HDD with the speed and responsiveness of a user-supplied solid-state drive (SSD).

希捷认为混合硬盘尚未过时。周二，该公司宣布推出全新的 FireCuda Forge 系列存储硬盘。这些新硬盘结合了传统机械硬盘（HDD）的大容量与用户自备固态硬盘（SSD）的速度和响应能力。

The new hybrid storage solution is Seagate’s first to be powered and managed by the new Alkemi intelligent storage platform. The FireCuda Forge is available right now through Seagate’s web store, and comes in 8TB ($699) or 20TB ($899) HDD configurations—neither of which includes the required SSD.

这一全新的混合存储解决方案是希捷首款由 Alkemi 智能存储平台驱动和管理的产品。FireCuda Forge 目前已在希捷在线商店发售，提供 8TB（699 美元）或 20TB（899 美元）的 HDD 配置，但均不包含所需的 SSD。

Users will have to bring their own SSD, with some specific restrictions. The FireCuda Forge is compatible with M.2 2280 NVMe drives that are PCIe Gen3 or higher. The maximum supported NVMe capacity is 1TB, which means that’s as much data as you’ll be able to dedicate to storage on the “fast side” of the hybrid setup.

用户必须自备 SSD，并需满足特定限制。FireCuda Forge 兼容 PCIe Gen3 或更高版本的 M.2 2280 NVMe 硬盘。支持的最大 NVMe 容量为 1TB，这意味着你只能将这么多数据分配给混合设置中的“高速端”。

The NVMe will require minimum speeds of 2,400MB/s read and 2,000MB/s write, and a unit with DRAM cache is recommended. Seagate claims 2,500MB/s read and 2,000MB/s write speeds for the combined system, along with USB4 and support for Windows 11 and macOS 26+.

NVMe 硬盘的最低读写速度要求分别为 2,400MB/s 和 2,000MB/s，且建议使用带有 DRAM 缓存的型号。希捷声称该组合系统的读写速度可达 2,500MB/s 和 2,000MB/s，并支持 USB4、Windows 11 和 macOS 26+。

After dipping to historical lows earlier in the decade, storage pricing per TB has exploded amid the AI boom. According to data collected by Tom’s Hardware, some solid-state drives now cost two to three times their historic lows, and it’s not just SSDs. Mechanical drives haven’t been immune either.

在本世纪初降至历史低点后，随着人工智能热潮的兴起，每 TB 的存储价格出现了暴涨。根据 Tom’s Hardware 收集的数据，一些固态硬盘现在的价格是其历史低点的两到三倍，而且不仅是 SSD，机械硬盘也未能幸免。

According to PCServerParts, a Seagate Exos 24TB drive that sold for $298.99 in the summer of 2025 was priced at $699.99 by August 2026. Hybrid drives had largely fallen out of fashion since their heyday in the early to mid 2010s. The promise then was similar to what Seagate is offering now.

据 PCServerParts 称，一款 2025 年夏季售价为 298.99 美元的希捷 Exos 24TB 硬盘，到 2026 年 8 月价格已涨至 699.99 美元。混合硬盘自 2010 年代中前期的鼎盛时期以来已基本过时。当时的承诺与希捷现在提供的方案如出一辙。

A large HDD is combined with a smaller SSD and handed to the user as a single storage pool. Apple introduced its own hybrid solution back in 2012 with the Mac mini’s Fusion Drive. When Ars opened up a unit, the Fusion Drive system was found to write in 128KB tiered blocks.

将大容量 HDD 与小容量 SSD 结合，并作为单一存储池提供给用户。苹果早在 2012 年就通过 Mac mini 的 Fusion Drive 推出了自己的混合解决方案。当 Ars 拆解该设备时，发现 Fusion Drive 系统是以 128KB 的分层块进行写入的。

Information was generally written to the SSD first, and was then migrated to the HDD as needed. If the files were frequently used, they might find a more permanent home on the SSD side of things. As solid-state storage continued getting cheaper, the hybrid approach was largely abandoned.

信息通常先写入 SSD，然后根据需要迁移到 HDD。如果文件被频繁使用，它们可能会在 SSD 端找到更持久的存储位置。随着固态存储价格的持续下降，混合存储方案在很大程度上被放弃了。

Fast-forward to our current storage cost predicament in 2026, and Seagate’s line of thinking that everything old is new again starts to hold water. All storage costs have exploded, but the 2012 math still works today. NAND flash is still fast, but expensive, and traditional mechanical drives are at least comparatively cheaper per TB for higher capacity storage.

快进到 2026 年我们当前面临的存储成本困境，希捷“旧事物重焕新生”的思路开始变得站得住脚。所有存储成本都已飙升，但 2012 年的逻辑在今天依然适用。NAND 闪存速度快但价格昂贵，而传统机械硬盘在实现大容量存储时，每 TB 的成本相对更低。

The problem with Apple’s Fusion solution was its fragility. If one of the two drives failed, that was likely it. Recovery depended on both drives coming together as a single volume. If one of those two disks experienced a physical failure, recovery became difficult since the remaining working drive depended on the now broken drive’s data to rebuild the full system picture.

苹果 Fusion 方案的问题在于其脆弱性。如果两块硬盘中的任何一块发生故障，整个系统很可能就报废了。数据恢复依赖于两块硬盘作为一个单一卷共同工作。如果其中一块磁盘发生物理故障，恢复将变得非常困难，因为剩余的正常硬盘需要依赖已损坏硬盘上的数据来重建完整的系统镜像。

According to Seagate’s press release, the Alkemi intelligent storage platform takes a different approach. Where Apple’s Fusion Drives used tiering managed by macOS, Alkemi manages the storage pool directly from the enclosure. Seagate says, “Alkemi learns and adapts over time to keep high-demand content fast and accessible on a user’s SSD, while the HDD provides space for growing libraries. Behind the scenes, Alkemi manages data placement, giving users the simplicity of a single storage drive.”

根据希捷的新闻稿，Alkemi 智能存储平台采用了不同的方法。苹果的 Fusion Drive 使用由 macOS 管理的分层技术，而 Alkemi 则直接从硬盘盒层面管理存储池。希捷表示：“Alkemi 会随着时间的推移进行学习和调整，将高需求内容保留在用户的 SSD 上以确保快速访问，同时由 HDD 为不断增长的资料库提供空间。在后台，Alkemi 会管理数据布局，为用户提供单一存储驱动器的简便性。”

The product data sheet PDF from the FireCuda Forge website describes the Alkemi system as a two-tier storage system, but doesn’t elaborate any further on whether the system uses SSD caching, automated tiering, a combination of the two, or something else entirely. The announcement doesn’t address how the Alkemi platform manages the data between the two drives.

FireCuda Forge 网站上的产品数据表 PDF 将 Alkemi 系统描述为双层存储系统，但并未进一步说明该系统是使用 SSD 缓存、自动分层、两者的结合，还是其他完全不同的技术。该公告也没有说明 Alkemi 平台如何在两块硬盘之间管理数据。

The old Intel Smart Response Technology kept failure risks low by mirroring data from the mechanical HDD to the faster SSD, with the HDD retaining the authoritative copy. The optional write-back mode could still hold new data on the SSD, though. Apple’s Fusion Drive instead tiered data in 128KB blocks across the storage pool, storing data on both drives.

旧的英特尔智能响应技术（Smart Response Technology）通过将数据从机械 HDD 镜像到更快的 SSD 来降低故障风险，并由 HDD 保留权威副本。不过，可选的回写模式仍可将新数据保留在 SSD 上。而苹果的 Fusion Drive 则是在存储池中以 128KB 为单位对数据进行分层，将数据存储在两块硬盘上。

The distinction matters, and is probably why Ars asked the same question after Apple’s hybrid announcement 14 years ago. Seagate’s announcement speaks of “intelligent data tiering” without further explanation. The data system promises that your most accessed files will be quickest to access via intelligent storage management, but stops short of explaining whether one drive going down means failure for the whole system.

这种区别至关重要，这可能也是 14 年前苹果发布混合存储方案后 Ars 提出同样问题的原因。希捷的公告提到了“智能数据分层”，但没有进一步解释。该数据系统承诺通过智能存储管理让用户最常访问的文件获得最快的访问速度，但并未说明其中一块硬盘损坏是否意味着整个系统的崩溃。

There’s also no mention of how the Alkemi system distributes data on first boot. Does all the new written data go directly to the SSD until the 1TB cap is crossed, and then the system starts determining which files are important enough to remain on the speedier drive? Seagate seems to be keeping things mum for the moment, but as the drives have already launched for sale, there should be answers to all of these questions coming soon.

此外，公告中也没有提到 Alkemi 系统在首次启动时如何分配数据。所有新写入的数据是否会直接进入 SSD，直到达到 1TB 的上限，然后系统才开始判断哪些文件重要到足以保留在更快的硬盘上？希捷目前似乎对此保持沉默，但由于硬盘已经上市销售，这些问题的答案应该很快就会揭晓。

Seagate hasn’t published technical documentation regarding how the Alkemi system handles drive failure or repair. Until it does, the new FireCuda Forge drives offer the same appeal as Apple’s old Fusion Drives: SSD speed with HDD capacity, along with the same questions Ars was asking about hybrid storage years ago.

希捷尚未发布关于 Alkemi 系统如何处理硬盘故障或修复的技术文档。在此之前，新款 FireCuda Forge 硬盘提供的吸引力与苹果旧款 Fusion Drive 相同：兼具 SSD 的速度和 HDD 的容量，同时也带来了 Ars 多年前对混合存储所提出的同样疑问。
