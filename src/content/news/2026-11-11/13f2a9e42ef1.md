While established venture firms continue to attract capital, first-time fund managers are facing one of the toughest fundraising environments in years. But that didn’t stop Sahir Raoof, a former SignalFire investor and executive at two healthcare startups, from raising $27.5 million for his first solo VC fund.

尽管老牌风险投资公司仍在吸引资本，但首次募资的基金经理正面临多年来最艰难的融资环境。然而，这并没有阻止前 SignalFire 投资人、两家医疗初创公司高管 Sahir Raoof 为其首支个人风险投资基金筹集到 2750 万美元。

Raoof’s firm, Proximal Ventures, is betting that AI can transform the tools, infrastructure, and services that support drug development. Pharmaceutical companies have historically outsourced various stages of drug development to specialized service providers, from synthesizing thousands of molecular compounds and figuring out how to manufacture drugs at scale to analyzing clinical trial data.

Raoof 的公司 Proximal Ventures 押注人工智能能够变革支持药物研发的工具、基础设施和服务。制药公司历来将药物研发的各个阶段外包给专业服务提供商，从合成数千种分子化合物、研究如何大规模生产药物，到分析临床试验数据，均涵盖在内。

“For a long time, the assumption was that pharma is not a great buyer of software, but a really good buyer of services,” Raoof told TechCrunch. Raoof believes AI could automate much of that work, creating an opportunity for startups to capture billions of dollars in pharmaceutical industry spending. By using AI, these companies could deliver the same services at a lower cost and in significantly less time, he said.

“长期以来，人们普遍认为制药行业不是软件的优质买家，但却是服务的优质买家，”Raoof 在接受 TechCrunch 采访时表示。Raoof 认为人工智能可以自动化大部分此类工作，从而为初创公司创造机会，去争夺制药行业数十亿美元的支出。他表示，通过使用人工智能，这些公司能够以更低的成本和更短的时间提供同样的服务。

Some of the most valuable applications of AI so far have been in service-oriented industries like law and finance, Raoof said. He sees similar opportunities in pharmaceutical services. Proximal plans to invest in pre-seed and seed-stage startups, writing checks from $500,000 to $1.5 million.

Raoof 指出，迄今为止，人工智能最具价值的应用主要集中在法律和金融等服务导向型行业。他在制药服务领域也看到了类似的机会。Proximal 计划投资种子轮前和种子轮阶段的初创公司，单笔投资金额在 50 万至 150 万美元之间。

The firm has already backed seven startups, including ChronicleBio, co-founded by former OpenAI executive Fidji Simo, which uses AI to analyze biological and medical data to better understand chronic diseases and identify potential treatments.

该公司目前已投资了七家初创公司，其中包括由前 OpenAI 高管 Fidji Simo 联合创立的 ChronicleBio。该公司利用人工智能分析生物和医疗数据，以更好地了解慢性疾病并识别潜在的治疗方法。

Before spending two years as a principal at SignalFire, Raoof led business development at clinical trial recruitment startup Iterative Health and previously oversaw partnerships at Flatiron Health, an oncology data company acquired by Roche for $1.9 billion in 2018.

在 SignalFire 担任两年负责人之前，Raoof 曾在临床试验招募初创公司 Iterative Health 负责业务开发，并曾监管 Flatiron Health 的合作伙伴关系。Flatiron Health 是一家肿瘤数据公司，于 2018 年被罗氏以 19 亿美元收购。

“If you’re building a company that’s selling into the life sciences, one of the hardest things to do is starting that initial commercial flywheel,” Raoof said. His sales chops, deep understanding of the healthcare ecosystem, and extensive industry relationships gave limited partners, the investors in VC funds, the confidence that he could spot strong startups and help them land customers.

“如果你正在建立一家向生命科学领域销售产品的公司，最困难的事情之一就是启动最初的商业飞轮，”Raoof 说道。他的销售能力、对医疗保健生态系统的深刻理解以及广泛的行业人脉，让风险投资基金的有限合伙人（LP）们相信，他能够发掘出强大的初创公司并帮助它们获得客户。

Proximal’s backers include VC firms SignalFire and Kleiner Perkins, Bertelsmann Healthcare Investments, and individual investors such as Dimension Capital co-founders Nan Li and Zavain Dar. Amy Abernethy, co-founder of Highlander Health and former principal deputy commissioner of the FDA, is also an investor.

Proximal 的支持者包括风险投资公司 SignalFire 和 Kleiner Perkins、贝塔斯曼医疗投资公司，以及 Dimension Capital 联合创始人 Nan Li 和 Zavain Dar 等个人投资者。Highlander Health 联合创始人、前美国食品药品监督管理局（FDA）首席副局长 Amy Abernethy 也是该基金的投资者之一。

She first met Raoof when they worked together at Flatiron Health, where she served as chief medical officer. She told TechCrunch that his ability to build relationships and spot industry bottlenecks made a lasting impression. Abernethy is convinced that his abilities, along with the understanding of what the healthcare market needs, will make him a successful investor. It is these qualities that have helped Proximal invest in startups alongside some of the better-known, much larger funds, she added.

她与 Raoof 初次相识是在 Flatiron Health 共事期间，当时她担任首席医疗官。她告诉 TechCrunch，Raoof 建立人际关系和发现行业瓶颈的能力给她留下了深刻印象。Abernethy 深信，凭借这些能力以及对医疗市场需求的理解，他将成为一名成功的投资者。她补充说，正是这些特质帮助 Proximal 能够与一些更知名、规模更大的基金共同投资初创公司。
