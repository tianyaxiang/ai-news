Mike Lothian owns a docking station (or dock) that uses a DisplayLink chipset that had, at least in his opinion, subpar performance on Linux. He decided to try improving the Linux driver, with LLM assistance, but ran into one problem that he could not solve—so he asked for help, hoping that Synaptics's developers would lend a hand. (DisplayLink was acquired by Synaptics in 2022.) Rather than help him in improving the driver, however, the Synaptics legal team responded with patent infringement claims, a demand that he cease unauthorized implementation of DisplayLink technology, and threats of expensive legal penalties. For now, it appears that Linux users of the company's docks will have to continue to live without feature parity with other platforms.

Mike Lothian 拥有一台使用 DisplayLink 芯片组的扩展坞（或称底座），在他看来，该设备在 Linux 上的性能表现不佳。他决定在大型语言模型（LLM）的辅助下尝试改进 Linux 驱动程序，但遇到了一个无法解决的问题，于是他寻求帮助，希望 Synaptics 的开发人员能伸出援手。（DisplayLink 已于 2022 年被 Synaptics 收购。）然而，Synaptics 的法律团队非但没有帮助他改进驱动程序，反而以专利侵权为由进行回应，要求他停止对 DisplayLink 技术的未经授权实现，并威胁要处以高额法律罚款。目前看来，该公司扩展坞的 Linux 用户将不得不继续忍受功能与其它平台不对等的情况。

Synaptics and Displaylink. The dock that he was trying to support is a Dell device that connects to a system via USB 3 and allows users to connect multiple monitors and other devices to their computer. It provides its DisplayLink Manager (DLM) software for Linux for the devices via an APT repository for Ubuntu. The company only provides DLM software in binary form, and only for x86-based platforms. The software is proprietary, but the company does maintain an open-source, out-of-tree kernel module that is included with DLM, the Extensible Virtual Display Interface (EVDI) project. It provides a driver that's compatible with Linux DRM and allows users to manage their docked displays with standard tools used for display settings, such as xrandr or GNOME and KDE Plasma's utilities.

Synaptics 与 DisplayLink。他试图支持的扩展坞是一款戴尔设备，通过 USB 3 连接到系统，允许用户将多台显示器及其它设备连接到计算机。该公司通过 Ubuntu 的 APT 存储库为这些设备提供 Linux 版 DisplayLink Manager (DLM) 软件。该公司仅以二进制形式提供 DLM 软件，且仅支持基于 x86 的平台。该软件是专有的，但公司确实维护着一个随 DLM 提供的开源、树外（out-of-tree）内核模块，即“可扩展虚拟显示接口”（EVDI）项目。它提供了一个与 Linux DRM 兼容的驱动程序，允许用户使用标准的显示设置工具（如 xrandr 或 GNOME 和 KDE Plasma 的实用程序）来管理连接的显示器。

A short article explains why the company has not released source code for DLM: the software uses a proprietary graphics technology called DL3 to create a compressed stream of the various channels (e.g., USB, DisplayLink video, audio) over a communication channel (USB 3 in this case) and decode it again for output to the user's devices. Unfortunately, the vendor-supplied software did not work well for Lothian. I spoke and chatted with him via Signal while writing this article; he said that the driver kept crashing under Linux, so he decided to use his Claude subscription to try to develop a new driver and support software.

一篇简短的文章解释了该公司为何没有发布 DLM 的源代码：该软件使用一种名为 DL3 的专有图形技术，通过通信通道（本例中为 USB 3）创建各种通道（如 USB、DisplayLink 视频、音频）的压缩流，并将其解码后输出到用户的设备上。不幸的是，供应商提供的软件在 Lothian 那里运行不佳。在撰写本文时，我通过 Signal 与他进行了交谈，他说驱动程序在 Linux 下不断崩溃，因此他决定利用自己的 Claude 订阅尝试开发一个新的驱动程序和支持软件。

Vino. He posted the first patch series for Vino—his Rust-based DRM/KMS driver—to the kernel's dri-devel list as an RFC on June 17. Lothian noted that it was in an incomplete state, and asked for help with a blocker that he had run into trying to communicate with the dock's control plane. "If you have knowledge of the DisplayLink DL3 control-plane engagement sequence, or ideas for isolating a whole-bus timing/ordering property a per-channel diff cannot see, help would be very welcome." He received feedback from a few developers, including guidance from Danilo Krummrich to rework the driver to use the Rust KMS bindings, but no advice specific to the problem he was trying to solve.

Vino。6 月 17 日，他将 Vino（他基于 Rust 的 DRM/KMS 驱动程序）的首个补丁系列作为 RFC 发布到了内核的 dri-devel 邮件列表中。Lothian 指出该项目尚处于未完成状态，并就他在尝试与扩展坞控制平面通信时遇到的阻碍寻求帮助。“如果您了解 DisplayLink DL3 控制平面的交互序列，或者有关于隔离全总线时序/排序属性的想法（这是逐通道差异分析无法看到的），欢迎提供帮助。”他收到了一些开发者的反馈，包括 Danilo Krummrich 关于重构驱动程序以使用 Rust KMS 绑定的建议，但没有针对他试图解决的问题提供具体建议。

Lothian also opened an issue on the EVDI GitHub repository on June 17. It explained the work he'd done so far, outlined the approach in trying to reverse-engineer the DL3 protocol, provided links to his GitHub repository with the Vino code, and asked for clarification on a few issues. There was no reply to his issue. He also reached out to Łukasz Spintzyk of Synaptics directly by email; his correspondence with the company between July 5 and October 7 is available in this PDF compiled by Lothian. He noted that he had found a few bugs in EVDI and opened a pull request (that went unreviewed for several months), and also asked for assistance where he was blocked on Vino.

Lothian 还于 6 月 17 日在 EVDI GitHub 存储库中提出了一个议题。该议题解释了他迄今为止所做的工作，概述了尝试逆向工程 DL3 协议的方法，提供了包含 Vino 代码的 GitHub 存储库链接，并就几个问题寻求澄清。他的议题没有得到回复。他还通过电子邮件直接联系了 Synaptics 的 Łukasz Spintzyk；他与该公司在 7 月 5 日至 10 月 7 日期间的通信记录收录在 Lothian 整理的这份 PDF 中。他指出自己在 EVDI 中发现了一些错误并提交了拉取请求（该请求数月未被审核），同时也请求在 Vino 受阻的地方提供协助。

On July 20, he received a reply, not from Spintzyk, but from Michal Lukaszek of Synaptics: The reason I'm writing is that DisplayLink is not an open standard. The protocol, codec, and other technologies used by DisplayLink devices are proprietary to Synaptics and are protected by a substantial portfolio of patents in a number of jurisdictions, including the UK. From what you've published, it appears that the VINO project is progressing beyond interoperability work and toward implementing technology that we believe is covered by those patents. While many developers understandably forget that patent law is different. An independently written or clean-room implementation does not necessarily avoid patent infringement if it implements patented inventions.

7 月 20 日，他收到了一封回复，发件人不是 Spintzyk，而是 Synaptics 的 Michal Lukaszek：我写信的原因是 DisplayLink 并非开放标准。DisplayLink 设备使用的协议、编解码器及其它技术均为 Synaptics 的专有技术，并在包括英国在内的多个司法管辖区受到大量专利组合的保护。从您发布的内容来看，VINO 项目似乎已经超出了互操作性工作的范畴，转向了实现我们认为受这些专利保护的技术。虽然许多开发者可以理解地忽略了专利法与版权法的不同。如果独立编写或“净室”实现方案中包含了受专利保护的发明，并不一定能避免专利侵权。

Lukaszek said it was entirely possible that Lothian was unaware of the "intellectual property landscape surrounding DisplayLink", so he wanted to make him aware now rather than "have the situation become more formal later". Lothian lives in Scotland and has a ".co.uk" email address, which explains why the email made reference to the UK.

Lukaszek 表示，Lothian 很可能不了解“围绕 DisplayLink 的知识产权环境”，因此他希望现在就让他知晓，而不是“等到以后情况变得更加正式”。Lothian 居住在苏格兰，拥有一个“.co.uk”的电子邮件地址，这解释了为什么邮件中提到了英国。

Cease and desist. On August 17, Andrew Bateman, an assistant general council of intellectual property for Synaptics, sent a cease-and-desist notice. The letter stated that Lothian had not "substantially responded" to Lukaszek's communication, and had continued with "public statements and further development activities". It included an appendix with a list of 125 patents that "cover fundamental aspects of DisplayLink's architecture and operation", but did not specify which patents might be infringed by the work.

停止侵权通知。8 月 17 日，Synaptics 知识产权助理总法律顾问 Andrew Bateman 发出了一份停止侵权通知。信中称，Lothian 没有对 Lukaszek 的沟通做出“实质性回应”，并继续进行“公开声明和进一步的开发活动”。信中附带了一份包含 125 项专利的清单，称这些专利“涵盖了 DisplayLink 架构和操作的基本方面”，但并未具体说明哪些专利可能因该工作而受到侵权。

Synaptics included a list of eight demands it expected immediate compliance with, including ceasing development of "any software or other implementation that infringes any Synaptics patent", withdrawing submissions of materials "made to Linux kernel maintainers, mailing lists, code-hosting platforms, package maintainers, distributions, or any other person or organization", and to identify in writing every location "in which the relevant materials have been publ[ished]".

Synaptics 列出了八项要求，并期望立即得到遵守，包括停止开发“任何侵犯 Synaptics 专利的软件或其它实现”，撤回“提交给 Linux 内核维护者、邮件列表、代码托管平台、软件包维护者、发行版或任何其它个人或组织”的材料，并以书面形式确定“相关材料已发布”的所有位置。
