I’ll sing the praises of Our Place to anyone who will listen. I’ve tried most of its kitchenware, and I have yet to use a product I disliked. Not only do they perform well, but they’re all thoughtfully designed and look great in my kitchen. The only downside is that it can be spendy. Right now, Shop is offering 15 to 30 percent off a huge variety of Our Place kitchenware, and you can get an extra $60 off when you spend $100 or more. This is an excellent way to save on a gift for yourself or someone else.

我会向任何愿意倾听的人赞美 Our Place。我试用过他们大部分的厨具，目前还没有用到过不喜欢的。它们不仅性能出色，而且设计精巧，放在我的厨房里看起来非常棒。唯一的缺点是价格可能偏高。目前，Shop 正在对多种 Our Place 厨具提供 15% 到 30% 的折扣，消费满 100 美元还可额外减免 60 美元。这是为自己或他人选购礼物并节省开支的绝佳机会。

The Our Place Always Pan is my number-one kitchen sidekick. I use it nearly every single time I cook because it’s so versatile. The design works well for frying eggs and flipping pancakes, but it’s also deep enough for spaghetti sauce or a quick veggie stir-fry. The nontoxic nonstick coating is still holding up well, though it does stain a bit, which I don’t mind. It’s easy to clean, too. While I think it’s worth $95 regularly, you might want to pick up some accessories to hit that $100 threshold for an extra $60 off. I especially like the Spruce Steamer basket for frozen dumplings and the Egg Poacher to help me hit my protein macros.

Our Place Always Pan 是我厨房里的头号助手。我几乎每次做饭都会用到它，因为它非常全能。它的设计既适合煎蛋和翻煎饼，深度也足以用来做意大利面酱或快速炒蔬菜。无毒不粘涂层依然保持良好，虽然会有一点染色，但我并不介意。它也很容易清洗。虽然我认为它平时的 95 美元售价物有所值，但你可能还是想买些配件来达到 100 美元的门槛，从而额外获得 60 美元的优惠。我特别喜欢用来蒸冷冻饺子的 Spruce 蒸笼，以及能帮我补充蛋白质摄入的煮蛋器。

I just started testing the Wonder Oven Pro, and I like it so much that I’m considering ditching my microwave. It takes a lot to make me like a countertop appliance given that my apartment kitchen is so tiny, but this sleek little machine has a myriad of functions, from roasting and broiling and baking to dehydrating and even a fancy steam infusion mode (which I have yet to try). The reheat mode is helping me actually eat my leftovers instead of leaving them to die in the fridge. The whole appliance is easy to clean, and the controls are pretty intuitive. If your rental’s appliances are subpar or you just want another multifunction kitchen doodad that works, this is an easy recommendation, especially at its sale price.

我刚开始测试 Wonder Oven Pro，它让我非常喜欢，以至于我正在考虑扔掉微波炉。考虑到我的公寓厨房非常狭小，要让我喜欢上一款台面电器并不容易，但这款精致的小机器拥有无数功能，从烘烤、炙烤、焙烤到脱水，甚至还有一个高级的蒸汽注入模式（我还没试过）。它的加热模式让我真正开始吃剩菜，而不是让它们在冰箱里被遗忘。整个设备很容易清洁，控制也非常直观。如果你的出租屋电器质量一般，或者你只是想要另一个好用的多功能厨房小工具，我很容易推荐这款产品，尤其是在它打折的时候。
