Senate Democrats today opposed President Trump’s nominee for an open Federal Communications Commission seat, and criticized Republicans for moving to confirm a new commissioner during the period before Democrats take control of Congress in January 2027. Democrats also plan to use their majority next year to launch probes of FCC Chairman Brendan Carr.

参议院民主党人今天反对特朗普总统提名的联邦通信委员会（FCC）空缺席位人选，并批评共和党人在2027年1月民主党控制国会之前的这段时期内急于确认新委员的任命。民主党人还计划在明年利用多数席位优势对FCC主席布伦丹·卡尔（Brendan Carr）展开调查。

“After the American people last week overwhelmingly rejected the MAGA agenda, there is no justification for this committee to advance partisan nominees during the lame-duck period,” Sen. Ed Markey (D-Mass.) said today at the Commerce Committee’s nomination hearing for Danielle Thumann Severs, Trump’s pick to fill an empty commissioner’s seat. Thumann Severs, an attorney and senior counsel to Carr, faced questions from Democrats about whether she supports Carr’s attacks on TV broadcasters.

马萨诸塞州民主党参议员埃德·马基（Ed Markey）在今天举行的商务委员会提名听证会上表示：“在上周美国人民以压倒性多数拒绝了‘让美国再次伟大’（MAGA）议程后，本委员会在‘跛脚鸭’时期推进党派提名人选是毫无道理的。”此次听证会针对的是特朗普提名的丹妮尔·图曼·西弗斯（Danielle Thumann Severs），她被提名为填补空缺的委员席位。作为一名律师兼卡尔的高级法律顾问，图曼·西弗斯面临民主党人关于她是否支持卡尔攻击电视广播公司的问题。

“Do you support Chairman Carr’s illegal campaign to remove Jimmy Kimmel from the nation’s airwaves?” Sen. Ben Ray Luján (D-N.M.) asked the nominee. Thumann Severs responded that she has not advised Carr on media-related topics and declined Luján’s request to identify any specific policy issues on which she disagrees with Carr.

新墨西哥州民主党参议员本·雷·卢汉（Ben Ray Luján）问这位提名人：“你是否支持卡尔主席将吉米·坎摩尔（Jimmy Kimmel）从全国广播中移除的非法行动？”图曼·西弗斯回应称，她并未就媒体相关议题向卡尔提供建议，并拒绝了卢汉要求她指出与卡尔意见不合的具体政策问题的请求。

“I don’t think Chairman Carr is going to be around more than another three or four months” Luján predicted trouble for Carr when Democrats take the majority in both chambers of Congress. He seemed to suggest that Carr will quickly grow tired of having to testify in Congress multiple times a month under the Democratic majorities. “I don’t think Chairman Carr is going to be around more than another three or four months because when we have the gavels in here, he will be here once a month,” Luján said. “And Mr. Carr, if you’re listening now, know you’re going to be in this body once a month, if not more, and in the House of Representatives as well, answering questions, and you don’t have the protection of the gavel any longer.”

“我认为卡尔主席的任期不会超过三四个月了。”卢汉预言，当民主党在国会两院占据多数席位时，卡尔将面临麻烦。他似乎暗示，在民主党掌握多数席位的情况下，卡尔很快会厌倦每月多次在国会作证。卢汉说：“我认为卡尔主席待不了三四个月了，因为当我们掌握了木槌（控制权）时，他每个月都得来这里一次。卡尔先生，如果你现在在听，要知道你每个月至少要来这个机构一次，众议院那边也是，你得回答问题，而且你再也没有木槌的保护了。”

Luján said Democrats will use “subpoena authorities that you have to respond to so we can get to the bottom of the nonsense and the cover-ups that have been taking place.” Markey accused Thumann Severs of evading questions about Carr threatening and taking actions against broadcasters on behalf of Trump. “Anyone seeking a seat on the Federal Communications Commission must be willing to stand up to such threats and to defend the First Amendment, and you’re not willing to do so here in your confirmation hearing,” Markey said.

卢汉表示，民主党人将使用“你必须回应的传票权力，以便我们查清正在发生的荒唐事和掩盖行为。”马基指责图曼·西弗斯回避关于卡尔代表特朗普威胁并针对广播公司采取行动的问题。马基说：“任何寻求联邦通信委员会席位的人都必须愿意抵制此类威胁并捍卫第一修正案，而你在今天的确认听证会上并不愿意这样做。”

Democrats similarly criticized Thumann Severs for not giving a clear answer on whether the FCC has authority to eliminate E-Rate, a $2 billion-a-year program that was created by Congress to connect students and library visitors to the Internet. Carr has proposed eliminating or scaling back the program. Thumann Severs said she helped Carr work on the E-Rate Notice of Proposed Rulemaking, which seeks public comment about the program and the FCC’s authority to change or eliminate it. “I can’t prejudge the open proceeding [on E-Rate] at the FCC right now,” she said today.

民主党人同样批评图曼·西弗斯未能就FCC是否有权取消“E-Rate”项目给出明确答复。该项目由国会设立，每年耗资20亿美元，旨在为学生和图书馆访客提供互联网连接。卡尔已提议取消或缩减该项目。图曼·西弗斯表示，她曾协助卡尔起草关于E-Rate的拟议规则制定通知，该通知旨在征求公众对该项目以及FCC是否有权更改或取消该项目的意见。她今天表示：“我目前无法预判FCC正在进行的（关于E-Rate的）公开程序。”

“I am not comfortable with your answers today” Sen. Tammy Duckworth (D-Ill.) criticized Thumann Severs’ answers. “If you’re confirmed, you’re going to be one of the votes that decides whether the FCC stays an independent regulator, or becomes an arm of the White House press office. We deserve to know where you stand, and I am not comfortable with your answers today,” Duckworth said.

“我对你今天的回答感到不安。”伊利诺伊州民主党参议员塔米·达克沃斯（Tammy Duckworth）批评了图曼·西弗斯的回答。达克沃斯说：“如果你被确认，你将成为决定FCC是保持独立监管机构地位，还是成为白宫新闻办公室分支的投票者之一。我们有权知道你的立场，而我对你今天的回答感到不安。”

Duckworth told Thumann Severs that the FCC cannot eliminate E-Rate without Congressional approval. “We never directed the FCC to eliminate the program and frankly, at a time when the price of gas, groceries, and utilities is skyrocketing, the last thing the FCC should be doing is making it harder for schools to stay connected,” Duckworth said.

达克沃斯告诉图曼·西弗斯，未经国会批准，FCC无权取消E-Rate项目。达克沃斯说：“我们从未指示FCC取消该项目。坦率地说，在汽油、食品杂货和公用事业价格飞涨的时候，FCC最不应该做的事情就是增加学校保持网络连接的难度。”

The Senate Commerce, Science & Transportation Committee held today’s hearing for Thumann Severs and three other nominees for federal positions. Senators have until Thursday to submit additional questions for the record, and nominees will have until November 16 to respond. Thumann Severs needs approval from the committee and then the full Senate to be confirmed to the FCC. Committee Chairman Ted Cruz (R-Texas) is trying to get her approved before Republicans have to give up their Senate majority.

参议院商业、科学与交通委员会今天为图曼·西弗斯及其他三名联邦职位提名人举行了听证会。参议员们需在周四前提交额外的问题以供记录，提名人则需在11月16日前做出回应。图曼·西弗斯需要获得委员会和参议院全体会议的批准才能正式进入FCC。委员会主席泰德·克鲁兹（Ted Cruz，得克萨斯州共和党人）正试图在共和党失去参议院多数席位之前促成她的任命。

Thumann Severs would give Republicans an unusual 3-1 advantage on the FCC, which usually operates with a 3-2 split. Democrats criticized Republicans for not pairing her nomination with a Democratic one. The commission has only one Democrat, Anna Gomez, and she would have to leave at the end of 2027 if she is not given another term. “For many decades, the Senate has paired commission nominees: A Republican and a Democrat moving together,” Sen. John Hickenlooper (D-Colo.) said. “Congress established the FCC as an independent, bipartisan agency… a bipartisan FCC is essential to avoid things like large-scale media consolidation, and I think also more fundamentally to protect a free and diverse press.”

图曼·西弗斯的加入将使共和党在FCC获得罕见的3比1优势，而该委员会通常以3比2的比例运作。民主党人批评共和党人没有将她的提名与民主党人选的提名进行配对。目前委员会中只有一名民主党人安娜·戈麦斯（Anna Gomez），如果她没有获得连任，她必须在2027年底离职。科罗拉多州民主党参议员约翰·希肯卢珀（John Hickenlooper）表示：“几十年来，参议院一直将委员会提名人进行配对：一名共和党人和一名民主党人同时推进。国会将FCC设立为一个独立的、两党合作的机构……两党合作的FCC对于避免大规模媒体整合至关重要，我认为从根本上讲，这对于保护自由和多元化的媒体也至关重要。”

Because US law requires the FCC to have three commissioners for a quorum, Gomez’s “presence in the current 2-1 Republican majority is necessary for the FCC to conduct business,” Hickenlooper said. The Communications Act prohibits a single party from holding more FCC seats than is necessary to form a majority, but Hickenlooper predicted that Trump will fire Gomez to give Republicans a 3-0 stranglehold. “If today’s nominee is confirmed, President Trump could then fire Commissioner Gomez and have an FCC with no Democratic voice at all,” Hickenlooper said.

由于美国法律要求FCC必须有三名委员才能达到法定人数，希肯卢珀表示，戈麦斯“在当前2比1的共和党多数席位中的存在，对于FCC开展业务是必要的。”《通信法》禁止单一政党占据超过形成多数席位所需的FCC席位，但希肯卢珀预测特朗普将解雇戈麦斯，从而让共和党获得3比0的绝对控制权。希肯卢珀说：“如果今天的提名人获得确认，特朗普总统随后可能会解雇戈麦斯委员，使FCC完全失去民主党的声音。”

Hickenlooper questioned whether Thumann Severs is sufficiently independent from Carr. “Ms. Thumann Severs, your career has largely been spent serving Brendan Carr,” Hickenlooper said. “[You are] currently Chairman Carr’s senior counsel; previously, you were legal advisor to Commissioner Carr.”

希肯卢珀质疑图曼·西弗斯是否足够独立于卡尔。希肯卢珀说：“图曼·西弗斯女士，你的职业生涯大部分时间都在为布伦丹·卡尔服务。（你）目前是卡尔主席的高级法律顾问；此前，你是卡尔委员的法律顾问。”
