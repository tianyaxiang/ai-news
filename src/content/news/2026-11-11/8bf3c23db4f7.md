The US military is taking a new approach to prop up commercial launch companies as SpaceX looks to wind down its Falcon rocket program, suggesting defense officials see the workhorse rocket’s retirement as a potential national security risk.

随着 SpaceX 计划逐步淘汰其猎鹰（Falcon）火箭项目，美国军方正在采取一种新的方式来支持商业发射公司，这表明国防官员将这款主力火箭的退役视为潜在的国家安全风险。

The Pentagon’s Office of Strategic Capital has twice in the last two weeks announced it was sending cash to two of SpaceX’s competitors in the launch business. First, on October 26, the office announced a $150 million conditional loan commitment to Stoke Space. Military officials revealed a second $150 million Pentagon loan Tuesday for Rocket Lab.

在过去两周内，五角大楼的战略资本办公室（OSC）两次宣布向 SpaceX 的两家发射业务竞争对手提供资金。首先，该办公室于 10 月 26 日宣布向 Stoke Space 提供 1.5 亿美元的有条件贷款承诺。周二，军方官员又披露了五角大楼向 Rocket Lab 提供的第二笔 1.5 亿美元贷款。

Stoke Space and Rocket Lab are the first two launch companies to receive significant support from the Office of Strategic Capital (OSC), an organization established in 2022 to infuse companies developing “vital” national security technologies with capital. OSC has been on a loan spree in recent weeks, committing hundreds of millions of dollars to companies specializing in batteries, drones, and semiconductors.

Stoke Space 和 Rocket Lab 是首批获得战略资本办公室（OSC）大力支持的两家发射公司。该组织成立于 2022 年，旨在为开发“至关重要”国家安全技术的公司注入资本。最近几周，OSC 一直在进行贷款发放，向专注于电池、无人机和半导体的公司承诺了数亿美元的资金。

SpaceX, the Pentagon’s leading launch provider, has stopped taking commercial orders for Falcon 9 launches after 2028 as the company shifts its focus to the next-generation, super-heavy-lift Starship rocket. Falcon 9 and Falcon Heavy launches for government customers, including NASA and the US Space Force, are expected to continue a while longer.

作为五角大楼的主要发射供应商，SpaceX 已停止接受 2028 年之后的猎鹰 9 号（Falcon 9）商业发射订单，因为该公司正将重心转向下一代超重型运载火箭“星舰”（Starship）。预计为包括美国国家航空航天局（NASA）和美国太空军在内的政府客户提供的猎鹰 9 号和猎鹰重型（Falcon Heavy）发射任务还将持续一段时间。

The Space Force’s launch contract with SpaceX allows the military to place Falcon 9 and Falcon Heavy orders through 2029, with the actual launches extending through 2033. Then, if Starship is fulfilling its promise, SpaceX plans to retire Falcon for good.

太空军与 SpaceX 签订的发射合同允许军方在 2029 年之前下达猎鹰 9 号和猎鹰重型火箭的订单，实际发射任务将持续到 2033 年。此后，如果“星舰”能够兑现其承诺，SpaceX 计划彻底淘汰猎鹰系列火箭。

Starship may eventually be capable of launching national security payloads, but SpaceX’s near-term priority is using the rocket’s massive cargo capacity for its own Starlink Internet satellites and Starmind orbital data centers.

“星舰”最终或许有能力发射国家安全载荷，但 SpaceX 近期的首要任务是利用该火箭巨大的载货能力来部署其自有的星链（Starlink）互联网卫星和 Starmind 轨道数据中心。
