# Oura is acquiring medical AI chatbot startup Counsel Health

Oura announced its quarterly earnings today and said it agreed to acquire Counsel Health for $625 million, after adding the company’s AI chatbot to its app earlier this year. Counsel Health calls itself an “AI-native primary care company,” using the app to deliver AI-generated responses and insights based on users’ health data reported from their wearables that can then proceed to a conversation with one of its in-house physicians.

Oura 今日公布了季度财报，并表示已同意以 6.25 亿美元收购医疗 AI 聊天机器人初创公司 Counsel Health。此前，Oura 已于今年早些时候将该公司的 AI 聊天机器人整合进其应用程序中。Counsel Health 自称为一家“AI 原生初级保健公司”，利用该应用程序根据用户可穿戴设备报告的健康数据提供 AI 生成的回复和见解，随后用户可与公司内部医生进行对话。

Oura says it expects the deal to close before the end of the year. Oura CEO Tom Hale said in an interview with Bloomberg that the acquisition is part of a plan to connect wearables, clinical AI, and access to doctors based on insights from biometric data to answer questions or monitor ongoing conditions.

Oura 表示，预计该交易将在今年年底前完成。Oura 首席执行官 Tom Hale 在接受彭博社采访时表示，此次收购是其计划的一部分，旨在将可穿戴设备、临床 AI 以及基于生物识别数据洞察的医生问诊服务连接起来，以解答健康问题或监测持续性病症。

Counsel Health’s AI chatbot can connect Oura users to a real doctor directly from the app and draw on users’ biometric data from their wearables in its answers to medical questions. The plan they describe in the press release is to focus on three areas, including preventative care, acute care for episodic health concerns, and specialty and chronic care for things like reproductive and cardiometabolic health.

Counsel Health 的 AI 聊天机器人可以直接在应用程序内将 Oura 用户与真实医生对接，并在回答医疗问题时调用用户可穿戴设备中的生物识别数据。他们在新闻稿中描述的计划重点关注三个领域，包括预防性护理、针对偶发性健康问题的急性护理，以及针对生殖和心脏代谢健康等方面的专科与慢性病护理。

According to Bloomberg, Counsel Health’s CEO Muthu Alagappan says it’s also planning to expand its network of on-demand physicians, which is currently “made up of about 50 people.” Counsel’s 70 employees will join Oura as part of the deal, with Alagappan becoming Oura’s chief clinical AI officer.

据彭博社报道，Counsel Health 首席执行官 Muthu Alagappan 表示，公司还计划扩大其按需医生网络，该网络目前“由约 50 人组成”。作为交易的一部分，Counsel 的 70 名员工将加入 Oura，Alagappan 将出任 Oura 的首席临床 AI 官。

Oura says in its announcement that Counsel will also continue offering its medical AI platform outside of Oura’s user base through new and existing enterprise partners, including “health plans, health systems, consumer health organizations, and employers.”

Oura 在公告中表示，Counsel 还将继续通过新的和现有的企业合作伙伴，向 Oura 用户群之外的群体提供其医疗 AI 平台，这些合作伙伴包括“健康计划、医疗系统、消费者健康组织和雇主”。
