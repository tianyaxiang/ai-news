# Rocco’s Mini Mini Fridge Is Here (2026)

If you've always coveted Rocco’s vintage-inspired beverage fridges but haven't been able to swing the $1,999-plus price tag, here's some news you might like: Today, the brand released the Mini Mini, a desktop version of its legendary mini fridge. It comes in Green, Graphite, Cream, and new Stainless Steel. (Only Stainless Steel is available right now.)

如果你一直垂涎 Rocco 复古风格的饮料冰箱，却又无法承担 1999 美元以上的价格，那么这里有个好消息：今天，该品牌发布了 Mini Mini，这是其传奇迷你冰箱的桌面版本。它有绿色、石墨色、奶油色和新款不锈钢色可供选择。（目前仅提供不锈钢色。）

We haven't tested it yet or even seen it in person, but Rocco says the Mini Mini can hold 12 standard 12-ounce cans and gets down to 41 degrees Fahrenheit. If you've ever tried other fridges of this size, you'll know this is an impressive stat. (Sorry, mini Frigidaire—you're cute but warm.)

我们尚未对其进行测试，甚至还没有亲眼见过它，但 Rocco 表示，Mini Mini 可以容纳 12 罐标准的 12 盎司饮料，并能降温至华氏 41 度。如果你尝试过其他同尺寸的冰箱，就会知道这是一个令人印象深刻的数据。（抱歉，迷你 Frigidaire——你很可爱，但不够凉。）

Rocco calls the Mini Mini “the first countertop fridge with real fridge standards.” It's got 360-degree ventilation with the refrigeration mechanics underneath the unit. That means it can sit flush against a wall, wherever that might be.

Rocco 将 Mini Mini 称为“第一款具备真正冰箱标准的台式冰箱”。它拥有 360 度通风系统，制冷机械装置位于设备底部。这意味着它可以紧贴墙壁放置，无论是在哪里。

The only note is that, unlike the larger fridges, like the Classic and the Essential, this Rocco does not have a smart-beverage recognition feature—it's just for looks and performance only.

唯一需要注意的是，与 Classic 和 Essential 等大型冰箱不同，这款 Rocco 没有智能饮料识别功能——它仅用于外观和性能。

It's available for $295, and while it’s not currently on sale, unlike the brand's larger fridges, you can get it for $50 off when you buy a full-sized Rocco Classic or Essential.

它的售价为 295 美元，虽然目前没有折扣，但与该品牌的大型冰箱不同，当你购买全尺寸的 Rocco Classic 或 Essential 时，可以享受 50 美元的优惠。
