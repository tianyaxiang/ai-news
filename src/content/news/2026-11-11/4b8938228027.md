# Google’s Pixel Tag tracker is now available

Pixel Tag 今日正式发布，单价 30 美元，四件套优惠价为 100 美元。

The Pixel Tag launches today for $30 each or discounted to $100 for a four-pack.

在经历了三星、摩托罗拉和 Pebblebee 等公司多年推出的第三方安卓追踪器后，谷歌的 Pixel Tag 现已上市。Pixel Tag 于去年 8 月首次发布，旨在与谷歌的追踪网络及去年更名为“Find Hub”的应用程序配合使用。Pixel Tag 的定价与苹果的 AirTag 2 相近，单个追踪器售价 29.99 美元，四件套优惠价为 99.99 美元。

After years of third-party Android trackers from companies like Samsung, Motorola, and Pebblebee, Google’s Pixel Tag is now available for purchase. First announced in August, the Pixel Tag is designed to work with Google’s tracking network and app that was renamed the Find Hub last year. The Pixel Tag is priced similar to Apple’s AirTag 2. A single tracker costs $29.99 while a four-pack is discounted to $99.99.

Pixel Tag 重 0.4 盎司，比 AirTag 2 略轻。虽然其长条形设计比苹果的追踪器更长，但谷歌的产品要薄一些。它没有设计用于连接钥匙、行李或其他追踪物品的孔洞，但你可以搭配配件将其轻松系在物体上。与苹果的 AirTag 不同，Pixel Tag 有一个按钮，如果你的手机放错了地方，可以按下该按钮触发声音来寻找手机，而不是寻找追踪器本身。

At 0.4 ounces, the Pixel Tag is slightly lighter than the AirTag 2 and while its oblong design is longer than Apple’s tracker, Google’s is a bit thinner. It doesn’t include a hole to attach it to keys, luggage, or other items you want to track, but you can pair it with accessories that make it easy to tether to objects. Unlike Apple’s AirTags, the Pixel Tag has a button that can be pressed to trigger a sound should your phone get misplaced instead of the tracker.

谷歌表示，Pixel Tag 使用一颗 CR2032 纽扣电池可续航一年以上，并具备 IP67 防尘防水等级，可以承受浅水浸泡。该追踪器还支持超宽带 (UWB) 技术以及通过蓝牙 6.0 实现的信道探测 (Channel Sounding) 功能。当用户靠近时，配备 UWB 芯片的安卓手机可以在 Find Hub 应用中显示 Pixel Tag 的精确距离和方向，而蓝牙信道探测功能则仅限于显示距离。

Google says the Pixel Tag will run for over a year on a single CR2032 coin battery, and it has an IP67 rating so it’s dustproof and can survive a shallow dunking. The tracker also features ultra wideband (UWB) support and Channel Sounding through Bluetooth 6.0. Android phones with a UWB chip can display the exact distance and direction to the Pixel Tag in the Find Hub app once you’re close to it, while Bluetooth Channel Sounding is limited to just distance.

Pixel Tag 兼容运行 Android 9 或更高版本的智能手机，但你需要使用专门配备 UWB 芯片或支持蓝牙 6.0 的设备，才能利用 Find Hub 的精确查找功能。

The Pixel Tag is compatible with smartphones running Android 9 or later, but you’ll need devices that specifically include a UWB chip or support Bluetooth 6.0 to take advantage of Find Hub’s precise locating features.
