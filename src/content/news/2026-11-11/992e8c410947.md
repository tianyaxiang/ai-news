# Pay for Hugging Face Hub with your Google Cloud Account

# 使用您的 Google Cloud 账户支付 Hugging Face Hub 费用

You can now link your Hugging Face organization to your Google Cloud account, so that your Hugging Face usage shows up on your Google Cloud bill instead of being charged to a credit card. Get started on the Google Cloud Marketplace.

现在，您可以将您的 Hugging Face 组织关联至 Google Cloud 账户，这样您的 Hugging Face 使用费用将直接显示在 Google Cloud 账单中，而无需通过信用卡扣款。请前往 Google Cloud Marketplace 开始使用。

Why pay through Google Cloud? Hugging Face is where teams find, build and run open models: from hosting private models and datasets, to running GPU workloads with Jobs, deploying models with Inference Endpoints, calling thousands of models through Inference Providers, or hosting demos on GPU Spaces. If your company already runs on Google Cloud, adding a new vendor and a corporate credit card just to use these services can slow things down.

为什么要通过 Google Cloud 支付？Hugging Face 是团队发现、构建和运行开源模型的地方：从托管私有模型和数据集，到通过 Jobs 运行 GPU 工作负载、使用 Inference Endpoints 部署模型、通过 Inference Providers 调用数千个模型，或在 GPU Spaces 上托管演示。如果您的公司已经在运行 Google Cloud，为了使用这些服务而额外增加新的供应商和企业信用卡可能会降低效率。

By linking your organization through the Google Cloud Marketplace: One bill: your Hugging Face usage appears on your existing Google Cloud invoice, next to the rest of your cloud spend. No new vendor to onboard: purchases go through your existing Google Cloud billing account and procurement process. Everything is covered: upgrading your org to a Team or Enterprise subscription, pay-as-you-go compute (Jobs, Inference Endpoints, Inference Providers, GPU Spaces, ZeroGPU quota extension), and additional storage at public Hugging Face pricing.

通过 Google Cloud Marketplace 关联您的组织，您可以获得以下优势：统一账单：您的 Hugging Face 使用费用将出现在现有的 Google Cloud 发票中，与您的其他云支出合并。无需引入新供应商：购买流程通过您现有的 Google Cloud 结算账户和采购流程完成。涵盖所有项目：包括将组织升级为团队或企业订阅、按需付费的计算资源（Jobs、Inference Endpoints、Inference Providers、GPU Spaces、ZeroGPU 配额扩展）以及按 Hugging Face 公开定价计算的额外存储空间。

If you're an admin of your Hugging Face organization, here's how to set it up, step by step. 1. Subscribe to Hugging Face on the Google Cloud Marketplace. Go to the Hugging Face Hub listing on the Google Cloud Marketplace, select the Google Cloud project you want to use, and click on "Subscribe". You are now on the subscription page. Select the billing account to be charged, review and accept the terms, then click "Subscribe". Once the order is placed, Google Cloud asks you to finish setting up your account with Hugging Face. Click on "Sign up with Hugging Face" to be redirected to Hugging Face.

如果您是 Hugging Face 组织的管理员，请按照以下步骤进行设置。1. 在 Google Cloud Marketplace 订阅 Hugging Face。前往 Google Cloud Marketplace 上的 Hugging Face Hub 页面，选择您要使用的 Google Cloud 项目，然后点击“订阅”。进入订阅页面后，选择要扣款的结算账户，阅读并接受条款，然后点击“订阅”。订单提交后，Google Cloud 会要求您完成与 Hugging Face 的账户设置。点击“使用 Hugging Face 注册”即可跳转至 Hugging Face。

2. Link your Hugging Face organization. You are now on Hugging Face, where you can select the organization you want to link to your Google Cloud account. Only organizations where you are an admin are listed. Select it and confirm. You are then redirected to the billing settings of your organization. The payment method section now shows the linked Google Cloud account, with a pending status. Every cloud marketplace subscription is reviewed by the Hugging Face team before paid usage starts being billed through it. While the subscription is pending, usage keeps being billed to the organization's existing payment method, if any.

2. 关联您的 Hugging Face 组织。跳转至 Hugging Face 后，您可以选择要关联至 Google Cloud 账户的组织。列表中仅显示您担任管理员的组织。选择并确认后，您将跳转至组织的结算设置页面。此时，支付方式部分会显示已关联的 Google Cloud 账户，状态为“待处理”。每项云市场订阅在开始通过其结算付费使用前，均需经过 Hugging Face 团队审核。在订阅处于待处理状态期间，使用费用将继续通过组织现有的支付方式（如有）进行结算。

3. Subscription approved. Once your subscription is approved, you will receive an email confirming that your Google Cloud account and Hugging Face organization are linked. Organizations that already have a credit card on file switch to cloud marketplace billing at the start of their next billing period. Organizations on the free tier with no credit card on file usually switch immediately, so their paid usage is billed through the cloud marketplace right away. To confirm it, open the billing settings of your organization: the Google Cloud account is now shown as the organization's active payment method.

3. 订阅获批。订阅获批后，您将收到一封确认邮件，告知您的 Google Cloud 账户与 Hugging Face 组织已成功关联。对于已绑定信用卡的组织，将在下一个计费周期开始时切换至云市场结算。对于未绑定信用卡的免费层级组织，通常会立即切换，从而直接通过云市场结算付费使用费用。您可以打开组织的结算设置进行确认：此时 Google Cloud 账户将显示为该组织的有效支付方式。

Congratulations! Your organization is now billed through your Google Cloud account. All members of your organization can use Hugging Face compute services, and the usage will show up on your Google Cloud bill. A few things worth knowing: Google Cloud replaces the credit card as the organization's payment method. Usage is metered and reported to Google Cloud, so adding credits is not available while the organization is billed through the marketplace. Included credits still apply: if your organization has a Team or Enterprise subscription, the monthly included credits are applied before any pay-as-you-go usage is reported to Google Cloud.

恭喜！您的组织现在已通过 Google Cloud 账户进行结算。组织的所有成员均可使用 Hugging Face 计算服务，相关使用费用将显示在您的 Google Cloud 账单中。需要注意的事项：Google Cloud 将取代信用卡成为组织的支付方式。使用量会被计量并上报给 Google Cloud，因此在通过云市场结算期间，无法使用充值额度。包含的额度依然有效：如果您的组织拥有团队或企业订阅，每月包含的额度会在按需付费的使用量上报给 Google Cloud 之前先行抵扣。

Monitor your usage at any time from the billing section of your organization settings, and find the charges in the Billing reports of the Google Cloud console. Linking and unlinking are audited: both actions are recorded in your organization's audit logs. Thanks for reading! You can find more details in the billing documentation. If you have any questions, please contact us at billing@huggingface.co.

您可以随时在组织设置的结算部分监控使用情况，并在 Google Cloud 控制台的“结算报告”中查看费用明细。关联与取消关联操作均受审计：这两项操作都会记录在您组织的审计日志中。感谢阅读！您可以在结算文档中了解更多详情。如有任何疑问，请通过 billing@huggingface.co 联系我们。
