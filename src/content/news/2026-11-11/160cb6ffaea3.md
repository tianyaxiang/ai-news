# The Grindr 1 Percent Is Getting Its Own Gated Community

在周五的一场 Reddit AMA（问答）活动中，Grindr 首席执行官乔治·阿里森（George Arison）在谈论同性恋“熊族”的笑话以及 JD 万斯（JD Vance）潜在用户名可能是“CushionCasanova”的调侃之余，回答了一个关于经济的问题。他直言不讳地写道：“通货膨胀非常严重，这个国家存在着巨大的负担能力危机，而我们的政府至少从 2020 年起就对此视而不见。”他在 r/wallstreetbets 子版块发表了上述言论，当时他正在那里回答用户关于公司季度财报发布后的提问。

It was a startling admission from the man whose company is about to release a subscription offering intended to price out most Grindr users. On November 17, Edge, a “premium tier” with some AI-powered features costing $120 a month, will be available to members worldwide, according to the report. Early access opened today.

这份报告显示，Edge 将帮助用户“更轻松地”建立联系，并将包含一项名为“洞察”（Insights）的个性化推荐功能，以“了解兼容性和回复的可能性”，以及名为“重新连接”（Reconnect）的功能，该功能可以“将过去的联系人重新带回视野”。该应用程序将拥有全新的设计、界面和图标，旨在为愿意支付高昂价格的用户创造一种不同的体验。

But even if you can afford it, you may not get in. As with Raya, an invite-only app where some people have been on the wait list for as long as seven years, the company is planning to turn Edge into a digital gated community of sorts: Sign-ups will be limited to 1 percent of global monthly users.

报告指出，其目标是“刻意管理 Edge 社区的增长以保持排他性”，服务于那些能够支付“明显更高费用”的“精选社区”。根据该公司的数据，在其目前的 1600 万用户中，只有 140 万人付费使用 Grindr。

Edge’s official rollout comes following pilot programs in Australia, New Zealand, Canada, and the US, and a year’s worth of speculation about how much it would cost and what exactly would separate it from other apps that provide comparable features at a lower price. “These VIP tiers make me think of [it as a] digital version of expensive hustler bars,” one user said on Instagram. “The men who will pay hundreds for access are rarely the hot men you would log on for.”

Grindr 是全球最大的同性恋、双性恋和跨性别者约会应用程序，但它一直饱受数据隐私和用户安全问题的困扰。随着其最新增值服务的推出，许多成员表示该应用程序的基础设施仍然存在严重缺陷。

“Grindr is a top-tier example of enshitification and predatory monetization,” one person wrote in the reddit AMA from last week. “You need to stop with the intrusive ads and start getting in touch with reality,” posted another.

今年 9 月，该公司就涉嫌与第三方供应商共享用户 HIV 状况和检测信息一事，达成了 3500 万美元的集体诉讼和解；和解协议的细节中不包含 Grindr 方面的任何责任承认。同月，Grindr 收购了远程医疗公司 Freddie，该公司旨在帮助人们获取 HIV 预防药物 PrEP。此次收购建立在阿里森对医疗保健的投资之上，旨在将该应用程序转型为社交网络。目前，Grindr 通过其 Woodwork 项目销售勃起功能障碍药物和 GLP-1 减肥药物。

Critics will likely argue that pivoting to luxury dating in an economy where more and more people are struggling isn’t great optics, but Arison is betting that improved features and a “deliberately limited membership” will create opportunity for “substantially higher pricing over time,” the report noted.
