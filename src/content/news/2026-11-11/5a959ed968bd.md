# OpenAI, Anthropic face new oversight as New York’s AI safety law takes effect

New York is gearing up to enforce one of the country’s most significant AI safety laws. The state opened a registration portal Tuesday, with which large frontier AI developers like Anthropic and OpenAI will be required to register by January 1, 2027. The RAISE Act, New York’s safety and transparency bill, kicks in at the start of the year.

纽约正准备执行该国最重要的 AI 安全法律之一。该州周二开通了一个注册门户网站，Anthropic 和 OpenAI 等大型前沿 AI 开发商必须在 2027 年 1 月 1 日之前通过该门户完成注册。纽约州的《RAISE 法案》（一项安全与透明度法案）将于年初生效。

It mandates that large frontier developers — companies with more than $500 million in annual revenue — have to publish safety frameworks on their websites, report critical safety incidents within 72 hours, and issue public model transparency reports. The newly formed Office of Digital Innovation, Governance, Integrity, and Trust (DIGIT) will implement the RAISE Act and oversee large frontier AI labs.

该法案规定，大型前沿开发商（年收入超过 5 亿美元的公司）必须在其网站上发布安全框架，在 72 小时内报告重大安全事件，并发布公开的模型透明度报告。新成立的数字创新、治理、诚信与信任办公室（DIGIT）将负责实施《RAISE 法案》并监管大型前沿 AI 实验室。

Penalties can be as high as $1 million for one violation and $3 million for subsequent violations. The state also can bring enforcement actions against companies that fail to register with the new portal, though a spokesperson declined to provide specifics.

单次违规的罚款最高可达 100 万美元，后续违规的罚款最高可达 300 万美元。该州还可以对未能在新门户网站注册的公司采取执法行动，尽管发言人拒绝提供具体细节。

“Tech moguls tell us that their vision for AI is inevitable,” Governor Kathy Hochul said Tuesday during a press briefing. “They tell us get on board or get left behind… That any collateral damage is worth it as long as we win the race to the AI cliff.”

“科技大亨们告诉我们，他们对 AI 的愿景是不可避免的，”州长凯西·霍楚尔（Kathy Hochul）周二在新闻发布会上说。“他们告诉我们要么加入，要么被抛在后面……只要我们能在通往 AI 悬崖的竞赛中获胜，任何附带损害都是值得的。”

On Tuesday, Hochul also launched the New York Center for AI Responsibility and Research based at State University of New York’s Binghamton University. The center will independently evaluate the safety, security, and operation of advanced AI systems and work with DIGIT to review companies’ safety frameworks. Hochul first announced the center in January with $55 million in donations and capital.

周二，霍楚尔还启动了位于纽约州立大学宾汉姆顿分校的纽约 AI 责任与研究中心。该中心将独立评估先进 AI 系统的安全性、保障性和运行情况，并与 DIGIT 合作审查公司的安全框架。霍楚尔于今年 1 月首次宣布成立该中心，并获得了 5500 万美元的捐款和资金。

“We’re building a national model for smart, sensible AI regulation, a model that puts communities above corporations and people above powerful technology,” Hochul said.

“我们正在建立一个全国性的智能、明智的 AI 监管模式，这种模式将社区置于企业之上，将人置于强大的技术之上，”霍楚尔说。

The RAISE Act isn’t the only safety law New York has passed. Last year, the state began enforcing a law that requires AI companion operators to regularly let users know they’re talking to a bot, and to make reasonable efforts to detect suicidal ideation and self-harm. In October, Hochul signed a separate chatbot safety law for minors, restricting behaviors like deceptive human-like interactions, emotionally manipulative or sycophantic responses, and encouragement of behaviors like suicide or disordered eating. That law takes effect June 1, 2027.

《RAISE 法案》并不是纽约通过的唯一安全法律。去年，该州开始执行一项法律，要求 AI 伴侣运营商定期告知用户他们正在与机器人交谈，并做出合理努力来检测自杀意念和自残行为。10 月，霍楚尔签署了另一项针对未成年人的聊天机器人安全法，限制了欺骗性的人类互动、情感操纵或谄媚的回复，以及鼓励自杀或饮食失调等行为。该法律将于 2027 年 6 月 1 日生效。

Other states have also passed similar AI safety and transparency bills to the RAISE Act. California’s SB 53, which took effect in January, mandates that frontier AI labs have in place and publish safety frameworks, enact whistleblower protections, and report critical safety incidents within 15 days. Illinois’ SB 315, which will go into effect at the start of 2027, has similar requirements, plus includes independent safety compliance audits.

其他州也通过了类似于《RAISE 法案》的 AI 安全和透明度法案。加利福尼亚州的 SB 53 法案于 1 月生效，要求前沿 AI 实验室建立并发布安全框架，制定举报人保护措施，并在 15 天内报告重大安全事件。伊利诺伊州的 SB 315 法案将于 2027 年初生效，其要求类似，并增加了独立的合规性安全审计。
