Oura announced on Tuesday that it’s acquiring AI-native primary care company Counsel Health for $625 million. The deal comes a few weeks after Oura postponed its up to $2.2 billion IPO indefinitely, citing “uncertainty in the IPO market.”

Oura 周二宣布，将以 6.25 亿美元收购人工智能原生初级保健公司 Counsel Health。此前几周，Oura 以“IPO 市场的不确定性”为由，无限期推迟了规模最高可达 22 亿美元的首次公开募股（IPO）。

Counsel Health is an AI-powered healthcare platform that combines a chat-based medical experience with access to licensed physicians. Its service was integrated into the Oura app in June. Users can connect with Counsel’s medical AI to get personalized medical advice, and chat with a physician when needed, all within the Oura app.

Counsel Health 是一个人工智能驱动的医疗保健平台，将基于聊天的医疗体验与执业医师服务相结合。其服务已于 6 月整合进 Oura 应用程序中。用户可以在 Oura 应用内连接 Counsel 的医疗 AI 获取个性化建议，并在需要时与医生进行交流。

Now, the companies plan to expand on their existing partnership to build across preventative care, acute care, and specialty and chronic care. While Oura has largely dominated the smart ring market for years, rivals like RingConn and Ultrahuman are emerging to dethrone it, putting pressure on the company to keep innovating.

目前，两家公司计划在现有合作基础上，将业务扩展至预防性护理、急性护理以及专科和慢性病护理领域。尽管 Oura 多年来在智能戒指市场占据主导地位，但 RingConn 和 Ultrahuman 等竞争对手正在崛起并试图挑战其地位，这迫使该公司必须持续创新。

By acquiring Counsel Health, Oura is looking to expand its business and help users access medical care. “Oura helps members understand their physiology and, with Counsel, they can take action on their health across a range of needs, from quick questions and prescriptions to lab results, chronic care, and referrals,” said Oura CEO Tom Hale in a press release.

通过收购 Counsel Health，Oura 希望扩大业务范围并帮助用户获得医疗服务。Oura 首席执行官 Tom Hale 在新闻稿中表示：“Oura 帮助会员了解自己的生理状况，而通过 Counsel，他们可以针对从快速咨询、处方到化验结果、慢性病护理和转诊等一系列健康需求采取行动。”

Counsel Health raised a $25 million Series A a year ago, led by Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) and GV. PitchBook estimates it had raised a total of $36 million. Following the acquisition, Oura says Counsel will continue serving existing and new enterprise partners, with the goal of expanding its AI-native care model to more people beyond Oura’s membership.

Counsel Health 一年前完成了由 Andreessen Horowitz (a16z) 和 GV 领投的 2500 万美元 A 轮融资。据 PitchBook 估计，该公司总融资额为 3600 万美元。收购完成后，Oura 表示 Counsel 将继续服务现有及新的企业合作伙伴，目标是将这种人工智能原生护理模式推广给 Oura 会员以外的更多人群。

Counsel CEO Muthu Alagappan will join Oura as chief clinical AI officer, alongside the company’s 70 employees, Bloomberg reports. “At Counsel, we’ve built medical AI around trust, safety and responsibility, keeping physicians in the loop for clinical decisions,” Alagappan said in the press release.

据彭博社报道，Counsel 首席执行官 Muthu Alagappan 将携公司 70 名员工加入 Oura，担任首席临床 AI 官。Alagappan 在新闻稿中表示：“在 Counsel，我们围绕信任、安全和责任构建了医疗 AI，确保医生参与临床决策。”

“Oura’s data gives clinicians objective context, trended biometrics, and member engagement to deliver more informed care. From the start, our teams bonded over a joint vision, shared culture, and mission, further solidifying as we negotiated our deal over the past few months.” The deal is expected to close before the end of 2027.

“Oura 的数据为临床医生提供了客观背景、趋势生物识别信息和会员参与度，从而提供更具针对性的护理。从一开始，我们的团队就因共同的愿景、文化和使命而紧密联系在一起，并在过去几个月的交易谈判中进一步巩固了这种关系。”该交易预计将于 2027 年底前完成。

Oura also announced that its revenue grew 153% year-over-year to $533 million for the quarter ended on September 30. For its fiscal year 2026, Oura generated revenue of approximately $1.75 billion, marking 92% year-over-year growth. The company reached an $11 billion valuation following a $900 million late-stage funding round in October 2025.

Oura 还宣布，截至 9 月 30 日的季度，其营收同比增长 153%，达到 5.33 亿美元。在 2026 财年，Oura 的营收约为 17.5 亿美元，同比增长 92%。在 2025 年 10 月完成 9 亿美元的后期融资后，该公司估值达到 110 亿美元。
