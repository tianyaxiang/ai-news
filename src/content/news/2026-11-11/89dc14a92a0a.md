Tubi users can now create and share playlists of movies and shows available on the streaming service. Tubi is a free ad-supported streaming television (FAST) service that has both live channels and on-demand content.

Tubi 的用户现在可以创建并分享该流媒体服务中电影和节目的播放列表。Tubi 是一项免费的广告支持流媒体电视（FAST）服务，提供直播频道和点播内容。

Today, the Fox-owned company announced “Mixtapes” as a way for users to make watchlists from Tubi’s “nearly 400,000 movies and TV episodes,” Tubi’s announcement said, adding: They can give each mixtape a title and description and arrange the entertainment inside in whatever order feels right to them. Viewers can build one entirely on their own, or describe what they’re looking for and let Tubi offer a starting place that they can edit, rearrange, or rebuild entirely. When their mixtape is finished, they can publish and share it with other people or keep it for themselves.

今天，这家福克斯旗下的公司宣布推出“Mixtapes”功能，让用户可以从 Tubi “近 40 万部电影和电视剧集”中制作播放列表。Tubi 的公告称，用户可以为每个 Mixtape 添加标题和描述，并按照自己喜欢的顺序排列其中的内容。观众可以完全自主构建列表，也可以描述自己的需求，让 Tubi 提供一个起点，然后进行编辑、重新排列或彻底重构。当 Mixtape 完成后，用户可以选择发布并分享给他人，或者仅供自己观看。

Tubi’s Mixtapes website already features numerous playlists, including: “Millennial Memory Lane”—with movies like Cruel Intentions (1999) and The Fifth Element (1997) and shows such as Everybody Hates Chris (2005–2009) and Degrassi: The Next Generation (2001–2015); and “Bald Baddies”—which includes Fast & Furious Presents: Hobbs & Shaw (2019) and Central Intelligence (2016). Watching a Mixtape doesn’t require a Tubi account, but making one does.

Tubi 的 Mixtapes 网站上已经展示了许多播放列表，包括：“千禧一代回忆录”（Millennial Memory Lane），其中包含《残酷动机》（1999 年）和《第五元素》（1997 年）等电影，以及《人人都恨克里斯》（2005–2009 年）和《迪格拉丝中学的下一代》（2001–2015 年）等剧集；还有“光头硬汉”（Bald Baddies），其中包括《速度与激情：特别行动》（2019 年）和《乌龙特工》（2016 年）。观看 Mixtape 不需要 Tubi 账户，但创建它则需要。

Tubi’s Mixtapes comes at a time when streaming service providers are seeking a way to address the content fragmentation that’s frequently cited as a frustration among streaming users, as well as users struggling to find something to watch. Gracenote’s “2025 State of Play” report, for instance, surveyed 3,000 consumers in the US, Brazil, France, Germany, Mexico, and the UK and found that “nearly 33 percent of streaming users feel content and service fragmentation negatively impacts their TV experience.”

Tubi 推出 Mixtapes 之际，正值流媒体服务提供商寻求解决内容碎片化问题的时刻，这常被流媒体用户视为一种困扰，同时也反映了用户在寻找观看内容时的挣扎。例如，Gracenote 的《2025 年游戏状态》报告调查了美国、巴西、法国、德国、墨西哥和英国的 3000 名消费者，发现“近 33% 的流媒体用户认为内容和服务碎片化对他们的电视观看体验产生了负面影响”。

Users are also likely to stop using a streaming service if they struggle to find something they want to watch, per the survey. Forty-nine percent of users said they “are willing to cancel a service based on difficulty finding what to watch.” Additionally, 19 percent of respondents said they would “abandon a viewing session if their content search is not successful.” Although Tubi users don’t have paid subscriptions to cancel, making it easier to find relevant content could help Tubi grow its user base and increase the time people spend watching, which supports the advertising business Tubi relies on.

根据调查，如果用户难以找到想看的内容，他们也很可能会停止使用某项流媒体服务。49% 的用户表示，他们“愿意因为难以找到想看的内容而取消服务”。此外，19% 的受访者表示，如果内容搜索不成功，他们会“放弃观看”。尽管 Tubi 用户没有需要取消的付费订阅，但让用户更容易找到相关内容有助于 Tubi 扩大用户群并增加观看时长，从而支持 Tubi 所依赖的广告业务。

Sharable watchlists are rare among streaming services. Mubi has a similar feature where users can make lists of movies and shows that people can follow, but the lists can feature content that’s not available on Mubi. Plex and JustWatch also let people create and share curated watchlists with other users. Still, it’s nice to see a streaming service, especially a free one, add new capabilities at a time when many other streaming services are consistently increasing subscription prices. And while some streaming service companies are turning to AI to help with content recommendations, a human connection may appeal more to certain users, while keeping costs lower for Tubi.

可分享的播放列表在流媒体服务中并不多见。Mubi 有类似的功能，用户可以制作电影和节目列表供他人关注，但这些列表可能包含 Mubi 上没有的内容。Plex 和 JustWatch 也允许用户创建并与其他用户分享精选播放列表。尽管如此，在许多其他流媒体服务不断提高订阅价格的时候，看到一项流媒体服务（尤其是免费服务）增加新功能，依然令人欣慰。虽然一些流媒体公司正转向人工智能来辅助内容推荐，但人与人之间的联系可能对某些用户更具吸引力，同时也为 Tubi 降低了成本。
