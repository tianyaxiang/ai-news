As Anthropic barrels toward an enormous IPO that could value it at $1.4 trillion, the company has disclosed a tidbit in its new S-1 filing about how many shares each of its seven co-founders plan to sell. Each will sell 3,378,378 shares. Anthropic has floated an unusually broad initial price range of $70 to $78 a share. At the midrange of $74, each founder would take in just shy of $250 million.

随着 Anthropic 迈向可能使其估值达到 1.4 万亿美元的巨额首次公开募股（IPO），该公司在其新的 S-1 文件中披露了一则消息，说明了其七位联合创始人每人计划出售的股份数量。每人将出售 3,378,378 股股票。Anthropic 给出了一个异常宽泛的初始价格区间，即每股 70 美元至 78 美元。按 74 美元的中位数计算，每位创始人将获得略低于 2.5 亿美元的收益。

At the low end, $70, each would have to settle for pocketing just over $236 million in cash; at the high end, $78, each would take in just under $264 million. However, in an IPO as eagerly anticipated as this one, companies can often price shares above the initial range. The S-1 is already hinting at high demand. Board member Reed Hastings has indicated he wants to buy $300 million worth of shares through his donor-advised fund. Board member Christopher Liddell also wants to buy $3 million worth of shares.

在 70 美元的低端，每人将不得不满足于获得略高于 2.36 亿美元的现金；在 78 美元的高端，每人将获得略低于 2.64 亿美元的收益。然而，在如此备受期待的 IPO 中，公司往往能以高于初始区间的价格定价。S-1 文件已经暗示了市场的高需求。董事会成员里德·黑斯廷斯（Reed Hastings）已表示，他希望通过其捐赠者建议基金购买价值 3 亿美元的股票。董事会成员克里斯托弗·利德尔（Christopher Liddell）也希望购买价值 300 万美元的股票。

The seven co-founders are Dario Amodei, CEO; Daniela Amodei, president and chair of the board; Tom Brown, chief compute officer; Jack Clark, head of public benefit; Jared Kaplan, chief science officer (pictured above); Sam McCandlish, chief architect; and Christopher Olah, interpretability research lead. After the sale, each of them will own about 3.2% of the total outstanding Class A shares, the S-1 says, or roughly 22% collectively. That’s not their share of the company overall.

这七位联合创始人分别是：首席执行官 Dario Amodei；总裁兼董事会主席 Daniela Amodei；首席计算官 Tom Brown；公共利益主管 Jack Clark；首席科学官 Jared Kaplan（见上图）；首席架构师 Sam McCandlish；以及可解释性研究负责人 Christopher Olah。S-1 文件显示，出售后，他们每人将拥有已发行 A 类股总数的约 3.2%，合计约 22%。这并非他们在公司整体中的持股比例。

Class A is just one of Anthropic’s share classes, and other holders, including Amazon and Google, hold nonvoting Class N shares, so the founders’ stake in the company’s total equity will be considerably smaller. Even still, they, as a group, remain in control of the company. Each will also receive one share of Class F stock, which carries supervoting rights. Collectively, the founders will control 51.1% of the votes, but no single founder will hold significantly more shares or votes than the others.

A 类股只是 Anthropic 的股票类别之一，其他持有者（包括亚马逊和谷歌）持有无投票权的 N 类股，因此创始人在公司总股本中的持股比例将小得多。即便如此，他们作为一个群体，仍然控制着公司。每位创始人还将获得一股带有超级投票权的 F 类股。总体而言，创始人将控制 51.1% 的投票权，但没有任何一位创始人持有的股份或投票权明显多于其他人。

As is now industry lore, the seven left OpenAI to start Anthropic in 2021 after disagreements with OpenAI’s leadership over how AI should be built. Siblings Dario and Daniela Amodei, who held senior research and policy roles at OpenAI, were joined by five fellow OpenAI colleagues in forming a company that has said, very publicly and from the start, that it’s more focused on AI safety than its rivals, including the founders’ former employer.

正如现在业内广为流传的故事，这七人在 2021 年因与 OpenAI 领导层在 AI 构建方式上存在分歧而离开 OpenAI，创立了 Anthropic。曾在 OpenAI 担任高级研究和政策职务的 Dario 和 Daniela Amodei 兄妹，与另外五位 OpenAI 同事共同创立了这家公司。该公司从一开始就非常公开地表示，与包括其前雇主在内的竞争对手相比，它更专注于 AI 安全。
