Google’s answer to Apple’s AirTag, the Google Pixel Tag, goes on sale starting Tuesday, allowing Android users to track items like their keys, wallet, or bag using their smartphone’s built-in Find Hub. First announced in August, the $29.99 device takes cues from the AirTag and other lost-item finders by offering connectivity via Bluetooth and ultra-wideband for precision finding. This lets Android device owners, including Pixel Watch wearers, locate their missing items using a combination of visual distance and directional guidance.

作为苹果 AirTag 的竞争对手，谷歌 Pixel Tag 于周二正式发售。安卓用户现在可以使用手机内置的“查找中心”（Find Hub）来追踪钥匙、钱包或包等物品。这款售价 29.99 美元的设备于今年 8 月首次发布，它借鉴了 AirTag 及其他失物追踪器的设计，通过蓝牙和超宽带技术实现精准定位。安卓设备用户（包括 Pixel Watch 的佩戴者）可以通过视觉距离和方向引导来定位丢失的物品。

Plus, the tag has a built-in speaker to play a chime that helps you locate your item if nearby. (Or, if you’ve lost your phone, you can press the button on the Pixel Tag twice to ring it, too.) You can choose between three different levels of loudness in the settings within the Find Hub, with the maximum volume around 80 decibels. Though the Pixel Tag serves the same purpose as an AirTag, Google has opted for a different design. Instead of a small, round device, like Apple’s, the Pixel Tag is slimmer and more elongated.

此外，该标签内置扬声器，可在物品靠近时播放提示音以帮助定位。（或者，如果你弄丢了手机，也可以双击 Pixel Tag 上的按钮让手机响铃。）你可以在“查找中心”的设置中选择三种不同的音量级别，最大音量约为 80 分贝。虽然 Pixel Tag 的用途与 AirTag 相同，但谷歌选择了不同的设计。Pixel Tag 没有采用苹果那种小巧圆润的造型，而是更加纤薄细长。

Spec-by-spec, the Pixel Tag is 1.1-inches wide, compared to the AirTag’s 1.26 inches. As the Pixel Tag is oval-shaped, it’s 1.8-inches high from top to bottom. Thickness-wise, the Pixel Tag clocks in at a depth of 0.2 inches, compared with the AirTag’s 0.31 inches (which Apple calls its height). The slimness of the device might make it more appealing for some, particularly if they’re adding the tag to a small item, like a keychain. The device weighs just 0.4 oz, with the battery included. Unlike Apple, Google opted for a physical button you can press on the tag, which makes it more obvious how it’s meant to work.

从规格上看，Pixel Tag 的宽度为 1.1 英寸，而 AirTag 为 1.26 英寸。由于 Pixel Tag 呈椭圆形，其高度为 1.8 英寸。在厚度方面，Pixel Tag 为 0.2 英寸，而 AirTag 为 0.31 英寸（苹果称之为高度）。这种纤薄的设计可能会吸引部分用户，尤其是当他们将标签挂在钥匙扣等小物件上时。该设备含电池重量仅为 0.4 盎司。与苹果不同，谷歌在标签上设置了一个物理按键，这使得其操作方式更加直观。

Google didn’t originally say that the Pixel Tag included NFC when it announced the device in August. NFC is used by Apple’s AirTag to aid people who find lost items, as they can tap the side of the tag to launch a webpage that displays the owner’s contact information. Google uses the tap gesture to instead connect the user to the device’s settings. Meanwhile, for lost items that are not nearby, the Pixel Tag relies on Google’s crowdsourced network of over a billion Android devices globally. The network, similar to competitive offerings from Apple and Tile, taps into the connectivity provided by other Android devices, which can identify and share the location of the lost item back to its owner.

谷歌在 8 月发布 Pixel Tag 时并未提及它包含 NFC 功能。苹果的 AirTag 利用 NFC 帮助拾获者，他们只需轻触标签侧面即可打开网页查看失主的联系信息。而谷歌则将轻触手势用于连接设备设置。同时，对于不在附近的丢失物品，Pixel Tag 依赖于谷歌在全球拥有超过 10 亿台安卓设备的众包网络。该网络与苹果和 Tile 的竞品类似，利用其他安卓设备提供的连接功能，识别并与失主共享丢失物品的位置。

This location information is displayed on a map inside the Find Hub app. While the person who found the item can’t tap it, as with NFC-enabled devices, Pixel Tags that are put into “Lost” mode will be able to share the owner’s email, number, and a short message so the person who finds the tag can contact you. When the tag comes back into range of the user’s phone, the item is automatically marked as found. The Pixel Tag’s location can also be shared with up to 10 people in the Find Hub app, which could be useful for shared items like the family car keys, which multiple people might rely on.

这些位置信息会显示在“查找中心”应用内的地图上。虽然拾获者无法像使用 NFC 设备那样通过轻触来获取信息，但处于“丢失”模式的 Pixel Tag 可以共享失主的电子邮件、电话号码和简短留言，以便拾获者与你联系。当标签回到用户手机的范围内时，物品会自动标记为已找回。Pixel Tag 的位置还可以与“查找中心”应用中的最多 10 人共享，这对于家庭车钥匙等多人共用的物品非常有用。

Like AirTag, the Pixel Tag is powered by a user-replaceable coin cell battery (the CR2032), which makes the device reusable for the long term. (Google claims the device has up to one year of battery life to start.) Meanwhile, the device itself is made from stainless steel and features IP67 water and dust resistance (maximum depth of 1 meter up to 30 minutes). It pairs with devices running Android 9 or newer via Fast Pair technology. The Pixel Tag comes in two shades: silver and a pale green, and can be bought individually or in a four-pack for $99.99.

与 AirTag 一样，Pixel Tag 由用户可更换的纽扣电池（CR2032）供电，这使得该设备可以长期重复使用。（谷歌声称该设备初始电池续航时间长达一年。）同时，设备本身由不锈钢制成，具备 IP67 级防尘防水性能（最深 1 米，最长 30 分钟）。它通过“快速配对”（Fast Pair）技术与运行 Android 9 或更高版本的设备配对。Pixel Tag 有银色和淡绿色两种颜色可选，可单独购买，也可购买 99.99 美元的四件装。

Alongside the launch, the company Bellroy is debuting its own keychain cases in a variety of colors and styles, which will also be sold on the Google Store. Post updated after publication to note that the Pixel Tag now supports NFC, which was a surprise addition not mentioned at the time of its announcement.

随着产品发布，Bellroy 公司也推出了各种颜色和款式的钥匙扣保护套，这些产品也将在 Google Store 上架销售。本文在发布后进行了更新，指出 Pixel Tag 现已支持 NFC 功能，这是发布时未提及的惊喜补充。
