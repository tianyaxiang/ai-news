Over the past few years, X has released a number of features to make itself a friendlier hub for influencers. The latest effort to attract social media talent? Its own clipping tool.

在过去几年中，X 发布了多项功能，旨在使其成为对网红更友好的中心。为了吸引社交媒体人才，X 最近采取了什么举措？那就是它自己的剪辑工具。

Clipping is the pivotal social media technique by which longer videos are spliced into TikTok-attention-span length “clips” and then strategically posted to engage as many eyeballs (and, thus, derive as much ad revenue) as possible. On Monday, X announced the tool, which, from what we can tell, appears to be little more than a straightforward video-trimming feature that lets users edit videos and post them to X.

剪辑是一种关键的社交媒体技术，通过这种技术，较长的视频被剪辑成符合 TikTok 注意力时长的“短片”，然后进行策略性发布，以尽可能多地吸引眼球（从而获得尽可能多的广告收入）。周一，X 宣布了这一工具。据我们观察，这似乎只是一个简单的视频剪辑功能，允许用户编辑视频并将其发布到 X 上。

The site says that every clip includes a “Clip from” label that links back to the original video from which it was derived, allowing X users to see more about the creator and the creator’s content. “Grab your favorite moment from any video, add your take, and post it without leaving X,” the company posted.

该网站表示，每个剪辑都包含一个“Clip from”（剪辑自）标签，链接回原始视频，让 X 用户能够了解更多关于创作者及其内容的信息。该公司发文称：“从任何视频中截取你最喜欢的时刻，加上你的看法，无需离开 X 即可发布。”

The platform added that both creators and clippers can “earn from views on the clip through Original Content Rewards,” X’s rewards program. The program is only available for eligible creators and pays out based on specific levels of audience engagement.

该平台补充说，创作者和剪辑者都可以“通过 X 的奖励计划‘原创内容奖励’从剪辑的观看量中获利”。该计划仅适用于符合条件的创作者，并根据特定的受众参与度水平进行支付。

In many cases, influencers pay special firms to handle clipping for them. More and more large companies are also turning to such services to run marketing campaigns, and the technique has been used in political campaigns as well.

在许多情况下，网红会付费给专门的公司来处理剪辑工作。越来越多的大型企业也开始转向此类服务来开展营销活动，这种技术也已被用于政治竞选活动中。

Over the last several years, X has tried to compete with other social sites by expanding the kind of content it supports. To take on sites like YouTube, X introduced long-form videos in 2022 (when the site was still known as Twitter), and they have since taken off on the platform.

在过去几年里，X 一直试图通过扩展其支持的内容类型来与其他社交网站竞争。为了挑战 YouTube 等网站，X 在 2022 年（当时该网站仍被称为 Twitter）引入了长视频，此后这些视频在平台上迅速流行起来。

To be more like Substack, it long ago dispensed with the site’s traditional character limits and introduced long-form blog posts, which are available to premium subscribers. X has kept at it this year.

为了更像 Substack，它很久以前就取消了网站传统的字符限制，并引入了长篇博客文章，供高级订阅用户使用。X 今年一直在坚持这样做。

In March, it added a feature called “exclusive threads” that lets creators put parts of a thread behind a paywall so only subscribers can read them, and in July it launched a live-streaming studio and set aside $1 million for creators who go live.

3 月，它增加了一项名为“独家推文串”（exclusive threads）的功能，允许创作者将推文串的部分内容设为付费墙，只有订阅者才能阅读；7 月，它推出了直播工作室，并为直播创作者预留了 100 万美元的资金。
