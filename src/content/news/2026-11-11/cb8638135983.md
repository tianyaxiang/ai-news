In an effort to make ChatGPT an even better consumer discovery engine, OpenAI is launching a host of new features designed to help companies control how users interact with their businesses. On Tuesday, the company rolled out a Business Hub that will serve as a place where companies can create business profiles that — in a feature that greatly resembles the kind of profiles that Google and other search engines already offer — allow enterprises to verify and update information about their products and services. The feature is currently in beta.

为了使 ChatGPT 成为更好的消费者发现引擎，OpenAI 正在推出一系列新功能，旨在帮助企业控制用户与其业务的互动方式。周二，该公司推出了一个“商业中心”（Business Hub），企业可以在此创建商业资料。这一功能与谷歌及其他搜索引擎提供的资料页面非常相似，允许企业验证并更新其产品和服务信息。该功能目前处于测试阶段。

Eligible companies will also be able to create and launch a specialized business agent, which gives ChatGPT users the ability to ask questions about the business and its products. “As people turn to AI to discover products, compare options and make decisions, ChatGPT has become the platform for people and businesses to connect through AI,” the company said on Tuesday. “Today’s announcement is an early step in bringing businesses more deeply into ChatGPT.”

符合条件的公司还将能够创建并发布专门的业务代理，使 ChatGPT 用户能够针对该企业及其产品进行提问。该公司周二表示：“随着人们转向人工智能来发现产品、比较选项并做出决策，ChatGPT 已成为人们与企业通过人工智能建立联系的平台。今天的公告是将企业更深入地引入 ChatGPT 的早期步骤。”

In a move that calls to mind Google Analytics, the Business Hub will also give users access to limited business insights. These insights will reveal how ChatGPT users interact with their products — including profile views, business mentions, website clicks, and other metrics. Businesses will have to verify their profiles through a formal process, although the company did not share specifics of what that process would entail. Benji Shomair, OpenAI’s VP of Monetization, told journalists on Monday that there are “multiple verification methods that we use for different types of business in different markets.”

此举让人联想到谷歌分析（Google Analytics），商业中心还将为用户提供有限的业务洞察。这些洞察将揭示 ChatGPT 用户如何与他们的产品互动，包括资料浏览量、业务提及次数、网站点击量以及其他指标。企业必须通过正式流程验证其资料，尽管该公司并未分享该流程的具体细节。OpenAI 变现副总裁 Benji Shomair 周一告诉记者，他们“针对不同市场中不同类型的企业使用了多种验证方法”。

OpenAI previously tried to position ChatGPT as an e-commerce hub by which users could buy products directly. This effort — which used a commission fee system — was viewed as a potentially huge source of revenue and a way to make ChatGPT a more direct Amazon competitor. However, that project ultimately failed to take off and, in March, the company retired the effort, which had even included a “checkout cart” feature.

OpenAI 此前曾试图将 ChatGPT 定位为电子商务中心，让用户可以直接购买产品。这一尝试采用了佣金制度，曾被视为潜在的巨大收入来源，也是使 ChatGPT 成为亚马逊更直接竞争对手的一种方式。然而，该项目最终未能成功，公司已于三月终止了该计划，该计划甚至曾包含“购物车”功能。

With the integration of business profiles and intelligence, ChatGPT seems to be attempting to become more like Google, which — since the advent of the internet — has been the place where web users go to understand the companies they want to shop at. It remains to be seen whether this initiative will bear better results than the one that sought to make ChatGPT a direct e-commerce hub.

随着商业资料和智能功能的整合，ChatGPT 似乎正试图变得更像谷歌。自互联网诞生以来，谷歌一直是网络用户了解其购物目标企业的首选之地。这一举措是否会比之前试图将 ChatGPT 打造成直接电子商务中心的尝试取得更好的成果，还有待观察。
