The US is winning the AI race. Jensen Huang could have blown it. Nvidia’s chips are powering America’s AI boom. Of course Chinese spies are interested.

美国正在赢得人工智能竞赛，但黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）差点搞砸了。英伟达（Nvidia）的芯片正在推动美国的人工智能热潮，中国间谍对此感兴趣自然不足为奇。

As espionage targets go, Jensen Huang is perhaps one of the least surprising on Earth. The Nvidia CEO runs one of the world’s most valuable companies, controls the in-demand technology fueling the AI boom, and is cozy with presidents, CEOs, and all manner of important people. He also wears a cool leather jacket.

作为间谍目标，黄仁勋或许是地球上最不令人意外的人选之一。这位英伟达首席执行官掌管着全球最有价值的公司之一，掌控着推动人工智能热潮的紧俏技术，并与各国总统、企业高管及各类重要人物关系密切。此外，他还总是穿着一件帅气的皮夹克。

Which makes Huang’s admission that he had an affair with a woman suspected of being a Chinese spy — reportedly while her spying was known to US intelligence and even President Donald Trump — all the more shocking. The fallout goes well beyond his marriage, his reputation, or Nvidia itself. Experts tell The Verge the incident threatens to disrupt the entire US AI industry, potentially jeopardize national security, and could undermine America’s efforts to maintain its fragile technological lead over China in AI.

这使得黄仁勋承认与一名涉嫌为中国间谍的女子有染——据报道，当时美国情报部门甚至唐纳德·特朗普总统本人都已经知晓其间谍身份——显得更加令人震惊。其影响远不止于他的婚姻、声誉或英伟达公司本身。专家告诉《The Verge》，这一事件可能会扰乱整个美国人工智能行业，危及国家安全，并可能削弱美国在人工智能领域保持对华脆弱技术领先地位的努力。

“With those chips, the US lead could vanish in a matter of weeks or months.” Nvidia is the undisputed leader in advanced computer chips, among the AI industry’s scarcest resources. While many ingredients go into building AI models — including talent, energy, capital, and data — top-tier chips are one of the most important, and Nvidia is one of a handful of companies with the expertise to design or manufacture them at the cutting edge.

“有了这些芯片，美国的领先优势可能会在几周或几个月内消失。”英伟达是先进计算机芯片领域无可争议的领导者，而这些芯片是人工智能行业最稀缺的资源之一。虽然构建人工智能模型需要许多要素——包括人才、能源、资本和数据——但顶级芯片是最重要的要素之一，而英伟达是少数几家拥有尖端设计或制造能力的公司之一。

That makes it a gatekeeper to hardware that has become a key bottleneck in global AI development. US companies — including Meta, Anthropic, and OpenAI — are racing to develop chips of their own, in part to reduce their dependence on Nvidia, even as they spend billions securing ever more compute.

这使其成为了硬件领域的守门人，而这些硬件已成为全球人工智能发展的关键瓶颈。包括 Meta、Anthropic 和 OpenAI 在内的美国公司正在竞相开发自己的芯片，部分原因是为了减少对英伟达的依赖，尽管它们仍在斥资数十亿美元以获取更多的计算能力。

For China, the US’s main rival at the frontier of AI, that bottleneck is even tighter. To maintain its technological lead, the US has restricted the sale of the most advanced AI chips to the country in the hopes of slowing its progress in the sector, which has been impressive and is thought to trail the US by a matter of months. In response, China has made developing domestic alternatives a strategic national priority, though its leaders still lag Nvidia at the bleeding edge. At the same time, a thriving black market for restricted Nvidia chips has emerged.

对于美国在人工智能前沿领域的主要竞争对手中国来说，这一瓶颈更为严峻。为了保持技术领先，美国限制向中国出售最先进的人工智能芯片，希望以此减缓其在该领域的发展速度——尽管中国的发展令人瞩目，且被认为仅落后美国几个月。作为回应，中国已将开发国产替代品作为国家战略重点，尽管其在尖端技术上仍落后于英伟达。与此同时，针对受限英伟达芯片的繁荣黑市也随之出现。

Experts tell The Verge that China could exploit access to Nvidia to accelerate its own development, or to impede the US development of AI. Getting its hands on more Nvidia chips is the most obvious win for Beijing, says Emily Kilcrease, a senior fellow and director of the energy, economics, and security program at the Center for a New American Security (CNAS) think tank.

专家告诉《The Verge》，中国可能会利用与英伟达的接触来加速自身发展，或阻碍美国的人工智能发展。新美国安全中心（CNAS）智库的高级研究员兼能源、经济与安全项目主任艾米丽·基尔克里斯（Emily Kilcrease）表示，获得更多的英伟达芯片对北京来说是最显而易见的胜利。

China has advantages in other parts of the AI race — including abundant energy and a solid ecosystem of open weight models — but without the latest Nvidia chips, it “cannot close the gap with US frontier capabilities,” Kilcrease tells The Verge. “With those chips, the US lead could vanish in a matter of weeks or months.”

基尔克里斯告诉《The Verge》，中国在人工智能竞赛的其他方面拥有优势——包括充足的能源和稳固的开放权重模型生态系统——但如果没有最新的英伟达芯片，它“无法缩小与美国前沿能力的差距”。“有了这些芯片，美国的领先优势可能会在几周或几个月内消失。”
