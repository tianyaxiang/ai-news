# Steam Deck performance gets a reality check with Valve's new FPS data

自 Valve 于 2021 年宣布其 Steam Deck 验证计划以来，玩家们发现游戏的“已验证”状态并不总是与其在掌机上的实际运行效果相符。现在，Valve 亲自共享的全新 Steam Deck 帧率数据，可以帮助玩家更深入地了解许多 Steam Deck 游戏所能提供的性能表现。

Valve has been collecting anonymized frame rate data from Steam players who opt in since February. Yesterday evening, the company announced it has started sharing that data on many Steam Store pages as averages and histograms for both the Steam Deck and Steam Deck OLED.

Valve 承认，玩家开启垂直同步（VSync）或帧率限制可能会在一定程度上影响所收集的数据，且这些测量结果并未考虑 Steam Deck 用户为优化游戏运行而可能进行的自定义设置。尽管如此，Valve 表示他们仍然“发现帧率数据集中在相对狭窄的范围内”。

Beyond the badge This kind of data can be extremely useful for distinguishing between the wide range of performance levels that can exist among “Verified” Steam Deck games. Both Cyberpunk 2077 and Skyrim: Special Edition earn the same Steam Deck Verified badge, for instance, but the former sees average Deck frame rates in the 30–40 FPS range, while the latter usually achieves 50–60 FPS on the hardware.

Valve 的帧率数据对于那些被公司认定为“不支持”的游戏更为有用，因为这些游戏在硬件上的“图形设置无法配置为良好运行”，或者 Valve “仍在努力为该游戏添加支持”。尽管有这些警告，但像《星空》、《寂静岭 2》和《刺客信条：枭雄》等游戏，都显示出实际 Steam Deck 用户报告的极高可玩帧率。

Assassin’s Creed Syndicate in particular approaches a 60 FPS average on the Steam Deck OLED hardware despite its “Unsupported” status. Frame rate data can also be useful for judging the single-player modes of games whose multiplayer doesn’t work under SteamOS. Watch Dogs 2, for example, is officially Unsupported on Steam Deck because of anti-cheat compatibility issues, but Steam Deck players report averages of 40+ FPS on the game’s single-player modes.

当然，由于 Steam Deck 拥有固定的硬件配置，收集和平均其帧率数据变得更加简单。在向更广泛的游戏 PC 传达预期帧率时，Valve 表示他们“一直在寻找新方法，以帮助开发者了解客户在游戏中的体验”。

Back in April, JSON files buried in the Steam client pointed to a potential Steam “Framerate Estimator” tool that could let users “Select an App and a PC config to get a chart of estimated framerates, based on the framerates of other Steam users,” according to strings contained within. That could be incredibly useful for Steam users who want to go beyond the listed Minimum and Recommended hardware requirements without relying on shady-looking third-party FPS-estimator websites.
