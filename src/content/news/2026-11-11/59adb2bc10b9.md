# Ring’s Indoor Drone Camera Is Back—and Now Powered by AI

Save this storySave this storyNo one's home tonight, but the sensor on your home office window upstairs alerts your phone: “motion detected.” Maybe it's just your cat or a flicker of a passing car’s headlights. Instead of shrugging it off, you ask Alexa to send your Ring Drone Cam to check it out.

今晚家里没人，但楼上家庭办公室窗户上的传感器向你的手机发送了警报：“检测到移动”。也许只是你的猫，或者是过往车辆闪烁的车灯。你没有置之不理，而是让 Alexa 指派 Ring 无人机摄像头去查看情况。

The tiny, 165-gram drone lifts out of its charging dock, buzzing loudly, and autonomously flies upstairs, giving you a first-person view on your phone’s screen as it scans the room. Since it's dark in the house, the Drone's spotlights kick in, making it look like someone's physically in the home and moving around—a potential deterrent to would-be thieves. All clear? It heads back to its base to recharge.

这架 165 克重的小型无人机从充电底座升起，发出巨大的嗡嗡声，自动飞往楼上，并在扫描房间时通过手机屏幕为你提供第一人称视角。由于屋内光线昏暗，无人机的聚光灯会自动开启，看起来就像有人在家里走动一样——这对潜在的窃贼是一种威慑。确认安全了吗？它会飞回底座充电。

That's the peace of mind Ring wants to offer with its latest home security solution, which it announced today for $250, alongside the Ring Rover Cam, a $1,000 roving security robot designed to help businesses monitor warehouses and factories. Both fall under the company's new Ring Robotics brand. The Rover Cam launches first on December 16, followed by the Drone Cam on January 27.

这就是 Ring 希望通过其最新的家庭安全解决方案所提供的安心感。该产品今日发布，售价 250 美元。同时发布的还有售价 1000 美元的 Ring Rover Cam，这是一款旨在帮助企业监控仓库和工厂的移动安全机器人。两者均隶属于该公司全新的 Ring Robotics 品牌。Rover Cam 将于 12 月 16 日率先上市，Drone Cam 则紧随其后，于 1 月 27 日推出。

This isn't the company's first at-home drone camera. CEO and founder Jamie Siminoff says he had the idea for a flying sentry in 2016 before Amazon bought Ring. The company previously announced an indoor security drone in 2020—the Ring Always Home Cam—and nearly launched it last year. But Siminoff says the team kept running into edge cases that forced them to add more sensors. Those additions required more computing resources and made the drone more power- and battery-hungry, which made it harder to fly. The solution? Move the intelligence off the vehicle and into the cloud.

这并不是该公司首次推出家用无人机摄像头。首席执行官兼创始人 Jamie Siminoff 表示，他在 2016 年亚马逊收购 Ring 之前就有了飞行哨兵的想法。该公司曾在 2020 年宣布过一款室内安全无人机——Ring Always Home Cam，并差点在去年发布。但 Siminoff 说，团队不断遇到边缘情况，迫使他们增加更多传感器。这些增加的传感器需要更多的计算资源，导致无人机对电力和电池的需求增加，从而使其飞行变得更加困难。解决方案是什么？将智能处理从设备上移至云端。

The Drone Cam has enough smarts to land if it loses internet connectivity, but the rest of its intelligence now sits entirely in the cloud; the drone sends video straight to Ring’s servers, where a custom AI Pilot agent tells it where to go. That lets the drone be lightweight and nimble.

Drone Cam 具备足够的智能，在失去互联网连接时可以自行降落，但其其余的智能功能现在完全位于云端；无人机将视频直接发送到 Ring 的服务器，由定制的 AI Pilot 代理指挥其飞行路径。这使得无人机能够保持轻便灵活。

It will avoid people and objects, and if you accidentally stick a finger into the propellers, Siminoff says, you will feel it, but “you'll be fine.” If your cat manages to swat it out of the air, however, that's a different story. A Ring spokesperson tells WIRED customers shouldn't “fly it” if it's damaged and should contact support for help (no word on repairability). The Drone Cam runs a motor health check before take-off every time and won't fly if it detects a problem.

它会避开人和物体。Siminoff 说，如果你不小心把手指伸进螺旋桨，你会感觉到疼痛，但“你会没事的”。然而，如果你的猫设法把它从空中拍落，那就是另一回事了。Ring 发言人告诉《连线》杂志，如果设备损坏，客户不应“继续飞行”，而应联系客服寻求帮助（目前没有关于可维修性的消息）。Drone Cam 每次起飞前都会进行电机健康检查，如果检测到问题，它将不会起飞。

Users can't manually control the drone but can also set up custom flight routes and patrol schedules. It can automatically respond to alerts from security sensors you may have set up in the home, like motion, door, or window sensors. It will not leave your home, as it needs a Wi-Fi network to operate and uses the Alexa Home Map you may have created with existing Echo devices to help it navigate. It can fly five to eight minutes per charge. (The final production model will be black, not white like in the photos.)

用户无法手动控制无人机，但可以设置自定义飞行路线和巡逻计划。它可以自动响应你在家中设置的安全传感器（如移动、门窗传感器）发出的警报。它不会离开你的家，因为它需要 Wi-Fi 网络才能运行，并使用你可能通过现有 Echo 设备创建的 Alexa 家庭地图来辅助导航。每次充电可飞行 5 到 8 分钟。（最终量产型号将为黑色，而非照片中的白色。）

“There are times when you do want to just be able to see everywhere around the home, but you don't necessarily want a [fixed] camera," Siminoff says. “That's why we invented Ring Robotics.”

“有时你确实希望能够看到家里的各个角落，但不一定想要安装（固定）摄像头，”Siminoff 说。“这就是我们发明 Ring Robotics 的原因。”

Because it doesn't work outdoors and since it's well below the 250-gram threshold, the Drone Cam doesn't need to be registered with the Federal Aviation Administration. Drones have also struggled in the US after the Federal Communications Commission banned DJI from importing foreign-made drones without certification. But Siminoff says the Drone Cam complies with all US regulations and is assembled in Texas.

由于它不能在户外工作，且重量远低于 250 克的门槛，Drone Cam 无需向美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）注册。在美国联邦通信委员会（FCC）禁止大疆进口未经认证的外国制造无人机后，无人机行业在美国也面临困境。但 Siminoff 表示，Drone Cam 符合所有美国法规，并在德克萨斯州组装。

But what if a bad actor gets access to the Drone Cam? Siminoff points to the company's TAKE technology, or Throw Away the Key Encryption. It encrypts video in transit and rotates keys every few minutes, but Ring temporarily holds the key to run cloud-based AI features before deleting its copy. That brief server-side access window makes it less foolproof than true end-to-end encryption. (Every Ring account requires two-factor authentication to log in.)

但如果坏人获得了 Drone Cam 的访问权限怎么办？Siminoff 指出了公司的 TAKE 技术，即“丢弃密钥加密”（Throw Away the Key Encryption）。它在传输过程中对视频进行加密，并每隔几分钟轮换一次密钥，但 Ring 会在删除副本之前暂时保留密钥以运行基于云的 AI 功能。这种短暂的服务器端访问窗口使其不如真正的端到端加密那样万无一失。（每个 Ring 账户登录都需要双重身份验证。）

Hackers have also invaded smart home devices and peeked through robot vacuum cameras, baby monitors, and other gadgets, so it's not hard to imagine a similar situation with the Drone Cam. To counter the threat of surreptitious surveillance, Siminoff says, the Drone Cam is loud by design—the extremely loud buzz of its propellers means a bad actor couldn't remotely snoop without tipping off people in the home.

黑客曾入侵过智能家居设备，通过扫地机器人摄像头、婴儿监视器和其他小工具进行窥探，因此不难想象 Drone Cam 也会出现类似情况。为了应对秘密监视的威胁，Siminoff 表示，Drone Cam 在设计上就是大声的——其螺旋桨发出的巨大嗡嗡声意味着坏人无法在不惊动家中人员的情况下进行远程窥探。

Concerns don't just stem from third parties trying to access your devices, though. Amazon's Ring has been accused of normalizing suburban surveillance, partly stemming from the company's cozy relationship with law enforcement, and it has even handed Ring videos to cops without warrants.

然而，担忧不仅来自试图访问你设备的第三方。亚马逊的 Ring 一直被指责使郊区监控常态化，这部分源于该公司与执法部门的密切关系，它甚至在没有搜查令的情况下将 Ring 视频交给警察。

Thorin Klosowski, a senior security and privacy activist at the Electronic Frontier Foundation, says a device like the Drone Cam is a personal choice and that people should “think through” how it will make others in the home—or visitors—feel.

电子前沿基金会（Electronic Frontier Foundation）的高级安全与隐私活动家 Thorin Klosowski 表示，像 Drone Cam 这样的设备是个人选择，人们应该“深思熟虑”它会给家中其他人或访客带来什么样的感受。

“I think the companies who make these sorts of surveillance products are invested in us feeling less safe at home,” Klosowski says. “If there is a way to sell a product that helps alleviate the problem they are creating, I think they will do it.”

“我认为制造这类监控产品的公司，其利益在于让我们感到在家中不够安全，”Klosowski 说。“如果有一种方法可以销售产品来缓解他们所制造的问题，我想他们会去做的。”

Ring Robotics' second robot is designed for businesses, not consumers. The Ring Rover Cam might look familiar—it shares many of the same hardware guts as Amazon's Astro, the short-lived home robot that didn't do much. Amazon had even pivoted it as a $2,350 security robot for businesses before eventually bricking them 10 months after launch.

Ring Robotics 的第二款机器人是为企业而非消费者设计的。Ring Rover Cam 可能看起来很眼熟——它与亚马逊 Astro 共享许多相同的硬件核心，Astro 是一款短命且功能有限的家用机器人。亚马逊甚至曾将其转型为售价 2350 美元的企业安全机器人，但在发布 10 个月后最终将其停用。
