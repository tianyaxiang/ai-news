Southeast Asia’s digital economy to hit $368 billion.

东南亚数字经济规模将达到3680亿美元。

Generative AI is now an essential part of daily life for 243 million Southeast Asians. Read about how this will impact the region’s economy in our 11th e-Conomy SEA report.

生成式人工智能现已成为2.43亿东南亚人日常生活中不可或缺的一部分。请阅读我们第11份《东南亚数字经济报告》，了解这将如何影响该地区的经济。

Southeast Asia’s digital economy is surging, with generative AI driving growth toward a projected $1 trillion valuation by 2035. Consumers are increasingly comfortable using AI for complex tasks, signaling a shift toward autonomous "agentic commerce."

东南亚数字经济正处于激增状态，生成式人工智能正推动其增长，预计到2035年估值将达到1万亿美元。消费者越来越习惯于使用人工智能处理复杂任务，这标志着向自主“代理商业”的转变。

To sustain this momentum, the region must now prioritize expanding data center infrastructure, improving energy efficiency, and establishing unified digital trade policies.

为了保持这一势头，该地区现在必须优先考虑扩大数据中心基础设施、提高能源效率并建立统一的数字贸易政策。

In Southeast Asia, generative AI has become an essential part of daily life, actively used by 243 million people (58% of the working population) and scaling four times faster than the rollout of the mobile internet.

在东南亚，生成式人工智能已成为日常生活中不可或缺的一部分，有2.43亿人（占劳动人口的58%）正在积极使用，其普及速度比移动互联网快四倍。

This has major implications for the region’s economy, according to the 11th edition of the annual e-Conomy SEA report. Building the Intelligent Economy, by Google, Temasek, and Bain & Company, finds that widespread AI implementation is propelling the region’s digital economy to grow 17% year-on-year (YoY) to reach $368 billion in Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) in 2026.

根据第11份年度《东南亚数字经济报告》，这对该地区经济具有重大影响。由谷歌、淡马锡和贝恩公司联合发布的《构建智能经济》报告发现，人工智能的广泛应用正在推动该地区数字经济同比增长17%，到2026年商品交易总额（GMV）将达到3680亿美元。

This puts it firmly on track to exceed $1 trillion by 2035. Using tokens as the new benchmark for the AI economy, the report projects that growing confidence in agentic commerce will drive a 60-fold surge in regional AI usage over the next five years.

这使其稳步迈向2035年超过1万亿美元的目标。报告以“代币”作为人工智能经济的新基准，预计对代理商业日益增长的信心将在未来五年内推动区域人工智能使用量激增60倍。
