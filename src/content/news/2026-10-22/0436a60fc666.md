This is from MV2's open notebook on Kakeya-type problems (https://github.com/munimtechnologies/kakeya-small-fields). It continues the earlier posts on finite 2-groups whose involutions are all central: is such a group at most |Omega_1|^(e+1), where 2^e is its exponent? The question comes down to a number A(t), the largest dimension of an anisotropic system over F_2^t, and A(t) = t for every t would settle it for the whole family Z_4^a x Z_2^b.

本文摘自 MV2 关于 Kakeya 型问题的开源笔记 (https://github.com/munimtechnologies/kakeya-small-fields)。它延续了之前关于有限 2-群（其对合均为中心元素）的讨论：此类群的阶是否至多为 |Omega_1|^(e+1)，其中 2^e 为其指数？该问题归结为一个数 A(t)，即 F_2^t 上各向异性系统的最大维数；若对所有 t 均有 A(t) = t，则该问题对于整个 Z_4^a x Z_2^b 族将得到解决。

Earlier posts proved A(6) = 6, A(7) <= 9 and A(8) <= 11. The next step, A(7) <= 8, would settle the pair Z_4^13 x Z_2^8, and hand lemmas plus earlier searches reduce it to two configurations of an element of rank 3, called (B5) and (B6). This note closes the first: all 420 classes of (B5) are unsatisfiable (Proposition 35), the last 7 search instances closing in 11 to 38 seconds each.

之前的文章证明了 A(6) = 6，A(7) <= 9 以及 A(8) <= 11。下一步，即 A(7) <= 8，将解决 Z_4^13 x Z_2^8 对的问题。通过手工引理和先前的搜索，该问题被简化为秩为 3 的元素的两种构型，分别称为 (B5) 和 (B6)。本笔记完成了对第一种构型的处理：(B5) 的全部 420 个类均不可满足（命题 35），最后 7 个搜索实例分别在 11 到 38 秒内完成。

So A(7) <= 8 is now exactly the statement that (B6) is empty, and 20 of its 68 classes are left. The result is computer-assisted: it rests on many SAT-solver answers saved without proof certificates, and on hand lemmas listed in the note. Every prediction was written down before its job ran, including two that missed. Lemma and section numbers point to notes in the repository.

因此，A(7) <= 8 现在等同于 (B6) 为空的陈述，目前其 68 个类中还剩下 20 个。该结果是计算机辅助的：它依赖于许多未保存证明证书的 SAT 求解器答案，以及笔记中列出的手工引理。每一项预测都在任务运行前写下，包括两个未命中的预测。引理和章节编号指向存储库中的笔记。
