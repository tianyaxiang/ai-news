# Hearing the words and understanding the request are different

Social capabilities across human development | Part 11 Listening to the end of someone's statement matters, but receiving words does not establish shared meaning. Listening also includes checking understanding, responding in a correctable form, and reflecting corrections in action.

人类发展中的社交能力 | 第 11 部分：听完某人的陈述很重要，但仅仅接收到话语并不能建立共同的理解。倾听还包括核实理解情况、以可修正的形式做出回应，并在行动中体现修正。

The blue bowl, not the red one In a kitchen, someone asks, "Could you pass that bowl?" The other person reaches toward a red bowl but pauses to ask, "This one?" The requester points to a blue bowl. It is handed over, and cooking continues. In this fictional scene, merely hearing and executing the words would have produced the wrong object. Clarification jointly resolves ambiguity. The correction is used as information for the task rather than treated as defeat or blame.

蓝碗，而非红碗。在厨房里，有人问：“能把那个碗递给我吗？”另一个人伸手去拿一个红碗，但停下来问道：“是这个吗？”请求者指向一个蓝碗。碗被递了过去，烹饪继续进行。在这个虚构的场景中，仅仅听见并执行话语会导致拿到错误的物品。澄清可以共同解决歧义。这种修正被用作任务所需的信息，而不是被视为失败或指责。

The difficulty of applying understanding Apperly and colleagues investigated adults' difficulties interpreting instructions in relation to another person's perspective. Their experiments provide material for questioning whether using others' information in action is always automatic. See Why are there limits on theory of mind use?. A communication task cannot be generalized directly to every household request. This article distinguishes understanding a perspective from selecting a target on that basis, and considers the value of everyday clarification. The adult experiments cited here do not establish how these abilities change during childhood. In this series, the practical question is how people can make their understanding correctable as situations and communication demands change.

应用理解的难度。Apperly 及其同事研究了成年人在根据他人视角解释指令时所面临的困难。他们的实验为质疑“在行动中使用他人的信息是否总是自动的”提供了素材。（参见《为什么心智理论的使用存在局限性？》）。沟通任务不能直接推广到每一个家庭请求中。本文区分了“理解视角”与“在此基础上选择目标”，并探讨了日常澄清的价值。此处引用的成人实验并未确定这些能力在童年时期是如何变化的。在本系列中，实际问题在于人们如何随着情境和沟通需求的变化，使自己的理解变得可修正。

Too much clarification can also impose a burden Repeating every request at length can increase the other person's workload. Focus clarification on differences affecting the outcome. Ask about color when bowl color matters, or time when timing matters. Avoid repeatedly questioning information the person already made clear. "Understood" alone does not show what was understood. A brief statement such as "I'll pass the blue bowl" makes a mistake correctable. Clarification coordinates joint action rather than testing the speaker's words.

过多的澄清也会造成负担。冗长地重复每一个请求会增加对方的工作量。应将澄清重点放在影响结果的差异上。当碗的颜色很重要时询问颜色，当时间很重要时询问时间。避免反复询问对方已经明确的信息。仅说“明白了”并不能表明理解了什么。像“我会递给你那个蓝碗”这样简短的陈述，使得错误变得可修正。澄清是为了协调共同行动，而不是为了测试说话人的话语。

A design hypothesis for physical AI A possible design separates speech recognition, candidate meanings, execution targets, and permission to act. Correctly recognized words can still leave the target ambiguous. Defer contact with the target while clarifying, then update the planned target after correction. Recording a correction in memory while continuing an old movement is not recovery. Test whether conversation and physical commands use the same confirmation result. Evaluation could include similar objects, reformulation, cancellation, and correction after movement begins. Examine useful clarification and unnecessary clarification burden together. Correcting the target matters more than declaring that the person's mind was understood.

物理人工智能的设计假设。一种可能的设计是将语音识别、候选含义、执行目标和行动许可分离开来。即使正确识别了话语，目标仍可能存在歧义。在澄清时推迟与目标的接触，然后在修正后更新计划目标。在继续旧动作的同时将修正记录在内存中并非恢复。测试对话和物理指令是否使用相同的确认结果。评估可以包括相似物体、重新表述、取消以及在动作开始后的修正。应同时审视有用的澄清和不必要的澄清负担。修正目标比宣称“理解了对方的想法”更为重要。

A decision in four panels A blue bowl is requested. The other person starts reaching toward a red bowl. They pause and check the color. They hand over the blue bowl. This fictional comic depicts bringing understanding and execution into agreement rather than competing in fluency.

四格决策。有人请求一个蓝碗。另一个人开始伸手去拿红碗。他们停下来核实颜色。他们递过了蓝碗。这幅虚构的漫画描绘了如何使理解与执行达成一致，而不是在流利度上进行竞争。
