# Physicist Christopher Stubbs joins Anthropic's Long-Term Benefit Trust

Dr. Christopher Stubbs, an experimental physicist and former Dean of Science at Harvard University, has joined Anthropic’s Long-Term Benefit Trust. He brings to the Trust decades of scientific expertise across observational cosmology and precision measurement. He has also advised the US government on national security and arms control.

哈佛大学实验物理学家、前理学院院长克里斯托弗·斯塔布斯（Christopher Stubbs）博士已加入 Anthropic 公司的长期利益信托基金（Long-Term Benefit Trust）。他为该信托基金带来了在观测宇宙学和精密测量领域数十年的科学专业知识，并曾就国家安全和军备控制问题为美国政府提供咨询。

“I am honored to join Anthropic’s Long-Term Benefit Trust and to help the company deliver on its public benefit mission,” said Stubbs. “I use AI tools in my research every day and see what they make possible, and as an educator I have confronted some of the hard questions they raise. As a grandfather, I want to help ensure that AI is developed and deployed for the long-term benefit of humanity.”

“我很荣幸能加入 Anthropic 的长期利益信托基金，并帮助公司实现其公共利益使命，”斯塔布斯表示。“我在日常研究中都会使用人工智能工具，并见证了它们所带来的可能性；作为一名教育工作者，我也曾面对它们所引发的一些棘手问题。作为一名祖父，我希望确保人工智能的开发和部署能够造福人类的长期利益。”

Stubbs is the Samuel C. Moncher Professor of Physics and of Astronomy at Harvard, where he served as Dean of Science from 2018 to 2024. In 1998, as a member of the High-Z Supernova Search Team, he helped discover the accelerating expansion of the universe, work that later earned the team the Breakthrough Prize in Fundamental Physics. He was also the inaugural project scientist for what is now the Vera C. Rubin Observatory, which started a 10-year survey of the southern sky in June. One of its aims is to study the accelerating expansion he helped discover.

斯塔布斯是哈佛大学塞缪尔·C·蒙彻（Samuel C. Moncher）物理学与天文学教授，曾于 2018 年至 2024 年担任理学院院长。1998 年，作为“高红移超新星搜寻小组”（High-Z Supernova Search Team）的成员，他协助发现了宇宙的加速膨胀，这项工作后来使该团队获得了基础物理学突破奖。他还是现今薇拉·C·鲁宾天文台（Vera C. Rubin Observatory）的首任项目科学家，该天文台于 6 月启动了为期 10 年的南天巡天计划，其目标之一就是研究他曾协助发现的宇宙加速膨胀现象。

More recently, he’s written about how to keep AI-assisted research reproducible when models can give different answers to the same question. He’s a member of JASON, an independent group of scientists who have advised the US government on defense and technology since 1960.

最近，他撰写了关于在模型对同一问题给出不同答案时，如何保持人工智能辅助研究的可重复性的文章。他是 JASON 的成员，这是一个自 1960 年以来一直就国防和技术问题向美国政府提供咨询的独立科学家小组。

“When Chris found evidence that the universe was expanding at a rate no one expected, he and his team followed the data even when it went against the prevailing wisdom of the field. As AI starts to transform how science itself is done, Chris’s intellectual honesty, rigor, and commitment to the truth are the values we want shaping the future of this technology, and I’m grateful he’s bringing his deep expertise to the Trust,” said Daniela Amodei, Co-founder and President of Anthropic.

“当克里斯发现宇宙正以无人预料的速度膨胀的证据时，即使这违背了当时学界的主流观点，他和他的团队依然坚持追随数据。随着人工智能开始改变科学研究本身的方式，克里斯的学术诚实、严谨以及对真理的执着，正是我们希望塑造这项技术未来的价值观。我很感激他能将深厚的专业知识带到信托基金中，”Anthropic 联合创始人兼总裁丹妮拉·阿莫代（Daniela Amodei）说道。

Anthropic is a Public Benefit Corporation, which means we’re structured to balance commercial growth with our public benefit purpose. The Trust exists to help us hold that balance over the long term. It has the authority to appoint a majority of Anthropic’s board, and Trustees advise the board and our leadership on critical decisions, particularly those involving risks and societal impacts of AI.

Anthropic 是一家公共利益公司，这意味着我们的结构旨在平衡商业增长与公共利益目标。信托基金的存在是为了帮助我们长期保持这种平衡。它有权任命 Anthropic 董事会的大多数成员，受托人则就关键决策向董事会和我们的领导层提供建议，特别是涉及人工智能风险和社会影响的决策。

This week we published our first Public Benefit Report, a public account of how we’re working toward our charter and where we’re falling short. The Trustees helped define the five charter pillars it measures us against, and the report closes with their assessment of our progress to date. Subject to stockholder approval, we’ve also committed to setting aside a portion of Anthropic’s shares to Anthropic Public Benefit Fund, a new entity with a structure that increases the stake as the company grows.

本周，我们发布了首份《公共利益报告》，公开说明了我们如何履行章程以及在哪些方面尚有不足。受托人协助定义了衡量我们表现的五大章程支柱，报告最后附上了他们对我们迄今为止进展的评估。在获得股东批准的前提下，我们还承诺将 Anthropic 的部分股份拨入“Anthropic 公共利益基金”（Anthropic Public Benefit Fund），这是一个新的实体，其结构旨在随着公司的成长而增加持股比例。

“Helping the world build powerful AI safely and making sure it meaningfully improves people’s lives both depend on a deep understanding of science and on leading with integrity,” added Neil Buddy Shah, Chair of the Long-Term Benefit Trust. “Not only has Chris worked on some of the hardest problems in science, but he has consistently been a high-integrity leader of large, complex, collaborative scientific endeavors across academia, government, and the national laboratories. That will be of great value to the Trust as we support Anthropic in developing AI for the long-term benefit of humanity.”

“帮助世界安全地构建强大的人工智能，并确保它能切实改善人们的生活，这两者都依赖于对科学的深刻理解以及诚信的领导，”长期利益信托基金主席尼尔·巴迪·沙（Neil Buddy Shah）补充道。“克里斯不仅致力于解决科学界一些最棘手的问题，而且一直是一位高诚信的领导者，在学术界、政府和国家实验室领导过大型、复杂且协作性的科学项目。当我们支持 Anthropic 为人类的长期利益开发人工智能时，这将对信托基金具有巨大的价值。”

Stubbs, the Trust’s first physicist, joins Shah, national security expert Richard Fontaine, and former Federal Reserve Chair Ben Bernanke. Trustees are independent of the company’s management and investors.

作为信托基金的首位物理学家，斯塔布斯加入了沙、国家安全专家理查德·方丹（Richard Fontaine）以及前美联储主席本·伯南克（Ben Bernanke）的行列。受托人独立于公司的管理层和投资者。
