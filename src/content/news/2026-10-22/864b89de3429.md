Abstract:Automated theorem provers often rely on sampling language models, but proof assistants like Lean reject each failed step with a precise error message --- information that pure sampling discards. To overcome this, we introduce a bounded, online step-level repair module. Our search samples $K$ candidate tactics at each step and repairs a failed tactic at most $R$ times. Instead of discarding failed tactics, we feed the compiler's error message to a pretrained repair model to fix individual rejected steps in place.

摘要：自动定理证明器通常依赖于对语言模型进行采样，但像 Lean 这样的证明助手会通过精确的错误消息拒绝每一个失败的步骤——而纯采样会丢弃这些信息。为了克服这一点，我们引入了一个有界的、在线的步骤级修复模块。我们的搜索在每一步采样 $K$ 个候选策略，并对失败的策略最多修复 $R$ 次。我们没有丢弃失败的策略，而是将编译器的错误消息输入到一个预训练的修复模型中，以原地修复被拒绝的单个步骤。

This approach improves the solve rate over a sampling-only baseline across all tested candidate budgets ($K \in \{1,3,5,8\}$), achieving up to a $32\%$ relative gain and solving $82$ additional theorems on a $1{,}990$-theorem subset of the LeanDojo benchmark. Repair is not free: it trades extra runtime for reachable proofs. But the trade is predictable: repair pays most when sampling is constrained; its return is front-loaded, with the first repair attempt capturing $84\text{--}96\%$ of the total gain; and the gain comes almost entirely from shallow tactic-execution failures rather than structural errors such as type mismatches.

这种方法在所有测试的候选预算（$K \in \{1,3,5,8\}$）下，相比仅采样的基准提高了求解率，实现了高达 $32\%$ 的相对增益，并在 LeanDojo 基准测试的 $1{,}990$ 个定理子集中额外解决了 $82$ 个定理。修复并非没有代价：它以额外的运行时间换取可达到的证明。但这种权衡是可预测的：当采样受限时，修复的收益最大；其回报是前置的，第一次修复尝试就获得了总增益的 $84\text{--}96\% $；且增益几乎完全来自浅层的策略执行失败，而非类型不匹配等结构性错误。
