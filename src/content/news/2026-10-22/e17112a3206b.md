Google would certainly prefer that everyone used its Gemini models with Google Cloud services, but soon, you’ll have more options. OpenAI and Google have come to an arrangement that will see the former’s frontier models arrive in Google Cloud services. Perhaps more importantly, GPT models will soon appear in Antigravity, Google’s agent-powered coding tool.

谷歌当然更希望每个人都能在谷歌云服务中使用其 Gemini 模型，但很快，你将拥有更多选择。OpenAI 和谷歌已达成一项协议，将把前者的前沿模型引入谷歌云服务。更重要的是，GPT 模型很快将出现在谷歌的智能体编程工具 Antigravity 中。

For enterprise customers and AI enthusiasts, OpenAI’s latest model will appear in Google’s “Model Garden” in the coming weeks, adding to its 200-plus options. The garden already offers access to Google’s Gemini models, as well as an assortment of open models like Mistral, Llama, and Gemma. Anthropic’s Claude AI has also been available for some time. Google says it’s now the only large cloud provider offering access to all three leading closed LLMs.

对于企业客户和人工智能爱好者来说，OpenAI 的最新模型将在未来几周内出现在谷歌的“模型花园”（Model Garden）中，使其现有的 200 多种选项进一步增加。该花园已经提供了对谷歌 Gemini 模型以及 Mistral、Llama 和 Gemma 等各种开源模型的访问权限。Anthropic 的 Claude AI 也已上线一段时间。谷歌表示，它现在是唯一一家提供所有三种领先闭源大语言模型（LLM）访问权限的大型云服务提供商。

While OpenAI notes that Microsoft continues to be its primary cloud partner, the full suite of current GPT 6 and 6.1 models—Astra, Sol, and Luna—will launch on Google Cloud. Astra will naturally cost the most to run, but Google’s system supports dynamically moving workloads between LLMs based on performance, latency, and efficiency. OpenAI models will be available to run entirely on Google’s infrastructure, with transactions handled via the Google Cloud Marketplace.

虽然 OpenAI 指出微软仍是其主要的云合作伙伴，但目前全套 GPT 6 和 6.1 模型——Astra、Sol 和 Luna——都将在谷歌云上发布。Astra 的运行成本自然最高，但谷歌的系统支持根据性能、延迟和效率在不同大语言模型之间动态迁移工作负载。OpenAI 模型将能够完全在谷歌的基础设施上运行，交易则通过谷歌云市场（Google Cloud Marketplace）进行处理。

For businesses with established Google Cloud spending commitments, this will make it easy to begin using GPT models in place of whatever they were using before. Google says all three OpenAI LLMs are rolling out now in private preview for select enterprise customers. Anyone interested in getting early access to the latest GPT AI in Google Cloud can submit a request, which will enable access through the API even if they’re using Codex CLI or Codex Desktop. The inference just runs on Google’s systems.

对于那些已有谷歌云支出承诺的企业来说，这将使他们能够轻松地开始使用 GPT 模型来替代之前使用的任何模型。谷歌表示，所有三款 OpenAI 大语言模型目前正面向部分企业客户进行私有预览。任何有兴趣在谷歌云中抢先体验最新 GPT AI 的用户都可以提交申请，即使他们使用的是 Codex CLI 或 Codex Desktop，也可以通过 API 获得访问权限。推理过程完全在谷歌的系统上运行。

Bringing GPT models to Google Cloud could be a big deal for enterprise, but even individual users could see some benefit from the deal. Alongside the Cloud deployment, GPT models will appear in the Google Antigravity client. Until now, Antigravity has supported Google’s Gemini as well as Claude. Presumably, the same trio of OpenAI models will appear in Antigravity, but Google has not specified.

将 GPT 模型引入谷歌云对企业来说可能意义重大，但即使是个人用户也能从这项交易中获益。除了云端部署外，GPT 模型还将出现在谷歌的 Antigravity 客户端中。到目前为止，Antigravity 仅支持谷歌的 Gemini 以及 Claude。据推测，同样的这三款 OpenAI 模型也会出现在 Antigravity 中，但谷歌尚未明确说明。

Google recently rolled out Gemini 4 Argon across its AI footprint, bringing its flagship AI back to parity with the competition—at least if you ask Google. It does seem like an improvement over Gemini 3.1 Pro, but it burns through tokens at an astonishing rate. Anthropic’s Claude models are already supported in Google Cloud, but the OpenAI deal will offer another alternative to Gemini for those not feeling the Argon love.

谷歌最近在其人工智能版图中全面推出了 Gemini 4 Argon，使其旗舰级人工智能重新与竞争对手处于同等水平——至少在谷歌看来是这样。它看起来确实比 Gemini 3.1 Pro 有所改进，但其消耗 Token 的速度惊人。Anthropic 的 Claude 模型已经在谷歌云中得到支持，但对于那些对 Argon 不太感冒的用户来说，OpenAI 的这笔交易将为 Gemini 提供另一种选择。
