# The Best Art TV Deals: Samsung Frame, Hisense Canvas, and More (2026)

We're big fans of art TVs here at WIRED. They're perfect for people who don't want a TV to be the constant center of attention in their living room. No art TV maker is better than the pioneer: Samsung. Specifically, Samsung's The Frame Pro series of TVs. The 2026 models (LS03HW) add tons of new features, including more intuitive software, a higher refresh rate for gaming, and even better picture quality.

在 WIRED，我们非常推崇艺术电视。对于那些不希望电视成为客厅焦点的人来说，它们是完美的选择。在艺术电视制造商中，没有谁比先驱三星做得更好，尤其是三星的 The Frame Pro 系列电视。2026 款机型（LS03HW）增加了大量新功能，包括更直观的软件、更高的游戏刷新率以及更出色的画质。

The problem with The Frame Pro has always been the price. The smallest 55-inch model dropped to $1,098 during Prime Day earlier this month, and I've been happy to see that the price has stayed around. (In fact, earlier this week, it dropped by an additional $100 to its lowest price ever.) Seeing it discounted to under $1,000 was fantastic, but even at $1,098, it's a really solid deal. It was selling for $200 more in September and for the full retail price of $1,498 in August. Beyond the smallest 55-inch model, you also have size options for The Frame Pro of a 65-inch, 75-inch, or 85-inch TV.

The Frame Pro 的问题一直在于价格。本月初的 Prime Day 期间，最小的 55 英寸型号降至 1,098 美元，我很高兴看到价格一直维持在这个水平。（事实上，本周早些时候，它又额外降价 100 美元，达到了历史最低点。）看到它降至 1,000 美元以下非常棒，但即使是 1,098 美元，也是一笔非常划算的交易。它在 9 月份的售价比现在高出 200 美元，而在 8 月份则是 1,498 美元的完整零售价。除了最小的 55 英寸型号外，The Frame Pro 还提供 65 英寸、75 英寸或 85 英寸的尺寸选择。

Samsung also has an entry-level version of The Frame, which has an even deeper discount right now. It's just $898, which is $100 off its Prime Day price—the cheapest it's even been! It's actually the same price as the older 2025 model. We haven't tested this model yet ourselves, but the main difference between The Frame and The Frame Pro is the brightness and image quality. They both use a matte finish, but The Frame Pro has nearly double the max brightness, while the standard Frame only goes up to around 650 nits. The Frame Pro also comes with a wireless connect box for a cleaner and simpler setup. It's definitely the one to get if you can swing the extra $200.

三星还有一款入门级的 The Frame，目前折扣力度更大。它仅售 898 美元，比 Prime Day 的价格又便宜了 100 美元，是有史以来的最低价！这实际上与旧款 2025 型号的价格相同。我们尚未亲自测试该型号，但 The Frame 和 The Frame Pro 之间的主要区别在于亮度和图像质量。它们都采用哑光饰面，但 The Frame Pro 的最大亮度几乎是标准版 The Frame 的两倍，后者仅能达到约 650 尼特。The Frame Pro 还配备了一个无线连接盒，使安装更整洁、更简单。如果你能多花 200 美元，它绝对是首选。

As we have documented, there's been an explosion of interest in art TVs in 2026, with competitors like Amazon, Hisense, and TCL joining the game. We've tested many of these new options—many of which try to undercut The Frame in price. The one on sale right now that I'd point to is the Hisense S7 CanvasTV (55S7SG). It's not even as bright as the standard Frame, but at $750 (for the 55-inch size), it's the cheapest art TV you can buy right now.

正如我们所记录的那样，2026 年人们对艺术电视的兴趣激增，亚马逊、海信和 TCL 等竞争对手也纷纷加入竞争。我们测试了许多这些新选择，其中许多试图在价格上击败 The Frame。目前正在促销且值得推荐的一款是海信 S7 CanvasTV (55S7SG)。它的亮度甚至不如标准版 The Frame，但 750 美元（55 英寸）的价格使其成为目前你能买到的最便宜的艺术电视。

With Black Friday coming up next month, it's certainly possible that prices on these art TVs will drop even more. The 2025 Samsung Frame and Frame Pro both dropped to their lowest prices during Black Friday last year. It's a gamble—you could wait for Black Friday or get a sure thing right now.

随着下个月黑色星期五的临近，这些艺术电视的价格很有可能会进一步下降。2025 款三星 Frame 和 Frame Pro 在去年的黑色星期五期间都降到了最低价。这是一种赌博——你可以选择等待黑色星期五，或者现在就入手一个确定的优惠。
