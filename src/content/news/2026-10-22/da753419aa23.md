YouTube says it isn’t testing hiding the dislike button.

YouTube 表示并未测试隐藏“不喜欢”按钮。

YouTube creators, on their own videos, will have the dislike button moved behind the three-dot menu, but that’s not the case for regular users.

YouTube 创作者在其个人视频页面上的“不喜欢”按钮将被移至三点菜单后方，但普通用户的情况并非如此。

On Tuesday, I published a story about how YouTube appeared to be testing hiding the dislike button on videos, with at least two users on X sharing screenshots of the dislike button moved into the three-dot menu.

周二，我发表了一篇关于 YouTube 似乎正在测试隐藏视频“不喜欢”按钮的报道，当时至少有两名 X 用户分享了截图，显示“不喜欢”按钮被移到了三点菜单中。

But a YouTube spokesperson, in a Wednesday statement to The Verge, confirmed that YouTube is “not testing hiding the dislike button on YouTube for users.” However, YouTube is introducing a new UI for creators on their uploaded videos where the dislike button will be moved.

但一位 YouTube 发言人在周三给 The Verge 的声明中证实，YouTube“并未在用户端测试隐藏‘不喜欢’按钮”。不过，YouTube 正在为创作者的上传视频引入一种新的用户界面，其中“不喜欢”按钮的位置将会发生变动。

Here’s the full statement, from spokesperson Kimberly Taylor: We are not testing hiding the dislike button on YouTube for users. We’re introducing a new UI for creators on their own uploaded videos, where dislike is behind the three-dot menu, and as always, they can find their full dislike counts in Studio, alongside other analytics.

以下是发言人 Kimberly Taylor 提供的完整声明：我们并未在用户端测试隐藏 YouTube 上的“不喜欢”按钮。我们正在为创作者的个人上传视频引入一种新的用户界面，将“不喜欢”按钮置于三点菜单后方；与往常一样，他们可以在 Studio 中查看完整的“不喜欢”计数以及其他分析数据。

The change for creators follows past adjustments to the dislike button on the platform. YouTube hid the public dislike count on videos starting in 2021 as a way to prevent harassment, and it removed the dislike button video from Shorts earlier this year.

针对创作者的这一变动，是该平台此前对“不喜欢”按钮进行调整的延续。YouTube 从 2021 年开始隐藏视频的公开“不喜欢”计数，以此作为防止骚扰的一种手段，并于今年早些时候移除了 Shorts 视频中的“不喜欢”按钮。
