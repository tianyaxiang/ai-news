Tesla has set a Q2 2027 target to let owners opt their cars into its robotaxi network—a major promise, but one that comes with a history of missed deadlines and steep regulatory hurdles.

特斯拉已设定 2027 年第二季度的目标，允许车主将其车辆加入其自动驾驶出租车（Robotaxi）网络。这是一个重大的承诺，但考虑到该公司过去屡次错过截止日期并面临严峻的监管障碍，这一目标仍存变数。

For years, Elon Musk has been making big promises to Tesla owners about unsupervised autonomous driving and the ability to add their own vehicles to the company’s robotaxi fleet. During today’s third-quarter earnings call, the company finally gave itself a deadline.

多年来，埃隆·马斯克一直向特斯拉车主承诺，将实现无人监督的自动驾驶，并允许车主将自己的车辆加入公司的自动驾驶出租车车队。在今天的第三季度财报电话会议上，该公司终于为这一目标设定了截止日期。

According to Tesla VP for Autonomous Driving Ashok Elluswamy, Tesla owners will be able to add their own vehicles to the company’s robotaxi fleet in the second quarter of 2027.

据特斯拉自动驾驶副总裁阿肖克·埃卢斯瓦米（Ashok Elluswamy）称，特斯拉车主将能够在 2027 年第二季度将自己的车辆加入公司的自动驾驶出租车车队。

It’s important to mark this deadline because it’s likely that Tesla will sail right past it. When it comes to autonomous driving and robotaxis, Musk has a pretty clear pattern: he sets bold, short-term deadlines that end up slipping by years — or never happening at all.

记录这个截止日期很重要，因为特斯拉很可能会错过它。在自动驾驶和自动驾驶出租车方面，马斯克有一个非常明显的模式：他设定大胆的短期截止日期，结果往往会推迟数年，甚至最终不了了之。

In 2019, Musk stood on stage at the company’s first AI Day and vowed that customer-owned vehicles could be used as part of a “Tesla Network” of robotaxi, earning their owners passive income while driving around autonomously, looking for fares.

2019 年，马斯克在公司首届“人工智能日”（AI Day）的舞台上承诺，客户拥有的车辆可以作为“特斯拉网络”自动驾驶出租车的一部分，在自动驾驶寻找乘客的同时为车主赚取被动收入。

But even if Tesla opens up its robotaxi fleet to customer-owned vehicles, major regulatory hurdles remain. Unsupervised autonomous ride-hailing requires specific local and state regulatory permits. Insurance may be difficult to obtain.

即便特斯拉向客户拥有的车辆开放其自动驾驶出租车车队，仍存在重大的监管障碍。无人监督的自动驾驶网约车服务需要特定的地方和州监管许可，且保险可能难以获得。

Tesla might be confident it can clear every technical and legal hurdle by Q2 2027. But history says this could easily become just another deadline that quietly slips by.

特斯拉或许有信心在 2027 年第二季度前扫清所有技术和法律障碍，但历史经验表明，这很可能只是另一个悄然错过的截止日期。
