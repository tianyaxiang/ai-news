Is Ruggable Worth It? While we stan Ruggable, it’s not without its shortcomings. WIRED editor Kat Merck has the Jonathan Adler Venom Flatwoven Rug in her kitchen; the edges curl up, and it drives her nuts. I will say my Ruggable’s nonslip pad has betrayed me more than once. It shifts around the kitchen and is the occasional tripping hazard. Aligning a rug onto the pad is also a two-person job, but it’s not impossible if you live alone. Customers online have also reported issues with prints fading over time, but that’s practically a given with high-traffic, machine-washable rugs.

Ruggable 值得吗？虽然我们支持 Ruggable，但它并非没有缺点。WIRED 编辑 Kat Merck 在她的厨房里铺了 Jonathan Adler Venom 平织地毯；边缘卷曲起来，让她抓狂。我不得不说，我的 Ruggable 防滑垫不止一次背叛了我。它在厨房里移位，偶尔会造成绊倒的危险。将地毯对准垫子也是一项需要两个人完成的工作，但如果你一个人住，也不是不可能。在线客户也报告了印花随时间褪色的问题，但这对于高人流量、可机洗的地毯来说几乎是必然的。
