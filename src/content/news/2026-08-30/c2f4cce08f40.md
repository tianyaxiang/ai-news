### 🎉 OpenMAIC v1.0.0 — 用智能体构建课程

One prompt in, a whole course out — and now you can steer. Released August 27, 2026, OpenMAIC v1.0.0 adds a Pro workbench alongside the classic one-click generator: chat with an agent that plans your curriculum, builds and revises every page, and works straight from your materials.
输入一个提示词，即可输出完整课程——现在你还可以进行干预。OpenMAIC v1.0.0 于 2026 年 8 月 27 日发布，在经典的“一键生成”功能之外新增了专业工作台：你可以与智能体对话，让它规划课程大纲、构建并修改每一页内容，并直接基于你提供的资料进行工作。

*   **🤖 Agent workbench** — a chat-first workspace that plans, builds, and revises whole courses
*   **🤖 智能体工作台** — 以对话为核心的工作空间，用于规划、构建和修改完整课程

*   **📎 会话资料** — 上传文档、音频和视频，或从网络搜索中获取内容；智能体将基于这些资料进行构建
*   **🧰 Course tools + 20 built-in skills** — slides, quizzes, interactives, PBL, images, video, voices, .pptx import
*   **🧰 课程工具 + 20 种内置技能** — 幻灯片、测验、交互式内容、PBL（项目式学习）、图像、视频、语音、.pptx 导入
*   **🔌 Neutral by design** — bring your own models, media, search providers, and storage backend
*   **🔌 中立设计** — 支持自带模型、媒体、搜索提供商和存储后端

### 📖 Overview
### 📖 项目概览

OpenMAIC (Open Multi-Agent Interactive Classroom) is an open-source AI platform that turns any topic or document into a rich, interactive classroom experience. Powered by multi-agent orchestration, it generates slides, quizzes, interactive simulations, and project-based learning activities — all delivered by AI teachers and AI classmates who can speak, draw on a whiteboard, and engage in real-time discussions with you. With built-in OpenClaw integration, you can generate classrooms directly from messaging apps like Feishu, Slack, or Telegram.
OpenMAIC (开放式多智能体交互课堂) 是一个开源 AI 平台，可将任何主题或文档转化为丰富、交互式的课堂体验。在多智能体编排技术的驱动下，它能生成幻灯片、测验、交互式模拟和项目式学习活动，并由 AI 教师和 AI 同学为你授课。它们能够说话、在白板上绘图，并与你进行实时讨论。通过内置的 OpenClaw 集成，你可以直接从飞书、Slack 或 Telegram 等通讯软件中生成课堂。

### 🚀 Quick Start
### 🚀 快速开始

**Prerequisites**
**前置要求**
*   Node.js >= 20
*   pnpm >= 10

**1. Clone & Install**
**1. 克隆与安装**
```bash
git clone https://github.com/THU-MAIC/OpenMAIC.git
cd OpenMAIC
pnpm install
```

**2. Configure**
**2. 配置**
```bash
cp .env.example .env.local
```
Fill in at least one LLM provider key:
请至少填写一个大模型提供商的 API Key：
```text
OPENAI_API_KEY=sk-...
AZURE_OPENAI_API_KEY=...
AZURE_OPENAI_BASE_URL=https://YOUR-RESOURCE.op
```
