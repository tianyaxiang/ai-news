# Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro Review: Master of Phone Calls
# Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro 评测：通话之王

The Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro did something I wasn't expecting: It made me call my family more.
Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro 做了一件我没想到的事：它让我更愿意给家人打电话了。

Here's my problem: I have aging parents who are losing their hearing, and I like to mumble. That's a bad combination for catching up over the phone during my New York City commute. I almost always have to talk loudly and repeat myself way more often than I would like. It makes the whole conversation feel unnatural, so much so that a little part of me dreads making that phone call.
我的问题是这样的：我的父母年事已高，听力正在下降，而我说话又喜欢嘟囔。这对于我在纽约通勤途中通过电话与他们叙旧来说，简直是糟糕的组合。我几乎总是不得不大声说话，并且比我预想的次数多得多地重复自己说的话。这让整个对话感觉很不自然，以至于我内心深处甚至有点害怕打这通电话。

I've tested over 50 pairs of wireless earbuds in my career as a headphones expert, and none of them have totally solved this problem. That is, until I started reviewing the Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro, with its Guinness World Records claim of “highest speech quality score.” And what sounded like pure marketing at first turned into something else—earbuds that are probably my new daily carry.
作为一名耳机专家，我在职业生涯中测试过 50 多款无线耳机，但没有一款能彻底解决这个问题。直到我开始评测 Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro，它拥有吉尼斯世界纪录认证的“最高语音质量评分”。起初听起来像是纯粹的营销噱头，但后来却变成了现实——这副耳机很可能成为我新的日常必备品。

### Put a Screen on It
### 给它装个屏幕

The Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro has a new design from its predecessor. The buds have a larger, flat pill shape (similar to the second-gen Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds) and are bigger and heavier than stem-shaped buds like Apple AirPods Pro 3. While they are a little big, they are very comfortable—I wore them for multiple hours without any irritation or fatigue. In the box, there are multiple alternative ear tips and wing-tip sizes to find your ideal fit.
Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro 采用了与前代产品不同的新设计。耳机主体呈较大的扁平药丸状（类似于第二代 Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds），比 Apple AirPods Pro 3 等杆状耳机更大、更重。虽然它们有点大，但佩戴起来非常舒适——我连续佩戴了几个小时，没有任何不适或疲劳感。包装盒内提供了多种备用耳塞和耳翼尺寸，以帮助你找到最适合自己的佩戴方式。

The pill-shaped surface is touch-sensitive, so you'll be doing a lot of tapping, swiping, and holding to control things like media, volume, and noise-canceling modes.
药丸状的表面支持触控，因此你需要通过大量的点击、滑动和长按来控制媒体播放、音量和降噪模式等功能。

The charging case has a touchscreen on the front that shows the buds' and case's battery levels, but if you swipe around, you can control settings for active noise cancellation (ANC), sound profiles, Dolby Audio, and more. The touchscreen is much larger and more useful than the display on the previous Liberty 4 Pro, but not as attention-grabbing as the giant screen that covers the entire case for the Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro Max. It's a nice addition, though I usually prefer to adjust settings using the on-ear controls or Soundcore app.
充电盒正面配有一块触摸屏，可以显示耳机和充电盒的电量。通过滑动屏幕，你还可以控制主动降噪 (ANC)、声音配置文件、杜比音效等设置。这块触摸屏比上一代 Liberty 4 Pro 的显示屏更大、更实用，虽然不像 Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro Max 那种覆盖整个充电盒的巨型屏幕那样引人注目，但它确实是一个不错的补充。不过，我通常还是更喜欢通过耳机上的触控或 Soundcore App 来调整设置。

Inside the earbuds, there's an even bigger change that makes them stand out from Soundcore's previous headphones: the Anker Thus AI chip (Anker is Soundcore's parent company), which uses a combined CPU and memory design (compute-in-memory) for vastly increased efficiency. I'm not going to get too in the weeds, but this chip is a big investment from the brand compared to using an off-the-shelf option (like many of its rivals do). For the layman, the actual result is that these earbuds are excellent at processing your voice and outside noise. I expect to see it featured in many future Soundcore products.
耳机内部有一个更大的变化，使其从 Soundcore 之前的耳机中脱颖而出：Anker Thus AI 芯片（Anker 是 Soundcore 的母公司）。该芯片采用了 CPU 与内存结合的设计（存内计算），极大地提高了效率。我不会深入探讨技术细节，但与使用现成方案（许多竞争对手的做法）相比，该品牌在这一芯片上投入巨大。对于普通用户来说，实际结果就是这些耳机在处理人声和外部噪音方面表现极其出色。我预计未来许多 Soundcore 产品都会采用这一技术。

### King of Calls
### 通话之王

New York City is one of the best places to test noise-canceling and microphone quality in the real world. You have easy access to busy roads, bustling cafes, highway overpasses, and loud subway platforms, all within one commute.
纽约市是测试现实世界中降噪和麦克风质量的最佳场所之一。在一次通勤中，你就能轻松接触到繁忙的道路、喧闹的咖啡馆、高速公路立交桥和嘈杂的地铁站台。

In all of those environments, the Liberty 5 Pro sounded astonishingly good on a call. My voice was always loud and clear, and all background noise essentially vanished. Fire truck sirens, a busy highway overpass, a crowd of NYC commuters, a loud cafe—none of it came through in any of my calls or audio recordings using the Liberty 5 Pro. “You don't hear that truck?” was a common refrain during all my calls. Apple AirPods Pro 3, Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds (2nd Gen), and Shokz OpenDots 2 sounded bad by comparison.
在所有这些环境中，Liberty 5 Pro 的通话效果都好得惊人。我的声音始终清晰响亮，所有的背景噪音几乎完全消失了。消防车的警笛声、繁忙的高速公路立交桥、纽约通勤的人群、嘈杂的咖啡馆——在使用 Liberty 5 Pro 进行通话或录音时，这些声音都没有被收录进去。“你没听到那辆卡车的声音吗？”是我所有通话中常被问到的一句话。相比之下，Apple AirPods Pro 3、Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds（第二代）和 Shokz OpenDots 2 的表现则逊色不少。

Noise canceling is also top-tier. The buds have the standard noise cancellation, transparency, adaptive, and off modes, and you can fine-tune ANC strength (from one to five) using the case or Soundcore app. The only earbuds that noticeably canceled more noise were the Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds (2nd Gen) and Sony WF-1000XM6.
降噪功能也处于顶级水平。耳机具备标准的降噪、通透、自适应和关闭模式，你还可以通过充电盒或 Soundcore App 微调降噪强度（从 1 到 5 级）。唯一在降噪效果上明显优于它的只有 Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds（第二代）和 Sony WF-1000XM6。

Out of the box, the sound profile (called Soundcore Signature) isn't an immediate slam dunk like call quality and ANC are. It's bass-heavy, so much so that I would bet even an untrained ear would notice the bass boost, especially compared to other earbuds from Apple or Bose. There are five preset sound profiles (I preferred Soundcore Balance), an eight-band custom equalizer to play with, and a personalized HearID mode, which tunes the profile to your preferences based on an audio quiz where you select which clip you prefer.
开箱即用的声音配置文件（称为 Soundcore Signature）并不像通话质量和降噪那样让人一听就惊艳。它的低音非常重，我敢打赌，即使是未经训练的耳朵也能注意到这种低音增强，尤其是与苹果或 Bose 的其他耳机相比时。它提供了五种预设声音配置文件（我更喜欢 Soundcore Balance）、一个八段式自定义均衡器，以及个性化的 HearID 模式——该模式通过音频测试让你选择自己喜欢的片段，从而根据你的偏好调整声音配置。

Using the Soundcore Balance or custom HearID profile, the Liberty 5 Pro sounds great. Again, it's not quite as detailed as AirPods Pro 3 or QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds, but it's close enough to be enjoyable for most people, especially given the significantly lower price.
使用 Soundcore Balance 或自定义 HearID 配置文件后，Liberty 5 Pro 的音质表现非常出色。虽然它的细节表现不如 AirPods Pro 3 或 QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds，但已经足够接近，能让大多数人感到满意，考虑到它显著更低的价格，这非常难得。

### 日常多功能性

很少有新耳机能成为我的首选。苹果生态系统的便利性通常意味着 AirPods Pro 3 不得不成为我的首选耳机。但我现在依然在使用它们……
