# Distraction-free writing gadget BYOK is adding custom extensions
# 专注写作设备 BYOK 即将支持自定义扩展功能

The company’s solution to adding features without bloat is an optional scripting system.
该公司在不增加臃肿功能的前提下，给出的解决方案是一套可选的脚本系统。

BYOK is a no-frills, single-purpose gadget that 90 percent of people will find absolutely frivolous. But for those who spend a significant chunk of their lives putting words to virtual paper, there is an obvious allure. Personally, I think it’s at its best when you just stick to the bare minimum, focusing on pure text drafting. Certain users, though, need more. They want BYOK to take notes, they want to use it for writing screenplays, or maybe they want to organize a sprawling novel.
BYOK 是一款极简的单功能设备，90% 的人可能会觉得它完全是多此一举。但对于那些生活中大部分时间都在进行文字创作的人来说，它有着显而易见的吸引力。就我个人而言，我认为它在保持极简、专注于纯文本草稿时表现最佳。然而，部分用户有更多需求。他们希望 BYOK 能用来记笔记、写剧本，或者整理长篇小说。

Balancing the wants and needs of these more demanding users has been a challenge that BYOK founder Nick Sjolinder has been trying to figure out how to tackle. The answer he seems to have landed on, is scripts. Rather than clutter BYOK with a ton of features that 80 percent of its users won’t touch, or crowd the Studio app with even more formats, the company is planning to implement optional scripts that expand the capabilities of BYOK.
如何平衡这些高需求用户的愿望与实际需求，一直是 BYOK 创始人 Nick Sjolinder 试图解决的难题。他最终给出的答案似乎是：脚本。公司并不打算在 BYOK 中堆砌 80% 用户都不会用到的功能，也不想在 Studio 应用中塞入过多的格式，而是计划通过引入可选脚本来扩展 BYOK 的功能。

A few examples of the kinds of thing people will be able to create are a screenplay assistant, a character note tool, and a writing analyzer. This could let users highlight text in a document and pull it out as a note or tell them when they’re using the passive voice. It’s still early in development and the scripting features haven’t rolled out publicly, but BYOK is asking for feedback from its users.
用户未来能够创建的功能示例包括：剧本助手、角色笔记工具以及写作分析器。这些功能可以让用户高亮文档中的文本并将其提取为笔记，或者在用户使用被动语态时给出提示。目前该功能仍处于开发早期，尚未公开发布，但 BYOK 正在积极征求用户的反馈意见。
