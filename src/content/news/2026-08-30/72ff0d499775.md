# Alt-right troll Milo Yiannopoulos has been deported
# 另类右翼网络喷子米洛·扬诺普洛斯已被驱逐出境

The controversial Gamergate figure has been sent back to the UK after overstaying his visa.
这位备受争议的“玩家门”（Gamergate）事件人物因签证逾期滞留，已被遣返回英国。

Alt-right troll Milo Yiannopoulos was arrested by ICE on Thursday, and today the Department of Homeland Security confirmed to Reuters and the Washington Post that he had been deported to the UK. Yiannopoulos came to the US legally in 2019, but overstayed his visa. After he failed to appear in court for an immigration hearing, a final order of removal was issued on July 22.
另类右翼网络喷子米洛·扬诺普洛斯（Milo Yiannopoulos）于周四被美国移民及海关执法局（ICE）逮捕。今日，美国国土安全部向路透社和《华盛顿邮报》证实，他已被驱逐出境并遣返回英国。扬诺普洛斯于2019年合法进入美国，但随后签证逾期。由于他未按时出席移民听证会，当局已于7月22日对他下达了最终驱逐令。

The British national made a name for himself as an editor at Steve Bannon’s Breitbart and an early supporter of Donald Trump’s. He has had a long history of controversial statements and associations, including reportedly helping arrange Trump’s dinner with Kanye West and white nationalist Nick Fuentes, and he was a key figure in the online harassment campaign Gamergate.
这位英国公民曾担任史蒂夫·班农（Steve Bannon）旗下布赖特巴特新闻网（Breitbart）的编辑，并作为唐纳德·特朗普的早期支持者而声名鹊起。他长期以来因发表争议言论和参与争议事件而闻名，据报道，他曾协助安排特朗普与坎耶·韦斯特（Kanye West）及白人民族主义者尼克·富恩特斯（Nick Fuentes）的晚宴，他也是“玩家门”网络骚扰运动中的关键人物。

In recent years, however, his antics have led to a falling out with most significant figures on the right, including MAGA influencer Laura Loomer. He has denied condoning statutory rape or pedophilia, but he has suggested it’s acceptable for an adult to have a sexual relationship with a 13-year-old.
然而近年来，他的种种怪异行径导致他与右翼阵营中的大多数重要人物决裂，其中包括“让美国再次伟大”（MAGA）的网红劳拉·卢默（Laura Loomer）。他曾否认纵容法定强奸或恋童癖，但他曾暗示成年人与13岁未成年人发生性关系是可以接受的。

There’s no word if the 44 interns in a trenchcoat who also constitute the entity known as Milo Yiannopoulos have also been deported.
目前尚不清楚那个由“44个穿着风衣的实习生”所组成的、被称为米洛·扬诺普洛斯的实体是否也已被一并驱逐出境。
