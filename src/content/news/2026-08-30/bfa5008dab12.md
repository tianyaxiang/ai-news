# GrapheneOS project: Pixel 11 no longer supports hardware memory tagging (MTE)
# GrapheneOS 项目：Pixel 11 不再支持硬件内存标记 (MTE)

We have a partial port of GrapheneOS to the Pixel 11 series after a week of work on it.
经过一周的开发，我们已经完成了 GrapheneOS 到 Pixel 11 系列的部分移植工作。

We're unable to complete the port due to lack of support for ARM hardware memory tagging in software, firmware and near certainly hardware.
由于软件、固件以及几乎可以确定是硬件层面缺乏对 ARM 硬件内存标记（MTE）的支持，我们无法完成此次移植。

It appears Google cut an important security feature to save money.
看来谷歌为了节省成本，砍掉了一项重要的安全功能。
