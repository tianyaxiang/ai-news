# RAG Is Not the Whole Toolkit: The NLP Techniques Real Problems Still Need
# RAG 并非万能工具箱：现实问题依然需要的 NLP 技术

A support request lands in the queue, and something has to work out what it is about before anyone can answer it. The hand goes to the prompt. That usually works, which is exactly why it became the reflex. It is also the slowest way to do it, the most expensive, and the one that can least explain itself afterwards.
当一条支持请求进入队列时，在有人回答之前，必须先弄清楚它是什么内容。人们习惯性地求助于提示词（Prompt）。这通常有效，也正是它成为条件反射的原因。但这也是处理该问题最慢、最昂贵，且事后最难以解释其逻辑的方法。

Six cheaper methods sit under that reflex. An exact match, when the request already carries a clean identifier. A spelling fix, when one wrong letter is all that stands between the question and the answer. A keyword search over a vocabulary an expert wrote. Embeddings, for the wording that vocabulary does not cover. Most requests are settled by one of these, in milliseconds, and each one can name the rule it fired on.
在这种条件反射之下，还有六种更廉价的方法。当请求已经带有清晰的标识符时，可以使用精确匹配；当仅仅因为一个字母错误而导致无法回答时，可以使用拼写修正；针对专家编写的词汇表进行关键词搜索；对于词汇表未涵盖的措辞，则使用嵌入（Embeddings）。大多数请求都能在几毫秒内通过这些方法解决，且每种方法都能明确指出其触发的规则。

The reflex jumps to rung 6; six cheaper rungs answer most requests and can show their work. - Image by author
Knowing the whole ladder, and reaching for the lowest rung that solves the case, is the engineering. These articles walk that ladder on real document problems: classifying a request, matching free text to a reference list, reading a table, absorbing OCR noise, and running the model on your own machine when the corpus cannot leave it.
条件反射直接跳到了第 6 级；而六个更低级的阶梯就能解决大多数请求，并且可以展示其工作过程。——图片由作者提供
了解整个阶梯，并选择能解决问题的最低一级，这就是工程学。这些文章通过真实的文档问题来演示这个阶梯：分类请求、将自由文本与参考列表匹配、读取表格、吸收 OCR 噪声，以及在语料库无法离开本地时在自己的机器上运行模型。

1. Why a bonus series at all
1. 为什么需要一个番外系列？

A working RAG pipeline produces real engineering questions the main spine cannot host without losing its line of argument. Four patterns recurred during the work on Volume 1, each of them surfacing a piece of work that did not fit into a single article of the spine. The bonus tier is what made each one land properly instead of decaying into a Twitter thread or a comment on a vendor blog.
一个可运行的 RAG 流水线会产生真实的工程问题，而主线文章若强行容纳这些问题，就会失去论证的连贯性。在第一卷的编写过程中，出现了四种反复出现的模式，每一项工作都无法塞进主线的单篇文章中。番外篇（Bonus tier）的作用就是让这些内容各得其所，而不是沦为 Twitter 上的讨论串或供应商博客下的评论。

Some concerns cut across several bricks. Spelling and OCR noise touch parsing (the corpus is noisy), question parsing (the user’s wording is noisy), and retrieval (the matching has to absorb both). Justifying “I don’t know” asks each of the four bricks for one piece of evidence. Tables in PDFs need a representation choice in parsing, a retrieval rule, and a generation contract. The discipline of writing faithful mocks touches every brick that has a contract worth testing. A single main-spine article on any of these would have either pulled too much from one brick or broken the spine’s rhythm. The bonus article carries the cross-cutting view in one place, so the reader can see all the bricks line up under one concern.
有些问题跨越了多个模块。拼写和 OCR 噪声涉及解析（语料库本身有噪声）、问题解析（用户的措辞有噪声）以及检索（匹配过程必须同时处理这两者）。证明“我不知道”需要从四个模块中各提取一条证据。PDF 中的表格需要在解析时选择表示方式、制定检索规则以及生成契约。编写忠实模拟数据的规范触及了每一个值得测试契约的模块。如果针对其中任何一个问题写一篇主线文章，要么会从某个模块中提取过多内容，要么会破坏主线的节奏。番外篇将这种跨模块的视角集中在一处，让读者能看到所有模块如何针对同一个问题协同工作。

Some pipelines have a different shape from the start. The series defends one shape: read a corpus the team did not author, with adversarial structure, with an expert in the loop. A few real cases invert that. The FAQ corpus is one: the team designs the corpus, so parsing is trivial, retrieval doubles as a cache, and few-shot prompting becomes a retrieval problem. The dispatched architecture is another, an editorial choice (named routes over autonomous agents) that the spine references but does not stop to defend on its own. Both deserve a piece that walks the alternative shape fully, and that piece is the bonus. The reader gets the contrast explicit, not implied.
有些流水线从一开始就具有不同的形态。本系列主要探讨一种形态：读取非团队编写的、具有对抗性结构的语料库，并有专家参与。但一些真实案例恰恰相反。FAQ 语料库就是一例：团队设计了语料库，因此解析变得微不足道，检索兼作缓存，少样本提示（few-shot prompting）变成了检索问题。分发式架构是另一种，这是一种编辑选择（使用命名路由而非自主智能体），主线文章提到了它，但没有专门论证。这两者都值得用专门的篇幅来完整剖析，而番外篇正是为此而生。读者可以获得明确的对比，而非隐晦的暗示。

Some work is a reproducible benchmark. Picking an LLM for a RAG pipeline is not a position to argue, it is an experiment to run. Picking a CV parser is the same. Bonus articles run the same pipeline on a fixed set of inputs while varying one dimension (the model, the parser, the embedder) and report the numbers per question type. The methodology bonus articles add is exactly the discipline the main spine practises: vary one thing, measure on real questions, report per failure mode. The numbers age with the model lineup, the discipline does not.
有些工作是可复现的基准测试。为 RAG 流水线选择 LLM 不是一个需要争论的立场，而是一个需要运行的实验。选择简历解析器也是如此。番外篇在固定的输入集上运行相同的流水线，同时改变一个维度（模型、解析器、嵌入器），并按问题类型报告数据。番外篇所增加的方法论正是主线文章所践行的准则：改变一个变量，在真实问题上进行测量，并按故障模式进行报告。数据会随着模型阵容的更新而过时，但这种准则不会。

A small local-LLM stack. Three articles run the cascade’s expensive stages against self-hosted Ollama models when the cloud is rate-limited, behind a VNet, or off-limits for the corpus. The numbers come from real runs against a single GPU box on the author’s desk, not against a vendor’s white paper. Self-hosting is one of the rare topics where the cost of not publishing concrete numbers is a reader making the wrong call; the local-LLM stack closes that gap.
一个小型本地 LLM 栈。当云端受到速率限制、位于 VNet 之后或语料库无法上传时，有三篇文章运行了级联中昂贵的阶段，并使用自托管的 Ollama 模型。这些数据来自作者桌面上单 GPU 机器的真实运行结果，而非供应商的白皮书。自托管是少数几个如果不发布具体数据就会导致读者做出错误决策的话题之一；本地 LLM 栈填补了这一空白。

The criterion is consistent across all of them: the piece would derail the spine if inlined, and matters too much to leave to a footnote.
所有番外篇的标准是一致的：如果将其放入主线会破坏结构，且其重要性又不容许仅仅作为脚注存在。

2. What every bonus article shares
2. 每个番外篇的共同点

A bonus article in this series is not a freer-form blog post next to a stricter spine. It follows the same discipline as the spine, just on a different shape of question. Five invariants run through every bonus.
本系列中的番外篇并非主线文章旁随意的博客文章。它遵循与主线相同的准则，只是针对不同形态的问题。每一篇番外篇都贯穿着五个不变的原则。

Real fixtures and real numbers. Every benchmark in the bonus series runs on documents the reader can find: NIST publications, arXiv papers, openly licensed corpora, or fictional cases the series authored itself (synthetic CVs in B08, the FAQ in B02). No screenshot of a result without the file that produced it. No anecdotal “in our experience” without the run that backs it.
真实的固定装置和真实的数据。番外系列中的每一个基准测试都运行在读者可以找到的文档上：NIST 出版物、arXiv 论文、开源语料库，或本系列自行编写的虚构案例（B08 中的合成简历，B02 中的 FAQ）。没有不带源文件的结果截图。没有缺乏运行数据支持的“根据我们的经验”这类轶事。

Brick-aware framing. Every bonus names which of the four bricks it touches and explains its claim against that brick’s contract. The article does not float in “RAG in general” space; it lands inside the relational tables, the typed contracts, and the audit trail the spine established. That is why the pairings at the end of each bonus point back to specific spine articles.
模块感知的框架。每一篇番外篇都会指明它触及了四个模块中的哪一个，并根据该模块的契约解释其主张。文章不会漂浮在“通用 RAG”的空间里；它扎根于主线建立的关系表、类型化契约和审计追踪中。这就是为什么每篇番外篇末尾的配对都会指向特定的主线文章。

Per-failure-mode reporting. Aggregate numbers lie (Manifesto M4, Position 8). The bonus benchmarks report per question type the same way the spine evaluation does. “This model wins on point lookups, that one wins on listings, both fail on cross-references” beats “model X scored 87% overall”.
按故障模式报告。汇总数据会撒谎（宣言 M4，立场 8）。番外篇的基准测试像主线评估一样，按问题类型进行报告。“该模型在点查询上胜出，另一个在列表查询上胜出，两者在交叉引用上都失败了”远比“模型 X 总分 87%”更有价值。

No magical tool claim. A bonus article that walked in saying “this new framework solves the table problem” would belong on a vendor blog, not in this series. The bonus articles run plain code, lean on the relational tables the spine defines, and report what the engineering produced. The frameworks each bonus does mention (SymSpell in B01, Ollama in B10 to B12, Pydant...
没有魔法工具的宣称。如果一篇番外篇一上来就说“这个新框架解决了表格问题”，那它应该出现在供应商的博客上，而不是本系列中。番外篇运行的是纯代码，依赖于主线定义的关系表，并报告工程实践的结果。每篇番外篇确实提到的框架（如 B01 中的 SymSpell，B10 到 B12 中的 Ollama，Pydant...）
