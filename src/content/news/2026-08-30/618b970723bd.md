# The Galaxy Z Flip 8 is at its best when there’s friction
# Galaxy Z Flip 8：在“阻力”中展现最佳状态

The cover screen feels more like a phone than ever, but the limitations are still what makes it.
外屏的使用体验比以往任何时候都更像是一部手机，但正是那些局限性，才成就了它的独特之处。

What do you want from a flip phone when it’s closed? A simple interface, perhaps notifications and a widget or two, for handling the basics? Or a fully fledged Android experience shrunk down for the Flip form factor? For years, Samsung has insisted on the former, even as some fans — and Razr rival Motorola — have pushed for the latter. With the Galaxy Z Flip 8, Samsung has given in.
当你合上翻盖手机时，你希望它能做什么？是一个简单的界面，比如处理基础事务的通知和一两个小组件？还是一个为翻盖形态缩小的完整安卓体验？多年来，三星一直坚持前者，尽管一些粉丝——以及竞争对手摩托罗拉的 Razr 系列——一直在推动后者。随着 Galaxy Z Flip 8 的发布，三星终于妥协了。

Although the Z Flip 8’s hardware is almost unchanged year-on-year, Samsung has fundamentally rethought how this phone is meant to be used when shut. The cover screen feels more like a regular Android phone, with easier access to the full versions of every single app, letting you browse social media, watch movies, and play games without ever opening the phone. Not every app works well on a tiny square screen with two cameras in the middle of it, but even when they don’t, that friction between me and the distractions of my phone still feels as much a feature as a bug.
尽管 Z Flip 8 的硬件与去年相比几乎没有变化，但三星从根本上重新思考了这款手机在合上时的使用方式。外屏感觉更像是一部普通的安卓手机，可以更轻松地访问每个应用程序的完整版本，让你无需打开手机即可浏览社交媒体、观看电影和玩游戏。并非所有应用程序都能在中间带有两个摄像头的微型方形屏幕上完美运行，但即使在它们表现不佳时，我与手机干扰项之间的那种“阻力”，依然感觉像是一种功能，而非缺陷。

Everything exciting about the Z Flip 8 lies in its 4-inch cover screen, which is now more versatile than ever thanks to a homescreen redesign. Swiping down from the top left now brings up your notifications, while swiping from the top right instead shows a cover-screen-optimized version of the Quick Settings panel. Immediately, this feels more like a phone.
Z Flip 8 最令人兴奋的地方在于其 4 英寸的外屏，得益于主屏幕的重新设计，它现在比以往任何时候都更加多功能。从左上角向下滑动可以调出通知，而从右上角向下滑动则会显示针对外屏优化的“快速设置”面板。瞬间，它感觉更像是一部手机了。

More exciting is what happens when you swipe up from the bottom: you get an app drawer. By default, this is populated with a selection of Samsung’s own apps that are optimized for the square screen, plus a “Labs” section with third-party options including Netflix and WhatsApp. But right below that, Samsung has included an easy prompt to install MultiStar, the Good Lock module that lets you run any app you’d like on the outer screen.
更令人兴奋的是从底部向上滑动时的操作：你会看到一个应用程序抽屉。默认情况下，这里填充了三星自家的、针对方形屏幕优化的应用程序，以及一个包含 Netflix 和 WhatsApp 等第三方选项的“实验室”部分。但在其下方，三星提供了一个简单的提示，可以安装 MultiStar——这是 Good Lock 的一个模块，让你可以在外屏上运行任何你想要的应用程序。

Samsung used to bury the option deep in the phone’s settings, only to be found by those who knew to look for it. The bottom of the app drawer isn’t quite front and center, but it’s much more prominent than it used to be.
三星过去常把这个选项深埋在手机设置中，只有那些知道去哪里找的人才能发现。虽然现在它在应用程序抽屉底部并不算完全处于中心位置，但比过去显眼多了。

You can still run apps in three forms: full-screen, with the cameras blocking part of the software; a square display that stops just above the level of the cameras; or a thin portrait-aspect ratio that letterboxes the sides. A small virtual button at the bottom of the screen lets you cycle through them.
你仍然可以以三种形式运行应用程序：全屏（摄像头会遮挡部分软件界面）；在摄像头上方停止的方形显示；或者两侧留黑边的细长纵向比例。屏幕底部的一个小虚拟按钮可以让你在这些模式间切换。

Good performance isn’t guaranteed. Some apps, like Withings’ health tracker companion app, are almost impossible to use on the cover screen, with no configuration that leaves enough space for its graphs to fit. Google Maps is only a bit better — I love the idea of quickly checking my walking route without opening the phone, but there are so many floating interface elements that it’s unwieldy at best. Others work smoothly, but feel pointless: I can stream HBO Max on the tiny outer display, but I’d never want to.
良好的性能并非总能保证。一些应用程序，比如 Withings 的健康追踪配套应用，几乎无法在外屏上使用，因为没有任何配置能为它的图表留出足够的空间。谷歌地图的情况稍好一些——我喜欢无需打开手机就能快速查看步行路线的想法，但由于浮动界面元素太多，使用起来非常笨拙。其他一些应用虽然运行流畅，但毫无意义：我可以在微小的外屏上播放 HBO Max，但我绝不会想这么做。

But for other apps, the new cover screen options feel genuinely freeing. I can open Todoist and tick off a task, dip into WhatsApp to send a message, or open MyFitnessPal to log the snack I just ate. They’re quick actions that work well enough on the small screen, and because other apps — *cough* Instagram *cough* — don’t, I can dive into them without the risk of getting dragged into doomscrolling. That little bit of friction is just enough to save me from wasting 20 minutes I’ll never get back.
但对于其他应用程序，新的外屏选项确实让人感到自由。我可以打开 Todoist 勾选任务，进入 WhatsApp 发送消息，或者打开 MyFitnessPal 记录我刚吃的零食。这些快速操作在小屏幕上运行良好，而且因为其他应用程序（咳咳，Instagram）表现不佳，我可以在使用它们时避免陷入“末日刷屏”（doomscrolling）的风险。那一点点阻力，足以让我免于浪费掉那 20 分钟宝贵的时间。

As for the hardware, little has changed. The Z Flip 8 is 0.6mm thinner and 8g lighter than its predecessor. This is the thickest of Samsung’s three current foldables, but it’s more compact than Motorola’s Razrs, its only real rivals.
至于硬件，变化不大。Z Flip 8 比前代产品薄了 0.6 毫米，轻了 8 克。这是三星目前三款折叠屏手机中最厚的一款，但比它唯一的真正竞争对手摩托罗拉 Razr 系列更紧凑。

Samsung didn’t upgrade the Z Flip 8 with the thinner, tougher titanium display stack found in the two new Z Fold 8 phones, which also means this has just as visible a crease as ever. It rarely bothers me, and is hard to see head-on, but you may feel differently. The IP48 rating is a more objective issue, and means that while this phone is mostly safe from water, it’s still vulnerable to dust.
三星并没有为 Z Flip 8 升级两款新 Z Fold 8 手机上那种更薄、更坚固的钛金属显示屏堆叠技术，这也意味着它的折痕依然清晰可见。这很少困扰我，而且从正面看很难察觉，但你的感受可能不同。IP48 防护等级是一个更客观的问题，这意味着虽然这款手机基本可以防水，但它仍然容易受到灰尘的影响。
