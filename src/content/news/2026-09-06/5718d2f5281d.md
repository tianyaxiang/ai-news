# Fantasy Footballers’ Andy Holloway is a dedicated zero-inbox kinda guy
# 《Fantasy Footballers》的 Andy Holloway：一位执着的“零收件箱”践行者

If you haven’t had your fantasy football draft yet, better get on it.
如果你还没进行梦幻橄榄球选秀，最好抓紧时间了。

Andy Holloway cohosts the Fantasy Footballers podcast with his friends Jason Moore and Mike Wright. The show is one of the premier fantasy sports podcasts, netting over 2 million monthly listeners and numerous awards, including Best Sports Podcast from iHeartRadio and People’s Choice at the Podcast Awards four years in a row.
Andy Holloway 与他的朋友 Jason Moore 和 Mike Wright 共同主持了《Fantasy Footballers》播客。该节目是顶尖的梦幻体育播客之一，每月拥有超过 200 万听众，并屡获殊荣，包括 iHeartRadio 的“最佳体育播客奖”，以及连续四年在播客奖（Podcast Awards）中获得“人民选择奖”。

Holloway’s goal has always been about bringing fantasy football to a wider audience, not just hardcore nerds. With the NFL season starting next week and fantasy drafts in full swing, now seemed like a great time to see how Holloway stays focused during what is probably the busiest part of the year for a serious fantasy football fan.
Holloway 的目标始终是将梦幻橄榄球推广给更广泛的受众，而不仅仅是硬核极客。随着 NFL 赛季下周开赛，梦幻选秀正如火如荼地进行，现在正是了解 Holloway 如何在这一年中最忙碌的时期保持专注的好时机。

**What is one thing you wish you could change about your phone?**
**你希望手机能做出什么改变？**

I wish it was less intrusive in everyday life. No matter how much I customize things, I am still overwhelmed with notifications and messages and things that “seem” required.
我希望它在日常生活中不要那么具有侵入性。无论我如何自定义设置，我仍然会被各种通知、消息以及那些“看起来”必须处理的事情所淹没。

**How many tabs do you have open right now?**
**你现在开了多少个浏览器标签页？**

Just 2! I’m very much a tidy tab user and a zero-inbox type of guy.
只有 2 个！我是一个非常注重标签页整洁的人，也是那种追求“零收件箱”的人。

**What is your favorite gadget you’ve ever owned?**
**你拥有过的最喜欢的电子产品是什么？**

I truly love my Whoop! It helps me stay motivated and accountable, barely needs to be charged, and builds camaraderie with friends. I resisted for years, thinking it was dumb, but I love it.
我真心喜欢我的 Whoop（智能穿戴设备）！它能帮助我保持动力和自律，几乎不需要充电，还能增进朋友间的互动。我曾抗拒了好几年，觉得它很蠢，但现在我爱死它了。

**What creation are you most proud of?**
**你最引以为傲的作品是什么？**

Certainly, building The Fantasy Footballers. People told us we couldn’t bootstrap it, but working with my best friends on a project we loved was the most satisfying accomplishment.
当然是创立《Fantasy Footballers》。人们曾告诉我们，白手起家是不可能的，但能与我最好的朋友们一起从事我们热爱的项目，这是我最有成就感的事情。

**What do you do when you need to focus?**
**当你需要专注时会做什么？**

I try to knock out a ton of little things in a small period of time to feel like I’ve cleared a bunch of stuff off my list. It helps me stay focused on high-priority items without the noise of a bunch of small things hanging over me!
我会尝试在短时间内快速处理掉一大堆琐事，这样会让我感觉清单上的任务被清空了。这能帮我专注于高优先级事项，而不会被一堆悬而未决的小事所干扰！

**What’s the last piece of physical media you bought?**
**你最近购买的实体媒体是什么？**

The vinyl album of Noah Kahan’s The Great Divide.
Noah Kahan 的黑胶唱片《The Great Divide》。

**What would the tagline for your biopic be?**
**如果为你拍一部传记片，标语会是什么？**

The Man Who Overthought Everything
那个对一切过度思考的人。
