# What’s the Best Pet DNA Test? We Tested the Most Popular Ones
# 哪款宠物 DNA 检测最靠谱？我们测试了市面上最热门的产品

An at-home cat or dog DNA test kit promises to give pet owners clarity on a heap of important health data, like breed ancestry, blood type, and health risks. To access this info, owners just swab the inside of their pet’s mouth, seal the sample in a prepaid-postage envelope, and wait (for up to five weeks) for results to be sent to their inbox. Each company has a testing lab, and the results live in your online account, where they can be downloaded and even shared with your vet.

居家猫狗 DNA 检测套件承诺为宠物主人提供大量重要的健康数据，例如品种血统、血型和健康风险。要获取这些信息，主人只需擦拭宠物的口腔内侧，将样本密封在预付邮资的信封中，然后等待（最长五周）结果发送到邮箱即可。每家公司都有自己的检测实验室，结果会保存在您的在线账户中，您可以下载这些结果，甚至可以与您的兽医分享。

But because of several factors, such as sample size and comparison, these tests often give dramatically different results for things like genetic ancestry and breed traits (more on that below). I tested several at-home DNA test kits, including two of the most popular kits—Basepaws ($104) and Wisdom Panel ($97)—on my two adult cats and talked to several animal experts to get insights into these tests—and whether they are worth your money, given the large disparity in results. Think of this as the WIRED guide to the increasingly popular at-home pet DNA test.

但由于样本量和比对数据库等多种因素，这些检测在遗传血统和品种特征等方面往往会给出截然不同的结果（下文将详细说明）。我用我的两只成年猫测试了几款居家 DNA 检测套件，包括两款最受欢迎的产品——Basepaws（104 美元）和 Wisdom Panel（97 美元），并咨询了几位动物专家，以深入了解这些检测——考虑到结果存在巨大差异，它们是否值得购买。您可以将此文视为 WIRED 针对日益流行的居家宠物 DNA 检测的指南。

### Wisdom Panel Complete for Cats DNA Test
### Wisdom Panel 猫用 DNA 全面检测

In my research, I've found that people widely consider Wisdom Panel the most trusted at-home pet DNA test kit. Wisdom Panel offers several kits for dogs but only one test for cats. It can identify more than 70 breeds, includes 45 health tests, determines blood type, and screens for more than 25 traits. It even provides a genetic consult for “at-risk” health findings and shows what the pet's family tree might look like based on DNA results.

在研究中，我发现人们普遍认为 Wisdom Panel 是最值得信赖的居家宠物 DNA 检测套件。Wisdom Panel 提供多种狗用套件，但猫用检测只有一种。它可以识别 70 多个品种，包含 45 项健康检测，可确定血型并筛查 25 种以上的性状。它甚至为“高风险”健康发现提供遗传咨询，并根据 DNA 结果展示宠物的家谱。

I especially liked that it tests for the most common conditions seen in cats or dogs with a similar breed makeup to your pet, including important markers like drug sensitivity, immune deficiency, and bleeding disorders. It also identified the cat’s blood type (both of my cats were A) and transfusion risk (both were moderate). The results arrived live online in my Wisdom Panel account within three weeks of mailing them. I really appreciated the interface, which was organized into clickable tabs that made the information easier to access and navigate.

我特别喜欢它能针对与您宠物品种构成相似的猫狗进行常见疾病筛查，包括药物敏感性、免疫缺陷和出血性疾病等重要指标。它还确定了猫的血型（我的两只猫都是 A 型）和输血风险（均为中等）。寄出样本后，结果在三周内就出现在了我的 Wisdom Panel 在线账户中。我非常欣赏它的界面，它被组织成可点击的标签页，使信息更易于访问和浏览。

### Basepaws Breed + Health Cat DNA Test
### Basepaws 猫用品种与健康 DNA 检测

WIRED reviewed Basepaws over half a decade ago, and a lot has changed since then. Basepaws offers a single breed and health test for cats that identifies up to 21 breeds, 64 genetic health markers, 50 genetic trait markers, blood type, and oral health. The wait time for results is five weeks, and when I first sent my cat Basil's test in, they couldn't extract enough DNA, so I had to send another sample and wait another five weeks.

WIRED 在五年多前评测过 Basepaws，从那时起，很多事情都发生了变化。Basepaws 提供一种猫用品种与健康检测，可识别多达 21 个品种、64 个遗传健康标记、50 个遗传性状标记、血型和口腔健康状况。结果等待时间为五周，当我第一次寄出我的猫 Basil 的样本时，他们无法提取足够的 DNA，所以我不得不寄送另一个样本并再等五周。

Rather than an easily digestible interactive screen with information like Wisdom Panel, Basepaws gives you essentially a mini book PDF to scroll through to find information. It feels more in-depth than Wisdom Panel, but it is also harder to disseminate the large pile of information. Basepaws tests genes for markers associated with disorders in musculoskeletal and connective tissue; eyes; metabolic, autoimmune, and endocrine systems; blood; and more.

与 Wisdom Panel 那种易于阅读的交互式界面不同，Basepaws 本质上是给你一份 PDF 格式的“小书”，你需要滚动浏览来查找信息。它感觉比 Wisdom Panel 更深入，但也更难消化这些海量信息。Basepaws 检测与肌肉骨骼和结缔组织、眼睛、代谢、自身免疫和内分泌系统、血液等疾病相关的基因标记。

Basepaws tests for oral health by comparing against an oral microbiome database. In this regard, Basepaws wins out. Dental health is a major concern for me, and I love that Basepaws screens for it and provides an oral health report.

Basepaws 通过与口腔微生物组数据库进行比对来检测口腔健康。在这方面，Basepaws 胜出。牙齿健康是我非常关心的问题，我很高兴 Basepaws 能对此进行筛查并提供口腔健康报告。
