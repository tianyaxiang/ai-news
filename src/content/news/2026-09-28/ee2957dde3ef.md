# Can Muse overcome Meta’s trust issues?
# Muse 能否克服 Meta 的信任危机？

Meta’s new AI agent Muse took the spotlight at the company’s annual Connect event, where CEO Mark Zuckerberg made it clear that Facebook’s parent company plans to push AI features everywhere. On the latest episode of TechCrunch’s Equity podcast, Kirsten Korosec, Sean O’Kane, and I discussed Meta’s AI announcements seemed to steal the spotlight during a week of new model launches from OpenAI and Anthropic.
Meta 的全新 AI 智能体 Muse 在公司年度 Connect 大会上大放异彩，首席执行官马克·扎克伯格明确表示，Facebook 的母公司计划将 AI 功能推向各个角落。在 TechCrunch 的《Equity》播客最新一期节目中，Kirsten Korosec、Sean O’Kane 和我讨论了 Meta 的 AI 发布，这些发布似乎在 OpenAI 和 Anthropic 发布新模型的这一周里抢尽了风头。

With other big AI companies focused on coding and enterprise tools, it was a little surprising to see Meta move in the opposite direction, with a consumer focus and a cute, Tamagotchi-style AI device that Meta insists is for adults only. But as Kirsten noted, this could be playing to Meta’s strengths.
在其他大型 AI 公司专注于编程和企业级工具时，Meta 反其道而行之，将重心放在消费者市场，并推出了一款可爱的、类似“电子宠物”的 AI 设备（Meta 坚称其仅供成人使用），这着实令人惊讶。但正如 Kirsten 所指出的，这可能正是发挥了 Meta 的长处。

Sean tried Muse for himself, and while he was pleased that the agent actually found him some unclaimed money, he described the feature as more “a party-trick type thing,” rather than something that will drive ongoing usage. Plus, there’s the question of whether users can trust Meta’s AI with sensitive information. “Meta’s business is to sell you ads,” Sean said. “And yes, they’ll make the argument that the more they know about you, the more accurate and interesting the ads will be — wake me up when we get to that fever dream.”
Sean 亲自试用了 Muse，虽然他很高兴该智能体确实帮他找到了一些未领取的资金，但他将此功能描述为更像是一种“派对戏法”，而不是能驱动持续使用的功能。此外，还有一个问题是用户是否能放心地将敏感信息交给 Meta 的 AI。“Meta 的生意就是向你兜售广告，”Sean 说，“没错，他们会辩称他们越了解你，广告就越精准、越有趣——等我们真的进入那种狂热梦境时再叫醒我吧。”
