人们社交的地点可能在改变，但对良好氛围的需求从未改变。传统的蓝牙音箱虽然方便且音质出色，但很难营造出派对氛围。而派对音箱凭借其震撼的音量、便携性和 LED 灯光，完全没有这个问题。

### What Is a Party Speaker?
### 什么是派对音箱？

Party speakers are large, high-output Bluetooth speakers designed for social gatherings, sitting somewhere between a conventional wireless speaker and a portable PA. Current battery-powered models range from around 100 to 800 watts of power, with most offering between 12 to 25 hours of battery life, if not more. Expect large drivers, bass-boost modes, LED lighting, handles, wheels, and even replaceable batteries to keep the party going. Microphone and instrument inputs, karaoke controls, and the ability to link multiple speakers are also common, as are partner apps that give you the chance to play DJ, curate the lighting, and alter the sound.

派对音箱是专为社交聚会设计的大功率蓝牙音箱，定位介于传统无线音箱和便携式扩声系统之间。目前的电池供电型号功率范围约为 100 到 800 瓦，大多数能提供 12 到 25 小时甚至更长的续航时间。你可以期待大尺寸驱动单元、低音增强模式、LED 灯光、把手、滚轮，甚至是可更换电池以确保持续狂欢。麦克风和乐器输入接口、卡拉 OK 控制以及多台音箱互联功能也很常见，配套的应用程序还能让你化身 DJ、调节灯光并改变音效。

### What Should I Look for in a Party Speaker?
### 选购派对音箱时应该注意什么？

**Power:** Party speakers range from boxes one person can comfortably carry to 60-pound-plus towers on wheels. Power varies, but I’ve tested designs from 100 to 800-plus watts. As a general rule, the more watts it can output, the louder it will be, but clever DSP means brands like JBL and Sony can often make more noise with less watts than you might think.

**功率：** 派对音箱的规格从单人即可轻松携带的箱体，到重达 60 磅以上、带有滚轮的塔式音箱不等。功率各异，但我测试过的设计功率从 100 瓦到 800 瓦以上都有。通常来说，输出功率越大，声音就越响，但得益于巧妙的 DSP 技术，像 JBL 和 Sony 这样的品牌往往能用比你想象中更低的功率制造出更大的声浪。

**Battery life:** Your needs will again depend on how you party, but between 12 to 25 hours is common. Remember, though, high volume and lighting can cut that number down. Soundboks, for example, quotes 40 hours at mid volume but only six to eight hours at maximum, depending on the speaker.

**电池续航：** 你的需求取决于你的派对方式，但 12 到 25 小时的续航是常见的。不过要记住，高音量和灯光效果会缩短续航时间。例如，Soundboks 标称在中等音量下可续航 40 小时，但在最大音量下仅能维持 6 到 8 小时（具体取决于型号）。

**Connectivity:** As well as Bluetooth for wireless convenience, 3.5-mm or other line inputs are useful for DJ controllers, mixers, and sources beyond your phone, while some models add RCA, quarter-inch, or XLR connections. Outdoor users should look for an IP rating. Replaceable batteries, wheels, sturdy handles, and physical controls become considerably more important once a speaker leaves the living room.

**连接性：** 除了蓝牙带来的无线便利外，3.5 毫米或其他线路输入接口对于连接 DJ 控制器、混音器以及手机以外的音源非常有用，有些型号还增加了 RCA、6.35 毫米（四分之一英寸）或 XLR 接口。户外用户应关注 IP 防护等级。一旦音箱离开客厅，可更换电池、滚轮、坚固的把手和物理控制按键就会变得尤为重要。

### Can I Use a Party Speaker With DJ Decks?
### 我可以用派对音箱连接 DJ 打碟机吗？

Yes, provided it has a suitable wired input. DJ controllers and mixers typically have a line-out option, commonly through RCA, quarter-inch, or XLR connections, although a 3.5-mm aux input can also work with the right cable.

可以，前提是它有合适的有线输入接口。DJ 控制器和混音器通常有线路输出选项，常见的是通过 RCA、6.35 毫米或 XLR 接口，当然，使用合适的线缆，3.5 毫米辅助输入接口也能工作。

If you are mixing, use a cable where possible, as Bluetooth introduces enough latency between moving a control and hearing the result to make accurate cueing and beat-matching difficult. Some specialist DJ systems like the Aiaiai Unit-4 Wireless+ Studio Monitors use proprietary low-latency wireless connections to get around this, but ordinary Bluetooth isn’t designed for the job.

如果你在混音，尽可能使用线缆，因为蓝牙在操作控制和听到结果之间会产生足够的延迟，导致难以进行精确的对点和节拍匹配。一些专业的 DJ 系统（如 Aiaiai Unit-4 Wireless+ 监听音箱）使用专有的低延迟无线连接来解决这个问题，但普通的蓝牙并不适合这项工作。

### What About Karaoke?
### 卡拉 OK 功能怎么样？

There’s plenty of crossover between party speakers and karaoke machines, but they’re not always the same thing. Some party speakers include one or two microphone inputs, while better-equipped models add wireless mics, separate vocal volume, echo or reverb, ample controls, and even vocal removal. The $350 Soundcore Rave 3S, for example, includes two wireless microphones and real-time vocal removal.

派对音箱和卡拉 OK 机之间有很多重叠，但它们并不总是同一回事。一些派对音箱包含一到两个麦克风输入接口，而配置更好的型号则增加了无线麦克风、独立人声调节、回声或混响、丰富的控制选项，甚至人声消除功能。例如，售价 350 美元的 Soundcore Rave 3S 就配备了两个无线麦克风和实时人声消除功能。

A dedicated karaoke setup puts singing rather than music playback first, and may offer more extensive microphone controls, song management, and accessories. WIRED’s Best Karaoke Gear guide covers those options in more detail.

专业的卡拉 OK 设备将演唱而非音乐播放放在首位，并可能提供更广泛的麦克风控制、歌曲管理和配件。WIRED 的《最佳卡拉 OK 设备》指南更详细地涵盖了这些选项。

### How Do I Connect Multiple Phones to the Same Bluetooth Speaker?
### 如何将多部手机连接到同一个蓝牙音箱？

Bluetooth multipoint lets two phones stay connected simultaneously, although switching between them varies by speaker. For larger groups, Spotify Jam and Apple Music SharePlay let guests choose music from their own phones while one device stays connected to the speaker. YouTube Music also supports collaborative playlists, enabling several people to add songs in advance or during the party.

蓝牙多点连接功能允许两部手机同时保持连接，尽管不同音箱之间的切换方式有所不同。对于大型聚会，Spotify Jam 和 Apple Music SharePlay 允许客人在自己的手机上点歌，而只需一台设备保持与音箱的连接。YouTube Music 也支持协作播放列表，使多人可以在派对前或派对期间添加歌曲。
