EXECUTIVE SUMMARY After a tough few years littered with some high-profile failures, there’s some positive news for next-generation batteries in the US. Sila just signed an agreement with Panasonic Energy, a global battery manufacturer, to supply silicon-carbon anode material, a possible key to smaller, lighter batteries. It’s a deal that Sila says could be worth more than $1 billion over time. This arrangement also shows how much the politics of batteries have shifted lately. A major part of the financial backing for this factory’s production is a $1.4 billion loan from the Trump administration’s Defense Department. While batteries can be a major tool to help drive the energy transition and cut emissions, right now much of the industry is more focused on geopolitics.

执行摘要：在经历了几年充斥着一些高调失败的艰难时期后，美国下一代电池领域传来了一些积极消息。Sila 刚刚与全球电池制造商松下能源（Panasonic Energy）签署了一项协议，将供应硅碳负极材料，这可能是实现更小、更轻电池的关键。Sila 表示，这笔交易的价值随着时间的推移可能超过 10 亿美元。这一安排也显示出近期电池领域的政治格局发生了多大变化。该工厂生产的主要资金支持来自特朗普政府国防部提供的 14 亿美元贷款。虽然电池可以成为推动能源转型和减少排放的重要工具，但目前该行业的大部分注意力都集中在地缘政治上。

Founded in 2011, Sila makes silicon-based anodes for lithium-ion batteries. The big selling point here is the potential to improve energy density—Sila’s anode weighs a fifth as much as the graphite used in anodes today and cuts the volume in half, according to the company. So using this new material enables batteries to pack more energy into the same weight and volume, allowing longer range for EVs, for example. The material can also deliver more power and recharge faster, compared with conventional chemistry.

Sila 成立于 2011 年，生产用于锂离子电池的硅基负极。其最大的卖点在于提高能量密度的潜力——据该公司称，Sila 的负极重量仅为当今负极所用石墨的五分之一，体积也减少了一半。因此，使用这种新材料使电池能够在相同的重量和体积下容纳更多的能量，例如，这可以使电动汽车拥有更长的续航里程。与传统化学材料相比，该材料还能提供更大的功率并实现更快的充电速度。

We’ve been following Sila (and its competitors) for years. My colleague James Temple covered the company when its battery first appeared in Whoop fitness wearables in 2021. Things have gotten a lot bigger since then. In 2022, Sila announced plans for a factory in Moses Lake, Washington, and received $100 million from the US Department of Energy, part of a multibillion-dollar round of grants under the Biden administration to support the US battery supply chain. The company opened its factory in September 2025, and it’s already operating.

多年来，我们一直关注着 Sila（及其竞争对手）。我的同事詹姆斯·坦普尔（James Temple）曾在 2021 年 Sila 的电池首次出现在 Whoop 健身可穿戴设备中时报道过该公司。从那时起，事情的规模已经大了很多。2022 年，Sila 宣布了在华盛顿州摩西湖（Moses Lake）建立工厂的计划，并从美国能源部获得了 1 亿美元的资助，这是拜登政府为支持美国电池供应链而提供的数十亿美元拨款的一部分。该公司于 2025 年 9 月开设了工厂，目前已投入运营。

But the deal with Panasonic will help it scale to at least 10 times more production than current levels, says Gene Berdichevsky, the company’s cofounder and CEO. Panasonic has committed to buying tens of thousands of tons of material, Berdichevsky says, though Sila isn’t sharing specific numbers on the total commitment or the price Panasonic is paying. This agreement is Sila’s first mass-market deal and a big one for the United States’ next-generation battery market, which has largely been on the ropes over the past few years. Two billion-dollar battery companies, 24M and ESS Tech, have declared bankruptcy and shut down just in the last year.

但该公司联合创始人兼首席执行官吉恩·别尔迪切夫斯基（Gene Berdichevsky）表示，与松下的这笔交易将帮助其产能扩大到目前水平的至少 10 倍。别尔迪切夫斯基说，松下已承诺购买数万吨材料，尽管 Sila 没有透露有关总承诺量或松下支付价格的具体数字。该协议是 Sila 的首笔大众市场交易，对于过去几年一直处于困境中的美国下一代电池市场来说，这是一笔大交易。两家价值十亿美元的电池公司 24M 和 ESS Tech 就在去年宣布破产并倒闭了。

The glimmer of hope here is tied up with something I’ve been noticing about this industry: the shifting politics. While batteries used to be firmly considered a climate technology, we’ve seen a lot of companies move away from messaging around the clean energy transition and lean into geopolitics. China dominates the battery world and particularly the battery anode market, controlling over 90% of graphite production today. For years, the US has been trying to curb reliance on Chinese batteries, battery materials, and other materials used in crucial technologies, like rare earth metals.

这里的一线希望与我一直在观察到的该行业的一个现象有关：政治格局的转变。虽然电池过去被坚定地视为一种气候技术，但我们已经看到许多公司不再围绕清洁能源转型进行宣传，而是转向地缘政治。中国主导着电池世界，尤其是电池负极市场，目前控制着全球 90% 以上的石墨产量。多年来，美国一直试图遏制对中国电池、电池材料以及稀土金属等关键技术所用材料的依赖。

But the Trump administration has ramped things up, and earlier this year the US Defense Department committed a $1.4 billion loan to Sila aimed at helping the US face a “significant technology sovereignty challenge,” as a press release put it. Sila’s announcement of the Panasonic deal does mention EVs—but not climate, emissions or the energy transition. Rather, it’s all about American industrialization. (And, of course, a mention that a North American battery supply chain can also help power the AI market.)

但特朗普政府加大了力度，今年早些时候，美国国防部承诺向 Sila 提供 14 亿美元贷款，旨在帮助美国应对“重大的技术主权挑战”，正如一份新闻稿所言。Sila 关于松下交易的公告确实提到了电动汽车，但没有提到气候、排放或能源转型。相反，这一切都与美国工业化有关。（当然，还提到北美电池供应链也有助于为人工智能市场提供动力。）

I asked Berdichevsky about this shift and whether the company holds decarbonization as a priority. “I think the politics of it has shifted around us much more than our motivations have shifted,” he said. “It’s really the energy efficiency that motivates engineers like myself and technologists at our company. And I still think that that’s still at the heart of it.”

我问别尔迪切夫斯基关于这种转变以及公司是否将脱碳作为优先事项。“我认为围绕我们的政治环境发生的变化，远比我们自身动机的变化要大得多，”他说。“真正激励像我这样的工程师和我们公司的技术人员的是能源效率。而且我仍然认为，这依然是核心所在。”
