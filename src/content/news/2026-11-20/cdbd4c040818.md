# What’s With All These Outages?

Microsoft, the FAA, Chase, United, the NSA, and Apple all went down within one week. Officials say none of the outages were cyberattacks, but the internet isn’t buying it. This week on Uncanny Valley, WIRED senior business editor Sophie Kleeman joins Brian Barrett and Leah Feiger to discuss what actually went wrong and why people jumped straight to blaming hackers and AI. Plus, Anthropic’s historic IPO at a reported $1.5 trillion, Nvidia’s first earnings report since Jensen Huang resigned, and the administration’s new rule to stop Chinese companies from renting AI chips just before Trump meets Xi.

微软、美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）、大通银行、联合航空、美国国家安全局（NSA）以及苹果公司都在一周内出现了故障。官方表示这些故障均非网络攻击，但互联网用户并不买账。本周的《恐怖谷》（Uncanny Valley）节目中，WIRED资深商业编辑苏菲·克里曼（Sophie Kleeman）与布莱恩·巴雷特（Brian Barrett）及莉亚·费格（Leah Feiger）共同探讨了究竟出了什么问题，以及为什么人们会直接将其归咎于黑客和人工智能。此外，节目还讨论了Anthropic公司以1.5万亿美元估值进行的史诗级首次公开募股（IPO）、黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）辞职后英伟达的首份财报，以及特朗普在会见习近平之前，政府为阻止中国公司租用人工智能芯片而制定的新规。
