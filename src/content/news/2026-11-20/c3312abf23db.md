Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.23242 (cs) [Submitted on 2 Sep 2026] Title: Biologically-Inspired Spike Encoding and Optimization for Spiking Neural Networks Authors: Ayaan Qayyum, Bruce Yi Bu, Zicong Li, Jingfeng Zhou.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.23242 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 9 月 2 日] 标题：用于脉冲神经网络的生物启发式脉冲编码与优化 作者：Ayaan Qayyum, Bruce Yi Bu, Zicong Li, Jingfeng Zhou。

Abstract: Spiking Neural Networks (SNNs) offer theoretical energy-efficiency advantages over conventional deep networks, but realizing these advantages on vision tasks requires sparse spike representations that naive rate-coding methods fail to provide. We combine biologically motivated spike-encoding schemes with two training improvements: Rouser adaptive-threshold surrogate gradients and Kronecker-Factored Approximate Curvature (K-FAC) second-order optimization.

摘要：脉冲神经网络（SNNs）在理论上比传统深度网络具有能效优势，但在视觉任务中实现这些优势需要稀疏的脉冲表示，而简单的速率编码方法无法提供。我们将生物驱动的脉冲编码方案与两项训练改进相结合：Rouser 自适应阈值代理梯度和 Kronecker 分解近似曲率（K-FAC）二阶优化。

The spike encoding schemes experimented with are rate coding, latency coding, DVS-style delta modulation, and Difference-of-Gaussians (DoG) filtering. Experiments on MNIST and Fashion-MNIST demonstrate that biologically inspired encoders yield the largest accuracy gains in capacity-constrained regimes and serve as an effective parameter-efficiency lever.

实验中使用的脉冲编码方案包括速率编码、延迟编码、DVS 风格的增量调制以及高斯差分（DoG）滤波。在 MNIST 和 Fashion-MNIST 上的实验表明，生物启发式编码器在容量受限的情况下能产生最大的准确率提升，并可作为一种有效的参数效率杠杆。

DoG and delta modulation encoders produce significantly sparser spike trains than rate coding. K-FAC yields modest but consistent accuracy improvements over Adam-trained baselines, and Rouser's learnable per-neuron thresholds improve Fashion-MNIST accuracy from 87.37% to 88.13% by enabling homeostatic firing-rate regulation.

DoG 和增量调制编码器产生的脉冲序列比速率编码稀疏得多。与 Adam 训练的基准相比，K-FAC 带来了适度但持续的准确率提升；Rouser 的可学习神经元阈值通过实现稳态放电率调节，将 Fashion-MNIST 的准确率从 87.37% 提高到了 88.13%。

Biologically grounded pre-processing methods can help close the accuracy gap between SNNs and non-spiking baselines without increasing model size.

基于生物学的预处理方法可以在不增加模型规模的情况下，帮助缩小 SNN 与非脉冲基准模型之间的准确率差距。
