In the wake of the Make America Healthy Again movement’s midterm wipeout, a top leader suggested the heavy losses came down to being outspent by wealthier, more powerful rivals. But a Stat report on MAHA’s own spending in the run-up to the election raises questions about whether the group’s priority was ever winning elections in the first place.

在“让美国再次健康”（Make America Healthy Again，简称MAHA）运动中期选举惨败后，一位高层领导人称，惨重的损失归因于对手财力更雄厚、势力更强大。但一份关于MAHA在选举前夕支出情况的Stat报告引发了质疑：该组织的首要任务是否真的是赢得选举。

The dive into the financial documents reveals that the MAHA PAC, led by Tony Lyons, spent more on allies, boosters, meals, luxuries, and its affiliated nonprofit than it did on political candidates. The findings are in stark contrast to an announcement from Lyons in the spring that he was heading a $100 million fundraiser to boost MAHA’s clout in the midterms. In reality, MAHA PAC took in $3.2 million. During the election cycle, the PAC spent more than $10.5 million, with over $7 million coming from money it had collected previously.

对财务文件的深入调查显示，由托尼·莱昂斯（Tony Lyons）领导的MAHA政治行动委员会（PAC）在盟友、支持者、餐饮、奢侈品及其附属非营利组织上的支出，远超其在政治候选人身上的投入。这些发现与莱昂斯春季宣布他将领导一项1亿美元的筹款活动以提升MAHA在中期选举中的影响力形成了鲜明对比。实际上，MAHA PAC仅筹集了320万美元。在整个选举周期内，该委员会支出超过1050万美元，其中超过700万美元来自此前筹集的资金。

According to Stat’s findings, of the $10.5 million the MAHA PAC spent on the midterms, less than a tenth—roughly $1 million—went to messaging on political candidates, which are expenses called “independent expenditures.” Andrew Mayersohn, a committees researcher at OpenSecrets, called the spending “pretty unusual,” explaining that “Major super PACs spend most of their money on independent expenditures.”

根据Stat的调查结果，在MAHA PAC为中期选举支出的1050万美元中，不到十分之一（约100万美元）用于政治候选人的宣传，这些支出被称为“独立支出”。OpenSecrets的委员会研究员安德鲁·梅耶索恩（Andrew Mayersohn）称这种支出“非常不寻常”，并解释说：“主要的超级政治行动委员会会将大部分资金用于独立支出。”

In some cases, the scant political spending was dwarfed by spending on luxuries. For instance, in a key House race in Northern California, MAHA supported Kevin Kiley over his political rival, pediatrician Richard Pan. Despite calling the race “the most direct ideological confrontation” between MAHA and its opposition “anywhere on the 2026 ballot,” the PAC only spent $49,900 to back Kiley. That’s a little less than the $50,100 it spent at Mastro’s Steakhouse, an upscale restaurant that offers $110 porterhouse steaks in Washington, DC. Further, it’s less than a third of the $155,900 the PAC spent at Ned’s Club, a luxury club near the White House that has become a popular gathering spot among Trump officials.

在某些情况下，微薄的政治支出被奢侈消费所掩盖。例如，在北加州一场关键的众议院竞选中，MAHA支持凯文·基利（Kevin Kiley）对抗其政治对手、儿科医生理查德·潘（Richard Pan）。尽管该委员会称这场竞选是MAHA与其反对派之间“2026年选票上任何地方最直接的意识形态对抗”，但它仅花费了49,900美元来支持基利。这比它在华盛顿特区高档餐厅Mastro’s Steakhouse（提供110美元的丁骨牛排）消费的50,100美元还要少。此外，这还不到该委员会在白宫附近的一家奢侈俱乐部Ned’s Club消费的155,900美元的三分之一，该俱乐部已成为特朗普政府官员的热门聚会场所。

Kiley lost the election to Pan by double digits. Of the roughly $1 million spent on political candidates, most of it—over $700,000—went to defeating Sen. Bill Cassidy (R-La.). Many other MAHA-endorsed candidates received $50,000 or less. Some got less than $2,000.

基利最终以两位数的差距输给了潘。在用于政治候选人的约100万美元中，大部分（超过70万美元）用于击败参议员比尔·卡西迪（Bill Cassidy，共和党，路易斯安那州）。许多其他获得MAHA背书的候选人收到的资金不足5万美元，有的甚至不到2000美元。

Outside of steaks and a private club, the MAHA PAC spent $500,000 on a media company, Merit Street Media, run by Phil McGraw, who hosted a MAHA special featuring Dr. Phil. MAHA leaders used PAC money to cover meals, payroll, and credit card bills. The PAC paid $275,000 to a security firm, Gavin de Becker & Associates, which is led by an ally of anti-vaccine Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr., MAHA’s top leader. Tens of thousands more went to LLCs that have little to no online information.

除了牛排和私人俱乐部，MAHA PAC还向菲尔·麦格劳（Phil McGraw）经营的媒体公司Merit Street Media支付了50万美元，麦格劳曾主持过一期以“菲尔博士”（Dr. Phil）为特色的MAHA特别节目。MAHA领导人利用PAC资金支付餐饮、工资和信用卡账单。该委员会还向一家名为Gavin de Becker & Associates的安保公司支付了27.5万美元，该公司由MAHA最高领导人、反疫苗卫生部长小罗伯特·F·肯尼迪（Robert F. Kennedy Jr.）的一位盟友领导。另有数万美元流向了几乎没有在线信息的有限责任公司（LLC）。

On the other hand, prominent online MAHA influencers also pocketed tens of thousands from the PAC, as did writers for The MAHA Report, a Substack that promotes Kennedy and MAHA interests. In the days after the midterms, as members of the movement were coming to grips with the election losses, The MAHA Report chose to publish a lengthy article defending convicted rapist Russel Brand, a Kennedy ally who became a MAHA fixture.

另一方面，知名的MAHA在线网红也从PAC中获得了数万美元，为Substack平台上的《MAHA报告》（The MAHA Report）撰稿的作者们也是如此，该平台旨在推广肯尼迪和MAHA的利益。在中期选举后的几天里，当该运动的成员们正努力接受选举失利的事实时，《MAHA报告》却选择发表了一篇长文，为被定罪的强奸犯拉塞尔·布兰德（Russel Brand）辩护，布兰德是肯尼迪的盟友，也是MAHA的常客。

The biggest chunk of the MAHA PAC’s spending—more than $3 million—went to the nonprofit MAHA Action, which is also run by Lyons. It’s unclear how all that money was spent.

MAHA PAC支出中最大的一笔——超过300万美元——流向了同样由莱昂斯运营的非营利组织MAHA Action。目前尚不清楚这笔钱是如何使用的。
