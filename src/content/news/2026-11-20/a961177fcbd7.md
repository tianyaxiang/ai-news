Goemans conjectured that any fractional single-source flow could be converted into an unsplittable flow without increasing total cost and while increasing any arc load by at most the largest demand. Recently, Dmitry Rybin announced a seven-vertex counterexample found through an interaction with ChatGPT, which disproved this long-standing conjecture.

Goemans 曾推测，任何分数单源流都可以在不增加总成本的情况下转化为不可拆分流，同时将任何弧负载的增加量限制在最大需求以内。最近，Dmitry Rybin 宣布通过与 ChatGPT 的交互发现了一个七顶点反例，从而推翻了这一长期存在的猜想。

However, the interaction did not reveal why this particular topology and its demand, fractional-flow, and cost choices work together, whether the example belongs to a broader family, or which structural features determine where counterexamples can occur. It also left unclear why the AI-assisted search succeeded where earlier human efforts had not.

然而，这种交互并未揭示为何这种特定的拓扑结构及其需求、分数流和成本选择能够共同起作用，该示例是否属于更广泛的类别，或者哪些结构特征决定了反例可能出现的位置。此外，它也未解释为何人工智能辅助搜索能够成功，而早期的人类尝试却未能做到。

In this paper, we develop a forward counterexample construction pipeline. It starts from an arborescence on which failure is impossible and adds terminal-entering shortcut arcs. The resulting load constraints define a finite set of admissible route choices; their convex hull, the conflict polytope, yields exact conditions for demands, fractional flows, and costs.

在本文中，我们开发了一种正向反例构建流程。它从一个不可能失败的树形结构开始，并添加进入终端的快捷弧。由此产生的负载约束定义了一组有限的可容许路径选择；它们的凸包（即冲突多面体）为需求、分数流和成本提供了精确的条件。

Starting from a simple arborescence, the pipeline selects Rybin's topology and characterizes all counterexamples it supports. Experiments further demonstrate the pipeline's transferability: when instructed to follow it while varying the initial arborescence and shortcut placement, ChatGPT produced counterexamples on topologies genuinely different from Rybin's and found valid constructions starting from 93 other rooted arborescence shapes.

从一个简单的树形结构开始，该流程选择了 Rybin 的拓扑结构并刻画了其支持的所有反例。实验进一步证明了该流程的可迁移性：当指示 ChatGPT 在改变初始树形结构和快捷方式位置的同时遵循该流程时，它在与 Rybin 完全不同的拓扑结构上生成了反例，并从其他 93 种根树形结构中找到了有效的构建方案。

The theory also explains why the original example was difficult to discover through blind enumeration and suggests that Rybin's interaction lies between blind enumeration and a complete theory-guided construction.

该理论还解释了为何原始示例难以通过盲目枚举发现，并指出 Rybin 的交互过程介于盲目枚举和完整的理论指导构建之间。
