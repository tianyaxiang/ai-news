NASA recently announced a significant shakeup to the Artemis program that will land humans on the Moon. The moves were designed to give the agency’s lunar lander providers—SpaceX with its Starship vehicle, and Blue Origin with Blue Moon—more flexibility in reaching the Moon. For Starship, this means docking with Orion in Earth orbit, and then traveling to the Moon in tandem. For Blue Moon, it shifts the rendezvous with Orion all the way to low-lunar orbit. These changes benefit the lunar landers, but in turn impose more of a burden on the Orion spacecraft, which is built for NASA by Lockheed Martin. To understand the implications for the spacecraft, Ars spoke with Kirk Shireman, Human Spaceflight Exploration and Orion program manager at Lockheed.

NASA 最近宣布对旨在将人类送上月球的“阿尔忒弥斯”（Artemis）计划进行重大调整。这些举措旨在为该机构的月球着陆器供应商——SpaceX 的“星舰”（Starship）和蓝色起源（Blue Origin）的“蓝月”（Blue Moon）——提供更大的登月灵活性。对于“星舰”而言，这意味着在地球轨道与“猎户座”（Orion）飞船对接，然后共同前往月球。对于“蓝月”而言，这意味着与“猎户座”的交会对接点被移至月球低轨道。这些变化虽然有利于月球着陆器，但同时也给由洛克希德·马丁公司为 NASA 制造的“猎户座”飞船带来了更多负担。为了了解这些变化对飞船的影响，Ars 采访了洛克希德公司人类航天探索与“猎户座”项目经理柯克·夏尔曼（Kirk Shireman）。

Starship challenges: For Starship, the evolved plan has SpaceX fueling a Starship lunar lander in low-Earth orbit before docking with Orion in high-Earth orbit. The crew will fly to the Moon on board Orion before two of the four astronauts transfer into Starship for a landing on the Moon. Due to the docking configuration with Starship, Orion must now fly backward to the Moon, with its Service Module at the top of the stack. This is a fundamentally different flying posture than Orion was designed for, Shireman said, and creates issues for Orion’s propulsion system. As a result, the Service Module, which is provided by the European Space Agency, will require modifications to its propellant tanks. This is because liquid propellant would move toward the gas inlet side of the tanks, rather than settling in the direction expected during normal operation. “When you push it the other way, the liquid goes to the gas end of the tank, and you don’t want the liquid in the gas part,” Shireman said. “Bad things can happen to you.”

“星舰”挑战：根据针对“星舰”的改进计划，SpaceX 将在近地轨道为“星舰”月球着陆器加注燃料，然后再与高地球轨道上的“猎户座”对接。机组人员将乘坐“猎户座”飞往月球，随后四名宇航员中的两人将转移至“星舰”进行登月。由于与“星舰”的对接配置，“猎户座”现在必须倒退着飞向月球，其服务舱位于组合体的顶部。夏尔曼表示，这与“猎户座”最初设计的飞行姿态截然不同，并给飞船的推进系统带来了问题。因此，由欧洲航天局提供的服务舱需要对其推进剂储箱进行改造。这是因为液体推进剂会流向储箱的进气侧，而不是像正常运行预期的那样沉淀在底部。“当你向反方向推进时，液体会流向储箱的气体端，而你不希望液体进入气体部分，”夏尔曼说，“这可能会导致严重后果。”

To address this, he said, the European Space Agency and its main contractor, Airbus, are modifying the vehicle’s tanks for Starship lunar missions. NASA and Lockheed engineers have also been studying the impact of increased acceleration on Orion when the vehicle is attached to Starship. Under Orion’s original mission architecture, the spacecraft would experience accelerations of about 0.1 to 0.15 g during the translunar injection burn, Shireman said. But with Starship providing the thrust, the acceleration would increase to approximately 0.8 to 1 g, acting in the opposite direction from the one originally planned. Orion’s structure must be certified to handle a substantially higher acceleration in the opposite direction, Shireman said.

为了解决这个问题，他说，欧洲航天局及其主要承包商空中客车公司正在为“星舰”登月任务改造飞船的储箱。NASA 和洛克希德的工程师们也一直在研究当飞船与“星舰”连接时，加速度增加对“猎户座”的影响。夏尔曼表示，在“猎户座”最初的任务架构下，飞船在月球转移轨道注入点火期间会经历约 0.1 到 0.15 g 的加速度。但由“星舰”提供推力后，加速度将增加到约 0.8 到 1 g，且作用方向与最初计划的相反。夏尔曼指出，“猎户座”的结构必须经过认证，以承受反方向上大幅增加的加速度。

Artemis III concerns: Before astronauts land on the Moon, NASA is also planning a mission in low-Earth orbit to test Orion’s interactions with the lunar landers. Shireman said this change in plans for Artemis III, announced earlier this year, has created new hazards, as Orion was not designed to spend more than a short period of time in low-Earth orbit. Heating is a concern, he said, because Orion was designed for deep space, which is colder than the thermal environment closer to Earth. For example, he said, Orion has passive thermal features designed to prevent the spacecraft from getting too cold in deep space. One of these is a silver-looking exterior tape that dissipates electrical charge and limits the radiation of heat into space. As a result, some of this tape will be removed for the Artemis III mission. Engineers are also analyzing how to manage electrical loads to reduce heating and protect the avionics and crew cabin.

“阿尔忒弥斯 3 号”的担忧：在宇航员登月之前，NASA 还计划在近地轨道执行一次任务，以测试“猎户座”与月球着陆器之间的交互。夏尔曼表示，今年早些时候宣布的“阿尔忒弥斯 3 号”计划变更带来了新的风险，因为“猎户座”并非为在近地轨道长时间停留而设计。他说，加热是一个令人担忧的问题，因为“猎户座”是为深空设计的，而深空的温度比靠近地球的热环境更冷。例如，他说，“猎户座”拥有旨在防止飞船在深空中过冷的被动热控功能。其中之一是一种银色的外部胶带，用于消散电荷并限制热量向太空辐射。因此，在“阿尔忒弥斯 3 号”任务中，部分胶带将被移除。工程师们也在分析如何管理电力负载，以减少发热并保护航空电子设备和乘员舱。

Another big change that stems from spending approximately two weeks in low-Earth orbit is the orbital debris environment, which is more congested than in deep space. Shireman said NASA and Lockheed have not completed their assessment of the orbital-debris risk associated with the new mission profile. This should be done by January, and, if the risks are too high, it might lead to modifications of Orion’s orbit for the mission. “There’s going to be a higher risk from an MMOD standpoint,” Shireman said of orbital debris near Earth. “It’ll be higher risk than flying out to the Moon.”

在近地轨道停留约两周带来的另一个重大变化是轨道碎片环境，这里比深空更加拥挤。夏尔曼表示，NASA 和洛克希德尚未完成与新任务方案相关的轨道碎片风险评估。这项工作预计在明年 1 月前完成，如果风险过高，可能会导致任务中“猎户座”轨道的调整。“从微流星体和轨道碎片（MMOD）的角度来看，风险会更高，”夏尔曼在谈到近地轨道碎片时说，“这比飞往月球的风险要大。”

Challenges of a low-Earth-orbit rendezvous: Earlier this year, NASA removed the lunar landing from Artemis III, turning the mission into a demonstration of rendezvous and docking operations in low-Earth orbit. The agency is now targeting 2027 for the mission, leaving Orion and the lunar landing companies with a compressed schedule to complete substantial technical integration work. This may seem like a fairly straightforward process, but docking is rather complex. It involves mechanical capabilities like hooks, connectors, and pins; there are thruster-plume interactions with the landers, including possible damage from hot or abrasive exhaust; concerns about whether Orion’s laser system might incorrectly interpret reflections from surfaces on Starship or Blue Moon; and more.

近地轨道交会的挑战：今年早些时候，NASA 将登月任务从“阿尔忒弥斯 3 号”中移除，将该任务转变为近地轨道交会对接操作的演示。该机构目前将任务目标定在 2027 年，这使得“猎户座”和月球着陆器公司在完成大量技术集成工作方面面临紧迫的时间表。这看起来可能是一个相当简单的过程，但对接实际上非常复杂。它涉及钩子、连接器和销钉等机械功能；还存在推进器羽流与着陆器的相互作用，包括高温或磨蚀性尾气可能造成的损坏；以及对“猎户座”激光系统是否会错误解读“星舰”或“蓝月”表面反射的担忧等等。

“We were going to do that work, but not on Artemis III, and not for both of the landers,” Shireman said of docking with Starship and Blue Moon. “It was going to be spaced out, so all that work gets crunched into this time frame.” Lockheed is working closely with NASA to prepare Orion to maintain an Artemis III schedule for a launch in summer or fall of 2027. “We’re working three shifts,” Shireman said. “We had a zero-margin schedule already, so we’re, I call it hand-to-hand combat. Literally every day we’re clawing to see, ‘Hey, can we save a shift here? Can we save half a shift there?’”

“我们原本打算进行这项工作，但不是在‘阿尔忒弥斯 3 号’上，也不是针对两个着陆器同时进行，”夏尔曼在谈到与“星舰”和“蓝月”对接时说，“原本计划是分阶段进行的，现在所有工作都被压缩到了这个时间框架内。”洛克希德正与 NASA 密切合作，准备让“猎户座”维持“阿尔忒弥斯 3 号”在 2027 年夏秋季发射的时间表。“我们正在三班倒工作，”夏尔曼说，“我们原本的时间表就已经没有余量了，所以我称之为‘肉搏战’。我们每天都在拼命争取，看看能不能在这里节省一个班次，或者在那里节省半个班次。”

Shireman said he supports the urgency of Isaacman and NASA in their contest with China over the Moon. But it is important to keep an eye on mission safety, said Shireman, who worked at NASA during both the Challenger and Columbia accidents. “We also have a responsibility to not go too fast and cut too many corners and make sure that we are not taking undue risks,” he said.

夏尔曼表示，他支持艾萨克曼（Isaacman）和 NASA 在与中国的登月竞赛中所表现出的紧迫感。但夏尔曼强调，关注任务安全至关重要，他曾在“挑战者号”和“哥伦比亚号”事故期间在 NASA 工作。“我们也有责任不操之过急，不走捷径，并确保我们没有承担不必要的风险，”他说。
