# You can now buy a Steam Machine without getting on a waitlist

Valve’s 512GB Steam Machine is available to buy now, but the 2TB model is out of stock.

Valve 的 512GB Steam Machine 现在可以购买，但 2TB 型号目前缺货。

Valve announced some updates about Steam Machine availability today, including good news if you want to buy one right now and potential bad news about future supply due to RAMaggedon.

Valve 今天宣布了一些关于 Steam Machine 供应情况的更新，其中包括如果你现在想购买该产品的好消息，以及由于内存危机（RAMaggedon）导致的未来供应方面的潜在坏消息。

The good news: You can now purchase a 512GB Steam Machine without getting on a waitlist. Initially, interested buyers had to get on Valve’s waitlist if they wanted to purchase one, but the console-like PC is now available in an “add-to-cart” status, according to the company.

好消息是：你现在可以购买 512GB 的 Steam Machine，而无需加入等待名单。据该公司称，最初有兴趣的买家如果想购买，必须加入 Valve 的等待名单，但现在这款类似游戏机的 PC 已处于“加入购物车”状态。

The bad news: If you’re on the waitlist for a 2TB model, you might not get a chance to buy one at all, as Valve says there are more people on the waitlist than there are units available. The company is closing the waitlists for its 2TB models.

坏消息是：如果你在 2TB 型号的等待名单上，你可能根本没有机会购买，因为 Valve 表示等待名单上的人数多于现有库存。该公司正在关闭其 2TB 型号的等待名单。

Valve says that while it’s still building more Steam machines, “memory and storage costs continue to increase at unprecedented levels” and that it doesn’t know the number it can make or “at what price we’ll be able to offer them.” The 2TB Steam Machine is “especially constrained.”

Valve 表示，虽然他们仍在制造更多的 Steam Machine，但“内存和存储成本持续以前所未有的水平上涨”，他们不知道能生产多少台，也不知道“我们将以什么价格提供它们”。2TB 的 Steam Machine 供应“尤其紧张”。

After delaying the Steam Machine’s expected early 2026 launch due to the memory and storage crisis, Valve launched the product in June with a price starting at $1,049 for the 512GB model without a bundled controller. A 2TB model without a controller costs $1,349.

由于内存和存储危机，Steam Machine 原定于 2026 年初的发布被推迟，Valve 最终于 6 月推出了该产品，512GB 型号（不含捆绑控制器）的起售价为 1,049 美元。不含控制器的 2TB 型号售价为 1,349 美元。
