Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.23246 (cs) [Submitted on 4 Sep 2026] Title: Chebyshev Polynomial Zonotope Abstractions for Certified Reachability of Learning-Enabled Systems Authors: Peng Xie, Amr Alanwar.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.23246 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 9 月 4 日] 标题：用于学习型系统可达性认证的切比雪夫多项式带形体抽象 作者：Peng Xie, Amr Alanwar。

Abstract: Nearly all current methods for computing reachable sets of nonlinear dynamics propagate them with truncated Taylor expansions plus rigorous remainders. The expansion is pointwise: once its argument is a whole set rather than a point, one polynomial must stay accurate across that set, and it reaches only as far as its disc of convergence.

摘要：目前几乎所有计算非线性动力学可达集的方法，都是通过截断泰勒展开式加上严格余项来进行传播的。这种展开是逐点的：一旦其自变量是一个整体集合而非单个点，多项式就必须在该集合范围内保持准确，且其覆盖范围仅限于其收敛圆盘内。

The limit binds hardest on the rational terms and sigmoid gates of modern controllers: smooth as these look on the real line, in the complex plane they blow up at points just off the real axis, closer as a gate sharpens, and those points cap the set width a Taylor expansion can cover, most tightly on the switching surface where the controller decides.

这一限制对现代控制器中的有理项和 Sigmoid 门电路约束最为严苛：尽管它们在实轴上看起来很平滑，但在复平面中，它们会在偏离实轴的点上发生爆炸，且随着门电路变得更尖锐，这些点会限制泰勒展开所能覆盖的集合宽度，在控制器进行决策的切换面上表现得尤为明显。

We prove a dichotomy: no Taylor degree certifies sets wider than that cap, while a Chebyshev abstraction of equal degree, anchored to the whole set in the same polynomial-zonotope pipeline, has no cap and gains width at a geometric rate in degree.

我们证明了一个二分法：没有任何泰勒阶数能够认证超过该上限的集合宽度，而相同阶数的切比雪夫抽象在相同的多项式带形体流水线中锚定于整个集合，它没有这种上限，且宽度随阶数呈几何级数增长。

Measured walls match the formulas to between 89 and 94 percent. We give closed-form rigorous remainders, among them the first polynomial softmax transformer; existing sound treatments are linear relaxations.

测量出的边界与公式的吻合度在 89% 到 94% 之间。我们给出了闭式严格余项，其中包括首个多项式 Softmax 变换器；现有的可靠处理方法均为线性松弛。

On closed loops, certificates survive where the Taylor ones diverge, and specifications flip from unknown to proven; on trained classifiers up to real MNIST digits, the Chebyshev frontier certifies input territory the Taylor basis surrenders.

在闭环系统中，当泰勒方法发散时，我们的认证依然有效，规格说明从“未知”转变为“已证明”；在针对真实 MNIST 数字训练的分类器上，切比雪夫边界能够认证泰勒基底所放弃的输入区域。

Every certified claim passed large Monte-Carlo harnesses with zero violations, and an applicability map states in advance where the basis provably cannot matter.

每一项认证声明都通过了大规模蒙特卡洛测试且零违规，并且一张适用性地图预先指出了该基底在何处被证明无法发挥作用。
