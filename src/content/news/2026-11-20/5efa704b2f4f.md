While refrigerators were getting hacked in Estonia, I half-seriously took a project I’m working on and asked Claude Code if there are any low-hanging fruit regarding security that we could improve. It found one issue that was easy to fix, simple oversight with a one line fix, so I just went along with it.

当爱沙尼亚的冰箱正遭到黑客攻击时，我半开玩笑地拿我正在进行的一个项目，询问 Claude Code 是否有什么容易解决的安全漏洞可以改进。它发现了一个很容易修复的问题，只需一行代码就能解决，所以我便照做了。

Then it got stopped because of bullshit “AI safeguards”. Oh no, how dare I, the senior product engineer with my product that I provably have control over, try to fix and prevent security issues and ensure compliance with local laws and regulations?

随后，它却因为那些狗屁“AI 安全防护”被拦截了。噢不，我作为一名高级产品工程师，对自己能够证明拥有控制权的产品进行修复、预防安全问题并确保符合当地法律法规，怎么就成了冒犯之举？

“Oh but you can just apply to get an exemption from them here bla bla fucking blah”

“噢，但你可以在这里申请豁免，巴拉巴拉他妈的废话。”

Have you READ the requirements? First of all, you will have to wait for multiple business days to get a response. You forfeit any data retention exemptions, so hope you don’t have any IP to protect! Oh, and oh so please give us your fucking ID or passport! We won’t leak it later, promise! And you may just get fucked anyway and not get approval. Approval to appropriately protect your own service!

你读过那些要求吗？首先，你得等上好几个工作日才能收到回复。你还得放弃任何数据保留豁免权，所以希望你没有什么知识产权需要保护！噢，还有，请务必交出你那该死的身份证或护照！我们保证以后不会泄露，真的！而且你最后可能还是会被耍，根本拿不到批准。仅仅是为了获得保护自己服务的批准而已！

This wasn’t the first time I ran into this issue. As maddening as it sounds, I try to keep up with the security landscape and to apply appropriate mitigations because leaking sensitive information is a big fucking no-no and I can’t let that happen.

这已经不是我第一次遇到这个问题了。尽管听起来很令人抓狂，但我一直在努力跟进安全领域的发展并采取适当的缓解措施，因为泄露敏感信息是绝对绝对不被允许的，我绝不能让这种事发生。

If there’s a popular exploit out there that applies for our technical stack, I need to know if the public proof-of-concept snippets can be adapted and used maliciously against my services. But no, Claude will tell you to fuck off and use an older model that still will try to stop you from applying adequate protection.

如果市面上出现了针对我们技术栈的流行漏洞，我需要知道那些公开的概念验证代码片段是否会被改编并恶意用于攻击我的服务。但不行，Claude 会让你滚蛋，并让你去用那些同样会阻止你采取充分保护措施的旧模型。

You know who don’t need to ask for approval? Criminals. They just go and use the models that don’t have safeguards in place. Abliterated open weights models don’t care. Bad actors reach their goals with or without your “safe” models.

你知道谁不需要申请批准吗？罪犯。他们直接使用那些没有安全防护的模型。被抹除限制的开源权重模型才不管这些。无论有没有你们那些“安全”模型，坏人都能达到他们的目的。

You can use a knife to cut up some food, or seriously hurt someone. Yet I don’t get asked to put in an application with my government-provided ID to buy one. Why is this tool so special?

你可以用刀切食物，也可以用它严重伤害他人。但我买刀时却不需要提交政府签发的身份证件来申请。为什么这个工具就这么特殊？

Predictably, the only way to actually make our own service more secure ended up involving the use of EU-hosted open weights models, such as GLM 5.3, that actually complied with our intentions, proved that the issue is there, and then helped fix and verify that the issue is now resolved, for good.

不出所料，最终能让我们服务变得更安全的唯一方法，是使用托管在欧盟的开源权重模型（例如 GLM 5.3）。它们确实符合我们的意图，证明了问题的存在，并帮助修复和验证了问题已彻底解决。

Congratulations, Anthropic, you’ve made the world an unsafer place. Or maybe that was your plan all along to get regulators to react and build that moat for you right before your IPO to fend off competitors?

恭喜你，Anthropic，你让这个世界变得更不安全了。又或者，这从一开始就是你们的计划，为了在 IPO 前让监管机构采取行动，为你们建立护城河以抵御竞争对手？

Either way, I’m just incredibly frustrated. Big AI labs use the same tools without restrictions to make the security landscape a fucking mess, and then they go and prohibit others from using the same tools to protect themselves from the threats they just created. Fucking asshats.

无论如何，我感到无比沮丧。大型 AI 实验室在没有任何限制的情况下使用同样的工具，把安全环境搞得一团糟，然后又禁止其他人使用同样的工具来保护自己免受他们所制造的威胁。真是一群混蛋。
