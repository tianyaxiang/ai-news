Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.23248 (cs) [Submitted on 5 Sep 2026] Title: Exploring Parkinson's Diagnosis Using Machine Learning Techniques Authors: Farhaan Qazi.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.23248 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 9 月 5 日] 标题：利用机器学习技术探索帕金森病诊断 作者：Farhaan Qazi。

Abstract: In recent years, machine learning (ML) techniques have emerged as promising tools for accurate and timely diagnosis of Parkinson's disease (PD) which is an incurable neurodegenerative disorder. This study explores the integration of ML algorithms and PD diagnosis, highlighting their potential applications in various aspects of the disease.

摘要：近年来，机器学习（ML）技术已成为准确、及时诊断帕金森病（PD）这一无法治愈的神经退行性疾病的有前途的工具。本研究探讨了机器学习算法与帕金森病诊断的结合，强调了它们在疾病各个方面的潜在应用。

ML methods such as support vector machines, decision trees, multilayer perceptron, k-nearest neighbours, logistic regression and random forest (with and without particle swarm optimisation) were applied on a PD dataset obtained from Leeds Teaching Hospitals NHS Trust containing data from 58 people.

研究人员将支持向量机、决策树、多层感知器、k-近邻算法、逻辑回归和随机森林（包含及不包含粒子群优化）等机器学习方法应用于从利兹教学医院 NHS 信托基金获取的包含 58 人数据的帕金森病数据集。

In terms of accuracy, the Decision Tree and Logistic Regression performed the best with scores of 72-76% while multilayer perceptron was least effective. All the models were remarkable in classifying positive PD cases with sensitivity scores going up to 97.56%, with SVM classifier at the pinnacle.

在准确率方面，决策树和逻辑回归表现最好，得分在 72-76% 之间，而多层感知器的效果最差。所有模型在分类帕金森病阳性病例方面表现出色，敏感性得分高达 97.56%，其中支持向量机（SVM）分类器表现最为突出。

However, ability to classify healthy controls from PD cases was insignificant throughout the board. Although, all the employed models delivered reasonable results in categorising PD patients in the presented dataset; access to more data, further study and advancement of these ML approaches could lead to enhanced diagnostic outcomes, supplementing to an improved understanding of Parkinson's disease.

然而，所有模型在区分健康对照组与帕金森病病例方面的能力均不显著。尽管所采用的所有模型在对现有数据集中的帕金森病患者进行分类时都提供了合理的结果，但获取更多数据、进行进一步研究以及推进这些机器学习方法，可能会带来更好的诊断结果，从而有助于加深对帕金森病的理解。
