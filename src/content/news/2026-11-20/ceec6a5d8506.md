Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.23432 (cs) [Submitted on 17 Nov 2026] Title: Bits, Not Boxes: Label-Free Layer Selection for Visual Localization in VLMs Authors: Haisheng Fu, Gexin Huang, Haikai Zhao, Jie Liang, Zhenman Fang, Jingning Han, Xiaoxiao Li.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.23432 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 17 日] 标题：比特而非框：视觉语言模型（VLM）中用于视觉定位的无标签层选择。作者：Haisheng Fu, Gexin Huang, Haikai Zhao, Jie Liang, Zhenman Fang, Jingning Han, Xiaoxiao Li。

Abstract: Attention maps cheaply show where a vision-language model (VLM) looks, but attention sinks make them unreliable for localization. Contrasting the question with a generic prompt suppresses sinks, yet only some layers localize the queried object. Existing methods find these layers with box annotations or fixed rules. We show that attention itself can identify them.

摘要：注意力图可以低成本地展示视觉语言模型（VLM）的关注点，但“注意力汇聚”（attention sinks）使其在定位任务中变得不可靠。通过将问题与通用提示词进行对比可以抑制这些汇聚点，但只有部分层能够定位到被查询的对象。现有的方法通常利用边界框标注或固定规则来寻找这些层。我们证明了注意力机制本身即可识别这些层。

A question changes how much a head attends to the image and where this attention goes. The two effects separate exactly, and the second, measured in bits on unlabeled questions, indicates which layers localize. WhereBits turns these where-to-look bits into a layer prior and combines it with per-head contrastive scores.

问题会改变注意力头对图像的关注程度以及关注的位置。这两种效应可以精确分离，而第二种效应（通过无标签问题上的比特数来衡量）能够指示哪些层具有定位功能。WhereBits 将这些“关注位置”比特转化为层先验，并将其与每个注意力头的对比分数相结合。

Its three design constants were fixed once on labeled COCO data, and a new model or resolution needs only unlabeled questions. Across eight VLMs, WhereBits matches the pointing accuracy of box-tuned layers on COCO and RefCOCO and ranks target tokens better. This advantage holds on a held-out model family and at higher resolutions.

其三个设计常数仅在带标签的 COCO 数据上固定了一次，对于新模型或新分辨率，仅需使用无标签问题即可。在八个 VLM 模型上的测试表明，WhereBits 在 COCO 和 RefCOCO 数据集上的指向准确率与经过边界框微调的层相当，且对目标标记的排序效果更好。这种优势在未见过的模型系列和更高分辨率下依然成立。

Its COCO pointing accuracy is 0.72, against 0.22 for mean attention. In training-free zoom-in, its crops raise V* accuracy by 6.0 points under forced-choice evaluation.

其在 COCO 上的指向准确率为 0.72，而平均注意力机制仅为 0.22。在无需训练的放大（zoom-in）任务中，其裁剪结果在强制选择评估下将 V* 准确率提升了 6.0 个百分点。
