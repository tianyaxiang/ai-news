Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.23245 (cs) [Submitted on 4 Sep 2026] Title: Response-Relevant Information Is Not Sufficient for Neural Training-State Equivalence Authors: Truong Xuan Khanh.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.23245 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 9 月 4 日] 标题：响应相关信息不足以判定神经训练状态等价性 作者：Truong Xuan Khanh。

Abstract: Response-relevant information is not sufficient evidence that two neural networks are in the same training state. We define training-state equivalence operationally: two checkpoints are equivalent, relative to an intervention family and horizon, if they respond alike when training is perturbed and continues.

摘要：响应相关信息不足以证明两个神经网络处于相同的训练状态。我们从操作层面定义了训练状态等价性：如果两个检查点在训练受到干扰并继续进行时表现出相似的响应，则相对于特定的干预族和时间跨度，这两个检查点是等价的。

On fresh runs of a one-layer transformer learning modular addition, response to a weight-decay pulse is highly reproducible relative to between-checkpoint differences (rho_state = 0.934 at 500 steps), establishing the target as measurable.

在学习模加法的一层 Transformer 的新运行中，对权重衰减脉冲的响应相对于检查点之间的差异具有高度可重复性（在 500 步时 rho_state = 0.934），这确立了该目标的可测量性。

Fifteen descriptors of weight spectra, representation geometry and optimiser geometry carry within-run response-relevant information beyond weight norm and loss on a development dataset, predicting held-out response deviation across seed blocks.

权重谱、表示几何和优化器几何的十五个描述符在开发数据集上携带了超出权重范数和损失的运行内响应相关信息，能够预测跨种子块的留出响应偏差。

But their apparent 16.1% advantage at matching checkpoints by future response does not separate from a conditional placebo preserving each descriptor's fitted dependence on the conventional observables, and a calibrated positive control makes simple insensitivity an unlikely explanation.

然而，它们在通过未来响应匹配检查点时表现出的 16.1% 的优势，无法与保留每个描述符对传统可观测变量拟合依赖性的条件安慰剂区分开来，且经过校准的阳性对照使得简单的“不敏感”解释不太可能成立。

Information a decoder can extract need not be organised in the pairwise geometry that similarity judgments require.

解码器能够提取的信息，未必需要以相似性判断所要求的成对几何结构进行组织。
