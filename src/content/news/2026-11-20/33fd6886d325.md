Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.23477 (cs) [Submitted on 17 Nov 2026] Title: mmTherm: Absolute 3D Human Pose Estimation from Low-Resolution Thermal Images and mmWave Radar Authors: Zhaoxin Chang, Badii Jouaber, Daqing Zhang.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.23477 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 17 日] 标题：mmTherm：基于低分辨率热成像与毫米波雷达的绝对 3D 人体姿态估计 作者：Zhaoxin Chang, Badii Jouaber, Daqing Zhang。

Abstract: Absolute 3D human pose estimation (HPE) aims to recover both a person's body pose and their global position in 3D space. Privacy-preserving millimeter-wave (mmWave) radar provides distance and direction measurement capabilities, but its sparse point clouds make HPE under-constrained.

摘要：绝对 3D 人体姿态估计 (HPE) 旨在恢复人体在 3D 空间中的身体姿态及其全局位置。具有隐私保护功能的毫米波 (mmWave) 雷达提供了距离和方向测量能力，但其稀疏的点云数据使得 HPE 处于欠约束状态。

Low-resolution thermal imaging can provide complementary observations of body direction and structure. However, our evaluation shows that a strong feature-level fusion baseline loses much of its localization and HPE advantage when generalizing to unseen subjects, with radial localization becoming less accurate than a radar-only baseline.

低分辨率热成像可以提供关于身体方向和结构的补充观测信息。然而，我们的评估表明，当推广到未见过的对象时，一种强大的特征级融合基准模型会丧失大部分定位和 HPE 优势，其径向定位精度甚至低于仅使用雷达的基准模型。

Motivated by this finding, we introduce mmTherm, a lightweight framework that explicitly incorporates the complementary geometric properties of thermal imaging and mmWave radar. It combines body structure and joint directions estimated from thermal images with range information from radar to localize the body and reconstruct its 3D pose.

受此发现启发，我们引入了 mmTherm，这是一个轻量级框架，明确结合了热成像和毫米波雷达的互补几何特性。它将从热图像中估计出的身体结构和关节方向与雷达的距离信息相结合，以定位身体并重建其 3D 姿态。

Experiments on OctoNet show that mmTherm reduces cross-user absolute joint error by 33% and root localization error by 38% relative to the strongest corresponding baselines, while requiring substantially fewer parameters and less computation at inference.

在 OctoNet 上的实验表明，与最强的对应基准相比，mmTherm 将跨用户绝对关节误差降低了 33%，根节点定位误差降低了 38%，同时在推理时所需的参数量和计算量显著减少。
