In October 2025, four Black high school students were sharing Cool Ranch Doritos after football practice, winding down and waiting for their rides home. It was then, in the lull of a moment any teen with after-school activities knows well, that the school’s AI gun detection system suddenly flagged one teen holding a bag of chips as a potential gunman.

2025年10月，四名黑人高中生在橄榄球训练结束后分享“酷爽牧场”味多力多滋薯片，他们当时正在放松并等待家人接送。就在那个任何参加课后活动的学生都非常熟悉的平静时刻，学校的人工智能枪支检测系统突然将一名拿着薯片袋的学生标记为潜在的持枪歹徒。

Within two minutes, Baltimore County Public Schools officials who reviewed security camera footage linked to the alert determined there was no actual threat. They recognized the Doritos bag and canceled the alert, which notified not only other school officials but also the Baltimore County Police Department that no response was needed. The false alert could have ended there—before a traumatizing campus clash with police—but it didn’t.

在两分钟内，审查了与警报相关联的监控录像的巴尔的摩县公立学校官员确定并没有实际威胁。他们认出了那个多力多滋袋子并取消了警报，该操作不仅通知了其他学校官员，还告知巴尔的摩县警察局无需出警。这场虚假警报本可以在此结束——在与警察发生令人创伤的校园冲突之前——但事实并非如此。

According to a lawsuit filed by the current and former students, Taki Allen, Yui-Osa Ogbeide, Devon Thompson Jr., and Keith Winfield III, due to “both technological and human errors,” school officials called police to the scene anyway, and the cops responded with force. At least six patrol cars arrived with lights flashing, and some cops emerged with guns drawn. The teens froze and cooperated, confused and scared for their lives, their lawsuit said.

根据现任及前任学生塔基·艾伦（Taki Allen）、尤伊-奥萨·奥格贝德（Yui-Osa Ogbeide）、德文·汤普森（Devon Thompson Jr.）和基思·温菲尔德三世（Keith Winfield III）提起的诉讼，由于“技术和人为的双重错误”，学校官员还是叫来了警察，而警察则采取了武力应对。至少有六辆闪烁着警灯的巡逻车抵达现场，一些警察拔枪而出。诉讼称，这些学生当时僵在原地并配合调查，他们感到困惑且因生命受到威胁而恐惧。

“No one told the students what they were suspected of,” the lawsuit said. “Officers separated the students, ordered them to their knees on the concrete, handcuffed them, and searched their persons and property.” Only after the cops failed to find a gun did students learn that it was the school’s AI gun detection system, Omnilert, that triggered the police response.

诉讼称：“没有人告诉学生他们因何受到怀疑。警察将学生们分开，命令他们跪在水泥地上，给他们戴上手铐，并搜查了他们的身体和财物。”直到警察没能搜出枪支，学生们才知道是学校的人工智能枪支检测系统Omnilert触发了警方的行动。

Allegedly, one of the responding officers muttered, “stupid AI cameras” and “fucking Omnilerts,” while another told the students that “AI is not the best.” Finally releasing the students, one officer seemed to acknowledge that they had put the teens in danger. “Hey, thanks for not running away, that would have been a real problem,” the officer allegedly said. Walking away, another joked that the boys should remember to “be careful with your chip bags.”

据称，其中一名出警警察嘟囔着“愚蠢的AI摄像头”和“该死的Omnilert”，而另一名警察则告诉学生“AI并不是最好的”。在最终释放学生时，一名警察似乎承认他们让这些青少年陷入了危险。据称该警察说：“嘿，谢谢你们没跑，不然那会是个大麻烦。”在离开时，另一名警察开玩笑说，这些男孩应该记住“小心你们的薯片袋”。

Teens traumatized. The cops did not just target the suspected gunman but apparently told the other teens that they were guilty “by association” when they asked why they were searched. Suing to stop any other teens from facing allegedly life-or-death situations due to officials’ blind trust of AI, the teens have asked for an injunction barring BCPS from using Omnilert until they can effectively establish protocols to prevent police responses to false alerts.

青少年受到创伤。警察不仅针对那名被怀疑持枪的学生，当其他学生询问为何搜查他们时，警察显然还称他们“因关联”而有罪。为了阻止其他青少年因官员对人工智能的盲目信任而面临所谓的生死关头，这些学生请求法院下达禁令，禁止巴尔的摩县公立学校（BCPS）使用Omnilert，直到他们能有效建立防止虚假警报导致警方出警的规程。

The lawsuit alleged that students’ constitutional rights were violated, as students were unlawfully searched and detained. Damon Hewitt, president and executive director of the Lawyers’ Committee for Civil Rights Under Law—a nonpartisan nonprofit that’s representing the teens in the suit—said that “overreliance on artificial intelligence technology mixed with inept communication and coordination among key officials is a toxic mix.”

诉讼指控学生的宪法权利受到侵犯，因为他们遭到了非法搜查和拘留。代表这些学生提起诉讼的无党派非营利组织“法律民权委员会”主席兼执行董事达蒙·休伊特（Damon Hewitt）表示：“过度依赖人工智能技术，再加上关键官员之间笨拙的沟通与协调，这是一种有毒的组合。”

The complaint noted that BCPS was among the earliest adopters of Omnilert, and officials have touted it as “the largest deployment of AI visual gun detection” as a means to supposedly protect students. Instead, they alleged that Omnilert is marketing a defective product as triggering “near-zero false positives” without effectively requiring the adequate human review that claim depends on.

诉状指出，BCPS是Omnilert最早的采用者之一，官员们曾吹嘘这是“最大规模的人工智能视觉枪支检测部署”，以此作为保护学生的手段。然而，他们指控Omnilert在推销一种有缺陷的产品时，声称其触发“近乎零的误报”，却并未有效要求实现该声明所依赖的充分人工审查。

“We need a reckoning on how AI is being used, especially concerning public safety and kids,” Hewitt said. “We are fighting to deliver justice in this case, but it also holds lessons for educators and police officials across the country who should think twice before putting our children’s fate in the hands of AI.”

休伊特说：“我们需要对人工智能的使用方式进行清算，特别是在涉及公共安全和儿童的问题上。我们正在努力为本案伸张正义，但这同时也给全国的教育工作者和警务官员敲响了警钟，他们在将我们孩子的命运交给人工智能之前应该三思而后行。”

All the teens feel less safe on campus and less trusting of police following the incident. They also suffered from physical pain—including ”deep red marks” on their wrists from handcuffs—as well as “acute and lasting emotional distress, fear, anxiety,” and “humiliation.” Further, the teens report that rumors follow them around, falsely claiming that there was a gun and damaging their reputations.

事件发生后，所有学生都感到校园不再安全，对警察的信任度也降低了。他们还遭受了身体上的痛苦——包括手铐在手腕上留下的“深红色印记”——以及“剧烈且持久的情绪困扰、恐惧、焦虑”和“羞辱感”。此外，学生们反映流言蜚语一直困扰着他们，虚假地声称当时有枪，损害了他们的名誉。

Car pick-up will never be the same. The student falsely flagged as the gunman, Taki Allen, “no longer feels safe waiting for rides or spending time outside the school after hours” and “frequently pays for rides home from private car services to avoid waiting for rides from friends or family.” Keith Winfield’s house was very close to the school, but his mother, Frieda Winfield, said his anxiety was so bad that he would call her “multiple times after football practice ended to confirm that she was on her way to pick him up.”

乘车接送再也回不到从前了。被错误标记为持枪者的学生塔基·艾伦“不再觉得在放学后等待接送或在校外逗留是安全的”，并且“经常花钱乘坐私人车辆回家，以避免等待朋友或家人的接送”。基思·温菲尔德的家离学校很近，但他的母亲弗里达·温菲尔德（Frieda Winfield）说，他的焦虑非常严重，以至于他在橄榄球训练结束后会“多次打电话给她，确认她是否已经在接他的路上”。

Frieda Winfield called for change following her son Keith’s chilling interaction. “The police told my son this could have been a real problem,” Frieda Winfield said. “No child in Baltimore County should face this kind of danger while waiting outside his own school for a ride home from his mother.”

在儿子基思经历了那次令人不寒而栗的遭遇后，弗里达·温菲尔德呼吁做出改变。弗里达·温菲尔德说：“警察告诉我儿子，这本可能成为一个真正的大麻烦。巴尔的摩县的任何孩子都不应该在自家学校外等待母亲接回家时，面临这种危险。”

What went wrong? The teens’ lawsuit targets Omnilert and responding Baltimore County Police Department officers. It also challenges Baltimore County Public Schools and individual school officials who allegedly failed to establish proper protocols to prevent a police response to a canceled alert.

出了什么问题？这些学生的诉讼针对的是Omnilert和出警的巴尔的摩县警察局警官。诉讼还质疑了巴尔的摩县公立学校及相关学校官员，指控他们未能建立适当的规程来防止在警报已取消的情况下仍导致警方出警。

In a later investigation, the Office of the Inspector General (OIG) for Education concluded that Omnilert’s system had no “bias.” But OIG acknowledged that the “delegation of authority” process that Kenwood High School set up didn’t stop its principal, Kate Smith, from noticing the alert, failing to see it was canceled, and texting a school resource officer to deploy police 15 minutes later—all while acknowledging in that text that the object in the alert was “maybe not” a gun.
