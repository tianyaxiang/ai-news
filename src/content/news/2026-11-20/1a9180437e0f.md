Meta now sells 3D movies on Quest VR headsets

Meta 公司现已在 Quest VR 头显上销售 3D 电影。

Use your Quest 3 to catch films like Avatar and Jurassic World Rebirth in 3D.

使用你的 Quest 3 头显，即可观看《阿凡达》和《侏罗纪世界：重生》等 3D 电影。

Meta now offers more than 150 3D movies that you can buy or rent to watch in VR on its Quest 3 and 3S headsets, the company announced today. The movies are available in Meta’s TV app and include titles like Avatar, Spider-Man: Far From Home, Jurassic World Rebirth, Wicked: For Good, and a Billie Eilish concert film co-directed by Eilish and James Cameron.

Meta 公司今日宣布，目前已提供超过 150 部 3D 电影，用户可在 Quest 3 和 3S 头显上购买或租赁并在 VR 环境中观看。这些电影可在 Meta 的 TV 应用中获取，片单包括《阿凡达》、《蜘蛛侠：英雄远征》、《侏罗纪世界：重生》、《魔法坏女巫：善行》以及由比莉·艾利什（Billie Eilish）与詹姆斯·卡梅隆共同执导的演唱会电影。

The company suggests watching movies in a Theater view that’s rolling out with a new Meta Quest update. Theater view “provides a big screen and dims your surroundings to create a cozy, personal cinema experience,” Meta says.

该公司建议用户通过随 Meta Quest 新更新推出的“剧院模式”（Theater view）观看电影。Meta 表示，剧院模式“提供了一个大屏幕，并会调暗周围环境，从而营造出舒适的个人影院体验”。

Meta promises that the library will “keep growing, with thousands of titles and international availability coming” when it launches its VR Glasses next spring. If you’re a Disney Plus subscriber, you can also watch 3D movies on Meta Quest hardware in the Disney Plus app thanks to an update rolled out earlier this month.

Meta 承诺，随着明年春天 VR 眼镜的发布，其电影库将“持续增长，届时将提供数千部影片并覆盖更多国际市场”。如果你是 Disney Plus 的订阅用户，得益于本月初推出的更新，你也可以在 Disney Plus 应用中通过 Meta Quest 硬件观看 3D 电影。
