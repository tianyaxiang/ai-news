A scanning electron micrograph of pancreatic cancer cells. A virus has stopped pancreatic cancer in its tracks in three people in a clinical trial in the US. Further evaluation is needed in larger trials, but the early results are encouraging, especially since only small doses of the virus were administered for initial safety testing.

胰腺癌细胞的扫描电子显微镜图像。在美国的一项临床试验中，一种病毒成功阻止了三名患者的胰腺癌进展。尽管还需要在更大规模的试验中进行进一步评估，但初步结果令人鼓舞，特别是因为在最初的安全性测试中仅使用了小剂量的病毒。

“We only injected one-tenth of the dose we are eventually aiming at, so the efficacy is better than I expected, especially as this is pancreatic cancer,” says Masato Yamamoto at the University of Minnesota, who led the development of the viral treatment.

“我们注射的剂量仅为最终目标剂量的十分之一，因此疗效比我预期的要好，尤其是考虑到这是胰腺癌，”领导该病毒疗法开发的明尼苏达大学的山本正人（Masato Yamamoto）表示。

Pancreatic cancer is notorious for being the deadliest type of the condition. One reason is that symptoms tend to appear late, when the cancer has already spread and cannot be surgically removed. Once diagnosed, people usually live only around three to six months.

胰腺癌以其极高的致死率而闻名。原因之一是症状往往出现较晚，此时癌症已经扩散且无法通过手术切除。一旦确诊，患者通常只能存活三到六个月左右。

Another reason is that pancreatic tumours have tough, fibrous exteriors that block chemotherapy drugs from getting in. “They’re hard as hockey pucks,” says Yamamoto. Immunotherapies that boost immune activity against cancer are also ineffective, because pancreatic tumours can hide from the immune system.

另一个原因是胰腺肿瘤具有坚硬的纤维外壳，阻碍了化疗药物的进入。“它们像冰球一样硬，”山本说。旨在增强抗癌免疫活性的免疫疗法也往往无效，因为胰腺肿瘤能够躲避免疫系统。

The first patient in the trial, who had a pancreatic tumour 7 centimetres across, was administered the treatment a year ago, and the other two have been since then. At the time, their tumours hadn’t spread beyond the pancreas. Since being treated, their tumours haven’t grown any further.

该试验的第一位患者在一年前接受了治疗，当时其胰腺肿瘤直径为7厘米，另外两名患者随后也接受了治疗。当时，他们的肿瘤尚未扩散到胰腺之外。自接受治疗以来，他们的肿瘤没有进一步生长。

“They are all still alive and have clinically stable disease,” says Yamamoto, who presented the results at the annual meeting of the American Society of Gene and Cell Therapy in Boston, Massachusetts, earlier this month. Another 15 patients will now be given higher doses to find the optimum level.

“他们目前都还活着，病情在临床上保持稳定，”山本在本月初于马萨诸塞州波士顿举行的美国基因与细胞治疗学会年会上展示了这些结果。接下来，另外15名患者将接受更高剂量的治疗，以确定最佳剂量水平。

“I think this is an interesting early signal, but as a pancreatic surgeon, I think it’s important to keep perspective,” says Kai Brown at Royal North Shore Hospital in Sydney. “The history of oncology is littered with promising early signals that vanished by the time rigorous phase III [late-stage] testing was done, so I think these preliminary conference results should probably just be seen as hypothesis-generating at this stage.”

“我认为这是一个有趣的早期信号，但作为一名胰腺外科医生，我认为保持客观很重要，”悉尼皇家北岸医院的凯·布朗（Kai Brown）表示。“肿瘤学史上充斥着许多早期看起来很有希望、但在进行严格的第三阶段（后期）测试时却消失的案例，所以我认为这些初步的会议结果在现阶段只能被视为假设的提出。”

He also notes that the trial so far hasn’t included a control group, so it is hard to know whether the cancer-killing virus works better than other treatments or none at all.

他还指出，该试验目前尚未设置对照组，因此很难判断这种抗癌病毒是否比其他疗法更有效，或者是否比不治疗更好。

The virus is an adenovirus that has been genetically engineered to replicate only inside tumours and not healthy tissue. Its replication is specifically activated by an enzyme called cyclooxygenase-2 (COX-2), which is present at much higher levels in cancer cells than in normal ones.

这种病毒是一种经过基因改造的腺病毒，仅在肿瘤内部复制，而不影响健康组织。其复制过程由一种名为环氧合酶-2（COX-2）的酶专门激活，该酶在癌细胞中的含量远高于正常细胞。

After cancer cells become infected with the virus, they burst open and die, releasing more virus that can infect neighbouring cancer cells. In the trial, the virus was injected directly into the patients’ tumours through a thin tube that was guided down a patient’s throat to their pancreas. The tube had an ultrasound probe on the end to allow visualisation of the tumours.

癌细胞感染病毒后会破裂死亡，释放出更多的病毒去感染邻近的癌细胞。在试验中，病毒通过一根细管直接注射到患者的肿瘤中，细管经由患者喉部引导至胰腺。管端装有超声探头，以便观察肿瘤。

The reason the tumours have stopped growing but haven’t become any smaller may be due to the low treatment dose, says Yamamoto. He thinks they may start to shrink as the virus has more time to replicate. As the tumour cells start to break apart and leak their contents, the immune system may also be able to recognise and fight the cancer, says Yamamoto.

山本认为，肿瘤停止生长但没有缩小，可能是因为治疗剂量较低。他认为随着病毒有更多时间进行复制，肿瘤可能会开始缩小。山本表示，当肿瘤细胞开始破裂并泄漏其内容物时，免疫系统也可能能够识别并对抗癌症。

“The patient’s immune system can realise something is wrong, and then go and attack the tumour,” he says. The hope is that the immune system will also recognise and destroy any tumour cells that have spread to other parts of the body. If so, the treatment could be effective against metastatic pancreatic cancer.

“患者的免疫系统能够意识到出了问题，进而去攻击肿瘤，”他说。研究人员希望免疫系统也能识别并摧毁扩散到身体其他部位的肿瘤细胞。如果是这样，这种疗法可能对转移性胰腺癌有效。

To try to supercharge this natural immune response, Yamamoto and his colleagues are now planning to combine the viral treatment with immunotherapies like checkpoint inhibitors – drugs that block proteins that stop the immune system from attacking the cancer cells – in future clinical trials.

为了尝试增强这种天然免疫反应，山本及其同事正计划在未来的临床试验中将这种病毒疗法与免疫疗法（如检查点抑制剂）相结合，这些药物可以阻断那些阻止免疫系统攻击癌细胞的蛋白质。

Adenoviruses, which cause cold- and flu-like symptoms in their unmodified forms, have a long history of being investigated as potential cancer treatments. In the 1950s, for instance, women with cervical cancer were injected with an unmodified adenovirus in a clinical trial, with partial success. However, it became clear that adenoviruses must be engineered to make them selectively target cancer cells, for safety and efficacy reasons.

腺病毒在未修饰状态下会引起类似感冒和流感的症状，作为潜在的癌症疗法，它有着悠久的研究历史。例如，在20世纪50年代，一项临床试验曾为宫颈癌患者注射未修饰的腺病毒，并取得了一定成功。然而，出于安全和疗效的考虑，人们逐渐明确腺病毒必须经过改造，才能使其选择性地靶向癌细胞。

The only cancer-killing virus that has been approved by the Food and Drug Administration in the US is T-VEC, a genetically modified herpes simplex virus that is injected directly into melanoma tumours, causing the cells to rupture and die.

目前唯一获得美国食品药品监督管理局（FDA）批准的抗癌病毒是T-VEC，这是一种经过基因改造的单纯疱疹病毒，直接注射到黑色素瘤中，导致细胞破裂死亡。
