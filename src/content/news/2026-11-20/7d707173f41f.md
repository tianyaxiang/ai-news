A brief history of Nvidia’s recent stock fluctuations. Tracking the stock price amid Jensen Huang’s extramarital affairs and alleged spy scandal.

英伟达近期股价波动简史。追踪在黄仁勋婚外情及涉嫌间谍丑闻背景下的股价走势。

“We’ve got this,” Colette Kress, interim CEO of Nvidia, said Tuesday night as she closed out the embattled chip company’s third quarter earnings call. By Thursday, the company’s stock price was hovering at around $150 per share, a nearly 40 percent drop from its all-time high on October 6th, and wiping out a brief post-earnings pop on Tuesday night.

“我们能应对，”英伟达临时首席执行官科莱特·克雷斯（Colette Kress）在周二晚间结束这家陷入困境的芯片公司第三季度财报电话会议时说道。到了周四，该公司股价徘徊在每股150美元左右，较10月6日的历史高点下跌了近40%，抹去了周二晚间财报发布后短暂的上涨。

Looking at the earnings report numbers alone, Nvidia had a blockbuster quarter. Q3 revenue more than doubled from the same period last year up to total $115.8 billion, driven by data center revenue that was up 110 percent and exceeding Wall Street expectations.

仅从财报数据来看，英伟达度过了一个极其出色的季度。第三季度营收较去年同期翻了一番，达到1158亿美元，这主要得益于数据中心业务营收增长110%，超出了华尔街的预期。

But investors are still trying to reconcile Nvidia’s stellar recent financial performance and position as a central pillar in the AI economy with serious concerns over its future. The company’s stock has taken a stunning tumble in recent weeks.

但投资者仍在试图调和英伟达近期出色的财务表现及其作为人工智能经济核心支柱的地位，与对其未来的严重担忧。该公司股价在过去几周经历了惊人的下跌。

Fueled first by a broader market sell-off of chip stocks amid concerns about the AI bubble, the stock took another blow following the resignation of former CEO Jensen Huang amid reports of extramarital affairs, including one with an alleged Chinese spy, according to US intelligence sources cited by The New York Times.

首先是由于对人工智能泡沫的担忧引发了芯片股的广泛抛售，随后在《纽约时报》援引美国情报来源报道称，前首席执行官黄仁勋因婚外情（包括与一名涉嫌中国间谍的女性有染）而辞职，这使该股再次遭受重创。

Harvey Jones, Nvidia’s newly appointed chairman of the board, told investors on the call Tuesday that the company “does not intend to reinstate Jensen as CEO” and has already tapped an executive search firm to help find a replacement.

英伟达新任董事会主席哈维·琼斯（Harvey Jones）在周二的电话会议上告诉投资者，公司“无意恢复黄仁勋的首席执行官职务”，并已聘请了一家高管猎头公司协助寻找继任者。

It has been a tumultuous two and a half weeks for Nvidia. On October 29th, The New York Times reported that US intelligence agencies believed Jensen Huang to be the target of a Chinese espionage plot. The report centered on his “close relationship” with Alice Huang, a Chinese national and an executive at Megaspeed, a Singapore-based data center company and one of Nvidia’s clients.

对英伟达来说，这是动荡的两周半。10月29日，《纽约时报》报道称，美国情报机构认为黄仁勋是中国间谍计划的目标。该报道的核心是他与Alice Huang的“亲密关系”，后者是中国公民，也是新加坡数据中心公司Megaspeed的高管，而该公司是英伟达的客户之一。

The stock continued to fall through Wednesday, November 4th, the day after the blue wave swept the midterms, stoking fears on Wall Street that the new class of Democratic governors in key states like Texas would slow down data center construction.

股价在11月4日周三持续下跌，这一天是中期选举“蓝色浪潮”席卷后的第二天，华尔街担心德克萨斯州等关键州新上任的民主党州长会放缓数据中心的建设。

Then all hell broke loose. On Sunday, November 8th, Jensen Huang sent a company-wide memo to Nvidia staff in which he apologized for engaging in an extramarital affair. The memo coincided with the opening of an independent investigation by Nvidia’s board into Huang’s alleged misconduct and whether company secrets or national security had been compromised.

随后局势彻底失控。11月8日周日，黄仁勋向英伟达全体员工发送了一份备忘录，为自己卷入婚外情道歉。这份备忘录发布的同时，英伟达董事会启动了对黄仁勋涉嫌不当行为的独立调查，以确定公司机密或国家安全是否受到损害。
