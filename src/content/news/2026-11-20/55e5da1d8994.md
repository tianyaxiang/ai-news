Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.23484 (cs) [Submitted on 17 Nov 2026] Title: Reference-Guided Data Curation for 3D Medical Vision-Language Pretraining Authors: Faidra Patsatzi, Ashwin Kumar, Chong Wang, Curtis P. Langlotz, Reinhard Heckel, Akshay S. Chaudhari.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.23484 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 17 日] 标题：用于 3D 医学视觉语言预训练的参考引导数据整理 作者：Faidra Patsatzi, Ashwin Kumar, Chong Wang, Curtis P. Langlotz, Reinhard Heckel, Akshay S. Chaudhari。

Abstract: Training 3D vision-language models (VLMs) on large medical imaging datasets collected across institutions is constrained by data quality. Vast, uncurated pools of image-report pairs are characterized by redundancy, class imbalance, and cross-institutional heterogeneity. We address this challenge of data curation for improving 3D VLM pretraining by proposing an offline, training-free, reference-guided data curation recipe.

摘要：在跨机构收集的大型医学影像数据集上训练 3D 视觉语言模型 (VLM) 受限于数据质量。庞大且未经整理的图像-报告对集合具有冗余性、类别不平衡和跨机构异质性的特征。我们通过提出一种离线、无需训练、参考引导的数据整理方案，解决了这一用于改进 3D VLM 预训练的数据整理挑战。

Our approach leverages a small, high-quality reference set to independently extract quality signals for each modality of a large, uncurated pool and merges them through a rank-aggregation step into a single ranking that guides sample selection. We validate this method on datasets comprising 35k 3D abdominal CT volume-report pairs from two institutions and 58k 2D chest X-ray image-report pairs.

我们的方法利用一个小规模的高质量参考集，独立提取大型未整理池中每种模态的质量信号，并通过排名聚合步骤将其合并为单一排名，从而指导样本选择。我们在包含来自两个机构的 3.5 万个 3D 腹部 CT 体积-报告对和 5.8 万个 2D 胸部 X 光图像-报告对的数据集上验证了该方法。

On 3D CT, using our reference-guided data curation strategy, our curated model matches the zero-shot disease classification performance of a state-of-the-art baseline (Merlin) while using 3.3× less training data and a reference set of 5k samples. On 2D chest X-rays, our method yields a curated model that matches or exceeds the zero-shot disease classification performance of a model trained on the full pool while using up to 10× less training data and a reference set of only 74 samples.

在 3D CT 上，使用我们的参考引导数据整理策略，我们的整理模型在仅使用 1/3.3 的训练数据和 5000 个样本的参考集的情况下，达到了最先进基线 (Merlin) 的零样本疾病分类性能。在 2D 胸部 X 光片上，我们的方法产生的整理模型在仅使用 1/10 的训练数据和仅 74 个样本的参考集的情况下，匹配或超过了在完整数据池上训练的模型的零样本疾病分类性能。

Ablations show that the selection is stable under resampling of the reference, that smaller reference sets still outperform random sampling, and that label rebalancing does not improve performance. To support reproducibility, we release our code.

消融实验表明，该选择在参考集重采样下是稳定的，较小的参考集仍然优于随机采样，且标签重平衡并不能提高性能。为了支持可重复性，我们发布了代码。
