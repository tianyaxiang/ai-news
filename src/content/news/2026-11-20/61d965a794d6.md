Back in my day, the only “Doorbuster” sales were at physical retail stores. But now, you can get ultra-low prices before Black Friday whether you want to shop in-store or online. One that stands out is the previous-generation Sony WH-1000XM5 noise-canceling headphones, which are marked down to their lowest price ever, just $169.99 at Best Buy. While their MSRP is $399.99, they’ve been spotted as low as $198 in the last few months.

在过去，只有实体零售店才有“开门大抢购”活动。但现在，无论您想在店内还是在线购物，都能在“黑色星期五”之前享受到超低价格。其中最引人注目的是上一代索尼 WH-1000XM5 降噪耳机，其价格已降至历史最低点，在百思买（Best Buy）仅售 169.99 美元。虽然其建议零售价为 399.99 美元，但在过去几个月里，它们曾出现过低至 198 美元的价格。

The WH-1000XM5 launched in 2022, and despite having been succeeded by the WH-1000XM6, they’re still an excellent set of headphones. Their active noise cancellation effect is one of the best out there — competitive with the latest models from Bose and Apple — and the sound profile is tighter and more balanced than the older Sony headphones. There are some minor downsides, like a bulky case and all-plastic design, but those seem easier to overlook at this lower price point.

WH-1000XM5 于 2022 年推出，尽管已被 WH-1000XM6 取代，但它们仍然是一套出色的耳机。它们的主动降噪效果是目前市面上最好的耳机之一，足以与 Bose 和苹果的最新型号相媲美，而且其声音表现比老款索尼耳机更紧凑、更平衡。虽然存在一些小缺点，比如外壳笨重和全塑料设计，但在目前这个较低的价格点上，这些似乎更容易被忽略。

The newer Sony WH-1000XM6 improve the sound and noise cancellation, with quality of life upgrades not present in the XM5, like a folding design and more comfortable headband. However, they’re significantly more expensive, currently discounted to $348 during Black Friday (Amazon, Best Buy), down from their MSRP of $450.

新款索尼 WH-1000XM6 改进了音质和降噪效果，并具备 XM5 所没有的舒适度升级，例如可折叠设计和更舒适的头带。然而，它们的价格要贵得多，在“黑色星期五”期间目前的折扣价为 348 美元（亚马逊、百思买），低于其 450 美元的建议零售价。

To make shopping a little more confusing, the newer WH-1000XM4C sit between the XM5 and XM6 in terms of price, and are a slightly updated version of the older XM4 model with its foldable design. Our reviewer John Higgins thinks the XM4C’s noise cancellation lags behind, so I’d opt for the XM5 over them, especially at the sale price.

为了让购物变得更令人困惑，新款 WH-1000XM4C 在价格上介于 XM5 和 XM6 之间，它是老款 XM4 型号的略微更新版本，保留了可折叠设计。我们的评论员 John Higgins 认为 XM4C 的降噪能力稍显落后，所以我更倾向于选择 XM5，尤其是在当前促销价格的情况下。
