### For the Home Theater

### 家庭影院类

**The Best OLED TV: Samsung S95H QD-OLED TV**
Quite simply the best OLED TV we've tested, the Samsung S95H is bright, clear, and colorful, with outstanding picture quality and AI optimizations that eke out as much detail as possible. It's also a looker, with a silver bezel around the screen that gives the illusion of a metal picture frame. It comes in sizes ranging from 55 inches to 83 inches.

**最佳 OLED 电视：三星 S95H QD-OLED 电视**
三星 S95H 无疑是我们测试过的最好的 OLED 电视。它明亮、清晰、色彩丰富，拥有出色的画质和人工智能优化功能，能尽可能地呈现细节。它的外观也非常迷人，屏幕周围的银色边框营造出一种金属相框的视觉效果。它提供从 55 英寸到 83 英寸不等的多种尺寸。

**Paired With the Best Sound Systems: Sonos Arc Ultra**
It's the best soundbar you can get, as well as the best all-in-one home theater audio solution. Everything from the unboxing down to the texture of the bar itself is premium with the Sonos Arc Ultra. And most importantly, its sound is stunning, especially considering it's slim footprint.

**搭配最佳音响系统：Sonos Arc Ultra**
这是你能买到的最好的条形音箱，也是最佳的一体化家庭影院音频解决方案。从开箱体验到音箱本身的质感，Sonos Arc Ultra 的一切都显得高端大气。最重要的是，它的音质令人惊叹，尤其是考虑到它纤薄的体积。

**The Best Projector for Movie Night: Leica Cine Play 1**
A premium home theater projector can make movies look bigger and better than even some of the best TVs. This Leica Cine Play 1 is a perfect example. Colors pop with vivid clarity, and you can project a 65- to 300-inch image—big enough for even the largest of parties.

**电影之夜的最佳投影仪：徕卡 Cine Play 1**
高端家庭影院投影仪能让电影看起来比一些顶级电视更大、更震撼。这款徕卡 Cine Play 1 就是一个完美的例子。色彩鲜艳清晰，你可以投射出 65 到 300 英寸的画面，足以满足最大规模的聚会需求。

**Bookshelf Speakers That Do It All: KEF LSX II**
Bookshelf speakers have a certain je ne sais quoi about them that other speakers can only dream of. The KEF LSX II have that classy bookshelf look with seriously modern smarts inside. You can plug these into your TV, turntable, and additional subwoofers, or forego cables entirely and use them via Wi-Fi or Bluetooth.

**全能书架音箱：KEF LSX II**
书架音箱有一种其他音箱无法企及的独特魅力。KEF LSX II 既拥有经典的书架音箱外观，内部又蕴含着极其现代的智能科技。你可以将它们连接到电视、唱机和额外的低音炮上，或者完全抛弃线缆，通过 Wi-Fi 或蓝牙使用。

**Big Screen Energy: LG Micro RGB Evo**
Want a 100-inch TV that will truly impress? You need an RGB TV like the LG Micro RGB Evo above. RGB is the latest innovation to hit TV screens, with vibrant and bold colors you'd be hard-pressed to find elsewhere.

**大屏能量：LG Micro RGB Evo**
想要一台真正令人印象深刻的 100 英寸电视吗？你需要像 LG Micro RGB Evo 这样的 RGB 电视。RGB 是电视屏幕领域的最新创新，它能呈现出你在其他地方很难找到的鲜艳而大胆的色彩。

**Nanoleaf 4D Kit V1**
Light can add a new level of immersion to any home theater. The Nanoleaf 4D Kit did just that for WIRED reviewer Nena Farrell. “I watched Return of the King with 4D lighting, and I never want to go back.”

**Nanoleaf 4D 套件 V1**
灯光可以为任何家庭影院增添全新的沉浸感。Nanoleaf 4D 套件就为《连线》评论员 Nena Farrell 带来了这种体验。“我用 4D 灯光观看了《王者归来》，从此再也回不去了。”

**Keep It Cozy: Bearaby Cotton Napper Weighted Blanket & Cocoon Classic Blanket**
This weighted blanket comes recommended by WIRED reviewer Molly Higgins, and it makes any movie night instantly cozy. On the opposite end of the spectrum, the Cocoon Classic Blanket is like being surrounded by a cloud, says Higgins. “I promise this gift will be fought over at the next movie night.”

**保持舒适：Bearaby 棉质加重毯与 Cocoon 经典毯**
这款加重毯得到了《连线》评论员 Molly Higgins 的推荐，它能让任何电影之夜瞬间变得舒适惬意。而在另一端，Higgins 称 Cocoon 经典毯就像被云朵包围一样，“我保证，在下次电影之夜，大家一定会抢着要这份礼物。”

### For Whole-Home Entertainment

### 全屋娱乐类

**A Pop of Color: Audioengine Color of the Year Kit**
Entertainment equipment doesn't have to be drab blocks of black or white. The Audioengine Color of the Year kit comes with a turntable, bookshelf speakers, and a subwoofer, all in matching bright yellow. It's a cinch to set up and a great way to add music and color to any room.

**一抹亮色：Audioengine 年度色彩套装**
娱乐设备不必非得是沉闷的黑白方块。Audioengine 年度色彩套装包含唱机、书架音箱和低音炮，全部采用统一的亮黄色。它安装简单，是为任何房间增添音乐与色彩的绝佳方式。

**The Vinyl Touch: Fluance RT81+ & Fluance Ri71 Reference Bookshelf Speakers**
Entertainment isn't restricted to the TV room. A great turntable makes any room a space for gathering and enjoying some music. Pair the RT81+ turntable with a matching set of these Fluance bookshelf speakers and you've got a setup that can deliver incredible clarity for everything, including vinyl.

**黑胶情怀：Fluance RT81+ 与 Fluance Ri71 参考级书架音箱**
娱乐并不局限于电视房。一台出色的唱机能让任何房间变成聚会和享受音乐的空间。将 RT81+ 唱机与配套的 Fluance 书架音箱搭配使用，你就能拥有一套能为包括黑胶唱片在内的所有音频提供极致清晰度的系统。
