Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.23442 (cs) [Submitted on 17 Nov 2026] Title: TransFIRA+: Transfer Learning for Biometric Recognizability Assessment Authors: Allen Tu, Kartik Narayan, Joshua Gleason, Jennifer Xu, Matthew Meyn, Tom Goldstein, Vishal M. Patel.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.23442 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 17 日] 标题：TransFIRA+：用于生物识别可识别性评估的迁移学习 作者：Allen Tu, Kartik Narayan, Joshua Gleason, Jennifer Xu, Matthew Meyn, Tom Goldstein, Vishal M. Patel。

Abstract: Biometric recognition in surveillance, video, and web imagery must contend with extreme variation in pose, blur, illumination, occlusion, and distance, where visual quality metrics fail to predict whether inputs are recognizable to the deployed encoder. Existing face image quality assessment methods rely on visual heuristics, curated annotations, or costly generative pipelines, leaving their predictions detached from the encoder's decision geometry.

摘要：监控、视频和网络图像中的生物识别必须应对姿态、模糊、光照、遮挡和距离方面的极端变化，而视觉质量指标往往无法预测输入内容对于已部署的编码器是否可识别。现有的面部图像质量评估方法依赖于视觉启发式算法、精选标注或昂贵的生成式流程，导致其预测结果与编码器的决策几何结构脱节。

We introduce TransFIRA, a lightweight framework that grounds recognizability directly in embedding space. TransFIRA derives class-center similarity (CCS) and class-center angular separation (CCAS) from a pretrained encoder's embeddings and learns to predict them from images without quality annotations.

我们引入了 TransFIRA，这是一个直接在嵌入空间中建立可识别性基础的轻量级框架。TransFIRA 从预训练编码器的嵌入中导出类中心相似度 (CCS) 和类中心角分离度 (CCAS)，并学习在没有质量标注的情况下从图像中预测这些指标。

Using predicted CCS for template weighting and CCAS>0 as a natural filtering threshold yields state-of-the-art face verification performance on BRIAR, raising TAR at $10^{-6}$ FMR from 0.085 with uniform averaging to 0.598. The predicted scores also reveal how image degradations affect encoder-specific recognizability.

使用预测的 CCS 进行模板加权，并将 CCAS>0 作为自然过滤阈值，在 BRIAR 上实现了最先进的人脸验证性能，将 $10^{-6}$ FMR 下的 TAR 从均匀平均的 0.085 提高到了 0.598。预测的分数还揭示了图像退化如何影响特定于编码器的可识别性。

TransFIRA+ extends this framework to cross-camera multimodal person identification in untrimmed surveillance video. In this setting, body recognizability scores saturate within a narrow range, making direct weighting effectively uniform. We introduce a post-hoc sigmoid calibration that transforms these saturated scores into informative weights without retraining.

TransFIRA+ 将此框架扩展到未剪辑监控视频中的跨摄像头多模态人员识别。在这种环境下，身体可识别性分数会在一个狭窄范围内饱和，使得直接加权实际上变得均匀。我们引入了一种事后 Sigmoid 校准方法，无需重新训练即可将这些饱和分数转换为信息量大的权重。

After calibration, the BRIAR-trained TransFIRA predictor improves fused face and body identification on two unseen multi-camera datasets, outperforming detector-confidence weighting and uncalibrated CCS-rank weighting. Together, TransFIRA and TransFIRA+ demonstrate that encoder-grounded recognizability generalizes across biometric modalities and recognition settings.

校准后，经过 BRIAR 训练的 TransFIRA 预测器在两个未见过的多摄像头数据集上改进了融合的人脸和身体识别效果，优于检测器置信度加权和未经校准的 CCS 排序加权。总之，TransFIRA 和 TransFIRA+ 证明了基于编码器的可识别性可以推广到不同的生物识别模态和识别场景中。
