Google announced Android developer verification last year, and despite numerous objections from developers and users, the company is forging ahead. It began blocking the installation of unregistered apps in some countries this fall, and the system is expanding globally soon. Google says it will start rolling out the requirement in all regions in January 2027, and it urges devs who still haven’t registered their apps to do so soon.

谷歌去年宣布了安卓开发者验证计划，尽管开发者和用户提出了诸多反对意见，该公司仍执意推进。今年秋季，谷歌已开始在部分国家阻止未注册应用的安装，该系统即将推广至全球。谷歌表示，将于 2027 年 1 月在所有地区开始推行这一要求，并敦促尚未注册其应用的开发者尽快完成注册。

The apps you download from the Play Store all come from verified developers, and Google’s new requirement expands that requirement to apps from third-party sources. Once developer verification is live in your region, you won’t be able to sideload apps unless the developer has registered with Google, which is framed as a security measure. Verification requires providing government identification and paying a $25 fee, which will allegedly make it harder for nefarious actors to distribute malware and scammy apps.

你从 Play 商店下载的应用均来自经过验证的开发者，而谷歌的新规定将这一要求扩展到了第三方来源的应用。一旦开发者验证在你所在的地区生效，除非开发者已在谷歌注册，否则你将无法侧载应用，谷歌称此举是一项安全措施。验证过程需要提供政府颁发的身份证件并支付 25 美元的费用，据称这将增加不法分子分发恶意软件和诈骗应用的难度。

In September, Google launched verification in Brazil, Indonesia, Singapore, and Thailand. Apparently, nothing exploded, so the company is now ready to expand. It previously said verification would go global in 2027, and it’s not wasting any time with the January start. It says that verification will reach all devices within “several months.” So far, Google is getting what it wanted from this initiative, with “millions” of non-Play apps already registered. The company says this accounts for more than 99 percent of global Android app installs.

9 月，谷歌在巴西、印度尼西亚、新加坡和泰国启动了验证工作。显然，一切运行正常，因此该公司现在准备扩大范围。此前谷歌曾表示验证计划将于 2027 年在全球推行，而它选择在 1 月份就开始行动，可谓雷厉风行。谷歌称，验证功能将在“几个月内”覆盖所有设备。到目前为止，谷歌已通过该计划实现了预期目标，已有“数百万”个非 Play 商店应用完成了注册。该公司表示，这涵盖了全球超过 99% 的安卓应用安装量。

The verifier system service has already been rolled out to Google-certified devices running Android 8 Oreo or higher, which accounts for just about every Android device in the world outside of China and Russia. Google has also partnered with the biggest third-party app stores to enforce verification: Galaxy Store, Honor, Oppo, Xiaomi, Transsion, and Vivo. The system-level verifier is responsible for blocking installs, but these stores will only list verified apps once Google flips the switch.

验证系统服务已推送至运行 Android 8 Oreo 或更高版本的谷歌认证设备，这几乎涵盖了中国和俄罗斯以外全球所有的安卓设备。谷歌还与各大第三方应用商店合作以强制执行验证：包括 Galaxy Store、荣耀、OPPO、小米、传音和 vivo。系统级验证器负责阻止安装，但一旦谷歌开启开关，这些商店将仅列出经过验证的应用。

Feedback accepted: Google has gotten a lot of feedback since announcing this change, and it has listened to some of it. Hobbyists will be able to register a free account to distribute apps to a small group. Google initially planned to limit this to 25 total installs, but that has since been increased to 50 installs. There’s also now an API that developers can use to streamline registering new application packages.

反馈采纳：自宣布此项变更以来，谷歌收到了大量反馈，并采纳了其中部分建议。业余开发者将能够注册免费账户，向小范围群体分发应用。谷歌最初计划将总安装量限制在 25 次，但随后增加到了 50 次。此外，现在还有一个 API 可供开发者使用，以简化新应用包的注册流程。

Google also relented and created an opt-out system for users who don’t want their installations limited. The so-called “advanced flow” will let you install whatever you want regardless of verification status, but it’s not a simple toggle. The option will be buried, and there will be plenty of scary warnings about the risks. You’ll also have to wait 24 hours to fully activate the feature. This setting is already live, so you can tell your phone to ignore verification right now. You’ll have to wait 24 hours to bypass verification.

谷歌也做出了让步，为不希望安装受到限制的用户创建了一套退出机制。所谓的“高级流程”将允许你安装任何应用，无论其验证状态如何，但这并非一个简单的开关。该选项会被隐藏，并伴有大量关于风险的警告提示。你还需要等待 24 小时才能完全激活该功能。此设置现已上线，因此你现在就可以设置手机忽略验证，但仍需等待 24 小时才能绕过验证。

Even if Google has managed to make verification a little more warm and cuddly, it still gives the company a lot more control. Developers in sanctioned nations won’t be able to verify their identities, locking them out of app distribution. Sideloading an app may also require a Google server connection. Only popular app IDs will be cached on devices for verification purposes. For others, your phone will have to check with Google before installing. These will be edge cases, certainly, but still very annoying ones.

即使谷歌设法让验证过程变得稍微“温和”了一些，它仍然赋予了该公司更大的控制权。受制裁国家的开发者将无法验证身份，从而被排除在应用分发之外。侧载应用可能还需要连接谷歌服务器。只有热门应用 ID 会被缓存到设备上用于验证，对于其他应用，你的手机在安装前必须向谷歌进行核实。这当然属于极端情况，但依然非常令人困扰。

As Google moves toward full verification enforcement, it has created guides for developers and app distributors to outline all the salient points. These guides suggest Google expects verification to be fully online by March 1, 2027.

随着谷歌迈向全面强制执行验证，它已为开发者和应用分发商制定了指南，概述了所有要点。这些指南表明，谷歌预计验证系统将于 2027 年 3 月 1 日全面上线。
