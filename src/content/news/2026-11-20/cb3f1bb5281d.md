Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.23240 (cs) [Submitted on 2 Sep 2026] Title: Neural Network-Based Modeling of Hysteretic Systems: From Prandtl and Preisach Neural Networks to Neuro-Madelung and Neuroplastic Continuous Models Authors: Mehrdad Shafiei Dizaji, Farzad Shafiei Dizaji.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.23240 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 9 月 2 日] 标题：基于神经网络的滞后系统建模：从 Prandtl 和 Preisach 神经网络到神经-马德隆（Neuro-Madelung）及神经塑性连续模型 作者：Mehrdad Shafiei Dizaji, Farzad Shafiei Dizaji。

Abstract: Hysteresis is a complex nonlinear phenomenon characterized by memory dependence, path dependence, and, in many physical systems, degradation, non-congruency, asymmetry, and rate dependence. This review examines the development and capabilities of Prandtl neural networks (PNNs), Preisach neural networks (PreisachNNs), generalized Prandtl neural networks (GPNNs), the neuro-Madelung model (NMM), extended Preisach neural networks (EPNNs), and dynamic plastic continuous neural networks (DPCNNs).

摘要：滞后是一种复杂的非线性现象，其特征在于记忆依赖性、路径依赖性，以及在许多物理系统中表现出的退化、非全等性、不对称性和速率依赖性。本综述考察了 Prandtl 神经网络 (PNN)、Preisach 神经网络 (PreisachNN)、广义 Prandtl 神经网络 (GPNN)、神经-马德隆模型 (NMM)、扩展 Preisach 神经网络 (EPNN) 以及动态塑性连续神经网络 (DPCNN) 的发展与功能。

Particular attention is given to their mechanisms for representing memory, network architectures, activation functions, training algorithms, treatment of deterioration and rate dependence, and applications to nonlinear structural and material systems. The PNN introduced hysteretic memory directly into neural activation through stop operators and demonstrated accurate nonlinear dynamic analysis of structures.

文中特别关注了它们表示记忆的机制、网络架构、激活函数、训练算法、对退化和速率依赖性的处理，以及在非线性结构和材料系统中的应用。PNN 通过停止算子将滞后记忆直接引入神经激活中，并展示了对结构进行精确非线性动力学分析的能力。

Preisach-NNs subsequently extended this concept through an additional nonlinear hidden layer to represent non-Masing hysteresis. GPNNs introduced deteriorating-stop neurons to capture stiffness and strength degradation and non-congruent hysteresis. A different generalization was provided by the NMM, which combined Madelung's hysteresis rules with multilayer feedforward neural networks to represent diverse rate-independent and rate-dependent behaviors.

Preisach-NN 随后通过一个额外的非线性隐藏层扩展了这一概念，以表示非 Masing 滞后。GPNN 引入了退化停止神经元来捕捉刚度和强度退化以及非全等滞后。NMM 提供了一种不同的推广方式，它将马德隆（Madelung）滞后规则与多层前馈神经网络相结合，以表示多种与速率无关和与速率相关的行为。

These developments culminated in the EPNN, which combines deteriorating hysteretic operators, nonlinear neural mappings, and input-rate information to accommodate Masing and nonMasing, symmetric and asymmetric, congruent and noncongruent, and rate-independent and rate-dependent hysteresis.

这些发展最终形成了 EPNN，它结合了退化滞后算子、非线性神经映射和输入速率信息，以适应 Masing 和非 Masing、对称和非对称、全等和非全等，以及与速率无关和与速率相关的滞后现象。
