Computer Science > Machine Learning arXiv:2611.23241 (cs) [Submitted on 2 Sep 2026] Title: GridForge: An Open Physics-Informed Digital Twin and Benchmark Suite for UK 11 kV Underground Distribution Cables Authors: Lilliane Linnet Musoke.

计算机科学 > 机器学习 arXiv:2611.23241 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 9 月 2 日] 标题：GridForge：用于英国 11 kV 地下配电电缆的开放式物理信息数字孪生与基准测试套件 作者：Lilliane Linnet Musoke。

Abstract: The UK distribution-cable monitoring sector is data-rich but framework-poor: each Distribution Network Operator (DNO) collects megabytes of telemetry per asset per day, but there is no public benchmark dataset, no shared reference model, and no commonly agreed evaluation protocol for benchmarking sectoral, data-intensive models for operational decision support; notably for maintenance planning and prioritisation.

摘要：英国配电电缆监测行业数据丰富但缺乏框架：每个配电网络运营商 (DNO) 每天为每个资产收集数兆字节的遥测数据，但目前尚无公开的基准数据集、共享参考模型，也没有用于评估行业数据密集型模型以支持运营决策（特别是维护规划和优先级排序）的通用评估协议。

We address this gap with GridForge, an open-source physics-informed digital twin and benchmark suite for fault diagnosis and prediction, integrating four building blocks: (i) a verified steady state and transient thermal model based on IEC 60287 and IEC 60853; (ii) a calibrated synthetic dataset of 64 cables (covering 320 cable-years) representing four canonical UK 11 kV thermal archetypes and four condition modes; (iii) a Physics-Informed Neural Network (PINN) surrogate that satisfies the algebraic balance defined by IEC 60287 during training and reaches an RMSE of 0.148 °C on an independent held out set against the reference analytical oracle; and (iv) a five-task benchmark suite that includes three reference baseline models (pure physics, gradient-boosted trees, and the PINN).

我们通过 GridForge 填补了这一空白，这是一个用于故障诊断和预测的开源物理信息数字孪生与基准测试套件，集成了四个构建模块：(i) 基于 IEC 60287 和 IEC 60853 的经验证稳态和瞬态热模型；(ii) 包含 64 条电缆（覆盖 320 个电缆年）的校准合成数据集，代表了四种典型的英国 11 kV 热原型和四种状态模式；(iii) 一种物理信息神经网络 (PINN) 代理模型，在训练期间满足 IEC 60287 定义的代数平衡，并在针对参考分析预言机的独立留出集上达到 0.148 °C 的均方根误差 (RMSE)；以及 (iv) 一个包含三个参考基准模型（纯物理模型、梯度提升树和 PINN）的五任务基准测试套件。

All numerical parameters are sourced from public standards, the code is licensed under the MIT licence, and the trained PINN converges in only two minutes (as run on the computing platform in Section 10.1), enabling independent verification by DNO engineering and research practitioners.

所有数值参数均源自公共标准，代码采用 MIT 许可证授权，且训练后的 PINN 仅需两分钟即可收敛（在第 10.1 节所述的计算平台上运行），从而使 DNO 工程和研究从业者能够进行独立验证。

At present only the virtual-sensor task (T4) discriminates between the reference baselines; the other tasks tie or are undefined on this test split, so extending the baselines with task-specific logic is a priority future enhancement. For open validation, the dataset, test sets and models are made available at this https URL.

目前，只有虚拟传感器任务 (T4) 能够区分参考基准；其他任务在此测试集上表现持平或未定义，因此通过特定任务逻辑扩展基准是未来的优先改进方向。为了进行开放式验证，数据集、测试集和模型已在提供的 HTTPS 链接中发布。
