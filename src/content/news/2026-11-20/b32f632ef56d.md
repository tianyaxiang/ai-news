# Amazon fights the Delivery Protection Act with homepage banner, in-app pop-up

In an unprecedented move, Amazon is using its app and website to try to convince shoppers to not support a New York City bill. When I log into Amazon today, I’m met with a vertical banner that reads: “ATTENTION NYC Council could add up to $5 per order” with a button that says “Act now.”

亚马逊正采取前所未有的举措，利用其应用程序和网站试图说服购物者不要支持纽约市的一项法案。今天当我登录亚马逊时，看到一个垂直横幅，上面写着：“注意：纽约市议会可能会为每笔订单增加高达 5 美元的费用”，并附有一个写着“立即行动”的按钮。

I also saw a smaller version of the notice on an order confirmation page on Amazon’s website. The message is delivered as a pop-up for users of the Amazon app, Bloomberg reported. Amazon hasn’t specified whether the messaging targets NYC residents based on addresses saved in their Amazon account or uses other techniques, such as device location services, but I only see the banner if I’m logged into Amazon.

我还在亚马逊网站的订单确认页面上看到了该通知的较小版本。据彭博社报道，该消息以弹窗形式推送给亚马逊应用程序的用户。亚马逊尚未说明这些信息是基于用户账户中保存的地址来定位纽约市居民，还是使用了设备定位服务等其他技术，但我只有在登录亚马逊后才能看到该横幅。

Amazon is referring to Introduction 0518-2026 ( 518), also known as the Delivery Protection Act. If passed, it would require Amazon to directly employ the third-party delivery contractors that it uses. Clicking on “Act now” on Amazon’s homepage or in the app brings up a page that gives shoppers a way to contact their NYC Council member and further states: Right now, Amazon works with a network of small delivery businesses that are deeply embedded in your communities. If this legislation passes, we’d no longer be able to work with them, and we’d be forced to consider moving our operations outside of the city. That would likely mean slower and more expensive delivery for millions of customers in New York City, while also threatening small businesses and their thousands of employees.

亚马逊所指的是第 0518-2026 号提案（518），也称为《配送保护法》。如果该法案获得通过，将要求亚马逊直接雇佣其使用的第三方配送承包商。点击亚马逊主页或应用程序上的“立即行动”会跳转到一个页面，为购物者提供联系其纽约市议员的途径，并进一步声明：目前，亚马逊与深耕于社区的小型配送企业网络合作。如果该立法通过，我们将无法再与他们合作，并被迫考虑将业务迁出城市。这很可能意味着纽约市数百万客户的配送速度变慢且成本更高，同时也会威胁到小型企业及其数千名员工。

It’s not unusual for Amazon to take a stance on political matters, and it has publicly opposed the Delivery Protection Act since April. What is new is Amazon using its online marketplace to push a political view. It’s uncommon to see a pop-up about legislation when visiting a shopping website. The messaging suggests that Amazon is concerned about the potential impact the proposed law would have on its costs. Besides Amazon, the bill targets any company with last-mile delivery facilities in NYC, like FedEx and DoorDash.

亚马逊在政治问题上表明立场并不罕见，自四月以来，它一直公开反对《配送保护法》。新鲜的是亚马逊利用其在线市场来推行某种政治观点。在访问购物网站时看到关于立法的弹窗并不常见。这些信息表明，亚马逊担心拟议法律对其成本可能产生的影响。除亚马逊外，该法案还针对所有在纽约市拥有“最后一公里”配送设施的公司，如联邦快递（FedEx）和 DoorDash。

It also raises questions about the potential impact on shoppers should Amazon expand its use of its e-commerce platform for additional political messaging. The pop-up launched today impacts only NYC residents but sets a precedent for Amazon to sway political views on other issues. Amazon has made similar moves internally, including reportedly using an app for its employees to campaign against unionizing. But the new pop-up shows Amazon extending that strategy directly to its users. Given Amazon’s considerable influence in commerce, other companies could copy its actions. Uber and Lyft have previously done so, CNBC reports.

这也引发了人们对如果亚马逊扩大其电子商务平台用于其他政治宣传，可能对购物者产生何种影响的质疑。今天推出的弹窗仅影响纽约市居民，但为亚马逊在其他问题上左右政治观点开了先例。亚马逊在内部曾采取过类似举措，据报道包括使用应用程序让员工开展反对工会化的活动。但新的弹窗显示亚马逊正将这一策略直接延伸至其用户。鉴于亚马逊在商业领域的巨大影响力，其他公司可能会效仿其行为。据 CNBC 报道，优步（Uber）和 Lyft 此前也曾这样做过。

Finally, there’s the question of whether Amazon is fairly depicting the situations surrounding the bill. Notable supporters of the Delivery Protection Act include NYC Mayor Zohran Mamdani and the Amazon division of the International Brotherhood of Teamsters. Using a platform with massive reach to communicate only one side of an ongoing debate could lead to misinformation and a skewed public perception. The Delivery Protection Act is currently stalled in the City Council’s Committee on Consumer and Worker Protection. The last hearing on the bill was in April. A committee vote on whether to advance the act to the full City Council hasn’t been set.

最后，亚马逊是否公正地描述了围绕该法案的情况也是一个问题。《配送保护法》的著名支持者包括纽约市议员佐兰·马姆达尼（Zohran Mamdani）和国际卡车司机工会（International Brotherhood of Teamsters）的亚马逊分部。利用一个覆盖面巨大的平台仅传达正在进行的辩论的一方观点，可能会导致错误信息和公众认知的偏差。《配送保护法》目前在市议会的消费者和工人保护委员会中陷入停滞。该法案的最后一次听证会是在四月举行的。委员会尚未安排关于是否将该法案提交至全体市议会进行投票的日期。
