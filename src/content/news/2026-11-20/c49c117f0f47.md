I’ve been testing the Hatch Sleep Clock for over two months, and it’s earned a permanent spot on my nightstand. As someone who already wears a smart ring and smartwatch to bed, I’m not exactly short on sleep-tracking data. But I’ve never owned a sunrise alarm clock, and I instantly became a convert. Unlike my iPhone or Amazon Echo Dot, it doesn’t wake me up by blaring an obnoxious alarm in my pitch-black bedroom. Instead, it gradually illuminates the room within my scheduled wake-up time. You can customize the sunrise's duration and brightness, and pair it with a soundscape of your choosing. It truly makes getting out of bed feel less jarring, especially as the mornings get darker.

我已经测试 Hatch Sleep Clock 两个多月了，它已经在我的床头柜上占据了固定位置。作为一个已经佩戴智能戒指和智能手表睡觉的人，我不缺睡眠追踪数据。但我从未拥有过日出闹钟，使用后我立刻被它折服了。与 iPhone 或 Amazon Echo Dot 不同，它不会在漆黑的卧室里用刺耳的闹铃声把我吵醒。相反，它会在我设定的起床时间内逐渐照亮房间。你可以自定义日出的持续时间和亮度，并搭配你选择的音景。这确实让起床的感觉不再那么突兀，尤其是在早晨天色渐暗的时候。

Most notably, the Sleep Clock also doubles as a contactless sleep tracker, using a built-in motion sensor to detect your movements and breathing throughout the night. I compared its readings against my Oura Ring 5 and Apple Watch Series 12, and while sleep duration estimates were generally aligned, the sleep-stage readings were less consistent. The device doesn’t measure physiological metrics like heart rate, and it can’t detect snoring or other audio-based sleep disruptions. I also found that placement significantly matters. My nightstand tends to collect clutter, so I had to rearrange my trinkets to make room for the clock.

最值得注意的是，Sleep Clock 还兼作非接触式睡眠追踪器，利用内置的运动传感器来检测你整晚的动作和呼吸。我将其读数与我的 Oura Ring 5 和 Apple Watch Series 12 进行了对比，虽然睡眠时长估算基本一致，但睡眠阶段的读数不太稳定。该设备无法测量心率等生理指标，也无法检测打鼾或其他基于音频的睡眠干扰。我还发现摆放位置非常重要。我的床头柜容易堆放杂物，所以我不得不重新整理小物件来为闹钟腾出空间。

Each morning, the Hatch app gives you a breakdown of when you went to bed, when you fell asleep, when you woke up, and how much time you spent in different sleep stages. The color-coded reports are easy to understand without burying me in random metrics. Plus, unlike most sleep trackers, Hatch doesn't require a subscription to access your sleep data. (You will need that Hatch+ membership ($6 per month) if you want to access the full audio library, however.)

每天早上，Hatch 应用程序都会为你提供一份详细报告，包括上床时间、入睡时间、起床时间以及在不同睡眠阶段所花费的时间。颜色编码的报告简单易懂，不会让我淹没在杂乱的指标中。此外，与大多数睡眠追踪器不同，Hatch 不需要订阅即可查看睡眠数据。（不过，如果你想访问完整的音频库，则需要购买每月 6 美元的 Hatch+ 会员资格。）

Overall, the Hatch Sleep Clock is the best all-in-one bedside device. I wouldn’t recommend replacing your dedicated wearable with it if detailed health tracking is your priority. But if you’ve been wanting a sunrise alarm clock that can also offer a little insight into your sleep, do not sleep on this Black Friday deal.
