A Russian national has been indicted on suspicions he was a Russian spy who stalked and surveilled employees of a Silicon Valley company that was assisting Ukraine in its ongoing war with its eastern neighbor. Igor D’iu, 25, allegedly photographed the employees and their computer screens as they worked at a coffee shop located near the company’s Palo Alto office, federal prosecutors said Wednesday. He also kept a list of home addresses of employees and a senior executive.

一名俄罗斯公民因涉嫌充当俄罗斯间谍而被起诉，他被指控跟踪并监视一家硅谷公司的员工，该公司一直在为乌克兰与邻国的战争提供援助。联邦检察官周三表示，25 岁的伊戈尔·迪乌（Igor D’iu）涉嫌在帕洛阿尔托公司办公室附近的一家咖啡馆工作时，拍摄了员工及其电脑屏幕的照片。他还保存了一份员工和一名高级主管的家庭住址名单。

At one point, prosecutors said, D’iu trailed an employee as she traveled home from Palo Alto to San Francisco. An affidavit filed in the case referred to the employee as FEMP1, short for female employee, and said the event left her “in a state of emotional distress.”

检察官称，有一次，迪乌跟踪了一名从帕洛阿尔托开车回旧金山的员工。此案提交的一份宣誓书将该员工称为 FEMP1（女性员工的缩写），并称这一事件使她“处于情绪困扰状态”。

“FEMP1 made evasive and irregular turns, took back roads, and turned into a grocery store parking lot where D’IU continued aggressively following her vehicle,” an FBI special agent wrote. “D’IU followed FEMP1 through U-turns, illegal left turns, and ran a red light to stay with FEMP1 for nearly thirty miles. FEMP1 called 911 while driving and was instructed to drive directly to San Francisco Police Department (“SFPD”) Mission Station. Near SFPD Mission Station, FEMP1 flagged an SFPD patrol vehicle and reported the incident. D’IU left once FEMP1 made contact with the patrol vehicle.”

“FEMP1 进行了规避和不规则的转弯，走了小路，并转入一家杂货店的停车场，而迪乌继续咄咄逼人地跟随她的车辆，”一名联邦调查局特工写道。“迪乌跟随 FEMP1 进行了掉头、非法左转，并闯红灯，紧跟了 FEMP1 近三十英里。FEMP1 在开车时拨打了 911，并被指示直接开车前往旧金山警察局（SFPD）任务分局。在任务分局附近，FEMP1 向一辆 SFPD 巡逻车示意并报告了该事件。当 FEMP1 与巡逻车取得联系后，迪乌便离开了。”

When police later questioned D’iu about the incident, the defendant allegedly said he saw a woman get into a car and thought she was “beautiful.” He then told the investigators that he followed her because he “wanted to ask her out on a date.”

当警方随后就此事询问迪乌时，被告声称他看到一名女子上车，觉得她“很漂亮”。他随后告诉调查人员，他跟踪她是因为他“想约她出去约会”。

Prosecutors didn’t name the company, but both Reuters and Bloomberg, citing unnamed people with knowledge of the case, identified it as Palantir. Ars is unable to confirm that reporting. Palantir representatives didn’t respond to an email seeking confirmation.

检察官没有透露该公司名称，但路透社和彭博社均援引知情人士的话称，该公司为 Palantir。Ars 无法证实该报道。Palantir 的代表没有回复寻求确认的电子邮件。

D’iu allegedly entered the US unlawfully in the summer of 2026 after being recruited by someone over Telegram. Prosecutors said the party gave D’iu a clandestine assignment to target employees of a large technology and defense company. D’iu received instructions, equipment, and financial support along with information that his work was on behalf of “the motherland.”

据称，迪乌在通过 Telegram 被人招募后，于 2026 年夏天非法进入美国。检察官称，该方给了迪乌一项秘密任务，目标是一家大型科技和国防公司的员工。迪乌收到了指示、设备和经济支持，以及他是在为“祖国”工作的消息。

His instructions were to collect information about the company because it was supplying weapons for use against Russian forces in Ukraine. Prosecutors said D’iu traveled from Istanbul, Turkey, to Mexico City in June. He then crossed the border into the US and was instructed to buy laptops, cell phones, wireless networking equipment, and remote access software.

他的指示是收集有关该公司的信息，因为该公司正在提供用于对抗乌克兰境内俄罗斯军队的武器。检察官称，迪乌于 6 月从土耳其伊斯坦布尔前往墨西哥城。随后他越境进入美国，并被指示购买笔记本电脑、手机、无线网络设备和远程访问软件。

Besides instructions to monitor company employees at the coffee shop, D’iu’s orders also included positioning his laptop at certain locations near the company so it could be remotely accessed in an attempt to collect information from company employees nearby. D’iu allegedly obtained fraudulent identification documents to conceal his identity as he conducted his activities.

除了在咖啡馆监视公司员工的指示外，迪乌的命令还包括将他的笔记本电脑放置在公司附近的特定位置，以便远程访问，试图从附近的员工那里收集信息。据称，迪乌在进行活动时获得了伪造的身份证件以掩盖其身份。

The documents included a falsified California ID card, an altered US passport, and a Social Security card bearing the assumed name of “Hugo Landry Johns.” The passport was allegedly an authentic US passport that originally had been issued to someone elsewhere in the country. It was modified to display D’iu’s photograph and assumed identity.

这些文件包括一张伪造的加州身份证、一本经过篡改的美国护照，以及一张印有假名“雨果·兰德里·约翰斯”（Hugo Landry Johns）的社会保障卡。据称，该护照是一本真实的美国护照，最初签发给该国其他地方的某个人。它经过修改，显示了迪乌的照片和假身份。

D’iu was arrested on October 30 and remains in custody. He is charged with one count of Conspiracy to Act as an Agent of a Foreign Government, one count of Acting as an Agent of a Foreign Government Without Notice to the Attorney General, and one count of Interstate Stalking.

迪乌于 10 月 30 日被捕，目前仍被拘留。他被控一项串谋充当外国政府代理人的罪名、一项未向司法部长通报而充当外国政府代理人的罪名，以及一项跨州跟踪罪。

If convicted for Acting as an Agent of a Foreign Government Without Notice to the Attorney General, he faces a maximum sentence of 10 years in prison, a fine of $250,000, and three years of supervised release. He faces a maximum sentence of five years in prison, a fine of $250,000, and three years of supervised release for each conviction on the other two charges.

如果因未向司法部长通报而充当外国政府代理人的罪名成立，他将面临最高 10 年监禁、25 万美元罚款和 3 年监督释放。对于另外两项指控，每项定罪他都面临最高 5 年监禁、25 万美元罚款和 3 年监督释放。

D’iu had his initial appearance in federal court on November 2. He is next scheduled to appear in federal court on November 24.

迪乌于 11 月 2 日在联邦法院首次出庭。他定于 11 月 24 日再次在联邦法院出庭。
