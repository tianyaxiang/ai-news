Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.23450 (cs) [Submitted on 17 Nov 2026] Title: NIRJALA: Draining the Sea out of Gaussian Splatting with Three-Path Optics and Reversible Physics Authors: Latchan Chhetri, Aman Kumar.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.23450 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 17 日] 标题：NIRJALA：通过三路径光学和可逆物理从高斯溅射中抽干海水 作者：Latchan Chhetri, Aman Kumar。

Abstract: Water does not just tint an underwater photograph; it rewrites it. Each pixel mixes attenuated scene radiance, backscattered light, and scene light scattered forward on its way to the camera, and many combinations of scene and water explain the same pixels. Most Gaussian-splatting methods for underwater scenes model only the first two effects and remove the water by dividing by a transmission that decays exponentially with range, so their inversion gain grows without bound.

摘要：水不仅会给水下照片染色，还会重写照片。每个像素都混合了衰减的场景辐射、背向散射光以及在到达相机途中向前散射的场景光，而场景与水的多种组合都可以解释相同的像素。大多数用于水下场景的高斯溅射方法仅模拟前两种效应，并通过除以随距离呈指数衰减的透射率来去除水，因此它们的反演增益会无限制地增长。

We present NIRJALA (Sanskrit for "without water"), a 3D Gaussian Splatting framework that recovers water-free appearance and geometry together. TRIVENI, a three-path image formation model, adds a range-saturating forward-scattering term to direct transmission and backscatter; we prove that this keeps the inversion gain bounded at every range and bound how errors in the estimated backscatter and attenuation propagate into the recovered colours.

我们提出了 NIRJALA（梵语意为“无水”），这是一个能够同时恢复无水外观和几何形状的 3D 高斯溅射框架。TRIVENI 是一种三路径图像形成模型，它在直接透射和背向散射的基础上增加了一个距离饱和的前向散射项；我们证明了这使得反演增益在任何距离上都保持有界，并限制了估计的背向散射和衰减误差如何传播到恢复的颜色中。

PRATIBIMBA, a reversible physics anchor, ties a flexible neural water model to a ten-parameter physical model in both directions, so scene content cannot hide inside the water. SAMARASA collects the physics-informed objectives, including a range-binned colour prior. To score water removal against ground truth, we built SAJALA ("with water"), a 30-scene suite path-traced in Blender Cycles with water-free images, depth, and normals under controlled water colour and turbidity.

PRATIBIMBA 是一种可逆物理锚点，它将灵活的神经水模型与十参数物理模型双向绑定，从而使场景内容无法隐藏在水中。SAMARASA 收集了物理信息目标，包括距离分箱颜色先验。为了根据地面实况评估去水效果，我们构建了 SAJALA（“有水”），这是一个包含 30 个场景的套件，在 Blender Cycles 中进行路径追踪，并在受控的水色和浊度下提供无水图像、深度和法线。

On SAJALA, NIRJALA raises PSNR from 24.34 to 24.76 dB and lowers depth AbsRel from 0.109 to 0.091 against the strongest baseline; on real SeaThru-NeRF scenes it lowers LPIPS from 0.194 to 0.175 while rendering at 44 FPS. We also discuss where appearance metrics and geometry disagree underwater, and the limits of our evaluation.

在 SAJALA 上，与最强的基准相比，NIRJALA 将 PSNR 从 24.34 dB 提高到 24.76 dB，并将深度 AbsRel 从 0.109 降低到 0.091；在真实的 SeaThru-NeRF 场景中，它将 LPIPS 从 0.194 降低到 0.175，同时以 44 FPS 的速度进行渲染。我们还讨论了外观指标和几何形状在水下不一致的地方，以及我们评估的局限性。
