Computer Science > Computer Vision and Pattern Recognition arXiv:2611.23446 (cs) [Submitted on 17 Nov 2026] Title: Where Global Context and Weight Sharing Work in Compact Vision Backbones Authors: Michael Schroeder.

计算机科学 > 计算机视觉与模式识别 arXiv:2611.23446 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 17 日] 标题：全局上下文与权重共享在紧凑型视觉骨干网络中的作用 作者：Michael Schroeder。

Abstract: Compact convolutional backbones such as EfficientNet achieve strong parameter efficiency through local depthwise separable convolutions, yet two structural questions remain central to hybrid design: where does global self-attention improve recognition over pure convolution, and can repeated blocks share weights without losing representational capacity?

摘要：诸如 EfficientNet 之类的紧凑型卷积骨干网络通过局部深度可分离卷积实现了强大的参数效率，但混合设计中仍有两个核心结构问题：全局自注意力机制在何处能比纯卷积更好地提升识别能力，以及重复的模块能否在不损失表征能力的情况下共享权重？

We investigate both questions on ImageNet-1k (50,000-image validation) using controlled EfficientNet-B0 experiments. First, we show that wrapping a classification backbone in multi-scale feature pyramids (BiFPN) fails to outperform a plain MBConv trunk (69.99% - 71.81% vs. 72.86% plain B0) until paired with stage attention (73.15%).

我们使用受控的 EfficientNet-B0 实验在 ImageNet-1k（50,000 张图像验证集）上研究了这两个问题。首先，我们展示了将分类骨干网络封装在多尺度特征金字塔（BiFPN）中，除非与阶段注意力机制（73.15%）结合，否则无法超越普通的 MBConv 主干（69.99% - 71.81% 对比 72.86% 的普通 B0）。

In contrast, keeping the MBConv trunk intact and inserting lightweight zero-gated residual self-attention adapters at stages P3 (28x28 windowed), P4 (14x14 global), and P5 (7x7 global) reaches 74.02% standalone and outperforms a bit-for-bit matched plain-B0 control by +0.954 percentage points (73.88% vs. 72.92%) across an 88-epoch stateless replay schedule, with learned attention amplitude rising monotonically with semantic depth (27.2% at P3 -> 45.4% at P4 -> 66.3% at P5).

相比之下，保持 MBConv 主干完整，并在 P3（28x28 窗口化）、P4（14x14 全局）和 P5（7x7 全局）阶段插入轻量级零门控残差自注意力适配器，其独立准确率达到了 74.02%。在 88 个周期的无状态重放计划中，它比完全匹配的普通 B0 对照组高出 0.954 个百分点（73.88% 对比 72.92%），且学习到的注意力幅度随语义深度单调上升（P3 为 27.2% -> P4 为 45.4% -> P5 为 66.3%）。

Second, we evaluate recurrent weight sharing across repeated MBConv blocks with pass-specific BatchNorm statistics (+1,536 parameters per pass). While post-hoc kernel averaging degrades accuracy to 17.03% and single-template stage tying (-27.6% parameters) plateaus at 66.10%, alternating two shared block templates ([B1, A, B, A]) in Stage 6 cuts total model parameters by 10.9% (582K weights) and recovers by +10.82 points in a single epoch to 70.92% (-1.84 points vs. unshared control).

其次，我们评估了在重复的 MBConv 模块中进行循环权重共享的效果，并使用了特定于传递的 BatchNorm 统计信息（每次传递增加 1,536 个参数）。虽然事后核平均会将准确率降低至 17.03%，且单模板阶段绑定（参数减少 27.6%）在 66.10% 处趋于平稳，但在第 6 阶段交替使用两个共享模块模板（[B1, A, B, A]）可使模型总参数减少 10.9%（582K 个权重），并在单个周期内恢复 10.82 个百分点至 70.92%（与未共享的对照组相比低 1.84 个百分点）。
