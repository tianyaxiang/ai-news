Many studies over the years have examined how people encounter climate science information. Some focused on the top results that Google surfaced (back when Google was still in the business of serving links), while others examined social media content or newspaper and TV news coverage. With large language models (LLMs) now forming a share of the public information diet, it’s worth looking at how they handle climate change.

多年来，许多研究考察了人们如何接触气候科学信息。一些研究关注谷歌搜索出的置顶结果（在谷歌还主要提供链接的时代），另一些则研究了社交媒体内容或报纸与电视新闻报道。随着大型语言模型（LLM）如今已成为公众信息获取渠道的一部分，审视它们如何处理气候变化问题显得尤为重要。

But there’s no direct way for researchers to look at the information people have seen. It is served ephemerally to individual users, confined to their chat session. A study led by researchers from University College London and the United Nations Educational, Scientific, and Cultural Organization (UNESCO) tried to tackle this question from multiple angles, testing LLMs with different prompts and evaluating their output.

然而，研究人员无法直接查看人们所看到的信息。这些信息是短暂地提供给个人用户的，仅限于他们的聊天会话中。一项由伦敦大学学院和联合国教育、科学及文化组织（UNESCO）的研究人员领导的研究，试图从多个角度解决这个问题，通过不同的提示词测试大语言模型并评估其输出结果。

They started with benchmarks of simple factual questions (benchmarks that might even be in models’ training data at this point) but also analyzed more complex interactions—including when users think they’re right to push back on an answer.

他们从简单的客观事实问题基准测试开始（这些基准测试甚至可能已经包含在模型的训练数据中），但也分析了更复杂的互动——包括当用户认为自己正确并对回答提出质疑时的情况。

LLM research often faces (no-true-Scotsman-tinged) criticism for focusing on a particular model rather than some other frontier model that may have been released after the experiment concluded. In this case, the study used 33 models, including multiple versions of the major frontier and open-weight models.

大语言模型研究经常面临（带有“没有真正的苏格兰人”谬误色彩的）批评，即研究只关注特定模型，而非实验结束后可能发布的其他前沿模型。在此次研究中，团队使用了33个模型，包括多个主要前沿模型和开源权重模型的版本。

The flagship frontier models used were Claude Fable 5, Claude Opus 5, Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview, Gemini 3.6 Flash, and GPT-5.6 Sol. (You can find the full list in the report.) All the models were scored for correct answers to 600 questions from several benchmark sets.

所使用的旗舰前沿模型包括 Claude Fable 5、Claude Opus 5、Gemini 3.1 Pro Preview、Gemini 3.6 Flash 和 GPT-5.6 Sol。（完整列表可在报告中找到。）所有模型都针对来自多个基准测试集的600个问题进行了正确率评分。

This included multiple-choice textbook questions, “reading comprehension” questions based on text snippets from scientific papers, and questions about how confident climate scientists are in different conclusions drawn from IPCC reports. That last one was the most difficult, with the flagship models answering around 78 percent correctly and the smallest open-weight models coming in at around 47 percent.

这包括教科书式的选择题、基于科学论文片段的“阅读理解”题，以及关于气候科学家对IPCC报告中不同结论的置信度的问题。最后这一类问题难度最高，旗舰模型的正确率约为78%，而最小的开源权重模型正确率仅为47%左右。

For the other benchmarks, frontier models and large open-weight models generally scored around 90 percent, while small open-weight models scored in the 70s. Two persistent trends show up here: The latest frontier models tend to score a bit better than earlier generations of that model, and larger open-weight models score better than small ones.

对于其他基准测试，前沿模型和大型开源权重模型通常得分在90%左右，而小型开源权重模型得分在70%左右。这里出现了两个持续的趋势：最新的前沿模型往往比该模型的早期版本得分稍高，且大型开源权重模型比小型模型得分更高。

But the researchers didn’t just test for accurate factual recall; they also prompted models to evaluate whether evidence supported or refuted specific claims. (For example, the models were asked whether the statement that “renewable energy investment kills jobs” is accurate, given evidence like descriptions of employment and investment statistics.) Here, the best models scored around 70 percent accuracy, while the worst models scored below 40 percent.

但研究人员不仅测试了准确的事实回忆能力；他们还提示模型评估证据是支持还是反驳了特定主张。（例如，模型被问及在就业和投资统计数据等证据面前，“可再生能源投资会扼杀就业”这一说法是否准确。）在此项测试中，表现最好的模型准确率约为70%，而表现最差的模型准确率低于40%。

Finally, the researchers tested 656 questions about the potential impact of policies on global temperatures. Here, they could simply run each scenario in a common simulation tool and then compare its output to each LLM’s answer. Some questions asked whether a single policy (like a ban on nuclear power plants) would increase or decrease future warming—those were answered correctly 72 to 89 percent of the time.

最后，研究人员测试了656个关于政策对全球气温潜在影响的问题。在这里，他们只需在通用的模拟工具中运行每个场景，然后将其输出与每个大语言模型的回答进行比较。一些问题询问单一政策（如禁止核电站）是会增加还是减少未来的变暖——这些问题的回答正确率在72%到89%之间。

Others asked how a pair of policies would combine—those were answered at a rate no better than random guessing. Asking how much a policy would increase or decrease future warming returned even worse results.

另一些问题询问两种政策结合会产生什么影响——这些回答的正确率并不比随机猜测好。询问某项政策会增加或减少多少未来变暖程度，其结果甚至更糟。

Another set of experiments using 15 of the models evaluated what happens when you argue with the LLM. The researchers tried more than responding “nuh-uh” or “no it isn’t.” They also tried claiming to be a specific kind of person. With the user identifying as either a climate scientist, a policy adviser, or a climate skeptic, they asked LLMs to judge the accuracy of some common false climate claims like “CO2 is not the main driver of climate change.”

另一组实验使用了其中的15个模型，评估了当你与大语言模型争论时会发生什么。研究人员尝试的不仅仅是回应“不对”或“不是这样”。他们还尝试声称自己是特定身份的人。用户分别以气候科学家、政策顾问或气候怀疑论者的身份，要求大语言模型判断一些常见错误气候主张的准确性，例如“二氧化碳不是气候变化的主要驱动因素”。

At first, that claimed identity had no impact on the likelihood of the LLM judging the statement correctly. But then the researchers started pushing back. Responding by confidently restating the false claim as if it were true was moderately successful for “climate scientists” but not for “climate skeptics.” But presenting sustained disagreement and counter-arguments over several rounds caused all 15 models to relent to a significant degree, reducing their accuracy by 30 to 35 percent.

起初，这种声称的身份对大语言模型正确判断陈述的可能性没有影响。但随后研究人员开始进行反驳。自信地重申错误主张并将其视为事实，这种做法对“气候科学家”身份有一定效果，但对“气候怀疑论者”身份则不然。然而，在多轮对话中持续提出异议和反驳，导致所有15个模型在很大程度上妥协，其准确率下降了30%到35%。

Sycophancy is a familiar issue for LLMs, which will bend output to be agreeable to the user’s prompts. It didn’t take much pushing in these tests, either. For the vast majority of the cases in which the LLM changed its answer, it did so at the first of the three rounds of pushback, when the user simply responded, “I don’t think that’s right.”

“阿谀奉承”是大语言模型的一个常见问题，它们会为了迎合用户的提示而扭曲输出。在这些测试中，并不需要太多的推动。在绝大多数大语言模型改变答案的情况下，它们在三轮反驳的第一轮中就改变了，当时用户只是简单地回应说：“我不认为那是对的。”

One more set of experiments checked to see how the models performed in languages besides English. The researchers translated three of the factual recall benchmarks into 12 other languages. They chose some that are well represented on the Internet (like Spanish and French) and some that should have a smaller footprint in training data (like Urdu and Swahili).

最后一组实验检查了模型在英语以外语言中的表现。研究人员将三个事实回忆基准测试翻译成了其他12种语言。他们选择了一些在互联网上广泛使用的语言（如西班牙语和法语），以及一些在训练数据中占比较小的语言（如乌尔都语和斯瓦希里语）。

For this test, they dropped down to just five models. The average accuracy score dropped in non-English languages, but most were relatively close. The top six only dropped by about two percentage points or less.

在此次测试中，他们仅使用了五个模型。非英语语言的平均准确率有所下降，但大多数相对接近。前六名的准确率仅下降了约两个百分点或更少。
