How Do Sunrise Alarm Clocks Work? Your body is built to wake naturally with the sunrise and feel sleepy as the sun sets. The light at both the beginning and end of the day is warm and golden, unlike the bright, slightly blue midday sunlight that keeps you wide awake. A sunrise alarm clock mimics this transition from morning to bright daytime light, helping signal your body to wake up (or fall asleep, if the clock also has a sunset option).

日出闹钟是如何工作的？你的身体天生就是随着日出自然醒来，并随着日落感到困倦。一天之初和结束时的光线是温暖而金黄的，这与让你保持清醒的明亮且略带蓝色的正午阳光不同。日出闹钟模拟了从清晨到明亮日间光线的过渡，帮助向你的身体发出起床（如果闹钟有日落选项，则为入睡）的信号。

A sunrise alarm has a built-in light you can set to brighten at a certain time. It doesn’t happen instantly; it will slowly transition over a period of anywhere from 10 minutes to an hour, and better sunrise alarms let you choose your exact brightening window. I usually choose a half-hour window for the light to start brightening, and let the gradually brightening light wake me.

日出闹钟内置了灯光，你可以设置在特定时间变亮。这不会瞬间完成；它会在 10 分钟到一小时的时间内缓慢过渡，更好的日出闹钟允许你选择精确的变亮时长。我通常选择半小时的窗口让光线开始变亮，并让逐渐增强的光线唤醒我。

There are also sound effects. Sunrise alarms feature a mix of relaxing and classic sounds that usually start when the light begins to brighten, helping to rouse you. I’d prefer the light to go first, with music activating later, but that isn’t always an option with these lights.

此外还有音效。日出闹钟具有多种放松和经典的音效，通常在光线开始变亮时启动，以帮助唤醒你。我更倾向于先亮灯，稍后再播放音乐，但这些灯具并不总是提供这种选项。

Some sunrise alarms also offer a sunset routine, meaning you can turn them on at night and they'll slowly dim to darkness to help you feel sleepy. It’s another good visual cue for your brain and body that it’s time for bed, and I prefer sunrise alarms like Hatch and Lumie that let you choose when to activate a routine (for example, pressing a button starts the routine over the course of 30 minutes) rather than needing to be in bed by a set time to use the routine.

一些日出闹钟还提供日落程序，这意味着你可以在晚上开启它们，它们会缓慢变暗直至熄灭，帮助你产生困意。这是给大脑和身体发出的另一个良好的视觉信号，提示该睡觉了。我更喜欢像 Hatch 和 Lumie 这样允许你选择何时启动程序的日出闹钟（例如，按下一个按钮即可在 30 分钟内运行程序），而不是必须在特定时间上床才能使用该功能。

Ultimately, these lights can do a lot. They can double as a sound machine, help you wake up and fall asleep, and even serve as a regular bedside lamp if they’re bright enough. Not all sunrise alarms have all of these features, though, so you have to decide how much you want to spend and which features are most important to you.

归根结底，这些灯具功能多样。它们可以兼作音响设备，帮助你起床和入睡，如果亮度足够，甚至可以当作普通的床头灯使用。不过，并非所有的日出闹钟都具备所有这些功能，因此你需要决定预算以及哪些功能对你最重要。

What Features Should You Look for in a Sunrise Alarm Clock? You might see a range of features listed for a sunrise alarm, and more expensive models will include more of these than cheaper models. If you’re not sure which features you want, try this series of questions to figure out which features you need.

在选择日出闹钟时应该关注哪些功能？你可能会看到日出闹钟列出了一系列功能，更昂贵的型号通常比便宜的型号包含更多功能。如果你不确定自己想要什么功能，可以尝试通过以下一系列问题来确定你的需求。

Do you struggle to fall asleep? Splurge on a sunrise alarm with a nighttime or wind-down routine. These help establish a routine that makes it easier to fall asleep. Do you need one device that doubles as an alarm and a bedside lamp? Get a brighter sunrise clock with easy controls to turn it on as a bedside lamp. Not all sunrise clocks have these features, so check the details carefully (and reviews like mine!) and note that cheaper, smaller sunrise alarm clocks usually won’t brighten an entire bedroom.

你是否难以入睡？那就花钱买一个带有夜间或放松程序的日出闹钟吧。这些功能有助于建立一种让你更容易入睡的习惯。你需要一台既是闹钟又是床头灯的设备吗？那就买一个更亮、且易于控制以作为床头灯使用的日出闹钟。并非所有的日出闹钟都有这些功能，所以请仔细检查细节（以及像我这样的评论！），并注意更便宜、更小的日出闹钟通常无法照亮整个卧室。

Are you picky about your alarm sounds? Check how many sounds are offered. Just about every sunrise clock has some sound machine features and options, but cheaper models tend to offer only a couple of sounds and might not have the sound you’re looking for. Do you want app control? Some options in this guide don’t include a companion app or Wi-Fi capabilities, especially some of my favorites. An app doesn’t necessarily make a sunrise clock better, but it can be convenient to use. If you prefer an app to set up your sunrise lamp, try Casper, Hatch, and Loftie.
