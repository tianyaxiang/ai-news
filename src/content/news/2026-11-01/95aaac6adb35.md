# Happy Pikmin Day Eve

游戏娱乐新闻：皮克敏日（Pikmin Day）前夕。任天堂正在通过推出一系列新的短片来庆祝首届年度皮克敏日。作者：Terrence O'Brien，2026年10月31日，世界标准时间下午5:50。图片来源：任天堂。Terrence O'Brien是《The Verge》的周末编辑。他报道科技行业已超过18年，对合成器也颇有了解。

This morning, Nintendo announced that tomorrow, November 1st, would be the first annual Pikmin Day. As part of the short Pikmin Day Direct, Shigeru Miyamoto shared new info about Pikmin 4 for the Switch 2, as well as an update for the mobile app Pikmin Bloom, and announced the Hello! Pikmin series of shorts.

今天早上，任天堂宣布明天（11月1日）将是首个年度皮克敏日。作为简短的“皮克敏日直面会”的一部分，宫本茂分享了关于Switch 2版《皮克敏4》的新信息，以及移动应用《皮克敏：花朵》（Pikmin Bloom）的更新，并宣布了《Hello! Pikmin》系列短片。

Players will be able to use the Switch 2’s built-in microphone to issue voice commands to Oatchi, the space dog. Pikmin 4 will also have a Dandori Academy mode that will test players’ skills in specific challenges. There will be limited tests on Friday, Saturday, and Sunday, complete with leaderboards where players can compare stats.

玩家将能够使用Switch 2的内置麦克风向太空犬欧庆（Oatchi）发出语音指令。《皮克敏4》还将包含一个“丹多里学院”（Dandori Academy）模式，该模式将在特定挑战中测试玩家的技能。周五、周六和周日将进行限时测试，并配有排行榜，供玩家比较数据。

Pikmin Bloom will be getting a new feature called Secret Caves, where players can win treasure by battling enemies and exploring said caves. Lastly, Miyamoto showed the first installment of a new series of shorts called Hello! Pikmin. A second episode will premiere tomorrow, with a third later in November. The series will then debut one short per month starting in December. The shorts build on the success of Close to You, a Pikmin animation released last year.

《皮克敏：花朵》将增加一项名为“秘密洞穴”（Secret Caves）的新功能，玩家可以通过击败敌人和探索这些洞穴来赢取宝藏。最后，宫本茂展示了名为《Hello! Pikmin》的新系列短片的第一集。第二集将于明天首播，第三集将于11月晚些时候播出。此后，该系列将从12月开始每月发布一部短片。这些短片建立在去年发布的皮克敏动画《Close to You》的成功基础之上。
