# Best Buy is bundling a $100 gift card with touchscreen MacBook Pro preorders

百思买（Best Buy）正在为预购触控屏 MacBook Pro 的用户提供 100 美元的礼品卡。

You have options for preordering the new MacBook Pro, but only one retailer is offering this sweet perk.

你有多种渠道可以预购新款 MacBook Pro，但只有一家零售商提供了这项诱人的福利。

While the new touch-enabled MacBook Pro with the PROLED XDR screen is available for preorder across a few major retailers, Best Buy is sweetening the deal by including a $100 gift card.

虽然配备 PROLED XDR 屏幕的新款触控 MacBook Pro 已在几家主要零售商处开启预购，但百思买通过赠送 100 美元礼品卡的方式让这笔交易更具吸引力。

The gift card will be automatically added to your cart when you preorder one of Apple’s highest-end laptops available in 14- or 16-inch configurations, and even one of its 14-inch MacBook Pro laptops with the M6 chip. You can verify that it’s been added to your order in the “Complete your purchase” section of the checkout page.

当你预购苹果最高端的 14 英寸或 16 英寸配置笔记本电脑，甚至是搭载 M6 芯片的 14 英寸 MacBook Pro 时，礼品卡会自动添加到你的购物车中。你可以在结账页面的“完成购买”部分确认它已添加到订单中。

My colleague Antonio G. Di Benedetto got a chance to check out the new laptop at Apple’s event, and came away impressed by the Tandem OLED panel’s brightness and contrast, but described the update as “kind of boring” given the relatively small changes to the system otherwise.

我的同事安东尼奥·G·迪·贝内代托（Antonio G. Di Benedetto）在苹果发布会上体验了这款新笔记本电脑，他对 Tandem OLED 面板的亮度和对比度印象深刻，但鉴于系统其他方面的变化相对较小，他将此次更新描述为“有点无聊”。

There are some unique changes to macOS that help make the new touch experience smoother, like magnifying the traffic light-colored buttons on windows when you move your finger near them. The PROLED laptops are also a bit thinner and lighter than standard models, and the Apple logo on the back glows like on older versions.

macOS 中有一些独特的改进，有助于使新的触控体验更加流畅，例如当你将手指靠近窗口时，窗口上的交通灯颜色按钮会放大。PROLED 笔记本电脑也比标准型号更轻薄，背面的苹果标志也像旧版本一样可以发光。
