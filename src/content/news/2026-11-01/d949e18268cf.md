The Drop: The new iPad Mini. With apologies to the touchscreen MacBook, this is the most intriguing new Apple product of the week. I, too, am annoyed that there’s no ProMotion screen, but I’m excited about thinner and lighter, and I’m very excited about the idea of a functionally water- and dustproof tablet.

精选推荐：新款 iPad Mini。虽然对触摸屏 MacBook 感到抱歉，但这确实是本周最引人注目的苹果新产品。我也对没有 ProMotion 屏幕感到恼火，但我对它更轻薄的设计感到兴奋，并且对一款具备实用防水防尘功能的平板电脑这一概念感到非常激动。

The Alexa Photo Frame. The Echo Show 16 is a cool smart home hub, but $500 is just too much for me. But a $200 digital photo frame that is also, sometimes, only when you need it, a smart home hub and timer-setter? That will end up in my living room. And my parents’. And my in-laws’.

Alexa 相框。Echo Show 16 是一款很酷的智能家居中枢，但 500 美元对我来说太贵了。但如果是一个 200 美元的数码相框，同时在您需要时偶尔充当智能家居中枢和定时器呢？那它一定会出现在我的客厅里。还有我父母和岳父母的客厅里。

nvAlt. One of the all-time great note-taking apps got its first update in forever, and now it can run on modern Macs. (The developer is also working on nvUltra, which is in beta and even better.) If you’re a plain-text notetaker, you’re gonna want to get back into this app.

nvAlt。这款史上最伟大的笔记应用之一终于迎来了久违的更新，现在可以在现代 Mac 上运行了。（开发者也在开发处于测试阶段且功能更强大的 nvUltra。）如果您是纯文本笔记用户，您一定会想重新用回这款应用。

Chromagraph. The director Rian Johnson — yes, that one — worked with his favorite cinematographer to make it possible to process iPhone photos the way they like best. An extremely opinionated camera app from people who have very much earned those opinions. I love it.

Chromagraph。导演莱恩·约翰逊（Rian Johnson）——没错，就是那位——与他最喜欢的摄影师合作，实现了以他们最喜欢的方式处理 iPhone 照片。这是一款极具个性的相机应用，出自那些完全有资格发表这种见解的人之手。我爱死它了。

“I love the iPod. It ruined everything.” This Mic The Snare video is both a very good history of the iPod and a surprisingly cogent theory that the iPod is to blame for some of the worst things about modern life. I still miss my gold iPod Mini.

“我爱 iPod。它毁了一切。”Mic The Snare 的这段视频既是对 iPod 历史的精彩回顾，也提出了一个令人信服的理论：现代生活中一些最糟糕的事情都要归咎于 iPod。我依然怀念我那台金色的 iPod Mini。

Readwise Books. A really nice-looking, really powerful app for buying, reading, and tracking your books. Right now it’s a little bit “better Kindle” and a lot “better Goodreads.” So far I’m mostly using the social feed to get recommendations from strangers, which suits me just fine. This went right onto my Boox Palma homescreen.

Readwise Books。一款外观精美、功能强大的应用，用于购买、阅读和追踪您的书籍。目前它有点像“更好的 Kindle”，很大程度上是“更好的 Goodreads”。到目前为止，我主要使用社交信息流来获取陌生人的推荐，这非常适合我。它已经直接进入了我的 Boox Palma 主屏幕。

Phantom Blade Zero. Currently burning up the Steam charts, this one. People love the martial arts-inspired combat system and the game’s overall aesthetic, and I’m even hearing good things about the music.

《影之刃零》（Phantom Blade Zero）。目前这款游戏正在 Steam 排行榜上火爆。人们喜爱它那受武术启发的战斗系统和游戏的整体美学，我甚至听到了对它音乐的好评。

No Man’s Sky. This is definitely one of the biggest and most ambitious games to ever come to iOS (and all it took was a decade!). It works on pretty much all recent iPhones and iPads, too. Just don’t be fooled by the Free sign at the top; the game costs $20. But it’s worth it.

《无人深空》（No Man’s Sky）。这绝对是 iOS 平台上最大、最雄心勃勃的游戏之一（而且足足花了一十年才实现！）。它几乎可以在所有近期的 iPhone 和 iPad 上运行。只是别被顶部的“免费”标志骗了；游戏售价 20 美元。但它物有所值。

The Matic Serene. Two things here. One, $1,700 is an absolutely ludicrous price for a robot vacuum, no matter how good it is. Two, “we made it quieter” is maybe the quickest way to get me in particular to want your robot vacuum. Please, friends, do not let me buy this.

The Matic Serene。这里有两点。第一，1700 美元对于扫地机器人来说绝对是一个荒谬的价格，无论它有多好。第二，“我们让它更安静了”也许是让我个人想要购买这款扫地机器人的最快方式。拜托了，朋友们，千万别让我买这个。
