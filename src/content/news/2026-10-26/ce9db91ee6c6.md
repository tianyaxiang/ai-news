# 6 Best Folding Phones (2026): Samsung, Apple, Google, Motorola

Save this story. Featured in this article: Best Folding Phone Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8, Best Folding iPhone Apple iPhone Duo, Best Flip Phone Motorola Razr Ultra (2026), Best Budget Folding Phone Motorola Razr (2026).

You may be scoffing as you read this. The best folding phones? Really? Aren’t those just gimmicks? I'm here to tell you they're pretty darn useful. And yes, there are quite a few now (even Apple is in on the action), so you have options. Folding phones are an evolution of the traditional single-screen slab of glass we've all been using for nearly two decades.

读到这里，你可能会嗤之以鼻。最好的折叠屏手机？真的吗？那不就是噱头吗？但我得告诉你，它们确实非常实用。没错，现在市面上的选择已经很多了（连苹果也加入了这一行列），所以你有不少选择。折叠屏手机是我们近二十年来一直在使用的传统单屏玻璃板手机的进化版。

They largely come in two forms: a folding flip phone or a folding book-like phone, enabling you to either relive the glory days of small phones or expand your multitasking options while on the go. A folding phone is a personal decision—arguably even more than a traditional smartphone, since it hinges (heh) on what style you prefer. We have tested almost all of them, and these are our favorites.

它们主要有两种形式：翻盖式折叠手机或书本式折叠手机，让你既能重温小屏手机的辉煌岁月，也能在移动办公时扩展多任务处理能力。选择折叠屏手机是个人的决定——甚至比选择传统智能手机更甚，因为它取决于（嘿嘿）你偏好的风格。我们测试了几乎所有机型，以下是我们的最爱。

Updated October 2026: We've added the Apple iPhone Duo, more on the Z Flip8 after testing, and updated prices.

2026 年 10 月更新：我们增加了苹果 iPhone Duo，在测试后补充了更多关于 Z Flip8 的内容，并更新了价格。

Best Folding Phone: Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8. The Galaxy Z Fold8 features fresh proportions for a form factor that feels just right and purpose-built. It is shorter and wider than the Galaxy Z Folds you might be used to, yet when you open it up, it feels even more tablet-like than ever. The crease is significantly less obvious than previous models, and the Fold8 is lighter and easier to handle than the larger Fold8 Ultra. You lose a little bit of screen real estate, but it's marginal.

最佳折叠屏手机：三星 Galaxy Z Fold8。Galaxy Z Fold8 采用了全新的比例，外观设计恰到好处且目标明确。它比你可能习惯的三星 Galaxy Z Fold 系列更短、更宽，但当你展开它时，它比以往任何时候都更像平板电脑。折痕比之前的型号明显减少，而且 Fold8 比更大的 Fold8 Ultra 更轻、更易于操作。虽然屏幕空间稍微减少了一点，但影响微乎其微。

The front 5.5-inch screen has a nice 10:16 aspect ratio that doesn't cause ridiculous letterboxing. I also found I opened the inner 7.6-inch screen far more often than with the Ultra, and you can comfortably have three apps side by side in split-screen mode. The Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 chipset ensures the smoothest of rides with apps and games. The Fold8's 4,800-mAh battery (Samsung's first with silicon-carbon battery technology) lasts about five to six hours of screen-on time.

前置 5.5 英寸屏幕拥有 10:16 的良好纵横比，不会出现夸张的黑边。我还发现，相比 Ultra，我打开 7.6 英寸内屏的频率要高得多，而且你可以在分屏模式下舒适地并排运行三个应用程序。高通骁龙 8 Elite Gen 5 芯片组确保了应用程序和游戏的流畅运行。Fold8 的 4,800 mAh 电池（三星首款采用硅碳电池技术的机型）可提供约五到六小时的亮屏时间。

Best Folding iPhone: Apple iPhone Duo. The iPhone Duo is astonishingly good for a folding-phone debut, and we're impressed by its polished hardware, graceful hinge, and durability. The outer screen is big enough to be useful, and it opens to a small tablet size with a clever nanotexture finish that almost completely hides the crease. But it is heavy at 254 grams (the Fold8 above only weighs 201 grams).

最佳折叠屏 iPhone：苹果 iPhone Duo。作为苹果的首款折叠屏手机，iPhone Duo 的表现令人惊叹，其精致的硬件、优雅的铰链和耐用性给我们留下了深刻印象。外屏足够大，非常实用，展开后呈现出小平板电脑的尺寸，巧妙的纳米纹理涂层几乎完全隐藏了折痕。但它重量达到 254 克，比较沉（上述 Fold8 仅重 201 克）。

The side-by-side view for optimized apps on the inner screen is lovely, and Apple has shifted iOS navigation elements to the right, which works well in apps that take advantage of it. As a great choice for multitaskers, you may even consider working on the iPhone Duo. The battery life is excellent, and you’ll likely have around 40 percent left after an average day’s use.

内屏上针对优化应用提供的并排视图非常棒，苹果将 iOS 导航元素移到了右侧，这在利用该功能的应用程序中表现良好。作为多任务处理者的绝佳选择，你甚至可以考虑在 iPhone Duo 上办公。电池续航表现出色，在平均一天的使用后，你很可能还剩下约 40% 的电量。
