# The hottest AI wearables and gadgets you should know about

A slew of AI-powered gadgets on the market aims to integrate artificial intelligence into our daily lives like never before. Some of these AI wearables — including necklaces, rings, and wristbands, as well as portable devices — serve as productivity tools, while others claim to act as friendly companions listening to your everyday thoughts. Even OpenAI is working on a compact AI companion device. Below, we’ve rounded up some of the most notable devices.

市面上涌现出一大批人工智能驱动的小工具，旨在以前所未有的方式将人工智能融入我们的日常生活。其中一些人工智能可穿戴设备——包括项链、戒指、腕带以及便携式设备——充当生产力工具，而另一些则声称可以作为倾听你日常想法的友好伙伴。甚至 OpenAI 也在研发一款紧凑型人工智能伴侣设备。以下是我们整理的一些最值得关注的设备。

Bee Image Credits:Bee AI Bee is an affordable pendant priced at $49.99 that can either be clipped to your clothing or worn like a fitness band. This device records everything it hears and learns your routines and preferences to create reminders and notes for you. It even features a mute button for those times you want some privacy. The companion app (currently available only on iOS) is included with a $19 monthly subscription. The app allows you to interact with Bee directly and ask it questions. You can also get key takeaways from your day and chronological transcripts of your conversations.

Bee（图片来源：Bee AI）：Bee 是一款售价 49.99 美元的平价吊坠，既可以夹在衣服上，也可以像健身手环一样佩戴。该设备会记录它听到的所有内容，并学习你的日常习惯和偏好，从而为你创建提醒和笔记。它甚至还设有一个静音按钮，供你在需要隐私时使用。配套应用程序（目前仅适用于 iOS）包含在每月 19 美元的订阅费中。该应用程序允许你直接与 Bee 互动并向其提问。你还可以获取每日重点摘要以及对话的时间顺序转录。

Amazon acquired the wearables startup in July 2025, launching its first new products earlier this month. Image Credits:Amazon Amazon introduced two new devices: the Bee Frontier, priced at $74.99, which can be worn on the wrist or clipped to clothing, and the Bee Eclipse, priced at $99.99, a credit-card-sized device that attaches magnetically to the back of a smartphone. Both offer the same core functionality as the previous generation, positioning Bee as a more affordable alternative to AI devices such as the Plaud Note and Plaud NotePin. Users can also choose to connect Bee to Alexa.

亚马逊于 2025 年 7 月收购了这家可穿戴设备初创公司，并于本月初推出了首批新产品。（图片来源：Amazon）亚马逊推出了两款新设备：售价 74.99 美元的 Bee Frontier，可佩戴在手腕上或夹在衣服上；以及售价 99.99 美元的 Bee Eclipse，这是一款信用卡大小的设备，可通过磁吸方式附着在智能手机背面。两者都提供与上一代相同的核心功能，使 Bee 成为 Plaud Note 和 Plaud NotePin 等人工智能设备更经济实惠的替代品。用户还可以选择将 Bee 连接到 Alexa。

Friend Image Credits:Friend Friend is one of the most hyped entrants in the “personal AI” device market. This $129 white pendant hangs around your neck and functions as an emotional support companion. It recognizes your tone and mood, allowing you to chat with it as if it were a friend. It connects to your phone via Bluetooth and constantly listens, ready to respond or send you proactive messages, like wishing you good luck before an interview. However, it has also faced criticism, including backlash against its subway ad campaign in NYC. People vandalized the ads, writing messages like “surveillance capitalism.” Likely as an attempt to stay relevant, the company recently unveiled a major product update, introducing a new voice feature. Under the name “Friend 2.0,” the updated device includes a built-in speaker that allows it to communicate with users and express a more distinctive personality.

Friend（图片来源：Friend）：Friend 是“个人人工智能”设备市场上最受热捧的产品之一。这款售价 129 美元的白色吊坠挂在脖子上，充当情感支持伴侣。它能识别你的语调和情绪，让你像对待朋友一样与它聊天。它通过蓝牙连接到你的手机并持续监听，随时准备回应或向你发送主动消息，例如在面试前祝你好运。然而，它也面临着批评，包括对其在纽约市地铁广告活动的强烈抵制。人们破坏了这些广告，写下了诸如“监控资本主义”之类的标语。为了保持市场相关性，该公司最近发布了一项重大产品更新，引入了新的语音功能。在“Friend 2.0”的名义下，更新后的设备内置了扬声器，使其能够与用户交流并表达出更独特的个性。

Limitless Image Credits:Limitless Formerly known as Rewind, Limitless is another conversation-recording pendant priced at $99. This device continuously listens, transcribing meetings, calls, and conversations (with consent) into searchable and summarized knowledge. It’s ideal for professionals, especially journalists, looking to recall important discussions. The companion app comes with 10 hours of AI features per month — such as transcription and summaries — with the option to unlock unlimited features for $29 per month.

Limitless（图片来源：Limitless）：Limitless 前身为 Rewind，是另一款售价 99 美元的对话录音吊坠。该设备持续监听，将会议、通话和对话（在获得同意的情况下）转录为可搜索和总结的知识。它非常适合专业人士，尤其是需要回顾重要讨论的记者。配套应用程序每月提供 10 小时的人工智能功能（如转录和摘要），并可选择每月支付 29 美元解锁无限功能。

Muse Charm Image Credits:Mark Zuckerberg on X Meta introduced its first AI wearable in September, offering a new way for users to access its personal AI assistant, Muse. The device has not yet shipped, but it is expected to feature Jolly, a playful digital avatar that represents the AI assistant. Housed in a pocket-sized device that can be carried around or attached to a keychain, the wearable includes a miniature display showing the avatar. Users will be able to interact with Muse through voice commands and have it complete tasks for them.

Muse Charm（图片来源：X 平台上的马克·扎克伯格）：Meta 在 9 月推出了其首款人工智能可穿戴设备，为用户访问其个人人工智能助手 Muse 提供了一种新方式。该设备尚未发货，但预计将搭载 Jolly，这是一个代表人工智能助手的俏皮数字头像。这款可穿戴设备采用袖珍设计，可以随身携带或挂在钥匙扣上，并配有一个显示头像的微型显示屏。用户将能够通过语音指令与 Muse 互动，并让它完成任务。

Nirva Image Credits:Nirva Nirva is a startup founded by former Meta Reality Labs employees, building an AI wearable focused on personal growth rather than productivity or meeting notes. Worn as a pendant or bracelet, the device listens to the wearer’s speech and uses AI to turn everyday experiences into a personal journal, helping users better understand their emotions, communication style, and habits. Through its companion app, users can explore patterns in their behavior, gain insights into how they communicate, and find ways to improve. Nirva plans to open orders in November, with shipments to early users targeted for the end of the year. The device will cost $249.

Nirva（图片来源：Nirva）：Nirva 是一家由前 Meta Reality Labs 员工创立的初创公司，正在打造一款专注于个人成长而非生产力或会议记录的人工智能可穿戴设备。该设备可作为吊坠或手链佩戴，通过监听佩戴者的语音并利用人工智能将日常经历转化为个人日记，帮助用户更好地了解自己的情绪、沟通方式和习惯。通过配套应用程序，用户可以探索自己的行为模式，深入了解自己的沟通方式，并找到改进的方法。Nirva 计划于 11 月开放预订，目标是在年底前向早期用户发货。该设备售价为 249 美元。

Omi Image Credits:Omi Priced at $89, this device can answer your questions, summarize your conversations, create to-do lists, and help schedule meetings. Additionally, Omi is constantly listening and running your conversations through ChatGPT, allowing it to remember the context about you and offer personalized advice. Omi can be worn as a necklace, but another notable aspect is that it can be attached to the side of your head with medical tape and can detect when you’re speaking to it.

Omi（图片来源：Omi）：这款设备售价 89 美元，可以回答你的问题、总结你的对话、创建待办事项列表并帮助安排会议。此外，Omi 会持续监听并将你的对话通过 ChatGPT 进行处理，使其能够记住关于你的背景信息并提供个性化建议。Omi 可以作为项链佩戴，但另一个值得注意的方面是，它可以用医用胶带贴在头部侧面，并能检测到你何时在与它交谈。

Plaud’s NotePin Image Credits:Plaud At $159, Plaud’s NotePin is one of the pricier options on this list; however, its built-in AI transcription and summarization features make it a valuable tool for lawyers, journalists, and students attending meetings or lectures. The tiny wearable voice recorder can be worn on your wrist or attached magnetically to your clothing. The recordings are saved in real time on your phone, eliminating the hassle of manual note-taking. The device includes 300 free monthly transcription minutes, but with the $8.33 per month Pro plan, you upgrade your transcription time to 1,200 minutes. This year, the company is gearing up to launch a $179 ultra-thin note-taking device called the Plaud Note Pro, which is now available for preorder.

Plaud’s NotePin（图片来源：Plaud）：Plaud 的 NotePin 售价 159 美元，是此列表中较昂贵的选择之一；然而，其内置的人工智能转录和摘要功能使其成为律师、记者和参加会议或讲座的学生的重要工具。这款微型可穿戴录音机可以戴在手腕上或通过磁吸方式附着在衣服上。录音会实时保存在手机上，省去了手动记笔记的麻烦。该设备每月包含 300 分钟的免费转录时间，但通过每月 8.33 美元的 Pro 计划，你可以将转录时间升级至 1,200 分钟。今年，该公司正准备推出一款名为 Plaud Note Pro 的超薄笔记设备，售价 179 美元，目前已开放预订。

This small, retro-styled handheld device features a touchscreen and rotating camera, at a price point of $199. The device is designed to be phone-adjacent, allowing you to perform tasks such as booking flights, ordering meals, and controlling app

Rabbit R1（图片来源：Rabbit）：Rabbit R1 是另一款在科技界迅速成为热门话题的人工智能小工具，尽管它在首次发布时面临了一些挑战。这款小型复古风格的手持设备配有触摸屏和旋转摄像头，售价为 199 美元。该设备旨在作为手机的辅助工具，允许你执行诸如预订航班、订餐和控制应用程序等任务。
