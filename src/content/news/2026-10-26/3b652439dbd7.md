# A Recognizable Character Can Still Be Hard to Read

Can players recognize a character instantly and still struggle to control them? Kirby offers a useful example. A round silhouette can identify the character while giving the player surprisingly little information about which way they are facing. Those are different design problems. For a character artist or gameplay programmer, separating them creates a practical question: which information must survive when the attractive illustration becomes a small moving object on screen?

玩家能否在瞬间认出一个角色，却依然难以操控它？卡比（Kirby）就是一个很好的例子。圆形的轮廓可以让人认出角色，却几乎无法向玩家提供关于其朝向的任何信息。这些是不同的设计问题。对于角色美术师或游戏玩法程序员来说，将两者区分开来会引出一个实际问题：当精美的插画变成屏幕上一个小小的移动物体时，哪些信息必须被保留下来？

Recognition and direction are different jobs. In Nintendo's 2022 interview about *Kirby and the Forgotten Land*, director Tatsuya Kamiyama describes the difficulty of reading Kirby's facing direction from behind. He also explains why keeping an aiming arrow visible was unsatisfactory: the player could feel as though they were controlling the arrow rather than Kirby. The team describes an alternative: accounting for Kirby's and the camera's positions so attacks that appear to connect can land, even when they would otherwise narrowly miss. This is a developer account of a specific design solution, not a controlled study of character attachment. The interesting lesson is that the answer need not be another visible accessory. Camera, feedback, and gameplay rules can help preserve a character's existing form.

识别和辨别方向是两项不同的工作。在任天堂 2022 年关于《星之卡比：探索发现》的采访中，总监神山达哉描述了从背后观察卡比朝向的难度。他还解释了为什么保留瞄准箭头效果不佳：玩家可能会感觉自己是在控制箭头，而不是在控制卡比。团队描述了一种替代方案：通过计算卡比和摄像机的位置，使得看起来击中的攻击能够真正命中，即使在原本会擦肩而过的情况下也是如此。这是开发者对特定设计方案的说明，而非关于角色依恋的对照研究。有趣的一点是，答案并不一定非得是增加一个可见的辅助配件。摄像机、反馈和游戏规则都可以帮助保留角色原有的形态。

Give the outline a job before giving it more detail. Riot's *Clarity in League* describes silhouettes as central to champion recognition. It identifies a distinctive primary feature, such as Senna's large gun, and says facing direction should also be readable. Animation can contribute to identity, but relying heavily on it can make visual redesigns more difficult. These are production principles for a competitive game. They do not show that a particular shape makes people like a character more, nor do they prescribe one style for every genre. A useful design hypothesis is narrower: if the feature that expresses a character's personality also survives the gameplay camera, players have more opportunities to notice that personality during play.

在增加细节之前，先赋予轮廓明确的功能。拳头游戏（Riot）的《英雄联盟清晰度指南》将轮廓描述为英雄识别的核心。它指出了一些独特的首要特征（例如赛娜的大枪），并强调朝向也应该是可读的。动画可以增强角色特征，但过度依赖动画会使视觉重设计变得更加困难。这些是竞技游戏的制作原则。它们并不证明某种特定的形状会让人们更喜欢一个角色，也不为所有游戏类型规定同一种风格。一个有用的设计假设更为具体：如果表达角色个性的特征在游戏摄像机视角下依然清晰可见，玩家在游玩过程中就有更多机会注意到这种个性。

Consider a hypothetical creature that carries an oversized lantern. The lantern could suggest a cautious explorer. But if it overlaps the entire body at the default camera angle, the distinctive prop may conceal the creature's facing direction. Adding straps and engraving would not address that particular problem. Before detailing it, try changing one thing: the lantern's offset, the arm pose, or the camera angle. Keep the other variables fixed. The goal is to find the smallest change that restores the missing information without replacing the character's main visual idea.

设想一个携带超大灯笼的虚构生物。灯笼可能暗示它是一个谨慎的探险家。但如果灯笼在默认摄像机角度下遮挡了整个身体，这个独特的道具可能会掩盖该生物的朝向。增加绑带和雕刻并不能解决这个问题。在细化它之前，尝试改变一件事：灯笼的偏移量、手臂姿势或摄像机角度。保持其他变量不变。目标是找到最小的改动，在不替换角色核心视觉概念的前提下恢复缺失的信息。

Test three questions separately. A rough review can use screenshots or a simple playable scene. No finished textures are required. Identity: After introducing the roster, can someone match this figure to the correct character at normal gameplay size? Direction: Without a nameplate or aiming aid, which way do they think it is facing? Appeal: Which version do they prefer, and what feature explains that preference? Use the actual camera and a representative background. Test the smallest expected on-screen size, not only the presentation close-up. Include turns and ordinary poses rather than selecting the one angle where the design works best. Start with a flat silhouette to isolate overlap and contour problems, then repeat with the full presentation. Color, lighting, sound, and motion may legitimately carry information in the finished game. Removing them is a diagnostic step, not a demand that every frame work without them.

分别测试三个问题。初步审查可以使用截图或简单的可玩场景，无需完成纹理。识别度：在介绍角色阵容后，有人能在正常游戏尺寸下将此图形与正确的角色对应起来吗？方向感：在没有铭牌或瞄准辅助的情况下，他们认为角色朝向哪里？吸引力：他们更喜欢哪个版本，什么特征解释了这种偏好？使用实际的游戏摄像机和代表性背景。测试最小的预期屏幕尺寸，而不仅仅是展示用的特写。包含转弯和普通姿势，而不是只选择设计效果最好的那个角度。从扁平的轮廓开始以隔离重叠和轮廓问题，然后重复进行完整展示的测试。颜色、光照、声音和动作在成品游戏中确实可以承载信息。移除它们只是诊断步骤，而不是要求每一帧在没有这些元素时都能正常工作。

Record specific errors. “Mistook the backpack for the face in the rear view” suggests a different fix from “recognized the character but disliked the new proportions.” If comparing variants, vary their presentation order and avoid explaining the intended personality before asking. This is a proposed review procedure, not a reported experiment. A few colleagues' preferences cannot establish universal appeal or predict long-term attachment.

记录具体的错误。“在后视图中把背包误认为是脸”所对应的修复方案，与“认出了角色但讨厌新的比例”是完全不同的。如果比较多个变体，请改变它们的展示顺序，并在询问前避免解释预期的个性。这是一种建议的审查程序，而非正式的实验报告。少数同事的偏好无法建立普遍的吸引力，也无法预测长期的依恋感。

Preserve the character, fix the misunderstanding. A rounded, ambiguous shape may be exactly what a gentle exploration game wants. A competitive game may require stronger directional cues. A dialogue-heavy game may communicate identity mainly through faces and writing. The appropriate test depends on what players actually need to do. Do not automatically apply forgiving hit detection to competitive play, where it could change balance and expectations. Nor should every readability problem trigger a visual redesign: first identify whether the confusion comes from the model, pose, camera, effects, or control feedback. A useful result is more precise than “make the character stand out.” It is: “keep the lantern that makes this creature distinctive, but let players see where it is looking.” That gives art and gameplay a shared problem they can solve.

保留角色，修正误解。圆润、模糊的形状可能正是轻松探索类游戏所需要的。竞技游戏可能需要更强的方向提示。对话密集型游戏可能主要通过面部和文字来传达身份。合适的测试取决于玩家实际需要做什么。不要自动将宽容的碰撞检测应用于竞技游戏，因为这可能会改变平衡性和预期。同样，并非每个可读性问题都应触发视觉重设计：首先确定困惑是来自模型、姿势、摄像机、特效还是控制反馈。一个有用的结果比“让角色更突出”要精确得多。它应该是：“保留让这个生物与众不同的灯笼，但要让玩家看到它在看哪里。”这为美术和游戏玩法提供了一个可以共同解决的问题。
