A woman was arrested in Philly for blocking a delivery robot.

一名女子因阻挡送货机器人而在费城被捕。

Carolyn Marvin was charged with ‘disorderly conduct’ for protesting delivery robots taking jobs.

卡罗琳·马文（Carolyn Marvin）因抗议送货机器人抢走工作岗位而被控“扰乱治安”。

Carolyn Marvin was arrested and charged with disorderly conduct after blocking the path of an Uber delivery robot and refusing to get out of the way. Marvin told Philadelphia’s ABC13 “I know they’re taking the jobs of human delivery workers and so, I have been protesting them whenever I saw them by standing in front of them and preventing them from going forward.”

卡罗琳·马文在阻挡一辆优步（Uber）送货机器人的去路并拒绝让开后，被捕并被控扰乱治安。马文告诉费城的ABC13电视台：“我知道它们正在抢走人类送货员的工作，所以我每次见到它们时，都会通过站在它们面前阻止它们前进来进行抗议。”

Police responded to the scene and asked Marvin to move out of the robot’s way. When she refused, they placed her in cuffs and arrested her. The incident was captured on video and has now garnered considerable media attention.

警方赶到现场并要求马文从机器人前方移开。当她拒绝配合时，警方给她戴上手铐并将她逮捕。这一事件被视频记录下来，目前已引起了媒体的广泛关注。

The delivery robots have become a common sight on the streets of downtown Philadelphia and have sparked backlash. This is not even Marvin’s first brush with them. And earlier this year, one was kicked over in Center City.

送货机器人在费城市中心的街道上已随处可见，并引发了强烈抵制。这甚至不是马文第一次与它们发生冲突。今年早些时候，就曾有一台机器人在市中心被踢翻。

Broad concern has grown about how autonomous systems, including robotaxis, delivery robots, and AI agents, are impacting jobs, especially for lower-skill workers.

人们对于自动驾驶系统（包括自动驾驶出租车、送货机器人和人工智能代理）如何影响就业，尤其是对低技能工人的影响，感到日益担忧。
