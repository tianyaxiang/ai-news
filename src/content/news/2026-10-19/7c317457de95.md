The best AirPods cases are subjective, but luckily, you have thousands of options to match your style and mood. You can find a ton of novelty cases online, most of which are just fine. For this guide, we focused on cases with a WIRED angle. Rather than another case that looks like food or a licensed character, the recommendations below add some functionality or protection beyond the basics.

最好的 AirPods 保护壳因人而异，但幸运的是，你有成千上万种选择来匹配你的风格和心情。你可以在网上找到大量新奇的保护壳，其中大多数都还不错。在本指南中，我们重点介绍了具有 WIRED 视角的保护壳。与其选择那些看起来像食物或授权角色的保护壳，以下推荐的产品在基础功能之外增加了一些实用性或保护性。

**Best AirPods Case Overall: dbrand Grip Case**
The Dbrand Grip Case looks fantastic, and it doesn't add an irksome amount of bulk or weight to my headphones. The case is made of black rubber and can then be personalized with your choice of skins, plus an optional lanyard. There are more than 40 options, and you can swap them out whenever, though the sticker-like skins aren't reusable.

**最佳 AirPods 保护壳：dbrand Grip Case**
Dbrand Grip Case 看起来非常棒，而且它不会给我的耳机增加令人烦恼的体积或重量。这款保护壳由黑色橡胶制成，你可以选择自己喜欢的贴纸进行个性化定制，还可以选择搭配挂绳。它有超过 40 种选择，你可以随时更换，不过这些类似贴纸的皮肤是不可重复使用的。

**Best Rugged Case: UAG Scout AirPods Case**
UAG is in the business of making tough accessories, and this sturdy option adds a thick layer of rubber around the original AirPods case to protect it from drops. It has a nice grippy texture and snug fit, with squared edges that help me get a better grip when walking around. It also comes with a sweet-looking carabiner that is optional but I quite liked.

**最佳三防保护壳：UAG Scout AirPods Case**
UAG 专注于制造坚固的配件，这款结实的产品在 AirPods 原装充电盒外增加了一层厚厚的橡胶，以防止跌落损坏。它具有良好的防滑纹理和贴合度，方形边缘有助于我在走动时更好地抓握。它还附带了一个外观精美的登山扣，虽然是可选配件，但我非常喜欢。

**Best MagSafe Case: ESR Orbit Hybrid Case**
Most cases in this guide have support for wireless charging, but if you're all in on MagSafe accessories, you'll love ESR’s Orbit case. It comes with magnets that attach securely to a MagSafe charger. The top and bottom pieces also snap shut with magnets, which helps keep the lid from accidentally sliding off.

**最佳 MagSafe 保护壳：ESR Orbit Hybrid Case**
本指南中的大多数保护壳都支持无线充电，但如果你是 MagSafe 配件的忠实用户，你会喜欢 ESR 的 Orbit 保护壳。它配有磁铁，可以牢固地吸附在 MagSafe 充电器上。顶部和底部组件也通过磁铁扣合，这有助于防止盖子意外滑落。

**Best Leather Case: Nomad Modern Leather Case**
This leather case is made from Horween leather, which is minimally treated and develops a rich patina over time. It feels slippery, which I didn't love, but it's still protective without adding meaningful bulk to your headphones. It fits snugly and has cutouts for the light and speaker holes.

**最佳皮革保护壳：Nomad Modern Leather Case**
这款皮革保护壳由 Horween 皮革制成，经过极简处理，随着时间的推移会产生丰富的铜绿光泽。它摸起来有点滑，我不太喜欢这一点，但它在不增加耳机明显体积的情况下依然提供了保护。它贴合紧密，并为充电指示灯和扬声器孔留出了开口。
