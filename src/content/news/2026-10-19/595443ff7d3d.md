A Cannon for Pepper. Shaking isn't manly. Grinding is manly. Cannons are manly. Grinding so dominantly that your pepper grinder can plausibly be called a cannon is manly. So is deftly tooled precision of any kind. But Männkitchen’s Pepper Cannon isn't an expensive gimmick for the insecure. Its stainless steel burrs will grind peppercorns to the exact right size, among 50 settings, and this will end up mattering to anybody who spends a lot of time seasoning meat or building a rub. The all-metal construction will last decades. And it'll grind your pepper so fast it'll feel like the Dust Bowl got spicy.
胡椒大炮。摇晃胡椒瓶不够硬汉，研磨才够硬汉。大炮才够硬汉。研磨得如此霸气，以至于你的胡椒研磨器可以被称为“大炮”，这才是硬汉。任何精巧的工具精度也是如此。但Männkitchen的胡椒大炮并非为缺乏安全感的人准备的昂贵噱头。其不锈钢磨芯可在50种设置中将胡椒粒研磨至精确尺寸，这对那些花大量时间腌制肉类或制作干擦料的人来说至关重要。全金属结构可以使用几十年。它研磨胡椒的速度之快，会让你感觉仿佛沙尘暴变得辛辣起来。

Lasers for Measuring Everything. You know what men like? Knowing how long something is, or how far away it is. Know what they (I) don't like? Stretching out a dinky tape measure that keeps shimmying or bending or snapping back into the roll. The solution to everything is always lasers. Lasers can do anything. Just ask Elon Musk. Anyway, just point this Mileseey S50 at anything, press the button, set your starting measuring point, and it'll tell you how far away it is. (It measures to 1/32 of an inch, but I wouldn't trust it past 1/16 of an inch.) Get a tripod for it and you can also do point-to-point measurements from a distance. I've measured windows for fittings. Door jambs. Most of the rooms and spaces in my house. I've barely used tape for four months. Why use tape when you can use lasers? Mileseey says the green laser light on this S50 is more visible than red light during the daytime. Maybe this is true. There are also a whole lot of trigonometric functions I haven't figured out, and the included instructions are only half helpful. But mostly I just want to know: How big is that thing? How far away?
测量万物的激光。你知道男人喜欢什么吗？喜欢知道某样东西有多长，或者离得有多远。你知道他们（包括我）不喜欢什么吗？不喜欢拉开一把总是晃动、弯曲或缩回卷尺盒里的劣质卷尺。解决一切问题的方案永远是激光。激光无所不能，问问埃隆·马斯克就知道了。总之，只需将这台Mileseey S50对准任何物体，按下按钮，设定起始测量点，它就会告诉你距离有多远。（它能测量到1/32英寸，但我建议不要完全信任超过1/16英寸的精度。）配上三脚架，你还可以进行远距离的点对点测量。我用它测量过窗户安装尺寸、门框，以及家里大部分房间和空间。我已经四个月没用过卷尺了。既然能用激光，为什么要用卷尺呢？Mileseey称，S50的绿光在白天比红光更清晰。也许是真的。它还有很多我没搞懂的三角函数功能，附带的说明书也只解释了一半。但归根结底，我只想知道：那东西有多大？离得有多远？

The Manliest Grill Gloves. Nothing is manlier than overkill. Lots of grill gloves can take a little heat, and save you from a hospital visit on a grill day. But can they take you to the gates of hell? These Hell Hands grill gloves from Big Green Egg are rated up to 900 degrees Fahrenheit. They're made with thick cowhide that goes most of the way up to your elbows—and look like something you'd wear for the zombie apocalypse. The pads are made with thick, puncture-resistant Armortex fabric and stitched with Kevlar. I don't know if this makes these gloves bulletproof. But what I do know is that I have placed my palm flat onto a scalding 500-degree cast-iron pan while wearing one of these Hell Hands. I barely felt warmth, let alone heat. These gloves are monsters. But note that these gloves are thick enough that your hands will feel more like mittens than hands. You'll need to use manly finger strength to flex them.
最硬汉的烧烤手套。没有什么比“过度配置”更硬汉的了。很多烧烤手套能抵御一点热量，让你在烧烤日免于去医院。但它们能带你直抵地狱之门吗？这款来自Big Green Egg的“地狱之手”（Hell Hands）烧烤手套额定耐热高达900华氏度。它们由厚实的牛皮制成，长度几乎覆盖到手肘，看起来就像你在僵尸末日中会穿戴的装备。手掌部位采用厚实、防穿刺的Armortex面料，并用凯夫拉纤维缝合。我不知道这是否意味着手套能防弹，但我知道的是，我曾戴着其中一只“地狱之手”将手掌平放在滚烫的500度铸铁锅上。我几乎感觉不到温暖，更别提热量了。这些手套简直是怪物。但请注意，这些手套非常厚，你的手戴上后感觉更像连指手套。你需要用硬汉般的指力来弯曲它们。
