It used to be so easy to get away with things in school. The teacher, after all, was always turned around writing at the chalkboard! But then came a bit of technology, previously mostly used for military purposes, called the overhead projector. It would change far more than just the disciplinary record of students everywhere.

过去在学校里做小动作总是很容易。毕竟，老师总是背对着学生在黑板上写字！但随后出现了一项此前主要用于军事目的的技术，即高射投影仪。它所改变的远不止是各地学生的纪律记录。

In this episode of Version History, we tell the story of the overhead projector. David Pierce is joined by The Verge’s Jake Kastrenakes and Stanford professor Antero Garcia to discuss how overhead projectors were developed, how they made their way into classrooms, and how they changed so much about the way kids learn. They also try to figure out why overhead projectors have mostly gone away, and whether the things that replaced them — from PowerPoint presentations to computer screens to those new-fangled smart boards — actually improved on the OHP.

在本期《版本历史》中，我们讲述了高射投影仪的故事。David Pierce与The Verge的Jake Kastrenakes以及斯坦福大学教授Antero Garcia一起，讨论了高射投影仪是如何被开发出来的，它们是如何进入教室的，以及它们在多大程度上改变了孩子们的学习方式。他们还试图找出高射投影仪为何大多已销声匿迹，以及取代它们的事物——从PowerPoint演示文稿到电脑屏幕，再到那些新颖的智能白板——是否真的比高射投影仪有所改进。
