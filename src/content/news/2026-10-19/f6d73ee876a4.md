# TechCrunch Mobility: Rivian’s next act

Welcome back to TechCrunch Mobility, your hub for the future of transportation and now, more than ever, the role AI is playing in it. To get this in your inbox, sign up here for free — just click TechCrunch Mobility! As I wrapped my TechCrunch Disrupt interview with Rivian CEO and founder RJ Scaringe, I kept circling back to one question: What does Scaringe want Rivian to become? If you look at Rivian’s moves over the past year, it’s clear that Scaringe wants the company to be much more than an EV maker. But he doesn’t want to stray completely from that mission either.

欢迎回到 TechCrunch Mobility，这里是您了解未来交通以及人工智能在其中所扮演角色的中心。如需在收件箱中接收此内容，请在此处免费注册——只需点击 TechCrunch Mobility！在结束我对 Rivian 首席执行官兼创始人 RJ Scaringe 的 TechCrunch Disrupt 采访时，我不断回到一个问题：Scaringe 希望 Rivian 成为什么？如果你观察 Rivian 过去一年的举动，很明显 Scaringe 希望该公司不仅仅是一家电动汽车制造商。但他也不想完全偏离这一使命。

Rivian is far more vertically integrated than it was just a couple of years ago. It has developed its own AI models to power its in-vehicle assistant and is building an end-to-end automated driving stack for its passenger vehicles and future robotaxis through a partnership with Uber. It’s even developing custom silicon, a 5 nm processor in collaboration with Arm and TSMC. And then there is its $5.8 billion joint technology venture with VW Group focused on electric architecture and software.

与几年前相比，Rivian 的垂直整合程度要高得多。它开发了自己的 AI 模型来驱动车载助手，并通过与 Uber 的合作，为其乘用车和未来的自动驾驶出租车构建端到端的自动驾驶技术栈。它甚至正在与 Arm 和台积电合作开发定制芯片，即一款 5 纳米处理器。此外，它还与大众集团成立了一家价值 58 亿美元的合资技术公司，专注于电气架构和软件。

Scaringe’s ambitions extend beyond Rivian as well, although there is always a link back. He created a micromobility skunkworks program that has since spun out into a startup called Also. And he founded an industrial robotics startup called Mind Robotics, which is now 31% owned by Rivian and counts the automaker as a prospective customer. (Scaringe announced onstage that former Ford, Apple, and Tesla executive Doug Field has been hired as president of Mind Robotics.) Scaringe’s aspirations were on display as he talked of not only expanding the joint venture with VW to cover AI and autonomy, but also licensing Rivian’s tech to “many different brands.”

Scaringe 的雄心壮志也延伸到了 Rivian 之外，尽管它们之间总是有所关联。他创建了一个微型交通“臭鼬工厂”项目，该项目后来分拆成了一家名为 Also 的初创公司。他还创立了一家名为 Mind Robotics 的工业机器人初创公司，该公司目前由 Rivian 持股 31%，并将这家汽车制造商视为潜在客户。（Scaringe 在台上宣布，前福特、苹果和特斯拉高管 Doug Field 已被聘为 Mind Robotics 的总裁。）Scaringe 的抱负在谈话中展露无遗，他不仅谈到了将与大众的合资企业扩展到人工智能和自动驾驶领域，还谈到了将 Rivian 的技术授权给“许多不同的品牌”。

So what does Rivian look like at the end of the decade? Certainly not just a maker of electric trucks and SUVs. The term I kept coming back to was “tech supplier.” Hear me out. Rivian is clearing a path toward licensing its electric architecture and software as well as its AI models and autonomy stack. Meanwhile, the Mind Robotics startup it partially owns is building industrial robots to not only be used in Rivian factories, but also sold to other companies. Then there’s the robotaxi business. A successful launch with Uber could open the door to more deals.

那么，十年后的 Rivian 会是什么样子？肯定不仅仅是电动卡车和 SUV 的制造商。我脑海中不断浮现的词是“技术供应商”。听我解释。Rivian 正在为授权其电气架构和软件以及 AI 模型和自动驾驶技术栈铺平道路。与此同时，其部分持股的 Mind Robotics 初创公司正在制造工业机器人，这些机器人不仅用于 Rivian 的工厂，还将出售给其他公司。此外还有自动驾驶出租车业务。与 Uber 的成功合作可能会为更多交易打开大门。

And Scaringe is already imagining how autonomy could reshape Rivian’s own vehicles. “Where it gets really interesting is then you think beyond R2 and R3 and start to imagine the form factors that you want to build for a personally owned autonomous vehicle. You know, it opens up all kinds of design opportunities. It can create dramatically different experiences,” he said in an interview with TechCrunch. “I’m wildly excited about what the next 10 years look like in transportation because of what this enables us to dream and think about.”

Scaringe 已经在构想自动驾驶将如何重塑 Rivian 自己的车辆。“真正有趣的地方在于，当你超越 R2 和 R3，开始想象你想为个人拥有的自动驾驶汽车构建什么样的外形时。你知道，这开启了各种各样的设计机会。它可以创造出截然不同的体验，”他在接受 TechCrunch 采访时说。“我对未来 10 年的交通运输前景感到无比兴奋，因为这使我们能够去梦想和思考。”
