Valerion’s VisionMaster Kickstarter campaign raised more than $8 million in 2024, making it the most-funded campaign on the platform that year. After spending the past few weeks with the flagship VisionMaster Max, I’m confident there are plenty of satisfied backers.

Valerion 的 VisionMaster 在 2024 年的 Kickstarter 众筹活动中筹集了超过 800 万美元，成为该平台当年筹款最多的项目。在过去几周体验了旗舰款 VisionMaster Max 后，我相信会有许多满意的支持者。

At $3,999, it costs far more than excellent point-and-play rivals, such as the Nebula X1 (9/10 WIRED Recommends), but considerably less than native 4K home-theater projectors from brands like Sony and JVC. That leaves the Max straddling two very different camps.

其 3,999 美元的售价远高于 Nebula X1（WIRED 推荐评分 9/10）等优秀的“即插即用”型竞争对手，但又远低于索尼和 JVC 等品牌的原生 4K 家庭影院投影仪。这使得 Max 处于两个截然不同的阵营之间。

Its compact design, built-in Google TV, and automated setup suggest the classification of a premium lifestyle projector, while optical zoom, vertical lens shift, a mechanical iris, and sophisticated black-level processing point toward something far more capable. It’s easy to use and almost too pretty to ceiling-mount, but arguably too sophisticated to spend its life propped up on a side table. So the question remains: Who is the VisionMaster Max actually for?

其紧凑的设计、内置的 Google TV 和自动化设置使其被归类为高端生活方式投影仪，而光学变焦、垂直镜头位移、机械光圈和复杂的黑位处理技术则表明它具备更强大的性能。它易于使用，外观精美到让人不忍心吊装，但又过于精密，不适合长期放在边桌上。因此问题依然存在：VisionMaster Max 到底适合谁？

Setting Up ShopPhotograph: Chris HaslamThe 16.5-pound VisionMaster Max is simple to get up and running. Google TV grants instant access to many streaming services, while auto-keystone and autofocus make positioning a frictionless process. (However, because keystone correction digitally reshapes the image, when used heavily, it can reduce resolution—but that's true for all devices with the feature.) Optical zoom, with a 0.9 to 1.5:1 throw ratio, lets you resize the picture without moving the projector. There's also vertical lens shift, which moves the image up or down without needing to resort to digital correction. All of this means that if you’ve got a blank wall and five minutes, you can stream Emily in Paris on a 90-inch screen in no time.

设置与安装（摄影：Chris Haslam）：这款 16.5 磅重的 VisionMaster Max 启动非常简单。Google TV 可即时访问多种流媒体服务，自动梯形校正和自动对焦使定位过程顺畅无阻。（然而，由于梯形校正会对图像进行数字重塑，过度使用可能会降低分辨率——但这对于所有具备此功能的设备都是如此。）0.9 到 1.5:1 投射比的光学变焦功能让你无需移动投影仪即可调整画面大小。此外还有垂直镜头位移功能，无需数字校正即可上下移动图像。这意味着只要有一面空白墙和五分钟时间，你就能立刻在 90 英寸屏幕上观看《艾米丽在巴黎》。

It’s clever tech, though not fully unique, with the XGIMI Horizon range and Nebula X1 offering similar optical-image manipulation. Even so, the VisionMaster Max offers considerably more control than most point-and-play designs. Praise is also due for the remote, which looks and feels great. Many brands scrimp on details like accessories, but Valerion maintains its premium feel throughout.

这是一种巧妙的技术，虽然并非独一无二，极米 Horizon 系列和 Nebula X1 也提供类似的光学图像处理功能。即便如此，VisionMaster Max 提供的控制力仍远超大多数“即插即用”型设计。遥控器也值得称赞，外观和手感都很棒。许多品牌在配件等细节上会缩减成本，但 Valerion 始终保持着高端质感。

Looks Great, Sounds OKPhotograph: Chris HaslamThe VisionMaster Max produces a seriously bright image. Its 3,500 ISO lumen RGB triple-laser light source easily projected a 75-inch picture I could happily watch during the day in a bright room. Valerion says it’s good for images between 40 and 300 inches. I maxed out at 130 inches but was impressed by how even the detail remained right to the edges at that size.

外观精美，音质尚可（摄影：Chris Haslam）：VisionMaster Max 产生的图像非常明亮。其 3,500 ISO 流明的 RGB 三色激光光源可以轻松投射出 75 英寸的画面，即使在明亮的房间里白天观看也很舒适。Valerion 表示它适用于 40 到 300 英寸的画面。我测试了最大 130 英寸，令我印象深刻的是，即使在那个尺寸下，边缘的细节依然保持得非常均匀。

While I watched during the day, the colors appeared a bit washed out in this environment, but much less so than I expected. Once I watched in the dark, however, I enjoyed punchy high-dynamic-range (HDR) highlights, excellent detail, and smooth motion.

虽然我在白天观看时，环境光导致色彩显得有些暗淡，但比我预期的要好得多。然而，一旦在黑暗环境中观看，我便能享受到强劲的高动态范围 (HDR) 高光、出色的细节和流畅的动态效果。

In terms of color, the Max has 110 percent Rec.2020 coverage, meaning the lasers can reproduce an exceptionally wide range of colors, far exceeding the needs for ultra-high-definition (UHD) and HDR video. In my living room, colors looked rich without becoming cartoonish, while the combination of brightness, detail, and contrast gave movies cinematic quality.

在色彩方面，Max 拥有 110% 的 Rec.2020 色域覆盖率，这意味着激光可以还原极其广泛的色彩范围，远超超高清 (UHD) 和 HDR 视频的需求。在我的客厅里，色彩看起来丰富而不显虚假，亮度、细节和对比度的结合赋予了电影影院级的质感。

Black levels with most home projectors are good, but not brilliant. Here, however, the proprietary six-stage iris self-adjusts the aperture from scene to scene, closing for darker images to deepen blacks, and opening up when a scene calls for more brightness. Enhanced Black Level (EBL) works alongside the iris, electronically reducing laser output in darker scenes, adjusting the image curve to maintain details. Together, these features dramatically reduce the charcoal-gray blacks common with this type of projector. I was astonished by the depth and shadow detail it produced without losing brighter details. Even the film The Dark Knight, with its muted color grading, looked good.

大多数家用投影仪的黑位表现尚可，但并不出众。然而，这里采用了专有的六级光圈，可根据场景自动调整孔径：在较暗的画面中关闭以加深黑色，在需要更高亮度的场景中打开。增强黑位 (EBL) 功能与光圈协同工作，在较暗场景中以电子方式降低激光输出，并调整图像曲线以保持细节。这些功能共同作用，显著减少了此类投影仪常见的炭灰色黑位问题。它所呈现的深度和阴影细节令我惊叹，且没有丢失高光细节。即使是色调暗淡的电影《蝙蝠侠：黑暗骑士》，看起来效果也很好。

Photograph: Chris HaslamThough I didn't put the VisionMaster Max through its paces for video-game testing (I’m not much of a gamer, unless you count Mario Kart battles on the Nintendo Switch), the projector's specifications are nonetheless impressive. It supports refresh rates up to 240 Hz at 1080p, with claimed input lag as low as 4 milliseconds (ms); for 4K, 60-Hz gaming, that input lag is rated at 15 ms. Two HDMI 2.1 inputs and auto low-latency mode further strengthen its gaming credentials, although it lacks a variable refresh rate (VRR) to dynamically smooth out delays.

（摄影：Chris Haslam）虽然我没有对 VisionMaster Max 进行深入的电子游戏测试（我不太玩游戏，除非算上任天堂 Switch 上的《马力欧卡丁车》对战），但该投影仪的规格依然令人印象深刻。它支持 1080p 下高达 240Hz 的刷新率，声称输入延迟低至 4 毫秒 (ms)；对于 4K 60Hz 游戏，输入延迟额定为 15 毫秒。两个 HDMI 2.1 输入接口和自动低延迟模式进一步增强了其游戏性能，尽管它缺乏用于动态平滑延迟的可变刷新率 (VRR) 功能。

Less impressive than its picture is its sound: The VisionMaster Max has two 12-watt speakers, and audio is clean, detailed, with effects neatly separated from dialog. But it still suffers from its box form factor. To be fair, with the exception of Anker’s removable satellite speaker designs like the Nebula X1, audio fails to keep up with image quality in many lifestyle projectors. I'd contend that at this price, an all-in-one projector should provide audio that matches the pixel-perfect picture. The Max’s speakers are fine, but push it beyond level 25 of 100, and it just sounds loud.

相比画质，其音质表现稍逊一筹：VisionMaster Max 配备了两个 12 瓦扬声器，音频清晰、细节丰富，音效与对白分离得当。但它仍然受限于其盒式外形。平心而论，除了像 Nebula X1 那样采用可拆卸卫星音箱设计的 Anker 产品外，许多生活方式投影仪的音频都无法跟上画质。我认为，在这个价位上，一体式投影仪应该提供与像素级完美画质相匹配的音频。Max 的扬声器表现尚可，但音量一旦超过 100 级中的 25 级，听起来就只是单纯的“大声”而已。

Thankfully, since the projector supports both DTS and Dolby Atmos, you can connect it to a decent 5.1/7.1 home theater audio system. You can also wirelessly link to a Bluetooth speaker, which makes for a clean living-room setup, although latency can be an issue. For reliable synchronization and true surround sound, HDMI eARC remains the better audio option.

值得庆幸的是，由于该投影仪同时支持 DTS 和杜比全景声，你可以将其连接到一套不错的 5.1/7.1 家庭影院音响系统。你也可以无线连接蓝牙音箱，这能让客厅布置更整洁，尽管延迟可能是一个问题。为了获得可靠的同步和真正的环绕声，HDMI eARC 仍然是更好的音频选择。
