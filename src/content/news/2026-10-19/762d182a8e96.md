“Fuck you you fucking whore. Fuck you”. A text I received from a colleague I was told was “too valuable to reprimand". I'm so tired of sitting through “Women in tech” panels where women are told what they can do to survive in the tech industry. The ones where women are coached on confidence, on negotiating, on resilience, on not shrinking. The implicit message: the problem is fixable if women just try harder.

“去你妈的，你这臭婊子。去死吧。”这是我收到的一条短信，来自一位被告知“太有价值而不能训斥”的同事。我厌倦了参加那些“科技界女性”座谈会，会上人们告诉女性如何在科技行业生存。在这些会上，女性被教导要自信、要学会谈判、要坚韧、不要退缩。其隐含的信息是：只要女性更加努力，问题就能解决。

This talk isn't that. This will be an uncomfortable account of what men actually said and did to one woman, over one career. Ask any woman in the room. They've got similar stories. This isn't uncommon, it's a fucking Tuesday.

这次演讲并非如此。这将是一份令人不安的陈述，讲述了男性在一段职业生涯中对一名女性实际说过和做过的事情。问问在场的任何女性，她们都有类似的故事。这并不罕见，这简直是家常便饭。

I'm not going to suggest solutions. That's on the men in the audience. I ask you to reckon with what is happening around you. Talk to your friends about it. See it, say it, fucking sort it.

我不会提出解决方案。那是台下男性观众的事。我要求你们正视周围正在发生的事情。和你们的朋友谈谈。看见它，说出来，他妈的去解决它。
