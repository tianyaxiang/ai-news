# The microscope is the mask: privileged views and labels from a cryo-ET forward model
# 显微镜即掩码：来自冷冻电镜断层成像（cryo-ET）正向模型的特权视图与标签

**Abstract:** We explore the use of simulated data for training a model for protein annotation in crowded cryo-electron tomography volumes reconstructed from images collected at limited tilt angles and severely corrupted by the measurement operator.
**摘要：** 我们探索了如何利用模拟数据来训练模型，以对拥挤的冷冻电子断层扫描（cryo-ET）体积进行蛋白质注释。这些体积是从有限倾斜角度采集的图像中重建的，并受到测量算子的严重干扰。

Firstly, we leverage the corruptions imposed by the forward model to generate domain-specific augmented paired views of the exact same scene for an invariance objective integrated into the LeJEPA self-supervised training framework.
首先，我们利用正向模型所带来的干扰，为同一场景生成特定领域的增强配对视图，并将其整合到 LeJEPA 自监督训练框架的不变性目标中。

Secondly, we use additional information from the simulation pipeline such as the positions and identity of proteins in the simulated volumes to inform the architecture of the model and the loss function, so that semantic information is localised at protein positions in the resulting dense feature volume.
其次，我们利用模拟流程中的额外信息（如模拟体积中蛋白质的位置和身份）来指导模型架构和损失函数的设计，从而使语义信息能够定位在所得密集特征体积中的蛋白质位置上。

The resulting model, CARNIVAL, is evaluated without finetuning on classification and detection tasks in real tomograms, using a benchmark dataset containing multiple protein types and two tomogram processing types.
所得模型 CARNIVAL 在无需微调的情况下，在真实断层扫描图的分类和检测任务上进行了评估，评估使用了包含多种蛋白质类型和两种断层扫描处理类型的基准数据集。

We show that CARNIVAL outperforms a state-of-the-art model trained using a contrastive objective on simulated data but without forward model-based paired views or privileged information.
我们证明，CARNIVAL 的表现优于目前最先进的模型；该对比模型同样在模拟数据上使用对比目标进行训练，但未使用基于正向模型的配对视图或特权信息。
