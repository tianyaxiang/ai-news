# Object Concepts Emerge from Motion
# 从运动中涌现的物体概念

**Abstract:** Object-centric visual representations are important for physical-world perception, but existing visual pretraining methods often capture semantic categories without preserving the identity and coherence of individual instances. We present a biologically inspired framework that learns object-centric representations for single images from raw videos. Our approach uses motion boundaries as a source of object-level grouping: off-the-shelf optical flow and clustering produce pseudo-instance masks, which supervise a single-image encoder with pixel-level pairwise metric learning. The framework requires neither human annotations nor camera calibration.

**摘要：** 以物体为中心的视觉表征对于物理世界的感知至关重要，但现有的视觉预训练方法往往只能捕捉语义类别，而无法保持单个实例的身份和连贯性。我们提出了一个受生物学启发的框架，能够从原始视频中学习单张图像的物体中心表征。我们的方法利用运动边界作为物体级分组的来源：通过现成的光流法和聚类算法生成伪实例掩码，并利用像素级的成对度量学习来监督单图像编码器。该框架既不需要人工标注，也不需要相机标定。

We first obtain 195 million pseudo-labeled frames from 7,163 hours of driving and web videos, then expand the supervision to 421 million frames with Motion-Verified Self-Training, which combines model proposals with motion evidence. We train encoders up to Swin-H and distill the learned representations into a family of Swin backbones. Across monocular depth estimation, 3D object detection, 3D occupancy prediction, and end-to-end planning, the resulting models achieve competitive or superior performance relative to supervised and self-supervised pretraining baselines, with particularly strong transfer on geometry- and instance-sensitive tasks.

我们首先从 7,163 小时的驾驶和网络视频中获取了 1.95 亿帧伪标签图像，随后通过“运动验证自训练”（Motion-Verified Self-Training）将监督规模扩展至 4.21 亿帧，该方法结合了模型建议与运动证据。我们将编码器训练至 Swin-H 规模，并将学习到的表征蒸馏到一系列 Swin 主干网络中。在单目深度估计、3D 物体检测、3D 占用预测和端到端规划等任务中，所得模型相比监督学习和自监督预训练基线表现出竞争性或更优的性能，特别是在对几何和实例敏感的任务中表现出极强的迁移能力。

These results show that motion-derived supervision can teach static image encoders to represent visual instances, providing a complementary direction for scalable visual pretraining.

这些结果表明，源自运动的监督信号可以教会静态图像编码器如何表征视觉实例，为可扩展的视觉预训练提供了一个互补的方向。
