# Inbundly – Google Inbox-style bundles for Gmail

Inbundly brings Google Inbox-style bundles back to Gmail: emails sharing a label are grouped into collapsible bundles so you can skim, prioritize, and archive in batches. An actively maintained, privacy-friendly continuation of the open-source Inboxy, plus custom bundles, priority rules, and label colors. 100% local. Chrome + Firefox.

Inbundly 将 Google Inbox 风格的邮件分组功能带回了 Gmail：共享同一标签的邮件会被归类到可折叠的组中，方便你进行快速浏览、优先级排序和批量归档。它是开源项目 Inboxy 的一个积极维护且注重隐私的延续版本，并增加了自定义分组、优先级规则和标签颜色等功能。该插件 100% 本地运行，支持 Chrome 和 Firefox 浏览器。

I built Inbundly because I still miss Inbox's way of organizing email by intent, and because I now triage my inbox together with AI agents, which work far better on a structured inbox than a firehose. Inboxy (open source) deserved a modern, actively maintained continuation, so I've been building this on weekends and polishing it until it was ready to ship. Zero data collection, GPL-3.0, full credit upstream.

我开发 Inbundly 是因为我依然怀念 Inbox 那种按意图组织邮件的方式，而且我现在会配合 AI 代理来处理收件箱，相比于杂乱无章的邮件流，结构化的收件箱能让 AI 发挥出更好的效果。Inboxy（开源项目）值得拥有一个现代且持续维护的后续版本，所以我利用周末时间开发并打磨了它，直到现在正式发布。该项目零数据收集，采用 GPL-3.0 协议，并完全保留了上游项目的贡献者署名。
