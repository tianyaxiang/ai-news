# A secret new Elizabeth Holmes documentary stuns Telluride
# 一部关于伊丽莎白·霍姆斯的神秘新纪录片惊艳特柳赖德电影节

Telluride just managed one of its best-kept secrets in years, per The Hollywood Reporter. On Sunday night, festivalgoers filed into a 650-seat theater with their phones locked away, having no idea what they were about to see, told only that it ran nearly three hours, hadn’t been shown anywhere, and that its principal creators would be in the audience.

据《好莱坞报道》称，特柳赖德电影节刚刚成功保守了近年来最严密的秘密之一。周日晚上，电影节的观众们在手机被统一收缴的情况下进入了一个拥有650个座位的影院。他们对即将看到的内容一无所知，只被告知该片时长近三小时，此前从未在任何地方放映过，且主要创作者将亲临现场。

The surprise premiere they went on to enjoy is a new documentary from co-directors Nathan Fielder and Lance Oppenheim about Theranos founder Elizabeth Holmes. Called “You Can See Everything,” the film is said to capture Holmes and her husband, Billy Evans, in the 34 days before she reported to federal prison in May 2023 to begin an 11-year sentence for defrauding investors about her blood-testing technology.

他们随后欣赏到的这部惊喜首映片，是由内森·菲尔德（Nathan Fielder）和兰斯·奥本海姆（Lance Oppenheim）联合执导的关于 Theranos 创始人伊丽莎白·霍姆斯（Elizabeth Holmes）的纪录片。这部名为《你可以看到一切》（You Can See Everything）的影片，据称记录了霍姆斯及其丈夫比利·埃文斯（Billy Evans）在2023年5月霍姆斯入狱前的34天生活。当时，她因在血液检测技术上欺诈投资者而被判处11年监禁。

Since then, her legal fight has limped along without success. Her appeals to overturn the conviction and shorten her sentence were rejected in February of last year. She then went a different route last year, asking President Trump to commute her sentence. That clemency petition is still listed as pending, meaning it’s officially been opened and remains under review.

自那以后，她的法律抗争一直进展缓慢且未获成功。她推翻定罪和缩短刑期的上诉在去年2月被驳回。随后，她在去年尝试了另一条途径，请求特朗普总统为其减刑。该减刑请愿书目前仍处于“待处理”状态，这意味着它已被正式受理并仍在审查中。
