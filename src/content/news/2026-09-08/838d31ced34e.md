# FailSAE: Towards Interpretable Failure Prediction for Vision-Language Models via Sparse Autoencoders
# FailSAE：通过稀疏自编码器实现视觉-语言模型的可解释性故障预测

Vision-language models (VLMs), such as CLIP, have achieved strong performance across multimodal tasks by aligning visual and textual representations in a shared embedding space. As VLMs are increasingly used for high-stakes domains, failure prediction becomes critical for risk-aware deployment and human intervention.
视觉-语言模型（VLM，如 CLIP）通过在共享嵌入空间中对齐视觉和文本表示，在多模态任务中取得了卓越的性能。随着 VLM 越来越多地应用于高风险领域，故障预测对于风险感知部署和人工干预变得至关重要。

Existing failure prediction methods typically rely on confidence scores or auxiliary classifiers. Although these methods are effective on predicting VLM failures, they provide limited interpretability. In this work, we investigate the use of Sparse Autoencoders (SAEs) for interpretable failure prediction in VLMs.
现有的故障预测方法通常依赖于置信度分数或辅助分类器。尽管这些方法在预测 VLM 故障方面行之有效，但它们的可解释性有限。在这项工作中，我们研究了利用稀疏自编码器（SAE）来实现 VLM 的可解释性故障预测。

We formulate failure prediction as a classification task over sparse SAE latent activations and introduce a three-stage failure-aware training pipeline that encourages the learned latent directions to remain interpretable while becoming more informative for failure prediction.
我们将故障预测建模为基于稀疏 SAE 潜在激活的分类任务，并引入了一个三阶段的“故障感知”训练流程。该流程旨在促使学习到的潜在方向在保持可解释性的同时，为故障预测提供更丰富的信息。

Our experiments show that the resulting framework outperforms the evaluated baselines in failure prediction. Further analysis suggests that failure-aware training encourages SAE latent directions to capture more class-specific concepts.
实验结果表明，该框架在故障预测性能上优于现有的基准方法。进一步分析表明，故障感知训练能够促使 SAE 的潜在方向捕捉到更多与类别相关的概念。

We also use the SAE to provide a concept-level analysis of how model representations change during failures, revealing a shift from class-specific concepts toward more ambiguous or style-related concepts. Finally, we explore how the learned SAE latent directions can support runtime failure recovery.
我们还利用 SAE 对模型表示在故障期间的变化进行了概念层面的分析，揭示了模型从关注类别特定概念向关注更模糊或与风格相关概念的转变。最后，我们探讨了学习到的 SAE 潜在方向如何支持运行时的故障恢复。
