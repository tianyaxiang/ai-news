# ProToMEx: Rapid, Interpretable Explanations via Structured Representations
# ProToMEx：通过结构化表示实现快速、可解释的解释

**Abstract:** Existing post-hoc explainers for machine learning classifiers primarily focus on feature attribution, assigning importance scores to individual features. While valuable, this approach struggles to articulate the complex, combinatorial patterns that often drive a model's decision-making process.
**摘要：** 现有的机器学习分类器事后解释器（post-hoc explainers）主要关注特征归因，即为单个特征分配重要性分数。虽然这种方法很有价值，但它难以阐明通常驱动模型决策过程的复杂组合模式。

To overcome this limitation, we introduce ProToMEx, a new paradigm for explainability that leverages Probabilistic Topic Models (PTMs). Our model-agnostic framework learns latent "topics" that represent distinct, high-level reasons for a classification, moving beyond simple feature importance to reveal underlying semantic structures.
为了克服这一局限性，我们引入了 ProToMEx，这是一种利用概率主题模型（PTMs）的可解释性新范式。我们这种与模型无关的框架能够学习代表分类中不同高层原因的潜在“主题”，从而超越简单的特征重要性，揭示潜在的语义结构。

ProToMEx naturally provides both global explanations of a model's overall behaviour and local explanations that can disentangle multiple co-existing reasons for a specific prediction.
ProToMEx 自然地提供了模型整体行为的全局解释，以及能够解构特定预测中多个共存原因的局部解释。

We demonstrate empirically that ProToMEx not only produces explanations of comparable fidelity to popular methods like SHAP and LIME but also drastically reduces the amortised computational cost of generating local explanations, making it highly suitable for real-time applications. Specifically, we show that ProToMEx is ~30-40x faster than SHAP and LIME over standardised tabular datasets and synthetic datasets.
我们通过实证证明，ProToMEx 不仅能产生与 SHAP 和 LIME 等流行方法保真度相当的解释，而且大幅降低了生成局部解释的摊销计算成本，使其非常适合实时应用。具体而言，我们展示了在标准化表格数据集和合成数据集上，ProToMEx 的速度比 SHAP 和 LIME 快约 30-40 倍。
