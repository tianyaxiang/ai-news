# Spectral-Target Physical Latent Structuring for JEPA-Style World Models
# 面向 JEPA 风格世界模型的谱目标物理潜空间结构化

**Abstract:** Latent world models have become increasingly popular as a method to predict and plan in latent space rather than pixel space. Recent architectures, such as LeWorldModel (LeWM), jointly train the encoder and predictor using regularization techniques like SIGReg to prevent representation collapse.

**摘要：** 潜空间世界模型作为一种在潜空间而非像素空间进行预测和规划的方法，正变得越来越流行。近期的架构（如 LeWorldModel，简称 LeWM）通过使用 SIGReg 等正则化技术联合训练编码器和预测器，以防止表征坍缩。

Even with such regularization preventing representation collapse, we identify a new world model failure mode of \textit{physical representation laziness}, particularly noted in highly dynamic environments. For these lazy cases, the learned latent states do not collapse but nonetheless fail to represent key physical properties, causing ubiquitous downstream planning failure.

尽管有此类正则化手段防止表征坍缩，我们仍发现了一种新的世界模型失效模式——“物理表征惰性”（physical representation laziness），这在高度动态的环境中尤为明显。在这些“惰性”案例中，学习到的潜状态虽然没有坍缩，却无法表征关键的物理属性，从而导致下游规划任务普遍失败。

To resolve this issue, we propose training-time auxiliary supervision with a lightweight "Fourier auxiliary head", which enforces physically-informed structuring of the latent space with no additional inference-time cost and can be generalized to any environment.

为了解决这一问题，我们提出了一种在训练阶段使用的辅助监督方法，即轻量级的“傅里叶辅助头”（Fourier auxiliary head）。该方法在不增加推理成本的前提下，强制对潜空间进行物理信息驱动的结构化，并可推广至任何环境。

Experimentally, we show that the auxiliary head substantially improves planning success rates in dynamic environments where the baseline LeWM exhibits physical representation laziness. It also leads to modest improvements in other environments, even when the baseline does not exhibit physical representation laziness.

实验表明，在基准模型 LeWM 表现出物理表征惰性的动态环境中，该辅助头显著提高了规划成功率。即使在基准模型未表现出物理表征惰性的其他环境中，该方法也能带来适度的性能提升。

We further observe superior planning performance being accompanied by higher latent space correlations with key physical properties, indicating both the ability of our method to physically structure latent states and the potential planning-side benefit to the learned representation being physically structured.

我们进一步观察到，更优的规划性能伴随着潜空间与关键物理属性之间更高的相关性，这既证明了我们的方法能够对潜状态进行物理结构化，也表明了学习到的表征在物理结构化后对规划任务具有潜在益处。

We also see in low-data regimes, auxiliary supervision is particularly impactful in increasing success rate. These findings support the use of our Fourier辅助 head method to improve both overall success rate and data efficiency, while avoiding representation laziness in latent world models.

我们还发现，在数据稀缺的情况下，辅助监督对于提高成功率尤为有效。这些研究结果支持使用我们的傅里叶辅助头方法，在提升整体成功率和数据效率的同时，避免潜空间世界模型中的表征惰性问题。
