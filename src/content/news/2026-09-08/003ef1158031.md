# Evaluating Large Language Models for Forced Outage Risk Prediction: Benefits and Comparison to Machine Learning
# 评估大型语言模型在强制停电风险预测中的应用：优势及与机器学习的对比

**Abstract:** This study examines the ability of large language models (LLMs) to predict the risk of weather-related forced outages in the distribution grid in a zero-shot framework, without labeled training data.
**摘要：** 本研究探讨了大型语言模型（LLMs）在零样本（zero-shot）框架下预测配电网中与天气相关的强制停电风险的能力，且无需标注的训练数据。

The problem is formulated as a binary severity classification task across three forecast horizons (3h, 6h, 12h), using six years of outage records and high-resolution weather data for a utility service area in central Texas.
该问题被设定为跨越三个预测时段（3小时、6小时、12小时）的二元严重程度分类任务，研究使用了德克萨斯州中部某公用事业服务区六年的停电记录和高分辨率天气数据。

Four zero-shot LLMs are benchmarked against two supervised classifiers across two input configurations: one using current weather observations and the other using weather forecast data.
研究将四种零样本LLM与两种监督分类器进行了基准测试，并采用了两种输入配置：一种使用当前天气观测数据，另一种使用天气预报数据。

Results show that supervised models outperform LLMs on macro-F1 and precision, while newer LLM generations achieve competitive scores.
结果表明，监督模型在宏观F1分数（macro-F1）和精确度方面优于LLM，但较新的LLM版本已能达到具有竞争力的分数。

Beyond accuracy, LLMs offer complementary strengths in actionable reasoning and geographic scalability, suggesting that combining them with supervised models may be the best practice.
除了准确性之外，LLM在可操作性推理和地理可扩展性方面具有互补优势，这表明将它们与监督模型相结合可能是最佳实践。
