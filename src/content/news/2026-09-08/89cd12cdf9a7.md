# A Data Fusion Framework for Grounding Aerospace Surrogate Model via Experimental Wind-Tunnel Observations
# 基于实验风洞观测的航空航天代理模型校准数据融合框架

**Abstract:** Aerodynamic surrogate models trained on high-fidelity CFD data reproduce numerical predictions of both scalar outputs and entire fields accurately, yet their predictive fidelity is limited by systematic discrepancies between CFD and experimental observations. 

**摘要：** 基于高保真计算流体力学（CFD）数据训练的空气动力学代理模型，能够准确复现标量输出和全场数值预测，但其预测保真度受到 CFD 与实验观测之间系统性差异的限制。

We present an experimentally grounded correction framework that adapts a CFD-trained deep learning surrogate using wind-tunnel PSP measurements. 

我们提出了一种基于实验校准的修正框架，利用风洞压力敏感漆（PSP）测量数据对经过 CFD 训练的深度学习代理模型进行适配。

A Geotransolver surrogate trained on 2,300 high-fidelity CFD simulations of the NASA CRM wing-body configuration, spanning geometric variation, Mach 0.70-0.85, and angles of attack 0 to 4 degrees, reproduces the CFD integrated aerodynamic forces and pitching moment to R² > 0.99 but does not match the experimental data. 

一个基于 Geotransolver 的代理模型在 NASA CRM 翼身组合体的 2,300 次高保真 CFD 模拟数据上进行了训练，涵盖了几何变化、马赫数 0.70-0.85 以及 0 到 4 度的攻角范围。该模型对 CFD 集成气动力和俯仰力矩的复现 R² 超过 0.99，但与实验数据并不吻合。

To incorporate experimental information without retraining the surrogate, a correction network is trained on spatially registered PSP measurements at two freestream Mach numbers (0.70 and 0.85) across the same angle-of-attack range, learning the discrepancy between the surrogate-predicted and experimentally measured surface-pressure distributions. 

为了在不重新训练代理模型的情况下整合实验信息，我们训练了一个修正网络。该网络基于在相同攻角范围内、两个自由流马赫数（0.70 和 0.85）下进行空间配准的 PSP 测量数据，学习代理模型预测值与实验测量表面压力分布之间的差异。

At Mach 0.85 the correction substantially improves agreement with PSP, particularly at the wing suction peak, shock location, and subsequent pressure recovery, reducing both the magnitude of the prediction error and the fraction of wetted surface on which it exceeds 0.05 in Cp, and it does so from a limited experimental dataset without modifying the pretrained surrogate parameters. 

在马赫数 0.85 时，该修正显著提高了与 PSP 数据的一致性，特别是在机翼吸力峰值、激波位置及随后的压力恢复区域。它不仅降低了预测误差的大小，还减少了压力系数（Cp）误差超过 0.05 的湿表面比例，且这一切是在不修改预训练代理模型参数的情况下，仅利用有限的实验数据集实现的。

On held-out angles of attack the grounded surrogate agrees with measurement to within 2.3-2.7% of the measured Cp range, and outperforms direct interpolation between the measured conditions at every state tested. 

在留出的测试攻角上，校准后的代理模型与测量值的偏差在测量 Cp 范围的 2.3-2.7% 以内，并且在所有测试状态下，其表现均优于测量条件之间的直接插值。

Experimental measurements can therefore ground a large-scale simulation-trained surrogate by learning systematic CFD-to-experiment discrepancies while preserving its generalization capability and computational efficiency.

因此，实验测量可以通过学习 CFD 与实验之间的系统性差异，对大规模模拟训练的代理模型进行校准，同时保持其泛化能力和计算效率。
