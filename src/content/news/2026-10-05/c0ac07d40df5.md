# The new Fitbit Edge leaks
# 新款 Fitbit Edge 泄露

Google’s midrange Fitbit Edge shows off its many sides and colors in leaked images.
谷歌中端产品 Fitbit Edge 在泄露的图片中展示了其多角度外观与多种配色。

We don’t know a ton about the Fitbit Edge, but it appears to be a successor to the midrange Charge line. It had leaked previously, but we can clearly see in these images posted by Android Headlines, you can see that Google’s rumored fitness tracker sort of splits the difference between the Pixel Watch and the Fitbit Air. It has a small screen that is able to display some widgets, but clearly isn’t as expansive as a proper smartwatch.
目前关于 Fitbit Edge 的信息还不多，但它似乎是中端 Charge 系列的继任者。此前该产品已有过泄露，但从 Android Headlines 发布的这些图片中，我们可以清楚地看到，这款传闻中的谷歌健身追踪器在 Pixel Watch 和 Fitbit Air 之间找到了某种平衡。它配备了一块可以显示部分小组件的小屏幕，但显然不像真正的智能手表那样功能全面。

Supposedly, this fitness-first wearable will have a seven-day battery life, as well as built-in GPS and an altimeter, making it far more capable than the minimalist Air. It will also have limited app support for things like Google Maps and YouTube Music. Beyond that, not a ton is known.
据推测，这款主打健身的可穿戴设备将拥有七天的续航能力，并内置 GPS 和高度计，使其功能远超极简风格的 Air。它还将提供有限的应用程序支持，例如 Google Maps 和 YouTube Music。除此之外，目前所知甚少。

The Fitbit Charge line was last updated in 2023, so a new midrange tracker is long overdue. While we don’t have any word on price or release date yet, somewhere in the $150 to $200 range seems like a safe bet. The Air costs $99.99 and the Watch starts at $399.99, so the Edge would clearly fall somewhere in the middle. The Charge 6 currently goes for $159.95.
Fitbit Charge 系列上一次更新是在 2023 年，因此推出一款新的中端追踪器已是迟来的举措。虽然目前还没有关于价格或发布日期的消息，但 150 美元到 200 美元之间的定价似乎是一个合理的猜测。Air 的售价为 99.99 美元，而 Watch 的起售价为 399.99 美元，因此 Edge 的定位显然介于两者之间。目前 Charge 6 的售价为 159.95 美元。
