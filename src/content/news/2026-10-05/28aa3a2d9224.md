# Meta Glasses Nova (Gen 3) Review: Not Much New
# Meta Nova 智能眼镜（第三代）评测：升级有限

**LIGHT:** Lightweight. Lots of style options. Better battery life. Better mics for call quality.
**亮点：** 轻便、款式选择丰富、电池续航更佳、通话麦克风质量提升。

**TIRED:** Many of the same last-gen features. Meta's AI isn't all that smart. Audio playback could be louder. People might think you're a creep.
**槽点：** 大多功能与上一代无异、Meta AI 不够智能、音频播放音量有待提高、佩戴时可能会被他人误解。

I’ve been on a call with my insurance provider for over an hour when the fire alarm in my apartment starts blaring. The sound is extraordinarily loud, piercing through the entire building. It overwhelms the voice on the other line, which comes through the speakers of my new Meta Nova smart glasses.
我正在和保险公司通话，通话时长已超过一小时，这时公寓的火警警报突然大作。警报声极其刺耳，响彻整栋大楼，完全盖过了我新买的 Meta Nova 智能眼镜扬声器里传来的对方声音。

I manage to dart outside, keep the call going, and eventually learn this is a false alarm. I breathe a sigh of relief, but the helpful human on the other end sounds very worried and asks if I’m OK. Turns out, they hadn’t heard the sirens at all, just me screeching, “OH JESUS WHAT IS HAPPENING?” The six-mic system on Meta’s new smart glasses does quite a good job of culling background noise over calls. I hadn’t expected to try that feature out in that way, but I can confirm it works.
我设法冲到室外并保持通话，最终得知这是一场虚惊。我松了一口气，但电话那头热心的工作人员听起来非常担心，问我是否安好。原来，他们根本没听到警报声，只听到我尖叫着：“天哪，发生什么事了？”Meta 新款智能眼镜的六麦克风系统在通话降噪方面表现相当出色。我没想到会以这种方式测试该功能，但我可以确认它确实有效。

This may not be a use case that would inspire someone to buy a new pair of Meta’s smart glasses. Especially not its camera-equipped ones, which have become prime targets of controversy about them being used by creeps and abusers. (Meta launched camera-free audio-only glasses, but we haven't tested them yet.)
这可能并不是促使人们购买 Meta 智能眼镜的理由。尤其是那些配备摄像头的版本，它们已成为争议的焦点，常被质疑会被变态或不法分子利用。（Meta 虽然推出了无摄像头的纯音频眼镜，但我们尚未进行测试。）

In fact, none of the upgrades in Meta’s new Gen 3 glasses are revolutionary. The glasses get nine hours of battery life, one more than the previous generation. They have a superior mic array—though identical to the Meta Glasses that debuted in June—a dedicated button for Meta’s AI assistant, and a slimmer, lighter fit that comes in dozens of color and frame combinations. They have the same 12-MP cameras that can capture 3K video as the Gen 2 models.
事实上，Meta 第三代眼镜的升级并没有什么革命性的变化。续航时间达到 9 小时，比上一代多出 1 小时。它们配备了更出色的麦克风阵列（尽管与 6 月份发布的 Meta 眼镜相同）、一个专门的 Meta AI 助手按钮，以及更纤薄轻便的佩戴感，并提供数十种颜色和镜框组合。它们依然搭载了与第二代相同的 1200 万像素摄像头，可拍摄 3K 视频。

The pair of glasses I’ve been using are the Meta-designed, non-Ray-Ban-branded Meta Nova glasses. You can also shop styles like the Adventurer Lisa or Capri editions. (The Ray-Ban-branded Gen 3 models are more expensive, starting at $499.) The Nova comes with much of the same software for $299. They have translucent brown frames and rounded lenses and are the first pair of Meta glasses I’ve worn that I feel actually look decent on my face.
我一直在使用的是 Meta 自家设计、非雷朋（Ray-Ban）联名款的 Meta Nova 眼镜。你也可以选择 Adventurer Lisa 或 Capri 等款式。（雷朋联名款的第三代机型价格更贵，起售价为 499 美元。）Nova 售价 299 美元，搭载了大部分相同的软件功能。它采用半透明棕色镜框和圆形镜片，是我戴过的第一款让我觉得戴在脸上还算美观的 Meta 眼镜。

The camera housing on my Meta Nova glasses is smaller than on the Gen 2 glasses. From a distance, they might even read as rivets at a glance. (I asked my colleague Reece Rogers how they looked on my face, and from a few feet away in the WIRED office, he didn’t even notice they were camera-enabled glasses at first.)
我这款 Meta Nova 眼镜的摄像头外壳比第二代更小。从远处看，它们甚至可能被误认为是铆钉。（我问同事 Reece Rogers 我戴着它看起来如何，在 WIRED 办公室几英尺外，他起初甚至没发现这是带摄像头的眼镜。）

They feel and function the same as before. You can interact with an AI assistant or snap photos and video with the press of a button. Conversation Focus, a mode that helps hard-of-hearing people better pick up spoken words, is a feature that only works in early access on Meta’s Ray-Ban and Oakley smart glasses. It seems very helpful, but my Meta-designed frames don’t have it yet.
它们的使用感受和功能与以往相同。你可以通过按键与 AI 助手互动，或拍摄照片和视频。“对话聚焦”（Conversation Focus）模式可以帮助听力障碍者更好地捕捉语音，但该功能目前仅在 Meta 的雷朋和 Oakley 智能眼镜上提供早期体验。它看起来非常实用，但我这款 Meta 自家设计的镜框目前还不支持。

They also don’t have integration with Meta’s Muse AI, which the company says is coming later this year. I’m intrigued by that, even if having access to a camera on your face will certainly help a service that seems better at surveilling than helping its users. That said, I’ve found Muse more useful than Meta’s current AI assistant, and I look forward to asking my entirely too cute AI buddy to assist me via my glasses.
它们也没有集成 Meta 的 Muse AI，公司称该功能将于今年晚些时候推出。我对此很感兴趣，尽管在脸上安装摄像头显然会助长某种“监控能力强于服务能力”的服务。话虽如此，我发现 Muse 比 Meta 目前的 AI 助手更有用，我很期待通过眼镜向我那个过于可爱的 AI 伙伴寻求帮助。

Unfortunately, Meta’s current AI assistant on the glasses is still terrible. I tried to ask what features the glasses I was wearing had, and it failed miserably. The AI couldn’t get any of the details right, even when I told it specifically which frames I was wearing and let it snap a picture of the Meta product page for reference. I wouldn’t want to depend on the AI to help me sort out anything with some real complexity.
遗憾的是，眼镜上目前的 Meta AI 助手依然表现糟糕。我试着问它我戴的眼镜有什么功能，结果它回答得一塌糊涂。即使我明确告诉它我戴的是哪款镜框，并让它拍下 Meta 产品页面作为参考，AI 也无法给出任何准确的细节。我可不敢指望这个 AI 来帮我处理任何真正复杂的事情。

If you pretty much ignore the AI entirely, the Gen 3 glasses do just about everything Meta promises. They capture nice photos and video, all while maintaining an indicator light that has recently been made more tamper-proof. (That’s not necessarily enough to deter the creeps.) The frames are wearable, and the software is competent enough. They’re also pricey, so maybe you can save some cash sticking with the Gen 2. They’ll all do what you probably expect them to do. Just maybe don’t be weird about it, all right?
如果你基本忽略 AI 功能，第三代眼镜确实能做到 Meta 承诺的几乎所有事情。它们能拍摄不错的照片和视频，同时保留了最近经过防篡改升级的指示灯。（但这未必足以震慑那些变态。）镜框佩戴舒适，软件也足够胜任。它们价格不菲，所以或许你坚持用第二代还能省点钱。它们都能完成你预期的任务。只是，别表现得太怪异，好吗？
