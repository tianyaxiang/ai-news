# The iPad Mini is slightly cheaper again during Prime Day
# iPad Mini 在 Prime Day 期间再次小幅降价

With a $100 discount, the Apple iPad Mini is temporarily back to its pre-RAMageddon price.
随着 100 美元的折扣，Apple iPad Mini 暂时回到了“内存危机”（RAMageddon）之前的价格水平。

Apple bumped up prices on several of its devices in June, and we haven’t seen a good discount on the iPad Mini since. Just ahead of Amazon’s October Prime Big Deals Days, however, both Wi-Fi only and cellular-connected models are $100 off. The Wi-Fi version with 128GB of storage is currently $499, the 256GB model is $599, and the cellular model with 128GB of storage is $649.
苹果公司在今年 6 月上调了多款设备的价格，此后我们一直没能看到 iPad Mini 的好价。然而，就在亚马逊 10 月“Prime Big Deals Days”大促活动前夕，Wi-Fi 版和蜂窝网络版机型均迎来了 100 美元的优惠。目前，128GB 存储空间的 Wi-Fi 版售价为 499 美元，256GB 版本为 599 美元，而 128GB 存储空间的蜂窝网络版售价为 649 美元。

This base configuration of this tablet often sold for around $400 throughout 2025, so we get it if $500 for a tablet from late 2024 is still too much. But it’s the best price since late June, and if there’s a new version coming out later this year, it might be significantly more expensive.
这款平板电脑的基础配置在 2025 年期间经常以 400 美元左右的价格出售，所以如果大家觉得 2024 年末发布的平板卖 500 美元仍然偏贵，我们完全理解。但这是自 6 月下旬以来的最佳价格；如果今年晚些时候有新版本发布，价格可能会贵得多。
