On an average day in San Francisco, you might see dozens (hundreds?) of Waymo robotaxis zipping around the city — and with little disruption. But, as TechCrunch has reported, there have been numerous incidents of robotaxis impeding traffic, driving into crime scenes, and interfering with first responders. And not just in the Bay Area. In some cases, companies have relied on first responders to move their robotaxis.
在旧金山的普通日子里，你可能会看到几十（甚至几百？）辆 Waymo 自动驾驶出租车在城市中穿梭，且几乎没有造成什么干扰。然而，正如 TechCrunch 所报道的那样，已经发生了多起自动驾驶出租车阻碍交通、驶入犯罪现场以及干扰急救人员的事件。这种情况不仅限于湾区。在某些情况下，公司甚至不得不依赖急救人员来移动他们的自动驾驶出租车。

These disruptions are occurring in the early stages of the robotaxi era. California has a new law hoping to get ahead of the problem before the next impending wave of robotaxis land. Senate Bill 1246, which was signed into law by Governor Gavin Newsom this week, sets a series of new rules for AVs designed to improve safety and response times when they become disabled or interfere with emergency responders. The law also creates ways to hold these companies accountable if they don’t.
这些干扰正发生在自动驾驶出租车时代的早期阶段。加利福尼亚州出台了一项新法律，希望在下一波自动驾驶出租车浪潮到来之前防患于未然。本周由州长加文·纽森（Gavin Newsom）签署成为法律的第 1246 号参议院法案（Senate Bill 1246），为自动驾驶汽车制定了一系列新规则，旨在提高它们在发生故障或干扰急救人员时的安全性和响应速度。该法律还规定了在这些公司未能遵守时追究其责任的途径。

Autonomous vehicle technology companies like Tesla, Waymo, and Zoox will have to provide first responders with local, on-the-ground support when their robotaxis cause problems and could also face penalties if a robotaxi blocks police or firefighters for more than 30 minutes. The law also says AV developers can only employ remote drivers who are based in the United States and those drivers must hold a U.S. driver’s license. They must also notify cities, towns, and other local jurisdictions about the location and status of vehicles during system-wide failures and must provide “local incident technicians” who can assist with AV accidents and obstructions.
特斯拉、Waymo 和 Zoox 等自动驾驶技术公司必须在自动驾驶出租车引发问题时，为急救人员提供当地的现场支持；如果自动驾驶出租车阻碍警察或消防员超过 30 分钟，公司还可能面临处罚。该法律还规定，自动驾驶汽车开发商只能聘用位于美国的远程驾驶员，且这些驾驶员必须持有美国驾照。此外，在系统性故障期间，他们必须向城市、城镇和其他地方司法管辖区通报车辆的位置和状态，并提供能够协助处理自动驾驶汽车事故和障碍的“当地事故技术人员”。

There are still some details to hammer out, which the Department of Motor Vehicles will handle since it’s the main regulator of autonomous vehicles in the state. But when this law goes into effect in July 2028, will it actually keep robotaxis out of the way of emergency responders? It likely won’t, but hopefully this law reduces the chaos and raises the accountability when something does happen. Perhaps the better question is will other states follow California’s lead or wait for the federal government to weigh in?
仍有一些细节需要敲定，这将由机动车管理局（DMV）负责，因为它是该州自动驾驶汽车的主要监管机构。但当这项法律于 2028 年 7 月生效时，它真的能让自动驾驶出租车避开急救人员吗？这可能很难做到，但希望这项法律能减少混乱，并在事故发生时提高问责制。也许更好的问题是：其他州会效仿加州的先例，还是等待联邦政府介入？
