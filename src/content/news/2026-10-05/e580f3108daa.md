# Trump unveils his new Super Intelligence Force
# 特朗普宣布成立新的“超级智能部队”

As promised, President Donald Trump has announced the formation of a new Super Intelligence Force, which he said will be led by national intelligence director Jay Clayton and other members of his administration.
正如他所承诺的那样，唐纳德·特朗普总统宣布成立一支新的“超级智能部队”（Super Intelligence Force）。他表示，该部队将由国家情报总监杰伊·克莱顿（Jay Clayton）及政府其他成员领导。

“The Super Intelligence Force is tasked with coordinating the effort of the Federal Government to ensure that America continues to lead the World in Super Intelligence, which many say is bigger than the Industrial Revolution, and the Internet, and will protect the interests, and improve the lives, of all Americans,” Trump said in a Sunday morning post on his social network Truth Social.
特朗普在周日上午于其社交网络“真实社交”（Truth Social）上发帖称：“超级智能部队的任务是协调联邦政府的工作，确保美国在超级智能领域继续保持世界领先地位。许多人认为，超级智能的影响力将超过工业革命和互联网，它将保护所有美国人的利益并改善他们的生活。”

Back in September, in response to an AI safety debate that the president has largely dismissed as a hoax created by “Radical Left Dumocrats,” Trump said he would nevertheless form an AI Force and appoint an AI czar (“Only High I.Q. individuals need apply!”).
早在今年9月，针对一场被总统斥为“激进左翼民主党人”（Radical Left Dumocrats）捏造的骗局的AI安全辩论，特朗普曾表示，尽管如此，他仍将组建一支AI部队并任命一位AI沙皇（“只有高智商人士才能申请！”）。

Since then, Trump signed an executive order seeking to rebrand AI as “super intelligence,” a term seemingly embraced, with some stumbles, by the tech executives who gathered at the White House this week to sign a non-binding safety pledge. (The pledge included an impressive typo.) So it’s only natural that Trump’s new task force is named in accordance with this new branding.
此后，特朗普签署了一项行政命令，试图将AI重新命名为“超级智能”。本周，聚集在白宫签署一份非约束性安全承诺的科技高管们似乎（尽管有些磕绊）接受了这一术语。（该承诺书中还包含了一个令人印象深刻的拼写错误。）因此，特朗普的新工作组以这一新品牌命名也就顺理成章了。

The Wall Street Journal reports that Clayton will chair the Super Intelligence Force, effectively making him the new AI/SI czar, with Federal Trade Commission Chair Andrew Ferguson, Undersecretary of War for Research and Engineering Emil Michael, and Office of Personnel Management Director Scott Kupor serving as vice chairs.
据《华尔街日报》报道，克莱顿将担任超级智能部队的主席，这实际上使他成为了新的AI/SI沙皇。联邦贸易委员会主席安德鲁·弗格森（Andrew Ferguson）、负责研究与工程的陆军副部长埃米尔·迈克尔（Emil Michael）以及人事管理局局长斯科特·库珀（Scott Kupor）将担任副主席。

The task force will reportedly have 120 days to create a report on the risks and opportunities presented by AI. Clayton, who previously said that it would be irresponsible for the United States to “back away” from AI while countries like China advance, made similar comments to the WSJ, insisting that one of the biggest risks is “not being first.”
据报道，该工作组将有120天的时间来撰写一份关于AI所带来的风险与机遇的报告。克莱顿此前曾表示，在像中国这样的国家不断进步的情况下，美国从AI领域“退缩”是不负责任的。他向《华尔街日报》发表了类似的评论，坚称最大的风险之一就是“没能做到第一”。

The task force’s charter reportedly says that it will “develop plans for responding to SI-enabled threats to our society, while preventing overregulation and regulatory capture that would stifle innovation and competition.”
据报道，该工作组的章程指出，它将“制定应对超级智能（SI）对我们社会所构成威胁的计划，同时防止过度监管和监管俘获，以免扼杀创新与竞争。”
