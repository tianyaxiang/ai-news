# The MacBook Air M5 is $200 off for the first time in months
# MacBook Air M5 迎来数月来首次 200 美元降价

Apple’s midrange laptop is a much better proposition with a discount.
苹果这款中端笔记本电脑在降价后，性价比显著提升。

Amazon’s October Prime Day has effectively chopped off Apple’s June price increases. Usually $1,299, the 13-inch MacBook Air with the M5 chip and 512GB of storage is on sale for $1,099 at Amazon. That’s a more palatable price for a machine that’s significantly faster than Apple’s $699 MacBook Neo.
亚马逊十月会员日（Prime Day）活动有效地抵消了苹果六月份的涨价。原价 1,299 美元的 13 英寸 MacBook Air（搭载 M5 芯片，512GB 存储空间）目前在亚马逊上的售价为 1,099 美元。对于一台性能远超苹果 699 美元 MacBook Neo 的机器来说，这个价格更容易让人接受。

The 15-inch version, also with 512GB of storage, is $1,299 at Amazon (was $1,499). In addition to the larger screen, it has two additional speakers, for a total of six, and two additional GPU cores for better overall graphical performance.
15 英寸版本（同样配备 512GB 存储空间）在亚马逊上的售价为 1,299 美元（原价 1,499 美元）。除了更大的屏幕外，它还额外增加了两个扬声器（总计六个），并多出两个 GPU 核心，从而提供更好的整体图形性能。

Apple’s 2026 MacBook Air has an M5 chip and starts with 512GB of storage that’s significantly faster than previous generations. It’s still as excellent as ever despite a higher base price. Read our review.
苹果 2026 款 MacBook Air 搭载了 M5 芯片，起步存储空间为 512GB，其速度较前几代产品有显著提升。尽管起售价有所上涨，但它依然保持了一贯的卓越品质。阅读我们的评测。

The latest update to Apple’s midrange laptop includes a noticeable improvement to the speed of both the processor and storage. Even without flashy new features compared to the last-gen model, it’s still one of our favorite laptops, thanks to its long battery life, bright screen, and excellent speakers.
苹果这款中端笔记本电脑的最新升级在处理器和存储速度上都有显著提升。尽管与上一代机型相比没有花哨的新功能，但凭借其超长的续航能力、明亮的屏幕和出色的扬声器，它依然是我们最喜爱的笔记本电脑之一。
