# The AirPods Pro 3 are a fantastic deal at $179
# AirPods Pro 3 现售价 179 美元，性价比极高

It’s been a while since we’ve seen a good discount on the Apple AirPods Pro 3, but like the latest iPad Mini and the M5 MacBook Airs, a great one is happening now for October Prime Day. You can snag a pair of the noise-canceling earbuds that include five ear tip sizes, live translation, and fantastic sound for $179 at Amazon (originally $249). I expect other retailers to offer a similar price in the coming days.

距离上次看到 Apple AirPods Pro 3 的大幅折扣已经过去一段时间了。不过，正如最新的 iPad Mini 和 M5 MacBook Air 一样，借着十月“会员日”（Prime Day）的东风，这款耳机现在迎来了超值优惠。你现在可以在亚马逊以 179 美元的价格（原价 249 美元）入手这款降噪耳机，它配备了五种尺寸的耳塞、实时翻译功能以及出色的音质。预计未来几天其他零售商也会跟进提供类似的价格。

This is the first time the price has gone below $190 since Prime Day in June. Since then, Apple launched the AirPods 5, and the most feature-complete version with a wireless charging case costs $149.99. Those are great if you prefer earbuds that don’t form a seal in your ears, but for everyone else, the AirPods Pro 3 are a much better value at just $30 more right now.

这是自六月会员日以来，其价格首次跌破 190 美元。在此期间，苹果发布了 AirPods 5，其中功能最全的无线充电盒版本售价为 149.99 美元。如果你偏好不入耳式的耳机，那款产品非常不错；但对于大多数用户而言，AirPods Pro 3 目前仅需多花 30 美元，性价比要高得多。

The AirPods Pro 3 have a new design that improves their fit, superior noise cancellation, better bass, and fairly accurate heart rate sensors. Read our review.

AirPods Pro 3 采用了全新的设计，提升了佩戴贴合度，拥有更出色的降噪效果、更强的低音表现以及相当精准的心率传感器。欢迎阅读我们的详细评测。
