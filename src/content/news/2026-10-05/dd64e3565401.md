# VGHF Digital Archive passes 5000 magazines. Here's what's next
# 电子游戏历史基金会数字档案馆藏突破 5000 本，未来规划揭晓

The Video Game History Foundation has added over 5000 total magazines to our digital archive, a major milestone in our project to build a first-in-class video game history research library. When we launched our digital archive last year, we announced an initial collection of over 1500 out-of-print, text-searchable magazines, which are free for research access.
电子游戏历史基金会（VGHF）已将超过 5000 本杂志收录至我们的数字档案馆，这是我们构建一流电子游戏历史研究图书馆项目中的一个重要里程碑。去年我们推出数字档案馆时，首批收藏了超过 1500 本已绝版且支持全文检索的杂志，并向研究人员免费开放。

In the time since we launched, we’ve more than tripled the size of that collection—now totaling nearly 600,000 pages, the equivalent of nearly 100 feet of shelf space! Our magazine library now spans 46 years of video game history, from 1981–2026. Major new additions since launch include hundreds of trade magazines, a historically significant newsletter, and publications for niche gaming communities. Altogether, these account for about 60 percent of the Video Game History Foundation’s entire physical magazine collection that we’ve cataloged so far.
自上线以来，我们的馆藏规模扩大了三倍多，目前总页数已接近 60 万页，相当于近 100 英尺（约 30 米）的书架空间！我们的杂志图书馆现已涵盖 1981 年至 2026 年间，长达 46 年的电子游戏历史。自上线以来的主要新增内容包括数百本行业杂志、具有历史意义的通讯刊物以及针对小众游戏社区的出版物。总计来看，这些内容约占电子游戏历史基金会目前已编目的全部实体杂志馆藏的 60%。

This milestone would not have been possible without support from community-run scanning projects. With our combined efforts, we’ve turned their magazine scans into a powerful research tool. Around one-third of the magazines in our digital archive were digitized by the Video Game History Foundation, with the rest provided by external groups. We want to thank the groups and individuals who have helped: The community-run scanning groups Retromags, Out-of-Print Archive, Sega Retro, AtariAge, Atari Compendium, CGW Museum, The Odyssey² Homepage, TOSEC-PIX, eXoDOS, and Gaming Alexandria. Industry partners and alumni from Electronic Gaming Monthly, Game Developer, Game Informer, and MCV. …and a special shoutout to VGHF Patreon member Chris Chapman, who provided us with 157 scans and digital magazines, the most of any unaffiliated contributor.
如果没有社区扫描项目的支持，这一里程碑将无法实现。通过共同努力，我们将他们的扫描件转化为了强大的研究工具。我们数字档案馆中约三分之一的杂志由电子游戏历史基金会亲自数字化，其余部分则由外部团体提供。我们要感谢所有提供帮助的团体和个人：包括 Retromags、Out-of-Print Archive、Sega Retro、AtariAge、Atari Compendium、CGW Museum、The Odyssey² Homepage、TOSEC-PIX、eXoDOS 和 Gaming Alexandria 等社区扫描小组；以及来自《Electronic Gaming Monthly》、《Game Developer》、《Game Informer》和《MCV》的行业合作伙伴与校友。特别感谢 VGHF 的 Patreon 会员 Chris Chapman，他为我们提供了 157 份扫描件和数字杂志，是所有非关联贡献者中贡献最多的。

It’s only been a year and a half since we launched our digital archive, but we’re already seeing its transformative effect on video game history research. In fact, the VGHF Digital Archive has already become the preferred reference source for out-of-print game magazines on Wikipedia, with over 500 citations and counting. We’re grateful that researchers have responded so quickly to our vision for a well-organized, freely accessible research platform. We have also heard from the community about what additional features and series they’d like to see in our magazine library. We’ve taken that feedback to heart, and that’s helped us decide where we’re heading next.
数字档案馆上线仅一年半，我们已经看到了它对电子游戏历史研究产生的变革性影响。事实上，VGHF 数字档案馆已成为维基百科上绝版游戏杂志的首选参考来源，引用次数已超过 500 次且仍在增加。我们非常感谢研究人员对我们构建组织有序、免费开放的研究平台这一愿景的迅速响应。我们也听取了社区关于希望在图书馆中看到哪些额外功能和系列的建议。我们认真对待这些反馈，这也有助于我们决定下一步的发展方向。

Introducing Japanese magazines: You asked, and we’ve delivered: we are now supporting Japanese-language magazines in our digital archive. Japanese magazines are a vital resource for studying video game history; in particular, they can be a rich source for interviews with developers—most of which have rarely if ever been reprinted—and first-hand accounts of Japanese player culture. While there are a number of community projects to digitize Japanese magazines, they’ve often been met with unique cultural and legal challenges. We intend for our digital archive to build on the community’s work, much like we’ve done for English-language magazines.
引入日语杂志：你们提出了要求，我们做到了：我们的数字档案馆现在开始支持日语杂志。日语杂志是研究电子游戏历史的重要资源；特别是它们包含了丰富的开发者访谈（其中大多数几乎从未被重印过）以及关于日本玩家文化的第一手资料。虽然目前已有许多社区项目致力于日语杂志的数字化，但它们往往面临独特的文化和法律挑战。我们希望在社区工作的基础上，进一步完善我们的数字档案馆，就像我们对待英语杂志那样。

How we’re doing it: Today, as our pilot project, we’re launching research access to *Neo Geo Freak*, a magazine that covered games for Neo Geo home and arcade platforms from 1995–2000. *Neo Geo Freak* felt like a good practical starting place for us. Last year, a generous community member donated the entire run of *Neo Geo Freak* to our library, and the magazine had already been digitized by the user Japanese Magazine Scans, both of which reduced the amount of work on our end to start exploring what it would take to add Japanese materials to our digital archive.
我们是如何做到的：今天，作为试点项目，我们正式开放了对《Neo Geo Freak》的研究访问权限，这是一本涵盖 1995 年至 2000 年间 Neo Geo 家用机和街机平台游戏的杂志。《Neo Geo Freak》对我们来说是一个很好的切入点。去年，一位慷慨的社区成员向我们的图书馆捐赠了全套《Neo Geo Freak》，且该杂志此前已由用户“Japanese Magazine Scans”完成数字化，这两点都减轻了我们探索如何将日语资料加入数字档案馆的工作量。

More importantly, though, *Neo Geo Freak* is also a good example of the unique material that can found in Japanese publications. Neo Geo platforms performed especially well in Japan relative to the rest of the world, making this an excellent candidate for the sort of sustained coverage you wouldn’t find in the English-language press. However, adding Japanese magazines to our library was not as simple as uploading a bunch of PDFs. We maintain a high standard for how we present materials in our archive, and for a content-dense magazine written in a language that our staff isn’t fluent in, meeting those standards was easier said than done.
更重要的是，《Neo Geo Freak》也是日本出版物中独特资料的一个典型例子。Neo Geo 平台在日本的表现远好于世界其他地区，这使其成为那种在英语媒体中难以找到的持续性报道的绝佳候选对象。然而，将日语杂志加入我们的图书馆并非上传一堆 PDF 那么简单。我们对档案馆资料的呈现方式保持着高标准，对于一本内容密集且使用我们员工并不精通的语言编写的杂志来说，达到这些标准说起来容易做起来难。

First, we worked with our community of peers to determine the best methods for Japanese script recognition. Professional archivists in Japan pointed us towards their preferred libraries for Japanese document transcription, which we integrated into the text mapping toolset that we use for English-language magazines. We believe the result is the most accurate text-searchable Japanese magazines currently in any digital archive. We verified the results with game history researchers who use Japanese materials to ensure that the output is reliable and usable at scale.
首先，我们与同行社区合作，确定了日语文字识别的最佳方法。日本的专业档案管理员向我们推荐了他们首选的日语文档转录库，我们将其整合到了我们用于英语杂志的文本映射工具集中。我们相信，其结果是目前任何数字档案馆中最准确的可全文检索日语杂志。我们与使用日语资料的游戏历史研究人员核实了结果，以确保输出内容可靠且可大规模使用。

To make *Neo Geo Freak* more discoverable for an English-speaking audience, we took the additional step to index the major articles from complete run of the magazine in English. Rather than attempting to translate 10,000 pages of Japanese text, we assembled a group of Japanese-fluent volunteers to create a list of major articles and interviews for each issue. We think this is a practical and efficient method for making these magazines’ contents more accessible, while avoiding the pitfalls associated with automated bulk translation. Users are encouraged to translate magazine text using their own tools of choice as needed—even just using their phone camera.
为了让英语受众更容易发现《Neo Geo Freak》，我们额外采取了措施，对该杂志全集的重点文章进行了英文索引。我们没有尝试翻译 1 万页的日语文本，而是召集了一群精通日语的志愿者，为每一期杂志创建了主要文章和访谈列表。我们认为这是一种实用且高效的方法，既能让这些杂志的内容更易于获取，又能避免自动批量翻译带来的陷阱。我们鼓励用户根据需要使用自己选择的工具（甚至只需使用手机摄像头）来翻译杂志文本。

Full support for Japanese magazines also required some improvements to our digital archive platform. New features we’ve added for this pilot project include filtering items by language, displaying right-to-left page layouts, and supporting Japanese text without interword spacing in search.
对日语杂志的全面支持还需要对我们的数字档案馆平台进行一些改进。我们为该试点项目添加的新功能包括：按语言筛选条目、显示从右向左的页面布局，以及在搜索中支持无词间空格的日语文本。
