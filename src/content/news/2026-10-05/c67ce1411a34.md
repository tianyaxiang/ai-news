# The Best Gifts Under $25 for Everyone on Your List (2026)
# 2026年送礼指南：25美元以下的最佳礼物清单

$25 doesn't go as far as it used to. As recently as a decade ago, $25 could fill up your gas tank and buy a pretty good gift, with plenty of options for just about every interest and occasion. Nowadays, it’s a struggle to even find a $25 takeout meal for one person and average national gas prices are inching toward $5 a gallon. And we all know wages have not kept up.
25美元的购买力已大不如前。就在十年前，25美元不仅能加满一箱油，还能买到一份相当不错的礼物，涵盖各种兴趣和场合。如今，想用25美元买一份单人外卖都有些吃力，全国平均油价也正逼近每加仑5美元。我们都知道，工资的涨幅远没跟上物价。

Good news: There are still plenty of thoughtful, interesting items out there to be had for $25, which are sure to appeal to everyone from coworkers to neighbors, relatives you don't know well, and the friend who seems to have everything. All things on this list have been tested and vetted by at least one person on the WIRED Reviews team—often more than one—to ensure it is indeed a good gift you'd be proud to give.
好消息是：市面上依然有很多既贴心又有趣的礼物，价格都在25美元以内。无论是送给同事、邻居、不太熟悉的亲戚，还是那位“什么都不缺”的朋友，这些礼物都能投其所好。清单上的所有商品都经过了WIRED评测团队至少一人（通常是多人）的测试和审核，确保它们是值得你送出的好礼物。

**MoMA Design Store Colorplay Pen & Pencil Cup Holder ($15)**
**MoMA设计商店 Colorplay 笔筒 ($15)**

WIRED senior Gear editor Julian Chokkattu recommends this colorful acrylic desk accessory that keeps pencils, scissors, and other large items on top and smaller items like paper clips (or, in Julian's case as a phone reviewer, SIM ejection pins) on the bottom. Shown is the pink-and-amber color combination, but there's also a blue-and-green option.
WIRED资深装备编辑Julian Chokkattu推荐了这款色彩鲜艳的亚克力桌面收纳盒。它的上层可以放置铅笔、剪刀等大件物品，下层则适合存放回形针等小物件（对于身为手机评测员的Julian来说，这里正好放SIM卡取卡针）。文中展示的是粉色与琥珀色的组合，此外还有蓝绿配色可选。

**Field Notes National Parks Series (3-Pack) ($15)**
**Field Notes 国家公园系列笔记本（三本装） ($15)**

I've used Field Notes for years—they’re lightweight, sturdy, and perfectly pocketable. The National Parks Series elevates what would otherwise be a utilitarian notebook to its giftable status, with artist-created covers inspired by our nation's natural wonders—from Acadia to the Hawai'i Volcanoes.
我使用Field Notes笔记本已经很多年了——它们轻便、耐用，而且非常适合随身携带。国家公园系列将原本实用的笔记本提升到了礼品级别，封面由艺术家创作，灵感源自美国各地的自然奇观，从阿卡迪亚到夏威夷火山国家公园，应有尽有。

**Muji Ballpoint Pens (10 Pack) ($19)**
**无印良品 (Muji) 圆珠笔（十支装） ($19)**

My city recently got its own brick-and-mortar Muji store, and if you’ve never visited the famed Japanese retailer in person, it’s basically a mecca for stationery and desk accessories. These pens in particular are famous for their smooth ink and precision tips, and make a perfect companion for the Field Notes notebooks at left.
我所在的城市最近开了第一家无印良品实体店。如果你从未亲身逛过这家著名的日本零售商，它简直就是文具和桌面配件的圣地。这款圆珠笔以其顺滑的墨水和精准的笔尖而闻名，是搭配左侧Field Notes笔记本的绝佳选择。
