# Can ‘super intelligence’ and a non-binding safety pact solve AI’s image problem?
# “超级智能”与非约束性安全协议能解决人工智能的形象问题吗？

President Donald Trump hosted many of the biggest names in artificial intelligence this week — in part to announce that the U.S. government isn’t calling it artificial intelligence anymore. Now it’s super intelligence.
唐纳德·特朗普总统本周接待了人工智能领域的许多重量级人物，部分原因是为了宣布美国政府不再将其称为“人工智能”（Artificial Intelligence），现在它被称为“超级智能”（Super Intelligence）。

On the latest episode of TechCrunch’s Equity podcast, Kirsten Korosec, Sean O’Kane, and I discussed the motivation behind the meeting and what effect it might have on the AI industry. We were all pretty skeptical that the “Joint Commitment on Frontier Responsibilities” that the executives signed will amount to much, especially since — as Sean noted — it’s “deeply non-binding,” at least from a legal prospective. (Instead, Trump said he considers it “morally binding.”)
在 TechCrunch 的《Equity》播客最新一期节目中，Kirsten Korosec、Sean O’Kane 和我讨论了这次会议背后的动机及其对人工智能行业可能产生的影响。我们都对高管们签署的《前沿责任联合承诺》（Joint Commitment on Frontier Responsibilities）能否产生实质性作用持怀疑态度，正如 Sean 所指出的，从法律角度来看，它“完全没有约束力”。（相反，特朗普表示他认为这是“道德约束”。）

Kirsten suggested that the meeting’s real significance may have been as “a rebranding effort around AI.” (An effort that continued this weekend with Trump’s announcement of a new Super Intelligence Force.) For her, the big message was, “AI is going to kill us, it’s scary, it is going to take jobs — but super intelligence is not.”
Kirsten 认为，这次会议的真正意义可能在于“围绕人工智能的品牌重塑”。（这一努力在本周末随着特朗普宣布成立新的“超级智能部队”而得以延续。）对她而言，其核心信息是：“人工智能会杀死我们，它很可怕，它会夺走工作——但超级智能不会。”

**Anthony Ha：** 正式地，根据美国总统的命令，它不再是人工智能，而是超级智能。我们上周就在讨论这个问题，因为这是[特朗普]一直在暗示的事情——他其实不怎么玩暗示，他已经说了有一段时间了。但现在它已被签署为行政命令。如果你是美国官方代表，你就应该说“超级智能”，而不是“人工智能”。

And this was also part of this broader announcement around AI safety, which I think is both interesting for this pact that was announced, but also the fact that it happened at all. It seemed to have come together fairly quickly, because just two weeks ago, a week ago, President Trump was saying, “We don’t need any of this, we need to lead in AI, we can’t constrain it in any way, we don’t need any regulation, anyone who’s saying that there’s any danger from AI is essentially a Chinese or Democratic plant.” And yet all these AI CEOs got together with him earlier this week and signed this pact.
这也是围绕人工智能安全这一更广泛声明的一部分。我认为，不仅宣布的这份协议很有趣，而且它能够达成这一事实本身也很有趣。它似乎是在相当短的时间内促成的，因为就在两周前、一周前，特朗普总统还在说：“我们不需要这些，我们需要在人工智能领域保持领先，我们不能以任何方式限制它，我们不需要任何监管，任何说人工智能有危险的人本质上都是中国或民主党的安插人员。”然而，本周早些时候，所有这些人工智能公司的首席执行官都与他会面并签署了这份协议。
