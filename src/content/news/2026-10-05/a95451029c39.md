# This toolless modular lever-action wallet is the coolest I’ve stuck to my phone
# 这款无需工具的模块化杠杆式钱包，是我用过最酷的手机贴附式钱包

This toolless modular lever-action wallet is the coolest I’ve stuck to my phone.
这款无需工具的模块化杠杆式钱包，是我用过最酷的手机贴附式钱包。

Remember when I tested the ultra-thin and convenient OhSnap Snap Grip Stand and liked it so much I bought my own? Now, OhSnap has a magnetic lever-action card wallet that can stack with that stand. It’s the coolest wallet I’ve ever stuck to my phone.
还记得我曾测试过那款超薄且便捷的 OhSnap Snap Grip 支架，并因太喜欢而自掏腰包买了一个吗？现在，OhSnap 推出了一款磁吸式杠杆卡包，它可以与那个支架叠加使用。这是我用过最酷的手机贴附式钱包。

Pull the integrated springy lever, and your cards fan out. Want a thicker or thinner wallet? Press in the right place and it comes apart without tools, letting you swap in a 3, 5, or 7-card “core” that grips your cards so they don’t fall out.
拉动内置的弹簧杠杆，你的卡片就会呈扇形展开。想要更厚或更薄的钱包？只需按压特定位置，无需工具即可将其拆解，让你能够更换 3 张、5 张或 7 张容量的“核心”组件，它能牢牢夹住卡片，防止滑落。

Want an ID card, work badge, elastic band for more cards, or a money clip on the end? You can change out the front or back magnetic plates for any of those. Or, change out both for ultra-thin aluminum covers if you don’t need the wallet to magnetically stick to your phone.
想要添加身份证位、工牌位、用于收纳更多卡片的弹力带，或是末端的钞票夹？你可以更换前后的磁吸面板来实现这些功能。或者，如果你不需要钱包磁吸在手机上，也可以将前后盖都换成超薄铝制盖板。

You can see it in action in my Today I’m Toying With video embedded in this post (also on YouTube and on Instagram if you prefer).
你可以在本文嵌入的“今日把玩 (Today I’m Toying With)”视频中看到它的实际演示（如果你愿意，也可以在 YouTube 或 Instagram 上观看）。

My favorite part is that the whole kit snaps together using a compliant mechanism — the plastic arms of the card-carrying core act like springs, pushing a little locking mechanism into the notches of each modular cover you add as you slide the parts together. Once your cards are inside, they keep the arms apart so it can’t open accidentally (unless you drop the entire wallet). It’s all very clever.
我最喜欢的部分是整个套件通过柔性机构扣合在一起——卡片核心的塑料臂就像弹簧一样，当你滑动组装部件时，它会将一个小锁定机构推入你添加的每个模块化盖板的凹槽中。一旦卡片放入，它们会撑开塑料臂，从而防止钱包意外打开（除非你把整个钱包摔了）。这一切设计非常巧妙。

Even when I did drop the wallet (thanks for the suggestion, hugo!), it didn’t explode, typically. Usually, it just came apart slightly, though I did drop it hard on concrete one time and was able to make a small explosion: one half popped off, and two cards came out, but everything landed in a two-foot radius. The wallet has a big dent now.
即使我确实摔过这个钱包（感谢 hugo 的建议！），它通常也不会散架。一般情况下，它只会轻微松动。不过有一次我把它重重地摔在水泥地上，确实造成了一次“小爆炸”：一半盖板弹开了，两张卡掉了出来，但所有东西都落在两英尺的范围内。现在钱包上留下了一个大凹痕。

There are definitely some downsides. While OhSnap advertises the Wallet 2 as “impossibly thin,” it’s only truly surprisingly sleek in its thinnest 3-card variant, without the thicker and heavier MagSafe and MagStack (which lets you snap other magnets to it) plates attached.
当然，它也有一些缺点。虽然 OhSnap 将 Wallet 2 宣传为“薄到不可思议”，但只有在最薄的 3 卡版本且不安装更厚、更重的 MagSafe 和 MagStack（允许你吸附其他磁性配件）面板时，它才真正令人惊艳地轻薄。

But the reason I’m not buying one is the price and weight. The 7-card version makes my phone feel too heavy, adding 127 grams, and while I happily made the 3-card my wallet for a few weeks, it’d cost me $61 on Kickstarter or $76 retail to get that with a double-magnet kit. OhSnap’s also charging more than I’d like ($35 for an ID cover or a money clip!) for extras.
但我没有购买的原因是价格和重量。7 卡版本让我的手机感觉太重了，增加了 127 克。虽然我很高兴地用了几周 3 卡版本，但要获得带有双磁铁套件的版本，在 Kickstarter 上需要 61 美元，零售价则要 76 美元。OhSnap 对配件的收费也超出了我的预期（一个身份证盖板或钞票夹就要 35 美元！）。

OhSnap didn’t get me this time. But I could see giving one as a present.
OhSnap 这次没能让我掏腰包。但我认为把它当作礼物送人倒是不错的选择。
