# Apple's "Clean Design" Is Stupidity When It Comes to Hiding Fire Extinguishers
# 苹果的“极简设计”在隐藏灭火器这件事上简直是愚蠢至极

**Key Takeaways**
**核心要点**

*   Concealing fire extinguishers behind unmarked panels creates dangerous, potentially noncompliant installations.
*   将灭火器隐藏在无标识的面板后会造成危险，且可能不符合相关规定。
*   OSHA and NFPA regulations require extinguishers to be visible, labeled, and accessible along travel paths.
*   OSHA（职业安全与健康管理局）和 NFPA（美国消防协会）法规要求灭火器必须可见、有标识，并位于通行路径上且易于取用。
*   Combine coordinated finishes with prominent signage to achieve compliant, aesthetically pleasing safety designs.
*   将协调的装修风格与醒目的标识相结合，才能实现既合规又美观的安全设计。

***

Smoke is filling a room. Someone is shouting. You know a workplace safety tool like a fire extinguisher is nearby, but the designer made it disappear behind a flush panel that matches the wall perfectly. That is not a design triumph.
浓烟充斥着房间，有人在呼喊。你知道附近有灭火器这样的工作场所安全工具，但设计师却将其隐藏在与墙面完美融合的平齐面板后。这绝非设计上的成功。

**The Price of Aesthetic Perfection in a Crisis**
**危机时刻，审美完美的代价**

A clean environment is worth pursuing, but erasing safety infrastructure from the visual field is a different thing entirely. A clean wall that conceals emergency equipment without any visible identification is not good design. It is a functional failure dressed up in a coordinated finish.
追求整洁的环境固然值得，但将安全设施从视野中抹去则是另一回事。一面隐藏了应急设备却没有任何可见标识的整洁墙面，绝非好的设计。这只是披着协调外观外衣的功能性失败。

Reportedly, an Apple retail or office installation has been circulating as an example of this trade-off. It allegedly shows fire suppression equipment integrated so seamlessly into the surrounding architecture that it effectively disappears from view.
据报道，苹果公司的一处零售店或办公场所的装修案例因这种“权衡”而流传开来。据称，该案例中的消防设备与周围建筑结构融合得天衣无缝，以至于在视觉上完全消失了。

No official Apple statement, architectural document, or verified primary image has surfaced to confirm the specifics. No such source was identified in the research reviewed for this article.
目前尚未有苹果公司的官方声明、建筑文档或经过核实的原始图片来证实具体细节。在本文的调研过程中，也未发现此类来源。

What the circulating example does clarify, regardless of its origin, is a pattern worth examining. Brand loyalty can short-circuit critical thinking fast enough to make observers praise a design choice before asking whether it actually works under pressure.
无论该案例来源如何，它所揭示的现象值得深思。品牌忠诚度可能会迅速阻断人们的批判性思维，以至于观察者在还没弄清楚它在紧急情况下是否有效之前，就先对这种设计选择大加赞赏。

The regulatory framework is not ambiguous:
监管框架并不含糊：

*   OSHA 1910.157 requires workplace extinguishers to be mounted, located, and identified so employees can reach them without being subjected to possible injury.
*   OSHA 1910.157 法规要求工作场所的灭火器必须安装、定位并标识清楚，以便员工在取用时不会受到伤害。
*   NFPA guidance requires extinguishers along normal paths of travel, generally visible or clearly marked with signage when an obstruction exists.
*   NFPA 指南要求灭火器应放置在常规通行路径上，若存在遮挡，则必须可见或有清晰的标识。
*   A recessed cabinet is not automatically noncompliant; concealment without effective identification is the core problem.
*   嵌入式柜体本身并不违规；问题的核心在于在没有有效标识的情况下进行遮蔽。
*   Maximum travel distance to an extinguisher depends on hazard classification, and accessibility is treated as a functional requirement, not a preference.
*   到达灭火器的最大行程距离取决于危险等级，且可及性被视为一项功能性要求，而非个人偏好。

The rules leave room for elegant solutions. They leave no room for unmarked panels and hidden handles.
这些规则为优雅的解决方案留有余地，但绝不容许无标识面板和隐藏式把手的存在。

**Good-Looking and Compliant Are Not Mutually Exclusive**
**美观与合规并不冲突**

A clearly labeled cabinet with a coordinated finish is not a design compromise; it is the correct answer. NFPA guidance describes portable extinguishers as a first line of defense against incipient fires, with accessibility treated as a non-negotiable functional requirement. That framing reorients the entire debate: an extinguisher is infrastructure you design for, not furniture you style around.
一个带有清晰标识且装修协调的柜体并非设计上的妥协，而是正确答案。NFPA 指南将便携式灭火器描述为应对初期火灾的第一道防线，并将可及性视为不可妥协的功能性要求。这一框架重新定义了整个争论：灭火器是你必须为其进行设计的“基础设施”，而不是你为了美观而绕开的“家具”。

The confusion underlying this kind of praise is treating visual cleanliness as a proxy for good design. Consider the difference between a minimal app interface that buries the emergency logout button six menus deep and one that simply puts it where you can find it. Both might look polished. Only one works when it counts.
这种赞美背后的误区在于将视觉上的整洁等同于好的设计。试想一下，一个将紧急注销按钮深埋在六层菜单下的极简应用界面，与一个直接将其放在显眼位置的界面有何不同？两者看起来都很精致，但只有后者在关键时刻才有用。

Facilities teams and designers can still use recessed installations, coordinated finishes, and orderly placement. The non-negotiable constraints are prominent labeling, unobstructed access, and location along normal travel paths. Those are not aesthetic concessions.
设施团队和设计师依然可以使用嵌入式安装、协调的装修风格和有序的布局。但不可妥协的底线是：醒目的标识、无障碍的取用路径以及位于常规通行路线上。这些绝非审美上的让步。

During an emergency, a recessed cabinet without signage is indistinguishable from drywall.
在紧急情况下，没有标识的嵌入式柜体与干墙（石膏板）毫无区别。

The standard applied to an allegedly hidden extinguisher in any sleek, brand-conscious space should be the same standard applied to every warehouse, office park, and retail chain: accessible, identified, and reachable without delay. Emergency equipment is not a brand decision. Design around it accordingly.
任何追求时尚、注重品牌的空间中，针对所谓“隐藏式灭火器”的标准，都应与仓库、办公园区和连锁零售店所遵循的标准一致：即必须易于取用、标识清晰且能立即触及。应急设备不是品牌决策的范畴，请务必围绕它进行合理设计。
