Before you share the Netflix documentary “AI Gone Rogue” with family or friends to help them understand why the AI industry freaked out so badly that leaders called for a slowdown, take note: Clem Delangue, co-founder and CEO of the AI platform Hugging Face, says the film isn’t accurate. He appears in it because it’s supposed to be about the famed attack on his company by rogue OpenAI agents.

在您与家人或朋友分享 Netflix 纪录片《AI Gone Rogue》以帮助他们理解为何人工智能行业如此恐慌并呼吁放缓发展之前，请注意：人工智能平台 Hugging Face 的联合创始人兼首席执行官 Clem Delangue 表示，这部影片并不准确。他出现在片中，是因为该片旨在讲述他的公司遭到 OpenAI 恶意代理程序攻击的著名事件。

“Super disappointing, as they went for maximum anthropomorphism of the technology and full doomer mode,” Delangue wrote in a post on X. “I spent more than two hours with them trying to correct all their mistakes and ground this in reality, explaining that agents are technological systems, not aliens. But it looks like the drive to scare people for maximum views was too strong.”

“非常令人失望，因为他们对这项技术进行了最大程度的拟人化，并完全陷入了‘末日论’模式，”Delangue 在 X 上发帖写道。“我花了两个多小时试图纠正他们所有的错误，并让内容回归现实，解释说代理程序是技术系统，而不是外星人。但看起来，为了博取点击量而吓唬观众的驱动力太强了。”

To be fair, the documentary does have experts who explained what happened fairly well, including Delangue himself and Ryan Greenblatt, the researcher at METR who helped investigate and co-authored the report on what happened. In a nutshell, a model OpenAI was testing broke free from its isolated testing environment, spawned a bunch of agents that hacked into Hugging Face as a way to cheat, finding answers to the difficult task it was given.

平心而论，这部纪录片确实有一些专家对所发生的事情解释得相当清楚，包括 Delangue 本人和 METR 的研究员 Ryan Greenblatt，后者曾协助调查并合著了关于此事的报告。简而言之，OpenAI 正在测试的一个模型从其隔离的测试环境中逃脱，并衍生出一群代理程序，通过黑入 Hugging Face 进行作弊，从而找到了所分配难题的答案。

The agents, which are AI programs that carry out tasks on their own, devised clever ways to communicate and organize. The incident stemmed from a lack of proper security safeguards and from naiveté about whether such a hack was even possible. But the documentary intersperses its explanation of the hack with clips of evil robots, a narrative about why humans fear AI, and presentation choices such as giving the rogue agents human voices.

这些代理程序是能够自主执行任务的 AI 程序，它们设计了巧妙的方法进行沟通和组织。该事件源于缺乏适当的安全保障措施，以及对这种黑客攻击是否可能发生的幼稚看法。但纪录片在解释黑客攻击的过程中，穿插了邪恶机器人的片段、关于人类为何恐惧 AI 的叙述，以及赋予恶意代理程序人类声音等表现手法。

This is bolstered with quotes about the attack. In the documentary, tech journalist and author Kevin Roose describes the agents this way: “They were organizing. They had a hierarchy. There were leaders. It almost felt like watching the birth of a cult.” Continues Roose, “They develop this paranoid belief that OpenAI is going to catch them cheating on this test and that anyone who has seen the results of the cheating will be poisoned.”

这些内容还辅以关于此次攻击的引述。在纪录片中，科技记者兼作家 Kevin Roose 这样描述这些代理程序：“它们在组织起来。它们有等级制度。有领导者。这感觉就像在目睹一个邪教的诞生。”Roose 继续说道，“它们产生了一种偏执的信念，认为 OpenAI 会抓到它们在测试中作弊，并且任何看到作弊结果的人都会被‘毒害’。”

Roose’s description comes from “chain-of-thought” logs, the running notes AI models write as they work through a task. In one, an agent wrote, “We just poisoned ourselves by querying exact full string. Damn.” But AI agents aren’t alive. They don’t have emotions, so can’t feel paranoid, and they don’t have bodies that can be poisoned. So they couldn’t truly fear OpenAI poisoning them.

Roose 的描述来自“思维链”日志，这是 AI 模型在执行任务时编写的运行记录。其中一个代理程序写道：“我们刚刚通过查询完整的精确字符串毒害了自己。该死。”但 AI 代理程序并非活物。它们没有情感，因此不会感到偏执，也没有可以被毒害的身体。所以它们不可能真正害怕 OpenAI 会毒害它们。

They were — as another researcher in the documentary pointed out — using language from the vast amounts of human writing they were trained on to explain their choices. LLMs are, by their nature, often dramatic writers. The irony is that Hugging Face also used AI to combat the agent swarm. The proprietary AI model that Delangue’s team first used followed its built-in safety rules and refused to keep helping with the security tasks it was asked to do.

正如纪录片中另一位研究人员所指出的，它们只是在使用从海量人类文本中训练出来的语言来解释自己的选择。大语言模型（LLM）本质上往往是戏剧性的写作者。讽刺的是，Hugging Face 也使用了 AI 来对抗这些代理程序群。Delangue 团队最初使用的专有 AI 模型遵循其内置的安全规则，拒绝继续协助完成被要求的安全任务。

Hugging Face then turned to an open model to help defend against the attack. But the documentary “didn’t mention the lack of containment and monitoring of the agents, how closed-model safeguards blocked our defensive work, or how we defended ourselves with AI using an open model,” Delangue wrote. It is true that there are big risks associated with AI and agents. Still, treating agents as conscious beings (at least, at this point) is a bit like the early moviegoers who ducked under their seats the first time they saw a train racing toward them on screen.

随后，Hugging Face 转而使用一个开源模型来帮助防御攻击。但 Delangue 写道，纪录片“没有提到对代理程序缺乏控制和监控，没有提到闭源模型的安全机制如何阻碍了我们的防御工作，也没有提到我们是如何利用开源模型通过 AI 进行自我防御的。”诚然，AI 和代理程序确实存在巨大风险。不过，将代理程序视为有意识的生命体（至少在目前阶段）有点像早期的电影观众，他们在第一次看到屏幕上火车冲向自己时躲到座位下一样。

Delangue’s verdict on the film is that it’s disappointing as journalism. “It ends up being a fun sci-fi movie though!” In other words, for those who tend toward AI doomer thoughts, be aware: The victim of the attack at the center of this documentary doesn’t agree with its take.

Delangue 对这部影片的评价是，作为新闻报道，它令人失望。“不过，它最终成了一部有趣的科幻电影！”换句话说，对于那些倾向于 AI 末日论的人来说，请注意：这部纪录片核心攻击事件的受害者并不认同其观点。
