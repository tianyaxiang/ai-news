The PHP security team would like to give advance notice of an upcoming security release. It is scheduled for Wednesday, 21 October 2026 at 14:00 UTC. It addresses two critical vulnerabilities, both rated CVSS v4.0 9.2. All PHP users are encouraged to prepare now so they can upgrade as soon as the release is available. We reserve the right to release earlier should details of either vulnerability become public, or should evidence of active exploitation appear in the wild. Full details, affected versions, and the fixed versions will be published with the release. Thank you for helping us keep PHP secure.

PHP 安全团队谨此提前通知即将发布的安全性更新。该版本定于 2026 年 10 月 21 日星期三 14:00 UTC 发布。此次更新旨在解决两个关键漏洞，两者的 CVSS v4.0 评分均为 9.2。我们鼓励所有 PHP 用户立即做好准备，以便在发布后尽快升级。如果任何漏洞的详细信息被公开，或者出现实际利用的证据，我们保留提前发布的权利。完整的详细信息、受影响版本及修复版本将在发布时公布。感谢您帮助我们维护 PHP 的安全。
