Google’s more personalized Gemini AI is heading to your car

Google 正在将更具个性化的 Gemini AI 带入您的汽车。

Gemini AI can now draw from Gmail, Calendar, Maps, Photos, and other apps while you drive.

Gemini AI 现在可以在您驾驶时从 Gmail、日历、地图、照片和其他应用程序中提取信息。

Google is bringing Personal Intelligence to Gemini AI in your car. With this update, Gemini in Android Auto and cars with Google built-in can tap into your information across Google apps, including Gmail, Calendar, Maps, Photos, YouTube, and Search.

Google 正在为车载 Gemini AI 引入“个人智能”（Personal Intelligence）功能。通过此次更新，Android Auto 以及内置 Google 系统的汽车中的 Gemini 可以调用您在各个 Google 应用中的信息，包括 Gmail、日历、地图、照片、YouTube 和搜索。

As an example, Google says you can ask Gemini about your flight status if you’re driving to the airport. The AI assistant can pull your flight details from a confirmation message in Gmail and then use that information to look up the status. You can also ask Gemini to “take me to my doctor’s appointment” instead of pulling up directions manually, or ask it to “find the car wash from the photo I took last week and navigate there.”

以开车去机场为例，Google 表示您可以询问 Gemini 航班状态。该 AI 助手可以从 Gmail 的确认邮件中提取航班详情，并利用这些信息查询状态。您还可以要求 Gemini“带我去预约的医生那里”，而无需手动查找路线，或者让它“找到我上周拍照的那家洗车店并导航过去”。

Google brought its Gemini assistant to Android Auto last year before rolling it out to cars with Google built-in in April. Several carmakers—including Ford, GM, Honda, Nissan, Volvo, and Polestar—have shipped newer cars with Google built-in, which adds support for native Android apps.

Google 去年将 Gemini 助手引入了 Android Auto，并于四月将其推广至内置 Google 系统的汽车。包括福特、通用、本田、日产、沃尔沃和极星在内的多家汽车制造商已经推出了内置 Google 系统的新车型，这些车型增加了对原生 Android 应用程序的支持。

Google says it will connect your apps to Gemini only if you enable its Personal Intelligence setting. It will also share personalized responses only if you have your phone connected to your car. “If you don’t use Personal Intelligence, everything in the car still works: You can use navigation, listen to music, make calls, and ask questions, but the answers won’t be tailored to you,” Google says.

Google 表示，只有在您启用“个人智能”设置后，才会将您的应用程序与 Gemini 连接。此外，只有在您的手机连接到汽车时，它才会提供个性化的回复。Google 称：“如果您不使用‘个人智能’，车内的一切功能依然正常：您可以使用导航、听音乐、拨打电话和提问，但回答将不会针对您个人进行定制。”

Personal Intelligence is rolling out to Android Auto today, with support coming to cars with Google built-in in the coming months.

“个人智能”功能于今日开始向 Android Auto 推送，内置 Google 系统的汽车将在未来几个月内获得支持。
