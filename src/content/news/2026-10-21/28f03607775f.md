# Judge dismisses Sam Altman’s sister’s sexual abuse lawsuit

法官驳回了萨姆·奥特曼（Sam Altman）妹妹提起的性虐待诉讼。

Ann Altman’s lawsuit against her brother was dismissed with prejudice, meaning it can’t be refiled.

安·奥特曼（Ann Altman）针对其兄弟提起的诉讼已被驳回，且不得再次起诉。

On Tuesday, US District Judge Zachary Bluestone dismissed the lawsuit Ann Altman filed last year alleging that her brother, OpenAI CEO Sam Altman, had sexually abused her throughout her childhood. As reported earlier by The Guardian, the judge wrote that over the past year, Ann had failed to appear for an independent medical examination or meet other deadlines despite warnings that it would result in the case being dismissed.

周二，美国地方法官扎卡里·布鲁斯通（Zachary Bluestone）驳回了安·奥特曼去年提起的诉讼，该诉讼指控其兄弟、OpenAI首席执行官萨姆·奥特曼在她的童年时期对其进行过性虐待。据《卫报》早前报道，法官在裁决书中写道，在过去的一年中，尽管已收到若不配合将导致案件被驳回的警告，安仍未参加独立的医学检查，也未能遵守其他截止日期。

When the case was filed last year, a statement shared by Sam Altman, his mother, and his brothers said, “Annie has made deeply hurtful and entirely untrue claims about our family, and especially Sam… All of these claims are utterly untrue. This situation causes immense pain to our entire family.”

当该案件去年被提起时，萨姆·奥特曼及其母亲和兄弟们发表声明称：“安妮对我们的家庭，尤其是对萨姆，做出了极其伤人且完全不实的指控……所有这些指控都完全是虚假的。这种情况给我们整个家庭带来了巨大的痛苦。”

Bluestone granted Sam Altman’s motion to dismiss the case with prejudice, meaning it can’t be refiled in that court, while denying Sam’s request for attorney fees and costs. Santos Perez, a lawyer who had represented Ann until withdrawing this month, told The Guardian in a statement that “I am no longer authorized to comment on this case but I continue to believe in Annie.”

布鲁斯通批准了萨姆·奥特曼驳回案件的动议，并裁定不得再次起诉，同时拒绝了萨姆提出的律师费和诉讼费用请求。曾代理安的案件直至本月退出的律师桑托斯·佩雷斯（Santos Perez）在给《卫报》的一份声明中表示：“我已不再被授权就此案发表评论，但我依然相信安妮。”
