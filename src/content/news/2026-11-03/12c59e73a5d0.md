# If you love a website, back it up

这个故事始于一个小小的悲剧。你看，我有一些珍藏的旧网页，时不时就想回去看看。有些网站多年甚至几十年都没有变过，时不时去看看它们，确认这位“老朋友”依然安好，总有一种温馨的感觉。当然，有时悲剧也会发生。

There was this fansite called teapuppies.com for an old anime series and and toy line called Ochaken. It’s about these cute cartoon dog characters who have tea leaves for ears, each of them named after a different kind of tea. The site had been online since 2004 and had basically not changed since 2005, but it was nice just having it around. Except when I went to visit it a few weeks ago, and discovered that not only was the site gone but the domain is now being used to serve online gambling ads.

曾经有一个名为 teapuppies.com 的粉丝网站，是关于一部名为《茶犬》（Ochaken）的旧动画系列和玩具线的。它讲述的是一群可爱的卡通狗角色，它们的耳朵是茶叶，每只狗都以一种不同的茶命名。该网站自 2004 年上线以来，基本从 2005 年起就没变过，但能留着它感觉真好。直到几周前我去访问时，才发现网站不仅消失了，域名现在还被用来投放网络赌博广告。

I work in web archiving, by the way. Which is to say that I should have known the site would go offline someday, and that I have the tools to have done something about it. I could have archived that site while it was still around, but I didn’t. This post is here to beg you to do what I didn’t think to, and to teach you how.

顺便提一下，我从事的是网络归档工作。也就是说，我本该预料到这个网站迟早会下线，而且我有工具可以为此做点什么。我本可以在它还在的时候将其归档，但我没有。写这篇文章是为了恳求你去做我没想到的事，并教你如何操作。

You see, when I talk to a lot of people about web archiving, they always seem to believe it’s something they couldn’t do. It’s something a big organization like the Internet Archive does, and if you want a webpage to be saved you just have to wait and hope it gets done. But that’s not true! What I really want everyone to know is that you have control over the world around you, you can take action to preserve things and make things better. And not just can, but should! Don’t just wait for someone else to act - get out there and and make it happen! Got a favourite site? Back it up! Got some information you don’t want to get lost? Back it up! Favourite post somewhere? All together now. Back! It! Up!

你看，当我与许多人谈论网络归档时，他们似乎总认为这是他们做不到的事情。他们觉得这是像“互联网档案”（Internet Archive）这样的大型组织才会做的事，如果你想保存一个网页，只能等待并祈祷它被保存下来。但事实并非如此！我真正想让大家知道的是，你对周围的世界拥有掌控权，你可以采取行动来保存事物并让世界变得更好。不仅可以，而且应该！不要只是等待别人行动——走出去，让它发生！有喜欢的网站吗？备份它！有不想丢失的信息吗？备份它！某处有喜欢的帖子吗？大家一起喊：备份！它！

The above options are great for single pages or sites that are small enough for you to browse manually. But if you’ve got a webpage that’s big enough that it’s going to be annoying visiting all of the pages by hand, you probably want to look at a web crawler tool for web archiving. There are a bunch of options out there, but the easiest to use as an individual is probably Browsertrix. A web crawler is basically an automation tool built around a web browser; you provide instructions on what page to start from and what pages it should visit, and it will go ahead and click links to automatically find pages so that it can archive the page as completely as possible. Unlike the other tools, which you mainly run on your own computer, Browsertrix is primarily a web app. It’s also the only paid tool in this post - there’s a seven day trial, but after that you do have to pay per use. Since it’s open source, though, technically inclined users can run their own copies on their computer or deploy a copy somewhere if they like.
