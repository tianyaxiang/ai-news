I haven’t owned a TV in years and would like one that respects my privacy. Are there any TV’s which do this well? I am looking for something in the 60”to 80”range and would like a 4k display with at least 60hz display. I don’t mind paying a premium.

我多年来一直没有电视，想要一台尊重我隐私的电视。有哪款电视能做到这一点吗？我正在寻找 60 到 80 英寸范围内的产品，希望是 4K 分辨率且至少 60Hz 的显示屏。我不介意支付溢价。

I have heard good things about Apple TV as a better alternative to most smart TV native apps. Would some TV’s pair better with this than others for privacy? I don’t want the TV doing anything other than being the display for Apple TV.

我听说 Apple TV 是大多数智能电视原生应用之外更好的选择。在隐私方面，是否有些电视与它的搭配效果更好？我不希望电视除了作为 Apple TV 的显示器之外做任何其他事情。

Buy an LG OLED TV. They're as gorgeous as they are notorious. I just got a 77" LG C6 and it is crazy good. Root it. Install Glasshouse and disable all telemetry. YOU now own the glass. Now you can chromecast to the TV, use Adfree YouTube, access IP TV, etc etc etc. No point crippling your TV by pulling the wireless module and voiding your warranty.

买一台 LG OLED 电视。它们既华丽又臭名昭著。我刚买了一台 77 英寸的 LG C6，效果好得惊人。给它获取 Root 权限。安装 Glasshouse 并禁用所有遥测功能。现在这块屏幕归你了。你可以投屏到电视、使用无广告版 YouTube、访问 IP 电视等等。没必要通过拆除无线模块来损坏电视并使保修失效。

This seems excessive. Easier to disconnect it from the internet completely, and have it boot to HDMI1, hook up an external HDMI-CEC switch, and never think about it again. With "Hotel Mode" you can make this effectively permanent and never see LG's UI again unless you want it.

这似乎有点过头了。更简单的方法是将其完全断开网络，设置开机进入 HDMI1，连接一个外部 HDMI-CEC 切换器，然后就不用再管它了。通过“酒店模式”，你可以让这种设置永久生效，除非你自己想看，否则永远不会再看到 LG 的用户界面。

I personally wouldn't trust these not to spy on you even disconnected. They can still scan for networks and bluetooth devices around you. It won't be long until they have their own cellular modem so they don't need your network at all of the spying parts.

我个人即使在断网的情况下也不会相信它们不会监视你。它们仍然可以扫描你周围的网络和蓝牙设备。用不了多久，它们就会内置自己的蜂窝调制解调器，这样在进行监视时根本不需要你的网络。

I used to work for an IoT connectivity provider; I know that some of the manufacturers of various devices have been investigating adding cellular and soldered eUICC. So it's not even that it won't be long; I'm sure they already have designs and are just waiting for eUICC support to catch up.

我曾经为一家物联网连接提供商工作；我知道一些设备制造商一直在研究添加蜂窝网络和焊接式 eUICC。所以这不仅仅是“用不了多久”的问题；我相信他们已经有了设计方案，只是在等待 eUICC 的支持跟上。

There has never been a shred of evidence for any TV model actually doing this despite how often it's speculated about in comment sections. It's kinda weird how the most sensible, straightforward mitigation for the TV spying people claim to be concerned always gets this evidence-free FUD thrown out to claim it's pointless.

尽管评论区经常猜测，但从未有任何证据表明有哪款电视型号真的在这样做。奇怪的是，人们声称担心的电视监视问题，其最合理、最直接的缓解措施，总是被这种毫无根据的恐慌言论（FUD）所否定，声称这样做毫无意义。
