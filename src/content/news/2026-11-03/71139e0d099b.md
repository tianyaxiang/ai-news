# Apple is ‘not able to predict’ when it’ll have enough iPhone Duos to go around

苹果公司表示“无法预测”何时能有足够的 iPhone Duo 供应市场。

Apple CEO John Ternus doesn’t have a timeline for when iPhone Duo supply constraints could lift. During an interview with Reuters, Ternus said the company is “working hard to get it to as many customers as quickly as possible, but at this point, we’re not able to predict when supply and demand will be in balance.”

苹果公司首席执行官约翰·特努斯（John Ternus）对于 iPhone Duo 的供应限制何时能够解除并没有给出时间表。在接受路透社采访时，特努斯表示，公司正在“努力尽快将产品送到尽可能多的客户手中，但目前我们无法预测供需何时能达到平衡。”

Apple launched its first foldable last month for $1,999, featuring a 5.4 outer display and 7.6-inch inner screen. Preorders for the iPhone Duo quickly sold out, with some shipments expected to arrive in 2027. Apple also made a limited number of iPhone Duo models available in-store at launch.

苹果公司上个月推出了其首款折叠屏手机，售价 1,999 美元，配备 5.4 英寸外屏和 7.6 英寸内屏。iPhone Duo 的预购很快售罄，部分订单预计要到 2027 年才能送达。苹果还在发布时于店内提供了少量现货。

In his interview with Reuters, Ternus also addressed reports about cracked screens, saying “a very small percentage” of iPhone Duos were returned for this issue, and that Apple is replacing them.

在接受路透社采访时，特努斯还回应了有关屏幕破裂的报道，称“极小比例”的 iPhone Duo 因该问题被退回，苹果公司正在为这些用户进行更换。

The company has contended with supply chain constraints and rising memory costs for months now. This resulted in price increases across its product lineup, along with higher iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max starting prices. “What we’re seeing with memory pricing right now is unprecedented,” Ternus said during Apple’s Q4 2026 earnings call on Monday.

几个月来，该公司一直面临供应链限制和内存成本上涨的问题。这导致其全线产品价格上涨，iPhone 18 Pro 和 Pro Max 的起售价也随之提高。特努斯在周一的苹果 2026 财年第四季度财报电话会议上表示：“我们目前看到的内存定价是前所未有的。”

Ternus added that the company expects to see “even higher memory costs” during Apple’s December quarter but notes that this is “partly offset” by recent price increases. Even without a standard iPhone 18 model, Apple reported record-breaking iPhone revenue at $57.58 billion during its fourth quarter.

特努斯补充说，公司预计在 12 月季度将面临“更高的内存成本”，但指出这已被近期的价格上涨“部分抵消”。尽管没有推出标准版 iPhone 18，苹果在第四季度仍报告了创纪录的 575.8 亿美元 iPhone 营收。
