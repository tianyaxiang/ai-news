### Birdbuddy vs. Birdfy: Which Budget Smart Feeder Should You Buy? (2026)

Save this story. Hard to believe, but smart bird feeders have been around for six years. The idea started with Birdbuddy (then styled Bird Buddy), which launched a Kickstarter campaign in November 2020 for the world’s first app-connected, AI-powered smart bird feeder. Birdfy followed in 2021 with its first camera-equipped feeder, and today the two companies remain the most popular brands in the smart bird-feeder space, with a sizable catalog of offerings, including smart bird baths and smart nesting boxes.

保存这篇文章。令人难以置信的是，智能喂鸟器已经存在六年了。这个想法始于 Birdbuddy（当时名为 Bird Buddy），该公司于 2020 年 11 月发起了众筹活动，推出了世界上第一款连接应用程序、由人工智能驱动的智能喂鸟器。Birdfy 紧随其后，于 2021 年推出了其首款配备摄像头的喂鸟器。如今，这两家公司仍然是智能喂鸟器领域最受欢迎的品牌，拥有庞大的产品目录，包括智能鸟浴盆和智能巢箱。

Thanks to two new releases from Birdfy and Birdbuddy, you can now get a premium smart bird feeder experience for under $100—something that just a year or two ago would have cost $200 or more. I tested both Birdfy’s Rookie and Birdbuddy’s Nook in my backyard for four weeks each, noting annoyances, especially helpful features, and which features were available without a subscription.

得益于 Birdfy 和 Birdbuddy 的两款新产品，你现在可以用不到 100 美元的价格获得优质的智能喂鸟器体验——而在仅仅一两年前，这可能需要 200 美元或更多。我在后院分别测试了 Birdfy 的 Rookie 和 Birdbuddy 的 Nook 四周，记录了使用过程中的烦恼、特别有用的功能，以及哪些功能无需订阅即可使用。

While both have a significant number of pluses and minuses, Birdbuddy’s Nook, despite costing $20 more and failing to capture every visit, came out just slightly ahead due to its more premium hardware experience.

虽然两者都有不少优缺点，但 Birdbuddy 的 Nook 尽管价格高出 20 美元且未能捕捉到每一次造访，但凭借其更优质的硬件体验，还是略胜一筹。

Released in October of last year, Birdfy’s Rookie was positioned as a smart feeder for beginners, with USB-C charging only, no solar (though you can buy a more expensive model that has it), and no complicated assembly process. It functions basically as a lightweight gravity pet feeder with a 1.3-liter seed capacity and a camera on the side—just snap the three main pieces together, charge the camera, and you’re good to go. It can only be pole- or wall-mounted, not hung.

Birdfy 的 Rookie 于去年 10 月发布，定位为初学者智能喂鸟器，仅支持 USB-C 充电，没有太阳能功能（尽管你可以购买更昂贵的型号），且没有复杂的组装过程。它基本上是一个轻量级的重力式宠物喂食器，拥有 1.3 升的种子容量和侧置摄像头——只需将三个主要部件扣在一起，给摄像头充电，就可以使用了。它只能安装在杆子或墙壁上，不能悬挂。

While I loved the simplicity of the Rookie and appreciated that it uses the same excellent app as the pricier Birdfy feeders, the 1080p camera’s picture quality was noticeably lower than the Nook’s, and while it did capture every bird that visited, there were also plenty of false-alarm motion detections.

虽然我很喜欢 Rookie 的简洁性，也很欣赏它使用了与更昂贵的 Birdfy 喂鸟器相同的优秀应用程序，但其 1080p 摄像头的画质明显低于 Nook。虽然它确实捕捉到了每一只造访的鸟，但也出现了大量的误报动作检测。

I also found some significant drawbacks to the design over my four-week test. First, though the feeder itself is IP66 rated for water resistance, there’s no overhang on the roof, which means water is going to get into the seed if it rains (as it did off and on throughout my testing period). Second, the lid just sits loosely on the top, so it kept blowing off whenever there was a big gust of wind. And third, perhaps most frustratingly, because the camera is on the side, it didn’t do the greatest job capturing larger birds, though the camera lens stayed relatively clean, being farther away from the action, and small-bird shots were well framed. Birdfy advertises the battery charge as lasting seven weeks, but my test unit lasted less than two.

在四周的测试中，我还发现该设计存在一些重大缺陷。首先，虽然喂鸟器本身具有 IP66 防水等级，但屋顶没有外伸部分，这意味着如果下雨（正如我测试期间断断续续发生的那样），水会进入种子槽。其次，盖子只是松散地放在顶部，所以每当有大风时，它就会被吹掉。第三，也许最令人沮丧的是，由于摄像头在侧面，它在捕捉大型鸟类方面表现不佳，尽管摄像头镜头保持得相对干净（因为它离活动区域较远），且小鸟的拍摄构图很好。Birdfy 宣称电池续航可达七周，但我的测试设备续航不到两周。

One big advantage the Rookie has, however, is its seed capacity. At 1.3 liters, it’s only slightly less voluminous than Birdfy’s popular original feeder, which packs 1.5 liters. (Though note that this still pales in comparison to Birdfy’s Bamboo feeder, a top pick in our guide to the Best Smart Bird Feeders, which holds 3.5 liters.)

然而，Rookie 的一个巨大优势是其种子容量。1.3 升的容量仅比 Birdfy 受欢迎的原始喂鸟器（1.5 升）略小。（但请注意，与我们“最佳智能喂鸟器”指南中的首选产品 Birdfy Bamboo 喂鸟器相比，这仍然相形见绌，后者容量为 3.5 升。）

Feeder capacity aside, Birdfy’s app is well designed and simple, and it makes it easy to see which birds visited that day. You can even watch your feeder on TV if you have Roku—the Birdfy app is available on devices running Roku OS 15.1 or higher. Best of all, as of November 1, Birdfy offers unlimited automatic AI-based identification without a subscription, which is a rarity among smart bird feeders. Birdfy’s ID is pretty decent, with nearly no mistakes during my test.

撇开喂食器容量不谈，Birdfy 的应用程序设计精良且简单，可以轻松查看当天有哪些鸟类造访。如果你有 Roku，甚至可以在电视上观看喂鸟器画面——Birdfy 应用程序适用于运行 Roku OS 15.1 或更高版本的设备。最棒的是，自 11 月 1 日起，Birdfy 提供无需订阅的无限自动 AI 识别功能，这在智能喂鸟器中非常罕见。Birdfy 的识别功能相当不错，在我的测试中几乎没有出错。

If you want to spring for more storage or longer video recording (free tier is limited to 5 GB and 20 seconds), you can opt for Birdfy Plus for $80/year or Birdfy Pro for $150/year.

如果你想要更多的存储空间或更长的视频录制时间（免费层级限制为 5 GB 和 20 秒），你可以选择每年 80 美元的 Birdfy Plus 或每年 150 美元的 Birdfy Pro。

Birdbuddy’s Nook launched in late October this year and is also aimed at beginners and budget-conscious shoppers. It looks a lot like Birdbuddy’s flagship Home (previously the 2 Pro), just smaller and squatter. It has the same thick plastic construction with a roof to keep out rain, but the Nook’s hinged lid makes filling a breeze. (Note that the $99 version does not come with the extended perch or solar roof, as pictured.)

Birdbuddy 的 Nook 于今年 10 月下旬推出，同样针对初学者和注重预算的购物者。它看起来很像 Birdbuddy 的旗舰产品 Home（之前称为 2 Pro），只是更小、更矮。它具有相同的厚塑料结构和防雨屋顶，但 Nook 的铰链式盖子使填充变得轻而易举。（请注意，99 美元的版本不附带图片中所示的加长栖木或太阳能屋顶。）

Like the Home, the camera is a half-sphere about the size of a ping-pong ball that pops in and out with a magnet; you can position it vertically or horizontally depending on which style of photos you prefer. My test unit came with the solar panel, so I was not able to test the battery life of the camera, but know that the Nook’s battery only delivers 3,900 mAh compared to the Rookie’s 5,200 mAh.

与 Home 一样，摄像头是一个乒乓球大小的半球体，通过磁铁吸附，可以轻松拆卸；你可以根据自己喜欢的照片风格将其垂直或水平放置。我的测试设备配有太阳能电池板，所以我无法测试摄像头的电池续航，但需要知道的是，Nook 的电池容量仅为 3,900 mAh，而 Rookie 为 5,200 mAh。

The Nook shoots in 2K HDR, resulting in noticeably crisper imagery and sound. Unlike the Rookie’s side camera, the Nook’s is centered on the feeder—which makes for better full-bird shots, but its close proximity means it tends to get dirtier and smudged more easily than the Rookie’s side camera.
