The agentic web is here. In 2001, a decade after inventing the World Wide Web, Tim Berners-Lee articulated a new vision: an internet navigated by autonomous software agents that address our every need, without requiring us to muck about with browsers and search engines ourselves. At the time, the idea seemed far-fetched. But a quarter-century on, AI agents are beginning to make it a reality. They can triage your inbox, do your grocery shopping, and even access your bank accounts. This new era is being called the agentic web. It will inevitably transform the internet—and whether the human web survives this transition is anyone’s guess. Find out how AI agents are transforming the internet, and what we stand to lose in the process. —Grace Huckins

“代理网络”时代已经到来。2001年，在发明万维网十年后，蒂姆·伯纳斯-李提出了一个新愿景：一个由自主软件代理导航的互联网，它们能满足我们的一切需求，而无需我们亲自摆弄浏览器和搜索引擎。当时，这个想法似乎遥不可及。但四分之一个世纪过去了，人工智能代理正开始将其变为现实。它们可以整理你的收件箱、代购杂货，甚至访问你的银行账户。这个新时代被称为“代理网络”。它将不可避免地改变互联网——人类网络能否在这一转型中幸存，谁也说不准。了解人工智能代理如何改变互联网，以及我们在此过程中可能失去什么。——格蕾丝·哈金斯

Are we there yet? Eliminating traffic deaths. Every year, traffic crashes kill more than 1 million people worldwide. They’re also the leading cause of death for children and young adults. Cheap, proven ways to make roads safer exist, including tightening intersections with planters and lowering speed limits. But government logjams and red tape can make it difficult to roll out these changes. Still, some cities are making impressive progress by investing in public transit, extending curbs, and using cameras to spot danger zones in need of a redesign. Here’s how close we are to eradicating traffic deaths. —Sara Kiley Watson

我们快到了吗？消除交通死亡事故。每年，全球有超过100万人死于交通事故。它们也是儿童和年轻人死亡的主要原因。目前已有廉价且行之有效的方法来提高道路安全性，包括利用花坛收窄路口和降低限速。但政府的僵局和官僚作风可能使这些变革难以推行。尽管如此，一些城市通过投资公共交通、扩建路缘石以及利用摄像头识别需要重新设计的危险区域，正在取得令人瞩目的进展。了解我们距离根除交通死亡事故还有多远。——萨拉·凯利·沃森
