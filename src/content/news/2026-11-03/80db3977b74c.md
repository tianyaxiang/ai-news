Meta will end Quest 2 OS updates in 2027.

Meta 将于 2027 年停止 Quest 2 的操作系统更新。

The Quest 2 and Pro were discontinued in 2024, and next year, Meta will stop releasing OS updates for the VR headsets.

Quest 2 和 Pro 已于 2024 年停产，明年 Meta 将停止为这些 VR 头显发布操作系统更新。

Meta is planning to release the last OS update for its Quest 2 and Quest Pro VR headsets by March 2027, the company announced today. The last OS update for those devices is planned to be v213, and after that, Meta will continue to ship security patches and critical bug fixes through the end of 2027. Apps that support the Quest 2 or Quest Pro can also be submitted through December 2027, Meta says.

Meta 公司今日宣布，计划在 2027 年 3 月前为其 Quest 2 和 Quest Pro VR 头显发布最后一次操作系统更新。这些设备的最后一次系统更新计划为 v213 版本，此后 Meta 将在 2027 年底前继续提供安全补丁和关键错误修复。Meta 表示，支持 Quest 2 或 Quest Pro 的应用程序提交截止日期也为 2027 年 12 月。

Meta is calling the Quest 3 and 3S “now the baseline for Meta VR gamers.” The company is announcing this shift ahead of the planned release of its new VR Glasses, which are set to launch sometime in spring 2027.

Meta 将 Quest 3 和 3S 称为“目前 Meta VR 玩家的基准设备”。该公司在计划于 2027 年春季发布的全新 VR 眼镜之前宣布了这一转变。

“Quest 2 and Quest Pro laid the foundation for this platform, and millions of people still play on them,” Meta says. “Quest 3 and Quest 3S give your apps more performance headroom and mixed reality built in, and apps built for them carry forward to Meta VR Glasses, which run on the same OS and SDKs.”

Meta 表示：“Quest 2 和 Quest Pro 为该平台奠定了基础，目前仍有数百万人使用它们。Quest 3 和 Quest 3S 为应用程序提供了更多的性能空间和内置的混合现实功能，且为这些设备构建的应用程序可以延续到运行相同操作系统和 SDK 的 Meta VR 眼镜上。”

Meta launched the Quest 2 in 2020 and the Quest Pro in 2022. It discontinued both products in 2024.

Meta 于 2020 年推出了 Quest 2，并于 2022 年推出了 Quest Pro。这两款产品均已于 2024 年停产。
