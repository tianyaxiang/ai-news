The latest AI news we announced in October 2026.

我们于 2026 年 10 月发布的最新人工智能新闻。

Here’s a recap of some of our biggest AI updates from October, led by Gemini 4 Argon and Carbon, alongside our new world model Genie 4.

以下是我们 10 月份一些重大人工智能更新的回顾，其中包括 Gemini 4 Argon 和 Carbon，以及我们全新的世界模型 Genie 4。

In October, we released new AI tools to help you work, stay safe, and learn. We introduced Gemini 4 Argon for complex tasks, Genie 4 for building interactive simulations, and updated Android to block sophisticated scams. You can also use our new SynthID Detector to verify if content is AI-generated. These updates aim to make technology more helpful for your daily projects, personal security, and creative hobbies.

10 月份，我们发布了新的人工智能工具，旨在帮助您工作、保持安全并进行学习。我们推出了用于处理复杂任务的 Gemini 4 Argon、用于构建交互式模拟的 Genie 4，并更新了 Android 系统以拦截复杂的诈骗行为。您还可以使用我们新的 SynthID 检测器来验证内容是否由人工智能生成。这些更新旨在使技术在您的日常项目、个人安全和创意爱好中发挥更大的作用。

For more than 20 years, we’ve invested in machine learning and AI research, tools, and infrastructure to build products that make everyday life better for more people. Teams across Google are working on ways to unlock AI’s benefits in fields as wide-ranging as healthcare, crisis response, and education.

二十多年来，我们一直致力于机器学习和人工智能的研究、工具开发及基础设施建设，旨在打造能够改善更多人日常生活的产品。Google 各地的团队正在努力探索如何在医疗保健、危机应对和教育等广泛领域发挥人工智能的优势。

October underscored our commitment to AI innovation, as we released one frontier model globally and announced our next one just two weeks later. After rolling out Gemini 4 Argon — a frontier reasoning model — to developers, enterprises, scientists, and consumers, we announced Gemini 4 Carbon, our strongest model yet for long-horizon engineering and cybersecurity defense.

10 月凸显了我们对人工智能创新的承诺，我们在全球范围内发布了一个前沿模型，并在两周后宣布了下一个模型。在向开发者、企业、科学家和消费者推出前沿推理模型 Gemini 4 Argon 后，我们又宣布了 Gemini 4 Carbon，这是我们迄今为止在长周期工程和网络安全防御方面最强大的模型。

Step into real-time interactive worlds with Genie 4. Genie 4 brings unprecedented realism, advanced physics, lifelike human movement, and native audio to real-time interactive simulation. You can steer scenes as they unfold using natural language.

通过 Genie 4 进入实时交互世界。Genie 4 为实时交互模拟带来了前所未有的真实感、先进的物理特性、逼真的人体运动和原生音频。您可以使用自然语言引导场景的发展。

Having already watermarked more than 180 billion images and videos and 240,000 years of audio with SynthID, we’re expanding SynthID Detector globally in English so anyone can check whether an image, video, or audio clip was generated with AI.

我们已经使用 SynthID 为超过 1800 亿张图像和视频以及 24 万年的音频添加了水印，目前我们正在全球范围内以英语扩展 SynthID 检测器，以便任何人都能检查图像、视频或音频片段是否由人工智能生成。

Stay protected from mobile scams with new Android safeguards. AI-powered Scam Detection in Phone by Google and Google Messages already flags more than 5 million suspected scam calls and texts every month.

通过新的 Android 安全防护措施防范移动诈骗。Google 手机应用和 Google 信息中基于人工智能的诈骗检测功能每月已能标记超过 500 万次疑似诈骗电话和短信。

Meet Google Fitbit Edge, our all-new smartest fitness tracker ever. Available now in a slim, comfortable design with up to seven days of battery life, Fitbit Edge pairs precision GPS and automatic workout recognition across 50+ activities with personalized guidance from the Gemini-powered Google Health Coach.

隆重推出 Google Fitbit Edge，这是我们迄今为止最智能的健身追踪器。它采用纤薄舒适的设计，电池续航时间长达七天，并将精准 GPS 和 50 多项活动的自动锻炼识别功能与由 Gemini 提供支持的 Google 健康教练的个性化指导相结合。
