# Meta opens early access program for new Muse features
# Meta 开启 Muse 新功能抢先体验计划

After announcing a host of new features for Meta’s Muse AI app at its Connect 2026 developer conference this week, the company is opening up requests for early access. Meta shared on Friday that you can now ask Muse to put you on the list to try out its latest capabilities before anyone else.

在本周的 Connect 2026 开发者大会上宣布了 Meta Muse AI 应用的一系列新功能后，该公司现已开放抢先体验申请。Meta 周五表示，用户现在可以让 Muse 将自己加入名单，以便在其他人之前试用其最新功能。

Meta posted a link to a prompt on X that, when shared with Muse, will log that you’re interested in joining Meta’s early access program.

Meta 在 X 上发布了一个提示词链接，将其分享给 Muse 后，系统就会记录下你加入 Meta 抢先体验计划的意愿。

(If you don’t want to click through, you can just copy and paste the prompt: “Can you let the Muse team know I want to be part of the Muse early access program?”)

（如果你不想点击链接，也可以直接复制并粘贴这段提示词：“Can you let the Muse team know I want to be part of the Muse early access program?”）

While typically companies offer pilot tests or betas with select users, they often A/B test with a randomized group to gain feedback and measure results. In this case, however, Meta is looking for AI enthusiasts to try its features first. That tactic could help it stay ahead of the competition, given that this crowd tends to use multiple AI apps and agents and has a good understanding of the market.

虽然公司通常会向特定用户提供试点测试或 Beta 版本，并经常通过随机分组进行 A/B 测试以获取反馈和衡量结果，但这一次，Meta 希望由 AI 爱好者率先试用其功能。考虑到这一群体往往会使用多种 AI 应用和智能体，且对市场有深入了解，这种策略有助于 Meta 在竞争中保持领先。

As to what the new features will be? Meta teased many of those at Connect, including the launch of a digital avatar for Muse that you’ll be able to video chat with, tons more shopping partnerships and connectors, and an expansion of the Muse Mac app, which will soon be able to use your computer to complete various tasks.

至于有哪些新功能？Meta 在 Connect 大会上预告了其中许多内容，包括为 Muse 推出的数字虚拟形象（你可以与其进行视频聊天）、更多的购物合作伙伴关系和连接器，以及 Muse Mac 应用的扩展——它很快就能调用你的电脑来完成各种任务。

The company also said that Muse would be coming to Meta’s lineup of AI glasses, so users can interact with the agent just by speaking a wake word, then issuing a command.

该公司还表示，Muse 将登陆 Meta 的 AI 眼镜系列，用户只需说出唤醒词并下达指令，即可与该智能体进行交互。
