# Small yet Assistive: Spatially-Aware Post-Training for Low Vision
# 小而实用：面向低视力人群的空间感知后训练模型

**Abstract:** An estimated 1 billion people worldwide live with vision impairment, yet current vision-language models (VLMs) produce descriptions too vague for safe navigation by blind and low-vision (BLV) users. Large VLMs can generate high-quality audio-description-compliant narrations but cannot run on mobile devices; small VLMs offer competitive latency but lack spatial detail, directional cues, and hazard awareness for navigational assistance.

**摘要：** 全球估计有 10 亿人患有视力障碍，然而目前的视觉语言模型（VLM）所生成的描述过于模糊，无法满足盲人和低视力（BLV）用户安全导航的需求。大型 VLM 虽然能生成符合音频描述要求的高质量叙述，但无法在移动设备上运行；小型 VLM 虽然具有竞争力的延迟表现，但在导航辅助所需的空间细节、方向提示和危险感知方面存在欠缺。

We present Smol-VL-BLV, a compact VLM for blind and low-vision users that closes this gap using a 500M decoder transformer model and two post-training mechanisms: (1) teacher-student distillation and (2) Group Relative Policy Optimization (GRPO) with a composite BLV reward targeting directional language, metric distances, and hazard detection. Because multi-stage post-training can induce catastrophic forgetting, we add a lightweight finetuning stage after the last stage GRPO finetuning to recover general descriptive quality while preserving BLV-specific spatial grounding.

我们提出了 Smol-VL-BLV，这是一款专为盲人和低视力用户设计的紧凑型 VLM。该模型通过 5 亿参数的解码器 Transformer 模型以及两种后训练机制弥补了上述差距：(1) 教师-学生蒸馏；(2) 采用复合 BLV 奖励的组相对策略优化（GRPO），该奖励针对方向性语言、公制距离和危险检测进行了优化。由于多阶段后训练可能导致灾难性遗忘，我们在最后一阶段的 GRPO 微调之后增加了一个轻量级微调阶段，以在保留 BLV 特定空间定位能力的同时，恢复模型的通用描述质量。

Our best model substantially outperforms the baseline across various benchmarks, including tasks: VQA, BLV captioning, OCR, and latency. Compared with the baseline for relative improvement, it improves the Spatial score gain of 19.3%, and the Social score gain of 14.8%. It also increases OCR-Bench by 101.5%, and raises TextVQA accuracy by 44.2%. These results show that BLV-focused post-training improves both accessibility-specific spatial grounding and general visual-text reasoning.

我们的最佳模型在包括 VQA（视觉问答）、BLV 字幕生成、OCR（光学字符识别）和延迟等多个基准测试中均显著优于基准模型。与基准模型相比，其空间得分提升了 19.3%，社交得分提升了 14.8%。此外，OCR-Bench 得分提高了 101.5%，TextVQA 准确率提升了 44.2%。这些结果表明，专注于 BLV 的后训练不仅提升了针对无障碍需求的空间定位能力，还增强了通用的视觉-文本推理能力。

Deployed on a mid-range Android smartphone via Mixed-Precision Quantization, the model remains approx. 450 MB and runs entirely on-device, offline and without network dependency, generating descriptions with latency dependent on host hardware capabilities. Our model, dataset, and code is publicly released at this https URL.

该模型通过混合精度量化部署在中端 Android 智能手机上，模型大小约为 450 MB，可完全在设备端离线运行，无需网络依赖。其生成描述的延迟取决于宿主硬件的性能。我们的模型、数据集和代码已通过此链接公开。
