# When Explanations Cannot Be Read: Measuring and Correcting SHAP and LIME Rendering for Right-to-Left Languages
# 当解释无法被阅读时：针对从右向左书写语言的 SHAP 和 LIME 渲染测量与修正

**Abstract:** Post hoc explanation methods such as SHAP and LIME are widely used to interpret text classifiers, but their visualizations are mainly designed for left-to-right languages. When applied to right-to-left (RTL) languages such as Urdu, Arabic, Persian, and Hebrew, the attribution values remain mathematically valid, while their visual presentation fails. Tokens appear out of sequence, connected letterforms break apart, and plot layouts do not follow the natural reading direction.

**摘要：** SHAP 和 LIME 等事后解释方法被广泛用于解释文本分类器，但其可视化设计主要针对从左向右书写的语言。当应用于乌尔都语、阿拉伯语、波斯语和希伯来语等从右向左（RTL）书写的语言时，虽然归因值在数学上依然有效，但其视觉呈现却会失效。标记（Tokens）顺序错乱，连写的字形断裂，且图表布局不符合自然的阅读方向。

This study addresses this gap as a visualization problem rather than a limitation of the explanation methods themselves. We present SHAP-RTL, a rendering layer that corrects reading direction and script shaping in SHAP and LIME visualizations, with per-language font selection, while preserving the original attribution values, feature ordering, and model outputs.

本研究将这一差距视为一个可视化问题，而非解释方法本身的局限性。我们提出了 SHAP-RTL，这是一个渲染层，旨在修正 SHAP 和 LIME 可视化中的阅读方向和字体形态，并支持针对不同语言的字体选择，同时保留原始的归因值、特征排序和模型输出。

The approach is evaluated on Urdu, Arabic, Hebrew, and Persian hate and offensive-language datasets using TF-IDF and logistic regression classifiers. Rendering correctness is measured by an OCR round trip over 200 feature words per language. Default rendering yields character error rates of 0.820 to 0.979, meaning the label no longer carries its token; the common reshape-and-reorder workaround fails for Urdu at 0.998, worse than no correction; and the Matplotlib 3.11.0 text rewrite inverts that workaround, while SHAP-RTL remains correct under both versions.

该方法在乌尔都语、阿拉伯语、希伯来语和波斯语的仇恨与攻击性语言数据集上进行了评估，并使用了 TF-IDF 和逻辑回归分类器。渲染的正确性通过每种语言 200 个特征词的 OCR 往返测试进行衡量。默认渲染的字符错误率高达 0.820 到 0.979，这意味着标签已无法正确承载其对应的标记；常见的“重塑与重排”变通方法在乌尔都语上的错误率高达 0.998，效果甚至不如不做修正；而 Matplotlib 3.11.0 的文本重写功能则会使该变通方法失效，相比之下，SHAP-RTL 在两个版本中均能保持正确。

The framework also verbalizes the same attributions as short contextual explanations in the reader's language, constrained to the identified features. Evaluation in this paper concerns rendering correctness; assessment of the generated explanations is left to future work. The study highlights the importance of language-aware visualization in making post hoc explainability more accessible across different writing systems.

该框架还将相同的归因结果转化为读者母语的简短上下文解释，并限定在已识别的特征范围内。本文的评估重点在于渲染的正确性；对生成解释的评估将留待后续工作。本研究强调了语言感知可视化在提升不同书写系统下事后可解释性方面的关键作用。
