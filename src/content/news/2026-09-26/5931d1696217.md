# 16 Products From Women Designers You Need to Know
# 你需要了解的 16 款由女性设计师打造的产品

### Slate Auto Fastback SUV
### Slate Auto Fastback SUV

Tisha Johnson is a rarity in the car world: an automotive design leader who is not a man. Women are sometimes put in charge of the interiors or the materials, but few get to shape the whole look of a vehicle. As head of design at the EV-maker Slate, Johnson does. The pickup truck version of its EV starts at just under $25,000, making it the cheapest pickup on sale in the US, electric or not. Trouble is, cheap products are usually a compromise, and compromises rarely inspire affection. Johnson’s Slate, however, has won design awards, not just praise for its price tag.
在汽车界，Tisha Johnson 是一个罕见的存在：一位非男性的汽车设计领袖。女性有时会被安排负责内饰或材料，但很少有人能主导车辆的整体外观。作为电动汽车制造商 Slate 的设计主管，Johnson 做到了。其电动皮卡版本的起售价不到 25,000 美元，使其成为美国市场上最便宜的皮卡（无论是否为电动）。问题在于，廉价产品通常意味着妥协，而妥协很少能激发人们的喜爱。然而，Johnson 设计的 Slate 不仅因其价格受到赞誉，还赢得了多项设计大奖。

“We didn’t want [Slate] to be a jerk,” Johnson says, “or look like a beast on the road.” That thinking shows in the truck itself—not just in its modular, add-on-friendly design (pictured here in its Fastback guise), but in the fact the EV was designed to be loved. Strong shoulders and approachable round headlamps were favored over macho arrowed surfacing. “The attraction that draws you to Slate is based on something that is lasting from inside out. It’s a deeper connection,” Johnson says. Some 180,000 reservations and counting for the Slate, along with those awards, seem to more than prove her point.
“我们不想让 Slate 看起来像个‘混蛋’，”Johnson 说，“也不想让它在路上看起来像个野兽。”这种理念体现在了卡车本身——不仅在于其模块化、易于添加配件的设计（如图所示的 Fastback 版本），更在于这款电动汽车的设计初衷就是为了被人们所喜爱。相比于充满雄性荷尔蒙的尖锐线条，它采用了强壮的肩部设计和亲和力十足的圆形大灯。“吸引你选择 Slate 的原因，源于一种由内而外的持久特质。这是一种更深层次的连接，”Johnson 说道。目前 Slate 已收到超过 18 万份预订，加上所获的奖项，似乎足以证明她的观点。

### Away The Carry-On: Aluminum Edition
### Away The Carry-On：铝合金版

Away was founded in 2015 by entrepreneurs Jen Rubio and Steph Korey, both with backgrounds at the eyewear maker Warby Parker. In its first year, Away generated $12 million in sales and sold more than 100,000 stylish, reasonably priced suitcases. It has been an impressive rise, and the Softside Carry-on currently tops WIRED’s best buy list. Here however, we’ve chosen the aluminum version of the company’s handsome roll-aboard that’s built tough but perfectly sized for the overhead bin. It has a 32-liter capacity, dual TSA locks, 360-degree wheels, and an internal compression system. The signature hidden laundry bag remains a brilliant accoutrement.
Away 由企业家 Jen Rubio 和 Steph Korey 于 2015 年创立，两人此前均在眼镜制造商 Warby Parker 工作。在成立的第一年，Away 就创造了 1200 万美元的销售额，售出了超过 10 万个时尚且价格合理的行李箱。这一崛起令人印象深刻，其 Softside Carry-on 目前位居《连线》(WIRED) 最佳购买清单榜首。不过，我们在此选择了该公司那款外观精美、坚固耐用且尺寸完美适配飞机行李架的铝合金版登机箱。它拥有 32 升的容量、双 TSA 锁、360 度万向轮以及内部压缩系统。其标志性的隐藏式洗衣袋依然是一个绝妙的配件。

### Jelenew Mont Blanc Ultralight Long Windproof Jacket
### Jelenew Mont Blanc 超轻长款防风夹克

Cofounded by six-time cycling world champion Marion Clignet, Jelenew combines female-specific articulated shaping, recycled nylon, mesh ventilation and an adjustable hem in a windproof shell that packs into a 6.3 x 9.8-inch bag. Clignet brought elite racing experience directly into the brand’s development, with the fit shaped around women’s proportions rather than scaled down from men’s clothing. This particular jacket uses 94 percent recycled nylon, a PFAS-free water-repellent treatment, UV protection, and mesh ventilation. Generous waist pockets and adjustable drawstrings add practical detail.
由六届自行车世界冠军 Marion Clignet 联合创立的 Jelenew，将女性专属的关节剪裁、再生尼龙、网眼通风设计和可调节下摆结合在一起，打造出一款可收纳进 6.3 x 9.8 英寸小袋中的防风外壳。Clignet 将顶尖的竞赛经验直接带入了品牌开发中，其版型是根据女性比例量身定制的，而非简单地将男装缩小。这款夹克使用了 94% 的再生尼龙、不含 PFAS 的防水处理、防紫外线功能以及网眼通风设计。宽大的腰部口袋和可调节抽绳增添了实用细节。

### TaylorMade Kalea Gold Driver
### TaylorMade Kalea Gold 发球木

Light, easy to launch, and forgiving, Kaela has been designed for women who want more performance than they can get from hacking a set of men’s clubs. Principal product-development engineer Michelle Penney helped lead the development of the range, with input from female TaylorMade employees, players, and a dedicated women’s advisory board. The final design takes into account the key differences in women’s swing profiles. The 460 cc carbon driver is 44 inches long, with 11.5- or 13.5-degree lofts and a lightweight 47-gram shaft intended to help generate speed without making the club harder to control.
Kalea 轻盈、易于起球且容错率高，专为那些希望获得比使用男士球杆更好表现的女性而设计。首席产品开发工程师 Michelle Penney 领导了该系列的开发，并参考了 TaylorMade 女性员工、球员以及专门的女性咨询委员会的意见。最终设计充分考虑了女性挥杆特征的关键差异。这款 460cc 的碳纤维发球木长 44 英寸，提供 11.5 度或 13.5 度倾角，并配备 47 克轻量化杆身，旨在帮助提升挥杆速度，同时又不增加球杆的操控难度。

### Natural Cycles NC° Band
### Natural Cycles NC° 手环

Cofounded by CERN physicist Elina Berglund Scherwitzl, who helped discover the Higgs boson, Natural Cycles makes the world’s first FDA-cleared birth-control app. Its screenless NC° Band syncs its health readings to the app. Wear it on your wrist while you sleep and it tracks your temperature, heart rate, and movement during the night, then automatically syncs all that data to your phone in the morning. Those numbers are fed into the app’s algorithm to give you accurate—Natural Cycles claims an impressive 93 percent accuracy—daily fertility guidance. A recently announced Whoop partnership helps strengthen both brand’s offerings too.
Natural Cycles 由曾参与发现希格斯玻色子的欧洲核子研究中心 (CERN) 物理学家 Elina Berglund Scherwitzl 联合创立，该公司开发了全球首款获得 FDA 认证的避孕应用程序。其无屏幕的 NC° 手环可将健康读数同步至应用程序。在睡眠时佩戴，它会追踪你的体温、心率和夜间活动，并在早晨自动将所有数据同步到手机。这些数据会被输入到应用程序的算法中，为你提供准确的每日生育指导（Natural Cycles 声称准确率高达 93%）。最近宣布的与 Whoop 的合作也进一步增强了两个品牌的产品实力。

### Adafruit Pimoroni Badgeware Badger
### Adafruit Pimoroni Badgeware Badger

Ladyada, aka Limor Fried, became the first female engineer to appear on the cover of WIRED in April 2011, just six years after she founded Adafruit from her MIT dorm room. Her alias was inspired by 19th-century English mathematician and computing pioneer Ada Lovelace, which in turn provided inspiration for the brand’s name. Fried has grown Adafruit into a 100 percent woman-owned force in open-source electronics, making components, tools, and educational kits that lower the barrier to coding and hardware hacking. The company’s range spans beginner-friendly boards, wearable controllers, and touchscreens, alongside quirky maker gear such as the pictured programmable, wearable e-paper name badge.
Ladyada（原名 Limor Fried）在 2011 年 4 月成为首位登上《连线》(WIRED) 封面的女性工程师，而此时距离她在麻省理工学院宿舍创立 Adafruit 仅过去六年。她的化名灵感来源于 19 世纪英国数学家、计算机先驱 Ada Lovelace，这也成为了品牌名称的灵感来源。Fried 将 Adafruit 发展成为一家 100% 由女性拥有的开源电子领域力量，生产各种组件、工具和教育套件，降低了编程和硬件开发的门槛。该公司的产品范围涵盖了适合初学者的开发板、可穿戴控制器和触摸屏，以及如图所示的可编程、可穿戴电子纸名牌等奇特的创客装备。

### Raide Women’s TrailTech Cargo Tank
### Raide 女士 TrailTech 货运背心

More than just another running top, this multifunctional design combines an unpadded sports bra, a vest, and much of the carrying capacity of a small hydration pack in a single 4.6-ounce layer. Five well-positioned pockets hold up to 30 fluid ounces of water, a phone, your rain shell, and up to six fuel gels, all while keeping the load close to your body to help minimize bounce. Laser-perforated chest panels add ventilation, and the fit is designed around female anatomy rather than adapted from a men’s cut. It is a neat solution for runners who want to go the distance without adding layers on layers.
这不仅仅是一件普通的跑步上衣，这种多功能设计将无衬垫运动内衣、背心以及小型水袋包的大部分承载能力结合在了一件仅 4.6 盎司的单层衣物中。五个位置合理的口袋可容纳多达 30 液量盎司的水、手机、雨衣以及多达六个能量胶，同时将负重紧贴身体以减少晃动。激光打孔的胸部面板增加了透气性，版型设计完全基于女性身体结构，而非从男装剪裁改编而来。对于那些想要长距离奔跑又不想层层叠叠穿衣的跑者来说，这是一个绝佳的解决方案。
