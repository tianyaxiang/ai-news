# Dutch government builds alternative for Microsoft based on NixOS
# 荷兰政府基于 NixOS 构建微软替代方案

### Working together on digital autonomy
### 携手共建数字自主权

The DAWO community is an open community in which government, industry and society jointly build a digitally autonomous workplace for the Dutch government.
DAWO 社区是一个开放社区，政府、行业和社会各界在此共同为荷兰政府构建一个数字自主的工作环境。

### What DAWO stands for
### DAWO 的宗旨

DAWO brings public values and technology together. The community pursues five primary goals.
DAWO 将公共价值与技术融为一体。该社区追求五个主要目标。

*   **Digital autonomy:** Strengthening the digital autonomy of the Netherlands.
    **数字自主：** 加强荷兰的数字自主权。
*   **Collaboration:** Improving collaboration and knowledge sharing between governments and society.
    **协作：** 改善政府与社会之间的协作和知识共享。
*   **Security:** Safeguarding security and data protection.
    **安全：** 保障安全与数据保护。
*   **Innovation:** Encouraging innovation and more efficient ways of working.
    **创新：** 鼓励创新和更高效的工作方式。
*   **Verifiability:** Making government IT systems easier to inspect and verify.
    **可验证性：** 使政府 IT 系统更易于检查和验证。

### An open workplace is made of replaceable building blocks
### 一个由可替换组件构成的开放式工作环境

Not one single product, but separate building blocks that work together. Every part can be inspected and replaced.
它不是单一的产品，而是协同工作的独立组件。每一个部分都可以被检查和替换。

*   **AI:** Open and verifiable AI building blocks for the digital workplace.
    **人工智能：** 为数字工作环境提供开放且可验证的 AI 组件。
*   **Operating system:** DAWO-NixOS and installation building blocks for a reproducible workplace.
    **操作系统：** 提供 DAWO-NixOS 及用于构建可复现工作环境的安装组件。
*   **Cloud:** Building blocks for autonomous and verifiable cloud infrastructure.
    **云服务：** 用于构建自主且可验证的云基础设施的组件。
*   **Collaboration:** Open solutions for communication, documents and working together.
    **协作：** 用于沟通、文档处理和协同工作的开放解决方案。

### Events, news and taking part
### 活动、新闻与参与

The calendar, the latest news, the blog and the forum are on the member portal. Everything is public, and that is also where you sign up.
日历、最新新闻、博客和论坛均位于成员门户网站上。所有内容均为公开，您也可以在那里进行注册。

*   **Events:** See the calendar and register for meetups and workshops.
    **活动：** 查看日历并注册参加聚会和研讨会。
*   **News:** Releases, developments and DAWO in the media.
    **新闻：** 发布信息、进展动态以及媒体对 DAWO 的报道。
*   **Blog:** Stories and reports from the community.
    **博客：** 来自社区的故事和报告。
*   **Forum:** Ask questions and join the discussion. Anyone can read; posting requires an account.
    **论坛：** 提问并参与讨论。任何人都可以阅读，但发帖需要账户。

### Not for the community. With the community.
### 不仅仅是为了社区，而是与社区同行。

DAWO brings government, industry and open source together. Public and private build the same blueprint here. You can take part through conversations, code, documentation, pilots and events.
DAWO 将政府、行业和开源界汇聚在一起。公共部门和私营企业在此共同构建同一蓝图。您可以通过对话、代码、文档、试点项目和活动参与其中。
