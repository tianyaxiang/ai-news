# i built a cookieless analytics tool (and deleted my cookie banner)
# 我开发了一个无需 Cookie 的分析工具（并删除了我的 Cookie 横幅）

i'm om yaduvanshi, 18. i build ai agents, and my second project is cloudline — website analytics without cookies.
我是 Om Yaduvanshi，今年 18 岁。我从事 AI 智能体开发，我的第二个项目是 Cloudline——一个无需 Cookie 的网站分析工具。

the problem: i put GA4 on a side project, added the cookie banner the lawyers want, and watched a chunk of my visitors click “decline” and disappear from my stats forever. you're not measuring traffic. you're measuring consent.
问题在于：我在一个副业项目中接入了 GA4，并按照律师的要求添加了 Cookie 横幅，结果我眼睁睁看着一大批访客点击了“拒绝”，从此在我的统计数据中彻底消失。你测量的根本不是流量，而是在测量“同意率”。

so i built the alternative: one script tag under 2kb, pasted from your dashboard. no cookies, no ip storage, no banner needed for the analytics itself. a pageview stores exactly four things: page, referrer, screen size, time. that's the whole list.
所以我开发了一个替代方案：只需从仪表盘复制一段小于 2kb 的脚本标签。无需 Cookie，不存储 IP，分析本身也不需要横幅。一次页面浏览仅存储四项内容：页面、引荐来源、屏幕尺寸、时间。这就是全部数据。

what you get: top pages, traffic sources (search, social, newsletters, even ai assistants), scroll depth, button clicks, funnels, live visitor count. up to 50 sites in one dashboard.
你能获得的功能包括：热门页面、流量来源（搜索、社交媒体、新闻通讯，甚至 AI 助手）、滚动深度、按钮点击、转化漏斗以及实时访客统计。一个仪表盘最多可管理 50 个网站。

why this matters for you: if you've ever stared at a GA4 dashboard that felt built for a fortune 500 company, or delayed launching because the cookie banner copy wasn't ready — this is the tool that skips all of that. it's free while in early access, no card: trycloudline.space
这对你意味着什么：如果你曾对着为世界 500 强企业设计的 GA4 仪表盘感到头疼，或者因为还没写好 Cookie 横幅文案而推迟上线——这个工具能帮你跳过所有这些麻烦。目前处于早期访问阶段，免费且无需绑定信用卡：trycloudline.space

i'm posting about building this in public for the next 90 days. next up: how scroll depth tracking works without storing anything personal.
接下来的 90 天里，我将公开分享这个项目的开发过程。下一篇预告：如何在不存储任何个人信息的情况下实现滚动深度追踪。
