# Leakage-Safe Machine Learning for Hydrogen Embrittlement Detection in 316L Stainless Steel: A Region-Held-Out Evaluation of Texture and Deep Features in SEM Micrographs
# 用于316L不锈钢氢脆检测的防泄漏机器学习：SEM显微照片中纹理与深度特征的区域留出评估

**Abstract:** Scanning electron microscopy (SEM) is routinely used to characterize the microstructural changes caused by hydrogen embrittlement (HE) in structural steels. Machine learning can automate this characterization, but models are often evaluated using image-level splits. When several images come from the same specimen region, such splits leak information between the training and test sets. 

**摘要：** 扫描电子显微镜（SEM）常用于表征结构钢中由氢脆（HE）引起的微观结构变化。机器学习可以实现这种表征的自动化，但模型通常使用图像级分割进行评估。当多张图像来自同一试样区域时，这种分割方式会导致训练集和测试集之间出现信息泄露。

Here, we propose a region-held-out protocol for classifying as-received (AR) and hydrogen-charged (H2) SEM micrographs of 316L stainless steel, based on Leave-One-Region-Out (LORO) cross-validation over 14 spatial regions (8 AR, 6 H2; 31 images). We compared six feature-classifier combinations built on local binary patterns (LBP), grey-level co-occurrence matrices (GLCM), self-supervised convolutional embeddings pretrained on 143 unlabeled SEM images, and a convolutional neural network (CNN). 

在此，我们提出了一种用于分类316L不锈钢原始态（AR）和充氢态（H2）SEM显微照片的区域留出协议，该协议基于对14个空间区域（8个AR，6个H2；共31张图像）进行的“留一区域法”（LORO）交叉验证。我们比较了六种特征-分类器组合，这些组合基于局部二值模式（LBP）、灰度共生矩阵（GLCM）、在143张未标记SEM图像上预训练的自监督卷积嵌入，以及卷积神经网络（CNN）。

The simplest texture approach, LBP with a support vector machine (LBP+SVM), performed best, achieving a balanced accuracy of 0.79, H2 recall of 0.69, and H2 precision of 0.82, outperforming every deep-learning and combined-feature model. A group-level permutation test (500 permutations sampled from the 3,003 possible region-to-label assignments) yielded p = 0.008, indicating that the result cannot be explained by a chance alignment of the region structure. 

最简单的纹理方法——LBP结合支持向量机（LBP+SVM）表现最佳，其平衡准确率为0.79，H2召回率为0.69，H2精确率为0.82，优于所有深度学习和组合特征模型。组级置换检验（从3,003种可能的区域到标签分配中抽样500次置换）得出的p值为0.008，表明该结果并非由区域结构的偶然对齐所致。

Grad-CAM maps from a CNN trained on the full dataset tended to concentrate on localized surface and grain-boundary features, where hydrogen-induced morphological changes are known to occur. Under a leakage-safe, statistically validated protocol, texture descriptors recover a hydrogen-charging signature from SEM micrographs even with few samples, and the same protocol can be extended to larger HE detection studies in other alloy systems.

在完整数据集上训练的CNN生成的Grad-CAM热力图倾向于集中在局部表面和晶界特征上，而这些区域正是已知会发生氢致形态变化的地方。在防泄漏且经过统计验证的协议下，即使样本量很少，纹理描述符也能从SEM显微照片中提取出充氢特征，且该协议可扩展至其他合金体系中更大规模的氢脆检测研究。
