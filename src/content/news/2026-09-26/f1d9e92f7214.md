# CFD Correction of Open Tip Clearance Flow in a Compressor Cascade Using VAE Latent Space Adaptation
# 基于VAE潜在空间自适应的压气机叶栅叶顶间隙流CFD修正

**Abstract:** CFD predictions of open tip clearance flow in compressor cascades are subject to discrepancies relative to experiments, while experimental observations are sparse and high-resolution experimental ground truth is unavailable. 

**摘要：** 压气机叶栅叶顶间隙流的计算流体力学（CFD）预测结果与实验数据之间存在偏差，而实验观测数据稀疏，且缺乏高分辨率的实验真值。

This study proposes a non-intrusive correction method based on a variational autoencoder (VAE) and latent-space adaptation. A VAE is first trained using a dataset of 166 parametrically sampled CFD total pressure loss fields to learn a low-dimensional statistical representation of these fields. 

本研究提出了一种基于变分自编码器（VAE）和潜在空间自适应的非侵入式修正方法。首先，利用包含166个参数化采样CFD总压损失场的数据集训练VAE，以学习这些流场的低维统计表示。

The VAE is then frozen, and a low-rank latent-space adapter is trained using only 12 paired CFD--experiment operating conditions. An observation operator maps the corrected high-resolution fields to the experimental observation space, allowing supervision to be applied only at the available measurement locations and within the measured pitchwise windows. 

随后，冻结VAE模型，并仅使用12组CFD与实验配对的工况数据来训练一个低秩潜在空间适配器。通过一个观测算子将修正后的高分辨率流场映射到实验观测空间，从而仅在可用的测量位置和测量的叶栅节距窗口内进行监督学习。

In the current 12-fold cross-validation, the mean absolute error decreases from 0.1335 to 0.0473, the root mean square error from 0.1717 to 0.0621, and the relative $L_2$ error from 0.5108 to 0.1871. 

在当前的12折交叉验证中，平均绝对误差从0.1335降低至0.0473，均方根误差从0.1717降低至0.0621，相对$L_2$误差从0.5108降低至0.1871。

These results indicate that the method improves agreement between CFD predictions and sparse experimental observations of open tip clearance flow without modifying the RANS solver or constructing artificial high-resolution experimental labels.

这些结果表明，该方法在无需修改雷诺平均纳维-斯托克斯（RANS）求解器或构建人工高分辨率实验标签的情况下，有效提升了CFD预测与稀疏实验观测数据在叶顶间隙流方面的一致性。
