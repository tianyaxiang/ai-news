# Phones don’t have lights
# 手机没有指示灯

Mark Zuckerberg has a new defense of the Ray-Ban Meta glasses: They’re actually doing more to signal they’re taking a photo than phones do. He’s brought this up in at least two recent interviews, noting that the glasses have a light that comes on to signal when a photo is being taken, while phones do not. In one of the interviews, Joanna Stern notes that, while phones do not always emit a light, someone does have to hold a phone up in the air, which is itself pretty noticeable.

马克·扎克伯格为 Ray-Ban Meta 智能眼镜提出了一个新的辩护理由：相比手机，它们在拍摄照片时其实提供了更明显的信号。他在最近的至少两次采访中都提到了这一点，指出这款眼镜在拍照时会亮起指示灯，而手机则不会。在其中一次采访中，乔安娜·斯特恩（Joanna Stern）指出，虽然手机并不总是会亮灯，但人们必须把手机举在空中拍摄，这本身就已经非常显眼了。

On today’s Vergecast, we’re talking about Meta Connect, the Muse AI assistant, all the new smart glasses hardware, and Zuckerberg’s response to the privacy uproar around wearable cameras. First, we spend time diving into Muse, which has quickly turned the vibes around on Meta’s progress in AI by making agentic features vastly more accessible than others have in the past. That ties right into Meta’s glasses problem and the question of whether people will trust Meta enough with their privacy to link all their services up to Muse.

在今天的《Vergecast》节目中，我们将讨论 Meta Connect 大会、Muse AI 助手、所有新款智能眼镜硬件，以及扎克伯格对围绕可穿戴摄像头的隐私争议所作出的回应。首先，我们深入探讨了 Muse，它通过让智能代理功能变得比以往任何时候都更容易使用，迅速扭转了外界对 Meta 在人工智能领域进展的看法。这直接关联到 Meta 的眼镜产品所面临的问题，以及人们是否足够信任 Meta 的隐私保护，从而愿意将他们所有的服务与 Muse 关联起来。

There’s also a ton of hardware to discuss. We have thoughts on the new audio-only Ray-Ban Meta glasses and the new Meta VR Glasses. Then it’s onto Googlebooks, which arrived this week with surprisingly refined hardware and compelling integrations between desktop and mobile. It’s going to be a big hurdle to convince people to switch from Windows, but the laptops are full of interesting enough ideas that we’re excited to check them out.

此外，还有大量的硬件产品需要讨论。我们分享了对新款纯音频 Ray-Ban Meta 眼镜和新款 Meta VR 眼镜的看法。接着是本周推出的 Googlebooks，它拥有令人惊喜的精致硬件，以及桌面端与移动端之间极具吸引力的整合功能。要说服用户从 Windows 转向 Googlebooks 将是一个巨大的挑战，但这些笔记本电脑充满了足够有趣的创意，让我们非常期待去体验它们。

Finally, everyone’s favorite podcast within a podcast returns after a brief hiatus. And then it’s on to the Lightning Round, where we’re discussing the Paramount settlement, YouTube’s embrace of AI tools, the state of Xbox, and Rode hooking its users up with 4K.

最后，在短暂的停播后，大家最喜爱的“播客中的播客”环节回归了。随后进入“闪电轮”环节，我们将讨论派拉蒙的和解协议、YouTube 对 AI 工具的拥抱、Xbox 的现状，以及 Rode 为其用户提供的 4K 支持。
