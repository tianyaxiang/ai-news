### From Hollywood to business venture
### 从好莱坞到商业投资

His acting career is really just part of the picture. Over the past two decades, Wahlberg has built a production company, a restaurant chain, apparel and fitness ventures, a slate of angel investments, and the now-25-year-old Mark Wahlberg Youth Foundation, which supports inner-city kids and teens. He’s used his Hollywood success to launch big projects well outside the movie business — and in a fireside chat at this year’s Disrupt, he’s going to give us a peek into how.

他的演艺生涯其实只是他人生版图的一部分。在过去的二十年里，沃尔伯格建立了一家制作公司、一个连锁餐厅、服装和健身企业、一系列天使投资项目，以及成立至今已有 25 年的“马克·沃尔伯格青年基金会”（Mark Wahlberg Youth Foundation），该基金会致力于支持城市贫困地区的儿童和青少年。他利用在好莱坞取得的成功，在电影行业之外开展了许多大型项目——在今年 Disrupt 的炉边谈话中，他将向我们揭示他是如何做到这一切的。

### Building an investing mindset
### 建立投资思维

Wahlberg, who’s leaned almost entirely on entrepreneurial instinct about people, culture, products, and markets, is now someone who’s been building institutional-level investing discipline, with Lee as his guide, and with a growing focus on healthcare and wellness startups. It’s a rare, unvarnished look at how someone with Wahlberg’s cultural intelligence has worked his way into some of the most sophisticated rooms in business and finance. He also discusses where high-profile entertainers and athletes sometimes get it wrong (Wahlberg is candid about having had to unlearn some of his own old instincts); how he’s used the trust built over a massive career to drive access and deal flow; and which technologies he’s most focused on right now and why.

沃尔伯格过去几乎完全依赖于对人、文化、产品和市场的创业直觉，而现在，在李（Lee）的指导下，他正在建立机构级的投资纪律，并日益关注医疗保健和健康领域的初创公司。这将是一次难得且坦诚的分享，展示像沃尔伯格这样具备敏锐文化洞察力的人，是如何一步步进入商业和金融界最顶尖圈层的。他还将讨论知名艺人和运动员有时会犯的错误（沃尔伯格坦言自己不得不摒弃一些旧有的直觉）；他如何利用在漫长职业生涯中建立的信任来获取资源和交易流；以及他目前最关注哪些技术，以及原因所在。
