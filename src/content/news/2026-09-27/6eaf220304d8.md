# 12 Best White Elephant Gifts, Plus a Prank Box to Put Them In (2026)
# 2026年最棒的12款“白象”交换礼物，外加一个恶搞包装盒

You Can’t Escape the white elephant parties. My friend who hosts an annual white elephant is debating sending out an invite before October even begins, and my mother’s ornament-themed white elephant has split into two parties this year. On top of all your regular Christmas and holiday shopping, trying to pick a gift that won’t embarrass you at multiple gift exchanges feels like mental torture.

你根本逃不掉“白象”（White Elephant）交换礼物派对。我那位每年举办白象派对的朋友，甚至在10月还没开始时就在考虑发送邀请函了；而我母亲举办的装饰品主题白象派对，今年更是拆分成了两场。除了常规的圣诞和节日购物外，还要在多场交换活动中挑选出一份不会让你丢脸的礼物，这简直是一种精神折磨。

That’s because white elephant parties in particular present a unique challenge: Can you find a cool or funny white elephant gift within a set budget that a group of people—who are potentially strangers—will battle each other to take home? None of us want to be the person who brought the gift nobody wanted. Some folks bring joke gifts, some bring booze to be safe, and some gifts will feel like garbage to you but gems to others. Some of my favorite white elephant gifts include a coffee-pot-shaped mug, a fun Lego set, and sushi-shaped magnets. Here’s every white elephant gift I recommend bringing, ranging from quirky to cool to actually useful.

这是因为白象派对带来了一个独特的挑战：你能在预算范围内找到一份既酷又有趣，且能让一群人（甚至可能是陌生人）争相抢夺的礼物吗？没人想成为那个带了没人想要的礼物的人。有些人带恶搞礼物，有些人为了保险带酒，有些礼物在你看来是垃圾，但在别人眼里却是宝贝。我最喜欢的一些白象礼物包括咖啡壶形状的马克杯、有趣的乐高套装和寿司形状的磁贴。以下是我推荐的所有白象礼物，从古怪的、酷炫的到真正实用的应有尽有。

Updated October 2026: I’ve added new Lego sets, a cozy beverage set, and new e-reader case options, and checked to make sure everything else is still in stock.

2026年10月更新：我增加了新的乐高套装、一套舒适的饮品用具以及新的电子阅读器保护壳选项，并核实了其他所有商品仍有库存。

### Nerdy Legos
### 极客乐高

Being a nerd isn’t as lame as it used to be, nor does it seem so weird to play Dungeons & Dragons like it used to, either. If you’ve got a few dungeon divers in your white elephant group (or maybe it’s a white elephant with your D&D party!) like I do, these Lego sets are a hit. My husband was the lucky last grabber for a white elephant gift that included three of these. They’re mystery bags, making it a fun discovery for the winner, and with 12 different designs you should be safe to buy a couple without doubling up on the model type. But even if you do, that’s something for the gift winner to discover and laugh about.

当个“极客”不再像过去那样被视为无趣，玩《龙与地下城》（D&D）也不再显得那么怪异。如果你的白象派对里也有像我这样喜欢地下城探险的朋友（或者这干脆就是你们D&D小队的派对！），这些乐高套装绝对大受欢迎。我丈夫曾是白象派对中最后一位幸运的抢夺者，他抢到的礼物里就包含三个这种盲袋。它们是神秘包装，对获胜者来说充满了发现的乐趣。共有12种不同设计，买几个也不太容易重复。即便真的重复了，那也是获胜者发现并开怀大笑的趣事。

### Cool E-Reader Cases
### 酷炫电子阅读器保护壳

Maybe you’re headed to your book club white elephant, or maybe you’ve talked everyone into getting an e-reader this year. Maybe that’s just me, though. Either way, if you know there’s people with e-readers coming to your gift exchange, a cool new case makes for an easy win. PopSockets has an entire Kindle case line that has a lot of really cool and gift-worthy designs. This dragon sterling case is one of my favorites, and the black opaque base allows Kindle colors to show through or it can dress up the plain black Kindle models. It also has a built-in MagSafe ring so the winner can attach their favorite PopSocket Grip to it for easier reading—or you can include one for them. This one’s my personal favorite.

也许你要去参加读书俱乐部的白象派对，或者你已经说服大家今年都买个电子阅读器（虽然可能只有我这么做）。无论如何，如果你知道参加交换礼物的人里有电子阅读器用户，一个酷炫的新保护壳绝对是稳赢的选择。PopSockets拥有完整的Kindle保护壳系列，有很多非常酷且适合送礼的设计。这款“龙之纯银”（Dragon Sterling）保护壳是我的最爱之一，其黑色不透明底座既能透出Kindle本身的颜色，也能为纯黑色的Kindle机型增色。它还内置了MagSafe磁吸环，获胜者可以安装他们最喜欢的PopSocket气囊支架以便阅读，或者你也可以顺便送他们一个。这是我个人的心头好。

If your group is more of a Kobo club, I also really love Kobo's SleepCover cases ($40) that can prop the e-reader up thanks to the foldable portion of the cover. You might want to include a gift receipt so they can exchange it if you get the wrong size—Kobo has SleepCovers for both Libra and Clara models, but PopSockets’ cases only fit the Kindle Paperwhite and Kindle Colorsoft.

如果你们的群体更偏向Kobo用户，我也非常喜欢Kobo的SleepCover保护壳（40美元），它可以通过折叠部分将阅读器支撑起来。你可能需要附上一张礼品收据，以便对方在尺寸不对时进行更换——Kobo为Libra和Clara机型都提供了SleepCover，但PopSockets的保护壳仅适用于Kindle Paperwhite和Kindle Colorsoft。

### A Fun Mug
### 有趣的马克杯

Everyone hydrates, so a drinking vessel is a safe gift all around. And most people drink some kind of caffeinated drink, whether it’s coffee or tea or perhaps an energy drink. This fun mug looks like a miniature coffee pot and can handle hot drinks. It even comes with a plastic lid so you can take it on the go. I like how comfortable the handle is to hold, and it’s just so funny to look at while still doing a great job holding my morning tea. The lid is pretty flimsy, so I usually just drink out of the glass top instead, but I still like that there is an included lid rather than none at all.

每个人都需要补水，所以水杯是一份绝对不会出错的礼物。大多数人都会喝含咖啡因的饮料，无论是咖啡、茶还是能量饮料。这个有趣的马克杯看起来像个迷你咖啡壶，而且可以盛放热饮。它甚至配有一个塑料盖，方便你随身携带。我喜欢它手柄的舒适握感，看着它就觉得很有趣，同时它装我的晨茶也表现得非常出色。盖子比较薄，所以我通常直接用杯口喝，但我依然觉得有盖子总比没有强。

### Tasty-Looking Magnets
### 看起来很美味的磁贴

Jazz up someone’s fridge with adorable sushi magnets. They almost look a little too real—the salmon nigiri looks ready to eat—but are still fun to look at on a fridge. Every single visitor I have comments on how cute these magnets are. After two years of rocking these on my fridge, they’re all holding up strong. While the magnets on the plastic rolls are small, they’re still plenty strong enough to hold up papers and children’s artwork on someone’s fridge without slipping or falling. I'd only put two corners of paper underneath one of these max since they’re not massively strong, and I usually put two magnets for each piece of art. I’m craving ikura just looking at them, and the foodies at your white elephant can battle it out for who gets to take these home and deal with the same cravings.

用可爱的寿司磁贴装饰一下冰箱吧。它们看起来简直太逼真了——那块三文鱼握寿司看起来就像能直接吃掉一样——但作为冰箱贴又非常有趣。我家的每一位访客都会夸这些磁贴很可爱。在我的冰箱上用了两年后，它们依然完好如初。虽然塑料卷上的磁铁很小，但它们足以在冰箱上固定纸张和孩子的画作，不会滑落。因为磁力不是特别强，我建议每张纸最多压住两个角，我通常每张画用两个磁贴。看着它们我就馋鲑鱼籽了，你们白象派对上的吃货们一定会为了把它们带回家而展开争夺，然后去面对同样的馋劲儿。

### A Punchable Key
### 可敲击的“回车键”

I’ll tell you right now: The Big Enter Supersized Key enter key is a little too much fun to have on my desk, and a great thing to bring for a white elephant with your coworkers, fellow work-from-home folks, or friends who are allowed to really decorate their cubicle. The punchable enter key’s function is simple: You punch it to hit enter. It connects to a computer via a USB cord, and is nice and responsive so you don’t have to punch it super hard. It’s a fun addition to lively Slack chats, or a secret way to be passive-aggressive without anyone knowing.

我得直接告诉你：这个超大号回车键（Big Enter Key）放在桌面上实在太好玩了。如果你要参加同事、居家办公伙伴或那些可以自由装饰工位的办公室朋友的白象派对，带上它绝对是个好主意。这个可敲击回车键的功能很简单：你敲它一下，就等于按下了回车键。它通过USB线连接电脑，反应灵敏，你不需要用力猛砸。它能为活跃的聊天氛围增添乐趣，也是一种不为人知的、隐晦表达不满的“秘密武器”。
