# TikTok agrees to pay at least $100M in Alabama settlement
# TikTok 同意支付至少 1 亿美元以达成阿拉巴马州和解协议

TikTok will pay Alabama at least $100 million in a settlement tied to allegations that the short-form video platform misled users about safety and was designed to addict children. The case was scheduled to go to trial on Monday.
TikTok 将向阿拉巴马州支付至少 1 亿美元，以就该短视频平台误导用户安全问题及被设计为让儿童上瘾的指控达成和解。该案件原定于周一开庭审理。

According to the Alabama attorney general’s office, TikTok could end up paying the state a total of $300 million, depending on “certain conditions.” The company also agreed to introduce a two-hour daily time limit for underage users, with additional parental controls, as well as restrictions on overnight usage and cosmetic filters.
据阿拉巴马州总检察长办公室称，根据“特定条件”，TikTok 最终可能向该州支付总计 3 亿美元。该公司还同意为未成年用户引入每日两小时的使用时间限制，并增加家长控制功能，同时对夜间使用和美颜滤镜进行限制。

In a statement, Alabama Attorney General Steve Marshall said that parents in his state can now “rest easier knowing real protections are in place to shield their children from the dangers of social media addiction.”
阿拉巴马州总检察长史蒂夫·马歇尔（Steve Marshall）在一份声明中表示，该州的家长们现在可以“更加安心，因为他们知道已有切实有效的保护措施，能让他们的孩子免受社交媒体成瘾的危害。”

TikTok also released a statement saying that the settlement “builds on our commitment and core objective to continually enhance our robust safety tools to protect teens.”
TikTok 也发表声明称，此次和解“建立在我们持续增强强大的安全工具以保护青少年的承诺和核心目标之上。”

In August, TikTok reached a $400 million settlement with the Department of Justice over allegations that the platform violated child privacy laws.
今年 8 月，TikTok 就其平台违反儿童隐私法的指控，与美国司法部达成了 4 亿美元的和解协议。
