# Game Development Budget Breakdown: Where Does the Money Go?
# 游戏开发预算解析：钱都花在哪儿了？

One of the most common questions we hear from clients is deceptively simple: "How much will my game cost?" The honest answer is always "it depends," but that does not mean we cannot give you a clear framework for understanding where the money goes and why.
我们从客户那里听到的最常见的问题之一，听起来简单，实则不然：“我的游戏要花多少钱？”诚实的回答永远是“视情况而定”，但这并不意味着我们不能为你提供一个清晰的框架，让你了解资金的去向及其原因。

Between David and Adam, our experience spans mobile, PC, educational games, legacy modernisation, and large live-game codebases. That gives us a useful planning framework for where budgets tend to go, even though the exact split changes substantially from project to project. This post breaks down the major cost categories in a typical game development budget, explains what drives spending in each one, and offers guidance on where to prioritise when funds are limited.
David 和我的经验涵盖了移动端、PC 端、教育类游戏、旧系统现代化改造以及大型在线游戏代码库。这为我们提供了一个有用的规划框架，可以预估预算的流向，尽管具体的分配比例会因项目而异。本文将拆解典型游戏开发预算中的主要成本类别，解释各项支出的驱动因素，并就资金有限时如何进行优先级排序提供建议。

### The Five Core Budget Categories
### 五大核心预算类别

For planning, we split game-development budgets into five core categories. The exact percentages shift with genre, scope, art requirements, platform targets, and team structure. The ranges below are heuristics based on the kinds of projects we scope, not universal industry benchmarks.
在规划时，我们将游戏开发预算分为五个核心类别。具体的百分比会随着游戏类型、规模、美术需求、目标平台和团队结构而变化。以下范围是基于我们所评估的项目类型得出的经验法则，而非通用的行业基准。

| Category | Typical Share | What It Covers |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **类别** | **典型占比** | **涵盖内容** |
| Engineering | 40-50% | Gameplay programming, systems architecture, networking, platform porting |
| 工程开发 | 40-50% | 游戏玩法编程、系统架构、网络、平台移植 |
| Art & Audio | 20-30% | 2D/3D art, animation, UI design, VFX, music, sound effects |
| 美术与音频 | 20-30% | 2D/3D 美术、动画、UI 设计、视觉特效 (VFX)、音乐、音效 |
| Design | 10-15% | Game design, level design, economy balancing, UX research |
| 设计 | 10-15% | 游戏设计、关卡设计、经济平衡、用户体验 (UX) 研究 |
| QA & Testing | 5-10% | Functional testing, device testing, performance profiling, regression |
| 质量保证与测试 | 5-10% | 功能测试、设备测试、性能分析、回归测试 |
| Project Management | 5-10% | Production, scheduling, client communication, tooling |
| 项目管理 | 5-10% | 制作管理、进度安排、客户沟通、工具开发 |

These numbers assume you are working with an external development partner or agency. If you are building in-house, the ratios still hold, but the absolute numbers will include salary overheads, equipment, and office costs that are baked into agency rates.
这些数字假设你正在与外部开发合作伙伴或代理机构合作。如果你是内部开发，比例依然适用，但绝对数值将包含薪资管理费用、设备和办公成本，而这些成本通常已包含在代理机构的报价中。

### Engineering: 40-50% of the Budget
### 工程开发：预算的 40-50%

Engineering is usually the largest cost on the projects we scope, and for good reason. Your engineers are not just writing gameplay code - they are building the foundational systems that everything else sits on top of.
在我们评估的项目中，工程开发通常是最大的成本支出，这不无道理。你的工程师不仅仅是在编写游戏逻辑代码，他们还在构建所有其他功能所依赖的基础系统。

**What Drives Engineering Costs**
**工程成本的驱动因素**

*   **Core systems architecture:** The skeleton of your game. This includes input handling, scene management, save systems, and the gameplay loop itself. A robust architecture pays dividends later; a fragile one creates exponential technical debt.
    **核心系统架构：** 游戏的骨架。这包括输入处理、场景管理、存档系统以及游戏循环本身。稳健的架构在后期会带来回报；而脆弱的架构则会产生指数级的技术债务。
*   **Networking and multiplayer:** If your game has any online component, expect engineering costs to increase significantly. Server-authoritative backends, state synchronisation, lag compensation, and matchmaking systems are complex and require specialist expertise.
    **网络与多人联机：** 如果你的游戏包含任何在线组件，工程成本预计会显著增加。服务器权威后端、状态同步、延迟补偿和匹配系统非常复杂，需要专业知识。
*   **Platform-specific work:** Shipping on iOS and Android is not simply "building once." Each platform has its own APIs, compliance requirements, input systems, and performance characteristics. Console porting adds another layer of complexity with certification requirements (TRC, XR, Lotcheck).
    **平台特定工作：** 在 iOS 和 Android 上发布不仅仅是“构建一次”。每个平台都有自己的 API、合规要求、输入系统和性能特征。主机移植则增加了认证要求（如 TRC、XR、Lotcheck），复杂程度更高。
*   **Performance optimisation:** Mobile devices have strict thermal, memory, and battery constraints. Even much smaller games need profiling and optimisation passes on real devices.
    **性能优化：** 移动设备在散热、内存和电池方面有严格限制。即使是规模较小的游戏，也需要在真实设备上进行性能分析和优化。
*   **Third-party integrations:** Analytics, ad mediation, in-app purchases, authentication, cloud save, and push notifications. Each SDK integration carries its own implementation and maintenance cost.
    **第三方集成：** 分析工具、广告聚合、应用内购买、身份验证、云存档和推送通知。每个 SDK 的集成都伴随着相应的实现和维护成本。

**Key Takeaway:** Do not underestimate engineering costs for "simple" games. A 2D puzzle game still needs save systems, monetisation integration, device testing, and store compliance. The visible gameplay is often the smallest part of the engineering workload.
**核心要点：** 不要低估“简单”游戏的工程成本。一款 2D 益智游戏仍然需要存档系统、变现集成、设备测试和商店合规性。可见的游戏玩法往往只是工程工作量中最小的一部分。

**Where to Save**
**如何节省成本**

*   Use proven frameworks and libraries rather than building everything from scratch.
    使用成熟的框架和库，而不是从零开始构建一切。
*   Prioritise your core loop. Get the one thing that makes your game fun working before investing in ancillary systems.
    优先考虑核心循环。在投入辅助系统之前，先确保让游戏变得有趣的那一个核心功能能够正常运行。
*   Choose your platforms carefully at launch. Shipping on one platform first and porting later is almost always cheaper than simultaneous multi-platform development.
    发布时谨慎选择平台。先在一个平台上发布，后续再进行移植，几乎总是比同时进行多平台开发更便宜。

### Art and Audio: 20-30% of the Budget
### 美术与音频：预算的 20-30%

Art is what players see first. It drives store page conversion, first impressions, and perceived quality. But it is also the category where scope creep is most dangerous.
美术是玩家第一眼看到的东西。它决定了商店页面的转化率、第一印象和感知质量。但这也是范围蔓延（Scope Creep）最危险的领域。

**What Drives Art Costs**
**美术成本的驱动因素**

*   **Visual fidelity:** A hyper-casual game with flat 2D art costs a fraction of a game with hand-painted environments and 3D character animations. The gap is enormous.
    **视觉保真度：** 一款采用扁平 2D 美术的超休闲游戏，其成本仅为拥有手绘环境和 3D 角色动画游戏的一小部分。两者差距巨大。
*   **Asset volume:** An open-world game needs hundreds or thousands of unique assets. A puzzle game might need dozens.
    **资源量：** 一款开放世界游戏需要成百上千个独特的资源，而益智游戏可能只需要几十个。
*   **Animation:** Character animation, UI animation, and VFX all require specialised skills. Skeletal animation systems reduce costs compared to frame-by-frame approaches, but still require skilled artists.
    **动画：** 角色动画、UI 动画和视觉特效都需要专业技能。与逐帧动画相比，骨骼动画系统可以降低成本，但仍需要熟练的美术师。
*   **Audio:** Music, sound effects, and voice acting. Often underbudgeted, audio is critical to game feel. A good sound designer can make a £10,000 game feel like a £100,000 game.
    **音频：** 音乐、音效和配音。音频往往被低估预算，但它对游戏质感至关重要。一位优秀的音效设计师能让一款 1 万英镑的游戏听起来像 10 万英镑的作品。
*   **UI/UX design:** Interface design for games is a specialised discipline, particularly for mobile. Touch targets, safe areas, adaptive layouts, and platform-specific conventions all matter.
    **UI/UX 设计：** 游戏界面设计是一门专门的学科，尤其是在移动端。触摸区域、安全区域、自适应布局和平台特定的规范都非常重要。

**Where to Save**
**如何节省成本**

*   Define your art style early and stick to it. Style changes mid-production are the single most expensive art decision.
    尽早确定美术风格并坚持下去。在制作过程中更改风格是美术成本中最昂贵的决策。
*   Use asset stores strategically for non-hero content (background foliage, generic sound effects), but invest in custom work for anything the player interacts with directly.
    战略性地使用资源商店来获取非核心内容（如背景植被、通用音效），但对于玩家直接交互的任何内容，应投入定制开发。
