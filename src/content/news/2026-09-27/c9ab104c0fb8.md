# This Browser Extension Lets You Snooze Open Tabs Until Later
# 这款浏览器插件让你能将打开的标签页“稍后提醒”

Tabs are the best. They're also the worst. Sometimes I end up drowning in browser tabs containing web pages I don't remember opening, and sometimes I have a few tabs that I want to close, but I can't, because I know I'll need them later.

标签页既是最好的工具，也是最糟糕的负担。有时我会被淹没在各种浏览器标签页中，里面全是些我不记得何时打开的网页；有时我有一些想关掉却又不敢关的标签页，因为我知道稍后还会用到它们。

Tuck is a completely free browser extension for all the major browsers that helps with this. You know how, in Gmail, you can “snooze” an email so that it's hidden from your inbox until later? This is like that, but for your browser tabs. Tuck can also automatically close idle tabs after a certain amount of time, saving them to a list so you can open them again when you need to.

Tuck 是一款适用于所有主流浏览器的完全免费插件，可以帮你解决这个问题。就像 Gmail 中可以将邮件“稍后提醒”（Snooze）以暂时从收件箱隐藏一样，这款插件的功能如出一辙，只不过对象换成了浏览器标签页。Tuck 还能在标签页闲置一段时间后自动将其关闭，并保存到列表中，以便你在需要时重新打开。

To get started, simply install the extension. It works in just about every browser: Chrome, Safari, Edge, Firefox, Brave, Arc, Vivaldi, and a few others. There is no need to sign up for an account.

想要开始使用，只需安装该插件即可。它几乎适用于所有浏览器：Chrome、Safari、Edge、Firefox、Brave、Arc、Vivaldi 等。无需注册账户。

After installing the extension, you'll see the icon—an owl wrapped in a blanket—in your browser bar. Click it and you'll see the snoozing options: Later today, Tomorrow, Weekend, and Next week.

安装插件后，你会看到浏览器栏中出现一个图标——一只裹着毯子的猫头鹰。点击它，你就能看到“稍后提醒”的选项：今天晚些时候、明天、周末和下周。

Click any of these options and the tab will close, ready to reopen later at the appointed time. If you prefer keyboard commands, the extension has shortcuts for Windows (Ctrl-Shift-1, 2, or 3) and macOS (Cmd-Shift-1, 2, or 3) that allow you to snooze tabs to reopen later today, tomorrow, or next week, respectively.

点击其中任何一个选项，标签页就会关闭，并会在设定的时间自动重新打开。如果你更喜欢使用键盘操作，该插件也提供了快捷键：Windows 用户可使用 Ctrl-Shift-1/2/3，macOS 用户可使用 Cmd-Shift-1/2/3，分别对应“今天晚些时候”、“明天”和“下周”重新打开。

There's also a text box, allowing you to type a time that you’d like the current tab to reopen. This text field uses natural language processing, meaning you can type something like "Monday at 2 pm" and the extension will figure out what you mean.

此外还有一个文本框，允许你输入希望当前标签页重新打开的具体时间。该文本框支持自然语言处理，这意味着你可以输入类似“周一下午 2 点”这样的指令，插件会自动识别你的意图。

The extension, by default, will automatically close any tabs you leave idle for 24 hours. These auto-closed tabs are collected in the extension, allowing you to find them, click them, and open them back up if you need to. You can change how long tabs need to sit idle before this happens—three hours, 12 hours, 24 hours, three days, and seven days are all options. You can also turn this auto-close functionality off, if you prefer.

默认情况下，该插件会自动关闭闲置 24 小时的标签页。这些被自动关闭的标签页会收集在插件中，方便你查找、点击并重新打开。你可以更改自动关闭前的闲置时长，选项包括 3 小时、12 小时、24 小时、3 天和 7 天。如果你愿意，也可以关闭此自动关闭功能。

Pinned tabs and tab groups will never auto-close, and you can prevent tabs from any given domain from closing—useful for making sure your Google Docs or Gmail never close.

固定标签页和标签组永远不会被自动关闭，你还可以设置禁止特定域名的标签页被关闭——这对于确保 Google Docs 或 Gmail 始终保持开启非常有用。

The snoozing and auto-closing features combined do a lot to curb your worst tab instincts. Try this extension out if you're tired of drowning in tabs you never actually get around to.

“稍后提醒”和“自动关闭”功能的结合，在很大程度上抑制了你胡乱堆积标签页的坏习惯。如果你厌倦了被那些永远处理不完的标签页淹没，不妨试试这款插件。

You don't need an account to use the extension, and it doesn't gather any information about you. Tuck can sync your tabs between devices, but it uses your browser's built-in syncing functions for this: "Chrome syncs with Chrome (via your Google account), Firefox with Firefox (via Firefox Sync), and Safari syncs across your Macs via iCloud," according to the changelog. This makes it possible to browse your tucked tabs on other devices, though you can't tuck a tab on one device and have it pop up on another. It's also worth noting that there's no mobile app, meaning you can only use the extension on your computer.

使用该插件无需账户，它也不会收集你的任何信息。Tuck 可以实现设备间的标签页同步，但它利用的是浏览器内置的同步功能：根据更新日志，Chrome 通过 Google 账户同步，Firefox 通过 Firefox Sync 同步，Safari 则通过 iCloud 在 Mac 设备间同步。这意味着你可以在其他设备上查看已“收纳”的标签页，但无法在设备 A 上收纳标签页后让它在设备 B 上自动弹出。此外值得注意的是，该插件没有移动端应用，只能在电脑上使用。

The extension won't solve your tab hoarding habits—only you can do that for yourself. But it can go a long way toward making things manageable.

这款插件无法根治你囤积标签页的习惯——这只能靠你自己。但它确实能让你的浏览器管理变得轻松许多。
