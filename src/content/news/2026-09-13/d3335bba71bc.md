# Where to preorder the iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max
# 如何预购 iPhone 18 Pro 和 Pro Max

The faster, better iPhones are almost here, and we’ve got all the details for preordering them.
更快、更出色的 iPhone 即将到来，我们为您整理了所有预购详情。

The iPhone 18 Pro and 18 Pro Max are almost here. Announced at Apple’s September 2026 “Sunrise and shine” event alongside the iPhone Duo and other new gear, the two upgraded phones feature the faster A20 Pro processor with more GPU power as well as more neural cores for AI-related tasks, plus some welcome improvements to the camera.
iPhone 18 Pro 和 18 Pro Max 即将上市。这两款升级版手机在苹果 2026 年 9 月的“Sunrise and shine”发布会上与 iPhone Duo 及其他新设备一同亮相。它们搭载了更快的 A20 Pro 处理器，具备更强的图形处理能力和更多用于人工智能任务的神经核心，同时在摄像头方面也进行了令人欣喜的改进。

The phones will arrive on Friday, September 18th, and preordering one is never a bad idea if you want to ensure you get the color and storage capacity configuration you’re seeking. Speaking of, preorders are officially open.
新机将于 9 月 18 日（周五）正式发售。如果您想确保买到心仪的颜色和存储容量配置，提前预购绝对是个明智之举。目前，预购通道已正式开启。

The 6.3-inch iPhone 18 Pro starts at $1,199 for the 256GB model, and goes all the way up to $2,399 for the version with 2TB of storage. The larger 6.9-inch iPhone 18 Pro Max starts at $1,299 for 256GB, with the 2TB model costing $2,499. Both phones are available in four colors: deep black, silver, glacier, and burgundy.
6.3 英寸的 iPhone 18 Pro 256GB 版本起售价为 1,199 美元，2TB 版本最高售价为 2,399 美元。更大的 6.9 英寸 iPhone 18 Pro Max 256GB 版本起售价为 1,299 美元，2TB 版本售价为 2,499 美元。两款手机均提供四种配色：深邃黑、银色、冰川色和勃艮第红。

At the time of publication, the only way to preorder an unlocked version of the Pro or Pro Max is directly from Apple. You have some options if you don’t want to pay the full price upfront. Financing through Apple is an option (36 monthly payments with zero percent APR), and the new phones are also eligible for the recently-announced Apple Upgrade program. You can also trade in your existing phone for up to $885 in credit towards the cost of the new phone, with carriers offering additional credits.
截至本文发布时，预购无锁版 Pro 或 Pro Max 的唯一途径是直接通过苹果官网。如果您不想一次性付清全款，还有其他选择：可以通过苹果提供的分期付款方案（36 期免息），新机也适用于近期公布的 Apple Upgrade 计划。此外，您还可以通过以旧换新获得最高 885 美元的抵扣额度，运营商也会提供额外的购机优惠。

The likes of Best Buy and Walmart only have the carrier-locked versions listed at the start of the preorder phase. Best Buy is offering the 18 Pro through Verizon or AT&T, along with the Pro Max tied to Verizon or AT&T. Walmart is offering the 18 Pro through Verizon, AT&T, and T-Mobile, as well as the Pro Max with T-Mobile, Verizon, or AT&T.
在预购初期，百思买 (Best Buy) 和沃尔玛 (Walmart) 等零售商仅提供运营商锁定版本。百思买提供绑定 Verizon 或 AT&T 的 18 Pro 和 Pro Max；沃尔玛则提供绑定 Verizon、AT&T 和 T-Mobile 的 18 Pro，以及绑定 T-Mobile、Verizon 或 AT&T 的 Pro Max。

AT&T will offer to cover the cost of an iPhone 18 Pro via billing credits paid back to you over 36 months if you trade in an iPhone 14 or newer. You’ll also need to sign up for the AT&T Extra plan, with slightly lower discounts for less expensive plans. You can find these options on Apple’s checkout page, with the option to pay the $99 difference between the Pro and Pro Max out of pocket.
如果您以旧换新（iPhone 14 或更新机型），AT&T 将通过 36 个月的账单抵扣方式为您承担 iPhone 18 Pro 的费用。您需要订阅 AT&T Extra 套餐，选择更低价套餐的折扣力度会稍小一些。您可以在苹果的结账页面找到这些选项，并可选择自付 99 美元的差价来升级到 Pro Max。

T-Mobile is offering the iPhone 18 Pro for free by way of bill credits when you transfer your number or trade in a qualifying phone on the Experience Beyond plan. The credits aren’t as generous as AT&T’s offer if you’re an existing customer already, with only the iPhone 16 and newer covering the cost of a new phone, but it’s very similar if you’re new to T-Mobile.
在 Experience Beyond 套餐下，如果您携号转网或以旧换新符合条件的手机，T-Mobile 将通过账单抵扣方式免费提供 iPhone 18 Pro。对于现有用户，其抵扣力度不如 AT&T（仅限 iPhone 16 及更新机型可抵扣全额），但对于 T-Mobile 新用户来说，优惠力度非常接近。

The Verizon deal is fairly straightforward. The Simplicity Pro plan, which comes to a total of $80 per month if you’re switching from another carrier, includes a free iPhone 18 Pro, with the option to upgrade your phone after one year. Like other carriers, the “free” phone is paid off to you via monthly bill credits, so you’ll need to stick with the carrier for three years to get the full value.
Verizon 的优惠方案则相当直接。Simplicity Pro 套餐（从其他运营商转网总价为每月 80 美元）包含一部免费的 iPhone 18 Pro，并提供一年后升级手机的选项。与其他运营商一样，这部“免费”手机是通过每月账单抵扣来支付的，因此您需要使用该运营商服务满三年才能获得全部优惠价值。
