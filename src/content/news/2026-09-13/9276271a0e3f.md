# Sam Altman says OpenAI going public in 2026 would be ‘ill-advised’
# 山姆·奥特曼称 OpenAI 在 2026 年上市是“不明智的”

OpenAI CEO Sam Altman confirmed that there would be no OpenAI IPO in 2026 during an interview with Fortune. Over the course of 45 minutes, Altman discussed a variety of subjects including the Hugging Face hacking incident, recursive self-improvement, and the possibility of building an AI that was beyond human control. On the latter, he said it was “absolutely” possible, but vowed to take actions to prevent that from happening, even if it meant pausing training, adding that “there are risks we should not be able to incur on behalf of humanity.”

OpenAI 首席执行官山姆·奥特曼（Sam Altman）在接受《财富》杂志采访时确认，OpenAI 不会在 2026 年进行首次公开募股（IPO）。在 45 分钟的访谈中，奥特曼讨论了多个议题，包括 Hugging Face 黑客事件、递归自我改进，以及构建超越人类控制的 AI 的可能性。关于后者，他表示这“绝对”是有可能的，但他承诺会采取行动防止这种情况发生，即使这意味着暂停训练。他补充道：“有些风险是我们不应该代表全人类去承担的。”

“We’re not rushing into an IPO. I actually think that, given everything happening with safety, this would be, right now would be an ill-advised moment to go public. And we don’t feel pressure on that. We’ve said for a long time, we’ll do it when we’re ready… I would say not 2026. We’ve got a lot of stuff to do.”

“我们并不急于 IPO。事实上，考虑到目前在安全方面发生的一切，我认为现在上市是一个不明智的时机。我们并没有感受到这方面的压力。我们早就说过，等我们准备好了就会去做……我想说不会是 2026 年。我们还有很多事情要做。”
