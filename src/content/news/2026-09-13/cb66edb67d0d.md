# 10 Best Standing Desks Worth Buying in 2026
# 2026年值得购买的10款最佳升降桌

Not every standing desk is worth the investment, but the ones that are can change the entire rhythm of your workday. Switching between sitting and standing not only encourages movement and better posture, but it also breaks up the long, sluggish stretches of time that come with desk work.
并非每一款升降桌都值得投资，但那些优秀的产品确实能改变你整个工作日的节奏。在坐姿和站姿之间切换，不仅能促进身体活动并改善姿势，还能打破办公时那种漫长而沉闷的疲劳感。

Our team has been testing desks at home for years, putting each one through several weeks of daily use. Mostly, that means standard desk work, but also tasks like building Lego sets and wrapping holiday gifts. These are the models that stand out. They’re durable, comfortable, and practical (some even come with techy upgrades), and they make it easy enough to make standing a small, everyday habit.
我们的团队多年来一直在家中测试各种办公桌，每一款都经过了数周的日常使用。这不仅包括标准的办公任务，还包括拼搭乐高积木和包装节日礼物等活动。以下是脱颖而出的几款型号。它们耐用、舒适且实用（有些甚至配备了科技升级），让你能轻松地将站立办公养成一种日常习惯。

### Best Standing Desk for Most: Vari ComfortEdge Standing Desk
### 大多数人的最佳选择：Vari ComfortEdge 升降桌

The Vari ComfortEdge sits right in the sweet spot at the intersection of value, style, and functionality. After testing a half-dozen other standing desks, the ComfortEdge made former WIRED reviewer Martin Cizmar an evangelist. This, in turn, led me to the same conclusions about Vari's desks. At its price range, it offers the greatest adjustability, the easiest assembly, and certainly the longest warranty: Vari certifies its desks for life.
Vari ComfortEdge 在性价比、外观设计和功能性之间找到了完美的平衡点。在测试了半打其他升降桌后，ComfortEdge 让前 WIRED 评测员 Martin Cizmar 成为了它的忠实拥趸。这也让我对 Vari 的办公桌得出了同样的结论。在同价位产品中，它提供了最出色的调节性、最简单的组装过程，以及无疑是最长的保修期：Vari 为其办公桌提供终身质保。

The desk's laminated wood top is easy to clean, and the “waterfall” sloped front edge allows you to comfortably rest your forearms for long periods while working and typing. Over eight hours, I can attest that this softer, sculpted edge makes a big difference. But also, there's the nearly incredible ease of assembly and disassembly, which Vari has made a hallmark since introducing their first standing desk conversion kits more than a decade ago.
这款桌子的层压木桌面易于清洁，“瀑布式”倾斜前缘让你在长时间工作和打字时，能舒适地支撑前臂。经过八小时的使用，我可以证明这种更柔软、经过塑形的边缘确实带来了巨大的舒适感。此外，它极其简单的拆装过程也是一大亮点，自十多年前推出首款升降桌转换套件以来，这已成为 Vari 的标志性特色。

### A Personal Favorite: Branch Four Leg Standing Desk
### 个人最爱：Branch 四腿升降桌

There's something about a classic four-legged desk that looks so darn nice. It's not obvious that it's a standing desk until you see the little control panel on the desktop. Branch has several wood grains you can choose from for its plywood top, which adds to this desk's elegance. Rounded corners eliminate sharp edges. Four motors inside the telescoping powder-coated steel and aluminum legs help this desk glide up and down relatively quickly, though they're a little louder than I expected.
经典的四腿桌设计看起来确实非常美观。除非你看到桌面上的小控制面板，否则很难看出它是一款升降桌。Branch 为其胶合板桌面提供了多种木纹选择，增添了桌子的优雅感。圆角设计消除了尖锐的边缘。伸缩式粉末涂层钢铝桌腿内藏四个电机，帮助桌子快速平稳地升降，尽管运行声音比我预期的要大一点。

I've been quite happy with this desk after several months of use. Assembly was easy—just be prepared to handle a lot of screws. The control panel is, thankfully, simple too. Press the up and down arrows to your preferred height, press the M button, then press 1, 2, or 3 to save that height. No complicated settings menu here!
经过几个月的使用，我对这款桌子非常满意。组装很容易，只是要做好处理大量螺丝的准备。值得庆幸的是，控制面板也很简单。按下上下箭头调整到你喜欢的高度，按下 M 键，然后按 1、2 或 3 即可保存高度。这里没有复杂的设置菜单！
