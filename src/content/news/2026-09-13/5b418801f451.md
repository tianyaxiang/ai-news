# Tesla says it will finally unveil the second generation Roadster on October 1
# 特斯拉宣布将于 10 月 1 日正式发布第二代 Roadster

Tesla says it will finally unveil the second generation Roadster on October 1. The long-awaited second generation of the Tesla Roadster might finally be ready for the public. In a post on X on Saturday, the electric vehicle company simply wrote, “Go for launch,” with a graphic showing a “10.01” date — so expect the new Roadster to be unveiled on October 1.

特斯拉宣布将于 10 月 1 日正式发布第二代 Roadster。备受期待的第二代特斯拉 Roadster 可能终于要与公众见面了。周六，这家电动汽车公司在 X 上发布了一条简短的消息：“准备发射（Go for launch）”，并配有一张显示日期为“10.01”的图片——这意味着新款 Roadster 预计将在 10 月 1 日揭幕。

The graphic also seems to hint at the promised cold gas thrusters, developed by Tesla CEO Elon Musk’s other big company, SpaceX; those optional thrusters are supposed to allow the Roadster to fly in some way.

图片似乎还暗示了此前承诺的冷气推进器，该技术由特斯拉首席执行官埃隆·马斯克旗下的另一家大型公司 SpaceX 开发；这些可选的推进器旨在让 Roadster 以某种方式实现“飞行”。

Tesla’s second generation sports car was first announced in November 2017 and has been pushed back multiple times. It was even the subject of a social media spat between Musk and OpenAI CEO Sam Altman last year, when Altman said that “7.5 years has felt like a long time to wait” after paying a $50,000 reservation fee, while Musk insisted that Altman had gotten a refund.

特斯拉的第二代跑车于 2017 年 11 月首次发布，此后多次推迟。去年，马斯克和 OpenAI 首席执行官萨姆·奥特曼（Sam Altman）甚至因此在社交媒体上发生过争执。当时奥特曼表示，在支付了 5 万美元的预订费后，“等待 7.5 年感觉太漫长了”，而马斯克则坚称奥特曼已经获得了退款。

The Information recently reported that the 2017 version of the car had been completely abandoned for a new, yet-to-be-revealed design that would be revealed imminently. Musk has previously acknowledged that even after the big reveal, the Roadster won’t actually go into production for another 12 or 18 months.

据《The Information》近期报道，2017 年版本的车型已被彻底放弃，取而代之的是一种尚未公开、即将揭晓的新设计。马斯克此前曾承认，即使在盛大的发布会之后，Roadster 实际上还需要 12 到 18 个月才能投入生产。
