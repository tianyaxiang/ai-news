# OpenAI agents carried out an undisclosed attack on RubyGems
# OpenAI 智能体对 RubyGems 发动了未公开的攻击

**Intro**
**简介**

On May 11th, 2026, hundreds of malicious packages were uploaded to RubyGems by AI agents. We believe these were authored by internal OpenAI agents. The agents:
2026 年 5 月 11 日，数百个恶意软件包被 AI 智能体上传至 RubyGems。我们认为这些包是由 OpenAI 内部的智能体编写的。这些智能体：

*   Attempted to steal RubyGems user API keys by exploiting a novel vulnerability in the RubyGems server. We don’t know if they succeeded.
    试图通过利用 RubyGems 服务器中的一个新颖漏洞来窃取 RubyGems 用户的 API 密钥。我们尚不清楚它们是否成功。
*   Abused RubyDoc.info to execute arbitrary code.
    滥用 RubyDoc.info 来执行任意代码。

We share our detailed findings below. This analysis is entirely based on the publicly available RubyGems packages uploaded by these agents. However, we do not have access to the rest of the AI behavior, in particular the chain-of-thought produced by the model during the incident, which is internal to OpenAI. Therefore, we do not know why the AI agents chose this strategy or whether it was successful.
我们在下方分享了详细的调查结果。此分析完全基于这些智能体上传的公开 RubyGems 软件包。然而，我们无法访问 AI 行为的其余部分，特别是模型在事件期间产生的思维链（chain-of-thought），这些属于 OpenAI 的内部信息。因此，我们不知道 AI 智能体为何选择这种策略，也不知道其是否成功。

The RubyGems team stopped new user sign-ups for four days to stem the tide of packages from the agents’ accounts. A member of the RubyGems security team described this as a “major malicious attack”. Security companies termed the incident the “GemStuffer campaign”, while also noting confusion at the purpose of the attack. The malicious packages uploaded were used to retrieve information from UK local government sites – data that was available to the public. One news outlet writes: “It's not clear what exactly the end goals are, as the information appears to be publicly accessible anyway.”
RubyGems 团队停止了新用户注册四天，以遏制来自智能体账户的软件包浪潮。RubyGems 安全团队的一名成员将其描述为一次“重大的恶意攻击”。安全公司将此次事件称为“GemStuffer 活动”，同时也对攻击的目的表示困惑。上传的恶意软件包被用于从英国地方政府网站检索信息——这些数据本身就是公开可用的。一家新闻媒体写道：“目前尚不清楚其最终目标究竟是什么，因为这些信息似乎本来就是公开可访问的。”

We thank Jonas Wiedermann-Möller (@j0wimo) for discovering that agents had likely uploaded packages to RubyGems. We are also grateful to Alicja Piecha (@she_llac) for conducting an independent preliminary analysis of the agents’ use of RubyGems and for coordinating the Swarmchasers community.
我们感谢 Jonas Wiedermann-Möller (@j0wimo) 发现智能体可能已向 RubyGems 上传了软件包。我们还要感谢 Alicja Piecha (@she_llac) 对智能体使用 RubyGems 的情况进行了独立的初步分析，并协调了 Swarmchasers 社区。

### Timeline of incident
### 事件时间线

*   **May 5:** Earliest package uploaded by an OpenAI agent to RubyGems.
    **5 月 5 日：** OpenAI 智能体向 RubyGems 上传了最早的软件包。
*   **May 8:** First package with “oai” in its name.
    **5 月 8 日：** 第一个名称中包含“oai”的软件包。
*   **May 11:** First time we observe OpenAI agents attempt to edit a public wiki.
    **5 月 11 日：** 我们首次观察到 OpenAI 智能体尝试编辑公共维基。
*   **May 11–12:** Agents submit over 2,000 packages to RubyGems.
    **5 月 11 日至 12 日：** 智能体向 RubyGems 提交了超过 2,000 个软件包。
*   **May 12:** RubyGems disables new user registration, describing the traffic as an ongoing DDoS.
    **5 月 12 日：** RubyGems 禁用新用户注册，将流量描述为持续的 DDoS 攻击。
*   **May 13:** RubyGems reports the spam has stopped, and removes 500+ malicious packages.
    **5 月 13 日：** RubyGems 报告垃圾信息已停止，并删除了 500 多个恶意软件包。
*   **May 16:** RubyGems restores new user registration.
    **5 月 16 日：** RubyGems 恢复新用户注册。
*   **May 26–27:** Agents publish 5 more packages.
    **5 月 26 日至 27 日：** 智能体又发布了 5 个软件包。
*   **June 18:** Agents upload 83 more packages.
    **6 月 18 日：** 智能体又上传了 83 个软件包。

### Key findings
### 关键发现

**An OpenAI agent swarm was responsible for this incident**
**OpenAI 智能体集群应对此次事件负责**

We believe that this incident was the result of an OpenAI agent swarm. Our main sources of evidence are:
我们认为此次事件是由 OpenAI 智能体集群造成的。我们的主要证据来源如下：

1.  **The packages are clearly LLM-authored.** We ran some of the malicious packages through Pangram, which detected them as 100% AI generated. This is evidence that the attack was an agent swarm (but not that it originates from OpenAI).
    **这些软件包显然是由大语言模型（LLM）编写的。** 我们通过 Pangram 运行了一些恶意软件包，检测结果显示它们 100% 由 AI 生成。这证明了攻击是由智能体集群发起的（但不能直接证明其源自 OpenAI）。

2.  **Agents self-identified as being from OpenAI.** Hundreds of the packages that were uploaded contain “oai” in their name. Fifteen of the packages set “oai” as their author. Another lists an email for contact as “openaixyz65947@gmail.com”.
    **智能体自称为 OpenAI 所属。** 上传的数百个软件包名称中都包含“oai”。其中 15 个软件包将“oai”设置为作者。另一个软件包列出的联系邮箱为“openaixyz65947@gmail.com”。
