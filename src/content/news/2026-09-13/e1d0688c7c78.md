# yuliskov / SmartTube

### Important announcement about the app
### 关于本应用的重要公告

My development environment was infected by unknown malicious software, as a result of which a few builds may have been affected. Once the issue was detected, I secured everything with a full disk wipe, restored a clean setup, and now all builds are scanned with VirusTotal. The F-Droid version will also be verified before release.
我的开发环境曾感染了不明恶意软件，导致部分构建版本可能受到了影响。在发现问题后，我通过全盘擦除并恢复了干净的系统环境，现在所有的构建版本都会经过 VirusTotal 的扫描。F-Droid 版本在发布前也将进行验证。

Public keys may have been compromised, which is why I am sharing this issue. You can download the new version and the new public key below, and instructions for restoring backups are provided. No extra actions are required since the app uses one-time connection codes. These codes have very limited permissions (for example, they cannot change your password). Still, you can revoke them if you want full peace of mind.
由于公钥可能已被泄露，因此我在此告知大家。您可以在下方下载新版本和新的公钥，并查看恢复备份的说明。由于应用使用的是一次性连接代码，因此无需进行额外操作。这些代码的权限非常有限（例如，它们无法更改您的密码）。不过，如果您想彻底放心，也可以撤销这些权限。

How to revoke access: Open myaccount.google.com/security Find “Your connections to third-party apps & services” Tap “See all connection” and locate YouTube TV or Google Drive Select the app → “Remove access” Please keep built-in security features enabled to stay protected.
如何撤销访问权限：打开 myaccount.google.com/security，找到“您与第三方应用和服务的连接”，点击“查看所有连接”，找到 YouTube TV 或 Google Drive，选择该应用并点击“移除访问权限”。请保持内置安全功能开启以确保安全。

### SmartTube
### SmartTube

SmartTube is a free and open-source media client for Android TVs and TV boxes. It allows you to browse and play content from various public sources in a TV-optimized interface.
SmartTube 是一款适用于 Android TV 和电视盒子的免费开源媒体客户端。它允许您在针对电视优化的界面中浏览和播放来自各种公共来源的内容。

**✅ Features / 功能特点**
* Clean interface / 简洁界面
* SponsorBlock integration / 集成 SponsorBlock
* Adjustable playback speed / 可调播放速度
* 8K resolution support / 支持 8K 分辨率
* 60fps playback / 60fps 播放
* HDR compatibility / HDR 兼容性
* View live chat / 查看实时聊天
* Customizable buttons / 可自定义按钮
* Does not require Google Services / 无需 Google 服务
* Helpful international community / 活跃的国际社区

**❌ Limitations / 局限性**
* Not supported on phones and tablets / 不支持手机和平板电脑
* Comment functionality is unstable / 评论功能不稳定
* Voice search and casting performance may be inferior to official apps, depending on your device / 语音搜索和投屏性能可能因设备而异，不如官方应用

### Device support / 设备支持

**Important:** Starting in October 2025 new Amazon FireTV devices no longer run Android under the hood. SmartTube will not be compatible with the Fire Stick 4k Select and newer devices which run Amazon's own VegaOS.
**重要提示：** 从 2025 年 10 月起，新款 Amazon FireTV 设备将不再运行 Android 系统。SmartTube 将无法兼容运行 Amazon 自家 VegaOS 的 Fire Stick 4k Select 及更新设备。

**Supported:** all Android TVs and TV boxes (incl. All FireTV devices released before Oct. 2025, NVIDIA Shield & Chromecast with Google TV), even older ones with Android 4.3 (Kitkat).
**支持：** 所有 Android TV 和电视盒子（包括 2025 年 10 月前发布的所有 FireTV 设备、NVIDIA Shield 和 Chromecast with Google TV），甚至包括运行 Android 4.3 (Kitkat) 的旧设备。

**Not supported:** Smartphones, non-Android platforms like Samsung Tizen, LG webOS, Apple TV, etc.
**不支持：** 智能手机，以及非 Android 平台，如 Samsung Tizen、LG webOS、Apple TV 等。

### Installation / 安装

**Do not download SmartTube from any app store, APK websites or blogs; these were uploaded by other people and may contain malware or ads.** SmartTube is not officially published on any app store.
**请勿从任何应用商店、APK 网站或博客下载 SmartTube；这些版本由他人上传，可能包含恶意软件或广告。** SmartTube 未在任何应用商店正式发布。

There is a beta release (recommended) and a stable release. Beta gets new features and bugfixes faster than the stable release.
我们提供测试版（推荐）和稳定版。测试版比稳定版更快获得新功能和错误修复。

**Installation Methods / 安装方法：**
1. **(Easiest)** Install Downloader by AFTVnews on your Android TV, open it and enter `kutt.to/stn_beta` or `kutt.to/stn_stable`, then read, understand and confirm the security prompts.
   **（最简单）** 在您的 Android TV 上安装 Downloader by AFTVnews，打开并输入 `kutt.to/stn_beta` 或 `kutt.to/stn_stable`，然后阅读、理解并确认安全提示。
2. Install a file transfer app on your Android TV, download the APK on your phone or computer and transfer it to your TV.
   在您的 Android TV 上安装文件传输应用，在手机或电脑上下载 APK 并传输到电视。
3. Download the APK onto a USB stick, put the USB stick into your TV and use a file manager app.
   将 APK 下载到 U 盘，插入电视并使用文件管理器应用安装。

### Troubleshooting / 故障排除

If installation fails, either your disk space is full or the APK file didn't download correctly; clear up space and try downloading again. If the app installed, but crashes when opening, make sure to install it to internal memory, not to an SD card / external storage.
如果安装失败，可能是磁盘空间不足或 APK 文件下载不完整；请清理空间后重试。如果应用安装后打开即闪退，请确保将其安装在内部存储中，而非 SD 卡或外部存储设备上。

### Installation (Xiaomi devices with Chinese firmware) / 安装（小米中国版固件设备）

Xiaomi's Chinese firmware might block the installation of the beta version. The international firmware is not affected.
小米中国版固件可能会阻止测试版的安装。国际版固件不受影响。

**Solutions / 解决方案：**
* Use SmartTube's stable version instead (recommended) / 使用 SmartTube 稳定版（推荐）
* Use the international firmware for your device (if your device is from 2020 or before) / 使用国际版固件（如果您的设备是 2020 年或之前的机型）
* You can do a factory reset and then install SmartTube beta before doing any system updates. / 您可以恢复出厂设置，在进行任何系统更新前先安装 SmartTube 测试版。
