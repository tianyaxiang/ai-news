# The Best Label Makers (2026): Brother, Niimbot, Dymo
# 2026年最佳标签打印机：Brother、Niimbot、Dymo

There’s something oddly satisfying about a perfectly labeled spice rack or a pantry with bins that specify exactly what they contain—even if it’s plain as day that the “pasta” bin contains boxes of dried penne. Taking a label maker to the most clutter-laden corners of my home helps me feel organized. And frankly, feeling organized externally often helps me feel organized internally, too.

看着调料架或储物柜上贴着完美的标签，明确标示出里面装的是什么，总有一种莫名的满足感——哪怕“意大利面”盒子里装的是干通心粉，这显而易见。用标签打印机整理家中杂乱的角落能让我感到井井有条。坦白说，外部环境的整洁往往也能帮助我获得内心的秩序感。

With that in mind, I researched, selected, and tested some of the top home label makers to see how intuitive different devices were to use and how well their labels stuck to my kids’ dresser drawers, toy bins, water bottles, and lunchboxes. Read on for my thoughts about the best machines for various needs. Just beware that household organizing with a label printer can get addicting and lead to joy-inducing clean spaces.

考虑到这一点，我研究、挑选并测试了一些顶级的家用标签打印机，以观察不同设备的使用直观程度，以及它们的标签在孩子梳妆台抽屉、玩具箱、水壶和午餐盒上的粘贴效果。请继续阅读我对不同需求下最佳机型的看法。但请注意，用标签打印机进行居家整理可能会让人上瘾，并带来令人愉悦的整洁空间。

### Best Overall: Brother P-touch PT-n25bt
### 最佳综合奖：Brother P-touch PT-n25bt

There’s not a whole bunch more the average person could want in a handheld label maker other than what the PT-n25bt offers, particularly at such an approachable price. With this device, even a label-making newbie should have no issue making a custom tag in less than a minute. After clicking the tape cartridge into place, simply close the back of the machine, power it on, and type directly onto the standard QWERTY keyboard. Press print, and then use the on-device cutting mechanism to get a clean, straight edge every time.

对于普通用户来说，PT-n25bt 提供的功能已经绰绰有余，尤其是考虑到它如此亲民的价格。有了这款设备，即使是标签制作新手，也能在不到一分钟的时间内轻松制作出自定义标签。将色带盒卡入到位后，只需合上机器后盖，开机，然后直接在标准的 QWERTY 键盘上输入内容即可。按下打印键，再使用机身上的切割装置，每次都能获得整齐平直的边缘。

If you’re not in a huge rush, you can putter around with three font options, seven font styles (think: bold, underline, italic), 15 text frames, and hundreds of symbols. The LCD screen lets you preview any edits you make to your label, minimizing unintended surprises you get when it, say, spits out a thick border design you really hate.

如果你不赶时间，还可以尝试三种字体选项、七种字体样式（如：粗体、下划线、斜体）、15 种文本边框和数百种符号。LCD 屏幕让你能够预览对标签所做的任何编辑，从而减少意外情况，比如打印出你非常讨厌的粗边框设计。

For even more design flexibility, you can opt for the Brother Design&Print 2 companion app that’s available on iOS and Android devices. It connects via Bluetooth and provides dozens of templates geared toward specific purposes, like gift tags, file folders, or food containers. You can also design your own custom label on the app without using a template.

为了获得更高的设计灵活性，你可以选择适用于 iOS 和 Android 设备的 Brother Design&Print 2 配套应用程序。它通过蓝牙连接，提供数十种针对特定用途的模板，如礼品标签、文件夹或食品容器。你也可以在不使用模板的情况下，在应用程序上设计自己的自定义标签。

### Best Upgrade: Brother P-touch Cube Plus (PT-P710bt)
### 最佳升级奖：Brother P-touch Cube Plus (PT-P710bt)

If what you value in a label maker is a result that looks polished, professional, and decidedly not homemade, the P-touch Cube Plus is calling your name. It allows for great versatility in its labels, printing on tape as narrow as 6 mm and as wide as 24 mm, meaning you can use it to organize the small jars on your spice rack and large boxes in your storage unit.

如果你看重的是标签打印机呈现出的精致、专业且绝非“手工感”的效果，那么 P-touch Cube Plus 就是你的不二之选。它在标签使用上具有极高的灵活性，支持打印 6 毫米到 24 毫米宽的色带，这意味着你可以用它来整理调料架上的小罐子，也能整理储物间里的大箱子。

The Cube Plus also supports a huge selection of designs, with hundreds of fonts, borders, and graphics, available via your choice of three design apps and platforms accessible via Bluetooth connection: Brother Design&Print 2 (also compatible with the Brother PT-n25bt), iPrint&Label, and P-touch Editor software that you can use with a desktop.

Cube Plus 还支持海量的设计选择，包括数百种字体、边框和图形，你可以通过蓝牙连接选择三款设计应用程序和平台进行操作：Brother Design&Print 2（也兼容 Brother PT-n25bt）、iPrint&Label，以及可用于台式机的 P-touch Editor 软件。
