Proponents of autonomous vehicle technology have long argued that robotaxis and other self-driving vehicles will reduce crash incidents and make roads safer. And there is some evidence of that. But that doesn’t mean there hasn’t been a cost to those who are working (or have worked) for the companies developing that tech.
自动驾驶技术的支持者长期以来一直认为，自动驾驶出租车和其他自动驾驶车辆将减少事故发生，使道路更安全。确实有一些证据支持这一点。但这并不意味着那些为开发该技术的公司工作（或曾经工作）的人员没有付出代价。

Sean O’Kane, senior reporter, special projects, dug into data submitted to the Occupational Safety and Health Administration and found that test drivers for Waymo and Zoox sustained more than two dozen injuries in 2024 and 2025 from hard braking or other sudden movements made by the autonomous vehicles. In some cases, these workers were sidelined for months after sustaining injuries like whiplash when the AVs stopped abruptly, and hard. Check out his full story, which includes interviews with former and current workers.
特别项目高级记者 Sean O’Kane 深入研究了提交给职业安全与健康管理局（OSHA）的数据，发现 Waymo 和 Zoox 的测试驾驶员在 2024 年和 2025 年因自动驾驶车辆的急刹车或其他突然动作而遭受了超过二十多次伤害。在某些情况下，这些员工在自动驾驶车辆突然急停导致颈部扭伤等伤害后，被迫休假数月。请查看他的完整报道，其中包含了对前任和现任员工的采访。

There is one important item to consider. You might ask yourself, well what about the other AV developers? Surely, this isn’t a problem isolated to Waymo and Zoox? And you’re probably right. It’s likely that test drivers for other AV developers are also getting injured, but the companies they work for may be exempt from OSHA’s reporting requirements.
有一点很重要。你可能会问，那其他自动驾驶开发商呢？这肯定不是 Waymo 和 Zoox 独有的问题吧？你很可能是对的。其他自动驾驶开发商的测试驾驶员很可能也在受伤，但他们所工作的公司可能豁免于 OSHA 的报告要求。

### Deals! / 交易动态！

I’ve been writing about Gatik, the autonomous vehicle startup known for its self-driving box trucks, since 2019 when it came out of “stealth.” At the time, I wasn’t sure if the startup would survive. The hype cycle was already chewing up and spitting out AV startups and no one seemed close to commercializing their tech.
自 2019 年 Gatik 走出“隐身模式”以来，我一直在报道这家以自动驾驶厢式货车闻名的初创公司。当时，我不确定这家初创公司能否生存下来。当时的炒作周期已经让许多自动驾驶初创公司折戟沉沙，似乎没有哪家公司接近商业化。

Gatik has not only survived, but it has also crossed over from R&D lab to commercial operator, albeit at a small-scale compared with traditional human-driven delivery companies. (The company’s box trucks are driverless and deliver freight from distribution centers to retails stores like Walmart.) And now, with $200 million in fresh funding, it’s pushing to scale. The round was led by Qatar Investment Authority and Koch Disruptive Technologies, with participation from Millennium Management, ARK Invest, and Intact Private Capital. This is Gatik’s largest funding to date.
Gatik 不仅生存了下来，还从研发实验室转型为商业运营商，尽管与传统的人工驾驶物流公司相比规模较小。（该公司的厢式货车是无人驾驶的，负责将货物从配送中心运送到沃尔玛等零售店。）现在，随着 2 亿美元的新融资，它正在推动规模化扩张。本轮融资由卡塔尔投资局和 Koch Disruptive Technologies 领投，Millennium Management、ARK Invest 和 Intact Private Capital 跟投。这是 Gatik 迄今为止最大的一笔融资。

But I might argue that its multiyear commercial agreement with PepsiCo, which was signed in June and is part of $600 million in contracted revenue, is the bigger deal here.
但我认为，它与百事公司签署的多年期商业协议（于 6 月签署，是 6 亿美元合同收入的一部分）才是这里更重要的交易。
