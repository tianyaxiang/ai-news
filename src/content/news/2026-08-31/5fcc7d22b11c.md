# Enormous 12TB Steam leak includes abandoned Half-Life 2: Episode 3 assets
# 12TB Steam 数据泄露事件曝光，包含《半衰期 2：第三章》废弃素材

Over 12 terabytes of data, containing builds of every game uploaded to Steam between 2003 and 2013, has been leaked. We don’t know everything in the archives yet because of its massive size. But people have already dug up assets related to the canceled Half-Life 2: Episode 3, content cut from Portal 2, and a build of a game called F-Stop that’s set in the Portal universe but is built around a camera mechanic.

超过 12TB 的数据遭到泄露，其中包含了 2003 年至 2013 年间上传至 Steam 的所有游戏版本。由于数据量极其庞大，我们目前尚无法完全知晓档案中的所有内容。但人们已经挖掘出与已取消项目《半衰期 2：第三章》（Half-Life 2: Episode 3）相关的素材、《传送门 2》（Portal 2）的删减内容，以及一款名为《F-Stop》的游戏版本——该作设定在《传送门》宇宙中，但核心玩法围绕相机机制展开。

The leak also includes titles from third-party developers. Early versions of Call of Duty and Resident Evil installments have been dug up, as have builds of Mirror’s Edge, Batman: Arkham Asylum, and Dragon Age: Origins. And more is likely to come to light as people dig through the trove.

此次泄露还涵盖了第三方开发商的游戏。目前已挖掘出《使命召唤》（Call of Duty）和《生化危机》（Resident Evil）系列早期版本，以及《镜之边缘》（Mirror’s Edge）、《蝙蝠侠：阿卡姆疯人院》（Batman: Arkham Asylum）和《龙腾世纪：起源》（Dragon Age: Origins）的游戏构建版本。随着人们对这批宝库的深入挖掘，未来可能会有更多内容浮出水面。

But arguably the most interesting discoveries are related to Valve’s Portal 2. Sleuths have dug up deleted dialog that completely changes how you’ll look at Companion Cubes, alternative intro and GlaDOS sequences, as well as a version of the Weaponizer intended for Half-Life 2: Episode 3.

但可以说，最有趣的发现与 Valve 的《传送门 2》有关。调查人员挖掘出了被删除的对话，这些内容彻底改变了玩家对“同伴方块”（Companion Cubes）的看法；此外还发现了替代性的开场动画、GlaDOS 序列，以及一个原计划用于《半衰期 2：第三章》的“武器化工具”（Weaponizer）版本。
