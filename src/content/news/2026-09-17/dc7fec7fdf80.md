# Claude Cowork and chat are now one Claude
# Claude Cowork 与聊天功能现已合二为一

Claude Cowork and chat are now one Claude. You don’t have to choose where a task goes. Claude does more of the work.
Claude Cowork 与聊天功能现已合二为一。你无需再纠结任务该放在哪里，Claude 将承担更多工作。

Starting today, Claude Cowork and chat are merging into one Claude. Bring a quick question, or hand over a report due at noon, and Claude takes it from there, even after you’ve closed your laptop. It’s rolling out on Pro and Max plans over the next few weeks, with more plans to follow.
从今天起，Claude Cowork 与聊天功能正式合并为一个 Claude。无论是提出一个简单的问题，还是交办一份中午截止的报告，Claude 都能接手处理，即使在你合上笔记本电脑后，它依然在工作。该功能将在未来几周内向 Pro 和 Max 计划用户推出，后续将覆盖更多订阅计划。

What Claude makes doesn’t need its own place either. Claude Docs and Claude Slides are new today, and Claude Design now works inside your conversations too. Ask for a document, and you and Claude write it together. Ask for a presentation, and Claude drafts the slides. You can edit directly, present straight from Claude, or download as PowerPoint or PDF. All three are in beta on paid plans, and Enterprise admins choose when to turn them on. If you use Claude Design on its own, it keeps working as before.
Claude 生成的内容也不再需要单独存放。今天我们推出了 Claude Docs 和 Claude Slides，Claude Design 现在也可以直接在对话中使用。要求撰写文档，你和 Claude 就能共同完成；要求制作演示文稿，Claude 会为你起草幻灯片。你可以直接进行编辑、通过 Claude 直接演示，或将其下载为 PowerPoint 或 PDF 文件。这三项功能目前在付费计划中处于测试阶段，企业管理员可自行决定启用时间。如果你单独使用 Claude Design，它将保持原有的工作方式。

We built Cowork as a separate place for bigger work, and Design for visual work. People used both, and told us the frustrating part was deciding where a task belonged. What they’d started in one also didn’t carry into the other. So we stopped making you choose. Claude can now figure out what a task needs, so what Cowork and Design can do is available from any conversation, with the context, skills, and connectors you already have.
我们最初将 Cowork 设计为处理大型任务的独立空间，将 Design 设计为处理视觉工作的空间。用户在使用两者时反馈称，最令人沮丧的是需要判断任务该归于何处，且在一个空间开始的工作无法同步到另一个空间。因此，我们不再让你做选择。Claude 现在能够识别任务需求，因此 Cowork 和 Design 的功能在任何对话中均可使用，并能调用你现有的上下文、技能和连接器。

“I could have Claude pull up [my legal research database], and it would pull all the cases, read them, figure out which other cases I might need, download them, and store them in a folder for my personal review.” - Andrew Keller, Senior Economist
“我可以让 Claude 调用[我的法律研究数据库]，它会提取所有案例、阅读它们、找出我可能需要的其他案例、下载并存入文件夹，供我个人审阅。”——高级经济学家 Andrew Keller

What it looks like: A weekly report is due at noon. Before you head out, you ask Claude what moved in the pipeline last week, then add: “Write it the way we always do, flag anything that slipped, and put the highlights in five slides for the leadership meeting.” If something’s unclear, Claude asks. You can check progress from your phone on the way to the office. By the time you’re at your desk, the report is waiting as a doc with notes from your teammate, and the slides are ready to open, adjust, and download as PowerPoint. Both came out of the same conversation, so the slides already match the report. You fix a line yourself, leave a comment for Claude on a slide, and share it. Schedule it for every Monday, and Claude starts on the report without being asked.
实际应用场景：一份周报在中午截止。在你出发前，你询问 Claude 上周工作流程的进展，然后补充道：“按我们一贯的风格撰写，标记出任何延误的项目，并将重点整理成五页幻灯片用于领导层会议。”如果有不明确的地方，Claude 会主动询问。你在去办公室的路上可以通过手机查看进度。当你坐到办公桌前时，报告已作为文档准备好，其中还包含同事的备注，幻灯片也已就绪，可直接打开、调整并下载为 PowerPoint。两者均来自同一对话，因此幻灯片内容与报告完全匹配。你可以自行修改一行文字，在幻灯片上给 Claude 留言，然后分享出去。将其设置为每周一自动执行，Claude 就会在无需提醒的情况下开始撰写报告。

Anything you make with Claude Design, Slides, or Docs lives at one shareable link you can open on your phone. You can select an element and move it, or tell Claude what you want changed.
任何通过 Claude Design、Slides 或 Docs 创建的内容都将保存在一个可共享的链接中，你可以在手机上打开。你可以选中某个元素并移动它，或者告诉 Claude 你想要进行的修改。

You can choose how Claude checks in with you. By default, Claude asks before taking an action. If you’d rather let it keep working and check in only when something needs a closer look, you can turn that on. You keep the final say.
你可以选择 Claude 与你沟通的方式。默认情况下，Claude 在采取行动前会先询问。如果你希望它持续工作，仅在需要仔细检查时才与你沟通，你可以开启该模式。最终决定权始终在你手中。

Getting started: If you mostly use chat, you don’t have to do anything different. When you want to hand over something bigger, try the next report or deck you’d normally build yourself. If you’ve been working in Cowork, everything is where you left it: your chats, projects, artifacts, connectors, and skills. When you open the app, pick up where you left off.
入门指南：如果你主要使用聊天功能，无需进行任何改变。当你想要交办更大型的任务时，尝试将下一份报告或演示文稿交给它处理。如果你一直在使用 Cowork，所有内容都将保留在原处：你的聊天记录、项目、工件（Artifacts）、连接器和技能。打开应用，即可从上次中断的地方继续工作。

This is rolling out to Pro and Max plans first, in the Claude app on web, desktop, and mobile over the coming weeks to existing and new users on these plans. There’s nothing to turn on. Team and Free plans will follow soon, and Enterprise admins will hear from us at least 30 days before anything changes for their organizations. The full list of capabilities is in the Help Center. If you’ve been saving up a big messy project, now’s the time.
该功能将在未来几周内首先向 Pro 和 Max 计划的现有及新用户推出，覆盖网页版、桌面版和移动版 Claude 应用。无需手动开启。Team 和 Free 计划用户也将很快获得支持，企业管理员将在其组织功能发生变动前至少 30 天收到我们的通知。完整的功能列表可在帮助中心查看。如果你一直积压着一个复杂的大项目，现在正是处理它的好时机。
