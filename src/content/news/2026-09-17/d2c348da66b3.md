# DenseFace: Bias Mitigation in Face Recognition via Density-Aware Probabilistic Matching
# DenseFace：通过密度感知概率匹配缓解人脸识别中的偏见

**Abstract:** Despite steady progress in face recognition, current face recognition models still suffer from significant demographic biases. While approaches for bias mitigation have been proposed, existing methods often impose constraints on the training procedure and result in the degradation of recognition accuracy.
**摘要：** 尽管人脸识别技术取得了稳步进展，但当前的人脸识别模型仍存在显著的人口统计学偏见。虽然已有多种缓解偏见的方法被提出，但现有方法往往会对训练过程施加限制，并导致识别准确率下降。

To address this issue, we here introduce a method that reduces racial bias in pre-trained face recognition models without compromising their accuracy. To this end, we model face embeddings of each person by von Mises-Fisher (MF) distribution.
为了解决这一问题，我们在此引入了一种在不牺牲准确率的前提下，减少预训练人脸识别模型中种族偏见的方法。为此，我们使用冯·米塞斯-费舍尔（von Mises-Fisher, MF）分布对每个人的面部嵌入（face embeddings）进行建模。

We next observe the dependency between demographic attributes and the density of MF distributions, and propose DenseFace, a probabilistic face matching procedure that accounts for differences in MF distributions.
接下来，我们观察了人口统计学属性与 MF 分布密度之间的依赖关系，并提出了 DenseFace——一种能够考虑 MF 分布差异的概率人脸匹配程序。

Our extensive experiments demonstrate DenseFace to consistently reduce racial bias in strong face recognition models varying in network architectures, training datasets and loss functions. Notably, DenseFace preserves recognition accuracy and requires no retraining of the underlying face recognition model. Our work also investigates previously adopted bias measures and makes suggestions.
我们的大量实验表明，DenseFace 在网络架构、训练数据集和损失函数各异的强大人脸识别模型中，均能持续降低种族偏见。值得注意的是，DenseFace 在保持识别准确率的同时，无需对底层人脸识别模型进行重新训练。我们的工作还对先前采用的偏见衡量标准进行了研究并提出了建议。
