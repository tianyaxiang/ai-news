# How to Make Linear Regression Survive Outliers
# 如何让线性回归在异常值面前“幸存”

A simple model with a serious weakness: A straight line can look surprisingly convincing—until a few bad measurements pull it somewhere it should never have gone.
一个简单模型却有着严重的弱点：一条直线看起来可能非常有说服力——直到几个糟糕的测量数据将其拉向了它本不该去的地方。

Linear regression is often one of the first predictive models practitioners learn—and one of the first they set aside when more sophisticated machine-learning methods become available. Yet linear models remain valuable when coefficients need a physical interpretation, predictions must run on a resource-constrained device, computational latency matters, or a simple benchmark is needed before introducing a higher-capacity model. They are also useful as local approximations: even a complex nonlinear relationship may behave approximately linearly over a sufficiently small region.
线性回归通常是从业者学习的第一个预测模型之一，也是当更复杂的机器学习方法出现时，他们最先放弃的模型之一。然而，在线性系数需要物理意义解释、预测必须在资源受限的设备上运行、计算延迟至关重要，或者在引入更高容量的模型前需要一个简单的基准时，线性模型依然具有价值。它们作为局部近似也非常有用：即使是复杂的非线性关系，在足够小的区域内也可能表现为近似线性。

Its simplicity, however, comes with an important weakness: Ordinary Least Squares treats every observation as trustworthy. In real data, that assumption is easy to violate. A faulty sensor, communication error, calibration problem, or biased measurement can produce observations far from the relationship we actually want to estimate. Because Ordinary Least Squares (OLS) squares every residual, a few such observations can have a disproportionate effect. Robust estimators try to prevent those observations from dominating the fit.
然而，它的简单性伴随着一个重要的弱点：普通最小二乘法（OLS）将每一个观测值都视为可信的。在真实数据中，这一假设很容易被破坏。故障传感器、通信错误、校准问题或有偏差的测量都可能产生远离我们实际想要估计的关系的观测值。由于普通最小二乘法会对每个残差进行平方，少数此类观测值可能会产生不成比例的影响。稳健估计器（Robust estimators）旨在防止这些观测值主导拟合结果。

A representative robust fit, however, stays much closer to the relationship supported by the nominal observations.
图1和图2展示了情况变化得有多快。在观测值干净的情况下，OLS能紧密跟随标称关系。而在30%的响应值被异常值替换后，同一个估计器会被严重拉偏。然而，一个具有代表性的稳健拟合模型则能更贴近由正常观测值所支持的关系。

That leads to the practical question I want to explore in this article: How do different robust estimators behave when we do not know the outlier statistics in advance—and when the contamination becomes progressively harder?
这引出了我在本文中想要探讨的实际问题：当我们预先不知道异常值的统计特征，且污染程度逐渐加剧时，不同的稳健估计器表现如何？

Knowing that a dataset contains outliers is only part of the problem. In practice, we rarely know their percentage, bias, variance, distribution, or structure beforehand. A method that works well for one convenient outlier model may behave very differently under another. The estimators here are therefore tested across several deliberately different forms of contamination.
知道数据集包含异常值只是问题的一部分。在实践中，我们很少预先知道它们的百分比、偏差、方差、分布或结构。一种在某种特定异常值模型下表现良好的方法，在另一种模型下可能会表现得截然不同。因此，本文中的估计器将在几种刻意设计的不同污染形式下进行测试。

This article compares OLS as the non-robust baseline (Legendre, 1805) with five robust estimators: Huber regression (Huber, 1964), Random Sample Consensus (RANSAC; Fischler and Bolles, 1981), Graduated Non-Convexity with the Geman–McClure loss (GNC-GM), Graduated Non-Convexity with the Truncated Least-Squares loss (GNC-TLS; Yang et al., 2020), and Adaptive Selective Outlier Rejecting (ASOR; Chughtai et al., 2024).
本文将作为非稳健基准的OLS（Legendre, 1805）与五种稳健估计器进行了比较：Huber回归（Huber, 1964）、随机抽样一致性算法（RANSAC; Fischler and Bolles, 1981）、带有Geman–McClure损失的渐进非凸优化（GNC-GM）、带有截断最小二乘损失的渐进非凸优化（GNC-TLS; Yang et al., 2020）以及自适应选择性异常值剔除（ASOR; Chughtai et al., 2024）。

Huber regression and RANSAC are classical robust-estimation methods, whereas GNC-GM, GNC-TLS, and ASOR represent more recent approaches based on non-convex continuation and adaptive residual weighting. Their central algorithmic steps are implemented directly so that the weighting, rejection, sampling, stopping, and continuation mechanisms remain visible.
Huber回归和RANSAC是经典的稳健估计方法，而GNC-GM、GNC-TLS和ASOR则代表了基于非凸连续化和自适应残差加权的较新方法。它们的核心算法步骤被直接实现，以便加权、剔除、采样、停止和连续化机制保持可见。

The estimators are evaluated using prediction error and runtime to capture both statistical accuracy and computational efficiency.
这些估计器通过预测误差和运行时间进行评估，以同时衡量统计准确性和计算效率。

Disclosure. The author developed ASOR in the original study cited here. To ensure a transparent comparison, all estimators are evaluated on the same Monte Carlo realizations using fixed and documented settings.
披露：作者在本文引用的原始研究中开发了ASOR。为确保比较透明，所有估计器均在相同的蒙特卡洛模拟实现上，使用固定且有记录的设置进行评估。

### Why squared loss can be dominated by a few observations
### 为什么平方损失会被少数观测值主导

To see why a few bad measurements can have so much influence, consider the scalar linear model:
为了理解为什么少数糟糕的测量数据会有如此大的影响，请考虑标量线性模型：

$$y_i = \beta_0 + \beta_1 x_i + \epsilon_i$$

Here, $\beta_0$ is the intercept, $\beta_1$ is the slope, and the nominal measurement error follows a Gaussian distribution:
其中，$\beta_0$ 是截距，$\beta_1$ 是斜率，标称测量误差服从高斯分布：

$$\epsilon_i \sim \mathcal{N}\left(0,\sigma_{\mathrm{in}}^2\right)$$

For $N$ independent observations, maximizing the likelihood with respect to $\beta$ is equivalent to minimizing the OLS objective:
对于 $N$ 个独立观测值，关于 $\beta$ 的最大似然估计等同于最小化OLS目标函数：

$$\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}} = \underset{\boldsymbol{\beta}}{\operatorname{arg\,min}} \left\|\mathbf{y} - \mathbf{X}\boldsymbol{\beta}\right\|_2^2$$

where $\mathbf{X} = \begin{bmatrix} 1 & 1 & \cdots & 1 \\ x_1 & x_2 & \cdots & x_N \end{bmatrix}^{\top},\ \mathbf{y} = \begin{bmatrix} y_1 & y_2 & \cdots & y_N \end{bmatrix}^{\top},\ \boldsymbol{\beta} = \begin{bmatrix} \beta_0 & \beta_1 \end{bmatrix}^{\top}$

When $\mathbf{X}$ has full column rank, the familiar closed-form expression is:
当 $\mathbf{X}$ 具有满列秩时，我们熟悉的闭式解为：

$$\hat{\boldsymbol{\beta}} = \left(\mathbf{X}^{\top}\mathbf{X}\right)^{-1} \mathbf{X}^{\top}\mathbf{y}$$

A numerical least-squares solver is preferable to explicitly forming the inverse:
使用数值最小二乘求解器优于显式计算逆矩阵：

```python
def fit_ols(x, y):
    X = np.column_stack((np.ones_like(x), x))
    return np.linalg.lstsq(X, y, rcond=None)[0]
```

The same squared-loss objective that makes OLS simple and efficient also creates its main weakness. The contribution of an observation grows quadratically with its residual magnitude:
使OLS简单高效的平方损失目标函数，同时也造成了它的主要弱点。观测值的贡献随其残差幅度的平方而增长：

$$1^2 = 1,\ 10^2 = 100,\ 100^2 = 10000$$

Thus, a normalized residual of 100 contributes as much to the OLS objective as 10000 normalized residuals of 1. A small number of severe outliers can therefore pull the fitted model away from the relationship supported by most observations, as demonstrated in Figure 2.
因此，一个归一化残差为100的观测值对OLS目标函数的贡献，等同于10000个归一化残差为1的观测值。因此，少数严重的异常值可以将拟合模型从大多数观测值所支持的关系中拉偏，如图2所示。

Practical note on missing values. Rows containing a missing predictor or response can be excluded before fitting:
关于缺失值的实用说明：包含缺失预测变量或响应值的行可以在拟合前剔除：

```python
valid = np.isfinite(X).all(axis=1) & np.isfinite(y)
X = X[valid]
y = y[valid]
```
