# Apple iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max Review: For Camera Fiends
# Apple iPhone 18 Pro 与 iPhone 18 Pro Max 评测：摄影爱好者的福音

**Rating: 9/10**
**评分：9/10**

**WIRED:** Stellar performance and cooling. Great battery life. Excellent and fun cameras with a customizable camera app. Bright, responsive screens with a smaller Dynamic Island.
**WIRED 评价：** 出色的性能与散热表现。优秀的电池续航。功能强大且有趣的摄像头，并配有可自定义的相机应用。屏幕明亮灵敏，灵动岛（Dynamic Island）尺寸更小。

**TIRED:** Pricier than ever. No base model (yet). Living in the iPhone Duo's shadow.
**不足之处：** 价格比以往更高。尚无基础款机型。活在 iPhone Duo 折叠屏手机的阴影之下。

There is no iPhone 18 this year. If you were hoping to upgrade your smartphone right now, Apple is only offering the new iPhone 18 Pro models (and a very expensive folding iPhone coming next month). Don't let that be a reason to spend extra money on a phone you might not need.
今年没有 iPhone 18 标准版。如果你正打算升级手机，苹果目前仅提供新款 iPhone 18 Pro 系列（以及下个月即将推出的昂贵折叠屏 iPhone）。不要仅仅因为这个原因，就去花冤枉钱购买你可能并不需要的手机。

Apple hasn't confirmed the next base-model yet, but the iPhone 18 is expected in spring 2027 alongside a mid-tier iPhone 18e. If you've historically been more than satisfied with the base iPhone model, you should absolutely wait to upgrade. Don't let the hype machine sway you.
苹果尚未确认下一款基础机型，但预计 iPhone 18 将于 2027 年春季与中端机型 iPhone 18e 一同发布。如果你一直以来对基础款 iPhone 的表现感到满意，那么绝对应该等待升级。不要被营销炒作所左右。

That said, if you're a Pro connoisseur, the iPhone 18 Pro ($1,199) and its bigger sibling, the 18 Pro Max ($1,299), are very good phones, despite the $100 price hike over last year’s Pro models. No, you don't need to upgrade if you're coming from an iPhone 17 Pro, 16 Pro, or even a 15 Pro—hold onto your devices for longer!—but Apple's flagships deliver where it matters, even if they feel eclipsed by the spectacle of the foldable iPhone Duo.
话虽如此，如果你是 Pro 系列的忠实拥趸，iPhone 18 Pro（1,199 美元）及其大尺寸版本 18 Pro Max（1,299 美元）依然是非常出色的手机，尽管它们比去年的 Pro 机型涨价了 100 美元。当然，如果你正在使用 iPhone 17 Pro、16 Pro 甚至 15 Pro，你并不需要升级——请继续使用你的设备！——但苹果的旗舰机型在关键领域依然表现强劲，即便它们在折叠屏 iPhone Duo 的光芒下显得有些黯然失色。

### Minor Refresh
### 小幅升级

Apple says the iPhone 18 Pro models have significant upgrades, but let's be a little more measured here.
苹果声称 iPhone 18 Pro 系列有重大升级，但我们对此需要保持客观。

Battery life has been stellar on the Pro models over the last few years, and that remains true here. The Pro Max is the more notable of the two because it packs a denser battery. With average use (around 5 hours of screen-on time), I hovered around 50 percent by the end of the day, and easily made it through day two on a single charge. I spent a little less time with the smaller Pro, but so far it has also comfortably gotten me through a day with around 40 percent left by bedtime. Safe to say, most people will very much enjoy the battery life, but the update doesn't feel drastic.
过去几年 Pro 系列的电池续航一直表现出色，这一点在新款上得以延续。Pro Max 的表现更为亮眼，因为它配备了密度更高的电池。在日常使用（约 5 小时亮屏时间）下，我到一天结束时电量仍保持在 50% 左右，单次充电轻松撑过第二天。我对小尺寸 Pro 的使用时间稍短，但到目前为止，它也能轻松支撑一整天，睡前电量剩余约 40%。可以肯定的是，大多数人会对续航感到满意，但这种升级感并不强烈。

Next, there's the processor: the A20 Pro chip. I hardly run into problems with Apple's flagship silicon, and that hasn't changed. The phones run every app fluidly, and my benchmark tests show a surprisingly sizable leap over the A19 Pro in the 17 series.
接下来是处理器：A20 Pro 芯片。我几乎从未在苹果的旗舰芯片上遇到过问题，这一点依然没变。手机运行所有应用都非常流畅，我的基准测试显示，它比 17 系列中的 A19 Pro 有了令人惊讶的显著提升。

It runs Control—a AAA game from 2019 ported to iOS earlier this year—decently well. However, aggressive upscaling makes the graphics look grainy, and character movements can feel a little sluggish. (I maxed out the graphics settings.) It's certainly not perfect, but I still can't believe I'm playing such a demanding title on an iPhone.
它运行《控制》（Control）——一款今年早些时候移植到 iOS 平台的 2019 年 3A 大作——表现尚可。然而，激进的超分辨率缩放让画面看起来有些颗粒感，角色动作有时会感到迟滞。（我开启了最高画质设置。）虽然肯定不算完美，但我依然无法相信自己能在 iPhone 上玩到如此高要求的游戏。

More impressive is Apple's thermal management on these phones. The company has expanded the vapor chamber's surface area by three times—the phone's cooling technology that helps spread heat around—meaning the entire phone felt toasty after close to an hour of gameplay in Control, with no uncomfortable localized hot spots. This larger vapor chamber also lets you game longer before phone’s thermal throttling kicks in and slows everything down.
更令人印象深刻的是苹果在这些手机上的散热管理。该公司将均热板（用于散热的冷却技术）的表面积扩大了三倍，这意味着在玩了近一小时《控制》后，整机只是感觉温热，没有出现令人不适的局部过热点。更大的均热板也让你在手机触发过热降频导致卡顿之前，能够玩更长时间的游戏。

The iPhone 18 Pro models come with iOS 27 out of the box, which features the all-new Siri AI. Yes, Siri AI is infinitely more capable than any version of Siri that Apple has offered before. It still doesn't quite have the spark or versatility of other AI assistants, but you might be fine with that. If you throw Siri AI a question, there's a good chance it can actually give you a satisfactory answer now. Remember that Siri AI and other Apple Intelligence features aren't exclusive to the latest models, though you do need an iPhone 15 Pro or newer to access them.
iPhone 18 Pro 系列出厂预装 iOS 27，搭载了全新的 Siri AI。没错，Siri AI 的能力远超苹果以往提供的任何版本。虽然它在灵活性或多功能性上仍不及其他 AI 助手，但你可能已经足够满意了。如果你向 Siri AI 提问，它现在很有可能给出令人满意的答案。请记住，Siri AI 和其他 Apple Intelligence 功能并非最新机型独占，但你需要 iPhone 15 Pro 或更新机型才能使用。

The Dynamic Island, the pill-shaped bar housing the selfie camera and Face ID sensors, is noticeably smaller this year. To take full advantage of it, you can now view three live activities—like a DoorDash order status or your Uber ETA—simultaneously inside the Island, up from two. I was navigating in Apple Maps, chatting on a phone call, and had a DoorDash order en route to my home, and it spaced out all of these activities neatly.
灵动岛（Dynamic Island）——那个容纳自拍镜头和 Face ID 传感器的药丸状区域——今年明显变小了。为了充分利用它，你现在可以在岛内同时查看三项实时活动（例如 DoorDash 订单状态或 Uber 预计到达时间），比之前的两项有所增加。我当时正在使用 Apple Maps 导航、进行通话，同时还有一个 DoorDash 订单正在配送中，灵动岛将这些活动整齐地排列显示。

Speaking of the iPhone 18 Pro design, I do like the new burgundy color, though I miss brighter, more playful options like last year's Cosmic Orange. But the real headline feature is almost invisible—embedded into the main camera on the back of the iPhone.
说到 iPhone 18 Pro 的设计，我很喜欢新的勃艮第红配色，尽管我还是怀念去年像“宇宙橙”那样更明亮、更有趣的选项。但真正的重头戏几乎是隐形的——它嵌入在 iPhone 背面的主摄像头中。

### Aperture Priority
### 光圈优先

Apple is debuting a variable aperture in the iPhone 18 Pro models. This is by no means the first time we've seen a smartphone with a variable aperture—Samsung and Huawei both launched phones with the capability years ago. While Samsung abandoned the tech, Huawei and Xiaomi still ship it on their flagships.
苹果在 iPhone 18 Pro 系列中首次引入了可变光圈。这绝不是我们第一次在智能手机上看到可变光圈——三星和华为多年前就推出了具备此功能的手机。虽然三星放弃了这项技术，但华为和小米仍在旗舰机型上使用它。

What is a variable aperture? Simply put, the aperture is the physical opening that lets light reach the camera sensor. A wider aperture lets in more light and has a lower “f-stop” number, while a narrower aperture restricts light. Most smartphones rely on fixed apertures—the iPhone 17 Pro's main camera, for example, is locked at f/1.78. A variable aperture gives the lens mechanical blades that physically expand or contract in a circular shape, adjusting how much light hits the sensor. Apple gives you four f-stops to select from: f/1.48, f/1.78, f/2.8, and f/4.
什么是可变光圈？简单来说，光圈是让光线进入相机传感器的物理开口。更大的光圈允许更多光线进入，对应的“f值”更小；而较小的光圈则会限制光线。大多数智能手机依赖固定光圈——例如，iPhone 17 Pro 的主摄像头锁定在 f/1.78。可变光圈为镜头提供了机械叶片，可以物理性地以圆形方式扩张或收缩，从而调节进入传感器的光量。苹果提供了四个 f 值供选择：f/1.48、f/1.78、f/2.8 和 f/4。

Open the aperture to f/1.48 on the iPhone 18 Pro and more light floods in, delivering cleaner low-light shots and faster Night Mode captures. This also creates a real optical bokeh—the soft blurred background effect phone makers have spent years simulating with computational Portrait modes. Step it down to f/4, this blur recedes, keeping more of the scene and background in crisp focus.
将 iPhone 18 Pro 的光圈开大到 f/1.48，更多的光线涌入，带来更纯净的暗光拍摄效果和更快的夜间模式捕捉速度。这也创造了真实的光学焦外成像（bokeh）——即手机厂商多年来一直试图通过计算摄影人像模式来模拟的柔和背景虚化效果。将光圈缩小到 f/4，虚化效果减弱，使更多的场景和背景保持清晰对焦。
