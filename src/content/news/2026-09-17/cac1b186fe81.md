# Sequence Recognition in Bharatnatyam dance
# 婆罗多舞（Bharatnatyam）中的序列识别

**Abstract:** Bharatanatyam is the oldest Indian Classical Dance (ICD) which is learned and practiced across India and the world. Adavu is the core of this dance form. There exist 15 Adavus and 58 variations. Each Adavu variation comprises a well-defined set of motions and postures (called dance steps) that occur in a particular order. So, while learning Adavus, students not only learn the dance steps but also take care of its sequence of occurrences. This paper proposed a method to recognize these sequences.

**摘要：** 婆罗多舞（Bharatanatyam）是印度最古老的古典舞蹈（ICD），在印度乃至全球范围内都有学习和实践。Adavu 是这种舞蹈形式的核心。目前存在 15 种 Adavu 以及 58 种变体。每种 Adavu 变体都包含一组定义明确的动作和姿态（称为舞步），并按特定顺序排列。因此，在学习 Adavu 时，学生不仅要学习舞步，还要注意其出现的顺序。本文提出了一种识别这些序列的方法。

In this work, firstly, we recognize the involved Key Postures (KPs) and motions in the Adavu using Convolutional Neural Network (CNN) and Support Vector Machine (SVM), respectively. In this, CNN achieves 99% and SVM's recognition accuracy becomes 84%. Next, we compare these KP and motion sequences with the ground truth to find the best match using the Edit Distance algorithm with an accuracy of 98%. The paper contributes hugely to the state-of-the-art in the form of digital heritage, dance tutoring system, and many more.

在这项工作中，我们首先分别使用卷积神经网络（CNN）和支持向量机（SVM）来识别 Adavu 中涉及的关键姿态（KPs）和动作。其中，CNN 的识别准确率达到了 99%，SVM 的识别准确率达到了 84%。接下来，我们使用编辑距离（Edit Distance）算法将这些关键姿态和动作序列与基准真值（Ground Truth）进行比较，以找到最佳匹配，准确率达到 98%。本文在数字遗产、舞蹈辅导系统等领域为现有技术做出了巨大贡献。

The paper addresses three novelties; (a) Recognizing the sequences based on the KPs and motions rather than only KPs as reported in the earlier works. (b) The performance of the proposed work is measured by analyzing the prediction time per sequence. We also compare our proposed approach with the previous works that deal with the same problem statement. (c) It tests the scalability of the proposed approach by including all the Adavu variations, unlike the earlier literature, which uses only one/two variations.

本文提出了三个创新点：（a）基于关键姿态（KPs）和动作来识别序列，而不是像早期研究那样仅基于关键姿态。（b）通过分析每个序列的预测时间来衡量所提方案的性能。我们还将所提方法与处理相同问题的先前研究进行了比较。（c）通过涵盖所有 Adavu 变体来测试所提方法的扩展性，这与早期文献仅使用一两种变体的情况不同。
