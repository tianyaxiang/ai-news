# Reasoning with Image Generation / 通过图像生成进行推理

**Title:** Reasoning with Image Generation
**标题：** 通过图像生成进行推理

**Abstract:** Chain-of-thought reasoning has revolutionized natural language processing by enabling large language models (LLMs) to decompose problems into intermediate steps before answering. Yet confining reasoning to the textual domain presents limitations for tasks requiring direct manipulation of visual representations.
**摘要：** 思维链（Chain-of-thought）推理通过使大型语言模型（LLM）能够在回答前将问题分解为中间步骤，彻底改变了自然语言处理领域。然而，将推理局限于文本领域，对于需要直接操作视觉表征的任务而言存在局限性。

Recent efforts augment multimodal LLMs with external visual expert tools such as depth estimation or object detection modules, but these remain fundamentally limited by their reliance on narrow, rigid operations that cannot flexibly generate or transform visual content.
近期的研究尝试通过外部视觉专家工具（如深度估计或目标检测模块）来增强多模态 LLM，但这些方法从根本上受到限制，因为它们依赖于狭窄且僵化的操作，无法灵活地生成或转换视觉内容。

We propose ReImaGin, which leverages image generation models as a flexible visual reasoning mechanism for multimodal LLMs: unlike fixed-function tools, they accept natural language commands and can perform open-ended visual operations, like removing an occlusion or generating a floorplan from multiple disjoint views of a room.
我们提出了 ReImaGin，它利用图像生成模型作为多模态 LLM 的一种灵活视觉推理机制：与固定功能的工具不同，它们可以接受自然语言指令，并执行开放式的视觉操作，例如移除遮挡物或根据房间的多个不连续视图生成平面图。

Across six diverse visual reasoning tasks including multi-view spatial reasoning and collision prediction, ReImaGin consistently outperforms both text-only reasoning and specialist vision-tool baselines, with gains of up to 25%, demonstrating the advantage of flexible, generative visual reasoning.
在包括多视图空间推理和碰撞预测在内的六项不同视觉推理任务中，ReImaGin 的表现始终优于纯文本推理和专业视觉工具基准，性能提升高达 25%，证明了灵活的生成式视觉推理的优势。
