# You Don't Need To Train: Agentic Heuristic Learning Studio for Executable Human Activity Recognition
# 无需训练：用于可执行人类活动识别的智能启发式学习工作室

Human activity recognition (HAR) is usually framed as gradient-based training of neural networks. Agentic Heuristic Learning (AHL) Studio explores a complementary view inspired by human cognitive learning: people learn activities by remembering examples, forming rules, and repairing mistakes, not by backpropagating.
人类活动识别（HAR）通常被定义为基于梯度的神经网络训练过程。智能启发式学习（AHL）工作室探索了一种受人类认知学习启发的互补视角：人类学习活动是通过记忆示例、形成规则和修正错误来实现的，而不是通过反向传播。

This proposed tool implements AHL for HAR: a learning-time agent reasons over sensor protocols, proposes executable heuristic policies, records repair traces, and exports an LLM-free policy for edge deployment.
该工具为 HAR 实现了 AHL：一个学习阶段的智能体通过传感器协议进行推理，提出可执行的启发式策略，记录修复轨迹，并导出用于边缘部署的无大模型（LLM-free）策略。

We focus on the HAR benchmark family and provide an end-to-end workflow from dataset observation to edge-oriented export. On eleven HAR datasets evaluated so far, AHL policies reach strong executable-policy performance while remaining inspectable, editable, and replayable.
我们专注于 HAR 基准测试系列，并提供从数据集观察到面向边缘部署导出的端到端工作流程。在目前评估的 11 个 HAR 数据集上，AHL 策略在保持可检查、可编辑和可重现的同时，达到了强大的可执行策略性能。
