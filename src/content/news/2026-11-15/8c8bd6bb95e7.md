Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.14769 (cs) [Submitted on 10 Nov 2026] Title: Leaderboard Hacking: Preference-Based Model Evaluations are Vulnerable to Manipulation Authors: Eve Fleisig, Joachim Baumann, Dylan Hadfield-Menell, Dirk Hovy.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.14769 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 10 日] 标题：排行榜黑客攻击：基于偏好的模型评估易受操纵 作者：Eve Fleisig, Joachim Baumann, Dylan Hadfield-Menell, Dirk Hovy。

Abstract: Arena-style rankings of language models are a widely used evaluation framework in online leaderboards, research papers, and model evaluation in production. These rankings aim to realistically assess model performance using pairwise comparisons of anonymous models on user prompts, aggregating votes via the Bradley-Terry model to produce the final ranking.

摘要：竞技场风格的语言模型排名是在线排行榜、研究论文和生产环境模型评估中广泛使用的评估框架。这些排名旨在通过用户提示词对匿名模型进行成对比较，并利用 Bradley-Terry 模型汇总投票结果，从而得出最终排名，以此来真实地评估模型性能。

However, we find that these leaderboards are highly vulnerable to several manipulation strategies that unscrupulous providers could use to boost a model's rank: strategic voting (individual votes that boost a model's rank, including votes on irrelevant models), strategic prompting (choosing prompts that favor a model), and strategic nomination (boosting the rank of a model by inserting irrelevant models).

然而，我们发现这些排行榜极易受到多种操纵策略的影响，不道德的提供商可能会利用这些策略来提升模型的排名：策略性投票（通过个人投票提升模型排名，包括对无关模型的投票）、策略性提示（选择有利于特定模型的提示词）以及策略性提名（通过插入无关模型来提升目标模型的排名）。

We demonstrate these effects on public leaderboard data, analyze the circumstances that cause these vulnerabilities, and describe approaches to safeguard against each attack. Notably, these issues hold for any preference-based evaluation using the Bradley-Terry model that allows open prompting, voting, or model submission.

我们在公共排行榜数据上演示了这些影响，分析了导致这些漏洞的情况，并描述了针对每种攻击的防御方法。值得注意的是，这些问题适用于任何使用 Bradley-Terry 模型且允许开放提示、投票或模型提交的基于偏好的评估。

These manipulations boost the rank of nearly every tested model; each attack can boost a model by up to 15-20 places in a ranking and, combined, they can boost a model by up to 45 places.

这些操纵手段提升了几乎所有被测模型的排名；每种攻击都能使模型在排名中上升 15 到 20 位，而综合使用这些手段，甚至可以将模型排名提升多达 45 位。
