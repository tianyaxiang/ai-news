Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.14544 (cs) [Submitted on 26 Oct 2026] Title: Zugwise: Domain-Aware Game Reconstruction for Digitizing Handwritten Chess Scoresheets Authors: Gerhard Trippen (University of Toronto).

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.14544 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 10 月 26 日] 标题：Zugwise：用于数字化手写国际象棋记谱表的领域感知博弈重构，作者：Gerhard Trippen（多伦多大学）。

Abstract: Digitizing handwritten chess scoresheets has been studied as a handwriting-recognition problem: read the move in each box and check that it is legal. This framing misses the errors that matter most. A misread that yields an illegal move is loud. One that yields a legal-but-wrong move is silent, because a legality-only pipeline accepts it and reads every later move against a wrong position.

摘要：数字化手写国际象棋记谱表一直被视为一个手写识别问题：读取每个方格中的走法并检查其是否合法。这种框架忽略了最关键的错误。导致非法走法的误读是显而易见的。而导致合法但错误走法的误读则是隐蔽的，因为仅基于合法性的流程会接受它，并根据错误的局面读取后续的每一个走法。

We therefore treat the task as game reconstruction and present Zugwise, which repurposes computer-chess machinery (quiescence search, static exchange evaluation, pin detection) to ask not "what should be played?" but "could a human plausibly have written this?" An absurdity detector makes silent errors loud at or near where they occur, and a backward search traces late symptoms to their cause.

因此，我们将此任务视为博弈重构，并提出了 Zugwise。它重新利用了计算机国际象棋机制（静止搜索、静态交换评估、牵制检测），所问的不是“应该走什么？”，而是“人类是否可能写下这个？”。一个荒谬检测器使隐蔽错误在发生处或附近变得显眼，而反向搜索则能将后续的症状追溯到其根源。

Candidate ranking excludes chess-quality signals. A Needleman-Wunsch merge adds the second player's sheet as evidence, and review stops send every disagreement between the sheets' legal readings to a human. In oracle replay of two tournament sections, a release adding them cuts silent errors from 16 to 1 and from 50 to 13.

候选排序排除了国际象棋质量信号。Needleman-Wunsch 合并算法将第二位选手的记谱表作为证据添加进来，审查机制会将两份记谱表在合法读取结果上的每一次分歧发送给人工处理。在对两个锦标赛部分进行的预言机重演中，添加这些功能的版本将隐蔽错误分别从 16 个减少到 1 个，以及从 50 个减少到 13 个。

Two learned signals, CTC re-scoring against the recognizer's raw output and a Maia-style human-move prior, raise the correct fix's top-rank rate from 58.8% to 78.0% of the replacement decisions where it is a candidate, and hold without refitting on a later section. On the first section, the human-verified output matches an independent expert transcription move for move on 34 of 45 games, and adjudication against the sheets found errors in both records, on different plies.

两个学习信号——针对识别器原始输出的 CTC 重评分和 Maia 风格的人类走法先验——将正确修正方案在候选替换决策中的最高排名率从 58.8% 提高到了 78.0%，并且在后续部分中无需重新拟合即可保持效果。在第一部分中，经人工验证的输出在 45 局比赛中的 34 局与独立专家的逐手转录完全一致，而针对记谱表的裁决发现两份记录在不同的回合中都存在错误。

We do not establish that either workflow is faster or more accurate. The system runs entirely in the browser and is free, private, and offline-capable; alongside it we offer a three-way adjudication protocol for a domain where no single record is ground truth.

我们并未断定哪种工作流程更快或更准确。该系统完全在浏览器中运行，免费、私密且支持离线使用；与此同时，我们针对一个没有单一记录作为事实标准的领域，提供了一套三方裁决协议。
