Agents that perform state estimation using pretrained perception models, e.g., Vision-Language Models (VLMs), face the fundamental challenge that predictions are uncertain. Still, most approaches determinize these predictions into a single best-guess state and make decisions as if it were ground truth. This is brittle because when the guess is wrong, the agent's decisions may be unsafe or infeasible.

使用预训练感知模型（例如视觉语言模型，VLMs）执行状态估计的智能体面临一个根本性挑战，即预测结果具有不确定性。然而，大多数方法将这些预测确定化为一个单一的最佳猜测状态，并将其视为真实情况进行决策。这种做法非常脆弱，因为一旦猜测错误，智能体的决策可能会变得不安全或不可行。

At the other extreme, maintaining a full probability distribution over symbolic states is often intractable. We propose a middle path, drawing on the underlying probabilities the perception model already produces (e.g., a VLM's output-layer token distributions) to construct a $\theta$-confidence Minimal Belief Set (MBS$_\theta$), a minimal subset of states whose cumulative probability meets a user-specified belief-mass threshold.

在另一个极端，维护符号状态的完整概率分布通常是难以实现的。我们提出了一种折中方案，利用感知模型本身产生的底层概率（例如 VLM 输出层的标记分布）来构建一个 $\theta$-置信度最小信念集（MBS$_\theta$），即一个满足用户指定信念质量阈值的最小状态子集。

The minimality of an MBS$_\theta$ ensures that the agent considers only the most likely states, enhancing robustness in downstream decision-making while maintaining computational tractability. Using a novel three-layered belief system, we efficiently approximate individual state probabilities from per-feature predictions and task-level constraints.

MBS$_\theta$ 的最小性确保了智能体仅考虑最可能的状态，从而在保持计算可行性的同时增强了下游决策的鲁棒性。通过使用一种新颖的三层信念系统，我们能够根据特征预测和任务级约束有效地近似单个状态的概率。

On this belief construction, we base our MBS$_\theta$ construction, while maintaining only a lean factored representation of the belief. Our algorithm, Probability-Ranked Valid State Search (PRaVaSS), takes advantage of the unique structure of this belief system to construct an MBS$_\theta$ with correctness guarantees and polynomial complexity in the unconstrained case.

我们在这种信念构建的基础上进行 MBS$_\theta$ 的构建，同时仅维护信念的精简因子表示。我们的算法——概率排序有效状态搜索（PRaVaSS），利用该信念系统的独特结构来构建具有正确性保证的 MBS$_\theta$，并在无约束情况下具有多项式复杂度。

Using a VLM as the backbone of our state estimator, we validate our method's theoretical properties, scalability with problem size, and improvement of downstream decision-making when used in a task-planning pipeline.

通过使用 VLM 作为我们状态估计器的骨干，我们验证了该方法的理论属性、随问题规模的可扩展性，以及在任务规划流程中使用时对下游决策的改进效果。
