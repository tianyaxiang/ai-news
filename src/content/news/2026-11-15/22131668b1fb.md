# Watch SpaceX successfully execute the first ‘chopstick’ catch of Starship

SpaceX captured Starship using the arms of ‘Mechazilla’ its launch and catch tower for the first time.

SpaceX 首次利用其发射与回收塔“Mechazilla”的机械臂成功捕获了星舰（Starship）。

After aborting a planned catch of Starship Flight 15’s booster, SpaceX successfully captured Starship with its “Mechazilla” launch and catch tower for the first time. The company has performed ‘chopstick’ catches of its boosters before, but this is the first time the maneuver has successfully been executed on the Starship vehicle itself.

在取消了原定对星舰第 15 次飞行助推器的回收计划后，SpaceX 首次利用其“Mechazilla”发射与回收塔成功捕获了星舰本体。该公司此前曾多次执行过助推器的“筷子”回收任务，但这是该机动首次在星舰飞行器本身上取得成功。

SpaceX CEO Elon Musk said during the company’s third-quarter earnings call that “success is not guaranteed on these first flights, but I’d say it’s more likely than not that this succeeds next flight.” While not a complete success, in that the company was unable to recover the entire launch vehicle, it still stands as a milestone for the Starship program.

SpaceX 首席执行官埃隆·马斯克在公司第三季度财报电话会议上曾表示：“这些首飞任务并不能保证成功，但我认为下一次飞行成功的可能性很大。”虽然此次任务并未取得完全成功（公司未能回收整个运载火箭），但这仍然是星舰项目的一个里程碑。

And, since it has previously caught boosters on previous launches, there is reason to believe the failure today was not an insurmountable obstacle. SpaceX is still trying to determine what caused the booster to abort its landing and detonate over the Gulf of Mexico.

此外，鉴于此前在多次发射中已成功回收过助推器，我们有理由相信今天的失败并非不可逾越的障碍。SpaceX 目前仍在调查导致助推器中止着陆并在墨西哥湾上空爆炸的原因。
