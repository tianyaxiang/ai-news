More than 500 vineyards are scattered across the slopes and valleys of South Africa’s Western Cape, one of the world’s most biologically diverse landscapes. The region produces about 90 percent of South Africa’s wine, but winemakers there are increasingly struggling with drought, excessive heat and erratic rainfall.

南非西开普省的斜坡和山谷中分布着 500 多个葡萄园，这里是世界上生物多样性最丰富的景观之一。该地区生产了南非约 90% 的葡萄酒，但当地的酿酒师正日益受到干旱、极端高温和降雨不规律的困扰。

Temperatures in the region have risen 1.5 to 2 times faster than the global average over the past five decades. And extreme storms have become more intense as climate variability increases. The late 2010s brought a significant challenge for winemakers, as three years of severe drought placed vineyards under intense stress and reduced grape production.

在过去的五十年里，该地区的气温上升速度比全球平均水平快 1.5 到 2 倍。随着气候变率的增加，极端风暴也变得更加强烈。2010 年代末给酿酒师带来了严峻挑战，三年的严重干旱使葡萄园承受了巨大压力，并导致葡萄产量下降。

As climate variability places increasing pressure on grape production, many wine producers are building on sustainability measures that have long been part of vineyard management. A sustainability practice found in some South Africa vineyards is regenerative agriculture, which focuses on restoring and nurturing the soil naturally. Cows provide natural fertilizer and work organic material into the soil with their hooves as they graze on weeds and grasses.

随着气候变率给葡萄生产带来越来越大的压力，许多葡萄酒生产商正在加强长期以来作为葡萄园管理一部分的可持续发展措施。在南非的一些葡萄园中，一种可持续的做法是再生农业，其重点是自然地恢复和培育土壤。奶牛在吃杂草和青草时，通过蹄子将有机物质踩入土壤，并提供天然肥料。

We are a team of geographers and sustainability scientists who study environmental change, environmental health, conservation, agriculture and land management. Our team wanted to better understand how farmers in South Africa’s wine-producing regions are responding as a range of environmental and economic pressures rise. We talked with growers, conservation practitioners and wine industry experts in the Western Cape and analyzed how 107 vineyards, about 20% of the total, were using sustainable land stewardship practices to adapt.

我们是一个由地理学家和可持续发展科学家组成的团队，研究环境变化、环境健康、保护、农业和土地管理。我们的团队希望更好地了解南非葡萄酒产区的农民在各种环境和经济压力上升时如何应对。我们与西开普省的种植者、保护从业者和葡萄酒行业专家进行了交谈，并分析了 107 个葡萄园（约占总数的 20%）如何利用可持续的土地管理实践来适应环境。

In many agricultural landscapes, production and conservation goals are frequently viewed as competing priorities. But our research found that many vineyard operators in the Western Cape see long-term stewardship and conservation of soil and wildlife habitat as essential strategies for business success.

在许多农业景观中，生产目标和保护目标经常被视为相互竞争的优先事项。但我们的研究发现，西开普省的许多葡萄园经营者将长期的土地管理以及对土壤和野生动物栖息地的保护视为商业成功的必要策略。

Conservation as an investment, not a constraint. The Western Cape is home to the Cape Floral Region, a global biodiversity hot spot. It includes fynbos, a unique biome containing thousands of endemic plant species, including types of proteas, ericas and restios that are found nowhere else on Earth. Sustainability certification and conservation programs, such as World Wildlife Fund Conservation Champions, have helped encourage vineyards to protect biodiversity in the region.

保护是一种投资，而非限制。西开普省是全球生物多样性热点地区——开普植物区系的所在地。它包括凡波斯（fynbos），这是一种独特的生物群落，包含数千种特有植物物种，包括在地球上其他任何地方都找不到的山龙眼、欧石楠和灯心草。可持续发展认证和保护项目（如世界自然基金会的“保护冠军”）已帮助鼓励葡萄园保护该地区的生物多样性。

Many growers use these sustainability certifications to increase their wine’s marketability. But we found they have also embraced conservation for how it can benefit their vineyards. Rather than viewing conservation as land taken out of production, many said they see it supporting the ecological processes essential to their business.

许多种植者利用这些可持续发展认证来提高其葡萄酒的市场竞争力。但我们发现，他们也因为保护措施能使葡萄园受益而拥护它。许多人表示，他们并不认为保护意味着土地退出生产，而是将其视为支持其业务所必需的生态过程。

The majority of South Africa’s vineyards today participate in the Integrated Production of Wine certification system, created by the South African wine industry and researchers in 1998 to reduce environmental harm. The system sets standards governing pesticide use, water management, wastewater treatment and environmental stewardship. It also uses independent auditing of participating farms and wineries to ensure those claiming certification meet the standards.

如今，南非大多数葡萄园都参与了“葡萄酒综合生产”认证体系，该体系由南非葡萄酒行业和研究人员于 1998 年创建，旨在减少环境危害。该体系制定了管理农药使用、水资源管理、废水处理和环境管理的标准。它还对参与的农场和酒庄进行独立审计，以确保获得认证的单位符合标准。

To manage the water supplies their vineyards depend on, growers described restoring native vegetation, including the removal of alien species, along waterways and reducing erosion to protect wetlands and rivers. Research shows that healthy natural systems can improve water retention in the soil and contribute to the overall resilience of the landscape in the face of more extreme climate conditions.

为了管理葡萄园所依赖的水源，种植者描述了恢复水道沿线的原生植被（包括清除外来物种）并减少侵蚀以保护湿地和河流的做法。研究表明，健康的自然系统可以改善土壤的保水能力，并有助于景观在面对更极端气候条件时提高整体韧性。

To boost their soil’s health, some growers are planting cover crops, such as legumes, cereals like oats and rye, and brassicas such as radish and mustard, between vine rows. Growers broadly described using cover crops, compost, mulching and other practices to improve soil structure and increase organic matter in the soil. Cover crops require maintenance and can provide habitat for pests, but research shows that the right cover crops, when well maintained, can benefit vineyards by reducing erosion and increasing water retention and nutrients in the soil.

为了促进土壤健康，一些种植者在葡萄行间种植覆盖作物，如豆类、燕麦和黑麦等谷物，以及萝卜和芥菜等十字花科植物。种植者普遍描述了使用覆盖作物、堆肥、覆盖物和其他做法来改善土壤结构并增加土壤中的有机质。覆盖作物需要维护，也可能为害虫提供栖息地，但研究表明，如果维护得当，合适的覆盖作物可以通过减少侵蚀和增加土壤中的保水能力和养分来使葡萄园受益。

Several producers also described efforts to reduce their reliance on chemical pesticides, which is beneficial not only for the environment but also their businesses as chemical costs continue to rise. We heard about vineyards that monitor pest populations closely and respond in targeted ways rather than using pesticides broadly. Some growers encourage beneficial insects and manage habitats that support natural pest control. The creation of habitats for natural pest predators can reduce the overall disease risk too.

一些生产商还描述了减少对化学农药依赖的努力，这不仅对环境有利，而且随着化学品成本的持续上涨，对他们的业务也有利。我们了解到一些葡萄园会密切监测害虫种群并采取有针对性的应对措施，而不是广泛使用农药。一些种植者鼓励有益昆虫并管理支持自然害虫防治的栖息地。为天然害虫捕食者创造栖息地也可以降低整体疾病风险。

While chemical treatments remain part of many vineyard operations, growers reported increasing interest in other approaches that prevent disease and pest outbreaks, such as pruning, to reduce their total chemical use. Sustainable practices like these are well established in South Africa’s wine industry, but our interviews suggest their use has been expanding in recent years. Rather than focusing solely on treating problems after they arise, these approaches emphasize prevention, monitoring and ecological balance. Sustainability must also make economic sense.

虽然化学处理仍然是许多葡萄园运营的一部分，但种植者报告称，他们对其他预防疾病和害虫爆发的方法（如修剪）越来越感兴趣，以减少化学品的总使用量。像这样的可持续做法在南非葡萄酒行业中已经很成熟，但我们的采访表明，近年来它们的使用范围一直在扩大。这些方法不再仅仅关注问题出现后的处理，而是强调预防、监测和生态平衡。可持续发展也必须具有经济意义。
