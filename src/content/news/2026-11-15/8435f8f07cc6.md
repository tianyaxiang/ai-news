Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.14765 (cs) [Submitted on 10 Nov 2026] Title: SynthCorp: A Platform for Creating Benchmarks for Data Agents Authors: Jiani Zhang, Manav Garg, Eugenie Lai, Cheng Zhen, Eva Sharma, Yang Pan, Yaming Lin, Alon Halevy.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.14765 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 10 日] 标题：SynthCorp：一个用于创建数据智能体基准测试的平台 作者：Jiani Zhang, Manav Garg, Eugenie Lai, Cheng Zhen, Eva Sharma, Yang Pan, Yaming Lin, Alon Halevy。

Abstract: Current benchmarks for LLM-driven data agents saturate quickly and cannot target specific capabilities. We present SynthCorp, a platform that creates customizable data estates and benchmarks by simulating how business events produce and modify corporate data over time.

摘要：当前针对大模型驱动的数据智能体的基准测试很快就会饱和，且无法针对特定能力进行评估。我们提出了 SynthCorp，这是一个通过模拟业务事件如何随时间产生和修改企业数据，从而创建可定制数据资产和基准测试的平台。

Each data estate combines relational databases, a document store, and a data catalog, paired with evaluation tasks and executable gold programs. First, given a scenario and target complexity dimensions, SynthCorp synthesizes reusable event types and compiles each into a program over predefined simulation primitives.

每个数据资产都结合了关系数据库、文档存储和数据目录，并配有评估任务和可执行的黄金程序。首先，给定场景和目标复杂度维度，SynthCorp 会合成可重用的事件类型，并将每个事件类型编译为基于预定义模拟原语的程序。

Second, SynthCorp schedules event instances on a virtual calendar, interleaving routine and disruption events so that tables, documents, and catalog entries remain consistent. Third, SynthCorp executes the calendar to materialize the data estate and records every operation in a simulation log.

其次，SynthCorp 在虚拟日历上调度事件实例，交替执行常规事件和中断事件，以确保表、文档和目录条目保持一致。第三，SynthCorp 执行日历以实现数据资产的具体化，并将每项操作记录在模拟日志中。

Fourth, SynthCorp generates analytical tasks and derives verified gold programs from the log. We use SynthCorp to generate initial evaluation benchmarks comprising seven data estates (up to 1K operational tables) and 256 tasks across five domains.

第四，SynthCorp 生成分析任务并从日志中导出经过验证的黄金程序。我们使用 SynthCorp 生成了初始评估基准，其中包括七个数据资产（最多 1000 个操作表）和跨五个领域的 256 个任务。

The data estates pass deterministic pipeline checks (such as foreign-key violations, undeclared writes, recurrence violations, and database execution failures) with a near-zero error rate, introduce cross-asset complexities, and cover all requested dimensions.

这些数据资产通过了确定性流水线检查（如外键违规、未声明的写入、递归违规和数据库执行失败），错误率接近于零，引入了跨资产的复杂性，并涵盖了所有要求的维度。

Evaluating three models across four data-context settings shows that the benchmarks separate data contexts and model tiers. Auditing candidate queries against causal provenance in the simulation log verifies gold programs without manual annotation, including on multi-event composite tasks that expose reasoning failures in state-of-the-art agents.

在四种数据上下文设置下对三个模型进行评估表明，这些基准测试能够区分数据上下文和模型层级。通过对照模拟日志中的因果溯源审计候选查询，无需人工标注即可验证黄金程序，包括那些暴露了最先进智能体推理缺陷的多事件复合任务。

We hope the community uses SynthCorp to generate more challenging and useful data estates and benchmarks that foster the development of enterprise data agents.

我们希望社区能够利用 SynthCorp 生成更具挑战性和实用性的数据资产与基准测试，从而促进企业数据智能体的发展。
