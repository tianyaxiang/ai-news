Computer Science > Artificial Intelligence arXiv:2611.14736 (cs) [Submitted on 10 Nov 2026] Title: When Should Semantic Priors Enter Partially-Identified Causal Discovery? Authors: Muhammad Ahmed Mohsin, Saad Bin Ather, Valentin Six, Lancelot Da Costa, Tommaso Salvatori, Bernhard Scholkopf.

计算机科学 > 人工智能 arXiv:2611.14736 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 11 月 10 日] 标题：语义先验何时应进入部分识别的因果发现？作者：Muhammad Ahmed Mohsin, Saad Bin Ather, Valentin Six, Lancelot Da Costa, Tommaso Salvatori, Bernhard Scholkopf。

Abstract: Observational causal discovery often leaves causal directions unresolved or unstable. Large language models (LLMs) can provide semantic information to resolve these directions, but incorrect proposals can exclude the true causal graph and invalidate downstream inference.

摘要：观测因果发现往往会导致因果方向无法解析或不稳定。大型语言模型（LLMs）可以提供语义信息来解析这些方向，但错误的建议可能会排除真实的因果图并使下游推理失效。

We study three separate decisions: where to allocate semantic queries, which proposals to admit, and which causal graphs to retain. Our framework distinguishes population non-identifiability from finite-sample instability, evaluates semantic orientation against statistical and random controls, and examines calibrated admission and error-tolerant DAG retention.

我们研究了三个独立的决策：在哪里分配语义查询，采纳哪些建议，以及保留哪些因果图。我们的框架区分了总体不可识别性和有限样本不稳定性，根据统计和随机对照评估了语义导向，并检查了校准后的采纳和容错的有向无环图（DAG）保留。

Experiments across benchmark networks show that semantic information improves recovery over coin-flip orientations, but targeting unstable edges has no established advantage over matched-random allocation. The tested admission certificate cannot support nontrivial admission with the available calibration tasks.

在基准网络上的实验表明，语义信息在恢复方面优于随机抛硬币的导向，但针对不稳定边的做法相比匹配随机分配并没有确定的优势。所测试的采纳证书无法通过现有的校准任务支持非平凡的采纳。

Moreover, downstream retention depends on reference adequacy: learned references contain the true DAG in only three of 80 planned tasks. These findings separate the value of semantic information from the reliability of its allocation, admission, and retention, and identify calibration size and reference adequacy as key limitations.

此外，下游的保留取决于参考的充分性：在 80 个计划任务中，学习到的参考仅在三个任务中包含了真实的 DAG。这些发现将语义信息的价值与其分配、采纳和保留的可靠性分离开来，并确定了校准规模和参考充分性是关键的局限性。
