Beta Pictoris is a young star, roughly 20 million years old, and located about 64 light-years away from Earth. It is surrounded by a vast disk of dust and rubble where planets are still forming—astronomers have already found three. In a new Science study, a team led by Sarah Betti, an astronomer at the Space Telescope Science Institute in Baltimore, used the James Webb Space Telescope to look for ice in this disk. Researchers found both water and carbon dioxide ice. The water ice is glassy rather than crystalline, and it is mixed with carbon dioxide in roughly the same proportions we see in comets of our own Solar System. This suggests ingredients necessary to form water-rich worlds might be common around young stars.

绘架座β星（Beta Pictoris）是一颗约2000万年历史的年轻恒星，距离地球约64光年。它被一个巨大的尘埃和碎石盘所环绕，行星仍在其中形成——天文学家已经发现了三颗。在《科学》杂志的一项新研究中，由巴尔的摩太空望远镜科学研究所的天文学家莎拉·贝蒂（Sarah Betti）领导的团队，利用詹姆斯·韦伯太空望远镜搜寻了该星盘中的冰。研究人员发现了水冰和二氧化碳冰。这些水冰呈玻璃状而非结晶状，并与二氧化碳混合，其比例与我们太阳系彗星中的比例大致相同。这表明形成富水世界所需的成分在年轻恒星周围可能很常见。

The missing ice: Beta Pictoris is one of the best studied stars in the sky. Its disk was among the first ever imaged, back in 1984, and we see it almost exactly edge-on, which makes it a natural laboratory for studying how planetary systems grow. It’s what astronomers call a debris disk, where the dust is constantly replenished by collisions among comets and planetesimals, small bodies that are the building blocks of planets. The outer parts of the disk are far enough from the star that water and other volatile molecules should freeze into solid ice. The disk holds a large reservoir of carbon monoxide gas, which ultraviolet light from the star should destroy within 50 to 300 years. For that gas to still be there, something must keep replenishing it—most likely it’s released from ice when volatile-rich comets collide.

缺失的冰：绘架座β星是天空中研究最透彻的恒星之一。它的星盘是1984年最早被成像的星盘之一，我们几乎可以完全侧向观察它，这使其成为研究行星系统如何演化的天然实验室。这就是天文学家所说的碎片盘，其中的尘埃通过彗星和微行星（构成行星的基石）之间的碰撞不断得到补充。星盘的外围距离恒星足够远，水和其他挥发性分子应该会冻结成固体冰。该星盘中含有大量的一氧化碳气体，恒星发出的紫外线本应在50到300年内将其摧毁。为了让这些气体依然存在，必然有某种机制在不断补充它——最有可能的是，当富含挥发物的彗星发生碰撞时，这些气体从冰中释放出来。

The properties of the planet Beta Pictoris b also suggest it formed from ice-rich material. Yet despite plenty of reasons to suspect the system is full of ice, earlier searches have failed to find it. Attempts to detect water vapor found nothing, and a search for the telltale dip that water ice leaves in starlight at a wavelength of 3 micrometers also failed. According to theoretical models, ultraviolet radiation from the star can destroy small grains of pure ice faster than collisions can produce them. On the other hand, grains of dirty ice, mixed with other molecules, should survive longer.

行星绘架座β星b的特性也表明它是由富冰物质形成的。然而，尽管有充分的理由怀疑该系统充满了冰，但早期的搜寻工作均未发现其踪迹。探测水蒸气的尝试一无所获，寻找水冰在3微米波长处留下的星光特征凹陷的尝试也以失败告终。根据理论模型，恒星的紫外线辐射摧毁纯冰小颗粒的速度比碰撞产生它们的速度要快。另一方面，与其他分子混合的“脏冰”颗粒应该能存活更长时间。

Reading the reflection: Betti and her colleagues observed Beta Pictoris on December 7, 2023, using Webb’s Near Infrared Spectrograph (NIRSpec). They operated it in a mode that records a full spectrum, from 0.6 to 5.2 micrometers, for every point in the image. After subtracting the overwhelming glare of the star, the team measured how much starlight the disk’s dust reflects at each wavelength to look for the fingerprints of ices. Water ice absorbs light strongly near 3 micrometers, while carbon dioxide ice absorbs either at 4.27 micrometers or 4.39 micrometers when it contains the heavier carbon-13 isotope. The team first mapped the water ice features across the disk and found they were strikingly uniform. They then extracted detailed spectra from two spots on opposite sides of the star, each roughly 100 astronomical units out. Both spectra showed water and carbon dioxide ices, the latter in both its common form and the rarer carbon-13 version. But the shape of those features turned out to tell us more than the fact they were there.

解读反射：贝蒂和她的同事于2023年12月7日使用韦伯的近红外光谱仪（NIRSpec）观测了绘架座β星。他们以一种记录图像中每个点从0.6到5.2微米全光谱的模式进行操作。在减去恒星强烈的眩光后，团队测量了星盘尘埃在每个波长下反射的星光量，以寻找冰的指纹。水冰在3微米附近强烈吸收光线，而二氧化碳冰在含有较重的碳-13同位素时，会在4.27微米或4.39微米处吸收光线。团队首先绘制了整个星盘的水冰特征图，发现它们惊人地均匀。随后，他们从恒星两侧各约100个天文单位处的两个点提取了详细光谱。两个光谱都显示了水冰和二氧化碳冰的存在，后者既有常见形式，也有较罕见的碳-13版本。但这些特征的形状所揭示的信息，远不止它们存在这一事实。

Dirty glass: The carbon dioxide features were shifted slightly toward shorter wavelengths and were much broader than those of pure carbon dioxide ice measured in the lab. The team says in the paper that this is how carbon dioxide behaves when its molecules are trapped inside water ice. In other words, the two ices aren’t sitting side by side as separate grains—they are thoroughly mixed, which makes it the kind of dirty ice that models suggested could survive the star’s ultraviolet radiation. The team calculated that there is slightly more than one carbon dioxide molecule for every 10 water molecules, meaning it’s about 11 percent of the ice. That falls within the 2.5 to 30 percent range measured in Solar System comets and the 7 to 20 percent seen in the ice of disks around other newborn stars. It means the ices of Beta Pictoris may have formed through the same processes as in our own Solar System, building up on dust grains in cold, dense, water-rich clouds during the earliest stages of star formation.

脏玻璃：二氧化碳的特征略微向短波长方向偏移，并且比实验室中测量的纯二氧化碳冰的特征宽得多。团队在论文中指出，这就是二氧化碳分子被困在水冰内部时的表现。换句话说，这两种冰并不是作为独立的颗粒并排存在，而是彻底混合在一起，这使其成为了模型所预言的那种能够抵御恒星紫外线辐射的“脏冰”。团队计算得出，每10个水分子中就有略多于1个二氧化碳分子，这意味着它约占冰成分的11%。这一比例处于太阳系彗星测得的2.5%至30%范围内，也处于其他新生恒星周围星盘冰中观察到的7%至20%范围内。这意味着绘架座β星的冰可能与我们太阳系的形成过程相同，是在恒星形成的最早阶段，在寒冷、致密且富含水分的云团中于尘埃颗粒上积聚而成的。

The structure of the water ice also pointed in this direction. When water freezes at moderate temperatures, its molecules settle into an orderly crystal lattice, and crystalline ice produces a sharp peak in the spectrum just above 3 micrometers. That peak was absent at Beta Pictoris. The ice is almost entirely amorphous, a disordered, glass-like solid that forms when water molecules freeze so fast and so cold that they stick wherever they land. The team estimates that less than 7 percent of the water ice in the system is crystalline, which is somewhat surprising. Models predicted that ice in debris disks should mostly be crystalline. For amorphous ice to stay in that form, it has to remain colder than about 100 Kelvin, or minus 173° Celsius. Otherwise, something has to keep breaking up its crystal structure, such as high-energy ultraviolet light, cosmic rays, or particles streaming from the star. Either way, the researchers concluded the ice most likely formed in the interstellar medium, long before the planets of Beta Pictoris began to take shape. The remaining question was how the icy grains got spread so evenly across the disk.

水冰的结构也指向了这一方向。当水在适中温度下冻结时，其分子会排列成有序的晶格，而结晶冰会在3微米以上的光谱中产生一个尖峰。但在绘架座β星中，这个尖峰并不存在。这些冰几乎完全是无定形的，这是一种无序的、玻璃状的固体，形成于水分子冻结得极快且极冷，以至于它们落在哪里就粘在哪里。团队估计，该系统中只有不到7%的水冰是结晶状的，这有些令人惊讶。模型曾预测碎片盘中的冰应该大部分是结晶状的。为了让无定形冰保持这种形态，它必须保持在约100开尔文（即零下173摄氏度）以下。否则，必须有某种力量不断破坏其晶体结构，例如高能紫外线、宇宙射线或来自恒星的粒子流。无论如何，研究人员得出结论，这些冰极有可能是在绘架座β星的行星开始形成之前，在星际介质中形成的。剩下的问题是，这些冰颗粒是如何在星盘中如此均匀地分布开来的。

Constant collisions: The team argues that this even ice distribution requires frequent collisions among planetesimals. Each collision would release fresh icy dust, including ice that had been buried and protected deep inside the colliding bodies, replenishing what the starlight destroys. We already have evidence that such collisions do happen. Earlier infrared images revealed a curved, lopsided streak of dust in the southwestern arm of the disk, thought to be debris from a violent collision.

持续的碰撞：团队认为，这种均匀的冰分布需要微行星之间频繁的碰撞。每一次碰撞都会释放出新鲜的冰尘，包括那些被埋藏并保护在碰撞体深处的冰，从而补充被星光摧毁的部分。我们已经有证据表明此类碰撞确实在发生。早期的红外图像显示，在星盘的西南臂上有一条弯曲且倾斜的尘埃带，被认为是剧烈碰撞产生的碎片。
