This tiny gamepad with dual analog thumbsticks pushes the limits of portable controllers. Larger hands may struggle with the Atom Plus, but it squeezes a lot of functionality into an extremely tiny controller.

这款配备双模拟摇杆的小型游戏手柄挑战了便携式控制器的极限。对于手掌较大的人来说，Atom Plus 可能用起来有些吃力，但它在极其小巧的机身中塞入了大量功能。

Measuring around 3.5 inches wide, the $29.99 Atom Plus is about a half-inch smaller than the 8BitDo Zero 2 but squeezes in considerably more features and functionality. The most obvious is a pair of symmetrical analog thumbsticks with anti-drift tunneling magnetoresistance technology. You also get shoulder buttons and triggers, rumble feedback, a dedicated home button, motion controls, and customization through a mobile app.

Atom Plus 宽度约为 3.5 英寸，售价 29.99 美元，比 8BitDo Zero 2 小了约半英寸，但却集成了更多功能。最显著的特点是配备了一对采用抗漂移隧道磁阻技术的对称模拟摇杆。此外，它还具备肩键和扳机键、震动反馈、专用主页键、体感控制，并支持通过移动应用程序进行自定义。

Like many third-party controllers, the Atom Plus works with multiple platforms, including the Switch and Switch 2, PCs, Android, iOS, and various smart TVs. And just like other gamepads, pairing to different devices requires putting the Atom Plus into one of several Bluetooth modes by holding down a combination of buttons at power-up, which in this case is the home button plus a specific direction on the D-pad.

与许多第三方控制器一样，Atom Plus 适用于多个平台，包括 Switch 和 Switch 2、PC、Android、iOS 以及各种智能电视。和其他游戏手柄一样，连接不同设备时，需要通过在开机时按住组合键（在本例中为主页键加上方向键的特定方向）来将 Atom Plus 置于多种蓝牙模式之一。

While the overall build quality feels excellent, the Atom Plus’ tiny footprint creates some compromises and challenges. The front action buttons and D-pad feel nice and squishy, but the gamepad’s controls are a little crowded. When pressing down or right on the D-pad, my thumb regularly bumps the left thumbstick. It doesn’t hinder gameplay, but I do find it an annoying distraction.

虽然整体做工感觉非常出色，但 Atom Plus 的微小尺寸也带来了一些妥协和挑战。正面的动作按键和方向键手感柔软舒适，但控制区域显得有些拥挤。当按下方向键的下或右时，我的拇指经常会碰到左摇杆。这虽然不会阻碍游戏操作，但我确实觉得这是一种恼人的干扰。

The shoulder buttons use digital microswitches, as do the triggers, which means the latter can’t be used for racing games that support analog acceleration controls. I like that the two sets of buttons on the top edge sit at slightly different heights, but I would have preferred a little more differentiation since I’ve occasionally accidentally pressed the triggers when I meant to hit the shoulder buttons.

肩键和扳机键都使用了数字微动开关，这意味着后者无法用于支持模拟加速控制的赛车游戏。我喜欢顶部边缘两组按键高度略有不同的设计，但我希望它们的区分度能更高一些，因为我有时想按肩键时会不小心误触到扳机键。

I was impressed with the Atom Plus’ customizability, however. When paired to an accompanying mobile app, you can check the controller’s remaining charge, turn off rumble feedback, see its unique product number (by tapping your phone to an NFC chip inside the controller), and remap any of its controls. That includes the pair of dual-function shortcut levers that replicate the Switch’s plus and minus buttons and screenshot shortcut.

不过，Atom Plus 的可定制性令我印象深刻。通过配套的移动应用程序，你可以查看控制器的剩余电量、关闭震动反馈、查看其唯一产品编号（通过将手机靠近控制器内的 NFC 芯片），并重新映射任何控制按键。这包括那对双功能快捷拨杆，它们可以模拟 Switch 的加号键、减号键以及截图快捷键。

Those levers move up and down and cleverly reduce the number of buttons crowding the front of the Atom Plus, but they feel a little too small, which is more or less the general theme. It’s a tiny gamepad that’s great for travel or stashing in your everyday carry. But, unsurprisingly, it probably won’t find its way into your regular gaming rotation.

这些拨杆可以上下拨动，巧妙地减少了 Atom Plus 正面按键的拥挤感，但它们感觉有点太小了，这或多或少是该产品的整体基调。这是一款非常适合旅行或放入日常携带包中的小型游戏手柄。但不出所料，它可能不会成为你日常游戏的首选。
