2026年11月13日星期五，Cpt.Jank发布文章《英伟达被指通过资助人工智能初创公司购买其GPU来制造短缺》，发布时间为昨日15:31，讨论（61条评论）。

Following the departure of now-former NVIDIA CEO, Jensen Huang, a new report by The Wall Street Journal (via Wccftech) has emerged alleging that NVIDIA artificially inflated the ongoing GPU and memory shortage by investing in AI startups that would go on to buy NVIDIA GPUs and build data centers.

随着现已离职的英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋的离开，《华尔街日报》（通过Wccftech报道）出现了一份新报告，指控英伟达通过投资那些随后购买英伟达GPU并建立数据中心的人工智能初创公司，人为地加剧了持续的GPU和内存短缺。

The reason behind this was to maintain the perception of ongoing high demand and to move an oversupply of semiconductors from TSMC. The WSJ report cites an August meeting between Huang and his senior finance team, including NVIDIA's CFO and acting CEO, Colette Kress, during which Kress pushed for a reduction in circular financing agreements, in response to which Huang allegedly lay into Kress, calling her a "deer in the headlights," after she had pushed to scale back NVIDIA's $250 billion deal with OpenAI.

其背后的原因是维持市场对持续高需求的认知，并消化来自台积电的半导体供应过剩。《华尔街日报》的报道援引了8月份黄仁勋与其高级财务团队（包括英伟达首席财务官兼代理首席执行官科莱特·克雷斯）之间的一次会议。在会议中，克雷斯推动减少循环融资协议，而黄仁勋据称对克雷斯进行了抨击，称她为“惊弓之鸟”，此前她曾推动缩减英伟达与OpenAI之间2500亿美元的交易。

Jensen's push for further investment into small-scale cloud providers was seemingly in response to the changing tide that saw AI hyperscalers move away from off-the-shelf components in favor of custom solutions. The situation described in the report saw NVIDIA on the hook for an "avalanche" of GPUs from TSMC, for which it would have no immediate buyers.

黄仁勋推动对小型云服务提供商的进一步投资，似乎是为了应对人工智能超大规模企业转向定制解决方案而非现成组件的趋势。报告中描述的情况显示，英伟达面临着来自台积电的“雪崩式”GPU供应，而这些产品短期内没有买家。

Part of the issue highlighted by Kress was also that these small-scale cloud providers NVIDIA was investing in could not afford to buy NVIDIA GPUs, meaning they, too, could be a financial dead end.

克雷斯强调的问题还包括，英伟达投资的这些小型云服务提供商无力购买英伟达的GPU，这意味着它们也可能成为财务上的死胡同。
