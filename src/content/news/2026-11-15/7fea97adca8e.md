# Work to replace old copper wire led to the FAA’s ground stop

更换旧铜线的工程导致了美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）的停飞令。

Issues with a new router related to a fiber optic upgrade grounded thousands of flights on Friday.

与光纤升级相关的新路由器问题导致周五数千架次航班停飞。

At a press conference today, Transportation Secretary Sean Duffy said that Friday’s nationwide ground stop ordered by the FAA was related to work replacing outdated copper wire with a fiber optic line at a “remote FAA facility.” The secretary said that this was “routine maintenance,” rather than the result of a major modernization effort. He told reporters that it was “such an old wire that it was being discontinued, meaning that line was going to get shut down,” necessitating the upgrade.

在今天的新闻发布会上，交通部长肖恩·达菲（Sean Duffy）表示，周五FAA下令的全国性停飞与在“FAA远程设施”将过时的铜线更换为光纤线路的工作有关。部长称这是“例行维护”，而非重大现代化工程的结果。他告诉记者，由于“线路过于陈旧，即将被淘汰，意味着该线路将被关闭”，因此必须进行升级。

What wasn’t clear, according to Secretary Duffy, was the root cause of the problem. He said that it could have been faulty hardware, a misconfigured router, or “incompetence” on the part of contractor L3Harris. Regardless, what followed was the newly installed router “flooding” other routers in the Air Traffic Control system. Duffy said that there were “over 1,000 connection attempts every 4 to 5 seconds,” causing disruption to radar feeds and communication with planes.

据达菲部长称，目前尚不清楚问题的根本原因。他表示，这可能是硬件故障、路由器配置错误，或是承包商L3Harris的“失职”所致。无论如何，随之而来的是新安装的路由器“淹没”了空中交通管制系统中的其他路由器。达菲说，当时“每4到5秒就有超过1000次连接尝试”，导致雷达信号和飞机通信中断。

Secretary Duffy also took the opportunity to call on additional funding from Congress for modernizing the Air Traffic Control system, and to tout the work the FAA has done so far. “We are building, we are building safely, but we are building fast,” he said, but said that Congress had only given him a “partial payment” to complete the necessary work.

达菲部长还借此机会呼吁国会提供额外资金，用于空中交通管制系统的现代化改造，并吹捧了FAA迄今为止所做的工作。他说：“我们正在建设，我们正在安全地建设，但我们建设的速度很快。”但他同时表示，国会只给了他完成必要工作所需的“部分款项”。

In a statement posted to their site, L3Harris said: L3Harris has been modernizing the communications infrastructure supporting the National Airspace System in close coordination with the FAA. With safety and reliability as our highest priorities, L3Harris, together with its telco partners and the FAA, has completed more than 70% of the planned airspace communications conversion from legacy copper wires to a fiber optic system. We are supporting the FAA in its investigation to understand the conditions that led to the Nov. 13 ground stop of our nation’s air travel. L3Harris takes the nationwide ground stop and its impact on travelers, airlines and air traffic operations seriously.

L3Harris在其网站上发表声明称：L3Harris一直与FAA密切配合，对支持国家空域系统的通信基础设施进行现代化改造。我们将安全和可靠性视为最高优先级，L3Harris与其电信合作伙伴及FAA已完成了计划中超过70%的空域通信转换工作，即将传统铜线更换为光纤系统。我们正在支持FAA进行调查，以了解导致11月13日全国航空旅行停飞的情况。L3Harris非常重视此次全国性停飞及其对旅客、航空公司和空中交通运营的影响。
