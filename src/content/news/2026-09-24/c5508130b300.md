# Meta ditches the camera on its newest smart glasses
# Meta 在其最新款智能眼镜上取消了摄像头

Walking around Meta Connect 2026, everyone’s sporting smart glasses in all sorts of shapes, colors, and sizes. It’s a marked difference here, a tech bubble where “pervert glasses” are not a concern.
在 Meta Connect 2026 大会现场走动时，每个人都戴着各种形状、颜色和尺寸的智能眼镜。这里是一个科技泡沫，与外界不同，“变态眼镜”（pervert glasses）在这里并不是人们担忧的问题。

Outside Connect, the public backlash against wearable surveillance tech — a catchall term that encompasses not just smart glasses, but also smartwatches and AI pendants — is gaining steam. So it’s incredibly curious timing that this year, the company is launching Ray-Ban Meta Audio Glasses: smart glasses infused with Meta AI, but with no camera for recording others.
在大会之外，公众对可穿戴监控技术的抵制情绪正日益高涨——这是一个涵盖了智能眼镜、智能手表和 AI 吊坠等设备的统称。因此，Meta 在今年推出 Ray-Ban Meta 音频眼镜的时机显得格外耐人寻味：这是一款植入了 Meta AI 的智能眼镜，但没有用于拍摄他人的摄像头。

To hear Meta tell it, that’s a coincidence. Audio-only glasses have been years in the making.
据 Meta 称，这纯属巧合。纯音频眼镜的研发已经进行了多年。

“Three years ago, it wasn’t clear if AI glasses were going to be a thing. Back then, every other company was backing out of the space,” says Alex Himel, Meta’s vice president of wearables. “Then two years ago, we saw that audio was the top use case, the biggest potential for AI, and that it should work well in a pair of audio glasses.”
“三年前，还不确定 AI 眼镜是否会成为主流。当时，其他所有公司都在退出这个领域，”Meta 可穿戴设备副总裁 Alex Himel 表示，“后来在两年前，我们发现音频是首要的使用场景，也是 AI 最大的潜力所在，而且它应该能很好地适配音频眼镜。”

Himel has a point. Open-ear audio is the least controversial part of the Ray-Bans — and the thing everyone can agree is convenient. Plus, the camera-less glasses are lighter. Himel says that by removing the cameras, Meta could “rearchitect” the glasses. “That allowed us to take a lot of electronics out of the front frame and have a lot more design flexibility.”
Himel 说得有道理。开放式音频是 Ray-Ban 眼镜中最没有争议的部分，也是大家公认的便捷功能。此外，没有摄像头的眼镜更轻。Himel 表示，通过移除摄像头，Meta 可以“重新架构”眼镜，“这使我们能够将大量电子元件从前框中移出，从而拥有更大的设计灵活性。”

The audio-only models start at 43 grams — heavier than normal wire-frame glasses, but noticeably lighter than the roughly 50g Ray-Ban Meta Gen 2 and the 69g Meta Ray-Ban Display. The arms are thinner. They come in two styles: the Mad Men-esque Clubmaster and the more Wayfarer-reminiscent Burbank. The hinges extend for wider faces; the temple arms and nose pads are adjustable. There are 23 color and lens combinations, and they can support the same wider prescription range as the Ray-Ban Meta Optics lineup. Even more so than the standard Meta Ray-Bans, these look and wear like normal glasses. And with a $349 starting price, they’re cheaper too.
纯音频型号重量从 43 克起——比普通金属框眼镜重，但明显比约 50 克的 Ray-Ban Meta 第二代和 69 克的 Meta Ray-Ban Display 轻。镜腿更细。它们有两种款式：带有《广告狂人》风格的 Clubmaster 和更具 Wayfarer 风格的 Burbank。铰链可为宽脸型延伸；镜腿和鼻托均可调节。共有 23 种颜色和镜片组合，并支持与 Ray-Ban Meta 光学系列相同的更广处方范围。与标准的 Meta Ray-Ban 相比，它们看起来和戴起来更像普通眼镜。而且 349 美元的起售价也更便宜。

Free of a power-hungry camera, the battery lasts an estimated 12 hours on a single charge. The most technical things you can do with them are interact with Meta AI, take phone calls, and listen to podcasts or music. And that’s it.
由于没有耗电的摄像头，单次充电预计电池续航可达 12 小时。你能用它们做的最复杂的技术操作就是与 Meta AI 互动、接听电话以及收听播客或音乐。仅此而已。

“They don’t record,” Himel says. “The only thing they do, which is the same thing as an Alexa device in your house or some other devices, they’re just listening for a specific wake word. They’re not sending anything … If they hear [the wake word], then they start listening to what you’re saying. When you’re done talking, then it’s processing, and that’s visible to the AI. It gets processed, and that’s kind of it.”
“它们不会录音，”Himel 说，“它们唯一的功能，就像你家里的 Alexa 设备或其他设备一样，只是在监听特定的唤醒词。它们不会发送任何东西……如果它们听到[唤醒词]，就会开始监听你说的话。当你停止说话时，它会进行处理，这对 AI 来说是可见的。它会被处理，基本就是这样。”

Slipping them on, they’re comfortable and, I think, quite fetching on me. Regardless of how one might feel about Meta, it’s fair to guess most people would feel less like a creeper wearing these.
戴上它们感觉很舒适，而且我认为戴在我脸上还挺好看的。无论人们对 Meta 有什么看法，可以肯定的是，大多数人戴着这款眼镜时，会感觉自己没那么像个“偷窥狂”了。

I feel differently slipping on the new Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3. Partly because the camera is back in play, partly because the pair Meta gives me is the new aviator style with transition lenses. I’m told I can pull it off, but I keep asking my colleague Jay Peters if I’m giving ’80s serial killer vibes. The other new style is called Zena — not the warrior princess — and it’s a bolder, geometric cat-eye frame.
戴上新款 Ray-Ban Meta 第三代时，我的感觉就不同了。部分原因是摄像头又回来了，部分原因是我拿到的这款是带有变色镜片的新款飞行员风格。有人说我戴着很合适，但我还是忍不住问同事 Jay Peters，我看起来是不是有种 80 年代连环杀手的气质。另一种新风格叫 Zena——不是那个女战士公主——它是一种更大胆的几何猫眼框。

The third-generation glasses feature fairly minor refinements. There’s a sixth microphone, compared to the second generation’s five, for clearer phone calls. Battery life has gotten a slight boost to nine hours, an hour longer than the second gen. The temple arms, like the audio-only glasses, are adjustable for a better fit. All sound will now be captured in Dolby Atmos for spatial audio. Sure! That’s neat if I’m in my quiet hotel room, but it’s harder to appreciate when reviewing footage amid the hustle and bustle of Meta Connect. The new model comes in 27 color and lens combinations, plus three seasonal options.
第三代眼镜的改进相当细微。相比第二代的五个麦克风，它增加了一个麦克风，总共六个，通话更清晰。电池续航略有提升，达到 9 小时，比第二代长了一小时。和纯音频眼镜一样，镜腿可调节以获得更好的贴合度。所有声音现在都将以杜比全景声（Dolby Atmos）进行空间音频捕捉。当然！如果我在安静的酒店房间里，这很棒，但在 Meta Connect 的喧嚣中查看素材时，就很难体会到这一点了。新款有 27 种颜色和镜片组合，外加 3 种季节性选择。

Along with the hardware changes, the Gen 3 glasses are also getting a price bump. They start at $449, up from $379 for the second-gen models last year.
除了硬件变化外，第三代眼镜的价格也有所上涨。起售价为 449 美元，高于去年第二代机型的 379 美元。

Meta Glasses — the off-brand, more affordable entry-level glasses launched a few months ago — also have more options. There are two new styles, Capri and Nova. Capri is some kind of cat-eye shape, while Nova… honestly, the styles are all starting to blur together. They’re round and remind me of several other Ray-Ban Meta glasses, but slightly different. There’s a new ivory-colored Kylie Jenner pair that costs $399.
Meta Glasses——几个月前推出的非品牌、更实惠的入门级眼镜——也有了更多选择。有两种新风格：Capri 和 Nova。Capri 是一种猫眼形状，而 Nova……老实说，这些风格开始变得模糊了。它们是圆形的，让我想起其他几款 Ray-Ban Meta 眼镜，但略有不同。还有一款售价 399 美元的象牙色 Kylie Jenner 联名款。

But maybe Kylie Jenner isn’t the celebrity collab you’ve been dreaming about. That’s fine. Because Blackpink’s Lisa — a K-pop icon and The White Lotus actress — also has Meta Glasses. They have pink lenses and come with a blind-box-type charm styled after Lisa’s pets. These also start at $399. (Non-celebrity versions of the Meta Glasses start at $249.)
但也许 Kylie Jenner 并不是你梦寐以求的明星联名款。没关系，因为 Blackpink 的 Lisa——一位 K-pop 偶像和《白莲花度假村》演员——也有自己的 Meta 眼镜。它们配有粉色镜片，并附赠一个以 Lisa 的宠物为原型的盲盒式挂饰。这些款式起售价也是 399 美元。（非明星联名版的 Meta Glasses 起售价为 249 美元。）

Why the celebrity collabs? Options for more people, including women.
为什么要进行明星联名？是为了给更多人提供选择，包括女性用户。
