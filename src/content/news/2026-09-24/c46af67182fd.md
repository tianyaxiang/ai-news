# Geometric and Semantic Coupling for Interaction Understanding in 3D Scenes
# 三维场景交互理解中的几何与语义耦合

**Abstract:** Interaction understanding in 3D scenes requires a joint description of movable parts, their motion, and the regions through which they can be operated. We present Segment-Snap, which connects these outputs through the physical relationship between parts and handles.
**摘要：** 三维场景中的交互理解需要对可移动部件、其运动方式以及可操作区域进行联合描述。我们提出了 Segment-Snap，通过部件与把手之间的物理关系将这些输出连接起来。

Learned predictors identify broad part surfaces and small handles. A geometric decoder uses planar and upright priors to constrain motion, then selects hinge lines using predicted handle locations, without training a motion regressor. Conversely, a joint part-and-handle predictor supplies additional handle candidates, whose motion classes are refined using containing parts. Each information transfer is applied once, without iterative feedback.
学习型预测器能够识别宽大的部件表面和小型把手。几何解码器利用平面和垂直先验来约束运动，随后利用预测的把手位置选择铰链线，而无需训练运动回归器。反之，联合部件与把手预测器提供额外的把手候选，并利用所属部件对这些把手的运动类别进行细化。每种信息传递仅应用一次，无需迭代反馈。

On Articulate3D validation, handle guidance raises motion-gated AP from 13.74% to 40.98% at fixed masks and axes. Additional handle candidates raise handle AP from 24.63% to 29.65%; part-based class correction adds 0.98 points, and full context reaches 30.99%. Repeated training, learned-decoder controls and paired visualizations establish the benefits and limitations of combining geometric and semantic evidence for interaction understanding.
在 Articulate3D 的验证中，在固定掩码和轴的情况下，把手引导将运动门控平均精度（AP）从 13.74% 提升至 40.98%。额外的把手候选将把手 AP 从 24.63% 提升至 29.65%；基于部件的类别校正增加了 0.98 个百分点，而完整上下文则达到了 30.99%。通过重复训练、学习型解码器控制以及配对可视化，我们确立了结合几何与语义证据进行交互理解的优势与局限性。
