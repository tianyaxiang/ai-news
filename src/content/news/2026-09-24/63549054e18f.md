# Meta Connect 2026: The biggest news and announcements
# Meta Connect 2026：最重磅的新闻与发布

It’s about time for Meta Connect, the company’s annual product launch event. This year, given the company’s major focus on AI and wearables like smart glasses, it seems likely that we’ll see updates from CEO Mark Zuckerberg and his team on those categories. The company has been facing significant scrutiny because of how some users have been covertly recording other people using the glasses, meaning that one of the biggest announcements of the show could be its rumored camera-free pair.
Meta Connect 是该公司一年一度的产品发布会，现在正是时候。鉴于今年公司将重心放在人工智能（AI）和智能眼镜等可穿戴设备上，我们很可能会看到首席执行官马克·扎克伯格及其团队在这些领域带来的更新。由于一些用户利用智能眼镜秘密录制他人，该公司一直面临巨大的舆论压力，这意味着本次发布会最重大的公告之一可能就是传闻已久的无摄像头版本眼镜。

Connect has also traditionally been a venue for updates on Meta’s VR hardware and software. While the company has scaled back its VR ambitions, a recent leak seemingly revealing some kind of unannounced mixed reality glasses could indicate that Meta will show off new immersive headwear technology during the event.
Connect 历来也是 Meta 发布 VR 硬件和软件更新的平台。尽管该公司已经缩减了其 VR 业务的雄心，但最近泄露的一款尚未公布的混合现实眼镜似乎表明，Meta 将在本次活动中展示新的沉浸式头戴设备技术。

Zuckerberg’s keynote is scheduled to begin at 7PM ET / 4PM PT on Wednesday, September 23rd. Follow along with all of our coverage right here.
扎克伯格的主题演讲定于美国东部时间 9 月 23 日星期三晚上 7 点（太平洋时间下午 4 点）开始。请在此处关注我们的全程报道。

### Meta’s next VR device isn’t a headset — it’s glasses
### Meta 的下一款 VR 设备不是头显，而是眼镜

The Meta VR Glasses are launching as new VR hardware: a pair of glasses. The new Meta VR Glasses sit on your ears like a typical pair of glasses instead of being strapped over your head, but they still have immersive displays for watching movies, playing games, and getting work done. Imagine a pair of hefty black sunglasses with blacked-out lenses on the outside, but the components of a typical VR headset distributed between the device on your head and a computing puck in your pocket.
Meta VR 眼镜作为全新的 VR 硬件正式发布。这款新眼镜像普通眼镜一样架在耳朵上，而不是绑在头上，但它依然配备了用于观看电影、玩游戏和办公的沉浸式显示屏。想象一下一副厚重的黑色太阳镜，外侧镜片全黑，而典型 VR 头显的组件则被拆分，一部分在头戴设备上，另一部分在口袋里的计算单元中。

The idea is to give you a way to experience VR in a much lighter and more approachable form factor than Vision Pro or Meta Quest-style headsets. I got to try the VR Glasses at Meta’s campus this week, and as Meta walked me through some familiar VR demos like watching content, playing a game, and working from multiple screens, I was impressed by how much lighter the whole experience felt. Some of that was about the literal weight, as the glasses come in at just 100g, far less than the Quest 3’s 515g. But there was also something lighter in a more intangible way: the glasses form factor made the headset seem like something I could easily jump in and out of instead of being a whole cumbersome commitment with big head straps. It felt like a lightweight Vision Pro, and is perhaps a better execution of some of what Apple wanted to do with that device.
其设计理念是提供一种比 Vision Pro 或 Meta Quest 类头显更轻便、更易于上手的 VR 体验方式。本周我在 Meta 园区试用了这款 VR 眼镜，当 Meta 工作人员带我体验了观看内容、玩游戏和多屏办公等熟悉的 VR 演示时，整个体验的轻盈感令我印象深刻。部分原因在于物理重量，这款眼镜仅重 100 克，远低于 Quest 3 的 515 克。但还有一种更无形的“轻盈感”：眼镜的外形让它看起来像是我可以随时轻松佩戴和摘下的设备，而不是那种需要大头带、笨重且繁琐的负担。它感觉就像是一个轻量级的 Vision Pro，或许这才是苹果公司当初想要实现的目标的更好版本。

### Muse is coming to Meta smart glasses
### Muse 即将登陆 Meta 智能眼镜

Just a couple of weeks after launching Muse, Meta announced that it’s “working on” bringing the agent to its smart glasses, including the new glasses it unveiled at Meta Connect. Users will be able to activate Muse from their glasses by saying its name and ask it to handle tasks like guiding a workout, logging meals, or helping them shop for products they’re looking at.
在推出 Muse 仅几周后，Meta 宣布正在努力将该智能助手引入其智能眼镜，包括在 Meta Connect 上发布的新款眼镜。用户可以通过语音唤醒眼镜上的 Muse，并要求它处理诸如指导锻炼、记录饮食或帮助购买正在查看的商品等任务。

Muse is just one of several major updates coming to Meta’s glasses. They’re also getting some new accessibility features, like a Hearing Enhancement feature Meta says is “FDA-cleared software for adults with perceived mild to moderate hearing loss.” It will be available later this year with a Meta One subscription or as a $149 add-on for compatible眼镜. Meta AI will also be able to give “more context-rich answers” when users ask the glasses what they’re looking at, potentially making Meta’s glasses more effective for helping blind and low-vision users interpret their surroundings. A new audio customization feature will also allow users to shift their smart glasses’ volume between their left and right ears, including sending all audio to one side.
Muse 只是 Meta 眼镜即将迎来的几项重大更新之一。它们还将获得一些新的辅助功能，例如 Meta 所称的“经 FDA 批准的、针对轻度至中度听力损失成年人的听力增强软件”。该功能将于今年晚些时候通过 Meta One 订阅提供，或作为兼容眼镜的 149 美元附加组件购买。当用户询问眼镜正在看什么时，Meta AI 也将能够提供“更具上下文丰富性的回答”，这可能使 Meta 眼镜在帮助盲人和低视力用户解读周围环境方面更加有效。此外，一项新的音频自定义功能将允许用户在左右耳之间调节智能眼镜的音量，包括将所有音频发送到一侧。

### Meta ditches the camera on its newest smart glasses
### Meta 在其最新智能眼镜上取消了摄像头

Walking around Meta Connect 2026, everyone’s sporting smart glasses in all sorts of shapes, colors, and sizes. It’s a marked difference here, a tech bubble where “pervert glasses” are not a concern. Outside Connect, the public backlash against wearable surveillance tech — a catchall term that encompasses not just smart glasses, but also smartwatches and AI pendants — is gaining steam. So it’s incredibly curious timing that this year, the company is launching Ray-Ban Meta Audio Glasses: smart glasses infused with Meta AI, but with no camera for recording others.
在 Meta Connect 2026 的会场里，每个人都戴着各种形状、颜色和尺寸的智能眼镜。这里是一个截然不同的科技泡沫，人们不必担心所谓的“变态眼镜”。在会场之外，公众对可穿戴监控技术（这是一个涵盖智能眼镜、智能手表和 AI 吊坠的统称）的抵制情绪正在升温。因此，该公司今年推出 Ray-Ban Meta 音频眼镜的时机非常耐人寻味：这是一款融入了 Meta AI 的智能眼镜，但没有用于录制他人的摄像头。

### Meta is making Muse more powerful and will let you video chat with it, too
### Meta 正在增强 Muse 的功能，并允许你与其进行视频聊天

Meta is quickly iterating on its new Muse AI agent, announcing a bunch of updates today that make the bot more capable and able to chat with you in more ways. Muse agents are getting their own email addresses that they can use for accomplishing tasks. You’ll also be able to communicate with your Muse agent over the new email addresses, too. In addition, the recently launched Muse Mac app will be getting the ability to use your computer.
Meta 正在快速迭代其新的 Muse AI 助手，并在今天宣布了一系列更新，使该机器人功能更强大，并能以更多方式与你交流。Muse 助手将拥有自己的电子邮件地址，可用于完成任务。你也可以通过这些新的电子邮件地址与你的 Muse 助手进行沟通。此外，最近推出的 Muse Mac 应用程序将获得操控电脑的能力。
