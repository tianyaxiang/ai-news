# Meta VR Glasses, Ray-Ban Meta Audio, Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3: Specs, Features, Prices
# Meta VR 眼镜、Ray-Ban Meta Audio、Ray-Ban Meta 第三代：规格、功能与价格

After months of controversy about the privacy of its smart glasses, Meta has unveiled the next iteration of its product lineup. At its Meta Connect event in Menlo Park, CEO Mark Zuckerberg announced all the new glasses the company is putting out into the world.
在经历了数月关于其智能眼镜隐私问题的争议后，Meta 发布了其产品线的最新迭代。在门洛帕克举行的 Meta Connect 大会上，首席执行官马克·扎克伯格宣布了公司即将推出的所有新款眼镜。

Its popular Meta Ray-Ban glasses are now in their third generation, but two new models steal the show: a powerful, slimmed-down version of Meta's Quest headsets capable of virtual and augmented reality modes, and a camera-free pair of Ray-Ban-branded smart glasses, called Ray-Ban Meta Audio. Meta is also bringing its Muse AI agent to its smart glasses platform.
其广受欢迎的 Meta Ray-Ban 眼镜现已进入第三代，但两款新机型抢尽了风头：一款是功能强大且轻量化的 Meta Quest 头显版本，支持虚拟现实和增强现实模式；另一款是不带摄像头的 Ray-Ban 品牌智能眼镜，名为 Ray-Ban Meta Audio。此外，Meta 还将其 Muse AI 智能体引入了智能眼镜平台。

### Meta VR Glasses
### Meta VR 眼镜

The next evolution of Meta’s Quest VR headset is something truly different. Instead of a heavy helmet-like headset, the new Meta VR Glasses look like big goggles. They’re five times lighter than the Meta Quest 3. They sit on the nose, right in front of your face like glasses, with no headstrap needed.
Meta Quest VR 头显的下一次进化带来了真正与众不同的产品。新款 Meta VR 眼镜不再是沉重的头盔式设备，看起来更像是一副大护目镜。它们的重量比 Meta Quest 3 轻了五倍。它们像普通眼镜一样架在鼻梁上，无需头带即可佩戴。

They look and feel more like the virtual display glasses made by companies like Viture, and what Xreal’s Project Aura seems to be shaping up to be—lightweight mixed reality lenses that let you access virtual spaces without the burden of a VR headset.
它们的外观和佩戴感更像是 Viture 等公司制造的虚拟显示眼镜，也类似于 Xreal 的 Project Aura 项目——即轻量级的混合现实镜片，让你无需背负 VR 头显的沉重负担即可进入虚拟空间。

There are no included controllers, though the device can connect to Meta’s previous Quest controllers or gamepads like an Xbox controller. Instead, the wearer controls the device with hand movements and finger pinches, similar to Android XR and Apple’s Vision Pro. Eye tracking pinpoints what you’re trying to access. You can pinch with two fingers to open an app or interact with something on the screen. Form a fist and flick your thumb up or down to scroll the screen.
该设备不附带控制器，但可以连接 Meta 之前的 Quest 控制器或 Xbox 手柄等游戏手柄。佩戴者通过手势和手指捏合来控制设备，这与 Android XR 和 Apple Vision Pro 类似。眼球追踪功能可以精确定位你想访问的内容。你可以用两根手指捏合来打开应用程序或与屏幕上的内容交互。握拳并上下拨动拇指即可滚动屏幕。

Inside, the lenses feature a 5K micro-OLED display with a resolution of 2412 x 2288 pixels per eye. The computing power is offloaded to an attached compute puck, which also houses the battery.
在内部，镜片采用了 5K micro-OLED 显示屏，单眼分辨率为 2412 x 2288 像素。计算能力被卸载到一个外接的计算单元（compute puck）上，该单元同时也容纳了电池。

Small prescription lens inserts can be magnetically clipped inside the lenses. (Meta has not shared how much those will cost.)
小型的近视镜片插件可以通过磁吸方式固定在镜片内部。（Meta 尚未公布这些插件的价格。）

The software is the same that runs on Meta’s Quest VR devices, so movie and game titles in the Quest ecosystem will work on the VR Glasses. That includes games like Beat Saber, live sports apps, and streaming services like Disney+. There are digital workspace modes that let you have multiple monitor screens around you. The VR Glasses can also display a virtual keyboard on a surface in front of you, then use the embedded cameras to track specific finger placements.
其软件与 Meta Quest VR 设备运行的系统相同，因此 Quest 生态系统中的电影和游戏标题都可以在这款 VR 眼镜上运行。这包括《Beat Saber》等游戏、体育直播应用以及 Disney+ 等流媒体服务。此外还有数字工作空间模式，允许你在周围设置多个显示屏。VR 眼镜还可以在你面前的平面上显示虚拟键盘，并利用内置摄像头追踪手指的具体位置。

The only immediate downside compared to other VR headsets—at least that I could tell from a brief demo at Meta Connect—is that the VR glasses don’t cover your entire field of vision, so it doesn’t feel as immersive as a traditional VR headset.
与其他 VR 头显相比，目前唯一的直接缺点——至少从我在 Meta Connect 上的简短演示中观察到的来看——是这款 VR 眼镜无法覆盖你的整个视野，因此感觉不如传统 VR 头显那样具有沉浸感。

Meta says the VR Glasses cost $1,300 and will ship in the spring of 2027.
Meta 表示，这款 VR 眼镜售价为 1300 美元，将于 2027 年春季发货。

### Ray-Ban Meta Audio
### Ray-Ban Meta Audio

The Ray-Ban Meta Audio glasses have all the audio capabilities of a standard pair of Meta’s smart glasses, minus image and video capture. At 43 grams, they’re also lighter than the others. They have better battery life—12 hours on a single charge and up to 48 hours with the included charging case.
Ray-Ban Meta Audio 眼镜具备 Meta 标准智能眼镜的所有音频功能，但去掉了图像和视频拍摄功能。其重量仅为 43 克，比其他型号更轻。它们的电池续航能力更强——单次充电可使用 12 小时，配合附带的充电盒最长可达 48 小时。

They come in 23 different color and style options, including the retro Ray-Ban Clubmaster frame and a more rectangular Burbank. The Audio glasses cost $349 and are available for preorder now. They start shipping on October 10.
它们提供 23 种不同的颜色和款式选择，包括复古的 Ray-Ban Clubmaster 镜框和更偏长方形的 Burbank 款式。Audio 眼镜售价 349 美元，现已开启预订，将于 10 月 10 日开始发货。

### Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3
### Ray-Ban Meta 第三代

Meta's flagship line of smart glasses is getting a “Gen 3” upgrade. They have 12-MP cameras that can capture, like last year, plus 3K Dolby Atmos-enabled video capture. The glasses will get up to 9 hours of battery life, two more than their predecessors. A new six-microphone array offers better sound and can cut out background noise more easily.
Meta 的旗舰智能眼镜系列迎来了“第三代”升级。它们配备了 1200 万像素摄像头，可以像去年一样进行拍摄，并支持 3K 杜比全景声视频录制。这款眼镜的电池续航时间长达 9 小时，比上一代增加了两小时。全新的六麦克风阵列提供了更好的音质，并能更轻松地消除背景噪音。

A new hearing aid feature called Hearing Enhancement uses the speakers in Meta's glasses to enhance speech and audio around the wearer, similar to the Nuance glasses from EssilorLuxottica.
一项名为“听力增强”（Hearing Enhancement）的新助听功能，利用 Meta 眼镜中的扬声器来增强佩戴者周围的语音和音频，这与依视路陆逊梯卡（EssilorLuxottica）的 Nuance 眼镜类似。

Earlier this summer, Meta debuted smart glasses under its own branding, which cost a little less but were functionally identical to the Ray-Ban models. Now, it is already following them up with Gen 3 versions. These Meta-branded glasses come in a cat-eye shape called Capri and a rounder lens called Nova.
今年夏天早些时候，Meta 推出了自有品牌的智能眼镜，价格略低，但在功能上与 Ray-Ban 型号完全相同。现在，它已经推出了第三代版本。这些 Meta 品牌眼镜有猫眼形状的 Capri 款和镜片更圆的 Nova 款。

The Ray-Ban Meta Gen 3 glasses start at $449. Special edition aviator glasses are $579. There are also additional models with colors and styles designed in collaboration with Kylie Jenner and rapper Lisa. (They come with optional charms.) They're available now.
Ray-Ban Meta 第三代眼镜起售价为 449 美元。特别版飞行员眼镜售价为 579 美元。此外还有与凯莉·詹娜（Kylie Jenner）和说唱歌手 Lisa 合作设计的其他款式（配有可选的装饰挂件）。现已上市。

It’s unclear how much the Meta-branded Gen 3 glasses cost. We’ll update this story soon with preorder and sale date information.
目前尚不清楚 Meta 品牌第三代眼镜的价格。我们将尽快更新本文，提供预订和发售日期信息。

### Meta Ray-Ban Display
### Meta Ray-Ban 显示功能

Meta's Display glasses are also getting some updates. With the implementation of Meta AI, users can create photorealistic holograms of their faces, which can be helpful for video calls. There are better cycling options and improved navigation apps.
Meta 的显示眼镜也进行了一些更新。随着 Meta AI 的引入，用户可以创建逼真的面部全息图，这对视频通话很有帮助。此外，还有更好的骑行选项和改进的导航应用程序。
