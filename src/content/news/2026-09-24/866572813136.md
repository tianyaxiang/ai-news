# Entropy Can Flow, or It Can Guide. Be Entropy. LEDFlow: Introducing Entropy-guided Generation Order into Uniform Discrete Flow

**Title:** Entropy Can Flow, or It Can Guide. Be Entropy. LEDFlow: Introducing Entropy-guided Generation Order into Uniform Discrete Flow
**标题：** 熵可以流动，也可以引导。成为熵。LEDFlow：将熵引导的生成顺序引入均匀离散流

**Abstract:** Uniform discrete flow permits repeated updates at every generation position. While continued revision supports correction of wrong tokens, it also exposes correct intermediate predictions to later errors. An experiment on Sudoku puzzles shows that 9.4% of generated cells are correct at an intermediate step but incorrect in the final output.
**摘要：** 均匀离散流允许在每个生成位置进行重复更新。虽然持续的修订有助于纠正错误的标记，但它也使正确的中间预测暴露在后续的错误中。一项关于数独谜题的实验表明，9.4% 的生成单元在中间步骤是正确的，但在最终输出中却是错误的。

We introduce generation order into uniform discrete flow through selective absorption, which fixes chosen predictions while preserving the uniform-flow velocity at active positions. To prevent absorbing incorrect predictions, we propose Low-Entropy Discrete Flow (LEDFlow), a training-free sampler that adaptively orders absorption by local entropy.
我们通过选择性吸收将生成顺序引入均匀离散流，这在固定所选预测的同时，保持了活跃位置的均匀流速度。为了防止吸收错误的预测，我们提出了低熵离散流（LEDFlow），这是一种无需训练的采样器，它根据局部熵自适应地对吸收进行排序。

By decomposing absorption error into joint dependence and conditional prediction terms, we show that selecting the lowest-entropy positions under a fixed absorption budget minimizes an upper bound on the conditional term. We further support the choice of local entropy by showing that the decision-error bound of global lookahead grows with the lookahead window under an imperfect denoiser.
通过将吸收误差分解为联合依赖项和条件预测项，我们证明了在固定的吸收预算下选择最低熵的位置可以最小化条件项的上限。我们进一步支持了局部熵的选择，证明了在不完美的去噪器下，全局前瞻的决策误差界限会随着前瞻窗口的增大而增加。

Across reasoning benchmarks, LEDFlow attains 0.845 Nikoli Sudoku solve accuracy, with the largest gains on strongly constrained tasks. On text-to-image generation it attains the best overall score, and on multimodal understanding it improves over the native sampler on all six benchmarks, at an inference cost comparable to standard flow sampling.
在推理基准测试中，LEDFlow 达到了 0.845 的 Nikoli 数独求解准确率，在强约束任务上增益最为显著。在文本到图像生成方面，它获得了最佳的总体评分；在多模态理解方面，它在所有六个基准测试中均优于原生采样器，且推理成本与标准流采样相当。
