# Uncertainty-Aware 3D Residual Wavelet Diffusion for Ultra Low-Field MRI Super-Resolution
# 用于超低场磁共振成像超分辨率的感知不确定性 3D 残差小波扩散模型

**Abstract:** Ultra low-field MRI expands global access to neuroimaging but produces scans with low signal-to-noise ratio, reduced contrast, and thick slices. While regression-based super-resolution can recover anatomical detail for segmentation, it returns a single deterministic estimate that gives no indication of regions where the low-field input leaves anatomy underdetermined.

**摘要：** 超低场磁共振成像（MRI）扩大了全球神经影像的获取范围，但其生成的扫描图像存在信噪比低、对比度降低和层厚较厚等问题。虽然基于回归的超分辨率方法可以恢复用于分割的解剖细节，但它仅返回单一的确定性估计，无法指示低场输入中解剖结构不明确的区域。

Generative diffusion models offer an alternative by sampling the posterior distribution of plausible high-field images, quantifying this anatomical ambiguity. However, applying them to 3D whole-brain MRI is restricted by memory bottlenecks, slow sampling, and scanner domain shifts. We propose a 3D residual wavelet diffusion model that combines three ideas to overcome these hurdles.

生成式扩散模型提供了一种替代方案，通过对合理的高场图像后验分布进行采样，从而量化这种解剖学上的模糊性。然而，将这些模型应用于 3D 全脑 MRI 时，受到内存瓶颈、采样速度慢以及扫描仪域偏移的限制。我们提出了一种 3D 残差小波扩散模型，结合了三种思路来克服这些障碍。

A lossless wavelet reparameterisation shrinks the spatial grid to fit a whole brain on a single GPU, residual shifting accelerates sampling by starting from the low-field input, and domain randomisation promotes scanner generalisation without paired training data. As the high-field reference is not a voxel-aligned ground truth, we evaluate downstream volumetric agreement.

无损小波重参数化技术缩小了空间网格，使得在单个 GPU 上即可处理全脑数据；残差偏移通过从低场输入开始采样来加速过程；域随机化则在无需配对训练数据的情况下提升了扫描仪的泛化能力。由于高场参考图像并非体素对齐的真实值（Ground Truth），我们评估了下游的体积一致性。

On a healthy cohort (n=19) imaged at 0.064T and 3T, our method matches a leading general-purpose regression approach in volumetric accuracy while additionally generating per-voxel uncertainty maps highlighting underdetermined regions. Furthermore, on a pilot dataset (n=11) of participants with cognitive impairment, disease-relevant atrophy is preserved rather than normalised towards a healthy prior. Our framework brings whole-brain posterior sampling to low-field super-resolution without sacrificing volumetric accuracy.

在一个于 0.064T 和 3T 下成像的健康队列（n=19）中，我们的方法在体积准确性上与领先的通用回归方法相当，同时还能生成体素级的不确定性图，以突出显示解剖结构不明确的区域。此外，在针对认知障碍参与者的试点数据集（n=11）中，与健康先验归一化不同，该方法保留了与疾病相关的萎缩特征。我们的框架在不牺牲体积准确性的前提下，将全脑后验采样引入了低场超分辨率领域。
