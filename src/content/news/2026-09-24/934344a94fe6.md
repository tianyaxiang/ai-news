# Brain-Inspired Hierarchical Modularity for General Continual Learning
# 受大脑启发的通用持续学习分层模块化方法

**Abstract:** Continual learning, the ability to learn from sequential experience while retaining and adapting prior knowledge, is central to intelligent systems operating in changing environments. However, conventional continual learning is typically studied with offline task-wise training and clear task boundaries, leaving a substantial gap from general continual learning under online, uncertain, and evolving data streams.

**摘要：** 持续学习是指在从连续经验中学习的同时，保留并适应先前知识的能力，这对在不断变化的环境中运行的智能系统至关重要。然而，传统的持续学习研究通常基于离线任务训练和明确的任务边界，这与在线、不确定且不断演变的数据流下的通用持续学习之间存在巨大差距。

In this regime, intelligent systems must separate conflicting experience to reduce interference while integrating compatible experience to promote generalization. Inspired by the organization of the Drosophila learning and memory system, we identify a hierarchical modular principle that coordinates both functions through expert specialization and ensemble integration.

在这种模式下，智能系统必须分离冲突的经验以减少干扰，同时整合兼容的经验以促进泛化。受果蝇学习和记忆系统组织的启发，我们确定了一种分层模块化原则，该原则通过专家专业化和集成整合来协调这两种功能。

We instantiate this principle as lightweight modular adaptation of pretrained foundation models, combining brain-inspired random expansion for expert routing and diversified modular integration across spatial and temporal scales.

我们将这一原则实例化为预训练基础模型的轻量级模块化适配，结合了受大脑启发的专家路由随机扩展，以及跨空间和时间尺度的多样化模块集成。

Across visual recognition, vision-language understanding, ego-exo video understanding, and embodied vision-language-action learning, our method consistently improves learning under online and uncertain data streams, with gains exceeding 50 percentage points over replay-free alternatives in embodied manipulation. These findings support hierarchical modularity as a biologically grounded path for learning from dynamic experience.

在视觉识别、视觉-语言理解、第一/第三人称视角视频理解以及具身视觉-语言-动作学习等任务中，我们的方法在在线和不确定数据流下持续提升了学习效果，在具身操作任务中，其性能比无重放（replay-free）的替代方案提升了超过 50 个百分点。这些发现支持分层模块化作为一种基于生物学原理的、从动态经验中学习的有效路径。
