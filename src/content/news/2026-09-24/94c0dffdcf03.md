# Meta Connect 2026 live blog: On the ground at Mark Zuckerberg’s next big product launch
# Meta Connect 2026 直播博客：马克·扎克伯格下一场重大产品发布会现场直击

It’s time once again for Meta’s annual September product launch event, and The Verge is on the ground in Menlo Park to cover the show live. Meta says that today’s keynote by Mark Zuckerberg will be about how “Meta is building a future for everyone” — something he wrote at length about in a recent essay about AI, smart glasses, and more. That means we can expect more news from Zuckerberg and his team on those topics; perhaps we’ll hear more about Meta’s recently launched Muse AI agent and new glasses hardware.

又到了 Meta 一年一度的九月产品发布会时间，《The Verge》已抵达门洛帕克现场进行实时报道。Meta 表示，马克·扎克伯格今天的主题演讲将围绕“Meta 如何为每个人构建未来”展开——这与他近期关于人工智能、智能眼镜等主题的文章内容相呼应。这意味着我们可以期待扎克伯格及其团队在这些领域发布更多消息；或许我们还会听到更多关于 Meta 最近推出的 Muse AI 智能体以及新款眼镜硬件的信息。

Whatever Zuckerberg and Meta have to say about smart glasses will be particularly notable. The company is dealing with an outpouring of privacy and harassment concerns stemming from the product line’s built-in camera, which has led to them being derided as “pervert glasses.” Meta has already tried to tackle that reputation with recent privacy fixes and a new marketing campaign, but it could introduce more updates on the Connect stage.

无论扎克伯格和 Meta 对智能眼镜有何表态，都将备受关注。该公司目前正面临因产品内置摄像头而引发的隐私和骚扰担忧，这些担忧导致其产品被嘲讽为“变态眼镜”。Meta 此前已尝试通过近期的隐私修复措施和新的营销活动来改善这一形象，但他们可能会在 Connect 大会上推出更多更新。

The show is scheduled to begin at 7PM ET / 4PM PT, and you can follow our live updates here.
