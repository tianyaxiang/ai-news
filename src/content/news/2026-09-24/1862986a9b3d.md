# Google Beam expands with new regions, partners, and customers
# Google Beam 扩展业务：新增多个地区、合作伙伴与客户

We’re expanding Google Beam to five new countries, partnering with Industrious for an extended network, and proving our impact at Google and beyond.
我们正在将 Google Beam 扩展至五个新国家，与 Industrious 合作建立扩展网络，并验证其在 Google 及其他领域的影响力。

Since its early days, Google Beam has been driven by a singular vision: bringing people together so they can experience genuine, face-to-face connection, no matter the distance. We’ve tested Beam with early users, including in our own offices, and shown how it can help transform everyday meetings into authentic moments of connection. Today, we’re announcing a major expansion of our global footprint, new data on our proven impact, and the launch of our first-ever extended network.
自创立之初，Google Beam 就秉持着一个愿景：将人们联系在一起，无论距离多远，都能体验到真实的面对面交流。我们已在早期用户（包括我们自己的办公室）中测试了 Beam，并证明了它如何将日常会议转化为真实的连接时刻。今天，我们宣布全球业务的大幅扩张，发布关于其影响力的最新数据，并推出首个扩展网络。

### Expanding our global footprint
### 扩展全球版图

We are officially expanding our global reach, with Google Beam units shipping to customers across six countries, including the U.S., Canada, U.K., France, Germany, and Japan. Backing this rollout is a robust, global ecosystem of 18 channel partners who are fully equipped to deploy and support Beam. Delivered by our flagship hardware partner as HP Dimension with Google Beam, this experience is built for modern enterprise collaboration. By integrating seamlessly with both Google Meet and Zoom, Beam can provide the flexibility organizations need while helping to elevate everyday conversations.
我们正式扩大了全球业务范围，Google Beam 设备现已向包括美国、加拿大、英国、法国、德国和日本在内的六个国家的客户发货。此次推广得到了由 18 个渠道合作伙伴组成的强大全球生态系统的支持，他们已完全具备部署和支持 Beam 的能力。该体验由我们的旗舰硬件合作伙伴以“HP Dimension with Google Beam”的形式提供，专为现代企业协作而打造。通过与 Google Meet 和 Zoom 的无缝集成，Beam 不仅能提供组织所需的灵活性，还能提升日常沟通的质量。

### Proving our real-world impact
### 验证实际影响力

Through extensive internal testing at Google, we’ve learned how transformative Beam can be for areas like recruiting, talent development, and cross-functional collaboration. In a recent eight-week internal study, Google teams using Beam felt 50% more connected to one another, found it 33% easier to ensure their feedback was clearly understood, and saw a 21% drop in the need for follow-up meetings. All of this has reinforced our own belief in expanding our own deployment of Beam for Googlers, alongside our customers.
通过在 Google 内部进行的广泛测试，我们了解到 Beam 在招聘、人才发展和跨职能协作等领域具有变革性意义。在最近一项为期八周的内部研究中，使用 Beam 的 Google 团队成员之间的连接感提升了 50%，反馈被清晰理解的难度降低了 33%，后续跟进会议的需求减少了 21%。这一切都坚定了我们为员工及客户进一步推广 Beam 的信心。

We are also working closely with leaned-in partners to prove out these exact use cases in the field. Earlier this year, we partnered with Bain and Company to test how HP Dimension with Google Beam could help transform their campus recruiting and candidate interviews. By using this technology, Bain was able to create authentic, face-to-face connections with candidates from anywhere—without the need for extensive travel.
我们还与积极的合作伙伴密切合作，在实际场景中验证这些用例。今年早些时候，我们与贝恩公司（Bain and Company）合作，测试了 HP Dimension with Google Beam 如何帮助其转型校园招聘和候选人面试。通过使用这项技术，贝恩公司无需长途跋涉，就能与身处各地的候选人建立真实的面对面联系。

### Creating the first-ever Beam extended network
### 创建首个 Beam 扩展网络

To help make Beam accessible to organizations of all sizes, we are partnering with Industrious, a global operator of premium flexible workspaces worldwide. Together, we are creating the first-ever Beam extended network.
为了让各种规模的组织都能使用 Beam，我们正与全球高端灵活办公空间运营商 Industrious 合作，共同打造首个 Beam 扩展网络。

We conducted a pilot earlier this year, in which we heard firsthand how distributed teams are connecting for manager check-ins, interviews, and client consultations. Now, this network will increase access to Google Beam, opening the doors for more startups and businesses of all sizes to better connect and make their meetings feel much more natural.
今年早些时候，我们进行了试点项目，亲耳听取了分布式团队如何利用该技术进行经理沟通、面试和客户咨询。现在，该网络将扩大 Google Beam 的覆盖范围，为更多初创企业和各种规模的公司提供便利，帮助他们更好地建立联系，让会议体验更加自然。

Starting in October, you can book and experience HP Dimension with Google Beam directly at select Industrious locations across the U.S., including Atlanta, Chicago, New York City and Palo Alto.
从 10 月开始，您可以直接在美国部分 Industrious 办公地点（包括亚特兰大、芝加哥、纽约和帕洛阿尔托）预订并体验 HP Dimension with Google Beam。
