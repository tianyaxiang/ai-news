# Muse is coming to Meta smart glasses
# Muse 即将登陆 Meta 智能眼镜

Just a couple of weeks after launching Muse, Meta announced that it’s “working on” bringing the agent to its smart glasses, including the new glasses it unveiled at Meta Connect. Users will be able to activate Muse from their glasses by saying its name and ask it to handle tasks like guiding a workout, logging meals, or helping them shop for products they’re looking at.

在 Muse 发布仅几周后，Meta 宣布正致力于将其智能助手引入智能眼镜，包括在 Meta Connect 大会上发布的新款眼镜。用户只需呼唤 Muse 的名字即可激活它，并要求它处理各种任务，例如指导健身、记录饮食，或协助选购眼前看到的商品。

Muse is just one of several major updates coming to Meta’s glasses. They’re also getting some new accessibility features, like a Hearing Enhancement feature Meta says is “FDA-cleared software for adults with perceived mild to moderate hearing loss.” It will be available later this year with a Meta One subscription or as a $149 add-on for compatible glasses. Meta AI will also be able to give “more context-rich answers” when users ask the glasses what they’re looking at, potentially making Meta’s glasses more effective for helping blind and low-vision users interpret their surroundings. A new audio customization feature will also allow users to shift their smart glasses’ volume between their left and right ears, including sending all audio to one side.

Muse 只是 Meta 眼镜即将迎来的多项重大更新之一。眼镜还将获得一些新的辅助功能，例如“听力增强”（Hearing Enhancement）功能，Meta 表示这是“一款经 FDA 批准的软件，适用于有轻度至中度听力损失的成年人”。该功能将于今年晚些时候推出，用户可通过 Meta One 订阅获取，或以 149 美元的价格为兼容眼镜购买附加组件。当用户询问眼镜眼前看到的是什么时，Meta AI 也将能够提供“更具语境的回答”，这有望使 Meta 眼镜在帮助盲人和视障用户解读周围环境方面更加有效。此外，一项新的音频自定义功能将允许用户调节智能眼镜左右耳的音量，甚至可以将所有音频输出到单侧。

Meta’s introducing some new audio and photo features as well, including Dolby Atmos Capture, which will allow users to record videos with 360-degree spatial audio. A new Dynamic Photo feature automatically captures multiple frames when you take a photo with Meta’s glasses, then uses Meta AI to suggest the best frame from each batch.

Meta 还引入了一些新的音频和照片功能，包括杜比全景声捕捉（Dolby Atmos Capture），它允许用户录制带有 360 度空间音频的视频。全新的“动态照片”（Dynamic Photo）功能会在用户使用 Meta 眼镜拍照时自动捕捉多帧画面，然后利用 Meta AI 从中推荐最佳照片。

The Meta Ray-Ban Display glasses are getting the new “Hologram” feature Meta announced for its new VR Glasses, which it says is “a photorealistic digital version of your face for calls from Meta Ray-Ban Display” that “infers” your expression using your voice. The feature’s “rolling out in Early Access on WhatsApp to Meta Ray-Ban Display users in the US later this fall.”

Meta Ray-Ban Display 眼镜将获得 Meta 为其新款 VR 眼镜宣布的“全息图”（Hologram）功能。Meta 称这是“一种用于 Meta Ray-Ban Display 通话的逼真数字面部形象”，它能通过语音“推断”用户的表情。该功能将于今年秋季晚些时候以抢先体验版形式在 WhatsApp 上向美国地区的 Meta Ray-Ban Display 用户推出。

A couple new heads-up navigation features are coming to the Meta Ray-Ban Display as well. Starting later this month, users in the US and Canada will have an option to use their voice to ask for real-time cycling or public transit directions. The Meta Ray-Ban Display will also start using “the world in front of you rather than street signs in turn-by-turn walking directions,” like referencing a house, car, or landmark the glasses detect. A few other new features are on the way for the Display glasses, including an option for editing photos directly on the glasses’ display, a new “Explore” app for finding new apps, an improved calendar, and mirroring for phone notifications.

Meta Ray-Ban Display 还将迎来几项新的抬头导航功能。从本月晚些时候开始，美国和加拿大的用户将可以选择通过语音查询实时的骑行或公共交通路线。Meta Ray-Ban Display 还将开始在步行导航中“使用眼前的现实世界而非路标”，例如参考眼镜检测到的房屋、汽车或地标。Display 眼镜还将迎来其他几项新功能，包括直接在眼镜显示屏上编辑照片的选项、用于发现新应用的“探索”（Explore）应用、改进后的日历，以及手机通知镜像功能。
