# The Best Samsung Phones of 2026: Ultra, Fold, Budget
# 2026年最佳三星手机：Ultra、Fold 及入门机型

There's the iPhone, and then there are Samsung Galaxy phones—the two most popular phone brands in the US. But whereas Apple is the sole company behind the iOS mobile operating system, even the best Samsung phones face stiff competition in the Android world. (And yes, Galaxy phones are Android phones, though Samsung calls its layer over Android “One UI.”)

市面上主要分为 iPhone 和三星 Galaxy 手机，这是美国最受欢迎的两个手机品牌。但与苹果作为 iOS 移动操作系统的唯一开发者不同，即使是最好的三星手机在安卓阵营中也面临着激烈的竞争。（没错，Galaxy 手机就是安卓手机，尽管三星将其在安卓系统之上的界面称为“One UI”。）

Google's Pixel phones are our favorite Android devices, but Samsung's offerings are just as great and often have better hardware. Alongside Google, Samsung promises the longest software support for its cheap and high-end devices. That means you don't have to upgrade as often, since it will stay up-to-date, secure, and bug-free longer. But the Korean giant has many, many models at varying prices, so what's the best Samsung phone? We sort it out here in this guide. We also have tips and tricks to get the most out of your shiny new hardware.

谷歌的 Pixel 手机是我们最喜欢的安卓设备，但三星的产品同样出色，且往往拥有更好的硬件。与谷歌一样，三星为其廉价和高端设备承诺了最长的软件支持。这意味着你无需频繁更换手机，因为它们能更长时间地保持系统更新、安全且无漏洞。但这家韩国巨头拥有众多不同价位的机型，究竟哪款三星手机最好？我们在本指南中为您梳理。我们还提供了一些技巧，帮助您充分利用崭新的硬件。

### Why Buy Samsung?
### 为什么要购买三星手机？

The best part of the Android world is the choice of hardware. Want a phone with a headphone jack? Sure! Want a phone that fast-charges in around 30 minutes? It exists. So why stick with the stalwart Samsung?

安卓世界最棒的地方在于硬件选择的多样性。想要带耳机孔的手机？没问题！想要 30 分钟左右就能充满电的手机？也有。那么，为什么要坚持选择老牌的三星呢？

In our experience and research, Samsung's smartphones have a track record of reliability, and because it's a major player, it has a wider network of authorized repair centers than competing Android manufacturers. Yes, there was the Galaxy Note 7 fiasco and even the issues with the first-gen Galaxy Fold, but Samsung now has a customer satisfaction score equal to Apple's, according to the American Customer Satisfaction Index. That said, we haven't read great things about the company's US customer support team.

根据我们的经验和研究，三星智能手机在可靠性方面有着良好的记录。作为行业巨头，它拥有比其他安卓竞争对手更广泛的授权维修中心网络。诚然，三星曾经历过 Galaxy Note 7 的惨败，甚至第一代 Galaxy Fold 也出现过问题，但根据美国顾客满意度指数（ACSI），三星目前的客户满意度已与苹果持平。话虽如此，我们对该公司美国客户支持团队的评价并不高。

### Is Now the Best Time to Buy?
### 现在是购买的最佳时机吗？

Samsung announced its new foldable range in August, and it won't upgrade the S-series until next year. If you're after the best Samsung phone, now's a good time to buy. But keep the company's launch time frames in mind if you want the latest and greatest.

三星在 8 月发布了其新款折叠屏系列，而 S 系列要到明年才会更新。如果您正在寻找最好的三星手机，现在是一个不错的购买时机。但如果您想要追求最新、最强的机型，请务必留意该公司的发布时间表。

The flagship Galaxy S series launches at the start of the year during Samsung's Galaxy Unpacked event. The Galaxy S26 series went on sale March 11. The budget Galaxy A series typically arrives in the spring. The folding Galaxy Z series always lands in the summer, and 2026's event was in August. The midrange Galaxy FE series is the “Fan Edition,” but this is more or less a slightly cheaper variant of the Galaxy S-series. The Galaxy S26 FE was just released this month (September 2026).

旗舰级 Galaxy S 系列通常在年初的三星 Galaxy Unpacked 发布会上亮相。Galaxy S26 系列于 3 月 11 日开售。入门级 Galaxy A 系列通常在春季推出。折叠屏 Galaxy Z 系列总是在夏季发布，2026 年的发布会是在 8 月。中端 Galaxy FE 系列是“粉丝版”，本质上是 Galaxy S 系列的平价变体。Galaxy S26 FE 刚刚在本月（2026 年 9 月）发布。

### How Long Are Samsung Phones Supported?
### 三星手机的软件支持周期有多长？

Android phones used to have disastrously poor software support. Two years after you bought your $1,000 phone, it would no longer receive software updates. Thankfully, Google began promising longer software update cycles for its Pixel phones, which pushed Samsung to match it.

过去，安卓手机的软件支持非常糟糕。你花 1000 美元买的手机，两年后就收不到软件更新了。值得庆幸的是，谷歌开始为其 Pixel 手机承诺更长的软件更新周期，这促使三星也跟进了这一政策。

The following Samsung phones will receive seven years of software updates from their release date, which includes Android operating system updates and monthly security patches. The latter keeps your phone secure, and new OS versions can add new features.

以下三星手机将从发布之日起获得七年的软件更新，包括安卓操作系统更新和每月安全补丁。后者能确保您的手机安全，而新的操作系统版本则能带来新功能。

*   **Galaxy S26 FE, S26, Galaxy S26+, Galaxy S26 Ultra**
*   **Galaxy S25 FE, Galaxy S25, Galaxy S25+, Galaxy S25 Edge, and Galaxy S25 Ultra**
*   **Galaxy S24 FE, Galaxy S24, Galaxy S24+, and Galaxy S24 Ultra**
*   **Galaxy Z Fold8, Z Fold8 Ultra, and Z Flip8**
*   **Galaxy Z Fold7, Z Flip7, Z Flip7 FE, Galaxy Z Fold6, and Z Flip6**

Samsung's Galaxy A-series phones, from the A16 and up, will receive six years of updates. Most other older Samsung devices, like some in the Galaxy A-series or older flagships, will receive four years of OS updates and five years of security patches starting from the release date.

三星 Galaxy A 系列手机（A16 及以上机型）将获得六年的更新。大多数其他较旧的三星设备（如部分 Galaxy A 系列或更早的旗舰机型）将从发布之日起获得四年的操作系统更新和五年的安全补丁。

### Why We Recommend Buying Unlocked
### 为什么我们建议购买无锁版？

Most people buy phones directly from their carrier, but if you're reading this guide, we suggest you buy an unlocked smartphone. When you buy a carrier phone, it's “locked” to that network. That means if you want to switch carriers, like moving from Verizon to T-Mobile, you'll have to ask your carrier to unlock the device, and this can be a huge headache.

大多数人直接从运营商处购买手机，但如果您正在阅读本指南，我们建议您购买无锁版智能手机。当您购买运营商定制机时，它会被“锁定”在该网络上。这意味着如果您想更换运营商（例如从 Verizon 换到 T-Mobile），您必须要求运营商解锁设备，这可能会非常麻烦。
