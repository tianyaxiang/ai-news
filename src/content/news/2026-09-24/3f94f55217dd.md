**COURTESY OF PAINCHEK: AI is changing how we quantify pain**
**图片来源：PAINCHEK：人工智能正在改变我们量化疼痛的方式**

At Orchard Care Homes, nurses used to rely on an observational scale to assess pain in residents who couldn’t communicate verbally. But agitated residents were sometimes assumed to have behavioral issues, while their pain went untreated. Then, in 2021, the care-home chain began trialing PainChek, a smartphone app that scans a resident’s face for microscopic muscle movements and uses AI to output a pain score. Within weeks, the pilot unit saw fewer prescriptions and calmer corridors. Now researchers are racing to turn pain into something a camera or sensor can score as reliably as blood pressure. But when algorithms measure our suffering, does that change how we understand and treat it? Discover how AI is changing the way we assess pain. —Deena Mousa
在 Orchard 养老院，护士过去依靠观察量表来评估无法进行语言交流的住户的疼痛程度。但焦躁不安的住户有时会被误认为是行为问题，而他们的疼痛却未得到治疗。2021 年，该养老院连锁机构开始试用 PainChek，这是一款智能手机应用程序，可以通过扫描住户面部的微小肌肉运动，并利用人工智能输出疼痛评分。几周内，试点单位的处方药用量减少，走廊也变得更加安静。现在，研究人员正竞相将疼痛转化为一种摄像头或传感器可以像测量血压一样可靠地评分的指标。但是，当算法衡量我们的痛苦时，这是否会改变我们理解和治疗痛苦的方式？了解人工智能如何改变我们评估疼痛的方式。——Deena Mousa
