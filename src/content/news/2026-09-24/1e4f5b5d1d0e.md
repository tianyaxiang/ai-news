# Stable Unsupervised Continual Chunking with Sheaf SyncMap
# 基于层束同步映射（Sheaf SyncMap）的稳定无监督持续分块

**Abstract:** Unsupervised Continual chunking is a fundamental problem in machine learning and neuroscience, where the goal is to identify groups of states that frequently co-occur in temporal sequences. A key challenge is to form accurate chunks while maintaining their stability over time.

**摘要：** 无监督持续分块（Unsupervised Continual chunking）是机器学习和神经科学中的一个基础问题，其目标是识别在时间序列中频繁共同出现的状态组。一个核心挑战在于如何在形成准确分块的同时，保持其随时间推移的稳定性。

In this work, we propose sheaf regularization to reduce local inconsistencies in Decentralized SyncMap, a self-organizing system, and thereby stabilize its chunking dynamics. We introduce a radial sheaf structure that penalizes distance-dependent radial motion between pairs of variables.

在这项工作中，我们提出了层束正则化（sheaf regularization）方法，旨在减少去中心化同步映射（Decentralized SyncMap，一种自组织系统）中的局部不一致性，从而稳定其分块动力学。我们引入了一种径向层束结构，用于惩罚变量对之间与距离相关的径向运动。

Experimental results show that the proposed method achieves the highest normalized mutual information (NMI) among the evaluated SyncMap variants on 12 of 18 probabilistic Continual General Chunking Problem (CGCP) graphs with two-state memory and on 17 of 18 graphs with dynamic memory.

实验结果表明，在 18 个具有双状态记忆的概率持续通用分块问题（CGCP）图中，该方法在 12 个图中达到了所评估 SyncMap 变体中的最高归一化互信息（NMI）；而在具有动态记忆的 18 个图中，该方法在 17 个图中表现最优。

In the sequential adaptation experiment, Sheaf SyncMap also achieves high NMI after shifts in the input distribution, indicating that it can adapt to new knowledge while avoiding the negative transfer commonly observed in modern machine learning systems such as neural networks.

在序列适应实验中，Sheaf SyncMap 在输入分布发生偏移后依然保持了较高的 NMI，这表明它能够适应新知识，同时避免了在神经网络等现代机器学习系统中常见的负迁移现象。
