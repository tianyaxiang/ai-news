# The first broken iPhone Duos are here

首批损坏的 iPhone Duo 已经出现。

Now that the first folding iPhone has launched, we’ll find out quickly what can go wrong.

随着首款折叠屏 iPhone 的发布，我们很快就会发现它会出现什么问题。

The iPhone Duo officially goes on sale today, but a few units already have broken screens. According to posts on Reddit about the affected units, the issue appears to be happening at a similar spot on all of them: Some kind of problem shows up on the inner screen along the hinge and a strange bright line (or lines) across the right side of the display.

iPhone Duo 今日正式开售，但已有少量设备出现了屏幕破损。根据 Reddit 上关于受影响设备的帖子，所有设备的问题似乎都出现在相似的位置：内屏铰链处出现某种故障，且显示屏右侧出现了一条（或多条）奇怪的亮线。

Here’s how one user in Australia described what happened to their phone: Had my iPhone Duo for about six hours and had probably opened it fewer than 20 times. I was showing it to a friend, and as I opened it, I felt something around the internal hinge, underneath the screen. As I continued opening it, I heard an audible crack, and the screen broke right along the hinge. There were no marks or damage on the inner screen’s surface or the external hinge to suggest anything had been caught or damaged.

以下是一位澳大利亚用户对其手机故障的描述：我的 iPhone Duo 用了大约六个小时，打开次数可能不到 20 次。当时我正向朋友展示它，打开时感觉到内部铰链、屏幕下方有什么东西。继续打开时，我听到了一声明显的碎裂声，屏幕就在铰链处破裂了。内屏表面或外部铰链上没有任何痕迹或损坏，无法看出是否有东西被卡住或受损。

The user said that the Apple Store replaced their phone with “a brand-new one on the spot.” A post showing an image of another broken screen includes a video in a comment demonstrating yet another. And a separate Reddit thread collected other pictures of affected Duos.

该用户表示，Apple Store “当场为其更换了一部全新的手机”。一篇展示另一块破损屏幕图片的帖子在评论中附带了一段视频，展示了又一起案例。此外，另一个 Reddit 帖子收集了更多受影响 iPhone Duo 的照片。

Apple didn’t immediately reply to a request for comment.

苹果公司未立即回复置评请求。
