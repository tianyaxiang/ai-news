Now Google says it might use your Gmail and Google Photos data to target ads. According to the new terms, sharing your data for better AI recommendations also means opening it up for more personalized ads.

谷歌现在表示，它可能会使用你的 Gmail 和 Google Photos 数据来投放定向广告。根据新条款，为了获得更好的 AI 推荐而共享数据，也意味着这些数据将被用于更具针对性的个性化广告。

Google has updated its privacy policy in a way that could mean you’ll start seeing targeted ads based on your personal data if you’re using personalized AI features. The update, spotted by Android Authority, adds an exception to its promise about not using your Drive, Gmail, or Google Photos for personalized ads.

谷歌更新了其隐私政策，这意味着如果你正在使用个性化 AI 功能，你可能会开始看到基于个人数据的定向广告。据 Android Authority 报道，此次更新在其关于不使用 Drive、Gmail 或 Google Photos 进行个性化广告的承诺中增加了一个例外条款。

While the previous version simply said it wouldn’t do that, as of October 16th, Google has added the line, “unless you choose to make data from these services available to other Google services that support personalized ads.”

虽然之前的版本只是简单地声明不会这样做，但自 10 月 16 日起，谷歌增加了一行文字：“除非你选择将这些服务中的数据提供给支持个性化广告的其他谷歌服务。”

That means anyone who has connected their Drive, Gmail, or Google Photos to AI Mode and other Search tools via Google’s Personal Intelligence feature — allowing those tools to provide better responses or recommendations by knowing more about you — may now see ads targeted on the basis of that content.

这意味着任何通过谷歌“个人智能”（Personal Intelligence）功能将 Drive、Gmail 或 Google Photos 连接到 AI 模式及其他搜索工具的用户——即允许这些工具通过了解更多关于你的信息来提供更好的回复或建议——现在可能会看到基于这些内容投放的定向广告。

Advertisers don’t get access to your Drive, Gmail, and Photos information or personally identifying details such as your name or email address, and Google won’t show you ads based on sensitive categories like race, sexual orientation, religion, or health.

广告商无法访问你的 Drive、Gmail 和 Photos 信息，也无法获取你的姓名或电子邮件地址等个人身份信息；同时，谷歌也不会基于种族、性取向、宗教或健康等敏感类别向你展示广告。

If you want to prevent your personal data from being used for targeted ads, you can choose which apps you want to disconnect from Search’s Personal Intelligence features or disable Personalized Recommendations entirely via the Search Services Personalization settings page.

如果你想防止个人数据被用于定向广告，可以通过“搜索服务个性化”设置页面，选择断开哪些应用与搜索“个人智能”功能的连接，或者完全禁用个性化推荐。
