With collaborators at the University of Pennsylvania, we developed LitPivot, a system that helps researchers navigate these pivots. Using papers retrieved through Asta Paper Finder, LitPivot organizes literature around the part of a proposal being edited, offers critiques supported by excerpts from selected studies, and suggests revisions that researchers can accept, edit, or reject. LitPivot is intended to be used for early-stage research ideation, but the idea could possibly extend to longer writing tasks (i.e., writing the introduction section of a paper).

我们与宾夕法尼亚大学的合作者共同开发了 LitPivot，这是一个帮助研究人员应对这些转向的系统。LitPivot 使用通过 Asta Paper Finder 检索到的论文，围绕正在编辑的提案部分组织文献，提供由选定研究摘录支持的评论，并提出研究人员可以接受、编辑或拒绝的修改建议。LitPivot 旨在用于早期研究构思，但该理念可能扩展到更长的写作任务（例如，撰写论文的引言部分）。

In a study with 17 researchers, domain experts rated research ideas developed with LitPivot as better grounded in the literature than those developed with a text editor and an AI chat tool. Participants also perceived their revised research ideas as more novel and useful, and reported a stronger understanding of the surrounding literature. Why pivoting is hard Research ideas evolve through engaging with the literature. In our analysis of meeting notes from four completed computer science research projects, we identified 21 literature-initiated pivots across all four projects. These fell into two types: differentiating an idea from existing work to establish an idea’s novelty, and aligning an idea with existing work to build on it. This project itself went through two pivots before we settled on its contributions.

为什么转向很困难？研究构思是通过与文献的互动而演变的。在对四个已完成的计算机科学研究项目的会议记录进行分析时，我们在所有四个项目中确定了 21 次文献驱动的转向。这些转向分为两类：将构思与现有工作区分开来以确立其新颖性，以及将构思与现有工作保持一致以在此基础上进行构建。本项目在确定其贡献之前也经历了两次转向。

These changes can be difficult to navigate. In a formative study, five researchers developed proposals using a text editor and standard search tools. They struggled to develop their ideas while critically evaluating them against the literature. When a paper challenged an idea’s novelty, they wanted concrete suggestions for how to revise it, supported by prior work. These findings shaped LitPivot’s assessments, which pair literature-supported critiques with specific suggestions for revising the selected part of an idea.

这些变化可能难以驾驭。在一项形成性研究中，五名研究人员使用文本编辑器和标准搜索工具撰写了提案。他们在发展构思的同时，难以根据文献对其进行批判性评估。当一篇论文对构思的新颖性提出质疑时，他们希望得到关于如何修改的具体建议，并有前人工作作为支持。这些发现塑造了 LitPivot 的评估机制，该机制将文献支持的评论与修改构思选定部分的具体建议相结合。

How LitPivot works In LitPivot, a researcher starts by writing a short proposal in an editor and enabling what we call facet highlighting, which segments an articulated idea into different facets (i.e., a problem, contribution, and evaluation). To check a proposed facet, the researcher clicks that segment in the draft. An adjacent panel groups related papers based on the selected facet (e.g., grouping papers by the evaluation methods they use when the evaluation facet is selected) and highlights the most relevant groups.

LitPivot 的工作原理 在 LitPivot 中，研究人员首先在编辑器中撰写一份简短的提案，并启用我们所谓的“切面高亮”（facet highlighting），它将表达出的构思分割成不同的切面（即问题、贡献和评估）。为了检查某个切面，研究人员点击草稿中的该部分。相邻的面板会根据所选切面将相关论文进行分组（例如，当选择评估切面时，按论文使用的评估方法进行分组），并突出显示最相关的组。

The researcher selects papers, requests an assessment, and reviews a suggested revision alongside excerpts supporting the critique. The literature is then reranked based on relevance to the revised facet. In particular, LitPivot separates a research idea into its problem, proposed contribution, and evaluation plan. Each facet has its own set and relevance ranking of related literature. LitPivot groups retrieved papers around the facet being edited, letting the researcher choose which ones the system should use to assess the draft.

研究人员选择论文、请求评估，并查看建议的修改内容以及支持该评论的摘录。随后，文献会根据与修改后切面的相关性进行重新排序。具体而言，LitPivot 将研究构思拆分为问题、拟议贡献和评估计划。每个切面都有其自己的一套相关文献及其相关性排名。LitPivot 围绕正在编辑的切面组织检索到的论文，让研究人员选择系统应使用哪些论文来评估草稿。

Alongside scientific-writing environments such as Prism and agentic assistants such as Claude Code, LitPivot explores a new workflow for developing an early research idea: choosing literature to assess it against, using that evidence to revise it, and reconsidering which papers matter as the idea changes. Its paper groups and linked critiques make those steps explicit for each part of the proposal, while researchers control both the draft and the evidence used to assess it.

除了像 Prism 这样的科学写作环境和像 Claude Code 这样的代理助手外，LitPivot 还探索了一种开发早期研究构思的新工作流程：选择文献进行评估，利用这些证据进行修改，并随着构思的变化重新考虑哪些论文更为重要。其论文分组和关联评论使提案的每个部分的这些步骤变得明确，同时研究人员可以控制草稿以及用于评估草稿的证据。

For a problem statement, LitPivot looks for evidence that the problem exists and reasons it matters. To assess a proposed contribution, it compares what the selected papers have already addressed with the limitations and future work they describe, looking for remaining gaps. For an evaluation plan, it checks whether the proposed tests address the research question and how similar work has been evaluated. The system then suggests a rewrite with supporting quotes attached.

对于问题陈述，LitPivot 会寻找问题存在及其重要性的证据。为了评估拟议的贡献，它会将选定论文已经解决的问题与它们所描述的局限性和未来工作进行比较，寻找剩余的空白。对于评估计划，它会检查拟议的测试是否解决了研究问题，以及类似的工作是如何进行评估的。然后，系统会建议进行重写，并附上支持性的引用。

Changing one facet of an idea can leave another out of date. LitPivot flags passages that may need revision, such as an evaluation plan that no longer adequately evaluates a revised contribution. Researchers can accept, edit, or reject suggestions, and request new papers or refresh the literature ranking as their proposal develops. Paraphrased example from one study participant.

改变构思的一个切面可能会导致另一个切面过时。LitPivot 会标记出可能需要修改的段落，例如不再能充分评估已修改贡献的评估计划。研究人员可以接受、编辑或拒绝建议，并随着提案的发展请求新的论文或刷新文献排名。来自一位研究参与者的改写示例。

Our findings In our lab study, 17 researchers each developed one assigned idea with LitPivot and another with a baseline tool—a text editor and an AI tool for asking questions about selected papers. Both conditions used the same collection of papers. Two domain experts, unaware of which system had been used, rated the resulting proposals on a single criterion: how well they used relevant literature to support and justify the idea.

我们的研究结果 在我们的实验室研究中，17 名研究人员分别使用 LitPivot 和基准工具（文本编辑器和用于询问所选论文问题的 AI 工具）各开发了一个指定的构思。两种情况使用了相同的论文集。两名不知道使用了哪种系统的领域专家根据单一标准对最终提案进行了评分：即它们在多大程度上使用了相关文献来支持和证明该构思。

Proposals developed with LitPivot received higher ratings. Participants in the same study also reported a stronger understanding of the surrounding literature and greater self-reported improvements in their ideas’ novelty and usefulness with LitPivot than with the baseline.
