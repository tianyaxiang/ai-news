### TDM Neo Hybrid Headphones Review: Two-in-One Woes

TDM Neo 混合耳机评测：二合一的烦恼

Unveiled at CES 2026, the TDM Neo is a pair of on-ear headphones that transform into a Bluetooth speaker. It caught the imagination of the attending media, won a handful of awards, and inspired countless column inches.

TDM Neo 在 2026 年国际消费电子展（CES）上首次亮相，是一款可以变身为蓝牙音箱的压耳式耳机。它吸引了在场媒体的想象力，赢得了多个奖项，并引发了无数篇幅的报道。

Given the current state of the portable audio industry, it's easy to understand why. Most designs seem to revolve around incremental updates and new colorways rather than genuine technical innovation. Neo is an exciting alternative, and it’s little wonder the startup brand went on to smash its Kickstarter targets.

考虑到便携音频行业的现状，这不难理解。大多数设计似乎都围绕着渐进式更新和新配色，而非真正的技术创新。Neo 是一个令人兴奋的替代品，难怪这家初创品牌后来在 Kickstarter 上的众筹目标被一举打破。

I’ve spent the past couple of weeks listening to the headphones and Bluetooth speaker, and have lost count of the number of times I’ve showed off the clever twist-and-fold mechanism to friends. But has TDM managed to produce a genuine tech twofer, or designed a novelty product that gives you the worst of both worlds?

在过去几周里，我一直在使用这款耳机和蓝牙音箱，我已经数不清多少次向朋友展示它那巧妙的扭转折叠机制了。但 TDM 究竟是打造出了一款真正的二合一科技产品，还是设计出了一款让你体验到“两头都不讨好”的新奇玩意儿？

The twist-and-fold mechanism on the Neo is clever and addictive. Rotate the ear cups around the cushioned but flexible headband, bring them together, and strong magnets snap everything firmly in place. Once connected, the headphone drivers disable, and the outward-facing Bluetooth speakers kick in. Twist them apart and you've got your headphones back.

Neo 的扭转折叠机制既巧妙又让人上瘾。绕着带有衬垫但灵活的头梁旋转耳罩，将它们合在一起，强力磁铁就会将一切牢牢固定。连接后，耳机驱动单元会禁用，外向的蓝牙扬声器随即启动。将它们扭开，你就又变回了耳机模式。

It’s innovative, and the engineering is impressive. Once in speaker mode, the two rubber TDM logos sit closely together and form a protective base, while the asymmetrical twisted headband cocoons the ear cups together. It works well—it never once popped open accidentally. The action is smooth, the connection reassuringly solid, and switching between modes works exactly as intended.

这很有创新性，工程设计也令人印象深刻。进入音箱模式后，两个橡胶材质的 TDM 标志紧贴在一起形成一个保护底座，而不对称的扭转头梁则将耳罩包裹在一起。它的效果很好——从未发生过意外弹开的情况。操作顺滑，连接稳固得令人放心，模式切换也完全符合预期。

The Neo has four 40-mm drivers—two for the headphones and two for the speakers. The outward-facing speakers are each rated at 5 watts (for a total of 10). Ten watts is not a lot for a modern Bluetooth speaker, but wattage doesn't automatically tell you how loud or good a speaker will sound, especially in the current age of digital signal processing.

Neo 配备了四个 40 毫米驱动单元——两个用于耳机，两个用于音箱。外向扬声器的额定功率各为 5 瓦（总计 10 瓦）。对于现代蓝牙音箱来说，10 瓦并不算大，但瓦数并不能自动决定音箱的音量或音质，尤其是在数字信号处理技术普及的今天。

Battery life is quoted at more than 200 hours for headphones and 16 hours in speaker mode, which is pretty impressive, and a quick five-minute charge gives you an extra eight hours.

据称，耳机模式下的电池续航时间超过 200 小时，音箱模式下为 16 小时，这相当令人印象深刻；快速充电五分钟即可额外使用八小时。

On-device control is basic but effective, with rubber power and volume buttons together, and a separate one for Bluetooth. One quirk of the twist design is that in speaker mode the volume button is inverted, and on first use I instinctively turned the volume down instead of up. The power and 3.5-mm AUX input are also left facing up, rather than being hidden from view. This also means the USB-C cable sticks out the top—not the most aesthetic solution.

设备上的控制功能基础但有效，橡胶材质的电源键和音量键放在一起，蓝牙键则单独设置。扭转设计的一个怪癖是，在音箱模式下音量键是反向的，我第一次使用时本能地把音量调小了而不是调大。电源接口和 3.5 毫米 AUX 输入接口也朝上，没有隐藏起来。这也意味着 USB-C 线缆会从顶部伸出——这并不是最美观的解决方案。

The TDM companion app allows you to customize what happens when the headphones are twisted. You can switch between headphone and speaker playback, pause the music, or power off. You can also lock them in speaker mode to listen with the Neo around your neck, but please, if you have any love for your fellow commuters, don’t do this on the train.

TDM 配套应用程序允许你自定义耳机扭转时的操作。你可以切换耳机和音箱播放、暂停音乐或关机。你还可以将它们锁定在音箱模式，挂在脖子上听，但请注意，如果你还顾及身边的通勤者，千万别在火车上这么做。

The TDM are on-ear headphones with no active noise canceling (ANC). That’s something of a rarity these days, with brands eschewing this style in favor of larger, plusher over-ear designs with powerful noise cancellation.

TDM 是一款没有主动降噪（ANC）功能的压耳式耳机。如今这相当罕见，因为各大品牌都在摒弃这种风格，转而青睐更大、更舒适且具备强大降噪功能的包耳式设计。

While the TDM don’t have ANC, they do have relatively firm earpads and a tight fit that blocks out noise the old-fashioned way: with noise isolation. Unfortunately, maybe for this very reason, the earpads are not as plush as I’d like, and the whole thing feels tight on my admittedly large skull and less than dainty ears.

虽然 TDM 没有主动降噪，但它们有相对坚硬的耳垫和紧密的贴合度，通过传统方式阻隔噪音：即被动隔音。遗憾的是，也许正是因为这个原因，耳垫并不像我希望的那样柔软，而且对于我那确实较大的头骨和并不娇小的耳朵来说，整个耳机感觉很紧。

At 11.9-ounces, the Neo are also heavy for on-ear headphones. It’s no surprise, given that they’ve got four 40-mm drivers inside, but it is noticeable. For comparison, they’re 3 ounces (or 34 percent) heavier than the Sony WH-1000XM6, 1.9 ounces heavier than the more affordable Soundcore Space One Pro, and a full 4.25 ounces heavier than the on-ear Beats Solo 4.

Neo 重达 11.9 盎司，对于压耳式耳机来说太重了。考虑到内部装有四个 40 毫米驱动单元，这并不奇怪，但确实很明显。相比之下，它比索尼 WH-1000XM6 重 3 盎司（即 34%），比更实惠的 Soundcore Space One Pro 重 1.9 盎司，比压耳式的 Beats Solo 4 整整重了 4.25 盎司。

The Neo sound detailed and pleasant, with clear bass and appreciably distinct vocals, but the presentation feels quite boxed in. Music lacks the scale and openness I expect from great headphones, and individual instruments are crowded for space. If they were comfier to wear, I think I’d enjoy the audio a bit more, but I found it all quite tiring, and certainly not on a par with similarly priced alternatives.

Neo 的声音细节丰富且悦耳，低音清晰，人声也相当突出，但整体表现感觉相当局促。音乐缺乏我所期待的优秀耳机应有的规模感和开阔感，各种乐器挤在一起。如果佩戴更舒适，我想我会更享受它的音质，但我发现长时间佩戴很累人，而且肯定无法与同价位的替代品相提并论。

They are not awful, but they do feel compromised, and given the price, they also present a bit of a quandary for travel. You gain a Bluetooth speaker at your destination but may spend the journey wishing you'd packed more comfortable headphones with proper ANC.

它们并不糟糕，但确实感觉有所妥协。考虑到价格，它们在旅行时也带来了一些困扰。你在目的地获得了一个蓝牙音箱，但可能在旅途中会后悔没带上更舒适且具备适当主动降噪功能的耳机。

Just to reiterate, I really like the engineering and execution of the Neo, and I love how small the headphones can fold away. The Bluetooth speaker looks cool too, and it gives the headphones a second function. Sadly, the TDM can’t compete with the best Bluetooth speakers I’ve tested.

重申一下，我真的很喜欢 Neo 的工程设计和执行力，也喜欢这款耳机折叠后的小巧体积。蓝牙音箱看起来也很酷，它赋予了耳机第二种功能。遗憾的是，TDM 无法与我测试过的顶级蓝牙音箱相提并论。

There's enough volume for casual listening, but the sound is thin and underwhelming. It keeps pace with the tiny-but-mighty JBL Go 5, but given that it has twice the power and double the drivers, it really should sound better.

它的音量足以满足日常休闲聆听，但声音单薄且令人失望。它与小巧而强大的 JBL Go 5 不相上下，但考虑到它拥有两倍的功率和两倍的驱动单元，它的音质理应更好。
