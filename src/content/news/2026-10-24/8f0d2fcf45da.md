In Brief Posted: 9:26 AM PDT · October 23, 2026 Image Credits:Daytona / CEO Ivan Burazin flanked by chief architect Goran Draganić (left) and CTO Vedran Jukic Julie Bort Agent cloud startup Daytona hits $1B valuation backed by FirstMark and a who’s who of angels

简讯发布于太平洋夏令时间 2026 年 10 月 23 日上午 9:26。图片来源：Daytona / 首席执行官 Ivan Burazin 与首席架构师 Goran Draganić（左）及首席技术官 Vedran Jukic。作者：Julie Bort。代理云初创公司 Daytona 获得 FirstMark 及多位知名天使投资人支持，估值达到 10 亿美元。

Daytona Platforms announced on Friday that it had raised a $60 million Series B round at a $1 billion valuation led by FirstMark Growth. The company says it has now raised a total of $91 million.

Daytona Platforms 周五宣布，公司已完成 6000 万美元的 B 轮融资，估值达到 10 亿美元，本轮融资由 FirstMark Growth 领投。该公司表示，目前累计融资总额已达 9100 万美元。

The New York-based startup has attracted not just FirstMark (which also led its $24 million Series A in February), but also a host of well-known angels in this latest round. Daytona says they include Stripe’s Patrick Collison, Shopify’s Tobi Lütke, Perplexity’s Aravind Srinivas, Zoom’s Eric Yuan, Datadog’s Olivier Pomel, Quora’s Adam D’Angelo (who is also an OpenAI board member), and Mercor’s Brendan Foody. It also names Qualcomm Ventures, Samsung Next, and Okta Ventures as participants.

这家总部位于纽约的初创公司不仅吸引了 FirstMark（该公司也曾领投其 2 月份的 2400 万美元 A 轮融资），还在本轮融资中获得了多位知名天使投资人的青睐。Daytona 表示，这些投资人包括 Stripe 的 Patrick Collison、Shopify 的 Tobi Lütke、Perplexity 的 Aravind Srinivas、Zoom 的 Eric Yuan、Datadog 的 Olivier Pomel、Quora 的 Adam D’Angelo（同时也是 OpenAI 董事会成员）以及 Mercor 的 Brendan Foody。此外，Qualcomm Ventures、Samsung Next 和 Okta Ventures 也参与了本轮投资。

Three-year-old Daytona is building a cloud specifically for long-running agents, which have different infrastructure needs than yesteryear’s software, the startup contends. “Agents don’t work like the long-lived services today’s cloud was designed for. They spin up thousands of short, stateful jobs nobody planned in advance, and they need a cloud built for that,” founder Ivan Burazin wrote in a blog post announcing the new round.

成立三年的 Daytona 正在构建一个专门针对长期运行代理（agents）的云平台。该初创公司认为，这些代理与过去软件的基础设施需求截然不同。创始人 Ivan Burazin 在宣布新一轮融资的博文中写道：“代理的工作方式与当今云平台所设计的长寿命服务不同。它们会启动成千上万个无人预先规划的短期有状态任务，因此需要一个为此而生的云平台。”

Daytona’s cloud, he said, is like giving every agent its own computer, where agents have access to sandboxed versions of Linux, Windows, macOS, and GPUs, among other services, on the one platform.

他表示，Daytona 的云平台就像是为每个代理提供了一台专属计算机，代理可以在同一个平台上访问 Linux、Windows、macOS 的沙盒版本以及 GPU 等其他服务。
