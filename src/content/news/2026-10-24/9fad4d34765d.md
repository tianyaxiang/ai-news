# Revival Rugs Birthday Sale: 20 Percent Off Sitewide Through Sunday

Revival Rugs is turning 9 this month, and evidently, the birthday girl is feeling generous. The rug retailer is taking 20 percent off everything sitewide (exclusions may apply, as always). But don’t dillydally—the party ends Sunday, October 25.

Revival Rugs 本月迎来 9 周年庆，显然，这位“寿星”出手相当大方。这家地毯零售商目前全场商品均享 8 折优惠（一如既往，部分商品可能除外）。但请别犹豫太久——活动将于 10 月 25 日星期日结束。

Sure, Black Friday is around the corner, and there will be deals aplenty. But will they be sitewide and stackable? Revival’s 20 percent stacks, meaning it also applies to marked-down and clearance items. Will the rug you’ve been eyeing for months still be available in November? Are you willing to risk it? I wouldn’t.

诚然，“黑色星期五”即将来临，届时会有大量促销活动。但它们能做到全场通用且可叠加优惠吗？Revival 的 8 折优惠支持叠加，这意味着它同样适用于已降价和清仓的商品。你心仪已久的地毯在 11 月还会剩下吗？你愿意冒这个险吗？反正我不愿意。

Why We Love Revival Rugs: Revival is a retailer we love here at WIRED, mainly because of its lovely selection of hand-knotted and handwoven wool rugs at reasonable prices. The company imports rugs from Turkey, Morocco, and India, and also works with its own supplier in Oakland, California. Revival also offers a variety of washable rugs, à la Ruggable (which, by the way, is having its own October Hosting Sale). Not every Revival rug is machine washable, but many are easy to maintain with spot cleaning and a low-suction vacuum.

我们为何喜爱 Revival Rugs：Revival 是我们 WIRED 编辑部非常钟爱的零售商，主要原因在于其精选的手工打结和手工编织羊毛地毯价格合理。该公司从土耳其、摩洛哥和印度进口地毯，同时也与位于加利福尼亚州奥克兰的自有供应商合作。Revival 还提供多种可清洗地毯，类似于 Ruggable（顺便提一下，Ruggable 目前也在进行十月促销活动）。并非所有 Revival 地毯都能机洗，但许多地毯只需局部清洁和低吸力吸尘器即可轻松打理。

WIRED reviewer Nena Farrell has the Bronto Hand-Tufted Rug in her toddler’s bedroom, and they are both fans. Farrell appreciates how soft it is and how it makes the room feel playful without looking tacky. Meanwhile, her son is mostly here for the giant dinosaur, which, honestly, is fair. WIRED operations coordinator Molly Higgins has the Garland Washable Area Rug in her living room, where its Turkish-inspired folk-art pattern adds plenty of color and disguises everyday stains. WIRED commerce director Cooper Fleishman just bought a vibrant marmalade wool rug for his new office from the Birthday Sale; sadly, it’s sold out, but this tonal design is a close cousin.

WIRED 评论员 Nena Farrell 在她幼儿的卧室里铺设了 Bronto 手工簇绒地毯，他们母子俩都很喜欢。Farrell 很欣赏它的柔软质感，以及它在不显俗气的前提下为房间增添的趣味感。与此同时，她儿子主要是冲着上面的巨型恐龙图案去的，说实话，这很合理。WIRED 运营协调员 Molly Higgins 在客厅里铺设了 Garland 可清洗地毯，其受土耳其启发的民间艺术图案增添了丰富的色彩，还能掩盖日常污渍。WIRED 商务总监 Cooper Fleishman 刚在生日促销中为他的新办公室购买了一块充满活力的橘子色羊毛地毯；遗憾的是，该款已售罄，但这款同色系设计与之非常相似。

As with any sale, there are a few exclusions. Swatches and gift cards aren’t included, and be sure to read the terms and conditions for more information.

与任何促销活动一样，该活动也有一些除外条款。色卡和礼品卡不参与优惠，请务必阅读条款和细则以获取更多信息。
