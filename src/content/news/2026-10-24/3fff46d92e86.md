# Renhuai123 / ziwei-doushu

Ziwei Doushu · Open Source Charting Engine. Based on the Ni Haisha "Tian Ji" teaching system, this Ziwei Doushu (Purple Star Astrology) charting system includes a complete algorithm, the Four Transformations (Si Hua) system, a pattern knowledge base, original ancient text data, and a comprehensive open-source interpretation dataset: v4.0 Interpretation Dataset—675,000 charts × 13 dimensions = 8.77 million interpretation segments, totaling approximately 20.4 billion characters, 56.6 GB after decompression (Released 2026-10-15).

紫微斗数 · 开源排盘引擎。本系统基于倪海厦《天纪》教学体系，包含完整排盘算法、四化系统、格局知识库、古籍原文数据，以及一套完整开源的紫微斗数解读数据：v4.0 解读数据集——67.5 万张命盘 × 13 维 = 877 万段解读，正文约 204 亿字，解压后 56.6 GB（2026-10-15 发布）。

### Note: Regarding Judgment of Six Relations
Ziwei Doushu has certain limitations in judging the "Six Relations" (parents, siblings, children, etc.). As Master Ni pointed out in *Tian Ji*: "Doushu is inherently weaker at evaluating the Six Relations." (2-Upper · 00:25:27). In traditional arts, *Tie Ban Shen Shu* has unique advantages in verifying these relations (2-Upper · 00:03:15). Master Ni maintained Ziwei Doushu as the core because it combines geography, environment, and human affairs to form a complete system that "knows astronomy above, geography below, and human affairs in between" (2-Upper · 00:25:43). Therefore, analyses related to the Six Relations are for reference only; the dimensions for parents, siblings, and children on metisziwei.com include this same disclaimer.

### 注：关于六亲判断
紫微斗数在六亲（父母、兄弟、子女等）判断上存在一定局限。倪师在《天纪》中指出：“斗数批六亲本来就比较差一点。”（2上 · 00:25:27）在传统术数中，铁板神数在核对六亲方面有其独特优势（2上 · 00:03:15）；倪师仍以紫微斗数为核心，是因为它能结合地理、环境与人事进行综合判断，形成“上知天文、下知地理、中知人事”的完整体系（2上 · 00:25:43）。因此，与六亲相关的分析仅供参考；metisziwei.com 上父母、兄弟、子女三个维度也附有同样的说明。
