# Soundcore’s latest noise-crushing headphones are on sale for a new low price

Soundcore 最新的降噪耳机目前正以历史最低价促销。

The Space 2 offer better ANC and sound over the original, making them an easy rec.

Space 2 在主动降噪和音质方面均优于前代产品，因此非常值得推荐。

My colleague John Higgins considered Soundcore’s Space One some of the best budget noise-canceling headphones you could buy when they launched in 2023 — and their successor has only improved upon their winning formula, albeit with a $30 price increase. Thankfully, the Soundcore Space 2 are currently on sale at Amazon, Best Buy, and Walmart in all three colors for an all-time low of $106.49 ($23 off), making them even easier to recommend.

我的同事 John Higgins 曾认为 Soundcore Space One 是 2023 年发布时市面上性价比最高的降噪耳机之一，而其继任者在保持原有成功配方的基础上进行了改进，尽管价格上涨了 30 美元。好消息是，Soundcore Space 2 目前在亚马逊、百思买和沃尔玛均有促销，三种颜色均降至 106.49 美元的历史最低价（优惠 23 美元），这让它们变得更加值得入手。

The Space 2 builds on the original with improved active noise cancellation, particularly when it comes to the low-frequency sounds associated with trains and airplane engines. They also deliver more accurate sound performance thanks to various design upgrades, while retaining many of the same features that made the Space One sound so good for the price, including support for high-resolution LDAC audio. You also still get access to HearID 3.0, which lets you personalize the sound by listening to a series of samples and adjusting accordingly.

Space 2 在前代基础上改进了主动降噪功能，特别是在针对火车和飞机引擎等低频噪音的处理上。得益于多项设计升级，它们还提供了更精准的音质表现，同时保留了让 Space One 在同价位中表现出色的多项功能，包括对高解析度 LDAC 音频的支持。此外，你依然可以使用 HearID 3.0 功能，通过聆听一系列音频样本并进行相应调整，从而实现个性化的听感体验。

Battery life is another welcome improvement. The Space 2 can last up to 50 hours with ANC enabled or 70 hours with it switched off, up from 40 and 55 hours, respectively, on the original. And when your battery dies, you can still use the equipped 3.5mm jack for wired listening. They’ll also automatically pause playback when you take them off, which is handy when you need to step away without losing your place in a song or podcast.

电池续航是另一项令人欣喜的改进。Space 2 在开启主动降噪的情况下续航可达 50 小时，关闭后则可达 70 小时，而前代产品的续航分别为 40 小时和 55 小时。当电量耗尽时，你仍可通过配备的 3.5 毫米接口进行有线收听。此外，当你摘下耳机时，它们会自动暂停播放，这在你需要暂时离开而不想错过歌曲或播客进度时非常方便。
