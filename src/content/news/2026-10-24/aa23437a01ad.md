No two mattresses are the same, and what’s happening beneath the sheets has a lot to do with why. Memory foam contours to your body, latex has a springier feel, and hybrid mattresses combine foam or latex layers with a supportive coil system for the best of both worlds.

没有两张床垫是完全一样的，而床单之下所蕴含的构造正是原因所在。记忆棉能贴合你的身体，乳胶触感更具弹性，而混合床垫则结合了泡沫或乳胶层与支撑性线圈系统，兼具两者的优点。

How to Choose the Right Mattress Type: Knowing your memory foam from your latex is a good start, but how do you actually put that knowledge into practice and choose a bed you’ll be excited to climb into every night? A few factors can help narrow down your options:

如何选择合适的床垫类型：分清记忆棉和乳胶只是一个好的开始，但你该如何将这些知识付诸实践，并选出一张让你每晚都迫不及待想躺上去的床呢？以下几个因素可以帮助你缩小选择范围：

Feel preference: Do you like sinking into your mattress or feeling like you’re sleeping on top of it? Memory foam tends to closely contour to your body, and latex feels springier and makes it easier to change positions. Hybrids can go either way, depending on comfort layers and coil system.

触感偏好：你喜欢陷入床垫的感觉，还是喜欢睡在床垫表面的感觉？记忆棉往往能紧密贴合你的身体，而乳胶触感更具弹性，更容易变换睡姿。混合床垫则取决于舒适层和线圈系统，两者皆可。

Sleeping position: Side sleepers typically benefit from mattresses that have enough cushioning to relieve pressure around the shoulders and hips. Back sleepers usually need a balance of cushioning and support, while stomach sleepers often prefer a firmer surface that keeps their midsection from sinking into the bed.

睡眠姿势：侧卧者通常受益于具有足够缓冲力以缓解肩部和臀部压力的床垫。仰卧者通常需要缓冲与支撑的平衡，而俯卧者往往更喜欢较硬的表面，以防止腹部陷入床垫中。

How hot you sleep: If you’re a sweaty sleeper, consider a latex or hybrid mattress. Both offer more airflow than memory foam, which tends to trap heat. That said, cooling features can help, too; some memory foam mattresses use gel infusions or phase-change fabrics that are designed to pull heat away from the body.

睡眠时的体温：如果你睡觉时容易出汗，请考虑乳胶或混合床垫。与容易积聚热量的记忆棉相比，这两者都能提供更好的空气流通。话虽如此，冷却功能也有帮助；一些记忆棉床垫使用凝胶注入或相变织物，旨在将热量从身体带走。

Bed sharing: If you or your sleeping partner toss and turn, proper motion isolation should be at the top of your wish list. Memory foam absorbs movement well, so sleeping partners won’t disturb each other. Pocketed coils have a bit more bounce without wiggling the entire mattress. Innerspring, on the other hand, tends to be the boounciest of the bunch. And then there’s firmness: If you and your partner disagree on this crucial mattress characteristic, a split or adjustable mattress may work best.

共享床铺：如果你或你的伴侣睡觉时翻来覆去，良好的运动隔离应该是你的首要需求。记忆棉能很好地吸收震动，因此伴侣之间不会互相干扰。袋装弹簧在保持一定弹性的同时，不会晃动整张床垫。另一方面，传统弹簧床垫往往是其中弹性最大的。此外还有硬度问题：如果你和伴侣对这一关键特性有分歧，分体式或可调节床垫可能是最好的选择。

Hybrid: Traditional coil mattresses have evolved into hybrids, which is what you will likely encounter on today’s mattress market (aside from latex and foam options). Hybrid mattresses are highly customizable, allowing you to pick and choose what you want in your bed, whether that’s a combination of foam and coils, latex and coils, or even a mix of the three.

混合床垫：传统的弹簧床垫已经演变为混合床垫，这也是你在当今床垫市场上（除了乳胶和泡沫选项外）最可能遇到的类型。混合床垫具有高度的可定制性，让你能够根据需求选择床垫的构造，无论是泡沫与线圈的组合、乳胶与线圈的组合，甚至是三者的混合。

Many mattress reviews will use the phrase “best of both worlds” to describe hybrids, as this mattress lets you sample the design aspects of the materials involved. You can get the pressure relief you need from memory foam or latex on top, but ample support from the coil core (not to mention the aforementioned responsiveness from coil inclusion).

许多床垫测评会用“兼具两者优点”来形容混合床垫，因为这种床垫让你能够体验到所用材料的设计优势。你可以从顶部的记忆棉或乳胶中获得所需的压力缓解，同时从线圈核心中获得充足的支撑（更不用说前述的线圈所带来的响应性）。

Speaking of coils and harking back to traditional mattress design, the type of coil used in hybrid mattresses matters too, as it improves upon the design of the Bonnell coil. The keywords to look for are “pocketed coils” or “individually wrapped coils,” which make up the main support mechanism of a mattress. Instead of being interconnected, each coil stays in its own lane (pocket, really) and will compress uninhibited by the other coils around it. In other words, where there is more weight, the bed will feel firmer.

说到线圈并回顾传统床垫设计，混合床垫中使用的线圈类型也很重要，因为它改进了邦内尔（Bonnell）线圈的设计。需要寻找的关键词是“袋装线圈”或“独立包装线圈”，它们构成了床垫的主要支撑机制。每个线圈不再相互连接，而是保持在自己的轨道（实际上是口袋）中，压缩时不会受到周围其他线圈的阻碍。换句话说，受力越大的地方，床垫感觉越硬。

This is especially applicable for areas where body weight is centralized when you’re lying down, particularly around the hips and shoulders. Pocketed coils are also meant to outperform traditional ones in the motion isolation arena, meaning that when you move around, the individual coil takes the heat motion-wise and stops the bed from reenacting a small earthquake. With the interconnectivity of traditional coils, it doesn’t take much for the feeling of movement to travel along the entire surface of the bed.

这尤其适用于躺下时身体重量集中的区域，特别是臀部和肩部周围。袋装线圈在运动隔离方面也优于传统线圈，这意味着当你移动时，单个线圈会吸收震动，防止床垫像发生小地震一样晃动。而传统线圈由于相互连接，运动感很容易传导至整个床面。

Fun fact: You do not need a box spring with a hybrid mattress. This is a recurring question I see, and pocketed coils in hybrids eliminate the need for one. Box springs were designed to give more support and elevate performance to traditional coil beds, which would align certain sections of coils to yield more support. Nowadays, you may actually void your warranty if you use a box spring with a hybrid mattress—be sure to check that fine print.

有趣的事实：混合床垫不需要弹簧底座。这是一个我经常看到的问题，混合床垫中的袋装线圈消除了对它的需求。弹簧底座最初是为了给传统弹簧床提供更多支撑并提升性能而设计的，它能对齐某些部分的线圈以产生更多支撑。如今，如果你在混合床垫上使用弹簧底座，实际上可能会导致保修失效——请务必查看细则。
