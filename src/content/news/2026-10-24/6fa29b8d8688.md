If you crave a compact phone that doesn’t feel like a brick in your pocket and slips easily into a small bag, the flip style is for you. Samsung’s eighth-generation clamshell is seriously refined, and as svelte and slick as it comes. It may not offer a tablet-sized screen or the fashionable new passport shape, but it is arguably the most practical type of folding phone.

如果你渴望拥有一款不会让口袋显得沉重、又能轻松塞进小包的紧凑型手机，那么翻盖式设计非常适合你。三星的第八代翻盖手机经过了深度打磨，外观既纤薄又时尚。虽然它没有平板大小的屏幕，也不是那种时髦的护照形状，但它无疑是目前最实用的折叠屏手机类型。

Like almost every other phone this year, the Z Flip8 offers tiny improvements over its predecessor and costs $100 more. If you’re wondering whether it’s worth updating from last year’s Z Flip7, it’s not. The Flip8 is a teeny bit lighter and slimmer with a newer processor, and that’s about it. But if you’re looking to dip a toe in folding-phone waters or just want something small, the Z Flip8 should be in the conversation.

和今年几乎所有其他手机一样，Z Flip8 相比前代产品只有细微的改进，价格却贵了 100 美元。如果你在考虑是否值得从去年的 Z Flip7 升级，答案是不值得。Flip8 只是稍微轻薄了一点，换装了更新的处理器，仅此而已。但如果你正打算尝试折叠屏手机，或者单纯想要一款小巧的设备，Z Flip8 值得列入考虑范围。

Slimmer and More FoldablePhotograph: Simon HillEveryone is excited about the Samsung Galaxy Z Fold8’s sexy new form factor, but Samsung continues to iterate on the flip phone format for folks who prize compact devices. The Z Flip8 shaves off 0.02 inches (0.55 millimeters) and a third of an ounce (8 grams) compared to its predecessor, making it the slimmest and lightest option in this category.

更纤薄、更易折叠（摄影：Simon Hill）。虽然大家都在为三星 Galaxy Z Fold8 迷人的新外形感到兴奋，但三星仍在为那些追求紧凑型设备的用户不断迭代翻盖手机。Z Flip8 比前代产品薄了 0.02 英寸（0.55 毫米），轻了三分之一盎司（8 克），成为该类别中最纤薄、最轻的选择。

The improved hinge makes the crease slightly less noticeable and a little easier to open. I still found it a bit tricky to open one-handed, but you get the knack after a while. The new hinge is also supposed to be more durable. The Flip8 has an IP48 rating, so you don’t need to worry about dropping it in the bath, but tiny dust and dirt particles (less than 1 millimeter) can still cause damage.

改进后的铰链使折痕变得不那么明显，开合也更容易了一些。我仍然觉得单手打开它有点困难，但用久了就能掌握技巧。据说新铰链也更加耐用。Flip8 具备 IP48 防护等级，所以不必担心掉进浴缸，但微小的灰尘和污垢颗粒（小于 1 毫米）仍可能造成损坏。

It comes in pink, like my review unit, as well as graphite, cream, and mint. It still has a physical SIM slot, and the power button has a fingerprint sensor in it that works pretty well. The screens are the same as last year's. It has a sharp 4.1-inch AMOLED display on the outside with a 60- or 120-Hz refresh rate. Open it up to reveal the 6.9-inch main display, also AMOLED but with a variable refresh rate (1 to 120 Hz) that’s better for battery life.

它有粉色（我评测的这款）、石墨色、奶油色和薄荷色可选。它依然保留了物理 SIM 卡槽，电源键集成了指纹传感器，识别效果相当不错。屏幕与去年相同，外屏是一块清晰的 4.1 英寸 AMOLED 显示屏，支持 60Hz 或 120Hz 刷新率。展开后是 6.9 英寸的主显示屏，同样是 AMOLED 材质，但支持 1 到 120Hz 的可变刷新率，这对续航更有利。

Both screens are reasonably bright (2,600 nits peak), and I never struggled to read the screen outdoors. Powerful PerformerPhotograph: Simon HillFlip phones used to get palmed off with lesser processors, but the Z Flip8 boasts the Qualcomm Snapdragon 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy (the same cutting-edge chip that's in its two bigger siblings). It has 12 GB of RAM and 256 GB of storage. You can jump to 512 GB for an extra $200, but there’s no option to increase the RAM.

两块屏幕的亮度都相当不错（峰值 2,600 尼特），我在户外阅读屏幕时从未感到吃力。性能强劲（摄影：Simon Hill）。过去，翻盖手机往往会被塞进性能较差的处理器，但 Z Flip8 搭载了高通骁龙 8 Elite Gen 5 for Galaxy（与其两款更大尺寸的兄弟机型采用了相同的尖端芯片）。它配备 12GB 内存和 256GB 存储空间。你可以额外支付 200 美元升级到 512GB，但没有增加内存的选项。

I encountered no stutters or lag and found performance to be generally excellent, whether jumping in and out of apps, firing up the camera, or gaming. The Z Flip8 can get warm, and this may throttle the processor somewhat if you’re playing a demanding game for a while (more than an hour). This is mostly down to the compact design.

我没有遇到任何卡顿或延迟，无论是切换应用、启动相机还是玩游戏，整体性能都非常出色。Z Flip8 可能会发热，如果你长时间（超过一小时）玩大型游戏，处理器可能会因此出现一定程度的降频。这主要是由于其紧凑的设计所致。

The stereo speakers are better than I expected. They get quite loud without distortion, and they’ll serve you just fine for watching videos. Call quality is also solid, and slamming it shut to end a call never gets tired. I also like opening it to answer calls, but you must enable this in the Phone app settings under Flip options.

立体声扬声器的表现超出了我的预期。它们声音很大且不会失真，看视频完全够用。通话质量也很扎实，合上手机挂断电话的感觉永远不会让人厌倦。我也喜欢通过翻开手机来接听电话，但你必须在电话应用的“翻盖选项”设置中开启此功能。

Connectivity includes 5G (no mmWave), Wi-Fi 7, and Bluetooth 5.4, but there’s no ultra-wideband (UWB) for more precise positioning. The Fold8 models have Bluetooth 6 and UWB. While it’s very capable, the Z Flip8 is only average when it comes to battery life, another drawback of its size. Samsung put in a 4,300-mAh silicon-carbon battery that’s good enough for casual use.

连接功能包括 5G（无毫米波）、Wi-Fi 7 和蓝牙 5.4，但没有用于更精确定位的超宽带（UWB）技术。Fold8 系列机型则配备了蓝牙 6 和 UWB。虽然 Z Flip8 功能强大，但在续航方面表现平平，这也是其尺寸带来的另一个缺点。三星内置了一块 4,300 毫安时的硅碳电池，足以满足日常使用。

If you’re on your phone a lot, you’ll want a power bank handy. Charging speeds are disappointing at 25 watts wired and 15 watts wireless, and I’m annoyed by Samsung’s refusal to implement the magnetic feature of Qi2. Snap ShotIf you’re a keen photographer, you can get many better camera systems in a range of Android phones for the same money or less. That said, the main 50-megapixel shooter is perfectly good in many situations, and most folks will be happy with the photos it captures.

如果你经常使用手机，最好随身携带充电宝。充电速度令人失望，有线仅 25 瓦，无线 15 瓦，而且三星拒绝支持 Qi2 磁吸功能也让我感到恼火。拍照方面，如果你是摄影爱好者，花同样的钱甚至更少的钱，可以在其他安卓手机上获得更好的相机系统。话虽如此，5000 万像素的主摄在许多情况下表现非常出色，大多数人会对它拍出的照片感到满意。

Some noise creeps in when it’s dark. The 12-megapixel ultrawide is useful when you want to cram a lot in or take a group selfie, but it’s a definite step down in quality. Sadly, there’s no telephoto lens, but you can emulate 2X zoom without losing detail by cropping, and it offers passable results in good lighting up to 10X.

在光线较暗时会出现一些噪点。1200 万像素的超广角镜头在需要容纳更多画面或拍摄集体自拍时很有用，但画质确实有所下降。遗憾的是，它没有长焦镜头，但你可以通过裁剪来模拟 2 倍变焦且不损失细节，在光线充足的情况下，最高 10 倍变焦也能提供尚可的结果。

I also love Samsung’s Horizon Lock, which lets you shoot video with a steady horizontal plane no matter how you turn or move the phone.

Another new AI feature from Samsung is My FanCam, which lets you pick a subject to focus on in group videos and reframes them as though your chosen subject is the star. Handy for school plays or soccer games, if you remember to use it. (You can also apply it to old videos.) Speaking of AI, Samsung offers Now Brief to float up calendar events, reminders, and useful info like local weather, and Now Nudge to suggest adding dates to your calendar from messages, sharing locations or photos, and auto-filling forms.

三星的另一项新 AI 功能是“My FanCam”，它允许你在群组视频中选择一个主体进行对焦，并重新构图，让选定的主体看起来像主角一样。如果你记得使用的话，这在学校演出或足球比赛中非常方便。（你也可以将其应用于旧视频。）说到 AI，三星还提供了“Now Brief”功能，可以浮动显示日历事件、提醒和本地天气等有用信息；以及“Now Nudge”功能，用于建议将消息中的日期添加到日历、分享位置或照片，以及自动填充表单。
