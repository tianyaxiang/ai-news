### ‘Bring Back THE Boss’: Nvidia Employees Petition for Jensen Huang’s Return

根据《连线》（WIRED）审阅的内部通信，尽管黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）被曝出多次婚外情并突然辞职，一些英伟达（Nvidia）员工仍呼吁他回归。

Some Nvidia employees are calling for Jensen Huang’s return even after his multiple reported extramarital affairs and abrupt resignation, according to internal correspondence reviewed by WIRED.

在黄仁勋辞职前，英伟达的一位资深工程师在内部“意见箱”中发帖，敦促公司董事会在采取任何行动前考虑员工的情绪。自那时起，该帖已吸引了超过1600条评论，其中几条要求董事会设法让黄仁勋回归。

An internal “suggestion box” post submitted before Huang’s resignation by a longtime engineer at Nvidia urged the company’s board to take employee sentiment into consideration prior to taking any action. The submission has attracted more than 1,600 comments since then, with several asking board members to find a way to bring back Huang.

“如果你相信这个使命，就把老板带回来，”一位匿名评论者写道。“我们都是人，都会犯错。黄仁勋做了这么多好事，他应该得到第二次机会，”另一位写道。

“If you believe in the mission, bring back THE boss,” wrote one anonymous commenter. “We are all humans and can make mistakes. Jensen did so many good things and he needs a second chance,” wrote another.

一名员工甚至发送了一封全公司范围的电子邮件，要求黄仁勋离职一事保持透明。“我支持黄仁勋，”该员工写道。“不是因为我相信任何人都是完美无缺的，而是因为我拒绝相信，坚持公平就必须与一位领导力对我们意义重大的人保持距离。”

One employee went as far as sending a companywide email demanding transparency into Huang’s exit. “I stand by Jensen,” the staffer wrote. “Not because I believe any person is infallible, but because I refuse to believe that standing for fairness must mean standing at a distance from someone whose leadership has meant so much to us.”

该员工无法联系到以获取评论，而发布意见箱帖子的工程师拒绝置评。英伟达及其新任董事会主席、投资者哈维·琼斯（Harvey Jones）没有立即回应置评请求。

The employee couldn’t be reached for comment, while the engineer behind the suggestion box post declined to comment. Nvidia and its new board chair, investor Harvey Jones, didn’t immediately respond to requests for comment.

现年63岁的黄仁勋自1993年英伟达成立以来一直领导该公司。他在周日发给员工的一封电子邮件中写道，他“对妻子不忠”，并称“没有任何借口”。在此之前，有报道称他与一名疑似中国情报人员有染。董事会在周四上午宣布了黄仁勋的辞职，此前有报道称他与一名直接下属有染。（该员工未就此事发表评论。）此后，路透社报道称他与另一名英伟达员工也有染。自周日的电子邮件以来，黄仁勋尚未就这些报道发表评论。

Huang, a 63-year-old who had led Nvidia since its founding in 1993, wrote in an email to employees on Sunday that he had been “unfaithful to [his] wife” and that there was “no excuse.” The acknowledgement followed reports of an affair with a suspected Chinese intelligence asset. The board announced Huang’s resignation on Thursday morning, following reports of an alleged second affair with a direct report. (This employee has not commented on the matter.) Since then, Reuters reported on an alleged affair with yet another Nvidia employee. Huang hasn’t commented about these reports since his Sunday email.

英伟达董事会主要由英伟达早期投资者和黄仁勋的长期亲信组成。董事会任命英伟达首席财务官科莱特·克雷斯（Colette Kress）为临时首席执行官，并任命前新思科技（Synopsys）首席执行官哈维·琼斯为董事会主席。自指控浮出水面以来，这场危机已导致公司市值蒸发了1万亿美元，引发了美国立法者和联邦调查局（FBI）的调查，并提出了对芯片实施新出口管制的可能性。

Nvidia’s board is largely made up of early Nvidia investors and Huang’s longtime confidants. It named Nvidia chief financial officer Colette Kress interim CEO, and former Synopsys CEO Harvey Jones as chair of the board. The crisis has cost the company $1 trillion in market value since the allegations surfaced, prompted investigations from US lawmakers and the FBI, and raised the prospect of new export controls on chips.

本周公司内部一切照常，但黄仁勋的辞职让许多员工感到震惊。此后的几个小时里，大约有两打员工在领英（LinkedIn）上发帖支持黄仁勋，其中一人称他们对黄仁勋领导力的信念坚定不移。在此期间，《连线》没有发现任何英伟达员工发帖支持他的辞职。

It’s been business as usual inside the company throughout this week, but Huang’s resignation left many employees stunned. In the hours after, about two dozen employees posted on LinkedIn in support of Huang, with one describing their faith in his leadership as unwavering. Over the same period, WIRED couldn’t identify any posts from Nvidia employees in support of his resignation.

许多员工曾预计董事会调查会在任何重大变动发生前结束。例如，意见箱中的帖子呼吁董事会与员工分享调查结果，并正式征求他们“关于黄仁勋继续担任我们首席执行官”的意见。

Many employees had expected a board investigation to conclude before any big changes would occur. For instance, the suggestion box post called on the board to share its findings with employees and formally solicit their sentiment “about Jensen’s continuing as our CEO.”

然而，调查刚开始，黄仁勋就离开了。员工们正试图更好地了解原因。“如果美国辜负了这位帮助建立其未来的人，”那封全公司范围的电子邮件中的员工写道，“那么这个世界就不配拥有黄仁勋。”

Instead, Huang left just as the inquiry had begun. Staffers are seeking to better understand why. “If America fails the man who helped build its future,” the employee in the companywide email stated, “then this world does not deserve Jensen Huang.”
