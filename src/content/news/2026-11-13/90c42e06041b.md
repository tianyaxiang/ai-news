After initially testing the feature in September, Meta’s consumer AI agent Muse is now able to place calls to U.S. businesses on behalf of its users. However, only paid subscribers have access to the feature, the company says.

在九月份初步测试该功能后，Meta 的消费级 AI 智能体 Muse 现在可以代表用户拨打美国企业的电话。然而，该公司表示，只有付费订阅用户才能使用此功能。

Meta’s Muse, which now has over 10 million users, the company said last month, has been working to make consumer AI a mainstream product for everyday users. Placing calls is one of the ways it aims to get there, as it could handle another task on people’s to-do lists.

Meta 上个月表示，Muse 目前拥有超过 1000 万用户，该公司一直致力于让消费级 AI 成为日常用户的主流产品。拨打电话是其实现这一目标的方式之一，因为它可以处理人们待办事项清单上的另一项任务。

Notes Alexandr Wang, Meta’s chief AI officer and head of its Superintelligence Labs division, the feature could be used to take care of calls people don’t want to make, like those to their cable company, the DMV, or to a restaurant that doesn’t offer online reservations.

Meta 首席 AI 官兼超级智能实验室部门负责人亚历山大·王（Alexandr Wang）指出，该功能可用于处理人们不想拨打的电话，例如打给有线电视公司、车管所（DMV）或不提供在线预订服务的餐厅。

Previously, only a select number of Muse early adopters were able to try this feature after asking the AI for access. Now, anyone who pays for Muse can make this type of request. Currently, Muse subscription plans start at $20 per month for 500 million Muse tokens per week. Heavier AI users can opt to pay $100 per month for 3 billion tokens.

此前，只有少数 Muse 早期采用者在向 AI 请求访问权限后才能尝试此功能。现在，任何为 Muse 付费的用户都可以提出此类请求。目前，Muse 的订阅计划起价为每月 20 美元，每周可获得 5 亿个 Muse 代币。重度 AI 用户可以选择每月支付 100 美元以获得 30 亿个代币。

Muse is not the only AI agent able to place calls to businesses; the startup Instinct has been offering a similar option, as has Google’s Gemini. However, while the AI tools are now certainly capable of handling the task, the reception on the other end of the line can be mixed. Many businesses and receptionists hang up on calls from AI agents as soon as they identify that it’s not a human on the phone.

Muse 并不是唯一能够拨打企业电话的 AI 智能体；初创公司 Instinct 和谷歌的 Gemini 也提供了类似的选择。然而，虽然这些 AI 工具现在确实有能力处理这项任务，但电话另一端的反应却不尽相同。许多企业和接待员一旦识别出电话那头不是真人，就会挂断 AI 智能体的来电。

But with AI now better able to mimic a real human voice, being able to tell the difference between a customer and an AI working on the customer’s behalf will get tougher. Despite the challenges, this feature is one that could appeal to consumers who have grown frustrated with the state of customer service over the years.

但随着 AI 现在能更好地模仿真实人声，区分客户本人和代表客户工作的 AI 将变得越来越困难。尽管存在挑战，但对于多年来对客户服务现状感到沮丧的消费者来说，这项功能可能具有吸引力。

For too long, customers have been forced to navigate complex phone trees, and then sit on hold waiting for a human representative to pick up. If they’re lucky, that person can actually help them, and not transfer them to another department. Now, those consumers could potentially use their AI, with its infinite patience, for this tedious task instead.

长期以来，客户被迫在复杂的电话语音菜单中导航，然后还要在等待人工客服接听时保持通话。如果运气好，对方确实能提供帮助，而不是将他们转接到其他部门。现在，这些消费者或许可以利用他们拥有无限耐心的 AI 来代替他们完成这项繁琐的任务。

While calls remain a paid-only feature for the time being, the recently added option to make a Muse email address will soon roll out to free users, Wang said this week.

王本周表示，虽然目前拨打电话仍是付费专属功能，但最近新增的创建 Muse 电子邮件地址的选项很快将向免费用户开放。
