CHICAGO—Families joined protests in seven cities nationwide on Wednesday, rallying against what they see as Google’s bid to entrench its AI models in schools, turn kids of all ages into Gemini guinea pigs, and hook them on AI.

周三，全美七个城市的家庭举行了抗议活动，反对他们所认为的谷歌试图在学校中植入其人工智能模型的行为，他们认为谷歌正将各年龄段的孩子变成 Gemini 的实验品，并诱导他们对人工智能产生依赖。

The backlash follows a Google statement last week, confirming that there will be no way for students to use Google Search without AI. While Google claimed it was giving schools “more choice, not less”—by turning off Search, Gemini, and Gemini Notebook by default—it would not provide an option that allowed Search without AI.

此次反弹源于谷歌上周发表的一份声明，该声明确认学生将无法在不使用人工智能的情况下使用谷歌搜索。尽管谷歌声称通过默认关闭搜索、Gemini 和 Gemini Notebook 为学校提供了“更多而非更少的选择”，但它并未提供一种允许在没有人工智能的情况下进行搜索的选项。

For students, the only way to avoid interacting with AI was to remember to add “-ai” to every query, parents worried. In Chicago, many families showed up to protest the company, despite cold, rainy weather. Waving signs, dozens of protesters marched up to Google’s downtown office as workers seemed to be leaving for the day, shouting chants like “Hey Google, nice try, we don’t want your Gemini” and “Hey, Google, leave our kids alone!”

家长们担心，对于学生来说，避免与人工智能互动的唯一方法就是记住在每次查询时添加“-ai”。在芝加哥，尽管天气寒冷多雨，许多家庭还是赶来抗议。数十名抗议者挥舞着标语，在谷歌员工似乎准备下班时游行至其市中心办公室，高呼“嘿谷歌，别费劲了，我们不需要你的 Gemini”和“嘿，谷歌，离我们的孩子远点！”等口号。

Called to action by a nonprofit grassroots advocacy group, the Illinois Families for Public Schools (ILFPS), some parents and students had previously won a major AI fight. In August, the group pressured Chicago Public Schools (CPS) to pull out of a Gemini pilot that would have roped in all high school students districtwide—without consulting parents.

在非营利性基层倡导组织“伊利诺伊州公立学校家庭”（ILFPS）的号召下，一些家长和学生此前曾赢得了一场重要的人工智能抗争。今年八月，该组织向芝加哥公立学校（CPS）施压，迫使其退出了一个本会将全区所有高中生纳入其中的 Gemini 试点项目，而该项目此前并未征求家长的意见。

And now, ILFPS is demanding a moratorium on AI until risks to students are better understood and CPS can draft a policy to protect students from potential learning setbacks or more harmful outcomes from engaging Google’s AI bots. If the group wins, it will join cities like Los Angeles and New York City in cautiously banning AI in schools.

现在，ILFPS 要求暂停使用人工智能，直到学生面临的风险得到更好的了解，并且 CPS 能够起草相关政策，以保护学生免受因使用谷歌人工智能机器人而导致的潜在学习倒退或更严重的负面后果。如果该组织获胜，它将加入洛杉矶和纽约市的行列，在学校中谨慎地禁止人工智能。

Most alarming to parents, Google now faces a lawsuit from a 13-year-old student in Durham, North Carolina named “Emma Doe.” As Emma has alleged, she was a happy, well-adjusted, straight-A student when she started using Gemini on her school-issued Chromebook. Emma’s parents had no idea she was using Gemini, as the lawsuit alleged that Google “deliberately” kept them in the dark while driving Emma into so-called “AI psychosis.”

最令家长感到震惊的是，谷歌目前正面临北卡罗来纳州达勒姆市一名 13 岁学生“艾玛·多伊”（Emma Doe）的诉讼。据艾玛称，她在学校发放的 Chromebook 上开始使用 Gemini 时，曾是一名快乐、适应良好且成绩优异的学生。艾玛的父母完全不知道她在接触 Gemini，诉讼称谷歌“蓄意”隐瞒了这一情况，同时将艾玛推向了所谓的“人工智能精神错乱”。

Over time, normal school-related interactions with the chatbot led to more intense chats, and ultimately the chatbot allegedly coached Emma to plan her own suicide and funeral. The school warned her parents and averted the danger after an internal system flagged a chat labeled “Funeral,” but Emma’s story stoked fears of parents joining protests not just in Durham, but also in Boston; Boulder, Colorado; Chicago; Los Angeles; New York City; and Portland, Oregon.

随着时间的推移，与聊天机器人之间正常的学校相关互动演变成了更激烈的对话，最终该机器人据称引导艾玛策划了自己的自杀和葬礼。在内部系统标记了一个名为“葬礼”的聊天记录后，学校通知了她的父母并避免了危险，但艾玛的故事引发了家长们的恐惧，他们不仅在达勒姆，还在波士顿、科罗拉多州博尔德、芝加哥、洛杉矶、纽约市和俄勒冈州波特兰等地加入了抗议活动。

Emma’s lawsuit accuses Google of exploiting its dominant position in education technology and “sneaking” Gemini into Chromebooks, while falsely marketing them as safe. “As a direct consequence of Google’s choices, Emma was baited by its defectively designed products into repeated, unmonitored, sycophantic AI chatbot interactions on her school Chromebook without her parents ever knowing. And, but for a fortunate turn of events, Google’s AI may well have manipulated Emma into taking her own life,” the lawsuit alleged.

艾玛的诉讼指控谷歌利用其在教育技术领域的垄断地位，将 Gemini“偷偷”植入 Chromebook，同时虚假宣传其安全性。诉讼称：“由于谷歌的选择，艾玛被其设计有缺陷的产品诱导，在学校的 Chromebook 上进行了反复、不受监控且带有谄媚性质的人工智能聊天互动，而她的父母对此一无所知。如果不是因为幸运的转折，谷歌的人工智能很可能已经操纵艾玛结束了自己的生命。”
