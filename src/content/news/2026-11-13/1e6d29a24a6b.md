In a significant update to its guidelines for prostate cancer screening, the American Cancer Society is now recommending that all men get routinely screened for prostate cancer with a PSA test. Men at average risk should get a PSA test every two years from age 50 to 69, according to the new guidelines released Thursday.

美国癌症协会（ACS）在其前列腺癌筛查指南中进行了重大更新，现建议所有男性通过前列腺特异性抗原（PSA）检测进行常规筛查。根据周四发布的新指南，平均风险的男性应从50岁到69岁每两年进行一次PSA检测。

Men considered at high risk due to race and family history should start testing earlier, at 45, and most are recommended to get tested every year. Specifically, yearly testing starting at age 45 is recommended for all Black men, who have higher rates of prostate cancer. Non-Black men should also get yearly testing starting at 45 if a close relative was diagnosed with prostate cancer before age 65, or if more than one of their relatives has had prostate cancer.

因种族和家族史被视为高风险的男性应更早开始检测（45岁），且大多数建议每年进行一次。具体而言，建议所有患前列腺癌比例较高的黑人男性从45岁开始每年进行检测。非黑人男性若有近亲在65岁前被诊断出患有前列腺癌，或有超过一名亲属患有此病，也应从45岁开始每年进行检测。

On the other hand, if only one close relative was diagnosed with prostate cancer and it was diagnosed at age 65 or older, non-Black men in this case should start PSA screening at 45, but are recommended to get testing only every two years. Men ages 70 to 74 should consult their doctor on continuing screening, while those 75 and up are not recommended to get screened.

另一方面，如果只有一名近亲在65岁或以上被诊断出患有前列腺癌，此类非黑人男性应从45岁开始进行PSA筛查，但建议仅每两年检测一次。70至74岁的男性应咨询医生是否继续筛查，而75岁及以上的男性则不建议进行筛查。

The new guidance departs from ACS’s previous iteration, published in 2010, which made no universal recommendations for routine screening. Men at age 50 were recommended to use shared-decision making with their doctors on whether or not to get tested. The recommendation was similar to that from the US Preventive Services Task Force, a federal panel that regularly evaluates evidence and makes recommendations on such preventive medical care.

该新指南与ACS 2010年发布的上一版本有所不同，当时并未对常规筛查提出普遍建议。当时建议50岁的男性与医生共同商议是否进行检测。该建议与美国预防服务工作组（USPSTF）的建议类似，该联邦小组定期评估证据并就此类预防性医疗护理提出建议。

(The USPSTF is currently working on an update to its prostate cancer screening recommendation, though it should be noted that the panel has recently been overhauled by anti-vaccine Health Secretary Robert F. Kennedy Jr. and new members are reportedly rife with conflicts of interest.)

（USPSTF目前正在更新其前列腺癌筛查建议，但值得注意的是，该小组最近已被反疫苗的卫生部长小罗伯特·F·肯尼迪改组，据报道新成员中存在严重的利益冲突。）

In announcing the guidelines today, the ACS says its updates are based on new evidence since 2010 that shows screening saves lives, and that advances in case management can avoid the concerning harms from overdiagnosis and overtreatment. For many men with prostate cancer, their cancer will never grow or cause harm in their lifetime, while treatment can run the risk of erectile dysfunction and urinary incontinence.

ACS在今天宣布这些指南时表示，其更新是基于2010年以来的新证据，这些证据表明筛查可以挽救生命，且病例管理的进步可以避免过度诊断和过度治疗带来的令人担忧的危害。对于许多前列腺癌患者来说，癌症在他们的一生中可能永远不会生长或造成伤害，而治疗却可能带来勃起功能障碍和尿失禁的风险。

In the new guidance, the ACS adds guardrails, firstly advising all men to be informed about the risks and benefits. If a PSA test comes back high—defined as 3 ng/mL or higher for men under 70 and 6.5 ng/mL or higher for those 70 and up—the ACS recommends repeating the test before taking any next steps. If repeat testing is still high, then men should get either a prostate MRI or a validated biomarker test to assess risk.

在新指南中，ACS增加了保障措施，首先建议所有男性了解相关的风险和益处。如果PSA检测结果偏高（70岁以下男性定义为3 ng/mL或更高，70岁及以上男性为6.5 ng/mL或更高），ACS建议在采取任何后续步骤之前重复检测。如果重复检测结果依然偏高，男性应进行前列腺MRI或经过验证的生物标志物检测以评估风险。

Only if there is a high suspicion of cancer—such as having a very high PSA test result—should men jump to a prostate biopsy. For men diagnosed with prostate cancer, active surveillance should be the initial management strategy for men whose cancer appears to be at low risk of growing or spreading.

只有在高度怀疑患癌的情况下（例如PSA检测结果极高），男性才应直接进行前列腺活检。对于被诊断出患有前列腺癌的男性，如果其癌症生长或扩散的风险较低，主动监测应作为最初的管理策略。

“By catching aggressive cancers with a simple PSA blood test, you open the door to highly effective treatments that protect your future,” Dr. Shanthi Sivendran, interim chief patient officer for the ACS, said in a statement. “Meanwhile, lower-risk findings can be safely managed through careful, active surveillance.”

ACS临时首席患者官Shanthi Sivendran博士在一份声明中表示：“通过简单的PSA血液检测发现侵袭性癌症，可以开启高效治疗的大门，从而保护您的未来。同时，对于低风险的发现，可以通过谨慎的主动监测进行安全管理。”

Prostate cancer is one of the most common cancers in men and the second leading cause of cancer death in US men, the ACS notes. Nearly 334,00 men are estimated to be diagnosed with prostate cancer in 2026, and it’s estimated that over 36,320 will die from the disease.

ACS指出，前列腺癌是男性最常见的癌症之一，也是美国男性癌症死亡的第二大原因。据估计，2026年将有近33.4万名男性被诊断出患有前列腺癌，预计将有超过36,320人死于该疾病。
