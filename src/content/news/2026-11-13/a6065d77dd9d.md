# Man's secret use of synthetic testosterone for cows nearly destroyed his heart

For 11 years, a Massachusetts man had an alarming array of health problems, most notably that his heart kept failing. He landed in the hospital over and over again, needed life support at one point, spent years going to specialists, and underwent a barrage of tests along the way. But through it all, he never revealed a key detail behind his ailments: He was taking powerful, illicit synthetic testosterone derivatives (aka anabolic-androgenic steroids, or AAS).

在过去的11年里，一名马萨诸塞州男子出现了一系列令人担忧的健康问题，最显著的是他的心脏不断衰竭。他反复入院，一度需要生命维持系统，多年来辗转于各科专家之间，并接受了大量的检查。然而，在这一切过程中，他从未透露导致其病痛的关键细节：他一直在服用强效的非法合成睾酮衍生物（即合成代谢-雄激素类固醇，简称AAS）。

One of the drugs he took was approved only for use in animals; it’s primarily used to beef up cows. Left in the dark, his doctors had to puzzle this out on their own. In a case report in this week’s issue of the New England Journal of Medicine, they explain how they did it. They also offer a warning: These cases will only become more common, and clinicians need to know what they look like.

他服用的其中一种药物仅获准用于动物，主要用于给牛“增肌”。由于被蒙在鼓里，他的医生们不得不自行破解这一谜题。在本周出版的《新英格兰医学杂志》的一份病例报告中，他们解释了诊断过程。他们还发出警告：此类病例只会越来越普遍，临床医生需要了解其表现。

“Use of AAS has now become a major international substance use disorder,” they wrote. In the US, it’s estimated that between 2.9 and 4 million people, mostly male athletes, use AAS. To put this in a public health perspective, this is more than the number of people in the US with an HIV infection.

他们写道：“AAS的使用现已成为一种主要的国际性物质使用障碍。”据估计，在美国有290万至400万人（主要是男性运动员）使用AAS。从公共卫生的角度来看，这一数字超过了美国艾滋病毒感染者的数量。

“Clinicians should be prepared to encounter increasing numbers of patients presenting with the long-term effects of AAS exposure, including cardiomyopathy, atherosclerotic disease, and anabolic steroid–induced hypogonadism.” Patients also need to be prepared for what recovery looks like, which they described as being “lengthy and varied.” The man’s case offers a stark example.

“临床医生应做好准备，迎接越来越多的患者，他们表现出AAS暴露的长期影响，包括心肌病、动脉粥样硬化疾病和合成代谢类固醇诱导的性腺功能减退症。”患者也需要对康复过程做好心理准备，医生将其描述为“漫长且因人而异”。该男子的病例就是一个鲜明的例子。

Troubling tale The problems started when he was 39. He fell ill after returning from a trip to the Caribbean and went to Massachusetts General Hospital in Boston with a fever, chills, and trouble breathing. Tests suggested he had an acute kidney injury, liver damage, and an injured heart. Testing of his heart showed that the left chamber was pumping out just 21 percent of its blood in a beat (a normal amount is above 50 percent).

令人不安的故事：问题始于他39岁时。从加勒比海旅行回来后，他病倒了，因发烧、寒战和呼吸困难前往波士顿的马萨诸塞州总医院就诊。检查显示他患有急性肾损伤、肝损伤和心脏受损。心脏检查显示，其左心室每次搏动仅能泵出21%的血液（正常量应在50%以上）。

His heart only got worse from there, and within hours, he ended up on a type of life support called ECMO (venoarterial extracorporeal membrane oxygenation), a machine that does the work of both the heart and lungs in dire circumstances. He luckily recovered—and quickly. But doctors were baffled by the cause. When he was weaned off ECMO a few days later, his heart function normalized.

此后他的心脏状况持续恶化，几小时内便用上了名为ECMO（静脉-动脉体外膜肺氧合）的生命维持系统，这是一种在危急情况下替代心肺功能的机器。幸运的是，他很快康复了。但医生对病因感到困惑。几天后，当他脱离ECMO时，心脏功能恢复了正常。

Doctors looked for evidence of anything that could have sparked the sudden cardiac spiral. They found nothing but a mildly elevated level of antibodies against a toxin made by Streptococcus bacteria. In rare instances, an infection with these germs can lead to a condition called poststreptococcal myocarditis, which could potentially explain some of his symptoms. It was their best guess. They prescribed him antibiotics, as well as drugs to lower his blood pressure.

医生们寻找可能引发心脏骤然恶化的证据，但除了针对链球菌毒素的抗体水平轻微升高外，一无所获。在极少数情况下，感染这些细菌会导致一种称为链球菌感染后心肌炎的疾病，这可能解释他的一些症状。这是他们当时最好的猜测。他们为他开了抗生素和降压药。

Two years later, he went to another hospital. This time it was for acute pain in his abdomen and legs. Doctors found signs of cell death in his kidneys. The arteries that supply blood to his kidneys appeared narrowed, suggesting a condition called renal-artery stenosis. There were also signs of inflammation in his blood vessels. But the doctors at the hospital couldn’t pinpoint the problem. They prescribed him anti-inflammatory drugs and sent him on his way.

两年后，他去了另一家医院，这次是因为腹部和腿部剧烈疼痛。医生发现他的肾脏有细胞死亡的迹象。为肾脏供血的动脉出现狭窄，提示患有肾动脉狭窄。他的血管也有炎症迹象。但该院医生无法确定问题所在，便开了抗炎药让他出院了。

For the next five years, the man started seeing a cadre of specialists to monitor his health, including doctors in primary care, cardiology, nephrology, rheumatology, general medicine, sleep medicine, and psychiatry. When he was about 46, he reported fatigue to his primary care doctor, who found that his testosterone level was low. The man then went to an endocrinologist, reporting not just fatigue but low libido and poor sleep.

在接下来的五年里，该男子开始看各科专家以监测健康状况，包括初级保健、心脏病学、肾脏病学、风湿病学、全科医学、睡眠医学和精神病学医生。大约46岁时，他向初级保健医生报告了疲劳症状，医生发现他的睾酮水平偏低。随后他去看内分泌科医生，不仅报告了疲劳，还提到了性欲减退和睡眠质量差。

The endocrinologist recommended treatment for obstructive sleep apnea, which can increase the risk of low testosterone. A year later, the man added a hematologist to his rotation of care providers. A routine blood test revealed abnormally high levels of red blood cells (erythrocytosis). The hematologist noted the man also had high blood pressure (150/108 mm Hg) but found everything else to be normal. The hematologist ended up attributing the erythrocytosis to the man’s sleep apnea and renal-artery stenosis.

内分泌科医生建议治疗阻塞性睡眠呼吸暂停，因为这会增加睾酮水平偏低的风险。一年后，该男子又增加了血液科医生作为护理提供者。常规血液检查显示红细胞水平异常升高（红细胞增多症）。血液科医生注意到他还有高血压（150/108 mm Hg），但发现其他一切正常。最终，血液科医生将红细胞增多症归因于他的睡眠呼吸暂停和肾动脉狭窄。

Tangled toxicity It wasn’t until he arrived again at Mass General in 2026 that doctors finally put it all together. Now 50, the man was once again experiencing heart failure—and he had again just returned from the Caribbean. In the case report, doctors provided a more complete picture of the man, noting that he lived with his wife and children in eastern Massachusetts, worked as a facilities manager, and described himself as a workout “fanatic.”

错综复杂的毒性：直到2026年他再次来到马萨诸塞州总医院，医生们才终于将所有线索串联起来。此时他已50岁，再次出现心力衰竭，且同样是刚从加勒比海回来。在病例报告中，医生提供了关于该男子的更完整画像：他与妻子和孩子住在马萨诸塞州东部，担任设施经理，并自称是健身“狂热分子”。

He used tanning beds and applied spray tans. He told doctors he had erectile dysfunction and reduced libido for several years. They also noticed that he had mildly enlarged breast tissue (a condition called gynecomastia), progressive insulin resistance, decreased levels of “good” cholesterol (HDL or high-density lipoprotein), and fatty liver disease.

他使用日光浴床并涂抹喷雾美黑剂。他告诉医生，他患有勃起功能障碍且性欲减退已有数年。医生还注意到他有轻微的乳房组织增大（称为男性乳房发育症）、进行性胰岛素抵抗、“好”胆固醇（高密度脂蛋白）水平降低以及脂肪肝疾病。

When he arrived at the hospital, he reported five days of worsening chest tightness and pressure, fatigue, swelling in his legs, and trouble breathing. His heart was racing at 134 beats per minute. His blood pressure was high at 146/106 mm Hg. Doctors described him as “muscular, tanned, anxious, and in mild distress.” Tests showed his heart was once again failing, with the left chamber pumping out just 20 percent of its blood.

他入院时报告称，胸闷、胸压、疲劳、腿部肿胀和呼吸困难的症状已持续五天并不断恶化。他的心率高达每分钟134次，血压为146/106 mm Hg。医生形容他“肌肉发达、皮肤黝黑、焦虑且处于轻度痛苦中”。检查显示他的心脏再次衰竭，左心室泵血量仅为20%。

They ruled out infectious diseases that can cause heart inflammation (myocarditis), as well as autoimmune conditions, medication side effects, and toxins.
