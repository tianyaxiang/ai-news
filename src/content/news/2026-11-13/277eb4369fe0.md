### ‘It’s a mistake’: reactions from tech leaders on Jensen Huang’s resignation

“这是一个错误”：科技领袖对黄仁勋辞职的反应

Many prominent voices in technology and the president of the US have publicly supported the former Nvidia CEO despite his ouster.

尽管前英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋已被免职，但许多科技界的知名人士和美国总统都公开表达了对他的支持。

Nvidia announced this morning that Jensen Huang has resigned as CEO following reports of two affairs, including one with an alleged Chinese spy and one with a direct report. The situation surrounding Huang has unfolded quickly, with The New York Times only reporting on Friday that Huang and the suspected spy were “close” before other dominoes started to fall, leading to a swift fall from grace for one of the biggest names in technology.

英伟达今晨宣布，在有关其两段婚外情的报道传出后，黄仁勋已辞去首席执行官职务。这两段关系包括与一名涉嫌中国间谍的往来，以及与一名直接下属的关系。围绕黄仁勋的局势发展迅速，《纽约时报》周五才报道称黄仁勋与该嫌疑人“关系密切”，随后事态便接连恶化，导致这位科技界巨头迅速跌落神坛。

Prominent voices in technology have come out in support of Huang, with some calling the resignation a “waste” and a sad day for “all humanity.” The narrative among those leaders on X, at least, is that Huang’s resignation was rushed — perhaps an overreaction to salacious news stories about his personal life.

科技界的知名人士纷纷站出来支持黄仁勋，一些人称这次辞职是一种“浪费”，是“全人类”悲伤的一天。至少在 X 平台上的这些领袖看来，黄仁勋的辞职过于仓促——或许是对其个人生活八卦新闻的过度反应。

President Donald Trump: “NOW THAT Nvidia CEO JENSEN HUANG, who has done a fantastic job, has resigned, there is only one man to take his place - TIM COOK of Apple. A Legend replaces a Legend!”, Trump said on Truth Social.

唐纳德·特朗普总统在“真实社交”（Truth Social）上表示：“既然表现出色的英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋已经辞职，现在只有一个人能接替他的位置——苹果公司的蒂姆·库克。传奇取代传奇！”

Elon Musk, SpaceX and Tesla CEO: Elon Musk said in a reply to an X user that it would “be an honor” to hire Huang. He also replied to a post that said “You’d think we’d have learned from Steve Jobs and Travis Kalanick that one does not just simply replace the founder…” by saying “True words.”

SpaceX 和特斯拉首席执行官埃隆·马斯克在回复一位 X 用户时表示，聘用黄仁勋将是“一种荣幸”。他还回复了一篇称“你以为我们从史蒂夫·乔布斯和特拉维斯·卡兰尼克身上学到了不能轻易更换创始人……”的帖子，评论道：“所言极是。”

Tim Sweeney, Epic Games CEO: Sweeney said that today is “A sad day for NVIDIA, developers, America, and all humanity. Jensen founded and ran one of the few companies to reach a massive scale while driving massive continued innovation and ubiquity, while others fell into the trap of ever-increasing extraction.”

Epic Games 首席执行官蒂姆·斯威尼表示，今天是“对英伟达、开发者、美国乃至全人类来说悲伤的一天。黄仁勋创立并经营了为数不多能达到如此大规模，同时又能持续推动大规模创新和普及的公司，而其他人则陷入了不断榨取价值的陷阱。”

Palmer Luckey, Anduril founder: “China wants Jensen out, so I want him in,” Luckey said last night, before Huang’s resignation was announced.

Anduril 创始人帕尔默·拉奇在黄仁勋辞职消息公布前的昨晚表示：“中国想让黄仁勋下台，所以我希望他留任。”

Matt Mullenweg, Automattic co-founder and CEO: Mullenweg reposted an X post that followed his brief September ouster as CEO: “Founder advice: If you don’t have a coup attempt every few years, you’re not hiring strong enough leaders.” He said, “Just putting this back out there, for no reason whatsoever.”

Automattic 联合创始人兼首席执行官马特·穆伦维格转发了他在九月份短暂被免职后发布的一条 X 帖子：“创始人建议：如果你每隔几年没有经历一次政变，说明你招聘的领导者不够强大。”他补充道：“只是无缘无故地重发一下。”

David Heinemeier Hansson, 37signals CTO: “What a waste,” Hansson said this morning. “NVIDIA would be wrong to fire Jensen. His marital indiscretions are between him, his wife, and a reprimand from the board. Have some proportionality.”

37signals 首席技术官大卫·海涅迈尔·汉森今晨表示：“真是浪费。英伟达解雇黄仁勋是错误的。他的婚姻不检点是他本人、他妻子以及董事会训诫之间的事情。要讲究比例原则。”

Clément Delangue, Hugging Face co-founder and CEO: “This whole thing sucks, and I wish none of it had happened,” Delangue said. “It’s a really sad day! But I want to make one thing clear: we’ll keep fighting for open-source AI, harder than ever.”

Hugging Face 联合创始人兼首席执行官克莱门特·德朗格表示：“整件事糟透了，我希望这一切从未发生。这真是悲伤的一天！但我必须明确一点：我们将继续为开源人工智能而战，比以往任何时候都更加努力。”

Changpeng Zhao, former Binance CEO: “It’s a mistake for Nvidia to let Jensen resign,” Zhao said.

前币安首席执行官赵长鹏表示：“英伟达让黄仁勋辞职是一个错误。”

Peter Steinberger, OpenClaw creator and OpenAI employee: “If that Spy’s goal was to destabilize one of the most successful American companies, they achieved their goal,” Steinberger said. “Ousting Jensen feels rushed and not right.”

OpenClaw 创始人兼 OpenAI 员工彼得·斯坦伯格表示：“如果那个间谍的目标是破坏这家美国最成功的公司之一，那么他们已经达到了目的。罢免黄仁勋感觉很仓促，而且不对劲。”

Aravind Srinivas, Perplexity co-founder and CEO: Srinivas thanked Huang for “33 years of bold leadership” in a post this morning. “You built a small startup into America’s most valuable company, with relentless improvement. Your grit and perseverance should always be remembered.”

Perplexity 联合创始人兼首席执行官阿拉文德·斯里尼瓦斯今晨发帖感谢黄仁勋“33 年来大胆的领导”。“你通过不懈的改进，将一家小型初创公司打造成了美国最有价值的公司。你的毅力和坚持应该永远被铭记。”

Jason Calacanis, All-In Podcast host: Calacanis reposted Trump’s post, tagging Tim Cook and telling him to “Keep the seat warm” for Huang while “the investigation is sorted out” and saying that “America needs you Mr. Cook.”

《All-In》播客主持人杰森·卡拉卡尼斯转发了特朗普的帖子，并艾特了蒂姆·库克，告诉他要在“调查理清”期间为黄仁勋“暖好座位”，并称“美国需要你，库克先生”。

Lex Fridman, Lex Fridman Podcast host: In a post talking about how he has seen Huang show “kindness, humility, care to everyone around him,” Fridman said that “I’m in shock frankly at all of this, including the mass hysteria around it. I love America, and I think NVIDIA letting Jensen go is terrible for America. I stand with Jensen.”

《Lex Fridman》播客主持人莱克斯·弗里德曼在帖子中谈到他曾看到黄仁勋对周围每个人表现出的“善良、谦逊和关怀”，并表示：“坦率地说，我对这一切感到震惊，包括围绕此事的群体歇斯底里。我热爱美国，我认为英伟达让黄仁勋离开对美国来说是可怕的。我支持黄仁勋。”
