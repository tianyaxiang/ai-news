We caught the first trailer for the upcoming Netflix animated film Ray Gunn in September and were charmed by director Brad Bird’s blend of a Jetsons-like future with classic 1930s noir detective thrillers. Bird has described it as a mashup of The Maltese Falcon and Buck Rogers, and the latest trailer definitely gives off that energy.

我们在九月份看到了 Netflix 即将上映的动画电影《雷·冈》（Ray Gunn）的首支预告片，并被导演布拉德·伯德（Brad Bird）将“杰森一家”（Jetsons）式的未来感与 1930 年代经典黑色侦探惊悚片相结合的风格所吸引。伯德将其描述为《马耳他之鹰》（The Maltese Falcon）与《巴克·罗杰斯》（Buck Rogers）的结合体，而最新的预告片确实散发着这种气息。

As previously reported, Bird has directed some of our all-time favorite animated films: The Iron Giant (1999), The Incredibles (2004), and Ratatouille (2007), with the latter two snagging well-deserved Oscar wins. He’s been kicking around the idea for Ray Gunn for the last 30 years, working on his many other film projects while the Ray Gunn script—co-written by Matthew Robbins, who also co-wrote 1987’s Batteries Not Included with Bird—“stayed in a filing cabinet at Warner Brothers,” the director said earlier this year.

正如之前报道的那样，伯德曾执导过我们有史以来最喜欢的一些动画电影：《钢铁巨人》（1999 年）、《超人总动员》（2004 年）和《料理鼠王》（2007 年），后两部作品都赢得了当之无愧的奥斯卡奖。伯德在过去 30 年里一直构思着《雷·冈》的创意，在忙于其他电影项目的同时，这部由他和马修·罗宾斯（Matthew Robbins，曾与伯德共同创作 1987 年的《鬼马小精灵》）合写的剧本，“一直被锁在华纳兄弟的档案柜里”，导演在今年早些时候说道。

The film’s inspiration came when Bird heard the opening of the B-52s’ “Planet Claire” (a personal favorite that also provides the soundtrack to the new trailer) and initially thought it was a cover of Henry Mancini’s theme music from the classic TV show Peter Gunn. Bird is a longtime fan of film noir and hard-boiled detectives, and he came up with the idea of a futuristic detective named Ray Gunn (a play on “raygun,” of course).

这部电影的灵感来源于伯德听到 B-52 合唱团的《Planet Claire》（这是他个人的最爱，也为新预告片提供了配乐）的开头时，最初以为那是亨利·曼西尼（Henry Mancini）为经典电视剧《彼得·冈》（Peter Gunn）创作的主题曲的翻唱版。伯德是黑色电影和硬汉派侦探小说的长期粉丝，他由此构思出了一位名叫雷·冈（Ray Gunn，当然这是对“射线枪”raygun 的谐音梗）的未来侦探。

“It’s film noir and a hard-boiled detective story, but it’s got a lot more comedy and a lot more action than the average film noir,” said Bird. Per the official premise: “In Metropia, a gigantic city in an alternate future as seen from 1939, private eye Raymond Gunn (voiced by Sam Rockwell) is drawn into a case involving aliens, murder, and a multimedia star named Venus Nova (voiced by Scarlett Johansson).”

“这是一部黑色电影和硬汉侦探故事，但它比一般的黑色电影有更多的喜剧和动作元素，”伯德说。根据官方剧情简介：“在 1939 年视角下的平行未来，一座名为大都会（Metropia）的巨大城市中，私家侦探雷蒙德·冈（由山姆·洛克威尔配音）被卷入了一起涉及外星人、谋杀案以及一位名叫维纳斯·诺瓦（由斯嘉丽·约翰逊配音）的多媒体明星的案件中。”

Unlike Bird’s Pixar fare, Ray Gunn targets a slightly older demographic with more mature themes. In addition to Rockwell and Johansson, the all-star voice cast includes Tom Waits as Ray’s one-eyed alien colleague, Eyera (pronounced Ira); Matt Berry as alien mob boss Francis “Sluggy” Xavier; Bobby Cannavale as Venus’ husband, who hires Ray to spy on his wife; Alan Tudyk as Venus’ manager, Barry Vogel; Michael Chernus as a studio fixer named Sam; and Patton Oswalt as a hologram.

与伯德在皮克斯的作品不同，《雷·冈》针对的是年龄稍大的观众群体，主题也更为成熟。除了洛克威尔和约翰逊，全明星配音阵容还包括：汤姆·威兹（Tom Waits）饰演雷的独眼外星同事艾拉（Eyera，发音为 Ira）；马特·贝里（Matt Berry）饰演外星黑帮老大弗朗西斯·“鼻涕虫”·泽维尔（Francis “Sluggy” Xavier）；鲍比·坎纳瓦尔（Bobby Cannavale）饰演维纳斯的丈夫，他雇佣雷去监视自己的妻子；艾伦·图代克（Alan Tudyk）饰演维纳斯的经纪人巴里·沃格尔（Barry Vogel）；迈克尔·切尔努斯（Michael Chernus）饰演名为萨姆的制片厂“清理人”；以及帕顿·奥斯瓦尔特（Patton Oswalt）饰演的全息影像。

Bird provides the voices for Xavier’s alien henchmen, Gunsol and Musker. Kathryn Hunter, John Ratzenberger, and Jamie Costa will also voice as-yet-undisclosed characters. Ray Gunn releases on Netflix on December 18, 2026—the same day both Avengers: Doomsday and Dune: Part 3 hit theaters, so it will be alternative family fare for those disinclined to brave the holiday theatrical crowds. The film will also screen in select theaters on December 4, 2026.

伯德本人为泽维尔的外星手下冈索尔（Gunsol）和马斯克（Musker）配音。凯瑟琳·亨特（Kathryn Hunter）、约翰·拉岑贝格（John Ratzenberger）和杰米·科斯塔（Jamie Costa）也将为尚未公布的角色配音。《雷·冈》将于 2026 年 12 月 18 日在 Netflix 上映——这一天正好也是《复仇者联盟：末日》（Avengers: Doomsday）和《沙丘 3》（Dune: Part 3）在影院上映的日子，因此对于那些不想在假期去影院挤人群的观众来说，它将是一个不错的家庭观影选择。该片还将于 2026 年 12 月 4 日在部分影院上映。
