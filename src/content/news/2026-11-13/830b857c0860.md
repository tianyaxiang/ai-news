The Oura Ring 4 is almost as good as the newer model, but $150 cheaper.

Oura Ring 4 的性能几乎与新款型号不相上下，但价格却便宜了 150 美元。

Oura’s last-gen smart ring sports the same sensors and software as the latest version.

Oura 的上一代智能戒指采用了与最新版本相同的传感器和软件。

The previous-generation Oura Ring 4 is on sale for as low as $249 at Amazon, Best Buy, and Target, $100 off the retail price, and the first big discount on the device since July. The price varies a bit by size and color, with most silver and black models in stock at the lowest price between sizes 8 and 13. Best Buy also has an exclusive deep blue “midnight” color in stock for $279. If you’re not sure about your size, you can pick up an Oura Ring 4 sizing kit for $10 at Amazon or Target, which includes $10 in store credit towards an Oura Ring 4 purchase.

上一代 Oura Ring 4 目前在亚马逊、百思买 (Best Buy) 和塔吉特 (Target) 的售价低至 249 美元，比零售价优惠了 100 美元，这是该设备自 7 月以来的首次大幅折扣。价格会因尺寸和颜色略有不同，大多数 8 到 13 号的银色和黑色款式均以最低价格有货。百思买还独家提供售价 279 美元的深蓝色“午夜”款。如果您不确定自己的尺寸，可以在亚马逊或塔吉特以 10 美元的价格购买 Oura Ring 4 尺寸测量套件，其中包含可用于购买 Oura Ring 4 的 10 美元店内抵用金。

My colleague Victoria Song tested six different smart rings to compare their health and fitness tracking features, and Oura beat them all handily. The Oura Ring 4 improved on its predecessor with more accurate sensors, a slimmer design that leaves the competition in the dust, not to mention frequent software and feature updates. Accessing all of the features requires an active subscription, which is either $5.99 per month or $69.99 per year. The newer Oura Ring 5 is lighter and thinner, but otherwise includes the same sensors and software features as the Oura Ring 4. That makes this previous-generation deal even more appealing.

我的同事维多利亚·宋 (Victoria Song) 测试了六款不同的智能戒指，以比较它们的健康和健身追踪功能，而 Oura 轻松击败了所有竞争对手。Oura Ring 4 在前代产品的基础上进行了改进，配备了更精确的传感器，其更纤薄的设计让竞争对手望尘莫及，更不用说它还拥有频繁的软件和功能更新。要使用所有功能，需要订阅服务，费用为每月 5.99 美元或每年 69.99 美元。新款 Oura Ring 5 更轻、更薄，但在其他方面与 Oura Ring 4 包含相同的传感器和软件功能。这使得上一代产品的促销活动更具吸引力。
