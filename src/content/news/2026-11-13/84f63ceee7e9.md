Nvidia founder Jensen Huang was having a separate affair with an employee that has not been previously reported, Reuters reported on Thursday. Huang stepped down as CEO and board member for the company earlier today, in the wake of news of extramarital affairs with accused spy Alice Huang and Nvidia VP of Healthcare Kimberly Powell.

据路透社周四报道，英伟达创始人黄仁勋此前曾与一名员工有过一段未被披露的婚外情。在被曝出与被指控为间谍的爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang）以及英伟达医疗保健副总裁金伯利·鲍威尔（Kimberly Powell）存在婚外情后，黄仁勋已于今日早些时候辞去公司首席执行官和董事会成员职务。

CFO Colette Kress has stepped in as interim CEO, but no permanent successor has been named. Reuters is withholding the name of the new employee linked to Huang but says she is an employee of Nvidia.

首席财务官科莱特·克雷斯（Colette Kress）已出任临时首席执行官，但尚未任命正式继任者。路透社未透露与黄仁勋有关联的这名新员工的姓名，但表示她是英伟达的一名员工。

Huang has been called before Congress to testify about the national security implications of his relationship with Alice Huang in the coming weeks — but it’s unclear how much more will be revealed before the hearings take place. Already, his abrupt fall from grace is expected to have significant repercussions for the industry at large.

黄仁勋已被传唤在未来几周内前往国会，就他与爱丽丝·黄的关系所涉及的国家安全影响作证——但目前尚不清楚在听证会举行之前还会披露多少信息。可以预见，他突然跌落神坛将对整个行业产生重大影响。
