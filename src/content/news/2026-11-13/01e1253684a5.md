Hugging Face CEO Clément Delangue briefly commented on Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s departure on Thursday, calling it a “really sad day.” Nvidia had announced its plan to acquire the platform for open source AI models in September for $12.9 billion, making it the second-largest acquisition in the company’s history, just behind that of chipmaker Groq’s assets for $20 billion in December 2025.

Hugging Face 首席执行官克莱门特·德朗格（Clément Delangue）周四简要评论了英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋的离职，称这是“非常悲伤的一天”。英伟达曾在 9 月宣布计划以 129 亿美元收购该开源人工智能模型平台，这是该公司历史上第二大收购案，仅次于 2025 年 12 月以 200 亿美元收购芯片制造商 Groq 的资产。

“This whole thing sucks, and I wish none of it had happened. It’s a really sad day! But I want to make one thing clear: we’ll keep fighting for open-source AI, harder than ever. This is too important to slow down now. Let’s go!,” Delangue wrote on X.

“这一切糟透了，我希望这一切都没有发生。这真是悲伤的一天！但我想要明确一点：我们将继续为开源人工智能而战，比以往任何时候都更加努力。这件事太重要了，不能现在放慢脚步。加油！”德朗格在 X 上写道。

The post is interesting as it implies that Huang’s departure is potentially worrying to open source advocates, and he’s attempting to offer reassurance. As Nvidia’s CEO, Huang was known as a strong advocate for open source AI and open-weight models, which is why there could be concerns about how a company that’s no longer under his leadership will now approach this space.

这篇帖子很有意思，因为它暗示黄仁勋的离职可能会让开源倡导者感到担忧，而他正试图提供安抚。作为英伟达的首席执行官，黄仁勋以坚定支持开源人工智能和开放权重模型而闻名，这就是为什么人们可能会担心一家不再由他领导的公司将如何对待这一领域。

After Nvidia announced its plans to acquire Hugging Face, the U.S. Department of Justice began making inquiries about how the deal would impact competition in the AI market, Reuters reported in October. While Nvidia pledged that the platform would preserve open support, it would also allow the company to control a major distribution channel for AI models. The Hugging Face deal is still scheduled to close in the first half of 2027.

据路透社 10 月报道，在英伟达宣布收购 Hugging Face 的计划后，美国司法部开始调查该交易将如何影响人工智能市场的竞争。虽然英伟达承诺该平台将保持开放支持，但这也将使该公司能够控制人工智能模型的一个主要分发渠道。Hugging Face 的交易仍计划于 2027 年上半年完成。

Huang’s advocacy for open source and open-weight AI has long extended beyond Hugging Face. In July, the CEO published an AI policy letter as his first-ever post on the social network X. The letter, which was signed by 25 companies, including Microsoft, Meta, Hugging Face, IBM, Palantir, and others, urged the U.S. government not to over-restrict open-weight AI models.

黄仁勋对开源和开放权重人工智能的倡导早已超出了 Hugging Face 的范畴。7 月，这位首席执行官在社交网络 X 上发布了一封人工智能政策信，这也是他发布的首条帖子。这封信由包括微软、Meta、Hugging Face、IBM、Palantir 等在内的 25 家公司签署，敦促美国政府不要过度限制开放权重人工智能模型。

It said models that “anyone can download, inspect, modify, and run on their own infrastructure” are an important part of the AI ecosystem because they make AI “more accessible, adaptable, and widely available.” Huang also wrote that open-weight models expand access to the AI economy, strengthen competition, and give customers greater control, as they know they won’t become locked into a single provider.

信中称，“任何人都可以下载、检查、修改并在自己的基础设施上运行”的模型是人工智能生态系统的重要组成部分，因为它们使人工智能“更易于获取、更具适应性且更广泛可用”。黄仁勋还写道，开放权重模型扩大了对人工智能经济的参与，加强了竞争，并赋予客户更大的控制权，因为他们知道自己不会被锁定在单一供应商身上。

In August, Nvidia also announced a $6 billion deal with the coding startup Poolside to help build out an open AI ecosystem in the U.S. to help the country compete against Chinese companies like DeepSeek and Kimi K3, The Wall Street Journal reported at the time. Last month, Nvidia expanded its Nemotron family of open-weight models with a large 120-billion-parameter model for agents used by the enterprise as well as with two new lightweight Bolt models that run on PCs.

据《华尔街日报》当时报道，8 月，英伟达还宣布与编码初创公司 Poolside 达成 60 亿美元的交易，以帮助在美国建立一个开放的人工智能生态系统，助力该国与 DeepSeek 和 Kimi K3 等中国公司竞争。上个月，英伟达扩展了其 Nemotron 开放权重模型系列，推出了一个用于企业代理的 1200 亿参数大型模型，以及两个可在个人电脑上运行的轻量级 Bolt 模型。
