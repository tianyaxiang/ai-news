During an hour-long event on Thursday, Abel Avellan, the founder and chief executive of the telecommunications company AST SpaceMobile, laid out his vision for developing a large rocket to launch his Bluebird satellites into orbit. Avellan emphasized, over and over again, that AST plans to build a “simple” rocket. He paraphrased SpaceX founder Elon Musk several times, saying, “the best part is no part.”

在周四长达一小时的活动中，电信公司 AST SpaceMobile 的创始人兼首席执行官 Abel Avellan 阐述了他开发大型火箭以将 Bluebird 卫星送入轨道的愿景。Avellan 反复强调，AST 计划制造一枚“简单”的火箭。他多次引用 SpaceX 创始人埃隆·马斯克的话说：“最好的部分就是没有部分。”

Avellan emphasized that AST has already procured engines for its proposed Blacklift rocket, asserting that engine development accounts for 80 percent of a typical rocket’s development cycle. “Of course rocket engineers are going to get mad with me, but you can simplify it as a big tank feeding engines,” he said of the rocket. “So big tanks—we know how to do them. We know how to do them. We know how to do them in composite.”

Avellan 强调，AST 已经为其提议的 Blacklift 火箭采购了发动机，并断言发动机开发占典型火箭开发周期的 80%。他在谈到火箭时说：“当然，火箭工程师会对我生气，但你可以将其简化为一个为发动机提供燃料的大型储罐。所以大型储罐——我们知道怎么做。我们知道怎么做。我们知道如何用复合材料来做。”

I am not a rocket engineer, and I am not mad at Avellan. But he sure seems to be substantially underestimating the challenge of building rockets.

我不是火箭工程师，我也不生 Avellan 的气。但他似乎确实大大低估了制造火箭的挑战。

AST feels its hand is forced. Avellan also has a problem. AST SpaceMobile appears to be successful in building very large satellites, Bluebirds, that are capable of providing direct-to-cell capability from an orbit about 550km above Earth. The problem is there is a commercial launch crunch, and the company is struggling to get its satellites on orbit today, and projects to do so into the future.

AST 感到被迫采取行动。Avellan 也面临一个问题。AST SpaceMobile 似乎成功制造了非常大的 Bluebird 卫星，这些卫星能够在距离地球约 550 公里的轨道上提供直接连接手机的功能。问题在于目前存在商业发射紧缺，该公司目前难以将其卫星送入轨道，且预计未来也会如此。

“We’re not doing this thing because we like rockets,” he said during the presentation. “We’re not doing this thing because we want to be in the launch business. We want this because we have so much demand of our satellites, and as you have seen it, we cannot get enough launches.”

“我们做这件事不是因为我们喜欢火箭，”他在演讲中说。“我们做这件事不是因为我们想进入发射行业。我们想要这个是因为我们的卫星需求量很大，正如你们所见，我们无法获得足够的发射机会。”

The Blacklift rocket will be “simple,” he said, because it will be solely tailored to lift five Bluebird satellites into low-Earth orbit at a time. It will have 20 metric tons of lift capacity, a reusable first stage, and launch twice a month from a pad at Wallops Flight Facility in Virginia that formerly served the Antares rocket. It will have all composite tanks and three stages. And, Avellan said, it will be ready to launch in 2028.

他说，Blacklift 火箭将是“简单”的，因为它将专门用于一次将五颗 Bluebird 卫星送入近地轨道。它将拥有 20 公吨的运载能力，配备可重复使用的第一级，并每月从弗吉尼亚州瓦勒普斯飞行设施（曾用于安塔瑞斯火箭）的发射台发射两次。它将拥有全复合材料储罐和三个级。而且，Avellan 说，它将在 2028 年准备好发射。

If that timeline sounds preposterous, that’s because it is. However, Avellan believes AST has a workaround: It is committed to spending $1.3 billion to acquire enough engines for the first three Blacklift launches from Firefly Aerospace, as well as a license to manufacture additional engines over time. He is basing this argument on the Miranda first stage engine, which he said is “ready to fly.” But that’s not quite true.

如果这个时间表听起来荒谬，那是因为它确实如此。然而，Avellan 认为 AST 有变通办法：它承诺斥资 13 亿美元从 Firefly Aerospace 购买足够用于前三次 Blacklift 发射的发动机，并获得随时间推移制造额外发动机的许可。他的这一论点基于 Miranda 第一级发动机，他称该发动机“已准备好飞行”。但这并不完全属实。

However, Miranda has not yet completed its formal qualification campaign, much less flown on an orbital rocket. Firefly chief executive Jason Kim said this week that qualification testing remains underway. Moreover, little is known publicly about the readiness of the upper stage engine, Vira, that AST is also acquiring from Firefly for its rocket.

然而，Miranda 尚未完成其正式的鉴定工作，更不用说在轨道火箭上飞行了。Firefly 首席执行官 Jason Kim 本周表示，鉴定测试仍在进行中。此外，关于 AST 也从 Firefly 为其火箭采购的上级发动机 Vira 的准备情况，公众知之甚少。

Composites are hard, too. Another questionable assertion is Avellan’s statement that 80 percent of a rocket’s design cycle is due to engine development. “I mean, that has been proven,” he said. I’m not sure it has. Propulsion is definitely one of the most challenging aspects of developing a rocket. However, Rocket Lab, which has considerably more experience building orbital rockets than AST, has suffered two significant setbacks this year involving Neutron’s composite propellant tanks.

复合材料也很难。Avellan 关于火箭设计周期的 80% 是由于发动机开发的说法是另一个值得怀疑的断言。“我的意思是，这已经被证明了，”他说。我不确定是否如此。推进系统绝对是开发火箭最具挑战性的方面之一。然而，比 AST 在制造轨道火箭方面拥有更多经验的 Rocket Lab，今年在 Neutron 的复合材料推进剂储罐方面遭遇了两次重大挫折。

These structures, as well as flight software, avionics, stage separation, payload fairings, qualification testing, and so much more, can lead to significant delays in the development of a rocket. And then there are the equally difficult challenges of scaling from development to production at rate, and recovering the first stage.

这些结构，以及飞行软件、航空电子设备、级间分离、有效载荷整流罩、鉴定测试等等，都可能导致火箭开发出现重大延误。此外，从开发扩展到批量生产以及回收第一级也同样具有挑战性。

Avellan and Scott Wisniewski, president of AST SpaceMobile, also did not get into specifics about financing the development of Blacklift. The company has already invested more than $1 billion in engines and will likely need billions more to develop and scale the vehicle. Wisniewski said AST SpaceMobile plans to spend “several hundred million dollars” in 2027 on Blacklift development, but it is not clear whether that includes milestone payments to Firefly for propulsion.

Avellan 和 AST SpaceMobile 总裁 Scott Wisniewski 也没有详细说明 Blacklift 的开发融资情况。该公司已经在发动机上投资了超过 10 亿美元，可能还需要数十亿美元来开发和扩展该运载工具。Wisniewski 表示，AST SpaceMobile 计划在 2027 年在 Blacklift 开发上花费“数亿美元”，但不清楚这是否包括向 Firefly 支付的推进系统里程碑款项。

According to the company’s balance sheet, it had about $2.8 billion in unrestricted cash and cash equivalents at the end of September, and it has historically been successful in raising additional capital.

根据该公司的资产负债表，截至 9 月底，它拥有约 28 亿美元的非限制性现金和现金等价物，并且在历史上成功筹集了额外资金。

“We don’t insult them.” The company’s underlying business plan makes sense. There is a potentially very large market for space-to-ground connectivity for mobile devices. Traditional mobile service operators are growing wary about working with SpaceX, viewing the company as a competitor rather than a partner. And the recent issues with Starlink in Ukraine may lead some nations to seek alternative partners for these services.

“我们不侮辱他们。”该公司的基本商业计划是有道理的。移动设备的空对地连接市场潜力巨大。传统的移动服务运营商对与 SpaceX 合作越来越谨慎，将该公司视为竞争对手而非合作伙伴。最近乌克兰星链（Starlink）出现的问题可能会导致一些国家为这些服务寻求替代合作伙伴。

“First of all, I’m no Musk, and I don’t want to be,” Avellan said on Thursday. “We partner. We don’t compete. We enhance. … Why? Because we partner with them. We don’t compete with them. We don’t insult them. You know, we partner with them.”

“首先，我不是马斯克，我也不想成为他，”Avellan 周四说。“我们合作。我们不竞争。我们增强……为什么？因为我们与他们合作。我们不与他们竞争。我们不侮辱他们。你知道，我们与他们合作。”

SpaceX went from being a rocket company to operating satellites. Now Avellan and AST seek to go from being a satellite operator to launching rockets. Consider that SpaceX took eight years from its founding to launch the Falcon 9, another five years to successfully land a booster, and several more years to achieve a high flight rate. AST is seeking to compress much of that process into approximately two years.

SpaceX 从一家火箭公司转变为运营卫星的公司。现在，Avellan 和 AST 寻求从卫星运营商转变为发射火箭的公司。考虑到 SpaceX 从成立到发射猎鹰 9 号用了八年时间，又用了五年时间成功回收助推器，又用了几年时间才实现高飞行频率。AST 试图将大部分过程压缩在大约两年内。

But rocket science is in no way simple, and the road ahead is sure to be rocky. Avellan said AST doesn’t really want to be in the launch business. Given how difficult, demanding, and expensive the launch business is, he may get his wish in a few years.

但火箭科学绝非简单，前方的道路注定坎坷。Avellan 表示，AST 并不真的想进入发射行业。考虑到发射业务是多么困难、苛刻且昂贵，他几年后可能会如愿以偿。
