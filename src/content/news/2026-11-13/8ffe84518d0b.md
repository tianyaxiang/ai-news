Google Search is getting better at understanding complicated questions, including those with multiple parts, highly specific requests, or confusing typos. The updates are coming via the company’s newly released Gemini 4 Hydrogen model, which Google claims is its “biggest model improvement to Search yet.”

谷歌搜索在理解复杂问题方面正变得越来越好，包括那些包含多个部分、高度具体的要求或令人困惑的拼写错误的问题。这些更新来自该公司新发布的 Gemini 4 Hydrogen 模型，谷歌称这是其“迄今为止对搜索进行的最大模型改进”。

While Google’s frontier models, Gemini 4 Carbon and Gemini 4 Argon, are best at deep reasoning for hard problems, Hydrogen is designed for fast execution across a variety of agentic workflows, or tasks in which an AI carries out multiple steps on its own, the company explained in an announcement. The idea behind the model’s release is that using the most powerful model isn’t always needed — sometimes a faster, cheaper, and reasonably smart one will do.

虽然谷歌的前沿模型 Gemini 4 Carbon 和 Gemini 4 Argon 最擅长处理难题的深度推理，但 Hydrogen 的设计初衷是在各种代理工作流（即人工智能自主执行多个步骤的任务）中实现快速执行，该公司在公告中解释道。发布该模型的理念是，并不总是需要使用最强大的模型——有时一个更快、更便宜且足够聪明的模型就足够了。

Google says Hydrogen completes tasks 2.5 times faster than Gemini 3.8 Flash, its previous fast model, but it’s still pretty smart, even beating the more powerful Gemini 4 Argon on one broad intelligence benchmark, the Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index, at its default high thinking level. In Search, Hydrogen brings several improvements, including a better understanding of user intent, the ability to offer more helpful links and visual responses, and better answers across the different languages supported by Google’s AI Mode.

谷歌表示，Hydrogen 完成任务的速度比其之前的快速模型 Gemini 3.8 Flash 快 2.5 倍，但它依然非常聪明，甚至在默认的高思维水平下，在“人工智能分析智能指数”（Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index）这一广泛的智能基准测试中击败了更强大的 Gemini 4 Argon。在搜索方面，Hydrogen 带来了多项改进，包括更好地理解用户意图、提供更有帮助的链接和视觉响应的能力，以及在谷歌 AI 模式支持的不同语言中提供更好的答案。

In terms of intent, Google says Hydrogen can handle more nuanced and complicated questions more quickly. This means people can ask their questions in their natural train of thought, including those that have multiple parts or that include highly specific requests. Hydrogen is also better at navigating hard-to-understand typos, Google says.

在意图方面，谷歌表示 Hydrogen 可以更快地处理更细致、更复杂的问题。这意味着人们可以用自然的思维方式提出问题，包括那些有多个部分或包含高度具体要求的问题。谷歌称，Hydrogen 在处理难以理解的拼写错误方面也表现得更好。

Because of its improvements and better ability to follow instructions and call the right tools, Hydrogen is able to more precisely address each part of a user’s question. Its advanced reasoning also improves Search’s fan-out technique, which breaks down a single user’s prompt into multiple subqueries. This allows people to discover websites related to the query that they may have otherwise missed, the blog post explains.

由于其改进以及在遵循指令和调用正确工具方面能力的提升，Hydrogen 能够更精确地解决用户问题的每个部分。其先进的推理能力还改进了搜索的“扇出”（fan-out）技术，该技术将用户的单个提示分解为多个子查询。博客文章解释说，这使得人们能够发现与查询相关但原本可能会错过的网站。

The addition of Hydrogen to Search will also mean more images, structured responses, comparison tables, and custom tools will appear as visual responses to users’ queries in AI Mode.

将 Hydrogen 加入搜索还意味着在 AI 模式下，更多的图像、结构化响应、比较表格和自定义工具将作为用户查询的视觉响应出现。

Google also says Hydrogen can work on its own for everyday work in areas like writing code, running tests, or verifying code changes, or it can be paired with Carbon and Argon in workflows that combine several models. The model offers four thinking levels: low, medium, high (default), and a new, extra-high tier (“xhigh”), all of which are designed to complete tasks faster with fewer output tokens.

谷歌还表示，Hydrogen 可以独立完成日常工作，如编写代码、运行测试或验证代码更改，也可以与 Carbon 和 Argon 配合使用，在结合多个模型的工作流中发挥作用。该模型提供四个思维级别：低、中、高（默认）以及一个新的超高层级（“xhigh”），所有这些级别都旨在以更少的输出标记更快地完成任务。

On multi-step tasks like deep research, data analysis, and broad codebase navigation, Hydrogen ranks No. 5 on Agent Arena’s benchmarks, the company pointed out. In addition to Search, Gemini 4 Hydrogen is coming to all tiers of Google’s AI Studio, the Gemini API, the Gemini App, and the paid AI Pro and Ultra subscribers in Google’s Antigravity development platform. Google says the Hydrogen update is rolling out to all Search users globally.

该公司指出，在深度研究、数据分析和广泛的代码库导航等多步骤任务中，Hydrogen 在 Agent Arena 的基准测试中排名第五。除了搜索之外，Gemini 4 Hydrogen 还将进入谷歌 AI Studio 的所有层级、Gemini API、Gemini 应用，以及谷歌 Antigravity 开发平台中付费的 AI Pro 和 Ultra 订阅用户。谷歌表示，Hydrogen 更新正在向全球所有搜索用户推出。
