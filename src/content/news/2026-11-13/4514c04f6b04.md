Google is continuing its elemental AI naming scheme with the new Gemini 4 Hydrogen. These names are less descriptive than the Pro/Flash monikers of earlier releases, but Hydrogen’s purpose is clear: It replaces the faster, more economical Flash models most people in Google’s ecosystem use.

谷歌正在延续其以元素命名的 AI 命名方案，推出了全新的 Gemini 4 Hydrogen。这些名称相较于早期版本中的 Pro/Flash 称呼，描述性有所减弱，但 Hydrogen 的定位非常明确：它旨在取代谷歌生态系统中大多数用户所使用的、更快速且更经济的 Flash 模型。

With Google’s recent Argon and Carbon releases, the rollout was staged and began with cybersecurity researchers. Even when general availability began, Google restricted access to users paying for its most expensive AI subscription. The limits for Hydrogen are much more relaxed, with access beginning immediately. This model has replaced Gemini 3.8 Flash across the company’s products.

在谷歌近期发布 Argon 和 Carbon 时，其推广采取了分阶段策略，首先面向网络安全研究人员开放。即便在全面开放后，谷歌也仅限付费订阅其最昂贵 AI 服务的用户使用。而 Hydrogen 的限制则宽松得多，用户可以立即获得访问权限。目前，该模型已在谷歌的各项产品中取代了 Gemini 3.8 Flash。

Google claims that Hydrogen represents a major improvement versus the previous Flash model. It runs in one of four thinking levels (low, medium, high, and the new xhigh), and even in high mode, it’s 2.5 times faster than Flash 3.8. Plus, it reduces “fabricated claims” by more than 5x, according to Google’s analysis.

谷歌声称，与之前的 Flash 模型相比，Hydrogen 实现了重大改进。它提供四种思维级别（低、中、高以及全新的超高模式），即使在“高”模式下，其运行速度也比 Flash 3.8 快 2.5 倍。此外，根据谷歌的分析，它将“虚假陈述”减少了 5 倍以上。

Users will also see gains in code generation, with Hydrogen more than tripling 3.8 Flash’s score in Terminal-Bench. In some cases, Hydrogen even trades blows with Argon. It managed a score of 54 in the popular Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index with xhigh mode versus 50 for Argon.

用户在代码生成方面也将获得提升，Hydrogen 在 Terminal-Bench 测试中的得分是 Flash 3.8 的三倍多。在某些情况下，Hydrogen 甚至能与 Argon 旗鼓相当。在流行的“人工智能分析指数”（Artificial Analysis Intelligence Index）中，Hydrogen 在超高模式下获得了 54 分，而 Argon 为 50 分。

Google believes Hydrogen will be a key part of how developers work with agentic coding tools. While it still positions Argon and Carbon as the best for long-horizon tasks and security, Hydrogen’s speed makes it ideal for generating simpler code and running tests without blowing through token budgets.

谷歌认为，Hydrogen 将成为开发者使用代理编码工具的关键部分。虽然谷歌仍将 Argon 和 Carbon 定位为处理长期任务和安全问题的最佳选择，但 Hydrogen 的速度使其成为生成简单代码和运行测试的理想工具，且不会耗尽 Token 预算。

Hydrogen is also being integrated with Google Search, which the company says is the biggest update to its AI-powered search tools ever. The new model should better handle long, multipart questions, and the responses in both AI Overviews and AI Mode will have more visual elements. The company also claims Hydrogen’s fan-out search has been tuned to link to more sources, but we’ve heard that from Google before.

Hydrogen 也正在整合进谷歌搜索中，公司称这是其 AI 搜索工具史上最大的一次更新。新模型应能更好地处理冗长且包含多个部分的问题，AI 概览（AI Overviews）和 AI 模式下的回复也将包含更多视觉元素。谷歌还声称 Hydrogen 的扇出式搜索（fan-out search）经过调整，可以链接到更多来源，但我们以前也听过谷歌类似的说法。

Everyone will reportedly get access to Hydrogen in search, but it’s a bit more restricted in the Gemini app and AI Studio. The new model is live for AI Pro and Ultra subscribers, but AI Plus and free users are still stuck with the old 3.5 Flash-Lite model.

据报道，所有人都能在搜索中使用 Hydrogen，但在 Gemini 应用和 AI Studio 中则受到更多限制。新模型已向 AI Pro 和 Ultra 订阅用户开放，但 AI Plus 和免费用户仍只能使用旧的 3.5 Flash-Lite 模型。

Developers can plug into Hydrogen via the API at $1.50 per million input tokens and $7.50 per million output tokens, which is unchanged from 3.8 Flash. Carbon and Argon are currently $2/$10, but they will go up to $4/$20 starting next month.

开发者可以通过 API 接入 Hydrogen，价格为每百万输入 Token 1.50 美元，每百万输出 Token 7.50 美元，这与 3.8 Flash 的价格保持一致。Carbon 和 Argon 目前的价格为 2 美元/10 美元，但从下个月起将涨至 4 美元/20 美元。
