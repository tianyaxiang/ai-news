# The unfolding Jensen Huang scandal: a timeline

Nvidia co-founder and now former CEO Jensen Huang has never been as flashy or controversy-prone as AI leaders like OpenAI’s Sam Altman or SpaceXAI’s Elon Musk. Now, the 63-year-old industry kingpin has admitted that he cheated on his wife of 40 years in affairs that could have ramifications beyond Huang’s personal life and U.S. national security.

英伟达联合创始人、现已离任的首席执行官黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）从未像 OpenAI 的萨姆·奥特曼（Sam Altman）或 SpaceXAI 的埃隆·马斯克（Elon Musk）那样张扬或充满争议。如今，这位 63 岁的行业巨头承认，他在长达 40 年的婚姻中出轨，这些婚外情可能带来的影响已超出了黄仁勋的个人生活范畴，甚至波及美国国家安全。

U.S. officials believe that one of Huang’s likely romantic interests, Megaspeed executive Alice Huang, is a Chinese spy who has been feeding information back to Beijing. (Alice Huang, a Chinese citizen, is of no relation to Jensen Huang.) Megaspeed is a Singapore-based AI infrastructure company that reportedly purchased $4.8 billion in Nvidia chips over the last two years. The U.S. and Singapore have both previously investigated Megaspeed over suspicions that the firm was illegally sharing chips with China. The Justice Department has since taken over the federal investigation into the company, The New York Times has reported.

美国官员认为，黄仁勋可能的绯闻对象之一、Megaspeed 高管爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang）是一名中国间谍，一直在向北京输送情报。（爱丽丝·黄是中国公民，与黄仁勋并无亲属关系。）Megaspeed 是一家总部位于新加坡的人工智能基础设施公司，据报道在过去两年中购买了价值 48 亿美元的英伟达芯片。美国和新加坡此前都曾因怀疑该公司非法向中国转售芯片而对其进行调查。据《纽约时报》报道，司法部此后已接手对该公司的联邦调查。

The story took a sudden new turn on Wednesday. Citing five people who worked at Nvidia, the Times reported that Huang also had an extramarital relationship with Kimberly Powell, the company’s vice president of healthcare, who reports directly to him. Nvidia, Huang, and Powell have not confirmed the report. Reached by a Times reporter, Huang reportedly said, “I don’t think you should be talking to me directly,” before hanging up. By early Thursday morning, Huang was no longer CEO of the company he co-founded 33 years ago.

周三，事态突然出现了新的转折。《纽约时报》援引五名英伟达员工的话报道称，黄仁勋还与公司医疗保健副总裁金伯利·鲍威尔（Kimberly Powell）存在婚外情，而鲍威尔直接向他汇报工作。英伟达、黄仁勋和鲍威尔均未证实该报道。据报道，当《纽约时报》记者联系到黄仁勋时，他说：“我认为你不应该直接跟我谈，”随后挂断了电话。到周四清晨，黄仁勋已不再担任他 33 年前共同创立的公司的首席执行官。

Then, Thursday afternoon, Reuters dropped another bombshell, publishing a report alleging that Huang had yet another extramarital affair with another unnamed Nvidia employee. The U.S. and China are embroiled in a fierce technological arms race to build the most advanced AI infrastructure, which could shape each country’s economic future, military prowess, and capacity for innovation. Nvidia is valuable in this competition as one of the world’s largest makers of AI chips, which are essential for accelerating AI development. But as an American company, Nvidia faces government restrictions on the sale of its most powerful chips to Chinese entities.

随后，周四下午，路透社投下另一枚重磅炸弹，发布报道称黄仁勋与另一名未具名的英伟达员工还有一段婚外情。美中两国正陷入一场激烈的技术军备竞赛，旨在构建最先进的人工智能基础设施，这可能决定两国的经济未来、军事实力和创新能力。作为全球最大的 AI 芯片制造商之一，英伟达在这一竞争中价值巨大，因为这些芯片对于加速人工智能发展至关重要。但作为一家美国公司，英伟达在向中国实体销售其最强大的芯片时面临政府限制。
