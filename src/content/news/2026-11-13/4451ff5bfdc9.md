Representatives from Nvidia told Illinois representative Bill Foster that it would be technically possible to implement the anti-smuggling measures called for in the Chip Security Act, Foster told MIT Technology Review in an interview on Thursday, even while an industry group claimed they were “potentially infeasible.”

英伟达的代表告诉伊利诺伊州众议员比尔·福斯特（Bill Foster），实施《芯片安全法案》（Chip Security Act）所要求的反走私措施在技术上是可行的。福斯特在周四接受《麻省理工科技评论》采访时透露了这一点，尽管一个行业组织曾声称这些措施“可能不可行”。

Foster, a Democrat who serves on the Committee on Science, Space, and Technology, is a cosponsor of the act, which would introduce geotracking requirements to verify the location of advanced AI chips in order to prevent their smuggling to China. It is one of three chip-security bills under consideration for inclusion in the Senate’s National Defense Authorization Act, each of which is drawing renewed attention after Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang’s resignation and reported affair with an alleged Chinese spy.

福斯特是一位民主党人，在科学、空间和技术委员会任职，他也是该法案的共同发起人。该法案将引入地理追踪要求，以核实先进人工智能芯片的位置，从而防止其被走私到中国。这是参议院《国防授权法案》正在考虑纳入的三项芯片安全法案之一，在英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋辞职及其被曝与一名涉嫌中国间谍的人员有染后，这些法案都受到了新的关注。

When the Chip Security Act was introduced, in May 2025, lawmakers said the technology to implement it existed, something Nvidia has not publicly confirmed. In March 2026, the Semiconductor Industry Association, of which Nvidia is a member, explicitly opposed the bill and wrote that its mandates for chip tracking were “new, untested, and potentially infeasible.”

2025年5月《芯片安全法案》出台时，立法者表示实施该法案的技术已经存在，但英伟达并未公开证实这一点。2026年3月，英伟达所属的半导体行业协会明确反对该法案，并写道其关于芯片追踪的规定是“新的、未经测试的，且可能不可行”。

But privately, Nvidia representatives working with Foster told the lawmaker that the company could implement them. “They never put anything in writing,” Foster says, but during technical discussions on the possibility of such safeguards, their reaction was “yeah, of course we can do this.” This private acknowledgment to Foster goes further than anything Nvidia has previously made public.

但在私下里，与福斯特合作的英伟达代表告诉这位议员，公司是可以实施这些措施的。福斯特说：“他们从未留下任何书面记录”，但在讨论此类保障措施可能性的技术会议上，他们的反应是“是的，我们当然能做到”。这种对福斯特的私下承认，比英伟达此前公开的任何表态都要深入。

Other technology Nvidia has built for its customers suggests that the company can indeed offer accurate tracking of its chips’ locations. In December 2025, for example, it announced a new tool called Fleet Intelligence that would allow customers to monitor how its advanced chips were performing across different data center locations. In doing so, the tool could indicate which country chips were in, Nvidia confirmed to Reuters, but the company would not elaborate on whether this technology could be used to prevent smuggling.

英伟达为客户构建的其他技术表明，该公司确实能够提供对其芯片位置的精确追踪。例如，2025年12月，该公司宣布推出一款名为“车队智能”（Fleet Intelligence）的新工具，允许客户监控其先进芯片在不同数据中心位置的运行情况。英伟达向路透社证实，该工具可以显示芯片所在的国家，但该公司拒绝详细说明这项技术是否可用于防止走私。

Nvidia did not respond to requests for comment or confirmation of Foster’s characterization. In early October, when lawmakers were reportedly increasingly concerned about Nvidia’s role in smuggling, MIT Technology Review asked the company if Fleet Intelligence could be used to prevent the practice. It declined to answer directly, instead sending a statement: “Governments and media have found insignificant diversion of controlled US products—a drop in the bucket compared to the ocean of domestic compute China already has.”

英伟达没有回应置评请求，也没有确认福斯特的说法。10月初，据报道立法者对英伟达在走私中的角色日益担忧，《麻省理工科技评论》询问该公司“车队智能”是否可用于防止此类行为。该公司拒绝直接回答，而是发表了一份声明：“政府和媒体发现受控美国产品的转移微不足道——与中国已经拥有的庞大国内计算能力相比，这只是沧海一粟。”

The recent fallout at Nvidia involves a number of people and companies alleged to be involved in chip smuggling. Alice Huang, with whom Jensen Huang reportedly had an affair, runs Megaspeed, a Singaporean firm now under investigation for smuggling advanced GPUs to China. A US Commerce Department inquiry into the company was reportedly halted after invention by Nvidia. Megaspeed reportedly bought chips from Supermicro, a company that packages Nvidia’s chips onto servers.

英伟达近期的风波涉及多名被指控参与芯片走私的人员和公司。据报道与黄仁勋有染的爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang）经营着一家名为Megaspeed的新加坡公司，该公司目前正因向中国走私先进GPU而受到调查。据报道，在美国商务部对该公司进行调查后，英伟达进行了干预，调查随后被叫停。据称，Megaspeed从超微电脑（Supermicro）购买芯片，该公司负责将英伟达的芯片封装到服务器中。

The Chip Security Act as drafted would require a mechanism to track the locations of chips, either by inspections of facilities, attestations by companies, or ping-based location mechanisms. The ping-based technique, the hardest option to circumvent, would require the GPU to send a signal of its location when it comes online. In September, the Center for Security and Emerging Technology found it would be the most cost-effective option.

《芯片安全法案》草案要求建立一种追踪芯片位置的机制，可以通过设施检查、公司证明或基于“ping”的定位机制来实现。基于“ping”的技术是最难规避的选择，它要求GPU在上线时发送其位置信号。9月，安全与新兴技术中心发现，这将是最具成本效益的选择。

Foster said he did not ask Nvidia’s representatives why they weren’t voluntarily adopting such chip-tracking tools if they could technically do so. But if the requirements of the bill pass as part of the Senate’s National Defense Authorization Act, the new tracking mechanisms would only tell investigators where the chips are, not offer a way to disconnect them if it’s revealed they have been smuggled.

福斯特表示，他没有问英伟达的代表，如果他们在技术上能做到，为什么不主动采用这种芯片追踪工具。但如果该法案的要求作为参议院《国防授权法案》的一部分获得通过，新的追踪机制将只能告诉调查人员芯片在哪里，而无法在发现芯片被走私时提供断开连接的方法。

Foster told MIT Technology Review he intends to introduce new, stricter legislation in the new year that would offer a workaround. The new rule would require licenses to operate advanced GPUs, and before a GPU could connect, its valid license would need to be verified. In the meantime, the controversy at Nvidia, Foster says, has fueled support among his colleagues for the rules in the bill: “There will be a lot of political pressure, in the wake of everything that’s happened, on the companies to actually do a very good job of the geographic validation of where the chip actually is.”

福斯特告诉《麻省理工科技评论》，他打算在新的一年里提出更严格的新立法，以提供解决方案。新规定将要求操作先进GPU必须获得许可，在GPU连接之前，必须验证其有效许可证。与此同时，福斯特表示，英伟达的争议激发了他同事们对法案中规则的支持：“在发生这一切之后，公司将面临巨大的政治压力，要求它们切实做好芯片实际地理位置的验证工作。”
