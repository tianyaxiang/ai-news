Each capture was scaled to 1600x1200 with nearest-neighbour pixels and 4:3 aspect correction, so every original pixel keeps the shape it had on a period monitor. The PC 16-color screens come from a Spanish copy and the Atari ST mono ones from a French copy; all the others are in English.

[■]CRT shader · DOSBox Staging The CRT monitors of DOSBox Staging, ported to WebGL2: its presets for Hyllian's CRT shader, and its own VGA shaders where a screen has few pixels. Each version is drawn from its native pixels on the monitor closest to its own, with a whole number of screen pixels for every scanline where the size allows. PC, FM Towns31 kHz VGA monitor. Each of the 200 lines is drawn twice: 400 fine scanlines. MacThe same kind of monitor, at 480 lines. EGA, CGAThe 31 / 15 kHz button swaps the VGA monitor for the card's own 15.7 kHz one: 200 lines. Amiga, ST, Sega CD15 kHz monitor or TV, with DOSBox's arcade presets: dark gaps between the 200 lines. Hercules, ST monoNo mask. Hercules in white, green or amber phosphor.
