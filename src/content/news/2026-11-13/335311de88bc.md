# Google Search gets a major upgrade with Gemini 4 Hydrogen

Google Search 迎来重大升级，Gemini 4 Hydrogen 正式发布。

We are bringing Gemini 4 Hydrogen to Google Search globally to provide you with more intuitive, visual, and precise answers. This update helps you navigate complex questions by understanding your intent, conducting simultaneous queries, and displaying structured information like tables and images.

我们将 Gemini 4 Hydrogen 带到全球 Google Search，旨在为您提供更直观、更具视觉效果且更精准的答案。此次更新通过理解您的意图、执行同步查询并展示表格和图像等结构化信息，帮助您应对复杂的问题。

We are introducing Gemini 4 Hydrogen to provide more helpful and intuitive responses in Google Search. You can now ask complex, multi-part questions because our model understands your intent with greater precision. Our improved reasoning capabilities connect you to a wider range of relevant websites and helpful links.

我们引入 Gemini 4 Hydrogen，旨在为 Google Search 提供更有帮助且更直观的响应。现在，您可以提出复杂的多部分问题，因为我们的模型能以更高的精度理解您的意图。我们改进后的推理能力将为您连接到更广泛的相关网站和实用链接。

Today, Google Search is bringing cutting-edge intelligence to everyone with Gemini 4 Hydrogen. This is the biggest model upgrade to Search yet. Hydrogen is now available globally in Google Search for all users, delivering more helpful, visual, and intuitive responses with improved model capabilities.

今天，Google Search 通过 Gemini 4 Hydrogen 将前沿智能带给每一个人。这是搜索迄今为止最大规模的模型升级。Hydrogen 现已在全球 Google Search 中面向所有用户开放，凭借改进的模型能力，提供更有帮助、更具视觉效果且更直观的响应。

Better understanding of intent: Search can now handle even more nuanced and complicated questions, quickly understanding what you need. Just ask in your natural flow of thought — including questions with multiple parts, hard-to-understand typos, or highly specific requests.

更好地理解意图：搜索现在可以处理更细致、更复杂的问题，并快速理解您的需求。只需按照您自然的思维方式提问即可，包括包含多个部分的问题、难以理解的错别字或高度具体的要求。

Improved query fan-out and linking: Hydrogen’s advanced reasoning supercharges our query fan-out technique in Search, connecting you to a broader range of helpful links than before. By more thoroughly understanding your question and conducting simultaneous queries across all the relevant subtopics, Search helps you find sites you previously might not have discovered.

改进的查询分发与链接：Hydrogen 先进的推理能力增强了搜索中的查询分发技术，为您连接到比以往更广泛的实用链接。通过更透彻地理解您的问题并在所有相关子主题中进行同步查询，搜索能帮助您发现以前可能未曾注意到的网站。

More visual responses: You get highly adaptive, visual, and easy-to-scan responses that are uniquely relevant to your needs — at the speed of Search. You’ll see more images, structured responses, comparison tables, and custom tools built for you.

更具视觉效果的响应：您将获得高度自适应、可视化且易于浏览的响应，这些响应与您的需求高度相关，且具备搜索的速度。您将看到更多图像、结构化响应、对比表格以及为您定制的工具。

Richer language understanding: No matter what language you ask in, you’ll now get more highly nuanced and helpful responses across all supported AI Mode languages. Try out Gemini 4 Hydrogen in Search today.

更丰富的语言理解：无论您使用何种语言提问，现在都能在所有支持的 AI 模型语言中获得更细致、更有帮助的响应。今天就来搜索中体验 Gemini 4 Hydrogen 吧。
