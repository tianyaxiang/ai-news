# FAA expands AI advice to manage air traffic congestion across entire US airspace

The US government has expanded the use of an AI tool for advising air traffic controllers to assist in managing air traffic across the entire national airspace system. Airline executives have voiced support for the rollout, but the US labor union representing air traffic controllers has said it was not involved in designing or testing the AI-driven system.

美国政府已扩大使用一种人工智能工具，旨在为航空交通管制员提供建议，以协助管理整个国家空域系统的空中交通。航空公司高管对这一举措表示支持，但代表航空交通管制员的美国工会表示，他们并未参与该人工智能系统的设计或测试。

The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) says the $875 million SMART system has helped reroute 180 flights since being implemented in airspace over Washington, DC, on September 21. The agency claims that this saved an average of 10 minutes per flight and almost 80 gallons of fuel per flight—and it is apparently hoping such results can be replicated nationwide through the expanded deployment.

美国联邦航空管理局（FAA）表示，自9月21日在华盛顿特区上空实施以来，耗资8.75亿美元的SMART系统已帮助180个航班重新规划了航线。该机构声称，此举平均为每个航班节省了10分钟的飞行时间，并节省了近80加仑的燃油，显然希望通过此次扩大部署，能在全国范围内复制这些成果。

“After a successful initial rollout to the National Capital Region in September, the FAA will now use SMART in the airspace of all 22 centers across the country to reduce delays, reduce fuel consumption and improve the travel experience for the flying public,” according to the agency statement posted on November 10. The FAA fact sheet describes SMART as a cloud-based system that combines information about “weather patterns, flight paths, traffic flow and controller staffing metrics” to predict air traffic patterns.

根据该机构11月10日发布的声明：“继9月份在国家首都地区成功进行初步部署后，FAA现在将在全国所有22个中心的空域使用SMART系统，以减少延误、降低燃油消耗并改善公众的飞行体验。”FAA的情况说明书将SMART描述为一个基于云的系统，它结合了关于“天气模式、飞行路径、交通流量和管制员人员配置指标”的信息，以预测空中交通模式。

It is designed to make scheduling recommendations, identify optimal flight routes, and determine the best order of arrivals for aircraft. However, a human team of FAA specialists based in DC is ultimately responsible for validating the SMART system recommendations before passing them along to air traffic control facilities across the country, according to the agency.

该系统旨在提供调度建议、识别最佳飞行路线，并确定飞机的最佳到达顺序。然而，据该机构称，位于华盛顿特区的一个FAA专家团队最终负责验证SMART系统的建议，然后再将其传达给全国各地的空中交通管制设施。

Furthermore, the AI-powered SMART system “cannot be used to replace the critical role air traffic controllers play or take over control of an aircraft,” the FAA clarified in the September 21 announcement about the initial deployment. Local air traffic controllers can decide whether or not to accept the SMART system recommendations.

此外，FAA在9月21日关于初步部署的公告中明确指出，人工智能驱动的SMART系统“不能用于取代航空交通管制员的关键作用，也不能接管飞机的控制权”。当地的航空交通管制员可以自行决定是否接受SMART系统的建议。

The previous FAA announcement included supportive statements from Delta Air Lines CEO Ed Bastian and American Airlines CEO Robert Isom. But the National Air Traffic Controllers Association, which represents more than 15,000 members, also told Flying magazine that it was not consulted or involved in the “design, testing, or implementation of SMART and therefore cannot speak to its effect on the air traffic control workforce.”

FAA之前的公告中包含了达美航空首席执行官埃德·巴斯蒂安（Ed Bastian）和美国航空首席执行官罗伯特·伊索姆（Robert Isom）的支持性声明。但代表超过15,000名成员的全国航空交通管制员协会也告诉《飞行》（Flying）杂志，他们并未就“SMART的设计、测试或实施”进行过咨询或参与，因此无法评价其对航空交通管制人员的影响。

Following the initial deployment in airspace above DC, the FAA expanded the SMART system’s coverage to air route traffic control centers in Atlanta along with Jacksonville and Miami in Florida, according to The Dallas Morning News. The FAA also shared an example with The Dallas Morning News about how SMART recommended a shorter and faster route for a cargo flight arriving at Dallas-Fort Worth International Airport, although the newspaper noted that the agency did not identify the flight involved and has not provided information about how SMART may have assisted passenger flight traffic.

据《达拉斯晨报》报道，继在华盛顿特区上空进行初步部署后，FAA已将SMART系统的覆盖范围扩大到亚特兰大以及佛罗里达州杰克逊维尔和迈阿密的航路交通管制中心。FAA还与《达拉斯晨报》分享了一个案例，说明SMART如何为一架抵达达拉斯-沃斯堡国际机场的货运航班推荐了更短、更快的航线，尽管该报指出，该机构并未说明涉及的具体航班，也没有提供关于SMART如何协助客运航班交通的信息。
