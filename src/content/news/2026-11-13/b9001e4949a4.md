# Jensen Huang Is Out. Will Nvidia’s Rivals Cash In?

Nvidia cofounder Jensen Huang officially resigned after admitting to an affair with an alleged Chinese intelligence asset. He also faces reports of at least two more alleged affairs with Nvidia employees. In this bonus episode, Brian Barrett, Zoë Schiffer, and Leah Feiger break down how Nvidia’s board turned on its founder, what interim CEO Colette Kress means for the company, and how this moment could be a golden opportunity for Nvidia’s competitors.
