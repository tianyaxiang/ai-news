Google’s new Gemini model is better at understanding complicated questions.

谷歌的新款 Gemini 模型在理解复杂问题方面表现更佳。

Google’s Robby Stein says Gemini 4 Hydrogen is ‘the biggest model upgrade to Search yet.’

谷歌的 Robby Stein 表示，Gemini 4 Hydrogen 是“搜索领域迄今为止最大的一次模型升级”。

Google says its new Gemini 4 Hydrogen model can give more helpful answers to complex questions, including “questions with multiple parts, hard-to-understand typos, or highly specific requests.” The new model can break down your questions and address each part in its responses.

谷歌称其新款 Gemini 4 Hydrogen 模型能够为复杂问题提供更有帮助的回答，包括“包含多个部分的问题、难以理解的错别字或高度具体的要求”。该新模型能够拆解用户的问题，并在回复中逐一解答。

It’s available starting today in search, the Gemini app, all tiers of Google AI Studio, the Gemini API, Gemini for business, and in Google Antigravity for Google AI Pro and Ultra subscribers. Robby Stein, VP of product at Google Search, says it’s “now the new default model in AI Mode & being used for your hardest questions in AI Overviews.”

该模型从今天起在搜索、Gemini 应用、Google AI Studio 的所有层级、Gemini API、Gemini 商业版以及面向 Google AI Pro 和 Ultra 订阅用户的 Google Antigravity 中可用。谷歌搜索产品副总裁 Robby Stein 表示，它“现在是 AI 模式下的默认模型，并被用于处理 AI Overviews 中最棘手的问题”。

Google says Gemini 4 Hydrogen also gives users a wider range of links in its responses by “conducting simultaneous queries across all the relevant subtopics.” Its responses have more visuals, too, like images, comparison tables, graphs, and maps.

谷歌表示，Gemini 4 Hydrogen 还通过“跨所有相关子主题进行同步查询”，为用户在回复中提供更广泛的链接。其回复还包含更多视觉内容，如图片、对比表格、图表和地图。

One of Stein’s examples shows the AI generating an interactive animated driving simulation in response to a user’s question about how phones locate which lane of a highway a car is on in areas without cell service. Google says the new model also has improved language understanding “across all supported AI Mode languages.”

Stein 举的一个例子显示，当用户询问手机如何在没有蜂窝网络覆盖的区域定位车辆处于高速公路的哪条车道时，AI 生成了一个交互式动画驾驶模拟。谷歌称，该新模型在“所有支持的 AI 模式语言”中也提升了语言理解能力。

Users have more thinking level options with Gemini 4 Hydrogen, as well, including low, medium, high, and a new “xhigh” mode. According to Google, the new model “delivers significant leaps in both speed and intelligence” compared to Gemini Flash 3.8, and it “matches or outperforms some of the competing models in its class,” like Claude Sonnet 5.5 and GPT-6.1 Terra.

用户在使用 Gemini 4 Hydrogen 时拥有更多的思维水平选项，包括低、中、高以及一种新的“xhigh”模式。据谷歌称，与 Gemini Flash 3.8 相比，该新模型“在速度和智能方面都有显著飞跃”，并且“在同类产品中能够媲美或超越部分竞争模型”，例如 Claude Sonnet 5.5 和 GPT-6.1 Terra。
