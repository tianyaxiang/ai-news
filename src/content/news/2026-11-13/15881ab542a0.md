# Microsoft’s Outlook email hit by new outage

In Brief Posted: 11:03 AM PST · November 12, 2026 Image Credits:David Ryder/Bloomberg / Getty Images Zack Whittaker Microsoft’s Outlook email hit by new outage

简讯发布于：太平洋标准时间 2026 年 11 月 12 日上午 11:03。图片来源：David Ryder/Bloomberg / Getty Images。Zack Whittaker：微软 Outlook 电子邮件服务遭遇新一轮中断。

Microsoft said its Outlook email service is currently experiencing an outage, preventing both business and consumer users from accessing their inboxes. The issue was initially flagged as affecting the U.S. and the Americas, and later expanded to affect “any region.”

微软表示，其 Outlook 电子邮件服务目前正经历中断，导致企业和个人用户均无法访问收件箱。该问题最初被标记为影响美国和美洲地区，随后范围扩大至“任何地区”。

Microsoft hasn’t given a specific cause, but said that it was providing updates in its admin center, which wasn’t reachable at the time of publication. In a post on X for users who can’t access the admin center, Microsoft said some of its infrastructure was experiencing “elevated load,” and that it was “redistributing connection traffic across our broader worldwide infrastructure to help balance the load.”

微软尚未给出具体原因，但表示正在其管理中心提供更新，而该中心在本文发布时无法访问。针对无法访问管理中心的用户，微软在 X 上发帖称，其部分基础设施正经历“负载升高”，公司正在“将连接流量重新分配到更广泛的全球基础设施中，以帮助平衡负载”。

In its latest update, Microsoft attributed this incident to “a large number of servers being unexpectedly unavailable,” for reasons it did not explain. Microsoft said it continues to investigate the outage.

在最新的更新中，微软将此次事件归因于“大量服务器意外不可用”，但未解释具体原因。微软表示将继续调查此次中断事件。

Microsoft says its video conferencing tool Teams, file sharing platform SharePoint, and its AI tool Copilot are also unavailable. The company says its Defender XDR security system is also affected by the outage. Consumers are also unable to access their OneDrive storage, Office (for the web), and several other Microsoft services during the ongoing outage.

微软称，其视频会议工具 Teams、文件共享平台 SharePoint 以及人工智能工具 Copilot 也处于不可用状态。该公司表示，其 Defender XDR 安全系统也受到了此次中断的影响。在中断持续期间，消费者也无法访问其 OneDrive 存储、Office（网页版）以及其他几项微软服务。

TechCrunch will reach out to Microsoft for comment, as soon as our own email service has resumed. The company’s last major incident was in September, when a days-long outage hit Microsoft 365, its online productivity suite that includes Outlook. During that outage, Microsoft blamed a “core authentication configuration” issue that prevented users from accessing the service.

一旦我们自己的电子邮件服务恢复，TechCrunch 将联系微软寻求置评。该公司上一次重大事故发生在 9 月，当时微软 365（包含 Outlook 的在线生产力套件）遭遇了持续数天的中断。在那次中断期间，微软将原因归咎于“核心身份验证配置”问题，该问题导致用户无法访问服务。
