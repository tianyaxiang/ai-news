In its third-quarter earnings call Thursday, the neocloud company Nebius laid out $40 billion in planned spending for 2027, far more than Wall Street had expected. It’s an ambitious plan, but it rests on a string of planned projects that are already starting to show cracks.

在周四的第三季度财报电话会议上，新云（neocloud）公司 Nebius 公布了 2027 年 400 亿美元的计划支出，远超华尔街预期。这是一个雄心勃勃的计划，但它建立在一系列已经开始出现裂痕的规划项目之上。

Nebius plans to fund the buildout by relying on customer pre-payments and loans backed by customer contracts — essentially using the future value of its data centers to finance their construction. But that can be a risky strategy if those projects get bogged down by delays or cancellations.

Nebius 计划通过依赖客户预付款和以客户合同为担保的贷款来资助建设，本质上是利用其数据中心的未来价值来为其建设融资。但如果这些项目因延误或取消而陷入困境，这可能是一种高风险的策略。

“Building at this scale is hard, and delays are part of our industry right now,” CEO Arkady Volozh said during Thursday’s earnings call. “We are not immune.” The earnings announcement comes on the heels of reports that Microsoft is cooling on neocloud deals that aren’t delivering on time.

“以这种规模进行建设非常困难，延误是我们目前行业的一部分，”首席执行官 Arkady Volozh 在周四的财报电话会议上表示。“我们也不能幸免。”在此次财报发布之前，有报道称微软对未能按时交付的新云交易态度转冷。

That’s a problem for companies like Nebius, whose financing strategy is becoming more dependent on repayments. Because if it can’t get to capacity on time, it will keep piling on costs while revenues slip.

这对像 Nebius 这样的公司来说是个问题，因为其融资策略正变得越来越依赖回款。因为如果它不能按时实现产能，它将不断增加成本，同时收入却在下滑。

One of Nebius’ largest customers is Microsoft, which is paying for AI computing capacity at a Vineland, New Jersey, data center that is now months behind schedule. Nebius’ contract with Microsoft is worth up to $19.4 billion, but it’s already letting deadlines slip.

Nebius 最大的客户之一是微软，微软正在为新泽西州瓦恩兰（Vineland）一个数据中心的 AI 计算能力付费，但该项目目前已比原计划落后了数月。Nebius 与微软的合同价值高达 194 亿美元，但目前已经出现了延期。

Only just over half of the Microsoft capacity is now planned for delivery by the end of the year, with the rest coming in Q2 2027. The delay is because DataOne, the third-party operator of the site, “decided to wind down the temporary generation faster than it was expected,” according to Andrey Korolenko, chief product and infrastructure officer, as the site switches over to Bloom Energy fuel cells.

目前计划在今年年底前交付的微软产能仅略超一半，其余部分将在 2027 年第二季度交付。据首席产品和基础设施官 Andrey Korolenko 称，延误的原因是该站点的第三方运营商 DataOne “决定比预期更快地缩减临时发电量”，因为该站点正在转向使用 Bloom Energy 燃料电池。

Still, that transition wasn’t entirely by choice. In August, the investigative site Floodlight identified at least 45 natural gas generators in operation at the DataOne site, even though it had not received any permits for operating gas generators. New Jersey regulators fined the DataOne facility $1.1 million in response, finding that the use of the generators violated federal law.

不过，这种转型并非完全出于自愿。今年 8 月，调查网站 Floodlight 发现 DataOne 站点至少有 45 台天然气发电机在运行，尽管它并未获得任何运营天然气发电机的许可。新泽西州监管机构对此处以 110 万美元罚款，认定这些发电机的运行违反了联邦法律。

DataOne is also facing a noise-complaint lawsuit from nearby residents over a constant humming noise allegedly emitted by the data center. According to Nebius’ summary of its Microsoft contract, Microsoft can terminate affected service after a grace period if dates are missed and no alternative capacity is offered.

DataOne 还面临附近居民因数据中心发出持续嗡嗡声而提起的噪音投诉诉讼。根据 Nebius 对其微软合同的摘要，如果错过交付日期且未提供替代产能，微软可以在宽限期后终止受影响的服务。

The tranches were originally meant to be deployed in 2025 and 2026, so a Q2 2027 delivery date falls well beyond that window. The contract also includes financial penalties for late delivery. Nebius said that Microsoft “has been part of the planning process.” Nebius did not say whether Microsoft has agreed to the new dates, but Microsoft has not publicly canceled anything.

这些分期交付原定于 2025 年和 2026 年进行，因此 2027 年第二季度的交付日期已远远超出了该窗口期。合同还包含针对延迟交付的经济处罚条款。Nebius 表示，微软“一直参与规划过程”。Nebius 没有说明微软是否同意了新的日期，但微软尚未公开取消任何项目。

TechCrunch has reached out to Nebius, Microsoft, and DataOne for comment. On the call, Marc Boroditsky, Nebius’s chief revenue officer, said the company’s deals with Microsoft and Meta both came with significant prepayment, which arrived long before delivery.

TechCrunch 已联系 Nebius、微软和 DataOne 请求置评。在电话会议上，Nebius 首席营收官 Marc Boroditsky 表示，该公司与微软和 Meta 的交易都附带了巨额预付款，这些款项在交付前很久就已经到账。

But Bloomberg reported last week that Microsoft is tightening oversight on projects run by third parties, including Nebius, IREN, and Nscale, and debating whether it’s worth signing new agreements. Microsoft also sent employees to the site in New Jersey, per Bloomberg.

但彭博社上周报道称，微软正在加强对包括 Nebius、IREN 和 Nscale 在内的第三方运营项目的监管，并正在讨论是否有必要签署新的协议。据彭博社报道，微软还向新泽西州的站点派遣了员工。

In its own quarterly earnings report, Microsoft noted that its capex spend was lower than expected due to third-party delays. The company is clearly not above pulling back from planned agreements where its partner doesn’t meet its share of the bargain.

微软在自己的季度财报中指出，由于第三方延误，其资本支出低于预期。显然，如果合作伙伴未能履行其协议份额，该公司并不介意退出既定协议。

Bloomberg also reported that Microsoft won’t use an option to more than double its Nscale agreement for a Texas data center and recently stopped being an anchor customer for Nscale’s planned Essex site after power delays.

彭博社还报道称，微软将不会行使将其德克萨斯州数据中心的 Nscale 协议规模扩大一倍以上的选择权，并且在电力延误后，最近已停止担任 Nscale 计划中的埃塞克斯（Essex）站点的基石客户。

Nebius says the delays it faces are coming from sites it doesn’t control, with the data centers it is building itself coming online on time or ahead of schedule. Those, Nebius says, will supply most of its new capacity from 2027.

Nebius 表示，它面临的延误来自其无法控制的站点，而其自行建设的数据中心则按时或提前上线。Nebius 称，这些自建中心将从 2027 年起提供其大部分新增产能。

New deals signed in Q3 came with prepayments that more than cover the cost of building capacity behind them, says Boroditsky. Nebius ended the quarter with $7.4 billion in cash. Total prepayments plus asset-backed financing of investment-grade contracts are expected to cover up to 85% of the $40 to $45 billion 2027 capex spend.

Boroditsky 表示，第三季度签署的新交易附带的预付款足以覆盖其背后的产能建设成本。Nebius 本季度结束时拥有 74 亿美元现金。预计总预付款加上投资级合同的资产支持融资将覆盖 2027 年 400 亿至 450 亿美元资本支出的 85%。

CFO Maria Del Dado Alonso Sanchez named Microsoft and Meta as contracts that can be financed this way. Boroditsky also said Nebius could “tap prepayments to shore up any additional cash requirements.”

首席财务官 Maria Del Dado Alonso Sanchez 指出，微软和 Meta 的合同可以通过这种方式进行融资。Boroditsky 还表示，Nebius 可以“利用预付款来满足任何额外的现金需求”。

Nebius forecast 2027 revenue of $12 to $13 billion, and said it expects to break even on a pre-tax basis by the fourth quarter. Nebius reported third-quarter revenue of $981 million, up 571% from a year earlier. Roughly a third of that was a one-time payment tied to a new partnership model.

Nebius 预测 2027 年收入为 120 亿至 130 亿美元，并表示预计到第四季度实现税前盈亏平衡。Nebius 公布第三季度收入为 9.81 亿美元，同比增长 571%。其中约三分之一是一笔与新合作模式相关的一次性付款。

Nebius cut its 2026 revenue forecast by $200 million to $3 billion, citing delays to third-party sites like New Jersey. Shares ended the day down 17%.

Nebius 将其 2026 年收入预期下调了 2 亿美元至 30 亿美元，理由是新泽西州等第三方站点出现延误。公司股价当日收盘下跌 17%。
