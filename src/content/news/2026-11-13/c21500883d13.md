So, you think you’re an Elder Scrolls superfan? You say you’ve been there since The Elder Scrolls Arena on MS-DOS and have logged hundreds of hours in The Elder Scrolls Online? You say you’ve bought all of the roughly 5,000 re-releases and remasters of Skyrim that have been made available in the last 15 years? Tell me, then: What do you think of The Elder Scrolls Travels: Shadowkey?

所以，你认为自己是《上古卷轴》的超级粉丝吗？你说你从 MS-DOS 时代的《上古卷轴：竞技场》就开始玩了，并且在《上古卷轴 Online》中投入了数百小时？你说你买下了过去 15 年里发行的所有大约 5000 个版本的《天际》重制版和复刻版？那么告诉我：你觉得《上古卷轴：旅程——暗影之钥》（The Elder Scrolls Travels: Shadowkey）怎么样？

What’s that? You’ve never played Shadowkey? You weren’t there day one back in 2004, Nokia N-Gage in hand, ready to experience the game’s 176 x 208 resolution mobile hack-and-slash RPG combat for yourself? And you call yourself a real Elder Scrolls fan? Well, don’t worry. Now you can fill in this crucial gap in your history without even having to track down functional, decades-old N-Gage hardware. That’s thanks to Shadowkey: Reforged, a free fan-made PC port that pairs updated art with the original game files and scripts for an upscaled, 4K experience that runs natively on PC, no N-Gage or emulator needed.

怎么了？你从来没玩过《暗影之钥》？2004 年游戏首发时，你没能手持诺基亚 N-Gage，亲自体验那 176 x 208 分辨率的移动端砍杀 RPG 战斗吗？那你还敢自称是真正的《上古卷轴》粉丝？不过别担心，现在你可以补上这段历史空白，甚至不需要去寻找那些还能运行的、几十年前的 N-Gage 硬件。这要归功于《暗影之钥：重铸版》（Shadowkey: Reforged），这是一个免费的粉丝自制 PC 移植版，它将更新后的美术资源与原始游戏文件和脚本相结合，实现了在 PC 上原生运行的 4K 高清体验，无需 N-Gage 或模拟器。

Moving from a pre-iPhone mobile game to a modern PC obviously comes with major graphical upgrades, powered by a completely rewritten PC graphics engine. What was once a cramped portrait orientation on the N-Gage has now been expanded for a 16:9 monitor standard that displays at up to 120 fps (though the game’s logic still matches the original game’s 25 fps). In addition to newly retextured character and environmental models, the new port also sports small touches like bloom lighting, rippling water, and an upgraded interface with sharper text rendering than was possible on an ancient mobile phone.

从一款 iPhone 时代之前的手机游戏转向现代 PC，显然带来了重大的图形升级，这得益于完全重写的 PC 图形引擎。曾经在 N-Gage 上局促的纵向显示模式，现在已经扩展为 16:9 的标准显示比例，最高支持 120 帧（尽管游戏逻辑仍与原版的 25 帧保持一致）。除了重新贴图的角色和环境模型外，新移植版还加入了一些细节，如泛光照明、波光粼粼的水面，以及比古老手机上更清晰的文本渲染界面。

Using the original game files (which you can obtain from an easy-to-find legitimate copy of the game, of course) means that Reforged‘s quests, dialogue, and game rules are “worked out from the original program,” as the game’s website puts it. That said, the game’s “Modern” mode adds a few touches like smarter enemy AI, fall damage, searchable containers, and a fast travel system that are missing from the original game (and from Reforged‘s more authentic “Classic” mode). The Modern mode also includes some welcome quality-of-life improvements like controller rumble, a smoother on-screen interface, and “better death poses.”

使用原始游戏文件（当然，你可以从容易找到的正版游戏中获取）意味着《重铸版》的任务、对话和游戏规则都是“从原始程序中提取出来的”，正如游戏网站所言。话虽如此，游戏的“现代”模式增加了一些原版游戏（以及《重铸版》更原汁原味的“经典”模式）中所缺失的功能，例如更聪明的敌人 AI、坠落伤害、可搜索的容器以及快速旅行系统。现代模式还包含了一些受欢迎的便利性改进，如手柄震动、更流畅的屏幕界面以及“更好的死亡姿势”。

Those with a disposition against vibe-coded clones and ports should know that Reforged author Dan Whelan writes that he used “AI to assist with coding and building the engine, and with debugging,” as well as for the creation of “most of the modern, higher resolution artwork.” But those who are OK with this AI assistance should be happy to see the project moved from its first playable version in late October to a full-featured “release day” build for Windows, Mac, and Android that came out Wednesday. Whelan has been releasing daily updates to the project and seems set to continue, if the current “Version 0.2.9” designation is any indication.

那些对 AI 生成的克隆和移植作品持抵触态度的人应该知道，《重铸版》的作者 Dan Whelan 写道，他使用了“AI 来辅助编码、构建引擎和调试”，并用它创作了“大部分现代的高分辨率美术资源”。但对于那些能够接受 AI 辅助的人来说，看到该项目从 10 月下旬的第一个可玩版本，发展到周三发布的适用于 Windows、Mac 和 Android 的全功能“正式版”构建，应该会感到高兴。Whelan 一直在为该项目发布每日更新，如果目前的“0.2.9 版本”命名有任何参考意义的话，他似乎打算继续下去。

The inherent limitations of 2000s-era N-Gage game development (which helped earn the original release some middling review scores) are still pretty apparent in even this Reforged remake. Still, it’s nice that Elder Scrolls completionists can now experience one of the series’ least-played releases. At the very least, it’s something to tinker with as we all continue the long wait for Elder Scrolls VI.

2000 年代 N-Gage 游戏开发的固有局限性（这使得原版游戏获得了一些平庸的评价）即使在这次《重铸版》中依然非常明显。不过，对于《上古卷轴》的完美主义者来说，能够体验该系列中玩过人数最少的作品之一，依然是一件好事。至少，在我们所有人继续漫长等待《上古卷轴 6》的过程中，这算是一个可以消磨时间的东西。
