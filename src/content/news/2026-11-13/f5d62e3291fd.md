Democrats on Thursday opened an inquiry into how the Trump administration handled Nvidia’s chip sales to China. Earlier on Thursday, the company ousted its CEO Jensen Huang. He also agreed to testify to a different set of Republican-led Congressional committees.

民主党人周四对特朗普政府处理英伟达（Nvidia）对华芯片销售的方式展开了调查。周四早些时候，该公司罢免了其首席执行官黄仁勋（Jensen Huang）。他还同意向另一组由共和党领导的国会委员会作证。

Huang was removed from the company he founded after he confessed to an extramarital affair with a woman accused of being a Chinese spy. (He has since been accused of having more affairs, with Nvidia employees.) The alleged Chinese spy, Alice Huang, was an executive of Nvidia customer Megaspeed, a Singapore-based company that U.S. officials suspect of helping route advanced Nvidia chips into China.

黄仁勋在承认与一名被指控为中国间谍的女性发生婚外情后，被他创立的公司解职。（此后，他又被指控与英伟达员工有更多婚外情。）这名被指控的中国间谍爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang）是英伟达客户Megaspeed的高管，这是一家总部位于新加坡的公司，美国官员怀疑该公司协助将英伟达的高端芯片转运至中国。

The Democratic inquiry comes from Rep. Richard Neal (D-Mass.), the top Democrat on the House Ways and Means Committee, and Sen. Ron Wyden (D-Ore.), the top Democrat on the Senate Finance Committee. As the minority party, they can ask questions but can’t compel answers. Their humdinger of a letter to Commerce Secretary Howard Lutnick asks the department to preserve records about an arrangement they find questionable and, they allege, illegal.

这项民主党的调查由众议院筹款委员会民主党领袖理查德·尼尔（Richard Neal）和参议院财政委员会民主党领袖罗恩·怀登（Ron Wyden）发起。作为少数党，他们可以提出质询，但无法强制要求对方回答。他们致商务部长霍华德·卢特尼克（Howard Lutnick）的一封措辞严厉的信件，要求该部门保留有关他们认为可疑且涉嫌违法的安排的记录。

“The scheme worked like this,” as the letter describes it. President Trump agreed to sales of Nvidia’s H200 chips to China as long as the chips, manufactured by Taiwan, were first shipped to the United States. On arriving, Trump added a tariff “effectively taking a cut of Nvidia’s sales to China through what appears to be an unconstitutional export tax system,” the letter said. Then the Commerce Department, under Lutnick, issued an export license.

信中描述道：“该计划的运作方式如下。”特朗普总统同意向中国销售英伟达的H200芯片，前提是这些由台湾制造的芯片必须先运往美国。信中称，芯片抵达后，特朗普加征了关税，“通过一种看似违宪的出口税制度，实际上从英伟达对华销售中分了一杯羹”。随后，在卢特尼克的领导下，商务部签发了出口许可证。

By the way, the deal between Trump and Nvidia to share revenue was public. In August 2025, Nvidia and AMD both agreed to share 15% of their revenue from chip sales to China with the U.S. government as part of a deal to secure export licenses, the AP reported at the time. But the letter questions why Lutnick continued to allow Megaspeed to buy chips even after he was reportedly informed by U.S. intelligence of their suspicions of Alice Huang and of Jensen Huang’s relationship with her.

顺便提一下，特朗普与英伟达之间分享收入的协议是公开的。据美联社当时报道，2025年8月，英伟达和AMD均同意将其对华芯片销售收入的15%上交给美国政府，作为获得出口许可证协议的一部分。但信中质疑，为何卢特尼克在据称收到美国情报部门关于对爱丽丝·黄的怀疑以及黄仁勋与她关系的通报后，仍继续允许Megaspeed购买芯片。

It questions why the department reportedly green-lit other Asian companies to buy Nvidia chips “with no physical presence at their listed addresses.” So the inquiry is aimed at the administration, not just at Nvidia and Huang. The letter also questions how Nvidia’s donations to Trump’s projects factored in. It notes that the export deal was approved after Trump learned of “Mr. Huang’s compromised position” in sleeping with an alleged spy, and after Nvidia donated $26 million toward Trump’s projects, including the ballroom.

信中还质疑该部门为何据称批准了其他在“注册地址没有实体办公场所”的亚洲公司购买英伟达芯片。因此，这项调查针对的是政府，而不仅仅是英伟达和黄仁勋。信中还质疑英伟达对特朗普项目的捐款在其中起到了什么作用。信中指出，出口协议是在特朗普得知“黄先生因与一名涉嫌间谍的人发生关系而处于受损地位”，且英伟达向特朗普的项目（包括宴会厅）捐赠了2600万美元之后才获得批准的。

It appears to be asking if Trump used his knowledge of the affair to secure donations from Nvidia. Interestingly, in a “60 Minutes” interview with President Trump that aired on Sunday, Trump mentioned how he and his team had threatened an unnamed AI exec with jail, and after that, the exec “totally changed.”

这似乎是在询问特朗普是否利用他掌握的婚外情信息从英伟达获取了捐款。有趣的是，在周日播出的特朗普总统接受《60分钟》采访时，特朗普提到他和他的团队曾威胁一位未具名的AI高管要将其送进监狱，此后，该高管“彻底改变了”。

This Congressional investigation into Nvidia’s China sales and its dealings with Trump is the second one, so far, since news of the alleged spy affair broke. The Republican-led group involves leaders from the House Select Committee on the Chinese Communist Party, the House Committee on Foreign Affairs, and the House Intelligence Committee. That group has demanded that Huang come to them and testify in a closed-door session. They had threatened to subpoena him if he didn’t voluntarily agree. On Thursday, after his resignation, he agreed.

这是自所谓间谍丑闻曝光以来，国会对英伟达对华销售及其与特朗普交易进行的第二次调查。由共和党领导的调查小组包括众议院中国共产党问题特设委员会、众议院外交事务委员会和众议院情报委员会的领导人。该小组已要求黄仁勋前往参加闭门听证会。他们曾威胁称，如果他不自愿同意，将传唤他。周四，在辞职后，他表示同意。
