The anti-marketing aspect of the Gemini network by Ploum on 2026-11-12 And why your niche should stay a niche I like Gemini. Not the Google Chatbot but the network that existed before Google named its product. If you are enough of a geek, you may want to give it a try. Creating a Blog in Gemini:// (brennan.day)

Ploum 于 2026 年 11 月 12 日发表的《Gemini 网络的反营销面》，以及为什么你的小众领域应该保持小众。我喜欢 Gemini。不是那个 Google 聊天机器人，而是那个在 Google 给其产品命名之前就已存在的网络。如果你足够极客，你可能想尝试一下。在 Gemini:// 创建博客 (brennan.day)

Eoin is counting the number of "capsules" on the Gemini network. A capsule is the equivalent of a "website" on the Web or a "hole" on Gopher. It’s a personal space, a bunch of pages. Blogs become "gemlogs". Eoin’s first observation is that the number of active capsules is slowly growing. The network is healthy. But his second observation is really interesting: there’s absolutely no correlation between the number of new capsules in a given month and articles about the Gemini protocol being promoted on the first page of Hacker News. Counting Capsules (spool-five.com) same content but on the Web

Eoin 正在统计 Gemini 网络上的“胶囊”（capsules）数量。胶囊相当于万维网上的“网站”或 Gopher 上的“洞”。它是一个个人空间，包含一系列页面。博客在这里变成了“gemlogs”。Eoin 的第一个观察结果是，活跃胶囊的数量正在缓慢增长。网络很健康。但他的第二个观察结果非常有趣：某个月新增胶囊的数量与 Hacker News 首页上推广的关于 Gemini 协议的文章之间完全没有相关性。统计胶囊 (spool-five.com) 同一内容但在 Web 上

This is a confirmation of what I’ve been trying to explain for years: your metrics and web statistics are completely useless. The number of visitors, the number of clicks are worthless if you are not a marketer trying to extract money from a big brand. They don’t mean anything. By the way: you should really avoid Hacker News these days. The front page is heavily manipulated to promote pro-AI stuff and bury everything that might question wether fascism-capitalism is good. Why is Hacker News like that? (drewdevault.com)

这证实了我多年来一直试图解释的观点：你的指标和网络统计数据完全没用。如果你不是一个试图从大品牌那里榨取金钱的营销人员，那么访问量和点击量毫无价值。它们没有任何意义。顺便说一句：你最近真的应该避开 Hacker News。其首页受到严重操纵，旨在推广亲人工智能的内容，并掩盖任何可能质疑法西斯资本主义是否良好的言论。为什么 Hacker News 会变成这样？(drewdevault.com)

A Mozilla employee is leaving after 15 years and comes to a similar conclusion. Mozilla is awesome because it has always been abnormal. It is mostly used by abnormal people. This means that hiring "normal managers" who try to make it "normal" and chase "normal" metrics is, of course, a recipe for failure. jr conlin's ink stained banana (blog.unitedheroes.net)

一位 Mozilla 员工在工作 15 年后离职，得出了类似的结论。Mozilla 之所以棒，是因为它一直都很“不正常”。它主要被“不正常”的人使用。这意味着雇佣那些试图让它变得“正常”并追求“正常”指标的“正常经理”，当然是通往失败的秘诀。jr conlin 的墨渍香蕉 (blog.unitedheroes.net)

I always failed to understand those marketing people who try to be "like others but a bit cheaper". While all the value is being different enough to nurture your own niche. I completely removed statistics from my blog in 2013. At the time, I had a very strong realisation that the blog posts that mattered most, those I was proudest of, and those that prompted people to mail me even years later all had mediocre statistics. How I Learned to Stop Worrying and Love the Web (ploum.net)

我一直无法理解那些试图“像别人一样但更便宜一点”的营销人员。而所有的价值都在于足够与众不同，从而培育出你自己的小众领域。我在 2013 年彻底从我的博客中删除了统计数据。当时，我强烈地意识到，那些最重要的博客文章，那些我最引以为豪的文章，以及那些即使在多年后仍促使人们给我写信的文章，其统计数据都很平庸。我如何学会停止担忧并爱上网络 (ploum.net)

On the contrary, most of my hit, my "buzz", were quickly forgotten. But, being addicted to statistics, I was unconsciously adapting my writing style to improve those numbers. I was scrolling through social media posts that were highly popular while trying to create some myself. Closing my social media accounts and, instead, procrastinating on Gemini had the opposite effect: making me read other ideas without caring about reactions, about popularity. Jsreed5 made the same observation while he was trying the Nostr protocol. While Nostr’s technicalities may be interesting by themselves, the content on it is similar to the one found of mainstream social networks. And our smolnet Gemini-formatted brains cannot take it anymore! A Geminaut's View of Nostr (jsreed5.org)

相反，我大部分的“热门”和“轰动”很快就被遗忘了。但是，由于沉迷于统计数据，我无意识地调整了写作风格以提高那些数字。我浏览着社交媒体上非常受欢迎的帖子，同时试图自己创作一些。关闭我的社交媒体账户，转而在 Gemini 上消磨时间，产生了相反的效果：让我阅读其他想法时不再关心反应和受欢迎程度。Jsreed5 在尝试 Nostr 协议时也得出了同样的观察结果。虽然 Nostr 的技术细节本身可能很有趣，但其内容与主流社交网络上的内容相似。而我们这些习惯了 Gemini 格式的“小网”（smolnet）大脑已经无法再忍受了！Geminaut 眼中的 Nostr (jsreed5.org)

We really need a simple Gemini hosting provider that would also provide a Web version, to welcome less technical-minded people who still may want to access a quieter, non-commercial, out-of-time virtual space. Lot of people are trying Gemini and find that there’s no content on it, that they can’t have their news and dopamine hit on it. Well, that’s exactly the point. There Is No Content on Gemini (ploum.net)

我们确实需要一个简单的 Gemini 托管服务商，它也能提供 Web 版本，以欢迎那些技术水平较低但仍希望访问一个更安静、非商业、超脱于时间之外的虚拟空间的人。许多人尝试使用 Gemini，却发现上面没有内容，无法在上面获取新闻和多巴胺刺激。嗯，这正是重点所在。Gemini 上没有内容 (ploum.net)

The fact that only a niche minority want to use it is also part of the point. Gemini might no be for you. That’s fine. The modern Web and even the real world (where every device is actively working against me and every human interaction seems to be about AI) is increasingly not for me. So let me have my strange niche space. UPDATE: Those Gemini hosting providers exist: Gemcities Smol.pub

只有少数小众群体想要使用它，这也是重点的一部分。Gemini 可能不适合你。没关系。现代网络，甚至现实世界（每个设备都在积极地与我作对，每一次人类互动似乎都与人工智能有关），正越来越不适合我。所以，让我拥有我那奇怪的小众空间吧。更新：这些 Gemini 托管服务商确实存在：Gemcities Smol.pub
