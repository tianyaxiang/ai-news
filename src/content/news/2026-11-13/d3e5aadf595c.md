Ukraine President Volodymyr Zelenskyy said today that Starshield has again been disabled on fast-moving drones in Ukrainian territory occupied by Russian forces, and that the US has provided “very little” information about the return of the restriction. “We have detected speed restrictions on Starshield on our Ukrainian territory again, in the temporarily occupied parts of Ukraine,” Zelenskyy said in a speech at the 9th European Political Community Summit in Ireland. “The speed limit is back at 161 kilometers per hour—that’s 100 miles per hour.”

乌克兰总统弗拉基米尔·泽连斯基今日表示，在俄罗斯军队占领的乌克兰领土上，Starshield（星盾）再次在高速无人机上被禁用，且美国对于该限制措施的恢复仅提供了“极少”信息。泽连斯基在爱尔兰举行的第九届欧洲政治共同体峰会上发表讲话称：“我们再次在乌克兰领土上，即乌克兰被临时占领的地区，检测到了对Starshield的速度限制。速度限制回到了每小时161公里，也就是每小时100英里。”

Zelenskyy’s allegation is that Starshield—the military variant of SpaceX’s Starlink satellite service—stops working on Ukrainian military drones when they travel above 161 kilometers per hour. SpaceX today denied Zelenskyy’s allegation. “This is false. There has been no change,” SpaceX’s media relations department told Ars today when asked if SpaceX has any response to Zelenskyy’s statement that Starshield is being restricted again.

泽连斯基指控称，作为SpaceX星链卫星服务的军事变体，Starshield在乌克兰军用无人机飞行速度超过每小时161公里时便会停止工作。SpaceX今日否认了泽连斯基的指控。当被问及SpaceX对泽连斯基关于Starshield再次受到限制的声明有何回应时，SpaceX媒体关系部门今日告诉Ars：“这是虚假的。没有任何改变。”

SpaceX has received multiple Pentagon contracts to give Ukraine access to the military-focused Starshield. SpaceX CEO Elon Musk said last month that any decisions on Starshield restrictions are made by the US government. The Pentagon inspector general’s office has since declined a request from US senators to investigate who ordered the restrictions. Ukraine officials reportedly said today that they believe the limit was reinstated to put pressure on Ukraine in the latest round of peace talks with Russia.

SpaceX已获得多份五角大楼合同，旨在为乌克兰提供对专注于军事用途的Starshield的访问权限。SpaceX首席执行官埃隆·马斯克上个月表示，关于Starshield限制的任何决定均由美国政府做出。此后，五角大楼监察长办公室拒绝了美国参议员提出的调查谁下令实施这些限制的请求。据报道，乌克兰官员今日表示，他们认为恢复该限制是为了在最新一轮俄乌和平谈判中向乌克兰施压。

Zelenskyy said in today’s speech that without satellite connectivity on its drones, Ukraine cannot intercept Russian drones that fly in from the occupied parts of Ukraine. It has often been unclear whether US and Ukrainian government officials discussing the restrictions were referring to Starlink, the civilian service that isn’t supposed to be used for offensive military purposes, or Starshield, which relies on the same technology but is intended for military use. But today, Zelenskyy clearly stated that the current restrictions affect Starshield.

泽连斯基在今日的讲话中表示，如果无人机没有卫星连接，乌克兰就无法拦截从乌克兰被占领地区飞来的俄罗斯无人机。此前，美国和乌克兰政府官员在讨论这些限制时，往往不清楚他们指的是不应被用于进攻性军事目的的民用服务Starlink，还是依赖相同技术但旨在用于军事用途的Starshield。但今日，泽连斯基明确指出，当前的限制影响的是Starshield。

“This directly affects our ability to intercept Russian jet-powered Shahed drones,” Zelenskyy said. “These drones come from those [occupied] areas, and because of the speed restrictions, we simply cannot catch up with them in time. Near the front line, we cannot use our aircraft. Only drone interceptors can operate there. And without the speed we need, shooting down these Shaheds becomes much harder. This means more Russian strikes get through, and more of our people are killed, our civilians are killed, by Russia.”

泽连斯基说：“这直接影响了我们拦截俄罗斯喷气式‘见证者’（Shahed）无人机的能力。这些无人机来自那些（被占领的）地区，由于速度限制，我们根本无法及时追上它们。在前线附近，我们无法使用飞机，只能依靠无人机拦截器。如果没有我们需要的速度，击落这些‘见证者’无人机就会变得困难得多。这意味着更多的俄罗斯袭击得以通过，更多我们的人民和公民被俄罗斯杀害。”

Ukraine officials reported the first round of restrictions about a month ago, but the limits were removed by October 24. Zelenskyy said today that Ukraine doesn’t know why the limit is in place again. “We are trying to get the details from the American side, but so far we know very little,” Zelenskyy said. “But some things are very clear. If someone in America is making decisions that basically treat Ukrainian territory as if it is not Ukrainian, then this tells us where things are going. We need to be clear and never recognize Russia’s occupation of Ukrainian territory in any way.”

乌克兰官员在大约一个月前报告了第一轮限制，但这些限制在10月24日之前已被取消。泽连斯基今日表示，乌克兰不知道为什么该限制再次生效。泽连斯基说：“我们正试图从美方获取细节，但到目前为止我们知之甚少。但有些事情非常清楚。如果美国有人做出的决定基本上是将乌克兰领土视为非乌克兰领土，那么这告诉了我们事态的发展方向。我们需要明确立场，绝不以任何方式承认俄罗斯对乌克兰领土的占领。”

A Financial Times article today described Ukrainian officials’ allegations that the Starshield limit was put back in place to influence peace talks: Senior Ukrainian officials told the FT they believed the speed restrictions were imposed in an effort to pressure Kyiv during the latest peace talks with Russia, which just concluded in Abu Dhabi. US special envoy Steve Witkoff and Donald Trump’s son-in-law, Jared Kushner, were in Moscow on Thursday to meet Russian President Vladimir Putin, after which they were set to travel to Ireland to meet Zelenskyy.

《金融时报》今日的一篇文章描述了乌克兰官员的指控，称恢复Starshield限制是为了影响和平谈判：乌克兰高级官员告诉《金融时报》，他们认为实施速度限制是为了在刚刚于阿布扎比结束的最新一轮俄乌和平谈判期间向基辅施压。美国特使史蒂夫·威特科夫和唐纳德·特朗普的女婿贾里德·库什纳周四在莫斯科会见了俄罗斯总统弗拉基米尔·普京，此后他们计划前往爱尔兰会见泽连斯基。

One senior Ukrainian official said it appeared that Washington wanted to strengthen Russia’s position in the talks. “These are not random errors, but systematic speed restrictions,” Zelenskyy said. “For us, this primarily means a significant complication in intercepting Russian jet-powered drones.”

一位乌克兰高级官员表示，华盛顿似乎想要加强俄罗斯在谈判中的地位。泽连斯基说：“这些不是随机错误，而是系统性的速度限制。对我们来说，这主要意味着拦截俄罗斯喷气式无人机的难度显著增加。”

Musk last month accused Ukraine of using the Starlink commercial service for offensive drone strikes, which he said violates the terms of service. A Ukraine official denied Musk’s accusation and said previous limits imposed by SpaceX already prevented Ukraine’s military from using the commercial Starlink service in the way Musk described. As for the Starshield military variant, Musk has said that “Starshield systems are owned and controlled by the US government. Any limits on speed or usage are decided by the US military and ultimately POTUS.”

马斯克上个月指责乌克兰将Starlink商业服务用于进攻性无人机袭击，称这违反了服务条款。一位乌克兰官员否认了马斯克的指控，并表示SpaceX此前实施的限制已经阻止了乌克兰军方以马斯克所描述的方式使用商业Starlink服务。至于Starshield军事变体，马斯克曾表示：“Starshield系统由美国政府拥有和控制。任何关于速度或使用的限制均由美国军方，并最终由美国总统决定。”

Trump denied making the decision to limit Ukraine’s satellite connectivity, and Secretary of State Marco Rubio said the administration had “no foreknowledge” of the slowdown. We contacted the White House today and will update this article if we get a response. European Commission spokesperson Thomas Regnier said that restrictions on Ukraine’s use of Starshield are unacceptable.

特朗普否认做出限制乌克兰卫星连接的决定，国务卿马可·卢比奥表示，政府对此次减速“事先不知情”。我们今日联系了白宫，如果收到回复，我们将更新本文。欧盟委员会发言人托马斯·雷尼尔表示，对乌克兰使用Starshield的限制是不可接受的。

“It is not the first time that Ukraine is facing Starshield disruptions. These reports are deeply concerning and, if confirmed, unacceptable,” Regnier said at a press conference. “SpaceX must have a consistent approach in sharing its services with Ukraine. Starshield services must be restored urgently for Ukraine to keep its soldiers connected, to protect its citizens, and to defend its territory.”

雷尼尔在新闻发布会上说：“这已经不是乌克兰第一次面临Starshield中断了。这些报道令人深感担忧，如果属实，这是不可接受的。SpaceX在与乌克兰共享服务时必须采取一致的方法。必须紧急恢复Starshield服务，以便乌克兰能够保持士兵的通信畅通，保护其公民，并保卫其领土。”
