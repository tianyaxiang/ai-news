Here are the 25 original Xbox games you can now play on PC.

以下是您现在可以在 PC 上游玩的 25 款初代 Xbox 游戏。

Microsoft’s adding achievements to original Xbox games for the first time, too.

微软也首次为初代 Xbox 游戏增加了成就系统。

For the 25th anniversary of Xbox, Microsoft is beginning to make good on its paid “next-gen game preservation” promise. Starting today, you can play 25 different original Xbox games on your Windows PC, as long as you have (or are willing to pay) for digital copies.

为了庆祝 Xbox 诞生 25 周年，微软开始兑现其付费“次世代游戏保存”的承诺。从今天开始，只要您拥有（或愿意购买）数字版副本，就可以在 Windows PC 上游玩 25 款不同的初代 Xbox 游戏。

In July, Microsoft originally launched the idea with just four Xbox games, so 25 is progress, and Microsoft says all these games are now playable with keyboard and mouse.

微软最初在 7 月份推出了这一构想，当时仅包含 4 款 Xbox 游戏，因此 25 款是一个进步。微软表示，所有这些游戏现在都可以使用键盘和鼠标进行游玩。

Plus, Microsoft says it’s adding achievements to original Xbox games for the first time, starting with four games: Star Wars: Knights of the Old Republic and its sequel, Blinx: The Time Sweeper, and Conker: Live and Reloaded. You can unlock achievements playing them on Xbox One and Xbox Series consoles as well, after a system update. More games should get them in future.

此外，微软表示首次为初代 Xbox 游戏增加了成就系统，首批支持的游戏有四款：《星球大战：旧共和国武士》及其续作、《Blinx: The Time Sweeper》以及《Conker: Live and Reloaded》。在系统更新后，您也可以在 Xbox One 和 Xbox Series 主机上游玩这些游戏并解锁成就。未来会有更多游戏加入此功能。

You also won’t be able to use an original Xbox disc to play these games, I’m afraid, as original Xbox (and Xbox 360) discs aren’t part of Microsoft’s disc-to-digital program; they predate the unique serial number that Microsoft embedded into Xbox One discs. In case you’re wondering, Xbox on PC backward compatibility appears to be powered by an emulator.

恐怕您无法使用初代 Xbox 光盘来游玩这些游戏，因为初代 Xbox（以及 Xbox 360）光盘并不在微软的“光盘转数字版”计划之内；它们早于微软在 Xbox One 光盘中嵌入的唯一序列号。如果您感到好奇，PC 上的 Xbox 向后兼容功能似乎是由模拟器驱动的。

Also, most of the new games aren’t currently included with Xbox Game Pass, according to Microsoft; just Blinx, Conker, Crimson Skies, Fusion Frenzy, Morrowind, and Grabbed by the Ghoulies.

此外，据微软称，大多数新游戏目前并未包含在 Xbox Game Pass 中；仅有《Blinx》、《Conker》、《Crimson Skies》、《Fusion Frenzy》、《Morrowind》和《Grabbed by the Ghoulies》在列。
