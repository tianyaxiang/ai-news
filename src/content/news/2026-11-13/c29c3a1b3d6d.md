Nectar, an AI-powered operating system for marketing, is launching a new AI agent designed to take a lot of the manual work out of influencer marketing, from finding the right creators to contacting them, managing partnerships, and tracking results.

Nectar 是一家人工智能营销操作系统，目前推出了一款全新的 AI 代理，旨在将网红营销中繁琐的手动工作自动化，涵盖从寻找合适的创作者到联系他们、管理合作关系以及追踪营销成果等各个环节。

Founded by sisters Misbah and Farah Uraizee, both former Meta employees, Nectar came out of stealth last year and uses AI agents to help brands manage social media, customer conversations, content moderation, creator partnerships, and competitor research.

Nectar 由前 Meta 员工 Misbah 和 Farah Uraizee 姐妹创立，于去年结束隐身状态。该公司利用 AI 代理帮助品牌管理社交媒体、客户对话、内容审核、创作者合作以及竞争对手研究。

Announced Thursday, its new “Creator Agent” can search for creators, check their content and brand partnerships, send personalized messages, coordinate collaborations, and monitor how campaigns perform.

周四宣布推出的“创作者代理”（Creator Agent）能够搜索创作者、核查其内容与品牌合作情况、发送个性化消息、协调合作事宜，并监控营销活动的表现。

Instead of having marketing teams spend weeks scrolling through social media, building spreadsheets, and sending individual DMs, Nectar wants its agent to handle much of that work automatically.

Nectar 希望通过其代理自动处理大部分工作，从而让营销团队无需再花费数周时间在社交媒体上浏览、制作电子表格以及发送个人私信。

Nectar joins other companies introducing agentic AI assistants designed to automate influencer outreach, including Aspire and GRIN. The launch also comes as more brands turn to creators to reach customers.

Nectar 加入了其他推出代理式 AI 助手的公司行列，旨在实现网红联络的自动化，其中包括 Aspire 和 GRIN。此次发布正值越来越多的品牌转向通过创作者来触达客户之际。

U.S. spending on creator advertising is expected to reach $44 billion in 2026, according to the Interactive Advertising Bureau. As companies work with hundreds or even thousands of creators, keeping track of relationships and measuring results can get pretty difficult.

根据互动广告局（Interactive Advertising Bureau）的数据，预计到 2026 年，美国在创作者广告上的支出将达到 440 亿美元。随着企业与数百甚至数千名创作者合作，追踪关系和衡量成果变得相当困难。

Nectar says its Creator Agent searches more than 300 million creator profiles to find potential partners, including smaller creators who may not yet have a large following but are gaining traction.

Nectar 表示，其“创作者代理”会搜索超过 3 亿个创作者资料以寻找潜在合作伙伴，其中包括那些粉丝量尚不多但正处于上升期的中小型创作者。

The system analyzes videos to understand what creators post about, how their content fits a brand, and whether their previous partnerships are appropriate. It also checks brand safety by reviewing content in context rather than simply searching for specific keywords.

该系统通过分析视频来了解创作者发布的内容主题、其内容与品牌的契合度，以及他们之前的合作是否得当。它还通过在语境中审查内容而非仅仅搜索特定关键词，来确保品牌安全。

Nectar then creates ranked lists of recommendations and adds new matches as creators become more popular. Once it finds a potential match, the agent can draft and send messages in a brand’s voice, create custom landing pages to collect creator information, and help manage contracts, product gifting, and shipping details.

随后，Nectar 会创建排名推荐列表，并随着创作者人气的提升不断添加新的匹配对象。一旦找到潜在匹配者，该代理便能以品牌口吻起草并发送消息、创建自定义落地页以收集创作者信息，并协助管理合同、产品赠送及物流详情。

It also remembers previous conversations and interactions, so brands can keep track of relationships without relying on spreadsheets.

它还能记住之前的对话和互动，因此品牌无需依赖电子表格即可追踪合作关系。

“Brands can share voice guidelines in any format, and Nectar picks up the rest from their history,” CEO Misbah Uraizee tells TechCrunch. “On top of that sits a set of proprietary guardrails that keep every draft on tone and in context. Nectar understands what is being said in a video, reads the tone of the conversation, and knows the difference between a quote post on X and a TikTok comment section. A brand should not sound identical in both, and with Nectar it doesn’t.”

“品牌可以以任何格式分享语调指南，Nectar 会从历史记录中获取其余信息，”首席执行官 Misbah Uraizee 告诉 TechCrunch。“在此之上，还有一套专有的护栏机制，确保每一份草稿都符合语调和语境。Nectar 能理解视频内容，解读对话语气，并区分 X 平台上的引用帖和 TikTok 的评论区。品牌在不同平台上的声音不应完全相同，而使用 Nectar 则能做到这一点。”

Another feature looks for personal milestones in creators’ lives, such as an engagement, a new baby, or completing a marathon. The agent can then suggest a personalized message or gift, helping brands build relationships that feel less like one-off advertising deals.

另一项功能是关注创作者生活中的个人里程碑，例如订婚、添丁或完成马拉松比赛。该代理随后可以建议发送个性化消息或礼物，帮助品牌建立起不像一次性广告交易那样的深度关系。

Creator Agent is available today globally and covers Instagram creators and TikTokers. The company plans to make the agent available through its Model Context Protocol (MCP), allowing teams to use it through AI assistants such as Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, and Copilot, as well as their own custom-built agents.

“创作者代理”即日起在全球范围内可用，覆盖 Instagram 和 TikTok 创作者。该公司计划通过其模型上下文协议（MCP）提供该代理，允许团队通过 Claude、ChatGPT、Gemini 和 Copilot 等 AI 助手以及他们自己定制的代理来使用它。

This past May, Nectar raised a $30 million Series A round led by Menlo Ventures and its Anthology Fund, which is a joint initiative launched with Anthropic.

今年 5 月，Nectar 完成了 3000 万美元的 A 轮融资，由 Menlo Ventures 及其与 Anthropic 联合发起的 Anthology Fund 领投。
