The open source community just lost one of its strongest advocates. Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang resigned Thursday after he admitted to an affair with an alleged Chinese spy, raising concerns about U.S. national security and the motives behind the executive’s support for softer chip export controls on China.

开源社区刚刚失去了一位最坚定的倡导者。英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋周四辞职，此前他承认与一名据称是中国间谍的女子有染，这引发了人们对美国国家安全以及这位高管支持放宽对华芯片出口管制背后动机的担忧。

Huang has been a staunch supporter of open source AI, framing the ecosystem as good for safety, innovation, diffusion, and sovereignty. And as frontier AI labs increasingly move to reduce their reliance on Nvidia and build their own chips, supporting open source is also a good way for the company to diversify its customer base by selling chips to nation-states, enterprises, and any organization that wants to own its own models.

黄仁勋一直是开源人工智能的坚定支持者，他认为该生态系统有利于安全、创新、传播和主权。随着前沿人工智能实验室越来越多地减少对英伟达的依赖并开始制造自己的芯片，支持开源也是该公司通过向主权国家、企业以及任何希望拥有自己模型的组织销售芯片来多元化客户群的有效途径。

Still, Huang’s resignation raises questions about whether Nvidia will carry on his legacy by continuing to support the open model community. Jensen no longer being CEO would be very very bad for the open model cause in the US.— Nathan Lambert (@natolambert) November 11, 2026

尽管如此，黄仁勋的辞职引发了人们的质疑：英伟达是否会延续他的遗志，继续支持开源模型社区。内森·兰伯特（Nathan Lambert）在2026年11月11日的推文中表示：“黄仁勋不再担任首席执行官，这对美国的开源模型事业来说将是非常非常糟糕的消息。”

Huang’s departure also comes as Nvidia has yet to finalize a deal to acquire open source platform Hugging Face for $13 billion. Hugging Face CEO Clem Delangue said in a post on X that the company would continue to fight for open source, whatever happens next. Nvidia also agreed to pay $6 billion for a non-exclusive license for open AI coding startup Poolside’s technology.

黄仁勋的离职正值英伟达尚未完成以130亿美元收购开源平台Hugging Face的交易之际。Hugging Face首席执行官克莱姆·德朗格（Clem Delangue）在X上发文称，无论接下来发生什么，公司都将继续为开源而战。英伟达此前还同意支付60亿美元，以获得开源人工智能编码初创公司Poolside技术的非独家许可。

Within Nvidia, Huang pushed for the development of the Nemotron family of open-weight AI models built for long-running agentic AI systems and reasoning workloads — potential competitors to closed frontier models. One company insider told TechCrunch that they don’t expect a change in leadership to have an impact on Nemotron development.

在英伟达内部，黄仁勋推动了Nemotron系列开源权重人工智能模型的开发，这些模型专为长期运行的代理人工智能系统和推理工作负载而构建，是闭源前沿模型的潜在竞争对手。一位公司内部人士告诉TechCrunch，他们预计领导层的变动不会对Nemotron的开发产生影响。

Huang also backed companies that would make American open models more competitive, investing in startups like Reflection AI and Thinking Machines Lab. Huang’s influence extended beyond Nvidia’s investments and product roadmap. He also emerged as one of the most prominent voices pushing back against Washington’s efforts to restrict the open AI ecosystem.

黄仁勋还支持那些能让美国开源模型更具竞争力的公司，投资了Reflection AI和Thinking Machines Lab等初创企业。黄仁勋的影响力不仅限于英伟达的投资和产品路线图。他还成为了反对华盛顿限制开源人工智能生态系统努力的最主要声音之一。

Earlier this year, Huang wrote an open letter signed by 25 companies, including Microsoft, Meta, IBM, and Palantir, urging the U.S. government not to over-restrict open-weight AI models, many of which come from China. The impetus for that letter was Washington’s debate over how the U.S. should respond to allegations that Chinese AI labs are stealing intellectual property from American counterparts like Anthropic and OpenAI, both of which have reportedly appealed directly to Washington on the matter, urging stronger protections.

今年早些时候，黄仁勋写了一封公开信，由包括微软、Meta、IBM和Palantir在内的25家公司签署，敦促美国政府不要过度限制开源权重人工智能模型，其中许多模型来自中国。这封信的起因是华盛顿关于美国应如何应对有关中国人工智能实验室窃取Anthropic和OpenAI等美国同行知识产权的指控的辩论，据报道，这两家公司都已就此事直接向华盛顿提出申诉，敦促加强保护。

That combination of Huang’s personal visibility and Nvidia’s market power made him the strongest counterweight to arguments from closed-model providers like Anthropic and OpenAI, which have advocated for greater restrictions on advanced AI models due to safety reasons. Without Huang, the concern among open source community members is that whoever takes over will be more cautious.

黄仁勋的个人知名度与英伟达的市场力量相结合，使他成为对抗Anthropic和OpenAI等闭源模型提供商观点的最强力量，后者出于安全原因一直主张对先进人工智能模型实施更严格的限制。在失去黄仁勋后，开源社区成员担心接任者会更加谨慎。

Nvidia has named CFO Colette Kress as interim CEO. It’s not clear how Kress will approach the Hugging Face deal or support for the open model ecosystem, but founder-driven priorities tend to get a downgrade when leadership changes.

英伟达已任命首席财务官科莱特·克雷斯（Colette Kress）为临时首席执行官。目前尚不清楚克雷斯将如何处理Hugging Face的交易或对开源模型生态系统的支持，但当领导层更迭时，由创始人驱动的优先事项往往会被降级。

Huang’s exit could also strengthen the arguments of those in Washington who are arguing for tougher restrictions on Chinese AI companies, particularly as the scandal surrounding his relationship with Alice Huang, the Megaspeed executive and alleged spy, puts renewed scrutiny on his efforts to loosen chip export controls. Restrictions on open-weight models from China could kneecap competition for American companies, the most powerful of which have kept their models closed.

黄仁勋的离职也可能加强华盛顿那些主张对中国人工智能公司实施更严厉限制的人的论点，特别是围绕他与Megaspeed高管、涉嫌间谍的爱丽丝·黄（Alice Huang）之间关系的丑闻，使他放宽芯片出口管制的努力再次受到审视。对来自中国的开源权重模型进行限制可能会削弱美国公司的竞争力，而其中最强大的公司一直保持着模型的闭源状态。

Still, supporting open source isn’t simply a passion project Huang pursued out of idealism. It’s increasingly central to Nvidia’s business strategy. As some of Nvidia’s biggest customers, including Google and OpenAI, develop their own chips, Nvidia has an incentive to cultivate a much broader ecosystem of AI developers and customers. More players training, fine-tuning, and running models mean more GPUs sold, and less dependence on a handful of frontier labs.

尽管如此，支持开源不仅仅是黄仁勋出于理想主义而追求的热情项目。它正日益成为英伟达商业战略的核心。随着包括谷歌和OpenAI在内的一些英伟达最大客户开发自己的芯片，英伟达有动力去培育一个更广泛的人工智能开发者和客户生态系统。更多的参与者进行训练、微调和运行模型意味着更多的GPU销量，以及对少数几个前沿实验室的依赖减少。

Huang may be gone, but Nvidia still needs an open AI ecosystem. The question now is whether that commitment can survive without the man who made it a priority.

黄仁勋可能已经离开，但英伟达仍然需要一个开放的人工智能生态系统。现在的问题是，在失去这位将其视为优先事项的人之后，这一承诺能否继续存在。
