Drone and missile strikes have inflicted unprecedented damage on data centers in Eastern Europe and the Middle East this year, disrupting cloud services for millions of people and permanently deleting some Amazon Web Services customer data. This trend represents a new concern for Big Tech companies—but also for governments and everyone else who depends on common digital services in modern life.

今年，无人机和导弹袭击对东欧和中东的数据中心造成了前所未有的破坏，导致数百万人的云服务中断，并永久删除了部分亚马逊网络服务（AWS）的客户数据。这一趋势不仅对大型科技公司构成了新的担忧，也对各国政府以及现代生活中依赖通用数字服务的每一个人提出了挑战。

To explore what this all means for modern society’s reliance on cloud resilience, I will be hosting a live conversation titled “Drone attacks and data centers: New Risks for Big Tech” on Tuesday, November 17 at 1 pm ET with Joseph Jarnecki, a senior research fellow at the Royal United Services Institute (RUSI), a UK defense and security think tank. He leads RUSI’s work on how cloud services factor into national security and defense, along with tech sovereignty efforts.

为了探讨这一切对于现代社会依赖云韧性意味着什么，我将于美国东部时间 11 月 17 日星期二下午 1 点主持一场名为“无人机袭击与数据中心：大型科技公司面临的新风险”的直播对话，嘉宾是英国国防与安全智库——皇家联合军种研究所（RUSI）的高级研究员约瑟夫·贾内基（Joseph Jarnecki）。他负责领导 RUSI 关于云服务如何影响国家安全与国防，以及技术主权相关的工作。

Jarnecki has done extensive research on how Ukraine strengthened its cloud resilience with Western partners despite facing both cyberattacks and kinetic attacks at the start of the full-scale Russian invasion, and explored what that means for NATO and European governments’ cloud adoption plans. This year, Jarnecki has written about Iran’s drone and missile strikes on Amazon data centers in the Middle East during the US-Israeli war with Iran, and the broader implications for governments and Big Tech.

贾内基对乌克兰如何在俄罗斯全面入侵初期，在面临网络攻击和物理攻击的情况下，通过与西方合作伙伴加强云韧性进行了深入研究，并探讨了这对北约和欧洲各国政府的云采用计划意味着什么。今年，贾内基撰文分析了在美以与伊朗的战争期间，伊朗对中东亚马逊数据中心的无人机和导弹袭击，以及这对各国政府和大型科技公司带来的更广泛影响。

He has also commented on the impacts of Russia and Ukraine exchanging strikes on each other’s data centers. For more background on the data center attacks that have transpired so far in 2026, you can see my previous Ars coverage of how the Iranian strikes left Amazon with months of repairs, and caused enough damage to result in the permanent loss of some AWS customer data.

他还就俄罗斯和乌克兰互相攻击对方数据中心所产生的影响发表了评论。关于 2026 年迄今为止发生的数据中心袭击事件的更多背景信息，您可以参阅我之前在 Ars 上的报道，了解伊朗的袭击如何让亚马逊陷入长达数月的修复工作，并造成了足以导致部分 AWS 客户数据永久丢失的破坏。

I’ve also covered why Russia was not ready for the data center war it started with Ukraine, with the systematic Russian strike campaign targeting Ukraine’s data centers provoking Ukrainian counterstrikes that damaged Russian government data centers and AI data centers owned by the Russian tech giant Yandex. My conversation with Jarnecki will explore why data centers have become much more prominent military targets, whether defending or hardening data centers against drones and missiles is a realistic option, how cloud resiliency can be improved in wartime conditions, and the choices governments and cloud providers face in deploying data centers during an era of growing geopolitical risks.

我还报道过为什么俄罗斯对其发起的针对乌克兰的数据中心战争准备不足，俄罗斯针对乌克兰数据中心的系统性打击行动引发了乌克兰的反击，导致俄罗斯政府数据中心以及俄罗斯科技巨头 Yandex 旗下的 AI 数据中心受损。我与贾内基的对话将探讨为什么数据中心已成为更加突出的军事目标，针对无人机和导弹对数据中心进行防御或加固是否是一个现实的选择，在战时条件下如何提高云韧性，以及在日益增长的地缘政治风险时代，各国政府和云服务提供商在部署数据中心时面临的选择。
