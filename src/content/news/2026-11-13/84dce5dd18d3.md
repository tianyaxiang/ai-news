# Major Microsoft Outlook Outage Leaves People With Email Jobs Unable to Email

It’s not just you. Microsoft’s 365 business and enterprise services suffered a major outage on Thursday, preventing users of its popular cloud suite from accessing their accounts.

不是只有你遇到这种情况。微软的 365 商业和企业服务周四遭遇了重大故障，导致其热门云套件的用户无法访问账户。

Around 10:40 am ET on Thursday, Microsoft posted on its 365 status page on X that it was “investigating reports of users experiencing issues when accessing or using Microsoft Teams functionality in North America.” By just before 1 pm ET, it said it was aware of “issues accessing Exchange Online mailboxes through multiple connection methods in the Americas.”

美国东部时间周四上午 10:40 左右，微软在其 X 平台的 365 状态页面上发文称，正在“调查有关北美用户在访问或使用 Microsoft Teams 功能时遇到问题的报告”。到下午 1 点前，微软表示已获悉“美洲地区通过多种连接方式访问 Exchange Online 邮箱时出现的问题”。

Microsoft later said that its network was also “experiencing elevated load across multiple regions, which is likely contributing to the connectivity errors,” but that the issue was related to servers going offline, not due to increased traffic.

微软随后表示，其网络在“多个地区也出现了负载升高的情况，这很可能是导致连接错误的原因”，但该问题与服务器离线有关，并非由流量增加引起。

Around an hour later, Microsoft said it had “begun redistributing connection traffic across our broader worldwide infrastructure to help balance the load” and was “aggressively pursuing all pathways to reduce impact.”

大约一小时后，微软表示已“开始在更广泛的全球基础设施中重新分配连接流量，以帮助平衡负载”，并正在“积极寻求一切途径来减少影响”。

“We've paused all deployments. We're moving additional traffic off the affected infrastructure, and we're restarting affected servers to return them to service,” Microsoft wrote.

微软写道：“我们已暂停所有部署。我们正在将额外流量从受影响的基础设施中转移出去，并重启受影响的服务器以恢复其服务。”

In an update on its status page, Microsoft said impacted services may include Exchange Online, Microsoft Teams, SharePoint Online, Microsoft Copilot 365, and Microsoft Defender XDR.

在其状态页面的更新中，微软表示受影响的服务可能包括 Exchange Online、Microsoft Teams、SharePoint Online、Microsoft Copilot 365 和 Microsoft Defender XDR。

“We are aware and investigating reports of users experiencing issues with Microsoft 365 services, primarily issues with Exchange Online,” a Microsoft spokesperson tells WIRED in a statement. “We appreciate our customer's patience while we work to restore services as soon as possible.”

一位微软发言人在声明中告诉《连线》杂志：“我们已知悉并正在调查有关用户在使用 Microsoft 365 服务时遇到问题的报告，主要是 Exchange Online 的问题。在我们要尽快恢复服务的同时，感谢客户的耐心等待。”

Outage reports on the service uptime tracking website Downdetector began falling around 1:15 pm ET after a large spike in users reporting issues with Outlook and other Microsoft 365 products around noon.

在中午时分大量用户报告 Outlook 和其他 Microsoft 365 产品出现问题后，服务正常运行时间跟踪网站 Downdetector 上的故障报告于美国东部时间下午 1:15 左右开始下降。

It is currently unclear when the outage may be over or what its cause may be. In a status update on X posted just before 3 pm ET, Microsoft said “a problem” was “causing servers to fail automated health checks and become unavailable” but that it was attempting to reboot its systems to hopefully bring them back online.

目前尚不清楚故障何时结束或原因是什么。在美东时间下午 3 点前发布的一条 X 状态更新中，微软表示“一个问题”导致“服务器无法通过自动健康检查并变得不可用”，但公司正在尝试重启系统，希望能使其恢复在线。

Outages in cloud services occur frequently, and their causes can vary. Configuration errors, resolution issues with the Domain Name System, and cyberattacks can all cause connection errors. There is currently no indication that a cyberattack caused the current Microsoft outage.

云服务故障频繁发生，原因各不相同。配置错误、域名系统解析问题以及网络攻击都可能导致连接错误。目前没有迹象表明此次微软故障是由网络攻击引起的。

While major Microsoft outages are relatively rare, they are far from unprecedented. In July 2024, a corrupted update from the security firm CrowdStrike caused the “blues screen of death” on Windows machines around the world, while a separate but nearly simultaneous outage knocked out Microsoft Azure services for hours, causing untold millions of dollars in lost revenue due to companies’ inability to function as normal.

虽然微软的重大故障相对罕见，但并非史无前例。2024 年 7 月，安全公司 CrowdStrike 的一个损坏更新导致全球 Windows 机器出现“蓝屏死机”，而另一次几乎同时发生的独立故障导致 Microsoft Azure 服务中断数小时，由于企业无法正常运作，造成了数百万美元的收入损失。
