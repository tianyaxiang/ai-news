# Microsoft Outlook is still down

Microsoft 365 issues have been cutting people off from their email since early Thursday afternoon, and they still aren’t fixed.

自周四下午早些时候以来，Microsoft 365 的问题一直导致用户无法访问其电子邮件，且目前尚未得到解决。

Since just before 1PM ET on Thursday, reports have come in from around the world that people can’t access their Outlook email, either through the web or apps. Microsoft quickly acknowledged the issue from its Microsoft 365 Status account, saying that “We’ve determined a portion of infrastructure is experiencing elevated load across multiple regions, which is likely contributing to the connectivity errors.”

自美国东部时间周四下午 1 点前不久开始，全球各地陆续有报告称用户无法通过网页或应用程序访问其 Outlook 邮箱。微软通过其 Microsoft 365 Status 账号迅速确认了该问题，并表示：“我们已确定部分基础设施在多个地区面临负载过高的问题，这很可能是导致连接错误的原因。”

There were also some reports of problems with Teams, but not to the same extent, while Microsoft’s official updates said customers may experience issues across its suite of 365 business services. Microsoft comms director Ben Hope said in a statement that “We are aware and investigating reports of users experiencing issues with Microsoft 365 services, primarily issues with Exchange Online. We appreciate our customers’ patience while we work to restore services as soon as possible.”

此外，也有一些关于 Teams 出现问题的报告，但程度不及 Outlook。微软的官方更新则指出，客户可能会在其 365 商业服务套件中遇到问题。微软公关总监 Ben Hope 在一份声明中表示：“我们已知悉并正在调查有关用户在使用 Microsoft 365 服务时遇到问题的报告，主要是 Exchange Online 相关问题。在我们将服务尽快恢复的过程中，感谢客户的耐心等待。”

Several hours later, Microsoft has gone through several variations of update notes regarding the issues and its attempts to fix them, saying it’s had problems rebooting servers and being able to monitor their health. As of about 6PM ET, Microsoft has a note saying it’s managed to get some of the affected servers running:

几小时后，微软针对这些问题及其修复尝试发布了多个版本的更新说明，称在重启服务器和监控其健康状况方面遇到了困难。截至美国东部时间下午 6 点左右，微软发布说明称已成功恢复部分受影响服务器的运行：

We’ve begun returning a portion of the affected servers to service, and they’re remaining stable so far. We’ve also shifted traffic and connections to alternative pathways wherever possible and have observed limited recovery in some regions.

“我们已开始恢复部分受影响服务器的服务，目前它们保持稳定。我们还尽可能将流量和连接转移到替代路径，并观察到部分地区出现了有限的恢复。”

We’ve streamlined our recovery process to expedite returning the remaining affected servers to service. To further support recovery, we’re implementing protective traffic management measures in a specific location to reduce load on the affected infrastructure, and we’ll expand these measures to additional locations if we observe positive results. As capacity is restored, users may experience a gradual recovery as their clients reconnect to the service.

“我们简化了恢复流程，以加快剩余受影响服务器的服务恢复速度。为进一步支持恢复工作，我们正在特定地点实施保护性流量管理措施，以减轻受影响基础设施的负载；如果观察到积极效果，我们将把这些措施扩展到更多地点。随着容量的恢复，用户可能会随着客户端重新连接到服务而体验到逐渐恢复的过程。”

There’s still no ETA for full restoration, but we will continue to monitor this ongoing outage.

目前仍没有全面恢复的具体时间表，但我们将继续关注此次持续的中断事件。
