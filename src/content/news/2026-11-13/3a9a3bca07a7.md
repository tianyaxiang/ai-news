# Silicon Valley Is Still Defending Jensen Huang

News of Jensen Huang’s affair with an alleged asset of China’s Ministry of State Security was first met with a muted response in Silicon Valley—including inside Nvidia itself. Huang’s resignation, after news of two other alleged romantic relationships with Nvidia staffers, has left both the company and the broader AI industry stunned.

有关黄仁勋与一名据称是中国国家安全部特工的人员发生婚外情的报道，最初在硅谷（包括英伟达内部）引发的反应相当平淡。在另外两段与英伟达员工的所谓恋情曝光后，黄仁勋的辞职让英伟达公司乃至整个AI行业都感到震惊。

As revelations about Huang’s initial affair came out earlier this week, rank-and-file Nvidia employees at first pursued business as usual, according to two current and three former employees at the chipmaker, speaking anonymously to protect relationships at the company. They trusted the board to investigate and act accordingly, but few workers thought Huang would actually leave.

据两名现任和三名前英伟达员工匿名透露，本周早些时候，随着黄仁勋首段婚外情的细节披露，英伟达的普通员工起初仍照常工作。他们信任董事会会进行调查并采取相应行动，但很少有员工认为黄仁勋真的会离职。

Nvidia’s roughly 60,000 employees are loyal to the point that its global turnover rate is reportedly among the lowest in tech. Workers say they have heard it called having “green in your blood” or being “green-pilled” or on “team green,” referring to the company’s signature color. Huang was the company’s soul and the founder who stuck with it through challenges for 33 years. Employees appreciated that he kept layoffs rare and red tape sparing.

英伟达约6万名员工的忠诚度极高，据报道其全球员工流失率在科技行业中处于最低水平。员工们表示，他们常听到这种忠诚被称为“血液里流着绿色”、“被绿色药丸洗脑”或属于“绿色团队”，这指的是公司的标志性颜色。黄仁勋是公司的灵魂，也是33年来历经挑战始终坚守的创始人。员工们很感激他极少裁员，且官僚作风较少。

“In just three days we went from superhero cape to ‘Oops, I forgot my pants,’ and nobody’s quite sure how to swallow that,” one employee said. One former employee insisted in Huang’s defense that something “more sinister” must have been in play, and that affairs alone shouldn’t have led to his departure.

“仅仅三天时间，我们就从超级英雄变成了‘哎呀，我忘了穿裤子’，没人确定该如何接受这一切，”一名员工说道。一位前员工坚持为黄仁勋辩护，称其中一定有“更阴险”的因素在作祟，仅凭婚外情不应导致他离职。

Huang’s peers in Silicon Valley echoed that sentiment, remaining vocal in their support of the embattled CEO even after his ouster. “One mistake does not erase a lifetime of leadership and contribution in building NVDA,” venture capitalist Antonio Gracias wrote on X earlier this week, using the company’s ticker symbol.

硅谷的同行们也表达了同样的观点，即使在黄仁勋被免职后，他们仍公开支持这位陷入困境的首席执行官。风险投资家安东尼奥·格拉西亚斯（Antonio Gracias）本周早些时候在X上写道：“一个错误并不能抹杀他一生在领导和建设英伟达（NVDA）方面的贡献。”

The New York Times reported on Wednesday that Huang has also had an extramarital affair with a vice president at Nvidia, and Reuters revealed on Thursday a second “intimate relationship” with an employee. Before those alleged affairs became public, multiple industry executives told WIRED that Huang is “irreplaceable” and that his biggest mistake, aside from his relationship with Alice Huang, was admitting the affair in writing to the whole company.

《纽约时报》周三报道称，黄仁勋还与英伟达的一名副总裁有过婚外情，路透社周四则披露了他与另一名员工的“亲密关系”。在这些所谓的婚外情曝光之前，多位行业高管曾告诉《连线》（WIRED）杂志，黄仁勋是“不可替代的”，他除了与Alice Huang的关系外，最大的错误就是以书面形式向全公司承认了这段恋情。

That appears to still be the prevailing sentiment. “A founder who has spent 30 years building such a unique company so capably has earned the presumption that he should keep leading it,” venture capitalist Sarah Guo wrote on X after Huang resigned, and a few hours before the Reuters report.

这似乎仍然是主流观点。在黄仁勋辞职后、路透社报道发布前几个小时，风险投资家莎拉·郭（Sarah Guo）在X上写道：“一位花了30年时间如此出色地建立起这样一家独特公司的创始人，理应被推定为应该继续领导它。”

Authorities and Nvidia’s board have yet to formally accuse Huang of wrongdoing in his interactions with the businesswoman and suspected intelligence asset Alice Huang, and China has denied that she’s a spy. President Donald Trump said on Tuesday that the FBI “is looking into it.” Nvidia’s board has stated that it doesn’t believe Huang compromised any secrets, but it has hired the law firm Wachtell Lipton to investigate.

当局和英伟达董事会尚未正式指控黄仁勋在与女商人及涉嫌情报人员Alice Huang的交往中存在不当行为，中国方面也否认她是间谍。唐纳德·特朗普总统周二表示，联邦调查局（FBI）“正在调查此事”。英伟达董事会表示，不认为黄仁勋泄露了任何机密，但已聘请Wachtell Lipton律师事务所进行调查。

Add to all that, some corners of Nvidia had already been whispering about the relationship with Alice Huang; one venture capitalist who declined to be named recalled a recent dinner where an Nvidia executive claimed there had been rumors about Jensen’s romantic relationship with a young Asian woman.

此外，英伟达内部的一些角落早已在私下议论他与Alice Huang的关系；一位不愿透露姓名的风险投资家回忆起最近的一次晚宴，当时一位英伟达高管声称，关于黄仁勋与一名年轻亚裔女性恋情的传闻早已存在。

On Monday, the venture capitalist had estimated a “less than 10 percent” chance Huang would lose his job at the top of the world’s most valuable company. “The wind’s going to blow over on this,” they said. “People are going to hate him for three months, and forget this ever happened.”

周一，这位风险投资家曾估计黄仁勋失去这家全球市值最高公司掌门人职位的可能性“不到10%”。他们当时说：“这阵风很快就会过去。人们会恨他三个月，然后就会忘记这件事。”

Huang’s resignation left them in shock.

黄仁勋的辞职让他们感到震惊。

The big anxiety in Silicon Valley remains the possibility that instability at Nvidia will burst an AI bubble, but analysts aren’t so sure.

硅谷目前最大的焦虑仍然是英伟达的不稳定性可能会刺破AI泡沫，但分析师们对此并不确定。

The chief executive of one AI company, who spoke on condition of anonymity so as not to affect business relationships, says Nvidia’s recent bankrolling of AI startups and even buying companies such as model-hosting provider Hugging Face has helped prop up the need for chips. A “new professional CEO won’t do any of that,” meaning “systemic risk for the AI trade and AI bubble increases substantially,” the entrepreneur says.

一家AI公司的首席执行官在匿名条件下表示，英伟达近期对AI初创公司的注资，甚至收购像模型托管服务商Hugging Face这样的公司，都有助于支撑对芯片的需求。这位企业家认为，一位“新的职业经理人CEO不会做这些事”，这意味着“AI行业和AI泡沫的系统性风险将大幅增加”。

Between bubble talk and Huang rumors, Nvidia shares in recent days have sunk to their lowest point in more than a year. Nvidia reports its latest quarterly earnings next week.

在泡沫论和黄仁勋传闻的双重影响下，英伟达股价近日跌至一年多来的最低点。英伟达将于下周发布最新季度财报。

Inside Nvidia, meetings, projects, and the company’s aggressive culture are rapidly pressing ahead with what’s left of Huang’s vision.

在英伟达内部，会议、项目以及公司激进的企业文化正在迅速推进黄仁勋留下的愿景。
