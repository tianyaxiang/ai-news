ChatGPT is about to get a lot more visual, thanks to a new feature that OpenAI calls Intelligent UI. The new interface, which is being rolled out Wednesday with a new GPT-6 model, will integrate a significant amount of visuals into users’ conversations. OpenAI says many of those visuals will be interactive.

得益于 OpenAI 称之为“智能界面”（Intelligent UI）的新功能，ChatGPT 即将变得更加直观。这一新界面将随全新的 GPT-6 模型于周三推出，它会在用户的对话中整合大量视觉元素。OpenAI 表示，其中许多视觉内容都将具备交互性。

In other words, instead of just flat, cartoonish pictures, the chatbot will frequently include things like tappable buttons, a customized calculator for a specific task, interactive charts, editable graphs, and other elements the user can work with directly. The big goal here, according to OpenAI, is to make “learning complex topics easier.”

换句话说，聊天机器人将不再仅仅提供扁平的卡通图片，而是经常包含可点击的按钮、针对特定任务的定制计算器、交互式图表、可编辑的图形以及其他用户可以直接操作的元素。据 OpenAI 称，此举的主要目标是让“学习复杂主题变得更容易”。

It may also make ChatGPT friendlier and more accessible and help bring in a wider audience, though OpenAI didn’t say so directly. In a call with journalists, OpenAI staff gave a brief demonstration of Intelligent UI’s capabilities, showing how the new chatbot can instantly whip up various diagrams and visuals that help users understand more abstract topics.

尽管 OpenAI 没有直接说明，但这或许也会让 ChatGPT 变得更友好、更易于使用，并有助于吸引更广泛的用户群体。在与记者的通话中，OpenAI 的工作人员简要演示了“智能界面”的功能，展示了新的聊天机器人如何即时生成各种图表和视觉效果，帮助用户理解更抽象的主题。

Aarush Selvan, a product manager at the company, said that while “ChatGPT has predominantly been a text-based interface,” the company really wants to help users “get things done — from everyday things like planning a recipe or a trip or even trying to learn something — the most helpful answers aren’t just text.”

该公司产品经理 Aarush Selvan 表示，虽然“ChatGPT 此前主要是一个基于文本的界面”，但公司确实希望帮助用户“完成任务——从规划食谱或旅行等日常琐事，到尝试学习新知识，最有帮助的答案往往不仅仅是文本。”

Selvan gave the example of a college student wanting to know how an airplane wing functions. He asked ChatGPT to “explore how an airplane wing generates lift” and the chatbot quickly spun up a number of diagrams designed to show how planes stay in the air.

Selvan 举了一个大学生想了解飞机机翼如何工作的例子。当他要求 ChatGPT “探索飞机机翼如何产生升力”时，聊天机器人迅速生成了多个旨在展示飞机如何保持在空中的图表。

Other examples from the company included things like visuals for recipes, a diagram explaining bicycle mechanics, a map for a multi-day hiking excursion, a personal savings calculator, and more. This new feature will be customizable. As with other aspects of ChatGPT’s personality, if users don’t want so many visuals in their responses, they’ll be able to dial them back.

该公司提供的其他示例还包括食谱视觉效果、解释自行车机械原理的图表、多日徒步旅行地图、个人储蓄计算器等。这一新功能将支持自定义。正如 ChatGPT 个性的其他方面一样，如果用户不希望回复中包含过多视觉内容，他们可以调低其频率。

Intelligent UI rolls out globally with GPT-6 for Pro, Plus, Business, and Enterprise users, and arrives Thursday for users of the free and lower-cost Go tiers.

“智能界面”将随 GPT-6 全球同步推出，面向 Pro、Plus、Business 和 Enterprise 用户，并于周四向免费版及低成本的 Go 层级用户开放。
