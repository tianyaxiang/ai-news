# The first Nvidia RTX Spark laptops cost up to $7,000

首批 Nvidia RTX Spark 笔记本电脑售价高达 7,000 美元

The first array of laptops powered by Nvidia’s new RTX Spark chip are designed to compete with high-end MacBook Pros — and they’ve got some high-end prices to match.

首批搭载 Nvidia 新款 RTX Spark 芯片的笔记本电脑旨在与高端 MacBook Pro 竞争，其价格也同样处于高端水平。

The flagship Surface Laptop Ultra and RTX Spark laptops from Dell, Lenovo, MSI, HP, and Asus start as low as $2,599 for configurations with an 18-core CPU, 24GB of unified memory, and 512GB of storage. That’s close in price to a similarly specced 14-inch MacBook Pro with M5 Pro chip, though Apple includes 1TB of storage. But the prices for both RTX Spark and Apple laptops of course go much, much higher.

旗舰款 Surface Laptop Ultra 以及来自戴尔、联想、微星、惠普和华硕的 RTX Spark 笔记本电脑起售价为 2,599 美元，配置包括 18 核 CPU、24GB 统一内存和 512GB 存储空间。这一价格与配置相似的搭载 M5 Pro 芯片的 14 英寸 MacBook Pro 相近，尽管苹果提供了 1TB 的存储空间。当然，RTX Spark 和苹果笔记本电脑的价格上限都要高得多。

Of the pricing we’ve seen so far, a kitted-out Asus ProArt P16 RTX Spark laptop with a 20-core chip, 128GB of unified memory, and 2TB of storage costs up to $6,999.99. We don’t yet know the full breadth of configuration options for Microsoft’s Surface Laptop Ultra, as the preorder page only has an email sign-up box to get updates, but a 48GB model on Best Buy costs $3,999.99.

根据目前已知的定价，一台配备 20 核芯片、128GB 统一内存和 2TB 存储空间的顶配版华硕 ProArt P16 RTX Spark 笔记本电脑售价高达 6,999.99 美元。我们尚不清楚微软 Surface Laptop Ultra 的全部配置选项，因为预购页面目前仅提供电子邮件注册以获取更新，但在百思买（Best Buy）上，一款 48GB 内存的机型售价为 3,999.99 美元。

Wildly expensive laptops aren’t really anything new: you can configure a 16-inch MacBook Pro with M5 Max chip to nearly $10,000 if you max out all options. But the first volley of RTX Spark options are obviously aimed at the high-end side of creators and local AI users. And RAMageddon certainly isn’t helping make this any easier on peoples’ wallets.

昂贵的笔记本电脑并非新鲜事：如果你将 16 英寸 MacBook Pro（搭载 M5 Max 芯片）的所有选项拉满，价格可以接近 10,000 美元。但首批推出的 RTX Spark 产品显然瞄准了高端创作者和本地 AI 用户群体。而“内存灾难”（RAMageddon）显然也让消费者的钱包更加吃紧。

Will they be worth it? We’ll find out soon. The Surface Laptop Ultra and other RTX Spark laptops are launching this month — as early as October 16th.

它们值得购买吗？我们很快就会知道。Surface Laptop Ultra 和其他 RTX Spark 笔记本电脑将于本月发布，最早发布日期为 10 月 16 日。
