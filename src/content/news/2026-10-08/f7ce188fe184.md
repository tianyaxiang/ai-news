Title: Everything announced at Microsoft’s Surface Laptop Ultra event

TechUpdated Oct 7, 2026, 8:11 PM UTC Everything announced at Microsoft’s Surface Laptop Ultra event by Verge Staff Microsoft just wrapped up a big Windows and Surface-focused keynote in San Francisco.

科技更新于 2026 年 10 月 7 日，世界标准时间晚上 8:11。微软在旧金山刚刚结束了一场以 Windows 和 Surface 为重点的大型发布会。

The biggest announcement was arguably the release details about the Surface Laptop Ultra, its new laptop that’s powered by Nvidia’s RTX Spark Arm-based chip. The machine will start at $2,599 for a configuration with an 8-core CPU, 24GB of RAM, and 512GB of storage, and it will be released on October 16th.

最重大的公告可以说是关于 Surface Laptop Ultra 的发布细节，这是其搭载英伟达 RTX Spark 基于 Arm 架构芯片的新款笔记本电脑。该机型起售价为 2,599 美元，配置包括 8 核 CPU、24GB 内存和 512GB 存储空间，并将于 10 月 16 日发布。

The laptop also has built-in magnetic USB-C charging instead of the proprietary Surface Connect magnetic charging port. The Verge’s Tom Warren published a deep dive all about the new laptop. Microsoft also announced that the Surface RTX Spark Dev Box, a mini PC for developers, is available for preorder for $5,999 ahead of shipping in November.

这款笔记本电脑还内置了磁吸式 USB-C 充电功能，取代了专有的 Surface Connect 磁吸充电接口。《The Verge》的 Tom Warren 发布了一篇关于这款新笔记本电脑的深度报道。微软还宣布，面向开发者的迷你电脑 Surface RTX Spark Dev Box 已开启预购，售价 5,999 美元，将于 11 月发货。

In addition, Microsoft shared details about some updates coming to Windows, including “Hybrid Intelligence” features that lets Copilot use files on your Windows PC to take actions and a new search experience that lets you do quick actions from the search bar.

此外，微软还分享了 Windows 即将迎来的一些更新细节，包括“混合智能”（Hybrid Intelligence）功能，该功能允许 Copilot 使用你 Windows PC 上的文件来执行操作，以及一种新的搜索体验，让你能直接从搜索栏执行快速操作。

Before Microsoft releases a laptop to the public, it needs to put the device through its paces. That all happens inside a windowless, warehouse-like lab on the company’s Redmond, Washington, campus, where the team developing Surface devices programs robots to press their buttons thousands of times, pelts devices with radio waves from giant antennas, and builds contraptions that drop objects from increasingly daring heights to see whether the products can hold up.

在微软向公众发布笔记本电脑之前，它需要对设备进行严格测试。这一切都发生在其位于华盛顿州雷德蒙德园区内一个无窗、类似仓库的实验室里。在那里，Surface 设备开发团队通过编程让机器人按下按钮数千次，用巨型天线发射的无线电波轰击设备，并建造各种装置从越来越高的高度投掷物体，以观察产品是否能经受住考验。

The first array of laptops powered by Nvidia’s new RTX Spark chip are designed to compete with high-end MacBook Pros — and they’ve got some high-end prices to match.

首批搭载英伟达新款 RTX Spark 芯片的笔记本电脑旨在与高端 MacBook Pro 竞争，它们也拥有与之相匹配的高端价格。

At today’s Windows and Surface event, Microsoft showed off an upgrade to its Copilot AI system that will give it access to local files on your PC and the ability to take actions across the OS. It’s part of an idea Microsoft is calling “Hybrid Intelligence,” where apps and tools rely on a mix of local and cloud AI models to accomplish tasks efficiently.

在今天的 Windows 和 Surface 发布会上，微软展示了其 Copilot AI 系统的升级版，该系统将能够访问你 PC 上的本地文件，并具备跨操作系统执行操作的能力。这是微软所谓的“混合智能”理念的一部分，即应用程序和工具依靠本地和云端 AI 模型的结合来高效完成任务。

Months after revealing its Surface Laptop Ultra, Microsoft has announced that the Nvidia RTX Spark-equipped device will launch on October 16th. Pricing starts at $2,599 for the base configuration, featuring an 18-core CPU, 24GB of RAM, and 512GB of storage.

在发布 Surface Laptop Ultra 数月后，微软宣布这款搭载英伟达 RTX Spark 的设备将于 10 月 16 日上市。基础配置起售价为 2,599 美元，配备 18 核 CPU、24GB 内存和 512GB 存储空间。

Microsoft’s Nvidia-powered Surface RTX Spark Dev Box is available for preorder now and is slated to ship in November for just about $6,000. It’s pricier than the DGX Spark mini PC Nvidia launched last year, but PC prices have been climbing due to shortages of RAM and other components.

微软搭载英伟达芯片的 Surface RTX Spark Dev Box 现已开启预购，预计将于 11 月发货，售价约为 6,000 美元。它比英伟达去年推出的 DGX Spark 迷你电脑更贵，但由于内存和其他组件的短缺，PC 价格一直在上涨。

Microsoft has created a magnetic charging solution for its Surface Laptop Ultra that uses USB-C. After scrapping its proprietary Surface Connect magnetic charging on its smaller.

微软为其 Surface Laptop Ultra 创建了一种使用 USB-C 的磁吸充电解决方案。在取消了其较小机型上专有的 Surface Connect 磁吸充电功能之后。
