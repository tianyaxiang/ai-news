Microsoft is giving Copilot more control over Windows and your files.

微软正在赋予 Copilot 对 Windows 和您的文件更多的控制权。

With features powered by ‘Hybrid Intelligence,’ Copilot can use files on your Windows PC to take actions.

借助由“混合智能”驱动的功能，Copilot 可以使用您 Windows 电脑上的文件来执行操作。

At today’s Windows and Surface event, Microsoft showed off an upgrade to its Copilot AI system that will give it access to local files on your PC and the ability to take actions across the OS. It’s part of an idea Microsoft is calling “Hybrid Intelligence,” where apps and tools rely on a mix of local and cloud AI models to accomplish tasks efficiently.

在今天的 Windows 和 Surface 发布会上，微软展示了其 Copilot AI 系统的升级，该升级将使其能够访问您电脑上的本地文件，并具备跨操作系统执行操作的能力。这是微软所谓的“混合智能”理念的一部分，即应用程序和工具通过结合本地和云端 AI 模型来高效完成任务。

To show off how Hybrid Intelligence works, Jacob Andreou, Microsoft’s EVP of Copilot, walked through a video demo onstage asking Microsoft’s Autopilot tool to help with filing taxes. Andreou told the AI agent that he got an email from his accountant and asked Autopilot to get her what she needs. Autopilot then used Hybrid Intelligence to search across different folders to find the documents, rename the files, zip them into a folder, and draft an email to send to the accountant with the zip file attached.

为了展示“混合智能”的工作原理，微软 Copilot 执行副总裁 Jacob Andreou 在台上演示了一段视频，要求微软的 Autopilot 工具协助报税。Andreou 告诉 AI 代理他收到了会计师的电子邮件，并要求 Autopilot 为她准备所需的文件。随后，Autopilot 利用“混合智能”在不同文件夹中搜索并找到这些文档，对文件进行重命名，将其压缩到一个文件夹中，并起草了一封附带压缩文件的电子邮件发送给会计师。

Features powered by Hybrid Intelligence will be coming to Copilot over the “next couple months,” Andreou said.

Andreou 表示，由“混合智能”驱动的功能将在“未来几个月内”登陆 Copilot。

Microsoft also showed off a new Windows search experience that lets you take quick actions from the search bar, like turning on dark mode, turning up your mic volume with an in-line slider, or even sending a text. The new experience will be available starting this fall on Windows 11 PCs, according to Windows and Surface boss Pavan Davuluri.

微软还展示了一种全新的 Windows 搜索体验，让您可以直接从搜索栏执行快速操作，例如开启深色模式、通过内置滑块调高麦克风音量，甚至发送短信。据 Windows 和 Surface 部门负责人 Pavan Davuluri 称，这项新体验将于今年秋季在 Windows 11 电脑上推出。
