Title: ChatGPT’s ‘Intelligent UI’ update fills its responses with pictures, charts, and buttons

ChatGPT 的“智能 UI”更新在回复中填充了图片、图表和按钮

With GPT-6, the chatbot’s responses can include interactive responses directly inside your conversation.

借助 GPT-6，聊天机器人的回复可以直接在对话中包含交互式内容。

OpenAI is launching a new Intelligent UI feature in ChatGPT that allows the chatbot to answer your questions with interactive visuals. The update, which is rolling out to all users alongside GPT-6, gives ChatGPT the ability to combine a text response with diagrams, charts, forms, tappable buttons, and more.

OpenAI 正在 ChatGPT 中推出一项新的“智能 UI”功能，允许聊天机器人通过交互式视觉效果来回答用户的问题。此次更新将与 GPT-6 一起向所有用户推送，使 ChatGPT 能够将文本回复与图表、表格、表单、可点击按钮等内容相结合。

In a blog post explaining the change, OpenAI says it trained GPT-6 when to generate interactive visuals over text, as well as how to format them in its response. An example shared by OpenAI shows how ChatGPT might show a diagram of a seven-speed bicycle if you ask about its design, complete with interactive buttons that highlight different parts.

在一篇解释此次变更的博文中，OpenAI 表示已训练 GPT-6 何时生成交互式视觉效果而非纯文本，以及如何在回复中对其进行格式化。OpenAI 分享的一个示例显示，如果你询问七速自行车的结构，ChatGPT 可能会展示一张自行车图表，并配有可突出显示不同部件的交互式按钮。

Another shows how ChatGPT can teach you to play Mahjong by generating visuals of each tile and placing them into categories that you can scroll through. You can also ask ChatGPT to generate tools for different tasks, such as a retirement savings calculator, retro game, or bill splitter. It will then generate the tool in-line with its response.

另一个示例展示了 ChatGPT 如何通过生成每张麻将牌的视觉效果并将其分类，让你能够滚动查看，从而教你打麻将。你还可以要求 ChatGPT 为不同的任务生成工具，例如退休储蓄计算器、复古游戏或账单拆分器。随后，它会在回复中直接生成该工具。

OpenAI announced its first GPT-6 models last month, but now it’s bringing the Sol and Luna versions to ChatGPT. In addition to enabling Intelligent UI, OpenAI says its GPT-6 allows ChatGPT to perform better web searches and provide quicker, partial answers to users while it gathers information. The company notes that GPT-6 “showed stronger resistance to attempts to bypass its safety training” as well.

OpenAI 上个月发布了首批 GPT-6 模型，现在正将 Sol 和 Luna 版本引入 ChatGPT。OpenAI 表示，除了启用“智能 UI”外，GPT-6 还使 ChatGPT 能够进行更好的网络搜索，并在收集信息的同时为用户提供更快速的部分答案。该公司还指出，GPT-6 在“抵御绕过其安全训练的尝试方面表现出更强的能力”。

GPT-6 with Intelligent UI is rolling out globally to ChatGPT Plus, Pro, Business, and Enterprise users starting today, but will expand to Go and free tiers on Thursday. While higher-tier subscribers will gain access to the mid-range GPT-6 Sol model, Go and free users will get to use the more efficient GPT-6 Luna.

搭载“智能 UI”的 GPT-6 从今天起向全球 ChatGPT Plus、Pro、Business 和 Enterprise 用户推送，并将于周四扩展至 Go 和免费层级。高级订阅用户将获得中端 GPT-6 Sol 模型的使用权限，而 Go 和免费用户将使用效率更高的 GPT-6 Luna 模型。
