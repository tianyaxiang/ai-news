A font recreated from photographs of classic Commodore 64 keycaps, with cleaned-up shapes and consistent proportions.

这款字体根据经典 Commodore 64 键帽的照片重新制作，并对形状进行了修整，使其比例保持一致。

Try it in your browser · Download TTF · OTF · Webfont · Complete package

在浏览器中试用 · 下载 TTF · OTF · Webfont · 完整包

Install the TTF or OTF, then select C64 Keyboard in your application.

安装 TTF 或 OTF 文件，然后在您的应用程序中选择“C64 Keyboard”。

To try it out, open the online preview, or download and extract the complete package and open preview.html. Click a function-key or PETSCII button to insert its symbol into the type tester, then copy the text into your application and select C64 Keyboard.

如需试用，请打开在线预览，或下载并解压完整包后打开 preview.html。点击功能键或 PETSCII 按钮，将符号插入到文字测试器中，然后将文本复制到您的应用程序中并选择“C64 Keyboard”。

If you have an earlier version installed, replace it with version 1.112 and restart applications that still show the old font. The former U+E000 symbol and the U+E020–U+E028 key-name legends are no longer included.

如果您安装了旧版本，请将其替换为 1.112 版本，并重启仍显示旧字体的应用程序。原有的 U+E000 符号以及 U+E020–U+E028 键名说明已不再包含在内。

Lowercase input displays as uppercase. This recreates printed key legends, including smooth geometric reconstructions of the PETSCII graphics, rather than the screen bitmap font.

小写输入将显示为大写。这还原了印刷的键帽字符，包括对 PETSCII 图形的平滑几何重构，而非屏幕上的位图字体。

The lettering comes from my own Commodore 64, a Central European unit. Its keycaps are the reference for every letter, number, and symbol in the font.

这些字符源自本人的一台 Commodore 64（中欧版）。其键帽是字体中每个字母、数字和符号的参考依据。

Open the online preview or preview.html and use the PETSCII buttons to insert the symbols, then copy them from the type tester. Left legends correspond to Commodore + key, right legends to Shift + key in uppercase/graphics mode. Pi on the up-arrow key also works with Commodore.

打开在线预览或 preview.html，使用 PETSCII 按钮插入符号，然后从文字测试器中复制。左侧字符对应“Commodore + 按键”，右侧字符对应“大写/图形模式下的 Shift + 按键”。向上箭头键上的 Pi 符号同样适用于 Commodore 组合键。

These are reconstructed outlines: identities and key assignments come from the Ultimate Commodore 64 Reference; frame thickness, curves, and print proportions are inferred from the keyboard photograph. They are not exact photo traces. Pi is unframed as on the key.

这些是重构的轮廓：字符标识和按键分配来自《Ultimate Commodore 64 Reference》；边框厚度、曲线和印刷比例则是根据键盘照片推断得出。它们并非照片的精确描摹。Pi 符号与键帽上一样，没有边框。

The preview handles the special character codes for you. Copied symbols need this font to display correctly. See the PETSCII key map and encoding guide for codes and keyboard combinations.

预览页面会自动处理特殊字符代码。复制的符号需要安装此字体才能正确显示。有关代码和键盘组合，请参阅 PETSCII 键位图和编码指南。

Editable outlines are in glyphs/, with build tools in scripts/.

可编辑的轮廓文件位于 glyphs/ 文件夹中，构建工具位于 scripts/ 文件夹中。
