# Roku’s OLED TVs are up to $400 off during Prime Day, starting at $700

Roku 的 OLED 电视在 Prime Day 期间最高优惠 400 美元，起售价为 700 美元。

Native 120Hz refresh rate, four HDMI 2.1 ports, and support for Dolby Vision and HDR10 Plus.

原生 120Hz 刷新率，配备四个 HDMI 2.1 接口，并支持杜比视界 (Dolby Vision) 和 HDR10 Plus。

Just over a week ago, we covered a deal at Amazon that knocked around 30 percent off Roku’s new (and first-ever) OLED TVs. The deal expired, but has returned for the final day of October Prime Day.

就在一周多前，我们报道了亚马逊上的一项优惠活动，Roku 的新款（也是首款）OLED 电视价格下调了约 30%。该优惠活动此前已结束，但现已在十月 Prime Day 的最后一天回归。

The 55-inch Pro Series OLED is $699.99 at Amazon, which is 30 percent off its list price. The 65-inch model is $799.99, which is $400 off its list price, also at Amazon. Both models support a native 120Hz refresh rate, which is great for gaming, plus four HDMI 2.1 ports for 4K gaming at its full refresh rate, and support for Dolby Vision and HDR10 Plus. You’ll also get a backlit Roku remote control.

55 英寸 Pro 系列 OLED 电视在亚马逊上的售价为 699.99 美元，相当于标价的 7 折。65 英寸型号售价为 799.99 美元，比标价优惠了 400 美元，同样在亚马逊有售。两款机型均支持原生 120Hz 刷新率（非常适合游戏），配备四个 HDMI 2.1 接口以实现 4K 全刷新率游戏体验，并支持杜比视界和 HDR10 Plus。此外，你还将获得一个带背光的 Roku 遥控器。

There have been a few TV deals during Prime Day, though these are the lowest prices on OLEDs by a wide margin. We haven’t tested this model, but it’s an affordable way to get unbeatable contrast for your next movie or game night.

Prime Day 期间出现了一些电视促销活动，但这些 OLED 电视的价格优势非常明显。我们尚未测试过该型号，但对于想要在下一次电影或游戏之夜获得极致对比度体验的用户来说，这是一种经济实惠的选择。
