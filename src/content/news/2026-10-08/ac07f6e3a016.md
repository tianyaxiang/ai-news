Games are one of the most creative forms of expression, and the gaming community keeps growing. But game creation has historically been limited to a select group of people who have technical experience and knowledge of complex game engines. Today, we’re introducing Playground, an experimental gaming platform that lets you create, play, and share custom games. Playground lowers the barriers to creation, so bringing game ideas to life can now be as easy as describing it through a few prompts — no coding experience required.

游戏是最具创造力的表达形式之一，游戏社区也在不断壮大。然而，游戏创作在历史上一直局限于少数拥有技术经验和复杂游戏引擎知识的人群。今天，我们推出了 Playground，这是一个实验性游戏平台，让你能够创建、游玩和分享自定义游戏。Playground 降低了创作门槛，让实现游戏创意变得像通过几个提示词描述一样简单——无需任何编程经验。

Create your custom game: With Playground’s conversational interface, you can direct the game just by typing what you want to build. Start from a blank canvas, remix starter prompts, or use guided support to shape your concept — and then test it right away. Want to tweak the physics, rewrite the rules, customize the characters, or overhaul the environment? Just ask. You stay in complete control of the creative direction from rough concept to final product.

创建你的自定义游戏：通过 Playground 的对话式界面，你只需输入想要构建的内容即可引导游戏开发。从空白画布开始，重组初始提示词，或使用引导支持来塑造你的概念——然后立即进行测试。想要调整物理效果、重写规则、自定义角色或彻底改变环境？只需提出要求即可。从粗略的概念到最终产品，你始终完全掌控创作方向。

Play, compete, and share across devices: The fun doesn’t stop at creation. Playground is home to an ever-growing catalog of community games. And because it’s browser-based, you can play these games on your phone or laptop. Once your game is ready, you can keep your game private, drop a shareable link to challenge friends and family, or publish it to the Playground Explore gallery for the broader Playground community.

跨设备游玩、竞技与分享：乐趣不止于创作。Playground 拥有一个不断增长的社区游戏目录。由于它是基于浏览器的，你可以在手机或笔记本电脑上游玩这些游戏。游戏准备就绪后，你可以将其设为私有，发送分享链接挑战亲友，或将其发布到 Playground Explore 画廊，供更广泛的 Playground 社区游玩。

Stay tuned for Unity Spark: For dedicated creators ready to expand their concepts, Playground will soon offer an integration with Unity Spark. Unity Spark unlocks robust, professional-level mechanics, high-fidelity 3D capabilities, and the flexibility of the Unity runtime. The accessibility of Playground and the world-class, game-building expertise of Unity provides the building blocks for an ecosystem where you can start with a simple text prompt and eventually grow into building immersive experiences with Unity.

敬请期待 Unity Spark：对于准备扩展其概念的专注创作者，Playground 即将提供与 Unity Spark 的集成。Unity Spark 解锁了强大的专业级机制、高保真 3D 功能以及 Unity 运行时的灵活性。Playground 的易用性与 Unity 世界级的游戏构建专业知识相结合，为生态系统提供了基石，让你能够从简单的文本提示开始，最终成长为使用 Unity 构建沉浸式体验。
