# Hot Girl Hotline is like ‘Dear Abby’ for the AI era
# Hot Girl Hotline：AI 时代的“亲爱的艾比”信箱

Women throughout history have always sought out relationship advice, whether from newspaper columnists like “Dear Abby,” letters to Cosmo, or just chatting with girlfriends. In the social media era, you can instead post anonymously on Reddit relationship forums or look to social media creators who discuss the topic. Now, two sisters are launching a startup for young women, Hot Girl Hotline, which focuses on bringing AI into these conversations.
纵观历史，女性总是在寻求情感建议，无论是通过像“亲爱的艾比”（Dear Abby）这样的报纸专栏、写信给《时尚》（Cosmo）杂志，还是仅仅与闺蜜聊天。在社交媒体时代，人们转而选择在 Reddit 的情感论坛上匿名发帖，或者向讨论此类话题的社交媒体创作者寻求帮助。如今，一对姐妹为年轻女性创办了一家名为“Hot Girl Hotline”的初创公司，专注于将人工智能引入这些对话中。

I know what you may be thinking: With all the advances in AI technology, founders are building something for women to talk about their dating woes? Well … yes! The reality is that people have already been turning to large language models (LLMs) via apps like ChatGPT for this type of advice, and the team believes this type of activity should be handled more carefully.
我知道你可能在想什么：在人工智能技术突飞猛进的今天，创始人竟然在开发一个让女性倾诉约会烦恼的产品？嗯……是的！现实情况是，人们已经开始通过 ChatGPT 等应用向大语言模型（LLM）寻求此类建议，而该团队认为，这类活动应当得到更谨慎的处理。

Unfortunately, as we’ve seen, chatting with a general-purpose chatbot about personal topics can sometimes lead to disastrous effects — especially for those who were already mentally fragile. ChatGPT, for example, has faced a wave of lawsuits over its alleged manipulative conversation tactics that plaintiffs say contributed to negative mental health effects, including, in some cases, suicide.
不幸的是，正如我们所见，与通用聊天机器人讨论个人话题有时会导致灾难性的后果——尤其是对于那些心理本就脆弱的人群而言。例如，ChatGPT 就面临了一波诉讼，原告称其具有操纵性的对话策略导致了负面的心理健康影响，在某些案例中甚至引发了自杀。

The idea behind Hot Girl Hotline is to reach young women who are looking to have these sorts of private conversations while navigating dating life and relationships, but in a safer way. “We’re not trying to be an AI companion,” explained Baila Mudgil, formerly a social media consultant, who co-founded the startup with her sister Sumrin (“Sumi”), who studied computer science at Stanford. “A companion is really going after solving loneliness, escapism, boredom — and you can rely on a lot of emotional dependency from that AI companion.”
Hot Girl Hotline 的初衷是触达那些在约会和人际关系中寻求私密对话的年轻女性，并提供一种更安全的方式。“我们并不是要成为一个 AI 伴侣，”曾任社交媒体顾问的 Baila Mudgil 解释道。她与曾在斯坦福大学学习计算机科学的妹妹 Sumrin（昵称“Sumi”）共同创立了这家公司。“伴侣型 AI 主要是为了解决孤独、逃避现实和无聊问题，而你可能会对这种 AI 伴侣产生严重的心理依赖。”

Baila says the startup’s approach is to focus on offering quality advice grounded in behavioral science, but in a way that makes it feel like you’re chatting with a trusted friend or big sister. The conversation is often Socratic, with the AI asking you questions to help you arrive at the answer yourself. “This is not to replace speaking to a human. This is not clinical advice, and we have a lot of safeguards in place … we take safety really seriously,” Baila said.
Baila 表示，该初创公司的核心在于提供基于行为科学的高质量建议，但呈现方式让你感觉就像是在与一位值得信赖的朋友或大姐姐聊天。对话通常采用苏格拉底式提问，AI 会通过向你提问来引导你自己得出答案。“这并不是要取代与人的交流。这不是临床建议，我们设置了许多保障措施……我们非常重视安全性，”Baila 说道。

If conversations raise flags, for instance, the user is referred to resources such as the crisis hotline 988. “We believe the key to quality advice is not just knowing what to do next; it’s understanding why you’re doing it. So that’s a big piece of it,” added Sumi. Notably, the AI also knows when to end the conversation; it doesn’t keep asking follow-up questions to drag you further down a rabbit hole. Instead, it pushes users to go take action out in the real world rather than ruminate on the problem.
如果对话触发了风险预警，用户会被引导至 988 危机热线等资源。“我们认为高质量建议的关键不仅在于知道下一步该做什么，还在于理解你为什么要这样做。这是其中非常重要的一部分，”Sumi 补充道。值得注意的是，该 AI 还知道何时结束对话；它不会不断追问，把你拖入更深的思维死胡同。相反，它会鼓励用户在现实世界中采取行动，而不是沉溺于问题本身。

The sisters believe there’s room for an app like this because not everyone wants to post on the web for advice, and there are times when you don’t want to bring up a personal, dating, or relationship-focused issue with a friend. Friends can bring their own biases into the conversation, or the user may not want to put their partner on blast for something they did before they determine whether they’re going to work through it or end the relationship. “It doesn’t mean you’re replacing your friend — you’re just trying to get another perspective,” says Baila of the hotline.
这对姐妹认为，这类应用有其生存空间，因为并非每个人都愿意在网上发帖寻求建议，而且有时你并不想与朋友讨论私人的约会或情感问题。朋友可能会将自己的偏见带入对话，或者用户在决定是修复关系还是结束关系之前，可能不想公开指责伴侣的行为。“这并不意味着你在取代你的朋友——你只是在寻求另一种视角，”Baila 谈到该热线时说道。

The app also personalizes its advice for each user to reflect their values, goals, identity, and tastes. Under the hood, the startup relies on a mix of AI models and encrypts users’ data in transit and at rest. It doesn’t sell or train on the data, either. When it uses a third-party AI model, it follows a zero-data-retention policy there as well.
该应用还会根据每位用户的价值观、目标、身份和喜好来个性化定制建议。在技术层面，该初创公司结合使用了多种 AI 模型，并对用户在传输和存储过程中的数据进行加密。它不会出售用户数据，也不会利用这些数据进行模型训练。在使用第三方 AI 模型时，它同样遵循零数据保留政策。

Hot Girl Hotline plans to monetize through a $9.99 per month subscription, which hundreds of its most active users are already paying for. Later, it may incorporate ads, too. Relationship advice is just the beginning of how the company wants to cater to young women. It also plans to expand into other areas, like beauty and lifestyle.
Hot Girl Hotline 计划通过每月 9.99 美元的订阅费实现盈利，目前已有数百名最活跃的用户在付费使用。未来，它也可能引入广告。情感建议只是该公司服务年轻女性的起点，它还计划扩展到美容和生活方式等其他领域。

New York-based Hot Girl Hotline is currently bootstrapped and will be participating in the Startup Battlefield 200 at TechCrunch Disrupt 2026, taking place October 13-15 in San Francisco. It’s available on iOS and the web.
总部位于纽约的 Hot Girl Hotline 目前处于自筹资金阶段，并将参加于 2026 年 10 月 13 日至 15 日在旧金山举行的 TechCrunch Disrupt 大会，入选 Startup Battlefield 200 榜单。该应用目前已在 iOS 和网页端上线。
