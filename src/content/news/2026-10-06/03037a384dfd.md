# Googlebook Is Google's Gemini-Enabled Laptop Family With Android Integration
# Googlebook 是谷歌推出的搭载 Gemini 智能并深度集成 Android 的笔记本电脑系列

Google has formally introduced Googlebook, a new family of laptops designed around Gemini Intelligence and closer integration with Android phones. The product line combines elements of Android and ChromeOS, supports Android apps, and is positioned around workflows that move between a phone and laptop.
谷歌正式推出了 Googlebook，这是一个围绕 Gemini 智能设计并与 Android 手机深度集成的新型笔记本电脑系列。该产品线结合了 Android 和 ChromeOS 的元素，支持 Android 应用程序，并定位于手机与笔记本电脑之间的无缝工作流。

Googlebook is not a rumored device or a renamed existing Chromebook. Google described the new category in its May 12, 2026 announcement, Google's official Googlebook introduction confirms. The company says the laptops are built for Gemini and designed to work seamlessly with Android devices, with features including Magic Pointer, Create Widgets, Quick Access and app continuity.
Googlebook 并非传闻中的设备，也不是现有 Chromebook 的更名。谷歌在 2026 年 5 月 12 日的公告中描述了这一新品类，谷歌官方的 Googlebook 介绍也证实了这一点。该公司表示，这些笔记本电脑专为 Gemini 而生，旨在与 Android 设备无缝协作，功能包括 Magic Pointer（魔法指针）、Create Widgets（创建小部件）、Quick Access（快速访问）和应用连续性。

### What Googlebook adds to Google's device lineup
### Googlebook 为谷歌设备阵容带来了什么

A laptop built around Gemini and Android. The central distinction is Google's attempt to make a laptop a more direct extension of an Android phone. Googlebook brings together Android and ChromeOS strengths rather than treating the laptop and phone as separate environments. That approach matters because many day-to-day tasks begin on a phone but require a larger screen, keyboard or desktop-style workspace to finish.
一款围绕 Gemini 和 Android 构建的笔记本电脑。其核心区别在于，谷歌试图让笔记本电脑成为 Android 手机更直接的延伸。Googlebook 汇集了 Android 和 ChromeOS 的优势，而不是将笔记本电脑和手机视为独立的运行环境。这种方法至关重要，因为许多日常任务始于手机，但需要更大的屏幕、键盘或桌面式工作空间才能完成。

Google has confirmed that Googlebook supports Android apps and cross-device features. Quick Access and app continuity are intended to make information and activity available across the phone and laptop, while Magic Pointer and Create Widgets are part of the new Googlebook experience.
谷歌已确认 Googlebook 支持 Android 应用程序和跨设备功能。“快速访问”和“应用连续性”旨在让信息和活动能够在手机与笔记本电脑之间同步，而“魔法指针”和“创建小部件”则是全新 Googlebook 体验的一部分。

Google has not, in the supplied material, published complete technical specifications for every configuration, so buyers should not assume common details such as processor, memory, display or battery specifications across all partner models.
谷歌在提供的资料中并未公布每种配置的完整技术规格，因此买家不应假设所有合作伙伴机型都具备相同的处理器、内存、显示屏或电池规格。

### Price, partners and U.S. rollout
### 价格、合作伙伴与美国市场发布

Googlebook starts at $899. Google is developing the device family with hardware partners including Acer, ASUS, Dell, HP and Lenovo, among others. That partner model means Googlebook should be understood as a platform and product category rather than a single, uniform laptop SKU.
Googlebook 起售价为 899 美元。谷歌正与宏碁 (Acer)、华硕 (ASUS)、戴尔 (Dell)、惠普 (HP) 和联想 (Lenovo) 等硬件合作伙伴共同开发该系列设备。这种合作伙伴模式意味着 Googlebook 应被理解为一个平台和产品类别，而非单一、统一的笔记本电脑 SKU。

The launch was framed as a fall 2026 rollout. Initial preorders began around late September 2026, with U.S. store availability and shipping beginning October 4, 2026. Availability outside the United States, as well as individual model configurations and regional pricing, should be checked with the relevant hardware partner or retailer.
此次发布定于 2026 年秋季。初步预订于 2026 年 9 月下旬开始，美国门店供货和发货于 2026 年 10 月 4 日开始。美国以外地区的供货情况，以及各型号的具体配置和区域定价，应咨询相关的硬件合作伙伴或零售商。

For buyers, the starting price establishes Googlebook as a premium laptop purchase rather than a low-cost secondary device. The more important question is whether its phone integration and Gemini-led experience fit the tools a team already uses. A business relying heavily on Android apps and Google services may see more value in the continuity features than a team whose work is primarily tied to software available only on another desktop platform.
对于买家而言，这一起售价将 Googlebook 定位为高端笔记本电脑，而非低成本的辅助设备。更重要的问题是，其手机集成功能和以 Gemini 为核心的体验是否契合团队现有的工具。对于严重依赖 Android 应用和谷歌服务的企业来说，这些连续性功能可能比那些工作主要依赖于其他桌面平台独占软件的团队更具价值。

### What the new category could mean for business workflows
### 这一新品类对企业工作流意味着什么

Googlebook's practical promise is a more continuous workspace. A manager could begin an activity on an Android phone, then continue on a laptop with a larger screen and keyboard without treating the phone as a disconnected device. Android app support may also reduce friction for teams that use mobile-first business software, although the usefulness will depend on each app's capabilities and the specific Googlebook implementation.
Googlebook 的实际承诺是提供一个更连续的工作空间。经理可以在 Android 手机上开始一项活动，然后转到拥有更大屏幕和键盘的笔记本电脑上继续工作，而无需将手机视为断开连接的设备。对于使用“移动优先”业务软件的团队来说，Android 应用支持也可能减少摩擦，尽管其实用性取决于每个应用程序的功能以及具体的 Googlebook 实现方式。

The Gemini focus is also significant, but it should be evaluated as part of a real workflow rather than as a hardware feature alone. Businesses should identify which tasks benefit from an AI-enabled laptop experience, such as drafting, research, organizing information or moving work between devices. They should also confirm which Gemini features are available on the particular model, in their location and under their existing Google accounts or subscriptions.
对 Gemini 的关注同样重要，但应将其作为实际工作流的一部分来评估，而不是仅仅将其视为一项硬件功能。企业应确定哪些任务能从 AI 赋能的笔记本电脑体验中受益，例如起草文档、研究、整理信息或在设备间迁移工作。他们还应确认特定机型、所在地区以及现有谷歌账户或订阅下可以使用哪些 Gemini 功能。

Googlebook does not replace the need to assess software compatibility, device management needs or staff workflows. Its launch does, however, give companies using Android a new option when selecting laptops, especially where reducing context switching between mobile and desktop work is a priority.
Googlebook 并不能取代对软件兼容性、设备管理需求或员工工作流的评估。然而，它的推出确实为使用 Android 的公司在选择笔记本电脑时提供了一个新选项，特别是在优先考虑减少移动端和桌面端工作之间的上下文切换时。
