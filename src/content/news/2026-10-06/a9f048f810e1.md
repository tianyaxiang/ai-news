# The Download：人工智能的流行悖论与 EmTech Future 2026

This is today's edition of The Download, our weekday newsletter that provides a daily dose of what's going on in the world of technology.

People really hate AI, so why can’t they get enough? —Will Douglas Heaven
人们真的很讨厌人工智能，那为什么他们又对其趋之若鹜？——Will Douglas Heaven

Over the summer I talked to the CEO of Springboards, a startup building an LLM designed to come up with a wide variety of responses. He said something that’s been stuck in my head since: “We often say that we’re a self-loathing AI company. We don’t know if we really like what we’re doing.”
今年夏天，我采访了 Springboards 的首席执行官，这是一家正在构建大语言模型（LLM）的初创公司，旨在生成各种各样的回复。他当时说的一句话一直萦绕在我的脑海中：“我们常说自己是一家‘自我厌恶’的人工智能公司。我们不知道自己是否真的喜欢正在做的事情。”

My reply? “I guess that makes me a self-loathing AI journalist.” It was a joke! I love my job. But I do not love many of the things the technology I write about has become: warped by hype, steered by zealots, inescapable—and I’m far from alone.
我的回答是：“那我想我就是一名‘自我厌恶’的人工智能记者了。”这当然是个玩笑！我热爱我的工作。但我并不喜欢我所报道的这项技术如今的许多面貌：被炒作扭曲、被狂热分子引导、无处不在——而且我绝非孤身一人。

Around the world, a love/hate attitude toward AI is very much the vibe right now. Public sentiment is souring fast, with more people saying AI will have a negative impact than a positive one. At the same time, AI use is skyrocketing. So why do people hate AI but keep coming back for more? Here’s my theory.
在世界各地，对人工智能又爱又恨的态度是当下的主流情绪。公众舆论正在迅速恶化，认为人工智能负面影响大于正面影响的人越来越多。与此同时，人工智能的使用率却在飙升。那么，为什么人们讨厌人工智能却又不断地使用它呢？这就是我的理论。
