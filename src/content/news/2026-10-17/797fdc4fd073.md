# Tesla driver says he was asleep and using FSD when he crashed into highway patrol cars

特斯拉司机称其在撞上高速公路巡逻车时处于睡眠状态并正在使用全自动驾驶（FSD）功能。

Video shows the crash that injured two CHP officers responding to an earlier incident on I-80.

视频显示了这起撞车事故，事故导致两名正在处理 I-80 公路早前事故的加州公路巡警（CHP）受伤。

After this horrific crash involving a Tesla driver who said he was asleep and using the driver assist mode, the California Highway Patrol warns that “Despite its name, full self-driving is not fully autonomous. Technology does not relieve drivers of their responsibility to safely operate their vehicles.”

在这起涉及一名特斯拉司机的可怕车祸发生后，该司机声称自己当时处于睡眠状态并使用了驾驶辅助模式。加州公路巡警对此警告称：“尽管名字叫全自动驾驶，但它并非完全自主。技术并不能免除驾驶员安全操作车辆的责任。”

Video released by CHP shows the Tesla speeding past flares, cones, and emergency lights where officers were on the scene of an earlier accident, before ramming into the back of a parked patrol vehicle. CHP says two officers were seriously injured in the accident and taken to a hospital.

加州公路巡警发布的视频显示，这辆特斯拉在经过早前事故现场的信号弹、锥形桶和应急灯时加速行驶，随后撞上了一辆停放的巡逻车尾部。加州公路巡警表示，两名警官在事故中受重伤并被送往医院。

Following numerous collisions with parked emergency vehicles, NHTSA launched an investigation into Tesla’s Autopilot software in 2021. It eventually issued a report finding the driver-assist features didn’t do enough to keep drivers engaged, linking them to hundreds of crashes and dozens of deaths when both the system and driver failed to respond.

在发生多起与停放的应急车辆相撞的事故后，美国国家公路交通安全管理局（NHTSA）于 2021 年对特斯拉的自动辅助驾驶（Autopilot）软件展开了调查。该机构最终发布报告称，这些驾驶辅助功能在保持驾驶员注意力方面做得不够，并将数百起车祸和数十起死亡事故归咎于系统和驾驶员均未能做出反应。

Tesla released an update for Autopilot in 2023 adding more controls and alerts to prompt drivers to be in full control of the car when Autosteer is enabled, but NHTSA then had to open another investigation into whether those changes went far enough.

特斯拉在 2023 年发布了自动辅助驾驶的更新，增加了更多的控制和警报，以提示驾驶员在启用自动转向功能时必须完全掌控车辆，但 NHTSA 随后不得不再次展开调查，以确定这些改进措施是否足够充分。
