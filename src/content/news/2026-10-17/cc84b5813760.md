# Microsoft Surface Laptop Ultra Review: The First Real AI PC

Performs well unplugged. Music-ready speakers.TIREDBattery life can’t compete with the MacBook Pro. Some very popular games aren’t supported yet. Lower-tier RAM options limit AI capabilities.**

保存此文章，评分：7/10，打开评分说明。WIRED：有史以来最亮的笔记本电脑显示屏。类似 MacBook 的设计和制造质量。磁吸式 USB-C 接口非常巧妙。不插电时性能表现出色。适合播放音乐的扬声器。TIRED：电池续航无法与 MacBook Pro 竞争。一些非常热门的游戏尚不支持。低配内存选项限制了 AI 能力。

**A new fleet of Windows laptops has arrived, and the Surface Laptop Ultra is its flagship vessel. Ever since it was announced, it's been one of my anticipated laptops of the year. It not only promises to be a beefed-up evolution of the Surface Laptop (a laptop I already really liked), but also debuts a brand-new piece of silicon: the Nvidia RTX Spark N1X "superchip."**

一批全新的 Windows 笔记本电脑已经到来，而 Surface Laptop Ultra 是其中的旗舰机型。自发布以来，它一直是我今年最期待的笔记本电脑之一。它不仅承诺成为 Surface Laptop（一款我本来就很喜欢的笔记本电脑）的强化升级版，还首次搭载了一款全新的芯片：Nvidia RTX Spark N1X “超级芯片”。

**The goal is a single laptop that can handle on-device AI, gaming, content creation, and nearly anything else you might throw at it. If that sounds a bit like a MacBook Pro, you're on the right track. But unlike previous attempts to dethrone Apple, the Surface Laptop Ultra is trading blows with the very best. I just wish it had the battery life to truly keep up with the MacBook Pro.**

其目标是打造一台能够处理端侧 AI、游戏、内容创作以及几乎任何其他任务的笔记本电脑。如果这听起来有点像 MacBook Pro，那你的方向是对的。但与以往试图挑战苹果地位的产品不同，Surface Laptop Ultra 确实具备了与顶级产品一较高下的实力。我只是希望它的电池续航能真正跟上 MacBook Pro 的水平。

**Killer Looks and Sounds. This isn't Microsoft's first foray into a more powerful laptop. The company first tried that in 2021 with the Surface Laptop Studio. At the time, it was the company's answer to the MacBook Pro: a laptop-tablet hybrid oriented toward the growing class of content creators and media professionals. It even came with a discrete graphics card (though not a particularly good one).**

惊艳的外观与音效。这并不是微软首次尝试制造更强大的笔记本电脑。该公司在 2021 年通过 Surface Laptop Studio 首次进行了尝试。当时，它是微软对 MacBook Pro 的回应：一款面向日益增长的内容创作者和媒体专业人士群体的笔记本与平板电脑混合设备。它甚至配备了独立显卡（尽管性能并不算特别出色）。

**The Surface Laptop Ultra rejects the Studio's form-factor experimentation and opts for a more straightforward but polished approach. It's a powerful clamshell laptop that's designed for people who need the extra performance—whether for gaming, content creation, or on-device AI workflows. The ideal demographic, I suppose, is someone who does some of all of that.**

Surface Laptop Ultra 摒弃了 Studio 的外形实验，选择了更直接但更精致的设计方案。这是一款功能强大的翻盖式笔记本电脑，专为需要额外性能的用户设计——无论是用于游戏、内容创作还是端侧 AI 工作流。我想，理想的目标用户应该是那些同时涉及这些领域的人。

**At 4.4 pounds, the Surface Laptop Ultra is lighter than the 16-inch MacBook Pro but slightly thicker. It's actually the thickest of all the new N1X laptops, with HP's OmniBook Ultra being the thinnest. The Surface Laptop Ultra appears thinner than it actually is, though, thanks to the inventive way it hides its side vents—so discreet that it's hard to photograph them. The design has just enough small touches like that to make it stand out.**

Surface Laptop Ultra 重 4.4 磅，比 16 英寸的 MacBook Pro 更轻，但略厚一些。实际上，它是所有新款 N1X 笔记本电脑中最厚的一款，而惠普的 OmniBook Ultra 是最薄的。不过，得益于其隐藏侧面通风口的巧妙方式，Surface Laptop Ultra 看起来比实际更薄——这些通风口非常隐蔽，以至于很难拍摄到它们。这种设计中充满了类似的小巧思，使其脱颖而出。

**There's also a soft dip into the keyboard well, with a unique look that matches the curved lip of the underside vents. I appreciate the modern vibe of the rounded screen corners, though some apps don't account for them well and nearly cut off elements of the Title Bar. One other minor annoyance is that there's no lip for your finger when the device is closed. You can slide your fingernail in between, but in most cases, you'll need two hands to open it.**

键盘区域有一个柔和的凹陷，其独特的外观与底部通风口的弧形边缘相呼应。我很欣赏屏幕圆角带来的现代感，尽管有些应用程序对圆角的适配并不好，几乎切掉了标题栏的元素。另一个小麻烦是，设备闭合时没有供手指抠开的边缘。你可以用指甲滑进去，但在大多数情况下，你需要双手才能打开它。

**Photograph: Luke Larsen. But once opened, the Surface Laptop Ultra gets mostly compliments from me. The keyboard is fantastic, as is the extra-large touchpad with haptic feedback. I still dislike the power button placement (why not put it in the top right like every other laptop, as an obvious button, instead of between keys?), and the keycaps could be a bit larger. But overall, I was very happy and comfortable typing and swiping on the laptop.**

摄影：Luke Larsen。但一旦打开，Surface Laptop Ultra 大多赢得了我的好评。键盘非常棒，带有触觉反馈的超大触摸板也是如此。我仍然不喜欢电源键的位置（为什么不像其他笔记本电脑那样把它放在右上角作为一个显眼的按钮，而是放在按键之间？），而且键帽可以再大一点。但总的来说，我在使用这台笔记本电脑打字和滑动时感到非常愉快和舒适。

**I was also very pleased with the audio. The aforementioned underside vents also hide the four speakers, which sound fabulous. I was really impressed by how loud, rich, and full these sound, perhaps only second to the 16-inch MacBook Pro (and 15-inch MacBook Air). There's even a hint of bass!**

我对音效也非常满意。前面提到的底部通风口还隐藏了四个扬声器，听起来效果极佳。它们声音的响度、丰富度和饱满度给我留下了深刻印象，可能仅次于 16 英寸的 MacBook Pro（和 15 英寸的 MacBook Air）。甚至还有一丝低音效果！

**Next to those speakers are lots of useful ports. You have the usual for a laptop of this type, including HDMI, USB-A, a headphone jack, an SD card slot, and three USB4 Type-C ports. The system can support up to three 4K external monitors or a single 5K display. Regardless of the resolution, you're maxed out at a 120 Hz refresh rate. This is slightly behind the latest MacBook Pro, which can support two 5K displays at a 120 Hz refresh rate. But overall, I'm really happy with this mix of connectivity—in fact, it just might be the most well-rounded offering I can imagine.**

在这些扬声器旁边有许多实用的接口。它具备此类笔记本电脑常见的接口，包括 HDMI、USB-A、耳机插孔、SD 卡槽和三个 USB4 Type-C 接口。该系统最多可支持三台 4K 外接显示器或一台 5K 显示器。无论分辨率如何，最高刷新率都限制在 120Hz。这略逊于最新的 MacBook Pro，后者可以支持两台 120Hz 刷新率的 5K 显示器。但总的来说，我对这种接口组合非常满意——事实上，这可能是我能想象到的最全面的配置了。

**The Surface Laptop Ultra has a trick up its sleeve involving magnets. The Surface Laptop has long used the Surface Connect, a proprietary magnetic power port that can also plug into Microsoft's Surface Dock. The Surface Laptop Ultra now uses USB-C, and how Microsoft engineered it to make it magnetic is impressive. Disappointingly, only the USB-C port on the right side has this magnetic feature, but all USB-C ports deliver power. You've always been able to buy third-party add-ons that do something similar, but it's a really neat bit of innovation.**

Surface Laptop Ultra 还有一个关于磁铁的“秘密武器”。Surface Laptop 长期以来一直使用 Surface Connect，这是一种专有的磁吸电源接口，也可以插入微软的 Surface Dock。Surface Laptop Ultra 现在改用了 USB-C，微软将其设计为磁吸式的方式令人印象深刻。令人失望的是，只有右侧的 USB-C 接口具有此磁吸功能，但所有 USB-C 接口都支持供电。虽然你一直可以购买实现类似功能的第三方配件，但这确实是一个非常巧妙的创新。

**Photograph: Luke Larsen. Shines Bright. What I appreciate most about all the Surface Laptop designs is the 3:2 aspect ratio display. I often find that more vertical space comes in handy, especially if you frequently work with documents, spreadsheets, and websites. Combined with the smaller 15-inch touchscreen (compared to the typical 16-incher), this makes for a more compact laptop to carry. At a full inch narrower than the 16-inch MacBook Pro, it fits in a laptop bag a bit more easily.**

摄影：Luke Larsen。光芒四射。我最欣赏所有 Surface Laptop 设计的一点是 3:2 的屏幕比例。我经常发现更多的垂直空间非常有用，特别是如果你经常处理文档、电子表格和网页的话。结合较小的 15 英寸触摸屏（相比典型的 16 英寸机型），这使得笔记本电脑更便于携带。它比 16 英寸的 MacBook Pro 窄了整整一英寸，放入笔记本电脑包时会更容易一些。

**The screen is extremely sharp, with a pixel density of 257 pixels per inch, just barely exceeding the 16-inch MacBook Pro. Like the MacBook Pro, the Surface Laptop Ultra's display uses Mini-LED backlighting. OLED is far more commonly used in Windows laptops, though, which make up for their lack of brightness with extreme contrast and stronger color performance. But here, the Surface Laptop Ultra clearly has its targets on the MacBook Pro. That may change soon with the rumored launch of the OLED MacBook Pro.**

屏幕非常清晰，像素密度为每英寸 257 像素，仅略高于 16 英寸的 MacBook Pro。与 MacBook Pro 一样，Surface Laptop Ultra 的显示屏采用了 Mini-LED 背光技术。不过，OLED 在 Windows 笔记本电脑中更为常见，它们通过极高的对比度和更强的色彩表现来弥补亮度的不足。但在这里，Surface Laptop Ultra 的目标显然是 MacBook Pro。随着传闻中 OLED MacBook Pro 的发布，这种情况可能很快就会改变。
