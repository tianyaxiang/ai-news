Large language model (LLM) agents can solve complex interactive tasks, yet reasoning and tool calls take time. In continuously changing environments, an otherwise correct action may arrive too late.

大型语言模型（LLM）智能体能够解决复杂的交互式任务，但推理和工具调用需要时间。在不断变化的环境中，一个原本正确的操作可能会因为响应太慢而失效。

We introduce RealtimeBench, with 180 tasks across six game types, to evaluate agents while games continue running during inference. Paired evaluations show that GPT-6.1-sol outperforms the faster Jev when games are paused, but falls behind when they keep running.

我们引入了 RealtimeBench，包含六种游戏类型下的 180 项任务，旨在评估智能体在推理过程中游戏持续运行时的表现。配对评估显示，当游戏暂停时，GPT-6.1-sol 的表现优于速度更快的 Jev，但当游戏持续运行时，其表现则会落后。

We propose Two-Timescale Offline Recursive Self-Improvement (TORSI): a slow teacher studies failures and tests corrections in paused games between attempts, then writes reusable instructions for a fast student. The student acts without teacher calls during gameplay, and validation scores determine which updates are adopted.

我们提出了双时间尺度离线递归自我改进（TORSI）方法：一个“慢速教师”在尝试间隙通过暂停的游戏研究失败案例并测试修正方案，随后为“快速学生”编写可重用的指令。学生在游戏过程中无需教师调用即可自主行动，验证分数则决定了哪些更新会被采纳。

Two rounds raise Jev's average test score from 13.51 to 24.19 on a 0--100 progress scale, improving all six game types. The instructions also improve average scores for three other models without retuning.

经过两轮迭代，Jev 在 0-100 分的进度量表上的平均测试分数从 13.51 提高到了 24.19，且在所有六种游戏类型中均有提升。这些指令还在无需重新调整的情况下，提高了其他三个模型的平均分数。

These findings point toward real-time intelligence: enabling powerful LLM agents to remain effective in a continuously changing world by organizing deliberation, learning, and action across timescales.

这些发现指向了实时智能的方向：通过跨时间尺度组织审议、学习和行动，使强大的 LLM 智能体能够在不断变化的世界中保持有效性。
