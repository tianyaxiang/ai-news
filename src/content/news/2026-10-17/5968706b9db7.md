Apple’s updated App Store rules go after ‘nudify’ AI apps

苹果公司更新了应用商店规则，旨在打击“一键脱衣”类人工智能应用。

The terms more explicitly ban apps that create ‘nonconsensual intimate imagery.’

这些条款更明确地禁止了那些制作“非自愿私密影像”的应用程序。

Apple’s App Store rules now more explicitly ban so-called “nudify” apps that generate intimate images of people without their consent using AI tools.

苹果的应用商店规则现在更明确地禁止了所谓的“一键脱衣”应用，这些应用利用人工智能工具在未经他人同意的情况下生成其私密图像。

The rule changes follow significant criticism toward Apple after X users used Grok earlier this year to make nonconsensual, sexualized deepfakes, including minors. Apple didn’t pull X or Grok from the App Store, though it told senators that it threatened to yank Grok.

此前，X平台用户在今年早些时候利用Grok制作了包括未成年人在内的非自愿性深伪内容，苹果因此面临巨大批评，随后出台了这些规则变更。苹果并未将X或Grok从应用商店下架，尽管其曾告知参议员称已威胁要移除Grok。

At Apple’s WWDC event in June, protestors also called on Apple to “remove Grok, X, and all nudify technology from the App Store,” and in July, a San Francisco city attorney ordered Apple to remove 13 “nudify” apps, which it said it did.

在六月份的苹果全球开发者大会（WWDC）上，抗议者呼吁苹果“将Grok、X以及所有‘一键脱衣’技术从应用商店中移除”。七月份，旧金山市检察官要求苹果下架13款“一键脱衣”应用，苹果表示已执行该要求。

As part of updates announced Friday, Apple says that developers “must proactively prevent and remove” nonconsensual, sexual images as part of its content moderation requirements for App Store apps. Developers who “fail to implement adequate safeguards” or “don’t remedy violations after notice” may be removed from the App Store.

作为周五宣布的更新内容的一部分，苹果表示，作为应用商店内容审核要求的一部分，开发者“必须主动预防并移除”非自愿的性图像。未能“实施充分保障措施”或“在收到通知后未纠正违规行为”的开发者可能会被从应用商店中移除。

Apple also clarified a rule that bars “bait-and-switch” apps, or apps that add or change features, including “nudification,” after they have been approved.

苹果还明确了一项规则，禁止“诱导切换”类应用，即那些在获得批准后添加或更改功能（包括“脱衣”功能）的应用程序。

Apple’s rules also more clearly spell out that any apps that “facilitate human trafficking or the exploitation of children” will be pulled from the App Store and “blocked from alternative distribution.”

苹果的规则还更明确地指出，任何“助长人口贩卖或剥削儿童”的应用都将被从应用商店中下架，并“禁止通过其他渠道分发”。
