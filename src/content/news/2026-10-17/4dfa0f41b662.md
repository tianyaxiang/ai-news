Analogue is re-releasing its SNES and Genesis consoles with some nice upgrades.

Analogue 正在重新发布其 SNES 和 Genesis 游戏机，并进行了一些不错的升级。

The Super Nt Plus and Mega Sg Plus add USB-C and USB-A ports plus built-in wireless connections for controllers.

Super Nt Plus 和 Mega Sg Plus 增加了 USB-C 和 USB-A 端口，以及用于控制器的内置无线连接功能。

Analogue announced new versions of its Super Nt and Mega Sg retro consoles today with a “reworked industrial design” and built-in wireless connectivity for controllers and accessories. Like the original versions, the new Super Nt Plus and Mega Sg Plus are able to play Nintendo and Sega Genesis cartridges using an FPGA processor that perfectly recreates the functionality of Nintendo and Sega’s 16-bit consoles without the need for emulation.

Analogue 今日宣布推出其 Super Nt 和 Mega Sg 复古游戏机的新版本，采用了“重新设计的工业外观”，并内置了用于控制器和配件的无线连接功能。与原版一样，新款 Super Nt Plus 和 Mega Sg Plus 能够通过 FPGA 处理器运行任天堂和世嘉 Genesis 卡带，该处理器无需模拟即可完美重现任天堂和世嘉 16 位游戏机的功能。

Although neither of the new consoles allows SNES or Genesis titles to be played in 4K like the Analogue 3D does with Nintendo 64 titles, the 1080p Super Nt and Mega Sg Plus add a USB-C port so you can power them with the cables you already use with your phone and mobile devices, and an additional USB-A port for other peripherals. Both consoles also introduce a built-in Bluetooth or a low-latency 2.4Ghz wireless connection for connecting to gamepads without requiring an additional controller port dongle.

尽管这两款新游戏机不像 Analogue 3D 运行任天堂 64 游戏那样支持 4K 分辨率，但 1080p 的 Super Nt 和 Mega Sg Plus 增加了一个 USB-C 端口，因此您可以使用手机和移动设备现有的线缆为其供电，此外还有一个用于连接其他外设的 USB-A 端口。这两款游戏机还引入了内置蓝牙或低延迟 2.4GHz 无线连接功能，无需额外的控制器端口加密狗即可连接游戏手柄。

Despite their redesigns, both upgraded consoles look very similar to the original versions that launched in 2017 and 2018.

尽管经过了重新设计，这两款升级版游戏机看起来与 2017 年和 2018 年推出的原版非常相似。

Wired controllers are still an option, and the upgraded consoles can both be powered up remotely using whatever gamepad is connected as the primary controller. Analogue also says game cartridges can now be hot swapped without having to completely power down either console after quitting the game through a menu.

有线控制器仍然是一个可选方案，且两款升级版游戏机都可以通过连接的主控制器手柄进行远程开机。Analogue 还表示，现在可以通过菜单退出游戏后，无需完全关闭游戏机即可进行卡带热插拔。

Both the Super Nt Plus and Mega Sg Plus will be available for preorder starting on October 19th at 9AM PT/11AM ET for $219.99 with shipping expected to start sometime in December, later this year.

Super Nt Plus 和 Mega Sg Plus 将于太平洋时间 10 月 19 日上午 9 点（东部时间上午 11 点）开启预购，售价为 219.99 美元，预计将于今年 12 月的某个时间开始发货。
