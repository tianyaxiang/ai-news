I transition between different pieces of audio equipment on any given day. I listen to music with my earbuds, but they always fall out when I talk, so for meetings I tend to go without. If I’m in a loud setting and need to speak in a virtual meeting, I reach for my old, reliable headset for its higher-quality mic, but I often sacrifice call quality in non-gaming environments. And when I’m gaming, I love my light-up kitty-ear headset, but I don’t necessarily want to wear that at a coffee shop or in a professional setting. Which is why the new Razer Barracuda V2 Pro is made for people like me. It’s an elegant, multipurpose headset that does the job, no matter what the job is.

我每天都会在不同的音频设备之间切换。我用耳塞听音乐，但说话时它们总是会掉出来，所以开会时我通常不戴。如果我身处嘈杂的环境中需要参加视频会议，我会拿出那款老旧但可靠的耳机，因为它麦克风质量更好，但在非游戏环境下，我往往不得不牺牲通话质量。而玩游戏时，我喜欢戴着那款带灯光的猫耳耳机，但我并不想在咖啡馆或职场环境中佩戴它。这就是为什么新款 Razer Barracuda V2 Pro 是为我这类人设计的。它是一款优雅的多功能耳机，无论什么场合都能胜任。

Working 9 to 5
Photograph: Louryn Strampe
The Barracuda V2 Pro feels more like a good pair of over-ear headphones than a traditional gaming headset. During my testing period, I regularly listened to music with it. It sounds clear and balanced—there’s a bit of quality loss in the lows and highs, but it’s still perfectly acceptable, and the hybrid active noise canceling (ANC) is fantastic. Press the Multifunction button to cycle between ambient, ANC-on, and ANC-off modes. Hold it to activate your voice assistant or customize it in the Razer Synapse Windows/Mac software or Razer Audio smartphone app. (Customizations include cycling ANC modes, adjusting volume via Bluetooth, or using your voice assistant, so you can choose what each button press does.) You can tweak the EQ in Razer Audio and cycle between presets—Movie, Music, Game, and Default—with the press of a button. Press the same button twice to cycle between connectivity modes, including Bluetooth, HyperSpeed Wireless, and Simultaneous Listening. You can also go wired with the included cable.

Barracuda V2 Pro 给人的感觉更像是一副优质的头戴式耳机，而非传统的游戏耳机。在测试期间，我经常用它听音乐。它的声音清晰且平衡——虽然在高音和低音部分略有损耗，但完全在可接受范围内，而且其混合主动降噪（ANC）功能非常出色。按下多功能键可在环境音、开启降噪和关闭降噪模式之间循环切换。长按该键可激活语音助手，或在 Razer Synapse（Windows/Mac）软件或 Razer Audio 手机应用中进行自定义设置。（自定义选项包括切换降噪模式、通过蓝牙调节音量或使用语音助手，你可以自行决定每次按键的功能。）你可以在 Razer Audio 中调整均衡器，并通过按键在电影、音乐、游戏和默认等预设模式间切换。连续按两次该键可在蓝牙、HyperSpeed 无线和同步收听等连接模式间切换。你也可以使用附带的线缆进行有线连接。

I love that the HyperSpeed wireless dongle is cleverly stored behind a magnetic panel in the left earcup. The wide range of connectivity options means this headset is compatible with PC, Mac, PlayStation, Steam Deck, and mobile devices. You can also use it with your Xbox in Bluetooth mode. Simultaneous audio lets you answer a phone call while still in the game, or play a video in the background while you’re in a matchmaking queue. THX Spatial Audio makes gaming on my PC feel even more immersive.

我非常喜欢它将 HyperSpeed 无线接收器巧妙地收纳在左耳罩磁吸面板后的设计。丰富的连接选项意味着这款耳机兼容 PC、Mac、PlayStation、Steam Deck 和移动设备。你也可以在蓝牙模式下将其与 Xbox 配合使用。同步音频功能让你在玩游戏时也能接听电话，或者在等待匹配时在后台播放视频。THX 空间音频让我在 PC 上的游戏体验更加沉浸。

Onboard controls include two buttons for volume adjustment. I wish this was a wheel, but that boils down to personal preference. There’s also a dedicated mute toggle, and you can tap the power button to pause, double-tap to skip the next song, or triple-tap to go back; you can also use this button to accept or reject incoming calls.

五点到九点的娱乐
摄影：Louryn Strampe
机身控制按键包括两个音量调节键。我个人希望它是滚轮设计，但这纯属个人偏好。此外还有一个专门的静音开关，你可以点击电源键来暂停，双击跳至下一首歌曲，或三击返回上一首；你也可以使用此按钮来接听或拒绝来电。

The Barracuda V2 Pro features internal beamforming mics and an optional, detachable external boom microphone (HyperClear Full Band Mic). Both offer clear, concise sound, according to the colleagues and gamers who helped me assess them. The leatherette-covered memory foam earcups are soft, squishy, and comfortable, though the base felt a little tight on my jaw and earlobes after hours of use. I have a smaller-than-average head and can imagine this would be more of a nuisance for people with impressive noggin circumferences. The headset is lightweight, coming in at 285 grams.

Barracuda V2 Pro 配备了内置波束成形麦克风和可选的可拆卸外接麦克风（HyperClear 全频麦克风）。据协助我进行评估的同事和玩家反馈，两者都能提供清晰、准确的声音。人造革覆盖的记忆棉耳罩柔软、有弹性且舒适，不过在佩戴数小时后，底座对我的下颌和耳垂感觉有些紧。我的头围比平均水平小，我可以想象对于头围较大的人来说，这可能会更令人困扰。这款耳机重量很轻，仅为 285 克。

I have yet to deplete the impressive 70-hour battery (50 with ANC on), even while listening to music and playing games every day. A fifteen-minute USB-C charge gets you about six hours of battery life.

尽管我每天都在听音乐和玩游戏，但我至今还没用完它那令人印象深刻的 70 小时续航（开启降噪后为 50 小时）。通过 USB-C 充电 15 分钟即可获得约 6 小时的续航时间。

There are a couple of quirks. I wish it folded down more compactly, and I need to find a carrying case that has space for the external microphone when it isn’t attached. This headset also lacks Razer’s well-known RGB lights, which may be a draw for users taking it to the office or on the train. But if you see the brand name and expect a Chroma-compatible confection, this isn’t the right set for you. Ultimately, though, it’s very well-rounded and versatile, and it’s an easy recommendation for anyone seeking a headset that can work during the day and into the night.

它也有一些小毛病。我希望它能折叠得更紧凑一些，而且我需要找一个能容纳外接麦克风的便携包。这款耳机还缺少雷蛇标志性的 RGB 灯效，对于那些在办公室或火车上使用它的用户来说，这可能反而是一个优点。但如果你看到这个品牌就期待一款支持 Chroma 灯效的产品，那它可能不适合你。总而言之，它非常全面且多功能，对于任何寻求一款既能白天工作又能晚上娱乐的耳机的人来说，它都是一个值得推荐的选择。
