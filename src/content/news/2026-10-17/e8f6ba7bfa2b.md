The new phone has a 5.4-inch outer display and a 7.6-inch inner display, a “nano‑texture” finish on the inner screen, and barely any visible crease. Instead of Face ID, it uses Touch ID on the power button. It has two cameras, a main lens and a wide-angle lens. Apple completely revamped iOS 27’s controls and apps for the foldable, including putting the dock on the side of the phone. And it has a flashy opening animation when you unfold the phone.

这款新手机拥有 5.4 英寸外屏和 7.6 英寸内屏，内屏采用“纳米纹理”涂层，几乎看不到折痕。它没有使用面容 ID，而是将触控 ID 集成在电源键上。手机配备两颗摄像头，分别为一颗主镜头和一颗广角镜头。苹果为这款折叠屏手机彻底重构了 iOS 27 的控制界面和应用程序，包括将程序坞移至手机侧边。此外，当你展开手机时，还会出现炫酷的开屏动画。

The iPhone Duo is a big new bet from Apple, though one that’s a little late to the game compared to competitors like Samsung and Google. The phone isn’t cheap: it starts at $1,999 for the base 256GB model, and the prices only go up from there. Still, Apple said that preorder demand was “extraordinary,” so it could have a hit on its hands.

iPhone Duo 是苹果公司的一项重大新赌注，尽管与三星和谷歌等竞争对手相比，它进入市场的时间稍晚。这款手机价格不菲：256GB 基础款起售价为 1,999 美元，且价格随配置增加。尽管如此，苹果表示预购需求“非常惊人”，这可能意味着他们手中握有一款热门产品。

The first foldable iPhone is finally here, and following its announcement, we got the chance to try the phone for ourselves. We’ve long been told that Apple would make a foldable iPhone once the tech was good enough — has it been worth the wait?

首款折叠屏 iPhone 终于问世了，在发布会后，我们有机会亲自试用了这款手机。长期以来，人们一直说苹果会在技术足够成熟时推出折叠屏 iPhone——这值得等待吗？

It definitely feels like something different. The inner screen has a decidedly matte finish that handled even some glare from the windows above the hands-on area well. The folding mechanism is smooth, and even IP68 rated, and the crease is almost impossible to find with my finger. The 5.4-inch outer screen looks petite by modern slab phone standards.

它确实给人一种与众不同的感觉。内屏采用了明显的哑光涂层，即使在上手区域上方窗户的强光照射下，表现依然出色。折叠机构非常顺滑，且具备 IP68 级防护，用手指几乎摸不到折痕。以现代直板手机的标准来看，5.4 英寸的外屏显得有些小巧。
