Maybe it was the grey color, the slightly bulbous roofline, or the vaguely SUV-like proportions of the electric Porsche Macan, but as I shuttled kids and groceries around city and suburban streets, I never once noticed a longing stare from other drivers. Unlike with other cars I’ve tested, no enthusiasts stopped to ask me questions. The 2026 Porsche Macan Turbo simply blended in, leaving in its wake a raft of existential questions. What is a sports car, anyway? Does it need to be overwhelmingly extroverted? Should it match the leaner, lighter look of its gas-powered sibling?

也许是因为那灰色的车身、略显臃肿的车顶线条，又或是保时捷电动 Macan 那模糊的 SUV 比例，当我在城市和郊区的街道上接送孩子、采购杂货时，从未感受到其他司机投来渴望的目光。与我测试过的其他车型不同，没有车迷停下来向我询问。2026 款保时捷 Macan Turbo 就这样融入了车流，留下一连串关于存在意义的疑问：到底什么是跑车？它必须表现得极其张扬吗？它是否应该与其燃油版车型一样，拥有更精简、更轻盈的外观？

Drive one and the questions cease. Maybe that’s because the acceleration makes it hard to breathe? Or maybe it’s because, by the numbers, the Macan is a sports car at heart despite its rather humdrum crossover body style. The classic Porsche Sport Chrono is an analog touch to an otherwise digital dashboard. My passengers would agree. The kids I drove to soccer practice, both my own and others, gave the punchy acceleration high marks. The G-forces it reports on the head-up display ($2,660) can easily mimic the pull of gravity. Stab the accelerator at 70 mph and it lurches as though it were standing still.

开上一回，这些疑问便烟消云散了。或许是因为那令人窒息的加速感？又或许是因为从数据上看，尽管 Macan 拥有平庸的跨界车外壳，但其内核却是一台纯粹的跑车。经典的保时捷 Sport Chrono 组件为数字化仪表盘增添了一抹机械质感。我的乘客们也深表赞同。无论是我的孩子还是其他一起去足球训练的孩子，都对那强劲的加速表现给出了高分。抬头显示器（价值 2,660 美元）上显示的 G 值可以轻松模拟重力牵引。在时速 70 英里时猛踩油门，车辆会像从静止状态起步一样猛然前冲。

(Don’t worry, fellow parents. I did not try that with children on board.) On winding roads, the car comes alive, thanks in part to the four-wheel steering ($2,150). Drop it into Sport Plus mode, and the car literally drops on its air suspension. Are we sure this is another anonymous crossover? Maybe anonymity is a useful trait in a car this responsive and powerful. Unlike most two-row crossovers, you get the feeling of sitting in the Macan, not on it. The wheel extends far enough to make a sporty driving position possible, and the classic Porsche chronometer ($1,040) perched atop the dash ticks off the seconds it takes to get the kid to soccer practice.

（各位家长请放心，我并没有在载着孩子时尝试这样做。）在蜿蜒的道路上，得益于四轮转向系统（价值 2,150 美元），这台车仿佛活了过来。切换到 Sport Plus 模式，车身会随着空气悬架的降低而下沉。我们确定这只是另一款平庸的跨界车吗？或许对于这样一台响应灵敏且动力强劲的车来说，低调反而是一种有用的特质。与大多数两排座跨界车不同，坐在 Macan 里，你会感觉自己是“嵌入”在车内，而不是“坐在”车上。方向盘的伸展范围足以实现运动化的驾驶姿势，而仪表盘上方经典的保时捷计时器（价值 1,040 美元）则记录着送孩子去足球训练所花费的每一秒。

Nuts and bolts: The 2026 Porsche Macan Turbo EV is a two-row crossover with 630 horsepower and a 100-kilowatt-hour battery. The EV, which the automaker provided to me for a week, starts at $112,700. However, the one I drove rang up at $149,610, including destination fees. Yes, the options alone could buy a nicely equipped Chevy Bolt. With Porsche and options, if you have to ask…well, it’s better not to. The Porsche Macan Turbo EV I drove came in Slate Grey Neo (a $2,620 upgrade), which was really well executed and among the best greys I’ve seen. If you crave luxurious anonymity, then this grey is your color.

基本参数：2026 款保时捷 Macan Turbo 电动版是一款两排座跨界车，拥有 630 马力和 100 千瓦时的电池组。这台由厂商提供给我试驾一周的电动车起售价为 112,700 美元。然而，我驾驶的这台车总价高达 149,610 美元（含运费）。没错，光是选配的价格就足以买下一辆配置不错的雪佛兰 Bolt。对于保时捷和选配，如果你还要问价格……那最好还是别问了。我驾驶的这台保时捷 Macan Turbo 电动版采用了板岩灰（Slate Grey Neo，选配价 2,620 美元），漆面质感极佳，是我见过最好的灰色之一。如果你渴望奢华的低调，那么这种灰色就是你的不二之选。

There were a slew of other options, and in my view, not all of them are worth it. I’d skip the passenger display ($1,710); my kids liked that it could play YouTube videos, but they can’t ride up front, and my wife was underwhelmed. “Our washing machine can play YouTube videos,” she said. I’d also forgo the illuminated Porsche wordmark on the front seatbacks ($690), puddle lamps that shine “PORSCHE” onto the pavement ($420), Porsche crests on the headrests ($640), and an illuminated Turbo badge on the door sills ($1,230).

车上还有一大堆其他选配，在我看来，并非每一项都物有所值。我会跳过副驾显示屏（1,710 美元）；虽然我的孩子们喜欢它能播放 YouTube 视频，但他们不能坐在前排，而我妻子对此也毫无兴趣。“我们的洗衣机都能播放 YouTube 视频，”她说。我还会放弃前排座椅靠背上的发光保时捷字样（690 美元）、将“PORSCHE”投射到地面的迎宾灯（420 美元）、头枕上的保时捷盾徽（640 美元）以及门槛上的发光 Turbo 徽标（1,230 美元）。

That Turbo badge became a point of contention for some of my friends, who were quick to point out that EVs technically can’t have turbos. That is true, though automotive names have long been divorced from reality. Almost every Porsche these days has a turbo, even those not labeled as such. It’s not just Porsche: BMW’s model numbers, for example, stopped reflecting engine displacement decades ago, and the Ford Galaxie never traveled between star systems using its hyperdrive. Sorry, friends, I’m not taking the bait. At least the Macan Turbo’s EV qualities don’t raise too many questions. It charges fast — exactly 20 minutes from 19% to 80% on a 350-kilowatt charger — and gets about 285 miles of highway range.

那个 Turbo 徽标成了我一些朋友争论的焦点，他们很快指出电动车在技术上不可能有涡轮增压器。这确实没错，但汽车命名早已脱离了现实。如今几乎每一辆保时捷都有涡轮增压器，即使那些没有标注的车型也是如此。这不仅仅是保时捷的问题：例如，宝马的车型编号几十年前就不再反映发动机排量了，而福特 Galaxie 也从未通过超空间驱动在星系间穿梭。抱歉，朋友们，我不会上钩的。至少 Macan Turbo 的电动性能没有引起太多质疑。它的充电速度很快——在 350 千瓦的充电桩上，从 19% 充到 80% 仅需 20 分钟——高速续航里程约为 285 英里。

The advanced driver assistance system ($1,140) works well enough, even if it hunts a bit in the lane. The AI-powered voice assistant did a solid job of interpreting my queries and commands. For a while, I thought it was the only way to turn on the heated steering wheel ($720), which was actually fine because it worked so well. (Surprise! There’s a small button hidden on the inner rim of the steering wheel between two spokes. IYKYK, I guess.) When it’s time to leave the Macan, you can just open the door and walk away. The Burmester sound system ($4,960) will keep bumpin’ until you lock the door, which is a helpful reminder.

高级驾驶辅助系统（1,140 美元）表现尚可，尽管在车道内偶尔会有些晃动。人工智能语音助手在理解我的查询和指令方面做得不错。有一段时间，我以为这是开启方向盘加热（720 美元）的唯一方式，其实这也没什么不好，因为它确实好用。（惊喜！在方向盘内侧轮辐之间隐藏着一个小按钮。懂的都懂。）当需要离开 Macan 时，你只需打开车门走开即可。柏林之声（Burmester）音响系统（4,960 美元）会一直播放音乐直到你锁车，这算是一个有用的提醒。

Where it fell short: The navigation system, and specifically how it integrates with the EV, was underwhelming. When the car was at a 22% state of charge, I asked it to find the nearest fast charger. It directed me to two. Not two different options, but two chargers that I was supposed to use sequentially. The first was a 50-kilowatt charger in a mall parking lot 10 minutes away. There, I was supposed to add a few percentage points before unplugging and driving across the street to another mall’s parking lot, where I would finish at a 350-kilowatt charger. Wait, what? I ignored the machine and drove straight to the 350-kilowatt charger, arriving at 19%.

不足之处：导航系统，特别是它与电动系统的整合方式，令人失望。当车辆电量为 22% 时，我让它寻找最近的快充桩。它指引我去了两个。不是两个不同的选项，而是让我按顺序使用两个充电桩。第一个是 10 分钟路程外商场停车场里的 50 千瓦充电桩。按照指引，我得在那里充入几个百分点的电量，然后拔掉插头，开车穿过街道去另一个商场的停车场，在那里用 350 千瓦的充电桩完成充电。等等，什么？我无视了机器的建议，直接开往 350 千瓦的充电桩，到达时电量为 19%。
