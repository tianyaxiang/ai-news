It takes a special home theater projector to get me to stick through a movie like Supergirl. Critics panned it for dark themes, and its visually dark tones make it literally tough to see. But when I used the AWOL Vision Aetherion Max to project it onto a 120-inch screen with Dolby Atmos sound blasting from every corner of my basement, the film looked stunning.

需要一台特别的家庭影院投影仪，才能让我坚持看完《女超人》这样的电影。影评人批评其主题阴暗，且视觉上的暗色调让画面看起来非常吃力。但当我使用 AWOL Vision Aetherion Max 将其投射到 120 英寸屏幕上，并让杜比全景声从地下室的各个角落轰鸣而出时，这部电影看起来令人惊艳。

The Aetherion Max, with its precise picture-quality settings and sharp 4K clarity, provides an experience rivaling that of a movie theater. Supergirl might suffer from bad CGI and even worse dialog, but the projector's features, like Dark Detail and Enhanced Black Level, brought the film to life, making reds and blues pop with amazing vividness.

Aetherion Max 凭借其精确的画质设置和清晰的 4K 分辨率，提供了媲美电影院的体验。《女超人》可能受困于糟糕的 CGI 和更差的对白，但该投影仪的“暗部细节”（Dark Detail）和“增强黑电平”（Enhanced Black Level）等功能让电影焕发了生机，使红色和蓝色呈现出惊人的鲜艳感。

Priced at $4,499, this ultrashort throw model sits right next to the wall or projector screen. In some ways, it reminds me of the LG Micro RGB Evo television with highly adjustable picture-quality settings. Ultimately, though, I'm a fan of the Aetherion Max because it projects a breathtakingly colorful image.

这款超短焦机型售价 4,499 美元，可紧贴墙壁或投影幕布放置。在某些方面，它让我想起了拥有高度可调画质设置的 LG Micro RGB Evo 电视。但归根结底，我喜欢 Aetherion Max，因为它投射出的图像色彩令人叹为观止。

An Engineering Marvel, Except for the Remote. Before taking stock of its powerhouse specs, I noticed the Aetherion Max's design. Shaped a bit like a Tesla Cybertruck, the silver projector has sharp edges that lend a modern look. The front of the machine (the side that faces you) has no buttons except a single white line that animates when you power it on—a detail I found to be distracting and then powered off.

工程奇迹，除了遥控器。在评估其强大的规格之前，我注意到了 Aetherion Max 的设计。这款银色投影仪形状有点像特斯拉 Cybertruck，锋利的边缘赋予了它现代感。机器正面（面向你的一侧）没有任何按钮，只有一条在开机时会产生动画效果的白线——我发现这个细节很让人分心，于是将其关闭了。

The other side (which faces the screen) is where you'll find three HDMI 2.1 ports (including one for eARC passthrough audio), a USB-A port for a local storage device, optical and 3.5mm audio-out ports, and a 1 GBps Ethernet port for wired streaming.

另一侧（面向屏幕的一侧）设有三个 HDMI 2.1 端口（包括一个用于 eARC 音频传输的端口）、一个用于本地存储设备的 USB-A 端口、光纤和 3.5 毫米音频输出端口，以及一个用于有线流媒体传输的 1 Gbps 以太网端口。

This model runs on Google TV, and that’s a relief because it meant setup only took a few minutes. Auto-keystoning with manual screen-size options worked just fine. There’s also a QR code you can use to automate keystone correction from your phone, but my room was too dark, and it didn't work for me. (AWOL Vision reps told me it should have worked fine even in a dark room like mine.)

该机型运行 Google TV 系统，这让人松了一口气，因为这意味着设置只需几分钟。自动梯形校正配合手动屏幕尺寸选项运行良好。此外还有一个二维码，可用于通过手机自动进行梯形校正，但我的房间太暗了，没能成功。（AWOL Vision 的代表告诉我，即使在我这样的暗室里，它也应该能正常工作。）

The portable projector weighs 19.3 pounds—notably less than its 23-pound competitor Hisense XR10 Smart Projector (8/10, WIRED Recommends)—and I found it both easy to grip and move around between rooms. For wireless streaming, the Aetherion Max supports Wi-Fi 7, offering nice compatibility and speed. It also runs quite fast thanks to 8 GB of internal RAM and offers 128 GB of internal storage for storing content, though I relied mostly on streaming.

这款便携式投影仪重 19.3 磅，明显轻于其 23 磅重的竞争对手海信 XR10 智能投影仪（8/10，WIRED 推荐），我发现它很容易抓握并在房间之间移动。在无线流媒体方面，Aetherion Max 支持 Wi-Fi 7，提供了良好的兼容性和速度。得益于 8GB 的内存，它运行速度相当快，并提供 128GB 的内部存储空间用于存储内容，尽管我主要依赖流媒体。

Unlike the highly rated Xgimi Titan Noir Max, the Aetherion Max's iris does not adjust dynamically as you watch content. But similar to the Hisense XR10, you can adjust picture-quality settings to match the room. For example, Dolby Vision Bright works best in a bright room, allowing the iris to let in more light.

与广受好评的极米 Titan Noir Max 不同，Aetherion Max 的光圈不会随着观看内容动态调整。但与海信 XR10 类似，你可以调整画质设置以匹配房间环境。例如，杜比视界明亮模式（Dolby Vision Bright）在明亮的房间里效果最好，允许光圈进入更多光线。

For color accuracy, the projector hits 110 percent of the Rec. 2020 spec. That means if you watch a movie that meets that color spec, like Guardians of the Galaxy Vol. 2 or Inside Out, you will see the entire color palette. AWOL Vision also lists the contrast ratio as 6,000:1 and lumens at 3,300 ISO, but—as I've learned from personal experience—those specs are not the final arbiter of picture quality. Many other factors come into play, including the darkness level of a room.

在色彩准确度方面，该投影仪达到了 Rec. 2020 标准的 110%。这意味着如果你观看符合该色彩标准的电影，如《银河护卫队 2》或《头脑特工队》，你将看到完整的色域。AWOL Vision 还列出了 6,000:1 的对比度和 3,300 ISO 流明，但正如我从个人经验中学到的，这些规格并不是画质的最终决定因素。许多其他因素也会发挥作用，包括房间的黑暗程度。

The weak spot here is the remote, which is functional if not impressive. I like its large Home button that’s centered and easy to find in the dark without looking. There’s also a button for Google Gemini and convenient shortcuts for apps like Netflix. My main issue is with the volume buttons, placed just above the shortcuts, but small and hard to find. Only the upper half of the remote is backlit, which uselessly doesn't extend to all of the buttons, including volume.

这里的弱点是遥控器，它功能尚可但并不令人印象深刻。我喜欢它居中的大号主页按钮，在黑暗中无需查看也能轻松找到。它还有一个 Google Gemini 按钮和 Netflix 等应用的便捷快捷键。我的主要问题在于音量按钮，它们位于快捷键正上方，但太小且难以找到。遥控器只有上半部分有背光，这很没用，因为背光没有覆盖到所有按钮，包括音量键。

Don’t Fear the Bright Sunshine. The Aetherion Max's ability to work in a brightly lit room blew me away throughout testing. I watched the movie Mayday on Apple TV+ from start to finish in broad daylight, projecting the image on a wall that measures nearly 16 feet diagonally. I didn't notice any contrast problems and could see nighttime scenes just fine. The slight warping on my wall distorted the image a bit, but that's to be expected of ultrashort throw models.

别怕明媚的阳光。在整个测试过程中，Aetherion Max 在明亮房间里的工作能力让我大吃一惊。我在光天化日之下在 Apple TV+ 上从头到尾看完了电影《五月天》，将图像投射到一面对角线近 16 英尺的墙上。我没有注意到任何对比度问题，并且可以很好地看清夜间场景。墙壁上的轻微翘曲使图像有些变形，但这对于超短焦机型来说是意料之中的。

In my windowless testing room, with a full surround-sound system that even includes height channel speakers in the ceiling, the Aetherion Max performed even better. To test a projector's contrast ratio and brightness, I typically play dark scenes in the movies Awake and The Creator, and I've never seen the scenes look as great as they did when rendered by the Aetherion Max. I didn’t even have to tweak the picture-quality settings (and generally when I test these specs, such is not the case).

在我的无窗测试室里，配备了包括天花板高度声道扬声器在内的全环绕声系统，Aetherion Max 的表现甚至更好。为了测试投影仪的对比度和亮度，我通常会播放电影《觉醒》和《造物主》中的暗场景，我从未见过这些场景在 Aetherion Max 的渲染下表现得如此出色。我甚至不需要调整画质设置（通常当我测试这些规格时，情况并非如此）。

Dark scenes in House of the Dragon, which I streamed using the HBO Max app, did require some user intervention, though. A beach scene in the moonlight involving a mysterious dragon would be hard to render even on high-end home cinema projectors, but once I enabled Dark Detail and Enhanced Black Level, I could suddenly see everything clearly, just as the TV show creators intended.

不过，使用 HBO Max 应用播放的《龙之家族》中的暗场景确实需要用户进行一些干预。即使在高端家庭影院投影仪上，月光下的海滩场景（涉及一条神秘的龙）也很难渲染，但一旦我启用了“暗部细节”和“增强黑电平”，我突然就能清楚地看到一切，正如电视剧创作者所预期的那样。

Indeed, every time I ran into a slight issue with picture quality, I found a matching setting that solved the problem. In a dark hallway scene during the movie The Batman, gun flashes looked bright red and clear, but some amber tones on Batman looked a shade too dark. Once I switched to Dolby Vision Bright, I could easily make out the grimaces and muscle flexes.

事实上，每当我遇到画质上的轻微问题时，我都能找到相应的设置来解决。在电影《蝙蝠侠》的一个黑暗走廊场景中，枪火看起来明亮且清晰，但蝙蝠侠身上的一些琥珀色调看起来太暗了。一旦我切换到杜比视界明亮模式，我就能轻松看清表情和肌肉的抽动。
