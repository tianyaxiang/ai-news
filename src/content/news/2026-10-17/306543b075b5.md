# We can finally hear some of Wu-Tang Clan’s Once Upon A Time in Shaolin album

我们终于可以听到武当派（Wu-Tang Clan）专辑《Once Upon A Time in Shaolin》的部分内容了。

Pablo Torre is playing the one-of-one album owned by Martin Shkreli, the feds, and now a crypto group called PleasrDAO.

Pablo Torre 正在播放这张由马丁·什克雷利（Martin Shkreli）、联邦政府，以及现在的加密货币组织 PleasrDAO 所拥有的独一无二的专辑。

Wu-Tang’s Once Upon A Time in Shaolin album has, for the most part, been extremely difficult to listen to, but you can now hear two snippets of the album thanks to the podcast Pablo Torre Finds Out, which just published an episode all about the exclusive record.

武当派的《Once Upon A Time in Shaolin》专辑在很大程度上一直极难听到，但多亏了播客节目《Pablo Torre Finds Out》，你现在可以听到该专辑的两个片段。该播客刚刚发布了一期专门介绍这张独家唱片的节目。

Once Upon A Time in Shaolin has a long history, which Pablo Torre Finds Out goes into in detail. Disgraced pharmaceutical executive Martin Shkreli bought the only copy of the album, which cannot be commercially released until 2103, in 2015 at auction. Shkreli had to forfeit the album to the US government, and the government sold it in 2021 to the crypto art collective PleasrDAO, which has allowed it to be played at special exhibitions.

《Once Upon A Time in Shaolin》有着悠久的历史，《Pablo Torre Finds Out》对此进行了详细探讨。声名狼藉的制药高管马丁·什克雷利在 2015 年的拍卖会上买下了这张专辑的唯一副本，该专辑在 2103 年之前不得进行商业发行。什克雷利后来被迫将专辑没收给美国政府，政府于 2021 年将其出售给了加密艺术团体 PleasrDAO，该团体允许在特别展览中播放它。

Pablo Torre Finds Out, working with PleasrDAO, hosted a listening party for the album where they played it for Wu-Tang Clan member Method Man, who had never heard it up until that point. The podcast recorded the event and Method Man’s reaction, and you can hear two snippets from the album at 32:30 and 39:02 during the show.

《Pablo Torre Finds Out》与 PleasrDAO 合作举办了一场专辑试听会，并为武当派成员 Method Man 播放了这张专辑，在此之前他从未听过。播客录制了这一活动以及 Method Man 的反应，你可以在节目 32:30 和 39:02 处听到专辑的两个片段。

Method Man’s review? “I do not hate this album.”

Method Man 的评价如何？“我不讨厌这张专辑。”
