### ruanyf / weekly

**科技爱好者周刊**
**Tech Enthusiast Weekly**

记录每周值得分享的科技内容，周五发布。欢迎投稿文章/软件/资源，请提交 issue。
This weekly publication records tech content worth sharing, released every Friday. Contributions of articles, software, or resources are welcome; please submit an issue.

P.S. 讨论区的《谁在招人》，是一个免费的程序员招聘帖，提供大量就业信息，欢迎发布工作/实习岗位。
P.S. The "Who is Hiring" section in the discussion area is a free job board for programmers, providing a wealth of employment information. You are welcome to post job or internship openings.
