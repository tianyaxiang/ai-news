# The Download: investigating deaths at the US border’s “virtual wall”
# 《下载》：调查美国边境“虚拟墙”下的死亡事件

This is today's edition of The Download, our weekday newsletter that provides a daily dose of what's going on in the world of technology.
这是今日份的《下载》，我们平日更新的简报，为您提供每日科技界动态。

The US spent billions on border surveillance. Why can’t it catch people before they die? When José Morales Bernal crossed the border into the US in April 2024, the day before his 32nd birthday, he was within range of three surveillance towers equipped with cameras and AI to automatically detect and track people. If the system worked as intended, Morales should have been apprehended. If he needed medical help, agents were trained to provide it. None of that happened. Instead, Morales died just 360 feet from the closest tower. It was local landfill workers, rather than Border Patrol, who first spotted him. An autopsy concluded that he had died of “environmental exposure.”
美国在边境监控上花费了数十亿美元。为什么它却无法在人们死亡前发现他们？2024年4月，何塞·莫拉莱斯·伯纳尔（José Morales Bernal）在32岁生日前夕越境进入美国时，他正处于三座监控塔的覆盖范围内，这些塔配备了摄像头和人工智能，可以自动探测并追踪人员。如果系统按预期工作，莫拉莱斯本应被拦截。如果他需要医疗救助，特工们也受过相关培训以提供帮助。但这一切都没有发生。相反，莫拉莱斯死在距离最近的塔仅360英尺的地方。最先发现他的不是边境巡逻队，而是当地的垃圾填埋场工人。尸检结论显示，他死于“环境暴露”。

A first-of-its-kind investigation by MIT Technology Review reveals that deaths like Morales’s are startlingly common. After mapping nearly 4,000 locations where human remains were found against information on nearly 600 surveillance towers, we discovered that a humanitarian crisis at the border has unfolded in view of the government’s own cameras. Read our full investigation into the deadly failings of the virtual wall. —James O'Donnell and Eileen Guo
《麻省理工科技评论》一项首创性的调查显示，像莫拉莱斯这样的死亡事件惊人地普遍。在将近4000处发现人类遗骸的地点与近600座监控塔的信息进行比对后，我们发现，一场人道主义危机正在政府摄像头的注视下上演。阅读我们关于虚拟墙致命缺陷的完整调查报告。——James O'Donnell 和 Eileen Guo

The US government is about to spend another $1 billion to triple the virtual wall’s size, yet we found it has systemic flaws: broken towers, algorithms that failed to detect people and agents who simply did not respond to alerts. Here are four ideas for what Customs and Border Protection should do to rectify those failures.
了解更多：作为我们15个月调查的一部分，我们制作了第一份美墨边境监控塔附近死亡事件的综合地图。查看该地图，并了解我们是如何制作它的。美国政府即将再投入10亿美元将虚拟墙的规模扩大三倍，但我们发现它存在系统性缺陷：塔台损坏、算法无法探测到人，以及特工根本不对警报做出反应。以下是海关及边境保卫局应采取的四项建议，以纠正这些失误。

An analysis by the Times of San Diego found at least 138 cases since 2022 in which migrants’ remains were found within the nominal range of a nearby surveillance tower. A separate MIT Technology Review analysis estimated that more than half of those people were likely within a tower’s field of view. Gómez Hernández was one of them. Here’s her story, written by our partners at Times of San Diego. All of these stories are part of Dying on Camera, our new series investigating the failures of border surveillance technology and their human cost.
《圣地亚哥时报》的一项分析发现，自2022年以来，至少有138起移民遗骸在附近监控塔的名义覆盖范围内被发现的案例。《麻省理工科技评论》的另一项分析估计，其中超过一半的人很可能处于塔台的视野范围内。戈麦斯·埃尔南德斯（Gómez Hernández）就是其中之一。这是她的故事，由我们的合作伙伴《圣地亚哥时报》撰写。所有这些报道都是我们新系列《镜头下的死亡》（Dying on Camera）的一部分，该系列旨在调查边境监控技术的失败及其背后的人道代价。
