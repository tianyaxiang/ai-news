# Image-Derived PM10 Estimation in Cattle Feedlot Using Machine Learning: Addressing Concentration Ranges Beyond Existing Digital Imaging Methods
# 利用机器学习进行牛饲养场 PM10 图像估算：应对现有数字成像方法之外的浓度范围

**Abstract:** Affordable dust monitoring remains a pressing need for the cattle feedlot industry, yet camera-based PM estimation, despite its growing body of research in urban air quality settings, has not been evaluated under the extended concentration ranges characteristic of intensive livestock operations.

**摘要：** 低成本粉尘监测仍然是牛饲养场行业迫切的需求。尽管基于摄像头的颗粒物（PM）估算在城市空气质量领域的研究日益增多，但尚未在集约化畜牧作业所特有的高浓度范围内进行过评估。

This study developed an image-based approach using contrast panel features and machine learning to estimate PM10 concentrations in a commercial cattle feedlot, where hourly average PM10 ranged from 250 to 1,000 ug/m^-3 and instantaneous concentrations reached 5,000 to 20,000 ug/m^-3.

本研究开发了一种基于图像的方法，利用对比度面板特征和机器学习来估算商业牛饲养场中的 PM10 浓度。在该环境中，每小时平均 PM10 浓度范围为 250 至 1,000 ug/m^-3，瞬时浓度可达 5,000 至 20,000 ug/m^-3。

Grayscale images were captured during the evening dust peak period, and features including panel contrast, black and white panel pixel values, and overall image brightness were extracted. The model also incorporated recent past values from preceding images and solar zenith angle as predictors.

研究人员在傍晚粉尘高峰期拍摄了灰度图像，并提取了包括面板对比度、黑白面板像素值以及整体图像亮度在内的特征。该模型还将先前图像的近期历史数值和太阳天顶角作为预测因子纳入其中。

Among the candidate models evaluated, XGBoost achieved the highest predictive performance, with an R^2 of 0.792 and a median absolute error of 103 ug/m^-3. Feature importance analysis revealed that (a) panels positioned farthest from the camera contributed most strongly to predictions and (b) that black panel pixel values were more sensitive than white panel values to changes in PM10 concentration.

在评估的候选模型中，XGBoost 实现了最高的预测性能，其 R^2 为 0.792，中位绝对误差为 103 ug/m^-3。特征重要性分析显示：(a) 距离摄像头最远的面板对预测的贡献最大；(b) 黑色面板像素值对 PM10 浓度变化的敏感度高于白色面板像素值。

Prediction accuracy during the sunset transition, which coincides with the onset of the feedlot evening dust peak, remains an area for further refinement. These findings demonstrate the feasibility of image-based PM10 estimation across PM concentration ranges substantially exceeding those reported in prior urban studies and provide practical guidelines for future deployment in feedlot environments.

日落过渡期间（恰逢饲养场傍晚粉尘高峰开始时）的预测准确性仍有待进一步改进。这些研究结果证明了在远超以往城市研究报告的 PM 浓度范围内进行基于图像的 PM10 估算的可行性，并为未来在饲养场环境中的部署提供了实用指南。
