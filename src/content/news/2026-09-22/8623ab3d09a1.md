# OnomatoBridge: Onomatopoeia Translation and Rendering Pipeline in Manga
# OnomatoBridge：漫画中的拟声词翻译与渲染流程

**Abstract:** Manga is a comic drawn by black and white paints gaining popularity around the world. Onomatopoeia in Manga specifically appeals to the audience with its unique visual styles, which convey sound, motion, and emotion. 

**摘要：** 漫画是一种以黑白绘画为主的连环画，正风靡全球。漫画中的拟声词以其独特的视觉风格吸引着读者，传达出声音、动作和情感。

Visual onomatopoeia translation requires the clean replacement of Japanese onomatopoeia with onomatopoeia in the other language while preserving their visual style. Existing approaches often produce residual artifacts or style inconsistency when removing the Japanese onomatopoeia and rendering stylized English onomatopoeia. 

视觉拟声词翻译要求在用其他语言的拟声词替换日语拟声词时，既要做到干净利落，又要保留其原有的视觉风格。现有的方法在去除日语拟声词并渲染风格化的英语拟声词时，往往会产生残留伪影或风格不一致的问题。

To approach these problems, we present OnomatoBridge, a filtering pipeline for visual onomatopoeia translation. We evaluate OnomatoBridge from Japanese to English on the Manga109 onomatopoeia dataset and compare it with baseline image editing models. 

为了解决这些问题，我们提出了 OnomatoBridge，这是一个用于视觉拟声词翻译的过滤流程。我们在 Manga109 拟声词数据集上评估了从日语到英语的 OnomatoBridge 效果，并将其与基准图像编辑模型进行了比较。

Experimental results show that the filtered outputs by the proposed method outperform those of conventional methods. OnomatoBridge improves English text correctness by roughly 10 to 25 points and reduces residual Japanese text by about 20 to 50% in relative terms.

实验结果表明，该方法过滤后的输出优于传统方法。OnomatoBridge 将英语文本的准确性提高了约 10 到 25 个百分点，并将残留的日语文本相对减少了约 20% 到 50%。
