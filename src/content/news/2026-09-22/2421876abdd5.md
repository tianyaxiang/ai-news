# ZuckOff：随时感知房间内是否有摄像头

**ZuckOff merch is here. Tees, hoodies, hats and stickers. Shop now Help us test more hardware. We need camera glasses to capture.**

ZuckOff 周边商品现已上架，包括 T 恤、连帽衫、帽子和贴纸。立即选购，帮助我们测试更多硬件。我们需要更多智能眼镜来进行捕捉测试。

**ZuckOff: Know when a camera is in the room. Camera glasses announce themselves over Bluetooth. ZuckOff listens for that and tells you when Ray-Ban Meta, Oakley Meta, Snap Spectacles or similar hardware is nearby. Nothing leaves your phone and there is no account.**

ZuckOff：随时感知房间内是否有摄像头。智能眼镜会通过蓝牙广播自身的存在。ZuckOff 会监听这些信号，并在 Ray-Ban Meta、Oakley Meta、Snap Spectacles 或类似硬件靠近时提醒你。所有数据均保留在你的手机本地，无需注册账户。

**Flags camera glasses by their manufacturer signature. Shows the evidence behind every flag, so you can disagree with it. Logs every Bluetooth device it hears, glasses or not. Own a pair? Mark them once and they stop raising alerts. Background alerts, a Home Screen widget, and a Lock Screen Live Activity on iPhone. Start and stop a scan from the Shortcuts app, Siri or an automation. Export the whole log as CSV whenever you want it.**

通过制造商签名识别智能眼镜。显示每次识别背后的证据，你可以选择不采纳。记录它探测到的所有蓝牙设备（无论是否为眼镜）。如果你自己拥有一副？标记一次后，它们将不再触发警报。支持后台警报、主屏幕小组件以及 iPhone 锁屏实时活动。可通过快捷指令 (Shortcuts)、Siri 或自动化功能启动和停止扫描。随时可将完整日志导出为 CSV 文件。

**Signal: What it means**
**0x0D53 Luxottica: Ray-Ban Meta, Oakley Meta**
**0x058E Meta Platforms Technologies wearable**
**0x03C2 Snap: Spectacles**
**0xFD5F Service UUID registered to Oculus VR**
**Names: Product names like Spectacles, HeyCyan, VisionPro, VistaView, at lower confidence**

信号含义：
0x0D53 Luxottica：Ray-Ban Meta, Oakley Meta
0x058E Meta Platforms Technologies 可穿戴设备
0x03C2 Snap：Spectacles
0xFD5F 注册给 Oculus VR 的服务 UUID
名称：如 Spectacles、HeyCyan、VisionPro、VistaView 等产品名称（置信度较低）

**What it cannot do: Glasses are loudest when they power on, pair or leave the case. Most pairs keep advertising while worn, so you can usually hear them, but a few standalone models stay silent. Quiet is not proof that nobody is recording, and a detection is not proof that anyone is. Signal strength gives a rough distance and no direction.**

局限性：眼镜在开机、配对或离开充电盒时信号最强。大多数眼镜在佩戴时会持续广播，因此你通常能探测到它们，但少数独立型号会保持静默。没有探测到信号并不代表没人录像，探测到信号也不代表一定有人在录像。信号强度仅能提供大致距离，无法指示方向。
