# iPhone owners can now submit claims in Apple’s $250 million Siri AI settlement
# iPhone 用户现可就苹果 2.5 亿美元 Siri AI 和解案提交索赔申请

Apple is paying $250 million to settle claims that it failed to deliver an AI-upgraded Siri — and now, eligible iPhone owners can submit a claim for a payout. If you live in the US and purchased an iPhone 15 Pro, iPhone 15 Pro Max, or any iPhone 16 model between June 10th, 2024, and March 29th, 2025, you can file a claim on the settlement’s website, as reported earlier by MacRumors.

苹果公司将支付 2.5 亿美元，以就其未能提供 AI 升级版 Siri 的指控达成和解。目前，符合条件的 iPhone 用户可以提交索赔申请以获得赔偿。据 MacRumors 此前报道，如果您居住在美国，并在 2024 年 6 月 10 日至 2025 年 3 月 29 日期间购买了 iPhone 15 Pro、iPhone 15 Pro Max 或任何 iPhone 16 机型，即可在和解网站上提交申请。

You can receive an estimated $25 per eligible device, but the settlement’s website notes that this can increase to $95 depending on how many people file claims. To file a claim, you’ll need to enter your name, contact information, and iPhone serial number on the settlement’s website before the December 21st, 2026 deadline.

每台符合条件的设备预计可获得 25 美元的赔偿，但和解网站指出，根据索赔人数的不同，金额最高可增至 95 美元。如需提交申请，您需要在 2026 年 12 月 21 日截止日期前，在和解网站上输入您的姓名、联系方式以及 iPhone 序列号。

The class action lawsuit alleged Apple created a “clear and reasonable consumer expectation” that new AI features previewed during WWDC 2024 would launch alongside the iPhone 16. Instead, the iPhone 16 launched with a more limited set of capabilities despite being labeled as “built for Apple Intelligence.” The company later delayed the rollout of its AI-upgraded Siri, which only just arrived with iOS 27 earlier this month. Apple has denied any wrongdoing.

该集体诉讼指控苹果公司制造了“清晰且合理的消费者预期”，即在 WWDC 2024 上预览的新 AI 功能将随 iPhone 16 一同发布。然而，尽管 iPhone 16 被标记为“为 Apple Intelligence 而生”，但其发布时功能却更为有限。苹果公司随后推迟了 AI 升级版 Siri 的推出，该功能直到本月初才随 iOS 27 正式上线。苹果公司否认存在任何不当行为。
