# Do Quantum Models Scale Like LLMs?
# 量子模型是否像大语言模型（LLM）一样具有扩展性？

**Abstract:** In this work, we study the neural scaling laws of RydbergGPT, an autoregressive transformer model trained on qubit projective measurement data gathered from interacting Rydberg atom arrays. The quantum system is known to exhibit a finite-size remnant of a critical point as the laser detuning parameter is varied.

**摘要：** 在这项工作中，我们研究了 RydbergGPT 的神经扩展定律（neural scaling laws）。RydbergGPT 是一种自回归 Transformer 模型，其训练数据来自相互作用的里德堡原子阵列（Rydberg atom arrays）的量子比特投影测量数据。已知当改变激光失谐参数时，该量子系统会表现出临界点的有限尺寸残余。

We find that near the critical point the transformer loss as a function of training dataset size is well described by a power-law with a loss floor correction. However, away from criticality the quality of the power-law description is substantially reduced.

我们发现，在临界点附近，Transformer 的损失函数（作为训练数据集大小的函数）可以通过带有损失下限修正的幂律（power-law）很好地描述。然而，远离临界点时，幂律描述的质量会显著下降。

We then compare the statistical structure of both Rydberg measurements and natural-language corpora using an entropy-normalised, finite sample corrected mutual information "two-point" function. We find that near-critical statistics of the two point functions are closest to those observed in natural-language, whilst other qubit configurations far from the critical point have two-point functions that decay more rapidly.

随后，我们使用一种经过熵归一化和有限样本修正的互信息“两点”函数，比较了里德堡测量数据与自然语言语料库的统计结构。我们发现，临界点附近的“两点”函数统计特征与自然语言中观察到的最为接近，而远离临界点的其他量子比特配置，“两点”函数的衰减速度则更快。

This supports the hypothesis that multi-scale dependence contributes to stable neural scaling, and that scaling behaviour should be viewed as a property of the model-data pair.

这一发现支持了以下假设：多尺度依赖性有助于实现稳定的神经扩展，且扩展行为应被视为模型与数据对（model-data pair）的一种属性。
