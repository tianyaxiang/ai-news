# Meta’s AI agent has been blocked from using Amazon.com
# Meta 的 AI 智能体已被禁止访问 Amazon.com

Meta’s AI agent has been blocked from using Amazon.com. Sunday night, users of Meta’s AI assistant Muse started getting a strange error message when they tried to buy goods on Amazon. As spotted by GeekWire, the error message read: “Continued access by an unauthorized AI agent violates Amazon’s Conditions of Use, to which our customers have agreed.”
Meta 的 AI 智能体已被禁止访问 Amazon.com。周日晚上，Meta AI 助手 Muse 的用户在尝试通过亚马逊购买商品时，开始收到一条奇怪的错误信息。据 GeekWire 报道，该错误信息显示：“未经授权的 AI 智能体持续访问违反了亚马逊的使用条款，我们的客户已同意该条款。”

In other words, Muse isn’t welcome as a shopper — and anyone trying to buy something on Amazon through the AI agent will have to find it somewhere else. It’s easy to see the block as a tête-à-tête between tech giants. Amazon has its own cohort of foundation models, after all, along with one of the most popular inference platforms on the internet. As long as they’re under no legal obligation to open the doors to Muse, why would they?
换句话说，Muse 作为购物者并不受欢迎——任何试图通过该 AI 智能体在亚马逊上购物的人都必须另寻他处。人们很容易将此次封锁视为科技巨头之间的博弈。毕竟，亚马逊拥有自己的基础模型群，以及互联网上最受欢迎的推理平台之一。只要他们没有法律义务向 Muse 敞开大门，他们为什么要这样做呢？

But there are also substantive reasons Amazon might not want to be in the agentic commerce business just yet. If Muse makes a bad order, Amazon is going to be the one to clean it up — dealing with both the angry customer and the angry vendor. Muse has one of the lower hallucination rates, as AI models go, but it’s still pretty far from zero. Even if Amazon sees an opportunity in agent-driven commerce, they may want to wait a few more release cycles before rolling anything out.
但亚马逊目前可能不想涉足智能体驱动的商业领域，这背后也有实质性的原因。如果 Muse 下错了订单，亚马逊将不得不负责处理后续问题——既要应对愤怒的客户，也要应对愤怒的供应商。在目前的 AI 模型中，Muse 的幻觉率相对较低，但距离零幻觉仍有很大差距。即使亚马逊看到了智能体驱动商业的机遇，他们可能也希望在推出任何相关功能之前，再等待几个发布周期。
