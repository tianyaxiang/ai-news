# 4DGS-Fixer: Generative Sparse-View 4D Gaussian Splatting with Iterative Refinement Guided by Video Diffusion Priors
# 4DGS-Fixer：基于视频扩散先验引导的迭代优化生成式稀疏视角 4D 高斯溅射

**Abstract:** This paper addresses the challenges of dynamic scene synthesis from sparse-view videos. Existing methods employ geometric priors, adaptive optimization, or density-control strategies to improve 4D Gaussian modeling under sparse observations. However, they cannot fundamentally resolve the ill-posed problem caused by insufficient observations and missing scene information.
**摘要：** 本文探讨了从稀疏视角视频中进行动态场景合成所面临的挑战。现有方法通常采用几何先验、自适应优化或密度控制策略来改善稀疏观测下的 4D 高斯建模。然而，这些方法无法从根本上解决因观测不足和场景信息缺失所导致的病态问题。

Moreover, sparse-view 4D Gaussian Splatting (4DGS) often suffers from poor geometric initialization: with only a few input views, COLMAP typically reconstructs sparse and incomplete point clouds, leaving large scene regions without sufficient Gaussian support and making them difficult to recover through subsequent optimization.
此外，稀疏视角 4D 高斯溅射（4DGS）往往受限于较差的几何初始化：由于输入视角较少，COLMAP 通常只能重建出稀疏且不完整的点云，导致场景中大片区域缺乏足够的高斯支撑，难以通过后续的优化过程进行恢复。

To address these limitations, we propose a novel iterative refinement framework based on a video diffusion model to improve the completeness and consistency of dynamic 4D scenes. Specifically, we first estimate multi-view depth maps and fuse them into dense point clouds to provide more complete geometric initialization for a dynamic 4DGS representation.
为了解决这些局限性，我们提出了一种基于视频扩散模型的新型迭代优化框架，旨在提高动态 4D 场景的完整性和一致性。具体而言，我们首先估计多视角深度图并将其融合为稠密点云，从而为动态 4DGS 表示提供更完整的几何初始化。

We then employ a pretrained video restoration model to refine sequences rendered along novel camera trajectories at different time steps. The restored sequences serve as pseudo-supervision to regularize and iteratively refine the 4DGS representation. Experiments on a widely used benchmark dataset demonstrate that our method substantially outperforms existing baselines, achieving nearly a 2 dB PSNR improvement over the previous best-performing method.
随后，我们利用预训练的视频修复模型，对沿新颖相机轨迹在不同时间步渲染出的序列进行优化。这些修复后的序列作为伪监督信号，用于正则化并迭代优化 4DGS 表示。在广泛使用的基准数据集上的实验表明，我们的方法显著优于现有的基准模型，相较于此前性能最好的方法，PSNR 提升了近 2 dB。
