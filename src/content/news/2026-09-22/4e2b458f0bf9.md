# Vivo X500 Pro Max 具备 17 档动态范围与 4K 240fps 慢动作拍摄功能

Vivo’s new X500 flagship phones have arrived in China, and as usual the company’s focus is firmly on photography. The X500 Pro Max — a new tier in its lineup — is the first phone to use new sensors and silicon from Sony and MediaTek that enable impressive dynamic range and slow-motion video recording.

Vivo 全新 X500 系列旗舰手机已在中国正式发布，正如以往一样，该公司的重心依然牢牢锁定在影像领域。作为产品线中新增的顶级机型，X500 Pro Max 首次搭载了索尼与联发科的全新传感器及芯片，实现了令人惊叹的动态范围与慢动作视频录制能力。

The X500 Pro Max uses Sony’s new Lytia L910 image sensor, its first to use LOFIC, a sensor technology that enables expanded dynamic range. The 50-megapixel, 1/1.28-inch-type sensor delivers what Vivo calls “true 17 EV ultra-high dynamic range,” more than the 16.5 stops claimed by the Xiaomi 17 Ultra. The excellent 200-megapixel, 3.7x telephoto from the X300 Ultra returns here too, along with support for that phone’s 400mm teleconverter lens.

X500 Pro Max 采用了索尼全新的 Lytia L910 图像传感器，这是索尼首款应用 LOFIC 技术（一种可扩展动态范围的传感器技术）的产品。这款 5000 万像素、1/1.28 英寸的传感器实现了 Vivo 所称的“真 17 EV 超高动态范围”，超过了小米 17 Ultra 所宣称的 16.5 档。此外，X300 Ultra 上那颗出色的 2 亿像素 3.7 倍长焦镜头也得以回归，并继续支持该机型的 400mm 增距镜。

The X500 Pro and Pro Max are also the first phones run on MediaTek’s new Dimensity 9600 Pro, with the 9600M found in the base X500. The 2nm Pro chip brings with it the usual performance bumps, but also the ability to record 4K 240fps slow-motion video. 10bit Log recording and new cinematic video styles are included too.

X500 Pro 和 Pro Max 还是首批搭载联发科全新天玑 9600 Pro 芯片的手机，而标准版 X500 则配备了 9600M 芯片。这款 2nm 工艺的 Pro 芯片不仅带来了常规的性能提升，还支持录制 4K 240fps 的慢动作视频。此外，新机还支持 10bit Log 录制以及全新的电影级视频风格。

Display sizes range from the compact 6.36-inch Pro to the 6.85-inch Pro Max, with the base X500 in-between. All three phones feature OLED displays, silicon-carbon batteries between 6,510mAh and 8,000mAh in size, and run on Vivo’s OriginOS 7 software. Today’s launch is China-only, but a global launch is coming, though we don’t yet know when.

屏幕尺寸方面，从紧凑的 6.36 英寸 Pro 版到 6.85 英寸的 Pro Max 版不等，标准版 X500 则介于两者之间。这三款手机均配备 OLED 显示屏，搭载容量在 6,510mAh 到 8,000mAh 之间的硅碳负极电池，并运行 Vivo 的 OriginOS 7 系统。目前该系列仅在中国发布，全球发布计划虽已确定，但具体时间尚未公布。
