# Bambu P2S Combo 3D Printer Review: My Own Little Plastics Factory
# 拓竹 P2S Combo 3D 打印机评测：我私人的小型塑料工厂

**Rating: 9/10**
**评分：9/10**

**THE GOOD:** Easy for anyone to use. Consistently perfect prints. Enclosed chamber for temperature control. New tool-free nozzle-swap feature. AMS add-on makes multicolor printing a breeze.
**优点：** 任何人都能轻松上手。打印效果始终完美。配备用于温度控制的封闭式机箱。全新的免工具喷嘴更换功能。AMS 配件让多色打印变得轻而易举。

**THE BAD:** Speed causes a lot of shaking—needs a very stable surface to sit on. Filament waste disposal makes a mess. Fumes from high-temperature filaments are still very intense.
**缺点：** 打印速度快导致震动明显，需要放置在非常稳固的表面上。耗材废料处理比较麻烦。高温打印时的气味依然非常刺鼻。

Does the average consumer actually need a 3D printer? Isn’t it just for people who want to sell fidgets at the flea market, design obscure brackets in CAD, or print groups of Warhammer figurines? Yes, they do, and no, it isn’t.
普通消费者真的需要 3D 打印机吗？它难道不是只适合那些想在跳蚤市场上卖指尖陀螺、在 CAD 中设计冷门支架，或者打印一堆战锤模型的人吗？答案是：他们确实需要，而且它的用途远不止于此。

In my four weeks of testing the Bambu Lab P2S Combo, I have printed a sponge holder for my kitchen sink; caps and parts for my hydroponic gardens; various clips and hangers; a Mason jar lid organizer; a shovel; a couple of funny signs; plant pot coasters; a shoehorn; truck bed tie-downs; a pair of earrings; and, of course, a Benchy—every piece perfectly smooth and looking like it was purchased from a store. Owning a 3D printer is like having an Amazon fulfillment center right in your house.
在我测试拓竹（Bambu Lab）P2S Combo 的四周里，我打印了厨房水槽的海绵架、水培花园的盖子和零件、各种夹子和挂钩、梅森罐盖收纳架、一把铲子、几个有趣的标牌、花盆垫、鞋拔子、卡车货箱固定扣、一对耳环，当然还有一只 Benchy（测试船）——每一件作品都极其光滑，看起来就像从商店买来的一样。拥有一台 3D 打印机，就像在家里拥有了一个亚马逊物流中心。

While I'm no stranger to 3D printing—I owned the open-frame Bambu Lab A1 before I started testing 3D printers—I have never used a machine as easy to operate as the P2S. In fact, when we reviewed Bambu Lab’s P2S predecessor, the popular and bestselling P1S, earlier this year, tester Brad Bourque declared it the easiest-to-use 3D printer he’d ever tried, claiming its lack of complicated steps and futzing has changed the way he thinks about 3D printing.
虽然我对 3D 打印并不陌生——在开始评测 3D 打印机之前，我就拥有一台开放式结构的拓竹 A1——但我从未用过像 P2S 这样易于操作的机器。事实上，当我们今年早些时候评测拓竹 P2S 的前身、那款广受欢迎且畅销的 P1S 时，评测员 Brad Bourque 称其为他用过最易用的 3D 打印机，并表示它省去了复杂的步骤和调试，彻底改变了他对 3D 打印的看法。

The P2S builds on the already excellent P1S design with a new 5-inch color touchscreen, tool-free nozzle swapping, a beefed-up 1080p 30-frames-per-second camera with improved AI failure detection, and a redesigned airflow system. I wasn’t exactly in love with the latter, but one complaint for a system with thousands of failure points is more than enough to earn the P2S a solid A-grade.
P2S 在 P1S 本已出色的设计基础上进行了升级，配备了全新的 5 英寸彩色触摸屏、免工具喷嘴更换功能、增强型 1080p 30fps 摄像头（具备改进的 AI 故障检测功能）以及重新设计的风道系统。虽然我对后者并不完全满意，但对于一个拥有数千个潜在故障点的系统来说，只有一个槽点完全足以让 P2S 获得优异的评价。

### It Really Is That Easy
### 它真的就是这么简单

If you own another brand of mid- or lower-tier 3D printer, any Bambu Lab machine will already feel like an upgrade. Much like an iPhone, any Bambu Lab printer will work seamlessly right out of the box, with easy-to-use software and intuitive design elements. With a little assembly, you’re ready to go.
如果你拥有其他品牌的中低端 3D 打印机，那么任何一台拓竹打印机都会让你感到明显的升级。就像 iPhone 一样，任何拓竹打印机开箱即用，配合易用的软件和直观的设计元素，只需简单的组装，你就可以立即开始打印。

The large touchscreen allows you to perform many functions and troubleshooting right on the machine. These printers are easy for even the most clueless newbie to use and understand—so much so that some call the company “the Apple of 3D printing.” (Even better, the company’s free, flagship Bambu Studio software is open source.)
大尺寸触摸屏让你可以在机器上直接执行许多功能和故障排除。这些打印机即使是对 3D 打印一窍不通的新手来说也简单易懂——以至于有人称该公司为“3D 打印界的苹果”。（更棒的是，该公司免费的旗舰软件 Bambu Studio 是开源的。）

What makes both the P1S and P2S worth the upgrade from the A1 is the enclosed chamber that provides environmental control for acrylonitrile filaments like ASA and ABS, which require high temperatures to melt and are notoriously prone to warping. In the P2S, Bambu Lab incorporates a new airflow system that switches modes depending on the filament—it pulls in cooler air for more easily melted PLA, or retains air (and therefore heat) for ASA and ABS.
P1S 和 P2S 相比 A1 更值得升级的地方在于其封闭式机箱，它为 ASA 和 ABS 等丙烯腈类耗材提供了环境控制。这类耗材需要高温熔化，且以容易翘曲而闻名。在 P2S 中，拓竹引入了一套新的风道系统，可根据耗材类型切换模式——打印易熔的 PLA 时引入冷空气，而打印 ASA 和 ABS 时则保留空气（从而保持热量）。

However, I did notice that when printing in ASA (note that the P2S comes with only a rough-textured build plate; I had to buy the smooth $30 engineering plate separately), the resulting fumes were overwhelming—I had to close the door, open the windows, and turn on an air purifier. There is a carbon filter in the back of the device, but during high-heat prints, the airflow is supposed to circulate inside the unit; instead, it seemed to leak out around the door and other cracks.
然而，我注意到在打印 ASA 时（注意 P2S 仅附带粗糙纹理的打印板；我不得不额外购买了 30 美元的平滑工程板），产生的气味非常刺鼻——我不得不关上房门、打开窗户并开启空气净化器。设备背面虽然有一个碳过滤器，但在高温打印时，气流本应在机箱内循环，但实际上似乎从门缝和其他裂缝中泄漏了出来。

Fumes are emitted from all 3D printers, so this is not really a flaw on the company’s part, just an indication that the redesigned airflow system has room for improvement. Make sure you keep the printer in a room with a door that can close if you’re printing with any kind of impact-modified plastics. Bambu Lab’s helpful Wiki also offers a 3D-printable exhaust kit that can redirect fumes outside.
所有 3D 打印机都会产生气味，所以这并非公司的过错，只是说明重新设计的风道系统还有改进空间。如果你要打印任何抗冲击改性塑料，请务必将打印机放在有门的房间里。拓竹实用的 Wiki 页面还提供了一个可 3D 打印的排气套件，可以将废气引导至室外。

And speaking of locations, you will also want to ensure the 15 x 16 x 19-inch P2S is on a very firm surface—perhaps even on the ground. At 600 mm per second, the nozzle moves vigorously, which will shake whatever it’s on. I was using a low wooden table, but since I found myself using the printer near-daily, the shaking eventually loosened the table’s leg joints, resulting in multiple X-Y axis error messages on the machine. Research showed this to be a somewhat common issue, almost always resolved by finding a firmer surface.
说到放置位置，你还需要确保这台 15 x 16 x 19 英寸的 P2S 放置在非常坚固的表面上——甚至最好放在地面上。在 600 毫米/秒的速度下，喷嘴移动非常剧烈，这会震动它所处的任何物体。我当时用的是一张矮木桌，但由于我几乎每天都在使用打印机，震动最终导致桌腿连接处松动，导致机器多次出现 X-Y 轴错误提示。研究表明这是一个比较常见的问题，通常只要换个更稳固的表面就能解决。

I was not able to test the AI failure detection, as none of my 30-plus prints failed (aside from a couple of mismeasurements on my end), but I did have occasion to view through the 1080p camera—if you download the free Bambu Handy app, it will store a little time-lapse video of your object being printed, which is fascinating to watch.
我没能测试 AI 故障检测功能，因为我打印的 30 多个作品中没有一个失败（除了我自己测量失误的几次），但我确实有机会通过 1080p 摄像头进行观察——如果你下载免费的 Bambu Handy 应用程序，它会保存一段打印过程的延时视频，看起来非常迷人。

### In Living Color
### 鲜活色彩

The “Combo” in the name refers to the Automatic Material System (AMS) that sits on top of the printer. (You can also buy it separately at $299.) It holds four spools of filament that auto-feed to your project—a game changer if you’re printing something with multiple colors. Without it, a multicolor print would necessitate stopping the print, removing the spool of filament, and installing a new one on the side. For the P2S, the AMS also has the ability to dry the filament—this removes any absorbed ambient moisture that would create steam when heated and mess up your print.
名称中的“Combo”指的是放置在打印机顶部的自动材料系统（AMS）。（你也可以花 299 美元单独购买。）它可以容纳四卷耗材并自动送入打印机——如果你要打印多色作品，这简直是颠覆性的体验。如果没有它，多色打印就需要暂停打印、取下旧耗材卷并安装新耗材。对于 P2S 来说，AMS 还具备干燥耗材的功能——这可以去除耗材吸收的环境水分，防止加热时产生蒸汽从而破坏打印效果。
