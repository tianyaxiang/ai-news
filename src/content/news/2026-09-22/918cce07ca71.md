# When AI Reviews Train AI Reviewers: Scientific-Judgment Collapse and Mitigation
# 当 AI 评审训练 AI 评审员：科学判断的崩塌与缓解

**Abstract:** Large language models (LLMs) increasingly participate in scientific evaluation, both as automated reviewers and as assistants to human reviewers. As model-generated reviews enter public data and future training corpora, AI peer review can become recursive: later reviewers learn from judgments produced by earlier models. 

**摘要：** 大型语言模型（LLMs）正越来越多地参与科学评估，既作为自动化评审员，也作为人类评审员的助手。随着模型生成的评审意见进入公共数据和未来的训练语料库，AI 同行评审可能会变得递归：后续的评审员会从早期模型产生的判断中进行学习。

We study one step of this feedback loop in a controlled setting. Starting from Llama 3.1 8B, we first fine-tune a reviewer on official ICLR reviews from 2018--2023 and then train four successor models on ICLR 2024 data with systematically varied mixtures of official and model-generated reviews. 

我们在受控环境下研究了这一反馈循环的一个环节。以 Llama 3.1 8B 为起点，我们首先在 2018 年至 2023 年的 ICLR 官方评审意见上微调了一个评审员模型，随后在 ICLR 2024 的数据上训练了四个后续模型，并系统地改变了官方评审与模型生成评审的混合比例。

Our study shows that introducing synthetic reviews compresses rating distributions and reduces both same-paper and corpus-level semantic diversity. We call this pattern $\textbf{scientific-judgment collapse}$. 

研究表明，引入合成评审会压缩评分分布，并降低针对同一篇论文的评审以及整个语料库层面的语义多样性。我们将这种模式称为**“科学判断崩塌”（scientific-judgment collapse）**。

To mitigate this failure mode, we introduce $\textbf{TrustReviewer}$, an open-source LLM-based system for generating peer reviews of AI and machine learning papers. TrustReviewer intervenes at two complementary stages. For training-time prevention, we train the core reviewer in a single stage on a curated corpus designed to reduce low-quality and semantically degenerate supervision. 

为了缓解这种失效模式，我们推出了 **TrustReviewer**，这是一个基于开源 LLM 的系统，用于生成 AI 和机器学习论文的同行评审。TrustReviewer 在两个互补的阶段进行干预。在训练阶段的预防方面，我们在一个经过精心筛选的语料库上对核心评审员进行单阶段训练，旨在减少低质量和语义退化的监督信息。

For test-time correction, paired activation steering aims to further mitigate residual tendencies toward collapsed judgments without further training or additional expert annotation. Together, these results characterize a concrete risk of recursive reviewer training and provide practical interventions for preserving judgment diversity and improving recommendation alignment in AI-assisted scientific evaluation.

在测试阶段的修正方面，配对激活引导（paired activation steering）旨在进一步缓解残余的判断崩塌倾向，且无需额外的训练或专家标注。总之，这些结果揭示了递归评审员训练的具体风险，并为在 AI 辅助的科学评估中保持判断多样性和提高推荐一致性提供了实用的干预手段。
