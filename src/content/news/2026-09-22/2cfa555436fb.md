# Sparse Priors for Efficient Distribution Learning
# 用于高效分布学习的稀疏先验

**Abstract:** Despite the widespread use and success of generative AI techniques today, theoretical guarantees on learning a distribution supported in $d$ dimensions from $n$ samples degrade as $O(n^{-1/\Theta(d)})$, though shown to be minimax optimal. We hypothesize that present bounds are too pessimistic because smoothness assumptions are not enough to capture the structure of distributions that often appear in real applications.

**摘要：** 尽管生成式人工智能技术在当今得到了广泛应用并取得了成功，但从 $n$ 个样本中学习 $d$ 维分布的理论保证会以 $O(n^{-1/\Theta(d)})$ 的速度衰减，尽管这已被证明是极小极大最优的。我们假设目前的界限过于悲观，因为平滑性假设不足以捕捉现实应用中经常出现的分布结构。

Consequently, we introduce the class of sparse priors and define the "Sparse Dimension" as a measure of sparsity of a prior over the space of all distributions. We show that distribution learning under a $k$-sparse prior achieves a Bayesian risk lower bound of $\Omega(\sqrt{k/n})$ under common distance metrics, and show a matching (up to logarithmic terms asymptotically in $n,k$) upper bound for the TV distance under mild additional assumptions.

因此，我们引入了稀疏先验类，并将“稀疏维度”（Sparse Dimension）定义为所有分布空间上先验稀疏性的一种度量。我们证明了在 $k$-稀疏先验下的分布学习在常用距离度量下可达到 $\Omega(\sqrt{k/n})$ 的贝叶斯风险下界，并在温和的附加假设下，证明了总变差距离（TV distance）的匹配上界（在 $n, k$ 的渐近意义上，相差对数项）。

We show the statistical equivalence of distribution learning and learning to sample in the Bayesian setting so that our results apply to learning to sample as well. While $k$ can still depend on the dimension $d$, or a notion of intrinsic dimension, our results show that learning under an appropriate prior overcomes the curse of dimensionality with respect to the dependence on $n$.

我们证明了在贝叶斯框架下，分布学习与采样学习在统计上是等价的，因此我们的结果同样适用于采样学习。虽然 $k$ 仍然可能取决于维度 $d$ 或某种内在维度的概念，但我们的结果表明，在适当的先验下进行学习，可以克服关于 $n$ 依赖性的维度灾难。
