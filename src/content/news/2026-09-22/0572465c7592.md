# MAGIC: Marginal-Guided Compression with Optimal Transport for Efficient Visual Document Retrieval
# MAGIC：用于高效视觉文档检索的边缘引导最优传输压缩方法

**Abstract:** Recent visual document retrieval (VDR) systems such as ColPali use multi-vector page embeddings, in which patch-level vectors enable fine-grained evidence matching but incur substantial index storage and MaxSim scoring overhead. 
**摘要：** 近期的视觉文档检索（VDR）系统（如 ColPali）使用多向量页面嵌入。在这种机制中，补丁级（patch-level）向量虽然能够实现细粒度的证据匹配，但会导致巨大的索引存储开销和 MaxSim 评分负担。

Post-hoc merging offers a practical route to efficient VDR by reducing this cost without retraining the retriever, but its uniform reconstruction objectives are poorly aligned with the sparse, non-uniform patch usage induced by late-interaction retrieval. 
事后合并（Post-hoc merging）提供了一种实现高效 VDR 的实用途径，它无需重新训练检索器即可降低成本。然而，其统一的重构目标与延迟交互检索（late-interaction retrieval）所导致的稀疏、非均匀的补丁使用模式并不匹配。

Under aggressive compression, this misalignment can preserve rarely used patches while concentrating retrieval activity on too few retained representatives. 
在激进压缩的情况下，这种不匹配会导致系统保留了很少使用的补丁，同时将检索活动过度集中在极少数保留的代表性向量上。

To address this misalignment, we propose Marginal-Guided Compression with Optimal Transport (MAGIC), a training-free post-hoc compressor for efficient retrieval with frozen multi-vector embeddings. 
为了解决这一不匹配问题，我们提出了边缘引导最优传输压缩（MAGIC）。这是一种无需训练的事后压缩器，旨在利用冻结的多向量嵌入实现高效检索。

MAGIC derives a MaxSim-induced compression surrogate and optimizes it through a two-marginal entropic optimal-transport formulation, where a retrieval-demand source marginal prioritizes high-use patches and a balanced target marginal regularizes retained-facet usage. 
MAGIC 推导出一个由 MaxSim 诱导的压缩代理，并通过双边缘熵最优传输公式对其进行优化。其中，检索需求源边缘（retrieval-demand source marginal）优先考虑高频使用的补丁，而平衡目标边缘（balanced target marginal）则对保留的特征面（retained-facet）使用进行正则化。

Across ViDoRe benchmarks, keep ratios, and retrieval backbones, MAGIC consistently outperforms strong post-hoc compressors, with particularly large gains in the aggressive-compression regime; component ablations verify the complementary effects of its two marginals. 
在 ViDoRe 基准测试、不同保留比例以及多种检索骨干网络上，MAGIC 始终优于现有的强力事后压缩器，特别是在激进压缩场景下增益显著；组件消融实验验证了其两个边缘机制的互补效应。
