# Your Website May Rank and Still Lose Traffic: A Practical AI-Search SEO Checklist
# 你的网站即便拥有排名也可能流失流量：一份实用的 AI 搜索 SEO 清单

Ranking on Google is no longer the same thing as earning a click. Search engines increasingly answer questions directly through AI Overviews, featured snippets, People Also Ask boxes, local results, and other search features. In early 2026, a SparkToro study reported by Search Engine Land estimated that 68.01% of U.S. Google searches ended without a click during the first four months of the year. The comparison needs to be interpreted carefully because different studies use different data panels, but the direction is clear: a search impression does not automatically become a website visit.
在 Google 上获得排名不再等同于获得点击。搜索引擎正越来越多地通过 AI 概览（AI Overviews）、精选摘要、相关问题（People Also Ask）、本地搜索结果及其他搜索功能直接回答用户的问题。2026 年初，Search Engine Land 报道的一项 SparkToro 研究估计，当年头四个月，美国有 68.01% 的 Google 搜索以“零点击”告终。虽然这项对比需要谨慎解读，因为不同研究使用的数据面板不同，但趋势很明确：搜索展示量并不会自动转化为网站访问量。

For small websites, this does not mean that SEO is dead. It means the goal is becoming broader. A useful page should be easy to discover, easy to understand, worthy of being cited, and valuable enough that a searcher wants to continue reading after seeing the short answer.
对于小型网站而言，这并不意味着 SEO 已死，而是意味着 SEO 的目标正在变得更加广泛。一个有用的页面应当易于发现、易于理解、值得被引用，并且具备足够的价值，让搜索者在看到简短的回答后仍有兴趣继续阅读。

### SEO still matters in AI search
### AI 搜索时代，SEO 依然重要

Google's official guidance says that SEO remains relevant for generative search features because AI Overviews and AI Mode are grounded in Google's core Search ranking and quality systems. Google recommends the same fundamentals that have always helped users and crawlers: valuable original content, clear organization, crawlability, good page experience, and accurate technical implementation. This is important because there is no reliable shortcut called “GEO magic.” Google specifically says that site owners do not need special AI-only markup or an llms.txt file to appear in Google Search. The practical approach is still to build a website that people can use and trust.
Google 的官方指南指出，SEO 对于生成式搜索功能依然有效，因为 AI 概览和 AI 模式均基于 Google 核心的搜索排名和质量系统。Google 建议遵循那些一贯有助于用户和爬虫的基础原则：有价值的原创内容、清晰的组织结构、良好的可抓取性、优质的页面体验以及准确的技术实现。这一点至关重要，因为不存在所谓的“GEO（生成式引擎优化）魔法”捷径。Google 明确表示，网站所有者无需特殊的 AI 专用标记或 llms.txt 文件即可出现在 Google 搜索中。最务实的方法依然是建立一个人们能够使用并信任的网站。

The question is therefore not only, “How do I rank for this keyword?” A better question is, “If an AI system or search feature reads my page, will it find a clear, specific, well-supported answer that represents my experience?”
因此，问题不仅在于“我如何为这个关键词获得排名？”，更好的问题是：“如果 AI 系统或搜索功能读取了我的页面，它能否找到一个清晰、具体、有据可查且代表我个人经验的答案？”

### The four layers of visibility
### 可见性的四个层面

A small website can think about search visibility in four layers:
小型网站可以从四个层面来思考搜索可见性：

| Layer | What it means | Example signal |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Discovery** | Search engines can find and crawl the page | Internal links, accessible HTML, no accidental noindex |
| **Understanding** | The page structure and topic are clear | Descriptive title, headings, semantic sections |
| **Trust** | The page has a reason to be believed | First-hand experience, examples, sources, author context |
| **Action** | The visitor has a useful next step | A checklist, template, comparison, or clear navigation |

| 层面 | 含义 | 示例信号 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **发现 (Discovery)** | 搜索引擎能够找到并抓取页面 | 内部链接、可访问的 HTML、无意外的 noindex 标签 |
| **理解 (Understanding)** | 页面结构和主题清晰 | 描述性标题、标题标签、语义化区块 |
| **信任 (Trust)** | 页面有被信任的理由 | 第一手经验、案例、来源、作者背景 |
| **行动 (Action)** | 访客有明确的后续步骤 | 清单、模板、对比表或清晰的导航 |

Many sites focus almost entirely on discovery. They publish a page, add keywords, and wait. In AI-assisted search, understanding and trust are just as important because systems need to identify which parts of a page answer a question and whether the source appears useful and reliable.
许多网站几乎只关注“发现”层面。他们发布页面、添加关键词，然后等待。在 AI 辅助搜索中，“理解”和“信任”同样重要，因为系统需要识别页面的哪些部分回答了问题，以及该来源是否看起来有用且可靠。

### A practical checklist for small websites
### 小型网站的实用清单

**1. Answer the main question immediately**
**1. 立即回答核心问题**

Do not hide the answer behind a long introduction. Start with a direct response, then explain the reasoning. For example, a page targeting “how to reduce image size in Blogger” could begin like this: To reduce image size in Blogger, resize the image before uploading it, use an appropriately sized WebP or JPEG file, add descriptive alternative text, and avoid uploading a much larger image than the displayed container requires. The rest of the article can explain tools, trade-offs, and a step-by-step workflow. The first paragraph should make the page's purpose obvious to both the reader and the crawler.
不要把答案藏在冗长的引言之后。从直接的回答开始，然后再解释原因。例如，针对“如何在 Blogger 中减小图片大小”的页面可以这样开头：要在 Blogger 中减小图片大小，请在上传前调整图片尺寸，使用大小合适的 WebP 或 JPEG 文件，添加描述性的替代文本（Alt Text），并避免上传远大于显示容器所需的图片。文章的其余部分可以解释工具、权衡方案和具体的操作流程。第一段话应当让读者和爬虫都能一眼看出页面的目的。

**2. Use headings that match real questions**
**2. 使用符合真实问题的标题**

Organize the page around the questions a real visitor asks. Instead of repeating a keyword unnaturally, use headings such as: What image size is suitable for a Blogger post? Should I use WebP or JPEG? How can I check whether the image slows down the page? What should I do if the image looks blurry on mobile? Question-based headings improve scanning and create a logical structure. They also make it easier to write a complete answer instead of a page that only targets one phrase.
围绕真实访客会问的问题来组织页面。不要不自然地重复关键词，而应使用诸如“Blogger 文章适合多大的图片？”、“我应该使用 WebP 还是 JPEG？”、“如何检查图片是否拖慢了页面速度？”、“如果图片在手机上看起来模糊怎么办？”这类标题。基于问题的标题可以改善阅读体验并建立逻辑结构，同时也更容易写出完整的答案，而不是仅仅针对某一个短语的页面。

**3. Add evidence that a generic article cannot copy**
**3. 添加通用文章无法复制的证据**

Generic advice is easy to reproduce. First-hand evidence is harder to replace. A Blogger tutorial becomes more useful when it includes a real theme, a before-and-after screenshot, the exact CSS rule that caused a layout problem, or a short explanation of what failed during testing. If you have changed a template, describe the decision and its result. If a recommendation depends on a limitation, say so. This does not require pretending to be an expert in everything. It requires being precise about what you actually tested and separating personal experience from general guidance.
通用的建议很容易被复制，但第一手证据却难以替代。当一篇 Blogger 教程包含真实的主题、修改前后的截图、导致布局问题的具体 CSS 规则，或对测试中失败原因的简短解释时，它会变得更有用。如果你修改过模板，请描述你的决策及其结果。如果某项建议取决于某种限制，请说明这一点。这并不要求你假装成全能专家，而是要求你对自己实际测试过的内容保持精确，并将个人经验与一般性指导区分开来。

**4. Make the technical structure boring and reliable**
**4. 让技术结构保持简单可靠**

For small sites, technical SEO should remove obstacles rather than become a collection of hacks. Check that important pages can be crawled and indexed, that mobile layouts work, that titles and descriptions describe the actual page, and that internal links connect related articles. Use semantic HTML when it improves readability and accessibility. Keep the main content distinguishable from navigation and decorative elements. If JavaScript is used, make sure essential content and links are not hidden from users or crawlers unnecessarily. On Blogger, this means being careful when editing the theme. Make a backup before changing the template, test the result on a narrow screen, and avoid adding scripts simply because another site uses them. A lightweight page with clear content is usually a better foundation than a visually impressive page that is slow or difficult to navigate.
对于小型网站，技术 SEO 应当是消除障碍，而不是堆砌各种“黑科技”。检查重要页面是否可被抓取和索引、移动端布局是否正常、标题和描述是否准确反映页面内容、内部链接是否连接了相关文章。在能提升可读性和可访问性的地方使用语义化 HTML。确保主要内容与导航及装饰性元素区分开来。如果使用了 JavaScript，请确保核心内容和链接没有被不必要地隐藏。在 Blogger 上，这意味着编辑主题时要格外小心。修改模板前请备份，在窄屏设备上测试结果，并避免仅仅因为其他网站用了某个脚本就盲目添加。一个内容清晰的轻量级页面，通常比一个视觉华丽但加载缓慢或难以导航的页面基础更好。

**5. Design for the next action, not only the impression**
**5. 为后续行动而设计，而不仅仅是为了展示**

A zero-click impression is not always a failure. Someone may see your brand or explanation in a search feature and remember it later. Still, a page should give interested readers a reason to continue. Add something that the short answer cannot provide: a worked example, a downloadable checklist, a comparison table, a troubleshooting section, or a small tool. The goal is not to withhold basic information. The goal is to make the full page genuinely more useful than a one-sentence answer.
“零点击”展示并不总是失败。有人可能会在搜索功能中看到你的品牌或解释，并在以后记住它。尽管如此，页面仍应给感兴趣的读者一个继续阅读的理由。添加一些简短回答无法提供的内容：实操案例、可下载的清单、对比表、故障排除部分或小工具。目标不是隐瞒基础信息，而是让整个页面真正比一句简单的回答更有价值。
