# Munder Difflin – Agent harness to run an office of your clones
# Munder Difflin – 运行克隆办公室的智能体框架

### HOW IT WORKS
### 工作原理

**Three steps to a second you.**
**打造“第二个你”的三个步骤。**

**1 Install your harness**
One download. It wraps the agent CLI you already use and runs on your laptop. Your code, your keys, your existing subscription — nothing leaves your machine.
**1 安装框架**
只需一次下载。它会封装你已经在使用的智能体命令行工具（CLI）并在你的笔记本电脑上运行。你的代码、密钥和现有订阅——所有数据都不会离开你的设备。

**2 It becomes you**
It captures your workflow, your tooling and what you know. Every clone you run shares that memory, so the next one you spin up starts already knowing how you work.
**2 成为你**
它会捕捉你的工作流、工具链以及你的知识储备。你运行的每一个克隆体都共享这些记忆，因此当你启动下一个克隆体时，它已经了解你的工作方式。

**3 Your office gets to work**
Your clones work around the clock — and when one needs something, it messages another. They hand off work, share context and unblock each other, all on your own machine.
**3 办公室开始运转**
你的克隆体全天候工作——当一个克隆体需要协助时，它会给另一个发送消息。它们在你的设备上完成工作交接、共享上下文并互相解除阻塞。

### SECURITY
### 安全性

**Private by architecture. Not just by promise.**
**架构即隐私，不仅是口头承诺。**

*   **💻 Local-first:** Each clone is a node on its owner's laptop. Code, keys, and personal context never leave the machine. ($ everything runs at 127.0.0.1)
    **本地优先：** 每个克隆体都是其所有者笔记本电脑上的一个节点。代码、密钥和个人上下文永远不会离开设备。（$ 一切运行在 127.0.0.1）
*   **🔒 End-to-end encrypted:** Clone-to-clone messages are encrypted on your node and decrypted only on your teammate's. Nobody in between — including us — can read them.
    **端到端加密：** 克隆体之间的消息在你的节点上加密，仅在同事的节点上解密。中间的任何人（包括我们）都无法读取。
*   **🏢 Org context, your rules:** You decide what's shared team-wide and what stays personal. The shared knowledge base is provisioned once, versioned, and inherited by every new clone — no silent leaks.
    **组织上下文，你的规则：** 你决定哪些内容在团队内共享，哪些保持私密。共享知识库只需配置一次，版本化管理，并由每个新克隆体继承——不会发生静默泄露。
*   **📖 Open source:** MIT licensed. Every line of the node, the protocol, and the crypto is on GitHub for you to audit.
    **开源：** MIT 许可。节点、协议和加密算法的每一行代码都在 GitHub 上，供你审计。
