# Amazon just hiked the prices for Echo, Fire TV, and Kindle products by up to 60 percent
# 亚马逊将 Echo、Fire TV 和 Kindle 产品的价格上调了最高 60%

Citing “significant increases in memory and storage component costs,” Amazon has raised prices on Echos, Kindles, Fire TVs, and Eeros by up to 60 percent, as first reported by Fortune.
据《财富》杂志率先报道，亚马逊以“内存和存储组件成本大幅上涨”为由，将其 Echo、Kindle、Fire TV 和 Eero 产品的价格上调了最高 60%。

The cheapest products in Amazon’s lineup were hit hardest, with the Echo Dot smart speaker jumping from $49.99 to $79.99. The Fire TV Stick 4K Max also rose by over 40 percent to $84.99, and the base level Kindle now costs $149.99, a nearly 37 percent increase.
亚马逊产品线中价格最低的产品受到的冲击最大，其中 Echo Dot 智能音箱的价格从 49.99 美元跃升至 79.99 美元。Fire TV Stick 4K Max 的价格也上涨了超过 40%，达到 84.99 美元；入门级 Kindle 现在售价为 149.99 美元，涨幅近 37%。

In a statement to The Verge, Kristy Schmidt, senior communications manager of Amazon’s Devices & Services division, said, “The consumer electronics industry is facing significant increases in memory and storage component costs. After absorbing these increases for as long as we could, we recently adjusted pricing across our product lines. Our approach has always been to offer great products at accessible prices, and that commitment hasn’t changed.”
亚马逊设备与服务部门高级公关经理 Kristy Schmidt 在给《The Verge》的一份声明中表示：“消费电子行业正面临内存和存储组件成本的大幅上涨。在尽可能长时间地消化这些成本增长后，我们最近调整了各产品线的定价。我们的方针始终是提供价格亲民的优质产品，这一承诺从未改变。”

The Echo Dot Max is Amazon’s biggest little speaker yet. Amazon’s flagship Echo Dot Max smart speaker, which launched last year, now costs $119.99, up from $99.99. Apple increased the price of its comparable HomePod Mini this summer to $129 from $99.99, also in response to rising costs. The Google Home Speaker remains at $99.99 for now.
Echo Dot Max 是亚马逊迄今为止最大的小型音箱。亚马逊去年推出的旗舰级 Echo Dot Max 智能音箱现在的售价为 119.99 美元，高于之前的 99.99 美元。今年夏天，苹果公司也将同类产品 HomePod Mini 的价格从 99.99 美元上调至 129 美元，同样是为了应对成本上涨。目前，Google Home 音箱的价格仍维持在 99.99 美元。

Amazon hardware products apparently not affected include its line of Ring cameras and video doorbells, and the Echo Studio, its higher-end $219.99 smart speaker.
目前看来，亚马逊未受影响的硬件产品包括其 Ring 系列摄像头和可视门铃，以及售价 219.99 美元的高端智能音箱 Echo Studio。
