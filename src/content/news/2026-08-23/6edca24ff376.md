# Saily Ultra eSIM Premium Plan Review: Packed With Perks
# Saily Ultra eSIM 高级套餐评测：福利满满

**$60 at Saily**
**售价：60 美元（Saily 官网）**

**GOOD:** Good coverage up to 5G in 121 countries. Handy perks like airport lounge and fast-track access. Includes NordVPN and other security tools.
**优点：** 在 121 个国家/地区提供良好的 5G 网络覆盖。包含机场贵宾室和快速安检通道等实用福利。附赠 NordVPN 及其他安全工具。

**TIRED:** Data throttled after 30 GB. Relatively expensive, so must visit multiple countries and use perks to get value.
**缺点：** 30GB 后限速。价格相对较高，因此必须前往多个国家并充分利用各项福利才能体现其价值。

Traveling is stressful enough without worrying about fiddling with SIM cards and setting up data service in foreign countries. The top-tier Saily Ultra eSIM service covers 121 countries, is packed with perks, and aims to keep you connected wherever you roam, but the Premium plan costs $60 per month. This plan is aimed squarely at jet-setters and will likely best suit business travelers or folks embarking on a multicountry tour.
旅行本身已经够让人操心了，没必要再为更换 SIM 卡和在国外设置数据服务而烦恼。Saily Ultra 高端 eSIM 服务覆盖 121 个国家，福利丰富，旨在确保您在漫游时随时保持在线，但其高级套餐每月费用为 60 美元。该套餐明确针对“空中飞人”，最适合商务旅客或进行多国巡游的人士。

An international eSIM service can be set up on your phone before you even leave home, and it will connect you with local networks the moment you land. If you have a phone with dual eSIM support, like the iPhone 16 I tested Saily Ultra with, you can keep your regular number and add a second one for your international trip, or just use the international eSIM for mobile data.
国际 eSIM 服务可以在离家前就设置好，落地后即可自动连接当地网络。如果您使用的是支持双 eSIM 的手机（例如我测试 Saily Ultra 时使用的 iPhone 16），您可以保留常用号码，并添加第二个号码用于国际旅行，或者仅将国际 eSIM 用于移动数据。

### Frequent Flyers
### 频繁出差的旅客

Most of us have been stung by painfully expensive roaming charges at some point. While it’s worth checking what kind of international service options your usual provider offers, a separate international eSIM service can make life easier and keep costs transparent. Saily Ultra comes in three tiers, all covering the same 121 countries and offering the same perks.
我们大多数人都曾被昂贵的漫游费“刺痛”过。虽然查看您常用运营商提供的国际服务选项很有必要，但独立的国际 eSIM 服务可以让生活更轻松，并保持费用透明。Saily Ultra 提供三个等级的套餐，均覆盖相同的 121 个国家，并提供相同的福利。

*   **Standard ($30/month):** 5 GB of high-speed data
*   **Plus ($45/month):** 10 GB of high-speed data
*   **Premium ($60/month):** 30 GB of high-speed data, then unlimited data throttled at up to 1 Mbps
*   **标准版（30 美元/月）：** 5GB 高速数据
*   **进阶版（45 美元/月）：** 10GB 高速数据
*   **高级版（60 美元/月）：** 30GB 高速数据，之后提供最高 1Mbps 的无限限速数据

I tested the Premium plan extensively in the UK and briefly in Europe. It was quick and easy to set up in the Saily app and kept me connected as I roamed, offering decent speeds up to around 300 Mbps on 5G networks in city center locations, inevitably dropping off to 4G and around 15 Mbps in more rural spots. The specific speeds you’ll get depend entirely on the local network. Saily piggybacks on local providers, mainly connecting to EE and Vodafone during my month of testing.
我在英国进行了广泛测试，并在欧洲进行了简短测试。在 Saily 应用中设置非常快捷简单，漫游时连接稳定。在市中心的 5G 网络下，速度可达 300 Mbps 左右；在偏远地区则会降至 4G，速度约为 15 Mbps。具体速度完全取决于当地网络。Saily 依托于当地运营商，在我一个月的测试中，主要连接的是 EE 和 Vodafone。

The Ultra plan includes 30 GB of high-speed data. In this case, high-speed simply means unfettered; the actual speed you get depends on coverage where you are. After that 30 GB is gone, you get unlimited throttled data at up to 1 Mbps. In practice, I found this lower speed was enough for messaging, web scrolling, and navigation, but anything data-hungry, like video, is out.
Ultra 套餐包含 30GB 高速数据。这里的高速仅指“无限制”，实际速度取决于您所在位置的覆盖情况。30GB 用完后，您将获得最高 1Mbps 的无限限速数据。在实际使用中，我发现这个速度足以进行即时通讯、网页浏览和导航，但对于视频等高流量需求的应用则无能为力。

It’s worth noting that the 30 GB limit is for the month. Most folks use around 20 GB of data in a month, so 30 GB sounds like a lot. But consider that you are probably connected to Wi-Fi most of the time at home or work, and you may not be when traveling. That 30 GB can disappear surprisingly quickly, and you should take advantage of Wi-Fi whenever you can on your travels. One of my top travel tips is to download videos and maps before you go, or when you’re connected to Wi-Fi.
值得注意的是，30GB 的限制是按月计算的。大多数人每月使用约 20GB 数据，所以 30GB 听起来很多。但请考虑到，您在家或工作中大部分时间都连接 Wi-Fi，而旅行时可能并非如此。这 30GB 可能会消耗得非常快，因此在旅行中应尽可能利用 Wi-Fi。我的一条旅行建议是：在出发前或连接 Wi-Fi 时，提前下载好视频和地图。

If you’re only visiting one or two countries, it’s probably better to get a pay-as-you-go plan for that specific region or country. For example, Saily offers unlimited data in the UK for 15 days for $49, but there’s a fair usage policy that will throttle you after 5 GB in a single 24-hour period (until the next day). Unlimited data across Europe (35 countries) for a month will cost you $72, with the same 5 GB daily limit. You could also just buy 20 GB of data to use over 30 days for $23 in France or $31 in the UK.
如果您只去一两个国家，购买针对特定地区或国家的按需付费套餐可能更划算。例如，Saily 提供英国 15 天无限数据套餐，售价 49 美元，但有公平使用政策，即 24 小时内使用超过 5GB 后会限速（直到次日）。欧洲（35 国）一个月无限数据套餐售价 72 美元，同样有每日 5GB 的限制。您也可以选择在法国购买 20GB（30 天有效，23 美元）或在英国购买（31 美元）。

Having used a few different international eSIM and regular SIM services for travel over the years, Saily compares favorably on performance and ease of use but is relatively expensive. That said, most other eSIM services don’t come with a bundle of extras.
多年来，我使用过多种国际 eSIM 和普通 SIM 卡服务，Saily 在性能和易用性方面表现出色，但价格相对较高。话虽如此，大多数其他 eSIM 服务并不包含这些额外的福利包。

### Plenty of Perks
### 福利多多

Saily Ultra will be a hard sell for most folks simply looking at the data, but frequent travelers will appreciate the plentiful perks. Here’s what you get:
如果仅从数据流量来看，Saily Ultra 对大多数人来说吸引力有限，但对于频繁旅行的人来说，这些丰富的福利非常实用。以下是您将获得的权益：

*   **1x Airport Lounge Pass per month:** Airports can be noisy and uncomfortable, but airport lounges offer quieter spaces where you can work or relax, with fast Wi-Fi, comfier seating, and complimentary food and drink. If you’re stuck in an airport for a few hours, a lounge pass can make your wait as painless as possible. With Saily Ultra, you get one free pass per month for one person. It generates a QR code in the app that’s valid for 30 minutes. You also get a free lounge pass for that trip if your flight is delayed for more than two hours (contact support in the app).
*   **每月 1 次机场贵宾室通行证：** 机场往往嘈杂且不便，但贵宾室提供更安静的空间供您工作或放松，并配有高速 Wi-Fi、舒适座椅以及免费餐饮。如果您在机场滞留数小时，贵宾室通行证能让等待过程尽可能轻松。Saily Ultra 每月提供一张免费通行证（限一人）。应用内会生成一个 30 分钟有效的二维码。如果航班延误超过两小时，您还可以获得该行程的额外免费通行证（需在应用内联系客服）。

*   **1x Fast-Track Pass per month:** You can skip the long security queue with fast-track and get straight through into the terminal. With Saily Ultra, you get one free fast-track pass for one person, generated as a QR code in the app. Availability is far more limited than with the lounge pass, so I was unable to test this.
*   **每月 1 次快速安检通道通行证：** 您可以通过快速通道跳过漫长的安检排队，直接进入候机楼。Saily Ultra 每月提供一张免费快速通道通行证（限一人），同样在应用内生成二维码。由于其覆盖范围远少于贵宾室，我未能测试此功能。

*   **Online Security Tools:** You get access to NordVPN, NordPass, NordLocker, and Incogni with an activation code in the Saily app. Saily’s parent company is Nord Security, so it makes sense that its popular VPN service, password manager, and secure cloud storage are included. Incogni is a privacy tool that can help you remove personal data from the internet.
*   **在线安全工具：** 您可以通过 Saily 应用中的激活码使用 NordVPN、NordPass、NordLocker 和 Incogni。Saily 的母公司是 Nord Security，因此包含其广受欢迎的 VPN 服务、密码管理器和安全云存储也就不足为奇了。Incogni 是一款隐私工具，可以帮助您从互联网上删除个人数据。

Saily lists several other perks on its website, including priority support, an ad blocker, and 8 percent back in Saily credits. The ad blocker is handy and also reduces your data usage, and you can use any credits you accrue to buy data plans or extra perks, like additional lounge passes. It's also worth noting that lounge and fast-track benefits rollover for up to three months if unused, though you can't share them.
Saily 官网还列出了其他几项福利，包括优先客服支持、广告拦截器以及 8% 的 Saily 积分返还。广告拦截器非常实用，还能减少数据消耗；您可以使用累积的积分购买数据套餐或额外福利（如更多的贵宾室通行证）。值得注意的是，未使用的贵宾室和快速通道权益最多可滚动保留三个月，但不可转让。
