# The Best Mattress Toppers I've Tried (2026): Supportive, Plush, Memory Foam
# 我尝试过的最佳床垫罩（2026年版）：支撑性、舒适感与记忆棉

My hunt for the best mattress toppers has taught me how transformative they can be. These toppers can alleviate your aches, sink you into insanely comfortable memory foam, and even pack supportive coils like a mattress. 这些床垫罩可以缓解你的疼痛，让你沉浸在极其舒适的记忆棉中，甚至像床垫一样内置支撑弹簧。我最喜欢的床垫罩种类繁多，从柔软的羊毛层和记忆棉，到坚实的款式，甚至是混合型床垫罩（没错，你确实可以买到带弹簧的床垫罩），总有一款能满足你和你的床垫需求。

### The Best Mattress Topper: Birch Plush Organic Mattress Topper
### 最佳床垫罩：Birch Plush 有机床垫罩

Birch’s plush mattress topper combines cotton, “fiberglass-free fire protection,” and latex for a super comfortable piece that’s soft while still giving some support and pressure relief. The latex base gives a firmness that other mattress toppers can lack, while the cotton and wool layers on top of it give it a plush feel. I used it on the firmest side of the Plank Firm Natural mattress, which usually gives me shoulder stiffness as a side sleeper, but I woke without a hint of stiffness thanks to this topper, and my lower back felt properly supported all night long. I woke up pain-free from my usual pressure points, too, which isn't something I always find on softer toppers.

Birch的这款舒适型床垫罩结合了棉花、“无玻璃纤维防火层”和乳胶，打造出一款既柔软又具备一定支撑力和压力缓解功能的超舒适产品。乳胶底座提供了其他床垫罩可能缺乏的坚实感，而顶部的棉花和羊毛层则带来了柔软的触感。我把它铺在Plank Firm Natural床垫最硬的一面使用，作为侧卧者，我通常会感到肩部僵硬，但多亏了这款床垫罩，我醒来时完全没有僵硬感，下背部整晚都得到了良好的支撑。我醒来时也没有了往常压力点带来的疼痛，这在较软的床垫罩上并不常见。

### Best Memory Foam Topper (and for College Dorms): Brooklyn Bedding CopperFlex 401 Mattress Topper
### 最佳记忆棉床垫罩（及大学生宿舍首选）：Brooklyn Bedding CopperFlex 401 床垫罩

First off, this memory foam topper is 4 inches tall—practically a mattress in and of itself. I can’t think of a bed that this wouldn't convert into something totally luxe, and indeed, Brooklyn Bedding markets this as being an option for dorms due to the transformational and antimicrobial properties. You could probably sleep on the floor with it and be perfectly fine.

首先，这款记忆棉床垫罩厚达4英寸——几乎相当于一张独立的床垫。我想不出有什么床铺在加上它之后不会变得极其奢华。事实上，Brooklyn Bedding正是因为它具备的改造能力和抗菌特性，将其作为宿舍床铺的升级选项进行推广。你甚至可以直接把它铺在地板上睡觉，也会感觉非常舒适。

Part of Brooklyn Bedding's CopperFlex line featuring copper-infused memory foam, this topper draws heat away from the body and provides antimicrobial protection. It's CertiPur-US certified, has GlacioTex cooling covers, and comes in three versions: the 201, which is 2 inches tall; the 301, which is 3 inches tall; and the big-kahuna 401 that I tested and loved.

作为Brooklyn Bedding CopperFlex系列的一部分，这款床垫罩采用了注入铜离子的记忆棉，能够将热量从身体带走并提供抗菌保护。它获得了CertiPur-US认证，配有GlacioTex凉感面料，并有三个版本：2英寸厚的201型、3英寸厚的301型，以及我测试过并非常喜爱的大尺寸401型。

### Best Serene Foam Topper: The Company Store Serene Foam Memory Foam Topper
### 最佳Serene泡沫床垫罩：The Company Store Serene Foam 记忆棉床垫罩

The Company Store markets this topper as memory foam, but it’s actually Serene foam, which uses microscopic air capsules to address the heat retention and firmness problems of memory foam while keeping the same plush, pressure-relieving feel. It accomplishes these goals for me, and I have yet to take this topper off my bed. However, it should be said I’m a proponent of a soft bed, and this thing is capital-S Soft.

The Company Store将这款产品作为记忆棉床垫罩销售，但它实际上是Serene泡沫。这种材料利用微小的空气胶囊解决了传统记忆棉蓄热和过硬的问题，同时保持了同样的舒适感和压力缓解效果。对我来说，它确实达到了这些目标，以至于我至今还没把它从床上撤下来。不过需要说明的是，我个人偏爱软床，而这款产品是“超级”柔软。
