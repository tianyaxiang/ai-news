# Jensen Huang puts Trump on speakerphone onstage to announce robots won’t take over the world
# 黄仁勋在舞台上通过免提电话连线特朗普，宣布机器人不会统治世界

Nvidia CEO Jensen Huang took a call from President Trump on Monday while onstage at the All-In Podcast’s All-In Summit. It’s not the first time Huang has taken a call from the president during work, but this time he put Trump on speakerphone before a big crowd.
英伟达首席执行官黄仁勋周一在“All-In 峰会”的舞台上接听了特朗普总统的电话。这并非黄仁勋第一次在工作期间接听总统的电话，但这一次，他在大批观众面前开启了免提功能。

During the call, the president launched into his take on recent fears about AI development, which he called a “hoax,” and told the crowd that “the robots will not be taking over.”
在通话中，总统发表了他对近期有关人工智能发展担忧的看法，称其为“骗局”，并告诉现场观众：“机器人不会统治世界。”

It’s already a frothy week for AI news. The call with the president followed Anthropic CEO Dario Amodei’s long essay published this weekend titled “We Must Pace the Frontier” where he argued for slowing down AI development and coordination between governments. The essay sparked many responses from AI executives, who generally agreed. But Trump pushed back in Truth Social posts on Monday and repurposed some of what he said in those posts at the event.
本周的人工智能新闻热度高涨。在与总统通话之前，Anthropic 首席执行官达里奥·阿莫代（Dario Amodei）于本周末发表了一篇题为《我们必须放慢前沿步伐》（We Must Pace the Frontier）的长文，主张放缓人工智能发展并加强政府间的协调。这篇文章引发了许多人工智能高管的回应，他们大多表示赞同。但特朗普周一在“真实社交”（Truth Social）平台上发帖进行了反驳，并在活动中重申了他在这些帖子中的部分观点。

Heard onstage, Trump also commented on data centers, claiming there would be communities that would be “dying” without data centers but were now “really wealthy” (though without providing specifics).
在舞台上，特朗普还谈到了数据中心，声称有些社区如果没有数据中心就会“消亡”，但现在却变得“非常富有”（尽管他没有提供具体细节）。

Huang did not push back, saying instead that “We’re going to make sure that everybody wins in the AI race in America. Every industry, every company, every state, every people.” Huang also appealed to Trump by telling him he was speaking to “thousands” of people.
黄仁勋并未反驳，而是表示：“我们将确保每个人都能在美国的人工智能竞赛中获胜。每一个行业、每一家公司、每一个州、每一个人。”黄仁勋还向特朗普示好，告诉他现场有“数千人”在听他讲话。
