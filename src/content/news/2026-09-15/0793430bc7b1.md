# Beyond Argmax: A Mechanistic Study of Semantic Retention in Frozen Foundation-Model Composition for Generalized Few-Shot 3D Segmentation

**超越 Argmax：针对广义少样本 3D 分割中冻结基础模型组合的语义保留机制研究**

---

### Abstract / 摘要

Classical classifier-combination work distinguishes score-level fusion from hard decision-level voting. We revisit this distinction where independently pretrained, frozen foundation models are composed at inference time for generalized few-shot 3D segmentation. We ask: how much useful semantic information is lost when heterogeneous sources are collapsed to a single class before they can interact?

传统的分类器组合研究区分了分数级融合（score-level fusion）与硬决策级投票（hard decision-level voting）。我们重新审视了这一区别，探讨了在广义少样本 3D 分割任务中，如何将独立预训练且冻结的基础模型在推理阶段进行组合。我们提出了一个问题：当异构源在交互前被压缩为单一类别时，会损失多少有用的语义信息？

We answer with a same-input semantic-retention intervention. Dense RegionPLC and sparse cross-view SAM3 evidence, model weights, masks, geometry, vocabularies, and fusion rules are frozen; only the number of semantic alternatives retained before interaction is varied via a matched top-k ladder.

我们通过一种“同输入语义保留干预”方法回答了这个问题。我们将密集 RegionPLC 和稀疏跨视图 SAM3 的证据、模型权重、掩码、几何结构、词汇表以及融合规则全部冻结；仅通过匹配的 top-k 阶梯来改变交互前保留的语义备选项数量。

On 156 held-out ScanNet200 scenes, top-1 reaches 28.47 harmonic-mean (HM) IoU while full distribution fusion reaches 34.87 HM (+6.40, 95% CI [+5.24,+7.64]). The pattern replicates on 50 ScanNet++ scenes: 23.02 vs. 26.50 HM (+3.48, 95% CI [+1.64,+5.93]). The conclusion is robust: full-distribution HM is stable across sparse-source weights 0.3--0.7; alternative operators (max, geometric pooling) also outperform top-1; and a GroundingDINO--SAM2.1 source-replacement diagnostic shows monotonic HM increase from 14.77 to 18.75 with full retention.

在 156 个留出的 ScanNet200 场景中，top-1 的调和平均（HM）IoU 达到 28.47，而全分布融合达到了 34.87 HM（+6.40，95% 置信区间 [+5.24, +7.64]）。这一模式在 50 个 ScanNet++ 场景中得到复现：23.02 对比 26.50 HM（+3.48，95% 置信区间 [+1.64, +5.93]）。结论非常稳健：全分布 HM 在 0.3--0.7 的稀疏源权重范围内保持稳定；替代算子（如 max、几何池化）的表现也优于 top-1；此外，GroundingDINO--SAM2.1 的源替换诊断显示，随着完全保留，HM 从 14.77 单调增加至 18.75。

Calibration diagnostics reveal opposite miscalibration of the two sources, yet correcting calibration does not eliminate the retention advantage. Across datasets and source stacks, most information is recovered by retaining a compact set of plausible alternatives. The contribution is a controlled diagnosis of premature semantic collapse as a repeatable information bottleneck in heterogeneous frozen-model composition.

校准诊断揭示了两个源存在相反的校准偏差，但修正校准并不能消除保留策略带来的优势。在不同的数据集和源堆栈中，通过保留一组紧凑的合理备选项，大部分信息得以恢复。本文的贡献在于对“过早语义坍缩”进行了受控诊断，将其确定为异构冻结模型组合中一个可重复的信息瓶颈。
