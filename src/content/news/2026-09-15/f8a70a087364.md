# DevFest is back / DevFest 回归了

DevFest 2026 is here, and from October 1 – December 31, 2026, you can join the nearly 1 million developers expected to participate to build, secure, and scale in the agentic era. Here’s how to join.
DevFest 2026 现已开启！从 2026 年 10 月 1 日至 12 月 31 日，欢迎加入预计近百万开发者的行列，共同在“智能体时代”（Agentic Era）进行构建、保障安全并实现规模化扩展。以下是参与方式。

DevFest season is officially back. From October 1 through December 31, 2026, nearly a million developers and builders will gather for the world’s largest community-led technology conference. Hosted around the world by Google Developer Groups (GDGs), DevFest helps everyone — from seasoned software engineers to emerging solution builders — gain practical, hands-on experience with Google’s AI technologies.
DevFest 季节正式回归。从 2026 年 10 月 1 日到 12 月 31 日，近百万名开发者和构建者将齐聚这场全球规模最大的社区主导型技术大会。DevFest 由全球各地的谷歌开发者社区（GDG）主办，旨在帮助每一位参与者——从资深软件工程师到新兴的解决方案构建者——获得谷歌 AI 技术的实践经验。

### What to expect at DevFest 2026 / DevFest 2026 有哪些看点

Under this year’s theme: "Build, Secure, Scale: Developers and Builders in the Agentic Era," the 2026 season prioritizes hands-on experimentation at every event. Attendees will engage directly with tools across Google’s stack — including Gemini, Google AI Studio, Google Antigravity, Google Cloud, Firebase, Android, Flutter, Angular, and Web MCP — through live codelabs, workshops, and agent-athons.
今年大会的主题是“构建、保障、扩展：智能体时代的开发者与构建者”。2026 赛季的每一场活动都将优先考虑动手实践。通过现场代码实验室（Codelabs）、研讨会和“智能体马拉松”（Agent-athons），与会者将直接接触谷歌技术栈中的各项工具，包括 Gemini、Google AI Studio、Google Antigravity、Google Cloud、Firebase、Android、Flutter、Angular 和 Web MCP。

The tracks this year reflect three core realities for modern software development:
今年的议程反映了现代软件开发的三个核心现实：

*   **It’s never been easier to build.** New tools allow more people and teams to prototype and deploy faster than ever.
    **构建从未如此简单。** 新工具让更多个人和团队能够比以往更快地进行原型设计和部署。
*   **What you build should be safe and secure.** Speed cannot come at the expense of stability. Building in the agentic era requires rigorous focus on secure deployment, data privacy, and responsible AI guardrails.
    **构建成果必须安全可靠。** 速度不能以牺牲稳定性为代价。在智能体时代进行构建，需要严格关注安全部署、数据隐私和负责任的 AI 防护机制。
*   **You can work with production-ready infrastructure.** Google provides top-of-class models to help builders and developers scale reliably from day one.
    **你可以使用生产就绪的基础设施。** 谷歌提供顶尖的模型，帮助构建者和开发者从第一天起就能实现可靠的规模化扩展。

### How to connect with local communities, globally / 如何与全球本地社区建立联系

DevFest is built by the community, for the community. While backed by Google, each GDG curates its agenda and tailors each event to serve the specific needs and challenges of its local tech ecosystem. GDGs design their DevFests to foster an inclusive, welcoming environment where participants collaborate and uplevel their skills together.
DevFest 由社区构建，并为社区服务。虽然有谷歌的支持，但每个 GDG 都会策划自己的议程，并量身定制活动，以满足其本地技术生态系统的特定需求和挑战。GDG 设计 DevFest 的初衷是营造一个包容、友好的环境，让参与者能够共同协作并提升技能。

### Get involved / 如何参与

With more than 800 events planned across 115 countries, DevFest offers a space to sharpen your skills, test new architectures, and connect with the community.
DevFest 计划在 115 个国家举办超过 800 场活动，为您提供了一个磨练技能、测试新架构并与社区建立联系的平台。

You can find Your Local Event by visiting the DevFest Event Directory and keep up with the conversation by using #DevFest across social channels.
您可以访问 DevFest 活动目录（DevFest Event Directory）查找您身边的活动，并通过在社交媒体上使用 #DevFest 标签来关注相关讨论。
