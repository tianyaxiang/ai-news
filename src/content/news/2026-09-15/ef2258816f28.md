# macOS 27 is available now
# macOS 27 现已发布

macOS 27 Golden Gate is out for Apple Silicon Macs.
macOS 27 Golden Gate 现已面向 Apple Silicon Mac 用户发布。

Apple released macOS 27 Golden Gate on Monday, which brings the new Siri AI assistant, Liquid Glass improvements, improved performance, and more. The update is compatible with all Macs with Apple Silicon, but not Intel Macs — Apple released its final big update for Intel-based Macs last year with macOS Tahoe.
苹果于周一发布了 macOS 27 Golden Gate，带来了全新的 Siri AI 助手、Liquid Glass（液态玻璃）改进、性能提升等功能。该更新兼容所有搭载 Apple Silicon 的 Mac，但不适用于 Intel Mac——苹果去年通过 macOS Tahoe 发布了针对 Intel Mac 的最后一次重大更新。

Siri AI is arguably the biggest feature in macOS 27, and it allows you to ask questions, search through things involving your personal context like photos and emails, and take actions in apps such as Messages and Reminders. There’s a new dedicated Siri app that keeps an archive of your chats for easier reference. However, Siri AI will initially launch as a beta and will only be available in English.
Siri AI 可以说是 macOS 27 中最重要的功能，它允许你提出问题、搜索涉及个人背景的内容（如照片和电子邮件），并在“信息”和“提醒事项”等应用中执行操作。系统新增了一个专门的 Siri 应用，用于保存聊天记录以便查阅。不过，Siri AI 最初将以测试版形式推出，且仅支持英语。

As for Liquid Glass, Apple says the update offers “uniform refraction and improved contrast,” and there is a new slider that makes Liquid Glass elements more or less opaque. And there’s one other major UI change worth celebrating: consistent window corner radii.
至于 Liquid Glass，苹果表示此次更新提供了“均匀的折射和改进的对比度”，并新增了一个滑块，可以调节 Liquid Glass 元素的透明度。此外，还有一个值得称赞的重大 UI 变化：统一的窗口圆角半径。
