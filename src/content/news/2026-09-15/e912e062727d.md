# Physics as the label for measuring and correcting materials reasoning in multimodal models
# 以物理定律作为标签：衡量并修正多模态模型中的材料推理

Vision-language and language models increasingly interpret materials data, yet benchmarks report that they hallucinate invalid properties and violate physical law. Evaluation matches final answers to scarce human labels, while discovery agents verify final proposals or density functional theory (DFT) execution. Neither measures the physical consistency of a model's reasoning chain.
视觉语言模型和语言模型在解读材料数据方面的应用日益广泛，但基准测试显示，这些模型经常产生无效属性的幻觉，并违反物理定律。目前的评估方法通常是将最终答案与稀缺的人类标注进行比对，而发现代理（discovery agents）则通过验证最终方案或密度泛函理论（DFT）的执行结果来评估。然而，这些方法都无法衡量模型推理链的物理一致性。

Materials data carries its own physics, making a large class of materials reasoning verifiable without annotation. We introduce MatPCR, a label-free benchmark whose programmatic oracles check diffraction geometry through Bragg's law, scale bars, spectral peaks, and Materials Project-grounded checks of near-hull stability, computed band-gap class, and net magnetization.
材料数据本身蕴含物理规律，这使得一大类材料推理无需人工标注即可进行验证。我们引入了 MatPCR，这是一个无需标签的基准测试。其程序化预言机（programmatic oracles）通过布拉格定律（Bragg's law）、比例尺、光谱峰值，以及基于 Materials Project 的近壳稳定性（near-hull stability）、计算带隙类别和净磁化强度检查，来验证衍射几何结构。

We define the Physical-Consistency Rate over image and structure inputs; introduce Constraint-Grounded Self-Verification, an agentic loop whose gain survives self-refinement and equal-compute re-prompting controls; release an open verifier useful in distribution but near chance on all six held-out constraint types; and derive an exact identity for how oracle error displaces the reported rate.
我们定义了针对图像和结构输入的“物理一致性率”（Physical-Consistency Rate）；引入了“基于约束的自我验证”（Constraint-Grounded Self-Verification），这是一个代理循环，其增益在自我修正和等计算量重提示控制下依然有效；发布了一个在分布内（in-distribution）有效，但在所有六种留出约束类型上表现接近随机猜测的开源验证器；并推导出了预言机误差如何影响报告比率的精确恒等式。
