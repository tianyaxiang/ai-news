# Roborock Qrevo 2 Review (2026): A New Budget Vacuum King?
# 石头科技 Qrevo 2 评测（2026）：新的平价扫地机之王？

**WIRED Rating: 7/10**
**WIRED 评分：7/10**

**WIRED:** Well-priced vacuum for the features. Easy to control. Great vacuuming and mopping. Nice corner coverage that got into one of my trickier corner tests. Plenty of high-power suction.
**优点：** 性价比高，功能丰富。易于操控。扫拖效果出色。边角覆盖能力强，成功通过了我的一项高难度边角测试。吸力强劲。

**TIRED:** Struggles to identify small items like cat toys or flat table legs. Mediocre results with cleaning up my spill tests.
**缺点：** 难以识别猫玩具或扁平桌腿等小物件。清理液体泼溅测试的表现平平。

Roborock's been a favorite of the robot vacuum game for a while. Here at WIRED, our reviewers are longtime fans of the affordable Qrevo line, but it was left without updates for years. The company has been more focused on more unique designs, such as the Saros Z70, which has an entire arm attached to it to grab your socks. These have a way to go before they're actually useful.
石头科技（Roborock）在扫地机器人领域一直备受青睐。在 WIRED，我们的评测人员长期以来都是高性价比 Qrevo 系列的粉丝，但该系列已经多年没有更新了。公司此前更专注于一些独特的设计，例如带有机械臂可以抓取袜子的 Saros Z70，但这些产品在真正实用化之前还有很长的路要走。

But this year, the Qrevo line is finally getting some love again. The Qrevo Curv line got a new model that we reviewed back in January, and there's now the Qrevo 2 Pro that just launched at the end of August. After living with it for the better part of two weeks, it's easily one of my favorite sub-$1,000 robot vacuums, especially for its corner cleaning, and has the power to rival my current budget pick, the Eufy Omni C28. My only complaint is little things. I mean that literally: this robot is great at spotting furniture and navigating large obstacles, but it won't spot your cords or cat toys laying on the floor.
但今年，Qrevo 系列终于迎来了更新。我们在 1 月份评测了 Qrevo Curv 系列的新机型，而 8 月底刚刚发布了 Qrevo 2 Pro。在使用了近两周后，它无疑是我最喜欢的 1000 美元以下扫地机器人之一，特别是在边角清洁方面，其性能足以媲美我目前的首选平价机型 Eufy Omni C28。我唯一的抱怨是关于“小东西”——字面意思：这款机器人非常擅长识别家具和绕过大型障碍物，但它无法识别地板上的电线或猫玩具。

The Roborock Qrevo 2 Pro has a nice range of specs. It comes with a 20,000 Pa digital motor, 200 RPM rotation speed for the mop pads, LiDar navigation to map your home, a FlexiArm mop that promises to get in hard-to-reach spots (and it delivered in my tests!), and an easy-to-use app. It also has Matter support if you wanted to voice command it with your smart home speaker and an AI-powered “SmartPlan” to better clean your home. I haven't noticed a difference in using SmartPlan compared to just running a regular cycle, but I've only been using this robot for about two weeks. Hopefully, the robot will learn more over time and improve—at least, in theory. It's not the kind of AI that's going to spot spills and stains, though, so keep that in mind when you run this robot vacuum.
Roborock Qrevo 2 Pro 的规格相当出色。它配备了 20,000 Pa 的数字电机、转速为 200 RPM 的拖布、用于家庭建图的激光雷达（LiDAR）、承诺能深入难以触及区域的 FlexiArm 机械臂拖布（在我的测试中表现确实不错！），以及易于使用的应用程序。它还支持 Matter 协议，方便你通过智能音箱进行语音控制，并配备了 AI 驱动的“SmartPlan”来优化清洁。虽然我只用了两周，还没感觉到 SmartPlan 与普通清洁模式的区别，但理论上，随着时间的推移，机器人会不断学习并改进。不过，这种 AI 并不能识别液体泼溅和污渍，使用时请务必注意。

Most of all, I was impressed by the edge cleaning with the FlexiArm mop, and the reversible side brush. Both work together to dislodge debris and make sure furniture and wall edges are clean. The vacuum was able to knock out one of my trickier Cheerio tests—specifically a Cheerio I stash in a corner next to my fridge—with the help of the side brush and mop. It didn't achieve a perfect corner score, missing one of the three Cheerios, but I've yet to meet a robot vacuum that can achieve all three. The slim 3.8-inch high design lets this vacuum squeeze underneath many kitchen counter toe kicks to suck up all the debris hiding under your counter, and clean right up into the edge.
最让我印象深刻的是 FlexiArm 拖布和可反转边刷带来的边缘清洁效果。两者协同工作，能有效清除碎屑，确保家具和墙角干净整洁。在我的高难度“麦圈测试”中（特指我藏在冰箱旁角落里的麦圈），它在边刷和拖布的帮助下成功清理干净。虽然它没能拿到满分（三个麦圈漏掉了一个），但我还没见过哪款扫地机器人能全部清理干净。3.8 英寸（约 9.6 厘米）的纤薄机身让它能钻进许多厨房橱柜底部，吸走隐藏的碎屑，并清洁到最边缘。

The suction level also worked well on carpets and rugs alike, though it worked better on pet hair than my long hair. My low-pile rug downstairs had a nice fluff to it after a run of this robot vacuum, but more importantly the tufts of cat hair were nowhere to be seen in my carpeted bedrooms after this vacuum ran through the top floor of my home. My hair was vacuumed up, but I found it rolled into a massive ball near a corner of the bed. I'll caveat that it had been a handful of weeks since my last good vacuuming upstairs, and I doubt you'd see this with regular cleanings. My husband didn't notice the hairball at all, and immediately complimented how nice the upstairs looked.
其吸力在地毯和地垫上的表现同样出色，不过清理宠物毛发的效果比清理我的长发更好。楼下的短绒地毯在清洁后显得蓬松舒适，更重要的是，在楼上铺设地毯的卧室里，清洁后完全看不到猫毛的踪迹。我的头发虽然被吸走了，但最后在床角卷成了一个大球。需要说明的是，我已经好几周没彻底打扫过楼上了，如果是定期清洁，应该不会出现这种情况。我丈夫完全没注意到那个发球，反而立刻夸赞楼上看起来非常整洁。

The Qrevo 2 Pro comes with a nice low profile base station, with containers at the top for clean and dirty water, easily accessible to carry to and from the sink and toilet. (That's where dirty water should go—don't channel my husband and clog your kitchen drain with your dirty mop water.) It will self-clean at 149 degrees Fahrenheit, and uses 113 degree Fahrenheit air to dry the mop pads.
Qrevo 2 Pro 配备了一个低矮的基站，顶部设有清水箱和污水箱，方便搬运至水槽或马桶处倾倒（污水应该倒进马桶——别学我丈夫，把脏拖布水倒进厨房下水道导致堵塞）。它能以 149 华氏度（约 65 摄氏度）的热水进行自清洁，并使用 113 华氏度（约 45 摄氏度）的热风烘干拖布。

But what I like most of all is how quiet the base station is, and the vacuum to boot. The Qrevo 2 Pro's base station's noisiest stage is refilling and draining the vacuum's water, like most robot vacuums. It's still reminiscent of a jet engine taking off like most robot vacuums, though, maybe for a much smaller airplane, and the drying stage is very quiet. The vacuum itself isn't too loud either. It wasn't distracting to work in the same room as the robot vacuum, particularly on mop mode—and I couldn't hear it running at all when in the other room. Vacuum mode is a little noisy, and it’s the loudest on carpets and rugs. But it never sounds like a little airplane buzzing around the house as some other vacuums do.
但我最喜欢的是基站和扫地机本身的静音表现。Qrevo 2 Pro 基站最吵的时候是加水和排水阶段，这和大多数扫地机一样。虽然它依然像大多数扫地机一样听起来像喷气式飞机起飞（只不过是架小飞机），但烘干阶段非常安静。扫地机本身也不算太吵。在它工作时，即使在同一个房间里办公也不会分心，尤其是在拖地模式下——而在另一个房间时，我几乎听不到它的声音。吸尘模式稍微吵一点，在地毯上声音最大。但它绝不会像其他一些扫地机那样，听起来像架小飞机在屋里嗡嗡作响。

While there's a lot I liked about this vacuum, it didn't ace every test. My sticky spill test was its weakest point, taking two tries to clean the spilled spots. It doesn't have any AI or features to spot stains (which is very normal for this price point), so it's not a huge surprise. But I would've liked to see it clean it up in one swoop. I'd also recommend spot cleaning sticky spots; doing an all-over clean that happened to include a big spill gave area around the spill a stickier feel than normal.
虽然我很喜欢这款扫地机，但它并没有通过所有测试。粘性污渍测试是它的弱点，需要两次才能清理干净。它没有任何 AI 或功能来识别污渍（在这个价位很正常），所以这并不令人意外。但我还是希望它能一次性清理干净。此外，我建议对粘性污渍进行定点清洁；如果进行全屋清洁时正好遇到大面积泼溅，反而会让污渍周围的区域感觉比平时更粘。

It also still struggled with thresholds, which is an issue we've seen before on Roborock vacuums. It can roll over them just fine without getting stuck, but I noticed this vacuum would usually leave some debris behind when crossing certain thresholds. It was particularly noticeable when moving between hard floors and carpet. My former colleague Adrienne So noted it when she tested both the Qrevo Curv 2 and the Saros 20 this year.
它在越障方面依然有些吃力，这是我们在石头科技扫地机上见过的问题。它虽然能顺利翻越门槛而不会被卡住，但我注意到它在跨越某些门槛时通常会留下一些碎屑。在硬地板和地毯之间切换时尤为明显。我的前同事 Adrienne So 在今年测试 Qrevo Curv 2 和 Saros 20 时也指出了这一点。
