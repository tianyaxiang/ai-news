# Best Noise-Canceling Earbuds (2026): Bose, Apple, Samsung, Beats, and More
# 2026年最佳降噪耳机：Bose、Apple、Samsung、Beats 等

The best noise-canceling earbuds are indispensable tech we all use daily for listening to any and everything—from phone calls, podcasts, and TV shows to workouts, commuting, and air travel. The list is endless. If you buy only one pair of headphones, you should make it a good pair of noise-canceling earbuds.
最好的降噪耳机是我们日常生活中不可或缺的科技产品，无论是接听电话、收听播客、观看电视节目，还是在健身、通勤或乘飞机时，我们都会用到它。应用场景不胜枚举。如果你只打算买一副耳机，那么选一副出色的降噪耳机准没错。

Most wireless earbuds come with built-in noise cancellation, but the difference between a good pair and a great pair is vast. Trust me, I’ve tested every one of our top picks in the real world—think busy New York City streets, loud cafes, crowded gyms, and rush-hour subway platforms—and sometimes, the noise cancellation is less effective at doing its one job. My top picks come from brands like Bose, Anker, Apple, and Beats, and while none is best overall, a few great options fit different needs.
大多数无线耳机都内置了降噪功能，但“好”与“优秀”之间的差距巨大。相信我，我在现实场景中测试了我们所有的首选产品——比如繁忙的纽约街道、嘈杂的咖啡馆、拥挤的健身房和高峰期的地铁站——有时，降噪功能在执行其核心任务时表现并不理想。我的首选品牌包括 Bose、Anker、Apple 和 Beats，虽然没有哪一款是绝对的“全能冠军”，但有几款出色的产品可以满足不同的需求。

### Best ANC Earbuds: Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds (2nd Gen)
### 最佳降噪耳机：Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds (二代)

If you’re seeking the absolute best active noise canceling (ANC), the Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds (2nd Gen) should be at the top of your shortlist. In all my testing, I've yet to find a pair of noise-canceling earbuds that can knock these off the throne, though some models come close. In any environment, whether it’s a noisy subway platform or an international flight, these earbuds largely block out noise. Plus, they sound great, with most music and TV shows coming in clearly and with ample detail.
如果你追求极致的主动降噪（ANC）体验，Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds (二代) 应该排在你选购清单的首位。在我所有的测试中，还没有哪款降噪耳机能撼动它们的地位，尽管有些型号已经非常接近了。无论是在嘈杂的地铁站还是国际航班上，这些耳机都能有效隔绝噪音。此外，它们的音质也非常出色，播放大多数音乐和电视节目时，声音清晰且细节丰富。

This second-generation version improves upon the original in small but important ways, including enhanced ANC, more stable connectivity, wireless charging, better personalized sound tuning, and improved customization options within the app.
这一代产品在细节上进行了重要改进，包括增强的降噪效果、更稳定的连接、无线充电功能、更个性化的音效调节，以及应用内更丰富的自定义选项。

### Best Budget ANC Earbuds: Soundcore by Anker Liberty 5 Pro
### 最佳性价比降噪耳机：Soundcore by Anker Liberty 5 Pro

It’s hard to find effective noise-canceling earbuds at lower price points, but the Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro bucks the trend, delivering excellent ANC for well under $200. They have a similar shape and size to the Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds (though they are a bit smaller), with similar touch-sensitive controls on each bud.
在较低价位段很难找到降噪效果出色的耳机，但 Soundcore Liberty 5 Pro 打破了这一常规，以远低于 200 美元的价格提供了卓越的降噪体验。它们的外形和尺寸与 Bose QuietComfort Ultra Earbuds 相似（尽管稍微小一点），并且每个耳塞上都有类似的触控操作。

While the noise-canceling isn’t as ironclad as the Bose, I actually find these earbuds more versatile. They have better battery life, a more secure fit, and a much higher dust and water resistance rating (IP55). But the biggest differentiator is call quality—these are some of the best earbuds for calls, full stop. They outperformed every other wireless earbud I tested them against on noisy city streets.
虽然它们的降噪能力不如 Bose 那样“密不透风”，但我发现这些耳机其实更全能。它们拥有更长的续航、更稳固的佩戴感，以及更高的防尘防水等级（IP55）。但最大的区别在于通话质量——毫不夸张地说，这是通话效果最好的耳机之一。在嘈杂的城市街道测试中，它们的表现优于我测试过的所有其他无线耳机。

### Best ANC Earbuds for iPhone: Apple AirPods Pro 3
### 最佳 iPhone 降噪耳机：Apple AirPods Pro 3

If you have an iPhone, you should probably just get the Apple AirPods Pro 3 earbuds. That’s doubly true if you have lots of Apple gadgets—think iPhone, MacBook, and Apple TV. They offer the easiest connectivity for Apple users, and you can swap between your devices instantly and seamlessly. Plus, they connect to Apple's Find My network for pinpoint location tracking.
如果你使用 iPhone，那么直接购买 Apple AirPods Pro 3 是最明智的选择。如果你拥有多款苹果设备（如 iPhone、MacBook 和 Apple TV），这一点尤为重要。它们为苹果用户提供了最便捷的连接体验，你可以在设备间即时、无缝地切换。此外，它们还支持苹果的“查找”（Find My）网络，实现精准定位。

But they’re also just good earbuds, with excellent noise-canceling and a particularly impressive transparency mode. They also have heart rate sensors built into each earbud, meaning you can start and track a workout and get accurate, detailed stats without wearing a fitness tracker.
它们本身也是非常出色的耳机，具备优秀的降噪功能和令人印象深刻的通透模式。每个耳塞内还内置了心率传感器，这意味着你无需佩戴健身追踪器，就能开启并记录锻炼过程，获取准确、详细的运动数据。
