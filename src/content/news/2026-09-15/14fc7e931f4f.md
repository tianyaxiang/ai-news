# EuroBirdPortal – Live bird movements across Europe

**EuroBirdPortal (EBP)** is a collaborative project that provides a dynamic, real-time visualization of bird movements across the European continent. By aggregating data from various national bird-watching portals, the platform offers researchers and enthusiasts a comprehensive view of avian migration patterns and population shifts.

**EuroBirdPortal (EBP)** 是一个协作项目，旨在提供欧洲大陆鸟类迁徙的动态实时可视化视图。通过整合来自各国观鸟门户网站的数据，该平台为研究人员和爱好者提供了关于鸟类迁徙模式和种群变动的全面视角。
