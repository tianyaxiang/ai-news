# Graph Engineering for AI Agents: From Prompts and Loops to Workflows
# AI 智能体的图工程：从提示词与循环到工作流

### 1. 引言：从 X 平台上的病毒式争论到本周即可亲手构建的图

But it did, and a few hours later, AI engineer Hamel Husain answered it directly, publishing an X Article with a title catching everyone's attention: "Loop Engineering Is Dead. 2026 年年中，OpenClaw 的创始人 Peter Steinberger 在 X 上发布了一个十二个单词的问题：“我们还在谈论循环，还是已经转向图了？”这本不该引起什么波澜。这本是开发者们在 Slack 私信中交流的问题，而不是那种能让半个 AI 推特圈都参与回复的话题。但它确实火了，几个小时后，AI 工程师 Hamel Husain 直接回应了这个问题，并发布了一篇引人注目的 X 文章，标题为：“循环工程已死，图工程时代来临。”

You’ve probably also seen where the simple “give it a task and let it loop” approach starts to break down: a check gets skipped, a weak answer gets accepted too early, or something that looked right on the first pass falls apart on the second.
本文面向那些已经将 AI 用于实际、可重复工作的人，而不仅仅是偶尔使用提示词的人。也许你用它来研究想法、分类支持工单、审查代码、起草内容，或从杂乱的信息中做出决策。你可能也已经发现，简单的“给它一个任务并让它循环”的方法在某些情况下会失效：检查被跳过、薄弱的答案过早被采纳，或者第一遍看起来正确的内容在第二遍时就崩溃了。

You don’t need to be building agents or working with an orchestration framework to follow along. The goal is to make the loops-versus-graphs debate concrete enough that you can see what actually changes in practice. By the end, you’ll be able to take one workflow you already run with AI, sketch it as a graph on paper, and decide whether adding structure would actually make it better.
你不需要正在构建智能体或使用编排框架也能理解本文。本文的目标是将“循环与图”的辩论具体化，让你看到实践中到底发生了什么变化。读完后，你将能够把你现有的一个 AI 工作流画成一张图，并判断增加结构是否真的能让它变得更好。

### 3. The problem with single-agent loops
### 3. 单智能体循环的问题

#### 3.1 How is Graph Engineering different?
#### 3.1 图工程有何不同？

Here is a brief explanation of the differences. Prompt engineering optimizes what you say to a model. Context engineering optimizes what the model can see when it answers. Both still operate within a single turn.
以下是这些差异的简要说明。提示词工程优化的是你对模型说的话。上下文工程优化的是模型在回答时能看到的内容。两者仍然都在单次交互内运行。

Loop engineering moves up a level. It gives the model tools, memory, and the ability to iterate—but control still lives inside one continuous run. The model decides what happens next.
循环工程提升了一个层级。它赋予模型工具、记忆和迭代能力，但控制权仍然存在于一次连续的运行中。由模型决定下一步做什么。

Graph engineering changes who is in control. Instead of letting the model manage the process as it goes, you define the nodes, routing logic, and checkpoints in advance. The model contributes judgment only at the steps that actually require it; the graph decides what happens next.
图工程改变了控制权。你不再让模型在运行过程中管理流程，而是预先定义节点、路由逻辑和检查点。模型仅在真正需要判断的步骤中贡献判断力；由图来决定下一步发生什么。

In short: from trusting a model to manage the entire process to trusting a structure to manage the process—and using models only where their judgment is genuinely needed.
简而言之：从信任模型来管理整个流程，转变为信任结构来管理流程，并且仅在真正需要模型判断的地方使用模型。

#### 3.2 Why a single-agent loop causes issue
#### 3.2 为什么单智能体循环会引发问题

Most people are using AI in much the same way: open a chat, ask a big question, get a confident answer, and move on. Sometimes the answer seems right, sometimes it's too broad, sometimes it's incorrect. Then they have to be validated and rechecked over and over, without a fix workflow or structure, which leads to different quality each time.
大多数人使用 AI 的方式大同小异：打开聊天窗口，问一个大问题，得到一个自信的回答，然后继续。有时答案看起来是对的，有时太宽泛，有时则是错误的。然后他们必须反复验证和检查，由于缺乏固定的工作流或结构，导致每次的结果质量参差不齐。

Let's look at a simple example to understand why this manual technique is inefficient. Imagine you’re trying to validate a startup idea: should you build an AI bookkeeping tool for Shopify merchants?
让我们看一个简单的例子来理解为什么这种手动技术效率低下。想象一下，你正在尝试验证一个创业想法：你应该为 Shopify 商家构建一个 AI 记账工具吗？

You ask the model. It researches the market, finds a few competitors, sketches a go-to-market plan, and gives you something that looks like a finished memo. By the end, it feels like you’ve done the research, and the plan looks good.
你询问模型。它研究市场，找到几个竞争对手，勾勒出一个市场进入计划，并给你一份看起来像完成的备忘录。最后，感觉你已经完成了研究，计划看起来也不错。

But look at what actually happened. One model decided what was worth investigating. Then, it gathered the evidence, interpreted what it found, made a recommendation, and judged its own confidence in that recommendation. It did all of this in a single run, with no one checking its work along the way—no one confirming whether its initial direction was good enough, and no one catching where its reasoning went wrong.
但看看实际发生了什么。一个模型决定了什么值得调查。然后，它收集证据、解读发现、提出建议，并判断自己对该建议的信心。它在一次运行中完成了所有这些工作，过程中没有人检查它的工作——没有人确认其最初的方向是否足够好，也没有人发现它的推理在哪里出了错。

That’s the single-agent loop. It looks good on the surface, but the underlying process is flawed. This is also what causes many of the problems we see when agents are used in real systems: they get stuck repeating unproductive steps, miss important business rules, or run out of context halfway through a task.
这就是单智能体循环。表面上看起来不错，但底层流程是有缺陷的。这也是我们在实际系统中使用智能体时看到的许多问题的原因：它们陷入重复无效步骤的困境、遗漏重要的业务规则，或者在任务中途耗尽上下文。

The answer isn’t a better prompt. It’s a smaller job.
答案不是更好的提示词，而是更小的任务。

One thing from my experience: if you already know a business rule or have domain knowledge, put it directly in code instead of hoping the model remembers it. Use the model only for the parts that actually require judgment—interpreting something ambiguous, comparing messy information, or choosing between possible next steps.
根据我的经验：如果你已经知道某项业务规则或拥有领域知识，请直接将其写入代码，而不是寄希望于模型能记住它。仅在真正需要判断的部分使用模型——例如解读模糊信息、比较杂乱的数据，或在可能的后续步骤之间进行选择。

Don’t give the model control over the entire workflow. Give it one clearly defined step at a time. That’s the idea behind graph engineering: instead of trusting one model to make an entire decision from start to finish, you build a workflow that determines what happens next. The model provides judgment where judgment is useful. The graph provides structure everywhere else.
不要让模型控制整个工作流。一次只给它一个定义明确的步骤。这就是图工程背后的理念：不再信任一个模型从头到尾做出所有决定，而是构建一个决定下一步走向的工作流。模型在需要判断的地方提供判断，而图在其他所有地方提供结构。

Keep the Shopify bookkeeping question in mind. For the rest of this piece, we’ll build a graph around that question one piece at a time, until “structure instead of judgment” becomes something concrete you can actually see.
请记住 Shopify 记账的问题。在本文的剩余部分，我们将围绕这个问题一点一点地构建一个图，直到“用结构代替判断”变成你可以亲眼看到的具体事物。

### 4. 智能体图的构建模块

Strip away the diagrams, and an agent graph is surprisingly simple. It comes down to three core pieces: nodes, edges, and state. There are also a couple of things—reducers and checkpoints—that become important once you move from a demo to production.
剥离掉图表，智能体图其实非常简单。它归结为三个核心部分：节点（nodes）、边（edges）和状态（state）。一旦你从演示阶段转向生产环境，还有一些东西——归约器（reducers）和检查点（checkpoints）——也会变得很重要。
