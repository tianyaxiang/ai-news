# Expanding AI access and cyber defense for federal, state, local, and tribal governments
# 为联邦、州、地方及部落政府扩展人工智能访问权限与网络防御能力

September 10, 2026 | Global Affairs
2026年9月10日 | 全球事务

A first-of-its-kind agreement will provide free access and 50% off usage for federal, state, local, and tribal governments, alongside expanded support and access for public-sector cyber defenders.
一项首创性的协议将为联邦、州、地方及部落政府提供免费访问权限及 50% 的使用费折扣，同时为公共部门的网络防御者提供更广泛的支持与访问权限。

America’s public servants, including those on the front lines of cyber defense, should have access to the best AI tools available. Today, OpenAI for Government and the U.S. General Services Administration (GSA) are announcing a new multi-year agreement that builds on last year’s federal offer. This first-of-its-kind agreement will provide $0 access—normally $15 per user per month—for the license fee and 50% off usage, alongside expanded support for public-sector cyber defenders. OpenAI is also extending the same offer to all state, local and tribal governments.
美国的公职人员，包括那些处于网络防御前线的人员，应当能够使用最顶尖的人工智能工具。今天，OpenAI 政府事务部门与美国总务管理局（GSA）宣布达成一项新的多年期协议，该协议建立在去年联邦政府优惠政策的基础上。这项首创性的协议将提供零成本访问权限（通常为每位用户每月 15 美元）以减免许可费，并提供 50% 的使用费折扣，同时为公共部门的网络防御者提供更广泛的支持。OpenAI 也将此优惠扩展至所有州、地方及部落政府。

Helping government work better—making services faster, easier, safer, and more reliable—is a key way to bring the benefits of AI to everyone. Public servants should help shape how AI is used in government, and that starts by giving them access to best-in-class tools, including GPT‑6 Astra, with strong safeguards, cost predictability and respect for their public mission. More than one million government employees already have access to ChatGPT through our existing agreements, and this expanded partnership extends eligibility across a U.S. public-sector workforce of approximately 23 million people.
助力政府高效运作——使服务更快捷、更简便、更安全、更可靠——是将人工智能红利惠及每个人的关键途径。公职人员应当参与塑造人工智能在政府中的应用方式，而这一切始于让他们能够使用一流的工具（包括 GPT-6 Astra），并确保这些工具具备强大的安全保障、成本可预测性，且尊重其公共使命。通过我们现有的协议，已有超过一百万名政府雇员能够使用 ChatGPT，而此次扩展的合作伙伴关系将使美国约 2300 万公共部门从业人员均具备使用资格。

Over the past year, public servants have shown what broader access to OpenAI tools can make possible:
在过去的一年中，公职人员已经展示了更广泛地使用 OpenAI 工具所能带来的可能性：

*   **Daybreak Access** is improving the speed and quality of vulnerability research, malware analysis, and security-tool development for government cyber defenders.
    **Daybreak Access** 正在提升政府网络防御者在漏洞研究、恶意软件分析及安全工具开发方面的速度与质量。
*   **The CDC** accelerated public-health literature reviews that previously took days or months, producing most initial reports in under 30 minutes, with productivity gains reported by 92% of participating experts.
    **美国疾控中心（CDC）** 加速了公共卫生文献的审查工作，以往需要数天或数月的工作，现在大多数初步报告可在 30 分钟内完成，92% 的参与专家报告称生产力得到了提升。
*   **Georgia’s Department of Revenue** reduced the time needed to digitize a tax form from as much as two weeks to 15 minutes.
    **佐治亚州税务局** 将数字化税务表格所需的时间从最长两周缩短至 15 分钟。
*   **In North Carolina, the Department of State Treasurer** used ChatGPT to identify millions of dollars in potential unclaimed property that could be returned to residents.
    **北卡罗来纳州财政部** 利用 ChatGPT 识别出了数百万美元的潜在未认领财产，这些财产有望返还给居民。
*   **Lawrence Livermore National Laboratory** used ChatGPT to accelerate development of a preliminary fusion-research model, compressing work that could have taken months into a matter of hours.
    **劳伦斯利弗莫尔国家实验室** 利用 ChatGPT 加速了初步聚变研究模型的开发，将原本可能耗时数月的工作压缩至数小时内完成。

With this announcement, we are also expanding support for government cyber defenders protecting emergency response, transportation, public health, utilities, and other critical systems. OpenAI President Greg Brockman’s recent essay, *The Defender’s Window*, and an industry-wide open letter both call for urgent, collective action across industry and government. As part of our new policy, every verified government entity will be approved for our advanced cyber-defender Daybreak Blue access, all at 50% off standard commercial pricing, with scaled training and enablement to support adoption. These defenders can also request access to Daybreak Red for advanced vulnerability research, exploit validation, and red teaming at standard commercial pricing.
随着这一公告的发布，我们还将扩大对保护应急响应、交通、公共卫生、公用事业及其他关键系统的政府网络防御者的支持。OpenAI 总裁 Greg Brockman 最近发表的文章《防御者之窗》（The Defender’s Window）以及一封全行业公开信，都呼吁行业与政府采取紧急的集体行动。作为我们新政策的一部分，每个经过验证的政府实体都将获准使用我们先进的“Daybreak Blue”网络防御访问权限，所有费用均享受标准商业价格的 5 折优惠，并提供规模化的培训与赋能以支持其应用。这些防御者还可以申请访问“Daybreak Red”，以标准商业价格进行高级漏洞研究、漏洞利用验证及红队测试。

This announcement builds upon Daybreak for Frontline Defenders, which OpenAI announced last week with a $1 billion global commitment to subsidized access, training, technical support, and partnerships for frontline cyber defenders.
此公告建立在“Daybreak for Frontline Defenders”项目的基础上。OpenAI 上周宣布该项目，承诺投入 10 亿美元在全球范围内为一线网络防御者提供补贴访问、培训、技术支持及合作伙伴关系。

As part of today’s announcement, OpenAI will provide:
作为今天公告的一部分，OpenAI 将提供：

*   **Broader access:** For the first time, the benefits of this agreement will be available to state, local, and tribal governments in addition to the federal government.
    **更广泛的访问权限：** 协议的红利首次在联邦政府之外，扩展至州、地方及部落政府。
*   **More affordable use:** The standard $15-per-user monthly license fee is reduced to $0, with no minimum commitment and 50% off usage costs for eligible federal, state, local, and tribal organizations.
    **更经济的使用成本：** 标准的每用户每月 15 美元许可费降至 0 美元，且无最低承诺要求，符合条件的联邦、州、地方及部落组织可享受 50% 的使用费折扣。
*   **Daybreak Blue access and training:** Every verified government entity will be approved for Daybreak Blue at 50% off standard commercial pricing, with scaled training and enablement to help government leaders and cyber defenders use the model effectively and find vulnerabilities first.
    **Daybreak Blue 访问权限与培训：** 每个经过验证的政府实体均可获准以标准商业价格的 5 折使用 Daybreak Blue，并获得规模化的培训与赋能，帮助政府领导者和网络防御者有效利用模型并抢先发现漏洞。
*   **Long-term access:** The agreement runs for 27 months, from October 1, 2026, through December 31, 2028, giving agencies a stable foundation for AI adoption.
    **长期访问权限：** 协议有效期为 27 个月，从 2026 年 10 月 1 日至 2028 年 12 月 31 日，为各机构采用人工智能提供稳定的基础。
*   **Practical adoption support:** OpenAI will provide buyer guidance, usage estimates, spend controls, onboarding, FinOps guidance, webinars, and an expanded Government Academy to help all public servants become confident AI users.
    **实用的应用支持：** OpenAI 将提供采购指南、使用估算、支出控制、入职培训、FinOps 指导、网络研讨会以及扩展的“政府学院”（Government Academy），帮助所有公职人员成为自信的人工智能用户。
*   **Secure and responsible deployment:** ChatGPT Enterprise does not use business data, including inputs or outputs, to train or improve OpenAI models, and participating organizations will retain the protections associated with the eligible services they choose.
    **安全且负责任的部署：** ChatGPT 企业版不会使用业务数据（包括输入或输出内容）来训练或改进 OpenAI 模型，参与组织将保留其所选合格服务相关的保护措施。

AI should give public servants more time for what matters: serving people, protecting critical systems relied on by the American people, and solving hard problems. By expanding access across every level of government, we can help turn human potential into public impact and ensure the benefits of AI reach communities across America.
人工智能应当让公职人员有更多时间专注于重要事务：服务民众、保护美国人民所依赖的关键系统，以及解决棘手问题。通过在各级政府中扩展访问权限，我们可以帮助将人类潜能转化为公共影响力，并确保人工智能的红利惠及全美各地的社区。
