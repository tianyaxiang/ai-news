This geneticist’s age-reversal tech could help restore sight. Yuancheng (Ryan) Lu is obsessed with aging. And with eyes. As he steps outside the Whitehead Institute in Cambridge, Massachusetts, his aviator glasses darken automatically in the sun. Age-related blindness runs in his family, and his own 23andMe test came back with a mutation for macular degeneration, a top cause of vision loss in old age. That obsession extends to his work. Lu is behind one of the coolest results in rejuvenation science and eye research: an age-reversal technique called reprogramming that repaired the optic nerves of blind mice, restoring their vision. Now, nearly the same genetic therapy he developed as a student has entered human clinical trials. Learn more about Lu’s work on restoring sight with age-reversal therapy. —Antonio Regalado
这位遗传学家的逆龄技术有望帮助恢复视力。陆源成（Yuancheng "Ryan" Lu）痴迷于衰老，也痴迷于眼睛。当他走出位于马萨诸塞州剑桥市的怀特黑德研究所时，他的飞行员眼镜在阳光下自动变暗。他的家族有与年龄相关的失明史，他自己的 23andMe 检测结果也显示他携带黄斑变性的突变基因，这是老年视力丧失的主要原因。这种痴迷延伸到了他的工作中。陆源成是再生科学和眼科研究中最酷成果之一的幕后推手：一种被称为“重编程”的逆龄技术，它修复了失明小鼠的视神经，恢复了它们的视力。现在，他作为学生时期开发的几乎相同的基因疗法已经进入了人体临床试验阶段。了解更多关于陆源成利用逆龄疗法恢复视力的工作。——Antonio Regalado
