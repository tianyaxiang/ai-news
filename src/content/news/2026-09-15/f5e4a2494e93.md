# The Best Soundbars (2026): Sonos, LG, TCL, Bose, and More
# 2026年最佳回音壁（Soundbar）推荐：Sonos、LG、TCL、Bose 等

A soundbar is one of the smartest upgrades you can make for your entertainment room. The latest models sound loud, clear, and distinct, with realistic sound that is truly transportive—a thunderstorm sounds like rain from every direction, a sword fight reverberates with every clang.
回音壁是你为娱乐室所能做的最明智的升级之一。最新的型号声音响亮、清晰且层次分明，逼真的音效能让你身临其境——雷雨声仿佛从四面八方传来，剑斗时的每一次碰撞都回响在耳畔。

There are tons of options to choose from, but all of my top picks went through rigorous hands-on (and ears open) testing. I chose the best models in a few categories, including ones that are ideal for bass, small spaces, or expandability.
市面上的选择数不胜数，但我推荐的所有产品都经过了严格的实测（以及“实听”）。我挑选了几个类别中的最佳型号，包括那些在低音表现、小空间适配或扩展性方面表现出色的产品。

***

### Best Overall Soundbar: Sonos Arc Ultra
### 最佳综合回音壁：Sonos Arc Ultra

The Sonos Arc Ultra launched in 2024, but it’s still my soundbar champion, with the most pristine sound for movies, games, and music. With some of my other top picks outclass it in specific areas like surround sound or bass, the Arc Ultra’s great all-around performance makes it easy to recommend.
Sonos Arc Ultra 于 2024 年发布，但它依然是我心目中的回音壁冠军，在电影、游戏和音乐方面都能提供最纯净的音质。虽然我推荐的其他一些产品在环绕声或低音等特定领域表现更强，但 Arc Ultra 出色的综合性能使其成为最值得推荐的选择。

This model is a prime example of why soundbars are a great addition to any home theater. Dolby Atmos and DTS:X surround sound are convincing—the upward-firing and side channels make spear clanks and grunts in *Predator: Badlands* appropriately spatial. Play a song like "Want Want" by Maggie Rogers and you can feel the pounding electric guitars and synth in your bones.
这款产品是回音壁为何能成为家庭影院绝佳补充的典范。其 Dolby Atmos 和 DTS:X 环绕声效果令人信服——向上发声和侧向声道让《铁血战士：荒地》中的长矛碰撞声和低吼声具有极佳的空间感。播放 Maggie Rogers 的《Want Want》时，你能感受到强劲的电吉他和合成器在骨子里震动。

### Best Soundbar for Surround Sound: LG Sound Suite H7
### 最佳环绕声回音壁：LG Sound Suite H7

You won’t find a better surround sound experience than the one the LG Sound Suite H7 provides, especially if you add the optional LG W7 subwoofer and LG M7 satellite speakers. They aren’t cheap additions, but they create a very immersive experience all together.
你很难找到比 LG Sound Suite H7 更好的环绕声体验，特别是当你添加了可选的 LG W7 低音炮和 LG M7 卫星音箱后。虽然这些配件价格不菲，但它们组合在一起能创造出极具沉浸感的体验。

The system uses Dolby Atmos FlexConnect so you can place the extra speakers anywhere you want. Using the LG ThinQ app, you can then group the speakers for Dolby Atmos surround sound that’s ultra-realistic. When I watched *The Last House*, which has an exceptional audio design, the system placed creepy sound effects across every corner of the room.
该系统采用了 Dolby Atmos FlexConnect 技术，因此你可以将额外的音箱放置在任何位置。通过 LG ThinQ 应用程序，你可以将这些音箱组合起来，实现超逼真的杜比全景声环绕效果。当我观看音效设计出色的《The Last House》时，该系统将令人毛骨悚然的音效精准地投射到房间的每一个角落。

### Best Compact Soundbar With Subwoofer: TCL A65K Soundbar
### 最佳带低音炮紧凑型回音壁：TCL A65K Soundbar

The TCL A65K soundbar is much shorter, thinner, and cheaper than most on my list, but it still packs a punch, especially for music and dialogue. It includes a subwoofer that connects wirelessly without any complicated setup. It’s a huge step up from any built-in TV or projector speakers, with much clearer dialogue, louder bass, and more detailed vocals.
TCL A65K 回音壁比我名单上的大多数产品更短、更薄、更便宜，但它依然动力十足，特别是在音乐和对白表现上。它包含一个无线连接的低音炮，无需复杂的设置。相比任何电视或投影仪的内置扬声器，它都有巨大的提升，对白更清晰，低音更强劲，人声细节也更丰富。

***

### Most Compact Soundbar: Bose Smart Soundbar
### 最紧凑回音壁：Bose Smart Soundbar

Former WIRED reviewer Ryan Waniata is a big fan of the Bose Smart Soundbar for its incredibly sleek and small design—it’s the most compact soundbar on this list by far at just over 27 inches across. Despite its small size, it has central, side-firing, and up-firing drivers, meaning more convincing spatial audio than rivals like the Sonos Beam Gen 2.
前 WIRED 评测员 Ryan Waniata 非常推崇 Bose Smart Soundbar，因为它设计极其纤薄小巧——它是这份名单中最紧凑的回音壁，宽度仅 27 英寸出头。尽管体积小，但它配备了中央、侧向和向上发声的驱动单元，这意味着它比 Sonos Beam Gen 2 等竞争对手能提供更具说服力的空间音频。
