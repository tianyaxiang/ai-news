### Physics-Informed Conformal Prediction: Embedding PDE Consistency into Distribution-Free Uncertainty Quantification for Neural Operators
### 物理信息共形预测：将偏微分方程一致性嵌入神经算子的无分布不确定性量化

**Abstract:**
Neural operators such as the Fourier Neural Operator (FNO) achieve remarkable accuracy in approximating solutions to partial differential equations (PDEs). However, providing rigorous uncertainty estimates remains an open challenge. 

**摘要：**
诸如傅里叶神经算子（FNO）之类的神经算子在逼近偏微分方程（PDE）解方面取得了显著的精度。然而，提供严谨的不确定性估计仍然是一个尚未解决的挑战。

We propose Physics-Informed Conformal Prediction (PI-CP), a framework that embeds PDE residuals into the nonconformity score of split conformal prediction, producing prediction intervals that are (i) distribution-free with provable coverage guarantees, and (ii) spatially adaptive when the PDE residual correlates with prediction error -- tighter where physics is well-satisfied, wider where it is violated. 

我们提出了物理信息共形预测（PI-CP），这是一个将 PDE 残差嵌入到分割共形预测（split conformal prediction）非一致性得分中的框架。该框架生成的预测区间具有以下特点：（i）无分布特性且具有可证明的覆盖保证；（ii）当 PDE 残差与预测误差相关时具有空间自适应性——在物理规律满足良好的区域区间更紧凑，在违反物理规律的区域则更宽。

Additionally, we prove that FNO's translation equivariance creates a fundamental approximation barrier for PDEs with Dirichlet boundary conditions, and show that coordinate channels resolve this with up to 63x error reduction. 

此外，我们证明了 FNO 的平移等变性为具有狄利克雷边界条件的 PDE 带来了根本性的逼近障碍，并展示了通过坐标通道可以解决这一问题，使误差降低高达 63 倍。

We validate PI-CP across six physics scenarios -- heat conduction (2D/3D), structural mechanics (2D/3D), Darcy flow, and Navier-Stokes -- demonstrating consistent 89-91% coverage for all four Conformal methods, while MC Dropout and Deep Ensembles are unstable (82-100%). FNO outperforms CNN and DeepONet by 10-12x.

我们在六种物理场景中验证了 PI-CP，包括热传导（2D/3D）、结构力学（2D/3D）、达西流和纳维-斯托克斯方程。结果表明，所有四种共形方法均表现出 89-91% 的稳定覆盖率，而 MC Dropout 和深度集成方法则表现不稳定（82-100%）。FNO 的性能比 CNN 和 DeepONet 高出 10-12 倍。
