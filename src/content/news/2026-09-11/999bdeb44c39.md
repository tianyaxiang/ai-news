# DeepSeek v4.1 Flash

DeepSeek@deepseek_ai 🚀 Introducing DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash: smarter, faster, more efficient. 🔹 Introducing the smallest model in our new architecture family, with native visual understanding. 🔹 Designed for greater capability, faster inference, higher throughput, and scaling to larger models.

DeepSeek@deepseek_ai 🚀 隆重推出 DeepSeek-V4.1-Flash：更智能、更快速、更高效。🔹 介绍我们新架构系列中最小的模型，具备原生视觉理解能力。🔹 专为更强性能、更快的推理速度、更高的吞吐量以及向更大规模模型扩展而设计。

***

DeepSeek@deepseek_ai 🧠 Asymmetric architecture. More intelligence, less cost. 🔹 552B-parameter MoE. 🔹 New Causal Encoder–Decoder architecture: just 8B active parameters for input, 16B for output. 🔹 New pre-training methods + larger-scale RL post-training deliver benchmark results ahead of.

DeepSeek@deepseek_ai 🧠 非对称架构。更高的智能，更低的成本。🔹 5520 亿参数的 MoE（混合专家模型）。🔹 全新的因果编码器-解码器（Causal Encoder–Decoder）架构：输入端仅需 80 亿激活参数，输出端为 160 亿。🔹 新的预训练方法加上更大规模的强化学习（RL）后训练，带来了领先的基准测试结果。

***

DeepSeek@deepseek_ai 💾 Smaller KV cache. Bigger savings. Compared with the previous generation, V4.1-Flash’s KV cache needs just: 🔹 1/4 the HBM 🔹 1/8 the SSD storage. Cache-hit charges often account for a large share of agent costs. Compressing the cache cuts those costs significantly.

DeepSeek@deepseek_ai 💾 更小的 KV 缓存，更大的节省。与上一代相比，V4.1-Flash 的 KV 缓存仅需：🔹 1/4 的高带宽内存（HBM）🔹 1/8 的 SSD 存储空间。缓存命中费用通常占智能体（Agent）成本的很大一部分，压缩缓存可显著降低这些成本。

***

DeepSeek@deepseek_ai ⚡ V4.1-Flash is now live on the DeepSeek API with native multimodal support. Set your model to deepseek-flash. 🔹 V4-Flash & V4-Flash-Vision-Exp are retired. For compatibility, deepseek-v4-flash and deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp temporarily route to V4.1-Flash.

DeepSeek@deepseek_ai ⚡ V4.1-Flash 现已上线 DeepSeek API，并支持原生多模态。请将您的模型设置为 deepseek-flash。🔹 V4-Flash 和 V4-Flash-Vision-Exp 已退役。为保持兼容性，deepseek-v4-flash 和 deepseek-v4-flash-vision-exp 将暂时重定向至 V4.1-Flash。

***

DeepSeek@deepseek_ai 💰 More efficient architecture. Lower API prices. V4.1-Flash lets us serve more users at a lower cost. We’re passing the savings on to you. 🔹 Peak/off-peak pricing continues to balance demand. 🔹 Off-peak rates are 50% of peak rates. Schedule flexible workloads off-peak.

DeepSeek@deepseek_ai 💰 更高效的架构，更低的 API 价格。V4.1-Flash 使我们能够以更低的成本服务更多用户，我们将这些节省的成本回馈给您。🔹 高峰/非高峰定价继续用于平衡需求。🔹 非高峰时段费率为高峰时段的 50%。请将灵活的工作负载安排在非高峰时段。
