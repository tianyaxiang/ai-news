# Robustness of LLM-Generated SystemVerilog Assertions to Semantics-Preserving RTL Transformations
# 大语言模型生成的 SystemVerilog 断言对保持语义的 RTL 转换的鲁棒性研究

Large language models (LLMs) are increasingly being explored for automating SystemVerilog Assertion (SVA) generation, yet most evaluations report correctness on a single syntactic representation of an input. Such point accuracy does not reveal whether a model's correct output is stable when the same RTL behavior is written differently.
大语言模型（LLM）正越来越多地被探索用于自动化生成 SystemVerilog 断言（SVA），然而大多数评估仅报告了模型在单一语法输入表示下的正确性。这种点准确率（point accuracy）无法揭示当相同的 RTL 行为以不同方式编写时，模型的正确输出是否依然稳定。

This paper presents a controlled metamorphic evaluation of LLM-based SVA generation under semantics-Preserving RTL transformations. Starting from the VERT dataset, we construct a quality-filtered conditional-control pool and a stratified 40-program evaluation set containing 295 assignment behaviors. We evaluate two open code models, Qwen2.5-Coder-7B and DeepSeek-Coder-V2-Lite, with an identical evaluation prompt and greedy decoding.
本文针对基于 LLM 的 SVA 生成，提出了一种在保持语义的 RTL 转换下的受控蜕变测试（metamorphic evaluation）。我们以 VERT 数据集为基础，构建了一个经过质量过滤的条件控制池，以及一个包含 295 个赋值行为的分层 40 程序评估集。我们使用相同的评估提示词和贪婪解码策略，对 Qwen2.5-Coder-7B 和 DeepSeek-Coder-V2-Lite 这两个开源代码模型进行了评估。

Three transformations are studied: operand reordering, deterministic identifier renaming, and redundant parenthesization. Beyond baseline and transformed accuracy, we measure conditional robustness, invariance failure, and any-flip rate, with 10,000-sample clustered bootstrap intervals at the RTL-program level.
研究涉及三种转换方式：操作数重排序、确定性标识符重命名以及冗余括号化。除了基准准确率和转换后的准确率外，我们还测量了条件鲁棒性、不变性失效和任意翻转率，并在 RTL 程序级别进行了 10,000 次样本的聚类自助法（clustered bootstrap）区间估计。

Across all six model-transformation conditions, 9.7%-27.0% of behaviors that were correct on the original RTL become incorrect after a semantics-preserving transformation. Aggregate accuracy can therefore hide substantial instability: under identifier renaming, DeepSeek-Coder-V2-Lite improves from 53.9% to 63.7% accuracy while 19.5% of its originally correct behaviors fail.
在所有六种模型-转换组合条件下，9.7% 到 27.0% 在原始 RTL 上正确的行为在经过保持语义的转换后变得不正确。因此，总体准确率可能会掩盖实质性的不稳定性：在标识符重命名测试中，DeepSeek-Coder-V2-Lite 的准确率从 53.9% 提升至 63.7%，但其原本正确的行为中有 19.5% 出现了失效。

Manual review of 30 sampled correct-to-wrong transitions identifies dropped path predicates, branch-polarity errors, Boolean-structure corruption, and output-contract violations. The results show that point accuracy alone is insufficient for characterizing LLM reliability in assertion generation and motivate robustness-aware evaluation for AI-assisted hardware verification.
通过对 30 个从“正确”转为“错误”的样本进行人工审查，我们发现了路径谓词丢失、分支极性错误、布尔结构损坏以及输出契约违规等问题。研究结果表明，仅凭点准确率不足以表征 LLM 在断言生成中的可靠性，并强调了在 AI 辅助硬件验证中进行鲁棒性感知评估的必要性。
