### The Best Overall Windows Laptop: Dell XPS 13 (2026)
### 最佳综合Windows笔记本电脑：Dell XPS 13 (2026)

The Dell XPS 13 (8/10, WIRED Recommends) is back. It's still extremely compact and portable, which has always been its calling card. Its small size makes it an ideal laptop to work on an airplane, and at only 2.2 pounds, you might not even notice it in your backpack. But this time, its completely new design focuses on two things: affordability and quality. The base configuration starts at just $700, even though it comes with a high-resolution display that's both bright and color-accurate. It also includes an excellent trackpad, a responsive keyboard, and 512 GB of storage.
戴尔XPS 13（评分8/10，WIRED推荐）回归了。它依然极其小巧便携，这始终是它的招牌特色。其小巧的尺寸使其成为在飞机上工作的理想笔记本电脑，仅2.2磅（约1公斤）的重量让你在背包里几乎感觉不到它的存在。但这一次，它全新的设计聚焦于两点：性价比与品质。基础配置起售价仅为700美元，却配备了一块既明亮又色彩精准的高分辨率显示屏。它还包含出色的触控板、响应灵敏的键盘以及512 GB的存储空间。

The only catch is that the $700 model comes with only 8 GB of RAM. So here's what I recommend instead: spend the extra $200 to upgrade to 16 GB of RAM. It has all the same specs otherwise and is still an absolutely killer laptop for $900. The 16 GB of memory will help you avoid snags while multitasking, which matters if you sit in front of a computer all day for work. I tested the 8 GB model myself—trust me, you want the 16 GB.
唯一的问题是，700美元的型号仅配备8 GB内存。因此，我的建议是：多花200美元升级到16 GB内存。除内存外，其他规格完全相同，900美元的价格依然让它成为一款极具杀伤力的笔记本电脑。16 GB内存能帮你避免多任务处理时的卡顿，如果你整天坐在电脑前工作，这一点至关重要。我亲自测试了8 GB版本——相信我，你需要16 GB。

### The Best Cheap Windows Laptop: HP OmniBook 5
### 最佳高性价比Windows笔记本电脑：HP OmniBook 5

If $900 for the Dell XPS 13 is out of your budget, I strongly recommend the HP OmniBook 5 14. You have to be very careful when shopping in the budget-level price tier, which is anything under $750. Lots of junk creeps in that no one should buy. But the OmniBook 5 14 stands out in two important ways: its OLED display and its portability. An OLED display on a laptop at this price is almost unheard of, and it's definitely the device's highlight.
如果900美元的戴尔XPS 13超出了你的预算，我强烈推荐HP OmniBook 5 14。在750美元以下的入门级价位选购时，你必须非常谨慎，因为其中充斥着大量不值得购买的劣质产品。但OmniBook 5 14在两个重要方面脱颖而出：OLED显示屏和便携性。在这个价位的笔记本电脑上配备OLED屏幕几乎闻所未闻，这绝对是该设备的最大亮点。

Then there's the HP's overall build quality and portability. It just doesn't feel like most laptops in this price range. It's well-built, yes, but it's also incredibly thin at just half an inch, which matches both the Dell XPS 13 and the MacBook Neo. The design language is nice too, featuring soft, rounded corners, small bezels, and modern branding.
此外，惠普的整体制造工艺和便携性也值得称道。它给人的感觉完全不像这个价位的大多数笔记本电脑。它不仅做工精良，而且极其轻薄，厚度仅为0.5英寸（约1.27厘米），与戴尔XPS 13和MacBook Neo不相上下。其设计语言也很出色，采用了柔和的圆角、窄边框以及现代化的品牌标识。

### The Best 15-Inch Windows Laptop: Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5x
### 最佳15英寸Windows笔记本电脑：Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5x

My top picks for premium, affordable Windows laptops are often 13-inch or 14-inch. But many people want a larger screen, and one strength of the Windows ecosystem is its diversity and choice. Bigger laptop displays often cost more, but not the Lenovo IdeaPad Slim 5x.
我首选的高端且实惠的Windows笔记本电脑通常是13英寸或14英寸的。但许多人需要更大的屏幕，而Windows生态系统的一大优势就是其多样性和选择性。更大的笔记本屏幕通常价格更高，但联想IdeaPad Slim 5x却并非如此。
