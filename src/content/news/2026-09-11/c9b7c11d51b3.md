# Evidence-Order Calibration for Selective Visual Reasoning under Progressive Loss of Question-Critical Evidence
# 针对问题关键证据渐进式丢失的选择性视觉推理的证据顺序校准

**Abstract:** Vision-language model (VLM) confidence may change in aggregate when visual evidence is degraded while remaining structurally inconsistent within individual examples. We study answer-level reliability along five-step, question-conditioned evidence-loss trajectories. Using a frozen Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct model, we construct 176 accepted GQA-derived trajectories (880 masking conditions) by progressively masking scene-graph-localized question-critical regions.

**摘要：** 当视觉证据退化时，视觉语言模型（VLM）的置信度在总体上可能会发生变化，但在单个样本内部却保持结构上的不一致。我们研究了沿五步、以问题为条件的证据丢失轨迹下的答案级可靠性。通过使用冻结的 Qwen2.5-VL-3B-Instruct 模型，我们通过对场景图定位的问题关键区域进行渐进式遮蔽，构建了 176 条被采纳的 GQA 衍生轨迹（共 880 种遮蔽条件）。

Native sequence confidence has an evidence monotonicity violation rate (EMVR) of 0.436, and 92.0% of trajectories contain at least one adjacent violation. A matched non-critical-region control shows that full critical masking reduces accuracy by 28.2 percentage points, compared with 0.6 points for equally sized non-critical masks; the paired difference is 27.6 points (95% CI [20.0, 34.7]).

原生序列置信度的证据单调性违规率（EMVR）为 0.436，且 92.0% 的轨迹至少包含一次相邻违规。一项匹配的非关键区域对照实验显示，完全遮蔽关键区域会导致准确率下降 28.2 个百分点，而同样大小的非关键区域遮蔽仅导致 0.6 个百分点的下降；配对差异为 27.6 个百分点（95% 置信区间 [20.0, 34.7]）。

We train a lightweight post-hoc reliability head on frozen hidden states, sequence confidence, and entropy. Adding evidence-order supervision to binary cross-entropy (BCE) reduces masking EMVR from 0.330 to 0.303 (paired difference -0.027, 95% CI [-0.044, -0.010]). The same mask-trained objective reduces EMVR from 0.449 to 0.402 on held-out question IDs under unseen local Gaussian blur (difference -0.0468, 95% CI [-0.0739, -0.0199]).

我们在冻结的隐藏状态、序列置信度和熵的基础上，训练了一个轻量级的事后可靠性头（post-hoc reliability head）。在二元交叉熵（BCE）中加入证据顺序监督，将遮蔽 EMVR 从 0.330 降低至 0.303（配对差异 -0.027，95% 置信区间 [-0.044, -0.010]）。在未见过的局部高斯模糊条件下，相同的遮蔽训练目标将保留问题 ID 上的 EMVR 从 0.449 降低至 0.402（差异 -0.0468，95% 置信区间 [-0.0739, -0.0199]）。

AUROC, Brier, and AURC differences between the two learned heads are statistically inconclusive, and native confidence remains stronger for selective-risk ranking. The results separate evidence-order consistency from conventional correctness discrimination rather than establishing generic confidence superiority.

两个学习头之间的 AUROC、Brier 和 AURC 差异在统计学上尚无定论，且原生置信度在选择性风险排序方面仍然表现更强。这些结果将证据顺序的一致性与传统的正确性判别区分开来，而非确立了某种通用的置信度优势。
