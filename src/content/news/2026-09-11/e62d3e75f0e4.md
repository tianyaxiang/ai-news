# List of references on Sony websites to players "owning" their digital games
# 关于索尼网站上提及玩家“拥有”数字游戏的相关参考列表

From Consumer Rights Wiki: Four California PlayStation buyers who each spent hundreds of dollars on digital goods over the past few years say they received only a limited, revocable license rather than ownership, and paid more than they otherwise would have.[1]
根据消费者权益维基（Consumer Rights Wiki）的消息：四名加利福尼亚州的 PlayStation 用户在过去几年中花费数百美元购买数字商品，他们表示自己获得的仅是有限且可撤销的许可，而非所有权，且为此支付了高于预期的费用。[1]

The complaint alleges the PlayStation Store advertises digital games with ownership language such as "Buy Now" & "Confirm Purchase," and that the brief license disclosure above the "Confirm Purchase" button appears in comparatively small, unhighlighted text, short of the clear and conspicuous disclosure California law requires.[1]
诉状称，PlayStation Store 在宣传数字游戏时使用了诸如“立即购买”（Buy Now）和“确认购买”（Confirm Purchase）等暗示所有权的措辞；此外，“确认购买”按钮上方的简短许可披露信息字体较小且未加高亮，未能达到加州法律所要求的清晰且显著的披露标准。[1]

On June 18, 2026, Andrew Garcia, Edward Heycock, Jason Mendoza & John Salinas sued Sony Interactive Entertainment, LLC in the U.S. District Court for the Northern District of California, Case No. 3:26-cv-06016, alleging the storefront's checkout language violates California's digital-goods transparency law.[1][2]
2026年6月18日，Andrew Garcia、Edward Heycock、Jason Mendoza 和 John Salinas 在美国加利福尼亚北区联邦地区法院起诉索尼互动娱乐有限公司（Sony Interactive Entertainment, LLC），案件编号为 3:26-cv-06016，指控该商店的结账用语违反了加州的数字商品透明度法案。[1][2]

On August 21, 2026, Sony moved to compel individual arbitration under the PlayStation Terms of Service and, in the alternative, to dismiss the complaint with prejudice.[3][4] Under a heading reading "Reasonable Consumers Would Not Be Misled," Sony argued that it is not plausible that reasonable consumers believed they were obtaining ownership of a digital game.[3] The case remains active, with a hearing scheduled for October 1, 2026.[5][3]
2026年8月21日，索尼根据《PlayStation 服务条款》申请强制进行个人仲裁，并要求驳回该诉讼且不得再次起诉。[3][4] 在题为“理性消费者不会被误导”的章节中，索尼辩称，理性消费者认为自己获得了数字游戏所有权是不合理的。[3] 目前该案件仍在审理中，听证会定于2026年10月1日举行。[5][3]

### Background
### 背景

The complaint alleges the PlayStation Store markets digital games to shoppers with ownership language.[1] When a consumer proceeds to checkout, the store displays a confirmation screen with a button labeled "Confirm Purchase," and selecting it finalizes the transaction & charges the payment method.[1] Above that button, PlayStation includes a brief disclosure referencing the Software Product License Agreement, stating that the consumer's purchase of the digital product amounts to a license subject to that agreement.[1]
诉状称，PlayStation Store 在向消费者推销数字游戏时使用了所有权相关的措辞。[1] 当消费者进入结账页面时，商店会显示一个带有“确认购买”按钮的确认界面，点击该按钮即完成交易并扣款。[1] 在该按钮上方，PlayStation 包含了一段简短的披露信息，引用了《软件产品许可协议》，声明消费者购买数字产品仅等同于获得受该协议约束的许可。[1]

The complaint says the disclosure appears in comparatively small text relative to other portions of the interface & is not visually highlighted or otherwise designed to draw the consumer's attention.[1] The transaction grants a limited, non-exclusive, revocable license governed by Sony's Software Product License Agreement, which the complaint says "expressly provides that the software is 'licensed to you, not sold.'"[1]
诉状指出，该披露信息相对于界面其他部分而言字体较小，且未在视觉上进行高亮处理，也未采取其他旨在引起消费者注意的设计。[1] 该交易授予的是一种受索尼《软件产品许可协议》管辖的有限、非排他性且可撤销的许可，诉状称该协议“明确规定软件是‘许可给您使用，而非出售’”。[1]

KitGuru, reporting on Sony's fiscal earnings report as collated by Push Square, said 85% of game sales in the quarter ending March 31, 2026 were digital, with 15% physical.[6] On July 1, 2026, Sony announced it would end physical disc production for new PlayStation games starting January 2028, moving new releases to digital distribution.[7] Fortune noted that Sony once championed the sharing of physical media and now argues that customers do not own the digital games they purchase.[8]
据 KitGuru 援引 Push Square 整理的索尼财报显示，截至2026年3月31日的季度中，85%的游戏销售额来自数字版，15%来自实体版。[6] 2026年7月1日，索尼宣布将从2028年1月起停止生产新的 PlayStation 实体光盘游戏，将新发布的游戏全面转向数字发行。[7] 《财富》杂志指出，索尼曾一度支持实体媒体的共享，如今却辩称客户并不拥有他们购买的数字游戏。[8]

### Class action complaint
### 集体诉讼诉状

The complaint alleges that the four named plaintiffs are California residents who have each spent hundreds of dollars on PlayStation digital goods over the past few years.[1] Each plaintiff states he was not aware the games were only a revocable license, and that the belief he was buying ownership led him to pay more than he otherwise would have.[1]
诉状称，四名原告均为加州居民，过去几年中每人都在 PlayStation 数字商品上花费了数百美元。[1] 每位原告均表示，他们此前并不知道这些游戏仅是可撤销的许可，正是因为误以为自己购买的是所有权，才导致他们支付了高于预期的费用。[1]

The plaintiffs voluntarily dismissed Sony Corporation of America on August 20, one day before Sony's motion, leaving Sony Interactive Entertainment, LLC as the sole defendant.[5] Filed by Noah Heinz & Albert Pak of Pak Heinz PLLC, the complaint brings three counts.[1]
原告于8月20日（即索尼提交动议的前一天）自愿撤销了对索尼美国公司（Sony Corporation of America）的起诉，索尼互动娱乐有限公司成为唯一被告。[5] 该诉状由 Pak Heinz PLLC 律师事务所的 Noah Heinz 和 Albert Pak 提交，共包含三项指控。[1]

Count I alleges a violation of California Business and Professions Code § 17500.6, the operative section of AB 2426.[1][9] Count II alleges a violation of California's False Advertising Law, Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code § 17500 et seq. Count III alleges a violation of the California Consumer Legal Remedies Act, Cal. Civ. Code § 1750 et seq., citing subsections 1770(a)(5), (a)(9) & (a)(14).[1]
第一项指控涉及违反《加州商业和职业法典》第 17500.6 条（即 AB 2426 法案的执行条款）。[1][9] 第二项指控涉及违反《加州虚假广告法》（Cal. Bus. & Prof. Code § 1750 et seq.）。第三项指控涉及违反《加州消费者法律救济法》（Cal. Civ. Code § 1750 et seq.），引用了第 1770(a)(5)、(a)(9) 和 (a)(14) 款。[1]

The proposed class covers California residents who purchased digital video games through the PlayStation Store after § 17500.6's effective date, who saw representations such as "Buy," "Buy Now," or "Confirm Purchase," but who received only a revocable license to a digital good.[1] The complaint invokes federal jurisdiction under the Class Action Fairness Act, stating the aggregate amount in controversy exceeds $5,000,000.[1]
拟议的集体诉讼涵盖了在第 17500.6 条生效后通过 PlayStation Store 购买数字视频游戏、看到了“购买”、“立即购买”或“确认购买”等表述，但实际仅获得数字商品可撤销许可的加州居民。[1] 诉状根据《集体诉讼公平法》援引联邦管辖权，称争议总金额超过 500 万美元。[1]

The prayer for relief seeks actual damages, restitution & disgorgement of Sony's revenues from the practice, statutory & actual damages under Cal. Civ. Code § 1780(a), an injunction prohibiting Sony from resuming its unlawful practices, and reasonable attorneys' fees & costs.[1]
诉讼请求包括实际损害赔偿、退款及追缴索尼通过该行为获得的收入、根据《加州民法典》第 1780(a) 条规定的法定及实际损害赔偿、禁止索尼继续从事非法行为的禁令，以及合理的律师费和诉讼费用。[1]
