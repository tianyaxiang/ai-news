# AgenticGen: Reward-Guided Agentic Video Generation for Advertising
# AgenticGen：面向广告的奖励引导式智能体视频生成

**Abstract:** Advertising video generation is not only a video synthesis task, but also a product-conditioned reasoning problem whose success is measured by online business metrics. Recent video foundation models can generate realistic clips from multimodal conditions, yet they do not optimize how a product should be transformed into an effective advertisement or how future generation should be improved from online business feedback.

**摘要：** 广告视频生成不仅是一项视频合成任务，更是一个以产品为条件的推理问题，其成功与否取决于在线业务指标。尽管近期的视频基础模型能够根据多模态条件生成逼真的片段，但它们并未针对“如何将产品转化为有效的广告”或“如何利用在线业务反馈改进未来生成”进行优化。

To close this loop, we propose AgenticGen, a reward-guided agentic framework that decomposes advertising video generation into two trainable reasoning stages, strategy selection and draft generation, thereby exposing optimization targets that online business feedback can supervise. AgenticGen learns a performance-based reward from accumulated online feedback and a complementary rubric-based reward aligned with human quality standards, then uses them to supervise policy optimization.

为了闭环这一过程，我们提出了 AgenticGen，这是一个奖励引导的智能体框架。它将广告视频生成分解为两个可训练的推理阶段：策略选择和草稿生成，从而明确了可由在线业务反馈进行监督的优化目标。AgenticGen 从积累的在线反馈中学习基于性能的奖励，并结合符合人类质量标准的补充性准则奖励，进而利用这些奖励来监督策略优化。

DPO first moves the agentic policies toward online preferences, and GRPO further refines both stages with process and outcome rewards. Offline experiments validate the reward models and successive policy optimization. Online A/B experiments in the TikTok advertising system show that AgenticGen after DPO and GRPO improves CTR by 2.72%, CVR by 2.63%, and Advv by 9.61% over the SFT baseline.

DPO（直接偏好优化）首先将智能体策略导向在线偏好，而 GRPO（组相对策略优化）则通过过程奖励和结果奖励进一步优化了这两个阶段。离线实验验证了奖励模型和后续策略优化的有效性。在 TikTok 广告系统中的在线 A/B 测试表明，经过 DPO 和 GRPO 优化后的 AgenticGen 相比 SFT（监督微调）基线，CTR（点击率）提升了 2.72%，CVR（转化率）提升了 2.63%，Advv（广告价值）提升了 9.61%。
