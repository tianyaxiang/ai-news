# Gleam Gathering 2027

The Gathering is back and in London. We'll have an: Exciting schedule of talks from members of the friendliest community in programming. Lunch, coffee, tea and snacks and stickers. Plenty of time to mingle in the hallway track.
Gleam Gathering 回归了，地点就在伦敦。我们将带来：来自编程界最友好社区成员的精彩演讲日程。现场提供午餐、咖啡、茶、零食和贴纸，并有充足的时间在会场走廊进行交流。

Make a weekend of it: Friday: Get to know the community, we will reserve a space for the evening where you can buy a beverage and chat. Saturday: The main event. Sunday: Chill get together, we're ironing out the details on a space to hack.
让它成为一个充实的周末：周五：结识社区成员，我们将预留一个场地，供大家晚上买杯饮料畅聊。周六：主活动日。周日：轻松聚会，我们正在敲定一个供大家进行编程实践（Hack）的场地细节。

Speakers & Panellists: Louis Pilfold, Creator of the Gleam Language. You? See our call for talks.
演讲嘉宾与小组成员：Louis Pilfold，Gleam 语言创始人。还有你吗？请查看我们的演讲征集。

Venue: The 2027 event will be in the center of London at dedicated event space 44 Hallam St. Visit their directions page for detailed information on getting there.
场地：2027 年的活动将在伦敦市中心的专业活动场地 44 Hallam St 举行。请访问他们的路线页面，获取详细的交通信息。

🎤 Call for talks: We’re now accepting talk proposals for Gleam Gathering 2027. Whether you’re an experienced speaker or new to presenting, we want to hear your ideas. This is your chance to contribute to the community, share knowledge, and inspire others.
🎤 演讲征集：我们现正接受 Gleam Gathering 2027 的演讲提案。无论您是经验丰富的演讲者还是初次登台，我们都期待听到您的想法。这是您为社区做出贡献、分享知识并激励他人的绝佳机会。

Gleam welcomes everyone and we want to ensure this is continued in the GG27. As this is a dedicated Gleam conference, we are targeting more technical, advanced talks. However, we want everyone to feel included, whether you're an old hand with Gleam or a newcomer. We will ensure that we have a section in the agenda for lightning talks, so if you think your talk would be better suited to that structure, you'll have the opportunity to sign up on the day.
Gleam 欢迎所有人，我们希望在 GG27 中延续这一传统。由于这是一场专门的 Gleam 会议，我们的目标是更具技术性和深度的演讲。但我们希望每个人都能有参与感，无论您是 Gleam 的老手还是新手。我们将在议程中安排闪电演讲环节，如果您认为您的演讲更适合这种形式，当天将有机会报名参加。

What We’re Looking For: We welcome talks that are: Sounds obvious, but about Gleam. About Gleam in production – what’s working, what’s not, what have you discovered? Using Gleam to solve real world problems. Pushing the use of Gleam to the extreme – what novel approaches have you taken?
我们寻找的内容：我们欢迎以下演讲：显而易见，必须关于 Gleam。关于 Gleam 的生产环境应用——哪些有效，哪些无效，您发现了什么？使用 Gleam 解决现实世界的问题。将 Gleam 的使用推向极致——您采取了哪些新颖的方法？

What we aren’t looking for this time: Product/language update (we will have this covered, and we want your talks to demonstrate real innovation within the community). Beginner demos – we absolutely welcome Gleam novices, but we will be diving into more detail in the long form talks. Please consider a lightning talk format if you’d love to share anything on this topic. Keynotes (we are really fortunate to already have this one sorted).
我们本次不寻找的内容：产品/语言更新（我们会涵盖这些内容，我们希望您的演讲能展示社区内的真正创新）。初学者演示——我们绝对欢迎 Gleam 新手，但长篇演讲将深入探讨更多细节。如果您想分享此类主题，请考虑闪电演讲形式。主题演讲（我们非常幸运已经安排好了）。

Formats accepted: Talks of either: 30mins (20-25 min presentation with 5-10 min Q&A session), 45mins (30-35 min presentation with 10-15 min Q&A session).
接受的格式：演讲时长为：30 分钟（20-25 分钟演示，5-10 分钟问答），或 45 分钟（30-35 分钟演示，10-15 分钟问答）。

Organisers' Responsibilities: We’re committed to supporting every speaker who submits a proposal. Here’s what you can expect from us: Fair Review Process: All submissions will be reviewed by an anonymous committee of experienced speakers and Gleam users. Clear Communication: You’ll hear back from us whether your talk is accepted or not. Respect for Your Work: Your content remains your intellectual property. However, if you are selected as a speaker, we will be recording all sessions and making them available after the event.
组织者职责：我们致力于支持每一位提交提案的演讲者。您可以期待我们做到：公平的评审流程：所有提交的内容将由一个由资深演讲者和 Gleam 用户组成的匿名委员会进行评审。清晰的沟通：无论您的演讲是否被录用，您都会收到我们的回复。尊重您的工作：您的内容仍属于您的知识产权。但如果您被选为演讲者，我们将录制所有会议并在活动结束后发布。

Key Dates: Deadline for submissions: Midnight UK time, 14 October 2026. Notifications sent: 01 November 2026. Event date: Saturday 27 February 2027.
关键日期：提交截止日期：英国时间 2026 年 10 月 14 日午夜。通知发送日期：2026 年 11 月 1 日。活动日期：2027 年 2 月 27 日，星期六。
