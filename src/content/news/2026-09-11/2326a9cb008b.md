# HB-PVI: A Hierarchical Bayesian Personalization and Value-of-Information Framework for Complex Activity Recognition
# HB-PVI：用于复杂活动识别的分层贝叶斯个性化与信息价值框架

**Abstract:** Personalization can improve activity-recognition performance, but participant-specific gains are heterogeneous, and every additional calibration label has an acquisition cost. This study presents HB-PVI, a hierarchical Bayesian personalization and value-of-information framework jointly modeling participant heterogeneity, the benefit and harm of four personalization mechanisms, and the economic value of an additional label, for the 47-participant MUSIC-CAR complex-activity cohort.

**摘要：** 个性化可以提高活动识别的性能，但针对特定参与者的收益存在异质性，且每一个额外的校准标签都伴随着获取成本。本研究提出了 HB-PVI，这是一个分层贝叶斯个性化与信息价值框架，旨在为包含 47 名参与者的 MUSIC-CAR 复杂活动队列建模，综合考虑了参与者异质性、四种个性化机制的利弊，以及额外标签的经济价值。

A leakage-safe, leave-one-participant-out evaluation combines a sequential-Monte-Carlo participant-effect updater with a Student-$t$ hierarchical gain model and a one-step expected-value-of-sample-information (EVSI) stopping rule. Adapter personalization produced small positive mean F1 gains, growing from 0.00099 at one label to 0.00198 at ten, while adapter-plus-head and prototype-residual personalization were negative on average.

一种防泄漏的“留一法”（leave-one-participant-out）评估方法结合了序贯蒙特卡洛参与者效应更新器、Student-$t$ 分层增益模型以及一步预期样本信息价值（EVSI）停止规则。适配器（Adapter）个性化产生了微小的正向平均 F1 增益，从单标签时的 0.00099 增长到十标签时的 0.00198，而适配器加头部（adapter-plus-head）和原型残差（prototype-residual）个性化在平均水平上表现为负值。

Under the primary practical-benefit threshold ($\Delta_{\min}=0.01$) and cost setting, one-step EVSI was zero at every decision state, so the policy purchased no labels and retained population inference for all 47 participants, matching always-stop exactly (region-of-practical-equivalence probability $=1$). Relative to fixed ten-shot adapter personalization, this reduced labeling by 100\% while keeping the posterior mean F1 loss at 0.00217 (95\% credible interval, 0.00048 to 0.00389), with posterior probability 0.9992 of remaining below the 0.005 tolerance.

在主要的实际收益阈值（$\Delta_{\min}=0.01$）和成本设置下，一步 EVSI 在每个决策状态下均为零，因此该策略未购买任何标签，并对所有 47 名参与者保留了群体推断，与“始终停止”策略完全匹配（实际等效区域概率 $=1$）。与固定的十样本（ten-shot）适配器个性化相比，该方法将标注量减少了 100%，同时将后验平均 F1 损失保持在 0.00217（95% 可信区间为 0.00048 至 0.00389），且保持在 0.005 容差以下的后验概率为 0.9992。

HB-PVI was utility-optimal in 199 of 216 cost-threshold settings and in every setting at or above the primary label cost. These results argue for a population-first deployment policy whenever personalization gains are small relative to labeling, computation, and harm costs, and show that value-of-information reasoning, not raw predictive accuracy, should drive personalization decisions in health-sensing applications.

HB-PVI 在 216 种成本阈值设置中的 199 种以及所有等于或高于主要标签成本的设置中均实现了效用最优。这些结果表明，当个性化收益相对于标注、计算和危害成本较小时，应优先采用“群体优先”的部署策略；同时也证明了在健康感知应用中，驱动个性化决策的应该是信息价值推理，而非单纯的预测准确率。
