# The iPhone Duo’s hardware doesn’t look special, but its software might be
# iPhone Duo 的硬件看起来并不出众，但其软件或许能带来惊喜

With the iPhone Duo, Apple has pulled off a familiar trick. It arrives into a mature Android foldable market with a handful of hardware features we’ve mostly already seen elsewhere, but paired with a level of software polish that makes a lot of them feel new again. It’s those software flourishes that have me excited to try the iPhone Duo when it launches next month, but I’m less convinced that its hardware will be anything special.

苹果凭借 iPhone Duo 再次施展了其熟悉的“拿手好戏”。它进入了一个已经成熟的安卓折叠屏市场，虽然其硬件功能大多已在其他产品中出现过，但配合苹果精湛的软件打磨，让这些功能焕然一新。正是这些软件层面的巧思让我对下个月发布的 iPhone Duo 充满期待，但我对它的硬件是否具备独特性持保留意见。

If Apple had gotten there first, the Duo’s biggest surprise would have been its squat, wide, passport-inspired shape. But its slow-moving supply chain meant that Huawei, Samsung, and Xiaomi all managed to beat Apple to market with similarly sized rivals, so the unusual design already feels fairly familiar — helped by Samsung’s aggressive efforts to market its new design.

如果苹果是第一个推出此类产品的厂商，Duo 最令人惊喜的莫过于其矮宽、类似护照的造型。但由于苹果供应链的缓慢，华为、三星和小米都已抢先推出了尺寸相近的竞品，因此这种独特的设计已经让人感到相当熟悉——三星对其新设计的积极推广也加深了这种印象。

More has been made of Apple’s efforts to eliminate the foldable display’s crease, which seem to have been largely successful. Apple attributes it to a 100-component hinge, custom adhesives that allow the display layers to “glide” over one another, and a matte finish that reduces glare and reflections to make the crease harder to spot. Trying the phone at Apple’s event yesterday, Verge editor-in-chief Nilay Patel said that you can’t really see the crease, but that “if you go looking for it, you can definitely feel one.”

苹果在消除折叠屏折痕方面的努力备受关注，且似乎已大获成功。苹果将其归功于由 100 个组件组成的铰链、允许显示层相互“滑动”的定制粘合剂，以及能减少眩光和反射、使折痕更难察觉的哑光涂层。在昨天的苹果发布会上体验该手机时，《The Verge》主编 Nilay Patel 表示，你几乎看不到折痕，但“如果你刻意去寻找，确实能感觉到它的存在。”

It sounds, and looks, impressive, but Apple is far from the first to do it. Samsung made great strides in eliminating the crease on its Z Fold 8 and Z Fold 8 Ultra thanks to new titanium display layers. Before that, Oppo all but eliminated its Find N6 crease by using 3D printing to fill the minute gaps across the hinge. I’d have to see all those phones side by side to see which crease is subtlest, but even if Apple does win, it won’t be by much.

这听起来和看起来都很令人印象深刻，但苹果绝非首创。得益于全新的钛金属显示层，三星在 Z Fold 8 和 Z Fold 8 Ultra 上在消除折痕方面取得了巨大进步。在此之前，OPPO 通过 3D 打印技术填充铰链处的微小缝隙，几乎消除了 Find N6 的折痕。我必须将这些手机放在一起对比才能看出谁的折痕最不明显，但即便苹果胜出，优势也不会太大。

None of the iPhone Duo’s other standout hardware features are true firsts either. Google beat it to an IP68 rating, now even surpassed by Honor’s IP69. Samsung, Oppo, and Honor have all offered stylus support before — though Samsung has since dropped it. Samsung also moved on from the under-display selfie camera, which has always been hamstrung by poor photo and video quality and a distractingly visible patch on the screen.

iPhone Duo 的其他硬件亮点也并非首创。谷歌在 IP68 防护等级上领先于它，而荣耀甚至已经达到了 IP69。三星、OPPO 和荣耀此前都提供过手写笔支持——尽管三星后来放弃了这一功能。三星还放弃了屏下自拍摄像头，因为该技术一直受限于较差的拍照和视频质量，且屏幕上那块显眼的区域非常干扰视觉。

In other ways, the hardware even feels behind the competition. The Duo is the thickest of this new wave of wide foldables, though at 5.2mm when open it’s still thinner than the iPhone Air. The weight is a more obvious issue: The 254g Duo is 26 percent heavier than the Z Fold 8 — a 53g difference — which is going to be pretty noticeable. You have to go back to 2022’s Z Fold 4 to find a Samsung foldable that’s heavier, TriFold excluded.

在其他方面，其硬件甚至感觉落后于竞争对手。Duo 是这波新款宽屏折叠手机中最厚的一款，尽管展开后 5.2 毫米的厚度仍比 iPhone Air 更薄。重量是一个更明显的问题：254 克的 Duo 比 Z Fold 8 重了 26%（相差 53 克），这在手感上会非常明显。除了三折叠机型外，你得追溯到 2022 年的 Z Fold 4 才能找到比它更重的三星折叠屏手机。

There’s also no evidence that Apple has adopted silicon-carbon batteries yet, which have allowed Samsung, Motorola, and other Android manufacturers to fit bigger capacities into the smaller spaces of foldable phones. Apple says the phone should still deliver a full 24 hours of regular use, but Honor’s Magic V6 uses silicon-carbon to deliver two days of life.

目前也没有证据表明苹果采用了硅碳电池，而这种电池已让三星、摩托罗拉和其他安卓厂商能够在折叠屏手机有限的空间内塞入更大的电池容量。苹果表示该手机仍能提供 24 小时的常规使用时间，但荣耀 Magic V6 通过硅碳电池技术可以实现两天的续航。

The rear cameras are pretty basic too, with a pair of 48-megapixel shooters and no telephoto. That’s almost an exact match for the cameras on the Z Fold 8, but Google, Motorola, Xiaomi and others are now providing capable triple cameras across their foldable lines, and the iPhone Duo already lags behind.

后置摄像头也相当基础，配备了一对 4800 万像素的镜头，且没有长焦镜头。这与 Z Fold 8 的摄像头配置几乎完全一致，但谷歌、摩托罗拉、小米等厂商目前在其折叠屏产品线上都提供了功能强大的三摄系统，iPhone Duo 在这一点上已经落后了。

None of this is to say that the Duo looks like a bad piece of hardware. Every foldable phone is a compromise, and Apple has picked a similar set of compromises to Samsung in order to get this phone out the door at $1,999. It looks as good as any Android foldable on paper, but it doesn’t look better than many of them.

这并不是说 Duo 的硬件很糟糕。每一款折叠屏手机都是一种妥协，苹果为了以 1,999 美元的价格推出这款手机，选择了与三星类似的妥协方案。从纸面参数上看，它看起来和任何安卓折叠屏手机一样好，但并没有比其中许多产品更出色。

Where that might change is the software. Google has made an effort to make Android more flexible and adaptable to suit folding phones, while OEMs deliver custom solutions to push things further, but none have rewritten the rulebook quite like Apple.

软件方面的情况可能会有所不同。谷歌一直在努力让安卓系统变得更加灵活，以适应折叠屏手机，而各手机厂商也提供了定制化方案来进一步优化体验，但没有哪家公司像苹果这样彻底改写了规则。

The version of iOS 27 running on the iPhone Duo isn’t the same as on other iPhones, but it doesn’t match the iPad either. The biggest change takes the dock from the bottom of the display and lays it out vertically along the right-hand side. The Dynamic Island is turned vertical and thrown in here too, while the status bar collapses into a small circular icon in the top-right corner. This big change is bolstered by clever little touches throughout, like a dynamic blur effect that shifts as you open the screen, creating a fluidity no Android foldable has so far matched. It’s a total transformation of the iOS layout that specifically has foldables in mind.

运行在 iPhone Duo 上的 iOS 27 版本与其他 iPhone 不同，但也与 iPad 版本不尽相同。最大的变化是将底部的 Dock 栏移到了屏幕右侧，并改为垂直布局。灵动岛也被垂直化并整合其中，而状态栏则收缩为右上角的一个小圆形图标。这一重大变化辅以贯穿始终的巧妙细节，例如当你展开屏幕时会发生位移的动态模糊效果，创造出目前没有任何安卓折叠屏手机能比拟的流畅感。这是针对折叠屏设备对 iOS 布局进行的彻底改造。

The point of all that change is to ensure consistency between the outer and inner screens. Open the iPhone Duo on the homescreen and almost everything will stay in place, making the move between screens feel seamless. That effect won’t hold in full-screen apps of course, which will adapt to fill the space, but that right-hand UI rail will be fixed.

所有这些改变的目的都是为了确保内外屏之间的一致性。在主屏幕上打开 iPhone Duo，几乎所有内容都会保持原位，使内外屏切换感觉天衣无缝。当然，这种效果在全屏应用中不会保持，因为应用会适配以填满空间，但右侧的 UI 栏将保持固定。
