# Video-MOPD: Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation for Video Understanding

**Title:** Video-MOPD: Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation for Video Understanding
**标题：** Video-MOPD：用于视频理解的多教师在线策略蒸馏

**Abstract:** Video understanding demands a convergence of complementary capabilities across perception, temporal understanding, and complex reasoning, which are difficult to jointly optimize within a single model.
**摘要：** 视频理解需要感知、时间理解和复杂推理等互补能力的融合，而这些能力很难在单一模型中进行联合优化。

We introduce Video-MOPD-8B, an open-weight model dedicated to video understanding tasks. To fundamentally enhance its capabilities, we conduct targeted reinforcement learning (RL) optimization across three core domains: video temporal grounding (VTG), general video comprehension, and video STEM reasoning.
我们推出了 Video-MOPD-8B，这是一个专用于视频理解任务的开源权重模型。为了从根本上增强其能力，我们在三个核心领域进行了针对性的强化学习（RL）优化：视频时间定位（VTG）、通用视频理解以及视频 STEM 推理。

We then unify their complementary capabilities via Multi-Teacher On-Policy Distillation (MOPD), which consolidates expert knowledge by supervising student-generated trajectories with routed teacher feedback.
随后，我们通过多教师在线策略蒸馏（MOPD）统一了这些互补能力，该方法通过路由教师反馈来监督学生生成的轨迹，从而整合专家知识。

We further introduce Reliability-Aware Informative Sampling (RAIS), which selects examples with consistently reliable teacher supervision and large teacher-student performance gaps.
我们进一步引入了可靠性感知信息采样（RAIS），该方法能够筛选出具有持续可靠的教师监督以及较大师生性能差距的样本。

Together, these components enable Video-MOPD-8B to achieve coordinated and comprehensive performance gains across diverse video understanding tasks.
这些组件共同使 Video-MOPD-8B 能够在各种视频理解任务中实现协调且全面的性能提升。

Extensive experiments on comprehensive benchmarks covering general video understanding, temporal grounding, video reasoning, and video STEM tasks demonstrate that Video-MOPD-8B achieves state-of-the-art performance among existing models at a comparable scale.
在涵盖通用视频理解、时间定位、视频推理和视频 STEM 任务的综合基准测试上的大量实验表明，Video-MOPD-8B 在同等规模的现有模型中达到了最先进的性能水平。
