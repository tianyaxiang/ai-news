**What per-feature pricing changes**
**按功能定价带来的改变**

Two things. First, the product has to earn each dollar on its own. If monitoring is not worth $3 to you, you do not buy it, and I find out. A tier hides which features people actually value. A price list does not. Second, the upgrade moment is yours. You add a capability when you want it, not when you hit a wall that was placed in your way.

主要有两点改变。首先，产品必须靠自身价值赚取每一美元。如果监控功能对你来说不值 3 美元，你就不会购买，我也会因此获知反馈。套餐制会掩盖用户真正看重的功能，而价目表则不会。其次，升级的主动权在你手中。你可以在需要时添加功能，而不是在遇到人为设置的障碍时才被迫升级。

**The part that matters more than the price**
**比价格更重要的部分**

A status page is worth nothing on the day your site is down if the status page goes down with it. InBrief's outage continuity is a separate page, served independently of your main site, that visitors see when your site cannot answer. It is the feature I would keep if I could keep only one, and it costs $3.

如果你的网站宕机时状态页面也随之瘫痪，那么这个状态页面就毫无意义。InBrief 的“宕机持续性保障”是一个独立页面，与你的主站分开托管，当你的网站无法响应时，访客依然能看到它。如果我只能保留一个功能，那一定是这个，它的价格是 3 美元。

The other thing I insisted on is that one incident update reaches everyone. You write it once. It goes to the status page, to email, to RSS, to every chat channel you connected, and into every language you publish. Writing the same update four times during an outage is how updates stop being written.

我坚持的另一件事是：一次事故更新必须触达所有人。你只需写一次，它就会自动同步到状态页面、电子邮件、RSS、你连接的所有聊天频道，以及你发布的所有语言版本中。在宕机期间重复写四次同样的更新，只会导致更新工作最终被放弃。
