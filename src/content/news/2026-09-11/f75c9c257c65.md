### Don't let anyone take away your big box of cables
### 别让任何人拿走你的“线材大宝箱”

Speaking of being on the internet and finding things that make you go, “Hey! It’s not just me!” I scrolled across this skeet from Tyler Gaw: I just dug to the bottom of my Big Box of Cables to find two cables that I needed for something. They've been in the bottom for 10+ years. So, "when are you ever gonna use these?" was today. Don't ever let anyone take your Big Box of Cables away.

说到在网上冲浪时发现那些让你感叹“嘿！原来不止我一个人这样！”的内容，我刷到了 Tyler Gaw 发的一条推文：我刚刚翻到了我的“线材大宝箱”底部，找到了两根我急需的线。它们在箱底躺了十多年了。所以，“你到底什么时候才会用到这些东西？”这个问题的答案就是今天。千万别让任何人拿走你的“线材大宝箱”。

I laughed. I cried. I felt inspired. So inspired, in fact, that I decided I was gonna do something about it. Something that would make me forever remember the value of that advice. I screenshotted that skeet. I printed it (in black and white with the ole’ trusty Brother). I cut it out. I pulled out my big box of cables (the one my wife lovingly labeled “FAMILY TECHNO BOX”). I cut some clear packing tape. And I plastered that thing on the front on my box.

我笑了，也哭了，更感到深受启发。事实上，我深受鼓舞，决定为此做点什么。做一件能让我永远铭记这条建议价值的事。我截下了那条推文，用我那台老当益壮的兄弟（Brother）打印机把它打印出来（黑白版），然后剪了下来。我搬出了我的线材大宝箱（我妻子亲切地给它贴上了“家庭科技宝箱”的标签），剪下一段透明胶带，把那张纸贴在了箱子正面。

Now every time I pull that box out to add YAC (yet another cable), instead of asking myself, “Why do I keep this box?” I’ll see that skeet and I’ll be reminded why I do what I do. I’ll feel a spark of joy, motivation, and purpose rekindled. (Not to mention the fact that it will serve as a warning to anyone in the family who thinks “I should just throw this away…”)

现在，每当我拿出这个箱子往里塞进又一根线（YAC）时，我不再会问自己“我为什么要留着这个箱子？”，而是会看到那条推文，提醒自己为什么要这么做。我会感到一丝喜悦、动力和使命感重新燃起。（更不用说，它还能给家里任何想动念头说“我应该把这玩意儿扔了……”的人起到警示作用。）

One day my kids will find that box in the attic amongst my other belongings. I hope they will pay heed to the timeless advice lovingly taped on the outside.

总有一天，我的孩子们会在阁楼里我的其他遗物中发现这个箱子。我希望他们能留意那条被我深情地贴在箱子外面的、永不过时的建议。
