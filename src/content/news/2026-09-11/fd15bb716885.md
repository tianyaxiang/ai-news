# Lensless Gaze Is Not Private by Default: Auditing Identity Leakage Across Disclosure Surfaces
# 无透镜注视追踪并非默认隐私：跨披露界面的身份泄露审计

**Abstract:** Lensless near-eye sensing is often described as privacy-friendly because its coded measurements are visually unintelligible. Yet visual unintelligibility reflects human interpretation, not what a learned adversary can recover. We therefore treat identity privacy as a systems property of disclosure surfaces: representations crossing sensing, storage, computation, and output boundaries.

**摘要：** 无透镜近眼传感常被描述为对隐私友好，因为其编码后的测量结果在视觉上是不可理解的。然而，视觉上的不可理解性仅反映了人类的解读能力，而非机器学习对手所能恢复的内容。因此，我们将身份隐私视为披露界面的一种系统属性，即跨越传感、存储、计算和输出边界的表征。

We audit a simulated lensless gaze pipeline under a 36-subject known-gallery closed-set identification protocol with a fixed, known PSF; privacy from an unknown or varying optical key is outside our scope. Reported accuracies are empirical attack success rates under matched linear and MLP probes and do not upper-bound stronger adversaries.

我们针对一个包含 36 名受试者的已知图库闭集识别协议，对模拟的无透镜注视追踪流程进行了审计，并采用了固定且已知的点扩散函数（PSF）；对于来自未知或变化的光学密钥的隐私保护不在本文研究范围内。报告的准确率是在匹配的线性探测器和多层感知机（MLP）探测器下的经验攻击成功率，并不代表更强攻击者的上限。

Simulated lensless measurements yield 96.7% top-1 identification versus 97.7% for matched original eye crops, while an MAE embedding retains 94.3%. Compression alone offers little protection: 8-D PCA and a matched 8-D bottleneck retain 93.2% and 91.8%, whereas separately trained 8-D GSPL bottlenecks yield 77.5% mean recovery across three seeds.

模拟的无透镜测量结果在 Top-1 识别率上达到了 96.7%，而匹配的原始眼部裁剪图像为 97.7%，同时 MAE 嵌入保留了 94.3% 的识别率。仅靠压缩几乎无法提供保护：8 维 PCA 和匹配的 8 维瓶颈分别保留了 93.2% 和 91.8% 的识别率，而单独训练的 8 维 GSPL 瓶颈在三个随机种子下的平均恢复率为 77.5%。

A released 128-way gaze token lowers single-frame recovery to 38.1%, while its residual and continuous gaze output expose 62.1% and 72.6%, respectively. Under a source-frame-disjoint tiled protocol, token summaries reach 39.9% at T=25, showing that repeated-output risk depends on representation and aggregation.

发布的 128 路注视 Token 将单帧恢复率降低至 38.1%，而其残差和连续注视输出分别暴露了 62.1% 和 72.6% 的信息。在源帧不相交的平铺协议下，Token 摘要在 T=25 时达到 39.9%，这表明重复输出的风险取决于表征方式和聚合方式。

These rates reflect all subject-correlated information in the evaluated dataset, including acquisition and behavioral cues, rather than isolating intrinsic ocular biometrics. Ordinary least squares residualization against a six-dimensional crop geometry and intensity summary still leaves lensless recovery at 95.1%. Our results show that privacy claims for lensless sensing must be tested at disclosure boundaries rather than inferred from appearance.

这些比率反映了评估数据集中所有与受试者相关的信息，包括采集过程和行为线索，而非仅仅隔离出内在的眼部生物特征。针对六维裁剪几何形状和强度摘要进行普通最小二乘残差化处理后，无透镜恢复率仍保持在 95.1%。我们的研究结果表明，无透镜传感的隐私声明必须在披露边界进行测试，而不是根据外观进行推断。
