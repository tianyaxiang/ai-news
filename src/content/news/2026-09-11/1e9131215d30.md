# Endogenous Exploration in Reinforcement Learning with Intrinsic Curiosity
# 基于内在好奇心的强化学习内源性探索

**Abstract:** We propose a reinforcement learning framework in which exploration is driven by intrinsic curiosity, designed for scenarios where environments are non-stationary and rewards are sparse, delayed, uninformative, or absent.

**摘要：** 我们提出了一种强化学习框架，其探索过程由内在好奇心驱动，旨在应对环境非平稳，以及奖励稀疏、延迟、信息量不足或缺失的场景。

In our model, action selection is guided by a combination of external rewards and an epistemic motivation mechanism that biases the agent toward structured exploratory directions.

在我们的模型中，动作选择由外部奖励与一种认知动机机制共同引导，该机制使智能体倾向于结构化的探索方向。

The central hypothesis is that effective exploration emerges at intermediate levels of incoherence, while performance degrades under both overly rigid and overly disordered dynamics.

其核心假设是：有效的探索出现在不连贯性处于中等水平时，而当动力学过于僵化或过于混乱时，性能都会下降。

To test this idea, we implement the framework on top of a Liquid State Machine (LSM) substrate and evaluate it on two standard benchmarks: the discrete-action LunarLanderv2 and the continuous-control BipedalWalkerv3.

为了验证这一想法，我们在液态状态机（LSM）基底上实现了该框架，并在两个标准基准测试中进行了评估：离散动作的 LunarLanderv2 和连续控制的 BipedalWalkerv3。

The proposed method achieves competitive performance on both tasks relative to established deep RL algorithms, including Proximal Policy Optimization (PPO) and Intrinsic Curiosity Module (ICM).

与现有的深度强化学习算法（包括近端策略优化 PPO 和内在好奇心模块 ICM）相比，该方法在两项任务中均取得了具有竞争力的性能。

We further show that the curiosity window is not recovered in Active Inference agents under the same analysis, suggesting that the proposed dynamics capture a distinct exploration regime.

我们进一步证明，在相同的分析下，主动推理（Active Inference）智能体无法复现这种好奇心窗口，这表明所提出的动力学捕捉到了一种独特的探索机制。
