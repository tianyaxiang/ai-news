# PAC-Private Autoregressive Generation: Calibrating Noise to Ensemble Disagreement

**PAC-Private Autoregressive Generation: Calibrating Noise to Ensemble Disagreement**

Language models adapted on private text are often served through APIs, so privacy leakage occurs through generated outputs rather than exposed weights. Private prediction protects these releases. Methods such as PMixED incur privacy cost at each release and increasingly rely on the public model over long horizons. PAC privacy instead calibrates noise to output variability across possible secrets, adding less noise when predictions are stable.

在私有文本上进行适配的语言模型通常通过 API 提供服务，因此隐私泄露更多是通过生成的输出而非暴露的模型权重发生的。私有预测（Private prediction）旨在保护这些发布内容。诸如 PMixED 之类的方法在每次发布时都会产生隐私成本，并且在长序列生成中越来越依赖于公共模型。相比之下，PAC 隐私通过根据不同潜在秘密（secrets）之间的输出变异性来校准噪声，当预测结果稳定时，添加的噪声更少。

To our knowledge, PAC-private prediction has not previously been extended from classification to autoregressive generation. We construct $m=128$ overlapping worlds from the private corpus, with each record appearing in exactly $m/2$ worlds, and train one adapter per world over a frozen public model. The realized world is the secret. At each token, the public model defines a candidate set, the worlds vote, and their posterior-weighted disagreement determines the PAC noise; unanimity requires no calibration noise. We prove $I(S;Y_{1:T}) \leq I(S;H_T) \leq bT$.

据我们所知，PAC 私有预测此前尚未从分类任务扩展到自回归生成任务。我们从私有语料库中构建了 $m=128$ 个重叠的世界（overlapping worlds），其中每条记录恰好出现在 $m/2$ 个世界中，并在冻结的公共模型之上为每个世界训练一个适配器（adapter）。实际实现的世界即为秘密。在生成每个 token 时，公共模型定义一个候选集，各个世界进行投票，其后验加权后的分歧程度决定了 PAC 噪声；如果投票一致，则无需校准噪声。我们证明了 $I(S;Y_{1:T}) \leq I(S;H_T) \leq bT$。

Our contributions are extending PAC privacy to autoregressive generation, handling adaptive self-generated contexts, and introducing coupled decoding that preserves privacy accounting while avoiding greedy degeneration. On WikiText-103 with GPT-2-small, we retain 74% of the fine-tuning gain at a per-token budget of $2^{-32}$, while membership-inference success is bounded by 51.08% after $10^6$ tokens; posterior-entropy estimates of leakage are roughly 17% of the charged budget.

我们的贡献在于将 PAC 隐私扩展到自回归生成，处理自适应的自生成上下文，并引入了耦合解码（coupled decoding）机制，该机制在保持隐私核算的同时避免了贪婪解码带来的退化问题。在 WikiText-103 数据集和 GPT-2-small 模型上的实验表明，在每个 token $2^{-32}$ 的预算下，我们保留了 74% 的微调增益，同时在生成 $10^6$ 个 token 后，成员推理（membership-inference）的成功率被限制在 51.08% 以内；后验熵估计的泄露量约为所消耗预算的 17%。

Inference privacy is not content protection: even when membership advantage on a memorized canary is indistinguishable from zero, the canary is emitted at the same rate. Against PMixED under matched membership-inference bounds on the same data universe and test set, we retain 98% of non-private headroom from $10^2$ to $10^6$ tokens, versus at most 56%, with no crossover.

推理隐私并不等同于内容保护：即使在记忆的“金丝雀”（canary）数据上的成员推理优势无法与零区分，该“金丝雀”仍会以相同的频率被输出。在相同数据空间和测试集下，与 PMixED 在匹配的成员推理边界下进行对比，我们在 $10^2$ 到 $10^6$ 个 token 的范围内保留了 98% 的非私有性能空间（headroom），而对比方法最高仅为 56%，且不存在交叉点。
