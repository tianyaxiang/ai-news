# Integrating Unimodal and Vision-Language Representations in Latent Space for Multi-Label Chest X-Ray Classification
# 在潜在空间中整合单模态与视觉-语言表征以进行多标签胸部 X 光分类

**Abstract:** Multi-label chest X-ray classification has attracted considerable attention in recent years, with the effective use of visual representations and clinical semantic knowledge playing an important role. 
**摘要：** 近年来，多标签胸部 X 光分类引起了广泛关注，其中视觉表征和临床语义知识的有效利用发挥了重要作用。

This study proposes a framework that combines unimodal representations from RAD-DINO with vision-language representations from BioViL-T for the classification of 14 labels in the MIMIC-CXR-JPG dataset. 
本研究提出了一个框架，结合了来自 RAD-DINO 的单模态表征和来自 BioViL-T 的视觉-语言表征，用于 MIMIC-CXR-JPG 数据集中 14 个标签的分类。

The RAD-DINO and BioViL-T embeddings and their combined representation are refined separately in latent space before being normalized and fused across the three branches. 
RAD-DINO 和 BioViL-T 的嵌入及其组合表征在潜在空间中分别进行优化，随后进行归一化，并在三个分支间进行融合。

In addition to improving classification performance, the study aims to clarify the role of each embedding source and the degree to which they complement one another. 
除了提高分类性能外，本研究还旨在阐明每个嵌入源的作用及其相互补充的程度。

Experiments show that RAD-DINO outperforms BioViL-T when used independently, whereas early fusion further improves the results, indicating that the two embedding sources contain complementary information. 
实验表明，RAD-DINO 在独立使用时优于 BioViL-T，而早期融合进一步改善了结果，这表明这两个嵌入源包含互补信息。

The best-performing model achieves a mean AUROC of 0.840 and an mAP of 0.467. 
表现最好的模型实现了 0.840 的平均 AUROC 和 0.467 的 mAP。

Ablation analysis shows that hybrid fusion provides consistent and statistically significant improvements over early fusion when each embedding source is refined in latent space, suggesting that fusion effectiveness depends on the quality of the representation supplied by each branch. 
消融分析表明，当每个嵌入源在潜在空间中得到优化时，混合融合比早期融合提供了持续且具有统计学意义的改进，这表明融合的有效性取决于每个分支所提供的表征质量。

However, the study has only been evaluated internally on MIMIC-CXR-JPG; its generalizability to data from other healthcare institutions therefore remains to be validated. 
然而，本研究仅在 MIMIC-CXR-JPG 上进行了内部评估；因此，其对其他医疗机构数据的泛化能力仍有待验证。
