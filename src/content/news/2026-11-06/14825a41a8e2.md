Apple doesn’t change its mind very often. Its history is one of sticking to its guns, even when pressed on certain design or technology decisions. And yet, I’ve been working on a MacBook Pro with a touchscreen for the past few days—a device I never thought would come into being.

苹果公司并不常改变主意。其历史一贯是坚持己见，即使在某些设计或技术决策上受到压力时也是如此。然而，过去几天我一直在使用一台带有触摸屏的 MacBook Pro——这是一款我从未想过会出现的设备。

Ignoring the (very reasonable) arguments about the poor ergonomics of touchscreen laptops made in the past, Apple has brought such a display to the MacBook Pro in the most un-Apple way I could have imagined. I’m not sure why it’s here, but it is.

抛开过去关于触摸屏笔记本电脑人体工程学设计不佳的（非常合理的）论点，苹果以一种我能想象到的最“不苹果”的方式将这样的显示屏带到了 MacBook Pro 上。我不确定它为什么会出现在这里，但它确实存在。

I do know, though, that I love the new “PROLED XDR” screen. Combine that with a hole-punch cutout for the webcam and the return of the light-up Apple logo on the lid—and it's enough improvements to call this a big upgrade over the rest of the MacBook lineup. But compared to a similarly specced MacBook Pro from a year ago, the OLED model costs almost $1,000 more by comparison.

不过，我确实很喜欢这款新的“PROLED XDR”屏幕。再加上用于网络摄像头的打孔设计以及顶盖上发光苹果 Logo 的回归，这些改进足以称其为 MacBook 产品线中的一次重大升级。但与一年前配置相似的 MacBook Pro 相比，OLED 型号的价格要高出近 1,000 美元。

Small Touches Add UpPhotograph: Luke LarsenWith the arrival of the OLED MacBook Pro, Apple now offers two flavors of its flagship premium laptop. The standard Mini-LED option now comes with the M6 processor with improved graphics and battery life, but otherwise looks largely the same as before.

细节累积。摄影：Luke Larsen。随着 OLED MacBook Pro 的到来，苹果现在为其旗舰高端笔记本电脑提供了两种选择。标准的 Mini-LED 版本现在配备了 M6 处理器，图形处理能力和电池续航有所提升，但在外观上与之前基本相同。

The OLED MacBook Pro, however, is the biggest change since 2021. You won’t notice it immediately on the outside. Color options are still just Silver and Space Black. You still have a choice between 14- and 16-inch. Ports are the same. Open it up, and you’ll see the familiar keyboard and trackpad. It’s all there as you’d expect.

然而，OLED MacBook Pro 是自 2021 年以来最大的变化。你在外观上不会立即注意到它。颜色选择仍然只有银色和深空黑。你仍然可以在 14 英寸和 16 英寸之间进行选择。接口保持不变。打开它，你会看到熟悉的键盘和触控板。一切都如你所料。

The only visual indication that you’re using something different is the glowing Apple logo on the lid. As a feature only available in the very expensive OLED MacBook Pro, it’s a pure, unabashed status symbol. And I love it. It even matches the display’s brightness, so it doesn’t distract in a dark room.

唯一能让你看出区别的视觉标志是顶盖上发光的苹果 Logo。作为仅在昂贵的 OLED MacBook Pro 上才有的功能，它是一个纯粹且毫不掩饰的身份象征。我很喜欢它。它甚至能与显示屏的亮度相匹配，因此在黑暗的房间里不会造成干扰。

Another discernible difference in the OLED MacBook Pro is something you’ll notice with your hands rather than your eyes. The 16-inch model I tested now weighs just 4 pounds, well over half a pound lighter than the non-OLED equivalent. Most of the weight savings come from OLED, which uses much thinner, lighter layers (more on OLED later). This is a very large laptop, but the weight reduction makes it much easier to carry around in a bag.

OLED MacBook Pro 的另一个明显区别是你用手而不是用眼睛能感受到的。我测试的 16 英寸型号现在仅重 4 磅，比非 OLED 的同类产品轻了半磅多。大部分重量的减轻归功于 OLED，它使用了更薄、更轻的层（稍后会详细介绍 OLED）。这是一台非常大的笔记本电脑，但重量的减轻使其更容易装在包里随身携带。

Photograph: Luke LarsenAnd the notch is gone! No Apple design feature has bothered me more than this large cutout at the top of the screen, which houses the webcam and sensors (it remains in the non-OLED model). The punch-hole camera is a huge improvement. As someone who found the notch a complete eyesore since it was introduced, this might be the change I’ve been most excited about.

摄影：Luke Larsen。刘海屏消失了！没有哪项苹果设计比屏幕顶部的这个大缺口更让我困扰了，它容纳了摄像头和传感器（在非 OLED 型号中依然存在）。打孔摄像头是一个巨大的改进。作为一个自刘海屏推出以来就觉得它极其碍眼的人，这可能是我最兴奋的改变。

Like on newer iPhones, the MacBook Pro is also getting Dynamic Island, which makes the punch-hole camera more useful. It can show interactive Live Activities, such as timers or currently-playing music. I imagine it won’t be used too often, though it does its job by hiding the punch-hole and showing off those narrow bezels.

就像在新款 iPhone 上一样，MacBook Pro 也引入了“灵动岛”（Dynamic Island），这使得打孔摄像头更加实用。它可以显示交互式的实时活动，例如计时器或正在播放的音乐。我想它不会被频繁使用，但它确实完成了隐藏打孔并展示窄边框的任务。

There are some subtler tweaks. The rubber feet on the bottom have softer, rounded edges. The bezels around the screen are slightly smaller.

还有一些更细微的调整。底部的橡胶脚垫边缘更柔软、更圆润。屏幕周围的边框稍微变窄了一些。

Even Brighter OLEDIt’s been over two years since Apple first brought its tandem OLED displays to the iPad Pro. In that time, MacBook Pro users have been patiently using the same Mini-LED display. Nevertheless, the OLED MacBook Pro has finally arrived, and Apple has pushed the tandem OLED panel further than ever before. This is the brightest laptop display I’ve ever tested, capable of reaching up to 1,000 nits in SDR. That’s on everyday content throughout the operating system and across all apps. The screen can, however, crank out 2,000 nits of brightness—but only in specific circumstances.

更明亮的 OLED。距离苹果首次将串联 OLED 显示屏引入 iPad Pro 已经过去了两年多。在此期间，MacBook Pro 用户一直耐心地使用着同样的 Mini-LED 显示屏。尽管如此，OLED MacBook Pro 终于到来了，苹果将串联 OLED 面板推向了前所未有的高度。这是我测试过的最亮的笔记本电脑显示屏，在 SDR 模式下最高可达 1,000 尼特。这适用于整个操作系统和所有应用程序中的日常内容。不过，屏幕最高可以达到 2,000 尼特的亮度，但仅限于特定情况下。

Photograph: Luke LarsenFor example, under very bright light or outdoors, the ambient light sensor built into the webcam module will detect those conditions and tune the display beyond the 1,000-nit upper limit. Combine that with the optional nano-texture screen, and you have what is undoubtedly the best laptop for working outside. That’s especially impressive considering glare is a big problem with typical OLED laptop screens. (You’ll need to turn on the “Automatically adjust brightness” setting, which I typically don’t.) Apple sent me the OLED with standard glass for testing, which comes with an antireflective coating that the company calls “groundbreaking." Next to the non-OLED M6 MacBook Pro, it looks about as reflective. If you really want to use this thing outside, the nano-texture upgrade is well worth it.

摄影：Luke Larsen。例如，在强光下或户外，内置于摄像头模块中的环境光传感器会检测到这些条件，并将显示屏亮度调节至超过 1,000 尼特的上限。再加上可选的纳米纹理屏幕，这无疑是户外办公的最佳笔记本电脑。考虑到眩光是典型 OLED 笔记本电脑屏幕的一大问题，这一点尤其令人印象深刻。（你需要开启“自动调节亮度”设置，我通常是不开启的。）苹果寄给我测试的是配备标准玻璃的 OLED 版本，它带有苹果公司称之为“突破性”的防反射涂层。与非 OLED 的 M6 MacBook Pro 放在一起对比，它的反光程度看起来差不多。如果你真的想在户外使用它，纳米纹理升级是非常值得的。

HDR content is the second situation in which the screen can be cranked up to 2,000 nits of peak brightness. Here, the idea isn’t to blast your eyes with brightness; it’s about details. In HDR-rendered photos, videos, movies, and games, 2,000 nits would be bright enough to deliver incredible depth of detail and color. I’ve never seen HDR like this on a laptop, and you really have to experience it to truly get it. In my HDR testing, my OLED MacBook Pro never quite reached 2,000 nits, maxing out at 1,849 nits. But amazingly, it can even produce this in a 25 percent window, and 1,025 nits with every pixel lit up. The only place you’ll find brighter OLED is in the very latest OLED TVs.

HDR 内容是屏幕可以提升至 2,000 尼特峰值亮度的第二种情况。这里的目的不是用亮度刺伤你的眼睛，而是为了细节。在 HDR 渲染的照片、视频、电影和游戏中，2,000 尼特的亮度足以呈现令人难以置信的细节深度和色彩。我从未在笔记本电脑上见过这样的 HDR 效果，你必须亲身体验才能真正理解。在我的 HDR 测试中，我的 OLED MacBook Pro 从未完全达到 2,000 尼特，最高为 1,849 尼特。但令人惊奇的是，它甚至可以在 25% 的窗口中达到这个亮度，而在全像素点亮时也能达到 1,025 尼特。唯一比这更亮的 OLED 屏幕只能在最新的 OLED 电视中找到。

Now, this is actually only 12 percent brighter than the Mini-LED MacBook Pro, which I retested to make this comparison. But when you pair a brighter OLED screen with the deep blacks it can produce, you have absolutely stellar HDR, giving the content a range of depth never before seen on a laptop.I watc

实际上，这仅比我为了对比而重新测试的 Mini-LED MacBook Pro 亮了 12%。但是，当你将更亮的 OLED 屏幕与它能产生的深邃黑色相结合时，你就能获得绝对出色的 HDR 效果，为内容带来笔记本电脑上前所未有的深度范围。我观看了……
