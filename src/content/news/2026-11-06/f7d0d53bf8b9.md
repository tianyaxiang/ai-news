Computer Science > Computation and Language arXiv:2611.05389 (cs) [Submitted on 11 Sep 2026] Title: AI-Driven SaaS Platform Integration: AI Express for Unified Generative AI Services with Pay-As-You-Go Credit Systems and Scalable Microservice Architecture Authors: Deepit Sapru.

计算机科学 > 计算与语言 arXiv:2611.05389 (cs) [提交于 2026 年 9 月 11 日] 标题：人工智能驱动的 SaaS 平台集成：用于统一生成式 AI 服务、按需付费信用系统及可扩展微服务架构的 AI Express，作者：Deepit Sapru。

Abstract: Generative AI is currently fragmented, with separate subscriptions required for text generation, image synthesis, and code assistance, leading to high costs and workflow disruptions. AI Express is a SaaS platform that consolidates these capabilities into a unified pay-as-you-go service.

摘要：生成式人工智能目前处于碎片化状态，文本生成、图像合成和代码辅助需要单独订阅，导致成本高昂且工作流程中断。AI Express 是一个 SaaS 平台，将这些功能整合为统一的按需付费服务。

The platform provides a range of AI tools, including a Core Code Reviewer that evaluates GitHub repositories, a Text-to-Website generator that converts text into fully functional websites, script generation, grammar checking, data analysis, and watermark removal, all accessible through a single interface without requiring technical expertise.

该平台提供了一系列人工智能工具，包括评估 GitHub 存储库的核心代码审查器、将文本转换为功能齐全网站的文本转网站生成器、脚本生成、语法检查、数据分析和水印去除，所有这些功能都可通过单一界面访问，无需专业技术知识。
