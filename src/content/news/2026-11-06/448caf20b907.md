# Perplexity launches its own take on AI agents for work

Perplexity 推出其针对工作场景的 AI 智能体

Like OpenAI’s Dots and Claude Duet, Perplexity’s Teammates can perform an array of tasks while running 24/7.

与 OpenAI 的 Dots 和 Claude Duet 类似，Perplexity 的“Teammates”（队友）可以在全天候运行的同时执行一系列任务。

Perplexity is taking on OpenAI and Anthropic with its own work-focused AI agents. The startup’s new always-on “Teammates” can take on multiple roles within a workspace, while pulling existing projects, skills, and memory from the Perplexity tools you already use.

Perplexity 正凭借其专注于工作的 AI 智能体与 OpenAI 和 Anthropic 展开竞争。这家初创公司推出的全新常驻型“Teammates”可以在工作区内担任多种角色，同时调用你已在使用的 Perplexity 工具中的现有项目、技能和记忆。

Teammates integrate with Perplexity Computer, an AI system that can deploy agents on your device to complete tasks. But Perplexity says Teammates “build on top” of Computer by offering the ability to collaborate and complete “ongoing, large-scale work.”

“Teammates”与 Perplexity Computer 进行了集成，后者是一个可以在你的设备上部署智能体以完成任务的 AI 系统。但 Perplexity 表示，“Teammates”是在 Computer 的基础上构建的，提供了协作并完成“持续性、大规模工作”的能力。

You can assign each Teammate different jobs that they can perform simultaneously — much like OpenAI’s Dots and Claude Duet. Perplexity’s take on the feature is much more expensive, however, as it requires a subscription to its $200 / month Max plan.

你可以为每个“Teammate”分配不同的工作，让他们同时执行——这与 OpenAI 的 Dots 和 Claude Duet 非常相似。然而，Perplexity 对该功能的实现成本要高得多，因为它需要订阅每月 200 美元的 Max 套餐。

Perplexity notes that you can keep tabs on Teammates’ progress through individual threads in the Perplexity app, as well as interact with them in Slack. “Over time, we envision every org having hundreds of teammates working for each person,” Perplexity CEO Aravind Srinivas says on X.

Perplexity 指出，你可以通过 Perplexity 应用中的独立对话线程来跟踪“Teammates”的进度，也可以在 Slack 中与他们互动。Perplexity 首席执行官 Aravind Srinivas 在 X 上表示：“随着时间的推移，我们设想每个组织中的每个人都将拥有数百名‘队友’为其工作。”
