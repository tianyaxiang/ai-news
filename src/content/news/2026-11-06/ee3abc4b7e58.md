ACME Inc. 是一家快速成长的初创公司，致力于帮助小型企业监控财务状况。我们与全球的企业主合作，将他们的反馈转化为可执行的洞察。在马耳他猎鹰资本（Maltese Falcon Capital）的支持下，我们最近完成了1500万美元的A轮融资，旨在为每一家实体店提供轻松便捷的监控服务。

You’ll be joining a high-performance, close-knit team of AI natives! We’ve been AI-first for the past 2 years, with every part of the product agent-friendly and interconnected: design, development, QA, CI/CD, infra, data analysis. We like to say that our stack is English!

你将加入一个高效且紧密的AI原生团队！过去两年我们一直坚持“AI优先”战略，产品的每一个环节——设计、开发、质量保证、CI/CD、基础设施和数据分析——都对智能体友好且相互关联。我们常说，我们的技术栈就是英语！

While English has gotten us 99.5% of the way there, we’re in need of a hands-on engineer ready to close the final 0.5%. Over time, we’ve noticed some issues that are immune to prompts and catch agents in loops. Sometimes, the issue at hand gets fixed, but it results in another issue. This is where you step in.

虽然英语已经帮我们完成了99.5%的工作，但我们现在需要一位实干型工程师来解决最后的0.5%。随着时间推移，我们发现了一些提示词无法解决的问题，这些问题会让智能体陷入循环。有时，当前的问题解决了，却引发了另一个问题。这就是你需要介入的地方。

As a line specialist, your job will be to investigate and fix complex issues, primarily by reading the underlying code. We’re looking for someone ready to open an IDE and dive deep into an unknown codebase — a fearless detective who is comfortable inspecting logs and database rows, and has the know-how to piece all the information together.

作为代码行专家，你的工作是通过阅读底层代码来调查并修复复杂问题。我们寻找的是那种准备好打开IDE并深入研究未知代码库的人——一位无畏的侦探，能够自如地检查日志和数据库行，并具备将所有信息拼凑在一起的专业知识。

The perfect candidate should be willing to work on a problem for hours, sometimes even days. This is a new position for us. We expect someone who works autonomously, makes sound decisions independently and takes full ownership of their work.

理想的候选人应该愿意为一个问题钻研数小时，甚至数天。这对我们来说是一个新职位。我们期望候选人能够自主工作，独立做出合理的决策，并对自己的工作承担全部责任。

We are happy to help in any way we can (you’ll have access to agents’ reasoning traces), but ultimately we depend on your confidence in understanding the problem and shipping a solution. Your responsibilities take over unresolved issues from agents read the codebase, go over application logs, query the database prepare, test, review and ship fixes by yourself write postmortems for agent training occasionally consult on unreleased features onboard and mentor a future junior line specialist.

我们乐于提供一切力所能及的帮助（你将有权访问智能体的推理轨迹），但最终我们依赖于你对理解问题并交付解决方案的信心。你的职责包括：接手智能体未解决的问题；阅读代码库、查看应用日志、查询数据库；独立准备、测试、审查并发布修复程序；为智能体训练编写事后分析报告；偶尔就未发布的功能提供咨询；入职并指导未来的初级代码行专家。

We think you’d be great if you… have at least 5 years of pre-2022 TypeScript experience are comfortable running tests manually and using a “debugger” can reason from first principles can work on a problem with sustained attention for hours are autonomous and an independent thinker.

如果你具备以下条件，我们认为你会非常出色：至少5年2022年之前的TypeScript经验；习惯手动运行测试并使用“调试器”；能够从第一性原理进行推理；能够长时间专注于一个问题；具备自主性和独立思考能力。

Nice to have previous experience as a line specialist DB administration experience (our agents say it’s Postgres) AI prompting basics.

加分项：曾担任代码行专家的经验；数据库管理经验（我们的智能体说是Postgres）；AI提示词基础。
