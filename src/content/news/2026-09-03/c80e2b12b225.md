# A Lagrangian View of Flow Matching
# 流匹配的拉格朗日视角

**Abstract:** Modern explicit-time generative models, such as Flow Matching [Lipman et al., 2023] and Rectified Flow [Liu et al., 2023], are typically derived top-down via Optimal Transport and the continuity equation. This standard Eulerian approach focuses on the macroscopic transport of probability mass.

**摘要：** 现代显式时间生成模型，如流匹配（Flow Matching, Lipman 等人，2023）和修正流（Rectified Flow, Liu 等人，2023），通常是通过最优传输（Optimal Transport）和连续性方程自上而下推导出来的。这种标准的欧拉方法侧重于概率质量的宏观传输。

In this paper, we present an alternative, bottom-up mechanical derivation grounded in a Lagrangian (particle-centric) perspective. By analyzing the local Taylor expansion of a continuous denoiser, we motivate a strict invariance condition required for optimal, single-step generation: the conservation of target identity.

在本文中，我们提出了一种基于拉格朗日（以粒子为中心）视角的替代性自下而上的力学推导方法。通过分析连续去噪器的局部泰勒展开，我们推导出了实现最优单步生成所需的严格不变性条件：目标恒等性守恒。

Enforcing this condition yields a governing quasi-linear advection Partial Differential Equation (PDE). We demonstrate that solving this PDE via the Method of Characteristics analytically yields the straight-line trajectories of Flow Matching.

强制执行该条件会导出一个控制性的拟线性平流偏微分方程（PDE）。我们证明，通过特征线法求解该偏微分方程，在解析上可以得到流匹配的直线轨迹。

This geometric perspective isolates the Jacobian of the denoiser as the primary source of trajectory curvature, providing a direct mathematical explanation for why straight-line flows enable massive step sizes, and why empirical models require distillation to flatten intersecting characteristics.

这种几何视角将去噪器的雅可比矩阵（Jacobian）确定为轨迹曲率的主要来源，从而为以下现象提供了直接的数学解释：为什么直线流能够支持巨大的步长，以及为什么经验模型需要通过蒸馏来平滑相交的特征线。
