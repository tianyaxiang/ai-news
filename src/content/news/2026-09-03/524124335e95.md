# True Rate of Unemployment / 真实失业率

**True Rate of Unemployment**
**真实失业率**

The percentage of the U.S. labor force that is functionally unemployed. The functional unemployment rate increased 0.2 percentage points in July, the fourth consecutive monthly increase.
该指标衡量美国劳动力中处于“功能性失业”状态的比例。7月份功能性失业率上升了0.2个百分点，这是连续第四个月增长。

**True Rate of Unemployment: 24.9% (July 2026)**
**真实失业率：24.9%（2026年7月）**

**Headline Rate of Unemployment: 4.1% (July 2026)**
**官方失业率：4.1%（2026年7月）**

Using data compiled by the federal government’s Bureau of Labor Statistics, the True Rate of Unemployment tracks the percentage of the U.S. labor force that does not have a full-time job (35+ hours a week) but wants one, has no job, or does not earn a living wage, conservatively pegged at $26,000 (in 2025 dollars) annually before taxes.
“真实失业率”利用联邦政府劳工统计局汇编的数据，追踪美国劳动力中未能获得全职工作（每周35小时以上）但有工作意愿的人群比例，以及失业人群或收入未达到生活工资标准（保守估计为税前年薪2.6万美元，按2025年美元计算）的人群比例。

Just as an accurate census is a prerequisite to funding American communities equitably, policymakers depend on economic indicators to shape economic policy. LISEP developed the True Rate of Unemployment to provide analysts and decision-makers with a more accurate measure of Americans’ financial well-being.
正如准确的人口普查是公平资助美国社区的前提一样，决策者也依赖经济指标来制定经济政策。LISEP开发“真实失业率”旨在为分析师和决策者提供衡量美国人财务状况的更准确指标。

For a more in-depth explanation of the True Rate of Unemployment, please reference LISEP’s TRU white paper.
如需深入了解“真实失业率”，请参阅LISEP的TRU白皮书。
