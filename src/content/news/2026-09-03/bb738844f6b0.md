# What We Miss About Missing Values
# 我们对“缺失值”的缺失认知

As the old adage goes, a wise man once said nothing at all. Unfortunately, the same reverence is rarely extended to missing data. A row with a blank cell is often treated as a problem to be solved before analysis can begin: drop it, fill it with an average, do whatever is easiest and move on. The blank cell is, by this logic, a defect in the record rather than a fact about the world. But the absence of a measurement can provide real insight, and how we handle it may dramatically alter the conclusions we draw. Missingness is better understood not as an unfortunate nuisance, but as a byproduct of the process that also generates the data we observe.

正如那句老话所说，智者往往沉默寡言。遗憾的是，人们很少以同样的敬意对待缺失数据。一行带有空白单元格的数据通常被视为分析前必须解决的“麻烦”：删掉它、用平均值填充，或者用最简单的方法处理掉然后继续。按照这种逻辑，空白单元格被视为记录中的缺陷，而非关于现实世界的事实。然而，测量值的缺失本身就能提供深刻的洞察，我们处理缺失值的方式可能会极大地改变最终得出的结论。与其将缺失视为一种不幸的干扰，不如将其理解为生成观测数据这一过程的副产品。

In a clinical trial, the patients who drop out before the end-of-study assessment are not a random sample of those who enrolled. They are disproportionately the ones whose treatment failed, whose adverse events were severe, or whose underlying condition deteriorated. On a signup form, an optional income field is more likely to be left blank when the true figure is one the user would rather not disclose. In a product experiment, users who churn before the measurement window closes never produce the outcome that was meant to be recorded, and may be disproportionately likely to do so because of the treatment they received. In each case, the process determining whether something is observed may itself be related to the phenomenon we are trying to understand.

在临床试验中，在研究结束评估前退出的患者并非入组人群的随机样本。他们往往是那些治疗失败、出现严重不良反应或基础病情恶化的人。在注册表单中，如果用户不愿透露真实收入，那么可选的收入字段就更有可能被留空。在产品实验中，在测量窗口关闭前流失的用户无法产生预期的记录结果，而他们流失的原因恰恰可能与他们所接受的实验处理有关。在上述每种情况下，决定某项数据是否被观测到的过程，本身就可能与我们试图理解的现象密切相关。

The same concern applies to sensors. Their impartiality over human self-report is real in that they cannot refuse to answer an awkward question or forget to make a recording, but this is often overstated as a guarantee of unbiasedness. Whether a value is recorded at all depends on the operating conditions of the instrument, which can itself be shaped by the phenomenon it was built to measure. A PM2.5 sensor sends a laser beam through a sample of air, using measurements of the light scattered by particulates to estimate pollutant concentration. But the particles being measured can also affect the instrument itself: high particulate loading can degrade sensor performance and lead to outages (Safarov et al., 2025). In this case, gaps may be more likely during the high-pollution events the network was built to capture. This is an increasingly common pattern in our brave new "Internet of Things", with seismometers that clip during earthquakes, strain gauges that fail under heavy loads, and smart meters which drop outage reports precisely when a blackout is at its peak.

同样的担忧也适用于传感器。传感器相比人类自述确实更加客观，因为它们不会拒绝回答尴尬的问题，也不会忘记记录数据，但这往往被过度夸大为“无偏见”的保证。一个数值能否被记录，取决于仪器的运行状况，而仪器状况本身又可能受到其所测量现象的影响。PM2.5 传感器通过空气样本发射激光，利用颗粒物散射的光线来估算污染物浓度。但被测量的颗粒物也会影响仪器本身：高浓度的颗粒物会降低传感器性能并导致故障（Safarov et al., 2025）。在这种情况下，数据缺失反而更容易发生在网络旨在捕捉的高污染事件期间。在我们这个崭新的“物联网”时代，这种模式正变得越来越普遍：地震仪在强震时会发生削波，应变计在重载下会失效，智能电表恰恰在停电最严重时无法发送停电报告。

Rubin (1976) formalises three mechanisms by which data go missing, expressed as constraints on the missingness probability $P(R \mid Y_{obs}, Y_{mis})$, where $Y$ is the variable of interest partitioned into observed values $Y_{obs}$ and unobserved values $Y_{mis}$, and $R$ is a binary indicator of whether a given value of $Y$ is observed. Data are missing completely at random (MCAR) when $P(R \mid Y_{obs}, Y_{mis}) = P(R)$: the probability of a gap is independent of everything else in the dataset. Under MCAR, dropping the incomplete rows does not bias an estimator, though it reduces the sample size and inflates the standard errors, so the loss is one of precision rather than of validity. Data are missing at random (MAR) when $P(R \mid Y_{obs}, Y_{mis}) = P(R \mid Y_{obs})$: the probability of a gap depends only on the values one does observe. Under MAR, unbiased estimation is possible in principle using methods that correctly condition on the observed variables driving missingness. Data are missing not at random (MNAR) when $P(R \mid Y_{obs}, Y_{mis})$ depends on $Y_{mis}$; the gap encodes information about the very quantity one cannot see, and no method conditioning only on observed values can identify the unobserved distribution without further assumptions. The distinction between MAR and MNAR is fundamentally untestable from observed data alone: the definition of MAR depends on values we cannot see. Which regime applies must be argued for on grounds outside the data itself, from expert knowledge of the mechanism, features of the study design, or structural understanding of what makes the record incomplete. In the PM2.5 case, it is the physical understanding of how pollution conditions affect the monitoring process that can make MNAR a plausible mechanism, not any statistical test. Every method for handling missingness therefore begins from an assumption about the process that produced the gaps, an assumption the incomplete dataset cannot supply on its own.

Rubin (1976) 将数据缺失的机制形式化为三种，表示为缺失概率 $P(R \mid Y_{obs}, Y_{mis})$ 的约束条件。其中 $Y$ 是目标变量，被划分为观测值 $Y_{obs}$ 和未观测值 $Y_{mis}$，$R$ 是一个二元指标，表示 $Y$ 的给定值是否被观测到。当 $P(R \mid Y_{obs}, Y_{mis}) = P(R)$ 时，数据为完全随机缺失 (MCAR)：缺失概率与数据集中的其他任何因素无关。在 MCAR 下，删除不完整行不会使估计量产生偏差，尽管它会减小样本量并增大标准误，因此损失的是精度而非有效性。当 $P(R \mid Y_{obs}, Y_{mis}) = P(R \mid Y_{obs})$ 时，数据为随机缺失 (MAR)：缺失概率仅取决于已观测到的值。在 MAR 下，原则上可以通过正确调节导致缺失的观测变量的方法来进行无偏估计。当 $P(R \mid Y_{obs}, Y_{mis})$ 取决于 $Y_{mis}$ 时，数据为非随机缺失 (MNAR)；缺失部分编码了我们无法看到的变量本身的信息，如果不引入额外假设，任何仅基于观测值的方法都无法识别未观测到的分布。MAR 和 MNAR 之间的区别从根本上无法仅通过观测数据来检验：MAR 的定义依赖于我们无法看到的值。哪种机制适用，必须基于数据之外的理由进行论证，例如对机制的专家知识、研究设计的特征，或对导致记录不完整原因的结构性理解。在 PM2.5 的案例中，是关于污染状况如何影响监测过程的物理理解使得 MNAR 成为一种合理的机制，而非任何统计检验。因此，每种处理缺失值的方法都始于对产生缺失过程的某种假设，而这种假设是残缺的数据集本身无法提供的。

One response is to model the selection process directly. Suppose we want to estimate the value of a marketing offer using subsequent customer spending. Spending is only observed for customers who remain active long enough to make a purchase, and the factors that determine whether a customer remains active may also affect how much they would have spent. Simply analysing the customers who generated a transaction therefore selects on a process related to the outcome itself. Heckman (1979) formalised this class of problem by modelling the process that determines whether an outcome is observed. His canonical example was wages, which are only recorded for individuals who choose to work: if the same unobserved characteristics that influence the decision to work also influence wages, the observed workers are not a random sample of the population.

一种应对方法是直接对选择过程进行建模。假设我们想通过后续的客户消费来评估某项营销优惠的价值。我们只能观测到那些保持活跃时间足够长并产生购买行为的客户的消费情况，而决定客户是否保持活跃的因素，也可能影响他们的消费金额。因此，仅仅分析那些产生交易的客户，实际上是在一个与结果本身相关的过程上进行了选择。Heckman (1979) 通过对决定结果是否被观测到的过程进行建模，将这类问题形式化了。他最经典的例子是工资，工资数据仅记录在选择工作的人群中：如果影响工作决策的未观测特征同时也影响工资，那么被观测到的劳动者就不是总体的随机样本。

Heckman's approach was to treat this selection process as part of the model. An outcome equation for $Y$ is paired with a selection equation describing whether $Y$ is observed, allowing the unobserved factors affecting selection and the outcome to be correlated. The original two-step estimator first models the probability that an observation appears in the dataset, then uses this information to correct the outcome equation for the fact that the observed sample is not random.
