# Google is sending MrBeast into the wilderness, armed with AI
# Google 携手 MrBeast，利用 AI 征服荒野

One of YouTube’s biggest creators has a new deal to promote Gemini and Google Health.
YouTube 最具影响力的创作者之一已达成一项新协议，将推广 Gemini 和 Google Health。

MrBeast will feature Gemini, Google Health, and the Fitbit Air in upcoming videos as part of a multi-year partnership with Google. The deal will kick off with a video featuring Jimmy “MrBeast” Donaldson turning to Gemini for wilderness survival advice.
作为与 Google 多年合作关系的一部分，MrBeast 将在未来的视频中展示 Gemini、Google Health 和 Fitbit Air。此次合作将以一段视频拉开序幕，视频中 Jimmy “MrBeast” Donaldson 将向 Gemini 寻求荒野生存建议。

First up on September 5 is a new MrBeast video following Jimmy and his crew as they attempt to survive some of the most extreme and treacherous climates on Earth. Teams will race to navigate three of the planet’s most unforgiving landscapes — the jungle, the desert, and the Arctic — using Gemini to help them identify dangers, survive brutal weather changes, and stay one step ahead.
9 月 5 日即将发布的一段 MrBeast 新视频，将记录 Jimmy 和他的团队在地球上一些最极端、最险恶的气候中求生的过程。各队将竞相穿越地球上三个最严酷的地带——丛林、沙漠和北极，并利用 Gemini 帮助他们识别危险、应对恶劣的天气变化，从而抢占先机。

Google says Donaldson will also use a Fitbit Air in one of his upcoming challenges and appear in a Gemini campaign from Google. The deal comes a couple of months after Donaldson’s YouTube channel became the first to hit 500 million subscribers, and a recent Bloomberg report saying YouTube has been offering prominent creators deals to keep their content exclusive to its site as more creators strike deals with Netflix.
Google 表示，Donaldson 还将在未来的挑战中使用 Fitbit Air，并出现在 Google 的 Gemini 宣传活动中。此前，Donaldson 的 YouTube 频道刚刚成为首个订阅量突破 5 亿的频道。据彭博社近期报道，随着越来越多的创作者与 Netflix 达成合作，YouTube 一直在向知名创作者提供协议，以确保他们的内容仅在 YouTube 平台独家发布。
