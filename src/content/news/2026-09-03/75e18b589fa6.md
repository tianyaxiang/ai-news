# Dutch central bank moves share of gold from U.S., Canada to London
# 荷兰央行将部分黄金储备从美加转移至伦敦

**Dutch central bank moves €10 billion in gold out of U.S., Canada, citing "instability"**
**荷兰央行以“不稳定”为由，将价值100亿欧元的黄金从美国和加拿大撤出**

The Nederlandsche Bank (DNB) announced on Wednesday it relocated the equivalent of over 10 billion euros in gold from facilities in the United States and Canada over concern for global tensions. The Dutch central bank essentially moved 86,000 kilograms of gold to the Bank of England in London to make it easier to liquidate reserves should a crisis situation arise.
荷兰中央银行（DNB）周三宣布，出于对全球局势紧张的担忧，已将价值超过100亿欧元的黄金从美国和加拿大的设施中转移。荷兰央行实际上将86,000公斤黄金转移到了伦敦的英格兰银行，以便在发生危机时更容易变现储备。

The Dutch national gold reserve currently consists of 612.4 tonnes, which was valued at 72.2 billion euros at the end of last year. Roughly 32 percent of that total is currently stored in London, and 31 percent is in Zeist, where the DNB has stockpiled gold after moving it out of Amsterdam. Approximately 18.5 percent is still in New York, and an equivalent amount is in Ottawa.
荷兰国家黄金储备目前为612.4吨，去年年底估值为722亿欧元。其中约32%目前存放在伦敦，31%存放在宰斯特（Zeist）——DNB在将黄金从阿姆斯特丹迁出后，将其囤积于此。约18.5%的黄金仍存放在纽约，等量的黄金存放在渥太华。

A total of more than 27 tonnes of gold was physically transferred from the locations in North America to DNB Cash Centre in Zeist, Utrecht, between March and August. Another 59 tonnes of gold kept in New York was also sold off during this time, while the equivalent amount of gold was purchased in London. To maintain the spread without increasing the supply held in the Netherlands, 27 tonnes of gold was also transferred from Zeist to London.
今年3月至8月间，总计超过27吨的黄金从北美地点被实物转移至位于乌得勒支省宰斯特的DNB现金中心。在此期间，存放在纽约的另外59吨黄金被出售，同时在伦敦购入了等量的黄金。为了在不增加荷兰境内持有量的情况下维持储备分布，另有27吨黄金从宰斯特转移到了伦敦。

“Due to the increasing geopolitical instability, the DNB is working on improving its crisis readiness. Improving the marketability of Dutch gold, and with that how quickly it can be used in crisis situations, is a part of that,” the DNB said. The Bank of England "is regarded as the world's most easily tradable gold and will therefore be the most readily available for DNB in a crisis situation," the central bank said.
DNB表示：“由于地缘政治不稳定性增加，DNB正致力于提高其危机应对能力。提高荷兰黄金的适销性，从而提高其在危机情况下的使用速度，是其中的一部分。”该央行称，英格兰银行“被认为是全球黄金交易最便利的场所，因此在危机情况下，它将成为DNB最容易调用的储备。”

At the end of February, the DNB held the largest share of Dutch gold, 31.3 percent, in New York. Approximately 78.39 tonnes of gold owned by the Netherlands is no longer stored there. A portion of the Dutch reserve equal to 7.35 tonnes is no longer stored at the Ottawa location.
截至2月底，DNB在纽约持有的荷兰黄金份额最大，占31.3%。目前，荷兰拥有的约78.39吨黄金已不再存放在那里。此外，有7.35吨的荷兰储备也不再存放在渥太华。

The gold that was relocated had an estimated value of 10.11 billion euros at the end of 2025. The value was estimated at 10.34 billion euros as of 3 p.m. on Wednesday in the Netherlands.
被转移的黄金在2025年底的估值为101.1亿欧元。截至荷兰当地时间周三下午3点，其估值为103.4亿欧元。

Aside from nearly a third being held in New York earlier this year, just under 20 percent was in Ottawa, and 18 percent was kept in London. The remaining 31 percent was in Zeist. “A more equal spread of the gold supply between North America, the United Kingdom, and the U.S. also helps to spread the risk.”
除了今年早些时候近三分之一的黄金存放在纽约外，当时约有不到20%存放在渥太华，18%存放在伦敦，其余31%存放在宰斯特。“在北美、英国和美国之间更均衡地分配黄金储备，也有助于分散风险。”

The Netherlands still maintains a reserve of around 113,300 kilograms of gold in New York, and the same in Ottawa. More than 188,600 kilograms is in Zeist, and about 196,600 kilograms is in London.
荷兰目前在纽约仍保留约113,300公斤的黄金储备，渥太华的储备量相同。宰斯特存有超过188,600公斤，伦敦则存有约196,600公斤。

The U.S. has been involved in a conflict with Iran in recent months, leading to global oil price increases. It has also been in conflict with Canada and Cuba and has conducted military operations in Caracas, Venezuela.
美国近几个月卷入了与伊朗的冲突，导致全球油价上涨。此外，美国还与加拿大和古巴存在冲突，并在委内瑞拉加拉加斯进行了军事行动。
