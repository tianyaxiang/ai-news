# DISTAL: Distillation and Self-Supervised Pretraining for Structure-Agnostic Materials Property Prediction
# DISTAL：用于结构无关材料属性预测的蒸馏与自监督预训练方法

**Abstract:** Materials property prediction remains difficult in low-data settings, where many target properties are supported by only a limited number of labeled samples. Models with the strongest predictive accuracy often depend on crystal structures, which restricts their use in early-stage screening when structural information is limited or unavailable.
**摘要：** 在数据匮乏的情况下，材料属性预测仍然是一项艰巨的任务，因为许多目标属性仅有少量标记样本支持。预测精度最高的模型往往依赖于晶体结构，这限制了它们在结构信息有限或缺失的早期筛选阶段的应用。

To address this challenge, we propose DISTAL, a dual-prior framework for structure-agnostic materials property prediction that combines self-supervised compositional pretraining with structure-aware knowledge distillation. DISTAL first learns transferable compositional representations from a large virtual composition space using 145 composition-derived descriptors.
为了应对这一挑战，我们提出了 DISTAL，这是一个用于结构无关材料属性预测的双先验框架，它结合了自监督成分预训练和结构感知知识蒸馏。DISTAL 首先利用 145 个成分衍生描述符，从一个庞大的虚拟成分空间中学习可迁移的成分表征。

It then distills structural knowledge from a pretrained ALIGNN teacher into a composition-conditioned student. This setting allows structural priors to be used during training without requiring structural inputs at inference. By integrating explicit compositional descriptors, pretrained latent features, and distilled structural features within a unified prediction pipeline, DISTAL captures complementary signals that are difficult to recover from any single representation alone.
随后，它将预训练的 ALIGNN 教师模型的结构知识蒸馏到以成分为条件的“学生”模型中。这种设置允许在训练过程中使用结构先验，而无需在推理时输入结构信息。通过将显式成分描述符、预训练潜在特征和蒸馏结构特征整合到一个统一的预测流程中，DISTAL 能够捕获那些仅靠单一表征难以恢复的互补信号。

Across 39 benchmark tasks, the best-performing multimodal configuration combines all three signals, and improves over the reference benchmark on 37 tasks. DISTAL achieves the strongest overall performance among all evaluated feature combinations. These results indicate that compositional pretraining and structural distillation provide complementary priors and offer a practical route to robust composition-only prediction in small-data materials informatics.
在 39 项基准测试任务中，表现最好的多模态配置结合了上述三种信号，并在 37 项任务中优于参考基准。在所有评估的特征组合中，DISTAL 实现了最强的整体性能。这些结果表明，成分预训练和结构蒸馏提供了互补的先验，为小数据材料信息学中稳健的“仅成分”预测提供了一条切实可行的途径。
