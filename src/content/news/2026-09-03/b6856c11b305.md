# Delivery Hero board backs Uber’s $15B takeover bid
# Delivery Hero 董事会支持 Uber 150 亿美元的收购要约

Delivery Hero’s board has signed off on Uber’s $15 billion takeover offer and recommended shareholders approve the deal, which would create one of the largest on-demand food delivery platforms in the world.
Delivery Hero 董事会已签署 Uber 150 亿美元的收购要约，并建议股东批准该交易。此举将打造出全球最大的按需食品配送平台之一。

After reviewing the offer, Delivery Hero’s supervisory and management boards said it was in the best interests of the company, its shareholders, employees, and other stakeholders. The boards also deemed that the price was “fair and adequate” and said the deal had the “potential to accelerate product innovation.”
在审阅了该要约后，Delivery Hero 的监事会和管理委员会表示，此举符合公司、股东、员工及其他利益相关者的最佳利益。董事会还认为该价格“公平且合理”，并表示该交易“有潜力加速产品创新”。

The deal, if approved, would double Uber’s global footprint and make its delivery platform one of the largest in the world outside of China. It could also put Uber in a better position to compete with DoorDash and Just Eat Takeaway.
如果该交易获得批准，Uber 的全球业务版图将扩大一倍，使其配送平台成为中国以外全球最大的平台之一。这也将使 Uber 在与 DoorDash 和 Just Eat Takeaway 的竞争中处于更有利的地位。

Uber, which was already the largest shareholder in Delivery Hero, set a minimum acceptance threshold of 50%, plus one share of Delivery Hero’s outstanding share capital. Prosus, another major shareholder, has agreed to sell its 17% stake in Delivery Hero as well, according to the announcement.
作为 Delivery Hero 最大的股东，Uber 设定了最低接受门槛，即 Delivery Hero 已发行股本的 50% 外加一股。据公告显示，另一大股东 Prosus 也已同意出售其持有的 Delivery Hero 17% 的股份。

Delivery Hero previously agreed to sell its businesses in 14 markets where Uber Eats 已经运营的 14 个市场的业务出售给总部位于纽约的投资公司 SSW Partners，交易金额为 16 亿美元。

The tie-up is the latest example of consolidation in the on-demand delivery industry. In the past 18 months, Uber agreed to acquire Turkey-based Getir for $335 million, Grab said it would buy Delivery Hero’s Foodpanda business in Taiwan for $600 million in cash, and DoorDash said it would pay $3.87 billion for the U.K.’s Deliveroo.
此次合并是按需配送行业整合的最新案例。在过去 18 个月中，Uber 同意以 3.35 亿美元收购土耳其公司 Getir；Grab 表示将以 6 亿美元现金收购 Delivery Hero 在台湾的 Foodpanda 业务；DoorDash 则宣布将以 38.7 亿美元收购英国的 Deliveroo。
