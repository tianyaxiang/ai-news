# ZimaBlue: Evolving Generalizable World Action Models through Scalable Video Pre-training
# ZimaBlue：通过可扩展视频预训练演进通用世界动作模型

**Abstract:** Robotic manipulation faces a fundamental scaling challenge: robust generalization demands broad physical experience, yet action-labeled robot trajectories are expensive to collect and inherently limited in diversity. Egocentric videos offer a far more scalable source of embodied experience, capturing object interactions, contact dynamics, tool use, and long-horizon behaviors across diverse environments. The central challenge is how to convert this abundant but action-free experience into effective robot control.

**摘要：** 机器人操作面临着一个根本性的扩展挑战：稳健的泛化能力需要广泛的物理经验，然而带有动作标签的机器人轨迹收集成本高昂，且在多样性上存在固有局限。第一人称视角（Egocentric）视频提供了一种更具可扩展性的具身经验来源，能够捕捉不同环境下的物体交互、接触动力学、工具使用以及长程行为。核心挑战在于如何将这些丰富但缺乏动作标签的经验转化为有效的机器人控制。

We introduce ZimaBlue, a scalable framework for learning generalizable World Action Models (WAMs) from large-scale video. ZimaBlue follows a three-stage training curriculum: it first performs causal embodied video pre-training on large-scale human and robot egocentric videos, then grounds the learned visual dynamics in heterogeneous robot trajectories through video-action mid-training with a unified action representation, and finally specializes the model to a target robot for deployment.

我们推出了 ZimaBlue，这是一个从大规模视频中学习通用世界动作模型（WAMs）的可扩展框架。ZimaBlue 遵循三阶段训练课程：首先，在大规模人类和机器人第一人称视频上进行因果具身视频预训练；其次，通过统一的动作表示，利用视频-动作中间训练将学到的视觉动力学落地到异构机器人轨迹中；最后，将模型针对目标机器人进行专门化处理以供部署。

To make generative WAMs practical for real-time control, ZimaBlue further adopts an asynchronous Slow-Fast dual-system architecture, where a high-capacity Slow world model provides generalizable spatiotemporal representations and a lightweight Fast branch enables 30 Hz action prediction on NVIDIA RTX 4090.

为了使生成式 WAMs 能够应用于实时控制，ZimaBlue 进一步采用了异步的“慢-快”（Slow-Fast）双系统架构：其中，高容量的“慢”世界模型提供通用的时空表征，而轻量级的“快”分支则在 NVIDIA RTX 4090 上实现了 30 Hz 的动作预测。

On real-robot zero-shot evaluations, scaling from target-robot data alone to over 120,000 hours of embodied video improves success from 36.1% to 77.8%. ZimaBlue further delivers strong performance across multiple benchmarks, with particularly pronounced gains on unseen tasks.

在真实机器人的零样本（zero-shot）评估中，从仅使用目标机器人数据扩展到超过 120,000 小时的具身视频训练，成功率从 36.1% 提升至 77.8%。ZimaBlue 在多个基准测试中均表现出色，特别是在未见过的任务上增益尤为显著。
