# Beyond Point Predictions: A Practical Introduction to Bayesian Neural Networks
# 超越点预测：贝叶斯神经网络实用入门

### The problem with point estimates
### 点估计的问题

When a machine learning model estimates the median value of a house in California, it usually hands you a single number: $385,000. That number may look precise. But hiding behind that estimate is uncertainty, and that uncertainty can arise from a variety of factors. Is the prediction uncertain because the neighborhood features are inherently noisy, or because the model has simply never seen a location quite like this one?
当机器学习模型估算加利福尼亚州房屋的中位数价格时，它通常会给你一个单一的数字：385,000 美元。这个数字看起来很精确，但其背后隐藏着不确定性，而这种不确定性可能源于多种因素。预测的不确定是因为社区特征本身就存在噪声，还是因为模型从未见过类似的地段？

Standard neural networks outputting single numbers cannot answer these questions. They will report error metrics, such as mean absolute error (MAE). Yet, this is just an average across a test dataset. MAE tells me nothing about the specific house I’m looking at right now. Adopting a Bayesian framework allows us to move from asking "What is the predicted home value?" to "What is the range of plausible values, and how sure are we?"
输出单一数值的标准神经网络无法回答这些问题。它们会报告诸如平均绝对误差 (MAE) 之类的指标，但这仅仅是测试数据集上的平均值。MAE 无法告诉我关于我当前正在查看的特定房屋的任何信息。采用贝叶斯框架使我们能够从询问“预测的房价是多少？”转变为“合理的价格范围是多少，以及我们有多大把握？”

Several recent Towards Data Science articles, such as Bayesian Guardrails for AI Decisions, have made excellent cases for considering uncertainty in automated decision-making. In this article, we’ll walk through a practical implementation and the engineering decisions it entails. Specifically, we'll be constructing a Bayesian Neural Network using Python and the California Housing dataset. You will learn how to parameterize weight distributions, run variational inference, and extract actionable uncertainty bounds that reveal exactly when your model should and shouldn't be trusted.
最近《Towards Data Science》上的几篇文章（例如《AI 决策的贝叶斯护栏》）为在自动化决策中考虑不确定性提供了极好的论据。在本文中，我们将通过一个实际的实现过程来探讨其涉及的工程决策。具体来说，我们将使用 Python 和加州住房数据集构建一个贝叶斯神经网络。你将学习如何参数化权重分布、运行变分推理，并提取可操作的不确定性边界，从而准确揭示何时该信任你的模型，何时不该信任。

Ideally, we’d like to plug some numbers into Bayes' theorem and compute the exact solution for our neural network. Unfortunately, this exact calculation is computationally intractable. A neural network with even a few thousand parameters creates a math problem that can be solved in theory but takes way too much time or memory to be useful in practice. As a result, implementing a Bayesian Neural Network leaves us with several design choices and training strategies. This article is focused on understanding those choices and how to implement them. Our shared Python notebooks are not optimized for network layer size or other hyperparameters. Rather, they are intended to highlight the engineering challenges involved in Bayesian Neural Network implementation. For illustrative purposes, each notebook highlights one specific design choice. A production system should include combinations of these choices.
理想情况下，我们希望将数字代入贝叶斯定理并计算出神经网络的精确解。遗憾的是，这种精确计算在计算上是不可行的。即使只有几千个参数的神经网络也会产生一个在理论上可解，但在实践中却因耗时或占用内存过多而无法使用的数学问题。因此，实现贝叶斯神经网络需要我们在多种设计选择和训练策略之间做出权衡。本文旨在理解这些选择以及如何实现它们。我们分享的 Python 笔记本并未针对网络层大小或其他超参数进行优化，而是旨在突出贝叶斯神经网络实现过程中涉及的工程挑战。为了说明起见，每个笔记本都重点介绍了一个特定的设计选择。生产系统应该包含这些选择的组合。

### Uncertainty Quantification
### 不确定性量化

Before getting into the data and code, we need to further explore uncertainty quantification, as not all uncertainty is created equal. Philosophically, there are two broad types of uncertainty that have been discussed since the 17th century. Epistemic uncertainty is generally presented in terms of model uncertainty. We may be asking for a prediction on a home with features our model has never seen before. Conversely, aleatoric uncertainty is commonly presented as uncertainty from natural variability. Our model has seen plenty of houses like this one. Yet, the value of those similar homes is volatile, and our model is uncertain what to predict.
在进入数据和代码之前，我们需要进一步探讨不确定性量化，因为并非所有的不确定性都是一样的。从哲学上讲，自 17 世纪以来，人们一直在讨论两大类不确定性。认知不确定性 (Epistemic uncertainty) 通常表现为模型不确定性。例如，我们可能要求模型对一个具有其从未见过的特征的房屋进行预测。相反，偶然不确定性 (Aleatoric uncertainty) 通常表现为源于自然变异的不确定性。我们的模型可能见过很多类似的房子，但这些类似房屋的价值波动很大，导致模型无法确定该预测什么。

Distinguishing between these two types of uncertainty is useful in practice, and there are mathematical formalisms that claim to separate aleatoric from epistemic uncertainty. Yet, the research literature has ended up with multiple mathematical definitions for the same philosophical concepts, and in some cases, conflicting definitions for the same concept. Current research is pointing us toward the unfortunate realization that aleatoric and epistemic uncertainty are mathematically intertwined in much of machine learning.
在实践中区分这两种不确定性非常有用，并且有一些数学形式声称可以将偶然不确定性与认知不确定性分离开来。然而，研究文献最终对相同的哲学概念给出了多种数学定义，在某些情况下，甚至对同一个概念给出了相互冲突的定义。目前的研究正指向一个令人遗憾的现实：在许多机器学习场景中，偶然不确定性和认知不确定性在数学上是交织在一起的。

The blog post Reexamining the Aleatoric and Epistemic Uncertainty Dichotomy has an excellent deep dive into this issue. For this article, we’ll restrict ourselves to a single uncertainty estimator without the specific labeling of aleatoric and epistemic.
博客文章《重新审视偶然与认知不确定性的二分法》对这一问题进行了深入探讨。在本文中，我们将仅使用单一的不确定性估计器，而不对偶然不确定性和认知不确定性进行具体标注。

### Comparison of traditional neural networks and Bayesian neural networks
### 传统神经网络与贝叶斯神经网络的比较

A standard neural network is essentially a massive machine with millions of tiny knobs called weights. Each weight controls how much one piece of information influences the final answer. In supervised learning, a network is given inputs and known outputs; the neural network makes predictions and looks at how far off each prediction is. It goes back through all those millions of knobs and slightly adjusts them so that, next time, the answer is a little closer to the correct answer. A standard network gives us a single, firm answer. It doesn’t know how to say “I don’t know.”
标准神经网络本质上是一台拥有数百万个微小旋钮（称为权重）的庞大机器。每个权重控制着一条信息对最终答案的影响程度。在监督学习中，网络被赋予输入和已知的输出；神经网络进行预测并查看每个预测的偏差程度。它会回溯这数百万个旋钮并进行微调，以便下次预测时答案更接近正确结果。标准网络给我们的是一个单一、确定的答案。它不知道如何说“我不知道”。
