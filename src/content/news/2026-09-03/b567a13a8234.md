# Pangram’s Max Spero on why AI detection is harder than ‘Real or Fake’
# Pangram 联合创始人 Max Spero：为何 AI 检测远不止“真假辨别”那么简单

The internet has a trust problem, and it’s not just because social media feeds are filling up with AI slop. AI-generated text and images are now making their way into job applications, product reviews, and even insurance claims, leaving platforms and users alike scrambling to figure out what’s real.
互联网正面临信任危机，这不仅仅是因为社交媒体信息流中充斥着 AI 生成的垃圾内容。如今，AI 生成的文本和图像已经渗透到求职申请、产品评论甚至保险理赔中，导致平台和用户都在忙于辨别真伪。

A handful of startups have cropped up in the past couple of years to become the “trust layer” the internet needs — including Pangram. The startup recently snapped up $9 million for its AI detection system and landed a partnership with Substack, which is now using Pangram’s tech to show readers which of their favorite authors use AI to write their newsletters. Pangram also recently dropped a new AI image detection tool.
过去几年里，涌现出了一批初创公司，旨在成为互联网所急需的“信任层”，Pangram 就是其中之一。这家初创公司最近为其 AI 检测系统筹集了 900 万美元，并与 Substack 达成了合作。Substack 目前正在使用 Pangram 的技术，向读者展示他们喜爱的作者是否使用了 AI 来撰写时事通讯。此外，Pangram 最近还发布了一款全新的 AI 图像检测工具。

Watch as Pangram co-founder and CEO Max Spero joins TechCrunch’s Equity podcast to dig into the promise of AI detection tools and where to draw the line between AI assisted and AI generated.
