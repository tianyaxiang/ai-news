# Proactive cyber defense for governments and enterprises
# 面向政府与企业的积极主动式网络防御

Today, we’re launching our Fairwind Program, a limited access program for governments and trusted partners to use our most advanced cyber defense capabilities.
今天，我们正式推出“Fairwind计划”（Fairwind Program）。这是一项针对政府及受信任合作伙伴的有限访问计划，旨在让他们能够使用我们最先进的网络防御功能。

Defenders wanting to use advanced AI have faced a difficult dilemma: adopt enormous frontier models that could be expensive to deploy and difficult to control across enterprise codebases, or turn to smaller open-weight models that might struggle with complex vulnerability remediation and require teams to build their own tooling and infrastructure from scratch. Until now.
想要利用先进人工智能的防御者们一直面临着一个两难困境：要么采用庞大的前沿模型，但这些模型部署成本高昂且难以在企业代码库中进行管控；要么转向较小的开放权重模型，但它们可能难以处理复杂的漏洞修复，且需要团队从零开始构建自己的工具和基础设施。而现在，这一切将有所改变。

Today, we’re launching our Fairwind Program to bring the best of Google’s AI and cyber defense capabilities to a trusted group of Google Cloud customers, government agencies, and cybersecurity partners, to help them proactively solve cyber risks at scale. As a first step, the Fairwind Program will give defenders access to powerful and advanced Gemini models to help them autonomously find and fix vulnerabilities, protecting critical infrastructure, public services, and national security.
今天，我们推出Fairwind计划，旨在将Google最顶尖的AI与网络防御能力带给受信任的Google Cloud客户、政府机构及网络安全合作伙伴，帮助他们大规模地主动解决网络风险。作为第一步，Fairwind计划将为防御者提供强大的先进Gemini模型，帮助他们自主发现并修复漏洞，从而保护关键基础设施、公共服务和国家安全。

### Finding and autonomously fixing vulnerabilities
### 发现并自主修复漏洞

The Fairwind Program offerings bring together our most advanced cyber model, Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber, with our CodeMender harness, to help defenders find, verify, and fix vulnerabilities at agentic scale. Spotting weaknesses creates awareness and fear; autonomously finding and fixing vulnerabilities delivers security.
Fairwind计划的产品组合汇集了我们最先进的网络模型“Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber”以及我们的“CodeMender”工具，旨在帮助防御者以智能体（Agentic）规模发现、验证并修复漏洞。发现弱点能带来警觉与忧虑，而自主发现并修复漏洞才能真正实现安全。

CodeMender with Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber delivers the specialized reasoning to write and validate code fixes, at a fraction of the operating cost of traditional frontier models. Instead of taking weeks to manually fix vulnerabilities, defenders can now generate verified, deployment-ready patches in minutes — within an organization’s secure cloud environment.
搭载Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber的CodeMender具备编写和验证代码修复方案的专业推理能力，其运营成本仅为传统前沿模型的一小部分。防御者无需再耗费数周时间手动修复漏洞，现在可以在几分钟内——在组织的安全云环境中——生成经过验证且可直接部署的补丁。

### Scaling frontline defense
### 扩展前线防御

Providing early access to these powerful cyber capabilities gives trusted defenders a vital adaptation window to harden their systems before bad actors have a chance to exploit new capabilities. We’re staging initial access to government and enterprise partners most critical to society’s resilience:
为受信任的防御者提供这些强大网络功能的早期访问权限，能为他们争取到至关重要的适应窗口期，以便在恶意攻击者利用新技术之前加固系统。我们正在为对社会韧性至关重要的政府和企业合作伙伴分阶段开放访问权限：

*   **Governments and national cyber authorities:** Hardening public-sector networks and citizen services against targeted intrusions.
*   **政府与国家网络安全机构：** 加固公共部门网络和公民服务，抵御针对性入侵。
*   **Critical infrastructure operators:** Protecting essential services across healthcare, telecommunications, energy, and financial networks from operational disruption.
*   **关键基础设施运营商：** 保护医疗、电信、能源和金融网络中的基本服务，防止运营中断。
*   **Core technology platforms:** Securing widespread software foundations to uplift digital security for millions of downstream users at once.
*   **核心技术平台：** 保护广泛使用的软件基础，从而一次性提升数百万下游用户的数字安全水平。

To ensure these powerful AI capabilities are used responsibly, participating organizations agree to strict operational standards, including limiting access to employees within their internal cybersecurity, incident response, or penetration testing teams and deploying protections like multi-factor authentication.
为确保这些强大的AI功能得到负责任的使用，参与组织需遵守严格的运营标准，包括将访问权限限制在内部网络安全、事件响应或渗透测试团队成员范围内，并部署多因素身份验证等保护措施。

### What our Fairwind Program partners are saying
### Fairwind计划合作伙伴的反馈

Trusted defenders and industry leaders are already putting Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber into practice: The Fairwind Program will evolve alongside our partners and users' needs. We will adapt our product offerings and expand partner access, collaborating closely with industry, governments, and open-weight community leaders to strike the right balance between open access and robust security.
受信任的防御者和行业领袖们已经开始将Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber投入实践：Fairwind计划将随着合作伙伴和用户的需求不断演进。我们将调整产品供应并扩大合作伙伴访问权限，与行业、政府及开放权重社区的领导者密切合作，在开放访问与稳健安全之间取得恰当的平衡。

While we are prioritizing Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber access for customers in the Fairwind Program, any Google Cloud customer can proactively secure their code by using CodeMender with publicly available models hosted on Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform, in combination with industry-leading solutions offered through AI Threat Defense.
虽然我们优先为Fairwind计划的客户提供Gemini 3.8 Flash Cyber访问权限，但任何Google Cloud客户都可以通过使用CodeMender，结合Gemini Enterprise Agent Platform上托管的公开模型，以及AI Threat Defense提供的行业领先解决方案，来主动保护其代码安全。

The Fairwind Program builds on this experience and is part of our broader commitment to global cyber resilience across the entire digital ecosystem, including helping to fortify grassroots cyber defense.
多年来，Google在零信任架构、先进AI防御和内置安全方面的开创性工作，使我们能够每天保护数十亿个账户，确保比任何其他公司都多的个人和组织在线安全。Fairwind计划正是建立在这些经验之上，是我们致力于提升整个数字生态系统全球网络韧性的更广泛承诺的一部分，其中包括帮助加强基层网络防御。

通过Google.org，我们最新的承诺使我们在全球范围内的网络安全资助总额超过了1亿美元。我们很高兴发布《2026年Google.org美国网络安全影响报告》，报告详细说明了迄今为止为35家网络安全诊所提供的3600万美元资助，这些诊所为美国超过1250家医院、公立学区和市政公用事业机构提供了免费的实操安全支持。

### Providing a security advantage
### 提供安全优势

The defender’s edge comes from shrinking the time between detecting a flaw and patching it. Through Google’s Fairwind Program, government and enterprise partners gain autonomous tools to repair systems faster and at scale, keeping them one step ahead of agentic-speed threats.
防御者的优势在于缩短从发现漏洞到修复漏洞的时间。通过Google的Fairwind计划，政府和企业合作伙伴获得了能够更快、更大规模修复系统的自主工具，使他们能够在面对智能体速度的威胁时始终领先一步。
