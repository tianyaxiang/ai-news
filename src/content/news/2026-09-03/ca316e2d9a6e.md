# Beyond Blind Compliance: Benchmarking Task Verification in OCR Reasoning
# 超越盲目顺从：OCR 推理中的任务验证基准测试

Multimodal Large Language Models (MLLMs) have achieved strong performance on OCR-centric document understanding and text-rich visual reasoning benchmarks. Yet existing evaluations largely assume that every task is valid and answerable. In real-world OCR scenarios, this assumption often fails: questions may rely on illegible text, occluded evidence, nonexistent visual targets, contradictory premises, or missing variables.
多模态大语言模型（MLLMs）在以 OCR 为中心的文档理解和富文本视觉推理基准测试中取得了优异表现。然而，现有的评估方法大多假设每个任务都是有效且可回答的。在现实世界的 OCR 场景中，这一假设往往失效：问题可能依赖于难以辨认的文本、被遮挡的证据、不存在的视觉目标、矛盾的前提或缺失的变量。

We study this reliability gap as OCR-grounded Task Verification: before answering, a model should determine whether the Image Premise (IP), Textual Premise (TP), and Question (Q) jointly define an executable task. We introduce VeriOCRBench, a 1,800-sample human-verified benchmark built from source images drawn from 8 OCR-related datasets and spanning 8 real-world image domains, with controlled, image-grounded diagnostic tasks.
我们将这种可靠性差距定义为“基于 OCR 的任务验证”：在回答之前，模型应判断图像前提（IP）、文本前提（TP）和问题（Q）是否共同构成了一个可执行的任务。我们引入了 VeriOCRBench，这是一个包含 1,800 个样本的人工验证基准，其源图像取自 8 个 OCR 相关数据集，涵盖 8 个现实世界的图像领域，并包含受控的、基于图像的诊断任务。

It contains 1,600 trap-injected invalid tasks across 8 trap types and four verification dimensions---Visual, Contextual, Factual, and Logical---plus 200 trap-free controls for measuring over-refusal. Built with a Visual Atomic Fact (VAF)-anchored pipeline and full human auditing, VeriOCRBench enables decoupled evaluation of task verification, root-cause diagnosis, and over-refusal.
该基准包含 1,600 个注入了陷阱的无效任务，涵盖 8 种陷阱类型和四个验证维度（视觉、语境、事实和逻辑），以及 200 个用于衡量“过度拒绝”的无陷阱对照组。VeriOCRBench 基于视觉原子事实（VAF）锚定流程并经过全面的人工审核，能够对任务验证、根本原因诊断和过度拒绝进行解耦评估。

Evaluating 15 leading MLLMs reveals persistent blind compliance, diagnosis failures, and prompt-induced over-refusal, exposing a critical reliability gap in current OCR reasoning systems.
对 15 个主流 MLLM 的评估揭示了持续存在的盲目顺从、诊断失败以及由提示词诱导的过度拒绝现象，暴露了当前 OCR 推理系统中存在的严重可靠性差距。
