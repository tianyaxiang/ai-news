# Distributed Implicit Harm: A Compositional Safety Blind Spot in MLLM-Based Video Moderation
# 分布式隐性危害：基于多模态大模型（MLLM）视频审核中的组合安全性盲点

**Abstract:** Despite their growing use in video moderation, multimodal large language models (MLLMs) exhibit a compositional safety blind spot: videos composed of seemingly benign components can convey harmful meaning when interpreted as a whole. We refer to this phenomenon as Distributed Implicit Harm (DIH), where harm arises from relations among components distributed along a decomposition axis of the video, rather than from any single explicit cue.

**摘要：** 尽管多模态大模型（MLLM）在视频审核中的应用日益广泛，但它们表现出一个组合安全性盲点：由看似良性的组件构成的视频，在整体解读时却可能传达出有害含义。我们将这种现象称为“分布式隐性危害”（Distributed Implicit Harm, DIH）。在这种情况下，危害并非源于任何单一的显性线索，而是源于沿视频分解轴分布的各组件之间的关系。

Among many possible axes, we study two representative cases: temporally distributed harm across visual segments (DIH-T) and cross-modal harm between audio and visual streams (DIH-M). Studying and mitigating DIH at scale requires data that is difficult to collect: such videos lack compositional harm annotations, evade retrieval based on local visual cues, keywords, or single-modality signals, and are consequently absent from existing safety datasets.

在众多可能的维度中，我们研究了两个代表性案例：跨视觉片段的时间分布型危害（DIH-T）以及音频与视觉流之间的跨模态危害（DIH-M）。在大规模研究和缓解 DIH 时，需要获取难以收集的数据：这类视频缺乏组合性危害标注，无法通过局部视觉线索、关键词或单模态信号进行检索，因此在现有的安全数据集中缺失。

To bridge this gap, we develop a multi-agent synthesis framework that composes individually benign components into harmful scenarios and generates diverse DIH videos with explicit reasoning annotations, yielding a dataset of over 9,000 videos spanning visual-only and audio-visual settings.

为了弥补这一差距，我们开发了一个多智能体合成框架，将单独良性的组件组合成有害场景，并生成带有明确推理标注的多样化 DIH 视频，从而构建了一个包含超过 9,000 个视频的数据集，涵盖了纯视觉和视听结合的场景。

Benchmarking over 30 MLLMs spanning frontier proprietary models and leading open-source systems reveals substantial and consistent deficits in detecting both DIH-T and DIH-M. Notably, this failure persists even among the strongest frontier models: they often correctly assess individual components in isolation but fail to recognize the harmful meaning that emerges from their composition.

对涵盖前沿专有模型和领先开源系统的 30 多种 MLLM 进行基准测试后发现，它们在检测 DIH-T 和 DIH-M 方面存在显著且持续的缺陷。值得注意的是，即使在最强大的前沿模型中，这种失败依然存在：它们通常能正确评估孤立的单个组件，却无法识别由这些组件组合而产生的有害含义。

We further evaluate these models on a manually collected set of real-world DIH videos from social media and observe the same failure mode, highlighting DIH as a practical and underexplored challenge for video moderation.

我们进一步在从社交媒体手动收集的一组真实世界 DIH 视频上评估了这些模型，并观察到了同样的失败模式，这凸显了 DIH 是视频审核领域一个亟待探索的实际挑战。
