# The best tech and gadgets announced at IFA so far
# IFA 2026 迄今为止发布的最佳科技产品与小工具

A gaming handheld you can use for work, a robovac that seals itself to floors, and more laptops you can repair yourself.
一款既能工作又能玩游戏的掌机、一款能自动密封贴合地面的扫地机器人，以及更多可自行维修的笔记本电脑。

The doors to Europe’s largest consumer tech show haven’t opened to the public yet, but there’s already plenty of news coming out of IFA 2026 in Berlin, Germany. If you’re already struggling to keep up with what has been announced, here are some of the best new gadgets and upgrades from the show — including more competition for Apple’s MacBook Neo, an excellent portable controller for gaming-on-the-go, and a concept handheld designed to balance work and play.
尽管欧洲最大的消费电子展尚未正式向公众开放，但德国柏林的 IFA 2026 已经传出了大量新闻。如果你已经跟不上发布节奏，以下是展会上一些最棒的新设备和升级产品——包括针对苹果 MacBook Neo 的更多竞争对手、一款出色的便携式游戏手柄，以及一款旨在平衡工作与娱乐的概念掌机。

### Acer Project DualPlay Mini Concept
### 宏碁 Project DualPlay Mini 概念机

Flipping the gaming laptop script, Acer’s latest concept is a Windows-based gaming handheld, similar to its Predator Atlas 8, that can transform into a tiny portable PC thanks to a swiveling design that repositions its screen atop a mini keyboard. The DualPlay Mini’s display is just 8-inches in size, which could make productivity a challenge if work involves sprawling spreadsheets, but the concept could be used for answering emails during a daily commute and then connected to a larger display at work for more demanding tasks.
宏碁的最新概念机颠覆了游戏笔记本电脑的传统模式，这是一款基于 Windows 的游戏掌机，类似于其 Predator Atlas 8。得益于旋转设计，它可以将屏幕重新定位在迷你键盘上方，从而变身为一台微型便携式 PC。DualPlay Mini 的显示屏仅为 8 英寸，如果工作涉及庞大的电子表格，生产力可能会面临挑战，但该概念机可用于日常通勤时回复邮件，并在工作中连接到更大的显示器以处理更繁重的任务。

### Dell’s next MacBook Neo Competitor
### 戴尔的下一款 MacBook Neo 竞争对手

The Dell 14S isn’t as thin or light as its recently launched XPS 13, but the company is looking to make this model even more accessible for students or others on a tighter budget. The laptop starts with 8GB of RAM and 256GB of storage, and those specs can be bumped to 16GB/1TB. It features a 1080P webcam with face unlock support, two USB-C ports, a headphone jack, and like Apple’s MacBook Neo, will be available in four bright color options: linen, dusty rose, washed denim, and velvet green.
戴尔 14S 虽然不如近期发布的 XPS 13 那样轻薄，但戴尔旨在让该型号更适合学生或其他预算有限的用户。这款笔记本电脑起步配置为 8GB 内存和 256GB 存储空间，最高可升级至 16GB/1TB。它配备了支持人脸解锁的 1080P 网络摄像头、两个 USB-C 接口、一个耳机插孔，并且像苹果的 MacBook Neo 一样，提供四种明亮的配色：亚麻色、灰粉色、水洗牛仔蓝和天鹅绒绿。

### iRobot Roomba Max 875 Combo Robovac
### iRobot Roomba Max 875 Combo 扫地机器人

The $1,199 Roomba Max 875 Combo is iRobot’s latest flagship floor cleaner and introduces a new feature the company calls “SealForce.” When it detects it’s cleaning carpeting, the robovac lowers a skirt around the cleaning head to create a better seal that increases suction for a deeper clean. The Max 875 also offers up to 35,000 Pa of suction power, mopping capabilities, lidar and camera-based autonomous navigation, and the ability to climb over obstacles and thresholds up to 3.5cm tall.
售价 1,199 美元的 Roomba Max 875 Combo 是 iRobot 最新的旗舰级地面清洁设备，引入了一项名为“SealForce”的新功能。当它检测到正在清洁地毯时，扫地机器人会降低清洁头周围的裙边，以形成更好的密封效果，从而增加吸力进行深度清洁。Max 875 还提供高达 35,000 Pa 的吸力、拖地功能、基于激光雷达和摄像头的自主导航，以及攀爬高达 3.5 厘米障碍物和门槛的能力。

### Jsaux SkyLark S1 Pro Portable Controller
### Jsaux SkyLark S1 Pro 便携式手柄

Released under Jsaux’s new Voidjoy brand, the $49.99 SkyLark S1 Pro is a compact portable controller that appears to do a lot of things right. It’s got drift-resistant TMR thumbsticks that are recessed so they won’t get snagged on other devices in your bag, a pair of analog triggers ideal for racing games, and three different ways to connect to devices and consoles: Bluetooth, USB-C, and 2.4GHz. If you opt for a 2.4GHz connection, you can also pair two S1 Pros to a single transmitter and swap back-and-forth between what character you’re controlling in a multiplayer game, making it useful for parents playing with a kid who may struggle with some challenges.
售价 49.99 美元的 SkyLark S1 Pro 在 Jsaux 的新品牌 Voidjoy 下发布，是一款紧凑型便携手柄，在多方面表现出色。它配备了防漂移的 TMR 摇杆，且采用内凹设计，不会在包里被其他设备钩住；它还拥有一对适合赛车游戏的模拟扳机键，并支持三种连接方式：蓝牙、USB-C 和 2.4GHz。如果选择 2.4GHz 连接，还可以将两个 S1 Pro 配对到同一个发射器上，并在多人游戏中切换控制的角色，这对于与孩子一起玩游戏、帮助孩子应对挑战的家长来说非常实用。

### Acer Vero 16 Upgradeable Laptop
### 宏碁 Vero 16 可升级笔记本电脑

Is Framework’s approach to easily repairable and upgradeable laptops finally rubbing off on other companies? Acer’s 16-inch Vero 16 offers tool-free access to several internal components including its user-replaceable SSD and battery. The company says the laptop’s processor can also be upgraded down the line, but that will require it to be sent in to an Acer service center. Pricing is still unknown, but the Vero 16 is expected to launch early next year outside North America.
Framework 易于维修和升级的笔记本电脑理念终于影响到其他公司了吗？宏碁 16 英寸的 Vero 16 允许用户无需工具即可访问多个内部组件，包括可自行更换的 SSD 和电池。宏碁表示，该笔记本电脑的处理器未来也可以升级，但这需要送至宏碁服务中心进行操作。目前价格尚不清楚，但 Vero 16 预计将于明年年初在北美以外地区上市。

### Jsaux Elite Extender
### Jsaux Elite Extender 扩展器

If you’re lucky enough to have an original Nintendo Switch Pro controller that hasn’t succumbed to joystick drift and want to expand its capabilities, Jsaux’s new Elite Extender is an easy aftermarket upgrade. For $39.99 it attaches to the back of the controller and adds four mappable buttons that can be programmed to reproduce multiple button presses. It’s got its own rechargeable battery, but does hinder Amiibo functionality while it’s piggybacking.
如果你足够幸运，拥有一款尚未出现摇杆漂移的原装任天堂 Switch Pro 手柄，并希望扩展其功能，那么 Jsaux 的新款 Elite Extender 是一个简单的售后升级方案。售价 39.99 美元，它可安装在手柄背面，增加四个可映射的按钮，并可编程以模拟多次按键操作。它拥有独立的充电电池，但在安装后会阻碍 Amiibo 功能的使用。
