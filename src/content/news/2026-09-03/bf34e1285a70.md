# Attention Sensitivity Is Not Enough: Dissociating Attention-Level and Behavioural In-Context Learning under Fine-Tuning

**Title:** Attention Sensitivity Is Not Enough: Dissociating Attention-Level and Behavioural In-Context Learning under Fine-Tuning
**标题：** 注意力敏感度并不足够：微调下注意力层面与行为层面上下文学习的解耦

**Abstract:** In-context learning (ICL) lets large language models adapt to new tasks from demonstrations, and fine-tuning can erode this behaviour. Many preservation diagnostics inspect attention: if attention changes when demonstrations change, the model is treated as context-sensitive. This paper asks how far that proxy can be trusted once it is optimised.
**摘要：** 上下文学习（ICL）使大型语言模型能够通过演示适应新任务，而微调可能会侵蚀这种行为。许多保存诊断方法会检查注意力：如果注意力随演示的变化而改变，则模型被视为具有上下文敏感性。本文探讨了在优化该代理指标后，它究竟在多大程度上值得信赖。

We formalise \emph{In-Context Sensitivity} (ICS), the average row distance between last-token attention on matched and mismatched demonstration prefixes, and pair it with \emph{ICL-GAP}, the behavioural accuracy gap between the same prefixes. In a controlled four-arm ablation on Llama-2-7B, an ICS-maximising regulariser ($\armKL$) drives ICS to $1.413$, within $0.5\%$ of its geometric ceiling.
我们形式化了“上下文敏感度”（ICS），即匹配与不匹配演示前缀上最后一个 token 注意力之间的平均行距离，并将其与“ICL-GAP”（即相同前缀之间的行为准确率差距）配对。在 Llama-2-7B 上进行的受控四臂消融实验中，一种最大化 ICS 的正则化器（$\armKL$）将 ICS 推至 1.413，处于其几何上限的 0.5% 以内。

The behavioural readout tells a different story: ICL-GAP stays near zero and MMLU accuracy moves from $0.371$ to $0.279$, a Goodhart dissociation of the bounded attention proxy. Endpoint statistics locate the mechanism: attention grows sharp and near-disjoint across prefixes yet routes to formatting and demonstration-body tokens rather than labels.
行为层面的结果则截然不同：ICL-GAP 保持在接近零的水平，而 MMLU 准确率从 0.371 下降至 0.279，这体现了受限注意力代理指标的古德哈特定律（Goodhart's Law）解耦现象。端点统计定位了其机制：注意力在不同前缀间变得尖锐且几乎不相交，但却指向了格式和演示主体 token，而非标签。

A random-label protocol confirms that the behavioural probe family retains dynamic range at the same checkpoints. In a constructive sweep, behaviour gating partially mitigates the effect, while objectives anchored to pretrained computation hold the high-MMLU, moderate-ICS region that divergence maximisers leave.
随机标签协议证实，行为探测族在相同的检查点仍保留了动态范围。在建设性的扫描中，行为门控部分缓解了这种影响，而锚定于预训练计算的目标函数则保持了高 MMLU 和中等 ICS 的区域，这是发散最大化方法所遗漏的。

The main lesson is diagnostic: attention-level ICL proxies earn their place as training targets only after validation against behavioural gaps.
主要启示在于诊断层面：注意力层面的 ICL 代理指标只有在经过行为差距验证后，才有资格作为训练目标。
