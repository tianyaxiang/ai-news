# LG’s RGB LED TV is good for certain situations, but an OLED is better

LG 的 RGB LED 电视在某些情况下表现不错，但 OLED 电视会是更好的选择。

A bunch of little issues add up to make the MRGB95B not worth the price when competitors do it better.

一系列小问题累积起来，使得 MRGB95B 在竞争对手表现更好的情况下显得物无所值。

The LG’s picture is bright and punchy, but its panel is very reflective.

LG 的画面明亮且富有冲击力，但其面板的反光非常严重。

When it comes to TVs, the LG name has long been synonymous with OLEDs, continually leading Samsung and Sony as the top OLED manufacturer. But 2026 is all about RGB LED TVs. Almost every major company has released models with the new technology — including LG and its $3,600 75-inch Micro RGB evo MRGB95B that I’ve been testing.

在电视领域，LG 的名字长期以来一直是 OLED 的代名词，作为顶级 OLED 制造商持续领先于三星和索尼。但 2026 年是 RGB LED 电视的天下。几乎每家大公司都发布了采用这项新技术的产品，包括我一直在测试的 LG 售价 3600 美元的 75 英寸 Micro RGB evo MRGB95B。

And while LG’s RGB LED TV benefits from the brightness and color capability the technology provides, it has issues I couldn’t overlook that its less expensive competitors don’t.

虽然 LG 的这款 RGB LED 电视受益于该技术所提供的亮度和色彩能力，但它存在一些我无法忽视的问题，而其价格更低的竞争对手却没有这些问题。

The MRGB95B comes in three sizes, with the 75-inch being the smallest. It has four HDMI 2.1 ports, supports Dolby Vision — although not Dolby Vision 2 or HDR10+ — and comes with the Magic Remote design LG unveiled last year.

MRGB95B 有三种尺寸，其中 75 英寸是最小的。它拥有四个 HDMI 2.1 端口，支持杜比视界（尽管不支持杜比视界 2 或 HDR10+），并配备了 LG 去年推出的动感应遥控器设计。

As with all LG TVs, the MRGB95B runs on webOS. It runs quickly, but I find it less intuitive to navigate than Google TV, Apple TV, or Roku. It supports all of the major streaming apps.

与所有 LG 电视一样，MRGB95B 运行 webOS 系统。它运行速度很快，但我发现其导航界面不如 Google TV、Apple TV 或 Roku 直观。它支持所有主流流媒体应用程序。

Speaking of software, a quick note: LG has recently come under increased scrutiny after a video from Gamers Nexus showed security vulnerabilities and continuous audio recording on its TVs, which LG has denied.

说到软件，提一下：在 Gamers Nexus 的一段视频显示其电视存在安全漏洞和持续录音问题后，LG 最近受到了越来越多的审查，LG 对此予以否认。

When setting up the MRGB95B, it’s only necessary to accept the terms of use and privacy policy if you want to use webOS. Otherwise, as with any TV, it can be disconnected from the internet, although an external streaming device would be needed for any streaming services.

在设置 MRGB95B 时，只有在想要使用 webOS 时才需要接受使用条款和隐私政策。否则，就像任何电视一样，它可以断开互联网连接，尽管使用任何流媒体服务都需要外接流媒体设备。

RGB LED TVs use zones of individual red, green, and blue LEDs as backlights as opposed to a field of white or blue LEDs that rely on a color filter or quantum dots to create the colors that reach our eyes.

RGB LED 电视使用独立的红、绿、蓝 LED 区域作为背光，而不是依靠滤色片或量子点来产生进入我们眼睛的颜色的白光或蓝光 LED 面板。

As with all of the RGB LED TVs I’ve seen this year, the 95B gets very bright — I measured a peak of 4,011 nits in HDR Filmmaker mode — allowing it to deliver an impactful image even in a sunlit living room.

正如我今年看到的所有 RGB LED 电视一样，95B 的亮度非常高——我在 HDR 电影制作人模式下测得峰值亮度为 4011 尼特——这使得它即使在阳光充足的客厅里也能提供极具冲击力的画面。

At the other end of the spectrum, though, the LG struggles a bit. Near-black shadow detail is difficult to see. And when there are bright moments within a dark portion of the screen, the LG exhibits more blooming than I’ve seen on other RGB LED TVs.

然而，在光谱的另一端，LG 的表现有些吃力。近乎黑色的阴影细节很难看清。当屏幕的黑暗部分出现明亮时刻时，LG 表现出的光晕现象比我在其他 RGB LED 电视上看到的要多。

The LG 95B can display really vibrant colors thanks to the RGB LED technology. It’s able to cover 100 percent of the P3 color gamut and nearly 90 percent of the larger BT.2020 color area. But even in Filmmaker mode out of the box, those colors still show visible accuracy issues.

得益于 RGB LED 技术，LG 95B 可以显示非常鲜艳的色彩。它能够覆盖 100% 的 P3 色域和近 90% 的更大范围的 BT.2020 色域。但即使是在开箱即用的电影制作人模式下，这些色彩仍然显示出明显的准确性问题。

While the TV performs well in a bright room during the day, when the sun goes down and the lamps turn on, they can wreak havoc on the image. The 95B’s glossy coating is one of the most reflective I’ve seen in a long time.

虽然这台电视在白天的明亮房间里表现良好，但当太阳下山、灯光亮起时，它们会严重破坏画面。95B 的光面涂层是我很长一段时间以来见过的反光最严重的涂层之一。

Viewing angles are always a problem for LED TVs — RGB or not — and that’s evident on the LG, too. When only moderately off-axis, the colors become washed out, losing their vibrancy for anyone who’s sitting off to the side.

视角问题对于 LED 电视（无论是否为 RGB）来说一直是个难题，这一点在 LG 电视上也显而易见。当视角稍微偏离中心时，色彩就会变得暗淡，对于坐在侧面的人来说，色彩会失去活力。
